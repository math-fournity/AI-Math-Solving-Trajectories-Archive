## Solution

We find a transversal $S \subset \mathbb{Q}_2^\times$ for the subgroup of square
s $\mathbb{Q}_2^{\times 2}$, i.e. a set of representatives for the quotient $\ma
thbb{Q}_2^\times / \mathbb{Q}_2^{\times 2}$.

**Step 1: Decomposition of $\mathbb{Q}_2^\times$.** Every $x \in \mathbb{Q}_2^\t
imes$ has a unique factorization
$$x = 2^{v_2(x)} \cdot u, \qquad u \in \mathbb{Z}_2^\times.$$
Thus $\mathbb{Q}_2^\times \cong \mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}_2^\times$ (via $v_2
$ and the unit part). Squaring acts as $(n, u) \mapsto (2n, u^2)$, so
$$\mathbb{Q}_2^\times / \mathbb{Q}_2^{\times 2} \cong (\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}) \
times \big(\mathbb{Z}_2^\times / (\mathbb{Z}_2^\times)^2\big).$$

**Step 2: Structure of $\mathbb{Z}_2^\times$.** We use the standard splitting
$$\mathbb{Z}_2^\times = \{\pm 1\} \times (1 + 4\mathbb{Z}_2),$$
where the map $\epsilon(1+4a) \mapsto (\epsilon, 1+4a)$ is a bijection (since $1
+4\mathbb{Z}_2$ is a subgroup and $\{\pm1\}$ is a complement: $-1 \notin 1+4\mat
hbb{Z}_2$ because $-1 \equiv 3 \pmod 4$). The subgroup $1+4\mathbb{Z}_2$ is pro-
cyclic, isomorphic to $(\mathbb{Z}_2, +)$ via the $2$-adic logarithm.

**Step 3: Computing $(\mathbb{Z}_2^\times)^2$.** For $u = \epsilon(1+4a)$ with $
\epsilon \in \{\pm 1\}$,
$$u^2 = (1+4a)^2 = 1 + 8a + 16a^2 \in 1 + 8\mathbb{Z}_2.$$
Conversely, every element of $1+8\mathbb{Z}_2$ is a square in $\mathbb{Z}_2^\tim
es$: given $y \in 1+8\mathbb{Z}_2$, the polynomial $f(T) = T^2 - y$ has $f(1) =
1-y \in 8\mathbb{Z}_2$ and $f'(1) = 2 \notin 4\mathbb{Z}_2$... — more directly,
since $1+4\mathbb{Z}_2 \cong \mathbb{Z}_2$ (additively) and squaring corresponds
 (under $\log$) to multiplication by $2$, the image is $2\mathbb{Z}_2$, i.e. $1+
8\mathbb{Z}_2$. Hence
$$(\mathbb{Z}_2^\times)^2 = 1 + 8\mathbb{Z}_2.$$

**Step 4: The unit quotient.** Therefore
$$\mathbb{Z}_2^\times / (\mathbb{Z}_2^\times)^2 = \mathbb{Z}_2^\times / (1+8\mat
hbb{Z}_2) \cong (\mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z})^\times = \{1, 3, 5, 7\},$$
a group of order $4$. A set of unit representatives is $\{1, 3, 5, 7\}$ (the odd
 residues mod $8$).

**Step 5: Assembling the transversal.** Combining Steps 1 and 4, the quotient $\
mathbb{Q}_2^\times / \mathbb{Q}_2^{\times 2}$ has order $2 \times 4 = 8$, and a
complete set of representatives is obtained by taking $2^a \cdot r$ with $a \in
\{0, 1\}$ and $r \in \{1, 3, 5, 7\}$:
$$\boxed{S = \{1,\; 3,\; 5,\; 7,\; 2,\; 6,\; 10,\; 14\}.}$$

**Verification.** Two elements $2^a r$ and $2^{a'} r'$ (with $a,a' \in \{0,1\}$,
 $r,r' \in \{1,3,5,7\}$) lie in the same coset of $\mathbb{Q}_2^{\times 2}$ iff
$a \equiv a' \pmod 2$ and $r \equiv r' \pmod{8}$. The first condition forces $a
= a'$; the second forces $r = r'$ (since $r, r' \in \{1,3,5,7\}$ are distinct mo
d $8$). Hence the $8$ elements are pairwise in distinct cosets, and since $|\mat
hbb{Q}_2^\times / \mathbb{Q}_2^{\times 2}| = 8$, they form a complete transversa
l.
