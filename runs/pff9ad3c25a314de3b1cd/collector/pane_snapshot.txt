## Proof

**Setup.** The symplectic group $\mathrm{Sp}(n)$ acts transitively on the unit s
phere $S^{4n-1} \subset \mathbb{H}^n$ (quaternionic $n$-space) by $A \cdot v = A
v$. The stabilizer of the point $e_1 = (1,0,\dots,0)$ consists of symplectic mat
rices fixing $e_1$, which is precisely $\mathrm{Sp}(n-1)$ embedded in the lower-
right $(n-1)\times(n-1)$ block. This yields the fiber bundle (principal fibratio
n)

$$\mathrm{Sp}(n-1) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Sp}(n) \xrightarrow{\;p\;} S^{4n-1}.$
$

**Applying the fibration with index $n+1$.** Replacing $n$ by $n+1$ throughout,
we obtain the fibration

$$\mathrm{Sp}(n) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Sp}(n+1) \xrightarrow{\;p\;} S^{4(n+1)-
1} = S^{4n+3}.$$

**Long exact sequence of homotopy groups.** This fibration induces the long exac

$$\cdots \to \pi_2(S^{4n+3}) \to \pi_1(\mathrm{Sp}(n)) \to \pi_1(\mathrm{Sp}(n+1
)) \to \pi_1(S^{4n+3}) \to \pi_0(\mathrm{Sp}(n)) \to \pi_0(\mathrm{Sp}(n+1)) \to
 \pi_0(S^{4n+3}).$$

**Vanishing of the sphere terms.** For $n \geq 1$, the sphere $S^{4n+3}$ has dim
ension $\geq 7$. In particular:
- $\pi_1(S^{4n+3}) = 0$, since $S^{4n+3}$ is simply connected (dimension $\geq 2
$).
- $\pi_2(S^{4n+3}) = 0$, since $\pi_k(S^m) = 0$ for $k < m$ (here $2 < 4n+3$).
- $\pi_0(S^{4n+3}) = 0$, since $S^{4n+3}$ is connected.

(For $n = 0$, the fibration is $\mathrm{Sp}(0) \to \mathrm{Sp}(1) \to S^3$; the
same vanishings $\pi_1(S^3) = \pi_2(S^3) = \pi_0(S^3) = 0$ hold, so the argument

**Deduction of $\pi_1(\mathrm{Sp}(n+1)) = \pi_1(\mathrm{Sp}(n))$.** Substituting
 the vanishing terms, the relevant portion of the long exact sequence becomes

$$0 \to \pi_1(\mathrm{Sp}(n)) \xrightarrow{\;i_*\;} \pi_1(\mathrm{Sp}(n+1)) \to
0.$$

}(n+1)) \to \pi_1(S^{4n+3})) = \ker(0) = \pi_1(\mathrm{Sp}(n+1))$, so $i_*$ is *
*surjective**. Hence $i_*$ is an isomorphism:

$$\pi_1(\mathrm{Sp}(n+1)) \cong \pi_1(\mathrm{Sp}(n)).$$

**Deduction of $\pi_0(\mathrm{Sp}(n+1)) = \pi_0(\mathrm{Sp}(n))$.** The tail of
the long exact sequence becomes

$$0 \to \pi_0(\mathrm{Sp}(n)) \xrightarrow{\;i_*\;} \pi_0(\mathrm{Sp}(n+1)) \to
0.$$

Exactness at $\pi_0(\mathrm{Sp}(n))$ gives $\ker i_* = \operatorname{im}(\pi_1(S

$$\pi_0(\mathrm{Sp}(n+1)) \cong \pi_0(\mathrm{Sp}(n)).$$

**Conclusion.** The long exact homotopy sequence of the fibration $\mathrm{Sp}(n

$$\pi_1(\mathrm{Sp}(n+1)) = \pi_1(\mathrm{Sp}(n)) \quad \text{and} \quad \pi_0(\
