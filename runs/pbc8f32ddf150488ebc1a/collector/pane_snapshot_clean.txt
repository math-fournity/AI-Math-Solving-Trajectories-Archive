**Proof.**

$$h=\frac{1-u^{2}}{v},\qquad 0<h<v.$$
(Indeed the altitude from $A$ is $x=u$, and the altitude from $B$ has slope $(1-
u)/v$, yielding $H=(u,(1-u^{2})/v)$.) Set $s:=v+h$, so $vh=1-u^{2}$.

The relevant points are
$$M=\Bigl(u,\tfrac{s}{2}\Bigr),\quad E=\Bigl(\tfrac{u+1}{2},\tfrac{v}{2}\Bigr),\

1. **Circumcircle of $DEF$ (the nine-point circle).** Since $D,E,F$ are the side

$$\omega_{1}:\ x^{2}+y^{2}-ux-\tfrac{s}{2}y=0. \tag{1}$$

$$\omega_{2}:\ x^{2}+y^{2}-\Bigl(2u-\tfrac1u\Bigr)x-sy=0. \tag{2}$$

$$\omega_{3}:\ x^{2}+y^{2}-2my-1=0. \tag{3}$$

s}\,x \quad\text{(since }1-u^{2}=vh\text{)}.$$

$$x\bigl[(1+t^{2})x-u-\tfrac{s}{2}t\bigr]=0.$$
