## Solution

Let $X \sim \mathrm{Arcsine}[-2,2]$ and $Y \sim \mathrm{Uniform}[0,1]$ be indepe
ndent, with
$$f_X(x)=\frac{1}{\pi\sqrt{4-x^2}},\quad x\in[-2,2], \qquad f_Y(y)=1,\quad y\in[
0,1].$$
Set $W=\sqrt{Y(1-Y)}$ so that $Z=XW$.

**Step 1: Distribution of $W$.** Let $U=Y(1-Y)$. For $u\in[0,\tfrac14]$ the equa
tion $y(1-y)=u$ has two roots $y_{1,2}=\tfrac12(1\mp\sqrt{1-4u})$, with $|1-2y_{
1,2}|=\sqrt{1-4u}$. Hence
$$f_U(u)=\frac{2}{\sqrt{1-4u}},\quad u\in\!\left[0,\tfrac14\right].$$
Since $W=\sqrt U$, $U=W^2$, $du=2w\,dw$:
$$f_W(w)=\frac{2}{\sqrt{1-4w^2}}\cdot 2w=\frac{4w}{\sqrt{1-4w^2}},\quad w\in\!\l
eft[0,\tfrac12\right].$$

**Step 2: Convolution for $Z=XW$.** Since $X$ and $W$ are independent and $W>0$,
$$f_Z(z)=\int f_X\!\left(\tfrac{z}{w}\right)f_W(w)\,\frac{dw}{w},$$
over $w\in[0,\tfrac12]$ with $z/w\in[-2,2]$, i.e. $w\ge |z|/2$. Thus $w\in[|z|/2
,\,1/2]$, which is nonempty iff $|z|\le 1$.

Using $f_X(z/w)=\dfrac{w}{\pi\sqrt{4w^2-z^2}}$ (for $w>0$):
$$f_Z(z)=\int_{|z|/2}^{1/2}\frac{w}{\pi\sqrt{4w^2-z^2}}\cdot\frac{4w}{\sqrt{1-4w
^2}}\cdot\frac{dw}{w}=\frac{4}{\pi}\int_{|z|/2}^{1/2}\frac{w\,dw}{\sqrt{4w^2-z^2
}\,\sqrt{1-4w^2}}.$$

**Step 3: Evaluate.** Substitute $t=4w^2$, $dt=8w\,dw$, so $w\,dw=dt/8$. Limits:
 $w=|z|/2\Rightarrow t=z^2$; $w=1/2\Rightarrow t=1$:
$$f_Z(z)=\frac{4}{\pi}\int_{z^2}^{1}\frac{dt/8}{\sqrt{t-z^2}\,\sqrt{1-t}}=\frac{
1}{2\pi}\int_{z^2}^{1}\frac{dt}{\sqrt{t-z^2}\,\sqrt{1-t}}.$$
With $s=t-z^2$, $ds=dt$, limits $0$ to $1-z^2$:
$$f_Z(z)=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{0}^{1-z^2}\frac{ds}{\sqrt{s}\,\sqrt{(1-z^2)-s}}.$$
This is the Beta integral $\displaystyle\int_0^a s^{-1/2}(a-s)^{-1/2}\,ds=B\!\le
ft(\tfrac12,\tfrac12\right)=\pi$, with $a=1-z^2$. Therefore
$$f_Z(z)=\frac{1}{2\pi}\cdot\pi=\frac12,\qquad z\in[-1,1].$$

**Conclusion.** The random variable $Z=X\sqrt{Y(1-Y)}$ is uniformly distributed
on $[-1,1]$:
$$\boxed{\,f_Z(z)=\begin{cases}\dfrac12,& -1\le z\le 1,\\[4pt] 0,&\text{otherwis
e.}\end{cases}\,}$$

(Consistency check: $\int_{-1}^{1}\tfrac12\,dz=1$, and the support $[-1,1]$ matc
hes $X\in[-2,2]$, $W\in[0,\tfrac12]$, giving $|Z|\le 1$.)
