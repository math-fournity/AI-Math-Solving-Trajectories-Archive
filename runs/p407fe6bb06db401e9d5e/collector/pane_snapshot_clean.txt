### Part 1 — Upper Bound: $k \leq H_N$

**Proof.** Since $A_1, \dots, A_k$ are pairwise disjoint and each $A_i \subseteq
 \{1, \dots, N\}$, their union $U = A_1 \cup \cdots \cup A_k$ is a subset of $\{
1, \dots, N\}$. By disjointness and the hypothesis $\sum_{n \in A_i} \frac{1}{n}

$$k = \sum_{i=1}^{k} \sum_{n \in A_i} \frac{1}{n} = \sum_{n \in U} \frac{1}{n}.$
$

Since $U \subseteq \{1, \dots, N\}$ and every summand $\frac{1}{n} > 0$:

$$\sum_{n \in U} \frac{1}{n} \leq \sum_{n=1}^{N} \frac{1}{n} = H_N.$$

Therefore $k \leq H_N$. $\square$

$$R_j := \sum_{n \in S_j} \frac{1}{n} = H_N - \sum_{i=1}^{j}\sum_{n \in A_i}\fra
