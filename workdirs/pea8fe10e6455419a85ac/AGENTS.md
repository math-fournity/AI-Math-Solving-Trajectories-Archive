# Problem

To ensure the design is aesthetically balanced, the artist must follow two strict rules:
1. No two panes that share a common edge may be made of the same color glass.
2. To keep costs manageable for the expensive Cobalt Blue glass, exactly 2 of the 9 panes in the grid must be Cobalt Blue.

How many different ways can the artist choose the glass panes for the window to satisfy these requirements?
