## Proof

**Key identity.** The Chebyshev polynomial of the first kind satisfies $T_N(x) =
 \cos(N\arccos x)$, so in particular
$$T_N(1) = \cos(N\cdot 0) = 1 \quad \text{for all } N \geq 0.$$

**Leading coefficient.** It is well known (and follows from the recurrence $T_{N
+1}(x)=2x\,T_N(x)-T_{N-1}(x)$ with $T_0=1,\;T_1=x$) that the leading coefficient
 of $T_N$ is
$$c_N^N = 2^{N-1}, \qquad N \geq 1.$$

**Evaluating the sum.** Since $T_N(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{N}c_n^N x^n$, evaluating at $x
=1$ gives
$$\sum_{n=0}^{N} c_n^N = T_N(1) = 1.$$

Separating the leading term:
$$\sum_{n=0}^{N-1} c_n^N = 1 - c_N^N.$$

Dividing by $c_N^N = 2^{N-1}$:
$$\boxed{S_N = \sum_{n=0}^{N-1}\frac{c_n^N}{c_N^N} = \frac{1-c_N^N}{c_N^N} = \fr
ac{1}{2^{N-1}} - 1.}$$

**Verification on small cases.**
- $N=1$: $T_1=x$, $S_1 = 0/1 = 0 = \tfrac{1}{2^0}-1$. ✓
- $N=2$: $T_2=2x^2-1$, $S_2 = (-1)/2 = -\tfrac12 = \tfrac{1}{2^1}-1$. ✓
- $N=3$: $T_3=4x^3-3x$, $S_3 = (-3)/4 = -\tfrac34 = \tfrac{1}{2^2}-1$. ✓
- $N=4$: $T_4=8x^4-8x^2+1$, $S_4 = (1-8)/8 = -\tfrac78 = \tfrac{1}{2^3}-1$. ✓

**Limit.** As $N\to\infty$,
$$S_N = \frac{1}{2^{N-1}} - 1 \;\longrightarrow\; 0 - 1 = -1.$$

Therefore the series **converges**, and its limit is $\boxed{-1}$.
