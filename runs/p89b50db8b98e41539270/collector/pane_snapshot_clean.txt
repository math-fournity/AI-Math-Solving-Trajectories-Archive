## Solution

**Goal:** Compute $S^5(2018^{2018^{2018}})$, where $S(n)$ is the digit-sum funct
ion.

### Step 1: Show 5 iterations reach a single digit

Let $N = 2018^{2018^{2018}}$ and $E = 2018^{2018}$, so $N = 2018^E$.

**Digits of $N$:** $\lfloor \log_{10} N \rfloor + 1 = \lfloor E \cdot \log_{10}
2018 \rfloor + 1$. Since $\log_{10} 2018 \approx 3.3049$, $N$ has $D_0 \approx 3
.305 \cdot 2018^{2018}$ digits — astronomically large.

| Iteration | Bound on value | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| $S(N)$ | $\leq 9 D_0 \approx 29.74 \cdot 2018^{2018}$ | digit sum $\leq 9 \tim
es$ number of digits |
| $S^2(N)$ | $\leq 9 \cdot 6672 = 60048$ | $S(N)$ has $\leq 1.47 + 2018\cdot 3.3
05 + 1 \approx 6672$ digits |
| $S^3(N)$ | $\leq 9 \cdot 5 = 45$ | $S^2(N) \leq 60048$ has at most 5 digits |
| $S^4(N)$ | $\leq 12$ | max digit sum of a number $\leq 45$ is $S(39)=12$ |
| $S^5(N)$ | $\leq 9$ (single digit) | max digit sum of a number $\leq 12$ is $S
(9)=9$ |

So $S^5(N)$ is a single digit in $\{1, 2, \ldots, 9\}$ (since $N > 0$).

### Step 2: Use the congruence $S(n) \equiv n \pmod{9}$

The digit-sum function satisfies $S(n) \equiv n \pmod{9}$, and by induction $S^k
(n) \equiv n \pmod{9}$ for all $k$. Therefore:

$$S^5(N) \equiv N = 2018^{2018^{2018}} \pmod{9}.$$

### Step 3: Compute $2018^{2018^{2018}} \pmod{9}$

**Reduce the base:** $2018 \equiv 2+0+1+8 = 11 \equiv 2 \pmod{9}$.

So $2018^{2018^{2018}} \equiv 2^{2018^{2018}} \pmod{9}$.

**Order of 2 mod 9:** $2^1\!=\!2,\; 2^2\!=\!4,\; 2^3\!=\!8,\; 2^4\!\equiv\!7,\;
2^5\!\equiv\!5,\; 2^6\!\equiv\!1 \pmod{9}$. The order is $6$.

So $2^{2018^{2018}} \equiv 2^{\,2018^{2018} \bmod 6} \pmod{9}$.

**Compute $2018^{2018} \bmod 6$:** $2018 \equiv 2 \pmod{6}$, so $2018^{2018} \eq
uiv 2^{2018} \pmod{6}$.

Powers of 2 mod 6 cycle: $2^1 \equiv 2$, $2^2 \equiv 4$, $2^3 \equiv 2$, $2^4 \e
quiv 4, \ldots$ — i.e., $2^k \equiv 2$ (odd $k$), $4$ (even $k$).

Since $2018$ is even: $2^{2018} \equiv 4 \pmod{6}$, so $2018^{2018} \equiv 4 \pm
od{6}$.

**Final exponent:** $2^{2018^{2018}} \equiv 2^4 = 16 \equiv 7 \pmod{9}$.

### Step 4: Conclude

$$S^5(N) \equiv 7 \pmod{9}, \qquad S^5(N) \in \{1,\ldots,9\}.$$

The only single digit congruent to $7 \pmod{9}$ is $7$.

$$\boxed{S^5\!\left(2018^{2018^{2018}}\right) = 7}$$
