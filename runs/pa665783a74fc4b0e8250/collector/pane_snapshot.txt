## Solution

We count the number of 2-regular simple graphs on 9 labeled vertices (the "tower
s"). Each tower links to exactly two others, so every vertex has degree exactly
2. A 2-regular graph decomposes uniquely into connected components, each a cycle
 of length ≥ 3. Thus $N$ equals the number of ways to partition the 9 labeled ve
rtices into unordered blocks of sizes ≥ 3, with each block of size $k$ arranged
into an (undirected) cycle.

**Cycle count on $k$ labeled vertices:** $(k-1)!/2$ (cyclic orderings $(k-1)!$,
divided by 2 for reversal).

**Formula for a partition** with block sizes $k_1,\dots,k_m$ and multiplicities
$m_s$ for each distinct size $s$:
$$\frac{9!}{\prod k_i!}\cdot\frac{1}{\prod_s m_s!}\cdot\prod_i \frac{(k_i-1)!}{2
}.$$

The partitions of 9 into parts ≥ 3 are: $9$; $6+3$; $5+4$; $3+3+3$.

**Partition $9$:** one 9-cycle.
$$\frac{9!}{9!}\cdot\frac{8!}{2} = 1\cdot 20160 = 20160.$$

**Partition $6+3$:** choose the 6-block, then form a 6-cycle and a 3-cycle.
$$\binom{9}{6}\cdot\frac{5!}{2}\cdot\frac{2!}{2} = 84\cdot 60\cdot 1 = 5040.$$

**Partition $5+4$:** choose the 5-block, then form a 5-cycle and a 4-cycle.
$$\binom{9}{5}\cdot\frac{4!}{2}\cdot\frac{3!}{2} = 126\cdot 12\cdot 3 = 4536.$$

**Partition $3+3+3$:** three 3-cycles; divide by $3!$ for the three identical-si
zed blocks.
$$\frac{9!}{(3!)^3\cdot 3!}\cdot\left(\frac{2!}{2}\right)^3 = \frac{362880}{216\
cdot 6}\cdot 1 = \frac{362880}{1296} = 280.$$

**Total:**
$$N = 20160 + 5040 + 4536 + 280 = 30016.$$

**Remainder:**
$$30016 \bmod 1000 = 16.$$

The remainder when $N$ is divided by 1000 is $\boxed{16}$.
