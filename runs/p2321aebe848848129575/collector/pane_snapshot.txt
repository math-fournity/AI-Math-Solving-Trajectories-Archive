## Solution

We solve $\nabla^2 u = 3u - 1$ on the unit square with homogeneous Dirichlet bou
ndary conditions, i.e.

$$u_{xx}+u_{yy}-3u = -1, \qquad u\big|_{\partial\Omega}=0,\quad \Omega=(0,1)^2.$
$

### Step 1. Eigenfunctions of the Laplacian

The eigenfunctions of $-\nabla^2$ on the unit square with $u=0$ on the boundary
are

$$\phi_{mn}(x,y)=\sin(m\pi x)\sin(n\pi y),\qquad m,n\ge 1,$$

with eigenvalues

$$\lambda_{mn}=(m^2+n^2)\pi^2,\qquad\text{so that}\qquad -\nabla^2\phi_{mn}=\lam
bda_{mn}\phi_{mn}.$$

These form a complete orthogonal set on $L^2(\Omega)$.

### Step 2. Expand $u$ and the source term

Write

$$u(x,y)=\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} b_{mn}\,\sin(m\pi x)\sin(n\pi y)
.$$

Then

$$\nabla^2 u - 3u = \sum_{m,n\ge 1} b_{mn}\bigl(-\lambda_{mn}-3\bigr)\sin(m\pi x
)\sin(n\pi y).$$

Expand the constant source $-1$ in the same basis:

$$-1=\sum_{m,n\ge 1} c_{mn}\sin(m\pi x)\sin(n\pi y),$$

where, using $\int_0^1\sin(k\pi x)\,dx=\frac{1-(-1)^k}{k\pi}$ and the normalizat
ion $\int_0^1\sin^2(k\pi x)\,dx=\tfrac12$,

$$c_{mn}=4\int_0^1\!\!\int_0^1 (-1)\sin(m\pi x)\sin(n\pi y)\,dx\,dy = -\frac{4\,
(1-(-1)^m)(1-(-1)^n)}{mn\,\pi^2}.$$

Hence $c_{mn}=0$ unless **both** $m$ and $n$ are odd, in which case

$$c_{mn}=-\frac{16}{mn\,\pi^2}.$$

### Step 3. Match coefficients

Equating coefficients of $\sin(m\pi x)\sin(n\pi y)$:

$$b_{mn}\bigl(-\lambda_{mn}-3\bigr)=c_{mn} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad b_{mn}=-\fr
ac{c_{mn}}{\lambda_{mn}+3}=\frac{-c_{mn}}{(m^2+n^2)\pi^2+3}.$$

For $m$ or $n$ even: $c_{mn}=0\Rightarrow b_{mn}=0$.

For $m,n$ both odd:

$$b_{mn}=\frac{16/(mn\pi^2)}{(m^2+n^2)\pi^2+3}=\frac{16}{mn\,\pi^2\bigl((m^2+n^2
)\pi^2+3\bigr)}.$$

### Step 4. Final answer

$$\boxed{\,u(x,y)=\sum_{\substack{m\ge 1,\,n\ge 1\\ m,n\ \mathrm{odd}}}\frac{16\
,\sin(m\pi x)\,\sin(n\pi y)}{mn\,\pi^2\bigl((m^2+n^2)\pi^2+3\bigr)}\,}$$

### Verification

- **Boundary conditions:** Each $\sin(m\pi x)\sin(n\pi y)$ vanishes on $x=0,1$ a
nd $y=0,1$, so $u=0$ on $\partial\Omega$. ✓
- **PDE:** Term-by-term, $\nabla^2 u-3u=\sum b_{mn}(-\lambda_{mn}-3)\phi_{mn}=\s
um c_{mn}\phi_{mn}=-1$, i.e. $\nabla^2 u=3u-1$. ✓
- **Convergence:** $|b_{mn}|\sim O\!\bigl((m^2+n^2)^{-1}m^{-1}n^{-1}\bigr)$, so
the series converges absolutely and uniformly, justifying term-by-term different
iation. ✓
