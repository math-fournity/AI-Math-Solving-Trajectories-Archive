**Key fact.** $\pi_1(\mathbb{RP}^2) \cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$.

**Classification principle.** Connected covering spaces of a (path-connected, lo
cally path-connected, semilocally simply connected) base $X$ correspond bijectiv
ely to conjugacy classes of subgroups of $\pi_1(X)$, with the degree of the cove
r equal to the index of the subgroup. A general (possibly disconnected) $n$-fold
 covering space is a disjoint union of connected covering spaces whose degrees s
um to $n$.

**Subgroups of $\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$.** There are exactly two:
- $H_1 = \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$, of index $1$ — giving the trivial (1-fold) cov
er $\mathbb{RP}^2 \to \mathbb{RP}^2$.
- $H_2 = \{0\}$, of index $2$ — giving the universal (2-fold) cover $S^2 \to \ma
thbb{RP}^2$.

Since $\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$ is abelian, each subgroup is its own conjugacy cl
ass, so these are the only two connected covers up to equivalence. In particular
, **there is no connected 3-fold cover**, because that would require a subgroup
of index $3$, but $3 \nmid 2$.

**Disconnected 3-fold covers.** Every 3-fold cover is a disjoint union of connec
ted covers with degrees summing to $3$. The only available degrees are $1$ and $
2$, so we enumerate the partitions of $3$ into parts from $\{1, 2\}$:

| Partition | Total space | Components |
|---|---|---|
| $3 = 1+1+1$ | $\mathbb{RP}^2 \sqcup \mathbb{RP}^2 \sqcup \mathbb{RP}^2$ | 3 |
| $3 = 1+2$ | $\mathbb{RP}^2 \sqcup S^2$ | 2 |

**Non-homeomorphism.** These two total spaces are not homeomorphic: the first ha
s $3$ path-connected components, the second has $2$, and the number of connected
 components is a topological invariant.

**Validity as covering spaces.** Each is a covering space of $\mathbb{RP}^2$:
- $\mathbb{RP}^2 \sqcup \mathbb{RP}^2 \sqcup \mathbb{RP}^2$: each component maps
 homeomorphically onto $\mathbb{RP}^2$; every point has a 3-point fiber (one pre
image in each component).
- $\mathbb{RP}^2 \sqcup S^2$: the $\mathbb{RP}^2$ summand maps by identity (1 pr
eimage), the $S^2$ summand maps by the standard 2-fold quotient (2 preimages); e
very fiber has $1+2 = 3$ points.

**Conclusion.** Up to homeomorphism of the total space, there are exactly **two*
* non-homeomorphic 3-fold covering spaces of $\mathbb{RP}^2$:

$$\boxed{\mathbb{RP}^2 \sqcup \mathbb{RP}^2 \sqcup \mathbb{RP}^2 \quad\text{and}
\quad \mathbb{RP}^2 \sqcup S^2.}$$
