## Solution

**Setup.** We choose $(A,B,C)$ uniformly from $\{0,1,\dots,9\}^3$, giving $10^3
= 1000$ equally likely triples. We seek the count of triples for which $A^{B^C}
\equiv 6 \pmod{10}$.

**Answer-leak check.** The problem statement contains no solution, answer, or pr
oof sketch. Proceeding.

**Key observation.** The units digit of $A^n$ depends only on $A \bmod 10$ and $
n$ (with periodicity). Checking each base:

| $A$ | $A^n \bmod 10$ pattern | Gives 6 when |
|-----|------------------------|--------------|
| 0 | $0,0,0,\dots$ ($n\ge1$) | never |
| 1 | $1,1,1,\dots$ | never |
| 2 | $2,4,8,6,2,4,8,6,\dots$ (period 4) | $n\equiv 0\pmod 4,\ n\ge 4$ |
| 3 | $3,9,7,1,\dots$ | never |
| 4 | $4,6,4,6,\dots$ (period 2) | $n$ even, $n\ge 2$ |
| 5 | $5,5,\dots$ | never |
| 6 | $6,6,6,\dots$ ($n\ge1$) | $n\ge 1$ |
| 7 | $7,9,3,1,\dots$ | never |
| 8 | $8,4,2,6,8,4,2,6,\dots$ (period 4) | $n\equiv 0\pmod 4,\ n\ge 4$ |
| 9 | $9,1,9,1,\dots$ | never |

Only $A \in \{2,4,6,8\}$ can yield units digit 6. We count favorable $(B,C)$ for
 each.

---

**Case $A=6$:** Need $B^C \ge 1$.
- $B \in \{1,\dots,9\}$: $B^C \ge 1$ for every $C \in \{0,\dots,9\}$ (since $B^0
=1$). → $9\times 10 = 90$.
- $B=0$: $0^0$ undefined; $0^C=0$ for $C\ge1$ gives $6^0=1\ne 6$. → $0$.

**Subtotal: 90.**

---

**Case $A=4$:** Need $B^C$ even and $B^C \ge 2$.
- $B$ even, $B\in\{2,4,6,8\}$, $C\ge 1$: $B^C$ is even and $\ge 2$. → $4\times 9
 = 36$.
- $B=0$, $C\ge1$: $0^C=0 < 2$. → $0$.
- $B$ odd: $B^C$ odd. → $0$.
- $C=0$: $B^0=1$ (odd). → $0$.

**Subtotal: 36.**

---

**Case $A=2$ (and $A=8$):** Need $B^C$ a positive multiple of 4, i.e., $B^C \equ
iv 0\pmod 4$ and $B^C \ge 4$.

- $B=4$: $4^C \equiv 0\pmod 4$ for $C\ge 1$; $4^C\ge 4$. → $C=1,\dots,9$: **9**.
- $B=8$: $8^C \equiv 0\pmod 4$ for $C\ge 1$; $\ge 8$. → **9**.
- $B=2$: $2^C \equiv 0\pmod 4$ iff $C\ge 2$; $2^C\ge 4$. → $C=2,\dots,9$: **8**.
- $B=6$: $6^C \equiv 0\pmod 4$ iff $C\ge 2$ (since $6^2=36=4\cdot 9$); $\ge 36$.
 → $C=2,\dots,9$: **8**.
- $B\in\{0,1,3,5,7,9\}$: $B^C$ is $0$ (not positive) or odd. → **0**.

**Subtotal (each of $A=2$ and $A=8$): $9+9+8+8 = 34$.**

---

**Total favorable:** $90 + 36 + 34 + 34 = 194$.

**Probability:** $\dfrac{194}{1000} = \dfrac{97}{500}$.

The fraction $\frac{97}{500}$ is irreducible ($97$ is prime and $\gcd(97,500)=1$
), so $a=97$, $b=500$.

$$a + b = 97 + 500 = \boxed{597}$$
