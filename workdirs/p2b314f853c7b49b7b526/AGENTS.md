# Problem

On a vast circular space station, there are 20 distinct research sectors arranged in a ring. Each sector employs 20 unique robotic security units, for a total of 400 robots across the station. Every single robot has a unique power output rating, meaning no two robots are equally powerful.

A protocol exists for comparing any two adjacent sectors, Sector $A$ and Sector $B$. Every robot from Sector $A$ must engage in a one-on-one efficiency duel against every robot from Sector $B$, resulting in exactly 400 individual duels. In each duel, the robot with the higher power output wins. Sector $A$ is officially designated "Superior" to Sector $B$ if the robots from Sector $A$ win at least $k$ out of the 400 total duels.

Engineers have discovered a rare configuration of power ratings where every sector in the ring is "Superior" to its immediate neighbor in a clockwise direction. Determine the maximum possible integer value of $k$ that allows this clockwise chain of superiority to exist.
