## Solution

We wish to solve $f(x) = x + e^x = 0$, i.e. find $x$ with $e^x = -x$, or equival
ently a fixed point of
$$g(x) = -e^x.$$

### Step 1. Locate the root

$f(-1) = -1 + e^{-1} \approx -0.632 < 0$ and $f(0) = 0 + 1 = 1 > 0$, so by the i
ntermediate value theorem a root lies in $(-1, 0)$.

### Step 2. Choose a closed interval on which $g$ is a contraction mapping itsel
f into itself

Take $I = [-0.7,\,-0.45]$. Since $g$ is decreasing,
$$g(I) = [g(-0.45),\, g(-0.7)] = [-e^{-0.45},\, -e^{-0.7}] \approx [-0.6376,\, -
0.4966].$$
Both endpoints satisfy $-0.7 \le -0.6376$ and $-0.4966 \le -0.45$, so $g(I) \sub
seteq I$.

The derivative is $g'(x) = -e^x$, hence
$$|g'(x)| = e^x \le e^{-0.45} \approx 0.6376 < 1 \quad \text{for all } x \in I.$
$
So $g$ is a contraction on the complete metric space $I$ with constant $L = e^{-
0.45} < 1$.

### Step 3. Apply the Banach contraction theorem

By the Banach fixed-point theorem, $g$ has a unique fixed point $x^* \in I$, whi
ch is the unique solution of $x + e^x = 0$ in $I$. The iterates $x_{n+1} = g(x_n
) = -e^{x_n}$ converge to $x^*$ for any $x_0 \in I$. Take $x_0 = -0.5$:

### Step 4. Error bound

The Banach theorem gives
$$|x_n - x^*| \le \frac{L^n}{1-L}\,|x_1 - x_0|.$$
Here $L = e^{-0.45} \approx 0.6376$, $1 - L \approx 0.3624$, $|x_1 - x_0| \appro
x 0.106531$, so
$$|x_n - x^*| \le 0.6376^n \cdot \frac{0.106531}{0.3624} \approx 0.6376^n \cdot
0.294.$$
For three-decimal accuracy we need error $< 0.0005$:
$$0.6376^n \cdot 0.294 < 0.0005 \;\Longrightarrow\; n > \frac{\ln(0.0005/0.294)}
{\ln(0.6376)} \approx \frac{-6.377}{-0.450} \approx 14.2.$$
So $n \ge 15$ suffices. At $n = 18$ the iterate is $x_{18} \approx -0.567142$, a
nd the bound guarantees the true value lies within $0.0005$ of this, namely in $
(-0.567642,\,-0.566642)$, which rounds to $-0.567$.

### Verification

$e^{-0.567} \approx 0.56725$, so $-0.567 + 0.56725 \approx 0.00025 \approx 0$; a
nd $e^{-0.567143} \approx 0.567143$, giving $x + e^x \approx 0$.

### Answer

The solution of $x + e^x = 0$, correct to three decimal places, is

$$\boxed{x \approx -0.567}.$$
