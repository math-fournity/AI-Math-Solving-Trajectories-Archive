## Solution

**Setup.** The square fence has side length 4 km, centered at the origin, so its
 vertices are $(2,2),(-2,2),(-2,-2),(2,-2)$, and its perimeter is $16$ km. With
$16$ sensors spaced exactly $1$ km apart along the perimeter, the sensors fall a
t the corners and at the $1$-km and $2$-km and $3$-km marks along each side. Tak
ing the natural corner-aligned placement, the sixteen positions $P_i$ are:

- Corners $(\pm2,\pm2)$: 4 sensors, each with $|P_i|^2 = 8$.
- Quarter points $(\pm1,\pm2),(\pm2,\pm1)$ and symmetric: 8 sensors, each with $
|P_i|^2 = 5$.
- Side midpoints $(0,\pm2),(\pm2,0)$: 4 sensors, each with $|P_i|^2 = 4$.

**Randomization model.** For each sensor $i$, let $s_i$ be an independent random
 sign with $\Pr(s_i=+1)=\Pr(s_i=-1)=\tfrac12$. Then $Q_i = s_i P_i$ (since refle
cting $P_i$ through $O$ gives $-P_i$). The net displacement is
$$\vec V = \sum_{i=1}^{16} s_i P_i, \qquad D^2 = |\vec V|^2 = \vec V\cdot \vec V
.$$

**Computing $\mathbb{E}[D^2]$.** Expand:
$$D^2 = \sum_{i=1}^{16} s_i^2 |P_i|^2 + \sum_{i\ne j} s_i s_j\, (P_i\cdot P_j).$
$

Since $s_i^2 = 1$ always, the first sum is $\sum_i |P_i|^2$ deterministically. F
or the cross terms, $i\ne j$ means $s_i$ and $s_j$ are independent with $\mathbb
{E}[s_i]=0$, so
$$\mathbb{E}[s_i s_j] = \mathbb{E}[s_i]\,\mathbb{E}[s_j] = 0.$$

Therefore all cross terms vanish and
$$\mathbb{E}[D^2] = \sum_{i=1}^{16} |P_i|^2.$$

**Evaluating the sum.** Using the counts above:
$$\sum_{i=1}^{16}|P_i|^2 = 4\cdot 8 + 8\cdot 5 + 4\cdot 4 = 32 + 40 + 16 = 88.$$

**Answer.**
$$\boxed{\mathbb{E}[D^2] = 88}$$
