# Problem

A high-tech industrial zone consists of 20 server hubs arranged in a perfect circle around a central cooling plant. Each hub houses 20 specialized cybersecurity agents. Across the entire zone, no two agents possess the exact same skill level; in any simulated digital confrontation between two agents, the one with the higher skill level always succeeds.

In the zone’s security protocol, a hub $A$ is classified as "$n$-dominant" over hub $B$ if, when every agent from $A$ is paired against every agent from $B$ in individual matches, the agents from hub $A$ achieve at least $n$ total victories. 

Given that $n$ must be a natural number, determine the maximum possible value of $n$ such that every hub in the circle can be simultaneously $n$-dominant over its immediate neighbor in the clockwise direction.
