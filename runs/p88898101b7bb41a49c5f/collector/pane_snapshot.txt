## Impossibility for p ≠ 2, 5

**Proof:** WLOG suppose row 1 is the supposedly non-divisible row, so $R_i \equi
v 0 \pmod{p}$ for $i \geq 2$ and $C_j \equiv 0 \pmod{p}$ for all $j$.

From $C_j \equiv 0 \pmod{p}$:
$$a_{1j} \cdot 10^{2016} \equiv -\sum_{i=2}^{2017} a_{ij} \cdot 10^{2017-i} \pmo
d{p} \quad \forall\, j$$

Since $\gcd(10, p) = 1$, we can multiply by $10^{-2016}$:
$$a_{1j} \equiv -10^{-2016} \sum_{i=2}^{2017} a_{ij} \cdot 10^{2017-i} \pmod{p}
\quad \forall\, j$$

Now compute $R_1$:
$$R_1 = \sum_{j=1}^{2017} a_{1j} \cdot 10^{2017-j} \equiv -10^{-2016} \sum_{j=1}
^{2017} \sum_{i=2}^{2017} a_{ij} \cdot 10^{2017-i} \cdot 10^{2017-j} \pmod{p}$$

Rearranging the double sum:
$$= -10^{-2016} \sum_{i=2}^{2017} 10^{2017-i} \underbrace{\sum_{j=1}^{2017} a_{i
j} \cdot 10^{2017-j}}_{= R_i \equiv 0} \equiv 0 \pmod{p}$$

This contradicts $R_1 \not\equiv 0 \pmod{p}$. By symmetry (transposition), the s
ame holds if the non-divisible code is a column. **Therefore no prime $p \neq 2,

## p = 2 works

Divisibility by 2 depends only on the last digit. Set:
- Last row: $a_{2017,j} = 2$ for all $j$ (all even)
- Last column: $a_{1,2017} = 1$ (odd), $a_{i,2017} = 2$ for $i \geq 2$ (even)
- Interior ($1 \leq i,j \leq 2016$): all 1s, except $a_{i,i} = 2$ (diagonal) and

## p = 5 works
