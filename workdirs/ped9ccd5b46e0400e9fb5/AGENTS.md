# Problem

A galactic trade federation consists of exactly 100 distinct star systems. To establish a new economic hierarchy, every system engaged in a mandatory one-on-one trade negotiation with every other system in the federation. In these negotiations, a clear "dominant" party was always established; there were no neutral outcomes or ties.

After all negotiations were concluded, the systems were ranked from 1st to 100th based on their total number of dominant victories. It was discovered that the system finishing in the $k$-th position achieved a total number of victories that no other system achieved. Furthermore, a strange paradox occurred: this $k$-th ranked system managed to emerge dominant in its negotiations against every single system ranked higher than itself (1st through $k-1$), yet it was defeated in its negotiations by every single system ranked lower than itself ($k+1$ through 100).

Find the smallest possible value of $k$.
