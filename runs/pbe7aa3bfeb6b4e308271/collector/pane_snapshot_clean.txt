## Solution

**Burgers equation** $u_t + u\,u_x = 0$ with the given initial data $\phi(x)$.

### Method of Characteristics

Along a characteristic curve emanating from $x_0$ at $t=0$:
- $u$ is conserved: $u = \phi(x_0)$
- The characteristic is the straight line $x = x_0 + \phi(x_0)\,t$

The solution remains classical as long as the map $x_0 \mapsto x = x_0 + \phi(x_
0)\,t$ is invertible, i.e., as long as characteristics do not cross.

### Breaking Condition

The map loses invertibility when
$$\frac{\partial x}{\partial x_0} = 1 + \phi'(x_0)\,t = 0,$$
which first occurs at
$$t_B = \min_{\{x_0:\,\phi'(x_0)<0\}}\left(-\frac{1}{\phi'(x_0)}\right) = -\frac
{1}{\displaystyle\min_{x_0}\phi'(x_0)}.$$

### Computing $\phi'(x_0)$

$$\phi'(x) = \begin{cases} 0, & x < \pi/2 \\ \cos x, & \pi/2 < x < 3\pi/2 \\ 0,
& x > 3\pi/2 \end{cases}$$

Only the middle region contributes negative slopes. On $[\pi/2,\,3\pi/2]$:
$$\phi'(x) = \cos x, \qquad \cos x \in [-1,\,0].$$

The minimum (most negative) value is attained at $x_0 = \pi$:
$$\phi'(\pi) = \cos\pi = -1.$$

### Breaking Time

$$t_B = -\frac{1}{\phi'(\pi)} = -\frac{1}{-1} = \boxed{1}.$$

### Verification

At $t_B = 1$, the characteristic from $x_0 = \pi$ has $\frac{\partial x}{\partia
l x_0} = 1 + (-1)(1) = 0$, meaning neighboring characteristics first focus/cross
 at this point. For any $t < 1$, we have $1 + \phi'(x_0)\,t \geq 1 - t > 0$ ever
ywhere, so the map remains invertible and the classical solution persists. For $
t > 1$, a shock forms.

**The breaking time is $t_B = 1$.**
