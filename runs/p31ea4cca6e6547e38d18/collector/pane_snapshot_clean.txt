## Solution

We want the last three digits of the power tower $T_{1001} = 7^{7^{7^{\cdots}}}$
 (1001 sevens), i.e. $T_{1001} \bmod 1000$, where $T_1 = 7$ and $T_{k+1} = 7^{T_
k}$.

Since $1000 = 8 \times 125$ with $\gcd(8,125)=1$, we use CRT on $T_{1001} \bmod
8$ and $T_{1001} \bmod 125$.

### Step 1: $T_{1001} \bmod 8$

$7 \equiv -1 \pmod 8$. Every $T_k$ is a power of $7$, hence odd, so
$$T_k = 7^{(\text{odd})} \equiv (-1)^{\text{odd}} \equiv -1 \equiv 7 \pmod 8.$$
Thus $T_{1001} \equiv 7 \pmod 8$.

### Step 2: $T_{1001} \bmod 125$

Since $\gcd(7,125)=1$, the exponent reduces modulo the order of $7$ mod $125$. C
ompute:
$$7^{10} \equiv -1 \pmod{125}, \qquad 7^{20} \equiv 1 \pmod{125}.$$
(Check: $7^2=49,\;7^4\equiv26,\;7^5\equiv57,\;7^{10}\equiv57^2=3249\equiv124\equ
iv-1$.) The order is exactly $20$ (since $7^{10}\not\equiv1$ and $7^5=57\not\equ
iv1$). So
$$T_{1001} \bmod 125 = 7^{\,T_{1000} \bmod 20} \bmod 125.$$

### Step 3: $T_{1000} \bmod 20$

$20 = 4 \times 5$.

- **Mod 4:** $7\equiv -1\pmod4$, and $T_{999}$ is odd, so $T_{1000}=7^{T_{999}}\
equiv (-1)^{\text{odd}}\equiv 3\pmod4$.
- **Mod 5:** $7\equiv 2\pmod5$, order of $2$ mod $5$ is $4$. $T_{999}\bmod 4 = 3
$ (odd), so $T_{1000}\equiv 2^3 = 8\equiv 3\pmod5$.

CRT: $x\equiv3\pmod4,\;x\equiv3\pmod5 \Rightarrow x\equiv 3\pmod{20}$.

So $T_{1000}\bmod 20 = 3$, giving
$$T_{1001}\bmod 125 = 7^3 \bmod 125 = 343 \bmod 125 = 93.$$

### Step 4: CRT for $T_{1001} \bmod 1000$

Solve $x\equiv 7\pmod8$ and $x\equiv 93\pmod{125}$.

Let $x = 125k + 93$. Then $125k+93\equiv 7\pmod8$, i.e. $5k+5\equiv7\pmod8$, so
$5k\equiv2\pmod8$. The inverse of $5$ mod $8$ is $5$, so $k\equiv 10\equiv 2\pmo
d8$. Taking $k=2$:
$$x = 125\cdot 2 + 93 = 343.$$

Verification: $343\bmod 8 = 7$ ✓; $343\bmod 125 = 93$ ✓.

### Answer

The last three digits are $\boxed{343}$.
