## Proof

Recall that $\mathbb{Z}_{(p)} = S^{-1}\mathbb{Z}$ where $S = \mathbb{Z}\setminus
(p) = \{n \in \mathbb{Z} : p \nmid n\}$ is the multiplicative set of integers no
t divisible by $p$.

**Step 1: Reduce to a localization.**

For any $\mathbb{Z}$-module $M$, there is a natural isomorphism of $\mathbb{Z}$-
modules
$$S^{-1}\mathbb{Z}\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} M \;\cong\; S^{-1}M,$$
given by $\frac{a}{s}\otimes m \mapsto \frac{am}{s}$. Applying this with $M = \m
athbb{Z}/q^{k}\mathbb{Z}$,
$$\mathbb{Z}_{(p)}\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Z}/q^{k}\mathbb{Z} \;\cong\; (\mat
hbb{Z}/q^{k}\mathbb{Z})_{(p)} = S^{-1}(\mathbb{Z}/q^{k}\mathbb{Z}).$$

**Step 2: Show the localization is zero.**

In the localization $S^{-1}(\mathbb{Z}/q^{k}\mathbb{Z})$, every element of $S$ b
ecomes invertible. Since $p$ and $q$ are distinct primes, $p\nmid q$, so $q\in S
$. Hence the element $\frac{q}{1}$ is invertible in $S^{-1}(\mathbb{Z}/q^{k}\mat
hbb{Z})$.

On the other hand, in $\mathbb{Z}/q^{k}\mathbb{Z}$ we have $q^{k}\equiv 0\pmod{q
^{k}}$, so
$$\left(\frac{q}{1}\right)^{k} = \frac{q^{k}}{1} = \frac{0}{1} = 0 \quad\text{in
 } S^{-1}(\mathbb{Z}/q^{k}\mathbb{Z}).$$

Since $\frac{q}{1}$ is invertible, so is $\left(\frac{q}{1}\right)^{k}$, with in
verse $\left(\frac{q}{1}\right)^{-k}$. Multiplying the equation above by this in
verse yields
$$\frac{1}{1} = \left(\frac{q}{1}\right)^{-k}\cdot\left(\frac{q}{1}\right)^{k} =
 \left(\frac{q}{1}\right)^{-k}\cdot 0 = 0.$$

Thus the identity element of $S^{-1}(\mathbb{Z}/q^{k}\mathbb{Z})$ equals zero, w
hich forces the entire module to be the zero module:
$$S^{-1}(\mathbb{Z}/q^{k}\mathbb{Z}) = 0.$$

**Conclusion.**

Combining Steps 1 and 2,
$$\boxed{\mathbb{Z}_{(p)}\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Z}/q^{k}\mathbb{Z} \;\cong\
; S^{-1}(\mathbb{Z}/q^{k}\mathbb{Z}) \;=\; 0.}$$

The key point is that $q$ (being coprime to $p$) lies in the multiplicative set
$S$ and hence becomes a unit in the localization, while $q^{k}=0$ in $\mathbb{Z}
/q^{k}\mathbb{Z}$; a unit that is nilpotent must be zero, collapsing the whole m
odule.
