- **Ivan**: walks from $0$ at $5$ km/h. Position $= 5t$.
- **Krešo**: bikes from $0$ at $15$ km/h. Position $= 15t$.
- **Marko**: walks from $15$ at $5$ km/h. Position $= 15 - 5t$.

$$15t = 15 - 5t \implies 20t = 15 \implies t = \tfrac{3}{4},\quad \text{position

Krešo gives the bike to Marko and walks the remaining $3.75$ km at $5$ km/h: tim

$$T_K = 0.75 + 0.75 = 1.5,\qquad W_K = 0.75.$$

### Meeting 2: Marko (biking) meets Ivan (walking)

tfrac{3}{4})$. Ivan still walks: position $= 5t$.

$$5t = 11.25 - 15\!\left(t - \tfrac{3}{4}\right) = 22.5 - 15t \implies 20t = 22.
