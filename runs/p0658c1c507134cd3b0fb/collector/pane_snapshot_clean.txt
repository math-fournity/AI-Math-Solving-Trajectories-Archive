## Proof

**Claim:** Yes, there exists a constant $C > 0$, independent of $f$, such that $
\|f - \tilde f\|_p \le C\|f\|_p$ for all $f \in L^p(\Omega)$. In fact, $C = 2$ w
orks.

**Setup.** Let $\Omega \subset \mathbb{R}^N$ be open and bounded (so $0 < |\Omeg
a| < \infty$), and let $1 \le p \le \infty$. For $f \in L^p(\Omega)$, set
$$\tilde f := \frac{1}{|\Omega|}\int_\Omega f(x)\,dx,$$
which is a constant (a scalar times the function $\mathbf{1}_\Omega$).

**Step 1: Bound $\|\tilde f\|_p$ in terms of $\|f\|_p$.**

*Case $1 \le p < \infty$.* By Hölder's inequality with exponents $p$ and $p' = p
/(p-1)$,
$$\left|\int_\Omega f\right| \le \|f\|_p \cdot \|\mathbf{1}_\Omega\|_{p'} = \|f\
|_p \cdot |\Omega|^{1/p'}.$$
Hence
$$|\tilde f| = \frac{1}{|\Omega|}\left|\int_\Omega f\right| \le \frac{\|f\|_p}{|
\Omega|^{1 - 1/p'}} = \frac{\|f\|_p}{|\Omega|^{1/p}}.$$
Since $\tilde f$ is constant on $\Omega$,
$$\|\tilde f\|_p = |\tilde f|\cdot|\Omega|^{1/p} \le \|f\|_p.$$

*Case $p = \infty$.* We have $|\tilde f| \le \frac{1}{|\Omega|}\int_\Omega |f| \
le \|f\|_\infty$, so $\|\tilde f\|_\infty \le \|f\|_\infty$.

In both cases, $\|\tilde f\|_p \le \|f\|_p$.

**Step 2: Apply Minkowski's inequality.**

$$\|f - \tilde f\|_p \le \|f\|_p + \|\tilde f\|_p \le \|f\|_p + \|f\|_p = 2\|f\|
_p.$$

**Conclusion.** Taking $C = 2$ (which depends only on the triangle inequality /
Minkowski and Hölder, and is independent of $f$), we have
$$\|f - \tilde f\|_p \le 2\,\|f\|_p \qquad \forall\, f \in L^p(\Omega).$$

**Remark.** This is a trivial consequence of the fact that the averaging operato
r $P: f \mapsto \tilde f$ is a bounded linear projection on $L^p(\Omega)$ with $
\|P\| \le 1$, so $\|I - P\| \le 1 + \|P\| \le 2$. (This should not be confused w
ith the Poincaré–Wirtinger inequality $\|f - \tilde f\|_p \le C\|\nabla f\|_p$,
which requires $f \in W^{1,p}(\Omega)$ and geometric hypotheses on $\Omega$; her
e the right-hand side is simply $\|f\|_p$, making the estimate immediate for all
 of $L^p$.)
