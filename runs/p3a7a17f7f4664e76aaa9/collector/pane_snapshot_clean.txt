So $s \in \{7, 16, 25, \ldots\}$.

**Checking $s=16$:** The smallest 17 numbers with digit sum 16 are $79, 88, 97,
169, 178, 187, 196, 259, 268, 277, 286, 295, 349, 358, 367, 376, 385$, which sum
 to $4214 > 2018$. Not viable. Larger $s$ values are even worse.

**So $s = 7$ is the only option.**

## Step 2: Find the maximum M

The 16 smallest positive integers with digit sum 7 are:

with sum $= 1444$.

$$\{7, 16, 25, 34, 43, 52, 61, 70, 106, 115, 124, 133, 142, 151, 205, 214, 520\}
$$
All have digit sum 7, all distinct, sum $= 2018$ ✓.

## Step 3: Count sets containing 520

I need to count the number of ways to choose 16 distinct numbers with digit sum

Total: 32 numbers. The 16 smallest (base set $B$) sum to 1444. The 16 "upgrade"
numbers ($U$) are the rest. Any valid selection of 16 is obtained by swapping $k

**$k=1$:** Need $u - b = 54$ for some $u \in U$, $b \in B$. Checking all pairs:

**$k \geq 3$:** Minimum increase even larger. **No solutions.**
