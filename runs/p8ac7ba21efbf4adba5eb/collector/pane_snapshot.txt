## Proof

Let $X$ be a locally Noetherian scheme over $k$, $p \in X(k)$ a rational point w
hich is regular of codimension $d$. Set $A = \mathscr{O}_{X,p}$, $\mathfrak{m}$
its maximal ideal, and $\widehat{A} = \varprojlim A/\mathfrak{m}^n$ its $\mathfr
ak{m}$-adic completion. We construct an explicit topological-ring isomorphism
$$\Phi : k[[X_1,\dots,X_d]] \xrightarrow{\;\sim\;} \widehat{A}, \qquad X_i \maps
to t_i,$$
where $t_1,\dots,t_d$ is a regular system of parameters.

### Step 1. A coefficient field $k \hookrightarrow A$

The structure morphism $X \to \operatorname{Spec} k$ gives a local $k$-algebra s
tructure $k \to A$. Composing with the residue map $A \to \kappa(p)$ and using t
hat $p$ is $k$-rational ($\kappa(p) = k$, with $k \to \kappa(p)$ an isomorphism)
 yields
$$k \longrightarrow A \longrightarrow \kappa(p) = k,$$
whose composite is $\operatorname{id}_k$. Hence $k \to A$ is injective (a field
map admitting a left inverse) and identifies $k$ with a subfield of $A$ mapping
isomorphically onto $A/\mathfrak{m}$. This subfield is a **coefficient field** o
f $A$. It survives completion: the induced $k \to \widehat{A}$ satisfies $\wideh
at{A}/\mathfrak{m}\widehat{A} = k$, so $k$ is a coefficient field of $\widehat{A
}$.

### Step 2. A regular system of parameters

Since $p$ is regular of codimension $d$, the local ring $A$ is a **regular local
 ring** of dimension $d$: its maximal ideal satisfies $\dim_k \mathfrak{m}/\math
frak{m}^2 = d = \dim A$. Lift a $k$-basis of $\mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2$ to el
ements $t_1,\dots,t_d \in \mathfrak{m}$. By Nakayama, $t_1,\dots,t_d$ generate $
\mathfrak{m}$; this is a **regular system of parameters**. Their images (still d
enoted $t_i$) generate $\mathfrak{m}\widehat{A}$ in $\widehat{A}$, since $\mathf
rak{m}\widehat{A}/(\mathfrak{m}\widehat{A})^2 \cong \mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2$
 has the same $k$-basis.

### Step 3. The explicit map (Cohen structure theorem, equicharacteristic case)

$\widehat{A}$ is a complete Noetherian local ring of equicharacteristic (it cont
ains the field $k$) with coefficient field $k \subseteq \widehat{A}$ and maximal
 ideal generated by $t_1,\dots,t_d$. By the **Cohen structure theorem** for equi
characteristic complete local rings, the $k$-algebra homomorphism
$$\Phi : k[[X_1,\dots,X_d]] \longrightarrow \widehat{A}, \qquad X_i \longmapsto
t_i,$$
is **surjective**: every element of $\widehat{A}$ can be written as a convergent
 power series $\sum_{\alpha} c_\alpha t^\alpha$ with $c_\alpha \in k$.

### Step 4. Injectivity (dimension argument)

Completion preserves regularity and dimension for Noetherian local rings, so $\w
idehat{A}$ is again a regular local ring of dimension $d$; in particular $\wideh
at{A}$ is an integral domain and $\dim \widehat{A} = d$.

The source $k[[X_1,\dots,X_d]]$ is a regular local ring of dimension $d$ (hence
a domain, and catenary). Let $\mathfrak{P} = \ker\Phi$. Surjectivity gives
$$k[[X_1,\dots,X_d]]/\mathfrak{P} \;\cong\; \widehat{A}.$$
Since $\widehat{A}$ is a domain, $\mathfrak{P}$ is prime. By the dimension formu
la for the regular (hence catenary, equidimensional) local ring $k[[X_1,\dots,X_
d]]$,
$$\dim\bigl(k[[X_1,\dots,X_d]]/\mathfrak{P}\bigr) = d - \operatorname{ht}(\mathf
rak{P}).$$
The left-hand side equals $\dim \widehat{A} = d$, so $\operatorname{ht}(\mathfra
k{P}) = 0$. As $k[[X_1,\dots,X_d]]$ is a domain, its only height-$0$ prime is $(
0)$. Thus $\mathfrak{P} = (0)$, and $\Phi$ is **injective**.

### Step 5. Homeomorphism of topological rings

Both rings carry their natural adic topologies: $(X_1,\dots,X_d)$-adic on $k[[X_
1,\dots,X_d]]$ and $\mathfrak{m}\widehat{A}$-adic on $\widehat{A}$. Since the $t
_i$ generate $\mathfrak{m}\widehat{A}$,
$$\Phi\bigl((X_1,\dots,X_d)^n\bigr) = (t_1,\dots,t_d)^n = (\mathfrak{m}\widehat{
A})^n \quad \text{for all } n \ge 0.$$
So $\Phi$ maps the fundamental system of neighborhoods of $0$ in the source bije
ctively onto that of the target. Being a bijective ring homomorphism that is con
tinuous (preimage of $(\mathfrak{m}\widehat{A})^n$ is $(X)^n$) with continuous i
nverse (image of $(X)^n$ is $(\mathfrak{m}\widehat{A})^n$), $\Phi$ is a **homeom

$$\widehat{\mathscr{O}}_{X,p} \;\cong\; k[[X_1,\dots,X_d]].$$
