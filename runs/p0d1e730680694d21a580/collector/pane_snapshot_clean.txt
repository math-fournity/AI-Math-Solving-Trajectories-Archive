$$[B]_\gamma = \sup_{\substack{s,t \in [0,1] \\ s \neq t}} \frac{|B_t - B_s|}{|t

$$\Delta_n = \max_{k=0,\dots,2^n-1} |B_{(k+1)/2^n} - B_{k/2^n}|.$$

$$B_t - B_s = (B_{t^{(n)}} - B_{s^{(n)}}) + \sum_{m=n}^{\infty}(B_{t^{(m+1)}} -
B_{t^{(m)}}) - \sum_{m=n}^{\infty}(B_{s^{(m+1)}} - B_{s^{(m)}}).$$

$$|B_t - B_s| \leq \Delta_n + 2\sum_{m=n+1}^{\infty} \Delta_m.$$
