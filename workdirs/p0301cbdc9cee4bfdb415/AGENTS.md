# Problem

In a specialized solar energy facility, engineers have mapped out an $8 \times 8$ grid of potential installation plots. The grid is oriented with the rows running East-West and the columns running North-South. Each $1 \times 1$ plot can host at most one solar harvester unit.

A solar harvester is classified as "Inefficient" if it is simultaneously flanked by other harvesters in the plot immediately to its South, the plot immediately to its East, and the plot immediately to its West. Because they lack neighbors beyond the grid's perimeter, no harvester installed on the southernmost row, the easternmost column, or the westernmost column of the grid can ever be classified as "Inefficient."

The project manager needs to maximize the density of the facility while maintaining peak performance. What is the maximum number of solar harvester units that can be installed on the $8 \times 8$ grid such that not a single unit is classified as "Inefficient"?
