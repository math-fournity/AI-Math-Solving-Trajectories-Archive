## Solution

**Step 1: Evaluate the limit defining f(x).**

The inner sum is:
$$\sum_{k=1}^{n} kx = x\sum_{k=1}^{n} k = x\cdot\frac{n(n+1)}{2}$$

So the first factor becomes:
$$\frac{2}{n^2}\cdot x\cdot\frac{n(n+1)}{2} = x\cdot\frac{n+1}{n} \to x \quad\te
xt{as } n\to\infty$$

For the second factor $\dfrac{3^{nx}-1}{3^{nx}+1}$, let $t=3^{nx}$:

- **x > 0**: $3^{nx}\to\infty$, so $\frac{t-1}{t+1}\to 1$.
- **x = 0**: $3^{nx}=1$, so $\frac{0}{2}=0$.
- **x < 0**: $3^{nx}\to 0$, so $\frac{-1}{1}=-1$.

Combining with the first factor ($\to x$):

$$f(x) = \begin{cases} x\cdot 1 = x & x>0\\ 0 & x=0\\ x\cdot(-1) = -x & x<0\end{
cases} \;=\; |x|$$

**Step 2: Solve $f(x)=|x^2-2|$, i.e. $|x|=|x^2-2|$.**

$|a|=|b|$ gives $a=b$ or $a=-b$:

- **Case 1**: $x = x^2-2 \Rightarrow x^2-x-2=0 \Rightarrow (x-2)(x+1)=0 \Rightar
row x=2$ or $x=-1$.
- **Case 2**: $x = -(x^2-2) \Rightarrow x^2+x-2=0 \Rightarrow (x+2)(x-1)=0 \Righ
tarrow x=-2$ or $x=1$.

**Verification:**
- $x=2$: $|2|=2$, $|4-2|=2$ ✓
- $x=-1$: $|-1|=1$, $|1-2|=1$ ✓
- $x=-2$: $|-2|=2$, $|4-2|=2$ ✓
- $x=1$: $|1|=1$, $|1-2|=1$ ✓

**Step 3: Sum of all solutions.**

$$(-2)+(-1)+1+2 = 0$$

The sum of all solutions is $\boxed{0}$.
