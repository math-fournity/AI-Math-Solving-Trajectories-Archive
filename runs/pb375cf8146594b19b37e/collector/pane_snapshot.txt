$$\left(\frac{15}{13}\right) = \left(\frac{15 \bmod 13}{13}\right) = \left(\frac

$$\left(\frac{2}{p}\right) = (-1)^{(p^2-1)/8}.$$

For $p = 13$:

$$\frac{13^2 - 1}{8} = \frac{168}{8} = 21, \quad \text{which is odd},$$

$$\left(\frac{2}{13}\right) = (-1)^{21} = -1.$$
