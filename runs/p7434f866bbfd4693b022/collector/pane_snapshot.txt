Setup: Reduce via the Functional Equation

The completed functional equation of the Riemann zeta-function is

$$ \zeta(s)=\chi(s),\zeta(1-s),\qquad \chi(s)=2^{s}\pi^{s-1}\sin!
\Bigl(\tfrac{\pi s}{2}\Bigr),\Gamma(1-s). $$

Taking the logarithmic derivative of $\log\zeta(s)-\log\zeta(1-s)=\log\chi(s)$
gives (note $\frac{d}{ds}\log\zeta(1-s)=-\zeta'/\zeta(1-s)$):

$$ \frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}(s)+\frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}(1-s)=\frac{\chi'}{\chi}(s)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}(s)=\frac{\chi'}{\chi}(s)-
\frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}(1-s). $$

Setting $s=-\tfrac12+it$ (so $1-s=\tfrac32-it$):

$$ \frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}!\Bigl(-\tfrac12+it\Bigr)=\frac{\chi'}{\chi}!\Bigl(-
\tfrac12+it\Bigr)-\frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}!\Bigl(\tfrac32-it\Bigr). \tag{1} $$

Step 1: The second term in (1) is bounded

For $\sigma=\Re(s)=\tfrac32>1$, the Dirichlet series and Euler product converge
absolutely. In particular

  • $\zeta(\tfrac32+it)=\sum_{n\ge1}n^{-3/2-it}$ converges absolutely, and $|
  \zeta(\tfrac32+it)|\ge 1/\zeta(\tfrac32)>0$ (from $|1/\zeta(s)|
  \le\sum_{n\ge1}|\mu(n)|n^{-\sigma}\le\zeta(\sigma)$, equivalently the Euler
  product bound).
  • $\zeta'(\tfrac32+it)=-\sum_{n\ge2}(\log n),n^{-3/2-it}$ converges
  absolutely.

Hence $\zeta'/\zeta$ is bounded on the line $\Re(s)=\tfrac32$:

$$ \frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}!\Bigl(\tfrac32-it\Bigr)=O(1),\qquad t\to\infty. \tag{2}
$$

Step 2: Compute $\chi'/\chi(s)$

From $\log\chi(s)=s\log 2+(s-1)\log\pi+\log\sin(\tfrac{\pi s}{2})+\log\Gamma(1-
s)$,

$$ \frac{\chi'}{\chi}(s)=\log(2\pi)+\frac{\pi}{2}\cot!\Bigl(\tfrac{\pi s}
{2}\Bigr)-\psi(1-s), \tag{3} $$

where $\psi=\Gamma'/\Gamma$ is the digamma function (using $\frac{d}
{ds}\log\Gamma(1-s)=-\psi(1-s)$).

Evaluate at $s=-\tfrac12+it$, so $1-s=\tfrac32-it$:

$$ \frac{\chi'}{\chi}!\Bigl(-\tfrac12+it\Bigr)=\log(2\pi)+\frac{\pi}{2}\cot!
\Bigl(-\tfrac{\pi}{4}+\tfrac{i\pi t}{2}\Bigr)-\psi!\Bigl(\tfrac32-it\Bigr).
\tag{4} $$

Step 3: The cotangent term is bounded

Write $z=-\tfrac{\pi}{4}+i\tfrac{\pi t}{2}$ with $y=\tfrac{\pi t}{2}\to+\infty$.
Using

$$ \cot(x+iy)=\frac{\cos x\cosh y-i\sin x\sinh y}{\sin x\cosh y+i\cos x\sinh y},
$$

as $y\to+\infty$ with $\cosh y\sim\sinh y\sim\tfrac12 e^{y}$:

$$ \cot(x+iy)\longrightarrow\frac{\cos x-i\sin x}{\sin x+i\cos x}=\frac{e^{-ix}}
{i,e^{-ix}}=-i.$$

Therefore $\cot!\bigl(-\tfrac{\pi}{4}+i\tfrac{\pi t}{2}\bigr)\to -i$, and in
particular

$$ \frac{\pi}{2}\cot!\Bigl(-\tfrac{\pi}{4}+\tfrac{i\pi t}{2}\Bigr)=O(1). \tag{5}
$$

Step 4: The digamma term grows like $\log t$

Stirling's expansion for the digamma function gives, for $|z|\to\infty$ with $|
\arg z|<\pi$,

$$ \psi(z)=\log z-\frac{1}{2z}+O!\Bigl(\frac{1}{z^{2}}\Bigr). $$

With $z=\tfrac32-it$: $|z|\sim t$ and $\arg z\to -\tfrac{\pi}{2}$ (well within
$|\arg z|<\pi$), so

$$ \psi!\Bigl(\tfrac32-it\Bigr)=\log!\Bigl(\tfrac32-it\Bigr)+O!\Bigl(\frac{1}
{t}\Bigr)=\log t+\arg!\Bigl(\tfrac32-it\Bigr)\cdot i+O!\Bigl(\frac{1}{t}\Bigr).
$$

Since $\arg(\tfrac32-it)\to-\tfrac{\pi}{2}$,

$$ \psi!\Bigl(\tfrac32-it\Bigr)=\log t-\frac{i\pi}{2}+o(1),\qquad t\to\infty.
\tag{6} $$

In particular $\bigl|\psi(\tfrac32-it)\bigr|=\sqrt{(\log t)^{2}+\tfrac{\pi^{2}}
{4}}\sim\log t$.

Step 5: Assemble the asymptotic

Combining (1)–(6):

$$ \frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}!\Bigl(-\tfrac12+it\Bigr)=\underbrace{\log(2\pi)+
\frac{\pi}{2}\cot(\cdots)}{O(1)}-\underbrace{\psi!\bigl(\tfrac32-it\bigr)}{\sim,
\log t-,i\pi/2}-\underbrace{\frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}!\bigl(\tfrac32-it\bigr)}
_{O(1)}. $$

All terms except $-\psi(\tfrac32-it)$ are $O(1)$, while $\psi(\tfrac32-it)=\log
t+O(1)$. Hence

$$ \frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}!\Bigl(-\tfrac12+it\Bigr)=-\log t+O(1),\qquad t\to\infty,
$$

$$ \boxed{;\left|\frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}!\Bigl(-\tfrac12+it\Bigr)\right|\sim\log
