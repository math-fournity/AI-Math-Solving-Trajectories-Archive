## Computing the Cross-Ratio on the Circle

$$AD \cdot BC = AC \cdot DB + CD \cdot BA = 35 + 6L$$

Since the cyclic order is A, C, D, B, the pairs (A,B) and (C,D) do **not** separ

$$(A, B; C, D) = \frac{35}{35 + 6L}$$
