# Problem

A high-tech research facility is decommissioning its supply of liquid coolant. The total volume of coolant to be disposed of is at most 1000 liters, stored in a finite number of specialized canisters. Due to strict chemical stability regulations, each individual canister contains a volume of liquid $v$ such that $0 \le v \le 1$ liter.

The facility has exactly 100 disposal shielding units. To ensure safe transport, the safety board must determine a universal capacity limit $c$ (in liters) for these units. This limit must be large enough so that, regardless of the specific number of canisters or the exact volume of coolant in each (provided the total is $\le 1000$ and each canister is $\le 1$), the entire collection can always be partitioned into the 100 units without any single unit exceeding the capacity $c$.

Find the smallest constant $c > 0$ that guarantees this distribution is possible in every possible scenario.
