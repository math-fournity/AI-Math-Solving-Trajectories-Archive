## Setup and key objects

Let $G = \mathrm{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q})$, so $|G| = p^r$. For the prime $v$ of $K$ l
ying above $p$, define the **decomposition group**:
$$D_v = \{\sigma \in G : \sigma(v) = v\}.$$

## Step 1: $K_v/\mathbb{Q}_p$ is Galois with $\mathrm{Gal}(K_v/\mathbb{Q}_p) \co
ng D_v$

Since $K/\mathbb{Q}$ is Galois, every embedding of $K_v$ into $\overline{\mathbb
{Q}_p}$ over $\mathbb{Q}_p$ arises from an element of $D_v$ (an automorphism of
$K$ fixing $v$, hence extending continuously to $K_v$). This gives a natural iso
morphism
$$\mathrm{Gal}(K_v/\mathbb{Q}_p) \xrightarrow{\;\sim\;} D_v.$$
Therefore:
$$\boxed{[K_v : \mathbb{Q}_p] = |D_v|}.$$

## Step 2: $|D_v|$ is a power of $p$ dividing $p^r$

Since $D_v \leq G$ and $G$ is a $p$-group of order $p^r$, Lagrange's theorem giv
es $|D_v| \mid p^r$. Hence
$$[K_v : \mathbb{Q}_p] = |D_v| = p^s \quad \text{for some } 0 \le s \le r.$$

## Step 3: Refining via ramification — $e$ and $f$ are both $p$-powers

Writing $e = e(v|p)$ (ramification index) and $f = f(v|p)$ (residue degree), the
 fundamental identity for the Galois extension $K/\mathbb{Q}$ at $p$ gives
$$|G| = e \cdot f \cdot g, \qquad g = [G : D_v] = \text{number of primes of } K
\text{ above } p,$$
and $|D_v| = ef$.

- $I_v \trianglelefteq D_v$ (inertia group) has $|I_v| = e$, so $e$ is a $p$-pow
er (subgroup of $p$-group).
- $D_v/I_v \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Gal}(\kappa(v)/\mathbb{F}_p) \cong \mathbb{Z}
/f\mathbb{Z}$ is cyclic of order $f$. Being a quotient of a $p$-group, $f$ is a
$p$-power.
- $g = [G:D_v]$ is a $p$-power.

So $[K_v:\mathbb{Q}_p] = ef = p^r/g = p^s$ with $s = r - \log_p g$.

## Step 4: The degree is not pinned to $p^r$ in general — it depends on $g$

The value of $g$ (hence $s$) is **not** determined by $p$ and $r$ alone. Concret

The degree of $K_v/\mathbb{Q}_p$ is
$$[K_v : \mathbb{Q}_p] = |D_v| = \frac{p^r}{g},$$
