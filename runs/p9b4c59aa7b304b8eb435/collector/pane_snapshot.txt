## Solution

**Setting up the recurrence.** The regulatory balance gives:
$$n(a_{n+1} - a_n + 3) = a_{n+1} + a_n + 3$$

Solving for $a_{n+1}$ (for $n \geq 2$ so $n-1 \neq 0$):
$$(n-1)a_{n+1} = (n+1)a_n - 3(n-1) \implies a_{n+1} = \frac{(n+1)}{(n-1)}\,a_n -
 3$$

**Finding the general solution.** A particular solution $a_n = 3(n-1)$ is easily
 verified. The homogeneous equation $a_{n+1}^{(h)} = \frac{n+1}{n-1}a_n^{(h)}$ t
elescopes:
$$a_n^{(h)} = a_2^{(h)}\prod_{k=2}^{n-1}\frac{k+1}{k-1} = a_2^{(h)}\cdot\frac{n(
n-1)}{2}$$

So $a_n = 3(n-1) + C\cdot\frac{n(n-1)}{2}$. Setting $n=2$: $a_2 = 3 + C$, so $C
= a_2 - 3$.

Since $a_2$ is odd, $a_2 - 3$ is even; write $a_2 - 3 = 2m$ with $m \in \mathbb{
Z}$. Then:
$$\boxed{a_n = (n-1)(mn + 3)}, \qquad m = \frac{a_2-3}{2} \in \mathbb{Z}$$

(Check: $n=1$ gives $a_1 = 0$, consistent with the $n=1$ recurrence.)

**Using the divisibility condition on $a_{2009}$.** We have $a_{2009} = 2008(200
9m+3)$. Since $2008 = 2^3\cdot 251$ and $2010 = 2\cdot 3\cdot 5\cdot 67$, we get
 $\gcd(2008,2010)=2$. Thus:
$$1005 \mid (2009m + 3)$$

Since $2009 \equiv -1 \pmod{1005}$, this gives $-m + 3 \equiv 0 \pmod{1005}$, i.
e., $m = 3 + 1005t$ for some $t \in \mathbb{Z}$.

**Reducing $a_n \pmod{2010}$.** Substituting:
$$a_n = (n-1)\bigl(3(n+1) + 1005tn\bigr) = 3(n^2-1) + 1005\,t\,n(n-1)$$

Since $n(n-1)$ is always even, $1005\,t\,n(n-1)$ is divisible by $2010$. Therefo
re:
$$a_n \equiv 3(n^2 - 1) \pmod{2010}$$

This is **independent of $t$** (and hence of the particular valid sequence). So:
$$2010 \mid a_n \iff 670 \mid (n^2 - 1) = (n-1)(n+1)$$

**Solving $n^2 \equiv 1 \pmod{670}$.** Factor $670 = 2\cdot 5\cdot 67$. By CRT w
e need:
- $n$ odd (from $\bmod\; 2$),
- $n \equiv \pm 1 \pmod{5}$,
- $n \equiv \pm 1 \pmod{67}$.

The four sign combinations (mod $335 = 5\times 67$), combined with the parity co
nstraint, give:

| $n \bmod 5$ | $n \bmod 67$ | $n \bmod 335$ | Smallest odd $n>1$ |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| $1$ | $1$ | $1$ | $671$ |
| $-1$ | $-1$ | $334$ | $669$ |
| $1$ | $-1$ | $66$ | $401$ |
| $-1$ | $1$ | $269$ | $269$ |

The last row: $n = 1 + 67k$ with $1+2k\equiv 4\pmod{5}$ gives $k\equiv 4\pmod{5}
$, so $k=4\Rightarrow n=269$ (odd). ✓

**Verification of $n=269$:** $269$ is odd; $269 = 4\cdot 67 + 1 \equiv 1\pmod{67
}$ ✓; $269 = 53\cdot 5 + 4 \equiv -1\pmod{5}$ ✓. Then $269^2 - 1 = 72360 = 670\t
imes 108$ ✓, so $670\mid(n^2-1)$ and thus $2010\mid a_{269}$.

No smaller $n>1$ satisfies all three congruences (checked all odd $n<269$ with $
n\equiv\pm1\pmod{67}$: candidates $133, 135, 267$ all fail the $\bmod\;5$ condit
ion).

**Answer:** The smallest integer index $n > 1$ such that $2010 \mid a_n$ is $\bo
xed{269}$.
