**Statement:** For `d : ℤ` squarefree, `d ≠ 1`, with `d ≡ 1 [ZMOD 4]`, we have `
discr (QuadraticAlgebra ℚ d 0) = d`.

**Proof.** The algebra `QuadraticAlgebra ℚ d 0` is the field `K = ℚ(√d)`. Since
`d` is squarefree, `d ≠ 1`, and `d ≡ 1 mod 4`, the ring of integers of `K` is

$$\mathcal{O}_K = \mathbb{Z}\!\left[\frac{1+\sqrt{d}}{2}\right],$$

with integral basis $\{1, \omega\}$ where $\omega = \frac{1+\sqrt{d}}{2}$. The d
iscriminant is $\det(\operatorname{tr}_{K/\mathbb{Q}}(\alpha_i \alpha_j))$ for t
his basis. The nontrivial automorphism of $K$ sends $\sqrt{d} \mapsto -\sqrt{d}$
, hence $\omega \mapsto \frac{1-\sqrt{d}}{2} = 1 - \omega$.

Computing the trace form:
- $\operatorname{tr}(1 \cdot 1) = 1 + 1 = 2$
- $\operatorname{tr}(1 \cdot \omega) = \omega + (1-\omega) = 1$
- $\operatorname{tr}(\omega \cdot \omega) = \operatorname{tr}(\omega^2)$. Since
$\omega^2 = \omega + \frac{d-1}{4}$ (from $\omega^2 - \omega - \frac{d-1}{4} = 0
$, which follows from $(2\omega-1)^2 = d$), we get $\operatorname{tr}(\omega^2)
= \operatorname{tr}(\omega) + \frac{d-1}{4} \cdot 2 = 1 + \frac{d-1}{2} = \frac{
d+1}{2}$.

The discriminant is:
$$\det \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & \frac{d+1}{2} \end{pmatrix} = 2 \cdot \frac{
d+1}{2} - 1 = (d+1) - 1 = d. \qquad \blacksquare$$
