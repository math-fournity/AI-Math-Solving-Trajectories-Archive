## Solution

**Setting up coordinates.** Place $B=(0,0)$ and $C=(14,0)$. From $AB=13$, $AC=15
$:
$$x^2+y^2=169,\quad (x-14)^2+y^2=225 \implies x=5,\; y=12.$$
So $A=(5,12)$, $B=(0,0)$, $C=(14,0)$.

**Finding $D$.** Triangle $ACD$ is isosceles right with $\angle ADC=90°$, so $D$
 lies on the perpendicular bisector of $AC$ at distance $AC/2=15/2$ from the mid
point $M=(19/2, 6)$. A unit perpendicular to $\vec{AC}=(9,-12)$ is $(4/5,3/5)$.
The two candidates are $M\pm(15/2)(4/5,3/5)=M\pm(6,9/2)$, giving $(31/2,21/2)$ a
nd $(7/2,3/2)$. Checking the side of line $AC$ (equation $12x+9y=168$): $B$ give
s $-168<0$ and $(7/2,3/2)$ gives $55.5-168<0$, so $D=(7/2,3/2)$.

**Finding $F=AD\cap CB$.** Line $AD$: direction $(-3/2,-21/2)$, slope $7$, equat
ion $y=7x-23$. Setting $y=0$: $x=23/7$, so $F=(23/7,0)$.

**Finding $E$.** Triangle $BCE$ is isosceles right with $\angle BEC=90°$, so $E$
 lies on the perpendicular bisector of $BC$ at distance $7$ from midpoint $(7,0)
$. Since $E$ and $A$ are on the same side of $BC$ (above), $E=(7,7)$.

**Finding $G=BE\cap CA$.** Line $BE$: $y=x$. Line $CA$: slope $-4/3$, equation $
y=-\frac{4}{3}(x-14)$. Setting $y=x$: $3x=-4x+56\implies x=8$, so $G=(8,8)$.

**Computing $\cos\angle AGF$.**
$$\vec{GA}=A-G=(-3,4),\qquad \vec{GF}=F-G=\left(\tfrac{23}{7}-8,\,-8\right)=\lef
t(-\tfrac{33}{7},-8\right).$$
$$\vec{GA}\cdot\vec{GF}=(-3)\!\left(-\tfrac{33}{7}\right)+4(-8)=\tfrac{99}{7}-32
=\tfrac{99-224}{7}=-\tfrac{125}{7}.$$
$$|\vec{GA}|=5,\qquad |\vec{GF}|=\sqrt{\tfrac{1089}{49}+64}=\sqrt{\tfrac{4225}{4
9}}=\tfrac{65}{7}.$$
$$\cos\angle AGF=\frac{-125/7}{5\cdot 65/7}=\frac{-125}{325}=-\frac{5}{13}.$$
