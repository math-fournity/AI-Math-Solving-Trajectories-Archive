## Solution

**Construction analysis.** At each stage, the operation removes *vertical* strip
s of full height 1 and width equal to one-third of each remaining strip's width.
 The $y$-coordinate is never restricted — it always ranges over $[0,1]$. Therefo
re $C_n$ is a Cartesian product:

$$C_n = K_n \times [0,1],$$

where $K_n \subset [0,1]$ is the standard 1D Cantor set at stage $n$ (the set ob
tained by repeatedly removing middle thirds from $[0,1]$).

**Measure of $K_n$.** At stage $n$, the 1D Cantor construction leaves $2^n$ inte
rvals, each of length $(1/3)^n$, so

$$\lambda(K_n) = 2^n \cdot \left(\tfrac{1}{3}\right)^n = \left(\tfrac{2}{3}\righ
t)^n.$$

**Direct verification of the recurrence.** Equivalently, at step $n$ we remove $
2^{n-1}$ vertical strips, each of width $(1/3)^n$ and height $1$, so the area re
moved is $2^{n-1}\cdot(1/3)^n$. Summing all removed areas:

$$\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} 2^{k-1}\left(\tfrac{1}{3}\right)^k = \frac{1}{3}\sum_{k=0}
^{\infty}\left(\tfrac{2}{3}\right)^k = \frac{1}{3}\cdot\frac{1}{1-2/3} = 1.$$

Since the total area removed equals the area of the original square (1), the rem
aining measure is $1 - 1 = 0$.

**Measure of $C_n$ via the product.** By the product structure and Fubini/Tonell
i (the sets are measurable and the factors are independent),

$$\lambda_2(C_n) = \lambda_1(K_n)\cdot\lambda_1([0,1]) = \left(\tfrac{2}{3}\righ
t)^n \cdot 1 = \left(\tfrac{2}{3}\right)^n.$$

**Limit.** Taking $n \to \infty$,

$$\lim_{n\to\infty}\lambda_2(C_n) = \lim_{n\to\infty}\left(\tfrac{2}{3}\right)^n
 = 0.$$

The limit set $C = \bigcap_{n=0}^{\infty} C_n = K \times [0,1]$, where $K$ is th
e classical Cantor set with $\lambda_1(K)=0$. By continuity of measure from abov
e (the $C_n$ are nested and $\lambda_2(C_0)<\infty$),

$$\lambda_2(C) = \lim_{n\to\infty}\lambda_2(C_n) = 0.$$

**Conclusion.** The Lebesgue measure of $C_n$ is $(2/3)^n$, which tends to $0$ a
s $n\to\infty$. **Yes, the Lebesgue measure of the 2D Cantor set is zero.**
