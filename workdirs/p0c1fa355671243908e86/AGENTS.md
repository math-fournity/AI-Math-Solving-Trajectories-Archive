# Problem

In a vast desert reclamation project, a survey team has identified exactly $1998$ specific sites where rare cacti are growing. The environmental impact report states a unique geographic property of these locations: if you were to randomly select any group of $17$ cacti, you would always be able to find a subgroup of $11$ cacti that can all be enclosed within a single circular research fence of diameter $1$ kilometer.

The conservation department now needs to protect every single one of the $1998$ cacti. They plan to do this by deploying heavy-duty geodesic domes. Each dome has a fixed diameter of $2$ kilometers. A dome is said to protect a cactus if the cactus is located anywhere inside the dome or exactly on its perimeter.

Based on the distribution property provided, what is the minimum number of $2$-kilometer diameter domes required to ensure that all $1998$ cacti are covered?
