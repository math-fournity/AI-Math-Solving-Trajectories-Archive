## Proof

Let $X = \ell^1(\mathbb{N})$ with norm $\|x\| = \sum_{n=1}^\infty |x_n|$. The op
erator $B$ is defined by

$$(Bx)_n = \sum_{k=1}^\infty \frac{b^n}{n!}\, x_{n+k} = \frac{b^n}{n!}\sum_{k=1}
^\infty x_{n+k}, \qquad n \ge 1.$$

We show $\|B\| = e^b - 1$.

---

### Step 1: Upper bound $\|B\| \le e^b - 1$.

For $x \in \ell^1$, the series $\sum_{k=1}^\infty x_{n+k}$ converges absolutely
since $\sum_{k=1}^\infty |x_{n+k}| \le \|x\|_1$. We estimate:

$$\|Bx\| = \sum_{n=1}^\infty |(Bx)_n| \le \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{b^n}{n!}\sum_{
k=1}^\infty |x_{n+k}|.$$

All terms are non-negative, so by Tonelli's theorem we may interchange the order
 of summation. Setting $j = n+k$ (so $k \ge 1$ gives $j \ge n+1$, equivalently $
n \le j-1$):

$$\|Bx\| \le \sum_{j=2}^\infty |x_j| \sum_{n=1}^{j-1} \frac{b^n}{n!}.$$

Since $\frac{b^n}{n!} > 0$ for all $n \ge 1$,

$$\sum_{n=1}^{j-1} \frac{b^n}{n!} \le \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{b^n}{n!} = e^b -
 1.$$

Therefore

$$\|Bx\| \le (e^b - 1)\sum_{j=2}^\infty |x_j| \le (e^b - 1)\,\|x\|.$$

This holds for all $x \in \ell^1$, so $\|B\| \le e^b - 1$.

---

### Step 2: Lower bound $\|B\| \ge e^b - 1$.

Let $e_m \in \ell^1$ be the $m$-th standard basis vector: $(e_m)_j = \delta_{jm}
$. Then $\|e_m\| = 1$ and

$$(Be_m)_n = \frac{b^n}{n!}\sum_{k=1}^\infty \delta_{n+k,\,m}.$$

The inner sum equals $1$ if there exists $k \ge 1$ with $n+k = m$ (i.e., $1 \le
n \le m-1$), and $0$ otherwise. Hence

$$(Be_m)_n = \begin{cases} \dfrac{b^n}{n!}, & 1 \le n \le m-1,\\[6pt] 0, & n \ge
 m. \end{cases}$$

Therefore

$$\|Be_m\| = \sum_{n=1}^{m-1} \frac{b^n}{n!}.$$

As $m \to \infty$,

$$\|Be_m\| = \sum_{n=1}^{m-1}\frac{b^n}{n!} \;\longrightarrow\; \sum_{n=1}^{\inf
ty}\frac{b^n}{n!} = e^b - 1.$$

Since $\|B\| \ge \|Be_m\|$ for every $m$ (as $\|e_m\| = 1$), we obtain

$$\|B\| \ge \lim_{m\to\infty}\|Be_m\| = e^b - 1.$$

---

### Conclusion

Combining both bounds:

$$\boxed{\|B\| = e^b - 1.}$$
