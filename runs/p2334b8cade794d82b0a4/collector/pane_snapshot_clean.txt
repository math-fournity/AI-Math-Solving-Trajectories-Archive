## Solution

**Goal.** The rightmost nonzero digit of $n!$ in base $p$ is the residue of the
"$p$-adic unit part" of $n!$, namely
$$U(n) = \frac{n!}{p^{v_p(n!)}} \pmod{p}.$$

**Key formula.** Let $a_0, a_1, \dots, a_m$ be the base-$p$ digits of $n$. Then
$$U(n) = (-1)^{v_p(n!)} \prod_{i=0}^{m} (a_i!) \pmod{p}.$$

*Derivation.* Split $1\cdot 2 \cdots n$ into the $p$-free factors and the multip
les of $p$:
$$n! = \Bigl(\prod_{\substack{1\le j\le n\\ p\nmid j}} j\Bigr)\cdot p^{\lfloor n
/p\rfloor}\cdot \lfloor n/p\rfloor!.$$
In each complete block of $p$ consecutive integers, the units $1,2,\dots,p-1$ ha
ve product $(p-1)! \equiv -1 \pmod{p}$ (Wilson). With $\lfloor n/p\rfloor$ full
blocks and a partial block of $a_0 = n\bmod p$ units (product $a_0!$), the unit
product is $(-1)^{\lfloor n/p\rfloor} a_0!$. Hence
$$U(n) = (-1)^{\lfloor n/p\rfloor}\, a_0!\, U(\lfloor n/p\rfloor) \pmod{p}.$$
Iterating over successive quotients $n_j = \lfloor n/p^j\rfloor$ and telescoping
, the total sign exponent is $\sum_{j\ge 1} n_j = v_p(n!)$, giving the closed fo
rm above.

**Computation for $n=2023$, $p=7$.**

*Base-7 expansion.* $7^3=343$, $7^4=2401>2023$.
$$2023 = 5\cdot343 + 6\cdot 49 + 2\cdot 7 + 0 = (5620)_7.$$
So $a_0=0,\ a_1=2,\ a_2=6,\ a_3=5$.

*Digit sum and valuation.*
$$s_7(2023)=5+6+2+0=13,\qquad v_7(2023!)=\frac{2023-13}{7-1}=\frac{2010}{6}=335.
$$

*Sign.* $(-1)^{335}=-1$.

*Factorial product mod 7.*
$$\prod_i (a_i!) = 0!\cdot 2!\cdot 6!\cdot 5! = 1\cdot 2\cdot 720\cdot 120.$$
Reduce each factor mod $7$:
- $720 = 7\cdot102 + 6 \equiv 6 \pmod 7$,
- $120 = 7\cdot17 + 1 \equiv 1 \pmod 7$.

So $\prod_i(a_i!) \equiv 1\cdot 2\cdot 6\cdot 1 = 12 \equiv 5 \pmod 7$.

*Combine.*
$$U(2023) = (-1)\cdot 5 = -5 \equiv 2 \pmod 7.$$

1\equiv 6$. ✓

✓
- $n=14$: $14!/7^2 = 1779148800 \equiv 2$. Formula: $(-1)^2(0!\cdot2!\cdot1!) =
2$. ✓
