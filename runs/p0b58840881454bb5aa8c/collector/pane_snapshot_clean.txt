$$
\nabla_k \rho \;=\; \partial_k \rho \;-\; w\,\Gamma^{i}{}_{ki}\,\rho .
$$

$$
\nabla_k \rho \;=\; \partial_k \rho \;-\; \Gamma^{i}{}_{ki}\,\rho . \tag{1}
$$

So the task reduces to finding $\Gamma^{i}{}_{ki}$ **without** invoking the expl

$$
Contract (2) with $g^{ij}$ and sum over $i,j$:
$$
g^{ij}\,\partial_k g_{ij} \;-\; \Gamma^{l}{}_{ki}\,g_{lj}\,g^{ij} \;-\; \Gamma^{
l}{}_{kj}\,g_{il}\,g^{ij} \;=\; 0 .

$$
g^{ij}\,\partial_k g_{ij} \;-\; \Gamma^{l}{}_{ki}\,\delta^{i}_{l} \;-\; \Gamma^{
