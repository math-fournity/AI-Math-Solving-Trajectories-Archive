## Solution

**Setting up the equation.**

Mixture Alpha potency: $100A + 10B + C$
Mixture Beta potency: $100B + 10A + D$

The equilibrium condition is:

$$\frac{100A + 10B + C}{100B + 10A + D} = \frac{C}{D}$$

Cross-multiplying:

$$D(100A + 10B + C) = C(100B + 10A + D)$$

$$100AD + 10BD + CD = 100BC + 10AC + CD$$

Canceling $CD$ and dividing by 10:

$$10AD + BD = 10BC + AC$$

Rearranging:

$$A(10D - C) = B(10C - D)$$

$$\frac{A}{B} = \frac{10C - D}{10D - C}$$

**Key observations.** Since $C, D \in \{1,\ldots,9\}$, we have $10C - D \geq 10
- 9 = 1 > 0$ and $10D - C \geq 1 > 0$, so the ratio is always positive. We need
the reduced form of $\frac{10C-D}{10D-C}$ to have both numerator and denominator
 $\leq 9$ (so they can serve as $A, B \in \{1,\ldots,9\}$), with $A, B, C, D$ al
