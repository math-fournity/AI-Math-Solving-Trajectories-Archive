## Setup

$$\pm\mathbf{e}_1 = (\pm1,0),\quad \pm\mathbf{e}_3 = (0,\pm1),\quad \pm\mathbf{e
}_2 = \pm(1,1)$$
where **e₂** = **e₁** + **e₃** (since the 60° direction is the sum of the two ba
sis directions).

A flight of length n in direction **v** contributes n·**v** to the displacement.
 Since vector addition is commutative, the order of flights doesn't matter. We n
eed:
$$\sum_{n=1}^{N} n\,\mathbf{v}_n = \mathbf{0}, \quad \mathbf{v}_n \in \{(\pm1,0)

## N = 1 and N = 2 are impossible

**N = 1:** A single unit vector cannot be **0**.

**N = 2:** Need **v₁** + 2**v₂** = **0**, so **v₁** = −2**v₂**. But **v₁** is a
unit vector while 2**v₂** has magnitude 2. Impossible.
