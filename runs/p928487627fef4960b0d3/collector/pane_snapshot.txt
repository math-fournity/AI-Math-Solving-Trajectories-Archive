## Proof

The statement "1/9 of 60 is 5" is **false** under ordinary arithmetic, since 60
÷ 9 ≈ 6.67, not 5. This signals that the puzzle is not about computation but abo
ut a hidden pattern.

**Finding the pattern.** Consider the English word for the number 60: **"sixty"*
*. Counting its letters:

s-i-x-t-y → **5 letters**.

So the claim "1/9 of 60 is 5" is true if "X of N is k" means **"the English word
 for N has k letters."** The fractions 1/9 and 1/20 are distractors — they play
no role; only the number and its spelled-out form matter.

**Verification of the pattern.** Under this rule, "1/9 of 60 is 5" holds because
 "sixty" has 5 letters. ✓

**Applying to the question.** We need "1/20 of 80." The number is 80, whose Engl
ish word is **"eighty"**. Counting its letters:

e-i-g-h-t-y → **6 letters**.

Therefore, 1/20 of 80 is **6**.
