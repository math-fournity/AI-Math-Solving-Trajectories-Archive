## Solution

Consider the function

$$g(z) = 2z.$$

**Claim.** $g$ is entire and satisfies $g(n) = 2n$ for every natural number $n$.

**Proof.**

The function $g(z) = 2z$ is a polynomial of degree $1$ in $z$. Every polynomial
is holomorphic on the entire complex plane $\mathbb{C}$ (its complex derivative
$g'(z) = 2$ exists everywhere), so $g$ is entire.

For any natural number $n \in \mathbb{N}$, evaluating at $z = n$ gives

$$g(n) = 2n.$$

Thus $g(z) = 2z$ is an entire function satisfying the required interpolation con
dition $g(n) = 2n$ for all natural numbers $n$. $\blacksquare$
