## Solution

**Setup.** The cube has vertices at the standard unit-cube positions, with the r
otation axis being the space diagonal $OF$ from $O(0,0,0)$ to $F(1,1,1)$, in dir
ection $\mathbf{u}=(1,1,1)/\sqrt{3}$.

**Perpendicular distance from the axis.** For a point $\mathbf{p}=(x,y,z)$, the
squared distance to the axis is
$$r^2 = \|\mathbf{p}\|^2 - (\mathbf{p}\cdot\mathbf{u})^2 = x^2+y^2+z^2 - \frac{(
x+y+z)^2}{3}.$$
Let $s = x+y+z$, so the axial coordinate is $t = s/\sqrt{3}$, with $dt = ds/\sqr
t{3}$.

**Volume of revolution.** Since the cube is convex, each perpendicular cross-sec
tion is a convex polygon, and rotating it sweeps a disk of radius $R(s) = \max r
$ over all cube points with $x+y+z=s$. Thus
$$V = \int_0^{\sqrt{3}} \pi R(t)^2\,dt = \frac{\pi}{\sqrt{3}}\int_0^3 R(s)^2\,ds
.$$

**Finding $R(s)^2 = \max(x^2+y^2+z^2) - s^2/3$.** Since $x^2+y^2+z^2$ is convex,
 its maximum over the cross-section polygon is attained at a vertex.

- **$s\in[0,1]$:** cross-section is the triangle $(s,0,0),(0,s,0),(0,0,s)$. Max
$= s^2$, so $R^2 = s^2 - s^2/3 = \tfrac{2}{3}s^2$.

- **$s\in[1,2]$:** cross-section is a hexagon with vertices $(1,0,s{-}1),(1,s{-}
1,0),(s{-}1,1,0),(0,1,s{-}1),(0,s{-}1,1),(s{-}1,0,1)$. Each gives $x^2+y^2+z^2 =
 1+(s-1)^2$. With $u=s-1$: $R^2 = 1+u^2 - \tfrac{(u+1)^2}{3} = \tfrac{2}{3}(u^2-
u+1)$.

- **$s\in[2,3]$:** by the cube's symmetry $(x,y,z)\mapsto(1-x,1-y,1-z)$ (which s
ends $s\mapsto 3-s$), this mirrors segment 1. Directly: triangle $(1,1,s{-}2),(1
,s{-}2,1),(s{-}2,1,1)$, max $= 2+(s-2)^2$. With $v=s-2$: $R^2 = 2+v^2-\tfrac{(v+
2)^2}{3} = \tfrac{2}{3}(v-1)^2$.

**Evaluating the integral.**

$$\int_0^3 R(s)^2\,ds = \underbrace{\frac{2}{3}\int_0^1 s^2\,ds}_{I_1} + \underb
race{\frac{2}{3}\int_0^1(u^2-u+1)\,du}_{I_2} + \underbrace{\frac{2}{3}\int_0^1(v
-1)^2\,dv}_{I_3}.$$

$$I_1 = \tfrac{2}{3}\cdot\tfrac{1}{3} = \tfrac{2}{9},\qquad I_2 = \tfrac{2}{3}\!
\left(\tfrac{1}{3}-\tfrac{1}{2}+1\right) = \tfrac{2}{3}\cdot\tfrac{7}{6} = \tfra
c{7}{9},\qquad I_3 = \tfrac{2}{3}\cdot\tfrac{1}{3} = \tfrac{2}{9}.$$

$$\int_0^3 R(s)^2\,ds = \frac{2+7+2}{9} = \frac{11}{9}.$$

**Final volume.**

$$V = \frac{\pi}{\sqrt{3}}\cdot\frac{11}{9} = \frac{11\pi}{9\sqrt{3}} = \frac{11
\pi\sqrt{3}}{27}.$$

The total volume of the spatial region swept out by the rotating cube is

$$\boxed{\dfrac{11\pi\sqrt{3}}{27}}.$$
