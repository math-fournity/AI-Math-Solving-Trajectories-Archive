## Setup and Definitions

A **Toeplitz sequence** $x \in \{0,1\}^{\mathbb{Z}}$ is one where every coordina

at $x(i) = x(i + kp_i)$ for all $k \in \mathbb{Z}$. A **dyadic** Toeplitz sequen
ce has all periods being powers of $2$.

The **dyadic Toeplitz system** is the orbit closure $X = \overline{\{\sigma^n x
: n \in \mathbb{Z}\}}$ under the shift $\sigma$, which is a minimal subshift of
$\{0,1\}^{\mathbb{Z}}$.

The **topological entropy** is $h_{\text{top}}(X) = \lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{1}{n
}\log_2 p(n)$, where $p(n) = |B_n(X)|$ is the block complexity (number of distin

## Key Structural Fact: Periodic Skeleton

A dyadic Toeplitz sequence is built in stages. At stage $k$ (using period $2^k$)
, we fill a subset of the remaining "holes" with a periodic pattern of period $2
^k$. Define:
- $P_k$ = set of positions determined by stage $k$ (periodic with period dividin
g $2^k$).
- $H_k = \mathbb{Z} \setminus P_k$ = remaining holes after stage $k$.

The sets are nested: $P_1 \subseteq P_2 \subseteq \cdots$ and $H_1 \supseteq H_2
 \supseteq \cdots$. Each $H_k$ is periodic with period $2^k$ (it is a union of r
esidue classes mod $2^k$). Let $\rho_k = d(H_k)$ be the density of holes after s
tage $k$. Since at each stage we fill a positive fraction of remaining holes, $\
rho_k \to 0$ as $k \to \infty$. The sequence is **regular**: $\bigcup_k P_k = \m
athbb{Z}$, so the periodic skeleton has full density.

## Bounding the Complexity

**Claim:** For any block of length $n$ appearing in $X$, the number of positions

*Proof of claim:* Since $H_k$ is periodic with period $2^k$, in any interval of

Now, the positions determined by stage $k$ are **forced** (they take a fixed val

$$p(n) \leq 2^{\lceil n\rho_k \rceil + 2^k}$$

## Computing the Entropy

Given $\varepsilon > 0$, choose $k$ large enough that $\rho_k < \varepsilon$. Th

$$\frac{\log_2 p(n)}{n} \leq \frac{n\varepsilon + 2^k + 1}{n} = \varepsilon + \f

$$\frac{\log_2 p(n)}{n} \leq 2\varepsilon.$$

$$h_{\text{top}}(X) = \lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{\log_2 p(n)}{n} = 0.$$

## Conclusion

The topological entropy of a dyadic Toeplitz system is $\boxed{0}$.

The essential reason is that the periodic skeleton of a (regular) dyadic Toeplit
