# Problem

A logistics company operates a massive server warehouse represented by a $100 \times 100$ grid of tile slots. To stabilize the flooring, they have installed $5000$ cooling units, each of which is a rectangular block covering exactly $2 \times 1$ slots. The entire grid is perfectly covered by these units, with no overlaps or gaps.

A $2 \times 2$ section of the grid is classified as "Electrically Isolated" if it is covered by exactly two cooling units (meaning the four slots in that $2 \times 2$ area are accounted for by two $2 \times 1$ blocks lying side-by-side or stacked).

Given that the units can be oriented horizontally or vertically in any arrangement that covers the grid, what is the minimum number of Electrically Isolated $2 \times 2$ sections that must exist in the warehouse?
