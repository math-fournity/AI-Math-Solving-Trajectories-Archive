## Proof

Let $\nu_{n,d} : \mathbb{P}^n \to \mathbb{P}^N$ be the degree-$d$ Veronese embed
ding, where $N = \binom{n+d}{d} - 1$. It is defined by sending $[x_0 : \cdots :
x_n]$ to the point whose coordinates are all monomials of degree $d$ in $x_0, \l
dots, x_n$.

**Key property.** The Veronese embedding satisfies
$$\nu_{n,d}^*\, \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^N}(1) \;=\; \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^n}(d
).$$
Indeed, a hyperplane in $\mathbb{P}^N$ is cut out by a linear form in the coordi
nates of $\mathbb{P}^N$, and these coordinates are precisely the degree-$d$ mono
mials in $x_0,\ldots,x_n$. Hence the pullback of a hyperplane section is a degre
e-$d$ hypersurface in $\mathbb{P}^n$.

**Degree computation.** The degree of $\nu_{n,d}(C)$ is, by definition, the numb
er of intersection points of $\nu_{n,d}(C)$ with a general hyperplane $H \subset
 \mathbb{P}^N$:
$$\deg\bigl(\nu_{n,d}(C)\bigr) \;=\; \#\bigl(\nu_{n,d}(C) \cap H\bigr) \;=\; \#\
bigl(C \cap \nu_{n,d}^{-1}(H)\bigr).$$

Since $\nu_{n,d}$ is an embedding (in particular injective on points), the preim
age $\nu_{n,d}^{-1}(H)$ is a degree-$d$ hypersurface $F \subset \mathbb{P}^n$. F
or a general hyperplane $H$, the hypersurface $F$ is general of degree $d$, and
$F$ intersects the curve $C$ properly (transversely, in finitely many reduced po
ints).

By Bézout's theorem, a curve $C$ of degree $a$ in $\mathbb{P}^n$ intersected wit
h a general hypersurface of degree $d$ meets in exactly $a \cdot d$ points (coun
ted with multiplicity, which is $1$ for a general, transverse intersection):
$$\#(C \cap F) \;=\; a \cdot d.$$

Therefore
$$\boxed{\deg\bigl(\nu_{n,d}(C)\bigr) \;=\; a\,d.}$$
