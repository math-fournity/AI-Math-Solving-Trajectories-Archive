# Problem

A specialized logistics hub is designed as a massive rectangular floor plan measuring 19 meters in width and 2015 meters in length, divided into 1-meter square storage slots.

The facility houses "L-modular racks." Each rack is a rigid structure composed of 101 unit-square modules. The shape is formed by a $10 \times 10$ square base of modules, plus exactly one additional module attached to the right side of the module in the top-right corner of the base. These racks must be placed flat on the floor, aligned perfectly with the grid of storage slots. They cannot be rotated or flipped, and no two racks can overlap.

Let $N$ represent the maximum possible number of these L-modular racks that can be packed into the facility. Suppose there are $W$ distinct ways to arrange exactly $N$ racks within the grid.

Find the value of $k$ such that $W = \binom{250}{k}$.
