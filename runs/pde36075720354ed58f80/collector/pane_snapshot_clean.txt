$$d=\frac{u^2+v^2}{2u-1}, \quad\text{so}\quad u^2+v^2=d(2u-1).$$
Since $DC$ is tangent to $\Omega$ at $C$, the tangent–secant theorem gives $DC^2

$$d=\frac{\lambda(1-\mu)}{\lambda-\mu}, \quad\text{equivalently}\quad \lambda(d-

$$P=(1-\mu+\sigma u,\;\sigma v).$$
