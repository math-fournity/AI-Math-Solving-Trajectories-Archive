**Problem.** Find the condition on $0<r<R$ such that there exists a conformal ma
p mapping $\{|z|\le R\}$ onto $\{|w|\le 2\}$ and simultaneously mapping $\{|z|\l
e r\}$ onto $\{|w|\le 1\}$.

---

## Proof

**Step 1. The conformal map must be Möbius.**

A conformal (bijective holomorphic) map from the disk $\{|z|<R\}$ onto the disk
$\{|w|<2\}$ is, after rescaling $z\mapsto z/R$ and $w\mapsto w/2$, an automorphi
sm of the unit disk. Every automorphism of the unit disk is a Möbius (fractional
 linear) transformation. Hence

$$
f(z)=\frac{2}{R}\,e^{i\theta}\,\frac{z-a}{\,1-\bar a\, z/R^{2}\,},\qquad |a|<R,\
 \theta\in\mathbb R,
$$

is the general form of such a map. (The factor $2/R$ rescales the radius-$R$ dis
k to radius $2$.)

**Step 2. The image of $|z|=r$ must be the circle $|w|=1$ centered at the origin
.**

Since $f$ maps $\{|z|\le r\}$ onto $\{|w|\le 1\}$, the boundary circle $|z|=r$ i
s mapped onto $|w|=1$. A Möbius transformation maps circles to circles, so this
is consistent; the requirement is that the image circle be **centered at the ori
gin**, i.e. $|f(z)|$ is constant (equal to $1$) on $|z|=r$.

Write $z=re^{it}$ and set $u=\operatorname{Re}(\bar a\, z)$, which ranges over $
[-r|a|,\,r|a|]$ as $t$ varies. On $|z|=r$,

$$
|z-a|^{2}=r^{2}+|a|^{2}-2u,\qquad
\bigl|1-\bar a z/R^{2}\bigr|^{2}=1+\frac{|a|^{2}r^{2}}{R^{4}}-\frac{2u}{R^{2}}.
$$

Thus

$$
|f(z)|^{2}=\frac{4}{R^{2}}\cdot\frac{r^{2}+|a|^{2}-2u}{\,1+|a|^{2}r^{2}/R^{4}-2u
/R^{2}\,}.
$$

**Step 3. Constancy forces $a=0$.**

For $|f(z)|^{2}$ to be independent of $u$, the ratio of the two **linear** funct
ions of $u$ in the numerator and denominator must be constant. A ratio $L_{1}(u)
/L_{2}(u)$ of two affine functions is constant on an interval only if they are p
roportional: $L_{1}=k\,L_{2}$. Comparing coefficients of $u$:

$$
-2 = k\!\left(-\frac{2}{R^{2}}\right)\ \Longrightarrow\ k=R^{2}.
$$

Comparing constant terms:

$$
r^{2}+|a|^{2}=R^{2}\!\left(1+\frac{|a|^{2}r^{2}}{R^{4}}\right)=R^{2}+\frac{|a|^{
2}r^{2}}{R^{2}}.
$$

Rearranging:

$$
|a|^{2}\!\left(1-\frac{r^{2}}{R^{2}}\right)=R^{2}-r^{2}
\ \Longrightarrow\
|a|^{2}\cdot\frac{R^{2}-r^{2}}{R^{2}}=R^{2}-r^{2}
\ \Longrightarrow\
|a|^{2}=R^{2}.
$$

Since $r<R$, we may divide by $R^{2}-r^{2}\neq 0$, yielding $|a|=R$. But the Möb
ius form requires $|a|<R$ (otherwise the map has a pole inside the disk and is n
ot conformal on $\{|z|\le R\}$). This is a contradiction.

The proportionality argument assumed $u$ genuinely varies, i.e. $|a|>0$. The onl
y escape is $|a|=0$, i.e. **$a=0$**, in which case $u\equiv 0$ and the ratio is
trivially constant.

**Step 4. Determine the condition from $a=0$.**

With $a=0$,

$$
f(z)=\frac{2}{R}\,e^{i\theta}\,z.
$$

This maps $\{|z|\le R\}\to\{|w|\le 2\}$ as required. On $|z|=r$,

$$
|f(z)|=\frac{2r}{R}.
$$

For the image of $\{|z|\le r\}$ to be exactly $\{|w|\le 1\}$ we need $|f(z)|=1$
on $|z|=r$, i.e.

$$
\frac{2r}{R}=1\ \Longleftrightarrow\ R=2r\ \Longleftrightarrow\ r=\frac{R}{2}.
$$

**Step 5. Converse.**

When $R=2r$, the map $f(z)=\dfrac{2}{R}\,z=\dfrac{z}{r}$ is conformal, sends $\{
|z|\le R\}$ onto $\{|w|\le 2\}$, and sends $\{|z|\le r\}$ onto $\{|w|\le 1\}$. H
ence the condition is sufficient.

---

## Conclusion

The required condition is

$$
\boxed{R=2r\quad\text{(equivalently, }r=\tfrac{R}{2}\text{)}.}
$$
