## Solution

We determine all real $x$ for which
$$S(x) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} H_n \,\frac{\sin(nx)}{n}, \qquad H_n = 1+\tfrac12+\
cdots+\tfrac1n,$$
converges.

**Claim.** The series converges for **every** real number $x$.

---

### Step 1. The case $x \in 2\pi\mathbb{Z}$.

If $x = 2k\pi$, then $\sin(nx) = \sin(2kn\pi) = 0$ for every $n$, so every term
vanishes and $S(x)=0$. The series converges trivially.

### Step 2. The case $x \notin 2\pi\mathbb{Z}$ — reduction to Dirichlet's test.

We use **Dirichlet's test**: if $(a_n)$ is a monotone sequence with $a_n \to 0$,
 and the partial sums $B_N = \sum_{n=1}^{N} b_n$ are bounded, then $\sum a_n b_n
$ converges.

Set
$$a_n = \frac{H_n}{n}, \qquad b_n = \sin(nx).$$

**Boundedness of partial sums of $b_n$.** For $x \notin 2\pi\mathbb{Z}$, the geo
metric-series identity gives
$$\sum_{n=1}^{N}\sin(nx) = \frac{\sin\!\tfrac{Nx}{2}\,\sin\!\tfrac{(N+1)x}{2}}{\
sin\!\tfrac{x}{2}},$$
so
$$\left|\sum_{n=1}^{N}\sin(nx)\right| \le \frac{1}{|\sin(x/2)|},$$
a uniform bound independent of $N$.

### Step 3. The sequence $a_n = H_n/n$ is monotone decreasing and tends to $0$.

**Limit.** Since $H_n = \log n + \gamma + O(1/n)$, we have $a_n = H_n/n \sim (\l
og n)/n \to 0$.

**Monotonicity.** Compare consecutive terms:
$$\frac{H_{n+1}}{n+1} - \frac{H_n}{n} = \frac{n\,H_{n+1} - (n+1)\,H_n}{n(n+1)} =
 \frac{n\!\left(H_n + \tfrac{1}{n+1}\right) - (n+1)H_n}{n(n+1)} = \frac{\tfrac{n
}{n+1} - H_n}{n(n+1)}.$$
This is $\le 0$ iff $H_n \ge \dfrac{n}{n+1}$. Since $H_n \ge 1$ for all $n \ge 1
$ and $\dfrac{n}{n+1} < 1$, the inequality holds for every $n \ge 1$. Hence
$$\frac{H_{n+1}}{n+1} \le \frac{H_n}{n} \qquad \text{for all } n \ge 1,$$
so $(a_n)$ is (non-strictly) monotonically decreasing.

### Step 4. Conclusion via Dirichlet's test.

For $x \notin 2\pi\mathbb{Z}$:
- $a_n = H_n/n$ is monotone decreasing with $a_n \to 0$ (Step 3),
- the partial sums $\sum_{n=1}^{N}\sin(nx)$ are bounded (Step 2).

By Dirichlet's test, $S(x) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \dfrac{H_n}{n}\sin(nx)$ converg
es.

Combining with Step 1, the series converges for **every** $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

---
