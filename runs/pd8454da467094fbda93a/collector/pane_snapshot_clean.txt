$$S_b \not\equiv S_a \pmod 7 \quad \text{for all } 0\le a<b\le 7 \text{ with } b

1} = a_k$, the only way to get $S_k = S_{k-1}$ is $a_k=0$. The key $0$ creates t

$$\text{Answer} = 5 \times N,$$
