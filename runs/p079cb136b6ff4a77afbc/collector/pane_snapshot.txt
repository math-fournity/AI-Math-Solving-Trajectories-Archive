## Solution

The joint characteristic function has the form:

$$\phi_{X,Y}(t_1, t_2) = \sum_{(x,y)} P(X=x, Y=y)\, e^{i(t_1 x + t_2 y)}$$

Each term $p \cdot e^{i(t_1 a + t_2 b)}$ corresponds to a point mass at $(a, b)$
 with probability $p$. Reading off the four terms:

| Term | Point $(X, Y)$ | Probability |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| $\frac{1}{2}e^{i(t_1+t_2)}$ | $(1, 1)$ | $\frac{1}{2}$ |
| $\frac{1}{4}e^{i(2t_1+t_2)}$ | $(2, 1)$ | $\frac{1}{4}$ |
| $\frac{1}{12}e^{it_2}$ | $(0, 1)$ | $\frac{1}{12}$ |
| $\frac{1}{6}e^{i(4t_1+3t_2)}$ | $(4, 3)$ | $\frac{1}{6}$ |

**Probability check:** $\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{12} + \frac{1}{6} =
 \frac{6+3+1+2}{12} = 1$. ✓

**Evaluating $P(X < Y)$ at each support point:**

- $(1, 1)$: $X = 1 \not< Y = 1$ → contributes $0$
- $(2, 1)$: $X = 2 \not< Y = 1$ → contributes $0$
- $(0, 1)$: $X = 0 < Y = 1$ → contributes $\frac{1}{12}$
- $(4, 3)$: $X = 4 \not< Y = 3$ → contributes $0$

Therefore:

$$P(X < Y) = \frac{1}{12}$$
