# Problem

A specialized logistics company is tasked with organizing a massive shipment of crates. The total number of crates in the shipment is exactly equal to the sum of all integers from 1 to 2007 (i.e., $1 + 2 + 3 + \dots + 2007$). 

The warehouse manager needs to arrange these crates into several delivery trucks. To maintain balance and simplified inventory tracking, the manager insists on a "Consecutive Loading Protocol":
1. Each truck used must contain at least one crate.
2. Every truck must contain a positive integer number of crates.
3. When the trucks are lined up, the number of crates in each truck must form a sequence of consecutive integers (for example, if there are three trucks, they might contain 10, 11, and 12 crates respectively).

In how many different ways can the total shipment of $1 + 2 + \dots + 2007$ crates be distributed into such a sequence of trucks?
