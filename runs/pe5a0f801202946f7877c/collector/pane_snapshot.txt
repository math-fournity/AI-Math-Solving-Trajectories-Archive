$$\sum_w (-1)^{\mathrm{ME}(w)} = (a+c) - (b+d) = a + c - b - d,$$

$$2(a-b) = \sum_w (-1)^{\mathrm{ME}(w)} + \sum_w (-1)^{\mathrm{MO}(w)}.$$

$$\sum_w (-1)^{\mathrm{MO}(w)} = \sum_w (-1)^{\mathrm{MO}(\phi(w))} = \sum_w (-1

The two sums are equal, so:

$$a - b = \sum_w (-1)^{\mathrm{ME}(w)}.$$
