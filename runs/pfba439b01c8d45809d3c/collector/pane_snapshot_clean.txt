## Proof

Let $S \subset \mathbb{R}^n$ be a smooth hypersurface defined locally by $u(x) =
 C$ with $\nabla u \neq 0$. Let $\gamma_0(t) \in S$ be a geodesic. We derive the
 ODE in standard form.

### Setup and constraints

Since $\gamma_0(t) \in S$, the defining constraint holds for all $t$:
$$u(\gamma_0(t)) = C.$$

Differentiating once (chain rule):
$$\nabla u(\gamma_0(t)) \cdot \gamma_0'(t) = 0, \tag{1}$$
so the velocity is tangent to $S$.

Differentiating again:
$$\nabla u(\gamma_0) \cdot \gamma_0''(t) + \gamma_0'(t)^{\mathsf T}\, H_u(\gamma
_0(t))\, \gamma_0'(t) = 0, \tag{2}$$
where $H_u = \left(\partial^2 u / \partial x^i \partial x^j\right)$ is the Hessi
an of $u$.

### Geodesic condition

A curve in a submanifold of $\mathbb{R}^n$ is a geodesic if and only if its acce
leration is **entirely normal** to the surface, i.e. the tangential component of
 $\gamma_0''$ vanishes. Since $\nabla u \neq 0$ everywhere on $S$, the unit norm
al is $n = \nabla u / \|\nabla u\|$, and the normal direction is spanned by $\na
bla u$. Hence the geodesic condition is
$$\gamma_0''(t) = \lambda(t)\, \nabla u(\gamma_0(t)) \tag{3}$$
for some scalar function $\lambda(t)$.

### Determining $\lambda$

Substitute (3) into the second-derivative constraint (2):
$$\lambda(t)\, \|\nabla u(\gamma_0(t))\|^2 + \gamma_0'(t)^{\mathsf T}\, H_u(\gam
ma_0(t))\, \gamma_0'(t) = 0,$$
giving
$$\lambda(t) = -\,\frac{\gamma_0'(t)^{\mathsf T}\, H_u(\gamma_0(t))\, \gamma_0'(
t)}{\|\nabla u(\gamma_0(t))\|^2}. \tag{4}$$

### Standard-form ODE

Substituting (4) back into (3), the geodesic equation is
$$\boxed{\;\gamma_0''(t) \;=\; -\,\frac{\gamma_0'(t)^{\mathsf T}\, H_u(\gamma_0(
t))\, \gamma_0'(t)}{\|\nabla u(\gamma_0(t))\|^2}\;\nabla u(\gamma_0(t))\;} \tag{
5}$$

In component form, writing $\gamma_0(t) = (x^1(t),\dots,x^n(t))$:
$$\frac{d^2 x^i}{dt^2} \;=\; -\,\frac{\displaystyle\sum_{j,k=1}^{n} \frac{\parti
al^2 u}{\partial x^j \partial x^k}(\gamma_0)\,\frac{dx^j}{dt}\,\frac{dx^k}{dt}}{
\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{n}\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial x^j}(\gamma_0)\right
)^2}\;\frac{\partial u}{\partial x^i}(\gamma_0), \qquad i = 1,\dots,n. \tag{6}$$

### Verification

This is a system of $n$ second-order ODEs in standard form $\ddot x = F(x,\dot x
)$. The right-hand side is smooth wherever $\nabla u \neq 0$, so local existence
 and uniqueness hold. Any solution of (5)–(6) with initial data satisfying the c
onstraint (1) at $t=0$ (i.e. $\nabla u(\gamma_0(0))\cdot \gamma_0'(0)=0$) remain
s on $S$: differentiating $\nabla u \cdot \gamma_0'$ along a solution of (5) and
 using (2) shows $\frac{d}{dt}(\nabla u \cdot \gamma_0') = 0$, so the constraint
 $\nabla u \cdot \gamma_0' = 0$ is preserved, and consequently $u(\gamma_0(t))$
is constant, keeping $\gamma_0$ in $S$.

Equation (5) [equivalently (6)] is the desired ODE in standard form for a geodes
ic of the hypersurface $S$.
