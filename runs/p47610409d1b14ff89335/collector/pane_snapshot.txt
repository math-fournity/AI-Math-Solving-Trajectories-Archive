**A useful bound on $n!$.** For every $n \geq 1$,
$$n! = 1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 \cdots n \geq 2^{n-1},$$
because each factor $2, 3, \dots, n$ is $\geq 2$ (there are $n-1$ such factors f

**Bounding the tail.** For $n \geq 2$ we have $n! \geq 2^{n-1} \geq 2$, so $(n!)
^v \geq (2^{n-1})^v = 2^{v(n-1)}$. Hence
$$\frac{\lambda^n}{(n!)^v} \leq \frac{\lambda^n}{2^{v(n-1)}} = \lambda \cdot \le
ft(\frac{\lambda}{2^v}\right)^{n-1}.$$

1)$. Then summing the geometric series,
$$0 \leq R(v) \leq \sum_{n=2}^{\infty} \lambda \, q_v^{\,n-1} = \lambda \sum_{k=
1}^{\infty} q_v^{\,k} = \lambda \cdot \frac{q_v}{1 - q_v} = \lambda \cdot \frac{
\lambda/2^v}{1 - \lambda/2^v} = \frac{\lambda^2 / 2^v}{1 - \lambda/2^v}.$$

$$\lim_{v \to +\infty} R(v) = 0.$$
