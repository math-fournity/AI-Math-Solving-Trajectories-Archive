**Step 1: Embedding $D(X) \hookrightarrow D(\mathbb{C}(X))$.**

Every differential operator on $\mathbb{C}[X]$ extends uniquely to a differentia

**Step 2: $d$ kills $T^\#(\mathbb{C}[Y])$ implies $d$ kills $\mathbb{C}(Y) = \op
eratorname{Frac}(T^\#(\mathbb{C}[Y]))$.**

We prove by induction on the order $k$ of $d$ that if $d$ kills a subalgebra $A
\subset \mathbb{C}(X)$ (with $1 \in A$), then $d$ kills $\operatorname{Frac}(A)$
.

- **Base case $k=0$:** $d$ is multiplication by $c \in \mathbb{C}(X)$. Since $1
\in A$ and $d(1)=c=0$, we get $d=0$.

- **Inductive step ($k \geq 1$):** For any $b \in A$, the commutator $[d,b] = db
 - bd$ has order $\leq k-1$. For $a \in A$: $[d,b](a) = d(ba) - b\cdot d(a) = 0
- 0 = 0$ (since $ba \in A$ and $d$ kills $A$). By induction, $[d,b]$ kills $\ope
ratorname{Frac}(A)$.

  Now for $a/b \in \operatorname{Frac}(A)$ with $a,b \in A$, $b \neq 0$: since $
d(1)=0$ and $1 = b \cdot b^{-1}$,
  $$0 = d(b \cdot b^{-1}) = b \cdot d(b^{-1}) + [d,b](b^{-1}) = b \cdot d(b^{-1}
) + 0,$$
  so $d(b^{-1}) = 0$. Then
  $$d(a/b) = d(a \cdot b^{-1}) = a \cdot d(b^{-1}) + [d,a](b^{-1}) = 0 + 0 = 0.$
$

Applying this with $A = T^\#(\mathbb{C}[Y])$, we conclude $d$ kills $\mathbb{C}(
Y)$.

**Step 3: $d$ kills $\mathbb{C}(Y)$ implies $d = 0$.**

We prove by induction on the order $k$ that any $d \in D(\mathbb{C}(X))$ killing
 $\mathbb{C}(Y)$ must be zero.

- **Base case $k=0$:** $d$ is multiplication by $c$; $d(1)=c=0$, so $d=0$.

- **Inductive step ($k \geq 1$):** For any $b \in \mathbb{C}(Y)$, the commutator

  Therefore $d$ commutes with every element of $\mathbb{C}(Y)$, i.e., $d$ is $\m
athbb{C}(Y)$-linear: $d \in D(\mathbb{C}(X)/\mathbb{C}(Y))$.

(separable $\Rightarrow$ $\Omega_{\mathbb{C}(X)/\mathbb{C}(Y)}=0$). Consequently

tration stabilizes at order $0$ since there are no relative derivations to gener

  Thus $d$ is multiplication by some $c \in \mathbb{C}(X)$. Since $d$ kills $\ma
thbb{C}(Y)$ and $1 \in \mathbb{C}(Y)$, we get $d(1) = c = 0$, hence $d = 0$.

**Conclusion.** Since $d = 0$ in $D(\mathbb{C}(X))$ and the embedding $D(X) \hoo
