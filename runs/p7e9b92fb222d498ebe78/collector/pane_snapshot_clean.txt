## Setting Up the Graph Structure

Let the row heights be $r_1, \dots, r_m$ and column widths be $c_1, \dots, c_n$.
 Cell $(i,j)$ has area $r_i c_j$, and the total area is $(\sum r_i)(\sum c_j)$.

**Model as a bipartite graph**: Create a bipartite graph with $m$ row-vertices a
nd $n$ column-vertices. Each chosen cell $(i,j)$ becomes an edge. We're given th
e products $r_i c_j$ on each edge.

## When is the Total Area Determined?

**Connected case (tree)**: If the bipartite graph on $m+n$ vertices with $m+n-1$
 edges is connected, it's a spanning tree. Set $r_1 = t$. BFS through the tree:
each edge $(i,j)$ with known $r_i$ gives $c_j = (r_i c_j)/t$; each edge with kno
wn $c_j$ gives $r_i = (r_i c_j)/c_j$. Every $r_i = a_i t$ and $c_j = b_j/t$ for
constants $a_i, b_j$ determined by the edge values. The total area becomes:
$$(\textstyle\sum a_i t)\left(\textstyle\sum b_j/t\right) = \left(\textstyle\sum
 a_i\right)\left(\textstyle\sum b_j\right)$$
**independent of $t$** — so the total area is determined.

**Disconnected case**: With $k \geq 2$ components, each component $C_\ell$ has i
ts own scaling parameter $\lambda_\ell$. The total area becomes:
$$\left(\textstyle\sum_\ell \lambda_\ell A_\ell\right)\left(\textstyle\sum_\ell
B_\ell/\lambda_\ell\right) = \sum_\ell A_\ell B_\ell + \sum_{\ell \neq \ell'} A_
\ell B_{\ell'} \frac{\lambda_\ell}{\lambda_{\ell'}}$$
which varies with the $\lambda_\ell$ — so the total area is **not** determined.

**Conclusion**: $C_{m,n}$ equals the number of spanning trees of $K_{m,n}$, whic
h by the Matrix-Tree Theorem is:
$$C_{m,n} = m^{n-1}\, n^{m-1}$$

## Computing the Sum

Substituting into $A$ with $s = m+n$:
$$A = \sum_{s=2}^{\infty} \frac{1}{s^s} \sum_{m=1}^{s-1} \binom{s}{m}\, m^{s-m-1
}(s-m)^{m-1}$$

**Key Identity**: I claim $f(s) := \sum_{m=1}^{s-1} \binom{s}{m}\, m^{s-m-1}(s-m
)^{m-1} = 2s^{s-2}$.

*Combinatorial proof*: Every labeled tree on $[s]$ is bipartite (all trees are 2
-colorable), and for $s \geq 2$, each has exactly **2** proper 2-colorings. The
LHS counts all (labeled tree, 2-coloring) pairs by partitioning $[s]$ into color

rees of the resulting complete bipartite graph ($m^{s-m-1}(s-m)^{m-1}$ ways). Th
e RHS is $2 \times s^{s-2}$, i.e., twice the number of labeled trees.

*Verification*: $f(2)=2=2\cdot1$, $f(3)=6=2\cdot3$, $f(4)=32=2\cdot16$, $f(5)=25
0=2\cdot125$, $f(6)=2592=2\cdot1296$. ✓

## Evaluating $A$

$$A = \sum_{s=2}^{\infty} \frac{2s^{s-2}}{s^s} = \sum_{s=2}^{\infty} \frac{2}{s^
2} = 2\left(\frac{\pi^2}{6} - 1\right) = \frac{\pi^2}{3} - 2$$
