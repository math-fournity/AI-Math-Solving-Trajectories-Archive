### Step 1. Characters of $C(K)$ are exactly the evaluation functionals.

For $x\in K$ define $\mathrm{ev}_x:C(K)\to\mathbb C$ by $\mathrm{ev}_x(f)=f(x)$.

### Step 3. Topology: the two pieces are clopen and each is homeomorphic to its

The weak-$*$ topology on $\widehat A$ is the topology of pointwise convergence o
