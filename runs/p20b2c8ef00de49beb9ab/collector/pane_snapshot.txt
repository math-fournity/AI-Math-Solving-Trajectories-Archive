## Proof

**Claim:** There exists a cardinal number $\kappa$ such that $|\kappa \times \ka
ppa| \neq \kappa$.

**Witness:** $\kappa = 2$.

**Verification.** The cardinal $2 = \{0, 1\}$. Its Cartesian square is
$$2 \times 2 = \{(0,0),\; (0,1),\; (1,0),\; (1,1)\},$$
which has exactly $4$ elements. Since $4 \neq 2$, we have
$$|\kappa \times \kappa| = 4 \neq 2 = \kappa.$$

More generally, for every **finite** cardinal $n \geq 2$,
$$|n \times n| = n^2 > n,$$
so $|n \times n| \neq n$. Thus there are infinitely many such cardinals (every f
inite $n \geq 2$).

### Remark (the infinite case, for completeness)

The situation for **infinite** cardinals is fundamentally different. Assuming th
e Axiom of Choice (ZFC), one has the classical theorem:

> **Theorem (Hessenberg–Tarski).** For every infinite cardinal $\kappa$, $|\kapp
a \times \kappa| = \kappa$.

*Proof sketch.* By transfinite induction on $\kappa$.

- **Base case $\kappa = \aleph_0$:** Cantor's pairing function $\pi(m,n) = \frac
{(m+n)(m+n+1)}{2} + n$ is a bijection $\omega \times \omega \to \omega$, giving
$|\aleph_0 \times \aleph_0| = \aleph_0$.

- **Successor case $\kappa = \lambda^+$:** Using AC, well-order $\kappa$. For ea
ch $\alpha < \kappa$, the initial segment $|\alpha| \leq \lambda$, so by inducti
on $|\alpha \times \alpha| = |\alpha| \leq \lambda$. One constructs an injection
 $\kappa \times \kappa \hookrightarrow \kappa$ by assigning to each pair $(\alph
a, \beta)$ a rank bounded using the inductive hypothesis on $\max(\alpha,\beta)+
1$, whose cardinality is $<\kappa$. This gives $|\kappa \times \kappa| \leq \kap
pa$. The reverse inequality $|\kappa \times \kappa| \geq \kappa$ is immediate (f
ix one coordinate). Hence equality.

- **Limit case:** Follows by taking suprema over smaller cardinals, each of whic
h satisfies the induction hypothesis.

Thus, under ZFC, the cardinals $\kappa$ with $|\kappa \times \kappa| \neq \kappa
$ are **exactly** the finite cardinals $n \geq 2$.

Yes, such a cardinal exists. The cardinal $\kappa = 2$ satisfies $|\kappa \times
