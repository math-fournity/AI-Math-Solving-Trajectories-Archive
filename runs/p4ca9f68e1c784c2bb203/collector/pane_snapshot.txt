## Solution

**Key identity.** For any $\theta$,
$$1 + 2\cos\theta = \frac{\sin(3\theta/2)}{\sin(\theta/2)}.$$
*Proof.* Using $\sin 3x = 3\sin x - 4\sin^3 x$ with $x = \theta/2$:
$$\frac{\sin(3\theta/2)}{\sin(\theta/2)} = 3 - 4\sin^2(\theta/2) = 3 - 2(1-\cos\
theta) = 1 + 2\cos\theta. \quad\checkmark$$

**Apply with $\theta = \pi/3^n$.** We get
$$1 + 2\cos\!\left(\frac{\pi}{3^n}\right) = \frac{\sin\!\bigl(\frac{\pi}{2\cdot
3^{n-1}}\bigr)}{\sin\!\bigl(\frac{\pi}{2\cdot 3^{n}}\bigr)},$$
since $\frac{3}{2}\cdot\frac{\pi}{3^n} = \frac{\pi}{2\cdot 3^{n-1}}$. Therefore
$$a_n := \frac{3}{1+2\cos(\pi/3^n)} = \frac{3\,\sin\!\bigl(\frac{\pi}{2\cdot 3^{
n}}\bigr)}{\sin\!\bigl(\frac{\pi}{2\cdot 3^{n-1}}\bigr)}.$$

**Telescoping.** For the partial product,
$$P_N = \prod_{n=1}^{N} a_n = 3^{N}\prod_{n=1}^{N}\frac{\sin\!\bigl(\frac{\pi}{2
\cdot 3^{n}}\bigr)}{\sin\!\bigl(\frac{\pi}{2\cdot 3^{n-1}}\bigr)}.$$
The sine factors telescope (the denominator of term $n$ is the numerator of term
 $n-1$):
$$\prod_{n=1}^{N}\frac{\sin\!\bigl(\frac{\pi}{2\cdot 3^{n}}\bigr)}{\sin\!\bigl(\
frac{\pi}{2\cdot 3^{n-1}}\bigr)} = \frac{\sin\!\bigl(\frac{\pi}{2\cdot 3^{N}}\bi
gr)}{\sin(\pi/2)} = \sin\!\left(\frac{\pi}{2\cdot 3^{N}}\right).$$
Hence
$$P_N = 3^{N}\sin\!\left(\frac{\pi}{2\cdot 3^{N}}\right).$$

**Limit.** Let $t_N = \frac{\pi}{2\cdot 3^{N}}\to 0$. Then $3^{N} = \frac{\pi}{2
\,t_N}$, so
$$P_N = \frac{\pi}{2\,t_N}\sin t_N = \frac{\pi}{2}\cdot\frac{\sin t_N}{t_N}\xrig
htarrow[N\to\infty]{}\frac{\pi}{2}\cdot 1 = \frac{\pi}{2}.$$

**Conclusion.**
$$\prod_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{3}{1+2\cos(\pi/3^{n})} = \frac{\pi}{2}\approx 1.5708
.$$

The integral part is $\left\lfloor \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right\rfloor = \boxed{1}$.
