# Problem

To maintain legal operational standards, the company must distribute its cargo into two types of containers: Type-A heavy units and Type-B light units. The quantities of these units, represented by $a$ and $b$, must be positive integers.

The company’s shipping protocols require the following:
1. On the North-Bound line, the combined weight of one Type-A unit and 13 Type-B units must be exactly divisible by 11 tons to balance the freighter.

The manager wants to minimize the total number of containers used for these two specific batches. What is the least possible value for the total number of units, $a + b$?
