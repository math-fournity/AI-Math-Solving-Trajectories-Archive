# Problem

A network of 2010 logistics hubs, numbered 1 through 2010, is to be connected by a single delivery route. A driver must plan a route $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_{2010}$ that visits every hub exactly once, adhering to the following regulations:

1. The journey must always begin at Hub 1 ($a_1 = 1$).
2. For every consecutive pair of hubs in the route, the numerical difference between their labels must be no more than 2 (i.e., $|a_i - a_{i+1}| \leq 2$ for all $i = 1, 2, \dots, 2009$).

Let $f(2010)$ represent the total number of unique valid routes that satisfy these conditions. Determine the remainder when $f(2010)$ is divided by 3.
