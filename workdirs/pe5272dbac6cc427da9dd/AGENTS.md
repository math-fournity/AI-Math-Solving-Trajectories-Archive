# Problem

A specialized telecommunications company is designing a regional network involving $n$ distinct cities, where $n \geq 4$. The engineers plan to establish direct fiber-optic links between every possible pair of cities in the region. 

Due to technical limitations in the signal hardware, the project manager identifies a specific category of "clear-run" links. A link is classified as a "clear-run" if its path across the map does not cross any other fiber-optic link, except at the specific cities where the links originate or terminate. 

Under the assumption that the cities are positioned in the most advantageous geographic arrangement possible, what is the maximum number of "clear-run" links that can exist within this network?
