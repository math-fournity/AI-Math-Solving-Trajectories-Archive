### Setup and definitions

Let $(M,g)$ be a smooth Riemannian manifold with Levi-Civita connection $\nabla$
. The connection $\nabla$ extends canonically from $TM$ to the tensor bundle $\t
au^h_k(M) = (\otimes^h TM) \otimes (\otimes^k T^*M)$ by requiring compatibility
with tensor products and contractions:
$$\nabla_X(S \otimes T) = (\nabla_X S)\otimes T + S \otimes (\nabla_X T), \qquad
 \nabla_X(\mathrm{tr}\, S) = \mathrm{tr}(\nabla_X S).$$

The **bundle metric** $\tilde g$ on $\tau^h_k(M)$ is the fiberwise inner product
 induced by $g$. In local coordinates, for two $(h,k)$-tensors $T, S$:
$$\tilde g(T, S) \;=\; g_{i_1 j_1}\cdots g_{i_h j_h}\, g^{a_1 b_1}\cdots g^{a_k
b_k}\, T^{i_1\cdots i_h}{}_{a_1\cdots a_k}\, S^{j_1\cdots j_h}{}_{b_1\cdots b_k}
.$$

Equivalently, $\tilde g$ is obtained from $g$ (the metric on $TM$) and $g^{-1}$
(the dual metric on $T^*M$) by taking tensor products and contracting covariant
with contravariant slots. This is a canonical construction: $\tilde g = (\otimes
^h g)\otimes(\otimes^k g^{-1})$ followed by the natural pairing between the $h$
covariant and $h$ contravariant indices of the two tensor arguments, and likewis
e for the $k$ slots.

Parallel transport $\mathcal{P}^{\sigma}_{\tau^h_k(M)}$ is defined by the parall

### Key step: $\nabla \tilde g = 0$ on the tensor bundle

The Levi-Civita connection is **metric compatible**: $\nabla g = 0$. Differentia
ting the identity $g_{ia}g^{aj} = \delta_i^j$ gives
$$0 = \nabla_X(g_{ia}\,g^{aj}) = (\nabla_X g)_{ia}\,g^{aj} + g_{ia}\,(\nabla_X g
^{-1})^{aj} = g_{ia}\,(\nabla_X g^{-1})^{aj},$$
so $(\nabla_X g^{-1})^{aj} = 0$ for all $X$; hence $\nabla g^{-1} = 0$.

Now $\tilde g$ is built from $g$ and $g^{-1}$ by tensor products and contraction
s. Since the induced connection on $\tau^h_k(M)$ satisfies the Leibniz rule for
tensor products and commutes with contractions, and since $\nabla g = 0$ and $\n
abla g^{-1} = 0$, the Leibniz rule gives
$$\nabla_X \tilde g \;=\; 0 \qquad \text{for every vector field } X.$$

Concretely: $\tilde g$ is a linear combination (with structure constants given b
y index pairings) of tensor products of copies of $g$ and $g^{-1}$; each factor
is parallel, so the whole expression is parallel. Thus **$\tilde g$ is a paralle
l section of the bundle $\tau^h_k(M)^* \otimes \tau^h_k(M)^*$**.

### Parallel transport preserves $\tilde g$

Let $u, v \in (\tau^h_k)_{\sigma(0)}(M)$. Let $U(t) = \mathcal{P}^{\sigma|_{[0,t
]}}_{\tau^h_k(M)}(u)$ and $V(t) = \mathcal{P}^{\sigma|_{[0,t]}}_{\tau^h_k(M)}(v)

$$\nabla_{\dot\sigma} U = 0, \qquad \nabla_{\dot\sigma} V = 0.$$

Consider the scalar function $f(t) = \tilde g_{\sigma(t)}(U(t), V(t))$ along the
 curve. Differentiating using the compatibility of $\nabla$ with $\tilde g$ (the
 Leibniz rule for the connection on the relevant bundle):
$$\frac{d}{dt} f(t) \;=\; \frac{d}{dt}\,\tilde g_{\sigma(t)}(U(t), V(t)) \;=\; (
\nabla_{\dot\sigma}\tilde g)(U, V) \;+\; \tilde g(\nabla_{\dot\sigma} U,\, V) \;
+\; \tilde g(U,\, \nabla_{\dot\sigma} V).$$

Each term vanishes:
- $(\nabla_{\dot\sigma}\tilde g)(U,V) = 0$ because $\nabla\tilde g = 0$ (establi
shed above),
- $\tilde g(\nabla_{\dot\sigma} U, V) = 0$ because $\nabla_{\dot\sigma} U = 0$,
- $\tilde g(U, \nabla_{\dot\sigma} V) = 0$ because $\nabla_{\dot\sigma} V = 0$.

Therefore $\frac{d}{dt}f(t) = 0$, so $f$ is constant on $[0,1]$. Evaluating at t

$$\tilde g_{\sigma(0)}(u, v) \;=\; f(0) \;=\; f(1) \;=\; \tilde g_{\sigma(1)}\!\
bigl(\mathcal{P}^{\sigma}_{\tau^h_k(M)}(u),\; \mathcal{P}^{\sigma}_{\tau^h_k(M)}
(v)\bigr).$$
