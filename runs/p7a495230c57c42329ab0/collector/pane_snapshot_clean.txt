## Setting Up the Framework

Each person $P_k$ always has exactly 2 cards. I'll track the **larger card** $b_
k(t)$ (the "kept" card) and the **smaller card** $a_k(t)$ (the "passed" card) at
 position $k$ after step $t$.

**Initial state:** $a_k(0) = 2k-1$, $b_k(0) = 2k$ for $k = 1, \ldots, n$.

**Recurrence:** At each step, each person passes their smaller card right and ke
eps their larger card, then receives the smaller card from their left neighbor:
$$b_k(t+1) = \max(b_k(t),\, a_{k-1}(t)), \qquad a_k(t+1) = \min(b_k(t),\, a_{k-1
}(t))$$
(indices mod $n$, so $a_0 = a_n$).

**Key observation:** $b_k(t)$ is **non-decreasing** and bounded by $2n$, so it s
tabilizes. $S(n)$ is the step when all $b_k$ have stabilized — at that point, $n
$ cards (the $b_k$ values) remain with the same people forever.

## Identifying the Final State

Once all $b_k$ stabilize, the $a_k$ values simply rotate right each step (each v
isits every position). Therefore each $b_k$ must exceed **all** moving cards. Th
is forces the fixed cards to be $\{n+1, \ldots, 2n\}$ and the moving cards to be
 $\{1, \ldots, n\}$.

## Tracing the Capture Process

The "moving" cards travel right one position per step. When a moving card arrive
s at a position where $b$ is smaller, it gets **captured** (becomes the new $b$)
, displacing the old $b$ as a new moving card.

**Card $2n-1$** (the largest odd card) starts at position $n$. In step 1 it move

**Card $2n-3$** starts at position $n-1$. It takes 2 steps to reach position $n$

**General pattern:** Card $2n - 2k + 1$ starts at position $n - k + 1$. It trave

**Capture condition:** $2n - 2k + 1 > 2k \iff k < \frac{2n+1}{4}$. Since $\frac{

$$k^* = \left\lfloor \frac{2n+1}{4} \right\rfloor$$

## Verifying Stability After the Last Capture

After step $2k^* - 1$, the $b$ values are:

n$)
