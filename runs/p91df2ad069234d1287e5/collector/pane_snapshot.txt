## Solution

**Setup and key observation.** At $t=0$ both the Pigeon and Burrito are at $(1,1
)$. The process consists of 10 scrub events, each taking 1 minute, interleaved w
ith 9 "chase" periods. After each scrub the Burrito is rerouted uniformly at ran
dom to one of the 100 grid nodes, while the Pigeon remains at the scrub location
.

**Travel time equals Manhattan distance.** During a chase, the Burrito is statio
nary and the Pigeon moves each minute to an adjacent node that reduces the Manha
ttan distance. Any such move reduces the Manhattan distance by *exactly* 1 (adja
cent nodes differ by 1 in Manhattan distance, and a "reducing" move decreases it
). As long as the distance is positive, a reducing move always exists (move towa
rd the Burrito along whichever coordinate differs). Therefore the chase takes **
exactly** $d$ minutes, where $d$ is the initial Manhattan distance — the random
tie-breaking is irrelevant to the count.

**Indexing the reroute locations.** Let $R_0 = (1,1)$ (where scrub 1 occurs). Af
ter scrub $i$, the Burrito is rerouted to $R_i$, drawn uniformly at random over
the $10\times 10$ grid, independent of all history. The Pigeon then chases from
$R_{i-1}$ to $R_i$, taking $T_i = \text{ManhattanDist}(R_{i-1}, R_i)$ minutes, a
nd scrub $i+1$ occurs at $R_i$.

The total time is:
$$x = \underbrace{10}_{\text{scrubs}} + \sum_{i=1}^{9} T_i.$$

**Computing $E[T_1]$.** Here $R_0=(1,1)$ is fixed and $R_1=(a,b)$ is uniform on
$\{1,\dots,10\}^2$:
$$T_1 = (a-1)+(b-1), \qquad E[T_1] = E[a]+E[b]-2 = 5.5+5.5-2 = 9.$$

**Computing $E[T_i]$ for $i\ge 2$.** Now $R_{i-1},R_i$ are independent uniform o
n $\{1,\dots,10\}^2$. Writing $R=(a,b)$, $R'=(a',b')$:
$$E[\text{ManhattanDist}(R,R')] = E[|a-a'|] + E[|b-b'|] = 2\,E[|a-a'|],$$
by symmetry. For $a,a'$ i.i.d. uniform on $\{1,\dots,10\}$:
$$E[|a-a'|] = \frac{2}{100}\sum_{d=1}^{9} d\,(10-d) = \frac{2}{100}\cdot 165 = 3
.3,$$
since $\sum_{d=1}^{9} d(10-d) = 10\cdot 45 - 285 = 165$.

Thus $E[T_i] = 2 \cdot 3.3 = 6.6$ for $i = 2,\dots,9$.

**Total expected time.**
$$x = 10 + E[T_1] + 8\cdot E[T_2] = 10 + 9 + 8 \cdot 6.6 = 10 + 9 + 52.8 = 71.8.
$$

**Final answer:**
$$\lfloor 10^1 \cdot x \rfloor = \lfloor 718 \rfloor = \boxed{718}.$$
