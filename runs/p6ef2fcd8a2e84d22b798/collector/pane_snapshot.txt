## Solution

**Setup.** Let the circumscribed circle have radius $R$. The five sides subtend
central angles. Since four sides have length 16 and one has length 6, let each s
ide of 16 subtend central angle $\alpha$ and the side of 6 subtend central angle
 $\beta$, with
$$4\alpha + \beta = 2\pi.$$
Setting $x = \alpha/2$ and $y = \beta/2$, we get $4x + y = \pi$, i.e., $y = \pi
- 4x$.

**Finding $R$ and the angles.** A chord of length $s = 2R\sin(\theta/2)$ where $
\theta$ is the central angle, so:
$$16 = 2R\sin x, \qquad 6 = 2R\sin y = 2R\sin(\pi - 4x) = 2R\sin(4x).$$
Thus $R = \frac{8}{\sin x} = \frac{3}{\sin 4x}$, giving $8\sin(4x) = 3\sin x$. U
sing $\sin(4x) = 4\sin x\cos x\cos(2x)$:
$$32\cos x\cos(2x) = 3.$$
Let $c = \cos x$, $\cos(2x) = 2c^2 - 1$:
$$64c^3 - 32c - 3 = 0 = (4c - 3)(16c^2 + 12c + 1).$$
The quadratic $16c^2 + 12c + 1 = 0$ gives $c = \frac{-3 \pm \sqrt{5}}{8} < 0$, w
hich would force $x > \pi/2$ and $\alpha > \pi$, impossible for a convex cyclic
pentagon. Hence $c = \cos x = \frac{3}{4}$, $\sin x = \frac{\sqrt{7}}{4}$, and
$$R = \frac{8}{\sqrt{7}/4} = \frac{32}{\sqrt{7}}, \qquad R^2 = \frac{1024}{7}.$$

**Computing the needed sines.**
$$\sin\alpha = \sin(2x) = 2\cdot\tfrac{3}{4}\cdot\tfrac{\sqrt{7}}{4} = \tfrac{3\
sqrt{7}}{8}, \qquad \cos(2x) = 2\cdot\tfrac{9}{16}-1 = \tfrac{1}{8}.$$
For $\beta = 2\pi - 4\alpha$, $\sin\beta = -\sin(4\alpha) = -\sin(8x)$. Now
$$\sin(4x) = \tfrac{3\sqrt{7}}{32}, \quad \cos(4x) = 2\cos^2(2x)-1 = \tfrac{2}{6
4}-1 = -\tfrac{31}{32},$$
$$\sin(8x) = 2\sin(4x)\cos(4x) = 2\cdot\tfrac{3\sqrt{7}}{32}\cdot\bigl(-\tfrac{3
1}{32}\bigr) = -\tfrac{93\sqrt{7}}{512}.$$
Therefore $\sin\beta = \frac{93\sqrt{7}}{512}$.

**Area.** The area of a cyclic polygon equals the sum of the five isosceles tria
ngles from the center:
$$A = \tfrac{1}{2}R^2\bigl(4\sin\alpha + \sin\beta\bigr) = \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\tfr
ac{1024}{7}\Bigl(\tfrac{3\sqrt{7}}{2} + \tfrac{93\sqrt{7}}{512}\Bigr).$$
Combining:
$$\tfrac{3\sqrt{7}}{2} + \tfrac{93\sqrt{7}}{512} = \sqrt{7}\Bigl(\tfrac{768}{512
} + \tfrac{93}{512}\Bigr) = \tfrac{861\sqrt{7}}{512}.$$
$$A = \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\tfrac{1024}{7}\cdot\tfrac{861\sqrt{7}}{512} = \frac{1024
\cdot 861\sqrt{7}}{2\cdot 7\cdot 512} = \frac{2\cdot 861\sqrt{7}}{2\cdot 7} = \f
rac{861\sqrt{7}}{7} = 123\sqrt{7}.$$

**Numerical check.** $R \approx 12.10$, $\alpha \approx 82.8°$, $\beta \approx 2
8.7°$. Area $\approx 0.5 \times 146.39 \times (4\times0.9922 + 0.4807) \approx 3
25.4$, and $123\sqrt{7} \approx 325.4$. ✓

The area of the pentagon is $\boxed{123\sqrt{7}}$.
