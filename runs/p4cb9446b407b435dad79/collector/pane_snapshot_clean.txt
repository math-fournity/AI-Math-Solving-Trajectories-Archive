## Solution

Let $a = \sqrt{10}-\sqrt{2}$ and $b = \sqrt{10}+\sqrt{2}$. Note that

$$ab = (\sqrt{10})^2 - (\sqrt{2})^2 = 10 - 2 = 8.$$

Rewrite the expression by splitting the exponent on $b$:

$$a^{1/3}\, b^{7/3} = a^{1/3}\, b^{1/3}\, b^{6/3} = (ab)^{1/3}\, b^{2}.$$

Since $ab = 8$, we have $(ab)^{1/3} = 8^{1/3} = 2$, so the expression equals

$$2\,b^2 = 2(\sqrt{10}+\sqrt{2})^2.$$

Expanding,

$$(\sqrt{10}+\sqrt{2})^2 = 10 + 2\sqrt{10}\sqrt{2} + 2 = 12 + 2\sqrt{20} = 12 +
4\sqrt{5}.$$

Therefore

$$a^{1/3}\, b^{7/3} = 2(12 + 4\sqrt{5}) = 24 + 8\sqrt{5}.$$
