# Problem

The station’s automated alarm system is triggered whenever the stability of the permafrost satisfies a specific energy imbalance. This imbalance is defined by the following industrial safety inequality:

Eight times the quantity of three raised to the power of (the square root of the saturation index plus the fourth root of the saturation index), plus nine times the fourth root of the saturation index, plus one, is greater than or equal to nine times the square root of the saturation index.

The range of saturation levels ($x$) for which this safety inequality holds true is restricted to a specific closed interval of real numbers, $[a, b]$. 

Determine the value of $b - a$.
