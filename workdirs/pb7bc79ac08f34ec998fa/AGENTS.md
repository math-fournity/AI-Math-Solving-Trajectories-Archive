# Problem

In a specialized robotic efficiency assessment, a drone named Unit-Z achieved a performance rating $S$ higher than 80. The drone’s technician, Jiang, was informed of the exact rating $S$ and was immediately able to determine the unique combination of tasks completed and errors made to produce that result. 

Another drone, Unit-M, achieved a performance rating that was slightly lower than Unit-Z’s, yet still strictly above 80. However, when Jiang was informed of Unit-M’s rating, he found it impossible to deduce the exact number of tasks completed, as multiple combinations of task outcomes could result in that same rating.

The assessment consists of a maximum of 30 tasks. The rating formula is $S = 30 + 4c - w$, where $c$ is the number of tasks completed correctly, $w$ is the number of tasks where an error occurred, and any tasks not attempted result in 0 points. What was the performance rating $S$ achieved by Unit-M?
