## Solution

We evaluate $\int_C (x+2y)\,dx + (2z+2x)\,dy + (z+y)\,dz$ using Stokes' theorem,
 where $C$ is the intersection of the cylinder $x^2+y^2=2y$ and the plane $y=z$,
 oriented counterclockwise viewed from above.

### Step 1: Compute the curl

Let $\vec{F} = (P,Q,R) = (x+2y,\; 2z+2x,\; z+y)$.

$$\nabla\times\vec{F} = \left(R_y - Q_z,\; P_z - R_x,\; Q_x - P_y\right)$$

- $R_y - Q_z = \partial_y(z+y) - \partial_z(2z+2x) = 1 - 2 = -1$
- $P_z - R_x = \partial_z(x+2y) - \partial_x(z+y) = 0 - 0 = 0$
- $Q_x - P_y = \partial_x(2z+2x) - \partial_y(x+2y) = 2 - 2 = 0$

$$\nabla\times\vec{F} = (-1,\, 0,\, 0)$$

### Step 2: Choose the surface and its normal

Take $S$ to be the portion of the plane $y = z$ bounded by $C$ (inside the cylin
der $x^2+(y-1)^2=1$).

The plane $y - z = 0$ has normal $\nabla(y-z) = (0,1,-1)$. The two unit normals
are $\pm(0,1,-1)/\sqrt{2}$.

**Orientation check:** The curve $C$ is counterclockwise when viewed from above
(from $+z$ looking down). By the right-hand rule, the induced normal must point
upward, i.e., have positive $z$-component. The normal $(0,-1,1)/\sqrt{2}$ has $z
$-component $+1/\sqrt{2} > 0$, so

$$\hat{n} = \frac{(0,\,-1,\,1)}{\sqrt{2}}.$$

### Step 3: Apply Stokes' theorem

$$\int_C \vec{F}\cdot d\vec{r} = \iint_S (\nabla\times\vec{F})\cdot\hat{n}\,dS$$

$$= \iint_S (-1,\,0,\,0)\cdot\frac{(0,\,-1,\,1)}{\sqrt{2}}\,dS = \iint_S 0\,dS =
 0.$$

### Step 4: Verification by direct parametrization

Parametrize $C$ (on $y=z$, inside $x^2+(y-1)^2=1$):
$$x=\cos t,\quad y=1+\sin t,\quad z=1+\sin t,\quad t\in[0,2\pi].$$
This traverses the projected circle counterclockwise. With $dx=-\sin t\,dt$, $dy
=dz=\cos t\,dt$:

$$P=\cos t+2+2\sin t,\quad Q=2+2\sin t+2\cos t,\quad R=2+2\sin t.$$

The integrand becomes
$$3\sin t\cos t - 2\sin t - 2\sin^2 t + 4\cos t + 2\cos^2 t.$$

Integrating over $[0,2\pi]$: the $\sin t$, $\cos t$, and $\sin t\cos t$ terms va
nish; $-2\sin^2 t$ contributes $-2\pi$ and $2\cos^2 t$ contributes $+2\pi$. The
total is $0$, confirming the result.

### Answer

$$\boxed{\int_C (x+2y)\,dx + (2z+2x)\,dy + (z+y)\,dz = 0}$$
