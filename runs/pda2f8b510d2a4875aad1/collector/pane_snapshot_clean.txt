## Solution

**Step 1: Determine M.**

Given $N = 32$, the final digit is $M = 2$.

**Step 2: Identify the roots of unity.**

The $M$-th roots of unity with $M=2$: $\omega = e^{2\pi i/2} = -1$, giving
$$\omega^0 = 1, \quad \omega^1 = -1.$$

**Step 3: Determine the test input $x$.**

$$x = \sqrt{\lfloor 1250/N \rfloor} = \sqrt{\lfloor 1250/32 \rfloor} = \sqrt{\lf
loor 39.0625 \rfloor} = \sqrt{39}.$$

**Step 4: Compute $\mathbb{E}[P(x)]$.**

The transfer function is
$$P(x) = \prod_{k=1}^{M}(x - \omega^{i_k}) = (x - \omega^{i_1})(x - \omega^{i_2}
),$$
where $i_1, i_2$ are chosen independently and uniformly from $\{0, 1\}$.

Since $i_1$ and $i_2$ are **independent**, the expectation of the product equals
 the product of expectations:
$$\mathbb{E}[P(x)] = \mathbb{E}[x - \omega^{i_1}] \cdot \mathbb{E}[x - \omega^{i
_2}].$$

For each $k$, $\omega^{i_k}$ equals $1$ with probability $\tfrac{1}{2}$ and $-1$
 with probability $\tfrac{1}{2}$, so
$$\mathbb{E}[\omega^{i_k}] = \tfrac{1}{2}(1) + \tfrac{1}{2}(-1) = 0.$$

Therefore
$$\mathbb{E}[x - \omega^{i_k}] = x - \mathbb{E}[\omega^{i_k}] = x - 0 = x = \sqr
t{39}.$$

**Step 5: Final result.**

$$\mathbb{E}[P(x)] = \sqrt{39} \cdot \sqrt{39} = \boxed{39}.$$
