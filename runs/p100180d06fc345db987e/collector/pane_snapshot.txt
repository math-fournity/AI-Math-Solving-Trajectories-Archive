## Proof

ring and maximal ideal of $L$ (resp. $K$), and let $\lambda = \mathcal{O}_L/\mat
hfrak{m}_L$ and $\kappa = \mathcal{O}_K/\mathfrak{m}_K$ be the residue fields.

**Setup.** Since $K/L$ is a finite extension of complete discretely valued field

$$v_K(x) \geq 0 \iff v_L(x) \geq 0, \qquad v_K(x) > 0 \iff v_L(x) > 0.$$

**The natural map.** The inclusion $L \hookrightarrow K$ restricts to $\mathcal{
O}_L \hookrightarrow \mathcal{O}_K$ (by the first equivalence above), and carrie
s $\mathfrak{m}_L$ into $\mathfrak{m}_K$ (by the second). Therefore it induces a

$$\varphi : \lambda = \mathcal{O}_L/\mathfrak{m}_L \longrightarrow \mathcal{O}_K
/\mathfrak{m}_K = \kappa.$$

**Injectivity.** The kernel of $\varphi$ is $(\mathcal{O}_L \cap \mathfrak{m}_K)
/\mathfrak{m}_L$. We compute:
$$\mathcal{O}_L \cap \mathfrak{m}_K = \{x \in \mathcal{O}_L : v_K(x) > 0\} = \{x
 \in \mathcal{O}_L : v_L(x) > 0\} = \mathfrak{m}_L,$$
where the middle equality uses $v_K|_L = e \cdot v_L$ with $e \geq 1$. Hence $\k
er\varphi = \mathfrak{m}_L/\mathfrak{m}_L = 0$, so $\varphi$ is injective.

**Conclusion.** Since $\lambda$ and $\kappa$ are fields (the residue fields of l

ia this embedding, of degree $f = f(K/L) = [\kappa : \lambda]$, satisfying $[K:L

**Yes**, for any finite extension $K/L$ of nonarchimedean local fields, there al
ways exists an extension of their residue fields $\kappa/\lambda$, given by the
canonical embedding induced by the inclusion of valuation rings.
