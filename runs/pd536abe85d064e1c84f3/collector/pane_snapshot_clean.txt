$$\phi:X\to X\times\{0\},\qquad \phi(x)=(x,0).$$
This is a bijection. Every measurable subset of $X\times\{0\}$ is of the form $A

$$(\mu\otimes c)(A\times\{0\})=\mu(A)\cdot c(\{0\})=\mu(A)\cdot 1=\mu(A)=\mu(\ph

Hence $\phi$ is a measure-space isomorphism between $(X,B,\mu)$ and $(X\times\{0

$$\phi(Tx)=(Tx,0)=S(x,0)=S(\phi(x)),$$
so $\phi\circ T = S\circ\phi$. Thus $T$ and $S$ are conjugate via the isomorphis

$$(\mu\otimes c)(A\times\{0\})=\mu(A).$$
