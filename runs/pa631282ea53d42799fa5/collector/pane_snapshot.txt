## Proof

We prove

$$\sum_{k=0}^{88} \frac{1}{\cos k°\,\cos(k+1)°} \;=\; \frac{\cos 1°}{\sin^2 1°}.
$$

**Key identity.** For any angle $A$ and $B$ with $\cos A\cos B\neq 0$,

$$\tan A - \tan B \;=\; \frac{\sin(A-B)}{\cos A\,\cos B}.$$

This follows directly from $\tan A-\tan B = \dfrac{\sin A}{\cos A}-\dfrac{\sin B
}{\cos B} = \dfrac{\sin A\cos B - \cos A\sin B}{\cos A\cos B} = \dfrac{\sin(A-B)
}{\cos A\cos B}$.

**Apply with $A=(k+1)°$, $B=k°$.** Since $(k+1)°-k° = 1°$,

$$\tan(k+1)° - \tan k° \;=\; \frac{\sin 1°}{\cos k°\,\cos(k+1)°},$$

hence

$$\frac{1}{\cos k°\,\cos(k+1)°} \;=\; \frac{\tan(k+1)° - \tan k°}{\sin 1°}.$$

**Telescope.** Summing over $k=0,1,\dots,88$,

$$\sum_{k=0}^{88} \frac{1}{\cos k°\,\cos(k+1)°} \;=\; \frac{1}{\sin 1°}\sum_{k=0
}^{88}\bigl(\tan(k+1)° - \tan k°\bigr).$$

The sum collapses:

$$\sum_{k=0}^{88}\bigl(\tan(k+1)° - \tan k°\bigr) \;=\; \tan 89° - \tan 0°.$$

Since $\tan 0° = 0$ and $\tan 89° = \cot 1° = \dfrac{\cos 1°}{\sin 1°}$,

$$\sum_{k=0}^{88} \frac{1}{\cos k°\,\cos(k+1)°} \;=\; \frac{1}{\sin 1°}\cdot\fra
c{\cos 1°}{\sin 1°} \;=\; \frac{\cos 1°}{\sin^2 1°}.$$

This is exactly the right-hand side, completing the proof. $\blacksquare$
