# Problem

Architect Elara is designing a high-tech modular pavilion. The floor plan is a perfectly symmetrical plaza in the shape of a regular polygon with $n$ sides of equal length. 

To create a structural support system, Elara must divide the entire area of the plaza into exactly $n-2$ triangular zones. The constraints of the construction material require that:
1. Each triangular zone must be formed by drawing diagonals between the existing corners of the plaza.
2. These diagonals must not cross each other inside the plaza (they must be disjoint).
3. Every single resulting triangular zone must be an isosceles triangle (having at least two sides of equal length).

For what specific value of $n$ is it possible to partition a regular $n$-gon according to these structural requirements?
