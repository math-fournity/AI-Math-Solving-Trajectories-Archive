This is Erdős Problem 203, which is tagged as `category research open` in the Fo

The problem asks whether there exists an integer $m$ with $\gcd(m, 6) = 1$ such
that $2^k \cdot 3^\ell \cdot m + 1$ is composite for all $k, \ell \geq 0$.

This is a two-dimensional generalization of the Sierpiński number problem. While
 Sierpiński numbers (odd $m$ such that $2^k \cdot m + 1$ is always composite) ar
e known to exist — the smallest being $78557$, proven via a covering system over
 the single exponent $k$ — the present problem requires a covering system that w
orks over the **two-dimensional lattice** of exponent pairs $(k, \ell)$. One wou
ld need a finite set of primes $p_i$ and congruence conditions on $(k \bmod a_i,
 \ell \bmod b_i)$ that:

1. Cover all of $\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}^2$, and
2. For each class, force $p_i \mid 2^k \cdot 3^\ell \cdot m + 1$.

Constructing such a two-dimensional covering system with compatible prime divisi
bility conditions is substantially harder than the one-dimensional Sierpiński ca
se, and no such construction is known. The problem remains open as listed on erd
osproblems.com and in the Formal Conjectures repository.
