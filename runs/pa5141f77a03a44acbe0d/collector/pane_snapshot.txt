## Proof

**Claim:** Yes, $\tau(xp) \geq 0$ for all $x \in A^{+}$.

**Key observation.** The hypothesis $\tau(pa)=\tau(ap)$ for all $a\in A$ says th
at $p$ belongs to the centralizer $C_\tau(A)$. We exploit only this commutation
relation together with the idempotence $p^2=p$.

**Step 1: Reduce $\tau(xp)$ to the compression $\tau(pxp)$.**

Since $p\in C_\tau(A)$, applying the defining relation with $a=x$ gives
$$\tau(xp)=\tau(px). \tag{1}$$

Decompose $px = pxp + px(1-p)$. We claim the second term vanishes under $\tau$.
Indeed, applying the centralizer relation with $a = x(1-p)$:
$$\tau\bigl(p\,x(1-p)\bigr)=\tau\bigl(x(1-p)\,p\bigr)=\tau\bigl(x(1-p)p\bigr).$$
Since $p$ is a projection, $(1-p)p = p-p^2 = 0$, so
$$\tau\bigl(px(1-p)\bigr)=\tau(0)=0. \tag{2}$$

Combining (1) and (2):
$$\tau(xp)=\tau(px)=\tau(pxp)+\tau\bigl(px(1-p)\bigr)=\tau(pxp). \tag{3}$$

**Step 2: $pxp$ is positive.**

Let $x\in A^{+}$, so $x=y^{*}y$ for some $y\in A$ (take $y=x^{1/2}$). Then
$$pxp = p\,y^{*}y\,p = (yp)^{*}(yp)\geq 0.$$
Hence $pxp\in A^{+}$.

**Step 3: Positivity of $\tau$ finishes the argument.**

Since $\tau$ is a state, it is a positive linear functional: $\tau(z)\geq 0$ for
 every $z\in A^{+}$. Applying this to $z=pxp\in A^{+}$ and using (3):
$$\tau(xp)=\tau(pxp)\geq 0.$$

**Conclusion.** For every positive element $x\in A^{+}$,
$$\boxed{\;\tau(xp)=\tau(pxp)\geq 0,\;}$$
so $\tau(xp)\geq 0$ for all $x\in A^{+}$. The equality $\tau(xp)=\tau(pxp)$ is t
he crux: the centralizer hypothesis kills the off-corner contribution $\tau(px(1
-p))$, leaving only the compression $pxp$, which is positive and therefore evalu
ated non-negatively by the state $\tau$.
