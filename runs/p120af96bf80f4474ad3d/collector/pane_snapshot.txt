We determine $|\mathcal{F}|$.

Every $A \in \mathcal{F}$ has the form $A = \mathbb{R}\setminus C$ where $C = \m
athbb{R}\setminus A$ is a countably infinite subset of $\mathbb{R}$. Conversely,

setminus C$ satisfies $\mathbb{R}\setminus A = C$, which is countably infinite,
so $A \in \mathcal{F}$.

$$\Phi : [\mathbb{R}]^{\aleph_0} \to \mathcal{F}, \qquad \Phi(C) = \mathbb{R}\se

$$|\mathcal{F}| = \big|[\mathbb{R}]^{\aleph_0}\big|,$$

where $[\mathbb{R}]^{\aleph_0}$ denotes the family of all countably infinite sub
sets of $\mathbb{R}$.

**Step 2: Upper bound — $\big|[\mathbb{R}]^{\aleph_0}\big| \le |\mathbb{R}|$.**

$$|\mathbb{R}|^{\aleph_0} = (2^{\aleph_0})^{\aleph_0} = 2^{\aleph_0 \cdot \aleph

$$\big|[\mathbb{R}]^{\aleph_0}\big| \ge |\mathbb{I}| = |\mathbb{R}|.$$

$$\big|[\mathbb{R}]^{\aleph_0}\big| = |\mathbb{R}| = 2^{\aleph_0}.$$

By the bijection in Step 1,

$$\boxed{|\mathcal{F}| = 2^{\aleph_0}.}$$
