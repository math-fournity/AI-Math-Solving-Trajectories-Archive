## Solution

The 100 sector capacities are the odd numbers $1, 3, 5, 7, \ldots, 199$ (the $n$
-th sector has capacity $2n-1$). The Total System Volume is

$$P = 1 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 7 \cdots 199 = \prod_{\substack{1 \le m \le 199 \\
 m \text{ odd}}} m.$$

We seek the largest $k$ with $3^k \mid P$, i.e. $k = v_3(P)$, the 3-adic valuati
on of $P$.

**Legendre-type count.** By the standard layer-cake identity,

$$v_3(P) = \sum_{j=1}^{\infty} \#\{\,m : 1 \le m \le 199,\ m \text{ odd},\ 3^j \
mid m\,\}.$$

Since $3^j$ is odd, $3^j \mid m$ and $m$ odd means $m = 3^j \cdot a$ with $a$ od
d. So each count becomes "number of odd $a$ with $3^j a \le 199$", i.e. odd $a \
le \lfloor 199/3^j \rfloor$.

The number of odd positive integers $\le N$ is $\lceil N/2 \rceil = \lfloor (N+1
)/2 \rfloor$.

- **$j=1$:** $3^1 = 3$. Odd $a \le \lfloor 199/3 \rfloor = 66$. Odd $a \in \{1,3
,\ldots,65\}$: $\lceil 66/2\rceil = 33$.
- **$j=2$:** $3^2 = 9$. Odd $a \le \lfloor 199/9 \rfloor = 22$. Odd $a \in \{1,3
,\ldots,21\}$: $\lceil 22/2\rceil = 11$.
- **$j=3$:** $3^3 = 27$. Odd $a \le \lfloor 199/27 \rfloor = 7$. Odd $a \in \{1,
3,5,7\}$: $4$.
- **$j=4$:** $3^4 = 81$. Odd $a \le \lfloor 199/81 \rfloor = 2$. Odd $a \in \{1\
}$: $1$.
- **$j=5$:** $3^5 = 243 > 199$. Count $= 0$. All higher $j$ also give $0$.

**Summing:**

$$v_3(P) = 33 + 11 + 4 + 1 = 49.$$

**Verification of the contributing terms directly.** The odd multiples of $3$ in
 $[1,199]$ are $3,9,15,\ldots,195$ (33 terms, each contributing at least one fac
tor of $3$). Among these, the odd multiples of $9$ are $9,27,45,63,81,99,117,135
,153,171,189$ (11 terms, each contributing a second factor). The odd multiples o
f $27$ are $27,81,135,189$ (4 terms, a third factor each). The odd multiple of $
81$ is $81 = 3^4$ alone (a fourth factor). No odd number $\le 199$ is divisible
by $243$. The layer-cake total is $33+11+4+1 = 49$, confirming the computation.

Therefore the largest integer $k$ such that $3^k$ divides the Total System Volum
e $P$ is

$$\boxed{49}.$$
