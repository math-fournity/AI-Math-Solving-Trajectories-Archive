Looking at this problem, I need to find all possible values of $n$ (the number o
f politicians) and sum them.

## Setup

Let $G$ be the friendship graph on 2020 vertices. The condition states: for any
4 vertices, at least one is adjacent to the other 3. Consider the complement gra
ph $\bar{G}$, where the condition becomes:

**For any 4 vertices, at least one is isolated in the induced subgraph** (i.e.,
no 4-vertex induced subgraph has minimum degree $\geq 1$).

A politician in $G$ is a universal vertex, which corresponds to an **isolated ve
rtex** in $\bar{G}$. So $n$ = number of isolated vertices in $\bar{G}$.

## Analyzing the non-isolated part

Let $H$ be the subgraph of $\bar{G}$ induced by the non-isolated vertices, with
$m = 2020 - n$ vertices. Then:
- $H$ has **no isolated vertices** (by construction).
- Every 4-vertex induced subgraph of $H$ has an isolated vertex (inheriting the
condition from $\bar{G}$; any 4-subset containing a politician already has an is
olated vertex, so only 4-subsets within $H$ matter).

**Key constraint — no $2K_2$:** If $H$ contained two disjoint edges $ab$ and $cd
$, then $\{a,b,c,d\}$ induces a graph where every vertex has degree $\geq 1$, co
ntradicting the condition. So $H$ has **matching number $\leq 1$**.

By the classical characterization, a graph with matching number 1 and no isolate
d vertices is either:
- a **star** $K_{1,k}$, or
- a **triangle** $K_3$.

**Key constraint — no $K_{1,3}$:** If $H$ is a star $K_{1,k}$ with $k \geq 3$, t
hen the center plus any 3 leaves induces $K_{1,3}$, which has minimum degree 1 —
 forbidden. So $k \leq 2$, meaning $m \leq 3$.

A triangle $K_3$ has $m = 3$.

## Determining possible $m$

- **$m = 0$:** $H$ is empty, $n = 2020$. Valid (condition vacuous). ✓
- **$m = 1$:** Impossible — a single vertex in $H$ must have degree $\geq 1$, bu
t there are no other non-isolated vertices to connect to. ✗
- **$m = 2$:** $H = K_2$ (single edge). No 4-vertex subset exists. $n = 2018$. ✓
- **$m = 3$:** $H = K_3$ or $P_3$ (both have no isolated vertices, no 4-vertex s
ubset). $n = 2017$. ✓
- **$m \geq 4$:** Impossible — $H$ must be a star with $k \leq 2$ ($m \leq 3$) o
r a triangle ($m = 3$). ✗

## Verifying achievability

- **$n = 2020$:** Complete graph $K_{2020}$. Every 4-subset: everyone is friends
 with everyone. ✓
- **$n = 2018$:** 2018 politicians + 2 non-politicians who are not friends (in $
G$). Any 4-subset contains at least 2 politicians, each friends with all others.
 ✓
- **$n = 2017$:** 2017 politicians + 3 non-politicians forming an independent se
t in $G$ (triangle in $\bar{G}$). Any 4-subset must include at least 1 politicia
n, who is friends with all others. ✓

(For $P_3$ in $\bar{G}$: the 3 non-politicians have one friendship among them in
 $G$; any 4-subset still includes a politician. ✓)

## Result

The possible values of $n$ are $\{2020, 2018, 2017\}$.

$$2020 + 2018 + 2017 = \boxed{6055}$$
