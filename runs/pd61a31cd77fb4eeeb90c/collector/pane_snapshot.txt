# Proof

**Claim:** In a ring with identity that is both right co-Hopfian and left co-Hop
fian, every element is either a unit or a zero divisor. The answer is **yes**.

## Definitions and setup

Let $R$ be a ring with $1 \neq 0$.

- $R$ is **right co-Hopfian** if $R_R$ (the right regular module) is co-Hopfian:
 every injective $R$-linear endomorphism of $R_R$ is surjective.
- $R$ is **left co-Hopfian** if ${}_R R$ (the left regular module) is co-Hopfian
: every injective $R$-linear endomorphism of ${}_R R$ is surjective.

**Endomorphisms of $R_R$.** Any $R$-linear map $f : R_R \to R_R$ is determined b
y $f(1) = a$, giving $f(r) = f(1 \cdot r) = f(1)\,r = ar$. So $\operatorname{End
}_R(R_R) = \{L_a : r \mapsto ar \mid a \in R\}$.

- $L_a$ is injective $\iff \ker L_a = 0 \iff \{r : ar = 0\} = \{0\}$ $\iff$ $a$
is **not a left zero divisor**.
- $L_a$ is surjective $\iff aR = R$ $\iff$ $a$ has a **right inverse**.

So right co-Hopfianity says:

> **(RC)** If $a$ is not a left zero divisor, then $a$ has a right inverse.

**Endomorphisms of ${}_R R$.** Dually, $\operatorname{End}_R({}_R R) = \{R_a : r
 \mapsto ra \mid a \in R\}$.

- $R_a$ is injective $\iff$ $a$ is **not a right zero divisor**.
- $R_a$ is surjective $\iff Ra = R$ $\iff$ $a$ has a **left inverse**.

So left co-Hopfianity says:

> **(LC)** If $a$ is not a right zero divisor, then $a$ has a left inverse.

## The argument

Let $a \in R$ be arbitrary. If $a$ is a zero divisor (left or right), we are don
e. Suppose $a$ is **not** a zero divisor. Then:

- $a$ is not a left zero divisor. By **(RC)**, $a$ has a right inverse: there ex
ists $b \in R$ with $ab = 1$.
- $a$ is not a right zero divisor. By **(LC)**, $a$ has a left inverse: there ex
ists $c \in R$ with $ca = 1$.

Now compute:
$$c = c \cdot 1 = c(ab) = (ca)b = 1 \cdot b = b.$$

Thus $b = c$ and $ab = ba = 1$, so $a$ is a **unit**.

## Conclusion

Every element of $R$ falls into exactly one of two categories:

- **zero divisor** (if $a$ is a left or right zero divisor), or
- **unit** (if $a$ is neither, then right and left co-Hopfianity force $a$ to be
 invertible).

Therefore, in a right-and-left co-Hopfian ring with identity, every element is e
ither a unit or a zero divisor. $\blacksquare$
