## Solution

**Answer: The minimum value of $k(n)$ is $\boxed{3}$.**

### Setup

$k(n)$ = Hamming weight (number of 1s) of $2023n$ in binary. Since $2023$ is odd
, as $n$ ranges over positive integers, $2023n$ ranges over all positive multipl
es of $2023$. So we seek the minimum Hamming weight of a positive multiple of $2
023$.

Factor: $2023 = 7 \cdot 17^2 = 7 \cdot 289$.

### Weight 1 is impossible

A weight‑1 number is $2^a$. Since $2023>1$ is odd, $2023\nmid 2^a$.

### Weight 2 is impossible

A weight‑2 number is $2^a+2^b = 2^b(2^{a-b}+1)$ with $a>b\ge 0$. Since $2023$ is
 odd, we would need $2023\mid 2^m+1$ for some $m\ge 1$, in particular $2^m\equiv
 -1\pmod 7$.

But $\operatorname{ord}_7(2)=3$, so $\{2^m \bmod 7 : m\ge 0\}=\{1,2,4\}$, which
does **not** contain $-1\equiv 6\pmod 7$. Contradiction. So no multiple of $2023
$ has Hamming weight $2$.

### Weight 3 is achievable

We exhibit a multiple of $2023$ of the form $1+2^u+2^v$ (three 1‑bits). Take
$$u = 136,\qquad v = 341.$$

**Mod $289$:** The order of $2$ modulo $17$ is $8$ (since $2^4\equiv -1\pmod{17}
$), and $2^8=256\not\equiv 1\pmod{289}$, so $\operatorname{ord}_{289}(2)=8\cdot
17=136$. Hence $2^{136}\equiv 1\pmod{289}$. Also
$$2^{69}\equiv -2\pmod{289}$$
(one verifies $2^{68}\equiv -1\pmod{289}$, since $2^{68}\equiv -1\pmod{17}$ lift
s: $2^{68}\bmod 289 = 288 \equiv -1$). Therefore
$$2^{341}=2^{272}\cdot 2^{69}=(2^{136})^2\cdot 2^{69}\equiv 1\cdot(-2)\equiv -2\
pmod{289}.$$
Thus
$$1+2^{136}+2^{341}\equiv 1+1+(-2)\equiv 0\pmod{289}.$$

**Mod $7$:** $136\equiv 1\pmod 3$ and $341\equiv 2\pmod 3$, so $2^{136}\equiv 2^
1\equiv 2\pmod 7$ and $2^{341}\equiv 2^2\equiv 4\pmod 7$. Hence
$$1+2^{136}+2^{341}\equiv 1+2+4\equiv 7\equiv 0\pmod 7.$$

Since $\gcd(7,289)=1$ and $7\cdot 289=2023$,
$$2023 \mid 1+2^{136}+2^{341}.$$

The number $1+2^{136}+2^{341}$ has exactly three 1s in binary (bits at positions
 $0,136,341$). Setting $n=(1+2^{136}+2^{341})/2023$ gives a positive integer wit
h $k(n)=3$.

### Conclusion

Weights $1$ and $2$ are impossible, and weight $3$ is attained. Therefore
$$\min_{n\ge 1} k(n) = 3.$$
