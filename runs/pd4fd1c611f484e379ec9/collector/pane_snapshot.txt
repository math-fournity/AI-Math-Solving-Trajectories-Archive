**Decomposition.** Write $v=u+w$ with $u=Av\in U$ and $w\in U^\perp$. Set
$$t:=\|u\|^2=\langle v,Av\rangle\in[0,1],\qquad \|w\|^2=1-t.$$
Choose an orthonormal basis $\{e_1,e_2,e_3\}$ with $e_1\parallel u$ (when $u\ne0

$$\lambda_\pm=\frac{2\pm\sqrt{4-4(1-t)}}{2}=1\pm\sqrt t.$$
