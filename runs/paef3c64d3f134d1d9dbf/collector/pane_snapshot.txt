## Solution

**Setup.** For $n$ fair six-sided dice, let the faces be $X_1, \dots, X_n \in \{
1,2,3,4,5,6\}$. We want $p(n) = \Pr\!\left[\prod_{i=1}^n X_i \text{ is prime}\ri
ght]$ and the sum $S = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} p(n)$.

**Base case $n=0$.** The empty product equals $1$, which is not prime, so $p(0)=
0$.

**Characterizing when the product is prime (for $n \ge 1$).** Each $X_i \in \{1,
2,3,4,5,6\}$. The product $\prod X_i$ is prime iff **exactly one** die shows a p
rime face $\in \{2,3,5\}$ and **all the remaining** dice show $1$.

*Proof of the characterization.* If all dice show $1$, the product is $1$, not p
rime. If exactly one die shows a value $v > 1$ and the rest show $1$, the produc
t is $v$, which is prime iff $v \in \{2,3,5\}$. If two or more dice show values
$> 1$, the product factors as (at least two factors each $\ge 2$), so it is $\ge
 4$ and composite. Hence primality forces exactly one prime face with all others
 equal to $1$. $\square$

**Computing $p(n)$ for $n \ge 1$.** Choose which die shows the prime: $n$ choice
s. That die shows a prime with probability $\tfrac{3}{6} = \tfrac{1}{2}$; each o
f the other $n-1$ dice shows $1$ with probability $\tfrac{1}{6}$. Thus

$$
p(n) \;=\; n \cdot \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot \left(\tfrac{1}{6}\right)^{n-1} \;=\; \tfr
ac{n}{2}\left(\tfrac{1}{6}\right)^{n-1}.
$$

*Check:* $p(1) = \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot 1 = \tfrac{1}{2}$, matching the three prime f
aces $\{2,3,5\}$ out of six. ✓

**Summing the series.** With $p(0)=0$,

$$
S \;=\; \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \tfrac{n}{2}\left(\tfrac{1}{6}\right)^{n-1} \;=\; \t
frac{1}{2}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n\, r^{\,n-1}, \qquad r = \tfrac{1}{6}.
$$

Using the hint: differentiate the geometric series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} r^n = \f
rac{1}{1-r}$ (valid for $|r|<1$, and $r=\tfrac16$ qualifies):

$$
\frac{d}{dr}\!\left[\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} r^n\right] = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n\, r^{
\,n-1} = \frac{d}{dr}\!\left[\frac{1}{1-r}\right] = \frac{1}{(1-r)^2}.
$$

Therefore

$$
S \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{(1-\tfrac{1}{6})^2} \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\fr
ac{1}{\left(\tfrac{5}{6}\right)^2} \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{36}{25} \;=\; \b
oxed{\dfrac{18}{25}}.
$$
