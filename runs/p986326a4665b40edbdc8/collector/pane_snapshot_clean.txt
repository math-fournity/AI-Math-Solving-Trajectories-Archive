## Proof

Let $X = \mathbb{P}_k^1$ with $k$ algebraically closed. We cover $X$ by the stan
dard affine charts:
- $U_0 = \operatorname{Spec} k[t]$, with coordinate $t$,
- $U_1 = \operatorname{Spec} k[s]$, with $s = 1/t$ on the overlap $U_0 \cap U_1$
.

The point $x = (t - a) \in \operatorname{Spec} k[t]$ is a closed point of $U_0$
(corresponding to $a \in k$, which exists since $k$ is algebraically closed). Si
nce $a \in k$ is finite, $x$ lies in the chart $U_0$ and not at the "point at in
finity" (which would lie only in $U_1$, corresponding to $s = 0$).

**Key fact:** The stalk of the structure sheaf is local. If $x \in U$ for any af
fine open $U = \operatorname{Spec} A \subseteq X$, then
$$\mathcal{O}_{X,x} \cong \mathcal{O}_{U,x} = A_{\mathfrak{p}},$$
where $\mathfrak{p} \in \operatorname{Spec} A$ is the prime corresponding to $x$
. This is because the stalk depends only on arbitrarily small neighborhoods of $
x$, and any affine open neighborhood refines to give the same local ring.

Applying this with $U = U_0 = \operatorname{Spec} k[t]$ and $\mathfrak{p} = (t -
 a)$:

$$\boxed{\mathcal{O}_{X,x} = k[t]_{(t-a)} = \left\{ \frac{f(t)}{g(t)} \;\middle|
\; f, g \in k[t],\; g(a) \neq 0 \right\}.}$$

**Properties of this ring:**

1. **It is a local ring** with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}_x = (t-a)\,k[t]_{(t-a
)}$, generated by the uniformizer $t - a$. The units are precisely those fractio
ns $f/g$ with $f(a) \neq 0$ and $g(a) \neq 0$.

2. **It is a DVR (discrete valuation ring).** Since $k[t]$ is a PID and $(t-a)$
is a nonzero prime ideal, the localization $k[t]_{(t-a)}$ is a DVR. The associat
ed discrete valuation is
$$v_x(f/g) = \operatorname{ord}_{t=a}(f) - \operatorname{ord}_{t=a}(g),$$
i.e., the order of vanishing of $f/g$ at $t = a$. The uniformizer is $t - a$.

3. **Its residue field is $k$:**
$$\mathcal{O}_{X,x}/\mathfrak{m}_x \cong k[t]_{(t-a)}/(t-a) \cong \operatorname{
Frac}(k[t]/(t-a)) \cong k,$$
since $k[t]/(t-a) \cong k$ is already a field (as $k$ is algebraically closed, $
(t-a)$ is maximal).

4. **It is a regular local ring of dimension 1**, consistent with $X$ being a re
gular (smooth) curve over $k$.

**Remark on the point at infinity.** Had $x$ been the point at infinity (the poi
nt $s = 0$ in $U_1$, not visible in $U_0$), the same argument in the chart $U_1
= \operatorname{Spec} k[s]$ would give $\mathcal{O}_{X,x} = k[s]_{(s)}$, which i
s isomorphic to $k[t]_{(t)}$ via $s \leftrightarrow 1/t$. Thus every closed poin
t of $\mathbb{P}^1_k$ has a stalk isomorphic to a DVR of the form $k[u]_{(u-b)}$
 for some $b \in k$, reflecting the homogeneity of $\mathbb{P}^1_k$ as a regular
 curve.
