# Problem

In a specialized logistics hub, items are identified by unique serial numbers. For any given starting integer $m \geqslant 2$, a sequence of consecutive serial numbers is generated, starting from $m$ and ending at a terminal value $n$. The facility manager must organize these items into two separate storage containers. 

The goal is to determine the smallest possible terminal value $n(m)$ such that, regardless of how the items $\{m, m+1, \dots, n\}$ are distributed between the two containers, there will always be at least one container holding three serial numbers $a, b,$ and $c$ (where $a, b,$ and $c$ do not have to be unique) that satisfy the manufacturing relationship $ab = c$.

Calculate the sum of the terminal values for the two specific starting scenarios where the first serial number is 2 and where the first serial number is 3. Specifically, find the value of $n(2) + n(3)$.
