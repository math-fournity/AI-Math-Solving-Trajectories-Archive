**Answer: TRUE.**

**Proof.**

Let $H$ be a Hilbert space with a countable orthonormal basis (the argument work
s for any Hilbert space, separable or not, but we use only the given hypothesis)
. We show that $H$ is unitarily isomorphic to a reproducing kernel Hilbert space
 of functions on a suitable set, hence $H$ "can be made into" an RKHS.

**Construction.** Take the underlying set to be $X := H$ itself (the set of all
vectors of $H$). Define a kernel
$$
K : X \times X \to \mathbb{C}, \qquad K(x, y) := \langle x, y\rangle_H .
$$

**Step 1. $K$ is positive definite.** For any finite collection $x_1, \dots, x_n
 \in X$ and scalars $c_1, \dots, c_n \in \mathbb{C}$,
$$
\sum_{i,j=1}^n c_i \overline{c_j}\, K(x_i, x_j)
= \sum_{i,j} c_i \overline{c_j}\, \langle x_i, x_j\rangle_H
= \Big\langle \sum_i c_i x_i,\; \sum_j c_j x_j \Big\rangle_H
= \Big\| \sum_i c_i x_i \Big\|_H^2 \;\geq\; 0,
$$
with equality iff $\sum_i c_i x_i = 0$. Hence $K$ is a positive‑definite kernel
on $X$.

**Step 2. Apply Moore–Aronszajn.** By the Moore–Aronszajn theorem, there exists
a unique RKHS $\mathcal{H}_K$ of functions on $X$ whose reproducing kernel is $K
$. Concretely, $\mathcal{H}_K$ is the completion of the linear span of the funct
ions
$$
K_y(\cdot) := K(\cdot, y), \qquad y \in X,
$$
with inner product satisfying $\langle K_{y_1}, K_{y_2}\rangle_{\mathcal{H}_K} =
 K(y_2, y_1) = \langle y_2, y_1\rangle_H$.

**Step 3. The natural map is a unitary.** Define
$$
U : H \longrightarrow \mathcal{H}_K, \qquad U(y) := K_y, \quad \text{i.e. } U(y)
(x) = \langle x, y\rangle_H .
$$
This map is linear, and it is isometric:
$$
\langle U(y_1), U(y_2)\rangle_{\mathcal{H}_K} = \langle K_{y_1}, K_{y_2}\rangle_
{\mathcal{H}_K} = K(y_2, y_1) = \langle y_2, y_1\rangle_H = \langle y_1, y_2\ran
gle_H .
$$
It is surjective because, by construction, $\mathcal{H}_K$ is the closed linear
span of $\{K_y : y \in H\} = \{U(y) : y \in H\}$. Thus $U$ is a unitary isomorph
ism from $H$ onto the RKHS $\mathcal{H}_K$.

**Step 4. Reproducing property.** For any $f = U(y) \in \mathcal{H}_K$ and any $
x \in X$,
$$
f(x) = U(y)(x) = \langle x, y\rangle_H = \langle U(x), U(y)\rangle_{\mathcal{H}_
K} = \langle K_x, f\rangle_{\mathcal{H}_K},
$$
so evaluation at $x$ is the bounded functional $f \mapsto \langle f, K_x\rangle_
{\mathcal{H}_K}$, confirming the reproducing property with kernel $K$.

**Conclusion.** Every Hilbert space $H$ with a countable basis is unitarily isom
orphic to the RKHS $\mathcal{H}_K$ on the set $X = H$ with kernel $K(x,y) = \lan
gle x, y\rangle_H$. Therefore every such Hilbert space can be made into a reprod
ucing kernel Hilbert space. $\blacksquare$
