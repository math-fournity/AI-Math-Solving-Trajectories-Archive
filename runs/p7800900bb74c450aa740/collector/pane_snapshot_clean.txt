## Proof

**Claim:** $\lim_{\lambda \in \Lambda}\|\pi_\omega(u_\lambda)\xi_\omega - \xi_\o
mega\| = 0$.

We establish this through the following steps.

---

**Step 1. The approximate unit converges strongly to $\operatorname{id}_H$ in $B
(H)$.**

Since the inclusion $A \subseteq B(H)$ is non-degenerate (i.e., $\overline{\oper
atorname{span}\{a\xi : a \in A,\, \xi \in H\}} = H$), a standard result in opera
tor algebra theory states that **every** approximate unit $\{u_\lambda\}_{\lambd
a \in \Lambda}$ for $A$ converges to $\operatorname{id}_H$ in the strong operato
r topology of $B(H)$:

$$u_\lambda \xrightarrow{\text{SOT}} \operatorname{id}_H, \qquad \text{i.e., } u
_\lambda \eta \to \eta \text{ for all } \eta \in H.$$

*Proof of this fact:* For any $a \in A$ and $\eta \in H$, we have $\|u_\lambda(a
\eta) - a\eta\| = \|(u_\lambda a - a)\eta\| \leq \|u_\lambda a - a\|\,\|\eta\| \
to 0$ since $u_\lambda a \to a$ in norm. By non-degeneracy, vectors of the form
$a\eta$ are dense in $H$, and since $\|u_\lambda\| \leq 1$ (the approximate unit
 is contractive), a standard $\epsilon/3$-argument extends the convergence to al
l $\eta \in H$.

---

**Step 2. This strong convergence takes place inside $B$.**

Since $u_\lambda \in A \subseteq B$ for all $\lambda$, and $\operatorname{id}_H
\in B$ (by hypothesis), the net $\{u_\lambda\}$ together with its strong limit $
\operatorname{id}_H$ all lie in $B$. The strong operator topology on $B$ (as a v
on Neumann subalgebra of $B(H)$) is the restriction of the strong operator topol
ogy of $B(H)$. Therefore:

$$u_\lambda \xrightarrow{\text{SOT}} \operatorname{id}_H \quad \text{in } B.$$

---

**Step 3. The GNS representation $\pi_\omega$ is normal.**

Since $B$ is a von Neumann algebra and $\omega \in B^*$ is a **normal** state, t
he associated GNS representation $\pi_\omega : B \to B(H_\omega)$ is a **normal*
* $*$-representation. (Normality of $\omega$ is equivalent to the normality of $
\pi_\omega$; this is a standard characterization—see, e.g., Takesaki, *Theory of
 Operator Algebras I*.)

---

**Step 4. Normal $*$-representations are strongly continuous on bounded sets.**

A normal $*$-homomorphism $\pi : M \to N$ between von Neumann algebras is $\sigm
a$-strongly continuous (normality of a positive map is equivalent to $\sigma$-st
rong continuity, and a $*$-homomorphism is positive). On **bounded** subsets of
$M$, the $\sigma$-strong topology coincides with the strong operator topology:

- *Strong $\Rightarrow$ $\sigma$-strong on bounded sets:* If $\|x_\lambda\| \leq
 C$ and $x_\lambda \to 0$ strongly, then for any sequence $\{\eta_n\}$ with $\su
m_n \|\eta_n\|^2 < \infty$, given $\epsilon > 0$ pick $N$ with $\sum_{n > N} \|\
eta_n\|^2 < \epsilon/(2C^2)$. Then $\sum_{n > N}\|x_\lambda \eta_n\|^2 \leq C^2
\sum_{n>N}\|\eta_n\|^2 < \epsilon/2$, while $\sum_{n=1}^{N}\|x_\lambda\eta_n\|^2
 \to 0$. Hence $\sum_n \|x_\lambda \eta_n\|^2 \to 0$.
- *$\sigma$-strong $\Rightarrow$ strong:* Take the sequence $(\eta, 0, 0, \dots)
$.

Therefore $\pi_\omega$ is strongly continuous on bounded subsets of $B$.

---

**Step 5. Apply strong continuity to conclude.**

The approximate unit $\{u_\lambda\}$ is contractive, hence bounded ($\|u_\lambda
\| \leq 1$). By Step 2, $u_\lambda \to \operatorname{id}_H$ strongly in $B$. By
Step 4, $\pi_\omega$ is strongly continuous on bounded sets, so:

$$\pi_\omega(u_\lambda) \xrightarrow{\text{SOT}} \pi_\omega(\operatorname{id}_H)
 \quad \text{in } B(H_\omega).$$

Since $B$ is unital (it contains $\operatorname{id}_H$) and the GNS representati
on of a unital $C^*$-algebra is unital, $\pi_\omega(\operatorname{id}_H) = \oper
atorname{id}_{H_\omega}$. Therefore:

$$\pi_\omega(u_\lambda) \xrightarrow{\text{SOT}} \operatorname{id}_{H_\omega}.$$

Strong operator convergence means $\|\pi_\omega(u_\lambda)\zeta - \zeta\| \to 0$
 for every $\zeta \in H_\omega$. In particular, for the cyclic vector $\xi_\omeg
a \in H_\omega$:

$$\lim_{\lambda \in \Lambda}\|\pi_\omega(u_\lambda)\xi_\omega - \xi_\omega\| = 0
.$$

---

**Conclusion.** The limit is $0$. Each hypothesis plays an essential role:

- **Non-degeneracy of $A \subseteq B(H)$** ensures $u_\lambda \to \operatorname{
id}_H$ strongly (Step 1).
- **$\operatorname{id}_H \in B$** ensures the strong limit lies in $B$ and that
$\pi_\omega$ is unital (Steps 2, 5).
- **Normality of $\omega$** ensures $\pi_\omega$ is normal, hence strongly conti
nuous on bounded sets (Steps 3–4).
- **$A'' = B$** guarantees that $A$ is weakly dense in $B$, providing the struct
ural context in which the approximate unit for $A$ can recover the unit of $B$.

$$\boxed{\lim_{\lambda \in \Lambda}\|\pi_\omega(u_\lambda)\xi_\omega - \xi_\omeg
a\| = 0.}$$
