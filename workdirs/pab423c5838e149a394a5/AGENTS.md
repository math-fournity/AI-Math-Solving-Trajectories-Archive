# Problem

An avant-garde architectural firm is designing a modular space station shaped like a regular octahedron—a structure formed by two square-based pyramids joined at their square bases, resulting in eight equilateral triangular hull plates. The firm has decided to coat each of the eight plates in one of three functional thermal paints: "Thermal Red," "Cryo Blue," or "Shield Green."

To prevent sensor interference, the engineering specifications dictate that no two triangular plates that share an edge can be coated with the same color paint.

The space station will be deployed in a zero-gravity environment where it can be freely rotated into any orientation. Therefore, two coating designs are considered identical if one can be rotated to perfectly match the color configuration of the other.

How many distinct color schemes are possible for the space station?
