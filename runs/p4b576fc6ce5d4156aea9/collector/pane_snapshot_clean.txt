## Solution

**Setup.** We have a circle with four points Alpha, Bravo, Charlie, Delta on its
 circumference. Two chords and two interior points are given:

- Chord Alpha–Bravo has length 11, with P on it: PA = 6, PB = 5.
- Chord Charlie–Delta has length 19, with Q on it: QC = 7, QD = 12.
- The line through P and Q meets the circle again at X and Y, with PQ = 27.

**Power of a point.** Since P lies inside the circle on chord Alpha–Bravo, by th
e intersecting-chords theorem:

$$PA \cdot PB = PX \cdot PY = 6 \cdot 5 = 30.$$

Likewise for Q on chord Charlie–Delta:

$$QC \cdot QD = QX \cdot QY = 7 \cdot 12 = 84.$$

**Setting up the line.** Place the line XY on an axis with X at the left and Y a
t the right. Both P and Q lie between X and Y (they are interior points of the c
ircle). Let

$$PX = u, \quad PY = v, \quad \text{so } u v = 30 \text{ and } XY = u + v.$$

Since PQ = 27 and the order along the line is X, P, Q, Y (the other ordering giv
es the same XY by symmetry), we have

$$QX = u + 27, \quad QY = v - 27.$$

Applying the power of Q:

$$(u + 27)(v - 27) = 84.$$

**Solving.** Expand:

$$uv + 27v - 27u - 729 = 84.$$

Substitute $uv = 30$:

$$30 + 27(v - u) - 729 = 84 \implies 27(v - u) = 783 \implies v - u = 29.$$

Now solve the system $uv = 30$, $v - u = 29$:

$$v = u + 29, \quad u(u + 29) = 30 \implies u^2 + 29u - 30 = 0 \implies (u + 30)
(u - 1) = 0.$$

Taking the positive root: $u = 1$, so $v = 30$.

**Result.**

$$XY = u + v = 1 + 30 = \boxed{31}.$$

*Verification.* PX · PY = 1 · 30 = 30 ✓. QX · QY = 28 · 3 = 84 ✓. PQ = 28 − 1 =
27 ✓.

### PROOF COMPLETE
