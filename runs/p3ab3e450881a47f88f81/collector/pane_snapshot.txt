**Step 3: Upper bound $f(x) \leq 2x$ for $x \in (0, 1/2]$.** For any positive in

$$2^n f(x) + f(1 - 2^n x) \leq 1.$$
Since $f(1 - 2^n x) \geq 0$, this gives $f(x) \leq 1/2^n$.

$$f(x) \leq \frac{1}{2^n} \leq 2x.$$
