## Proof

**Claim.** Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field of characteristic $0$, $G$ a
 simply connected semisimple algebraic group over $k$, and $\rho: G \hookrightar
row GL(V)$ a faithful representation. If $g \in G(k)$ acts by scalar multiplicat
ion on $V$ (i.e. $\rho(g) = \lambda\,\mathrm{id}_V$ for some $\lambda \in k^\tim
es$), then $g \in Z(G)$.

**Proof.** The argument uses only faithfulness; the hypotheses "simply connected
," "semisimple," and "algebraically closed of characteristic $0$" are not needed
.

Let $g \in G(k)$ with $\rho(g) = \lambda\,\mathrm{id}_V$. For any $h \in G(k)$,
$$\rho(ghg^{-1}) = \rho(g)\rho(h)\rho(g)^{-1} = (\lambda\,\mathrm{id}_V)\,\rho(h
)\,(\lambda\,\mathrm{id}_V)^{-1} = \rho(h).$$
Since $\rho$ is faithful (injective), $\rho(ghg^{-1}) = \rho(h)$ implies $ghg^{-
1} = h$. As this holds for every $h \in G(k)$, we conclude $g \in Z(G)$.

Hence any group element acting by a scalar on a faithful representation is centr
al. $\square$
