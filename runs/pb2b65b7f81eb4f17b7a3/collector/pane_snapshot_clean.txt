## Solution

**Setup.** By translation invariance, fix one point at the origin $O$. The secon
d point $P$ lies on the circle of radius $d$ centered at $O$. Since the original
 selection is uniform over the plane, the angle $\theta$ of $\vec{OP}$ relative
to the $x$-axis is uniform on $[0, 2\pi)$.

So $P = (d\cos\theta,\, d\sin\theta)$ with $\theta \sim \mathrm{Uniform}(0, 2\pi
)$.

**Manhattan distance.**
$$m = |d\cos\theta - 0| + |d\sin\theta - 0| = d\bigl(|\cos\theta| + |\sin\theta|
\bigr).$$

**Expectation.**
$$E[m \mid d] = d \cdot E\bigl[|\cos\theta| + |\sin\theta|\bigr] = d \cdot \frac
{1}{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}\bigl(|\cos\theta| + |\sin\theta|\bigr)\,d\theta.$$

By symmetry of $|\cos|$ and $|\sin|$ over $[0,2\pi)$, each contributes the same
integral:
$$\int_0^{2\pi}|\cos\theta|\,d\theta = 4\int_0^{\pi/2}\cos\theta\,d\theta = 4\cd
ot 1 = 4,$$
$$\int_0^{2\pi}|\sin\theta|\,d\theta = 4\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin\theta\,d\theta = 4\cd
ot 1 = 4.$$

Hence
$$\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}\bigl(|\cos\theta| + |\sin\theta|\bigr)\,d\theta =
\frac{8}{2\pi} = \frac{4}{\pi}.$$

**Result.**
$$\boxed{E[m \mid d] = \frac{4d}{\pi}}.$$

**Sanity check.** For any $(x,y)$, we have $\sqrt{x^2+y^2} \le |x|+|y| \le \sqrt
{2}\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$, i.e. $d \le m \le \sqrt{2}\,d$. The constant $\frac{4}{\pi}
\approx 1.273$ lies in $[1, \sqrt{2}\approx 1.414]$. ✓
