## Proof

**Setup.** Substitute $t = N - k$, so $t$ ranges from $2$ to $N$. The sum become
s:
$$S_N = \sum_{t=2}^{N} N^3 \left(1 - \frac{t}{N}\right)^{N \ln N}$$

All terms are non-negative, so $S_N$ is at least as large as any single term.

**Key asymptotic.** For fixed $t$ and $N \to \infty$:
$$\ln\left[\left(1-\frac{t}{N}\right)^{N\ln N}\right] = N\ln N \cdot \ln\left(1-
\frac{t}{N}\right) = N\ln N\left(-\frac{t}{N} - \frac{t^2}{2N^2} - O(N^{-3})\rig
ht) = -t\ln N - \frac{t^2 \ln N}{2N} + O\!\left(\frac{\ln N}{N^2}\right)$$

Therefore:
$$\left(1-\frac{t}{N}\right)^{N\ln N} = e^{-t\ln N}\cdot e^{-t^2\ln N/(2N)+O(\ln
 N/N^2)} = N^{-t}\left(1 - \frac{t^2\ln N}{2N} + O\!\left(\frac{(\ln N)^2}{N^2}\
right)\right)$$

**The $t=2$ term (dominant).** Setting $t=2$:
$$a_{N-2} = N^3 \cdot N^{-2}\left(1 - \frac{2\ln N}{N} + O\!\left(\frac{(\ln N)^
2}{N^2}\right)\right) = N\left(1 - \frac{2\ln N}{N} + O\!\left(\frac{(\ln N)^2}{
N^2}\right)\right) \sim N$$

So $a_{N-2} \to +\infty$.

**The $t=3$ term.** Setting $t=3$:
$$a_{N-3} = N^3 \cdot N^{-3}\left(1 - \frac{9\ln N}{2N} + O\!\left(\frac{(\ln N)
^2}{N^2}\right)\right) = 1 - \frac{9\ln N}{2N} + O\!\left(\frac{(\ln N)^2}{N^2}\
right) \to 1$$

**The $t \geq 4$ terms.** For fixed $t \geq 4$:
$$a_{N-t} = N^3 \cdot N^{-t}(1+o(1)) = N^{3-t}(1+o(1)) \to 0$$

For $t$ growing with $N$ (i.e., $k$ bounded away from $N$), the terms are even s
maller: if $k/N \leq 1-\delta$ for some $\delta > 0$, then $(k/N)^{N\ln N} \leq
(1-\delta)^{N\ln N} \to 0$ super-exponentially, making $N^3(k/N)^{N\ln N} \to 0$
.

**Bounding the tail.** For $t \geq 4$, using $(1-t/N)^{N\ln N} \leq e^{-t\ln N}
= N^{-t}$ (since $\ln(1-x) \leq -x$):
$$\sum_{t=4}^{N} N^3\left(1-\frac{t}{N}\right)^{N\ln N} \leq \sum_{t=4}^{N} N^{3
-t} = N^{-1}\sum_{t=4}^{N} N^{4-t} \leq N^{-1}\sum_{s=0}^{\infty} N^{-s} = \frac
{1}{N}\cdot\frac{1}{1-1/N} \to 0$$

(The substitution $s = t-4$ shows the sum is $O(1/N)$.)

**Conclusion.** Combining:
$$S_N = \underbrace{a_{N-2}}_{\sim\, N} + \underbrace{a_{N-3}}_{\to\, 1} + \unde
rbrace{\sum_{t=4}^{N} a_{N-t}}_{\to\, 0} + \underbrace{\sum_{\text{small } k} a_
k}_{\to\, 0} = N + 1 + o(1) \to +\infty$$

Since $S_N \geq a_{N-2} \sim N \to +\infty$, the sum diverges.

$$\boxed{\lim_{N\to\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{N-2} N^3 \left(\frac{k}{N}\right)^{N\ln N}
 = +\infty}$$
