I'll solve this directly in the TUI using the parametrized circle approach.

**Setup.** Let the incircle ω be the unit circle. Parametrize a point on ω by $P
(t)=\bigl(\frac{1-t^{2}}{1+t^{2}},\frac{2t}{1+t^{2}}\bigr)$; the tangent at $P(t
)$ is $(1-t^{2})x+2ty=1+t^{2}$. Denote the tangent points of ω with $AB,BC,CD,DA
$ by parameters $a,e,c,f$ respectively, so $E=P(e)$, $F=P(f)$. The vertices are
intersections of consecutive tangents; using the standard formula, $\text{tangen
t}(p)\cap\text{tangent}(q)=\bigl(\frac{1-pq}{1+pq},\frac{p+q}{1+pq}\bigr)$.

A rotation of the circle acts on the parameter by $t\mapsto\frac{t-r}{1+rt}$; th
is preserves all tangency/concurrence/parallelism notions we need. **Rotate so t
hat $f=0$**, i.e. $F=P(0)=(1,0)$ and the tangent at $F$ is the line $x=1$. Then
$$A=\text{tangent}(a)\cap\{x=1\}=(1,a),\qquad D=\text{tangent}(c)\cap\{x=1\}=(1,
c),$$
both lying on $x=1$, and $E=P(e)=\bigl(\frac{1-e^{2}}{1+e^{2}},\frac{2e}{1+e^{2}
}\bigr)$.

**Concurrence condition.** Lines $AB=\text{tangent}(a)$, $CD=\text{tangent}(c)$
meet at $Q=\bigl(\frac{1-ac}{1+ac},\frac{a+c}{1+ac}\bigr)$. The chord $EF$ throu
gh $P(e),P(0)$ has equation $(1-e\cdot0)x+(e+0)y=1+e\cdot0$, i.e. $x+ey=1$. Requ
iring $Q\in EF$:
$$\frac{1-ac}{1+ac}+e\cdot\frac{a+c}{1+ac}=1 \;\Longrightarrow\; (a+c)e=2ac.$$
Set $s=a+c$, $p=ac$; then $\boxed{e=\frac{2p}{s}}$ (and $s\neq0$ in the non-dege
nerate case).

**Finding $E_{1}$.** The circumcircle of $A(1,a),D(1,c),E$ has its center on the
 perpendicular bisector $y=s/2$ of $AD$, say $(h,s/2)$. Equating its squared rad
ius computed from $A$ and from $E$ gives
$$h=\frac{3s^{2}-4p^{2}}{16p}.$$
The radical axis of this circumcircle and the unit circle (center $O$, radius $1
$) is the line $EE_{1}$. From $|X|^{2}-1=|X-(h,s/2)|^{2}-R^{2}$ with $R^{2}=(1-h
)^{2}+\frac{s^{2}-4p}{4}$, this axis is
$$2hx+sy=2h+p \;\Longleftrightarrow\; (3s^{2}-4p^{2})x+8ps\,y=3s^{2}+4p^{2}.$$
Intersect with the unit circle via $x=\frac{1-t^{2}}{1+t^{2}},y=\frac{2t}{1+t^{2
}}$:
$$(3s^{2}+4p^{2}-3s^{2}+4p^{2})\;\Longrightarrow\; 6s^{2}t^{2}-16ps\,t+8p^{2}=0
\;\Longrightarrow\; 3s^{2}t^{2}-8ps\,t+4p^{2}=0,$$
with roots $t=\frac{8ps\pm4ps}{6s^{2}}$, i.e. $t=\frac{2p}{s}=e$ (the point $E$,
 as expected) and
$$\boxed{e_{1}=\frac{2p}{3s}=\frac{e}{3}}.$$

**Finding $F_{1}$.** Now $B=\text{tangent}(a)\cap\text{tangent}(e)$, $C=\text{ta
ngent}(c)\cap\text{tangent}(e)$ both lie on $\text{tangent}(e)$, and $F=P(0)$ li
es on ω. Apply the rotation $t\mapsto\frac{t-e}{1+et}$, sending $E\mapsto P(0)$
so that $\text{tangent}(e)$ becomes $x=1$. In this rotated frame the tangent par
ameters become $a'=\frac{a-e}{1+ae}$, $c'=\frac{c-e}{1+ce}$, and $F\mapsto P(f')
$ with $f'=-e$. The concurrence of $\text{tangent}(a'),\text{tangent}(c'),\text{
chord}(0,f')$ gives, by exactly the computation above, the second-intersection p
arameter $\frac{f'}{3}=-\frac{e}{3}$. Rotating back via $t\mapsto\frac{t+e}{1-et
}$:
$$f_{1}=\frac{-e/3+e}{1-e(-e/3)}=\frac{2e/3}{1+e^{2}/3}=\boxed{\frac{2e}{3+e^{2}
}}.$$

**Parallelism.** The chord through $P(u),P(v)$ has normal direction $(1-uv,\,u+v
)$. For $EF$ (params $e,0$): normal $\vec n_{EF}=(1,e)$. For $E_{1}F_{1}$ (param
s $\frac{e}{3},\frac{2e}{3+e^{2}}$):
$$1-t_{1}t_{2}=1-\frac{e}{3}\cdot\frac{2e}{3+e^{2}}=\frac{9+e^{2}}{3(3+e^{2})},\
qquad t_{1}+t_{2}=\frac{e}{3}+\frac{2e}{3+e^{2}}=\frac{e(9+e^{2})}{3(3+e^{2})}.$
$
Thus $\vec n_{E_{1}F_{1}}=\frac{9+e^{2}}{3(3+e^{2})}\,(1,e)$, which is a scalar
multiple of $\vec n_{EF}=(1,e)$.

Two chords whose normal directions are proportional are parallel. Hence $EF\para
llel E_{1}F_{1}$. $\blacksquare$
