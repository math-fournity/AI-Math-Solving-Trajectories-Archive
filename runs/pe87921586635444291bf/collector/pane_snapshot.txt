Looking at this problem, I need to determine whether the Main Conjecture for ell
iptic curves (the Iwasawa Main Conjecture / Mazur's Main Conjecture) is invarian
t under Q-isogeny.

**Answer: Yes.** If the Main Conjecture holds for E₁, it also holds for E₂.

**Proof.**

Let φ: E₁ → E₂ be a Q-isogeny, and fix a prime p (of good ordinary reduction for
 both curves) at which the Main Conjecture is considered. The Main Conjecture fo
r E/Q asserts an equality of ideals in the Iwasawa algebra Λ = Z_p[[T]]:

> char_Λ(X(E/Q_∞)) = (L_p(E, T) · g_E(T))

where Q_∞/Q is the cyclotomic Z_p-extension, X(E/Q_∞) is the Pontryagin dual of
the p-primary Selmer group Sel_p(E/Q_∞), L_p(E, T) is the p-adic L-function, and
 g_E(T) is an explicit element of Frac(Λ)× (built from E(Q)[p]-torsion and Tamag
awa/local factors at bad primes).

The key structural fact is that **the Main Conjecture is fundamentally a stateme
nt about the p-adic Galois representation V_p(E)**, and isogenous curves yield i
somorphic representations. The argument proceeds in three steps.

---

**Step 1: Isogenous curves share the same Galois representation and L-function.*
*

The isogeny φ induces, for every prime ℓ, an isomorphism of G_Q-representations
V_ℓ(E₁) ≅ V_ℓ(E₂) (the isogeny gives an isomorphism after tensoring the ℓ-adic T
ate modules with Q_ℓ). By the modularity theorem, E₁ and E₂ correspond to the **
same newform** f of weight 2, and therefore:

> L(E₁, s) = L(E₂, s) = L(f, s).

In particular, all special L-values L(f, χ, 1) appearing in the interpolation fo
rmula for the p-adic L-function are identical for E₁ and E₂.

---

**Step 2: The p-adic L-functions differ by a known, explicit factor.**

The p-adic L-function L_p(E, T) interpolates values of the form:

> L_p(E, χ) = L(f, χ⁻¹, 1) / Ω_E^{±}

where Ω_E^± are the Néron periods (real/imaginary) of E. Since the L-values L(f,
 χ⁻¹, 1) are the same for E₁ and E₂, but the Néron periods differ, we obtain:

> L_p(E₂, T) = (Ω_{E₁}/Ω_{E₂}) · L_p(E₁, T)

where the ratio Ω_{E₁}/Ω_{E₂} ∈ Q× is determined by the isogeny: if φ*ω_{E₂} = c
 · ω_{E₁} (with ω_E the Néron differential), then Ω_{E₁}/Ω_{E₂} = 1/c.

Additionally, at primes q dividing the degree of φ where the integral local Galo
is representations on T_p(Eᵢ) differ, there are **local correction factors** — e
xplicit elements of Q_p× (or Λ×) — that modify the p-adic L-function. These fact
ors are determined entirely by the local behavior of the isogeny at q.

---

**Step 3: The Selmer group characteristic ideals differ by the same factor.**

The isogeny φ and its dual φ̂: E₂ → E₁ induce maps on Selmer groups:

> φ_*: Sel_p(E₁/Q_∞) → Sel_p(E₂/Q_∞),    φ̂_*: Sel_p(E₂/Q_∞) → Sel_p(E₁/Q_∞)

with φ̂_* ∘ φ_* = [d] and φ_* ∘ φ̂_* = [d] (d = deg φ). These fit into exact seque
nces relating the two Selmer groups, with kernels and cokernels controlled by:
- The finite torsion subgroups Eᵢ(Q)[p] (related by the isogeny), and
- The local cohomology at primes q | d (where φ is not a local isomorphism).

A standard result in Iwasawa theory for elliptic curves (see Greenberg, *"Introd
uction to Iwasawa theory for elliptic curves,"* and the treatment in Coates–Suja
tha, *Galois Cohomology of Elliptic Curves*) shows that the characteristic ideal
s of the Pontryagin duals satisfy:

> char_Λ(X(E₂/Q_∞)) = (Ω_{E₁}/Ω_{E₂}) · char_Λ(X(E₁/Q_∞)) · (same local factors)

where the local factors are **exactly** those appearing in Step 2. Moreover, the
 correction term g_E(T) transforms compatibly:

> g_{E₂}(T) = (Ω_{E₁}/Ω_{E₂}) · g_{E₁}(T) · (same local factors).

The reason both sides transform identically is that they both depend on the same
 integral data — the lattice T_p(E) ⊂ V_p(E) and the local integral structure —
and the isogeny changes this data in the same way on both sides.

---

**Step 4: Conclusion.**

Combining Steps 2 and 3, both sides of the Main Conjecture — the characteristic
ideal of the Selmer group dual (algebraic side) and the ideal generated by the p
-adic L-function times correction factors (analytic side) — transform by the **s
ame multiplicative factor** under the isogeny E₁ → E₂. Therefore:

> char_Λ(X(E₁/Q_∞)) = (L_p(E₁, T) · g_{E₁}(T))  ⟹  char_Λ(X(E₂/Q_∞)) = (L_p(E₂,
T) · g_{E₂}(T)).

The Main Conjecture for E₁ implies the Main Conjecture for E₂. By symmetry (appl
ying the same argument to the dual isogeny φ̂: E₂ → E₁), the converse holds as we
ll. Hence the Main Conjecture is **invariant under Q-isogeny**: it holds for one
 curve in an isogeny class if and only if it holds for all curves in that class.
 ▮

**Remark.** The essential reason is that the Main Conjecture is really a stateme
nt about the rational p-adic Galois representation V_p(E) ≅ V_p(E₂), which is an
 isogeny invariant. The specific curve E within its isogeny class only affects t
he choice of integral lattice T_p(E) ⊂ V_p(E) and the local integral structure,
and these affect both sides of the conjecture in identically the same way.
