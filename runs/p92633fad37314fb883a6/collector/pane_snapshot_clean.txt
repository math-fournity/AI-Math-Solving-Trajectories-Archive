$$g(\alpha x + \beta y) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{\alpha x_n + \beta y_n}{n} =
 \alpha \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{x_n}{n} + \beta \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{y_n}{n

where the termwise manipulation is justified once we show the series converge ab

$$|g(x)| \le \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left|\frac{x_n}{n}\right| \le \|a\|_2\,\|x\|_2
