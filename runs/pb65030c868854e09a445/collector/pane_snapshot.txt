## Solution

Let $\nu_{n,d}\colon \mathbb{P}^n \to \mathbb{P}^N$ be the Veronese embedding, $
N=\binom{n+d}{d}-1$, given by all degree-$d$ monomials. This is the map associat
ed to the complete linear system $|\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^n}(d)|$.

**Degree via intersection theory.** For a projective variety $X$ of dimension $r
$ embedded by a very ample line bundle $L$, the degree of the image $\nu_L(X)\su
bset\mathbb{P}^N$ is

$$\deg(\nu_L(X)) = \int_X c_1(L)^r.$$

**Apply to $H$.** Here $H\subset\mathbb{P}^n$ is a hypersurface of degree $b$, s
o $\dim H = r = n-1$ and $\deg H = b$. The Veronese map restricts to $H$ via the
 line bundle $L = \mathcal{O}_H(d)$. Let $h$ denote the hyperplane class on $\ma
thbb{P}^n$; then $c_1(L) = d\cdot h\big|_H$, and

$$\deg(\nu_{n,d}(H)) = \int_H \bigl(d\cdot h\big|_H\bigr)^{n-1} = d^{\,n-1}\int_
H h^{n-1}\big|_H.$$

**Compute the integral.** Since $[H] = b\cdot h$ in $A^1(\mathbb{P}^n)$ (the cla
ss of a degree-$b$ hypersurface is $b$ times the hyperplane class), by the proje
ction formula

$$\int_H h^{n-1}\big|_H = \int_{\mathbb{P}^n} h^{n-1}\cdot [H] = \int_{\mathbb{P
}^n} h^{n-1}\cdot b\,h = b\int_{\mathbb{P}^n} h^n = b\cdot 1 = b.$$

**Conclusion.**

$$\deg\bigl(\nu_{n,d}(H)\bigr) = d^{\,n-1}\cdot b = b\,d^{\,n-1}.$$

**Sanity checks.**
- $H=\mathbb{P}^n$ (formally $b=1$, $r=n$): $\deg\nu_{n,d}(\mathbb{P}^n)=d^n$. ✓
 (classical Veronese degree).
- $n=2$, $d=2$, $H$ a line ($b=1$): the image is a conic in $\mathbb{P}^5$, degr
ee $1\cdot 2^1=2$. ✓
- $n=2$, $d=2$, $H$ a conic ($b=2$): $\mathcal{O}_H(2)$ has degree $2\cdot 2=4$,
 image curve has degree $2\cdot 2^1=4$. ✓

$$\boxed{b\,d^{\,n-1}}$$
