## Proof

Let $v = \dfrac{du}{dt}$. The equation $\dfrac{d^2 u}{dt^2} + Au = f(t)$ rewrite
s as the first-order system
$$\frac{dv}{dt} = -Au + f(t), \qquad \frac{du}{dt} = v.$$

Define the energy norm
$$\|u(t)\|_{*}^2 = \Big\|\frac{du}{dt}\Big\|^2 + \|u\|_A^2 = (v, v) + (Au, u).$$

**Step 1. Energy identity.** Differentiate $\|u\|_{*}^2$ in $t$:
$$\frac{d}{dt}\|u\|_{*}^2 = 2\Big(v,\,\frac{dv}{dt}\Big) + 2\Big(Au,\,\frac{du}{
dt}\Big) = 2(v,\,-Au + f) + 2(Au,\,v).$$

Expanding,
$$\frac{d}{dt}\|u\|_{*}^2 = -2(v, Au) + 2(v, f) + 2(Au, v).$$

Since $A = A^*$ is self-adjoint, $(v, Au) = (Au, v)$, so the first and third ter
ms cancel:
$$\boxed{\frac{d}{dt}\|u\|_{*}^2 = 2(v, f).}$$

**Step 2. Differential inequality.** By the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality and the el
ementary estimate $2ab \le a^2 + b^2$,
$$2(v, f) \le 2\|v\|\,\|f\| \le \|v\|^2 + \|f\|^2 \le \|u\|_{*}^2 + \|f\|^2,$$
since $\|v\|^2 \le \|u\|_{*}^2$. Hence
$$\frac{d}{dt}\|u\|_{*}^2 \le \|u\|_{*}^2 + \|f(t)\|^2.$$

**Step 3. Gronwall-type argument.** Multiply both sides by the integrating facto
r $e^{-t}$:
$$e^{-t}\frac{d}{dt}\|u\|_{*}^2 - e^{-t}\|u\|_{*}^2 \le e^{-t}\|f(t)\|^2,$$
i.e.
$$\frac{d}{dt}\Big(e^{-t}\|u\|_{*}^2\Big) \le e^{-t}\|f(t)\|^2.$$

Integrate from $0$ to $t$:
$$e^{-t}\|u(t)\|_{*}^2 - \|u(0)\|_{*}^2 \le \int_0^t e^{-\theta}\|f(\theta)\|^2\
,d\theta.$$

**Step 4. Initial data.** At $t = 0$,
$$\|u(0)\|_{*}^2 = \Big\|\frac{du}{dt}(0)\Big\|^2 + \|u(0)\|_A^2 = \|u_1\|^2 + \
|u_0\|_A^2 = \|v^0\|^2 + \|u^0\|_A^2.$$

Substituting,
$$e^{-t}\|u(t)\|_{*}^2 \le \|u^0\|_A^2 + \|v^0\|^2 + \int_0^t e^{-\theta}\|f(\th
eta)\|^2\,d\theta.$$

**Step 5. Conclusion.** Multiplying through by $e^{t}$ yields the desired stabil
ity estimate:
$$\|u(t)\|_{*}^2 \le \exp(t)\left(\|u^0\|_A^2 + \|v^0\|^2 + \int_0^t \exp(-\thet
a)\|f(\theta)\|^2\,d\theta\right). \qquad\blacksquare$$
