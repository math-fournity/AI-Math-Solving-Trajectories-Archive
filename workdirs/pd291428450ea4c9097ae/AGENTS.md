# Problem

These four reactors are positioned such that every reactor is perfectly touching (tangent to) the other three, forming a stable tetrahedral cluster.

To regulate the energy output, two additional containment shells must be manufactured:

1.  **The Inner Core (Shell 5):** A small spherical vacuum chamber is placed in the central void created between the four reactors. This shell is engineered to be externally tangent to all four plasma reactors. Let its volume be $V_5$.
2.  **The Outer Hull (Shell 6):** A massive spherical shield is constructed to enclose the entire assembly. This hull is internally tangent to all four plasma reactors, touching each one at their outermost points. Let its volume be $V_6$.

Determine the exact ratio of the volume of the outer hull to the volume of the inner core, expressed as $\frac{V_6}{V_5}$.
