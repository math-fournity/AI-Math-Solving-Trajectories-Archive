## Proof

Let $z = e^{2\pi i/17}$ be a primitive 17th root of unity. The vertices of the r
egular 17-gon are $1, z, z^2, \ldots, z^{16}$, all on the unit circle.

### Step 1: Chord lengths in terms of $z$

The length of the chord (diagonal or side) connecting $z^a$ to $z^b$ is:
$$|z^a - z^b| = |z^b|\cdot|z^{a-b} - 1| = |z^{a-b} - 1|$$
since $|z^b| = 1$. This depends only on $d = a - b \pmod{17}$.

By symmetry, $|z^d - 1| = |z^{17-d} - 1|$, so the distinct chord lengths corresp
ond to $d = 1, 2, \ldots, 8$. For each such $d$, there are exactly **17 chords**
 of length $|z^d - 1|$ (each vertex pairs with the vertex $d$ steps away).

### Step 2: Product over all chords

The product of the **squares** of the lengths of all chords is:
$$\prod_{\text{all chords}} |z^a - z^b|^2 = \prod_{d=1}^{8} |z^d - 1|^{2 \cdot 1
7} = \prod_{d=1}^{8} |z^d - 1|^{34} = \left(\prod_{d=1}^{8} |z^d - 1|\right)^{34
}$$

### Step 3: Evaluate $\prod_{d=1}^{8} |z^d - 1|$

From the factorization $x^{17} - 1 = (x-1)\prod_{d=1}^{16}(x - z^d)$, differenti
ating and evaluating at $x=1$ (or directly substituting):
$$\prod_{d=1}^{16}(1 - z^d) = 17$$

Taking absolute values:
$$\prod_{d=1}^{16} |1 - z^d| = 17$$

Since $|1 - z^d| = |1 - z^{17-d}|$, pairing $d$ with $17-d$:
$$\prod_{d=1}^{8} |1 - z^d|^2 = 17 \implies \prod_{d=1}^{8} |1 - z^d| = \sqrt{17
}$$

### Step 4: Final computation

$$\prod_{\text{all chords}} |z^a - z^b|^2 = \left(\sqrt{17}\right)^{34} = 17^{17
}$$

### Step 5: Diagonals only (excluding sides)

The 17 sides correspond to $d = 1$ (adjacent vertices). The product of the squar
es of the **side lengths** is:
$$\prod_{\text{sides}} |z^a - z^{a+1}|^2 = |z - 1|^{2 \cdot 17} = |z-1|^{34}$$

Note $|z-1|^2 = (z-1)(z^{-1}-1) = 2 - z - z^{-1} = 2 - 2\cos\frac{2\pi}{17}$.

Therefore, the product of the squares of the lengths of all **diagonals** (exclu
ding sides) is:
$$\boxed{\frac{17^{17}}{|z-1|^{34}} = \frac{17^{17}}{\left(2 - 2\cos\frac{2\pi}{
17}\right)^{17}}}$$

If the problem intends "diagonals" to include all chords (sides and diagonals to
