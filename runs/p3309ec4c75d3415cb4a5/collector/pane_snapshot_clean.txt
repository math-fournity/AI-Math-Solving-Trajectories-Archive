**Guess:** the multiset consists of $c$ copies of $10$, $f$ copies of $-10$, $d$

- **Odd $n$:** $(c - f) \cdot 10^n + (-d) + 0 = 10^n - 1$, giving $c - f = 1$ an

Solving: $c = 6$, $f = 5$, $d = 1$. The total count so far is $6 + 5 + 1 = 12$,

The multiset is uniquely determined: **6 copies of $10$, 5 copies of $-10$, 1 co

## Counting Valid Assignments

**Parity constraint on $b_k$:** For even $k$, $b_k = a_k^k \geq 0$ (since $a_k^k

**Choices for $a_k$ given $b_k$:**

## Summing Over Cases
