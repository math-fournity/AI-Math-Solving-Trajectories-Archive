## Solution

The problem asks for the moment generating function (MGF) of the limit distribut
ion of $X_n$ (from "the beta distribution problem") evaluated at $t = 0$.

**Key fact.** For *any* random variable $X$ possessing a moment generating funct
ion, the MGF is defined as
$$M_X(t) = \mathbb{E}\!\left[e^{tX}\right].$$

Evaluating at $t = 0$:
$$M_X(0) = \mathbb{E}\!\left[e^{0 \cdot X}\right] = \mathbb{E}[1] = 1.$$

This holds for **every** distribution whose MGF exists — including, in particula
r, any limit distribution that $X_n$ may converge to in the beta distribution pr
oblem. The value $M_X(0) = 1$ is a universal normalization property of moment ge
nerating functions: it is the constant term of the power series $M_X(t) = \sum_{
k=0}^{\infty} \frac{\mathbb{E}[X^k]}{k!}\,t^k$, whose $k=0$ term is always $1$.

Therefore, regardless of the specific form of the limit distribution of $X_n$,

$$\boxed{M(0) = 1}$$
