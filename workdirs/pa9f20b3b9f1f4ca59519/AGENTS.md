# Problem

In a remote industrial facility, a pressure monitoring system utilizes a series of four synchronized rotary gauges, each indexed with the digits 0 through 9. These gauges operate identically to a standard four-dial gas meter, where each dial represents a power of ten: the rightmost dial (units) completes a full rotation as the value increases by 10, the second dial (tens) completes one rotation for every 100 units, the third (hundreds) for every 1,000, and the leftmost (thousands) for every 10,000. 

Because each dial is geared to the next, the "pointers" or needles rotate at different rates: for every full 360-degree rotation of the units dial, the tens dial rotates 36 degrees, the hundreds dial 3.6 degrees, and the thousands dial 0.36 degrees.

A technician observes the panel and notes a unique event: all four needles are pointing in the exact same direction, rendering them perfectly parallel to one another.

What is the highest possible numerical reading on the meter at which this configuration occurs?
