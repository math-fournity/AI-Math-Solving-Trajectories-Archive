## Solution

We solve the quasilinear first-order PDE
$$-x\,u_x + u\,u_y = y, \qquad u(x,2x)=0$$
by the **method of characteristic curves**.

### Characteristic ODEs

For a PDE of the form $a(x,y,u)\,u_x + b(x,y,u)\,u_y = c(x,y,u)$, the characteri
stic system is
$$\frac{dx}{ds}=a,\quad \frac{dy}{ds}=b,\quad \frac{du}{ds}=c.$$
Here $a=-x$, $b=u$, $c=y$, giving
$$\frac{dx}{ds}=-x,\qquad \frac{dy}{ds}=u,\qquad \frac{du}{ds}=y.$$

### Solving the characteristic equations

**The $x$–equation.** $\dfrac{dx}{ds}=-x$ separates immediately:
$$x(s)=x_0\,e^{-s}.$$

**The $(y,u)$–subsystem.** Since $\dfrac{dy}{ds}=u$ and $\dfrac{du}{ds}=y$, diff
erentiating the first gives
$$\frac{d^{2}y}{ds^{2}}=\frac{du}{ds}=y,$$
a linear ODE $y''-y=0$ with general solution
$$y(s)=A\,e^{s}+B\,e^{-s},\qquad u(s)=y'(s)=A\,e^{s}-B\,e^{-s}.$$

### Imposing the initial (Cauchy) data

Parametrize the initial curve $\Gamma:\;(x,y,u)=(r,\,2r,\,0)$ at $s=0$. The init
ial conditions are
$$x(0)=r,\qquad y(0)=2r=A+B,\qquad u(0)=0=A-B.$$

From $A-B=0$ we get $A=B$; from $A+B=2r$ we get $A=B=r$. Hence along the charact
eristic issuing from $\Gamma$ at parameter $r$:
$$x(s)=r\,e^{-s},\qquad y(s)=r\bigl(e^{s}+e^{-s}\bigr),\qquad u(s)=r\bigl(e^{s}-
e^{-s}\bigr).$$

### Eliminating the parameters $(r,s)$

From $x=r\,e^{-s}$ we have $r=x\,e^{s}$. Substituting into the expression for $y
$:
$$y = x\,e^{s}\bigl(e^{s}+e^{-s}\bigr)=x\bigl(e^{2s}+1\bigr),$$
so
$$e^{2s}=\frac{y}{x}-1=\frac{y-x}{x}.$$

Likewise
$$u = r\bigl(e^{s}-e^{-s}\bigr)=x\,e^{s}\bigl(e^{s}-e^{-s}\bigr)=x\bigl(e^{2s}-1
\bigr).$$

Substituting $e^{2s}=\dfrac{y-x}{x}$:
$$u = x\!\left(\frac{y-x}{x}-1\right)=x\cdot\frac{y-x-x}{x}=y-2x.$$

### Verification

With $u(x,y)=y-2x$:
- $u_x=-2$, $\;u_y=1$.
- PDE: $-x\,u_x+u\,u_y = -x(-2)+(y-2x)(1)=2x+y-2x=y$. ✓
- Boundary: $u(x,2x)=2x-2x=0$. ✓

### Result

$$\boxed{\,u(x,y)=y-2x\,}$$
