## Solution

**Reformulation.** The "compatible" security IDs are precisely the units modulo
$n$, i.e., elements of the multiplicative group $(\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z})^*$. Th
e Symmetry Protocol requires that for all units $a, b$:
$$a \equiv b \pmod{n} \iff ab \equiv 1 \pmod{n}.$$

In group-theoretic terms, condition 1 says $a = b$ and condition 2 says $b = a^{
-1}$. The equivalence $a = b \iff b = a^{-1}$ for all units $a, b$ means:

- **(⇒)** Setting $b = a$: $a = a$ implies $a = a^{-1}$, so $a^2 \equiv 1 \pmod{
n}$ for every unit $a$.
- **(⇐)** If $b = a^{-1}$, then $a = b$, i.e., $a^{-1} = a$, which is again $a^2
 \equiv 1$.

Both directions reduce to the single requirement: **every unit $a$ modulo $n$ sa
tisfies $a^2 \equiv 1 \pmod{n}$**, i.e., the group $(\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z})^*$
has exponent dividing 2.

**Applying the Chinese Remainder Theorem.** Write $n = 2^{a_0} \prod_{i=1}^{k} p
_i^{a_i}$ with odd primes $p_i$. Then
$$(\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z})^* \cong (\mathbb{Z}/2^{a_0}\mathbb{Z})^* \times \prod
_{i=1}^{k} (\mathbb{Z}/p_i^{a_i}\mathbb{Z})^*.$$
The exponent of a direct product is the lcm of the exponents of the factors, so
we need each factor to have exponent dividing 2.

**Odd prime powers $p^a$:** $(\mathbb{Z}/p^a\mathbb{Z})^*$ is cyclic of order $\
phi(p^a) = p^{a-1}(p-1)$. A cyclic group has exponent dividing 2 iff its order d
ivides 2:
- $a = 1$: need $p - 1 \le 2$, giving $p = 3$ (since $p$ is odd). ✓
- $a \ge 2$: $p^{a-1}(p-1) \ge 3 \cdot 2 = 6 > 2$. ✗

So the only valid odd prime power is $3^1 = 3$.

**Powers of 2:**
- $a_0 = 0$: trivial group. ✓
- $a_0 = 1$: $(\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z})^*$ is trivial. ✓
- $a_0 = 2$: $(\mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z})^* \cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$. ✓
- $a_0 = 3$: $(\mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z})^* \cong \mathbb{Z}/2 \times \mathbb{Z}/2$
, exponent 2. ✓
- $a_0 \ge 4$: $(\mathbb{Z}/2^{a_0}\mathbb{Z})^* \cong \mathbb{Z}/2 \times \math
bb{Z}/2^{a_0-2}$, and $2^{a_0-2} \ge 4 > 2$. ✗

**Combining.** We have $n = 2^{a_0} \cdot 3^{b}$ with $a_0 \in \{0,1,2,3\}$ and
$b \in \{0,1\}$. Requiring $n \ge 2$:

| $b \backslash a_0$ | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1 | 2 | 4 | 8 |
| 1 | 3 | 6 | 12 | 24 |

Excluding $n = 1$, the valid values are $n \in \{2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24\}$.

**Verification.** For each of these, every unit squares to 1:
- $n=2$: units $\{1\}$; $n=3$: $\{1,2\}$, $2^2=4\equiv1$; $n=4$: $\{1,3\}$, $3^2
\equiv1$; $n=6$: $\{1,5\}$, $5^2\equiv1$; $n=8$: $\{1,3,5,7\}$, all square to 1;
 $n=12$: $\{1,5,7,11\}$, all square to 1; $n=24$: $\{1,5,7,11,13,17,19,23\}$, al
l square to 1. ✓

**Counterexamples for excluded values:** $n=5$: $2^2=4\not\equiv1$; $n=9$: $2^2=
4\not\equiv1$; $n=16$: $3^2=9\not\equiv1$; $n=48$: $3^2=9\not\equiv1$. ✗

**Sum.**
$$2 + 3 + 4 + 6 + 8 + 12 + 24 = 59.$$
