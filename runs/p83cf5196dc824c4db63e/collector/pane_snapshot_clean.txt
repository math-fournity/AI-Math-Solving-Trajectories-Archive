## Solution

**Setup.** We have 1994 points, no three collinear, partitioned into 83 groups,
each of size $\geq 3$. Within a group of size $n_i$, every triple of points form
s a (non-degenerate) triangle since no three points are collinear. Hence the tot
al number of triangles is

$$m(G) = \sum_{i=1}^{83} \binom{n_i}{3}, \qquad \sum_{i=1}^{83} n_i = 1994,\quad
 n_i \geq 3.$$

**Convexity.** The function $f(n)=\binom{n}{3}$ is convex on integers $n\geq 3$,
 since its discrete derivative $f(n)-f(n-1)=\binom{n-1}{2}=\frac{(n-1)(n-2)}{2}$
 is increasing in $n$.

**Equalization argument.** Suppose two groups have sizes $a \geq b+2$. Moving on
e point from the group of size $a$ to the group of size $b$ changes the total by

$$-\binom{a-1}{2} + \binom{b}{2} < 0$$

since $a-1 > b$. So the total strictly decreases. Repeating, the minimum is achi
eved when all group sizes differ by at most 1.

**Optimal distribution.** Divide $1994$ by $83$:

$$1994 = 83 \times 24 + 2.$$

So the optimal partition has **2 groups of size 25** and **81 groups of size 24*
* (check: $2\cdot 25 + 81\cdot 24 = 50 + 1944 = 1994$).

**Computation.**

$$\binom{25}{3} = \frac{25\cdot 24\cdot 23}{6} = 2300, \qquad \binom{24}{3} = \f
rac{24\cdot 23\cdot 22}{6} = 2024.$$

$$m_{\min} = 2\binom{25}{3} + 81\binom{24}{3} = 2(2300) + 81(2024) = 4600 + 1639
44 = 168544.$$

**Final Answer: $\boxed{168544}$**
