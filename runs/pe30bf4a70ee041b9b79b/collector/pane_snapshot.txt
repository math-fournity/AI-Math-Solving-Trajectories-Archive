## Solution

We interpret the equation as the heat equation Green's function problem on the h
alf-line:

$$G_t - a^2\,G_{xx} = \delta(x-\xi)\,\delta(t-\tau), \qquad x>0,\; x\neq\xi,$$

with boundary condition $G(0,t;\xi,\tau)=0$ and causality (retarded) condition $
G\equiv 0$ for $0<t<\tau$.

---

### Step 1. Free-space (full-line) fundamental solution

On the entire real line $-\infty<x<\infty$, the retarded Green's function for $G
_t-a^2G_{xx}=\delta(x-\xi)\delta(t-\tau)$ is the well-known heat kernel

$$G_0(x,t;\xi,\tau)=\frac{H(t-\tau)}{2a\sqrt{\pi\,(t-\tau)}}\;\exp\!\Bigl(-\frac
{(x-\xi)^2}{4a^2(t-\tau)}\Bigr),$$

where $H$ is the Heaviside step function. One verifies:

- **Causality:** $H(t-\tau)=0$ for $t<\tau$, so $G_0\equiv 0$ for $t<\tau$.
- **PDE for $t\neq\tau$:** For $t>\tau$ the function $\frac{1}{\sqrt{t-\tau}}\ex
p\!\bigl(-\frac{(x-\xi)^2}{4a^2(t-\tau)}\bigr)$ is a classical solution of the h
omogeneous heat equation $u_t-a^2u_{xx}=0$ (direct substitution).
- **Delta source:** Integrating the PDE over a small rectangle around $(\xi,\tau
)$ and using the fact that $G_0$ has total mass $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}G_0\,dx
= H(t-\tau)\to 1$ as $t\downarrow\tau$ confirms $\delta(x-\xi)\delta(t-\tau)$ on
 the right-hand side.

---

### Step 2. Method of images for the Dirichlet half-line

We need $G(0,t;\xi,\tau)=0$. The free-space kernel $G_0(x,t;\xi,\tau)$ does **no
t** vanish at $x=0$. To enforce the Dirichlet condition we use the **method of i
mages**: place a *negative* image source at the mirror point $x=-\xi$ (which lie
s outside the physical domain $x>0$). Define

$$\boxed{G(x,t;\xi,\tau)=\frac{H(t-\tau)}{2a\sqrt{\pi\,(t-\tau)}}\left[\exp\!\Bi
gl(-\frac{(x-\xi)^2}{4a^2(t-\tau)}\Bigr)-\exp\!\Bigl(-\frac{(x+\xi)^2}{4a^2(t-\t
au)}\Bigr)\right].}$$

Equivalently,

$$G = G_0(x,t;\xi,\tau) - G_0(x,t;-\xi,\tau).$$

---

### Step 3. Verification of all conditions

**(a) Causality ($G=0$ for $0<t<\tau$):**
Since $H(t-\tau)=0$ when $t<\tau$, both exponential terms are multiplied by zero
, hence $G\equiv 0$ for $t<\tau$. ✓

**(b) Boundary condition ($G=0$ at $x=0$):**
Setting $x=0$:

$$G(0,t;\xi,\tau)=\frac{H(t-\tau)}{2a\sqrt{\pi(t-\tau)}}\left[\exp\!\Bigl(-\frac
{\xi^2}{4a^2(t-\tau)}\Bigr)-\exp\!\Bigl(-\frac{\xi^2}{4a^2(t-\tau)}\Bigr)\right]
=0.$$

The two exponentials are identical at $x=0$ (since $(0-\xi)^2=(0+\xi)^2=\xi^2$),
 so they cancel exactly. ✓

**(c) Homogeneous PDE for $x>0$, $x\neq\xi$, $t\neq\tau$:**
Each term individually satisfies the heat equation $u_t-a^2u_{xx}=0$ for $t>\tau
$ (being a translate of the free-space kernel). Their difference therefore also
satisfies the homogeneous equation. Hence

$$G_t - a^2\,G_{xx}=0 \qquad \text{for } x>0,\;x\neq\xi,\;t\neq\tau.$$ ✓

**(d) Correct delta singularity at $(x,t)=(\xi,\tau)$:**
The first term $G_0(x,t;\xi,\tau)$ carries the singularity $\delta(x-\xi)\delta(
t-\tau)$ in the full domain. The second (image) term $G_0(x,t;-\xi,\tau)$ is smo
oth and bounded in a neighborhood of $x=\xi>0$ (its source is at $x=-\xi<0$, out
side the domain). Therefore, in the sense of distributions on $x>0$,

$$G_t - a^2\,G_{xx} = \delta(x-\xi)\,\delta(t-\tau) - 0 = \delta(x-\xi)\,\delta(
t-\tau).$$ ✓

**(e) Symmetry (reciprocity):**
Note $G(x,t;\xi,\tau)=G(\xi,t;x,\tau)$ since $(x-\xi)^2=(\xi-x)^2$ and $(x+\xi)^
2=(\xi+x)^2$, confirming the self-adjoint reciprocity of the Green's function. ✓

---

### Conclusion

The Green's function for the half-line heat equation with Dirichlet boundary con
dition at $x=0$ and retarded (causal) condition is

$$\boxed{G(x,t;\xi,\tau)=\frac{H(t-\tau)}{2a\sqrt{\pi\,(t-\tau)}}\left[\exp\!\Bi
gl(-\frac{(x-\xi)^2}{4a^2(t-\tau)}\Bigr)-\exp\!\Bigl(-\frac{(x+\xi)^2}{4a^2(t-\t
au)}\Bigr)\right],}$$

valid for $x>0$, $\xi>0$, $t>0$. The first term is the free-space heat kernel (d
irect source at $\xi$); the second is the image source at $-\xi$ with a minus si
gn that enforces $G=0$ at the boundary $x=0$.
