This is a linear isomorphism (indeed a $*$-isomorphism onto $M_n(\mathbb{C})$):
a bounded operator on $H$ is completely determined by its matrix entries $\langl

**Step 1. WOT equals the product topology on $\mathbb{C}^{n^2}$.**

The WOT is the coarsest topology on $B(H)$ making every functional $T\mapsto |\l
angle Tx,y\rangle|$ continuous, for $x,y\in H$. Since $x,y\in\operatorname{span}
\{e_1,\dots,e_n\}$, each $\langle Tx,y\rangle$ is a finite linear combination of

is exactly the initial topology induced by the coordinate maps $\Phi_{ij}$, i.e.

any norm on the finite-dimensional space $M_n(\mathbb{C})$.
