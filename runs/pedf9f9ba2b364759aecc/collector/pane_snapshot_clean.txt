## Setup and definitions

Let $K$ be a number field and $\mathscr{O}_K$ its ring of integers. Recall that
$\mathscr{O}_K$ is a **Dedekind domain**: it is Noetherian, integrally closed, a
nd every nonzero prime ideal is maximal (dimension $1$).

**Ideal class group.** The (ideal) class group $\mathrm{Cl}(\mathscr{O}_K)$ is t
he group of nonzero fractional $\mathscr{O}_K$-ideals modulo the subgroup of pri
ncipal fractional ideals $(x) = x\mathscr{O}_K$ for $x \in K^\times$.

**Class group of the scheme.** For a scheme $X = \operatorname{Spec} \mathscr{O}
_K$, the class group is the **Picard group**
$$\operatorname{Pic}(X) = \operatorname{Pic}(\operatorname{Spec} \mathscr{O}_K),
$$
i.e. the group of invertible $\mathscr{O}_X$-modules (line bundles) under tensor
 product, up to isomorphism. Since $X$ is affine, this coincides with the Picard

$$\operatorname{Pic}(\operatorname{Spec} \mathscr{O}_K) \cong \operatorname{Pic}
(\mathscr{O}_K),$$
the group of finitely generated projective $\mathscr{O}_K$-modules of rank $1$ m
odulo isomorphism.

The claim is that there is a natural group isomorphism
$$\operatorname{Pic}(\operatorname{Spec} \mathscr{O}_K) \;\cong\; \mathrm{Cl}(\m

## The isomorphism

### Step 1. Every nonzero fractional ideal is a projective rank-$1$ module.

Let $\mathfrak a \subseteq K$ be a nonzero fractional $\mathscr{O}_K$-ideal. Bec
ause $\mathscr{O}_K$ is a Dedekind domain, every nonzero fractional ideal is **i
nvertible**: there exists a fractional ideal $\mathfrak a^{-1}$ with $\mathfrak
a \mathfrak a^{-1} = \mathscr{O}_K$. In particular $\mathfrak a$ is a finitely g
enerated $\mathscr{O}_K$-module (Noetherian) and, being invertible, is projectiv
e of rank $1$: after tensoring with $K$,
$$\mathfrak a \otimes_{\mathscr{O}_K} K \cong K,$$
so $\operatorname{rank}_{\mathscr{O}_K} \mathfrak a = 1$.

Thus each nonzero fractional ideal gives an element of $\operatorname{Pic}(\math
scr{O}_K)$, i.e. of $\operatorname{Pic}(\operatorname{Spec} \mathscr{O}_K)$.

### Step 2. Every projective rank-$1$ module is isomorphic to a fractional ideal
.

Conversely, let $P$ be a finitely generated projective $\mathscr{O}_K$-module of
 rank $1$. Pick any nonzero $\mathscr{O}_K$-linear functional $\varphi : P \to K
$ (e.g. via $P \otimes K \cong K$). Then
$$\mathfrak a := \varphi(P) \subseteq K$$
is a nonzero finitely generated $\mathscr{O}_K$-submodule of $K$, hence a fracti
onal ideal, and $\varphi : P \xrightarrow{\;\sim\;} \mathfrak a$ is an isomorphi
sm. So every element of $\operatorname{Pic}(\mathscr{O}_K)$ is represented by a
fractional ideal.

### Step 3. Two fractional ideals are isomorphic iff they differ by a principal
ideal.

Let $\mathfrak a, \mathfrak b$ be nonzero fractional ideals. An $\mathscr{O}_K$-
module isomorphism $f : \mathfrak a \xrightarrow{\sim} \mathfrak b$ extends (aft
er tensoring with $K$) to a $K$-linear automorphism of the one-dimensional $K$-v
ector space $K$, i.e. multiplication by some $x \in K^\times$. Hence
$$\mathfrak b = x\,\mathfrak a = (x)\mathfrak a.$$
Conversely, multiplication by $x \in K^\times$ gives an isomorphism $\mathfrak a
 \xrightarrow{\sim} (x)\mathfrak a$. Therefore
$$\mathfrak a \cong \mathfrak b \;\text{as } \mathscr{O}_K\text{-modules} \quad\
Longleftrightarrow\quad \mathfrak b = (x)\mathfrak a \text{ for some } x \in K^\
times.$$

## Summary

The identification rests on three facts specific to $\mathscr{O}_K$ being a Dede
kind domain:

1. **Invertible ideals = projective rank-$1$ modules**, so fractional ideals par
ametrize line bundles on $\operatorname{Spec} \mathscr{O}_K$.
2. **Isomorphism classes of such modules = ideal classes**, since $\mathfrak a \
cong \mathfrak b \iff \mathfrak b = (x)\mathfrak a$.
3. **Tensor product = ideal multiplication**, matching the two group laws.

Putting these together yields a natural group isomorphism
$$\boxed{\;\operatorname{Pic}(\operatorname{Spec} \mathscr{O}_K) \;\xrightarrow{
\;\sim\;}\; \mathrm{Cl}(\mathscr{O}_K),\qquad [\widetilde{\mathfrak a}] \longmap
sto [\mathfrak a]\;}$$
with inverse $[\mathfrak a] \mapsto [\widetilde{\mathfrak a}]$. This is the desi
red identification of the ideal class group of $\mathscr{O}_K$ with the class gr
oup (Picard group) of the scheme $\operatorname{Spec} \mathscr{O}_K$.
