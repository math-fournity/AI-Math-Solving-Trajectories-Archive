# Problem

A shipping logistics hub manages a square grid of 100 loading docks by 100 storage bays, creating a total of 10,000 designated storage zones. Each zone contains a specialized battery that can hold a charge state ranging from -1 units (fully discharged) to 1 unit (fully charged). 

The facility operates on a strictly neutral energy balance, meaning the sum of the charge states across all 10,000 zones is exactly 0. 

The facility's safety protocol requires a minimum level of activity in every sector. For every single row of 100 zones, the absolute value of the sum of their charges must be at least $c$. Similarly, for every single column of 100 zones, the absolute value of the sum of their charges must be at least $c$.

Determine the maximum possible value that the constant $c$ can take.
