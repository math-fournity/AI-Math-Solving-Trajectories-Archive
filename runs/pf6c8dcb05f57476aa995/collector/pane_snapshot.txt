Looking at this problem, I need to find how many slots remain after deleting all
 rows and columns containing perfect square slots in a 2013×2013 grid.

## Setup

Slot in row $r$, column $c$ (1-indexed) has number $2013(r-1)+c$. A perfect squa
re $k^2$ (for $k=1,\dots,2013$) sits in row $\lceil k^2/2013\rceil$ and column $
((k^2-1)\bmod 2013)+1$.

If $R$ rows and $C$ columns are deleted, remaining slots $= (2013-R)(2013-C)$.

## Counting Deleted Columns

The set of deleted columns equals the set of distinct values of $k^2 \bmod 2013$
 for $k=1,\dots,2013$ (a complete residue system). This is the set of quadratic
residues mod 2013 (including 0).

Factor: $2013 = 3 \times 11 \times 61$. By CRT:

| Modulus | QRs (incl. 0) | Count |
|---------|---------------|-------|
| 3 | $\{0,1\}$ | 2 |
| 11 | $\{0,1,3,4,5,9\}$ | 6 |
| 61 | $(61+1)/2$ | 31 |

$$C = 2 \times 6 \times 31 = 372$$

Remaining columns $= 2013 - 372 = 1641$.

## Counting Deleted Rows

Row $r$ is hit iff the interval $(2013(r-1),\, 2013r]$ contains a perfect square
, i.e., $\lfloor\sqrt{2013r}\rfloor > \lfloor\sqrt{2013(r-1)}\rfloor$.

Equivalently, a value $m \in \{1,\dots,2013\}$ is **achieved** iff there exists
a multiple of 2013 in $[m^2, (m+1)^2)$ (with the multiple being $2013j$, $j \in
\{1,\dots,2013\}$). The number of hit rows equals the number of achieved $m$.

**Case 1: $m \geq 1007$.** The interval $[m^2,(m+1)^2)$ has length $2m+1 \geq 20
15 > 2013$, so it must contain at least one multiple of 2013. All achieved. This
 gives $2013 - 1007 + 1 = 1007$ values.

**Case 2: $m = 1006$.** Interval length $= 2013$, exactly one multiple. Indeed $
2013 \times 503 = 1012539 \in [1006^2, 1007^2) = [1012036, 1014049)$. Achieved.
($1$ value.)

**Case 3: $1 \leq m \leq 1005$.** Interval length $2m+1 \leq 2011 < 2013$, so at
 most one multiple per interval. The multiples of 2013 in $[1, 1006^2) = [1, 101
2036)$ are $2013, 2\cdot2013, \dots, 502\cdot2013 = 1010526$ (since $503\cdot201
3 = 1012539 \geq 1012036$). That's **502 multiples**, each landing in a distinct
 interval (since interval length $< 2013$). So **502 values** of $m$ are achieve
d.

**Total hit rows:**
$$R = 502 + 1 + 1007 = 1510$$

Remaining rows $= 2013 - 1510 = 503$.

## Verification of the Method

*Small check ($n=5$):* Perfect squares 1, 4, 9, 16, 25 land in rows {1, 2, 4, 5}
 (row 3 missed) and columns {1, 4, 5}. Formula gives: multiples of 5 in $[1,25]$
: 5 values → achieved $m$: $\{2,3,4,5\}$ → 4 hit rows ✓. QRs mod 5 = 3 → 3 colum
ns ✓. Remaining $= 1 \times 2 = 2$ ✓ (matches direct enumeration).

## Final Computation

$$\text{Remaining slots} = 503 \times 1641 = 825423$$

$$503 \times 1600 = 804800, \quad 503 \times 41 = 20623, \quad 804800 + 20623 =
825423$$

## Answer

$$\boxed{825423}$$
