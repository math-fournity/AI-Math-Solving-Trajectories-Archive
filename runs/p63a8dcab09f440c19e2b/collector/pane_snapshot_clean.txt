## Proof

**Setup.** The 10 containers correspond to the 10 edges of the complete graph $K
_5$ on vertices $\{1,2,3,4,5\}$. Assigning container $\{i,j\}$ to bay $i$ or bay
 $j$ is equivalent to **orienting** the edge toward the chosen endpoint. The num
ber of containers in bay $k$ equals the **indegree** $d(k)$ of vertex $k$. The p
lan is optimal iff $d(1) > d(k)$ for all $k \in \{2,3,4,5\}$.

Since $\sum_{k=1}^{5} d(k) = 10$ and vertex 1 has degree 4 in $K_5$, we have $d(
1) \le 4$. Also $d(1) \ge 3$, because if $d(1) \le 2$ then the other four indegr
ees sum to $\ge 8$ with each $\le 1$, giving max total $4 < 8$, impossible. So $
d(1) \in \{3, 4\}$.

---

**Case 1: $d(1) = 4$.** All 4 edges incident to vertex 1 are oriented toward 1.
The remaining 6 edges form $K_4$ on $\{2,3,4,5\}$, oriented freely. Since the ma
ximum indegree in $K_4$ is 3 $< 4$, every orientation works.

$$\text{Count} = 2^6 = 64.$$

---

**Case 2: $d(1) = 3$.** Exactly 3 of the 4 edges incident to vertex 1 point towa
rd 1; the remaining edge points toward some neighbor $v \in \{2,3,4,5\}$ (4 choi
ces). This contributes $+1$ to $d(v)$.

Let $e(k)$ be the indegree of vertex $k$ from the $K_4$ edges (among $\{2,3,4,5\
}$), with $\sum e(k) = 6$. Then:
- $d(v) = e(v) + 1 \le 2 \implies e(v) \le 1$,
- $d(k) = e(k) \le 2$ for $k \ne v$.

We count valid $K_4$ orientations per choice of $v$:

- **$e(v) = 0$:** the others are $(2,2,2)$. This is score type $(0,2,2,2)$: the
vertex with indegree 0 beats all others; the remaining three form a 3-cycle (2 o
rientations). Count $= 2$.

- **$e(v) = 1$:** the others sum to 5 with each $\le 2$, giving arrangements $(2
,2,1)$ in 3 orderings. Each is score type $(1,1,2,2)$. For a specific labeled as
signment of this type, the number of tournaments is 4 (verified by direct enumer
ation of the 6 binary edge variables under the 4 indegree equations). So count $
= 3 \times 4 = 12$.

Total per $v$: $2 + 12 = 14$. Over 4 choices of $v$:

$$\text{Count} = 4 \times 14 = 56.$$

---

**Verification of the $(1,1,2,2)$ count.** For vertices $a,b,c,d$ with target in
degrees $a{=}1, b{=}1, c{=}2, d{=}2$, setting up the 6 binary orientation variab
les and solving the 4 linear indegree equations yields exactly 4 solutions (enum
erated over $x_{ab} \in \{0,1\}$, each giving 2 solutions). This confirms 4 tour
naments per specific labeled assignment. The four score-sequence types of $K_4$
tournaments account for $24 + 8 + 8 + 24 = 64 = 2^6$ total, cross-checking all c
ounts.

---

**Total:**

$$64 + 56 = \boxed{120}.$$
