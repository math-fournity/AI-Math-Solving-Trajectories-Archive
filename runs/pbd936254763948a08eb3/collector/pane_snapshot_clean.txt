## Proof

**Setup.** In a preadditive category $\mathscr{C}$, every hom-set $\mathscr{C}(X
,Y)$ carries the structure of an abelian group, and composition is $\mathbb{Z}$-
bilinear. Let $0$ be a zero object (both initial and terminal). For objects $A,
B$, the *zero morphism* $f: A \to B$ is defined as the composite
$$f \;=\; \big(A \xrightarrow{z} 0 \xrightarrow{w} B\big),$$
where $z$ is the unique morphism $A \to 0$ (since $0$ is terminal) and $w$ is th
e unique morphism $0 \to B$ (since $0$ is initial). We must show $f$ equals the
additive identity $0_{A,B}$ of the abelian group $\mathscr{C}(A,B)$.

**Step 1. The morphisms through $0$ are the zero elements of their respective ho
m-groups.**

Since $0$ is terminal, $\mathscr{C}(A,0)$ has exactly one element, namely $z$. A
 one-element abelian group is the trivial group, so its unique element is its ow
n additive identity: $z = 0_{A,0}$. Likewise, since $0$ is initial, $\mathscr{C}
(0,B)$ has exactly one element $w$, and $w = 0_{0,B}$.

**Step 2. Composition with a zero element yields a zero element.**

We use the $\mathbb{Z}$-bilinearity of composition. Consider $w \circ z = 0_{0,B
} \circ 0_{A,0}$. By bilinearity (distributivity on the left),
$$(0_{0,B} + 0_{0,B}) \circ 0_{A,0} \;=\; 0_{0,B}\circ 0_{A,0} \;+\; 0_{0,B}\cir
c 0_{A,0}.$$
Since $0_{0,B} + 0_{0,B} = 0_{0,B}$ in the group $\mathscr{C}(0,B)$, the left si
de equals $0_{0,B}\circ 0_{A,0}$. Hence
$$0_{0,B}\circ 0_{A,0} \;=\; 0_{0,B}\circ 0_{A,0} \;+\; 0_{0,B}\circ 0_{A,0}.$$
This says the element $0_{0,B}\circ 0_{A,0} \in \mathscr{C}(A,B)$ is idempotent
under addition. In an abelian group the only additively idempotent element is th
e identity, so
$$0_{0,B}\circ 0_{A,0} \;=\; 0_{A,B}.$$

**Step 3. Conclusion.**

Combining Steps 1 and 2,
$$f \;=\; w \circ z \;=\; 0_{0,B}\circ 0_{A,0} \;=\; 0_{A,B}.$$

Therefore the unique zero morphism $f: A \to B$ **is** the zero element of the a
belian group $\mathscr{C}(A,B)$. $\blacksquare$
