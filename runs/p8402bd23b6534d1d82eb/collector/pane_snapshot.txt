orthonormal basis ${e_n}{n=0}^{\infty}$. The unilateral shift $S \in \mathbb{B}

an isometry: $|Sx| = |x|$ for all $x$, hence $|S| = 1$.

We prove $d(S, \mathbb{K}(\mathcal{H})) = 1$ by establishing matching upper and
lower bounds.

Upper bound: $d(S, \mathbb{K}(\mathcal{H})) \leq 1$

Taking $K = 0$ (which is compact), $$d(S, \mathbb{K}(\mathcal{H})) \leq |S - 0|
= |S| = 1.$$

Lower bound: $d(S, \mathbb{K}(\mathcal{H})) \geq 1$

We show $|S - K| \geq 1$ for every compact operator $K$.

Let $K \in \mathbb{K}(\mathcal{H})$ be arbitrary. Consider the sequence of unit
vectors ${e_n}{n \geq 0}$. Since $K$ is compact, the image sequence ${Ke_n}$ has
a norm-convergent subsequence. That is, there exist indices $n_j \to \infty$ and
a vector $y \in \mathcal{H}$ such that $$Ke{n_j} \to y \quad \text{in norm.}$$

Now evaluate $(S - K)$ on this subsequence: $$(S - K)e_{n_j} = Se_{n_j} -
Ke_{n_j} = e_{n_j + 1} - Ke_{n_j}.$$

Compute the squared norm: $$|(S-K)e_{n_j}|^2 = |e_{n_j+1}|^2 -
2\operatorname{Re}\langle e_{n_j+1},, Ke_{n_j}\rangle + |Ke_{n_j}|^2.$$

We analyze each term as $j \to \infty$:

  1. First term: $|e_{n_j+1}|^2 = 1$ for all $j$.
  2. Cross term: $\langle e_{n_j+1},, Ke_{n_j}\rangle \to 0$. Indeed, since $n_j
  \to \infty$, the basis vectors $e_{n_j+1} \to 0$ weakly (for any fixed $x$, $

  $\bigl|\langle e_{n_j+1},, Ke_{n_j}\rangle\bigr| \leq \bigl|\langle
  e_{n_j+1},, y\rangle\bigr| + \bigl|\langle e_{n_j+1},, Ke_{n_j} -
  y\rangle\bigr| \leq \bigl|\langle e_{n_j+1},, y\rangle\bigr| + |Ke_{n_j} -
