## Solution

The domain is the disk $\Omega: x^2+y^2<4$ (radius $R=2$), and we seek the harmo
nic function $u$ on $\Omega$ with boundary value $u=1+2x^2$ on $\partial\Omega$
(the circle $x^2+y^2=4$).

**Finding a harmonic polynomial extension.** Since the boundary data is a polyno
mial, we look for a degree-2 harmonic polynomial matching it on the boundary. Le
t

$$u = A + Bx^2 + Cy^2.$$

Harmonicity requires $\Delta u = 2B + 2C = 0$, so $C = -B$, giving

$$u = A + B(x^2 - y^2).$$

On the boundary $x^2+y^2=4$, we have $y^2 = 4-x^2$, hence

$$u\big|_{\partial\Omega} = A + B\bigl(x^2-(4-x^2)\bigr) = (A-4B) + 2Bx^2.$$

Matching this to $1+2x^2$:
- $2B = 2 \Rightarrow B = 1$,
- $A - 4B = 1 \Rightarrow A = 5$.

Therefore $u(x,y) = 5 + x^2 - y^2$.

**Verification.** $\Delta u = 2 - 2 = 0$ ✓. On $x^2+y^2=4$: $u = 5 + x^2 - (4-x^
2) = 1 + 2x^2$ ✓. By uniqueness of the Dirichlet problem, this is the solution.

**Evaluating at $(0,1)$:**

$$u(0,1) = 5 + 0^2 - 1^2 = 4.$$

$$\boxed{u(0,1)=4}$$
