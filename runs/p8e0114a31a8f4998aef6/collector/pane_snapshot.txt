ell^1 \subset \ell^\infty$), this is well-defined and is a metric on $\ell^1$. T
hus $(\ell^1, d_*)$ is the set $\ell^1$ equipped with the sup-norm metric, i.e.,

$$\iota : (\ell^1, \|\cdot\|_\infty) \hookrightarrow (\ell^\infty, \|\cdot\|_\in

**Key inclusions.** We have the chain
$$c_{00} \subset \ell^1 \subset c_0 \subset \ell^\infty.$$
The inclusion $\ell^1 \subset c_0$ holds because absolute summability forces $|a
_n| \to 0$.

### Step 3. $c_0$ is complete

$(\ell^\infty, \|\cdot\|_\infty)$ is a Banach space. The subspace $c_0$ is close

### Step 4. $\ell^1$ is dense in $c_0$ (sup-norm)

So $c_{00}$ (hence $\ell^1$) is dense in $(c_0, \|\cdot\|_\infty)$.
