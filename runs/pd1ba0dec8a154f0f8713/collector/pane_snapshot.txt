## Solution

**Setup.** A tetrahedron with three pairs of equal opposite edges ($AB=CD$, $AC=
BD$, $AD=BC$) is a *disphenoid* (isosceles tetrahedron). Such a tetrahedron admi
ts the symmetric coordinate parametrization:

$$A=(x,y,z),\quad B=(x,-y,-z),\quad C=(-x,y,-z),\quad D=(-x,-y,z).$$

A direct check confirms the opposite-edge equalities:
- $AB = CD = 2\sqrt{y^2+z^2}$
- $AC = BD = 2\sqrt{x^2+z^2}$
- $AD = BC = 2\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$

**Determining $x^2,y^2,z^2$.** From the given values $a=\sqrt{13},\, b=\sqrt{15}
,\, c=\sqrt{20}$:

$$y^2+z^2=\tfrac{13}{4},\qquad x^2+z^2=\tfrac{15}{4},\qquad x^2+y^2=\tfrac{20}{4
}=5.$$

Adding all three: $2(x^2+y^2+z^2)=\tfrac{13}{4}+\tfrac{15}{4}+5=\tfrac{28}{4}+5=
12$, so $x^2+y^2+z^2=6$. Subtracting each equation:

$$x^2=6-\tfrac{13}{4}=\tfrac{11}{4},\qquad y^2=6-\tfrac{15}{4}=\tfrac{9}{4},\qqu
ad z^2=6-5=1.$$

**Equation of face $ABC$.** With $\vec{AB}=(0,-2y,-2z)$ and $\vec{AC}=(-2x,0,-2z
)$, the normal is

$$\vec{AB}\times\vec{AC}=4\,(yz,\,xz,\,-xy).$$

The plane through $A$ is $yz\,X+xz\,Y-xy\,Z=xyz$.

**Height from $D=(-x,-y,z)$.** The distance from $D$ to this plane is

$$h=\frac{|yz(-x)+xz(-y)-xy(z)-xyz|}{\sqrt{y^2z^2+x^2z^2+x^2y^2}}=\frac{4\,|xyz|
}{\sqrt{y^2z^2+x^2z^2+x^2y^2}}.$$

Hence

$$h^2=\frac{16\,x^2y^2z^2}{y^2z^2+x^2z^2+x^2y^2}.$$

**Substituting values:**

$$x^2y^2z^2=\tfrac{11}{4}\cdot\tfrac{9}{4}\cdot 1=\tfrac{99}{16},$$

$$y^2z^2+x^2z^2+x^2y^2=\tfrac{9}{4}+\tfrac{11}{4}+\tfrac{99}{16}=\tfrac{36+44+99
}{16}=\tfrac{179}{16}.$$

Therefore

$$h^2=\frac{16\cdot\tfrac{99}{16}}{\tfrac{179}{16}}=\frac{99}{\tfrac{179}{16}}=\
frac{1584}{179}.$$

**Verification via volume.** The volume is $V=\tfrac{1}{6}|\det[\vec{AB},\vec{AC
},\vec{AD}]|=\tfrac{8}{3}|xyz|$, and the area of face $ABC$ is $\tfrac{1}{2}|\ve
c{AB}\times\vec{AC}|=2\sqrt{\tfrac{179}{16}}=\tfrac{\sqrt{179}}{2}$. Then $h=\tf
rac{3V}{\text{Area}}=\tfrac{16|xyz|}{\sqrt{179}}$, giving $h^2=\tfrac{256\,x^2y^
2z^2}{179}=\tfrac{256\cdot 99/16}{179}=\tfrac{1584}{179}$, confirming the result
.

$$\boxed{h^2=\dfrac{1584}{179}}$$
