# Problem

In a futuristic circular space station, there are exactly 21 docking ports arranged at perfectly regular intervals around the perimeter. The station commander decides to assign special designations to some of these ports: $m$ ports are designated as "Red Sector" hubs, and $n$ ports are designated as "Blue Sector" hubs.

A "Red Fleet Formation" is defined as any triangular flight path formed by connecting three distinct Red Sector hubs. Similarly, a "Blue Fleet Formation" is any triangular flight path formed by connecting three distinct Blue Sector hubs.

Two fleet formations are considered "identical in shape" if the triangles formed by their paths are congruent.

Suppose the commander designates exactly $m = 6$ ports as Red Sector hubs. What is the smallest positive integer $n$ such that, regardless of which specific ports are chosen for the Red and Blue sectors, there must always exist at least one Red Fleet Formation and at least one Blue Fleet Formation that are identical in shape?
