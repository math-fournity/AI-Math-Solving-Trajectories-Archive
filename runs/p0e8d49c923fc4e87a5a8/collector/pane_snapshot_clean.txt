## Proof

**Notation.** $PSU_3 = SU_3(\mathbb{C})/Z(SU_3)$ where $Z(SU_3) = \mu_3 = \{\zet
a I : \zeta^3 = 1\}$ is the center (cyclic of order 3). We seek the smallest $k$
 such that $PSU_3$ embeds faithfully into $GL_k(\mathbb{C})$.

**Step 1: Classify which representations descend to $PSU_3$.**

The irreducible complex representations of $SU_3$ are parametrized by highest we
ights $m\omega_1 + n\omega_2$ with $m, n \geq 0$, denoted $V(m,n)$, of dimension
$$\dim V(m,n) = \frac{(m+1)(n+1)(m+n+2)}{2}.$$

The central element $\zeta I \in \mu_3$ acts on $V(m,n)$ by the scalar $\zeta^{m
-n}$ (verified: on the defining rep $V(1,0)$ it acts by $\zeta$; on the dual $V(
0,1)$ by $\zeta^{-1}$). Therefore $V(m,n)$ descends to a representation of $PSU_
3$ (i.e., $\mu_3$ acts trivially) if and only if
$$m \equiv n \pmod{3}.$$

**Step 2: Faithfulness on $PSU_3$.**

$SU_3$ is a compact, simply-connected, simple Lie group. Its normal subgroups ar
e exactly $\{e\}$, $\mu_3$, and $SU_3$ (since $PSU_3 = SU_3/\mu_3$ is simple). T
he kernel of any irreducible representation is normal, hence is one of these thr
ee. For a nontrivial $V(m,n)$ with $m \equiv n \pmod{3}$, the center $\mu_3$ lie
s in the kernel but the representation is nontrivial, so the kernel is exactly $
\mu_3$. Thus every nontrivial irreducible representation with $m \equiv n \pmod{
3}$ is **faithful on $PSU_3$**.

**Step 3: Find the minimum dimension.**

We need the smallest $\dim V(m,n)$ with $(m,n) \neq (0,0)$ and $m \equiv n \pmod
{3}$. Candidates:

| $(m,n)$ | $m-n$ | $\dim V(m,n)$ |
|---------|-------|---------------|
| $(1,1)$ | $0$   | $\frac{2\cdot 2\cdot 4}{2} = 8$ |
| $(3,0)$ | $3$   | $\frac{4\cdot 1\cdot 5}{2} = 10$ |
| $(0,3)$ | $-3$  | $10$ |
| $(2,2)$ | $0$   | $\frac{3\cdot 3\cdot 6}{2} = 27$ |

The minimum is achieved at $(m,n) = (1,1)$, giving the **adjoint representation*
* of dimension $8 = \dim SU_3$.

**Step 4: Reducible representations cannot do better.**

Any representation of $PSU_3$ lifts to a representation of $SU_3$ that is a dire
ct sum of irreducibles $V(m_i, n_i)$ with each $m_i \equiv n_i \pmod{3}$. To be
faithful on $PSU_3$, at least one summand must be nontrivial (otherwise the whol
e representation is trivial). The smallest nontrivial summand has dimension $\ge
q 8$, so the total dimension is $\geq 8$.

**Conclusion.** The adjoint representation $V(1,1)$ of $SU_3$ has kernel exactly
 $\mu_3$ and dimension $8$, yielding a faithful embedding $PSU_3 \hookrightarrow
 GL_8(\mathbb{C})$. No representation of smaller dimension is faithful on $PSU_3
$.

$$\boxed{k = 8}$$
