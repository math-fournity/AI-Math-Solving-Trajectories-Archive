# Problem

In the city of Gridhaven, a district is laid out in a perfect $5 \times 5$ square of construction plots. Each of the 25 plots can be developed in one of two ways: it can either be occupied by a "Solid Obstacle" or left as an "Open Plaza." 

A district configuration is classified as "Logistically Connected" if a pedestrian can travel from the northwest corner plot to the southeast corner plot by moving only between adjacent Open Plazas (up, down, left, or right). For a district to meet this classification, both the northwest corner plot and the southeast corner plot must themselves be Open Plazas.

Let $N$ represent the total number of distinct "Logistically Connected" configurations possible for this $5 \times 5$ district.

Provide an estimate $E$ for the value of $N$. Your score will be calculated as $\lfloor 20 \min(\frac{N}{E}, \frac{E}{N})^2 \rfloor$. What is your estimate $E$?
