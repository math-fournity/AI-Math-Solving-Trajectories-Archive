**Sampler algorithm:**
1. For each group $j = 1, \ldots, t$:
   - (a) Pick a random seed $s_j \in \{0,1\}^{2n}$ specifying a pairwise indepen
dent hash function $h_j : [m] \to \{0,1\}^n$ via the standard construction over

   - (c) Compute the group average $A_j = \frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^{m} v(h_j(i))$.
2. Output $\operatorname{median}(A_1, \ldots, A_t)$.

**Correctness analysis.**

$\operatorname{Var}(v) \leq 1/4$:

$$\operatorname{Var}(A_j) = \frac{\operatorname{Var}(v)}{m} \leq \frac{1}{4m} \l
eq \frac{\varepsilon^2}{32}.$$

By Chebyshev's inequality:

$$\Pr\!\bigl[|A_j - \bar{v}| > \varepsilon\bigr] \leq \frac{\operatorname{Var}(A
_j)}{\varepsilon^2} \leq \frac{1}{32}.$$

*The median amplifies success probability.* The $t$ groups use independent seeds

\mathbf{1}[|A_j - \bar{v}| > \varepsilon]$, so $\mathbb{E}[B_j] \leq 1/32$. The
median fails only if more than half the groups fail, i.e., $\sum_{j=1}^t B_j > t

$$\Pr\!\left[\sum_{j=1}^t B_j > \frac{t}{2}\right] \leq \exp\!\left(-\frac{t}{2}
\cdot D\!\left(\tfrac{1}{2} \,\Big\|\, \tfrac{1}{32}\right)\right) \leq e^{-\Ome

**Complexity analysis.**

**Sample complexity:** The sampler makes $t \cdot m$ oracle calls to $v$:

$$t \cdot m = O\!\left(\frac{\ln(1/\delta)}{\varepsilon^2}\right).$$

$$\text{Randomness} = 2n \cdot t = O\!\left(n \ln(1/\delta)\right) \text{ bits.}
