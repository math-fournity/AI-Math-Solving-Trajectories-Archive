## Proof

We evaluate $\displaystyle L = \lim_{x\to +\infty}\frac{\zeta\!\left(1+\tfrac{1}
{x}\right)}{\Gamma(x)}$ by analyzing numerator and denominator separately as $x\
to+\infty$.

### The numerator: $\zeta(1+1/x)$

The Riemann zeta function has a simple pole at $s=1$ with Laurent expansion

$$\zeta(s) = \frac{1}{s-1} + \gamma + O(s-1) \quad (s\to 1),$$

where $\gamma$ is the Euler–Mascheroni constant. Setting $s = 1 + 1/x$ (so $s-1
= 1/x \to 0^+$ as $x\to+\infty$),

$$\zeta\!\left(1+\tfrac{1}{x}\right) = x + \gamma + O\!\left(\tfrac{1}{x}\right)
.$$

In particular $\zeta(1+1/x) \sim x$, i.e. it grows **linearly** in $x$.

### The denominator: $\Gamma(x)$

By Stirling's formula,

$$\Gamma(x) \sim \sqrt{\frac{2\pi}{x}}\left(\frac{x}{e}\right)^{x} \quad (x\to+\
infty),$$

which grows **super-exponentially**: $\Gamma(x) \geq (x/e)^{x-1}$ for all suffic
iently large $x$.

### The ratio

Combining the two estimates, for large $x$ there is a constant $C>0$ with

$$0 \;<\; \frac{\zeta(1+1/x)}{\Gamma(x)} \;\leq\; \frac{C\,x}{\sqrt{2\pi/x}\,(x/
e)^x} \;=\; \frac{C\,x^{3/2}\,e^{x}}{x^{x}\sqrt{2\pi}} \;=\; \frac{C\,x^{3/2}}{\
sqrt{2\pi}}\cdot\frac{e^{x}}{x^{x}}.$$

Since $x^{x} = e^{x\ln x}$, we have $\dfrac{e^{x}}{x^{x}} = e^{x - x\ln x} = e^{
-x(\ln x - 1)} \to 0$ super-exponentially as $x\to+\infty$ (because $\ln x - 1 \
to +\infty$). The polynomial prefactor $C\,x^{3/2}/\sqrt{2\pi}$ cannot compensat
e for this super-exponential decay. Hence

$$\frac{\zeta(1+1/x)}{\Gamma(x)} \;\longrightarrow\; 0 \quad (x\to+\infty).$$

### Conclusion

$$\boxed{\displaystyle \lim_{x\to +\infty}\frac{\zeta\!\left(1+\frac{1}{x}\right
)}{\Gamma(x)} = 0.}$$
