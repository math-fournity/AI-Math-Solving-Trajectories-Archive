# Problem

In the city of Arithmos, a series of $n$ storage lockers are arranged in a single row, numbered consecutively from $1$ to $n$. A logistics company decides to organize these lockers by assigning each one a specific security clearance level: Level Red, Level Blue, or Level Green. Every single locker must be assigned exactly one of these three levels.

The company's safety protocol requires that there must always be at least two distinct lockers, $a$ and $b$, that share the same security clearance level, such that the physical distance between them (calculated as the absolute difference $|a - b|$) is equal to a perfect square (e.g., $1, 4, 9, 16, \dots$).

What is the smallest possible number of lockers $n$ that ensures this protocol is satisfied regardless of how the three security levels are distributed across the lockers?
