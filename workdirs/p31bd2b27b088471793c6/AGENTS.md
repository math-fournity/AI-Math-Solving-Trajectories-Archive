# Problem

A specialized satellite mapping project is tracking a laser-relay system between three research stations: Alpha ($A$), Bravo ($B$), and Charlie ($C$). These stations are positioned such that the paths $AB$ and $AC$ are equal in length and meet at a perfect $90^\circ$ angle at station $A$.

A surveying drone follows a precise elliptical flight path that passes directly through stations $A$ and $B$. The signal processing center for this drone is located at station $C$, which serves as one of the two focal points of the elliptical orbit. The second focal point, where the drone’s backup transmitter is located, is situated somewhere within the triangular region formed by the three stations.

The orbital eccentricity of the drone's path is a constant value that can be expressed in the form $\sqrt{k} - \sqrt{m}$, where $k$ and $m$ are positive integers.

Find the value of $k + m$.
