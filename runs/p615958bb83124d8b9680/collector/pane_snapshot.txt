# Derivation of the Linearized Einstein Tensor

## Setup

We work to first order in the perturbation $h_{\mu\nu}$ on the flat background $
\eta_{\mu\nu}$ (signature convention irrelevant to the algebra; we keep $\eta_{\
mu\nu} = \mathrm{diag}(-1,+1,+1,+1)$ or any fixed signature). The full metric is

$$g_{\mu\nu} = \eta_{\mu\nu} + h_{\mu\nu}, \qquad |h_{\mu\nu}| \ll 1.$$

All indices on $h$ are raised/lowered with $\eta$. Define the trace

$$h := \eta^{\mu\nu} h_{\mu\nu} = h^\mu_{\ \mu}.$$

**Inverse metric.** Expanding $g^{\mu\nu}g_{\nu\lambda} = \delta^\mu_\lambda$ to
 first order gives

$$g^{\mu\nu} = \eta^{\mu\nu} - h^{\mu\nu} + O(h^2).$$

---

## Step 1 — Christoffel symbols

$$\Gamma^\lambda_{\ \mu\nu} = \tfrac{1}{2} g^{\lambda\sigma}\bigl(\partial_\mu g
_{\nu\sigma} + \partial_\nu g_{\mu\sigma} - \partial_\sigma g_{\mu\nu}\bigr).$$

Since $\partial_\mu \eta_{\nu\sigma} = 0$ and $g^{\lambda\sigma} = \eta^{\lambda
\sigma} + O(h)$, the first-order contribution is

$$\boxed{\Gamma^\lambda_{\ \mu\nu} = \tfrac{1}{2}\eta^{\lambda\sigma}\bigl(\part
ial_\mu h_{\nu\sigma} + \partial_\nu h_{\mu\sigma} - \partial_\sigma h_{\mu\nu}\
bigr) \equiv \bar\Gamma^\lambda_{\ \mu\nu}.}$$

The trace (contraction on the first and third indices) is

$$\Gamma^\mu_{\ \mu\sigma} = \tfrac{1}{2}\eta^{\mu\lambda}\bigl(\partial_\mu h_{
\sigma\lambda} + \partial_\sigma h_{\mu\lambda} - \partial_\lambda h_{\mu\sigma}
\bigr) = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(\partial^\lambda h_{\sigma\lambda} + \partial_\sigma
h - \partial^\mu h_{\mu\sigma}\bigr) = \tfrac{1}{2}\partial_\sigma h,$$

since $\partial^\lambda h_{\sigma\lambda} = \partial^\mu h_{\mu\sigma}$ (dummy i
ndex relabeling).

---

## Step 2 — Riemann tensor (linearized)

$$R^\rho_{\ \sigma\mu\nu} = \partial_\mu \Gamma^\rho_{\ \nu\sigma} - \partial_\n
u \Gamma^\rho_{\ \mu\sigma} + \Gamma^\rho_{\ \mu\lambda}\Gamma^\lambda_{\ \nu\si
gma} - \Gamma^\rho_{\ \nu\lambda}\Gamma^\lambda_{\ \mu\sigma}.$$

The $\Gamma\Gamma$ terms are $O(h^2)$ and are dropped:

$$\boxed{R^\rho_{\ \sigma\mu\nu} = \partial_\mu \bar\Gamma^\rho_{\ \nu\sigma} -
\partial_\nu \bar\Gamma^\rho_{\ \mu\sigma}.}$$

---

## Step 3 — Ricci tensor

Contracting $R^\rho_{\ \sigma\mu\nu}$ on $\rho = \mu$:

$$R_{\sigma\nu} = \partial_\mu \bar\Gamma^\mu_{\ \nu\sigma} - \partial_\nu \bar\
Gamma^\mu_{\ \mu\sigma}.$$

Using $\bar\Gamma^\mu_{\ \nu\sigma} = \tfrac{1}{2}(\partial_\sigma h_\nu^{\ \mu}
 + \partial_\nu h_\sigma^{\ \mu} - \partial^\mu h_{\nu\sigma})$ and $\bar\Gamma^
\mu_{\ \mu\sigma} = \tfrac{1}{2}\partial_\sigma h$:

$$\partial_\mu \bar\Gamma^\mu_{\ \nu\sigma} = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(\partial_\mu\par
tial_\sigma h_\nu^{\ \mu} + \partial_\mu\partial_\nu h_\sigma^{\ \mu} - \Box h_{
\nu\sigma}\bigr), \qquad \partial_\nu \bar\Gamma^\mu_{\ \mu\sigma} = \tfrac{1}{2
}\partial_\nu\partial_\sigma h,$$

where $\Box := \eta^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu\partial_\nu$. Therefore

$$\boxed{R_{\mu\nu} = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(\partial_\alpha\partial_\mu h_\nu^{\ \al
pha} + \partial_\alpha\partial_\nu h_\mu^{\ \alpha} - \Box h_{\mu\nu} - \partial
_\mu\partial_\nu h\bigr).}$$

---

## Step 4 — Ricci scalar

$R = g^{\mu\nu}R_{\mu\nu} = \eta^{\mu\nu}R_{\mu\nu}$ at first order. Contracting
:

- $\eta^{\mu\nu}\partial_\alpha\partial_\mu h_\nu^{\ \alpha} = \partial_\alpha\p
artial_\mu h^{\mu\alpha}$,
- $\eta^{\mu\nu}\partial_\alpha\partial_\nu h_\mu^{\ \alpha} = \partial_\alpha\p
artial_\nu h^{\nu\alpha} = \partial_\alpha\partial_\mu h^{\mu\alpha}$ (relabel),
- $\eta^{\mu\nu}\Box h_{\mu\nu} = \Box h$,
- $\eta^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu\partial_\nu h = \Box h$.

Hence

$$\boxed{R = \partial_\alpha\partial_\beta h^{\beta\alpha} - \Box h.}$$

---

## Step 5 — Linearized Einstein tensor (general form)

$$G_{\mu\nu} = R_{\mu\nu} - \tfrac{1}{2}\eta_{\mu\nu} R,$$

so

$$\boxed{G_{\mu\nu} = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(\partial_\alpha\partial_\mu h_\nu^{\ \al
pha} + \partial_\alpha\partial_\nu h_\mu^{\ \alpha} - \Box h_{\mu\nu} - \partial
_\mu\partial_\nu h\bigr) - \tfrac{1}{2}\eta_{\mu\nu}\bigl(\partial_\alpha\partia
l_\beta h^{\beta\alpha} - \Box h\bigr).}$$

This is the **linearized Einstein tensor in a general gauge**.

---

## Step 6 — Trace-reversed form and the Lorenz gauge

Define the **trace-reversed perturbation**

$$\bar h_{\mu\nu} := h_{\mu\nu} - \tfrac{1}{2}\eta_{\mu\nu} h, \qquad \bar h :=
\eta^{\mu\nu}\bar h_{\mu\nu} = h - 2h = -h.$$

Inverting: $h_{\mu\nu} = \bar h_{\mu\nu} - \tfrac{1}{2}\eta_{\mu\nu}\bar h$, and
 $h = -\bar h$.

Substituting into $R_{\mu\nu}$:

- $\partial_\alpha\partial_\mu h_\nu^{\ \alpha} = \partial_\alpha\partial_\mu \b
ar h_\nu^{\ \alpha} - \tfrac{1}{2}\partial_\mu\partial_\nu \bar h$,
- $\partial_\alpha\partial_\nu h_\mu^{\ \alpha} = \partial_\alpha\partial_\nu \b
ar h_\mu^{\ \alpha} - \tfrac{1}{2}\partial_\mu\partial_\nu \bar h$,
- $\Box h_{\mu\nu} = \Box\bar h_{\mu\nu} - \tfrac{1}{2}\eta_{\mu\nu}\Box\bar h$,
- $\partial_\mu\partial_\nu h = -\partial_\mu\partial_\nu\bar h$.

The three $\partial_\mu\partial_\nu\bar h$ pieces combine as $-\tfrac12 - \tfrac
12 + 1 = 0$; they **cancel**. Thus

$$R_{\mu\nu} = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(\partial_\alpha\partial_\mu \bar h_\nu^{\ \alph
a} + \partial_\alpha\partial_\nu \bar h_\mu^{\ \alpha} - \Box\bar h_{\mu\nu}\big
r) + \tfrac{1}{4}\eta_{\mu\nu}\Box\bar h.$$

For the scalar: $\partial_\alpha\partial_\beta h^{\beta\alpha} = \partial_\alpha
\partial_\beta\bar h^{\beta\alpha} - \tfrac{1}{2}\Box\bar h$ and $-\Box h = +\Bo
x\bar h$, so

$$R = \partial_\alpha\partial_\beta\bar h^{\beta\alpha} + \tfrac{1}{2}\Box\bar h
.$$

Now $G_{\mu\nu} = R_{\mu\nu} - \tfrac{1}{2}\eta_{\mu\nu}R$:

$$G_{\mu\nu} = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(\partial_\alpha\partial_\mu \bar h_\nu^{\ \alph
a} + \partial_\alpha\partial_\nu \bar h_\mu^{\ \alpha} - \Box\bar h_{\mu\nu}\big
r) + \tfrac{1}{4}\eta_{\mu\nu}\Box\bar h - \tfrac{1}{2}\eta_{\mu\nu}\partial_\al
pha\partial_\beta\bar h^{\beta\alpha} - \tfrac{1}{4}\eta_{\mu\nu}\Box\bar h.$$

The $\tfrac14\eta_{\mu\nu}\Box\bar h$ terms cancel, leaving

$$\boxed{G_{\mu\nu} = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(\partial_\alpha\partial_\mu \bar h_\nu^{
\ \alpha} + \partial_\alpha\partial_\nu \bar h_\mu^{\ \alpha} - \Box\bar h_{\mu\
nu}\bigr) - \tfrac{1}{2}\eta_{\mu\nu}\,\partial_\alpha\partial_\beta\bar h^{\bet
a\alpha}.}$$

This is the linearized Einstein tensor in terms of the trace-reversed perturbati
on, valid in **any gauge**.

---

## Step 7 — Lorenz (harmonic) gauge

Impose the **Lorenz gauge condition**

$$\boxed{\partial^\mu \bar h_{\mu\nu} = 0.}$$

This can always be achieved locally by an infinitesimal diffeomorphism $x^\mu \t
o x^\mu + \xi^\mu$ with $\Box\xi_\mu = \partial^\nu\bar h_{\nu\mu}$ (transverse
to the residual gauge). Under this condition:

- $\partial_\alpha \bar h_\nu^{\ \alpha} = \partial^\alpha \bar h_{\alpha\nu} =
0$,
- $\partial_\alpha \bar h_\mu^{\ \alpha} = 0$,
- $\partial_\beta \bar h^{\beta\alpha} = 0$.

All the derivative-contraction terms vanish, and we obtain the **wave equation f
orm**:

$$\boxed{G_{\mu\nu} = -\tfrac{1}{2}\,\Box\,\bar h_{\mu\nu}.}$$

Equivalently, the vacuum Einstein equation $G_{\mu\nu} = 0$ becomes

$$\Box\,\bar h_{\mu\nu} = 0,$$

the **linearized Einstein field equations** — a set of decoupled wave equations
for the trace-reversed perturbation in Lorenz gauge.

## Summary

For $g_{\mu\nu} = \eta_{\mu\nu} + h_{\mu\nu}$ with $|h_{\mu\nu}|\ll 1$, working
to first order:

1. **General gauge, in terms of $h_{\mu\nu}$:**
$$G_{\mu\nu} = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(\partial_\alpha\partial_\mu h_\nu^{\ \alpha} +
\partial_\alpha\partial_\nu h_\mu^{\ \alpha} - \Box h_{\mu\nu} - \partial_\mu\pa
rtial_\nu h\bigr) - \tfrac{1}{2}\eta_{\mu\nu}\bigl(\partial_\alpha\partial_\beta
 h^{\beta\alpha} - \Box h\bigr).$$

2. **General gauge, in terms of $\bar h_{\mu\nu} = h_{\mu\nu} - \tfrac{1}{2}\eta
_{\mu\nu}h$:**
$$G_{\mu\nu} = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(\partial_\alpha\partial_\mu \bar h_\nu^{\ \alph
a} + \partial_\alpha\partial_\nu \bar h_\mu^{\ \alpha} - \Box\bar h_{\mu\nu}\big
r) - \tfrac{1}{2}\eta_{\mu\nu}\partial_\alpha\partial_\beta\bar h^{\beta\alpha}.
$$

3. **Lorenz gauge $\partial^\mu\bar h_{\mu\nu}=0$:**
$$G_{\mu\nu} = -\tfrac{1}{2}\,\Box\,\bar h_{\mu\nu}, \qquad \text{so } G_{\mu\nu
}=0 \iff \Box\bar h_{\mu\nu}=0.$$

The key algebraic mechanism is that the trace pieces ($\propto \partial_\mu\part
ial_\nu h$ and $\propto \eta_{\mu\nu}\Box h$) cancel exactly when one passes to
the trace-reversed variable, leaving a clean wave operator acting on $\bar h_{\m
u\nu}$ once the Lorenz gauge is imposed.
