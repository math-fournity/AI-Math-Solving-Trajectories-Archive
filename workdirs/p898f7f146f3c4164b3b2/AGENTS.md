# Problem

In a specialized digital logistics network, three processing nodes—Alpha, Beta, and Gamma—handle data packets represented by positive integers $x$, $y$, and $z$, respectively.

The network’s efficiency protocol requires that specific throughput ratios must always result in whole numbers. Specifically, the system is constrained such that:
1. The capacity of Alpha divided by the square of Beta’s capacity ($x/y^2$) is an integer.
2. The capacity of Beta divided by the square of Gamma’s capacity ($y/z^2$) is an integer.
3. The capacity of Gamma divided by the square of Alpha’s capacity ($z/x^2$) is an integer.

An encryption key is generated by the formula:
$$\frac{xyz}{(x+y+z)^l}$$
where $l$ is a fixed positive integer constant of the system.

Determine the minimum positive integer value of $l$ such that for every possible triplet of capacities $(x, y, z)$ satisfying the protocol constraints, the resulting encryption key is guaranteed to be an integer.
