**Angles of a triangle.** For three lines with directions $\alpha < \beta < \gam
ma$ (on the circle), the triangle's angles are $\beta - \alpha$, $\gamma - \beta

**Isosceles condition.** A triple $\{a, b, c\}$ is isosceles iff two of the thre

the other two. Equivalently, for some permutation, $2x \equiv y + z \pmod{\pi}$.

**Key reformulation.** For each point $p$ in our set $S$, define $m(p)$ = number
 of unordered pairs $\{q, r\} \subseteq S \setminus \{p\}$ with $q + r \equiv 2p
 \pmod{\pi}$ (i.e., $p$ is the midpoint of an arc between $q$ and $r$). Each non

p)$), while each equilateral triangle has three apexes (contributing 3). So:

$$\sum_{p \in S} m(p) = I + 3E$$

where $E$ = number of equilateral triples and $I$ = number of isosceles non-equi
lateral triples. The total coins are $3E + I = 3E + (I + 3E) - 2E = \sum m(p) +
E - 2E$... let me recompute:

$$\text{Coins} = 3E + I, \quad \sum m(p) = I + 3E \implies \text{Coins} = \sum_{
p \in S} m(p).$$

**Upper bound.** For each $p$, the reflection $\sigma_p: q \mapsto 2p - q \pmod{
\pi}$ is an involution on $S \setminus \{p\}$ (which has 19 elements). The pairs
 contributing to $m(p)$ are orbits of size 2 under $\sigma_p$ where both element

$$m(p) \leq 9 \quad \text{for all } p, \qquad \text{Coins} = \sum_{p} m(p) \leq
20 \times 9 = 180.$$

**Construction achieving 180.** Place the 20 directions equally spaced: $S = \{k
\pi/20 : k = 0, 1, \ldots, 19\}$. For any $p = j\pi/20 \in S$, the reflection $\
sigma_p$ maps $k\pi/20 \mapsto (2j - k)\pi/20 \pmod{\pi}$, which is always in $S

points form 9 pairs, so $m(p) = 9$ for every $p$.

Since $20/3$ is not an integer, no three equally-spaced points have spacing $\pi

**Conclusion.** The maximum number of coins is $\boxed{180}$.
