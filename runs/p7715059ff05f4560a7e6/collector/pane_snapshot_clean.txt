Let $g = \gcd(a,b)$, and write $a = gx$, $b = gy$ with $\gcd(x,y)=1$ and $x \le
y$ (since $a \le b$). Then $\text{lcm}(a,b) = gxy$.

The equilibrium equation becomes:
$$g + gxy = 4(gx + gy) + 2021$$
$$g(1 + xy - 4x - 4y) = 2021$$

**Factoring 2021.** $2021 = 43 \times 47$ (both prime), so the positive divisors
 are $1, 43, 47, 2021$.

**Completing the rectangle.** Setting $d = 2021/g$, we get $xy - 4x - 4y + 1 = d
$, i.e.
$$(x-4)(y-4) = d + 15.$$

Since $x,y \ge 1$, we have $x-4, y-4 \ge -3$. The product $d+15 > 0$, so both fa
ctors are positive (both-negative gives product $\le 9$, too small in every case
 below). We seek factorizations $(x-4)(y-4) = d+15$ with $x-4 \le y-4$, $x,y \ge
 1$, and $\gcd(x,y)=1$.
