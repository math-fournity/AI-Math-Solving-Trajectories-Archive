Summing: $\sum_{i=1}^m |C_i| = \sum_v d_v \geq 3 \cdot 2008 = 6024$. Since $|C_i

One verifies each element $i \in \{1,\ldots,6\}$ appears in exactly $2 + 3 = 5$
