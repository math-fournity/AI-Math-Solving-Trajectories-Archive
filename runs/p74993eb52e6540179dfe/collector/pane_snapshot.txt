## Proof

Let $\alpha_1, \dots, \alpha_n$ be the $n$th roots of unity, i.e. the roots of
$$p(x) = x^n - 1.$$

We seek
$$D = \prod_{i<j}(\alpha_i - \alpha_j)^2,$$
the discriminant of $p$.

**Discriminant via the derivative.** For a monic polynomial $p(x)=\prod_{i=1}^n
(x-\alpha_i)$, the discriminant satisfies
$$D = \prod_{i<j}(\alpha_i-\alpha_j)^2 = (-1)^{\binom{n}{2}}\prod_{i=1}^n p'(\al
pha_i).$$

*Derivation of the identity.* Since $p(x)=\prod_k(x-\alpha_k)$,
$$p'(x)=\sum_{i}\prod_{k\ne i}(x-\alpha_k),\qquad p'(\alpha_i)=\prod_{k\ne i}(\a
lpha_i-\alpha_k).$$
Hence
$$\prod_{i=1}^n p'(\alpha_i)=\prod_{i}\prod_{k\ne i}(\alpha_i-\alpha_k)=\prod_{i
\ne k}(\alpha_i-\alpha_k).$$
Each unordered pair $\{i,k\}$ with $i<k$ contributes both $(\alpha_i-\alpha_k)$
and $(\alpha_k-\alpha_i)=-(\alpha_i-\alpha_k)$, so
$$\prod_{i=1}^n p'(\alpha_i)=\prod_{i<k}(\alpha_i-\alpha_k)(\alpha_k-\alpha_i)=(
-1)^{\binom{n}{2}}\prod_{i<k}(\alpha_i-\alpha_k)^2.$$
Rearranging gives the stated identity.

**Apply to $p(x)=x^n-1$.** Here $p'(x)=nx^{n-1}$, so
$$\prod_{i=1}^n p'(\alpha_i)=\prod_{i=1}^n n\,\alpha_i^{\,n-1}=n^n\prod_{i=1}^n
\alpha_i^{\,n-1}=n^n\Bigl(\prod_{i=1}^n \alpha_i\Bigr)^{n-1}.$$

The product of all roots of $x^n-1$ equals $(-1)^n\cdot(\text{constant term})=(-
1)^n(-1)=(-1)^{n+1}$. (Equivalently, $\prod \alpha_i = \alpha_1\cdots\alpha_n =
e^{2\pi i(0+1+\cdots+(n-1))/n}=e^{\pi i(n-1)}=(-1)^{n-1}=(-1)^{n+1}$.) Therefore
$$\prod_{i=1}^n p'(\alpha_i)=n^n\bigl((-1)^{n+1}\bigr)^{n-1}=n^n\,(-1)^{(n+1)(n-
1)}=n^n\,(-1)^{n^2-1}.$$

**Combine the signs.**
$$D=(-1)^{\binom{n}{2}}\cdot n^n\,(-1)^{n^2-1}=n^n\,(-1)^{\,\frac{n(n-1)}{2}+n^2
-1}.$$
The exponent simplifies:
$$\frac{n(n-1)}{2}+n^2-1=\frac{n^2-n+2n^2-2}{2}=\frac{3n^2-n-2}{2}=\frac{(n-1)(3
n+2)}{2}.$$

**Verification on small cases.**
- $n=2$: roots $1,-1$; $D=(1-(-1))^2=4=2^2$. Formula: $(-1)^{(1)(8)/2}\cdot 4=(-
1)^4\cdot 4=4$. ✓
- $n=3$: roots $1,\omega,\omega^2$; the discriminant of $x^3-1$ is $-27$. Formul
a: $(-1)^{(2)(11)/2}\cdot 27=(-1)^{11}\cdot 27=-27$. ✓

Therefore
$$\boxed{\displaystyle\prod_{i<j}(\alpha_i-\alpha_j)^2=(-1)^{\frac{(n-1)(3n+2)}{
2}}\,n^n.}$$

### PROOF COMPLETE
