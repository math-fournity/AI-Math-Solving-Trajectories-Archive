## Proof

**Setup.** Let $X_1,\dots,X_n$ be i.i.d. $N(\mu,\sigma^2)$. We test $H_0:\mu=\mu
_0$ against the simple alternative $H_1:\mu=\mu_1$ ($\mu_1\ne\mu_0$). We treat $
\sigma^2$ as known; the same asymptotic limit is obtained if $\sigma^2$ is profi
led out (shown at the end). The statistic is
$$
\mathrm{LR}(\mu_0,\mu_1)=-2\ln\frac{L(\mathbf x\mid\mu_0,\sigma^2)}{\max\{L(\mat
hbf x\mid\mu_0,\sigma^2),\,L(\mathbf x\mid\mu_1,\sigma^2)\}}.
$$

**Step 1 — Exact finite-sample form.** The normal likelihood is $L(\mu)=(2\pi\si
gma^2)^{-n/2}\exp\!\big(-\tfrac{1}{2\sigma^2}\sum(x_i-\mu)^2\big)$, so
$$
-2\ln\frac{L(\mu_0)}{L(\mu_1)}=\frac{1}{\sigma^2}\Big[\sum(x_i-\mu_0)^2-\sum(x_i
-\mu_1)^2\Big].
$$
Expanding,
$$
\sum(x_i-\mu_0)^2-\sum(x_i-\mu_1)^2=(\mu_1-\mu_0)\sum(2x_i-\mu_0-\mu_1)=n(\mu_1-
\mu_0)(2\bar x-\mu_0-\mu_1).
$$
Since $\max\{L(\mu_0),L(\mu_1)\}=L(\mu_0)$ when $L(\mu_0)\ge L(\mu_1)$ (giving $
\mathrm{LR}=0$) and $=L(\mu_1)$ otherwise,
$$
\mathrm{LR}=\frac{n(\mu_1-\mu_0)(2\bar x-\mu_0-\mu_1)}{\sigma^2}\,\mathbf 1\!\le
ft\{\bar x>\frac{\mu_0+\mu_1}{2}\right\}
$$
for $\mu_1>\mu_0$ (the indicator is reversed for $\mu_1<\mu_0$).

Under $H_0$, set $Z:=\frac{\sqrt n(\bar x-\mu_0)}{\sigma}\sim N(0,1)$ and $\delt
a_n:=\frac{\sqrt n(\mu_1-\mu_0)}{\sigma}$. Then $2\bar x-\mu_0-\mu_1=\frac{2\sig
ma}{\sqrt n}Z-\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}\delta_n$, and
$$
\mathrm{LR}=\delta_n(2Z-\delta_n)\,\mathbf 1\!\left\{Z>\frac{\delta_n}{2}\right\
}\qquad(\mu_1>\mu_0),
$$
with the indicator $\mathbf 1\{Z<\delta_n/2\}$ when $\mu_1<\mu_0$. Noting $\delt
a_n(2Z-\delta_n)=Z^2-(Z-\delta_n)^2$, the two cases unify as
$$
\boxed{\;\mathrm{LR}=\max\{0,\;Z^2-(Z-\delta_n)^2\},\quad Z\sim N(0,1),\quad \de
lta_n=\tfrac{\sqrt n(\mu_1-\mu_0)}{\sigma}.\;}
$$

**Step 2 — Asymptotics under $H_0$ with fixed alternative.** If $\mu_1\ne\mu_0$
is held fixed, $\delta_n\to\pm\infty$. Then $P(|Z|>|\delta_n|/2)\to 0$, so $Z^2-
(Z-\delta_n)^2<0$ with probability tending to 1, hence
$$
\mathrm{LR}\xrightarrow{p}0\quad(\text{degenerate point mass at }0).
$$

**Step 3 — Non-degenerate local asymptotic distribution.** The meaningful non-de
generate limit is obtained under contiguous (Pitman) local alternatives $\mu_1-\
mu_0=h\,\sigma/\sqrt n$ with $h\in\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}$ fixed, so that $\delt
a_n\to h$. Then, since $Z$ has a fixed $N(0,1)$ law independent of $n$,
$$
\mathrm{LR}\xrightarrow{d}\max\{0,\;Z^2-(Z-h)^2\}=\max\{0,\;2hZ-h^2\},\qquad Z\s
im N(0,1).
$$
This is the **asymptotic distribution** of the statistic: a truncated (at $0$) l
inear function of a standard normal. Equivalently, letting $W=Z^2\sim\chi^2_1$ a
nd $V=(Z-h)^2\sim\chi^2_1(h^2)$ (non-central, correlated with $W$), the limit is
 $\max\{0,\,W-V\}$, the positive part of the difference of two correlated $\chi^
2_1$ variables.

tio gives
$$

ar x>(\mu_0+\mu_1)/2\}.
