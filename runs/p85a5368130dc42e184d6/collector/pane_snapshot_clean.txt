n $2k$ for $k = 0, 1, \dots, n$, and no cells in odd dimensions. This is seen vi

$$\mathbb{CP}^0 \subset \mathbb{CP}^1 \subset \cdots \subset \mathbb{CP}^n,$$
where $\mathbb{CP}^k$ is obtained from $\mathbb{CP}^{k-1}$ by attaching a single
 $2k$-cell (the affine chart $\mathbb{C}^k$ compactified).

**Cellular chain complex with $\mathbb{Z}_2$-coefficients.** The cellular chain
group $C_{2k}(\mathbb{CP}^n; \mathbb{Z}_2) \cong \mathbb{Z}_2$ for $k = 0, \dots
, n$, and $C_j = 0$ for $j$ odd or $j > 2n$. The cellular boundary map $d_j : C_
j \to C_{j-1}$ has domain or codomain zero whenever $j$ is odd (since adjacent c
ells differ in dimension by 2, the boundary of a $2k$-cell lands in dimension $2
k-1$, where $C_{2k-1} = 0$). Hence every boundary map is the zero homomorphism.

**Homology.** Since all boundary maps vanish,
$$H_k(\mathbb{CP}^n; \mathbb{Z}_2) = C_k(\mathbb{CP}^n; \mathbb{Z}_2).$$
Therefore
$$H_k(\mathbb{CP}^n; \mathbb{Z}_2) \cong \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z}_2 & k = 0, 2,
4, \dots, 2n, \\ 0 & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}$$

**Cohomology.** Since $\mathbb{Z}_2$ is a field, the universal coefficient theor
em for cohomology gives $H^k(\mathbb{CP}^n; \mathbb{Z}_2) \cong \operatorname{Ho
m}_{\mathbb{Z}_2}(H_k(\mathbb{CP}^n; \mathbb{Z}_2), \mathbb{Z}_2)$ with no $\ope
ratorname{Ext}$ term. Thus
$$H^k(\mathbb{CP}^n; \mathbb{Z}_2) \cong \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z}_2 & k = 0, 2,
4, \dots, 2n, \\ 0 & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}$$

**Cohomology ring structure.** Let $\alpha \in H^2(\mathbb{CP}^n; \mathbb{Z}_2)$

c_1 \in H^2(\mathbb{CP}^n; \mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}$ (the first Chern class

[c_1]/(c_1^{n+1})$, with $c_1^k$ generating $H^{2k}(\mathbb{CP}^n; \mathbb{Z}) \

{CP}^n; \mathbb{Z}) \to H^*(\mathbb{CP}^n; \mathbb{Z}_2)$ is a ring homomorphism

c_1)$, we obtain
$$\alpha^k = \rho(c_1^k) = \rho(\text{generator of } H^{2k}(\mathbb{CP}^n;\mathb
b{Z})).$$
Since reduction mod 2 sends the integral generator of $\mathbb{Z}$ to the genera
tor of $\mathbb{Z}_2$, each $\alpha^k$ is the nonzero class in $H^{2k}(\mathbb{C

$$\boxed{\,H^*(\mathbb{CP}^n; \mathbb{Z}_2) \cong \mathbb{Z}_2[\alpha]\,/\,(\alp
ha^{n+1}), \qquad |\alpha| = 2.\,}$$

This is a truncated polynomial algebra over $\mathbb{Z}_2$ on one generator of d
egree $2$.
