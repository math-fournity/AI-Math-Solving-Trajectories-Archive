# Problem

In the grand archives of the Chronos Federation, a master clockmaker is assembling a monumental "Century Gear." This device is constructed by layering 100 distinct brass rings.

For example, the second ring is engraved with 66, the third with 666, and so on, until the 100th ring, which is engraved with a sequence of exactly one hundred 6s.

How many times does the digit 7 occur in the calculated value of $N$?
