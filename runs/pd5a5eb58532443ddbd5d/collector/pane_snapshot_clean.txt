$$x_i \, y_i = R^2 - d^2 =: S > 0,$$

$$\frac{x_i - y_i}{2} = \mathbf{o}\cdot\mathbf{u}_i.$$

$$x_i y_i = \frac{L_i^2 - 4(\mathbf{o}\cdot\mathbf{u}_i)^2}{4} = S \implies (\ma
thbf{o}\cdot\mathbf{u}_i)^2 = \frac{L_i^2 - 4S}{4}.$$
