# Problem

In the city of Centuria, an urban planner is designing a high-security district laid out in a perfect 100x100 grid of plots. Each plot must either be designated as a "Sensor Station" or left as an "Open Park." To ensure total coverage and rapid response, the city council has mandated two strict zoning laws:

1. **The Proximity Rule:** In every pair of adjacent plots (any 1x2 or 2x1 rectangular area), there must be at least one Sensor Station.
2. **The Redundancy Rule:** In every strip of six consecutive plots (any 1x6 or 6x1 rectangular area), there must be at least one pair of Sensor Stations located in plots that are immediately next to each other.

The planner needs to minimize the budget by installing as few Sensor Stations as possible while adhering to these security regulations. What is the minimum number of Sensor Stations required for the entire 100x100 grid?
