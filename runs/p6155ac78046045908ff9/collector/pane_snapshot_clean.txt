**Setting up residues.** Each $d_i \in \{1,3,5,7,9\}$. Modulo 4: $\{1,3,5,7,9\}

 preimages $\{1,5,9\}$ and $e_i=3$ has 2 preimages $\{3,7\}$.

$$e_i \cdot e_{i+1} \equiv \begin{cases} 1 \pmod{4} & \text{if } e_i = e_{i+1} \
\ 3 \pmod{4} & \text{if } e_i \neq e_{i+1}\end{cases}$$

 2k_1 \pmod{4}.$$
