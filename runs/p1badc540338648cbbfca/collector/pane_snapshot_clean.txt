## Solution

We compute
$$L = \lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{1}{n^{n}}\int_{0}^{n}x^{n}\arctan(\sqrt[n]{x})\,dx
.$$

**Step 1: Substitution.** Let $x = nt$, so $dx = n\,dt$, and the limits $x\in[0,
n]$ become $t\in[0,1]$:
$$L = \lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{1}{n^{n}}\int_{0}^{1}(nt)^{n}\arctan\!\bigl(\sqrt[
n]{nt}\bigr)\,n\,dt = \lim_{n\to\infty} n\int_{0}^{1} t^{n}\arctan\!\bigl(\sqrt[
n]{nt}\bigr)\,dt.$$

**Step 2: Pointwise limit of the integrand factor.** For $t\in(0,1]$,
$$\sqrt[n]{nt} = \exp\!\Bigl(\tfrac{\ln n + \ln t}{n}\Bigr) \xrightarrow{n\to\in
fty} e^{0}=1,$$
since $\frac{\ln n}{n}\to 0$ and $\frac{\ln t}{n}\to 0$. Hence
$$\arctan\!\bigl(\sqrt[n]{nt}\bigr) \xrightarrow{n\to\infty} \arctan(1) = \frac{
\pi}{4}.$$

**Step 3: The kernel $n\,t^{n}$ concentrates at $t=1$.** The probability measure
s $n\,t^{n}\,dt$ on $[0,1]$ satisfy, for every $\varepsilon>0$,
$$n\int_{0}^{1-\varepsilon} t^{n}\,dt = \frac{n}{n+1}\bigl(1-(1-\varepsilon)^{n+
1}\bigr)^{-1}\!\cdot n\int_0^{1-\varepsilon}t^n\,dt\;\to\;0,$$
more directly: $n\int_0^{1-\varepsilon}t^n\,dt = \frac{n}{n+1}(1-\varepsilon)^{n
+1}\to 0$, and $n\int_0^1 t^n\,dt = \frac{n}{n+1}\to 1$. So the mass concentrate
s in any neighborhood of $t=1$.

**Step 4: Uniform convergence near $t=1$.** For $t\in[1-\varepsilon,\,1]$,
$$\frac{\ln(nt)}{n} = \frac{\ln n + \ln t}{n} \in \Bigl[\frac{\ln n+\ln(1-\varep
silon)}{n},\;\frac{\ln n}{n}\Bigr],$$
and both endpoints tend to $0$ as $n\to\infty$. Therefore $\sqrt[n]{nt}\to 1$ **
uniformly** on $[1-\varepsilon,1]$, and consequently
$$\arctan\!\bigl(\sqrt[n]{nt}\bigr) \to \frac{\pi}{4}\quad\text{uniformly on }[1
-\varepsilon,1].$$

**Step 5: Combine.** Split the integral at $1-\varepsilon$:
$$n\int_0^1 t^n\arctan\!\bigl(\sqrt[n]{nt}\bigr)\,dt = n\int_0^{1-\varepsilon}(\
cdots)\,dt + n\int_{1-\varepsilon}^1(\cdots)\,dt.$$

- **Tail $[0,1-\varepsilon]$:** bounded by $\frac{\pi}{2}\,n\int_0^{1-\varepsilo
n}t^n\,dt = \frac{\pi}{2}\cdot\frac{n}{n+1}(1-\varepsilon)^{n+1}\to 0$.

- **Main part $[1-\varepsilon,1]$:** by uniform convergence,
$$n\int_{1-\varepsilon}^1 t^n\arctan\!\bigl(\sqrt[n]{nt}\bigr)\,dt = \frac{\pi}{
4}\,n\int_{1-\varepsilon}^1 t^n\,dt + o\!\Bigl(n\int_{1-\varepsilon}^1 t^n\,dt\B
igr).$$
Since $n\int_{1-\varepsilon}^1 t^n\,dt = \frac{n}{n+1}\bigl(1-(1-\varepsilon)^{n
+1}\bigr)\to 1$, this part tends to $\frac{\pi}{4}$.

Letting $\varepsilon\to 0$ (the tail vanishes for every fixed $\varepsilon>0$),
we conclude
$$L = \frac{\pi}{4}.$$
