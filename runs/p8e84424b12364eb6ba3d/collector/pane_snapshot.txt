## Solution

**Setup.** We work in $\mathbb{R}^5$ with coordinates $(p_1, p_2, q_1, q_2, t)$
and the 1-form
$$a = p_1\,dq_1 + p_2\,dq_2 - p_1 p_2\,dt.$$

---

### Computing $da$

Using $d(p_i\,dq_i) = dp_i \wedge dq_i$ (since $d^2 q_i = 0$) and $d(p_1 p_2\,dt
) = d(p_1 p_2)\wedge dt = (p_2\,dp_1 + p_1\,dp_2)\wedge dt$:

$$da = dp_1 \wedge dq_1 + dp_2 \wedge dq_2 - p_2\,dp_1 \wedge dt - p_1\,dp_2 \we
dge dt.$$

---

### Computing $da \wedge da$

Write $da = A + B$ with
$$A = dp_1 \wedge dq_1 + dp_2 \wedge dq_2, \qquad B = -p_2\,dp_1\wedge dt - p_1\
,dp_2\wedge dt.$$

Since $A$ and $B$ are 2-forms, $A\wedge B = B\wedge A$, and $B\wedge B = 0$ (eve
ry term in $B$ contains $dt$, so any wedge of two terms has $dt\wedge dt = 0$).
Thus
$$da\wedge da = A\wedge A + 2\,A\wedge B.$$

**Term $A\wedge A$:** The self-wedge terms vanish (repeated 1-forms). The cross
terms:
$$dp_1\wedge dq_1\wedge dp_2\wedge dq_2 + dp_2\wedge dq_2\wedge dp_1\wedge dq_1.
$$
Reordering the second to match the first requires 4 transpositions (each of $dp_
2, dq_2$ crosses each of $dp_1, dq_1$), giving sign $+1$. Hence
$$A\wedge A = 2\,dp_1\wedge dq_1\wedge dp_2\wedge dq_2 = -2\,dp_1\wedge dp_2\wed
ge dq_1\wedge dq_2.$$

**Term $A\wedge B$:**
$$A\wedge B = (dp_1\wedge dq_1 + dp_2\wedge dq_2)\wedge(-p_2\,dp_1\wedge dt - p_
1\,dp_2\wedge dt).$$

Expanding (terms with $dp_1\wedge dp_1$ or $dp_2\wedge dp_2$ vanish):
- $-p_1\,dp_1\wedge dq_1\wedge dp_2\wedge dt = +p_1\,dp_1\wedge dp_2\wedge dq_1\
wedge dt$ (one swap of $dq_1, dp_2$),
- $-p_2\,dp_2\wedge dq_2\wedge dp_1\wedge dt = -p_2\,dp_1\wedge dp_2\wedge dq_2\
wedge dt$ (two swaps, sign $+$).

So
$$A\wedge B = p_1\,dp_1\wedge dp_2\wedge dq_1\wedge dt - p_2\,dp_1\wedge dp_2\we
dge dq_2\wedge dt.$$

**Combining:**
$$\boxed{da\wedge da = -2\,dp_1\wedge dp_2\wedge dq_1\wedge dq_2 + 2p_1\,dp_1\we
dge dp_2\wedge dq_1\wedge dt - 2p_2\,dp_1\wedge dp_2\wedge dq_2\wedge dt.}$$

---

### Evaluating $\int_S t\,da\wedge da$

The surface $S$ is defined by $p_1^2+p_2^2\le 1$, $q_1^2+q_2^2\le 2$, $t=3$. Sin
ce $t$ is fixed at $3$ on $S$, the pullback of $dt$ to $S$ is zero. Therefore **
all terms of $da\wedge da$ containing $dt$ vanish** when restricted to $S$:

$$(da\wedge da)\big|_S = -2\,dp_1\wedge dp_2\wedge dq_1\wedge dq_2.$$

Also $t = 3$ on $S$, so
$$t\,da\wedge da\big|_S = -6\,dp_1\wedge dp_2\wedge dq_1\wedge dq_2.$$

With the natural orientation induced by the parametrization $(p_1,p_2,q_1,q_2)\m
apsto(p_1,p_2,q_1,q_2,3)$, i.e. the volume form $dp_1\wedge dp_2\wedge dq_1\wedg
e dq_2$, the integral factors:

$$\int_S t\,da\wedge da = -6\int_{p_1^2+p_2^2\le 1}dp_1\,dp_2 \cdot \int_{q_1^2+
q_2^2\le 2}dq_1\,dq_2.$$

The first disk has radius $1$, area $\pi$. The second has radius $\sqrt{2}$, are
a $2\pi$. Therefore

$$\int_S t\,da\wedge da = -6 \cdot \pi \cdot 2\pi = \boxed{-12\pi^2}.$$
