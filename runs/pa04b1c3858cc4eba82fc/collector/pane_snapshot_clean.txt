## Solution

We evaluate $\iint_{S_1}(xz\cos\alpha + yz\cos\beta + \cos\gamma)\,dS$, where $S
_1$ is the spherical cap of $x^2+y^2+z^2=25$ cut off by $z=3$, and $n=(\cos\alph
a,\cos\beta,\cos\gamma)$ is the outward unit normal.

**Reformulation.** The integrand equals $F\cdot n$ with $F=(xz,\,yz,\,1)$. Hence
 the surface integral is the flux $\iint_{S_1} F\cdot n\,dS$.

**Closing the surface.** Let $D$ be the disk $z=3,\ x^2+y^2\le 16$ (radius $4$).
 The closed surface $\partial V = S_1\cup D$ bounds the cap volume $V=\{x^2+y^2+
z^2\le 25,\ z\ge 3\}$. By the divergence theorem,
$$\iint_{S_1}F\cdot n\,dS + \iint_{D}F\cdot n_D\,dS = \iiint_V \nabla\cdot F\,dV
.$$

**Divergence.** $\nabla\cdot F = \partial_x(xz)+\partial_y(yz)+\partial_z(1) = z
+z+0 = 2z$.

**Volume integral (cylindrical coordinates).** At height $z\in[3,5]$, the cross-
section is a disk of radius $\sqrt{25-z^2}$, so
$$\iiint_V 2z\,dV = 2\int_3^5 z\cdot\pi(25-z^2)\,dz = 2\pi\int_3^5(25z-z^3)\,dz.
$$
$$=2\pi\Big[\tfrac{25z^2}{2}-\tfrac{z^4}{4}\Big]_3^5 = 2\pi\Big[\Big(\tfrac{625}
{2}-\tfrac{625}{4}\Big)-\Big(\tfrac{225}{2}-\tfrac{81}{4}\Big)\Big] = 2\pi\Big[\
tfrac{625}{4}-\tfrac{369}{4}\Big]=2\pi\cdot\tfrac{256}{4}=128\pi.$$

**Disk contribution.** On $D$ the outward normal (pointing out of $V$, i.e. down
ward) is $n_D=(0,0,-1)$, so $\cos\gamma=-1$, $\cos\alpha=\cos\beta=0$. Thus $F\c
dot n_D = -1$, and
$$\iint_D F\cdot n_D\,dS = \iint_D (-1)\,dS = -\pi\cdot 4^2 = -16\pi.$$

**Combining.**
$$\iint_{S_1}F\cdot n\,dS = 128\pi - (-16\pi) = 128\pi + 16\pi = 144\pi.$$

$$\boxed{\iint_{S_1}(xz\cos\alpha+yz\cos\beta+\cos\gamma)\,dS = 144\pi.}$$
