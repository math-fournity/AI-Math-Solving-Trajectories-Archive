## Setting Up the DP

**Model:** Each shipment i is assigned to a processing day g(i) with g(i) ≥ i (p
rocessed on or after arrival), g(1) = 1, g(n) = n, every shipment processed exac
tly once, and no two consecutive days with zero shipments processed.

**State:** After processing day j, track u = number of unprocessed shipments. Sp
lit into:
- **a(u):** day j was idle (k_j = 0 processed), u unprocessed
- **b(u):** day j was not idle, u unprocessed

**Transitions (day j, shipment j arrives, available = u+1):**
- From b(u): choose k ≥ 0 to process → a(u+1-k) or b(u+1-k), weighted by C(u+1,
k)
- From a(u): **must** process k ≥ 1 → b(u+1-k), weighted by C(u+1, k)

**Day 1:** Must process shipment 1 → a = {}, b = {0: 1}.

**Last day n:** Must process all remaining (including shipment n) → f(n) = Σ[a(u
) + b(u)] over all states after day n−1.

## Computing f(n) mod 3

Tracking the full state modulo 3:

| After day | a (mod 3) | b (mod 3) | Total mod 3 | = f(n) mod 3 |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|-------------|--------------|
| 1 | {} | {0:1} | 1 | f(2)=1 |
| 2 | {1:1} | {0:1} | 2 | f(3)=2 ✓ |
| 3 | {1:1} | {0:2, 1:2} | 2 | f(4)=2 ✓ |
| 4 | {1:2, 2:2} | {0:2, 1:0} | 0 | f(5)=0 |
| 5 | {1:2, 2:0} | {0:0, 1:1} | 0 | f(6)=0 |
| 6 | {2:1} | {0:0, 1:0} | 1 | f(7)=1 |
| **7** | **{}** | **{0:1}** | **1** | f(8)=1 |

**State after day 7 = state after day 1** (both: a = {}, b = {0: 1}).

Since the transition is deterministic and depends only on the current state, the
 states repeat with **period 6**.

## The Periodic Pattern

For n ≥ 2, f(n) mod 3 has period 6:

| n mod 6 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 0 | 1 |
|---------|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| f(n) mod 3 | 1 | **2** | **2** | 0 | 0 | 1 |

**f(n) ≡ 2 (mod 3) if and only if n ≡ 3 or 4 (mod 6).**

## Finding the 2021st Value

The qualifying n values come in pairs: (6k+3, 6k+4) for k = 0, 1, 2, ...

- k=0: 3, 4 (positions 1, 2)
- k=1: 9, 10 (positions 3, 4)
- k=m: 6m+3, 6m+4 (positions 2m+1, 2m+2)

Setting 2m+1 = 2021 gives m = 1010, so:

$$N = 6 \cdot 1010 + 3 = 6063$$
