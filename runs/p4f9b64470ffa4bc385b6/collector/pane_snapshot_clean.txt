## Answer: The function is $\log(z - 1)$.

## Proof

The cut is along the ray $1 \leq x < \infty,\, y = 0$, i.e., $[1, \infty)$ on th

**Step 2: Analyze the Riemann surface of $v^n = z - 1$.**

The equation $v^n = z - 1$ defines the algebraic function $v = (z-1)^{1/n}$. Its
 Riemann surface is an $n$-sheeted branched cover of the $z$-sphere, with branch

ng $[1, \infty)$ separates the $n$ sheets, and gluing them cyclically across the
 cut (upper edge of sheet $j$ to lower edge of sheet $j+1 \pmod{n}$) reconstruct
s the surface. A single loop around $z = 1$ cycles through all $n$ sheets.

**Step 3: The nesting (directed system) property.**

For $n = mk$, the Riemann surface of $(z-1)^{1/n}$ is a $k$-fold cover of the Ri
emann surface of $(z-1)^{1/m}$, since $((z-1)^{1/n})^k = (z-1)^{1/m}$. Concretel

by the map $j \mapsto j \bmod m$ on sheet indices. Thus the Riemann surfaces $\{
R_n\}_{n \geq 1}$ form a directed system under these covering maps.

**Step 4: The direct limit is the Riemann surface of $\log(z-1)$.**

The direct limit $R_\infty = \varinjlim R_n$ is an infinite-sheeted covering of
$\mathbb{C} \setminus \{1\}$. Its sheets are indexed by $\mathbb{Z}$ (the colimi

1$ acts by $k \mapsto k + 1$ on $\mathbb{Z}$.

The function $\log(z - 1)$ is infinitely many-valued:
$$\log(z-1) = \ln|z-1| + i\arg(z-1) + 2\pi i k, \quad k \in \mathbb{Z}.$$
Its Riemann surface has sheets indexed by $k \in \mathbb{Z}$, with the monodromy

$R_\infty$.

**Step 5: Verification of all stated properties.**

1. **Infinitely many-valued**: $\log(z-1)$ takes values differing by $2\pi i k$
for all $k \in \mathbb{Z}$ — infinitely many values. ✓

**Conclusion.** The infinitely many-valued function described is:

$$\boxed{\log(z - 1)}$$
