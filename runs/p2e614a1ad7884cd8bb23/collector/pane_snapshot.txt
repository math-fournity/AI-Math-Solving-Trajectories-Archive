## Proof

We are given that $\lim_{n\to\infty}\sqrt[n]{|a_n|}=0$, and we must show that $\
lim_{n\to\infty}\sqrt[n]{|a_{n+1}|}=0$.

**Key observation.** Rewrite the shifted root in terms of the original one. For
each $n\ge 1$,

$$
\sqrt[n]{|a_{n+1}|} \;=\; |a_{n+1}|^{1/n} \;=\; \Bigl(|a_{n+1}|^{1/(n+1)}\Bigr)^
{(n+1)/n}.
$$

Set $c_n := \sqrt[n]{|a_n|} = |a_n|^{1/n}$. The hypothesis says $c_n \to 0$. The
 quantity we must analyze is

$$
\sqrt[n]{|a_{n+1}|} \;=\; c_{n+1}^{\,(n+1)/n}.
$$

**Bounding the exponent.** For every $n\ge 1$,
$$
\frac{n+1}{n} \;=\; 1 + \frac{1}{n} \;\ge\; 1.
$$

**Monotonicity of $x\mapsto x^r$ on $(0,1)$.** For $0 < x < 1$, the function $r
\mapsto x^r$ is *decreasing* in $r$. Hence for any $r \ge 1$ and $0<x<1$,
$$
x^r \;\le\; x^1 \;=\; x.
$$

**Applying the bound.** Since $c_{n+1} \to 0$, there exists $N$ such that for al
l $n \ge N$, $0 \le c_{n+1} < 1$. For such $n$,

$$
0 \;\le\; \sqrt[n]{|a_{n+1}|} \;=\; c_{n+1}^{\,(n+1)/n} \;\le\; c_{n+1}^{\,1} \;
=\; c_{n+1}.
$$

Since $c_{n+1} \to 0$, the squeeze theorem gives

$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}\sqrt[n]{|a_{n+1}|} \;=\; 0.
$$

**Conclusion.** The shifted sequence $\sqrt[n]{|a_{n+1}|}$ also tends to $0$. Eq
uivalently, if $f(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_n z^n$ is entire (radius of convergenc
e $R=\infty$, characterized by $\limsup\sqrt[n]{|a_n|}=0$), then the shifted ser
ies $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_{n+1}z^n$ is also entire. Indeed the derivative $f'(z)
=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n\,a_n z^{n-1}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(n+1)a_{n+1}z^n$ is entire
 (derivatives of entire functions are entire), and the shift $\sum a_{n+1}z^n$ i
s obtained from $f'(z)$ by dividing coefficients by $(n+1)$, which only *decreas
es* $\sqrt[n]{|a_{n+1}|}$-type growth — consistent with the direct argument abov
e.
