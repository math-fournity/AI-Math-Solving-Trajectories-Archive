## Proof

$$\frac{x^5 - x^2}{x^5 + y^2 + z^2} \geq \frac{x^2 - yz}{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}.$$

ss-multiply. The difference between the two sides (times the product of denomina
tors) is:

$$D = (x^5 - x^2)(x^2 + y^2 + z^2) - (x^2 - yz)(x^5 + y^2 + z^2).$$

Expanding both products:

- First: $x^7 + x^5 y^2 + x^5 z^2 - x^4 - x^2 y^2 - x^2 z^2$
- Second: $x^7 + x^2 y^2 + x^2 z^2 - x^5 yz - y^3 z - yz^3$

Subtracting and collecting:

$$D = x^5(y^2 + z^2 + yz) - x^4 - 2x^2(y^2 + z^2) + yz(y^2 + z^2).$$

Set $P = y^2 + z^2$ and $Q = yz$. Then:

$$D = P(x^5 - 2x^2 + Q) + x^4(Qx - 1).$$

We show both terms are non-negative:

1. **Second term:** Since $xyz \geq 1$, we have $Qx = xyz \geq 1$, so $Qx - 1 \g
eq 0$, and $x^4 \geq 0$. Thus $x^4(Qx - 1) \geq 0$. ✓

2. **First term:** We have $P = y^2 + z^2 \geq 0$. For the factor $x^5 - 2x^2 +
Q$: since $xyz \geq 1$ gives $Q = yz \geq \frac{1}{x}$, we obtain
$$x^5 - 2x^2 + Q \geq x^5 - 2x^2 + \frac{1}{x} = \frac{x^6 - 2x^3 + 1}{x} = \fra
c{(x^3 - 1)^2}{x} \geq 0.$$
Thus $P(x^5 - 2x^2 + Q) \geq 0$. ✓

Therefore $D \geq 0$, proving the claim. $\square$

**Summing the claim cyclically** over $(x, y, z)$, $(y, z, x)$, $(z, x, y)$:

$$\sum_{\text{cyc}} \frac{x^5 - x^2}{x^5 + y^2 + z^2} \geq \frac{\frac{1}{2}\big

This completes the proof. $\blacksquare$
