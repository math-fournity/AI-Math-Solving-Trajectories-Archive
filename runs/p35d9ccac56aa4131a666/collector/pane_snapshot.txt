 are interleaved in the circular order. The condition that no more than two chor
ds pass through any interior point ensures no three chords are concurrent, so **
the number of intersection points equals the number of crossing pairs**. Denote
this by $\operatorname{cr}(M)$.

The desired difference is $\displaystyle\sum_M (-1)^{\operatorname{cr}(M)}$, whe

**Step 1: Crossing parity equals the sign of the matching.**

pi_M = (i_1, j_1, i_2, j_2, \ldots, i_N, j_N)$, and let $\operatorname{sgn}(M) =
 \operatorname{sgn}(\pi_M)$, which equals $(-1)$ raised to the number of inversi

**Claim:** $\operatorname{sgn}(M) = (-1)^{\operatorname{cr}(M)}$ for every match

*Proof of claim.* The total inversion count is the sum over all pairs of chords
$(i_k, j_k)$, $(i_l, j_l)$ with $k < l$ (so $i_k < i_l$) of the inversions among

tions are ordered and $i_k$ is the smaller in its pair), the inversions contribu

$$[j_k > i_l] + [i_k > j_l] + [j_k > j_l],$$

(where $[\cdot]$ is the indicator), coming from the pairs of positions $(2k, 2l{
-}1)$, $(2k{-}1, 2l)$, $(2k, 2l)$ respectively; the other three pairs are non-in

Now consider cases (all points are distinct, and $j_k \neq i_l$ since they belon

$$\operatorname{Pf}(A_{2N}) = \sum_{M} \operatorname{sgn}(M) \prod_{(i,j) \in M,
