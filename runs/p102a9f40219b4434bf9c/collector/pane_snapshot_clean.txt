## Solution

**Key formula (Legendre).** The exponent of 2 in $n!$ is

$$v_2(n!) = \sum_{k\ge 1}\left\lfloor \frac{n}{2^k}\right\rfloor = n - s_2(n),$$

where $s_2(n)$ denotes the number of $1$'s in the binary representation of $n$.

**Condition.** We need $2^{n-2}\mid n!$, i.e.

$$v_2(n!) \ge n-2 \iff n - s_2(n) \ge n-2 \iff s_2(n) \le 2.$$

So we must count positive integers $n\le 500$ whose binary representation has **
at most two** $1$'s.

**Case 1: $s_2(n)=1$ (powers of 2).** Here $n=2^k$ with $2^k\le 500$. Since $2^8
=256\le 500 < 512 = 2^9$, we get $k=0,1,\dots,8$: **9 values**.

**Case 2: $s_2(n)=2$ (sum of two distinct powers of 2).** Here $n=2^a+2^b$ with
$a>b\ge 0$ and $2^a+2^b\le 500$. For each fixed $a$, the admissible $b$ are $0\l
e b\le a-1$ with $2^a+2^b\le 500$. Since $2^a+2^{a-1}<2^{a+1}$, the binding cons
traint is $2^a\le 500$, i.e. $a\le 8$ (as $2^9=512>500$). For each $a=1,\dots,8$
, all $b\in\{0,\dots,a-1\}$ work (the largest being $2^8+2^7=384\le 500$). This
gives

$$\sum_{a=1}^{8} a = 1+2+3+4+5+6+7+8 = 36 \text{ values}.$$

**Total.** $9 + 36 = \boxed{45}$.
