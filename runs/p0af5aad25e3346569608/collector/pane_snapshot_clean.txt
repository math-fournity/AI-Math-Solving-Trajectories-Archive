## Solution

**Setup.** Let $p = 2017$ (prime), so the multiplicative group $(\mathbb{Z}/p\ma
thbb{Z})^\times$ is cyclic of order $m = p - 1 = 2016 = 2^5 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 7$.

A signal $n$ is Ultra-Stable for strength $k$ if there is an infinite chain $n_0
 = n, n_1, n_2, \ldots$ with $n_{i+1}^k \equiv n_i \pmod{p}$. Equivalently, $n$
lies in the image of the $k$-th power map iterated infinitely many times.

**The stable image.** The map $\phi_k: x \mapsto x^k$ on a cyclic group of order
 $m$ has image equal to the unique subgroup of order $m/\gcd(m,k)$. Iterating $r
$ times gives image of order $m/\gcd(m, k^r)$. As $r \to \infty$, for each prime
 $q \mid m$:
$$v_q(\gcd(m, k^r)) = \min(v_q(m),\, r \cdot v_q(k)) \;\longrightarrow\; \begin{
cases} v_q(m) & \text{if } q \mid k, \\ 0 & \text{if } q \nmid k. \end{cases}$$
So $\gcd(m, k^\infty) := g = \prod_{q \mid m,\; q \mid k} q^{v_q(m)}$, and the s
table image (on the multiplicative group) has order $m/g$.

**Including $n = 0$.** Since $0^k \equiv 0$, the signal $0$ always has itself as
 a $k$-th root ad infinitum, so $0$ is always Ultra-Stable. Therefore:
$$f(2017, k) = 1 + \frac{2016}{g(k)}, \qquad g(k) = \prod_{\substack{q \in \{2,3
