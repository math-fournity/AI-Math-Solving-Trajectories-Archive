## Proof

**Forward direction.** Suppose $n = \dfrac{a^2+a}{2}+\dfrac{b^2+b}{2}$ for integ
ers $a,b$. Then

$$4n+1 = 2(a^2+a)+2(b^2+b)+1 = 2a^2+2a+2b^2+2b+1.$$

Set

$$x = a+b+1, \qquad y = a-b.$$

Then

$$x^2+y^2 = (a+b+1)^2+(a-b)^2.$$

Expanding:

$$(a+b+1)^2 = a^2+b^2+1+2ab+2a+2b,$$
$$(a-b)^2 = a^2-2ab+b^2.$$

Adding, the $2ab$ and $-2ab$ cancel, giving

$$x^2+y^2 = 2a^2+2b^2+2a+2b+1 = 2a^2+2a+2b^2+2b+1 = 4n+1.$$

Hence $4n+1 = x^2+y^2$ with $x=a+b+1$ and $y=a-b$. $\checkmark$

**Converse direction.** Suppose $4n+1 = x^2+y^2$ for integers $x,y$.

Since $4n+1$ is odd, $x^2+y^2$ is odd, so exactly one of $x,y$ is even and the o
ther is odd. Consequently both $x+y$ and $x-y$ are odd.

Define

$$a = \frac{x+y-1}{2}, \qquad b = \frac{x-y-1}{2}.$$

Because $x+y$ and $x-y$ are odd, the numerators $x+y-1$ and $x-y-1$ are even, so
 $a$ and $b$ are integers.

We verify the defining relations:

$$a+b+1 = \frac{x+y-1+x-y-1}{2}+1 = \frac{2x-2}{2}+1 = x,$$

$$a-b = \frac{x+y-1-(x-y-1)}{2} = \frac{2y}{2} = y.$$

Therefore, applying the identity established in the forward direction,

$$4\!\left(\frac{a^2+a}{2}+\frac{b^2+b}{2}\right)+1 = (a+b+1)^2+(a-b)^2 = x^2+y^
2 = 4n+1.$$

Subtracting $1$ and dividing by $4$,

$$\frac{a^2+a}{2}+\frac{b^2+b}{2} = n.$$

Thus $n$ is the sum of two triangular numbers. $\checkmark$

**Summary.** The bijection is given by

$$\boxed{\,x = a+b+1,\quad y = a-b\,} \qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad \boxed{\,a
 = \tfrac{x+y-1}{2},\quad b = \tfrac{x-y-1}{2}\,}$$

which establishes that $n$ is a sum of two triangular numbers if and only if $4n
+1$ is a sum of two squares.
