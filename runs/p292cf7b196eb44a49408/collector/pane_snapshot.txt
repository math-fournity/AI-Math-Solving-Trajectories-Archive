## Solution

We wish to compute
$$S=\sum_{m=1}^\infty\sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{m^2 n}{3^m\,(n\,3^m+m\,3^n)}.$$

**Key observation: symmetry.** The denominator factor $D(m,n):=n\,3^m+m\,3^n$ is
 symmetric in $m$ and $n$, i.e. $D(m,n)=D(n,m)$.

Define the "swapped" sum
$$T:=\sum_{m=1}^\infty\sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{n^2 m}{3^n\,(n\,3^m+m\,3^n)}.$$

Since the double sum runs over all $m,n\ge 1$, relabeling $m\leftrightarrow n$ s
hows $T=S$.

**Adding the two sums.** Using $S=T$,
$$2S = S+T = \sum_{m,n\ge 1}\frac{1}{D(m,n)}\left(\frac{m^2 n}{3^m}+\frac{n^2 m}
{3^n}\right).$$

Factor the numerator:
$$\frac{m^2 n}{3^m}+\frac{n^2 m}{3^n}=mn\!\left(\frac{m}{3^m}+\frac{n}{3^n}\righ
t)=mn\cdot\frac{m\,3^n+n\,3^m}{3^{m+n}}=mn\cdot\frac{D(m,n)}{3^{m+n}}.$$

Substituting back, the $D(m,n)$ cancels:
$$2S=\sum_{m,n\ge 1}\frac{mn}{3^{m+n}}.$$

**Evaluating the product.** The double sum factors:
$$\sum_{m=1}^\infty\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{mn}{3^{m+n}}=\left(\sum_{k=1}^\infty\f
rac{k}{3^k}\right)^{\!2}.$$

Using the standard identity $\sum_{k=1}^\infty k\,x^k=\frac{x}{(1-x)^2}$ for $|x
|<1$, at $x=\tfrac13$:
$$\sum_{k=1}^\infty\frac{k}{3^k}=\frac{1/3}{(2/3)^2}=\frac{1/3}{4/9}=\frac{3}{4}
.$$

Therefore
$$2S=\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^2=\frac{9}{16},\qquad S=\frac{9}{32}.$$

$$\boxed{\dfrac{9}{32}}$$
