# Problem

In a remote deep-sea exploration site, a spherical underwater base is being designed to house a central sensor hub. To stabilize this hub, three straight titanium support struts are installed. These struts have lengths of 5 meters, 6 meters, and 7 meters, respectively.

The three struts are positioned such that they all intersect at a single junction point inside the sphere. Furthermore, they are installed in a perfectly orthogonal configuration, meaning each strut is perpendicular to the other two. Each strut spans the interior of the sphere, with both ends of every strut touching the inner spherical hull.

Engineers are looking to minimize the size of the base. For the smallest possible radius $R$ of the sphere that can contain this configuration, calculate the value of $R^2$. If $R^2$ is expressed as an irreducible fraction $\frac{a}{b}$, find the sum $a + b$.
