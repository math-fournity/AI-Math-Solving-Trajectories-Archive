## Proof

Let the closed rigid space curve be parametrized by arc length $s$, with positio
n vector $\mathbf{r}(s)$, and let $[0,L]$ be the parameter domain so that $\math
bf{r}(0)=\mathbf{r}(L)$ and all Frenet frame vectors agree at the endpoints (the
 curve is closed with continuous curvature). Denote the Frenet frame by $\mathbf
{T},\mathbf{N},\mathbf{B}$ (unit tangent, principal normal, binormal) and the cu
rvature by $\kappa = 1/r$.

The Frenet–Serret formula gives
$$\frac{d\mathbf{T}}{ds} = \kappa\,\mathbf{N} = \frac{1}{r}\mathbf{N}.$$

The force acting on the line element $ds$ is, by hypothesis,
$$d\mathbf{F} = \frac{1}{r}\mathbf{N}\,ds = \kappa\,\mathbf{N}\,ds = \frac{d\mat
hbf{T}}{ds}\,ds = d\mathbf{T}.$$

### Net force

The resultant force on the whole curve is
$$\mathbf{F}_{\text{tot}} = \oint \kappa\,\mathbf{N}\,ds = \oint d\mathbf{T}.$$

Since the curve is closed and the curvature is continuous, the tangent $\mathbf{
T}$ returns to its initial value after one traversal, so
$$\oint d\mathbf{T} = \mathbf{T}(L)-\mathbf{T}(0) = \mathbf{0}.$$

Hence the net force vanishes.

### Net moment

The total moment about the origin is
$$\mathbf{M}_{\text{tot}} = \oint \mathbf{r}\times d\mathbf{F} = \oint \mathbf{r
}\times(\kappa\,\mathbf{N}\,ds) = \oint \mathbf{r}\times d\mathbf{T}.$$

Consider the derivative of the vector product $\mathbf{r}\times\mathbf{T}$:
$$\frac{d}{ds}(\mathbf{r}\times\mathbf{T}) = \frac{d\mathbf{r}}{ds}\times\mathbf
{T} + \mathbf{r}\times\frac{d\mathbf{T}}{ds} = \mathbf{T}\times\mathbf{T} + \mat
hbf{r}\times\frac{d\mathbf{T}}{ds} = \mathbf{r}\times\frac{d\mathbf{T}}{ds}.$$

Thus $\mathbf{r}\times d\mathbf{T} = d(\mathbf{r}\times\mathbf{T})$, and
$$\mathbf{M}_{\text{tot}} = \oint d(\mathbf{r}\times\mathbf{T}) = \bigl[\mathbf{
r}\times\mathbf{T}\bigr]_{0}^{L} = \mathbf{0},$$
since both $\mathbf{r}$ and $\mathbf{T}$ coincide at $s=0$ and $s=L$ on a closed
 curve.

### Conclusion

Both the resultant force and the resultant moment about an arbitrary point vanis
h. A rigid body subjected to a system of forces with zero resultant and zero mom
ent is in equilibrium. Therefore the closed rigid space curve is in equilibrium
under the given force distribution.
