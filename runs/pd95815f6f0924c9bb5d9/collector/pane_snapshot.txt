## Proof

**Answer-leak check:** The problem statement contains no solutions, solution ske
tches, or answer-revealing formalization. Proceeding.

---

Given $x+y = xy = w$ with $w \in \mathbb{R}^+$, the numbers $x, y$ are the roots
 of
$$t^2 - wt + w = 0,$$
with discriminant $\Delta = w^2 - 4w = w(w-4)$.

**Case 1: $w \geq 4$.** Here $\Delta \geq 0$, so $x, y \in \mathbb{R}$. Since $x
y = w > 0$, they share the same sign; since $x+y = w > 0$, both are positive: $x
, y > 0$. For positive real base and real exponent, $x^w = e^{w\ln x}$ and $y^w
= e^{w\ln y}$ are positive reals. Hence $x^w + y^w \in \mathbb{R}$.

**Case 2: $0 < w < 4$.** Here $\Delta < 0$, so $x, y$ are complex conjugates:
$$x = \frac{w + i\sqrt{w(4-w)}}{2}, \qquad y = \bar{x} = \frac{w - i\sqrt{w(4-w)
}}{2}.$$
Compute $|x|^2 = \tfrac{1}{4}\bigl(w^2 + w(4-w)\bigr) = w$, so $|x| = \sqrt{w}$,
 and
$$\arg(x) = \arctan\sqrt{\tfrac{4-w}{w}} \in (0, \tfrac{\pi}{2}) \subset (-\pi,
\pi].$$
Using the principal branch, $\log x = \ln\sqrt{w} + i\arg(x)$, and since $\arg(x
) \in (-\pi,\pi]$,
$$\log \bar{x} = \ln\sqrt{w} - i\arg(x) = \overline{\log x}.$$
Therefore
$$y^w = \bar{x}^{\,w} = e^{w\log\bar{x}} = e^{w\,\overline{\log x}} = \overline{
e^{w\log x}} = \overline{x^w}.$$
Consequently
$$x^w + y^w = x^w + \overline{x^w} = 2\operatorname{Re}(x^w) \in \mathbb{R}.$$

**Boundary $w = 4$:** $\Delta = 0$, giving $x = y = 2$, and $x^4 + y^4 = 32 \in
\mathbb{R}$.

---

All cases covered ($0 < w < 4$, $w = 4$, $w > 4$) yield $x^w + y^w \in \mathbb{R
}$. Thus $x^w + y^w$ is **necessarily** a real number. $\blacksquare$
