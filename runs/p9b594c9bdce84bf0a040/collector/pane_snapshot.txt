## Solution

**Interpretation.** The integral is taken over a single pair $(\theta_i, \epsilo
n_i)$, and $\max_i\{\theta_i+\epsilon_i-1,\,0\}$ denotes the positive part $(\th
eta_i+\epsilon_i-1)^+ = \max\{\theta_i+\epsilon_i-1,\,0\}$. With $\theta\sim U[1
,2]$ (density $1$) and $\epsilon\sim U[-\tfrac12,\tfrac12]$ (density $1$), indep
endent,

$$R^* = \int_{1}^{2}\int_{-1/2}^{1/2} \max\{\theta+\epsilon-1,\,0\}\,d\epsilon\,
d\theta = \mathbb{E}\big[(\theta+\epsilon-1)^+\big].$$

Let $X=\theta+\epsilon-1$. Since $\theta\in[1,2]$ and $\epsilon\in[-\tfrac12,\tf
rac12]$, we have $X\in[-\tfrac12,\tfrac32]$, and the sign boundary is $\theta+\e
psilon-1=0\iff \epsilon=1-\theta$.

---

**Inner integral over $\epsilon$ for fixed $\theta$.** The threshold $1-\theta$
lies in $[-1,0]$ as $\theta$ ranges over $[1,2]$.

*Case 1: $1-\theta\le -\tfrac12$, i.e. $\theta\ge \tfrac32$.* Then $\theta+\epsi
lon-1>0$ for all $\epsilon\in[-\tfrac12,\tfrac12]$, so

$$\int_{-1/2}^{1/2}(\theta+\epsilon-1)\,d\epsilon = (\theta-1)\cdot 1 + 0 = \the
ta-1.$$

*Case 2: $-\tfrac12 < 1-\theta \le \tfrac12$, i.e. $1\le \theta < \tfrac32$.* On
ly $\epsilon\in[1-\theta,\tfrac12]$ contributes. Setting $a=1-\theta$ (so $\thet
a-1=-a$),

$$\int_{a}^{1/2}(-a+\epsilon)\,d\epsilon = -a\Big(\tfrac12-a\Big)+\Big(\tfrac18-
\tfrac{a^2}{2}\Big) = -\tfrac{a}{2}+\tfrac{a^2}{2}+\tfrac18 = \tfrac{(a-\tfrac12
)^2}{2} = \tfrac{(\theta-\tfrac12)^2}{2}.$$

---

**Outer integral over $\theta$.**

$$R^* = \int_{1}^{3/2}\frac{(\theta-\tfrac12)^2}{2}\,d\theta + \int_{3/2}^{2}(\t
heta-1)\,d\theta.$$

First piece:
$$\frac12\cdot\frac{(\theta-\tfrac12)^3}{3}\Big|_{1}^{3/2} = \frac16\Big[1^3-\Bi
g(\tfrac12\Big)^3\Big] = \frac16\cdot\frac78 = \frac{7}{48}.$$

Second piece:
$$\frac{(\theta-1)^2}{2}\Big|_{3/2}^{2} = \frac12\Big[1-\tfrac14\Big] = \frac38
= \frac{18}{48}.$$

Therefore
$$R^* = \frac{7}{48}+\frac{18}{48} = \frac{25}{48}.$$

---

**Cross-check via $\mathbb{E}[X]=\mathbb{E}[X^+]-\mathbb{E}[X^-]$.** We have $\m
athbb{E}[X]=\mathbb{E}[\theta]+\mathbb{E}[\epsilon]-1=\tfrac32+0-1=\tfrac12=\tfr
ac{24}{48}$. The negative part contributes only for $\theta\in[1,\tfrac32)$:

$$\mathbb{E}[X^-]=\int_{1}^{3/2}\frac{(3/2-\theta)^2}{2}\,d\theta = \frac12\cdot
\frac{(3/2-\theta)^3}{-3}\Big|_{1}^{3/2}=\frac{1}{48}.$$

Hence $\mathbb{E}[X^+]=\tfrac{24}{48}+\tfrac{1}{48}=\tfrac{25}{48}$, confirming
the direct computation.

---

$$\boxed{R^* = \dfrac{25}{48}}$$
