At most 4 distinct lines through the origin in $\mathbb{R}^2$ divide $\mathbb{R}

coordinate $x_i$ has constant sign (since $x_i$ can only change sign by crossing
 $H_i$). Therefore the number of distinct sign patterns is at most **8**.

$$2x_1 - 3x_2 + x_3 = 0, \qquad 6x_1 - 7x_2 + x_4 = 0.$$
