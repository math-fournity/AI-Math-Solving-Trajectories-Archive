## Solution

**Setup.** Place the rail along the x-axis. Let Lens 1 have center $O_1=(0,0)$,
radius $r_1=4$, and Lens 2 have center $O_2=(14,0)$, radius $r_2=10$ (the center
s are $4+10=14$ apart since the lenses touch externally).

**Finding Lens 3.** Let Lens 3 have center $(c,0)$ and radius $R$. Internal tang
ency gives:
$$|c| = R-4, \qquad |c-14| = R-10.$$
With $0\le c\le 14$: $c=R-4$ and $14-c=R-10$. Adding: $14=2R-14$, so $R=14$ and
$c=10$. Thus Lens 3 has center $(10,0)$, radius $14$.

**The common external tangent.** Let the upper tangent be $y=mx+b$. Distances fr
om the two centers to the line equal the respective radii (line above both cente
rs):
$$\frac{b}{\sqrt{m^2+1}}=4,\qquad \frac{14m+b}{\sqrt{m^2+1}}=10.$$
Subtracting: $14m=6\sqrt{m^2+1}$, so $m^2=\tfrac{9}{40}$, giving $m=\tfrac{3\sqr
t{10}}{20}$ and $\sqrt{m^2+1}=\tfrac{7\sqrt{10}}{20}$. Then $b=4\cdot\tfrac{7\sq
rt{10}}{20}=\tfrac{7\sqrt{10}}{5}$.

The line is $\tfrac{3\sqrt{10}}{20}x - y + \tfrac{7\sqrt{10}}{5}=0$.

**Chord length inside Lens 3.** The perpendicular distance from $(10,0)$ to the
line:
$$d=\frac{\left|\tfrac{3\sqrt{10}}{20}\cdot 10 + \tfrac{7\sqrt{10}}{5}\right|}{\
sqrt{\tfrac{9}{40}+1}}=\frac{\tfrac{29\sqrt{10}}{10}}{\tfrac{7}{2\sqrt{10}}}=\fr
ac{29\sqrt{10}}{10}\cdot\frac{2\sqrt{10}}{7}=\frac{58}{7}.$$

The chord length is
$$2\sqrt{R^2-d^2}=2\sqrt{196-\frac{3364}{49}}=2\sqrt{\frac{9604-3364}{49}}=2\sqr
t{\frac{6240}{49}}=\frac{2\sqrt{6240}}{7}.$$

Since $6240=16\cdot 390$ and $390=2\cdot3\cdot5\cdot13$ is square-free:
$$\text{length}=\frac{2\cdot 4\sqrt{390}}{7}=\frac{8\sqrt{390}}{7}.$$

**Verification of form.** $m=8$, $n=390$, $p=7$: $\gcd(8,7)=1$ ✓, $390$ square-f
ree ✓.

$$m+n+p = 8+390+7 = \boxed{405}.$$
