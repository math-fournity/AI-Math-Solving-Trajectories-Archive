Looking at this problem, I need to find pairs of points $A, B$ on the hyperbola
$C: x^2 + 2x - my^2 = 0$ such that $\triangle AOB$ is an isosceles right triangl
e with the right angle at $O$.

## Setup

For $\triangle AOB$ to be an isosceles right triangle with right angle at $O$: w
e need $|OA| = |OB|$ and $OA \perp OB$. This means $B$ is obtained from $A$ by a
 90° rotation about $O$. So $B = R_{90}(A) = (-y, x)$ (counterclockwise) or $B =
 R_{270}(A) = (y, -x)$ (clockwise).

Since $\{A, R_{270}(A)\} = \{R_{270}(A), A\}$ and setting $A' = R_{270}(A)$ give
s $\{A', R_{90}(A')\}$, both cases are captured by counting pairs $\{A, R_{90}(A
)\}$ where both $A, R_{90}(A) \in C$ and $A \neq O$.

**Key observation:** For $A \neq O$, each valid starting point $A$ yields a *dis
tinct* triangle $\{A, R_{90}(A)\}$. (If $\{A_1, R_{90}(A_1)\} = \{A_2, R_{90}(A_
2)\}$ with $A_1 \neq A_2$, then $A_1 = R_{90}(A_2)$ and $R_{90}(A_1) = A_2$, giv
ing $R_{180}(A_2) = A_2$, i.e., $A_2 = O$.) So $N(m)$ equals the number of point
s $A \in C$, $A \neq O$, with $R_{90}(A) \in C$.

## Solving the System

Let $A = (x, y)$. The conditions are:
- **(I):** $x^2 + 2x - my^2 = 0$ (A on C)
- **(II):** $y^2 - 2y - mx^2 = 0$ (R₉₀(A) on C)

**Subtracting (II) from (I):** $(x^2 - y^2) + 2(x + y) = m(y^2 - x^2)$, which fa
ctors as:
$$(x+y)\big[(x-y)(1+m) + 2\big] = 0$$

### Subcase 1: $x + y = 0$ (i.e., $y = -x$)

From (I): $x^2(1-m) + 2x = 0$, so $x = 0$ (degenerate, $A = O$) or $x = \frac{2}
{m-1}$ (for $m \neq 1$).

For $m \neq 1$: $P_1 = \left(\frac{2}{m-1},\, -\frac{2}{m-1}\right)$. Verificati
on: $x^2 + 2x - my^2 = \frac{4(1-m)}{(m-1)^2} + \frac{4}{m-1} = 0$ ✓, and $R_{90
}(P_1) = \left(\frac{2}{m-1},\, \frac{2}{m-1}\right)$ also satisfies $C$ ✓.

**This gives 1 valid point for $m \neq 1$, and 0 for $m = 1$.**

### Subcase 2: $x - y = \frac{-2}{1+m}$ (i.e., $y = x + \frac{2}{1+m}$)

Substituting into (I) and simplifying (for $m \neq 1$) yields a quadratic in $x$
 with discriminant proportional to $\frac{1+3m}{1-m}$.

- **$0 < m < 1$:** $\frac{1+3m}{1-m} > 0$ → **2 real solutions**
- **$m = 1$:** Subcase degenerates; direct check gives $-1 = 0$, no solution → *
*0 solutions**
- **$m > 1$:** $\frac{1+3m}{1-m} < 0$ → **0 real solutions**

None of these solutions equal $O$ (since $O$ doesn't satisfy $x - y = \frac{-2}{
1+m}$).

## Computing $N(m)$

| Range | Subcase 1 | Subcase 2 | $N(m)$ |
|-------|-----------|-----------|--------|
| $0 < m < 1$ | 1 | 2 | **3** |
| $m = 1$ | 0 | 0 | **0** |
| $m > 1$ | 1 | 0 | **1** |

### Verification for $m = 0.5$:
- **Subcase 1:** $P_1 = (-4, 4)$, $R_{90}(P_1) = (-4, -4)$. Check: $16 - 8 - 0.5
(16) = 0$ ✓
- **Subcase 2:** $x = \frac{2(-1 \pm \sqrt{5})}{3}$, $y = x + \frac{4}{3}$. Two
points $P_2, P_3$ with irrational coordinates. Direct substitution confirms both
 $P_i$ and $R_{90}(P_i)$ lie on $C$ ✓

All three starting points are distinct (different coordinates, different subcase
s), giving 3 distinct triangles.

### Verification for $m = 1$:
$(x+1)^2 - y^2 = 1$. Adding (I) and (II) gives $x = y$; substituting into (I) gi
ves $2x = 0$, so $A = O$ only. **$N(1) = 0$** ✓

### Verification for $m = 2$:
- **Subcase 1:** $P_1 = (2, -2)$, $R_{90}(P_1) = (2, 2)$. Check: $4 + 4 - 2(4) =
 0$ ✓
- **Subcase 2:** $\frac{1+6}{1-2} = -7 < 0$, no real solutions. **$N(2) = 1$** ✓

## Final Answer

$$N(0.5) + N(1) + N(2) = 3 + 0 + 1 = \boxed{4}$$
