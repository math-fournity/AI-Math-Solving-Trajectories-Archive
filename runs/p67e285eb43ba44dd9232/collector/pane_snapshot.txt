Looking at this problem, I need to find the smallest N such that (2016^2016, N)
is regimented, then compute the 2-adic valuation of their difference.

## Solution

**Step 1: Characterize the regimented condition.**

If $m = \prod p_i^{a_i}$, then $d(m^k) = \prod (a_i k + 1)$. The pair $(m,n)$ is
 regimented iff $\prod(a_i k+1) = \prod(b_j k+1)$ for all $k \geq 0$. Since both
 sides are polynomials in $k$ agreeing on infinitely many values, they are ident
ical as polynomials. Their roots are $\{-1/a_i\}$ and $\{-1/b_j\}$, so the multi
sets $\{a_i\}$ and $\{b_j\}$ must be equal. The leading coefficients $\prod a_i
= \prod b_j$ then agree automatically.

**Conclusion:** $(m,n)$ is regimented iff $m$ and $n$ have the **same multiset o
f exponents** in their prime factorizations.

**Step 2: Factorize and find N.**

$2016 = 2^5 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 7$, so $2016^{2016} = 2^{10080} \cdot 3^{4032} \cdot
 7^{2016}$, with exponent multiset $\{10080, 4032, 2016\}$.

$N$ must have exactly 3 distinct prime factors with exponents $\{10080, 4032, 20
16\}$. To minimize $N$, assign the largest exponent to the smallest prime:

$$N = 2^{10080} \cdot 3^{4032} \cdot 5^{2016}.$$

**Step 3: Compute $v_2(2016^{2016} - N)$.**

$$2016^{2016} - N = 2^{10080} \cdot 3^{4032}\left(7^{2016} - 5^{2016}\right).$$

Since $3^{4032}$ is odd:

$$v_2(2016^{2016} - N) = 10080 + v_2\!\left(7^{2016} - 5^{2016}\right).$$

**Step 4: Apply the Lifting the Exponent Lemma (LTE) for $p=2$.**

For odd $a, b$ and even $n$:
$$v_2(a^n - b^n) = v_2(a-b) + v_2(a+b) + v_2(n) - 1.$$

With $a=7, b=5, n=2016$:
- $v_2(7-5) = v_2(2) = 1$
- $v_2(7+5) = v_2(12) = 2$
- $v_2(2016) = v_2(2^5 \cdot 63) = 5$

$$v_2(7^{2016} - 5^{2016}) = 1 + 2 + 5 - 1 = 7.$$

**Verification of LTE with small cases:** For $n=2$: $7^2-5^2=24$, $v_2=3=1+2+1-
1$ ✓. For $n=4$: $7^4-5^4=1776=2^4\cdot111$, $v_2=4=1+2+2-1$ ✓. For $n=8$: $v_2=
5=1+2+3-1$ ✓.

**Step 5: Final answer.**

$$v = 10080 + 7 = \boxed{10087}.$$
