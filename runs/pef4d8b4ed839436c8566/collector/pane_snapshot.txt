## Proof

We claim the answer is $k = \binom{n}{2} - 1$.

### Part 1: Foma wins for every graph with $k \leq \binom{n}{2}-1$ edges

If $k \leq \binom{n}{2}-1$, the graph $G$ is **not complete**, so there exists a
 pair of non-adjacent vertices. Foma chooses such a pair, naming one $A$ and the
 other $B$, and places the token at $A$.

**Foma's strategy:** After each of Erema's moves, if the edge from the token's c
urrent vertex to $B$ exists, Foma removes it. If it does not exist, Foma removes
 any remaining edge incident to $B$ (if one exists); otherwise, Foma removes any
 edge.

**Claim:** Erema can never move the token to $B$.

We prove this by induction on the round number.

**Base case (round 1):** The token is at $A$. Since $A$ and $B$ are non-adjacent
, Erema cannot move to $B$. Erema moves the token to some vertex $v_1 \neq B$. F
oma then removes edge $(v_1, B)$ if it exists.

**Inductive step:** Suppose at the start of round $r$, the token is at vertex $v
_{r-1}$, and the edge $(v_{r-1}, B)$ has been removed (or never existed). Then E
rema cannot move to $B$ from $v_{r-1}$. Erema moves to some vertex $v_r \neq B$.
 Foma removes edge $(v_r, B)$ if it exists. The invariant is maintained for roun
d $r+1$.

By induction, Erema can **never** move the token to $B$. Since the graph has fin
itely many edges and Foma removes one per round, the game eventually terminates
with Erema unable to move—all without the token ever reaching $B$. Foma wins. $\
checkmark$

### Part 2: Foma loses when $k = \binom{n}{2}$

When $k = \binom{n}{2}$, the graph is the complete graph $K_n$. Every pair of ve
rtices is adjacent. Whatever $A$ and $B$ Foma chooses, the edge $(A, B)$ exists.
 On Erema's very first turn, he moves the token directly from $A$ to $B$ and win
s immediately—before Foma gets to remove a single edge. Foma cannot prevent this
. $\checkmark$

### Conclusion

Foma can guarantee a win for **every** graph on $n$ vertices with at most $\bino
m{n}{2}-1$ edges (by choosing a non-adjacent pair as $A, B$ and cutting edges to
 $B$ as Erema approaches), but cannot win on the complete graph $K_n$ with $\bin
om{n}{2}$ edges. Therefore, the maximum $k$ is:

$$\boxed{k = \binom{n}{2} - 1}$$
