## Setup

Place the circle at the origin with radius $r$. Take the tangency point $P = (0,
 r)$ (the top of the circle), so the tangent line is the horizontal line $\ell:
y = r$.

A variable point on the circle is $R = (r\cos\theta,\, r\sin\theta)$.

The perpendicular from $R$ to the horizontal line $\ell$ is vertical, so the foo
t is
$$Q = (r\cos\theta,\, r).$$

## Area of $\triangle PQR$

Both $P$ and $Q$ lie on $\ell$ (the line $y = r$), so $PQ$ is a natural base:
$$|PQ| = |r\cos\theta - 0| = r|\cos\theta|.$$

The height from $R$ to the line $PQ$ (which is $y = r$) is
$$h = r - r\sin\theta = r(1 - \sin\theta) \ge 0.$$

Hence
$$A(\theta) = \tfrac{1}{2}\,r|\cos\theta|\cdot r(1-\sin\theta) = \frac{r^{2}}{2}
\,|\cos\theta|\,(1-\sin\theta).$$

## Maximizing

By symmetry in $\cos\theta$, restrict to $\cos\theta \ge 0$ (i.e. $\theta\in[-\p
i/2,\pi/2]$) and maximize
$$g(\theta) = \cos\theta\,(1-\sin\theta).$$

Differentiate:
$$g'(\theta) = -\sin\theta(1-\sin\theta) + \cos\theta(-\cos\theta) = -\sin\theta
 + \sin^{2}\theta - \cos^{2}\theta.$$

Using $\cos^{2}\theta = 1 - \sin^{2}\theta$:
$$g'(\theta) = 2\sin^{2}\theta - \sin\theta - 1.$$

Set $u = \sin\theta$:
$$2u^{2} - u - 1 = 0 \implies (2u+1)(u-1) = 0 \implies u = -\tfrac{1}{2}\ \text{
or}\ u = 1.$$

- $u = 1$ ($\theta = \pi/2$): $R = P$, degenerate, area $0$.
- $u = -\tfrac{1}{2}$ ($\theta = -\pi/6$): $\cos\theta = \sqrt{1 - \tfrac14} = \
tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.

At this critical point:
$$g = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\left(1 - \left(-\tfrac{1}{2}\right)\right) = \frac{\sqr
t{3}}{2}\cdot\frac{3}{2} = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{4}.$$

Check the endpoints of $[-\pi/2,\pi/2]$: $g(-\pi/2) = 0$, $g(\pi/2) = 0$, and $g
(0) = 1 < \tfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{4} \approx 1.299$. So the interior critical point is
 the global maximum.

## Result

$$A_{\max} = \frac{r^{2}}{2}\cdot\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{4} = \boxed{\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}\,
r^{2}}{8}}.$$
