## Setup

Triangle ABC with ∠A = 60°, ∠B = 70°, ∠C = 50°. D is the foot of the altitude fr
om A to BC, with AD = 1/2. O is the circumcenter. Line AO extended meets BC at O
'. Find AO'.

## Coordinate placement

Place D at the origin, BC along the x-axis, A at (0, 1/2).

- In right triangle ABD: BD = AD/tan B = 1/(2 tan 70°), so **B = (−1/(2 tan 70°)
, 0)**.
- In right triangle ACD: CD = AD/tan C = 1/(2 tan 50°), so **C = (1/(2 tan 50°),
 0)**.

## Circumradius

By the sine rule, a = BC = 2R sin A = 2R sin 60° = R√3. The area is

$$\text{Area} = \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot a \cdot AD = \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot R\sqrt{3} \cd
ot \tfrac{1}{2} = \frac{R\sqrt{3}}{4}.$$

Using Area = 2R² sin A sin B sin C = 2R² sin 60° sin 70° sin 50°:

$$2R^2 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sin 70°\sin 50° = \frac{R\sqrt{3}}{4} \implies R
 = \frac{1}{4\sin 70°\sin 50°}.$$

## Circumcenter coordinates

Since B, C lie on the x-axis, the perpendicular bisector of BC is vertical, so x
₀ = (x_B + x_C)/2. From |OA|² = |OB|²:

$$y_0 = \tfrac{1}{4} + 2x_0 x_B - x_B^2 = \tfrac{1}{4} + (x_B+x_C)x_B - x_B^2 =
\tfrac{1}{4} + x_B x_C.$$

Since x_B x_C = −1/(4 tan 70° tan 50°):

$$y_0 = \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{4\tan 70°\tan 50°} = \frac{\tan 70°\tan 50° - 1}{
4\tan 70°\tan 50°}.$$

## Finding AO'

The line through A = (0, 1/2) and O = (x₀, y₀) is parametrized as P(t) = A + t(O
 − A). It meets BC (the x-axis, y = 0) when:

$$\tfrac{1}{2} + t\!\left(y_0 - \tfrac{1}{2}\right) = 0 \implies t = \frac{1}{1
- 2y_0}.$$

Since |O − A| = R (circumradius):

$$AO' = t \cdot R = \frac{R}{1 - 2y_0}.$$

Compute 1 − 2y₀:

$$1 - 2y_0 = 1 - \frac{\tan 70°\tan 50° - 1}{2\tan 70°\tan 50°} = \frac{\tan 70°
\tan 50° + 1}{2\tan 70°\tan 50°}.$$

Therefore:

$$AO' = R \cdot \frac{2\tan 70°\tan 50°}{\tan 70°\tan 50° + 1} = \frac{1}{4\sin
70°\sin 50°} \cdot \frac{2\tan 70°\tan 50°}{\tan 70°\tan 50° + 1}.$$

Substituting tan = sin/cos:

$$AO' = \frac{2\sin 70°\sin 50°}{4\sin 70°\sin 50°\cos 70°\cos 50°\,(\tan 70°\ta
n 50°+1)} = \frac{1}{2\cos 70°\cos 50°\,(\tan 70°\tan 50°+1)}.$$

Now use the product-to-sum identity:

$$\tan 70°\tan 50° + 1 = \frac{\sin 70°\sin 50° + \cos 70°\cos 50°}{\cos 70°\cos
 50°} = \frac{\cos(70°-50°)}{\cos 70°\cos 50°} = \frac{\cos 20°}{\cos 70°\cos 50
°}.$$

Substituting back:

$$AO' = \frac{1}{2\cos 70°\cos 50° \cdot \dfrac{\cos 20°}{\cos 70°\cos 50°}} = \
frac{1}{2\cos 20°}.$$

$$\boxed{AO' = \dfrac{1}{2\cos 20°}}$$
