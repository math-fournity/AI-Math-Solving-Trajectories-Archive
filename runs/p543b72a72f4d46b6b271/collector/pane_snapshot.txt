**Value function.** Let $V(s)$ be the optimal winning probability when the curre

- **Stop:** we win iff the next $X > k-s$, which has probability $1-(k-s)=1-k+s$

- **Continue:** reveal $X\sim U(0,1)$. If $s+X>k$ we lose immediately; if $s+X\l

$$\int_0^{k-s} V(s+x)\,dx$$
(since $X$ has density $1$ on $[0,1]$, and $k-s\le 1$).

Hence
$$V(s)=\max\!\left\{1-k+s,\;\int_0^{k-s}V(s+x)\,dx\right\}.$$

t)$. Then $s=k-t$, the stop payoff is $1-t$, and
$$\int_0^{k-s}V(s+x)\,dx=\int_0^t V(k-t+x)\,dx=\int_0^t W(t-x)\,dx=\int_0^t W(u)

So
$$\boxed{W(t)=\max\!\left\{1-t,\;\int_0^t W(u)\,du\right\}},\qquad t\in[0,k],$$
and the winning probability for parameter $k$ is $W(k)$.
