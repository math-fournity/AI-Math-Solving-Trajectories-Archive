## Solution

**Simplify the logarithmic term.**

The key fraction inside the logarithm is:

$$\frac{((n+1)!)^{2n}}{n^{2n}(n!)^{2n}} = \left(\frac{(n+1)!}{n\cdot n!}\right)^
{2n} = \left(\frac{n+1}{n}\right)^{2n} = \left(1+\frac{1}{n}\right)^{2n}$$

since $(n+1)! = (n+1)\cdot n!$. Therefore:

$$n\ln\!\left(\frac{((n+1)!)^{2n}}{n^{2n}(n!)^{2n}}\right) = n\ln\!\left(\left(1
+\tfrac{1}{n}\right)^{2n}\right) = 2n^{2}\ln\!\left(1+\tfrac{1}{n}\right).$$

**Asymptotic expansion of the numerator.**

Using the Taylor expansion $\ln(1+x) = x - \frac{x^{2}}{2} + \frac{x^{3}}{3} - \
cdots$ with $x = 1/n$:

$$2n^{2}\ln\!\left(1+\tfrac{1}{n}\right) = 2n^{2}\!\left(\frac{1}{n} - \frac{1}{
2n^{2}} + O\!\left(\tfrac{1}{n^{3}}\right)\right) = 2n - 1 + O\!\left(\tfrac{1}{
n}\right).$$

The remaining terms in the numerator are negligible:

$$\frac{\arctan(n)}{n} = O\!\left(\tfrac{1}{n}\right), \qquad \frac{\sin(n)}{n}
= O\!\left(\tfrac{1}{n}\right),$$

since $\arctan(n)\to\pi/2$ and $|\sin(n)|\le 1$. Hence the numerator satisfies:

$$\text{Numerator} = 2n - 1 + O\!\left(\tfrac{1}{n}\right) = 2n\!\left(1 - \tfra
c{1}{2n} + O\!\left(\tfrac{1}{n^{2}}\right)\right).$$

**Asymptotic expansion of the denominator.**

$$(-1)^{n}\sqrt{n} - 4n = -4n\!\left(1 - \frac{(-1)^{n}}{4\sqrt{n}}\right).$$

The correction term $\frac{(-1)^{n}}{4\sqrt{n}}\to 0$ as $n\to\infty$.

**Combine.**

$$\frac{\text{Numerator}}{\text{Denominator}} = \frac{2n\!\left(1 - \frac{1}{2n}
 + O\!\left(\frac{1}{n^{2}}\right)\right)}{-4n\!\left(1 - \frac{(-1)^{n}}{4\sqrt
{n}}\right)} = \frac{2}{-4}\cdot\frac{1 - \frac{1}{2n} + O\!\left(\frac{1}{n^{2}
}\right)}{1 - \frac{(-1)^{n}}{4\sqrt{n}}}.$$

As $n\to+\infty$, both $\frac{1}{2n}\to 0$ and $\frac{(-1)^{n}}{4\sqrt{n}}\to 0$
, so the fraction of corrections tends to $1$. Therefore:

$$\lim_{n\to+\infty}\frac{n\ln\!\left(\frac{((n+1)!)^{2n}}{n^{2n}(n!)^{2n}}\righ
t)+\frac{\arctan(n)}{n}+\frac{\sin(n)}{n}}{(-1)^{n}\sqrt{n}-4n} = \frac{2}{-4} =
 \boxed{-\dfrac{1}{2}}.$$
