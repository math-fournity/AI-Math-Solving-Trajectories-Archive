**Setup.** Place $B=(0,0)$, $C=(9,0)$. From $AB=8$, $AC=10$:
$$A=\left(\tfrac{5}{2},\,\tfrac{\sqrt{231}}{2}\right), \qquad \text{since } x_A^
2+y_A^2=64,\ (x_A-9)^2+y_A^2=100 \Rightarrow x_A=\tfrac{5}{2}.$$

**Finding $T$.** The tangent at $A$ to the circumcircle meets line $BC$ at $T$.
By the tangent–symmedian property,
$$\frac{TB}{TC}=\frac{AB^2}{AC^2}=\frac{64}{100}=\frac{16}{25}.$$
$T$ is external (beyond $B$, since $16/25<1$): $TB=16$, $TC=25$, so $T=(-16,0)$.
 Check: $TA^2=TB\cdot TC=400$, giving $TA=20$.

**Finding $S$.** The circle centered at $T$ with radius $20$ has equation $(x+16
)^2+y^2=400$. Parametrize line $AC$ by $A+s(C-A)$:
$$\left(\tfrac{5+13s}{2},\,\tfrac{\sqrt{231}(1-s)}{2}\right).$$
Substituting:
$$\tfrac{(37+13s)^2+231(1-s)^2}{4}=400 \;\Longrightarrow\; 400s^2+500s=0 \;\Long
rightarrow\; s=0\ (A)\ \text{or}\ s=-\tfrac{5}{4}\ (S).$$
Thus $S=\left(-\tfrac{45}{8},\,\tfrac{9\sqrt{231}}{8}\right)$, and
$$SA = \tfrac{5}{4}\cdot AC = \tfrac{5}{4}\cdot 10 = \tfrac{25}{2}.$$

**Finding $SB$.**
$$SB^2=\left(\tfrac{45}{8}\right)^2+\left(\tfrac{9\sqrt{231}}{8}\right)^2=\tfrac
{2025+18711}{64}=\tfrac{20736}{64}=324 \;\Longrightarrow\; SB=18.$$
*(Cross-check via power of $C$: $CA\cdot CS = 10\cdot 22.5 = 225 = CT^2-TA^2=625
-400$. ✓)*

**Angle bisector.** In $\triangle SBA$, the bisector of $\angle SBA$ from $B$ me
ets $SA$ at $P$. By the angle bisector theorem:
$$\frac{SP}{PA}=\frac{SB}{BA}=\frac{18}{8}=\frac{9}{4}.$$
Since $SP+PA=SA=\tfrac{25}{2}$:
$$SP=\frac{9}{9+4}\cdot\frac{25}{2}=\frac{9}{13}\cdot\frac{25}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{
225}{26}}.$$
