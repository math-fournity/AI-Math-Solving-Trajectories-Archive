## Proof

Let $\varphi \in \mathcal{D}(\mathbb{R})$ be a test function (smooth with compac
t support). We analyze

$$\langle f_n, \varphi \rangle = n\!\left(\varphi\!\left(\tfrac{1}{n}\right) - \
varphi\!\left(-\tfrac{1}{n}\right)\right).$$

**Taylor expansion around the origin.** Since $\varphi$ is smooth, we expand abo
ut $0$:

$$\varphi(h) = \varphi(0) + \varphi'(0)\,h + \tfrac{1}{2}\varphi''(0)\,h^2 + \tf
rac{1}{6}\varphi'''(0)\,h^3 + O(h^4), \qquad h \to 0.$$

Setting $h = 1/n$ and $h = -1/n$:

$$\varphi\!\left(\tfrac{1}{n}\right) = \varphi(0) + \frac{\varphi'(0)}{n} + \fra
c{\varphi''(0)}{2n^2} + \frac{\varphi'''(0)}{6n^3} + O\!\left(\tfrac{1}{n^4}\rig

$$\varphi\!\left(-\tfrac{1}{n}\right) = \varphi(0) - \frac{\varphi'(0)}{n} + \fr
ac{\varphi''(0)}{2n^2} - \frac{\varphi'''(0)}{6n^3} + O\!\left(\tfrac{1}{n^4}\ri
ght).$$

Subtracting, all even-order terms cancel and all odd-order terms double:

$$\varphi\!\left(\tfrac{1}{n}\right) - \varphi\!\left(-\tfrac{1}{n}\right) = \fr
ac{2\varphi'(0)}{n} + \frac{\varphi'''(0)}{3n^3} + O\!\left(\tfrac{1}{n^5}\right
).$$

Multiplying by $n$:

$$\langle f_n, \varphi\rangle = 2\varphi'(0) + \frac{\varphi'''(0)}{3n^2} + O\!\

**Taking the limit.** As $n \to \infty$, the remainder vanishes, giving

$$\lim_{n\to\infty} \langle f_n, \varphi\rangle = 2\varphi'(0).$$

**Identifying the distribution.** The distributional derivative of the Dirac del
ta acts as

$$\langle \delta', \varphi \rangle = -\varphi'(0).$$

Therefore

$$2\varphi'(0) = -2\langle \delta', \varphi\rangle = \langle -2\delta', \varphi\
rangle.$$

**Conclusion.** The sequence $f_n$ converges in $\mathcal{D}'(\mathbb{R})$ to th
e distribution

$$\boxed{f = -2\,\delta'}.$$
