## Solution

The envelope of a one-parameter family $F(x,y,\alpha)=0$ is obtained by eliminat
ing $\alpha$ from the system
$$F(x,y,\alpha)=0,\qquad \frac{\partial F}{\partial \alpha}(x,y,\alpha)=0.$$

### (a) Family $y=\alpha x-\alpha^{2}$

Write $F(x,y,\alpha)=y-\alpha x+\alpha^{2}=0$.

Differentiate with respect to $\alpha$:
$$\frac{\partial F}{\partial \alpha}=-x+2\alpha=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \alph
a=\frac{x}{2}.$$

Substitute back into $F=0$:
$$y=\frac{x}{2}\,x-\Big(\frac{x}{2}\Big)^{2}=\frac{x^{2}}{2}-\frac{x^{2}}{4}=\fr
ac{x^{2}}{4}.$$

**Verification.** Every curve $y=\alpha x-\alpha^{2}$ is the line tangent to the
 parabola $y=x^{2}/4$ at the point $\big(2\alpha,\alpha^{2}\big)$: the tangent t
o $y=x^{2}/4$ at $x_{0}=2\alpha$ has slope $x_{0}/2=\alpha$ and equation $y=\alp
ha x-\alpha^{2}$. Hence the parabola is indeed tangent to every member of the fa
mily, confirming it is the envelope.

$$\boxed{\,y=\dfrac{x^{2}}{4}\,}$$

### (b) Family $\dfrac{x^{2}}{\alpha}+\dfrac{y^{2}}{1-\alpha}=1$, $\;0<\alpha<1$

Write $F(x,y,\alpha)=\dfrac{x^{2}}{\alpha}+\dfrac{y^{2}}{1-\alpha}-1=0$.

Differentiate with respect to $\alpha$:
$$\frac{\partial F}{\partial \alpha}=-\frac{x^{2}}{\alpha^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{(1-\
alpha)^{2}}=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{y^{2}}{(1-\alpha)^{2}}=\frac{x^{2}}
{\alpha^{2}}.\tag{1}$$

From (1), $y^{2}=x^{2}\,\dfrac{(1-\alpha)^{2}}{\alpha^{2}}$. Substitute into $F=
0$:
$$\frac{x^{2}}{\alpha}+\frac{x^{2}(1-\alpha)^{2}/\alpha^{2}}{1-\alpha}=1
\;\Longrightarrow\;
\frac{x^{2}}{\alpha}+\frac{x^{2}(1-\alpha)}{\alpha^{2}}=1
\;\Longrightarrow\;
x^{2}\!\left(\frac{\alpha}{\alpha^{2}}+\frac{1-\alpha}{\alpha^{2}}\right)=1
\;\Longrightarrow\;
\frac{x^{2}}{\alpha^{2}}=1.$$

Thus $x^{2}=\alpha^{2}$, i.e. $\alpha=|x|$ (taking the value in $(0,1)$). Then f
rom (1):
$$y^{2}=(1-\alpha)^{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad |y|=1-\alpha=1-|x|.$$

Therefore
$$|x|+|y|=1.$$

**Verification.** The family consists of ellipses centered at the origin with se
mi-axes $a=\sqrt{\alpha}$, $b=\sqrt{1-\alpha}$, so $a^{2}+b^{2}=1$. The curve $|
x|+|y|=1$ is the diamond (square rotated $45^{\circ}$) with vertices $(\pm1,0),(
0,\pm1)$.

Check tangency at a point in the first quadrant, say $(x_{0},y_{0})$ with $x_{0}
,y_{0}>0$ and $x_{0}+y_{0}=1$. The ellipse tangent to the line $x+y=1$ at $(x_{0
},y_{0})$ must have gradient parallel to $(1,1)$:
$$\Big(\frac{2x_{0}}{\alpha},\frac{2y_{0}}{1-\alpha}\Big)=\lambda(1,1)\;\Longrig
htarrow\;\frac{x_{0}}{\alpha}=\frac{y_{0}}{1-\alpha}\;\Longrightarrow\;\alpha=\f
rac{x_{0}}{x_{0}+y_{0}}=x_{0}.$$
Then $1-\alpha=y_{0}$, and the ellipse is $x^{2}/x_{0}+y^{2}/y_{0}=1$, which con
tains $(x_{0},y_{0})$ since $x_{0}+y_{0}=1$. The tangent line there is $x x_{0}/
x_{0}+y y_{0}/y_{0}=1$, i.e. $x+y=1$, matching the side of the diamond. The othe
r three sides are handled by symmetry. Hence the diamond is tangent to every ell
ipse of the family, confirming it is the envelope.

$$\boxed{\,|x|+|y|=1\,}$$
