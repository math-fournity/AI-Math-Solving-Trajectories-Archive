Looking at this problem, I need to determine whether the Quillen-Suslin theorem
(every finitely generated projective module over k[x₁,…,xₘ] is free, for a field
 k) remains true when k is replaced by ℤ.

**Answer: Yes, the theorem remains true.** Below is the proof.

---

## Theorem

Every finitely generated projective module over ℤ[x₁,…,xₘ] is free.

## Proof

We proceed by induction on m ≥ 0, using two classical tools: **Horrocks' theorem
** (proved in full below) and **Quillen's patching theorem** (stated with proof
sketch).

### Base case (m = 0)

ℤ is a PID. Every finitely generated projective ℤ-module is torsion-free, hence
free (over a PID, finitely generated + torsion-free ⟹ free). ✓

### Inductive step

Assume every finitely generated projective module over R := ℤ[x₁,…,xₘ₋₁] is free
. Let M be a finitely generated projective R[x]-module (writing x = xₘ). We show
 M is free.

---

### Tool 1 — Horrocks' Theorem (with full proof)

**Statement.** Let (A, 𝔫) be a commutative local ring and P a finitely generated
 projective A[x]-module. If P/𝔫P is free over (A/𝔫)[x], then P is free over A[x]
.

**Proof.** Since P/𝔫P is free over κ[x] (κ = A/𝔫), choose an isomorphism κ[x]ʳ →
 P/𝔫P and lift it to a map φ: A[x]ʳ → P. Since P is projective, φ splits:

$$A[x]^r = P \oplus K, \quad K = \ker(\varphi).$$

Reducing mod 𝔫 gives κ[x]ʳ = (P/𝔫P) ⊕ (K/𝔫K). Since P/𝔫P ≅ κ[x]ʳ, we obtain K/𝔫K
 = 0, i.e., **K = 𝔫K**.

Now let f ∈ A[x] be any **monic** polynomial. Since f is monic, A[x]/(f) is a fi
nite free A-module, hence integral over A. Every maximal ideal of A[x]/(f) lies
over 𝔫 (the unique maximal ideal of A), so 𝔫·A[x]/(f) is contained in the **Jaco
bson radical** of A[x]/(f). The module K/fK is finitely generated over A[x]/(f)
and satisfies K/fK = 𝔫·(K/fK) (from K = 𝔫K). By **Nakayama's lemma**, K/fK = 0,
so **K = fK**.

Take **f = xᵈ** for d sufficiently large. Then K = xᵈK means every element k ∈ K
 satisfies k = xᵈk′ for some k′ ∈ K, hence k = x²ᵈk″, and so on. So every compon
ent of k (viewing K ⊂ A[x]ʳ) is divisible by xⁿᵈ for all n ≥ 1. A polynomial div
isible by xⁿᵈ for all n must be zero (its first nd coefficients vanish for every
 n). Therefore **K = 0**, and P ≅ A[x]ʳ is free. ∎

---

### Tool 2 — Quillen's Patching Theorem (stated, with proof sketch)

**Statement.** Let A be a commutative ring and P a finitely generated projective
 A[x]-module. Then P is **extended from A** (i.e., P ≅ Q ⊗_A A[x] for some finit
ely generated projective A-module Q) if and only if P_𝔪 is extended from A_𝔪 for
 every maximal ideal 𝔪 of A.

**Proof sketch.** The "only if" direction is immediate (extension commutes with
localization). For the "if" direction, the key ingredient is **Quillen's Lemma**
: given s ∈ A with P_s extended from A_s, and t ∈ A with (s, t) = A, there exist
s n ≥ 1 such that the extension over A_s patches compatibly over A_{sⁿt} with th
e situation over A_t. The proof uses a polynomial substitution trick (replacing
x by x + sⁿt) to align the transition matrices defining the local extensions. It
erating this patching over a finite cover of Spec(A) by principal open sets yiel
ds a global extension Q over A. (Full details: Quillen, *Invent. Math.* 36, 1976
; or Lam, *Serre's Problem on Projective Modules*, Ch. V.) □

---

### Completing the induction

**Step 1 (Local freeness).** Let 𝔪 be a maximal ideal of R. Then (R_𝔪, 𝔪R_𝔪) is
a local ring. The residue field κ(𝔪) = R/𝔪 is a field — by **Zariski's lemma**,
since R = ℤ[x₁,…,xₘ₋₁] is a finitely generated ℤ-algebra, so R/𝔪 is a finite ext
ension of 𝔽_p or ℚ, hence a field.

The reduction

$$M_\mathfrak{m} / \mathfrak{m}R_\mathfrak{m} \cdot M_\mathfrak{m} \;\cong\; M \
otimes_R \kappa(\mathfrak{m})$$

is a finitely generated projective module over κ(𝔪)[x]. Since κ(𝔪) is a field, κ
(𝔪)[x] is a **PID** (polynomial ring in one variable over a field), so every fin
itely generated projective module over it is **free**. By **Horrocks' theorem**,
 M_𝔪 is free over R_𝔪[x]. ✓

**Step 2 (Patching).** By Step 1, M_𝔪 is free — hence extended — over R_𝔪[x] for
 every maximal ideal 𝔪 of R. By **Quillen's patching theorem**, M is extended fr
om R: there exists a finitely generated projective R-module Q with M ≅ Q ⊗_R R[x
]. ✓

**Step 3 (Freeness).** By the **inductive hypothesis**, every finitely generated
 projective R-module is free. So Q ≅ Rⁿ for some n, and therefore

$$M \;\cong\; R^n \otimes_R R[x] \;\cong\; R[x]^n$$

is free. ✓

---

### Conclusion

By induction on m, every finitely generated projective module over ℤ[x₁,…,xₘ] is
 free for all m ≥ 0. The proof uses exactly the same blueprint as the classical
Quillen–Suslin theorem over a field: the base case relies on the fact that finit
ely generated projectives over the base ring are free (trivial for a field, a cl
assical fact for the PID ℤ), and the inductive step uses Horrocks' theorem and Q
uillen's patching, which are independent of the base ring being a field.

**The Quillen-Suslin theorem remains true when 𝕜 is replaced by ℤ.** ∎
