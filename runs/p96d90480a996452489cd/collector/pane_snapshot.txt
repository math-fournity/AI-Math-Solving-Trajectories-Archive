# Traffic Flow Problem Solution

## Setup

The PDE is the scalar conservation law $\partial_t \rho + \partial_x f(\rho) = 0
$ with flux

$$f(\rho) = \rho\, u(\rho) = \rho\, u_{\max}\!\left(1 - \tfrac{\rho}{\rho_{\max}
}\right) = u_{\max}\!\left(\rho - \tfrac{\rho^2}{\rho_{\max}}\right).$$

The characteristic speed is

$$c(\rho) = f'(\rho) = u_{\max}\!\left(1 - \tfrac{2\rho}{\rho_{\max}}\right), \q
quad f''(\rho) = -\tfrac{2u_{\max}}{\rho_{\max}} < 0.$$

Since $f$ is **strictly concave**, $f'$ is strictly decreasing in $\rho$. The Ri
emann-problem rule for concave flux is:

- $\rho_L > \rho_R \;\Rightarrow\;$ **rarefaction fan** (left characteristics sl
ower than right),
- $\rho_L < \rho_R \;\Rightarrow\;$ **shock**.

The self-similar rarefaction connecting $\rho_L$ to $\rho_R$ (with $\rho_L>\rho_
R$) is

$$\rho(x,t) = (f')^{-1}\!\left(\tfrac{x}{t}\right) = \tfrac{\rho_{\max}}{2}\!\le
ft(1 - \tfrac{x}{t\,u_{\max}}\right), \qquad f'(\rho_L) < \tfrac{x}{t} < f'(\rho
_R).$$

---

## (a) $\rho_L = \rho_{\max}$, $\rho_R = 0$ (red light turns green)

Characteristic speeds at the two states:

$$c(\rho_{\max}) = u_{\max}(1-2) = -u_{\max}, \qquad c(0) = u_{\max}.$$

Since $-u_{\max} < u_{\max}$, the characteristics diverge: a **centered rarefact
ion fan** emanates from the origin.

$$\boxed{\rho(x,t) = \begin{cases} \rho_{\max}, & \dfrac{x}{t} < -u_{\max},\\[6p
t] \dfrac{\rho_{\max}}{2}\!\left(1 - \dfrac{x}{t\,u_{\max}}\right), & -u_{\max}
< \dfrac{x}{t} < u_{\max},\\[6pt] 0, & \dfrac{x}{t} > u_{\max}. \end{cases}}$$

**Interpretation.** The front of the jam (leftmost cars, $\rho=\rho_{\max}$) mov
es backward at speed $u_{\max}$ (information propagates upstream), while the lea
d cars ($\rho\to 0$) move forward at speed $u_{\max}$. The density decreases lin
early across the fan — the jam dissolves smoothly.

## (b) Three-state initial data

$$\rho(x,0) = \begin{cases} \rho_{\max}, & x<0,\\ \rho_{\max}/2, & 0<x<a,\\ 0, &
 x>a. \end{cases}$$

This is **two Riemann problems**: at $x=0$ ($\rho_{\max}\to \rho_{\max}/2$) and
at $x=a$ ($\rho_{\max}/2\to 0$). Both have $\rho_L>\rho_R$ with concave flux, so
 both produce rarefaction fans.

**Fan at $x=0$.** Speeds: $c(\rho_{\max})=-u_{\max}$, $c(\rho_{\max}/2)=u_{\max}
(1-1)=0$. Fan occupies $-u_{\max} < x/t < 0$:
$$\rho = \tfrac{\rho_{\max}}{2}\!\left(1 - \tfrac{x}{t\,u_{\max}}\right), \qquad
 -u_{\max}t < x < 0.$$

**Fan at $x=a$.** Speeds: $c(\rho_{\max}/2)=0$, $c(0)=u_{\max}$. Fan occupies $0
 < (x-a)/t < u_{\max}$:
$$\rho = \tfrac{\rho_{\max}}{2}\!\left(1 - \tfrac{x-a}{t\,u_{\max}}\right), \qqu
ad a < x < a + u_{\max}t.$$

**Interaction check.** The right edge of the left fan sits at $x=0$ (since $c(\r
ho_{\max}/2)=0$, it is stationary). The left edge of the right fan sits at $x=a$
 (also stationary). The two fans therefore **never overlap**; the middle slab $0
<x<a$ remains at the constant state $\rho_{\max}/2$ for all $t>0$.

$$\boxed{\rho(x,t) = \begin{cases} \rho_{\max}, & \dfrac{x}{t} < -u_{\max},\\[6p
t] \dfrac{\rho_{\max}}{2}\!\left(1 - \dfrac{x}{t\,u_{\max}}\right), & -u_{\max}
< \dfrac{x}{t} < 0,\\[6pt] \dfrac{\rho_{\max}}{2}, & 0 < x < a,\\[6pt] \dfrac{\r
ho_{\max}}{2}\!\left(1 - \dfrac{x-a}{t\,u_{\max}}\right), & 0 < \dfrac{x-a}{t} <
 u_{\max},\\[6pt] 0, & \dfrac{x-a}{t} > u_{\max}. \end{cases}}$$

**Interpretation.** The middle block of half-density traffic is "sandwiched" bet
ween two rarefactions. Its left boundary (where $\rho=\rho_{\max}/2$ meets the l
eft fan) is stationary because $c(\rho_{\max}/2)=0$; likewise its right boundary
 is stationary. So the block of density $\rho_{\max}/2$ neither expands nor cont
racts — it persists unchanged while the two fans dissipate the jam on the left a
nd the vacuum on the right.

## (c) $\rho_L = 3\rho_{\max}/5$, $\rho_R = \rho_{\max}/5$

Characteristic speeds:

$$c\!\left(\tfrac{3\rho_{\max}}{5}\right) = u_{\max}\!\left(1 - \tfrac{6}{5}\rig
ht) = -\tfrac{u_{\max}}{5}, \qquad c\!\left(\tfrac{\rho_{\max}}{5}\right) = u_{\
max}\!\left(1 - \tfrac{2}{5}\right) = \tfrac{3u_{\max}}{5}.$$

Since $-u_{\max}/5 < 3u_{\max}/5$, the characteristics diverge: a **centered rar
efaction fan**.

$$\boxed{\rho(x,t) = \begin{cases} \dfrac{3\rho_{\max}}{5}, & \dfrac{x}{t} < -\d
frac{u_{\max}}{5},\\[6pt] \dfrac{\rho_{\max}}{2}\!\left(1 - \dfrac{x}{t\,u_{\max
}}\right), & -\dfrac{u_{\max}}{5} < \dfrac{x}{t} < \dfrac{3u_{\max}}{5},\\[6pt]
\dfrac{\rho_{\max}}{5}, & \dfrac{x}{t} > \dfrac{3u_{\max}}{5}. \end{cases}}$$

**Verification of fan endpoints.**
- At $x/t = -u_{\max}/5$: $\rho = \tfrac{\rho_{\max}}{2}(1+\tfrac{1}{5}) = \tfra
c{\rho_{\max}}{2}\cdot\tfrac{6}{5} = \tfrac{3\rho_{\max}}{5}$. ✓
- At $x/t = 3u_{\max}/5$: $\rho = \tfrac{\rho_{\max}}{2}(1-\tfrac{3}{5}) = \tfra
c{\rho_{\max}}{2}\cdot\tfrac{2}{5} = \tfrac{\rho_{\max}}{5}$. ✓

The rarefaction smoothly interpolates between the two constant states; the left
edge travels upstream at $u_{\max}/5$ and the right edge travels downstream at $
3u_{\max}/5$.

## Summary of method

All three parts are Riemann (or multi-Riemann) problems for a concave flux $f(\r
ho)=u_{\max}(\rho-\rho^2/\rho_{\max})$. Because $f''<0$, every case with $\rho_L
>\rho_R$ (decreasing density, i.e. cars accelerating away) yields a **rarefactio
n fan** whose interior is the linear profile $\rho = \tfrac{\rho_{\max}}{2}(1 -
x/(t\,u_{\max})) = (f')^{-1}(x/t)$, bounded by the characteristic speeds $c(\rho
_L)$ and $c(\rho_R)$ of the adjacent constant states. In part (b) the two fans a
re pinned at $x=0$ and $x=a$ by the stationary speed $c(\rho_{\max}/2)=0$, so th
ey never interact and the intermediate state $\rho_{\max}/2$ persists.
