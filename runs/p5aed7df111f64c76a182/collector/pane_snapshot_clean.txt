# Solution

## Setup

Let $R = K[x_1, \dots, x_5]$ localized at $\mathfrak{m} = (x_1, \dots, x_5)$, th
e maximal ideal of the origin. The subscheme $V(\mathfrak{m}^k)$ is defined by t
he ideal $\mathfrak{m}^k$.

The condition $V(\mathfrak{m}^3) \subset \Gamma \subset V(\mathfrak{m}^4)$ means
, at the ideal level:
$$\mathfrak{m}^4 \subset I(\Gamma) \subset \mathfrak{m}^3.$$

## Classifying the ideals

Let $I = I(\Gamma)$ with $\mathfrak{m}^4 \subset I \subset \mathfrak{m}^3$. Set
$W = I/\mathfrak{m}^4$, a $K$-vector subspace of $\mathfrak{m}^3/\mathfrak{m}^4$
.

**Claim:** Every $K$-vector subspace $W \subset \mathfrak{m}^3/\mathfrak{m}^4$ y
ields a valid ideal $I = \mathfrak{m}^4 + \widetilde{W}$ (where $\widetilde{W}$
is any lift of $W$ to $\mathfrak{m}^3$).

*Proof.* We must check $I$ is an ideal. Take $f \in I$, $g \in R$:
- If $f \in \mathfrak{m}^4$: then $fg \in \mathfrak{m}^4 \subset I$. ✓
- If $f \in \widetilde{W} \subset \mathfrak{m}^3$: write $g = c + h$ with $c \in
 K$, $h \in \mathfrak{m}$. Then $fg = cf + fh$. Since $f \in \mathfrak{m}^3$ and
 $h \in \mathfrak{m}$, we get $fh \in \mathfrak{m}^4 \subset I$. And $cf \in \wi
detilde{W}$ since $\widetilde{W}$ is a $K$-vector space. So $fg \in I$. ✓

Thus ideals $I$ with $\mathfrak{m}^4 \subset I \subset \mathfrak{m}^3$ are in bi
jection with subspaces $W \subset \mathfrak{m}^3/\mathfrak{m}^4$.

## Computing the degree constraint

The space $\mathfrak{m}^3/\mathfrak{m}^4$ is the degree-3 component of the grade
d ring, with dimension equal to the number of monomials of degree exactly 3 in 5
 variables:
$$\dim_K(\mathfrak{m}^3/\mathfrak{m}^4) = \binom{5+3-1}{3} = \binom{7}{3} = 35.$
$

The coordinate ring of $\Gamma$ is:
$$R/I \cong (R/\mathfrak{m}^4)\big/ W,$$
where $W$ sits in the degree-3 part. Now $\dim_K(R/\mathfrak{m}^4)$ counts monom
ials of degree $0,1,2,3$ in 5 variables:
$$\underbrace{1}_{\deg 0} + \underbrace{5}_{\deg 1} + \underbrace{15}_{\deg 2} +
 \underbrace{35}_{\deg 3} = 56.$$

The degree of $\Gamma$ is $\dim_K(R/I) = 56 - \dim_K(W)$. Setting this equal to
25:
$$\dim_K(W) = 56 - 25 = 31.$$

## Dimension of the family

The family of such subschemes $\Gamma$ is parameterized by the **Grassmannian**
$\operatorname{Gr}(31, 35)$ of 31-dimensional subspaces of a 35-dimensional vect
or space.

Its dimension is:
$$\dim \operatorname{Gr}(31, 35) = 31 \cdot (35 - 31) = 31 \cdot 4 = \boxed{124}
.$$
