Looking at this problem, I need to find the range of λ₁/λ₂ + λ₂/λ₁ where the λ's
 come from a line through M(1,-1) intersecting the right branch of x²/4 - y² = 1
.

## Setup

**Line through M(1,-1) with slope k:** y = kx − k − 1.

**Point N on x-axis:** Setting y = 0 gives N = (1 + 1/k, 0), requiring k ≠ 0.

**Parametrize the line** by t: P(t) = M + t(N − M) = (1 + t/k, −1 + t), where t
= 0 gives M and t = 1 gives N.

**Substitute into the hyperbola** x²/4 − y² = 1:

$$t^2(1 - 4k^2) + t(2k + 8k^2) - 7k^2 = 0$$

with roots t₁, t₂ (parameters for A, B).

**Relating λ to t:** Since MA = λ₁·AN and A = P(t₁), we get t₁(N−M) = λ₁(1−t₁)(N
−M), so **λᵢ = tᵢ/(1−tᵢ)**.

## Computing the expression

Let S = t₁ + t₂, P = t₁t₂. From the quadratic:

- S = 2k(1+4k)/(4k²−1), P = 7k²/(4k²−1)

After algebra:

$$\frac{\lambda_1}{\lambda_2} + \frac{\lambda_2}{\lambda_1} = \frac{(S-2P)^2}{(1
-S+P)P} - 2 = \frac{-2(3k^2 - 2k - 9)}{7(3k^2 - 2k - 1)}$$

Denote this as **f(k)**.

## Domain of k

**Two intersection points on the right branch (x ≥ 2):** Working with x-coordina
tes of intersections, both roots x₁, x₂ ≥ 2 requires:

- (x₁−2)(x₂−2) = 4(k−1)²/(4k²−1) ≥ 0 → |k| > 1/2
- x₁ + x₂ ≥ 4 → k ∈ [(1−√3)/2, (1+√3)/2]

Combined with the discriminant condition (1−√7)/3 < k < (1+√7)/3, this gives:

$$k \in \left(\tfrac{1}{2},\, \tfrac{1+\sqrt{7}}{3}\right)$$

Excluding k = 1 (where N coincides with a vertex point, making λ infinite), the
domain is:

$$k \in \left(\tfrac{1}{2}, 1\right) \cup \left(1, \tfrac{1+\sqrt{7}}{3}\right)$
$

## Monotonicity and range

$$f'(k) = \frac{-32(3k-1)}{7(3k^2 - 2k - 1)^2}$$

Since k > 1/2 > 1/3, we have 3k−1 > 0, so **f'(k) < 0** — f is strictly decreasi
ng on each piece.

**On (1/2, 1):**
- k → (1/2)⁺: f → −2·(37/5)/7 = **−74/35**
- k → 1⁻: f → −∞

Range: **(−∞, −74/35)**

**On (1, (1+√7)/3):**
- k → 1⁺: f → +∞
- k → ((1+√7)/3)⁻: At this value, 3k²−2k = 2, so f = −2(−7)/(7·1) = **2**

Range: **(2, +∞)**

## Result

The range of λ₁/λ₂ + λ₂/λ₁ is (−∞, −74/35) ∪ (2, +∞), matching the form (−∞, a)
∪ (b, +∞) with:

$$a = -\frac{74}{35}, \quad b = 2$$

$$35a + b = 35 \cdot \left(-\frac{74}{35}\right) + 2 = -74 + 2 = \boxed{-72}$$
