## Solution

We need to count lattice points $(x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_{10}) \in \mathbb{Z}^{10}$
 at distance exactly $3$ from the origin, i.e., integer solutions to

$$x_1^2 + x_2^2 + \cdots + x_{10}^2 = 9.$$

Since each $x_i^2 \ge 0$ and $4^2 = 16 > 9$, every $|x_i| \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$. T
he possible squared values are $\{0, 1, 4, 9\}$. We partition $9$ into a multise
t of these squared values; each partition determines the absolute values of the
nonzero coordinates, and we then assign positions and signs.

**The partitions of $9$ into parts from $\{1, 4, 9\}$:**

1. $\{9\}$ — one coordinate with $|x_i| = 3$.
2. $\{4, 4, 1\}$ — two coordinates with $|x_i| = 2$, one with $|x_i| = 1$.
3. $\{4, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1\}$ — one coordinate with $|x_i| = 2$, five with $|x_i| =
1$.
4. $\{1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1\}$ — nine coordinates with $|x_i| = 1$.

(No partition can include $9$ together with anything else, since $9 + (\text{pos
itive}) > 9$. Three $4$'s give $12 > 9$, so at most two $4$'s. These four cases
are exhaustive.)

**Counting each case:**

**Case 1: $\{3\}$.** Choose the coordinate: $10$ ways. Choose the sign: $2$ ways
.
$$10 \times 2 = 20.$$

**Case 2: $\{2, 2, 1\}$.** Choose $2$ coordinates (out of $10$) for the $2$'s: $
\binom{10}{2} = 45$. Choose $1$ coordinate (out of remaining $8$) for the $1$: $
8$ ways. Choose signs: $2^3 = 8$.
$$45 \times 8 \times 8 = 2880.$$

**Case 3: $\{2, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1\}$.** Choose $1$ coordinate for the $2$: $10$ ways
. Choose $5$ coordinates (out of remaining $9$) for the $1$'s: $\binom{9}{5} = 1
26$. Choose signs: $2 \times 2^5 = 2^6 = 64$.
$$10 \times 126 \times 64 = 80640.$$

**Case 4: $\{1 \times 9\}$.** Choose $9$ coordinates (out of $10$) to be nonzero
: $\binom{10}{9} = 10$. Choose signs: $2^9 = 512$.
$$10 \times 512 = 5120.$$

**Total:**
$$20 + 2880 + 80640 + 5120 = 88660.$$

GM Bisain can land on $\boxed{88660}$ lattice points.
