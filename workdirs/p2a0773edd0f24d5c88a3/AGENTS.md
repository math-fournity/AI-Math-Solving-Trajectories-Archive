# Problem

In the coastal city of Aethelgard, a Grand Council oversees the distribution of resources across various districts. For any given number of districts $n$, the Council aims to establish a network of "Trade Guilds." Each Trade Guild must consist of exactly 3 distinct districts. To ensure balanced competition and interconnectedness, the Council enforces a strict "Unity Rule": every pair of Trade Guilds must share exactly one district in common.

Let $k_n$ represent the maximum possible number of Trade Guilds that can be formed in a city with $n$ districts while adhering to the Unity Rule.

A regional governor is analyzing the growth of these networks as the city expands. Calculate the total sum of these maximums as the number of districts $n$ increases from 3 to 20:

$$\sum_{n=3}^{20} k_n$$
