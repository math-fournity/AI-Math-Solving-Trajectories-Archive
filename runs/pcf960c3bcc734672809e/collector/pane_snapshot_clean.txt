## Solution

**Setup.** Let $a(n)$ denote the EUs generated on Day $n$. The sequence satisfie
s:
$$a(1)=a(2)=a(3)=1, \qquad a(n)=a(n-1)+a(n-2)+a(n-3)\text{ for }n\ge 4.$$

Let $S(n)=\sum_{k=1}^{n}a(k)$ be the cumulative sum through Day $n$. We seek $S(
28)\bmod 1000$.

**A recurrence for the partial sums.** Since $a(k)=a(k-1)+a(k-2)+a(k-3)$ for $k\
ge 4$, summing over $k=4,\dots,n$:

$$\sum_{k=4}^{n}a(k)=\sum_{k=4}^{n}a(k-1)+\sum_{k=4}^{n}a(k-2)+\sum_{k=4}^{n}a(k
-3).$$

Re-indexing each sum in terms of $S$:

$$(S(n)-S(3))=(S(n-1)-S(2))+(S(n-2)-S(1))+S(n-3).$$

With $S(1)=1,\,S(2)=2,\,S(3)=3$, the constants cancel ($S(3)-S(2)-S(1)=3-2-1=0$)
, giving:

$$\boxed{S(n)=S(n-1)+S(n-2)+S(n-3)},\qquad S(1)=1,\;S(2)=2,\;S(3)=3.$$

So $S$ obeys the **same Tribonacci recurrence** with shifted initial values.
