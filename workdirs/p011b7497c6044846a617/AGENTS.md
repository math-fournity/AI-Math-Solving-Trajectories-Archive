# Problem

In a sprawling coastal region, three massive construction companies are laying down high-speed transit tracks across a vast, flat plain. Each company is responsible for a "corridor" consisting of exactly 10 perfectly parallel tracks. 

The three corridors are oriented in different directions so that no two corridors are parallel to one another. Furthermore, to maximize the complexity of the network, the engineers have ensured that no three individual tracks from different companies ever intersect at the same exact point.

As these 30 tracks crisscross the plain, they create various enclosed regions. A "transit hub" is formed wherever three tracks intersect to create a triangular enclosure. What is the maximum number of such triangular enclosures that can be formed across the entire plane?
