Looking at this problem, I need to solve a damped wave equation with Dirichlet b
oundary conditions on $[0,\pi]$.

## Setup: Separation of Variables

The eigenfunctions satisfying $u(0,t)=u(\pi,t)=0$ are $\sin(nx)$, $n=1,2,3,\dots
$. Expand:

$$u(x,t)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}T_n(t)\sin(nx).$$

Substituting into $u_{tt}+u_t=u_{xx}$ gives the ODE for each mode:

$$T_n''+T_n'+n^2 T_n=0.$$

## Solving the Temporal ODE

Characteristic equation $r^2+r+n^2=0$ yields $r=\tfrac{-1\pm\sqrt{1-4n^2}}{2}$.
For $n\geq 1$ the discriminant is negative, so set

$$\omega_n=\frac{\sqrt{4n^2-1}}{2},\qquad r=-\frac{1}{2}\pm i\,\omega_n.$$

Thus

$$T_n(t)=e^{-t/2}\bigl(A_n\cos\omega_n t+B_n\sin\omega_n t\bigr).$$

## Sine-Series Expansion of $x\sin x$

Compute $c_n=\frac{2}{\pi}\int_0^\pi x\sin x\,\sin(nx)\,dx$.

Using $\sin x\sin(nx)=\tfrac12[\cos((n{-}1)x)-\cos((n{+}1)x)]$ and $\int_0^\pi x
\cos(kx)\,dx=\frac{(-1)^k-1}{k^2}$ for integer $k\neq 0$:

**Case $n=1$:** the $k=0$ term gives $\int_0^\pi x\,dx=\pi^2/2$, and $\int_0^\pi
 x\cos(2x)\,dx=0$, so

$$\int_0^\pi x\sin^2 x\,dx=\tfrac12\bigl(\tfrac{\pi^2}{2}-0\bigr)=\tfrac{\pi^2}{
4},\qquad c_1=\frac{2}{\pi}\cdot\frac{\pi^2}{4}=\frac{\pi}{2}.$$

**Case $n\geq 2$:** with $\varepsilon=(-1)^{n-1}$,

$$\int_0^\pi x\sin x\sin(nx)\,dx=\frac{\varepsilon-1}{2}\left[\frac{1}{(n-1)^2}-
\frac{1}{(n+1)^2}\right]=\frac{2n(\varepsilon-1)}{(n-1)^2(n+1)^2},$$

$$c_n=\frac{4n(\varepsilon-1)}{\pi(n-1)^2(n+1)^2}.$$

- **Odd $n\geq 3$:** $\varepsilon=1\Rightarrow c_n=0$.
- **Even $n=2k$:** $\varepsilon=-1\Rightarrow c_{2k}=\dfrac{-16k}{\pi(2k-1)^2(2k
+1)^2}$.

(Verified: $c_2=\frac{-16}{9\pi}$ matches direct integration; at $x=\pi/2$ the s
eries gives $\frac{\pi}{2}$, matching $x\sin x$.)

## Applying Initial Conditions

From $u(x,0)=x\sin x$: $\;T_n(0)=A_n=c_n$.

From $u_t(x,0)=0$: $\;T_n'(0)=-\frac12 A_n+B_n\omega_n=0\;\Rightarrow\;B_n=\frac
{c_n}{2\omega_n}$.

Therefore

$$T_n(t)=c_n\,e^{-t/2}\left[\cos(\omega_n t)+\frac{1}{2\omega_n}\sin(\omega_n t)
\right].$$

## Solution

$$\boxed{u(x,t)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}c_n\,e^{-t/2}\left[\cos\!\left(\tfrac{\sqrt{4
n^2-1}}{2}\,t\right)+\frac{1}{\sqrt{4n^2-1}}\sin\!\left(\tfrac{\sqrt{4n^2-1}}{2}
\,t\right)\right]\sin(nx),}$$

where

$$c_1=\frac{\pi}{2},\qquad c_{2k}=\frac{-16k}{\pi(2k-1)^2(2k+1)^2}\;(k\geq 1),\q
quad c_n=0\;\text{for odd }n\geq 3.$$

Equivalently, written out explicitly:

$$u(x,t)=\frac{\pi}{2}\,e^{-t/2}\!\left[\cos\omega_1 t+\frac{\sin\omega_1 t}{\sq
rt{3}}\right]\sin x-\frac{16}{\pi}\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{k\,e^{-t/2}}{(2k-1)^2
(2k+1)^2}\left[\cos\omega_{2k}\,t+\frac{\sin\omega_{2k}\,t}{\sqrt{16k^2-1}}\righ
t]\sin(2kx),$$

with $\omega_n=\frac{\sqrt{4n^2-1}}{2}$.

## Verification

- **PDE:** Each term $e^{-t/2}[\cos\omega_n t+\frac{1}{2\omega_n}\sin\omega_n t]
\sin(nx)$ satisfies $T_n''+T_n'+n^2T_n=0$ by construction. ✓
- **Boundary conditions:** $\sin(0)=\sin(n\pi)=0$ for all $n$. ✓
- **$u(x,0)=x\sin x$:** $T_n(0)=c_n$ and $\sum c_n\sin(nx)=x\sin x$ by the sine
series. ✓
- **$u_t(x,0)=0$:** $T_n'(0)=-\frac12 c_n+\frac{c_n}{2\omega_n}\omega_n=0$. ✓
