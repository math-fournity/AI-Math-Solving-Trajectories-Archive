The generalized (Hurwitz) zeta function satisfies
$$\zeta(3,n)=\sum_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^{3}}.$$

Since $f(x)=x^{-3}$ is positive and strictly decreasing on $(0,\infty)$, for eve

$$\int_{k}^{k+1}x^{-3}\,dx \;\le\; \frac{1}{k^{3}} \;\le\; \int_{k-1}^{k}x^{-3}\
,dx.$$

Summing the right-hand inequality over $k=n,n+1,n+2,\dots$ gives
$$\zeta(3,n)=\sum_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^{3}}\;\le\;\int_{n-1}^{\infty}x^{-3}\
,dx=\frac{1}{2(n-1)^{2}}.$$

Summing the left-hand inequality over $k=n,n+1,n+2,\dots$ gives
$$\zeta(3,n)=\sum_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^{3}}\;\ge\;\int_{n}^{\infty}x^{-3}\,d
x=\frac{1}{2n^{2}}.$$

Thus, for all $n\ge 2$,
$$\frac{1}{2n^{2}}\;\le\;\zeta(3,n)\;\le\;\frac{1}{2(n-1)^{2}}.$$

The denominator satisfies $\frac{1}{n}+\frac{1}{3n^{3}}\ge\frac{1}{n}>0$, so div
iding through preserves the inequalities:
$$\frac{\frac{1}{2n^{2}}}{\frac{1}{n}+\frac{1}{3n^{3}}}\;\le\;\frac{\zeta(3,n)}{
\frac{1}{n}+\frac{1}{3n^{3}}}\;\le\;\frac{\frac{1}{2(n-1)^{2}}}{\frac{1}{n}+\fra
c{1}{3n^{3}}}.$$

**Upper bound.** Since $\frac{1}{n}+\frac{1}{3n^{3}}\ge\frac{1}{n}$,
$$\frac{\zeta(3,n)}{\frac{1}{n}+\frac{1}{3n^{3}}}\;\le\;\frac{\frac{1}{2(n-1)^{2

$$0\;\le\;\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\zeta(3,n)}{\frac{1}{n}+\frac{1}{3n^{3}}}\;\le\
