# Problem

In a remote desert, three research stations—Alpha (A), Beta (B), and Gamma (C)—form a perfectly equilateral triangle of outposts connected by straight service roads. A technician, M, is stationed exactly halfway between Alpha and Beta along the northern road. Meanwhile, a supply depot, D, is located on the road between Beta and Gamma such that the distance from Beta to the depot is three times the distance from the depot to Gamma.

To optimize communications, a drone is launched from Gamma into the interior of the triangular region. The drone flies along a path that remains perfectly parallel to the straight line connecting technician M and depot D. The drone eventually hovers at a specific observation point, T. From this coordinate, the angle formed by looking toward Gamma and then toward Alpha (angle CTA) is measured to be exactly 150 degrees.

Based on the spatial coordinates of this configuration, determine the measure of the angle MTD (the angle formed between the lines connecting the observation point T to the technician M and the depot D) in degrees.
