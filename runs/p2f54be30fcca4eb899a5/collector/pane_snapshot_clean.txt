$$S - a = (n-1)(b + (k-1)m), \quad S - b = (n-1)(b + km),$$

$$T_a \ge a \implies b + (k-1)m \ge b + m(n-1) \implies k \ge n.$$
This is impossible since $k \le n-1$. Therefore $k-1 = 0$, i.e., **$k = 1$**: ex
