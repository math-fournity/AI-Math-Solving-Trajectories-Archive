## Solution

Let
$$L_m = \sqrt[m]{m+1}\cdot\sqrt[m^2+m]{\binom{m}{1}\binom{m}{2}\cdots\binom{m}{m
}}.$$

Taking logarithms,
$$\ln L_m = \underbrace{\frac{\ln(m+1)}{m}}_{\to\,0} + \frac{1}{m(m+1)}\sum_{j=1
}^{m}\ln\binom{m}{j}.$$

The first term tends to $0$, so we focus on the second.

**Product identity.** Since $\binom{m}{0}=\binom{m}{m}=1$,
$$\prod_{j=1}^{m}\binom{m}{j}=\prod_{j=0}^{m}\binom{m}{j}=\prod_{j=0}^{m}\frac{m
!}{j!(m-j)!}=\frac{(m!)^{m+1}}{\left(\prod_{k=0}^{m}k!\right)^{2}},$$
using $\prod_{j=0}^{m}(m-j)!=\prod_{k=0}^{m}k!$. Hence
$$S_m:=\sum_{j=1}^{m}\ln\binom{m}{j}=(m+1)\ln m!-2\sum_{k=1}^{m}\ln k!.$$

**Stirling expansion.** With $\ln n!=n\ln n-n+\tfrac12\ln(2\pi n)+O(1/n)$ and $\
sum_{k=1}^{m}k\ln k=\tfrac{m^2}{2}\ln m-\tfrac{m^2}{4}+O(m\ln m)$ (Euler–Maclaur
in / integral $\int_1^m x\ln x\,dx$):

- $(m+1)\ln m!=(m^2+m)\ln m-m^2-m+\tfrac{m}{2}\ln(2\pi m)+O(\ln m)$;
- $\sum_{k=1}^{m}\ln k!=\sum(k\ln k-k)+\tfrac12\sum\ln(2\pi k)+O(\ln m)$
  $=\bigl(\tfrac{m^2}{2}\ln m-\tfrac{m^2}{4}\bigr)-\tfrac{m(m+1)}{2}+\tfrac12\bi
gl(m\ln(2\pi)+\ln m!\bigr)+O(\ln m)$
  $=\tfrac{m^2}{2}\ln m-\tfrac{3m^2}{4}+\tfrac{m}{2}\ln m+\tfrac{m}{2}\ln(2\pi)-
m+O(\ln m)$.

Therefore, keeping only terms up to $O(m\ln m)$,
$$S_m=\bigl[(m^2+m)\ln m-m^2-m\bigr]-2\bigl[\tfrac{m^2}{2}\ln m-\tfrac{3m^2}{4}\
bigr]+O(m\ln m)$$
$$=m\ln m+\tfrac{m^2}{2}+O(m\ln m).$$

(The precise subleading term is $\tfrac{m}{2}\ln\tfrac{m}{2\pi}+m+O(\ln m)$, but
 it is irrelevant for the limit.)

**The limit.** Dividing by $m(m+1)=m^2+m$:
$$\frac{S_m}{m(m+1)}=\frac{m\ln m}{m^2+m}+\frac{m^2/2}{m^2+m}+O\!\left(\frac{\ln
 m}{m}\right)\;\longrightarrow\;0+\frac12=\frac12.$$

Thus
$$\lim_{m\to\infty}\ln L_m = 0+\frac12=\frac12,$$
and consequently
$$\boxed{\lim_{m\to\infty}\left(\sqrt[m]{m+1}\cdot\sqrt[m^2+m]{\binom{m}{1}\bino
m{m}{2}\cdots\binom{m}{m}}\right)=e^{1/2}=\sqrt{e}.}$$
