## Proof

We use the arctangent subtraction identity: for $ab > -1$,

$$\arctan a - \arctan b = \arctan\!\left(\frac{a-b}{1+ab}\right).$$

**Key identity.** For every integer $n \ge 1$, set $a = n+1$ and $b = n-1$. Sinc
e $ab = (n+1)(n-1) = n^2-1 \ge 0 > -1$, the identity applies and gives

$$\arctan(n+1) - \arctan(n-1) = \arctan\!\left(\frac{(n+1)-(n-1)}{1+(n+1)(n-1)}\
right) = \arctan\!\left(\frac{2}{1+n^2-1}\right) = \arctan\!\left(\frac{2}{n^2}\
right).$$

Thus
$$\arctan\!\left(\frac{2}{n^2}\right) = \arctan(n+1) - \arctan(n-1), \qquad n \g
e 1.$$

**Telescoping.** Let $S_N = \sum_{n=1}^{N}\arctan(2/n^2)$. Then

$$S_N = \sum_{n=1}^{N}\bigl[\arctan(n+1)-\arctan(n-1)\bigr] = \sum_{n=1}^{N}\arc
tan(n+1) - \sum_{n=1}^{N}\arctan(n-1).$$

The first sum runs over $\arctan(2),\arctan(3),\dots,\arctan(N+1)$ (indices $2,\
dots,N+1$), and the second over $\arctan(0),\arctan(1),\dots,\arctan(N-1)$ (indi
ces $0,\dots,N-1$). The common indices $2,\dots,N-1$ cancel, leaving

$$S_N = \arctan(N) + \arctan(N+1) - \arctan(0) - \arctan(1) = \arctan(N) + \arct
an(N+1) - \frac{\pi}{4}.$$

**Limit.** As $N \to \infty$, $\arctan(N) \to \tfrac{\pi}{2}$ and $\arctan(N+1)
\to \tfrac{\pi}{2}$. Therefore

$$S = \lim_{N\to\infty} S_N = \frac{\pi}{2} + \frac{\pi}{2} - \frac{\pi}{4} = \f
rac{3\pi}{4}.$$

$$\boxed{S = \dfrac{3\pi}{4}}$$
