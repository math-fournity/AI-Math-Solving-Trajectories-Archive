## Solution

We solve $xu_x + yu_y + zu_z = 1$ with $u=0$ on the plane $x+y+z=1$ by the metho
d of characteristics.

**Characteristic equations.** Setting $\frac{dx}{x}=\frac{dy}{y}=\frac{dz}{z}=\f
rac{du}{1}$, we parametrize by $s$:

$$\frac{dx}{ds}=x,\quad \frac{dy}{ds}=y,\quad \frac{dz}{ds}=z,\quad \frac{du}{ds
}=1.$$

Integrating:

$$x=x_0 e^s,\quad y=y_0 e^s,\quad z=z_0 e^s,\quad u=s+u_0.$$

**Invariants.** The ratios $y/x$ and $z/x$ are constant along each characteristi
c (since $x,y,z$ all scale by $e^s$), confirming the characteristics are rays th
rough the origin.

**Applying the initial condition.** The initial data is prescribed on $\Sigma:\,
x+y+z=1$. Choose the initial point $(x_0,y_0,z_0)\in\Sigma$, so $x_0+y_0+z_0=1$,
 with $u_0=u|_{s=0}=0$.

Along the characteristic through this point:

$$x+y+z=(x_0+y_0+z_0)e^s = 1\cdot e^s = e^s.$$

Hence $s=\ln(x+y+z)$, and since $u_0=0$,

$$u = s = \ln(x+y+z).$$

**Verification.** With $u=\ln(x+y+z)$:

$$u_x=u_y=u_z=\frac{1}{x+y+z},$$

so

$$xu_x+yu_y+zu_z=\frac{x+y+z}{x+y+z}=1.\checkmark$$

On $x+y+z=1$: $u=\ln 1=0.\checkmark$

**Answer:**

$$\boxed{u(x,y,z)=\ln(x+y+z)}$$

defined on the region where $x+y+z>0$ (so the characteristic through each point
meets the initial plane $x+y+z=1$).
