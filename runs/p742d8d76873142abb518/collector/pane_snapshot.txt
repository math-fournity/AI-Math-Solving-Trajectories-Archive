## Setup

Let $k$ be the total number of players. In a round-robin tournament, each pair p
lays once, so total games $= \binom{k}{2}$. Each game awards exactly 1 point tot
al (1 for a win, or 0.5+0.5 for a tie), so **total points $= \binom{k}{2} = \fra
c{k(k-1)}{2}$**.

**Known scores:**
- Garfunkle: 8 wins, 0 ties → 8 points.
- Simon: 8 points (including 1 for beating Garfunkle).
- Each of the other $k-2$ players: $n$ points.

## Deriving the Formula

Total points equation:
$$8 + 8 + (k-2)n = \frac{k(k-1)}{2}$$

Solving:
$$(k-2)n = \frac{k^2 - k - 32}{2} = \frac{(k-2)(k+1) - 30}{2}$$

$$n = \frac{k+1}{2} - \frac{15}{k-2}$$

Since every score is a multiple of $\frac{1}{2}$, we need $2n = (k+1) - \frac{30
}{k-2}$ to be an integer, so **$k-2 \mid 30$**.

## Bounding $k$

Garfunkle has 8 wins and at least 1 loss (to Simon), so $k - 1 \geq 9$, meaning
$k \geq 10$.

The divisors of 30 are $\{1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 10, 15, 30\}$. Only $k-2 \in \{10, 15,
30\}$ give $k \geq 10$:

| $k-2$ | $k$ | $n = \frac{k+1}{2} - \frac{15}{k-2}$ |
|-------|-----|------|
| 10 | 12 | $\frac{13}{2} - \frac{3}{2} = 5$ |
| 15 | 17 | $9 - 1 = 8$ |
| 30 | 32 | $\frac{33}{2} - \frac{1}{2} = 16$ |
