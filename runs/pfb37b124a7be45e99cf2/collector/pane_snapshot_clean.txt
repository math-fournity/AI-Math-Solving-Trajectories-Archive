## Proof

Let the rectangular parallelepiped have dimensions $x, y, z$ along edges $AB, AD
, AA'$ respectively, with $A$ at the origin. The three cutting planes (parallel
to the faces) divide each dimension into two sub-intervals:

- $x$-direction: $p$ and $q$ (with $p+q = x$),
- $y$-direction: $r$ and $s$ (with $r+s = y$),
- $z$-direction: $t$ and $u$ (with $t+u = z$).

Each of the 8 parts has volume equal to a product of one sub-interval from each
direction. Assigning the sub-intervals to vertices:

| Vertex | Volume |
|--------|--------|
| $A$ | $p\,r\,t = 40$ |
| $C$ | $q\,s\,t = 300$ |
| $B'$ | $q\,r\,u = 360$ |
| $C'$ | $q\,s\,u = 90$ |

**Step 1: Find $r/s$.**
From $B'$ and $C'$: $\dfrac{q\,r\,u}{q\,s\,u} = \dfrac{360}{90} = 4$, so $r = 4s
$.

**Step 2: Find $p/q$.**
From $A$ and $C$: $\dfrac{p\,r\,t}{q\,s\,t} = \dfrac{40}{300} = \dfrac{2}{15}$.
Substituting $r = 4s$:
$$\frac{p \cdot 4s}{q \cdot s} = \frac{4p}{q} = \frac{2}{15} \implies \frac{p}{q
} = \frac{1}{30}, \quad\text{so } q = 30p.$$

**Step 3: Find $t/u$.**
From $C$ and $C'$: $\dfrac{q\,s\,t}{q\,s\,u} = \dfrac{300}{90} = \dfrac{10}{3}$,
 so $t = \dfrac{10}{3}\,u$.

**Step 4: Find $p\,s\,u$.**
Using $V_A = p\,r\,t = 40$ with $r = 4s$ and $t = \tfrac{10}{3}u$:
$$p \cdot 4s \cdot \tfrac{10}{3}\,u = 40 \implies \tfrac{40}{3}\,p\,s\,u = 40 \i
mplies p\,s\,u = 3.$$

**Step 5: Compute the total volume.**
$$V = (p+q)(r+s)(t+u) = p(1+30)\cdot s(4+1)\cdot u\!\left(\tfrac{10}{3}+1\right)
 = 31p \cdot 5s \cdot \tfrac{13}{3}\,u.$$
$$V = 31 \cdot 5 \cdot \tfrac{13}{3} \cdot (p\,s\,u) = 31 \cdot 5 \cdot \tfrac{1
3}{3} \cdot 3 = 31 \cdot 5 \cdot 13 = \boxed{2015}.$$

**Verification.** With $p\,s\,u = 3$, $r=4s$, $q=30p$, $t=\tfrac{10}{3}u$:
- $V_A = p\cdot 4s\cdot\tfrac{10}{3}u = \tfrac{40}{3}\cdot 3 = 40$ ✓
- $V_C = 30p\cdot s\cdot\tfrac{10}{3}u = 100\cdot 3 = 300$ ✓
- $V_{B'} = 30p\cdot 4s\cdot u = 120\cdot 3 = 360$ ✓
- $V_{C'} = 30p\cdot s\cdot u = 30\cdot 3 = 90$ ✓
