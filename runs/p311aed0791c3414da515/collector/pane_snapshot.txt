\operatorname{rank}(\widehat{G}) = n$.

$$0 \to \operatorname{Ann}(H) \to \widehat{G} \xrightarrow{\operatorname{res}} \

$$\operatorname{rank}(\widehat{G}) = \operatorname{rank}(\operatorname{Ann}(H))

Therefore:
$$\dim H = \operatorname{rank}(\widehat{H}) = n - \operatorname{rank}(\operatorn
