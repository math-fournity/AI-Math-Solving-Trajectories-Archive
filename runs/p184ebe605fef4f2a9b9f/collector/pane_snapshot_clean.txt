## Solution

We count 2×2 matrices over $\mathbb{F}_p$ satisfying $a+d \equiv 1 \pmod{p}$ and
 $ad - bc \equiv 0 \pmod{p}$.

**Step 1: Eliminate $d$.** From $a + d \equiv 1$, we get $d \equiv 1 - a \pmod{p
}$. Substituting into the determinant condition:

$$a(1-a) - bc \equiv 0 \pmod{p} \implies bc \equiv a(1-a) \pmod{p}.$$

So for each $a \in \mathbb{F}_p$, we count pairs $(b, c) \in \mathbb{F}_p^2$ wit
h $bc \equiv t \pmod{p}$, where $t = a(1-a)$.

**Step 2: Count pairs $(b,c)$ with $bc \equiv t$.**

- **If $t = 0$:** $bc \equiv 0$ means $b = 0$ (any $c$: $p$ choices) or $b \neq
0$ with $c = 0$ ($p-1$ choices). Total: $p + (p-1) = 2p - 1$.
- **If $t \neq 0$:** $b$ must be nonzero (else $bc = 0 \neq t$). For each $b \ne
q 0$, $c$ is uniquely determined as $c \equiv t \cdot b^{-1} \pmod{p}$. Total: $
p - 1$.

**Step 3: Determine when $t = a(1-a) \equiv 0$.** Since $p$ is prime, $a(1-a) \e
quiv 0$ iff $a \equiv 0$ or $a \equiv 1 \pmod{p}$.

- $a = 0$: $t = 0$ → $2p - 1$ pairs.
- $a = 1$: $t = 1 \cdot 0 = 0$ → $2p - 1$ pairs.
- $a \in \{2, 3, \dots, p-1\}$ (i.e., $a \neq 0, 1$): $t \neq 0$ → $p-1$ pairs e
ach. There are $p - 2$ such values.

**Step 4: Sum.**

$$\text{Total} = (2p-1) + (2p-1) + (p-2)(p-1) = 2(2p-1) + (p-2)(p-1).$$

Expanding:

$$= (4p - 2) + (p^2 - 3p + 2) = p^2 + p = p(p+1).$$

**Verification:**
- $p = 2$: $a=0$ gives 3 pairs, $a=1$ gives 3 pairs → total $6 = 2 \cdot 3$. ✓
- $p = 3$: $a=0$ gives 5, $a=1$ gives 5, $a=2$ gives $t = 2(-1) \equiv 1 \neq 0$
 → 2 pairs → total $12 = 3 \cdot 4$. ✓

The number of distinct matrices is $\boxed{p(p+1)}$.
