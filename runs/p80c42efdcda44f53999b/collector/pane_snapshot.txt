## Solution

**Step 1: Compute moduli and arguments of each vertex.**

| Vertex | Modulus | Argument (in units of $\pi/4$) |
|---|---|---|
| $\sqrt{2}\,i$ | $\sqrt{2}$ | $+2$ |
| $-\sqrt{2}\,i$ | $\sqrt{2}$ | $-2 \equiv 6$ |
| $\frac{1}{\sqrt{8}}(1+i)$ | $\frac{1}{2}$ | $+1$ |
| $\frac{1}{\sqrt{8}}(-1+i)$ | $\frac{1}{2}$ | $+3$ |
| $\frac{1}{\sqrt{8}}(1-i)$ | $\frac{1}{2}$ | $-1 \equiv 7$ |
| $\frac{1}{\sqrt{8}}(-1-i)$ | $\frac{1}{2}$ | $-3 \equiv 5$ |

We call the first two **large** (modulus $\sqrt{2}$) and the last four **small**
 (modulus $\tfrac{1}{2}$).

**Step 2: Modulus condition.** Let $a$ = number of large vertices chosen, $b = 1
2-a$ = number of small. Then
$$|P| = (\sqrt{2})^a \cdot \left(\tfrac{1}{2}\right)^b = 2^{a/2 - b}.$$
For $P = -1$ we need $|P|=1$, so $a/2 = b$, giving $a = 2b$. With $a+b=12$: $\bo
xed{a=8,\; b=4}$.

**Step 3: Argument condition.** We need $\arg(P) \equiv \pi \pmod{2\pi}$, i.e.,
the total argument (in $\pi/4$ units) $\equiv 4 \pmod{8}$.

*Large contribution:* 8 vertices, each contributing $+2$ or $-2$ (mod 8: $2$ or
$6$). If $p$ of them contribute $+2$, the total is $2p + 6(8-p) = 48 - 4p \equiv
 -4p \pmod{8}$.
- $p$ even: large total $\equiv 0 \pmod{8}$, so we need small total $\equiv 4 \p
mod{8}$.
- $p$ odd: large total $\equiv 4 \pmod{8}$, so we need small total $\equiv 0 \pm
od{8}$.

*Small contribution:* 4 vertices, each chosen uniformly from $\{1, 3, 5, 7\} \pm
od{8}$ (all odd residues). The set $\{1,3,5,7\} = 1 + \{0,2,4,6\}$, where $\{0,2
,4,6\}$ is a subgroup of $\mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z}$. The sum of 4 elements, each u
niform over this coset, equals $4 + (\text{sum of 4 uniform elements of the subg
roup})$, which is **uniform over $\{0,2,4,6\}$**.

**Verification:** The generating function $(x+x^3+x^5+x^7)^4 \pmod{x^8-1}$ equal
s $64(1+x^2+x^4+x^6)$, confirming each even residue class has exactly $64$ out o
f $256$ arrangements.

Therefore, regardless of whether $p$ is even or odd, the probability (over small
 vertex choices) of satisfying the argument condition is $\frac{64}{256} = \frac
{1}{4}$.

**Step 4: Compute the total probability.**

$$P = \frac{\binom{12}{8} \cdot 2^8 \cdot 4^4 \cdot \tfrac{1}{4}}{6^{12}} = \fra
c{495 \cdot 256 \cdot 64}{6^{12}}.$$

Simplifying:
$$P = \frac{495 \cdot 2^{14}}{2^{12} \cdot 3^{12}} = \frac{495 \cdot 4}{3^{12}}
= \frac{4 \cdot 5 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 11}{3^{12}} = \frac{2^2 \cdot 5 \cdot 11}{3^{1
0}}.$$

This matches $\textbf{(E)}$.
