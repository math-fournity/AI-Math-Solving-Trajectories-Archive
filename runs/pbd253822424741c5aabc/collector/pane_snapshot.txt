## Answer: No.

A line bundle $L$ with $\dim \Gamma(X, L^{\otimes k}) \to \infty$ need not be am
ple. The growth of global sections to infinity is strictly weaker than ampleness
. Here is an explicit counterexample.

### Counterexample

Let $X = \operatorname{Bl}_p \mathbb{P}^2$ be the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^2$ at a
 point $p$, and let $H = \pi^*\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^2}(1)$ be the pullback of
the hyperplane class and $E$ the exceptional divisor. Set

$$L = \mathcal{O}_X(H - E).$$

**Claim 1:** $\dim \Gamma(X, L^{\otimes k}) \to \infty$.

The complete linear system $|k(H - E)|$ corresponds to degree-$k$ plane curves p
assing through $p$ with multiplicity $\geq k$. A degree-$k$ homogeneous polynomi
al vanishing to order $\geq k$ at $p$ is a product of $k$ linear forms, each van
ishing at $p$ — i.e., a union of $k$ lines through $p$. These are parametrized b
y the $k$-th symmetric power of the pencil of lines through $p$:

$$|k(H-E)| \cong \operatorname{Sym}^k \mathbb{P}^1 \cong \mathbb{P}^k.$$

Hence $\dim |k(H-E)| = k$, so

$$h^0(X, L^{\otimes k}) = k + 1 \xrightarrow{k \to \infty} \infty.$$

**Claim 2:** $L$ is **not** ample.

Let $C \subset X$ be the proper transform of a line in $\mathbb{P}^2$ passing th
rough $p$. As a curve class, $C = H - E$. Computing the intersection number:

$$L \cdot C = (H - E)\cdot(H - E) = H^2 - 2\,H\cdot E + E^2 = 1 - 0 + (-1) = 0.$
$

Since $C$ is a complete curve and $L \cdot C = 0$, the line bundle $L$ is **not*
* positive on every curve. By the Nakai–Moishezon (or Kleiman) criterion, $L$ fa
ils to be ample. (In fact $L$ is nef but lies on the boundary of the ample cone:
 a divisor $aH - bE$ on $X$ is ample iff $a > b > 0$, and here $a = b = 1$.)

### Conclusion

We have exhibited a projective variety $X$ and a line bundle $L$ on it such that
 $h^0(X, L^{\otimes k}) = k+1 \to \infty$, yet $L$ is not ample. Therefore the i
mplication

$$\dim \Gamma(X, L^{\otimes k}) \to \infty \;\Longrightarrow\; L \text{ ample}$$

is **false**. The correct characterization (for projective $X$) is that $L$ is a
mple if and only if $h^0(X, L^{\otimes k})$ grows like a degree-$\dim X$ polynom
ial with positive leading coefficient — mere divergence to infinity is insuffici
ent.
