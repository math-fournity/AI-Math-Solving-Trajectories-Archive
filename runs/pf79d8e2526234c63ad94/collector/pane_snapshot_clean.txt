## Solution

**Setup.** We place 3 indistinguishable white (Daylight) beacons and 3 indisting
uishable black (Midnight) beacons on an 8×8 grid. The constraint: no white beaco
n shares a row or column with any black beacon. Equivalently, the set of rows oc
cupied by white beacons and the set of rows occupied by black beacons must be **
disjoint**, and likewise for columns.

**Strategy.** Place the 3 white beacons first, then count valid placements of th
e 3 black beacons. If the white beacons occupy exactly $r$ distinct rows and $c$
 distinct columns, the black beacons must live entirely in the complementary $(8
-r)\times(8-c)$ subgrid, giving $\binom{(8-r)(8-c)}{3}$ choices. So

$$\text{Total} = \sum_{r,c} N(r,c)\cdot\binom{(8-r)(8-c)}{3},$$

where $N(r,c)$ = number of ways to place 3 white beacons using **exactly** $r$ r
ows and $c$ columns.

**Computing $N(r,c)$.** We have $N(r,c)=\binom{8}{r}\binom{8}{c}\,M(r,c,3)$, whe
re $M(r,c,3)$ counts 3-cell subsets of an $r\times c$ grid that are **surjective
** in both rows and columns (every chosen row and column is hit). The feasible p
airs $(r,c)$ with $1\le r,c\le 3$ and $rc\ge 3$ are:

| $(r,c)$ | $M(r,c,3)$ | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| $(1,3)$ | $1$ | One row, 3 cols, 3 cells: must fill all 3 |
| $(3,1)$ | $1$ | Symmetric |
| $(2,2)$ | $4$ | $\binom{4}{3}=4$; removing any 1 cell from a full $2\times2$ s
till covers both rows/cols |
| $(2,3)$ | $6$ | 3 cols each get 1 cell; assign each to one of 2 rows: $2^3-2=6
$ (subtract all-same-row) |
| $(3,2)$ | $6$ | Symmetric |
| $(3,3)$ | $6$ | 3 cells, 3 rows, 3 cols → permutation matrix: $3!=6$ |

Then:

| $(r,c)$ | $\binom{8}{r}\binom{8}{c}$ | $M$ | $N(r,c)$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $(1,3)$ | $8\cdot56=448$ | $1$ | $448$ |
| $(3,1)$ | $56\cdot8=448$ | $1$ | $448$ |
| $(2,2)$ | $28\cdot28=784$ | $4$ | $3136$ |
| $(2,3)$ | $28\cdot56=1568$ | $6$ | $9408$ |
| $(3,2)$ | $56\cdot28=1568$ | $6$ | $9408$ |
| $(3,3)$ | $56\cdot56=3136$ | $6$ | $18816$ |

**Check:** $\sum N(r,c) = 448+448+3136+9408+9408+18816 = 41664 = \binom{64}{3}$.
 ✓

**Assembling the total.** For each $(r,c)$, the black beacons choose 3 cells fro
m the $(8-r)\times(8-c)$ subgrid:

| $(r,c)$ | $(8{-}r)(8{-}c)$ | $\binom{\cdot}{3}$ | $N(r,c)$ | Term |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $(1,3)$ | $35$ | $6545$ | $448$ | $2{,}932{,}160$ |
| $(3,1)$ | $35$ | $6545$ | $448$ | $2{,}932{,}160$ |
| $(2,2)$ | $36$ | $7140$ | $3136$ | $22{,}391{,}040$ |
| $(2,3)$ | $30$ | $4060$ | $9408$ | $38{,}196{,}480$ |
| $(3,2)$ | $30$ | $4060$ | $9408$ | $38{,}196{,}480$ |
| $(3,3)$ | $25$ | $2300$ | $18816$ | $43{,}276{,}800$ |

**Summing:**

$$2{,}932{,}160 + 2{,}932{,}160 + 22{,}391{,}040 + 38{,}196{,}480 + 38{,}196{,}4
80 + 43{,}276{,}800$$

$$= 5{,}864{,}320 + 22{,}391{,}040 + 76{,}392{,}960 + 43{,}276{,}800 = 147{,}925
{,}120.$$

**Verification of key binomial values:**
- $\binom{35}{3}=\frac{35\cdot34\cdot33}{6}=6545$ ✓
- $\binom{36}{3}=\frac{36\cdot35\cdot34}{6}=7140$ ✓
- $\binom{30}{3}=\frac{30\cdot29\cdot28}{6}=4060$ ✓
- $\binom{25}{3}=\frac{25\cdot24\cdot23}{6}=2300$ ✓

**Answer.** The number of distinct valid configurations is

$$\boxed{147{,}925{,}120}$$
