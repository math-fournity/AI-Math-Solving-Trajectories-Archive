## Proof

**Claim.** Yes. Every separable Banach space $X$ admits an isometric (linear) em
bedding into $\ell^{\infty}$.

**Construction.** Since $X$ is separable, there exists a sequence $\{x_n\}_{n=1}
^{\infty}$ dense in $X$. For each $n \ge 1$, by the Hahn–Banach theorem there is
 a norming functional $f_n \in X^{*}$ with

$$
\|f_n\| = 1, \qquad f_n(x_n) = \|x_n\|.
$$

(Existence: define a functional on $\mathrm{span}\{x_n\}$ by $g_n(\lambda x_n) =
 \lambda\|x_n\|$; then $\|g_n\| = 1$ on that span, and Hahn–Banach extends it to
 $f_n \in X^*$ with $\|f_n\| = 1$.)

Define

$$
T : X \to \ell^{\infty}, \qquad T(x) = \bigl(f_n(x)\bigr)_{n=1}^{\infty}.
$$

**$T$ is well-defined and lands in $\ell^{\infty}$.** For every $n$,
$$
|f_n(x)| \le \|f_n\|\,\|x\| = \|x\|,
$$
so $\sup_n |f_n(x)| \le \|x\| < \infty$, hence $T(x) \in \ell^{\infty}$.

**$T$ is linear.** Each $f_n$ is linear, so $T(\alpha x + \beta y) = (\alpha f_n
(x) + \beta f_n(y))_n = \alpha T(x) + \beta T(y)$.

**$T$ is an isometry.** We show $\|T(x)\|_{\infty} = \|x\|$ for every $x \in X$.

*Upper bound.* From the inequality above, $\|T(x)\|_{\infty} = \sup_n |f_n(x)| \
le \|x\|$.

*Lower bound.* Fix $x \in X$. Since $\{x_n\}$ is dense in $X$, there is a subseq
uence $\{x_{n_k}\}$ with $x_{n_k} \to x$. Using $f_{n_k}(x_{n_k}) = \|x_{n_k}\|$
 and $\|f_{n_k}\| = 1$,

$$
\bigl|\, f_{n_k}(x) - \|x_{n_k}\| \,\bigr|
= \bigl|\, f_{n_k}(x) - f_{n_k}(x_{n_k}) \,\bigr|
\le \|f_{n_k}\|\,\|x - x_{n_k}\|
= \|x - x_{n_k}\| \xrightarrow{k \to \infty} 0.
$$

Also $\|x_{n_k}\| \to \|x\|$. Therefore

$$
f_{n_k}(x) \to \|x\|.
$$

Consequently

$$
\|T(x)\|_{\infty} = \sup_n |f_n(x)| \ge \lim_{k \to \infty} |f_{n_k}(x)| = \|x\|
.
$$

Combining both bounds: $\|T(x)\|_{\infty} = \|x\|$ for all $x \in X$.

**$T$ is injective.** If $T(x) = 0$, then $\|x\| = \|T(x)\|_{\infty} = 0$, so $x
 = 0$.

**Conclusion.** $T : X \to \ell^{\infty}$ is a linear isometry, hence an isometr
ic embedding of $X$ into $\ell^{\infty}$. Therefore such an embedding always exi
sts for every separable Banach space.
