## Proof

**Claim:** Yes, the dominated convergence theorem can be applied.

**Setup.** Let $X$ be a random variable with $\mathbb{E}[|X|] < +\infty$. We wis
h to show
$$\lim_{a \to +\infty} \mathbb{E}\bigl(|X|\,\mathbf{1}_{\{|X|\geq a\}}\bigr)=0.$
$

**Reduction to a sequential limit.** It suffices to show that for every sequence
 $(a_n)_{n\geq 1}$ with $a_n \to +\infty$,
$$\mathbb{E}\bigl(|X|\,\mathbf{1}_{\{|X|\geq a_n\}}\bigr)\to 0,$$
since the existence of the limit $a\to+\infty$ together with its value is equiva
lent to convergence along every such sequence (the map $a\mapsto \mathbb{E}(|X|\
mathbf{1}_{|X|\geq a})$ is monotone non-increasing, so the sequential characteri
zation gives the full limit).

**Applying the Dominated Convergence Theorem.** Fix any sequence $a_n\to+\infty$
 and define
$$Y_n := |X|\,\mathbf{1}_{\{|X|\geq a_n\}}.$$

We verify the three hypotheses of the dominated convergence theorem:

1. **Integrability of the dominator.** For every $n$,
$$|Y_n| = |X|\,\mathbf{1}_{\{|X|\geq a_n\}} \leq |X|,$$
and $|X|$ is integrable by assumption, i.e. $|X|\in L^1(\mathbb{P})$.

2. **Pointwise convergence.** For every $\omega$ with $|X(\omega)|<\infty$ (whic
h holds $\mathbb{P}$-a.s. since $\mathbb{E}[|X|]<\infty$ implies $|X|<\infty$ a.
s.), there exists $N(\omega)$ such that $a_n > |X(\omega)|$ for all $n\geq N(\om
ega)$. Hence $\mathbf{1}_{\{|X(\omega)|\geq a_n\}}=0$ for $n\geq N(\omega)$, and
 so
$$Y_n(\omega)\to 0 \quad \mathbb{P}\text{-a.s.}$$

3. **Domination.** As shown in step 1, $|Y_n|\leq |X|\in L^1$ for all $n$, provi
ding a single integrable dominating function independent of $n$.

All hypotheses of the dominated convergence theorem are satisfied. Therefore
$$\lim_{n\to\infty}\mathbb{E}[Y_n]=\mathbb{E}\!\left[\lim_{n\to\infty}Y_n\right]
=\mathbb{E}[0]=0.$$

**Conclusion.** Since this holds for every sequence $a_n\to+\infty$, and the fun
ction $a\mapsto\mathbb{E}(|X|\mathbf{1}_{|X|\geq a})$ is monotone non-increasing
 in $a$ (so its limit as $a\to+\infty$ exists in $[0,\mathbb{E}|X|]$), we conclu
de
$$\lim_{a\to+\infty}\mathbb{E}\bigl(|X|\,\mathbf{1}_{\{|X|\geq a\}}\bigr)=0.$$

Thus the dominated convergence theorem **can** be applied, with the integrable r
andom variable $|X|$ serving as the dominating function and the almost-sure conv
ergence $|X|\mathbf{1}_{|X|\geq a_n}\to 0$ providing the pointwise limit.
