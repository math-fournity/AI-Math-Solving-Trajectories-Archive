We seek the smallest positive integer $n$ such that $10^{2007} \mid F_n$, i.e.,
$v_2(F_n) \ge 2007$ and $v_5(F_n) \ge 2007$, where $v_p$ denotes the $p$-adic va
luation.

### Key tool: Lengyel's valuation formulas

We use the following classical results (Lengyel, 1995) for the Fibonacci sequenc
e:

**2-adic valuation:**
$$v_2(F_n) = \begin{cases} 0 & \text{if } n \not\equiv 0 \pmod{3},\\ 1 & \text{i
f } n \equiv 3 \pmod{6},\\ v_2(n) + 2 & \text{if } n \equiv 0 \pmod{6}. \end{cas
es}$$

**5-adic valuation:**
$$v_5(F_n) = \begin{cases} v_5(n) & \text{if } 5 \mid n,\\ 0 & \text{if } 5 \nmi
d n. \end{cases}$$

*Verification with small cases:* $F_3=2\Rightarrow v_2=1$ ✓; $F_6=8\Rightarrow v
_2=3=v_2(6)+2$ ✓; $F_{12}=144\Rightarrow v_2=4=v_2(12)+2$ ✓; $F_5=5\Rightarrow v
_5=1=v_5(5)$ ✓; $F_{25}=75025=25\cdot3001\Rightarrow v_5=2=v_5(25)$ ✓.

### Determining the 2-adic condition

To get $v_2(F_n)\ge 2007$:
- The case $n\not\equiv 0\pmod 3$ gives $v_2=0$: impossible.
- The case $n\equiv 3\pmod 6$ gives $v_2=1<2007$: impossible.
- So we need $n\equiv 0\pmod 6$ and $v_2(n)+2\ge 2007$, i.e., $v_2(n)\ge 2005$.

Thus $2^{2005}\mid n$ and $3\mid n$ (from $6\mid n$), giving $3\cdot 2^{2005}\mi
d n$.

### Determining the 5-adic condition

To get $v_5(F_n)\ge 2007$: we need $5\mid n$ and $v_5(n)\ge 2007$, i.e., $5^{200
7}\mid n$.

### Combining

We need $n$ divisible by both $3\cdot 2^{2005}$ and $5^{2007}$. Since
$$\gcd\!\big(3\cdot 2^{2005},\; 5^{2007}\big)=1,$$
the smallest such $n$ is
$$n = \operatorname{lcm}\!\big(3\cdot 2^{2005},\; 5^{2007}\big) = 3\cdot 2^{2005
}\cdot 5^{2007}.$$

### Verification at $n = 3\cdot 2^{2005}\cdot 5^{2007}$

- $n\equiv 0\pmod 6$ (since $3\cdot 2\mid n$) and $v_2(n)=2005$, so $v_2(F_n)=20
05+2=2007$. ✓
- $5\mid n$ and $v_5(n)=2007$, so $v_5(F_n)=2007$. ✓

Hence $10^{2007}\mid F_n$, and by the divisibility analysis above no smaller $n$
 can satisfy both conditions simultaneously.

### Conclusion

The smallest positive integer $n$ such that $F_n$ ends with 2007 zeroes is

$$\boxed{n = 3\cdot 2^{2005}\cdot 5^{2007}}.$$
