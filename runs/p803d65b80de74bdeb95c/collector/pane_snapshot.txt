## Proof

Suppose, for contradiction, that $(x_1, x_2, x_3) \neq (0,0,0)$ is a solution to
 the system. Let

$$M = \max(|x_1|, |x_2|, |x_3|) > 0,$$

and let $k \in \{1,2,3\}$ be an index with $|x_k| = M$.

From the $k$-th equation we isolate the diagonal term:

$$a_{kk}\, x_k = -\sum_{j \neq k} a_{kj}\, x_j.$$

Taking absolute values and using the triangle inequality:

$$a_{kk}\,|x_k| = \left|\sum_{j \neq k} a_{kj}\, x_j\right| \leq \sum_{j \neq k}
 |a_{kj}|\,|x_j|.$$

Since $|x_j| \leq M$ for every $j$, and $|x_k| = M$:

$$a_{kk}\, M \leq \sum_{j \neq k} |a_{kj}|\, M.$$

As $M > 0$, we may divide by $M$:

$$a_{kk} \leq \sum_{j \neq k} |a_{kj}|.$$

By condition (B), every off-diagonal entry $a_{kj}$ ($j \neq k$) is negative, so
 $|a_{kj}| = -a_{kj}$. Therefore

$$a_{kk} \leq -\sum_{j \neq k} a_{kj},$$

which rearranges to

$$a_{k1} + a_{k2} + a_{k3} = a_{kk} + \sum_{j \neq k} a_{kj} \leq 0.$$

But condition (C) states that the sum of the coefficients in each row is **posit
ive**:

$$a_{k1} + a_{k2} + a_{k3} > 0.$$

This is a contradiction.

Hence our assumption that a non-trivial solution exists is false. The only solut
ion is

$$x_1 = x_2 = x_3 = 0. \qquad \blacksquare$$
