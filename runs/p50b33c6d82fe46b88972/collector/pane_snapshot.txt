**Apply the Divergence Theorem.** The flux through $\partial\Omega$ equals

$$\iint_{\partial\Omega} F\cdot n\,dS = \iiint_{\Omega} \tfrac14 z\,dV = \tfrac1
4 \iiint_{\Omega} z\,dV.$$
