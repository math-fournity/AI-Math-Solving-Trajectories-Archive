### Key Fact (Lucas' Theorem)

By Lucas' theorem, the number of odd entries in Row $n$ of Pascal's triangle equ
als $2^{s_2(n)}$, where $s_2(n)$ is the number of 1s in the binary representatio
n of $n$.

We need $2^{s_2(n)} = 256 = 2^8$, so we must count integers $n$ with $0 \le n \l
e 2009$ satisfying $s_2(n) = 8$.

### Binary representation of 2009

Computing: $2009 = 1024+512+256+128+64+16+8+1 = 2^{10}+2^9+2^8+2^7+2^6+2^4+2^3+2
^0$.

So $2009 = \mathtt{11111011001}_2$ (11 bits, positions 10 down to 0). Note $2009
$ itself has 8 ones.

### Counting via the digit-by-digit method

We process the bits of 2009 from MSB (bit 10) to LSB (bit 0). At each position w
here 2009 has a 1, we branch:
- **Branch A** (place 0): the number becomes strictly less than 2009; remaining
lower bits are free. We count ways to place the remaining needed 1s among the fr
ee positions.
- **Branch B** (place 1): match 2009 and continue.

Where 2009 has a 0, we must place 0 (only Branch B).

| Bit pos | 2009's bit | Branch A count | ones_so_far (Branch B) |
|---------|-----------|----------------|------------------------|
| 10 | 1 | $\binom{10}{8}=45$ | 1 |
| 9 | 1 | $\binom{9}{7}=36$ | 2 |
| 8 | 1 | $\binom{8}{6}=28$ | 3 |
| 7 | 1 | $\binom{7}{5}=21$ | 4 |
| 6 | 1 | $\binom{6}{4}=15$ | 5 |
| 5 | 0 | — (forced 0) | 5 |
| 4 | 1 | $\binom{4}{3}=4$ | 6 |
| 3 | 1 | $\binom{3}{2}=3$ | 7 |
| 2 | 0 | — (forced 0) | 7 |
| 1 | 0 | — (forced 0) | 7 |
| 0 | 1 | $\binom{0}{1}=0$ | 8 |

After fully matching 2009 (all Branch B), ones_so_far = 8, so 2009 itself qualif
ies: **+1**.

### Total

$$45 + 36 + 28 + 21 + 15 + 4 + 3 + 0 + 1 = \boxed{153}$$

### Verification (alternative method)

All 11-bit numbers (0 to $2^{11}-1 = 2047$) with exactly 8 ones: $\binom{11}{8}
= 165$.

Numbers from 2010 to 2047 all share the top 5 bits $11111$ (contributing 5 ones)
, so the bottom 6 bits must contribute exactly 3 ones. The bottom 6 bits range f
rom $011010_2 = 26$ to $111111_2 = 63$.

- 6-bit numbers (0–63) with 3 ones: $\binom{6}{3} = 20$.
- 6-bit numbers (0–25) with 3 ones: processing $25 = 011001_2$ gives $4 + 3 + 0
+ 1 = 8$.
- So numbers 26–63 with 3 ones: $20 - 8 = 12$.

Therefore numbers 0–2009 with 8 ones: $165 - 12 = 153$. ✓
