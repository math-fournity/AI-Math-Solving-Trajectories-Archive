# Verification of the Six Cross-Ratio Identity

**Definition.** For complex numbers $a, b, c, d$, the cross-ratio is
$$(a, b; c, d) = \frac{(a-c)(b-d)}{(a-d)(b-c)}.$$

We verify
$$(u, w; v, z)\cdot(u', w'; v', z') = \frac{(v, w'; v', w)\cdot(z, u'; z', u)}{(
u, v'; u', v)\cdot(w, z'; w', z)}.$$

## Expanding the Left-Hand Side

$$
(u, w; v, z) = \frac{(u-v)(w-z)}{(u-z)(w-v)}, \qquad (u', w'; v', z') = \frac{(u
'-v')(w'-z')}{(u'-z')(w'-v')}.
$$

Hence
$$
\mathrm{LHS} = \frac{(u-v)(w-z)(u'-v')(w'-z')}{(u-z)(w-v)(u'-z')(w'-v')}. \tag{1
}
$$

## Expanding the Right-Hand Side

Each factor:
$$
(v, w'; v', w) = \frac{(v-v')(w'-w)}{(v-w)(w'-v')}, \quad (z, u'; z', u) = \frac
{(z-z')(u'-u)}{(z-u)(u'-z')},
$$
$$
(u, v'; u', v) = \frac{(u-u')(v'-v)}{(u-v)(v'-u')}, \quad (w, z'; w', z) = \frac
{(w-w')(z'-z)}{(w-z)(z'-w')}.
$$

Therefore
$$
\mathrm{RHS} = \frac{(v-v')(w'-w)(z-z')(u'-u)}{(v-w)(w'-v')(z-u)(u'-z')} \cdot \
frac{(u-v)(v'-u')(w-z)(z'-w')}{(u-u')(v'-v)(w-w')(z'-z)}. \tag{2}
$$

## Sign Reduction

Apply the sign relations $w'-w = -(w-w')$, $u'-u = -(u-u')$, $z-z' = -(z'-z)$, $
v-v' = -(v'-v)$, $v-w = -(w-v)$, $z-u = -(u-z)$, $v'-u' = -(u'-v')$, $z'-w' = -(
w'-z')$.

**Numerator of (2):** the eight sign flips give $(-1)^8 = +1$, so
$$
(v'-v)(w-w')(z'-z)(u-u')(u-v)(u'-v')(w-z)(w'-z').
$$

**Denominator of (2):** only $v-w = -(w-v)$ and $z-u = -(u-z)$ contribute sign f
lips, giving $(-1)^2 = +1$, so
$$
(w-v)(w'-v')(u-z)(u'-z')(u-u')(v'-v)(w-w')(z'-z).
$$

Thus
$$
\mathrm{RHS} = \frac{(v'-v)(w-w')(z'-z)(u-u')(u-v)(u'-v')(w-z)(w'-z')}{(w-v)(w'-
v')(u-z)(u'-z')(u-u')(v'-v)(w-w')(z'-z)}.
$$

## Cancellation

The four factors $(v'-v)$, $(w-w')$, $(z'-z)$, $(u-u')$ appear in both numerator
 and denominator and cancel, leaving
$$
\mathrm{RHS} = \frac{(u-v)(u'-v')(w-z)(w'-z')}{(w-v)(w'-v')(u-z)(u'-z')}.
$$

Reordering the commutative factors in numerator and denominator:
$$
\mathrm{RHS} = \frac{(u-v)(w-z)(u'-v')(w'-z')}{(u-z)(w-v)(u'-z')(w'-v')}.
$$

This is precisely equation (1), i.e. $\mathrm{LHS}$.

## Conclusion

$$
(u, w; v, z)\cdot(u', w'; v', z') = \frac{(v, w'; v', w)\cdot(z, u'; z', u)}{(u,
 v'; u', v)\cdot(w, z'; w', z)}. \qquad \blacksquare
$$
