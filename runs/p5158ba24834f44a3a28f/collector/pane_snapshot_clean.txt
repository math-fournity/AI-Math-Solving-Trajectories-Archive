- $PU\parallel CA \Rightarrow p+u=1$,

So $s=1-t$, $u=1-p$, $r=1-q$, leaving three free parameters $p,q,t$.

$$T=((1-t)\alpha,(1-t)\beta),\quad R=(q+(1-q)\alpha,(1-q)\beta),\quad S=(t+(1-t)

**Concyclicity conditions.** A circle through $P$ and $Q$ (both on the $x$-axis)

$$x^2+y^2-(p+q)x+Ey+pq=0.$$
