**Key factorization.** Since $\alpha = xy+yz+zx$, we compute:
$$1+\frac{\alpha}{x^2} = \frac{x^2+xy+yz+zx}{x^2} = \frac{(x+y)(x+z)}{x^2}.$$
By symmetry,
$$1+\frac{\alpha}{y^2}=\frac{(x+y)(y+z)}{y^2},\qquad 1+\frac{\alpha}{z^2}=\frac{
(x+z)(y+z)}{z^2}.$$
Therefore
$$\text{LHS}=\frac{(x+y)^2(x+z)^2(y+z)^2}{x^2y^2z^2}=\left(\frac{(x+y)(y+z)(z+x)
}{xyz}\right)^{\!2}.$$

Also $\dfrac{x}{z}+\dfrac{z}{x}+2=\dfrac{(x+z)^2}{xz}$, so the inequality become
s
$$\left(\frac{(x+y)(y+z)(z+x)}{xyz}\right)^{\!2}\ge C\cdot\frac{(x+z)^2}{xz}.\ta
g{$*$}$$

**Reduction to two variables.** Set $u=x/z>0$, $v=y/z>0$. The constraint $xy+yz+
zx=\alpha$ determines $z$ via $z^2(uv+u+v)=\alpha$, but both sides of $(*)$ are
scale-invariant (degree 0 in $z$), so it suffices to find
$$C=\inf_{u,v>0}\frac{(1+v)^2(u+v)^2}{uv^2}.$$
Indeed, substituting $x=uz,\,y=vz$:
- $\dfrac{(x+y)(y+z)(z+x)}{xyz}=\dfrac{(u+v)(1+v)(1+u)}{uv}$,
- $\dfrac{(x+z)^2}{xz}=\dfrac{(1+u)^2}{u}$,

and $(*)$ reduces (after dividing by $(1+u)^2/u>0$) to
$$\frac{(1+v)^2(u+v)^2}{uv^2}\ge C.$$

**Minimization.** For fixed $v>0$, minimize over $u$:
$$\frac{(u+v)^2}{u}=u+2v+\frac{v^2}{u}\ge 4v,$$
with equality at $u=v$ (AM–GM on $u$ and $v^2/u$, giving $u=v$). Hence
$$\frac{(1+v)^2(u+v)^2}{uv^2}\ge \frac{(1+v)^2\cdot 4v}{v^2}=\frac{4(1+v)^2}{v}.
$$
Now minimize $h(v)=\dfrac{4(1+v)^2}{v}=4\!\left(v+2+\dfrac{1}{v}\right)$ over $v
>0$. By AM–GM, $v+\dfrac{1}{v}\ge 2$, so
$$h(v)\ge 4(2+2)=16,$$
with equality at $v=1$.

**Conclusion.** The infimum is $16$, attained at $u=v=1$, i.e. $x=y=z$. The cons
traint then gives $3x^2=\alpha$, so $x=y=z=\sqrt{\alpha/3}$.

**Verification of equality.** At $x=y=z=\sqrt{\alpha/3}$: $\alpha/x^2=3$, so
$$\text{LHS}=(1+3)^3=64,\qquad \text{RHS}=16\cdot(1+1+2)=64.\;\checkmark$$

$$\boxed{C=16},\qquad \text{equality iff }x=y=z=\sqrt{\alpha/3}.$$
