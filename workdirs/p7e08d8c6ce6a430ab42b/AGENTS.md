# Problem

In a remote industrial facility, a central storage unit initially holds a stockpile of exactly 2014 fuel canisters. All other automated technicians in the facility currently have 0 canisters. The facility operates under a strict resource-sharing protocol: whenever two technicians meet, they must pool their canisters and redistribute them. 

If the combined total of canisters between two meeting technicians is an even number, they divide the total perfectly in half so each leaves with an equal amount. However, if the combined total is an odd number, the system's safety valve automatically ejects 1 canister into a waste disposal bin, and the technicians then divide the remaining even total equally between themselves.

Over the course of the day, various technicians met in pairs multiple times until every single one of the 2014 canisters had eventually been ejected into the waste disposal bin. Considering the central storage unit is counted as one of the technicians (the one who started with all the fuel), what is the minimum number of technicians that must be employed at the facility for this outcome to be possible?
