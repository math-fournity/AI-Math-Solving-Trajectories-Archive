Looking at this problem, I need to find the minimum integer diameter $d$ of a se
micircle with points $C, D$ on the arc such that $BC = CD = a$, $DA = b$, with $
a, b, d$ distinct positive integers.

## Setup

Place the semicircle with center $O$ at the origin, radius $R = d/2$, with $A =
(-R, 0)$ at angle $\pi$ and $B = (R, 0)$ at angle $0$. Points $C, D$ lie on the
upper arc in order $B, C, D, A$.

Let $C$ be at angle $2u$, $D$ at angle $2u + 2v$, and $A$ at angle $\pi$, so the
 remaining arc to $A$ gives half-angle $w$ where:
$$u + v + w = \frac{\pi}{2}$$

The chord length formula $2R\sin(\theta/2)$ gives:
- $BC = d\sin u = a$
- $CD = d\sin v = a$
- $DA = d\sin w = b$

## Deriving the Diophantine Equation

Since $a = d\sin u = d\sin v$ and $u, v \in (0, \pi/2)$, we get $u = v$. Then $w
 = \pi/2 - 2u$, so:
$$b = d\sin w = d\cos(2u)$$

Using $\cos(2u) = 1 - 2\sin^2 u$ with $\sin u = a/d$:
$$\frac{b}{d} = 1 - 2\left(\frac{a}{d}\right)^2 \implies d^2 - bd - 2a^2 = 0$$

Setting $c = d - b$ (a positive integer since $d > b > 0$), this becomes:
$$dc = 2a^2$$

**Geometric constraints:** $u \in (0, \pi/4)$ requires $a < d/\sqrt{2}$, and $b
= d - c \in (0, d)$ requires $c \in \{1, \ldots, d-1\}$.

## Searching for Minimum $d$

I need $dc = 2a^2$ with $a, b=d-c, d$ all distinct positive integers.

| $d$ | $c$ values giving integer $a$ | Result |
|-----|-------------------------------|--------|
| 2 | $c=1 \Rightarrow a=1, b=1$ | $a=b$, fail |
| 3 | none | — |
| 4 | $c=2 \Rightarrow a=2, b=2$ | $a=b$, fail |
| 5 | none | — |
| 6 | $c=3 \Rightarrow a=3, b=3$ | $a=b$, fail |
| 7 | none | — |
| **8** | $c=1 \Rightarrow a=2, b=7$ | **all distinct ✓** |

**Verification for $d=8, a=2, b=7$:**
- $dc = 8 \cdot 1 = 8 = 2(2^2) = 2a^2$ ✓
- $a=2, b=7, d=8$ are all distinct ✓
- $a < d/\sqrt{2}$: $2 < 8/\sqrt{2} \approx 5.66$ ✓
- $0 < b < d$: $0 < 7 < 8$ ✓
- $\sin u = 1/4$, $\cos(2u) = 1 - 2/16 = 7/8 = b/d$ ✓
- $u = \arcsin(1/4) \approx 0.253$ rad $\in (0, \pi/4)$, so $4u \approx 1.01 < \
pi$ ✓

All points lie on the semicircle with valid angles.

The minimum possible length of the docking pier is $\boxed{d = 8}$, achieved wit
h $a = 2$ and $b = 7$.
