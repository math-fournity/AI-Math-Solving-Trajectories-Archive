# Problem

In the city of Gridhaven, a telecommunications company is designing a network across a high-tech industrial park. The park is divided into a strictly regulated $10 \times 10$ grid of plots. The company plans to install satellite hubs, with each hub located exactly at the center of a grid plot. Due to zoning regulations, at most one hub can be installed in any single plot.

Engineers have discovered a critical interference risk: if any four hubs are positioned such that they form the vertices of a rectangle with sides parallel to the grid lines, the resulting signal feedback will crash the entire system.

What is the maximum number of satellite hubs that can be installed in the industrial park such that no four hubs form a rectangle with sides parallel to the edges of the $10 \times 10$ grid?
