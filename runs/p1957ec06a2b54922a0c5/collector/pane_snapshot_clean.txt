**Answer: True**

 The fixed field is $K = L^{G} = \mathbb{Q}(\zeta)^{\tau}$, which has degree $(p

**Step 1: $\mathcal{O}_L^{G} = \mathcal{O}_L \cap L^{G}$.**

If $\alpha \in \mathcal{O}_L^{G}$, then $\alpha$ is fixed by every element of $G

$$\mathcal{O}_L^{G} = \mathcal{O}_L \cap L^{G} = \mathcal{O}_L \cap K.$$

**Step 2: $\mathcal{O}_L \cap K = \mathcal{O}_K$.**

($\subseteq$) If $\alpha \in \mathcal{O}_L \cap K$, then $\alpha \in K$ and $\al

$$\mathbb{Z}[\zeta]^{\tau} = \mathcal{O}_L^{G} = \mathcal{O}_L \cap K = \mathcal
{O}_K.$$

Therefore $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta]^{\tau}$ is exactly the ring of integers of $\mathbb
{Q}(\zeta)^{\tau}$.
