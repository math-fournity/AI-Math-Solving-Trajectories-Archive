## Solution

**Problem:** Find $N \bmod 1000$, where $N$ is the number of trailing zeros in
$P = 1!\times 2!\times 3!\times\cdots\times 100!$.

### Step 1: Trailing zeros come from factors of 5

Each trailing zero requires one factor of $2$ and one factor of $5$. In factoria
ls, factors of $2$ always outnumber factors of $5$, so the number of trailing ze
ros is governed entirely by the total number of factors of $5$:

$$N = \sum_{k=1}^{100} v_5(k!)$$

where $v_5(k!)$ is the 5-adic valuation of $k!$.

### Step 2: Legendre's formula

$$v_5(k!) = \left\lfloor\frac{k}{5}\right\rfloor + \left\lfloor\frac{k}{25}\righ
t\rfloor + \left\lfloor\frac{k}{125}\right\rfloor + \cdots$$

Since $k \le 100 < 125$, the term $\lfloor k/125\rfloor = 0$ and all higher term
s vanish. Thus:

$$v_5(k!) = \left\lfloor\frac{k}{5}\right\rfloor + \left\lfloor\frac{k}{25}\righ
t\rfloor$$

### Step 3: Compute each sum

**Sum A** $= \displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{100}\left\lfloor\frac{k}{5}\right\rfloor$:

- $k=1,2,3,4 \to 0$ (4 values)
- $k=5,\dots,9 \to 1$; $k=10,\dots,14\to 2$; $\dots$; $k=95,\dots,99\to 19$ (eac
h block has 5 values)
- $k=100 \to 20$

$$\text{Sum A} = 4\cdot 0 + 5\cdot(1+2+\cdots+19) + 20 = 5\cdot\frac{19\cdot 20}
{2} + 20 = 5\cdot 190 + 20 = 970$$

**Sum B** $= \displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{100}\left\lfloor\frac{k}{25}\right\rfloor$
:

- $k=1,\dots,24 \to 0$ (24 values)
- $k=25,\dots,49 \to 1$; $k=50,\dots,74\to 2$; $k=75,\dots,99\to 3$ (each block
has 25 values)
- $k=100 \to 4$

$$\text{Sum B} = 24\cdot 0 + 25\cdot(1+2+3) + 4 = 25\cdot 6 + 4 = 154$$

### Step 4: Total and final remainder

$$N = 970 + 154 = 1124$$

$$N \bmod 1000 = \boxed{124}$$
