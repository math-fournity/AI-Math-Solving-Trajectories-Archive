# Problem

In the coastal city of Aethelgard, a lighthouse uses a sequence of 8 colored flares to send messages to ships. Each position in the sequence can only hold one of two colors: Crimson (0) or Emerald (1). A complete signal is a sequence of 8 flares.

Due to heavy fog and atmospheric interference, the harbor master warns that signals can be easily misread. To ensure that a message is not confused for another, the "Signal Divergence" between any two valid sequences must be high. The Divergence is defined as the number of positions in the 8-flare sequence where the colors do not match. 

The safety protocol requires that the Divergence between any two distinct valid signals in the lighthouse's codebook must be at least 3. 

Find as many distinct 8-flare signals as possible to include in the codebook such that any two of them differ in at least 3 positions. You get one point per signal.
