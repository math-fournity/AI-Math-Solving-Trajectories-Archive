## Solution

The question asks for the proportion (as $v \to \infty$) of nonplanar graphs on
$v$ vertices that are *certified* nonplanar by the edge-count criteria:
- $e > 3(v-2)$, or
- triangle-free with $e > 2(v-2)$.

These are exactly the contrapositives of the planar edge bounds ($e \le 3v-6$ fo
r planar, $e \le 2v-4$ for triangle-free planar).

### Key counts

Let $N = \binom{v}{2}$ be the number of possible edges. The total number of (lab
eled) graphs on $v$ vertices is $2^N$.

**Graphs with $e \le 3(v-2)$ are negligible.** The number of such graphs is
$$S = \sum_{k=0}^{3(v-2)} \binom{N}{k}.$$
Since $3(v-2) = O(v)$ while $N/2 = \Theta(v^2)$, the threshold $3(v-2)$ lies far
 in the left tail of the binomial distribution $\text{Bin}(N, 1/2)$. By a Cherno
ff bound,
$$S \le 2^N \cdot \exp\!\bigl(-\Omega(v^2)\bigr),$$
so $S / 2^N \to 0$ exponentially fast.

**Planar graphs are negligible.** The number of labeled planar graphs on $v$ ver
tices is $2^{\Theta(v \log v)}$ (Giménez–Noy), while $2^N = 2^{\Theta(v^2)}$. He
nce the fraction of planar graphs among all graphs tends to $0$.

### Computing the proportion

Let:
- $A$ = set of nonplanar graphs,
- $B$ = set of nonplanar graphs satisfying the edge-count condition ($e > 3(v-2)
$, or triangle-free with $e > 2(v-2)$).

Since $B \supseteq \{\text{graphs with } e > 3(v-2)\} \setminus \{\text{planar g
raphs}\}$, and every planar graph has $e \le 3(v-2)$ (so no planar graph has $e
> 3(v-2)$):

$$|B| \ge \bigl|\{\text{graphs with } e > 3(v-2)\}\bigr| = 2^N - S.$$

The total number of nonplanar graphs is
$$|A| = 2^N - (\text{number of planar graphs}) \le 2^N.$$

Therefore the proportion satisfies
$$\frac{|B|}{|A|} \ge \frac{2^N - S}{2^N} = 1 - \frac{S}{2^N} \xrightarrow{v\to\
infty} 1,$$
and trivially $|B|/|A| \le 1$.

By the squeeze theorem,
$$\lim_{v \to \infty} \frac{|B|}{|A|} = 1.$$

### Conclusion

As $v \to \infty$, essentially every nonplanar graph has so many edges ($\sim v^
2/4$ on average, versus the linear threshold $3(v-2)$) that it is certified nonp
lanar by the elementary edge-count criterion. The negligible exceptions — nonpla
nar graphs that are nonetheless sparse enough to evade the edge bound — form a v
anishing fraction. The proportion is

$$\boxed{1}.$$
