## Solution

**Setup.** We have $m$ Type E containers with distinct positive even weights and
 $n$ Type O containers with distinct positive odd weights. The total weight is e
xactly $1987$ kg. We maximize $3m + 4n$.

**Minimum total weight.** For fixed $m, n$, the minimum possible total weight is
 obtained by choosing the *smallest* $m$ even numbers and the *smallest* $n$ odd
 numbers:

$$S_{\min}(m,n) = \underbrace{2+4+\cdots+2m}_{m(m+1)} + \underbrace{1+3+\cdots+(
2n-1)}_{n^2} = m(m+1) + n^2.$$

**Achievability and parity.** Starting from the minimum configuration, we can in
crease any container's weight by $2$ (stepping to the next unused even/odd numbe
r), each step adding $2$ to the total. Hence every total of the form $S_{\min} +
 2k$ ($k \ge 0$) is achievable. Since $m(m+1)$ is always even and $1987$ is odd,
 we need $n^2$ odd, i.e., **$n$ must be odd**. Then $1987 - S_{\min}$ is even an
d non-negative, so the target is reachable.

**Optimization.** We maximize $3m + 4n$ subject to:
$$m(m+1) + n^2 \le 1987, \qquad n \text{ odd}, \qquad m \ge 1.$$

Using a Lagrangian relaxation on $m^2 + n^2 \approx 1987$: the ratio $m/n = 3/4$
 is optimal, giving $m \approx 26.7$, $n \approx 35.6$. Testing nearby candidate
s with $n$ odd:

| $m$ | $n$ | $m(m+1)+n^2$ | $\le 1987$? | $3m+4n$ |
|-----|-----|--------------|-------------|---------|
| 27 | 35 | $756+1225=1981$ | ✓ | **221** |
| 26 | 35 | $702+1225=1927$ | ✓ | 218 |
| 28 | 33 | $812+1089=1901$ | ✓ | 216 |
| 29 | 33 | $870+1089=1959$ | ✓ | 219 |
| 24 | 37 | $600+1369=1969$ | ✓ | 220 |
| 23 | 37 | $552+1369=1921$ | ✓ | 217 |
| 35 | 25 | $1260+625=1885$ | ✓ | 205 |
| 11 | 43 | $132+1849=1981$ | ✓ | 205 |

**Ruling out 222 and above.** For $3m+4n = 222$ with $n$ odd and $n \equiv 0 \pm
od{3}$: every feasible $(m,n)$ pair (e.g., $(30,33), (22,39), (38,27), (14,45)$)
 gives $m(m+1)+n^2 > 1987$. For $3m+4n = 223$ with $n$ odd and $n \equiv 1\pmod{
3}$: every pair (e.g., $(25,37), (33,31), (17,43)$) also exceeds $1987$. Higher
values are even worse. Hence $222$ is unreachable.

**Verification of the optimum.** With $m=27$, $n=35$:
- $S_{\min} = 27\cdot28 + 35^2 = 756 + 1225 = 1981$.
- Difference: $1987 - 1981 = 6 = 3\times 2$. Increase the largest even weight $5
4 \to 56 \to 58 \to 60$ (three steps of $2$), keeping all weights distinct. ✓
- Performance index: $3(27) + 4(35) = 81 + 140 = \boxed{221}$.
