## Prior Predictive Density of $(\bar X, S^2)$ under a Normal–Gamma Prior

**Setup and notation.** Let
$$\bar X=\tfrac1n\sum_{i=1}^n X_i,\qquad S^2=\tfrac1n\sum_{i=1}^n(X_i-\bar X)^2,
\qquad \nu:=n-1,\qquad \tau_n^2:=\tau_0^2+\tfrac1n.$$
(With $S^2=\frac1{n-1}\sum$ instead, replace every $nS^2$ below by $(n-1)S^2$.)
The prior is the conjugate Normal–Gamma:
$$\mu\mid\sigma^2\sim N(\mu_0,\tau_0^2\sigma^2),\qquad \lambda:=1/\sigma^2\sim\m
athrm{Gamma}(\alpha_0,\beta_0),$$
where $\mathrm{Gamma}(\alpha_0,\beta_0)$ uses the **rate** parameterization, $p(
\lambda)\propto \lambda^{\alpha_0-1}e^{-\beta_0\lambda}$.

---

**Step 1 — Conditional law of $(\bar X,S^2)\mid(\mu,\sigma^2)$.** Standard norma
l-sample facts:
$$\bar X\mid\mu,\sigma^2\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2/n),\qquad nS^2/\sigma^2\sim\chi^2_\n
u,$$
with $\bar X\perp S^2\mid(\mu,\sigma^2)$. Since $S^2$ does not involve $\mu$, th
e pair remains conditionally independent given $\sigma^2$ alone once $\mu$ is in
tegrated out.

**Step 2 — Marginal of $\bar X\mid\sigma^2$ (integrate out $\mu$).** The convolu
tion of $N(\mu,\sigma^2/n)$ with $N(\mu_0,\tau_0^2\sigma^2)$ gives
$$\bar X\mid\sigma^2\sim N\!\bigl(\mu_0,\;(\tau_0^2+1/n)\sigma^2\bigr)=N(\mu_0,\
tau_n^2\sigma^2).$$

**Step 3 — Joint of $(\bar X,S^2)\mid\lambda$.** Using $U:=nS^2\mid\lambda\sim\m
athrm{Gamma}(\nu/2,\text{rate }\lambda/2)$ and $S^2=U/n$,
$$p(\bar x,s^2\mid\lambda)=\sqrt{\frac{\lambda}{2\pi\tau_n^2}}\,e^{-\lambda(\bar
 x-\mu_0)^2/(2\tau_n^2)}\;\cdot\; n\,\frac{(\lambda/2)^{\nu/2}}{\Gamma(\nu/2)}(n
s^2)^{\nu/2-1}e^{-\lambda ns^2/2}.$$

**Step 4 — Integrate out $\lambda$.** Multiplying by $p(\lambda)=\frac{\beta_0^{
\alpha_0}}{\Gamma(\alpha_0)}\lambda^{\alpha_0-1}e^{-\beta_0\lambda}$ and collect
ing powers of $\lambda$:
$$\lambda^{\alpha_0+\frac{\nu+1}{2}-1}\exp\!\Bigl[-\lambda\Bigl(\beta_0+\tfrac{(
\bar x-\mu_0)^2}{2\tau_n^2}+\tfrac{ns^2}{2}\Bigr)\Bigr],\qquad \tfrac{\nu+1}{2}=
\tfrac n2.$$
The integral is a Gamma integral:
$$\int_0^\infty\!\lambda^{\alpha_0+n/2-1}e^{-\lambda C}\,d\lambda=\frac{\Gamma(\
alpha_0+n/2)}{C^{\,\alpha_0+n/2}},\qquad C:=\beta_0+\frac{(\bar x-\mu_0)^2}{2\ta
u_n^2}+\frac{ns^2}{2}.$$

---

### Closed-form prior predictive density

$$\boxed{\;p(\bar x,s^2)=\frac{n\,\beta_0^{\alpha_0}\,\Gamma\!\bigl(\alpha_0+\tf
rac n2\bigr)}{(2\pi\tau_n^2)^{1/2}\,2^{\nu/2}\,\Gamma(\nu/2)\,\Gamma(\alpha_0)}\
,(ns^2)^{\nu/2-1}\left[\beta_0+\frac{(\bar x-\mu_0)^2}{2\tau_n^2}+\frac{ns^2}{2}
\right]^{-\left(\alpha_0+n/2\right)}\;}$$

with $\nu=n-1$ and $\tau_n^2=\tau_0^2+1/n$. This is a closed expression in Gamma
 functions and a single power — **evaluable without any further integration**.

---

**Verification via marginals.**

*Marginal of $\bar X$:* integrating out $s^2$ (Beta-type integral $\int u^{a-1}(
c+u/2)^{-b}du$ with $a=\nu/2$, $b=\alpha_0+n/2$) yields
$$p(\bar x)=\frac{\Gamma(\alpha_0+\tfrac12)}{(2\pi\tau_n^2\beta_0)^{1/2}\,\Gamma
(\alpha_0)}\left[1+\frac{(\bar x-\mu_0)^2}{2\tau_n^2\beta_0}\right]^{-(\alpha_0+
1/2)},$$
i.e. a Student-$t$ with $2\alpha_0$ degrees of freedom, location $\mu_0$, scale
$\tau_n^2\beta_0/\alpha_0$ — exactly the $t$-distribution obtained from $\bar X\
mid\sigma^2\sim N(\mu_0,\tau_n^2\sigma^2)$ with $\sigma^2\sim\mathrm{InvGamma}(\
alpha_0,\beta_0)$. ✓

*Marginal of $S^2$:* integrating out $\bar x$ (Gaussian integral $\int(y^2+d)^{-
p}dy$ with $p=\alpha_0+n/2$) yields
$$p(s^2)=\frac{n\,\beta_0^{\alpha_0}\,\Gamma(\alpha_0+\nu/2)}{2^{\nu/2}\,\Gamma(
\nu/2)\,\Gamma(\alpha_0)}(ns^2)^{\nu/2-1}\left[\beta_0+\frac{ns^2}{2}\right]^{-(
\alpha_0+\nu/2)},$$
which is precisely the marginal of $nS^2\sim\mathrm{Gamma}(\nu/2,\lambda/2)$ mix
ed over $\lambda\sim\mathrm{Gamma}(\alpha_0,\beta_0)$, i.e. a Gamma–Gamma (compo
und Gamma) law. ✓

Both marginals match the known conjugate results, confirming the joint formula.
