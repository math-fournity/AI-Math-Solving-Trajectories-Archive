# Problem

In a remote high-tech logistics facility, an automated maintenance robot is assigned to inspect a grid-based storage floor consisting of an 8x8 array of 64 square scanning zones. The robot must execute a mission consisting of exactly 64 movements.

The mission parameters are strictly defined: 
1. The robot must visit every scanning zone exactly once.
2. The robot must return to its starting zone at the end of the 64th move.
3. The robot's path must never cross itself; the sequence of movements must form a single, non-intersecting closed loop.
4. From any zone, the robot can move to any of the 8 adjacent zones (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally).

During the operation, the robot can perform two types of transitions: lateral moves (shifting to an adjacent zone sharing a side) and diagonal moves (shifting to an adjacent zone sharing only a corner).

Let $M$ be the total number of diagonal moves performed by the robot during its mission. Find the maximum possible value of $M$.
