## Proof

Place the circumcenter O at the origin. Let **a**, **b**, **c**, **p** be the po
sition vectors of A, B, C, P, with |**a**| = |**b**| = |**c**| = R.

**Lemma (Nine-point center formula).** For a triangle with vertices U, V, W and
circumcenter O', the nine-point center equals (U + V + W − O')/2.

*Proof.* Taking O' as origin, the orthocenter equals (U−O')+(V−O')+(W−O') (stand
ard result: with circumcenter at origin, orthocenter = sum of vertex vectors). S
hifting back, the orthocenter is U+V+W−2O'. The nine-point center is the midpoin
t of circumcenter and orthocenter: (O' + U+V+W−2O')/2 = (U+V+W−O')/2. □

**Computing D and X.** Let O₁ be the circumcenter of △PBC. By the lemma:

D = (p + b + c − O₁)/2.

Let O₂ be the circumcenter of △DBC. By the lemma:

X = (d + b + c − O₂)/2 = ½[(p + b + c − O₁)/2 + b + c − O₂] = (p + 3b + 3c − O₁
− 2O₂)/4.

**Key observation.** Since |b| = |c| = R, both O₁ (circumcenter of PBC) and O₂ (
circumcenter of DBC) lie on the perpendicular bisector of BC, which passes throu
gh O and is perpendicular to BC. Therefore:

O₁ · (c − b) = 0, &nbsp;&nbsp; O₂ · (c − b) = 0.

Also (b + c) · (c − b) = |c|² − |b|² = 0.

**Showing p/4 lies on the line through X perpendicular to BC.** Compute:

p/4 − X = [p − (p + 3b + 3c − O₁ − 2O₂)]/4 = [−3(b+c) + O₁ + 2O₂]/4.

Dotting with (c − b):

(p/4 − X) · (c − b) = [−3(b+c)·(c−b) + O₁·(c−b) + 2·O₂·(c−b)]/4 = [−3·0 + 0 + 2·
0]/4 = 0.

So **p/4 − X is perpendicular to BC**, meaning p/4 lies on the line through X pe
rpendicular to BC.

**Cyclic symmetry.** The identical argument applies to the other two lines:

- For Y (nine-point center of △ECA, where E is nine-point center of △PCA): since
 |c| = |a| = R, the circumcenters of △PCA and △ECA lie on the perpendicular bise
ctor of CA, giving O₃·(a−c) = 0, O₄·(a−c) = 0, and (a+c)·(a−c) = 0. The same com
putation yields (p/4 − Y)·(a−c) = 0, so p/4 lies on the line through Y perpendic
ular to CA.

- For Z (nine-point center of △FAB, where F is nine-point center of △PAB): since
 |a| = |b| = R, the circumcenters of △PAB and △FAB lie on the perpendicular bise
ctor of AB, giving O₅·(b−a) = 0, O₆·(b−a) = 0, and (a+b)·(b−a) = 0. The same com
putation yields (p/4 − Z)·(b−a) = 0, so p/4 lies on the line through Z perpendic
ular to AB.

**Conclusion.** The point Q = p/4 lies on all three lines. Since the three direc
tions (perpendicular to BC, CA, AB) are distinct for a non-degenerate triangle,
these lines are concurrent at Q = p/4.

Therefore OQ = |p/4| = ¼|p| = ¼·OP, and:

$$\frac{OQ}{OP} = \frac{1}{4}.$$
