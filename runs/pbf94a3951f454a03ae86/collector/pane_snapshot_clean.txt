## Proof

Let $F(T_1,\ldots,T_n) \in L[T_1,\ldots,T_n]$ be homogeneous of degree $d < n$,
with $L = K(X)$ and $K$ algebraically closed. Each coefficient $a_{i_1\ldots i_n
}$ is a rational function in $X$, so we may write

$$a_{i_1\ldots i_n} = \frac{p_{i_1\ldots i_n}(X)}{q_{i_1\ldots i_n}(X)}, \qquad
p_{i_1\ldots i_n},\, q_{i_1\ldots i_n} \in K[X],\quad q_{i_1\ldots i_n} \neq 0.$
$

There are only finitely many coefficients, so finitely many denominators. Let

$$D(X) = \operatorname{lcm}\bigl\{\, q_{i_1\ldots i_n}(X) : \text{over all coeff
icients of } F \,\bigr\} \in K[X].$$

Since $K$ is a field (in particular an integral domain) and each $q_{i_1\ldots i
_n} \neq 0$, we have $D \neq 0$. By construction, $D$ is divisible by every deno
minator, so

$$b_{i_1\ldots i_n} := D(X)\cdot a_{i_1\ldots i_n} = \frac{D(X)\, p_{i_1\ldots i
_n}(X)}{q_{i_1\ldots i_n}(X)} \in K[X] \qquad \text{for every coefficient.}$$

Define

$$G(T_1,\ldots,T_n) = D(X)\cdot F(T_1,\ldots,T_n) = \sum b_{i_1\ldots i_n}\, T_1
^{i_1}\cdots T_n^{i_n}.$$

Then $G$ is homogeneous of the same degree $d < n$, and every coefficient of $G$
 lies in $K[X]$.

**Key observation.** $D(X)$ is a *nonzero element of the field* $L = K(X)$. Mult
iplying a polynomial by a nonzero scalar from the base field does not change its
 zero set: for any $(t_1,\ldots,t_n)$ in $L^n$ (or in any extension of $L$),

$$G(t_1,\ldots,t_n) = 0 \iff D(X)\cdot F(t_1,\ldots,t_n) = 0 \iff F(t_1,\ldots,t
_n) = 0,$$

since $D(X) \neq 0$ in $L$ and $L$ is a field (hence an integral domain). In par
ticular $F$ has a nontrivial zero if and only if $G$ has a nontrivial zero.

Therefore, proving the theorem (existence of a nontrivial zero for every homogen
eous $F$ of degree $d < n$ over $L$) reduces to proving it for $G$, whose coeffi
cients all lie in $K[X]$. Hence **it is sufficient to consider the case where ev
ery coefficient belongs to $K[X]$**. $\blacksquare$
