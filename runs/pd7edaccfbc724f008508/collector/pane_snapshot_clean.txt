## Solution

**Setup.** A point on the parabolic profile is $P(u)=(u,\,u^2,\,0)$, and the rec
eiver is $R=(0,-10,a)$. The beam from $P(u)$ to $R$ is parametrized as $(1-t)P(u
)+tR$. It pierces the wall $y=0$ when
$$(1-t)u^2-10t=0 \;\Longrightarrow\; t=\frac{u^2}{u^2+10}.$$
The piercing point on the $xz$-plane is therefore
$$X(u)=\frac{10u}{u^2+10},\qquad Z(u)=\frac{au^2}{u^2+10}.$$

As $u\to\pm\infty$, $(X,Z)\to(0,a)$; at $u=0$, $(X,Z)=(0,0)$. The positive-$u$ b
ranch traces the right half of the loop from $(0,0)$ up to $(0,a)$, and the nega
tive-$u$ branch traces the left half back down — a simple closed curve.

**Area via Green's theorem.** With
$$dX=\frac{10(10-u^2)}{(u^2+10)^2}\,du,\qquad dZ=\frac{20au}{(u^2+10)^2}\,du,$$
we compute
$$X\,dZ-Z\,dX=\frac{10au^2}{(u^2+10)^2}\,du.$$
Thus
$$A=\frac12\left|\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{10au^2}{(u^2+10)^2}\,du\right|.$$

**Evaluating the integral.** Using $\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{u^
2}{(u^2+c)^2}\,du=\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt{c}}$ with $c=10$:
$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{u^2}{(u^2+10)^2}\,du=\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt{10}}.$$
Therefore
$$A=\frac12\cdot 10a\cdot\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt{10}}=\frac{a\pi\sqrt{10}}{4}.$$

**Solving for $a$.** Setting $A=\pi$:
$$\frac{a\sqrt{10}}{4}=1 \;\Longrightarrow\; a=\frac{4}{\sqrt{10}}=\frac{2\sqrt{
10}}{5}.$$

This gives $p=2$, $q=10$, $r=5$, with $\gcd(2,5)=1$ and $10$ square-free. Hence
$$p+q+r=2+10+5=\boxed{17}.$$
