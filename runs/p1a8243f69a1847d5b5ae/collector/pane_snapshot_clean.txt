## Proof

**Notation.** Let $x = A^{-1}b$ be the exact solution, $\tilde{x}$ a computed ap
proximation, and $r = b - A\tilde{x}$ the residual. All absolute-value operation
s on matrices/vectors are componentwise.

---

### Step 1. Express the error in terms of the residual

From $Ax = b$ and $A\tilde{x} = b - r$, subtracting gives
$$A(x - \tilde{x}) = r \quad\Longrightarrow\quad x - \tilde{x} = A^{-1}r.$$

Taking absolute values componentwise and using $|A^{-1}r| \leq |A^{-1}|\,|r|$:
$$|x - \tilde{x}| \leq |A^{-1}|\,|r|. \tag{1}$$

---

### Step 2. Bound $|r|$ using the componentwise backward error

By definition of the Oettli–Prager componentwise backward error,
$$\omega_*(\tilde{x}) = \max_{j=1,\dots,m}\frac{|r_j|}{(|A|\,|\tilde{x}|)_j},$$
so for every component $j$:
$$|r_j| \leq \omega_*(\tilde{x})\,(|A|\,|\tilde{x}|)_j.$$

In vector form this is the componentwise inequality
$$|r| \leq \omega_*(\tilde{x})\,|A|\,|\tilde{x}|. \tag{2}$$

---

### Step 3. Combine (1) and (2)

Substituting (2) into (1):
$$|x - \tilde{x}| \leq |A^{-1}|\,|r| \leq |A^{-1}|\,\bigl(\omega_*(\tilde{x})\,|
A|\,|\tilde{x}|\bigr) = \omega_*(\tilde{x})\,|A^{-1}|\,|A|\,|\tilde{x}|.$$

Taking the $\infty$-norm (which is monotone on nonneg vectors):
$$\|x - \tilde{x}\|_\infty \leq \omega_*(\tilde{x})\,\bigl\||A^{-1}|\,|A|\,|\til
de{x}|\bigr\|_\infty.$$

Dividing both sides by $\|\tilde{x}\|_\infty$:
$$\frac{\|x - \tilde{x}\|_\infty}{\|\tilde{x}\|_\infty} \leq \omega_*(\tilde{x})
\;\frac{\bigl\||A^{-1}|\,|A|\,|\tilde{x}|\bigr\|_\infty}{\|\tilde{x}\|_\infty} =
 \operatorname{cond}(A,\tilde{x})\cdot\omega_*(\tilde{x}). \qquad\blacksquare$$

---

### Step 4. Why this is sharper than the normwise estimate (15.3)

The normwise forward error estimate (15.3) is
$$\frac{\|x-\tilde{x}\|_\infty}{\|\tilde{x}\|_\infty} \leq \kappa_\infty(A)\cdot
\frac{\|r\|_\infty}{\|A\|_\infty\,\|\tilde{x}\|_\infty},$$
where $\kappa_\infty(A) = \|A^{-1}\|_\infty\|A\|_\infty$ is the normwise conditi
on number. The componentwise estimate replaces **both** factors by sharper (smal
ler-or-equal) quantities:

**(a) The condition number satisfies $\operatorname{cond}(A,\tilde{x}) \leq \kap
pa_\infty(A)$.**

Indeed, set $y = |A|\,|\tilde{x}| \geq 0$. Then
$$\bigl\||A^{-1}|\,|A|\,|\tilde{x}|\bigr\|_\infty = \bigl\||A^{-1}|\,y\bigr\|_\i
nfty \leq \|A^{-1}\|_\infty\,\|y\|_\infty = \|A^{-1}\|_\infty\,\bigl\||A|\,|\til
de{x}|\bigr\|_\infty.$$

By submultiplicativity of the $\infty$-norm (valid for nonneg matrices/vectors):
$$\bigl\||A|\,|\tilde{x}|\bigr\|_\infty \leq \|A\|_\infty\,\|\tilde{x}\|_\infty.
$$

Therefore
$$\operatorname{cond}(A,\tilde{x}) = \frac{\bigl\||A^{-1}|\,|A|\,|\tilde{x}|\big
r\|_\infty}{\|\tilde{x}\|_\infty} \leq \|A^{-1}\|_\infty\,\|A\|_\infty = \kappa_
\infty(A).$$

This inequality can be **dramatically strict**: $\||A^{-1}|\,|A|\|_\infty$ ignor
es sign cancellations that $\|A^{-1}\|_\infty\|A\|_\infty$ cannot, and $\operato
rname{cond}(A,\tilde{x})$ further depends on the direction of $|\tilde{x}|$. For
 matrices with poorly scaled rows/columns (common in practice), $\kappa_\infty(A
)$ can be orders of magnitude larger than $\operatorname{cond}(A,\tilde{x})$.

**(b) Under componentwise backward stability, both backward errors are $O(u)$.**

When $\tilde{x}$ is computed in a componentwise backward stable fashion, the Oet
tli–Prager theorem guarantees
$$\omega_*(\tilde{x}) = O(u),$$
where $u$ is the unit roundoff. Componentwise stability implies normwise stabili
ty, so the normwise backward error $\frac{\|r\|_\infty}{\|A\|_\infty\|\tilde{x}\
|_\infty} = O(u)$ as well.

**(c) Conclusion on sharpness.**

Under componentwise backward stability, the two estimates become:
- **Normwise:** $\displaystyle\frac{\|x-\tilde{x}\|_\infty}{\|\tilde{x}\|_\infty
} \lesssim \kappa_\infty(A)\cdot u$
- **Componentwise:** $\displaystyle\frac{\|x-\tilde{x}\|_\infty}{\|\tilde{x}\|_\
infty} \lesssim \operatorname{cond}(A,\tilde{x})\cdot u$

Since $\operatorname{cond}(A,\tilde{x}) \leq \kappa_\infty(A)$—with the gap pote
ntially enormous for badly scaled $A$—the componentwise estimate is **strictly s
harper**. It exploits both the structure of $A$ (via $|A^{-1}||A|$ rather than $
\|A^{-1}\|\|A\|$) and the direction of $\tilde{x}$, whereas the normwise estimat
e must pessimistically bound over all possible right-hand sides and ignores sign
 structure. $\blacksquare$
