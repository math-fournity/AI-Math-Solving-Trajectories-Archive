## Proof

We want to evaluate $\lim_{a\to\infty} a\cdot P(|X|>a)$ under the assumption $X\
in L^1(P)$, i.e., $E[|X|]<\infty$.

**Key identity (tail integral formula).** For a non-negative random variable $Y$
,
$$E[Y]=\int_0^\infty P(Y>t)\,dt.$$
Applying this to $Y=|X|$ gives
$$E[|X|]=\int_0^\infty P(|X|>t)\,dt<\infty. \tag{1}$$

**Monotonicity.** The map $t\mapsto P(|X|>t)$ is non-negative and non-increasing
.

**Tail integral vanishes.** From (1), the tail integral satisfies
$$\int_a^\infty P(|X|>t)\,dt \xrightarrow{a\to\infty} 0. \tag{2}$$

**Bounding the quantity of interest.** For any $a>0$, since $P(|X|>t)$ is non-in
creasing, for all $t\in[a,2a]$ we have $P(|X|>t)\ge P(|X|>2a)$. Integrating over
 $[a,2a]$:
$$\int_a^{2a} P(|X|>t)\,dt \;\ge\; \int_a^{2a} P(|X|>2a)\,dt \;=\; a\cdot P(|X|>
2a). \tag{3}$$

Combining (2) and (3):
$$0 \;\le\; a\cdot P(|X|>2a) \;\le\; \int_a^{2a} P(|X|>t)\,dt \;\le\; \int_a^\in
fty P(|X|>t)\,dt \;\xrightarrow{a\to\infty}\; 0.$$

Therefore $a\cdot P(|X|>2a)\to 0$ as $a\to\infty$. Substituting $a\mapsto a/2$ (
i.e., replacing $2a$ by $a$):
$$a\cdot P(|X|>a)\;\xrightarrow{a\to\infty}\;0.$$

**Conclusion.**

$$\boxed{\lim_{a\to\infty} a\cdot P(|X|>a)=0.}$$
