# Problem

In a remote high-tech agricultural facility, nine automated sensors are installed on a perfectly flat grid. The sensors are positioned at every possible coordinate $(x, y)$ where $x$ and $y$ are integers from the set $\{-1, 0, 1\}$. Specifically, sensors are located at:
$(-1,-1), (-1,0), (-1,1), (0,-1), (0,0), (0,1), (1,-1), (1,0),$ and $(1,1)$.

To enable data synchronization, an engineer must lay down fiber-optic cables to connect these nine sensors. The cables can be laid in any direction and can meet at any point, including locations where no sensors are present (Steiner points). The requirement is that the final network of cables must allow a signal to travel between any two sensors in the set, either directly or through a series of connected cables.

Find the smallest total length of fiber-optic cable required to interconnect all nine sensors.
