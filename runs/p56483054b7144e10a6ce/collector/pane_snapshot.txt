Set $a_k = \left(\frac{en}{2k}\right)^{2k}\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi k}}\right)^{\

$$\ln a_k = 2k\ln\!\left(\frac{en}{2k}\right) - \frac{3n\ln 2}{2\ln n}\,\ln(\pi

$$f\!\left(\tfrac12\right) = 1 - \frac{3\ln 2}{2} \approx -0.0397 < 0.$$

Therefore $f(\alpha) \le f(\tfrac12) < 0$ for all $\alpha \in (0,\tfrac12]$.
