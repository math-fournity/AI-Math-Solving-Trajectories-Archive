## Proof

**Setup.** The Schwartz space $\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^d)$ is the Fréchet space o
f smooth, rapidly decreasing functions, topologized by the countable family of s
eminorms
$$\|f\|_{\alpha,\beta} \;=\; \sup_{x\in\mathbb{R}^d}\bigl|x^{\alpha}\,\partial^{
\beta}f(x)\bigr|,\qquad \alpha,\beta\in\mathbb{N}_0^d.$$
The standard metric $d_S$ is constructed by enumerating all pairs of multiindice
s as $\{(\alpha_k,\beta_k)\}_{k=1}^{\infty}$ and setting
$$d_S(f,g)\;=\;\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}2^{-k}\,\frac{\|f-g\|_{\alpha_k,\beta_k}}{1+\|
f-g\|_{\alpha_k,\beta_k}}.$$
Each summand lies in $[0,2^{-k}]$, so the series converges and $d_S$ is a well-d
efined translation-invariant metric inducing the Fréchet topology.

We prove the equivalence of:
- (A) $d_S(f_n,f)\to 0$, and
- (B) $\|f_n-f\|_{\alpha,\beta}\to 0$ for every $\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb{N}_0^d$.

---

**(B) $\Rightarrow$ (A).** Assume $\|f_n-f\|_{\alpha,\beta}\to 0$ for all multii
ndices. Define
$$a_k^{(n)} \;=\; \frac{\|f_n-f\|_{\alpha_k,\beta_k}}{1+\|f_n-f\|_{\alpha_k,\bet
a_k}} \;\in\; [0,1].$$
For each fixed $k$, hypothesis (B) gives $\|f_n-f\|_{\alpha_k,\beta_k}\to 0$, he
nce $a_k^{(n)}\to 0$ as $n\to\infty$. Moreover $0\le 2^{-k}a_k^{(n)}\le 2^{-k}$
and $\sum_{k}2^{-k}=1<\infty$. By the dominated convergence theorem applied to t
he counting measure on $\mathbb{N}$,
$$d_S(f_n,f)\;=\;\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}2^{-k}\,a_k^{(n)}\;\xrightarrow{n\to\infty}\
;0.$$

---

**(A) $\Rightarrow$ (B).** Assume $d_S(f_n,f)\to 0$. Fix arbitrary multiindices
$\alpha,\beta$. By the enumeration, there is an index $K$ with $(\alpha_K,\beta_
K)=(\alpha,\beta)$. Since every summand is non-negative,
$$2^{-K}\,\frac{\|f_n-f\|_{\alpha,\beta}}{1+\|f_n-f\|_{\alpha,\beta}} \;\le\; d_
S(f_n,f)\;\xrightarrow{n\to\infty}\;0.$$
The map $t\mapsto \frac{t}{1+t}$ is a homeomorphism $[0,\infty)\to[0,1)$ with co
ntinuous inverse $s\mapsto\frac{s}{1-s}$. Therefore $\frac{\|f_n-f\|_{\alpha,\be
ta}}{1+\|f_n-f\|_{\alpha,\beta}}\to 0$ forces $\|f_n-f\|_{\alpha,\beta}\to 0$. S
ince $\alpha,\beta$ were arbitrary, (B) holds.

---

**Conclusion.** Convergence in the Schwartz space with respect to the metric $d_
S$ is equivalent to the convergence of $\|f_n-f\|_{\alpha,\beta}$ for all multii
ndices $\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb{N}_0^d$. The key mechanism is that $d_S$ is built
 from the seminorms via a bounded, monotone, termwise-faithful encoding (the $\f
rac{t}{1+t}$ transform with summable weights $2^{-k}$), so vanishing of the metr
ic is equivalent to simultaneous vanishing of every seminorm.
