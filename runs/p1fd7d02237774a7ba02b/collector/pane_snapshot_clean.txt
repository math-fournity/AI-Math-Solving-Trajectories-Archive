## Proof

**Setup.** Let $O$ be the point from which the three lines diverge, and place th
e origin at $O$. Let the three lines have direction vectors $\mathbf{u}_1, \math
bf{u}_2, \mathbf{u}_3$ (any two of which are linearly independent). The vertices
 of the triangle are
$$A = a\,\mathbf{u}_1,\qquad B = b\,\mathbf{u}_2,\qquad C = c\,\mathbf{u}_3$$
for scalars $a, b, c$.

**The fixed-direction constraint forces proportionality.** The side $AB$ has a f
ixed direction, so $B - A = b\,\mathbf{u}_2 - a\,\mathbf{u}_1$ is always paralle
l to some fixed vector $\mathbf{v}_{12}$. Writing $\mathbf{v}_{12} = \alpha\,\ma
thbf{u}_1 + \beta\,\mathbf{u}_2$ (possible since $\mathbf{u}_1, \mathbf{u}_2$ ar
e independent), the condition $b\,\mathbf{u}_2 - a\,\mathbf{u}_1 = \lambda(\alph
a\,\mathbf{u}_1 + \beta\,\mathbf{u}_2)$ gives $-a = \lambda\alpha$ and $b = \lam
bda\beta$, hence
$$\frac{a}{b} = -\frac{\alpha}{\beta} = \text{const.}$$
Applying the same argument to sides $BC$ and $CA$, the ratios $b/c$ and $c/a$ ar
e also constant. Therefore there exist fixed constants $a_0, b_0, c_0$ and a sin
gle parameter $t$ such that
$$a = t\,a_0,\quad b = t\,b_0,\quad c = t\,c_0.$$

**The triangle is a homothetic family.** Setting $A_0 = a_0\,\mathbf{u}_1,\; B_0
 = b_0\,\mathbf{u}_2,\; C_0 = c_0\,\mathbf{u}_3$, the triangle at parameter $t$
is
$$A = t\,A_0,\quad B = t\,B_0,\quad C = t\,C_0.$$
This is the image of triangle $A_0B_0C_0$ under the homothety $h(O, t)$ centered
 at $O$ with ratio $t$.

**Circumcenter under homothety.** A homothety maps circles to circles and preser
ves incidence, so it maps the circumcircle of $A_0B_0C_0$ (center $P_0$, radius
$R_0$) to a circle of radius $|t|\,R_0$ centered at $t\,P_0$. This image circle
passes through $A, B, C$, so it is the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$. Hence the
 circumcenter of $ABC$ is
$$P(t) = t\,P_0.$$

**Locus.** As $t$ ranges over all nonzero real values, $P(t) = t\,P_0$ traces ou
t the straight line through $O$ and $P_0$ (excluding the degenerate point $O$ it
self, which corresponds to $t=0$).

**Conclusion.** The locus of the center of the circumscribed circle is a **strai
ght line passing through the point from which the three given lines diverge.**
