# Problem

A metropolitan city is planning a new fiber-optic grid laid out over a rectangular district of $m \times n$ blocks. The grid consists of all the street segments that form the perimeters of these $m \times n$ individual square blocks.

The city council has contracted three different telecommunications providers—Red-Link, Blue-Stream, and Green-Fiber—to install the lines. Each individual street segment must be assigned to exactly one of these three providers.

However, the Department of Urban Planning has issued a strict aesthetic and technical requirement for every single square block in the grid: each block must be serviced by exactly two different providers, such that two sides of the block are assigned to one provider and the remaining two sides are assigned to a different provider.

How many distinct ways can the entire grid's street segments be assigned to the three providers to satisfy this requirement across all $m \times n$ blocks?
