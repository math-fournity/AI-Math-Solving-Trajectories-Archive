$$W = \{y \in V : g(y) = g(x)\}.$$

Suppose $f: V \to \mathbb{R}$ is a linear functional with $W = \{y \in V : f(y)

**Step 3 ($K$ lies on one side of $W$).**

We claim $g(x) \in \{m, M\}$, i.e., $g(x)$ is an extreme value of $g$ on $K$. Su

$$K \cap \{y \in V : g(y) = g(x)\} = K \cap W$$

- **Case A:** $g(x) = M$, so $g(y) \le g(x)$ for all $y \in K$, or
