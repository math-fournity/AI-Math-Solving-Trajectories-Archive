$$\langle S\phi, S\psi\rangle_1 = \langle \phi, \psi\rangle_2 \quad \text{for al

Thus $S$ is an isometry (inner-product preserving map) from $(V, \langle\cdot,\c

$$S = U_1^{-1} \circ U_2 : (V, \langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_2) \to (V, \langle\cdot
,\cdot\rangle_1).$$
Then $S$ is unitary, hence $\|S\phi\|_1 = \|\phi\|_2$ for all $\phi \in V$.

**Step 4: Boundedness.** The operator $S$ is an isometry from $(V, \|\cdot\|_2)$
