# Problem

In a specialized technology cluster, there are 30 high-tech startups participating in a collaborative evaluation period. Throughout this period, every possible combination of two startups engages in exactly one joint project to determine a performance outcome. For each project, there are three potential results:
1. One startup outperforms the other, earning 3 "Innovation Credits" while the other earns 0.
2. The two startups achieve a technical stalemate, earning 1 "Innovation Credit" each.

After the evaluation period concluded, an audit of the records revealed a specific consistency in the results: if you select any group of three startups from the cluster, at least two of those startups earned the exact same total number of credits from the three specific projects they conducted against each other within that trio.

Based on this structural constraint, what is the minimum possible number of projects that resulted in a technical stalemate across the entire cluster?
