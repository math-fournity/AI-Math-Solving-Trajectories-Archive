**Problem.** For prime $p$, find $S(p)=\sum_{k=1}^{(p-1)(p-2)}\lfloor\sqrt[3]{kp

ples of $p$-multiples in $[n^3,(n+1)^3)$. Since $kp$ is always a multiple of $p$

$$c_n=\#\{k: n^3\le kp\le (n+1)^3-1\}=\Big\lfloor\frac{(n+1)^3-1}{p}\Big\rfloor-
\Big\lfloor\frac{n^3-1}{p}\Big\rfloor.$$

$$S=(p-2)\,h(p-1)-\sum_{m=1}^{p-2}h(m).$$

$$h(p-1)=\Big\lfloor\frac{(p-1)^3-1}{p}\Big\rfloor=\Big\lfloor p^2-3p+3-\tfrac{2
