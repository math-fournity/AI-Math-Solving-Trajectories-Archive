## Setup

BC at B, so center O₁ = (0, r₁). Hub C is tangent at C, so O₂ = (6, r₂). Both ci

**Tangency condition:** |O₁O₂| = r₁ + r₂ gives 36 + (r₁−r₂)² = (r₁+r₂)², so **r₁
r₂ = 9**.

**Tangency point:** T = (6r₁/s, 18/s) where s = r₁+r₂. Computing |T−M|²:
- T_x − 3 = 3(r₁−r₂)/s, T_y = 18/s
- |T−M|² = 9(r₁−r₂)²/s² + 324/s² = 9((r₁−r₂)²+36)/s² = 9s²/s² = **9**

So **T always lies on a circle of radius 3 centered at M**, regardless of r₁, r₂

## Power of a Point

Since T is the external tangency point, O₁, T, O₂ are collinear with TO₁ = r₁, T
O₂ = r₂. The drone circle passes through O₁ and O₂, so line O₁O₂ is a chord with
 T between O₁ and O₂ (T is inside the drone circle).

**Power of T w.r.t. drone circle** = −TO₁·TO₂ = −r₁r₂ = **−9**.

Line AT meets the drone circle at A and F, with T between them (T inside circle)
:
−TA·TF = −9 ⟹ 6·TF = 9 ⟹ **TF = 3/2**.

So F lies on the opposite side of T from A, at distance 3/2 from T. This gives *
*F = T + (TF/TA)(T−A) = T + (1/4)(T−A) = (5T−A)/4**, hence **A = 5T − 4F**.

## Using BF² + CF² = 45/2

With B=(0,0), C=(6,0):
BF² + CF² = 2F_x² − 12F_x + 36 + 2F_y² = 45/2

⟹ (F_x−3)² + F_y² = 9/4, i.e., **|F−M|² = 9/4** (F lies on a circle of radius 3/
2 centered at M).

## Key Deduction: Direction of F from M

Work in M-centered coordinates. We have |T| = 3, |F| = 3/2, and |A−T| = 6 with A
 = 5T − 4F:

|A − T| = |4T − 4F| = 4|T − F| = 6 ⟹ |T − F| = 3/2

|T − F|² = |T|² + |F|² − 2T·F = 9 + 9/4 − 2T·F = 9/4

⟹ 2T·F = 9 ⟹ T·F = 9/2 = 3·(3/2)·cos(θ−φ)

⟹ **cos(θ−φ) = 1**, so T and F point in the **same direction** from M.

Therefore **F = T/2** (same direction, half the distance).

Substituting back: **A = 5T − 4(T/2) = 3T**.

## Computing AB² + AC²

Since A = 3T (in M-centered coords): |A| = 3|T| = 9, so **AM² = 81**.

Using the identity (with M the midpoint of BC):
AB² + AC² = 2·AM² + (BC²/2) = 2·81 + 18 = **180**
