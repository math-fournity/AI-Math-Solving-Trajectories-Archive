## Solution

**Setup.** Place the rectangle in coordinates:
- $A = (0,0)$, $B = (25,0)$ (since $AE+BE = 8+17 = 25$),
- $C = (25, h)$, $D = (0, h)$, where $h$ is the unknown height.

Then $E = (8, 0)$ on $AB$, and $F = (22, h)$ on $CD$ (since $CF = 3$ places $F$
three units from $C$ toward $D$).

**Reflection condition.** Reflecting $B = (25,0)$ across line $EF$ lands at $B'$
 on edge $AD$ (the line $x = 0$), so $B' = (0, y)$ for some $0 \le y \le h$.

Two conditions hold for a reflection across a line:

1. **Midpoint of $BB'$ lies on $EF$.** The midpoint is $M = (12.5,\, y/2)$. Para
metrize $EF$ as $(8+14t,\, ht)$. Then
$$8 + 14t = 12.5 \implies t = \frac{9}{28}, \qquad ht = \frac{y}{2} \implies y =
 \frac{9h}{14}.$$

2. **$BB'$ is perpendicular to $EF$.** The direction of $EF$ is $(14, h)$; the d
irection of $BB'$ is $(-25, y)$. Their dot product is zero:
$$-25\cdot 14 + y\cdot h = 0 \implies yh = 350.$$

**Solve for $h$.** Substituting $y = \frac{9h}{14}$:
$$\frac{9h}{14}\cdot h = 350 \implies h^2 = \frac{4900}{9} \implies h = \frac{70
}{3}.$$

**Verification.** $y = \frac{9}{14}\cdot\frac{70}{3} = 15$, and $15 \le \frac{70
}{3}\approx 23.33$, so $B'$ indeed lies on segment $AD$. Check $yh = 15\cdot\fra
c{70}{3} = 350$ ✓. Midpoint $M = (12.5, 7.5)$ lies on $EF$ at $t = 9/28$ ✓.

**Perimeter.**
$$P = 2\!\left(25 + \frac{70}{3}\right) = 2\cdot\frac{145}{3} = \frac{290}{3}.$$

With $\gcd(290, 3) = 1$, we have $m = 290$, $n = 3$.

$$m + n = \boxed{293}$$
