# Problem

In the heart of a vast desert, three specialized research outposts—Alpha, Beta, and Gamma—form a triangular territory. A central communications hub, Point P, is positioned within this triangle to relay signals between the stations.

The hub's orientation is precisely calibrated such that the angle formed between the transmission line to Beta and the border to Alpha is exactly equal to the angle between the line to Gamma and the border to Beta, which is also equal to the angle between the line to Alpha and the border to Gamma.

Engineers have measured the direct distances from the hub to each station:
- The distance from the hub to Outpost Alpha is 4 kilometers.

- The distance from the hub to Outpost Gamma is 9 kilometers.

Calculate the total area of the triangular territory defined by Alpha, Beta, and Gamma.
