$$f(g(g(x))) = 4\big(g(g(x)) - g(x)\big)$$

Since $f(g(g(x))) = g(g(g(x))) - g(g(x))$, this simplifies to:

$$g(g(g(x))) = 5\,g(g(x)) - 4\,g(x) \qquad (\star)$$
