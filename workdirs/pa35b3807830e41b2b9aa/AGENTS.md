# Problem

In a futuristic city, three giant solar collectors, each shaped like an equilateral triangle with a side length of $x$ meters, are being installed inside a hexagonal power hub shaped like a large equilateral triangle with a side length of 6 meters. 

The three collectors are oriented so that their bases are perfectly parallel to the base of the power hub. They are arranged symmetrically such that each collector has exactly one of its vertices touching a different side of the large power hub's perimeter. Furthermore, the collectors are positioned so that they touch one another: each collector has a second vertex that makes contact with a vertex of one of its neighboring collectors.

Due to this specific arrangement, a gap is formed in the dead center between the three collectors. This central gap is itself a small equilateral triangle with a side length of exactly 1 meter.

Calculate the side length $x$ of the solar collectors. If your answer is expressed as an irreducible fraction $\frac{a}{b}$, find the final value of $a + b$.
