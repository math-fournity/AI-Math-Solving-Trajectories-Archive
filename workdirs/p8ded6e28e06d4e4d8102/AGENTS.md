# Problem

A boutique tech company is designing a high-end minimalist server rack. The base of the rack is a perfectly rectangular metal plate. To cool the components, two circular fans of different sizes are mounted flat onto this base.

The layout is highly specific: both fans are positioned such that they are tangent to each other at a single point. Furthermore, each fan is pushed into a different corner of the rectangular plate so that they are both tangent to two adjacent sides of the rectangle (the edges of the plate).

The larger of the two cooling fans has a radius of exactly 1 decimeter. The smaller fan is sized specifically so that it fits perfectly in the remaining gap while maintaining its tangency to the larger fan and the two sides of the rectangle. 

Calculate the total surface area of the rectangular base plate in square decimeters.
