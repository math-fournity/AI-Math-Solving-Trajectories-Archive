### 1. Branch points

Write $f(z)=\sqrt{z-a}+\sqrt{z-b}$. The square root $\sqrt{z-a}$ has branch poin
ts at $a$ and $\infty$; $\sqrt{z-b}$ at $b$ and $\infty$. To find the branch poi
nts of $f$, examine the monodromy:

- **Around $a$** (small loop): $\sqrt{z-a}$ changes sign, $\sqrt{z-b}$ does not.
 Hence $f\mapsto -\sqrt{z-a}+\sqrt{z-b}\neq f$. So $a$ is a branch point.
- **Around $b$**: $\sqrt{z-b}$ changes sign, $\sqrt{z-a}$ does not. Hence $f\map
sto \sqrt{z-a}-\sqrt{z-b}\neq f$. So $b$ is a branch point.
- **Around $\infty$** (large loop enclosing both): both change sign, $f\mapsto -
f\neq f$. So $\infty$ is a branch point.

Branch points: $\{a,\,b,\,\infty\}$.

### 2. Number of sheets

The two independent sign choices for $(\sqrt{z-a},\sqrt{z-b})$ give four values:
$$f_1=+\sqrt{z-a}+\sqrt{z-b},\quad f_2=+\sqrt{z-a}-\sqrt{z-b},\quad f_3=-\sqrt{z
-a}+\sqrt{z-b},\quad f_4=-\sqrt{z-a}-\sqrt{z-b}.$$

Monodromy:
- around $a$: $f_1\leftrightarrow f_3$, $f_2\leftrightarrow f_4$;
- around $b$: $f_1\leftrightarrow f_2$, $f_3\leftrightarrow f_4$.

All four values are reachable (e.g. $f_1\xrightarrow{a}f_3\xrightarrow{b}f_4$),
so the surface is **4-sheeted**.

*Algebraic confirmation.* Setting $w=f(z)$:
$$w^2=2z-a-b+2\sqrt{(z-a)(z-b)},\qquad (w^2-2z+a+b)^2=4(z-a)(z-b),$$
$$\boxed{\,w^4-2(2z-a-b)\,w^2+(a-b)^2=0\,}.$$
This is a quartic in $w$ (4 sheets). Its discriminant as a quadratic in $w^2$ is
 $16(z-a)(z-b)$, vanishing at $a,b$ — confirming the finite branch points.

### 3. Branch cuts

Assume $0<a<b$ (the case $b<a$ is symmetric). Take two cuts along the real axis:

- **$C_1=(-\infty,\,a]$** — the branch cut of $\sqrt{z-a}$,
- **$C_2=[\,b,\,+\infty)$** — the branch cut of $\sqrt{z-b}$.

The interval $(a,b)$ is left uncut.

### 4. Sheet labels and gluing

Label each sheet by the sign pair $(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2)=\bigl(\operatorname{s
gn}\sqrt{z-a},\;\operatorname{sgn}\sqrt{z-b}\bigr)$:

| Sheet | $(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2)$ | $f(z)$ |
|:---:|:---:|:---|
| I  | $(+,+)$ | $+\sqrt{z-a}+\sqrt{z-b}$ |
| II | $(+,-)$ | $+\sqrt{z-a}-\sqrt{z-b}$ |
| III| $(-,+)$ | $-\sqrt{z-a}+\sqrt{z-b}$ |
| IV | $(-,-)$ | $-\sqrt{z-a}-\sqrt{z-b}$ |

**Crossing $C_1=(-\infty,a]$:** the argument of $z-a$ jumps by $\pm\pi$, so $\ep
silon_1$ flips while $\epsilon_2$ is preserved. The upper edge of $C_1$ on one s
heet is glued to the lower edge on the paired sheet:
$$\text{I}\;\leftrightarrow\;\text{III},\qquad \text{II}\;\leftrightarrow\;\text
{IV}.$$

**Crossing $C_2=[b,+\infty)$:** the argument of $z-b$ jumps by $\pm\pi$, so $\ep
silon_2$ flips while $\epsilon_1$ is preserved:
$$\text{I}\;\leftrightarrow\;\text{II},\qquad \text{III}\;\leftrightarrow\;\text
{IV}.$$

### 5. Monodromy verification

| Loop | Cuts crossed | Effect | Sheet map |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---|
| around $a$ | $C_1$ once | flip $\epsilon_1$ | I↔III, II↔IV |
| around $b$ | $C_2$ once | flip $\epsilon_2$ | I↔II, III↔IV |
| around $\infty$ | $C_1$ and $C_2$ | flip both | I↔IV, II↔III |

All three monodromies are products of two disjoint transpositions, consistent wi
th each branch point joining the four sheets in two pairs.

### 6. Analyticity on the surface

On each sheet, $f$ is a sum of two functions analytic away from their respective
 cuts; the cross-cut gluing makes $f$ single-valued and analytic on the whole su
rface. At the ramification points:

- **$z=a$ and $z=b$:** simple branch points (ramification index 2). The function
 $f$ takes finite values there ($f(a)=\pm\sqrt{a-b}$, $f(b)=\pm\sqrt{b-a}$), but
 the projection to the $z$-plane is ramified — these are exactly the points the
problem excludes.
- **$z=\infty$:** $f\sim 2\sqrt{z}\to\infty$. Using the local coordinate $w=z^{-
1/2}$ on each ramified pair, $f\sim 2/w$, a simple pole. The surface is ramified
 here as well.

### 7. Genus (Riemann–Hurwitz)

4-sheeted cover $\Sigma\to\mathbb{P}^1$, each of the three branch points contrib
uting ramination $R_i=2$ (two simple ramification points per branch point):
$$2g-2=4(2\cdot0-2)+\sum R_i=-8+6=-2\;\Longrightarrow\;g=0.$$

The surface is a sphere.

### Summary

The Riemann surface of $f(z)=\sqrt{z-a}+\sqrt{z-b}$ is a **4-sheeted, genus-0 co
ver** of the Riemann sphere, branched at $z=a$, $z=b$, and $z=\infty$. With the
branch cuts $(-\infty,a]$ and $[b,+\infty)$ on the real axis (for $a<b$), the fo
ur sheets — labeled by the signs of $\sqrt{z-a}$ and $\sqrt{z-b}$ — are glued as
 follows:

- crossing $(-\infty,a]$ flips $\operatorname{sgn}\sqrt{z-a}$: **I↔III, II↔IV**;
- crossing $[b,+\infty)$ flips $\operatorname{sgn}\sqrt{z-b}$: **I↔II, III↔IV**.

The function $f$ is single-valued and analytic on this surface except at the bra
nch points $z=a$ and $z=b$ (and has simple poles at the two points above $\infty
$).
