## Solution

**Setup.** The integrity is $A(S)=\sum_{(\mathbf s,\mathbf s')\in S\times S} w(\
mathbf s-\mathbf s')$, where $w(a,b)=0$ whenever $|a|>2$ or $|b|>2$. The table i
s fully symmetric: $w(a,b)=w(-a,-b)=w(|a|,|b|)$, so I only need the absolute-dis
placement values:

| | b=0 | b=1 | b=2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| a=0 | 12 | -4 | 2 |
| a=1 | -4 | 4 | -2 |
| a=2 | 2 | -2 | -1 |

---

**Computing $A(S_1)$.** $S_1=\{(0,0),(1,0),(0,1)\}$. Each diagonal pair contribu
tes $w(0,0)=12$, giving $3\cdot 12=36$.

The six off-diagonal ordered pairs (grouped by unordered pair, each pair counted
 twice since $w(a,b)=w(-a,-b)$):

- $(0,0)\leftrightarrow(1,0)$: displacement $(\pm1,0)$, $w=-4$, contribution $2(
-4)=-8$.
- $(0,0)\leftrightarrow(0,1)$: displacement $(0,\pm1)$, $w=-4$, contribution $-8
$.
- $(1,0)\leftrightarrow(0,1)$: displacement $(\pm1,\mp1)$, $w(1,-1)=4$, contribu
tion $2\cdot4=8$.

Off-diagonal total: $-8-8+8=-8$.

$$A(S_1)=36-8=\boxed{28}.$$

**Computing $A(S_2)$.** $S_2=\{(0,1),(0,2),(2,0),(3,1)\}$. Diagonal: $4\cdot 12=
48$.

The six unordered pairs (each counted twice):

- $(0,1)\leftrightarrow(0,2)$: displacement $(0,\pm1)$, $w=-4$, contribution $-8
$.

tion $8$.

Off-diagonal total: $-8-4+0-2+0+8=-6$.

$$A(S_2)=48-6=\boxed{42}.$$

---

**Product.**

$$A(S_1)\cdot A(S_2)=28\cdot 42=1176.$$
