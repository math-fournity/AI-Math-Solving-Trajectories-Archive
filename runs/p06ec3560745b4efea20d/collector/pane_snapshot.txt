## Solution

**Setup.** Place the equilateral triangle in coordinates: $B = (0,0)$, $C = (12,
0)$, $A = (6, 6\sqrt{3})$. The fold sends $A$ to the point $A' = (9,0)$ on $\ove
rline{BC}$ (distance 9 from $B$).

**The fold line is the perpendicular bisector of $\overline{AA'}$.**

- Midpoint of $AA'$: $\left(\frac{6+9}{2}, \frac{6\sqrt{3}+0}{2}\right) = \left(
\frac{15}{2}, 3\sqrt{3}\right)$.
- Direction of $AA'$: $(3, -6\sqrt{3})$, so the perpendicular bisector has direc
tion $(6\sqrt{3}, 3)$.

Parametrize the fold line: $P(t) = \left(\frac{15}{2} + 6\sqrt{3}\,t,\; 3\sqrt{3
} + 3t\right)$.

**Intersection with $\overline{AB}$** (parametrized as $(6s, 6\sqrt{3}\,s)$):

$$\frac{15}{2} + 6\sqrt{3}\,t = 6s, \qquad 3\sqrt{3} + 3t = 6\sqrt{3}\,s.$$

From the second equation $s = \frac{\sqrt{3}+t}{2\sqrt{3}}$. Substituting:

$$\frac{15}{2} + 6\sqrt{3}\,t = 3 + \sqrt{3}\,t \implies \frac{9}{2} = -5\sqrt{3
}\,t \implies t = -\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{10}.$$

Then $s = \frac{7}{20}$, giving $M = \left(\frac{21}{10},\, \frac{21\sqrt{3}}{10
}\right)$.

**Intersection with $\overline{AC}$** (parametrized as $(6+6u,\, 6\sqrt{3}-6\sqr
t{3}\,u)$):

$$\frac{15}{2} + 6\sqrt{3}\,t = 6 + 6u, \qquad 3\sqrt{3} + 3t = 6\sqrt{3} - 6\sq
rt{3}\,u.$$

From the second equation $u = \frac{\sqrt{3}-t}{2\sqrt{3}}$. Substituting:

$$\frac{15}{2} + 6\sqrt{3}\,t = 9 - \sqrt{3}\,t \implies \frac{3}{2} = 7\sqrt{3}
\,t \implies t = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{14}.$$

Then $u = \frac{13}{28}$, giving $N = \left(\frac{123}{14},\, \frac{45\sqrt{3}}{
14}\right)$.

**Computing $|MN|$:**

$$\Delta x = \frac{123}{14} - \frac{21}{10} = \frac{234}{35}, \qquad \Delta y =
\frac{45\sqrt{3}}{14} - \frac{21\sqrt{3}}{10} = \frac{39\sqrt{3}}{35}.$$

$$|MN|^2 = \frac{234^2 + 39^2 \cdot 3}{35^2} = \frac{54756 + 4563}{1225} = \frac
{59319}{1225}.$$

Factoring: $59319 = 27 \cdot 2197 = 3^3 \cdot 13^3 = 39^3$, and $1225 = 35^2$. T
herefore:

$$|MN| = \frac{\sqrt{39^3}}{35} = \frac{39\sqrt{39}}{35}.$$

This has the form $\frac{m\sqrt{p}}{n}$ with $m = 39$, $p = 39$, $n = 35$. We ve

$$m + n + p = 39 + 35 + 39 = \boxed{113}.$$
