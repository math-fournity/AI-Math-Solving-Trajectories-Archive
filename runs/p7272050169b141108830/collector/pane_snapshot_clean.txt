## Solution

We evaluate $I = \displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{x^2 \cos x}{(x^2+1)^2
}\,dx$ by the method of residues.

**Setup.** Since $\cos x = \operatorname{Re}(e^{ix})$, set
$$f(z)=\frac{z^2 e^{iz}}{(z^2+1)^2}=\frac{z^2 e^{iz}}{(z-i)^2(z+i)^2}.$$
We integrate $f$ over the closed contour $C_R$ consisting of $[-R,R]$ on the rea
l axis followed by the upper semicircle $\Gamma_R$ of radius $R$, and let $R\to\
infty$.

**Jordan estimate.** On $\Gamma_R$, $|e^{iz}|=e^{-\operatorname{Im}z}\le 1$ and
$\left|\frac{z^2}{(z^2+1)^2}\right|\sim \frac{1}{R^2}$, with arc length $\pi R$,
 so
$$\left|\int_{\Gamma_R} f(z)\,dz\right|\le \frac{\pi R\cdot R^2}{(R^2-1)^2}\to 0
\quad\text{as }R\to\infty.$$
Hence $\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{x^2 e^{ix}}{(x^2+1)^2}\,dx=\lim
_{R\to\infty}\oint_{C_R}f(z)\,dz$.

**Poles.** $f$ has double poles at $z=\pm i$; only $z=i$ lies inside $C_R$ (for
$R>1$).

**Residue at $z=i$ (double pole).** Write $f(z)=\frac{g(z)}{(z-i)^2}$ with $g(z)
=\dfrac{z^2 e^{iz}}{(z+i)^2}$. The residue is $g'(i)$. Using logarithmic differe
ntiation,
$$\frac{g'(z)}{g(z)}=\frac{2}{z}+i-\frac{2}{z+i}.$$
At $z=i$:
$$g(i)=\frac{i^2\,e^{i\cdot i}}{(2i)^2}=\frac{(-1)e^{-1}}{-4}=\frac{1}{4e},$$
$$\frac{g'}{g}\bigg|_{z=i}=\frac{2}{i}+i-\frac{2}{2i}=-2i+i-(-i)=-2i+i+i=0.$$
Therefore
$$\operatorname{Res}_{z=i}f(z)=g'(i)=\frac{1}{4e}\cdot 0=0.$$

**Applying the residue theorem.**
$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{x^2 e^{ix}}{(x^2+1)^2}\,dx=2\pi i\cdot\operatorna
me{Res}_{z=i}f(z)=2\pi i\cdot 0=0.$$

Taking real parts,
$$I=\operatorname{Re}\!\left(\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{x^2 e^{ix}}{(x^2+1)^2}
\,dx\right)=\operatorname{Re}(0)=0.$$

**Verification (independent).** Using the algebraic identity
$$\frac{x^2}{(x^2+1)^2}=\frac{1}{x^2+1}-\frac{1}{(x^2+1)^2},$$
and the standard residue results
$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{\cos x}{x^2+1}\,dx=\frac{\pi}{e},\qquad \int_{-\i
nfty}^{\infty}\frac{\cos x}{(x^2+1)^2}\,dx=\frac{\pi}{e},$$
we get $I=\dfrac{\pi}{e}-\dfrac{\pi}{e}=0$, confirming the residue computation.

$$\boxed{\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{x^2 \cos x}{(x^2+1)^2}\,dx=0}
$$
