- $L$ = foot of perpendicular from $B$ to line $CA$ (the $x$-axis): $L = (3360\s
qrt{2},\, 0)$.

$$P = (3360\sqrt{2},\; -3400\sqrt{2}).$$

$$H' = (2660 - 7460,\; 1680 - 1680) = (-4800,\; 0).$$
