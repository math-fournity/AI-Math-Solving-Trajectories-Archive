## Isosceles cases $f(n,n)$

lfloor u\rfloor$ (for non-integer $u$), so
$$g(n,n)=(-1)^{n-1}\int_0^n (-1)^{\lfloor u\rfloor}A(u)\,du = (-1)^{n-1}\cdot\tf
rac12 A(n)^2,$$
since $\frac{d}{du}\bigl[\tfrac12 A(u)^2\bigr]=A(u)\,A'(u)=A(u)(-1)^{\lfloor u\r
floor}$. As $A(n)=0$ ($n$ even) or $1$ ($n$ odd):
$$f(n,n)=\tfrac12\,(n\bmod 2).$$
Therefore $f(21,21)=\tfrac12$ and $f(20,20)=0$.
