## Points F and G

$$F = (b-c:a:a) \text{ in trilinear}, \quad\text{barycentric } (b-c:b:c),\text{

**G** (bisector of B: $x=z$): Substituting $x=z$ gives $(c-a)(s-b)z + b(s-b)y=0$

$$G = (b:a-c:b) \text{ in trilinear}, \quad\text{barycentric } (a:a-c:c),\text{

## Distance Formulas

## Solving the System

From $FG^2 = GA^2 = 36$:

So $a=2,\; b=c=9,\; s=10$.
