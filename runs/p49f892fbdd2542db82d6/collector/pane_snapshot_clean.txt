**Claim:** For every integer $n \geq 2$, we have $a > b$.

**Step 1: Locate $a$ and $b$.**

From $a^n = a+1$ with $a > 0$: since $a^n > a$, we get $a^{n-1} > 1$, hence $a >
 1$. The function $g(x) = x^n - x - 1$ is strictly increasing for $x \geq 1$ (as
 $g'(x) = nx^{n-1}-1 > 0$ there), with $g(1) = -1 < 0$ and $g(x) \to \infty$, so
 $a$ is the unique root, with $a > 1$.

From $b^{2n} = b + 3a$ with $b > 0$: if $0 < b \leq 1$, then $b^{2n} \leq b \leq
 1 < 3 < 3a < b+3a$, contradicting $b^{2n} = b+3a$. Hence $b > 1$. The function
$h(x) = x^{2n} - x - 3a$ is strictly increasing for $x \geq 1$ (since $h'(x) = 2
n\,x^{2n-1}-1 > 0$), so $b$ is the unique root with $b > 1$.

**Step 2: Reduce to comparing $f(a)$ and $f(b)$ where $f(x) = x^{2n} - x$.**

The function $f(x) = x^{2n} - x$ has $f'(x) = 2n\,x^{2n-1} - 1 > 0$ for all $x \
geq 1$ (since $2n \geq 4$ and $x^{2n-1} \geq 1$). Thus $f$ is **strictly increas
ing** on $[1, \infty)$.

Since both $a, b > 1$, comparing $a$ and $b$ is equivalent to comparing $f(a)$ a
nd $f(b)$.

**Step 3: Compute $f(a)$ and $f(b)$.**

$$f(b) = b^{2n} - b = 3a.$$

$$f(a) = a^{2n} - a = (a^n)^2 - a = (a+1)^2 - a = a^2 + 2a + 1 - a = a^2 + a + 1
.$$

**Step 4: Compare.**

$$f(a) - f(b) = (a^2 + a + 1) - 3a = a^2 - 2a + 1 = (a-1)^2.$$

Since $a > 1$, we have $(a-1)^2 > 0$, so $f(a) > f(b)$.

By strict monotonicity of $f$ on $[1,\infty)$ and $a, b > 1$, this gives $a > b$
.

**Conclusion:** For every integer $n \geq 2$, $\boxed{a > b}$.
