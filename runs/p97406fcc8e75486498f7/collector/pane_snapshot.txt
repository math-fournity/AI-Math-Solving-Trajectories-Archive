## Solution

**Setup.** Let the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ intersect at point $P$. The diagonal
$AC$ splits the angle at $C$ into $\angle BCA = 40°$ and $\angle ACD = \alpha$;
the diagonal $BD$ splits the angle at $D$ into $\angle BDA = 20°$ and $\angle BD
C = 25°$; the diagonal $AC$ splits the angle at $A$ into $\angle BAC = 50°$ and
$\angle CAD = \beta$; the diagonal $BD$ splits the angle at $B$ into $\angle ABD
 = x$ and $\angle DBC = 90° - x$ (since $\angle BAC + \angle BCA = 90°$ gives $\
angle ABC = 90°$).

**Relating the angles via opposite angles at $P$.** Since $\angle APB = \angle D
PC$ (vertical angles):

$$180° - 50° - x = 180° - 25° - \alpha \implies \alpha = 25° + x.$$

Since $\angle BPC = \angle DPA$:

$$180° - 40° - (90° - x) = 180° - 20° - \beta \implies \beta = 20° + (90° - x) =
 110° - x.$$

**Applying the sine rule around $P$.** From the four triangles $ABP$, $BCP$, $CD
P$, $DAP$:

$$\frac{a}{b} = \frac{\sin x}{\sin 50°},\quad \frac{b}{c} = \frac{\sin 40°}{\sin
(90°-x)},\quad \frac{c}{d} = \frac{\sin 25°}{\sin\alpha},\quad \frac{d}{a} = \fr
ac{\sin\beta}{\sin 20°}.$$

Multiplying all four (the product equals 1) and substituting $\alpha = 25° + x$,
 $\beta = 110° - x$, $\sin(90°-x) = \cos x$:

$$\frac{\sin x \cdot \sin 40° \cdot \sin 25° \cdot \sin(110°-x)}{\sin 50° \cdot
\cos x \cdot \sin(25°+x) \cdot \sin 20°} = 1.$$

Using $\sin 40°/\sin 50° = \tan 40°$, this becomes:

$$\tan 40° \cdot \tan x \cdot \frac{\sin 25° \cdot \sin(110°-x)}{\sin(25°+x) \cd
ot \sin 20°} = 1.$$

**Testing $x = 45°$.**

**LHS:** $\tan 40° \cdot \tan 45° \cdot \sin 25° \cdot \sin 65° = \tan 40° \cdot
 \sin 25° \cdot \cos 25° = \tan 40° \cdot \tfrac{1}{2}\sin 50° = \frac{\sin 40°}
{\cos 40°}\cdot\tfrac{1}{2}\cos 40° = \tfrac{1}{2}\sin 40°.$

**RHS:** $\sin 70° \cdot \sin 20° = \cos 20° \cdot \sin 20° = \tfrac{1}{2}\sin 4
0°.$

$$\text{LHS} = \tfrac{1}{2}\sin 40° = \text{RHS}. \quad\checkmark$$

So $x = \angle ABD = 45°$.

**Computing the crossing angle.**

$$\angle APB = 180° - 50° - 45° = 85°, \qquad \angle BPC = 180° - 85° = 95°.$$

**Verification.** $\alpha = 70°$, $\beta = 65°$. Angles of the quadrilateral: $\
angle A = 115°$, $\angle B = 90°$, $\angle C = 110°$, $\angle D = 45°$; sum $= 3
60°$ ✓, all $< 180°$ (convex) ✓. Cross-checks: $\angle DPC = 180° - 25° - 70° =
85° = \angle APB$ ✓; $\angle DPA = 180° - 20° - 65° = 95° = \angle BPC$ ✓.

The obtuse angle formed by the intersection of the two cables is $\boxed{95°}$.
