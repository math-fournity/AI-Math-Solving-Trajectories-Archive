## Solution

Let drone 1 start at time $X$ and drone 2 start at time $Y$, where $X, Y$ are in
dependent and uniformly distributed on $[0, 60]$ (measuring minutes after 9:00).
 Each drone is active for exactly $m$ minutes, so drone 1 is active on $[X, X+m]
$ and drone 2 on $[Y, Y+m]$.

**Overlap condition.** The two active periods overlap if and only if at least on
e drone begins while the other is still active. The second drone to begin starts
 while the first is still active precisely when $|X - Y| \le m$ (the case $X = Y
$ is included, as both begin simultaneously while the other is active). Thus

$$P(\text{overlap}) = P(|X - Y| \le m).$$

**Computing the probability.** In the unit square $[0,60]^2$, the region $|x - y
| > m$ consists of two right triangles, each with legs of length $60 - m$. Their
 total area is $(60 - m)^2$, so

$$P(|X - Y| > m) = \frac{(60 - m)^2}{60^2} = \left(1 - \frac{m}{60}\right)^2,$$

and therefore

$$P(|X - Y| \le m) = 1 - \left(1 - \frac{m}{60}\right)^2.$$

**Solving for $m$.** Setting this equal to $0.40$:

$$1 - \left(1 - \frac{m}{60}\right)^2 = 0.40 \implies \left(1 - \frac{m}{60}\rig
ht)^2 = 0.60.$$

Since $m \le 60$, we take the positive root:

$$1 - \frac{m}{60} = \sqrt{0.6} = \sqrt{\frac{3}{5}} = \frac{\sqrt{15}}{5},$$

$$\frac{m}{60} = 1 - \frac{\sqrt{15}}{5}, \qquad m = 60 - \frac{60\sqrt{15}}{5}
= 60 - 12\sqrt{15}.$$

**Verification.** $\sqrt{15} \approx 3.873$, so $m \approx 60 - 46.48 = 13.52$ m
inutes, which lies in $[0, 60]$. Checking: $\left(1 - \frac{13.52}{60}\right)^2
\approx (0.7747)^2 \approx 0.600$, giving $P \approx 0.400$. ✓

**Extracting $a, b, c$.** We have $m = 60 - 12\sqrt{15}$, so $a = 60$, $b = 12$,
 $c = 15$. Since $15 = 3 \cdot 5$ is square-free, the form is valid.

$$a + b + c = 60 + 12 + 15 = \boxed{87}.$$
