**Step 1: Relate the sum to a difference of powers.**

$$(1+\sqrt{5})^{100} = \sum_{j=0}^{100}\binom{100}{j}(\sqrt{5})^j, \qquad (1-\sq

$$(1+\sqrt{5})^{100}-(1-\sqrt{5})^{100} = 2\sum_{\substack{0\le j\le 100\\ j\tex
