## Solution

**Reformulating the formula.** The "Optimized" rating is

$$n = \lceil x/2\rceil + y + xy, \qquad x,y \in \mathbb{Z}_{>0}.$$

Write $x$ in terms of $k = \lceil x/2\rceil \ge 1$:

- If $x = 2k-1$ (odd): $\lceil x/2\rceil = k$, so $n = k + y + (2k-1)y = k + 2ky
 = k(2y+1)$.
- If $x = 2k$ (even): $\lceil x/2\rceil = k$, so $n = k + y + 2ky = k(1+2y) = k(
2y+1)$.

In both cases $n = k(2y+1)$ with $k\ge 1$ and $y\ge 1$, i.e. $m := 2y+1$ is an *
*odd integer $\ge 3$**.

**Characterization.** $n$ is Optimized $\iff$ $n = k\cdot m$ for some $k\ge 1$ a
nd odd $m\ge 3$ $\iff$ $n$ has an odd divisor $\ge 3$.

An integer $n$ has **no** odd divisor $\ge 3$ exactly when its odd part is $1$,
i.e. when $n$ is a **power of $2$** (including $1 = 2^0$). Hence:

$$n \text{ is Optimized} \iff n \text{ is not a power of } 2.$$

**Computing the sum.** The sum of all positive integers $\le 2015$ is

$$\frac{2015\cdot 2016}{2} = 2015 \cdot 1008 = 2\,031\,120.$$

The powers of $2$ not exceeding $2015$ are $1,2,4,\dots,1024$ (since $2048>2015$
). Their sum is

$$1+2+4+\cdots+1024 = 2^{11}-1 = 2047.$$

These are exactly the non-Optimized numbers, so we subtract them:

$$2\,031\,120 - 2\,047 = 2\,029\,073.$$

**Verification of the characterization.** Quick checks:
- $n=3$: $x=1,y=1 \Rightarrow 1+1+1=3$. ✓ (odd part $3\ge 3$)
- $n=4=2^2$: only odd divisor is $1<3$, not Optimized. ✓
- $n=6=2\cdot 3$: odd divisor $3$, Optimized; $x=2,y=1\Rightarrow 1+1+2=4$? No—t
ry $x=1,y=2$: $1+2+2=5$. Try $k=2,m=3$: $x=3,y=1$: $\lceil 3/2\rceil+1+3=2+1+3=6
$. ✓

**Answer:**

$$\boxed{2\,029\,073}$$
