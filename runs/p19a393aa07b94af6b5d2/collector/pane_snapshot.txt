## Solution

**Setup.** A disc centered at $(a, a^2 - \tfrac{3}{4})$ on the curve $y = x^2 -
\tfrac{3}{4}$, with radius $r$, is completely contained in $\{(x,y): y < 0\}$ if
 and only if its topmost point satisfies $a^2 - \tfrac{3}{4} + r < 0$, i.e., $r
< \tfrac{3}{4} - a^2$. This requires $a^2 < \tfrac{3}{4}$, so $a \in (-\tfrac{\s
qrt{3}}{2}, \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2})$.

Since we may take $r$ arbitrarily close to $\tfrac{3}{4} - a^2$, the union (for
area purposes) equals the union over all $a \in (-\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, \tfrac{\s
qrt{3}}{2})$ of discs centered at $(a,\, a^2-\tfrac{3}{4})$ with radius $R = \tf
rac{3}{4} - a^2$.

**Characterizing the union.** Setting $c = a^2 - \tfrac{3}{4}$ (so $R = -c > 0$)
, a point $(x,y)$ lies in the disc for center $a$ iff
$$(x-a)^2 + (y - c)^2 < c^2 \iff (x-a)^2 + y^2 < 2yc.$$
Substituting $c = a^2 - \tfrac{3}{4}$:
$$f(a) := (1-2y)\,a^2 - 2xa + x^2 + y^2 + \tfrac{3}{2}y < 0.$$

Since $y < 0$, the coefficient $1 - 2y > 0$, so $f$ is an upward parabola in $a$
, minimized at $a^* = \frac{x}{1-2y}$. The minimum value is:
$$f(a^*) = \frac{y\bigl(-2x^2 - 2y^2 - 2y + \tfrac{3}{2}\bigr)}{1-2y}.$$

Since $y < 0$ and $1-2y > 0$, we have $f(a^*) < 0$ iff $-2x^2 - 2y^2 - 2y + \tfr
ac{3}{2} > 0$, i.e.,
$$x^2 + y^2 + y < \tfrac{3}{4} \iff x^2 + \bigl(y + \tfrac{1}{2}\bigr)^2 < 1.$$

**Checking the constraint $a^* \in (-\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2})$.
** If $a^*$ lies outside this interval, say $a^* \geq \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, then
 $f$ is decreasing on the interval and $\inf f = f(\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}) = (x - \
tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2})^2 + y^2 > 0$ (since $y < 0$), so no valid $a$ exists. One ve
rifies that $x^2 + y^2 + y < \tfrac{3}{4}$ automatically implies $|x| < \tfrac{\
sqrt{3}}{2}(1-2y)$ (i.e., $a^*$ is in range), since
$$\tfrac{3}{4}(1-2y)^2 - \bigl(\tfrac{3}{4} - y^2 - y\bigr) = 2y(2y-1) > 0 \quad
 \text{for } y \in (-1,0),$$
and the case $y \leq -1$ is immediate. So the constraint is redundant.

**The union** is therefore:
$$\left\{(x,y) : y < 0,\; x^2 + \bigl(y + \tfrac{1}{2}\bigr)^2 < 1\right\},$$
the portion of the open unit disc centered at $(0, -\tfrac{1}{2})$ lying below t
he $x$-axis.

**Computing the area.** The disc $x^2 + (y+\tfrac{1}{2})^2 < 1$ has center $(0,-
\tfrac{1}{2})$ and radius $1$. The line $y = 0$ is at distance $d = \tfrac{1}{2}
$ from the center. The area of the circular cap above $y = 0$ is:
$$A_{\text{cap}} = r^2 \arccos\!\left(\tfrac{d}{r}\right) - d\sqrt{r^2 - d^2} =
\arccos\!\left(\tfrac{1}{2}\right) - \tfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{\tfrac{3}{4}} = \frac{\pi
}{3} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}.$$

The total disc area is $\pi$, so the area below $y = 0$ is:
$$\pi - \left(\frac{\pi}{3} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\right) = \frac{2\pi}{3} + \frac
{\sqrt{3}}{4}.$$

The area of the union of all elements of $S$ is $\boxed{\dfrac{2\pi}{3} + \dfrac
{\sqrt{3}}{4}}$.
