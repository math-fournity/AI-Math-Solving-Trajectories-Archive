Looking at this problem, I need to find the number of green cells in a 7×8 table
 with the given row and column color constraints.

## Proof

**Notation.** For row $i$, let $r_i, y_i, g_i$ be the counts of red, yellow, gre
en cells. For column $j$, let $R_j, Y_j, G_j$ be the counts. Let $R, Y, G$ be th
e total counts. We have $R + Y + G = 56$.

**Step 1: Establish $R = Y$.**

Row constraints give $r_i \ge y_i$ for all $i$. Summing: $R \ge Y$.
Column constraints give $Y_j \ge R_j$ for all $j$. Summing: $Y \ge R$.
Therefore $R = Y$, and $G = 56 - 2R$.

**Step 2: Bound $R$ from above using column constraints.**

In each column, $Y_j \ge R_j$ and $R_j + Y_j + G_j = 7$ with $G_j \ge 0$, so $R_
j + Y_j \le 7$ and $2R_j \le R_j + Y_j \le 7$, giving $R_j \le 3$.
Summing over 8 columns: $R \le 24$.

**Step 3: Bound $Y$ from below using column constraints.**

In each column, $Y_j \ge R_j$ and $Y_j \ge G_j$, so $2Y_j \ge R_j + G_j = 7 - Y_
j$, giving $3Y_j \ge 7$, hence $Y_j \ge 3$.
Summing over 8 columns: $Y \ge 24$.

**Step 4: Conclude $R = Y = 24$ and $G = 8$.**

Since $R = Y$, $R \le 24$, and $Y \ge 24$, we get $R = Y = 24$, and therefore:
$$G = 56 - 2 \times 24 = 8.$$

**Step 5: Verify feasibility by constructing such a table.**

We need 3 rows with $(r,y,g) = (4,4,0)$ and 4 rows with $(3,3,2)$ (since $r_i =
y_i$ in every row — because $\sum(r_i - y_i) = 0$ with each term $\ge 0$ — givin
g $2r_i + g_i = 8$, so $r_i \in \{3,4\}$, $g_i \in \{2,0\}$; solving $4k + 3(7-k
) = 24$ gives $k=3$). Each column has $R_j = 3$, $G_j = 1$, $Y_j = 3$.

| 2 | R | Y | Y | Y | Y | R | R | R |

| 5 | R | R | G | G | Y | R | Y | Y |
| 6 | Y | Y | R | R | G | G | R | Y |
