Looking at this problem, I need to find all possible areas of a rectangle (with
sides parallel to the 20×20 square, vertices at grid points) such that exactly 2

**Setup.** Place the 20×20 square on a coordinate grid with unit square vertices

\in [a,b]$, $y \in [c,d]$ where $a,b,c,d$ are integers with $0 \le a < b \le 20$
, $0 \le c < d \le 20$. Let $w = b-a$ (width), $h = d-c$ (height), so area $= wh
$.

**Counting unit squares touching the boundary.** A unit square $[i,i{+}1]\times[
j,j{+}1]$ intersects the closed rectangle $[a,b]\times[c,d]$ iff $[i,i{+}1]\cap[
a,b]\neq\emptyset$ and $[j,j{+}1]\cap[c,d]\neq\emptyset$, i.e., $i \in \{\max(0,
a{-}1),\ldots,\min(19,b)\}$ and $j \in \{\max(0,c{-}1),\ldots,\min(19,d)\}$.

Define $\alpha = \mathbf{1}[a{=}0]+\mathbf{1}[b{=}20]$ (edges touched in $x$) an
d $\beta = \mathbf{1}[c{=}0]+\mathbf{1}[d{=}20]$ (edges touched in $y$). Then th
e count of intersecting squares is $N_x \cdot N_y$ where $N_x = w+2-\alpha$, $N_
y = h+2-\beta$.

A unit square lies **strictly interior** to $(a,b)\times(c,d)$ iff $i \in \{a{+}
1,\ldots,b{-}2\}$, $j \in \{c{+}1,\ldots,d{-}2\}$, giving $\max(0,w{-}2)\cdot\ma
x(0,h{-}2)$ squares. These are exactly the intersecting squares that do **not**
touch the boundary.

Therefore:
$$\text{Count} = (w+2-\alpha)(h+2-\beta) - \max(0,w-2)\max(0,h-2) = 24.$$
