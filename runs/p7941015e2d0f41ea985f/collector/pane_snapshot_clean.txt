## Solution

**Setting up the equation.** The equilibrium equation is:

$$\frac{\log(px)}{\log(x+1)} = 2, \quad x > 0$$

**Domain requirements:** $\log(px)$ requires $px > 0$; $\log(x+1)$ requires $x+1
 > 0$ (satisfied since $x>0$) and $\log(x+1)\neq 0$ (satisfied since $x>0$ means
 $x+1>1$).

**Reducing to a quadratic.** From $\log(px) = 2\log(x+1) = \log((x+1)^2)$, we ge
t:

$$px = (x+1)^2 \implies x^2 + (2-p)x + 1 = 0$$

**Discriminant analysis.** $\Delta = (2-p)^2 - 4 = p(p-4)$.

By Vieta's formulas: product of roots $= 1 > 0$ (same sign), sum of roots $= p-2
$.

**Case $\Delta = 0$:** $p = 0$ (excluded) or $p = 4$. At $p=4$: $(x-1)^2=0 \Righ
tarrow x=1$. Check: $x=1>0$ ✓, $px=4>0$ ✓. Verify: $\frac{\log 4}{\log 2} = 2$ ✓
. **Exactly one solution.**

**Case $\Delta > 0$ ($p < 0$ or $p > 4$):** Two distinct real roots.
- **$p > 4$:** Sum $= p-2 > 0$, product $= 1 > 0$ → both roots positive. Since $
p>0$, $px>0$ for both. **Two solutions** (not exactly one).
- **$p < 0$:** Sum $= p-2 < 0$, product $= 1 > 0$ → both roots negative. Fails $
x > 0$. **Zero solutions.** (Also, $px > 0$ with $p<0$ requires $x<0$, contradic
ting $x>0$ directly.)

**Case $\Delta < 0$ ($0 < p < 4$):** No real roots. **Zero solutions.**

**Step 1 — Positive integers $p \in [1, 10]$:**

| $p$ | $\Delta = p(p-4)$ | Solutions | Exactly one? |
|-----|-------------------|-----------|--------------|
| 1 | $-3 < 0$ | 0 | No |
| 2 | $-4 < 0$ | 0 | No |
| 3 | $-3 < 0$ | 0 | No |

**Step 1 sum = 4.**

**Step 2 — Negative integers $p \in [-10, -1]$:** For every negative $p$, $px >
0$ requires $x < 0$, which contradicts $x > 0$. No solutions exist for any negat

**Step 2 count = 0.**
