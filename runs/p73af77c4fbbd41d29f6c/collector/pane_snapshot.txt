Consider $Y_n = \dfrac{S_n}{\mathbb{E}(S_n)} = \dfrac{S_n}{\Lambda_n}$. Then
$$\mathbb{E}(Y_n) = \frac{\Lambda_n}{\Lambda_n} = 1, \qquad \operatorname{Var}(Y

$$\mathbb{P}\!\left(\left|Y_n - 1\right| > \varepsilon\right) \le \frac{\operato

$$\frac{S_n}{\mathbb{E}(S_n)} \xrightarrow{\mathbb{P}} 1.$$

The normalized sum converges in probability to $1$.
