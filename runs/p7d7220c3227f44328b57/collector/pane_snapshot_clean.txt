The incenter of a right triangle with right angle at O lies on the angle bisecto
r. Setting u = A/p, v = B/q (unit vectors along the legs), since u ⊥ v, the ince

**I = r(u + v)**

where r = (p + q − h)/2 is the inradius (derived from: incenter is at distance r

So r²·2 = 2015²·50, giving **r = 2015·5 = 10075**.
