## Proof

**Construction.** Let $n = 5m$ for a positive integer $m$ (since $n \equiv 0 \pm
od{5}$). We construct a graph $G$ on $n$ vertices as follows.

Partition the vertex set into five parts $V_0, V_1, V_2, V_3, V_4$, each of size
 $m$. Place no edges within any part (each $V_i$ is an independent set). For eac
h $i \in \mathbb{Z}_5$, add all possible edges between $V_i$ and $V_{i+1 \pmod{5
}}$. This is the complete blow-up of the 5-cycle $C_5$.

We verify the three required properties.

---

**Minimum degree $\delta = \frac{2n}{5}$.** Every vertex $v \in V_i$ is adjacent
 to exactly the vertices in $V_{i-1}$ and $V_{i+1}$ (indices mod 5), and to no o
thers. Thus
$$\deg(v) = |V_{i-1}| + |V_{i+1}| = m + m = 2m = \frac{2 \cdot 5m}{5} = \frac{2n
}{5}.$$
Since this holds for every vertex, $\delta(G) = \frac{2n}{5}$.

---

**Triangle-free.** Suppose for contradiction that $u, v, w$ form a triangle. Sin
ce each $V_i$ is independent, no two of these vertices lie in the same part, so
they lie in three distinct parts $V_a, V_b, V_c$. The edge $uv$ requires $b \equ
iv a \pm 1 \pmod{5}$; the edge $vw$ requires $c \equiv b \pm 1 \pmod{5}$; the ed
ge $uw$ requires $c \equiv a \pm 1 \pmod{5}$. From the first two conditions, $c
\equiv a \pm 2 \pmod{5}$. But the third condition requires $c \equiv a \pm 1 \pm
od{5}$. Since $a \pm 2 \not\equiv a \pm 1 \pmod{5}$ (as $5 > 3$), this is a cont
radiction. Hence $G$ is triangle-free.

---

**Nonbipartite.** Select one vertex $v_i$ from each part $V_i$ for $i = 0, 1, 2,
 3, 4$. Then $v_0 v_1 v_2 v_3 v_4 v_0$ is a cycle of length 5, since $v_i$ is ad
jacent to $v_{i+1 \pmod{5}}$ for every $i$. A graph containing an odd cycle is n
onbipartite.

---

**Conclusion.** For every $n \equiv 0 \pmod{5}$, the complete blow-up of $C_5$ w
ith each part of size $m = n/5$ is a nonbipartite, triangle-free graph on $n$ ve
rtices with minimum degree exactly $\frac{2n}{5}$. $\blacksquare$
