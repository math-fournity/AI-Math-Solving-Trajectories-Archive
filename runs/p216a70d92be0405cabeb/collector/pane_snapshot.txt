## Proof

Let $H$ be a Hilbert space and $\mathfrak{F} = \bigoplus_{n=0}^{\infty} \bigwedg
e^n H$ the fermionic Fock space, with vacuum vector $\Omega$. For $f \in H$, the
 creation operator $c^*(f)$ and annihilation operator $c(f)$ satisfy the canonic
al anticommutation relations (CAR):

$$\{c(f), c^*(g)\} = \langle f, g\rangle I, \qquad \{c^*(f), c^*(g)\} = 0, \qqua
d \{c(f), c(g)\} = 0.$$

**Claim.** $c^*(f)$ is bounded and $\|c^*(f)\| = \|f\|$.

### Step 1. Upper bound: $\|c^*(f)\| \le \|f\|$

From the CAR with $g = f$:
$$c(f)\,c^*(f) + c^*(f)\,c(f) = \|f\|^2 I. \tag{1}$$

The operator $c^*(f)\,c(f)$ is positive (it is of the form $A^*A$ with $A = c(f)
$), so $c^*(f)\,c(f) \ge 0$. Hence from (1):

$$c(f)\,c^*(f) = \|f\|^2 I - c^*(f)\,c(f) \;\le\; \|f\|^2 I.$$

Since $c(f)\,c^*(f) = (c^*(f))^*\, c^*(f)$ is itself positive, taking the norm (
i.e., the largest spectral value of this positive operator) gives

$$\|c^*(f)\|^2 = \|(c^*(f))^* c^*(f)\| = \|c(f)\,c^*(f)\| \le \|f\|^2.$$

Therefore
$$\|c^*(f)\| \le \|f\|. \tag{2}$$

### Step 2. Lower bound: $\|c^*(f)\| \ge \|f\|$

Apply $c^*(f)$ to the vacuum $\Omega \in \bigwedge^0 H$. By definition $c^*(f)\,
\Omega = f \in \bigwedge^1 H \subset \mathfrak{F}$, so

$$\|c^*(f)\,\Omega\| = \|f\| = \|f\|\,\|\Omega\|.$$

Since $\|\Omega\| = 1$, the operator norm satisfies
$$\|c^*(f)\| \ge \frac{\|c^*(f)\,\Omega\|}{\|\Omega\|} = \|f\|. \tag{3}$$

### Step 3. Conclusion

Combining (2) and (3):

$$\boxed{\|c^*(f)\| = \|f\|}.$$

In particular, $c^*(f)$ is a bounded linear operator on $\mathfrak{F}$ for every
 $f \in H$, with norm exactly $\|f\|$. (By the same argument with the roles of $
c$ and $c^*$ swapped — using $c^*(f)c(f) \le \|f\|^2 I$ from (1) and $c(f)\Omega
 = 0$ — one also gets $\|c(f)\| = \|f\|$, but this was not asked.)
