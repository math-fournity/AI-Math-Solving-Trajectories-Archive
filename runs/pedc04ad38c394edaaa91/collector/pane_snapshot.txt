## Solution

**Parametrization.** Introduce $u = y+z$ and $v = z-y$, so $y=\tfrac{u-v}{2}$, $
z=\tfrac{u+v}{2}$.

From the first surface: $4x = u^2$, hence $x = \dfrac{u^2}{4}$.

From the second surface: $4x^2+3y^2=3z^2 \iff 3(z^2-y^2)=4x^2 \iff 3(z-y)(z+y)=4
x^2 \iff 3vu = 4x^2$.

Substituting $x = u^2/4$: $\;3vu = 4\cdot\dfrac{u^4}{16} = \dfrac{u^4}{4}$, so f
or $u\neq 0$: $v = \dfrac{u^3}{12}$.

This gives a smooth parametrization of the curve by $u$ (with $u=0$ giving the o
rigin $O$):

$$x = \frac{u^2}{4},\qquad y = \frac{u}{2}-\frac{u^3}{24},\qquad z = \frac{u}{2}
+\frac{u^3}{24}=\frac{u(u^2+12)}{24}.$$

**Arc length element.**

$$\frac{dx}{du}=\frac{u}{2},\quad \frac{dy}{du}=\frac{1}{2}-\frac{u^2}{8},\quad
\frac{dz}{du}=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{u^2}{8}.$$

Then

$$\left(\frac{dy}{du}\right)^2+\left(\frac{dz}{du}\right)^2 = 2\!\left[\frac14+\
frac{u^4}{64}\right]=\frac12+\frac{u^4}{32},$$

and adding $\left(\frac{dx}{du}\right)^2 = \frac{u^2}{4}$:

$$\left(\frac{ds}{du}\right)^2 = \frac{u^2}{4}+\frac12+\frac{u^4}{32} = \frac{1}
{2}\!\left(\frac{u^2}{4}+1\right)^{\!2}.$$

Hence

$$\frac{ds}{du} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left(\frac{u^2}{4}+1\right).$$

**Integrate from $O$ ($u=0$) to $M$ ($u=u_M$):**

$$L = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\int_0^{u_M}\!\left(\frac{u^2}{4}+1\right)du = \frac{1}{
\sqrt{2}}\left[\frac{u^3}{12}+u\right]_0^{u_M} = \frac{u_M(u_M^2+12)}{12\sqrt{2}
}.$$

**Express in terms of $z$.** From the parametrization,

$$z = \frac{u_M(u_M^2+12)}{24}\;\;\Longrightarrow\;\; u_M(u_M^2+12) = 24\,z.$$

Substituting:

$$L = \frac{24\,z}{12\sqrt{2}} = \frac{2z}{\sqrt{2}} = \sqrt{2}\,z.$$

$$\boxed{L = \sqrt{2}\,z}$$
