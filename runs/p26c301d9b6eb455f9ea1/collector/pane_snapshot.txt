, is the canonical database of advisor–student lineages in mathematics and relat
ed fields. Its records extend back to medieval scholars of the 1300s–1400s (e.g.
, scholars from the Islamic mathematical tradition and early European universiti

etc.) starts far later — none reach the 14th century. The 14th-century anchor un
iquely identifies the MGP.

**Identifying the most prolific founding node.** In a genealogical forest, the n
ode with the most descendants is the root of the largest tree — the earliest rec
orded advisor whose lineage has branched the most over ~700 years. The MGP's lar
gest connected component is rooted in an early-modern-era scholar (the deepest a
ncestor in the dominant European mathematical lineage), whose descendants includ

**Estimating the descendant count.**

- The MGP currently contains approximately 300,000+ records (it has grown steadi
