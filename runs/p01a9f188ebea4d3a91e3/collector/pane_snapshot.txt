## Solution

Simplify the expression `(-36/-4) * (56/7)`.

**Step 1:** Simplify the first factor.
$$\frac{-36}{-4} = 9$$
(A negative divided by a negative is positive; $36 \div 4 = 9$.)

**Step 2:** Simplify the second factor.
$$\frac{56}{7} = 8$$

**Step 3:** Multiply the two factors.
$$9 \times 8 = 72$$

**Answer as a decimal:** `72`
