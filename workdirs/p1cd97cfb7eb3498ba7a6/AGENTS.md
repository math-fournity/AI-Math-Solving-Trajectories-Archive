# Problem

A specialized logistics company is tasked with organizing a sequence of cargo containers selected from a pool of 1,967 uniquely numbered crates, labeled 1 through 1967. The operations manager must arrange a selection of these crates in a single file line according to two strict weight-class protocols based on their identification numbers:

1.  **The Quad-Block Rule:** To prevent resonance during transport, the sum of the identification numbers of any four consecutive crates in the sequence must **not** be divisible by 3.
2.  **The Penta-Sync Rule:** To maintain balance on the conveyor system, the sum of the identification numbers of any five consecutive crates in the sequence **must** be divisible by 3.

Each crate from the pool can be used at most once. Determine the maximum number of crates the manager can include in a single sequence that satisfies both protocols.
