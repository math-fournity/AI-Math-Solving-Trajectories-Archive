## Solution

**Setup.** Let $Y = \mathbb{P}^2 \times \mathbb{P}^1$ (dimension 3) and let $X =
 V_+(f) \subset Y$ with $f \in H^0(\mathcal{O}(1,2))$ a general (hence smooth) s
ection, so $X$ is a smooth surface. Denote by $\iota: X \hookrightarrow Y$ the i
nclusion.

**Chow ring of $Y$.** Let $h_1 = c_1(\mathcal{O}(1,0))$ and $h_2 = c_1(\mathcal{
O}(0,1))$. Then
$$A^*(Y) = \mathbb{Z}[h_1, h_2]\big/\big(h_1^3,\; h_2^2\big),$$
with $\int_Y h_1^2 h_2 = 1$ (the class of a point).

**Class of $X$.** Since $X$ is the zero locus of a section of $\mathcal{O}(1,2)$
,
$$[X] = c_1(\mathcal{O}(1,2)) = h_1 + 2h_2.$$

**Projection formula.** For any classes $\alpha, \beta \in A^*(Y)$,
$$\int_X \iota^*\alpha \cdot \iota^*\beta = \int_Y \alpha\,\beta\,[X],$$
since $\iota_*(\iota^*\alpha\cdot\iota^*\beta) = \alpha\,\beta\,\iota_*1 = \alph
a\,\beta\,[X]$.

**Apply.** We have $c_1(\mathcal{O}(3,2)) = 3h_1 + 2h_2$ and $c_1(\mathcal{O}(1,
4)) = h_1 + 4h_2$, so
$$\iota^*\mathcal{O}(3,2)\cdot \iota^*\mathcal{O}(1,4) = \int_Y (3h_1+2h_2)(h_1+
4h_2)(h_1+2h_2).$$

**Expand.** First:
$$(3h_1+2h_2)(h_1+4h_2) = 3h_1^2 + 12h_1h_2 + 2h_1h_2 + 8h_2^2 = 3h_1^2 + 14h_1h
_2 \quad(\text{since } h_2^2=0).$$

Then:
$$(3h_1^2+14h_1h_2)(h_1+2h_2) = 3h_1^3 + 6h_1^2h_2 + 14h_1^2h_2 + 28h_1h_2^2 = 2
0\,h_1^2 h_2 \quad(\text{since } h_1^3=h_2^2=0).$$

**Integrate:**
$$\int_Y 20\,h_1^2 h_2 = 20 \cdot 1 = 20.$$

**Answer.** The intersection number is $\boxed{20}$.
