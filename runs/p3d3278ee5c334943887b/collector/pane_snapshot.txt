## Solution

**Setup.** We have 12 distinct sheets (4 red, 4 white, 4 blue) and 12 distinct s
tars (4 red, 4 white, 4 blue). A random bijection places one star per sheet. Tot
al arrangements: $12!$.

**Matrix formulation.** Let $x_{ij}$ = number of stars of color $i$ placed on sh
eets of color $j$, with $i,j \in \{R,W,B\}$. The constraints are:
- Row sums $= 4$ (each star color used 4 times),
- Column sums $= 4$ (each sheet color has 4 sheets),
- Diagonal $x_{ii} = 0$ (no star on a same-color sheet).

With the diagonal forced to zero, the matrix is:
$$M(t) = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 4-t & t \\ t & 0 & 4-t \\ 4-t & t & 0 \end{pmatrix}
, \qquad t \in \{0,1,2,3,4\},$$
where one verifies that all row/column sum constraints reduce to a single free p
arameter $t$.

**Counting bijections for a fixed matrix.** For a given matrix $(x_{ij})$, the n
umber of bijections is:
$$N = \prod_i \frac{4!}{\prod_j x_{ij}!}\;\cdot\;\prod_j \frac{4!}{\prod_i x_{ij
}!}\;\cdot\;\prod_{i,j} x_{ij}! \;=\; \frac{(4!)^6}{\prod_{i,j} x_{ij}!}.$$

For $M(t)$, the nine entries are three $0$'s, three $(4{-}t)$'s, and three $t$'s
, so:
$$N(t) = \frac{(4!)^6}{\big((4{-}t)!\big)^3\,(t!)^3} = \frac{24^6}{\big((4{-}t)!
\big)^3 (t!)^3}.$$

**Computing each term** ($24^6 = 191{,}102{,}976$):

| $t$ | denominator | $N(t)$ |
|-----|-------------|--------|
| 0 | $24^3 \cdot 1 = 13{,}824$ | $13{,}824$ |
| 1 | $6^3 \cdot 1 = 216$ | $884{,}736$ |
| 2 | $2^3 \cdot 2^3 = 64$ | $2{,}985{,}984$ |
| 3 | $1 \cdot 6^3 = 216$ | $884{,}736$ |
| 4 | $1 \cdot 24^3 = 13{,}824$ | $13{,}824$ |

**Total favorable:**
$$\sum_t N(t) = 2(13{,}824) + 2(884{,}736) + 2{,}985{,}984 = 27{,}648 + 1{,}769{
,}472 + 2{,}985{,}984 = 4{,}783{,}104.$$

**Probability:**
$$P = \frac{4{,}783{,}104}{479{,}001{,}600}.$$

Dividing numerator and denominator by $\gcd = 27{,}648$:
$$4{,}783{,}104 / 27{,}648 = 173, \qquad 479{,}001{,}600 / 27{,}648 = 17{,}325.$
$

So $P = \dfrac{173}{17325}$, with $\gcd(173, 17325) = 1$ (since $17325 = 173\cdo
t100 + 25$ and $\gcd(173,25)=1$).

Thus $m = 173$, $n = 17{,}325$, and
$$n - 100m = 17{,}325 - 17{,}300 = \boxed{25}.$$
