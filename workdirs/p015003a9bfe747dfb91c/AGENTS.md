# Problem

In a futuristic city, seven defensive outposts—$A, B, C, D, E, F,$ and $G$—are positioned at the vertices of a perfect regular heptagon. A central surveillance drone, identified as unit $H$, is stationed at a fixed coordinate within the perimeter formed by these outposts.

Technicians have recorded that the straight-line distance from drone $H$ to outpost $D$ is exactly equal to the distance between outposts $D$ and $E$. Furthermore, a directional sensor at outpost $G$ indicates that the interior angle formed between the drone and the adjacent outpost $F$ (specifically $\angle HGF$) measures exactly $\frac{270}{7}$ degrees.

To complete the synchronization of the defensive grid, the command center needs to determine the angular spread between outposts $D$ and $G$ as measured from the drone’s perspective. Find the measure of $\angle DHG$ in degrees.
