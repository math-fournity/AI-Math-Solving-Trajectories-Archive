### Impossibility for n ≤ 33

{16, 18, 20, 22, 24, 26, 28, 30, 32} — **9 values**.

The 7 even-indexed drones k ∈ {2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14} each require an **even**
a_k (any multiple of an even number is even). Additionally:

- **k = 13**: The only multiple of 13 in [16, 33] is 26 (since 13 < 16 and 39 >
33). So a_{13} = 26, which is **even**.
- **k = 15**: The only multiple of 15 in [16, 33] is 30 (since 15 < 16 and 45 >
33). So a_{15} = 30, which is **even**.

Thus **9 drones** ({2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 13, 14, 15}) each require an even value,

**Forced assignments among even-value consumers:**

- k = 14: only multiple in range is 28 → a_{14} = 28.

Remaining even values: {16, 22, 32}, for k ∈ {2, 4, 8}:
