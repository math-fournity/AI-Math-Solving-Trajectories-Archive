## Solution

We count functions $f:\{1,2,3,4,5\}\to\{1,2,3,4,5\}$ satisfying $f(k+1)\le f(k)+
1$ for $k=1,2,3,4$.

**Dynamic programming.** Let $\text{dp}[k][v]$ = number of ways to assign $f(1),
\dots,f(k)$ with $f(k)=v$.

**Base case:** $\text{dp}[1][v]=1$ for all $v\in\{1,2,3,4,5\}$.

**Recurrence:** The constraint $f(k+1)\le f(k)+1$ means $w\le v+1$, i.e., $v\ge
w-1$. So:
$$\text{dp}[k+1][w]=\sum_{v=\max(1,\,w-1)}^{5}\text{dp}[k][v].$$

**Computation:**

| $k$ | $v{=}1$ | $v{=}2$ | $v{=}3$ | $v{=}4$ | $v{=}5$ |
|-----|---------|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| 1   | 1       | 1       | 1       | 1       | 1       |
| 2   | 5       | 5       | 4       | 3       | 2       |
| 3   | 19      | 19      | 14      | 9       | 5       |
| 4   | 66      | 66      | 47      | 28      | 14      |
| 5   | 221     | 221     | 155     | 89      | 42      |

**Verification of row 2:** Total unrestricted functions on $\{1,2\}$ is $25$; th
ose violating $f(2)>f(1)+1$: $f(1)=1\to 3$ choices, $f(1)=2\to 2$, $f(1)=3\to 1$
, total $6$; valid $=25-6=19=5+5+4+3+2$. ✓

**Final answer:**
$$\text{dp}[5][1]+\text{dp}[5][2]+\text{dp}[5][3]+\text{dp}[5][4]+\text{dp}[5][5
]=221+221+155+89+42=\boxed{728}.$$
