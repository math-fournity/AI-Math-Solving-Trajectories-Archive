## Setup

Place $B=(0,0)$, $C=(7,0)$. From $AB=3$, $AC=5$:
$$x^2+y^2=9,\quad (x-7)^2+y^2=25 \implies x=\tfrac{33}{14},\ y=\tfrac{15\sqrt3}{
14}.$$
So $A=\bigl(\tfrac{33}{14},\tfrac{15\sqrt3}{14}\bigr)$, and its reflection over
$\overline{BC}$ (the $x$-axis) is
$$E=\Bigl(\tfrac{33}{14},-\tfrac{15\sqrt3}{14}\Bigr).$$

## Circumcircle and point $D$

The circumcircle of $ABC$ has equation $x^2+y^2-7x+\tfrac{7\sqrt3}{3}y=0$ (from
$B,C,A$).

Line $BE$: parametrize as $(33t,\,-15\sqrt3\,t)$. Substituting:
$$1089t^2+675t^2-231t-105t=0\implies 1764t^2-336t=0\implies t=\tfrac{4}{21}.$$
Thus $D=\bigl(\tfrac{44}{7},-\tfrac{20\sqrt3}{7}\bigr)$.

## Triangle $ABD$ and its incenter $I$

Side lengths:
- $AB=3$,
- $BD=\sqrt{(44/7)^2+(20\sqrt3/7)^2}=\sqrt{3136/49}=8$,
- $AD=\sqrt{(55/14)^2+(55\sqrt3/14)^2}=\tfrac{55}{14}\cdot 2=\tfrac{55}{7}$.

Using $I=\frac{aA+bB+cD}{a+b+c}$ with $a=BD=8$, $b=AD=\tfrac{55}{7}$, $c=AB=3$:
$$I=\frac{8A+\tfrac{55}{7}B+3D}{8+\tfrac{55}{7}+3}.$$
Numerator: $8A+3D=\bigl(\tfrac{132}{7},\tfrac{60\sqrt3}{7}\bigr)+\bigl(\tfrac{13
2}{7},-\tfrac{60\sqrt3}{7}\bigr)=\bigl(\tfrac{264}{7},0\bigr)$.
Denominator: $\tfrac{132}{7}$. Hence
$$I=(2,0).$$

## Computing $\cos^2\angle AEI$

At vertex $E$:
$$\vec{EA}=A-E=\Bigl(0,\,\tfrac{15\sqrt3}{7}\Bigr),\qquad \vec{EI}=I-E=\Bigl(-\t
frac{5}{14},\,\tfrac{15\sqrt3}{14}\Bigr).$$

$$\vec{EA}\cdot\vec{EI}=\frac{675}{98},\quad |\vec{EA}|=\frac{15\sqrt3}{7},\quad
 |\vec{EI}|=\sqrt{\tfrac{25+675}{196}}=\frac{5\sqrt7}{7}.$$

$$\cos\angle AEI=\frac{675/98}{(15\sqrt3/7)(5\sqrt7/7)}=\frac{675\cdot49}{98\cdo
t75\sqrt{21}}=\frac{9}{2\sqrt{21}}.$$

$$\cos^2\angle AEI=\frac{81}{4\cdot21}=\frac{81}{84}=\frac{27}{28}.$$

Since $\gcd(27,28)=1$, we have $m=27$, $n=28$.

$$m+n=\boxed{55}.$$
