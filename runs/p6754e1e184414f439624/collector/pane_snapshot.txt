# Highest Weight of the Dual Representation

**Claim.** Let $\mathfrak{g}$ be a semisimple Lie algebra over an algebraically
closed field of characteristic $0$, $\mathfrak{h}$ a Cartan subalgebra, $\Phi^+$
 a system of positive roots with simple roots $\Delta$, and $W$ the Weyl group w
ith longest element $w_0$. If $V$ is the irreducible representation of highest w
eight $\lambda$, then the highest weight of the dual representation $V^*$ is

$$\boxed{-w_0\,\lambda}.$$

---

## Proof

**Step 1: The action on the dual.** The dual representation $V^*$ is defined by
$$(x\cdot f)(v) = -f(x\cdot v), \qquad x\in\mathfrak{g},\; f\in V^*,\; v\in V.$$

**Step 2: Weights of $V^*$.** Let $V = \bigoplus_{\mu} V_\mu$ be the weight-spac
e decomposition. For $h\in\mathfrak{h}$ and $f\in V^*$,
$$(h\cdot f)(v) = -f(h\cdot v) = -\mu(h)\,f(v) \quad\text{for } v\in V_\mu.$$
Hence the functional that vanishes on every $V_\nu$ with $\nu\neq\mu$ lies in th
e $(-\mu)$-weight space of $V^*$. Therefore
$$\operatorname{Weights}(V^*) = \{-\mu : \mu\in\operatorname{Weights}(V)\}.$$

**Step 3: Lowest weight of $V$.** The set of weights of $V(\lambda)$ is $W$-inva
riant. The lowest weight (the unique weight minimal in the dominance partial ord
er $\leq$) is $w_0\,\lambda$, since $w_0$ sends $\Phi^+$ to $\Phi^-$, making $w_
0\lambda$ the most negative weight.

**Step 4: Highest weight of $V^*$.** The highest weight of $V^*$ is the maximal
element of $\{-\mu:\mu\in\operatorname{Weights}(V)\}$, which corresponds to the
*minimal* element of $\operatorname{Weights}(V)$, namely $w_0\lambda$. Thus the
highest weight of $V^*$ is
$$-w_0\,\lambda.$$

**Step 5: Dominance of $-w_0\lambda$.** We verify $-w_0\lambda$ is dominant. Sin
ce $w_0(\Phi^+)=\Phi^-$, the map $w_0$ sends each simple root $\alpha_i$ to $-\a
lpha_{\sigma(i)}$ for some permutation $\sigma$ of $\{1,\dots,n\}$. Using $w_0^{
-1}=w_0$ and the $W$-invariance of the pairing,
$$\langle -w_0\lambda,\,\alpha_i^\vee\rangle = -\langle\lambda,\, w_0^{-1}\alpha
_i^\vee\rangle = -\langle\lambda,\,(-\alpha_{\sigma(i)})^\vee\rangle = \langle\l
ambda,\,\alpha_{\sigma(i)}^\vee\rangle \geq 0,$$
since $\lambda$ is dominant. Hence $-w_0\lambda\in\Lambda^+$, confirming it is a
 valid highest weight.

**Step 6: Irreducibility of $V^*$.** Since $V$ is irreducible, $V^*$ is irreduci
ble (any submodule of $V^*$ has an annihilator that is a submodule of $V$). By t
he classification theorem, $V^*$ is the irreducible representation of highest we
ight $-w_0\lambda$.

---

**Conclusion.** The highest weight of $V^*$ is $\,-w_0\lambda\,$, where $w_0$ is
 the longest element of the Weyl group. $\blacksquare$
