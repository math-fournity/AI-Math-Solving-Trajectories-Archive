## Solution

The number of perfect squares in $\{1, 2, \ldots, n\}$ is $\lfloor \sqrt{n} \rfl
oor$. We need

$$\frac{\lfloor \sqrt{n} \rfloor}{n} = \frac{2}{100} = \frac{1}{50},$$

so $\lfloor \sqrt{n} \rfloor = \dfrac{n}{50}$. This forces $50 \mid n$. Write $n
 = 50k$ for a positive integer $k$. Then the condition becomes

$$\lfloor \sqrt{50k} \rfloor = k,$$

which is equivalent to

$$k \le \sqrt{50k} < k+1.$$

Squaring (all quantities positive):

$$k^2 \le 50k < (k+1)^2 = k^2 + 2k + 1.$$

**Left inequality:** $k^2 \le 50k$ gives $k \le 50$ (since $k > 0$).

**Right inequality:** $50k < k^2 + 2k + 1$ gives $k^2 - 48k + 1 > 0$. The roots
of $k^2 - 48k + 1 = 0$ are

$$k = \frac{48 \pm \sqrt{2300}}{2} = 24 \pm \sqrt{575}.$$

Since $\sqrt{575} \approx 23.979$, the roots are approximately $0.021$ and $47.9
79$. The quadratic is positive outside this interval, so for positive integers w
e need $k \ge 48$.

Combining: $48 \le k \le 50$, giving $k \in \{48, 49, 50\}$.

**Verification:**

| $k$ | $n = 50k$ | $\sqrt{n}$ | $\lfloor\sqrt{n}\rfloor$ | $\lfloor\sqrt{n}\rfl
oor / n$ |
|-----|-----------|------------|--------------------------|---------------------
---------|
| 48  | 2400      | $48.989\ldots$ | 48 | $48/2400 = 1/50$ ✓ |
| 49  | 2450      | $49.497\ldots$ | 49 | $49/2450 = 1/50$ ✓ |
| 50  | 2500      | $50$           | 50 | $50/2500 = 1/50$ ✓ |

**Boundary check at $k=47$:** $n = 2350$, $(k+1)^2 = 2304 \le 2350$, so $\lfloor
\sqrt{2350}\rfloor \ge 48 \ne 47$. ✗ — confirms $k \ge 48$ is required.

The valid values are $n \in \{2400, 2450, 2500\}$, and their sum is

$$2400 + 2450 + 2500 = \boxed{7350}.$$
