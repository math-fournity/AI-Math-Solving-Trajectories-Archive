## Solution

Let $\phi(z) = \dfrac{z - \tfrac{1}{2}}{1 - \tfrac{1}{2}z}$, the automorphism of
 $\mathbb{D}$ sending $\tfrac{1}{2} \mapsto 0$.

**Setup.** For $f \in \mathcal{F}$, define $F = f \circ \phi^{-1} : \mathbb{D} \
to \mathbb{D}$. Then:
- $F(0) = f(\phi^{-1}(0)) = f(\tfrac{1}{2}) = 0$.
- $F'(0) = f'(\tfrac{1}{2}) \cdot (\phi^{-1})'(0)$.

Computing $(\phi^{-1})'(0)$: since $\phi^{-1}(w) = \dfrac{w + \tfrac{1}{2}}{1 +
\tfrac{1}{2}w}$,

$$
(\phi^{-1})'(0) = \frac{1\cdot(1) - \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\tfrac{1}{2}}{1} = \frac{3}
{4}.
$$

Hence $F'(0) = 0 \cdot \tfrac{3}{4} = 0$.

**Applying Schwarz lemma twice.** Since $F:\mathbb{D}\to\mathbb{D}$ is holomorph
ic with $F(0)=0$, the Schwarz lemma gives $|F(w)| \le |w|$ and $F(w)/w$ holomorp
hic with modulus $\le 1$. Since additionally $F'(0)=0$, the function $F(w)/w$ va
nishes at $0$, so applying Schwarz lemma again yields

$$
|F(w)| \le |w|^2, \qquad w \in \mathbb{D},
$$

with $G(w) := F(w)/w^2$ holomorphic and $|G(w)| \le 1$ on $\mathbb{D}$.

**Bounding $|f(0)|$.** Since $f(z) = F(\phi(z))$,

$$
|f(0)| = |F(\phi(0))| = \left|F\!\left(-\tfrac{1}{2}\right)\right| \le \left|-\t
frac{1}{2}\right|^2 = \frac{1}{4}.
$$

**Attainment.** Take $F(w) = w^2$ (i.e. $G \equiv 1$), giving

$$
f(z) = \phi(z)^2 = \left(\frac{z - \tfrac{1}{2}}{1 - \tfrac{1}{2}z}\right)^2.
$$

Verification:
- $f$ is holomorphic on $\mathbb{D}$ and $|f(z)| = |\phi(z)|^2 < 1$ since $\phi$
 is an automorphism of $\mathbb{D}$, so $f(\mathbb{D}) \subset \mathbb{D}$. ✓
- $f(\tfrac{1}{2}) = \phi(\tfrac{1}{2})^2 = 0$. ✓
- $f'(z) = 2\phi(z)\,\phi'(z)$, so $f'(\tfrac{1}{2}) = 2 \cdot 0 \cdot \phi'(\tf
rac{1}{2}) = 0$. ✓
- $|f(0)| = |\phi(0)|^2 = |-\tfrac{1}{2}|^2 = \tfrac{1}{4}$. ✓

Therefore the supremum is achieved and equals

$$
\sup_{f \in \mathcal{F}} |f(0)| = \boxed{\dfrac{1}{4}}.
$$
