## Derivation of the Normalization Condition for Dirac Wave Functions in an Exte
rnal Field

### Setup

Consider the Dirac field $\psi(x)$ in the presence of an external electromagneti
c field $A^\mu_{\text{ext}}(x)$. The field satisfies

$$(i\gamma^\mu \partial_\mu - e\gamma^\mu A^\mu_{\text{ext}}(x) - m)\,\psi(x) =
0,$$

equivalently $i\partial_t \psi = H_D \psi$, where the Dirac Hamiltonian

$$H_D = \boldsymbol{\alpha}\cdot(\mathbf{p} - e\mathbf{A}_{\text{ext}}) + \beta
m + e\Phi_{\text{ext}}$$

is Hermitian. Let $\{\psi_N\}$ be the complete set of eigenstates of $H_D$:

$$H_D\,\psi_N(\mathbf{x}) = E_N\,\psi_N(\mathbf{x}).$$

We separate solutions into **positive-energy** ($E_N > 0$), denoted $u_N(\mathbf
{x})$, and **negative-energy** ($E_N < 0$), denoted $v_N(\mathbf{x})$.

---

### Step 1: Field Expansion

The field operator is expanded in the complete set of external-field solutions:

$$\psi(x) = \sum_N \bigl[a_N\, u_N(x) + b_N^\dagger\, v_N(x)\bigr],$$

where $a_N$ annihilates an electron in state $N$, and $b_N^\dagger$ creates a po
sitron associated with the negative-energy state $v_N$. These operators satisfy
the canonical anticommutation relations:

$$\{a_N, a_{N'}^\dagger\} = \delta_{NN'}, \qquad \{b_N, b_{N'}^\dagger\} = \delt
a_{NN'},$$

with all other anticommutators ($\{a_N, b_{N'}\}$, $\{a_N, b_{N'}^\dagger\}$, et
c.) vanishing.

---

### Step 2: Compute the Equal-Time Anticommutator

The canonical equal-time anticommutation relation for the Dirac field is:

$$\{\psi_\alpha(\mathbf{x}, t),\, \psi_\beta^\dagger(\mathbf{y}, t)\} = \delta_{
\alpha\beta}\,\delta^3(\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{y}). \tag{1}$$

Substituting the field expansion into the left-hand side:

$$\{\psi_\alpha(x), \psi_\beta^\dagger(y)\} = \sum_{N,N'} \Bigl[ u_{N,\alpha}(x)
\,u_{N',\beta}^*(y)\,\{a_N, a_{N'}^\dagger\} + v_{N,\alpha}(x)\,v_{N',\beta}^*(y
)\,\{b_N^\dagger, b_{N'}\} \Bigr].$$

All cross terms vanish since $\{a_N, b_{N'}^\dagger\} = 0$ and $\{b_N^\dagger, a
_{N'}^\dagger\} = 0$. Using $\{b_N^\dagger, b_{N'}\} = \delta_{NN'}$ (the antico
mmutator is symmetric), we obtain:

$$\{\psi_\alpha(x), \psi_\beta^\dagger(y)\} = \sum_N \Bigl[ u_{N,\alpha}(x)\,u_{
N,\beta}^*(y) + v_{N,\alpha}(x)\,v_{N,\beta}^*(y) \Bigr]. \tag{2}$$

---

### Step 3: Completeness (Closure) Relation

Setting (2) equal to the right-hand side of (1), we obtain the **completeness re
lation**:

$$\boxed{\sum_N \Bigl[ u_N(x)\,u_N^\dagger(y) + v_N(x)\,v_N^\dagger(y) \Bigr] =
\delta^3(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y})\,\mathbb{I}_{4\times4}}, \tag{3}$$

where both sides are evaluated at equal times $x^0 = y^0$, and $\mathbb{I}_{4\ti
mes4}$ is the identity in spinor space.

---

### Step 4: Orthogonality from Hermiticity of $H_D$

Since $H_D$ is Hermitian, eigenstates with different eigenvalues are orthogonal.
 For $E_N \neq E_{N'}$:

$$E_N \int d^3x\, \psi_{N'}^\dagger(\mathbf{x})\,\psi_N(\mathbf{x}) = \int d^3x\
, \psi_{N'}^\dagger\, H_D\, \psi_N = \int d^3x\, (H_D \psi_{N'})^\dagger \psi_N
= E_{N'} \int d^3x\, \psi_{N'}^\dagger\,\psi_N,$$

so $(E_N - E_{N'})\int d^3x\, \psi_{N'}^\dagger \psi_N = 0$, giving orthogonalit
y. In particular:

- $u_N$ and $u_{N'}$ are orthogonal for $N \neq N'$ (different eigenvalues),
- $v_N$ and $v_{N'}$ are orthogonal for $N \neq N'$,
- $u_N$ and $v_{N'}$ are orthogonal (since $E_N > 0$ and $E_{N'} < 0$, so $E_N \
neq E_{N'}$).

For degenerate states, we choose orthonormal linear combinations. Thus:

$$\int d^3x\, u_N^\dagger\, u_{N'} = C_N\,\delta_{NN'}, \qquad \int d^3x\, v_N^\
dagger\, v_{N'} = D_N\,\delta_{NN'}, \qquad \int d^3x\, u_N^\dagger\, v_{N'} = 0
, \tag{4}$$

where $C_N, D_N$ are the (real, positive) normalization constants to be determin
ed.

---

### Step 5: Extract the Normalization by Projection

**Projecting onto $u_{N'}$:** Multiply the completeness relation (3) by $u_{N'}^
\dagger(\mathbf{y})$ and integrate over $\mathbf{y}$:

$$\sum_N \left[\int d^3y\, u_N^\dagger(\mathbf{y})\, u_{N'}(\mathbf{y})\right] u
_N(\mathbf{x}) + \sum_N \left[\int d^3y\, v_N^\dagger(\mathbf{y})\, u_{N'}(\math
bf{y})\right] v_N(\mathbf{x}) = u_{N'}(\mathbf{x}).$$

By the orthogonality (4), the second sum vanishes entirely, and in the first sum
 only $N = N'$ survives:

$$C_{N'}\, u_{N'}(\mathbf{x}) = u_{N'}(\mathbf{x}).$$

Since $u_{N'}(\mathbf{x}) \not\equiv 0$, we conclude $C_{N'} = 1$:

$$\boxed{\int d^3x\, u_N^\dagger(\mathbf{x})\, u_{N'}(\mathbf{x}) = \delta_{NN'}
}. \tag{5a}$$

**Projecting onto $v_{N'}$:** Multiply (3) by $v_{N'}^\dagger(\mathbf{y})$ and i
ntegrate over $\mathbf{y}$:

$$\sum_N \left[\int d^3y\, u_N^\dagger\, v_{N'}\right] u_N(\mathbf{x}) + \sum_N
\left[\int d^3y\, v_N^\dagger\, v_{N'}\right] v_N(\mathbf{x}) = v_{N'}(\mathbf{x
}).$$

By orthogonality, the first sum vanishes, and only $N = N'$ survives in the seco
nd:

$$D_{N'}\, v_{N'}(\mathbf{x}) = v_{N'}(\mathbf{x}).$$

Since $v_{N'}(\mathbf{x}) \not\equiv 0$, we conclude $D_{N'} = 1$:

$$\boxed{\int d^3x\, v_N^\dagger(\mathbf{x})\, v_{N'}(\mathbf{x}) = \delta_{NN'}
}. \tag{5b}$$

---

### Step 6: Full Orthonormality Conditions

Combining all results, the complete set of normalization and orthogonality condi
tions for the Dirac wave functions in an external field is:

$$\int d^3x\, u_N^\dagger(\mathbf{x})\, u_{N'}(\mathbf{x}) = \delta_{NN'}, \tag{
6a}$$

$$\int d^3x\, v_N^\dagger(\mathbf{x})\, v_{N'}(\mathbf{x}) = \delta_{NN'}, \tag{
6b}$$

$$\int d^3x\, u_N^\dagger(\mathbf{x})\, v_{N'}(\mathbf{x}) = 0. \tag{6c}$$

For **continuum (scattering) states**, the discrete sums $\sum_N$ become integra
ls $\int dE\,\rho(E)$ (or $\int d^3p$), and the Kronecker deltas $\delta_{NN'}$
are replaced by Dirac delta functions $\delta(E_N - E_{N'})$ (or $\delta^3(\math
bf{p} - \mathbf{p}')$), with the normalization adjusted accordingly.

---

### Summary of the Logic

The normalization conditions (6) are **uniquely determined** by the equal-time a
nticommutation relation through the following chain:

1. **Field expansion** in the complete set $\{u_N, v_N\}$ of external-field Dira
c solutions, with canonical anticommutators $\{a_N, a_{N'}^\dagger\} = \{b_N, b_
{N'}^\dagger\} = \delta_{NN'}$.

2. **Equal-time anticommutator** $\{\psi_\alpha(\mathbf{x}), \psi_\beta^\dagger(
\mathbf{y})\} = \delta_{\alpha\beta}\,\delta^3(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y})$ yields th
e **completeness relation** (3): $\sum_N [u_N u_N^\dagger + v_N v_N^\dagger] = \
delta^3\,\mathbb{I}$.

3. **Hermiticity of $H_D$** guarantees orthogonality of eigenstates with differe
nt eigenvalues, including $u$–$v$ orthogonality (opposite energy signs).

4. **Projection** of the completeness relation onto individual states $u_{N'}$ a
nd $v_{N'}$, using orthogonality to collapse the sums, forces the normalization
constants to unity: $C_N = D_N = 1$.

Both $u_N$ and $v_N$ are normalized to $+1$ (not $-1$), because both terms in th
e completeness relation (3) enter with a **positive sign** — a direct consequenc
e of $\{b_N^\dagger, b_{N'}\} = +\delta_{NN'}$.
