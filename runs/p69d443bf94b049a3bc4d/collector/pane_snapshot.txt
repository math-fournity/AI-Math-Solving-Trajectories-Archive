## Similarity Solution for Nonlinear Heat Conduction

### Step 1: Scaling Group Analysis

The PDE is:
$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\!\left(k(u)\,\frac
{\partial u}{\partial x}\right), \quad 0<x<\infty,\; t>0$$

with $u(x,0)=0$ for $x>0$ and $u(0,t)=1$ for $t>0$.

I look for a one-parameter scaling group $x^* = \lambda^a x,\; t^* = \lambda^b t
,\; u^* = \lambda^c u$ that leaves the PDE and boundary conditions invariant.

**Boundary condition** $u(0,t)=1$ is a **fixed constant**, so $u$ must not scale
: $c=0$.

Since $c=0$, $k(u)$ is unchanged under scaling (for **any** form of $k$). The PD
E terms scale as:
$$u_t \sim \lambda^{-b}, \qquad (k(u)\,u_x)_x \sim \lambda^{-2a}$$

Invariance requires $-b = -2a$, i.e., $b = 2a$. The **invariant** (similarity va
riable) is:
$$\eta = \frac{x}{t^{a/b}} = \frac{x}{\sqrt{t}}$$

and the solution takes the self-similar form $u(x,t) = F(\eta)$ (no amplitude fa
ctor, since $c=0$).

**Key point:** Because $c=0$, this reduction works for **arbitrary** $k(u)$, not
 just power laws.

$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} = F'(\eta)\,t^{-1/2}, \qquad k(u)\,\frac{\partia

$$\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\!\left(k(u)\,\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\right)

$$\boxed{\frac{d}{d\eta}\!\left[k(F)\,F'\right] + \frac{\eta}{2}\,F' = 0}$$

- $u(0,t) = 1 \;\Rightarrow\; F(0) = 1$

- Consistency at $x=0, t=0$: $F(0)=1$ and $F(\infty)=0$ are compatible.

$$k_0\,\frac{d}{d\eta}\!\left[F^n\,F'\right] + \frac{\eta}{2}\,F' = 0$$

$$\frac{k_0}{n+1}\,(F^{n+1})'' + \frac{\eta}{2}\,F' = 0$$

with $F(0)=1$, $F(\infty)=0$.

- **Monotonicity:** $F'(\eta) < 0$ for $\eta > 0$ (the profile decreases from 1
