**Proof.**

Let $V$ be a normed vector space and $U \subseteq V$ a subspace. Suppose $U$ con
tains an open ball of $V$, i.e., there exist $x_0 \in V$ and $r > 0$ such that

$$B(x_0, r) = \{x \in V : \|x - x_0\| < r\} \subseteq U.$$

Since the ball is contained in $U$, its center $x_0$ belongs to $U$.

We claim $U = V$. Let $y \in V$ be arbitrary. If $y = 0$, then $y \in U$ since $
U$ is a subspace. Suppose $y \neq 0$. Define

$$z = x_0 + \frac{r}{2}\cdot\frac{y}{\|y\|}.$$

Then

$$\|z - x_0\| = \left\|\frac{r}{2}\cdot\frac{y}{\|y\|}\right\| = \frac{r}{2} < r
,$$

so $z \in B(x_0, r) \subseteq U$. Since $x_0 \in U$ and $z \in U$, and $U$ is a
subspace (closed under subtraction), we have

$$z - x_0 = \frac{r}{2}\cdot\frac{y}{\|y\|} \in U.$$

Since $U$ is closed under scalar multiplication,

$$\frac{y}{\|y\|} = \frac{2}{r}\left(\frac{r}{2}\cdot\frac{y}{\|y\|}\right) \in
U,$$

and consequently

$$y = \|y\|\cdot\frac{y}{\|y\|} \in U.$$

Since $y \in V$ was arbitrary, $V \subseteq U$. Combined with $U \subseteq V$, w
e conclude $U = V$. $\blacksquare$
