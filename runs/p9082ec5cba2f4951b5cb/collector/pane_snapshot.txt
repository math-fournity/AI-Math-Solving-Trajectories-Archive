**Theorem.** Let $A$ be a local Artin $k$-algebra with residue field $k$ (where
$k$ is algebraically closed). Then every vector bundle on $\operatorname{Spec}(A
)$ is trivial, i.e., every finitely generated locally free $A$-module is free.

**Proof.**

Since $A$ is a local Artin ring, let $\mathfrak{m}$ be its maximal ideal, so tha
t $A/\mathfrak{m} \cong k$. We establish the result by proving the following sta
ndard algebraic fact and then explaining why it applies here.

> **Lemma.** Over any local ring $(R, \mathfrak{m})$, every finitely generated p
rojective $R$-module is free.

*Proof of Lemma.* Let $P$ be a finitely generated projective $R$-module. The quo
tient $P/\mathfrak{m}P$ is a finite-dimensional vector space over the residue fi
eld $\kappa = R/\mathfrak{m}$; let $r = \dim_\kappa(P/\mathfrak{m}P)$. Choose $e
_1, \dots, e_r \in P$ whose images form a $\kappa$-basis of $P/\mathfrak{m}P$. B
y **Nakayama's Lemma** (applicable since $P$ is finitely generated and $\mathfra
k{m}$ is the Jacobson radical), $e_1, \dots, e_r$ generate $P$. We thus obtain a
 surjection
$$\varphi: R^r \twoheadrightarrow P, \quad \mathrm{std}_i \mapsto e_i.$$
Since $P$ is projective, $\varphi$ splits: there exists $\psi: P \to R^r$ with $
\varphi \circ \psi = \mathrm{id}_P$. The composition $M := \psi \circ \varphi \i
n \mathrm{End}_R(R^r) \cong M_r(R)$ is then an **idempotent**:
$$M^2 = \psi \circ (\varphi \circ \psi) \circ \varphi = \psi \circ \varphi = M.$
$
Moreover, $P = \operatorname{im}(M) \subseteq R^r$.

Reducing modulo $\mathfrak{m}$: the induced map $\bar\varphi: \kappa^r \to P/\ma
thfrak{m}P$ is an isomorphism (by construction the $e_i$ reduce to a basis), so
$\bar M = \bar\psi \circ \bar\varphi$ is the identity matrix $I_r \in M_r(\kappa
)$.

Write $M = I_r + N$ where $N = M - I_r$ has all entries in $\mathfrak{m}$. The i
dempotent relation $M^2 = M$ gives:
$$(I_r + N)^2 = I_r + N \implies N + N^2 = 0 \implies N = -N^2.$$
Iterating: $N = -N^2 = -N^4 = \cdots = (-1)^{s+1} N^{2^s}$ for every $s \geq 1$.
 Since $A$ is **Artinian**, its maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$ is **nilpotent**: $
\mathfrak{m}^N = 0$ for some $N$. The entries of $N^{2^s}$ lie in $\mathfrak{m}^
{2^s}$, which vanishes for $2^s \geq N$. Hence $N^{2^s} = 0$ for $s$ large enoug
h, giving $N = 0$.

Therefore $M = I_r$, i.e., $\psi \circ \varphi = \mathrm{id}_{R^r}$. This shows
$\varphi$ is an isomorphism, so $P \cong R^r$. $\square$

**Application to the problem.** A vector bundle of rank $r$ on $\operatorname{Sp
ec}(A)$ corresponds to a locally free sheaf of $\mathcal{O}_{\operatorname{Spec}
(A)}$-modules of rank $r$, which in turn corresponds to a finitely generated loc
ally free $A$-module $P$ (via the global sections functor, since $\operatorname{
Spec}(A)$ is affine).

Since $A$ is local, every finitely generated locally free $A$-module is in parti
cular a finitely generated projective $A$-module (locally free $\Rightarrow$ pro
jective, and over an affine scheme this is an equivalence). By the Lemma, $P \co
ng A^r$ for some $r$.

(Equivalently, one may observe that $A$ is Artinian and local, so $\operatorname
{{Spec}}(A)$ consists of a single point — the unique prime ideal is $\mathfrak{m
}$. On a one-point space, "locally free" trivially means "free," since there is
only one open set on which to check the local trivialization. The algebraic Lemm
a above gives the same conclusion in a way that does not even require this topol
ogical observation.)

**Conclusion.** Every vector bundle on $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ is trivial; equi
valently, every finitely generated locally free module over $A$ is free. $\black
square$
