## Proof

**Claim:** $\|m\| \le \|\tau\|$.

**Setup.** Since $u = [u_{i,j}]$ is unitary in $M_n(A)$, we have $u^*u = uu^* =
I_n$, so $A$ is unital (or we work in the unitization). We view $\xi, \eta \in \
mathbb{C}^n$ as column vectors; $\eta^* = (\overline{\eta_1},\dots,\overline{\et
a_n})$ denotes the conjugate-transpose row vector, with entries identified with
elements of $\mathbb{C}\cdot 1_A \subset A$.

**Step 1: Reduction to a scalar estimate.** For unit vectors $\xi, \eta \in \mat
hbb{C}^n$,
$$
\langle m\,\xi,\,\eta\rangle = \sum_{i,j=1}^n \tau(u_{i,j})\,\xi_j\,\overline{\e
ta_i} = \tau\!\Bigl(\sum_{i,j}\overline{\eta_i}\,u_{i,j}\,\xi_j\Bigr) = \tau(a),
$$
where we set
$$
a \;:=\; \eta^*\, u\, \xi \;=\; \sum_{i,j}\overline{\eta_i}\,u_{i,j}\,\xi_j \;\i
n\; A.
$$
Since $\|m\| = \sup_{\|\xi\|=\|\eta\|=1}|\langle m\xi,\eta\rangle|$, it suffices
 to show $\|a\| \le 1$ for all unit vectors $\xi,\eta$, for then $|\tau(a)|\le\|
\tau\|\,\|a\|\le\|\tau\|$.

**Step 2: The contraction estimate $\|a\|\le 1$.** Compute
$$
a^*a = (\eta^* u\,\xi)^*(\eta^* u\,\xi) = \xi^*\,u^*\,(\eta\,\eta^*)\,u\,\xi.
$$
The rank-one matrix $\eta\,\eta^* \in M_n(\mathbb{C})\subset M_n(A)$ is positive
 with $\|\eta\,\eta^*\| = \|\eta\|^2 = 1$, hence
$$
\eta\,\eta^* \;\le\; I_n \quad\text{in } M_n(A).
$$
Since $u$ is unitary, conjugation by $u$ preserves the order:
$$
u^*\,(\eta\,\eta^*)\,u \;\le\; u^*\,I_n\,u \;=\; I_n.
$$
The map $B \mapsto \xi^* B\,\xi$ from $M_n(A)$ to $A$ is completely positive (it
 is a compression: if $B = C^*C$ then $\xi^*B\,\xi = (C\xi)^*(C\xi)\ge 0$, and o
rder is preserved). Applying it to the inequality above:
$$
a^*a = \xi^*\bigl(u^*\,\eta\,\eta^*\,u\bigr)\xi \;\le\; \xi^*\,I_n\,\xi = \|\xi\
|^2\cdot 1_A = 1_A.
$$
Therefore $\|a\|^2 = \|a^*a\| \le \|1_A\| = 1$, giving $\|a\|\le 1$.

**Step 3: Conclusion.** Combining Steps 1 and 2:
$$
|\langle m\,\xi,\,\eta\rangle| = |\tau(a)| \le \|\tau\|\,\|a\| \le \|\tau\|
$$
for all unit vectors $\xi,\eta$. Taking the supremum,
$$
\boxed{\|m\| \le \|\tau\|.}
$$

**Sharpness.** The bound is attained: take $A = M_k(\mathbb{C})$, $\tau = \opera
torname{tr}/k$ (the normalized trace, a state with $\|\tau\|=1$), and $u = I_n \
in M_n(A)$ (the identity, which is unitary). Then $u_{i,j} = \delta_{ij}\,I_k$,
so $m_{i,j} = \tau(\delta_{ij}I_k) = \delta_{ij}$, giving $m = I_n$ and $\|m\| =
 1 = \|\tau\|$.
