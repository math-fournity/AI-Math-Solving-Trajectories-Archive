Looking at this problem, I need to find the maximum number of people in a graph
(max degree 3) where 2023 are initially infected, infection spreads by threshold
-2 bootstrap percolation, and everyone eventually gets infected.

## Proof

**Setup.** Model CMI as a graph $G=(V,E)$ with maximum degree 3. Let $S_t$ denot
e the set of infected people after day $t$, with $|S_0|=2023$ and $S_\infty=V$.
A person joins $S_{t+1}$ if they have $\geq 2$ friends in $S_t$.

**Upper Bound.** Define the *cut size* $c_t = |E(S_t,\, V\setminus S_t)|$, the n
umber of edges crossing from infected to uninfected.

Let $T_t = S_{t+1}\setminus S_t$ be the newly infected set on day $t+1$. For eac
h $v\in T_t$, let $a_v$ = edges from $v$ to $S_t$, $b_v$ = edges from $v$ to $T_
t\setminus\{v\}$, $c_v$ = edges from $v$ to $V\setminus S_{t+1}$. Then:
- $a_v \geq 2$ (infection requirement),
- $a_v+b_v+c_v \leq 3$ (degree bound), so $c_v \leq 1$.

The cut change is:
$$c_t - c_{t+1} = \sum_{v\in T_t}(a_v - c_v).$$

For every day **except the last**, $V\setminus S_{t+1}\neq\emptyset$ is possible
, and we have $a_v - c_v \geq 2-1 = 1$. On the **last** day, $V\setminus S_{t+1}
=\emptyset$, so $c_v=0$ for all $v\in T_{\text{last}}$, giving $a_v-c_v\geq 2$.

Summing over all days (with $c_\infty=0$):
$$c_0 = \sum_{t}(c_t-c_{t+1}) \geq \sum_{t\neq\text{last}}|T_t|\cdot 1 + |T_{\te
xt{last}}|\cdot 2 = (N-2023) + |T_{\text{last}}|.$$

Since $|T_{\text{last}}|\geq 1$:
$$N \leq c_0 + 2023 - 1.$$

Now $c_0 \leq 3\cdot 2023 = 6069$ (each initially infected person has degree $\l
eq 3$, and edges among them don't contribute to the cut). Therefore:
$$\boxed{N \leq 6069 + 2022 = 8091.}$$

**Construction achieving 8091.** We build a graph with 2023 *seed* vertices $s_1
,\dots,s_{2023}$ (initially infected, forming an independent set) and 6068 *chai
n* vertices $v_1,\dots,v_{6068}$.

**Edges:**
- $v_1$ is adjacent to two distinct seeds, say $s_1, s_2$, and to $v_2$. (degree
 3)
- For $2\leq i \leq 6067$: $v_i$ is adjacent to $v_{i-1}$, one seed $s_{j_i}$, a
nd $v_{i+1}$. (degree 3)
- $v_{6068}$ is adjacent to $v_{6067}$ and one seed $s_{j_{6068}}$. (degree 2)

The seed assignments $j_i$ are chosen in round-robin fashion so that each seed i
s used exactly 3 times total (2 from $v_1$'s two seeds contribute 1 each; the re
maining 6067 chain vertices contribute 1 each; total $= 2+6067 = 6069 = 2023\tim
es 3$). Each seed connects to 3 distinct chain vertices, so every seed has degre
e 3.

**Verification of infection spread:**
- Day 0: All seeds infected.
- Day 1: $v_1$ has friends $s_1, s_2$ (both infected). ✓
- Day $i$ ($i\geq 2$): $v_i$ has friends $v_{i-1}$ (infected on day $i-1$) and $
s_{j_i}$ (infected on day 0). Both infected by day $i-1$. ✓
- Day 6068: $v_{6068}$ has friends $v_{6067}$ (infected on day 6067) and $s_{j_{
6068}}$ (infected on day 0). ✓

All degrees are $\leq 3$, all 2023 seeds form an independent set (so $c_0=6069$)
, each non-final chain vertex has exactly 2 edges to the previously infected and
 1 edge forward, and the final vertex has degree 2. Everyone gets infected.

**Total:** $2023 + 6068 = 8091$.
