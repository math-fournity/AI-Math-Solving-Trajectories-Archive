## Setting Up the Modular Framework

Since $100 \equiv 19 \pmod{27}$ and $10 \equiv 10 \pmod{27}$:

- $\overline{abc} \equiv 19a + 10b + c \pmod{27}$
- $\overline{bca} \equiv 19b + 10c + a \pmod{27}$
- $\overline{cab} \equiv 19c + 10a + b \pmod{27}$

**Key observation — all three remainders must be equal.** Computing the differen
ces:

$$\overline{bca} - \overline{abc} \equiv 9(b+c-2a) \pmod{27}$$

This difference is a multiple of 9. Since both remainders lie in $\{1,2,3,4,5\}$
, their actual integer difference lies in $\{-4,\ldots,4\}$. The only multiple o
f 9 in this range is **0**, so the remainders are equal. The same argument appli
es to all pairs. Let $r_1 = r_2 = r_3 = r$.

$$s \equiv 0 \pmod{3}$$
