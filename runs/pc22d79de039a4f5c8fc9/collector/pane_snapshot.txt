## Proof

Let $u = x^2 \in (0, r^2]$, and define $a = R^2 - u \ge R^2 - r^2 > 0$ and $b =
r^2 - u \ge 0$. Then

$$A^2 = u\bigl(\sqrt{a}+\sqrt{b}\bigr)^2 = u(a+b) + 2u\sqrt{ab}.$$

Since $a+b = R^2+r^2-2u$ and $ab = (R^2-u)(r^2-u)$,

$$A^2 = u(R^2+r^2-2u) + 2u\sqrt{(R^2-u)(r^2-u)}.$$

**Key step.** Consider the difference $R^2r^2 - A^2$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2r^2 - A^2
&= R^2r^2 - u(R^2+r^2-2u) - 2u\sqrt{(R^2-u)(r^2-u)} \\
&= \bigl[R^2r^2 - u(R^2+r^2) + u^2\bigr] + \bigl[u^2 - 2u\sqrt{(R^2-u)(r^2-u)}\b
igr] \\
&= (R^2-u)(r^2-u) + u^2 - 2u\sqrt{(R^2-u)(r^2-u)} \\
&= \Bigl(\sqrt{(R^2-u)(r^2-u)} - u\Bigr)^2 \\
&\ge 0.
\end{aligned}
$$

The last line is a perfect square, hence non‑negative — this uses only the trivi
al fact $(\,\cdot\,)^2 \ge 0$ (equivalently, AM–GM: $P + u^2 \ge 2u\sqrt{P}$ for
 $P=(R^2-u)(r^2-u)\ge 0$). No calculus is involved.

Therefore $A^2 \le R^2r^2$, and since $A, R, r > 0$,

$$\boxed{A \le Rr.}$$

**Equality condition.** Equality holds iff $\sqrt{(R^2-u)(r^2-u)} = u$, i.e. $(R
^2-u)(r^2-u)=u^2$, which simplifies to

$$R^2r^2 = u(R^2+r^2) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad u = \frac{R^2r^2}{R^2+r^2}, \qua
d\text{i.e.}\quad x = \frac{Rr}{\sqrt{R^2+r^2}}.$$

This value is admissible: $x > 0$, and $x \le r$ because $\dfrac{R}{\sqrt{R^2+r^
2}} < 1$. At this value,

- $\sqrt{R^2-x^2} = \dfrac{R^2}{\sqrt{R^2+r^2}}$, $\sqrt{r^2-x^2} = \dfrac{r^2}{
\sqrt{R^2+r^2}}$,
- $A = \dfrac{Rr}{\sqrt{R^2+r^2}}\cdot\dfrac{R^2+r^2}{\sqrt{R^2+r^2}} = Rr.$

Hence the maximum value of $A$ is

$$\boxed{A_{\max} = Rr}.$$
