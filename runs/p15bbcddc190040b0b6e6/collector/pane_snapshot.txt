**Solution.**

**Step 1: Scaling reduction.** Substitute $u=\Lambda x$, $v=\Lambda y$, so $x=u/

$$
\frac{e^{-\Lambda x}-e^{-\Lambda y}}{x-y}
= \frac{e^{-u}-e^{-v}}{(u-v)/\Lambda}
= \Lambda\,\frac{e^{-u}-e^{-v}}{u-v}.
$$

Squaring and multiplying by $dx\,dy=du\,dv/\Lambda^2$, the factor $\Lambda^2$ ca
ncels:

$$
F(\Lambda)=\int_0^\infty\!\!\int_0^\infty\left(\frac{e^{-u}-e^{-v}}{u-v}\right)^
2 du\,dv =: F,
$$

which is **independent of $\Lambda$**. It suffices to evaluate $F$.

**Step 2: Integral representation of the kernel.** For real $a,b$,

$$
\int_0^1 e^{-(1-t)a-tb}\,dt
= e^{-a}\int_0^1 e^{-t(b-a)}dt
= \frac{e^{-a}-e^{-b}}{b-a}
= -\,\frac{e^{-a}-e^{-b}}{a-b}.
$$

Since the integrand is squared, the sign is irrelevant:

$$
\left(\frac{e^{-u}-e^{-v}}{u-v}\right)^2
= \left(\int_0^1 e^{-(1-t)u-tv}\,dt\right)^2.
$$

**Step 3: Expand and integrate.** By Tonelli (integrand non-negative),

$$
F=\int_0^\infty\!\!\int_0^\infty\int_0^1\!\!\int_0^1
e^{-[(1-t)+(1-s)]u}\,e^{-[t+s]v}\,dt\,ds\,du\,dv.
$$

The $u$- and $v$-integrals factor:

$$
\int_0^\infty e^{-[(1-t)+(1-s)]u}du=\frac{1}{(1-t)+(1-s)}=\frac{1}{2-t-s},
\qquad
\int_0^\infty e^{-(t+s)v}dv=\frac{1}{t+s}.
$$

Hence

$$
F=\int_0^1\!\!\int_0^1 \frac{dt\,ds}{(2-t-s)(t+s)}.
$$

**Step 4: Change of variables.** Set $w=t+s$. The integrand depends only on $w$,

$$
\ell(w)=\begin{cases} w, & 0\le w\le 1,\\ 2-w, & 1\le w\le 2.\end{cases}

Therefore

$$
F=\int_0^1\frac{w}{(2-w)w}\,dw+\int_1^2\frac{2-w}{(2-w)w}\,dw
=\int_0^1\frac{dw}{2-w}+\int_1^2\frac{dw}{w}.
$$

Evaluating,

$$
\int_0^1\frac{dw}{2-w}=\Big[-\ln(2-w)\Big]_0^1=\ln 2,\qquad
\int_1^2\frac{dw}{w}=\ln 2.
$$

Thus

$$
F = \ln 2 + \ln 2 = 2\ln 2.
$$
