# Problem

In a specialized laboratory, a robotic assembly floor is represented by a perfect $8 \times 8$ grid of individual sensor tiles. A technician is tasked with deploying "Plus-Link" modular servers onto this floor. Each "Plus-Link" server is a rigid unit composed of exactly 5 tiles: a central processing tile and four peripheral connector tiles extending directly up, down, left, and right from the center (forming a 5-square cross shape). 

To ensure cooling and stability, these server units must be placed such that they align perfectly with the floor tiles and do not overlap with one another at any point. No part of a server may extend beyond the boundaries of the $8 \times 8$ grid.

What is the maximum number of "Plus-Link" servers that can be installed on the floor simultaneously?
