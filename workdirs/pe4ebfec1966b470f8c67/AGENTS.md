# Problem

A specialized logistics drone is stationed at Dock 0 of a vertical automated warehouse that has exactly ten docking levels, numbered 0 through 9. Nine unique packages are waiting to be processed, one on each level from Level 1 through Level 9.

To begin the sorting process, a technician places nine identification chips, labeled 1 through 9, into a container and shakes them thoroughly. The drone must travel to the levels in the exact order the chips are drawn from the container. The drone begins its journey at Dock 0, travels to the level indicated by the first chip drawn, then proceeds directly to the level indicated by the second chip, and continues this sequence until it has visited the level of the final chip drawn.

The vertical distance between any two adjacent docking levels is exactly 4 meters. Given that every possible sequence of the nine chips is equally likely, what is the expected total vertical distance in meters traveled by the drone?
