**Setting up the equation.** We seek integers $k>6$ and $n>k$ with
$$1+2+\cdots+k = k+(k+1)+\cdots+n.$$

The left side is $\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}$. The right side is $\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}-\dfra
c{(k-1)k}{2}$. Setting them equal:

$$\frac{k(k+1)}{2} = \frac{n(n+1)}{2} - \frac{(k-1)k}{2}.$$

Adding $\dfrac{(k-1)k}{2}$ to both sides:

$$\frac{k(k+1)+(k-1)k}{2} = \frac{n(n+1)}{2} \implies \frac{k\bigl[(k+1)+(k-1)\b
igr]}{2} = \frac{n(n+1)}{2} \implies k^2 = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}.$$

So we need $2k^2 = n(n+1) = n^2+n$.

**Reducing to a Pell equation.** Completing the square: $4n^2+4n = 8k^2$, so $(2
n+1)^2 = 8k^2+1$. Setting $m = 2n+1$ (which is odd and $\geq 3$):

$$m^2 - 8k^2 = 1.$$

This is the Pell equation $x^2 - 8y^2 = 1$ with $x=m,\; y=k$.

**Solving the Pell equation.** The fundamental (smallest positive) solution is $
(x_1,y_1)=(3,1)$, since $9-8=1$. All positive solutions are given by
$$(x_t + y_t\sqrt{8}) = (3+\sqrt{8})^t, \qquad t=1,2,3,\dots$$
