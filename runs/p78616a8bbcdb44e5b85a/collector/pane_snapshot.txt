## Solution

We count functions $f:\{1,\dots,7\}\to\{1,\dots,7\}$ such that $f\circ f$ is con
stant, i.e., $f(f(x))=c$ for all $x$ and some fixed $c$.

**Structural analysis.** Let $A=\operatorname{im}(f)$. For any $y\in A$, $y=f(x)
$ for some $x$, so $f(y)=f(f(x))=c$. Hence $f$ maps every element of $A$ to $c$.
 Since $c=f(y)$ for $y\in A$, we have $c\in A$, and because $f|_A$ is constantly
 $c$ with $c\in A$, we get $f(c)=c$ (a fixed point).

Let $B=\{1,\dots,7\}\setminus A$. Elements of $B$ are not in the image, so $f(B)
\subseteq A$ and nothing maps into $B$ (elements of $A$ map to $c\in A$; element
s of $B$ map into $A$). Thus $\operatorname{im}(f)=\{c\}\cup f(B)=A$, requiring
$f(B)\supseteq A\setminus\{c\}$.

**Counting.** Fix $c$ and a set $A$ with $c\in A$, $|A|=k$. Then $f|_A$ is deter
mined (all map to $c$). The map $f|_B:B\to A$ (with $|B|=7-k$) must have image c
ontaining $A\setminus\{c\}$ (size $k-1$). By inclusion–exclusion, the count of s
uch maps is
$$\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}(-1)^j\binom{k-1}{j}(k-j)^{7-k}.$$
There are $\binom{6}{k-1}$ choices for $A\setminus\{c\}$ and $7$ choices for $c$
. Setting $m=k-1$, $n=6-m$:

| $m$ | $k$ | $n$ | inner sum | $\binom{6}{m}$ | $7\cdot\binom{6}{m}\cdot\text{i
nner}$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1 | 6 | $1^6=1$ | 1 | $7$ |
| 1 | 2 | 5 | $2^5-1^5=31$ | 6 | $1302$ |
| 2 | 3 | 4 | $3^4-2\cdot2^4+1=50$ | 15 | $5250$ |
| 3 | 4 | 3 | $4^3-3\cdot3^3+3\cdot2^3-1=6$ | 20 | $840$ |
| 4 | 5 | 2 | $0$ (can't cover 4 elements with 2) | 15 | $0$ |
| 5 | 6 | 1 | $0$ (can't cover 5 with 1) | 6 | $0$ |
| 6 | 7 | 0 | $0$ | 1 | $0$ |

**Verification of key entries:**
- $m=3$: $B$ has 3 elements bijecting onto the 3 specific elements $A\setminus\{
c\}$, giving $3!=6$. ✓
- $m=2$: $3^4-2\cdot2^4+1^4=81-32+1=50$. ✓
- $m\geq 4$: $|B|=7-k\leq 2 < k-1$, so the image of $f|_B$ (size $\leq|B|$) cann
ot cover $A\setminus\{c\}$. ✓

**Total:**
$$7 + 1302 + 5250 + 840 = \boxed{7399}.$$
