## Proof

**Step 1: Wedderburn–Artin decomposition.**

Since $R$ is a finite-dimensional semisimple $k$-algebra, by the Wedderburn–Arti
n theorem there exist division $k$-algebras $D_1, \dots, D_s$ and positive integ
ers $n_1, \dots, n_s$ such that
$$R \;\cong\; \prod_{i=1}^{s} M_{n_i}(D_i).$$

**Step 2: Simple right $R$-modules and their dimensions.**

The simple right $R$-modules are $S_1, \dots, S_s$, where $S_i = D_i^{n_i}$ (row
 vectors) with the right $R$-action factoring through the projection $R \to M_{n
_i}(D_i)$ and then acting by matrix multiplication. As a $k$-vector space,
$$\dim_k S_i = n_i \cdot \dim_k D_i.$$

The right regular module decomposes as
$$R_R \;\cong\; \bigoplus_{i=1}^{s} S_i^{\,n_i},$$
since $M_{n_i}(D_i)$ as a right module over itself is $S_i^{n_i}$.

**Step 3: Endomorphism rings of simples.**

We compute $\operatorname{End}_R(S_i)$. Consider the case $R = M_n(D)$, $S = D^n
$. A $k$-linear map $\phi: D^n \to D^n$ is $R$-linear iff it commutes with all m
atrix units $E_{ab} \in M_n(D)$. From $\phi(v \cdot E_{ab}) = \phi(v) \cdot E_{a
b}$, one deduces that $\phi$ must act componentwise: $\phi(v_1, \dots, v_n) = (\
psi(v_1), \dots, \psi(v_n))$ for a single $k$-linear map $\psi: D \to D$. Compat
ibility with scalar matrices $d \cdot I_n$ forces $\psi(xd) = \psi(x)d$ for all
$x, d \in D$, i.e., $\psi$ is right $D$-linear. Such maps are exactly left multi
plications $\psi_d(x) = dx$ for $d \in D$, and $\psi_d \circ \psi_e = \psi_{de}$
, giving a ring isomorphism
$$\operatorname{End}_R(S_i) \;\cong\; D_i.$$
In particular, $\dim_k \operatorname{End}_R(S_i) = \dim_k D_i$. By Schur's lemma
, $\operatorname{Hom}_R(S_i, S_j) = 0$ for $i \neq j$.

**Step 4: Dimension of $\operatorname{Hom}_R(S_i, R_R)$.**

Using the decomposition $R_R \cong \bigoplus_j S_j^{n_j}$ and the vanishing of c
ross-Homs:
$$\operatorname{Hom}_R(S_i,\, R_R) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_R(S_i,\, S_i^{n_
i}) \;\cong\; \operatorname{End}_R(S_i)^{n_i} \;\cong\; D_i^{n_i}.$$
Therefore
$$\dim_k \operatorname{Hom}_R(S_i, R_R) \;=\; n_i \cdot \dim_k D_i \;=\; \dim_k
S_i. \tag{$\star$}$$

**Step 5: Decomposition of $M_R$ and conclusion.**

Since $\dim_k M < \infty$ and $R$ is finite-dimensional over $k$, the right $R$-
module $M_R$ is finitely generated. Since $R$ is semisimple, every finitely gene
rated right $R$-module is semisimple, so
$$M_R \;\cong\; \bigoplus_{i=1}^{s} S_i^{\,m_i}$$
for some non-negative integers $m_i$. Then
$$M^* = \operatorname{Hom}_R(M_R,\, R_R) \;\cong\; \bigoplus_{i=1}^{s} \operator
name{Hom}_R(S_i, R_R)^{m_i}.$$
Taking $k$-dimensions and applying $(\star)$:
$$\dim_k M^* \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{s} m_i \cdot \dim_k \operatorname{Hom}_R(S_i, R_R
) \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{s} m_i \cdot \dim_k S_i \;=\; \dim_k M.$$

**Remark (Frobenius perspective).** Semisimple $k$-algebras are Frobenius (indee
d symmetric) algebras: there exists a non-degenerate associative $k$-bilinear fo
rm $\lambda: R \times R \to k$, yielding an $R$-module isomorphism $R_R \cong \o
peratorname{Hom}_k(R, k)$. By tensor-hom adjunction this gives $\operatorname{Ho
m}_R(M_R, R_R) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_k(M, k)$ as $k$-vector spaces, immediate
ly implying $\dim_k M^* = \dim_k M$. The explicit Wedderburn–Artin computation a
bove confirms this concretely.

**Conclusion.** For every finite-dimensional $R$-bimodule $M$ over a finite-dime
nsional semisimple $k$-algebra $R$,
$$\boxed{\dim_k M^* \;=\; \dim_k M.}$$
Yes, $\dim_k M^*$ is always equal to $\dim_k M$.
