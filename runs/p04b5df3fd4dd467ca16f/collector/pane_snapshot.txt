$$f_k = 1 - \frac{f_{k+1}}{p_{k+1}}, \quad \text{so} \quad f_{k+1} = p_{k+1}(1 -

Each $f_k \in (1 - \frac{1}{p_{k+1}},\, 1)$ since the tail is an alternating ser

At step $k$: we need $f_{k+1} = p_{k+1}(1 - f_k) \in (1 - \frac{1}{p_{k+2}}, 1)$

**Lower bound:** $p_{k+1}(1-f_k) > \frac{p_{k+1}}{p_{k+1}+1} \Rightarrow p_{k+1}
+1 > \frac{1}{1-f_k} \Rightarrow p_{k+1} > \frac{1}{1-f_k} - 1$.

These pin $p_{k+1}$ to a unique integer when $\frac{1}{1-f_k}$ is not itself an
integer.
