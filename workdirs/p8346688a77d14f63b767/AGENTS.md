# Problem

In a remote desert valley, a team of archaeologists has identified a sacred triangular region, designated Site $T$, which has an area of exactly $E$ square units. The boundaries of Site $T$ are defined by three straight ancient stone walls.

The archaeologists are deploying a specialized "Mirror-Imaging" drone to map the surrounding terrain. The drone’s operation is governed by a specific protocol:
1. The drone must travel along the entire perimeter of the stone walls of Site $T$.
2. At every single point $O$ located on the boundary of Site $T$, the drone projects a virtual "Reflected Zone." This zone is a perfect point-reflection (180-degree rotation) of the entire Site $T$ across the drone's current position $O$.

Let $\Phi$ be the total geographic region covered by the union of all these virtual "Reflected Zones" generated as the drone completes its circuit around the perimeter. 

Calculate the total area of the region $\Phi$ in terms of $E$.
