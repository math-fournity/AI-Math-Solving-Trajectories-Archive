## Proof

The grid has $n$ rows and $m$ columns of panels ($n, m \geq 3$). Two panels are
adjacent if they share an edge (horizontally or vertically), and one link is cre
ated per adjacent pair.

**Counting the links:**

- **Horizontal links:** In each row of $m$ panels, there are $m-1$ horizontal ad
jacencies. Across $n$ rows: $n(m-1)$.
- **Vertical links:** In each column of $n$ panels, there are $n-1$ vertical adj
acencies. Across $m$ columns: $m(n-1)$.

Total links:
$$n(m-1) + m(n-1) = nm - n + nm - m = 2nm - n - m = 252.$$

**Solving the Diophantine equation:**

Multiply both sides by 2 and add 1:
$$4nm - 2n - 2m + 1 = 505,$$
$$(2n-1)(2m-1) = 505.$$

Factor $505$:
$$505 = 5 \times 101,$$
where $101$ is prime. The full factorization gives the factor pairs $(1, 505)$,
$(5, 101)$, $(101, 5)$, $(505, 1)$.

Since $n, m \geq 3$, we have $2n-1 \geq 5$ and $2m-1 \geq 5$, which rules out th
e pairs containing $1$.

The valid solution is:
$$2n - 1 = 5,\quad 2m - 1 = 101 \implies n = 3,\; m = 51,$$
(or symmetrically $n = 51,\, m = 3$).

**Verification:** With $n = 3$, $m = 51$:
- Horizontal links: $3 \times 50 = 150$,
- Vertical links: $2 \times 51 = 102$,
- Total: $150 + 102 = 252$. ✓

The total number of panels is:
$$nm = 3 \times 51 = 153.$$
