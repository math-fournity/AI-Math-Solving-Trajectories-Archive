## Solution

We seek positive integer $m$ and integer $n$ with $m^2 = 4n^2 - 5n + 16$, maximi
zing $|m-n|$.

**Completing the square.** Multiply by 16:
$$16m^2 = 64n^2 - 80n + 256 = (8n-5)^2 + 231.$$
Setting $x = 8n-5$ and $y = 4m$ (so $y > 0$), this becomes
$$y^2 - x^2 = 231, \quad\text{i.e.,}\quad (y-x)(y+x) = 231.$$

**Parity and sign.** Since $231 = 3\cdot 7\cdot 11$ is odd, every divisor is odd
, so $y-x$ and $y+x$ are both odd and $y = \tfrac{(y-x)+(y+x)}{2}$ is an integer
. Since $y>0$ and the product is positive, both factors must be positive (both n
egative would force $y<0$).

**Factor pairs.** The divisor pairs $(d,\, 231/d)$ with $d \le 231/d$ are:

| $d$ | $231/d$ | $y=\frac{d+231/d}{2}$ | $x=\pm\frac{231/d-d}{2}$ | $m=y/4$ | $
n=\frac{x+5}{8}$ | valid? | $\|m-n\|$ |
|-----|---------|------|------|------|------|--------|------|
| 1 | 231 | 116 | $\pm 115$ | 29 | $15$ / $-110/8$ | ✓ ($x=115$) | 14 |
| 3 | 77 | 40 | $\pm 37$ | 10 | $42/8$ / $-4$ | ✓ ($x=-37$) | 14 |
| 7 | 33 | 20 | $\pm 13$ | 5 | $18/8$ / $-1$ | ✓ ($x=-13$) | 6 |
| 11 | 21 | 16 | $\pm 5$ | 4 | $10/8$ / $0$ | ✓ ($x=-5$) | 4 |

For each pair, exactly one sign of $x$ yields an integer $n$ (since $8n = x+5$ r
equires $x \equiv 3 \pmod{8}$). The valid solutions are:

- $(m,n) = (29, 15)$: check $4(225)-75+16 = 841 = 29^2$. ✓ $|m-n|=14$.
- $(m,n) = (10, -4)$: check $4(16)+20+16 = 100 = 10^2$. ✓ $|m-n|=14$.
- $(m,n) = (5, -1)$: check $4+5+16 = 25 = 5^2$. ✓ $|m-n|=6$.
- $(m,n) = (4, 0)$: check $0+0+16 = 16 = 4^2$. ✓ $|m-n|=4$.

These are **all** solutions, since every factor pair of 231 has been enumerated.

The maximum value of $|m-n|$ is $\boxed{14}$.
