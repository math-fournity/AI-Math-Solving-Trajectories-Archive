## Solution

**Setup: unwrapping the cone.** A right circular cone with base radius $r=1$ km
and slant height $\ell=3$ km can be developed (unwrapped) onto a plane as a circ
ular sector. The sector has:

- radius $= \ell = 3$ km (the slant height, from vertex $V$ to the base perimete
r),
- arc length $= 2\pi r = 2\pi$ km (the base circumference).

The central angle of this sector is
$$\theta = \frac{\text{arc length}}{\text{radius}} = \frac{2\pi}{3}\ \text{rad}
= 120^\circ.$$

The vertex $V$ of the cone maps to the center $O$ of the sector.

**Identifying the cable path.** A point $P$ on the base perimeter lies on the ar
c of the sector. Because the cone is reassembled by gluing the two straight edge
s (radii) of the sector together, the single physical point $P$ appears **twice*
* on the developed sector: once on each radial edge, at distance $3$ from $O$.

A cable that starts at $P$, wraps once completely around the cone, and returns t
o $P$ corresponds, on the developed sector, to a curve connecting the two copies
 of $P$. The **shortest** such curve on a flat sector is a straight line segment
 — the chord joining the two copies of $P$.

**Computing the minimum distance from $V$ to the cable.** The two copies of $P$
are points on the circle of radius $R=3$ centered at $O$, separated by the secto
r angle $\theta=120^\circ$. The cable is the chord between them.

The distance from the center $O$ (= vertex $V$) to this chord is
$$d = R\cos\!\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right) = 3\cos(60^\circ) = 3\cdot\frac{1}{2}
 = \frac{3}{2}.$$

This is the perpendicular distance from $O$ to the chord, attained at the chord'
s midpoint (by symmetry, the closest point on the chord to $O$). Since the chord
 lies entirely inside the sector (the sector angle $120^\circ < 180^\circ$, so t
he chord does not pass through $O$), this perpendicular foot is a genuine point
on the cable, and no other point of the chord is closer to $O$.

**Answer.** The minimum distance from the vertex $V$ to the sensor cable is

$$\boxed{\dfrac{3}{2}\ \text{km}}.$$
