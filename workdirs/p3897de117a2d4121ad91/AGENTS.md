# Problem

In a remote logistics hub, a technician named Elena is tasked with arranging ten specialized canisters, uniquely labeled with serial numbers 1 through 10, into a single linear cooling rack. Because of the facility's strict safety protocols and the specific weights of the canisters, the arrangement must adhere to the following structural requirements:

(a) The canister labeled 1 must be placed in the first position at the far left of the rack.
(b) The canister labeled 10 must be placed in the final position at the far right of the rack.
(c) Along the sequence, there must be exactly one canister (excluding canisters 1 and 10) that acts as a "local minimum." Specifically, there is exactly one canister whose serial number is smaller than both the serial number of the canister immediately to its left and the serial number of the canister immediately to its right.

How many different ways can Elena arrange the ten canisters to satisfy these safety protocols?
