Let $x^3 - x = x(x-1)(x+1)$. Since $p$ shares no nonconstant factor with $x^3-x$
, we have $p(0),p(1),p(-1)\neq 0$.

**Partial fractions.** Divide $p$ by $x^3-x$:
$$p(x) = (x^3-x)\,q(x) + r(x),\qquad \deg r < 3.$$
Since $\deg p < 1992$, $\deg q < 1989$, so $q^{(1992)}\equiv 0$. Decompose
$$\frac{r(x)}{x(x-1)(x+1)} = \frac{a}{x} + \frac{b}{x-1} + \frac{c}{x+1},$$
where $a=-r(0)=p(0)\neq 0$, $b=\tfrac{r(1)}{2}=\tfrac{p(1)}{2}\neq 0$, $c=\tfrac
{r(-1)}{-2}\cdot(-1)=\tfrac{p(-1)}{2}\neq 0$ (up to nonzero scaling). In particu
lar $a,b,c$ are **arbitrary nonzero constants** (choosing $p = r + (x^3-x)s$ wit
h any suitable $s$ realizes any triple with $a,b,c\neq 0$).

Writing $r(x)=(a+b+c)x^2+(b-c)x-a$ confirms $a,b,c$ can be freely chosen (nonzer
o).

**The 1992nd derivative.** Using $\dfrac{d^n}{dx^n}\dfrac{1}{x-\alpha}=\dfrac{(-
1)^n n!}{(x-\alpha)^{n+1}}$ and $n=1992$ (even):
$$\frac{d^{1992}}{dx^{1992}}\frac{p(x)}{x^3-x} = 1992!\left(\frac{a}{x^{1993}}+\
frac{b}{(x-1)^{1993}}+\frac{c}{(x+1)^{1993}}\right).$$

Putting this over the common denominator $g(x)=(x^3-x)^{1993}=x^{1993}(x-1)^{199
3}(x+1)^{1993}$ gives numerator (up to the nonzero constant $1992!$):
$$F(x)=a(x^2-1)^{1993}+b\,x^{1993}(x+1)^{1993}+c\,x^{1993}(x-1)^{1993}.$$

**Lowest terms.** Evaluate at the roots of $g$:
- $F(0)=a(-1)^{1993}=-a\neq 0$,
- $F(1)=b\cdot 2^{1993}\neq 0$,
- $F(-1)=c\cdot 2^{1993}\neq 0$.

So none of $x,\,x-1,\,x+1$ divides $F$; hence $\gcd(F,g)=1$. The fraction is alr
eady reduced, and $\deg f = \deg F$.

**Minimizing $\deg F$.** All three summands have degree $3986$. The leading coef
ficients of $x^{3986},x^{3985},x^{3984}$ in $F$ are linear in $(a,b,c)$:

| power | coefficient |
|---|---|
| $x^{3986}$ | $a+b+c$ |
| $x^{3985}$ | $1993(b-c)$ |
| $x^{3984}$ | $-1993\,a + \tbinom{1993}{2}(b+c)$ |

(using $(x^2-1)^{1993}$ contributes only even powers; $(x\pm1)^{1993}$'s $x^{199
1}$-coefficient is $\binom{1993}{2}(\pm 1)^2=\binom{1993}{2}$).

To minimize degree, kill leading coefficients in order:

1. $a+b+c=0$,
2. $b-c=0 \Rightarrow b=c,\ a=-2b$.

With $(a,b,c)=(-2b,b,b)$, $b\neq 0$ (all nonzero ✓), the $x^{3984}$ coefficient
becomes
$$-1993(-2b)+\tbinom{1993}{2}(2b)=1993\cdot 2b + \tfrac{1993\cdot 1992}{2}\cdot
2b = 1993\,b(2+1992)=1993\cdot 1994\,b\neq 0.$$

We have exhausted all three parameters (up to scaling) killing two coefficients;
 the third is forced nonzero. Any other choice either leaves $x^{3986}$ or $x^{3
985}$ nonzero, giving degree $\geq 3985 > 3984$.

**Realization.** Taking $a=-2,b=c=1$ gives $r(x)=2$, so $p(x)=2$ (degree $0<1992
$, $p(0)=p(1)=p(-1)=2\neq 0$) is admissible and achieves $\deg F = 3984$.
