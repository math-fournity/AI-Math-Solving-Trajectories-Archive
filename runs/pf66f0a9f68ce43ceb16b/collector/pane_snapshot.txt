## Proof

Let $P_1, P_2, \ldots, P_n$ be $n$ points on the unit sphere, so $|P_i|^2 = 1$ f
or all $i$. We wish to show

$$S := \sum_{1 \le i < j \le n} |P_i - P_j|^2 \le n^2.$$

**Key identity.** We compute

$$2S = \sum_{i=1}^n \sum_{j=1}^n |P_i - P_j|^2 = \sum_{i,j} \bigl(|P_i|^2 + |P_j
|^2 - 2\,P_i \cdot P_j\bigr).$$

Splitting the three terms:

$$2S = \sum_{i,j} |P_i|^2 + \sum_{i,j} |P_j|^2 - 2\sum_{i,j} P_i \cdot P_j = n\s
um_i |P_i|^2 + n\sum_j |P_j|^2 - 2\left(\sum_i P_i\right)\cdot\left(\sum_j P_j\r
ight).$$

The first two sums are equal, and the last term is $\left|\sum_i P_i\right|^2$,
so

$$2S = 2n\sum_i |P_i|^2 - 2\left|\sum_i P_i\right|^2,$$

hence

$$S = n\sum_{i=1}^n |P_i|^2 - \left|\sum_{i=1}^n P_i\right|^2. \tag{$\ast$}$$

**Applying the sphere constraint.** Since every $P_i$ lies on the unit sphere, $
|P_i|^2 = 1$ for each $i$, giving $\sum_i |P_i|^2 = n$. Substituting into $(\ast
)$:

$$S = n \cdot n - \left|\sum_{i=1}^n P_i\right|^2 = n^2 - \left|\sum_{i=1}^n P_i
\right|^2.$$

Since $\left|\sum_i P_i\right|^2 \ge 0$, we conclude

$$S = n^2 - \left|\sum_{i=1}^n P_i\right|^2 \le n^2.$$

**Equality.** Equality holds if and only if $\sum_{i=1}^n P_i = 0$, i.e., the ce
ntroid of the $n$ points is the origin. This is achievable (for example, place t
he points so their centroid is the origin), confirming the bound $n^2$ is sharp.
 $\blacksquare$
