The condition $|f| = |g|$ on $\partial D$ controls only the **modulus** (the rea
l part of $\log$) on the boundary, not the **argument**. The number of zeros of
an analytic function $h$ in $D$ is

$$N_h = \frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_{\partial D} \frac{h'}{h}\,dz = \frac{1}{2\pi}\,\D
elta_{\partial D}\arg h,$$

which depends on the **argument** of $h$ on the boundary, not on $|h|$. Two func
tions with identical boundary modulus can have winding numbers (hence zero count
