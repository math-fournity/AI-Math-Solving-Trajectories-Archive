## Solution

**Setup.** An $n$-digit number uses digits from $\{1,2,3,4,5\}$. We count those
that (i) contain the digit 5, and (ii) have no digit 3 appearing before the firs
t occurrence of 5.

**Structure.** Suppose the first occurrence of 5 is at position $k$ (positions n
umbered $1$ to $n$ from left). Then:

- **Positions $1, \dots, k{-}1$** (before the first 5): each digit is drawn from
 $\{1,2,4\}$ — excluded are 5 (since $k$ is the *first* 5) and 3 (by the problem
 condition). This gives $3^{k-1}$ choices.
- **Position $k$**: must be 5, giving $1$ choice.
- **Positions $k{+}1, \dots, n$** (after the first 5): any digit from $\{1,2,3,4
,5\}$ is allowed, giving $5^{n-k}$ choices.

Summing over all possible positions $k$ of the first 5:

$$f(n) = \sum_{k=1}^{n} 3^{k-1} \cdot 5^{n-k}.$$

**Closed form.** Substituting $j = k-1$:

$$f(n) = \sum_{j=0}^{n-1} 3^{j} \cdot 5^{n-1-j} = 5^{n-1}\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\left(\
frac{3}{5}\right)^{j} = 5^{n-1}\cdot\frac{1-(3/5)^{n}}{1-3/5} = \frac{5^{n}-3^{n
}}{2}.$$

**Verification.**
- $f(1) = (5-3)/2 = 1$: only the number "5". ✓
- $f(2) = (25-9)/2 = 8$: first 5 at position 1 gives $5$ numbers; first 5 at pos
ition 2 gives $3$ numbers; total $8$. ✓
- $f(3) = (125-27)/2 = 49$: $25 + 15 + 9 = 49$. ✓

**Computing the sum.**

| $n$ | $5^n$ | $3^n$ | $f(n) = (5^n-3^n)/2$ |
|-----|-------|-------|----------------------|
| 1   | 5     | 3     | 1                    |
| 2   | 25    | 9     | 8                    |
| 3   | 125   | 27    | 49                   |
| 4   | 625   | 81    | 272                  |
| 5   | 3125  | 243   | 1441                 |

$$\sum_{n=1}^{5} f(n) = \frac{1}{2}\left(\sum_{n=1}^{5}5^n - \sum_{n=1}^{5}3^n\r
ight) = \frac{1}{2}\big((5+25+125+625+3125) - (3+9+27+81+243)\big)$$

$$= \frac{1}{2}(3905 - 363) = \frac{3542}{2} = \boxed{1771}.$$
