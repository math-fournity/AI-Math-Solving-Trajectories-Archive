## Solution

**Setup.** Let time $t$ be measured in minutes, $0 \le t < 1440$ (24 hours). The
 angular positions (mod 360°) are:
- S-Arm: $S(t) = 360t \pmod{360}$
- M-Arm: $M(t) = 6t \pmod{360}$ (360°/hour = 6°/min)
- H-Arm: $H(t) = 0.5t \pmod{360}$ (360°/12hr = 0.5°/min)

**Relative to H.** Define $a = (S - H) \bmod 360 = 359.5t \bmod 360$ and $b = (M
 - H) \bmod 360 = 5.5t \bmod 360$. Then H sits at 0°, S at $a$°, M at $b$°. The
velocities are $\dot a = 359.5 \equiv -0.5 \pmod{360}$ and $\dot b = 5.5$ deg/mi
n.

**Target configurations.** "One arm is 30° from each of the other two" means one
 arm is the bisector. Enumerating all three choices of bisector and both sub-cas
es (the other two arms either **overlap** at the same point, or sit **60° apart*
* on opposite sides), the target points $(a, b)$ on the torus $[0,360)^2$ are:

| Bisector | Overlap case | 60°-arc case |
|---|---|---|
| H | $(30,30),\ (330,330)$ | $(30,330),\ (330,30)$ |
| S | $(30,0),\ (330,0)$ | $(30,60),\ (330,300)$ |
| M | $(0,30),\ (0,330)$ | $(60,30),\ (300,330)$ |

That is 12 candidate points.

**Which points lie on the trajectory?** A point $(a_0, b_0)$ is reached iff ther
e exist integers $k, m$ with $359.5t = a_0 + 360k$ and $5.5t = b_0 + 360m$. Elim
inating $t$ (using $359.5 = 719/2$, $5.5 = 11/2$):

$$11\,a_0 + 3960\,k = 719\,b_0 + 258840\,m.$$

The set $\{258840\,m - 3960\,k\}$ equals $\gcd(258840, 3960)\cdot\mathbb{Z} = 36
0\cdot\mathbb{Z}$. Since $719 \equiv -1 \pmod{360}$, the solvability condition r
educes to:

$$11\,a_0 + b_0 \equiv 0 \pmod{360}.$$

Checking all 12 points:

| Point | $11a_0 + b_0 \bmod 360$ | On trajectory? |
|---|---|---|
| $(30,30)$ | $360 \equiv 0$ | **Yes** |
| $(330,330)$ | $3960 \equiv 0$ | **Yes** |
| $(30,330)$ | $660 \equiv 300$ | No |
| $(330,30)$ | $3660 \equiv 60$ | No |
| $(30,0)$ | $330$ | No |
| $(30,60)$ | $390 \equiv 30$ | No |
| $(330,300)$ | $3930 \equiv 330$ | No |
| $(330,0)$ | $3630 \equiv 30$ | No |
| $(0,30)$ | $30$ | No |
| $(60,30)$ | $690 \equiv 330$ | No |
| $(300,330)$ | $3630 \equiv 30$ | No |
| $(0,330)$ | $330$ | No |

**Only two points are reachable: $(30,30)$ and $(330,330)$** — both are the "S a
nd M overlap, H is 30° away" configuration. The 60°-arc configurations never occ
ur.

**Injectivity and period.** The map $t \mapsto (359.5t \bmod 360,\; 5.5t \bmod 3
60)$ is injective on $[0, T)$ where $T$ is the smallest positive value with $359
.5T, 5.5T \in 360\mathbb{Z}$. Solving $11p = 719q$ (with $\gcd(11,719)=1$, since
 719 is prime) gives $T = 720$ minutes = 12 hours. So each reachable point is hi
t **exactly once per 12-hour period**, hence **twice** in 24 hours.

**Finding the times.** Solving $5.5t \equiv 30 \pmod{360}$ (i.e. $11t \equiv 60
\pmod{720}$, with $11^{-1} \equiv 131 \pmod{720}$):

- $(30,30)$: $t \equiv 60 \cdot 131 \equiv 660 \pmod{720}$ → $t = 660,\ 1380$ mi
n.
- $(330,330)$: $t \equiv 660 \cdot 131 \equiv 60 \pmod{720}$ → $t = 60,\ 780$ mi
n.

**Verification.**
- $t = 60$: $a = 359.5 \times 60 = 21570 \equiv 330$, $b = 5.5 \times 60 = 330$.
 ✓ (S=M at 330°, H at 0°, 30° apart)
- $t = 660$: $a = 237270 \equiv 30$, $b = 3630 \equiv 30$. ✓ (S=M at 30°, H at 0
°, 30° apart)
- $t = 780$ and $t = 1380$ repeat the same two configurations one period later.
✓

All four times lie strictly inside $(0, 1440)$. At $t = 0$ and $t = 1440$ all th
ree arms are aligned (0° apart), which does not qualify.

**Answer.** The event occurs $\boxed{4}$ times during the 24-hour observation pe
riod.
