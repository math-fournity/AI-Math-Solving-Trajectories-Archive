## Solution

The quotient group $F/N$ has the presentation
$$F/N \cong \langle a, b \mid a^3 = 1,\; b^2 = 1,\; aba^{-1}b^{-1} = 1 \rangle.$
$

**Step 1: The relations force $a$ and $b$ to commute.**

The third relation $aba^{-1}b^{-1} = 1$ is equivalent to $ab = ba$. Hence every
element of $F/N$ can be written (using commutativity to collect powers of $a$ an
d $b$) in the form $a^i b^j$.

**Step 2: Bound the order.**

Using $a^3 = 1$ and $b^2 = 1$, every element reduces to $a^i b^j$ with $0 \le i
\le 2$ and $0 \le j \le 1$. Thus
$$|F/N| \le 3 \times 2 = 6.$$

**Step 3: Show the order is exactly 6.**

Consider the group $G = \mathbb{Z}/3\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$, w
hich has order 6. Let $\alpha$ be a generator of $\mathbb{Z}/3\mathbb{Z}$ and $\
beta$ a generator of $\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$. Then $\alpha^3 = 1$, $\beta^2 = 1
$, and $\alpha\beta = \beta\alpha$ (since $G$ is abelian). So the map $a \mapsto
 \alpha$, $b \mapsto \beta$ defines a surjective homomorphism from $F/N$ onto $G
$.

This means $|F/N| \ge |G| = 6$. Combined with the upper bound from Step 2,
$$|F/N| = 6.$$

**Conclusion.**

The index $[F : N] = |F/N| = 6$.
