## Proof

**Goal.** Compute the topological Euler characteristic $\chi\!\left(\operatornam
e{Bl}_{p_1,\dots,p_n}\mathbb{CP}^2\right)$.

**Step 1. Euler characteristic of $\mathbb{CP}^2$.**

The complex projective plane has the standard CW-structure with one cell in each
 even dimension $0, 2, 4$:
$$\mathbb{CP}^2 = e^0 \cup e^2 \cup e^4.$$
Hence
$$\chi(\mathbb{CP}^2) = 1 - 0 + 1 - 0 + 1 = 3.$$

**Step 2. Effect of a single blow-up on the Euler characteristic.**

Let $X$ be a smooth complex surface and $p \in X$. The blow-up $\widetilde X = \
operatorname{Bl}_p X$ is obtained by replacing $p$ with the exceptional divisor
$E \cong \mathbb{CP}^1$. Topologically, choose a small closed disk $D \subset X$
 centered at $p$ (a contractible neighborhood, $\chi(D)=1$). Then
$$\widetilde X = (X \setminus \mathring D) \cup_{\partial D \cong S^1} \mathring
{\mathcal O},$$
where $\mathring{\mathcal O}$ is the disk bundle of $\mathcal O_{\mathbb{CP}^1}(
-1)$ restricted to its interior; its boundary is the circle bundle over $\mathbb
{CP}^1$, identified with $\partial D \cong S^1$. The piece replacing $D$ deforma
tion-retracts onto $E \cong \mathbb{CP}^1$, which has $\chi(\mathbb{CP}^1) = 2$.

By additivity of the Euler characteristic over the union (the two pieces are glu
ed along $S^1$, with $\chi(S^1)=0$):
$$\chi(\widetilde X) = \chi(X \setminus \mathring D) + \chi(\mathbb{CP}^1) - \ch
i(S^1).$$
Since $X \setminus \mathring D = X \setminus \mathring{D}$ and $\chi(X) = \chi(X
 \setminus \mathring D) + \chi(\mathring D) - \chi(\emptyset)$... more cleanly,
using excision:
$$\chi(\widetilde X) - \chi(X) = \chi(E) - \chi(\{p\}) = 2 - 1 = 1.$$
So **each blow-up increases the Euler characteristic by $1$**:
$$\chi(\operatorname{Bl}_p X) = \chi(X) + 1.$$

**Step 3. Iterating $n$ blow-ups.**

Blowing up distinct points $p_1, \dots, p_n \in \mathbb{CP}^2$ (chosen successiv
ely; the result is independent of order, and each blow-up acts on the current su
rface) applies Step 2 exactly $n$ times:
$$\chi\!\left(\operatorname{Bl}_{p_1,\dots,p_n}\mathbb{CP}^2\right) = \chi(\math
bb{CP}^2) + n = 3 + n.$$

**Verification via Betti numbers (sanity check).** The blown-up surface has Bett
i numbers $b_0=1$, $b_1=0$, $b_2 = 1 + n$ (the original hyperplane class plus $n
$ exceptional classes), $b_3=0$, $b_4=1$. Thus
$$\chi = 1 - 0 + (1+n) - 0 + 1 = 3 + n. \checkmark$$

**Conclusion.**

$$\boxed{\chi\!\left(\operatorname{Bl}_{p_1,\dots,p_n}\mathbb{CP}^2\right) = 3 +
 n.}$$
