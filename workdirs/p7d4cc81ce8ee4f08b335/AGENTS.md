# Problem

A specialized architectural firm is designing a high-tech museum pavilion in the shape of a regular quadrilateral pyramid, $VABCD$. To install a decorative glass mezzanine, a flat structural plane is positioned to pass through the corner anchor point $A$. This plane is engineered to be perfectly parallel to the horizontal floor joist $BD$ that connects the two opposite corners of the base. To maximize solar reflection, the plane is tilted at a precise orientation such that the structural edge $AB$ of the pyramid’s base forms an angle of $30^\circ$ with the surface of the mezzanine.

Calculate the total surface area of the glass mezzanine section that is contained within the interior of the pyramid.
