## Solution

**Setup.** Place $A=(0,0)$, $B=(13,0)$. From $AC=15$, $BC=14$:
$$C=\left(\tfrac{99}{13},\,\tfrac{168}{13}\right).$$

**Circumcenter $O$.** Since $O$ is equidistant from $A$ and $B$, $O=(\tfrac{13}{
2},k)$. Solving equidistance to $C$ gives $k=\tfrac{273}{56}$, so
$$O=\left(\tfrac{13}{2},\,\tfrac{273}{56}\right),\quad R=\tfrac{455}{56}.$$

**Orthocenter $H$.** Using $\vec H = \vec A+\vec B+\vec C - 2\vec O$:
$$H=\left(\tfrac{99}{13},\,\tfrac{165}{52}\right).$$

**Circle through $A,O,H$.** Since it passes through the origin, its equation is
$x^2+y^2+ux+vy=0$. Substituting $O$ and $H$:

$$\tfrac{13}{2}u+\tfrac{273}{56}v=-\tfrac{207025}{3136},\qquad \tfrac{99}{13}u+\
tfrac{165}{52}v=-\tfrac{184041}{2704}.$$

Simplifying (dividing by common factors):
$$4u+3v=-\tfrac{2275}{56},\qquad 12u+5v=-\tfrac{5577}{52}.$$

Solving: from the first, $u=-\tfrac{2275}{224}-\tfrac{3v}{4}$. Substituting:
$$-4v = -\tfrac{5577}{52}+\tfrac{6825}{56} = \tfrac{10647}{728} \implies v=-\tfr
ac{117}{32}.$$
Then $4u = -\tfrac{2275}{56}+\tfrac{351}{32} = -\tfrac{6643}{224}$, giving
$$u=-\tfrac{949}{128},\qquad v=-\tfrac{117}{32}.$$

**Finding $D$ on line $AB$.** Setting $y=0$: $x^2+ux=0 \Rightarrow x=0$ (point $
A$) or $x=-u$. Thus
$$AD = |u| = \tfrac{949}{128}.$$

**Finding $E$ on line $AC$.** Parametrize $AC$ as $(x,y)=t\cdot C$. Substituting
 into the circle equation:
$$t^2\cdot|C|^2 + t\bigl(u\cdot\tfrac{99}{13}+v\cdot\tfrac{168}{13}\bigr)=0,$$
where $|C|^2=225$. The nonzero root gives $AE=15t$:
$$u\cdot\tfrac{99}{13}+v\cdot\tfrac{168}{13} = -\tfrac{7227}{128}-\tfrac{189}{4}
 = -\tfrac{13275}{128},$$
$$AE = \frac{13275/128}{15} = \frac{885}{128}.$$

**Verification.** Both points lie on the respective segments: $AD/AB = \tfrac{94
9}{1664}\in(0,1)$, $AE/AC=\tfrac{59}{128}\in(0,1)$. Direct substitution confirms
 $O$ and $H$ satisfy the circle equation (both yield remainder $0$).

**Ratio.**
$$\frac{AD}{AE} = \frac{949/128}{885/128} = \frac{949}{885}.$$
Since $949=13\cdot 73$ and $885=3\cdot 5\cdot 59$, they are coprime, so $m=949$,
 $n=885$.

$$m - n = 949 - 885 = \boxed{64}.$$
