## Solution

**Key observation:** On the contour $L = \{|z|=1,\ 0 \le \arg z \le \pi\}$, we h
ave $|z|=1$, so

$$z\bar{z} = |z|^2 = 1.$$

Therefore the integrand simplifies on $L$:

$$z\cos(2\pi z\bar{z}) = z\cos(2\pi \cdot 1) = z\cos(2\pi) = z \cdot 1 = z.$$

So the integral reduces to

$$\int_L z\,dz.$$

**Parametrize the semicircle.** Let $z = e^{i\theta}$ with $\theta \in [0,\pi]$.
 Then $dz = ie^{i\theta}\,d\theta$, and

$$\int_L z\,dz = \int_0^{\pi} e^{i\theta}\cdot ie^{i\theta}\,d\theta = i\int_0^{
\pi} e^{2i\theta}\,d\theta.$$

**Evaluate:**

$$i\int_0^{\pi} e^{2i\theta}\,d\theta = i\cdot \frac{e^{2i\theta}}{2i}\bigg|_0^{
\pi} = \frac{1}{2}\Big(e^{2\pi i} - e^{0}\Big) = \frac{1}{2}(1 - 1) = 0.$$

**Result:**

$$\boxed{\int_L z\cos(2\pi z\bar{z})\,dz = 0}$$
