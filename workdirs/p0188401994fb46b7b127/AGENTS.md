# Problem

A team of six specialized logistics drones is carrying out a cargo transport mission. At the start of the mission, the drones are carrying $1, 2, 3, 4, 5,$ and $6$ metric tons of fuel, respectively. 

By the end of the mission, some fuel has been redistributed among the drones to optimize the return flight, but the total amount of fuel across all six drones remains exactly the same as the starting total. Due to safety protocols, no individual drone is permitted to end the mission with more than $1$ ton less fuel than it started with. Furthermore, the final fuel amount for each drone must be a distinct, non-negative integer value in metric tons.

At the conclusion of the mission, the drones are ranked from 1st to 6th place based on the amount of fuel they are carrying (the drone with the most fuel is 1st, and the drone with the least is 6th). How many different finishing rankings are possible?
