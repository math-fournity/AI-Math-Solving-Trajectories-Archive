## Solution

We roll a fair 100-sided die twice, getting $a, b \in \{1, 2, \ldots, 100\}$. We
 need $a^2 - 4b$ to be a perfect square.

**Setup.** Let $a^2 - 4b = k^2$ for some non-negative integer $k$. Then:
$$4b = a^2 - k^2 = (a-k)(a+k).$$

Since $4b$ is even and $a^2 - k^2 \equiv 0 \pmod{4}$, we need $a$ and $k$ to hav
e the same parity. Set $a - k = 2s$ and $a + k = 2t$, where $s, t$ are non-negat
ive integers with $s \le t$. Then:
- $a = s + t$
- $4b = 4st$, so $b = st$
- $k = t - s \ge 0$

Since $b \ge 1$, we need $s \ge 1$ and $t \ge 1$. Each valid pair $(s, t)$ with
$1 \le s \le t$ produces a unique valid pair $(a, b) = (s+t, st)$ (since $s, t$
are the roots of $x^2 - ax + b = 0$, determined up to order).

**Counting.** We count pairs $(s, t)$ with $1 \le s \le t$, $s + t \le 100$, and
 $st \le 100$. For each $s$, $t$ ranges from $s$ to $\min\!\big(\lfloor 100/s \r
floor,\; 100 - s\big)$.

| $s$ | $\lfloor 100/s \rfloor$ | $100 - s$ | upper bound | count |
|-----|------------------------|-----------|-------------|-------|
| 1   | 100 | 99  | 99  | 99 |
| 2   | 50  | 98  | 50  | 49 |
| 3   | 33  | 97  | 33  | 31 |
| 4   | 25  | 96  | 25  | 22 |
| 5   | 20  | 95  | 20  | 16 |
| 6   | 16  | 94  | 16  | 11 |
| 7   | 14  | 93  | 14  | 8  |
| 8   | 12  | 92  | 12  | 5  |
| 9   | 11  | 91  | 11  | 3  |
| 10  | 10  | 90  | 10  | 1  |
| $\ge 11$ | $\le 9$ | — | $< s$ | 0 |

**Total:** $99 + 49 + 31 + 22 + 16 + 11 + 8 + 5 + 3 + 1 = 245$.

**Verification of a few cases:**
- $s=1, t=99$: $a=100, b=99$, $a^2-4b = 10000 - 396 = 9604 = 98^2$. ✓
- $s=10, t=10$: $a=20, b=100$, $a^2-4b = 400 - 400 = 0 = 0^2$. ✓

**Computing the answer.** The total number of equally likely outcomes is $100 \t
imes 100 = 10000$, so:
$$\frac{m}{n} = \frac{245}{10000} = \frac{49}{2000}.$$

Since $49 = 7^2$ and $2000 = 2^4 \cdot 5^3$, we have $\gcd(49, 2000) = 1$, so $m
 = 49$ and $n = 2000$.

$$100m + n = 100 \cdot 49 + 2000 = 4900 + 2000 = \boxed{6900}.$$
