## Setup and Key Facts

The Siegel modular variety for GSp₄ has dimension $d = \frac{g(g+1)}{2} = 3$ (ge
nus $g=2$). The Galois representation $\rho$ is 4-dimensional (matching the degr
ee 4 $L$-function) and occurs in $H^3_{\text{ét}}$ — the middle degree.

## Hodge–Tate Weights

For a cuspidal representation of GSp₄ of dominant weight $(k, j)$ (with $k \geq
j \geq 0$), the 4-dimensional Galois representation in degree 3 cohomology has H
odge–Tate weights:
$$0, \quad j-1, \quad k-2, \quad k+j-3$$

**Purity check:** The representation is pure of weight $w = k+j-3$, and the pair
ing condition $h_0 + h_3 = h_1 + h_2 = w$ is satisfied:
$$0 + (k+j-3) = (j-1) + (k-2) = k+j-3 = w. \quad \checkmark$$

## Hodge Types and Complex Conjugation

The Hodge types $(p, q)$ with $p + q = w$ are:

| Hodge–Tate weight | Hodge type |
|---|---|
| $h_0 = 0$ | $(0,\; w)$ |
| $h_1 = j-1$ | $(j-1,\; k-2)$ |
| $h_2 = k-2$ | $(k-2,\; j-1)$ |
| $h_3 = k+j-3$ | $(w,\; 0)$ |

Complex conjugation $c$ acts on the Hodge structure by sending $H^{p,q} \to H^{q
,p}$. This creates two pairs:

- **Pair 1:** $(0, w) \leftrightarrow (w, 0)$
- **Pair 2:** $(j-1, k-2) \leftrightarrow (k-2, j-1)$

## Eigenvalue Count

On each 2-dimensional piece $H^{p,q} \oplus H^{q,p}$ (with $p \neq q$), complex
conjugation acts as the swap involution, which has eigenvalues $+1$ and $-1$ (on
e each).

**Verification that pairs are distinct:** Pair 1 is always a genuine pair ($0 \n
eq w$ for $k+j \geq 4$, which holds for cohomological weights). For Pair 2, the
two Hodge types coincide only when $j-1 = k-2$, i.e., $j = k-1$. Even in this de
generate case, $H^{k-2,k-2}$ has multiplicity 2 and $c$ acts on it as an involut
ion on a 2-dim space defined over $\mathbb{R}$; by the irreducibility of $\rho$
and the general oddness theorem for Shimura varieties, the eigenvalues remain $\
{+1, -1\}$.

**Total eigenvalues of $c$:**
- From Pair 1: one $+1$, one $-1$
- From Pair 2: one $+1$, one $-1$

$$\text{Eigenvalues of } \rho(c) = \{+1, +1, -1, -1\}$$

## Conclusion

The number of $-1$ eigenvalues of complex conjugation under $\rho$ is:

$$\boxed{2}$$
