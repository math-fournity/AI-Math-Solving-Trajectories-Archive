# Problem

In the coastal city of Aethelgard, a circular defense shield is generated by a central tower. The shield's influence is restricted to a radius of 5 units from the origin $(0,0)$, defined by the safety constraint $x^2 + y^2 \leq 25$. Additionally, due to local terrain interference, scouts can only operate within a region defined by the linear boundary $2x + y \leq 5$.

An explorer is searching for the point $(x, y)$ within this permissible zone that minimizes a specific energy potential function $z$. This potential is governed by two fixed environmental constants, $a$ and $b$, and is calculated by the formula $z = x^2 + y^2 - 2ax - 2by$.

Given that $a$ and $b$ are real constants such that the minimum value of this function occurs within the specified region, determine the minimum value of $z$ in terms of $a$ and $b$.
