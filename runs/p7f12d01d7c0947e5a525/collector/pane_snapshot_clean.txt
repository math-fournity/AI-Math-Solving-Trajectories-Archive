## Proof

Let $H$ be a complex Hilbert space and $A \in \mathcal{B}(H)$ a bounded operator
 (compact in particular). Define the **modulus** of $A$ by
$$|A| := (A^*A)^{1/2},$$
the unique positive square root of the positive operator $A^*A$.

**Claim.** For every $x \in H$, the scalar $\langle x, |A|x\rangle$ is real (in
fact, real and non-negative).

We establish two standard facts.

**Fact 1 ($A^*A$ is positive).** For every $x \in H$,
$$\langle x, A^*A x\rangle = \langle Ax, Ax\rangle = \|Ax\|^2 \ge 0.$$
Moreover $A^*A$ is self-adjoint since $(A^*A)^* = A^*(A^*)^* = A^*A$. Hence $A^*
A$ is a positive self-adjoint operator.

**Fact 2 (the square root of a positive self-adjoint operator is positive self-a
djoint).** By the continuous functional calculus (or the spectral theorem for bo
unded self-adjoint operators), a positive self-adjoint operator $T$ admits a uni
que positive self-adjoint square root $T^{1/2}$. Concretely, since $A^*A \ge 0$,
 its spectrum lies in $[0,\infty)$, and
$$|A| = (A^*A)^{1/2} = f(A^*A), \qquad f(t) = \sqrt{t} \ge 0,$$
is obtained by applying the real-valued, non-negative continuous function $f$ to
 $A^*A$. The functional calculus preserves self-adjointness (because $f$ is real
-valued) and positivity (because $f \ge 0$). Therefore $|A|$ is **self-adjoint a
nd positive**.

**Conclusion.** Since $|A|$ is self-adjoint, for every $x \in H$,
$$\langle x, |A|x\rangle = \langle |A|x, x\rangle = \overline{\langle x, |A|x\ra
ngle},$$
so $\langle x, |A|x\rangle$ equals its own complex conjugate and is therefore **
real**. Since $|A|$ is moreover positive,
$$\langle x, |A|x\rangle \ge 0.$$

Thus $\langle x, |A|x\rangle$ is real-valued (indeed non-negative) for all $x \i
n H$.

**Remark on compactness.** The argument uses only that $A$ is bounded: $A^*A$ is
 positive for any bounded $A$, and its square root is positive self-adjoint by t
he spectral theorem for bounded self-adjoint operators. Compactness of $A$ is no
t needed; the conclusion holds for **every** bounded operator on a complex Hilbe
rt space. Compactness only adds extra structure (e.g., $|A|$ is then compact, wi
th a discrete spectrum accumulating at $0$), but it does not affect the reality
of the quadratic form.
