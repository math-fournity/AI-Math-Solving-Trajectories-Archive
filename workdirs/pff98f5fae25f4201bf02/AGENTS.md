# Problem

A sequence of 16 cargo containers is being loaded onto a freight train. Each container can be assigned to one of two logistics firms: Ruby Transport or Emerald Logistics. 

To ensure the train’s weight is distributed according to safety regulations, the company must follow a specific grouping rule: whenever consecutive containers are assigned to the same firm, the total number of containers in that specific contiguous block must be an odd number. For example, a single Emerald container is allowed, and three Ruby containers in a row are allowed, but two or four containers from the same firm in a row are strictly prohibited.

In how many different ways can the logistics manager assign firms to the 16 containers while satisfying this safety regulation?
