## Solution

**Cube structure.** Each unit cube has 4 orange faces and 2 unpainted faces that
 share an edge (are adjacent). When assembling the 3×3×3 cube, each position has
 a certain number of exterior faces that must all be orange, meaning both unpain
ted faces must land on interior faces.

**Position types in a 3×3×3 cube:**
- 8 corners: 3 exterior, 3 interior faces
- 12 edge-centers: 2 exterior, 4 interior faces
- 6 face-centers: 1 exterior, 5 interior faces
- 1 body-center: 0 exterior, 6 interior faces

**Counting valid orientations (out of 24 total).** The 2 unpainted faces share a
 specific edge. Under the 24 rotations, this edge maps to each of the 12 cube ed
ges exactly twice. A valid orientation requires this edge to map to an edge shar
ed by two interior faces.

**Corner (3 interior faces):** Any 2 of the 3 interior faces at a corner share a
n edge (they meet at the inner vertex), giving 3 valid edges → 3×2 = **6** valid
 orientations. Probability = 6/24 = **1/4**.

**Edge-center (4 interior faces):** The 4 interior faces form a band; all pairs
share an edge except the one opposite pair, giving C(4,2)−1 = 5 valid edges → 5×
2 = **10** valid orientations. Probability = 10/24 = **5/12**.

**Face-center (5 interior faces):** The single exterior face is involved in 4 of
 the 12 edges, leaving 12−4 = 8 valid edges → 8×2 = **16** valid orientations. P
robability = 16/24 = **2/3**.

**Body-center:** All 24 orientations valid. Probability = **1**.

**Total probability:**

$$P = \left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^8 \left(\frac{5}{12}\right)^{12} \left(\frac{2}{3
}\right)^6 \cdot 1$$

$$= \frac{5^{12}}{2^{16} \cdot (2^{24}\cdot 3^{12}) \cdot 3^6} \cdot 2^6 = \frac
{5^{12}}{2^{34}\cdot 3^{18}}$$

So $p=5,\; q=2,\; r=3,\; a=12,\; b=34,\; c=18$.

$$a+b+c+p+q+r = 12+34+18+5+2+3 = \boxed{74}$$
