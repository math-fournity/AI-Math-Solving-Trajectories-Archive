## Proof

We evaluate
$$L = \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{H_n}{(\pi^{n}+e^{n})^{1/n}\,\ln n}, \qquad H_n = 1+
\tfrac12+\cdots+\tfrac1n.$$

**Step 1: The harmonic number.** It is standard that
$$H_n = \ln n + \gamma + O(1/n),$$
where $\gamma$ is the Euler–Mascheroni constant. Hence
$$\frac{H_n}{\ln n} = 1 + \frac{\gamma}{\ln n} + O\!\left(\frac{1}{n\ln n}\right
) \xrightarrow{n\to\infty} 1.$$

**Step 2: The factor $(\pi^n+e^n)^{1/n}$.** Since $\pi > e > 0$, factor out $\pi
^n$:
$$(\pi^n+e^n)^{1/n} = \pi\left(1+\left(\frac{e}{\pi}\right)^n\right)^{1/n}.$$
Because $0 < e/\pi < 1$, we have $(e/\pi)^n \to 0$, so $1+(e/\pi)^n \to 1$. Rais
ing to the power $1/n\to 0$ and using $\ln(1+x)\to 0$ when $x\to 0$,
$$\left(1+\left(\frac{e}{\pi}\right)^n\right)^{1/n} = \exp\!\left(\frac{1}{n}\ln
\!\left(1+\left(\frac{e}{\pi}\right)^n\right)\right) \to \exp(0)=1.$$
Therefore $(\pi^n+e^n)^{1/n}\to \pi$.

**Step 3: Combine.** Both factors have finite nonzero limits, so
$$L = \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{H_n}{\ln n}\cdot\frac{1}{(\pi^n+e^n)^{1/n}} = 1\cdo
t\frac{1}{\pi} = \frac{1}{\pi}.$$

$$\boxed{L = \dfrac{1}{\pi}}$$
