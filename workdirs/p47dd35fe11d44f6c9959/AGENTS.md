# Problem

A specialized cargo ship is designed to transport a total of 2007 standard shipping containers. The containers are loaded in a linear sequence of $n$ distinct delivery zones, where each zone must contain a positive integer number of containers $(a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n)$.

The shipping company implements a strict safety protocol: if any group of one or more adjacent delivery zones contains exactly 30 containers in total, the ship is considered "optimally balanced."

What is the smallest possible number of delivery zones $n$ required to guarantee that, no matter how the 2007 containers are distributed among them, there will always exist a set of consecutive zones whose contents sum to exactly 30?
