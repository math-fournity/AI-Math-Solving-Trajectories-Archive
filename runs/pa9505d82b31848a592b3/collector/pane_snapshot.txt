## Proof

Consider the ideal $I = (X - Y^2,\; T - Y^4,\; T^3 - Z)$ in $R[X,Y,Z,T]$. We eli
minate variables step by step.

**Step 1.** The relation $X - Y^2 = 0$ lets us replace $X$ by $Y^2$. Formally,
$$R[X,Y,Z,T]/(X-Y^2) \cong R[Y,Z,T],$$
via the map sending $X \mapsto Y^2$ and fixing $Y, Z, T$.

**Step 2.** Under this isomorphism, the image of the second generator $T - Y^4$
in $R[Y,Z,T]$ is $T - Y^4$. Quotienting further,
$$R[Y,Z,T]/(T - Y^4) \cong R[Y,Z],$$
via $T \mapsto Y^4$.

**Step 3.** The image of the third generator $T^3 - Z$ under the substitutions $
T = Y^4$ becomes
$$T^3 - Z = (Y^4)^3 - Z = Y^{12} - Z.$$
So in $R[Y,Z]$ we quotient by $Y^{12} - Z$, i.e., $Z = Y^{12}$. This gives
$$R[Y,Z]/(Z - Y^{12}) \cong R[Y],$$
via $Z \mapsto Y^{12}$.

**Verification that the elimination is complete.** Define the surjective homomor
phism
$$\varphi: R[X,Y,Z,T] \to R[Y], \qquad X \mapsto Y^2,\; Y \mapsto Y,\; Z \mapsto
 Y^{12},\; T \mapsto Y^4.$$
Clearly $I \subseteq \ker\varphi$ since $\varphi(X-Y^2)=0$, $\varphi(T-Y^4)=0$,
$\varphi(T^3-Z)=Y^{12}-Y^{12}=0$.

Conversely, the generators $\{X - Y^2,\; T - Y^4,\; T^3 - Z\}$ form a Gröbner ba
sis for $I$ with respect to a monomial order eliminating $X, T, Z$ (e.g., lex or
der with $X > T > Z > Y$): their leading monomials $X, T, Z$ are pairwise coprim
e, so no $S$-polynomial reduction is needed. Hence every residue class in $R[X,Y
,Z,T]/I$ has a unique representative in $R[Y]$, obtained by replacing $X \to Y^2
$, $T \to Y^4$, $Z \to Y^{12}$. Therefore $\ker\varphi = I$, and by the first is
omorphism theorem,

$$R[X,Y,Z,T]/(X-Y^2,\; T-Y^4,\; T^3-Z) \;\cong\; R[Y].$$

The quotient ring is isomorphic to **$R[Y]$, the polynomial ring in one variable
 over $R$**.
