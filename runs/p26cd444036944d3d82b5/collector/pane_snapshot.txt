where $\{g^{ij}\}$ is the inverse of the matrix of $g$ in local coordinates $\{x
^i\}$ on $M$, and $\{\partial_i\}$ are the corresponding coordinate vectors.

**Using the immersion hypothesis.** We are given that $F:(N,h)\to(Q,q)$ is a pse

By the chain rule, $d(F\circ f)_x = dF_{f(x)} \circ df_x$, so

$$d(F\circ f)_x(\partial_i) = dF_{f(x)}\!\big(df_x(\partial_i)\big).$$
