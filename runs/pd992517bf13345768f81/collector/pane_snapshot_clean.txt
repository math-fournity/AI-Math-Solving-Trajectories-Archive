# Derivation of the Ricci Identity from the Parallel-Transport Definition of the
 Riemann Tensor

## 1. Setup and conventions

Let $(M, g)$ be a spacetime manifold with the Levi-Civita connection $\Gamma^{\m
u}{}_{\nu\rho}$ (torsion-free, metric-compatible). We use the convention

$$\nabla_{\rho} V^{\mu} = \partial_{\rho} V^{\mu} + \Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\rho}\, V
^{\nu},$$

so the parallel-transport equation along a curve $x^{\rho}(\lambda)$ is

$$\frac{dV^{\mu}}{d\lambda} + \Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\rho}\, V^{\nu}\,\frac{dx^{\rho
}}{d\lambda} = 0. \tag{1}$$

---

## 2. Definition of the Riemann tensor via parallel transport around an infinite
simal loop

At a point $p$ with coordinates $x^{\mu}$, choose two infinitesimal displacement
 vectors $\delta A^{\mu}$ and $\delta B^{\mu}$ and form the parallelogram with v
ertices

$$p \;\to\; p+\delta A \;\to\; p+\delta A+\delta B \;\to\; p+\delta B \;\to\; p.
$$

Parallel-transport a vector $V^{\mu}$ around this loop. The **Riemann curvature
tensor** is *defined* by the resulting change

$$\boxed{\Delta V^{\mu} \;=\; -\,R^{\mu}{}_{\nu\lambda\rho}\, V^{\nu}\,\delta A^
{\lambda}\,\delta B^{\rho}} \tag{2}$$

to second order in the displacements. We now compute the left-hand side explicit
ly and read off $R^{\mu}{}_{\nu\lambda\rho}$.

---

## 3. Explicit computation of the holonomy around the loop

For a small displacement $\delta x^{\rho}$, equation (1) gives the first-order c
hange

$$\delta V^{\mu} = -\,\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\rho}\, V^{\nu}\,\delta x^{\rho},$$

but $\Gamma$ must be evaluated at the *current* point, so its variation along th
e path contributes at second order. We track each leg, keeping terms up to $\mat
hcal{O}(\delta A\,\delta B)$.

### Leg 1: $p \to p+\delta A$ (displacement $\delta A^{\rho}$, connection at $p$
)

$$\Delta_{1} V^{\mu} = -\,\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\rho}\, V^{\nu}\,\delta A^{\rho}. \
tag{3}$$

### Leg 2: $p+\delta A \to p+\delta A+\delta B$ (displacement $\delta B^{\rho}$,
 connection at $p+\delta A$)

Here $\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\rho}(p+\delta A) = \Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\rho} + \partial
_{\lambda}\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\rho}\,\delta A^{\lambda}$, and the vector entering
 is $V^{\nu}+\Delta_{1}V^{\nu}$:

$$\Delta_{2} V^{\mu} = -\bigl(\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\rho} + \partial_{\lambda}\Gamm
a^{\mu}{}_{\nu\rho}\,\delta A^{\lambda}\bigr)\bigl(V^{\nu} - \Gamma^{\nu}{}_{\al
pha\beta}\,V^{\alpha}\,\delta A^{\beta}\bigr)\delta B^{\rho}.$$

Expanding to second order (relabel $\alpha\to\nu$, $\beta\to\lambda$ in the $\Ga
mma\Gamma$ term):

$$\Delta_{2} V^{\mu} = -\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\rho}\,V^{\nu}\,\delta B^{\rho} - \pa
rtial_{\lambda}\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\rho}\,V^{\nu}\,\delta A^{\lambda}\delta B^{\r
ho} + \Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\sigma\rho}\,\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\nu\lambda}\,V^{\nu}\,\del
ta A^{\lambda}\delta B^{\rho}. \tag{4}$$

### Leg 3: $p+\delta A+\delta B \to p+\delta B$ (displacement $-\delta A^{\rho}$
, connection at $p+\delta A+\delta B$)

$\Gamma(p+\delta A+\delta B) = \Gamma + \partial_{\lambda}\Gamma\,(\delta A^{\la
mbda}+\delta B^{\lambda})$. The vector entering has first-order part $V^{\nu} +
\Delta_{1}V^{\nu}+\Delta_{2}V^{\nu}$; only the first-order parts of $\Delta_{1},
\Delta_{2}$ matter at second order:

$$\Delta_{3} V^{\mu} = +\bigl(\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\rho}+\partial_{\lambda}\Gamma^
{\mu}{}_{\nu\rho}(\delta A^{\lambda}+\delta B^{\lambda})\bigr)\bigl(V^{\nu} - \G
amma^{\nu}{}_{\alpha\beta}V^{\alpha}\delta A^{\beta} - \Gamma^{\nu}{}_{\alpha\be
ta}V^{\alpha}\delta B^{\beta}\bigr)\delta A^{\rho}.$$

Collecting second-order terms and relabeling:

$$\Delta_{3} V^{\mu} = +\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\rho}\,V^{\nu}\,\delta A^{\rho} - \Ga
mma^{\mu}{}_{\sigma\rho}\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\nu\lambda}\,V^{\nu}\,\delta A^{\lamb
da}\delta A^{\rho} - \Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\sigma\lambda}\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\nu\rho}\,
V^{\nu}\,\delta B^{\rho}\delta A^{\lambda} + \partial_{\lambda}\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\
nu\rho}\,V^{\nu}\,\delta A^{\lambda}\delta A^{\rho} + \partial_{\rho}\Gamma^{\mu
}{}_{\nu\lambda}\,V^{\nu}\,\delta B^{\rho}\delta A^{\lambda}. \tag{5}$$

### Leg 4: $p+\delta B \to p$ (displacement $-\delta B^{\rho}$, connection at $p
+\delta B$)

$\Gamma(p+\delta B) = \Gamma + \partial_{\lambda}\Gamma\,\delta B^{\lambda}$. Th
e sum of first-order parts of $\Delta_{1}+\Delta_{2}+\Delta_{3}$ is $-\Gamma^{\n
u}{}_{\alpha\beta}V^{\alpha}\delta B^{\beta}$ (the $\delta A$ pieces cancel betw
een legs 1 and 3). Thus:

$$\Delta_{4} V^{\mu} = +\bigl(\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\rho}+\partial_{\lambda}\Gamma^
{\mu}{}_{\nu\rho}\,\delta B^{\lambda}\bigr)\bigl(V^{\nu} - \Gamma^{\nu}{}_{\alph
a\beta}V^{\alpha}\delta B^{\beta}\bigr)\delta B^{\rho}.$$

$$\Delta_{4} V^{\mu} = +\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\rho}\,V^{\nu}\,\delta B^{\rho} - \Ga
mma^{\mu}{}_{\sigma\rho}\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\nu\lambda}\,V^{\nu}\,\delta B^{\lamb
da}\delta B^{\rho} + \partial_{\lambda}\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\rho}\,V^{\nu}\,\delta
 B^{\lambda}\delta B^{\rho}. \tag{6}$$

---

## 4. Summing the four legs

**First-order terms** (one factor of $\delta A$ or $\delta B$): from (3)–(6) the
y are

$$-\Gamma V\,\delta A - \Gamma V\,\delta B + \Gamma V\,\delta A + \Gamma V\,\del
ta B = 0,$$

as expected for a closed loop.

**Pure $\delta A\,\delta A$ and $\delta B\,\delta B$ terms** (from (5) and (6)):

$$\bigl(\partial_{\lambda}\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\rho} - \Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\sigma\rho}
\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\nu\lambda}\bigr)V^{\nu}\,\delta A^{\lambda}\delta A^{\rho} \
;+\; \bigl(\partial_{\lambda}\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\rho} - \Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\sigma\r
ho}\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\nu\lambda}\bigr)V^{\nu}\,\delta B^{\lambda}\delta B^{\rho
}.$$

Each coefficient is **antisymmetric** under $\lambda\leftrightarrow\rho$ (one ch
ecks: swapping gives $\partial_{\rho}\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\lambda}-\Gamma^{\mu}{}_
{\sigma\lambda}\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\nu\rho}$, which is the negative), while $\del
ta A^{\lambda}\delta A^{\rho}$ and $\delta B^{\lambda}\delta B^{\rho}$ are **sym
metric**. Their contraction therefore **vanishes identically**.

**Cross terms $\delta A^{\lambda}\delta B^{\rho}$** survive. Collecting from (4)
 and (5):

$$\Delta V^{\mu}\Big|_{\text{cross}} = \Bigl[-\partial_{\lambda}\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{
\nu\rho} + \Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\sigma\rho}\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\nu\lambda} - \Gamma^{\
mu}{}_{\sigma\lambda}\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\nu\rho} + \partial_{\rho}\Gamma^{\mu}{}
_{\nu\lambda}\Bigr] V^{\nu}\,\delta A^{\lambda}\delta B^{\rho}.$$

Reordering:

$$\Delta V^{\mu} = -\Bigl[\partial_{\lambda}\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\rho} - \partial_
{\rho}\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\lambda} + \Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\sigma\lambda}\Gamma^{\sigma
}{}_{\nu\rho} - \Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\sigma\rho}\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\nu\lambda}\Bigr]
V^{\nu}\,\delta A^{\lambda}\delta B^{\rho}. \tag{7}$$

Comparing with the definition (2), we read off the **Riemann tensor in terms of
the connection**:

$$\boxed{R^{\mu}{}_{\nu\lambda\rho} = \partial_{\lambda}\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\rho}
 - \partial_{\rho}\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\lambda} + \Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\sigma\lambda}\,
\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\nu\rho} - \Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\sigma\rho}\,\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\n
u\lambda}}. \tag{8}$$

---

## 5. The commutator of covariant derivatives

Now compute $[\nabla_{\lambda},\nabla_{\rho}]\,V^{\mu} = \nabla_{\lambda}(\nabla
_{\rho}V^{\mu}) - \nabla_{\rho}(\nabla_{\lambda}V^{\mu})$, treating $\nabla_{\rh
o}V^{\mu}$ as a $(1,1)$ tensor (upper $\mu$, lower $\rho$):

$$\nabla_{\lambda}(\nabla_{\rho}V^{\mu}) = \partial_{\lambda}(\partial_{\rho}V^{
\mu}+\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\rho}V^{\nu}) + \Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\sigma\lambda}(\partial_
{\rho}V^{\sigma}+\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\nu\rho}V^{\nu}) - \Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\rho\l
ambda}(\partial_{\sigma}V^{\mu}+\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\sigma}V^{\nu}). \tag{9}$$

Swapping $\lambda\leftrightarrow\rho$ gives $\nabla_{\rho}(\nabla_{\lambda}V^{\m
u})$. Subtracting:

**Terms with $\partial\partial V$:** $\partial_{\lambda}\partial_{\rho}V^{\mu}-\
partial_{\rho}\partial_{\lambda}V^{\mu}=0$ (partial derivatives commute).

**Terms with $\Gamma\,\partial V$ (from the upper index):**

$$\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\rho}\,\partial_{\lambda}V^{\nu} - \Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\lamb
da}\,\partial_{\rho}V^{\nu} + \Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\sigma\lambda}\,\partial_{\rho}V^{
\sigma} - \Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\sigma\rho}\,\partial_{\lambda}V^{\sigma} = 0$$

after relabeling $\sigma\to\nu$ in the last two terms — they cancel pairwise.

**Terms with $\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\rho\lambda}\,\partial_{\sigma}V^{\mu}$ (from t
he lower index):**

$$-\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\rho\lambda}\,\partial_{\sigma}V^{\mu} + \Gamma^{\sigma}{}
_{\lambda\rho}\,\partial_{\sigma}V^{\mu} = \bigl(\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\lambda\rho}
-\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\rho\lambda}\bigr)\partial_{\sigma}V^{\mu} = T^{\sigma}{}_{\
lambda\rho}\,\partial_{\sigma}V^{\mu},$$

where $T^{\sigma}{}_{\lambda\rho}$ is the torsion. For the **Levi-Civita connect
ion of GR, $T^{\sigma}{}_{\lambda\rho}=0$**, so this vanishes.

**Terms with $(\partial\Gamma)\,V$:**

$$\bigl(\partial_{\lambda}\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\rho} - \partial_{\rho}\Gamma^{\mu}
{}_{\nu\lambda}\bigr)V^{\nu}.$$

**Terms with $\Gamma\Gamma\,V$ (from the upper index):**

$$\bigl(\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\sigma\lambda}\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\nu\rho} - \Gamma^{\mu}
{}_{\sigma\rho}\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\nu\lambda}\bigr)V^{\nu}.$$

**Terms with $\Gamma\Gamma\,V$ (from the lower index):**

$$-\bigl(\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\rho\lambda}\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\sigma} - \Gamma^{\si
gma}{}_{\lambda\rho}\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\sigma}\bigr)V^{\nu} = -T^{\sigma}{}_{\la
mbda\rho}\,\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\sigma}\,V^{\nu} = 0$$

again by torsion-freeness.

---

## 6. The Ricci identity

Assembling the surviving terms and using torsion-freeness ($T=0$):

$$[\nabla_{\lambda},\,\nabla_{\rho}]\,V^{\mu} = \Bigl[\partial_{\lambda}\Gamma^{
\mu}{}_{\nu\rho} - \partial_{\rho}\Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\nu\lambda} + \Gamma^{\mu}{}_{
\sigma\lambda}\,\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\nu\rho} - \Gamma^{\mu}{}_{\sigma\rho}\,\Gamm
a^{\sigma}{}_{\nu\lambda}\Bigr] V^{\nu}.$$

The bracket is precisely the expression (8) for $R^{\mu}{}_{\nu\lambda\rho}$ obt
ained from the parallel-transport definition. Therefore:

$$\boxed{[\nabla_{\lambda},\,\nabla_{\rho}]\,V^{\mu} \;=\; R^{\mu}{}_{\nu\lambda
\rho}\,V^{\nu}}. \tag{10}$$

This is the **Ricci identity** for a contravariant vector field.

---

## 7. Consistency check: the two definitions agree

The parallel-transport computation (Section 4) yielded

$$\Delta V^{\mu} = -\,R^{\mu}{}_{\nu\lambda\rho}\,V^{\nu}\,\delta A^{\lambda}\,\
delta B^{\rho},$$

while the commutator (Section 5–6) gives

$$[\nabla_{\lambda},\nabla_{\rho}]\,V^{\mu} = R^{\mu}{}_{\nu\lambda\rho}\,V^{\nu
}.$$

The geometric content is identical: going around the infinitesimal parallelogram
 "$\delta A$ then $\delta B$ then $-\delta A$ then $-\delta B$" produces the sam
e mismatch as the algebraic failure of $\nabla_{\lambda}\nabla_{\rho}$ to commut
e, up to the orientation sign encoded in the loop direction. Concretely,

$$\Delta V^{\mu} = -\,[\nabla_{\lambda},\nabla_{\rho}]\,V^{\mu}\;\delta A^{\lamb
da}\,\delta B^{\rho},$$

which is the precise sense in which **curvature = holonomy of an infinitesimal l
oop = non-commutativity of covariant differentiation**.

---

## 8. Remark on the general (torsionful) case

For a connection with torsion $T^{\sigma}{}_{\lambda\rho} = \Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\
lambda\rho}-\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\rho\lambda}$, the terms discarded in Section 5 s
urvive and the identity generalizes to

$$[\nabla_{\lambda},\nabla_{\rho}]\,V^{\mu} = R^{\mu}{}_{\nu\lambda\rho}\,V^{\nu
} - T^{\sigma}{}_{\lambda\rho}\,\nabla_{\sigma}V^{\mu},$$

where $R^{\mu}{}_{\nu\lambda\rho}$ is still given by (8). In general relativity
the connection is the torsion-free Levi-Civita connection, so the extra term dro
ps out and (10) is the Ricci identity. $\blacksquare$
