## Proof

Let $X = \mathbb{CP}^3$ with homogeneous coordinates $[z_0 : z_1 : z_2 : z_3]$,
and let

$$F = z_0 z_1^2 + z_2 z_3(z_2 - z_3) = z_0 z_1^2 + z_2^2 z_3 - z_2 z_3^2.$$

**Step 1: $F$ is a homogeneous polynomial of degree 3.**

Each monomial has total degree $3$: $z_0 z_1^2$ has degree $1+2=3$, and $z_2 z_3
(z_2-z_3) = z_2^2 z_3 - z_2 z_3^2$ has degree $2+1=3$. Hence $F$ is homogeneous
of degree $d = 3$, and its zero locus $V(F) \subset \mathbb{CP}^3$ is a well-def
ined hypersurface of degree $3$.

**Step 2: The associated line bundle.**

By definition, the line bundle associated to the effective divisor $D = [F]$ is

$$L_D = \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{CP}^3}(D).$$

A standard fact: for a hypersurface $V(F) \subset \mathbb{CP}^n$ cut out by a ho
mogeneous polynomial of degree $d$, the associated line bundle satisfies

$$\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{CP}^n}(V(F)) \cong \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{CP}^n}(d).$$

Indeed, $F$ is a global section of $\mathcal{O}(d)$, and the divisor of zeros of
 this section is exactly $D = [F]$, giving $\mathcal{O}(D) \cong \mathcal{O}(d)$
 via the canonical isomorphism $\text{div}(s) \mapsto \mathcal{O}(\text{div}(s))
$ for a section $s \in H^0(\mathbb{CP}^n, \mathcal{O}(d))$.

Since $d = 3$ here:

$$L_D = \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{CP}^3}(D) \cong \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{CP}^3}(3).$$

**Step 3: Compute $c_1$.**

The cohomology ring of $\mathbb{CP}^3$ is

$$H^*(\mathbb{CP}^3; \mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}[h]/(h^4),$$

where $h = c_1(\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{CP}^3}(1))$ is the hyperplane class, the pos
itive generator of $H^2(\mathbb{CP}^3; \mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}$.

Chern classes are additive on tensor products of line bundles:

$$c_1(\mathcal{O}(a) \otimes \mathcal{O}(b)) = c_1(\mathcal{O}(a)) + c_1(\mathca
l{O}(b)).$$

Since $\mathcal{O}(3) = \mathcal{O}(1)^{\otimes 3}$ and $c_1(\mathcal{O}(1)) = h
$,

$$c_1(L_D) = c_1(\mathcal{O}(3)) = 3\,c_1(\mathcal{O}(1)) = 3h.$$

**Step 4: Consistency check via the divisor–Chern class relation.**

For any effective Cartier divisor $D$ on a smooth variety, the first Chern class
 of the associated line bundle equals the Poincaré dual of the fundamental class
 of $D$:

$$c_1(\mathcal{O}(D)) = \operatorname{PD}([D]) \in H^2(X; \mathbb{Z}).$$

A degree-$d$ hypersurface in $\mathbb{CP}^3$ represents $d$ times the hyperplane
 class in $H_2$ (equivalently, its Poincaré dual is $d\,h \in H^2$). With $d = 3
$, this gives $c_1(L_D) = 3h$, confirming the computation above.

## Result

$$\boxed{c_1(L_D) = 3\,h \in H^2(\mathbb{CP}^3;\mathbb{Z}),}$$

where $h = c_1(\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{CP}^3}(1))$ is the hyperplane class (the pos
itive generator of $H^2(\mathbb{CP}^3;\mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}$). Equivalent
ly, $c_1(L_D) = c_1(\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{CP}^3}(3))$.
