# Problem

In the city of Graphia, a specialized power grid is composed of $n$ interconnected substations. A "Power Cell" is formed whenever exactly three substations are all directly connected to one another by transmission lines. 

The city council has established a strict safety protocol: the grid must be configured such that it is impossible to find two Power Cells that are completely separate. In other words, every pair of Power Cells in the grid must share at least one substation in common.

For any given number of substations $n \geq 3$, let $f(n)$ represent the maximum possible number of Power Cells that can exist within a single grid while still adhering to the safety protocol.

Calculate the total sum of these maximum values as the number of substations increases from 3 to 100:
$$\sum_{n=3}^{100} f(n)$$
