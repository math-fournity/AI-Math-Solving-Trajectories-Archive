# Problem

An elite security firm is designing a surveillance protocol for a square $7 \times 7$ grid of high-security storage vaults. Each vault is identified by its row number and column number, both ranging from $1$ to $7$.

The firm needs to station exactly $8$ robotic sentries within this grid, with each sentry occupying a single vault. Due to the high-frequency interference generated by the robots' sensors, a "Safety Buffer Rule" must be strictly followed:

For any two sentries, $X$ and $Y$, located at $(r_1, c_1)$ and $(r_2, c_2)$ respectively, the sentries must be sufficiently separated to avoid signal jamming. This means they must satisfy at least one of the following two conditions:
1. The vertical distance between them must be significant: $|r_1 - r_2| \geq 3$.
2. The horizontal distance between them must be significant: $|c_1 - c_2| \geq 3$.

In how many distinct ways can the $8$ vaults be selected to house these sentries while satisfying the Safety Buffer Rule?
