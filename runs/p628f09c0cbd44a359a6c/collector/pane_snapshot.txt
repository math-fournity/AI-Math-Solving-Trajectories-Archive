## Solution

**Setup.** Let $X_t$ be a Brownian bridge from $0$ to $0$ on $[0,1]$, i.e., $X_t
 = W_t - tW_1$ where $W$ is standard Brownian motion. Its covariance is $\operat
orname{Cov}(X_s, X_t) = \min(s,t) - st$.

**Goal.** Compute $E\!\left[e^{iu(X_{4/5}-\frac{1}{2}X_{3/5})}\mid X_{3/5}\right
]$.

---

**Step 1: Conditional distribution of $X_{4/5}\mid X_{3/5}$.**

The Brownian bridge is a Gaussian process, so the conditional distribution is Ga
ussian. Using the Markov property: given $X_s = x$, the future $\{X_t\}_{t\ge s}
$ is a Brownian bridge from $x$ to $0$ on $[s,1]$, giving

$$X_t \mid X_s = x \;\sim\; N\!\left(\frac{1-t}{1-s}\,x,\;\frac{(t-s)(1-t)}{1-s}
\right).$$

With $s = \tfrac{3}{5}$, $t = \tfrac{4}{5}$:

- **Mean:** $\dfrac{1-\tfrac{4}{5}}{1-\tfrac{3}{5}}\,x = \dfrac{1/5}{2/5}\,x = \
dfrac{x}{2}$.
- **Variance:** $\dfrac{(\tfrac{4}{5}-\tfrac{3}{5})(1-\tfrac{4}{5})}{1-\tfrac{3}
{5}} = \dfrac{(1/5)(1/5)}{2/5} = \dfrac{1/25}{2/5} = \dfrac{1}{10}$.

*Cross-check via covariance formulas:* $\operatorname{Cov}(X_{3/5},X_{4/5})=\tfr
ac{3}{5}-\tfrac{12}{25}=\tfrac{3}{25}$, $\operatorname{Var}(X_{3/5})=\tfrac{3}{5
}-\tfrac{9}{25}=\tfrac{6}{25}$, $\operatorname{Var}(X_{4/5})=\tfrac{4}{5}-\tfrac
{16}{25}=\tfrac{4}{25}$.

- Mean $= \tfrac{3/25}{6/25}x = \tfrac{x}{2}$. ✓
- Variance $= \tfrac{4}{25} - \tfrac{(3/25)^2}{6/25} = \tfrac{4}{25}-\tfrac{9/62
5}{6/25}=\tfrac{4}{25}-\tfrac{3}{50}=\tfrac{8-3}{50}=\tfrac{1}{10}$. ✓

So $X_{4/5}\mid X_{3/5}=x \;\sim\; N\!\left(\tfrac{x}{2},\,\tfrac{1}{10}\right)$
.

---

**Step 2: Conditional distribution of $X_{4/5}-\tfrac{1}{2}X_{3/5}\mid X_{3/5}=x
$.**

Since $X_{4/5}\mid X_{3/5}=x \sim N\!\left(\tfrac{x}{2},\tfrac{1}{10}\right)$, s
ubtracting the constant $\tfrac{x}{2}$ (which is $\tfrac{1}{2}X_{3/5}$ evaluated
 at $X_{3/5}=x$):

$$X_{4/5}-\tfrac{1}{2}X_{3/5}\;\Big|\;X_{3/5}=x \;\sim\; N\!\left(\tfrac{x}{2}-\
tfrac{x}{2},\,\tfrac{1}{10}\right) = N\!\left(0,\,\tfrac{1}{10}\right).$$

**Key observation:** The conditional distribution is $N(0,\,1/10)$, which is **i
ndependent of $x$** (hence independent of $X_{3/5}$).

---

**Step 3: Compute the conditional characteristic function.**

For $Y \sim N(0,\sigma^2)$, $E[e^{iuY}] = e^{-\frac{1}{2}u^2\sigma^2}$. With $\s
igma^2 = \tfrac{1}{10}$:

$$E\!\left[e^{iu(X_{4/5}-\frac{1}{2}X_{3/5})}\mid X_{3/5}\right] = e^{-\frac{1}{
2}u^2\cdot\frac{1}{10}} = e^{-u^2/20}.$$

---

**Result.**

$$\boxed{E\!\left[e^{iu(X_{4/5}-\frac{1}{2}X_{3/5})}\mid X_{3/5}\right] = e^{-u^
{2}/20}}$$

The conditional characteristic function is deterministic (does not depend on $X_
{3/5}$), reflecting that $X_{4/5}-\tfrac{1}{2}X_{3/5}$ is independent of $X_{3/5
}$ and distributed as $N(0,1/10)$.
