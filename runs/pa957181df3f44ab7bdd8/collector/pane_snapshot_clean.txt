**Note on the support.** The density $f(x;\theta)=\frac1\theta x^{(1-\theta)/\th
eta}$ does **not** integrate to 1 on $(0,\infty)$ for any $\theta>0$:

$$\int_0^\infty \tfrac1\theta x^{(1-\theta)/\theta}\,dx=\tfrac1\theta\int_0^\inf
ty x^{1/\theta}\,dx=\infty\quad(\text{since }1/\theta>0).$$

It is, however, a valid density on $(0,1)$:

$$\int_0^1 \tfrac1\theta x^{(1-\theta)/\theta}\,dx=\tfrac1\theta\cdot\frac{x^{1/
\theta}}{1/\theta}\Big|_0^1=1.$$

This is the standard form of this distribution (Hogg–McKean–Craig), so the state
d support $0<x<\infty$ is a typo for $0<x<1$. I solve the problem with the suppo
rt $(0,1)$, which is the only way the density is valid.

### Log-likelihood

Let $S=\sum_{i=1}^n \ln X_i$. Since $X_i\in(0,1)$, each $\ln X_i<0$.

$$\ell(\theta)=\sum_{i=1}^n\ln f(X_i;\theta)=-n\ln\theta+\frac{1-\theta}{\theta}
\,S=-n\ln\theta+\Bigl(\frac1\theta-1\Bigr)S.$$

### MLE

$$\frac{d\ell}{d\theta}=-\frac{n}{\theta}-\frac{S}{\theta^2}.$$

Setting the score to zero:

$$-\frac{n}{\theta}-\frac{S}{\theta^2}=0\;\Longrightarrow\;-n\theta-S=0\;\Longri
ghtarrow\;\hat\theta=-\frac{S}{n}=-\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\ln X_i>0.$$

### Fisher information

$$\frac{d^2\ell}{d\theta^2}=\frac{n}{\theta^2}+\frac{2S}{\theta^3}.$$

To take the expectation we need $E[\ln X]$. Set $Z=-\ln X\ge 0$. With $x=e^{-z}$
, $dx=-e^{-z}dz$,

$$f_Z(z)=\frac1\theta\,e^{-z(1-\theta)/\theta}\,e^{-z}=\frac1\theta\,e^{-z/\thet
a},\qquad z>0.$$

Thus $Z\sim\operatorname{Exp}(\text{rate }1/\theta)$, so $E[Z]=\theta$, i.e. $E[
\ln X]=-E[Z]=-\theta$, and

$$E[S]=\sum_{i=1}^n E[\ln X_i]=-n\theta.$$

Therefore the Fisher information is

$$I(\theta)=-E\!\left[\frac{d^2\ell}{d\theta^2}\right]=-\frac{n}{\theta^2}-\frac
{2}{\theta^3}E[S]=-\frac{n}{\theta^2}-\frac{2}{\theta^3}(-n\theta)=-\frac{n}{\th
eta^2}+\frac{2n}{\theta^2}=\frac{n}{\theta^2}.$$

### Rao–Cramér lower bound (asymptotic variance)

$$\operatorname{Var}_{\text{asym}}(\hat\theta)\ge\frac{1}{I(\theta)}=\frac{\thet
a^2}{n}.$$

### Efficiency check (the bound is attained)

Since $\hat\theta=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n Z_i$ with $Z_i=-\ln X_i\overset{\text{iid}
}{\sim}\operatorname{Exp}(1/\theta)$, we have $E[Z_i]=\theta$ and $\operatorname
{Var}(Z_i)=\theta^2$. Hence

$$E[\hat\theta]=\theta,\qquad \operatorname{Var}(\hat\theta)=\frac{\operatorname
{Var}(Z_i)}{n}=\frac{\theta^2}{n}.$$

By the CLT, $\hat\theta\overset{\text{asym}}{\sim}N\!\bigl(\theta,\,\theta^2/n\b
igr)$. The asymptotic variance equals the Cramér–Rao lower bound, so $\hat\theta
$ is efficient.
