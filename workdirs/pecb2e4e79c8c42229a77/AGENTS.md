# Problem

A specialized logistics company, Delta-Supply, operates three distribution hubs positioned at coordinates $A, B,$ and $C$, forming a triangle. A central maintenance depot, $M$, is located exactly at the midpoint of the direct transport route $AB$. To monitor regional traffic, a satellite tracking station, $O$, has been placed at the circumcenter of the triangle formed by the three hubs (the point equidistant from $A, B,$ and $C$).

The company maps its coverage using a circular signal zone that passes through hub $A$, maintenance depot $M$, and the tracking station $O$. Structural analysis of the signal grid reveals that this specific circular zone perfectly bisects the straight-line supply path $CM$ connecting the third hub to the maintenance depot.

The company requires the lengths of the three direct transport routes between the hubs ($AB, BC,$ and $CA$) to be represented by whole numbers of kilometers. Based on these geometric constraints, what is the smallest possible total length (perimeter), in kilometers, of the triangle formed by the three distribution hubs?
