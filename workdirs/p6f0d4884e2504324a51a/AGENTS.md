# Problem

14 students attend the IMO training camp. Every student has at least $k$ favourite numbers. The organisers want to give each student a shirt with one of the student's favourite numbers on the back.
Let $k_a$ be the least $k$ such that the students can always be arranged in a circle such that every two students sitting next to one another have different numbers.
Let $k_b$ be the least $k$ such that if 7 of the students are boys, the rest are girls, and there isn't a boy and a girl with the same favourite number, then it is always possible to assign each student one of their favourite numbers.
Calculate $10k_a + k_b$.
