Consider $h_\lambda(m,n) = e^{i\lambda mn}$. Then for elementary tensors:
$$\sum_{m,n} e^{i\lambda mn} a_m b_n = \sum_m a_m \sum_n e^{i\lambda mn} b_n =
\sum_m a_m \hat{b}(\lambda m)$$
