## Solution

We are to minimize
$$f(a)=\int_0^1 |ax+x\ln x|\,dx,\qquad a>0.$$

Since $x>0$ on $(0,1]$, we have $|ax+x\ln x|=x|a+\ln x|$.

**Locating the sign change.** The expression $a+\ln x$ vanishes at $x=e^{-a}$, w
hich lies in $(0,1)$ for every $a>0$. Moreover $a+\ln x<0$ for $x<e^{-a}$ and $a
+\ln x>0$ for $x>e^{-a}$. Hence
$$f(a)=\int_0^{e^{-a}} x(-a-\ln x)\,dx+\int_{e^{-a}}^1 x(a+\ln x)\,dx.$$

**Computing the antiderivatives.** Using $\int x\ln x\,dx=\frac{x^2}{2}\ln x-\fr
ac{x^2}{4}$:

- Antiderivative of $x(-a-\ln x)$ is $-\frac{ax^2}{2}-\frac{x^2}{2}\ln x+\frac{x
^2}{4}$.
- Antiderivative of $x(a+\ln x)$ is $\frac{ax^2}{2}+\frac{x^2}{2}\ln x-\frac{x^2
}{4}$.

**Evaluating the first integral.** At $x=e^{-a}$ (where $\ln x=-a$):
$$-\frac{ae^{-2a}}{2}-\frac{e^{-2a}}{2}(-a)+\frac{e^{-2a}}{4}=-\frac{ae^{-2a}}{2
}+\frac{ae^{-2a}}{2}+\frac{e^{-2a}}{4}=\frac{e^{-2a}}{4}.$$
The lower limit $x\to 0^+$ gives $0$ (since $x^2\ln x\to 0$). Thus
$$I_1=\frac{e^{-2a}}{4}.$$

**Evaluating the second integral.** At $x=1$: $\frac{a}{2}+0-\frac{1}{4}=\frac{a
}{2}-\frac{1}{4}$. At $x=e^{-a}$:
$$\frac{ae^{-2a}}{2}+\frac{e^{-2a}}{2}(-a)-\frac{e^{-2a}}{4}=\frac{ae^{-2a}}{2}-
\frac{ae^{-2a}}{2}-\frac{e^{-2a}}{4}=-\frac{e^{-2a}}{4}.$$
So
$$I_2=\Big(\frac{a}{2}-\frac{1}{4}\Big)-\Big(-\frac{e^{-2a}}{4}\Big)=\frac{a}{2}
-\frac{1}{4}+\frac{e^{-2a}}{4}.$$

**Closed form.** Adding,
$$f(a)=\frac{e^{-2a}}{4}+\frac{a}{2}-\frac{1}{4}+\frac{e^{-2a}}{4}=\frac{a}{2}+\
frac{e^{-2a}}{2}-\frac{1}{4}.$$

**Sanity checks.** As $a\to 0^+$: $f(0)=0+\frac12-\frac14=\frac14=\int_0^1 x(-\l
n x)\,dx$. ✓ As $a\to\infty$: $f(a)\sim \frac{a}{2}-\frac14\to\infty$. ✓

**Minimizing.** Differentiate:
$$f'(a)=\frac12-e^{-2a},\qquad f''(a)=2e^{-2a}>0.$$
Setting $f'(a)=0$ gives $e^{-2a}=\frac12$, i.e. $e^{2a}=2$, so
$$a^\*=\frac{\ln 2}{2}.$$
Since $f''>0$ everywhere, this unique critical point is the global minimum on $(
0,\infty)$.

**Minimum value.** At $a^\*=\frac{\ln 2}{2}$, we have $e^{-2a^\*}=e^{-\ln 2}=\fr
ac12$, so
$$f(a^\*)=\frac{\ln 2}{4}+\frac{1/2}{2}-\frac14=\frac{\ln 2}{4}+\frac14-\frac14=
\frac{\ln 2}{4}.$$

The minimum possible value of the total yield is

$$\boxed{\dfrac{\ln 2}{4}}.$$
