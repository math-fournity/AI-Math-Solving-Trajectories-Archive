## Setting Up the Characterization

Two distinct points $A, B \in S$ are $k$-friends iff there exists $C \in S$ with

A = (p, q)$ and $C - A = (r, s)$. The area condition becomes $|ps - qr| = 2k$.

Let $d = \gcd(p, q)$, write $p = dp'$, $q = dq'$ with $\gcd(p', q') = 1$. Then $
ps - qr = d(p's - q'r)$. Since $\gcd(p', q') = 1$, by Bézout's identity the set
$\{p's - q'r : r, s \in \mathbb{Z}\} = \mathbb{Z}$. So $\{ps - qr\} = d\mathbb{Z

> **$A, B$ are $k$-friends iff $\gcd(B_x - A_x,\, B_y - A_y) \mid 2k$.**

**Step 2: Reformulate the $k$-clique condition.**

$$\gcd(\Delta x, \Delta y) \mid 2k \iff \forall\, p \text{ prime}: \min\!\big(v_

$$\iff \forall\, p: A \not\equiv B \pmod{p^{a_p+1}} \text{ in } \left(\mathbb{Z}
/p^{a_p+1}\mathbb{Z}\right)^2.$$

> **$T$ is a $k$-clique iff for every prime $p$, the points of $T$ are pairwise
distinct modulo $p^{a_p+1}$**, where $a_p = v_p(2k)$.

## Finding the Maximum Clique Size

$$|T| \le \min_p \, p^{2(a_p+1)}.$$

**Sufficiency (construction).** Suppose $\min_p \, p^{2(a_p+1)} \ge n$. We const
ruct $n$ points $\{(i, f(i)) : i = 0, \dots, n-1\}$ for a suitable $f$.

For each prime $p$, the constraint is: within each residue class of $i$ mod $q_p

> **The maximum $k$-clique size equals $\min_p \, p^{2(v_p(2k)+1)}$.**
