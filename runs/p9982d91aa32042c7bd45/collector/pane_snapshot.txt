$$c_{S^n} \circ \eta_n = (\eta_n \vee \eta_n) \circ c_{S^{n+1}}.$$

$$(f + g) \circ \alpha = \nabla \circ (f \vee g) \circ c_{S^n} \circ \alpha = \n
