## Solution

The maximum value is $\boxed{1}$.

**Proof.**

Let $S_0 = 0$ and $S_m = c_1 + c_2 + \cdots + c_m$ for $1 \le m \le n$, so $c_i
= S_i - S_{i-1}$. By hypothesis $0 \le S_m \le m^k$ (non-negativity since each $
c_i \ge 0$).

**Step 1: Abel summation.** Using $c_i = S_i - S_{i-1}$,

$$
\sum_{i=1}^n c_i a_i^k = \sum_{i=1}^n (S_i - S_{i-1})a_i^k = S_n a_n^k + \sum_{i
=1}^{n-1} S_i\bigl(a_i^k - a_{i+1}^k\bigr).
$$

Since $a_1 \ge a_2 \ge \cdots \ge a_n \ge 0$ and $k>1$, each difference $a_i^k -
 a_{i+1}^k \ge 0$. Together with $0 \le S_i \le i^k$,

$$
\sum_{i=1}^n c_i a_i^k \le n^k a_n^k + \sum_{i=1}^{n-1} i^k\bigl(a_i^k - a_{i+1}
^k\bigr).
$$

Telescoping the right-hand side (re-indexing $\sum_{i=1}^{n-1} i^k a_{i+1}^k = \
sum_{j=2}^n (j-1)^k a_j^k$),

$$
n^k a_n^k + \sum_{i=1}^{n-1} i^k(a_i^k - a_{i+1}^k)
= a_1^k + \sum_{i=2}^n \bigl(i^k - (i-1)^k\bigr)a_i^k
= \sum_{i=1}^n w_i\, a_i^k,
$$

where $w_i = i^k - (i-1)^k$ (with $0^k = 0$). Note $w_i > 0$ and $\sum_{i=1}^n w
_i = n^k$.

**Step 2: Bounding $\sum w_i a_i^k \le 1$.** The function $F(a_1,\dots,a_n) = \s
um_{i=1}^n w_i a_i^k$ is **convex**, since each $a_i \mapsto w_i a_i^k$ is conve
x on $[0,\infty)$ for $k>1$ and $w_i>0$.

The feasible region
$$
\mathcal{P} = \bigl\{a \in \mathbb{R}^n : a_1 \ge a_2 \ge \cdots \ge a_n \ge 0,\
 \textstyle\sum a_i = 1\bigr\}
$$
is a compact convex polytope. A convex function on a compact convex polytope att
ains its maximum at a vertex.

**Vertices of $\mathcal{P}$.** A vertex requires $n-1$ of the inequalities $\{a_
i \ge a_{i+1}\}_{i=1}^{n-1} \cup \{a_n \ge 0\}$ to be tight (together with $\sum
 a_i = 1$). Tracing through the possibilities: either all $a_i = a_{i+1}$ are ti
ght (giving $a_1 = \cdots = a_n = 1/n$), or $a_n = 0$ is tight and a contiguous
suffix is forced to zero while a contiguous prefix is forced equal. Concretely t
he vertices are exactly
$$
v^{(j)} = \Bigl(\underbrace{\tfrac{1}{j},\ldots,\tfrac{1}{j}}_{j},\,0,\ldots,0\B
igr), \qquad j = 1,2,\ldots,n.
$$
(Any non-contiguous choice of tight equalities collapses to one of these, since
$a_i = a_{i+1}$ propagates equality within a block and $a_n=0$ propagates zeros
rightward.)

**Value at each vertex:**
$$
F\bigl(v^{(j)}\bigr) = \sum_{i=1}^j w_i \cdot \frac{1}{j^k} = \frac{1}{j^k}\sum_
{i=1}^j \bigl(i^k - (i-1)^k\bigr) = \frac{j^k}{j^k} = 1.
$$

Since $F$ is convex and equals $1$ at every vertex of $\mathcal{P}$, we have $F(
a) \le 1$ for all $a \in \mathcal{P}$. Combining with Step 1,

$$
\sum_{i=1}^n c_i a_i^k \le \sum_{i=1}^n w_i a_i^k \le 1.
$$

**Step 3: Achievability.** Take $c_i = i^k - (i-1)^k$, so $S_m = m^k$ (equality
in the constraint). Take $a_1 = 1$ and $a_2 = \cdots = a_n = 0$ (valid: decreasi
ng, sum $1$). Then
$$
\sum_{i=1}^n c_i a_i^k = c_1 \cdot 1^k = 1^k - 0^k = 1.
$$

Hence the maximum value is $\boxed{1}$.
