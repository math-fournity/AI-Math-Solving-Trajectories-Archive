$$
u_x(0,y,t)=0,\quad u_x(\ell,y,t)=0,\quad u_y(x,0,t)=0,\quad u_y(x,w,t)=0,
$$
i.e. $\partial_n u=0$ on the whole boundary. (The argument ordering in the state

### 1. Separation of variables

Seek non-trivial solutions of $u_t=u_{xx}+u_{yy}$ of the form $u=X(x)Y(y)T(t)$.
Substitution gives
$$
XYT' = X''YT + XY''T \;\Longrightarrow\; \frac{T'}{T}=\frac{X''}{X}+\frac{Y''}{Y
}=-\lambda,
$$
with $\lambda$ a separation constant. Split further: $X''/X=-\mu$, $Y''/Y=-\nu$,
 so $\lambda=\mu+\nu$.

### 2. Eigenvalue problems in $x$ and $y$

**$x$-problem:** $X''+\mu X=0$, $X'(0)=X'(\ell)=0$.
- $\mu_0=0$: $X_0(x)=1$.
- $\mu_m=(m\pi/\ell)^2$, $m=1,2,\dots$: $X_m(x)=\cos(m\pi x/\ell)$.

**$y$-problem:** $Y''+\nu Y=0$, $Y'(0)=Y'(w)=0$.
- $\nu_0=0$: $Y_0(y)=1$.
- $\nu_n=(n\pi/w)^2$, $n=1,2,\dots$: $Y_n(y)=\cos(n\pi y/w)$.

These are complete orthogonal families on $[0,\ell]$ and $[0,w]$ respectively (s
tandard Sturm–Liouville with Neumann data).
