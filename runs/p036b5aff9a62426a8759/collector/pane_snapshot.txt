$$x^2 + y^2 + 8a^2 = 4 + 4a(x+y)$$
$$x^2 - 4ax + y^2 - 4ay + 8a^2 - 4 = 0$$
Completing squares:
$$(x-2a)^2 + (y-2a)^2 = 4.$$

So equation 2 is a **circle of radius 2 centered at $(2a, 2a)$**. As $a$ ranges
over $\mathbb{R}$, the center moves along the line $y = x$.

**Step 2: Reformulate the existence condition.**

We need: there exists $a$ such that the parabola $y = -x^2 - b$ (equation 1) mee

Equivalently: there exists a point $P=(x_0, y_0)$ on the parabola that lies on s

A point $P$ lies on some circle of this family iff there is a center $C$ on line

y=x$ is $\le 2$.

The distance from $(x_0, y_0)$ to line $x - y = 0$ is $\dfrac{|x_0 - y_0|}{\sqrt
{2}}$. So the condition is:
$$|x_0 - y_0| \le 2\sqrt{2}.$$

**Step 3: Reduce to a one-variable condition.**

On the parabola, $y_0 = -x_0^2 - b$, so
$$x_0 - y_0 = x_0 + x_0^2 + b.$$

We need: there exists $x_0 \in \mathbb{R}$ with
$$|x_0^2 + x_0 + b| \le 2\sqrt{2}.$$

Let $f(x) = x^2 + x + b = \left(x+\tfrac12\right)^2 + b - \tfrac14$. Its minimum

**Step 4: Find when $\min_x |f(x)| \le 2\sqrt{2}$.**

(x)| = b - \tfrac14$. The condition becomes
$$b - \tfrac14 \le 2\sqrt{2} \iff b \le \tfrac14 + 2\sqrt{2}.$$

- For $b < \tfrac14$: always works.
- For $\tfrac14 \le b \le \tfrac14 + 2\sqrt{2}$: works.
