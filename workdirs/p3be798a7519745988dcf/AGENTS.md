# Problem

In a sprawling city designed on a triangular grid, a drone is programmed to lay down fiber-optic cables in straight paths of exactly one kilometer each. The drone begins its mission at a central hub (the origin), initially facing due North.

The drone operates according to the following protocol:
1. It travels 1 kilometer forward in its current direction, laying a cable segment.
2. At the end of the segment, it must change its direction. It chooses to rotate exactly $120^\circ$ from its current heading, with a 50% chance of rotating to the right (clockwise) and a 50% chance of rotating to the left (counterclockwise).
3. It then repeats this process: laying a 1-kilometer segment in the new direction and choosing its next $120^\circ$ turn.

The drone is equipped with a safety sensor. If the drone’s path ever touches or intersects any segment of cable it has already laid, the mission terminates immediately. 

What is the expected number of cable segments the drone has laid when the mission terminates?
