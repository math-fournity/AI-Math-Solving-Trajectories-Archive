This is **Andrica's conjecture**, a genuinely open problem in number theory. It
is tagged `category research open` in the problem statement itself, and no proof
 is known to mathematics.

The conjecture asserts that $\sqrt{p_{n+1}} - \sqrt{p_n} < 1$ for all $n$, where
 $p_n$ is the $n$-th prime. Equivalently, writing the prime gap $g_n = p_{n+1} -
 p_n$, the conjecture is $g_n < 2\sqrt{p_n} + 1$.

What is known:
- It has been verified computationally for very large ranges of primes.
- Ferreira (2023, arXiv:2307.08725) proved it holds for all sufficiently large $
n$ (the second theorem in the file, tagged `research solved`).
- However, the **full conjecture for all $n$** remains unproven. The gap between
 "sufficiently large $n$" and "all $n$" requires explicit verification up to the
 threshold where the asymptotic result takes over, and no complete proof closing
 this gap is known.
