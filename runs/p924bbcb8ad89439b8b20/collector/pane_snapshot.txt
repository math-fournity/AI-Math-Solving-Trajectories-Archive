## Proof

Let
$$M_i = \begin{bmatrix} A_i & B_i \\ B_i^\top & C_i \end{bmatrix} \succ 0, \qqua
d i = 1,2,$$
and assume $M_1 \succeq M_2$. The Schur complement (with respect to the $C$-bloc
k) is
$$S(M_i) = A_i - B_i C_i^{-1} B_i^\top.$$

**Variational characterization of the Schur complement.** For any fixed $u$ (of
the dimension of $A_i$), consider the quadratic form in $v$ (of the dimension of
 $C_i$):
$$f_i(v) := \begin{bmatrix} u \\ v \end{bmatrix}^\top M_i \begin{bmatrix} u \\ v
 \end{bmatrix} = u^\top A_i u + 2 u^\top B_i v + v^\top C_i v.$$
Since $C_i \succ 0$, $f_i$ is strictly convex in $v$ and is minimized at $v^\sta
r = -C_i^{-1} B_i^\top u$. Substituting,
$$\min_v f_i(v) = u^\top A_i u - u^\top B_i C_i^{-1} B_i^\top u = u^\top S(M_i)\
, u.$$
Thus, for every $u$,
$$u^\top S(M_i)\, u = \min_v \begin{bmatrix} u \\ v \end{bmatrix}^\top M_i \begi
n{bmatrix} u \\ v \end{bmatrix}. \tag{$\ast$}$$

**Comparison.** From $M_1 \succeq M_2$ we have, for every block vector $[u;v]$,
$$\begin{bmatrix} u \\ v \end{bmatrix}^\top M_1 \begin{bmatrix} u \\ v \end{bmat
rix} \;\ge\; \begin{bmatrix} u \\ v \end{bmatrix}^\top M_2 \begin{bmatrix} u \\
v \end{bmatrix}.$$
Taking the minimum over $v$ on both sides preserves the inequality (the minimum
of a pointwise-larger function is at least the minimum of the smaller one):
$$\min_v \begin{bmatrix} u \\ v \end{bmatrix}^\top M_1 \begin{bmatrix} u \\ v \e
nd{bmatrix} \;\ge\; \min_v \begin{bmatrix} u \\ v \end{bmatrix}^\top M_2 \begin{
bmatrix} u \\ v \end{bmatrix}.$$
By $(\ast)$ this gives
$$u^\top S(M_1)\, u \;\ge\; u^\top S(M_2)\, u \qquad \text{for all } u.$$
Since both sides are quadratic forms in $u$, this is equivalent to
$$S(M_1) - S(M_2) \succeq 0,$$
i.e.
$$(A_1 - B_1 C_1^{-1} B_1^\top) - (A_2 - B_2 C_2^{-1} B_2^\top) \succeq 0.$$

Therefore the Loewner order is preserved by the Schur complement, and the answer
 is **True**.
