**Theorem.** *The space of self-adjoint trace-class operators on a separable Hil
bert space $H$ is separable in the trace norm.*

**Proof.**

Let $H$ be a separable Hilbert space with a fixed orthonormal basis $\{e_n\}_{n=
1}^{\infty}$ (the finite-dimensional case is trivial, so assume $\dim H = \infty
$). Denote by $\mathcal{S}_1(H)$ the Banach space of trace-class operators equip
ped with the trace norm $\|T\|_1 = \operatorname{tr}|T|$, and by $\mathcal{S}_1^
{\mathrm{sa}}(H)$ the closed subspace of self-adjoint trace-class operators.

**Step 1: Finite-rank operators are dense in $\mathcal{S}_1(H)$.**

Let $P_N$ denote the orthogonal projection onto $\operatorname{span}\{e_1,\dots,
e_N\}$. For any $T \in \mathcal{S}_1(H)$, set $T_N = P_N T P_N$, which has finit
e rank. We show $\|T - T_N\|_1 \to 0$.

Write the singular value decomposition $T = \sum_{n} s_n\,|u_n\rangle\langle v_n
|$, where $\{u_n\}, \{v_n\}$ are orthonormal sets and $s_n \geq 0$ with $\sum_n
s_n = \|T\|_1 < \infty$. Then

$$T - T_N = (I - P_N)\,T + P_N T (I - P_N).$$

By the triangle inequality and the ideal property of $\mathcal{S}_1$:

$$\|T - T_N\|_1 \leq \|(I-P_N)T\|_1 + \|T(I-P_N)\|_1.$$

For the first term, $(I-P_N)T = \sum_n s_n\,|(I-P_N)u_n\rangle\langle v_n|$, so

$$\|(I-P_N)T\|_1 \leq \sum_n s_n\,\|(I-P_N)u_n\|\cdot\|v_n\| = \sum_n s_n\,\|(I-
P_N)u_n\|.$$

Since $\|(I-P_N)u_n\| \leq 1$ for all $N,n$, and $\|(I-P_N)u_n\| \to 0$ for each
 fixed $n$ as $N\to\infty$, the dominated convergence theorem (with summable dom
inating sequence $s_n$) gives $\sum_n s_n\,\|(I-P_N)u_n\| \to 0$. An identical a
rgument gives $\|T(I-P_N)\|_1 \to 0$. Hence $\|T-T_N\|_1\to 0$.

**Step 2: A countable dense set in $\mathcal{S}_1(H)$.**

Every finite-rank operator supported on $\operatorname{span}\{e_1,\dots,e_N\}$ i
s determined by its $N\times N$ matrix $(a_{ij})$ with $a_{ij}\in\mathbb{C}$. On
 this finite-dimensional space all norms are equivalent, so the set of such matr
ices with $a_{ij} \in \mathbb{Q}+i\mathbb{Q}$ is dense. Taking the union over al
l $N$, we obtain a countable set $\mathcal{D}$ of finite-rank operators that is
dense in the finite-rank operators, hence (by Step 1) dense in $\mathcal{S}_1(H)
$. Thus $\mathcal{S}_1(H)$ is separable.

**Step 3: $\mathcal{S}_1^{\mathrm{sa}}(H)$ is a closed subspace.**

The map $\Phi: \mathcal{S}_1(H) \to \mathcal{S}_1(H)$, $\Phi(T) = T - T^*$, is c
ontinuous (since $\|T^*\|_1 = \|T\|_1$), and $\mathcal{S}_1^{\mathrm{sa}}(H) = \
ker\Phi$ is closed.

**Step 4: Separability of $\mathcal{S}_1^{\mathrm{sa}}(H)$.**

A closed subspace of a separable metric space is separable. Therefore $\mathcal{
S}_1^{\mathrm{sa}}(H)$ is separable.

*(Direct argument, for completeness.)* For $T \in \mathcal{S}_1^{\mathrm{sa}}(H)
$, the truncation $P_N T P_N$ is self-adjoint, finite-rank, and $\|T - P_N T P_N
\|_1 \to 0$ by Step 1. The set of self-adjoint finite-rank operators with matrix
 entries in $\mathbb{Q}+i\mathbb{Q}$ satisfying $a_{ij}=\overline{a_{ji}}$ (equi
valently: real rational diagonal, and $a_{ij}\in\mathbb{Q}+i\mathbb{Q}$ with $a_
{ji}=\overline{a_{ij}}$ off-diagonal) is countable and dense in the self-adjoint
 finite-rank operators, hence dense in $\mathcal{S}_1^{\mathrm{sa}}(H)$.

**Conclusion.** The space of self-adjoint trace-class operators over a separable
 Hilbert space **is separable** with respect to the trace norm. $\blacksquare$
