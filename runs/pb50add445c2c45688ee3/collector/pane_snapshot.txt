$$M \leq \frac{T^2 + 2T - S}{m} \quad \text{for all configurations.}$$

**Setting up.** Write $b_i = \lfloor\sqrt{a_i}\rfloor \geq 1$ and $c_i = a_i - b

**Lower bound on the ratio.** Since $c_i \leq 2b_i$, we have $C \leq 2B$, so:
$$T^2 + 2T - S = B^2 + 2B - Q - C \geq B^2 - Q = \sum_{i \neq j} b_i b_j.$$

Let $b = \min_i b_i \geq 1$. Since $b_i \geq b$, each product $b_i b_j \geq b^2$

$$B^2 - Q = 2\sum_{i<j} b_i b_j \geq 2\binom{n}{2}b^2 = n(n-1)b^2.$$

2} = \frac{n(n-1)}{3}.$$
