### Setup and definitions

Consider the one-parameter family of logistic maps $f_r : [0,1] \to [0,1]$, $f_r
(x) = rx(1-x)$, with $r \in (0,4)$. We say the dynamics is **chaotic** (in the m
easure-theoretic sense relevant here) when the map admits an **absolutely contin
uous invariant probability measure** (acim) $\mu$ with respect to which the Lyap
unov exponent
$$\lambda(f_r) = \int_0^1 \log|f_r'(x)|\,d\mu(x)$$
is strictly positive. A positive Lyapunov exponent implies sensitive dependence
on initial conditions for $\mu$-a.e. point, which is the standard notion of chao
s (positive entropy, exponential divergence of nearby orbits). We take $S = \{r
\in (0,4) : f_r \text{ has an acim with positive Lyapunov exponent}\}$.

1. **Negative Schwarzian derivative.** For the logistic map, $Sf_r(x) = \frac{f_
r'''(x)}{f_r'(x)} - \frac{3}{2}\left(\frac{f_r''(x)}{f_r'(x)}\right)^2 = -\frac{

dimensional dynamics.

2. **Bifurcation structure.** For $r \in (0, 1]$, the fixed point $0$ is globall

c{r-1}{r}$ and all orbits in $(0,1)$ converge to it. The period-doubling cascade

gime is interleaved with **periodic windows** (open intervals of $r$ where a sta

### Jakobson's Theorem (1981)

The decisive result is:

> **Theorem (Jakobson, 1981).** Let $\{f_r\}_{r \in (a,b)}$ be a one-parameter f
amily of $C^3$ unimodal maps with negative Schwarzian derivative and a non-degen

at the parameter where the critical point is preperiodic (the "Collet–Eckmann /
Misiurewicz" parameter). Then the set of parameters $r$ for which $f_r$ admits a

The logistic family $f_r(x) = rx(1-x)$ on $[0,1]$ for $r \in (0,4)$ satisfies al

- unimodal with a unique non-degenerate (quadratic) critical point at $x = 1/2$,
- strictly negative Schwarzian derivative,
- the transversality/non-degeneracy condition holds at the Misiurewicz parameter

### From acim to chaos (positive Lyapunov exponent)

It remains to confirm that the existence of an acim implies *chaotic* behavior,
i.e., positive Lyapunov exponent. This is a standard consequence:

> **Theorem (Ledrappier, 1981; also follows from Mañé 1985).** For a $C^1$ unimo
dal map with negative Schwarzian derivative, if $\mu$ is an ergodic absolutely c
ontinuous invariant probability measure, then its Lyapunov exponent $\lambda(\mu

The intuition: if $\lambda \leq 0$, the invariant measure would be supported on
