## Solution

**Setup.** Let $P(t)$ be a degree-6 polynomial with leading coefficient $2009$,
satisfying
$$P(1)=2,\; P(2)=3,\; P(3)=5,\; P(4)=7,\; P(5)=9,\; P'(2)=2.$$

**Decomposition.** Define the vanishing polynomial
$$A(t)=(t-1)(t-2)(t-3)(t-4)(t-5),$$
which is monic of degree 5 and satisfies $A(1)=A(2)=A(3)=A(4)=A(5)=0$.

Let $R(t)$ be the unique polynomial of degree $\le 4$ interpolating the five giv
en values. Then every degree-6 polynomial matching the five values has the form
$$P(t)=R(t)+(at+b)\,A(t),$$
since $(at+b)A(t)$ is degree 6 and vanishes at $t=1,2,3,4,5$. The leading coeffi
cient of $(at+b)A(t)$ is $a$ (as $A$ is monic degree 5), so the leading coeffici
ent of $P$ is $a$. Hence
$$a=2009.$$

**Computing $R(t)$.** Using Newton's form with nodes $1,2,3,4,5$ (spacing $h=1$)
, the divided differences are:
- $f[1,2]=1,\; f[1,2,3]=\tfrac12,\; f[1,2,3,4]=-\tfrac16,\; f[1,2,3,4,5]=\tfrac1
{24}$.

Thus
$$R(t)=2+(t-1)+\tfrac12(t-1)(t-2)-\tfrac16(t-1)(t-2)(t-3)+\tfrac1{24}(t-1)(t-2)(
t-3)(t-4).$$

**Computing $R(6)$:**
$$R(6)=2+5+\tfrac12(5)(4)-\tfrac16(5)(4)(3)+\tfrac1{24}(5)(4)(3)(2)=2+5+10-10+5=
12.$$

**Computing $R'(2)$.** Differentiating term by term and evaluating at $t=2$:
- $\frac{d}{dt}(t-1)\big|_{2}=1$,
- $\tfrac12[(t-2)+(t-1)]\big|_{2}=\tfrac12$,
- $-\tfrac16[(t-2)(t-3)+(t-1)(t-3)+(t-1)(t-2)]\big|_{2}=-\tfrac16[0-1+0]=\tfrac1
6$,
- $\tfrac1{24}[\text{sum of triple products}]\big|_{2}=\tfrac1{24}[0+2+0+0]=\tfr
ac1{12}$.

So
$$R'(2)=1+\tfrac12+\tfrac16+\tfrac1{12}=\tfrac{12+6+2+1}{12}=\tfrac74.$$

**Computing $A'(2)$.** Since $2$ is a root of $A$,
$$A'(2)=\prod_{r\in\{1,3,4,5\}}(2-r)=(1)(-1)(-2)(-3)=-6.$$

**Determining $b$ from $P'(2)=2$.** Since $P(t)=R(t)+(2009t+b)A(t)$,
$$P'(t)=R'(t)+2009\,A(t)+(2009t+b)A'(t).$$
At $t=2$: $A(2)=0$, so
$$P'(2)=R'(2)+(2009\cdot 2+b)\,A'(2)=2.$$
$$\tfrac74+(4018+b)(-6)=2 \;\Longrightarrow\; (4018+b)(-6)=\tfrac14 \;\Longright
arrow\; 4018+b=-\tfrac1{24}.$$
$$b=-\tfrac1{24}-4018=-\tfrac{1+96432}{24}=-\tfrac{96433}{24}.$$

**Computing $P(6)$.** We have
$$P(6)=R(6)+(2009\cdot 6+b)\,A(6).$$
$$A(6)=(5)(4)(3)(2)(1)=120.$$
$$2009\cdot 6+b=12054-\tfrac{96433}{24}=\tfrac{289296-96433}{24}=\tfrac{192863}{
24}.$$
$$(2009\cdot 6+b)\,A(6)=\tfrac{192863}{24}\cdot 120=192863\cdot 5=964315.$$
$$P(6)=12+964315=\boxed{964327}.$$

**Verification.** The leading term of $(2009t+b)A(t)$ is $2009t^6$, giving leadi
ng coefficient $2009$ ✓. At $t=1,\dots,5$, $A(t)=0$ so $P(t)=R(t)$ matches the g
iven values ✓. The derivative condition $P'(2)=2$ was enforced by construction ✓
.
