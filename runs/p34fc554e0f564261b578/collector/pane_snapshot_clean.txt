## Solution

Let the target coordinates be $(u, v)$. The map is $f(x,y) = (x, xy)$, so $u = x
$ and $v = xy$.

**Determining the image.**

- If $x \neq 0$: then $u = x \neq 0$ is arbitrary, and for any $v \in K$ we set
$y = v/u = v/x$, giving $v = xy$. Hence every point $(u, v)$ with $u \neq 0$ lie
s in the image.
- If $x = 0$: then $u = 0$ and $v = xy = 0$, so the only point with $u = 0$ in t
he image is $(0,0)$.

Therefore
$$\operatorname{Im}(f) = \{(u,v) \in \mathbb{A}^2(K) : u \neq 0\} \cup \{(0,0)\}
 = \mathbb{A}^2(K) \setminus \bigl(V(u)\setminus\{(0,0)\}\bigr).$$

Equivalently, $\operatorname{Im}(f) = D(u) \cup \{(0,0)\}$, where $D(u) = \{u \n
eq 0\}$ is the standard principal open set.

**Dense.** The open set $D(u) = \{u \neq 0\}$ is dense in $\mathbb{A}^2(K)$: its
 closure is all of $\mathbb{A}^2(K)$ since the only polynomial vanishing on $D(u
)$ is the zero polynomial (a nonzero polynomial in $K[u,v]$ cannot vanish on the
 Zariski-dense set $\{u \neq 0\}$). Since $D(u) \subseteq \operatorname{Im}(f) \
subseteq \mathbb{A}^2(K)$, the image is dense.

**Not closed.** Consider the point $(0, 1)$. It is not in the image (the only po
int with $u=0$ in the image is $(0,0)$). Yet it lies in the closure: take $x = t
$, $y = 1/t$ for $t \neq 0$; then $f(t, 1/t) = (t, 1) \to (0, 1)$ as $t \to 0$ i
n the Zariski (hence also any finer) topology. More algebraically, the curve $\{
(t, 1) : t \in K\}$ is contained in the image for $t \neq 0$, and its Zariski cl
osure is the line $v = 1$, which contains $(0,1)$. Thus $(0,1) \in \overline{\op
eratorname{Im}(f)} \setminus \operatorname{Im}(f)$, so the image is not closed.

**Not open.** The origin $(0,0) \in \operatorname{Im}(f)$. Any Zariski-open neig
hborhood of $(0,0)$ contains a principal open set $D(g)$ with $g(0,0) \neq 0$...
 but more directly: every nonempty open set in $\mathbb{A}^2$ is cofinite on eac
h line and dense; in particular any open neighborhood of $(0,0)$ intersects the
line $\{u = 0\}$ in a cofinite subset of that line, hence contains points $(0, c
)$ with $c \neq 0$. Such points are not in the image. So $(0,0)$ is not an inter
ior point of $\operatorname{Im}(f)$, and the image is not open.

**Conclusion.**

$$\boxed{\operatorname{Im}(f) = \{(u,v) : u \neq 0\} \cup \{(0,0)\},}$$
which is **dense** in $\mathbb{A}^2(K)$ but **neither closed nor open** in the Z
ariski topology. (It is, however, a constructible set, consistent with Chevalley
's theorem on the constructibility of images of morphisms of finite type.)
