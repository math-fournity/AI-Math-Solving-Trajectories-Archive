## Proof

We wish to compute
$$L = \lim_{n\to\infty} \int_0^\infty n\, f(x)\, e^{-n|x|}\, dx.$$
Since $x \in [0,\infty)$, we have $|x|=x$, so
$$L = \lim_{n\to\infty} \int_0^\infty n\, f(x)\, e^{-nx}\, dx.$$

**Step 1: Use the hypothesis at the origin.** Since $\lim_{x\to 0^+} f(x) = 2016
$, there exists $\delta > 0$ such that $|f(x)| \le M := 2017$ for all $x \in [0,
\delta]$.

**Step 2: Split the integral.** Write
$$\int_0^\infty n\, f(x)\, e^{-nx}\, dx = \underbrace{\int_0^{\delta} n\, f(x)\,
 e^{-nx}\, dx}_{I_n} + \underbrace{\int_{\delta}^{\infty} n\, f(x)\, e^{-nx}\, d
x}_{J_n}.$$

**Step 3: The tail $J_n \to 0$.** For $x \ge \delta$ we have $e^{-nx} \le e^{-n\
delta}$, hence
$$|J_n| \le \int_{\delta}^{\infty} n\, |f(x)|\, e^{-nx}\, dx \le n\, e^{-n\delta
} \int_{\delta}^{\infty} |f(x)|\, dx \le n\, e^{-n\delta}\, \|f\|_{L^1}.$$
Since $n\, e^{-n\delta} \to 0$ as $n\to\infty$ (exponential decay beats polynomi
al growth) and $\|f\|_{L^1} < \infty$, we conclude $J_n \to 0$.

**Step 4: Evaluate $I_n$ via the Dominated Convergence Theorem.** Substitute $u
= nx$ (so $x = u/n$, $dx = du/n$):
$$I_n = \int_0^{n\delta} f(u/n)\, e^{-u}\, du = \int_0^{\infty} g_n(u)\, du,$$
where
$$g_n(u) := f(u/n)\, e^{-u}\, \mathbf{1}_{[0,\, n\delta]}(u).$$

*Pointwise limit.* Fix $u \ge 0$. For all $n$ large enough that $n\delta \ge u$,
 the indicator equals $1$, and $u/n \to 0^+$, so $f(u/n) \to f(0) = 2016$. There
fore
$$g_n(u) \to 2016\, e^{-u} \quad \text{pointwise on } [0,\infty).$$

*Dominating function.* For $u \in [0, n\delta]$ we have $u/n \in [0,\delta]$, so
 $|f(u/n)| \le M$. For $u > n\delta$ the indicator is $0$. Thus for all $u \ge 0
$,
$$|g_n(u)| \le M\, e^{-u}.$$
The function $u \mapsto M\, e^{-u}$ is integrable on $[0,\infty)$ (with integral
 $M$).

*Apply DCT.* By the Dominated Convergence Theorem,
$$\lim_{n\to\infty} I_n = \int_0^{\infty} \lim_{n\to\infty} g_n(u)\, du = \int_0
^{\infty} 2016\, e^{-u}\, du = 2016 \cdot 1 = 2016.$$

**Step 5: Combine.**
$$L = \lim_{n\to\infty} I_n + \lim_{n\to\infty} J_n = 2016 + 0 = 2016.$$

$$\boxed{L = 2016}$$
