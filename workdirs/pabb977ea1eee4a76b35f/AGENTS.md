# Problem

A specialized circular irrigation sprinkler is mounted at one vertex of a triangular stone garden bed. The garden bed is shaped like an equilateral triangle with a side length of exactly $4$ meters. The sprinkler is attached to a hose that allows it to reach a maximum radius of $8$ meters. However, the garden bed is built on a raised stone platform that acts as an impenetrable barrier, meaning the sprinkler's water cannot pass through the area of the triangular bed itself.

Let $A$ represent the total surface area of the ground outside the stone platform that can be reached by the water from this sprinkler. The area can be expressed in the form $A = \frac{m}{n} \pi - k \sqrt{\ell}$, where $m, n, k,$ and $\ell$ are positive integers such that $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime, and $\ell$ is square-free.

What is the value of $m + n + k + \ell$?
