## Proof

We evaluate
$$I = \int_{\mathbb{R}^3} e^{-|x|}\,dx, \qquad |x| = \sqrt{x_1^2+x_2^2+x_3^2}.$$

**Step 1: Pass to spherical coordinates.** The integrand depends on $x$ only thr
ough $|x| = r$, so we use spherical coordinates $(r,\theta,\varphi)$ with $r\ge
0$, $\theta\in[0,\pi]$, $\varphi\in[0,2\pi)$, and volume element
$$dx = r^2 \sin\theta\,dr\,d\theta\,d\varphi.$$
The integral separates:
$$I = \left(\int_0^\infty e^{-r} r^2\,dr\right)\left(\int_0^\pi \sin\theta\,d\th
eta\right)\left(\int_0^{2\pi} d\varphi\right).$$

**Step 2: Angular factors.**
$$\int_0^\pi \sin\theta\,d\theta = \big[-\cos\theta\big]_0^\pi = 2,$$
$$\int_0^{2\pi} d\varphi = 2\pi.$$

**Step 3: Radial factor.** For $n\ge 0$, the Gamma function gives
$$\int_0^\infty e^{-r} r^n\,dr = \Gamma(n+1) = n!.$$
With $n=2$:
$$\int_0^\infty e^{-r} r^2\,dr = \Gamma(3) = 2! = 2.$$

(One may also verify this directly by two integrations by parts, or by different
iating the identity $\int_0^\infty e^{-r}\,dr = 1$ twice under the parameter $e^
{-ar}$ at $a=1$.)

**Step 4: Combine.**
$$I = 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2\pi = 8\pi.$$

**Sanity check (general formula).** For $\mathbb{R}^d$,
$$\int_{\mathbb{R}^d} e^{-|x|}\,dx = \omega_{d-1}\int_0^\infty e^{-r}r^{d-1}\,dr
 = \frac{2\pi^{d/2}}{\Gamma(d/2)}\cdot\Gamma(d).$$
With $d=3$: $\omega_2 = 4\pi$, $\Gamma(3)=2$, giving $4\pi\cdot 2 = 8\pi$. Consi
stent.

### Answer

$$\boxed{\int_{\mathbb{R}^3} e^{-|x|}\,dx = 8\pi.}$$
