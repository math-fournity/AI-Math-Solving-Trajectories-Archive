# Galois Group of $f(X) = X^4 + 2X^2 + 4$ over $\mathbb{Q}$

## Step 1: Find the roots

Substituting $Y = X^2$, we solve $Y^2 + 2Y + 4 = 0$:
$$Y = \frac{-2 \pm \sqrt{4-16}}{2} = -1 \pm i\sqrt{3}.$$

Writing in polar form: $-1+i\sqrt{3} = 2e^{i\cdot 2\pi/3}$ and $-1-i\sqrt{3} = 2
e^{-i\cdot 2\pi/3}$. Taking square roots, the four roots of $f$ are:
$$\alpha = \tfrac{\sqrt{2}(1+i\sqrt{3})}{2},\quad -\alpha,\quad \beta = \tfrac{\
sqrt{2}(1-i\sqrt{3})}{2},\quad -\beta.$$

**Key relation:** $\alpha\beta = \frac{2\cdot(1+3)}{4} = 2$, so $\beta = 2/\alph
a$.

## Step 2: Identify the splitting field

Since $\beta = 2/\alpha$, the splitting field is $K = \mathbb{Q}(\alpha)$.

From $\alpha^2 = -1+i\sqrt{3}$, we get $i\sqrt{3} = \alpha^2+1 \in K$.
From $\alpha+\beta = \sqrt{2}$ and $\beta\in K$, we get $\sqrt{2}\in K$.

Conversely, $\alpha = \frac{\sqrt{2}(1+i\sqrt{3})}{2} \in \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2},\,
 i\sqrt{3})$.

Therefore:
$$K = \mathbb{Q}(\alpha) = \mathbb{Q}\!\left(\sqrt{2},\, i\sqrt{3}\right).$$

## Step 3: Compute the degree

$K$ is the compositum of $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2})$ and $\mathbb{Q}(i\sqrt{3}) = \ma
thbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$, both quadratic over $\mathbb{Q}$. Since $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2
})\subset\mathbb{R}$ while $\sqrt{-3}\notin\mathbb{R}$, these extensions are lin
early disjoint, giving:
$$[K:\mathbb{Q}] = 4.$$

## Step 4: Irreducibility of $f$ (consistency check)

Suppose $f = (X^2+aX+b)(X^2-aX+c)$ over $\mathbb{Q}$. Comparing coefficients: $a
(c-b)=0$, $b+c-a^2=2$, $bc=4$.

- **$a=0$:** $b+c=2,\; bc=4 \Rightarrow$ discriminant $4-16=-12<0$, no rational
solution.
- **$b=c$:** $b^2=4 \Rightarrow b=\pm2$. If $b=2$: $a^2=2$ (irrational). If $b=-
2$: $a^2=-6$ (impossible).

So $f$ is irreducible over $\mathbb{Q}$, consistent with $[K:\mathbb{Q}]=4$ and
a transitive Galois action.

## Step 5: Determine the Galois group

$K/\mathbb{Q}$ is the compositum of two independent quadratic extensions, so:
$$\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q}) \cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z
}/2\mathbb{Z} \;=\; V_4 \;\text{(Klein four-group)}.$$

The three non-trivial automorphisms, with their action on the roots:

| Automorphism | Action on $\sqrt{2},\, i\sqrt{3}$ | Permutation of roots |
|---|---|---|
| $\sigma_1$ | $\sqrt{2}\mapsto -\sqrt{2},\; i\sqrt{3}\mapsto i\sqrt{3}$ | $(\al
pha\;{-\alpha})(\beta\;{-\beta})$ |
| $\sigma_2$ | $\sqrt{2}\mapsto \sqrt{2},\; i\sqrt{3}\mapsto -i\sqrt{3}$ | $(\al
pha\;\beta)(-\alpha\;{-\beta})$ |
| $\sigma_3=\sigma_1\sigma_2$ | $\sqrt{2}\mapsto -\sqrt{2},\; i\sqrt{3}\mapsto -
i\sqrt{3}$ | $(\alpha\;{-\beta})(-\alpha\;\beta)$ |

**Verification:** $\sigma_1(\alpha)=\frac{-\sqrt{2}(1+i\sqrt{3})}{2}=-\alpha$ ✓;
 $\sigma_2(\alpha)=\frac{\sqrt{2}(1-i\sqrt{3})}{2}=\beta$ ✓; $\sigma_3(\alpha)=\
frac{-\sqrt{2}(1-i\sqrt{3})}{2}=-\beta$ ✓.

The orbit of $\alpha$ under the group is $\{\alpha, -\alpha, \beta, -\beta\}$, c
onfirming the action is transitive (consistent with irreducibility). Each non-id
entity element has order 2, and the group is abelian of order 4 — the Klein four
-group.

## Conclusion

$$\boxed{\operatorname{Gal}(f/\mathbb{Q}) \;\cong\; \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z} \time
s \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z} \;\cong\; V_4.}$$
