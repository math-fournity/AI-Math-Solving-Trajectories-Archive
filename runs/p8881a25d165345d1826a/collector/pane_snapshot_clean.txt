## Solution

Each vector **u**, **v**, **w** lies in {-1, 1}^n. Consider a single coordinate
i. The three entries u_i, v_i, w_i are each ±1, so their sum s_i = u_i + v_i + w
_i takes values in {-3, -1, 1, 3} (always odd, since it's a sum of three odd num
bers).

The L∞-norm condition ‖**u**+**v**+**w**‖_∞ ≤ 1 requires |s_i| ≤ 1 for every coo
rdinate i. Since s_i is always odd, this forces s_i ∈ {-1, 1} for every i — equi
valently, s_i ∉ {-3, 3}.

Now s_i = 3 occurs only when (u_i, v_i, w_i) = (1, 1, 1) (1 way), and s_i = -3 o
ccurs only when (u_i, v_i, w_i) = (-1, -1, -1) (1 way). Out of the 2^3 = 8 possi
ble triples per coordinate, exactly 2 are forbidden, leaving 8 - 2 = **6** valid
 triples per coordinate.

The coordinates are independent (the choice at coordinate i does not constrain t
he choice at coordinate j), so the total number of ordered triplets is

$$\underbrace{6 \cdot 6 \cdots 6}_{n \text{ times}} = 6^n.$$

**Answer:** $\boxed{6^n}$
