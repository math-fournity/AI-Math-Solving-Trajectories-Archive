## Setup

2. Fly from $P$ to a point $Q = (b, 0)$ on the $x$-axis (coastline).

The total distance is $|AP| + |PQ|$.

**Optimizing the second leg.** For a fixed $P = (a, a^2/8)$, the closest point o

$$f(a) = \sqrt{(a-7)^2 + \left(\tfrac{a^2}{8}-3\right)^2} \;+\; \frac{a^2}{8}.$$

## Finding the critical point
