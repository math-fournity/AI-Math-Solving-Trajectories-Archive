## Proof

**Setup and notation.** Let $u_n = \operatorname{Re}(f_n)$, and write $L = \lim_
{n\to\infty} f_n(a)$. Set $g_n(z) = f_n(z) - f_n(a)$, so $g_n$ is holomorphic on
 $\Omega$ with $g_n(a)=0$, and
$$\operatorname{Re}(g_n) = u_n - \operatorname{Re}(f_n(a)).$$
Since $(u_n)$ converges uniformly on compact subsets of $\Omega$ to some (necess
arily harmonic) function $u$, and $\operatorname{Re}(f_n(a)) \to \operatorname{R
e}(L)$, the sequence $\operatorname{Re}(g_n)$ converges uniformly on compact sub
sets to
$$v := u - \operatorname{Re}(L).$$

**Key claim.** $g_n$ converges uniformly on every compact subset of $\Omega$ to
a holomorphic function $g$ with $g(a)=0$ and $\operatorname{Re}(g)=v$.

*Proof of claim.* It suffices to show $(g_n)$ is Cauchy in the compact-open topo
logy. Fix a compact $K \subset \Omega$ and $\varepsilon > 0$. Choose $r>0$ small
 enough that the closed disk $\overline{D(a,r)} \subset \Omega$ and $K \subset D
(a, R)$ for some $R$ with $\overline{D(a,R)} \subset \Omega$ (possible since $K$
 is compact and $\Omega$ is open). For $m, n$ large, $\operatorname{Re}(g_n - g_
m)$ converges uniformly to $0$ on compact sets.

Let $h = g_n - g_m$, holomorphic with $h(a)=0$ and $\operatorname{Re}(h) \to 0$
uniformly on compact sets. By the Cauchy–Riemann equations, $h' = (\operatorname
{Re}\, h)_x - i\,(\operatorname{Re}\, h)_y$. Since $\operatorname{Re}(h)$ is har
monic and converges uniformly to $0$ on compact sets, its partial derivatives al
so converge uniformly to $0$ on compact sets (standard property of harmonic func
tions: uniform convergence of harmonic functions implies uniform convergence of
all derivatives on compact subsets). Hence $h' \to 0$ uniformly on compact sets.

For any $z \in K$, integrate along a path from $a$ to $z$ lying in a fixed compa
ct subset of $\Omega$:
$$h(z) = h(a) + \int_a^z h'(\zeta)\,d\zeta = \int_a^z h'(\zeta)\,d\zeta.$$
Since $h' \to 0$ uniformly on the relevant compact set and the path length is bo
unded, $h(z) \to 0$ uniformly on $K$. Thus $(g_n)$ is Cauchy uniformly on $K$, p
roving the claim. $\square$

**Conclusion.** Let $g$ denote the compact-open limit of $g_n$. Then $g$ is holo
morphic (uniform limit on compact sets), $g(a)=0$, and $\operatorname{Re}(g) = v
 = u - \operatorname{Re}(L)$. Since $g_n \to g$ uniformly on $K$,
$$\sup_{z \in K}|g_n(z)| \longrightarrow \sup_{z \in K}|g(z)|.$$

Therefore:
$$\boxed{\lim_{n\to\infty}\sup_{z\in K}|g_n(z)| \;=\; \sup_{z\in K}|g(z)|,}$$
where $g$ is the unique holomorphic function on $\Omega$ satisfying $g(a)=0$ and
 $\operatorname{Re}(g)=u-\operatorname{Re}(L)$, with $u=\lim u_n$ (uniform on co
mpact sets) and $L=\lim f_n(a)$.
