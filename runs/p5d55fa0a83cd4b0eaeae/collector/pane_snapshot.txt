$$K \to U \to U/K$$

This is a fiber bundle with fiber $K$, total space $U$, base $U/K$.

The long exact sequence of homotopy groups for this fibration:
$$\cdots \to \pi_1(K) \to \pi_1(U) \to \pi_1(U/K) \to \pi_0(K) \to \pi_0(U) \to
\pi_0(U/K) \to 0$$

connected implies connected).
