rtite graph).

**Model.** Form a bipartite graph $G$ with math problems $M$ ($|M|=20$) on one s
ide and physics problems $P$ ($|P|=11$) on the other. Each student is an edge. T
he condition states: for every edge $(m,p)$, $\deg(m)\le 2$ or $\deg(p)\le 2$.

**Upper bound.**

Let $M'=\{m\in M:\deg(m)\le 2\}$, $P'=\{p\in P:\deg(p)\le 2\}$, with $a=|M'|$, $
b=|P'|$. Every edge has at least one endpoint in $M'\cup P'$, so no edge connect
s $M\setminus M'$ to $P\setminus P'$.

Partition edges into: $E_{LL}$ (between $M'$ and $P'$), $E_{LH}$ (between $M'$ a
nd $P\setminus P'$), $E_{HL}$ (between $M\setminus M'$ and $P'$). Then $E_{HH}=\
emptyset$ and $|E|=|E_{LL}|+|E_{LH}|+|E_{HL}|$.

Degree constraints give:
$$|E_{LL}|+|E_{LH}|\le 2a,\qquad |E_{LL}|+|E_{HL}|\le 2b.$$

Adding: $2|E_{LL}|+|E_{LH}|+|E_{HL}|\le 2a+2b$, so $|E|\le 2a+2b-|E_{LL}|\le 2a+
2b$.

**Case 1: $M\setminus M'\ne\emptyset$ and $P\setminus P'\ne\emptyset$.** Each ve
rtex in $M\setminus M'$ has degree $\ge 3$, all going to $P'$, so $|E_{HL}|\ge 3
(20-a)$. Similarly $|E_{LH}|\ge 3(11-b)$. The bipartite capacity gives $|E_{HL}|
\le (20-a)b$ and $|E_{LH}|\le a(11-b)$.

Setting $|E_{LL}|=0$ for maximum: $|E|=|E_{LH}|+|E_{HL}|\le \min(2a,\,a(11-b))+\
min(2b,\,(20-a)b)$.

- For $|E_{LH}|=2a$: need $11-b\ge 2$, i.e., $b\le 9$.
- For $|E_{HL}|=2b$: need $20-a\ge 2$, i.e., $a\le 18$.

When $b\le 9$ and $a\le 18$: $|E|\le 2a+2b\le 2(18)+2(9)=54$.

When $b\ge 10$: $|E_{LH}|\le a(11-b)\le a$, and $|E_{HL}|\le 2b$, so $|E|\le a+2
b$. With $a\le 18$: $|E|\le 18+20=38<54$ (and checking $a=19$ gives even less du
e to capacity).

When $a\ge 19$: $|E_{HL}|\le (20-a)b\le b$, so $|E|\le 2a+b\le 38+9=47<54$.

**Case 2: $M\setminus M'=\emptyset$ ($a=20$).** All edges incident to $M'$, so $
|E|\le 2(20)=40<54$.

**Case 3: $P\setminus P'=\emptyset$ ($b=11$).** All edges incident to $P'$, so $
|E|\le 2(11)=22<54$.

In all cases, $|E|\le 54$.

**Construction achieving 54.** Set $a=18$, $b=9$: choose 18 math problems as $M'
$ (degree 2 each) and 2 as $M\setminus M'$ (degree 9 each); choose 9 physics pro
blems as $P'$ (degree 2 each) and 2 as $P\setminus P'$ (degree 18 each).

- $E_{LH}$: Each of the 18 $M'$-vertices connects to both 2 $P\setminus P'$-vert
ices: $18\times 2=36$ edges. Each $P\setminus P'$-vertex has degree 18.
- $E_{HL}$: Each of the 9 $P'$-vertices connects to both 2 $M\setminus M'$-verti
ces: $9\times 2=18$ edges. Each $M\setminus M'$-vertex has degree 9.
- $E_{LL}=0$.

Total: $36+18=54$ edges, all distinct. Every edge has an endpoint of degree 2 (t
he $M'$ or $P'$ vertex). ✓

**The maximum possible number of students is $\boxed{54}$.**
