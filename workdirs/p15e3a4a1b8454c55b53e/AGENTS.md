# Problem

In a high-tech logistics hub, a massive storage facility is organized into a rigid $200 \times 200$ grid of automated docking stations. Each station can either be empty or occupied by one of two types of robotic drones: "Red-Flash" models or "Blue-Swift" models.

The facility’s scanning system operates strictly along the grid lines: two drones are within "sensor range" of each other if they occupy the same row or the same column of the grid. 

The system protocol requires a specific balance of surveillance: every single drone currently in the facility must be within sensor range of exactly five drones of the opposite model. (Note that a drone may also be within sensor range of any number of drones of its own model).

Based on these operational constraints, what is the maximum possible number of total drones (Red-Flash and Blue-Swift combined) that can be stationed in the facility at one time?
