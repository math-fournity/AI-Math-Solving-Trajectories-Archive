## Setting Up the Model

Let $n$ be the total number of hubs. Each pair of hubs plays a "game" distributi
ng 1 point total (1–0 for a decisive outcome, 0.5–0.5 for a tie). Let $s_h$ be t
he total score of hub $h$.

Let $B$ = the 10 lowest-scoring hubs, $T$ = the remaining $m = n - 10$ hubs.

**Key condition:** For every hub $h$, the points earned from links to hubs in $B
$ equal $s_h / 2$.

## Summing Over B Hubs

For hub $i \in B$: let $b_i$ = points from other 9 B hubs, $t_i$ = points from T
 hubs. Then $s_i = b_i + t_i$ and $b_i = s_i/2$, so $t_i = s_i/2$, giving $b_i =
 t_i$.

Summing over all $i \in B$:
$$\sum_{i \in B} b_i = \sum_{i \in B} t_i$$

The left side is the total points from all B–B games $= \binom{10}{2} = 45$. So
B hubs collectively earn $P = 45$ points from B–T games. Since there are $10m$ B
–T games total, T hubs earn $10m - 45$ from B–T games.

## Summing Over T Hubs

For each $h \in T$, points from B-links $= s_h/2$, so:
$$\sum_{h \in T} s_h = 2(10m - 45) = 20m - 90$$

But also, $\sum_{h \in T} s_h$ = (T–T game points) + (B–T game points, T side) $
= \binom{m}{2} + (10m - 45)$.

Setting equal:
$$\binom{m}{2} + 10m - 45 = 20m - 90$$
$$\frac{m(m-1)}{2} = 10m - 45$$
$$m^2 - 21m + 90 = 0$$
$$(m - 6)(m - 15) = 0$$

So $m = 6$ or $m = 15$.

## Ruling Out $m = 6$

If $m = 6$ ($n = 16$): $\sum_B s_i = 90$ (average 9), $\sum_T s_h = 30$ (average
 5). Since $B$ must contain the **lowest**-scoring hubs, every B score $\le$ eve
ry T score. But average B score (9) > average T score (5), and $\max_B \ge 9 > 5
 \ge \min_T$ — contradiction. So $m \neq 6$.

## Verifying $m = 15$ ($n = 25$)

$\sum_B s_i = 90$ (average 9), $\sum_T s_h = 210$ (average 14). B average < T av
erage — consistent with B being the bottom 10.

**Explicit construction** achieving all conditions:

- **T–T games:** Regular tournament on 15 hubs (cyclic tournament) → each T hub
scores $(15-1)/2 = 7$.
- **B–T games:** Need each B hub to score 4.5 and each T hub to score 7. The B-s

**Checking all conditions:**
