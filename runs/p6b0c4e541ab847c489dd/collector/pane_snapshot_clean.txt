## Proof

Let $X$ be a smooth variety with $\dim X = 1$ (i.e., a curve), and let $\mathcal
{E}$ be a vector bundle of rank $r$ over $X$. The scroll $S = \mathbb{P}(\mathca
l{E})$ is the projectivization of $\mathcal{E}$, parametrizing one-dimensional q
uotients of $\mathcal{E}$ (Grothendieck convention), with projection $\pi: S \to
 X$ and tautological quotient line bundle $\mathcal{O}_S(1)$.

**Dimension.** Since $\dim X = 1$ and the fibers of $\pi$ are $\mathbb{P}^{r-1}$
, we have $\dim S = 1 + (r-1) = r$.

**Degree of the scroll.** The scroll $S$ is embedded by the complete linear syst
em of $\mathcal{O}_S(1)$, so its degree is the top self-intersection of the hype
rplane class:
$$\deg(S) = \int_S c_1(\mathcal{O}_S(1))^r = \int_S \zeta^r,$$
where $\zeta = c_1(\mathcal{O}_S(1))$.

**Chow ring relation.** On $S = \mathbb{P}(\mathcal{E})$, the tautological exact
 sequence
$$0 \to \mathcal{K} \to \pi^*\mathcal{E} \to \mathcal{O}_S(1) \to 0$$
gives $\mathcal{K}$ of rank $r-1$ and $c(\mathcal{K}) = \pi^*c(\mathcal{E})\cdot
(1+\zeta)^{-1}$. Since $c_r(\mathcal{K}) = 0$, we obtain the relation:
$$\zeta^r - c_1(\mathcal{E})\,\zeta^{r-1} + c_2(\mathcal{E})\,\zeta^{r-2} - \cdo
ts + (-1)^r c_r(\mathcal{E}) = 0.$$

**Specialization to $\dim X = 1$.** Since $X$ is a curve, the Chow groups $A^i(X
) = 0$ for $i \geq 2$, so $c_i(\mathcal{E}) = 0$ for $i \geq 2$. The relation co
llapses to:
$$\zeta^r = c_1(\mathcal{E})\cdot \zeta^{r-1}.$$

**Pushforward computation.** The standard pushforward formula for $\mathbb{P}(\m
athcal{E})$ gives $\pi_*(\zeta^{r-1}) = 1 \in A^0(X)$. Therefore:
$$\deg(S) = \int_S \zeta^r = \int_S c_1(\mathcal{E})\cdot \zeta^{r-1} = \int_X c
_1(\mathcal{E})\cdot \pi_*(\zeta^{r-1}) = \int_X c_1(\mathcal{E})\cdot 1 = \int_
X c_1(\mathcal{E}).$$

**Verification.** For $\mathcal{E} = \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^1}(a_1)\oplus\cdots
\oplus\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^1}(a_r)$, we have $c_1(\mathcal{E}) = (a_1+\cdots+
a_r)\,h$ where $h$ is the hyperplane class on $\mathbb{P}^1$, giving $\deg(S) =
a_1+\cdots+a_r$, which matches the classical degree of the rational normal scrol
l $S(a_1,\ldots,a_r)$. $\checkmark$

### Conclusion

When $\dim X = 1$, the degree of the scroll $S = \mathbb{P}(\mathcal{E})$ is:

$$\boxed{\deg(S) = \int_X c_1(\mathcal{E}) = \deg\, c_1(\mathcal{E})}$$

That is, the degree equals the degree of the first Chern class of $\mathcal{E}$
(equivalently, the degree of the vector bundle $\mathcal{E}$). All higher Chern
classes $c_i(\mathcal{E})$ for $i \geq 2$ vanish on a curve and do not contribut
e.
