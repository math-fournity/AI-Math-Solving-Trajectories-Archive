## Solution

Let $p = \cot^2 x$. As $x \to 0$, we have $\cot x \to \infty$, so $p \to \infty$
. Note also that $\tan^2 x = 1/p$. The expression becomes:

$$\lim_{p \to \infty}\; \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{n}{\left(\sum_{k=1}^{n} k^{p}\
right)^{1/p}}.$$

### Inner limit (fixed $p > 0$, $n \to \infty$)

For fixed $p > 0$, the well-known asymptotic for the power sum is:

$$\sum_{k=1}^{n} k^{p} \sim \frac{n^{p+1}}{p+1} \quad (n \to \infty).$$

Raising to the power $1/p$:

$$\left(\sum_{k=1}^{n} k^{p}\right)^{1/p} \sim \left(\frac{n^{p+1}}{p+1}\right)^
{1/p} = \frac{n^{1+1/p}}{(p+1)^{1/p}}.$$

Therefore:

$$\frac{n}{\left(\sum_{k=1}^{n} k^{p}\right)^{1/p}} \sim \frac{n \cdot (p+1)^{1/
p}}{n^{1+1/p}} = \frac{(p+1)^{1/p}}{n^{1/p}}.$$

Since $p > 0$ is fixed, $n^{1/p} \to \infty$ as $n \to \infty$, while $(p+1)^{1/
p}$ is a finite constant. Hence:

$$L(p) := \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{n}{\left(\sum_{k=1}^{n} k^{p}\right)^{1/p}}
= 0 \quad \text{for every } p > 0.$$

**Verification with examples:**
- $p=1$: $\sum k \sim n^2/2$, so $n/(n^2/2) = 2/n \to 0$. ✓
- $p=2$: $\sum k^2 \sim n^3/3$, so $n/(n^{3/2}/\sqrt{3}) = \sqrt{3}/\sqrt{n} \to
 0$. ✓

### Outer limit ($p \to \infty$)

Since $L(p) = 0$ for every $p > 0$:

$$\lim_{p \to \infty} L(p) = \lim_{p \to \infty} 0 = 0.$$

### Conclusion

$$\lim_{x \to 0}\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{n}{\left(1^{\cot^2 x}+2^{\cot^2 x}+\ld
ots+n^{\cot^2 x}\right)^{\tan^2 x}} = \boxed{0}.$$
