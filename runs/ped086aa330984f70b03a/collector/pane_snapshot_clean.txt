## Solution

We seek positive integer $m$ and integer $n$ with $m^2 = 4n^2 - 5n + 16$, maximi
zing $|m-n|$.

**Reformulation.** Rewrite as $(2n)^2 - m^2 = 5n - 16$, i.e.
$$(2n - m)(2n + m) = 5n - 16.$$
Set $a = 2n - m$, $b = 2n + m$. Then $a + b = 4n$ and $b - a = 2m$, so $n = (a+b
)/4$ and $m = (b-a)/2$. Substituting into $ab = 5n - 16$:
$$ab = \frac{5(a+b)}{4} - 16 \implies 4ab - 5a - 5b = -64.$$
Multiplying by 4 and completing the product:
$$(4a-5)(4b-5) = 16ab - 20a - 20b + 25 = -256 + 25 = -231.$$

**Divisor analysis.** Since $231 = 3 \cdot 7 \cdot 11$, we enumerate all ordered
 factor pairs $(d_1, d_2)$ with $d_1 d_2 = -231$, where $d_1 = 4a-5$, $d_2 = 4b-
5$. Integrality of $a, b$ requires $d_1 \equiv d_2 \equiv 3 \pmod{4}$, and integ
rality of $n = (a+b)/4$ requires $a+b \equiv 0 \pmod{4}$.

Checking residues mod 4: $-1\equiv3,\; 3\equiv3,\; 7\equiv3,\; 11\equiv3,\; -21\
equiv3,\; -33\equiv3,\; -77\equiv3,\; 231\equiv3$ (and their negatives are $\equ
iv 1$). The valid pairs (both $\equiv 3 \pmod 4$) are:

| $(d_1, d_2)$ | $a$ | $b$ | $n$ | $m$ | valid? | $\|m-n\|$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $(-1,\, 231)$ | $1$ | $59$ | $15$ | $29$ | yes | $14$ |
| $(3,\, -77)$ | $2$ | $-18$ | $-4$ | $-10$ | no ($m<0$) | — |
| $(7,\, -33)$ | $3$ | $-7$ | $-1$ | $-5$ | no ($m<0$) | — |
| $(11,\, -21)$ | $4$ | $-4$ | $0$ | $-4$ | no ($m<0$) | — |
| $(-21,\, 11)$ | $-4$ | $4$ | $0$ | $4$ | yes | $4$ |
| $(-33,\, 7)$ | $-7$ | $3$ | $-1$ | $5$ | yes | $6$ |
| $(-77,\, 3)$ | $-18$ | $2$ | $-4$ | $10$ | yes | $14$ |
| $(231,\, -1)$ | $59$ | $1$ | $15$ | $-29$ | no ($m<0$) | — |

**Verification of the extremal solutions.**
- $(m,n) = (29, 15)$: $4(225) - 75 + 16 = 841 = 29^2$. ✓, $|29-15| = 14$.
- $(m,n) = (10, -4)$: $4(16) + 20 + 16 = 100 = 10^2$. ✓, $|10-(-4)| = 14$.
- $(m,n) = (5, -1)$: $4 + 5 + 16 = 25 = 5^2$. ✓, $|5-(-1)| = 6$.
- $(m,n) = (4, 0)$: $16 = 4^2$. ✓, $|4-0| = 4$.

The factorization $(4a-5)(4b-5) = -231$ is exhaustive (only finitely many diviso
rs), so these are **all** solutions with $m > 0$.

**Conclusion.** The maximum possible value of $|m - n|$ is $\boxed{14}$.
