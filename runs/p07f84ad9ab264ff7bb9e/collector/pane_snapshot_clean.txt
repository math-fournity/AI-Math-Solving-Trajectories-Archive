## Solution

We solve the constant-coefficient first-order system
$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} + A\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} = b,\qquad u(x,
0)=u_0(x),$$
where $u:\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}\to\mathbb{R}^n$, $A\in\mathbb{R}^{n\
times n}$, $b\in\mathbb{R}^n$ are constant, and the eigenvalues $\lambda_1,\dots
,\lambda_n$ of $A^T$ (equivalently of $A$) are **distinct and real**, with corre
sponding (left) eigenvectors $g_k$:
$$A^T g_k = \lambda_k g_k \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad g_k^T A = \lambda_k g_k^
T,\qquad k=1,\dots,n.$$

---

### Step 1. Diagonalization via left eigenvectors

Since the eigenvalues are distinct, $\{g_1,\dots,g_n\}$ is a basis of $\mathbb{R
}^n$. Let $\{r_1,\dots,r_n\}$ be the right eigenvectors of $A$, i.e. $A r_k = \l
ambda_k r_k$. The sets $\{r_k\}$ and $\{g_k\}$ are biorthogonal; we normalize so
 that
$$g_k^T r_j = \delta_{kj}.$$
Then every vector $u\in\mathbb{R}^n$ expands as
$$u = \sum_{k=1}^n (g_k^T u)\, r_k.$$

### Step 2. Characteristic (Riemann) variables

Define the scalar characteristic variables
$$w_k(x,t) := g_k^T u(x,t),\qquad k=1,\dots,n.$$
Project the PDE onto $g_k$:
$$g_k^T\!\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} + A\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\ri
ght) = g_k^T b.$$
Using $g_k^T A = \lambda_k g_k^T$ and linearity,
$$\frac{\partial w_k}{\partial t} + \lambda_k\frac{\partial w_k}{\partial x} = \
beta_k,\qquad \beta_k := g_k^T b\ \text{(constant)}.$$
The system has decoupled into $n$ independent scalar transport equations with co
nstant source.

### Step 3. Solve each scalar equation along characteristics

For each $k$, the characteristic curves are straight lines
$$\frac{dx}{dt} = \lambda_k \quad\Longrightarrow\quad x - \lambda_k t = \text{co
nst}.$$
Along such a curve,
$$\frac{d}{dt}w_k\bigl(x_0+\lambda_k t,\,t\bigr) = \beta_k \quad\Longrightarrow\
quad w_k(x,t) = w_k^0(x-\lambda_k t) + \beta_k\, t,$$
where the initial value is
$$w_k^0(\xi) := w_k(\xi,0) = g_k^T u_0(\xi).$$
Hence
$$w_k(x,t) = g_k^T u_0(x - \lambda_k t) + t\,(g_k^T b).$$

### Step 4. Reconstruct $u$

Using the biorthogonal expansion $u = \sum_k w_k\, r_k$:

$$\boxed{\;u(x,t) \;=\; \sum_{k=1}^{n} r_k\Bigl[\,g_k^T\, u_0\!\bigl(x - \lambda
_k t\bigr) \;+\; t\,\bigl(g_k^T b\bigr)\Bigr]\;}$$

where $r_k$ are the right eigenvectors of $A$ normalized by $g_k^T r_j = \delta_
{kj}$.

---

### Verification

* **Initial condition.** At $t=0$: $u(x,0)=\sum_k r_k\, g_k^T u_0(x) = u_0(x)$ b
y the biorthogonal expansion. ✓
* **PDE.** Compute $\partial_t u + A\partial_x u$:
$$\partial_t u = \sum_k r_k\bigl[-\lambda_k\, g_k^T u_0'(x-\lambda_k t) + g_k^T
b\bigr],$$
$$A\,\partial_x u = \sum_k \lambda_k r_k\, g_k^T u_0'(x-\lambda_k t),$$
since $A r_k = \lambda_k r_k$. Adding, the $u_0'$ terms cancel:
$$\partial_t u + A\partial_x u = \sum_k r_k\,(g_k^T b) = \sum_k (g_k^T b)\, r_k
= b,$$
because $\sum_k (g_k^T b) r_k$ is exactly the biorthogonal expansion of the cons
tant vector $b$. ✓

### Interpretation

Each component $w_k = g_k^T u$ is a **Riemann invariant** advected at speed $\la
mbda_k$ along its own characteristic family $x - \lambda_k t = \text{const}$, wi
th a constant drift $t\,(g_k^T b)$ contributed by the source $b$. The full solut
ion is the superposition of these $n$ decoupled waves, recombined through the ri
ght-eigenvector basis $\{r_k\}$.
