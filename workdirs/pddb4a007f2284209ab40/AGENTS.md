# Problem

In a bustling coastal region, an architect is designing a complex network of 20 straight, high-speed fiber-optic cables. To ensure maximum connectivity across the terrain, the architect stipulates that no two cables can be laid perfectly parallel to one another.

The regional communications commission has established a grant system to fund this infrastructure based on the geometric intersections of the network:
- For every set of three cables that intersect to form a perfect equilateral triangle, the project receives a "Triple-A" grant worth 3 million dollars.
- For every set of three cables that intersect to form an isosceles triangle that is not equilateral, the project receives a "Standard" grant worth 1 million dollar.
- Any other triangle formed by the intersection of three cables receives no grant money.

Given that exactly 20 lines are drawn and no two are parallel, what is the maximum total number of millions of dollars the project can receive?
