## Proof

**Claim:** $A$ is infinite.

**Key observation.** If $f^{[2008]} = \mathrm{Id}_{\mathbb{N}}$, then $f$ is a b
ijection (with inverse $f^{[2007]}$). Any bijection of $\mathbb{N}$ decomposes $
\mathbb{N}$ into disjoint cycles. The condition $f^{[2008]} = \mathrm{Id}$ means
 every cycle length divides $2008$. The order of $f$ (the least positive $k$ wit
h $f^{[k]} = \mathrm{Id}$) equals the LCM of all cycle lengths. Thus $f \in A$ i
f and only if $f$ is a bijection whose cycle lengths all divide $2008$ and whose
 LCM is exactly $2008$.

Since $2008 = 2^3 \cdot 251$, a single cycle of length $2008$ already achieves L
CM $= 2008$.

**Construction of infinitely many elements of $A$.**

For each $n \in \mathbb{N}$, define $f_n : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ by:

$$f_n(m) = \begin{cases} m+1 & \text{if } n \le m \le n+2006, \\ n & \text{if }
m = n+2007, \\ m & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}$$

That is, $f_n$ forms a single $2008$-cycle on $\{n, n+1, \dots, n+2007\}$ and fi
xes every other element.

**Verification that $f_n \in A$:**

- The cycle decomposition of $f_n$ consists of one cycle of length $2008$ and in
finitely many fixed points (cycles of length $1$). The order of $f_n$ is $\mathr
m{lcm}(2008, 1) = 2008$, so $f_n^{[2008]} = \mathrm{Id}_{\mathbb{N}}$.
- For any $k \in \{1, 2, \dots, 2007\}$, since $k < 2008$, the element $n$ is no
t fixed by $f_n^{[k]}$ (it is sent to $n + k \neq n$), so $f_n^{[k]} \neq \mathr
m{Id}_{\mathbb{N}}$.

Hence $f_n \in A$ for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$.

**The $f_n$ are pairwise distinct.** If $n \neq m$, say $n < m$. If $m \ge n + 2
008$, then $f_n(n) = n+1 \neq n$ while $f_m(n) = n$, so $f_n \neq f_m$. If $n <
m < n + 2008$, then $f_n$ moves $n$ (sends it to $n+1$) while $f_m$ fixes $n$ (s
ince $n < m$), so again $f_n \neq f_m$. In all cases $f_n \neq f_m$.

**Conclusion.** The map $n \mapsto f_n$ is an injection from $\mathbb{N}$ into $
A$, so $A$ is infinite. $\blacksquare$
