# Solution

## Step 1: Solve the recurrence for $a_n$

The recurrence is
$$(n+1)a_n^2 - 2n\,a_{n+1}^2 + \sqrt{n^2+n}\;a_n a_{n+1} = 0.$$

Set $r_n = a_{n+1}/a_n$ (well-defined since all terms are positive) and divide b
y $a_n^2$:

$$(n+1) - 2n\,r_n^2 + \sqrt{n^2+n}\;r_n = 0,$$

i.e.
$$2n\,r_n^2 - \sqrt{n(n+1)}\;r_n - (n+1) = 0.$$

Applying the quadratic formula:
$$r_n = \frac{\sqrt{n(n+1)} \pm \sqrt{n(n+1) + 8n(n+1)}}{4n} = \frac{\sqrt{n(n+1
)} \pm \sqrt{9n(n+1)}}{4n} = \frac{\sqrt{n(n+1)}\,(1 \pm 3)}{4n}.$$

The two roots are:
- $r_n = \dfrac{\sqrt{n(n+1)}\cdot 4}{4n} = \sqrt{\dfrac{n+1}{n}} > 0$,
- $r_n = \dfrac{\sqrt{n(n+1)}\cdot(-2)}{4n} = -\dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{\dfrac{n+1}{n}}
 < 0$.

Since every $a_n$ is positive, $r_n > 0$, so
$$r_n = \sqrt{\frac{n+1}{n}}, \qquad a_{n+1} = a_n\sqrt{\frac{n+1}{n}}.$$

Telescoping from $a_1 = 1$:
$$a_n = \prod_{k=1}^{n-1}\sqrt{\frac{k+1}{k}} = \sqrt{\prod_{k=1}^{n-1}\frac{k+1
}{k}} = \sqrt{n}.$$

## Step 2: Relate $z_{2020}$ to $z_{2019}$

By definition,
$$z_n = \prod_{k=1}^{n}\left(1 - \frac{i}{a_k}\right) = \prod_{k=1}^{n}\left(1 -
 \frac{i}{\sqrt{k}}\right).$$

Hence
$$z_{2020} = z_{2019}\cdot\left(1 - \frac{i}{\sqrt{2020}}\right),$$

so
$$z_{2019} - z_{2020} = z_{2019}\left[1 - \left(1 - \frac{i}{\sqrt{2020}}\right)
\right] = z_{2019}\cdot\frac{i}{\sqrt{2020}}.$$

Taking moduli:
$$\left|z_{2019} - z_{2020}\right| = |z_{2019}|\cdot\frac{1}{\sqrt{2020}}.$$

## Step 3: Compute $|z_{2019}|$

$$|z_{2019}| = \prod_{k=1}^{2019}\left|1 - \frac{i}{\sqrt{k}}\right| = \prod_{k=
1}^{2019}\sqrt{1 + \frac{1}{k}} = \prod_{k=1}^{2019}\sqrt{\frac{k+1}{k}} = \sqrt
{\prod_{k=1}^{2019}\frac{k+1}{k}}.$$

The product telescopes:
$$\prod_{k=1}^{2019}\frac{k+1}{k} = \frac{2}{1}\cdot\frac{3}{2}\cdots\frac{2020}
{2019} = 2020.$$

Therefore
$$|z_{2019}| = \sqrt{2020}.$$

## Step 4: Final computation

$$\left|z_{2019} - z_{2020}\right| = \sqrt{2020}\cdot\frac{1}{\sqrt{2020}} = 1.$
