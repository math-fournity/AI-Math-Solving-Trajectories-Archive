$$F_{A,B}(x) \;=\; \sum_{n\ge 0} p_{A,B}(n)\,\frac{x^n}{n!}.$$

**Step 1 — EGF of a single block.** A "block" is a nonempty set of labeled eleme
nts. A block of size $a$ contributes the EGF term $x^a/a!$ (there is exactly one
 way to form an unlabeled block of $a$ given distinct elements, and the EGF conv
ention divides by $a!$). Restricting sizes to $A$, the EGF of the class $\mathca
l{B}_A$ of admissible blocks is

$$\beta_A(x) \;=\; \sum_{a\in A}\frac{x^a}{a!}.$$

**Step 2 — Partitions into exactly $k$ admissible blocks.** A set partition into

a_A(x)^k$; to pass from ordered tuples to unordered sets we divide by $k!$ (the
symmetric group $S_k$ acts freely on the $k$ distinct blocks). Hence the EGF for
 partitions into exactly $k$ admissible blocks is

$$\frac{\beta_A(x)^k}{k!} \;=\; \frac{1}{k!}\!\left(\sum_{a\in A}\frac{x^a}{a!}\
right)^{\!k}.$$

Concretely, the coefficient of $x^n/n!$ here is $\dfrac{1}{k!}\sum \dfrac{n!}{a_

$$F_{A,B}(x) \;=\; \sum_{k\in B}\frac{1}{k!}\,\beta_A(x)^k \;=\; \sum_{k\in B}\f
