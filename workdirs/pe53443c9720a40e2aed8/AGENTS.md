# Problem

A specialized logistics firm manages the security clearances for a set of researchers working on a project code-named $N$. The researchers are identified by the positive divisors of $N$ (including 1 and $N$ itself). To organize the team, the firm assigns each researcher one of four possible security badge colors.

The project $N$ is unique because its complexity is derived from more than one fundamental source; specifically, $N$ is not a power of any prime number.

The firm enforces a strict "Interference Rule" to prevent information leaks: If three researchers $a, b$, and $g$ are such that $g$ is the greatest common divisor of $a$ and $b$, and all three individuals $a, b,$ and $g$ are distinct people, then they must all be assigned different badge colors.

A "Valid Distribution" is any assignment of the four colors to the researchers that satisfies the Interference Rule for all possible combinations of researchers associated with project $N$.

What is the maximum possible number of Valid Distributions that can exist for such a project?
