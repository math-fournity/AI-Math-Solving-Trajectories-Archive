# Problem

A textile designer is commissioned to create a customized floor covering for a rectangular gallery space that measures exactly 9 meters by 18 meters. To complete this task, the designer must use exactly 18 specialized decorative fabric "banners."

Each banner is manufactured with a specific, identical geometry: it is derived from a 2-meter by 5-meter rectangular piece of fabric, but with exactly one of its four corner 1-meter squares removed (resulting in a banner with a total area of 9 square meters).

The banners must be laid flat to cover the entire 162-square-meter floor surface perfectly. The banners cannot overlap, they cannot be cut or folded, and they must stay within the boundaries of the gallery. Each banner can be rotated or flipped to fit into the arrangement, provided its edges align with the integer meter marks of the gallery floor.

In how many distinct ways can the designer arrange these 18 banners to cover the gallery floor completely?
