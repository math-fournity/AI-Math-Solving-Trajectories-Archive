Looking at this problem, I need to determine whether the matrix arithmetic-geome
tric mean iteration converges to a common limit, using the Kubo-Ando geometric m
ean $A \natural B = A^{1/2}(A^{-1/2}BA^{-1/2})^{1/2}A^{1/2}$.

**Answer: Yes, the sequences converge to the same matrix.**

## Proof

We use three standard properties of the Kubo-Ando geometric mean for positive de
finite Hermitian matrices:

- **(AM-GM)** $A \natural B \leq \frac{A+B}{2}$ in the Loewner order.
- **(Monotonicity)** If $A_1 \leq A_2$ and $B_1 \leq B_2$, then $A_1 \natural B_
1 \leq A_2 \natural B_2$.
- **(Idempotency)** $A \natural A = A$.

### Step 1: Establish $G_n \leq A_n$ for all $n \geq 1$.

For $n=0$: By (AM-GM), $G_1 = A_0 \natural G_0 = A \natural B \leq \frac{A+B}{2}
 = A_1$.

Inductively, suppose $G_n \leq A_n$. Then:
$$G_{n+1} = A_n \natural G_n \leq \frac{A_n + G_n}{2} = A_{n+1}$$
by (AM-GM). So $G_n \leq A_n$ for all $n \geq 1$. $\checkmark$

### Step 2: $\{A_n\}_{n\geq 1}$ is decreasing.

Since $G_n \leq A_n$:
$$A_{n+1} = \frac{A_n + G_n}{2} \leq \frac{A_n + A_n}{2} = A_n. \quad \checkmark
$$

### Step 3: $\{G_n\}_{n\geq 1}$ is increasing.

Since $A_n \geq G_n$ and $G_n \geq G_n$, by (Monotonicity):
$$G_{n+1} = A_n \natural G_n \geq G_n \natural G_n = G_n$$
by (Idempotency). $\checkmark$

### Step 4: Both sequences are bounded and hence converge.

From Steps 1–3, for all $n \geq 1$:
$$G_1 \leq G_n \leq A_n \leq A_1.$$

Both sequences are monotone and bounded in the Loewner order. **Monotone converg
ence for Hermitian matrices**: if $H_1 \geq H_2 \geq \cdots \geq C$ (bounded bel
ow), then $H_n$ converges. This follows because the telescoping partial sums $\s
um_{k=1}^{N}(H_k - H_{k+1}) = H_1 - H_{N+1}$ are bounded above by $H_1 - C$; sin
ce each summand is positive semidefinite and partial sums are bounded, each diag
onal entry (in any orthonormal basis) is a bounded non-negative series hence con
vergent, and off-diagonal entries converge by Cauchy–Schwarz. Thus $H_n \to H_\i
nfty$ in norm.

Therefore $A_n \to A_\infty$ and $G_n \to G_\infty$ for some positive definite H
ermitian matrices, with $G_\infty \leq A_\infty$.

### Step 5: The limits coincide.

Taking the limit as $n \to \infty$ in the arithmetic mean recurrence:
$$A_{n+1} = \frac{A_n + G_n}{2} \implies A_\infty = \frac{A_\infty + G_\infty}{2
}.$$

Solving: $2A_\infty = A_\infty + G_\infty$, hence:
$$\boxed{A_\infty = G_\infty.}$$

Both sequences converge to the same matrix. $\blacksquare$
