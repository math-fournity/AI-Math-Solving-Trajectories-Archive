## Computing $a$: Injective homomorphisms

$\phi_r$ is injective iff $\ker(\phi_r) = \{0\}$, i.e., $rx \equiv 0 \pmod{15} \
Rightarrow x \equiv 0 \pmod{15}$.

By CRT, $R \cong \mathbb{Z}/3\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/5\mathbb{Z}$, so the u
nit group has order:
$$|R^\times| = \varphi(15) = \varphi(3)\cdot\varphi(5) = 2 \cdot 4 = 8.$$

The units are $\{1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 11, 13, 14\}$.

$$\boxed{a = 8}$$
