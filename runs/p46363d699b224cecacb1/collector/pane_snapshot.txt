## Solution

### Step 1. Steady-state (equilibrium) solution

Set $u_t=0$, so $U''(x)=0$, giving $U(x)=Ax+B$.

- $U(0)=u_0 \Rightarrow B=u_0$.
- $U'(1)=-U(1)+u_1 \Rightarrow A=-(A+u_0)+u_1 \Rightarrow 2A=u_1-u_0 \Rightarrow
 A=\dfrac{u_1-u_0}{2}$.

$$U(x)=u_0+\frac{u_1-u_0}{2}\,x.$$

### Step 2. Reduction to homogeneous problem

Let $v(x,t)=u(x,t)-U(x)$. Then $v_{xx}=v_t$ with

$$v(0,t)=0,\qquad v_x(1,t)=-v(1,t),\qquad v(x,0)=u_0-U(x)=\frac{u_0-u_1}{2}\,x=:
\varphi(x).$$

### Step 3. Separation of variables — eigenvalue problem

With $v=XT$: $X''+\lambda X=0$, $X(0)=0$, $X'(1)=-X(1)$.

- $\lambda=0$: $X=Ax+B$; $X(0)=0\Rightarrow B=0$; $X'(1)=-X(1)\Rightarrow A=-A\R
ightarrow A=0$. Trivial.
- $\lambda<0$ ($\lambda=-\nu^2$): $X=B\sinh(\nu x)$; condition gives $\nu\cosh\n
u=-\sinh\nu$, i.e. $\tanh\nu=-\nu$, no positive solution.
- $\lambda=\mu^2>0$: $X=B\sin(\mu x)$; the Robin condition gives

$$\mu\cos\mu=-\sin\mu\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \boxed{\tan\mu=-\mu.}$$

Let $\mu_n>0$ ($n=1,2,\dots$) be the positive roots of $\tan\mu=-\mu$. The eigen
functions $X_n(x)=\sin(\mu_n x)$ form an orthogonal system on $[0,1]$ (regular S
turm–Liouville problem).

### Step 4. Series expansion

$$v(x,t)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}c_n\sin(\mu_n x)\,e^{-\mu_n^2 t},\qquad c_n=\frac{\i
nt_0^1\varphi(x)\sin(\mu_n x)\,dx}{\int_0^1\sin^2(\mu_n x)\,dx}.$$

**Numerator.** Using $\int_0^1 x\sin(\mu x)\,dx=-\dfrac{\cos\mu}{\mu}+\dfrac{\si
n\mu}{\mu^2}$ and $\sin\mu_n=-\mu_n\cos\mu_n$:

$$\int_0^1 x\sin(\mu_n x)\,dx=-\frac{\cos\mu_n}{\mu_n}+\frac{-\mu_n\cos\mu_n}{\m
u_n^2}=-\frac{2\cos\mu_n}{\mu_n}.$$

So the numerator is $\dfrac{u_0-u_1}{2}\cdot\left(-\dfrac{2\cos\mu_n}{\mu_n}\rig
ht)=-\dfrac{(u_0-u_1)\cos\mu_n}{\mu_n}$.

**Denominator.** $\displaystyle\int_0^1\sin^2(\mu_n x)\,dx=\frac{1}{2}-\frac{\si
n\mu_n\cos\mu_n}{2\mu_n}=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{\cos^2\mu_n}{2}=\frac{1+\cos^2\mu_n}{
2}$, again using $\sin\mu_n=-\mu_n\cos\mu_n$.

**Coefficient.**

$$c_n=\frac{-\dfrac{(u_0-u_1)\cos\mu_n}{\mu_n}}{\dfrac{1+\cos^2\mu_n}{2}}=\frac{
2(u_1-u_0)\cos\mu_n}{\mu_n(1+\cos^2\mu_n)}.$$

### Step 5. Full solution

$$\boxed{\,u(x,t)=u_0+\frac{u_1-u_0}{2}\,x+2(u_1-u_0)\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\c
os\mu_n}{\mu_n\bigl(1+\cos^2\mu_n\bigr)}\,\sin(\mu_n x)\,e^{-\mu_n^2 t}\,}$$

where $\{\mu_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ are the positive roots of $\tan\mu=-\mu$.

### Step 6. Verification

- **PDE:** Each term $\sin(\mu_n x)e^{-\mu_n^2 t}$ satisfies $X''=T'/(-\lambda)$
, i.e. $v_{xx}=v_t$; the linear part $U(x)$ has $U''=0=U_t$. ✓
- **BC at $x=0$:** $\sin(0)=0$ and $U(0)=u_0$, so $u(0,t)=u_0$. ✓
- **BC at $x=1$:** Using $\sin\mu_n=-\mu_n\cos\mu_n$,

$$u_x(1,t)=\frac{u_1-u_0}{2}+2(u_1-u_0)\sum_n\frac{\cos^2\mu_n}{1+\cos^2\mu_n}e^
{-\mu_n^2 t},$$

$$-u(1,t)+u_1=-u_0-\frac{u_1-u_0}{2}+2(u_1-u_0)\sum_n\frac{\cos^2\mu_n}{1+\cos^2
\mu_n}e^{-\mu_n^2 t}+u_1=\frac{u_1-u_0}{2}+2(u_1-u_0)\sum_n\frac{\cos^2\mu_n}{1+
\cos^2\mu_n}e^{-\mu_n^2 t}.$$

These are equal. ✓
- **IC:** At $t=0$ the series is the eigenfunction expansion of $\varphi(x)=\fra
c{u_0-u_1}{2}x$, so $u(x,0)=U(x)+\varphi(x)=u_0$. ✓
