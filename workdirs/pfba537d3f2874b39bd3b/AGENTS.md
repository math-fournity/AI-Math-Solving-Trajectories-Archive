# Problem

In the competitive world of high-speed data transmission, a communications firm is testing the signal integrity of various digital "packets." A packet is defined by its intensity value $x$, which must be an integer greater than $10^{10}$.

For every packet, the engineering team records a "Signal Signature" consisting of a pair of values: $(A, B)$.
- Value $A$ is the sequence of the first three significant digits of the intensity $x$.
- Value $B$ is the sequence of the first three significant digits of the intensity $x^4$ (the fourth power of the intensity).

For example, if a specific intensity $x$ results in $10^{10^{10}}$, the recorded Signal Signature pair would be $(100, 100)$, as both $x$ and $x^4$ begin with the digits "100".

Calculate the total number of distinct Signal Signature pairs $(A, B)$ that can possibly be generated across all valid integer intensities $x > 10^{10}$.
