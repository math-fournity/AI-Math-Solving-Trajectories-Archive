## Proof

The boundary curve is $y^2 + 2xy + 40|x| = 400$. I analyze by the sign of $x$.

**Case 1: $x \ge 0$, so $|x| = x$.**

$$y^2 + 2xy + 40x = 400$$

Completing the square in $y$:

$$(y+x)^2 - x^2 + 40x = 400 \implies (y+x)^2 = x^2 - 40x + 400 = (x-20)^2$$

Thus $y + x = \pm(x-20)$, giving two lines (restricted to $x \ge 0$):
- $y = -20$
- $y = -2x + 20$

**Case 2: $x \le 0$, so $|x| = -x$.**

$$y^2 + 2xy - 40x = 400$$

$$(y+x)^2 - x^2 - 40x = 400 \implies (y+x)^2 = x^2 + 40x + 400 = (x+20)^2$$

Thus $y + x = \pm(x+20)$, giving two lines (restricted to $x \le 0$):
- $y = 20$
- $y = -2x - 20$

**The closed boundary.** The curve is the union of four rays:
- $y = -20$ for $x \ge 0$ (from $(0,-20)$ rightward)
- $y = -2x+20$ for $x \ge 0$ (from $(0,20)$ to the right and down)
- $y = 20$ for $x \le 0$ (from $(0,20)$ leftward)
- $y = -2x-20$ for $x \le 0$ (from $(0,-20)$ to the left and up)

The four vertices where consecutive rays meet:
- $(0, 20)$: intersection of $y = -2x+20$ and $y = 20$
- $(20, -20)$: intersection of $y = -2x+20$ and $y = -20$
- $(0, -20)$: intersection of $y = -20$ and $y = -2x-20$
- $(-20, 20)$: intersection of $y = -2x-20$ and $y = 20$

**Area via the Shoelace formula.** Ordering the vertices $(0,20) \to (20,-20) \t
o (0,-20) \to (-20,20)$:

$$\sum x_i y_{i+1} = 0(-20) + 20(-20) + 0(20) + (-20)(20) = -800$$

$$\sum y_i x_{i+1} = 20(20) + (-20)(0) + (-20)(-20) + 20(0) = 800$$

$$\text{Area} = \left|\frac{-800 - 800}{2}\right| = 800$$

**Verification.** Opposite sides have direction vectors $(20,-40)$ and $(-20,40)
 = -(20,-40)$; and $(-20,0)$ and $(20,0) = -(-20,0)$. The quadrilateral is a **p
arallelogram** with base $20$ (along $y=-20$) and height $40$ (perpendicular dis
tance between $y=-20$ and $y=20$), giving area $20 \times 40 = 800$. ✓

The area of the botanical garden is $\boxed{800}$ square meters.
