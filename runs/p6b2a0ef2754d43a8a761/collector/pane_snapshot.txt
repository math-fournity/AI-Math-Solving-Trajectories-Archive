Looking at this problem, I need to find the smallest $n$ such that any $n$ integ
er-coordinate points contain two with squared distance divisible by $2016$.

**Factorization:** $2016 = 2^5 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 7 = 32 \cdot 9 \cdot 7$.

By CRT, $d^2 \equiv 0 \pmod{2016}$ iff $d^2 \equiv 0$ modulo each of $32, 9, 7$.
 I analyze each prime power separately, finding the maximum number of points wit
h no "bad pair" (squared distance $\equiv 0$) modulo each.

---

**Mod 7:** Since $(-1)^3 = -1 \not\equiv 1 \pmod 7$, $-1$ is not a quadratic res
idue mod 7. So $x^2 + y^2 \equiv 0 \pmod 7$ iff $x \equiv y \equiv 0 \pmod 7$. A
 bad pair mod 7 requires both coordinate differences divisible by 7, i.e., same
residue mod 7. This gives $7^2 = 49$ classes, each a singleton. **Max independen
t set: 49.**

**Mod 9:** The quadratic residues mod 9 are $\{0,1,4,7\}$; $-1 \equiv 8$ is not
among them. Checking all sums of two QRs mod 9: only $0+0 \equiv 0$. So $x^2+y^2
 \equiv 0 \pmod 9$ iff $3\mid x, 3\mid y$. A bad pair mod 9 requires same residu
e mod 3, giving $3^2 = 9$ classes of size 9. **Max independent set: 9.**

**Mod 32:** This is the subtle case. I determine when $dx^2 + dy^2 \equiv 0 \pmo
d{32}$ by examining 2-adic valuations $v_2$:

- **$v_2(dx) \neq v_2(dy)$ (both finite):** $v_2(dx^2+dy^2) = 2\min(v_2(dx),v_2(
dy))$. Need $\geq 5$, so $\min \geq 3$, meaning $8\mid dx, 8\mid dy$.
- **$v_2(dx) = v_2(dy) = a$ (finite):** Writing $dx=2^a u, dy=2^a v$ with $u,v$
odd, $u^2+v^2 \equiv 2\pmod{8}$ so $v_2 = 2a+1$. Need $a \geq 2$, meaning $4\mid
 dx, 4\mid dy$.
- **One coordinate $\equiv 0$:** Need $v_2$ of the other $\geq 3$, i.e., $8$ div
ides it.

Combining: the bad pairs are exactly those where $dx \equiv dy \equiv 0 \pmod{4}
$ **and** $dx \equiv dy \pmod{8}$. Equivalently, $(dx \bmod 8, dy \bmod 8) \in \
{(0,0), (4,4)\}$.

This partitions the 64 residue classes in $(\mathbb{Z}/8)^2$ into 32 pairs $\{(r
,s), (r+4, s+4)\}$. Two points form a bad pair mod 32 iff their mod-8 residues l
ie in the same pair. Each pair corresponds to a clique of size 32 in $(\mathbb{Z
}/32)^2$. **Max independent set: 32.**

---

**Combining via CRT:** A pair of points has $d^2 \equiv 0 \pmod{2016}$ iff it is
 a bad pair in **all three** moduli simultaneously. By CRT, the residue mod 2016
 is determined by residues mod 32, 9, 7. Two points are a bad pair mod 2016 iff
they share the same:
- mod-7 residue (49 options),
- mod-8 group in the 32-partition (32 options),
- mod-3 residue (9 options).

This gives $49 \times 32 \times 9 = 14112$ "color classes," each of size $1 \tim
es 32 \times 9 = 288$ (since the mod-7 residue is fully determined, while the mo
d-8 group allows 32 mod-32 residues and the mod-3 class allows 9 mod-9 residues)
. Two points in the same class are always a bad pair; two in different classes a
re never a bad pair.

**Upper bound:** Color each point by its triple (mod-7 residue, mod-8 group, mod
-3 residue). There are $14112$ colors. By pigeonhole, any $14113$ points contain
 two sharing a triple, hence a bad pair.

**Lower bound:** Select one representative from each of the $14112$ color classe
s (via CRT, pick any mod-32 residue in the group, any mod-9 residue in the class
, and the mod-7 residue). These $14112$ points have pairwise distinct triples, s
o no two form a bad pair.

---

**Answer:** The smallest $n$ is $\boxed{14113}$.
