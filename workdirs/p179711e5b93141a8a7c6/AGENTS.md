# Problem

In a remote sector of the galaxy, two spherical space stations are positioned in a stable orbit. The first station, Alpha, has a radius equivalent to the 2nd harmonic number, $H_2$. The second station, Beta, has a larger radius equivalent to the 7th harmonic number, $H_7$.

Engineers are planning to construct two types of straight-line transit tubes between the stations. The first is an "External Bridge," which connects a point on the hull of Alpha to a point on the hull of Beta such that the bridge remains entirely on one side of the line connecting the stations' centers (a common external tangent segment). The second is an "Internal Bridge," which connects a point on the hull of Alpha to a point on the hull of Beta by crossing the line segment connecting the two centers (a common internal tangent segment).

Upon reviewing the blueprints, the lead architect notices a specific geometric ratio: the length of the External Bridge is exactly twice the length of the Internal Bridge. 

Based on this structural constraint, calculate the exact distance between the centers of the two space stations.
