## Proof

**Step 1: Substitution to reduce to a Beta integral.**

Let $u = x^4$, so $x = u^{1/4}$, $dx = \frac{1}{4}u^{-3/4}\,du$. Then:
- $x^{4n-2} = u^{n-1/2}$
- $(1+x^4)^n = (1+u)^n$

$$I_n = \int_0^\infty \frac{u^{n-1/2}}{(1+u)^n}\cdot\frac{1}{4}u^{-3/4}\,du = \f
rac{1}{4}\int_0^\infty \frac{u^{n-5/4}}{(1+u)^n}\,du.$$

This is the Euler Beta integral $\int_0^\infty \frac{u^{a-1}}{(1+u)^{a+b}}\,du =
 B(a,b) = \frac{\Gamma(a)\Gamma(b)}{\Gamma(a+b)}$, with $a-1 = n-\tfrac{5}{4}$ (
so $a = n-\tfrac{1}{4}$) and $a+b = n$ (so $b = \tfrac{1}{4}$). Thus:

$$\boxed{I_n = \frac{1}{4}\,\frac{\Gamma\!\left(n-\tfrac{1}{4}\right)\,\Gamma\!\
left(\tfrac{1}{4}\right)}{\Gamma(n)}.}$$

**Step 2: Asymptotics of the Gamma ratio.**

Using the standard asymptotic $\frac{\Gamma(n+s)}{\Gamma(n)} \sim n^s$ as $n\to\
infty$ (a consequence of Stirling's formula), with $s = -\tfrac{1}{4}$:

$$\frac{\Gamma\!\left(n-\tfrac{1}{4}\right)}{\Gamma(n)} \sim n^{-1/4}.$$

*Verification via Stirling:* $\Gamma(n)\sim\sqrt{2\pi/n}\,(n/e)^n$ and $\Gamma(n
-\tfrac14)\sim\sqrt{2\pi/(n-\tfrac14)}\,((n-\tfrac14)/e)^{n-1/4}$, so

$$\frac{\Gamma(n-\tfrac14)}{\Gamma(n)} \sim \sqrt{\tfrac{n}{n-1/4}}\cdot e^{1/4}
\cdot n^{-1/4}\cdot\Bigl(1-\tfrac{1}{4n}\Bigr)^{n-1/4} \;\longrightarrow\; 1\cdo
t e^{1/4}\cdot n^{-1/4}\cdot e^{-1/4} = n^{-1/4}.$$

**Step 3: The limit.**

Combining Steps 1 and 2:

$$I_n \sim \frac{1}{4}\,\Gamma\!\left(\tfrac{1}{4}\right)\,n^{-1/4}.$$

Since $\Gamma(\tfrac14)$ is a finite positive constant and $n^{-1/4}\to 0$ as $n
\to\infty$:

$$\lim_{n\to\infty} I_n = 0.$$

**Step 4: Sanity check via Laplace-type analysis (independent confirmation).**

Rewrite $I_n = \int_0^\infty x^{-2}\!\left(\frac{x^4}{1+x^4}\right)^n dx$. Since
 $\frac{x^4}{1+x^4} = 1 - \frac{1}{1+x^4} \approx 1 - x^{-4}$ for large $x$, the
 integrand behaves like $x^{-2}e^{-n/x^4}$, with the transition at $x\sim n^{1/4
}$. Setting $x = n^{1/4}t$:

$$I_n \approx n^{-1/4}\int_0^\infty t^{-2}e^{-1/t^4}\,dt = n^{-1/4}\cdot\tfrac{1
}{4}\Gamma\!\left(\tfrac{1}{4}\right),$$

where the last equality follows from $s=1/t^4$: $\int_0^\infty t^{-2}e^{-1/t^4}d
t = \frac{1}{4}\int_0^\infty s^{-3/4}e^{-s}\,ds = \frac{1}{4}\Gamma(\tfrac14)$.
This matches the Beta-function result exactly, confirming $I_n \sim \frac{1}{4}\
Gamma(\tfrac14)\,n^{-1/4}\to 0$.

## Conclusion

$$\lim_{n\to\infty} I_n = 0.$$
