### Setup

A number with binary length $L$ (no leading zeros) is "High-Priority" if its pop
count (number of 1s) exceeds $L/2$, i.e. $\text{ones} \ge \lfloor L/2\rfloor + 1
$.

Since $2003 = 11111010011_2$ has 11 bits, we count:
- Complete bit-lengths $L = 1, \dots, 10$ (full ranges $[2^{L-1}, 2^L-1]$).
- $L=11$: partial range $[1024, 2003]$.

### Counting formula for full $L$-bit ranges

The leading bit is 1. Let $j$ = number of 1s among the remaining $L-1$ bits. Tot
al ones $= 1+j \ge \lfloor L/2\rfloor+1$, so $j \ge \lfloor L/2\rfloor$. The cou
nt is
$$\sum_{j=\lfloor L/2\rfloor}^{L-1} \binom{L-1}{j}.$$

| $L$ | $\lfloor L/2\rfloor$ | $L-1$ | Count |
|-----|-----|-----|-------|
| 1 | 0 | 0 | $\binom00=1$ |
| 2 | 1 | 1 | $\binom11=1$ |
| 3 | 1 | 2 | $2+1=3$ |
| 4 | 2 | 3 | $3+1=4$ |
| 5 | 2 | 4 | $6+4+1=11$ |
| 6 | 3 | 5 | $10+5+1=16$ |
| 7 | 3 | 6 | $20+15+6+1=42$ |
| 8 | 4 | 7 | $35+21+7+1=64$ |
| 9 | 4 | 8 | $70+56+28+8+1=163$ |
| 10 | 5 | 9 | $126+84+36+9+1=256$ |

**Subtotal ($L=1$ to $10$):** $1+1+3+4+11+16+42+64+163+256 = \mathbf{561}$.

### $L=11$ partial range $[1024, 2003]$

Full $L=11$ range $[1024, 2047]$ count:
$$\sum_{j=5}^{10}\binom{10}{j} = 252+210+120+45+10+1 = 638.$$

Excluded range $[2004, 2047]$ (44 numbers). Now $2004 = 11111010100_2$ and $2047
 = 11111111111_2$. All these numbers share the prefix $11111$ (five 1s), with a
6-bit suffix $s$ ranging from $010100_2 = 20$ to $111111_2 = 63$.

Total ones $= 5 + \text{popcount}(s) \ge 6 \iff \text{popcount}(s) \ge 1$. Since
 $s \ge 20 > 0$, every suffix has popcount $\ge 1$, so **all 44 excluded numbers
 are High-Priority**.

Therefore:
$$\text{count}_{[1024,\,2003]} = 638 - 44 = 594.$$

### Final tally

$$N = 561 + 594 = 1155.$$

$$\boxed{N \bmod 1000 = 155}.$$
