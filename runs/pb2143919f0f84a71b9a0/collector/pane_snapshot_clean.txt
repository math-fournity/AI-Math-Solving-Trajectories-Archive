## Solution

We evaluate $\displaystyle S = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{2^{k+7}}{5^{2^{k}} + 1}
$.

**Key identity.** For any $x>1$ and integer $k\ge 0$,

$$
\frac{2^k}{x^{2^k}-1} - \frac{2^{k+1}}{x^{2^{k+1}}-1}
= \frac{2^k}{x^{2^k}-1} - \frac{2^{k+1}}{(x^{2^k})^2-1}
= \frac{2^k}{x^{2^k}-1} - \frac{2^{k+1}}{(x^{2^k}-1)(x^{2^k}+1)}.
$$

Factoring out $\dfrac{2^k}{x^{2^k}-1}$:

$$
= \frac{2^k}{x^{2^k}-1}\left(1 - \frac{2}{x^{2^k}+1}\right)
= \frac{2^k}{x^{2^k}-1}\cdot\frac{x^{2^k}+1-2}{x^{2^k}+1}
= \frac{2^k}{x^{2^k}-1}\cdot\frac{x^{2^k}-1}{x^{2^k}+1}
= \frac{2^k}{x^{2^k}+1}.
$$

So, defining $\displaystyle a_k = \frac{2^k}{x^{2^k}-1}$, we have the telescopin
g relation

$$
\frac{2^k}{x^{2^k}+1} = a_k - a_{k+1}.
$$

**Telescoping sum.** Summing from $k=0$ to $N$:

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{N}\frac{2^k}{x^{2^k}+1} = a_0 - a_{N+1} = \frac{1}{x-1} - \frac{2^{N
+1}}{x^{2^{N+1}}-1}.
$$

For $x>1$, as $N\to\infty$ the denominator $x^{2^{N+1}}$ grows doubly-exponentia
lly while the numerator $2^{N+1}$ grows only exponentially, so $a_{N+1}\to 0$. T
herefore

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{2^k}{x^{2^k}+1} = \frac{1}{x-1}.
$$

**Applying to our series.** Factor out $2^7=128$ and set $x=5$:

$$
S = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{2^{k+7}}{5^{2^k}+1}
= 2^7\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{2^k}{5^{2^k}+1}
= 128\cdot\frac{1}{5-1}
= 128\cdot\frac{1}{4}
= 32.
$$

**Numerical verification.**
- $k=0$: $\frac{128}{6} = \frac{64}{3}\approx 21.333$
- $k=1$: $\frac{256}{26} = \frac{128}{13}\approx 9.846$, cumulative $\approx 31.
180$
- $k=2$: $\frac{512}{626} = \frac{256}{313}\approx 0.818$, cumulative $\approx 3
1.998$
- $k=3$: $\frac{1024}{390626}\approx 0.00262$, cumulative $\approx 32.001$

The partial sums converge rapidly to $32$, confirming the closed form.

$$
\boxed{\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{2^{k+7}}{5^{2^k}+1}=32}
$$
