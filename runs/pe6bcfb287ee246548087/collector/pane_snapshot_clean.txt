Looking at this problem, I need to count permutations of the multiset {A, A, L,
G, E, B, R} into 7 positions where no position receives its original letter (pos
itions 1 and 7 both forbid A; positions 2–6 forbid L, G, E, B, R respectively).

## Proof

**Setup.** The original word ALGEBRA occupies positions 1–7 as: A, L, G, E, B, R
, A. We seek the number of distinct permutations of the multiset {A, A, L, G, E,
 B, R} such that:
- Position 1 ≠ A, Position 7 ≠ A
- Position 2 ≠ L, Position 3 ≠ G, Position 4 ≠ E, Position 5 ≠ B, Position 6 ≠ R

**Key observation.** Since positions 1 and 7 both forbid A, the two A's must be
placed in positions from {2, 3, 4, 5, 6}. We choose 2 of these 5 positions for t
he A's, then place the remaining 5 distinct letters {L, G, E, B, R} into the rem
aining 5 positions (which include positions 1, 7, and the 3 unchosen positions f
rom {2,...,6}), subject to the restriction that each of those 3 positions doesn'
t receive its original letter.

**Step 1: Choose positions for the A's.** The A's go into 2 of the 5 positions {
2,3,4,5,6}: this gives $\binom{5}{2} = 10$ choices.

**Step 2: Count valid placements of the remaining letters.** After placing the A
's, the 5 remaining positions are $T = \{1, 7\} \cup (\{2,3,4,5,6\} \setminus \{
\text{A positions}\})$. We place {L, G, E, B, R} into $T$ with restrictions:
- Positions 1 and 7 are **free** (their forbidden letter is A, already placed).
- The 3 positions from {2,...,6} that remain in $T$ each forbid one specific let
ter.

This is a permutation of 5 distinct items into 5 positions with exactly 3 restri
cted positions. By inclusion-exclusion over the 3 restrictions:

$$\text{Count} = \sum_{j=0}^{3} (-1)^j \binom{3}{j}(5-j)!$$

Computing each term:
- $j=0$: $\binom{3}{0} \cdot 5! = 120$
- $j=1$: $\binom{3}{1} \cdot 4! = 3 \cdot 24 = 72$
- $j=2$: $\binom{3}{2} \cdot 3! = 3 \cdot 6 = 18$
- $j=3$: $\binom{3}{3} \cdot 2! = 1 \cdot 2 = 2$

$$\text{Count} = 120 - 72 + 18 - 2 = 64$$

**Crucially, this count is the same for every choice of A positions.** The inclu
sion-exclusion depends only on the *number* of restricted positions (3), not whi
ch specific positions they are, because: (a) all 5 letters are distinct, (b) eac
h restriction fixes one distinct letter to one distinct position, and (c) the fr
ee positions (1, 7) don't interact with any restriction.

**Step 3: Combine.** Total count = (choices for A positions) × (valid letter pla
cements):

$$N = 10 \times 64 = \boxed{640}$$

**Verification via direct inclusion-exclusion.** Using the full inclusion-exclus
ion over all 7 events $E_i$ (position $i$ gets its original letter), splitting b
y how many of {1,7} are in the subset $S$:

| $k$ | Neither {1,7}: $\binom{5}{k}\frac{(7-k)!}{2!}$ | Exactly one: $2\binom{5
}{k-1}(7-k)!$ | Both: $\binom{5}{k-2}(7-k)!$ | Total | $(-1)^k$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 2520 | 0 | 0 | 2520 | + |
| 1 | 1800 | 1440 | 0 | 3240 | − |
| 2 | 600 | 1200 | 120 | 1920 | + |
| 3 | 120 | 480 | 120 | 720 | − |
| 4 | 15 | 120 | 60 | 195 | + |
| 5 | 1 | 20 | 20 | 41 | − |
| 6 | 0 | 2 | 5 | 7 | + |
| 7 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1 | − |

$$N = 2520 - 3240 + 1920 - 720 + 195 - 41 + 7 - 1 = 640$$

Both methods agree: **the answer is 640**.
