## Proof

We are given a linear function $f$ on a Banach space $E$ with $f(x)=0$ for every
 $x$ satisfying $\|x\|<1$. We show $\|f\|=0$.

**Step 1: $f$ vanishes on all of $E$.**

Let $x\in E$ be arbitrary. If $x=0$, then $f(x)=f(0)=0$ by linearity. Suppose $x
\neq 0$, so $\|x\|>0$. Define
$$
y \;=\; \frac{x}{2\|x\|}.
$$
Then
$$
\|y\| \;=\; \frac{\|x\|}{2\|x\|} \;=\; \frac{1}{2} \;<\; 1,
$$
so by hypothesis $f(y)=0$. By linearity of $f$,
$$
0 \;=\; f(y) \;=\; f\!\left(\frac{x}{2\|x\|}\right) \;=\; \frac{1}{2\|x\|}\,f(x)
,
$$
and since $\frac{1}{2\|x\|}\neq 0$, we conclude $f(x)=0$.

Thus $f\equiv 0$ on $E$.

**Step 2: The operator norm is $0$.**

The operator norm is
$$
\|f\| \;=\; \sup_{\|x\|\le 1}|f(x)| \;=\; \sup_{x\neq 0}\frac{|f(x)|}{\|x\|}.
$$
Since $f(x)=0$ for every $x\in E$, both suprema equal $0$. Hence
$$
\boxed{\|f\| \;=\; 0.}
$$

**Conclusion.** Yes, the operator norm of $f$ is $0$. The hypothesis that $f$ va
nishes on the open unit ball forces $f$ to vanish everywhere by linearity (resca
ling any nonzero vector into the unit ball), so $f$ is the zero functional and i
ts norm is $0$.
