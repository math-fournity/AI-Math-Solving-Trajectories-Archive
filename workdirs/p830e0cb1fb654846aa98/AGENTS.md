# Problem

In a futuristic data-storage facility, a supercomputer’s total capacity, represented by the value $A$, is distributed across three primary processing cores. The energy consumption of these cores is measured in "bits of power," and the facility can only operate at peak efficiency if the total sum of these power values $A$ is a perfect square of a positive integer.

The power outputs of the three cores are as follows:
- The first core consumes exactly $2^{182}$ bits.
- The second core consumes $4^x$ bits, where $x$ is a positive integer representing the variable frequency setting.
- The third core consumes exactly $8^{700}$ bits.

The Chief Engineer needs to overclock the system to the highest possible frequency. What is the greatest possible positive integer $x$ such that the total power $A = 2^{182} + 4^x + 8^{700}$ is a perfect square?
