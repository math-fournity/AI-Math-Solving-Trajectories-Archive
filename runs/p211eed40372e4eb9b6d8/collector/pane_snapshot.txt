## Proof

Set $w = z - i$. The series becomes

$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n!\, w^{n!}.$$

This is a power series in $w$ of the form $\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} c_k w^k$ where th
e coefficient $c_k$ is nonzero only when $k = n!$ for some positive integer $n$,
 in which case $c_{n!} = n!$; all other coefficients are $0$.

### Radius of convergence via Cauchy–Hadamard

By the Cauchy–Hadamard formula,

$$\frac{1}{R} = \limsup_{k \to \infty} |c_k|^{1/k}.$$

For $k = n!$, we have $|c_k|^{1/k} = (n!)^{1/n!}$. Taking logarithms,

$$\log\!\big((n!)^{1/n!}\big) = \frac{\log(n!)}{n!}.$$

Since $n!$ grows faster than any power of $\log(n!)$, we have $\frac{\log(n!)}{n
!} \to 0$ as $n \to \infty$. Hence

$$(n!)^{1/n!} = e^{\,\log(n!)/n!} \longrightarrow e^0 = 1.$$

For all other $k$ (not of the form $n!$), $c_k = 0$, so $|c_k|^{1/k} = 0$.

The $\limsup$ over **all** $k \to \infty$ is therefore determined by the subsequ
ence $k = n!$:

$$\limsup_{k \to \infty} |c_k|^{1/k} = \lim_{n \to \infty} (n!)^{1/n!} = 1.$$

Thus $R = 1$.

### Behavior on the boundary $|w| = 1$

On the boundary $|w| = 1$, the general term satisfies

$$\left| n!\, w^{n!} \right| = n! \cdot |w|^{n!} = n! \cdot 1 = n! \longrightarr
ow \infty.$$

In particular, the general term does **not** tend to $0$, so the series **diverg
es at every point** of the boundary $|w| = 1$ by the divergence test.

### Conclusion

The series converges exactly on the open disk $\{w : |w| < 1\}$ and diverges eve
rywhere on $|w| = 1$. Translating back to $z$ via $w = z - i$:

$$\boxed{\,\{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z - i| < 1\}\,}$$

is the disk of convergence (open disk of radius $1$ centered at $i$), and the se
ries diverges at every point of the boundary $|z - i| = 1$.
