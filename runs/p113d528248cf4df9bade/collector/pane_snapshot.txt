## Solution

We solve the PDE
$$u_{xx} + 3u_{xy} + 2u_{yy} = 0.$$

### Step 1. Factor the differential operator

Writing $D_x = \partial/\partial x$ and $D_y = \partial/\partial y$, the operato
r is
$$D_x^2 + 3D_xD_y + 2D_y^2 = (D_x + D_y)(D_x + 2D_y),$$
since the two linear factors of $r^2 + 3r + 2 = (r+1)(r+2)$ give slopes $a_1 = 1
$, $a_2 = 2$.

### Step 2. Characteristics of each factor

For a first-order operator $D_x + aD_y$, the characteristic curves satisfy
$$\frac{dy}{dx} = a \quad\Longrightarrow\quad y - ax = \text{const}.$$
Hence the general solution of $(D_x + aD_y)w = 0$ is $w = \Phi(y - ax)$ for an a
rbitrary differentiable function $\Phi$.

For our two factors:
- $(D_x + D_y)$: invariant $\xi = y - x$,
- $(D_x + 2D_y)$: invariant $\eta = y - 2x$.

### Step 3. General solution of the factored equation

The equation $(D_x + D_y)(D_x + 2D_y)u = 0$ has general solution
$$u(x,y) = F(\xi) + G(\eta) = F(y - x) + G(y - 2x),$$
where $F$ and $G$ are arbitrary twice-differentiable functions. (The first term
is annihilated by $D_x + D_y$; the second by $D_x + 2D_y$; both are therefore an
nihilated by the full product operator.)

### Step 4. Verification

Let $\xi = y - x$, $\eta = y - 2x$, and $u = F(\xi) + G(\eta)$. Then
$$u_x = -F'(\xi) - 2G'(\eta), \qquad u_y = F'(\xi) + G'(\eta),$$
$$u_{xx} = F''(\xi) + 4G''(\eta), \qquad u_{yy} = F''(\xi) + G''(\eta), \qquad u
_{xy} = -F''(\xi) - 2G''(\eta).$$

Substituting:
$$u_{xx} + 3u_{xy} + 2u_{yy} = \bigl(1 - 3 + 2\bigr)F''(\xi) + \bigl(4 - 6 + 2\b
igr)G''(\eta) = 0\cdot F'' + 0\cdot G'' = 0. \checkmark$$

### Conclusion

The general solution is
$$\boxed{\,u(x,y) = F(y - x) + G(y - 2x)\,}$$
where $F$ and $G$ are arbitrary twice-continuously-differentiable functions of o
ne variable.
