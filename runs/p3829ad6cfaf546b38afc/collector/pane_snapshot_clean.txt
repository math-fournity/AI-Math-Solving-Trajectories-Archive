## Proof

Let $X$ be a projective curve over a field $k$ (a projective, integral, one-dime
nsional $k$-scheme), and let $g = p_a(X)$ denote its arithmetic genus. Let $K_X$
 be a canonical Cartier divisor on $X$.

**Riemann–Roch theorem for curves.** For every Cartier divisor $D$ on $X$,
$$
h^0(\mathcal{O}_X(D)) - h^0(\mathcal{O}_X(K_X - D)) = \deg D + 1 - g. \tag{RR}
$$
This is the Grothendieck–Riemann–Roch / Serre duality formulation valid for *any
* projective curve (including singular and non-smooth ones), with $g = p_a(X)$ t
he arithmetic genus.

**Key observation.** Since $X$ is integral, every non-zero effective Cartier div
isor has strictly positive degree, and the zero divisor has degree $0$. Equivale
ntly, if $\mathcal{O}_X(E)$ has a non-zero global section, then $E$ is linearly
equivalent to an effective divisor, so $\deg E \geq 0$. Contrapositively:
$$
\deg E < 0 \implies h^0(\mathcal{O}_X(E)) = 0. \tag{$\ast$}
$$

**Choosing $N$.** Set
$$
N = 2g - 2 = 2\,p_a(X) - 2.
$$
For any Cartier divisor $D$ with $\deg D > N$, we have
$$
\deg(K_X - D) = \deg K_X - \deg D = (2g - 2) - \deg D < (2g-2) - (2g-2) = 0.
$$
By $(\ast)$, $h^0(\mathcal{O}_X(K_X - D)) = 0$. Substituting into (RR):
$$
h^0(\mathcal{O}_X(D)) = \deg D + 1 - g = \deg D + 1 - p_a(X).
$$

**Sharpness.** The bound is sharp: taking $D = K_X$ (which has $\deg K_X = 2g -
2 = N$) gives
$$
h^0(\mathcal{O}_X(K_X)) - h^0(\mathcal{O}_X) = (2g-2) + 1 - g = g - 1,
$$
so $h^0(K_X) = g - 1 + h^0(\mathcal{O}_X) = g - 1 + 1 = g$ (since $X$ is integra
l, $h^0(\mathcal{O}_X)=1$). Meanwhile $\deg K_X + 1 - g = (2g-2)+1-g = g-1 \neq
g$ for $g \geq 1$. Hence at $\deg D = N$ the desired equality already fails in g
eneral, so $N$ cannot be lowered.

For $g = 0$, $X \cong \mathbb{P}^1_k$ (when $X$ is smooth; in general an integra
l curve of arithmetic genus $0$ is $\mathbb{P}^1$ if geometrically integral), $N
 = -2$, and indeed $\deg D > -2$ (i.e. $\deg D \geq -1$) gives $h^0 = \deg D + 1
$, which is the standard formula on $\mathbb{P}^1$.

**Conclusion.** Such a positive (well, integer) bound exists, and the optimal va
lue is
$$
\boxed{N = 2\,p_a(X) - 2.}
$$
