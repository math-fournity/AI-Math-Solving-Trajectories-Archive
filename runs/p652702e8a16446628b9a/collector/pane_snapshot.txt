**Step 1: Area of $f(\mathbb{D})$.** Since $f$ is univalent in $\mathbb{D}$ with

$$\operatorname{Area}(f(\mathbb{D})) = \iint_{\mathbb{D}} |f'(z)|^2\,dx\,dy.$$
Writing $f'(z) = \sum_{n=1}^\infty n\,a_n\,z^{n-1}$ and applying Parseval in the
 angular variable:
$$\int_0^{2\pi}|f'(re^{i\theta})|^2\,d\theta = 2\pi\sum_{n=1}^\infty n^2|a_n|^2
r^{2(n-1)},$$
so
$$\operatorname{Area}(f(\mathbb{D})) = 2\pi\sum_{n=1}^\infty n^2|a_n|^2 \int_0^1
 r^{2n-1}\,dr = \pi\sum_{n=1}^\infty n\,|a_n|^2.$$

**Step 2: Gronwall's Area Theorem.** On $|z|=1$, write $z = e^{i\theta}$. Then
$$g(e^{i\theta}) = e^{i\theta} + b_0 + b_1 e^{-i\theta} + b_2 e^{-2i\theta} + \c
dots$$
The curve $g(e^{i\theta})$ bounds $K$. Using Green's theorem, the signed area of

$$\operatorname{Area}(K) = \frac{1}{2i}\oint_{|z|=1} \overline{g(z)}\,g'(z)\,dz
\cdot (\text{orientation sign}).$$
On $|z|=1$: $\overline{g(z)} = \frac{1}{z} + \overline{b_0} + \overline{b_1}\,z
+ \overline{b_2}\,z^2 + \cdots$ and $g'(z) = 1 - \frac{b_1}{z^2} - \frac{2b_2}{z
^3} - \cdots$. Computing the residue of $\overline{g(z)}\,g'(z)$ at $z=0$ (the c

The constant term of $\overline{g(z)}\,g'(z)$ comes from pairing: $\frac{1}{z}\c

$$\operatorname{Area}(K) = \pi\left(1 - \sum_{n=1}^\infty n\,|b_n|^2\right) \geq
 0,$$
which yields the **Area Theorem**: $\sum_{n=1}^\infty n\,|b_n|^2 \leq 1$.

**Step 3: The key inequality.** Since $f(\mathbb{D}) \subseteq K$ and $f$ is uni

$$\operatorname{Area}(f(\mathbb{D})) \leq \operatorname{Area}(K).$$
Substituting:
$$\pi\sum_{n=1}^\infty n\,|a_n|^2 \leq \pi\left(1 - \sum_{n=1}^\infty n\,|b_n|^2
\right),$$
hence
$$\sum_{n=1}^\infty n\,|a_n|^2 + \sum_{n=1}^\infty n\,|b_n|^2 \leq 1. \tag{$\sta
r$}$$

$$|a_1|^2 \leq \sum_{n=1}^\infty n\,|a_n|^2 \leq 1 - \sum_{n=1}^\infty n\,|b_n|^

Therefore $|a_1| \leq 1$.
