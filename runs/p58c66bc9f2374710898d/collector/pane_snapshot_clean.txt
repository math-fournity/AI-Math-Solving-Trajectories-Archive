## Proof

We want
$$A=\lim_{n\to\infty}\Bigl(\prod_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\Bigr)^{1/(n(n+1))},\qquad
 \log A=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{1}{n(n+1)}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\log\binom{n}{k}.$$

**Step 1. Simplify the sum.** Since $\binom{n}{k}=\frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!}$,
$$\sum_{k=0}^{n}\log\binom{n}{k}=(n+1)\log n!-2\sum_{k=0}^{n}\log k!.$$
Using $\log k!=\sum_{j=1}^{k}\log j$, we swap the order:
$$\sum_{k=0}^{n}\log k!=\sum_{j=1}^{n}(n+1-j)\log j.$$
Hence, with $\log n!=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\log j$,
$$S_n:=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\log\binom{n}{k}=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\bigl[(n+1)-2(n+1-j)\bigr]\l
og j=\sum_{j=1}^{n}(2j-n-1)\log j.$$

**Step 2. Convert to a Riemann sum.** Write $\log j=\log n+\log(j/n)$:
$$S_n=\log n\sum_{j=1}^{n}(2j-n-1)+\sum_{j=1}^{n}(2j-n-1)\log\frac{j}{n}.$$
The first coefficient sum vanishes exactly:
$$\sum_{j=1}^{n}(2j-n-1)=2\cdot\frac{n(n+1)}{2}-n(n+1)=0.$$
So
$$S_n=\sum_{j=1}^{n}(2j-n-1)\log\frac{j}{n}=n\sum_{j=1}^{n}\Bigl(2\frac{j}{n}-1\
Bigr)\log\frac{j}{n}-\sum_{j=1}^{n}\log\frac{j}{n}.$$

**Step 3. Take the limit.** The first piece is a Riemann sum:
$$n\sum_{j=1}^{n}\Bigl(2\frac{j}{n}-1\Bigr)\log\frac{j}{n}=n^{2}\cdot\frac{1}{n}
\sum_{j=1}^{n}\Bigl(2\frac{j}{n}-1\Bigr)\log\frac{j}{n}\;\longrightarrow\;n^{2}\
int_{0}^{1}(2t-1)\log t\,dt.$$
The second piece satisfies $\sum_{j=1}^{n}\log\frac{j}{n}=n\cdot\frac{1}{n}\sum_
{j=1}^{n}\log\frac{j}{n}\to n\cdot\int_{0}^{1}\log t\,dt=-n$.

Compute the two integrals:
$$\int_{0}^{1}\log t\,dt=-1,\qquad \int_{0}^{1}t\log t\,dt=\Bigl[\tfrac{t^{2}}{2
}\log t\Bigr]_{0}^{1}-\int_{0}^{1}\tfrac{t}{2}\,dt=-\tfrac{1}{4}.$$
Therefore
$$\int_{0}^{1}(2t-1)\log t\,dt=2\Bigl(-\tfrac{1}{4}\Bigr)-(-1)=-\tfrac{1}{2}+1=\
tfrac{1}{2}.$$

Assembling,
$$S_n=\tfrac{1}{2}n^{2}+n+o(n),\qquad \frac{S_n}{n(n+1)}=\frac{\tfrac{1}{2}n^{2}
+n+o(n)}{n^{2}+n}=\frac{\tfrac{1}{2}+\tfrac{1}{n}+o(1/n)}{1+\tfrac{1}{n}}\;\long
rightarrow\;\frac{1}{2}.$$

(The lower-order correction $-\sum\log(j/n)\sim +n$ contributes only the $+n$ te
rm, which vanishes in the limit after division by $n(n+1)$.)

**Conclusion.**
$$\log A=\frac{1}{2},\qquad \boxed{A=\sqrt{e}}.$$
