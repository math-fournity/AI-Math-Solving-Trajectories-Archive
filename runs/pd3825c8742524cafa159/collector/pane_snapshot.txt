## Proof

The equation $x = \tan x$ has $x = 0$ as a root, and for each integer $k \geq 1$
, exactly one positive root in the interval $\left(\left(k - \tfrac{1}{2}\right)
\pi,\; \left(k + \tfrac{1}{2}\right)\right)\pi\right)$, since $\tan x$ increases
 continuously from $-\infty$ to $+\infty$ on each such interval and thus crosses
 the line $y = x$ exactly once. Denote these positive roots by $x_1 < x_2 < \cdo
ts$, so $x_n \in \left(\left(n - \tfrac{1}{2}\right)\pi,\; \left(n + \tfrac{1}{2
}\right)\pi\right)$.

**Location of $x_n$ for large $n$.** Since $x_n \to \infty$ and $\tan(x_n) = x_n
 \to \infty$, the root must approach the right endpoint of its interval (where $
\tan x \to +\infty$). Write
$$x_n = \left(n + \tfrac{1}{2}\right)\pi - \varepsilon_n, \qquad \varepsilon_n \
in \left(0, \pi\right), \quad \varepsilon_n \to 0^+.$$

**Asymptotics of $\varepsilon_n$.** Using $\tan\!\left(\left(n+\tfrac12\right)\p
i - \varepsilon_n\right) = \cot \varepsilon_n$, the equation becomes
$$\cot \varepsilon_n = \left(n + \tfrac{1}{2}\right)\pi - \varepsilon_n.$$

For small $\varepsilon_n > 0$, $\cot \varepsilon_n = \dfrac{1}{\varepsilon_n} -
\dfrac{\varepsilon_n}{3} - O(\varepsilon_n^3)$, so
$$\frac{1}{\varepsilon_n} = \left(n + \tfrac{1}{2}\right)\pi - \varepsilon_n + \
frac{\varepsilon_n}{3} + O(\varepsilon_n^3) = \left(n + \tfrac{1}{2}\right)\pi +
 O(\varepsilon_n).$$

Since the right-hand side $\to \infty$, we get $\varepsilon_n \to 0$, and
$$\varepsilon_n = \frac{1}{\left(n + \tfrac{1}{2}\right)\pi + O(\varepsilon_n)}
= \frac{1}{\left(n + \tfrac{1}{2}\right)\pi}\,(1 + o(1)) \to 0.$$

In particular $\varepsilon_n \to 0$ (and likewise $\varepsilon_{n+1} \to 0$).

**The limit.** Using $x_n = \left(n + \tfrac{1}{2}\right)\pi - \varepsilon_n$,
$$x_{n+1} - x_n = \left[\left(n+1+\tfrac12\right)\pi - \varepsilon_{n+1}\right]
- \left[\left(n+\tfrac12\right)\pi - \varepsilon_n\right] = \pi - \varepsilon_{n
+1} + \varepsilon_n.$$

Since $\varepsilon_n \to 0$ and $\varepsilon_{n+1} \to 0$ as $n \to \infty$,
$$\lim_{n \to \infty}(x_{n+1} - x_n) = \pi.$$
