## Proof

We compute the induced homomorphism $(f_n)_* : \pi_1(S^1, 1) \to \pi_1(S^1, 1)$
for $f_n(z) = z^n$.

**Setup.** Identify $S^1 = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z| = 1\}$ with basepoint $1 \in
 S^1$. The fundamental group $\pi_1(S^1, 1)$ is isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}$, via
the isomorphism sending the homotopy class of a loop $\gamma$ to its **winding n
umber** (degree) about the origin. Concretely, the generator of $\pi_1(S^1,1) \c
ong \mathbb{Z}$ is the homotopy class of the loop
$$\omega(t) = e^{2\pi i t}, \quad t \in [0,1],$$
which traverses the circle once counterclockwise and has winding number $1$.

**Goal.** We show $(f_n)_*$ is multiplication by $n$ on $\mathbb{Z}$, i.e.,
$$(f_n)_*([\omega]) = [\omega]^n \quad \text{in } \pi_1(S^1,1) \cong \mathbb{Z}.
$$

**Computation.** The image of the generator $\omega$ under $f_n$ is the loop
$$
(f_n \circ \omega)(t) = f_n(\omega(t)) = f_n(e^{2\pi i t}) = (e^{2\pi i t})^n =
e^{2\pi i n t}, \quad t \in [0,1].
$$

This is the loop that traverses $S^1$ exactly $n$ times counterclockwise as $t$
runs from $0$ to $1$. Its winding number is $n$, so under the isomorphism $\pi_1
(S^1,1) \cong \mathbb{Z}$,
$$(f_n \circ \omega)(t) = e^{2\pi i n t} = \omega(nt),$$
which is precisely the $n$-fold concatenation $\omega \cdot \omega \cdots \omega
$ ($n$ times) up to reparametrization. Hence
$$(f_n)_*([\omega]) = [f_n \circ \omega] = [\omega]^n.$$

**Verification via the covering space.** To make this rigorous, we use the unive
rsal covering $p : \mathbb{R} \to S^1$, $p(s) = e^{2\pi i s}$. The loop $\omega(
t) = e^{2\pi i t}$ lifts to the path $\widetilde{\omega}(t) = t$ in $\mathbb{R}$
, starting at $\widetilde{\omega}(0) = 0$ and ending at $\widetilde{\omega}(1) =
 1$. The endpoint $1$ is exactly the integer corresponding to $[\omega]$ under t
he isomorphism $\pi_1(S^1,1) \cong \mathbb{Z}$ (the deck transformation / liftin
g criterion).

Now lift $f_n \circ \omega$. The map $f_n \circ \omega(t) = e^{2\pi i n t}$ lift
s to $\widetilde{\gamma}(t) = nt$, which starts at $0$ and ends at $n$. Since th
e isomorphism $\pi_1(S^1,1) \to \mathbb{Z}$ sends a loop to the endpoint of its
lift starting at $0$, we conclude
$$(f_n)_*([\omega]) \mapsto n \in \mathbb{Z}.$$

**Linearity.** Since $(f_n)_*$ is a group homomorphism and $\pi_1(S^1,1) \cong \
mathbb{Z}$ is generated by $[\omega]$, the value on the generator determines the
 entire homomorphism. For any $k \in \mathbb{Z}$ corresponding to $[\omega]^k$,
$$(f_n)_*([\omega]^k) = (f_n)_*([\omega])^k = [\omega]^{nk},$$
so under the isomorphism with $\mathbb{Z}$,
$$(f_n)_*(k) = nk.$$

**Conclusion.** The induced homomorphism $(f_n)_* : \pi_1(S^1, 1) \to \pi_1(S^1,
 1)$, under the identification $\pi_1(S^1,1) \cong \mathbb{Z}$, is the map
$$\boxed{(f_n)_*(k) = nk, \quad k \in \mathbb{Z},}$$
i.e., **multiplication by $n$**.
