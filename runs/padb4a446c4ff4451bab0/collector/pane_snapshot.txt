n-1)\}$. The conjecture $F(N+k) \leq F(N)+1$ for large $N$ is equivalent to: for

**Why this is hard.** The known asymptotic is $F(N) = \sqrt{N} + O(N^{1/4})$ (Er

the **minimum** gap also tends to infinity — a much stronger statement about the
 regularity of $F$. The $O(N^{1/4})$ error term is far too large to control the
local behavior of $F$ at scale $k$.
