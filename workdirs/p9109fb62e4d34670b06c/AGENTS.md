# Problem

In the competitive world of professional logistics, three rival shipping companies—Alpha, Beta, and Gamma—each employ exactly $n$ specialized couriers. Every single courier across all three companies has a unique, distinct efficiency rating. 

To handle a new international contract, the $3n$ couriers must be organized into $n$ three-person task forces. Each task force must consist of exactly one courier from Alpha, one from Beta, and one from Gamma. Within every task force, the courier with the highest efficiency rating is officially designated as the "Lead Specialist."

An exhaustive audit of the efficiency ratings reveals a specific constraint: no matter how the couriers are assigned to these task forces, it is mathematically guaranteed that each of the three companies will have at least 10 of its employees designated as a "Lead Specialist."

Find the minimum possible value of $n$ that makes this guarantee hold true.
