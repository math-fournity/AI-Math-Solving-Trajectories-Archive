## Solution

We solve the Dirichlet problem for Laplace's equation in the first quadrant $\{x
>0,\, y>0\}$:

$$\Delta u_1 = 0,\qquad u_1(0,y)=0\;(y>0),\qquad u_1(x,0)=\begin{cases}100,&0<x<
1\\0,&x>1.\end{cases}$$

### Method: Poisson integral for the half-plane with odd reflection

The Poisson formula for the upper half-plane $\{(x,y):y>0\}$ with boundary data
$F$ on $y=0$ gives the harmonic function

$$u(x,y)=\frac{1}{\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{y}{(x-t)^2+y^2}\,F(t)\,dt.$$

To enforce $u_1(0,y)=0$, we extend the boundary data on $y=0$ to an **odd** func
tion of $x$:

$$F(t)=\begin{cases}f(t),&t>0,\\-f(-t),&t<0,\end{cases}\qquad f(t)=\begin{cases}
100,&0<t<1,\\0,&t>1.\end{cases}$$

Since $F$ is odd, the Poisson integral is odd in $x$, hence vanishes at $x=0$. S
ubstituting and combining the two half-line integrals (substituting $s=-t$ in th
e $t<0$ part):

$$u_1(x,y)=\frac{1}{\pi}\int_0^\infty y\!\left[\frac{1}{(x-t)^2+y^2}-\frac{1}{(x
+t)^2+y^2}\right]f(t)\,dt.$$

Since $f$ is supported on $(0,1)$ with value $100$:

$$u_1(x,y)=\frac{100}{\pi}\int_0^1 y\!\left[\frac{1}{(x-t)^2+y^2}-\frac{1}{(x+t)
^2+y^2}\right]dt.$$

### Evaluating the integrals

$$\int_0^1 \frac{y\,dt}{(x-t)^2+y^2}=\Big[\arctan\tfrac{t-x}{y}\Big]_{0}^{1}=\ar
ctan\tfrac{1-x}{y}+\arctan\tfrac{x}{y},$$

$$\int_0^1 \frac{y\,dt}{(x+t)^2+y^2}=\Big[\arctan\tfrac{x+t}{y}\Big]_{0}^{1}=\ar
ctan\tfrac{x+1}{y}-\arctan\tfrac{x}{y}.$$

Subtracting:

$$u_1(x,y)=\frac{100}{\pi}\!\left[\arctan\frac{1-x}{y}+2\arctan\frac{x}{y}-\arct
an\frac{x+1}{y}\right].$$

### Verification of boundary conditions

**On $x=0$, $y>0$:**
$$u_1(0,y)=\frac{100}{\pi}\!\left[\arctan\tfrac{1}{y}+0-\arctan\tfrac{1}{y}\righ
t]=0.\;\checkmark$$

**On $y\to0^+$, $0<x<1$:** each arctangent of a positive quantity $\to\pi/2$:
$$u_1\to\frac{100}{\pi}\!\left[\tfrac{\pi}{2}+2\cdot\tfrac{\pi}{2}-\tfrac{\pi}{2
}\right]=\frac{100}{\pi}\cdot\pi=100.\;\checkmark$$

**On $y\to0^+$, $x>1$:** here $\arctan\frac{1-x}{y}\to-\frac{\pi}{2}$ (since $1-
x<0$), while the other two $\to\frac{\pi}{2}$:
$$u_1\to\frac{100}{\pi}\!\left[-\tfrac{\pi}{2}+2\cdot\tfrac{\pi}{2}-\tfrac{\pi}{
2}\right]=\frac{100}{\pi}\cdot0=0.\;\checkmark$$

**Harmonicity:** $u_1$ is the Poisson integral of an $L^\infty$ boundary functio
n, hence harmonic in the open first quadrant. Each arctangent term is the imagin
ary part of an analytic logarithm, confirming $\Delta u_1=0$ directly.

### Result

$$\boxed{\,u_1(x,y)=\frac{100}{\pi}\!\left[\arctan\frac{1-x}{y}+2\arctan\frac{x}
{y}-\arctan\frac{x+1}{y}\right],\qquad x>0,\;y>0.\,}$$
