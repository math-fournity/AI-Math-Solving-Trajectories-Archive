# Problem

In a futuristic data-processing facility, an automated system generates "Resource Integrity Codes" (RICs) based on a signal frequency $x$, where $x$ can be any positive real-valued measurement. For a given frequency $x$, the resulting RIC value is calculated by summing two distinct processing components:

1.  The first component is the frequency $x$ raised to the power of its own integer floor (the greatest integer less than or equal to $x$).
2.  The second component is the integer floor of $x$ raised to the power of the original frequency $x$.

The system's output register only recognizes whole-number (integer) results that are strictly less than $1000$.

Determine the total number of distinct integers less than $1000$ that can be generated by the expression $x^{\lfloor x\rfloor}+\lfloor x\rfloor^{x}$ for some positive real number $x$.
