# Problem

A high-tech research conglomerate consists of 18 specialized laboratories. To facilitate cross-disciplinary innovation, the conglomerate organizes a multi-phase collaboration cycle. In each phase, the 18 laboratories are partitioned into 9 distinct pairs, and each pair conducts a joint experiment simultaneously. The entire cycle consists of exactly 17 phases, structured such that by the end of the cycle, every possible pair of laboratories has collaborated exactly once.

The director of the conglomerate monitors the progress of these collaborations. She observes that after $n$ phases have been completed, it is always possible to find a group of 4 specific laboratories such that, among the members of that group, exactly one collaboration has taken place during those $n$ phases.

Find the maximum integer value of $n$ for which this property is guaranteed to hold true.
