## Solution

Let $t = X^4 - X^{-4}$, so $F = \mathbb{R}(t)$ and $K = \mathbb{R}(X)$.

**Step 1: Minimal polynomial and $[K:F]$.**

Since $t = (X^8 - 1)/X^4$, the element $X$ satisfies
$$X^8 - tX^4 - 1 = 0 \quad \text{over } F.$$
Setting $u = X^4$, we get $u^2 - tu - 1 = 0$. The discriminant is $t^2 + 4$, whi
ch is **not** a square in $\mathbb{R}(t)$ (it is an irreducible quadratic), so $
[\mathbb{R}(u):\mathbb{R}(t)] = 2$. Then $X^4 = u$ is irreducible over $\mathbb{
R}(u)$ (as $u$ is transcendental and not a fourth power), giving $[\mathbb{R}(X)
:\mathbb{R}(u)] = 4$. Hence
$$[K:F] = [\mathbb{R}(X):\mathbb{R}(t)] = 8.$$
Equivalently, the rational map $X \mapsto (X^8-1)/X^4$ has degree $8$.

**Step 2: The roots of $X^8 - tX^4 - 1$.**

Let $\alpha = X$ (a root, $\alpha^4 = u_1$). The other value is $u_2 = -1/u_1 =
-\alpha^{-4}$ (since $u_1 u_2 = -1$). The eight roots are:
- Fourth roots of $u_1$: $\alpha,\ i\alpha,\ -\alpha,\ -i\alpha$.
- Fourth roots of $u_2 = -\alpha^{-4}$: $\alpha^{-1}\zeta_8,\ \alpha^{-1}\zeta_8
^3,\ \alpha^{-1}\zeta_8^5,\ \alpha^{-1}\zeta_8^7$, where $\zeta_8 = e^{i\pi/4}$
(the primitive 8th roots of unity, satisfying $\eta^4 = -1$).

Note $\zeta_8 = (1+i)/\sqrt{2}$, so adjoining $\zeta_8$ brings in both $i$ and $
\sqrt{2}$.

**Step 3: The splitting field.**

The splitting field over $F$ must contain $\alpha = X$ (a root), $i$ (since $i\a
lpha$ is a root, $i = (i\alpha)/\alpha$), and $\zeta_8$ (since $\alpha^{-1}\zeta
_8$ is a root, $\zeta_8 = (\alpha^{-1}\zeta_8)\cdot\alpha$). Conversely, all roo
ts lie in $\mathbb{R}(X, i, \zeta_8)$.

Now observe: $\mathbb{R}(X, i) = \mathbb{C}(X)$, and $\sqrt{2} \in \mathbb{C} \s
ubset \mathbb{C}(X)$, so $\zeta_8 = (1+i)/\sqrt{2} \in \mathbb{C}(X)$. Therefore
$$L = \mathbb{R}(X, i, \zeta_8) = \mathbb{R}(X, i) = \mathbb{C}(X).$$

**Step 4: The degree $[L:F]$.**

$$[L:F] = [\mathbb{C}(X):\mathbb{R}(t)] = [\mathbb{C}(X):\mathbb{R}(X)]\cdot[\ma
thbb{R}(X):\mathbb{R}(t)] = 2 \cdot 8 = 16.$$

Here $[\mathbb{C}(X):\mathbb{R}(X)] = 2$ (adjoining $i$) and $[\mathbb{R}(X):\ma
thbb{R}(t)] = 8$ (from Step 1).

**Verification that $\mathbb{C}(X)$ is Galois over $\mathbb{R}(t)$:** $\mathbb{C
}(X)$ is the splitting field of the separable polynomial $X^8 - tX^4 - 1$ over $
\mathbb{R}(t)$, hence it is a normal separable extension, i.e., Galois. The Galo
is group has order $16$.

$$\boxed{[L:F] = 16}$$
