## Proof

The space $X = (S^{1} \times [0,1])/(z,1)\sim(z^{2},0)$ is the **mapping torus**
 of the degree‑2 self‑map $f:S^{1}\to S^{1}$, $f(z)=z^{2}$. I describe an explic
it CW structure on $X$ and read off $\pi_{1}(X)$ and $H_{1}(X)$ from it.

### Explicit CW decomposition

Give $S^{1}$ the minimal CW structure: one $0$‑cell $v_{0}$ and one $1$‑cell $e^
{1}$ (an interval whose two endpoints are both attached to $v_{0}$). Give $[0,1]
$ the structure with two $0$‑cells $\{0\},\{1\}$ and one $1$‑cell. The product $
S^{1}\times[0,1]$ then has

- $0$‑cells: $(v_{0},0)$ and $(v_{0},1)$,
- $1$‑cells: $e^{1}\times\{0\}$ (bottom circle), $e^{1}\times\{1\}$ (top circle)
, $\{v_{0}\}\times[0,1]$ (vertical segment),
- $2$‑cell: $e^{1}\times(0,1)$ (the open rectangle).

Now impose the identification $(z,1)\sim(z^{2},0)$.

**Effect on cells.**

- **$0$‑cells.** Since $f$ fixes the basepoint, $(v_{0},1)\sim(v_{0},0)$. The tw
o $0$‑cells collapse to a single $0$‑cell, call it $v$.
- **$1$‑cells.** The bottom circle $e^{1}\times\{0\}$ becomes a loop at $v$; cal
l it $a$. The top circle $e^{1}\times\{1\}$ is glued onto the bottom circle by $
z\mapsto z^{2}$, i.e. it wraps **twice** around $a$; hence it is not a new cell
— it is identified with $a^{2}$. The vertical segment $\{v_{0}\}\times[0,1]$ has
 both endpoints at $v$ (its top endpoint $(v_{0},1)\sim(v_{0},0)=v$ and its bott
om endpoint is $v$), so it becomes a loop at $v$; call it $b$.
- **$2$‑cell.** The interior $e^{1}\times(0,1)$ is unaffected by the gluing (whi
ch only touches $t=0$ and $t=1$), so it descends to a single $2$‑cell $D$.

Thus $X$ has the CW structure
$$X = e^{0}\cup a\cup b\cup D,\qquad \text{one }0\text{-cell, two }1\text{-cells
, one }2\text{-cell.}$$

### Attaching map of the $2$‑cell

Parametrize the rectangle as $e^{1}\times[0,1]=[0,1]_{s}\times[0,1]_{t}$, with $
s=0$ and $s=1$ both mapping to $v_{0}\in S^{1}$. Traversing $\partial(e^{1}\time
s[0,1])$ counterclockwise:

1. **Bottom** ($t=0$, $s:0\to1$): $e^{1}\times\{0\}=a$. Contributes $+a$.
2. **Right** ($s=1$, $t:0\to1$): $\{v_{0}\}\times[0,1]=b$. Contributes $+b$.
3. **Top** ($t=1$, $s:1\to0$): $e^{1}\times\{1\}$ traversed backwards; under $(z
,1)\sim(z^{2},0)$ it maps to $f(e^{1})=a^{2}$, traversed backwards. Contributes
$-a^{2}$, i.e. $a^{-2}$.
4. **Left** ($s=0$, $t:1\to0$): $\{v_{0}\}\times[0,1]=b$ traversed backwards. Co
ntributes $-b$, i.e. $b^{-1}$.

Hence the attaching map of $D$ reads, as a word in $\pi_{1}(X^{1})=\langle a,b\r
angle$,
$$\varphi = a\,b\,a^{-2}\,b^{-1}.$$

### Fundamental group

From the CW presentation (one $0$‑cell, generators $a,b$ from the $1$‑cells, one
 relation from the $2$‑cell),
$$\pi_{1}(X) \;\cong\; \big\langle a,\,b \;\big|\; a\,b\,a^{-2}\,b^{-1}=1\big\ra
ngle.$$

The relation rearranges as
$$a\,b\,a^{-2}\,b^{-1}=1 \;\Longleftrightarrow\; a\,b = b\,a^{2} \;\Longleftrigh
tarrow\; b^{-1}a\,b = a^{2}.$$

Therefore
$$\boxed{\pi_{1}(X)\;\cong\;\langle a,b\mid b^{-1}ab=a^{2}\rangle\;\cong\;BS(1,2
),}$$
the Baumslag–Solitar group $BS(1,2)$ — as expected for the mapping torus of a de
gree‑$2$ map on $S^{1}$.

### First homology group

$H_{1}(X)$ is the abelianization of $\pi_{1}(X)$. Abelianizing the relation $b^{
-1}ab=a^{2}$ gives
$$a = 2a \;\Longrightarrow\; a=0,$$
while $b$ is unconstrained. Hence
$$\boxed{H_{1}(X)\;\cong\;\mathbb{Z},}$$
generated by (the class of) $b$.

**Cross‑check (Wang sequence).** For the mapping torus of $f:S^{1}\to S^{1}$ of
degree $d=2$, the Wang sequence gives
$$H_{1}(S^{1})\xrightarrow{\;f_{*}-\mathrm{id}\;}H_{1}(S^{1})\longrightarrow H_{
1}(X)\longrightarrow H_{0}(S^{1})\xrightarrow{\;f_{*}-\mathrm{id}\;}H_{0}(S^{1})
.$$
Here $f_{*}-\mathrm{id}$ on $H_{1}(S^{1})\cong\mathbb{Z}$ is multiplication by $
2-1=1$, an isomorphism, so its cokernel is $0$; and $f_{*}-\mathrm{id}$ on $H_{0
}(S^{1})\cong\mathbb{Z}$ is $1-1=0$, whose kernel is all of $\mathbb{Z}$. The ex
act sequence $0\to H_{1}(X)\to\mathbb{Z}\xrightarrow{0}\mathbb{Z}$ forces $H_{1}
(X)\cong\mathbb{Z}$, confirming the CW computation.

### Torsion subgroup

Since $H_{1}(X)\cong\mathbb{Z}$ is free abelian of rank $1$,
$$\boxed{\operatorname{Tor} H_{1}(X)=0,\qquad |\operatorname{Tor} H_{1}(X)|=1.}$
$

### Summary

- **CW decomposition of $X$:** one $0$‑cell $v$; two $1$‑cells $a$ (bottom circl
e $S^{1}\times\{0\}$) and $b$ (vertical segment $\{v_{0}\}\times[0,1]$); one $2$
‑cell $D=e^{1}\times(0,1)$ attached via $\varphi = a\,b\,a^{-2}\,b^{-1}$ (the to
p edge contributes $a^{-2}$ because the gluing $z\mapsto z^{2}$ wraps the top ci
rcle twice around the bottom).
- **$\pi_{1}(X)\cong\langle a,b\mid b^{-1}ab=a^{2}\rangle\cong BS(1,2)$.**
- **$H_{1}(X)\cong\mathbb{Z}$** (abelianization kills $a$ via $a=2a$, leaving $b
$ free).
- **Order of the torsion subgroup of $H_{1}(X)$: $1$.**
