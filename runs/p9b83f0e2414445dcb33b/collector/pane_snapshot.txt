## Proof

. Denote the common length by $\ell > 0$. We select 3 girders from the 10 and as

le inequality.

**Triangle inequality check.** For three segments of lengths $a, b, c$ to form a
 triangle, we need:
$$a + b > c, \quad a + c > b, \quad b + c > a.$$

Since all 10 girders have the same length $\ell$, any chosen triple has $a = b =
 c = \ell$. The three inequalities all become:
$$\ell + \ell > \ell \implies 2\ell > \ell,$$
which is true for every $\ell > 0$.

**Every triple forms a valid (equilateral) triangle.** There is no triple that f
ails the triangle inequality.

**Counting.** The number of ways to choose 3 girders from 10 is:
$$\binom{10}{3} = \frac{10 \times 9 \times 8}{3 \times 2 \times 1} = 120.$$

**Conclusion.** Since all 10 girders are of equal length, every one of the 120 t
riples satisfies the triangle inequality and forms an equilateral triangle. The
largest number of triangles that can be created is:

$$\boxed{120}$$
