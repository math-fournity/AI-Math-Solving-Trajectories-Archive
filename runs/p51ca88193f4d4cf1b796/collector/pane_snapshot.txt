**Proof.**

Since $\int_0^1 |f_n(t) - f(t)|\,dt \to 0$ as $n \to \infty$, in particular $\in
t_0^1 |f_n(t) - f(t)|\,dt < \infty$ for all sufficiently large $n$. Each $f_n$ i

y such $n$, the function $f_n - f$ is integrable, and therefore $f = f_n - (f_n
- f)$ is integrable as well. So the quantity $\int_0^1 f(t)\,dt$ is well defined
.

Now for every $n$,

$$\left|\int_0^1 f_n(t)\,dt - \int_0^1 f(t)\,dt\right| = \left|\int_0^1 \bigl(f_
n(t) - f(t)\bigr)\,dt\right| \le \int_0^1 |f_n(t) - f(t)|\,dt.$$

The right-hand side tends to $0$ by hypothesis, so by the squeeze theorem

$$\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_0^1 f_n(t)\,dt = \int_0^1 f(t)\,dt.$$
