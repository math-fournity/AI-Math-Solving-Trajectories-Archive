# Problem

In a remote archipelago, there are 27 research stations. Each station is assigned a unique identification code consisting of a string of $n$ light signals, where each signal is either a blue flash (0) or a red flash (1). 

The logistics of the archipelago are governed by two strict protocols based on the visual "distance" between station codes (the number of positions where the signals differ):
1. Two stations are designated as "Partners" if their identification codes differ in exactly 3 positions.
2. Two stations are designated as "Incompatible" if their identification codes differ in exactly 6 positions.

The regional governor has observed that there is at least one pair of "Partner" stations among the 27. Find the sum of all possible values for $n$.
