# Problem

A team of civil engineers is designing two separate structural projects involving the placement of standardized concrete blocks along a straight path.

**Project Alpha:**
A surveyor must identify a sequence of $2m + 1$ consecutive labeled blocks (where each label $n$ corresponds to a volume of $n^3$ cubic units). The lead engineer requires that the total combined volume of these $2m + 1$ blocks must exactly equal the volume of a single larger cube-shaped container with an integer side length. 

**Project Beta:**
A flooring specialist needs to arrange a sequence of $2k + 1$ consecutive square tiles (where each tile with label $n$ has an area of $n^2$ square units). The specialist requires that the total combined surface area of these $2k + 1$ tiles must exactly equal the area of a single larger square section with an integer side length.

Find the least positive integers $m$ and $k$ that allow these two projects to be physically possible. Output the value of $10m + k$.
