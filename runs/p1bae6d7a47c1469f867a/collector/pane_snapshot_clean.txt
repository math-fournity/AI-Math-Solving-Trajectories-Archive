## Proof

Let $m_k = \mathbb{E}[X^k]$. Since $X, Y$ are iid and $m_4 = \mathbb{E}[X^4] = 1
$:

$$\mathbb{E}[(X-Y)^4] = 2m_4 - 8m_1 m_3 + 6m_2^2 = 2 - 8m_1 m_3 + 6m_2^2.$$

We prove $\mathbb{E}[(X-Y)^4] \le 8$, i.e., $3m_4 - 3m_2^2 + 4m_1 m_3 \ge 0$, i.
e., $3 - 3m_2^2 + 4m_1 m_3 \ge 0$.

**Preliminary.** By Cauchy–Schwarz, $m_2 = \mathbb{E}[X^2] \le \sqrt{\mathbb{E}[
X^4]} = 1$, so $m_2 \in [0,1]$.

**Case 1: $m_1 m_3 \ge 0$.** Then $3 - 3m_2^2 + 4m_1 m_3 \ge 3 - 3m_2^2 \ge 0$ s
ince $m_2 \le 1$. ✓

**Case 2: $m_1 m_3 < 0$.** WLOG $m_1 > 0$, $m_3 < 0$ (otherwise replace $X$ by $
-X$). Set $a = m_1 > 0$, $b = -m_3 > 0$, $s = m_2 \in [0,1]$.

The truncated Hamburger moment problem requires the Hankel matrix
$$H = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & m_1 & m_2 \\ m_1 & m_2 & m_3 \\ m_2 & m_3 & m_4 \end{p
matrix} \succeq 0,$$
which yields (i) $a^2 \le s$, (ii) $b^2 \le s$, and (iii) $\det H \ge 0$:
$$s - a^2 - b^2 - 2abs - s^3 \ge 0 \implies a^2 + b^2 + 2abs \le s(1 - s^2).$$

By AM–GM, $a^2 + b^2 \ge 2ab$, so:
$$2ab(1 + s) \le s(1-s)(1+s) \implies ab \le \frac{s(1-s)}{2}.$$

Therefore:
$$3 - 3s^2 - 4ab \ge 3 - 3s^2 - 2s(1-s) = 3 - s^2 - 2s = (1-s)(s+3) \ge 0,$$
since $s \in [0,1]$. ✓

Combining both cases: $3m_4 - 3m_2^2 + 4m_1 m_3 \ge 0$, hence
$$\mathbb{E}[(X-Y)^4] = 2m_4 - 8m_1 m_3 + 6m_2^2 \le 2m_4 + 6m_4 = 8m_4 = 8.$$

**Equality.** Take $X = \pm 1$ each with probability $\tfrac{1}{2}$. Then $m_4 =
 1$, $m_1 = m_3 = 0$, $m_2 = 1$, and $X - Y \in \{0, \pm 2\}$ with probabilities
 $\tfrac{1}{2}, \tfrac{1}{4}, \tfrac{1}{4}$, giving $\mathbb{E}[(X-Y)^4] = 0 \cd
ot \tfrac{1}{2} + 16 \cdot \tfrac{1}{2} = 8$.

The best upper bound is $\boxed{8}$.
