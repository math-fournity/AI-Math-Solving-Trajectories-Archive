## Solution

**Setup.** Let $M=\{1,2,\dots,2021\}$. For each non-empty $X\subseteq M$, let $a
_X = \min(X)+\max(X)$. We compute the mean of $a_X$ over all $2^{2021}-1$ non-em
pty subsets.

**Decomposition by min and max.** Fix $i=\min(X)$, $j=\max(X)$.

- **Case $i=j$** (singletons): $X=\{i\}$, $a_X=2i$. Contributing sum: $\displays
tyle\sum_{i=1}^{2021}2i = 2021\cdot 2022$.

- **Case $i<j$**: $X$ must contain $i$ and $j$; the elements $\{i+1,\dots,j-1\}$
 (there are $j-i-1$ of them) may be included or not, giving $2^{j-i-1}$ subsets,
 each with $a_X=i+j$.

**Computing the $i<j$ contribution.** Set $d=j-i\in\{1,\dots,2020\}$ and $j=i+d$
. Then

$$\sum_{\substack{1\le i<j\le 2021}}(i+j)\cdot 2^{j-i-1} = \sum_{d=1}^{2020}2^{d
-1}\sum_{i=1}^{2021-d}(2i+d).$$

For $n=2021-d$:

$$\sum_{i=1}^{n}(2i+d)=n(n+1)+dn=n(n+1+d)=(2021-d)\cdot 2022.$$

So the contribution equals

$$2022\sum_{d=1}^{2020}(2021-d)\cdot 2^{d-1}.$$

**Evaluating the inner sum.** Let $k=d-1$, so $k\in\{0,\dots,2019\}$ and $2021-d
=2020-k$:

$$T=\sum_{k=0}^{2019}(2020-k)\cdot 2^{k}=2020\sum_{k=0}^{2019}2^k-\sum_{k=0}^{20
19}k\cdot 2^k.$$

Using $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}2^k=2^{n+1}-1$ and $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n
}k\cdot 2^k=(n-1)\cdot 2^{n+1}+2$ with $n=2019$:

$$T=2020(2^{2020}-1)-\bigl(2018\cdot 2^{2020}+2\bigr)=(2020-2018)\cdot 2^{2020}-
2022=2^{2021}-2022.$$

**Total sum.**

$$S = \underbrace{2021\cdot 2022}_{\text{singletons}}+\underbrace{2022\,(2^{2021
}-2022)}_{i<j}=2022\bigl(2021+2^{2021}-2022\bigr)=2022\,(2^{2021}-1).$$

**Mean.** The number of non-empty subsets is $2^{2021}-1$, so

$$\text{Mean}=\frac{S}{2^{2021}-1}=\frac{2022\,(2^{2021}-1)}{2^{2021}-1}=\boxed{
2022}.$$

**Sanity check.** For $M=\{1,2,3\}$: subsets give $a_X\in\{2,4,6,3,4,5,4\}$, sum
 $=28$, count $=7$, mean $=4=3+1$. The formula $n+1$ with $n=3$ gives $4$. ✓
