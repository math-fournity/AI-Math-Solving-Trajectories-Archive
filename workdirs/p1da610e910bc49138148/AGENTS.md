# Problem

A specialized irrigation zone is planned in a coastal territory represented by a coordinate grid. The zone is shaped like an isosceles triangle $OAB$. The control center is located at the origin $O(0,0)$. The first vertex of the triangle, $A$, is positioned somewhere in the first quadrant (where both coordinates are positive), and the second vertex, $B$, is located on the positive $x$-axis. The boundaries are designed such that the distance from the control center $O$ to vertex $A$ is equal to the distance between vertex $A$ and vertex $B$ ($AO = AB$).

The total land area of this triangular irrigation zone is exactly $s$ square units.

To prevent soil oversaturation, a local environmental regulation restricts high-intensity farming to the region where the product of the coordinates satisfies the inequality $xy \leq 1$. Determine the area of the portion of the triangular zone $OAB$ that falls within this regulated region, expressing your result as a function of the total area $s$.
