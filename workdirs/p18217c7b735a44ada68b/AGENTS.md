# Problem

In a futuristic industrial zone, there are $10^{10}$ different processing plants, indexed by their ID number $n$ from $1$ to $10^{10}$. Each plant $n$ is searching for a specific safety rating $m$ (where $m$ is a positive integer). 

For a plant with ID $n$ to be certified, the safety rating $m$ must meet two strict synchronization requirements:
1. The rating $m$ and the plant ID $n$ must share no common factors greater than 1.
2. The incremented rating $m+1$ and the incremented plant ID $n+1$ must also share no common factors greater than 1.

For every plant $n$, engineers calculate $f(n)$, which is defined as the smallest possible safety rating $m$ that satisfies both synchronization requirements.

Across all $10^{10}$ processing plants, how many unique values are produced in the set of safety ratings $\{f(1), f(2), \dots, f(10^{10})\}$?
