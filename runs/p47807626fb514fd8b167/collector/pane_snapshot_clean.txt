## Solution

Let $p = 57885161$ and $M = 2^p - 1$. The number $p = 57885161$ is a known Merse
nne prime exponent, so $M = 2^{57885161}-1$ is itself a (Mersenne) prime — the 4
8th known Mersenne prime, discovered in 2013.

We seek $3^{2^{p+1}} \bmod M$.

**Step 1: Reduce the exponent modulo $M-1$.**

Since $M$ is prime and $M \neq 3$, we have $\gcd(3, M) = 1$, so by Fermat's litt
le theorem
$$3^{M-1} \equiv 1 \pmod{M}.$$
Thus the value of $3^e \bmod M$ depends only on $e \bmod (M-1)$. Here $e = 2^{p+
1}$ and
$$M - 1 = 2^p - 2.$$

Compute:
$$2^{p+1} = 2 \cdot 2^p = 2(2^p - 2) + 4 = 2(M-1) + 4.$$
Therefore
$$2^{p+1} \equiv 4 \pmod{M-1}.$$

**Step 2: Conclude.**

$$3^{2^{p+1}} \equiv 3^4 = 81 \pmod{M}.$$

Since $0 \le 81 < M$, the remainder is exactly $81$.
