## Proof

Let $v_i = (x_i, y_i) \in \mathbb{R}^2$ with $\|v_i\|^2 = x_i^2 + y_i^2 = 1$. De
fine the $2 \times 2$ matrix
$$M = \sum_{i=1}^n a_i\, v_i v_i^T.$$
Since $a_i \geq 0$ and $v_i v_i^T \succeq 0$, $M$ is positive semidefinite, and
$$\operatorname{tr}(M) = \sum_i a_i \|v_i\|^2 = \sum_i a_i = 1.$$

### Step 1: Reformulation via $\operatorname{tr}(M^2)$

Since $v_i, v_j$ are unit vectors, $(x_i y_j - x_j y_i)^2 = \det(v_i,v_j)^2 = 1
- (v_i \cdot v_j)^2$. Thus
$$S = \sum_{i<j} a_i a_j\bigl(1 - (v_i \cdot v_j)^2\bigr) = \sum_{i<j} a_i a_j -
 \sum_{i<j} a_i a_j (v_i \cdot v_j)^2.$$

Using $\sum_{i<j} a_i a_j = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(1 - \sum_i a_i^2\bigr)$ and
$$\sum_{i<j} a_i a_j (v_i \cdot v_j)^2 = \tfrac{1}{2}\Bigl(\sum_{i,j} a_i a_j (v
_i \cdot v_j)^2 - \sum_i a_i^2\Bigr),$$
we get
$$S = \tfrac{1}{2}\Bigl(1 - \sum_{i,j} a_i a_j (v_i \cdot v_j)^2\Bigr).$$

Now observe that $(v_i \cdot v_j)^2 = \operatorname{tr}(v_i v_i^T\, v_j v_j^T)$,
 so
$$\sum_{i,j} a_i a_j (v_i \cdot v_j)^2 = \operatorname{tr}\!\Bigl(\Bigl(\sum_i a
_i\, v_i v_i^T\Bigr)^2\Bigr) = \operatorname{tr}(M^2).$$

Therefore:
$$\boxed{S = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(1 - \operatorname{tr}(M^2)\bigr).}$$

### Step 2: Lower bound on $\operatorname{tr}(M^2)$

Let $\lambda_1, \lambda_2 \geq 0$ be the eigenvalues of $M$, with $\lambda_1 + \
lambda_2 = \operatorname{tr}(M) = 1$. Then
$$\operatorname{tr}(M^2) = \lambda_1^2 + \lambda_2^2 \geq \frac{(\lambda_1 + \la
mbda_2)^2}{2} = \frac{1}{2},$$
by the QM–AM inequality (or convexity of $t \mapsto t^2$), with equality iff $\l
ambda_1 = \lambda_2 = \tfrac{1}{2}$, i.e., $M = \tfrac{1}{2}I$.

### Step 3: Upper bound on $S$

From Steps 1 and 2:
$$S = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(1 - \operatorname{tr}(M^2)\bigr) \leq \tfrac{1}{2}\Bigl(
1 - \tfrac{1}{2}\Bigr) = \tfrac{1}{4}.$$

### Step 4: The bound $\tfrac{1}{4}$ is achieved

Take $n = 2$ with $v_1 = (1,0)$, $v_2 = (0,1)$, and $a_1 = a_2 = \tfrac{1}{2}$.
Then
$$M = \tfrac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix} + \tfrac{1}{2}\begin{pmat
rix}0&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix} = \tfrac{1}{2}I,$$
so $\operatorname{tr}(M^2) = \tfrac{1}{2}$ and $S = \tfrac{1}{4}$.

Directly: $(x_1 y_2 - x_2 y_1)^2 = (1\cdot 1 - 0\cdot 0)^2 = 1$, and $a_1 a_2 =
\tfrac{1}{4}$, giving $S = \tfrac{1}{4}$. ✓

### Conclusion

The maximum value of $\displaystyle\sum_{1 \le i < j \le n} a_i a_j (x_i y_j - x
_j y_i)^2$ is

$$\boxed{\dfrac{1}{4}}.$$
