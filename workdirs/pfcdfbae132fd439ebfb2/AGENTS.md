# Problem

A high-tech server farm is arranged in a rigid $6 \times 6$ grid of data processing units. To optimize cooling, 18 heat-conducting plates are installed. Each plate is rectangular, exactly the size of two adjacent units ($2 \times 1$ or $1 \times 2$), and every processing unit is covered by exactly one plate.

Between the rows and columns of the units, there are 10 straight maintenance aisles (5 horizontal aisles between the rows and 5 vertical aisles between the columns) that span the entire grid. A "clear passage" is defined as a maintenance aisle that is not obstructed by any heat-conducting plates crossing over it.

Regardless of how the 18 plates are arranged to cover the grid, let $n$ be the minimum possible number of clear passages available in the facility. Determine the value of $n$.
