## Solution

The original sequence is **B-H-A-R-A-T**, with labels at positions 1–6 being B,
H, A, R, A, T. The two A's (positions 3 and 5) are identical. We count distinct
permutations of the multiset {B, H, A, A, R, T} such that no position keeps its
original label — a **derangement of a multiset**.

### Setup via Inclusion–Exclusion

Total distinct permutations: 6!/2! = **360**.

Define events E_i = "position i has its original label":
- E1: pos1 = B, E2: pos2 = H, E3: pos3 = A, E4: pos4 = R, E5: pos5 = A, E6: pos6
 = T.

We want 360 − |E1 ∪ … ∪ E6|, computed by inclusion–exclusion.

**Key observation.** For a subset S of fixed positions, let k = |S| and let a =
number of A-positions in S (a ∈ {0,1,2}, chosen from {3,5}). Fixing those positi
ons consumes a copies of A. The remaining (6−k) positions are filled with the re
maining multiset, which contains (2−a) identical A's, so:

$$\left|\bigcap_{i\in S} E_i\right| = \frac{(6-k)!}{(2-a)!}$$

The number of subsets of size k with exactly a A-positions is C(2,a)·C(4, k−a) (
choosing a from {3,5} and k−a from the 4 non-A positions {1,2,4,6}).

### Verification of key rows

- **k=1** (single fixed position): fixing a non-A position leaves {·,A,A,·,·} →
5!/2! = 60 (×4 positions = 240); fixing an A position leaves all-distinct → 5! =
 120 (×2 positions = 240). Total 480. ✓
- **k=2**: both non-A → 4!/2! = 12 (×6 = 72); one A + one non-A → 4! = 24 (×8 =
192); both A → 4! = 24 (×1). Total 288. ✓
- **k=5**: unfixed is non-A (a=2) → 1 (×4); unfixed is A (a=1) → 1 (×2). Total 6
. ✓
- **k=6**: all fixed → 1. ✓

### Final sum

$$360 - 480 + 288 - 108 + 29 - 6 + 1$$

$$= 360 - 480 = -120$$
$$-120 + 288 = 168$$
$$168 - 108 = 60$$
$$60 + 29 = 89$$
$$89 - 6 = 83$$
$$83 + 1 = \boxed{84}$$

The number of unique, distinguishable rearrangements satisfying the No-Overlap p
rotocol is **84**.
