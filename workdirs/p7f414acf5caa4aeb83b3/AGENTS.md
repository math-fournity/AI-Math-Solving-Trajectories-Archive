# Problem

In a large logistics hub, a delivery drone must navigate a strictly grid-based city layout from a starting warehouse at coordinates (0,0) to a distribution center at (7,7). The drone is programmed to only move in increments of 1 block either North (positive y-direction) or East (positive x-direction).

The city is bisected by a long, diagonal high-voltage power line that follows the precise linear path of $y = x - 2.021$.

As the drone moves from the warehouse to the distribution center, its path and the power line may create bounded areas. Specifically, the logistics team is interested in flight paths where the area enclosed between the drone's path and the power line—specifically the region(s) where the drone is situated South-East of the line (below it)—forms exactly one single, continuous bounded region.

How many distinct valid flight paths from (0,0) to (7,7) satisfy the condition that they enclose exactly one bounded region located below the line $y = x - 2.021$?
