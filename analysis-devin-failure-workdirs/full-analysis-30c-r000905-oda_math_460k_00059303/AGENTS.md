**Step 1: The Lemoine axis.**

The Lemoine point (symmedian point) $K$ has barycentric coordinates $K = (a^2 : b^2 : c^2)$. The trilinear polar of a point $(u:v:w)$ in barycentric coordinates is the line $\frac{x}{u} + \frac{y}{v} + \frac{z}{w} = 0$. The Lemoine axis is the trilinear polar of $K$:
$$\frac{x}{a^2} + \frac{y}{b^2} + \frac{z}{c^2} = 0.$$

**Step 2: The isotomic line of the Lemoine axis.**

The isotomic conjugate of a point $(x:y:z)$ in barycentric coordinates is $(1/x : 1/y : 1/z)$. The isotomic conjugate of $K$ is:
$$K^\iota = \left(\frac{1}{a^2} : \frac{1}{b^2} : \frac{1}{c^2}\right) = (b^2c^2 : a^2c^2 : a^2b^2).$$
