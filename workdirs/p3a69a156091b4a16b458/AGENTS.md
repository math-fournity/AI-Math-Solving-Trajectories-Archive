# Problem

A team of deep-sea researchers is collecting a set $A$ of distinct mineral samples from a trench. Each sample has a specific chemical weight, which is an integer (the weights can be positive, negative, or zero). 

The team's mission dictates that they must be able to select ten different groups of samples from their collection $A$. Each group (a non-empty subset $B_i$) must have a total combined weight exactly equal to a specific power of 10. Specifically, they need one group to sum to $10^1$, another to $10^2$, a third to $10^3$, and so on, continuing this pattern until the tenth group sums to $10^{10}$.

A sample can belong to more than one group, but the set $A$ represents the total collection of unique samples available.

Let $k$ be the minimum possible number of unique mineral samples the team must collect to satisfy these ten weight requirements. Find $k$.
