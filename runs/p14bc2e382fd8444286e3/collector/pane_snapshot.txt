## Solution

**Setup.** Square $BCDE$ has area $200$, so its side length is $s = \sqrt{200} =
 10\sqrt{2}$. Since the square is inscribed in circle $\omega$ with center $O$,
the point $O$ is the center of the square, and the circumradius equals half the
diagonal:

$$R = \frac{s\sqrt{2}}{2} = \frac{10\sqrt{2}\cdot\sqrt{2}}{2} = 10.$$

**Coordinates.** Place $O$ at the origin with the square axis-aligned:
- $B = (5\sqrt{2},\; 5\sqrt{2})$ — angle $45°$,
- $C = (-5\sqrt{2},\; 5\sqrt{2})$ — angle $135°$,
- $D = (-5\sqrt{2},\; -5\sqrt{2})$ — angle $225°$,
- $E = (5\sqrt{2},\; -5\sqrt{2})$ — angle $315°$.

**Segment $AB$.** $A$ is the reflection of $O$ over $B$, so $A = 2B - O = 2B$. T
hus
$$AB = |A - B| = |B| = R = 10.$$

**Major arc $\widehat{BE}$.** The arc from $B$ (at $45°$) to $E$ (at $315°$) pas
sing through $C$ and $D$ sweeps counterclockwise from $45°$ to $315°$, covering
$$315° - 45° = 270° = \frac{3}{4}\cdot 360°.$$

(Equivalently, the minor arc $BE$ is $90°$, so the major arc is $360° - 90° = 27
0°$.)

The arc length is
$$\widehat{BE}_{\text{major}} = \frac{270°}{360°}\cdot 2\pi R = \frac{3}{4}\cdot
 2\pi\cdot 10 = 15\pi.$$

**Hook length.**
$$\text{Hook} = AB + \widehat{BE}_{\text{major}} = 10 + 15\pi \approx 10 + 47.12
4 = 57.124.$$

To the nearest integer, the length of the hook is $\boxed{57}$.
