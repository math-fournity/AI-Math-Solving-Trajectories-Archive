# Problem

A team of master perfumers has just blended $N$ distinct vials of essential oils, which have a total combined weight of exactly 300 grams. Each individual vial contains a weight that is a whole number of grams (a positive integer).

The perfumers need to distribute these $N$ vials into three separate delivery cases such that each case carries an identical total weight of 100 grams. This distribution must be possible by simply placing the vials into the cases without opening or pouring them.

For which value of $N$ is it guaranteed that this distribution can be achieved, no matter how the 300 grams are distributed among the $N$ individual vials?
