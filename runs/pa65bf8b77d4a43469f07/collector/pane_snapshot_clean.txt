## Derivation of the Christoffel Symbols

u}$ are the components of the Levi-Civita connection — the unique connection tha
t is (i) **torsion-free** and (ii) **metric-compatible**. We derive $\Gamma^\lam
bda_{\mu\nu}$ from these two conditions plus the symmetry $g_{\mu\nu}=g_{\nu\mu}

**The two defining conditions.**

*Torsion-free* (symmetry in the lower indices):
$$\Gamma^\lambda_{\mu\nu} = \Gamma^\lambda_{\nu\mu}. \tag{T}$$

*Metric compatibility* ($\nabla_\rho g_{\mu\nu}=0$):
$$\partial_\rho g_{\mu\nu} - \Gamma^\sigma_{\rho\mu}\,g_{\sigma\nu} - \Gamma^\si
gma_{\rho\nu}\,g_{\mu\sigma} = 0,$$
i.e.
$$\partial_\rho g_{\mu\nu} = \Gamma^\sigma_{\rho\mu}\,g_{\sigma\nu} + \Gamma^\si
gma_{\rho\nu}\,g_{\mu\sigma}. \tag{M}$$

**Cyclic permutation trick.** Write (M) for the three cyclic permutations of the
 index triple $(\rho,\mu,\nu)$:

$$\partial_\rho g_{\mu\nu} = \Gamma^\sigma_{\rho\mu}\,g_{\sigma\nu} + \Gamma^\si
gma_{\rho\nu}\,g_{\mu\sigma}, \tag{1}$$
$$\partial_\mu g_{\nu\rho} = \Gamma^\sigma_{\mu\nu}\,g_{\sigma\rho} + \Gamma^\si
gma_{\mu\rho}\,g_{\nu\sigma}, \tag{2}$$
$$\partial_\nu g_{\rho\mu} = \Gamma^\sigma_{\nu\rho}\,g_{\sigma\mu} + \Gamma^\si
gma_{\nu\mu}\,g_{\rho\sigma}. \tag{3}$$

Form the combination **(1) + (2) − (3)**. The left-hand side is
$$\partial_\rho g_{\mu\nu} + \partial_\mu g_{\nu\rho} - \partial_\nu g_{\rho\mu}
.$$

For the right-hand side, use (T) to identify $\Gamma^\sigma_{\rho\mu}=\Gamma^\si
gma_{\mu\rho}$, $\Gamma^\sigma_{\rho\nu}=\Gamma^\sigma_{\nu\rho}$, $\Gamma^\sigm

a\nu}$ | $+\,\Gamma^\sigma_{\mu\rho}g_{\nu\sigma}=\Gamma^\sigma_{\rho\mu}g_{\sig
ma\nu}$ | — |

igma}$ | — | $-\,\Gamma^\sigma_{\nu\rho}g_{\sigma\mu}=-\Gamma^\sigma_{\rho\nu}g_

\sigma\rho}$ |

The second and third rows cancel pairwise; only the first row survives, giving a

$$\partial_\rho g_{\mu\nu} + \partial_\mu g_{\nu\rho} - \partial_\nu g_{\rho\mu}

**Relabel** dummy/free indices $\rho\to\mu,\ \mu\to\nu,\ \nu\to\sigma$ to obtain

$$\boxed{\;\Gamma^{\lambda}_{\mu\nu} \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\,g^{\lambda\sigma}\!\lef
