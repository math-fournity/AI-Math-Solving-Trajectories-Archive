## Solution

**Step 1: Structure of $2^n \pmod{1000}$.**

Since $1000 = 8 \times 125$ with $\gcd(8,125)=1$, I use CRT.

- For $n \geq 3$: $2^n \equiv 0 \pmod{8}$.
- Modulo 125: $\gcd(2,125)=1$ and $\phi(125)=100$. I compute the order of 2 mod
125:
  - $2^{10} = 1024 \equiv 24 \pmod{125}$
  - $2^{20} \equiv 24^2 = 576 \equiv 76 \pmod{125}$
  - $2^{50} \equiv 76^2 \cdot 2^{10} \equiv 76^2 \cdot 24$... more directly: $2^
{25} \equiv 76 \cdot 32 = 2432 \equiv 57 \pmod{125}$, then $2^{50} \equiv 57^2 =
 3249 \equiv 124 \equiv -1 \pmod{125}$.

So $2^{50} \equiv -1 \pmod{125}$, giving order 100. Since $\phi(125)=100$, **2 i
s a primitive root mod 125**.

**Step 2: Identify the set $R$.**

- $n=0,1,2$: remainders $1, 2, 4$ (none divisible by 8).
- $n \geq 3$: $2^n \equiv 0 \pmod{8}$ and $2^n \pmod{125}$ cycles through all 10
0 units mod 125 with period 100. By CRT, each pair $(0 \bmod 8,\; a \bmod 125)$
gives a unique value mod 1000, yielding **100 distinct values**.

No overlap since $1,2,4$ aren't $\equiv 0 \pmod{8}$. So $|R| = 103$.

**Step 3: Characterize the 100 values for $n \geq 3$.**

By CRT, the value with $x \equiv 0 \pmod{8}$, $x \equiv a \pmod{125}$ is $x \equ
iv 376a \pmod{1000}$ (since $8^{-1} \bmod 125 = 47$, so $x \equiv 8 \cdot 47 \cd
ot a = 376a \pmod{1000}$).

As $a$ ranges over all units mod 125, $376a \bmod 1000$ ranges over all **multip
les of 8 in $[0,999]$ that are not divisible by 5**. (A multiple of 8 is $\equiv
$ a unit mod 125 iff it's not divisible by 5; there are $125 - 25 = 100$ such va
lues.)

**Step 4: Compute the sum.**

$$\sum_{n=3}^{102}(2^n \bmod 1000) = \sum_{\substack{0 \le k \le 999 \\ 8\mid k,
\; 5\nmid k}} k$$

- Sum of all multiples of 8 in $[0,999]$: $8(0+1+\cdots+124) = 8 \cdot \tfrac{12
4\cdot125}{2} = 62000$.
- Sum of multiples of 40 in $[0,999]$ (those divisible by both 8 and 5): $40(0+1
+\cdots+24) = 40 \cdot \tfrac{24\cdot25}{2} = 12000$.
- Difference: $62000 - 12000 = 50000$.

**Step 5: Final answer.**

$$S = \underbrace{1 + 2 + 4}_{=7} + 50000 = 50007$$

$$S \bmod 1000 = \boxed{7}$$
