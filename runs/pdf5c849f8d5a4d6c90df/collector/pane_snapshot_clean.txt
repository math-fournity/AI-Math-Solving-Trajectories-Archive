## Solution

Let the table entries be $a_{ij} \in \{1,\dots,9\}$ for $1 \le i,j \le 2017$. Th
e row and column numbers are:
$$R_i = \sum_{j=1}^{2017} a_{ij}\, 10^{2017-j}, \qquad C_j = \sum_{i=1}^{2017} a
_{ij}\, 10^{2017-i}.$$

### Key identity

Consider the two sums:
$$\sum_{i=1}^{2017} R_i \cdot 10^{2017-i} = \sum_{i,j} a_{ij}\, 10^{(2017-j)+(20
17-i)} = \sum_{i,j} a_{ij}\, 10^{4034-i-j},$$
$$\sum_{j=1}^{2017} C_j \cdot 10^{2017-j} = \sum_{i,j} a_{ij}\, 10^{(2017-i)+(20
17-j)} = \sum_{i,j} a_{ij}\, 10^{4034-i-j}.$$

These are identical, so:
$$\sum_{i=1}^{2017} R_i \cdot 10^{2017-i} \;=\; \sum_{j=1}^{2017} C_j \cdot 10^{
2017-j}. \tag{$\star$}$$

### Ruling out $p \neq 2, 5$

**Case 1: The single non-divisible number is a row $R_k$.** Then every $C_j \equ
iv 0 \pmod{p}$ and every $R_i \equiv 0 \pmod{p}$ for $i \neq k$. Reducing $(\sta
r)$ mod $p$: the right side is $0$, and on the left every term vanishes except $
R_k \cdot 10^{2017-k}$, giving
$$R_k \cdot 10^{2017-k} \equiv 0 \pmod{p}.$$
If $p \neq 2, 5$, then $\gcd(10, p) = 1$, so $10^{2017-k}$ is invertible mod $p$
, forcing $R_k \equiv 0 \pmod{p}$—contradiction.

**Case 2: The single non-divisible number is a column $C_l$.** Then every $R_i \
equiv 0 \pmod{p}$ and every $C_j \equiv 0 \pmod{p}$ for $j \neq l$. Reducing $(\
star)$ mod $p$: the left side is $0$, and on the right every term vanishes excep
t $C_l \cdot 10^{2017-l}$, giving
$$C_l \cdot 10^{2017-l} \equiv 0 \pmod{p}.$$
Again, if $p \neq 2,5$, invertibility of $10$ forces $C_l \equiv 0 \pmod{p}$—con
tradiction.

Therefore $p \in \{2, 5\}$.

### Both $p=2$ and $p=5$ are achievable

**$p = 2$:** A number is even iff its last digit is even. The last digit of $R_i
$ is $a_{i,2017}$; the last digit of $C_j$ is $a_{2017,j}$. Choose $k \neq 2017$
. Set:
- $a_{k,2017} = 1$ (odd, so $R_k$ is odd — the unique non-divisible number),
- $a_{i,2017} = 2$ for all $i \neq k$ (so all other rows are even),
- $a_{2017,j} = 2$ for all $j$ (so all columns are even; note $a_{2017,2017}=2$
is consistent),
- all remaining entries $= 2$.

Then 4033 numbers are even and $R_k$ is odd. ✓

**$p = 5$:** A nonzero digit number is divisible by 5 iff its last digit is $5$.
 Choose $k \neq 2017$. Set:
- $a_{k,2017} = 1$ (so $R_k$ is not divisible by 5 — the unique non-divisible nu
mber),
- $a_{i,2017} = 5$ for all $i \neq k$ (so all other rows end in 5),
- $a_{2017,j} = 5$ for all $j$ (so all columns end in 5; $a_{2017,2017}=5$ is co
nsistent),
- all remaining entries $= 5$.

Then 4033 numbers are divisible by 5 and $R_k$ is not. ✓

### Conclusion

The possible values of $p$ are $2$ and $5$, and their sum is $\boxed{7}$.
