## Solution

**Problem.** Evaluate $\displaystyle S=\sum_{i=1}^{2021}\frac{z_i^{\,3}}{z_i+1}$
, where $z_1,\dots,z_{2021}$ are the roots of $P(z)=z^{2021}+z-1$.

**Step 1. Partial-fraction decomposition.**

Polynomial division gives
$$z^3=(z+1)(z^2-z+1)-1,\qquad\text{so}\qquad \frac{z^3}{z+1}=z^2-z+1-\frac{1}{z+
1}.$$

Hence
$$S=\sum_i(z_i^2-z_i+1)-\sum_i\frac{1}{z_i+1}=:A-T.$$

(Note $-1$ is not a root since $P(-1)=-3\neq 0$, so every term is defined.)

**Step 2. Computing $A$ via Vieta / Newton identities.**

For $P(z)=z^{2021}+z-1$ the only nonzero coefficients besides the leading one ar
e those of $z^1$ and $z^0$. By Vieta:
- $e_1=\sum z_i = 0$,
- $e_k=0$ for $2\le k\le 2019$,
- $e_{2020}=1$, $e_{2021}=1$.

Newton's identities give $p_1=e_1=0$ and $p_2=e_1p_1-2e_2=0$. Therefore
$$A=\sum_i z_i^2-\sum_i z_i+\sum_i 1 = 0-0+2021 = 2021.$$

**Step 3. Computing $T$ via the logarithmic derivative.**

For $P(z)=\prod_i(z-z_i)$,
$$\frac{P'(a)}{P(a)}=\sum_i\frac{1}{a-z_i},\qquad\text{so}\qquad \sum_i\frac{1}{
z_i-a}=-\frac{P'(a)}{P(a)}.$$

Taking $a=-1$:
$$T=\sum_i\frac{1}{z_i+1}=-\frac{P'(-1)}{P(-1)}.$$

Now
$$P(-1)=(-1)^{2021}+(-1)-1=-3,\qquad P'(z)=2021z^{2020}+1,\quad P'(-1)=2021+1=20
22.$$

Thus
$$T=-\frac{2022}{-3}=\frac{2022}{3}=674.$$

**Step 4. Final value.**

$$S=A-T=2021-674=1347.$$

$$\boxed{1347}$$
