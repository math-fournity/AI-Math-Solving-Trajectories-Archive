Since $a$ is a polynomial in $c$ and the recurrence uses only addition/multiplic

## Determining the Degree

$$d_{n+1} = 4 + d_n + d_{n-1}, \quad d_1 = 0,\ d_2 = 2$$

Setting $e_n = d_n + 4$, the recurrence becomes $e_{n+1} = e_n + e_{n-1}$ with $
e_1 = 4, e_2 = 6$ — the **Fibonacci recurrence**. One verifies $e_n = 2F_{n+2}$,

$$d_n = 2F_{n+2} - 4, \qquad d_{1006} = 2F_{1008} - 4$$

## Leading Coefficient (Monicity)

$x_n$ is monic for all $n$**.

Thus $p(c) := x_{1006}(c) - 2011$ is **monic of degree $d = 2F_{1008} - 4$**.
