## Solution

**Key identity.** The Chebyshev polynomial of the first kind satisfies $T_N(1)=\
cos(N\arccos 1)=\cos 0 = 1$. Since $T_N(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{N}c_n^N x^n$, evaluating
at $x=1$ gives

$$\sum_{n=0}^{N} c_n^N = T_N(1) = 1.$$

**Leading coefficient.** The leading coefficient of $T_N$ is $c_N^N = 2^{N-1}$ f
or $N\ge 1$ (standard fact: $T_N(x)=\cos(N\arccos x)$, and the leading term come
s from $\cos(N\theta)=2^{N-1}\cos^N\theta+\cdots$ with $x=\cos\theta$).

**Computing $S_N$.** Using the identity above,

$$\sum_{n=0}^{N-1} c_n^N = \sum_{n=0}^{N} c_n^N - c_N^N = 1 - c_N^N.$$

Therefore

$$S_N = \frac{\sum_{n=0}^{N-1} c_n^N}{c_N^N} = \frac{1-c_N^N}{c_N^N} = \frac{1}{
c_N^N}-1 = \frac{1}{2^{N-1}}-1.$$

**Convergence.** As $N\to\infty$,

$$S_N = \frac{1}{2^{N-1}} - 1 \longrightarrow 0 - 1 = -1.$$

**Verification with small cases.**
- $N=1$: $T_1(x)=x$, $S_1 = 0/1 = 0 = 1/2^0-1$. ✓
- $N=2$: $T_2(x)=2x^2-1$, $S_2=(-1)/2 = -1/2 = 1/2^1-1$. ✓
- $N=3$: $T_3(x)=4x^3-3x$, $S_3=(-3)/4 = -3/4 = 1/2^2-1$. ✓

**Conclusion.** The series $\{S_N\}$ is convergent, and

$$\boxed{\lim_{N\to\infty} S_N = -1}.$$
