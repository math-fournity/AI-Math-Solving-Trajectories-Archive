## Solution

**Setup.** A planing machine planes one face of a cuboidal workpiece to reduce i
ts thickness. The face being planed has dimensions equal to the two non-thicknes
s dimensions. The stroke runs along one direction (stroke length must accommodat
e that dimension), and the feed advances laterally 1.5 mm per stroke along the o
ther direction. We may orient the workpiece either way and choose either machine
 setting, seeking minimum time.

**Machine settings:**
- Setting 1: 46 strokes/min, max stroke length 180 mm.
- Setting 2: 108 strokes/min, max stroke length 77 mm.

**Time formula.** If the stroke direction has length $L$ (requiring $L \le$ max
stroke length) and the feed direction has width $W$, then:
$$\text{strokes} = \lceil W / 1.5 \rceil, \qquad T = \frac{\lceil W/1.5\rceil}{\
text{strokes per min}}.$$

---

**Workpiece 1: $120 \times 60 \times 17$.** Face to plane: $120 \times 60$.

- **Stroke along 120** (only Setting 1, since $120 \le 180$ but $120 > 77$): fee
d $= 60$, strokes $= \lceil 60/1.5\rceil = 40$, $T = 40/46 = 20/23 \approx 0.870
$ min.
- **Stroke along 60** (both settings, since $60 \le 77 \le 180$): feed $= 120$,
strokes $= \lceil 120/1.5\rceil = 80$.
  - Setting 1: $T = 80/46 = 40/23 \approx 1.739$ min.
  - Setting 2: $T = 80/108 = 20/27 \approx 0.741$ min.

Minimum: $T_1 = \dfrac{20}{27}$ min.

---

**Workpiece 2: $150 \times 50 \times 17$.** Face to plane: $150 \times 50$.

- **Stroke along 150** (only Setting 1, since $150 \le 180$ but $150 > 77$): fee
d $= 50$, strokes $= \lceil 50/1.5\rceil = \lceil 33.33\rceil = 34$, $T = 34/46
= 17/23 \approx 0.739$ min.
- **Stroke along 50** (both settings, since $50 \le 77 \le 180$): feed $= 150$,
strokes $= \lceil 150/1.5\rceil = 100$.
  - Setting 1: $T = 100/46 = 50/23 \approx 2.174$ min.
  - Setting 2: $T = 100/108 = 25/27 \approx 0.926$ min.

Comparing $17/23$ vs $25/27$: $17 \times 27 = 459 < 25 \times 23 = 575$, so $17/
23 < 25/27$.

Minimum: $T_2 = \dfrac{17}{23}$ min.

---

**Final computation.**

$$T_1 + T_2 = \frac{20}{27} + \frac{17}{23} = \frac{20 \cdot 23 + 17 \cdot 27}{2
7 \cdot 23} = \frac{460 + 459}{621} = \frac{919}{621}.$$

$$621 \cdot (T_1 + T_2) = 621 \cdot \frac{919}{621} = \boxed{919}.$$
