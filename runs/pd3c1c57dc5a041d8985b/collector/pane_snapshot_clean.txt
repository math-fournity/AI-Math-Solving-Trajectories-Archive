**Claim.** A Lie group $G$ acts on each of its coadjoint orbits $\Omega \subset
\mathfrak{g}^*$ by symplectomorphisms, where $\Omega$ is equipped with the Kiril
lov–Kostant–Souriau (KKS) symplectic form.

**Proof.**

Let $G$ be a Lie group with Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}$, and let $\operatorname{A
d}^*: G \to \mathrm{GL}(\mathfrak{g}^*)$ denote the coadjoint representation. Fo
r a fixed $\xi \in \mathfrak{g}^*$, the coadjoint orbit through $\xi$ is
$$\Omega = \{ \operatorname{Ad}^*_g \xi : g \in G \} \subset \mathfrak{g}^*.$$

**Step 1: The KKS symplectic form.** At a point $\eta \in \Omega$, the tangent s
pace is
$$T_\eta \Omega = \{ \operatorname{ad}^*_X \eta : X \in \mathfrak{g} \},$$
where $\operatorname{ad}^*_X \eta$ is the coadjoint action of $X \in \mathfrak{g
}$ on $\eta$. The KKS 2-form is defined at $\eta \in \Omega$ by
$$\omega_\eta(\operatorname{ad}^*_X \eta,\, \operatorname{ad}^*_Y \eta) = \eta([
X,Y]), \qquad X, Y \in \mathfrak{g}.$$

One verifies this is well-defined (independent of the representatives $X, Y$, si
nce $\ker(\operatorname{ad}^*(\cdot)\eta) = \mathfrak{g}_\eta$, the stabilizer L
ie algebra, and $\eta$ vanishes on $[\mathfrak{g}_\eta, \mathfrak{g}]$), is skew
-symmetric, and is nondegenerate on $T_\eta\Omega$ (its radical is exactly $\mat
hfrak{g}_\eta / \text{the kernel relation}$, which is trivial on the quotient $T
_\eta\Omega$). It is also closed; this follows from the identity
$$d\omega(\operatorname{ad}^*_X\eta, \operatorname{ad}^*_Y\eta, \operatorname{ad
}^*_Z\eta) = -\eta([X,[Y,Z]] + [Y,[Z,X]] + [Z,[X,Y]]) = 0$$
by the Jacobi identity. Hence $(\Omega, \omega)$ is a symplectic manifold.

**Step 2: The coadjoint action preserves $\omega$.** We must show that for every
 $g \in G$, the map
$$\operatorname{Ad}^*_g : \Omega \to \Omega, \qquad \eta \mapsto \operatorname{A
d}^*_g \eta$$
is a symplectomorphism, i.e. $(\operatorname{Ad}^*_g)^* \omega = \omega$.

Compute at a point $\eta \in \Omega$, with tangent vectors $\operatorname{ad}^*_
X \eta, \operatorname{ad}^*_Y \eta \in T_\eta \Omega$. The differential of $\ope
ratorname{Ad}^*_g$ at $\eta$ acts on tangent vectors by
$$d(\operatorname{Ad}^*_g)_\eta(\operatorname{ad}^*_X \eta) = \operatorname{ad}^
*_{\operatorname{Ad}_g X}(\operatorname{Ad}^*_g \eta),$$
which is the standard equivariance relation $\operatorname{Ad}^*_g \circ \operat
orname{ad}^*_X = \operatorname{ad}^*_{\operatorname{Ad}_g X} \circ \operatorname
{Ad}^*_g$ (equivalently, $\operatorname{ad}^*_X = -\left.\frac{d}{dt}\right|_{0}
\operatorname{Ad}^*_{\exp(tX)}$, differentiated version of the representation pr
operty).

Now evaluate:
$$\begin{aligned}
\big((\operatorname{Ad}^*_g)^* \omega\big)_\eta(\operatorname{ad}^*_X \eta, \ope
ratorname{ad}^*_Y \eta)
&= \omega_{\operatorname{Ad}^*_g \eta}\big(d(\operatorname{Ad}^*_g)_\eta(\operat
orname{ad}^*_X \eta),\, d(\operatorname{Ad}^*_g)_\eta(\operatorname{ad}^*_Y \eta
)\big) \\
&= \omega_{\operatorname{Ad}^*_g \eta}\big(\operatorname{ad}^*_{\operatorname{Ad
}_g X}(\operatorname{Ad}^*_g \eta),\, \operatorname{ad}^*_{\operatorname{Ad}_g Y
}(\operatorname{Ad}^*_g \eta)\big) \\
&= (\operatorname{Ad}^*_g \eta)\big([\operatorname{Ad}_g X, \operatorname{Ad}_g
Y]\big) \\
&= (\operatorname{Ad}^*_g \eta)\big(\operatorname{Ad}_g [X,Y]\big) \qquad (\text
{since } \operatorname{Ad}_g \text{ is a Lie algebra automorphism})\\
&= \eta([X,Y]) \qquad (\text{by definition of coadjoint: } (\operatorname{Ad}^*_
g \eta)(\operatorname{Ad}_g Z) = \eta(Z))\\
&= \omega_\eta(\operatorname{ad}^*_X \eta, \operatorname{ad}^*_Y \eta).
\end{aligned}$$

Since the tangent vectors of the form $\operatorname{ad}^*_X \eta$ span $T_\eta
\Omega$, this shows
$$\big((\operatorname{Ad}^*_g)^* \omega\big)_\eta = \omega_\eta \quad \text{for
all } \eta \in \Omega.$$

**Step 3: Conclusion.** The coadjoint action gives a smooth group homomorphism
$$G \to \operatorname{Symp}(\Omega, \omega), \qquad g \mapsto \operatorname{Ad}^
*_g|_\Omega,$$
i.e. $G$ acts on $(\Omega, \omega)$ by symplectomorphisms. $\blacksquare$
