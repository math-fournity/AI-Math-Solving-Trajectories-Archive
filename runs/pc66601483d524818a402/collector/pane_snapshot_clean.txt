## Proof

Let $\mathfrak{h}_n$ be the Heisenberg Lie algebra with basis $x_1,\dots,x_n,y_1
,\dots,y_n,c$ and brackets $[x_i,y_j]=\delta_{ij}c$, all other brackets on basis
 elements being zero.

**Step 1: Reduction to a symplectic condition.**

Write any element $v\in\mathfrak{h}_n$ as $v = X(v) + Y(v) + \gamma(v)\,c$, wher
e $X(v)=\sum_i a_i x_i$, $Y(v)=\sum_i b_i y_i$. For $v,w\in\mathfrak{h}_n$,
$$[v,w] = \Big(\sum_{i=1}^n a_i b'_i - a'_i b_i\Big)\,c,$$
where $w = \sum a'_i x_i + \sum b'_i y_i + \gamma'\,c$. In particular $[v,w]=0$
if and only if
$$\omega(v,w) := \sum_{i=1}^n (a_i b'_i - a'_i b_i) = 0.$$
Note $\omega$ depends only on the projections of $v,w$ onto $V:=\langle x_1,\dot
s,x_n,y_1,\dots,y_n\rangle$, and $\omega$ is the standard symplectic form on $V\
cong\mathbb{R}^{2n}$.

**Step 2: Upper bound.**

Let $A\subseteq\mathfrak{h}_n$ be an abelian Lie subalgebra, so $[A,A]=0$, equiv
alently $\omega(v,w)=0$ for all $v,w\in A$. Let $\pi:\mathfrak{h}_n\to V$ be the
 projection killing $c$. Then $\pi(A)$ is an **isotropic** subspace of the sympl
ectic space $(V,\omega)$:
$$\omega(\pi v,\pi w)=\omega(v,w)=0,\quad\forall v,w\in A.$$
A standard fact from symplectic linear algebra: every isotropic subspace of a $2
n$-dimensional symplectic vector space has dimension $\le n$. (Indeed, if $W\sub
seteq V$ is isotropic, then $W\subseteq W^\perp$, so $\dim W\le \dim W^\perp = 2
n - \dim W$, giving $\dim W\le n$.)

Now $\ker(\pi|_A) = A\cap\langle c\rangle$, which has dimension $0$ or $1$. Ther
efore
$$\dim A = \dim\pi(A) + \dim(A\cap\langle c\rangle) \le n + 1.$$

**Step 3: Achieving the bound.**

Consider $A_0 = \langle x_1,\dots,x_n,c\rangle$. By the defining relations, $[x_
i,x_j]=0$ for all $i,j$, and $[x_i,c]=0$ for all $i$. Hence $[A_0,A_0]=0$, so $A
_0$ is an abelian Lie subalgebra of dimension $n+1$.

**Conclusion.**

The maximal possible dimension of an abelian Lie subalgebra of $\mathfrak{h}_n$
is $\boxed{n+1}$.
