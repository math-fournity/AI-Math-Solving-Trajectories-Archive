# Problem

A specialized logistics hub is monitoring two pressure valves, Valve X and Valve Y. The internal pressure in each valve is measured in whole units (integers) starting at zero. According to safety protocols, the operation concludes the moment either valve reaches a pressure of 29 units.

Throughout the operation, a digital monitor displays the current pressure levels as a pair of numbers. An automated system logs an entry every time the sum of all individual digits visible on the monitor (across both pressure values) equals exactly 10. For instance, if Valve X is at 13 units and Valve Y is at 6 units, the sum of the digits (1+3+6) is 10, and a log entry is created. 

Given that the pressure in each valve only increases by 1 unit at a time (and they never increase simultaneously), what is the maximum possible number of log entries that could be recorded during a single operation?
