## Solution

**Given:** $x = 3^{1/6}$, and the system
$$x^{x^9} = \sqrt{3^{\sqrt{3}}}, \qquad y = x^{\,1/y^{y^x}}.$$

**Step 1: Verify equation 1.** With $x = 3^{1/6}$:
$$x^9 = 3^{9/6} = 3^{3/2} = 3\sqrt{3}, \qquad x^{x^9} = 3^{(1/6)\cdot 3\sqrt{3}}
 = 3^{\sqrt{3}/2} = \sqrt{3^{\sqrt{3}}}. \checkmark$$

**Step 2: Solve equation 2 via substitution.** Set $t = y^{y^x}$. Then $y = x^{1
/t}$, giving $\ln y = \frac{\ln x}{t}$, i.e., $t = \frac{\ln x}{\ln y}$.

Since $y = x^{1/t}$, we get $y^x = x^{x/t}$. Also $\ln t = y^x \ln y = x^{x/t}\c
dot\frac{\ln x}{t}$.

Let $s = x/t$ (so $t = x/s$). Substituting:
$$\ln x - \ln s = x^s \cdot \frac{s\ln x}{x} = s\ln x \cdot x^{s-1}.$$

**Step 3: Test $s = 1$:**
$$\text{LHS} = \ln x - \ln 1 = \ln x, \qquad \text{RHS} = 1\cdot\ln x\cdot x^{0}
 = \ln x. \quad \checkmark$$

So $s = 1$ is a solution, meaning $t = x$, and therefore:
$$y = x^{1/x}, \qquad y^{y^x} = (x^{1/x})^x = x.$$

**Verification of equation 2:** $y = x^{1/y^{y^x}} = x^{1/x}$. ✓

**Step 4: Compute $y^{3x}$.**
$$y^{3x} = \left(x^{1/x}\right)^{3x} = x^{(1/x)\cdot 3x} = x^3 = \left(3^{1/6}\r
ight)^3 = 3^{1/2} = \sqrt{3}.$$

**Answer:**

$$\boxed{y^{3x} = \sqrt{3}}$$
