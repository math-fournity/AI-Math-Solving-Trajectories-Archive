# Problem

In a specialized textile factory, a high-precision laser cutter is programmed to cut a perfectly circular hole with a radius of exactly $100$ cm into a massive sheet of grid-patterned fabric. The fabric consists of a repeating pattern of square embroidery cells, each with a side length of $1$ cm.

Due to the mechanical alignment of the fabric on the cutting table, the center of the circular cut is positioned such that the boundary of the circle never passes exactly through any corner vertex of the embroidery cells, nor does it ever run perfectly tangent to any of the horizontal or vertical threads forming the cell boundaries. 

Depending on the specific (non-integer) coordinates of the circle's center, the laser will pass through and intersect a specific number of individual $1$ cm squares. Let $S$ be the set of all possible integers representing the total number of cells that such a circular boundary can intersect. 

Calculate the sum of all elements in $S$.
