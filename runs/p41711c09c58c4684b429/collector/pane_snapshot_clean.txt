## Topological Euler Characteristic of a Smooth Hypersurface

**Problem.** Let $X \subset \mathbf{P}^{n+1}$ be a smooth hypersurface of degree
 $d$. Compute $\chi_{\text{top}}(X)$.

---

### Setup

$X$ is a smooth projective variety of dimension $n$. The topological Euler chara
cteristic equals the integral of the top Chern class (Gauss–Bonnet):

$$\chi_{\text{top}}(X) = \int_X c_n(T_X).$$

### Chern class computation

From the exact sequence of tangent bundles

$$0 \longrightarrow T_X \longrightarrow T_{\mathbf{P}^{n+1}}\big|_X \longrightar
row N_{X/\mathbf{P}^{n+1}} \longrightarrow 0,$$

with $N_{X/\mathbf{P}^{n+1}} \cong \mathcal{O}_X(d)$ and $c(T_{\mathbf{P}^{n+1}}
) = (1+H)^{n+2}$ (where $H$ is the hyperplane class), we get

$$c(T_X) = \frac{(1+H)^{n+2}}{1+dH}.$$

### Extracting $c_n$

The class $c_n(T_X)$ is the degree-$n$ part of the above, i.e. the coefficient o
f $H^n$ in

$$\frac{(1+H)^{n+2}}{1+dH} = (1+H)^{n+2}\sum_{k\ge 0}(-d)^k H^k.$$

The coefficient of $H^n$ is

$$[H^n]\,\frac{(1+H)^{n+2}}{1+dH} = \sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n+2}{n-k}(-d)^k = \sum_
{k=0}^{n}\binom{n+2}{k+2}(-d)^k.$$

Since $\int_X H^n = d$ (the degree of $X$), we obtain

$$\chi_{\text{top}}(X) = d\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n+2}{k+2}(-d)^k.$$

### Closed form

Setting $j = k+2$,

$$\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n+2}{k+2}(-d)^k = \frac{1}{d^2}\sum_{j=2}^{n+2}\binom{n+2
}{j}(-d)^j = \frac{(1-d)^{n+2} - 1 + (n+2)d}{d^2},$$

using the binomial theorem $\sum_{j=0}^{n+2}\binom{n+2}{j}(-d)^j = (1-d)^{n+2}$
and subtracting the $j=0,1$ terms. Multiplying by $d$:

$$\chi_{\text{top}}(X) = \frac{(1-d)^{n+2} + (n+2)d - 1}{d}.$$

### Verification

- **$n=1$** (smooth plane curve of degree $d$): $\frac{(1-d)^3+3d-1}{d} = \frac{
3d^2-d^3}{d} = d(3-d) = 2-2g$ with $g=\frac{(d-1)(d-2)}{2}$. ✓
- **$n=2$** (smooth surface in $\mathbf{P}^3$): $\frac{(1-d)^4+4d-1}{d} = d(d^2-
4d+6)$, the classical formula. ✓

### Result

$$\boxed{\displaystyle \chi_{\text{top}}(X) \;=\; \frac{(1-d)^{\,n+2} \;+\; (n+2
)\,d \;-\; 1}{d}}$$
