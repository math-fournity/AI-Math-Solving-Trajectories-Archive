## Proof

We wish to show that for all positive integers $m, n$,

$$\frac{(m+n)!}{(m+n)^{m+n}}<\frac{m!}{m^m}\frac{n!}{n^n}.$$

**Reformulation.** Dividing both sides by $\frac{m!\,n!}{(m+n)!}$, the inequalit
y is equivalent to

$$\binom{m+n}{m} = \frac{(m+n)!}{m!\,n!} < \frac{(m+n)^{m+n}}{m^m\,n^n}.$$

**Key observation.** Consider the binomial expansion

$$(m+n)^{m+n} = \sum_{k=0}^{m+n}\binom{m+n}{k}\,m^{k}\,n^{m+n-k}.$$

Every term in this sum is strictly positive (since $m,n\geq 1$). In particular,
the single term indexed by $k=m$ equals

$$\binom{m+n}{m}\,m^{m}\,n^{n}.$$

Since $m+n\geq 2$, the sum contains at least three terms, all strictly positive,
 so the full sum strictly exceeds any one of its terms:

$$(m+n)^{m+n} > \binom{m+n}{m}\,m^{m}\,n^{n}.$$

**Conclusion.** Dividing both sides by $m^m\,n^n > 0$ yields

$$\frac{(m+n)^{m+n}}{m^m\,n^n} > \binom{m+n}{m} = \frac{(m+n)!}{m!\,n!},$$

which, upon rearranging, gives

$$\frac{(m+n)!}{(m+n)^{m+n}} < \frac{m!\,n!}{m^m\,n^n} = \frac{m!}{m^m}\cdot\fra
c{n!}{n^n}.$$

This completes the proof. $\blacksquare$
