Looking at this problem, I need to determine whether the local decay of the Four
ier transform of $\mu$ on every bounded interval forces $\mu$ to be absolutely c
ontinuous.

**Answer: No.**

**Proof.**

It suffices to exhibit a singular continuous measure $\mu$ satisfying the hypoth
esis. We use the classical fact that **singular continuous Rajchman measures exi
st**—that is, there exist finite singular continuous measures $\nu$ (supported o
n, say, $[0,1]$) with $\hat{\nu}(t):=\int e^{itx}\,d\nu(x)\to 0$ as $|t|\to\inft
y$. Such measures arise, for instance, from Cantor-type constructions with disse
ction ratios $\xi_k\to 0$ sufficiently rapidly (Rajchman, Salem–Zygmund).

We claim that if $\nu$ is any Rajchman measure and $f\in L^1(|\nu|)$, then the m
easure $f\,d\nu$ is also Rajchman. Combined with $f=\mathbf{1}_{[a,b]}$, this sh
ows that every restriction $\nu|_{[a,b]}$ is Rajchman, giving the desired counte
rexample.

**Step 1: Smooth, compactly supported $f$.** Let $f\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb{R})$.
Using the Fourier inversion $f(x)=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int e^{-isx}\tilde{f}(s)\,ds$ w
here $\tilde{f}(s)=\int e^{isx}f(x)\,dx\in\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R})\subset L^1$, w
e compute:

$$\widehat{f\,d\nu}(t)=\int e^{itx}f(x)\,d\nu(x)=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int \tilde{f}(s)
\,\hat{\nu}(t-s)\,ds = \frac{1}{2\pi}(\tilde{f}*\hat{\nu})(t).$$

Since $|\hat{\nu}(u)|\le\|\nu\|$ (total variation) and $\hat{\nu}(u)\to 0$ as $|
u|\to\infty$, while $\tilde{f}\in L^1$, the dominated convergence theorem (with
dominating function $\|\nu\|\cdot|\tilde{f}(s)|\in L^1$) gives:

$$\widehat{f\,d\nu}(t)\to 0 \quad\text{as }|t|\to\infty.$$

**Step 2: General $f\in L^1(|\nu|)$.** Let $\epsilon>0$. Choose $g\in C_c^\infty
(\mathbb{R})$ with $\|f-g\|_{L^1(|\nu|)}<\epsilon$ (density of $C_c^\infty$ in $
L^1$). Then:

$$|\widehat{f\,d\nu}(t)|\le\|f-g\|_{L^1(|\nu|)}+|\widehat{g\,d\nu}(t)|<\epsilon+
|\widehat{g\,d\nu}(t)|.$$

By Step 1, $|\widehat{g\,d\nu}(t)|<\epsilon$ for $|t|$ sufficiently large. Hence
 $|\widehat{f\,d\nu}(t)|<2\epsilon$ for large $|t|$, proving $\widehat{f\,d\nu}(
t)\to 0$.

**Step 3: Conclusion.** Let $\nu$ be a singular continuous Rajchman measure supp
orted on $[0,1]$. For any $a<b$, set $f=\mathbf{1}_{[a,b]}\in L^1(|\nu|)$. By St
eps 1–2:

$$\int_a^b e^{itx}\,d\nu(x)=\widehat{\mathbf{1}_{[a,b]}\,d\nu}(t)\to 0\quad\text
{as }|t|\to\infty.$$

Thus $\nu$ satisfies the hypothesis. But $\nu$ is singular continuous, hence **n
ot** absolutely continuous with respect to Lebesgue measure. Therefore the impli
cation fails. $\blacksquare$
