## Solution

**Setup.** The line $\ell(P)$ through $P=(a,a^2)$ with slope $2a$ is the tangent
 to the parabola $y=x^2$ at $P$:
$$\ell(a):\ y = 2ax - a^2.$$

**Intersection points.** The intersection of $\ell(a_i)$ and $\ell(a_j)$ is
$$2a_i x - a_i^2 = 2a_j x - a_j^2 \implies x = \frac{a_i+a_j}{2},\quad y = a_i a
_j.$$
So the three vertices of $\triangle$ are
$$Q_{12}=\Big(\tfrac{a_1+a_2}{2},\,a_1a_2\Big),\quad Q_{13}=\Big(\tfrac{a_1+a_3}
{2},\,a_1a_3\Big),\quad Q_{23}=\Big(\tfrac{a_2+a_3}{2},\,a_2a_3\Big).$$

**Center.** Since $\triangle$ is equilateral, its center is the centroid:
$$C = \frac{Q_{12}+Q_{13}+Q_{23}}{3} = \Big(\tfrac{s}{3},\,\tfrac{p}{3}\Big),\qu
ad s=a_1+a_2+a_3,\ p=a_1a_2+a_1a_3+a_2a_3.$$

**Side lengths.**
$$Q_{12}-Q_{13}=(a_2-a_3)\Big(\tfrac12,\,a_1\Big) \implies |Q_{12}Q_{13}|^2=(a_2
-a_3)^2\Big(\tfrac14+a_1^2\Big).$$
By cyclic permutation:
$$L_1^2=(a_2-a_3)^2\big(\tfrac14+a_1^2\big),\ L_2^2=(a_1-a_3)^2\big(\tfrac14+a_2
^2\big),\ L_3^2=(a_1-a_2)^2\big(\tfrac14+a_3^2\big).$$

**Equilateral condition.** Setting $L_1^2=L_2^2$ and expanding (with $u=a_1,v=a_
2,w=a_3$):
$$(v-w)^2\big(\tfrac14+u^2\big)=(u-w)^2\big(\tfrac14+v^2\big).$$
After simplification this factors as $(u-v)\Big[(u+v)(4w^2-1)-2w(4uv-1)\Big]=0$.
 The non-degenerate case ($u\ne v$) gives
$$(u+v)(4w^2-1)=2w(4uv-1).$$
Substituting $u+v=s-w$ and $uv=p-ws+w^2$ and expanding:
$$12w^3-12sw^2+(8p-3)w+s=0.\tag{$*$}$$

**Key step.** By the cyclic symmetry of the equilateral condition, equation $(*)
$ holds for **each** of $w=a_1,a_2,a_3$. But each $a_i$ is a root of the cubic $
t^3-st^2+pt-q=0$ (where $q=a_1a_2a_3$), so $w^3=sw^2-pw+q$. Substituting into $(
*)$:
$$12(sw^2-pw+q)-12sw^2+(8p-3)w+s = -(4p+3)w+(12q+s)=0.$$
This is **linear** in $w$, yet holds for three distinct values $a_1,a_2,a_3$. Th
erefore both coefficients vanish:
$$4p+3=0,\qquad 12q+s=0.$$

**Conclusion.** The first relation gives $p=-\tfrac34$ always, so the center's $
y$-coordinate is
$$y = \frac{p}{3} = -\frac14.$$
The $x$-coordinate is $s/3$, which is free. It remains to confirm every real $s$
 is achievable: the cubic $t^3-st^2-\tfrac34 t+\tfrac{s}{12}=0$ has discriminant
$$\Delta = \frac{s^4}{3}+\frac{3s^2}{2}+\frac{27}{16}>0\quad\text{for all }s\in\
mathbb{R},$$
so it has three distinct real roots for every $s$, yielding a non-degenerate equ
ilateral triangle.

The locus of the center is the horizontal line

$$\boxed{\,y=-\dfrac{1}{4}\,}$$
