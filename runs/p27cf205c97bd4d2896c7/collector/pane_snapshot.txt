Gottschalk's surjunctivity conjecture (1973) is a genuine open problem in mathem
atics. It asserts that every group is surjunctive — that is, for every group $G$
 and every finite alphabet $A$, every injective cellular automaton (continuous,
$G$-equivariant map) on $A^G$ is surjective.

The current state of knowledge includes:
- **Finite groups** are surjunctive (an injective endomorphism of a finite set i
s surjective — this is the `isSurjunctive_of_finite` result already in the file)
.
- **Residually finite groups** are surjunctive (Gottschalk's original partial re
sult).
- **Sofic groups** are surjunctive (Gromov 1999, Weiss 2000) — this covers amena
ble groups, residually finite groups, and many others, but not all groups.
- The conjecture remains **open** for arbitrary (non-sofic) groups. No counterex
ample is known, and no proof is known in full generality. In fact, it is not eve
n known whether non-sofic groups exist.

Since this is an unsolved open problem in mathematics, I cannot provide a proof.

e main theorem, consistent with its open status.
