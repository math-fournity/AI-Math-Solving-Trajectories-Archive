**Cycle decomposition approach.** A permutation f decomposes into cycles. For a
cycle (a₁, a₂, ..., a_L) where f(a_i) = a_{i+1} (mod L), the constraint becomes
color(a_i) ≠ color(a_{i+1}) for all consecutive pairs including the wrap-around.
 This is exactly a proper coloring of the cycle graph C_L with 4 colors.

The number of proper 4-colorings of C_L is:
$$g(L) = 3^L + (-1)^L \cdot 3$$

(This is the chromatic polynomial of a cycle evaluated at q=4: (q−1)^L + (−1)^L(
q−1).)

L:

$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{A(n)}{n!} x^n = \exp\!\left(\sum_{L=1}^{\infty} \fra
c{g(L)}{L} x^L\right)$$

**Computing the inner sum:**

$$\sum_{L=1}^{\infty} \frac{3^L + 3(-1)^L}{L} x^L = \sum_{L=1}^{\infty} \frac{(3

Therefore:

$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{A(n)}{n!} x^n = \frac{1}{(1-3x)(1+x)^3}$$
