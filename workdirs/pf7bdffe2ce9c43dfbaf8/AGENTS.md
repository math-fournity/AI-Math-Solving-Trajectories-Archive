# Problem

In a remote digital archives facility, a data transmission is currently holding a specific integer value represented as a security clearance level. Two system administrators, Alice and Bob, are competing to recalibrate the system. They take turns modifying the clearance level, with Alice always initiating the sequence.

On any given turn, the acting administrator must replace the current clearance level with a new value using one of two authorized protocols:
1.  **De-escalation:** Subtract 1 from the current level.
2.  **Compression:** Divide the current level by 2.

A protocol can only be executed if the resulting clearance level remains a positive integer. The administrator who successfully recalibrates the system to Level 1 is granted master access and wins the round.

The administrators play optimally to win. Alice wants to find the most resilient starting configuration for her objective. Determine the smallest even integer clearance level that would require Alice to perform at least 2015 individual operations (turns) to guarantee her victory, regardless of Bob's defensive maneuvers.
