**Reduction modulo 3.** Let $D = (\text{Alpha's total}) - (\text{Beta's total})$

. A package of weight $w$:
- If $w \equiv 0$: assigning to either truck doesn't change $D \bmod 3$ — a **pa
ss token**.
- If $w \equiv 1$: the active player adds $+1$ (to Alpha's truck) or $+2 \equiv
-1$ (to Beta's) mod 3 — an **active token** offering choice $\{+1, +2\}$.
- If $w \equiv 2$: the active player adds $+2$ (to Alpha's) or $+1 \equiv -2$ (t
o Beta's) mod 3 — same choice $\{+1, +2\}$.

So all $\equiv 1$ and $\equiv 2$ packages are **equivalent active tokens**, and
$\equiv 0$ packages are **pass tokens**. Let $a$ = number of active tokens, $p$
= number of pass tokens, $D$ starts at $0$.

**Solving the reduced game.** I prove by strong induction on $a+p$ the following
 characterization (for $a \ge 2$; base cases $a=0$: Alpha wins iff $d\equiv 0$;
$a=1$: Alpha's turn → Alpha wins iff $d\not\equiv 0$, Beta's turn → Beta always)
:

| | $a$ **even**, $p$ **even** | $a$ **even**, $p$ **odd** | $a$ **odd** ($\ge 3
$), $p$ **even** | $a$ **odd** ($\ge 3$), $p$ **odd** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Alpha's turn** | Beta (all $d$) | Alpha iff $d\equiv 0$ | Alpha iff $d\not\e
quiv 0$ | Beta (all $d$) |
| **Beta's turn** | Alpha iff $d\equiv 0$ | Beta (all $d$) | Beta (all $d$) | Be
ta (all $d$) |

**Proof sketch (inductive step).** On each turn the player may play an active to
ken (choosing $+1$ or $+2$ mod 3) or a pass token. Each case is verified by chec
king that the player's best option among {active $+1$, active $+2$, pass} yields

- When $a$ is even and it's Alpha's turn with $p$ even: all active moves lead to

p{-}1 \text{ odd}, \text{Beta})$ which also gives Beta — so Beta wins.

The remaining cases follow analogously, completing the induction. $\square$
