## Strategy

5 separately, then apply CRT.

**Key observation:** $f(n)$ is a product of odd numbers, so it's always odd — ne

## Mod 4

The factors $1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, \ldots$ are $\equiv 1, 3, 1, 3, 1, 3, \ldots \pm
od{4}$ (alternating). So:

$$f(n) \bmod 4 = \prod_{i=1}^{n} a_i, \quad a_i = \begin{cases}1 & i \text{ odd}
 \\ 3 & i \text{ even}\end{cases}$$

Computing the pattern: $f(1)\equiv1,\; f(2)\equiv3,\; f(3)\equiv3,\; f(4)\equiv1
,\; f(5)\equiv1,\; f(6)\equiv3,\;\ldots$

The pattern is $\{1, 3, 3, 1\}$ with period 4, and each period sums to $1+3+3+1=
8\equiv 0\pmod{4}$.

Since $2016 = 4 \times 504$:

$$\sum_{n=1}^{2016} f(n) \equiv 504 \times 0 \equiv 0 \pmod{4}.$$

## Mod 25
