$$\partial E = \overline{E} \setminus E^{\circ}$$

$$\partial E = \overline{E} \cap \overline{E^c}$$

$$\partial E \text{ is closed.}$$

closed (because $E$ is open), confirming again that $\partial E$ is closed.

Now, a closed set is trivially a countable union of closed sets — specifically:
$$\partial E = \bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty} \partial E$$
This is a countable union (of a single closed set repeated countably many times,
