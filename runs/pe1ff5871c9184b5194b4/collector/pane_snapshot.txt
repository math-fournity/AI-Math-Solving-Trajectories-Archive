## Solution

**Setting up coordinates.** Place the midpoint $M$ at the origin, with $B_1 = (-
6, 0)$ and $C_1 = (6, 0)$ (since they are 12 km apart). Let $A = (a, b)$.

**Constructing $C$ from the isosceles right triangle $CB_1A$.** The right angle
is at $B_1$, so $\vec{B_1C}$ is a 90° rotation of $\vec{B_1A} = (a+6,\, b)$, wit
h equal length. Choosing the $+90°$ rotation:

$$C = B_1 + (-b,\; a+6) = (-6-b,\; a+6).$$

**Constructing $B$ from the isosceles right triangle $BC_1A$.** The right angle
is at $C_1$, so $\vec{C_1B}$ is a 90° rotation of $\vec{C_1A} = (a-6,\, b)$. Cho
osing the $-90°$ rotation:

$$B = C_1 + (b,\; -(a-6)) = (6+b,\; 6-a).$$

**Applying the distance constraints** $|MB|=7$, $|MC|=11$:

$$|B|^2 = (6+b)^2 + (6-a)^2 = 49, \qquad |C|^2 = (6+b)^2 + (a+6)^2 = 121.$$

Subtracting: $(a+6)^2 - (6-a)^2 = 72$, i.e., $24a = 72$, so $\boxed{a = 3}$.

Substituting back: $(6+b)^2 + 9 = 49 \Rightarrow (6+b)^2 = 40 \Rightarrow b = -6
 \pm 2\sqrt{10}$.

**Checking the "outside" condition.** The triangle $CB_1A$ (sharing edge $CA$ wi
th $\triangle ABC$) is outside $\triangle ABC$ iff $B_1$ and $B$ are on opposite
 sides of line $CA$. The triangle $BC_1A$ (sharing edge $BA$) is outside iff $C_
1$ and $C$ are on opposite sides of line $BA$.

For $b = -6 + 2\sqrt{10} \approx 0.325$: $A=(3,\,0.325)$, $B=(2\sqrt{10},\,3)$,
$C=(-2\sqrt{10},\,9)$.

- **Line $CA$ test:** Computing signed distances of $B_1$ and $B$ from line $CA$
 yields opposite signs. ✓
- **Line $BA$ test:** Computing signed distances of $C_1$ and $C$ from line $BA$
 yields opposite signs. ✓

For $b = -6 - 2\sqrt{10} \approx -12.325$: the $CA$ test gives $B_1$ and $B$ on
the **same** side. ✗

(The other two rotation-sign combinations, by symmetry, either fail the outside
condition or reduce to the mirror image of this solution with the same area.)

**Computing the area** via the shoelace formula with $A=(3,\, b)$, $B=(6+b,\, 3)
$, $C=(-6-b,\, 9)$:

$$\text{Area} = \tfrac{1}{2}\big|3(3-9) + (6+b)(9-b) + (-6-b)(b-3)\big|.$$

Expanding with $b = -6+2\sqrt{10}$:

$$= \tfrac{1}{2}\big|-18 + (2\sqrt{10})(15-2\sqrt{10}) + (-2\sqrt{10})(-9+2\sqrt
{10})\big|$$
$$= \tfrac{1}{2}\big|-18 + 30\sqrt{10} - 40 + 18\sqrt{10} - 40\big| = \tfrac{1}{
2}\big|48\sqrt{10} - 98\big| = 24\sqrt{10} - 49.$$

**Verification.** $24\sqrt{10} \approx 75.89$, so the area $\approx 26.89 > 0$.
✓

$$\boxed{\text{Area}(\triangle ABC) = 24\sqrt{10} - 49 \text{ km}^2}$$
