$$A_n \cap A_m = (Y_n \cup \{0\}) \cap (Y_m \cup \{0\}) = \underbrace{(Y_n \cap

$$c(a) = (a_0, a_1) \in \{(i, j) \in \omega^2 : i < j\}.$$
