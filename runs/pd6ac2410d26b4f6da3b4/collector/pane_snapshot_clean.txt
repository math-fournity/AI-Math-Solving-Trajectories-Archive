Looking at this problem, I need to find the number of red/blue colorings of all
subsets of $S = \{1,\ldots,n\}$ such that $f(T_1)f(T_2) = f(T_1 \cup T_2)f(T_1 \
cap T_2)$ for all $T_1, T_2 \subseteq S$, where $f(T)$ counts blue subsets of $T
$.

Let me work through the analysis by cases.

## Setup

Let $g(T) \in \{0,1\}$ indicate whether $T$ is blue, so $f(T) = \sum_{U \subsete
q T} g(U)$. Let $\mathcal{B} = \{T : g(T)=1\}$ be the family of blue sets.

## Case 1: $g(\emptyset) = 1$ (empty set is blue)

Then $f(T) \geq 1$ for all $T$, so $h(T) = \log f(T)$ is well-defined. The condi
tion becomes:
$$h(T_1) + h(T_2) = h(T_1 \cup T_2) + h(T_1 \cap T_2)$$
This is the **modularity** condition on the Boolean lattice, whose solutions are
 $h(T) = h(\emptyset) + \sum_{i \in T} \alpha_i$. Thus $f(T) = f(\emptyset) \pro
d_{i \in T} \beta_i$ where $\beta_i = e^{\alpha_i}$.

Since $f(\emptyset) = g(\emptyset) = 1$ and $f(\{i\}) = 1 + g(\{i\}) \in \{1,2\}
$, we get $\beta_i \in \{1,2\}$. By Möbius inversion, $g(T) = \prod_{i \in T} g(
\{i\})$ for all nonempty $T$, which is automatically in $\{0,1\}$. Each $g(\{i\}
)$ is chosen freely, giving $\mathbf{2^n}$ **colorings**.

## Case 2: $g(\emptyset) = 0$ (empty set is red)

### Subcase 2a: $\mathcal{B} = \emptyset$ (no blue sets)

Then $f \equiv 0$ and the condition $0 = 0$ holds trivially. This gives **1 colo
ring**.

### Subcase 2b: $\mathcal{B} \neq \emptyset$

**Unique minimal blue set.** Suppose $B_0, B_0'$ are two incomparable minimal bl
ue sets. Then $f(B_0) = 1$, $f(B_0') = 1$, but $B_0 \cap B_0' \subsetneq B_0$ so
 $f(B_0 \cap B_0') = 0$. The condition gives $1 \cdot 1 = f(B_0 \cup B_0') \cdot
 0 = 0$, a contradiction. So there is a **unique minimal blue set** $B_0$.

**All blue sets contain $B_0$.** If $B$ is blue with $B_0 \not\subseteq B$, then
 $B_0 \cap B \subsetneq B_0$ so $f(B_0 \cap B) = 0$. The condition with $T_1 = B
_0, T_2 = B$ gives $f(B_0) f(B) = f(B_0 \cup B) \cdot 0 = 0$, but $f(B_0) \geq 1
$ and $f(B) \geq 1$, contradiction.

**Reduction to multiplicative structure.** For $A \subseteq S \setminus B_0$, de
fine $F(A) = f(B_0 \cup A)$. Since all blue sets contain $B_0$:
- $F(\emptyset) = f(B_0) = 1$ (only blue subset of $B_0$ is $B_0$ itself).
- $F(A) \geq 1$ for all $A$.

For $T_1, T_2$ both containing $B_0$, writing $T_i = B_0 \cup A_i$, the conditio
n reduces to $F(A_1)F(A_2) = F(A_1 \cup A_2)F(A_1 \cap A_2)$. For $T_1, T_2$ not
 both containing $B_0$, at least one has $f = 0$ and $f(T_1 \cap T_2) = 0$, so t
he condition holds automatically.

Since $F > 0$ everywhere with $F(\emptyset) = 1$, the same log-modular argument
as Case 1 applies on the ground set $S \setminus B_0$ (of size $n - |B_0|$): $F(
A) = \prod_{i \in A} \beta_i$ with $\beta_i \in \{1,2\}$, and Möbius inversion g
ives the multiplicative structure. This yields $2^{n - |B_0|}$ colorings for eac
h choice of $B_0$.

**Counting.** Summing over $|B_0| = k$ from $1$ to $n$, with $\binom{n}{k}$ choi
ces of $B_0$ and $2^{n-k}$ colorings each:
$$\sum_{k=1}^{n} \binom{n}{k} 2^{n-k} = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k} 2^{n-k} - 2^
n = 3^n - 2^n$$

## Final Count

$$\underbrace{2^n}_{\text{Case 1}} + \underbrace{1}_{\text{Case 2a}} + \underbra
ce{(3^n - 2^n)}_{\text{Case 2b}} = \boxed{3^n + 1}$$

## Verification

- **$n=1$:** $3^1 + 1 = 4$. All $2^2 = 4$ colorings work (no nontrivial pair of
subsets with distinct union/intersection). ✓
- **$n=2$:** $3^2 + 1 = 10$. Direct enumeration: with $g(\emptyset)=1$, the mult
iplicative condition $g(\{1,2\}) = g(\{1\})g(\{2\})$ gives 4 colorings; with $g(
\emptyset)=0$, the constraint $g(\{1\})g(\{2\})=0$ plus free $g(\{1,2\})$ gives
6 colorings. Total $4+6=10$. ✓
