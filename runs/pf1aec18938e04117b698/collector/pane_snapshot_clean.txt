# Künneth Formula for the Wedge Product on de Rham Cohomology of $M \times N$

## Setup

Let $M$, $N$ be smooth manifolds, and let
$$\pi_1 : M \times N \to M, \qquad \pi_2 : M \times N \to N$$
be the canonical projections. The de Rham cohomology groups $H^*_{\mathrm{dR}}(M
)$, $H^*_{\mathrm{dR}}(N)$, $H^*_{\mathrm{dR}}(M\times N)$ are graded-commutativ
e $\mathbb{R}$-algebras under the wedge product. Define the **exterior product m
ap**
$$\Phi : H^*_{\mathrm{dR}}(M)\otimes_{\mathbb{R}} H^*_{\mathrm{dR}}(N) \longrigh
tarrow H^*_{\mathrm{dR}}(M\times N), \qquad \alpha\otimes\beta \;\longmapsto\; \
pi_1^*\alpha\wedge\pi_2^*\beta.$$

---

## Theorem (Künneth Formula — Additive and Multiplicative)

**(1) Additive structure.** For every $k\ge 0$, $\Phi$ restricts to an isomorphi
sm of $\mathbb{R}$-vector spaces
$$\bigoplus_{p+q=k} H^p_{\mathrm{dR}}(M)\otimes_{\mathbb{R}} H^q_{\mathrm{dR}}(N
) \;\xrightarrow{\;\sim\;}\; H^k_{\mathrm{dR}}(M\times N).$$
*(Since de Rham cohomology is taken over the field $\mathbb{R}$ of characteristi
c $0$, there is no $\mathrm{Tor}$ term; the Künneth short exact sequence splits,
 and the splitting is realized canonically by $\Phi$.)*

**(2) Multiplicative structure.** The map $\Phi$ is an isomorphism of **graded $
\mathbb{R}$-algebras**, where the domain is equipped with the **graded tensor pr
oduct** (Koszul) multiplication:
$$(\alpha_1\otimes\beta_1)\cdot(\alpha_2\otimes\beta_2) \;=\; (-1)^{\deg\beta_1\
,\cdot\,\deg\alpha_2}\;(\alpha_1\wedge\alpha_2)\otimes(\beta_1\wedge\beta_2),$$
for homogeneous $\alpha_i\in H^*_{\mathrm{dR}}(M)$, $\beta_i\in H^*_{\mathrm{dR}
}(N)$.

**(3) Relationship between the wedge products on $M$, $N$, and $M\times N$.** Un
der $\Phi$, the wedge product on $M\times N$ is completely determined by the wed
ge products on $M$ and $N$ plus the Koszul sign. Concretely, for homogeneous cla
sses:

- **Within a single factor** (pullbacks are algebra homomorphisms):
$$\pi_1^*\alpha_1\wedge\pi_1^*\alpha_2 = \pi_1^*(\alpha_1\wedge\alpha_2), \qquad
 \pi_2^*\beta_1\wedge\pi_2^*\beta_2 = \pi_2^*(\beta_1\wedge\beta_2).$$

- **Across factors** (graded commutativity, sign governed by the degrees of the
factor-classes):
$$\pi_1^*\alpha\wedge\pi_2^*\beta = (-1)^{\deg\alpha\,\cdot\,\deg\beta}\;\pi_2^*
\beta\wedge\pi_1^*\alpha.$$

- **General mixed product** (combining the two rules):
$$\pi_1^*\alpha_1\wedge\pi_2^*\beta_1\wedge\pi_1^*\alpha_2\wedge\pi_2^*\beta_2 =
 (-1)^{\deg\beta_1\,\cdot\,\deg\alpha_2}\;\pi_1^*(\alpha_1\wedge\alpha_2)\wedge\
pi_2^*(\beta_1\wedge\beta_2).$$

## Proof that $\Phi$ is a graded-algebra homomorphism (the sign verification)

It suffices to verify multiplicativity on homogeneous elements. Compute:
$$\Phi(\alpha_1\otimes\beta_1)\wedge\Phi(\alpha_2\otimes\beta_2) = (\pi_1^*\alph
a_1\wedge\pi_2^*\beta_1)\wedge(\pi_1^*\alpha_2\wedge\pi_2^*\beta_2).$$

To regroup, swap $\pi_2^*\beta_1$ (of degree $\deg\beta_1$) past $\pi_1^*\alpha_
2$ (of degree $\deg\alpha_2$). By graded commutativity of the wedge on $M\times
N$, this introduces the sign $(-1)^{\deg\beta_1\cdot\deg\alpha_2}$:
$$= (-1)^{\deg\beta_1\cdot\deg\alpha_2}\;\pi_1^*\alpha_1\wedge\pi_1^*\alpha_2\we
dge\pi_2^*\beta_1\wedge\pi_2^*\beta_2.$$

Now use that $\pi_1^*$, $\pi_2^*$ are algebra homomorphisms:
$$= (-1)^{\deg\beta_1\cdot\deg\alpha_2}\;\pi_1^*(\alpha_1\wedge\alpha_2)\wedge\p
i_2^*(\beta_1\wedge\beta_2) = \Phi\!\Big((-1)^{\deg\beta_1\cdot\deg\alpha_2}(\al
pha_1\wedge\alpha_2)\otimes(\beta_1\wedge\beta_2)\Big).$$

By the definition of the graded tensor product multiplication, the last expressi
on is exactly
$$\Phi\!\big((\alpha_1\otimes\beta_1)\cdot(\alpha_2\otimes\beta_2)\big). \qquad\
checkmark$$

Thus $\Phi$ respects the multiplication and the grading, so combined with the ad
ditive isomorphism from (1), $\Phi$ is an isomorphism of graded $\mathbb{R}$-alg
ebras. $\blacksquare$

**Summary.** The Künneth formula for de Rham cohomology states that the exterior
 product $\alpha\otimes\beta\mapsto\pi_1^*\alpha\wedge\pi_2^*\beta$ is a graded-
algebra isomorphism
$$H^*_{\mathrm{dR}}(M)\;\widehat{\otimes}\;H^*_{\mathrm{dR}}(N) \;\xrightarrow{\
;\sim\;}\; H^*_{\mathrm{dR}}(M\times N),$$
where $\widehat{\otimes}$ denotes the **graded (Koszul) tensor product** of grad
ed algebras. The wedge product on $M\times N$ is the wedge products on $M$ and $
N$ "glued together" with the Koszul sign $(-1)^{\deg\beta_1\cdot\deg\alpha_2}$ t
hat appears whenever a class from $N$ is moved past a class from $M$.
