# Problem

In a futuristic circular space station, there are 20 docking bays labeled $D_1, D_2, \dots, D_{20}$ arranged in a perfect circle at equal intervals. A specialized maintenance drone starts its shift at bay $D_1$. 

The drone’s propulsion system is restricted to only two types of maneuvers:
1. It can move to an immediately adjacent docking bay (for example, from $D_3$ to $D_2$ or $D_4$).
2. It can engage a long-range thruster to travel to the docking bay located diametrically opposite its current position (for example, from $D_1$ to $D_{11}$).

The drone must perform a "Full Circuit" routine. A Full Circuit is defined as a sequence of moves where the drone visits every single one of the 20 docking bays exactly once before finally returning to its starting position at $D_1$.

How many distinct sequences of moves (paths) can the drone take to complete this Full Circuit?
