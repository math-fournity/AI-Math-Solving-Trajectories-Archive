$$
\operatorname{Tr}(AB)=\operatorname{Tr}(B^{1/2}AB^{1/2})\ge 0,

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(T_N S_M)=\sum_{n\le N,\,m\le M}\lambda_n\mu_m\,\bigl|\langle e
