## Proof

**Setup.** Let $P = 2017$ (prime). The key labels are $A = \{2, 3, \dots, 2016\}
 = \mathbb{F}_{2017}^* \setminus \{1\}$, with $|A| = 2015$. For each $1007$-subs
et $T \subseteq A$, $f(T) = \prod_{x \in T} x \pmod{2017}$.

**Step 1: Symmetry.** Expand the summand:
$$(f(T) - f(T)^{-1})^2 = f(T)^2 - 2 + f(T)^{-2}.$$
So the total is $\sum_T f(T)^2 + \sum_T f(T)^{-2} - 2|S|$.

The map $x \mapsto x^{-1}$ is a permutation of $A$ (since $x^{-1}=1 \iff x=1 \no
tin A$). Under the bijection $T \mapsto T' = \{x^{-1} : x \in T\}$ on $1007$-sub
sets, $f(T') = f(T)^{-1}$, hence $f(T')^2 = f(T)^{-2}$. Therefore
$$\sum_T f(T)^{-2} = \sum_{T'} f(T')^2 = \sum_T f(T)^2.$$
The total becomes $2\sum_T f(T)^2 - 2|S|$.

**Step 2: Express $\sum_T f(T)^2$ as a symmetric polynomial.** Since $f(T)^2 = \
prod_{x \in T} x^2$,
$$\sum_T f(T)^2 = e_{1007}(2^2, 3^2, \dots, 2016^2),$$
the degree-$1007$ elementary symmetric polynomial in the squares of elements of
$A$.

**Step 3: Generating function.** The generating function is
$$G(t) = \prod_{x=2}^{2016}(1 + x^2 t) = \frac{\prod_{x=1}^{2016}(1+x^2 t)}{1+t}
.$$
The quadratic residues $Q$ mod $2017$ satisfy $|Q| = 1008$ and are exactly the r
oots of $u^{1008}-1=0$ (since $Q = \{u : u^{(p-1)/2}=1\}$). As $x$ ranges over $
\{1,\dots,2016\}$, each $q \in Q$ appears as $x^2$ exactly twice, so
$$\prod_{x=1}^{2016}(1+x^2 t) = \prod_{q \in Q}(1+qt)^2.$$
Using $\prod_{q \in Q}(u - q) = u^{1008}-1$ and substituting $u = -1/t$:
$$\prod_{q \in Q}(1+qt) = 1 - t^{1008}.$$
Therefore
$$G(t) = \frac{(1-t^{1008})^2}{1+t}.$$

**Step 4: Extract the coefficient of $t^{1007}$.** Since $(1+t) \mid (1-t^{1008}
)$ (as $1008$ is even), $G(t)$ is a polynomial of degree $2015$. Write $(1-t^{10
08})^2 = 1 - 2t^{1008} + t^{2016}$ and $1/(1+t) = \sum_{k \ge 0}(-1)^k t^k$. The
 coefficient of $t^{1007}$ receives contributions only from $1 \cdot \sum_k (-1)
^k t^k$ (the $-2t^{1008}$ and $t^{2016}$ terms start at degrees $\ge 1008$). Thu
s
$$[t^{1007}]\, G(t) = (-1)^{1007} = -1.$$
So $\sum_T f(T)^2 \equiv -1 \pmod{2017}$.

**Step 5: Compute $|S| = \binom{2015}{1007} \pmod{2017}$.** Using $\binom{p-1}{k
} \equiv (-1)^k \pmod p$ and $\binom{p-2}{k} = \binom{p-1}{k}\cdot\frac{p-1-k}{p
-1} \equiv (-1)^k(1+k) \pmod p$:
$$\binom{2015}{1007} \equiv (-1)^{1007}(1+1007) = -1008 \equiv 1009 \pmod{2017}.
$$

**Step 6: Final computation.**
$$\text{Total} = 2(-1) - 2(1009) = -2 - 2018 = -2020 \equiv -3 \equiv 2014 \pmod
{2017}.$$

The remainder when the Total Network Variance is divided by $2017$ is $\boxed{20
14}$.
