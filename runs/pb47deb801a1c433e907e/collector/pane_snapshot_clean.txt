## Proof

Let $T$ be the unlabeled RP-tree obtained by creating a new root $r$ and attachi
ng the roots of $T_1, T_2, \ldots, T_m$ (in this left-to-right order) as the $m$
 children of $r$.

Recall that a depth-first traversal of a rooted plane tree visits the children o
f each node in their prescribed left-to-right order, fully exploring the subtree
 of each child before moving to the next.

**Traversal of $T$.** Starting at the new root $r$, the depth-first traversal pr
oceeds as follows. For each $i = 1, 2, \ldots, m$:

1. The edge from $r$ to the root $r_i$ of $T_i$ is followed **downward** (father
 to son), contributing a $+1$.
2. The traversal then enters $T_i$ at its root $r_i$ and performs a complete dep
th-first traversal of $T_i$. By definition, this contributes exactly the sequenc
e $D(T_i)$.
3. Upon completing the traversal of $T_i$, the edge from $r_i$ back to $r$ is fo
llowed **upward** (son to father), contributing a $-1$.

After the $m$-th subtree is fully traversed and we return to $r$, the traversal
of $T$ is complete (there are no further children of $r$ to visit).

**Concatenation.** Since the depth-first traversal is sequential and the contrib
utions above occur in contiguous, non-overlapping blocks, the full sequence $D(T
)$ is the concatenation of these blocks in order:

$$\boxed{D(T) \;=\; +1,\; D(T_1),\; -1,\; +1,\; D(T_2),\; -1,\; \ldots,\; +1,\;
D(T_m),\; -1.}$$

Equivalently, writing $+1$ as "$+$" and $-1$ as "$-$" for readability:

$$D(T) = \bigplus_{i=1}^{m}\Big(+,\; D(T_i),\; -\Big),$$

i.e., $D(T)$ is obtained by surrounding each $D(T_i)$ with a leading $+1$ and a
trailing $-1$, and concatenating all $m$ resulting blocks.

**Verification (length check).** If $T_i$ has $n_i$ edges, then $D(T_i)$ contain
s $n_i$ entries equal to $+1$ (one per downward edge) and $n_i$ entries equal to
 $-1$ (one per upward edge), so $|D(T_i)| = 2n_i$. The tree $T$ has $n = m + \su
m_{i=1}^m n_i$ edges ($m$ edges from $r$ to the $r_i$, plus all edges within the
 $T_i$). Our formula gives $|D(T)| = \sum_{i=1}^m(1 + 2n_i + 1) = 2m + 2\sum n_i
 = 2(m + \sum n_i) = 2n$, which matches the expected $2 \times (\text{number of
edges})$. The number of $+1$'s is $m + \sum n_i = n$ and the number of $-1$'s is
 $m + \sum n_i = n$, confirming balance. The prefix-sum structure is also preser
ved: each block $+, D(T_i), -$ is itself a balanced excursion (it starts at the
current level, the $+1$ raises by one, $D(T_i)$ is a balanced excursion at that
raised level, and the $-1$ returns to the original level), so $D(T)$ is a valid
Łukasiewicz-type excursion as required.
