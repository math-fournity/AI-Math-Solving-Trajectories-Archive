$2^m + 3^n + 1 = 1 \Rightarrow 2^m + 3^n = 0$, impossible since both terms are $

- $m=2$: $3^n = 1 \Rightarrow n=0$. ✓ **(2, 0, 1)**

$$2^m + 3^n + 1 \equiv 0 \pmod{8}$$
