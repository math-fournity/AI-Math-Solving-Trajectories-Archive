## Proof

Let $h(z) = \dfrac{P(z)}{Q(z)}$, where
- $P(z) = 5z^{2015} + 7z^{2010} - 38z^5 + z^4 - 2z^3 + 5$ (degree 2015)
- $Q(z) = 7z^{2016} + 2z^{2013} - 6z^8 + 3z^7 + z^5 - 4z^2 - z + 111$ (degree 20
16)

**Key observation.** Since $\deg Q = 2016 > \deg P = 2015$, the point at infinit
y is **not** a pole of $h$ (in fact $h(z) \to 0$ as $z \to \infty$). All poles o
f $h$ are finite, occurring at the zeros of $Q(z)$.

**Residue theorem on the Riemann sphere.** For any meromorphic function on the e
xtended complex plane, the sum of all residues — including the residue at infini
ty — equals zero:
$$\sum_{\substack{a \text{ finite pole}}} \operatorname{Res}(h, a) \;+\; \operat
orname{Res}(h, \infty) \;=\; 0.$$

Therefore the sum of residues at all finite poles is $-\operatorname{Res}(h, \in
fty)$.

**Computing the residue at infinity.** By definition,
$$\operatorname{Res}(h, \infty) = -\operatorname{Res}\!\left(\frac{1}{z^2}\,h\!\
left(\frac{1}{z}\right),\; z=0\right).$$

We compute $h(1/z)$. Factor out the leading powers:

$$P(1/z) = z^{-2015}\bigl(5 + 7z^5 - 38z^{2010} + z^{2011} - 2z^{2012} + 5z^{201
5}\bigr),$$

$$Q(1/z) = z^{-2016}\bigl(7 + 2z^3 - 6z^{2008} + 3z^{2009} + z^{2011} - 4z^{2014
} - z^{2015} + 111z^{2016}\bigr).$$

Hence
$$h(1/z) = \frac{z^{-2015}}{z^{-2016}} \cdot \frac{5 + 7z^5 - 38z^{2010} + \cdot
s}{7 + 2z^3 - 6z^{2008} + \cdots} = z \cdot \frac{5 + 7z^5 - \cdots}{7 + 2z^3 -
\cdots}.$$

Dividing by $z^2$:
$$\frac{1}{z^2}\,h(1/z) = \frac{1}{z} \cdot \frac{5 + 7z^5 - 38z^{2010} + \cdots
}{7 + 2z^3 - 6z^{2008} + \cdots}.$$

The fraction on the right is analytic at $z = 0$ with value $\frac{5}{7}$. There
fore the Laurent expansion of $\frac{1}{z^2}h(1/z)$ about $z=0$ has the form

$$\frac{1}{z^2}h(1/z) = \frac{5/7}{z} + (\text{non-negative powers of } z),$$

so
$$\operatorname{Res}\!\left(\frac{1}{z^2}h(1/z),\, 0\right) = \frac{5}{7}, \qqua
d \operatorname{Res}(h, \infty) = -\frac{5}{7}.$$

**Conclusion.** The sum of the residues of $h$ at all its (finite) poles is

$$\sum_{\text{finite poles}} \operatorname{Res}(h, a) = -\operatorname{Res}(h, \
infty) = -\!\left(-\frac{5}{7}\right) = \boxed{\dfrac{5}{7}}.$$
