$\mathbb{Q}$ by Eisenstein's criterion with $p = 2$: the prime $2$ divides the c

alpha):\mathbb{Q}] = 4$ where $\alpha = \sqrt[4]{2}$.

**Step 2: The splitting field.** The roots of $x^4 + 2 = 0$, i.e. $x^4 = -2$, ar

$$\alpha_k = \sqrt[4]{2}\cdot e^{2\pi i k/4} = \alpha\cdot i^k, \quad k = 0,1,2,

namely $\alpha,\, i\alpha,\, -\alpha,\, -i\alpha$. The splitting field is
$$K = \mathbb{Q}(\alpha, i).$$

alpha) \subset \mathbb{R}$, so $i \notin \mathbb{Q}(\alpha)$. The minimal polyno

$$[K : \mathbb{Q}] = [\mathbb{Q}(\alpha,i):\mathbb{Q}(\alpha)]\cdot[\mathbb{Q}(\
alpha):\mathbb{Q}] = 2 \cdot 4 = 8.$$
Thus $|\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q})| = 8$.

**Step 4: Structure of the Galois group.** Every $\mathbb{Q}$-automorphism of $K
