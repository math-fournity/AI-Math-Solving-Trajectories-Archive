取 $x=0$：$2f(y)=f(y)+\alpha^y f(-y)$，即

取 $y=1$：$f(x)f(1)=f(x+1)+\alpha\, f(x-1)$，即
$$f(x+1)=t\,f(x)-\alpha\,f(x-1),\quad t:=f(1).\tag{2}$$

$$f(n)=\lambda_1^n+\lambda_2^n.\tag{3}$$
