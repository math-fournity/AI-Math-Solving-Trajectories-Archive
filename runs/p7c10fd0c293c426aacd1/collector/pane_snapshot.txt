## Setup

The position is a multiset of non-negative integers. Each integer $k \geq 2$ on
the board is an independent sub-game. A move replaces $k$ with five non-negative
 integers summing to $k$ with pairwise differences $\leq 1$.

**Key observation:** For $k = 5q + r$ ($0 \le r \le 4$), the replacement is **un
ique**: $r$ copies of $q+1$ and $(5-r)$ copies of $q$. So each $k \geq 2$ has ex
actly **one** move.

## Grundy Values

Let $g(k)$ be the Grundy value. Since there's only one move, $g(k) = \operatorna
me{mex}(\{S_k\})$ where $S_k$ is the nim-sum of the five children's Grundy value
s. This gives $g(k) \in \{0,1\}$ for all $k$:
- $g(k) = 1$ if $S_k = 0$
- $g(k) = 0$ if $S_k \neq 0$

Computing $S_k$: XOR of $r$ copies of $g(q+1)$ and $(5-r)$ copies of $g(q)$, whi
ch reduces to:
- **$r$ even** ($r \in \{0,2,4\}$): $S_k = g(q)$, so $g(k) = 1 - g(q)$
- **$r$ odd** ($r \in \{1,3\}$): $S_k = g(q+1)$, so $g(k) = 1 - g(q+1)$

Base cases: $g(0) = g(1) = 0$ (terminal — cannot be replaced).
