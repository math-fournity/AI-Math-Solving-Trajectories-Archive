## Solution

**Setup.** Place $B=(0,0)$, $C=(14,0)$. From $AB=13$, $AC=15$: solving gives $A=
(5,12)$ (since $5^2+12^2=169$ and $(5-14)^2+12^2=225$).

**Reflection condition.** Folding along $DE$ sends $A$ to $A'\in BC$, so $DE$ is
 the perpendicular bisector of $AA'$. Write $A'=(t,0)$ and set $a=5-t$. Then eve
ry point on $DE$ is equidistant from $A$ and $A'$.

- $E\in AB$: parametrize $E=(5-5v,\,12-12v)$, so $AE=13v$. The condition $EA=EA'
$ gives
$$169v^2=(a+5v)^2+144(1-v)^2 \;\Longrightarrow\; v=\frac{a^2+144}{288+10a}.$$

- $D\in AC$: parametrize $D=(5+9u,\,12-12u)$, so $AD=15u$. The condition $DA=DA'
$ gives
$$225u^2=(a+9u)^2+144(1-u)^2 \;\Longrightarrow\; u=\frac{a^2+144}{288-18a}.$$

**Cyclic condition via power of a point.** Since $BCDE$ is cyclic, lines $AB$ an
d $AC$ are secants from $A$ meeting the circle at $(B,E)$ and $(C,D)$. By power
of a point:
$$AB\cdot AE = AC\cdot AD \;\Longrightarrow\; 13\cdot 13v = 15\cdot 15u \;\Longr
ightarrow\; 169v=225u.$$

Substituting the expressions for $u,v$:
$$\frac{288+10a}{288-18a}=\frac{169}{225} \;\Longrightarrow\; 225(288+10a)=169(2
88-18a).$$
$$56\cdot 288 = -5292a \;\Longrightarrow\; a=-\frac{16128}{5292}=-\frac{64}{21}.
$$

So $t=5-a=5+\tfrac{64}{21}=\tfrac{169}{21}\in[0,14]$. ✓
