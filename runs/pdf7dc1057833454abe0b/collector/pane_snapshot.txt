### Step 1: Simplify the ratio of Gamma functions

Using $\Gamma(n+\tfrac12)=\dfrac{(2n)!}{4^n n!}\sqrt{\pi}$ and $\Gamma(n)=(n-1)!
$,

$$\frac{\Gamma(n+\tfrac12)}{\Gamma(n)}=\frac{(2n)!\,\sqrt{\pi}}{4^n\,n!\,(n-1)!}
.$$

Since $(n-1)! = n!/n$, we get $\dfrac{(2n)!}{n!\,(n-1)!}=n\,\dfrac{(2n)!}{(n!)^2
}=n\binom{2n}{n}$, hence

$$\frac{\Gamma(n+\tfrac12)}{\Gamma(n)}=\frac{n\,\binom{2n}{n}\,\sqrt{\pi}}{4^n}.
$$

### Step 2: Reduce the series

Substituting,

$$\frac{S}{\sqrt{\pi}}=\sum_{n\ge 1}\frac{(-1)^{n-1}\,n\binom{2n}{n}}{n^2\cdot 4
^n}=\sum_{n\ge 1}\frac{(-1)^{n-1}\binom{2n}{n}}{n\cdot 4^n}.$$

Define $f(x)=\displaystyle\sum_{n\ge 1}\frac{\binom{2n}{n}}{n}\,x^n$. Then

$$\frac{S}{\sqrt{\pi}}=-\sum_{n\ge 1}\frac{\binom{2n}{n}}{n}\Big(-\tfrac14\Big)^
n=-f\!\Big(-\tfrac14\Big).$$

### Step 3: Evaluate $f$ via the central-binomial generating function

From $\displaystyle\sum_{n\ge 0}\binom{2n}{n}x^n=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-4x}}$,

$$f'(x)=\sum_{n\ge 1}\binom{2n}{n}x^{n-1}=\frac{1}{x}\!\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-4x
}}-1\right),\qquad f(x)=\int_0^x\frac{1}{t}\!\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-4t}}-1\right
)dt.$$

### Step 4: Compute $f(-1/4)$

Substitute $u=1-4t$, i.e. $t=(1-u)/4$, $dt=-du/4$; limits $t:0\to -\tfrac14$ bec
ome $u:1\to 2$:

$$f\!\Big(-\tfrac14\Big)=\int_1^2\frac{1}{u-1}\!\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{u}}-1\right
)du.$$

Therefore

$$\frac{S}{\sqrt{\pi}}=-f\!\Big(-\tfrac14\Big)=\int_1^2\frac{1}{u-1}\!\left(1-\f
rac{1}{\sqrt{u}}\right)du=\int_1^2\frac{\sqrt{u}-1}{(u-1)\sqrt{u}}\,du.$$

Using $u-1=(\sqrt{u}-1)(\sqrt{u}+1)$,

$$\frac{S}{\sqrt{\pi}}=\int_1^2\frac{du}{\sqrt{u}\,(\sqrt{u}+1)}.$$

### Step 5: Final integration

Let $v=\sqrt{u}$, $du/\sqrt{u}=2\,dv$; limits $u:1\to 2$ become $v:1\to\sqrt{2}$
:

$$\frac{S}{\sqrt{\pi}}=\int_1^{\sqrt{2}}\frac{2\,dv}{v+1}=2\Big[\ln(v+1)\Big]_1^
{\sqrt{2}}=2\bigl(\ln(1+\sqrt{2})-\ln 2\bigr).$$

### Result

$$\boxed{\displaystyle\frac{S}{\sqrt{\pi}}=2\ln\frac{1+\sqrt{2}}{2}}.$$
