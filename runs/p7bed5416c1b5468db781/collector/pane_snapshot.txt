**Step 2: Compute $\dim_H(A+A)$.**

**Key observation — no carries occur.** For $x = \sum a_k 10^{-k}$ and $y = \sum

$x+y$ at position $k$ is exactly $d_k = a_k + b_k \in \{0,1,\dots,8\}$, with no
carry propagation.

Conversely, every sequence $(d_k)$ with $d_k \in \{0,\dots,8\}$ is realizable: w

$$A + A = \Bigl\{\sum_{k\ge 1} d_k\,10^{-k} : d_k \in \{0,1,\dots,8\}\Bigr\}.$$

This is the self-similar set generated by $9$ maps $T_j(x) = \frac{x+j}{10}$, $j
