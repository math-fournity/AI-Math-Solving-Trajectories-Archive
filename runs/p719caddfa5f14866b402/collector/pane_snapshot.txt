## Proof

**Setup.** Place $B=(-a/2,0)$ and $C=(a/2,0)$ so that $BC=a$ and the midpoint of
 $BC$ is the origin. Let $A=(x,y)$ with $y>0$.

**Brocard angle identity.** The Brocard angle $\varphi$ of $\triangle ABC$ satis
fies the classical relation
$$\cot\varphi=\cot A+\cot B+\cot C.$$

Using $\cos A=\frac{b^{2}+c^{2}-a^{2}}{2bc}$, $\sin A=\frac{2K}{bc}$ (and cyclic
ally), where $K$ is the area, we get
$$\cot A=\frac{b^{2}+c^{2}-a^{2}}{4K},\qquad \cot B=\frac{a^{2}+c^{2}-b^{2}}{4K}
,\qquad \cot C=\frac{a^{2}+b^{2}-c^{2}}{4K}.$$

Adding:
$$\cot\varphi=\cot A+\cot B+\cot C=\frac{a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}}{4K}.\tag{1}$$

**Express in coordinates.** With our placement:
$$b^{2}=CA^{2}=\Bigl(x-\frac{a}{2}\Bigr)^{2}+y^{2},\qquad c^{2}=AB^{2}=\Bigl(x+\
frac{a}{2}\Bigr)^{2}+y^{2},\qquad K=\frac{1}{2}\,a\,y.$$

Hence
$$a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}=a^{2}+\Bigl(x-\frac{a}{2}\Bigr)^{2}+y^{2}+\Bigl(x+\frac{a}{2
}\Bigr)^{2}+y^{2}=\frac{3a^{2}}{2}+2x^{2}+2y^{2},$$
and $4K=2ay$. Substituting into (1):
$$\cot\varphi=\frac{\tfrac{3a^{2}}{2}+2x^{2}+2y^{2}}{2ay}=\frac{3a^{2}+4x^{2}+4y
^{2}}{4ay}.$$

**Fix $\varphi$ and derive the locus.** Set $k:=\cot\varphi$ (constant). Then
$$3a^{2}+4x^{2}+4y^{2}=4kay,$$
i.e.
$$x^{2}+y^{2}-kay+\frac{3a^{2}}{4}=0.$$

Completing the square in $y$:
$$x^{2}+\Bigl(y-\frac{ka}{2}\Bigr)^{2}=\frac{k^{2}a^{2}}{4}-\frac{3a^{2}}{4}=\fr
ac{a^{2}}{4}(k^{2}-3).$$

Therefore the locus of $A$ is the circle
$$\boxed{\;x^{2}+\Bigl(y-\tfrac{a}{2}\cot\varphi\Bigr)^{2}=\Bigl(\tfrac{a}{2}\sq
rt{\cot^{2}\varphi-3}\Bigr)^{2}\;}$$

centered at $\bigl(0,\,\tfrac{a}{2}\cot\varphi\bigr)$ — on the perpendicular bis
ector of $BC$ — with radius
$$R=\frac{a}{2}\sqrt{\cot^{2}\varphi-3}.$$

**Validity.** The Brocard angle satisfies $\cot\varphi\ge\sqrt{3}$ (equality iff
 the triangle is equilateral), so $R\ge 0$. For a non-equilateral triangle $\cot
\varphi>\sqrt{3}$ and $R>0$, giving a genuine circle; for the equilateral case $
R=0$ and $A$ is the single apex point. This is precisely the **Neuberg circle**.
