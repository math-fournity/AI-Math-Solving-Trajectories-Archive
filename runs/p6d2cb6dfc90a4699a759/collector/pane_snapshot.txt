# Solution: Heat Equation in a Cylinder

## Setup and Separation of Variables

We solve the heat equation in cylindrical coordinates on the domain $0 \le r < a
$, $0 < \theta < 2\pi$, $0 < z < H$, with homogeneous boundary conditions $u(r,\
theta,0,t)=u(r,\theta,H,t)=0$, $u(a,\theta,z,t)=0$, and regularity (boundedness)
 at $r=0$, subject to the initial condition $u(r,\theta,z,0)=f(r,\theta,z)$.

We seek a separated solution $u = T(t)\,R(r)\,\Theta(\theta)\,Z(z)$. Substitutin
g into the PDE and dividing by $TR\Theta Z$:

$$\frac{T'}{T} = \frac{1}{r}\frac{(rR')'}{R} + \frac{1}{r^2}\frac{\Theta''}{\The
ta} + \frac{Z''}{Z} = -\lambda.$$

## Separation of $Z$

With $Z''/Z = -\mu$ and boundary conditions $Z(0)=Z(H)=0$:

$$Z_n(z) = \sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi z}{H}\right), \qquad \mu_n = \left(\frac{n\pi}
{H}\right)^2, \qquad n=1,2,3,\dots$$

## Separation of $\Theta$

With $\Theta''/\Theta = -\nu$ and periodicity $\Theta(\theta+2\pi)=\Theta(\theta
)$:

$$\Theta_m(\theta) = \cos(m\theta) \text{ or } \sin(m\theta), \qquad \nu_m = m^2
, \qquad m=0,1,2,\dots$$

## Separation of $R$ (Bessel's Equation)

The remaining radial equation becomes:

$$\frac{1}{r}\frac{(rR')'}{R} - \frac{m^2}{r^2} = -\lambda + \mu_n \implies r^2
R'' + rR' + \bigl(\kappa^2 r^2 - m^2\bigr)R = 0,$$

where $\kappa^2 = \lambda - \mu_n > 0$. This is **Bessel's equation of order $m$
**, with general solution $R = c_1 J_m(\kappa r) + c_2 Y_m(\kappa r)$. Since $Y_
m$ is singular at $r=0$, regularity forces $c_2 = 0$. The boundary condition $R(
a)=0$ requires:

$$J_m(\kappa\, a) = 0 \implies \kappa = \frac{\alpha_{m,k}}{a},$$

where $\alpha_{m,k}$ is the $k$-th positive zero of $J_m$ ($k=1,2,3,\dots$). Thu
s:

$$R_{mk}(r) = J_m\!\left(\frac{\alpha_{m,k}\, r}{a}\right), \qquad \lambda_{mkn}
 = \left(\frac{n\pi}{H}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{\alpha_{m,k}}{a}\right)^2.$$

## Time Factor

$$T_{mkn}(t) = e^{-\lambda_{mkn}\, t}.$$

## General Solution

$$\boxed{u(r,\theta,z,t) = \sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\sum_{n=1}^{\in
fty} \Bigl[A_{mkn}\cos(m\theta) + B_{mkn}\sin(m\theta)\Bigr]\, J_m\!\left(\frac{
\alpha_{m,k}\,r}{a}\right)\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi z}{H}\right) e^{-\lambda_{mkn}
t}}$$

where

$$\lambda_{mkn} = \left(\frac{n\pi}{H}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{\alpha_{m,k}}{a}\r
ight)^2.$$

## Determination of Coefficients

The coefficients follow from the initial condition $u(r,\theta,z,0)=f(r,\theta,z
)$ and the orthogonality relations:

- $\displaystyle\int_0^{2\pi}\cos(m\theta)\cos(m'\theta)\,d\theta = \begin{cases
}2\pi & m=m'=0 \\ \pi\,\delta_{mm'} & m\ge 1\end{cases}$

- $\displaystyle\int_0^{2\pi}\sin(m\theta)\sin(m'\theta)\,d\theta = \pi\,\delta_
{mm'}, \quad m\ge 1$

- $\displaystyle\int_0^H \sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi z}{H}\right)\sin\!\left(\frac{n'
\pi z}{H}\right)dz = \frac{H}{2}\,\delta_{nn'}$

- $\displaystyle\int_0^a r\, J_m\!\left(\frac{\alpha_{m,k}\,r}{a}\right)J_m\!\le
ft(\frac{\alpha_{m,j}\,r}{a}\right)dr = \frac{a^2}{2}\bigl[J_{m+1}(\alpha_{m,k})
\bigr]^2\,\delta_{kj}$

**For $m \ge 1$:**

$$A_{mkn} = \frac{4}{\pi H\, a^2\,\bigl[J_{m+1}(\alpha_{m,k})\bigr]^2}\int_0^a\!
\!\int_0^{2\pi}\!\!\int_0^H f(r,\theta,z)\,\cos(m\theta)\,J_m\!\left(\frac{\alph
a_{m,k}\,r}{a}\right)\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi z}{H}\right) r\,dz\,d\theta\,dr$$

$$B_{mkn} = \frac{4}{\pi H\, a^2\,\bigl[J_{m+1}(\alpha_{m,k})\bigr]^2}\int_0^a\!
\!\int_0^{2\pi}\!\!\int_0^H f(r,\theta,z)\,\sin(m\theta)\,J_m\!\left(\frac{\alph
a_{m,k}\,r}{a}\right)\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi z}{H}\right) r\,dz\,d\theta\,dr$$

**For $m = 0$** (only the cosine term survives, since $\sin(0)=0$, and $\int_0^{
2\pi}d\theta = 2\pi$):

$$A_{0kn} = \frac{1}{\pi H\, a^2\,\bigl[J_1(\alpha_{0,k})\bigr]^2}\int_0^a\!\!\i
nt_0^{2\pi}\!\!\int_0^H f(r,\theta,z)\,J_0\!\left(\frac{\alpha_{0,k}\,r}{a}\righ
t)\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi z}{H}\right) r\,dz\,d\theta\,dr, \qquad B_{0kn}=0.$$

## Verification

- **PDE**: Each term $e^{-\lambda_{mkn}t}J_m(\alpha_{m,k}r/a)\cos(m\theta)\sin(n
\pi z/H)$ is an eigenfunction of the spatial Laplacian with eigenvalue $-\lambda
_{mkn}$, so the time factor $e^{-\lambda_{mkn}t}$ satisfies $T'=-\lambda_{mkn}T$
. ✓

- **Boundary at $z=0$ and $z=H$**: $\sin(0)=0$ and $\sin(n\pi)=0$. ✓

- **Boundary at $r=a$**: $J_m(\alpha_{m,k})=0$ by definition of $\alpha_{m,k}$.
✓

- **Regularity at $r=0$**: $J_m$ is bounded at the origin; $Y_m$ was excluded. ✓

- **Periodicity in $\theta$**: $\cos(m\theta)$ and $\sin(m\theta)$ are $2\pi$-pe
riodic. ✓

- **Initial condition**: The completeness of $\{J_m(\alpha_{m,k}r/a)\}_{k\ge 1}$
 (Fourier–Bessel series), $\{\cos(m\theta),\sin(m\theta)\}_{m\ge 0}$ (Fourier se
ries), and $\{\sin(n\pi z/H)\}_{n\ge 1}$ (sine series) on their respective domai
ns guarantees that any sufficiently regular $f$ can be expanded in this basis, w
ith coefficients as given above. ✓
