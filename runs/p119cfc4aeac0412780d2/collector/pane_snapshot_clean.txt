Let $N = 2020!$ and $M = 2019!$, so $N = 2020 \cdot M$. The $\omega_k$ are the $
N$-th roots of unity. We compute

$$S = (2^M - 1)\sum_{k=0}^{N-1} \frac{1}{\omega_k^{2020} + 2}.$$

**Step 1: Reduce the sum.** As $k$ ranges over $\{0,\ldots,N-1\}$, we have $\ome
ga_k^{2020} = e^{2\pi i \cdot 2020k/N} = e^{2\pi i k/M}$, so $\omega_k^{2020}$ r
anges over all $M$-th roots of unity $\zeta_0,\ldots,\zeta_{M-1}$, each attained
 exactly $2020$ times. Hence

$$\sum_{k=0}^{N-1} \frac{1}{\omega_k^{2020}+2} = 2020\sum_{j=0}^{M-1}\frac{1}{\z
eta_j+2}.$$

**Step 2: Evaluate via logarithmic derivative.** Let $P(x) = x^M - 1 = \prod_{j=
0}^{M-1}(x-\zeta_j)$. Then

$$\sum_{j=0}^{M-1}\frac{1}{x-\zeta_j} = \frac{P'(x)}{P(x)} = \frac{Mx^{M-1}}{x^M
-1}.$$

Setting $x = -2$:

$$\sum_{j=0}^{M-1}\frac{1}{\zeta_j+2} = -\sum_{j=0}^{M-1}\frac{1}{-2-\zeta_j} =
-\frac{M(-2)^{M-1}}{(-2)^M-1}.$$

Since $M = 2019!$ is even: $(-2)^M = 2^M$ and $(-2)^{M-1} = -2^{M-1}$, giving

$$\sum_{j=0}^{M-1}\frac{1}{\zeta_j+2} = \frac{M\cdot 2^{M-1}}{2^M-1}.$$

**Step 3: Combine.**

$$S = (2^M-1)\cdot 2020 \cdot \frac{M\cdot 2^{M-1}}{2^M-1} = 2020\cdot M\cdot 2^
{M-1} = 2020!\cdot 2^{2019!-1}.$$

**Step 4: Compute $v_2(S)$.**

$$n = v_2(S) = v_2(2020!) + 2019! - 1.$$

By Legendre's formula:

$$v_2(2020!) = \left\lfloor\tfrac{2020}{2}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\tfrac{2020}
{4}\right\rfloor+\cdots = 1010+505+252+126+63+31+15+7+3+1 = 2013.$$

Therefore

$$n = 2013 + 2019! - 1 = 2019! + 2012.$$

**Step 5: Write $n = a! + b$ with $a$ maximal.** Since $2020! = 2020\cdot 2019!
\gg 2019!+2012$, we cannot take $a=2020$. Taking $a = 2019$ gives $b = 2012 \geq
 1$. ✓

So $a = 2019$, $b = 2012$, and

$$a + b = 4031 \equiv 31 \pmod{1000}.$$
