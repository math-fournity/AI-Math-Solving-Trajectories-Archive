$$abc \le \left(\frac{s}{3}\right)^3,$$

$$s\bigl[81(s+d)^5+16(s+2d)^5\bigr]\;\ge\;k\cdot\frac{s^3}{27}\,d^3,$$

$$27\bigl[81(s+d)^5+16(s+2d)^5\bigr]\;\ge\;k\,s^2 d^3.$$

**Critical point.** Differentiating,
$$G'(t)=\frac{N(t)}{t^4},\qquad N(t)=81(1+t)^4(2t-3)+16(1+2t)^4(4t-3).$$
