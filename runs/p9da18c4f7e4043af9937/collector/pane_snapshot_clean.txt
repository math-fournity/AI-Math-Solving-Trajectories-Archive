## Solution

**Setup.** Define $A_n = \{(x_0,\ldots,x_n)\in[0,1]^{n+1} : x_{i-1}+x_i<1,\; 1\l
e i\le n\}$ and let $V_n = \operatorname{Vol}_{n+1}(A_n)$.

**Step 1: Integral recurrence.** Condition on $x_0 = a \in [0,1]$. Given $x_0=a$
, the constraint $x_0+x_1<1$ becomes $x_1 < 1-a$, and the remaining constraints
$x_i+x_{i+1}<1$ for $i=1,\ldots,n-1$ involve $x_1,\ldots,x_n$. Define

$$I_n(a) = \operatorname{Vol}_n\{(y_1,\ldots,y_n)\in[0,1]^n : y_1 < 1-a,\; y_i+y
_{i+1}<1,\; i=1,\ldots,n-1\},$$

with $I_0(a)=1$ (the volume of the empty product). Then $V_n = \int_0^1 I_n(a)\,
da$, and

$$I_n(a) = \int_0^{1-a} I_{n-1}(t)\,dt \qquad (n\ge 1),$$

since we integrate out $y_1 \in [0,\,1-a)$ and the remaining variables satisfy t
he same type of constraints with $y_1=t$ playing the role of the "preceding" var
iable.

**Verification on small cases:** $I_1(a)=1-a$, $V_1=\int_0^1(1-a)\,da=1/2$. $I_2
(a)=\int_0^{1-a}(1-t)\,dt=(1-a^2)/2$, $V_2=1/3$. $V_3=5/24$, $V_4=2/15$. ✓

**Step 2: Generating function and ODE.** Define the formal power series $\Phi(x,
a)=\sum_{n\ge 0} I_n(a)\,x^n$. The recurrence gives

$$\Phi(x,a)-1 = \sum_{n\ge 1} x^n \int_0^{1-a} I_{n-1}(t)\,dt = x\int_0^{1-a} \P
hi(x,t)\,dt. \tag{$\star$}$$

Differentiate $(\star)$ with respect to $a$:

$$\frac{\partial \Phi}{\partial a}(x,a) = -x\,\Phi(x,1-a).$$

Differentiate again:

$$\frac{\partial^2 \Phi}{\partial a^2}(x,a) = x\,\frac{\partial\Phi}{\partial a}
(x,1-a) = x\cdot(-x\,\Phi(x,a)) = -x^2\,\Phi(x,a).$$

**Step 3: Boundary conditions and solution.** From $(\star)$ at $a=1$: $\Phi(x,1
)-1=0$, so $\Phi(x,1)=1$. From the first derivative at $a=0$: $\partial_a\Phi(x,
0)=-x\,\Phi(x,1)=-x$.

The ODE $\partial_a^2\Phi=-x^2\Phi$ has general solution $\Phi(x,a)=A(x)\cos(xa)
+B(x)\sin(xa)$. Applying the boundary conditions:
- $\partial_a\Phi(x,0)=B(x)\,x=-x \;\Longrightarrow\; B(x)=-1$.
- $\Phi(x,1)=A(x)\cos x-\sin x=1 \;\Longrightarrow\; A(x)=\frac{1+\sin x}{\cos x
}=\sec x+\tan x$.

Therefore

$$\Phi(x,a) = (\sec x+\tan x)\cos(xa)-\sin(xa).$$

**Step 4: Extract $V_n$.** Since $V_n=\int_0^1 I_n(a)\,da$,

$$\sum_{n\ge 0} V_n\,x^n = \int_0^1 \Phi(x,a)\,da = (\sec x+\tan x)\frac{\sin x}
{x}-\frac{1-\cos x}{x}.$$

Simplifying the numerator:
$$(\sec x+\tan x)\sin x+\cos x-1 = \frac{\sin x(1+\sin x)}{\cos x}+\cos x-1=\fra
c{\sin x+\sin^2 x+\cos^2 x-\cos x}{\cos x}=\frac{1+\sin x-\cos x}{\cos x}.$$

Hence

$$\sum_{n\ge 0} V_n\,x^n = \frac{\sec x+\tan x-1}{x}.$$

**Step 5: Identify with Euler zigzag numbers.** The Euler zigzag (up/down) numbe
rs $E_k$ are defined by the exponential generating function

$$\sum_{k\ge 0} E_k\,\frac{x^k}{k!} = \sec x+\tan x.$$

Therefore

$$\frac{\sec x+\tan x-1}{x}=\frac{1}{x}\sum_{k\ge 1}E_k\frac{x^k}{k!}=\sum_{n\ge
 0}\frac{E_{n+1}}{(n+1)!}\,x^n.$$

Comparing coefficients:

$$\boxed{V_n = \frac{E_{n+1}}{(n+1)!}},$$

where $E_{n+1}$ is the $(n+1)$-th Euler zigzag number (the number of alternating
 permutations of $\{1,2,\ldots,n+1\}$).

**Explicit series form.** Using the Mittag-Leffler expansion of $(1+\sin x)/\cos

$$V_n = \frac{2^{n+3}}{\pi^{n+2}}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\left[\frac{1}{(4k+1)^{n+2}}
-\frac{(-1)^{n+1}}{(4k+3)^{n+2}}\right],$$

which simplifies to:
- **$n$ even:** $\displaystyle V_n=\frac{2^{n+3}-2}{\pi^{n+2}}\,\zeta(n+2)$,
- **$n$ odd:** $\displaystyle V_n=\frac{2^{n+3}}{\pi^{n+2}}\,\beta(n+2)$, where
$\beta(s)=\sum_{k=0}^\infty\frac{(-1)^k}{(2k+1)^s}$ is the Dirichlet beta functi
on.
