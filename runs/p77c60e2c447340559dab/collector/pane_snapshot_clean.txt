## Solution

Let $g(x) = f(e^x)$. We establish a recurrence for the coefficients $a_{kn}$.

**Differentiation rule.** Each term $e^{kx}f^{(k)}(e^x)$ differentiates as
$$\frac{d}{dx}\bigl[e^{kx}f^{(k)}(e^x)\bigr] = k\,e^{kx}f^{(k)}(e^x) + e^{(k+1)x
}f^{(k+1)}(e^x),$$
since differentiating $e^{kx}$ gives $k\,e^{kx}$ and differentiating $f^{(k)}(e^
x)$ gives $e^x f^{(k+1)}(e^x)$.

Applying this to $\frac{d^n}{dx^n}f(e^x)=\sum_k a_{kn}\,e^{kx}f^{(k)}(e^x)$ yiel
ds the recurrence
$$a_{k,n+1} = k\,a_{k,n} + a_{k-1,n},\qquad a_{0,0}=1,\; a_{k,0}=0\;(k\ge 1),\;
a_{-1,n}=0.$$

**Identification with Stirling numbers of the second kind.** The Stirling number
s of the second kind $S(n,k)$ satisfy exactly this recurrence:
$$S(n+1,k)=k\,S(n,k)+S(n,k-1),\qquad S(0,0)=1,\; S(n,0)=0\;(n\ge1).$$
Hence $a_{kn}=S(n,k)$.

**Verification.** Base case $n=0$: $g(x)=f(e^x)$, so $a_{0,0}=1=S(0,0)$. For $n=
1$: $g'(x)=e^x f'(e^x)$, giving $(a_{0,1},a_{1,1})=(0,1)=(S(1,0),S(1,1))$. For $
n=2$: $g''(x)=e^x f'(e^x)+e^{2x}f''(e^x)$, giving $(1,1)=(S(2,1),S(2,2))$. For $
n=3$: $g'''(x)=e^x f'(e^x)+3e^{2x}f''(e^x)+e^{3x}f'''(e^x)$, giving $(1,3,1)=(S(
3,1),S(3,2),S(3,3))$. All agree.

**The polynomials $P_n$.** Thus
$$P_n(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}S(n,k)\,x^k,$$
which is the **Touchard (Bell) polynomial** $B_n(x)$.

**Exponential generating function.** Using the standard identity $\sum_{n\ge 0}S
(n,k)\frac{t^n}{n!}=\frac{(e^t-1)^k}{k!}$ (proved by noting $S(n,k)=\frac{1}{k!}
\sum_{j=0}^{k}(-1)^{k-j}\binom{k}{j}j^n$ and summing the geometric-type series i
n $n$), we compute:
$$F(t,x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{P_n(x)}{n!}t^n=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{
n}S(n,k)\,x^k\frac{t^n}{n!}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}x^k\sum_{n=k}^{\infty}S(n,k)\frac
{t^n}{n!}.$$
Substituting the EGF of $S(n,k)$:
$$F(t,x)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}x^k\cdot\frac{(e^t-1)^k}{k!}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\fra
c{\bigl(x(e^t-1)\bigr)^k}{k!}=\exp\!\bigl(x(e^t-1)\bigr).$$

**Closed form.**

$$\boxed{\,F(t,x)=\exp\!\bigl(x\,(e^{t}-1)\bigr)\,}$$
