## Proof

We seek the Green's function $g(x,t\,|\,\xi,\tau)$ satisfying
$$\frac{\partial g}{\partial t} - a^2 \frac{\partial^2 g}{\partial x^2} = \delta
(x-\xi)\,\delta(t-\tau),\qquad 0<x,\xi<L,\; t,\tau>0,$$
with homogeneous Dirichlet boundary conditions $g(0,t\,|\,\xi,\tau)=g(L,t\,|\,\x
i,\tau)=0$ and the causal initial condition $g(x,0\,|\,\xi,\tau)=0$.

### Step 1. Spatial eigenfunction basis

The operator $-\dfrac{d^2}{dx^2}$ on $(0,L)$ with Dirichlet conditions has the c
omplete orthonormal system
$$\phi_n(x)=\sqrt{\tfrac{2}{L}}\sin\!\Big(\tfrac{n\pi x}{L}\Big),\qquad \lambda_
n=\Big(\tfrac{n\pi}{L}\Big)^2,\qquad n=1,2,\dots$$
satisfying $-\phi_n''=\lambda_n\phi_n$ and $\phi_n(0)=\phi_n(L)=0$.

Expand the Green's function and the spatial delta in this basis:
$$g(x,t\,|\,\xi,\tau)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} G_n(t,\tau;\xi)\,\phi_n(x),\qquad \del
ta(x-\xi)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\phi_n(\xi)\,\phi_n(x).$$

### Step 2. Temporal ODE for each mode

Substituting into the PDE and using $\phi_n''=-\lambda_n\phi_n$:
$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\Big[\dot G_n + a^2\lambda_n G_n\Big]\phi_n(x)=\delta(t-\ta
u)\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\phi_n(\xi)\phi_n(x).$$
By orthogonality, each mode satisfies
$$\dot G_n(t,\tau;\xi)+a^2\lambda_n\,G_n(t,\tau;\xi)=\phi_n(\xi)\,\delta(t-\tau)
,\qquad G_n(0,\tau;\xi)=0.$$

### Step 3. Solving the temporal ODE (causal solution)

For $t<\tau$ the right-hand side vanishes and the zero initial condition gives $
G_n\equiv 0$. Integrating across $t=\tau$,
$$G_n(\tau^+,\tau;\xi)-G_n(\tau^-,\tau;\xi)=\phi_n(\xi)\;\Longrightarrow\;G_n(\t
au^+,\tau;\xi)=\phi_n(\xi),$$
since $G_n(\tau^-)=0$. For $t>\tau$ the ODE is homogeneous $\dot G_n+a^2\lambda_
n G_n=0$, so
$$G_n(t,\tau;\xi)=\phi_n(\xi)\,e^{-a^2\lambda_n(t-\tau)},\qquad t>\tau.$$
Writing the causal answer compactly with the Heaviside function $H$:
$$G_n(t,\tau;\xi)=\phi_n(\xi)\,e^{-a^2\lambda_n(t-\tau)}\,H(t-\tau).$$

### Step 4. Green's function — eigenfunction (series) form

Reassembling,
$$\boxed{\;g(x,t\,|\,\xi,\tau)=\frac{2}{L}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\sin\!\Big(\tfrac{n
\pi\xi}{L}\Big)\sin\!\Big(\tfrac{n\pi x}{L}\Big)\,\exp\!\Big[-a^2\Big(\tfrac{n\p
i}{L}\Big)^2(t-\tau)\Big]\,H(t-\tau)\;}$$

### Step 5. Verification

* **PDE.** Term-by-term: $\dot G_n+a^2\lambda_n G_n=\phi_n(\xi)\delta(t-\tau)$,
so summing reproduces $\delta(x-\xi)\delta(t-\tau)$. The series and its derivati
ves converge uniformly for $t>\tau>0$ (exponential decay in $n$), justifying ter
mwise differentiation.
* **Boundary conditions.** Each $\sin(n\pi x/L)$ vanishes at $x=0$ and $x=L$, he
nce $g(0,t\,|\,\xi,\tau)=g(L,t\,|\,\xi,\tau)=0$.
* **Initial/causal condition.** For $t<\tau$, $H(t-\tau)=0$ gives $g\equiv 0$; i
n particular $g(x,0\,|\,\xi,\tau)=0$ since $\tau>0$.
* **Jump at $t=\tau$.** Integrating the PDE over an infinitesimal time interval
around $\tau$ gives $\lim_{t\downarrow\tau}g(x,t\,|\,\xi,\tau)=\delta(x-\xi)$, w
hich is exactly the completeness relation $\sum_n\phi_n(\xi)\phi_n(x)=\delta(x-\
xi)$.
* **Reciprocity.** The expression is symmetric in $x\leftrightarrow\xi$, consist
ent with the self-adjointness of the spatial operator.

### Step 6. Equivalent closed (image-sum) form

Using the free-space heat kernel $K(x,t)=\dfrac{H(t)}{\sqrt{4\pi a^2 t}}\exp\!\B
ig[-\dfrac{x^2}{4a^2 t}\Big]$ and the method of images to antisymmetrize about $
x=0$ and $x=L$ (period $2L$), one obtains the equivalent representation
$$g(x,t\,|\,\xi,\tau)=\frac{H(t-\tau)}{\sqrt{4\pi a^2(t-\tau)}}\sum_{m=-\infty}^
{\infty}\Bigg[\exp\!\Bigg(-\frac{(x-\xi-2mL)^2}{4a^2(t-\tau)}\Bigg)-\exp\!\Bigg(
-\frac{(x+\xi-2mL)^2}{4a^2(t-\tau)}\Bigg)\Bigg].$$
The two forms are equal by the Poisson summation formula (the sine series is the
 Fourier expansion of the image sum). The image form makes the short-time behavi
or and the boundary cancellation manifest; the eigenfunction form makes the long
-time decay and modal structure manifest.

### Final Answer

$$\boxed{\,g(x,t\,|\,\xi,\tau)=\frac{2}{L}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\sin\!\Big(\tfrac{n
\pi\xi}{L}\Big)\sin\!\Big(\tfrac{n\pi x}{L}\Big)\,e^{-a^2(n\pi/L)^2(t-\tau)}\,H(
t-\tau)\,}$$

equivalently,

$$g(x,t\,|\,\xi,\tau)=\frac{H(t-\tau)}{\sqrt{4\pi a^2(t-\tau)}}\sum_{m=-\infty}^
{\infty}\Bigg[e^{-(x-\xi-2mL)^2/(4a^2(t-\tau))}-e^{-(x+\xi-2mL)^2/(4a^2(t-\tau))
}\Bigg].$$
