## Proof

We show that no such field exists, by considering all possible characteristics.

### Case 1: $\operatorname{char}(F) = 0$

The additive group $(F, +)$ is **torsion-free**: if $nx = 0$ for some positive i
nteger $n$ and $x \neq 0$, then $n \cdot 1 = (nx) \cdot x^{-1} = 0$, contradicti
ng $\operatorname{char}(F) = 0$.

However, $-1 \neq 1$ (since $-1 = 1$ would force $\operatorname{char}(F) = 2$),
so $-1$ is an element of **order 2** in $F^*$. Thus $F^*$ has 2-torsion while $(
F, +)$ is torsion-free. They cannot be isomorphic.

### Case 2: $\operatorname{char}(F) = p > 0$

The additive group $(F, +)$ is an **elementary abelian $p$-group**: every elemen
t $x$ satisfies $px = 0$, and every nonzero element has order exactly $p$. In pa
rticular, since $F$ has at least two elements, $(F, +)$ contains nontrivial $p$-
torsion.

Now consider $F^*$. We claim $F^*$ has **no nontrivial $p$-torsion**. Suppose $x
 \in F^*$ satisfies $x^p = 1$. In characteristic $p$, by the freshman's dream,
$$t^p - 1 = (t - 1)^p.$$
So $x^p - 1 = (x-1)^p = 0$. Since $F$ is a field (hence an integral domain), $(x
-1)^p = 0$ implies $x - 1 = 0$, i.e., $x = 1$.

Therefore the only element of $F^*$ with order dividing $p$ is the identity. But
 $(F, +)$ has nontrivial elements of order $p$. An isomorphism would preserve el
ement orders, so $(F, +) \not\cong F^*$.

(Note: this case also covers all finite fields $\mathbb{F}_{p^n}$, where additio
nally $|F| = p^n \neq p^n - 1 = |F^*|$, giving an even more immediate contradict
ion.)

### Conclusion

In every characteristic — whether $0$ or a prime $p$ — the additive group and th
e multiplicative group of a field have incompatible torsion structures:

| Characteristic | $(F,+)$ torsion | $F^*$ torsion |
|---|---|---|
| $0$ | torsion-free | has element of order 2 |
| $p > 0$ | every nonzero element has order $p$ | no nontrivial $p$-torsion |

In both cases they cannot be isomorphic. Therefore, **there does not exist a fie
ld whose multiplicative group is isomorphic to its additive group.** $\blacksqua
