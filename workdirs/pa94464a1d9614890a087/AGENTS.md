# Problem

In a remote industrial complex, there are 20 specialized chemical storage tanks, indexed from 1 to 20. Each tank $i$ contains a specific volume of reagent, denoted by a positive integer $a_i$ liters.

The facility operates under a strict safety protocol regarding "cluster stability." A cluster is defined as any non-empty collection of these 20 tanks. For any such cluster $S$ that is formed, the facility managers calculate the Greatest Common Divisor (GCD) of the volumes of all reagents currently in that cluster. The safety protocol dictates that for every possible cluster $S$, there must be at least one tank $s$ within that cluster such that its specific volume $a_s$ is less than or equal to the calculated GCD of the entire cluster.

Under the constraint of this safety protocol, what is the maximum possible value for the total product of all twenty volumes, $a_1 \times a_2 \times \dots \times a_{20}$?
