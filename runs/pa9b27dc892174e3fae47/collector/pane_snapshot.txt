## Solution

**Coordinate setup.** Place the city as a 4 km × 4 km square:
- A (Northwest) = (0, 4), B (Northeast) = (4, 4), C (Southeast) = (4, 0), D (Sou
thwest) = (0, 0).

The diagonal AC runs from (0,4) to (4,0). Parametrize M on AC as **M = (t, 4−t)*
* for t ∈ [0, 4].

The hub E is the midpoint of the northern border AB: **E = (2, 4)**.

**Computing the distances.**

$$EM = \sqrt{(t-2)^2 + (4-t-4)^2} = \sqrt{(t-2)^2 + t^2} = \sqrt{2t^2 - 4t + 4}$
$

$$MB = \sqrt{(t-4)^2 + (4-t-4)^2} = \sqrt{(t-4)^2 + t^2} = \sqrt{2t^2 - 8t + 16}
$$

Define $f(t) = EM + MB = \sqrt{2t^2-4t+4} + \sqrt{2t^2-8t+16}$.

**Finding the minimum.** Setting $f'(t)=0$:

$$\frac{2t-2}{\sqrt{2t^2-4t+4}} + \frac{2t-4}{\sqrt{2t^2-8t+16}} = 0$$

Squaring and simplifying yields $2t(3t-4)=0$, giving the interior critical point
 $t = 4/3$.

$$f(4/3) = \sqrt{20/9} + \sqrt{80/9} = \frac{2\sqrt{5}}{3} + \frac{4\sqrt{5}}{3}
 = 2\sqrt{5} \approx 4.472$$

**Endpoint values.**
- $f(0) = \sqrt{4} + \sqrt{16} = 2 + 4 = 6$.
- $f(4) = \sqrt{20} + \sqrt{16} = 2\sqrt{5} + 4 \approx 8.472$.

**Behavior of f.** Since $f'(0) = -2 < 0$ and $f'(2) = 1 > 0$, the function decr
eases from $f(0)=6$ to the minimum $2\sqrt{5}\approx 4.472$ at $t=4/3$, then inc
reases to $f(4)=4+2\sqrt{5}\approx 8.472$.

**Range of f:** $[2\sqrt{5},\; 4+2\sqrt{5}] \approx [4.472,\; 8.472]$.

The integers in this range are **5, 6, 7, 8** (since $4 < 2\sqrt{5} < 5$ and $8
< 4+2\sqrt{5} < 9$).

**Counting locations for each integer value:**

| Integer k | Decreasing part (t: 0 → 4/3, f: 6 → 2√5) | Increasing part (t: 4/3
 → 4, f: 2√5 → 4+2√5) | Locations |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | ✓ (once, since 5 ∈ [2√5, 6]) | ✓ (once, since 5 ∈ [2√5, 4+2√5]) | 2 |

| 8 | ✗ (8 > 6) | ✓ (once, since 8 < 4+2√5) | 1 |

**Total distinct locations:** 2 + 2 + 1 + 1 = **6**.

The answer is **(E) 6**.
