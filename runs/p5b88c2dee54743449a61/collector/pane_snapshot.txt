Looking at this problem, I need to determine the number of valid deployments of
5 drones (3 Standard: Alpha, Delta, Lima; 2 Specialized: November, Charlie) to 5
 stations along a one-way rail, where the carrier never holds exactly 1 Standard
 + 1 Specialized simultaneously.

## Proof

**Setup.** The carrier begins at the docking platform holding all 5 drones, then
 visits Stations 1 through 5 in order. With 5 drones and 5 stations, each drone
is deployed at exactly one station (a bijection). At each station, exactly one d
rone leaves the carrier.

**Identifying the critical moment.** The carrier's drone count over time is:

| Time | Drones remaining | Count |
|------|-----------------|-------|
| Start (before Station 1) | All 5 | 5 |
| After Station 1 | 4 | 4 |
| After Station 2 | 3 | 3 |
| After Station 3 | 2 | **2** |
| After Station 4 | 1 | 1 |
| After Station 5 | 0 | 0 |

The forbidden configuration is *exactly* 1 Standard + 1 Specialized = exactly 2
drones. The carrier holds exactly 2 drones only **after Station 3** (the drones
assigned to Stations 4 and 5). At all other times, the carrier holds ≠ 2 drones,
 so the constraint is automatically satisfied.

**Constraint.** The two drones assigned to Stations 4 and 5 must **not** be one
Standard and one Specialized. They must be **both Standard** or **both Specializ
ed**.

**Case 1: Both Specialized drones (November, Charlie) are at Stations 4 and 5.**
- Assign the 2 Specialized drones to Stations 4, 5: $2! = 2$ ways.
- Assign the 3 Standard drones to Stations 1, 2, 3: $3! = 6$ ways.
- Subtotal: $2 \times 6 = 12$.

**Case 2: Two of the three Standard drones are at Stations 4 and 5.**
- Choose 2 of {Alpha, Delta, Lima} for Stations 4, 5: $\binom{3}{2} = 3$ ways.
- Assign them to Stations 4, 5: $2! = 2$ ways.
- Assign the remaining 3 drones (1 Standard + 2 Specialized) to Stations 1, 2, 3
: $3! = 6$ ways.
- Subtotal: $3 \times 2 \times 6 = 36$.

**Total:** $12 + 36 = \boxed{48}$.

### PROOF COMPLETE
