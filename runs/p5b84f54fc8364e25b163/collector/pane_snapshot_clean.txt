## Proof

We have $f(n) = \dfrac{n^2+n}{2} = \dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$, the $n$-th triangular num
ber.

**Step 1: Determine the range of $n$.**

We need $f(n) \le 1000$, i.e., $n(n+1) \le 2000$.
Since $44 \times 45 = 1980 \le 2000$ and $45 \times 46 = 2070 > 2000$, we have $
1 \le n \le 44$.
