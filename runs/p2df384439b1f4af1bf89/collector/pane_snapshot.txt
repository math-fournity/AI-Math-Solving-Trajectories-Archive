## 1. Strong form (restated)

Find $u$ such that

$$
\begin{cases}
-u'' + \dfrac{k}{T}\,u = \dfrac{w}{T} & \text{in } (0,1),\\[4pt]
u(0)=0,\quad u(1)=0.
\end{cases}
$$

Denote $\mu := k/T$ (a non-negative constant, the reaction coefficient) and $f :
= w/T$ (the load). The problem becomes

$$
-u'' + \mu\, u = f \quad\text{in }(0,1),\qquad u(0)=u(1)=0.
$$

---

## 2. Functional setting

The Dirichlet boundary data are homogeneous, so the natural space is

$$
V := H^1_0(0,1) = \bigl\{ v\in H^1(0,1) : v(0)=v(1)=0 \bigr\},
$$

equipped with the norm $\|v\|_{H^1_0}^2 = \int_0^1 |v'|^2\,dx$.

---

## 3. Weak formulation

Multiply the PDE by a test function $v\in V$ and integrate over $(0,1)$:

$$
\int_0^1 \bigl(-u'' + \mu\, u\bigr) v\,dx = \int_0^1 f\, v\,dx.
$$

Integrate the second-derivative term by parts:

$$
-\int_0^1 u'' v\,dx
= \bigl[-u' v\bigr]_0^1 + \int_0^1 u' v'\,dx
= \int_0^1 u' v'\,dx,
$$

where the boundary term vanishes because $v(0)=v(1)=0$. We obtain

$$
\int_0^1 u' v'\,dx + \mu\int_0^1 u\, v\,dx = \int_0^1 f\, v\,dx.
$$

Define the bilinear form $a:V\times V\to\mathbb{R}$ and the linear functional $L
:V\to\mathbb{R}$ by

$$
a(u,v) := \int_0^1 \bigl(u' v' + \mu\, u\, v\bigr)\,dx,
\qquad
L(v) := \int_0^1 f\, v\,dx.
$$

**Weak formulation.** Find $u\in V$ such that

$$
\boxed{\;a(u,v) = L(v) \quad\text{for all } v\in V.\;}
$$

**Well-posedness.** $a$ is continuous on $V\times V$ (bounded by $1+\mu$ via Cau
chy–Schwarz and the Poincaré inequality $\|v\|_{L^2}\le C_P\|v'\|_{L^2}$) and co
ercive:

$$
a(v,v) = \|v'\|_{L^2}^2 + \mu\|v\|_{L^2}^2 \ge \min(1,\,\mu)\,\|v\|_{H^1_0}^2 \g
e \min(1,\,C_P^{-2}\mu)\,\|v\|_{H^1}^2,
$$

so by the Lax–Milgram theorem there exists a unique $u\in V$ solving the weak pr
oblem.

## 4. Galerkin–finite element formulation

### 4.1 Galerkin approximation

Let $\{V_h\}_{h>0}$ be a family of finite-dimensional subspaces of $V$ with $\di
m V_h = N_h$ and $V_h\to V$ as $h\to 0$. The **Galerkin problem** is:

Find $u_h\in V_h$ such that

$$
a(u_h, v_h) = L(v_h) \quad\text{for all } v_h\in V_h.
$$

### 4.2 Finite element space (P1 Lagrange)

Introduce a mesh of $(0,1)$ with nodes

$$
0 = x_0 < x_1 < \dots < x_{N} = 1,\qquad h_i = x_i - x_{i-1},\quad h = \max_i h_
i.
$$

On each element $K_i = [x_{i-1}, x_i]$ take $\mathbb{P}_1$ (affine) functions. E
nforcing continuity at the internal nodes and zero at the endpoints gives the st
andard hat-function basis $\{\varphi_i\}_{i=1}^{N-1}$ of $V_h\subset H^1_0(0,1)$
:

$$
\varphi_i(x_j) = \delta_{ij},\qquad i,j = 1,\dots,N-1.
$$

Write

$$
u_h(x) = \sum_{j=1}^{N-1} u_j\,\varphi_j(x),\qquad u_j = u_h(x_j).
$$

### 4.3 Linear system

Taking $v_h = \varphi_i$ for $i=1,\dots,N-1$ yields the $(N-1)\times(N-1)$ linea
r system

$$
\boxed{\;\sum_{j=1}^{N-1} A_{ij}\, u_j = b_i,\quad i=1,\dots,N-1,\;}
$$

with

$$
A_{ij} = a(\varphi_j, \varphi_i) = \underbrace{\int_0^1 \varphi_j'\,\varphi_i'\,
dx}_{K_{ij}\text{ (stiffness)}} \;+\; \mu\,\underbrace{\int_0^1 \varphi_j\,\varp
hi_i\,dx}_{M_{ij}\text{ (mass)}},
$$

$$
b_i = L(\varphi_i) = \int_0^1 f\,\varphi_i\,dx = \frac{w}{T}\int_0^1 \varphi_i\,
dx.
$$

In matrix form:

$$
\boxed{\;\bigl(K + \mu\, M\bigr)\,\mathbf{u} = \mathbf{b},\qquad \mu = \frac{k}{
T},\quad \mathbf{b} = \frac{w}{T}\,\mathbf{q},\quad q_i = \int_0^1 \varphi_i\,dx
.\;}
$$

### 4.4 Element-level assembly (uniform mesh)

For a uniform mesh with $h = 1/N$, the local stiffness and mass matrices on a ge
neric element of length $h$ are

$$
K^{(e)} = \frac{1}{h}\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ -1 & 1 \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
M^{(e)} = \frac{h}{6}\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix},
$$

so the local contribution is

$$
A^{(e)} = K^{(e)} + \mu\, M^{(e)} = \frac{1}{h}\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ -1 & 1
\end{pmatrix} + \frac{\mu\, h}{6}\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}.
$$

Assembling over all elements and applying the homogeneous Dirichlet conditions (
rows/columns of the endpoints removed) gives the tridiagonal global matrix

$$
A = \operatorname{tridiag}\!\left(-\frac{1}{h}+\frac{\mu h}{6},\;\; \frac{2}{h}+
\frac{\mu h}{3},\;\; -\frac{1}{h}+\frac{\mu h}{6}\right),
$$

and, for a constant load $f = w/T$, the load vector

$$
b_i = f\int_{x_{i-1}}^{x_{i+1}} \varphi_i\,dx = f\,h,\qquad i=1,\dots,N-1.
$$

### 4.5 Convergence (Céa's lemma)

Since $a$ is continuous and coercive and $V_h\subset V$, Céa's lemma gives

$$
\|u - u_h\|_{H^1_0} \le \frac{M}{\alpha}\,\inf_{v_h\in V_h}\|u - v_h\|_{H^1_0},
$$

and with $\mathbb{P}_1$ elements and $u\in H^2(0,1)$ (guaranteed here because $f
\in L^2$ and $\mu\ge 0$),

$$
\|u - u_h\|_{H^1_0} \le C\,h\,|u|_{H^2(0,1)},\qquad
\|u - u_h\|_{L^2(0,1)} \le C\,h^2\,|u|_{H^2(0,1)}.
$$

## Summary

- **Weak formulation:** find $u\in H^1_0(0,1)$ such that $\displaystyle\int_0^1\
!\bigl(u'v' + \tfrac{k}{T}uv\bigr)dx = \tfrac{w}{T}\!\int_0^1\! v\,dx$ for all $
v\in H^1_0(0,1)$.
- **Galerkin–FE formulation:** choose $V_h\subset H^1_0(0,1)$ (here $\mathbb{P}_
1$ hat functions on a mesh of $(0,1)$), expand $u_h=\sum_j u_j\varphi_j$, and so
lve $(K + \tfrac{k}{T}M)\,\mathbf{u} = \tfrac{w}{T}\,\mathbf{q}$, where $K$ is t
he stiffness matrix, $M$ the mass matrix, and $q_i=\int_0^1\varphi_i\,dx$.
