## Proof

x$ over its side-neighbors equals $0$ in $\mathbb{F}_2$.

Let $r_i=(x_{i1},\dots,x_{i,2n})^T\in\mathbb{F}_2^{2n}$ denote row $i$, and let
$B=B_{2n}$ be the $2n\times 2n$ path-graph adjacency matrix (tridiagonal with 1'
s on the super- and sub-diagonals). Setting $r_0=r_{2n+1}=0$ (boundary), the con
dition at every cell in row $i$ becomes the vector equation:
$$r_{i-1}+r_{i+1}+Br_i=0,\qquad i=1,\dots,2n.$$

**Construction from the first row.** Given any $r_1\in\mathbb{F}_2^{2n}$, set $r
_0=0$ and define recursively:
$$r_{i+1}=Br_i+r_{i-1},\qquad i=1,\dots,2n-1.$$
This determines $r_2,\dots,r_{2n}$ and automatically satisfies the conditions fo
r rows $1,\dots,2n-1$.

**Polynomial formulation.** Define $p_0=1,\;p_1=x,\;p_k=x\,p_{k-1}+p_{k-2}$. By
induction, $r_{i+1}=p_i(B)\,r_1$ for all $i\ge 0$.

res:
$$r_{2n-1}+Br_{2n}=0 \;\Longleftrightarrow\; \bigl(p_{2n-2}(B)+B\,p_{2n-1}(B)\bi
gr)\,r_1=0 \;\Longleftrightarrow\; p_{2n}(B)\,r_1=0,$$
using the recurrence $p_{2n}=x\,p_{2n-1}+p_{2n-2}$.

**Verification for small cases.**
- $n=1$ ($2\times 2$): $r_1=(a,b)$, $r_2=Br_1=(b,a)$. Board $\begin{pmatrix}a&b\

$$\boxed{2^{2n}}$$
