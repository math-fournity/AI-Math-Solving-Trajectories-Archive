**Theorem.** Let $A$ be a Noetherian ring. For every closed subset $V \subseteq
\operatorname{Spec} A$, the set of minimal prime ideals in $V$ is finite.

**Step 1: Reduction to minimal primes of a Noetherian ring.**

Every closed subset of $\operatorname{Spec} A$ is of the form $V(I) = \{\mathfra
k{p} \in \operatorname{Spec} A : I \subseteq \mathfrak{p}\}$ for some ideal $I$
of $A$. The minimal elements of $V(I)$ (under inclusion) are precisely the prime

Since $A/I$ is again Noetherian, it suffices to prove:

> **Claim.** Every Noetherian ring has finitely many minimal prime ideals.

**Step 2: Proof of the Claim.**
