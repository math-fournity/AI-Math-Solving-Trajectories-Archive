## Answer

In the topos $\mathbf{Set}^{\mathbf{L}^{op}}$ (presheaves on the category of loc
i $\mathbf{L}$), the real line $R$ is interpreted as the **representable preshea
f corresponding to the free $C^\infty$-ring on one generator**, namely $C^\infty
(\mathbb{R})$.

### Setup

Let $\mathbf{L}$ denote the category of **loci**, i.e., the dual of the category
 of finitely generated $C^\infty$-rings. Objects of $\mathbf{L}$ are loci $\ell_
A = \mathrm{Spec}_\infty(A)$ for finitely generated $C^\infty$-rings $A = C^\inf
ty(\mathbb{R}^n)/I$, and morphisms $\ell_A \to \ell_B$ in $\mathbf{L}$ correspon
d to $C^\infty$-ring homomorphisms $B \to A$.

The topos $\mathbf{Set}^{\mathbf{L}^{op}}$ is the category of presheaves on $\ma
thbf{L}$, i.e., functors $\mathbf{L}^{op} \to \mathbf{Set}$.

### The real line as a representable

The **real line locus** is the object $\ell_R := \mathrm{Spec}_\infty(C^\infty(\
mathbb{R})) \in \mathbf{L}$, where $C^\infty(\mathbb{R})$ is the ring of smooth
functions on $\mathbb{R}$. This is the **free $C^\infty$-ring on one generator**
 — the generator being the identity function $\mathrm{id}: \mathbb{R} \to \mathb
b{R}$.

The real line $R$ in the topos is the **Yoneda embedding** of this locus:

$$R = \mathbf{y}(\ell_R) = \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{L}}(-,\, \ell_R) : \mathbf{L}^{
op} \to \mathbf{Set}.$$

### Evaluation at each stage

For each locus $\ell_A = \mathrm{Spec}_\infty(A)$, we compute:

$$R(\ell_A) = \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbf{L}}(\ell_A,\, \ell_R) = \mathrm{Hom}_{C^\inf
ty\text{-rings}}\!\big(C^\infty(\mathbb{R}),\, A\big).$$

By the **universal property of the free $C^\infty$-ring on one generator**, a $C
^\infty$-ring homomorphism $\varphi: C^\infty(\mathbb{R}) \to A$ is uniquely det
ermined by the image of the generator $\mathrm{id} \in C^\infty(\mathbb{R})$, wh
ich may be any element $a \in |A|$ (the underlying set of $A$). Concretely, $\va
rphi$ maps a smooth function $f \in C^\infty(\mathbb{R})$ to $f^A(a)$, the inter
pretation of $f$ in $A$ applied to $a$.

This gives a natural isomorphism:

$$R(\ell_A) \;\cong\; |A|,$$

the **underlying set** of the $C^\infty$-ring $A$.

### Functoriality

For a morphism $f: \ell_A \to \ell_B$ in $\mathbf{L}$ (corresponding to a $C^\in
fty$-ring homomorphism $\phi: B \to A$), the presheaf action is:

$$R(f): R(\ell_B) \longrightarrow R(\ell_A), \qquad b \longmapsto \phi(b).$$

That is, $R$ acts by **applying $C^\infty$-ring homomorphisms to underlying elem
ents**. This is precisely the **underlying-set functor** $|\text{-}|: (C^\infty\
text{-rings}) \to \mathbf{Set}$, re-expressed as a presheaf on loci.

### Structure carried by $R$

Because $R$ is represented by $C^\infty(\mathbb{R})$, and $C^\infty(\mathbb{R})$
 is a $C^\infty$-ring, the object $R$ inherits:

1. **A commutative ring object structure** in the topos (addition, multiplicatio
n, zero, one — induced by the corresponding operations on $C^\infty(\mathbb{R})$
).

2. **All $C^\infty$-operations**: for every smooth map $f: \mathbb{R}^n \to \mat
hbb{R}$, there is an induced morphism $R^n \to R$ in the topos, given at each st
age by $f^A: |A|^n \to |A|$ (the interpretation of $f$ in $A$). This makes $R$ a
 **$C^\infty$-ring object** in the topos.

3. **The generic property**: $R$ is the **generic finitely generated $C^\infty$-
ring** — by Yoneda, morphisms $X \to R$ in the topos (for representable $X = \ma
thbf{y}(\ell_A)$) correspond to elements of $|A|$, i.e., generalized elements of
 $R$ at stage $\ell_A$ are exactly elements of the $C^\infty$-ring $A$.

### Summary

The real line $R$ in $\mathbf{Set}^{\mathbf{L}^{op}}$ is the **representable pre
sheaf** $\mathbf{y}(\mathrm{Spec}_\infty(C^\infty(\mathbb{R})))$, i.e., the Yone
da embedding of the smooth real line locus. At each locus $\ell_A$, it evaluates
 to the underlying set $|A|$ of the corresponding $C^\infty$-ring, with functori
ality given by applying $C^\infty$-ring homomorphisms. It is the **generic $C^\i
nfty$-ring object** in the topos, carrying the full $C^\infty$-ring structure an
d serving as the smooth real line for synthetic differential geometry.
