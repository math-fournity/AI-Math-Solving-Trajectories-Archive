## Solution

**Defining "k-Safe" precisely.** A minute $n$ is compromised by protocol $k$ if
$|n - mk| \le 2$ for some integer $m \ge 0$. Writing $n \equiv r \pmod{k}$ with
$0 \le r \le k-1$, the minute is within 2 of the multiple below when $r \le 2$,
and within 2 of the multiple above when $r \ge k-2$. So $n$ is **k-Safe** iff $r
 \in \{3, 4, \dots, k-3\}$.

**Safe residue sets:**
- **7-Safe:** $r \in \{3, 4\}$ — 2 residues
- **11-Safe:** $r \in \{3,4,5,6,7,8\}$ — 6 residues
- **13-Safe:** $r \in \{3,4,5,6,7,8,9,10\}$ — 8 residues

**Verification with the example:** For $k=10$, safe residues are $\{3,4,5,6,7\}$
, giving $\{3,4,5,6,7,13,14,15,16,17,23,\dots\}$ — matches the problem statement
. ✓

**Counting via CRT.** Since $7, 11, 13$ are pairwise coprime, the combined patte
rn has period $\text{lcm} = 7 \times 11 \times 13 = 1001$. By the Chinese Remain
der Theorem, each triple of residues $(r_7, r_{11}, r_{13})$ corresponds to exac
tly one residue mod $1001$. The number of valid residues per full period is:

$$2 \times 6 \times 8 = 96.$$

**Full periods in $\{1, \dots, 10000\}$:** $10000 = 9 \times 1001 + 991$, so the
re are 9 full periods covering $n = 1$ to $n = 9009$, contributing $9 \times 96
= 864$.

**Partial period:** The remaining $n = 9010$ to $n = 10000$ (991 values) have re
sidues mod $1001$ equal to $1, 2, \dots, 991$ (since $9010 \equiv 1 \pmod{1001}$
 and $10000 \equiv 991 \pmod{1001}$).

The count of valid residues in $\{1, \dots, 991\}$ equals:

$$\underbrace{96}_{\text{valid in } \{0,\dots,1000\}} - \underbrace{0}_{\text{va
lid at } r=0} - \underbrace{(\text{valid in } \{992,\dots,1000\})}_{\text{to com
pute}}.$$

**Checking $r = 0$:** $0 \bmod 7 = 0 \notin \{3,4\}$. Invalid. ✓

**Checking $r \in \{992, \dots, 1000\}$:**

| $r$ | $r \bmod 7$ | $r \bmod 11$ | $r \bmod 13$ | Valid? |
|-----|-------------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| 992 | 5 | — | — | No (mod 7) |
| 993 | 6 | — | — | No |
| 994 | 0 | — | — | No |
| 995 | 1 | — | — | No |
| 996 | 2 | — | — | No |
| 997 | **3** ✓ | **7** ✓ | **9** ✓ | **Yes** |
| 998 | **4** ✓ | **8** ✓ | **10** ✓ | **Yes** |
| 999 | 5 | — | — | No |
| 1000 | 6 | — | — | No |

Valid in $\{992, \dots, 1000\}$: **2** (namely $r = 997$ and $r = 998$).

**Partial period count:** $96 - 0 - 2 = 94$.

**Final total:**

$$864 + 94 = \boxed{958}.$$
