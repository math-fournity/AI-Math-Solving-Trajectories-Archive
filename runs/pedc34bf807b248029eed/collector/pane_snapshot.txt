**(ii) Geodesic additivity:** If $P$ lies on the geodesic segment from $X$ to $Y

**Step 1 — Recovering $\sigma[X,P]$ for all $X \in \mathcal{S}$.**

Fix $X \in \mathcal{S}$, $X \neq P$. The set of points $Y$ such that $P$ lies on

- For $\dim \mathcal{S} \geq 2$: a 1D ray has empty interior, so points $Y$ near

- For $\dim \mathcal{S} = 1$: we approach $P$ from the same side as $X$, so $P$
is not between $X$ and $Y_n$.

$$\sigma[X, P] = \lim_{n \to \infty} \sigma[X, Y_n] = \lim_{n \to \infty} \hat\s

Thus $\sigma[X,P]$ is determined by $\hat\sigma$ for every $X$. By the symmetry
$\sigma[P,Y] = \sigma[Y,P]$, the value $\sigma[P,Y]$ is determined for every $Y$

atorname{sgn}(\sigma[X,P])$ is known because Step 1 gives us the actual value (n
