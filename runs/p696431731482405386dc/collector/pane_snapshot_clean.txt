## Solution

We want the last two digits of the power tower $T_{2015} = 3^{3^{3^{\cdots}}}$ c
ontaining 2015 threes, i.e., $T_{2015} \bmod 100$.

**Step 1: Reduce mod 4.**

Since $100 = 4 \times 25$ with $\gcd(4,25)=1$, we compute mod 4 and mod 25 separ
ately.

Mod 4: $3 \equiv -1 \pmod{4}$, so $3^{\text{odd}} \equiv 3 \pmod 4$ and $3^{\tex
t{even}} \equiv 1 \pmod 4$. The exponent $T_{2014}$ is a power of 3, hence odd.
Therefore:
$$T_{2015} = 3^{T_{2014}} \equiv 3 \pmod{4}.$$

**Step 2: Reduce mod 25.**

Since $\gcd(3,25)=1$ and $\varphi(25)=20$, Euler's theorem gives $3^{20}\equiv 1
\pmod{25}$, so:
$$T_{2015} \equiv 3^{\,T_{2014}\bmod 20} \pmod{25}.$$

We need $T_{2014} \bmod 20$. Since $20 = 4\times 5$:

- **Mod 4:** $T_{2014} = 3^{T_{2013}}$, and $T_{2013}$ is odd, so $T_{2014}\equi
v 3\pmod 4$.
- **Mod 5:** $\varphi(5)=4$, so $T_{2014} = 3^{T_{2013}} \equiv 3^{\,T_{2013}\bm
od 4}\pmod 5$. Now $T_{2013}\bmod 4 = 3^{T_{2012}}\bmod 4 = 3$ (since $T_{2012}$
 is odd). Hence $T_{2014}\equiv 3^3 = 27\equiv 2\pmod 5$.

CRT for $T_{2014}\bmod 20$: solve $x\equiv 3\pmod 4$, $x\equiv 2\pmod 5$. Checki
ng $x\equiv 2\pmod 5$: $\{2,7,12,17\}$; mod 4 these are $\{2,3,0,1\}$. So $x=7$,
 i.e. $T_{2014}\equiv 7\pmod{20}$.

Therefore:
$$T_{2015}\equiv 3^{7}\pmod{25}.$$
Computing: $3^1=3,\; 3^2=9,\; 3^3\equiv 2,\; 3^4\equiv 6,\; 3^5\equiv 18,\; 3^6\
equiv 4,\; 3^7\equiv 12\pmod{25}$.

So $T_{2015}\equiv 12\pmod{25}$.

**Step 3: Combine via CRT.**

Solve $x\equiv 3\pmod 4$ and $x\equiv 12\pmod{25}$. Candidates $x\equiv 12\pmod{
25}$: $\{12,37,62,87\}$; mod 4 these are $\{0,1,2,3\}$. So $x=87$.

$$T_{2015}\equiv 87\pmod{100}.$$

The last two digits are $\boxed{87}$.
