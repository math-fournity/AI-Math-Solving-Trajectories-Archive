## Solution

**Key observation (cyclic structure).** Group the terms:
$$S_2=xy+yz+zt+tx=x(y+t)+z(y+t)=(x+z)(y+t).$$
Likewise
$$S_3=xyz+yzt+ztx+txy=\bigl(xyz+ztx\bigr)+\bigl(yzt+txy\bigr)=xz(y+t)+yt(z+x).$$

Set $a=x+z$ and $b=y+t$. Then
$$a+b=S_1=5,\qquad ab=S_2=4.$$
Hence $a,b$ are the roots of $u^2-5u+4=0=(u-1)(u-4)$, so $\{a,b\}=\{1,4\}$.

Now put $p=xz$ and $q=yt$. From the factored form of $S_3$ and from $S_4$:
$$b\,p+a\,q=S_3=3,\qquad pq=S_4=-1.$$

We treat the two cases.

**Case 1: $a=x+z=1,\; b=y+t=4$.** The system becomes $4p+q=3$, $pq=-1$.
Substituting $q=3-4p$ into $pq=-1$:
$$p(3-4p)=-1\;\Longrightarrow\;4p^2-3p-1=0=(4p+1)(p-1),$$

* $p=1$: then $x+z=1$, $xz=1$, giving $u^2-u+1=0$ with discriminant $-3<0$ — no
real roots.
