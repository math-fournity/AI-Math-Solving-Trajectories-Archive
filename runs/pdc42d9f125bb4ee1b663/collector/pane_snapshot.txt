## Solution

We solve the boundary-value problem for the vibrating beam (Euler–Bernoulli) equ
ation
$$u_{tt}+b^{2}u_{xxxx}=0,\qquad 0<x<L,\ t>0,$$
with
$$u(0,t)=u(L,t)=0,\quad u_{xx}(0,t)=u_{xx}(L,t)=0,\qquad u(x,0)=f(x),\quad u_t(x
,0)=g(x).$$

### 1. Separation of variables

Set $u(x,t)=X(x)T(t)$. Substitution gives
$$X\,T''+b^{2}X^{(4)}\,T=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{X^{(4)}}{X}=-\frac{T''
}{b^{2}T}=\lambda.$$

We take the spatial eigenvalue problem
$$X^{(4)}-\lambda X=0,\qquad X(0)=X(L)=0,\quad X''(0)=X''(L)=0.$$

### 2. Spatial eigenvalue problem

**Claim.** The eigenvalues are $\lambda_n=\left(\frac{n\pi}{L}\right)^4$ with ei
genfunctions $X_n(x)=\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right)$, $n=1,2,\dots$

*Proof.* Multiply $X^{(4)}=\lambda X$ by $X$ and integrate:
$$\int_0^L X X^{(4)}\,dx=\lambda\int_0^L X^2\,dx.$$
Two integrations by parts, using $X(0)=X(L)=0$ and $X''(0)=X''(L)=0$, give
$$\int_0^L X X^{(4)}\,dx=\int_0^L (X'')^2\,dx\ge 0.$$
Hence $\lambda\ge 0$. If $\lambda=0$, then $X''\equiv 0$ together with $X(0)=X(L
)=0$ forces $X\equiv 0$, so $\lambda>0$.

For $\lambda=\mu^4>0$ ($\mu>0$), the general solution is
$$X(x)=A\cosh\mu x+B\sinh\mu x+C\cos\mu x+D\sin\mu x.$$
From $X(0)=0$: $A+C=0$. From $X''(0)=0$: $\mu^2(A-C)=0$, so $A=C=0$. Thus
$$X(x)=B\sinh\mu x+D\sin\mu x.$$
From $X(L)=0$ and $X''(L)=0$:
$$B\sinh\mu L+D\sin\mu L=0,\qquad B\sinh\mu L-D\sin\mu L=0.$$
Adding and subtracting: $B\sinh\mu L=0$ and $D\sin\mu L=0$. Since $\sinh\mu L\ne
q 0$, we get $B=0$; the nontrivial solution requires $\sin\mu L=0$, i.e.
$$\mu L=n\pi,\qquad n=1,2,3,\dots$$
Therefore
$$\lambda_n=\mu_n^4=\left(\frac{n\pi}{L}\right)^4,\qquad X_n(x)=\sin\!\left(\fra
c{n\pi x}{L}\right).$$

The family $\{X_n\}$ is orthogonal and complete on $[0,L]$ (it is the standard F
ourier sine basis), so it forms a complete set of eigenfunctions.

### 3. Temporal equation

For each $n$, $T_n''+b^2\lambda_n T_n=0$, i.e.
$$T_n''+\left(\frac{bn\pi}{L}\right)^4 T_n=0.$$
With $\omega_n=b\left(\frac{n\pi}{L}\right)^2$, the general solution is
$$T_n(t)=A_n\cos\omega_n t+B_n\sin\omega_n t.$$

### 4. General solution and coefficients

$$u(x,t)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(A_n\cos\omega_n t+B_n\sin\omega_n t\right)\sin
\!\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right).$$

Initial conditions give the Fourier sine series of $f$ and $g$:
$$f(x)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}A_n\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right),\qquad g(x)=\su
m_{n=1}^{\infty}B_n\omega_n\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right).$$

By orthogonality $\int_0^L \sin^2\!\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right)dx=\frac{L}{2}$,
$$\boxed{\,A_n=\frac{2}{L}\int_0^L f(x)\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right)dx\,},
$$
$$\boxed{\,B_n=\frac{2}{L\omega_n}\int_0^L g(x)\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\righ
t)dx=\frac{2L}{bn^2\pi^2}\int_0^L g(x)\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right)dx\,}.$
$

### 5. Verification

- **PDE**: Each term satisfies $T_n''+\omega_n^2 T_n=0$ and $X_n^{(4)}=\lambda_n
 X_n$, so $u_{tt}+b^2 u_{xxxx}=\sum (T_n''+b^2\lambda_n T_n)X_n=0$. ✓
- **Boundary**: $\sin(0)=\sin(n\pi)=0$ and $X_n''=-\left(\frac{n\pi}{L}\right)^2
 X_n$, so $X_n''(0)=X_n''(L)=0$. ✓
- **Initial**: By construction the series matches $f$ (at $t=0$) and $g$ (via $u
_t=\sum(-A_n\omega_n\sin\omega_n t+B_n\omega_n\cos\omega_n t)X_n$, at $t=0$ givi
ng $\sum B_n\omega_n X_n=g$). ✓

### Final Answer

$$\boxed{\,u(x,t)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left[A_n\cos\!\left(b\!\left(\tfrac{n\pi}{
L}\right)^{\!2}t\right)+B_n\sin\!\left(b\!\left(\tfrac{n\pi}{L}\right)^{\!2}t\ri
ght)\right]\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right)\,}$$

with
$$A_n=\frac{2}{L}\int_0^L f(\xi)\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi\xi}{L}\right)d\xi,\qquad
B_n=\frac{2L}{bn^2\pi^2}\int_0^L g(\xi)\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi\xi}{L}\right)d\xi.
$$
