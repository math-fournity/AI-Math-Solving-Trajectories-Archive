$$\log_{10}!\left(e^{e^{10^{10^{2.8}}}}\right) = \log_{10}!
\left(10^{10^{10^{10^{x}}}}\right)$$

$$e^{10^{10^{2.8}}}\cdot \log_{10} e = 10^{10^{10^{x}}} \tag{1}$$

Step 2: Take $\log_{10}$ of (1)

$$\log_{10}!\left(e^{10^{10^{2.8}}}\cdot \log_{10} e\right) = \log_{10}!
\left(10^{10^{10^{x}}}\right)$$

$$10^{10^{2.8}}\cdot \log_{10} e ;+; \log_{10}(\log_{10} e) ;=; 10^{10^{x}}
\tag{2}$$

Now $10^{10^{2.8}} = 10^{630.957\ldots}$, a number with $\approx 631$ digits.

$$10^{10^{x}} = 10^{10^{2.8}}\cdot \log_{10} e = 10^{,10^{2.8},+,\log_{10}

$$10^{x} = 10^{2.8} + \log_{10}(\log_{10} e) \tag{3}$$

$$x = \log_{10}!\Big(10^{2.8} + \log_{10}(\log_{10} e)\Big) \tag{4}$$
