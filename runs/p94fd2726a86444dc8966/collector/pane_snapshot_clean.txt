## Proof

**Setup.** In $\mathbb{P}^2$ with homogeneous coordinates $[x_0 : x_1 : x_2]$, t
he locus defined by $x_0 x_1 x_2$ is the hypersurface
$$V(x_0 x_1 x_2) = V(x_0) \cup V(x_1) \cup V(x_2),$$
the union of the three coordinate lines (the "coordinate triangle").

**The complement.** The complement of this locus is the open set
$$U = \mathbb{P}^2 \setminus V(x_0 x_1 x_2) = \{[x_0 : x_1 : x_2] \in \mathbb{P}
^2 : x_0 x_1 x_2 \neq 0\}.$$
This is the set of points where all three homogeneous coordinates are nonzero. I
t is the intersection of the three standard affine opens $D_+(x_0) \cap D_+(x_1)
 \cap D_+(x_2)$, and it is isomorphic to the 2-dimensional algebraic torus $(\ma
thbb{G}_m)^2$ (on the chart $x_0 = 1$ with coordinates $u = x_1/x_0,\; v = x_2/x
_0$, the condition becomes $uv \neq 0$, giving $\operatorname{Spec}\,\mathbb{C}[
u^{\pm1}, v^{\pm1}]$).

**Density.** The open set $U$ is nonempty: for instance, $[1:1:1] \in U$. Since
$\mathbb{P}^2$ is an irreducible variety (its homogeneous coordinate ring $\math
bb{C}[x_0, x_1, x_2]$ is an integral domain), every nonempty (Zariski-)open subs
et of $\mathbb{P}^2$ is dense.

**Projective closure.** The projective closure of $U$ is its closure in the ambi
ent projective space $\mathbb{P}^2$. Since $U$ is a nonempty open subset of the
irreducible projective variety $\mathbb{P}^2$, it is dense, and therefore
$$\overline{U}^{\,\mathbb{P}^2} = \mathbb{P}^2.$$

**Conclusion.** The projective closure of the complement of $V(x_0 x_1 x_2)$ in
$\mathbb{P}^2$ is the entire projective plane:
$$\boxed{\mathbb{P}^2}.$$

Geometrically: the complement of the coordinate triangle is the open torus $(\ma
thbb{G}_m)^2$, which is dense in $\mathbb{P}^2$; closing it up inside $\mathbb{P
}^2$ adds back the three coordinate lines $V(x_0) \cup V(x_1) \cup V(x_2)$, reco
vering all of $\mathbb{P}^2$.
