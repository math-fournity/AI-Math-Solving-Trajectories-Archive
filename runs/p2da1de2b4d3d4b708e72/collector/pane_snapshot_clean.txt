## Solution

**Setup.** Let the square base have side length $x$ and the height be $h$. The p
rism is a right rectangular prism with a square base, so it has:
- 8 base edges (4 top + 4 bottom), each of length $x$,
- 4 vertical edges, each of length $h$.

**Constraint (volume):**
$$x^2 h = 12\sqrt{3}.$$

**Objective (total edge length to minimize):**
$$E = 8x + 4h.$$

**Minimization via Lagrange multipliers.** Minimize $L = 8x + 4h$ subject to $g(
x,h) = x^2 h - 12\sqrt{3} = 0$.

$$\frac{\partial}{\partial x}: \quad 8 = \lambda \cdot 2xh, \qquad \frac{\partia
l}{\partial h}: \quad 4 = \lambda \cdot x^2.$$

Dividing the first equation by the second:
$$\frac{8}{4} = \frac{2xh}{x^2} \;\Longrightarrow\; 2 = \frac{2h}{x} \;\Longrigh
tarrow\; h = x.$$

So the minimizing configuration is a **cube**: $x = h$.

**Verification of minimum.** From $h = x$ and $x^2 h = x^3 = 12\sqrt{3}$. The bo
rdered Hessian / second-order conditions confirm this is a minimum (the objectiv
e is linear and the constraint forces a trade-off between $x$ and $h$; the uniqu
e critical point is the global minimum).

**Compute the surface area.** The total surface area (including top and bottom)
is:
$$S = 2x^2 + 4xh.$$

Since $h = x$:
$$S = 2x^2 + 4x^2 = 6x^2.$$

Now compute $x^2$ from $x^3 = 12\sqrt{3}$:
$$x^2 = (x^3)^{2/3} = (12\sqrt{3})^{2/3} = \sqrt[3]{(12\sqrt{3})^2} = \sqrt[3]{1
44 \cdot 3} = \sqrt[3]{432}.$$

Factor $432 = 216 \cdot 2 = 6^3 \cdot 2$, so:
$$\sqrt[3]{432} = 6\sqrt[3]{2}.$$

Therefore:
$$S = 6x^2 = 6 \cdot 6\sqrt[3]{2} = 36\sqrt[3]{2}.$$

**Answer.** The total surface area of the unit in the edge-length-minimizing con
figuration is

$$\boxed{36\sqrt[3]{2}} \text{ square meters.}$$
