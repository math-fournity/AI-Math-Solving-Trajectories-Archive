_3$, $1-t$ is not a square in $F$, so $h(y)$ is irreducible over $F$. Let $u = \

$$y_1 = 1 + 2u, \qquad y_2 = 1 + u$$
(using $2^{-1}=2$ and $-1=2$ in $\mathbb{F}_3$, and noting $-2u = u$). One verif
