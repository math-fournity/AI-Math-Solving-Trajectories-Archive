## Solution

**Answer: $n = 4$.**

### Construction ($n = 4$ is achievable)

The four vertices of a rectangle work. Let $A, B, C, D$ be the vertices of a rec
tangle. Any three of them form a right triangle: in each triple, the vertex that
 serves as a corner of the rectangle (adjacent to the two others in the triple)
is the right-angle vertex. For instance, in $\triangle ABC$ the right angle is a
t $B$; in $\triangle ABD$ the right angle is at $A$; in $\triangle ACD$ the righ
t angle is at $D$; in $\triangle BCD$ the right angle is at $C$.

### Impossibility of $n = 5$

Suppose for contradiction that five distinct points $P_1, \dots, P_5$ satisfy th
e property that every three form a right-angled triangle.

**Diameter argument.** Among all $\binom{5}{2}$ pairs, choose $A, B$ at maximum
distance. For any other point $C$, in triangle $ABC$ the side $AB$ is the longes
t (since $AB$ is the global maximum distance), so $AB$ is the hypotenuse, and th
e right angle is at $C$. By Thales' theorem, $C$ lies on the circle $\Gamma$ wit
h diameter $AB$.

Since $n = 5$, there are at least three further points $C, D, E$ on $\Gamma$ (be
sides $A$ and $B$, which are the endpoints of the diameter and also lie on $\Gam
ma$).

**Key constraint.** For any three points on a circle, they form a right triangle
 if and only if one of the three sides is a diameter of the circle (converse of
Thales' theorem).

Apply this to triple $\{A, C, D\}$, all on $\Gamma$:
- $AC$ is a diameter only if $C$ is antipodal to $A$, i.e., $C = B$ — impossible
 since $C \neq B$.
- $AD$ is a diameter only if $D$ is antipodal to $A$, i.e., $D = B$ — impossible
 since $D \neq B$.
- Therefore $CD$ must be a diameter, so **$C$ and $D$ are antipodal** on $\Gamma
$.

Apply the same reasoning to triple $\{A, C, E\}$:
- $AC$ is not a diameter ($C \neq B$); $AE$ is not a diameter ($E \neq B$).
- Therefore $CE$ must be a diameter, so **$C$ and $E$ are antipodal** on $\Gamma
$.

But the antipodal point of $C$ on $\Gamma$ is unique, forcing $D = E$, contradic
ting the distinctness of the five points.

Therefore $n = 5$ is impossible, so $n \le 4$.

### Conclusion

Since $n = 4$ is achievable and $n = 5$ is impossible, the largest possible numb
er is

$$\boxed{n = 4}.$$
