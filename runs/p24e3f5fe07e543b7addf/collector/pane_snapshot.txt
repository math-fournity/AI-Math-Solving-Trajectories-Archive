## Solution

**Answer-leak check:** The problem statement contains only the problem and a pla
ceholder box — no solutions, sketches, or answer values. Proceeding.

Let $P(x) = (x^2-3x+1)^{1009} = \sum_{k=0}^{2018} a_k x^k$, and let $S = \sum_{k
=0}^{2018} a_k^2$.

**Step 1: Express $S$ as a constant term.**

The coefficient of $x^0$ in $P(x)P(x^{-1}) = \sum_{j,k} a_j a_k x^{j-k}$ is exac
tly $\sum_k a_k^2 = S$.

**Step 2: Simplify $P(x^{-1})$.**

Since $x^2-3x+1$ is palindromic (coefficients $1,-3,1$),
$$P(x^{-1}) = (x^{-2}-3x^{-1}+1)^{1009} = x^{-2018}(x^2-3x+1)^{1009} = x^{-2018}
P(x).$$
Hence
$$S = [x^0]\,x^{-2018}(x^2-3x+1)^{2018} = [x^{2018}](x^2-3x+1)^{2018}.$$

**Step 3: Reduce to a Laurent-polynomial constant term.**

Using $x^2-3x+1 = x(x+x^{-1}-3)$,
$$(x^2-3x+1)^{2018} = x^{2018}(x+x^{-1}-3)^{2018},$$
so
$$S = [x^0]\,(x+x^{-1}-3)^{2018}.$$

**Step 4: Work modulo $2017$.**

Note $2017$ is prime and $2018 = 2017+1$. In $\mathbb{F}_{2017}[x,x^{-1}]$, the
freshman's dream (multinomial, all cross-term coefficients divisible by $p$) giv
es
$$(x+x^{-1}-3)^{2017} \equiv x^{2017}+x^{-2017}-3^{2017} \equiv x^{2017}+x^{-201
7}-3 \pmod{2017},$$
using Fermat's little theorem $3^{2017}\equiv 3$.

Therefore
$$(x+x^{-1}-3)^{2018} \equiv (x^{2017}+x^{-2017}-3)(x+x^{-1}-3) \pmod{2017}.$$

**Step 5: Extract the constant term.**

Expanding the product, the only term with total exponent $0$ is $(-3)\cdot(-3)=9
$ (all other terms have exponents $\pm 2018, \pm 2016, \pm 2017, \pm 1$, none of
 which is $0$). Thus
$$S \equiv 9 \pmod{2017}.$$

$$\boxed{9}$$
