These cosets partition $\mathbb{R}$: they are pairwise disjoint, and their union

**Disjointness.** Two cosets $r_1 + \mathbb{Q}$ and $r_2 + \mathbb{Q}$ are eithe

}$, there exists $q \in \mathbb{Q}$ with $a - r < q < b - r$, which gives $a < r
 + q < b$ with $r + q \in r + \mathbb{Q}$. Thus every coset intersects every ope
