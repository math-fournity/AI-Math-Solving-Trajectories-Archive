## Proof

We have two curves:
$$C_1: y = x^3 - x, \qquad C_2: y = x^2 - a.$$

They meet at a point $P=(x_0,y_0)$ where they are **tangent**, so both the funct
ion values and the derivatives agree.

### Step 1: Find the tangency point

**Equal values:**
$$x_0^3 - x_0 = x_0^2 - a \;\Longrightarrow\; a = x_0^2 - x_0^3 + x_0. \tag{1}$$

**Equal derivatives:**
$$3x_0^2 - 1 = 2x_0 \;\Longrightarrow\; 3x_0^2 - 2x_0 - 1 = 0 \;\Longrightarrow\
; (3x_0+1)(x_0-1)=0.$$

So $x_0 = 1$ or $x_0 = -\tfrac{1}{3}$.

---

### Step 2: Case $x_0 = 1$

From (1): $a = 1 - 1 + 1 = 1$, giving $P=(1,0)$.

Intersections solve $x^3 - x = x^2 - 1$, i.e.
$$x^3 - x^2 - x + 1 = (x-1)^2(x+1) = 0.$$
Roots: $x=1$ (double, the tangency) and $x=-1$ (a transverse crossing at $(-1,0)
$).

On $[-1,1]$: $(x^3-x)-(x^2-1) = (x-1)^2(x+1) \ge 0$, so $C_1$ lies above $C_2$.

$$A = \int_{-1}^{1}\!\bigl(x^3 - x^2 - x + 1\bigr)\,dx = \left[\frac{x^4}{4}-\fr
ac{x^3}{3}-\frac{x^2}{2}+x\right]_{-1}^{1}.$$

At $x=1$: $\tfrac{1}{4}-\tfrac{1}{3}-\tfrac{1}{2}+1 = \tfrac{5}{12}$.

At $x=-1$: $\tfrac{1}{4}+\tfrac{1}{3}-\tfrac{1}{2}-1 = -\tfrac{11}{12}$.

$$A = \frac{5}{12} - \left(-\frac{11}{12}\right) = \frac{16}{12} = \frac{4}{3}.$
$

---

### Step 3: Case $x_0 = -\tfrac{1}{3}$

From (1): $a = \tfrac{1}{9} + \tfrac{1}{27} - \tfrac{1}{3} = -\tfrac{5}{27}$, gi
ving $P=(-\tfrac{1}{3},\tfrac{8}{27})$.

Intersections solve $x^3 - x = x^2 + \tfrac{5}{27}$, i.e.
$$x^3 - x^2 - x - \tfrac{5}{27} = 0.$$
Since $x=-\tfrac{1}{3}$ is a double root, factor as $(x+\tfrac{1}{3})^2(x - r)$.
 Expanding and matching coefficients gives $r = \tfrac{5}{3}$:
$$(x+\tfrac{1}{3})^2\!\left(x - \tfrac{5}{3}\right) = x^3 - x^2 - x - \tfrac{5}{
27}.$$

On $[-\tfrac{1}{3},\,\tfrac{5}{3}]$: $(x+\tfrac{1}{3})^2 \ge 0$ and $(x-\tfrac{5
}{3}) \le 0$, so $C_1 \le C_2$; the parabola is on top.

$$A = \int_{-1/3}^{5/3}\!\Bigl(-x^3 + x^2 + x + \tfrac{5}{27}\Bigr)\,dx = \left[
-\frac{x^4}{4}+\frac{x^3}{3}+\frac{x^2}{2}+\frac{5x}{27}\right]_{-1/3}^{5/3}.$$

At $x=\tfrac{5}{3}$: $-\tfrac{625}{324}+\tfrac{125}{81}+\tfrac{25}{18}+\tfrac{25
}{81} = \tfrac{-625+500+450+100}{324} = \tfrac{425}{324}$.

At $x=-\tfrac{1}{3}$: $-\tfrac{1}{324}-\tfrac{1}{81}+\tfrac{1}{18}-\tfrac{5}{81}
 = \tfrac{-1-4+18-20}{324} = -\tfrac{7}{324}$.

$$A = \frac{425}{324} - \left(-\frac{7}{324}\right) = \frac{432}{324} = \frac{4}
{3}.$$

---

### Conclusion

Both admissible values of the constant $a$ (namely $a=1$ and $a=-\tfrac{5}{27}$)
 yield the same enclosed area. Therefore the total surface area of the garden be
d enclosed between the two stone paths is

$$\boxed{\dfrac{4}{3}}.$$
