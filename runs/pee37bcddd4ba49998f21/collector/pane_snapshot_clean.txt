**Compute the orbit starting from 2:**

$$2+29+21+5+4 = 61 \equiv 30 \pmod{31}.$$

$$\sum \equiv 403 \times 30 + (2+29+21) \pmod{31}.$$

Compute each piece:

$$g(0)+g(1)+\dots+g(2017) \equiv 0 + 21 = 21 \pmod{31}.$$
