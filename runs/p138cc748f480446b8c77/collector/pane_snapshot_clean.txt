**Proof.**

Recall that a continuous function $v: G \to \mathbb{R}$ is harmonic if and only
if it satisfies the **mean value property (MVP)**: for every ball $\overline{B(x
,r)} \subset G$,
$$v(x) = \frac{1}{|B(x,r)|}\int_{B(x,r)} v(y)\,dy,$$
or equivalently, for every sphere $\partial B(x,r) \subset G$,
$$v(x) = \frac{1}{|\partial B(x,r)|}\int_{\partial B(x,r)} v(y)\,dS(y).$$

$$u_n(x) = \frac{1}{|\partial B(x,r)|}\int_{\partial B(x,r)} u_n(y)\,dS(y).$$

We want to pass the limit $n \to \infty$ through the integral. Since $u_n \to u$

$$\lim_{n\to\infty} u_n(x) = \lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{1}{|\partial B(x,r)|}\int_{
\partial B(x,r)} u_n(y)\,dS(y) = \frac{1}{|\partial B(x,r)|}\int_{\partial B(x,r

That is,
$$u(x) = \frac{1}{|\partial B(x,r)|}\int_{\partial B(x,r)} u(y)\,dS(y).$$

This holds for every $x \in G$ and every $r > 0$ with $\overline{B(x,r)} \subset
