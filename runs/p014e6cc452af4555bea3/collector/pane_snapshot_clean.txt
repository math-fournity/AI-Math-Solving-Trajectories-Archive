## Setup and Key Factorization

**Factor 2021:** $2021 = 43 \times 47$, where 43 and 47 are prime.

**Key observation about $966$:** $\phi(43) = 42$, $\phi(47) = 46$, and $\text{lc
m}(42, 46) = 2 \times 3 \times 7 \times 23 = 966$. So $966$ is divisible by both

By Fermat's Little Theorem, for any $a$ with $\gcd(a, 43) = 1$: $a^{966} = (a^{4
2})^{23} \equiv 1 \pmod{43}$. Similarly, for $\gcd(a, 47) = 1$: $a^{966} \equiv
1 \pmod{47}$.

## Congruence Conditions for $2021 \mid S$

## Lower Bound: $n \geq 43$

## Construction Achieving $n = 43$
