## Setup

Three skaters with speeds $v_1 = 1$, $v_2 = L$, $v_3 = 3.14 = \tfrac{157}{50}$ l
aps/min, where $1 < L < 3.14$. They start together at $t=0$. The race ends at th
e first time $T > 0$ when all three are at the same point.

## When do all three meet?

All three are at the same point at time $T$ iff $T(v_i - v_j) \in \mathbb{Z}$ fo
r all pairs. Setting:
$$T \cdot (v_3 - v_1) = T \cdot \frac{107}{50} = a, \qquad T \cdot (v_2 - v_1) =
 T(L-1) = b$$
for positive integers $a, b$. Then $T = \frac{50a}{107}$ and $L - 1 = \frac{b}{T
} = \frac{107b}{50a}$.

The constraint $1 < L < 3.14$ gives $0 < b < a$.

For $T$ to be the **first** meeting time, we need $\gcd(a, b) = 1$ (otherwise, d
ividing both by their gcd yields a smaller valid meeting time).

## Counting passings

A passing between a faster and slower skater occurs each time the faster gains a
 full lap. In the open interval $(0, T)$, the number of passings for each pair e
quals the number of integers in $(0, \text{lap-gain})$, i.e., $\text{lap-gain} -
 1$:

| Pair | Lap gain at $T$ | Passings in $(0,T)$ |
|------|----------------|---------------------|
| $(1,3)$ | $a$ | $a - 1$ |
| $(1,2)$ | $b$ | $b - 1$ |
| $(2,3)$ | $a - b$ | $a - b - 1$ |

**Total passings** $= (a-1) + (b-1) + (a-b-1) = 2a - 3$.

Note this is **independent of $b$** (hence independent of $L$).

## No intermediate triple coincidences

An intermediate triple coincidence at time $t \in (0, T)$ requires $t \cdot \fra
c{107}{50} \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $t \cdot (L-1) \in \mathbb{Z}$. Writing $t = \fra
c{50j}{107}$ for integer $j$ with $1 \le j < a$, the second condition gives $\fr
ac{jb}{a} \in \mathbb{Z}$, i.e., $a \mid jb$. Since $\gcd(a, b) = 1$, this requi
res $a \mid j$, but $0 < j < a$, so **no intermediate triple coincidence exists*
*. The formula $2a - 3$ is exact.

## Solving

$$2a - 3 = 117 \implies a = 60.$$

Therefore:
$$T = \frac{50 \cdot 60}{107} = \frac{3000}{107} \text{ minutes.}$$
