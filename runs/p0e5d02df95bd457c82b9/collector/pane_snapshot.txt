N}}$ can be encoded by a countable collection of binary sequences, i.e., by a fu

hbb{N}$ is countable, this is equivalent to a single binary sequence:

$$\mathbb{R}^{\mathbb{N}} \hookrightarrow \{0,1\}^{\mathbb{N} \times \mathbb{N}}

$$|\mathbb{R}^{\mathbb{N}}| = (2^{\aleph_0})^{\aleph_0} = 2^{\aleph_0 \cdot \ale

cardinality of the continuum).
