## Proof

**Setup.** Boats $A, B, C$ lie on a circle of radius $R = 1$ km centered at $O$
(the circumcenter of $\triangle ABC$). The "median circle" through the midpoints
 of the three sides is the **nine-point circle** of $\triangle ABC$. Its center
$N$ (the nine-point center) is the midpoint of the Euler-line segment $\overline
{OH}$, where $H$ is the orthocenter. Therefore

$$ON = \tfrac{1}{2}\,OH = \frac{\sqrt{3}+1}{2} \implies OH = \sqrt{3}+1.$$

**Key relation.** For a triangle with circumradius $R$ and orthocenter $H$,

$$OH^{2} = R^{2}\!\left(1 - 8\cos A\cos B\cos C\right).$$

With $R=1$ and $OH^{2}=(\sqrt{3}+1)^{2}=4+2\sqrt{3}$:

$$1 - 8\cos A\cos B\cos C = 4+2\sqrt{3} \implies \cos A\cos B\cos C = -\frac{3+2
\sqrt{3}}{8}.$$

**Using $\angle B = 150^{\circ}$.** Since $\cos 150^{\circ}=-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
,

$$\cos A\cos C = \frac{-(3+2\sqrt{3})/8}{-\sqrt{3}/2} = \frac{3+2\sqrt{3}}{4\sqr
t{3}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}+2}{4}.$$

**Determining $A$ and $C$.** Since $A+C = 180^{\circ}-150^{\circ}=30^{\circ}$, t
he product identity gives

$$\cos A\cos C = \frac{\cos(A+C)+\cos(A-C)}{2} = \frac{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}+\cos(A
-C)}{2}.$$

Setting this equal to $\frac{\sqrt{3}+2}{4}$:

$$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}+\cos(A-C) = \frac{\sqrt{3}+2}{2} \implies \cos(A-C)=1 \impl
ies A=C.$$

Hence $A=C=15^{\circ}$, and the triangle has angles $(15^{\circ},\,150^{\circ},\
,15^{\circ})$.

**Computing the area.** Using the circumradius-area formula $\text{Area}=2R^{2}\
sin A\sin B\sin C$:

$$\text{Area} = 2\cdot 1^{2}\cdot \sin^{2}15^{\circ}\cdot\sin 150^{\circ} = 2\si
n^{2}15^{\circ}\cdot\frac{1}{2} = \sin^{2}15^{\circ}.$$

Since $\sin 15^{\circ}=\frac{\sqrt{6}-\sqrt{2}}{4}$,

$$\sin^{2}15^{\circ} = \frac{(\sqrt{6}-\sqrt{2})^{2}}{16}=\frac{8-4\sqrt{3}}{16}
=\frac{2-\sqrt{3}}{4}.$$

**Verification.** Check $ON$: $\cos A\cos B\cos C = \cos^{2}15^{\circ}\cdot(-\tf
rac{\sqrt{3}}{2}) = \frac{2+\sqrt{3}}{4}\cdot(-\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}) = -\frac{3+2
\sqrt{3}}{8}$, so $OH^{2}=1+3+2\sqrt{3}=4+2\sqrt{3}=(\sqrt{3}+1)^{2}$, giving $O
N=\frac{\sqrt{3}+1}{2}$. ✓

**Answer.** The area of the triangular region is

$$\boxed{\dfrac{2-\sqrt{3}}{4}} \text{ square kilometers.}$$
