## Solution

The relative product $R \mid R$ (composition of $R$ with itself) is the binary r
elation defined by:

$$(a, c) \in R \mid R \iff \exists b\, \big[(a, b) \in R \;\wedge\; (b, c) \in R
\big].$$

Since $\varphi(v_1, v_2)$ expresses $R$ in $\mathfrak{M}$, i.e. for any elements
 $a, b$ in $|\mathfrak{M}|$,

$$\mathfrak{M} \models \varphi(a, b) \iff (a, b) \in R,$$

we obtain the desired formula by existentially quantifying the "intermediate" el
ement. Introducing a fresh variable $v_3$ (distinct from $v_1, v_2$ and not occu
rring in $\varphi$), define:

$$\psi(v_1, v_2) \;:=\; \exists v_3\,\Big(\varphi(v_1, v_3) \;\wedge\; \varphi(v
_3, v_2)\Big).$$

$$\mathfrak{M} \models \psi(a, c) \iff \exists b \in |\mathfrak{M}|\, \big[\math
