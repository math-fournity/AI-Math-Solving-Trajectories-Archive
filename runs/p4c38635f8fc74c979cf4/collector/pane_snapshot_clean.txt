## Proof

We wish to find $\mathbb{P}\!\left(\bigcap_{i=1}^{\infty}\bigcup_{n=i}^{\infty}A
_n\right)$, the probability of the limit superior $\limsup_{n\to\infty} A_n$ (th
e event that infinitely many $A_n$ occur).

**Step 1: Translate the hypothesis into a product condition.**

Since the events $\{A_n\}$ are independent, so are their complements $\{A_n^c\}$
. By De Morgan's law and independence,

$$
\mathbb{P}\!\left(\bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty}A_n\right)
= 1-\mathbb{P}\!\left(\bigcap_{n=1}^{\infty}A_n^c\right)
= 1-\prod_{n=1}^{\infty}\mathbb{P}(A_n^c)
= 1-\prod_{n=1}^{\infty}\bigl(1-\mathbb{P}(A_n)\bigr).
$$

The hypothesis $\mathbb{P}\!\left(\bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty}A_n\right)=1$ gives

$$
\prod_{n=1}^{\infty}\bigl(1-\mathbb{P}(A_n)\bigr)=0. \tag{$\ast$}
$$

**Step 2: Relate the infinite product to a divergent series.**

We use the standard fact: for a sequence $\{a_n\}$ with $0\le a_n<1$,

$$
\prod_{n=1}^{\infty}(1-a_n)=0 \iff \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n=+\infty.
$$

*Proof of the fact.* Taking logarithms, $\prod(1-a_n)=0\iff\sum\log(1-a_n)=-\inf
ty$.

- ($\Leftarrow$) Since $\log(1-x)\le -x$ for $x\in[0,1)$, divergence $\sum a_n=+
\infty$ forces $\sum\log(1-a_n)=-\infty$, hence the product is $0$.
- ($\Rightarrow$) If $\sum a_n<\infty$, then $a_n\to 0$, so eventually $a_n\le\t
frac12$. For $x\in[0,\tfrac12]$, $\log(1-x)\ge -2x$. Thus $\sum\log(1-a_n)\ge -2
\sum a_n>-\infty$, so the product is strictly positive — contradiction. $\square
$

Applying this with $a_n=\mathbb{P}(A_n)\in[0,1)$ (the upper bound holds by hypot
hesis), condition $(\ast)$ yields

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\mathbb{P}(A_n)=+\infty. \tag{$\ast\ast$}
$$

**Step 3: Apply the second Borel–Cantelli lemma.**

The events $\{A_n\}$ are independent (given) and $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\mathbb{P}(
A_n)=+\infty$ by $(\ast\ast)$. The second Borel–Cantelli lemma states that under
 these conditions,

$$
\mathbb{P}\!\left(\limsup_{n\to\infty}A_n\right)=1.
$$

**Step 4: Conclude.**

Since $\limsup_{n\to\infty}A_n=\bigcap_{i=1}^{\infty}\bigcup_{n=i}^{\infty}A_n$,
 we obtain

$$
\boxed{\mathbb{P}\!\left(\bigcap_{i=1}^{\infty}\bigcup_{n=i}^{\infty}A_n\right)=
1.}
$$
