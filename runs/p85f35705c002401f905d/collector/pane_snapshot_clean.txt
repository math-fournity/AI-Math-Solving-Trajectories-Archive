## Solution

**Degree check.** The left-hand side has degree
$$3\cdot 19 + 2\cdot 8 + 4\cdot 6 = 57 + 16 + 24 = 97,$$
matching the leading term $x^{97}$ on the right. Since every factor is monic, th
e leading coefficient is $1$, automatically matching.

**Extracting the coefficient of $x^{96}$.** Write each factor as $x^{d_i} + c_i\
, x^{d_i-1} + \cdots$. For a monic polynomial $f(x)=x^d + c\,x^{d-1}+\cdots$, th
e expansion of $f(x)^n$ begins
$$f(x)^n = x^{dn} + n\,c\,x^{dn-1} + \cdots.$$
In a product $\prod_i f_i(x)^{n_i}$ of monic polynomials, the coefficient of the
 second-highest power $x^{D-1}$ (where $D=\sum n_i d_i$) is therefore $\sum_i n_
i c_i$, since choosing the "second term" from exactly one factor and the leading
 term from all others produces $x^{D-1}$, and these contributions add.

Applying this:

| factor | $d_i$ | $c_i$ (coeff of $x^{d_i-1}$) | $n_i$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $x^3+px^2+2x-5$ | $3$ | $p$ | $19$ |
| $x^2+qx-41$ | $2$ | $q$ | $8$ |
| $x^4-x^3+x-7$ | $4$ | $-1$ | $6$ |

Hence the coefficient of $x^{96}$ on the left is
$$19p + 8q + 6(-1) = 19p + 8q - 6.$$
Setting this equal to $391$:
$$19p + 8q - 6 = 391 \implies 19p + 8q = 397.$$

**Integrality of the remaining coefficients.** Once $p$ and $q$ are integers, ev
ery factor has integer coefficients, so the entire product has integer coefficie
nts; thus $a_{95},\ldots,a_0$ are automatically integers. The only constraint is
 therefore the Diophantine equation
$$19p + 8q = 397.$$

**Solving for $p$.** Reducing modulo $8$:
$$19p + 8q = 397 \pmod 8 \implies 3p \equiv 5 \pmod 8.$$
Since $3^{-1}\equiv 3 \pmod 8$ (because $3\cdot 3=9\equiv 1$),
$$p \equiv 3\cdot 5 = 15 \equiv 7 \pmod 8.$$
So $p = 7 + 8k$ for $k\in\mathbb{Z}$, and correspondingly
$$q = \frac{397 - 19p}{8} = \frac{397 - 19(7+8k)}{8} = \frac{264 - 152k}{8} = 33
 - 19k,$$
which is an integer for every $k$.

The admissible values are $p = 7 + 8k$ ($k\in\mathbb{Z}$). The smallest positive
 integer value is attained at $k=0$:
$$p = 7, \qquad q = 33.$$

**Verification.** $19\cdot 7 + 8\cdot 33 = 133 + 264 = 397$, and $397 - 6 = 391$
. ✓

$$\boxed{p = 7}$$
