## Setup: Reduction to Symmetric Variables

Let $p = ab$, $q = bc$, $r = ca$, $s = abc$. Then:
- $p + q + r + s = 4$
- $pqr = (abc)^2 = s^2$

The inequality becomes $(k+p)(k+q)(k+r) \geq (k+1)^3$.

**Range of $u = p+q+r$:** Since $s = 4-u > 0$ and $pqr = s^2 = (4-u)^2$, AM-GM g
ives $(4-u)^2 \leq (u/3)^3$, i.e., $u^3 - 27(4-u)^2 \geq 0$. Factoring: $(u-3)(u
-12)^2 \geq 0$, so $u \in [3,4)$, meaning $s \in (0,1]$.

## Key Reduction: Worst Case is Two Variables Equal

Expanding the inequality with $e_1 = u$, $e_2 = pq+qr+rp$, $e_3 = pqr = (4-u)^2$
:

$$(k+p)(k+q)(k+r) - (k+1)^3 = k^2(u-3) + k(e_2-3) + (4-u)^2 - 1$$

$$= (u-3)\big[k^2 - (5-u)\big] + k(e_2 - 3)$$

This is **linear in $e_2$**, so for fixed $u$, the minimum occurs at an extreme
of $e_2$. By Lagrange multipliers, the extremes of $e_2$ (given $e_1 = u$, $e_3
= (4-u)^2$) occur when **two of $p,q,r$ are equal** — i.e., when two of $a,b,c$
are equal.

## Symmetric Case: $a = b$

Setting $a = b$: $p = a^2$, $q = r = ac$, $s = a^2c$. The constraint $a^2 + 2ac
+ a^2c = 4$ solves to give $q = 2-a$ (clean substitution), with $a \in (0,2)$.

The inequality becomes $f(a) = (k+a^2)(k+2-a)^2 - (k+1)^3 \geq 0$.

**Substituting $h = a-1$, $m = k+1$:** After expansion,

$$f(a) = h^2\big[h^2 - 2kh + (k+1)(k-2)\big] = h^2 \cdot g(h)$$

where $g(h) = h^2 - 2kh + (k+1)(k-2)$ is a quadratic in $h$ opening upward, with
 vertex at $h = k$ and $h \in (-1,1)$.

Since $h^2 \geq 0$, we need $g(h) \geq 0$ for all $h \in (-1,1)$.

**Evaluating at the endpoints** (the binding constraints):

- $g(-1) = 1 + 2k + (k+1)(k-2) = k^2 + k - 1$
- $g(1) = 1 - 2k + (k+1)(k-2) = k^2 - 3k - 1$

**Case $k \leq -1$** (vertex left of interval, $g$ increasing on $(-1,1)$): mini
mum at $h \to -1^+$. Need $g(-1) \geq 0$:
$$k^2 + k - 1 \geq 0 \implies k \leq \frac{-1-\sqrt{5}}{2}$$

**Case $k \geq 1$** (vertex right of interval, $g$ decreasing on $(-1,1)$): mini
mum at $h \to 1^-$. Need $g(1) \geq 0$:
$$k^2 - 3k - 1 \geq 0 \implies k \geq \frac{3+\sqrt{13}}{2}$$

**Case $k \in (-1,1)$** (vertex inside): $g(k) = -k-2 < 0$ always (since $k > -1
$). No solution.
