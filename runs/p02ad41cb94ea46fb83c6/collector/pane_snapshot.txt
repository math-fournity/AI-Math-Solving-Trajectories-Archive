## Solution

Let $\mathbb{P}^1_k = \operatorname{Proj} k[X,Y]$ with homogeneous coordinates $
[X:Y]$. The embedding $\mathbb{A}^1 \hookrightarrow \mathbb{P}^1$ sends $x \maps
to [x:1]$, i.e. it identifies $\mathbb{A}^1$ with the standard affine chart $U_Y
 = \{Y \neq 0\}$ via $x = X/Y$.

The **point at infinity** is the unique point of $\mathbb{P}^1 \setminus \mathbb
{A}^1$, namely
$$\infty = [1:0] \in U_X = \{X \neq 0\}.$$

### The affine chart at infinity

On the chart $U_X \cong \mathbb{A}^1$ we use the affine coordinate
$$t = \frac{Y}{X}.$$
Under this identification, $\infty = [1:0]$ corresponds to $t = 0$, and the coor
dinate ring of $U_X$ is $k[t]$.

### The stalk

The stalk $\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^1, \infty}$ is the local ring of $U_X \cong \
mathbb{A}^1$ at the point $t=0$:
$$\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^1, \infty} \;\cong\; k[t]_{(t)}.$$

### It is a DVR

Since $k$ is algebraically closed, $k[t]$ is a PID and $(t)$ is a nonzero prime
(hence maximal) ideal of height 1. Localizing at a height-one prime of a PID yie
lds a **discrete valuation ring**:
$$\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^1, \infty} = k[t]_{(t)}.$$

Its structure is:

- **Uniformizing parameter**: $t = Y/X$, the coordinate on the chart at infinity
 vanishing at $\infty$.
- **Valuation**: $v(f) = \operatorname{ord}_{t=0}(f)$, the order of vanishing of
 $f \in k(t)^\times$ at $t=0$. Concretely, for $f = t^n \cdot u$ with $u \in k[t
]_{(t)}^\times$, $v(f) = n$.
- **Maximal ideal**: $\mathfrak{m}_\infty = (t) = t\,k[t]_{(t)}$.
- **Residue field**: $\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^1,\infty}/\mathfrak{m}_\infty \con
g k[t]_{(t)}/(t) \cong k$.
- **Fraction field**: $k(t) = k(Y/X) = k(X/Y) = k(x)$, the function field of $\m
athbb{P}^1$.

### Relation to the original coordinate $x$

Since $x = X/Y = 1/t$, the valuation can be expressed in terms of the original a
ffine coordinate $x$ as
$$v_\infty(f) = -\operatorname{ord}_{x=\infty}(f),$$
i.e. $v_\infty(x) = -1$ and $v_\infty(t) = v_\infty(1/x) = 1$. A rational functi
on $f = p(x)/q(x) \in k(x)^\times$ with $\deg p = m$, $\deg q = n$ has
$$v_\infty(f) = n - m = \deg q - \deg p.$$

### Conclusion

The discrete valuation ring corresponding to the stalk at the point at infinity
is
$$\boxed{\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^1,\,\infty} \;\cong\; k[t]_{(t)}, \quad t = Y/X
,}$$
the localization of the polynomial ring $k[t]$ at the maximal ideal $(t)$, with
uniformizer $t = Y/X = 1/x$, residue field $k$, and valuation given by the order
 of vanishing at $t=0$ (equivalently, $\deg q - \deg p$ for a rational function
$p/q$).
