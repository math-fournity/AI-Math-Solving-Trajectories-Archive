# Problem

A courier company, "FairPath Express," operates a high-tech sorting facility where every package processed has an exactly 50% chance of being diverted into the "North Wing" and a 50% chance of being diverted into the "South Wing."

The facility’s automated rewards system is programmed to grant the manager a "Performance Credit" only during specific cycles of North Wing arrivals. Specifically, the manager receives one credit upon the arrival of the 1st North Wing package, the 3rd, the 5th, the 7th, and so on—earning a credit every time the total number of packages sent to the North Wing reaches an odd integer.

The facility operates continuously until the manager has accumulated exactly 10 Performance Credits. The expected total number of packages (including both North and South Wing diversions) processed by the facility at the moment the 10th credit is earned can be expressed as a simplified fraction $\frac{a}{b}$ for coprime positive integers $a$ and $b$.

Find $a + b$.
