## Setting Up the Structure

$$\left(\prod_{n \in S} n\right) x^{\sum_{n \in S} 3^n}$$

**Key observation:** Since the exponents are sums of *distinct* powers of 3, eac

1 only). So every coefficient $a_i$ equals $\prod_{n \in S_i} n$ for a single su

## Ordering the Exponents

Let $g(N)$ be the sorted list of exponents $\sum_{n \in S} 3^n$ for all $S \subs
eteq \{1,\ldots,N\}$ (including $S = \emptyset$ giving exponent 0).

**Critical fact:** Since $\sum_{n=1}^{N-1} 3^n = \frac{3^N - 3}{2} < 3^N$, every
 subset *not* containing $N$ gives a smaller exponent than every subset *contain
ing* $N$. This yields the recursion:
$$g(N) = g(N-1) \;\oplus\; (3^N + g(N-1))$$

where $\oplus$ is concatenation. Within each half, the relative order matches $g
(N-1)$.

**This is exactly counting in binary.** Position $p$ (0-indexed) in $g(N)$ corre
sponds to the subset $S$ where:
$$n \in S \iff \text{bit } (n-1) \text{ of } p \text{ is } 1$$
