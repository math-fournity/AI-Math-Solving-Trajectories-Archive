## Proof

Let $f(T) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n T^n \in K[[T]]$. We claim:

$$f \text{ converges on } D \text{ and } \|f\|_\infty < \infty \iff \sup_n |a_n|
\, c^n < \infty,$$

and in that case $\|f\|_\infty = \sup_n |a_n|\, c^n$.

*Proof of claim.* Since $c \in |K^\times| \subseteq |\overline{K}^\times| = p^{\
mathbb{Q}}$, the set of values $\{r \in |\overline{K}^\times| : r < c\}$ is cofi

 the series converges and by the non-archimedean maximum modulus principle:

$$\sup_{|x| = r} |f(x)| = \max_n |a_n|\, r^n.$$

Therefore:

$$\|f\|_\infty = \sup_{r < c,\, r \in |\overline{K}^\times|} \max_n |a_n|\, r^n.
$$

**($\Leftarrow$)** Suppose $M := \sup_n |a_n|\, c^n < \infty$. For $|x| = r < c$

$$|a_n|\, r^n = |a_n|\, c^n \cdot (r/c)^n \leq M \cdot (r/c)^n.$$

Since $r/c < 1$, the series $\sum |a_n| r^n$ converges (dominated by the geometr
ic series $M \sum (r/c)^n$), so $f$ converges on $D$. Moreover, $\max_n |a_n| r^
n \leq M$ for all $r < c$, giving $\|f\|_\infty \leq M < \infty$.

**($\Rightarrow$)** Suppose $\|f\|_\infty < \infty$, say $\|f\|_\infty \leq M$.
Then for every $r < c$ and every $n$: $|a_n| r^n \leq \max_m |a_m| r^m \leq M$.
Taking the supremum over $r < c$ (with $r \in p^{\mathbb{Q}}$): $|a_n|\, c^n \le
q M$ for every $n$, hence $\sup_n |a_n| c^n \leq M < \infty$.

**Equality of norms:** We have shown $\|f\|_\infty \leq \sup_n |a_n| c^n$. For t

y$. Taking $\sup$ over $n_0$ gives $\sup_n |a_n| c^n \leq \|f\|_\infty$. ∎

Thus $\Phi$ is an isometric isomorphism of normed $K$-vector spaces.
