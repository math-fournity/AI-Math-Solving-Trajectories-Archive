## Setup

Let H₂ be a general central conic centered at (h, k):
$$A(x-h)^2 + 2H(x-h)(y-k) + C(y-k)^2 = 1.$$

**Key fact:** The midpoint of a chord of H₂ with direction (dx, dy) lies on the
conjugate diameter, satisfying:
$$(A(x_0-h)+H(y_0-k))\,dx + (H(x_0-h)+C(y_0-k))\,dy = 0.$$

Setting (x₀, y₀) = P = (x₁, y₁) and (dx, dy) = (9y₁, 16x₁), the condition become

$$16H\,x_1^2 + (9A+16C)\,x_1 y_1 + 9H\,y_1^2 - (16Hh+16Ck)\,x_1 - (9Ah+9Hk)\,y_1

for all (x₁, y₁) on H₁, i.e., 16x₁² − 9y₁² = 144.

## Determining the coefficients

$$288H\sec^2\theta + 12(9A+16C)\sec\theta\tan\theta - 3(16Hh+16Ck)\sec\theta - 4
(9Ah+9Hk)\tan\theta - 144H = 0.$$

Since sec²θ, secθ tanθ, secθ, tanθ, and the constant are linearly independent fu

- **sec²θ:** 288H = 0 → **H = 0**
