## Step 3: Critical Unsolvability Conditions

A configuration is critically unsolvable if:
1. $\Phi < 0$ (unsolvable), and
2. Adding 1 pebble to **any** box makes $\Phi \geq 0$ (solvable).

The increase $\Delta_j$ from adding 1 to box $j$ is:
- $x_j = 0$: $\Delta_j = g(1) - g(0) = 0-(-1) = 1$
- $x_j = 2m$ (even, $\geq 2$): $\Delta_j = g(2m+1) - g(2m) = m - (m-1) = 1$
- $x_j = 2m+1$ (odd, $\geq 1$): $\Delta_j = g(2m+2) - g(2m+1) = m - m = 0$

From condition (1), $\Phi \leq -1$. From condition (2), $\Phi + \Delta_j \geq 0$
 for all $j$, so $\Delta_j \geq 1$ for all $j$. Since $\Delta_j \in \{0, 1\}$, w
e need **$\Delta_j = 1$ for every $j$** and **$\Phi = -1$**.

$\Delta_j = 1$ for all $j$ means: **no box has an odd positive count** — every $
x_i$ is either $0$ or a positive even number.

## Step 4: Counting for $n = 5$

Setting $\Phi = -1$ gives $\sum a_i = 4$.
