# Problem

In the high-stakes world of the "Starlight 31" lunar mission, a specialized team of $m$ astronauts is conducting research at a singular lunar observation post. The mission spans the 31 days of July, and the operational protocol for the month is governed by three strict efficiency constraints:

1. Throughout July, every astronaut visited the observation post at least once, but to avoid overcrowding, no astronaut was permitted to visit the post more than once on any given calendar day.
2. To ensure a diverse range of data collection, every astronaut ended the month having completed a unique total number of visits (no two astronauts visited the post the same total number of times).
3. To guarantee independent verification, for any pair of astronauts, say Astronaut A and Astronaut B, there must have been at least one day in July when Astronaut A was at the post while Astronaut B was not, and at least one other day when Astronaut B was at the post while Astronaut A was not.

Based on these constraints, determine the largest possible value of $m$.
