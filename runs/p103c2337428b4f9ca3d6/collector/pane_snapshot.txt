## Proof

**Setup.** Write A = ℝ[X][Y]/(Y²−(1−X²)). Then Y satisfies the monic polynomial
T²−(1−X²) ∈ ℝ[X][T], so **A is an integral extension of ℝ[X]** (a free ℝ[X]-modu
le of rank 2 with basis {1, Y}).

**Lower bound: A has at least 2^ℵ₀ maximal ideals.**

By the Cohen–Seidenberg Lying-Over theorem, the map Spec(A) → Spec(ℝ[X]) induced
 by the inclusion ℝ[X] ↪ A is **surjective**. In particular, every maximal ideal
 of ℝ[X] has a maximal ideal of A lying above it.

The maximal ideals of ℝ[X] include (X−a) for every a ∈ ℝ, since ℝ[X]/(X−a) ≅ ℝ i
s a field. The set ℝ has cardinality 2^ℵ₀, so ℝ[X] has at least 2^ℵ₀ maximal ide
als. Each lifts to at least one maximal ideal of A, giving:

> |MaxSpec(A)| ≥ 2^ℵ₀.

**Upper bound: A has at most 2^ℵ₀ maximal ideals.**

The ring ℝ[X,Y] has cardinality 2^ℵ₀ as a set (polynomials with real coefficient
s in two variables). Its quotient A therefore also has cardinality ≤ 2^ℵ₀. Since
 every ideal is a subset of A, the number of ideals — and hence the number of ma
ximal ideals — is at most 2^|A| · ... but more simply, the number of subsets of
A is at most 2^(2^ℵ₀).

However, we can be more precise: each maximal ideal of A is the kernel of a surj
ective ring homomorphism A → K where K ∈ {ℝ, ℂ} (since A is a finitely generated
 ℝ-algebra, Zariski's lemma gives K a finite extension of ℝ, hence ℝ or ℂ). Such
 a homomorphism is determined by the images of X and Y, i.e., by a pair (a,b) ∈
K² with a²+b²=1. The set of such pairs across K ∈ {ℝ,ℂ} has cardinality 2^ℵ₀, so
:

> |MaxSpec(A)| ≤ 2^ℵ₀.

**Conclusion.** Combining both bounds:

$$|\operatorname{MaxSpec}(A)| = 2^{\aleph_0}.$$

The ring ℝ[X,Y]/(X²+Y²−1) has **uncountably many** maximal ideals, with cardinal
ity equal to that of the continuum (2^ℵ₀).

**Remark on the structure.** Concretely, these maximal ideals are of two types:
- **Real points:** For each (a,b) ∈ ℝ² with a²+b²=1, the ideal (X−a, Y−b)/(X²+Y²
−1) is maximal with residue field ℝ. There are 2^ℵ₀ such points (the unit circle

- **Complex conjugate pairs:** For each conjugate pair {(a,b), (ā,b̄)} ⊂ ℂ²\ℝ² wi
th a²+b²=1, the kernel of evaluation ℝ[X,Y] → ℂ gives a maximal ideal with resid
ue field ℂ. There are 2^ℵ₀ such pairs.

Both types contribute 2^ℵ₀ maximal ideals, consistent with the bound argument ab
ove.
