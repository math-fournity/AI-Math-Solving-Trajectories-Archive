Claim

Yes. If $(M,\omega)$ is a symplectic manifold and $f:W\to M$ is an open
immersion, then $f^{*}\omega$ is a symplectic form on $W$.

Proof

Recall that a symplectic form on a smooth manifold is a 2-form that is (i)
closed and (ii) non-degenerate. We verify both properties for $f^{*}\omega$.

Step 1. An open immersion forces $\dim W=\dim M$, and $df$ is everywhere an
isomorphism.

By definition, an open immersion $f:W\to M$ is an immersion (so $df_w:T_wW\to
T_{f(w)}M$ is injective for every $w\in W$, hence $\dim W\le \dim M$) that is a
homeomorphism from $W$ onto $f(W)$, with $f(W)$ open in $M$.

Suppose for contradiction that $\dim W<\dim M$. By the constant-rank theorem,
$f$ is locally equivalent to $$(x_1,\dots,x_k)\mapsto (x_1,\dots,x_k,0,\dots,0),
\qquad k=\dim W<\dim M.$$ The local image is a $k$-dimensional slice, which has
empty interior in $M$. Hence $f(W)$ has empty interior and cannot be open —
contradiction. Therefore $\dim W=\dim M$.

Since $df_w$ is injective and the source and target have equal (finite)
dimension, $df_w$ is a linear isomorphism $T_wW\cong T_{f(w)}M$ for every $w\in
W$. In particular $f:W\to f(W)$ is a local diffeomorphism and a homeomorphism,
hence a diffeomorphism onto the open submanifold $f(W)\subseteq M$.

Step 2. $f^{*}\omega$ is closed.

This is immediate and does not even need the immersion hypothesis: $
$d(f^{}\omega)=f^{}(d\omega)=f^{*}(0)=0,$$ since $\omega$ is closed on $M$.

Step 3. $f^{*}\omega$ is non-degenerate.

Non-degeneracy is a pointwise condition. Fix $w\in W$ and let $p=f(w)$. We must
show the linear map $$\Phi_w:T_wW\longrightarrow T_w^{}W,\qquad v\longmapsto
(f^{}\omega)_w(v,,\cdot,)$$ is an isomorphism. Since $\dim W=\dim M$, the spaces
$T_wW$ and $T_w^{*}W$ have the same dimension, so it suffices to show $\Phi_w$
is injective.

Suppose $v\in T_wW$ with $\Phi_w(v)=0$, i.e. $(f^{}\omega)_w(v,u)=0$ for all
$u\in T_wW$. Unpacking the pullback, $
$0=(f^{}\omega)_w(v,u)=\omega_p\bigl(df_w(v),,df_w(u)\bigr)\qquad\forall,u\in
T_wW.$$ Because $df_w$ is an isomorphism (Step 1), as $u$ ranges over $T_wW$,
the vector $df_w(u)$ ranges over all of $T_pM$. Hence $$\omega_p\bigl(df_w(v),,
\xi\bigr)=0\qquad\forall,\xi\in T_pM.$$ Since $\omega_p$ is non-degenerate on
$T_pM$, this forces $df_w(v)=0$. But $df_w$ is injective, so $v=0$.

Thus $\Phi_w$ is injective, hence (equal dimensions) an isomorphism. This holds
for every $w\in W$, so $f^{*}\omega$ is non-degenerate.

Conclusion

$f^{*}\omega$ is a closed, non-degenerate 2-form on $W$, i.e. a symplectic form.
Equivalently, every open submanifold of a symplectic manifold inherits a
symplectic structure by restriction (pullback) of the ambient form. $
