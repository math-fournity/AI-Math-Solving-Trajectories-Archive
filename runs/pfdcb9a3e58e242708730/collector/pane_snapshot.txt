## Solution

We analyze the weakly nonlinear oscillator
$$\ddot{x}+\varepsilon(|x|-1)\dot{x}+x=0,\qquad 0<\varepsilon\ll 1,$$
using the Krylov–Bogoliubov averaging method.

### Polar (van der Pol) transformation

Set
$$x=r\cos\phi,\qquad \dot{x}=-r\sin\phi,$$
which is consistent with the linear part $\ddot{x}+x=0$ (frequency $=1$) to lead
ing order. Writing the equation as $\ddot{x}+x=-\varepsilon f$ with $f=(|x|-1)\d
ot{x}$, the exact polar equations are
$$\dot{r}=\varepsilon f\sin\phi,\qquad \dot{\phi}=1+\frac{\varepsilon}{r}f\cos\p
hi.$$
Substituting $x=r\cos\phi$, $\dot{x}=-r\sin\phi$:
$$f=(|r\cos\phi|-1)(-r\sin\phi)=r\sin\phi\,(1-|r\cos\phi|),$$
so
$$\boxed{\dot{r}=\varepsilon\,r\sin^{2}\!\phi\,(1-|r\cos\phi|),\qquad \dot{\phi}
=1+\varepsilon\,\sin\phi\cos\phi\,(1-|r\cos\phi|).}$$

### Averaging

Averaging over one period ($\phi:0\to 2\pi$) with $r$ held fixed:

**Radial part.**
$$\langle\sin^{2}\!\phi\rangle=\tfrac12,\qquad \langle|\cos\phi|\sin^{2}\!\phi\r
angle=\frac{1}{2\pi}\cdot4\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\!\cos\phi\,\sin^{2}\!\phi\,d\phi=\fra
c{1}{2\pi}\cdot\frac{4}{3}=\frac{2}{3\pi}.$$
Hence
$$\dot{r}=\varepsilon\,r\!\left(\frac12-\frac{2r}{3\pi}\right).$$

**Phase part.**
$$\langle\sin\phi\cos\phi\rangle=0,\qquad \langle|\cos\phi|\sin\phi\cos\phi\rang
le=0\;\;(\text{odd symmetry about }\phi=\pi/2),$$
so
$$\dot{\phi}=1+O(\varepsilon^{2}).$$

### Limit-cycle amplitude

Setting $\dot{r}=0$ (with $r>0$):
$$\frac12-\frac{2r}{3\pi}=0\;\Longrightarrow\; r_{0}=\frac{3\pi}{4}\approx 2.356
.$$

Stability: $\displaystyle\frac{d\dot{r}}{dr}\bigg|_{r_{0}}=\varepsilon\!\left(\f
rac12-\frac{4r_{0}}{3\pi}\right)=\varepsilon\!\left(\frac12-1\right)=-\frac{\var
epsilon}{2}<0$, so the cycle is **stable**.

$$\boxed{a=\dfrac{3\pi}{4}\approx 2.36.}$$

### Period

Since $\dot{\phi}=1$ to $O(\varepsilon)$ (the first-order frequency correction v
anishes),
$$\boxed{T=2\pi+O(\varepsilon^{2})\approx 2\pi.}$$

### Polar equations for phase paths near the limit cycle

Eliminating $t$ via $d\phi/dt=1$ (to leading order), the phase-path equation in
the $(r,\phi)$ plane is
$$\boxed{\frac{dr}{d\phi}=\varepsilon\,r\!\left(\frac12-\frac{2r}{3\pi}\right),\
qquad \phi=t+\text{const},}$$
or, equivalently, the averaged system
$$\boxed{\dot{r}=\varepsilon\,r\!\left(\frac12-\frac{2r}{3\pi}\right),\qquad \do
t{\phi}=1.}$$

Near $r_{0}=3\pi/4$, writing $r=r_{0}+\rho$ with $|\rho|\ll 1$:
$$\dot{\rho}\approx -\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\,\rho,$$
so perturbations decay as $\rho\sim e^{-\varepsilon t/2}$, confirming the stable
 limit cycle of amplitude $3\pi/4$ and period $2\pi$.
