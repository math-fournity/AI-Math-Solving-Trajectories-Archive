# Problem

An industrial laser-cutter is mounted on a cubic robotic frame with side lengths of exactly 1 meter. A sensor, used to calibrate the laser, is fixed at the precise midpoint of one of the frame's top edges. 

A single microscopic speck of dust, $P$, settles somewhere on the external surface of the 1-meter cube. The laser's guidance system calculates the distance $d(P)$ as the length of the absolute shortest path between the sensor and the speck, with the restriction that the path must travel entirely along the exterior faces of the cube (it cannot pass through the interior). 

Considering every possible location where the speck $P$ could land on the surface of the cube, find the maximum possible value of the square of this shortest path distance, $d(P)^2$.
