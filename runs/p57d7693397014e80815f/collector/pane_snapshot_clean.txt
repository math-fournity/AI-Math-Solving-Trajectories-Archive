## Equalizer of two morphisms $f, g: F(0) \to F(1)$

### Setup

Let $\mathscr{J}$ denote the **"walking parallel pair"** category: the small cat
egory with two objects $0, 1$ and, besides the identities, exactly two non-ident
ity morphisms $\alpha, \beta : 0 \to 1$, subject to no relations. A functor $F :
 \mathscr{J} \to \mathscr{D}$ amounts to specifying two objects $F(0), F(1)$ in
$\mathscr{D}$ and two parallel morphisms
$$f := F(\alpha),\quad g := F(\beta) : F(0) \longrightarrow F(1).$$

The **equalizer** of $f$ and $g$ is, by definition, the **limit of the diagram**
 $F : \mathscr{J} \to \mathscr{D}$.

### Definition

The equalizer of $f, g : F(0) \to F(1)$ is a pair $(E, e)$ consisting of

- an object $E \in \mathscr{D}$, and
- a morphism $e : E \to F(0)$ in $\mathscr{D}$,

satisfying the **equalizing condition**
$$f \circ e \;=\; g \circ e : E \longrightarrow F(1).$$

### Universal property

For every object $X \in \mathscr{D}$ and every morphism $h : X \to F(0)$ with
$$f \circ h \;=\; g \circ h,$$
there exists a **unique** morphism $h' : X \to E$ such that the following diagra
m commutes:

$$e \circ h' \;=\; h.$$

Equivalently, the map
$$\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathscr{D}}(X,\, E) \;\longrightarrow\; \{\, h \in \mathrm{Hom}
_{\mathscr{D}}(X,\, F(0)) \mid f \circ h = g \circ h \,\}, \qquad h' \;\longmaps
to\; e \circ h'$$
is a **bijection**, natural in $X$.

### Verification that this is the limit of $F : \mathscr{J} \to \mathscr{D}$

A cone over $F$ with apex $X$ is a pair of morphisms $(x_0 : X \to F(0),\; x_1 :
 X \to F(1))$ commuting with both arrows of the diagram:
$$f \circ x_0 = x_1 = g \circ x_0.$$
The condition $f \circ x_0 = g \circ x_0$ forces $x_1 = f \circ x_0$, so the con
e is **entirely determined** by $x_0 = h : X \to F(0)$ subject to $f \circ h = g
 \circ h$. Hence cones over $F$ with apex $X$ are in natural bijection with morp
hisms $h : X \to F(0)$ equalizing $f$ and $g$.

The limit cone is therefore a universal such $h$, i.e. a morphism $e : E \to F(0
)$ with $f \circ e = g \circ e$ through which every equalizing $h$ factors **uni
quely**. This is precisely the equalizer $(E, e)$ defined above. $\square$

### Summary

The equalizer of $f, g : F(0) \to F(1)$ is the limit of the parallel-pair diagra
m $F : \mathscr{J} \to \mathscr{D}$: an object $E$ equipped with a morphism $e :
 E \to F(0)$ satisfying $f \circ e = g \circ e$, universal among all such morphi
sms in that every $h : X \to F(0)$ with $f \circ h = g \circ h$ factors uniquely
 through $e$.
