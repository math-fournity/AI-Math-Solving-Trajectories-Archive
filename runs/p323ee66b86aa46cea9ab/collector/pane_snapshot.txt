## Proof

Let me set up coordinates. Place $A$ at the origin, and let $\vec{AB} = \mathbf{
b}$, $\vec{AD} = \mathbf{d}$. Then since $ABCD$ is a parallelogram, $C = \mathbf
{b} + \mathbf{d}$, and the area of $ABCD$ is $|\det(\mathbf{b}, \mathbf{d})|$.

**Locating $M$.** Since $M$ lies on diagonal $BD$ with $MD = 3\,BM$, point $M$ d
ivides $\overline{BD}$ in ratio $BM:MD = 1:3$, so
$$M = \tfrac{3}{4}B + \tfrac{1}{4}D = \tfrac{3\mathbf{b}+\mathbf{d}}{4}.$$

**Locating $N$.** The line through $A$ (origin) and $M$ is $\{tM : t \in \mathbb
{R}\} = \{t(3\mathbf{b}+\mathbf{d})/4\}$. The side $BC$ is $\{B + s\mathbf{d} :
s \in [0,1]\} = \{\mathbf{b}+s\mathbf{d}\}$. Setting these equal:
$$\tfrac{t}{4}(3\mathbf{b}+\mathbf{d}) = \mathbf{b} + s\mathbf{d}.$$
Comparing coefficients of $\mathbf{b}$ and $\mathbf{d}$: $\tfrac{3t}{4}=1 \Right
arrow t=\tfrac{4}{3}$, and $\tfrac{t}{4}=s \Rightarrow s=\tfrac{1}{3}$. Thus
$$N = \mathbf{b} + \tfrac{1}{3}\mathbf{d}, \qquad s=\tfrac{1}{3}\in[0,1]\ \check
mark.$$

**Computing the area of $\triangle MND$.**
$$N - M = \mathbf{b}+\tfrac{1}{3}\mathbf{d} - \tfrac{3\mathbf{b}+\mathbf{d}}{4}
= \tfrac{1}{4}\mathbf{b}+\tfrac{1}{12}\mathbf{d} = \tfrac{1}{4}\!\left(\mathbf{b
}+\tfrac{1}{3}\mathbf{d}\right),$$
$$D - M = \mathbf{d} - \tfrac{3\mathbf{b}+\mathbf{d}}{4} = \tfrac{3}{4}(\mathbf{
d}-\mathbf{b}).$$

The signed area is $\tfrac{1}{2}\det(N-M,\, D-M)$:
$$\det(N-M,\,D-M) = \tfrac{1}{4}\cdot\tfrac{3}{4}\,\det\!\left(\mathbf{b}+\tfrac
{1}{3}\mathbf{d},\ \mathbf{d}-\mathbf{b}\right).$$

Expanding the determinant (using $\det(\mathbf{b},\mathbf{b})=\det(\mathbf{d},\m
athbf{d})=0$ and $\det(\mathbf{d},\mathbf{b})=-\det(\mathbf{b},\mathbf{d})$):
$$\det\!\left(\mathbf{b}+\tfrac{1}{3}\mathbf{d},\ \mathbf{d}-\mathbf{b}\right) =
 \det(\mathbf{b},\mathbf{d}) - 0 + 0 - \tfrac{1}{3}\det(\mathbf{d},\mathbf{b}) =
 \det(\mathbf{b},\mathbf{d}) + \tfrac{1}{3}\det(\mathbf{b},\mathbf{d}) = \tfrac{
4}{3}\det(\mathbf{b},\mathbf{d}).$$

Therefore
$$\det(N-M,\,D-M) = \tfrac{3}{16}\cdot\tfrac{4}{3}\det(\mathbf{b},\mathbf{d}) =
\tfrac{1}{4}\det(\mathbf{b},\mathbf{d}),$$

$$[\triangle MND] = \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\tfrac{1}{4}\,|\det(\mathbf{b},\mathbf{d})|
 = \tfrac{1}{8}\,|\det(\mathbf{b},\mathbf{d})|.$$

**Taking the ratio:**
$$\frac{[\triangle MND]}{[ABCD]} = \frac{\tfrac{1}{8}\,|\det(\mathbf{b},\mathbf{
d})|}{|\det(\mathbf{b},\mathbf{d})|} = \boxed{\tfrac{1}{8}}.$$
