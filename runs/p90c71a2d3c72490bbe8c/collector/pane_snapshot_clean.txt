## Proof

**Claim:** The set of all $n \times n$ symmetric copositive matrices is convex.

**Definition.** A symmetric matrix $A \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ is *copositiv
e* if
$$x^T A x \geq 0 \quad \text{for all } x \in \mathbb{R}^n \text{ with } x \geq 0
.$$

Let $\mathcal{C}_n$ denote the set of all $n \times n$ symmetric copositive matr
ices. We show $\mathcal{C}_n$ is convex, i.e., for any $A, B \in \mathcal{C}_n$
and any $\lambda \in [0,1]$, the matrix
$$M = \lambda A + (1-\lambda) B$$
also belongs to $\mathcal{C}_n$.

**Verification.** Since $A$ and $B$ are symmetric, $M$ is symmetric (a convex co
mbination of symmetric matrices is symmetric). Now take any $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$
 with $x \geq 0$. Then:

$$x^T M x = x^T\bigl(\lambda A + (1-\lambda)B\bigr)x = \lambda\, x^T A x + (1-\l
ambda)\, x^T B x.$$

By copositivity of $A$: $x^T A x \geq 0$. By copositivity of $B$: $x^T B x \geq
0$. Since $\lambda \in [0,1]$, both $\lambda \geq 0$ and $(1-\lambda) \geq 0$, s
o each term is non-negative:

$$x^T M x = \underbrace{\lambda\, x^T A x}_{\geq\, 0} + \underbrace{(1-\lambda)\
, x^T B x}_{\geq\, 0} \geq 0.$$

This holds for every $x \geq 0$, so $M$ is copositive, i.e., $M \in \mathcal{C}_
n$.

**Conclusion.** $\mathcal{C}_n$ is closed under convex combinations, hence it is
 a convex set (indeed a convex cone, since it is also closed under non-negative
scaling: $A \in \mathcal{C}_n, \alpha \geq 0 \Rightarrow \alpha A \in \mathcal{C
}_n$).
