# Problem

In a remote industrial complex, an engineer manages a sequence of 15 chemical storage tanks. Each tank is initially assigned a specific pressure level corresponding to a unique integer value from 1 to 15 bar (so the tanks hold pressures of 1, 2, 3, ..., 15 bar).

The engineer follows a specific operational protocol: he selects any two tanks in the system, let their current pressure levels be $a$ and $b$. He then runs these through a pressure-equalization valve system that recalibrates them. One tank is adjusted to the Greatest Common Divisor of $a$ and $b$, while the other is adjusted to the Least Common Multiple of $a$ and $b$.

He repeats this recalibration process with different pairs of tanks until the set of pressure values in the 15 tanks becomes stable and can no longer be changed by any further recalibrations, regardless of which pair he selects.

Calculate the maximum possible total sum of the pressure levels in all 15 tanks once this stable state is achieved.
