$$f(z) = \frac{1}{2\pi i} \int_\Gamma \frac{f(\zeta)}{\zeta - z}\, d\zeta = \fra

Concretely, for each a ∈ K, choose r > 0 with D(a, r) ⊂ Ω. On the punctured-type
