## Proof

We compute the gradient (Fréchet derivative) of

$$f(M) = \operatorname{Tr}(M\log M - M), \qquad M \in \mathbb{S}^n_{++}.$$

**Step 1: Diagonalize.** Since $M$ is symmetric positive definite, write $M = V\
Lambda V^{T}$ with $\Lambda = \operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\dots,\lambda_n)$, $
\lambda_i>0$, and $V=[v_1\,\cdots\,v_n]$ orthogonal. By the spectral definition
of the matrix logarithm,

$$\log M = V\log(\Lambda)V^{T}, \qquad \log(\Lambda)=\operatorname{diag}(\log\la
mbda_1,\dots,\log\lambda_n).$$

Using cyclic invariance of the trace,

$$f(M) = \operatorname{Tr}\!\big(V(\Lambda\log\Lambda - \Lambda)V^{T}\big) = \op
eratorname{Tr}(\Lambda\log\Lambda - \Lambda) = \sum_{i=1}^{n}\big(\lambda_i\log\
lambda_i - \lambda_i\big) = \sum_{i=1}^{n}\varphi(\lambda_i),$$

where $\varphi(\lambda) = \lambda\log\lambda - \lambda$, so $\varphi'(\lambda) =
 \log\lambda + 1 - 1 = \log\lambda$.

**Step 2: First-order perturbation of eigenvalues.** Let $H\in\mathbb{S}^n$ be a
n arbitrary symmetric direction and consider $M(t)=M+tH$. For simple eigenvalues
, standard first-order perturbation theory gives

$$\dot\lambda_i(0) = v_i^{T} H\, v_i.$$

(The formula extends to the repeated case by continuity of $f$, which is a $C^1$
 function on $\mathbb{S}^n_{++}$.)

**Step 3: Directional derivative.** Differentiating $f(M(t))=\sum_i\varphi(\lamb
da_i(t))$ at $t=0$,

$$df_M[H] = \sum_{i=1}^{n}\varphi'(\lambda_i)\,\dot\lambda_i(0) = \sum_{i=1}^{n}
(\log\lambda_i)\,v_i^{T}H\,v_i.$$

**Step 4: Identify the gradient.** Since $\log M = \sum_i (\log\lambda_i)\,v_i v
_i^{T}$ is symmetric,

$$\operatorname{Tr}\!\big((\log M)\,H\big) = \sum_{i=1}^{n}(\log\lambda_i)\,v_i^
{T}H\,v_i = df_M[H].$$

By definition of the gradient on $\mathbb{S}^n$ (with the Frobenius inner produc
t $\langle A,B\rangle = \operatorname{Tr}(A^{T}B)$), we have $df_M[H] = \langle
\nabla f(M), H\rangle = \operatorname{Tr}(\nabla f(M)^{T}H)$. Matching with the
expression above and using symmetry of $\log M$,

$$\boxed{\;\nabla f(M) = \log M.\;}$$

**Interpretation.** This is the matrix analogue of the scalar fact $\frac{d}{dm}
(m\log m - m)=\log m$. The function $f(M)=\operatorname{Tr}(M\log M - M)$ is (up
 to sign) the von Neumann entropy / negative quantum relative entropy building b
lock, and its gradient being $\log M$ is the standard result used in quantum inf
ormation and convex optimization (it is the conjugate relationship underlying th
e log-determinant barrier and the entropy proximal operator).
