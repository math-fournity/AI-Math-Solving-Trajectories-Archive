# The Proof-Theoretic Ordinal of Primitive Recursive Arithmetic

## Answer

The proof-theoretic ordinal of Primitive Recursive Arithmetic (PRA) is **ε₀** (e
psilon-naught), the first fixed point of the ordinal exponential ω^α = α, i.e.,
the smallest ordinal satisfying ω^α = α.

## Proof

### 1. Preliminaries

**Primitive Recursive Arithmetic (PRA)** is the quantifier-free first-order theo
ry whose function symbols are those generated by the primitive recursive functio
ns (starting from zero, successor, projections, and closing under composition an
d primitive recursion), with induction restricted to quantifier-free formulas (e
quational induction).

**ε₀** is defined as the supremum of the sequence
$$\varepsilon_0 = \sup\{\omega, \omega^\omega, \omega^{\omega^\omega}, \omega^{\
omega^{\omega^\omega}}, \dots\} = \omega \uparrow\uparrow \omega,$$
equivalently the least ordinal α with ω^α = α.

The **proof-theoretic ordinal** |T| of a theory T is the least ordinal α such th
at the well-ordering of type α (suitably represented) is not provably well-found
ed in T; equivalently, it is the supremum of the order types of primitive recurs
ive well-orderings whose well-foundedness is provable in T.

### 2. Upper Bound: PRA proves transfinite induction up to every α < ε₀

**Claim.** For every ordinal α < ε₀, PRA proves the scheme of transfinite induct
ion up to α for every quantifier-free formula φ:

$$\text{TI}(\alpha, \varphi): \quad \bigl[\forall n\,(\forall m <_\alpha n\, \va
rphi(m) \to \varphi(n))\bigr] \to \forall n\, \varphi(n).$$

**Proof of claim.** We proceed by induction on the Cantor normal form of α.

Every α < ε₀ has a Cantor normal form
$$\alpha = \omega^{\alpha_1} \cdot c_1 + \cdots + \omega^{\alpha_k} \cdot c_k, \
quad \alpha \geq \alpha_1 \geq \cdots \geq \alpha_k,\quad c_i \in \mathbb{N}\set
minus\{0\},$$
where each αᵢ < α (so αᵢ < ε₀ as well). The key structural facts:

1. **Base case (α = 0):** TI(0, φ) is trivial.

2. **Successor case (α = β+1):** TI(β+1, φ) reduces to TI(β, φ) plus one step of
 the induction hypothesis. PRA handles successor steps directly via its equation
al induction.

3. **Limit case — the critical step is the ordinal exponentiation α = ω^β.** Gen
tzen showed that TI(ω^β, φ) can be reduced to TI(β, φ') where φ' is a (quantifie
r-free, primitive-recursively obtained) formula derived from φ. Concretely, the
reduction uses the **Gentzen assignment** (a primitive recursive mapping assigni
ng to each n an ordinal < ω^β) and the **Hardy hierarchy / fast-growing hierarch
y** at level β. The reduction of TI(ω^{β+1}, φ) to TI(ω^β, φ') is primitive recu
rsive, and the reduction of TI(ω^λ, φ) for limit λ to TI(λ, φ') uses the fundame
ntal sequences for λ, which are primitive recursive.

Since every α < ε₀ is built from 0 by finitely many applications of successor, o
rdinal addition, and ordinal exponentiation with base ω, and since each reductio
n step is primitive recursive and available in PRA, a **metainduction** (externa
l, finitely iterated) shows:

$$\text{PRA} \vdash \text{TI}(\alpha, \varphi) \quad \text{for every } \alpha <
\varepsilon_0 \text{ and every quantifier-free } \varphi.$$

The crucial point is that ε₀ is **closed** under the operation α ↦ ω^α (it is a
fixed point), so the metainduction never "reaches" ε₀ — it stops strictly below.
 For each *concrete* α < ε₀, the derivation is finite and lives entirely within
PRA.

### 3. Lower Bound: PRA does not prove TI(ε₀)

**Claim.** PRA does not prove transfinite induction up to ε₀ (even for a single,
 fixed quantifier-free formula representing the well-ordering of type ε₀).

**Proof.** Suppose for contradiction that PRA ⊢ TI(ε₀, φ) for the canonical prim
itive recursive well-ordering ≺ of type ε₀ (given by Cantor normal form comparis
on, which is primitive recursive).

By Gentzen's consistency proof methodology, TI(ε₀) suffices to prove the **consi
stency of PRA**: every PRA-proof can be assigned an ordinal < ε₀ (via the Gentze
n reduction / ordinal assignment on proof trees), and a cut-elimination / ordina
l-descent argument shows that no PRA-proof can be a proof of a contradiction, be
cause that would yield an infinite strictly descending sequence of ordinals belo
w ε₀, contradicting TI(ε₀).

Formally: if PRA ⊢ TI(ε₀), then by formalizing the Gentzen reduction inside PRA
(the reduction is primitive recursive), PRA would prove **Con(PRA)** — the consi
stency statement for PRA.

But by **Gödel's second incompleteness theorem** (applicable since PRA is suffic
iently strong, consistent, and recursively axiomatizable, and can represent prim
itive recursive predicates):

$$\text{PRA} \nvdash \text{Con(PRA)}.$$

This is a contradiction. Therefore:

$$\text{PRA} \nvdash \text{TI}(\varepsilon_0).$$

### 4. Combining the bounds

From Section 2: PRA proves TI(α) for every α < ε₀, so |PRA| ≥ ε₀ (every primitiv
e recursive well-ordering of type < ε₀ has its well-foundedness provable in PRA
— in fact the canonical one of each type does, and the supremum is ε₀).

From Section 3: PRA does not prove TI(ε₀), so |PRA| ≤ ε₀ (there is a primitive r
ecursive well-ordering of type ε₀ whose well-foundedness is not provable in PRA)
.

Therefore:

$$|\text{PRA}| = \varepsilon_0.$$

### 5. Remarks on robustness of the result

This result is robust across several equivalent formulations of PRA:

- **Quantifier-free PRA** (Skolem's original formulation, with equational induct
ion) and **first-order PRA** (with induction for all quantifier-free formulas) h
ave the same proof-theoretic ordinal ε₀, since the quantifier-free fragment alre
ady captures the full strength.

- The result is due to **Gentzen (1943)**, who established ε₀ as the ordinal for
 Peeno Arithmetic (PA); since PRA and PA are **Π⁰₂-equiconsistent** (Paris–Harri
ngton / Parsons / Mints showed that PA is Π⁰₂-conservative over PRA, and convers
ely PRA is interpretable in PA), they share the same proof-theoretic ordinal. Mo
re directly, Gentzen's assignment maps PRA proofs to ordinals below ε₀, and the
argument in Section 3 applies to PRA itself.

- The **fast-growing hierarchy** provides a witness: the provably total recursiv
e functions of PRA are exactly those dominated by $F_\alpha$ for some α < ε₀ (wh
ere F_α is the fast-growing function at level α). The slowest-growing function n
ot provably total in PRA is $F_{\varepsilon_0}$, confirming |PRA| = ε₀ from the
function-class perspective as well.

## Conclusion

The proof-theoretic ordinal of Primitive Recursive Arithmetic is **ε₀**, the fir
st epsilon number:

$$\boxed{|\text{PRA}| = \varepsilon_0}$$

established by the two-sided argument: (1) PRA proves transfinite induction for
every ordinal strictly below ε₀ (via Gentzen's primitive-recursive ordinal reduc
tions and the closure of ε₀ under ω^α), and (2) PRA cannot prove transfinite ind
uction up to ε₀ itself, because doing so would yield a proof of Con(PRA) within
PRA, contradicting Gödel's second incompleteness theorem.
