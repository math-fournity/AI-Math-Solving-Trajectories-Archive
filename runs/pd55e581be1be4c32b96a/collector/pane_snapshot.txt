Using product-to-sum formulas:
$$\cos x\cos y = \tfrac{1}{2}(\cos u + \cos v), \qquad \sin x\sin y = \tfrac{1}{
2}(\cos u - \cos v).$$

Therefore:
$$A = \frac{1+k}{2}\cos u + \frac{1-k}{2}\cos v,$$

which is a convex combination of $\cos u$ and $\cos v$ (since $\frac{1+k}{2}, \f

$$1 - A = \frac{1+k}{2}(1-\cos u) + \frac{1-k}{2}(1-\cos v) \geq 0,$$

with equality only at $(u,v)=(0,0)$, i.e., $(x,y)=(0,0)$.

$$\sin^2 x + \sin^2 y = 1 - \cos u\cos v, \qquad 2\sin x\sin y = \cos u - \cos v

$$D = 1 - \cos u\cos v - k(\cos u - \cos v).$$

Computing $2(1-A) - D$:
$$2(1-A) = (1+k)(1-\cos u) + (1-k)(1-\cos v) = 2 - (1+k)\cos u - (1-k)\cos v,$$
$$2(1-A) - D = \bigl[2 - (1+k)\cos u - (1-k)\cos v\bigr] - \bigl[1 - \cos u\cos
v - k\cos u + k\cos v\bigr]$$
$$= 1 - \cos u - \cos v + \cos u\cos v = (1-\cos u)(1-\cos v).$$

Thus:
$$\boxed{D = 2(1-A) - (1-\cos u)(1-\cos v).}$$
