## Problem

Compute $\pi_1(S^3 \setminus \gamma)$, where $\gamma$ is a (round, unknotted) ci
rcle in $\mathbb{R}^3 \subset S^3 = \mathbb{R}^3 \cup \{\infty\}$.

## Proof

**Setup.** Identify $S^3 = \{(z_1,z_2)\in\mathbb{C}^2 : |z_1|^2+|z_2|^2=1\}$. Up
 to homeomorphism of $S^3$ (which preserves the fundamental group of the complem
ent), we may take $\gamma$ to be the standard unknotted circle
$$\gamma = \{(z_1,0)\in S^3 : |z_1|=1\}.$$
A round circle in $\mathbb{R}^3$ is the unknot, and any two unknots in $S^3$ are
 ambiently isotopic, so this choice loses no generality.

**Step 1: Tubular neighborhood.** For small $\varepsilon>0$, the set
$$N(\gamma)=\{(z_1,z_2)\in S^3 : |z_2|\le \varepsilon\}$$
is a closed tubular neighborhood of $\gamma$, homeomorphic to $S^1\times D^2$ (c
oordinate $z_1$ gives $S^1$, and $z_2\in\{|z_2|\le\varepsilon\}$ gives $D^2$). I
ts boundary is the torus
$$\partial N(\gamma)=\{(z_1,z_2)\in S^3:|z_2|=\varepsilon\}.$$

**Step 2: The exterior is a solid torus.** Consider the exterior
$$E = S^3\setminus \operatorname{int}N(\gamma)=\{(z_1,z_2)\in S^3 : |z_2|\ge \va
repsilon\}.$$
Set $\rho=|z_1|=\sqrt{1-|z_2|^2}\in[0,\sqrt{1-\varepsilon^2}]$, $\theta=\arg z_1
\in S^1$, $\phi=\arg z_2\in S^1$. Then
$$E = \{(\rho,\theta,\phi): \rho\in[0,\sqrt{1-\varepsilon^2}],\;\theta\in S^1,\;
\phi\in S^1\},$$
with the convention that when $\rho=0$ (i.e. $|z_2|=1$, $z_1=0$) the angle $\the
ta$ is degenerate. Thus $E\cong D^2\times S^1$, a solid torus, where $D^2$ has p
olar coordinates $(\rho,\theta)$ and $S^1$ has coordinate $\phi$.

**Step 3: Deformation retraction.** We have $S^3\setminus\gamma \supset E$, and
the difference is
$$\operatorname{int}N(\gamma)\setminus\gamma \;\cong\; (S^1\times D^2)\setminus(
S^1\times\{0\}) \;\cong\; S^1\times(D^2\setminus\{0\}).$$
The annulus $D^2\setminus\{0\}$ deformation retracts onto $S^1=\partial D^2$, so
 $S^1\times(D^2\setminus\{0\})$ deformation retracts onto $S^1\times S^1=\partia
l N(\gamma)$. Extending by the identity on $E$, this yields a deformation retrac
tion
$$S^3\setminus\gamma \;\simeq\; E \;\cong\; D^2\times S^1.$$

**Step 4: Fundamental group.** Since $D^2$ is contractible,
$$\pi_1(S^3\setminus\gamma)\;\cong\;\pi_1(D^2\times S^1)\;\cong\;\pi_1(D^2)\time
s\pi_1(S^1)\;\cong\;1\times\mathbb{Z}\;\cong\;\mathbb{Z}.$$

The generator is the meridian of $\gamma$ (a small loop linking $\gamma$ once),
which corresponds to the $S^1$ factor in $D^2\times S^1$ (the $\phi$-circle, i.e
. the core of the complementary solid torus). Geometrically, this is the loop th
at winds once around $\gamma$, consistent with the linking number being $1$.

## Answer

$$\boxed{\pi_1(S^3\setminus\gamma)\cong\mathbb{Z}.}$$
