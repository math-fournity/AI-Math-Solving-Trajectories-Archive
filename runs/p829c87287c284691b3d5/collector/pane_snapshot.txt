## Solution

**Step 1: Identify curve C.**

$M(-1,0)$, $N(1,0)$, so $|MN|=2$. The perimeter of $\triangle MNQ$ is $6$, hence
 $|MQ|+|NQ| = 6-2 = 4$. By definition, $Q$ traces an **ellipse** with foci $M,N$
, semi-major axis $a=2$, focal distance $c=1$, and $b^2 = a^2-c^2 = 3$:

$$C:\ \frac{x^2}{4}+\frac{y^2}{3}=1.$$

**Step 2: The chord of contact (polar) $AB$.**

Let $P=(2\cos\theta,\,2\sin\theta)$ on $x^2+y^2=4$, with $\sin\theta\neq 0$. For
 the ellipse $\frac{x^2}{4}+\frac{y^2}{3}=1$, the polar (chord of contact) of $P
=(x_0,y_0)$ is

$$\frac{x\,x_0}{4}+\frac{y\,y_0}{3}=1.$$

Substituting $P$:

$$\frac{\cos\theta}{2}\,x + \frac{2\sin\theta}{3}\,y = 1. \tag{*}$$

This is the line $AB$.

**Step 3: Distance from $O$ to line $AB$.**

$$d = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\frac{\cos^2\theta}{4}+\frac{4\sin^2\theta}{9}}}.$$

Let $t=\sin^2\theta$. Then $\cos^2\theta=1-t$ and

$$\frac{\cos^2\theta}{4}+\frac{4\sin^2\theta}{9}=\frac{1-t}{4}+\frac{4t}{9}=\fra
c{9+7t}{36},$$

so $d = \dfrac{6}{\sqrt{9+7t}}$.

**Step 4: Length of chord $AB$.**

Parametrize the ellipse as $(2\cos\varphi,\,\sqrt3\sin\varphi)$. Substituting in
to $(*)$:

$$\cos\theta\cos\varphi + \tfrac{2\sqrt3}{3}\sin\theta\sin\varphi = 1.$$

Set $R=\sqrt{\cos^2\theta+\tfrac{4}{3}\sin^2\theta}=\sqrt{1+\tfrac{t}{3}}=\sqrt{
\tfrac{3+t}{3}}$, and write $\cos\theta=R\cos\psi$, $\tfrac{2\sqrt3}{3}\sin\thet
a=R\sin\psi$. Then $R\cos(\varphi-\psi)=1$, giving $\varphi=\psi\pm\delta$ with
$\cos\delta=1/R$.

Using $\cos(\psi+\delta)-\cos(\psi-\delta)=-2\sin\psi\sin\delta$ and $\sin(\psi+
\delta)-\sin(\psi-\delta)=2\cos\psi\sin\delta$:

$$|AB|^2 = 4\cdot 4\sin^2\psi\sin^2\delta + 3\cdot 4\cos^2\psi\sin^2\delta = 4\s
in^2\delta\,(3+\sin^2\psi).$$

Now $\sin^2\psi = \dfrac{\tfrac{4}{3}t}{1+\tfrac{t}{3}}=\dfrac{4t}{3+t}$, so $3+
\sin^2\psi = \dfrac{9+7t}{3+t}$. Also $\sin^2\delta = 1-\dfrac{1}{R^2}=\dfrac{t}
{3+t}$. Therefore

$$|AB|^2 = 4\cdot\frac{t}{3+t}\cdot\frac{9+7t}{3+t} = \frac{4t(9+7t)}{(3+t)^2},\
qquad |AB|=\frac{2\sin\theta\,\sqrt{9+7t}}{3+t}.$$

**Step 5: Area of $\triangle OAB$.**

$$S = \tfrac12|AB|\cdot d = \tfrac12\cdot\frac{2\sin\theta\,\sqrt{9+7t}}{3+t}\cd
ot\frac{6}{\sqrt{9+7t}} = \frac{6\sin\theta}{3+\sin^2\theta}.$$

**Step 6: Maximize.**

Let $u=\sin\theta\in(0,1]$. Then $f(u)=\dfrac{6u}{3+u^2}$ and

$$f'(u)=\frac{6(3-u^2)}{(3+u^2)^2}.$$

Since $f'(u)>0$ for $u\in(0,1]$ (as $3-u^2\ge 2>0$), $f$ is increasing on $(0,1]
$. The maximum is at $u=1$ ($\theta=\pi/2$):

$$S_{\max}=f(1)=\frac{6}{3+1}=\frac{3}{2}.$$

**Verification:** At $\theta=\pi/2$, $P=(0,2)$. The polar is $y=\tfrac32$, inter
secting the ellipse at $x^2/4+\tfrac34=1\Rightarrow x=\pm1$, so $A=(-1,\tfrac32)
,B=(1,\tfrac32)$. Then $|AB|=2$, $d=\tfrac32$, and $S=\tfrac12\cdot2\cdot\tfrac3
2=\tfrac32$. ✓

The maximum value of the area of $\triangle OAB$ is $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{2}}$.
