# Problem

A specialized industrial hopper is designed as a hollow vessel in the shape of a conical frustum. This vessel was originally modeled by taking a downward-pointing cone with a top rim radius of $12\text{ m}$ and slicing it horizontally so that the new circular floor of the hopper has a radius of $6\text{ m}$. The vertical distance between this floor and the top rim is exactly $10\text{ m}$.

Molten glass is being drained through a valve in the floor. At a specific moment during the drainage process, the depth of the glass (measured vertically from the floor of the hopper upward) is exactly $5\text{ m}$. At this same instant, sensors indicate that the depth of the glass is dropping at a constant rate of $3\text{ m/s}$. 

If the volume of the glass in the hopper is decreasing at a rate of $d\text{ m}^3/\text{s}$, compute $d$.
