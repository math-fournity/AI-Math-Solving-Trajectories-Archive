## Solution

**Setup.** We have a regular 2014-simplex with 2015 vertices $A_0, A_1, \ldots,
A_{2014}$ in $\mathbb{R}^{2014}$, with centroid/circumcenter $O = (0,0,\ldots,0)
$, circumradius $R = 1$ (since $|OA_i| = 1$), and $A_0 = (1,0,\ldots,0)$.

**Key identity.** For a regular simplex with centroid $O$ at the origin, the vec
tor sum of the vertices satisfies:
$$\sum_{i=0}^{n} \vec{OA_i} = \vec{0}$$
since the centroid is at the origin. For any point $P$:
$$\sum_{i=0}^{n} |PA_i|^2 = \sum_{i=0}^{n} |\vec{P} - \vec{A_i}|^2 = \sum_{i=0}^
{n}\left(|\vec{P}|^2 - 2\vec{P}\cdot\vec{A_i} + |\vec{A_i}|^2\right)$$
$$= (n+1)|\vec{P}|^2 - 2\vec{P}\cdot\sum_{i=0}^{n}\vec{A_i} + (n+1)R^2 = (n+1)\l
eft(|PO|^2 + R^2\right)$$

Here $n = 2014$, so $n+1 = 2015$ and $R = 1$:
$$\sum_{i=0}^{2014} PA_i^2 = 2015\left(|PO|^2 + 1\right)$$

**Computing $|PO|^2$.** The point $P = (20, 14, 20, 14, \ldots, 20, 14)$ lives i
n $\mathbb{R}^{2014}$, with 2014 coordinates forming $2014/2 = 1007$ pairs of $(
20, 14)$:
$$|PO|^2 = 1007 \times (20^2 + 14^2) = 1007 \times (400 + 196) = 1007 \times 596
$$
$$= 1000 \times 596 + 7 \times 596 = 596000 + 4172 = 600172$$

**Final computation.**
$$\sum_{i=0}^{2014} PA_i^2 = 2015 \times (600172 + 1) = 2015 \times 600173$$
$$= 2000 \times 600173 + 15 \times 600173 = 1200346000 + 9002595 = 1209348595$$

**Remainder modulo $10^6$:**
$$1209348595 \mod 10^6 = 348595$$

The remainder is $\boxed{348595}$.
