## Proof

**Setup.** Let $m \neq 0$ be square-free. We seek the natural density of rationa
l primes $p$ (excluding the finitely many ramified primes, which have density $0
$) satisfying:
1. $p$ splits completely in $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{m})$,
2. $p$ is inert in $\mathbb{Q}(2^{1/3})$.

We apply the Chebotarev density theorem to the compositum of the relevant Galois
 closures.

**Galois closure of $\mathbb{Q}(2^{1/3})$.** The splitting field of $x^3 - 2$ is
 $L = \mathbb{Q}(2^{1/3}, \omega)$, where $\omega = e^{2\pi i/3}$. This is a deg
ree-$6$ Galois extension with $\operatorname{Gal}(L/\mathbb{Q}) \cong S_3$, acti
ng on the three roots $\{2^{1/3}, \omega\, 2^{1/3}, \omega^2\, 2^{1/3}\}$.

For an unramified prime $p$ (i.e., $p \neq 2, 3$), the Frobenius conjugacy class
 $\operatorname{Frob}_p \in S_3$ determines the splitting type in $\mathbb{Q}(2^
{1/3})$:
- **Identity** (cycle type $1{,}1{,}1$): $p$ splits completely.
- **Transposition** (cycle type $2{,}1$): $p$ has one degree-$1$ and one degree-
$2$ prime.
- **3-cycle** (cycle type $3$): $p$ is **inert**.

Thus $p$ is inert in $\mathbb{Q}(2^{1/3})$ iff $\operatorname{Frob}_p$ is a 3-cy
cle. There are $2$ such elements in $S_3$ (out of $6$), giving inert density $2/
6 = 1/3$.

**Quadratic subfield of $L$.** The discriminant of $x^3 - 2$ is $-108 = -4 \cdot
 27$. The unique quadratic subfield of $L$ (fixed field of $A_3 \trianglelefteq
S_3$) is $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-108}) = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$.

**Case 1: $m \neq -3$.** Then $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{m}) \neq \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$,
 so $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{m})$ is not a subfield of $L$. Since both $\mathbb{Q}(\sqr
t{m})$ and $L$ are Galois over $\mathbb{Q}$, their intersection is $\mathbb{Q}$,
 hence they are **linearly disjoint**. The compositum $K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{m})
\cdot L$ has

$$\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q}) \cong \operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{m})
/\mathbb{Q}) \times \operatorname{Gal}(L/\mathbb{Q}) \cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z
} \times S_3,$$

of order $12$. The Frobenius in $K$ projects independently to each factor:

- **Condition 1** (split completely in $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{m})$): $\operatorname{F
rob}_p$ is trivial in the $\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$ factor — $1$ out of $2$ choic
es.
- **Condition 2** (inert in $\mathbb{Q}(2^{1/3})$): $\operatorname{Frob}_p$ is a
 3-cycle in the $S_3$ factor — $2$ out of $6$ choices.

By independence in the direct product, the number of compatible elements is $1 \
times 2 = 2$ out of $12$. By Chebotarev:

$$\text{density} = \frac{2}{12} = \boxed{\frac{1}{6}}.$$

**Case 2: $m = -3$.** Then $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{m}) = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3}) \subset
 L$, so $K = L$ and $\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q}) = S_3$.

- **Condition 1** (split completely in $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$): $\operatorname{
Frob}_p \in A_3$ (the subgroup fixing $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$), i.e., $\operator
name{Frob}_p$ is an even permutation — $\{e, (123), (132)\}$, $3$ out of $6$ ele
ments.
- **Condition 2** (inert in $\mathbb{Q}(2^{1/3})$): $\operatorname{Frob}_p$ is a
 3-cycle — $\{(123), (132)\}$, $2$ out of $6$ elements.

Since every 3-cycle lies in $A_3$, Condition 2 **implies** Condition 1. The join
t condition is simply "$\operatorname{Frob}_p$ is a 3-cycle," giving:

$$\text{density} = \frac{2}{6} = \boxed{\frac{1}{3}}.$$

**Ramified primes.** The finitely many primes ramifying in either extension (div
iding $\operatorname{disc}(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{m}))$ or equal to $2, 3$) form a set
 of density $0$ and do not affect the result.

**Conclusion.** By the Chebotarev density theorem:

$$\text{density} = \begin{cases} \dfrac{1}{3} & \text{if } m = -3, \\[6pt] \dfra
c{1}{6} & \text{if } m \neq -3 \text{ (and } m \neq 0 \text{ square-free).} \end
{cases}$$
