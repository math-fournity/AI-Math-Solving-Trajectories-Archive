## Proof

**Setup.** Since $2017$ is prime and $\{1,2,\ldots,2017\} \equiv \mathbb{F}_{201
7}$ as sets (mod $2017$), we work in $\mathbb{F}_{2017}$. Define coefficients $c
_0 = c_1 = 1$, $c_i = i$ for $2 \le i \le 63$. Note
$$\sum_{i=0}^{63} c_i = 1 + 1 + 2 + 3 + \cdots + 63 = 1 + \frac{63\cdot 64}{2} =
 1 + 2016 = 2017 \equiv 0 \pmod{2017}.$$

**Character-sum expansion.** Let $\omega = e^{2\pi i/2017}$ and $N_s$ denote the
 count of ordered 64-tuples of distinct elements with $S \equiv s \pmod{2017}$.
Then:
$$N_0 = \frac{1}{p}\sum_{t=0}^{p-1} \sum_{\substack{y_0,\ldots,y_{63} \in \mathb
b{F}_p \\ \text{distinct}}} \omega^{t\sum c_i y_i}, \quad p = 2017.$$

The $t=0$ term gives $P(2017,64)/2017 = P(2016,63) = \frac{2016!}{1953!}$.

For $t \neq 0$, substituting $y_i \mapsto t^{-1}y_i$ (a bijection on distinct tu
ples) shows every such term equals:
$$Q := \sum_{\substack{y_0,\ldots,y_{63} \in \mathbb{F}_p \\ \text{distinct}}} \
omega^{\sum c_i y_i}.$$

Therefore $N_0 = P(2016,63) + \frac{2016\,Q}{2017}$.

**Computing $Q$ via Möbius inversion on the partition lattice.** Using the stand
ard identity:
$$Q = \sum_{\pi \in \Pi_{64}} \mu(\hat{0},\pi)\prod_{B \in \pi} S_B, \quad S_B =
 \sum_{y \in \mathbb{F}_p} \omega^{(\sum_{i\in B} c_i)\,y},$$
where $\mu(\hat{0},\pi) = \prod_{B\in\pi}(-1)^{|B|-1}(|B|-1)!$ is the Möbius fun
ction of the partition lattice.

By orthogonality of additive characters: $S_B = p$ if $\sum_{i\in B} c_i \equiv
0\pmod{p}$, and $S_B = 0$ otherwise.

**Key observation.** Every $c_i \ge 1$ and $\sum c_i = 2017$. For any **proper,
nonempty** subset $B \subsetneq \{0,\ldots,63\}$:
$$1 \le \sum_{i\in B} c_i \le 2017 - 1 = 2016 < 2017,$$
so $\sum_{i\in B} c_i \not\equiv 0 \pmod{2017}$. Hence $S_B = 0$ for every prope
r block.

The **only** contributing partition is $\hat{1} = \{\{0,1,\ldots,63\}\}$ (the si
ngle block), for which $S_{\hat{1}} = p$ and $\mu(\hat{0},\hat{1}) = (-1)^{63}\c
dot 63! = -63!$.

Thus:
$$Q = (-63!)\cdot 2017 = -63!\cdot 2017.$$

**Final computation.**
$$N_0 = P(2016,63) + \frac{2016\cdot(-63!\cdot 2017)}{2017} = \frac{2016!}{1953!
} - 2016\cdot 63!.$$

**Equivalent form.** Factoring:
$$N_0 = 2016\cdot 62!\left(\binom{2015}{62} - 63\right),$$
since $\frac{2016!}{1953!} = 2016\cdot 62!\cdot\binom{2015}{62}$ and $2016\cdot
63! = 2016\cdot 62!\cdot 63$.

**Verification on a small case.** Take $p=5$, $n=3$, coefficients $(1,1,3)$ summ
ing to $5$. The formula gives $N_0 = P(4,2) + 4\cdot(-1)^2\cdot 2! = 12 + 8 = 20
$. Direct check: for each of the $20$ ordered pairs $(x_0,x_1)$ with $x_0\neq x_
1$, the value $x_2 \equiv 2(1-x_0-x_1)\pmod{5}$ is uniquely determined and one v
erifies $x_2 \neq x_0, x_2 \neq x_1$ always, giving $20$. ✓
