## Proof

Let $L$ be the number of levels per tower. Each level has 4 racks, so each tower
 contains $D = 4L$ racks. Racks are numbered sequentially across towers, so rack
 $n$ lies in tower $\lceil n/D \rceil$, at position $p = n - D(\lceil n/D\rceil
- 1)$ within that tower, and on level $\lceil p/4 \rceil$.

**Rack 83 is on the 5th level.** The 5th level of a tower holds positions $17, 1
8, 19, 20$. Hence
$$83 \bmod D \in \{17, 18, 19, 20\},$$
so $D$ divides one of $\{83-17,\, 83-18,\, 83-19,\, 83-20\} = \{66, 65, 64, 63\}
$.

**Rack 169 is on the 3rd level.** The 3rd level holds positions $9, 10, 11, 12$.
 Hence
$$169 \bmod D \in \{9, 10, 11, 12\},$$
so $D$ divides one of $\{169-9,\, 169-10,\, 169-11,\, 169-12\} = \{160, 159, 158
, 157\}$.

Thus $D = 4L$ is a common divisor of some element of $\{63, 64, 65, 66\}$ and so
me element of $\{157, 158, 159, 160\}$. Since rack 83 sits on the 5th level, we
need $L \ge 5$, i.e. $D \ge 20$.

Factorizations:
- $\{63, 64, 65, 66\} = \{3^2\!\cdot 7,\; 2^6,\; 5\!\cdot 13,\; 2\!\cdot 3\!\cdo
t 11\}$
- $\{157, 158, 159, 160\} = \{157,\; 2\!\cdot 79,\; 3\!\cdot 53,\; 2^5\!\cdot 5\
}$

($157$ is prime.) Checking all pairs for common divisors $\ge 20$:

| | 157 | 158 | 159 | 160 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 63 | 1 | 1 | 3 | 1 |
| 64 | 1 | 2 | 1 | **32** |
| 65 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 5 |
| 66 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 2 |

The only common divisor $\ge 20$ is $D = 32$, giving $L = 8$.

**Verification.** With $D = 32$ (8 levels, 32 racks per tower):
- Rack 83: $83 = 2\cdot 32 + 19$, so tower 3, position 19, level $\lceil 19/4\rc

- Rack 169: $169 = 5\cdot 32 + 9$, so tower 6, position 9, level $\lceil 9/4\rce

Both conditions are satisfied, and no other value of $D \ge 20$ satisfies them.
