# Quadratic Casimir Operator of a Compact Lie Algebra

## Setup

Let $\mathfrak{g}$ be a compact Lie algebra (real, with negative-definite Killin
g form on its semisimple part). Choose a basis $\{T_a\}_{a=1}^{\dim\mathfrak{g}}
$ that is **orthonormal** with respect to the invariant inner product $\kappa$ (
for simple $\mathfrak{g}$, a multiple of the Killing form; for a direct sum of s
imples, a sum of such on each factor). Orthonormality means

$$\kappa(T_a, T_b) = \delta_{ab}.$$

Let $D:\mathfrak{g}\to\mathrm{End}(V_\lambda)$ be the irreducible representation
 of highest weight $\lambda$.

## Construction

The **quadratic Casimir operator** in the representation $D$ is defined by

$$\boxed{\,C_2^{(D)} \;=\; \sum_{a=1}^{\dim\mathfrak{g}} D(T_a)\,D(T_a)\,}$$

i.e. the sum of the squares of the representing matrices, taken in an orthonorma
l basis.

**Well-definedness (basis independence).** Under an orthogonal change of basis $
T'_a = R_{ab}T_b$ with $R\in O(\dim\mathfrak{g})$, the sum transforms as $\sum_a
 D(T'_a)^2 = \sum_{a,b,c}R_{ab}R_{ac}D(T_b)D(T_c)=\sum_b D(T_b)^2$, using $R^T R
=I$. So $C_2^{(D)}$ depends only on the inner product $\kappa$, not on the ortho
normal basis chosen.

**Centrality.** For every $X\in\mathfrak{g}$, using $[D(X),D(T_a)]=D([X,T_a])=\s
um_b f_{Xa}^{\;\;b}D(T_b)$ (structure constants $f$) and the invariance $\kappa(
[X,T_a],T_b)+\kappa(T_a,[X,T_b])=0$, which gives $f_{Xa}^{\;\;b}=-f_{Xb}^{\;\;a}
$ (antisymmetry in $a,b$), we get

$$[D(X),C_2^{(D)}]=\sum_a\bigl(D([X,T_a])D(T_a)+D(T_a)D([X,T_a])\bigr)=\sum_{a,b
}f_{Xa}^{\;\;b}\bigl(D(T_b)D(T_a)+D(T_a)D(T_b)\bigr).$$

The expression in parentheses is symmetric in $a,b$ while $f_{Xa}^{\;\;b}$ is an
tisymmetric, so the contraction vanishes. Hence $C_2^{(D)}$ is central: it commu
tes with every $D(X)$.

## Eigenvalue

By Schur's lemma, a central operator on an irreducible representation is a scala
r. So there exists a constant $C_2(\lambda)$ with

$$C_2^{(D)} = C_2(\lambda)\cdot I_{V_\lambda}.$$

To compute it, evaluate on a **highest-weight vector** $v_\lambda$ (annihilated
by all positive-root generators $E_\alpha$, with $H v_\lambda=\lambda(H)v_\lambd
a$ for $H$ in the Cartan). Split the basis into Cartan generators $\{H_i\}_{i=1}
^r$ (orthonormal) and root generators $\{E_\alpha,E_{-\alpha}\}$ normalized so $
\kappa(E_\alpha,E_{-\alpha})=1$. Then

$$C_2^{(D)}v_\lambda=\Bigl(\sum_i D(H_i)^2+\sum_{\alpha>0}\bigl(D(E_\alpha)D(E_\
alpha)+D(E_{-\alpha})D(E_{-\alpha})\bigr)\Bigr)v_\lambda.$$

The $D(E_\alpha)v_\lambda=0$ terms vanish. For the paired term, using $[D(E_\alp
ha),D(E_{-\alpha})]=D(H_\alpha)=\alpha(H)$ on $v_\lambda$ (with $H_\alpha$ the c
oroot, $\kappa(H_\alpha,\cdot)=\alpha$), one obtains $D(E_\alpha)D(E_{-\alpha})v
_\lambda=\alpha(H)v_\lambda=(\alpha,\alpha)v_\lambda$ where the last equality us
es the standard identification. Summing the Cartan part $\sum_i\lambda(H_i)^2=(\
lambda,\lambda)$ and the root part $\sum_{\alpha>0}(\alpha,\alpha)$, and using t
he identity

$$(\lambda,\lambda)+\sum_{\alpha>0}(\alpha,\alpha)=\bigl(\lambda+\rho,\lambda+\r
ho\bigr)-(\rho,\rho),\qquad \rho=\tfrac12\sum_{\alpha>0}\alpha,$$

we arrive at the **Freudenthal–de Vries formula**:

$$\boxed{\,C_2(\lambda)\;=\;\bigl(\lambda+\rho,\,\lambda+\rho\bigr)\;-\;(\rho,\r
ho)\;=\;\bigl(\lambda,\lambda\bigr)+2(\lambda,\rho)\,}$$

where $\rho$ is the half-sum of positive roots and $(\cdot,\cdot)$ is the inner
product on the weight space induced by $\kappa$.

## Summary

- **Construction:** $C_2^{(D)}=\sum_{a=1}^{\dim\mathfrak{g}}D(T_a)^2$ in any $\k
appa$-orthonormal basis; it is basis-independent and central.
- **Eigenvalue on $V_\lambda$:** $\;C_2(\lambda)=(\lambda+\rho,\lambda+\rho)-(\r
ho,\rho)=(\lambda,\lambda)+2(\lambda,\rho)$, with $\rho$ the half-sum of positiv
e roots.
