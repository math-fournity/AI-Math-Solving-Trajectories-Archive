# Problem

A specialized logistics firm manages three distinct storage silos: Alpha, Beta, and Gamma. Collectively, these silos currently hold exactly 2021 kiloliters of a volatile chemical. Two technicians, Nina and Tadashi, are tasked with rebalancing the inventory through a series of shifts, with Nina acting first.

During a shift, a technician must choose one of the three silos and a positive integer volume $k$. They then transfer $k$ units into the chosen silo by extracting $k$ units from each of the other two silos. Due to safety protocols, a technician is prohibited from making a move if it would result in any silo containing a negative amount of the chemical. The technician who is unable to perform a valid move on their turn is dismissed.

Assuming both Nina and Tadashi perform with perfect mathematical strategy to avoid dismissal, find the number of initial distribution triples (Alpha, Beta, Gamma) such that Tadashi is guaranteed to win.
