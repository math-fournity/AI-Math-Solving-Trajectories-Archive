## Solution

We need to find positive integers $x, y$ satisfying the Pell equation $x^2 - 202
3y^2 = 1$.

**Step 1: Continued fraction of $\sqrt{2023}$.**

Since $44^2 = 1936$ and $45^2 = 2025 > 2023$, we have $a_0 = \lfloor\sqrt{2023}\
rfloor = 44$. Applying the standard algorithm $(m_{k+1} = a_k d_k - m_k,\ d_{k+1
} = (N - m_{k+1}^2)/d_k,\ a_{k+1} = \lfloor(a_0 + m_{k+1})/d_{k+1}\rfloor)$:

| $k$ | $m_k$ | $d_k$ | $a_k$ |
|-----|-------|-------|-------|
| 0 | 0 | 1 | 44 |
| 1 | 44 | 87 | 1 |
| 2 | 43 | 2 | 43 |
| 3 | 43 | 87 | 1 |
| 4 | 44 | 1 | 88 |

At $k=5$ we return to $(m,d)=(44,87)$, so the period is $\overline{(1, 43, 1, 88
)}$ with **period length 4** (even).

**Step 2: Fundamental solution from convergents.**

With even period $n=4$, the fundamental solution is $(p_3, q_3)$, the convergent
 at index $n-1=3$:

$$p_{-1}=1,\ p_0=44;\qquad q_{-1}=0,\ q_0=1.$$

$$p_1 = 1\cdot44+1 = 45,\quad q_1 = 1\cdot1+0 = 1.$$
$$p_2 = 43\cdot45+44 = 1979,\quad q_2 = 43\cdot1+1 = 44.$$
$$p_3 = 1\cdot1979+45 = 2024,\quad q_3 = 1\cdot44+1 = 45.$$

**Step 3: Verification.**

$$2024^2 = 4{,}096{,}576,$$
$$2023 \times 45^2 = 2023 \times 2025 = 4{,}096{,}575,$$
$$2024^2 - 2023\cdot 45^2 = 4{,}096{,}576 - 4{,}096{,}575 = 1.\ \checkmark$$

**Answer:** $\boxed{x = 2024,\ y = 45}$.
