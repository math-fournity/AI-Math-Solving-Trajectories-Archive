## Solution

We solve the integral equation
$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{y(u)\,du}{(x-u)^2+a^2}=\frac{1}{x^2+b^2},\qquad
0<a<b,$$
using the Fourier transform.

**Convention.** Define $\widehat{f}(\xi)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(x)\,e^{-ix\xi}
\,dx$ with inverse $f(x)=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\widehat{f}(\xi)\,
e^{ix\xi}\,d\xi$.

**Step 1: Identify the convolution.** The left-hand side is the convolution
$$(K*y)(x),\qquad K(x)=\frac{1}{x^{2}+a^{2}}.$$

**Step 2: Fourier transform of the kernel.** The standard residue/Laplace comput
ation gives
$$\widehat{K}(\xi)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{e^{-ix\xi}}{x^{2}+a^{2}}\,dx=\fr
ac{\pi}{a}\,e^{-a|\xi|}.$$

**Step 3: Transform the equation.** Convolution becomes multiplication, so
$$\widehat{K}(\xi)\,\widehat{y}(\xi)=\frac{\pi}{a}\,e^{-a|\xi|}\,\widehat{y}(\xi
)=\widehat{\left(\frac{1}{x^{2}+b^{2}}\right)}(\xi)=\frac{\pi}{b}\,e^{-b|\xi|}.$
$

Since $e^{-a|\xi|}>0$ for all $\xi$, we may divide:
$$\widehat{y}(\xi)=\frac{a}{b}\cdot\frac{e^{-b|\xi|}}{e^{-a|\xi|}}=\frac{a}{b}\,
e^{-(b-a)|\xi|}.$$

**Step 4: Invert.** Using the same transform pair in reverse,
$$\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-c|\xi|}\,e^{ix\xi}\,d\xi=\frac{c}{\pi
\bigl(x^{2}+c^{2}\bigr)},\qquad c>0,$$
with $c=b-a>0$ (guaranteed by $0<a<b$), we obtain
$$y(x)=\frac{a}{b}\cdot\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-(b-a)|\xi|}\,e^{
ix\xi}\,d\xi=\frac{a}{b}\cdot\frac{b-a}{\pi\bigl(x^{2}+(b-a)^{2}\bigr)}.$$

**Step 5: Verification.** With this $y$,
$$\widehat{K}(\xi)\,\widehat{y}(\xi)=\frac{\pi}{a}e^{-a|\xi|}\cdot\frac{a}{b}e^{
-(b-a)|\xi|}=\frac{\pi}{b}e^{-b|\xi|}=\widehat{\left(\frac{1}{x^{2}+b^{2}}\right
)}(\xi),$$
so the convolution recovers $\frac{1}{x^{2}+b^{2}}$, confirming the solution.

**Answer.**
$$\boxed{\,y(x)=\dfrac{a\,(b-a)}{\pi\,b\,\bigl(x^{2}+(b-a)^{2}\bigr)}\,}$$
