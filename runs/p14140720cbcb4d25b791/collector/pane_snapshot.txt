## Proof

Let $T = \text{ACF}_p$ be the complete theory of algebraically closed fields of
characteristic $p$ (where $p$ is a fixed prime, or $p=0$). We determine the numb
er of countable models of $T$ up to isomorphism.

**Key fact (classification of algebraically closed fields).** An algebraically c
losed field is determined up to isomorphism by its characteristic and its transc
endence degree over its prime subfield. This is a classical result: if $K, L$ ar
e algebraically closed fields of the same characteristic with $\operatorname{trd
eg}(K/\mathbb{F}_p) = \operatorname{trdeg}(L/\mathbb{F}_p)$, then $K \cong L$.

*Sketch:* Choose a transcendence basis $S_K$ of $K$ over $\mathbb{F}_p$ and $S_L
$ of $L$ over $\mathbb{F}_p$. Since $|S_K| = |S_L|$, there is a bijection $\varp
hi: S_K \to S_L$, which extends to an isomorphism $\mathbb{F}_p(S_K) \cong \math
bb{F}_p(S_L)$. Both $K$ and $L$ are the algebraic closures of these purely trans
cendental extensions, and algebraic closures are unique up to isomorphism fixing
 the base, so $K \cong L$. Conversely, distinct transcendence degrees give non-i
somorphic fields.

**Counting countable models.** A countable model $K \models \text{ACF}_p$ has $|
K| = \aleph_0$. The transcendence degree of $K$ over $\mathbb{F}_p$ satisfies:
$$\operatorname{trdeg}(K/\mathbb{F}_p) \leq |K| = \aleph_0.$$

Conversely, for any cardinal $\alpha$ with $0 \leq \alpha \leq \aleph_0$, there
exists a countable algebraically closed field of characteristic $p$ with transce
ndence degree $\alpha$ over $\mathbb{F}_p$: take the algebraic closure of $\math
bb{F}_p(S)$ where $S$ is a set of algebraically independent elements of size $\a
lpha$. When $\alpha < \aleph_0$, this is countable; when $\alpha = \aleph_0$, th
is is also countable (algebraic closure of a countable field is countable).

Therefore the countable models of $\text{ACF}_p$ are in bijection with the possi
ble transcendence degrees:
$$\{0, 1, 2, 3, \dots\} \cup \{\aleph_0\}.$$

This set has cardinality $\aleph_0$ (it is a countable set: the natural numbers
together with one additional point $\aleph_0$).

**Verification via categoricity.** $\text{ACF}_p$ is $\kappa$-categorical for ev
ery uncountable $\kappa$ (a single model of transcendence degree $\kappa$). For
$\kappa = \aleph_0$, categoricity fails precisely because the transcendence degr
ee can be any value in $\{0, 1, 2, \dots, \aleph_0\}$, giving $\aleph_0$ many no
n-isomorphic countable models. This is consistent with the general theory: a cou
ntable complete theory categorical in one uncountable cardinal has either $1$ or
 $\aleph_0$ countable models (it cannot have a finite number $> 1$, nor $2^{\ale
ph_0}$, by the Baldwin–Lachlan theorem). Here we exhibit $\aleph_0$ explicitly.
