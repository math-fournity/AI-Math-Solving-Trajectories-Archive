**Step 3. The scheme structure of $D$ and its reduction.** As a closed subscheme

$$D = \operatorname{Spec} \mathbb{C}[x,y,z]/(xy - z^2,\, x) = \operatorname{Spec

This is a non-reduced scheme supported on the $y$-axis $E := V(x,z)$. Its nilrad

$$D_{\mathrm{red}} = \operatorname{Spec} \mathbb{C}[y,z]/(z) = \operatorname{Spe
