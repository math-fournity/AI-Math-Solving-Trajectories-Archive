oundary $\partial\Omega$, and let $1 \leq p \leq \infty$. There exists a bounded

N)$ via zero extension.

**Proof.**

zero extension $\tilde{u} \in W^{1,p}(\mathbb{R}^N)$ by $\tilde{u}(x) = u(x)$ fo

rightarrow W^{1,p}(\mathbb{R}^N)$, $u \mapsto \tilde{u}$, is an isometric embedd

$$V := \iota\bigl(W_0^{1,p}(\Omega)\bigr) = \bigl\{v \in W^{1,p}(\mathbb{R}^N) :
 v = 0 \text{ a.e. on } \mathbb{R}^N \setminus \Omega\bigr\}$$

$$T: W^{1,p}(\Omega) \to W^{1-1/p,p}(\partial\Omega)$$

$$P_0 := I - L \circ T : W^{1,p}(\Omega) \to W^{1,p}(\Omega).$$
