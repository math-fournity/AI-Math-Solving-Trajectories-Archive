# Problem

In a futuristic data-management facility, a master server must store a specific volume of data, represented by a positive integer $N$. The storage protocols require that this data volume be partitioned in two distinct ways to ensure redundancy across different generations of hardware.

Under the "Legacy Protocol," the total volume $N$ must be divided into exactly $2002$ positive integer data packets such that every single packet has the exact same sum of its decimal digits.

Under the "Modern Protocol," the same total volume $N$ must be divided into exactly $2003$ positive integer data packets such that, again, every single packet has the exact same sum of its decimal digits (though this digit sum may differ from the one used in the Legacy Protocol).

Calculate the smallest possible natural number $N$ that can satisfy the requirements of both storage protocols simultaneously.
