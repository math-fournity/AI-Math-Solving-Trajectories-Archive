# Problem

In a remote territory, there is a high-security perimeter defined by a convex fence with exactly 10 vertices. At each vertex, a unique, glowing beacon is installed, and each beacon is labeled with a distinct, single letter from a 10-letter alphabet.

A drone is programmed to fly anywhere in the surrounding landscape (the plane outside the perimeter) to capture a "signal sequence." A sequence is recorded by looking toward the fence and listing the 10 letters as they appear in order from the leftmost point of the drone's field of vision to the rightmost point. To ensure data integrity, the drone refuses to record a sequence if any two beacons overlap from its current perspective (i.e., if one beacon is directly behind another, creating a line of sight containing two vertices).

The drone can move to any position outside the fence to find new perspectives. Each unique ordering of the 10 letters constitutes a distinct "signal sequence." For a fixed, convex 10-sided fence, what is the maximum possible number of these distinct 10-letter sequences that can be recorded?
