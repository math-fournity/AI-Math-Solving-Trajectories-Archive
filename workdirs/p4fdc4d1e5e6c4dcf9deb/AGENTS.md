# Problem

A shipping conglomerate is evaluating the performance of its 25 distinct cargo vessels over a three-stage transit mission. In each stage, every vessel is assigned a unique performance efficiency rating. Consequently, no two vessels ever receive the same rating within a single stage, nor do any two vessels have the same cumulative rating across all three stages combined.

One vessel, the *SS Kolya*, recorded the following performance rankings among the fleet:
- In the first stage, it achieved the 3rd highest efficiency rating.
- In the second stage, it achieved the 4th highest efficiency rating.
- In the third stage, it achieved the 5th highest efficiency rating.

The fleet’s final standings are determined by the sum of the efficiency ratings from all three stages, where a higher total sum results in a higher final rank. Based on this data, what is the lowest possible rank (where 1st is the highest and 25th is the lowest) that the *SS Kolya* could have occupied in the final fleet standings?
