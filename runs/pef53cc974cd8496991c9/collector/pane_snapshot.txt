**Lemma.** *For $D_m$ with $m$ odd, the normal subgroups are exactly the subgrou

$$r(sr^i)r^{-1} = sr^{i-2} \in N.$$

**Counting.** The group $\langle r \rangle$ is cyclic of order $p^n$, so it has
exactly one subgroup for each divisor of $p^n$. Since $p^n$ has $n+1$ divisors (
$1, p, p^2, \ldots, p^n$), $\langle r \rangle$ has $n+1$ subgroups. By the Lemma

Among these, the trivial subgroup $\{e\} = \langle r^{p^n}\rangle$ and $D_{p^n}$

$$\nu(D_{p^n}) = (n+2) - 2 = n.$$
