# Solution

## Setup

The equation is $y'''' - \lambda y = 0$ with $\lambda > 0$. Set $\lambda = \beta
^4$ ($\beta > 0$), so the characteristic equation $r^4 - \beta^4 = 0$ gives $r =
 \pm\beta,\ \pm i\beta$. The general solution is:

$$y(x) = A\cos(\beta x) + B\sin(\beta x) + C\cosh(\beta x) + D\sinh(\beta x)$$

---

## Case (a): Clamped–Clamped — $y(0)=y'(0)=0,\ y(L)=y'(L)=0$

**Applying $x=0$ conditions:**
- $y(0)=A+C=0 \Rightarrow C=-A$
- $y'(0)=\beta(B+D)=0 \Rightarrow D=-B$

So $y(x)=A[\cos\beta x-\cosh\beta x]+B[\sin\beta x-\sinh\beta x]$.

**Applying $x=L$ conditions** gives the system:
$$A[\cos\beta L-\cosh\beta L]+B[\sin\beta L-\sinh\beta L]=0$$
$$A[-\sin\beta L-\sinh\beta L]+B[\cos\beta L-\cosh\beta L]=0$$

**Eigenvalue equation** (determinant $=0$):

$$\boxed{\cos(\beta L)\cosh(\beta L)=1}$$

*Derivation:* The determinant is $(\cos\beta L-\cosh\beta L)^2+(\sin\beta L-\sin
h\beta L)(\sin\beta L+\sinh\beta L)$ $= \cos^2+\sin^2+\cosh^2-\sinh^2-2\cos\cosh
 = 2-2\cos\cosh$.

**Eigenfunctions:**

$$y_n(x)=\cosh(\beta_n x)-\cos(\beta_n x)-\sigma_n\bigl[\sinh(\beta_n x)-\sin(\b
eta_n x)\bigr]$$

where $\displaystyle\sigma_n=\frac{\cosh(\beta_n L)-\cos(\beta_n L)}{\sinh(\beta
_n L)-\sin(\beta_n L)}$.

**First two eigenvalues** (roots of $\cos u\cosh u=1$, $u=\beta L$, excluding $u
=0$):

| $n$ | $\beta_n L$ | $\lambda_n=\beta_n^4$ |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $4.7300$ | $500.56/L^4$ |
| 2 | $7.8532$ | $3803.5/L^4$ |

$$\boxed{\lambda_1\approx\frac{500.56}{L^4},\qquad \lambda_2\approx\frac{3803.5}
{L^4}}$$

---

## Case (b): Simply Supported–Simply Supported — $y(0)=y''(0)=0,\ y(L)=y''(L)=0$

**Applying $x=0$ conditions:**
- $y(0)=A+C=0\Rightarrow C=-A$
- $y''(0)=\beta^2(-A+C)=\beta^2(-2A)=0\Rightarrow A=0,\ C=0$

So $y(x)=B\sin(\beta x)+D\sinh(\beta x)$.

**Applying $x=L$ conditions:**
$$B\sin\beta L+D\sinh\beta L=0,\qquad -B\sin\beta L+D\sinh\beta L=0$$

Adding: $2D\sinh\beta L=0$. Since $\sinh\beta L\neq 0$ for $\beta L>0$, we get $
D=0$, then $\sin\beta L=0$.

**Eigenvalue equation:**

$$\boxed{\sin(\beta L)=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \beta_n L=n\pi,\ n=1,2,3,\dots
}$$

**Eigenfunctions:**

$$y_n(x)=\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right)$$

**Eigenvalues:**

$$\lambda_n=\frac{n^4\pi^4}{L^4}$$

$$\boxed{\lambda_1=\frac{\pi^4}{L^4}\approx\frac{97.409}{L^4},\qquad \lambda_2=\
frac{16\pi^4}{L^4}\approx\frac{1558.5}{L^4}}$$

---

## Case (c): Cantilevered (Clamped–Free) — $y(0)=y'(0)=0,\ y''(L)=y'''(L)=0$

**Applying $x=0$ (clamped) conditions** (same as case (a)):

$C=-A,\ D=-B$, so $y(x)=A[\cos\beta x-\cosh\beta x]+B[\sin\beta x-\sinh\beta x]$
.

**Applying $x=L$ (free) conditions:**
$$y''(L)=0:\ A[\cos\beta L+\cosh\beta L]+B[\sin\beta L+\sinh\beta L]=0$$
$$y'''(L)=0:\ A[-\sin\beta L+\sinh\beta L]+B[\cos\beta L+\cosh\beta L]=0$$

**Eigenvalue equation** (determinant $=0$):

$$\boxed{\cos(\beta L)\cosh(\beta L)=-1}$$

*Derivation:* The determinant is $(\cos\beta L+\cosh\beta L)^2-(\sin\beta L+\sin
h\beta L)(-\sin\beta L+\sinh\beta L)$ $= (\cos^2+\sin^2)+(\cosh^2-\sinh^2)+2\cos
\cosh = 2+2\cos\cosh$.

**Eigenfunctions:**

$$y_n(x)=\cosh(\beta_n x)-\cos(\beta_n x)-\sigma_n\bigl[\sinh(\beta_n x)-\sin(\b
eta_n x)\bigr]$$

where $\displaystyle\sigma_n=\frac{\cosh(\beta_n L)+\cos(\beta_n L)}{\sinh(\beta
_n L)+\sin(\beta_n L)}$.

**First two eigenvalues** (roots of $\cos u\cosh u=-1$, $u=\beta L$):

| $n$ | $\beta_n L$ | $\lambda_n=\beta_n^4$ |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $1.8751$ | $12.362/L^4$ |
| 2 | $4.6941$ | $485.52/L^4$ |

$$\boxed{\lambda_1\approx\frac{12.362}{L^4},\qquad \lambda_2\approx\frac{485.52}
{L^4}}$$

---

| Case | Eigenvalue equation | $\lambda_1$ | $\lambda_2$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| (a) Clamped–Clamped | $\cos\beta L\cosh\beta L=1$ | $500.56/L^4$ | $3803.5/L^4
$ |
| (b) Simply–Simply | $\sin\beta L=0$ ($\beta_nL=n\pi$) | $\pi^4/L^4\approx97.41
/L^4$ | $16\pi^4/L^4\approx1558.5/L^4$ |
| (c) Cantilever | $\cos\beta L\cosh\beta L=-1$ | $12.362/L^4$ | $485.52/L^4$ |
