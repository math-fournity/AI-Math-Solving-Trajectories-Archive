[i]$, the elements of norm $2^k$ are exactly $z = u(1+i)^k$ where $u \in \{1, i,
-1, -i\}$.

Proof: $N(u(1+i)^k) = |u|^2 \cdot |1+i|^{2k} = 1 \cdot 2^k = 2^k$. Conversely,
if $N(z) = 2^k$, then $z\bar{z} = 2^k = (-i)^k(1+i)^{2k}$, so $(1+i)^{2k} |
z\bar{z}$. Since $\mathbb{Z}[i]$ is a UFD and $1+i$ is prime (with $\bar{1+i} =
1-i = -i(1+i)$, associate to $1+i$), we get $(1+i)^k | z$ and $(1+i)^k |
\bar{z}$. Write $z = (1+i)^k \cdot w$ with $N(w) = 1$, so $w$ is a unit.
