## Stationary Temperature in a Prolate Spheroid with Uniform Heat Source

**Setup.** Let the prolate spheroid have polar (major) semiaxis $a$ (along $z$)
and equatorial (minor) semiaxis $b$ (along $x,y$), with $a > b$. Its surface is
$$\frac{x^2+y^2}{b^2}+\frac{z^2}{a^2}=1.$$
Heat is produced uniformly with volume density $Q$, the surface is held at $T=0$
, and the medium has thermal conductivity $k$. The stationary equation (Poisson)
 is
$$\nabla^2 T = -\frac{Q}{k}, \qquad T\big|_{\partial\Omega}=0.$$

**Key observation.** The Laplacian of a *quadratic* polynomial in $(x,y,z)$ is a
 *constant*. Therefore a quadratic polynomial can match a uniform source. Moreov
er, the function
$$\Phi(x,y,z)=1-\frac{x^2+y^2}{b^2}-\frac{z^2}{a^2}$$
vanishes identically on the spheroid surface and is positive inside. Hence we se
ek a solution of the form $T = A\,\Phi$ for a constant $A$ to be determined.

**Determining $A$.** Compute
$$\nabla^2\Phi = \frac{\partial^2}{\partial x^2}\!\left(-\frac{x^2}{b^2}\right)+
\frac{\partial^2}{\partial y^2}\!\left(-\frac{y^2}{b^2}\right)+\frac{\partial^2}
{\partial z^2}\!\left(-\frac{z^2}{a^2}\right)=-\frac{2}{b^2}-\frac{2}{b^2}-\frac
{2}{a^2}=-2\!\left(\frac{2}{b^2}+\frac{1}{a^2}\right).$$
Thus
$$\nabla^2 T = A\,\nabla^2\Phi = -2A\!\left(\frac{1}{a^2}+\frac{2}{b^2}\right).$
$
Setting this equal to $-Q/k$ gives
$$A=\frac{Q}{2k\left(\dfrac{1}{a^2}+\dfrac{2}{b^2}\right)}=\frac{Q\,a^2 b^2}{2k\
,(b^2+2a^2)}.$$

**Verification.**
- *Poisson equation:* $\nabla^2 T = -2A(1/a^2+2/b^2) = -Q/k$. ✓
- *Boundary condition:* On the surface $\Phi=0$, so $T=0$. ✓
- *Positivity:* Inside the spheroid $\Phi>0$ and $A>0$, so $T>0$, as expected fo
r an internal source with cold boundary. ✓
- *Symmetry:* $T$ depends on $x^2+y^2$ and $z^2$ only, respecting the full rotat
ional symmetry of the prolate spheroid about the $z$-axis. ✓

**Result.** The stationary temperature distribution is
$$\boxed{\,T(x,y,z)=\frac{Q\,a^2 b^2}{2k\,(b^2+2a^2)}\left(1-\frac{x^2+y^2}{b^2}
-\frac{z^2}{a^2}\right)\,}.$$

Equivalently, using the prolate spheroidal radial coordinate $\xi$ (with $x^2+y^
2 = (a^2-b^2)\sinh^2\xi\,\sin^2\eta$ and $z=(a^2-b^2)^{1/2}\cosh\xi\cos\eta$, su
rface at $\xi=\xi_0$ where $\cosh\xi_0=a/\sqrt{a^2-b^2}$), the factor in parenth
eses equals $1-\cosh^2\xi/\cosh^2\xi_0+\sinh^2\xi/\cosh^2\xi_0\cdot(\ldots)$; th
e Cartesian form above is the cleanest expression.

**Maximum temperature** occurs at the center $(0,0,0)$:
$$T_{\max}=\frac{Q\,a^2 b^2}{2k\,(b^2+2a^2)}.$$
