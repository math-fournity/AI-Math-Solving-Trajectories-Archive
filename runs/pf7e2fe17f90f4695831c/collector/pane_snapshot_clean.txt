**Claim:** No collection of $n \geq 2$ canisters with pairwise distinct masses c

**Proof:**

Suppose for contradiction that there exists a collection of $n \geq 2$ canisters
 with distinct masses satisfying the rule. Label the masses in increasing order:
$$a_1 < a_2 < a_3 < \cdots < a_n$$

Consider the pair consisting of the two lightest canisters, $\{a_1, a_2\}$, with
 combined mass $a_1 + a_2$.

By the Balance Symmetry Rule, there must exist a **different** pair $\{a_i, a_j\
} \neq \{a_1, a_2\}$ (with $i < j$) such that:
$$a_i + a_j = a_1 + a_2$$

I show this is impossible by examining all cases for the different pair:

**Case 1: $i = 1, j \geq 3$.** Since $j \geq 3$ and the masses are strictly incr
easing, $a_j > a_2$, so:
$$a_1 + a_j > a_1 + a_2$$

**Case 2: $i \geq 2$.** Then $j > i \geq 2$, so $j \geq 3$. This gives $a_i \geq
 a_2$ and $a_j \geq a_3 > a_2$, hence:
$$a_i + a_j \geq a_2 + a_3 > a_1 + a_2$$

In **both** cases, $a_i + a_j > a_1 + a_2$, which contradicts the requirement th
at $a_i + a_j = a_1 + a_2$.

Therefore, **no different pair** can have the same combined mass as $\{a_1, a_2\
}$. The sum $a_1 + a_2$ is **uniquely achieved** by the pair of the two lightest
 canisters.

By symmetry, the same argument applies to the two heaviest canisters $\{a_{n-1},
 a_n\}$: their sum $a_{n-1} + a_n$ is the unique maximum pair sum, so no differe
nt pair shares it.

**Conclusion:** For any collection of $n \geq 2$ canisters with distinct masses,
 the minimum pair sum $a_1 + a_2$ and the maximum pair sum $a_{n-1} + a_n$ are e
ach achieved by exactly one pair. This violates the Balance Symmetry Rule, which
 requires every pair sum to be achieved by at least two distinct pairs.

The condition is **vacuously true** for $n \leq 1$ (no pairs exist to check), bu
t this is degenerate—a "collection" from which no pair can be selected does not
meaningfully satisfy a rule about pairs.

**No non-degenerate collection of canisters with distinct masses can satisfy the
 Balance Symmetry Rule. The minimum number does not exist.**
