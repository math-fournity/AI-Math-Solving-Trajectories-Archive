## Proof

We seek the smallest $n$ such that every 2-coloring (red/blue) of exactly $n$ ed
ges of $K_9$ contains a monochromatic triangle. Equivalently, $n = M+1$ where $M
$ is the maximum number of edges that can be 2-colored without a monochromatic t
riangle.

### Step 1: Construction with 32 colored edges, no monochromatic triangle ($n \g
eq 33$)

Partition the 9 vertices into 5 parts:
$$P_1=\{1,2\},\; P_2=\{3,4\},\; P_3=\{5,6\},\; P_4=\{7,8\},\; P_5=\{9\}.$$

**Leave uncolored** the 4 intra-part edges $\{1,2\},\{3,4\},\{5,6\},\{7,8\}$.

**Color** all 32 inter-part edges (the Turán graph $T(9,5)$) as follows. Regard
the 5 parts as vertices of $K_5$. Color the 5-cycle $P_1P_2P_3P_4P_5P_1$ **red**
 and the complementary 5-cycle $P_1P_3P_5P_2P_4P_1$ **blue**. For each pair $(P_
i,P_j)$, color *every* edge between $P_i$ and $P_j$ with the color of $P_iP_j$.

This gives 16 red and 16 blue edges. Every triangle in $T(9,5)$ uses 3 vertices
from 3 distinct parts (there are no intra-part edges), so its three edge-colors
match the three part-level edge-colors. Since $R(3,3)=6>5$, the 2-coloring of $K
_5$ has **no monochromatic triangle**, so no triangle in $T(9,5)$ is monochromat
ic. Thus 32 colored edges can avoid a monochromatic triangle, giving $n \geq 33$
.

### Step 2: Every coloring of 33 edges forces a monochromatic triangle ($n \leq
33$)

With 33 colored edges, exactly 3 edges are uncolored. Let $U$ be the uncolored g
raph on 9 vertices with 3 edges. By Gallai's theorem, $\alpha(U) + \tau(U) = 9$,
 where $\tau(U)$ is the minimum vertex cover. Since $U$ has only 3 edges, $\tau(
U) \leq 3$ (take one endpoint per edge), so:
$$\alpha(U) \geq 9 - 3 = 6.$$

Thus $U$ has an independent set $S$ of size 6. By definition, no uncolored edge
has both endpoints in $S$, so all $\binom{6}{2}=15$ edges within $S$ are colored
 (red or blue). By the Ramsey number $R(3,3)=6$, every 2-coloring of $K_6$ conta
ins a monochromatic triangle. Therefore the coloring restricted to $S$ contains
a monochromatic triangle.

### Conclusion

Since 32 colored edges can avoid a monochromatic triangle but 33 cannot, the sma
llest such $n$ is:

$$\boxed{n = 33}$$
