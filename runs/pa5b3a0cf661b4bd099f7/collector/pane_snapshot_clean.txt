## Proof

Let the three roots of $x^3 - 9x^2 + 10x + 5 = 0$ be $a, b, c$. By Vieta's formu
las:
$$a+b+c = 9, \quad ab+bc+ca = 10, \quad abc = -5.$$

The three sensors are the vertices of an equilateral triangle with $x$-coordinat
es $a, b, c$. Any equilateral triangle with centroid $(h, k)$ and circumradius $
r$ can be written as
$$P_i = \bigl(h + r\cos\varphi_i,\; k + r\sin\varphi_i\bigr), \quad \varphi_i =
\theta + \tfrac{2\pi(i-1)}{3},\quad i=1,2,3.$$

**Sum of $x$-coordinates.** Since $\cos\theta + \cos(\theta+\tfrac{2\pi}{3}) + \
cos(\theta+\tfrac{4\pi}{3}) = 0$,
$$a+b+c = 3h = 9 \implies h = 3.$$

**Sum of squares of $x$-coordinates.** Using $\sum \cos^2 \varphi_i = \tfrac{3}{
2}$ and $\sum \cos\varphi_i = 0$:
$$a^2+b^2+c^2 = \sum(h + r\cos\varphi_i)^2 = 3h^2 + r^2\cdot\tfrac{3}{2} = 27 +
\tfrac{3}{2}r^2.$$

On the other hand, $(a+b+c)^2 = (a^2+b^2+c^2) + 2(ab+bc+ca)$, so
$$81 = \left(27 + \tfrac{3}{2}r^2\right) + 2\cdot 10 = 47 + \tfrac{3}{2}r^2.$$

Solving: $\tfrac{3}{2}r^2 = 34$, hence $r^2 = \tfrac{68}{3}$.

**Side length.** For an equilateral triangle, the side length $s$ and circumradi
us satisfy $s = r\sqrt{3}$, so
$$s^2 = 3r^2 = 3\cdot \tfrac{68}{3} = 68 \implies s = \sqrt{68} = 2\sqrt{17}.$$

**Consistency check (existence).** The product $abc$ must equal $-5$. Using the
identity $\prod \cos\varphi_i = \tfrac{1}{4}\cos 3\theta$ and $\sum_{i<j}\cos\va
rphi_i\cos\varphi_j = -\tfrac{3}{4}$:
$$abc = h^3 - \tfrac{3}{4}hr^2 + \tfrac{1}{4}r^3\cos 3\theta = 27 - \tfrac{3}{4}
\cdot 3\cdot\tfrac{68}{3} + \tfrac{1}{4}\left(\tfrac{68}{3}\right)^{3/2}\cos 3\t
heta = -24 + \tfrac{34\sqrt{51}}{9}\cos 3\theta.$$
Setting this equal to $-5$ gives $\cos 3\theta = \tfrac{19\cdot 9}{34\sqrt{51}}
= \tfrac{171}{34\sqrt{51}} \approx 0.704 \in [-1,1]$, confirming a valid real co
nfiguration exists.

Therefore the exact distance between any two sensors is $\boxed{2\sqrt{17}}$.
