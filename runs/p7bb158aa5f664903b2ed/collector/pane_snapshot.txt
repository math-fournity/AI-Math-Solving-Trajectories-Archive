## Solution

Let $P = \prod_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\mathbb{Z}$ be the Baer–Specker group. We compute

$$\operatorname{rank}(P) = \dim_{\mathbb{Q}}\bigl(\mathbb{Q}\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}
 P\bigr).$$

**Step 1. Identify the tensor product as a localization.**

Since $\mathbb{Q} = S^{-1}\mathbb{Z}$ with $S = \mathbb{Z}\setminus\{0\}$, for a
ny $\mathbb{Z}$-module $M$ there is a natural isomorphism

$$\mathbb{Q}\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} M \;\cong\; S^{-1}M.$$

Hence $\mathbb{Q}\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} P \cong S^{-1}P$, the localization of $P$
at all nonzero integers.

**Step 2. Describe $S^{-1}P$ concretely.**

The natural map $\varphi: S^{-1}P \to \mathbb{Q}^{\mathbb{N}}$ sending $\frac{(a
_n)}{s}\mapsto \bigl(\frac{a_n}{s}\bigr)$ is injective (localization is exact, a
nd $P\hookrightarrow\mathbb{Q}^{\mathbb{N}}$ is injective). Its image is

$$\operatorname{im}(\varphi) = \bigl\{(q_n)\in\mathbb{Q}^{\mathbb{N}} : \exists\
, s\in\mathbb{Z}\setminus\{0\}\text{ with } s\,q_n\in\mathbb{Z}\ \forall n\bigr\
},$$

i.e. rational sequences admitting a **common denominator**. Thus $V := \mathbb{Q
}\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} P$ is identified with this $\mathbb{Q}$-subspace of $\math
bb{Q}^{\mathbb{N}}$.

**Step 3. Compute the cardinality of $V$.**

- $|P| = |\mathbb{Z}|^{\aleph_0} = \aleph_0^{\aleph_0} = 2^{\aleph_0}$ (using $2
^{\aleph_0}\le\aleph_0^{\aleph_0}\le(2^{\aleph_0})^{\aleph_0}=2^{\aleph_0}$).
- $|S^{-1}P| \le |P|\cdot|S| = 2^{\aleph_0}\cdot\aleph_0 = 2^{\aleph_0}$.
- The injection $P\hookrightarrow S^{-1}P$, $p\mapsto p/1$, gives $|S^{-1}P|\ge|
P|=2^{\aleph_0}$.

Therefore $|V| = 2^{\aleph_0}$.

**Step 4. Convert cardinality to $\mathbb{Q}$-dimension.**

A $\mathbb{Q}$-vector space of dimension $\kappa$ (with $\mathbb{Q}$ countable)
consists of finite $\mathbb{Q}$-linear combinations of basis vectors, so its car
dinality is

$$|V| = \max(\kappa,\,\aleph_0).$$

Since $|V|=2^{\aleph_0}>\aleph_0$, we obtain $\kappa = 2^{\aleph_0}$.

**Step 5. Confirm the dimension is not smaller (independence from $\mathbb{Q}^{\
mathbb{N}}$).**

The full space $\mathbb{Q}^{\mathbb{N}}$ also has cardinality $2^{\aleph_0}$ and
 $\mathbb{Q}$-dimension $2^{\aleph_0}$. Our subspace $V$ has the same cardinalit
y $2^{\aleph_0}$, hence the same dimension $2^{\aleph_0}$. (One can also exhibit
 $2^{\aleph_0}$ linearly independent elements in $V$ directly, e.g. via almost-d
isjoint families, but the cardinality argument already suffices.)

## Answer

$$\boxed{\dim_{\mathbb{Q}}\bigl(\mathbb{Q}\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}\prod_{n\in\mathbb
{N}}\mathbb{Z}\bigr) = 2^{\aleph_0}}$$

The rank of the Baer–Specker group is $2^{\aleph_0}$, the cardinality of the con
tinuum. (This is a transfinite cardinal, not a finite integer; the "single integ
er" instruction in the prompt appears to be a generic template, as the mathemati
cally correct answer is the uncountable cardinal $2^{\aleph_0}$.)
