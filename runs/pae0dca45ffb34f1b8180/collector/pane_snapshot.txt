$$m \in S \iff g - m \notin S \quad \text{for all integers } m.$$

(a+a'+1)$ so $a+a'+1 \geq q$. Thus $pq \geq pq + qp = 2pq > pq$, contradiction.
So $m \in S \Rightarrow g - m \notin S$.
