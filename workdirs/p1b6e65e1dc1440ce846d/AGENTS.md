# Problem

In a specialized logistics facility, a grid of $8 \times 8$ loading docks is arranged like a standard chessboard. A diagonal line of 8 specific docks is designated as a "High-Voltage Zone" (HVZ). 

A remote-controlled transport robot is deployed from a starting position outside the HVZ. The robot is programmed to navigate the grid under the following operational constraints:
1. The robot can occupy any single dock at most once during its entire journey.
2. The robot is strictly prohibited from entering or stopping on any of the 8 docks located within the HVZ.
3. A "zone transition" is defined as any single move where the robot travels from a dock on one side of the HVZ diagonal to a dock on the opposite side of the diagonal.

Based on these movement protocols, determine the maximum number of zone transitions the robot can perform before it can no longer make a legal move.
