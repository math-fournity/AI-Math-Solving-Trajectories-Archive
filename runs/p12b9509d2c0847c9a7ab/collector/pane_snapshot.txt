## Solution

**Setup.** Place the square at $[-1,1]^2$ with center at the origin. The four ed
ges are $x=\pm1$, $y=\pm1$. A point $(x,y)$ is nearer to the center than to any
edge iff
$$\sqrt{x^2+y^2} < \min(1-|x|,\;1-|y|).$$

By the 8-fold symmetry of the square, it suffices to compute the favorable area
in the sector $0\le x\le y\le 1$ and multiply by 8.

**Reduction in the sector $0\le x\le y\le 1$.** Here $1-x\ge 1-y$, so the neares
t edge is $y=1$, and the condition becomes
$$\sqrt{x^2+y^2}<1-y.$$
Squaring (both sides nonnegative): $x^2+y^2 < 1-2y+y^2$, i.e.
$$y < \frac{1-x^2}{2}.$$
Together with $x\le y$ this requires $x < \frac{1-x^2}{2}$, i.e. $x^2+2x-1<0$, g
iving $0\le x\le \sqrt{2}-1$.

**Area of the sector.** With $u=\sqrt{2}-1$:
$$A_{\text{sector}}=\int_0^{u}\!\left(\frac{1-x^2}{2}-x\right)dx=\left[\frac{x}{
2}-\frac{x^3}{6}-\frac{x^2}{2}\right]_0^{u}=\frac{u}{2}-\frac{u^3}{6}-\frac{u^2}
{2}.$$

Using $u^2=3-2\sqrt2$ and $u^3=5\sqrt2-7$:
$$A_{\text{sector}}=\frac{\sqrt2-1}{2}-\frac{5\sqrt2-7}{6}-\frac{3-2\sqrt2}{2}=\
frac{4\sqrt2-5}{12}.$$

**Total favorable area.** Multiplying by 8:
$$A_{\text{fav}}=8\cdot\frac{4\sqrt2-5}{12}=\frac{2(4\sqrt2-5)}{3}.$$

**Probability.** The square has area $4$, so
$$P=\frac{A_{\text{fav}}}{4}=\frac{2(4\sqrt2-5)}{12}=\frac{4\sqrt2-5}{3}.$$

**Verification.** Numerically, $\frac{4\sqrt2-5}{3}\approx\frac{5.657-5}{3}\appr
ox 0.219$, a reasonable probability for the curved-diamond region bounded by the
 four parabolas $x^2=1-2|y|$ and $y^2=1-2|x|$.

The answer in the required form $(a\sqrt{b}+c)/d$ is

$$\boxed{\dfrac{4\sqrt{2}-5}{3}}.$$
