## Analyzing the Graph Structure

For k ≥ 2, each vertex i connects to i+(k−1) and i−(k−1) mod 100. Since k−1 ∈ {1
,...,49} and 2(k−1) ≢ 0 mod 100 (as k−1 < 50), these are two distinct neighbors,
 so **G_k is 2-regular** — a union of disjoint cycles.

The cycles come from the permutation σ(i) = i + (k−1) mod 100, which decomposes
into **gcd(k−1, 100) cycles**, each of length **100 / gcd(k−1, 100)**.

A union of cycles has a perfect matching **if and only if every cycle has even l
ength**. So we need:

$$\frac{100}{\gcd(k-1,\, 100)} \text{ is even}$$

This is equivalent to gcd(k−1, 100) | 50.

## Handling k = 1
