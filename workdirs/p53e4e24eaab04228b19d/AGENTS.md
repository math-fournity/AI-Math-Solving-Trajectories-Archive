# Problem

In the coastal city of Gridhaven, a new 8x8 solar array has been constructed, consisting of 64 individual panels arranged in a perfect square grid. Due to a recent atmospheric event, several panels have been supercharged with "Green Energy."

The city engineers have discovered that a specialized drone, known as a "B-Type Signal Relay," can only be deployed onto a panel if it is currently Green. However, these relays emit high-frequency interference along both diagonals of the grid. To ensure the system functions correctly, no two relays can be placed such that they occupy the same diagonal line, regardless of distance.

The Lead Engineer needs to determine the resilience of this system. Find the smallest integer $b$ such that, regardless of which $b$ specific panels are Green, it is always possible to select 7 of those Green panels to host 7 B-Type Signal Relays without any two relays interfering with each other.
