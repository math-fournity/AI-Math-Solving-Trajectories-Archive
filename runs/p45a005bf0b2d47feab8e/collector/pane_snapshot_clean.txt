Yes, $\phi$ is the restriction map. Here is the proof.

## Proof

Let $X$ be a scheme with structure sheaf $\mathcal{O}_X$, and let $i: U \hookrig
htarrow X$ be the inclusion of an open subscheme. By definition of an open immer
sion, $U$ carries the restricted sheaf $\mathcal{O}_U = \mathcal{O}_X|_U$, and t
he morphism of sheaves

$$i^\#: \mathcal{O}_X \longrightarrow i_*\mathcal{O}_U$$

is the canonical one attached to the open immersion. We recall its construction
on each open $V \subseteq X$.

**The pushforward on sections.** For any open $V \subseteq X$,

$$(i_*\mathcal{O}_U)(V) = \mathcal{O}_U(i^{-1}(V)) = \mathcal{O}_U(U \cap V).$$

Since $U$ is open in $X$, every open subset of $U$ is open in $X$, and $\mathcal
{O}_U = \mathcal{O}_X|_U$, so

$$(i_*\mathcal{O}_U)(V) = \mathcal{O}_X(U \cap V).$$

**The sheaf morphism on sections.** The map $i^\#_V: \mathcal{O}_X(V) \to (i_*\m
athcal{O}_U)(V) = \mathcal{O}_X(U \cap V)$ is, by construction of the open immer
sion, the restriction map of the sheaf $\mathcal{O}_X$:

$$i^\#_V = \rho^{V}_{U\cap V}:\ \mathcal{O}_X(V) \longrightarrow \mathcal{O}_X(U
 \cap V).$$

This is the defining property of the structure sheaf morphism associated to an o
pen immersion: it is built entirely from the restriction maps of $\mathcal{O}_X$
.

**Taking $V = X$.** The induced map on global sections is

$$\phi = i^\#_X:\ \mathcal{O}_X(X) \longrightarrow (i_*\mathcal{O}_U)(X) = \math
cal{O}_U(U) = \mathcal{O}_X(U).$$

By the identification above with $V = X$ (so $U \cap X = U$),

$$i^\#_X = \rho^{X}_{U}:\ \mathcal{O}_X(X) \longrightarrow \mathcal{O}_X(U),$$

which is exactly the restriction map of the structure sheaf $\mathcal{O}_X$ from
 $X$ to $U$.

**Conclusion.** The map on global sections induced by the inclusion $i: U \hookr
ightarrow X$ coincides with the restriction map $\rho^X_U: \mathcal{O}_X(X) \to
\mathcal{O}_X(U)$. The hypotheses that $X$ and $U$ are affine are not even neede
d for this identification; they hold for any open immersion of schemes. Hence

$$\boxed{\phi = \rho^X_U,}$$

i.e., $\phi$ **is** the restriction map. $\blacksquare$
