## Solution

and the constraints on parameters. No solution, answer, or proof sketch is leake

We evaluate
$$L := \lim_{k\to\infty} 2k(m+2k)\,\frac{e^{-m/(2k)}(m+2k)^{m/(2k)}}{(k!)^{1/k}\
,\Gamma\!\left(k+\tfrac{p}{2}\right)^{1/k}\,c^{m/(2k)}},\qquad m,p\in\mathbb N,\
;c>0.$$

### Step 1. The "small-exponent" factors tend to 1

Combine the three factors carrying the exponent $\tfrac{m}{2k}$:
$$e^{-m/(2k)}(m+2k)^{m/(2k)}c^{-m/(2k)}=\exp\!\Big(\tfrac{m}{2k}\big[\ln(m+2k)-1
-\ln c\big]\Big).$$
Since $\tfrac{\ln(m+2k)}{k}\to 0$ and $\tfrac{1}{k}\to 0$, the bracket divided b
y $k$ vanishes, so this product $\to e^{0}=1$.

Also $2k(m+2k)=4k^{2}\!\left(1+\tfrac{m}{2k}\right)$, and $\left(1+\tfrac{m}{2k}
\right)\to 1$.

Hence the limit reduces to
$$L=\lim_{k\to\infty}\frac{4k^{2}}{(k!)^{1/k}\,\Gamma\!\left(k+\tfrac{p}{2}\righ
t)^{1/k}}.$$

### Step 2. Stirling asymptotics for $(k!)^{1/k}$

Stirling's formula $k!=\sqrt{2\pi k}\,(k/e)^{k}\bigl(1+O(1/k)\bigr)$ gives
$$(k!)^{1/k}=(2\pi k)^{1/(2k)}\cdot\frac{k}{e}\cdot\bigl(1+O(1/k)\bigr)^{1/k}.$$
Now $(2\pi k)^{1/(2k)}=\exp\!\big(\tfrac{\ln(2\pi k)}{2k}\big)\to 1$ and $\bigl(
1+O(1/k)\bigr)^{1/k}\to 1$, so
$$(k!)^{1/k}\sim \frac{k}{e}.\tag{1}$$

### Step 3. Stirling asymptotics for $\Gamma\!\left(k+\tfrac{p}{2}\right)^{1/k}$

Using $\Gamma(x)\sim \sqrt{2\pi}\,x^{x-1/2}e^{-x}$ with $x=k+\tfrac{p}{2}$:
$$\Gamma\!\left(k+\tfrac{p}{2}\right)=\sqrt{2\pi}\,\left(k+\tfrac{p}{2}\right)^{
k+p/2-1/2}e^{-(k+p/2)}\bigl(1+O(1/k)\bigr).$$
Taking the $1/k$ power:
$$\Gamma\!\left(k+\tfrac{p}{2}\right)^{1/k}=(2\pi)^{1/(2k)}\left(k+\tfrac{p}{2}\
right)\left(k+\tfrac{p}{2}\right)^{(p-1)/(2k)}e^{-1-p/(2k)}\bigl(1+O(1/k)\bigr)^
{1/k}.$$
Each auxiliary factor tends to $1$: $(2\pi)^{1/(2k)}\to 1$, $\left(k+\tfrac{p}{2
}\right)^{(p-1)/(2k)}=\exp\!\big(\tfrac{(p-1)\ln(k+p/2)}{2k}\big)\to 1$, $e^{-1-
p/(2k)}\to e^{-1}$, and $\bigl(1+O(1/k)\bigr)^{1/k}\to 1$. Since $k+\tfrac{p}{2}
\sim k$,
$$\Gamma\!\left(k+\tfrac{p}{2}\right)^{1/k}\sim \frac{k}{e}.\tag{2}$$

### Step 4. Combine

From (1) and (2),
$$(k!)^{1/k}\,\Gamma\!\left(k+\tfrac{p}{2}\right)^{1/k}\sim \frac{k}{e}\cdot\fra
c{k}{e}=\frac{k^{2}}{e^{2}}.$$
Therefore
$$L=\lim_{k\to\infty}\frac{4k^{2}}{k^{2}/e^{2}}=4e^{2}.$$

The parameters $m,p,c$ drop out: $m,p$ appear only in vanishing $\tfrac{1}{k}$-o
rder corrections, and $c>0$ only inside the tending-to-$1$ factor $c^{m/(2k)}$.

$$\boxed{\lim_{k\to\infty} 2k(m+2k)\frac{e^{-m/(2k)}(m+2k)^{m/(2k)}}{(k!)^{1/k}\
,\Gamma(k+p/2)^{1/k}\,c^{m/(2k)}}=4e^{2}.}$$
