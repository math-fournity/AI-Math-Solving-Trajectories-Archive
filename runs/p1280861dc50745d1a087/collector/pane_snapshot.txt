## Proof

Let $\lambda = |A|$. We are given $\omega \leq \kappa \leq \lambda \leq 2^\kappa
$. We determine $|C|$ where $C = \{B \subseteq A : |B| \leq \kappa\} = [A]^{\leq
 \kappa}$.

**Claim.** $|C| = 2^\kappa$.

The proof proceeds in two stages: first we establish the general identity $|[A]^
{\leq \kappa}| = |A|^\kappa$ for infinite $\kappa$ with $2 \leq |A|$, then we ev
aluate $|A|^\kappa$ under the given bounds.

---

### Stage 1: $|[A]^{\leq \kappa}| = |A|^\kappa$

**Upper bound.** Every $B \in [A]^{\leq \kappa}$ with $|B| = \mu \leq \kappa$ ca
n be enumerated by a function $\mu \to A$ (choosing an injection's image), so th
e number of such $B$ is at most $|A|^\mu$. Hence
$$
|[A]^{\leq \kappa}| \leq \sum_{\mu \leq \kappa} |A|^\mu \leq \kappa \cdot |A|^\k
appa.
$$
Since $|A| \geq 2$ and $\kappa$ is infinite, $|A|^\kappa \geq 2^\kappa > \kappa$
 (Cantor), so $\kappa \cdot |A|^\kappa = |A|^\kappa$. Thus $|[A]^{\leq \kappa}|
\leq |A|^\kappa$.

**Lower bound.** Consider all functions $f: \kappa \to A$; there are $|A|^\kappa
$ of them. Each $f$ has image $\operatorname{im}(f) \in [A]^{\leq \kappa}$. For
a fixed image $B$ with $|B| = \mu \leq \kappa$, the number of functions $\kappa
\to A$ with image exactly $B$ is at most $\mu^\kappa \leq \kappa^\kappa$. Theref
ore
$$
|A|^\kappa \leq |[A]^{\leq \kappa}| \cdot \kappa^\kappa. \tag{$\ast$}
$$
Now $\kappa^\kappa \leq (2^\kappa)^\kappa = 2^{\kappa \cdot \kappa} = 2^\kappa$
(since $\kappa$ is infinite, $\kappa \cdot \kappa = \kappa$). Also $|[A]^{\leq \
kappa}| \geq |[\kappa]^{\leq \kappa}| = 2^\kappa$ (every subset of $\kappa$ has
size $\leq \kappa$, and $|\kappa| = \kappa \leq |A|$ so $[\kappa]^{\leq\kappa}$
embeds into $[A]^{\leq\kappa}$). Hence $|[A]^{\leq \kappa}| \geq 2^\kappa \geq \
kappa^\kappa$.

Since $|[A]^{\leq \kappa}|$ is infinite, $|[A]^{\leq \kappa}| \cdot \kappa^\kapp
a \leq |[A]^{\leq \kappa}| \cdot |[A]^{\leq \kappa}| = |[A]^{\leq \kappa}|$. Fro
m ($\ast$),
$$
|A|^\kappa \leq |[A]^{\leq \kappa}|.
$$

Combining both bounds: $|[A]^{\leq \kappa}| = |A|^\kappa$.

---

### Stage 2: Evaluate $|A|^\kappa$ under $\kappa \leq \lambda \leq 2^\kappa$

**Lower bound.** Since $\lambda = |A| \geq \kappa \geq \omega \geq 2$, we have $
|A| \geq 2$, so
$$
|A|^\kappa \geq 2^\kappa.
$$

**Upper bound.** Since $\lambda \leq 2^\kappa$,
$$
|A|^\kappa = \lambda^\kappa \leq (2^\kappa)^\kappa = 2^{\kappa \cdot \kappa} = 2
^\kappa,
$$
using $\kappa \cdot \kappa = \kappa$ for infinite $\kappa$.

Therefore $|A|^\kappa = 2^\kappa$.

---

### Conclusion

$$
|C| = |[A]^{\leq \kappa}| = |A|^\kappa = 2^\kappa.
$$
