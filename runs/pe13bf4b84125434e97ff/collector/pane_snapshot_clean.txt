The statement is true.

Proof.

It suffices to show that the support of a coherent sheaf on a noetherian scheme
is closed. Define $$\operatorname{Supp}(F) = {y \in X : F_y \neq 0}.$$ If $
\operatorname{Supp}(F)$ is closed and $F_x = 0$, then $x \notin
\operatorname{Supp}(F)$, so $U := X \setminus \operatorname{Supp}(F)$ is an open
neighborhood of $x$ on which $F|_U = 0$.

Claim. $\operatorname{Supp}(F)$ is closed in $X$.

Proof of claim. Cover $X$ by affine open subschemes $U_i = \operatorname{Spec}
A_i$ with each $A_i$ noetherian (possible since $X$ is noetherian, hence covered
by finitely many affine open noetherian subschemes). Since $F$ is coherent and
$X$ is noetherian, $F$ is of finite type, so $F|_{U_i} \cong \widetilde{M_i}$
for a finitely generated $A_i$-module $M_i$.

For a point $\mathfrak{p} \in \operatorname{Spec} A_i$, the stalk is $
$(\widetilde{M_i}){\mathfrak{p}} = (M_i){\mathfrak{p}}.$$ We have
$(M_i)_{\mathfrak{p}} = 0$ if and only if there exists $s \in A_i \setminus
\mathfrak{p}$ with $s \cdot M_i = 0$, i.e. $s \in \operatorname{Ann}(M_i)$.
Equivalently, $$\operatorname{Ann}(M_i) \not\subseteq \mathfrak{p}.$$ Therefore
$$\operatorname{Supp}(\widetilde{M_i}) = {\mathfrak{p} \in \operatorname{Spec}
A_i : \operatorname{Ann}(M_i) \subseteq \mathfrak{p}} = V(\operatorname{Ann}
(M_i)),$$ which is a closed subset of $\operatorname{Spec} A_i$ (here $
\operatorname{Ann}(M_i)$ is an ideal of $A_i$, finitely generated since $A_i$ is
noetherian, but $V(I)$ is closed for any ideal $I$).

Since $\operatorname{Supp}(F) \cap U_i = \operatorname{Supp}(F|_{U_i}) =
V(\operatorname{Ann}(M_i))$ is closed in $U_i$ for every $i$, and the $U_i$
cover $X$, the set $\operatorname{Supp}(F)$ is closed in $X$. $\square$

Now, given $F_x = 0$, we have $x \notin \operatorname{Supp}(F)$. Set $U = X
\setminus \operatorname{Supp}(F)$. Then $U$ is an open neighborhood of $x$, and
for every $y \in U$, $F_y = 0$, which means $F|_U = 0$.

Hence the statement is true. $\blacksquare$
