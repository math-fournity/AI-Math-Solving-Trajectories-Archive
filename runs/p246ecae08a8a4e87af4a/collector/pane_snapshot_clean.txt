Looking at this problem, I need to count arrangements of 20 children (5 tables ×
 4 chairs) where no two children originally at the same table end up at the same
 new table.

## Setting up the structure

Label original tables 1–5, each with 4 children. After rearrangement, each new t
able has 4 seats. Since children from the same original table cannot share a new
 table, each new table receives **at most one** child from each original table.
With 5 original tables and only 4 seats per new table, each new table receives c
hildren from exactly **4 of the 5** original tables.

This gives a 5×5 zero-one matrix where entry (i,j) = 1 if some child from origin
al table i sits at new table j. Each row sums to 4 (each original table's 4 chil
dren go to 4 distinct new tables) and each column sums to 4 (each new table has
4 children from 4 distinct original tables). The single zero in each row and eac
h column forms a **permutation matrix**, so the "skipping pattern" is a permutat
ion σ of {1,…,5}.

## Counting

**Step 1: Choose the permutation σ** (which new table each original table skips)
.
$$5! = 120 \text{ ways.}$$

**Step 2: Assign each original table's 4 children to its 4 allowed new tables.**
 For original table i, its 4 children must be bijected onto the 4 new tables ≠ σ
(i): 4! ways each.
$$(4!)^5 \text{ ways.}$$

**Step 3: Seat the children at each new table.** Each new table j now has 4 chil
dren (one from each original table i with σ(i) ≠ j). Assign them to the 4 distin
guishable chairs: 4! ways each.
$$(4!)^5 \text{ ways.}$$

These three steps are independent and every valid arrangement is produced exactl
y once, so:

$$\text{Total} = 5! \times (4!)^{10}.$$

## Prime factorization

$$4! = 24 = 2^3 \cdot 3, \qquad 5! = 120 = 2^3 \cdot 3 \cdot 5.$$

$$(4!)^{10} = (2^3 \cdot 3)^{10} = 2^{30} \cdot 3^{10}.$$

$$\text{Total} = 2^3 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 2^{30} \cdot 3^{10} = 2^{33} \cdot 3^
{11} \cdot 5^{1}.$$

## Reading off the answer

Comparing with $2^a\, 3^b\, 5^c$:

$$a = 33, \quad b = 11, \quad c = 1.$$

$$a + b + c = 33 + 11 + 1 = \boxed{45}.$$
