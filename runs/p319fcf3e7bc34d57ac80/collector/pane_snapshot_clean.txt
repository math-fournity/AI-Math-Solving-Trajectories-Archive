## Proof

We show that such a power exists, and in fact characterize exactly which rationa
l exponents work.

**The trivial solution.** Take the exponent $x = 0$. Then
$$\pi^{0} = 1 \in \mathbb{Q}.$$
Since $0$ is **not** a positive rational number, it is not among the excluded ex
ponents. Hence a power of $\pi$ (with exponent not a positive rational) that is
rational does exist.

**No other rational exponent works.** We verify that $x=0$ is the *only* rationa
l exponent producing a rational value, which also explains why the problem exclu
des positive rational powers (they never succeed).

*Positive rational exponents.* Let $x = p/q > 0$ with $p,q\in\mathbb{Z}_{>0}$. S
uppose for contradiction that $\pi^{p/q} = r \in \mathbb{Q}$. Then
$$\pi = r^{\,q/p},$$
i.e. $\pi$ is a root of $X^{p} - r^{q} = 0$, a nonzero polynomial with rational
coefficients. This makes $\pi$ algebraic, contradicting the Lindemann–Weierstras
s theorem (which implies $\pi$ is transcendental). So $\pi^{p/q}\notin\mathbb{Q}
$ for every positive rational $p/q$.

*Negative rational exponents.* Let $x = -p/q < 0$ with $p,q\in\mathbb{Z}_{>0}$.
If $\pi^{p/q}$ were algebraic, then $(\pi^{p/q})^{q} = \pi^{p}$ would be algebra
ic, and $\pi$ would be a root of $X^{p}-\pi^{p}=0$ with algebraic coefficients,
hence algebraic — again contradicting transcendence of $\pi$. Thus $\pi^{p/q}$ i
s transcendental, and so
$$\pi^{-p/q} = \frac{1}{\pi^{p/q}}$$
is also transcendental (the reciprocal of a transcendental number is transcenden
tal), hence not rational.

**Conclusion.** Among all rational exponents, $x=0$ is the unique one for which
$\pi^{x}\in\mathbb{Q}$, and it gives $\pi^{0}=1$. Since $0$ is not a positive ra
tional, this answers the question as posed in the affirmative.

(Whether some *irrational* exponent $\alpha$ satisfies $\pi^{\alpha}\in\mathbb{Q
}$ is equivalent to asking whether $\ln q/\ln\pi$ is irrational for some $q\in\m
athbb{Q}_{>0}$, a question tied to the still-open arithmetic nature of $\ln\pi$;
 it is not needed for the existence question.)
