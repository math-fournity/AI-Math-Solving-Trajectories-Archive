# Problem

In the center of a circular botanical garden with a radius of 9 decameters, a landscape architect is designing two circular flower beds. The first flower bed, planted with red tulips, has a radius of 3 decameters. The second flower bed, planted with yellow daisies, has a radius of 6 decameters. 

These two flower beds are positioned such that they touch each other at exactly one point on their perimeters (externally tangent). Furthermore, both flower beds are situated inside the garden so that each one touches the outer boundary of the circular garden at exactly one point (internally tangent).

To provide a walking path, the architect decides to install a straight stone walkway across the garden. This walkway is designed to be a chord of the large circular garden and is also perfectly tangent to the outer edges of both the tulip bed and the daisy bed, passing between them and the garden wall.

Find the square of the length of this straight stone walkway.
