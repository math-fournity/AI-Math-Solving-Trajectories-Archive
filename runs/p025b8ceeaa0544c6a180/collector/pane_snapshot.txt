Looking at this problem, I need to concatenate all integers from 1 to 10000 (exc
luding those divisible by 5 or 7) and find the result mod 11.

## Setup

**Key identity:** Since $10 \equiv -1 \pmod{11}$, the value of a digit string $d
_1 d_2 \cdots d_N$ mod 11 equals $\sum_{i=1}^N d_i \cdot (-1)^{N-i}$.

**Total digits $N$:** I first count remaining numbers by digit length:
- 1-digit (1–9): exclude 5,7 → **7 numbers**, 7 digits
- 2-digit (10–99): exclude 29 → **61 numbers**, 122 digits
- 3-digit (100–999): exclude 282 → **618 numbers**, 1854 digits
- 4-digit (1000–9999): exclude 2829 → **6171 numbers**, 24684 digits
- 10000: divisible by 5 → excluded

$N = 7 + 122 + 1854 + 24684 = 26667$ (odd).

**Sign of position $i$ (from left):** $(-1)^{26667-i} = (-1)^{i+1}$, so odd posi
tions get $+$, even positions get $-$.

**Per-number contribution:** For number $n$ starting at position $p = C(n)+1$ (w
here $C(n)$ = cumulative digits before $n$), with alternating digit sum $A(n) =
a_1 - a_2 + a_3 - \cdots$ from the left:

$$\text{contribution} = (-1)^{C(n)} \cdot A(n) \pmod{11}$$

**Crucial relation:** $A(n) \equiv n \pmod{11}$ for odd-digit numbers; $A(n) \eq
uiv -n \pmod{11}$ for even-digit numbers.

**Parity behavior:** Adding a $d$-digit number changes $C$ by $d$. So parity fli
ps for odd $d$ (1,3-digit groups: signs alternate), and stays fixed for even $d$
 (2,4-digit groups: signs constant).

## Group 1: 1-digit (signs alternate, $A(n)=n$)

Numbers: 1,2,3,4,6,8,9. Signs: $+,-,+,-,+,-,+$.

$$1 - 2 + 3 - 4 + 6 - 8 + 9 = 5$$

After: $C = 7$ (odd).

## Group 2: 2-digit (constant sign $-1$, $A(n) = -n$)

Contribution $= (-1)\sum(-n) = \sum n \pmod{11}$.

- Sum all 2-digit: $\frac{(10+99)\cdot 90}{2} = 4905$
- Sum div-by-5: $\frac{(10+95)\cdot 18}{2} = 945$; div-by-7: $\frac{(14+98)\cdot
 13}{2} = 728$; div-by-35: $105$
- Excluded: $945+728-105 = 1568$; Remaining sum: $4905-1568 = 3337$
- $3337 \equiv 4 \pmod{11}$

After: $C = 7 + 122 = 129$ (odd).

## Group 3: 3-digit (signs alternate starting $-1$, $A(n) = n$)

Contribution $= \sum_{i=1}^{618}(-1)^i n_i = -n_1+n_2-n_3+\cdots+n_{618}$.

**Structure:** The "divisible by 5 or 7" pattern has period 35, with 24 remainin
g per period. Range [100,999] splits into:
- **Part 1** (partial period k=2, residues 30–34): remaining 101,102,103,104 → $
-101+102-103+104 = 2$
- **Part 2** (25 full periods k=3..27): Since 24 is even, parity resets each per
iod. The alternating sum of residues $\{-1+2-3+4-6+8-9+11-12+13-16+17-18+19-22+2
3-24+26-27+29-31+32-33+34\} = 16$ per period. The $35k$ terms cancel. Total: $25
 \times 16 = 400$.
- **Part 3** (partial period k=28, residues 0–19): remaining residues 1,2,3,4,6,
8,9,11,12,13,16,17,18,19 (14 numbers). Base 980 cancels (even count). Alternatin
g sum: $-1+2-3+4-6+8-9+11-12+13-16+17-18+19 = 9$.

**Total:** $2 + 400 + 9 = 411 \equiv 4 \pmod{11}$.

After: $C = 129 + 1854 = 1983$ (odd).

## Group 4: 4-digit (constant sign $-1$, $A(n) = -n$)

Contribution $= (-1)\sum(-n) = \sum n \pmod{11}$.

- Sum all 4-digit: $\frac{(1000+9999)\cdot 9000}{2} = 49{,}495{,}500$
- Div-by-5: $\frac{(1000+9995)\cdot 1800}{2} = 9{,}895{,}500$
- Div-by-7: $\frac{(1001+9996)\cdot 1286}{2} = 7{,}071{,}071$
- Div-by-35: $\frac{(1015+9975)\cdot 257}{2} = 1{,}412{,}215$
- Excluded: $9{,}895{,}500 + 7{,}071{,}071 - 1{,}412{,}215 = 15{,}554{,}356$
- Remaining: $49{,}495{,}500 - 15{,}554{,}356 = 33{,}941{,}144$
- $33{,}941{,}144 \equiv 6 \pmod{11}$ (verified via alternating digit sum)
