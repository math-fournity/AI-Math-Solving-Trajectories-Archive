## Solution

The perimeter constraint $(|x| + |y| - 2)^2 = 1$ yields two cases:
$$|x| + |y| = 3 \quad \text{or} \quad |x| + |y| = 1$$

These are two concentric diamonds (squares rotated 45°) centered at the origin,
with "radii" $R = 3$ (outer) and $R = 1$ (inner). Each diamond has vertices $(\p
m R, 0)$ and $(0, \pm R)$.

### Intersections of $y = ax + 5$ with a single diamond of radius $R$

The four edges of $|x|+|y|=R$ are:
- **QII** (top-left): $y = R + x$, for $x \in [-R, 0]$
- **QI** (top-right): $y = R - x$, for $x \in [0, R]$
- **QIII** (bottom-left): $y = -R - x$, for $x \in [-R, 0]$
- **QIV** (bottom-right): $y = x - R$, for $x \in [0, R]$

Solving $ax + 5 = (\text{edge})$ on each edge and enforcing the domain constrain
t $x \in [\text{edge range}]$ (using $R < 5$ so that $R - 5 < 0$ and $-R-5 < 0$)
:

| Edge | Solution $x$ | Valid when |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| QII | $\dfrac{R-5}{a-1}$ | $a \geq 5/R$ |
| QIII | $\dfrac{-R-5}{a+1}$ | $a \geq 5/R$ |
| QI | $\dfrac{R-5}{a+1}$ | $a \leq -5/R$ |
| QIV | $\dfrac{-R-5}{a-1}$ | $a \leq -5/R$ |

At the boundary $a = 5/R$: both QII and QIII give $x = -R$ (the **left vertex**
$(-R,0)$) — a single tangent point. At $a = -5/R$: both QI and QIV give $x = R$
(the **right vertex** $(R,0)$) — a single tangent point.

Since the edges are straight line segments and the laser is a straight line, the
 only tangencies occur at **vertices** (no interior-edge tangency is possible).
Thus for a single diamond:

- $|a| > 5/R$: **2** intersection points
- $|a| = 5/R$: **1** intersection point (vertex tangent)
- $|a| < 5/R$: **0** intersection points

### Combining both diamonds ($R_1 = 1$, $R_3 = 3$; thresholds $5$ and $5/3$)

| Range of $a$ | $R=1$ count | $R=3$ count | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| $\|a\| > 5$ | 2 | 2 | 4 |
| $\|a\| = 5$ | 1 (vertex) | 2 | **3** ✓ |
| $5/3 < \|a\| < 5$ | 0 | 2 | 2 |
| $\|a\| = 5/3$ | 0 | 1 (vertex) | 1 |
| $\|a\| < 5/3$ | 0 | 0 | 0 |

The two diamonds are concentric but of different sizes, so no intersection point
s coincide between them. The only values yielding exactly 3 distinct breach poin
ts are $a = 5$ and $a = -5$.

**Verification at $a = 5$:**
- $R=1$: vertex tangent at $(-1, 0)$ → 1 point.
- $R=3$: QII gives $x = (3-5)/(5-1) = -1/2$, point $(-1/2,\, 5/2)$; QIII gives $
x = (-3-5)/(5+1) = -4/3$, point $(-4/3,\, -5/3)$. → 2 points.
- Total: 3 distinct points. ✓

**Verification at $a = -5$:** symmetric (right side), 3 distinct points. ✓

### Result

$$S = \{5,\, -5\}, \qquad \sum_{a \in S} a^2 = 5^2 + (-5)^2 = 25 + 25 = \boxed{5
0}$$
