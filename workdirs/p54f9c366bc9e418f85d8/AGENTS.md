# Problem

A specialized aerospace engineering firm is designing a high-precision kinetic flywheel component. The design consists of two perfectly spherical magnetic bearings that are placed in direct contact with one another. One bearing has a radius of $r$ and the other has a radius of $R$. 

To stabilize the system, a conical heat shield is fitted around the bearings. This shield is shaped as a right circular cone that is tangent to both spheres simultaneously, effectively "wrapping" around them. 

The engineers are focused on the "buffer zone" created within this assembly. This zone is the specific region of space bounded by the outer surfaces of both spheres and the inner surface of the conical shield. This region forms a continuous, airtight ring-shaped cavity encircling the point where the spheres touch.

Determine the exact volume of this ring-shaped buffer zone. The final expression should be a rational function of $r$ and $R$.
