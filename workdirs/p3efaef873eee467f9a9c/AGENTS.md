# Problem

A high-tech agricultural research facility consists of a massive rectangular grid of individual hydroponic pods, arranged in 32 rows and 31 columns. Every pod is programmed to maintain a unique, specific nutrient concentration level, such that no two pods in the entire facility have the exact same value.

A pod is classified as "Optimized" if its nutrient concentration is lower than or equal to the concentration of at most one of its neighboring pods. In this facility, two pods are considered neighbors if their positions in the grid share at least one corner (meaning a pod in the center of the grid has 8 neighbors, while those on the edges or corners have 5 or 3 neighbors, respectively).

Based on these spatial constraints and the requirement that all nutrient levels must be distinct, what is the maximum possible number of Optimized pods that can exist within this 32 by 31 grid?
