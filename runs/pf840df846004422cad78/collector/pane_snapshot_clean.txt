We compute the mixed partial derivative of $f(t,s) = e^{ta}e^{sb}e^{-ta}e^{-sb}$
 at the origin.

**Step 1: First-order expansion of each factor.**

Recall that for any matrix $X$,
$$e^{tX} = I + tX + \tfrac{t^2}{2}X^2 + O(t^3).$$

Applying this to each factor:

$$e^{ta} = I + ta + \tfrac{t^2}{2}a^2 + O(t^3),$$
$$e^{sb} = I + sb + \tfrac{s^2}{2}b^2 + O(s^3),$$
$$e^{-ta} = I - ta + \tfrac{t^2}{2}a^2 + O(t^3),$$
$$e^{-sb} = I - sb + \tfrac{s^2}{2}b^2 + O(s^3).$$

**Step 2: Compute $f(t,s)$ up to bilinear order $ts$.**

We only need terms up to total degree 2, and specifically the $ts$ term (since $
\frac{\partial^2}{\partial t\,\partial s}$ extracts the coefficient of $ts$, mul
tiplied by $1$).

Multiply the first two factors:
$$e^{ta}e^{sb} = (I + ta + \tfrac{t^2}{2}a^2)(I + sb + \tfrac{s^2}{2}b^2) + O(3)
$$
$$= I + ta + sb + ts\,ab + \tfrac{t^2}{2}a^2 + \tfrac{s^2}{2}b^2 + O(3),$$

where $O(3)$ denotes terms of total degree $\geq 3$ in $(t,s)$.

Multiply the last two factors:
$$e^{-ta}e^{-sb} = (I - ta + \tfrac{t^2}{2}a^2)(I - sb + \tfrac{s^2}{2}b^2) + O(
3)$$
$$= I - ta - sb + ts\,ab + \tfrac{t^2}{2}a^2 + \tfrac{s^2}{2}b^2 + O(3).$$

Now multiply these two results, keeping only terms up to total degree 2:
$$f(t,s) = \big(I + ta + sb + ts\,ab + \tfrac{t^2}{2}a^2 + \tfrac{s^2}{2}b^2\big
)\big(I - ta - sb + ts\,ab + \tfrac{t^2}{2}a^2 + \tfrac{s^2}{2}b^2\big) + O(3).$
$

Expanding and collecting terms by total degree:

- **Degree 0:** $I\cdot I = I$.
- **Degree 1:** $I(-ta) + (ta)I + I(-sb) + (sb)I = ta - ta + sb - sb = 0$.
- **Degree 2:** The degree-2 terms come from:
  - $I \cdot (ts\,ab) + (ts\,ab)\cdot I = 2\,ts\,ab$,
  - $I \cdot \tfrac{t^2}{2}a^2 + \tfrac{t^2}{2}a^2 \cdot I = t^2 a^2$,
  - $I \cdot \tfrac{s^2}{2}b^2 + \tfrac{s^2}{2}b^2 \cdot I = s^2 b^2$,
  - $(ta)(-ta) = -t^2 a^2$,
  - $(sb)(-sb) = -s^2 b^2$,
  - $(ta)(-sb) = -ts\,ab$,
  - $(sb)(-ta) = -ts\,ba$.

Combining all degree-2 terms:
- $t^2$ terms: $t^2 a^2 - t^2 a^2 = 0$.
- $s^2$ terms: $s^2 b^2 - s^2 b^2 = 0$.
- $ts$ terms: $2\,ts\,ab - ts\,ab - ts\,ba = ts\,(ab - ba)$.

Therefore:
$$f(t,s) = I + ts\,(ab - ba) + O(3).$$

**Step 3: Extract the mixed partial derivative.**

From the expansion,
$$f(t,s) = I + ts\,(ab - ba) + \text{terms of total degree} \geq 3.$$

The coefficient of $ts$ is $ab - ba$, so:
$$\frac{\partial f}{\partial s}\bigg|_{(t,s)} = t\,(ab - ba) + O(2),$$
and evaluating the $t$-derivative at $t=0$:
$$\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial t\,\partial s}\bigg|_{t=s=0} = ab - ba.$$

**Conclusion.** The mixed second partial derivative of the group commutator $f(t
,s) = e^{ta}e^{sb}e^{-ta}e^{-sb}$ at the origin equals the Lie bracket (commutat
or) of $a$ and $b$:

$$\boxed{\left(\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial t\,\partial s}\right)\bigg|_{t=s=0}
= ab - ba.}$$
