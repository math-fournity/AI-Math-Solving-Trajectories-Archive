## Proof

**Definition.** A function $f: X \to Y$ between topological spaces is *locally c
ontinuous* if for every $x \in X$ there exists an open neighborhood $U_x$ of $x$
 such that the restriction $f|_{U_x}: U_x \to Y$ is continuous (where $U_x$ carr
ies the subspace topology from $X$).

**Claim.** Every locally continuous function is continuous.

**Proof.** Let $f: X \to Y$ be locally continuous. We show $f$ is continuous by
proving that $f^{-1}(V)$ is open in $X$ for every open $V \subseteq Y$.

By local continuity, for each $x \in X$ there is an open neighborhood $U_x \subs
eteq X$ of $x$ such that $f|_{U_x}$ is continuous. Since $V$ is open in $Y$, the
 preimage
$$
(f|_{U_x})^{-1}(V) = f^{-1}(V) \cap U_x
$$
is open in the subspace topology of $U_x$.

**Key observation.** Since $U_x$ is itself open in $X$, every subset of $U_x$ th
at is open in the subspace topology of $U_x$ is also open in $X$. Indeed, if $A
\subseteq U_x$ is open in $U_x$, then $A = U_x \cap B$ for some $B$ open in $X$;
 as $U_x$ is open in $X$, the intersection $U_x \cap B$ is open in $X$.

Applying this to $A = f^{-1}(V) \cap U_x$, we conclude that $f^{-1}(V) \cap U_x$
 is open in $X$.

Now decompose:
$$
f^{-1}(V) = \bigcup_{x \in X}\bigl(f^{-1}(V) \cap U_x\bigr).
$$
Each set in the union is open in $X$, and an arbitrary union of open sets is ope
n. Therefore $f^{-1}(V)$ is open in $X$.

Since $V$ was an arbitrary open subset of $Y$, $f$ is continuous. $\blacksquare$
