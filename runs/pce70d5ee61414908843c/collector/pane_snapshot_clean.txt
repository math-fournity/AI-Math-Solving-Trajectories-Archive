## Single-Day Meeting Probability

**Setup.** Let $A \sim \text{Uniform}(0, 8)$ be your arrival time (in hours afte
r midnight), so you practice during $[A, A+3]$. Let $B \sim \text{Uniform}(5, 11
)$ be your friend's arrival time, so they practice during $[B, B+1]$. The joint
distribution lives on the rectangle $[0,8]\times[5,11]$ with area $8 \times 6 =
48$.

**Meeting condition.** The intervals $[A, A+3]$ and $[B, B+1]$ overlap iff
$$A \le B+1 \quad\text{and}\quad B \le A+3,$$
equivalently $A - 1 \le B \le A + 3$.

**Computing the meeting area.** For a fixed $A \in [0,8]$, the admissible $B$-ra
nge is $[\max(5,\, A-1),\; \min(11,\, A+3)]$.

- Since $A+3 \le 11$ for all $A \in [0,8]$, the upper bound is always $A+3$.
- The lower bound is $5$ when $A-1 \le 5$ (i.e., $A \le 6$), and $A-1$ when $A \
ge 6$.

So the length of the $B$-interval is:
$$\ell(A) = \begin{cases} (A+3) - 5 = A - 2, & 2 \le A \le 6 \quad(\text{and } 0
 \text{ for } A < 2),\\ 4, & 6 \le A \le 8. \end{cases}$$

Integrating:
$$\text{Meeting area} = \int_2^6 (A-2)\,dA + \int_6^8 4\,dA = \left[\frac{(A-2)^
2}{2}\right]_2^6 + 8 = \frac{16}{2} + 8 = 8 + 8 = 16.$$

**Verification via complement.** Non-meeting occurs when $B > A+3$ (you finish f
irst) or $B < A-1$ (friend finishes first):
- $B > A+3$: $\displaystyle\int_5^{11}(B-3)\,dB = \frac{(B-3)^2}{2}\Big|_5^{11}
= 32 - 2 = 30.$
- $B < A-1$: only for $B \in [5,7)$, $\displaystyle\int_5^7 (7-B)\,dB = 2.$

Non-meeting area $= 30 + 2 = 32$, so meeting area $= 48 - 32 = 16$. ✓

$$p = \frac{16}{48} = \frac{1}{3}.$$

## Five-Day Probability

Let $X$ be the number of days you meet in 5 days. Assuming independence, $X \sim
 \text{Binomial}\!\left(5,\, \tfrac{1}{3}\right)$.

$$P(X \ge 2) = 1 - P(X=0) - P(X=1).$$

$$P(X=0) = \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^5 = \frac{32}{243}, \qquad P(X=1) = \binom{5
}{1}\cdot\frac{1}{3}\cdot\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^4 = 5 \cdot \frac{1}{3} \cdot
\frac{16}{81} = \frac{80}{243}.$$

$$P(X \ge 2) = 1 - \frac{32}{243} - \frac{80}{243} = 1 - \frac{112}{243} = \boxe
