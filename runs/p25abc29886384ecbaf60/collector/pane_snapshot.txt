**Yes**, the sequence $0 \to V \hat{\otimes} U \to W \hat{\otimes} U \to L \hat{

## Proof

The proof combines three classical facts about topological tensor products of lo
cally convex spaces (lcs). We work over $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$.

### Fact 1 (Right exactness of the projective tensor product)

Let $0 \to V \xrightarrow{i} W \xrightarrow{q} L \to 0$ be a strict exact sequen
ce of complete lcs (so $i$ is a closed topological embedding and $q$ is an open
surjection). For any complete lcs $U$, the projective completed tensor product s
atisfies:

- $q \hat{\otimes}_\pi \mathrm{id}: W \hat{\otimes}_\pi U \to L \hat{\otimes}_\p
i U$ is an **open surjection** (the topology on $L \hat{\otimes}_\pi U$ is the q
uotient topology from $W \hat{\otimes}_\pi U$).
- $\ker(q \hat{\otimes}_\pi \mathrm{id}) = \overline{\operatorname{im}(i \hat{\o
times}_\pi \mathrm{id})}$, the closure of the image of $V \hat{\otimes}_\pi U$ i
n $W \hat{\otimes}_\pi U$.

*Justification.* Algebraically, over a field, the tensor product is exact: $\ker
(q \otimes \mathrm{id}) = \operatorname{im}(i \otimes \mathrm{id})$ on the algeb
raic tensor products. The projective topology on $L \otimes_\pi U$ is the quotie
nt of the projective topology on $W \otimes_\pi U$ by $V \otimes_\pi U$. Upon co
mpletion, $\widehat{E/F} \cong \hat{E}/\overline{F}$, giving $L \hat{\otimes}_\p
i U \cong W \hat{\otimes}_\pi U / \overline{\operatorname{im}(i \hat{\otimes}_\p
i \mathrm{id})}$. $\square$

### Fact 2 (Left exactness of the injective tensor product)

If $i: V \hookrightarrow W$ is a closed topological embedding of lcs and $U$ is
any lcs, then:

$$i \hat{\otimes}_\varepsilon \mathrm{id}: V \hat{\otimes}_\varepsilon U \to W \
hat{\otimes}_\varepsilon U$$

is a **closed topological embedding** (the topology on $V \hat{\otimes}_\varepsi

*Justification.* The injective tensor product topology is the topology of unifor
m convergence on products of equicontinuous sets. If $V$ is a closed subspace of
 $W$, the restriction of equicontinuous sets in $W'$ to $V$ yields equicontinuou
s sets in $V'$, and the injective tensor product respects subspaces: $V \hat{\ot
imes}_\varepsilon U$ identifies as a closed topological subspace of $W \hat{\oti
mes}_\varepsilon U$. (See Treves, *Topological Vector Spaces, Distributions and
Kernels*, Ch. 43; or Schaefer–Wolff, *Topological Vector Spaces*, Ch. IV.) $\squ

### Fact 3 (Nuclearity: $\pi = \varepsilon$)

If $U$ is nuclear, then for every complete lcs $E$:

$$E \hat{\otimes}_\pi U \cong E \hat{\otimes}_\varepsilon U$$

as complete lcs (the projective and injective tensor products coincide). This is
 the defining property of nuclear spaces (Grothendieck, *Produits tensoriels top
ologiques et espaces nucléaires*).

Moreover, the completed tensor product of two nuclear spaces is nuclear, so $V \
hat{\otimes} U$, $W \hat{\otimes} U$, $L \hat{\otimes} U$ are again complete nuc
lear spaces.

### Combining the three facts

Since $U$ is nuclear, by Fact 3 we have $\hat{\otimes}_\pi = \hat{\otimes}_\vare
psilon =: \hat{\otimes}$ for all three tensor products. We verify the three cond
itions for strict exactness of

$$0 \to V \hat{\otimes} U \xrightarrow{\alpha} W \hat{\otimes} U \xrightarrow{\b
eta} L \hat{\otimes} U \to 0.$$

**(i) $\alpha$ is a closed topological embedding.** Since $\hat{\otimes} = \hat{
\otimes}_\varepsilon$ and $i: V \hookrightarrow W$ is a closed embedding, Fact 2
 gives that $\alpha = i \hat{\otimes}_\varepsilon \mathrm{id}$ is a closed topol
ogical embedding. In particular, $\operatorname{im}(\alpha)$ is **closed** in $W
 \hat{\otimes} U$, and the topology on $V \hat{\otimes} U$ is the subspace topol
ogy from $W \hat{\otimes} U$.

**(ii) $\beta$ is an open surjection.** Since $\hat{\otimes} = \hat{\otimes}_\pi
$ and $q: W \to L$ is an open surjection, Fact 1 gives that $\beta = q \hat{\oti
mes}_\pi \mathrm{id}$ is an open surjection. The topology on $L \hat{\otimes} U$
 is the quotient topology from $W \hat{\otimes} U$.

**(iii) Exactness in the middle: $\ker(\beta) = \operatorname{im}(\alpha)$.** By
 Fact 1, $\ker(\beta) = \overline{\operatorname{im}(\alpha)}$. By (i), $\operato
rname{im}(\alpha)$ is already closed. Therefore:

$$\ker(\beta) = \overline{\operatorname{im}(\alpha)} = \operatorname{im}(\alpha)
.$$

### Conclusion

The sequence $0 \to V \hat{\otimes} U \to W \hat{\otimes} U \to L \hat{\otimes}
U \to 0$ is exact, with $\alpha$ a closed topological embedding (subspace topolo
gy on $V \hat{\otimes} U$) and $\beta$ an open surjection (quotient topology on
$L \hat{\otimes} U$). All three spaces are complete nuclear. Hence the sequence
is a **strict short exact sequence of complete nuclear spaces**.
