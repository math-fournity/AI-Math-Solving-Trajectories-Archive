$$v \cdot 10^k \leq 3^n < (v+1) \cdot 10^k.$$

$$\log_{10}(v) + k \leq n \log_{10}(3) < \log_{10}(v+1) + k.$$

**The interval is non-empty.** Its length is $\log_{10}\!\big(\tfrac{v+1}{v}\big

+1}{v} \leq 2 < 10$, so this length is strictly less than 1. Therefore the image
