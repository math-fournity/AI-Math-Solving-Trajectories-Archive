# Problem

A specialized chemical refinery operates two independent processing lines to produce a high-grade lubricant. The first line processes a raw additive, where the concentration $x$ must be maintained between $0$ and $2$ units. The second line utilizes a cooling agent, where the flow rate $y$ is restricted between $0$ and $3$ liters per second. 

The facility’s main pressure vessel is governed by a strict stability equation: the sum of the additive's instability factor, calculated as $\frac{8}{x(2-x)}$, and the cooling agent’s thermal resistance, $\frac{1}{3-y}$, must exactly equal a constant pressure of $10$ bar.

The plant manager wants to minimize the net energy consumption of the system, which is determined by the formula:
$$\frac{12}{x} - \frac{y^2}{4}$$

Find the minimum possible value of this energy consumption.
