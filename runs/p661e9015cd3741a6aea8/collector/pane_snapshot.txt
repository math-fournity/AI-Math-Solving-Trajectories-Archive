# Solution

## Setup

Place $O$ at the origin. Let $A_i$ have position vector $v_i$ with polar coordin
ates $(r_i, \theta_i)$. The area of triangle $OA_iA_j$ is
$$\sigma(OA_iA_j) = \tfrac{1}{2}|[ij]|, \quad \text{where } [ij] := \det(v_i, v_
j) = r_i r_j \sin(\theta_j - \theta_i).$$

Set $d_{ij} := |[ij]|$, so the area equals $d_{ij}/2$. The constraint $d_{ij} \g
e 2$ for all six pairs, and we minimize $M = \frac{1}{2}\max d_{ij}$.

## Key Tool: Plücker Relation

For any four vectors in $\mathbb{R}^2$, the **Plücker relation** holds:
$$[12][34] - [13][24] + [14][23] = 0.$$

(Verified: this is the single Plücker relation for the $2\times 4$ matrix $[v_1\
; v_2\; v_3\; v_4]$.)

## Lower Bound: $M^2 \ge 2$

Order the points by angle: $\theta_1 < \theta_2 < \theta_3 < \theta_4$. Let the
angular gaps be $\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta$ with $\alpha+\beta+\gamma+\delta
=2\pi$.

**Case 1: Span $< \pi$ (all points in a semicircle).** All $[ij]>0$ for $i<j$, s
o the Plücker relation gives:
$$[13][24] = [12][34] + [14][23].$$
Since all $d_{ij} \ge 2$:
$$d_{13}\cdot d_{24} \ge d_{12}\cdot d_{34} + d_{14}\cdot d_{23} \ge 4 + 4 = 8.$
$
Therefore $\max(d_{13}, d_{24}) \ge \sqrt{8} = 2\sqrt{2}$, so $M \ge \sqrt{2}$.

**Case 2: Span $\ge \pi$.** Then $[14] < 0$. The Plücker relation becomes $[12][
34] - [13][24] - d_{14}[23] = 0$, i.e., $[13][24] = [12][34] - d_{14}\cdot d_{23
}$.

- **Sub-case 2a** ($[13],[24] > 0$): Need $[13][24] \ge 4$, so $[12][34] \ge 4 +
 d_{14}d_{23} \ge 8$, giving $\max(d_{12},d_{34}) \ge 2\sqrt{2}$.

- **Sub-case 2b** ($[13] < 0$): Plücker gives $[12][34] = d_{14}d_{23} - d_{13}d
_{24}$. Since $[12][34] > 0$ and $d_{13}d_{24} \ge 4$: $d_{14}d_{23} \ge 4 + [12
][34] \ge 8$, giving $\max(d_{14},d_{23}) \ge 2\sqrt{2}$.

- **Sub-case 2c** ($[24] < 0$): By symmetry, same conclusion: $\max(d_{14},d_{23
}) \ge 2\sqrt{2}$.

In **every** case, $\max d_{ij} \ge 2\sqrt{2}$, hence:
$$M \ge \sqrt{2}, \qquad M^2 \ge 2.$$

## Construction Achieving $M^2 = 2$

Place four points at equal distance $r$ from $O$, at angles $0, \frac{\pi}{4}, \
frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{3\pi}{4}$ (span $= \frac{3\pi}{4} < \pi$). Choose $r^2 = 2\s
qrt{2}$.

The six pairwise $|\sin(\theta_j - \theta_i)|$ values:

| Pair | Angle diff | $|\sin|$ | $d_{ij} = r^2|\sin|$ | Area $= d_{ij}/2$ |
|------|-----------|----------|----------------------|-------------------|
| $(1,2),(2,3),(3,4)$ | $\pi/4$ | $\sqrt{2}/2$ | $2$ | $1$ |
| $(1,3),(2,4)$ | $\pi/2$ | $1$ | $2\sqrt{2}$ | $\sqrt{2}$ |
| $(1,4)$ | $3\pi/4$ | $\sqrt{2}/2$ | $2$ | $1$ |

All six areas are $\ge 1$ ✓, and the maximum area is $\sqrt{2}$, so $M = \sqrt{2
}$.

## Verification of Optimality via Plücker (Case 1)

In this construction (span $< \pi$, Case 1): $[13][24] = [12][34] + [14][23]$ be
comes $2\sqrt{2}\cdot 2\sqrt{2} = 2\cdot 2 + 2\cdot 2$, i.e., $8 = 4 + 4$ ✓. Equ
ality holds throughout, confirming the bound is tight.

## Conclusion

The lower bound $M^2 \ge 2$ holds for all configurations, and the construction a
chieves $M^2 = 2$.

$$\boxed{M^2 = 2}$$
