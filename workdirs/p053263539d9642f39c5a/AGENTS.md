# Problem

A specialized aerospace engineering firm, "Super-Int Precision," produces carbon-fiber struts for triangular support frames. A frame is classified as "Grade-A" if the lengths of all three sides are positive integers and at least one of its three altitudes (the perpendicular distance from a vertex to the opposite side) is also a positive integer.

The firm maintains a "Standard Inventory List," which is a set $S$ of specific integer lengths. This list follows a strict regulatory requirement for completeness: if any two side lengths of a Grade-A frame are already present in the list $S$, then the third side length of that frame must also be included in $S$.

Currently, the engineers have confirmed that a strut of length 5 is in the inventory ($5 \in S$). There exists a minimum integer $n$ such that every possible integer length $k$ greater than or equal to $n$ is guaranteed to be included in the inventory $S$ based on the rules above.

Find the value of $n$.
