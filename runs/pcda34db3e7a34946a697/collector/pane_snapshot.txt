## Solution

**Setup.** A polynomial $P(x,y)$ is *balanced* if for every $r \ge 0$,
$$\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi} P(r\cos\theta,\, r\sin\theta)\,d\theta = 0.$$

**Key integral.** For a monomial $x^a y^b$, substituting $x=r\cos\theta,\; y=r\s
in\theta$ gives
$$\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi} r^{a+b}\cos^a\theta\,\sin^b\theta\,d\theta = r^{a+
b}\,I(a,b),$$
where $I(a,b)=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}\cos^a\theta\,\sin^b\theta\,d\theta$.

**Claim:** $I(a,b)=0$ if and only if at least one of $a,b$ is odd.

*Proof.* If $a+b$ is odd, then $\cos^a(\theta+\pi)\sin^b(\theta+\pi)=(-1)^{a+b}\
cos^a\theta\sin^b\theta=-\cos^a\theta\sin^b\theta$, so the integral over $[0,2\p
i]$ vanishes by the half-period cancellation. If $a+b$ is even but $b$ is odd (h
ence $a$ odd too), then the integrand is odd under $\theta\mapsto -\theta$ (sinc
e $\cos$ is even, $\sin$ is odd), so the integral over $[-\pi,\pi]$ vanishes. Th
e only remaining case is both $a,b$ even, where the integrand is nonnegative and
 not identically zero, so $I(a,b)>0$. $\square$

**Structure of the constraint.** Writing $P=\sum c_{a,b}x^a y^b$, the circle ave
rage is
$$A(r)=\sum_{\substack{a,b\ge 0\\ a,b\text{ both even}}} c_{a,b}\,r^{a+b}\,I(a,b
).$$
For $A(r)\equiv 0$, each power of $r$ must vanish independently. Grouping by tot
al degree $d=a+b$ (necessarily even), the constraint for each even $d$ is:
$$\sum_{\substack{a+b=d\\a,b\text{ even}}} c_{a,b}\,I(a,b)=0.$$
This is a single linear equation in the monomials of degree $d$ with both expone
nts even. Constraints for different $d$ involve disjoint sets of variables, henc
e are independent.

**Counting.**

*Total monomials* of degree $\le 2009$: $\binom{2009+2}{2}=\binom{2011}{2}=\frac
{2011\cdot 2010}{2}=2{,}021{,}055$.

*Number of constraints:* one per even $d\in\{0,2,4,\dots,2008\}$. Since $2009$ i
s odd, the largest even degree is $2008$, giving $\frac{2008}{2}+1=1005$ constra
ints.

Each constraint is nontrivial (the monomial $x^0 y^d=y^d$ with $d$ even always a
ppears with $I(0,d)>0$), and they are independent across different $d$.

**Dimension:**
$$\dim V = 2{,}021{,}055 - 1{,}005 = 2{,}020{,}050.$$

**Sanity check (degree $\le 2$).** Total monomials: $6$. Even degrees $0,2$: two
 constraints ($c_{0,0}=0$; $c_{2,0}I(2,0)+c_{0,2}I(0,2)=0$, i.e.\ $c_{x^2}+c_{y^
2}=0$). Free variables: $x,y,xy$ (3) plus $x^2-y^2$ (1) $=4=\binom{4}{2}-2$. ✓

$$\boxed{\dim V = 2{,}020{,}050}$$
