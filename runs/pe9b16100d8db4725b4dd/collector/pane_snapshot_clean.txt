## Solution

**Setup.** I live on the ground floor (floor 0). I draw a random permutation $\p
i$ of $\{1,2,\ldots,9\}$ and visit floors $\pi(1), \pi(2), \ldots, \pi(9)$ in or
der, taking the elevator each time. The total distance (in floors) is

$$T = |\pi(1) - 0| + \sum_{i=1}^{8} |\pi(i+1) - \pi(i)|.$$

Each floor is 4 meters, so the answer is $4\,\mathbb{E}[T]$.

**First leg: ground floor to $\pi(1)$.** Since $\pi(1) \geq 1 > 0$,

$$\mathbb{E}[|\pi(1) - 0|] = \mathbb{E}[\pi(1)] = \frac{1+2+\cdots+9}{9} = \frac
{45}{9} = 5.$$

**Consecutive legs.** For each $i = 1,\ldots,8$, the pair $(\pi(i), \pi(i+1))$ i
s uniformly distributed over all ordered pairs $(a,b)$ with $a \neq b$ in $\{1,\
ldots,9\}$. By symmetry the expectation is the same for every $i$; call it $D$:

$$D = \frac{1}{9 \cdot 8}\sum_{a \neq b} |a - b| = \frac{1}{72}\sum_{a=1}^{9}\su
m_{b=1}^{9}|a-b|.$$

For fixed $a$:

$$\sum_{b=1}^{9}|a-b| = \frac{a(a-1)}{2} + \frac{(9-a)(10-a)}{2}.$$

Summing over $a$ and using the symmetry $a \leftrightarrow 10-a$:

$$\sum_{a=1}^{9}\sum_{b=1}^{9}|a-b| = \sum_{a=1}^{9} a(a-1) = \sum_{a=1}^{9} a^2
 - \sum_{a=1}^{9} a = \frac{9\cdot10\cdot19}{6} - 45 = 285 - 45 = 240.$$

Therefore

$$D = \frac{240}{72} = \frac{10}{3}.$$

**Total expected distance in floors:**

$$\mathbb{E}[T] = 5 + 8 \cdot \frac{10}{3} = 5 + \frac{80}{3} = \frac{95}{3}.$$

**Converting to meters** (4 meters per floor):

$$4 \cdot \frac{95}{3} = \boxed{\dfrac{380}{3}} \text{ meters}.$$
