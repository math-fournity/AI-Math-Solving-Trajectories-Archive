## Solution

**Setup.** The parabola is $C: y=x^2$. The tangent line at $(t,t^2)$ with $0<t<1
$ has slope $2t$, so
$$l:\ y = 2tx - t^2.$$
This line meets the $x$-axis at $x=t/2$ and the line $x=1$ at $y=2t-t^2$.

**Computing $S_1$.** The region enclosed by $C$, $l$, and the $x$-axis is bounde
d by: the $x$-axis from $(0,0)$ to $(t/2,0)$, the tangent line from $(t/2,0)$ to
 $(t,t^2)$, and the parabola from $(t,t^2)$ back to $(0,0)$. Thus
$$S_1 = \int_0^{t/2} x^2\,dx + \int_{t/2}^{t}(2tx-t^2)\,dx = \frac{t^3}{24} + \l
eft[tx^2-t^2x\right]_{t/2}^{t} = \frac{t^3}{24} + \frac{t^3}{4} = \frac{7t^3}{24
}.$$

**Computing $S_2$.** Since $2t-t^2 = 1-(1-t)^2 < 1$ for $0<t<1$, the tangent lin
e lies below the parabola at $x=1$. The region enclosed by $C$, $l$, and $x=1$ r
uns from $x=t$ to $x=1$ with the parabola above and the tangent line below:
$$S_2 = \int_t^1 \bigl(x^2 - (2tx-t^2)\bigr)\,dx = \int_t^1 (x-t)^2\,dx = \frac{
(1-t)^3}{3}.$$

**Minimizing.** Let $f(t) = \dfrac{7t^3}{24} + \dfrac{(1-t)^3}{3}$. Then
$$f'(t) = \frac{7t^2}{8} - (1-t)^2.$$
Setting $f'(t)=0$: $\dfrac{7t^2}{8}=(1-t)^2$, and taking the positive root (sinc
e $0<t<1$):
$$t\sqrt{\tfrac{7}{8}} = 1-t \implies t = \frac{1}{1+\sqrt{7/8}} = \frac{1}{1+\s
qrt{14}/4} = \frac{4}{4+\sqrt{14}}.$$
Since $f''(t)=\frac{7t}{4}+2(1-t)>0$ on $(0,1)$, this critical point is the mini
mum.

**Evaluating.** With $t=\frac{4}{4+\sqrt{14}}$ and $1-t=\frac{\sqrt{14}}{4+\sqrt
{14}}$:
$$f(t) = \frac{7\cdot 64}{24(4+\sqrt{14})^3} + \frac{(\sqrt{14})^3}{3(4+\sqrt{14
})^3} = \frac{56+14\sqrt{14}}{3(4+\sqrt{14})^3} = \frac{14(4+\sqrt{14})}{3(4+\sq
rt{14})^3}.$$
Expanding $(4+\sqrt{14})^3 = 232+62\sqrt{14} = 2(116+31\sqrt{14})$, and factorin
g $116+31\sqrt{14}=(4+\sqrt{14})(15+4\sqrt{14})$ (verified: $4\cdot15+14\cdot4=1
16$, $15+4\cdot4=31$), we get
$$f(t)=\frac{14}{3(4+\sqrt{14})^2}=\frac{14}{3(15+4\sqrt{14})}.$$
Rationalizing:
$$f(t)=\frac{14(15-4\sqrt{14})}{3(225-224)}=\frac{14(15-4\sqrt{14})}{3}=\frac{21
0-56\sqrt{14}}{3}=\frac{105-28\sqrt{14}}{3}.$$

**The minimum value of $S_1+S_2$ is $\dfrac{105-28\sqrt{14}}{3}$.**
