## Solution

I place the triangle in coordinates. Let $B = (0,0)$, $C = (14,0)$. Since $AB=13
$ and $CA=15$:
$$x_A^2 + y_A^2 = 169, \quad (x_A-14)^2 + y_A^2 = 225 \implies x_A = 5,\; y_A =
12.$$
So $A=(5,12)$, $B=(0,0)$, $C=(14,0)$, with area $84$. The triangle is counterclo
ckwise, so "outside" each edge means to the **right** of the directed edge, i.e.
, rotate the edge vector by $-90^\circ$: $(x,y)\mapsto (y,-x)$.

### Area of hexagon $A_5A_6B_5B_6C_5C_6$

Vertices in order: $(61,81),\; (-59,61),\; (-79,13),\; (-19,-82),\; (37,-82),\;
(97,33)$.

By the shoelace formula, $\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}\sum (x_i\, y_{i+1} - x_{i+1}
\, y_i)$:

$$\begin{aligned}
&(61)(61)-(-59)(81) = 3721+4779 = 8500\\
&(-59)(13)-(-79)(61) = -767+4819 = 4052\\
&(-79)(-82)-(-19)(13) = 6478+247 = 6725\\
&(-19)(-82)-(37)(-82) = 1558+3034 = 4592\\
&(37)(33)-(97)(-82) = 1221+7954 = 9175\\
&(97)(81)-(61)(33) = 7857-2013 = 5844
\end{aligned}$$

$$\text{Sum} = 8500+4052+6725+4592+9175+5844 = 38888.$$

$$\text{Area} = \frac{38888}{2} = \boxed{19444}.$$
