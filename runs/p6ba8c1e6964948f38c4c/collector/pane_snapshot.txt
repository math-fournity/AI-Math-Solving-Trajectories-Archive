## Proof

Define the kernel $K:[0,1]^2\to\mathbb{R}$ by
$$K(x,t)=\mathbf{1}_{[\,x^2,\,x\,]}(t)=\begin{cases}1,& x^2\le t\le x,\\0,&\text
{otherwise.}\end{cases}$$
Since $0\le x^2\le x\le 1$ for $x\in[0,1]$, the interval $[x^2,x]$ is well-defin
ed, and
$$Tu(x)=\int_{0}^{1}K(x,t)\,u(t)\,dt.$$

**Step 1. $K\in L^2([0,1]^2)$.**
$$\iint_{[0,1]^2}|K(x,t)|^2\,dx\,dt=\int_0^1\!\int_{x^2}^{x}1\,dt\,dx=\int_0^1(x
-x^2)\,dx=\frac12-\frac13=\frac16<\infty.$$

**Step 2. $T$ is a Hilbert–Schmidt operator, hence compact.**
For $u\in L^2([0,1])$,
$$\|Tu\|_2^2=\int_0^1\!\left|\int_0^1 K(x,t)u(t)\,dt\right|^2 dx\le\int_0^1\!\le
ft(\int_0^1|K(x,t)|^2dt\right)\!\left(\int_0^1|u(t)|^2dt\right)dx=\|K\|_{L^2}^2\
,\|u\|_2^2.$$
Thus $T$ is bounded with $\|T\|\le\|K\|_{L^2([0,1]^2)}=1/\sqrt6$, and being an i
ntegral operator with $L^2$ kernel, $T$ is Hilbert–Schmidt and therefore compact
 (every Hilbert–Schmidt operator on $L^2$ is compact).

**Step 3. Compact operators map bounded sets to precompact sets.**
A compact operator between Banach spaces sends bounded sets to relatively compac
t (i.e. precompact) sets. Since $B_1=\{u:\|u\|_2\le1\}$ is bounded and $T:L^2([0
,1])\to L^2([0,1])$ is compact, $T(B_1)$ is relatively compact in $L^2([0,1])$,
i.e. its closure is compact.

**Conclusion.** $T(B_1)$ is precompact in $L^2([0,1])$.
