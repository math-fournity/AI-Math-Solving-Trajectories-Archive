## Solution

We want $\prod_{n=1}^{32}(1-z^n)^{b_n} \equiv 1-2z \pmod{z^{33}}$, with all $b_n
$ positive integers.

**Step 1: Logarithmic reduction.**

For a power series $P(z)$ with constant term $1$, the formal logarithm is well-d
efined and injective modulo $z^{33}$ (since $\exp$ and $\log$ are mutual inverse
s on series with constant term $1$, working modulo any power of $z$). Taking log
arithms of both sides:

$$\log P(z) = \sum_{n=1}^{32} b_n \log(1-z^n).$$

Using $\log(1-z^n) = -\sum_{k\geq 1} \frac{z^{nk}}{k}$, and collecting the coeff
icient of $z^m$:

$$\log P(z) = -\sum_{m\geq 1} \frac{z^m}{m}\sum_{n\mid m} n\, b_n.$$

Define $a_m := \sum_{n\mid m} n\, b_n$. On the other hand,

$$\log(1-2z) = -\sum_{m\geq 1}\frac{(2z)^m}{m} = -\sum_{m\geq 1}\frac{2^m}{m}z^m
.$$

Equating coefficients of $z^m$ for $m=1,\dots,32$ gives

$$a_m = \sum_{n\mid m} n\, b_n = 2^m, \qquad m=1,\dots,32. \tag{$\ast$}$$

**Step 2: Möbius inversion.**

Applying Möbius inversion to $(\ast)$ (with $f(m)=2^m$ and $g(n)=n\,b_n$):

$$m\, b_m = \sum_{n\mid m}\mu(m/n)\,2^n = \sum_{d\mid m}\mu(d)\,2^{m/d}.$$

This is Moreau's necklace-counting function $M(2,m)$, which counts aperiodic bin
ary necklaces of length $m$. It is a classical result that $m \mid M(2,m)$ and $
M(2,m)>0$ for all $m\geq 1$, so every $b_m = M(2,m)/m$ is a positive integer — c
onfirming the existence and uniqueness of the solution.

**Step 3: Compute $b_{32}$.**

Since $32 = 2^5$, its divisors are $1,2,4,8,16,32$, and $\mu(d)\neq 0$ only for
$d=1$ ($\mu=1$) and $d=2$ ($\mu=-1$). Therefore

$$32\, b_{32} = \mu(1)\cdot 2^{32} + \mu(2)\cdot 2^{16} = 2^{32} - 2^{16}.$$

Hence

$$b_{32} = \frac{2^{32}-2^{16}}{32} = \frac{2^{32}}{2^5} - \frac{2^{16}}{2^5} =
2^{27} - 2^{11}.$$

**Verification of the reduction (sanity check).** For $m=1$: $b_1 = 2$, giving $
(1-z)^2 \equiv 1-2z \pmod{z^2}$. ✓. For $m=2$: $2b_2 = 2^2-2=2$, so $b_2=1$, and
 $(1-z)^2(1-z^2) = 1-2z+2z^3-z^4 \equiv 1-2z \pmod{z^3}$. ✓. The pattern continu
es: the logarithmic identity $a_m=2^m$ for $m\leq 32$ guarantees $P(z)\equiv 1-2
z\pmod{z^{33}}$.

**Answer.**

$$\boxed{b_{32} = 2^{27} - 2^{11}}$$
