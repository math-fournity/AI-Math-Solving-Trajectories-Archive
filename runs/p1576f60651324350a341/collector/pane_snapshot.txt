$$N = \frac{(3n)!}{(n!)^3} = \binom{3n}{n}\binom{2n}{n}.$$

Since $3n = \frac{3(p-1)}{2} = p + \frac{p-3}{2} = p + (n-1)$, we have $3n = p +

**Step 1: Compute $\binom{3n}{n} = \binom{p+n-1}{n} \pmod{p^2}$.**

$$\binom{p+n-1}{n} = \frac{(p+n-1)!}{n!\,(p-1)!} = \frac{\prod_{j=0}^{n-1}(p+j)}
{n!} = \frac{p\prod_{j=1}^{n-1}(p+j)}{n!}.$$

Writing $p+j = j(1 + p/j)$:

$$= \frac{p\cdot(n-1)!}{n!}\prod_{j=1}^{n-1}\left(1+\frac{p}{j}\right) = \frac{p
}{n}\prod_{j=1}^{n-1}\left(1+\frac{p}{j}\right) \equiv \frac{p}{n}\left(1 + pH_{
n-1}\right) \pmod{p^2},$$

where $H_{n-1} = \sum_{j=1}^{n-1}\frac{1}{j}$.

---

**Step 2: Compute $\binom{2n}{n} = \binom{p-1}{n} \pmod{p^2}$.**

Since $p - 1 - n = n$:

$$\binom{p-1}{n} = \prod_{k=1}^{n}\frac{p-k}{k} = \prod_{k=1}^{n}\frac{-(k-p)}{k
} = (-1)^n\prod_{k=1}^{n}\left(1-\frac{p}{k}\right) \equiv (-1)^n\left(1 - pH_n\
right) \pmod{p^2},$$

where $H_n = \sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac{1}{k}$.

**Step 3: Combine.**

$$N \equiv \frac{p}{n}\left(1+pH_{n-1}\right)\cdot(-1)^n\left(1-pH_n\right) \pmo

$$\left(1+pH_{n-1}\right)\left(1-pH_n\right) \equiv 1 + p(H_{n-1}-H_n) = 1 - \fr
ac{p}{n} \pmod{p^2},$$

since $H_n - H_{n-1} = \frac{1}{n}$.

**Step 4: Evaluate $\frac{p}{n} \pmod{p^2}$.**

Since $2n = p-1 \equiv -1 \pmod{p}$, we get $n^{-1} \equiv -2 \pmod{p}$, hence

$$\frac{p}{n} = p\cdot n^{-1} \equiv -2p \pmod{p^2}.$$

Therefore $1 - \frac{p}{n} \equiv 1 + 2p \pmod{p^2}$, and

$$N \equiv (-2p)\cdot(-1)^n\cdot(1+2p) \equiv (-1)^n(-2p)(1+2p) \pmod{p^2}.$$

Since $(-2p)(1+2p) = -2p - 4p^2 \equiv -2p \pmod{p^2}$:

$$N \equiv (-1)^n\cdot(-2p) = (-1)^{n+1}\cdot 2p \pmod{p^2}.$$

$$\boxed{N \equiv (-1)^{\frac{p+1}{2}}\cdot 2p \pmod{p^2}.}$$
