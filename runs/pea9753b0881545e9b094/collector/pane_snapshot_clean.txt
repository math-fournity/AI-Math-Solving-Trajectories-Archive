## Solution

**Model.** Let $T$ be the cycle at which failure first occurs (first time $n=0$
is drawn). Since each draw is independent with $P(n=0)=1/5$, we have $T\sim\text
{Geometric}(1/5)$, i.e. $P(T=t)=(4/5)^{t-1}(1/5)$ for $t\ge 1$.

**Canister lifecycle.** A canister placed at cycle $c$ starts at Station 0. In e
ach subsequent non-failure cycle it advances by $n\in\{1,2,3,4\}$. Since all ste
ps are positive, the cumulative advancement $S_k=n_c+\cdots+n_k$ is strictly inc
reasing. The canister is offloaded (earning 1 credit) the first time $S_k\ge 4$,
 i.e., when it reaches or passes Station 0. The canister placed at cycle $c$ rec
eives exactly $T-c$ advancement steps (cycles $c,\dots,T-1$; at cycle $T$ the sy
stem fails). Thus it earns a credit iff the sum of $T-c$ i.i.d. $\text{Unif}\{1,
2,3,4\}$ values reaches $\ge 4$.

**Per-canister success probability.** Define $g(m)=P(\text{sum of }m\text{ i.i.d
. Unif}\{1,2,3,4\}\ge 4)$. Since each value $\ge 1$:
- $g(0)=0$,
- $g(1)=P(n\ge 4)=\tfrac{1}{4}$,
- $g(2)=1-P(\text{sum}\le 3)=1-\tfrac{3}{16}=\tfrac{13}{16}$,
- $g(3)=1-P(\text{sum}=3)=1-\tfrac{1}{64}=\tfrac{63}{64}$,
- $g(m)=1$ for $m\ge 4$ (sum $\ge m\ge 4$).

**Linearity of expectation.** By linearity, with $m=T-c$:

$$E[\text{credits}]=\sum_{c=1}^{\infty}\sum_{t=c}^{\infty}P(T=t)\,g(t-c)=\sum_{c
=1}^{\infty}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}(4/5)^{c+m-1}(1/5)\,g(m).$$

Separating the sums:

$$=\Bigl(\sum_{c=1}^{\infty}(4/5)^{c-1}\Bigr)\cdot\frac{1}{5}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}
(4/5)^m g(m)=5\cdot\frac{1}{5}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}(4/5)^m g(m)=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty
}(4/5)^m g(m).$$

**Evaluating the series:**

| $m$ | $(4/5)^m$ | $g(m)$ | product |
|-----|-----------|--------|---------|
| 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| 1 | $4/5$ | $1/4$ | $1/5$ |
| 2 | $16/25$ | $13/16$ | $13/25$ |
| 3 | $64/125$ | $63/64$ | $63/125$ |
| $\ge 4$ | — | 1 | $\sum_{m=4}^{\infty}(4/5)^m=\frac{(4/5)^4}{1/5}=\frac{256}{1
25}$ |

$$E[\text{credits}]=\frac{1}{5}+\frac{13}{25}+\frac{63}{125}+\frac{256}{125}=\fr
ac{25+65+63+256}{125}=\frac{409}{125}.$$

**Verification (alternative conditioning on $T$).** $E[\text{credits}\mid T=t]=\
sum_{m=0}^{t-1}g(m)$. Computing $E[\text{credits}]=\sum_t P(T=t)\,E[\text{credit
s}\mid T=t]$ yields $\frac{2045}{625}=\frac{409}{125}$, confirming the result.

**Reducibility.** $409$ is prime (checked against all primes $\le\sqrt{409}\appr
ox 20.2$) and $125=5^3$, so $\gcd(409,125)=1$. The fraction is irreducible.

$$a+b=409+125=\boxed{534}.$$
