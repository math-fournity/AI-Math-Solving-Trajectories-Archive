Looking at this problem, I need to determine whether pointwise boundedness of $\
{T_n\}$ on a closed convex subset $A$ of the unit ball implies uniform boundedne
ss on $A$.

**Answer: Yes.**

**Proof.**

Define the closed sets
$$A_m = \{x \in A : \sup_n \|T_n x\| \le m\} = \bigcap_{n=1}^\infty \{x \in A :
\|T_n x\| \le m\}, \quad m = 1, 2, \dots$$

Each $A_m$ is closed in $A$ (intersection of closed sets, since each $T_n$ is co
ntinuous).

**Step 1: $A = \bigcup_{m=1}^\infty A_m$.**

By hypothesis, for every $x \in A$, $\sup_n \|T_n x\| < \infty$, so $x \in A_m$
for some integer $m \ge \sup_n \|T_n x\|$.

**Step 2: Baire category gives a relative interior.**

Since $A$ is a closed subset of the Banach space $X$, it is a complete metric sp
ace, hence a Baire space. The countable union $A = \bigcup_m A_m$ of closed sets
 forces some $A_{m_0}$ to have nonempty **relative interior** in $A$. That is, t
here exist $x_0 \in A$ and $\varepsilon > 0$ such that
$$B(x_0, \varepsilon) \cap A \subseteq A_{m_0}, \quad \text{i.e., } \sup_n \|T_n
 x\| \le m_0 \text{ for all } x \in B(x_0,\varepsilon)\cap A.$$

Note $x_0 \in A_{m_0}$, so $\sup_n \|T_n x_0\| \le m_0$.

**Step 3: Convexity propagates the local bound globally.**

If $\varepsilon > 2$, then $B(x_0, \varepsilon) \supseteq \overline{B(0,1)} \sup
seteq A$ (since $\|x_0\| \le 1$ and $A$ is in the unit ball), so $A \subseteq A_
{m_0}$ and $\sup_n \sup_{x \in A} \|T_n x\| \le m_0 < \infty$. Done.

Otherwise, set $t = \varepsilon/4 \in (0, 1/2]$. For any $z \in A$, the convex c
ombination
$$w = (1-t)\,x_0 + t\,z \in A$$
satisfies
$$\|w - x_0\| = t\,\|z - x_0\| \le t \cdot 2 = \frac{\varepsilon}{2} < \varepsil
on,$$
since $\|z - x_0\| \le \|z\| + \|x_0\| \le 2$. Thus $w \in B(x_0, \varepsilon) \
cap A \subseteq A_{m_0}$, giving $\sup_n \|T_n w\| \le m_0$.

Now $t(z - x_0) = w - x_0$, so
$$\sup_n \|T_n(z - x_0)\| = \frac{1}{t}\sup_n \|T_n(w - x_0)\| \le \frac{1}{t}\b
igl(\sup_n\|T_n w\| + \sup_n\|T_n x_0\|\bigr) \le \frac{2m_0}{t} = \frac{8m_0}{\
varepsilon}.$$

Therefore
$$\sup_n \|T_n z\| \le \sup_n \|T_n(z - x_0)\| + \sup_n \|T_n x_0\| \le \frac{8m
_0}{\varepsilon} + m_0.$$

The right-hand side is **independent of $z$**, so
$$\sup_n \sup_{x \in A} \|T_n x\| \le \frac{8m_0}{\varepsilon} + m_0 < \infty.$$

**Conclusion.** The pointwise boundedness hypothesis, combined with the Baire ca
tegory theorem applied to the complete metric space $A$ and the convexity of $A$

uniform boundedness of $\sup_{x \in A}\|T_n x\|$ in $n$. $\blacksquare$
