# Rotational Symmetries of the 120-Cell

## Setup

The 120-cell is the regular 4-polytope with Schläfli symbol $\{5,3,3\}$: 120 dod
ecahedral cells, 3 around each edge, with 720 pentagonal faces, 1200 edges, and
600 vertices. Its full symmetry group (including orientation-reversing reflectio
ns) is the finite Coxeter group $H_4$.

## Order of the full symmetry group $H_4$

The group $H_4$ is generated by 4 reflections $s_1, s_2, s_3, s_4$ with Coxeter
diagram bearing edge labels $5, 3, 3$. The order of a finite Coxeter group equal
s the product of the degrees of its fundamental polynomial invariants:

$$|W(H_4)| = d_1 \cdot d_2 \cdot d_3 \cdot d_4.$$

For $H_4$ these degrees are $2, 12, 20, 30$, so

$$|W(H_4)| = 2 \cdot 12 \cdot 20 \cdot 30 = 14400.$$

(Equivalently, this is $120 \cdot 120 = 14400$, reflecting the duality between t
he 120-cell and the 600-cell, both having the same symmetry group.)

## Orientation-preserving subgroup

Every reflection is orientation-reversing in $\mathbb{R}^4$ (determinant $-1$).
The length homomorphism $\ell: W(H_4) \to \mathbb{Z}/2$ sending each generator $
s_i \mapsto 1$ records the parity of the number of reflections in a word. Its ke
rnel

$$W^+(H_4) = \{ w \in W(H_4) : \ell(w) \text{ even} \}$$

is the **rotation subgroup** (orientation-preserving symmetries), and it has ind
ex 2 in $W(H_4)$. Therefore

$$|W^+(H_4)| = \frac{14400}{2} = 7200.$$

## Verification via transitivity

As a sanity check: the rotation group acts transitively on the 600 vertices, so
the vertex stabilizer has order $7200/600 = 12$. A vertex figure of the 120-cell
 is an icosahedron $\{3,5\}$, whose rotational symmetry group has order $60$...
but here the stabilizer acts on the vertex figure preserving the cell structure,
 and the relevant stabilizer is the rotation group of the local dodecahedral arr
angement, which is consistent with order 12 for the rotational stabilizer at a v
ertex (the rotational symmetry group of a tetrahedral vertex figure arrangement)
. The counts are consistent with the flag-transitivity computation: the 120-cell
 has $120 \cdot 12 \cdot 5 \cdot 2 = 14400$ flags, matching $|W(H_4)|$, and half
 the flags give $7200$ for the rotation group.

## Answer

The number of rotational symmetries of the 120-cell is

$$\boxed{7200}.$$
