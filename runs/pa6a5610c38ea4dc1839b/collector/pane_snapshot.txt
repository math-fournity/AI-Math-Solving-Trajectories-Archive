## Solution

**Ellipse parameters:** From $\frac{x^2}{4}+\frac{y^2}{3}=1$: $a^2=4,\ b^2=3$, s

### Part 1: Maximum inscribed circle radius of $\triangle PF_1F_2$

Let $P=(x_0,y_0)$ on the ellipse. The inradius is $r = \dfrac{\text{Area}}{s}$,
where $s$ is the semi-perimeter.

**Area:** Taking $F_1F_2$ as base (length 2), the height is $|y_0|$, so
$$\text{Area} = \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot 2\cdot |y_0| = |y_0|.$$

**Semi-perimeter:** By the ellipse focal property, $|PF_1|+|PF_2| = 2a = 4$. Hen
ce
$$s = \frac{|PF_1|+|PF_2|+|F_1F_2|}{2} = \frac{4+2}{2} = 3.$$

Therefore
$$r = \frac{|y_0|}{3}.$$

This is maximized when $|y_0|$ is maximal. Since $|y_0|\le b=\sqrt{3}$ (attained
 at $P=(0,\pm\sqrt{3})$),
$$r_{\max} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}.$$

### Part 2: Coordinates of $P$ when $\triangle POF_2$ is isosceles

Here $O=(0,0)$, $F_2=(1,0)$. We need two of $|OP|,\ |OF_2|,\ |PF_2|$ equal, with

Let $P=(x,y)$ with $y^2 = 3-\frac{3x^2}{4}$. Note $|OF_2|=1$ and $|PF_2| = a - e
x = 2-\frac{x}{2}$ (with $e=\frac{c}{a}=\frac{1}{2}$). Also $|OP|^2 = x^2+y^2 =
\frac{x^2}{4}+3$.

**Case 1: $|OP|=|OF_2|=1$.** Then $x^2+y^2=1$, giving $y^2=1-x^2$. Substituting
into the ellipse: $\frac{x^2}{4}+\frac{1-x^2}{3}=1 \Rightarrow 3x^2+4-4x^2=12 \R

**Case 2: $|OP|=|PF_2|$.** Squaring $\sqrt{\frac{x^2}{4}+3}=2-\frac{x}{2}$:
$$\frac{x^2}{4}+3 = 4-2x+\frac{x^2}{4} \Rightarrow 3=4-2x \Rightarrow x=\frac{1}

Then $y^2 = 3-\frac{3}{16}=\frac{45}{16}$, so $y=\pm\frac{3\sqrt{5}}{4}$.

Verification: $|OP|^2=\frac{1}{16}+3=\frac{49}{16}\Rightarrow |OP|=\frac{7}{4}$;
