## Proof

### Direction 1: $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(f\,\|\,g)=\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
f(x)\log\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}\,dx$

Compute the log-ratio:
$$\log\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}=\log f(x)-\log g(x)=\Bigl(-\tfrac12\log(2\pi)-\tfrac{x^{
2}}2\Bigr)-\Bigl(-\log\pi-\log(1+x^{2})\Bigr)$$
$$=-\tfrac12\log(2\pi)+\log\pi-\tfrac{x^{2}}2+\log(1+x^{2}).$$

Hence
$$D_{\mathrm{KL}}(f\,\|\,g)=-\tfrac12\log(2\pi)+\log\pi-\tfrac12\,\mathbb E_f[x^
{2}]+\mathbb E_f\!\bigl[\log(1+x^{2})\bigr].$$

- $\mathbb E_f[x^{2}]=\operatorname{Var}_f(X)=1<\infty$.
- For $\mathbb E_f[\log(1+x^{2})]$: using $\log(1+u)\le u$ for $u\ge0$ with $u=x

$$\mathbb E_f\!\bigl[\log(1+x^{2})\bigr]\le \mathbb E_f[x^{2}]=1<\infty.$$
  (Non-negativity of $\log(1+x^{2})$ gives the lower bound $0$, so the expectati

Every term is finite, therefore
$$\boxed{D_{\mathrm{KL}}(f\,\|\,g)<\infty.}$$
