## Lower Bound: 17

**Coloring argument.** Color each cell $(i,j)$ of the 13×13 board (with $0 \le i
,j \le 12$) by the pair $(i \bmod 2,\; j \bmod 2)$, giving 4 color classes.

**Each 2×6 piece covers exactly 3 cells of each color class**, regardless of ori
entation:
- *Horizontal* (2 rows × 6 cols): the 2 rows give one even and one odd row-parit
y; the 6 columns give three even and three odd column-parities. So each of the 4
 classes gets $1 \times 3 = 3$ cells.
- *Vertical* (6 rows × 2 cols): symmetric — three even/odd row-parities, one eve
n/odd column-parity. Again 3 cells per class.

**Count the $(0,0)$ class** (even row, even column): even rows are $\{0,2,4,6,8,
10,12\}$ (7 rows), even columns are $\{0,2,4,6,8,10,12\}$ (7 columns), giving $7
 \times 7 = 49$ cells.

If $k$ pieces are used, they cover at most $3k$ cells of color $(0,0)$. To cover
 all 49:
$$3k \ge 49 \implies k \ge \lceil 49/3 \rceil = 17$$
since $16 \times 3 = 48 < 49$. **So at least 17 pieces are needed.**

## Construction: 17 pieces suffice

- This gives $6 \times 2 = 12$ pieces covering all 144 cells of the 12×12 region

**Step 2 — Cover the L-shaped remainder** (column 12, all 13 rows; row 12, all 1
