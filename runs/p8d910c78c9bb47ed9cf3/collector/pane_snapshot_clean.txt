## Proof

**Setup.** Let $p(x)=ax^3+bx^2+cx+d$ with $\deg p\le 3$. Since $p''(x)=6ax+2b$ i
s affine in $x$, the function $|p''(x)|$ is convex, hence
$$\max_{|x|\le 1}|p''(x)|=\max\bigl(|p''(1)|,\,|p''(-1)|\bigr).$$
So it suffices to bound $|p''(\pm1)|$.

**Expressing $a,b$ via the four constraint values.** Set
$$y_1=p(1),\quad y_2=p(-1),\quad y_3=p(\tfrac12),\quad y_4=p(-\tfrac12),\qquad |
y_i|\le 1.$$
The four interpolation conditions give:
- $p(1)+p(-1)=2b+2d \Rightarrow b+d=\frac{y_1+y_2}{2}$,
- $p(1)-p(-1)=2a+2c \Rightarrow a+c=\frac{y_1-y_2}{2}$,
- $p(\frac12)+p(-\frac12)=\frac b2+2d \Rightarrow \frac b2+2d=y_3+y_4$,
- $p(\frac12)-p(-\frac12)=\frac a4+c \Rightarrow \frac a4+c=y_3-y_4$.

Solving for $a$ and $b$:
$$b=\tfrac23(y_1+y_2-y_3-y_4),\qquad a=\tfrac23(y_1-y_2-2y_3+2y_4).$$

**Computing $p''(\pm1)$.**
$$p''(1)=6a+2b=\tfrac43\bigl(4y_1-2y_2-7y_3+5y_4\bigr),$$
$$p''(-1)=-6a+2b=\tfrac43\bigl(-2y_1+4y_2+5y_3-7y_4\bigr).$$

**Upper bound.** By the triangle inequality and $|y_i|\le1$:
$$|4y_1-2y_2-7y_3+5y_4|\le 4+2+7+5=18,$$
$$|-2y_1+4y_2+5y_3-7y_4|\le 2+4+5+7=18.$$
Therefore
$$\max_{|x|\le1}|p''(x)|=\max\bigl(|p''(1)|,|p''(-1)|\bigr)\le \tfrac43\cdot 18=
24.$$

**Achievability.** Take $p(x)=4x^3-3x$ (the Chebyshev-like cubic). Then
$$p(1)=1,\quad p(-1)=-1,\quad p(\tfrac12)=\tfrac12-\tfrac32=-1,\quad p(-\tfrac12
)=-\tfrac12+\tfrac32=1,$$
so $p\in A$. Moreover $p''(x)=24x$, giving $|p''(\pm1)|=24$. Thus the bound $24$
 is attained.

**Conclusion.**
$$\sup_{p\in A,\;|x|\le1}|p''(x)|=24.$$

### PROOF COMPLETE
