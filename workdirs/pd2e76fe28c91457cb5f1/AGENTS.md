# Problem

In the high-tech logistics hub of Sector 7, a technician named Dima is tasked with auditing 100 specialized data canisters. Each canister has a unique, distinct weight, though the differences are too subtle to detect by hand. 

To organize them, Dima uses a high-capacity differential pressure scale. This machine has a strict operational constraint: it will only activate and provide a reading if exactly 10 canisters are placed on the left loading tray and exactly 10 canisters are placed on the right loading tray. Once loaded, the scale indicates which side is heavier. Dima can perform this measurement as many times as he likes with any combinations of canisters.

A pair of canisters is classified as "Identified" if Dima can definitively determine which individual canister in that pair is heavier than the other based on the results of his measurements.

What is the minimum possible number of "Identified" pairs Dima can have across the entire set of 100 canisters?
