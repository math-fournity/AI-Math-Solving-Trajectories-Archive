## Proof

Let $\mathcal{C}$ denote the set of all infinite sequences $(s_1, s_2, s_3, \dot
s)$ of finite binary strings such that each $s_i$ is a proper prefix of $s_{i+1}
$ (the "increasing" condition being exactly that each term strictly extends the
previous one). We claim $|\mathcal{C}| = 2^{\aleph_0}$.

**Bijection with pairs (infinite path, length sequence).**

Given a chain $C = (s_1, s_2, \dots) \in \mathcal{C}$, define:
- $\alpha \in \{0,1\}^{\omega}$ to be the unique infinite binary sequence of whi
ch every $s_i$ is a prefix (uniqueness: since the lengths $|s_i|$ grow without b
ound, the value of $\alpha$ on every coordinate is eventually determined);
- $(n_i) = (|s_1|, |s_2|, |s_3|, \dots)$, a strictly increasing sequence of natu
ral numbers (with $n_i \geq 0$).

Conversely, given any infinite binary sequence $\alpha \in \{0,1\}^{\omega}$ and
 any strictly increasing sequence $(n_i)$ of natural numbers, the chain
$$s_i = \alpha\!\upharpoonright\! n_i \quad (\text{the prefix of } \alpha \text{
 of length } n_i)$$
belongs to $\mathcal{C}$, since each $s_i$ is a proper prefix of $s_{i+1}$.

These two constructions are mutually inverse: from a chain we recover $\alpha$ a
nd $(n_i)$, and from $(\alpha, (n_i))$ we recover the chain. Hence
$$\mathcal{C} \;\cong\; \{0,1\}^{\omega} \times \mathcal{N},$$
where $\mathcal{N}$ is the set of infinite strictly increasing sequences of natu
ral numbers.

**Cardinality of each factor.**

- $|\{0,1\}^{\omega}| = 2^{\aleph_0}$ (the Cantor set).
- An infinite strictly increasing sequence of naturals is the same thing as an i
nfinite subset of $\mathbb{N}$ (listed in increasing order). The set of all subs
ets of $\mathbb{N}$ has cardinality $2^{\aleph_0}$, and the set of *finite* subs
ets of $\mathbb{N}$ is countable. Therefore the set of infinite subsets has card
inality $2^{\aleph_0} - \aleph_0 = 2^{\aleph_0}$. So $|\mathcal{N}| = 2^{\aleph_
0}$.

**Conclusion.**

$$|\mathcal{C}| = 2^{\aleph_0} \cdot 2^{\aleph_0} = 2^{\aleph_0}.$$

The cardinality of the set of infinite increasing sequences of finite binary str
ings (each a prefix of the next) is $\boxed{2^{\aleph_0}}$, the cardinality of t
he continuum.
