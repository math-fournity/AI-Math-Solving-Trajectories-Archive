## Solution

We want the largest integer $n$ such that $2^n \mid 7^{2048}-1$, i.e. $v_2(7^{20
48}-1)$.

**Lifting-the-Exponent Lemma (LTE) for $p=2$.** For odd $a$ and even positive in
teger $k$,
$$v_2(a^k-1) = v_2(a-1) + v_2(a+1) + v_2(k) - 1.$$

**Apply with $a=7$, $k=2048$:**

- $v_2(7-1) = v_2(6) = 1$,
- $v_2(7+1) = v_2(8) = 3$,
- $v_2(2048) = v_2(2^{11}) = 11$.

Therefore
$$v_2(7^{2048}-1) = 1 + 3 + 11 - 1 = 14.$$

**Sanity checks.**
- $7^2-1 = 48 = 2^4 \cdot 3$, so $v_2 = 4$. Formula gives $1+3+1-1 = 4$. ✓
- $7^4-1 = 2400 = 2^5 \cdot 75$, so $v_2 = 5$. Formula gives $1+3+2-1 = 5$. ✓

The largest integer $n$ such that $2^n \mid 7^{2048}-1$ is

$$\boxed{14}$$
