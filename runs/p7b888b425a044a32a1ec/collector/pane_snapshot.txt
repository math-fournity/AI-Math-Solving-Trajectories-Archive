## Proof

We compute the Lebesgue outer measure $m^*(E)$ of
$$E = (\mathbb{Q}\times\mathbb{R})\cup(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{Q})\subseteq\math
bb{R}^2.$$

**Step 1: $\mathbb{Q}\times\mathbb{R}$ has outer measure $0$.**

Enumerate the rationals as $\mathbb{Q}=\{q_1,q_2,q_3,\dots\}$. Then
$$\mathbb{Q}\times\mathbb{R}=\bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty}\bigl(\{q_n\}\times\mathbb{R}
\bigr).$$

Each "vertical line" $L_n=\{q_n\}\times\mathbb{R}$ has outer measure $0$. Indeed
, fix $n$ and $\varepsilon>0$. For each integer $k\in\mathbb{Z}$, cover the segm
ent $\{q_n\}\times[k,k+1]$ by the open rectangle
$$R_{n,k}=\left(q_n-\frac{\varepsilon}{2^{n+|k|+2}},\;q_n+\frac{\varepsilon}{2^{
n+|k|+2}}\right)\times(k-1,k+2).$$
Its area is $\frac{2\varepsilon}{2^{n+|k|+2}}\cdot 3=\frac{6\varepsilon}{2^{n+|k
|+2}}$. The total area of the cover $\{R_{n,k}\}_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}$ is
$$\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\frac{6\varepsilon}{2^{n+|k|+2}}=\frac{6\varepsilon}{2^{n
+2}}\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}2^{-|k|}=\frac{6\varepsilon}{2^{n+2}}\cdot 3=\frac{18\v
arepsilon}{2^{n+2}}<\varepsilon,$$
so $m^*(L_n)=0$.

By countable subadditivity of outer measure,
$$m^*(\mathbb{Q}\times\mathbb{R})\le\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}m^*(L_n)=0.$$

**Step 2: $\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{Q}$ has outer measure $0$.**

By the identical argument with the roles of the coordinates swapped (each horizo
ntal line $\mathbb{R}\times\{q_n\}$ has outer measure $0$, and there are countab
ly many), we get
$$m^*(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{Q})=0.$$

**Step 3: $E$ has outer measure $0$.**

By subadditivity,
$$m^*(E)=m^*\bigl((\mathbb{Q}\times\mathbb{R})\cup(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{Q})\b
igr)\le m^*(\mathbb{Q}\times\mathbb{R})+m^*(\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{Q})=0+0=0.$$

Since outer measure is nonnegative, $m^*(E)=0$.

$$\boxed{m^*(E)=0.}$$
