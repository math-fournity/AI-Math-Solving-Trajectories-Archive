|A_2|=|B_1|=|B_2|=n$.

c_q(r_q - 1) - c_{\sigma(q)} + S = 0. \quad (\star)$$

$$c_a(r_a-1) - c_b + S = 0, \qquad c_b(r_b-1) - c_a + S = 0.$$

$$S = \sum_{\text{couples}} (c_a + c_b) = -S \sum_{\text{couples}} \frac{r_a + r
