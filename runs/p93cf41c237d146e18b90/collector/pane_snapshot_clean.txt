$N$ is odd (even + odd), and $N \equiv 4 \pmod{5}$, so $\gcd(N, 20) = 1$.

## Step 4: Representability Criterion

For a divisor $d$ of $N$ (with $\gcd(d,20)=1$), $d$ is represented by $2x^2+2xy+
3y^2$ if and only if the number of prime factors of $d$ (with multiplicity) that

pal class, so proper vs. improper representation doesn't affect the class.)

## Step 5: Residue mod 20 Determines Parity

Write $d = \prod p_i^{e_i}$ where primes are $\equiv 1, 3, 7, 9 \pmod{20}$. Let

$$d \equiv 3^b \cdot 7^c \cdot 9^e \pmod{20}$$
