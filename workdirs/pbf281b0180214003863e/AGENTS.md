# Problem

Turbo the snail plays a game on a board with $2n$ rows and $2n$ columns. There are $2n^2$ monsters who first choose to occupy $2n^2$ distinct cells, with Turbo's knowledge. After this, Turbo chooses any cell and labels it $1$. Starting from this cell, Turbo then walks through all other $4n^2-1$ cells exactly once, labelling them in order with $2,3, \dots, 4n^2$. Turbo only moves between cells which share an edge, and never returns to a cell. The final score is the sum of the labels of the cells with monsters. The monsters are trying to place themselves to maximize the score, while Turbo is trying to minimize the score based on the monsters' positions. Find, in terms of $n$, the largest score which the monsters can guarantee.
