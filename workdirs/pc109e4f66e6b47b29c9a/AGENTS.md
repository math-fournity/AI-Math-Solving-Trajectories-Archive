# Problem

In a specialized cyber-security facility, an encryption key $n$ is generated by calculating the product of two prime security levels that are consecutive in the official sequence of primes. 

To determine the strength of this key, the system must calculate two specific metrics:
1.  The "Expansion Factor" $s(n)$, which is defined as the sum of all positive integers that divide $n$ without a remainder.
2.  The "Isolation Count" $p(n)$, which is the total count of all positive integers from $1$ up to $n$ that share no common factors with $n$ other than 1.

The facility’s master server requires a single "Complexity Value" for the key, which is obtained by multiplying the Expansion Factor by the Isolation Count: $s(n) \cdot p(n)$.

Express this Complexity Value as a polynomial in terms of the encryption key $n$.
