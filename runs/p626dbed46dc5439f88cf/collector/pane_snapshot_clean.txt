## Solution

**Answer leak check:** The problem statement contains no leaked answers, solutio
ns, or proof sketches. Proceeding.

We find all differentiable functions \(f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}\) satisfying
\[
f(xy)=f'(x)f(y)\qquad\text{for all }x,y\in\mathbb{R}. \tag{$\ast$}
\]

(The appearance of \(f'(x)\) for every \(x\) forces \(f\) to be differentiable o
n all of \(\mathbb{R}\).)

---

**Step 1: Evaluate at \(x=0\).**

Setting \(x=0\) in \((\ast)\):
\[
f(0)=f'(0)\,f(y)\qquad\text{for all }y\in\mathbb{R}. \tag{1}
\]

If \(f'(0)\neq 0\), then (1) gives \(f(y)=f(0)/f'(0)\), a constant function. But
 then \(f'\equiv 0\), in particular \(f'(0)=0\), contradicting \(f'(0)\neq 0\).
Hence
\[
f'(0)=0.
\]
Substituting back into (1) yields
\[
f(0)=0. \tag{2}
\]

---

**Step 2: Evaluate at \(y=1\).**

Setting \(y=1\) in \((\ast)\):
\[
f(x)=f'(x)\,f(1)\qquad\text{for all }x\in\mathbb{R}. \tag{3}
\]

Suppose, for contradiction, that \(f(1)\neq 0\). Then (3) gives
\[
f'(x)=\frac{f(x)}{f(1)}\qquad\text{for all }x.
\]
This is the linear ODE \(f'=\lambda f\) with \(\lambda=1/f(1)\), whose unique so
lution with initial value \(f(0)=0\) (from (2)) is
\[
f(x)=f(0)\,e^{\lambda x}=0\qquad\text{for all }x.
\]
But then \(f(1)=0\), contradicting \(f(1)\neq 0\).

Therefore \(f(1)=0\). Substituting into (3):
\[
f(x)=f'(x)\cdot 0 = 0\qquad\text{for all }x\in\mathbb{R}.
\]

---

**Step 3: Verification.**

The function \(f\equiv 0\) is differentiable with \(f'\equiv 0\), and
\[
f(xy)=0=0\cdot 0=f'(x)f(y),
\]
so it satisfies \((\ast)\).

---

**Conclusion.** The unique solution is
\[
\boxed{f(x)\equiv 0.}
\]
