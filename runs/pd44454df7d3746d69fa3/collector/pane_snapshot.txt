# Solution

**Answer:** It depends on the ultrafilter. An ultrapower $M = V^I / U$ of a mode
l $V \models \mathrm{ZFC}$ contains non-standard natural numbers **if and only i
f** the ultrafilter $U$ is **countably incomplete**. If $U$ is countably complet
e (e.g., principal, or a $\kappa$-complete measure on a measurable cardinal), th
en $\omega^M = \omega^V$ and there are no non-standard naturals.

---

## Proof

**Setup.** Let $V \models \mathrm{ZFC}$, let $I \in V$ be an index set, and let
$U$ be an ultrafilter on $I$. The ultrapower is $M = V^I / U$, whose elements ar
e equivalence classes $[f]$ of functions $f \in V$ with $\operatorname{dom}(f) =
 I$, under the relation $f \sim g \iff \{i \in I : f(i) = g(i)\} \in U$.

The **standard natural numbers** of $M$ are the classes $[c_n]$, where $c_n : I
\to \omega^V$ is the constant function with value $n \in \omega^V$. A **non-stan
dard natural number** is some $[f] \in M$ such that $M \models [f] \in \omega$ b
ut $[f] \neq [c_n]$ for every $n \in \omega^V$.

By Łoś's theorem:
$$M \models [f] \in \omega \iff \{i \in I : V \models f(i) \in \omega\} \in U.$$

**Key definition.** $U$ is **countably complete** if for every countable family
$\{C_n : n \in \omega\} \subseteq U$, we have $\bigcap_n C_n \in U$. $U$ is **co
untably incomplete** otherwise.

**Equivalent characterization.** $U$ is countably complete iff for every countab
le partition $\{D_n : n \in \omega\}$ of $I$ (i.e., $I = \bigsqcup_n D_n$), some
 $D_n \in U$.

*Proof of equivalence.* If $\{D_n\}$ is a countable partition with no $D_n \in U
$, then each $I \setminus D_n \in U$ (since $U$ is an ultrafilter). By countable
 completeness, $\bigcap_n (I \setminus D_n) \in U$. But $\bigcap_n (I \setminus
D_n) = I \setminus \bigcup_n D_n = \emptyset \notin U$, contradiction. The conve
rse is similar. $\square$

---

### ($\Rightarrow$) If $U$ is countably complete, then $M$ has no non-standard n
atural numbers.

Let $[f] \in M$ with $M \models [f] \in \omega$. Set $A = \{i \in I : f(i) \in \
omega^V\} \in U$. The family $\{B_n : n \in \omega^V\}$ where $B_n = \{i \in A :
 f(i) = n\}$, together with $B_* = I \setminus A$, forms a countable partition o
f $I$. By countable completeness of $U$, some piece is in $U$. Since $B_* \notin
 U$ (as $A \in U$ and $U$ is a proper filter), some $B_n \in U$. Then:
$$\{i \in I : f(i) = n\} = B_n \in U,$$
so $[f] = [c_n]$. Thus every natural number of $M$ is standard: $\omega^M = \ome
ga^V$.

---

### ($\Leftarrow$) If $U$ is countably incomplete, then $M$ has non-standard nat
ural numbers.

Since $U$ is countably incomplete, there exists a countable partition $\{D_n : n
 \in \omega\}$ of $I$ with no $D_n \in U$ (by the equivalent characterization ab
ove). Assume this partition is in $V$ (if $U$ is internal to $V$, the partition
witnessing incompleteness can be taken in $V$; if $U$ is external, we work in th
e meta-theory).

Define $f : I \to \omega^V$ by $f(i) = n \iff i \in D_n$. Then $f \in V$ and:

1. **$[f]$ is a natural number of $M$:** $\{i : f(i) \in \omega^V\} = I \in U$,
so by Łoś's theorem, $M \models [f] \in \omega$.

2. **$[f]$ is non-standard:** For each $k \in \omega^V$,
$$\{i \in I : f(i) = k\} = D_k \notin U,$$
so $[f] \neq [c_k]$ for every standard $k$.

Therefore $[f]$ is a non-standard natural number of $M$. $\square$

---

### Summary of cases

| Ultrafilter $U$ | Example | Non-standard $\omega$ in $M$? |
|---|---|---|
| Principal | $U = \{A \subseteq I : i_0 \in A\}$ | No ($M \cong V$) |
| Non-principal, countably complete | $\kappa$-complete measure on a measurable
cardinal $\kappa$ | No ($\omega^M = \omega^V$) |
| Countably incomplete | Any non-principal ultrafilter on $\omega$ | **Yes** |

**Remark.** A countably complete non-principal ultrafilter exists if and only if
 there is a measurable cardinal. Thus, if $V$ has no measurable cardinals, every
 non-principal ultrafilter in $V$ is countably incomplete, and every non-trivial
 ultrapower of $V$ (formed within $V$) contains non-standard natural numbers. Co
nversely, if $V$ has a measurable cardinal $\kappa$ and $U$ is a $\kappa$-comple
te ultrafilter on $\kappa$, the ultrapower $V^\kappa / U$ preserves $\omega$ and
 has no non-standard naturals — though it may move larger cardinals.
