## Proof

**Claim.** The vector bundles $T/E \to B$ and $E^{\perp} \to B$ are isomorphic.

**Construction.** Define a map
$$\varphi: E^{\perp} \longrightarrow T/E$$
as follows. For each $b \in B$ and each $v \in E^{\perp}_b \subseteq T_b$, set
$$\varphi(v) = [v] = v + E_b \in T_b / E_b.$$
This is simply the restriction to $E^{\perp}$ of the quotient projection $\pi: T
 \to T/E$.

**Fiberwise linear isomorphism.** Fix $b \in B$. Since $T$ carries an inner prod
uct, each fiber $T_b$ is an inner product space, and $E_b \subseteq T_b$ is a su
bspace. The orthogonal decomposition gives
$$T_b = E_b \oplus E^{\perp}_b.$$
The quotient map $\pi_b: T_b \to T_b/E_b$ has kernel exactly $E_b$. Restricted t
o the complementary subspace $E^{\perp}_b$, the map
$$\varphi_b = \pi_b\big|_{E^{\perp}_b}: E^{\perp}_b \to T_b/E_b$$
is therefore injective (its kernel is $E^{\perp}_b \cap E_b = \{0\}$) and surjec
tive: for any $[w] \in T_b/E_b$ with $w \in T_b$, write $w = w_E + w_{\perp}$ wi
th $w_E \in E_b$ and $w_{\perp} \in E^{\perp}_b$; then $[w] = [w_{\perp}] = \var
phi_b(w_{\perp})$. Thus $\varphi_b$ is a linear isomorphism on every fiber.

**Smoothness / continuity of $\varphi$ and $\varphi^{-1}$.** It remains to verif
y that $\varphi$ is a morphism of vector bundles (smooth or continuous, dependin
g on the category) and that its inverse is likewise, so that $\varphi$ is an iso
morphism of vector bundles, not merely a fiberwise linear bijection.

The quotient projection $\pi: T \to T/E$ is a vector bundle morphism by construc
tion of the quotient bundle. The inclusion $\iota: E^{\perp} \hookrightarrow T$
is a vector bundle morphism (the orthogonal complement of a subbundle is a subbu
ndle, hence $\iota$ is smooth/continuous and fiberwise linear). Therefore $\varp
hi = \pi \circ \iota$ is a vector bundle morphism.

For the inverse: locally over a trivializing open set $U \subseteq B$, choose a
local frame $\{e_1, \dots, e_k\}$ for $E|_U$. Applying the Gram–Schmidt process
to extend this to a local frame $\{e_1, \dots, e_k, f_1, \dots, f_m\}$ for $T|_U
$ that is orthonormal, the $\{f_1, \dots, f_m\}$ form a local frame for $E^{\per
p}|_U$, and $\{[f_1], \dots, [f_m]\}$ form a local frame for $(T/E)|_U$. In thes
e local frames, $\varphi|_U$ is represented by the identity matrix, so $\varphi^
{-1}|_U$ is given by the same identity matrix and is therefore smooth/continuous
. Since smoothness/continuity is local, $\varphi^{-1}$ is a vector bundle morphi
sm globally.

**Conclusion.** $\varphi: E^{\perp} \to T/E$ is a vector bundle morphism that is
 a linear isomorphism on every fiber, with inverse also a vector bundle morphism
. Hence $E^{\perp} \cong T/E$ as vector bundles over $B$. $\square$
