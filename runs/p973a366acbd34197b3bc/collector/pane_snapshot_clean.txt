Looking at this problem, I need to find a number $N$ (different from 2009) such
that $\text{rev}(N)/N = 9002/2009$.

## Setting Up the Ratio

First, note that $9002 = \text{rev}(2009)$, so $9002/2009 = \text{rev}(2009)/200
9$. I simplify this fraction:

$$9002 = 2 \times 7 \times 643, \quad 2009 = 7^2 \times 41$$

$$\gcd(9002, 2009) = 7, \quad \frac{9002}{2009} = \frac{1286}{287}$$

I verify $\gcd(1286, 287) = 1$ (via Euclidean algorithm: $1286 = 4 \times 287 +
138$, $287 = 2 \times 138 + 11$, $138 = 12 \times 11 + 6$, $11 = 1 \times 6 + 5$
, $6 = 1 \times 5 + 1$), so $1286/287$ is in lowest terms.

## Key Reduction

Since $\text{rev}(N)/N = 1286/287$ with $\gcd(1286,287)=1$, there must exist a p
ositive integer $k$ such that:
$$N = 287k \quad \text{and} \quad \text{rev}(N) = 1286k$$

## Searching by Digit Length

**4-digit:** $1000 \le 287k \le 9999$ gives $k \in [4, 34]$; $1000 \le 1286k \le
 9999$ gives $k \in [1, 7]$. Combined: $k \in \{4,5,6,7\}$. Only $k=7$ works: $N
 = 2009$, $\text{rev} = 9002 = 1286 \times 7$. This is the original number.

**5-digit:** $10000 \le 287k$ gives $k \ge 35$; $1286k \le 99999$ gives $k \le 7
7$. So $k \in [35, 77]$.

**Digit constraint:** The last digit of $1286k$ equals $(6k) \bmod 10$, which mu
st equal the first digit of $287k$. For $k \in [35,77]$, $287k$ ranges from $100
45$ to $22099$, so its first digit is $1$ or $2$.

- $(6k) \bmod 10 = 1$ is impossible (since $6k$ is always even).
- $(6k) \bmod 10 = 2$ requires $k \equiv 2 \pmod{5}$, giving $k \in \{37, 42, 47
, 52, 57, 62, 67, 72, 77\}$.

Among these, only $k = 72$ and $k = 77$ yield $287k$ starting with digit $2$:

- $k = 72$: $N = 20664$, $\text{rev} = 46602$, but $1286 \times 72 = 92592 \ne 4
6602$. ✗
- $k = 77$: $N = 22099$, $\text{rev} = 99022$, and $1286 \times 77 = 99022$. ✓

## Verification

$$\text{rev}(22099) = 99022$$

$$\frac{99022}{22099} = \frac{1286 \times 77}{287 \times 77} = \frac{1286}{287}
= \frac{9002}{2009} \checkmark$$

Cross-multiplying to confirm: $99022 \times 287 = 28{,}419{,}314$ and $22099 \ti
mes 1286 = 28{,}419{,}314$. ✓
