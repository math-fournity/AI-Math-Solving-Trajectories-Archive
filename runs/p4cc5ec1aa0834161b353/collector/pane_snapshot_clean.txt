## Setup

Consider a rod of length $l$, cross-sectional area $S$, density $\rho$, Young's
modulus $E$, occupying $x \in [0,l]$. A mass $M$ is attached at $x=0$. The wave
speed is $c = \sqrt{E/\rho}$. The longitudinal displacement $u(x,t)$ satisfies:

$$u_{tt} = c^2\, u_{xx}, \quad 0 < x < l.$$

**Boundary condition at $x=0$ (mass $M$):** The rod exerts force $ES\,u_x(0,t)$
on the mass (in $+x$ when stretched), so Newton's law gives:

$$M\,\ddot{u}(0,t) = ES\,u_x(0,t).$$

**Boundary condition at $x=l$:** (a) $u(l,t)=0$; (b) $u_x(l,t)=0$; (c) $ES\,u_x(
l,t)+\kappa\,u(l,t)=0$ (elastic support with spring constant $\kappa$).

Separation of variables $u = X(x)T(t)$: $T''+\omega^2 T=0$, $X''+k^2 X=0$ with $
k=\omega/c$. So $X(x)=A\cos kx + B\sin kx$.

The BC at $x=0$ gives $-M\omega^2 X(0)=ES\,X'(0)$. Since $X(0)=A$, $X'(0)=Bk$:

$$B = -\frac{M\omega^2}{ESk}\,A = -\frac{M\omega c}{ES}\,A.$$

Introduce the dimensionless parameters:
$$\xi = kl = \frac{\omega l}{c}, \qquad \mu = \frac{M}{\rho S l}\quad(\text{mass
 ratio}), \qquad \lambda = \frac{\kappa\, l}{ES}\quad(\text{stiffness ratio}).$$

Since $\frac{M\omega c}{ES} = \frac{Mk}{\rho S} = \mu\,\xi$, the eigenfunction t
akes the form:

$$\boxed{X(x) = A\bigl[\cos kx - \mu\xi\,\sin kx\bigr].}$$

The time dependence is $T(t) = C\cos\omega t + D\sin\omega t$, and the general v
ibration is:

$$u(x,t) = \sum_{n} X_n(x)\bigl(C_n\cos\omega_n t + D_n\sin\omega_n t\bigr).$$

## Orthogonality Condition

For two eigenfunctions $X_m, X_n$ with distinct eigenvalues $k_m \neq k_n$, the
Lagrange identity gives:

$$(k_n^2 - k_m^2)\int_0^l X_m X_n\,dx = \Bigl[X_m' X_n - X_n' X_m\Bigr]_0^l.$$

**At $x=l$:** In all three cases the boundary term vanishes:
- (a) $X(l)=0$ for all modes;
- (b) $X'(l)=0$ for all modes;
- (c) $X'(l) = -\frac{\kappa}{ES}X(l)$, so $X_m'(l)X_n(l)-X_n'(l)X_m(l) = -\frac
{\kappa}{ES}[X_m(l)X_n(l)-X_n(l)X_m(l)]=0$.

**At $x=0$:** Using $X'(0) = -\frac{Mk^2}{\rho S}X(0)$:

$$X_m'(0)X_n(0) - X_n'(0)X_m(0) = -\frac{M}{\rho S}(k_m^2 - k_n^2)\,X_m(0)\,X_n(
0).$$

Substituting:

$$(k_n^2 - k_m^2)\int_0^l X_m X_n\,dx = \frac{M}{\rho S}(k_m^2 - k_n^2)\,X_m(0)\
,X_n(0) = -\frac{M}{\rho S}(k_n^2-k_m^2)\,X_m(0)\,X_n(0).$$

For $m\neq n$:

$$\boxed{\rho S\int_0^l X_m(x)\,X_n(x)\,dx + M\,X_m(0)\,X_n(0) = 0.}$$

This is the orthogonality with respect to the total mass distribution (rod mass
$\rho S\,dx$ plus point mass $M$ at $x=0$), which is physically the correct kine
tic-energy inner product.
