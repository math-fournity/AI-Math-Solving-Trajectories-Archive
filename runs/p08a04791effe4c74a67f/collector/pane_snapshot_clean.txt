## Solution

Let the meeting time be $t$. The current velocity is $(0,2)$, so each bird's gro
und velocity equals its water-relative velocity plus $(0,2)$.

**Mallard** (from $(0,0)$ to $(x,22)$): ground velocity $\left(\dfrac{x}{t},\dfr
ac{22}{t}\right)$, so water-relative velocity $\left(\dfrac{x}{t},\dfrac{22}{t}-
2\right)$. With relative speed $4$:

$$\left(\frac{x}{t}\right)^2+\left(\frac{22}{t}-2\right)^2=16. \tag{1}$$

**Wigeon** (from $(25,0)$ to $(x,22)$): ground velocity $\left(\dfrac{x-25}{t},\
dfrac{22}{t}\right)$, water-relative velocity $\left(\dfrac{x-25}{t},\dfrac{22}{
t}-2\right)$. With relative speed $3$:

$$\left(\frac{x-25}{t}\right)^2+\left(\frac{22}{t}-2\right)^2=9. \tag{2}$$

Set $u=\dfrac{22}{t}-2$, so $t=\dfrac{22}{u+2}$ (requiring $u>-2$). Then:

$$\frac{x^2}{t^2}=16-u^2,\qquad \frac{(x-25)^2}{t^2}=9-u^2.$$

Since $0\le x\le 25$, we take
$$x=\frac{22\sqrt{16-u^2}}{u+2},\qquad x=25-\frac{22\sqrt{9-u^2}}{u+2}.$$

Equating:
$$22\bigl(\sqrt{16-u^2}+\sqrt{9-u^2}\bigr)=25(u+2). \tag{3}$$

**Solving (3).** Try $u=\dfrac{12}{5}$:

$$\sqrt{16-\tfrac{144}{25}}=\sqrt{\tfrac{256}{25}}=\tfrac{16}{5},\qquad \sqrt{9-
\tfrac{144}{25}}=\sqrt{\tfrac{81}{25}}=\tfrac{9}{5}.$$

$$\text{LHS}=22\left(\tfrac{16}{5}+\tfrac{9}{5}\right)=22\cdot 5=110,\qquad \tex
t{RHS}=25\left(\tfrac{12}{5}+2\right)=25\cdot\tfrac{22}{5}=110.\ \checkmark$$

So $u=\dfrac{12}{5}$, giving $t=\dfrac{22}{12/5+2}=\dfrac{22}{22/5}=5$.

**Finding $x$:**
$$x=\frac{22\sqrt{16-u^2}}{u+2}=\frac{22\cdot 16/5}{22/5}=\frac{22\cdot 16/5\cdo
t 5}{22}=16.$$
