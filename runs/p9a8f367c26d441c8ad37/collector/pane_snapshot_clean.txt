**Proof of "only if".** Suppose the coprime residues form an AP $1 = a_1 < a_2 <
 \dots < a_k = n-1$ with common difference $d$. (Note $n-1$ is always coprime to
 $n$, so it is the last term, giving $(k-1)d = n-2$.)

 2$, forcing $d = 1$. The AP is then $1, 2, 3, \dots, n-1$, so every integer fro
m $1$ to $n-1$ is coprime to $n$, meaning $n$ is prime.

**Case 2: $n$ is even.** All coprime residues are odd, so $d$ is even.
