$$\frac{1}{j}\sum_{i\,:\, j \in A_i} c_i = 0 \implies \sum_{i\,:\, j \in A_i} c_
i = 0,$$
since $1/j \neq 0$. This is exactly the condition $\sum_{i=1}^k c_i\,\chi_{A_i}(
j) = 0$ for all $j$, i.e., $\sum c_i\,\chi_{A_i} = 0$ pointwise. Therefore:

> $x_{A_1}, \dots, x_{A_k}$ are linearly independent **iff** $\chi_{A_1}, \dots,
 \chi_{A_k}$ are linearly independent.

So the problem reduces to finding the dimension of $\operatorname{span}\{\chi_A

$$\sum_{k \geq 1} |D|^k \cdot \mathfrak{c}^k = \sum_{k \geq 1} (2^{\aleph_0})^k
