## Solution

**Key identity (generalized Legendre).** For any base $b$ and integer $m\ge 0$,
$$m - s_b(m) = (b-1)\sum_{k\ge 1}\Big\lfloor \frac{m}{b^k}\Big\rfloor,$$
since writing $m=\sum_j d_j b^j$ gives $m-s_b(m)=\sum_j d_j(b^j-1)=(b-1)\sum_{k\
ge1}\lfloor m/b^k\rfloor$. Hence
$$s_b(m)=m-(b-1)\sum_{k\ge1}\Big\lfloor\frac{m}{b^k}\Big\rfloor.$$

**Floor-nesting identity.** $\big\lfloor\lfloor n/a\rfloor/b\big\rfloor=\big\lfl
oor n/(ab)\big\rfloor$.

---

Apply $s_{20}(m)=m-19\sum_{k\ge1}\lfloor m/20^k\rfloor$ with $m=\lfloor n/23^i\r
floor$:

$$
\sum_{i\ge1}s_{20}\!\Big(\Big\lfloor\frac{n}{23^i}\Big\rfloor\Big)
=\sum_{i\ge1}\Big\lfloor\frac{n}{23^i}\Big\rfloor
-19\sum_{i\ge1}\sum_{k\ge1}\Big\lfloor\frac{n}{23^i\,20^k}\Big\rfloor.
$$

Let $P=\sum_{i\ge1}\lfloor n/23^i\rfloor$, $\;Q=\sum_{i\ge1}\lfloor n/20^i\rfloo
r$, and $\;D=\sum_{i\ge1}\sum_{k\ge1}\lfloor n/(23^i 20^k)\rfloor$. The two give
n equations become

$$P-19D=103,\qquad Q-22D=115,$$

where the **same** $D$ appears in both (the products $23^i20^k$ and $20^k23^i$ a
re identical).

From generalized Legendre, $n-s_{23}(n)=22P$ and $n-s_{20}(n)=19Q$, so

$$s_{20}(n)-s_{23}(n)=\big(n-19Q\big)-\big(n-22P\big)=22P-19Q.$$

Substituting $P=103+19D$ and $Q=115+22D$:

$$22P-19Q=22(103+19D)-19(115+22D)=22\cdot103-19\cdot115+ (22\cdot19-19\cdot22)D.
$$

The $D$ terms cancel exactly, leaving

$$s_{20}(n)-s_{23}(n)=22\cdot103-19\cdot115=2266-2185=81.$$

The double-counting term $D$ — which depends on the unknown $n$ — cancels out, s
o the answer is determined purely by the two given measurements.

$$\boxed{s_{20}(n)-s_{23}(n)=81}$$
