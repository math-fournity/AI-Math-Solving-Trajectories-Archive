# Problem

In a specialized chemistry competition, several research teams are testing the stability of a new compound. In this tournament, every team must compete against every other team exactly once. For each head-to-head trial, a team earns 1.0 mg of a catalyst for a decisive success, 0.5 mg for a neutral result, and 0.0 mg for a failure.

Team Alpha and Team Beta are two of the participants. In their specific head-to-head trial, Team Alpha achieved a decisive success over Team Beta. By the end of the tournament, Team Beta had accumulated exactly 8 decisive successes and no neutral results. Team Alpha finished the tournament with a total of exactly 8.0 mg of catalyst (which includes the 1.0 mg earned from their victory over Team Beta).

Remarkably, every other team participating in the tournament finished with a final catalyst total of exactly $n$ mg. 

Find the sum of all possible values of $n$.
