## Solution

We compute $\operatorname{Tor}_2^{R}\!\bigl(R/(y,z),\; R/(x)\oplus R/(y,z)\bigr)
$ for $R = k[[x,y,z]]$, a regular local ring of dimension $3$.

**Additivity.** Tor is additive in each argument, so
$$\operatorname{Tor}_2^{R}\!\bigl(R/(y,z),\; R/(x)\oplus R/(y,z)\bigr) \;\cong\;
 \operatorname{Tor}_2^{R}\!\bigl(R/(y,z),\, R/(x)\bigr) \;\oplus\; \operatorname
{Tor}_2^{R}\!\bigl(R/(y,z),\, R/(y,z)\bigr).$$

---

**First summand: $\operatorname{Tor}_*^{R}(R/(y,z),\, R/(x))$.**

Since $x$ is a nonzerodivisor in $R$, the module $R/(x)$ has the length‑$1$ free
 resolution
$$0 \longrightarrow R \xrightarrow{\;\cdot x\;} R \longrightarrow R/(x) \longrig
htarrow 0.$$

Tensoring with $R/(y,z)$ gives the complex
$$0 \longrightarrow R/(y,z) \xrightarrow{\;\cdot x\;} R/(y,z) \longrightarrow 0.
$$

Now $R/(y,z)\cong k[[x]]$, and multiplication by $x$ on $k[[x]]$ is injective (i
t is a domain). Hence the displayed map is injective, so
$$\operatorname{Tor}_1^{R}(R/(y,z),\, R/(x)) = 0, \qquad \operatorname{Tor}_n^{R
}(R/(y,z),\, R/(x)) = 0 \text{ for all } n\ge 1.$$

In particular $\operatorname{Tor}_2^{R}(R/(y,z),\, R/(x)) = 0$.

---

**Second summand: $\operatorname{Tor}_*^{R}(R/(y,z),\, R/(y,z))$.**

The sequence $y, z$ is a regular sequence in $R$, so $R/(y,z)$ is resolved by th
e Koszul complex:
$$0 \longrightarrow R \xrightarrow{\;\begin{pmatrix} z \\ -y \end{pmatrix}\;} R^
{2} \xrightarrow{\;\begin{pmatrix} y & z \end{pmatrix}\;} R \longrightarrow R/(y
,z) \longrightarrow 0.$$

Tensoring with $R/(y,z)$ yields the complex
$$0 \longrightarrow R/(y,z) \xrightarrow{\;\begin{pmatrix} \bar z \\ -\bar y \en
d{pmatrix}\;} (R/(y,z))^{2} \xrightarrow{\;\begin{pmatrix} \bar y & \bar z \end{
pmatrix}\;} R/(y,z) \longrightarrow 0.$$

But in $R/(y,z)$ we have $\bar y = 0$ and $\bar z = 0$, so **both differentials
are the zero map**. Therefore the homology is the entire module in each degree:
$$\operatorname{Tor}_0^{R}(R/(y,z),\, R/(y,z)) \cong R/(y,z), \quad \operatornam
e{Tor}_1^{R}(R/(y,z),\, R/(y,z)) \cong (R/(y,z))^{2}, \quad \operatorname{Tor}_2
^{R}(R/(y,z),\, R/(y,z)) \cong R/(y,z),$$
and $\operatorname{Tor}_n = 0$ for $n\ge 3$.

---

**Combining.**
$$\operatorname{Tor}_2^{R}\!\bigl(R/(y,z),\; R/(x)\oplus R/(y,z)\bigr) \;\cong\;
 0 \;\oplus\; R/(y,z) \;\cong\; R/(y,z).$$

Since $R/(y,z)\cong k[[x]]$, we conclude

$$\boxed{\operatorname{Tor}_2^{R}\!\bigl(R/(y,z),\; R/(x)\oplus R/(y,z)\bigr) \;
\cong\; R/(y,z) \;\cong\; k[[x]].}$$
