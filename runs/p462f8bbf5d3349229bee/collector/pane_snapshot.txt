## Solution

We need the last 30 digits of $N-1$ to all be $9$, where $N = n!(n+1)!(2n+1)!$.
This means
$$N - 1 \equiv \underbrace{99\ldots9}_{30} \equiv -1 \pmod{10^{30}},$$
so $N \equiv 0 \pmod{10^{30}}$, i.e. $10^{30} = 2^{30}\cdot 5^{30}$ divides $n!(
n+1)!(2n+1)!$.

Let $v_p(m!)$ denote the exponent of prime $p$ in $m!$. We need:
- $v_2(n!)+v_2((n+1)!)+v_2((2n+1)!) \ge 30$
- $v_5(n!)+v_5((n+1)!)+v_5((2n+1)!) \ge 30$

Since factors of $5$ are far rarer than factors of $2$, the $5$-adic condition i
s binding. Define $S_5(n) = v_5(n!)+v_5((n+1)!)+v_5((2n+1)!)$.

Using Legendre's formula $v_5(m!) = \lfloor m/5\rfloor + \lfloor m/25\rfloor + \
lfloor m/125\rfloor+\cdots$, compute near $n\approx 30$ (since $S_5(n)\approx n$
):

| $n$ | $v_5(n!)$ | $v_5((n+1)!)$ | $2n+1$ | $v_5((2n+1)!)$ | $S_5$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 29 | 5 | 7 | 59 | 11+2=13 | 25 |
| 30 | 7 | 7 | 61 | 12+2=14 | 28 |
| 31 | 7 | 7 | 63 | 12+2=14 | 28 |
| 32 | 7 | 7 | 65 | 13+2=15 | 29 |
| 33 | 7 | 7 | 67 | 13+2=15 | 29 |
| **34** | **7** | **8** | **69** | **13+2=15** | **30** |

For $n=34$: $v_5(34!)=\lfloor34/5\rfloor+\lfloor34/25\rfloor=6+1=7$; $v_5(35!)=7
+1=8$ (since $35=5\cdot7$); $v_5(69!)=\lfloor69/5\rfloor+\lfloor69/25\rfloor=13+
2=15$. So $S_5(34)=7+8+15=30\ge 30$. ✓

For all $n\le 33$, $S_5(n)\le 29 < 30$ (verified above, and $S_5$ is non-decreas
ing in this range since each term only gains factors of $5$ as $n$ grows).

Check the $2$-adic condition at $n=34$:
$$v_2(34!)=17+8+4+2+1=32,\quad v_2(35!)=32,\quad v_2(69!)=34+17+8+4+2+1=66,$$
so $S_2(34)=32+32+66=130\ge 30$. ✓

Therefore $n!(n+1)!(2n+1)!$ is divisible by $10^{30}$ exactly when $n\ge 34$ (am
ong small $n$), and the smallest such $n$ is $\boxed{34}$.

Final Answer: $\boxed{34}$
