**Key observation.** Since $f(n) = 1! + 2! + \dots + n!$, consecutive values sat
isfy
$$f(n+1) = f(n) + (n+1)!,$$
so $(n+1)! = f(n+1) - f(n)$.

**Expressing $f(n+2)$ in terms of $f(n+1)$ and $f(n)$.** We have
$$f(n+2) = f(n+1) + (n+2)!.$$
Since $(n+2)! = (n+2)\cdot(n+1)! = (n+2)\bigl(f(n+1)-f(n)\bigr)$, substituting g
ives
$$
f(n+2) = f(n+1) + (n+2)\bigl(f(n+1)-f(n)\bigr)
       = \bigl(1+(n+2)\bigr)f(n+1) - (n+2)f(n)
       = (n+3)\,f(n+1) - (n+2)\,f(n).
$$

**Verification.**
- $n=1$: $f(1)=1,\ f(2)=3,\ f(3)=9$. Check: $4\cdot 3 - 3\cdot 1 = 12-3=9.$ ✓
- $n=2$: $f(2)=3,\ f(3)=9,\ f(4)=33$. Check: $5\cdot 9 - 4\cdot 3 = 45-12=33.$ ✓
- $n=3$: $f(3)=9,\ f(4)=33,\ f(5)=153$. Check: $6\cdot 33 - 5\cdot 9 = 198-45=15
3.$ ✓

**Conclusion.** The required polynomials are
$$\boxed{P(x) = x+3, \qquad Q(x) = -(x+2).}$$

Indeed, for every $n\ge 1$,
$$f(n+2) = (n+3)\,f(n+1) - (n+2)\,f(n).$$
