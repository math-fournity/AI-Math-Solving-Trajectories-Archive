## Solution

**Claim.** In an Abelian category $\mathscr{A}$, the Hom functor $\mathrm{Hom}_\
mathscr{A}(A,-)$ takes values in the category $\mathbf{Ab}$ of abelian groups.

**Proof.**

Recall that an Abelian category $\mathscr{A}$ is, in particular, an $\mathbf{Ab}
$-enriched category: by the definition of an Abelian category, every Hom-set $\m
athrm{Hom}_\mathscr{A}(X,Y)$ carries the structure of an abelian group, and comp
osition is bilinear with respect to this group structure.

Fix an object $A\in\mathscr{A}$. We must show that the functor
$$\mathrm{Hom}_\mathscr{A}(A,-):\mathscr{A}\longrightarrow \mathbf{Set}$$
(naively landing in $\mathbf{Set}$) in fact lifts to a functor
$$\mathrm{Hom}_\mathscr{A}(A,-):\mathscr{A}\longrightarrow \mathbf{Ab}.$$

**On objects.** For each $Y\in\mathscr{A}$, the set $\mathrm{Hom}_\mathscr{A}(A,
Y)$ is equipped with an abelian group structure (addition of morphisms), which i
s part of the $\mathbf{Ab}$-enrichment of $\mathscr{A}$. The zero element is the
 zero morphism $0:A\to Y$, and inverses exist because $\mathrm{Hom}_\mathscr{A}(
A,Y)$ is an abelian group.

**On morphisms.** For a morphism $f:Y\to Y'$ in $\mathscr{A}$, the induced map
$$f_*:\mathrm{Hom}_\mathscr{A}(A,Y)\longrightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_\mathscr{A}(A,Y'
),\qquad g\mapsto f\circ g,$$
is a group homomorphism. Indeed, by bilinearity of composition in the $\mathbf{A
b}$-enriched category $\mathscr{A}$,
$$f_*(g+h)=f\circ(g+h)=f\circ g+f\circ h=f_*(g)+f_*(h),$$
for all $g,h\in\mathrm{Hom}_\mathscr{A}(A,Y)$. Moreover $f_*(0)=0$.

**Functoriality.** The identities $(f'\circ f)_* = f'_*\circ f_*$ and $(\mathrm{
id}_Y)_*=\mathrm{id}$ follow directly from associativity and unitality of compos
ition, so $\mathrm{Hom}_\mathscr{A}(A,-)$ is a genuine functor into $\mathbf{Ab}
$.

**Additivity (stronger statement).** Since $\mathscr{A}$ is Abelian, $\mathrm{Ho
m}_\mathscr{A}(A,-)$ is in fact an *additive* functor: it preserves finite bipro
ducts. For if $Y_1\oplus Y_2$ is a biproduct with injections $\iota_i$ and proje
ctions $\pi_i$, then the map
$$\mathrm{Hom}_\mathscr{A}(A,Y_1\oplus Y_2)\longrightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_\mathscr
{A}(A,Y_1)\oplus\mathrm{Hom}_\mathscr{A}(A,Y_2),\qquad g\mapsto(\pi_1\circ g,\;\
pi_2\circ g),$$
is a group isomorphism with inverse $(g_1,g_2)\mapsto \iota_1\circ g_1+\iota_2\c
irc g_2$. Hence $\mathrm{Hom}_\mathscr{A}(A,-)$ preserves finite biproducts and
is therefore additive.

**Conclusion.** The Hom functor $\mathrm{Hom}_\mathscr{A}(A,-)$ takes values in
the category $\mathbf{Ab}$ of abelian groups; more precisely, it is an additive
functor $\mathscr{A}\to\mathbf{Ab}$. By the Freyd–Mitchell embedding theorem per
spective, this is the natural enrichment forced by the Abelian structure of $\ma
thscr{A}$: the abelian group structure on each Hom-set and the bilinearity of co
mposition are defining data of an Abelian category.
