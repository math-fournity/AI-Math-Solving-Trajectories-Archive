# Problem

In the city of Metropolia, the Department of Logistics is organizing a massive fleet of 30 different types of transport vehicles, labeled $1, 2, 3, \dots, 30$. Each vehicle type $n$ operates on a strict schedule where it completes a full circuit every $n$ hours. 

The Master Controller, $k$, is defined as the total number of hours in the shortest possible time cycle such that every single vehicle type in the fleet completes an integer number of full circuits. 

A "Sub-Coordinator" is a positive integer duration (in hours) that is a divisor of the Master Controller cycle $k$. The Chief Engineer is specifically looking for Sub-Coordinators that satisfy a "Near-Universal Compatibility" rule. To satisfy this rule, a Sub-Coordinator duration must be perfectly divisible by exactly 28 of the 30 vehicle types' cycle lengths in the set $\{1, 2, 3, \dots, 30\}$.

How many different Sub-Coordinator durations exist that satisfy this rule?
