## Solution

We need to find all real $x$ satisfying
$$\frac{\sqrt{-x^{2}+6 x+1}+5 x-15}{2 x-5} \geq 1.$$

**Domain.** We require $-x^2+6x+1 \geq 0$ and $2x-5\neq 0$. Solving $x^2-6x-1\le
 0$ gives
$$x\in\bigl[3-\sqrt{10},\;3+\sqrt{10}\bigr],$$
and we must exclude $x=\tfrac52$.

**Simplify the inequality.** Let $s=\sqrt{-x^2+6x+1}\ge 0$. Subtracting 1:
$$\frac{s+5x-15}{2x-5}-1 \ge 0 \;\Longleftrightarrow\; \frac{s+5x-15-(2x-5)}{2x-
5}\ge 0 \;\Longleftrightarrow\; \frac{s+3x-10}{2x-5}\ge 0.$$

So we study the sign of $N=s+3x-10$ and $D=2x-5$.

**Zeros of the numerator.** $N=0 \Leftrightarrow s=10-3x$, which requires $x\le
\tfrac{10}{3}$. Squaring:
$$-x^2+6x+1=(10-3x)^2=100-60x+9x^2 \;\Longrightarrow\; 10x^2-66x+99=0.$$
$$x=\frac{66\pm\sqrt{396}}{20}=\frac{66\pm 6\sqrt{11}}{20}=\frac{33\pm 3\sqrt{11
}}{10}.$$
Since $\sqrt{11}\approx 3.317$, we get $x_1=\frac{33-3\sqrt{11}}{10}\approx 2.30
5$ and $x_2=\frac{33+3\sqrt{11}}{10}\approx 4.295$. Only $x_1\le \tfrac{10}{3}$
is valid, so $x_2$ is rejected (extraneous from squaring; indeed for $x>\tfrac{1
0}{3}$ we have $3x-10>0$ and $s\ge0$, giving $N>0$).

**Sign of $N$.** $N$ is continuous on the domain and vanishes only at $x_1$. Che
cking:
- At $x=3-\sqrt{10}$: $s=0$, $N=3(3-\sqrt{10})-10<0$.
- At $x=3$: $s=\sqrt{10}$, $N=\sqrt{10}-1>0$.
- At $x=3+\sqrt{10}$: $s=0$, $N=3(3+\sqrt{10})-10>0$.

Hence $N<0$ on $[3-\sqrt{10},\,x_1)$, $N=0$ at $x_1$, and $N>0$ on $(x_1,\,3+\sq
rt{10}]$.

**Sign of $D=2x-5$.** $D<0$ for $x<\tfrac52$, $D>0$ for $x>\tfrac52$. Note $x_1\
approx 2.305 < \tfrac52=2.5$.

**Combine (need $N/D\ge 0$):**

| Region | $N$ | $D$ | $N/D$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $[3-\sqrt{10},\,x_1]$ | $\le 0$ | $<0$ | $\ge 0$ ✓ |
| $(x_1,\,\tfrac52)$ | $>0$ | $<0$ | $<0$ ✗ |
| $x=\tfrac52$ | — | $0$ | undefined ✗ |
| $(\tfrac52,\,3+\sqrt{10}]$ | $>0$ | $>0$ | $>0$ ✓ |

**Solution set:**
$$I_1\cup I_2=\bigl[3-\sqrt{10},\;\tfrac{33-3\sqrt{11}}{10}\bigr]\;\cup\;\bigl(\
tfrac52,\;3+\sqrt{10}\bigr].$$

So $a=3-\sqrt{10}$, $b=\dfrac{33-3\sqrt{11}}{10}$, $c=\dfrac52$, $d=3+\sqrt{10}$
.

**Compute $a+b+c+d$:**
$$a+b+c+d=(3-\sqrt{10})+\frac{33-3\sqrt{11}}{10}+\frac52+(3+\sqrt{10}).$$
The $\sqrt{10}$ terms cancel:
$$=6+\frac{33-3\sqrt{11}}{10}+\frac{25}{10}=6+\frac{58-3\sqrt{11}}{10}=\frac{60+
58-3\sqrt{11}}{10}.$$

$$\boxed{a+b+c+d=\dfrac{118-3\sqrt{11}}{10}}.$$
