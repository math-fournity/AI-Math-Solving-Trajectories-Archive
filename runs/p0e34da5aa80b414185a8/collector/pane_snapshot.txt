## Proof

Let $m$ be an odd positive integer and define

$$S = \sum_{k=1}^{m} e^{2\pi i k^{2}/m}.$$

**Step 1. Reindexing to a complete residue system.**

Since $m^{2}/m = m$, the term with $k=m$ is $e^{2\pi i m}=1=e^{2\pi i\cdot 0/m}$
, which coincides with the $k=0$ term. Hence

$$S = \sum_{k=1}^{m} e^{2\pi i k^{2}/m} = \sum_{k=0}^{m-1} e^{2\pi i k^{2}/m}.$$

So $S$ is a sum over a complete system of residues modulo $m$.

**Step 2. Compute $|S|^{2}$ by expanding the product.**

$$|S|^{2} = S\,\overline{S} = \sum_{j=0}^{m-1}\sum_{k=0}^{m-1} e^{2\pi i(k^{2}-j
^{2})/m}.$$

Factor the exponent using the difference of squares:

$$k^{2}-j^{2} = (k-j)(k+j).$$

Introduce the change of variables $a = k-j \pmod m$. For each fixed $j$, as $k$
runs over $\{0,1,\dots,m-1\}$, the quantity $a=k-j$ also runs over a complete sy
stem of residues modulo $m$. With $k = j+a$ we get $k+j = 2j+a$, so

$$k^{2}-j^{2} = a(2j+a) = 2aj + a^{2}.$$

Therefore

$$|S|^{2} = \sum_{a=0}^{m-1} e^{2\pi i a^{2}/m}\sum_{j=0}^{m-1} e^{2\pi i\, 2aj/
m}.$$

**Step 3. Evaluate the inner sum.**

The inner sum is a geometric series:

$$I(a) := \sum_{j=0}^{m-1} e^{2\pi i\, 2aj/m} = \sum_{j=0}^{m-1}\bigl(e^{2\pi i\
, 2a/m}\bigr)^{j}.$$

- If $2a \equiv 0 \pmod m$, then every term equals $1$ and $I(a)=m$.
- Otherwise the ratio $r = e^{2\pi i\, 2a/m}\neq 1$, and

$$I(a) = \frac{1-r^{m}}{1-r} = \frac{1-e^{2\pi i\, 2a}}{1-r} = \frac{1-1}{1-r}=0
.$$

Because $m$ is **odd**, $\gcd(2,m)=1$, so $m\mid 2a$ forces $m\mid a$. Within $\
{0,\dots,m-1\}$ the only such value is $a=0$. Hence the inner sum vanishes for e
very $a\neq 0$ and equals $m$ for $a=0$.

**Step 4. Conclude.**

Only the $a=0$ term survives:

$$|S|^{2} = e^{2\pi i\cdot 0/m}\cdot m = 1\cdot m = m.$$

Since $|S|^{2}=m$ and $|S|\ge 0$,

$$\boxed{|S| = \sqrt{m}.}$$

This derivation used only the factorization $k^{2}-j^{2}=(k-j)(k+j)$, a reindexi
ng over a complete residue system, and the orthogonality of additive characters
$\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}e^{2\pi i c j/m}\in\{0,m\}$; the Gauss sum evaluation itself wa
s never invoked.
