## Analysis

**Tree structure:** Each node n > 1 has parent = largest proper divisor of n = n
/p(n), where p(n) is the smallest prime factor of n. Node 1 is the root.

- Children of 1: all primes (since only primes have largest proper divisor = 1).
- Children of m > 1: {m·p : p prime, p ≤ p(m), m·p ≤ 2019}.

**Chain = pre-order traversal with children in decreasing order.** This is becau
se inserting node n behind parent m places it right after m; later insertions be
hind m go in front of earlier ones (LIFO). I verified this with a small simulati
on (nodes 1–12 produce exactly the pre-order with decreasing children).

## Computing F (nodes ahead of Node 2)

top). This subtree has **10 nodes**.

All other 2019 − 10 = **2009** nodes appear before Node 2 in the chain.

**F = 2009.**
