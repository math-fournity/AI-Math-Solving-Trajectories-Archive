## Proof

I model the 19 stations as points of a hexagonal grid in axial coordinates $(q,r
)$, with the center at the origin. Since the map from axial to Cartesian coordin
ates is affine, collinearity is preserved, so I may work directly in $(q,r)$-spa
ce.

**The 19 points.** Ring 0: $(0,0)$. Ring 1: $(1,0),(1,-1),(0,-1),(-1,0),(-1,1),(
0,1)$. Ring 2: $(2,0),(2,-1),(2,-2),(1,-2),(0,-2),(-1,-2),(-2,-1),(-2,0),(-2,1),
(-2,2),(-1,2),(0,2)$.

**Strategy.** There are $\binom{19}{2}=171$ pairs of stations. A pair lies on a
"dual-link" line (exactly 2 stations) iff no third station is collinear with it.
 Otherwise the pair lies on a high-capacity corridor (3+ stations) and is exclud
ed. Since two distinct lines share at most one point (hence zero pairs), no pair
 is double-counted. Thus

$$\text{dual-link lines} = 171 - (\text{pairs lying on 3+ station lines}).$$

**Finding all 3+ station lines.** For three integer-coordinate points to be coll
inear, they share a primitive direction $(a,b)$ with $\gcd(|a|,|b|)=1$, and the
coordinate span satisfies $(n_3-n_1)|a|\le 4$ and $(n_3-n_1)|b|\le 4$ (coordinat
es range over $[-2,2]$). Since $n_3-n_1\ge 2$, we need $|a|,|b|\le 2$. The primi
tive directions to check are:
$$(0,1),\ (1,0),\ (1,1),\ (1,-1),\ (2,1),\ (2,-1),\ (1,2),\ (1,-2).$$

For each direction I enumerate every parallel line and count its points:

- **Direction $(0,1)$ (fixed $q$):** $q=0\to 5$ pts; $q=-1\to 4$; $q=-2\to 4$; $
q=1\to 3$; $q=2\to 3$.
- **Direction $(1,0)$ (fixed $r$):** $r=0\to 5$ pts; $r=-1\to 4$; $r=-2\to 4$; $
r=1\to 3$; $r=2\to 3$.
- **Direction $(1,-1)$ (fixed $q+r$):** $q+r=0\to 5$ pts; $q+r=1\to 4$; $q+r=-1\
to 4$; ($q+r=\pm2$ have only 2 pts).
- **Direction $(1,1)$ (fixed $r-q$):** $r-q=\pm1\to 3$ pts each; $r-q=\pm2\to 3$
 pts each. (4 lines, all of 3 points.)
- **Direction $(2,1)$:** every line has at most 2 points (no 3+ line).
- **Direction $(2,-1)$:** three 3-point lines: $\{(-2,1),(0,0),(2,-1)\}$, $\{(-2
,2),(0,1),(2,0)\}$, $\{(-2,0),(0,-1),(2,-2)\}$.
- **Direction $(1,2)$:** every line has at most 2 points.
- **Direction $(1,-2)$:** three 3-point lines: $\{(-1,2),(0,0),(1,-2)\}$, $\{(0,
2),(1,0),(2,-2)\}$, $\{(-2,2),(-1,0),(0,-2)\}$.

**Tally of 3+ station lines.**
- 5-point lines: $q=0$, $r=0$, $q+r=0$ → **3 lines**, contributing $3\cdot\binom
{5}{2}=3\cdot10=30$ pairs.
- 4-point lines: $q=-1$, $q=-2$, $r=-1$, $r=-2$, $q+r=1$, $q+r=-1$ → **6 lines**
, contributing $6\cdot\binom{4}{2}=6\cdot6=36$ pairs.
- 3-point lines: $q=1$, $q=2$, $r=1$, $r=2$ (4 lines) $+$ $r-q=\pm1,\pm2$ (4 lin
es) $+$ three $(2,-1)$-lines $+$ three $(1,-2)$-lines → **14 lines**, contributi
ng $14\cdot\binom{3}{2}=14\cdot3=42$ pairs.

Total 3+ lines: $3+6+14=23$. Total pairs on them: $30+36+42=108$.

**Final count.**
$$\text{dual-link lines} = 171 - 108 = 63.$$
