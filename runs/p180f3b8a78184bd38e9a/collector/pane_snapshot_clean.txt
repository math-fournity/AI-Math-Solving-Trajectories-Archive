Each $x_\alpha^*$ defines a linear functional on $X \times X$ by:
$$x_\alpha^*(x_1, x_2) = x_{\alpha 1}^*(x_1) + x_{\alpha 2}^*(x_2).$$

**Step 2: Compute the operator norm of $x_\alpha^*$ on $(X \times X, \|\cdot\|_1

I claim $\|x_\alpha^*\|_{op} = \max\bigl(\|x_{\alpha 1}^*\|_{op},\, \|x_{\alpha
2}^*\|_{op}\bigr)$.

*Upper bound:* For $\|x_1\| + \|x_2\| \le 1$:
$$|x_{\alpha 1}^*(x_1) + x_{\alpha 2}^*(x_2)| \le \|x_{\alpha 1}^*\|_{op}\|x_1\|
 + \|x_{\alpha 2}^*\|_{op}\|x_2\| \le \max\bigl(\|x_{\alpha 1}^*\|_{op},\, \|x_{
\alpha 2}^*\|_{op}\bigr)\bigl(\|x_1\|+\|x_2\|\bigr) \le \max\bigl(\|x_{\alpha 1}
^*\|_{op},\, \|x_{\alpha 2}^*\|_{op}\bigr).$$

*Lower bound:* Assume WLOG $\|x_{\alpha 1}^*\|_{op} \ge \|x_{\alpha 2}^*\|_{op}$
. For any $\varepsilon > 0$, choose $x_1 \in X$ with $\|x_1\| = 1$ and $|x_{\alp
ha 1}^*(x_1)| > \|x_{\alpha 1}^*\|_{op} - \varepsilon$. Setting $x_2 = 0$, we ha

**Step 3: Apply the Uniform Boundedness Principle.**

The hypothesis states that for every $(x_1, x_2) \in X \times X$:
$$\sup_\alpha |x_\alpha^*(x_1, x_2)| = \sup_\alpha |x_{\alpha 1}^*(x_1) + x_{\al
pha 2}^*(x_2)| < \infty.$$

This is precisely **pointwise boundedness** of the family $\{x_\alpha^*\}_\alpha

 The Uniform Boundedness Principle (Banach–Steinhaus) applies to any family of b

ted set, i.e., a net), and yields:
$$\sup_\alpha \|x_\alpha^*\|_{op} < \infty.$$

By Step 2, this gives:
$$\sup_\alpha \max\bigl(\|x_{\alpha 1}^*\|_{op},\, \|x_{\alpha 2}^*\|_{op}\bigr)

$$\|x_{\alpha 1}^*\|_{op} + \|x_{\alpha 2}^*\|_{op} \le 2\max\bigl(\|x_{\alpha 1

$$\sup_\alpha \bigl(\|x_{\alpha 1}^*\|_{op} + \|x_{\alpha 2}^*\|_{op}\bigr) \le
2\sup_\alpha \max\bigl(\|x_{\alpha 1}^*\|_{op},\, \|x_{\alpha 2}^*\|_{op}\bigr)
< \infty.$$
