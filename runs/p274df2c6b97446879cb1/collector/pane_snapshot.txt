## Proof

A$. The polynomial $f(t) = t^3 - t = t(t-1)(t+1)$ annihilates $A$. Over $\mathbb

 their pairwise differences are $1$, $-1$, $-2$, all units in $S$ (since $2 \in
\mathbb{C}^\times$).

$$e_1 = 1 - t^2, \quad e_2 = \tfrac{t(t+1)}{2}, \quad e_3 = \tfrac{t(t-1)}{2}.$$

One verifies: $e_1 + e_2 + e_3 = 1$, $e_i e_j = 0$ for $i \neq j$, $e_i^2 = e_i$

Setting $E_i = e_i(A) \in M_m(S)$, we obtain matrix idempotents: $E_1 + E_2 + E_

$$S^m = \operatorname{Im}(E_1) \oplus \operatorname{Im}(E_2) \oplus \operatornam

Similarly, $\operatorname{Im}(E_2) = \ker(A - I)$ and $\operatorname{Im}(E_3) =

$$BAB^{-1} = D_0 = \operatorname{diag}(\underbrace{0,\dots,0}_{r_0},\underbrace{

$$\boxed{\dfrac{m!}{r_0!\; r_1!\; r_{-1}!}}$$
