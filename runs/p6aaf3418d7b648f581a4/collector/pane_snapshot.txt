## Solution

**Setting up the trapezoid dimensions.**

Let the shorter base be $a$ and the longer base be $b$. The median of a trapezoi
d equals the average of the bases:
$$\frac{a+b}{2} = 15 \implies a + b = 30.$$

The leg has length $13$ and the height is $12$. The horizontal projection of eac
h leg is:
$$\sqrt{13^2 - 12^2} = \sqrt{169 - 144} = \sqrt{25} = 5.$$

Since the trapezoid is isosceles, the longer base exceeds the shorter base by $2
 \times 5 = 10$:
$$b - a = 10.$$

Solving: $b = 20$, $a = 10$. So the shorter base is $10$ and the longer base is
$20$.

**Setting up coordinates.**

Place the shorter base along the $x$-axis (the axis of rotation), centered at th
e origin: from $x = -5$ to $x = 5$ at $y = 0$. The trapezoid extends upward to $
y = 12$, where the longer base runs from $x = -10$ to $x = 10$.

The left edge connects $(-5, 0)$ to $(-10, 12)$; the right edge connects $(5, 0)
$ to $(10, 12)$.

**Computing the volume by the disk method.**

Rotating around the $x$-axis (the shorter base), the cross-section at position $
x$ is a disk of radius $r(x)$ equal to the height of the trapezoid at that $x$:

- For $-10 \le x \le -5$: the left edge gives $r(x) = -\frac{12}{5}(x+5)$, decre
asing from $12$ to $0$.
- For $-5 \le x \le 5$: $r(x) = 12$ (constant, the full height).
- For $5 \le x \le 10$: the right edge gives $r(x) = \frac{12}{5}(x-5)$, increas
ing from $0$ to $12$.

$$V = \pi \left[\int_{-10}^{-5} \left(\frac{-12(x+5)}{5}\right)^2 dx + \int_{-5}
^{5} 144\, dx + \int_{5}^{10} \left(\frac{12(x-5)}{5}\right)^2 dx\right].$$

By symmetry, the first and third integrals are equal.

**End pieces (cones):** Each integral evaluates to
$$\frac{144}{25}\int_{0}^{5} u^2\, du = \frac{144}{25}\cdot\frac{125}{3} = \frac
{144 \cdot 5}{3} = 240.$$

**Middle piece (cylinder):**
$$\int_{-5}^{5} 144\, dx = 144 \times 10 = 1440.$$

**Total:**
$$V = \pi\,[\,240 + 1440 + 240\,] = 1920\pi.$$

**Geometric interpretation.** The solid decomposes into a cylinder of radius $12
$ and length $10$ (volume $1440\pi$) plus two cones, each with base radius $12$
and height $5$ (volume $\frac{1}{3}\pi(144)(5) = 240\pi$ each). This confirms:
$$V = 1440\pi + 2 \times 240\pi = 1920\pi.$$

$$\boxed{V = 1920\pi}$$
