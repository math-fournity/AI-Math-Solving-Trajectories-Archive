Looking at this problem, I need to count valid 2-colorings of a graph on {1, ...
, 2020} where x ~ y iff x + y is a power of 2 (and x ≠ y).

## Proof

**Step 1: Structural decomposition by 2-adic valuation.**

Let $v_2(x)$ denote the 2-adic valuation of $x$ (the largest power of 2 dividing
 $x$). I claim that if $x + y = 2^k$, then $v_2(x) = v_2(y)$.

*Proof:* Write $x = 2^a u$, $y = 2^b v$ with $u, v$ odd. If $a < b$, then $x + y
 = 2^a(u + 2^{b-a}v)$. Since $u$ is odd and $2^{b-a}v$ is even, the sum $u + 2^{
b-a}v$ is odd and $\geq 3$, so it cannot be a power of 2. Thus $a = b$, and $u +
 v = 2^{k-a}$ must be a power of 2. ∎

Therefore, **vertices with different 2-adic valuations are never adjacent**, and
 the graph decomposes into independent subgraphs indexed by $v_2 \in \{0, 1, 2,
\ldots, 10\}$ (since $\lfloor \log_2 2020 \rfloor = 10$). This gives **11 subgra
phs**.

**Step 2: Each subgraph is connected.**

For $v_2 = a$, the vertices are $\{2^a m : m \text{ odd},\; 2^a m \leq 2020\}$,
and two vertices $2^a m_1, 2^a m_2$ are adjacent iff $m_1 + m_2 = 2^{k-a}$ for s
ome $k$. This is isomorphic to the graph on odd numbers $\{1, 3, 5, \ldots, M_a\
}$ (where $M_a$ is the largest odd number $\leq 2020/2^a$) with edges when the s
um is a power of 2.

I claim this graph is connected. For any odd $m \geq 5$ in the set, let $2^c$ be
 the smallest power of 2 strictly greater than $m$. Then $2^{c-1} < m < 2^c$, so
 $2^c - m$ is a positive odd number with $2^c - m < 2^{c-1} < m$, hence $2^c - m
$ is in our set, $2^c - m \neq m$, and $m + (2^c - m) = 2^c$ is a power of 2. So
 $m$ is adjacent to the smaller odd number $2^c - m$. By induction, every odd nu
mber connects down to 1 or 3, and $1 \sim 3$ (since $1 + 3 = 4 = 2^2$). Thus eac
h subgraph is **connected** (a single component). ∎

**Step 3: Each subgraph is bipartite.**

Within the $v_2 = a$ subgraph, color vertex $2^a m$ (with $m$ odd) **Blue** if $
m \equiv 1 \pmod{4}$ and **Red** if $m \equiv 3 \pmod{4}$.

For any edge: $m_1 + m_2 = 2^{k-a}$ where $m_1, m_2$ are distinct odd numbers, s
o $m_1 + m_2 \geq 1 + 3 = 4$, giving $k - a \geq 2$. Thus $m_1 + m_2 = 2^{k-a} \
equiv 0 \pmod{4}$, which means $m_1$ and $m_2$ have **different** residues mod 4
. So every edge connects a Blue vertex to a Red vertex — this is a proper 2-colo
ring. ∎

**Step 4: Count the colorings.**

The graph is bipartite with exactly **11 connected components** (one per 2-adic
valuation class $v_2 = 0, 1, \ldots, 10$). Each connected component, being conne
cted and bipartite, admits exactly **2** valid 2-colorings (fixing one vertex's
color determines all others). The choices across components are independent.

Therefore, the total number of valid protocol distributions is:

$$2^{11} = \boxed{2048}$$
