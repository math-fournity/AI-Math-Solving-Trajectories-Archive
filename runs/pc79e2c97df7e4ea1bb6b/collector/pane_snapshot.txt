## Proof

**Claim:** For $0 \le k \le n-1$, we have $a_k = 3^k \cdot 2^{n-k} - 1$, and $a_
n = \dfrac{3^n - 1}{2}$.

**Base case ($k=0$):** $a_0 = 3^0 \cdot 2^n - 1 = 2^n - 1$. ✓

**Inductive step (for $k \le n-2$):** Assume $a_k = 3^k \cdot 2^{n-k} - 1$. Then
:
$$3a_k + 1 = 3(3^k \cdot 2^{n-k} - 1) + 1 = 3^{k+1} \cdot 2^{n-k} - 2 = 2\bigl(3
^{k+1} \cdot 2^{n-k-1} - 1\bigr).$$

Since $k \le n-2$, we have $n-k-1 \ge 1$, so $3^{k+1} \cdot 2^{n-k-1}$ is even,
making $3^{k+1} \cdot 2^{n-k-1} - 1$ **odd**. Therefore the odd part of $3a_k+1$
 is exactly $3^{k+1} \cdot 2^{n-(k+1)} - 1$, giving:
$$a_{k+1} = 3^{k+1} \cdot 2^{n-(k+1)} - 1. \quad \checkmark$$

This establishes the formula for $k = 0, 1, \dots, n-1$.

**Final step ($k = n-1$):** We have $a_{n-1} = 3^{n-1} \cdot 2^1 - 1 = 2 \cdot 3
^{n-1} - 1$. Then:
$$3a_{n-1} + 1 = 3(2 \cdot 3^{n-1} - 1) + 1 = 2 \cdot 3^n - 2 = 2(3^n - 1).$$

Now I need the odd part of $2(3^n - 1)$. This requires the 2-adic valuation of $
3^n - 1$.

**Computing $v_2(3^n - 1)$ for odd $n$:** Since $n = 2023$ is odd, by the Liftin
g the Exponent Lemma (for $p=2$, odd exponent case): for odd $n$ and odd $a$,
$$v_2(a^n - 1) = v_2(a - 1).$$

With $a = 3$: $v_2(3 - 1) = v_2(2) = 1$.

Therefore $v_2(3^{2023} - 1) = 1$, meaning $3^n - 1 = 2 \cdot q$ where $q$ is od
d. Consequently:
$$3a_{n-1} + 1 = 2(3^n - 1) = 2 \cdot 2q = 4q,$$
and the odd part is $q = \dfrac{3^n - 1}{2}$.

Thus:
$$a_n = \frac{3^n - 1}{2} = \frac{3^{2023} - 1}{2}.$$

**Verification with small case ($n=3$):**

- $3 \cdot 7 + 1 = 22 = 2 \cdot 11 \Rightarrow a_1 = 11 = 3^1 \cdot 2^2 - 1$ ✓
- $3 \cdot 11 + 1 = 34 = 2 \cdot 17 \Rightarrow a_2 = 17 = 3^2 \cdot 2^1 - 1$ ✓
- $3 \cdot 17 + 1 = 52 = 4 \cdot 13 \Rightarrow a_3 = 13 = \frac{3^3 - 1}{2} = \
frac{26}{2} = 13$ ✓

The formula is confirmed.
