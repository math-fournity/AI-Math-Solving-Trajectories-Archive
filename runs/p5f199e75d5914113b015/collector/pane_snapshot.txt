# The Unoriented Bordism Group $\Omega_2^O$

## Answer

$$\Omega_2^O \cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}.$$

## Proof

We use Thom's computation of the unoriented bordism ring. Recall the classical t
heorem of Thom (1954):

> **Theorem (Thom).** The unoriented bordism ring is
> $$\Omega_*^O \cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\big[x_i \mid i \geq 2,\ i \neq 2^k -
 1 \text{ for any } k \geq 1\big],$$
> a polynomial algebra over $\mathbb{F}_2$ with one generator in each degree $i
\geq 2$ that is **not** of the form $2^k - 1$.

The excluded degrees are $1, 3, 7, 15, 31, \dots$ (the numbers $2^k - 1$). Since
 $2$ is **not** of the form $2^k - 1$ (the values $2^k - 1$ are $1, 3, 7, 15, \l
dots$, never $2$), there is exactly one polynomial generator in degree $2$.

Therefore the degree-$2$ part of the ring is a one-dimensional $\mathbb{F}_2$-ve
ctor space:

$$\Omega_2^O \cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}.$$

### Explicit generator

The generator is represented by $\mathbb{RP}^2$. To see this concretely:

- Every closed (unoriented) $2$-manifold is a disjoint union of connected sums o
f tori $T^2$ and projective planes $\mathbb{RP}^2$, classified by orientability
genus $g$ (for orientable surfaces $\#_g T^2$) and non-orientable genus $n$ (for
 $\#_n \mathbb{RP}^2$).
- **Orientable surfaces bound:** $\#_g T^2$ is the boundary of a handlebody of g
enus $g$, so every orientable closed surface is null-bordant.
- **$\mathbb{RP}^2$ does not bound:** its Stiefel–Whitney number $\langle w_1^2,
 [\mathbb{RP}^2]\rangle = 1 \pmod 2$ is nonzero. By Thom's criterion (a closed m
anifold is a boundary iff all its Stiefel–Whitney numbers vanish), $\mathbb{RP}^
2$ is not null-bordant.
- **$\mathbb{RP}^2 \# \mathbb{RP}^2$ bounds:** this is the Klein bottle $K$, and
 $\langle w_1^2, [K]\rangle = 0$, so $K$ is null-bordant. More generally $\#_n \
mathbb{RP}^2$ has $\langle w_1^2\rangle = n \bmod 2$, hence is null-bordant iff
$n$ is even.

Thus every closed $2$-manifold is bordant to either $0$ (if it bounds) or $\math
bb{RP}^2$ (if it doesn't), and $\mathbb{RP}^2 \sqcup \mathbb{RP}^2 \sim_{\text{b
ord}} 0$. This gives exactly two bordism classes:

$$\Omega_2^O = \{0,\ [\mathbb{RP}^2]\} \cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}. \qquad \bla
cksquare$$
