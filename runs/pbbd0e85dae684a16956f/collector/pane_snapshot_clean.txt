## Proof

**Setup.** Let $X = \mathbb{P}^2$ over a field $k$, and let $Y = L_1 \cup L_2 \c
up L_3$ where $L_1: x=0$, $L_2: y=0$, $L_3: z=0$ are the three coordinate lines.
 Set $U = X \setminus Y$. The scheme $X = \mathbb{P}^2$ is noetherian, integral,
 separated, and regular (hence regular in codimension one). By hypothesis $U$ is
 also noetherian, integral, and regular in codimension one, so $\mathrm{Cl}(U)$
is well-defined.

**Method 1: Exact sequence for class groups.**

Since $X = \mathbb{P}^2$ is a noetherian integral separated scheme regular in co
dimension one, the standard exact sequence (Hartshorne II.6.5) for the open subs
et $U \subseteq X$ reads:

$$\bigoplus_{\substack{Z \subseteq X\setminus U \\ \mathrm{codim}(Z,X)=1}} \math
bb{Z}\cdot[Z] \xrightarrow{\;\alpha\;} \mathrm{Cl}(X) \longrightarrow \mathrm{Cl
}(U) \longrightarrow 0$$

where $\alpha$ sends the generator $[Z]$ to the class of $Z$ in $\mathrm{Cl}(X)$
.

The closed subset $X \setminus U = Y = L_1 \cup L_2 \cup L_3$. The codimension-o
ne subvarieties of $\mathbb{P}^2$ contained in $Y$ are exactly the three lines $
L_1, L_2, L_3$ (each is irreducible of codimension 1). Thus the direct sum is $\
mathbb{Z}[L_1] \oplus \mathbb{Z}[L_2] \oplus \mathbb{Z}[L_3] \cong \mathbb{Z}^3$
.

It is well known that $\mathrm{Cl}(\mathbb{P}^2) \cong \mathbb{Z}$, generated by
 the class of any line $[L]$. Each $L_i$ is a line in $\mathbb{P}^2$, so $[L_i]
= 1 \in \mathbb{Z}$ for $i = 1, 2, 3$.

The map $\alpha: \mathbb{Z}^3 \to \mathbb{Z}$ is therefore:
$$(n_1, n_2, n_3) \mapsto n_1[L_1] + n_2[L_2] + n_3[L_3] = n_1 + n_2 + n_3.$$

This map is **surjective**, since $\alpha(1, 0, 0) = 1$ generates all of $\mathb
b{Z}$. Hence:

$$\mathrm{Cl}(U) = \mathrm{coker}(\alpha) = \mathbb{Z}/\mathrm{im}(\alpha) = \ma
thbb{Z}/\mathbb{Z} = 0.$$

**Method 2: Direct identification of $U$ with $\mathbb{G}_m^2$ (verification).**

We identify $U = D_+(xyz) \subset \mathbb{P}^2$, so $U = \mathrm{Spec}\, A$ wher
e $A = (k[x,y,z]_{xyz})_0$ is the degree-zero part of the localization. An eleme
nt of $A$ has the form $f/(xyz)^n$ with $f$ homogeneous of degree $3n$.

Set $a = x/y$, $b = y/z$, $c = z/x$. Then $abc = 1$, and the generators of $A$ a
re $a, a^{-1}, b, b^{-1}, c, c^{-1}$ subject to $abc = 1$. Eliminating $c = (ab)
^{-1}$:

$$A \cong k[a^{\pm 1}, b^{\pm 1}].$$

Thus $U \cong \mathbb{G}_m^2 = \mathrm{Spec}\, k[a^{\pm 1}, b^{\pm 1}]$.

The ring $k[a^{\pm 1}, b^{\pm 1}]$ is a localization of the polynomial ring $k[a
,b]$, which is a UFD. A localization of a UFD is again a UFD, so $k[a^{\pm 1}, b
^{\pm 1}]$ is a UFD. For an affine noetherian integral scheme regular in codimen
sion one whose coordinate ring is a UFD, the class group vanishes. Therefore $\m
athrm{Cl}(U) = 0$.

**Conclusion.** Both methods agree:

$$\boxed{\mathrm{Cl}(U) = 0.}$$
