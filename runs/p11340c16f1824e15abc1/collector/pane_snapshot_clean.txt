Looking at this problem, I need to count convex hexagons from vertices of a regu
lar 21-gon that have at least one acute angle.

## Setup

Let $M$ be a regular $21$-gon (since $2n+1 = 21$). A convex hexagon is determine
d by choosing 6 vertices of $M$, giving $\binom{21}{6}$ total convex hexagons.

**Gap representation.** For a chosen hexagon with vertices $V_1, V_2, \ldots, V_
6$ in counterclockwise order, define gaps $a_1, \ldots, a_6$ where $a_i$ is the
number of edges of $M$ between $V_i$ and $V_{i+1}$ (cyclically). Then $a_i \geq
1$ and $\sum a_i = 21$.

## Acute Angle Condition

The interior angle at $V_i$ is an inscribed angle subtending the arc from $V_{i-
1}$ to $V_{i+1}$ **not** containing $V_i$. This arc has length $21 - a_i$ edges,
 so:

$$\angle V_i = \frac{1}{2} \cdot (21 - a_i) \cdot \frac{2\pi}{21} = \frac{(21 -
a_i)\pi}{21}$$

This is acute when:
$$\frac{(21 - a_i)\pi}{21} < \frac{\pi}{2} \implies 21 - a_i < 10.5 \implies a_i
 \geq 11$$

So **the angle at $V_i$ is acute if and only if $a_i \geq 11$**.

## Counting via Complement

I count hexagons with **no** acute angle (all $a_i \leq 10$) and subtract from t
he total.

**Counting ordered gap sequences.** Each hexagon corresponds to $21 \times 6$ or
dered pairs (starting vertex, cyclic rotation) but is overcounted by a factor of
 6 (one per vertex). So:

$$\text{# hexagons} = \frac{21}{6} \times (\text{# ordered gap sequences})$$

**Compositions with $1 \leq a_i \leq 10$, $\sum = 21$.** Set $b_i = a_i - 1 \in
\{0,\ldots,9\}$, $\sum b_i = 15$. By inclusion-exclusion:

- Unrestricted: $\binom{20}{5} = 15504$
- Subtract cases with some $b_i \geq 10$: set $c_i = b_i - 10$, $\sum c_i = 5$,
giving $\binom{10}{5} = 252$ per variable, times 6 variables: $6 \times 252 = 15
12$
- Two variables $\geq 10$ needs $\sum b_i \geq 20 > 15$: impossible.

$$\text{Compositions with all } a_i \leq 10 = 15504 - 1512 = 13992$$

$$\text{Hexagons with no acute angle} = \frac{21}{6} \times 13992 = 21 \times 23
32 = 48972$$

## Final Computation

$$\binom{21}{6} = \frac{21 \cdot 20 \cdot 19 \cdot 18 \cdot 17 \cdot 16}{720} =
54264$$

$$\text{Hexagons with at least one acute angle} = 54264 - 48972 = \boxed{5292}$$

## Verification

Since at most one $a_i$ can be $\geq 11$ (two would require $\sum a_i \geq 22 >
21$), I can directly count ordered gap sequences with exactly one $a_i \geq 11$:

For position $a_1 = 11 + t$ ($t = 0, \ldots, 5$), the remaining 5 parts sum to $
10 - t$ with each $\geq 1$:

| $t$ | $a_1$ | Remaining sum | Count $\binom{\cdot}{4}$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 11 | 10 | 126 |
| 1 | 12 | 9 | 70 |
| 2 | 13 | 8 | 35 |
| 3 | 14 | 7 | 15 |
| 4 | 15 | 6 | 5 |
| 5 | 16 | 5 | 1 |

Total per position: $126 + 70 + 35 + 15 + 5 + 1 = 252$. Times 6 positions: $1512
$.

$$\text{Hexagons with at least one acute angle} = \frac{21}{6} \times 1512 = 21
\times 252 = 5292 \checkmark$$

Both methods agree.
