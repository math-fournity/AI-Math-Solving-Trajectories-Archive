## Solution

The region $R$ is the Voronoi cell of $P_0=(0,0)$: all points $(x,y)$ with $x^2+
y^2 < (x-a_i)^2+(y-b_i)^2$ for every other $P_i=(a_i,b_i)$. Expanding, each cons
traint becomes $2a_i x + 2b_i y < a_i^2+b_i^2$, i.e. a half-plane bounded by the
 perpendicular bisector of $P_0P_i$.

**Bisectors:**
- $P_1=(0,4)$: $8y=16 \Rightarrow y=2$, constraint $y<2$.
- $P_2=(4,0)$: $8x=16 \Rightarrow x=2$, constraint $x<2$.
- $P_3=(-2,-2)$: $-4x-4y=8 \Rightarrow x+y=-2$, constraint $x+y>-2$.
- $P_4=(3,3)$: $6x+6y=18 \Rightarrow x+y=3$, constraint $x+y<3$.
- $P_5=(5,5)$: $10x+10y=50 \Rightarrow x+y=5$, constraint $x+y<5$.

Since $P_4$ and $P_5$ lie on the same ray from the origin, $x+y<3$ (from $P_4$)
implies $x+y<5$ (from $P_5$). **The $P_5$ constraint is redundant** and contribu
tes no boundary.

So $R = \{(x,y): x<2,\ y<2,\ x+y>-2,\ x+y<3\}$, bounded by four lines: $x=2$, $y
=2$, $x+y=-2$, $x+y=3$.

**Vertices** (pairwise intersections satisfying all constraints):
- $x=2,\ x+y=-2$: $(2,-4)$. Check $y<2$ ✓, $x+y<3$ ✓ → vertex.
- $x=2,\ x+y=3$: $(2,1)$. Check $y<2$ ✓, $x+y>-2$ ✓ → vertex.
- $y=2,\ x+y=3$: $(1,2)$. Check $x<2$ ✓, $x+y>-2$ ✓ → vertex.
- $y=2,\ x+y=-2$: $(-4,2)$. Check $x<2$ ✓, $x+y<3$ ✓ → vertex.
- $(x=2,y=2)$ gives $x+y=4\not<3$, rejected.
- $x+y=-2$ and $x+y=3$ are parallel, no intersection.

The four vertices, in order, form a quadrilateral:
$$(2,-4)\to(2,1)\to(1,2)\to(-4,2)\to(2,-4).$$

**Side lengths:**
- $(2,-4)$ to $(2,1)$ (on $x=2$): $|1-(-4)|=5$.
- $(2,1)$ to $(1,2)$ (on $x+y=3$): $\sqrt{1^2+1^2}=\sqrt{2}$.
- $(1,2)$ to $(-4,2)$ (on $y=2$): $|1-(-4)|=5$.
- $(-4,2)$ to $(2,-4)$ (on $x+y=-2$): $\sqrt{6^2+(-6)^2}=\sqrt{72}=6\sqrt{2}$.

**Perimeter:**
$$5+\sqrt{2}+5+6\sqrt{2}=10+7\sqrt{2}.$$
