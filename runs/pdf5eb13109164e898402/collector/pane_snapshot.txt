## Solution

Let $A = k[X,Y]/(Y - X^2)$ be the coordinate ring of $V = V(Y - X^2) \subset k^2
$. The origin $(0,0)$ lies on $V$ since $0 - 0^2 = 0$. The maximal ideal of $A$
corresponding to the origin is
$$\mathfrak{m} = (X, Y)/(Y - X^2) \subset A.$$

The local ring at the origin is $\mathcal{O}_{(0,0),V} = A_{\mathfrak{m}}$, with
 maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}_{(0,0),V} = \mathfrak{m} A_{\mathfrak{m}}$. The Zar
iski cotangent space is $\mathfrak{m}_{(0,0),V}/\mathfrak{m}_{(0,0),V}^2$, a $k$
-vector space (since $k = A/\mathfrak{m} = \mathcal{O}_{(0,0),V}/\mathfrak{m}_{(
0,0),V}$).

**Step 1: Simplify $\mathfrak{m}$.**

In $A$, the relation $Y - X^2 = 0$ gives $Y = X^2$. Hence in $A$,
$$\mathfrak{m} = (X, Y) = (X, X^2) = (X).$$
So $\mathfrak{m}$ is principal, generated by the class of $X$.

**Step 2: Compute $\mathfrak{m}^2$.**
$$\mathfrak{m}^2 = (X)^2 = (X^2) = (Y),$$
using again $Y = X^2$ in $A$.

**Step 3: Compute the quotient $\mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2$.**

Localization commutes with quotients for finitely generated modules, and $\mathf
rak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2$ is already a finite-dimensional $k = A/\mathfrak{m}$-vect
or space, so localization does not change it:
$$\mathfrak{m}_{(0,0),V}/\mathfrak{m}_{(0,0),V}^2 \cong \mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m
}^2 = (X)/(X^2).$$

As a $k$-vector space, $(X)/(X^2)$ is generated by the class of $X$. It is nonze
ro (since $X \notin (X^2)$ — otherwise $X = X^2 \cdot f$ in $A$, which is imposs
ible by degree considerations, as $A \cong k[X]$ via $X \mapsto X,\ Y \mapsto X^
2$, and in $k[X]$ we have $X \notin (X^2)$). Therefore
$$\dim_k \mathfrak{m}_{(0,0),V}/\mathfrak{m}_{(0,0),V}^2 = 1.$$

**Geometric check.** $V(Y - X^2)$ is a smooth (nonsingular) curve, so at every p
oint — including the origin — the cotangent space has dimension equal to $\dim V
 = 1$. This confirms the computation.

$$\boxed{\dim_k\, \mathfrak{m}_{(0,0),V}/\mathfrak{m}_{(0,0),V}^2 = 1}$$
