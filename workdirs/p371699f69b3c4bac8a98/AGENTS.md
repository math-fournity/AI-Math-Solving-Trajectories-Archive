# Problem

A regional logistics company operates a central warehouse divided into a grid of $n \times n$ storage zones. To ensure high safety standards, the company must install $n$ specialized fire-suppression sensors, placing exactly one sensor in each of $n$ distinct zones.

The warehouse layout is governed by a strict "Risk Coverage" regulation: every rectangular section of the grid—formed by the existing boundary lines—that has a total area of at least $n$ units must contain at least one zone equipped with a sensor. 

If the safety inspector determines that even a single rectangular area of size $n$ or larger is left without a sensor, the warehouse fails its certification. 

Find the largest integer $n$ for which it is possible to arrange the $n$ sensors such that this safety condition is satisfied.
