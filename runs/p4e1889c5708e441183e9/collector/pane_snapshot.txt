 even. A scanner at any square covers every square in its row and every square i
n its column. Thus a white square $(r,c)$ is covered if and only if **some scann
er sits in row $r$ or some scanner sits in column $c$**.

**Key reformulation.** Let $R$ = set of rows containing at least one scanner, $C
$ = set of columns containing at least one scanner. Let $R' = \{1,\dots,8\}\setm
inus R$ and $C' = \{1,\dots,8\}\setminus C$. Every white square is covered iff t
here is **no** white square $(r,c)$ with $r\in R'$ and $c\in C'$, i.e., every sq

**Color structure.** Partition rows into type $A$ (odd: 1,3,5,7) and type $B$ (e
ven: 2,4,6,8); likewise columns into type $A$ (1,3,5,7) and type $B$ (2,4,6,8).
A square $(r,c)$ is white exactly when $r$ and $c$ share the same type; it is bl
ack exactly when they have opposite types.

**Constraint on $R'\times C'$.** For $R'\times C'$ to be entirely black, every r
ow in $R'$ and every column in $C'$ must have opposite types. If $R'$ contained
both a type-$A$ row $r_A$ and a type-$B$ row $r_B$, then any column $c\in C'$ wo
uld need $(r_A,c)$ black (forcing $c$ type $B$) **and** $(r_B,c)$ black (forcing
 $c$ type $A$) — impossible. Hence either $C'=\varnothing$, or $R'$ is single-ty
pe. Symmetrically, either $R'=\varnothing$ or $C'$ is single-type. The viable no
ntrivial configurations are:
- $R'$ = all 4 type-$A$ rows, $C'$ = all 4 type-$B$ columns (or the symmetric sw
ap), giving $|R|=4, |C|=4$;
- or one of $R', C'$ empty, forcing $|C|=8$ or $|R|=8$.

**Lower bound: 3 scanners cannot suffice.** With $k$ scanners, $|R|\le k$ and $|
C|\le k$, so $|R'|\ge 8-k$ and $|C'|\ge 8-k$. For $k=3$: $|R'|\ge 5$. Since ther
e are only 4 rows of each type, $R'$ must contain both types, which (by the argu
ment above) forces $C'=\varnothing$. But $|C'|\ge 5>0$ — contradiction. So $k\ge
 4$.

**Upper bound: 4 scanners suffice.** Take $R'=\{1,3,5,7\}$ (type $A$ rows) and $
C'=\{2,4,6,8\}$ (type $B$ columns). Then $R=\{2,4,6,8\}$ and $C=\{1,3,5,7\}$. Pl

- White squares in type-$B$ rows $\{2,4,6,8\}$ lie in type-$B$ columns $\{2,4,6,
8\}$; these are covered by their **rows** (all in $R$). ✓
- White squares in type-$A$ rows $\{1,3,5,7\}$ lie in type-$A$ columns $\{1,3,5,
7\}$; these are covered by their **columns** (all in $C$). ✓

All 32 white squares are covered, and $R'\times C'$ consists of squares like $(1

**Conclusion.** The minimum number of scanners is $\boxed{4}$.
