$$a\cdot\frac{k(k+1)}{2}+b = \frac{m_k(m_k+1)}{2}, \qquad k\ge1. \tag{1}$$

$$m_k = \frac{-1+\sqrt{4ak^2+4ak+(8b+1)}}{2} = \sqrt{a}\,k + \frac{\sqrt{a}-1}{2
