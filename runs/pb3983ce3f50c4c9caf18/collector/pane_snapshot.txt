**Step 1: Count labeled circular colorings.**

Fix position $0 = K$ (multiply by 3 at the end for the choice of which nationali
ty sits at position 0). The remaining 2 K's occupy positions $p, q$ with $2 \le
p < q \le 7$ and $q \ge p+2$ (non-adjacency). There are $\binom{6}{2} - 5 = 10$
such pairs.

The 3 K-positions divide the circle into 3 arcs of non-K positions with lengths
$(a, b, c) = (p-1,\; q-p-1,\; 8-q)$, where $a, b, c \ge 1$ and $a+b+c = 6$. The
10 pairs give all 10 compositions of 6 into 3 positive parts.

Each arc is filled with alternating C/J (endpoints touch K, so no cross-arc cons
traint). For an arc of length $L$:
- **$L$ even:** both alternating patterns yield $L/2$ C's — 2 choices, fixed C-c
ount.
- **$L$ odd:** one pattern gives $\lceil L/2\rceil$ C's, the other $\lfloor L/2\
rfloor$ C's — 1 choice each.

We need total C-count $= 3$. I enumerate by parity type:

**All even:** Only $(2,2,2)$. Each arc contributes 1 C. Total $= 3$ ✓. Ways $= 2
^3 = 8$.

**Two odd, one even (even $= 4$):** Triples $(1,1,4), (1,4,1), (4,1,1)$. The eve
n arc contributes 2 C's (2 choices). The two length-1 arcs must contribute $0+1$
 or $1+0$ (2 ways). Total per triple $= 2 \times 2 = 4$. Subtotal $= 3 \times 4
= 12$.

**Two odd, one even (even $= 2$):** Triples $(1,2,3)$ and its 5 permutations. Th
e even arc contributes 1 C (2 choices). The two odd arcs must sum to 2 C's: eith
er $(\lfloor\cdot\rfloor, \lceil\cdot\rceil)$ or $(\lceil\cdot\rceil, \lfloor\cd
ot\rfloor)$ — 2 ways. Total per triple $= 2 \times 2 = 4$. Subtotal $= 6 \times
4 = 24$.

(All-odd is impossible since sum of three odds is odd $\ne 6$; two-even-one-odd
gives odd sum $\ne 6$.)

**Total with position $0 = K$:** $8 + 12 + 24 = 44$.

**Labeled circular colorings** $= 3 \times 44 = 132$.

**Step 2: Count distinct seatings up to rotation.**

$$\text{Distinct seatings} = \frac{28512}{9} = 3168.$$

**Verification of the no-symmetry claim:** A coloring with period 3 (e.g., K,C,J

ign 3 distinct Korean chefs to the 3 K-slots, 3 distinct Chinese chefs to C-slot

shifts which specific person occupies each seat. So Burnside with only the ident
ity is correct.
