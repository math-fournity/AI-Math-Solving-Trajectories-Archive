# Problem

A high-tech automated warehouse uses a vertical conveyor belt to manage a stack of 52 specialized storage crates. Initially, all 52 crates are oriented with their "Service Side" facing the left wall of the shaft.

The warehouse robot is programmed to perform a specific cycle: it removes the top 7 crates from the stack as a single unit, rotates that entire block of 7 crates 180 degrees so their "Service Side" now faces the right wall, and slides this block underneath the remaining crates at the bottom of the stack.

The robot continues to repeat this identical cycle—taking the top 7 crates, flipping their orientation, and placing them at the bottom—until the entire stack of 52 crates is once again oriented with every single "Service Side" facing the left wall.

How many times must the robot perform this cycle to return the stack to its original state?
