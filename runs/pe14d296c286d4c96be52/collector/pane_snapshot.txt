## Solution

**Step 1: $a$ must be an integer.**

The left-hand side $\lfloor a/2\rfloor + \lfloor a/3\rfloor + \lfloor a/5\rfloor
$ is a sum of integers, hence an integer. Therefore $a$ must be an integer. Writ
e $a = n \in \mathbb{Z}$.

**Step 2: Reduce modulo 30.**

We need $\lfloor n/2\rfloor + \lfloor n/3\rfloor + \lfloor n/5\rfloor = n$. Writ
e $n = 30q + r$ with $q \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $r \in \{0,1,\dots,29\}$. Then

$$\lfloor n/2\rfloor = 15q + \lfloor r/2\rfloor,\quad \lfloor n/3\rfloor = 10q +
 \lfloor r/3\rfloor,\quad \lfloor n/5\rfloor = 6q + \lfloor r/5\rfloor.$$

Summing: $31q + \lfloor r/2\rfloor + \lfloor r/3\rfloor + \lfloor r/5\rfloor = 3
0q + r$, i.e.

$$q = r - \lfloor r/2\rfloor - \lfloor r/3\rfloor - \lfloor r/5\rfloor =: g(r).$
