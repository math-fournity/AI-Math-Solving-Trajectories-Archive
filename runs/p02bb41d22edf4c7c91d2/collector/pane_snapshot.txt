## Solution

We need the last 30 digits of $n!(n+1)!(2n+1)! - 1$ to be all 9s, i.e.,
$$n!(n+1)!(2n+1)! - 1 \equiv 10^{30}-1 \pmod{10^{30}},$$
which is equivalent to
$$n!(n+1)!(2n+1)! \equiv 0 \pmod{10^{30}}.$$

This requires $v_{10}\bigl(n!(n+1)!(2n+1)!\bigr) \geq 30$, where $v_{10} = \min(
v_2, v_5)$. Since $v_2$ of a product of factorials is always far larger than $v_
5$, the binding constraint is:
$$v_5(n!) + v_5((n+1)!) + v_5((2n+1)!) \geq 30,$$
using Legendre's formula $v_5(m!) = \sum_{k\geq 1}\lfloor m/5^k\rfloor$.

**Tabulating for increasing $n$:**

| $n$ | $v_5(n!)$ | $v_5((n{+}1)!)$ | $v_5((2n{+}1)!)$ | Sum |
|-----|-----------|------------------|-------------------|-----|
| 30  | 7         | 7                | 14                | 28  |
| 31  | 7         | 7                | 14                | 28  |
| 32  | 7         | 7                | 15                | 29  |
| 33  | 7         | 7                | 15                | 29  |
| **34** | **7**   | **8**            | **15**            | **30** |

**Verification of the key entries:**

- $v_5(34!) = \lfloor 34/5\rfloor + \lfloor 34/25\rfloor = 6 + 1 = 7$.
- $v_5(35!) = \lfloor 35/5\rfloor + \lfloor 35/25\rfloor = 7 + 1 = 8$ (the jump
from 7→8 occurs because $35 = 5\cdot 7$ contributes an extra factor of 5).
- $v_5(69!) = \lfloor 69/5\rfloor + \lfloor 69/25\rfloor = 13 + 2 = 15$.

Sum at $n=34$: $7 + 8 + 15 = 30 \geq 30$. ✓

**Minimality:** For $n=33$: $v_5(33!) + v_5(34!) + v_5(67!) = 7 + 7 + 15 = 29 <
30$. ✗
For all $n \leq 33$ the sum is $\leq 29$, so none work.

**Checking $v_2$ at $n=34$:** $v_2(34!)+v_2(35!)+v_2(69!) = 32 + 32 + 66 = 130 \
geq 30$. ✓

Therefore $10^{30} \mid 34!\cdot 35!\cdot 69!$, so $34!\cdot 35!\cdot 69! - 1 \e
quiv -1 \equiv \underbrace{99\ldots9}_{30} \pmod{10^{30}}$, and $n=34$ is the sm
allest such positive integer.

$$\boxed{n = 34}$$
