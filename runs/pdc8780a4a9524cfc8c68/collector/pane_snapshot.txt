**Proof.** Suppose $\mathcal{O}_L$ is free of rank $r$ as an $\mathcal{O}_K[G]$-
module, i.e.,
$$\mathcal{O}_L \cong (\mathcal{O}_K[G])^r$$
as $\mathcal{O}_K[G]$-modules for some positive integer $r$.

We compare $\mathcal{O}_K$-module ranks on both sides.

**Left side.** Since $L/K$ is a finite extension of number fields with $[L:K] =
|G|$ (as $L/K$ is Galois), $\mathcal{O}_L$ is a free $\mathcal{O}_K$-module of r
ank $[L:K] = |G|$.

**Right side.** The group ring $\mathcal{O}_K[G]$ is a free $\mathcal{O}_K$-modu
le with basis $\{e_g : g \in G\}$, hence of $\mathcal{O}_K$-rank $|G|$. Therefor
e $(\mathcal{O}_K[G])^r$ is a free $\mathcal{O}_K$-module of rank $r \cdot |G|$.

**Comparison.** Since an isomorphism of $\mathcal{O}_K[G]$-modules is in particu
lar an isomorphism of $\mathcal{O}_K$-modules, we equate the $\mathcal{O}_K$-ran
ks:
$$|G| = r \cdot |G|.$$

Since $|G| \geq 1$ (the extension is nontrivial; $|G| = 1$ is trivial and also g
ives $r=1$), we divide to obtain:
$$r = 1. \qquad \blacksquare$$

**Remark.** The argument is a pure rank-counting argument over the Dedekind doma
in $\mathcal{O}_K$. The hypothesis that $G$ is abelian is not needed for this co
nclusion — the rank must be 1 for any finite Galois group. The abelian hypothesi
s is relevant to the deeper question of *when* freeness holds (e.g., the Hilbert
–Speiser theorem: a tame abelian extension $L/\mathbb{Q}$ admits a normal integr
al basis), but once freeness is assumed, the rank is forced to be 1 regardless.
