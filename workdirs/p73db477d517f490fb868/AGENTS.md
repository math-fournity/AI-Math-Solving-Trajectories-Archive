# Problem

In a remote sector of the ocean, a maritime rescue agency is establishing a triangular search grid defined by three buoys: $A$, $B$, and $C$. Buoys $B$ and $C$ are anchored exactly $12$ kilometers apart, forming the base of an isosceles triangle where the distances from $A$ to $B$ and $A$ to $C$ are equal.

The agency uses a specialized sonar drone to monitor the area. A "Type-Z Signal Location" is defined as any point $P$ where the drone’s distance to buoy $A$, its distance to buoy $B$, and its distance to buoy $C$ maintain a fixed ratio of $3:4:5$, respectively.

Upon calibrating the grid, technicians discover that the geometry of this specific triangle $ABC$ allows for exactly one such Type-Z Signal Location within the entire plane.

Based on this unique configuration, calculate the total area of the triangular search grid $ABC$ in square kilometers.
