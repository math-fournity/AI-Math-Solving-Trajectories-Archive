# Proof

**Claim:** Yes. Every finite extension of the form $\mathbb{Z}_p[x]/\langle p(x)
\rangle : \mathbb{Z}_p$, where $p$ is prime and $p(x)$ is irreducible over $\mat
hbb{Z}_p$, is a Galois extension.

**Setup.** Let $p$ be prime, $p(x) \in \mathbb{Z}_p[x]$ irreducible of degree $n
$, and $L = \mathbb{Z}_p[x]/\langle p(x)\rangle$. Then $L$ is a field extension
of $\mathbb{Z}_p$ of degree $n$, so $|L| = p^n$. Thus $L \cong \mathbb{F}_{p^n}$
, the finite field with $p^n$ elements. A finite extension $L/K$ is Galois iff i
t is **normal** and **separable**. We verify both.

---

### Step 1: Separability

We show $p(x)$ is separable over $\mathbb{Z}_p$ (i.e., has no repeated roots in
any extension), which immediately gives that $L/\mathbb{Z}_p$ is separable since
 $L$ is generated by a root of a separable polynomial.

Recall: an irreducible polynomial $f$ over a field of characteristic $p$ is inse
parable iff $f'(x) \equiv 0$, which (for irreducible $f$) happens iff $f(x) = g(
x^p)$ for some polynomial $g$.

Suppose for contradiction that $p(x)$ is inseparable, so $p(x) = g(x^p)$ for som
e $g(x) = \sum a_i x^i \in \mathbb{Z}_p[x]$. Consider the **Frobenius map** $\va
rphi: \mathbb{Z}_p \to \mathbb{Z}_p$, $\varphi(a) = a^p$. Since $\mathbb{Z}_p$ i
s finite and $\varphi$ is a ring homomorphism with trivial kernel ($a^p = 0 \Rig
htarrow a = 0$ in a field), $\varphi$ is injective, hence **bijective** on the f
inite set $\mathbb{Z}_p$. So every $a_i$ has a $p$-th root $b_i \in \mathbb{Z}_p
$ with $b_i^p = a_i$. Then

$$p(x) = g(x^p) = \sum a_i x^{ip} = \sum b_i^p x^{ip} = \sum (b_i x^i)^p = \left
(\sum b_i x^i\right)^p,$$

where the last equality uses the Frobenius endomorphism $(u+v)^p = u^p + v^p$ in
 characteristic $p$. This expresses $p(x)$ as a $p$-th power of a non-constant p
olynomial (since $\deg p \geq 1$), contradicting the irreducibility of $p(x)$.

Therefore $p(x)$ is separable, and $L/\mathbb{Z}_p$ is a separable extension.

---

### Step 2: Normality

We show $L$ is the splitting field of a polynomial over $\mathbb{Z}_p$, which is
 equivalent to $L/\mathbb{Z}_p$ being normal.

Consider the polynomial $f(x) = x^{p^n} - x \in \mathbb{Z}_p[x]$. Its roots in a
ny extension form a field: if $\alpha^{p^n} = \alpha$ and $\beta^{p^n} = \beta$,
 then $(\alpha+\beta)^{p^n} = \alpha^{p^n}+\beta^{p^n} = \alpha+\beta$ (Frobeniu
s), and similarly for products and inverses. The set of roots is thus a subfield
 of size at most $p^n$ (since $f$ has at most $p^n$ roots).

Now $L = \mathbb{F}_{p^n}$ has exactly $p^n$ elements, and **every** element $\a
lpha \in L$ satisfies $\alpha^{p^n} = \alpha$ (the multiplicative group $L^\time
s$ has order $p^n - 1$, so $\alpha^{p^n-1}=1$ for $\alpha \neq 0$, and $0^{p^n}=
0$). Hence all $p^n$ elements of $L$ are roots of $f$, and $f$ splits completely
 over $L$:

$$x^{p^n} - x = \prod_{\alpha \in L}(x - \alpha).$$

Since $f$ has degree $p^n$ and $p^n$ distinct roots (separable: $f'(x) = p^n x^{
p^n-1} - 1 = -1 \neq 0$ in $\mathbb{Z}_p$), all lying in $L$, the field $L$ is t
he splitting field of $f$ over $\mathbb{Z}_p$. Therefore $L/\mathbb{Z}_p$ is nor
mal.

---

### Step 3: Conclusion

The extension $L/\mathbb{Z}_p$ is:
- **separable** (Step 1: $p(x)$ is separable since the Frobenius is surjective o
n $\mathbb{Z}_p$, ruling out inseparable irreducibles), and
- **normal** (Step 2: $L = \mathbb{F}_{p^n}$ is the splitting field of $x^{p^n}-
x$ over $\mathbb{Z}_p$).

By the characterization theorem, a finite extension is Galois if and only if it
is both normal and separable. Hence $L/\mathbb{Z}_p$ is Galois.

**Answer: Yes, all such extensions are Galois.** In fact, every finite extension
 of a finite field is Galois (with cyclic Galois group generated by the Frobeniu
s automorphism $\alpha \mapsto \alpha^p$), and the extensions $\mathbb{Z}_p[x]/\
langle p(x)\rangle : \mathbb{Z}_p$ are precisely the finite extensions of $\math
bb{Z}_p$.
