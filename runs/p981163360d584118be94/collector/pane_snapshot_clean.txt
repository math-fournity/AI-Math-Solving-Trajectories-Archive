## Computing the Intersection Area

Decompose the intersection into 5 congruent sectors using the center $O$. Each s

- **Triangle** $OC_5C_1$ (isosceles, apex angle $72°$ at $O$)
- **Circular segment** of disk 1 between chord $C_5C_1$ and arc $C_5C_1$ (centra

**Distance $d = |OC_1|$:** By the law of cosines in $\triangle OV_1C_1$ with $\a

$$d^2 = R^2 + 1 - 2R\cos 6°$$

**Triangle area per sector:**
$$A_{\triangle} = \tfrac{1}{2} d^2 \sin 72°$$

**Segment area per sector:**
$$A_{\text{seg}} = \tfrac{1}{2}\left(\tfrac{\pi}{15} - \sin 12°\right)$$

**Total intersection area:**
$$A_{\text{int}} = 5\left[\tfrac{1}{2}d^2\sin 72° + \tfrac{1}{2}\left(\tfrac{\pi

$$= \tfrac{5}{2}d^2\sin 72° + \tfrac{5}{2}\cdot\tfrac{\pi}{15} - \tfrac{5}{2}\si

## Simplifying $d^2 \sin 72°$

Using $R = \frac{1}{2\sin 36°}$ and $\sin 72° = 2\sin 36°\cos 36°$:

$$R^2\sin 72° = \frac{\sin 72°}{4\sin^2 36°} = \frac{2\sin 36°\cos 36°}{4\sin^2
36°} = \frac{\cos 36°}{2\sin 36°} = \tfrac{1}{2}\cot 36°$$

$$2R\cos 6°\sin 72° = \frac{\cos 6° \cdot 2\sin 36°\cos 36°}{\sin 36°} = 2\cos 6
°\cos 36°$$

So: $d^2\sin 72° = \tfrac{1}{2}\cot 36° + \sin 72° - 2\cos 6°\cos 36°$

$$A_{\text{int}} = \tfrac{5}{4}\cot 36° + \tfrac{5}{2}\sin 72° - 5\cos 6°\cos 36
° + \tfrac{\pi}{6} - \tfrac{5}{2}\sin 12°$$

## Remaining Area

The pentagon area is $A_{\text{pent}} = \frac{5}{4}\cot 36°$. Therefore:

$$A_{\text{rem}} = A_{\text{pent}} - A_{\text{int}} = 5\cos 6°\cos 36° - \tfrac{
5}{2}\sin 72° + \tfrac{5}{2}\sin 12° - \tfrac{\pi}{6}$$

**Key simplifications using product-to-sum and sum-to-product:**

$$5\cos 6°\cos 36° = \tfrac{5}{2}\left[\cos 30° + \cos 42°\right] = \tfrac{5\sqr
t{3}}{4} + \tfrac{5}{2}\cos 42°$$

$$\sin 72° - \sin 12° = 2\cos 42°\sin 30° = \cos 42°$$

So:

$$\tfrac{5}{2}\sin 72° - \tfrac{5}{2}\sin 12° = \tfrac{5}{2}\cos 42°$$

Substituting:

$$A_{\text{rem}} = \tfrac{5\sqrt{3}}{4} + \tfrac{5}{2}\cos 42° - \tfrac{5}{2}\co
s 42° - \tfrac{\pi}{6}$$

The $\cos 42°$ terms **cancel completely**:

$$\boxed{A_{\text{rem}} = \frac{5\sqrt{3}}{4} - \frac{\pi}{6}}$$

**Numerical verification:** $\frac{5\sqrt{3}}{4} \approx 2.1651$, $\frac{\pi}{6}

\approx 1.7205$ and the removed region $\approx 0.0790$ (a small central region)
