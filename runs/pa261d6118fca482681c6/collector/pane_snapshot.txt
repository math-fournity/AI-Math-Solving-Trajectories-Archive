$$D = s^2 - \frac{4s(s-2^{55})}{3} = \frac{3s^2 - 4s^2 + 2^{57}s}{3} = \frac{s(2
^{57}-s)}{3}.$$

So $0 \le s \le 2^{57}$, and $s(2^{57}-s) \equiv 0 \pmod{3}$. Since $2^{57} \equ

$$v^2 + 3w^2 = 2^{112}. \tag{$\star$}$$

The parity condition $s \equiv w \pmod{2}$ becomes $v \equiv w \pmod{2}$ (since
