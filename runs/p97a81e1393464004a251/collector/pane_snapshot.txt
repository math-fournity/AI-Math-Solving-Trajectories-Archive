## Proof

**Setup.** Place $O$ at the origin. Let $\hat{x}, \hat{y}, \hat{z}$ be unit vect
ors along the rays $OX, OY, OZ$ respectively. Since $G$ lies in the interior of
the trihedral angle, the three direction vectors are linearly independent, so $\
{\hat{x}, \hat{y}, \hat{z}\}$ is a basis. Write

$$G = g_1\,\hat{x} + g_2\,\hat{y} + g_3\,\hat{z}, \qquad g_1, g_2, g_3 > 0.$$

A plane meeting the three rays at $A, B, C$ has $A = a\,\hat{x},\; B = b\,\hat{y
},\; C = c\,\hat{z}$ with $a, b, c > 0$.

**Volume.** The volume of tetrahedron $OABC$ is

$$V = \tfrac{1}{6}\,|\det(A,B,C)| = \tfrac{1}{6}\,a\,b\,c\,|\det(\hat{x},\hat{y}
,\hat{z})|.$$

Since $|\det(\hat{x},\hat{y},\hat{z})|$ is a fixed positive constant, minimizing
 $V$ is equivalent to minimizing the product $a\,b\,c$.

**Constraint.** The point $G$ lies in the plane $ABC$, so $G$ is an affine combi
nation of $A, B, C$:

$$G = \alpha\,A + \beta\,B + \gamma\,C, \qquad \alpha + \beta + \gamma = 1.$$

Since $G$ is in the interior of triangle $ABC$ (as $G$ is in the interior of the
 trihedral region), we have $\alpha, \beta, \gamma > 0$. Comparing coefficients
in the basis $\{\hat{x},\hat{y},\hat{z}\}$:

$$g_1 = \alpha\, a, \quad g_2 = \beta\, b, \quad g_3 = \gamma\, c.$$

The constraint $\alpha+\beta+\gamma=1$ becomes:

$$\frac{g_1}{a} + \frac{g_2}{b} + \frac{g_3}{c} = 1. \tag{$\star$}$$

**Minimization via AM–GM.** By the AM–GM inequality applied to the three positiv
e terms in $(\star)$:

$$1 = \frac{g_1}{a} + \frac{g_2}{b} + \frac{g_3}{c} \;\geq\; 3\sqrt[3]{\frac{g_1
\, g_2\, g_3}{a\, b\, c}}.$$

Rearranging:

$$a\,b\,c \;\geq\; 27\,g_1\,g_2\,g_3.$$

Equality holds if and only if

$$\frac{g_1}{a} = \frac{g_2}{b} = \frac{g_3}{c} = \frac{1}{3},$$

i.e., $a = 3g_1,\; b = 3g_2,\; c = 3g_3$.

**Construction.** The minimizing plane is obtained by taking

$$A = 3g_1\,\hat{x}, \quad B = 3g_2\,\hat{y}, \quad C = 3g_3\,\hat{z}.$$

Equivalently, $\alpha = \beta = \gamma = \tfrac{1}{3}$, so **$G$ is the centroid
 of triangle $ABC$**. This gives a clean geometric construction:

> Construct $G$ as the centroid of $\triangle ABC$. That is, on each ray mark th
e point at three times the "coordinate" of $G$ along that ray (in the trihedral
basis), and the plane through these three points is the desired one.

**Verification.** With $a=3g_1, b=3g_2, c=3g_3$: the constraint $(\star)$ gives
$\tfrac{1}{3}+\tfrac{1}{3}+\tfrac{1}{3}=1$ ✓, and the product is $abc = 27\,g_1
g_2 g_3$, achieving the lower bound.

**Minimum volume:**

$$V_{\min} = \tfrac{1}{6}\cdot 27\,g_1\,g_2\,g_3\cdot|\det(\hat{x},\hat{y},\hat{
z})| = \tfrac{9}{2}\,g_1\,g_2\,g_3\,|\det(\hat{x},\hat{y},\hat{z})|.$$

**Summary.** Among all planes through $G$ cutting the three rays, the one minimi
zing the volume of $OABC$ is the plane for which $G$ is the centroid of the tria
ngle $ABC$. The minimum volume equals $\tfrac{9}{2}\,g_1 g_2 g_3\,|\det(\hat{x},
\hat{y},\hat{z})|$, where $g_1, g_2, g_3$ are the coordinates of $G$ in the basi
s of the three ray directions.
