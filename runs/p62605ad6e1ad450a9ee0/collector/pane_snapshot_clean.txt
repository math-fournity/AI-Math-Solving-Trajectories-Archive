integers. Then:
$$a+1 = kb, \quad b+1 = mc, \quad c+1 = na$$

**Solving the system:** Substituting $b = mc-1$ and $c = na-1$ into $a = kb-1$:
$$a = k(mc-1)-1 = kmc - k - 1 = km(na-1) - k - 1 = kmn\cdot a - km - k - 1$$
$$a(kmn - 1) = km + k + 1 \implies a = \frac{km+k+1}{kmn-1}$$

By cyclic symmetry: $b = \frac{mn+m+1}{kmn-1}$, $\ c = \frac{nk+n+1}{kmn-1}$.

Let $d = kmn - 1 \geq 1$. We need $d \mid (km+k+1)$, $d \mid (mn+m+1)$, $d \mid
