## Proof

Let $X = \mathbb{C}/\Lambda$ be a complex torus and let $\wp(z)=\wp_\Lambda(z)$
be the Weierstrass $\wp$-function for the lattice $\Lambda$. We prove that every
 elliptic function $F\in\mathbb{C}(X)$ (i.e. every $\Lambda$-periodic meromorphi
c function on $\mathbb{C}$) is a rational function of $\wp$ and $\wp'$.

### Setup and key facts

Recall the following standard facts about $\wp$:

1. $\wp$ is an even elliptic function of order $2$ with a double pole at each la
ttice point and no other poles.
2. $\wp'$ is an odd elliptic function of order $3$.
3. $\wp$ and $\wp'$ are algebraically independent over $\mathbb{C}$, and the fie
ld $\mathbb{C}(\wp,\wp')$ is the full function field of $X$; equivalently the ma
p
$$\varphi:X\to \mathbb{P}^2,\qquad z\mapsto [\wp(z):\wp'(z):1]$$
(with $0\mapsto[0:1:0]$) embeds $X$ as a smooth cubic curve, and $\mathbb{C}(X)=
\mathbb{C}(\wp,\wp')$.

We give the constructive argument showing every elliptic $F$ lies in $\mathbb{C}
(\wp,\wp')$.

### Even/odd decomposition

Write $F(z)=F_e(z)+F_o(z)$ where
$$F_e(z)=\tfrac12(F(z)+F(-z)),\qquad F_o(z)=\tfrac12(F(z)-F(-z)).$$
Since $-\Lambda=\Lambda$, the functions $F_e$ (even) and $F_o$ (odd) are again e
lliptic. It suffices to show every **even** elliptic function is a rational func
tion of $\wp$, because then $F_o/F_e$ is odd, and an odd elliptic function equal
s $\wp'$ times an even elliptic function (since $G$ odd $\Rightarrow G/\wp'$ is
even, as $\wp'$ is odd). Hence
$$F = F_e + F_o = R_1(\wp) + \wp'\,R_2(\wp)$$
for rational functions $R_1,R_2\in\mathbb{C}(T)$, i.e. $F\in\mathbb{C}(\wp,\wp')
$.

### Even elliptic functions are rational in $\wp$

Let $G$ be an even elliptic function. Consider $h(w)=G(z)$ where $w=\wp(z)$. Bec
ause $G$ is even and $\wp(z)=\wp(-z)$, the value $G(z)$ depends only on $w=\wp(z
)$, so $h$ is a well-defined meromorphic function of $w$ on $\mathbb{P}^1$ (the
quotient of $X$ by the involution $z\mapsto -z$ is $\mathbb{P}^1$, with quotient
 map $\wp$).

Concretely: $\wp:\mathbb{C}/\Lambda\to\mathbb{P}^1$ is a degree-$2$ map branched
 over $e_1=\wp(\omega_1/2),e_2=\wp(\omega_2/2),e_3=\wp((\omega_1+\omega_2)/2)$ a
nd $\infty$. The evenness of $G$ means $G$ is constant on the fibers of $\wp$, s
o $G=h\circ\wp$ for a meromorphic $h$ on $\mathbb{P}^1$. Every meromorphic funct
ion on $\mathbb{P}^1$ is rational, so $h\in\mathbb{C}(w)$, giving $G(z)=h(\wp(z)
)$ with $h$ rational.

### Combining

For general $F$: $F_e=h_1(\wp)$ for some $h_1\in\mathbb{C}(w)$, and $F_o/\wp'$ i
s even elliptic, so $F_o/\wp'=h_2(\wp)$ for some $h_2\in\mathbb{C}(w)$. Therefor
e
$$F(z)=h_1(\wp(z))+\wp'(z)\,h_2(\wp(z)).$$

### Conclusion

The general form of any elliptic function $F\in\mathbb{C}(X)$ is

$$\boxed{\,F(z)=R_1\!\big(\wp(z)\big)+\wp'(z)\,R_2\!\big(\wp(z)\big)\,}$$

where $R_1,R_2\in\mathbb{C}(T)$ are rational functions of one variable. Equivale
ntly, $\mathbb{C}(X)=\mathbb{C}(\wp,\wp')$, the function field of $X$ is generat
ed by $\wp$ and $\wp'$, with the single relation $\wp'^2=4\wp^3-g_2\wp-g_3$ (whe
re $g_2,g_3$ are the lattice invariants).
