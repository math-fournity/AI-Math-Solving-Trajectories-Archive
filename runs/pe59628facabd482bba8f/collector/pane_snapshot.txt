## Proof

Let $n$ be the number of independent dives. Each dive succeeds with probability
$p = 0.01$ and fails with probability $1-p = 0.99$, independently.

The probability of locating the artifact **at least once** in $n$ dives is the c
omplement of failing on every single dive:

$$P = 1 - (1-p)^n = 1 - 0.99^n.$$

We require $P \geq 0.95$, i.e.

$$1 - 0.99^n \geq 0.95 \iff 0.99^n \leq 0.05.$$

Taking natural logarithms (and noting $\ln(0.99) < 0$, so the inequality reverse
s):

$$n \geq \frac{\ln(0.05)}{\ln(0.99)}.$$

Computing the values:

$$\ln(0.05) = -2.995732\ldots, \qquad \ln(0.99) = -0.01005034\ldots$$

$$\frac{\ln(0.05)}{\ln(0.99)} = \frac{-2.995732\ldots}{-0.01005034\ldots} = 298.
07\ldots$$

Since $n$ must be an integer and $n \geq 298.07\ldots$, we round up to $n = 299$
.

**Verification.**

- For $n = 298$: $\;298 \cdot \ln(0.99) = -2.99500\ldots$, so $0.99^{298} = e^{-
2.99500} = 0.05003\ldots > 0.05$. Thus $P = 1 - 0.05003 = 0.94997 < 0.95$. **Ins
ufficient.**
- For $n = 299$: $\;299 \cdot \ln(0.99) = -3.00505\ldots$, so $0.99^{299} = e^{-
3.00505} = 0.04955\ldots < 0.05$. Thus $P = 1 - 0.04955 = 0.95045 \geq 0.95$. **
Sufficient.**

Therefore the minimum number of dives required is

$$\boxed{n = 299}.$$
