# Problem

A botanical research station is monitoring a plot of land containing 100 rare succulents. Due to the species' root structures, the distance between the centers of any two succulents in the plot is at least 1 meter.

The researchers intend to install specialized sensors on a subset of these succulents to study cross-pollination. However, to prevent signal interference, any two succulents selected to hold a sensor must be separated by a distance of at least $\sqrt{3}$ meters. The team is instructed to select the largest possible number of succulents that satisfy this interference constraint.

Given all possible spatial configurations of the 100 succulents that satisfy the initial 1-meter rule, what is the minimum possible number of sensors that the researchers will be able to install?
