## Semiclassical Principal Symbol of $h^2\Delta - 1$

$$h\,\partial_{x_j} \;\longleftrightarrow\; i\,\xi_j, \qquad \text{equivalently}

This is the defining relation of the semiclassical calculus: the commutator $[h\

$$-h^2\,\partial_{x_j}^2 \;=\; -(h\,\partial_{x_j})^2.$$

$$(h\,\partial_{x_j})^2 \;\longleftrightarrow\; (i\,\xi_j)^2 \;=\; -\xi_j^2,$$

$$-(h\,\partial_{x_j})^2 \;\longleftrightarrow\; -(-\xi_j^2) \;=\; \xi_j^2.$$

$$h^2\Delta \;=\; -\sum_{j}(h\,\partial_{x_j})^2 \;\longleftrightarrow\; \sum_{j

$$p(x,\xi;h) \;=\; p_0(x,\xi) + h\,p_1(x,\xi) + h^2\,p_2(x,\xi) + \cdots$$

$$p(x,\xi;h) = |\xi|^2 - 1,$$
