# Problem

In the heart of a high-tech semiconductor fabrication plant, a robotic arm is programmed to perform a delicate sequence of 60 consecutive successful welds to complete a single microchip core. Each weld has a 50% chance of being "perfect" and a 50% chance of having a minor structural flaw, determined entirely by random thermal fluctuations. If a single flaw occurs, the sequence is broken, and the robot must start over from the very first weld.

Currently, the robot is in the middle of a run and has just successfully completed 28 perfect welds in a row. Let $x$ be the expected number of additional welding attempts the robot must perform from this specific point in time until it finally achieves a continuous streak of 60 perfect welds. 

Calculate the sum of all distinct prime factors of $x$.
