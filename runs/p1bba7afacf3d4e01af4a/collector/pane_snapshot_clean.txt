Let \((A, m)\) be a local ring (commutative, with unique maximal ideal \(m\)). F
or each integer \(n \geq 1\), the powers \(m^n\) form a descending chain of idea
ls

\[
A \supseteq m \supseteq m^2 \supseteq m^3 \supseteq \cdots
\]

and so the quotient rings \(A/m^n\) form an inverse system: for every \(n \geq k
 \geq 1\) the natural projection

\[
\pi_{n,k} : A/m^n \longrightarrow A/m^k, \qquad a + m^n \longmapsto a + m^k
\]

is a well-defined ring homomorphism (since \(m^n \subseteq m^k\)), and these map
s satisfy the compatibility conditions

\[
\pi_{n,n} = \mathrm{id}_{A/m^n}, \qquad \pi_{n,k} \circ \pi_{\ell,n} = \pi_{\ell
,k} \quad \text{for all } \ell \geq n \geq k.
\]

**Definition.** The **\(m\)-adic completion** of \(A\), denoted \(\widehat{A}\),
 is the inverse limit of this system:

\[
\widehat{A} \;=\; \varprojlim_{n} A/m^n.
\]

Concretely, \(\widehat{A}\) is the subring of the direct product \(\prod_{n \geq
 1} A/m^n\) consisting of those tuples \((a_1, a_2, a_3, \dots)\) with \(a_n \in
 A/m^n\) that are **coherent** with the projection maps, i.e.

\[
\widehat{A} = \Bigl\{ (a_n)_{n \geq 1} \in \prod_{n \geq 1} A/m^n \;\Big|\; \pi_
{n+1,n}(a_{n+1}) = a_n \text{ for all } n \geq 1 \Bigr\}.
\]

Equivalently, an element of \(\widehat{A}\) is a sequence of cosets \(a_n \in A/
m^n\) such that \(a_{n+1} \equiv a_n \pmod{m^n}\) for every \(n\). Such a sequen
ce is precisely a **Cauchy sequence in the \(m\)-adic topology**, modulo the equ
ivalence relation identifying two sequences that agree in every \(A/m^n\). Thus
\(\widehat{A}\) is the completion of \(A\) with respect to the \(m\)-adic topolo
gy.

There is a natural ring homomorphism

\[
\varphi : A \longrightarrow \widehat{A}, \qquad a \longmapsto (a + m,\; a + m^2,
\; a + m^3,\; \dots),
\]

whose kernel is \(\bigcap_{n \geq 1} m^n\). When \(A\) is Noetherian, by Krull's
 intersection theorem \(\bigcap_{n} m^n = 0\), so \(\varphi\) is injective and \
(A\) embeds into its completion. The ring \(\widehat{A}\) is itself a complete l
ocal ring with maximal ideal \(\widehat{m} = \varprojlim_n m/m^n = m\widehat{A}\
), and the residue field of \(\widehat{A}\) is \(A/m\), the same as that of \(A\
).
