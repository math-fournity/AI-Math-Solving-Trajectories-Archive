## Solution

**Setup.** The joint PDF is $f_{X,Y}(x,y)=\frac{2e^{-2x}}{x}$ on $0\le y\le x$,
$x\ge 0$. First verify normalization:
$$\int_0^\infty\!\int_0^x \frac{2e^{-2x}}{x}\,dy\,dx=\int_0^\infty \frac{2e^{-2x
}}{x}\cdot x\,dx=\int_0^\infty 2e^{-2x}dx=1.\ \checkmark$$

**Key observation.** With $U=Y/X$ and $V=XY$,
$$UV=\frac{Y}{X}\cdot XY=Y^2.$$
So $\operatorname{Cov}(U,V)=E[UV]-E[U]E[V]=E[Y^2]-E[Y/X]\,E[XY]$.

**Compute $E[Y^2]$:**
$$E[Y^2]=\int_0^\infty\!\int_0^x y^2\frac{2e^{-2x}}{x}\,dy\,dx=\int_0^\infty \fr
ac{2e^{-2x}}{x}\cdot\frac{x^3}{3}\,dx=\frac{2}{3}\int_0^\infty x^2 e^{-2x}dx.$$
Using $\int_0^\infty x^n e^{-ax}dx=\frac{n!}{a^{n+1}}$ with $n=2,a=2$: $\int_0^\
infty x^2 e^{-2x}dx=\frac{2}{8}=\frac14$.
$$E[Y^2]=\frac{2}{3}\cdot\frac14=\frac16.$$

**Compute $E[U]=E[Y/X]$:**
$$E\!\left[\frac{Y}{X}\right]=\int_0^\infty\!\int_0^x \frac{y}{x}\cdot\frac{2e^{
-2x}}{x}\,dy\,dx=\int_0^\infty \frac{2e^{-2x}}{x^2}\cdot\frac{x^2}{2}\,dx=\int_0
^\infty e^{-2x}dx=\frac12.$$

**Compute $E[V]=E[XY]$:**
$$E[XY]=\int_0^\infty\!\int_0^x xy\cdot\frac{2e^{-2x}}{x}\,dy\,dx=\int_0^\infty
2e^{-2x}\cdot\frac{x^2}{2}\,dx=\int_0^\infty x^2 e^{-2x}dx=\frac14.$$

**Combine:**
$$\operatorname{Cov}(U,V)=E[Y^2]-E[U]E[V]=\frac16-\frac12\cdot\frac14=\frac16-\f
rac18=\frac{4-3}{24}=\frac{1}{24}.$$

$$\boxed{\operatorname{Cov}(U,V)=\dfrac{1}{24}\approx 0.04167}$$
