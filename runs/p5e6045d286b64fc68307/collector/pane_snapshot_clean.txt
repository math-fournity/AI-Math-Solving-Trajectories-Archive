## Solution

**Step 1: Determine $q \bmod 3$.**

We have $q = 2^{2022} - 53$. Since $2 \equiv -1 \pmod{3}$:
$$2^{2022} \equiv (-1)^{2022} = 1 \pmod{3}.$$
Since $53 = 17\cdot 3 + 2$, we have $53 \equiv 2 \pmod{3}$. Therefore:
$$q \equiv 1 - 2 \equiv -1 \equiv 2 \pmod{3}.$$

**Step 2: The curve is non-singular over $\mathbb{F}_q$.**

The curve $y^2 = x^3 + 1$ has discriminant $\Delta = -27 \neq 0$ in $\mathbb{F}_
q$ since $q = 2^{2022}-53 > 3$ (in particular $q \neq 3$), so $E$ is an elliptic
 curve.

**Step 3: The cubing map is a bijection on $\mathbb{F}_q$.**

Since $q \equiv 2 \pmod{3}$, we have $q - 1 \equiv 1 \pmod{3}$, so $\gcd(3, q-1)
 = 1$. The map $\varphi: \mathbb{F}_q \to \mathbb{F}_q$ given by $\varphi(x) = x
^3$ is a group homomorphism on $\mathbb{F}_q^*$ with kernel of size $\gcd(3, q-1
) = 1$, hence injective, hence bijective on $\mathbb{F}_q^*$. Including $0 \maps
to 0$, the cubing map is a bijection on all of $\mathbb{F}_q$.

**Step 4: Count the points.**

For each $y \in \mathbb{F}_q$, the equation $x^3 = y^2 - 1$ has **exactly one**
solution $x \in \mathbb{F}_q$ (by bijectivity of the cubing map). This gives exa
ctly $q$ affine points $(x, y) \in \mathbb{F}_q^2$ satisfying $y^2 = x^3 + 1$.

Adding the unique point at infinity $\mathcal{O}$, the total number of points is
:
$$\#E(\mathbb{F}_q) = q + 1 = (2^{2022} - 53) + 1 = 2^{2022} - 52.$$

stein integers $\mathbb{Z}[\omega]$. For primes $p \equiv 2 \pmod{3}$, this curv

**Answer:** The number of points on $E$ over $\mathbb{F}_q$ is $\boxed{2^{2022}
