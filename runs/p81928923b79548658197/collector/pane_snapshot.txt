Looking at this problem, I need to find the minimum number of LANs (each holding
 ≤ 50 nodes) so that every pair of 100 nodes shares at least one LAN.

## Lower Bound: At least 6 LANs

**Step 1.** Fix any node $v$. It must share a LAN with each of the other 99 node
s. A single LAN containing $v$ holds at most 50 nodes total, so it connects $v$
to at most 49 others. Therefore $v$ must belong to at least $\lceil 99/49 \rceil
 = 3$ LANs.

**Step 2.** This holds for *every* node, so each of the 100 nodes belongs to at
least 3 LANs. The total number of (node, LAN) incidences is at least $100 \times
 3 = 300$.

**Step 3.** Each LAN contributes at most 50 incidences. If there are $m$ LANs, t
hen $50m \ge 300$, giving $m \ge 6$.

## Construction: 6 LANs suffice

Label the 6 LANs $\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}$. Partition the 100 nodes into 10 groups of 10
. Assign each group a 3-element subset of $\{1,\dots,6\}$ (the LANs it joins):

| Group | LANs | Group | LANs |
|-------|------|-------|------|
| 1 | {4,5,6} | 6 | {2,4,6} |
| 2 | {3,5,6} | 7 | {1,3,6} |
| 3 | {1,2,5} | 8 | {1,4,5} |
| 4 | {1,2,6} | 9 | {2,3,5} |
| 5 | {1,3,4} | 10 | {2,3,4} |

**These 10 subsets are obtained by selecting exactly one from each of the 10 com
plementary pairs** of 3-subsets of $\{1,\dots,6\}$ (there are $\binom{6}{3}/2 =
10$ such pairs). The selection is *balanced*: each element $1,\dots,6$ appears i
n exactly 5 of the 10 chosen subsets.

**Capacity check.** Each LAN $k$ is used by exactly 5 groups × 10 nodes = 50 nod
es. ✓

**Connectivity check.** Two 3-subsets of a 6-element set are disjoint *if and on
ly if* they are complements. Since we select at most one member from each comple
mentary pair, no two chosen subsets are disjoint — every pair of distinct subset
s intersects. Two nodes in the *same* group share all 3 LANs. Two nodes in *diff
erent* groups share at least one LAN (their subsets intersect). ✓

**Node count.** 10 groups × 10 nodes = 100 nodes. ✓

The lower bound proves $m \ge 6$, and the explicit construction achieves $m = 6$
. Therefore the minimum number of LANs is:
