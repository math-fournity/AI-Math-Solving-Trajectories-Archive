## Proof

**Setup.** Let $X_k \sim \mathrm{Exp}(\lambda_k)$ with rate $\lambda_k = \sqrt{k
}$, so the density is $f_{X_k}(x)=\sqrt{k}\,e^{-\sqrt{k}\,x}\mathbf{1}_{x\ge 0}$
, and $E[X_k]=1/\sqrt{k}$.

---

**Step 1 — Convergence of $X_k$ via characteristic functions.**

The characteristic function of $X_k$ is
$$
\varphi_{X_k}(t)=\frac{\lambda_k}{\lambda_k-it}=\frac{\sqrt{k}}{\sqrt{k}-it}=\fr
ac{1}{1-it/\sqrt{k}}.
$$
For every fixed $t\in\mathbb{R}$,
$$
\varphi_{X_k}(t)=\frac{1}{1-it/\sqrt{k}}\xrightarrow[k\to\infty]{}1,
$$
and $1$ is the characteristic function of the degenerate distribution $\delta_0$
 (point mass at $0$). By Lévy's continuity theorem,
$$
X_k \xrightarrow{d} 0 \quad (k\to\infty).
$$

---

**Step 2 — Convergence of $(X_k+1)^2$.**

The map $g(x)=(x+1)^2$ is continuous on $\mathbb{R}$. By the continuous mapping
theorem,
$$
(X_k+1)^2 = g(X_k) \xrightarrow{d} g(0) = 1.
$$
Hence $(X_k+1)^2$ converges in distribution to the **degenerate distribution $\d
elta_1$** (point mass at $1$).

(Equivalently, convergence in distribution to a constant implies convergence in
probability, so $(X_k+1)^2\to 1$ in probability as well.)

---

**Step 3 — The limit $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}P\!\big((X_n+1)^2\le 2\big)$
.**

Since $X_n\ge 0$,
$$
(X_n+1)^2\le 2 \iff 0\le X_n+1\le\sqrt{2}\iff X_n\le\sqrt{2}-1.
$$
Therefore
$$
P\!\big((X_n+1)^2\le 2\big)=P\!\big(X_n\le\sqrt{2}-1\big)=1-e^{-\sqrt{n}\,(\sqrt
{2}-1)}.
$$
Since $\sqrt{2}-1>0$ and $\sqrt{n}\to\infty$,
$$
e^{-\sqrt{n}\,(\sqrt{2}-1)}\xrightarrow[n\to\infty]{}0,
$$
so
$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}P\!\big((X_n+1)^2\le 2\big)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\bigl(1-e^{-\sqrt{
n}\,(\sqrt{2}-1)}\bigr)=1.
$$

This is consistent with the distributional limit: $(X_n+1)^2\to\delta_1$, and $2
$ is a continuity point of $\delta_1$ (the only discontinuity point being $1$),
so $P((X_n+1)^2\le 2)\to P(\delta_1\le 2)=1$.

---

**Conclusion.** $(X_k+1)^2$ converges in distribution to the degenerate distribu
tion $\delta_1$ (point mass at $1$), and
$$
\boxed{\lim_{n\to\infty}P\!\big((X_n+1)^2\le 2\big)=1.}
$$

### PROOF COMPLETE
