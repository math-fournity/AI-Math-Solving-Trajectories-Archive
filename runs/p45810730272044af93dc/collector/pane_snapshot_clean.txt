## Solution

We evaluate the series
$$S = \sum_{n=2}^\infty (-1)^n \left(\sum_{k=1}^\infty \frac{1}{k^n} - 1\right).
$$

**Step 1: Rewrite the inner sum.** For $n \geq 2$, $\sum_{k=1}^\infty \frac{1}{k
^n} = \zeta(n)$, so
$$\zeta(n) - 1 = \sum_{k=2}^\infty \frac{1}{k^n}.$$
Thus
$$S = \sum_{n=2}^\infty (-1)^n \sum_{k=2}^\infty \frac{1}{k^n} = \sum_{n=2}^\inf
ty \sum_{k=2}^\infty \frac{(-1)^n}{k^n}.$$

**Step 2: Justify swapping the order of summation.** We check absolute convergen
ce:
$$\sum_{n=2}^\infty \sum_{k=2}^\infty \frac{1}{k^n} = \sum_{k=2}^\infty \sum_{n=
2}^\infty \frac{1}{k^n} = \sum_{k=2}^\infty \frac{1/k^2}{1 - 1/k} = \sum_{k=2}^\
infty \frac{1}{k(k-1)}.$$
This telescopes:
$$\sum_{k=2}^\infty \frac{1}{k(k-1)} = \sum_{k=2}^\infty \left(\frac{1}{k-1} - \
frac{1}{k}\right) = 1 < \infty.$$
Since the double series converges absolutely, we may swap the order of summation
.

**Step 3: Evaluate the swapped series.**
$$S = \sum_{k=2}^\infty \sum_{n=2}^\infty \frac{(-1)^n}{k^n} = \sum_{k=2}^\infty
 \sum_{n=2}^\infty \left(\frac{-1}{k}\right)^n.$$
For each fixed $k \geq 2$, this is a geometric series with ratio $r = -1/k$ (and
 $|r| = 1/k < 1$):
$$\sum_{n=2}^\infty \left(\frac{-1}{k}\right)^n = \frac{(-1/k)^2}{1 - (-1/k)} =
\frac{1/k^2}{1 + 1/k} = \frac{1/k^2}{(k+1)/k} = \frac{1}{k(k+1)}.$$

**Step 4: Sum the resulting telescoping series.**
$$S = \sum_{k=2}^\infty \frac{1}{k(k+1)} = \sum_{k=2}^\infty \left(\frac{1}{k} -
 \frac{1}{k+1}\right).$$
The partial sums telescope:
$$\sum_{k=2}^{N} \left(\frac{1}{k} - \frac{1}{k+1}\right) = \frac{1}{2} - \frac{
1}{N+1} \xrightarrow{N \to \infty} \frac{1}{2}.$$

**Conclusion.**
$$\boxed{\sum_{n=2}^\infty (-1)^n \left(\sum_{k=1}^\infty \frac{1}{k^n} - 1\righ
t) = \frac{1}{2}}.$$
