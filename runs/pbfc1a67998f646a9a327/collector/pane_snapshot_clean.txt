## Proof

trees on $[n]$: the root is the largest label, every non-leaf vertex $v$ has chi

$$D(x)=\sum_{k\ge 0} D_k(x).$$

**Decomposition by root degree.** If the root has exactly $k$ children, those $k
$ subtrees sit on a partition of the remaining $n-1$ labels into $k$ nonempty bl
ocks, and each block independently carries a decreasing non-plane tree. Since th
e children are **unordered**, the collection of $k$ subtrees is a SET of $k$ com
ponents, whose EGF is $D(x)^k/k!$ (the $1/k!$ removes the ordering). Attaching t
he root — a single labeled atom — corresponds to integration in the EGF world, s

$$D_k(x)=\int_0^x \frac{D(t)^k}{k!}\,dt, \qquad\text{hence}\qquad D_k'(x)=\frac{
D(x)^k}{k!}.$$

This is the relation stated in the problem (the index $k$ on the right-hand side

**Summing over all root degrees.** Using $D(x)=\sum_{k\ge 0}D_k(x)$ and differen

$$D'(x)=\sum_{k\ge 0}D_k'(x)=\sum_{k\ge 0}\frac{D(x)^k}{k!}=e^{D(x)}, \qquad D(0

**Solving the ODE.** Separate variables: $e^{-D}\,dD = dx$. Integrating, $-e^{-D

$$\boxed{\,D(x)=-\ln(1-x)\,}.$$

$$D(x)=-\ln(1-x)=\sum_{n\ge 1}\frac{x^n}{n}.$$

$$\frac{d_n}{n!}=\frac{1}{n}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \boxed{\,d_n=(n-1)!\,}\qqu
