The direct logistics route from bay B to the charging station R intersects the path AC at a designated checkpoint, Q. In this layout, a circular security perimeter is established passing through A, R, and C. This perimeter crosses the primary transit line AB at a secondary gate, P (where P is distinct from A).

- The distance between bay A and gate P is 1 unit.
- The distance between gate P and bay B is 5 units.
- The distance between bay A and checkpoint Q is 2 units.

Based on this configuration, calculate the exact length of the direct path AR.
