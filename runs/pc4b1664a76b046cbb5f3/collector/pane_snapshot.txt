## Proof

**Setup and structure of $f$.** Define $f(x) = \sum_{i=1}^{50}|x-a_i| - \sum_{i=
1}^{50}|x-b_i|$. This is a continuous piecewise-linear function with breakpoints
 at the 100 distinct beacon positions $p_1 < p_2 < \cdots < p_{100}$. On each op
en interval between consecutive breakpoints, $f$ is linear.

**The slope of $f$.** At any non-breakpoint $x$, the derivative of $\sum|x-a_i|$
 is $\#\{i : a_i < x\} - \#\{i : a_i > x\} = 2\#\{a_i < x\} - 50$. Therefore the
 slope of $f$ is:
$$f'(x) = 2\bigl(\#\{a_i < x\} - \#\{b_i < x\}\bigr) = 2\,g(x),$$
where $g(x) = \#\{a_i < x\} - \#\{b_i < x\}$. The function $g$ is a step functio
n that starts at $g=0$ (for $x < p_1$), ends at $g=0$ (for $x > p_{100}$), and c
hanges by $+1$ at each $a$-breakpoint and $-1$ at each $b$-breakpoint. There are
 exactly 50 up-steps and 50 down-steps.

**Boundary values.** As $x \to -\infty$: $f(x) = (\sum a_i - 50x) - (\sum b_i -
50x) = S$ where $S = \sum a_i - \sum b_i$. As $x \to +\infty$: $f(x) = -S$. The
finiteness condition requires $S \neq 0$ (otherwise $f \equiv 0$ on a ray, givin
g infinitely many zeros) and that no flat interval of $f$ (where $g=0$) has valu
e $0$.

**Zeros via direction changes.** The local extrema of $f$ occur exactly where $g
$ changes sign (since the slope $2g$ changes sign there). Let $c$ denote the num
ber of sign changes of $g$. Then $f$ has $c$ local extrema, dividing it into $c+
1$ maximal monotone segments. Each monotone segment contains at most one zero, s
o $N \leq c+1$.

**Parity constraint.** The extremum values $v_1, \ldots, v_c$ (all nonzero by th

 sequence. The number of zeros equals the number of sign changes in $(v_0, v_1,
\ldots, v_{c+1})$. Since $v_0$ and $v_{c+1}$ have opposite signs ($S$ and $-S$),
 full alternation (all $c+1$ pairs changing sign) requires $(-1)^{c+1} = -1$, i.
e., $c$ even. Thus:
- If $c$ is even: $N \leq c+1$.
- If $c$ is odd: $N \leq c$.

**Maximum sign changes of $g$.** Each sign change of $g$ requires $g$ to pass th
rough $0$. Between consecutive passages through $0$, $g$ is entirely positive or
 entirely negative, constituting an "excursion" that uses at least 2 steps (one
up, one down). With 100 total steps, there are at most 50 excursions. Alternatin
g their signs yields at most $50 - 1 = 49$ sign changes. So $c \leq 49$.

Since $49$ is odd, we get $N \leq 49$.
