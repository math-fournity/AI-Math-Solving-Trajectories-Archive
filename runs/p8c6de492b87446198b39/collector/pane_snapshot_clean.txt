## Solution

We solve the first mixed problem for the inhomogeneous wave equation on $(0,\pi)
$ with homogeneous Dirichlet boundary conditions and zero initial data.

**Answer-leak check.** The problem statement contains no solution, proof sketch,
 or formalization notes. Proceeding.

---

### Eigenfunction expansion

The operator $-\partial_{xx}$ on $(0,\pi)$ with Dirichlet boundary conditions ha
s eigenfunctions $\varphi_n(x)=\sin(nx)$, $n=1,2,\dots$, with eigenvalues $\lamb
da_n=n^2$. We seek a solution of the form

$$
u(x,t)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} T_n(t)\sin(nx).
$$

Substituting into $u_{tt}-u_{xx}=f(x)$ gives, for each $n$,

$$
T_n''(t)+n^2 T_n(t)=f_n,\qquad T_n(0)=0,\quad T_n'(0)=0,
$$

where $f_n$ are the sine-coefficients of the forcing $f(x)=4\sin^3 x$.

### Expanding the forcing

Using the identity $\sin^3 x = \tfrac{3\sin x - \sin 3x}{4}$,

$$
4\sin^3 x = 3\sin x - \sin 3x.
$$

Hence $f_1 = 3$, $f_3 = -1$, and $f_n = 0$ for $n\neq 1,3$. Only the modes $n=1$
 and $n=3$ are excited.

### Solving the ODEs

**Mode $n=1$:** $T_1'' + T_1 = 3$. A constant particular solution is $T_1^{(p)}=
3$. The general solution is $T_1 = 3 + A\cos t + B\sin t$. From $T_1(0)=0$: $3+A
=0\Rightarrow A=-3$. From $T_1'(0)=0$: $B=0$. Thus

$$
T_1(t)=3(1-\cos t).
$$

**Mode $n=3$:** $T_3'' + 9T_3 = -1$. A constant particular solution is $T_3^{(p)
}=-\tfrac19$. The general solution is $T_3 = -\tfrac19 + A\cos 3t + B\sin 3t$. F
rom $T_3(0)=0$: $-\tfrac19+A=0\Rightarrow A=\tfrac19$. From $T_3'(0)=0$: $B=0$.
Thus

$$
T_3(t)=\frac{1}{9}(\cos 3t - 1)=-\frac{1}{9}(1-\cos 3t).
$$

All other $T_n\equiv 0$.

### The solution

$$
\boxed{\,u(x,t)=3(1-\cos t)\sin x - \frac{1}{9}(1-\cos 3t)\sin 3x\,}
$$

### Verification

**PDE.** Compute
$$
u_{tt}=3\cos t\,\sin x - \cos 3t\,\sin 3x,
$$
$$
u_{xx}=-3(1-\cos t)\sin x + (1-\cos 3t)\sin 3x.
$$
Therefore
$$
u_{tt}-u_{xx}=\bigl[3\cos t+3(1-\cos t)\bigr]\sin x+\bigl[-\cos 3t-(1-\cos 3t)\b
igr]\sin 3x=3\sin x-\sin 3x=4\sin^3 x.\ \checkmark
$$

**Initial data.** $u(x,0)=3(1-1)\sin x-\tfrac19(1-1)\sin 3x=0$. $\checkmark$

$u_t=3\sin t\,\sin x-\tfrac13\sin 3t\,\sin 3x$, so $u_t(x,0)=0$. $\checkmark$

**Boundary conditions.** $u(0,t)=0$ and $u(\pi,t)=0$ since $\sin 0=\sin\pi=\sin
3\pi=0$. $\checkmark$

All conditions of the mixed problem are satisfied.
