## Answer

**FALSE.**

### Proof

We exhibit a counterexample. Let $G = F_2$, the free group on two generators $a,
 b$, equipped with the **discrete topology**.

**$G$ is locally compact.** In a discrete group, every singleton $\{x\}$ is a co
mpact (finite) open neighborhood of $x$. Hence $G$ is locally compact.

**$G$ is compactly generated.** In a discrete group, compact sets are exactly fi
nite sets, and "compactly generated" coincides with "finitely generated." Since
$F_2 = \langle a, b \rangle$, the finite (hence compact) set $\{a, b\}$ generate
s $G$.

**A closed subgroup that is not compactly generated.** Consider the commutator s
ubgroup
$$H = [F_2, F_2] = \ker\!\big(F_2 \xrightarrow{\;\mathrm{ab}\;} \mathbb{Z}^2\big
).$$
Since $G$ is discrete, **every** subgroup is closed; in particular $H$ is closed
 in $G$.

We claim $H$ is not finitely generated (hence not compactly generated). By the N
ielsen–Schreier theorem, every subgroup of a free group is free, so $H$ is free.
 The abelianization map $\mathrm{ab}\colon F_2 \to \mathbb{Z}^2$ is surjective w
ith kernel $H$, so $H$ has **infinite index** in $F_2$. A standard consequence o
f the Nielsen–Schreier rank formula (or Schreier index formula) is:

> If $F$ is a free group of finite rank $r \geq 2$ and $K \leq F$ is a subgroup
of infinite index, then $K$ is free of **countably infinite rank** (provided $K
\neq \{e\}$).

Since $H = [F_2,F_2]$ is nontrivial (it contains, e.g., $[a,b] = aba^{-1}b^{-1}
\neq e$) and has infinite index, $H$ is free of countably infinite rank. A free
group of infinite rank is **not** finitely generated, and therefore—being a disc
rete group—**not** compactly generated.

### Conclusion

We have produced a locally compact, compactly generated group $G = F_2$ (discret
e) and a closed subgroup $H = [F_2, F_2]$ that is **not** compactly generated. H
ence it is **not** true that every closed subgroup of a locally compact, compact
ly generated group is compactly generated.
