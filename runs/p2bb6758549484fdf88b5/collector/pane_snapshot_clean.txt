## Proof

**Definition.** The dual cone of $K \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{n+1}$ is
$$K^* = \{(y, s) \in \mathbb{R}^{n+1} \mid \langle (y,s), (x,t) \rangle \geq 0 \
text{ for all } (x,t) \in K\},$$
where we write a generic vector in $\mathbb{R}^{n+1}$ as $(x,t)$ with $x \in \ma
thbb{R}^n$, $t \in \mathbb{R}$.

**Claim.** $K^* = \{(y,s) \in \mathbb{R}^{n+1} \mid \|y\| \leq s\} = K$. (The no
rm cone is self-dual.)

---

**Step 1: Reduction of the dual condition.**

A point $(y,s)$ belongs to $K^*$ iff
$$y^\top x + s\,t \geq 0 \quad \text{for all } (x,t) \text{ with } \|x\| \leq t.
$$
Since $\|x\| \geq 0$, the constraint $\|x\|\leq t$ forces $t \geq 0$. Fix any $t
 \geq 0$. The admissible $x$ range over the ball $\{x : \|x\| \leq t\}$. Over th
is ball, the minimum of the linear function $x \mapsto y^\top x$ is
$$\min_{\|x\|\leq t} y^\top x = -\|y\|\,t,$$
achieved at $x = -t\,y/\|y\|$ when $y \neq 0$ (and equal to $0$ when $y=0$, whic
h is consistent with the formula). Therefore
$$\min_{\|x\|\leq t} \bigl(y^\top x + s\,t\bigr) = t\,(s - \|y\|).$$

Thus $(y,s) \in K^*$ iff $t(s - \|y\|) \geq 0$ for every $t \geq 0$.

**Step 2: Deriving the constraint on $(y,s)$.**

- At $t = 0$: the only admissible $x$ is $x = 0$, giving $y^\top x + s\,t = 0 \g
eq 0$ automatically — no constraint.
- For $t > 0$: the inequality $t(s - \|y\|) \geq 0$ reduces to $s \geq \|y\|$.

Hence
$$(y,s) \in K^* \iff s \geq \|y\| \iff \|y\| \leq s.$$

**Step 3: Conclusion.**

$$K^* = \{(y,s) \in \mathbb{R}^{n+1} \mid \|y\| \leq s\} = K.$$

The second-order (norm/Lorentz) cone is **self-dual**. $\blacksquare$
