## Proof

Since $G$ acts smoothly, properly, and freely on $M$, the **Quotient Manifold Th
eorem** applies: $M/G$ is a smooth manifold and $\pi:M\to M/G$ is a smooth subme
rsion, in fact a principal $G$-bundle. We show that $S=\bigcup_{p\in M}T_p(G\cdo
t p)$ is a smooth sub vector bundle of $TM\to M$ of rank $\dim G$ (the *vertical
 bundle* of this principal bundle).

---

**Step 1 — Constant fiber dimension.** For each $p\in M$ let $\varphi_p:G\to M$,
 $\varphi_p(g)=g\cdot p$. Freeness gives $\operatorname{Stab}(p)=\{e\}$, so $\va
rphi_p$ is injective. Properness + freeness make $\varphi_p$ a proper injective
immersion, hence an embedding; its image $G\cdot p$ is an embedded submanifold d
iffeomorphic to $G$. Thus
$$\dim T_p(G\cdot p)=\dim G\quad\text{for all }p\in M,$$
so the fiber dimension of $S$ is the constant $k:=\dim G$.

---

**Step 2 — A smooth bundle morphism of constant rank.** Let $\mathfrak g=\operat
orname{Lie}(G)$. Define the infinitesimal action
$$\Phi:M\times\mathfrak g\longrightarrow TM,\qquad \Phi(p,X)=X^\#_p:=\left.\frac
{d}{dt}\right|_{t=0}\exp(tX)\cdot p\in T_pM.$$
This is smooth: it is the velocity at $t=0$ of the smooth map $(t,p,X)\mapsto \e
xp(tX)\cdot p$, and differentiating a smooth family of curves yields a smooth ma
p into $TM$. It covers $\operatorname{Id}_M$ and is linear on each fiber $\{p\}\
times\mathfrak g$, so it is a vector bundle morphism from the trivial rank-$k$ b
undle $M\times\mathfrak g$ to $TM$.

---

**Step 3 — Fiberwise injectivity (hence isomorphism onto $S_p$).** Fix $p$. If $
X^\#_p=0$, the curve $\gamma(t)=\exp(tX)\cdot p$ has $\gamma'(0)=0$; in fact $\g
amma$ is constant, so $\exp(tX)\in\operatorname{Stab}(p)=\{e\}$ for all $t$ (fre
eness), hence $X=0$. Therefore $X\mapsto X^\#_p$ is injective. Since $\dim\mathf
rak g=\dim G=\dim(G\cdot p)=\dim S_p$, it is a linear isomorphism
$$\mathfrak g\;\xrightarrow{\;\sim\;}\;S_p=T_p(G\cdot p).$$
So $\Phi$ has constant rank $k$ everywhere, and $\operatorname{im}\Phi=S$.

---

**Step 4 — Image of a constant-rank bundle morphism is a subbundle.** This is th
e standard constant-rank image theorem. For completeness, here is the local argu
ment. Trivialize $TM$ over an open set $U\subset M$ with a smooth frame $(E_1,\d
ots,E_n)$. Over $U$, $\Phi$ is represented by a smooth matrix-valued function $A
:U\to M_{n\times k}(\mathbb R)$ of constant rank $k$. By the constant-rank theor
em (applied pointwise, with bases chosen from the local frame), after shrinking
$U$ if necessary there are smooth frame changes putting $A$ into the block form
$\begin{pmatrix}I_k\\0\end{pmatrix}$. Consequently
$$\operatorname{im}\Phi\big|_U=\operatorname{span}(E'_1,\dots,E'_k)$$
for a smooth local frame $(E'_1,\dots,E'_n)$ of $TM|_U$. This gives a local triv
ialization of $S|_U$ as a rank-$k$ subbundle, and these local trivializations ar
e mutually compatible on overlaps. Hence $S$ carries a canonical smooth structur
e as a rank-$k$ sub vector bundle of $TM\to M$.

---

**Conclusion.** $S$ is a smooth sub vector bundle of $TM\to M$, of rank $\dim G$
; it is precisely the vertical bundle of the principal $G$-bundle $\pi:M\to M/G$
.
