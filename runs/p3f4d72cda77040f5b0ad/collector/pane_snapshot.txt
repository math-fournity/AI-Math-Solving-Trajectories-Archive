## Solution

**Setup.** The parabola $y=x^2$ lies in the $xy$-plane, so its points are $P(t)=
(t,\,t^2,\,0)$ for $t\in\mathbb{R}$. The fixed point is $F=(0,-10,a)$. The line
through $P(t)$ and $F$ is
$$L(s)=(1-s)(t,t^2,0)+s(0,-10,a)=\big((1-s)t,\;(1-s)t^2-10s,\;sa\big).$$

**Intersection with the $xz$-plane ($y=0$).** Setting the $y$-coordinate to zero
:
$$(1-s)t^2-10s=0\;\Longrightarrow\; t^2=s(t^2+10)\;\Longrightarrow\; s=\frac{t^2
}{t^2+10}.$$

Substituting back:
$$x=(1-s)t=\frac{10t}{t^2+10},\qquad z=sa=\frac{a\,t^2}{t^2+10}.$$

**Implicit equation.** Let $w=\dfrac{t^2}{t^2+10}=\dfrac{z}{a}$. Then
$$x^2=\frac{100\,t^2}{(t^2+10)^2}=100\cdot\frac{t^2}{t^2+10}\cdot\frac{1}{t^2+10
}=10w(1-w).$$
Replacing $w=z/a$:
$$x^2=10\cdot\frac{z}{a}\!\left(1-\frac{z}{a}\right)=\frac{10z}{a}-\frac{10z^2}{
a^2}.$$
Multiplying by $a^2$ and rearranging:
$$10z^2-10az+a^2x^2=0.$$

Completing the square in $z$:
$$10\!\left(z-\frac{a}{2}\right)^2+a^2x^2=\frac{5a^2}{2}.$$

This is an **ellipse** centered at $(0,\,a/2)$ with
- semi-axis in $z$: $\dfrac{a}{2}$,
- semi-axis in $x$: $\sqrt{\dfrac{5}{2}}=\dfrac{\sqrt{10}}{2}$.

**Area.**
$$\text{Area}=\pi\cdot\frac{a}{2}\cdot\frac{\sqrt{10}}{2}=\frac{\pi\,a\sqrt{10}}
{4}.$$

Setting this equal to $\pi$:
$$\frac{a\sqrt{10}}{4}=1\;\Longrightarrow\; a=\frac{4}{\sqrt{10}}=\frac{2\sqrt{1
0}}{5}.$$

**Extracting $p,q,r$.** We have $a=\dfrac{2\sqrt{10}}{5}$, so $p=2$, $q=10$, $r=
5$. Indeed $\gcd(2,5)=1$ and $10=2\cdot 5$ is squarefree. Therefore
$$p+q+r=2+10+5=\boxed{17}.$$
