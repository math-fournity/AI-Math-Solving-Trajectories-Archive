# Minimum Volume Outer Ellipsoid Covering a Union of Ellipsoids

## Setup

We are given ellipsoids $\mathcal{E}_i = \{x \in \mathbb{R}^n : T_i(x) \leq 0\}$
, where $T_i(x) = x^\top A_i x + 2b_i^\top x + c_i$, with $A_i \succ 0$ and each
 $\mathcal{E}_i$ having nonempty interior, i.e., $\rho_i^2 := b_i^\top A_i^{-1}
b_i - c_i > 0$.

We seek the minimum-volume ellipsoid $\mathcal{E}_0$ containing $\bigcup_{i=1}^p
 \mathcal{E}_i$.

## Step 1: Parameterize the Outer Ellipsoid

Write $\mathcal{E}_0$ in center–shape form:
$$\mathcal{E}_0 = \{x \in \mathbb{R}^n : (x - x_0)^\top P^{-1}(x - x_0) \leq 1\}
, \quad P \succ 0.$$

The volume is $\text{vol}(\mathcal{E}_0) = \kappa_n \sqrt{\det P}$, where $\kapp
a_n$ is the volume of the unit ball in $\mathbb{R}^n$. Minimizing volume is equi
valent to **maximizing $\log\det Q$** where $Q := P^{-1} \succ 0$.

## Step 2: Homogeneous (Lifted) Matrix Representation

Define the homogeneous (lifted) vector $\bar{x} = \binom{x}{1} \in \mathbb{R}^{n
+1}$. Each quadratic form $T_j(x) = \bar{x}^\top M_j \bar{x}$ where
$$M_j = \begin{pmatrix} A_j & b_j \\ b_j^\top & c_j \end{pmatrix} \in \mathbb{R}
^{(n+1)\times(n+1)}.$$

For $\mathcal{E}_0$ in center–shape form with $Q = P^{-1}$, $y = Qx_0$, $z = x_0
^\top Q x_0 = y^\top Q^{-1} y$, the homogeneous matrix is:
$$\hat{M}_0 = \begin{pmatrix} Q & -y \\ -y^\top & z - 1 \end{pmatrix}.$$

One can verify: $\bar{x}^\top \hat{M}_0\, \bar{x} = (x - x_0)^\top Q (x - x_0) -
 1$, so $T_0(x) \leq 0 \iff \bar{x}^\top \hat{M}_0\, \bar{x} \leq 0$.

## Step 3: Apply the S-lemma (Yakubovich)

**S-lemma.** Let $f(x), g(x)$ be quadratic functions with $g$ having a strictly
feasible point ($\exists\, \bar{x}: g(\bar{x}) < 0$). Then:
$$g(x) \leq 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; f(x) \leq 0 \quad\iff\quad \exists\, \lambda \
geq 0 : \; M_f \preceq \lambda\, M_g.$$

Since each $\mathcal{E}_i$ has nonempty interior ($\rho_i^2 > 0$), the Slater co
ndition holds. Applying the S-lemma with $f = T_0$ and $g = T_i$:

$$\mathcal{E}_i \subseteq \mathcal{E}_0 \quad\iff\quad \exists\, \lambda_i \geq
0 : \; \hat{M}_0 \preceq \lambda_i\, M_i.$$

This is **necessary and sufficient** (not merely sufficient), so the formulation
 is exact.

## Step 4: Convex Reformulation via Variable Substitution

The variables are $Q$, $y$, $z$, and $\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_p$. The containm
ent constraint $\hat{M}_0 \preceq \lambda_i M_i$ becomes the **linear matrix ine
quality (LMI)**:
$$\lambda_i M_i - \begin{pmatrix} Q & -y \\ -y^\top & z - 1 \end{pmatrix} \succe
q 0, \quad i = 1, \dots, p. \tag{C1}$$

The nonlinear relation $z = y^\top Q^{-1} y$ is replaced by the **Schur compleme
nt LMI**:
$$\begin{pmatrix} Q & y \\ y^\top & z \end{pmatrix} \succeq 0 \quad\iff\quad z \
geq y^\top Q^{-1} y. \tag{C2}$$

**Tightness of the relaxation.** The variable $z$ appears only in (C1) (in the $
(n{+}1,n{+}1)$ entry of $\hat{M}_0$, with a negative sign in $\lambda_i M_i - \h
at{M}_0$) and in (C2). Increasing $z$ makes $\hat{M}_0$ larger in the PSD order,
 which makes constraint (C1) *harder* to satisfy. Since the objective $\log\det
Q$ does not depend on $z$, the optimizer drives $z$ to its minimum feasible valu
e, where (C2) is tight: $z = y^\top Q^{-1} y$. Hence the relaxation is **exact a
t optimality**.

## Step 5: The MAXDET Formulation

The complete problem is:

$$\boxed{\max_{Q,\, y,\, z,\, \lambda_1,\dots,\lambda_p} \;\log\det Q}$$

subject to:
- $Q \succ 0$,
- $\begin{pmatrix} Q & y \\ y^\top & z \end{pmatrix} \succeq 0$,
- $\lambda_i \geq 0, \quad i = 1, \dots, p$,
- $\lambda_i \begin{pmatrix} A_i & b_i \\ b_i^\top & c_i \end{pmatrix} - \begin{
pmatrix} Q & -y \\ -y^\top & z - 1 \end{pmatrix} \succeq 0, \quad i = 1, \dots,
p.$

**Convexity verification:**
- **Objective**: $\log\det Q$ is concave on $Q \succ 0$. Maximizing a concave fu
nction is a convex optimization problem. ✓
- **Constraint (C2)**: LMI, linear in $(Q, y, z)$. ✓
- **Constraint (C1)**: $\lambda_i M_i$ is linear in $\lambda_i$ (since $M_i$ is
constant), and $\hat{M}_0$ is linear in $(Q, y, z)$. The difference is an LMI, l
inear in all variables. ✓
- **Nonnegativity**: $\lambda_i \geq 0$ is linear. ✓

This is a **MAXDET (determinant maximization) problem** — a standard class of co
nvex optimization problems solvable in polynomial time by interior-point methods
 (e.g., via the MAXDET solver, or reformulated as an SDP with an epigraph variab
le for $\log\det Q$).

## Step 7: Feasibility and Optimality

- **Feasibility**: A sufficiently large ellipsoid (e.g., centered at the centroi

E}_i$, so the feasible set is nonempty.
- **Boundedness**: Since $\mathcal{E}_0 \supseteq \mathcal{E}_i$ for each $i$, $

- **Exactness**: The S-lemma provides a necessary and sufficient condition for e
