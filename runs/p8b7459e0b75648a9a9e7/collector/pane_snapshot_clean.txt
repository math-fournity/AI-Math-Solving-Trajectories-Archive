## Proof

**Interpretation of the condition.** The condition $|X \cap Y| < 1$ on two 3-ele
ment subsets means $|X \cap Y| = 0$ would give only the trivial disjoint-packing
 problem. The meaningful competition interpretation (consistent with the denomin
ator $2015 = |S|-1$) is that any two distinct triples share **at most one** elem
ent, i.e. $|X \cap Y| \leq 1$. This is equivalent to requiring that every pair o
f elements of $S$ is contained in at most one triple of $\mathbb{F}$ (a 2-$(n,3,
1)$ packing).

---

**Upper bound.** For each $x \in S$, let $r_x$ denote the number of triples in $
\mathbb{F}$ containing $x$. Each triple containing $x$ pairs $x$ with exactly 2
other elements of $S$; since each pair $\{x,y\}$ can appear in at most one tripl
e, the $2r_x$ "slots" must be filled by distinct partners among the remaining $2
015$ elements. Hence
$$2r_x \leq 2015 \implies r_x \leq \left\lfloor \tfrac{2015}{2}\right\rfloor = 1
007.$$
Summing over all $x \in S$ and using $\sum_x r_x = 3T$:
$$3T \leq 2016 \cdot 1007 \implies T \leq \frac{2016 \cdot 1007}{3} = 676704.$$

---

**Lower bound (construction achieving the bound).** Since $2017 \equiv 1 \pmod{6
}$, by Kirkman's theorem a Steiner triple system $\mathrm{STS}(2017)$ exists on
the point set $\{0,1,2,\dots,2016\}$. It contains
$$\frac{2017 \cdot 2016}{6} = 677712$$
triples, and each point lies in exactly $\frac{2016}{2}=1008$ triples.

Delete point $0$ together with all $1008$ triples through it. The remaining
$$677712 - 1008 = 676704$$
triples are 3-element subsets of $\{1,2,\dots,2016\}$, and since deleting a poin
t from an STS cannot cause any pair to appear twice, they form a valid family $\
mathbb{F}$. Thus
$$T = 676704 = \frac{2016 \cdot 2014}{6}.$$

---

**Final computation.**
$$\frac{3T}{2015} = \frac{3 \cdot 676704}{2015} = \frac{2030112}{2015}.$$
Observe $2030112 = 2016 \cdot 1007 = (2015+1)\cdot 1007 = 2015\cdot 1007 + 1007$
, so
$$\frac{3T}{2015} = 1007 + \frac{1007}{2015}.$$
Since $0 < \frac{1007}{2015} < 1$,
$$\left\lfloor \frac{3T}{2015}\right\rfloor = \boxed{1007}.$$

### PROOF COMPLETE
