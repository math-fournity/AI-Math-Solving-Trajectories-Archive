**Minimum number of rides.** The elevator holds at most 8 people, and there are
11 residents, all going to the same floor. Since $8 < 11 \le 16$, the minimum nu
mber of rides required is **2**. The natural arrangement is: 8 people ride up fi
rst (full capacity), the elevator returns, and the remaining 3 ride up second. T
he 8 people for the first ride are chosen at random from the 11; the other 3 go
on the second ride.

**Computing the probability.** Fix one of the two feuding residents. This person
 lands in the first group (size 8) with probability $\frac{8}{11}$, or in the se
cond group (size 3) with probability $\frac{3}{11}$.

- If the first feuding resident is in the group of 8, the second feuding residen
t is among the remaining 10 people, of whom 7 spots remain in that group. So the
 conditional probability they are together is $\frac{7}{10}$.
- If the first feuding resident is in the group of 3, the second feuding residen
t is among the remaining 10 people, of whom 2 spots remain in that group. So the
 conditional probability they are together is $\frac{2}{10}$.

Therefore:

$$
P = \frac{8}{11}\cdot\frac{7}{10} + \frac{3}{11}\cdot\frac{2}{10} = \frac{56}{11
0} + \frac{6}{110} = \frac{62}{110} = \frac{31}{55}.
$$

**Verification by direct counting.** The total number of ways to choose 8 people
 out of 11 for the first ride is $\binom{11}{8} = 165$.

- Both feuding residents in the first ride (size 8): choose the other 6 from the
 remaining 9, giving $\binom{9}{6} = 84$ ways.
- Both feuding residents in the second ride (size 3): choose 1 more from the rem
aining 9, giving $\binom{9}{1} = 9$ ways.

$$
P = \frac{84 + 9}{165} = \frac{93}{165} = \frac{31}{55}.
$$

Both methods agree.

**The probability that the feuding residents are in the elevator together is $\b
oxed{\dfrac{31}{55}}$.**
