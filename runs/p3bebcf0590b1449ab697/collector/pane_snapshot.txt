## Solution

Let $n = 2015 = 5 \cdot 13 \cdot 31$ (which is **odd** and squarefree).

### Step 1: Evaluate the inner product for fixed $a$

Fix $a$ and let $d = \gcd(a, n)$. As $b$ ranges over $\{1, 2, \dots, n\}$, the v
alue $ab \bmod n$ ranges over the multiples of $d$ in $\{0,1,\dots,n-1\}$, each
attained exactly $d$ times. Setting $m = n/d$, the quantities $e^{2\pi i ab/n}$
are exactly the $m$-th roots of unity, each appearing $d$ times. Therefore

$$\prod_{b=1}^{n}\left(1+e^{2\pi i ab/n}\right)=\prod_{\zeta^m=1}(1+\zeta)^{\,d}
.$$

Using $x^m - 1 = \prod_{\zeta^m=1}(x-\zeta)$ at $x=-1$:

$$(-1)^m - 1 = \prod_{\zeta^m=1}(-1-\zeta)=(-1)^m\prod_{\zeta^m=1}(1+\zeta),$$

so $\prod_{\zeta^m=1}(1+\zeta) = 1-(-1)^m$. Since $n$ is odd, every divisor $d$
is odd, hence $m=n/d$ is **odd**, giving $1-(-1)^m = 2$. Thus

$$\prod_{b=1}^{n}\left(1+e^{2\pi i ab/n}\right)=2^d=2^{\gcd(a,n)}.$$

### Step 2: Combine over all $a$

$$P=\prod_{a=1}^{n}\prod_{b=1}^{n}\left(1+e^{2\pi iab/n}\right)=\prod_{a=1}^{n}2
^{\gcd(a,n)}=2^{\,\sum_{a=1}^{n}\gcd(a,n)}.$$

Hence $\log_2 P = \sum_{a=1}^{n}\gcd(a,n)$.

### Step 3: Compute the GCD sum

Using the standard identity $\sum_{a=1}^{n}\gcd(a,n)=\sum_{d\mid n} d\,\varphi(n
/d)$, which is the Dirichlet convolution $\mathrm{id}*\varphi$ — a **multiplicat
ive** function. For a prime $p$:

$$f(p)=1\cdot\varphi(p)+p\cdot\varphi(1)=(p-1)+p=2p-1.$$

Therefore:

$$f(5)=9,\qquad f(13)=25,\qquad f(31)=61.$$

Since $n=5\cdot 13\cdot 31$ is squarefree:

$$\sum_{a=1}^{2015}\gcd(a,2015)=f(5)\cdot f(13)\cdot f(31)=9\cdot 25\cdot 61=225
\cdot 61=13725.$$

**Verification via direct divisor enumeration:** listing all $8$ divisors $d$ of
 $2015$ and summing $d\cdot\varphi(2015/d)$:

| $d$ | $n/d$ | $\varphi(n/d)$ | $d\cdot\varphi(n/d)$ |
|-----|-------|----------------|----------------------|
| 1 | 2015 | 1440 | 1440 |
| 5 | 403 | 360 | 1800 |
| 13 | 155 | 120 | 1560 |
| 31 | 65 | 48 | 1488 |
| 65 | 31 | 30 | 1950 |
| 155 | 13 | 12 | 1860 |
| 403 | 5 | 4 | 1612 |
| 2015 | 1 | 1 | 2015 |

Total: $1440+1800+1560+1488+1950+1860+1612+2015 = 13725$. ✓

### Result

$$\log_2\!\left(\prod_{a=1}^{2015}\prod_{b=1}^{2015}\left(1+e^{2\pi iab/2015}\ri
ght)\right)=\boxed{13725}.$$
