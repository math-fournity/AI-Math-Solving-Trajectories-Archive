$$f(x) + f(g(x)) = \arctan x \quad (1)$$
$$f(g(x)) + f(g^2(x)) = \arctan(g(x)) \quad (2)$$
$$f(g^2(x)) + f(x) = \arctan(g^2(x)) \quad (3)$$

## Computing the integral

$$I = \int_0^1 f(x)\,dx = \frac{1}{2}\left[A - B + C\right]$$

$$A = \left[x\arctan x - \tfrac{1}{2}\ln(1+x^2)\right]_0^1 = \frac{\pi}{4} - \fr
