## Solution

We seek the adjoint $L^*$ defined by the Lagrange identity

$$\iint_D (L\phi)\,\psi\,dA = \iint_D \phi\,(L^*\psi)\,dA$$

for all smooth $\phi,\psi$ satisfying **Dirichlet** boundary conditions ($\phi=\
psi=0$ on $\partial D$), so that all boundary terms generated by integration by
parts vanish.

Write $L$ as the sum of four parts and adjoint each separately.

$$L = \underbrace{\nabla^2}_{L_1} + \underbrace{a\,\tfrac{\partial}{\partial x}}
_{L_2} + \underbrace{b\,\tfrac{\partial}{\partial y}}_{L_3} + \underbrace{c}_{L_
4}.$$

**Part 1: $L_1 = \nabla^2 = \partial_x^2 + \partial_y^2$.** Green's first identi
ty gives

$$\iint_D (\nabla^2\phi)\,\psi\,dA = \iint_D \phi\,(\nabla^2\psi)\,dA + \oint_{\
partial D}\!\left(\psi\,\frac{\partial\phi}{\partial n} - \phi\,\frac{\partial\p
si}{\partial n}\right)ds.$$

With $\phi|_{\partial D}=0$ and $\psi|_{\partial D}=0$, the boundary integral va
nishes. Hence $L_1^* = \nabla^2$ (the Laplacian is self-adjoint under Dirichlet
conditions).

**Part 2: $L_2 = a\,\partial_x$.** Integrating by parts in $x$ only:

$$\iint_D (a\,\phi_x)\,\psi\,dx\,dy = \int dy\!\left[\Big[a\,\phi\,\psi\Big]_{x_
{\min}}^{x_{\max}} - \int \phi\,\partial_x(a\,\psi)\,dx\right].$$

The boundary term $[a\,\phi\,\psi]$ vanishes because $\phi=0$ on $\partial D$. E
xpanding the product rule in the remaining integral,

$$\iint_D (a\,\phi_x)\,\psi\,dA = -\iint_D \phi\,(a\,\psi_x + a_x\,\psi)\,dA = \
iint_D \phi\,\bigl(-a\,\partial_x - a_x\bigr)\psi\,dA.$$

Therefore $L_2^* = -a\,\partial_x - a_x$.

**Part 3: $L_3 = b\,\partial_y$.** By the identical argument with $y$ in place o
f $x$,

$$L_3^* = -b\,\partial_y - b_y.$$

**Part 4: $L_4 = c$ (multiplication).** Multiplication by a function is self-adj
oint:

$$\iint_D (c\,\phi)\,\psi\,dA = \iint_D \phi\,(c\,\psi)\,dA,$$

so $L_4^* = c$.

**Assembling.** Since the adjoint is linear,

$$L^* = L_1^* + L_2^* + L_3^* + L_4^* = \nabla^2 - a\,\frac{\partial}{\partial x
} - a_x - b\,\frac{\partial}{\partial y} - b_y + c.$$

## Result

The adjoint of $L = \nabla^2 + a\,\dfrac{\partial}{\partial x} + b\,\dfrac{\part
ial}{\partial y} + c$ under Dirichlet boundary conditions is

$$\boxed{\,L^* = \nabla^2 - a\,\frac{\partial}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial a}{\
partial x} - b\,\frac{\partial}{\partial y} - \frac{\partial b}{\partial y} + c\
,}$$

or equivalently, collecting the first-order terms,

$$L^* = \nabla^2 - \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\!\bigl(a\,\cdot\bigr) - \frac{\pa
rtial}{\partial y}\!\bigl(b\,\cdot\bigr) + c,$$

where the dot denotes the function the operator acts upon. The key mechanism is
that each first-order term $a\,\partial_x$ picks up a sign reversal on the deriv
ative together with a compensating $-a_x$ term from the product rule, while the
Laplacian and the zeroth-order term $c$ remain self-adjoint thanks to the vanish
ing of boundary contributions under Dirichlet conditions.
