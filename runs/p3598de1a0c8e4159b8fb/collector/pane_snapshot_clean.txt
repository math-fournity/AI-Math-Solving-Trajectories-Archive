Looking at this problem, I need to find the minimum number of teams in a round-r
obin tournament where one team has the strictly highest score but the strictly f
ewest wins.

## Setup

Let there be $n$ teams. Each team plays $n-1$ matches. Win = 3 pts, Draw = 1 pt,
 Loss = 0 pts.

Let team $T$ have $w$ wins, $d$ draws, $\ell$ losses ($w+d+\ell = n-1$), with sc
ore $S_T = 3w + d$.

**Conditions:**
- $T$ has the **fewest wins**: every other team has $\geq w+1$ wins.
- $T$ has the **highest score**: every other team has score $\leq S_T - 1$.

## Key Inequality

The $d$ teams that drew with $T$ each have $\geq w+1$ wins and $\geq 1$ draw (ag
ainst $T$), so their score is $\geq 3(w+1)+1 = 3w+4$. Since $T$'s score must str
ictly exceed theirs:

$$3w + d > 3w + 4 \implies d \geq 5.$$

Since $d \leq n-1-w$, we get $n \geq w+6$.

## Eliminating $n \leq 5$

$n \geq w+6 \geq 6$, so $n \leq 5$ is impossible.

## Eliminating $n = 6$

Only $w=0$ is possible ($n \geq w+6$ gives $w \leq 0$). Then $d \geq 5$ and $d \
leq 5$, so $d=5$: $T$ has $0$W, $5$D, $0$L, score $5$. All 5 opponents drew with
 $T$, each having $\geq 1$ win + $1$ draw $= 4$ pts. For $T$ to be strictly high
est, each must have exactly $1$W, $1$D, $3$L $= 4$ pts, with $0$ draws among non
-$T$ matches. But the $5$ non-$T$ teams play $\binom{5}{2}=10$ matches, all deci
ded $\Rightarrow 10$ wins, while only $5$ are needed. **Contradiction.**

## Eliminating $n = 7$

**Case $w=0$:** $d \geq 5$.

- $d=6$: All 6 drew with $T$. Each has $\geq 1$ win, score $\geq 4$. $T$'s score
 $= 6$. Among 6 non-$T$ teams ($15$ matches), if total wins $= 6$, then $9$ draw
s are needed, giving each team $3$ draws among non-$T$ $+ 1$ vs $T$ $= 4$ draws,
 score $= 3+4=7 > 6$. **Contradiction.** More generally, any distribution with $
6$ total wins forces enough draws that scores exceed $6$.

- $d=5$: $T$ has $0$W, $5$D, $1$L. Five teams drew with $T$ (each $\geq 1$W $+ 1
$D $= 4$ pts, need $\leq 4$, so exactly $1$W, $1$D, $4$L, $0$ draws among non-$T
$). One team beat $T$. The $5$ teams have $0$ draws among non-$T$ matches; the b
eater's draws would be with them (impossible). So all $15$ non-$T$ matches are d
ecided $\Rightarrow 15$ wins, but only $5 + (\text{beater's non-}T\text{ wins})
\leq 5+0 = 5$ needed. **Contradiction.**

**Case $w=1$:** $d \geq 5$, $d \leq 5$, so $d=5$: $T$ has $1$W, $5$D, $0$L, scor
e $8$. Five teams drew with $T$ (each $\geq 2$ wins $+ 1$ draw $= 7$ pts, need $
\leq 7$, so exactly $2$W, $1$D, $3$L, $0$ draws among non-$T$). One team lost to
 $T$. The $5$ teams have $0$ draws among non-$T$; the loser's draws would be wit
h them (impossible). All $15$ non-$T$ matches decided $\Rightarrow 15$ wins, but
 $5\times 2 + 2 = 12$ needed. **Contradiction.**

**Case $w=2$:** $n \geq 8$, impossible for $n=7$.

So $n=7$ is impossible.

## Constructing $n = 8$

Take $w=2$, $d=5$: $T$ has $2$W, $5$D, $0$L, score $11$. $T$ beats teams $A, B$
and draws with $C, D, E, F, G$.

The $5$ teams that drew with $T$ need $\geq 3$ wins $+ 1$ draw $= 10$ pts, and m
ust have $\leq 10$, so exactly $3$W, $1$D (vs $T$), $3$L, score $10$.

The $2$ teams that lost to $T$ need $\geq 3$ wins, $0$ draws (to keep score $\le
q 9 < 11$), so $3$W, $0$D, $4$L, score $9$.

Among $\{A,B,C,D,E,F,G\}$: $7$ teams, $\binom{7}{2}=21$ matches, all decided, ea
ch team needs exactly $3$ wins and $3$ losses. This is a **regular tournament on
 $7$ vertices**, which exists since $7$ is odd (each vertex has out-degree $(7-1
)/2 = 3$).

**Explicit construction:** Label teams $0,1,\ldots,6$. Team $i$ beats teams $i+1
, i+2, i+3 \pmod{7}$. Each team gets exactly $3$ wins and $3$ losses.

Set $A=0, B=1, C=2, D=3, E=4, F=5, G=6$. $T$ beats $A,B$; draws with $C,D,E,F,G$
.

**Final standings:**

| Team | W | D | L | Score |
|------|---|---|---|-------|
| $T$ | 2 | 5 | 0 | **11** |
| $A$ | 3 | 0 | 4 | 9 |
| $B$ | 3 | 0 | 4 | 9 |
| $C$ | 3 | 1 | 3 | 10 |
| $D$ | 3 | 1 | 3 | 10 |
| $E$ | 3 | 1 | 3 | 10 |
| $F$ | 3 | 1 | 3 | 10 |
| $G$ | 3 | 1 | 3 | 10 |

- $T$ has the highest score: $11 > 10 > 9$. ✓
- $T$ has the fewest wins: $2 < 3$. ✓

## Conclusion

The minimum number of teams is $\boxed{8}$.
