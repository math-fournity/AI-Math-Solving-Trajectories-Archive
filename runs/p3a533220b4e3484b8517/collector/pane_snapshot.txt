## Solution

We seek $G(x,\xi)$ satisfying
$$G''(x) - G(x) = \delta(x-\xi), \qquad G(\pm\infty)=0.$$

### Step 1. Fourier transform

Define
$$\hat G(k)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} G(x)\,e^{-ikx}\,dx,\qquad G(x)=\frac{1}{2\pi
}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\hat G(k)\,e^{ikx}\,dk.$$

Under this convention $G''(x)\mapsto -k^{2}\hat G(k)$ and $\delta(x-\xi)\mapsto
e^{-ik\xi}$. Transforming the ODE:
$$-k^{2}\hat G(k)-\hat G(k)=e^{-ik\xi}\;\;\Longrightarrow\;\;\hat G(k)=-\frac{e^
{-ik\xi}}{k^{2}+1}.$$

### Step 2. Inversion

$$G(x)=-\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{e^{ik(x-\xi)}}{k^{2}+1}\,dk.$
$

Using the contour integral (close in the upper half-plane for $x>\xi$, lower for
 $x<\xi$; the pole at $k=\pm i$ gives)
$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{e^{ika}}{k^{2}+1}\,dk=\pi e^{-|a|},$$
we obtain
$$\boxed{\,G(x,\xi)=-\tfrac12\,e^{-|x-\xi|}\,}.$$

### Step 3. Verification

**Decay:** $G\to 0$ as $x\to\pm\infty$. ✓

**Away from $x=\xi$:** For $x>\xi$, $G=-\tfrac12 e^{-(x-\xi)}$, so $G''=-\tfrac1
2 e^{-(x-\xi)}=G$, hence $G''-G=0$. For $x<\xi$, $G=-\tfrac12 e^{(x-\xi)}$, so $
G''=-\tfrac12 e^{(x-\xi)}=G$, hence $G''-G=0$. ✓

**Delta singularity:** $G$ is continuous at $x=\xi$ with $G(\xi)=-\tfrac12$. The
 derivative jumps:
$$G'(\xi^{+})=\tfrac12,\qquad G'(\xi^{-})=-\tfrac12,\qquad G'(\xi^{+})-G'(\xi^{-
})=1.$$
Integrating the ODE over $(\xi-\varepsilon,\xi+\varepsilon)$ and letting $\varep
silon\to0$:
$$\big[G'\big]_{\xi^{-}}^{\xi^{+}}-\underbrace{\int_{\xi-\varepsilon}^{\xi+\vare
psilon}G\,dx}_{\to\,0}=1,$$
which matches the unit jump. ✓

Thus $G(x,\xi)=-\tfrac12 e^{-|x-\xi|}$ is the Green's function for $G''-G=\delta
(x-\xi)$, $G(\pm\infty)=0$.
