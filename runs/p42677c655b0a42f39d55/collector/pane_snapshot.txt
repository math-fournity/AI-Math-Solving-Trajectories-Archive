**Setup.** Let $\nu_2 \colon \mathbb{P}^2 \hookrightarrow \mathbb{P}^5$ be the 2
-uple (quadratic) Veronese embedding, sending $[x_0:x_1:x_2]$ to the point whose
 coordinates are the six degree-2 monomials in $x_0,x_1,x_2$. Let $V = \nu_2(\ma
thbb{P}^2) \subset \mathbb{P}^5$ be the Veronese surface, with homogeneous ideal
 $I_V$. We want $\dim H^0(\mathbb{P}^5, I_V(3))$.

**Step 1: The restriction sequence.** Twisting the ideal sheaf sequence of $V \s
ubset \mathbb{P}^5$ by $\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^5}(3)$ gives
$$
0 \to I_V(3) \to \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^5}(3) \to \mathcal{O}_V(3) \to 0.
$$

**Step 2: Projective normality kills $H^1$.** The 2-uple Veronese embedding is p
rojectively normal (Veronese embeddings of $\mathbb{P}^n$ are projectively norma
l for all $n$ and all degrees). Equivalently, the restriction maps $H^0(\mathbb{
P}^5,\mathcal{O}(k)) \to H^0(V,\mathcal{O}_V(k))$ are surjective for every $k \g
e 0$, i.e. $H^1(\mathbb{P}^5, I_V(k)) = 0$ for $k \ge 0$. In particular $H^1(I_V
(3)) = 0$, so the long exact sequence in cohomology yields
$$
\dim H^0(I_V(3)) = \dim H^0(\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^5}(3)) - \dim H^0(\mathcal{O
}_V(3)).
$$

**Step 3: Compute $H^0(\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^5}(3))$.** For $\mathbb{P}^5$,
$$
\dim H^0(\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^5}(3)) = \binom{5+3}{3} = \binom{8}{3} = 56.
$$

**Step 4: Compute $H^0(\mathcal{O}_V(3))$.** The Veronese map $\nu_2$ satisfies
$\nu_2^* \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^5}(1) = \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^2}(2)$. Hence o
n $V \cong \mathbb{P}^2$,
$$
\mathcal{O}_V(3) \cong \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^2}(6),
$$
so
$$
\dim H^0(\mathcal{O}_V(3)) = \dim H^0(\mathbb{P}^2, \mathcal{O}(6)) = \binom{2+6
}{6} = \binom{8}{6} = \binom{8}{2} = 28.
$$

**Step 5: Conclude.** Therefore
$$
\dim H^0(\mathbb{P}^5, I_V(3)) = 56 - 28 = \boxed{28}.
$$
