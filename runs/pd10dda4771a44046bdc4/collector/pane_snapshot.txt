## Solution

Let me model the game. The die has faces {1,2,3,4,5,6}. The odd faces are {1,3,5
}; the even faces are {2,4,6}.

A player wins on their turn if their roll is **even** or **already appeared** in
 any previous turn. Equivalently, a turn "misses" (continues the game) **only if
** the player rolls an odd number that has never been rolled before. Such a miss
 reveals a new odd number and adds it to the "seen" set.

### Key observation

By symmetry among the three odd numbers, the game state depends only on $k$, the
 number of distinct odd numbers seen so far, with $k\in\{0,1,2,3\}$.

Let $f(k)$ = probability that the player **whose turn it is** eventually wins, g
iven that $k$ odd numbers have already been seen.

On a turn with $k$ odds seen:
- Roll even: probability $\tfrac{3}{6}=\tfrac12$ → current player wins.
- Roll an odd already seen: probability $\tfrac{k}{6}$ → current player wins.
- Roll a new odd: probability $\tfrac{3-k}{6}$ → this odd is added to the seen s
et (now $k+1$ odds seen), and the turn passes to the opponent, whose win probabi
lity becomes $f(k+1)$. So the current player wins from this branch with probabil
ity $1-f(k+1)$.

Hence
$$f(k)=\frac12+\frac{k}{6}+\frac{3-k}{6}\bigl(1-f(k+1)\bigr).$$

### Computing the values

**$k=3$** (all odds seen): every roll is either even or a repeat, so the current
 player always wins:
$$f(3)=\frac12+\frac36+0=1.$$

**$k=2$**:
$$f(2)=\frac12+\frac26+\frac16(1-f(3))=\frac12+\frac13+0=\frac56.$$

**$k=1$**:
$$f(1)=\frac12+\frac16+\frac26(1-f(2))=\frac46+\frac13\cdot\frac16=\frac{24}{36}
+\frac{2}{36}=\frac{13}{18}.$$

**$k=0$** (start of game, Agent K's turn):
$$f(0)=\frac12+0+\frac36(1-f(1))=\frac12+\frac12\cdot\frac{5}{18}=\frac{18}{36}+
\frac{5}{36}=\frac{23}{36}.$$

So Agent K (the first player) wins with probability $\tfrac{23}{36}$, and theref
ore **Agent A wins with probability**
$$1-\frac{23}{36}=\frac{13}{36}.$$

### Direct verification by path enumeration

The game cannot last beyond turn 4: each "miss" reveals a new odd, there are onl
y 3 odds, so after at most 3 misses the next turn is a forced win.

- **A wins on turn 2:** K misses turn 1 (rolls a new odd, prob $\tfrac12$), then
 A wins turn 2 (even or repeats K's odd: $\tfrac12+\tfrac16=\tfrac23$).
  $$P=\tfrac12\cdot\tfrac23=\tfrac{12}{36}.$$

- **A wins on turn 4:** K misses turn 1 ($\tfrac12$), A misses turn 2 (rolls the
 2nd new odd, $\tfrac26=\tfrac13$), K misses turn 3 (rolls the 3rd new odd, $\tf
rac16$), then A wins turn 4 (forced, since all odds seen: probability $1$).
  $$P=\tfrac12\cdot\tfrac13\cdot\tfrac16\cdot1=\tfrac{1}{36}.$$

Total:
$$P(\text{A wins})=\frac{12}{36}+\frac{1}{36}=\frac{13}{36}.$$

Both methods agree.

### Answer

$$\boxed{\dfrac{13}{36}}$$
