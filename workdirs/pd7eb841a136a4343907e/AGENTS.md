# Problem

In a remote desert, a specialized communication outpost is built in the shape of a perfect right circular cone. The outpost has a circular base with a radius of exactly 1 kilometer. The slant height of the cone, measured from the ground-level perimeter up to the vertex $V$ at the very top, is exactly 3 kilometers.

A technician starts at a point $P$ on the edge of the circular base to install a fiber-optic sensor cable. The cable must be wrapped tightly around the cone's sloped surface, starting at point $P$, circling the structure completely, and returning to the exact same point $P$. To minimize costs, the technician ensures that the cable follows the shortest possible path around the cone.

While inspecting the installation, a supervisor needs to find the point on this cable that is closest to the vertex $V$ of the outpost. What is the minimum distance from the vertex $V$ to the sensor cable?
