## Proof

We seek the smallest positive integer $n$ such that $100! + n$ is not divisible
by any prime $p \le 50$.

**Key observation.** Since $100! = 1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 \cdots 100$, every prime $p
 \le 50$ appears as a factor in this product. Therefore

$$p \mid 100! \quad\text{for every prime } p \le 50,$$

or equivalently $100! \equiv 0 \pmod{p}$ for every such prime.

**Testing $n = 1$.** For every prime $p \le 50$,

$$100! + 1 \equiv 0 + 1 \equiv 1 \pmod{p} \neq 0.$$

Hence $100! + 1$ is **not** divisible by any prime $\le 50$.

**Minimality.** The integer $n = 1$ is the smallest positive integer. Since it a
lready satisfies the condition, no smaller positive integer exists.

Therefore, the smallest positive integer that must be added to $100!$ is

$$\boxed{1}.$$
