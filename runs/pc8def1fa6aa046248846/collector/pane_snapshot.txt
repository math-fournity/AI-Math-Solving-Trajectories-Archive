**Step 2: Refined expansion of $b_{n+1} - b_n$.**

c{1}{b^2} + O(1/b^3) = 1 + \frac{1}{2b} - \frac{1}{12b^2} + O(1/b^3).$$

Therefore
$$b_{n+1} = b_n + \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{12\,b_n} + O(1/b_n^2),$$
$$b_{n+1} - b_n = \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{12\,b_n} + O(1/b_n^2).$$

$$b_n = \frac{n}{2} - \frac{1}{6}\ln n + d + o(1).$$
