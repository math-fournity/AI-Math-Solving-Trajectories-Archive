# Problem

In a specialized logistics hub, there are 33 distinct storage containers, each labeled with a unique integer ID from 1 to 33. The facility operates under a specific "Consolidation Protocol": a technician may select any two containers currently in the hub, with IDs $a$ and $b$, provided that the product of their IDs ($a \times b$) results in a perfect square.

When such a pair is identified, the two containers are merged into a single new unit. To maintain the inventory system, the ID of this new unit is assigned the value of the geometric mean of the original IDs, $\sqrt{ab}$. This consolidation process is repeated iteratively as long as there are at least two containers in the hub whose IDs, when multiplied together, produce a perfect square. 

The technician's objective is to reach a state where no two remaining containers satisfy the consolidation criteria. Let $N$ be the minimum possible number of containers remaining in the hub when the process terminates. Find $N$.
