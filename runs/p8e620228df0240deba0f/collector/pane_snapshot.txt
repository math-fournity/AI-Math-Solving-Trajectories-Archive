## Setup

Let the counts of containers of capacities 200, 100, 50, 20, 10 be non-negative
integers $(a,b,c,d,e)$. The load equation is

$$200a+100b+50c+20d+10e=500.$$

Dividing by 10:

$$20a+10b+5c+2d+e=50.\tag{*}$$

Since $20a\le 50$, we have $a\in\{0,1,2\}$. For a fixed $a$, the residual $N=50-
20a$ must satisfy $10b+5c+2d+e=N$. Let $f(N)$ denote the number of non-negative
integer solutions of $10b+5c+2d+e=N$.

For fixed $(b,c)$, the equation $2d+e=M$ (with $M=N-10b-5c\ge 0$) has $\lfloor M
/2\rfloor+1$ solutions (one for each $d=0,\dots,\lfloor M/2\rfloor$, with $e$ de
