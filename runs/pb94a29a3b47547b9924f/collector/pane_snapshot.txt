## Solution

We compute the surface integral $\iint_S \sqrt{x^2+y^2}\,dS$ over the lateral su
rface of the cone
$$\frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{a^2}-\frac{z^2}{b^2}=0,\qquad 0\le z\le b.$$

### Step 1. Parametrize the surface

The cone equation gives $x^2+y^2=\dfrac{a^2}{b^2}z^2$, i.e. at height $z$ the cr
oss-section is a circle of radius $r(z)=\dfrac{a}{b}z$. Using parameters $(\thet
a,z)$ with $\theta\in[0,2\pi]$, $z\in[0,b]$:

$$\mathbf{r}(\theta,z)=\left(\tfrac{a}{b}z\cos\theta,\;\tfrac{a}{b}z\sin\theta,\
;z\right).$$

### Step 2. Compute the surface element $dS$

$$\mathbf{r}_\theta=\left(-\tfrac{a}{b}z\sin\theta,\;\tfrac{a}{b}z\cos\theta,\;0
\right),\qquad \mathbf{r}_z=\left(\tfrac{a}{b}\cos\theta,\;\tfrac{a}{b}\sin\thet
a,\;1\right).$$

The cross product:

$$\mathbf{r}_\theta\times\mathbf{r}_z=\left(\tfrac{a}{b}z\cos\theta,\;\tfrac{a}{
b}z\sin\theta,\;-\tfrac{a^2}{b^2}z\right).$$

Its magnitude:

$$\left|\mathbf{r}_\theta\times\mathbf{r}_z\right|=\sqrt{\tfrac{a^2}{b^2}z^2+\tf
rac{a^4}{b^4}z^2}=\tfrac{a}{b}z\sqrt{1+\tfrac{a^2}{b^2}}=\tfrac{a\,z}{b}\cdot\tf
rac{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}{b}=\tfrac{a\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}{b^2}\,z.$$

Hence $dS=\dfrac{a\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}{b^2}\,z\,dz\,d\theta$.

### Step 3. Evaluate the integral

On the surface, $\sqrt{x^2+y^2}=r=\dfrac{a}{b}z$. Therefore

$$\iint_S \sqrt{x^2+y^2}\,dS=\int_0^{2\pi}\!\!\int_0^b \frac{a}{b}z\cdot\frac{a\
sqrt{a^2+b^2}}{b^2}\,z\,dz\,d\theta=\frac{a^2\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}{b^3}\int_0^{2\pi}d\
theta\int_0^b z^2\,dz.$$

Computing each factor:

$$\int_0^{2\pi}d\theta=2\pi,\qquad \int_0^b z^2\,dz=\frac{b^3}{3}.$$

Thus

$$\iint_S \sqrt{x^2+y^2}\,dS=\frac{a^2\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}{b^3}\cdot 2\pi\cdot\frac{b
^3}{3}=\boxed{\dfrac{2\pi a^2\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}{3}}.$$
