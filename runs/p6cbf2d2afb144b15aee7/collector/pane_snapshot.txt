## Proof

**Setup.** Let $W:[0,1]^2\to[0,1]$ be a graphon (a symmetric measurable function
). We assume there exists a measurable set $S\subset[0,1]$ with $\lambda(S)>0$ a
nd a constant $p>0$ such that $W(x,y)\ge p$ for all $x,y\in S$.

A random graph $G(n,W)$ is generated as follows: sample $X_1,\dots,X_n\stackrel{
\text{i.i.d.}}{\sim}\mathrm{Uniform}[0,1]$, then independently connect vertices
$i$ and $j$ ($i<j$) with probability $W(X_i,X_j)$.

We say the graph is **dense** if it has $\Theta(n^2)$ edges. We prove:

$$\Pr\big[G(n,W)\text{ is dense}\big]\xrightarrow{n\to\infty}1.$$

---

**Step 1: Many vertices fall in $S$.**

Let $N_S=\bigl|\{i\in[n]:X_i\in S\}\bigr|$. Then $N_S\sim\mathrm{Binomial}(n,\la
mbda(S))$, so $\mathbb{E}[N_S]=n\,\lambda(S)$. By the multiplicative Chernoff bo
und,

$$\Pr\!\left[N_S<\tfrac{n\,\lambda(S)}{2}\right]\le \exp\!\left(-\frac{n\,\lambd
a(S)}{8}\right). \tag{1}$$

---

**Step 2: Many edges appear inside $S$.**

Condition on $N_S\ge \tfrac{n\,\lambda(S)}{2}$. The number of candidate pairs wi
th both endpoints in $S$ is $\binom{N_S}{2}\ge\binom{n\,\lambda(S)/2}{2}\ge \tfr
ac{n^2\lambda(S)^2}{8}$ for $n$ large enough.

Each such pair $(i,j)$ is connected independently with probability $W(X_i,X_j)\g
e p$. Let $e_S$ be the number of edges with both endpoints in $S$. Then

$$\mathbb{E}\bigl[e_S\bigm| N_S\ge\tfrac{n\,\lambda(S)}{2}\bigr]\ge p\cdot\frac{
n^2\lambda(S)^2}{8}.$$

By Hoeffding's inequality applied to the $\binom{N_S}{2}$ independent Bernoulli
trials (each with success probability $\ge p$),

$$\Pr\!\left[e_S<\frac{p\,n^2\lambda(S)^2}{16}\;\Big|\;N_S\ge\frac{n\,\lambda(S)
}{2}\right]\le\exp\!\left(-\Omega(n^2)\right). \tag{2}$$

---

**Step 3: Union bound.**

The total number of edges $e(G(n,W))\ge e_S$. Combining (1) and (2):

$$\Pr\!\left[e(G(n,W))<\frac{p\,n^2\lambda(S)^2}{16}\right]\le\exp\!\left(-\frac
{n\,\lambda(S)}{8}\right)+\exp\!\left(-\Omega(n^2)\right)\xrightarrow{n\to\infty
}0.$$

Therefore

$$\Pr\bigl[G(n,W)\text{ is dense}\bigr]\ge 1-\exp\!\left(-\frac{n\,\lambda(S)}{8
}\right)-\exp\!\left(-\Omega(n^2)\right)\xrightarrow{n\to\infty}1.$$

---

**Conclusion.** For a graphon $W$ possessing a set $S$ of positive measure on wh
ich $W\ge p>0$, the probability that $G(n,W)$ is dense (has $\Theta(n^2)$ edges)
 is

$$\boxed{\Pr\bigl[G(n,W)\text{ is dense}\bigr]=1-o(1)=1\quad\text{as }n\to\infty
.}$$

The key mechanism: by the law of large numbers, a positive-measure fraction $\la
mbda(S)$ of the $n$ sampled vertices lands in $S$ w.h.p.; among those vertices,
each pair connects with probability at least $p$, yielding $\Omega(n^2)$ edges w
.h.p. by concentration. $\blacksquare$
