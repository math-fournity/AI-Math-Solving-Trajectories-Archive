## Proof

We use the Binet formula for Fibonacci numbers:
$$F_{k+1}=\frac{\phi^{k+1}-\psi^{k+1}}{\sqrt{5}},\qquad \phi=\frac{1+\sqrt5}{2},
\;\;\psi=\frac{1-\sqrt5}{2},$$
with the key identities $\phi+\psi=1$, $\phi-\psi=\sqrt5$, $\phi\psi=-1$.

Let
$$S_n=\sum_{k=0}^{2n}(-1)^k\binom{2n}{k}2^k F_{k+1}.$$

Substituting Binet's formula:
$$S_n=\frac{1}{\sqrt5}\sum_{k=0}^{2n}(-1)^k\binom{2n}{k}2^k\bigl(\phi^{k+1}-\psi
^{k+1}\bigr).$$

Split the sum and factor out the leading $\phi$ (resp. $\psi$):
$$S_n=\frac{1}{\sqrt5}\left[\phi\sum_{k=0}^{2n}\binom{2n}{k}(-2\phi)^k\;-\;\psi\
sum_{k=0}^{2n}\binom{2n}{k}(-2\psi)^k\right].$$

Apply the binomial theorem $\sum_{k=0}^{m}\binom{m}{k}x^k=(1+x)^m$ with $m=2n$:
$$S_n=\frac{1}{\sqrt5}\Bigl[\phi\,(1-2\phi)^{2n}-\psi\,(1-2\psi)^{2n}\Bigr].$$

Now compute the two bases:
$$1-2\phi=1-(1+\sqrt5)=-\sqrt5,\qquad 1-2\psi=1-(1-\sqrt5)=\sqrt5.$$

Since the exponent is $2n$ (even), the sign disappears:
$$(-\sqrt5)^{2n}=(\sqrt5)^{2n}=5^n.$$

Therefore
$$S_n=\frac{1}{\sqrt5}\Bigl[\phi\cdot 5^n-\psi\cdot 5^n\Bigr]=\frac{5^n(\phi-\ps
i)}{\sqrt5}=\frac{5^n\cdot\sqrt5}{\sqrt5}=5^n.$$

### Verification of small cases

- $n=0$: $\displaystyle S_0=(-1)^0\binom00 2^0 F_1=1\cdot1\cdot1=1=5^0.$ ✓
- $n=1$: $\displaystyle S_1=\sum_{k=0}^{2}(-1)^k\binom2k 2^k F_{k+1}= \binom20\c
dot1\cdot F_1-\binom21\cdot2\cdot F_2+\binom22\cdot4\cdot F_3$
  $=1\cdot1-2\cdot2\cdot1+1\cdot4\cdot2=1-4+8=5=5^1.$ ✓

### Range of validity

The expression $\binom{2n}{k}$ summed from $k=0$ to $2n$ requires $2n\geq 0$, i.
e. $n\geq 0$. For every **non-negative integer** $n$, the derivation above gives
 $S_n=5^n$.

For **negative integers** $n$: the upper limit $2n$ is negative, so the sum is e
mpty and equals $0$, whereas $5^n\neq 0$. Hence the formula does **not** hold fo
r negative $n$.

### Conclusion

The formula
$$\sum_{k=0}^{2n}(-1)^k\binom{2n}{k}2^k F_{k+1}=5^n$$
holds for **all non-negative integers $n$** (and in particular for all integers
$n\geq 0$), but **fails for negative integers**. Under the standard convention t
hat the formula is meaningful only when $2n\geq 0$, it holds for every integer v
alue of $n$ for which the expression is defined.
