## Solution

**Setup.** Place square $ABCD$ with $A=(0,0)$, $B=(a,0)$, $C=(a,a)$, $D=(0,a)$ (
counterclockwise). The equilateral triangle $BAE$ is constructed outward on side
 $AB$, so $E = \left(\frac{a}{2},\, -\frac{a\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$, below the squa
re.

The triangle rolls counterclockwise around the square, pivoting successively at
each vertex $B, C, D, A$. Since the triangle's side length equals the square's s
ide length (both $a$), one full side of the triangle always covers one full side
 of the square — the triangle pivots from corner to corner without sliding along
 any side.

**Key observation.** The triangle $BAE$ is equilateral with side $a$, so every v
ertex-to-vertex distance equals $a$. At each pivot, the pivot point is a vertex
of the square that coincides with a vertex of the triangle, and $E$ is always th
e free vertex (the one not on the square's side). Therefore the distance from th
e pivot to $E$ is always exactly $a$.

**Rotation angle.** At each corner of the square, the exterior angle is $90° = \
frac{\pi}{2}$. When the triangle pivots around a vertex to move from one side of
 the square to the next, it rotates by exactly this exterior angle. Concretely:

- **Pivot at $B$:** Side $BA$ of the triangle rotates from direction $\overright
arrow{BA}=(-1,0)$ to direction $\overrightarrow{BC}=(0,1)$ — a $90°$ rotation. $
E$ traces a circular arc of radius $BE = a$ and angle $\frac{\pi}{2}$.

- **Pivot at $C$:** The triangle rotates $90°$ around $C$. $E$ traces an arc of
radius $CE = a$ and angle $\frac{\pi}{2}$.

- **Pivot at $D$:** The triangle rotates $90°$ around $D$. $E$ traces an arc of
radius $DE = a$ and angle $\frac{\pi}{2}$.

- **Pivot at $A$:** The triangle rotates $90°$ around $A$. $E$ traces an arc of
radius $AE = a$ and angle $\frac{\pi}{2}$.

**Verification that $E$ returns to start.** After 4 pivots (total rotation $4 \t
imes 90° = 360°$), the triangle is back on side $AB$ in its original orientation
. Tracing $E$ through each rotation:

| After pivot | $E$'s position |
|---|---|
| Start | $\left(\frac{a}{2},\, -\frac{a\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$ |
| Pivot at $B$ | $\left(a - \frac{a\sqrt{3}}{2},\, \frac{a}{2}\right)$ |
| Pivot at $C$ | $\left(\frac{a}{2},\, a + \frac{a\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$ |
| Pivot at $D$ | $\left(\frac{a\sqrt{3}}{2},\, \frac{a}{2}\right)$ |
| Pivot at $A$ | $\left(\frac{a}{2},\, -\frac{a\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$ ✓ |

$E$ returns to its starting position after exactly 4 pivots.

**Path length.** The path of $E$ consists of 4 circular arcs, each of radius $a$
 and central angle $\frac{\pi}{2}$:

$$L = 4 \times a \times \frac{\pi}{2} = 2\pi a.$$
