## Proof

We work with singular homology with integer coefficients. The circle $S^1 = \{z
\in \mathbb{C} : |z|=1\}$ has $H_1(S^1) \cong \mathbb{Z}$, generated by the homo
logy class $g = [\sigma]$, where $\sigma: [0,1] \to S^1$ is the standard loop $\
sigma(t) = e^{2\pi i t}$.

**Goal.** Compute $f_*(g)$ where $f(z) = z^n$.

**Step 1: The induced cycle.** The map $f_*$ sends the homology class $[\sigma]$
 to $[f \circ \sigma]$. The composite loop is
$$
(f \circ \sigma)(t) = f(e^{2\pi i t}) = e^{2\pi i n t}, \qquad t \in [0,1].
$$
This is the loop that winds $n$ times around $S^1$ counterclockwise.

**Step 2: Relating this loop to the generator.** Define $\sigma_n(t) = e^{2\pi i
 n t}$; this is exactly $f \circ \sigma$. We claim $[\sigma_n] = n \cdot [\sigma
]$ in $H_1(S^1)$.

Consider the $n$-fold concatenation $\sigma * \sigma * \cdots * \sigma$ ($n$ tim
es), which is the loop
$$
\tau(t) = \sigma\!\left(nt - \lfloor nt/1\rfloor \cdot 1\right) \quad \text{piec
ewise, i.e. } \tau(t) = e^{2\pi i n t} \text{ on each subinterval.}
$$
Explicitly, $\tau(t) = e^{2\pi i n t}$ for all $t \in [0,1]$, since on the $k$-t
h subinterval $[k/n, (k+1)/n]$ the concatenation gives $e^{2\pi i (nt - k)} = e^
{2\pi i n t}$. Thus $\tau = \sigma_n$ as maps $[0,1] \to S^1$.

By the homological property of concatenation (the Eilenberg–Zilber / subdivision
 argument at the chain level), the class of a concatenation equals the sum of th
e classes:
$$
[\sigma * \cdots * \sigma] = \underbrace{[\sigma] + \cdots + [\sigma]}_{n} = n \
cdot [\sigma].
$$
Hence
$$
[\sigma_n] = n \cdot [\sigma] = n \cdot g.
$$

**Step 3: Conclusion.** Therefore
$$
f_*(g) = f_*([\sigma]) = [f \circ \sigma] = [\sigma_n] = n \cdot g.
$$

The induced map $f_*: H_1(S^1) \to H_1(S^1)$ is multiplication by $n$:
$$
\boxed{f_*(g) = n\, g.}
$$
