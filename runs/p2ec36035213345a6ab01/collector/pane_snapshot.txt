## Proof

Let the three sectors be $R$ (Industrial/Rock), $P$ (Residential/Paper), and $S$
 (Commercial/Scissors). The drone starts at $R$ for mission 1.

**Define the recurrence.** Let $p_n$ be the probability that the drone's $n$-th
mission is to the Industrial Zone $R$.

At mission $n$, the drone must move to a sector *different* from mission $n-1$,
choosing between the two alternatives with equal probability $1/2$. There are tw
o cases for mission $n-1$:

- **Mission $n-1$ was $R$** (probability $p_{n-1}$): the drone must move to $P$
or $S$, so mission $n$ is $R$ with probability $0$.
- **Mission $n-1$ was not $R$** (probability $1 - p_{n-1}$): the drone moves to
one of the two alternatives, one of which is $R$, so mission $n$ is $R$ with pro
bability $1/2$.

Therefore:
$$p_n = p_{n-1}\cdot 0 + (1 - p_{n-1})\cdot \tfrac{1}{2} = \frac{1 - p_{n-1}}{2}
.$$

**Compute iteratively from the base case** $p_1 = 1$:

$$p_2 = \frac{1 - 1}{2} = 0,$$

$$p_3 = \frac{1 - 0}{2} = \frac{1}{2},$$

$$p_4 = \frac{1 - \tfrac{1}{2}}{2} = \frac{1}{4},$$

$$p_5 = \frac{1 - \tfrac{1}{4}}{2} = \frac{3}{8}.$$

**Verification by enumeration.** The drone's path is a sequence of 5 sectors wit
h the first fixed at $R$ and each subsequent sector differing from the previous.
 There are $2^4 = 16$ equally likely paths. Counting those ending at $R$:

- $R \to P \to R \to P \to R$ ✓
- $R \to P \to R \to S \to R$ ✓
- $R \to S \to R \to P \to R$ ✓
- $R \to S \to R \to S \to R$ ✓
- $R \to P \to S \to P \to ?$ (last is $R$ or $S$, neither path ends at $R$ via
this branch except $R\to P\to S\to P\to R$) ✓ — wait, let me recount carefully.

Paths ending at $R$ at mission 5 require mission 4 to be $P$ or $S$ (not $R$), a
nd mission 5 = $R$.

Mission 4 = $P$: paths $R\to ?\to ?\to P$. Mission 3 must be $R$ or $S$.
- $R\to P\to R\to P\to R$ and $R\to S\to R\to P\to R$: mission 3 = $R$, mission
2 = $P$ or $S$ → 2 paths.
- $R\to P\to S\to P\to R$ and $R\to S\to S$... no, mission 2 $\ne$ mission 1's n
eighbor constraint: mission 2 $\in\{P,S\}$, mission 3 = $S$ requires mission 2 $
\ne S$, so mission 2 = $P$: $R\to P\to S\to P\to R$ → 1 path.

Mission 4 = $S$: similarly,
- $R\to P\to R\to S\to R$, $R\to S\to R\to S\to R$: 2 paths.
- $R\to S\to P\to S\to R$: 1 path.

Total: $2 + 1 + 2 + 1 = 6$ paths out of $16$, giving $6/16 = 3/8$. ✓

**The probability that the drone's 5th mission is to the Industrial Zone is $\bo
xed{\dfrac{3}{8}}$.**
