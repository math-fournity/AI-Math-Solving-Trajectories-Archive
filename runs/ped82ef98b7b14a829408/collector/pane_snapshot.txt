The answer is **yes**: for every tempered distribution $u \in \mathcal{S}'(\math
bb{R}^n)$ there exist an integer $m \ge 0$ and a constant $C > 0$ such that $|u(
\phi)| \le C\|\phi\|_m$ for all $\phi \in \mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^n)$.

## Proof

**The topology of $\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^n)$.** For each integer $m \ge 0$ defi
ne
$$\|\phi\|_m = \sum_{|\alpha|+|\beta|\le m}\sup_{x\in\mathbb{R}^n}\bigl|x^\alpha
 \partial_x^\beta \phi(x)\bigr|.$$
Each $\|\cdot\|_m$ is a norm on $\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^n)$ (vanishing only at $
\phi=0$), and $\|\phi\|_m \le \|\phi\|_{m+1}$. The Fréchet topology on $\mathcal
{S}(\mathbb{R}^n)$ is generated by the increasing sequence of norms $\{\|\cdot\|
_m\}_{m\ge 0}$; a neighborhood base at the origin is
$$\bigl\{\,\{\phi : \|\phi\|_m < \varepsilon\} : m\ge 0,\ \varepsilon>0\,\bigr\}
.$$

**Continuity of $u$.** By definition $u \in \mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^n)$ means $u
:\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^n)\to\mathbb{C}$ is linear and continuous. Continuity at
 the origin gives: the preimage $u^{-1}(\{z\in\mathbb{C}:|z|<1\})$ is a neighbor
hood of $0$ in $\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^n)$. Hence there exist $m\ge 0$ and $\var
epsilon>0$ such that
$$\|\phi\|_m < \varepsilon \quad\Longrightarrow\quad |u(\phi)| < 1. \tag{$\ast$}
$$

**Deriving the bound.** Let $\phi\in\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^n)$, $\phi\ne 0$. Sin
ce $\|\cdot\|_m$ is a *norm*, $\|\phi\|_m>0$. Set
$$\psi = \frac{\varepsilon}{2\,\|\phi\|_m}\,\phi \;\in\; \mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^
n).$$
Then $\|\psi\|_m = \varepsilon/2 < \varepsilon$, so by $(\ast)$, $|u(\psi)|<1$.
By linearity of $u$,
$$\frac{\varepsilon}{2\,\|\phi\|_m}\,|u(\phi)| = |u(\psi)| < 1,$$
which yields
$$|u(\phi)| < \frac{2}{\varepsilon}\,\|\phi\|_m.$$
The case $\phi=0$ is trivial. Setting $C := 2/\varepsilon > 0$, we obtain
$$|u(\phi)| \le C\,\|\phi\|_m \qquad\text{for all } \phi\in\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R
}^n).$$

**Conclusion.** Every $u\in\mathcal{S}'(\mathbb{R}^n)$ is bounded by a single se
minorm $\|\cdot\|_m$ up to a constant; equivalently, the continuous dual of the
Fréchet space $(\mathcal{S},\{\|\cdot\|_m\})$ consists exactly of those linear f
unctionals dominated by some finite stage $m$ of the defining filtration. Theref
ore such $m\ge 0$ and $C>0$ always exist. $\blacksquare$
