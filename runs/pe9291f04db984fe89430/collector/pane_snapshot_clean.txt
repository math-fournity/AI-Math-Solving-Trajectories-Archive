## Solution

**Step 1: Factor the cubic coefficients.**

$$x^3+3x^2+3x+2 = (x+1)^3+1 = (x+2)(x^2+x+1),$$
$$x^3-3x^2+3x-2 = (x-1)^3-1 = (x-2)(x^2-x+1).$$

The equation becomes
$$(x+2)(x^2+x+1)\,P(x-1)=(x-2)(x^2-x+1)\,P(x).\tag{1}$$

Note $x^2\pm x+1$ have negative discriminant, so their only real-rooted factors
above are $(x+2)$ and $(x-2)$.

**Step 2: Force roots of $P$.**

Setting $x=-2$ in (1): $0=(-4)(7)\,P(-2)$, so $P(-2)=0$.
Setting $x=2$ in (1): $(4)(7)\,P(1)=0$, so $P(1)=0$.
Setting $x=1$: $(3)(3)\,P(0)=(-1)(1)\,P(1)=0$, so $P(0)=0$.
Setting $x=0$: $(2)(1)\,P(-1)=(-2)(1)\,P(0)=0$, so $P(-1)=0$.

Thus $P$ has roots $-2,-1,0,1$, and
$$P(x)=x(x-1)(x+1)(x+2)\,Q(x)\tag{2}$$
for some real polynomial $Q$.

**Step 3: Reduce the equation.**

From (2), $P(x-1)=(x-1)(x-2)x(x+1)\,Q(x-1)$. Substituting into (1) and using $x^
2-x-2=(x-2)(x+1)$, $x^2+x-2=(x+2)(x-1)$, $x^2-x=x(x-1)$, $x^2+x=x(x+1)$:

LHS $=(x+2)(x^2+x+1)\cdot x(x-1)(x+1)(x-2)\,Q(x-1)$,
RHS $=(x-2)(x^2-x+1)\cdot x(x-1)(x+1)(x+2)\,Q(x)$.

Canceling the common factor $x(x-1)(x+1)(x+2)(x-2)$ (valid as a polynomial ident
ity):
$$(x^2+x+1)\,Q(x-1)=(x^2-x+1)\,Q(x).\tag{3}$$

**Step 4: Solve the reduced equation.**

Let $f(x)=x^2+x+1$, so $f(x-1)=x^2-x+1$. Equation (3) is
$$f(x)\,Q(x-1)=f(x-1)\,Q(x).\tag{4}$$

Since $f(x)=x^2+x+1$ is irreducible over $\mathbb{R}$ and $\gcd(f(x),f(x-1))=1$
(their difference is $2x$, and $f(0)=1\neq 0$), from (4) we get $f(x)\mid Q(x)$.
 Write $Q(x)=f(x)\,S(x)$. Substituting into (4):
$$f(x)\,f(x-1)\,S(x-1)=f(x-1)\,f(x)\,S(x),$$
so $S(x-1)=S(x)$ for all real $x$. A polynomial periodic with period $1$ is cons
tant: $S(x)\equiv c$.

Hence $Q(x)=c\,(x^2+x+1)$.

**Step 5: Conclusion.**

$$\boxed{P(x)=c\,x(x-1)(x+1)(x+2)(x^2+x+1),\qquad c\in\mathbb{R}.}$$

**Verification.** With $P(x)=c\,x(x-1)(x+1)(x+2)(x^2+x+1)$:
- $P(x-1)=c\,x(x-1)(x-2)(x+1)(x^2-x+1)$.
- LHS $=c\,(x+2)(x^2+x+1)\,x(x-1)(x-2)(x+1)(x^2-x+1)$.
- RHS $=c\,(x-2)(x^2-x+1)\,x(x-1)(x+1)(x+2)(x^2+x+1)$.

Both sides are identical. ✓
