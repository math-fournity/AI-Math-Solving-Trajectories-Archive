We prove that

$$\lim_{r \to \infty}\sqrt{\frac{r}{2}}\;\frac{\Gamma\!\big((r-1)/2\big)}{\Gamma
\!\big(r/2\big)} = 1.$$

**Substitution.** Set $x = r/2$, so $(r-1)/2 = x - \tfrac12$ and $x \to \infty$
as $r \to \infty$. The limit becomes

$$L = \lim_{x \to \infty}\sqrt{x}\;\frac{\Gamma\!\big(x - \tfrac12\big)}{\Gamma(
x)}.$$

**Stirling's approximation.** For large $x$,

$$\Gamma(x) \sim \sqrt{2\pi}\,x^{\,x-1/2}\,e^{-x}.$$

Applying this to both $\Gamma(x)$ and $\Gamma\!\big(x-\tfrac12\big)$:

$$\Gamma\!\big(x-\tfrac12\big) \sim \sqrt{2\pi}\,\big(x-\tfrac12\big)^{x-1}\,e^{
-(x-1/2)}, \qquad \Gamma(x) \sim \sqrt{2\pi}\,x^{x-1/2}\,e^{-x}.$$

**Form the ratio.**

$$\frac{\Gamma\!\big(x-\tfrac12\big)}{\Gamma(x)} \sim \frac{\big(x-\tfrac12\big)
^{x-1}\,e^{-(x-1/2)}}{x^{x-1/2}\,e^{-x}} = \frac{\big(x-\tfrac12\big)^{x-1}}{x^{
x-1/2}}\,e^{1/2}.$$

Multiply by $\sqrt{x} = x^{1/2}$:

$$\sqrt{x}\;\frac{\Gamma\!\big(x-\tfrac12\big)}{\Gamma(x)} \sim x^{1/2}\cdot\fra
c{\big(x-\tfrac12\big)^{x-1}}{x^{x-1/2}}\,e^{1/2} = \frac{\big(x-\tfrac12\big)^{
x-1}}{x^{x-1}}\,e^{1/2}.$$

**Factor out $x$.** Write $\big(x-\tfrac12\big)^{x-1} = x^{x-1}\Big(1 - \tfrac{1
}{2x}\Big)^{x-1}$, so

$$\sqrt{x}\;\frac{\Gamma\!\big(x-\tfrac12\big)}{\Gamma(x)} \sim \Big(1 - \tfrac{
1}{2x}\Big)^{x-1}\,e^{1/2}.$$

**Take the limit.** Using the standard limit $(1 + a/n)^{n} \to e^{a}$,

$$\lim_{x\to\infty}\Big(1 - \tfrac{1}{2x}\Big)^{x-1} = \lim_{x\to\infty}\Big(1 -
 \tfrac{1}{2x}\Big)^{x}\cdot\Big(1-\tfrac{1}{2x}\Big)^{-1} = e^{-1/2}\cdot 1 = e
^{-1/2}.$$

Therefore

$$L = e^{-1/2}\cdot e^{1/2} = 1.$$

Since Stirling's approximation gives the ratio of $\Gamma$ to its asymptotic for
m tending to $1$, the $\sim$ may be replaced by equality in the limit, and the c
omputation is rigorous. $\blacksquare$
