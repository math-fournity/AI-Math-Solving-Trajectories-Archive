# Problem

A specialized vineyard is laid out in a grid of 10 rows and 15 columns, containing exactly 150 individual grapevines. At the start of the season, a farmer applies a permanent growth serum to $k$ specific vines.

The vineyard is monitored in small $2 \times 2$ clusters (any four adjacent vines forming a square). The vines are sensitive to their immediate neighbors: if at any point three vines in a $2 \times 2$ cluster are untreated (not containing the serum), the fourth vine in that cluster will lose its serum and wither due to lack of biological synergy. This process continues automatically until the system stabilizes.

It is determined that if the initial number of treated vines $k$ exceeds a specific threshold $N$, at least one vine is guaranteed to remain treated forever, no matter which specific $k$ vines the farmer initially chose. What is the value of $N$?
