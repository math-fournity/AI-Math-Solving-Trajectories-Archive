## Proof

Set $a_n := \sqrt{p_{n+1}}-\sqrt{p_n}\geq 0$ and $A_n := \sum_{k=1}^{n}a_k = \sq
rt{p_{n+1}}-\sqrt{2}$ (telescoping). By the Prime Number Theorem, $p_n\sim n\log
 n$, hence
$$A_n = \sqrt{p_{n+1}}-\sqrt{2}\sim \sqrt{n\log n}\qquad(n\to\infty). \tag{1}$$

**Abel summation.** For every $N\geq 1$,
$$\sum_{n=1}^{N}\frac{a_n}{n^b}=\frac{A_N}{N^b}+\sum_{n=1}^{N-1}A_n\!\left(\frac
{1}{n^b}-\frac{1}{(n+1)^b}\right). \tag{2}$$

We also need the asymptotic
$$\frac{1}{n^b}-\frac{1}{(n+1)^b}\sim \frac{b}{n^{b+1}}\qquad(n\to\infty), \tag{
3}$$
which follows from the mean value theorem applied to $x\mapsto x^{-b}$ (valid fo
r $b\neq 0$; the case $b=0$ is handled directly below).

---

### Case 1: $b>\tfrac12$ — convergence

*Boundary term.* By (1),
$$\frac{A_N}{N^b}\sim\frac{\sqrt{N\log N}}{N^b}=\frac{\sqrt{\log N}}{N^{\,b-1/2}
}\xrightarrow[N\to\infty]{}0,$$
since $b-\tfrac12>0$.

*Interior sum.* Using (1) and (3), the $n$-th summand satisfies
$$A_n\!\left(\frac{1}{n^b}-\frac{1}{(n+1)^b}\right)\sim \sqrt{n\log n}\cdot\frac
{b}{n^{b+1}}=\frac{b\,\sqrt{\log n}}{n^{\,b+1/2}}.$$
Since $b+\tfrac12>1$, pick $\varepsilon>0$ with $b+\tfrac12>1+\varepsilon$. For
large $n$, $\sqrt{\log n}\le n^{\varepsilon/2}\le n^{\varepsilon}$, so the summa
nd is $O\!\left(\frac{1}{n^{\,b+1/2-\varepsilon}}\right)$ with $b+\tfrac12-\vare
psilon>1$. Hence the interior series converges (all terms are non-negative for $
b>0$).

Both pieces in (2) have finite limits, so $\sum_{n\geq 1}\frac{a_n}{n^b}$ **conv
erges**.
