## Solution

**Goal:** Find the largest positive integer $n$ such that every positive divisor
 $d \mid n$ has $d-1$ or $d+1$ prime.

### Step 1: No prime $p \ge 5$ can divide $n$

If a prime $p \ge 5$ divides $n$, then $p$ is a divisor of $n$. But for any prim
e $p \ge 5$, both $p-1$ and $p+1$ are **even numbers $\ge 4$**, hence composite.
 So $p$ fails the integrity check.

Therefore the only possible prime factors of $n$ are $2$ and $3$, i.e. $n = 2^a
\cdot 3^b$ for some $a, b \ge 0$.

(Verification: $d=2 \Rightarrow 3$ prime ✓; $d=3 \Rightarrow 2$ prime ✓.)

### Step 2: Bound on $a$ — the exponent of $2$

Every divisor $2^i$ ($0 \le i \le a$) must be allowed. Check powers of $2$:

| $d$ | $d-1$ | $d+1$ | OK? |
|-----|-------|-------|-----|
| $1$ | — | $2$ ✓ | ✓ |
| $2$ | — | $3$ ✓ | ✓ |
| $4$ | $3$ ✓ | $5$ ✓ | ✓ |
| $8$ | $7$ ✓ | — | ✓ |
| $16$ | $15$ | $17$ ✓ | ✓ |
| $32$ | $31$ ✓ | — | ✓ |
| $64$ | $63=9\cdot7$ | $65=5\cdot13$ | **✗** |

Since $64 = 2^6$ fails, we need $a \le 5$.

### Step 3: Bound on $b$ — the exponent of $3$

Every divisor $3^j$ must be allowed. Check:
- $d=3$: $3-1=2$ prime ✓
- $d=9$: $9-1=8$ composite, $9+1=10$ composite → **✗**

Since $9 = 3^2$ fails, we need $b \le 1$.

### Step 4: Check all mixed divisors for $n = 2^5 \cdot 3 = 96$

With $a=5, b=1$, the divisors are $2^i$ and $2^i \cdot 3$ for $0 \le i \le 5$:

| $d$ | adjacent prime | $d$ | adjacent prime |
|-----|----------------|-----|----------------|
| $1$ | $2$ | $3$ | $2$ |
| $2$ | $3$ | $6$ | $5$ |
| $4$ | $3$ or $5$ | $12$ | $11$ |
| $8$ | $7$ | $24$ | $23$ |
| $16$ | $17$ | $48$ | $47$ |
| $32$ | $31$ | $96$ | $97$ (prime) |

All twelve divisors pass the check. ✓

### Step 5: Maximality

Since $n = 2^a \cdot 3^b$ with $a \le 5$ and $b \le 1$, the largest possible val
ue is
$$n = 2^5 \cdot 3^1 = 96.$$

Any larger value would require either $a \ge 6$ (introducing the forbidden divis
or $64$) or $b \ge 2$ (introducing the forbidden divisor $9$), or a prime factor
 $\ge 5$ (which is itself a forbidden divisor).

### Conclusion

The largest Shield-Resilient security level is

$$\boxed{96}$$
