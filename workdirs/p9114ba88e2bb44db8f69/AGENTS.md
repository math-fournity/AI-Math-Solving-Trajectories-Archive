# Problem

A specialized logistics hub processes various incoming shipments, each assigned a "priority weight" represented by an integer. The hub manager is designing a safety protocol involving a specific number of shipments, denoted as $n$, where $n \geq 9$. 

The protocol requires that for any collection of $n$ shipments (regardless of their individual priority weights), it must be possible to select a subset of exactly 9 shipments. Once selected, each of these 9 shipments is passed through one of two processing filters: a "Standard Filter" which quadruples the priority weight (multiplier of 4) or a "Premium Filter" which septuples the priority weight (multiplier of 7).

The protocol is successful if the sum of these 9 filtered weights is divisible by 9.

What is the smallest positive integer $n$ (where $n \geq 9$) that guarantees the manager can always find such a subset and assign a filter (either 4 or 7) to each selected shipment to satisfy the divisibility requirement?
