## Proof

Let $R' = R[x_1,\dots,x_n]/(r_1 x_1 + \cdots + r_n x_n - 1)$ and let $\varphi: R
 \to R'$ be the natural map, inducing $\varphi^*: \operatorname{Spec} R' \to \op
eratorname{Spec} R$.

**Claim.** The image of $\varphi^*$ is the open subset
$$D(r_1,\dots,r_n) := \{\mathfrak p \in \operatorname{Spec} R : (r_1,\dots,r_n)
\not\subseteq \mathfrak p\} = \operatorname{Spec} R \setminus V(r_1,\dots,r_n).$
$

**Proof.** A prime $\mathfrak p \in \operatorname{Spec} R$ lies in the image of
$\varphi^*$ if and only if there exists a prime $\mathfrak q \in \operatorname{S
pec} R'$ with $\mathfrak q \cap R = \mathfrak p$. By the fiber product descripti
on, this holds if and only if the fiber ring
$$R' \otimes_R \kappa(\mathfrak p) \neq 0,$$
where $\kappa(\mathfrak p) = R_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak p R_{\mathfrak p}$ is the
 residue field at $\mathfrak p$.

Computing this fiber:
$$R' \otimes_R \kappa(\mathfrak p) \cong \kappa(\mathfrak p)[x_1,\dots,x_n]\big/
\bigl(\bar r_1 x_1 + \cdots + \bar r_n x_n - 1\bigr),$$
where $\bar r_i$ denotes the image of $r_i$ in $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$.

Set $f = \bar r_1 x_1 + \cdots + \bar r_n x_n - 1 \in \kappa(\mathfrak p)[x_1,\d
ots,x_n]$. The quotient is the zero ring if and only if $(f) = (1)$, i.e., $f$ i
s a unit in the polynomial ring. A polynomial over a field is a unit if and only
 if it is a nonzero constant. The polynomial $f$ is constant if and only if $\ba
r r_1 = \cdots = \bar r_n = 0$, and in that case $f = -1$, which is indeed a non
zero constant (hence a unit) since $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ is a field (so $-1 \neq
 0$). Conversely, if any $\bar r_i \neq 0$, then $f$ is a nonconstant polynomial
, hence not a unit, so the quotient is nonzero.

Therefore:
$$R' \otimes_R \kappa(\mathfrak p) = 0 \iff \bar r_1 = \cdots = \bar r_n = 0 \if
f r_1, \dots, r_n \in \mathfrak p \iff (r_1,\dots,r_n) \subseteq \mathfrak p.$$

Taking the complement:
$$\mathfrak p \in \operatorname{im}(\varphi^*) \iff R' \otimes_R \kappa(\mathfra
k p) \neq 0 \iff (r_1,\dots,r_n) \not\subseteq \mathfrak p.$$

Hence
$$\operatorname{im}(\varphi^*) = \{\mathfrak p \in \operatorname{Spec} R : (r_1,
\dots,r_n) \not\subseteq \mathfrak p\} = D(r_1,\dots,r_n) = \operatorname{Spec}
R \setminus V(r_1,\dots,r_n). \qquad \blacksquare$$
