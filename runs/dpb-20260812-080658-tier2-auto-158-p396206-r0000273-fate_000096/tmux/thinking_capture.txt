Problem: Let $G$ be a group such that all subgroups of $G$ are normal in $G$. If
$a, b \in G$, prove that $b a = a^j b$ for some $j$.

No leaked answers. This is a standard problem about Dedekind groups (groups
where all subgroups are normal).

The key idea: Consider the subgroup generated by $a$, i.e., $\langle a \rangle$.
Since all subgroups are normal, $\langle a \rangle \trianglelefteq G$. Therefore
$b \langle a \rangle b^{-1} = \langle a \rangle$. In particular, $bab^{-1} \in
\langle a \rangle$, so $bab^{-1} = a^j$ for some integer $j$. Hence $ba = a^j
b$.

That's the complete proof.

设 $G$ 是一个所有子群都在 $G$ 中正规的群，$a, b \in G$。

考虑由 $a$ 生成的循环子群 $\langle a \rangle = {a^k : k \in \mathbb{Z}}$。由题

$$\langle a \rangle \trianglelefteq G.$$

$$b\langle a\rangle b^{-1} = \langle a\rangle.$$

因此 $bab^{-1} \in \langle a\rangle$，即存在整数 $j \in \mathbb{Z}$ 使得

$$bab^{-1} = a^{j}.$$

$$ba = a^{j}b.$$
