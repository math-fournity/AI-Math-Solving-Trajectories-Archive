$$g(z) = z^m e^b \prod_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(1 - \frac{z}{a_n}\right),$$

$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{|a_n|} < \infty. \tag{1}$$

If $g$ has no zeros, then $g = e^b$ is constant and both assertions are trivial.
 Henceforth assume $g$ has at least one nonzero zero. Set $r_n = |a_n|$, let $n(

**Key estimate: $n(r) = o(r)$.** Splitting at $\sqrt{r}$:

$$n(r) = \sum_{r_n \leq \sqrt{r}} 1 + \sum_{\sqrt{r} < r_n \leq r} 1 \leq \sqrt{
