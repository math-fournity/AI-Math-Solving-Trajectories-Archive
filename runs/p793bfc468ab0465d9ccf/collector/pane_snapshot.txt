Looking at this problem, I need to determine whether every Lie group G splits as
 a semidirect product G₀ ⋊ (G/G₀) where G₀ is the identity component (connected)
 and G/G₀ is discrete.

**Answer: FALSE.**

**Counterexample: Pin(2), the double cover of O(2).**

Pin(2) is a Lie group realized inside the Clifford algebra Cl(2), with generator
s e₁, e₂ satisfying eᵢ² = −1 and eᵢeⱼ = −eⱼeᵢ for i ≠ j. Its identity component
is Spin(2) ≅ S¹ (connected), and the component group is Pin(2)/Spin(2) ≅ Z/2 (di
screte).

**Claim:** The exact sequence 1 → Spin(2) → Pin(2) → Z/2 → 1 does **not** split.

**Proof of claim.** Elements of Pin(2) \ Spin(2) are odd elements of the form e₁
·a where a ∈ Spin(2). Writing a = cos(θ/2) + sin(θ/2)·e₁e₂, we compute:

$$e_1 \cdot a = \cos(\theta/2)\, e_1 - \sin(\theta/2)\, e_2.$$

Then, using e₁² = e₂² = −1 and e₁e₂ = −e₂e₁ (so (e₁e₂)² = −1):

$$(e_1 \cdot a)^2 = e_1 \cdot a \cdot e_1 \cdot a = \left(-\cos(\theta/2) + \sin
(\theta/2)\, e_1 e_2\right) \cdot a$$
$$= -\cos^2(\theta/2) + \sin^2(\theta/2)\,(e_1 e_2)^2 = -\cos^2(\theta/2) - \sin
^2(\theta/2) = -1.$$

Here −1 is the nontrivial central element lying in Spin(2) (the kernel of the co
vering map Pin(2) → O(2)). Therefore **every** element of Pin(2) \ Spin(2) has o
rder 4 (its square is −1 ≠ 1).

If Pin(2) ≅ Spin(2) ⋊ Z/2, there would exist a subgroup H ≅ Z/2 complementing Sp
in(2), requiring an element of order 2 in Pin(2) \ Spin(2). No such element exis
ts. Hence the extension does not split, and Pin(2) cannot be written as a semidi
rect product of its connected identity component and a discrete group. ∎

**On the "locally connected" clause:** Every Lie group is a smooth manifold, hen
ce locally Euclidean, hence locally connected. The condition is vacuous — but th
e answer is FALSE regardless, as the counterexample above shows.
