## Setup: Conjugate Pair

Let $\alpha = 5 + \sqrt{26}$ and $\beta = 5 - \sqrt{26}$.

Key properties:
- $\alpha + \beta = 10$, $\alpha\beta = 25 - 26 = -1$
- $\alpha, \beta$ are roots of $x^2 - 10x - 1 = 0$
- $|\beta| = \sqrt{26} - 5 \approx 0.09902 < 1$

## Integer Sequence

Define $S_n = \alpha^n + \beta^n$. From the characteristic equation, $S_n$ satis
fies:
$$S_n = 10\,S_{n-1} + S_{n-2}, \quad S_0 = 2,\; S_1 = 10$$

By induction, **$S_n$ is an integer for all $n$**.

## Fractional Part of $\alpha^{2019}$

Since $\alpha^n = S_n - \beta^n$:

- 2019 is **odd**, and $\beta < 0$, so $\beta^{2019} < 0$.
- Therefore $\alpha^{2019} = S_{2019} - \beta^{2019} = S_{2019} + |\beta|^{2019}
$.

Since $0 < |\beta| < 1$, we have $0 < |\beta|^{2019} < 1$, so:

$$\alpha^{2019} = S_{2019} + |\beta|^{2019}, \quad \text{fractional part} = |\be
ta|^{2019}.$$

## Size of the Fractional Part

Compute $\log_{10}|\beta|$:
$$|\beta| = \sqrt{26} - 5 \approx 0.0990195$$
$$\log_{10}|\beta| \approx -1.00436$$

Therefore:
$$|\beta|^{2019} \approx 10^{-1.00436 \times 2019} \approx 10^{-2027.8} \approx
1.6 \times 10^{-2028}$$

This means the decimal expansion of the fractional part begins with approximatel
