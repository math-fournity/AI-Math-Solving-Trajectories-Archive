## Lower bound: m ≥ 2

**One operation cannot suffice.** Starting from 111 crates all of weight 1, a si
ngle operation selects some subset of size *s*, partitions it into groups of equ
al size *k* (≥ 2 items per group, ≥ 2 groups), and fuses each group into a conta
iner of weight *k*. The unselected crates remain weight 1. Thus after one operat
ion, the dock contains only items of weight 1 and weight *k* — at most **2 disti
nct weights**. Since we need 11 distinct weights, **m ≥ 2**.
