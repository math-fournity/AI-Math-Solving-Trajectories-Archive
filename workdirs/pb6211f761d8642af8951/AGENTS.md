# Problem

A specialized waste disposal facility is tasked with processing 50 tons of hazardous chemical runoff. The runoff is stored in several individual canisters, and while the exact weight of each canister varies, none exceeds 1 ton.

The facility operates under strict safety protocols for each disposal cycle. During a single cycle, the disposal unit can process a maximum of 5 tons of chemicals. A safety regulation dictates that once a canister is unsealed and loaded into the unit, it must be completely processed within that same cycle; canisters cannot be left partially full. Consequently, the operators will never open a new canister if adding its contents to the current cycle’s load would cause the total weight to exceed the 5-ton limit.

What is the minimum number of disposal cycles that will definitely be sufficient to process all 50 tons of the chemical runoff, regardless of how the weights are distributed among the canisters?
