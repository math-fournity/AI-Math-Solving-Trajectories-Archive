# Problem

A specialized aerospace logistics company designs triangular support trusses for deep-space satellites. The three structural struts of a truss are designated as $a$, $b$, and $c$, and their lengths must all be positive integers.

One specific design requirement for stability is that the length of a internal reinforcing cable—which originates from the joint between struts $b$ and $c$ and bisects the angle formed by them—must be exactly equal to the length of strut $b$. 

The company classifies these trusses based on their total material footprint. For a truss to be viable for the upcoming mission, the total perimeter (the sum of the lengths of the three struts $a$, $b$, and $c$) must be exactly $10$ times a prime number $p$.

How many unique integer-sided trusses satisfy these design constraints?
