## Solution

**Setup and key identity.** Recall the factorization
$$x^3+y^3+z^3-3xyz = (x+y+z)\bigl(x^2+y^2+z^2-xy-yz-zx\bigr) = (x+y+z)\cdot\tfra
c{(x-y)^2+(y-z)^2+(z-x)^2}{2}.$$

Let $s=x+y+z$ and set $a=(x-y)^2,\;b=(y-z)^2,\;c=(z-x)^2$. Since $x^2+y^2+z^2=1$
,
$$a+b+c = 2(1-xy-yz-zx) = 2\!\left(1-\tfrac{s^2-1}{2}\right)=3-s^2.$$

**Expressing $I$ and $F$.** Writing $u=x-y,\;v=y-z$ so $z-x=-(u+v)$:
$$ab+bc+ca = u^2v^2+v^2(u+v)^2+u^2(u+v)^2 = (u^2+uv+v^2)^2 = \left(\tfrac{a+b+c}
{2}\right)^2=\frac{(3-s^2)^2}{4}.$$
Also $abc=\prod(x-y)^2=:\Delta$ (the discriminant). Hence
$$I=\frac{ab+bc+ca}{abc}=\frac{(3-s^2)^2/4}{\Delta},\qquad F=\left[\frac{s(3-s^2
)}{2}\right]^2=\frac{s^2(3-s^2)^2}{4}.$$
Therefore
$$\frac{I}{F}=\frac{1}{s^2\,\Delta}=\frac{1}{D},\qquad D:=s^2\prod(x-y)^2.$$
So $M=\inf I/F = 1/\max D$, and we must **maximize $D=s^2\Delta$** subject to $x
^2+y^2+z^2=1$.

**Maximizing $\Delta$ for fixed $s$.** With $p=xy+yz+zx=(s^2-1)/2$ fixed and $q=
xyz$ free, the discriminant of the cubic with roots $x,y,z$ is
$$\Delta=s^2p^2-4p^3-4s^3q-27q^2+18spq,$$
a concave quadratic in $q$. Its vertex $q^*=\frac{s(9p-2s^2)}{27}=\frac{s(5s^2-9
)}{54}$ gives the maximum
$$\Delta_{\max}=s^2p^2-4p^3+27(q^*)^2.$$
Setting $t=s^2$ and substituting $p=(t-1)/2$, $q^*=s(5t-9)/54$:
$$\Delta_{\max}(t)=\frac{27t(t-1)^2-54(t-1)^3+t(5t-9)^2}{108}=\frac{-2(t-3)^3}{1
08}=\frac{(3-t)^3}{54}.$$
(One checks $\Delta_{\max}\ge 0$ for $t\in[0,3]$, so real distinct roots exist —
 the extremal triple is valid.)

**Maximizing $D(t)=t\,\Delta_{\max}(t)=\dfrac{t(3-t)^3}{54}$.** Let $g(t)=t(3-t)
^3$ on $[0,3]$:
$$g'(t)=(3-t)^2(3-4t)=0\implies t=\tfrac34\quad(\text{interior}).$$
Then
$$g\!\left(\tfrac34\right)=\tfrac34\cdot\left(\tfrac94\right)^3=\tfrac34\cdot\tf
rac{729}{64}=\tfrac{2187}{256},\qquad D_{\max}=\frac{2187/256}{54}=\frac{2187}{1
3824}=\frac{81}{512}.$$

**Boundary check.** At $t=0$ ($s=0$) and $t=3$ ($x=y=z$), $D=0$, so $I/F\to+\inf
ty$; the infimum is the interior value. The $z=0$ subfamily gives $D\le 1/16<81/
512$, confirming the global max is at $t=3/4$.

**Conclusion.** The largest constant ensuring $I\ge M\cdot F$ for all valid posi
tions is
$$\boxed{M=\frac{1}{D_{\max}}=\frac{512}{81}}.$$
