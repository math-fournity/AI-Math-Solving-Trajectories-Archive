## Setup and Key Facts

Let $p$ be a prime and $\zeta_p$ a primitive $p$-th root of unity. The $p$-th cy
clotomic field $K = \mathbb{Q}[\zeta_p]$ has degree $p - 1$ over $\mathbb{Q}$, w
ith Galois group $\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q}) \cong (\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}
)^{\times}$, cyclic of order $p - 1$.

**Complex conjugation** acts on $K$ via $\zeta_p \mapsto \zeta_p^{-1}$, correspo
nding to the element $-1 \in (\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})^{\times}$, which has order
 2.

## The Totally Real Subfield

The **maximal totally real subfield** is the fixed field of complex conjugation:
$$K^+ = \mathbb{Q}[\zeta_p + \zeta_p^{-1}] \subseteq K.$$

By the Galois correspondence, $[K^+ : \mathbb{Q}] = (p-1)/2$.

Since $K = \mathbb{Q}[\zeta_p]$ is a **CM field** (a totally imaginary quadratic
 extension of a totally real field), its maximal totally real subfield $K^+$ is
**totally real**: every embedding of $K^+$ into $\mathbb{C}$ has image contained
 in $\mathbb{R}$.

## Orderings and Real Embeddings

For a number field $F$, the **orderings** (as an ordered field, i.e., total orde
rs compatible with the field structure) are in bijection with the **real embeddi
ngs** $F \hookrightarrow \mathbb{R}$. This is a standard result in the theory of
 formally real fields (Artin–Schreier theory): each real embedding $\sigma: F \t
o \mathbb{R}$ defines an ordering via $a < b \iff \sigma(a) < \sigma(b)$, and co
nversely every ordering arises this way.

## Counting

Since $K^+$ is totally real of degree $(p-1)/2$ over $\mathbb{Q}$, all $(p-1)/2$
 embeddings into $\mathbb{C}$ are real embeddings. Therefore:

$$\text{Number of orderings of } K^+ = \text{Number of real embeddings of } K^+
= \frac{p-1}{2}.$$

## Verification with small primes

- $p = 3$: $K^+ = \mathbb{Q}[\zeta_3 + \zeta_3^{-1}] = \mathbb{Q}[-1] = \mathbb{
Q}$, degree 1, one ordering. ✓ $(3-1)/2 = 1$.
- $p = 5$: $K^+ = \mathbb{Q}[\zeta_5 + \zeta_5^{-1}]$, degree 2, totally real (e
.g., $\zeta_5 + \zeta_5^{-1} = 2\cos(2\pi/5) \approx 0.618$), two orderings. ✓ $
(5-1)/2 = 2$.
- $p = 7$: degree 3, totally real, three orderings. ✓ $(7-1)/2 = 3$.

## Answer

The number of orderings of the totally real subfield of the $p$-th cyclotomic fi
eld is:

$$\boxed{\dfrac{p-1}{2}}$$
