## Solution

**Setup.** Let the cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$ have sides $a=AB=3$, $b=BC=5$, $c
=CD=8$, $d=DA=5$.

**Area via Brahmagupta's formula.** The semiperimeter is
$$s=\frac{3+5+8+5}{2}=\frac{21}{2}.$$
Then
$$K=\sqrt{(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)(s-d)}=\sqrt{\tfrac{15}{2}\cdot\tfrac{11}{2}\cdot\tfrac
{5}{2}\cdot\tfrac{11}{2}}=\frac{1}{4}\sqrt{15\cdot 121\cdot 5}=\frac{55\sqrt{3}}
{4}.$$

**Circumradius formula.** For a cyclic quadrilateral with sides $a,b,c,d$ and ar
ea $K$,
$$R=\frac{1}{4K}\sqrt{(ab+cd)(ac+bd)(ad+bc)}.$$
(This generalizes the triangle formula $R=\frac{abc}{4K}$, recovered by letting
one side tend to $0$.)

Compute the three factors:
- $ab+cd = 3\cdot5+8\cdot5 = 15+40 = 55$
- $ac+bd = 3\cdot8+5\cdot5 = 24+25 = 49$
- $ad+bc = 3\cdot5+5\cdot8 = 15+40 = 55$

Hence
$$\sqrt{(ab+cd)(ac+bd)(ad+bc)}=\sqrt{55\cdot49\cdot55}=55\cdot7=385.$$

Therefore
$$R=\frac{385}{4\cdot\frac{55\sqrt{3}}{4}}=\frac{385}{55\sqrt{3}}=\frac{7}{\sqrt
{3}}=\frac{7\sqrt{3}}{3}.$$

**Final form.** This is $\frac{a\sqrt{b}}{c}$ with $a=7$, $b=3$, $c=3$, where $\
gcd(a,c)=\gcd(7,3)=1$ and $b=3$ is squarefree. Thus
$$a+b+c = 7+3+3 = \boxed{13}.$$
