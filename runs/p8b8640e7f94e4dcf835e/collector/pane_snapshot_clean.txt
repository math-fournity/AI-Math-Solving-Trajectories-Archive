## Solution

**Setup.** Triangle $ABC$ with $AB=13$, $BC=14$, $CA=15$. By the Law of Cosines
at $A$:
$$\cos A = \frac{13^2+15^2-14^2}{2\cdot 13\cdot 15} = \frac{198}{390} = \frac{33
}{65}, \qquad \sin A = \frac{56}{65}.$$

Place $A=(0,0)$, $B=(13,0)$, so $C = (15\cos A, 15\sin A) = \left(\tfrac{99}{13}
, \tfrac{168}{13}\right)$.

**Feet of perpendiculars.** Since $AB$ is the $x$-axis, $F = \left(\tfrac{99}{13
}, 0\right)$. Projecting $B$ onto $\overrightarrow{AC}$: $AE = B\cdot \hat{AC}\c
dot|\text{-proj}|$; using $B\cdot C = 99$ and $|C|^2=225$ gives $E = \tfrac{99}{
225}C = \left(\tfrac{1089}{325}, \tfrac{1848}{325}\right)$.

**Circle $\omega$ through $A,E,F$.** With $A$ at the origin, write $x^2+y^2+Dx+E
_0 y=0$.
- From $F$: $\left(\tfrac{99}{13}\right)^2 + D\cdot\tfrac{99}{13}=0 \Rightarrow
D = -\tfrac{99}{13}$.
- From $E$ (using $|E|^2 = AE^2 = \tfrac{1089}{25}$): $\tfrac{1089}{25} - \tfrac
{99}{13}\cdot\tfrac{1089}{325} + E_0\cdot\tfrac{1848}{325}=0 \Rightarrow E_0 = -
\tfrac{165}{52}$.
