## Proof

We evaluate
$$I=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{e^{px}-e^{qx}}{1-e^{x}}\,dx,\qquad 0<p,q<1.$$

### 1. Convergence and substitution

As $x\to+\infty$: $\frac{e^{px}-e^{qx}}{1-e^x}\sim\frac{e^{px}-e^{qx}}{-e^x}=-e^
{(p-1)x}+e^{(q-1)x}\to0$ since $p,q<1$.

As $x\to-\infty$: $e^{px}\approx1+px$, $e^{qx}\approx1+qx$, $1-e^x\approx -x$, s
o the integrand $\to q-p$ (finite). Near $x=0$: $e^{px}-e^{qx}\approx(p-q)x$, $1
-e^x\approx-x$, so the integrand $\to q-p$; the singularity is removable. Hence
$I$ is a proper convergent integral.

Set $t=e^x$, $dx=dt/t$:
$$I=\int_0^\infty\frac{t^{p-1}-t^{q-1}}{1-t}\,dt=J(p)-J(q),\qquad J(s):=\operato
rname{PV}\int_0^\infty\frac{t^{s-1}}{1-t}\,dt.$$

(The apparent singularity at $t=1$ is removable in the original variable, so the
 PV equals the ordinary value.)

### 2. Contour evaluation of $J(s)$

Fix $s\in(0,1)$. Take $F(z)=\dfrac{z^{s-1}}{1-z}$ with branch cut on the positiv
e real axis and $\arg z\in(0,2\pi)$, so
$$z^{s-1}=e^{(s-1)\log z},\qquad \log z=\ln|z|+i\arg z.$$

Use the **keyhole contour** $\Gamma$ consisting of (counterclockwise):
- $\gamma_1$: the upper edge of the cut, $z=t+i0^+$, from $\varepsilon$ to $1-\d
elta$, then a small semicircle $C_1^+$ above $z=1$ ($z=1+\delta e^{i\theta}$, $\
theta:\pi\to0$), then from $1+\delta$ to $R$;
- $\gamma_2$: the large circle $C_R$ ($|z|=R$, $\theta:0\to2\pi$);
- $\gamma_3$: the lower edge of the cut, $z=t-i0^+$ ($\arg z=2\pi^-$), from $R$
to $1+\delta$, then a small semicircle $C_1^-$ below $z=1$ ($\theta:2\pi\to\pi$)
, then from $1-\delta$ to $\varepsilon$;
- $\gamma_4$: the small circle $C_\varepsilon$ around the origin ($|z|=\varepsil
on$, clockwise).

The pole $z=1$ is excluded by the indentation; the branch point $z=0$ is exclude
d by $C_\varepsilon$. No singularity lies inside $\Gamma$, so
$$\oint_\Gamma F(z)\,dz=0.$$

**Upper edge** ($\arg z=0^+$, $z^{s-1}=t^{s-1}$): as $\varepsilon\to0$, $\delta\
to0$, $R\to\infty$,
$$\int_{\gamma_1\setminus C_1^+}F\,dz\;\longrightarrow\; J(s).$$

**Lower edge** ($\arg z=2\pi^-$, $z^{s-1}=t^{s-1}e^{2\pi i(s-1)}=t^{s-1}e^{2\pi
i s}$), traversed right-to-left:
$$\int_{\gamma_3\setminus C_1^-}F\,dz\;\longrightarrow\; -e^{2\pi i s}\,J(s).$$

**Large circle** $C_R$: $|F(z)|=O(R^{s-2})$, length $2\pi R$, so the integral is
 $O(R^{s-1})\to0$ since $s<1$.

**Small circle** $C_\varepsilon$: $|F(z)|=O(\varepsilon^{s-1})$, length $2\pi\va
repsilon$, so the integral is $O(\varepsilon^s)\to0$ since $s>0$.

**Indentation semicircles around $z=1$.** On both, $\frac{1}{1-z}=\frac{-1}{\del
ta e^{i\theta}}$, $dz=i\delta e^{i\theta}\,d\theta$, so the integrand $\approx -
i\,z^{s-1}$.

- Upper semicircle $C_1^+$ ($\theta:\pi\to0$, $\arg z\approx0$, $z^{s-1}\to1$):
$$\int_{\pi}^{0}(-i)\,d\theta=i\pi.$$
- Lower semicircle $C_1^-$ ($\theta:2\pi\to\pi$, $\arg z\approx2\pi$, $z^{s-1}\t
o e^{2\pi i s}$):
$$\int_{2\pi}^{\pi}(-i)\,e^{2\pi i s}\,d\theta=i\pi\,e^{2\pi i s}.$$

Total indentation contribution: $i\pi(1+e^{2\pi i s})$.

### 3. Solving for $J(s)$

Assembling all pieces:
$$(1-e^{2\pi i s})\,J(s)+i\pi(1+e^{2\pi i s})=0,$$
$$J(s)=\frac{-i\pi(1+e^{2\pi i s})}{1-e^{2\pi i s}}.$$

Using $1+e^{2\pi i s}=e^{i\pi s}(e^{-i\pi s}+e^{i\pi s})=2e^{i\pi s}\cos\pi s$ a
nd $1-e^{2\pi i s}=-2ie^{i\pi s}\sin\pi s$:
$$\frac{1+e^{2\pi i s}}{1-e^{2\pi i s}}=\frac{2e^{i\pi s}\cos\pi s}{-2ie^{i\pi s
}\sin\pi s}=i\cot\pi s.$$

Therefore
$$J(s)=-i\pi\cdot i\cot\pi s=\pi\cot(\pi s).$$

### 4. Final result

$$I=J(p)-J(q)=\pi\cot(\pi p)-\pi\cot(\pi q),$$

i.e.,
$$\boxed{\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{e^{px}-e^{qx}}{1-e^{x}}\,dx=\
pi\bigl[\cot(\pi p)-\cot(\pi q)\bigr].}$$

**Sanity checks.** If $p=q$, both sides vanish. For $p=\tfrac14,q=\tfrac34$: $\c
ot\frac{\pi}{4}-\cot\frac{3\pi}{4}=1-(-1)=2$, giving $I=2\pi$, consistent with t
he integrand being odd about $x=0$ under $p\leftrightarrow q$ symmetry. For $p=\
tfrac12,q=\tfrac14$: $\cot\frac{\pi}{2}-\cot\frac{\pi}{4}=0-1=-1$, giving $I=-\p
i$; the integrand is then pointwise negative (verified by sign analysis on $(0,1
)$ and $(1,\infty)$ after the $t$-substitution), confirming the sign.
