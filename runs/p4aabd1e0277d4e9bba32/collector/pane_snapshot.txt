**Step 1: The conflict graph is perfect.**

Consider the complement graph $\overline{G_n}$, where $x, y$ (with $x > y$) are
adjacent iff $31x > 32y$. Orient each edge from the larger vertex to the smaller

**Transitivity:** Suppose $x \to y$ and $y \to z$, so $x > y > z$ with $31x > 32

$$31x > 32y > 32 \cdot \frac{32z}{31} = \frac{1024z}{31}$$

This transitive orientation makes $\overline{G_n}$ a **comparability graph**. By
 Lovász's Perfect Graph Theorem, the complement of a perfect graph is perfect, a

Since $G_n$ is perfect, $\chi(G_n) = \omega(G_n)$ (chromatic number equals maxim
