## Proof

**Claim.** $R' \to S'$ is quasi-finite at $\mathfrak{q}'$.

**Setup and notation.** We have a commutative diagram

$$\begin{CD}
S @>>> S \otimes_R R' @>>> S' \\
@AAA @AAA @AAA \\
R @= R @>>> R'
\end{CD}$$

where the map $S \otimes_R R' \to S'$ is surjective, so $S' \cong (S \otimes_R R
')/I$ for some ideal $I$. Let $\mathfrak{q}' \subset S'$ contract to $\mathfrak{
q} \subset S$ and to $\mathfrak{p}' \subset R'$. By commutativity, $\mathfrak{p}
'$ contracts to $\mathfrak{p}$ in $R$, and $\mathfrak{q}$ contracts to $\mathfra
k{p}$ in $R$.

**Step 1: Finite type.** Since $R \to S$ is of finite type, $R' \to S \otimes_R
R'$ is of finite type (base change). Since $S \otimes_R R' \to S'$ is surjective
, $R' \to S'$ is of finite type. ✓

**Step 2: The fiber of $S'/R'$ at $\mathfrak{p}'$ is a quotient of a base change
 of the fiber of $S/R$ at $\mathfrak{p}$.** The fiber of $S'$ over $\mathfrak{p}
'$ is

$$S' \otimes_{R'} \kappa(\mathfrak{p}') \cong \bigl(S \otimes_R R'\bigr) \otimes
_{R'} \kappa(\mathfrak{p}') \big/ \bar{I} \cong \bigl(S \otimes_R \kappa(\mathfr
ak{p}')\bigr) \big/ \bar{I},$$

where $\bar{I}$ is the image of $I$ in $S \otimes_R \kappa(\mathfrak{p}')$. Sinc
e $R \to R' \to \kappa(\mathfrak{p}')$ factors through $\kappa(\mathfrak{p})$ (a
s $\mathfrak{p}' \cap R = \mathfrak{p}$),

$$S \otimes_R \kappa(\mathfrak{p}') \cong S \otimes_R \kappa(\mathfrak{p}) \otim
es_{\kappa(\mathfrak{p})} \kappa(\mathfrak{p}').$$

Therefore

$$S' \otimes_{R'} \kappa(\mathfrak{p}') \cong \bigl(S \otimes_R \kappa(\mathfrak
{p}) \otimes_{\kappa(\mathfrak{p})} \kappa(\mathfrak{p}')\bigr) \big/ \bar{I}. \
tag{$\ast$}$$

**Step 3: The local ring of the fiber at $\mathfrak{q}'$ is finite over $\kappa(
\mathfrak{p}')$.** By hypothesis, $R \to S$ is quasi-finite at $\mathfrak{q}$, w
hich means

$$\bigl(S \otimes_R \kappa(\mathfrak{p})\bigr)_{\mathfrak{q}}$$

is a finite $\kappa(\mathfrak{p})$-algebra (equivalently, a finite-dimensional $
\kappa(\mathfrak{p})$-vector space). Base-changing to $\kappa(\mathfrak{p}')$:

$$\bigl(S \otimes_R \kappa(\mathfrak{p})\bigr)_{\mathfrak{q}} \otimes_{\kappa(\m
athfrak{p})} \kappa(\mathfrak{p}')$$

is a finite $\kappa(\mathfrak{p}')$-algebra, hence Artinian with finitely many p
rimes.

The prime $\mathfrak{q}'$ of $S'$ corresponds (via $(\ast)$) to a prime of $S \o
times_R \kappa(\mathfrak{p}) \otimes_{\kappa(\mathfrak{p})} \kappa(\mathfrak{p}'
)$ lying over $\mathfrak{q}$ in $\operatorname{Spec}(S \otimes_R \kappa(\mathfra
k{p}))$. The local ring of $S \otimes_R \kappa(\mathfrak{p}) \otimes_{\kappa(\ma
thfrak{p})} \kappa(\mathfrak{p}')$ at this prime is a localization of the finite
 $\kappa(\mathfrak{p}')$-algebra $\bigl(S \otimes_R \kappa(\mathfrak{p})\bigr)_{
\mathfrak{q}} \otimes_{\kappa(\mathfrak{p})} \kappa(\mathfrak{p}')$. Since a fin
ite algebra over a field is Artinian, any localization of it is a quotient (Arti
nian rings have finitely many primes, and localization at a prime selects one lo
cal factor), hence remains a finite $\kappa(\mathfrak{p}')$-algebra.

By $(\ast)$, the local ring $\bigl(S' \otimes_{R'} \kappa(\mathfrak{p}')\bigr)_{
\mathfrak{q}'}$ is a further quotient of this finite $\kappa(\mathfrak{p}')$-alg
ebra, hence is itself a finite $\kappa(\mathfrak{p}')$-algebra.

**Conclusion.** The map $R' \to S'$ is of finite type, and the local ring of the
 fiber at $\mathfrak{q}'$,

$$\bigl(S' \otimes_{R'} \kappa(\mathfrak{p}')\bigr)_{\mathfrak{q}'},$$

is a finite $\kappa(\mathfrak{p}')$-algebra. Therefore $R' \to S'$ is **quasi-fi
nite at $\mathfrak{q}'$**. $\blacksquare$
