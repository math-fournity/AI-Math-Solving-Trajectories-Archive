ent coefficient $a_{-1}$ equals
$$a_{-1} = \frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_{|z|=\rho} f(z)\,dz$$
for any $\rho \in (r, R)$. By the residue theorem, this integral equals the sum
of residues of $f$ at all poles inside $|z| < \rho$.

**Poles of $\cot(\pi z)$.** The function $\cot(\pi z) = \cos(\pi z)/\sin(\pi z)$
 has simple poles at every integer $z = n$, $n \in \mathbb{Z}$, where $\sin(\pi
z) = 0$.

**The annulus is $1 < |z| < 2$.** Choosing any $\rho \in (1, 2)$, the poles insi
de $|z| < \rho$ are exactly $z = 0, 1, -1$ (the integers with $|n| \leq 1$). The
 poles at $z = \pm 2, \pm 3, \ldots$ lie outside $|z| < \rho$ since $\rho < 2$.

**Residue at each integer.** Near $z = n$, we have $\sin(\pi z) \approx \pi(-1)^
n(z - n)$ and $\cos(\pi z) \approx (-1)^n$, so
$$\cot(\pi z) \approx \frac{(-1)^n}{\pi(-1)^n(z-n)} = \frac{1}{\pi(z-n)}.$$
Thus
$$\operatorname{Res}(\cot(\pi z),\, z=n) = \frac{1}{\pi}$$
for every integer $n$.

$$a_{-1} = \sum_{\substack{n \in \mathbb{Z} \\ |n| < \rho}} \operatorname{Res}(\
cot(\pi z),\, z=n) = \operatorname{Res}_{z=0} + \operatorname{Res}_{z=1} + \oper

\pi}.$$
