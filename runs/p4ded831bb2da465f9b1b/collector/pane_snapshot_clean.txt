Looking at this problem, I need to find the maximum total sum $S$ of a $2012 \ti
mes 2012$ grid with values in $[0,1]$, such that every single grid-line cut (hor
izontal or vertical) produces two rectangles where at least one has sum $\le 1$.

## Setting Up the Structure

Let $n = 2012$, and let $R_i$ be the sum of row $i$, $C_j$ the sum of column $j$
. Define prefix sums $P_k = \sum_{i=1}^k R_i$ and $Q_k = \sum_{j=1}^k C_j$, with
 $P_0 = Q_0 = 0$ and $P_n = Q_n = S$.

**Horizontal cut at row $k$** produces sums $P_k$ and $S - P_k$. The condition r
equires $\min(P_k, S - P_k) \le 1$ for all $k = 1, \ldots, n-1$, i.e., $P_k \le
1$ or $P_k \ge S - 1$.

Since $R_i \ge 0$, the $P_k$ are non-decreasing. For $S > 2$, the intervals $(-\
infty, 1]$ and $[S-1, \infty)$ are disjoint, so there exists a threshold $m$ wit
h:
- $P_k \le 1$ for $k \le m$, and $P_k \ge S - 1$ for $k \ge m+1$ (with $k \le n-
1$).

At the transition: $P_m \le 1$ and $P_{m+1} \ge S-1$, so **row $m+1$ has sum** $
R_{m+1} \ge (S-1) - 1 = S - 2$.

By identical reasoning on columns, there exists a **big column** $m'+1$ with sum
 $C_{m'+1} \ge S - 2$.

## Upper Bound: $S \le 5$

Let $r = m+1$ (big row) and $c = m'+1$ (big column). Then:

$$R_r + C_c = a_{r,c} + \sum_{\substack{(i,j):\\ i=r \text{ or } j=c}} a_{i,j}$$

The union of row $r$ and column $c$ is a subset of all cells, so its sum is $\le
 S$. Also $a_{r,c} \le 1$. Therefore:

$$R_r + C_c \le 1 + S$$

Since $R_r \ge S - 2$ and $C_c \ge S - 2$:

$$2(S - 2) \le R_r + C_c \le 1 + S \implies 2S - 4 \le S + 1 \implies S \le 5$$

## Construction Achieving $S = 5$

Take $m = 1$, $m' = 1$ (big row = row 2, big column = column 2). Set exactly fiv
e cells to $1$ and all others to $0$:

| Cell | Value |
|------|-------|
| $(1, 2)$ | $1$ |
| $(2, 1)$ | $1$ |
| $(2, 2)$ | $1$ |
| $(2, 3)$ | $1$ |
| $(3, 2)$ | $1$ |

All other cells $= 0$. Total $S = 5$.

**Verification of row conditions** ($P_k \le 1$ or $P_k \ge 4$):
- $P_1 = R_1 = 1 \le 1$ ✓
- $P_2 = 1 + 3 = 4 \ge 4$ ✓
- $P_k = 5 \ge 4$ for $k \ge 3$ ✓

**Verification of column conditions** ($Q_k \le 1$ or $Q_k \ge 4$):
- $Q_1 = C_1 = 1 \le 1$ ✓
- $Q_2 = 1 + 3 = 4 \ge 4$ ✓
- $Q_k = 5 \ge 4$ for $k \ge 3$ ✓

**Spot-checking cuts:**
- Cut at $k=1$: sums $1$ and $4$ → min $= 1 \le 1$ ✓
- Cut at $k=2$: sums $4$ and $1$ → min $= 1 \le 1$ ✓
- Cut at $k=3$: sums $5$ and $0$ → min $= 0 \le 1$ ✓
- Cut at $k \ge 4$: sums $5$ and $0$ → min $= 0 \le 1$ ✓
- All vertical cuts symmetric ✓

All values lie in $[0,1]$, and every valid cut satisfies the Nutrient Saturation
 Rule.

## Conclusion

The maximum possible total sum is $\boxed{5}$.
