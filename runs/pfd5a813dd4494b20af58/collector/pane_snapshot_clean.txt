## Solution

**Setup.** Place the station center $O$ at the origin with the circle $x^2+y^2=1
$. Since line $AC$ passes through $O$, it is a diameter. Set $A=(-1,0)$ and $C=(
1,0)$. Point $P$ lies on $AC$ with $PC=2/5$, so $P=(3/5,\,0)$.

Let $B=(\cos\alpha,\sin\alpha)$ and $D=(\cos\beta,\sin\beta)$ on the unit circle
, with chord $BD$ passing through $P$.

**Constraint 1: $BD = AB$.**

$$AB^2 = (\cos\alpha+1)^2+\sin^2\alpha = 2+2\cos\alpha,$$

$$BD^2 = (\cos\alpha-\cos\beta)^2+(\sin\alpha-\sin\beta)^2 = 2-2\cos(\alpha-\bet
a).$$

Setting $AB^2=BD^2$:

$$\cos(\alpha-\beta) = -\cos\alpha. \quad\text{(eq 2)}$$

**Constraint 2: $P$ lies on segment $BD$.** Using the collinearity condition $P
= B + t(D-B)$ and eliminating $t$:

$$\frac{3}{5} = \frac{\sin(\alpha-\beta)}{\sin\alpha - \sin\beta}.$$

Applying sum-to-product identities with $u=\tfrac{\alpha+\beta}{2}$, $v=\tfrac{\
alpha-\beta}{2}$:

$$\frac{3}{5} = \frac{\cos v}{\cos u}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \cos u = \tfrac{5
}{3}\cos v. \quad\text{(eq 1)}$$

**Solving the system.** From (eq 2): $\cos(2v) = -\cos(u+v)$, i.e.

$$1-2\cos^2 v = -\cos(u+v).$$

Expanding $\cos(u+v)=\cos u\cos v - \sin u\sin v$ and substituting $\cos u = \tf
rac{5}{3}\cos v$ (let $c=\cos v$):

$$\tfrac{5c^2}{3} - \sin u\sin v = 1 - 2c^2 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \sin u\sin
 v = \tfrac{11c^2}{3}-1.$$

Squaring and using $\sin^2 u = 1-\tfrac{25c^2}{9}$, $\sin^2 v = 1-c^2$:

$$\left(1-\tfrac{25c^2}{9}\right)(1-c^2) = \left(\tfrac{11c^2}{3}-1\right)^2.$$

Expanding both sides:

$$1 - \tfrac{34c^2}{9} + \tfrac{25c^4}{9} = 1 - \tfrac{66c^2}{9} + \tfrac{121c^4
}{9}.$$

$$32c^2 = 96c^4 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad c^2 = \tfrac{1}{3},\quad \cos v = \tfr
ac{1}{\sqrt{3}}.$$

Then $\cos u = \tfrac{5}{3\sqrt{3}}$, and

$$\sin u\sin v = \tfrac{11}{9}-1 = \tfrac{2}{9},\qquad \cos u\cos v = \tfrac{5}{
3\sqrt{3}}\cdot\tfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \tfrac{5}{9}.$$

**Computing $CD$.** Since $CD^2 = 2 - 2\cos\beta = 2 - 2\cos(u-v)$:

$$\cos(u-v) = \cos u\cos v + \sin u\sin v = \tfrac{5}{9}+\tfrac{2}{9} = \tfrac{7
}{9}.$$

$$CD^2 = 2 - 2\cdot\tfrac{7}{9} = \tfrac{4}{9}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad CD = \tf
rac{2}{3}.$$

**Verification.** With $u\approx 15.8°$, $v\approx 54.7°$: $\alpha\approx 70.5°$
, $\beta\approx -39.0°$. One checks $P=(0.6,0)$ lies between $B$ and $D$ (parame
ter $t=0.6\in[0,1]$), $AB=BD\approx 1.633$, and $PC=0.4=2/5$. All constraints sa
tisfied.

## Answer

The distance between tower Charlie and tower Delta is $\boxed{\dfrac{2}{3}}$ kil
ometers.
