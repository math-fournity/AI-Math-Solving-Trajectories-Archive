$$\prod_{b=1}^{n}(1+\omega^{ab}) = \left(\prod_{j=0}^{m-1}\left(1+e^{2\pi i j/m}

$$(-1)^m - 1 = (-1)^m\prod_{j=0}^{m-1}(1+e^{2\pi i j/m}) \implies \prod_{j=0}^{m

$$\prod_{b=1}^{n}(1+\omega^{ab}) = 2^{d} = 2^{\gcd(a,n)}.$$

$$\prod_{a=1}^{n}\prod_{b=1}^{n}(1+\omega^{ab}) = \prod_{a=1}^{n}2^{\gcd(a,n)} =
