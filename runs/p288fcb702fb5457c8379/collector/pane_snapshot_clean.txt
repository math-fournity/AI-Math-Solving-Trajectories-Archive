Looking at this problem, I need to find $k(n)$: the guaranteed maximum monochrom
atic-type matching (all Uniform or all Hybrid) for any 2-coloring of an $n \time
s n$ grid.

## Setup

The $n \times n$ grid graph is **bipartite**. Partition cells by parity of $i+j$
:
- $X$ = cells with $i+j$ even, $|X| = \lceil n^2/2 \rceil$
- $Y$ = cells with $i+j$ odd, $|Y| = \lfloor n^2/2 \rfloor$

Every edge connects $X$ to $Y$. The maximum matching of the grid is $|Y| = \lflo
or n^2/2 \rfloor$ (with $Y$ being a minimum vertex cover by König's theorem).

## Lower Bound

For **any** coloring, let $M$ be a maximum matching of the full grid, $|M| = \lf
loor n^2/2 \rfloor$. Split $M$ into uniform edges $M_U$ and hybrid edges $M_H$.
Then:
$$\nu(U) \geq |M_U|, \quad \nu(H) \geq |M_H|, \quad |M_U| + |M_H| = \lfloor n^2/
2 \rfloor$$
$$\max(\nu(U), \nu(H)) \geq \left\lceil \frac{\lfloor n^2/2 \rfloor}{2} \right\r
ceil$$

## Upper Bound (Construction)

Split $Y$ into $Y_1$ (size $\lceil |Y|/2 \rceil$) and $Y_2$ (size $\lfloor |Y|/2
 \rfloor$). Color all of $X \cup Y_1$ as **R**, and $Y_2$ as **B**.

Since all edges go between $X$ and $Y$:
- **Uniform edges** = edges between $X$ and $Y_1$ (both R). The set $Y_1$ is a v
ertex cover of this subgraph, so by König's theorem: $\nu(U) \leq |Y_1| = \lceil
 |Y|/2 \rceil$.
- **Hybrid edges** = edges between $X$ and $Y_2$ (R vs B). The set $Y_2$ is a ve
rtex cover: $\nu(H) \leq |Y_2| = \lfloor |Y|/2 \rfloor$.

Therefore $\max(\nu(U), \nu(H)) \leq \lceil |Y|/2 \rceil = \left\lceil \frac{\lf
loor n^2/2 \rfloor}{2} \right\rceil$.

## Combining

$$k(n) = \left\lceil \frac{\lfloor n^2/2 \rfloor}{2} \right\rceil$$

## Verification on small cases

- **$n=2$**: Construction gives coloring `R R / B R`. Uniform edges share cell (
1,2) → $\nu(U)=1$. Hybrid edges share cell (2,1) → $\nu(H)=1$. So $k(2)=1$. ✓
- **$n=3$**: Construction gives `R R R / B R B / R R R`. Computed $\nu(U)=2$, $\
nu(H)=2$. So $k(3)=2$. ✓
