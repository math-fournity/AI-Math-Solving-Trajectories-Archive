## Proof

Let $\mathscr{E}$ be a locally free sheaf of rank $n$ on $X$, and let $\{U_i\}$
be an open cover trivializing $\mathscr{E}$. On each $U_i$ we have a frame $e_i
= (e_i^1, \dots, e_i^n)$, and on overlaps $U_i \cap U_j$ the frames are related
by

$$e_j = e_i \cdot T_{ij},$$

i.e. $e_j^a = \sum_b (T_{ij})_b^{\,a}\, e_i^b$, where $T_{ij} \in \mathrm{GL}_n(
\mathscr{O}_X(U_i \cap U_j))$. These satisfy the cocycle condition $T_{ij} T_{jk
} = T_{ik}$ on triple overlaps, and $T_{ii} = I$.

The dual sheaf $\mathscr{E}^\vee = \mathcal{Hom}(\mathscr{E}, \mathscr{O}_X)$ is
 locally free of rank $n$; a frame on $U_i$ is the dual frame $e_i^\vee = ((e_i^
1)^\vee, \dots, (e_i^n)^\vee)$, where $(e_i^a)^\vee(e_i^b) = \delta^a_b$.

On $U_i \cap U_j$, the dual frames must satisfy the duality pairing $\langle (e_
i^a)^\vee, e_i^b\rangle = \delta^a_b$ and $\langle (e_j^a)^\vee, e_j^b\rangle =
\delta^a_b$. Writing $e_j^\vee = e_i^\vee \cdot S_{ij}$ for some matrix $S_{ij}$
 (so $(e_j^a)^\vee = \sum_c (S_{ij})_c^{\,a}(e_i^c)^\vee$), we require

$$\delta^a_b = \langle (e_j^a)^\vee, e_j^b\rangle = \Big\langle \sum_c (S_{ij})_
c^{\,a}(e_i^c)^\vee,\; \sum_d (T_{ij})_d^{\,b} e_i^d \Big\rangle = \sum_{c,d}(S_
{ij})_c^{\,a}(T_{ij})_d^{\,b}\delta^c_d = \sum_c (S_{ij})_c^{\,a}(T_{ij})_c^{\,b
}.$$

In matrix form this reads $S_{ij}\, T_{ij}^{\mathsf{T}} = I$, hence

$$S_{ij} = (T_{ij}^{\mathsf{T}})^{-1} = (T_{ij}^{-1})^{\mathsf{T}}.$$

**Verification of the cocycle condition.** On $U_i \cap U_j \cap U_k$, using $T_
{ij}T_{jk}=T_{ik}$ (so $T_{jk}^{-1}T_{ij}^{-1}=T_{ik}^{-1}$):

$$S_{ij}S_{jk} = (T_{ij}^{-1})^{\mathsf{T}}(T_{jk}^{-1})^{\mathsf{T}} = (T_{jk}^
{-1}T_{ij}^{-1})^{\mathsf{T}} = (T_{ik}^{-1})^{\mathsf{T}} = S_{ik},$$

and $S_{ii} = (T_{ii}^{-1})^{\mathsf{T}} = I$. So $\{S_{ij}\}$ is a valid $\math
rm{GL}_n$-cocycle.

**Conclusion.** The transition functions of $\mathscr{E}^\vee$ are

$$\boxed{\,S_{ij} = (T_{ij}^{-1})^{\mathsf{T}} = (T_{ij}^{\mathsf{T}})^{-1}\,}$$

i.e. the **inverse transpose** of the transition functions of $\mathscr{E}$. (Eq
uivalently, $\mathscr{E}^\vee$ is associated to the principal $\mathrm{GL}_n$-bu
ndle of $\mathscr{E}$ via the contragredient representation $g \mapsto (g^{-1})^
{\mathsf{T}}$.)
