Solution

Answer: The number of ways is $(2n-1)!! = 1 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdots (2n-1) =
\dfrac{(2n)!}{2^n \cdot n!}$.

Setup and Key Property

We have weights $2^0, 2^1, \ldots, 2^{n-1}$. We place them one at a time, each
on the left (L) or right (R) pan, maintaining $D = L - R \ge 0$ at all times.

The crucial property of powers of two is that each weight exceeds the sum of all
smaller weights:

$$2^k > 2^0 + 2^1 + \cdots + 2^{k-1} = 2^k - 1.$$

Lemma: Equivalent Condition

Lemma. A placement sequence (permutation $\pi$ of the weights, with side
assignments) is valid — i.e., $D \ge 0$ after every step — if and only if in
every prefix of the sequence, the largest weight appearing in that prefix is on
the left pan.

Proof. At any step, $D = \sum_{\text{left}} 2^k - \sum_{\text{right}} 2^k$,
where the sums are over weights placed so far. Let $2^m$ be the largest weight
placed so far.

  • If $2^m$ is on the right: $D = -2^m + (\text{rest})$, where $|\text{rest}|
  \le 2^0 + \cdots + 2^{m-1} = 2^m - 1$. So $D \le -2^m + (2^m - 1) = -1 < 0$.
  Invalid.
  • If $2^m$ is on the left: $D = 2^m + (\text{rest})$, where $|\text{rest}| \le
  2^m - 1$. So $D \ge 2^m - (2^m - 1) = 1 > 0$. Valid.

Since the largest weight in any prefix determines the sign of $D$, the condition
$D \ge 0$ for all prefixes is equivalent to: the maximum of every prefix is on
the left. $\square$

Reformulation via Records

A record (left-to-right maximum) of a permutation $\pi$ is an element larger
than all preceding elements. The lemma tells us:

│ A sequence is valid $\iff$ every record of the permutation is assigned to the
│ left pan.

Non-records (elements that are not left-to-right maxima) may be assigned to
either side freely — the lemma guarantees $D \ge 1 > 0$ regardless of their
placement.

Counting

For a permutation $\pi$ with $r$ records, the $r$ records must go left, while
the remaining $n - r$ weights can each go left or right independently. This
gives $2^{n-r}$ valid sign assignments for that permutation.

The number of permutations of ${1, \ldots, n}$ with exactly $r$ left-to-right
maxima equals the unsigned Stirling number of the first kind $c(n, r)$ (via the
Foata fundamental transformation, which bijects permutations with $r$ cycles to
permutations with $r$ left-to-right maxima). Therefore:

$$f(n) = \sum_{r=0}^{n} c(n, r) \cdot 2^{n-r}.$$

Evaluating the Sum

The unsigned Stirling numbers of the first kind satisfy the generating function
(rising factorial):

$$x^{(n)} = x(x+1)(x+2)\cdots(x+n-1) = \sum_{r=0}^{n} c(n, r), x^r.$$

Substituting $x = \tfrac{1}{2}$:

$$\sum_{r=0}^{n} c(n, r)\left(\tfrac{1}{2}\right)^r = \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot
\tfrac{3}{2} \cdot \tfrac{5}{2} \cdots \tfrac{2n-1}{2} = \frac{1 \cdot 3 \cdot 5
\cdots (2n-1)}{2^n}.$$

Multiplying both sides by $2^n$:

$$f(n) = \sum_{r=0}^{n} c(n, r) \cdot 2^{n-r} = 2^n \cdot \frac{1 \cdot 3 \cdot
5 \cdots (2n-1)}{2^n} = 1 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdots (2n-1) = (2n-1)!!.$$

Final Answer

The number of valid placement sequences is:

$$\boxed{(2n-1)!! = 1 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdots (2n-1) = \frac{(2n)!}{2^n \cdot
n!}}.$$
