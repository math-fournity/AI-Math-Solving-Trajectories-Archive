## Proof

**Key Claim.** For all positive reals $a, b, c$:
$$\frac{a}{\sqrt{a^2 + 8bc}} \geq \frac{a^{4/3}}{a^{4/3} + b^{4/3} + c^{4/3}}. \
tag{$\star$}$$

If $(\star)$ holds, then summing the three cyclic versions:

$$\sum_{\text{cyc}} \frac{a}{\sqrt{a^2 + 8bc}} \geq \frac{a^{4/3} + b^{4/3} + c^
{4/3}}{a^{4/3} + b^{4/3} + c^{4/3}} = 1,$$

which is the desired inequality. It remains to prove $(\star)$.

---

**Proof of Claim.** The inequality $(\star)$ is equivalent (all quantities posit
ive) to:

$$a\!\left(a^{4/3}+b^{4/3}+c^{4/3}\right) \geq a^{4/3}\sqrt{a^2+8bc}$$
$$\iff a^{1/3}\!\left(a^{4/3}+b^{4/3}+c^{4/3}\right) \geq \sqrt{a^2+8bc}$$
$$\iff a^{2/3}\!\left(a^{4/3}+b^{4/3}+c^{4/3}\right)^2 \geq a^2 + 8bc.$$

**Substitute** $a = x^3,\ b = y^3,\ c = z^3$ with $x, y, z > 0$. Then $a^{2/3} =
 x^2$, $a^{4/3} = x^4$, $a^2 = x^6$, $bc = y^3 z^3$, and the inequality becomes:

$$x^2\!\left(x^4 + y^4 + z^4\right)^2 \geq x^6 + 8y^3 z^3.$$

Dividing both sides by $x^2 > 0$ and expanding the square on the left:

$$\left(x^4+y^4+z^4\right)^2 \geq x^8 + 8x^2 y^3 z^3$$
$$\iff x^8 + y^8 + z^8 + 2x^4y^4 + 2y^4z^4 + 2x^4z^4 \geq x^8 + 8x^2y^3z^3$$
$$\iff y^8 + z^8 + 2x^4y^4 + 2y^4z^4 + 2x^4z^4 \geq 8x^2y^3z^3. \tag{$\dagger$}$
$

We prove $(\dagger)$ by the **AM–GM inequality**. Consider the eight positive qu
antities (with repetitions):

$$\underbrace{y^8}_{1},\ \underbrace{z^8}_{1},\ \underbrace{x^4y^4,\ x^4y^4}_{2}
,\ \underbrace{y^4z^4,\ y^4z^4}_{2},\ \underbrace{x^4z^4,\ x^4z^4}_{2}.$$

Their sum is exactly the left-hand side of $(\dagger)$. By AM–GM (8 terms):

$$\frac{y^8 + z^8 + 2x^4y^4 + 2y^4z^4 + 2x^4z^4}{8} \geq \left(y^8 \cdot z^8 \cd
ot (x^4y^4)^2 \cdot (y^4z^4)^2 \cdot (x^4z^4)^2\right)^{1/8}.$$

Computing the product inside the 8th root:

- **Power of $x$:** $(x^4)^2 \cdot (x^4)^2 = x^{16}$, so $x^{16/8} = x^2$.
- **Power of $y$:** $y^8 \cdot (y^4)^2 \cdot (y^4)^2 = y^{8+8+8} = y^{24}$, so $
y^{24/8} = y^3$.
- **Power of $z$:** $z^8 \cdot (z^4)^2 \cdot (z^4)^2 = z^{8+8+8} = z^{24}$, so $
z^{24/8} = z^3$.

Therefore:

$$\left(y^8 \cdot z^8 \cdot (x^4y^4)^2 \cdot (y^4z^4)^2 \cdot (x^4z^4)^2\right)^
{1/8} = x^2\, y^3\, z^3,$$

and AM–GM yields:

$$y^8 + z^8 + 2x^4y^4 + 2y^4z^4 + 2x^4z^4 \geq 8\,x^2 y^3 z^3.$$

This is precisely $(\dagger)$, completing the proof of the claim. $\square$

---

**Equality.** Equality in AM–GM requires all eight terms to be equal: $y^8 = z^8
 = x^4y^4 = y^4z^4 = x^4z^4$. From $y^8 = z^8$ we get $y = z$; from $y^8 = x^4y^
4$ we get $x^4 = y^4$, hence $x = y$. So $x = y = z$, i.e., $a = b = c$. One ver
ifies: when $a = b = c$, each term equals $\frac{a}{\sqrt{9a^2}} = \frac{1}{3}$,
 and the sum is $1$.

$$\frac{a}{\sqrt{a^2+8bc}} + \frac{b}{\sqrt{b^2+8ca}} + \frac{c}{\sqrt{c^2+8ab}}
 \geq \frac{a^{4/3}+b^{4/3}+c^{4/3}}{a^{4/3}+b^{4/3}+c^{4/3}} = 1. \qquad \black
square$$
