# Problem

In the futuristic city of Neoterra, a massive power grid is organized as a $19 \times 19$ square lattice of substations. To maintain the system, autonomous maintenance drones are deployed across the grid. Each drone occupies exactly one substation at a time.

To prevent collisions and wear-and-tear, the drones must follow a strict movement protocol:
1. Every minute, every drone must simultaneously move from its current substation to an adjacent substation (either horizontally or vertically).
2. No two drones can occupy the same substation at any given time.
3. To prevent motor fatigue, a drone is forbidden from moving along the same directional line in two consecutive minutes. For example, if a drone moves from a substation to the one east of it, its next move must be either north or south; it cannot move east again or back to the west.

What is the maximum number of drones that can be placed on the grid such that this maintenance cycle can continue indefinitely without ever violating these three rules?
