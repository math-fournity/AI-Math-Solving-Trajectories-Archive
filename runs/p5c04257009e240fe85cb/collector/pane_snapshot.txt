## Setting Up the Conjugacy

The map $f(x) = \frac{2x-1}{1-x}$ is a Möbius transformation with matrix $\begin
{pmatrix} 2 & -1 \\ -1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$, determinant 1, trace 3. Its eigenvalu
es are $\varphi^2$ and $\psi^2$ (where $\varphi = \frac{1+\sqrt5}{2}$, $\psi = \
frac{1-\sqrt5}{2}$), so the multiplier is $\varphi^4$.

**Fixed points:** $f(x) = x \implies x^2 + x - 1 = 0$, giving $\alpha = \frac{-1
+\sqrt5}{2} = \frac{1}{\varphi}$ and $\beta = \frac{-1-\sqrt5}{2} = -\varphi$.

Define $g(x) = \frac{x - \alpha}{x - \beta} = \frac{x - 1/\varphi}{x + \varphi}$
. Then:
$$g(f(x)) = \varphi^4 \cdot g(x)$$

So after $n$ steps: $g(x_n) = \varphi^{4n} \cdot g(x_0)$.

**Reaching 1:** $g(1) = \frac{1 - 1/\varphi}{1 + \varphi} = \frac{(\varphi-1)/\v
arphi}{\varphi^2} = \frac{1/\varphi^2}{\varphi^2} = \frac{1}{\varphi^4}$.

So $x_n = 1$ iff $\varphi^{4n} \cdot g(x_0) = \varphi^{-4}$, i.e., $g(x_0) = \va
rphi^{-4(n+1)} = \varphi^{-4N}$ for some integer $N \geq 1$.
