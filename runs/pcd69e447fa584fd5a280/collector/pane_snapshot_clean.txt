## Solution

Each player's deck is a random arrangement of 4 red (R) and 4 black (B) cards, s
o there are $\binom{8}{4}=70$ equally likely decks per player. The two decks are
 independent.

**Pair-type sequence.** Partition the 8 positions into four consecutive pairs: $
(1,2),(3,4),(5,6),(7,8)$. For pair $i$, let $r_i\in\{0,1,2\}$ be the number of r
ed cards in it. The pair *type* is determined by $r_i$: $0\Rightarrow BB$, $2\Ri
ghtarrow RR$, $1\Rightarrow RB$. Thus the pair-type sequence is in bijection wit
h $(r_1,r_2,r_3,r_4)$, subject to $r_i\in\{0,1,2\}$ and $\sum r_i = 4$.

**Count per sequence.** For a fixed valid $(r_1,r_2,r_3,r_4)$, the number of arr
angements realizing it is $\prod_{i=1}^4 \binom{2}{r_i}=2^{\,k}$ where $k$ is th
e number of pairs with $r_i=1$.

**Enumerate by $k$ (number of 1's).** The non-1 positions must sum to $4-k$ usin
g only 0's and 2's, forcing $\frac{4-k}{2}$ twos and $\frac{4-k}{2}$ zeros — so
$k$ must be even.

- **$k=0$:** exactly two 2's and two 0's. $\binom{4}{2}=6$ vectors, each with co
unt $2^0=1$.
- **$k=2$:** two 1's; among the other two positions, one is 2 and one is 0. $\bi
nom{4}{2}\cdot 2 = 12$ vectors, each with count $2^2=4$.
- **$k=4$:** all 1's. $1$ vector, count $2^4=16$.

Sanity check: $6\cdot 1 + 12\cdot 4 + 1\cdot 16 = 6+48+16 = 70$. ✓

**Probability of matching.** Hong and Song match for all four turns iff their pa
ir-type sequences coincide. Since decks are independent and uniform over 70,

$$P = \sum_{\text{valid } (r_1,\dots,r_4)} \left(\frac{\text{count}}{70}\right)^
2 = \frac{1}{70^2}\sum (\text{count})^2.$$

Computing the sum of squares:
$$6\cdot 1^2 + 12\cdot 4^2 + 1\cdot 16^2 = 6 + 192 + 256 = 454.$$

So
$$P = \frac{454}{4900} = \frac{227}{2450}.$$

Since $227$ is prime and $2450 = 2\cdot 5^2\cdot 7^2$ is not divisible by $227$,
 we have $\gcd(227,2450)=1$, giving $m=227$, $n=2450$.

$$100m + n = 100\cdot 227 + 2450 = 22700 + 2450 = \boxed{25150}.$$
