**IMO 1998 Problem 4.** Determine all pairs $(a,b)$ of positive integers such th
at $ab^2+b+7 \mid a^2b+a+b$.

## Solution

We claim the solutions are
$$
(a,b) \in \{(7k^2,\, 7k) : k\in\mathbb{Z}_{>0}\}\;\cup\;\{(11,1),\;(49,1)\}.
$$

### Key reduction

Set $D = ab^2+b+7$. Compute the combination
$$
b\,(a^2b+a+b) \;-\; a\,(ab^2+b+7) \;=\; (a^2b^2+ab+b^2)-(a^2b^2+ab+7a) \;=\; b^2
-7a.
$$
Since $D\mid a^2b+a+b$ and trivially $D\mid a\,D$, we get
$$
\boxed{D \mid b^2 - 7a.} \tag{$\star$}
$$

### Case 1: $b^2 - 7a = 0$

Then $7\mid b^2$, hence $7\mid b$; write $b=7k$, so $a = b^2/7 = 7k^2$ with $k\i
n\mathbb{Z}_{>0}$. Verify:
$$
D = 7k^2\cdot 49k^2 + 7k+7 = 7(49k^4+k+1),\qquad a^2b+a+b = 49k^4\cdot 7k+7k^2+7
k = 7k(49k^4+k+1).
$$
The quotient is $k\in\mathbb{Z}_{>0}$. ✓ So **every** $(a,b)=(7k^2,7k)$ works.

### Case 2: $b^2 - 7a \neq 0$

Then $(\star)$ forces $|b^2-7a|\ge D = ab^2+b+7 > 0$.

**Subcase 2a: $b^2-7a>0$.** Then $b^2-7a\ge ab^2+b+7$, i.e. $b^2(1-a)\ge b+7+7a>
0$. But $a\ge 1$ gives $b^2(1-a)\le 0$: contradiction. No solutions.

**Subcase 2b: $b^2-7a<0$.** Then $7a-b^2\ge ab^2+b+7$, i.e.
$$
a(7-b^2)\ge b^2+b+7>0,
$$
which forces $7-b^2>0$, so $b\in\{1,2\}$.

- **$b=2$:** $3a\ge 4+2+7=13$, so $a\ge 5$. Here $D=4a+9$ and $a^2b+a+b=4a^2+a+2
$. Since
  $$4a^2+a+2 = a(4a+9)-8a+2 = a(4a+9)-2(4a+9)+20,$$
  we need $4a+9\mid 20$. But $a\ge 5\Rightarrow 4a+9\ge 29>20$: impossible.

- **$b=1$:** $6a\ge 1+1+7=9$, so $a\ge 2$. Here $D=a+8$ and $a^2b+a+b=a^2+a+1$.
Since
  $$a^2+a+1 = a(a+8)-7a+1 = a(a+8)-7(a+8)+57,$$
  we need $a+8\mid 57$. The positive divisors of $57$ are $1,3,19,57$. With $a\g
e 2$ (so $a+8\ge 10$): $a+8=19\Rightarrow a=11$, or $a+8=57\Rightarrow a=49$.

  Verify:
  - $(a,b)=(11,1)$: $D=19$, $a^2b+a+b=121+11+1=133=7\cdot 19$. ✓
  - $(a,b)=(49,1)$: $D=57$, $a^2b+a+b=2401+49+1=2451=43\cdot 57$. ✓

### Conclusion

All pairs $(a,b)$ of positive integers satisfying $ab^2+b+7\mid a^2b+a+b$ are ex
actly
$$
\boxed{(a,b)=(7k^2,\,7k)\text{ for }k\in\mathbb{Z}_{>0},\quad\text{and}\quad (a,
b)=(11,1),\;(49,1).}
$$
