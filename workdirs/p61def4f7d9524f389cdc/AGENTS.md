# Problem

A specialized construction crew is tasked with dismantling a massive steel girder of length 2008 meters. The project protocol requires that the girder be reduced into individual 1-meter segments through a series of modular partitions. 

A partition is defined as "asymmetric" if the two resulting segments have different integer lengths. Each step of the process involves selecting one existing segment and performing a single cut to divide it into two smaller segments, each of which must have an integer length. These two resulting lengths are then recorded in the project log. The crew continues this process until every resulting segment is exactly 1 meter long.

What is the minimum possible number of asymmetric partitions required to complete the project?
