Looking at this problem, I need to determine whether every bounded distributive
lattice $L$ has a maximal Boolean sublattice containing $0$ and $1$.

## Answer: Yes.

**Proof.**

Let $L$ be a distributive lattice with least element $0$ and greatest element $1
$. Let $\mathcal{B}$ denote the collection of all Boolean sublattices of $L$ tha
t contain both $0$ and $1$, partially ordered by set-inclusion. We apply Zorn's
Lemma.

**$\mathcal{B}$ is non-empty.** The two-element set $\{0,1\}$ is a sublattice of
 $L$ since $0\wedge 1=0$ and $0\vee 1=1$. It forms a Boolean algebra (the 2-elem
ent Boolean algebra) with $0'=1$ and $1'=0$. Hence $\{0,1\}\in\mathcal{B}$.

**Every chain in $\mathcal{B}$ has an upper bound in $\mathcal{B}$.** Let $\{B_i
\}_{i\in I}$ be a chain in $\mathcal{B}$ and set $B=\bigcup_{i\in I}B_i$. We ver
ify $B\in\mathcal{B}$:

- *Contains $0$ and $1$:* Each $B_i$ contains $0$ and $1$, so $B$ does as well.
- *Sublattice:* If $a,b\in B$, then $a\in B_i$ and $b\in B_j$ for some $i,j$. Si
nce the family is a chain, one of $B_i\subseteq B_j$ or $B_j\subseteq B_i$ holds
; say $B_i\subseteq B_j$. Then $a,b\in B_j$, and since $B_j$ is a sublattice, $a
\wedge b,\,a\vee b\in B_j\subseteq B$.
- *Distributive:* $B$ is a sublattice of the distributive lattice $L$, hence dis
tributive.
- *Complemented:* If $a\in B$, then $a\in B_i$ for some $i$. Since $B_i$ is a Bo
olean algebra with bottom $0$ and top $1$ (the same $0,1$ as in $L$), there exis
ts $a'\in B_i$ with $a\wedge a'=0$ and $a\vee a'=1$. Since $B_i\subseteq B$, we
have $a'\in B$, and these equations hold in $B$ as well (meet and join are inher
ited from $L$, and $0,1$ are unchanged). Thus every element of $B$ has a complem
ent in $B$.

Therefore $B$ is a Boolean sublattice of $L$ containing $0$ and $1$, so $B\in\ma
thcal{B}$, and $B$ is an upper bound for the chain $\{B_i\}$.

**Conclusion.** Since $\mathcal{B}$ is non-empty and every chain has an upper bo
und in $\mathcal{B}$, Zorn's Lemma guarantees the existence of a maximal element
 $B^*\in\mathcal{B}$. This $B^*$ is a Boolean sublattice of $L$ containing $0$ a
nd $1$ that is maximal among all Boolean sublattices of $L$ containing $0$ and $
1$.

Hence every distributive lattice with $0$ and $1$ admits a maximal Boolean subla
