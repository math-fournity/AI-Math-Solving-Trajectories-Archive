## Proof

We compute $E\!\left[\int_{0}^{T} W_t^{\,n}\,dW_t\right]$, where $(W_t)_{t\ge 0}
$ is a standard Brownian motion and $n$ is a fixed (constant) power.

**Key fact (Itô integral is a martingale).** Let $H = (H_t)_{t\in[0,T]}$ be a pr
edictable process satisfying the square-integrability condition
$$
\int_0^T E\!\left[H_t^2\right]dt < \infty .
$$
Then the Itô integral $M_t := \int_0^t H_s\,dW_s$ is a (continuous) martingale w
ith $M_0 = 0$. In particular,
$$
E\!\left[\int_0^T H_t\,dW_t\right] = E[M_T] = E[M_0] = 0.
$$

**Verification of the hypotheses for $H_t = W_t^{\,n}$.**

1. *Predictability.* $W_t$ is continuous and adapted to the natural filtration $
(\mathcal F_t)$, hence so is $W_t^{\,n}$; a continuous adapted process is predic
table. Thus $H_t = W_t^{\,n}$ is predictable.

2. *Square-integrability.* Since $W_t \sim \mathcal N(0,t)$, its moments are
$$
E\!\left[W_t^{\,2n}\right] = (2n-1)!!\, t^{\,n} \quad (\text{for integer } n\ge
0),
$$
and more generally $E[W_t^{\,2n}] < \infty$ for every $t\in[0,T]$ whenever $2n$
is a finite moment of a Gaussian (which holds for all $n\ge 0$, and indeed for a
ll real $n$ with $2n$ a non-negative even integer, or even all real $n\ge 0$ sin
ce the Gaussian has all moments). Therefore
$$
\int_0^T E\!\left[(W_t^{\,n})^2\right]dt
= \int_0^T E\!\left[W_t^{\,2n}\right]dt
= (2n-1)!!\int_0^T t^{\,n}\,dt
= \frac{(2n-1)!!}{n+1}\,T^{\,n+1} < \infty .
$$

Both conditions are satisfied, so the Itô integral $\int_0^T W_t^{\,n}\,dW_t$ is
 a martingale starting at $0$.

**Conclusion.** By the martingale property,
$$
E\!\left[\int_{0}^{T} W_t^{\,n}\,dW_t\right] = 0.
$$
