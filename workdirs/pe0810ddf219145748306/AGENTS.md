# Problem

In the high-stakes world of Deep-Sea Salvage, five independent scouting drones are deployed to investigate a series of five underwater chambers. In each chamber, a drone must determine if a "Structural Breach" exists by submitting a binary signal: Signal-A (Yes) or Signal-B (No). Because the drones’ sensors are malfunctioning, each drone randomly generates its signal for every chamber, with each signal being equally likely.

Mission logs revealed a specific set of outcomes: for every one of the five chambers, a majority of the five drones successfully identified the correct status of that chamber. 

Let $p$ be the probability that, under these specific conditions, every single drone in the fleet correctly identified the status of exactly three out of the five chambers.

Given that $p$ can be expressed in the form $\frac{a}{2^b}$, where $a$ is an odd positive integer and $b$ is a nonnegative integer, compute the value of $100a + b$.
