The integral converges: near $t=0$ the integrand is $O(t^{1/2})$, and near $\inf
ty$ it is $O(t^{-3/2})$.

Set

$$\sum_{i,j}\sqrt{|x_i+x_j|}=\frac{1}{c}\int_0^\infty\frac{n^2-\bigl(A(t)^2-B(t)
