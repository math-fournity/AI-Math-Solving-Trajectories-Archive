## Solution

I need to compute $f(n) = \frac{1}{2^n}\sum_{i=0}^{(n+1)/2}(n-2i)\binom{n}{i}$ f
or $n = 1, 3, 5, 7$, then sum the results.

### $n = 1$: upper limit $(1+1)/2 = 1$

| $i$ | $n-2i$ | $\binom{1}{i}$ | product |
|-----|--------|----------------|---------|
| 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| 1 | -1 | 1 | -1 |

Sum $= 0$, so $f(1) = 0/2 = 0$.

### $n = 3$: upper limit $2$

| $i$ | $n-2i$ | $\binom{3}{i}$ | product |
|-----|--------|----------------|---------|
| 0 | 3 | 1 | 3 |
| 1 | 1 | 3 | 3 |
| 2 | -1 | 3 | -3 |

Sum $= 3$, so $f(3) = 3/8$.

### $n = 5$: upper limit $3$

| $i$ | $n-2i$ | $\binom{5}{i}$ | product |
|-----|--------|----------------|---------|
| 0 | 5 | 1 | 5 |
| 1 | 3 | 5 | 15 |
| 2 | 1 | 10 | 10 |
| 3 | -1 | 10 | -10 |

Sum $= 5+15+10-10 = 20$, so $f(5) = 20/32 = 5/8$.

### $n = 7$: upper limit $4$

| $i$ | $n-2i$ | $\binom{7}{i}$ | product |
|-----|--------|----------------|---------|
| 0 | 7 | 1 | 7 |
| 1 | 5 | 7 | 35 |
| 2 | 3 | 21 | 63 |
| 3 | 1 | 35 | 35 |
| 4 | -1 | 35 | -35 |

Sum $= 7+35+63+35-35 = 105$, so $f(7) = 105/128$.

### Total

$$f(1)+f(3)+f(5)+f(7) = 0 + \frac{3}{8} + \frac{5}{8} + \frac{105}{128}$$

$$= \frac{8}{8} + \frac{105}{128} = 1 + \frac{105}{128} = \frac{128+105}{128} =
\boxed{\frac{233}{128}}$$
