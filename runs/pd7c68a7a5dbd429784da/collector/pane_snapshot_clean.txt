Looking at this problem, I need to find the dimension of the family of degree-25
 zero-dimensional subschemes Γ of A⁵ with V(m³) ⊂ Γ ⊂ V(m⁴), where m = (x₁,…,x₅)
 is the maximal ideal at the origin.

## Setup and Inclusion Reversal

The condition V(m³) ⊂ Γ ⊂ V(m⁴) on subschemes reverses to conditions on ideals:

$$\mathfrak{m}^4 \subset I(\Gamma) \subset \mathfrak{m}^3$$

Since Γ ⊂ V(m⁴) and V(m⁴) is supported only at the origin, Γ is supported entire
ly at the origin.

## Computing Degrees

**Degree of V(m³):** dim K[x₁,…,x₅]/m³ = number of monomials of degree ≤ 2 in 5
variables:
$$1 + 5 + \binom{6}{2} = 1 + 5 + 15 = 21$$

**Degree of V(m⁴):** dim K[x₁,…,x₅]/m⁴ = monomials of degree ≤ 3:
$$1 + 5 + 15 + \binom{7}{3} = 1 + 5 + 15 + 35 = 56$$

**The quotient m³/m⁴** is the space of degree-3 homogeneous forms in 5 variables
:
$$\dim \mathfrak{m}^3/\mathfrak{m}^4 = \binom{7}{3} = 35$$

## Parametrization by a Grassmannian

We need dim K[x]/I(Γ) = 25. Since m⁴ ⊂ I(Γ) ⊂ m³:

$$\dim K[x]/I(\Gamma) = \dim K[x]/\mathfrak{m}^4 - \dim I(\Gamma)/\mathfrak{m}^4
 = 56 - \dim I(\Gamma)/\mathfrak{m}^4 = 25$$

So $\dim I(\Gamma)/\mathfrak{m}^4 = 31$, meaning I(Γ)/m⁴ is a **31-dimensional s
ubspace** of the 35-dimensional space m³/m⁴.

**Key observation:** Every K-subspace W with m⁴ ⊂ W ⊂ m³ is automatically an ide
al. *Proof:* For w ∈ W and g ∈ K[x], write g = c + h with c ∈ K, h ∈ m. Then gw
= cw + hw. We have cw ∈ W (K-subspace) and hw ∈ m·m³ = m⁴ ⊂ W. So gw ∈ W. ∎

This means the family of such ideals is in bijection with the **Grassmannian Gr(
31, 35)** of 31-dimensional subspaces of a 35-dimensional vector space.

## Dimension

The Grassmannian Gr(k, n) has dimension k(n − k). Therefore:

$$\dim \operatorname{Gr}(31, 35) = 31 \times (35 - 31) = 31 \times 4 = \boxed{12
4}$$
