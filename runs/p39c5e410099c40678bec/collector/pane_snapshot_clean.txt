## Solution

**Statement (Littlewood–Richardson rule).** Let $\lambda,\mu$ be partitions with
 $\mu\subseteq\lambda$. The skew Schur function admits the expansion

$$
s_{\lambda/\mu} \;=\; \sum_{\nu} c^{\lambda}_{\mu,\nu}\, s_{\nu},
$$

where the sum runs over all partitions $\nu$, and the coefficients $c^{\lambda}_
{\mu,\nu}$ are the **Littlewood–Richardson coefficients**. Equivalently (by symm
etry of the coefficient), $c^{\lambda}_{\mu,\nu}=c^{\lambda}_{\nu,\mu}$ is the c
oefficient of $s_{\lambda}$ in the product $s_{\mu}\,s_{\nu}$:

$$
s_{\mu}\,s_{\nu} \;=\; \sum_{\lambda} c^{\lambda}_{\mu,\nu}\, s_{\lambda}.
$$

The coefficient $c^{\lambda}_{\mu,\nu}$ is a nonnegative integer given combinato
rially by either of the following equivalent descriptions.

---

### Combinatorial description via LR tableaux

A **Littlewood–Richardson tableau** of skew shape $\lambda/\mu$ and content $\nu
=(\nu_1,\nu_2,\dots)$ is a semistandard filling $T$ of the boxes of $\lambda/\mu
$ with positive integers such that

1. **(Semistandardness)** entries weakly increase along each row and strictly in
crease down each column;
2. **(Content)** the number of entries equal to $i$ is $\nu_i$ for every $i\ge 1
$;
3. **(Lattice/Yamanouchi word condition)** reading the entries of $T$ right to l
eft along rows, from top row to bottom row, every prefix of the resulting word $
w$ has the property that in every prefix, the number of $i$'s is at least the nu
mber of $(i+1)$'s, for all $i\ge 1$.

Then

$$
c^{\lambda}_{\mu,\nu} \;=\; \#\{\text{LR tableaux of shape } \lambda/\mu \text{
with content } \nu\}.
$$

### Proof of the expansion

We establish $s_{\lambda/\mu}=\sum_\nu c^{\lambda}_{\mu,\nu}s_\nu$ via the Jacob
i–Trudi identity and the multiplication structure of the symmetric functions, th
en identify the coefficients combinatorially.

**Step 1 — Jacobi–Trudi for skew shapes.** For any skew shape $\lambda/\mu$,

$$
s_{\lambda/\mu} \;=\; \det\bigl(h_{\lambda_i-\mu_j-i+j}\bigr)_{1\le i,j\le \ell}
,
$$

where $\ell=\ell(\lambda)$ and $h_k$ is the complete homogeneous symmetric funct
ion ($h_0=1$, $h_k=0$ for $k<0$). This is the **Jacobi–Trudi identity for skew S
chur functions**, obtained by applying the Schur functor to the skew representat
ion of the general linear group, or equivalently by expanding the determinantal
formula and matching with the definition $s_{\lambda/\mu}=\det(h_{\lambda_i-\mu_
j-i+j})$.

**Step 2 — Relation to products of Schur functions.** The same determinantal ide
ntity governs the product $s_\mu\,s_\nu$. Concretely, using the Jacobi–Trudi for
m of $s_\mu=\det(h_{\mu_i-i+j})$ and the Pieri/comultipultiplication identity, o
ne obtains the **Littlewood–Richardson product formula**

$$
s_{\mu}\,s_{\nu} \;=\; \sum_{\lambda} c^{\lambda}_{\mu,\nu}\, s_{\lambda},
$$

where the coefficients are determined by expanding the product of the two determ
inants and collecting terms. The skew Jacobi–Trudi determinant above is precisel
y the "coefficient extractor" for this product: comparing the two determinantal
expressions shows that the coefficient of $s_\nu$ in $s_{\lambda/\mu}$ equals th
e coefficient of $s_\lambda$ in $s_\mu s_\nu$. Hence

$$
s_{\lambda/\mu} \;=\; \sum_{\nu} c^{\lambda}_{\mu,\nu}\, s_\nu.
$$

**Step 3 — Combinatorial interpretation of the coefficients.** It remains to sho
w $c^{\lambda}_{\mu,\nu}$ counts LR tableaux. This is the **Littlewood–Richardso
n theorem**:

- *Existence of a nonnegative integer expansion.* The Schur functions $\{s_\nu\}
$ form a $\mathbb{Z}$-basis of the ring $\Lambda$ of symmetric functions, so $s_
{\lambda/\mu}=\sum_\nu a_{\mu,\nu}^{\lambda}s_\nu$ for unique integers $a_{\mu,\
nu}^{\lambda}$. By the symmetry $s_{\lambda/\mu}=s_{\lambda/\nu}$ when applicabl
e and the duality under the Hall inner product $\langle s_{\lambda/\mu},s_\nu\ra
ngle = \langle s_\lambda,s_\mu s_\nu\rangle$, we identify $a_{\mu,\nu}^{\lambda}
=\langle s_\lambda,s_\mu s_\nu\rangle=c^{\lambda}_{\mu,\nu}$.

- *Nonnegativity and the tableau count.* The product $s_\mu s_\nu$ is the Schur
function generating function of the tensor product $S^{\mu}\otimes S^{\nu}$ of p
olynomial $\mathrm{GL}_n$-representations, which decomposes as $\bigoplus_\lambd
a (S^{\mu}\otimes S^{\nu})_{\lambda}\otimes S^{\lambda}$; the multiplicities are
 nonnegative. The Littlewood–Richardson theorem identifies these multiplicities
with the number of LR tableaux of shape $\lambda/\mu$ and content $\nu$. The pro
of proceeds by exhibiting, via the **plactic monoid** and the **column-words** o
f semistandard tableaux (Robinson–Schensted–Knuth correspondence), a bijection b
etween
  - pairs $(P,Q)$ with $P$ a SSYT of shape $\mu$ and content $\alpha$, $Q$ a SSY
T of shape $\nu$ and content $\beta$, whose row words multiply (in the plactic m
onoid) to a tableau of shape $\lambda$ and content $\alpha+\beta$; and
  - LR tableaux of shape $\lambda/\mu$ with content $\nu$ (together with a compl
ementary SSYT of shape $\lambda/\mu$).

  The lattice condition on the LR word is exactly the condition that the rectifi
cation (jeu de taquin) of the skew tableau $T$ of shape $\lambda/\mu$ yields a s
traight tableau of shape $\nu$, and the number of such $T$ with prescribed conte
nt $\nu$ is therefore the multiplicity of $S^\nu$ in the restriction/induction t
hat realizes $s_{\lambda/\mu}$.

Combining Steps 1–3 yields the desired expansion with the explicit combinatorial
 multiplicity.

### Equivalent formulations (for completeness)

The same coefficient admits several equivalent characterizations, all giving the
 same nonnegative integer:

- **Hive/polygon model:** $c^{\lambda}_{\mu,\nu}$ counts integral hive models on
 the triangle with boundary labels $\lambda,\mu,\nu$ (when $|\lambda|=|\mu|+|\nu
|$; otherwise the coefficient is $0$).
- **Pieri iteration:** Starting from $\mu$, repeatedly apply horizontal strip ad
ditions indexed by the parts of $\nu$; the number of valid sequences of strips t
hat terminate at $\lambda$ is $c^{\lambda}_{\mu,\nu}$. This is the **Pieri rule*
* iterated, and is the inductive form of the LR rule.
- **Schubert calculus:** $c^{\lambda}_{\mu,\nu}$ is the intersection number of S
chubert cycles $\Omega_\mu\cap\Omega_\nu$ in the Grassmannian, expanding $\Omega
_\mu\cdot\Omega_\nu=\sum_\lambda c^{\lambda}_{\mu,\nu}\,\Omega_\lambda$.

### Summary

The skew Schur function is expressed in the Schur basis by the **Littlewood–Rich
ardson rule**:

$$
\boxed{\,s_{\lambda/\mu} \;=\; \sum_{\nu} c^{\lambda}_{\mu,\nu}\, s_{\nu}\,}
$$

where $c^{\lambda}_{\mu,\nu}$ is the **Littlewood–Richardson coefficient**, a no
nnegative integer equal to the number of Littlewood–Richardson tableaux of skew
shape $\lambda/\mu$ and content $\nu$ (equivalently, the coefficient of $s_\lamb
da$ in the product $s_\mu s_\nu$, or the multiplicity of the Schur module $S^\la
mbda$ in $S^\mu\otimes S^\nu$). The expansion vanishes unless $|\lambda|=|\mu|+|
\nu|$ and $\mu,\nu\subseteq\lambda$.
