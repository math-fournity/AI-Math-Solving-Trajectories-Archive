## Solution

We evaluate $\oint_\Lambda f(z)\,dz$ where $f(z) = \sin(1/z) + \sin^2(1/z)$ and
$\Lambda$ is a circle centered at the origin. Since $z=0$ is the only singularit
y (an essential singularity) enclosed by $\Lambda$, by the residue theorem:

$$\oint_\Lambda f(z)\,dz = 2\pi i \cdot \operatorname{Res}_{z=0} f(z).$$

The residue equals the coefficient of $1/z$ in the Laurent expansion of $f$ abou
t $z=0$.

**Term 1: $\sin(1/z)$.** Using the Taylor series of $\sin$,

$$\sin\!\left(\frac{1}{z}\right) = \frac{1}{z} - \frac{1}{6z^3} + \frac{1}{120z^
5} - \cdots$$

The coefficient of $1/z$ is $\boxed{1}$.

**Term 2: $\sin^2(1/z)$.** Using the identity $\sin^2 w = \frac{1-\cos(2w)}{2}$
with $w = 1/z$:

$$\cos\!\left(\frac{2}{z}\right) = 1 - \frac{1}{2!}\left(\frac{2}{z}\right)^{\!2
} + \frac{1}{4!}\left(\frac{2}{z}\right)^{\!4} - \cdots = 1 - \frac{2}{z^2} + \f
rac{2}{3z^4} - \cdots$$

Hence

$$\sin^2\!\left(\frac{1}{z}\right) = \frac{1-\cos(2/z)}{2} = \frac{1}{z^2} - \fr
ac{1}{3z^4} + \cdots$$

This contains only **even** powers of $1/z$, so the coefficient of $1/z$ is $\bo
xed{0}$.

**Combining:**

$$\operatorname{Res}_{z=0} f(z) = 1 + 0 = 1.$$

Therefore

$$\oint_\Lambda \left[\sin\!\left(\frac{1}{z}\right) + \sin^2\!\left(\frac{1}{z}
\right)\right] dz = 2\pi i \cdot 1 = 2\pi i.$$

The correct answer is **(c) $2\pi i$**.
