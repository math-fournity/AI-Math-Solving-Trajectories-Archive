# Problem

A specialized architectural firm is monitoring the structural stability of a high-rise tower over several years. They use a stress-index sequence, $\{a_n\}_{n\geq 1}$, to measure the load on the foundation. In the first year, the index was recorded at $a_1 = 20$. In the second year, the load increased to $a_2 = 30$. For every subsequent year $n \geq 1$, the structural engineers determined that the load follows a specific recursive tension formula: the index for the year after next ($a_{n+2}$) is equal to triple the index of the next year ($3a_{n+1}$) minus the index of the current year ($a_n$).

The lead engineer is interested in "stability peaks," which occur only when a specific safety coefficient, calculated as $1 + 5a_n a_{n+1}$, results in a perfect square integer. Find all positive integers $n$ for which the tower reaches such a stability peak.
