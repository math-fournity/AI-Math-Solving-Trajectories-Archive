## Solution

We evaluate
$$L = \lim_{x\to\infty} \frac{\gamma(x+1,\,x)}{\Gamma(x+1)}.$$

**Key observation.** The ratio $P(a,z)=\gamma(a,z)/\Gamma(a)$ is the regularized
 lower incomplete gamma function, i.e. the CDF of a $\mathrm{Gamma}(a,1)$ random
 variable evaluated at $z$. Hence
$$\frac{\gamma(x+1,x)}{\Gamma(x+1)} = \Pr\!\big(Y_x \le x\big), \qquad Y_x \sim
\mathrm{Gamma}(x+1,1).$$

**Moments.** For $Y_x\sim\mathrm{Gamma}(x+1,1)$,
$$\mathbb{E}[Y_x]=x+1,\qquad \mathrm{Var}(Y_x)=x+1.$$
So the value $x$ at which we evaluate the CDF satisfies
$$x = (x+1) - 1 = \mathbb{E}[Y_x] - 1.$$

**Standardization.** Set
$$Z_x = \frac{Y_x-(x+1)}{\sqrt{x+1}}.$$
Then
$$\Pr(Y_x\le x)=\Pr\!\left(Z_x\le \frac{-1}{\sqrt{x+1}}\right).$$

**Asymptotic normality of $Z_x$.** The characteristic function of $Z_x$ is
$$\varphi_{Z_x}(t)=e^{-it\sqrt{x+1}}\left(1-\frac{it}{\sqrt{x+1}}\right)^{-(x+1)
}.$$
Taking logarithms and expanding $\log(1-u)=-u-u^2/2-\cdots$ with $u=it/\sqrt{x+1
}$:
$$\log\varphi_{Z_x}(t)=-it\sqrt{x+1}-(x+1)\!\left[-\frac{it}{\sqrt{x+1}}+\frac{t
^2}{2(x+1)}+O(x^{-3/2})\right]$$
$$= -it\sqrt{x+1}+it\sqrt{x+1}-\frac{t^2}{2}+O(x^{-1/2})=-\frac{t^2}{2}+O(x^{-1/
2}).$$
Thus $\varphi_{Z_x}(t)\to e^{-t^2/2}$, the characteristic function of $N(0,1)$.
By Lévy's continuity theorem,
$$Z_x \xrightarrow{d} N(0,1).$$

**Passing to the limit.** Since $-1/\sqrt{x+1}\to 0$ and the standard normal CDF
 $\Phi$ is continuous,
$$\lim_{x\to\infty}\Pr\!\left(Z_x\le \frac{-1}{\sqrt{x+1}}\right)=\Phi(0)=\frac1
2.$$

(Convergence in distribution gives $\Pr(Z_x\le a_x)\to\Phi(0)$ whenever $a_x\to
0$, since $0$ is a continuity point of $\Phi$ and the boundary $a_x$ drifts to t
hat continuity point.)

**Conclusion.**
$$\boxed{\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{\gamma(x+1,x)}{\Gamma(x+1)}=\frac12.}$$
