## Solution

**Set A.** For $x^2+mx+2020=0$ with positive integer roots $p,q$, Vieta's gives
$$p+q=-m,\qquad pq=2020.$$
So $m=-(p+q)$ where $p,q$ are positive integers with $pq=2020$.

Factor $2020 = 2^2\cdot 5\cdot 101$. The factor pairs $(p,q)$ with $p\le q$ and
their sums:

| $(p,q)$ | $p+q$ | $m=-(p+q)$ |
|---|---|---|
| $(1,2020)$ | $2021$ | $-2021$ |
| $(2,1010)$ | $1012$ | $-1012$ |
| $(4,505)$ | $509$ | $-509$ |
| $(5,404)$ | $409$ | $-409$ |
| $(10,202)$ | $212$ | $-212$ |
| $(20,101)$ | $121$ | $-121$ |

All $m$ values are negative, so the **largest** element is the one closest to ze
ro:
$$a = -121.$$

**Set B.** For $x^2+2020x+n=0$ with negative integer roots, write the roots as $
-p,-q$ with $p,q$ positive integers. Vieta's gives
$$p+q=2020,\qquad n = pq.$$

So $B = \{pq : p,q\in\mathbb{Z}^+,\ p+q=2020\}$. To find the **smallest** elemen
t, minimize $pq$ subject to $p+q=2020$. The product $pq$ is minimized when one f
actor is as small as possible, i.e. $p=1,\ q=2019$:
$$b = 1\cdot 2019 = 2019.$$

(Any other pair $(p, 2020-p)$ with $2\le p\le 1010$ gives a larger product, e.g.
 $2\cdot 2018=4036$, etc.)

**Final computation.**
$$a+b = -121 + 2019 = 1898.$$
Sum of digits of $1898$:
$$1+8+9+8 = 26.$$
