## Proof

**Answer: $k = 10$.**

**Conservation and closed form.** Let $S = x_n + y_n + z_n$. From the update rul
es,
$$x_n+y_n+z_n = (y_{n-1}+z_{n-1}-x_{n-1})+(z_{n-1}+x_{n-1}-y_{n-1})+(x_{n-1}+y_{
n-1}-z_{n-1}) = x_{n-1}+y_{n-1}+z_{n-1}.$$
So $S$ is invariant; since $S = 2016$, every packet satisfies
$$x_n = 2016 - 2x_{n-1},\qquad y_n = 2016 - 2y_{n-1},\qquad z_n = 2016 - 2z_{n-1
}.$$
The unique fixed point of $t \mapsto 2016 - 2t$ is $t^* = 2016/3 = 672$. Setting
 $a = x_0 - 672$, $b = y_0 - 672$, $c = z_0 - 672$, the recurrence linearizes to
$$x_n - 672 = -2(x_{n-1}-672),$$
hence
$$x_n = 672 + (-2)^n a,\quad y_n = 672 + (-2)^n b,\quad z_n = 672 + (-2)^n c.$$
Note $a + b + c = (x_0+y_0+z_0) - 3\cdot 672 = 0$.

**Using distinctness.** The three packets are distinct, so $(x_0,y_0,z_0) \neq (
672,672,672)$; equivalently $(a,b,c) \neq (0,0,0)$. Since $a+b+c = 0$ and not al
l are zero, at least one of $a,b,c$ is strictly negative. Let
$$m = \min(a,b,c) \leq -1.$$

**Upper bound: a crash occurs by step 10.** Consider the packet carrying deviati
on $m < 0$. At an **even** step $n$,
$$\text{size} = 672 + 2^n m = 672 - 2^n|m|.$$
This is negative precisely when $2^n|m| > 672$. Since $|m| \geq 1$ and $2^{10} =
 1024 > 672 \geq 512 = 2^9$, at $n = 10$ (even) we have
$$672 - 2^{10}|m| \leq 672 - 1024 = -352 < 0.$$
So regardless of the (distinct) initial distribution, some packet is negative by
 step 10. (If $|m| \geq 3$ the crash arrives even earlier, at $n = 8$; if $|m|\i
n\{1,2\}$ it arrives exactly at $n=10$. Positive deviations can only make some p
acket crash earlier, at odd steps, never later.)

**Lower bound: step 9 does not suffice.** Take the distinct positive-integer dis
tribution
$$(x_0,y_0,z_0) = (671,\,672,\,673),\qquad (a,b,c) = (-1,\,0,\,1).$$
Then
$$x_n = 672 - (-2)^n,\quad y_n = 672,\quad z_n = 672 + (-2)^n.$$
- For **odd** $n \leq 9$: $x_n = 672 + 2^n \geq 672 + 2 = 674 > 0$, and $z_n = 6
72 - 2^n \geq 672 - 512 = 160 > 0$.
- For **even** $n \leq 8$: $x_n = 672 - 2^n \geq 672 - 256 = 416 > 0$, and $z_n
= 672 + 2^n > 0$.
- Meanwhile $y_n = 672 > 0$ for all $n$.

So no packet is negative through step 9. At step 10 (even),
$$x_{10} = 672 - 2^{10} = 672 - 1024 = -352 < 0,$$
and the crash finally occurs. Thus no $k \leq 9$ can guarantee a crash.

**Conclusion.** Every distinct positive-integer distribution of the 2016 GB cras
hes by minute 10, and the distribution $(671,672,673)$ survives through minute 9
. Therefore the minimum guaranteed time is

$$\boxed{k = 10}.$$
