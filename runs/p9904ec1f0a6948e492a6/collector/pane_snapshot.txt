$$\operatorname{Res}{F/\mathbb{Q}}(G_F)(R) ;=; G_F(R \otimes{\mathbb{Q}} F) ;=;

\operatorname{Res}{F/\mathbb{Q}}(G_F)(\mathbb{A}{\mathbb{Q}}) ;\cong;

Applying the defining property of Weil restriction with $R = \mathbb{A}
_{\mathbb{Q}}$:

$$\operatorname{Res}{F/\mathbb{Q}}(G_F)(\mathbb{A}{\mathbb{Q}}) ;=; G(\mathbb{A}

$$\mathbb{A}{\mathbb{Q}} \otimes{\mathbb{Q}} F ;\cong; \mathbb{A}_F.$$

Step 2: Decompose $\mathbb{A}{\mathbb{Q}} \otimes{\mathbb{Q}} F$

The adele ring of $\mathbb{Q}$ is the restricted product: $$\mathbb{A}

(\text{with respect to } \mathbb{Z}_p \subset \mathbb{Q}_p).$$

\in S} \mathbb{Q}p \times \prod{p \notin S} \mathbb{Z}_p\right).$$

Since $F$ is a finite-dimensional $\mathbb{Q}$-vector space, the functor $(-)
\otimes_{\mathbb{Q}} F$ is exact and preserves all colimits (being a left
adjoint). Therefore:

$$\mathbb{A}{\mathbb{Q}} \otimes{\mathbb{Q}} F ;=; (F \otimes_{\mathbb{Q}}
\mathbb{R}) ;\times; \varinjlim_{S} \left(\prod_{p \in S} (F
\otimes_{\mathbb{Q}} \mathbb{Q}p) \times \prod{p \notin S} (F
\otimes_{\mathbb{Q}} \mathbb{Z}_p)\right).$$
