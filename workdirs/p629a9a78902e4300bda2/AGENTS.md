# Problem

In the competitive world of high-speed logistics, two rival dispatchers are managing a cargo load that is being filled in rounds. The game begins with the first dispatcher selecting an initial weight $n$, which must be a positive integer less than 10 units. 

Following this, the dispatchers take turns increasing the weight. If the current total weight is $n$, the next dispatcher must add a quantity such that the new total weight is an integer between $2n$ and $3n$, inclusive. 

The safety limit of the transport vessel is 2006 units. The first dispatcher forced to announce a weight of 2007 or greater causes the vessel to stabilize poorly, resulting in an immediate loss for that dispatcher and a win for their opponent.

Assuming the first dispatcher plays with a perfect winning strategy, for how many of the possible starting integers (1 through 9) can they guarantee a victory?
