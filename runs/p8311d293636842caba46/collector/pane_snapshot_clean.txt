$$Q(A-B)=Q(f(A)-f(B))\quad\text{for all }A,B.$$

**Step 1: Reduction to linear.** Set $g(A)=f(A)-f(0)$, so $g(0)=0$ and $Q(A-B)=Q

$$A\cdot B=\tfrac12\bigl(Q(A)+Q(B)-Q(A-B)\bigr)$$

Let $e_1=(1,0), e_2=(0,1)$. Then $\{g(e_1),g(e_2)\}$ is orthonormal:
$$g(e_i)\cdot g(e_j)=e_i\cdot e_j=\delta_{ij}.$$
Since $Q$ is nondegenerate, this is a basis. For $A=(a,b)=ae_1+be_2$,
$$g(A)\cdot g(e_1)=A\cdot e_1=a,\qquad g(A)\cdot g(e_2)=A\cdot e_2=b.$$
Expanding in the orthonormal basis: $g(A)=a\,g(e_1)+b\,g(e_2)$. Thus $g$ is **li
near**, determined by the orthonormal pair $(g(e_1),g(e_2))$.
