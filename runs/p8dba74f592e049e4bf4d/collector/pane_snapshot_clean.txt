**Step 2: Show $f$ is a cyclic right rotation on $\{0, 1, \ldots, 2^n - 1\}$.**

bit binary. Then:
$$f(x) = \sum_{i=1}^{n-1} b_i 2^{i-1} + 2^{n-1} b_0 = \sum_{i=0}^{n-2} b_{i+1} 2
^i + 2^{n-1} b_0.$$

The new bit at position $i$ (for $0 \le i \le n-2$) is $b_{i+1}$, and the new bi
t at position $n-1$ is $b_0$. This is exactly a **right cyclic rotation** of the

Since $f(x) \in \{0, \ldots, 2^n-1\}$ whenever $x$ is (the rotation stays within

$$f^{[n]}(x) = x \quad \text{for all } x \in \{0, 1, \ldots, 2^n - 1\}.$$

This gives $2^n = 1024$ solutions.
