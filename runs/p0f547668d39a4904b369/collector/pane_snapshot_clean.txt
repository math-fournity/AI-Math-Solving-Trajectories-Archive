## Proof

Let $x_n = 2^{3^n}$. The $n$-th term of the series is

$$\log\!\left(1+\frac{1}{x_n}+\frac{1}{x_n^2}\right)=\log\!\left(\frac{x_n^2+x_n
+1}{x_n^2}\right).$$

**Key factorization.** Since $x_n^2+x_n+1=\dfrac{x_n^3-1}{x_n-1}$ and $x_n^3 = \
left(2^{3^n}\right)^3 = 2^{3^{n+1}} = x_{n+1}$, we get

$$x_n^2+x_n+1 = \frac{x_{n+1}-1}{x_n-1}.$$

Therefore

$$\log\!\left(\frac{x_n^2+x_n+1}{x_n^2}\right)=\log(x_{n+1}-1)-\log(x_n-1)-2\log
 x_n.$$

**Telescoping.** Set $a_n = \log(2^{3^n}-1)$. Then the $n$-th term equals

$$a_{n+1}-a_n-2\cdot 3^n\log 2.$$

The partial sum $S_N=\sum_{n=0}^{N}$ is

$$S_N = \sum_{n=0}^{N}(a_{n+1}-a_n) - 2\log 2\sum_{n=0}^{N}3^n.$$

The first sum telescopes to $a_{N+1}-a_0$. Since $a_0=\log(2^1-1)=\log 1=0$, thi
s is just $a_{N+1}$. The geometric sum is $\dfrac{3^{N+1}-1}{2}$. Hence

$$S_N = \log(2^{3^{N+1}}-1) - \log 2\cdot(3^{N+1}-1).$$

Rewrite the second term as $\log(2^{3^{N+1}-1})$:

$$S_N = \log\!\left(\frac{2^{3^{N+1}}-1}{2^{3^{N+1}-1}}\right)=\log\!\left(2\cdo
t\frac{2^{3^{N+1}}-1}{2^{3^{N+1}}}\right)=\log 2+\log\!\left(1-\frac{1}{2^{3^{N+
1}}}\right).$$

**Limit.** As $N\to\infty$, $3^{N+1}\to\infty$, so $\dfrac{1}{2^{3^{N+1}}}\to 0$
 and $\log\!\left(1-\dfrac{1}{2^{3^{N+1}}}\right)\to 0$. Therefore

$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\log\!\left(1+\frac{1}{2^{3^n}}+\frac{1}{2^{2\cdot 3^n}}\ri
ght)=\log 2.$$

**Numerical check.** $n=0$: $\log(1+\tfrac12+\tfrac14)=\log\tfrac74\approx 0.559
6$. $n=1$: $\log(1+\tfrac18+\tfrac1{64})=\log\tfrac{73}{64}\approx 0.1316$. Part
ial sum $\approx 0.6912$, already close to $\log 2\approx 0.6931$. ✓
