## Solution

**Setup.** Let A be the origin. Triangle ABC has sides AB = 3/2, AC = 13, BC = 1
4.

**Step 1: Angle at A in triangle ABC.**

By the law of cosines:
$$\cos(\angle BAC) = \frac{AB^2 + AC^2 - BC^2}{2 \cdot AB \cdot AC} = \frac{(3/2
)^2 + 13^2 - 14^2}{2 \cdot (3/2) \cdot 13} = \frac{2.25 + 169 - 196}{39} = \frac
{-24.75}{39} = -\frac{33}{52}.$$

Let $\angle BAC = 2\alpha$. Then $\cos 2\alpha = -33/52$, giving:
$$\cos\alpha = \sqrt{\frac{19}{104}}, \quad \sin\alpha = \sqrt{\frac{85}{104}},
\quad \sin 2\alpha = \frac{\sqrt{1615}}{52}.$$

**Step 2: Coordinate system.** Place the bisector of $\angle BAC$ along the x-ax
is, with ABC in the xy-plane:
- $B = \left(\tfrac{3}{2}\cos\alpha,\; \tfrac{3}{2}\sin\alpha,\; 0\right)$
- $C = \left(13\cos\alpha,\; -13\sin\alpha,\; 0\right)$

The condition $\angle DAB = \angle DAC$ forces D into the bisector plane (xz-pla
ne), so $D = (x, 0, z)$.

**Step 3: Use DA = 18 and DB = DC.**

From $DA = 18$: $x^2 + z^2 = 324$.

From $DB = DC$, expanding $(x - \tfrac{3}{2}\cos\alpha)^2 + (\tfrac{3}{2}\sin\al
pha)^2 = (x - 13\cos\alpha)^2 + (13\sin\alpha)^2$:

$$-3x\cos\alpha + 2.25 = -26x\cos\alpha + 169 \implies 23x\cos\alpha = 166.75 =
\frac{667}{4}.$$

Since $667 = 23 \cdot 29$ and $\cos\alpha = \sqrt{19/104} = \frac{\sqrt{19}}{2\s
qrt{26}}$:

$$x = \frac{29\sqrt{26}}{2\sqrt{19}}, \quad x^2 = \frac{841 \cdot 26}{4 \cdot 19
} = \frac{10933}{38}.$$

$$z^2 = 324 - \frac{10933}{38} = \frac{12312 - 10933}{38} = \frac{1379}{38}.$$

**Step 4: Compute the volume.** Since A is the origin:

$$V = \frac{1}{6}\left|\det\begin{pmatrix} \tfrac{3}{2}\cos\alpha & \tfrac{3}{2}
\sin\alpha & 0 \\ 13\cos\alpha & -13\sin\alpha & 0 \\ x & 0 & z \end{pmatrix}\ri
ght| = \frac{1}{6}\cdot 39\,|z|\cos\alpha\sin\alpha = \frac{13}{2}|z|\cos\alpha\
sin\alpha.$$

Using $\cos\alpha\sin\alpha = \frac{\sin 2\alpha}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{1615}}{104}$
and $|z| = \sqrt{\frac{1379}{38}}$:

$$V = \frac{13}{2}\cdot\sqrt{\frac{1379}{38}}\cdot\frac{\sqrt{1615}}{104} = \fra
c{13\sqrt{1379\cdot 1615/38}}{208}.$$

Since $\frac{1615}{38} = \frac{85}{2}$ (as $1615 = 85\cdot 19$ and $38 = 2\cdot
19$):

$$V = \frac{13\sqrt{1379 \cdot 85/2}}{208} = \frac{13\sqrt{234430/4}}{208} = \fr
ac{13\sqrt{234430}}{416} = \frac{\sqrt{234430}}{32},$$

where the last step uses $416 = 32 \times 13$.

**Verification.** $234430 = 2\cdot 5\cdot 7\cdot 17\cdot 197$ (no perfect square
 factors), so the radical is fully reduced. Numerically, $V \approx 484.18/32 \a
pprox 15.13$.

Cross-check via base × height: Area(ABC) $= \frac{3\sqrt{1615}}{16}$ (Heron), he
ight $= |z| = \sqrt{1379/38}$, so $V = \frac{1}{3}\cdot\frac{3\sqrt{1615}}{16}\c
dot\sqrt{\frac{1379}{38}} = \frac{\sqrt{234430}}{32}$. ✓

$$\boxed{V = \dfrac{\sqrt{234430}}{32} \text{ cubic meters}}$$
