## Proof

Let $f(x) \in \mathbb{Q}[x]$ be irreducible of prime degree $p$, with exactly tw
o nonreal roots in $\mathbb{C}$. Let $G = \operatorname{Gal}(f/\mathbb{Q})$ be i
ts Galois group, viewed as a subgroup of $S_p$ via its action on the $p$ roots $
\alpha_1, \dots, \alpha_p$.

**Step 1: $G$ is transitive and contains a $p$-cycle.**

Since $f$ is irreducible over $\mathbb{Q}$, $G$ acts transitively on the roots.
Hence $p \mid |G|$, and by Cauchy's theorem $G$ contains an element of order $p$
. Any element of order $p$ in $S_p$ is a $p$-cycle (since $p$ is prime). So $G$
contains a $p$-cycle $\sigma$.

**Step 2: $G$ contains a transposition.**

Since $f$ has rational (hence real) coefficients, complex conjugation permutes t
he roots. Among the $p$ roots, exactly two are nonreal; these two are complex co
njugates of each other (nonreal roots of a real polynomial come in conjugate pai
rs), and the remaining $p - 2$ are real. Complex conjugation therefore fixes the
 $p - 2$ real roots and swaps the two nonreal roots. Thus it acts on the roots a
s a transposition $\tau \in G$.

**Step 3: A transitive subgroup of $S_p$ ($p$ prime) containing a $p$-cycle and
a transposition equals $S_p$.**

After relabeling, write $\sigma = (1\;2\;\dots\;p)$ and $\tau = (1\;k)$ for some
 $k$ with $2 \le k \le p$. Conjugating $\tau$ by powers of $\sigma$ gives all tr
anspositions
$$\sigma^{i}\tau\sigma^{-i} = (\sigma^{i}(1)\;\sigma^{i}(k)) = (i{+}1\;\; i{+}k)
, \qquad i \in \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$$
(indices mod $p$). These are the transpositions $(j,\; j + (k{-}1))$ for all $j$
. Since $p$ is prime and $1 \le k-1 \le p-1$, we have $\gcd(k-1, p) = 1$, so the
 step size $k-1$ generates $\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$. In particular, by composing
 these transpositions we obtain every adjacent transposition $(j\;\;j{+}1)$, and
 the adjacent transpositions generate all of $S_p$. Hence $G = S_p$.

**Conclusion.** The Galois group of $f(x)$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ is the full symmetr
ic group $S_p$.
