We evaluate

$$I=\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{\log\!\left(x+\tfrac{1}{x}\right)}{1+x^{2}}\,dx.$$

**Step 1: Substitute $x=\tan\theta$.** Then $dx=\sec^{2}\theta\,d\theta$, $1+x^{
2}=\sec^{2}\theta$, and as $x:0\to\infty$ we have $\theta:0\to\pi/2$. Hence

$$I=\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\log\!\left(\tan\theta+\cot\theta\right)\,d\theta.$$

**Step 2: Simplify the logarithm.** Using

$$\tan\theta+\cot\theta=\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}+\frac{\cos\theta}{\sin\the
ta}=\frac{\sin^{2}\theta+\cos^{2}\theta}{\sin\theta\cos\theta}=\frac{1}{\sin\the
ta\cos\theta}=\frac{2}{\sin 2\theta},$$

we get

$$I=\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\log\!\left(\frac{2}{\sin 2\theta}\right)d\theta=\int_{0}^{\
pi/2}\log 2\,d\theta-\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\log(\sin 2\theta)\,d\theta.$$

**Step 3: First term.**

$$\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\log 2\,d\theta=\frac{\pi}{2}\log 2.$$

**Step 4: Second term.** Let $u=2\theta$, $du=2\,d\theta$:

$$\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\log(\sin 2\theta)\,d\theta=\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{\pi}\log(\sin
 u)\,du.$$

**Step 5: The standard integral $\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\log(\sin\theta)\,d\theta=-\tfr
ac{\pi}{2}\log 2$.** For completeness, here is the derivation. Let

$$J=\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\log(\sin\theta)\,d\theta.$$

The substitution $\theta\mapsto \pi/2-\theta$ gives $J=\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\log(\cos
\theta)\,d\theta$. Therefore

$$2J=\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\log(\sin\theta\cos\theta)\,d\theta=\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\log\!\
left(\tfrac{1}{2}\sin 2\theta\right)d\theta=\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\log(\sin 2\theta)\,
d\theta-\frac{\pi}{2}\log 2.$$

With $u=2\theta$,

$$\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\log(\sin 2\theta)\,d\theta=\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{\pi}\log(\sin
 u)\,du=\frac{1}{2}\cdot 2\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\log(\sin u)\,du=J,$$

where the last equality uses the symmetry $\sin(\pi-u)=\sin u$. Substituting bac
k,

$$2J=J-\frac{\pi}{2}\log 2 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad J=-\frac{\pi}{2}\log 2.$$

Consequently $\int_{0}^{\pi}\log(\sin u)\,du=2J=-\pi\log 2$, and

$$\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\log(\sin 2\theta)\,d\theta=\frac{1}{2}(-\pi\log 2)=-\frac{\pi
}{2}\log 2.$$

**Step 6: Combine.**

$$I=\frac{\pi}{2}\log 2-\left(-\frac{\pi}{2}\log 2\right)=\frac{\pi}{2}\log 2+\f
rac{\pi}{2}\log 2=\pi\log 2.$$

---

$$\boxed{\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{\log\!\left(x+\tfrac{1}{x}\right)}{
1+x^{2}}\,dx=\pi\log 2.}$$
