### Step 1: Compute the partial derivatives

$$f_x = 3x^2 + 3y^2 - 36x - 18y + 57, \qquad f_y = 6xy - 18x - 36y + 138.$$

Setting $f_x = f_y = 0$ and dividing by common factors:

$$x^2 + y^2 - 12x - 6y + 19 = 0 \tag{1}$$
$$xy - 3x - 6y + 23 = 0 \tag{2}$$

### Step 2: Solve the system

From (2): $x(y-3) = 6y - 23$, so $x = \dfrac{6y-23}{y-3}$.

Substituting into (1) and multiplying through by $(y-3)^2$ yields:

$$y^4 - 12y^3 + 28y^2 + 48y - 128 = 0.$$

Testing roots: $y=2$ gives $16-96+112+96-128=0$ ✓; $y=4$ gives $256-768+448+192-
128=0$ ✓. Dividing by $(y-2)(y-4) = y^2-6y+8$ gives quotient $y^2-6y-16$, whose
roots are $y = 8$ and $y = -2$.

So $y \in \{2, 4, 8, -2\}$, giving the critical points:

| $y$ | $x = \frac{6y-23}{y-3}$ | Point |
|-----|--------------------------|-------|
| 2 | 11 | $(11, 2)$ |
| 4 | 1 | $(1, 4)$ |

### Step 3: Classify via the Hessian

$$f_{xx} = 6x-36,\quad f_{yy} = 6x-36,\quad f_{xy} = 6y-18.$$
$$H = f_{xx}f_{yy} - f_{xy}^2 = (6x-36)^2 - (6y-18)^2.$$

| Point | $f_{xx}$ | $f_{xy}$ | $H$ | Classification |

The local extrema are the **minimum at $(11,2)$** and the **maximum at $(1,4)$**
