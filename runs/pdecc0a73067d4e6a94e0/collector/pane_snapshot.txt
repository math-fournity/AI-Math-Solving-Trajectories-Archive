# Volume of $A \cup B \cup C$

## Setup

The three sets are solid cylinders of radius 1 and height 2 along each axis:
- $A$: cylinder along the $x$-axis, $V(A) = 2\pi$
- $B$: cylinder along the $y$-axis, $V(B) = 2\pi$
- $C$: cylinder along the $z$-axis, $V(C) = 2\pi$

By inclusion-exclusion and the full symmetry under permutation of coordinates:

$$V(A\cup B\cup C) = 6\pi - 3\,V(A\cap B) + V(A\cap B\cap C)$$

## Pairwise intersection $V(A\cap B)$

$A\cap B = \{(|x|\le 1,\; y^2+z^2\le 1) \;\wedge\; (|y|\le 1,\; z^2+x^2\le 1)\}$
, which reduces to $z^2 \le \min(1-x^2,\, 1-y^2)$ over $|x|,|y|\le 1$.

$$V(A\cap B) = \int_{-1}^{1}\!\int_{-1}^{1} 2\sqrt{\min(1-x^2,1-y^2)}\;dy\,dx$$

By symmetry in $x\leftrightarrow y$, split into $x^2\le y^2$ and $x^2\ge y^2$:

$$= 4\int_{-1}^{1}\sqrt{1-y^2}\left(\int_{-|y|}^{|y|}dx\right)dy = 8\int_{-1}^{1
}|y|\sqrt{1-y^2}\,dy = 16\int_0^1 y\sqrt{1-y^2}\,dy$$

Substituting $u=1-y^2$: $= 16\cdot\tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\tfrac{2}{3} = \tfrac{16}{3}$
.

(This is the classical Steinmetz solid volume, confirming correctness.)

## Triple intersection $V(A\cap B\cap C)$

$$A\cap B\cap C = \{x^2+y^2\le 1,\; y^2+z^2\le 1,\; z^2+x^2\le 1\}$$

(The $|x|,|y|,|z|\le 1$ constraints are automatic.) Integrate over $z$ first. Fo
r fixed $z$ with $|z|\le 1$, setting $s=\sqrt{1-z^2}$, the $(x,y)$-cross-section
 is the disk $x^2+y^2\le 1$ intersected with the square $|x|,|y|\le s$.

**Case $z^2 \ge \tfrac12$** ($s \le \tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$): the square lies entirel
y inside the disk (corner $(s,s)$ satisfies $2s^2\le 1$). Area $= 4s^2 = 4(1-z^2
)$.

**Case $z^2 < \tfrac12$** ($s > \tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$): subtract four non-overlappi
ng circular segments from the disk. Each segment (beyond $|x|>s$ or $|y|>s$) has
 area $\arcsin(z) - z\sqrt{1-z^2}$. Area $= \pi - 4\bigl(\arcsin(z) - z\sqrt{1-z
^2}\bigr)$.

By even symmetry, $V = 2\int_0^1 A(z)\,dz$, splitting at $z = 1/\sqrt{2}$:

$$V = 2\left[\underbrace{\int_0^{1/\sqrt2}\!\pi\,dz}_{I_1} - \underbrace{\int_0^
{1/\sqrt2}\!4\arcsin z\,dz}_{I_2} + \underbrace{\int_0^{1/\sqrt2}\!4z\sqrt{1-z^2
}\,dz}_{I_3} + \underbrace{\int_{1/\sqrt2}^{1}\!4(1-z^2)\,dz}_{I_4}\right]$$

- $I_1 = \dfrac{\pi}{\sqrt2}$
- $I_2 = 4\Big[z\arcsin z + \sqrt{1-z^2}\Big]_0^{1/\sqrt2} = \dfrac{\pi}{\sqrt2}
 + 2\sqrt2 - 4$
- $I_3 = \dfrac{4}{3}\Big(1 - \dfrac{1}{2\sqrt2}\Big) = \dfrac{4}{3} - \dfrac{\s
qrt2}{3}$
- $I_4 = 4\Big[z - \tfrac{z^3}{3}\Big]_{1/\sqrt2}^{1} = \dfrac{8}{3} - \dfrac{5\
sqrt2}{3}$

Combining:

$$I_1 - I_2 + I_3 + I_4 = \frac{\pi}{\sqrt2} - \frac{\pi}{\sqrt2} - 2\sqrt2 + 4
+ \frac{4}{3} - \frac{\sqrt2}{3} + \frac{8}{3} - \frac{5\sqrt2}{3}$$

$$= \bigl(-2 - \tfrac{1}{3} - \tfrac{5}{3}\bigr)\sqrt2 + \bigl(4 + \tfrac{12}{3}
\bigr) = -4\sqrt2 + 8$$

$$V(A\cap B\cap C) = 2(8 - 4\sqrt2) = 16 - 8\sqrt2$$

## Final assembly

$$V(A\cup B\cup C) = 6\pi - 3\cdot\frac{16}{3} + (16 - 8\sqrt2) = 6\pi - 16 + 16
 - 8\sqrt2$$

$$\boxed{V(A\cup B\cup C) = 6\pi - 8\sqrt{2}}$$

### PROOF COMPLETE
