## Proof

The crate is a rectangular prism with dimensions $20 \times 1 \times 7$ meters,
assembled from $1\text{ m}^3$ cubic containers. The number of containers is

$$20 \times 1 \times 7 = 140.$$

The gold sealant coats the **entire exterior surface** of the crate and nothing
on the interior (where containers touch). Each face of a unit cube has area $1\t
ext{ m}^2$, so the number of coated unit faces equals the total exterior surface
 area of the crate (measured in $\text{m}^2$).

The surface area of a rectangular prism with side lengths $a, b, c$ is $2(ab + b
c + ca)$, so

$$\text{Coated faces} = 2(20\cdot 1 + 20\cdot 7 + 1\cdot 7) = 2(20 + 140 + 7) =
2 \cdot 167 = 334.$$

Now consider the random experiment:
- A container is selected **uniformly at random** from the 140 containers (proba
bility $1/140$ each).
- It is dropped so that **one of its 6 faces is uniformly and randomly** facing
upward (probability $1/6$ each).

Equivalently, we are selecting uniformly at random one face from among all faces
 of all containers. The total number of unit faces across all containers is

$$140 \times 6 = 840.$$

Of these, exactly $334$ are coated (the exterior faces), and the remaining $840
- 334 = 506$ are interior touching faces (uncoated).

Therefore the probability that the upward face is coated is

$$\frac{334}{840} = \frac{167}{420}.$$

To confirm this is fully reduced: $167$ is prime (it is not divisible by $2, 3,
5, 7, 11, 13$, and $13^2 = 169 > 167$), and $420 = 2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 7$
shares no common factor with $167$. Hence $\gcd(167, 420) = 1$.

$$\boxed{\dfrac{167}{420}}$$
