**Step 2: $f$ does not factor as a product of two irreducible quadratics over $\

$$a + c = j, \quad b + d + ac = j+1, \quad ad + bc = 1, \quad bd = j^2.$$

$ad + bc = 1$:

- $a(d + b) + bj = as + bj = 1$, so $b = (1 + as) \cdot j^{-1} = (1+as)(j^2+1)$

We systematically test all $a \in \mathbb{F}_8$:

| $a$ | $c$ | $s = b{+}d$ | $b$ | $d$ | $bd$ | $= j^2$? |

**Step 3: $f$ is irreducible over $\mathbb{F}_8$.**

A degree-4 polynomial over a field either has a root (linear factor), factors as

**Step 4: Determine the Galois group.**

$$\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb{F}_{8^4}/\mathbb{F}_8) \cong \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}

of order 4:

$$\boxed{\operatorname{Gal}(f/\mathbb{F}_8) \cong \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}.}$$
