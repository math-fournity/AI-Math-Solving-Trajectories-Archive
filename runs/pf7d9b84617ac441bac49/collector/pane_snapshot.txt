## Proof

Let $P$ be an orthogonal projection on a Hilbert space $H$. We determine the cen
ter of the algebra $\mathcal{A} := PB(H)P$.

**Step 1: Structure of $PB(H)P$.**

Since $P$ is an orthogonal projection, $H = M \oplus N$ where $M = \operatorname
{ran}(P)$ and $N = \ker(P)$. With respect to this decomposition, every $A \in B(
H)$ has block form
$$A = \begin{pmatrix} A_{11} & A_{12} \\ A_{21} & A_{22} \end{pmatrix},$$
and
$$PAP = \begin{pmatrix} A_{11} & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}.$$
As $A_{11}$ ranges over $B(M)$, the set $PB(H)P$ is precisely
$$\mathcal{A} = \left\{ \begin{pmatrix} B & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} : B \in B(M
) \right\},$$
which is a unital algebra with unit $P = \begin{pmatrix} I_M & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{p
matrix}$, and is isomorphic to $B(M)$ via $B \mapsto \begin{pmatrix} B & 0 \\ 0
& 0 \end{pmatrix}$.

**Step 2: Center of $B(M)$.**

We claim $\mathcal{Z}(B(M)) = \mathbb{C}\, I_M$. The inclusion $\mathbb{C}\, I_M
 \subseteq \mathcal{Z}(B(M))$ is trivial. For the reverse, suppose $T \in B(M)$
commutes with every bounded operator on $M$. If $M = \{0\}$ this is vacuous (and
 gives $\{0\} = \mathbb{C}\cdot 0$). Otherwise, fix any unit vector $x \in M$ an
d any $y \in M$. Consider the rank-one operator $\theta_{x,y}(z) = \langle z, x\
rangle y$. The commutation relation $T\theta_{x,y} = \theta_{x,y}T$ gives, for a
ll $z \in M$:
$$\langle z, x\rangle\, Ty = \langle Tz, x\rangle\, y.$$
Setting $z = x$ (with $\|x\| = 1$):
$$Ty = \langle Tx, x\rangle\, y.$$
The scalar $\lambda := \langle Tx, x\rangle$ is independent of $y$, so $Ty = \la
mbda y$ for all $y \in M$, i.e., $T = \lambda I_M$.

**Step 3: Conclusion.**

An element $T \in \mathcal{A}$ lies in the center $\mathcal{Z}(\mathcal{A})$ iff
 its corresponding $B \in B(M)$ lies in $\mathcal{Z}(B(M)) = \mathbb{C}\, I_M$,
i.e., $B = \lambda I_M$ for some $\lambda \in \mathbb{C}$. Therefore
$$T = \begin{pmatrix} \lambda I_M & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} = \lambda \begin{pm
atrix} I_M & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} = \lambda P.$$

Hence the center of $PB(H)P$ is
$$\boxed{\mathcal{Z}(PB(H)P) = \mathbb{C}\, P = \{\lambda P : \lambda \in \mathb
b{C}\}.}$$
