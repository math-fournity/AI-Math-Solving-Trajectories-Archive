## Problem

Consider the sequence {c_n}, where c_1 = a_1 + a_2 + ... + a_8, c_2 = a_1^2 + a_2^2 + ... + a_8^2, ..., c_n = a_1^n + a_2^n + ... + a_8^n, in which a_1, a_2, ..., a_8 are real numbers not all equal to zero. Suppose that an infinite number of terms of the sequence {c_n} are equal to zero. Find all natural numbers n such that c_n = 0.
