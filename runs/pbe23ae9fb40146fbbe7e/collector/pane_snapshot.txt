 $\ell_1^n$ and $\ell_2^n$ (the only pair with $p,q \in \{1,2\}, p \neq q$).

**Answer: $\sqrt{n}$.**

---

## Proof

The Banach-Mazur distance is
$$d_{BM}(\ell_2^n, \ell_1^n) = \inf_{T\text{ iso}} \|T\|_{2\to 1}\,\|T^{-1}\|_{1
\to 2}.$$

### Upper bound: $\sqrt{n}$

Take $T = I$. Then
$$\|I\|_{2\to 1} = \max_{\|x\|_2=1}\|x\|_1 = \sqrt{n} \quad (\text{at } x=(1/\sq
rt n,\dots,1/\sqrt n)),$$
$$\|I\|_{1\to 2} = \max_{\|x\|_1=1}\|x\|_2 = 1 \quad (\text{at } x=e_i).$$
So $d_{BM} \le \sqrt n\cdot 1 = \sqrt n$.

### Lower bound: $\sqrt{n}$

For any invertible $A$ (matrix of $T$), set $f(x)=\|Ax\|_1$. Then
$$\|T\|_{2\to 1}\|T^{-1}\|_{1\to 2} = \frac{\max_{\|x\|_2=1}f(x)}{\min_{\|x\|_2=
1}f(x)}.$$

Equivalently, with the ellipsoid $E = A(B_2^n)$, this ratio equals the minimal $
R$ such that (after rescaling) $B_1^n \subseteq E' \subseteq R\,B_1^n$ for some
ellipsoid $E'$. So it suffices to show:

> For every ellipsoid $E = A B_2^n$ with $B_1^n \subseteq E \subseteq R\,B_1^n$,
 we have $R \ge \sqrt n$.

The inclusion $E \subseteq R\,B_1^n$ gives $\max_{\|y\|_2\le 1}\|Ay\|_1 \le R$.
Using $\|Ay\|_1 = \max_{\varepsilon\in\{-1,1\}^n}\langle \varepsilon, Ay\rangle
= \max_\varepsilon \langle A^T\varepsilon, y\rangle$,
$$R \;\ge\; \max_{\|y\|_2=1}\|Ay\|_1 \;=\; \max_{\varepsilon\in\{-1,1\}^n}\|A^T\
varepsilon\|_2.$$
Averaging over the $2^n$ sign vectors (using $\mathbb E\,\varepsilon_i\varepsilo
n_j=\delta_{ij}$):
$$\max_{\varepsilon}\|A^T\varepsilon\|_2^2 \;\ge\; \mathbb E_\varepsilon\|A^T\va
repsilon\|_2^2 \;=\; \sum_{j=1}^n\mathbb E\Big(\sum_i \varepsilon_i a_{ji}\Big)^
2 = \sum_{j,i}a_{ji}^2 = \|A\|_F^2.$$
Hence $R \ge \|A\|_F$.

The inclusion $B_1^n \subseteq A B_2^n$ is equivalent to $A^{-1}B_1^n \subseteq
B_2^n$. By convexity it suffices that the vertices $\pm e_i$ of $B_1^n$ map insi
de $B_2^n$, i.e. $\|A^{-1}e_i\|_2 \le 1$ for all $i$. Let $B=A^{-1}$ with column
s $b_i$; thus $\|b_i\|_2\le 1$.

Now
$$\|A\|_F^2 = \|B^{-1}\|_F^2 = \operatorname{tr}\big((BB^T)^{-1}\big) = \sum_{k=
1}^n \frac{1}{\lambda_k},$$
where $\lambda_1,\dots,\lambda_n>0$ are the eigenvalues of $BB^T=\sum_i b_i b_i^
T$. Their sum satisfies
$$\sum_k \lambda_k = \operatorname{tr}(BB^T) = \sum_i \|b_i\|_2^2 \le n.$$
By the AM–HM inequality,
$$\sum_{k=1}^n \frac{1}{\lambda_k} \;\ge\; \frac{n^2}{\sum_k \lambda_k} \;\ge\;
\frac{n^2}{n} = n.$$
Therefore $\|A\|_F \ge \sqrt n$, and consequently
$$R \;\ge\; \|A\|_F \;\ge\; \sqrt n.$$

### Conclusion

Both bounds match:
$$\boxed{d_{BM}(\ell_1^n,\ell_2^n) = \sqrt n.}$$
