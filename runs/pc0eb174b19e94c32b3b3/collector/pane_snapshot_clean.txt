## Proof

Define the following variables:
- $s$ = initial sludge volume per acre
- $r$ = seep rate (volume per acre per week)
- $p$ = processing rate (volume per filtration unit per week)

**Case 1: 3 units, 2 acres, 2 weeks.**

The sludge to be cleared is the initial sludge on 2 acres plus the seepage over
2 weeks:
$$\text{Total sludge} = 2s + 2 \cdot r \cdot 2 = 2s + 4r.$$

The total processing capacity is:
$$\text{Total processing} = 3p \cdot 2 = 6p.$$

Since the reservoir is exactly cleared:
$$6p = 2s + 4r \quad \Longrightarrow \quad 3p = s + 2r. \tag{1}$$

**Case 2: 2 units, 2 acres, 4 weeks.**

$$\text{Total sludge} = 2s + 2 \cdot r \cdot 4 = 2s + 8r.$$
$$\text{Total processing} = 2p \cdot 4 = 8p.$$

Equating:
$$8p = 2s + 8r \quad \Longrightarrow \quad 4p = s + 4r. \tag{2}$$

**Solving the system.**

From (1): $s = 3p - 2r$. Substituting into (2):
$$4p = (3p - 2r) + 4r = 3p + 2r,$$
$$p = 2r \quad \Longrightarrow \quad r = \tfrac{p}{2}.$$

Back-substituting:
$$s = 3p - 2\cdot\tfrac{p}{2} = 3p - p = 2p.$$

So the initial sludge per acre is $s = 2p$ and the seep rate per acre per week i
s $r = p/2$.

**Case 3: 6 acres, 6 weeks, $n$ units.**

$$\text{Total sludge} = 6s + 6 \cdot r \cdot 6 = 6s + 36r.$$
$$\text{Total processing} = n \cdot p \cdot 6 = 6np.$$

Equating:
$$6np = 6s + 36r \quad \Longrightarrow \quad np = s + 6r.$$

Substituting $s = 2p$ and $r = p/2$:
$$np = 2p + 6 \cdot \tfrac{p}{2} = 2p + 3p = 5p.$$

Since $p \neq 0$:
$$n = 5.$$

**The director must assign 5 filtration units.**
