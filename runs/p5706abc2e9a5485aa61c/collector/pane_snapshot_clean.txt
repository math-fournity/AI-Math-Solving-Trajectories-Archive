## Solution

**Setup.** Each disc is a square divided by its two diagonals into 4 congruent r
ight-triangular zones. Each disc has exactly one coated zone, and the 5 discs ha
ve distinct colors (Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Purple), making the discs **distin
guishable**.

When a disc is placed in the stack at rotation 0°, 90°, 180°, or 270°, its singl
e coated zone lands in exactly one of the 4 fixed triangular zones of the aligne
d stack. Thus each disc's rotation is equivalent to **choosing one of 4 zones**
to cover.

**Reformulation.** We need to count the number of ways to assign each of 5 disti
nguishable discs to one of 4 distinguishable zones such that **every zone is cov
ered by at least one disc** (the stack is completely opaque). This is exactly th
e number of **surjective (onto) functions** from a 5-element set to a 4-element
set.

**Counting via Inclusion–Exclusion.**

The total number of unrestricted assignments is $4^5 = 1024$.

Subtract assignments missing at least one zone. By inclusion–exclusion, the numb
er of surjections is:

$$\sum_{k=0}^{4}(-1)^k\binom{4}{k}(4-k)^5$$

Computing each term:

- $k=0$: $\binom{4}{0}\cdot 4^5 = 1 \cdot 1024 = 1024$
- $k=1$: $\binom{4}{1}\cdot 3^5 = 4 \cdot 243 = 972$
- $k=2$: $\binom{4}{2}\cdot 2^5 = 6 \cdot 32 = 192$
- $k=3$: $\binom{4}{3}\cdot 1^5 = 4 \cdot 1 = 4$
- $k=4$: $\binom{4}{4}\cdot 0^5 = 1 \cdot 0 = 0$

$$1024 - 972 + 192 - 4 + 0 = 240$$

**Verification via Stirling numbers.** A surjection from 5 elements onto 4 zones
 corresponds to partitioning the 5 discs into 4 nonempty groups (one group of si
ze 2, three of size 1), then ordering the groups onto the 4 zones:

$$S(5,4)\cdot 4! = \binom{5}{2}\cdot 4! = 10 \cdot 24 = 240.$$

Both methods agree.

**Answer.** The number of unique configurations of the five-disc stack that resu
lt in a completely opaque assembly is $\boxed{240}$.
