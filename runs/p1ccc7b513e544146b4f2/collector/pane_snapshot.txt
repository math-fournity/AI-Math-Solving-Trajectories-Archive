1. $\ker S$ is finite-dimensional,
2. $\operatorname{ran} S$ is closed in $X$, and
3. $\operatorname{coker} S := X / \operatorname{ran} S$ is finite-dimensional.

The *Fredholm index* is then
$$\mathrm{ind}(S) = \dim \ker S - \dim \operatorname{coker} S.$$

< \infty$. ✓

Thus $T$ is Fredholm.

$$\mathrm{ind}(T) = \dim \ker T - \dim \operatorname{coker} T = 0 - 0 = 0.$$
