**Intersections $K$ and $L$.** Solving the tangent equations at $A$ and $B$ give

**Finding $P$.** The line through $K$ parallel to $AB$ is $K+t\,d_1$; the line t

\[4pt] t\cos u-s\cos v=\dfrac{\sin v}{\cos q}-\dfrac{\sin u}{\cos p}. \end{cases
}$$
The determinant is $\sin(u-v)=-\sin r$. By Cramer's rule,
$$t=\frac{\cos(u-v)/\cos p-1/\cos q}{\sin(u-v)}=\frac{\cos r/\cos p-1/\cos q}{-\
sin r}=\frac{1/\cos q-\cos r/\cos p}{\sin r}=\frac{\cos p-\cos r\cos q}{\sin r\c

Since $r+q=\frac{\gamma-\beta}{2}+\frac{\alpha-\gamma}{2}=\frac{\alpha-\beta}{2}

$$t=-\frac{\sin q}{\cos p\cos q}.$$

**Verifying $BP=CP$.** Since $|B|=|C|=1$, the condition $BP=CP$ is equivalent to

$$C-B=2\sin r\,(-\sin w,\cos w),$$

\begin{align*}
