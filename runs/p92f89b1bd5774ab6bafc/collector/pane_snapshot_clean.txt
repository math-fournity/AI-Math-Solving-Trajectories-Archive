$$\sigma(K) = \left\{\tfrac{1}{n} : n \in \mathbb{N}\right\} \cup \{0\}.$$

$$r(K) = \sup\{|\lambda| : \lambda \in \sigma(K)\} = \sup_{n \ge 1} \frac{1}{n}

$$\|Kx\|^2 = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{|x_n|^2}{n^2} \le \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} |x_

$$\|K\|_{\infty} = \sup_{\|x\|=1} \|Kx\| = 1.$$
