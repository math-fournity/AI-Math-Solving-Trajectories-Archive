**$\partial M \cong \mathbb{R}P^3$.**

The Riemannian metric identifies $T^*S^2 \cong TS^2$, so $UT^*S^2 \cong UTS^2$,
the unit tangent bundle of $S^2$.

A point of $UTS^2$ is a pair $(p, v)$ with $p \in S^2 \subset \mathbb{R}^3$ and
$v \in T_p S^2$, $\|v\|=1$. Since $p$ is a unit normal to $S^2$ at $p$, the trip
le $(p,\, v,\, p \times v)$ is an oriented orthonormal frame of $\mathbb{R}^3$,
i.e., an element of $SO(3)$. Conversely, any $A \in SO(3)$ has columns $(e_1, e_
2, e_3)$ forming an oriented orthonormal frame; setting $p = e_1$ and $v = e_2$

his gives a diffeomorphism
$$UTS^2 \;\xrightarrow{\;\cong\;}\; SO(3).$$
