## Proof

**Setup.** Let $\omega$ have center $O$ and radius $r$. The four sensors $A, B,
C, D$ lie on $\omega$ in clockwise order. Define
$$E = \overline{AB}\cap\overline{DC}, \qquad F = \overline{AD}\cap\overline{BC}.
$$
The tangent from $E$ to $\omega$ has length $t_E = 3$, and the tangent from $F$
to $\omega$ has length $t_F = 4$.

**Power of a point.** Since $E$ lies on lines $AB$ and $DC$,
$$\operatorname{Pow}(E) = EA\cdot EB = EC\cdot ED = |OE|^2 - r^2 = t_E^2 = 9.$$
Likewise
$$\operatorname{Pow}(F) = FA\cdot FD = FB\cdot FC = |OF|^2 - r^2 = t_F^2 = 16.$$

**Key claim: $E$ and $F$ are conjugate with respect to $\omega$.**

Consider the complete quadrilateral formed by the four lines $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, $
DA$, all inscribed in the conic $\omega$. Its three diagonal points are
$$E = AB\cap CD,\quad F = AD\cap BC,\quad G = AC\cap BD.$$
A classical theorem on poles and polars states that **the three diagonal points
of a complete quadrilateral inscribed in a conic form a self-polar triangle**: t
he polar of each diagonal point is the line joining the other two. In particular
:
- the polar of $E$ is line $FG$ (which passes through $F$),
- the polar of $F$ is line $EG$ (which passes through $E$).

Hence $E$ lies on the polar of $F$ and $F$ lies on the polar of $E$; i.e., $E$ a
nd $F$ are conjugate points.

**Conjugacy in coordinates.** Place $O$ at the origin. The polar of a point with
 position vector $\mathbf{f}$ is the line $\{\mathbf{x} : \mathbf{f}\cdot\mathbf
{x} = r^2\}$. Since $E$ (position vector $\mathbf{e}$) lies on the polar of $F$,
$$\mathbf{e}\cdot\mathbf{f} = r^2.$$

**Computing $EF$.** Using $|EF|^2 = |\mathbf{e}-\mathbf{f}|^2 = |\mathbf{e}|^2 +
 |\mathbf{f}|^2 - 2\,\mathbf{e}\cdot\mathbf{f}$ and substituting $\mathbf{e}\cdo
t\mathbf{f}=r^2$:
$$EF^2 = |OE|^2 + |OF|^2 - 2r^2.$$
On the other hand,
$$t_E^2 + t_F^2 = (|OE|^2 - r^2) + (|OF|^2 - r^2) = |OE|^2 + |OF|^2 - 2r^2.$$
Therefore
$$\boxed{EF^2 = t_E^2 + t_F^2}.$$

**Final computation.** With $t_E = 3$ and $t_F = 4$:
$$EF = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{9+16} = \sqrt{25} = 5.$$

The total length of the line segment $EF$ is $\boxed{5}$ kilometers.
