Looking at this problem, I need to determine whether $\lim_{n \to \infty} \sum_{
i=1}^n \prod_{j=n-i+1}^n a_j = 0$ given $a_n \to 0$, $a_n \in (0,1)$, and $\sum
a_n = \infty$.

## Proof

**Setup and recurrence.** Define $S_n = \sum_{i=1}^n \prod_{j=n-i+1}^n a_j$. Rei
ndexing with $k = n-i+1$ gives $S_n = \sum_{k=1}^n \prod_{j=k}^n a_j$, with $S_1
 = a_1$.

For $n \geq 2$, factoring out $a_n$:
$$S_n = a_n + a_n\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}\prod_{j=k}^{n-1} a_j = a_n + a_n S_{n-1} = a_n
(S_{n-1}+1). \tag{$\star$}$$

Since all $a_j > 0$, we have $S_n \geq 0$ for all $n$.

**Step 1: $S_n$ eventually drops below 1.**

Fix $\delta \in (0, 1/2)$. Since $a_n \to 0$, there exists $N$ such that $a_n <
\delta$ for all $n > N$.

For any $n > N$ with $S_{n-1} \geq 1$:
$$S_n = a_n(S_{n-1}+1) < \delta(S_{n-1}+1) \leq 2\delta\, S_{n-1} < S_{n-1}.$$

So while $S_{n-1} \geq 1$, the sequence strictly decreases, and by induction $S_
{N+k} < (2\delta)^k S_N$. Since $2\delta < 1$, we get $(2\delta)^k S_N < 1$ for
all sufficiently large $k$. Hence there exists $m > N$ with $S_m < 1$.

**Step 2: Bounding the limsup.**

For $n > m$ (so $a_n < \delta$), define $g(x) = \delta(x+1)$. From ($\star$):
$$S_n = a_n(S_{n-1}+1) \leq \delta(S_{n-1}+1) = g(S_{n-1}).$$

Since $g$ is increasing, by induction: $S_{m+k} \leq g^k(S_m) \leq g^k(1)$.

The map $g(x) = \delta x + \delta$ has fixed point $x^* = \frac{\delta}{1-\delta
}$, and:
$$g(x) - x^* = \delta(x - x^*), \quad\text{so}\quad g^k(1) = x^* + \delta^k(1 -
x^*).$$

Since $\delta < 1$, $\delta^k \to 0$, giving:
$$\limsup_{n \to \infty} S_n \leq \lim_{k\to\infty} g^k(1) = \frac{\delta}{1-\de
lta}.$$

**Step 3: Conclusion.**

The choice of $\delta \in (0, 1/2)$ was arbitrary, so:
$$\limsup_{n \to \infty} S_n \leq \inf_{\delta \in (0,1/2)} \frac{\delta}{1-\del
ta} = 0.$$

Combined with $S_n \geq 0$, we conclude:
$$\lim_{n \to \infty} \sum_{i=1}^n \prod_{j=n-i+1}^n a_j = 0.$$

**Remark.** Only $a_n \to 0$ and $a_n \in (0,1)$ are used; the divergence condit
ion $\sum a_n = \infty$ is not needed for this conclusion.
