## Proof

**Answer: Yes.** We show that the hypothesis forces $T$ to be trace-class.

Assume, for a contradiction, that $T$ is **not** trace-class. Then $|T|=(T^{*}T)
^{1/2}$ is a positive operator with $\operatorname{Tr}(|T|)=\infty$.

Let $T=U|T|$ be the polar decomposition, where $U$ is a partial isometry with in
itial space $\overline{\operatorname{ran}|T|}=(\ker |T|)^{\perp}$ and final spac
e $\overline{\operatorname{ran}T}$. Set $P$ to be the orthogonal projection onto
 $\overline{\operatorname{ran}|T|}$, the initial space of $U$. Then

- $U:PH\to U(PH)=\overline{\operatorname{ran}T}$ is unitary,
- $|T|$ vanishes on $(I-P)H=\ker|T|=\ker T$.

Choose an orthonormal basis $\{e_k\}$ of $PH$ and an orthonormal basis $\{g_k\}$
 of $(I-P)H$. Then $\{Ue_k\}$ is an orthonormal basis of $\overline{\operatornam
e{ran}T}$; extend it to an orthonormal basis of $H$ by adjoining an orthonormal
basis $\{h_k\}$ of $(\overline{\operatorname{ran}T})^{\perp}=\ker T^{*}$.

Define orthonormal bases $\{\xi_j\}$ and $\{\eta_j\}$ of $H$ by interleaving:
$$
\xi_{2k-1}=e_k,\quad \xi_{2k}=g_k,\qquad \eta_{2k-1}=Ue_k,\quad \eta_{2k}=h_k.
$$
(If $PH$ or $(I-P)H$ is finite-dimensional or $\{0\}$, the indexing is adjusted
in the obvious way; the argument is unchanged.)

Now compute the terms of the series. For the odd indices,
$$
\langle T\xi_{2k-1},\eta_{2k-1}\rangle=\langle Te_k,Ue_k\rangle=\langle U|T|e_k,
Ue_k\rangle=\langle |T|e_k,e_k\rangle,
$$
since $U$ is isometric on $PH$. For the even indices, since $g_k\in\ker T$,
$$
\langle T\xi_{2k},\eta_{2k}\rangle=\langle Tg_k,h_k\rangle=0.
$$
Therefore
$$
\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}\langle T\xi_j,\eta_j\rangle=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\langle |T|e_
k,e_k\rangle.
$$

We now use the standard fact that for a positive operator $A$ on a Hilbert space
, the value of $\sum_k\langle Af_k,f_k\rangle\in[0,\infty]$ is **independent** o
f the orthonormal basis $\{f_k\}$ and equals $\operatorname{Tr}(A)$ (this follow
s from the spectral theorem: the sum is the integral of the identity function ag
ainst the basis-independent spectral trace measure). Applying this to the positi
ve operator $|T|$ restricted to $PH$ (equivalently, to $|T|$ on $H$, since it va
nishes on $(I-P)H$),
$$
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\langle |T|e_k,e_k\rangle=\operatorname{Tr}(|T|)=\infty.
$$

Hence $\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}\langle T\xi_j,\eta_j\rangle$ diverges, contradicting
the hypothesis that this series converges for **every** choice of orthonormal ba
ses $\{\xi_j\},\{\eta_j\}$.

The contradiction shows that $T$ must be trace-class. $\blacksquare$
