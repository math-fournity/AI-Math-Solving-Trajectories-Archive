$$d:\; y=\tfrac{x}{12}-5.$$

Intersecting with $AB$: $3x+12=\tfrac{t}{24}-\tfrac{5}{2}-12\left(x-\tfrac{t-4}{

$$y-\left(\tfrac{t}{12}-5\right)=\frac{145t+3396}{12(145t-204)}(x-t).$$
