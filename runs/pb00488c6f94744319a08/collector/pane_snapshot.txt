## Proof

**Strategy.** We show that the given bound $r^q$ (which $p_q$ satisfies) is itse
lf strictly less than $\frac{r^{4q}-2}{r^{2q+1}+1}$ for all $r \geq 5$ and $q \g
eq 6$. Transitivity then yields the result.

**Key claim.** For all integers $r \geq 5$ and $q \geq 6$:
$$r^q < \frac{r^{4q}-2}{r^{2q+1}+1}.$$

*Proof of claim.* Since both sides are positive, cross-multiplication preserves
the inequality. We need:
$$r^q\!\left(r^{2q+1}+1\right) < r^{4q}-2,$$
that is,
$$r^{3q+1}+r^q < r^{4q}-2,$$
equivalently,
$$r^q\!\left(r^{3q}-r^{2q+1}-1\right) > 2. \tag{$\star$}$$

We verify the factor $r^{3q}-r^{2q+1}-1$ is positive and large. Factoring:
$$r^{3q}-r^{2q+1}-1 = r^{2q+1}\!\left(r^{q-1}-1\right)-1.$$

For $r \geq 5,\; q \geq 6$:
- $r^{q-1} \geq 5^5 = 3125$, so $r^{q-1}-1 \geq 3124$.
- $r^{2q+1} \geq 5^{13} = 1{,}220{,}703{,}125$.

Therefore:
$$r^{2q+1}\!\left(r^{q-1}-1\right) \geq 5^{13}\times 3124 = 1{,}220{,}703{,}125
\times 3124 = 3{,}813{,}835{,}755{,}000,$$
so $r^{3q}-r^{2q+1}-1 \geq 3{,}813{,}835{,}754{,}999 > 0$.

Meanwhile $r^q \geq 5^6 = 15{,}625$. Hence:
$$r^q\!\left(r^{3q}-r^{2q+1}-1\right) \geq 15{,}625 \times 3{,}813{,}835{,}754{,
}999 \gg 2.$$

This establishes $(\star)$, proving the claim. $\square$

**Main result.** We are given that $p_q < r^q$. By the claim, $r^q < \frac{r^{4q
}-2}{r^{2q+1}+1}$ for all $r \geq 5$, $q \geq 6$. By transitivity:
$$p_q < r^q < \frac{r^{4q}-2}{r^{2q+1}+1}.$$

Since $r > 4$ is a positive integer, $r \geq 5$, and $q > 5$ means $q \geq 6$. T
he inequality holds for **all** integers $q > 5$.

**Remark on the margin.** The bound is extremely loose: $\frac{r^{4q}-2}{r^{2q+1
}+1} \approx r^{2q-1}$ asymptotically, which is exponentially larger than $r^q$
(let alone $p_q \sim q\ln q$). The gap $r^{4q}-r^{3q+1}-r^q$ grows super-exponen
tially, so the inequality holds with overwhelming margin for every admissible $(
r,q)$.
