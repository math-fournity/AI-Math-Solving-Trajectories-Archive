# Problem

In a futuristic circular city, there are 2017 communication towers positioned at the vertices of a perfect regular polygon. To secure the network, a central hub requires the entire city area to be partitioned into exactly 2015 triangular "security sectors." This must be achieved by installing 2014 fiber-optic cables, where each cable is a straight line (diagonal) connecting two non-adjacent towers, and no cables are allowed to cross one another.

A sector is classified as "Optimized" if it forms an isosceles triangle (a triangle where at least two sides, whether they are fiber-optic cables or the original perimeter boundaries between towers, are equal in length). 

As the lead network architect, you must determine the configuration that maximizes the efficiency of the grid. What is the maximum possible number of Optimized sectors that can be created within this 2017-tower network?
