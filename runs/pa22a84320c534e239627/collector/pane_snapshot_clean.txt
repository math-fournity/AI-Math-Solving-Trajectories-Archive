## Solution

**Setup.** The domain is $Q=(0,\pi)\times(0,2\pi)$ with Dirichlet boundary condi
tions $u|_{\partial Q}=0$ (the condition $u_t|_{\partial Q}=0$ follows by differ
entiating $u|_{\partial Q}=0$ in $t$). The eigenfunctions of $-\Delta$ on $Q$ wi
th Dirichlet boundary conditions are

$$\phi_{nm}(x,y)=\sin(nx)\,\sin\!\Big(\frac{my}{2}\Big),\qquad n\ge1,\;m\ge1,$$

with eigenvalues

$$\lambda_{nm}=n^{2}+\frac{m^{2}}{4}.$$

(For $x\in(0,\pi)$: $\sin(nx)$, eigenvalue $n^2$; for $y\in(0,2\pi)$: $\sin(my/2
)$, eigenvalue $m^2/4$.)

**Fourier expansion.** Write

$$u(x,y,t)=\sum_{n,m\ge1}T_{nm}(t)\,\phi_{nm}(x,y),\qquad xy=\sum_{n,m\ge1}f_{nm
}\,\phi_{nm}(x,y).$$

Since $\|\phi_{nm}\|^2=\int_0^\pi\!\sin^2(nx)\,dx\cdot\int_0^{2\pi}\!\sin^2(my/2
)\,dy=\frac{\pi}{2}\cdot\pi=\frac{\pi^2}{2}$,

$$f_{nm}=\frac{2}{\pi^2}\int_0^\pi\!\int_0^{2\pi}xy\,\sin(nx)\sin\!\Big(\frac{my
}{2}\Big)\,dy\,dx=\frac{2}{\pi^2}\,I_n\,J_m,$$

where

$$I_n=\int_0^\pi x\sin(nx)\,dx=\Big[-\frac{x\cos(nx)}{n}\Big]_0^\pi+\frac1n\int_
0^\pi\cos(nx)\,dx=\frac{\pi(-1)^{n+1}}{n},$$

$$J_m=\int_0^{2\pi}y\sin\!\Big(\frac{my}{2}\Big)\,dy=\Big[-\frac{2y\cos(my/2)}{m
}\Big]_0^{2\pi}+\frac{2}{m}\int_0^{2\pi}\cos\!\Big(\frac{my}{2}\Big)\,dy=\frac{4
\pi(-1)^{m+1}}{m}.$$

Hence

$$f_{nm}=\frac{2}{\pi^2}\cdot\frac{\pi(-1)^{n+1}}{n}\cdot\frac{4\pi(-1)^{m+1}}{m
}=\frac{8(-1)^{n+m}}{nm}.$$

**ODE for the time coefficients.** Substituting into $u_{tt}=u_{xx}+u_{yy}+xy$ a
nd using $\Delta\phi_{nm}=-\lambda_{nm}\phi_{nm}$:

$$T_{nm}''(t)=-\lambda_{nm}\,T_{nm}(t)+f_{nm},\qquad T_{nm}(0)=0,\quad T_{nm}'(0
)=0.$$

This is a forced harmonic oscillator with $\omega_{nm}=\sqrt{\lambda_{nm}}$. The
 particular solution is the constant $f_{nm}/\lambda_{nm}$; the general solution
 is

$$T_{nm}(t)=\frac{f_{nm}}{\lambda_{nm}}+A\cos(\omega_{nm}t)+B\sin(\omega_{nm}t).
$$

From $T_{nm}(0)=0$: $A=-f_{nm}/\lambda_{nm}$. From $T_{nm}'(0)=0$: $B=0$. Theref
ore

$$T_{nm}(t)=\frac{f_{nm}}{\lambda_{nm}}\bigl(1-\cos(\omega_{nm}t)\bigr).$$

**Solution.**

$$\boxed{\;u(x,y,t)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\frac{8\,(-1)^{n+m}}{n
m\!\left(n^{2}+\tfrac{m^{2}}{4}\right)}\Bigl(1-\cos\!\Big(\sqrt{n^{2}+\tfrac{m^{
2}}{4}}\;t\Big)\Bigr)\sin(nx)\,\sin\!\Big(\frac{my}{2}\Big)\;}$$

**Verification of boundary and initial conditions.**

- *Boundary:* On $x=0$ and $x=\pi$, every $\sin(nx)=0$; on $y=0$ and $y=2\pi$, e
very $\sin(my/2)=0$. Hence $u|_{\partial Q}=0$, and consequently $u_t|_{\partial
 Q}=0$.
- *Initial displacement:* At $t=0$, each factor $(1-\cos 0)=0$, so $u(x,y,0)=0$.
- *Initial velocity:* $T_{nm}'(t)=\frac{f_{nm}}{\lambda_{nm}}\,\omega_{nm}\sin(\
omega_{nm}t)$, so $T_{nm}'(0)=0$, giving $u_t(x,y,0)=0$.
- *PDE:* Each term satisfies $T_{nm}''=-\lambda_{nm}T_{nm}+f_{nm}$, so summing r
eproduces $u_{tt}=\Delta u+xy$.
