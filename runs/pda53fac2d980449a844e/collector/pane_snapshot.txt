## Proof

**Setup.** The set $\Omega$ is a torus with major radius $R=2$ (the radius of th
e circle $C$) and minor radius $r=1$ (the distance threshold to $C$).

**Coordinate transformation.** Parametrize $\Omega$ by toroidal coordinates $(\t
heta,\phi)$ with $\theta\in[0,2\pi)$ the angle around the $z$-axis and $\phi\in[
0,2\pi)$ the angle around the tube:

$$x=(2+\cos\phi)\cos\theta,\qquad y=(2+\cos\phi)\sin\theta,\qquad z=\sin\phi.$$

The Jacobian of this map is

$$\frac{\partial(x,y,z)}{\partial(\theta,\phi)}=(2+\cos\phi),$$

so $dx\,dy\,dz=(2+\cos\phi)\,d\theta\,d\phi$.

**The integrand.** Since $2+\cos\phi\ge 1>0$,

$$|x|=(2+\cos\phi)\,|\cos\theta|.$$

**Computing the integral.** The integral separates:

$$\iiint_\Omega |x|\,dx\,dy\,dz=\int_0^{2\pi}\!\!\int_0^{2\pi}(2+\cos\phi)^2\,|\
cos\theta|\,d\phi\,d\theta.$$

Split into two factors.

*Factor 1:*
$$\int_0^{2\pi}|\cos\theta|\,d\theta=4\int_0^{\pi/2}\cos\theta\,d\theta=4.$$

*Factor 2:*
$$\int_0^{2\pi}(2+\cos\phi)^2\,d\phi=\int_0^{2\pi}\bigl(4+4\cos\phi+\cos^2\phi\b
igr)\,d\phi.$$

Using $\int_0^{2\pi}\cos\phi\,d\phi=0$ and $\int_0^{2\pi}\cos^2\phi\,d\phi=\pi$:

$$\int_0^{2\pi}(2+\cos\phi)^2\,d\phi=4\cdot 2\pi+0+\pi=8\pi+\pi=9\pi.$$

**Result.** Multiplying,

$$\iiint_\Omega |x|\,dx\,dy\,dz=9\pi\cdot 4=\boxed{36\pi}.$$
