# Problem

In the coastal city of Gridhaven, an operations center manages a rectangular array of $15$ rows and $20$ columns of independent power stations. Each station can either be "Active" or "Deactivated." 

The grid operates under a specific safety protocol: due to local circuit dependencies, if any three power stations in a $2 \times 2$ block of neighboring stations are deactivated, the fourth station in that specific $2 \times 2$ block will immediately suffer a sympathetic power failure and turn off automatically. This process repeats and cascades across the entire $15 \times 20$ grid until no more stations can be automatically forced off.

A technician wants to manually deactivate a specific number of stations, denoted as $k$, such that even after all possible automatic failures occur, at least one power station in the $15 \times 20$ grid remains "Active." 

What is the maximum possible value of $k$ that ensures the grid can never be fully deactivated?
