# Problem

A specialized logistics firm operates based on a specific efficiency protocol involving two categories of assets: **Upgraded Units ($u$)** and **Mainframe Modules ($m$)**. Both quantities must be represented by positive integers.

A system is considered "Synchronized" if the total resource load, calculated as the sum of the number of Upgraded Units and the square of the number of Mainframe Modules, is perfectly divisible by the "Interference Factor." The Interference Factor is defined as one less than the product of the number of Upgraded Units and the number of Mainframe Modules.

Let $S$ be the set of all possible pairs $(u, m)$ that result in a Synchronized system. Your task is to identify all such pairs and then calculate the grand total sum of $(u + m)$ across every pair in $S$.
