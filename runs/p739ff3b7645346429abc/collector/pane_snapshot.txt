Looking at this problem, I need to find the largest $N$ such that a regular $N$-
gon can have all its sides and diagonals colored with 2018 colors, where no two
edges share the same color.

## Proof

A regular $N$-gon together with all its sides and diagonals forms the complete g
raph $K_N$ on $N$ vertices. The total number of edges (sides plus diagonals) is:
$$\binom{N}{2} = \frac{N(N-1)}{2}.$$

**The "harmonious coloring" condition** requires that no two sides or diagonals
share the same color — that is, every edge must receive a distinct color.

**Upper bound.** Since we have only 2018 colors available and each edge must rec
eive a distinct color, we need:
$$\frac{N(N-1)}{2} \leq 2018 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad N(N-1) \leq 4036.$$

Checking:
- $N = 64$: $\;64 \times 63 = 4032 \leq 4036$ ✓
- $N = 65$: $\;65 \times 64 = 4160 > 4036$ ✗

So $N \leq 64$.

**Construction for $N = 64$.** The complete graph $K_{64}$ has $\binom{64}{2} =
2016$ edges. Since $2016 \leq 2018$, we can assign a distinct color to each of t
he 2016 edges, using 2016 of the 2018 available colors (leaving 2 colors unused)
. In this coloring, no two edges share a color, so the harmonious condition is s
atisfied.

**$N = 65$ is impossible.** $K_{65}$ has $\binom{65}{2} = 2080$ edges, but only
2018 colors are available. By the pigeonhole principle, at least two edges must
share a color, violating the harmonious condition.

Therefore, the largest positive integer $N$ is $\boxed{64}$.
