Looking at this problem, I need to determine whether the radial projection $x \m
apsto x/|x|$, restricted to $\partial\Omega$, is bi-Lipschitz for a bounded conv
ex domain $\Omega$ containing the origin.

**Answer: Yes, the inequality holds.**

## Proof

Since $\Omega$ is an open convex set with $0 \in \Omega$ and $\Omega$ is bounded
, there exist constants $0 < r < R$ such that
$$B(0,r) \subset \Omega \subset B(0,R).$$
Consequently, for every $x \in \partial\Omega$, we have $r \le |x| \le R$.

**The radial projection is a homeomorphism.** Since $\Omega$ is convex and $0 \i
n \Omega$, every ray from the origin intersects $\partial\Omega$ at exactly one
point. (If a ray hit $\partial\Omega$ at two points $t_1 e, t_2 e$ with $t_1 < t
_2$, then $t_1 e$ lies on the segment $[0, t_2 e] \subset \bar\Omega$; since $0
\in \Omega$ (interior) and $t_2 e \in \partial\Omega$, the open segment $(0, t_2
 e) \subset \Omega$, contradicting $t_1 e \in \partial\Omega$.) Thus $\pi: \part
ial\Omega \to S^{n-1}$, $\pi(x) = x/|x|$, is a continuous bijection from a compa
ct space to a Hausdorff space, hence a homeomorphism.

---

### Upper bound: $|\pi(x) - \pi(y)|^2 \le C_1 |x-y|^2$

Write $x|y| - y|x| = (x-y)|y| + y(|y|-|x|)$. Then
$$|x|y| - y|x|| \le |x-y|\cdot|y| + |y|\cdot\big||y|-|x|\big| \le 2|y|\cdot|x-y|
.$$
Dividing by $|x|\cdot|y|$:
$$\left|\frac{x}{|x|} - \frac{y}{|y|}\right| \le \frac{2|x-y|}{|x|} \le \frac{2}
{r}|x-y|.$$
Squaring gives the upper bound with $C_1 = 4/r^2$.

---

### Lower bound: $|\pi(x)-\pi(y)|^2 \ge C_2 |x-y|^2$

This is equivalent to showing $\pi^{-1}: S^{n-1} \to \partial\Omega$ is Lipschit
z. The inverse is $\pi^{-1}(\theta) = \rho(\theta)\,\theta$, where $\rho(\theta)
 = 1/\|\theta\|_\Omega$ is the radial function and $\|\cdot\|_\Omega$ is the Min
kowski functional (gauge) of $\Omega$.

**Step 1: The Minkowski functional is Lipschitz on $S^{n-1}$.**

Since $\Omega$ is convex, open, and contains $0$, the Minkowski functional $\|\c
dot\|_\Omega: \mathbb{R}^n \to [0,\infty)$ is sublinear (subadditive and positiv
ely homogeneous), hence convex. A finite convex function on $\mathbb{R}^n$ is lo
cally Lipschitz; since $S^{n-1}$ is compact, $\|\cdot\|_\Omega$ is Lipschitz on
$S^{n-1}$ with some constant $L > 0$.

Moreover, from $B(0,r) \subset \Omega \subset B(0,R)$, for any unit vector $\the
ta$:
$$\frac{1}{R} \le \|\theta\|_\Omega \le \frac{1}{r}.$$

**Step 2: The radial function $\rho = 1/\|\cdot\|_\Omega$ is Lipschitz on $S^{n-
1}$.**

For $\theta, \phi \in S^{n-1}$:
$$|\rho(\theta) - \rho(\phi)| = \frac{\big|\|\theta\|_\Omega - \|\phi\|_\Omega\b
ig|}{\|\theta\|_\Omega \cdot \|\phi\|_\Omega} \le R^2 \cdot L \cdot |\theta - \p
hi|,$$
since $\|\theta\|_\Omega, \|\phi\|_\Omega \ge 1/R$. So $\rho$ is $(LR^2)$-Lipsch
itz.

**Step 3: $\pi^{-1}$ is Lipschitz.**

For $\theta, \phi \in S^{n-1}$:
$$|\pi^{-1}(\theta) - \pi^{-1}(\phi)| = |\rho(\theta)\theta - \rho(\phi)\phi| \l
e |\rho(\theta)-\rho(\phi)|\cdot|\theta| + \rho(\phi)\cdot|\theta-\phi| \le (LR^
2 + R)|\theta-\phi|.$$

Setting $M = LR^2 + R$, we get $|x - y| \le M\,|\pi(x) - \pi(y)|$ for all $x, y
\in \partial\Omega$, hence
$$|\pi(x) - \pi(y)|^2 \ge \frac{1}{M^2}|x-y|^2.$$

This gives the lower bound with $C_2 = 1/M^2 > 0$.

---

### Conclusion

Both bounds hold with positive constants $C_1 = 4/r^2$ and $C_2 = 1/(LR^2+R)^2$
that depend only on the domain $\Omega$ (through the inradius $r$, circumradius
$R$, and the Lipschitz constant $L$ of the Minkowski functional), and are indepe
ndent of $x$ and $y$.

**The inequality is true.**
