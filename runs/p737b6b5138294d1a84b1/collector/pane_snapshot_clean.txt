## Solution

A processor is "Stable" if its batch number is **squarefree** — i.e., not divisi
ble by any perfect square $> 1$. We need to count squarefree integers in $\{1, 2
, \dots, 1000\}$.

**Method.** The count of squarefree numbers up to $N$ is given by inclusion–excl
usion via the Möbius function:

$$Q(N) = \sum_{d=1}^{\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor} \mu(d)\left\lfloor \frac{N}{d^2}\ri
ght\rfloor.$$

Here $\sqrt{1000} \approx 31.62$, so $d$ ranges from $1$ to $31$.

**Möbius values and computation.** Only $d$ with $\mu(d) \neq 0$ contribute. The
 values with $\mu(d)=0$ (divisible by a square $>1$) are $d \in \{4, 8, 9, 12, 1
6, 18, 20, 24, 25, 27, 28\}$. The remaining $d$ with $\mu(d)\neq 0$:

| $d$ | $\mu(d)$ | $\lfloor 1000/d^2\rfloor$ | contribution |
|-----|----------|---------------------------|--------------|
| 1   | +1 | 1000 | +1000 |
| 2   | −1 | 250  | −250  |
| 3   | −1 | 111  | −111  |
| 5   | −1 | 40   | −40   |
| 6   | +1 | 27   | +27   |
| 7   | −1 | 20   | −20   |
| 10  | +1 | 10   | +10   |
| 11  | −1 | 8    | −8    |
| 13  | −1 | 5    | −5    |
| 14  | +1 | 5    | +5    |
| 15  | +1 | 4    | +4    |
| 17  | −1 | 3    | −3    |
| 19  | −1 | 2    | −2    |
| 21  | +1 | 2    | +2    |
| 22  | +1 | 2    | +2    |
| 23  | −1 | 1    | −1    |
| 26  | +1 | 1    | +1    |
| 29  | −1 | 1    | −1    |
| 30  | −1 | 1    | −1    |
| 31  | −1 | 1    | −1    |

**Running sum:**

$$1000 - 250 - 111 - 40 + 27 - 20 + 10 - 8 - 5 + 5 + 4 - 3 - 2 + 2 + 2 - 1 + 1 -
 1 - 1 - 1 = 608.$$

**Verification of key floor values:**
- $\lfloor 1000/9\rfloor = 111$ (since $9\times111=999$),
- $\lfloor 1000/36\rfloor = 27$ (since $36\times27=972$, $36\times28=1008>1000$)
,
- $\lfloor 1000/49\rfloor = 20$ (since $49\times20=980$),
- $\lfloor 1000/121\rfloor = 8$ (since $121\times8=968$),
- $\lfloor 1000/961\rfloor = 1$ (since $31^2=961$, $2\times961=1922>1000$).

All confirmed. The inclusion–exclusion correctly removes numbers divisible by $p
^2$ for each prime $p \le 31$, adds back those divisible by two distinct prime s
quares, and so on, yielding exactly the squarefree count.
