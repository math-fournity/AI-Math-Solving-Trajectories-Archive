## Solution

**Setup.** Place the center $O$ at the origin. Since segment $PQ$ passes through
 $O$, points $P$ and $Q$ lie on opposite sides of $O$ on a common line; by rotat
ional symmetry, take this line as the $x$-axis with $P = (-p, 0)$ and $Q = (q, 0
)$, where $p, q > 1$.

**Relating distance to tangent angle.** For a point at distance $d$ from $O$, th
e right triangle formed with a tangent point and $O$ gives $\sin(\angle OPP_1) =
 \frac{1}{d}$, so the full tangent angle is $2\arcsin(1/d)$.

- $\angle P_1PP_2 = 45° \Rightarrow 2\arcsin(1/p) = 45° \Rightarrow 1/p = \sin 2
2.5°$, so $p = \csc 22.5°$.
- $\angle Q_1QQ_2 = 30° \Rightarrow 2\arcsin(1/q) = 30° \Rightarrow 1/q = \sin 1
5°$, so $q = \csc 15°$.

**Locating tangent points on the circle.** A point $T = (\cos\theta, \sin\theta)
$ on the unit circle is a tangent point from $X$ iff $X \cdot T = 1$.

- For $P = (-p, 0)$: $-p\cos\theta = 1 \Rightarrow \cos\theta = -1/p = -\sin 22.
5° = \cos 112.5°$. So $P$-tangent points are at $\theta = 112.5°$ and $\theta =
247.5°$.
- For $Q = (q, 0)$: $q\cos\theta = 1 \Rightarrow \cos\theta = 1/q = \sin 15° = \
cos 75°$. So $Q$-tangent points are at $\theta = 75°$ and $\theta = 285°$.

**Computing the minimum arc.** The four tangent points (sorted by angle) are:

$$75°,\quad 112.5°,\quad 247.5°,\quad 285°.$$

The $P$-points are $\{112.5°, 247.5°\}$ and the $Q$-points are $\{75°, 285°\}$.
The arc between a $P$-point and a $Q$-point can be:

| $P$-point | $Q$-point | Arc |
|-----------|-----------|-----|
| $112.5°$ | $75°$ | $37.5°$ |
| $112.5°$ | $285°$ | $172.5°$ |
| $247.5°$ | $75°$ | $172.5°$ |
| $247.5°$ | $285°$ | $37.5°$ |

Choosing the labeling $P_2, Q_2$ to minimize the arc, the minimum is $37.5°$.

**Arc length.** Converting to arc length on the unit circle:

$$37.5° = 37.5 \times \frac{\pi}{180} = \frac{5\pi}{24}.$$

Since the radius is $1$, the arc length equals the angle in radians:

$$\boxed{\dfrac{5\pi}{24}}.$$
