# Problem

In the subterranean server farm of the "Centurion" data complex, there are 100 processing units, each assigned a unique power frequency—an integer ranging from 1 THz to 100 THz. To manage the facility, engineers have arranged these 100 units into a perfectly square grid of 10 rows and 10 columns.

The stability of the grid depends on the "interference load" between physical neighbors. If any two units are positioned directly adjacent to one another—either horizontally or vertically—the sum of their power frequencies must not exceed a specific safety threshold, $S$. 

The facility manager needs to organize the frequencies within the grid to minimize the risk of overheating. What is the minimum possible value of the safety threshold $S$ that allows all 100 unique frequencies to be placed in the 10x10 grid without any pair of adjacent units violating the sum limit?
