## Proof

$$x^6 + 2x^3 - 8 = (x^3 - 2)(x^3 + 4).$$

- Roots of $x^3 - 2$: $\alpha,\ \alpha\omega,\ \alpha\omega^2$.
- Roots of $x^3 + 4$: $-\sqrt[3]{4},\ -\sqrt[3]{4}\,\omega,\ -\sqrt[3]{4}\,\omeg
a^2$. Since $\sqrt[3]{4} = \alpha^2$, these are $-\alpha^2,\ -\alpha^2\omega,\ -
\alpha^2\omega^2$.

All six roots lie in $\mathbb{Q}(\alpha, \omega)$, and conversely $\alpha$ and $
\omega$ are expressible from the roots (e.g., $\alpha$ is a root, and $\omega =
(\alpha\omega)/\alpha$). Therefore the splitting field is
$$K = \mathbb{Q}(\alpha, \omega).$$

**Step 3: Compute $ : \mathbb{Q}]$.**

The polynomial $x^3 - 2$ is irreducible over $\mathbb{Q}$ (Eisenstein at $p=2$),
 so $[\mathbb{Q}(\alpha) : \mathbb{Q}] = 3$. The field $\mathbb{Q}(\alpha) \subs
eteq \mathbb{R}$ is a real field, while $\omega \notin \mathbb{R}$, so $\omega \
notin \mathbb{Q}(\alpha)$. Since $\omega$ satisfies the irreducible quadratic $x
^2 + x + 1$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ (hence over $\mathbb{Q}(\alpha)$), we get $[\mathb
b{Q}(\alpha,\omega) : \mathbb{Q}(\alpha)] = 2$. Thus
$$ : \mathbb{Q}] = 3 \cdot 2 = 6.$$

**Step 4: Determine the Galois group.**

Since $K/\mathbb{Q}$ is a splitting field of a separable polynomial (char $0$),
it is Galois, so $|\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q})| = 6$.

Every $\sigma \in \operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q})$ is determined by its action
on $\alpha$ and $\omega$:
- $\sigma(\alpha) \in \{\alpha,\ \alpha\omega,\ \alpha\omega^2\}$ (roots of $x^3
-2$),
- $\sigma(\omega) \in \{\omega,\ \omega^2\}$ (roots of $x^2+x+1$).

All $3 \times 2 = 6$ combinations are realized (consistent with $|G|=6$). Define

- $\sigma$: $\alpha \mapsto \alpha\omega$, $\omega \mapsto \omega$ (order $3$),
- $\tau$: $\alpha \mapsto \alpha$, $\omega \mapsto \omega^2$ (complex conjugatio
n, order $2$).

These generate $G$. To determine the group structure, compute the conjugation re
lation. We have $\tau^{-1} = \tau$, and:
$$\tau\sigma\tau(\alpha) = \tau\sigma(\alpha) = \tau(\alpha\omega) = \alpha\,\om
ega^2 = \alpha\,\omega^{-1},$$
$$\tau\sigma\tau(\omega) = \tau\sigma(\omega^2) = \tau(\omega^2) = \omega.$$

This equals $\sigma^{-1}$ (which sends $\alpha \mapsto \alpha\omega^2$, $\omega
\mapsto \omega$). Hence
$$\tau\sigma\tau^{-1} = \sigma^{-1}.$$

This is precisely the dihedral relation. Therefore
$$G = \langle \sigma, \tau \mid \sigma^3 = \tau^2 = 1,\ \tau\sigma\tau^{-1} = \s

The group is non-abelian (since $\sigma\tau \neq \tau\sigma$), confirming it is
$S_3$ and not $\mathbb{Z}/6\mathbb{Z}$.

**Conclusion.** The Galois group of $x^6 + 2x^3 - 8$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ is $\bold
symbol{S_3}$ (equivalently, the dihedral group $D_3$ of order $6$).
