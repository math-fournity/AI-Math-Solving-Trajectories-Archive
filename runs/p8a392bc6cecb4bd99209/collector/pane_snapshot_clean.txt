## Step 2: $u$ is Lipschitz continuous

$$\big|\|x\|_\infty - \|y\|_\infty\big| \leq \|x - y\|_\infty \leq \|x-y\|_2.$$

Therefore $u(x) = 1 - \|x\|_\infty$ is **1-Lipschitz** on $\Omega$.

## Step 4: The weak derivative equals the piecewise classical gradient

Since $u$ is continuous and piecewise $C^1$ (affine on each of the four open sec

**Justification:** For any $\varphi \in C_c^\infty(\Omega)$ and $j \in \{1,2\}$,

$$\int_{R_i} u\,\partial_j \varphi\,dx = -\int_{R_i} (\partial_j u)\,\varphi\,dx

$$\int_\Omega u\,\partial_j \varphi\,dx = -\int_\Omega g_j\,\varphi\,dx,$$

where $g_j$ is the piecewise classical $\partial_j u$. This confirms $g_j$ is th

The piecewise gradient is:

| Region | $\nabla u$ |

differentiable a.e., and its a.e. gradient is its weak gradient, bounded by the

$$u \in W^{1,p}(\Omega) \quad \text{for all } p \in [1, \infty].$$
