## Proof

**Setup.** Identify each subset $A \subseteq [n]$ with its characteristic vector
 $A \in \mathbb{F}_2^n$. For $X \in \mathbb{F}_2^n$, the parity of $|A \cap X|$
is exactly $\langle A, X \rangle \pmod{2}$, where $\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle$
 is the standard dot product over $\mathbb{F}_2$. Thus $|A \cap X|$ is even iff
$(-1)^{\langle A, X\rangle} = 1$.

**Reformulation.** Let $f : \mathbb{F}_2^n \to \{0,1\}$ be the indicator of $\ma
thcal{F}$. The Fourier transform of $f$ on the group $\mathbb{F}_2^n$ is
$$\widehat{f}(X) = \sum_{A \in \mathbb{F}_2^n} f(A)\,(-1)^{\langle A, X\rangle}
= \sum_{A \in \mathcal{F}} (-1)^{\langle A, X\rangle}, \qquad X \in \mathbb{F}_2
^n.$$

The condition "exactly half of $A \in \mathcal{F}$ have $|A \cap X|$ even" means
$$\#\{A \in \mathcal{F} : \langle A, X\rangle = 0\} = \#\{A \in \mathcal{F} : \l
angle A, X\rangle = 1\},$$
i.e., $\widehat{f}(X) = 0$, for every **nonzero** $X \in \mathbb{F}_2^n$.

**Key step.** By Fourier inversion on $\mathbb{F}_2^n$:
$$f(A) = \frac{1}{2^n}\sum_{X \in \mathbb{F}_2^n} \widehat{f}(X)\,(-1)^{\langle
A, X\rangle}.$$
Since $\widehat{f}(X) = 0$ for all $X \neq 0$, only the $X = 0$ term survives:
$$f(A) = \frac{1}{2^n}\,\widehat{f}(0) = \frac{|\mathcal{F}|}{2^n} \qquad \text{
for every } A \in \mathbb{F}_2^n.$$

So $f$ is **constant** on all of $\mathbb{F}_2^n$. Since $f$ is $\{0,1\}$-valued
, the constant is either $0$ or $1$.

- If $f \equiv 0$: $|\mathcal{F}| = 0$, so $\mathcal{F} = \emptyset$.
- If $f \equiv 1$: $|\mathcal{F}| = 2^n$, so $\mathcal{F} = 2^{[n]}$ (the full p
ower set).

**Verification.**

*Case $\mathcal{F} = 2^{[n]}$:* Fix nonempty $X$ with $|X| = k \geq 1$. The numb
er of $A \subseteq [n]$ with $|A \cap X|$ even is
$$\sum_{\substack{j=0 \\ j \text{ even}}}^{k} \binom{k}{j}\, 2^{n-k} = 2^{k-1}\c
dot 2^{n-k} = 2^{n-1} = \frac{2^n}{2}. \checkmark$$

*Case $\mathcal{F} = \emptyset$:* For every nonempty $X$, zero elements of $\mat
hcal{F}$ satisfy the condition, and $0 = \frac{0}{2}$. $\checkmark$

**Conclusion.** The only families satisfying the condition are
$$\boxed{\mathcal{F} = \emptyset \quad \text{and} \quad \mathcal{F} = 2^{[n]}.}$
