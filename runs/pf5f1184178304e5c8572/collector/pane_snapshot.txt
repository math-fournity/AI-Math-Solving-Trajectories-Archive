**Goal.** Find $f(z)$ such that $\sin(\pi z) = f(z)\prod_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(1-\

1. **Reflection formula:**
$$\Gamma(z)\,\Gamma(1-z)=\frac{\pi}{\sin(\pi z)},\qquad\text{i.e.}\quad \sin(\pi
 z)=\frac{\pi}{\Gamma(z)\,\Gamma(1-z)}.$$

**Step 1. A Weierstrass formula for $1/\Gamma(1-z)$.**

$$\frac{1}{\Gamma(-z)}=(-z)\,e^{-\gamma z}\prod_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(1-\frac{z}{n

Using the functional equation $\Gamma(1-z)=(-z)\,\Gamma(-z)$, we get $1/\Gamma(1

$$\frac{1}{\Gamma(1-z)}=\frac{1}{-z}\cdot\frac{1}{\Gamma(-z)}=\frac{1}{-z}\cdot(

$$\frac{1}{\Gamma(z)\,\Gamma(1-z)}=\left[z\,e^{\gamma z}\prod_{n=1}^{\infty}\lef

- **$n$-th factor:** $\left(1+\dfrac{z}{n}\right)\left(1-\dfrac{z}{n}\right)\cdo

The exponential factors $e^{\pm\gamma z}$ cancel in the prefactor, and the $e^{\

$$\frac{1}{\Gamma(z)\,\Gamma(1-z)}=z\prod_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(1-\frac{z^{2}}{n^{
