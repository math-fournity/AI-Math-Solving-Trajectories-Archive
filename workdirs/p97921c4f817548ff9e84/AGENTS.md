# Problem

In a specialized logistics yard, two massive circular storage silos, each with a radius of exactly 1 kilometer, have been constructed. 

The first silo, Silo A, is positioned such that its footprint is centered at the coordinates (0, 1) on the yard’s grid. The second silo, Silo B, is positioned with its center at (2, $y$), where $y$ represents a North-South shifting value between 0 and 1 kilometer.

A technician needs to place a third, smaller circular fuel pod on the ground. This fuel pod must be positioned so that its outer edge is perfectly flush (tangent) against the outer walls of both Silo A and Silo B. Furthermore, the pod must be placed so that its edge also touches the yard’s main East-West boundary line, which is defined by the x-axis.

The technician wants to select a pod size that minimizes the space used. What is the smallest possible radius for this third fuel pod?
