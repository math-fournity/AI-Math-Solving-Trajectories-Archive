## Solution

We seek points $p = (x,y,z) \in L = f^{-1}(1, a^2)$, with $0 < a < \tfrac{3}{2}$
, where $\operatorname{rank} Df(p) < 2$.

**Constraints defining $L$:**
$$x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1, \qquad (x-\tfrac12)^2 + y^2 = a^2.$$

**Rank condition.** The Jacobian is
$$Df = \begin{bmatrix} 2x & 2y & 2z \\ 2x-1 & 2y & 0 \end{bmatrix}.$$
$\operatorname{rank} Df < 2$ iff the two rows are linearly dependent, i.e. there
 exists $\lambda \in \mathbb{R}$ with
$$(2x-1,\; 2y,\; 0) = \lambda\,(2x,\; 2y,\; 2z). \tag{$\star$}$$

**Third component of ($\star$):** $0 = 2\lambda z$, so either $\lambda = 0$ or $
z = 0$.

### Case 1: $\lambda = 0$

Then $2x - 1 = 0$ and $2y = 0$, giving $x = \tfrac12$, $y = 0$. The second const
raint becomes $(\tfrac12 - \tfrac12)^2 + 0 = 0 = a^2$, contradicting $a > 0$. **
No solution.**

### Case 2: $z = 0$

($\star$) reduces to $(2x-1,\; 2y) = \lambda\,(2x,\; 2y)$.

- If $y \neq 0$: the second component gives $\lambda = 1$, then the first gives
$2x - 1 = 2x$, i.e. $-1 = 0$. Contradiction.
- Hence $y = 0$.

With $y = 0$ and $z = 0$, the constraints become
$$x^2 = 1 \;\Longrightarrow\; x = \pm 1, \qquad (x - \tfrac12)^2 = a^2.$$

- **$x = 1$:** $(\tfrac12)^2 = \tfrac14 = a^2$, so $a = \tfrac12$ (valid since $
0 < \tfrac12 < \tfrac32$). The point is $p = (1, 0, 0)$.
- **$x = -1$:** $(-\tfrac32)^2 = \tfrac94 = a^2$, so $a = \tfrac32$, which is **
excluded** by the strict inequality $a < \tfrac32$. **No solution.**

### Conclusion

The rank of $Df(p)$ is strictly less than $2$ at a point of $L$ **only when** $a

$$\boxed{p = (1,\, 0,\, 0)}.$$

For every other value of $a \in (0, \tfrac32) \setminus \{\tfrac12\}$, no point
of $L$ makes the Jacobian drop rank.
