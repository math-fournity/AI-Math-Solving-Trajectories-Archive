# Problem

A specialized logistics hub is managing a series of 2007 distinct delivery routes, indexed from $i = 1$ to $2007$. Each route $i$ is assigned a unique, positive integer-valued speed limit $a_i$, measured in kilometers per hour. To ensure safety and systematic flow, the routes are organized such that the speed limit of each subsequent route is strictly higher than the one before it ($a_1 < a_2 < \dots < a_{2007}$).

A technician calculates the "Systemic Efficiency Coefficient," denoted by a positive integer $N$. This coefficient is determined by summing the ratios of the route's index to its assigned speed limit:
$$N = \frac{1}{a_1} + \frac{2}{a_2} + \frac{3}{a_3} + \dots + \frac{2007}{a_{2007}}$$

Given that $N$ must be a positive integer, calculate the sum of all possible values that $N$ can take.
