## Proof

**Setup.** Place the track $\Delta$ on the $x$-axis with joint $A=(0,0)$. Since
the slider $AB$ extends in the positive direction, let $B=(s,0)$ where $s=|AB|$,
 with $0\le s\le a$ by constraint (i). The midpoint of $AB$ is
$$E=\Big(\frac{s}{2},0\Big).$$

The crossbar $CD$ is horizontal (parallel to $\Delta$). Let its midpoint be $F=(
u,v)$ and its length be $t=|CD|$, so
$$C=\Big(u-\frac{t}{2},\,v\Big),\qquad D=\Big(u+\frac{t}{2},\,v\Big).$$

**Constraint (ii).** $|EF|=l$ gives
$$\Big(u-\frac{s}{2}\Big)^2+v^2=l^2. \tag{1}$$

**Constraint (iii).** Compute $AD^2+BC^2$:
$$AD^2=\Big(u+\frac{t}{2}\Big)^2+v^2,\qquad BC^2=\Big(u-\frac{t}{2}-s\Big)^2+v^2
.$$

Adding, with $u$ as the variable:
$$\Big(u+\frac{t}{2}\Big)^2+\Big(u-s-\frac{t}{2}\Big)^2+2v^2.$$

Expanding both squares:
$$\Big(u+\frac{t}{2}\Big)^2=u^2+ut+\frac{t^2}{4},$$
$$\Big(u-s-\frac{t}{2}\Big)^2=(u-s)^2-(u-s)t+\frac{t^2}{4}=u^2-2us+s^2-ut+st+\fr
ac{t^2}{4}.$$

Summing:
$$2u^2-2us+s^2+st+\frac{t^2}{2}+2v^2.$$

Using (1), we have $2u^2-2us+2v^2=2\big(u-\frac{s}{2}\big)^2+2v^2=2l^2-\frac{s^2
}{2}$. Hence
$$AD^2+BC^2=2l^2-\frac{s^2}{2}+s^2+st+\frac{t^2}{2}=2l^2+\frac{(s+t)^2}{2}.$$

Setting this equal to $k$:
$$\frac{(s+t)^2}{2}=k-2l^2 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad (s+t)^2=2(k-2l^2). \tag{2}$
$

Denote the constant $m:=s+t$, so $m^2=2(k-2l^2)$ (the sign of $m$ is fixed by th
e configuration; it only affects which arc is traced).

**Locus of $D$.** The coordinates of $D$ are
$$x_D=u+\frac{t}{2}=u+\frac{m-s}{2}=\Big(u-\frac{s}{2}\Big)+\frac{m}{2},\qquad y
_D=v.$$

Set $w:=u-\frac{s}{2}$. Then by (1),
$$w^2+v^2=l^2,$$
so $(w,v)$ lies on the circle of radius $l$ centered at the origin. Since
$$x_D=w+\frac{m}{2},\qquad y_D=v,$$
we obtain
$$\Big(x_D-\frac{m}{2}\Big)^2+y_D^2=w^2+v^2=l^2.$$

Therefore $L_D$ is the circle with
$$\text{center }(x_c,y_c)=\Big(\frac{m}{2},0\Big),\qquad r=l.$$

(Constraint (i) restricts the range of $s$, selecting an arc of this circle, but
 does not alter the circle itself.)

**Final computation.**
$$x_c^2+y_c^2+r^2=\frac{m^2}{4}+0+l^2=\frac{2(k-2l^2)}{4}+l^2=\frac{k-2l^2}{2}+l
^2=\frac{k}{2}.$$

$$\boxed{x_c^2+y_c^2+r^2=\dfrac{k}{2}}$$
