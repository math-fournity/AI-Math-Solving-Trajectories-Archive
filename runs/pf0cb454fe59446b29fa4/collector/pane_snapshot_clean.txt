Let $x = a_n$ and $y = a_{n-1}$. The defining equation is:

$$x^2(y+1) + y^2(x+1) - 2xy(xy + x + 1) = 0.$$

**Expand all terms:**

- $x^2(y+1) = x^2 y + x^2$
- $y^2(x+1) = y^2 x + y^2$
- $2xy(xy+x+1) = 2x^2 y^2 + 2x^2 y + 2xy$

Collecting:

$$x^2 y + x^2 + y^2 x + y^2 - 2x^2 y^2 - 2x^2 y - 2xy = 0.$$

The $x^2 y$ terms combine as $x^2 y - 2x^2 y = -x^2 y$, giving:

$$x^2 + y^2 - 2xy + xy^2 - x^2 y - 2x^2 y^2 = 0.$$

Recognize $(x-y)^2 = x^2 + y^2 - 2xy$ and $xy^2 - x^2 y = -xy(x-y)$, so:

$$(x-y)^2 - xy(x-y) - 2x^2 y^2 = 0.$$

**Solve the quadratic in $u = x - y$:**

$$u^2 - (xy)\,u - 2x^2 y^2 = 0, \qquad \Delta = x^2 y^2 + 8x^2 y^2 = 9x^2 y^2.$$

$$u = \frac{xy \pm 3xy}{2} \implies u = 2xy \quad\text{or}\quad u = -xy.$$

**Case 1: $x - y = 2xy$, i.e. $x = \frac{y}{1-2y}$.**

For $x > 0$ we need $1 - 2y > 0$, i.e. $y < \tfrac12$. The constraint $x \le y$
gives $\frac{y}{1-2y} \le y \Rightarrow 1 \le 1-2y \Rightarrow y \le 0$, contrad
icting $y > 0$. Moreover at $n=2$ we have $y = a_1 = \tfrac12$, making the expre
ssion undefined. **Rejected.**

**Case 2: $x - y = -xy$, i.e. $x(1+y) = y$, so $x = \frac{y}{1+y}$.**

Since $y > 0$, we have $0 < x = \frac{y}{1+y} < y$, satisfying $0 < a_n \le a_{n
-1}$. **Accepted.**

Hence the recurrence is:

$$a_n = \frac{a_{n-1}}{1 + a_{n-1}}.$$

**Take reciprocals:**

$$\frac{1}{a_n} = \frac{1}{a_{n-1}} + 1.$$

This is an arithmetic progression with common difference $1$:

$$\frac{1}{a_n} = \frac{1}{a_1} + (n-1) = 2 + (n-1) = n+1.$$

Therefore:

$$\boxed{a_n = \frac{1}{n+1}}.$$

**Verification:** $a_1 = \frac{1}{2}$ ✓. $a_2 = \frac{1/2}{3/2} = \frac{1}{3}$ ✓
. The constraint $0 < a_n \le a_{n-1}$ holds since $\frac{1}{n+1} \le \frac{1}{n
}$.

**Compute $S$ and $11!\cdot S$:**

$$S = \sum_{n=1}^{10} \frac{1}{n+1} = \sum_{k=2}^{11} \frac{1}{k}, \qquad 11!\cd
ot S = \sum_{k=2}^{11} \frac{11!}{k}.$$
