## Formal Solution for the Mixed Boundary Wave Equation

**Problem.** Solve
$$u_{tt}=a^2 u_{xx},\quad 0<x<L,\ t>0,$$
$$u(0,t)=0,\qquad u_x(L,t)=0,$$
$$u(x,0)=f(x),\qquad u_t(x,0)=g(x).$$

---

### Step 1. Separation of variables

Set $u(x,t)=X(x)T(t)$. Substituting gives
$$\frac{X''}{X}=\frac{T''}{a^2 T}=-\lambda,$$
so
$$X''+\lambda X=0,\qquad T''+\lambda a^2 T=0.$$

The boundary conditions become $X(0)=0$ and $X'(L)=0$.

---

### Step 2. The eigenvalue problem

$$X''+\lambda X=0,\qquad X(0)=0,\quad X'(L)=0.$$

**Case $\lambda\le 0$.** The general solution satisfying $X(0)=0$ is $X(x)=B\sin
h(\sqrt{-\lambda}\,x)$ (for $\lambda<0$) or $X(x)=Bx$ (for $\lambda=0$). Then $X
'(L)=B\sqrt{-\lambda}\cosh(\sqrt{-\lambda}\,L)\neq0$ (resp. $X'(L)=B\neq0$), for
cing $B=0$. Only the trivial solution, so no $\lambda\le 0$ eigenvalues.

**Case $\lambda>0$.** Write $\lambda=\mu^2$, $\mu>0$. Then
$$X(x)=A\cos(\mu x)+B\sin(\mu x).$$
$X(0)=0\Rightarrow A=0$, so $X(x)=B\sin(\mu x)$. Then
$$X'(L)=B\mu\cos(\mu L)=0.$$
For a nontrivial solution $B\neq0$, we need
$$\cos(\mu L)=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \mu L=\frac{(2n+1)\pi}{2},\qquad n=0,1,
2,\dots$$

Hence the eigenvalues and eigenfunctions are
$$\lambda_n=\mu_n^2=\left(\frac{(2n+1)\pi}{2L}\right)^2,\qquad X_n(x)=\sin\!\lef
t(\frac{(2n+1)\pi x}{2L}\right),\qquad n=0,1,2,\dots$$

**Orthogonality.** Since $\{X_n\}$ are eigenfunctions of a Sturm–Liouville probl
em, they are orthogonal on $[0,L]$. Directly,
$$\int_0^L \sin^2\!\left(\frac{(2n+1)\pi x}{2L}\right)dx=\frac{L}{2}.$$

---

### Step 3. Time equation

For each $n$, $T_n''+\lambda_n a^2 T_n=0$, i.e.
$$T_n''+\omega_n^2 T_n=0,\qquad \omega_n=a\mu_n=\frac{a(2n+1)\pi}{2L}.$$
Thus
$$T_n(t)=A_n\cos(\omega_n t)+B_n\sin(\omega_n t).$$

---

### Step 4. Superposition

$$u(x,t)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left[A_n\cos(\omega_n t)+B_n\sin(\omega_n t)\right]
\sin\!\left(\frac{(2n+1)\pi x}{2L}\right).$$

---

### Step 5. Determine coefficients from initial data

**From $u(x,0)=f(x)$:**
$$f(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}A_n\sin\!\left(\frac{(2n+1)\pi x}{2L}\right).$$
By orthogonality (norm $L/2$),
$$\boxed{\,A_n=\frac{2}{L}\int_0^L f(x)\sin\!\left(\frac{(2n+1)\pi x}{2L}\right)
dx\,}.$$

**From $u_t(x,0)=g(x)$:** Differentiating the series,
$$u_t(x,t)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\omega_n\bigl[-A_n\sin(\omega_n t)+B_n\cos(\omega_
n t)\bigr]\sin\!\left(\frac{(2n+1)\pi x}{2L}\right).$$
Setting $t=0$:
$$g(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\omega_n B_n\sin\!\left(\frac{(2n+1)\pi x}{2L}\right).
$$
Again by orthogonality,
$$\omega_n B_n=\frac{2}{L}\int_0^L g(x)\sin\!\left(\frac{(2n+1)\pi x}{2L}\right)
dx,$$
so
$$\boxed{\,B_n=\frac{2}{L\,\omega_n}\int_0^L g(x)\sin\!\left(\frac{(2n+1)\pi x}{
2L}\right)dx=\frac{4}{a(2n+1)\pi}\int_0^L g(x)\sin\!\left(\frac{(2n+1)\pi x}{2L}
\right)dx\,}.$$

---

### Formal Solution

$$\boxed{\,u(x,t)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left[A_n\cos\!\left(\frac{a(2n+1)\pi t}{2L
}\right)+B_n\sin\!\left(\frac{a(2n+1)\pi t}{2L}\right)\right]\sin\!\left(\frac{(
2n+1)\pi x}{2L}\right)\,}$$

with
$$A_n=\frac{2}{L}\int_0^L f(\xi)\sin\!\left(\frac{(2n+1)\pi \xi}{2L}\right)d\xi,
\qquad B_n=\frac{4}{a(2n+1)\pi}\int_0^L g(\xi)\sin\!\left(\frac{(2n+1)\pi \xi}{2
L}\right)d\xi,$$

for $n=0,1,2,\dots$.

---

### Verification of boundary conditions

- **$u(0,t)=0$:** Each eigenfunction $\sin\!\bigl(\frac{(2n+1)\pi\cdot 0}{2L}\bi
gr)=0$. ✓
- **$u_x(L,t)=0$:** $u_x(L,t)=\sum_n[\cdots]\frac{(2n+1)\pi}{2L}\cos\!\bigl(\fra
c{(2n+1)\pi}{2}\bigr)=0$ since $\cos\!\bigl(\frac{(2n+1)\pi}{2}\bigr)=0$. ✓
- **Initial conditions** hold by construction of $A_n,B_n$ via orthogonal expans
ion. ✓

The series is the **formal solution**; it becomes a classical solution under sta
ndard regularity/compatibility assumptions on $f$ and $g$ (e.g., $f\in C^3$, $g\
in C^2$, with compatibility conditions $f(0)=0$, $f'(L)=0$, $g(0)=0$, $g'(L)=0$)
.
