## Solution

We solve the PDE
$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} = 2\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} + u, \qquad u(x
,0)=6e^{-3x},$$
with $u$ bounded for $t>0,\ x>0$.

**Step 1. Rewrite in characteristic form.** Rearranging,
$$u_x - 2u_t = u.$$
The characteristic system is
$$\frac{dx}{ds}=1,\qquad \frac{dt}{ds}=-2,\qquad \frac{du}{ds}=u.$$

**Step 2. Find the characteristic curves.** From $\frac{dt}{dx}=-2$ we get
$$t+2x = C,$$
a constant along each characteristic. Hence every characteristic is a line $t+2x
=C$ in the $(x,t)$-plane.

**Step 3. Locate where each characteristic meets the initial line $t=0$.** A cha
racteristic passing through a generic point $(x,t)$ has $C=t+2x$. It meets $t=0$
 at $x_0$ satisfying $2x_0 = t+2x$, i.e.
$$x_0 = x+\frac{t}{2}.$$

**Step 4. Solve along the characteristic.** Since $\frac{dx}{ds}=1$, we may use
$x$ itself as the parameter $s$. Along the characteristic,
$$\frac{du}{dx}=u \quad\Longrightarrow\quad u = K\,e^{x}$$
for a constant $K$ depending on the characteristic. Evaluating at $t=0$ where $x
=x_0$ and $u=6e^{-3x_0}$:
$$6e^{-3x_0} = K\,e^{x_0}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad K = 6e^{-4x_0}.$$
Thus along this characteristic
$$u = 6e^{-4x_0}\,e^{x} = 6\,e^{x-4x_0}.$$

**Step 5. Substitute $x_0 = x+\tfrac{t}{2}$.** Then
$$x - 4x_0 = x - 4\!\left(x+\frac{t}{2}\right) = -3x - 2t,$$
so
$$\boxed{\,u(x,t) = 6\,e^{-3x-2t}\,}.$$

**Verification.**
- $u_x = -18\,e^{-3x-2t}$, $\;u_t = -12\,e^{-3x-2t}$, $\;u = 6\,e^{-3x-2t}$.
- PDE: $u_x = -18\,e^{-3x-2t}$ and $2u_t+u = 2(-12)+6 = -18$ times $e^{-3x-2t}$.
 ✓
- Initial condition: $u(x,0)=6e^{-3x}$. ✓
- Boundedness: for $x>0,\ t>0$, $-3x-2t<0$, so $u\to 0$ as $x$ or $t$ grow; $u$
is bounded. ✓

The solution is unique among bounded functions on the domain $x>0,\ t>0$, since
the value at every interior point is determined by the unique characteristic tra
ced back to the initial line $t=0$.
