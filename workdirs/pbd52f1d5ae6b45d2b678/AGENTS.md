# Problem

In the interconnected Kingdom of Veridia, there are exactly 28 unique provinces. To facilitate trade, the Royal Ministry has established several two-way direct carriage routes between certain pairs of provinces.

Each province has a single designated trade hub. Every hub is classified into one of exactly three categories based on its capacity: Tier-1, Tier-2, or Tier-3. 

The Ministry has enforced a strict "Diversity Regulation" for the kingdom’s logistics: for any possible travel circuit that starts and ends at the same province, visits more than two provinces, and never visits the same province twice (a simple cycle), the set of hubs visited in that circuit must include at least one Tier-1 hub, at least one Tier-2 hub, and at least one Tier-3 hub.

Given these regulatory constraints on the formation of travel circuits and the fixed number of provinces, what is the maximum possible number of direct carriage routes that can exist in the Kingdom of Veridia?
