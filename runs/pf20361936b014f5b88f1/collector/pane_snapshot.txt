## Setting Up the Equation

$$\sqrt{1+x^2} = \frac{(1+x^2)^3}{6x^5 - 20x^3 + 6x}$$

$$(1+x^2)^{5/2} = 6x^5 - 20x^3 + 6x = 2x(x^2-3)(3x^2-1)$$

where I factored using $3x^4 - 10x^2 + 3 = (x^2-3)(3x^2-1)$.
