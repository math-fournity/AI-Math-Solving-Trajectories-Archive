  \leq C\delta |f_n|{L^{r_0}} \leq C\delta M$), hence uniformly Lipschitz,
  giving $|\tau_h g_n - g_n|{L^r} \leq C |h| \to 0$ uniformly in $n$. The
  approximation error $|f_n - g_n|{L^r}$ is controlled by $|f_n - g_n|{L^{r_0}}$
  via Hölder, and by choosing $\delta$ small and using the density of
  $C_c^\infty$ in $L^{r_0}$ together with a diagonal argument over a dense
  countable subset, one obtains $\sup_n |\tau_h f_n - f_n|{L^r(\Omega)} \to 0$
  as $h \to 0$.

By the Kolmogorov–Riesz theorem, ${f_n}$ admits a convergent subsequence in
$L^r(\Omega)$, proving (2).

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Step 3: Composition

The composition of a continuous embedding with a compact embedding is compact.
From (1) and (2):

$$B^s_{1,\theta}(\Omega) \xhookrightarrow{\text{cont.}} L^{r_0}(\Omega)
\xhookrightarrow{\text{comp.}} L^r(\Omega),$$

we conclude $$\boxed{B^s_{1,\theta}(\Omega) \hookrightarrow\hookrightarrow
L^r(\Omega).}$$

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Remark on the role of the condition $\theta \leq \frac{N}{N-s}$

The condition $\theta \leq \frac{N}{N-s}$ is the sharp condition for the
continuous embedding at the critical exponent: $$B^s_{1,\theta}(\Omega)
\hookrightarrow L^{N/(N-s)}(\Omega) \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \theta \leq
\frac{N}{N-s}.$$ This follows from the critical Sobolev embedding for Besov
spaces: $B^s_{p,q} \hookrightarrow L^{p^}$ (with $1/p^ = 1/p - s/N$) if and only
if $q \leq p^$. With $p = 1$ and $p^ = N/(N-s)$, this gives $\theta \leq N/(N-
s)$.

For the compact embedding in the subcritical range $r < N/(N-s)$, the above
proof shows that any $\theta \geq 1$ suffices — one simply works below the
critical exponent where the fine index plays no role. Thus the given condition $
\theta \leq N/(N-s)$ is sufficient (though not necessary) for the compact
embedding $B^s_{1,\theta}(\Omega) \hookrightarrow\hookrightarrow L^r(\Omega)$
with $r < N/(N-s)$.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Conclusion: Yes, under the conditions $0 < s < 1$, $1 \leq \theta \leq \frac{N}
{N-s}$, $r < \frac{N}{N-s}$, and $\Omega$ a bounded open subset of $\mathbb{R}
^N$ with smooth boundary, the space $B^{s;1,\theta}(\Omega)$ is compactly
embedded in $L^r(\Omega)$.
