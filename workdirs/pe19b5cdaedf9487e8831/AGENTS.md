# Problem

In the competitive world of artisanal clockmaking, a master craftsman is designing a series of limited-edition "Century Gears." Each gear is engraved with a unique identification number, $n$, which is a positive integer.

To ensure the authenticity of these gears, the craftsman applies a specific "Engraving Rule" based on the identification number:
1. First, he calculates the "Digital Product," which is the result of multiplying all the individual digits of $n$ together.
2. Second, he calculates a "Structural Value" using a specific quadratic formula based on the ID number: $n^2 - 42n + 440$.

A gear is considered a "Masterpiece" only if its Digital Product is exactly equal to its Structural Value.

Find the sum of all identification numbers $n$ that qualify as Masterpiece gears.
