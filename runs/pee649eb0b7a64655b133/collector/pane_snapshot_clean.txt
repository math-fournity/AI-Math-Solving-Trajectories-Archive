## Proof

We have six containers with colors forming the multiset {Emerald, Lavender, Pink
, Pink, Rust, Ultramarine}. The alphabetical ordering of distinct color names is
:

$$E < L < P < R < U$$

The total number of distinguishable arrangements is $\frac{6!}{2!} = 360$ (divid
ing by $2!$ for the two identical Pink containers).

**Target sequence:** $P, U, R, P, L, E$.

We compute the rank (1-indexed) by counting, at each position, the number of com
plete arrangements that are lexicographically smaller.

**Position 1 = P.** Remaining multiset: {E, L, P, R, U}. Colors alphabetically b
efore P that are available: E, L.
- Starting with E: $\frac{5!}{2!} = 60$ (remaining {L, P, P, R, U}).
- Starting with L: $\frac{5!}{2!} = 60$ (remaining {E, P, P, R, U}).

Subtotal: $60 + 60 = 120$.

**Position 2 = U.** Remaining: {E, L, P, R, U}. Colors before U: E, L, P, R.
- P,E: $4! = 24$ (remaining {L, P, R, U}).
- P,L: $4! = 24$ (remaining {E, P, R, U}).
- P,P: $4! = 24$ (remaining {E, L, R, U}).
- P,R: $4! = 24$ (remaining {E, L, P, U}).

Subtotal: $4 \times 24 = 96$.

**Position 3 = R.** Remaining: {E, L, P, R}. Colors before R: E, L, P.
- P,U,E: $3! = 6$ (remaining {L, P, R}).
- P,U,L: $3! = 6$ (remaining {E, P, R}).
- P,U,P: $3! = 6$ (remaining {E, L, R}).

Subtotal: $3 \times 6 = 18$.

**Position 4 = P.** Remaining: {E, L, P}. Colors before P: E, L.
- P,U,R,E: $2! = 2$ (remaining {L, P}).
- P,U,R,L: $2! = 2$ (remaining {E, P}).

Subtotal: $2 + 2 = 4$.

**Position 5 = L.** Remaining: {E, L}. Colors before L: E.
- P,U,R,P,E: $1! = 1$ (remaining {L}).

Subtotal: $1$.

**Position 6 = E.** Remaining: {E}. No choice; contributes 0.

**Total arrangements before the target:**

$$120 + 96 + 18 + 4 + 1 = 239$$

**Rank (1-indexed):**

$$n = 239 + 1 = \boxed{240}$$
