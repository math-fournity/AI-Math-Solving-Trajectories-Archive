## Solution

We count permutations of the 7 distinct chips {A, И, О, П, К, Р, С} that avoid t
hree forbidden consecutive patterns, using inclusion–exclusion.

**Total permutations:** 7! = 5040.

Define events:
- **E₁**: the permutation contains the consecutive substring **АСО**.
- **E₂**: the permutation contains the consecutive substring **КАРО**.
- **E₃**: the permutation contains the consecutive substring **ПИКА**.

### Single events

**|E₁|:** Treat АСО as one block. The block plus the 4 remaining chips {П, И, К,
 Р} give 5 distinct items:
|E₁| = 5! = **120**.

**|E₂|:** Treat КАРО as one block. The block plus {П, И, С} give 4 items:
|E₂| = 4! = **24**.

**|E₃|:** Treat ПИКА as one block. The block plus {О, Р, С} give 4 items:
|E₃| = 4! = **24**.

### Pairwise intersections

**E₁ ∩ E₂:** АСО and КАРО share chips A and О. In АСО, A's successor is С; in КА
РО, A's successor is Р. Since there is only one A, it cannot have two different
successors. Likewise, О's predecessor is С in АСО but Р in КАРО — also impossibl
e. Hence
|E₁ ∩ E₂| = **0**.

**E₁ ∩ E₃:** АСО and ПИКА share only A. In ПИКА, A is the last element of the bl
ock (predecessor К); in АСО, A is the first element (successor С). These are com
patible: A's predecessor is К and A's successor is С. This forces the combined c
onsecutive block **П-И-К-А-С-О** (6 chips). The remaining chip is Р, giving 2 it
ems (block + Р):
|E₁ ∩ E₃| = 2! = **2**.

**E₂ ∩ E₃:** КАРО and ПИКА share К and А. In both patterns, К is immediately fol
lowed by А — consistent. In ПИКА, К is preceded by И; in КАРО, А is followed by
Р-О. Combining the constraints П-И-К-А (from ПИКА) and К-А-Р-О (from КАРО) yield
s the combined consecutive block **П-И-К-А-Р-О** (6 chips). The remaining chip i
s С, giving 2 items:
|E₂ ∩ E₃| = 2! = **2**.

### Triple intersection

Since E₁ ∩ E₂ = ∅, we have E₁ ∩ E₂ ∩ E₃ = **∅**, contributing 0.

### Inclusion–exclusion

|E₁ ∪ E₂ ∪ E₃| = 120 + 24 + 24 − 0 − 2 − 2 + 0 = **164**.

### Valid arrangements

5040 − 164 = **4876**.

The number of valid ways to arrange the seven chips without triggering the locko
ut protocol is **4876**.
