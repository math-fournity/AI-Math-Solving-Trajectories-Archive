**IA₁² = r² + (k/2)² = r² + k²/4**

By identical reasoning (cyclically): IB₁² = r² + k²/4 and IC₁² = r² + k²/4. So:

**IA₁ = IB₁ = IC₁ = √(r² + k²/4)**
