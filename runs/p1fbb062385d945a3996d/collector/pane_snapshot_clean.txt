## Proof

**Setup.** Let $W = \{z \in \mathbb{C}^n : \|z\| < R\}$ be the open ball of radi
us $R$ centered at the origin, and let $B = \{z \in \mathbb{C}^n : \|z\| \leq r\
}$ be the closed ball of radius $r$ centered at the origin, with $0 < r < R$ so
that $B \subset W$. We use the standard Hermitian norm $\|z\|^2 = \sum_{j=1}^n |
z_j|^2$.

**Definition.** The holomorphically convex hull of $B$ in $W$ is
$$\widehat{B}_W = \bigl\{z \in W : |f(z)| \leq \sup_{B}|f| \text{ for all } f \i
n \mathcal{O}(W)\bigr\}.$$
We must show $\widehat{B}_W = B$.

**Inclusion $B \subseteq \widehat{B}_W$.** This is immediate: every point of $B$
 satisfies $|f(z)| \leq \sup_B |f|$ for all holomorphic $f$ on $W$.

**Inclusion $\widehat{B}_W \subseteq B$.** We show the contrapositive. Let $z_0
\in W \setminus B$, so $\|z_0\| > r$. We produce a function $f \in \mathcal{O}(W
)$ with $|f(z_0)| > \sup_B |f|$.

Consider the linear function
$$f_{z_0}(z) = \langle z,\, z_0\rangle = \sum_{j=1}^{n} z_j\,\overline{(z_0)_j},
$$
which is holomorphic on all of $\mathbb{C}^n$ (hence on $W$), since it is comple
x-linear in $z$.

*Evaluation at $z_0$:*
$$f_{z_0}(z_0) = \sum_{j=1}^n (z_0)_j\,\overline{(z_0)_j} = \sum_{j=1}^n |(z_0)_
j|^2 = \|z_0\|^2.$$

*Supremum on $B$:* By the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality,
$$|f_{z_0}(z)| = |\langle z, z_0\rangle| \leq \|z\|\,\|z_0\| \leq r\,\|z_0\| \qu
ad \text{for all } z \in B.$$
This bound is sharp: taking $z = r\,\dfrac{z_0}{\|z_0\|} \in B$ gives $|f_{z_0}(
z)| = r\|z_0\|$. Hence
$$\sup_B |f_{z_0}| = r\,\|z_0\|.$$

*Comparison:* Since $\|z_0\| > r$,
$$|f_{z_0}(z_0)| = \|z_0\|^2 = \|z_0\|\cdot\|z_0\| > \|z_0\|\cdot r = \sup_B |f_
{z_0}|.$$

Therefore $z_0 \notin \widehat{B}_W$. Since $z_0 \in W \setminus B$ was arbitrar
y, $\widehat{B}_W \subseteq B$.

**Conclusion.** Combining both inclusions, $\widehat{B}_W = B$. The closed ball
$B$ is holomorphically convex in $W$. $\blacksquare$
