## Proof

**Setup.** The Sundmann transformation introduces a fictitious time $s$ related
to the physical time $t$ by

$$\frac{ds}{dt} = \frac{1}{r}, \qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad \frac{dt}{ds} = r
,$$

where $r = \|\mathbf{r}\| = \sqrt{\sum_i r_i^2}$ is the radial distance.

**Goal.** Express the inertial velocity components $v_i = \dfrac{dr_i}{dt}$ in t
erms of derivatives taken with respect to $s$.

**Derivation.** By the chain rule, for each Cartesian component $r_i$ of the pos
ition vector,

$$\frac{dr_i}{dt} = \frac{dr_i}{ds}\,\frac{ds}{dt}.$$

Substituting the Sundmann relation $\dfrac{ds}{dt} = \dfrac{1}{r}$,

$$v_i = \frac{dr_i}{dt} = \frac{1}{r}\,\frac{dr_i}{ds}.$$

Introducing the prime notation $r_i' := \dfrac{dr_i}{ds}$ for differentiation wi
th respect to the fictitious time, this becomes compactly

$$\boxed{\,v_i = \frac{r_i'}{r}\,} \qquad\text{or equivalently}\qquad \boxed{\,r
_i' = r\,v_i\,.}$$

**Vector form.** Collecting components, the full velocity vector transforms as

$$\mathbf{v} = \frac{1}{r}\,\frac{d\mathbf{r}}{ds}, \qquad\text{i.e.}\qquad \fra
c{d\mathbf{r}}{ds} = r\,\mathbf{v}.$$

**Consistency check.** The speed magnitude transforms consistently:

$$v = \|\mathbf{v}\| = \frac{1}{r}\left\|\frac{d\mathbf{r}}{ds}\right\| = \frac{
1}{r}\,r'_{\text{geom}},$$

where $r'_{\text{geom}} = \left\|\dfrac{d\mathbf{r}}{ds}\right\|$ is the geometr
ic speed in fictitious time. This confirms the factor $1/r$ is the correct scali
ng between the two time parametrizations: the Sundmann time $s$ "slows down" the
 dynamics near collisions (small $r$) by stretching $dt = r\,ds$, and the veloci
ty correspondingly picks up the reciprocal factor $1/r$ when re-expressed per un
it $s$.

**Conclusion.** Under the Sundmann transformation $\dfrac{ds}{dt} = \dfrac{1}{r}
$, each inertial velocity component transforms as

$$v_i = \frac{1}{r}\frac{dr_i}{ds},$$

i.e., the physical velocity is the fictitious-time derivative of position scaled
 by $1/r$.
