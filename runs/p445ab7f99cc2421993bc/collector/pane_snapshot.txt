## Solution

**Setup.** For $N = 2025$, we have $\overline{ab} = 20$ and $\overline{cd} = 25$
. We seek the smallest positive integer $p = \overline{\alpha_1\cdots\alpha_k}$
(a $k$-digit number, so $10^{k-1} \le p \le 10^k - 1$) such that

$$M = \overline{20\,\alpha_1\cdots\alpha_k\,25} = 20\cdot 10^{k+2} + 100p + 25$$

is divisible by $2025$.

**Factoring.** Since $2025 = 81 \times 25$, we need $M \equiv 0 \pmod{25}$ and $
M \equiv 0 \pmod{81}$.

**Divisibility by 25 is automatic.** For any $k \ge 1$: $100p \equiv 0$, $25 \eq
uiv 0$, and $20\cdot 10^{k+2} = 20\cdot 2^{k+2}\cdot 5^{k+2}$ is divisible by $5
^2 = 25$ (since $k+2 \ge 3$). So $M \equiv 0 \pmod{25}$ always holds.

**Reducing to mod 81.** We need:

$$20\cdot 10^{k+2} + 100p + 25 \equiv 0 \pmod{81}.$$

Since $100 \equiv 19 \pmod{81}$, this becomes:

$$19p \equiv -20\cdot 10^{k+2} - 25 \pmod{81}.$$

**Computing $19^{-1} \pmod{81}$.** Via the extended Euclidean algorithm: $81 = 4
\cdot 19 + 5$, $19 = 3\cdot 5 + 4$, $5 = 1\cdot 4 + 1$, giving $1 = 4\cdot 81 -
17\cdot 19$, so $19^{-1} \equiv -17 \equiv 64 \pmod{81}$. (Check: $19 \times 64
= 1216 = 15\times 81 + 1$. ✓)

**Powers of 10 mod 81.** The order of $10$ modulo $81$ is $9$ (one checks $10^9
\equiv 1 \pmod{81}$ and no smaller positive power gives $1$). The values $10^m \
bmod 81$ for $m = 1,\ldots,9$ are:

$$10,\; 19,\; 28,\; 37,\; 46,\; 55,\; 64,\; 73,\; 1.$$

**Case $k = 1$ (1-digit $p$, range $[1,9]$).** Here $10^{k+2} = 10^3 \equiv 28 \
pmod{81}$, so:

$$19p \equiv -20\cdot 28 - 25 = -585 \equiv 63 \pmod{81},$$

$$p \equiv 64 \times 63 = 4032 \equiv 63 \pmod{81}.$$

The positive integers $\equiv 63 \pmod{81}$ are $63, 144, 225, \ldots$ — none li
e in $[1,9]$. **No solution for $k=1$.**

**Case $k = 2$ (2-digit $p$, range $[10,99]$).** Here $10^{k+2} = 10^4 \equiv 37
 \pmod{81}$, so:

$$19p \equiv -20\cdot 37 - 25 = -765 \equiv 45 \pmod{81},$$

$$p \equiv 64 \times 45 = 2880 \equiv 45 \pmod{81}.$$

The smallest positive integer $\equiv 45 \pmod{81}$ is $45$ itself, and $45 \in
[10, 99]$. ✓

**Verification.** With $p = 45$, the inserted number is $M = \overline{204525}$.
 We check:

$$204525 = 2025 \times 101 = 202500 + 2025. \quad\checkmark$$

**No smaller $p$ exists.** For $k = 1$ there is no valid $p$. For $k \ge 3$, any
 $k$-digit $p$ satisfies $p \ge 100 > 45$. Since $p = 45$ is achievable at $k =
2$, it is the global minimum.

$$\boxed{p(2025) = 45}$$
