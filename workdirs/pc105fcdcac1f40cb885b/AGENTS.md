# Problem

In a remote industrial shipyard, a specialized cylindrical observation tower stands exactly 13 feet tall. This tower is mounted vertically on the apex of a massive, perfectly spherical fuel storage tank that has a radius of 156 feet.

A maintenance drone is programmed to inspect the tank by traversing a specific path along the "horizon line"—the set of all points on the sphere's surface that are exactly visible from the very top point of the 13-foot tower (assuming the line of sight is tangent to the sphere).

The drone completes one full revolution around the tower, maintaining its path strictly along this visual horizon. What is the total distance (in feet) traveled by the drone?
