## Proof

**Setup and the key map.** Define the map
$$\Phi: E \longrightarrow \bigoplus_{i=1}^{n} \mathrm{PP}_{z_i},$$
where $\mathrm{PP}_{z_i}$ denotes the space of principal parts at $z_i$ of order
 $\leq 2$, i.e., expressions of the form $\frac{a_i}{(z - z_i)^2} + \frac{b_i}{z
 - z_i}$ in local coordinates. Each $\mathrm{PP}_{z_i}$ is a 2-dimensional compl
ex vector space (parameters $a_i, b_i$), so the target has dimension $2n$.

The map $\Phi$ sends $f \in E$ to the tuple of its principal parts $(\mathrm{pp}
_{z_1}(f), \ldots, \mathrm{pp}_{z_n}(f))$. This is well-defined since every $f \
in E$ has poles of order $\leq 2$ at each point, hence at each $z_i$ its princip
al part has order $\leq 2$. It is clearly $\mathbb{C}$-linear.

**The kernel is $F$.** We have $\ker(\Phi) = \{f \in E : \mathrm{pp}_{z_i}(f) =
0 \text{ for all } i\}$, which is exactly the set of functions in $E$ that are h
olomorphic at each $z_i$, i.e., $F$.

**Surjectivity via partial fractions.** Given any tuple of principal parts $p_i(
z) = \frac{a_i}{(z - z_i)^2} + \frac{b_i}{z - z_i}$ for $i = 1, \ldots, n$, cons
ider
$$f(z) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \left(\frac{a_i}{(z - z_i)^2} + \frac{b_i}{z - z_i}\righ
t).$$
This is a sum of rational functions on the Riemann sphere $\widehat{\mathbb{C}}$
, hence a rational function, hence meromorphic on $\widehat{\mathbb{C}}$. Its on
ly poles are at $z_1, \ldots, z_n$, each of order $\leq 2$, with exactly the pre
scribed principal parts. Since $U \subseteq \widehat{\mathbb{C}}$ is open and al
l $z_i \in U$, the restriction $f|_U$ is meromorphic on $U$ with all poles of or
der $\leq 2$, so $f|_U \in E$, and $\Phi(f|_U) = (p_1, \ldots, p_n)$. This prove
s surjectivity.

**Conclusion.** By the first isomorphism theorem,
$$E/F = E/\ker(\Phi) \cong \mathrm{im}(\Phi) = \bigoplus_{i=1}^{n} \mathrm{PP}_{
z_i} \cong \mathbb{C}^{2n}.$$

Therefore $E/F$ has finite dimension, and

$$\boxed{\dim(E/F) = 2n.}$$
