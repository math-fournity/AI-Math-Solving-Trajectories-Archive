## Solution

**Setup.** The triple $(a,b,c)$ is uniformly distributed on the 2-simplex $\{a+b
+c=1,\; a,b,c\ge 0\}$, which in $(a,b)$-coordinates (with $c=1-a-b$) is the tria
ngle $\{a\ge0,\,b\ge0,\,a+b\le1\}$ of area $\tfrac12$, so the density is $2$. Th
e value $d\sim\text{Unif}[0,1]$ is independent. We want
$$P = \Pr(d>a,\; d>b,\; d>c).$$

**Condition on $d$.** For fixed $d$, the favorable region is the simplex with al
l three coordinates $<d$, i.e. in $(a,b)$-space: $a<d,\; b<d,\; a+b>1-d$ (from $
c=1-a-b<d$), plus $a\ge0,b\ge0,a+b\le1$. Since $\max(a,b,c)\ge\tfrac13$, this re
gion is empty for $d\le\tfrac13$.

**Area via inclusion–exclusion.** Let $A_d$ be the area (in $(a,b)$-space) of th
e favorable region. Removing the three corner strips $\{a\ge d\},\{b\ge d\},\{c\
ge d\}$ from the full simplex (area $\tfrac12$):
- Each single strip has area $\tfrac{(1-d)^2}{2}$.
- Each pairwise overlap $\{a\ge d,b\ge d\}$ (a triangle) has area $\tfrac{(1-2d)
^2}{2}$, nonempty for $d\le\tfrac12$.
- The triple overlap $\{a\ge d,b\ge d,c\ge d\}$ has area $\tfrac{(1-3d)^2}{2}$,
nonempty for $d\le\tfrac13$.

**Case $\tfrac13\le d\le\tfrac12$:**
$$A_d = \tfrac12 - \tfrac{3(1-d)^2}{2} + \tfrac{3(1-2d)^2}{2} - 0.$$
Multiplying by $2$ (the density) and expanding:
$$2A_d = 1 - 3(1-d)^2 + 3(1-2d)^2 = 1 - 6d + 9d^2 = (1-3d)^2.$$

**Case $\tfrac12\le d\le 1$:** pairwise overlaps vanish, so
$$2A_d = 1 - 3(1-d)^2.$$

**Integrate over $d\in[0,1]$:**
$$P = \int_{1/3}^{1/2}(1-3d)^2\,dd \;+\; \int_{1/2}^{1}\bigl(1-3(1-d)^2\bigr)\,d
d.$$

First integral (antiderivative $-\tfrac{(1-3d)^3}{9}$):
$$\left[-\tfrac{(1-3d)^3}{9}\right]_{1/3}^{1/2} = -\tfrac{(-1/2)^3}{9} - 0 = \tf
rac{1}{72}.$$

Second integral (antiderivative $d+(1-d)^3$):
$$\bigl[d+(1-d)^3\bigr]_{1/2}^{1} = (1+0) - \bigl(\tfrac12+\tfrac18\bigr) = 1 -
\tfrac58 = \tfrac38 = \tfrac{27}{72}.$$

**Total:**
$$P = \tfrac{1}{72} + \tfrac{27}{72} = \tfrac{28}{72} = \tfrac{7}{18}.$$

Since $\gcd(7,18)=1$, we have $m=7,\; n=18$, and
$$m+n = 7+18 = \boxed{25}.$$
