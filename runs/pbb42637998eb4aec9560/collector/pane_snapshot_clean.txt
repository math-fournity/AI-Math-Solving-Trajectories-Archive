Looking at this problem, I need to find the minimum total Manhattan distance fro
m the origin for three robots forming a triangle of area 2021.

## Proof

**Setup.** Let the three robots end up at lattice points $P, Q, R$. The total nu
mber of commands equals $|P|_1 + |Q|_1 + |R|_1$ (sum of Manhattan distances from
 origin). The triangle area is $\frac{1}{2}|\det(Q-P, R-P)| = 2021$, so $|\det(Q
-P, R-P)| = 4042$.

**Lower bound.** Define:
- $X = \max(P_x, Q_x, R_x) - \min(P_x, Q_x, R_x)$ (x-span of the triangle)
- $Y = \max(P_y, Q_y, R_y) - \min(P_y, Q_y, R_y)$ (y-span of the triangle)

The triangle $PQR$ is inscribed in its bounding rectangle of dimensions $X \time
s Y$. Any triangle inscribed in a rectangle has area at most half the rectangle'
s area, so:
$$2021 \leq \frac{XY}{2} \implies XY \geq 4042$$

Now I bound the total cost. Let $M_x = \max(P_x, Q_x, R_x)$ and $m_x = \min(P_x,
 Q_x, R_x)$. Since $M_x$ and $m_x$ are two of the three x-coordinates:
$$|P_x| + |Q_x| + |R_x| \geq |M_x| + |m_x| \geq |M_x - m_x| = X$$

Similarly, $|P_y| + |Q_y| + |R_y| \geq Y$. Therefore:
$$\text{Total cost} = (|P_x|+|Q_x|+|R_x|) + (|P_y|+|Q_y|+|R_y|) \geq X + Y \geq
2\sqrt{XY} \geq 2\sqrt{4042}$$

Since $2\sqrt{4042} \approx 127.16$ and the total cost is an integer:
$$\text{Total cost} \geq 128$$

**Construction achieving 128.** Place the robots at:
$$P = (-54, -1), \quad Q = (10, 0), \quad R = (0, 63)$$

**Cost verification:**
$$|P|_1 = 54 + 1 = 55, \quad |Q|_1 = 10 + 0 = 10, \quad |R|_1 = 0 + 63 = 63$$
$$\text{Total} = 55 + 10 + 63 = 128 \checkmark$$

**Area verification:** Compute $u = Q - P = (64, 1)$ and $v = R - P = (54, 64)$:
$$\det(u, v) = 64 \times 64 - 1 \times 54 = 4096 - 54 = 4042$$
$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times 4042 = 2021 \checkmark$$

All three points are lattice points, so they are reachable by unit commands.

**How the construction was found.** Set $u = (64, 1)$, $v = (54, 64)$ so that $\
det(u,v) = 4042$. The x-coordinates $\{0, 64, 54\}$ have range $X = 64$, and the
 y-coordinates $\{0, 1, 64\}$ have range $Y = 64$, giving $X + Y = 128$ and $XY
= 4096 \geq 4042$. Placing $P$ at the coordinate-wise median $(-54, -1)$ of $\{0
, -u, -v\}$ achieves the minimum cost $X + Y = 128$.

**Conclusion.** The lower bound is 128 and the construction achieves 128, so the
 minimum total number of commands is $\boxed{128}$.
