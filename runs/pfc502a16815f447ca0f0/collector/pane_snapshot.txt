## Solution

**Setup.** Place $B=(0,0)$, $C=(7,0)$. Then $A=\left(\frac{33}{7},\frac{24\sqrt5
}{7}\right)$, area $K=12\sqrt5$, circumradius $R=\frac{21\sqrt5}{10}$, circumcen
ter $O=\left(\frac72,\frac{7\sqrt5}{5}\right)$.

**Tangent points.** From the ratios $BX{:}XC=CY{:}YA=AZ{:}ZB=1{:}2$:
$$X=\left(\tfrac{7}{3},0\right),\quad Y=\left(\tfrac{131}{21},\tfrac{8\sqrt5}{7}
\right),\quad Z=\left(\tfrac{22}{7},\tfrac{16\sqrt5}{7}\right).$$

**Key formula.** For a circle tangent to a chord $\ell$ at $P$, internally tange
nt to $\Omega$, with center $P + r\hat n$ (where $\hat n$ is the unit normal tow
ard the minor arc), the tangency condition $|P+r\hat n - O|=R-r$ gives:
$$r = \frac{R^2 - |P-O|^2}{2(R-h)},$$
where $h = \hat n\cdot(O-F)$ is the signed distance from $O$ to $\ell$ (in direc
tion $\hat n$), and $F$ is the chord midpoint. Since $R^2-|P-O|^2 = (\text{half-
chord})^2 - |P-F|^2$, this is purely in terms of chord geometry and $h$.

**Circle $\omega_A$** (tangent to $BC$ at $X$, minor arc below): $\hat n_A=(0,-1
)$, $F_A=(7/2,0)$, $h_A = -7\sqrt5/5$.
$$r_A = \frac{(7/2)^2-(7/6)^2}{2(21\sqrt5/10+7\sqrt5/5)} = \frac{98/9}{7\sqrt5}
= \frac{14\sqrt5}{45},\quad P_A=\left(\tfrac{7}{3},-\tfrac{14\sqrt5}{45}\right).
$$

**Circle $\omega_B$** (tangent to $CA$ at $Y$, minor arc away from $B$): $\hat n
_B=(3\sqrt5/7,\,2/7)$, $h_B=-11\sqrt5/10$.
$$r_B = \frac{4^2-(4/3)^2}{2(21\sqrt5/10+11\sqrt5/10)} = \frac{128/9}{32\sqrt5/5
} = \frac{4\sqrt5}{9},\quad P_B=\left(\tfrac{151}{21},\tfrac{80\sqrt5}{63}\right
).$$

**Circle $\omega_C$** (tangent to $AB$ at $Z$, minor arc away from $C$): $\hat n
_C=(-8\sqrt5/21,\,11/21)$, $h_C=-3\sqrt5/5$.
$$r_C = \frac{(9/2)^2-(3/2)^2}{2(21\sqrt5/10+3\sqrt5/5)} = \frac{18}{27\sqrt5/5}
 = \frac{2\sqrt5}{3},\quad P_C=\left(\tfrac{118}{63},\tfrac{166\sqrt5}{63}\right
).$$

**External tangent lengths.** Using $t = \sqrt{|P_i-P_j|^2 - (r_i-r_j)^2}$:

**$t_{AB}$:** $P_A-P_B = \left(-\frac{34}{7},\,-\frac{166\sqrt5}{105}\right)$, s
o $|P_A-P_B|^2 = \frac{397880}{11025}$. Also $(r_A-r_B)^2 = \frac{4}{45}=\frac{9
80}{11025}$.
$$t_{AB}^2 = \frac{396900}{11025}=36,\quad t_{AB}=6.$$

**$t_{BC}$:** $P_B-P_C = \left(\frac{335}{63},\,-\frac{86\sqrt5}{63}\right)$, so
 $|P_B-P_C|^2 = \frac{149205}{3969}$. Also $(r_B-r_C)^2=\frac{20}{81}=\frac{980}
{3969}$.
$$t_{BC}^2 = \frac{148225}{3969}=\frac{3025}{81},\quad t_{BC}=\frac{55}{9}.$$

**$t_{CA}$:** $P_C-P_A = \left(-\frac{29}{63},\,\frac{928\sqrt5}{315}\right)$, s
o $|P_C-P_A|^2 = \frac{4326945}{99225}$. Also $(r_C-r_A)^2=\frac{1280}{2025}=\fr
ac{62720}{99225}$.
$$t_{CA}^2 = \frac{4264225}{99225}=\frac{3481}{81},\quad t_{CA}=\frac{59}{9}.$$

**Sum.**
$$t_{AB}+t_{BC}+t_{CA} = 6 + \frac{55}{9}+\frac{59}{9} = \frac{54+55+59}{9} = \f
rac{168}{9} = \frac{56}{3}.$$

With $m=56$, $n=3$ (coprime), we get $m+n = \boxed{59}$.
