Key Adjunction Identity

A standard property of any adjunction $F \dashv G$ is that for any morphism $g:
A \to GB$:

  1. The adjoint (transpose) of $g$ is $\bar{g} := \epsilon_B \circ F(g): FA \to

  2. Recovering $g$ from its adjoint: $g = G(\bar{g}) \circ \eta_A$.

Combining these two facts, we get the identity: $$g = G(\epsilon_B \circ F(g))
\circ \eta_A = G(\epsilon_B) \circ GF(g) \circ \eta_A$$
