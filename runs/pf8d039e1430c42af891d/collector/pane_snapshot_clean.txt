**Answer: No.**

A perfect field of prime characteristic $p$ need not be finite.

**Counterexample.** Let $\overline{\mathbb{F}_p}$ be the algebraic closure of th
e finite field $\mathbb{F}_p$.

- **It has characteristic $p$.** Since it is an algebraic extension of $\mathbb{
F}_p$, it contains $\mathbb{F}_p$ and hence has characteristic $p$.

- **It is perfect.** A field of characteristic $p$ is perfect if and only if the
 Frobenius map $x \mapsto x^p$ is an automorphism. An algebraically closed field
 $K$ of characteristic $p$ is perfect: surjectivity of Frobenius holds because f
or any $a \in K$, the polynomial $X^p - a$ has a root in $K$ (by algebraic close
dness), so $a = b^p$ for some $b \in K$. Injectivity is automatic since $X^p - X
$ has at most $p$ roots and $b^p = 0 \Rightarrow b = 0$. Thus Frobenius is an au
tomorphism, and $\overline{\mathbb{F}_p}$ is perfect.

- **It is infinite.** It contains the subfields $\mathbb{F}_{p^n}$ for every $n
\geq 1$ (the splitting field of $X^{p^n}-X$ over $\mathbb{F}_p$), and these form
 a strictly increasing chain
  $$\mathbb{F}_p \subsetneq \mathbb{F}_{p^2} \subsetneq \mathbb{F}_{p^3} \subset
neq \cdots,$$
  since $[\mathbb{F}_{p^n}:\mathbb{F}_p] = n$. Hence $\overline{\mathbb{F}_p}$ c
ontains fields of arbitrarily large finite size and is therefore infinite.

Therefore $\overline{\mathbb{F}_p}$ is a perfect field of prime characteristic $
p$ that is infinite, so a perfect field of prime characteristic need not be fini
te.
