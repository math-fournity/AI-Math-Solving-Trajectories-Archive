## Solution

**Reformulation as an expectation.** Let $X \sim \operatorname{Bin}(n,\tfrac12)$
. Then $\frac{1}{2^n}\binom{n}{k} = \Pr(X=k)$, so

$$
S_n \;=\; \frac{1}{2^n}\sum_{k=\log n}^{n}\frac{1}{\sqrt{k}}\binom{n}{k}
\;=\; \mathbb{E}\!\left[\frac{\mathbf{1}_{\{X\,\ge\,\log n\}}}{\sqrt{X}}\right].
$$

(The term $k=0$ is excluded precisely so that $1/\sqrt{k}$ is well-defined; sinc
e $\log n \ge 1$ for $n\ge 3$, every $k$ in the sum satisfies $k\ge 1$.)

---

**Step 1 — The truncation $\mathbf{1}_{\{X\ge \log n\}}$ is negligible.** Since
$X$ is concentrated around $n/2$ and $\log n \ll n/2$, the event $\{X < \log n\}
$ has super-exponentially small probability. Indeed, by a Chernoff/Hoeffding bou
nd, for $n$ large,

$$
\Pr\!\big(X < \log n\big) \;\le\; \Pr\!\big(X < \tfrac{n}{4}\big) \;\le\; \exp\!
\big(-2(\tfrac14)^2 n\big) = e^{-n/8},
$$

(using $\log n < n/4$ for large $n$). On the event $\{X\ge \log n\}\supseteq\{X\
ge 1\}$ the integrand is at most $1$, so

$$
0 \;\le\; \mathbb{E}\!\left[\frac{1}{\sqrt X}\right] - S_n
\;=\; \mathbb{E}\!\left[\frac{\mathbf{1}_{\{X<\log n\}}}{\sqrt X}\right]
\;\le\; \Pr(X<\log n)\;\le\; e^{-n/8}.
$$

Thus $S_n = \mathbb{E}[1/\sqrt X] + O(e^{-n/8})$, and it suffices to analyze $\m
athbb{E}[1/\sqrt X]$.

---

**Step 2 — Asymptotic of $\mathbb{E}[1/\sqrt X]$.** Set $\mu = n/2$. Fix any $\v
arepsilon\in(0,\tfrac12)$ and split

$$
\mathbb{E}\!\left[\frac{1}{\sqrt X}\right]
= \mathbb{E}\!\left[\frac{1}{\sqrt X}\mathbf{1}_{A}\right] + \mathbb{E}\!\left[\
frac{1}{\sqrt X}\mathbf{1}_{A^c}\right],
\qquad A=\big\{|X-\mu|\le \varepsilon\mu\big\}.
$$

- **On $A$:** $\mu(1-\varepsilon)\le X\le \mu(1+\varepsilon)$, so
$$
\frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu(1+\varepsilon)}}\le \frac{1}{\sqrt X}\le \frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu(1
-\varepsilon)}}.
$$

- **On $A^c$:** $1/\sqrt X\le 1$ (since $X\ge 1$), and by Hoeffding $\Pr(A^c)\le
 2e^{-2\varepsilon^2\mu}=2e^{-\varepsilon^2 n}$.

Combining,

$$
\frac{1-\Pr(A^c)}{\sqrt{\mu(1+\varepsilon)}} \;\le\; \mathbb{E}\!\left[\frac{1}{
\sqrt X}\right] \;\le\; \frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu(1-\varepsilon)}} + \Pr(A^c).
$$

Substituting $\mu=n/2$ and $\Pr(A^c)=O(e^{-\varepsilon^2 n})$,

$$
\sqrt{\frac{2}{n}}\cdot\frac{1-o(1)}{\sqrt{1+\varepsilon}}
\;\le\; \mathbb{E}\!\left[\frac{1}{\sqrt X}\right]
\;\le\; \sqrt{\frac{2}{n}}\cdot\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-\varepsilon}} + O(e^{-\varepsilo
n^2 n}).
$$

Dividing by $\sqrt{2/n}$ and letting $n\to\infty$ (with $\varepsilon$ fixed),

$$
\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+\varepsilon}} \;\le\; \liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{\mathbb{E}[1/\s
qrt X]}{\sqrt{2/n}}
\;\le\; \limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac{\mathbb{E}[1/\sqrt X]}{\sqrt{2/n}}
\;\le\; \frac{1}{\sqrt{1-\varepsilon}}.
$$

Now letting $\varepsilon\downarrow 0$ squeezes both sides to $1$, giving

$$
\mathbb{E}\!\left[\frac{1}{\sqrt X}\right] \;\sim\; \sqrt{\frac{2}{n}}\qquad(n\t
o\infty).
$$

---

**Step 3 — Conclusion.** From Steps 1 and 2,

$$
S_n \;=\; \mathbb{E}\!\left[\frac{1}{\sqrt X}\right] + O(e^{-n/8}) \;=\; \sqrt{\
frac{2}{n}}\,(1+o(1)) \;+\; O(e^{-n/8}).
$$

In particular $S_n \sim \sqrt{2/n}\to 0$.

$$
\boxed{\displaystyle \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{1}{2^n}\sum_{k=\log n}^{n}\frac{1}{\
sqrt{k}}\binom{n}{k} \;=\; 0,}
\qquad\text{with the sharper asymptotic } S_n\sim\sqrt{\tfrac{2}{n}}.
$$
