# Problem

In the city of Gridhaven, a civil engineer is designing a solar panel array across a $12 \times 12$ plot of land, divided into 144 identical square plots. Each plot can host at most one high-capacity sensor. To ensure the electrical grid remains stable, the engineer must follow a strict "Even-Load Rule."

The rule states that for every possible straight line of plots across the grid, the number of sensors installed must be an even number. Specifically:
1. Every one of the 12 horizontal rows must contain an even number of sensors.
2. Every one of the 12 vertical columns must contain an even number of sensors.
3. Every diagonal path must contain an even number of sensors. This includes the two longest diagonals (12 plots each) and all shorter parallel diagonals on both orientations (ranging from 1 plot to 11 plots in length).

Under these constraints, what is the maximum total number of sensors that can be installed across the entire $12 \times 12$ plot?
