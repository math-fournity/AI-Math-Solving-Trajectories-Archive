## Proof

**Key Structural Lemma.** In a harmonious coloring, for each color $\alpha$, the
 graph $G_\alpha$ of $\alpha$-colored edges is a disjoint union of cliques (a cl
uster graph).

*Proof.* If edges $va$ and $vb$ both have color $\alpha$, then triangle $vab$ ha
s two $\alpha$-edges, so it must be monochromatic (not exactly 2 colors), forcin
g $c(a,b)=\alpha$. By induction, the $\alpha$-neighborhood of any vertex forms a
 clique. $\square$

**Linear Space Structure.** The maximal cliques across all color classes form a
**linear space**: every pair of vertices lies on exactly one "line" (the maximal
 clique of their edge's color). Lines of the same color are disjoint (since each
 $G_\alpha$ is a cluster graph).

**Upper Bound.** Suppose the coloring is non-monochromatic.

*Claim 1:* Every vertex lies on at least 2 lines. If vertex $p$ lies on only 1 l
ine $L$, all $N-1$ edges from $p$ belong to $L$, so $L$ contains all vertices an
d all edges share one color — contradicting non-monochromaticity.

*Claim 2:* Distinct lines through a vertex have distinct colors (since same-colo
red cliques sharing a vertex must be the same clique). So each vertex lies on at
 most 2018 lines.

*Claim 3:* Every line has size $\leq 2017$. Let $p$ be a vertex on lines $L_1, \
ldots, L_t$ ($t \geq 2$) with colors $\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_t$. Pick $i \neq
j$ and $b \in L_j \setminus \{p\}$. For any two vertices $a, a' \in L_i \setminu
s \{p\}$: triangle $a,a',b$ has $c(a,a') = \alpha_i$ while $c(a,b), c(a',b) \neq
 \alpha_i$ (from rainbow triangle $p,a,b$), so the triangle is rainbow, giving $
c(a,b) \neq c(a',b)$. Also $c(a,b) \notin \{\alpha_i, \alpha_j\}$ (from rainbow
triangles $p,a,b$ and $p,a',b$). Thus the $|L_i|-1$ edges from $b$ to $L_i \setm
inus \{p\}$ have **distinct colors, none in $\{\alpha_i, \alpha_j\}$**, requirin
g $|L_i| - 1 \leq 2016$, i.e., $|L_i| \leq 2017$.

*Combining:* At any vertex $p$ with lines of sizes $k_1, \ldots, k_t$:
$$N - 1 = \sum_{i=1}^{t}(k_i - 1) \leq 2018 \times 2016 = (2017+1)(2017-1) = 201
7^2 - 1.$$
Therefore $N \leq 2017^2 = 4{,}068{,}289$.

**Construction achieving the bound.** Since 2017 is prime, the affine plane $AG(
2, 2017)$ over $\mathbb{F}_{2017}$ exists. It has $2017^2$ points and $2017^2 +
2017$ lines, partitioned into $2017 + 1 = 2018$ parallel classes, each containin
g 2017 disjoint lines of size 2017.

Assign one color to each parallel class. Then:
- Every pair of points lies on exactly one line (linear space property), so ever
y edge receives exactly one color. ✓
- Lines of the same color are disjoint (parallel class property), consistent wit
h cluster graph structure. ✓
- **Triangle check:** Three points are either collinear (all on one line → monoc
hromatic triangle) or non-collinear (forming a triangle whose three sides lie on
 three pairwise-intersecting lines, which must be in three distinct parallel cla
sses → rainbow triangle). ✓
- Exactly 2018 colors are used (one per parallel class, all non-empty). ✓
- Not monochromatic (2018 > 1 colors). ✓

This gives $N = 2017^2 = 4{,}068{,}289$.

**Conclusion.** The upper bound $N \leq 2017^2$ matches the construction, so the
 largest $N$ is:

$$\boxed{N = 2017^2 = 4{,}068{,}289}$$
