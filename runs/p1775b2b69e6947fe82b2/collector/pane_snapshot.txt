## Key Formula

Place numbers $a_1, \ldots, a_n$ on the cycle. Each vertex $i$ participates in t
wo edges. Its contribution to $S$ is:
- $+2a_i$ if $a_i$ is a **local maximum** (larger than both neighbors)
- $-2a_i$ if $a_i$ is a **local minimum** (smaller than both neighbors)
- $0$ if $a_i$ is **neutral** (between its two neighbors)

Therefore: $$S = 2\left(\sum_{\text{local max}} a_i - \sum_{\text{local min}} a_
i\right)$$

In a cycle, local maxima and minima alternate, so $\#\text{max} = \#\text{min} =
 k$ for some $k$, with $n - 2k$ neutral vertices.

## Maximizing S

To maximize $S$, assign the $k$ largest values to maxima and $k$ smallest to min
ima:
$$\text{difference} = \sum_{i=n-k+1}^{n} i - \sum_{i=1}^{k} i = kn - k^2 = k(n-k
)$$
