# Problem

Given a positive integer $k=10$ and two integers $b=200$ and $w=150$. There are two strings of pearls: a string of $b$ black pearls and a string of $w$ white pearls. One cuts these strings in steps by the following rules. In each step:
(i) The strings are ordered by their lengths in a non-increasing order. If there are strings of equal lengths, the white ones precede the black ones. Then the $k$ first strings (if they consist of more than one pearl) are chosen; if there are fewer than $k$ strings longer than 1, all of them are chosen.
(ii) Each chosen string is cut into two parts differing in length by at most one.
The process stops immediately after the step when a first isolated white pearl (a string of length 1) appears. Let $n_b$ be the number of strings of at least two black pearls remaining when the process stops. Determine the minimum possible value of $n_b$ according to the rules described.
