## Solution

**Reformulation.** For $0<x<1$, rewrite using $x^n-1=-(1-x^n)$:
$$P(x)=\frac{(1-x^{2310})^6}{(1-x^{105})(1-x^{70})(1-x^{42})(1-x^{30})}.$$
(The numerator has even exponent so the sign is positive; the denominator has fo
ur factors, also positive.)

**Eliminating the numerator.** Since $(1-x^{2310})^6 = \sum_{j=0}^{6}\binom{6}{j
}(-1)^j x^{2310j}$ and $2310>2022$, only the $j=0$ term contributes to the coeff
icient of $x^{2022}$. Thus
$$[x^{2022}]P(x)=[x^{2022}]\frac{1}{(1-x^{105})(1-x^{70})(1-x^{42})(1-x^{30})}.$
$

**Counting representations.** Expanding each factor as a geometric series, $[x^{
2022}]$ equals the number of non-negative integer solutions of
$$105a+70b+42c+30d=2022.$$

**Step 1 (mod 2).** Since $70b,42c,30d$ are even and $2022$ is even, $105a\equiv
 a\pmod 2$ must be even, so $a=2a'$:
$$210a'+70b+42c+30d=2022 \;\Longrightarrow\; 105a'+35b+21c+15d=1011.$$

**Step 2 (mod 3).** Modulo 3: $35b\equiv 2b$ and $1011\equiv 0$, so $b\equiv 0\p
mod 3$. Write $b=3b'$:
$$105a'+105b'+21c+15d=1011 \;\Longrightarrow\; 35a'+35b'+7c+5d=337.$$

**Step 3 (mod 5).** Modulo 5: $7c\equiv 2c$ and $337\equiv 2$, so $c\equiv 1\pmo
d 5$. Write $c=5c'+1$:
$$35(a'+b'+c')+5d=330 \;\Longrightarrow\; 7(a'+b'+c')+d=66.$$

**Step 4 (count).** Let $t=a'+b'+c'\ge 0$. Then $d=66-7t\ge 0$ requires $0\le t\
le 9$. For each $t$, the number of non-negative triples $(a',b',c')$ summing to
$t$ is $\binom{t+2}{2}$. Hence
$$[x^{2022}]P(x)=\sum_{t=0}^{9}\binom{t+2}{2}=\sum_{k=2}^{11}\binom{k}{2}=\binom
{12}{3}=\frac{12\cdot 11\cdot 10}{6}=220.$$

The coefficient of $x^{2022}$ in $P(x)$ is $\boxed{220}$.
