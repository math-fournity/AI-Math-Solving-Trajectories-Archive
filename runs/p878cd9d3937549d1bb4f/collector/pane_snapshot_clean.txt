## Solution

**Setup.** The surface is the cone $z = 1 - \sqrt{x^2+y^2}$ (vertex at $(0,0,1)$
, opening downward). The plane is $z = -(x+1)/2$. We seek the surface area of th
e cone portion lying above the plane, i.e., where $z \geq -(x+1)/2$.

**Finding the projection region $D$ (the ellipse).** On the cone, the boundary w
ith the plane satisfies
$$1 - \sqrt{x^2+y^2} = -\frac{x+1}{2} \implies \sqrt{x^2+y^2} = \frac{x+3}{2}.$$
Squaring (valid since $x \geq -3$ here):
$$x^2 + y^2 = \frac{(x+3)^2}{4} \implies 4x^2 + 4y^2 = x^2 + 6x + 9$$
$$3x^2 - 6x + 4y^2 = 9 \implies 3(x-1)^2 + 4y^2 = 12$$
$$\frac{(x-1)^2}{4} + \frac{y^2}{3} = 1.$$

So $D$ is the ellipse centered at $(1,0)$ with semi-axes $a = 2$, $b = \sqrt{3}$
, and area
$$\text{Area}(D) = \pi \cdot 2 \cdot \sqrt{3} = 2\sqrt{3}\,\pi.$$

**Surface area element.** For $z = 1 - \sqrt{x^2+y^2}$:
$$z_x = -\frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}, \quad z_y = -\frac{y}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}, \quad
z_x^2 + z_y^2 = \frac{x^2+y^2}{x^2+y^2} = 1.$$
Hence
$$dS = \sqrt{1 + z_x^2 + z_y^2}\,dx\,dy = \sqrt{2}\,dx\,dy.$$

**The double integral.**
$$S = \iint_D \sqrt{1 + z_x^2 + z_y^2}\,dx\,dy = \sqrt{2}\iint_D dx\,dy = \sqrt{
2}\cdot\text{Area}(D).$$

**Evaluation.**
$$S = \sqrt{2} \cdot 2\sqrt{3}\,\pi = 2\sqrt{6}\,\pi.$$

The surface area is $\boxed{2\sqrt{6}\,\pi}$.
