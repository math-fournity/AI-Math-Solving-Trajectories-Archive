## Proof

)\subset\mathbb{T}$ be the group of eigenvalues (a countable subgroup of $\mathb
b{T}$, since $L^2(\mu)$ is separable). By the Halmos–von Neumann theorem, the sy
stem is isomorphic to a rotation $R_{t_0}$ on the compact Abelian group $\wideha
t{G}$ (Pontryagin dual of the discrete group $G$) equipped with Haar measure, wh
ere $t_0\in\widehat{G}$ is a **generator**, i.e., the cyclic subgroup $\{n\,t_0:
n\in\mathbb{Z}\}$ is dense in $\widehat{G}$. Equivalently (by Pontryagin duality
), the evaluation map
$$
\Phi:G\longrightarrow\mathbb{T},\qquad \Phi(g)=g(t_0)=\langle t_0,g\rangle
$$
is injective, so each $g\in G$ appears as an eigenvalue of $R_{t_0}$ with multip
licity one, the eigenfunction being the character $\chi_g(x)=\langle x,g\rangle$
