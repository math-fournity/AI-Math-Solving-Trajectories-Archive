# Problem

A vast solar farm is organized into a perfect grid of $27 \times 27$ plots. The facility manager is installing blue high-efficiency panels on some plots, while leaving others as standard grey. To ensure the integrity of the power grid, two strict regulatory constraints must be met:

1. Within every possible $2 \times 2$ square of plots, the total count of blue-panel plots must be an even number ($0, 2,$ or $4$).
2. Within every possible $3 \times 3$ square of plots, the total count of blue-panel plots must be an odd number ($1, 3, 5, 7,$ or $9$).

To prevent a specific type of feedback loop, it is required that at least one plot in the entire $27 \times 27$ grid is not a blue-panel plot.

Calculate the maximum possible number of plots that can be equipped with blue panels while satisfying all these conditions.
