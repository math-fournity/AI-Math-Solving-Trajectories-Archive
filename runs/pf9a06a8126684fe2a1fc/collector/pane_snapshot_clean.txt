## Proof

We prove the following lemma, then apply it to the compact set $K$.

**Lemma.** Let $X$ be a locally compact Hausdorff space, $x \in X$, and $U \subs
et X$ an open neighborhood of $x$. Then there exists an open set $V$ with $x \in
 V$, $\overline{V}$ compact, and $\overline{V} \subset U$.

*Proof of Lemma.* By local compactness, there is an open set $O$ with $x \in O$
and $\overline{O}$ compact. Set $C := \overline{O}$. Since $C$ is a compact Haus
dorff space, it is regular. Now $U \cap C$ is open in $C$ (as $U$ is open in $X$
) and contains $x$. By regularity of $C$, there exists a set $A$ open in $C$ wit
h
$$x \in A \quad\text{and}\quad \overline{A}^{\,C} \subset U \cap C.$$
Write $A = C \cap W$ for some $W$ open in $X$, and set $V := W \cap O$. Then $V$
 is open in $X$ and $x \in V \subset A \subset C$.

Since $V \subset C$ and $C$ is closed in $X$, we have $\overline{V}^{\,X} = \ove
rline{V}^{\,C} \subset \overline{A}^{\,C} \subset U \cap C \subset U$. Moreover
$\overline{V}^{\,X}$ is a closed subset of the compact set $C$, hence compact. ∎

**Main proof.** For each $x \in K$, apply the lemma to obtain an open set $V_x$
with $x \in V_x$, $\overline{V_x}$ compact, and $\overline{V_x} \subset U$. The
family $\{V_x\}_{x \in K}$ is an open cover of the compact set $K$, so there exi
st $x_1, \dots, x_n \in K$ with
$$K \subset V := V_{x_1} \cup \cdots \cup V_{x_n}.$$
The set $V$ is open. Since each $V_{x_i} \subset \overline{V_{x_i}} \subset U$,
we have $V \subset U$. Finally,
$$\overline{V} = \overline{V_{x_1}} \cup \cdots \cup \overline{V_{x_n}},$$
a finite union of compact sets, hence compact.

Thus $V$ satisfies $K \subset V \subset U$ with $\overline{V}$ compact. $\blacks
quare$
