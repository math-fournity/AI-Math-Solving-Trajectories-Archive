**Step 2: $\mathbb{Z}$ has finite quotients.**

**Step 3: $\mathbb{Z}$ has infinitely many prime ideals.**
The nonzero prime ideals of $\mathbb{Z}$ are precisely $(p)$ where $p$ ranges ov

*Proof of Euclid's theorem.* Suppose for contradiction that $p_1, p_2, \ldots, p

Since there are infinitely many rational primes, $\mathbb{Z}$ has infinitely man
