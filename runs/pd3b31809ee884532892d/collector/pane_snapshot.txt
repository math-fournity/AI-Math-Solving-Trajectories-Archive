## Solution

We want to evaluate
$$L = \lim_{x\to\infty} \frac{\int_0^x \cos(t^2)\,dt - \int_0^\infty \cos(t^2)\,
dt}{x^3}.$$

**Step 1: Rewrite the numerator.** Since $\int_0^x - \int_0^\infty = -\int_x^\in
fty$,
$$L = \lim_{x\to\infty} \frac{-\int_x^\infty \cos(t^2)\,dt}{x^3}.$$

**Step 2: Asymptotic expansion of the tail integral via integration by parts.**

Write $\cos(t^2) = \frac{1}{2t}\cdot 2t\cos(t^2)$ and integrate by parts with $u
 = \frac{1}{2t}$, $dv = 2t\cos(t^2)\,dt$ (so $v = \sin(t^2)$):
$$\int_x^\infty \cos(t^2)\,dt = \left[\frac{\sin(t^2)}{2t}\right]_x^\infty + \in
t_x^\infty \frac{\sin(t^2)}{2t^2}\,dt = -\frac{\sin(x^2)}{2x} + \int_x^\infty \f
rac{\sin(t^2)}{2t^2}\,dt,$$
where the boundary term at $\infty$ vanishes since $|\sin(t^2)|\le 1$ and $1/(2t
)\to 0$.

Integrate by parts again on the remainder, using $\frac{\sin(t^2)}{2t^2} = \frac
{1}{4t^3}\cdot 2t\sin(t^2)$ with $v = -\cos(t^2)$:
$$\int_x^\infty \frac{\sin(t^2)}{2t^2}\,dt = \left[-\frac{\cos(t^2)}{4t^3}\right
]_x^\infty - \int_x^\infty \frac{3\cos(t^2)}{4t^4}\,dt = \frac{\cos(x^2)}{4x^3}
- \int_x^\infty \frac{3\cos(t^2)}{4t^4}\,dt.$$

Combining:
$$\int_x^\infty \cos(t^2)\,dt = -\frac{\sin(x^2)}{2x} + \frac{\cos(x^2)}{4x^3} -
 \int_x^\infty \frac{3\cos(t^2)}{4t^4}\,dt.$$

**Step 3: Bound the remainder.**
$$\left|\int_x^\infty \frac{3\cos(t^2)}{4t^4}\,dt\right| \le \int_x^\infty \frac
{3}{4t^4}\,dt = \frac{1}{4x^3}.$$

**Step 4: Divide by $x^3$ and take the limit.**
$$\frac{-\int_x^\infty \cos(t^2)\,dt}{x^3} = \frac{\sin(x^2)}{2x^4} - \frac{\cos
(x^2)}{4x^6} + \frac{1}{x^3}\int_x^\infty \frac{3\cos(t^2)}{4t^4}\,dt.$$

Each term is bounded in absolute value:
- $\left|\frac{\sin(x^2)}{2x^4}\right| \le \frac{1}{2x^4} \to 0$,
- $\left|\frac{\cos(x^2)}{4x^6}\right| \le \frac{1}{4x^6} \to 0$,
- $\left|\frac{1}{x^3}\int_x^\infty \frac{3\cos(t^2)}{4t^4}\,dt\right| \le \frac
{1}{4x^6} \to 0$.

All three terms vanish as $x\to\infty$, so by the squeeze theorem,

$$L = 0.$$
