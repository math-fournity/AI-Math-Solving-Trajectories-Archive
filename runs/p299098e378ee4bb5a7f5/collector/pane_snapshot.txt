Looking at this problem, I need to find the largest $r$ guaranteed to fit inside
 **every** convex quadrilateral with area $S=2024$ and perimeter $p=184$.

## Proof

**Key Inequality.** Let $Q$ be any convex quadrilateral with area $S$ and perime
ter $p$. Let $r_Q$ be the radius of the largest circle inscribed in $Q$, with ce
nter $O$.

Since $O$ lies inside the convex quadrilateral, connecting $O$ to the four verti
ces decomposes $Q$ into four triangles. The area of the triangle formed by $O$ a
nd side $i$ (of length $\ell_i$) equals $\tfrac{1}{2}\,\ell_i\, d_i$, where $d_i
$ is the perpendicular distance from $O$ to side $i$. Therefore:

$$S = \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{4} d_i\, \ell_i.$$

Since the circle of radius $r_Q$ centered at $O$ fits inside $Q$, every side is
at distance $\geq r_Q$ from $O$, i.e., $d_i \geq r_Q$ for all $i$. Thus:

$$S = \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{4} d_i\, \ell_i \;\geq\; \frac{1}{2}\, r_Q \sum_{i=
1}^{4} \ell_i \;=\; \frac{1}{2}\, r_Q\, p \;=\; r_Q \cdot s,$$

where $s = p/2$ is the semi-perimeter. This gives:

$$r_Q \;\leq\; \frac{S}{s} \;=\; \frac{2S}{p}.$$

**Equality is achievable.** Equality holds if and only if $d_i = r_Q$ for all fo
ur sides, meaning the inscribed circle touches all four sides — i.e., $Q$ is a *
*tangential quadrilateral**.

Consider a **rhombus** with side length $a = 46$ and interior angle $\theta$ whe
re $\sin\theta = \frac{22}{23}$.

- **Perimeter:** $4 \times 46 = 184 = p$. ✓
- **Area:** $a^2 \sin\theta = 46^2 \cdot \frac{22}{23} = 2116 \cdot \frac{22}{23
} = 92 \times 22 = 2024 = S$. ✓
- Every rhombus is tangential (it has an inscribed circle), so equality holds in
 the inequality above.
- **Inradius:** $r = \frac{S}{s} = \frac{2024}{92} = 22$.

**Conclusion.** Every convex quadrilateral with area $S$ and perimeter $p$ has i
nradius at most $\frac{2S}{p}$, and this bound is achieved by the rhombus above.
 Therefore the maximum $r$ that is **guaranteed** to fit inside any such quadril

$$r = \frac{2S}{p} = \frac{2 \times 2024}{184} = \frac{4048}{184} = \boxed{22}.$
