## Solution

Madoka chooses $a, b, c, d$ uniformly in $[0,1]$, and we condition on $a+b+c=1$.
 We want $P(d > a,\ d > b,\ d > c)$.

**Step 1: Distribution of $(a,b,c)$ given $a+b+c=1$.**

Since $a,b,c$ are i.i.d. $\text{Uniform}[0,1]$, the joint density is constant on
 $[0,1]^3$. Conditioning on the plane $a+b+c=1$, the intersection with the cube
is exactly the simplex $\{a,b,c \ge 0,\ a+b+c=1\}$ (which lies entirely inside t
he cube since nonnegativity and sum $=1$ force each coordinate $\le 1$). Hence $
(a,b,c)$ is **uniform on this simplex**.

**Step 2: Reduce to $\mathbb{E}[\max(a,b,c)]$.**

$d$ is independent of $(a,b,c)$ and uniform on $[0,1]$. Let $M = \max(a,b,c)$. S
ince $M \le 1$ always,
$$P(d > M \mid M) = 1 - M,$$
so
$$P(d > a, d > b, d > c) = \mathbb{E}[1 - M] = 1 - \mathbb{E}[M].$$

**Step 3: Compute $\mathbb{E}[M]$ via the CDF.**

For $(a,b,c)$ uniform on the simplex, $P(a > t) = (1-t)^2$ and $P(a>t, b>t) = (1
-2t)^2$ for $t \le \tfrac12$ (and $0$ for $t > \tfrac12$). By inclusion–exclusio
n:

- For $t \in [\tfrac13, \tfrac12]$: $P(M > t) = 3(1-t)^2 - 3(1-2t)^2$.
- For $t \in [\tfrac12, 1]$: $P(M > t) = 3(1-t)^2$.
- For $t < \tfrac13$: $P(M > t) = 1$ (since $M \ge \tfrac13$ always).

Then
$$\mathbb{E}[M] = \int_0^{1/3} 1\,dt + \int_{1/3}^{1/2}\!\bigl[3(1-t)^2 - 3(1-2t
)^2\bigr]\,dt + \int_{1/2}^{1} 3(1-t)^2\,dt.$$

- First integral: $\tfrac13$.
- Third integral: $3 \cdot \tfrac{1}{24} = \tfrac18$.
- Second integral: $3\!\int_{1/3}^{1/2}(1-t)^2 dt - 3\!\int_{1/3}^{1/2}(1-2t)^2
dt = \tfrac{37}{216} - \tfrac{1}{54} = \tfrac{11}{72}$.

So
$$\mathbb{E}[M] = \tfrac13 + \tfrac{11}{72} + \tfrac18 = \tfrac{24 + 11 + 9}{72}
 = \tfrac{44}{72} = \tfrac{11}{18}.$$

*(Cross-check: the known formula $\mathbb{E}[\max] = H_n/n$ for the uniform simp
lex in $n$ dimensions gives $H_3/3 = \tfrac{11/6}{3} = \tfrac{11}{18}$. ✓)*

**Step 4: Final probability.**

$$P = 1 - \tfrac{11}{18} = \tfrac{7}{18}.$$

Since $\gcd(7, 18) = 1$, we have $m = 7$, $n = 18$, and

$$m + n = 7 + 18 = \boxed{25}.$$
