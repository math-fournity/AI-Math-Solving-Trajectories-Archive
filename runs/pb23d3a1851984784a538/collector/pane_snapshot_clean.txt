## Setup

The $2n$-th roots of unity are $e^{i\pi j/n}$ for $j = 0, 1, \ldots, 2n-1$, at a
ngles $\frac{\pi j}{n}$.

**First quadrant** (strictly): $0 < \frac{\pi j}{n} < \frac{\pi}{2}$, i.e., $0 <

So $j$ ranges from $1$ to $m_n = \lfloor (n-1)/2 \rfloor$.

The product (synergy value):
$$a_n = \prod_{j=1}^{m_n} e^{i\pi j/n} = e^{i\pi \cdot \frac{m_n(m_n+1)}{2n}}$$

For $n=1,2$: $m_n = 0$, so $a_1 = a_2 = 1$ (empty product).
