## Proof

We evaluate $S = \sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{(1/2)_n}{n!}\bigl(H_n - H_{n-1/2}\bigr)
$, where $H_z = \psi(z+1)+\gamma$.

**Step 1: Two generating functions.**

Let $F(x) = \sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{(1/2)_n}{n!}(H_n - H_{n-1/2})\,x^n$ for $|x|
<1$. We compute the two parts separately.

*Part A — the $H_{n-1/2}$ part.* From $\sum_{n\ge 0}\frac{(a)_n}{n!}x^n = (1-x)^
{-a}$, differentiating in $a$ gives
$$\sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{(a)_n}{n!}\bigl(\psi(a+n)-\psi(a)\bigr)x^n = -(1-x)^{-
a}\ln(1-x).$$
Since $\psi(n+\tfrac12)-\psi(\tfrac12) = H_{n-1/2}-H_{-1/2}$, setting $a=\tfrac1
2$:
$$\sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{(1/2)_n}{n!}H_{n-1/2}\,x^n = (1-x)^{-1/2}\bigl(H_{-1/2
}-\ln(1-x)\bigr). \tag{1}$$

*Part B — the $H_n$ part.* Using $H_n = \int_0^1 \frac{1-t^n}{1-t}\,dt$,
$$\sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{(1/2)_n}{n!}H_n\,x^n = \int_0^1 \frac{(1-x)^{-1/2}-(1-
xt)^{-1/2}}{1-t}\,dt.$$
Set $a=1-x$ and substitute $v=ar$ (with $r:1\to 1/a$), then $u=\sqrt{r}$:
$$\int_a^1 \frac{a^{-1/2}-v^{-1/2}}{v-a}\,dv = a^{-1/2}\int_1^{1/a}\frac{1-r^{-1
/2}}{r-1}\,dr = a^{-1/2}\int_1^{1/a}\frac{dr}{\sqrt{r}(\sqrt{r}+1)} = 2a^{-1/2}\
Bigl[\ln(\sqrt{r}+1)\Bigr]_1^{1/a}.$$
Thus
$$\sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{(1/2)_n}{n!}H_n\,x^n = 2(1-x)^{-1/2}\ln\!\frac{1+\sqrt
{1-x}}{2\sqrt{1-x}}. \tag{2}$$
(Check at $x=0$: both sides give $0$. ✓)

**Step 2: Combine.**

Subtracting (1) from (2):
$$F(x) = (1-x)^{-1/2}\left[2\ln\frac{1+\sqrt{1-x}}{2\sqrt{1-x}} + \ln(1-x) - H_{
-1/2}\right].$$
Expanding the logarithm, $2\ln\frac{1+\sqrt{a}}{2\sqrt{a}} + \ln a = 2\ln(1+\sqr
t{a}) - 2\ln 2$. Since $H_{-1/2}=\psi(\tfrac12)+\gamma = -2\ln 2$, the $-2\ln 2$
 and $-H_{-1/2}=+2\ln 2$ cancel, leaving
$$F(x) = \frac{2\ln\bigl(1+\sqrt{1-x}\bigr)}{\sqrt{1-x}}. \tag{3}$$

**Step 3: Take $x\to 1^-$.**

The series converges absolutely: for large $n$, $\frac{(1/2)_n}{n!}\sim \frac{1}
{\sqrt{\pi n}}$ and $H_n-H_{n-1/2}=\psi(n+1)-\psi(n+\tfrac12)\sim \frac{1}{2n}$,

x)$. Setting $t=\sqrt{1-x}\to 0^+$ in (3):
$$S = \lim_{t\to 0^+}\frac{2\ln(1+t)}{t} = 2.$$
