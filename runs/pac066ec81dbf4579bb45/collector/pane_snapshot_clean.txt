## Proof

**Claim.** For every $\beta > 1$, the series
$$g(x) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{\cos(xn)}{n^{\beta}}$$
defines a continuous function on $\mathbb{R}$.

**Step 1 — Pointwise bound on each summand.**
For every $n \geq 1$ and every $x \in \mathbb{R}$,
$$\left|\frac{\cos(xn)}{n^{\beta}}\right| \leq \frac{1}{n^{\beta}},$$
since $|\cos(xn)| \leq 1$.

**Step 2 — The majorant series converges.**
Because $\beta > 1$, the $p$-series
$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^{\beta}}$$
converges. Set $M_n := 1/n^{\beta}$, so $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} M_n < \infty$.

**Step 3 — Uniform convergence via the Weierstrass $M$-test.**
The bound in Step 1 holds for *all* $x \in \mathbb{R}$ simultaneously, and $\sum
 M_n$ converges. By the Weierstrass $M$-test, the series
$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{\cos(xn)}{n^{\beta}}$$
converges **uniformly** on $\mathbb{R}$.

**Step 4 — Continuity of the partial sums.**
Each partial sum
$$S_N(x) = \sum_{n=1}^{N} \frac{\cos(xn)}{n^{\beta}}$$
is a finite linear combination of the functions $x \mapsto \cos(xn)$, each of wh
ich is continuous on $\mathbb{R}$. Hence every $S_N$ is continuous on $\mathbb{R
}$.

**Step 5 — Uniform limit of continuous functions is continuous.**
A standard theorem of real analysis states: if a sequence of continuous function
s converges uniformly on a set, then its limit is continuous on that set. By Ste
ps 3 and 4, $(S_N)$ is a sequence of continuous functions on $\mathbb{R}$ conver
ging uniformly to $g$ on $\mathbb{R}$. Therefore $g$ is continuous on $\mathbb{R
}$.

**Conclusion.** Yes — for every $\beta > 1$, the series $g(x) = \sum_{n=1}^{\inf
ty} \frac{\cos(xn)}{n^{\beta}}$ defines a continuous function on $\mathbb{R}$. T
he key mechanism is that $\beta > 1$ supplies an $x$-independent summable majora
nt $1/n^{\beta}$, which gives uniform convergence on the entire real line, and u
niform convergence preserves continuity.
