## Solution

**Setup.** Place the intersection point at the origin, with the three pairwise-p
erpendicular chords along the coordinate axes. Let the chord of length 5 lie on
the x-axis, length 6 on the y-axis, length 7 on the z-axis. Write the endpoints
as:

- x-axis: $(a,0,0)$ and $(-b,0,0)$ with $a+b=5$, $a,b>0$.
- y-axis: $(0,c,0)$ and $(0,-d,0)$ with $c+d=6$, $c,d>0$.
- z-axis: $(0,0,e)$ and $(0,0,-f)$ with $e+f=7$, $e,f>0$.

Let the sphere have center $(h,k,\ell)$ and radius $R$.

**Finding the center.** Since $(a,0,0)$ and $(-b,0,0)$ are both on the sphere:
$$(a-h)^2+k^2+\ell^2 = (b+h)^2+k^2+\ell^2 \implies (a-h)^2=(b+h)^2 \implies h=\f
rac{a-b}{2}.$$

Similarly $k=\frac{c-d}{2}$ and $\ell=\frac{e-f}{2}$.

**Computing $R^2$ from each axis.** Note $a-h = \frac{a+b}{2}=\frac{5}{2}$, $c-k
=\frac{c+d}{2}=3$, $e-\ell=\frac{e+f}{2}=\frac{7}{2}$.

- From x-axis points: $R^2 = \frac{25}{4}+k^2+\ell^2$.
- From y-axis points: $R^2 = h^2+9+\ell^2$.
- From z-axis points: $R^2 = h^2+k^2+\frac{49}{4}$.

**Deriving constraints.** Equating the first two:
$$\frac{25}{4}+k^2 = h^2+9 \implies k^2-h^2=\frac{11}{4}.$$

Equating the first and third:
$$\frac{25}{4}+\ell^2 = h^2+\frac{49}{4} \implies \ell^2-h^2=6.$$

**Minimizing $R^2$.** Substituting $k^2=h^2+\frac{11}{4}$ and $\ell^2=h^2+6$:
$$R^2 = \frac{25}{4}+\left(h^2+\frac{11}{4}\right)+(h^2+6)=9+6+2h^2=15+2h^2.$$

To minimize $R^2$, minimize $h^2\geq 0$. Setting $h=0$ (i.e., $a=b=\frac{5}{2}$)
 gives $k^2=\frac{11}{4}$, $\ell^2=6$.

**Feasibility check.** We need $|k|<3$ (so that $d=3-k>0$) and $|\ell|<\frac{7}{
2}$ (so that $f=\frac{7}{2}-\ell>0$):
$$|k|=\frac{\sqrt{11}}{2}\approx 1.66 < 3,\qquad |\ell|=\sqrt{6}\approx 2.45 < 3
.5.\quad\checkmark$$

All six endpoints are positive, so the configuration is valid.

**Verification.** With center $(0,\frac{\sqrt{11}}{2},\sqrt{6})$:
- $(5/2,0,0)$: $\frac{25}{4}+\frac{11}{4}+6=15$. ✓
- $(0,3+\frac{\sqrt{11}}{2},0)$: $9+6=15$. ✓
- $(0,0,\frac{7}{2}+\sqrt{6})$: $\frac{11}{4}+\frac{49}{4}=15$. ✓

**Result.** The minimum is $R^2=15=\frac{15}{1}$, which is irreducible, so $a=15
$, $b=1$, and

$$a+b=16.$$
