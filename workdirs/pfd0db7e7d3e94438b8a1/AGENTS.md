# Problem

In a remote $3n \times 3n$ grid of solar energy collectors (where $n \ge 2$), a maintenance drone is deployed to perform a diagnostic circuit. The drone begins its task at a specific collector and must navigate the grid according to strict propulsion protocols:

1. **North Shift:** Move exactly one unit North to the adjacent collector.
2. **East Shift:** Move exactly one unit East to the adjacent collector.
3. **South-West Vector:** Move exactly one unit South and one unit West simultaneously (a single diagonal move).

The drone's mission parameters require it to follow a continuous path of shifts that eventually returns it to the starting collector. To ensure data integrity, the drone is programmed never to visit the same collector more than once during its circuit (excluding the return to the start).

Given the dimensions of the grid and the specific movement constraints, determine the maximum possible number of shifts the drone can perform in a single valid circuit.
