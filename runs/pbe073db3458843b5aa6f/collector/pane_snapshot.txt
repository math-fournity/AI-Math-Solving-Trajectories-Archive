## Solution

**Answer-leak check:** The problem statement contains only the problem, no solut
ions or answer values. Proceeding.

Let $u = \dfrac{a+b+c}{3}-\sqrt[3]{abc}$ and $v = \lambda\!\left(\dfrac{a+b}{2}-
\sqrt{ab}\right)$.

By AM–GM, $u \ge 0$ and $\dfrac{a+b}{2}-\sqrt{ab}=\dfrac{(\sqrt a-\sqrt b)^2}{2}
\ge 0$, so $v\ge 0$.

Since $f(x)=x^2+x+\sqrt3$ satisfies $f'(x)=2x+1>0$ on $[0,\infty)$, $f$ is stric
tly increasing on $[0,\infty)$. Hence
$$f(u)\ge f(v) \iff u\ge v \iff \frac{a+b+c}{3}-\sqrt[3]{abc}\;\ge\;\lambda\!\le
ft(\frac{a+b}{2}-\sqrt{ab}\right).$$

So we need
$$\lambda \;\le\; \frac{\,\frac{a+b+c}{3}-\sqrt[3]{abc}\,}{\frac{a+b}{2}-\sqrt{a
b}}\quad\text{for all }a,b,c>0,\ a\ne b,$$
and $\lambda_{\max}=\inf$ of this ratio.

**Homogeneity.** Both numerator and denominator are homogeneous of degree $1$, s
o the ratio is scale-invariant. Set $\sqrt a=p,\ \sqrt b=q,\ \sqrt c=r$ ($p,q,r>
0$), and by symmetry/scaling take $q=1$, $p=s>1$. Then
$$A:=\frac{a+b}{2}-\sqrt{ab}=\frac{(s-1)^2}{2},\qquad B:=\frac{a+b+c}{3}-\sqrt[3
]{abc}=\frac{s^2+1+r^2}{3}-(sr)^{2/3},$$
$$R(s,r)=\frac{B}{A}=\frac{2}{(s-1)^2}\left[\frac{s^2+1+r^2}{3}-(sr)^{2/3}\right
].$$

**Minimize over $r$ for fixed $s$.** Let $g(r)=\dfrac{s^2+1+r^2}{3}-(sr)^{2/3}$.
 Then
$$g'(r)=\frac{2r}{3}-\frac{2s}{3(sr)^{1/3}},\qquad g''(r)=\frac{2}{3}+\frac{2s^2
}{9(sr)^{4/3}}>0.$$
So $g$ is strictly convex. Setting $g'(r)=0$:
$$r=\frac{s}{(sr)^{1/3}}\;\Longrightarrow\;r^{4/3}=s^{2/3}\;\Longrightarrow\;r=\
sqrt{s}.$$
At $r=\sqrt{s}$: $(sr)^{2/3}=(s\cdot\sqrt s)^{2/3}=(s^{3/2})^{2/3}=s$, hence
$$g(\sqrt s)=\frac{s^2+1+s}{3}-s=\frac{s^2-2s+1}{3}=\frac{(s-1)^2}{3}.$$
Therefore
$$R(s,r)\;\ge\;\frac{2}{(s-1)^2}\cdot\frac{(s-1)^2}{3}=\frac{2}{3},$$
with equality iff $r=\sqrt s$, i.e. $c=r^2=s=\sqrt{s^2\cdot 1}=\sqrt{ab}$.

**The bound is attained.** Take $a=4,\ b=1,\ c=2$ (so $c=\sqrt{ab}=\sqrt4=2$):
$$A=\frac{4+1}{2}-2=\frac12,\qquad B=\frac{4+1+2}{3}-\sqrt[3]{8}=\frac73-2=\frac
13,\qquad R=\frac{1/3}{1/2}=\frac23.$$

Thus $\inf R = \dfrac{2}{3}$, so $\lambda_{\max}=\dfrac{2}{3}$.

**Verification of the inequality for $\lambda=\frac23$.** For all $a,b,c>0$,
$$\frac{a+b+c}{3}-\sqrt[3]{abc}\;\ge\;\frac{2}{3}\!\left(\frac{a+b}{2}-\sqrt{ab}
\right),$$
which is exactly $R\ge \frac23$, proven above. Equality holds when $c=\sqrt{ab}$
 (and $a\ne b$).

---

Final Answer: $\boxed{\dfrac{2}{3}}$
