## Analytic Center of a Linear Matrix Inequality

We are given an affine matrix function
$$F(x) = F_0 + x_1 F_1 + \cdots + x_n F_n, \qquad F(x): \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb

$$x^\star = \arg\min_{x:\, F(x)\succ 0}\; \phi(x) \;=\; \arg\max_{x:\, F(x)\succ

We assume the feasible set $\{x : F(x) \succ 0\}$ is nonempty and bounded (so th

The function $g(S) = -\log\det S$ is strictly convex on $\mathbb{S}_{++}^p$ (the

x) = g(F(x))$ is strictly convex on $\{x : F(x) \succ 0\}$. Therefore, **if a mi
