## Proof

Let $X = \frac{1}{T}\int_0^T W_t\,dt$. Since $W_t$ is a centered Gaussian proces
s, $\mathbb{E}[X]=0$, so

$$\operatorname{Var}(X)=\mathbb{E}[X^2]=\frac{1}{T^2}\mathbb{E}\left[\left(\int_
0^T W_t\,dt\right)^2\right].$$

Using the Fubini–Itô isometry identity (valid since the covariance is integrable
 on $[0,T]^2$):

$$\mathbb{E}\left[\left(\int_0^T W_t\,dt\right)^2\right]=\int_0^T\!\!\int_0^T \m
athbb{E}[W_s W_t]\,ds\,dt.$$

For standard Brownian motion, $\mathbb{E}[W_s W_t]=\min(s,t)$. Hence

$$\mathbb{E}\left[\left(\int_0^T W_t\,dt\right)^2\right]=\int_0^T\!\!\int_0^T \m
in(s,t)\,ds\,dt.$$

By symmetry, split the square $[0,T]^2$ along $s=t$:

$$\int_0^T\!\!\int_0^T \min(s,t)\,ds\,dt = 2\int_0^T\!\!\int_0^t s\,ds\,dt = 2\i
nt_0^T \frac{t^2}{2}\,dt = \int_0^T t^2\,dt = \frac{T^3}{3}.$$

Therefore

$$\operatorname{Var}(X)=\frac{1}{T^2}\cdot\frac{T^3}{3}=\frac{T}{3}.$$
