## Proof

Write $z_k = a_k + i b_k$ where $a_k = \operatorname{Re} z_k$ and $b_k = \operat
orname{Im} z_k$. Set
$$S = z_1^2 + z_2^2 + \cdots + z_n^2 = A + iB,$$
where
$$A = \operatorname{Re} S = \sum_{k=1}^n (a_k^2 - b_k^2), \qquad B = \operatorna
me{Im} S = 2\sum_{k=1}^n a_k b_k.$$

**Step 1: Express $|\operatorname{Re}(S^{1/2})|$ in terms of $A$ and $|S|$.**

Let $w = u + iv$ be either square root of $S$, so $w^2 = S$. Then $u^2 - v^2 = A
$ and $2uv = B$, while $u^2 + v^2 = |w|^2 = |S|$. Solving,
$$u^2 = \frac{|S| + A}{2}, \qquad v^2 = \frac{|S| - A}{2}.$$
(Note $|S| = \sqrt{A^2+B^2} \geq |A|$, so both right-hand sides are non-negative
.) The two square roots are $w$ and $-w$, whose real parts are $u$ and $-u$; hen
ce for **either** choice of square root,
$$\left|\operatorname{Re}\left(S^{1/2}\right)\right| = |u| = \sqrt{\frac{|S|+A}{
2}}.$$

**Step 2: Bound $|S| + A$ from above.**

By the triangle inequality and $|z_k^2| = |z_k|^2 = a_k^2 + b_k^2$,
$$|S| = \left|\sum_{k=1}^n z_k^2\right| \leq \sum_{k=1}^n |z_k|^2 = \sum_{k=1}^n
 (a_k^2 + b_k^2).$$
Therefore
$$|S| + A \leq \sum_{k=1}^n (a_k^2 + b_k^2) + \sum_{k=1}^n (a_k^2 - b_k^2) = 2\s
um_{k=1}^n a_k^2.$$

**Step 3: Conclude.**

Since all cross terms $|a_j|\cdot|a_k|$ are non-negative,
$$\sum_{k=1}^n a_k^2 \leq \left(\sum_{k=1}^n |a_k|\right)^2.$$
Combining Steps 1 and 2,
$$\left|\operatorname{Re}\left(S^{1/2}\right)\right|^2 = \frac{|S|+A}{2} \leq \s
um_{k=1}^n a_k^2 \leq \left(\sum_{k=1}^n |a_k|\right)^2.$$
Taking square roots (both sides non-negative),
$$\left|\operatorname{Re}\left[(z_1^2+z_2^2+\cdots+z_n^2)^{1/2}\right]\right| \l
eq \sum_{k=1}^n |a_k| = \sum_{k=1}^n |\operatorname{Re} z_k|. \qquad \blacksquar
e$$
