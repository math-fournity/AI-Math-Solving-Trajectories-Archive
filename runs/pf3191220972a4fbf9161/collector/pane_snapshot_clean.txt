Looking at this problem, I need to find how many integers $c \in [-2007, 2007]$
allow some integer $x$ with $x^2 + c \equiv 0 \pmod{2^{2007}}$, i.e., $x^2 \equi
v -c \pmod{2^{2007}}$.

## Quadratic Residue Criterion mod $2^n$

**Lemma.** For $n \geq 3$, a non-negative integer $a$ is a quadratic residue mod
 $2^n$ if and only if either $a \equiv 0 \pmod{2^n}$, or $a = 2^{2k} \cdot m$ wi
th $m$ odd, $2k < n$, and $m \equiv 1 \pmod{8}$.

*Proof sketch:* The odd QRs mod $2^n$ ($n \geq 3$) are exactly those $\equiv 1 \
pmod 8$ (classical result). For $a = 2^{2k} m$ with $m$ odd and $n - 2k \geq 3$,
 writing $x = 2^k y$ reduces to $y^2 \equiv m \pmod{2^{n-2k}}$, which has a solu
tion iff $m \equiv 1 \pmod 8$.

## Applying to the Problem

Since $|c| \leq 2007 \ll 2^{2007}$, I split into three cases.

**Case $c = 0$:** $-c = 0$ is a QR. Count: **1**.

**Case $c < 0$** (so $a = -c \in [1, 2007]$, a small positive number): Since $v_
2(a) \leq 10 \ll 2007$, the criterion gives: $a$ is a QR iff $v_2(a)$ is even an
d the odd part of $a$ is $\equiv 1 \pmod{8}$.

**Case $c > 0$** (so $-c \equiv 2^{2007} - c \pmod{2^{2007}}$): Write $c = 2^s t
$ with $t$ odd, $s \leq 10$. Then $2^{2007} - c = 2^s(2^{2007-s} - t)$, where $2
^{2007-s} - t$ is odd (since $2007 - s \geq 1997 \geq 3$, so $2^{2007-s}$ is eve
n). The odd part is $2^{2007-s} - t \equiv -t \pmod{8}$ (since $2007 - s \geq 3$
). So $-c$ is a QR iff $s$ is even and $-t \equiv 1 \pmod{8}$, i.e., $t \equiv 7
 \pmod{8}$.

## Counting

For $n \in [1, 2007]$, write $n = 2^{2k} \cdot m$ with $m$ odd. The possible eve
n valuations are $2k \in \{0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10\}$ (since $2^{12} = 4096 > 2007$).

| $2k$ | Range of odd $m$ | $m \equiv 1 \pmod{8}$ | $m \equiv 7 \pmod{8}$ |
|-----|------------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| 0   | $[1, 2007]$      | 251                  | 251                  |
| 2   | $[1, 501]$       | 63                   | 62                   |
| 4   | $[1, 125]$       | 16                   | 15                   |
| 6   | $[1, 31]$        | 4                    | 4                    |
| 8   | $[1, 7]$         | 1                    | 1                    |
| 10  | $[1, 1]$         | 1                    | 0                    |

**Verification of counts** (sample): For $2k=0$, $m \equiv 1$: $m = 1, 9, \ldots
, 2001 = 1 + 8 \cdot 250$, giving 251 values. For $2k=2$, $m \equiv 7$: $m = 7,
15, \ldots, 495 = 7 + 8 \cdot 61$, giving 62 values (next would be 503 > 501). A
ll entries checked similarly.

**Totals:**
- Odd part $\equiv 1 \pmod{8}$ (gives valid $c < 0$): $251 + 63 + 16 + 4 + 1 + 1
 = \mathbf{336}$
- Odd part $\equiv 7 \pmod{8}$ (gives valid $c > 0$): $251 + 62 + 15 + 4 + 1 + 0
 = \mathbf{333}$

## Verification with Small Case

For $n = 5$ (mod 32), range $c \in [-7, 7]$: QRs mod 32 are $\{0, 1, 4, 9, 16, 1
7, 25\}$. Direct checking yields valid $c = \{-4, -1, 0, 7\}$, count = 4. Formul
a gives $1 + 2 + 1 = 4$. ✓

## Final Answer

$$\text{Total} = 1 + 336 + 333 = \boxed{670}$$
