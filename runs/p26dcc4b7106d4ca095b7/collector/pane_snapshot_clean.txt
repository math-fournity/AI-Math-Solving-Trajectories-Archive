## Solution

**Setup.** Let the frustum $K$ have base radius $R$ (at $z=0$) and ceiling radiu
s $r$ (at $z=h$), with $R>r$. The base area is $\pi R^2$ and the ceiling area is
 $\pi r^2$. The condition gives
$$\pi R^2 = 4\pi r^2 \implies R = 2r.$$

**Locating the sphere.** Since the sphere $B$ is tangent to both the base plate
and the ceiling plate, its center lies on the symmetry axis at height $h/2$, and
 its radius is
$$a = \frac{h}{2}.$$

**Tangency to the lateral wall.** In an axial cross-section, the slanted wall is
 the line through $(R,0)$ and $(r,h)$. Its equation is
$$h\rho - hR + (R-r)z = 0.$$

The perpendicular distance from the center $(0,\, h/2)$ to this line is
$$d = \frac{\left|-hR + (R-r)\tfrac{h}{2}\right|}{\sqrt{h^2+(R-r)^2}} = \frac{h(
R+r)/2}{\sqrt{h^2+(R-r)^2}}.$$

Tangency requires $d = a = h/2$, so
$$\frac{R+r}{\sqrt{h^2+(R-r)^2}} = 1 \implies (R+r)^2 = h^2 + (R-r)^2.$$

Hence
$$h^2 = (R+r)^2 - (R-r)^2 = 4Rr \implies h = 2\sqrt{Rr}.$$

**Substituting $R=2r$:**
$$h = 2\sqrt{2r^2} = 2r\sqrt{2}, \qquad a = \frac{h}{2} = r\sqrt{2}.$$

**Volumes.**

Sphere:
$$V_B = \tfrac{4}{3}\pi a^3 = \tfrac{4}{3}\pi (r\sqrt{2})^3 = \tfrac{4}{3}\pi \c
dot 2\sqrt{2}\, r^3 = \tfrac{8\sqrt{2}}{3}\pi r^3.$$

Frustum:
$$V_K = \tfrac{1}{3}\pi h\,(R^2+Rr+r^2) = \tfrac{1}{3}\pi\cdot 2r\sqrt{2}\cdot\b
igl(4r^2+2r^2+r^2\bigr) = \tfrac{1}{3}\pi\cdot 2r\sqrt{2}\cdot 7r^2 = \tfrac{14\
sqrt{2}}{3}\pi r^3.$$

**Ratio:**
$$\frac{V_B}{V_K} = \frac{\tfrac{8\sqrt{2}}{3}\pi r^3}{\tfrac{14\sqrt{2}}{3}\pi
r^3} = \frac{8}{14} = \boxed{\dfrac{4}{7}}.$$
