## Setup

Columns 1–8 (left to right), rows 1–8 (bottom to top). Dark bishop starts at col
umn 1, light bishop at column 8. Each moves up one row per step, going diagonall
y left or right with equal probability. At edges (columns 1 or 8), only one diag
onal direction is available, so the move is forced. After 7 steps, both reach ro
w 8.

## Folding Technique

A reflecting random walk on {1,…,8} equals an unrestricted walk on ℤ **folded**
into {1,…,8} with period 14 (= 2×7). Define:

$$\text{fold}(x): \text{let } r = x \bmod 14 \in \{0,\ldots,13\}; \quad \text{fo
ld}(x) = \begin{cases} r & \text{if } r \le 7 \\ 14 - r & \text{if } r > 7 \end{
cases}$$

Board position = 1 + fold(unrestricted position). If the unrestricted walk start
s at 0, after 7 steps the position is $S_7 = \varepsilon_1 + \cdots + \varepsilo
n_7$, each $\varepsilon_i = \pm 1$ with probability 1/2.

## Dark Bishop Distribution

| $S_7$ | fold | $D$ | Probability |

## Light Bishop Distribution

| $T_7$ | $7+T_7$ | fold | $L$ | Probability |

## Key Observation

$D \in \{2,4,6,8\}$ (even) and $L \in \{1,3,5,7\}$ (odd), so $D \neq L$ always —
 the bishops remain on opposite-colored squares. Thus $P(D > L) + P(D < L) = 1$.
