## Solution

We solve the PDE $xz_x - yz_y = z$ by the method of characteristics.

**Characteristic equations.** For the PDE $a\,z_x + b\,z_y = c$ with $a = x$, $b
 = -y$, $c = z$, the characteristic system is

$$\frac{dx}{ds} = x, \qquad \frac{dy}{ds} = -y, \qquad \frac{dz}{ds} = z.$$

**Integrating the first two.** From $\frac{dx}{ds} = x$ we get $x = x_0\, e^s$.
From $\frac{dy}{ds} = -y$ we get $y = y_0\, e^{-s}$. Multiplying,

$$xy = x_0 y_0 = \text{const along each characteristic}.$$

**Integrating the third.** From $\frac{dz}{ds} = z$ we get $z = z_0\, e^s$.

**Applying the initial condition.** The data is prescribed on the curve $y = 1$,
 $z = 3x$. Parameterize this curve by $\xi$:

$$x_0 = \xi, \qquad y_0 = 1, \qquad z_0 = 3\xi, \qquad s = 0.$$

Along the characteristic emanating from the point $(\xi, 1, 3\xi)$:

$$x = \xi\, e^s, \qquad y = e^{-s}, \qquad z = 3\xi\, e^s.$$

**Eliminating the parameters.** Since $x = \xi e^s$ and $z = 3\xi e^s$, dividing
 gives

$$z = 3x.$$

The parameter $\xi$ and $s$ have both dropped out, so this relation holds everyw
here the characteristics reach (which covers the whole plane since $x = \xi e^s$
, $y = e^{-s}$ with $\xi \in \mathbb{R}$, $s \in \mathbb{R}$ gives arbitrary $(x
,y)$ with $y \neq 0$, and by continuity also $y = 0$).

**Verification.** With $z = 3x$:
- $z_x = 3$, $z_y = 0$,
- $xz_x - yz_y = x \cdot 3 - y \cdot 0 = 3x = z$. ✓
- At $y = 1$: $z = 3x$. ✓

Both the PDE and the initial condition are satisfied.

$$\boxed{z = 3x}$$
