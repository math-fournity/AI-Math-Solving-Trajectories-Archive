**Construction.** For each integer $N \ge 3$, define a directed graph $G_N$ on v
ertices $v_0, v_1, \dots, v_{N-1}$ with the following edges:

- $v_0 \to v_1$ (the only outgoing edge of $v_0$);
- for every $1 \le i \le N-2$: two outgoing edges $v_i \to v_{i+1}$ and $v_i \to
 v_0$;
- $v_{N-1} \to v_0$ (the only outgoing edge of $v_{N-1}$).

The random walk picks uniformly at random among the outgoing edges of the curren
t vertex.

**Strong connectivity.** From any vertex $v_i$ we can reach $v_0$: if $1 \le i \
le N-2$ use the edge $v_i \to v_0$ directly; if $i = N-1$ use $v_{N-1} \to v_0$.
 From $v_0$ we reach any $v_j$ by following the forward chain $v_0 \to v_1 \to \
cdots \to v_j$. Hence $G_N$ is strongly connected.

**Cover-time lower bound.** The vertex $v_{N-1}$ can be reached only by traversi
ng the entire forward chain
$$v_0 \to v_1 \to v_2 \to \cdots \to v_{N-1},$$
choosing the "forward" edge $v_i \to v_{i+1}$ at every internal vertex $v_1, v_2
, \dots, v_{N-2}$.

Call an *attempt* the segment of the walk that starts when the walk leaves $v_0$
 (deterministically, to $v_1$) and ends when it either reaches $v_{N-1}$ (succes
s) or returns to $v_0$ (failure, caused by choosing some edge $v_i \to v_0$ with
 $1 \le i \le N-2$). During a single attempt, success occurs iff the walk choose
s the forward edge at each of $v_1, v_2, \dots, v_{N-2}$, which happens with pro
bability
$$p = \left(\tfrac{1}{2}\right)^{N-2}.$$
Attempts are independent (each restarts from $v_0 \to v_1$), so the number $K$ o
f failed attempts before the first success is geometric with
$$\mathbb{E}] = \frac{1-p}{p} \ge 2^{N-2} - 1.$$
Every failed attempt contributes at least one step (it leaves $v_0$, reaches $v_
1$, and eventually returns to $v_0$), so if $\tau$ denotes the first time $v_{N-
1}$ is visited,
$$\mathbb{E}[\tau] \;\ge\; \mathbb{E}] \;\ge\; 2^{N-2} - 1.$$

Since $v_{N-1}$ must be visited for the walk to cover all vertices, the cover ti
me $C(G_N)$ (expected time to visit every vertex, maximized over the starting ve
rtex, and in particular starting from $v_0$) satisfies
$$C(G_N) \;\ge\; \mathbb{E}_{v_0}[\tau] \;\ge\; 2^{N-2} - 1.$$

**Superpolynomiality.** $2^{N-2}-1$ grows exponentially in $N$, so for every fix
ed polynomial $P(N)$ and all sufficiently large $N$,
$$C(G_N) \ge 2^{N-2} - 1 > P(N).$$
Thus $C(G_N)$ is not bounded above by any polynomial function of $N$, the number
 of vertices.

This gives a strongly connected directed graph with superpolynomial (indeed, exp
onential) cover time. $\blacksquare$
