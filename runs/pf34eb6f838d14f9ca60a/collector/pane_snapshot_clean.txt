## Weak Formulation

Let $V = \{v \in H^1(\Omega) : v|_{\Gamma_D}=0\}$. Multiply the PDE by $v\in V$
and integrate. Using the Neumann condition $-\gamma\cdot\mathbf n + \mu\nabla u\
cdot\mathbf n = 0$ on $\Gamma_N$ (so $\gamma\cdot\mathbf n = \mu\nabla u\cdot\ma
thbf n$ on $\Gamma_N$) and integrating the divergence terms by parts:

$$\int_\Omega \mu\nabla u\cdot\nabla v\,dx \;-\;\int_\Omega (\beta u)\cdot\nabla
 v\,dx \;+\;\int_\Omega \sigma u v\,dx \;=\;\int_\Omega f v\,dx,\qquad\forall v\
in V.$$

(The boundary term from $\operatorname{div}(\beta u)$ on $\Gamma_N$ vanishes bec
ause $\int_{\Gamma_N}(\beta u\cdot\mathbf n)v\,ds$ — note the Neumann data coupl
es $\gamma$ to $\mu\nabla u\cdot\mathbf n$, and the natural boundary condition i
s built into the weak form; with $\gamma=\beta u$ interpreted as the convective
flux, the boundary integral cancels against the diffusion flux term, leaving the
 above.) Define

$$a(u,v)=\int_\Omega\bigl[\mu\nabla u\cdot\nabla v - (\beta u)\cdot\nabla v + \s
igma u v\bigr]dx,\qquad \ell(v)=\int_\Omega f v\,dx.$$

Under coercivity (guaranteed e.g. when $\sigma-\tfrac12\operatorname{div}\beta\g
e\sigma_0>0$ and $\mu\ge\mu_0>0$, via the standard symmetrization $-\int(\beta u
)\cdot\nabla v = \tfrac12\int[-(\beta u)\cdot\nabla v+(\operatorname{div}\beta)u
v]$ on the diagonal $u=v$), Lax–Milgram gives existence/uniqueness.

## Galerkin–Linear FEM

Let $\{\mathcal T_h\}$ be a shape-regular family of triangulations of $\Omega$,
$h=\max_K \operatorname{diam}K$. The linear FE space is

$$V_h=\{v_h\in V : v_h|_K\in\mathbb P_1(K),\;\forall K\in\mathcal T_h\}\subset V
.$$

Galerkin: find $u_h\in V_h$ with

$$a(u_h,v_h)=\ell(v_h),\qquad\forall v_h\in V_h.$$

Writing $u_h=\sum_j U_j\varphi_j$ over the interior nodes (Dirichlet nodes elimi
nated) gives the linear system $A\mathbf U=\mathbf F$ with

$$A_{ij}=\int_\Omega\bigl[\mu\nabla\varphi_j\cdot\nabla\varphi_i-(\beta\varphi_j
)\cdot\nabla\varphi_i+\sigma\varphi_j\varphi_i\bigr]dx,\qquad F_i=\int_\Omega f\
varphi_i\,dx.$$

On each element $K$, element matrices assemble the diffusion $A^K_{ij}=\int_K\mu
\nabla\varphi_j\cdot\nabla\varphi_i$, convection $C^K_{ij}=-\int_K(\beta\varphi_
j)\cdot\nabla\varphi_i$, and reaction $R^K_{ij}=\int_K\sigma\varphi_j\varphi_i$
contributions.

## When the Method Is Inaccurate

The accuracy of the Galerkin method is governed by the **local Péclet number**

$$\mathrm{Pe}_K=\frac{\|\beta\|_{L^\infty(K)}\,h_K}{2\,\mu_K},\qquad \mu_K=\mu|_
K,$$

and by the **reaction–diffusion balance** $\sigma_K h_K^2/\mu_K$.

### Case 1 — Convection-dominated regime: $\mathrm{Pe}_K\gg 1$ (i.e. $\|\beta\|h
\gg\mu$)

When the convective term dominates the diffusive term, the continuous problem is
 **non-selfadjoint and close to a first-order hyperbolic transport**. The Galerk
in method with piecewise linears produces the discrete maximum principle only wh
en $\mathrm{Pe}_K\le 1$; for $\mathrm{Pe}_K>1$ the discrete system loses its M-m
atrix property and the numerical solution exhibits **unphysical oscillations (wi
ggles)** localized in a narrow region of width $O(\mu/\|\beta\|)$ around layers,
 and these oscillations propagate globally rather than being damped. The standar
d error estimate

$$\|u-u_h\|_{H^1}\le C\frac{h}{\mu}\|u\|_{H^2}$$

blows up as $\mu\to 0$ (or $\|\beta\|\to\infty$), and the $L^2$ estimate degrade
s similarly; the constant $C$ scales with $\mathrm{Pe}_K$. So the method is **in
accurate whenever**

$$\boxed{\|\beta\|_{L^\infty(\Omega)}\,h \;>\; 2\,\mu_{\min}}\qquad\text{(i.e. l
ocal Péclet number } >1\text{)}.$$

This is the **advection-dominated / singularly perturbed** regime. The boundary/
internal layers have thickness $O(\mu/\|\beta\|)\ll h$ and cannot be resolved by
 $V_h$.

### Case 2 — Reaction-dominated regime: $\sigma h^2/\mu$ large with rough $\sigm
a$, or $\sigma\gg\mu/h^2$

When $\sigma$ is very large relative to the diffusive scale, the solution develo
ps **exponential boundary layers** of thickness $O(\sqrt{\mu/\sigma})$. If $h\gg
\sqrt{\mu/\sigma}$ the layer is unresolved and Galerkin again produces oscillati
ons / overshoot near $\Gamma_D$. The inaccuracy condition is

$$\boxed{\sigma_{\max}\,h^2 \;>\; C\,\mu_{\min}}.$$

### Case 3 — Non-divergence-free / sharply varying $\beta$ with $\operatorname{d
iv}\beta\ne 0$

If $\beta$ has large divergence or jumps, the skew-symmetric structure is pertur
bed and the coercivity margin $\sigma-\tfrac12\operatorname{div}\beta$ may becom
e negative locally, breaking the stability estimate; Galerkin then gives spuriou
s mass creation and oscillations even at moderate Péclet number.

## Remedies (by case)

### Remedy for Case 1 (convection-dominated): upwinding / SUPG

**(a) Artificial diffusion (upwind).** Add an element-wise isotropic artificial
diffusion $\mu_h^{\rm art}=\tfrac12\|\beta\|_K h_K\,\psi(\mathrm{Pe}_K)$ with $\
psi$ a limiter (e.g. $\psi(z)=\coth z-1/z$, the **Kružkov–Boris–Book / Il'in** e
xponential fitting). This restores the M-matrix / discrete maximum principle at
the cost of $O(h)$ smearing of layers. Simple but only first-order accurate.

**(b) SUPG / Streamline-Upwind Petrov–Galerkin** (the standard remedy). Replace
the test function by

$$\tilde v_h = v_h + \tau_K\,\beta\cdot\nabla v_h\quad\text{on each }K,$$

with the stabilizing parameter

$$\tau_K=\frac{h_K}{2\|\beta\|_K}\,\xi(\mathrm{Pe}_K),\qquad \xi(z)=\frac{z}{\sq
rt{1+z^2}}\;\;\text{or}\;\;\xi(z)=\coth z-\frac1z.$$

The stabilized weak form is: find $u_h\in V_h$ such that

$$a(u_h,v_h)+\sum_{K}\tau_K\int_K\bigl(-\operatorname{div}(\mu\nabla u_h)+\opera
torname{div}(\beta u_h)+\sigma u_h-f\bigr)\,(\beta\cdot\nabla v_h)\,dx=\ell(v_h)
.$$

SUPG is **consistent** (the residual term vanishes for the exact solution), give
s optimal $O(h^{k+1/2})$ error in the advection-dominated norm, and suppresses o
scillations while keeping layers sharp along the streamline direction. It is the
 recommended remedy.

**(c) Galerkin least squares (GLS) / DWG.** Add residual least-squares terms; eq
uivalent to SUPG plus crosswind diffusion; useful when $\mu$ is also small.

**(d) Anisotropic / Shishkin meshes.** Resolve the layer by grading the mesh wit
h $h\sim\mu/\|\beta\|$ in the layer direction; combined with SUPG gives robust e
xponential convergence.

### Remedy for Case 2 (reaction-dominated)

- **Mesh refinement** so that $h\lesssim\sqrt{\mu/\sigma}$ near boundary layers.
- **Reaction-aligned stabilization**: add a residual-based term $\sum_K\tau_K^R\
int_K\mathcal L u_h\,(\sigma v_h)\,dx$ or use **GLS** which automatically handle
s the reaction contribution.
- **Exponential fitting** (Scharfetter–Gummel type) for 1D / structured meshes.

### Remedy for Case 3 (non-divergence-free / rough $\beta$)

- **Symmetrize the convective term**: rewrite $-\operatorname{div}(\beta u) = -\
beta\cdot\nabla u - (\operatorname{div}\beta)u$ and split the form into the skew
-symmetric part $\tfrac12[-(\beta u)\cdot\nabla v+(\beta v)\cdot\nabla u]$ plus
the conservative correction $\tfrac12\int(\operatorname{div}\beta)uv$. This guar
antees energy stability regardless of $\operatorname{div}\beta$.
- Require $\sigma-\tfrac12\operatorname{div}\beta\ge\sigma_*>0$ (coercivity hypo
thesis); if not satisfied, augment $\sigma$ or use **continuation / artificial r
eaction** stabilization.
- For discontinuous $\beta$: use **discontinuous Galerkin (DG)** with upwind num
erical fluxes, which handles rough coefficients robustly.

## Summary of Hypotheses Causing Inaccuracy

| Regime | Condition | Symptom | Remedy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Convection-dominated | $\|\beta\|h/(2\mu)>1$ | oscillations, lost DMP | SUPG /
 upwind / Shishkin mesh |
| Reaction-dominated | $\sigma h^2/\mu\gg 1$ | unresolved exponential layers | m
esh refinement + GLS / reaction stabilization |
| Non-divergence-free / rough $\beta$ | $\sigma-\tfrac12\operatorname{div}\beta\
le 0$ or $\beta$ discontinuous | lost coercivity, spurious mass | skew-symmetriz
ation, DG, augmented $\sigma$ |

The fundamental inaccuracy hypothesis is therefore: **the Galerkin linear FE met
hod is inaccurate when the problem is singularly perturbed, i.e. when the local
Péclet number $\mathrm{Pe}_K=\|\beta\|_K h_K/(2\mu_K)$ exceeds unity (convection
-dominated) or when $\sigma_K h_K^2/\mu_K$ is large (reaction-dominated), or whe
n the convective field $\beta$ is not sufficiently regular/divergence-free to pr
eserve coercivity.** The canonical remedy is the **SUPG stabilized formulation**
 (with appropriate $\tau_K$), supplemented by layer-resolving (Shishkin/Bakhvalo
v) meshes and, for rough $\beta$, discontinuous Galerkin with upwind fluxes.
