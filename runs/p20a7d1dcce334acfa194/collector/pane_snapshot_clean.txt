## Proof

We determine the number of distinct prime ideals in the quotient ring $R = \math
bb{Q}[x]/\langle x^m - 1\rangle$.

**Step 1: Reduction to irreducible factors.**

Since $\mathbb{Q}[x]$ is a principal ideal domain (in fact a Euclidean domain),
every ideal is principal. The prime ideals of $\mathbb{Q}[x]$ are:
- the zero ideal $(0)$, and
- the ideals $(f)$ where $f \in \mathbb{Q}[x]$ is irreducible.

By the lattice (correspondence) theorem for quotient rings, the prime ideals of
$R = \mathbb{Q}[x]/\langle x^m-1\rangle$ are in bijection with the prime ideals
$P$ of $\mathbb{Q}[x]$ satisfying $\langle x^m - 1\rangle \subseteq P$.

The zero ideal does not contain $\langle x^m-1\rangle$ (since $x^m-1 \neq 0$). S
o we need the irreducible polynomials $f$ with $\langle x^m-1\rangle \subseteq (
f)$, i.e., $f \mid (x^m - 1)$.

Thus the prime ideals of $R$ are exactly the ideals $(\bar{f}) = (f)/\langle x^m
-1\rangle$ where $f$ ranges over the **distinct irreducible factors** of $x^m -
1$ over $\mathbb{Q}$. Hence

$$\#\{\text{prime ideals of } R\} \;=\; \#\{\text{distinct irreducible factors o
f } x^m-1 \text{ over } \mathbb{Q}\}.$$

**Step 2: Factorization of $x^m - 1$ via cyclotomic polynomials.**

The classical factorization over $\mathbb{Q}$ is

$$x^m - 1 \;=\; \prod_{d \mid m} \Phi_d(x),$$

where $\Phi_d(x)$ is the $d$-th cyclotomic polynomial. Each $\Phi_d(x)$ is **irr
educible over $\mathbb{Q}$** (a standard theorem: $\Phi_d$ is the minimal polyno
mial over $\mathbb{Q}$ of any primitive $d$-th root of unity, hence irreducible)
. The $\Phi_d$ for distinct $d$ are distinct monic polynomials.

Therefore the distinct irreducible factors of $x^m - 1$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ are pr
ecisely

$$\{\,\Phi_d(x) : d \mid m,\ d \geq 1\,\}.$$

**Step 3: Count.**

The number of such factors equals the number of positive divisors of $m$:

$$\#\{\text{prime ideals of } \mathbb{Q}[x]/\langle x^m-1\rangle\} \;=\; \#\{d \
in \mathbb{Z}_{>0} : d \mid m\} \;=\; \tau(m),$$

where $\tau(m)$ denotes the number-of-divisors function.

**Conclusion.** The number of distinct prime ideals in $\mathbb{Q}[x]/\langle x^
m-1\rangle$ is

$$\boxed{\tau(m)},$$

the number of positive divisors of $m$ (equivalently, the number of cyclotomic f
actors $\Phi_d$ with $d\mid m$).
