## Proof

Let $A_n = \tan^{-1}\!\left(\dfrac{1}{P_{2n}}\right)$ for $n \ge 1$.

**Key identity.** I claim
$$\tan^{-1}\!\frac{1}{P_{2n}} - \tan^{-1}\!\frac{1}{P_{2n+2}} = \tan^{-1}\!\frac
{2}{P_{2n+1}}, \qquad n\ge 1.$$

Using the subtraction formula $\tan(\alpha-\beta)=\dfrac{\tan\alpha-\tan\beta}{1
+\tan\alpha\tan\beta}$ with $\alpha=\tan^{-1}(1/P_{2n})$, $\beta=\tan^{-1}(1/P_{
2n+2})$:

$$\tan(\alpha-\beta)=\frac{\dfrac{1}{P_{2n}}-\dfrac{1}{P_{2n+2}}}{1+\dfrac{1}{P_
{2n}P_{2n+2}}}=\frac{P_{2n+2}-P_{2n}}{P_{2n}P_{2n+2}+1}.$$

From the recurrence, $P_{2n+2}=2P_{2n+1}+P_{2n}$, so $P_{2n+2}-P_{2n}=2P_{2n+1}$
. Hence the numerator gives $2P_{2n+1}$.

For the denominator we use the Cassini identity for Pell numbers,
$$P_{k+1}P_{k-1}-P_k^{2}=(-1)^{k},$$
which is verified by induction on $k$ (base cases $P_2P_0-P_1^2=-1=(-1)^1$, $P_3
P_1-P_2^2=1=(-1)^2$, and the induction step follows from the linear recurrence).
 Setting $k=2n+1$:
$$P_{2n+2}P_{2n}-P_{2n+1}^{2}=(-1)^{2n+1}=-1 \;\;\Longrightarrow\;\; P_{2n}P_{2n
+2}+1=P_{2n+1}^{2}.$$

Therefore
$$\tan(\alpha-\beta)=\frac{2P_{2n+1}}{P_{2n+1}^{2}}=\frac{2}{P_{2n+1}}.$$

Since both sides are positive and $0<\alpha-\beta<\pi/2$, the identity follows:
$$A_n - A_{n+1} = \tan^{-1}\!\frac{2}{P_{2n+1}}.$$

Thus the series telescopes:
$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(A_n^{2}-A_{n+1}^{2})=A_1^{2}-\lim_{n\to\infty}A_{n+1}^{2}.
$$

Since $P_{2n}\to\infty$, we have $A_{n+1}=\tan^{-1}(1/P_{2n+2})\to 0$. Therefore
