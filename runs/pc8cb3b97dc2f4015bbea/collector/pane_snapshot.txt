### Construction

polynomial**

$$B_n(f)(t) \;=\; \sum_{k=0}^{n} f\!\left(\frac{k}{n}\right)\binom{n}{k}\, t^k(1
-t)^{n-k}, \qquad t \in [0,1].$$

Each summand is the product of a fixed vector $f(k/n) \in E$ with a scalar polyn
omial $\binom{n}{k}t^k(1-t)^{n-k}$. Hence $B_n(f)$ is a finite $E$-valued linear

a function is infinitely differentiable (each term $t^k(1-t)^{n-k}\, v$ is $C^\i

$$B_n(f) \in C^\infty([0,1], E) \subset C^1([0,1], E).$$

### Conclusion

For every $f \in C([0,1], E)$ and every $\varepsilon > 0$, we have produced $B_n
