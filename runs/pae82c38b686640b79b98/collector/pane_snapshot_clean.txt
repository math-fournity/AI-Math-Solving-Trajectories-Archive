Looking at this problem, I need to find the shortest surface path from G (centro
id of △ABE) to D' on the combined solid of a square pyramid on top of a cube.

## Setup

Place coordinates with base square ABCD at z=0:
- A=(0,0,0), B=(6,0,0), C=(6,6,0), D=(0,6,0)
- Pyramid apex E=(3,3,3√2) (height 3√2 since lateral edges = 6)
- Cube below: A'=(0,0,−6), B'=(6,0,−6), C'=(6,6,−6), D'=(0,6,−6)

All pyramid faces are **equilateral triangles** with side 6 (each has two latera
l edges + one base edge, all length 6). All cube faces are squares with side 6.

**G** = centroid of △ABE = (A+B+E)/3 = (3, 1, √2).

## Finding the Shortest Path by Unfolding

I systematically unfold sequences of faces and compute straight-line distances f
rom G to D'.

**Key path: ABE → DAE → DAA'D'** (crossing edge AE, then edge AD)

**Unfolding face ABE** (equilateral, side 6):
- A=(0,0), B=(6,0), E=(3, 3√3)
- G = (3, √3)

**Unfolding face DAE** across AE (equilateral, reflected B to get D):
- D = (−3, 3√3)

**Unfolding face DAA'D'** across AD (square, side 6, away from E):
- Direction A→D is (−1, √3); perpendicular away from E is (−√3/2, −1/2)
- A' = (0,0) + 6(−√3/2, −1/2) = (−3√3, −3)
- D' = (−3, 3√3) + 6(−√3/2, −1/2) = (−3−3√3, 3√3−3)

## Computing the Distance

G = (3, √3), D' = (−3−3√3, 3√3−3)

$$\Delta x = -6 - 3\sqrt{3}, \quad \Delta y = 2\sqrt{3} - 3$$

$$d^2 = (6+3\sqrt{3})^2 + (2\sqrt{3}-3)^2$$
$$= (36 + 36\sqrt{3} + 27) + (12 - 12\sqrt{3} + 9)$$
$$= 63 + 36\sqrt{3} + 21 - 12\sqrt{3}$$
$$= 84 + 24\sqrt{3}$$

## Path Validity Check

The straight line from G to D' must cross edges AE and AD within the segments:
- **Crossing AE** (line y = √3·x): parameter t = (4−√3)/13 ≈ 0.175 ∈ [0,1], inte
rsection point at s ≈ 0.35 on segment AE ✓
- **Crossing AD** (line y = −√3·x): parameter t = (√3−1)/2 ≈ 0.366 ∈ [0,1], inte
rsection point at s ≈ 0.37 on segment AD ✓

The path enters ABE, crosses AE into DAE, crosses AD into DAA'D', and reaches D'
. ✓
