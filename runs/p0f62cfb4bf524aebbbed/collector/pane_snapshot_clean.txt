## Proof

We need to find all natural numbers $n$ such that $5^n - 1$ is divisible by **ev
ery** prime greater than $3$.

The set of primes greater than $3$ is $\{5, 7, 11, 13, 17, \ldots\}$. In particu
lar, $5$ itself is a prime greater than $3$.

**Checking divisibility by $5$:** For any natural number $n \geq 1$,
$$5^n \equiv 0 \pmod{5},$$
so
$$5^n - 1 \equiv 0 - 1 \equiv -1 \equiv 4 \pmod{5}.$$

Therefore $5 \nmid (5^n - 1)$ for every natural number $n$.

Since $5$ is a prime greater than $3$, the condition "$5^n - 1$ is divisible by
every prime greater than $3$" already fails at the prime $5$, for **every** natu
ral number $n$.

Hence no natural number $n$ satisfies the condition, and the number of such natu
ral numbers is:

$$\boxed{0}$$
