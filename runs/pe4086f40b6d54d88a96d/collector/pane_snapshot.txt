**Claim:** The equality holds.

**Proof.**

Set $f_m := |u_n - u_m|$ for $m \geq n$. This is a countable family $\{f_m\}_{m
\geq n}$ of measurable functions (each in $L^\infty(E)$ since $u_n, u_m \in L^\i
nfty(E)$). We prove the general identity:

$$\esssup_E \sup_{m \geq n} f_m \;=\; \sup_{m \geq n}\, \esssup_E f_m.$$

**Direction 1 ($\sup_m \esssup f_m \leq \esssup \sup_m f_m$).**

For each fixed $m$, we have $f_m \leq \sup_{k \geq n} f_k$ pointwise on $E$, hen
ce $\esssup_E f_m \leq \esssup_E \sup_{k \geq n} f_k$. Taking the supremum over
$m \geq n$:

$$\sup_{m \geq n}\, \esssup_E f_m \;\leq\; \esssup_E \sup_{k \geq n} f_k.$$

**Direction 2 ($\esssup \sup_m f_m \leq \sup_m \esssup f_m$).**

Let $M := \sup_{m \geq n}\, \esssup_E f_m$ (an extended real number). For each $
m \geq n$, since $\esssup_E f_m \leq M$, there exists a null set $N_m \subset E$

$$f_m(x) \leq M \quad \text{for all } x \in E \setminus N_m.$$

(This is by definition of $\esssup$: $f_m \leq \esssup f_m$ a.e., and $\esssup f
_m \leq M$.)

Set $N := \bigcup_{m \geq n} N_m$. Since $\{m \geq n\}$ is countable, $N$ is a n
ull set. For every $x \in E \setminus N$ and every $m \geq n$, we have $f_m(x) \
leq M$, so

$$\sup_{m \geq n} f_m(x) \;\leq\; M \quad \text{for all } x \in E \setminus N.$$

Therefore $\esssup_E \sup_{m \geq n} f_m \leq M = \sup_{m \geq n}\, \esssup_E f_
m$.

**Combining both directions** yields the desired equality:

$$\sup_{m \geq n}\, \esssup_E |u_n - u_m| \;=\; \esssup_E\, \sup_{m \geq n} |u_n
 - u_m|.$$
