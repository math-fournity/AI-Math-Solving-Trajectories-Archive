## Proof

We want to evaluate

$$E = 448 \cdot \frac{\sin 12° \cdot \sin 39° \cdot \sin 51°}{\sin 24°}.$$

**Step 1: Use complementary angles.**

Since $\sin 51° = \sin(90° - 39°) = \cos 39°$, we pair $\sin 39°$ with $\sin 51°
$:

$$\sin 39° \cdot \sin 51° = \sin 39° \cdot \cos 39° = \tfrac{1}{2}\sin 78°,$$

using the double-angle identity $\sin 2\theta = 2\sin\theta\cos\theta$.

So
$$E = 448 \cdot \frac{\sin 12° \cdot \tfrac{1}{2}\sin 78°}{\sin 24°} = 224 \cdot
 \frac{\sin 12° \cdot \sin 78°}{\sin 24°}.$$

**Step 2: Simplify $\sin 12°/\sin 24°$.**

Using $\sin 24° = 2\sin 12°\cos 12°$,

$$\frac{\sin 12°}{\sin 24°} = \frac{1}{2\cos 12°}.$$

Thus
$$E = 224 \cdot \frac{\sin 78°}{2\cos 12°} = 112 \cdot \frac{\sin 78°}{\cos 12°}
.$$

**Step 3: Final cancellation.**

Since $\sin 78° = \sin(90° - 12°) = \cos 12°$,

$$E = 112 \cdot \frac{\cos 12°}{\cos 12°} = 112.$$

Therefore,

$$\boxed{112}.$$
