## Solution

The twisted cubic $C \subset \mathbb{P}^3$ is a smooth rational curve of degree
$3$. I compute its Hilbert polynomial via the minimal free resolution of its ide
al sheaf.

### Step 1: The minimal free resolution

The ideal $I_C = (XZ-Y^2,\; YW-Z^2,\; XW-YZ)$ is generated by $3$ quadrics with
$2$ linear syzygies (the Hilbert–Burch resolution). The resolution of $\mathcal{
I}_C$ is:

$$0 \to \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^3}(-3)^{2} \to \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^3}(-2)^{3
} \to \mathcal{I}_C \to 0.$$

### Step 2: Euler characteristic of $\mathcal{I}_C(n)$

Twisting by $n$ and taking Euler characteristics, using $\chi(\mathcal{O}_{\math
bb{P}^3}(m)) = \binom{m+3}{3}$ (as a polynomial in $m$):

$$\chi(\mathcal{I}_C(n)) = 3\binom{n+1}{3} - 2\binom{n}{3}.$$

Expanding:
- $3\binom{n+1}{3} = 3\cdot\dfrac{(n+1)n(n-1)}{6} = \dfrac{(n+1)n(n-1)}{2},$
- $2\binom{n}{3} = 2\cdot\dfrac{n(n-1)(n-2)}{6} = \dfrac{n(n-1)(n-2)}{3}.$

So
$$\chi(\mathcal{I}_C(n)) = n(n-1)\!\left[\dfrac{n+1}{2}-\dfrac{n-2}{3}\right] =
n(n-1)\cdot\dfrac{3(n+1)-2(n-2)}{6} = \dfrac{n(n-1)(n+7)}{6}.$$

### Step 3: Hilbert polynomial of $C$

From the exact sequence $0\to\mathcal{I}_C(n)\to\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^3}(n)\to
\mathcal{O}_C(n)\to 0$:

$$P_C(n) = \chi(\mathcal{O}_C(n)) = \chi(\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^3}(n))-\chi(\ma
thcal{I}_C(n)).$$

Compute each:
- $\chi(\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^3}(n)) = \dfrac{(n+3)(n+2)(n+1)}{6} = \dfrac{n^3
+6n^2+11n+6}{6},$
- $\chi(\mathcal{I}_C(n)) = \dfrac{n(n-1)(n+7)}{6} = \dfrac{n^3+6n^2-7n}{6}.$

Subtracting:

$$P_C(n) = \dfrac{(n^3+6n^2+11n+6)-(n^3+6n^2-7n)}{6} = \dfrac{18n+6}{6} = 3n+1.$
$

### Step 4: Consistency check

For a smooth curve of degree $d$ and genus $g$ in $\mathbb{P}^3$, the Hilbert po
lynomial is $P_C(n)=dn+1-g$. The twisted cubic has $d=3$, $g=0$, giving $P_C(n)=
3n+1$. This matches.

### Answer

$$\boxed{P_C(n) = 3n+1}$$
