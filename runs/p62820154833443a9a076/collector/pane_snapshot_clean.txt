Looking at this problem, I need to determine whether $H_*(X; A) \cong H_*(\tilde
{X} \times_{\pi_1(X)} A;\, \mathbb{Z})$ for a $\mathbb{Z}[\pi_1(X)]$-module $A$.

## Answer: The statement is **FALSE**.

### Setup and Key Distinction

Recall that homology with local coefficients is *defined* at the chain level:
$$H_*(X; A) := H_*\bigl(C_*(\tilde{X}) \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}[\pi]} A\bigr), \qquad
 \pi = \pi_1(X).$$

The proposed equality instead takes $A$ (viewed as a **discrete topological spac
e**, i.e., a set with $\pi$-action), forms the balanced product **space** $\tild
e{X} \times_\pi A$, and then takes its ordinary homology with $\mathbb{Z}$-coeff
icients.

The critical issue is that when we pass from the module $A$ to the discrete **sp
ace** $A$ and then take singular/cellular chains, the chain group in degree $0$
of a discrete space $A$ is the **free abelian group on the underlying set** $\ma
thbb{Z}[A] = \bigoplus_{a \in A} \mathbb{Z}$, which is generally much larger tha
n $A$ itself.

### Chain-Level Analysis

Suppose $X$ is a connected CW-complex with universal cover $\tilde{X}$, and $\pi
$ acts freely and cellularly on $\tilde{X}$. The $n$-cells of the balanced produ
ct $\tilde{X}\times_\pi A$ are indexed by pairs $(e^n_i, a)$ where $e^n_i$ range
s over orbit representatives of $n$-cells of $\tilde{X}$ and $a$ ranges over the
 **set** $A$. Using the balanced product relation $(x\cdot g,\, a) \sim (x,\, g\
cdot a)$, every cell reduces to $(e^n_i, a)$, and these are all distinct for dis
tinct $a$. Therefore:

$$C_n^{\text{cell}}(\tilde{X}\times_\pi A) \;\cong\; \bigoplus_i \mathbb{Z}[A] \
;\cong\; C_n^{\text{cell}}(\tilde{X})\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}[\pi]} \mathbb{Z}[A],$$

where $\mathbb{Z}[A]$ is the **free abelian group on the set $A$** equipped with
 the $\pi$-action induced from $A$. The boundary maps correspond under this iden
tification, giving:

$$H_*(\tilde{X}\times_\pi A;\,\mathbb{Z}) \;\cong\; H_*\bigl(C_*(\tilde{X})\otim
es_{\mathbb{Z}[\pi]}\mathbb{Z}[A]\bigr) \;=\; H_*(X;\,\mathbb{Z}[A]).$$

This is local-coefficient homology with coefficients in $\mathbb{Z}[A]$, **not**
 in $A$. Since $\mathbb{Z}[A] \neq A$ in general (e.g., $A = \mathbb{Z}$ gives $
\mathbb{Z}[\mathbb{Z}] = \bigoplus_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Z}$, a free abelian g
roup of countably infinite rank, versus $\mathbb{Z}$ of rank $1$), the proposed
equality fails.

### Explicit Counterexample

Take $X = S^1$, so $\pi_1(X) = \mathbb{Z}$, and take $A = \mathbb{Z}$ with the *
*trivial** $\mathbb{Z}$-action.

**Left-hand side (local coefficients in $A$):** Since the action is trivial, $H_
*(X;A) = H_*(S^1;\mathbb{Z})$, the ordinary homology:
$$H_0(S^1;\mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}, \qquad H_1(S^1;\mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb
{Z}, \qquad H_n = 0 \text{ for } n\geq 2.$$

**Right-hand side (homology of the balanced product space):** The universal cove
r is $\tilde{X} = \mathbb{R}$, with $\mathbb{Z}$ acting by translation $n\cdot x
 = x+n$. Since the action on $A=\mathbb{Z}$ is trivial, the balanced product is
$$\mathbb{R}\times_{\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Z} \;=\; (\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{Z})/\bi
gl((x+n,\,m)\sim(x,\,m)\bigr) \;\cong\; (\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z})\times\mathbb{Z}
\;\cong\; S^1\times\mathbb{Z},$$
where $\mathbb{Z}$ carries the discrete topology, so this is a disjoint union of
 countably many circles:
$$S^1\times\mathbb{Z} \;=\; \bigsqcup_{m\in\mathbb{Z}} S^1.$$

Its homology with $\mathbb{Z}$-coefficients is:
$$H_0\!\left(\bigsqcup_{m\in\mathbb{Z}} S^1;\,\mathbb{Z}\right) \cong \bigoplus_
{m\in\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Z}, \qquad H_1\!\left(\bigsqcup_{m\in\mathbb{Z}} S^1;\,\
mathbb{Z}\right) \cong \bigoplus_{m\in\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Z}.$$

**Comparison:**
$$\underbrace{H_0(X;A)\cong\mathbb{Z}}_{\text{LHS}} \;\neq\; \underbrace{H_0(\ti
lde{X}\times_\pi A;\mathbb{Z})\cong\bigoplus_{m\in\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Z}}_{\text{
RHS}},$$
and similarly in degree $1$. The two sides are not isomorphic.

### Conclusion

The statement is **false**. The homology of the balanced product *space* $\tilde
{X}\times_{\pi_1(X)} A$ (with $A$ discrete) computes local-coefficient homology
with coefficients in the **free abelian group** $\mathbb{Z}[A]$ on the underlyin
g set of $A$, not in $A$ itself. The correct formulation of local-coefficient ho
mology is the **chain-level** tensor product $H_*(C_*(\tilde{X})\otimes_{\mathbb
{Z}[\pi_1(X)]} A)$, which is a definition rather than the homology of a single a
uxiliary space (unless $A$ happens to be free abelian and one works with an appr
opriate modified construction).
