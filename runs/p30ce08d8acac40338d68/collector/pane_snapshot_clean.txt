## Proof

Let $X$ be the **Cantor set** $C \subseteq [0,1]$. We show $C$ is a compact metr
ic space with $|C|>1$ and $C \cong C \times C$.

### $C$ is a compact metric space with $|C|>1$

$C$ is a closed subset of the compact metric space $[0,1]$, hence compact and me
trizable. Since $0, 1 \in C$ (they belong to every stage of the construction), $
|C| \geq 2 > 1$.

### A convenient model: Cantor space $2^{\mathbb{N}}$

Let $2 = \{0,1\}$ with the discrete topology, and give $2^{\mathbb{N}}$ the prod
uct topology. This is compact (Tychonoff, or directly: countable product of fini
te discrete spaces is compact) and metrizable, e.g. via
$$d\big((a_n),(b_n)\big) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} 2^{-n}|a_n - b_n|.$$

The standard ternary expansion gives a homeomorphism $\varphi: 2^{\mathbb{N}} \t
o C$ by
$$\varphi\big((a_n)\big) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{2a_n}{3^n}.$$
This is well-defined (every such sum lies in $C$, having only $0$'s and $2$'s in
 its ternary expansion), continuous, bijective onto $C$, and since $2^{\mathbb{N
}}$ is compact and $C$ is Hausdorff, it is a homeomorphism.

### $C \times C \cong C$

It suffices to show $2^{\mathbb{N}} \times 2^{\mathbb{N}} \cong 2^{\mathbb{N}}$.

Define the **interleaving map**
$$\Phi: 2^{\mathbb{N}} \times 2^{\mathbb{N}} \to 2^{\mathbb{N}}, \qquad \Phi\big
((a_1,a_2,a_3,\dots),\, (b_1,b_2,b_3,\dots)\big) = (a_1,b_1,a_2,b_2,a_3,b_3,\dot
s).$$

- **Bijective**: the inverse splits a sequence into its odd-indexed and even-ind
exed subsequences:
$$\Phi^{-1}\big((c_1,c_2,c_3,c_4,\dots)\big) = \big((c_1,c_3,c_5,\dots),\, (c_2,
c_4,c_6,\dots)\big).$$

- **Continuous**: projection onto each coordinate of the output depends on a sin
gle coordinate of the input, so every coordinate projection $2^{\mathbb{N}} \to
2$ factors continuously through $\Phi$; since the product topology is the coarse
st making all coordinate projections continuous, $\Phi$ is continuous.

- **Homeomorphism**: $2^{\mathbb{N}} \times 2^{\mathbb{N}}$ is compact (product
of compact spaces) and $2^{\mathbb{N}}$ is Hausdorff, so the continuous bijectio
n $\Phi$ is a homeomorphism.

### Conclusion

Composing homeomorphisms,
$$C \times C \;\xrightarrow{\;\varphi\times\varphi\;}\; 2^{\mathbb{N}} \times 2^
{\mathbb{N}} \;\xrightarrow{\;\Phi\;}\; 2^{\mathbb{N}} \;\xrightarrow{\;\varphi^
{-1}\;}\; C,$$
we obtain $C \times C \cong C$. Thus $X = C$, the Cantor set, is a compact metri
c space with $|X| > 1$ and $X \cong X \times X$.
