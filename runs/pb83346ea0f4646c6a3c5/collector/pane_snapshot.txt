The curve $C_1$ is the quadratic twist by $2$ of $E: y^2 = x^3 + 1$ (via $Y=2y,
X=x$, giving $2y^2 = x^3+1$). Since $E$ has $j$-invariant $0$, it has CM by $\ma

**Torsion of $C_1(\mathbb{Q})$:**

- **2-torsion:** $Y=0 \Rightarrow 2X^3+2=0 \Rightarrow X=-1$. The only rational
2-torsion point is $(-1,0)$.

- **3-torsion:** The 3-division polynomial of $y^2=x^3+1$ is $\psi_3 = 3x(x^3+4)

By Olson's theorem (1974), for a CM curve with $j=0$, the torsion of any quadrat

$$C_1(\mathbb{Q})_{\text{tors}} = \{O, (-1,0)\} \cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}.$$

Since $P=(1,2) \notin \{O, (-1,0)\}$, the point $P$ has **infinite order**. Henc
