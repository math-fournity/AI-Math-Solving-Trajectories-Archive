## Proof

**Reformulation.** The last 30 digits of $n!(n+1)!(2n+1)!-1$ are all 9s if and o
nly if
$$n!(n+1)!(2n+1)!-1 \equiv \underbrace{99\ldots9}_{30} = 10^{30}-1 \pmod{10^{30}
},$$
which is equivalent to
$$n!(n+1)!(2n+1)! \equiv 0 \pmod{10^{30}}.$$

Since $10^{30} = 2^{30}\cdot 5^{30}$, we need both:
$$v_2\bigl(n!(n+1)!(2n+1)!\bigr) \geq 30 \quad\text{and}\quad v_5\bigl(n!(n+1)!(
2n+1)!\bigr) \geq 30.$$

**The 5-adic valuation is the binding constraint.** Using Legendre's formula $v_
p(m!) = \sum_{k\ge 1}\lfloor m/p^k\rfloor$, define
$$F(n) = v_5(n!) + v_5((n+1)!) + v_5((2n+1)!).$$

**Computing $F(n)$ for candidate values.** Since $v_5(m!)$ jumps at multiples of
 5, I evaluate around the region where $F$ approaches 30:

| $n$ | $v_5(n!)$ | $v_5((n+1)!)$ | $2n+1$ | $v_5((2n+1)!)$ | $F(n)$ |
|-----|-----------|---------------|--------|-----------------|--------|
| 29  | $5+1=6$   | $5+1+1=7$     | 59     | $11+2=13$       | 26     |
| 30  | $6+1=7$   | $6+1=7$       | 61     | $12+2=14$       | 28     |
| 31  | $6+1=7$   | $6+1=7$       | 63     | $12+2=14$       | 28     |
| 32  | $6+1=7$   | $6+1=7$       | 65     | $13+2=15$       | 29     |
| 33  | $6+1=7$   | $6+1=7$       | 67     | $13+2=15$       | 29     |
| **34** | $6+1=7$ | $7+1=8$     | 69     | $13+2=15$       | **30** |

(Detail for $n=34$: $v_5(34!)=\lfloor34/5\rfloor+\lfloor34/25\rfloor=6+1=7$; $\;
v_5(35!)=\lfloor35/5\rfloor+\lfloor35/25\rfloor=7+1=8$; $\;v_5(69!)=\lfloor69/5\
rfloor+\lfloor69/25\rfloor=13+2=15$; $\;F(34)=7+8+15=30$.)

**Monotonicity.** $F(n)$ is non-decreasing: increasing $n$ by 1 can only add fac
tors of 5 to each factorial term (the three arguments $n$, $n+1$, $2n+1$ each in
crease, never decrease). Therefore for all $n \leq 33$, $F(n) \leq F(33) = 29 <
30$, so the 5-adic condition fails. Hence no $n \leq 33$ works.

**Verifying the 2-adic condition at $n=34$.**
$$v_2(34!) = 17+8+4+2+1 = 32,$$
$$v_2(35!) = 17+8+4+2+1 = 32,$$
$$v_2(69!) = 34+17+8+4+2+1 = 66.$$
$$v_2\bigl(34!\cdot 35!\cdot 69!\bigr) = 32+32+66 = 130 \geq 30. \checkmark$$

**Conclusion.** At $n=34$ both $v_2 \geq 30$ and $v_5 \geq 30$ hold, so $10^{30}
\mid n!(n+1)!(2n+1)!$, giving $n!(n+1)!(2n+1)!-1\equiv 10^{30}-1\pmod{10^{30}}$
(last 30 digits all 9s). For every $n\leq 33$, $F(n)\leq 29<30$, so the conditio
n fails. Therefore the smallest such positive integer is

$$\boxed{n = 34}.$$
