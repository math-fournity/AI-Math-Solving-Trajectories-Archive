## Solution

The surface is
$$M = \{(x,y,z) : \sqrt{x^2+y^2} = 1+z^{2n},\; 0 < z < 1\}.$$

**Step 1: Identify the topology.**

Set $r = \sqrt{x^2+y^2}$. The defining equation gives $r = 1 + z^{2n}$, where $z
$ ranges over the open interval $(0,1)$. For each fixed $z \in (0,1)$, the set o
f $(x,y)$ satisfying $x^2+y^2 = (1+z^{2n})^2$ is a circle of radius $1+z^{2n} >
0$. Hence $M$ is a surface of revolution.

**Step 2: Explicit parametrization.**

Define
$$\Phi : S^1 \times (0,1) \to M, \qquad \Phi(\theta, z) = \bigl((1+z^{2n})\cos\t
heta,\; (1+z^{2n})\sin\theta,\; z\bigr).$$

This map is:
- **Well-defined into $M$**: $\sqrt{x^2+y^2} = 1+z^{2n}$ and $z \in (0,1)$. ✓
- **Smooth**, since $1+z^{2n}$ is smooth on $(0,1)$.
- **Injective**: different $(\theta,z)$ give different points (the $z$-coordinat
e recovers $z$, and $\theta$ is determined mod $2\pi$).
- **Surjective** onto $M$ by construction.
- **A diffeomorphism**: the inverse is smooth since $r = 1+z^{2n} > 0$ allows re
covering $z$ from $(x,y,z)$ and $\theta = \arctan(y/x)$.

Therefore $M \cong S^1 \times (0,1)$, an open cylinder (annulus).

**Step 3: Compute the Euler characteristic.**

The open interval $(0,1)$ is contractible. The projection
$$S^1 \times (0,1) \to S^1, \qquad (\theta, z) \mapsto \theta$$
with section $z \mapsto z_0$ (any fixed $z_0$) shows that $S^1 \times (0,1)$ def
ormation retracts onto $S^1 \times \{z_0\} \cong S^1$. Explicitly, $H_t(\theta,
z) = (\theta, (1-t)z + t z_0)$ is a deformation retraction.

Since the Euler characteristic is a homotopy invariant,
$$\chi(M) = \chi(S^1 \times (0,1)) = \chi(S^1).$$

A circle $S^1$ admits a CW-structure with one 0-cell and one 1-cell, giving
$$\chi(S^1) = 1 - 1 = 0.$$

**Conclusion.**

$$\boxed{\chi(M) = 0}$$
