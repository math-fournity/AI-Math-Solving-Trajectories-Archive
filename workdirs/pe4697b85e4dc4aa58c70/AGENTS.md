# Problem

In the futuristic city of Lattice-Grid, a square district measuring $100 \times 100$ city blocks is undergoing a solar installation project. The district is divided into $10,000$ identical unit square cells by a network of $101$ horizontal and $101$ vertical street segments, creating a grid of unit-length boundary segments.

The city council has purchased a supply of triangular solar panels. Each panel is an isosceles right triangle with legs of length $1$. When a panel is placed, it occupies exactly half of a square cell, with its two legs resting perfectly on two adjacent unit-length boundary segments of that cell.

The contractors must follow two strict regulations:
1. No two solar panels may overlap or share any interior space.
2. Every single unit-length street segment in the entire $100 \times 100$ grid (including all internal segments and the four outer boundary edges of the district) must be covered by exactly one leg of a solar panel.

Given these installation requirements, find the maximum possible number of cells in the district that do not contain any solar panels.
