Let $N$ denote the normalizer of the maximal torus in $G(L) = \mathrm{GL}_n(L)$.
The pair $(I, N)$ constitutes a BN-pair for $G(L)$ with Weyl group $\tilde{W} =
N / (N \cap I)$, the extended affine Weyl group. This is a foundational result

decomposition and the structure of the Hecke rings of $p$-adic Chevalley groups,

the affine Weyl group $W_a$) accounts for the fact that $\mathrm{GL}_n$ is not
simply connected; $\tilde{W} = W_a \rtimes \Sigma$ where $\Sigma$ is the diagram
automorphism group.

The BN-pair axioms satisfied by $(I, N)$ are:

  1. $G(L)$ is generated by $I$ and $N$.
  2. $H := I \cap N$ is normal in $N$, and $\tilde{W} = N/H$.
  3. For $w \in \tilde{W}$ and a simple reflection $s \in S$ (where $S$ is the
  set of simple reflections generating $\tilde{W}$):
    • $IwI \cdot IsI = IwsI$ whenever $\ell(ws) = \ell(w) + 1$,
    • $IsI \cdot IsI = I \cup IsI$ (equivalently, $IwI \cdot IsI \subseteq IwI
    \cup IwsI$).

hypothesis is $\ell(w_1 w_2) = \ell(w_1) + \ell(w_2) = \ell(w_1) + k$.

i$.

\cdots s_i) \le \ell(w_1 s_1 \cdots s_{i-1}) + 1$ for each $i \ge 1$, and $
\ell(w_1 s_1 \cdots s_0) = \ell(w_1)$. By induction, $\ell(w_1 s_1 \cdots s_i)

the $k$ steps increases the length by at most $1$, every step must increase the
length by exactly $1$. Hence $\ell(w_1 s_1 \cdots s_i) = \ell(w_1) + i$ for all
