$$v_5(100!) = \left\lfloor\frac{100}{5}\right\rfloor + \left\lfloor\frac{100}{25

The 2-adic valuation is
$$v_2(100!) = 50+25+12+6+3+1 = 97.$$

Therefore $v_5(N) = 24 - 20 = 4$ and $v_2(N) = 97$, so $v_{10}(N) = \min(97,4) =
 4$.

**Step 2: Reduce to a residue mod 10.**

The rightmost nonzero digit is
$$\frac{N}{10^4} \bmod 10 = \frac{100!}{5^{20}\cdot 10^4} \bmod 10 = \frac{100!}
{5^{24}\cdot 2^{4}} \bmod 10.$$

Write $100! = 2^{97}\cdot 5^{24}\cdot R$, where $R = \prod_{i=1}^{100} c_i$ and
$c_i$ is $i$ with all factors of $2$ and $5$ removed (so $\gcd(c_i,10)=1$). Then
$$Q := \frac{100!}{5^{24}\cdot 2^{4}} = 2^{93}\cdot R.$$

Since $Q$ is even but not divisible by $5$, we determine $Q \bmod 10$ via CRT:
- $Q \equiv 0 \pmod{2}$,
- $Q \equiv 2^{93}\cdot R \pmod{5}$.

**Step 3: Compute $R \bmod 5$.**

For each $i\in\{1,\dots,100\}$, let $r_i = c_i \bmod 5$. Tallying the residues:

| residue | count |
|---------|-------|
| 1 | 32 |
| 2 | 21 |
| 3 | 26 |
| 4 | 21 |

(Verified: $32+21+26+21 = 100$.) Hence
$$R \equiv 1^{32}\cdot 2^{21}\cdot 3^{26}\cdot 4^{21} \pmod{5}.$$

Using $2^4\equiv 3^4\equiv 4^2\equiv 1 \pmod{5}$:
- $2^{21} = (2^4)^5\cdot 2 \equiv 2$,
- $3^{26} = (3^4)^6\cdot 3^2 \equiv 9 \equiv 4$,
- $4^{21} = (4^2)^{10}\cdot 4 \equiv 4$.

So $R \equiv 2\cdot 4\cdot 4 = 32 \equiv 2 \pmod{5}$.

**Step 4: Finish.**

$2^{93} \equiv 2^{4\cdot 23+1} \equiv 2 \pmod{5}$, so
$$Q \equiv 2\cdot 2 = 4 \pmod{5}, \qquad Q \equiv 0 \pmod{2}.$$
