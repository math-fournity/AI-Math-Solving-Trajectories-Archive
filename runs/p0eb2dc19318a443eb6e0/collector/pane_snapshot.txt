$$d_{M,R}: M \longrightarrow \operatorname{Hom}_R(\operatorname{Hom}_R(M,\,R),\;

rm{ev}_m(\varphi) = \varphi(m)$ for all $\varphi \in M^*$.

By hypothesis, $M$ is reflexive, so $d_{M,R}$ is an isomorphism.

**Reduction from $N \cong R$ to $N = R$.** Let $\alpha: N \xrightarrow{\;\sim\;}

The isomorphism $\alpha$ induces:

$$\alpha_* : \operatorname{Hom}_R(M, N) \xrightarrow{\;\sim\;} \operatorname{Hom

with inverse $\alpha_*^{-1}(\theta) = \alpha^{-1} \circ \theta$.

$$\alpha_{**} : \operatorname{Hom}_R\!\bigl(\operatorname{Hom}_R(M,N),\; N\bigr)
