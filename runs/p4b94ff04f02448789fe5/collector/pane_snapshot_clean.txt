## Proof

Let car 1 start at time $0$ and car 2 start at time $a > 0$ (the cars start "at
different times," and "the first car" is the one that starts first). Both travel
 at one lap per hour. At time $t$, the number of **complete** laps car 1 has fin
ished is $\lfloor t \rfloor$, and the number of **complete** laps car 2 has fini
shed is $\lfloor t-a \rfloor$ (for $t \ge a$; it is $0$ for $t<a$).

We seek the total measure of the set of times $t \ge 0$ at which
$$\lfloor t \rfloor = 2\,\lfloor t-a \rfloor.$$

**Decomposing by the lap count of car 2.** Fix a non-negative integer $n = \lflo
or t-a \rfloor$. Car 2 has completed exactly $n$ laps during $t \in [a+n,\; a+n+
1)$, and car 1 has completed exactly $2n$ laps during $t \in [2n,\; 2n+1)$. The
desired condition holds precisely on the intersection
$$I_n \;=\; [2n,\,2n+1)\;\cap\;[a+n,\,a+n+1).$$

Since the intervals for different $n$ are disjoint, the total time is
$$T \;=\; \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} |I_n|.$$

**Computing $|I_n|$.** We have $I_n = \bigl[\max(2n,\,a+n),\;\min(2n+1,\,a+n+1)\
bigr)$, so
$$|I_n| = \max\!\bigl(0,\;\min(2n+1,\,a+n+1)-\max(2n,\,a+n)\bigr).$$

Note $2n \ge a+n \iff n \ge a$ and $2n+1 \ge a+n+1 \iff n \ge a$. Hence:

- **If $n \ge a$:** $\;|I_n| = \max(0,\;(a+n+1)-2n)=\max(0,\;a+1-n)$.
- **If $n < a$:** $\;|I_n| = \max(0,\;(2n+1)-(a+n))=\max(0,\;n+1-a)$.

In both cases this simplifies to
$$|I_n| = \max\!\bigl(0,\;1-|n-a|\bigr).$$

So $|I_n|>0$ only when $|n-a|<1$, i.e., when the integer $n$ lies in the open in
terval $(a-1,\;a+1)$.

**Summing.** Write $a = m + \alpha$ with $m = \lfloor a \rfloor \ge 0$ and $\alp
ha \in [0,1)$.

- **If $\alpha = 0$ (i.e., $a=m$ is an integer):** The only integer in $(m-1,\,m
+1)$ is $n=m$, giving $|I_m| = 1-|m-a| = 1$. All other terms vanish. Thus $T = 1
$.

- **If $\alpha > 0$:** The integers in $(m+\alpha-1,\;m+\alpha+1)$ are $n=m$ and
 $n=m+1$.
  - $n = m < a$: $\;|I_m| = 1-(a-m) = 1-\alpha$.
  - $n = m+1 > a$: $\;|I_{m+1}| = 1-(m+1-a) = 1-(1-\alpha) = \alpha$.
  - All other terms vanish.

  Therefore $T = (1-\alpha) + \alpha = 1$.

In every case,
$$T = 1 \text{ hour.} \qquad \blacksquare$$
