## Solution

We seek $|N_{S_8}(T)|$, where $T$ is the translation subgroup (elementary abelia
n $2$-group) of $AGL(3,2)$, viewed as a subgroup of $S_8$ via the natural action
 on the $8$ points of the affine space $\mathbb{F}_2^3$.

**The translation subgroup.** $AGL(3,2) = T \rtimes GL(3,2)$, where $T \cong (\m
athbb{Z}/2)^3$ is the group of translations, acting regularly on the $8$ points
of $\mathbb{F}_2^3$. As a subgroup of $S_8$, $T$ is a *regular* elementary abeli
an subgroup of order $8$.

**Normalizer of a regular subgroup.** A standard result in permutation group the
ory states: if $T$ acts regularly on a set $\Omega$ of size $n = |T|$, then the
normalizer of $T$ in $\mathrm{Sym}(\Omega)$ is the **holomorph** of $T$:
$$N_{\mathrm{Sym}(\Omega)}(T) = \mathrm{Hol}(T) = T \rtimes \mathrm{Aut}(T).$$

*Proof of this fact:* Identify $\Omega$ with $T$ via the regular action (pick a
basepoint $o$, map $t \in T \mapsto t \cdot o$). Any $\sigma \in N_{S_8}(T)$ sat
isfies $\sigma T \sigma^{-1} = T$. The map $\varphi_\sigma: T \to T$ defined by
$\sigma t \sigma^{-1} = \varphi_\sigma(t)$ is an automorphism of $T$. Writing $\
sigma(o) = a \cdot o$ for some $a \in T$, one checks that $\sigma = t_a \circ \v
arphi_\sigma$, where $t_a$ is left-translation by $a$. Conversely, every $t_a \c
irc \varphi$ with $a \in T$, $\varphi \in \mathrm{Aut}(T)$ normalizes $T$. Hence
 $N_{S_8}(T) = T \rtimes \mathrm{Aut}(T)$.

**Computing the order.** Here $T \cong (\mathbb{Z}/2)^3$, so
$$\mathrm{Aut}(T) \cong GL(3,2), \qquad |GL(3,2)| = (8-1)(8-2)(8-4) = 7 \cdot 6
\cdot 4 = 168.$$

Therefore
$$|N_{S_8}(T)| = |T| \cdot |\mathrm{Aut}(T)| = 8 \cdot 168 = \boxed{1344}.$$

This equals $|AGL(3,2)|$, confirming that $AGL(3,2)$ is the full normalizer of i
ts translation subgroup inside $S_8$.
