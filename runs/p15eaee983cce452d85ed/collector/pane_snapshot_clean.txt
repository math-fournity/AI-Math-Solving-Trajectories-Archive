## Solution

The smallest possible value is **n = 4**.

### Upper bound: n = 4 always suffices

Let $A$ be a non-unital $F$-algebra with $\dim_F A = 3$. Form the **unitization*
* $\widetilde{A} = A \oplus F\cdot 1$, a unital algebra of dimension $4$, with m
ultiplication
$$(a,\alpha)(b,\beta) = (ab + \alpha b + \beta a,\; \alpha\beta).$$
$\widetilde{A}$ contains $A \cong A\oplus\{0\}$ as a (non-unital) subalgebra of
codimension $1$.

Since $\widetilde{A}$ has a unit, the **left regular representation**
$$L: \widetilde{A} \to \operatorname{End}_F(\widetilde{A}), \qquad L_x(y) = xy$$
is injective: if $L_x = 0$, then $x = x\cdot 1 = L_x(1) = 0$. It is an algebra h
omomorphism, so restricting to $A \subseteq \widetilde{A}$ gives an injective ho
momorphism of (non-unital) algebras
$$A \hookrightarrow \operatorname{End}_F(\widetilde{A}) = A(\widetilde{A}), \qqu
ad \dim_F \widetilde{A} = 4.$$
Thus $A$ embeds into $A(V)$ with $n = 4$.

### Lower bound: n = 3 is not always enough

Consider the $3$-dimensional algebra $A$ with **zero multiplication** ($ab = 0$
for all $a,b\in A$). It is non-unital (unless $\dim = 1$) and, by the upper boun
d, is isomorphic to a subalgebra of some $A(V)$, so it satisfies the hypothesis.

Suppose for contradiction that $A$ embeds in $A(V)$ with $\dim_F V = 3$. Then th
ere exist linearly independent $T_1, T_2, T_3 \in \operatorname{End}_F(V)$ with
$T_i T_j = 0$ for all $i,j$.

Let $U = \operatorname{im}T_1 + \operatorname{im}T_2 + \operatorname{im}T_3 \sub
seteq V$, and set $d = \dim_F U$. Since $T_i T_j = 0$, we have $\operatorname{im
}T_j \subseteq \ker T_i$ for every $i$, hence $U \subseteq \bigcap_i \ker T_i$,
i.e. **every $T_i$ vanishes on $U$**. Also every $T_i$ maps $V$ into $U$. Theref
ore each $T_i$ factors through $V/U$:
$$T_i: V \longrightarrow V/U \xrightarrow{\;\bar T_i\;} U.$$
The space of all such linear maps has dimension
$$\dim_F(V/U)\cdot \dim_F U = (3 - d)\,d.$$
Since $T_1,T_2,T_3$ are three linearly independent elements of this space,
$$3 \;\le\; (3-d)\,d.$$
For integer $d \in \{0,1,2,3\}$, the maximum of $(3-d)d$ is $2$ (attained at $d=
1$ and $d=2$). This is strictly less than $3$ — contradiction.

Hence the zero-multiplication algebra of dimension $3$ cannot be embedded in $A(
V)$ when $n=3$.

### Conclusion

The smallest $n$ that works for every embeddable non-unital $3$-dimensional alge
bra is

$$\boxed{n = 4}.$$
