# Problem

A specialized logistics company, "Crate-Tech," manages 100 industrial sensors, each assigned a unique frequency ranging from $1, 2, \dots, 100$ megahertz (MHz). The company undergoes a 101-day testing sequence to calibrate these sensors.

During the first 100 days, on each specific day $n$ (where $1 \leq n \leq 100$), the engineers activate all sensors with a frequency of $n$ MHz or less. For every possible combination of 6 active sensors that results in a median frequency of exactly $n - 50$ MHz, the system generates a quality report consisting of two digital tokens. (Note: A single sensor can be part of multiple combinations simultaneously).

On the 101st day, the engineers activate all 100 sensors for a final diagnostic. During this final run, every possible combination of 6 sensors that results in a median frequency of exactly 50.5 MHz generates one additional digital token.

How many digital tokens are generated in total by the end of the 101st day?
