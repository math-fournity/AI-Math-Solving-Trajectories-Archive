# Problem

A specialized cybersecurity firm, "Spaska-Systems," utilizes a recursive encryption protocol based on a single master security key valued at 1. Within the firm’s hierarchy, every digital key originates from a lineage of two parental keys. If a specific key has a numerical strength of $n$, its "Primary Source" key is defined as having a strength of $3n$, and its "Secondary Source" key has a strength of $3n+1$. The lineage of the master key includes itself, its two source keys, the four source keys of those keys, and so on, continuing indefinitely.

A network port is classified as "Vulnerable" if its port number can be represented in exactly one way as the sum of the strengths of two distinct keys found within the master key’s lineage.

Determine the total count of Vulnerable port numbers that are strictly less than 2003.
