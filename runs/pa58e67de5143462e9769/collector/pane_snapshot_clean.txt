## Proof

Recall that for a sheaf of rings $\mathcal{O}_Y$ on $Y$, the sections over the e
mpty set are the zero ring:
$$\mathcal{O}_Y(\emptyset) \cong 0,$$
the unique ring in which $0 = 1$. (This is the terminal object in the category o
f rings, and it is forced by the sheaf axiom: $\mathcal{O}_Y(\emptyset)$ is the
product over the empty indexing family of copies of $\mathcal{O}_Y(\emptyset)$,
i.e. a one-point set carrying the unique ring structure with $0=1$.)

The stalk of the pushforward at $p$ is, by definition, the colimit over open nei
ghborhoods of $p$:
$$(f_*\mathcal{O}_Y)_p \;=\; \varinjlim_{p\in U\subseteq X} (f_*\mathcal{O}_Y)(U
) \;=\; \varinjlim_{p\in U\subseteq X} \mathcal{O}_Y\!\bigl(f^{-1}U\bigr),$$
where the colimit ranges over open neighborhoods $U$ of $p$, ordered by reverse
inclusion, with restriction maps of $\mathcal{O}_Y$ as the transition maps.

By hypothesis there exists an open neighborhood $U_0$ of $p$ with $f^{-1}U_0 = \
emptyset$. Hence the indexing category contains $U_0$, and the corresponding ter
m in the directed system is
$$\mathcal{O}_Y\!\bigl(f^{-1}U_0\bigr) = \mathcal{O}_Y(\emptyset) \cong 0.$$

For every open $U$ with $p \in U \subseteq U_0$, we have $f^{-1}U \subseteq f^{-
1}U_0 = \emptyset$, so $f^{-1}U = \emptyset$ as well, and $\mathcal{O}_Y(f^{-1}U
) \cong 0$. Thus the cofinal subcategory of neighborhoods contained in $U_0$ con
tributes only the zero ring, and the colimit over a cofinal subcategory equals t
he colimit over the whole category. It suffices therefore to compute the colimit
 of a directed system in which every term is the zero ring $0$ and every transit
ion map is the unique endomorphism $\mathrm{id}_0$ of $0$. This colimit is the z
ero ring:
$$\varinjlim (\cdots \to 0 \to 0 \to 0 \to \cdots) \;\cong\; 0.$$

Concretely: every element of the colimit is represented by some section $s \in \
mathcal{O}_Y(f^{-1}U)$ with $p \in U \subseteq U_0$, hence $s \in \mathcal{O}_Y(
\emptyset) = 0$, so $s = 0 = 1$ in that ring. The equivalence relation identifyi
ng sections on a common refinement preserves this, so the colimit has a single e
lement in which $0 = 1$.

Therefore
$$(f_*\mathcal{O}_Y)_p \;\cong\; 0,$$
the zero ring. $\blacksquare$
