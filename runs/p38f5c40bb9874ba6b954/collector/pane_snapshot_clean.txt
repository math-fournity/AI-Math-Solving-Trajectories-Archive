## Solution

We determine the fundamental group of the quotient space $X = \mathbb{S}^1 / \si
m$, where $x \sim r^k(x)$ for $k = 0, 1, \ldots, n-1$ and $r$ is rotation by $2\
pi/n$.

### Step 1. The quotient map is a covering map

Let $q: \mathbb{S}^1 \to X$ be the quotient projection. The cyclic group $G = \l
angle r \rangle \cong \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ acts freely and properly discontin
uously on $\mathbb{S}^1$ (every non-identity rotation has no fixed point, and th
e action is by a finite group on a Hausdorff space). Therefore $q: \mathbb{S}^1
\to X$ is a regular (normal) covering map with deck transformation group $\text{
Deck}(q) \cong G \cong \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$.

### Step 2. Identify the base space explicitly

Define the map
$$\varphi: \mathbb{S}^1 \to \mathbb{S}^1, \qquad \varphi(\cos\theta, \sin\theta)
 = (\cos(n\theta), \sin(n\theta)).$$
This is the $n$-fold wrapping map $z \mapsto z^n$ (viewing $\mathbb{S}^1 \subset
 \mathbb{C}$). It is constant on each equivalence class $\{x, r(x), \ldots, r^{n
-1}(x)\}$, since rotating $\theta$ by $2\pi/n$ multiplies $e^{i\theta}$ by an $n
$-th root of unity, which is killed by the $n$-th power. Hence $\varphi$ descend
s to a continuous map
$$\overline{\varphi}: X \to \mathbb{S}^1.$$

Conversely, every point $e^{i\alpha} \in \mathbb{S}^1$ has exactly $n$ preimages
 under $\varphi$, namely $e^{i(\alpha + 2\pi k)/n}$ for $k = 0, \ldots, n-1$, an
d these form exactly one equivalence class. So $\overline{\varphi}$ is a bijecti
on. Since $X$ is compact (continuous image of compact $\mathbb{S}^1$) and $\math
bb{S}^1$ is Hausdorff, $\overline{\varphi}$ is a homeomorphism.

Thus $X \cong \mathbb{S}^1$.

### Step 3. Compute the fundamental group

Since $X$ is homeomorphic to $\mathbb{S}^1$,
$$\pi_1(X) \cong \pi_1(\mathbb{S}^1) \cong \mathbb{Z}.$$

### Verification via covering space theory (consistency check)

The covering $q: \mathbb{S}^1 \to X \cong \mathbb{S}^1$ corresponds to the subgr
oup $n\mathbb{Z} \subset \mathbb{Z} = \pi_1(\mathbb{S}^1)$, i.e., it is the stan
dard $n$-sheeted covering $z \mapsto z^n$. The exact sequence of the covering
$$1 \to \pi_1(\mathbb{S}^1) \to \pi_1(X) \to \text{Deck}(q) \to 1$$
reads
$$1 \to \mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{\,\times n\,} \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb
{Z} \to 1,$$
which is exact, confirming $\pi_1(X) \cong \mathbb{Z}$ with the covering map ind
ucing multiplication by $n$ on $\pi_1$.

### Conclusion

The fundamental group of the quotient space is
$$\boxed{\pi_1\!\left(\mathbb{S}^1/\!\sim\right) \cong \mathbb{Z}.}$$
