## Proof

Let $X_1, X_2, \ldots$ be i.i.d. $\text{Uniform}(0,1)$ and $S_n = X_1+\cdots+X_n
$. The integral equals $E\!\left[\frac{n}{S_n}\right]$. Note $E[n/S_n]<\infty$ f
or $n\ge 2$ since the Irwin–Hall density satisfies $f_n(s)\sim s^{n-1}/(n-1)!$ n
ear $0$.

**Claim:** $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}E\!\left[\frac{n}{S_n}\right]=2$.

### Lower bound

Since $g(x)=1/x$ is convex on $(0,\infty)$, Jensen's inequality gives
$$E\!\left[\frac{n}{S_n}\right]=E\!\left[\frac{1}{S_n/n}\right]\ge \frac{1}{E[S_
n/n]}=\frac{1}{1/2}=2.$$

### Upper bound

Fix $\epsilon\in(0,1/2)$ and set $a=1/2-\epsilon$. Decompose:
$$E\!\left[\frac{n}{S_n}\right]=\underbrace{E\!\left[\frac{n}{S_n}\mathbf{1}_{S_
n\ge an}\right]}_{(I)}+\underbrace{E\!\left[\frac{n}{S_n}\mathbf{1}_{S_n<an}\rig
ht]}_{(II)}.$$

**Term (I) $\to 2$.** On $\{S_n\ge an\}$, $n/S_n\le 1/a$ (bounded). By the stron
g law of large numbers, $S_n/n\to 1/2$ a.s.; since $1/2>a$, eventually $\mathbf{
1}_{S_n\ge an}=1$ a.s., so $n/S_n\cdot\mathbf{1}_{S_n\ge an}\to 2$ a.s. Bounded
convergence yields $(I)\to 2$.

**Term (II) $\to 0$.** Write the integral against the density $f_n$ of $S_n$:
$$(II)=\int_0^{an}\frac{n}{s}\,f_n(s)\,ds=\int_0^1\frac{n}{s}\,f_n(s)\,ds+\int_1
^{an}\frac{n}{s}\,f_n(s)\,ds.$$

*First sub-integral:* For $s\in[0,1]$, $f_n(s)=s^{n-1}/(n-1)!$, so
$$\int_0^1\frac{n}{s}\cdot\frac{s^{n-1}}{(n-1)!}\,ds=\frac{n}{(n-1)!}\cdot\frac{
1}{n-1}=\frac{n}{(n-1)(n-1)!}\longrightarrow 0.$$

*Second sub-integral:* For $s\ge 1$, $n/s\le n$, so
$$\int_1^{an}\frac{n}{s}\,f_n(s)\,ds\le n\int_1^{an}f_n(s)\,ds\le n\,P(S_n<an).$
$

By **Hoeffding's inequality** ($X_i\in[0,1]$, $\mu=1/2$, $t=\epsilon$):
$$P(S_n<an)=P\!\left(\frac{S_n}{n}-\frac12\le -\epsilon\right)\le e^{-2n\epsilon
^2}.$$

Therefore $\displaystyle\int_1^{an}\frac{n}{s}f_n(s)\,ds\le n\,e^{-2n\epsilon^2}
\to 0$, and $(II)\to 0$.

### Conclusion

For every $\epsilon\in(0,1/2)$:
$$2\le\liminf_{n\to\infty}E\!\left[\frac{n}{S_n}\right]\le\limsup_{n\to\infty}E\
!\left[\frac{n}{S_n}\right]\le 2+0=2.$$

Hence $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}E\!\left[\frac{n}{S_n}\right]=2$.
