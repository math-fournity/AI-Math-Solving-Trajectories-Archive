# Problem

A massive hexagonal solar farm is being constructed with a boundary length of 100 meters per side. To optimize the placement of photovoltaic cells, each side of the perimeter is divided into exactly 87 equal segments. Using these segment markers and the six corners of the farm, engineers have laid down a dense grid of service paths that run parallel to each of the six perimeter walls, crisscrossing the interior to partition the entire farm into a mosaic of small, congruent equilateral triangles.

To complete the installation, the entire surface of the farm is covered by specialized rectangular sensor panels, where each panel (a rhombus) perfectly covers exactly two adjacent triangular tiles. 

A specialized fiber-optic diagnostic cable is then laid across the farm. In this specific configuration, we are analyzing the set of "main grid lines"—the original service paths drawn parallel to the perimeter walls. Given that $k = 87$ represents the number of segments per side, how many of these service paths intersect exactly the diagonals of the sensor panels?
