## Solution

We evaluate the limit using the asymptotic expansion of the two-parameter Mittag
-Leffler function.

**Definition.** The two-parameter Mittag-Leffler function is
$$E_{\alpha,\beta}(z) = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{z^k}{\Gamma(\alpha k + \beta)}
, \qquad \alpha > 0,\ \beta \in \mathbb{R}.$$

**Asymptotic expansion (Gorenflo–Mainardi).** For $0 < \alpha < 2$ and $\beta \i
n \mathbb{R}$, as $|z| \to \infty$ in the sector $|\arg z| > \frac{\pi\alpha}{2}
$ (i.e., outside the sector of exponential growth), the following algebraic expa
nsion holds:
$$E_{\alpha,\beta}(z) = -\sum_{k=1}^{N} \frac{z^{-k}}{\Gamma(\beta - \alpha k)}
+ O\!\left(|z|^{-N-1}\right).$$

**Applying with $\beta = \alpha + 1$.** Setting $\beta = \alpha + 1$, the leadin
g term ($k=1$) of the expansion is:
$$E_{\alpha,\,\alpha+1}(z) \sim -\frac{z^{-1}}{\Gamma\!\big((\alpha+1) - \alpha\
big)} = -\frac{z^{-1}}{\Gamma(1)} = -\frac{1}{z}, \qquad |z| \to \infty.$$

The next term ($k=2$) is $-z^{-2}/\Gamma(1-\alpha) = O(|z|^{-2})$, which is of l
ower order.

**Substituting $z = -a\,t^{\alpha}$.** As $|t| \to \infty$ (with $a > 0$ and $0
< \alpha < 2$, so that $z = -a\,t^{\alpha}$ lies in the sector $|\arg z| > \pi\a
lpha/2$ where the algebraic expansion is valid):
$$E_{\alpha,\,\alpha+1}\!\big(-a\,t^{\alpha}\big) \sim -\frac{1}{-a\,t^{\alpha}}
 = \frac{1}{a\,t^{\alpha}}.$$

Therefore:
$$t^{\alpha}\, E_{\alpha,\,\alpha+1}\!\big(-a\,t^{\alpha}\big) \sim t^{\alpha} \
cdot \frac{1}{a\,t^{\alpha}} = \frac{1}{a}, \qquad |t| \to \infty.$$

Multiplying by the constant $\beta$:
$$\lim_{t \to -\infty} \beta\, t^{\alpha}\, E_{\alpha,\,\alpha+1}\!\big(-a\,t^{\
alpha}\big) = \beta \cdot \frac{1}{a} = \frac{\beta}{a}.$$

**Conclusion.**

$$\boxed{\lim_{t\rightarrow -\infty} \beta\, t^\alpha\, E_{\alpha,\,\alpha+1}(-a
\,t^\alpha) = \dfrac{\beta}{a}}$$
