**Key observation — odd symmetry in \(y\):**

Consider the change of variables \((x,y) \mapsto (x, -y)\). Under this reflectio

- \(x^4 \mapsto x^4\) (unchanged, even in \(y\)),
- \(\sin y \mapsto \sin(-y) = -\sin y\) (odd in \(y\)),
- \(x^2 + y^2 + 1 \mapsto x^2 + (-y)^2 + 1 = x^2 + y^2 + 1\) (unchanged).

Hence the integrand transforms as
\[
\frac{x^4 \sin y}{x^2+y^2+1} \;\longmapsto\; \frac{x^4(-\sin y)}{x^2+y^2+1} = -\
frac{x^4\sin y}{x^2+y^2+1}.
\]

**The region is invariant.** Since \(D_R\) (a disk, or any origin-centered symme
tric exhaustion) satisfies \((x,-y)\in D_R \iff (x,y)\in D_R\), the reflection i
s a bijection of \(D_R\) onto itself with Jacobian determinant \(1\).

**The integral vanishes identically.** Applying the substitution \((x,y)=(u,-v)\

\[
I_R = \iint_{D_R} \frac{x^4\sin y}{x^2+y^2+1}\,dx\,dy
= \iint_{D_R} \frac{u^4\sin(-v)}{u^2+v^2+1}\,du\,dv
= -\iint_{D_R} \frac{u^4\sin v}{u^2+v^2+1}\,du\,dv

Therefore \(2I_R = 0\), i.e. \(I_R = 0\) for **every** \(R\).

**Taking the limit:**
