## Solution

Each square crate is a cube with 6 square faces. Two crates give 12 faces total.

**Hidden faces** (not visible to the manager):

1. **Bottom face of the lower crate** — pressed flat against the concrete dock,
not visible.
2. **Top face of the lower crate** — pressed against the bottom of the upper cra
te, not visible.
3. **Bottom face of the upper crate** — pressed against the top of the lower cra
te, not visible.

That is 3 hidden faces.

**Visible faces:**

- **Lower crate:** 4 side faces (visible from all four sides as the manager walk
s around).
- **Upper crate:** 4 side faces (visible from all four sides) + 1 top face (visi
ble from the catwalk directly above).

**Count:**

$$12 - 3 = 9$$

or equivalently,

$$4 + 4 + 1 = 9$$

The manager can see **9** total square exterior faces.
