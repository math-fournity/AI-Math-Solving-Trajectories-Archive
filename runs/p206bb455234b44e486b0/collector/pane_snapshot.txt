## Solution

We evaluate $\displaystyle S = \sum_{x=1}^{\infty} \frac{|\sin x|}{x}$.

**Claim:** The series diverges, i.e., $S = +\infty$.

**Proof.**

The key observation is the pointwise lower bound obtained by replacing $|\sin x|
$ with $\sin^2 x$ (which is valid since $|\sin x| \geq \sin^2 x$ for all real $x
$, as $0 \leq |\sin x| \leq 1$):

$$|\sin x| \geq \sin^2 x = \frac{1 - \cos(2x)}{2}.$$

Therefore, for every $x \geq 1$,

$$\frac{|\sin x|}{x} \geq \frac{1 - \cos(2x)}{2x} = \frac{1}{2x} - \frac{\cos(2x
)}{2x}.$$

Summing from $x=1$ to $N$:

$$\sum_{x=1}^{N} \frac{|\sin x|}{x} \geq \frac{1}{2}\sum_{x=1}^{N} \frac{1}{x} -
 \frac{1}{2}\sum_{x=1}^{N} \frac{\cos(2x)}{x}.$$

We now analyze the two sums on the right:

1. **The harmonic sum diverges:** $\displaystyle \sum_{x=1}^{N} \frac{1}{x} \to
+\infty$ as $N \to \infty$.

2. **The cosine sum converges:** $\displaystyle \sum_{x=1}^{\infty} \frac{\cos(2
x)}{x}$ converges by **Dirichlet's test**. Indeed:
   - The partial sums $\displaystyle \sum_{x=1}^{N} \cos(2x)$ are bounded. Using
 the identity for the sum of cosines in arithmetic progression:
     $$\sum_{x=1}^{N} \cos(2x) = \frac{\sin\!\left(N \cdot 1\right)\cos\!\left(1
 + (N-1)\cdot 1\right)}{\sin 1} = \frac{\sin N \cos(N)}{\sin 1},$$
     which is bounded in absolute value by $\frac{1}{|\sin 1|}$ (note $\sin 1 \n
eq 0$ since $1$ is not an integer multiple of $\pi$).
   - The sequence $\frac{1}{x}$ is positive, monotonically decreasing, and tends
 to $0$.

   By Dirichlet's test, $\sum_{x=1}^{\infty} \frac{\cos(2x)}{x}$ converges to so
me finite value $L$.

Combining these two facts:

$$\sum_{x=1}^{N} \frac{|\sin x|}{x} \geq \frac{1}{2}\sum_{x=1}^{N} \frac{1}{x} -
 \frac{1}{2}\sum_{x=1}^{N} \frac{\cos(2x)}{x}.$$

As $N \to \infty$, the first term on the right tends to $+\infty$ while the seco
nd term tends to the finite limit $\frac{L}{2}$. Therefore the right-hand side t
ends to $+\infty$, which forces

$$\sum_{x=1}^{\infty} \frac{|\sin x|}{x} = +\infty.$$

**Conclusion:** The series $\displaystyle \sum_{x=1}^{\infty} \frac{|\sin x|}{x}
$ diverges to $+\infty$.
