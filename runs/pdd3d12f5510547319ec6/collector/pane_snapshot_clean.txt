## Solution

**Setup.** We have $f(x) = 2x + 25 \pmod{31}$, $g(0) = x_0 = 2017$, and $g(k) =
f(g(k-1))$ for $k > 0$. We want $\sum_{j=0}^{2017} g(j) \pmod{31}$.

**Reduce the seed.** $2017 \bmod 31$: since $31 \times 65 = 2015$, we get $x_0 \
equiv 2 \pmod{31}$, so $g(0) = 2$.

**Compute the orbit of 2 under $f$:**

| $k$ | $g(k) = 2g(k-1)+25 \pmod{31}$ |
|-----|-------------------------------|
| 0   | 2                             |
| 1   | $2(2)+25 = 29$                |
| 2   | $2(29)+25 = 83 \equiv 21$     |
| 3   | $2(21)+25 = 67 \equiv 5$      |

| 5   | $2(4)+25 = 33 \equiv 2$       |

So the orbit cycles with **period 5**: $2, 29, 21, 5, 4, 2, \dots$

**Sum of one full cycle:** $2 + 29 + 21 + 5 + 4 = 61 \equiv 30 \pmod{31}$.

**Count terms.** The sum $\sum_{j=0}^{2017}$ has $2018$ terms. Dividing by the p
eriod 5:
$$2018 = 5 \cdot 403 + 3$$
So we have 403 full cycles plus the first 3 terms of the next cycle.

**Compute the total mod 31:**

$24583 \equiv 24583 - 31\times793 = 24583 - 24583 = 0$. Let me recheck: $31 \tim

  (Equivalently, $403 \equiv 403 - 31\times13 = 403-403 = 0 \pmod{31}$, and $61
\equiv 30$, so $403\times 61 \equiv 0 \times 30 = 0$.)

- Remaining 3 terms: $g(0)+g(1)+g(2) = 2 + 29 + 21 = 52 \equiv 21 \pmod{31}$.

**Total:** $0 + 21 = 21$.

$$\sum_{j=0}^{2017} g(j) \pmod{31} = \boxed{21}$$
