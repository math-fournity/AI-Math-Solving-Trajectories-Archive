## Solution

**Setup and definitions.** Place black and white coins on a $418\times 418$ grid
. Let

- $r$ = number of rows containing at least one white coin (call these *W-rows*);
 the remaining $418-r$ rows contain no white coin (call them *clean rows*).
- After step 1, a black coin survives iff its row has no white coin, i.e., iff i
t lies in a clean row. Let $c$ = number of distinct columns occupied by survivin
g black coins.
- After step 2, a white coin survives iff its column contains no surviving black
 coin, i.e., iff its column is **not** among those $c$ columns.

**Counting survivors.** The surviving black coins all lie in the $418-r$ clean r
ows and use $c$ columns, so
$$b \le (418-r)\,c,$$
with equality when every cell of the $(418-r)\times c$ rectangle is filled with
a black coin. The surviving white coins lie in the $r$ W-rows and in the $418-c$
 columns free of surviving black, so
$$w \le r\,(418-c),$$
with equality when every cell of the $r\times(418-c)$ rectangle is filled with a
 white coin. These two fillings are compatible (clean rows get only black; W-row
s get white in the non-black columns), so
$$bw \le \bigl[r(418-r)\bigr]\bigl[c(418-c)\bigr].$$

**Maximizing.** The two factors are independent (we freely choose $r$ W-rows and
 $c$ black-columns). For an integer $x\in\{1,\dots,417\}$, the quadratic $x(418-
x)$ is maximized at $x=209$, giving $209\cdot 209 = 43681$. (At $x=208$: $208\cd
ot210=43680$, strictly less.) Hence
$$\max bw = 43681^2 = 209^4 = 1{,}908{,}029{,}761.$$

**Achievability.** Take $r=c=209$: designate 209 rows as W-rows (no black needed
 there) and 209 columns as black-columns. Fill the $209\times 209$ rectangle of
clean rows × black-columns with black coins ($b=209^2$), and fill the $209\times
 209$ rectangle of W-rows × remaining columns with white coins ($w=209^2$). Ever
y W-row has white coins; every clean row has none; surviving black occupies exac
tly the 209 black-columns; surviving white occupies exactly the other 209 column
s. This gives $bw = 209^4$.

**Remainder mod 2007.** Since $2007 = 9\times 223$, compute directly:
$$209^2 = 43681 \equiv 1534 \pmod{2007},$$
$$209^4 \equiv 1534^2 = 2{,}353{,}156 \pmod{2007}.$$
Dividing: $2007\times 1172 = 2{,}352{,}204$, so
$$2{,}353{,}156 - 2{,}352{,}204 = 952.$$

Therefore the remainder when the maximum value of $bw$ is divided by $2007$ is $
\boxed{952}$.
