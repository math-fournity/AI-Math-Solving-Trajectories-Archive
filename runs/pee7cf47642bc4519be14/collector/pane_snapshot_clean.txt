*Proof of Lemma.* Since $f_n$ is absolutely monotone, $f_n'' \ge 0$, so $f_n$ is

$$f_n(y) \le (1-y)\, f_n(0) + y\, f_n(1).$$
Monotonicity gives $f_n(0) \le f_n(y)$. Combining, for $y \in (0,1)$:
$$\frac{f_n(y) - y\, f_n(1)}{1-y} \;\le\; f_n(0) \;\le\; f_n(y).$$

$$\frac{f(y) - y\, f(1)}{1-y} \;\le\; \liminf_{n} f_n(0) \;\le\; \limsup_{n} f_n

By the induction hypothesis, $\widetilde{G}_n(1) \to \widetilde{G}(1)$.

$$\widetilde{G}_n(y) = \frac{G_n(y) - \sum_{j=0}^{k-1} a_{j,n}\, y^j}{y^k}.$$

$$a_{k,n} = \widetilde{G}_n(0) \;\longrightarrow\; \widetilde{G}(0) = a_k.$$
