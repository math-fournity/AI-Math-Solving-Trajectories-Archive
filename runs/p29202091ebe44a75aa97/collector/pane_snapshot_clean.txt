## Solution

**Key identity.** I claim that for all $n \ge 1$,
$$\arctan\frac{1}{P_{2n}} - \arctan\frac{1}{P_{2n+2}} = \arctan\frac{2}{P_{2n+1}
}.$$

**Proof of identity.** For $x,y>0$, $\arctan x - \arctan y = \arctan\!\left(\dfr
ac{x-y}{1+xy}\right)$. With $x=1/P_{2n}$, $y=1/P_{2n+2}$:
$$\frac{x-y}{1+xy} = \frac{P_{2n+2}-P_{2n}}{P_{2n}P_{2n+2}+1}.$$

Two standard Pell identities:
- $P_{m+1}P_{m-1}-P_m^2 = (-1)^m$ (Cassini-type). With $m=2n+1$: $P_{2n+2}P_{2n}
-P_{2n+1}^2 = -1$, so $P_{2n}P_{2n+2}+1 = P_{2n+1}^2$.
- From the recurrence, $P_{2n+2}-P_{2n} = (2P_{2n+1}+P_{2n})-P_{2n} = 2P_{2n+1}$
.

Therefore
$$\frac{P_{2n+2}-P_{2n}}{P_{2n}P_{2n+2}+1} = \frac{2P_{2n+1}}{P_{2n+1}^2} = \fra
c{2}{P_{2n+1}},$$
confirming the identity. (The value lies in $(0,\pi/2)$, so the principal branch
 applies.)

**Telescoping.** Let $a_n = \arctan(1/P_{2n})$. The identity gives $\arctan(2/P_
{2n+1}) = a_n - a_{n+1}$. The summand is
$$\left(a_n + a_{n+1}\right)\left(a_n - a_{n+1}\right) = a_n^2 - a_{n+1}^2.$$

Hence the partial sums telescope:
$$\sum_{n=1}^{N}\left(a_n^2 - a_{n+1}^2\right) = a_1^2 - a_{N+1}^2.$$

Since $P_{2N+2}\to\infty$, $a_{N+1}=\arctan(1/P_{2N+2})\to 0$. Therefore
$$S = a_1^2 = \left(\arctan\frac{1}{P_2}\right)^2 = \left(\arctan\frac{1}{2}\rig
ht)^2.$$
