## Counting Permutations with Given Record Positions

**Formula:** The number of permutations of $[n]$ with left-to-right maxima at po
sitions $1 = p_1 < p_2 < \cdots < p_k$ is:
$$\frac{(n-1)!}{\prod_{j=2}^{k}(p_j - 1)}$$

*Derivation:* Fill blocks from last to first. Block $j$ (positions $p_j$ to $p_{
j+1}-1$) has record value $v_j$ (the largest remaining) at position $p_j$, and $
b_j - 1$ non-record positions filled from values $< v_j$. This gives $\binom{p_{
j+1}-2}{b_j-1}(b_j-1)! = \frac{(p_{j+1}-2)!}{(p_j-1)!}$ per block, which telesco
pes to $\frac{(n-1)!}{\prod_{j=2}^k (p_j-1)}$.
