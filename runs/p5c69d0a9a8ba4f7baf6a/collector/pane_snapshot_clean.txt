## Solution

We evaluate
$$L = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{(x-\sqrt{2})^4}{\sin^3(x)\cos^2(x)}\int_{0}^x \frac{t
^2}{t^4+1}\,dt.$$

**Step 1: The constant factor.** As $x \to 0$,
$$(x-\sqrt{2})^4 \to (-\sqrt{2})^4 = 4, \qquad \cos^2(x) \to 1.$$

**Step 2: The integral near $x=0$.** For $t$ near $0$, $\frac{1}{t^4+1} = 1 - t^
4 + O(t^8)$, so
$$\frac{t^2}{t^4+1} = t^2 - t^6 + O(t^{10}).$$
Integrating term by term,
$$\int_0^x \frac{t^2}{t^4+1}\,dt = \frac{x^3}{3} - \frac{x^7}{7} + O(x^{11}) \si
m \frac{x^3}{3} \quad (x\to 0).$$

**Step 3: The sine factor.** As $x\to 0$, $\sin x \sim x$, so $\sin^3(x) \sim x^
3$.

**Step 4: Combine.** Putting everything together,
$$L = \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{(x-\sqrt{2})^4}{\sin^3(x)\cos^2(x)}\cdot\int_0^x \frac{
t^2}{t^4+1}\,dt = \frac{4}{x^3\cdot 1}\cdot\frac{x^3}{3}\bigg|_{x\to 0} = \frac{
4}{3}.$$

More rigorously, writing each factor with its leading behavior:
$$\frac{(x-\sqrt{2})^4}{\sin^3 x\,\cos^2 x}\int_0^x\frac{t^2}{t^4+1}dt = \frac{4
+O(x)}{x^3(1+O(x^2))(1+O(x^2))}\cdot\frac{x^3}{3}(1+O(x^4)) \;\longrightarrow\;
\frac{4}{3}.$$

$$\boxed{\dfrac{4}{3}}$$
