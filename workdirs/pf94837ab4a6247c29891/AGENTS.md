# Problem

A high-tech logistics company uses a specialized cubic structural frame to transport sensitive materials. This frame consists of 12 structural struts (the edges) connected at 8 corner joints (the vertices). To ensure stability and avoid interference, the company must coat each individual strut in one of three experimental insulators: Yellow Polyimide, Red Ceramic, or Blue Carbon.

The safety protocol requires that no two struts connected to the same corner joint can have the same color coating. Furthermore, because these frames are handled by automated robotic arms that rotate and flip the containers in three-dimensional space, two frames are considered identical if one can be rotated to match the color configuration of the other.

In how many distinct ways can a frame be coated according to these specifications?
