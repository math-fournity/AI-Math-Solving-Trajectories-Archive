The arc AB *containing* C and D equals 2β + 2γ + 2δ = 360° − 2α. The condition t

360° − 2α = 4γ ⟹ **α = 180° − 2γ**, and then β + δ = γ.

Since ∠ACB is the inscribed angle subtending arc AB (measure 2α), we have **∠ACB

**Coordinate setup.** Place the circle as the unit circle with M (midpoint of mi

- C = (cos γ, −sin γ), D = (cos γ, sin γ)
- A = (cos a, sin a) where a = γ + 2δ
- B = (cos b, sin b) where b = 2δ − 3γ

**Distances.** Using the chord formula:
- AC² = 4 sin²(γ + δ)
- BD² = 4 sin²(δ − 2γ)

**Equations for E = (x, y).** From AE = AC and BE = BD, expanding on the unit ci

x² + y² − 2x cos a − 2y sin a + 1 = 4 sin²(γ + δ) ... (1)
x² + y² − 2x cos b − 2y sin b + 1 = 4 sin²(δ − 2γ) ... (2)

Subtracting (2) from (1):

x(cos b − cos a) + y(sin b − sin a) = 2[sin²(γ + δ) − sin²(δ − 2γ)] ... (4)

**Perpendicularity condition.** The perpendicular from E to line AB passes throu

(x − 1)(cos b − cos a) + y(sin b − sin a) = 0
⟹ x(cos b − cos a) + y(sin b − sin a) = cos b − cos a ... (3)

Combining (3) and (4):

**cos b − cos a = 2[sin²(γ + δ) − sin²(δ − 2γ)]**

**Evaluating both sides using sum-to-product:**

LHS = cos(2δ − 3γ) − cos(γ + 2δ) = 2 sin(2δ − γ) sin(2γ)

RHS = cos(2δ − 4γ) − cos(2γ + 2δ) = 2 sin(2δ − γ) sin(3γ)

sin(2δ − γ) · [sin(2γ) − sin(3γ)] = 0

- 2γ = 180° − 3γ ⟹ **5γ = 180° ⟹ γ = 36°**.
