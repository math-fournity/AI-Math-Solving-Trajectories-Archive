## Proof

Let $F = \langle a, b \rangle$ be the free group on two generators, and let $N \
trianglelefteq F$ be the normal subgroup generated by $\{a^2, b^2\}$.

**Step 1: Identify the quotient.** By definition of the normal closure, the quot
ient $F/N$ has the presentation
$$F/N \cong \langle a, b \mid a^2 = 1,\; b^2 = 1 \rangle.$$
This is the free product $\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z} * \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$.

**Step 2: Show this group is infinite.** The free product $G = \mathbb{Z}/2 * \m
athbb{Z}/2$ is the infinite dihedral group $D_\infty$. To see it is infinite, co
nsider the element $ab \in G$. We claim $ab$ has infinite order.

Suppose for contradiction that $(ab)^n = 1$ for some $n \geq 1$. The group $G$ a
cts faithfully on $\mathbb{Z}$: let $a$ act as reflection $x \mapsto -x$ and $b$
 act as reflection $x \mapsto 2 - x$ (so $ab$ acts as $x \mapsto x + 2$). Under
this action, $(ab)^n$ acts as $x \mapsto x + 2n$, which is the identity only if
$n = 0$. Hence $ab$ has infinite order, and $G$ is infinite.

(Equivalently, by the Kurosh/normal-form theorem for free products, every elemen
t of $G = \mathbb{Z}/2 * \mathbb{Z}/2$ has a unique reduced form as an alternati
ng product of $a$'s and $b$'s, and the elements $(ab)^n$ for $n \geq 1$ are all
distinct and nontrivial.)

**Step 3: Conclude.** Since $F/N \cong \mathbb{Z}/2 * \mathbb{Z}/2$ is an infini
te group, the index of $N$ in $F$ is
$$[F : N] = |F/N| = \infty.$$
