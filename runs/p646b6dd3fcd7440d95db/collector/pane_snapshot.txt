## Proof

Write the expression as $7 \uparrow 7 \uparrow 7 \uparrow 7 \uparrow 7 \uparrow
7 \uparrow 7$ (seven 7's in a fixed order). We exhibit two distinct parenthesiza
tions that produce the same number.

**Key identity (4 sevens).** Consider four 7's. Two different parenthesizations
give the same value:

$$\bigl(7^{(7^7)}\bigr)^7 = 7^{7 \cdot 7^7} = 7^{7^8}, \qquad \bigl(7^7\bigr)^{(
7^7)} = 7^{7 \cdot 7^7} = 7^{7^8}.$$

Both equal $7^{7^8} = 7^{5764801}$, yet the parenthesizations $((a_1 \uparrow (a
_2 \uparrow a_3)) \uparrow a_4)$ and $((a_1 \uparrow a_2) \uparrow (a_3 \uparrow
 a_4))$ are different binary trees.

**Extending to 7 sevens.** Split the seven 7's into a left block of 4 and a righ
t block of 3. The right block of 3 is parenthesized identically in both cases as
 $(7 \uparrow (7 \uparrow 7)) = 7^{7^7} = 7^{823543}$. The two full parenthesiza
tions are:

$$\textbf{(A)}\quad \Bigl(\bigl((7 \uparrow (7 \uparrow 7)) \uparrow 7\bigr) \up
arrow (7 \uparrow (7 \uparrow 7))\Bigr)$$

$$\textbf{(B)}\quad \Bigl(\bigl((7 \uparrow 7) \uparrow (7 \uparrow 7)\bigr) \up
arrow (7 \uparrow (7 \uparrow 7))\Bigr)$$

These differ only in how the first four 7's are grouped — (A) uses $((1 \uparrow
 (2 \uparrow 3)) \uparrow 4)$ while (B) uses $((1 \uparrow 2) \uparrow (3 \uparr
ow 4))$ — so they are genuinely different parenthesizations.

**Computation of (A):**
- $7^7 = 823543$.
- $7^{(7^7)} = 7^{823543}$.
- $\bigl(7^{823543}\bigr)^7 = 7^{7 \cdot 823543} = 7^{7^8} = 7^{5764801}$.
- Right side: $7^{(7^7)} = 7^{823543}$.
- Final: $\bigl(7^{5764801}\bigr)^{7^{823543}} = 7^{5764801 \cdot 7^{823543}} =
7^{7^8 \cdot 7^{7^7}} = 7^{7^{8 + 7^7}} = 7^{7^{823551}}$.

**Computation of (B):**
- $7^7 = 823543$.
- $\bigl(7^7\bigr)^{(7^7)} = 7^{7 \cdot 823543} = 7^{7^8} = 7^{5764801}$.
- Right side: $7^{(7^7)} = 7^{823543}$.
- Final: $\bigl(7^{5764801}\bigr)^{7^{823543}} = 7^{7^8 \cdot 7^{7^7}} = 7^{7^{8
+7^7}} = 7^{7^{823551}}$.

Both (A) and (B) equal $7^{7^{823551}}$, yet they are different parenthesization
s of the same expression. $\blacksquare$
