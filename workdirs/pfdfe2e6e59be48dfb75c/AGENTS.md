# Problem

A specialized architectural firm is designing a high-tech "Zenith Pavilion," which is shaped exactly like a regular hexagonal pyramid. To provide a unique lighting experience, a large, flat glass pane will be installed to bisect the interior space.

This glass pane is positioned according to three specific structural anchor points:
1. The midpoint of one edge of the hexagonal floor.
2. The midpoint of another edge of the hexagonal floor, specifically choosing an edge that is neither adjacent nor parallel to the first one.
3. The midpoint of the central vertical support pillar (the height of the pyramid), which rises from the center of the floor to the apex.

The project manager needs to calculate the ratio of the surface area of this glass pane (the cross-section within the pyramid) to the surface area of one of the six slanted outer walls (a lateral face) of the pavilion.

If the ratio of the glass area to one lateral face area is expressed as an irreducible fraction $\frac{a}{b}$, what is the value of $a + b$?
