## Solution

**Setup.** Place $M$ at the origin with the angle bisector of $\angle M$ along t
he positive $x$-axis. Let $\angle M = 2\alpha$, so the sides $CM$ and $FM$ make
angles $\alpha$ and $-\alpha$ with the $x$-axis. The incenter $O$ lies on the bi
sector at distance $MO = r/\sin\alpha = 6/\sin\alpha$, so $O = (6/\sin\alpha, 0)
$.

**Tangent points.** The foot of the perpendicular from $O$ to line $CM$ (directi
on $(\cos\alpha,\sin\alpha)$) is:
$$P = \tfrac{6\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha}(\cos\alpha,\sin\alpha) = \left(\tfrac{6\co
s^2\alpha}{\sin\alpha},\; 6\cos\alpha\right), \qquad K = \left(\tfrac{6\cos^2\al
pha}{\sin\alpha},\; -6\cos\alpha\right).$$

**Circumcircle of $PKM$.** By symmetry, its center $T$ lies on the $x$-axis: $T=
(t,0)$. From $|TM|^2=|TP|^2$:
$$t^2 = \left(t-\tfrac{6\cos^2\alpha}{\sin\alpha}\right)^2 + 36\cos^2\alpha \imp
lies t = \frac{3}{\sin\alpha}.$$
Thus $R = |TM| = 3/\sin\alpha = \frac{5\sqrt{13}}{2}$, giving $\sin\alpha = \fra
c{6}{5\sqrt{13}}$ and $\cos\alpha = \frac{17}{5\sqrt{13}}$.

**Triangle sides.** Write $C = c(\cos\alpha,\sin\alpha)$, $F = f(\cos\alpha,-\si
n\alpha)$, with $MC=b=c$, $MF=a=f$, $CF=m$. The tangent length from $M$ is $MP =
 6/\tan\alpha$, and also equals $s - m$ (where $s$ is the semi-perimeter), so $s
 - m = 6\cos\alpha/\sin\alpha$.

**Area ratio condition.** The area of $\triangle CFT$:
$$[\triangle CFT] = \tfrac12\bigl|3(c+f) - cf\sin 2\alpha\bigr|.$$
Using $cf\sin 2\alpha = 2[\triangle CFM] = 12s$ and $c+f = a+b = 2s-m$:
$$3(2s-m) - 12s = -6s - 3m \implies [\triangle CFT] = \tfrac12(6s+3m) = 3s + \tf
rac32 m.$$
The ratio $\frac{[\triangle CFT]}{[\triangle CFM]} = \frac{3s+\frac32 m}{6s} = \
frac{5}{8}$ yields $3s + \frac32 m = \frac{15}{4}s$, so $\frac32 m = \frac34 s$,
 i.e. $s = 2m$.

**Solving.** From $s=2m$: $s-m = m$, so $m = 6\cos\alpha/\sin\alpha = 6\cdot\fra
c{17}{6} = 17$. Then $s=34$, $a+b = 2s-m = 51$, and $S = rs = 6\cdot 34 = 204$.
From $S = \frac12 ab\sin 2\alpha$ with $\sin 2\alpha = \frac{204}{325}$:
$$ab = \frac{2\cdot 204}{204/325} = 650, \qquad a+b=51 \implies (a,b)=(26,25).$$

**Angle bisector length.** Using $L^2 = ab\!\left(1 - \frac{m^2}{(a+b)^2}\right)
 = \frac{4ab\,s(s-m)}{(a+b)^2}$:
$$L^2 = \frac{4\cdot 650\cdot 34\cdot 17}{51^2} = \frac{1502800}{2601} = \frac{5
200\cdot 17^2}{9\cdot 17^2} = \frac{5200}{9}.$$
Therefore $\frac{L^2}{13} = \frac{5200}{117} = \frac{400}{9}$.

**Final answer:**
$$\frac{L^2}{13} + S = \frac{400}{9} + 204 = \frac{400+1836}{9} = \boxed{\frac{2
236}{9}}.$$
