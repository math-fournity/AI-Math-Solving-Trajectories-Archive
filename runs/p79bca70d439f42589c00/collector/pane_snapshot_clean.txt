## Solution

Let $v_k = \cot^2\!\frac{k\pi}{7}$ for $k=1,2,3$. We seek $v_1^2+v_2^2+v_3^2$.

**Step 1: A cubic for $\sin^2\frac{k\pi}{7}$.**

Using $\sin(7x)=7\sin x-56\sin^3 x+112\sin^5 x-64\sin^7 x$ and dividing by $\sin
 x\neq 0$, the equation $\sin(7x)=0$ gives, with $u=\sin^2 x$:
$$64u^3-112u^2+56u-7=0.$$
Its three roots are $u_k=\sin^2\frac{k\pi}{7}$, $k=1,2,3$. By Vieta's:
$$u_1+u_2+u_3=\tfrac{7}{4},\quad u_1u_2+u_2u_3+u_3u_1=\tfrac{7}{8},\quad u_1u_2u
_3=\tfrac{7}{64}.$$

**Step 2: Convert to a cubic for $v_k=\cot^2\frac{k\pi}{7}$.**

Since $v_k=\frac{1}{u_k}-1$, we have $u_k=\frac{1}{v_k+1}$. Substituting $u=\fra
c{1}{v+1}$ into the cubic and multiplying by $(v+1)^3$:
$$64-112(v+1)+56(v+1)^2-7(v+1)^3=0.$$
Expanding:
$$-7v^3+35v^2-21v+1=0,$$
i.e.
$$7v^3-35v^2+21v-1=0.$$
So $v_1,v_2,v_3$ are its roots. By Vieta's:
$$v_1+v_2+v_3=5,\qquad v_1v_2+v_2v_3+v_3v_1=3,\qquad v_1v_2v_3=\tfrac{1}{7}.$$

**Step 3: Compute the fourth-power sum.**

$$v_1^2+v_2^2+v_3^2=(v_1+v_2+v_3)^2-2(v_1v_2+v_2v_3+v_3v_1)=5^2-2\cdot 3=25-6=19
.$$

Since $\cot^4\theta=(\cot^2\theta)^2=v^2$, the desired sum equals $19$.

**Numerical check.** $\cot^4\frac{\pi}{7}\approx 18.59$, $\cot^4\frac{2\pi}{7}\a
pprox 0.404$, $\cot^4\frac{3\pi}{7}\approx 0.0027$; sum $\approx 18.997\approx 1
9$. ✓

$$\cot^4\frac{\pi}{7}+\cot^4\frac{2\pi}{7}+\cot^4\frac{3\pi}{7}=\boxed{19}.$$
