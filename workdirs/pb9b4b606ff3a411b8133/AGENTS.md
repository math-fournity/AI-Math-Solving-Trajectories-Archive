# Problem

A high-tech manufacturing plant has a single assembly line consisting of 2024 sequential workstations, numbered 1 to 2024. Each workstation is currently assigned to exactly one specific production project. Due to the linear nature of the factory’s infrastructure, the projects are assigned in contiguous blocks; that is, if two workstations are working on the same project, every workstation located between them is also assigned to that same project.

Marvin, the floor supervisor, does not know which projects are assigned to which stations. However, he can visit any workstation $i$ and ask the technician there one question: "How many total workstations in this line of 2024 are currently assigned to your specific project?" 

Marvin needs to determine the exact project boundaries—specifically, he needs to be able to conclude for every possible pair of workstations whether they are working on the same project or different projects.

Determine the smallest positive integer $k$ such that Marvin can always guarantee he will have enough information to map out all project groupings by asking at most $k$ questions.
