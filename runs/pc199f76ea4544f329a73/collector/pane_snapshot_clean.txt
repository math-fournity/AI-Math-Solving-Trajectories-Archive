## Setup

Assume density $\rho = 1$ and $a>0$. The solid $V$ is bounded by

- $z^{2}=2ax$ (parabolic cylinder, requires $x\ge 0$),
- $z=0$ (the plane $xy$),
- $x^{2}+y^{2}=ax$ (circular cylinder).

From $z=0$ and $z^{2}=2ax$ the solid occupies $0\le z\le \sqrt{2ax}$. The base i
n the $xy$-plane is the disk $D=\{(x,y):x^{2}+y^{2}\le ax\}$, which forces $x\ge
 0$ (consistent with $z^{2}=2ax$). Rewriting $x^{2}+y^{2}=ax$ as $(x-a/2)^{2}+y^
{2}=(a/2)^{2}$ shows $D$ is a disk of radius $a/2$ centered at $(a/2,0)$.

The moment of inertia about $OZ$ is

$$I_{z}=\iiint_{V}(x^{2}+y^{2})\,dz\,dA.$$

## Polar coordinates

Set $x=r\cos\theta$, $y=r\sin\theta$, so $x^{2}+y^{2}=r^{2}$ and $dA=r\,dr\,d\th
eta$. The circle $x^{2}+y^{2}=ax$ becomes $r=a\cos\theta$, requiring $\cos\theta
\ge 0$, hence $\theta\in[-\pi/2,\pi/2]$, $0\le r\le a\cos\theta$. Also $\sqrt{2a
x}=\sqrt{2a\,r\cos\theta}=\sqrt{2a\cos\theta}\,r^{1/2}$.

Integrating over $z$ first:

$$I_{z}=\iint_{D}r^{2}\,\sqrt{2ax}\,dA=\iint_{D}r^{2}\cdot\sqrt{2a\cos\theta}\,r
^{1/2}\cdot r\,dr\,d\theta=\sqrt{2a}\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}\sqrt{\cos\theta}\int_{
0}^{a\cos\theta}r^{7/2}\,dr\,d\theta.$$

## Radial integral

$$\int_{0}^{a\cos\theta}r^{7/2}\,dr=\frac{(a\cos\theta)^{9/2}}{9/2}=\frac{2}{9}\
,a^{9/2}(\cos\theta)^{9/2}.$$

Substituting,

$$I_{z}=\sqrt{2a}\cdot\frac{2}{9}\,a^{9/2}\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}(\cos\theta)^{1/2
}(\cos\theta)^{9/2}\,d\theta=\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{9}\,a^{5}\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}\cos
^{5}\theta\,d\theta.$$

## Angular integral (Wallis)

$\cos^{5}\theta$ is even, so

$$\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}\cos^{5}\theta\,d\theta=2\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\cos^{5}\theta\,
d\theta=2\cdot\frac{4}{5}\cdot\frac{2}{3}=2\cdot\frac{8}{15}=\frac{16}{15}.$$

## Final result

$$I_{z}=\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{9}\,a^{5}\cdot\frac{16}{15}=\boxed{\dfrac{32\sqrt{2}}{1
35}\,a^{5}}.$$

**Dimensional check:** $V\sim a^{3}$ (mass $\sim a^{3}$), $(x^{2}+y^{2})\sim a^{
2}$, so $I_{z}\sim a^{5}$, consistent with the result. A quick volume cross-chec
k $V=\frac{8\sqrt{2}}{15}a^{3}$ confirms the geometry and the polar setup are co
rrect.
