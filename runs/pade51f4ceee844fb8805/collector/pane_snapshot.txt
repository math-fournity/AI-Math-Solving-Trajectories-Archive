## Solution

**Setup.** Place the ranger station $O$ at the origin, the diameter along the $x
$-axis, and the semicircular sanctuary in the upper half-plane: $x^2+y^2\le 1,\
y\ge 0$. The area of this semicircle is $\pi/2$.

Let $P=(x_1,y_1)$ and $Q=(x_2,y_2)$ be i.i.d. uniform over the semicircle. The a
rea of $\triangle OPQ$ is

$$A=\tfrac12\,|x_1 y_2 - x_2 y_1|.$$

**Polar coordinates.** Write $P=(r_1\cos\theta_1,\, r_1\sin\theta_1)$ and $Q=(r_
2\cos\theta_2,\, r_2\sin\theta_2)$ with $0\le r\le 1$, $0\le\theta\le\pi$. Then

$$A=\tfrac12\,r_1 r_2\,|\sin(\theta_2-\theta_1)|.$$

The uniform density over the semicircle in polar coordinates is $\frac{2r}{\pi}$
, so $r$ and $\theta$ are independent: $r$ has density $2r$ on $[0,1]$, and $\th
eta$ is uniform on $[0,\pi]$.

**Radial expectation.**

$$\mathbb{E}[r]=\int_0^1 r\cdot 2r\,dr = \frac{2}{3}, \qquad \mathbb{E}[r_1 r_2]
=\mathbb{E}[r_1]\,\mathbb{E}[r_2]=\frac{4}{9}.$$

**Angular expectation.** Let $\varphi=\theta_2-\theta_1$. Since $\theta_1,\theta
_2$ are i.i.d. $\mathrm{Uniform}(0,\pi)$, the density of $\varphi$ is triangular
:

$$f(\varphi)=\frac{\pi-|\varphi|}{\pi^2},\qquad |\varphi|\le\pi.$$

Then

$$\mathbb{E}[|\sin\varphi|]=\frac{2}{\pi^2}\int_0^\pi \sin\varphi\,(\pi-\varphi)
\,d\varphi.$$

Compute the integral:

$$\int_0^\pi \sin\varphi\,(\pi-\varphi)\,d\varphi = \pi\underbrace{\int_0^\pi\si
n\varphi\,d\varphi}_{=\,2} - \underbrace{\int_0^\pi \varphi\sin\varphi\,d\varphi
}_{=\,\pi} = 2\pi-\pi=\pi.$$

(The second integral: $\int_0^\pi\varphi\sin\varphi\,d\varphi=[-\varphi\cos\varp
hi]_0^\pi+\int_0^\pi\cos\varphi\,d\varphi=\pi+0=\pi$.)

Hence

$$\mathbb{E}[|\sin\varphi|]=\frac{2}{\pi^2}\cdot\pi=\frac{2}{\pi}.$$

**Combine.** Since $r_1,r_2$ are independent of $\theta_1,\theta_2$,

$$\mathbb{E}[A]=\frac12\,\mathbb{E}[r_1 r_2]\,\mathbb{E}[|\sin(\theta_2-\theta_1
)|]=\frac12\cdot\frac{4}{9}\cdot\frac{2}{\pi}=\boxed{\dfrac{4}{9\pi}}.$$
