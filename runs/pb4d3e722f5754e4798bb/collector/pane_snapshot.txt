## Proof

$$\omega_+ = \frac{(x-1)\,dy\wedge dz + y\,dz\wedge dx + z\,dx\wedge dy}{\big((x

dge dx + z\,dx\wedge dy}{\big((x+1)^2+y^2+z^2\big)^{3/2}}.$$

Each $\omega_\pm$ is the **flux 2-form** of the Coulomb-type vector field

$$\mathbf{F}_+ = \frac{(x-1,\, y,\, z)}{\big((x-1)^2+y^2+z^2\big)^{3/2}}, \qquad
 \mathbf{F}_- = \frac{(x+1,\, y,\, z)}{\big((x+1)^2+y^2+z^2\big)^{3/2}},$$

i.e. $\omega_\pm = \iota_{\mathbf{F}_\pm}(dx\wedge dy\wedge dz)$. Equivalently,
$\omega_\pm = \phi_\pm^*(\eta)$ where $\eta$ is the standard area form on $S^2$
and $\phi_\pm(p) = \frac{p - p_\pm}{\|p-p_\pm\|}$ is the radial projection onto

**Step 2: Each $\omega_\pm$ is closed on $\mathbb{R}^3\setminus\{p_\pm\}$, and i

$$\int_S \omega_\pm = \int_{\Sigma_\varepsilon}^{\text{outward}} \omega_\pm.$$
