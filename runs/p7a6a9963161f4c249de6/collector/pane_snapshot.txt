## Proof

**Claim.** Yes, the mapping $t \mapsto E[M_t]$ is right-continuous.

**Step 1. Reduction to the cadlag supermartingale $N$.**

Since $N$ is a modification of $M$, for every $t$ we have $P(N_t = M_t)=1$, henc
e
$$E[N_t] = E[M_t] \quad\text{for all } t.$$
Therefore it suffices to prove that $t \mapsto E[N_t]$ is right-continuous, wher
e $N$ is a cadlag supermartingale.

**Step 2. Setup.**

Fix $t$ and let $(s_n)_{n\geq 1}$ be any sequence with $s_n \downarrow t$. Since
 $N$ is a supermartingale, $u \leq v \Rightarrow E[N_v] \leq E[N_u]$, so for $s_
n \geq s_{n+1} \geq t$,
$$E[N_{s_n}] \leq E[N_{s_{n+1}}] \leq E[N_t].$$
Thus $a_n := E[N_{s_n}]$ is non-decreasing and bounded above by $E[N_t]$, so $a_
n \uparrow L$ for some $L \leq E[N_t]$. We must show $L = E[N_t]$.

Since $N$ has cadlag (hence right-continuous) paths, $N_{s_n} \to N_t$ almost su
rely.

**Step 3. A non-negative lower bound via the supermartingale property.**

For every $n \geq 1$, since $s_n \leq s_1$, the supermartingale property gives
$$N_{s_n} \;\geq\; E\!\left[N_{s_1}\,\middle|\,\mathcal{F}_{s_n}\right] =: Y_n.$
$
Set $Z_n := N_{s_n} - Y_n \geq 0$.

**Step 4. Convergence of $(Y_n)$.**

The $\sigma$-algebras $\mathcal{F}_{s_n}$ are decreasing (since $s_n \downarrow$
 and the filtration is increasing), and by right-continuity of the filtration,
$$\bigcap_{n\geq 1} \mathcal{F}_{s_n} \;=\; \mathcal{F}_{t+} \;=\; \mathcal{F}_t
.$$
Since $N_{s_1}$ is integrable, Lévy's downward (backward martingale) convergence
 theorem yields
$$Y_n = E\!\left[N_{s_1}\,\middle|\,\mathcal{F}_{s_n}\right] \;\xrightarrow{\tex
t{a.s. and } L^1}\; E\!\left[N_{s_1}\,\middle|\,\mathcal{F}_t\right].$$
Consequently $Z_n \to N_t - E[N_{s_1}\mid\mathcal{F}_t]$ almost surely.

**Step 5. Applying Fatou to the non-negative $(Z_n)$.**

Since $Z_n \geq 0$, Fatou's lemma gives
$$E\!\left[N_t - E\!\left[N_{s_1}\,\middle|\,\mathcal{F}_t\right]\right] \;\leq\
; \liminf_{n\to\infty} E[Z_n].$$
The left-hand side equals $E[N_t] - E[N_{s_1}]$ (tower property). For the right-
hand side, since $E[Y_n] = E[N_{s_1}]$ for all $n$ (tower property, $s_n \leq s_
1$),
$$\liminf_{n} E[Z_n] = \liminf_{n}\bigl(E[N_{s_n}] - E[Y_n]\bigr) = L - E[N_{s_1
}].$$
Thus
$$E[N_t] - E[N_{s_1}] \;\leq\; L - E[N_{s_1}],$$
which gives $E[N_t] \leq L$.

**Step 6. Conclusion.**

Combining $L \leq E[N_t]$ (from Step 2) with $E[N_t] \leq L$ (from Step 5), we o
btain $L = E[N_t]$. Since the sequence $(s_n)$ with $s_n \downarrow t$ was arbit
rary,
$$\lim_{s \downarrow t} E[N_s] = E[N_t] \quad\text{for every } t.$$
Hence $t \mapsto E[N_t]$ is right-continuous, and by Step 1 the same holds for $
t \mapsto E[M_t]$. $\blacksquare$
