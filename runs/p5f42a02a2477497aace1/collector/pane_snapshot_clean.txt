## Solution

**Langford pairings.** We have two copies of each value $1,2,\dots,n$ placed in
$2n$ positions, with the two copies of $k$ separated by exactly $k$ intervening
positions (i.e., at positions $i$ and $i+k+1$). The filled row is read left-to-r
ight as a $2n$-digit integer. We enumerate all valid rows for $n=3$ and $n=4$ an
d sum the resulting integers.

**Existence.** Langford pairings exist only when $n\equiv 0$ or $3\pmod 4$. Both
 $n=3$ ($\equiv 3$) and $n=4$ ($\equiv 0$) qualify.

---

### Case $n=3$ (positions $1,\dots,6$)

The pair $k$ occupies positions $(i,\,i+k+1)$.

**Pair 3** (gap 3, distance 4): options $(1,5)$ or $(2,6)$.

- **3 at $(1,5)$.** Remaining slots $\{2,3,4,6\}$. Pair 2 (distance 3): only $(3
,6)$ fits. Remaining $\{2,4\}$. Pair 1 (distance 2): $(2,4)$ fits.
  Row: $3,1,2,1,3,2 \;\Rightarrow\; 312132$.

- **3 at $(2,6)$.** Remaining $\{1,3,4,5\}$. Pair 2 (distance 3): only $(1,4)$ f
its. Remaining $\{3,5\}$. Pair 1 (distance 2): $(3,5)$ fits.
  Row: $2,3,1,2,1,3 \;\Rightarrow\; 231213$.

No other branches survive. So $N_3 = 2$, and

$$S_3 = 231213 + 312132 = \boxed{543345}.$$

### Case $n=4$ (positions $1,\dots,8$)

**Pair 4** (gap 4, distance 5): options $(1,6),(2,7),(3,8)$.

**Branch A: 4 at $(1,6)$.** Remaining $\{2,3,4,5,7,8\}$.
- Pair 3 (distance 4): $(3,7)$ or $(4,8)$.
  - 3 at $(3,7)$: remaining $\{2,4,5,8\}$. Pair 2 (distance 3): $(2,5)$ or $(5,8
)$.
    - 2 at $(2,5)$: remaining $\{4,8\}$; pair 1 needs distance 2 but $(4,8)$ has
 distance 4. ✗
    - 2 at $(5,8)$: remaining $\{2,4\}$; pair 1 at $(2,4)$ ✓.
      Row: $4,1,3,1,2,4,3,2 \;\Rightarrow\; 41312432$.
  - 3 at $(4,8)$: remaining $\{2,3,5,7\}$. Pair 2 (distance 3): only $(2,5)$. Re
maining $\{3,7\}$; pair 1 needs distance 2, $(3,7)$ has distance 4. ✗

**Branch B: 4 at $(2,7)$.** Remaining $\{1,3,4,5,6,8\}$.
- Pair 3 (distance 4): $(1,5)$ or $(4,8)$.
  - 3 at $(1,5)$: remaining $\{3,4,6,8\}$. Pair 2 (distance 3): only $(3,6)$. Re
maining $\{4,8\}$; pair 1 needs distance 2, $(4,8)$ distance 4. ✗
  - 3 at $(4,8)$: remaining $\{1,3,5,6\}$. Pair 2 (distance 3): only $(3,6)$. Re
maining $\{1,5\}$; pair 1 needs distance 2, $(1,5)$ distance 4. ✗

**Branch C: 4 at $(3,8)$.** Remaining $\{1,2,4,5,6,7\}$.
- Pair 3 (distance 4): $(1,5)$ or $(2,6)$.
  - 3 at $(1,5)$: remaining $\{2,4,6,7\}$. Pair 2 (distance 3): only $(4,7)$. Re
maining $\{2,6\}$; pair 1 needs distance 2, $(2,6)$ distance 4. ✗
  - 3 at $(2,6)$: remaining $\{1,4,5,7\}$. Pair 2 (distance 3): $(4,7)$ or $(1,4
)$.
    - 2 at $(4,7)$: remaining $\{1,5\}$; pair 1 needs distance 2, $(1,5)$ distan
ce 4. ✗
    - 2 at $(1,4)$: remaining $\{5,7\}$; pair 1 at $(5,7)$ ✓.
      Row: $2,3,4,2,1,3,1,4 \;\Rightarrow\; 23421314$.

All branches exhausted. So $N_4 = 2$, and

$$S_4 = 23421314 + 41312432 = \boxed{64733746}.$$
