# Problem

In a remote industrial warehouse, a technician is operating a specialized automated inspection drone across a grid of storage containers arranged in a 5-row by 6-column rectangular array. 

The drone has a unique mechanical constraint in its navigation software. It must alternate its movement patterns: its first move, and every subsequent odd-numbered move, must be a "Standard Leap" (moving 2 containers in one cardinal direction and 1 container perpendicularly, similar to a chess knight). Its second move, and every subsequent even-numbered move, must be a "Short Diagonal" (moving to any container that shares a single corner with its current position).

The drone's battery is highly efficient, but its memory is limited: it is strictly forbidden from landing on any container it has already visited, including the starting container of its mission.

The technician may deploy the drone onto any single container of their choice to begin the operation. What is the maximum number of moves the drone can execute before it is unable to reach a new, unvisited container?
