> **Picking $x=2$ wins for the current player iff $T+3$ is NOT a perfect square.

Similarly, picking $x=1$ wins iff $T+1$ is a perfect square.

## Critical Lemma: $T+3$ perfect square $\Rightarrow$ $T+1$ not perfect square

If $T+3 = k^2$ and $T+1 = j^2$, then $k^2 - j^2 = 2$, so $(k-j)(k+j)=2$, giving
$k=\frac{3}{2}$ — impossible. So **whenever $T+3$ is a perfect square, $T+1$ is
automatically not a perfect square**.
