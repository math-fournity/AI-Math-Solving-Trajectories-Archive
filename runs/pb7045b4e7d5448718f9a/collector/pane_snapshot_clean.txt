## Step 2: Formula for $A_n$

For coprime $p, q \geq 2$, the numerical semigroup $\langle p, q \rangle$ has:
- Number of gaps: $g = \frac{(p-1)(q-1)}{2}$
- Sum of gaps: $\frac{(p-1)(q-1)(2pq - p - q - 1)}{12}$

So the **average** of the gaps is:
$$A = \frac{2pq - p - q - 1}{6}$$

**Verification:** For $\langle 3, 7 \rangle$, gaps are $\{1,2,4,5,8,11\}$, avera
ge $= 31/6$. Formula: $(42-3-7-1)/6 = 31/6$. ✓

With $p = n$, $q = 2017$:
$$A_n = \frac{2\cdot 2017n - n - 2017 - 1}{6} = \frac{4033n - 2018}{6}$$

## Step 3: Computing the sum

\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{2^{2017k}} = \frac{1}{T-1}$$

$$S = \frac{1}{6}\left[4033\!\left(\frac{3}{2} - \frac{2017\,T}{(T-1)^2}\right)

## Step 4: Taking the floor

$$\frac{10081}{12} = 840 + \frac{1}{12}$$

The correction term $\epsilon = \frac{8132543\,T + 2018}{6(T-1)^2}$ is **positiv

$$\epsilon \approx \frac{8132543}{6 \cdot 2^{2017}} \ll \frac{1}{12}$$

$$S = 840 + \frac{1}{12} - \epsilon, \qquad 0 < \epsilon \ll \frac{1}{12}$$

$$\left\lfloor \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{A_n}{2^n} \right\rfloor = \boxed{840}$$
