## Proof

Let $R = k[[x,y,z]]$, the formal power series ring in three variables over a fie
ld $k$. This is a regular local ring, and $x, y, z$ form a regular system of par
ameters (hence a regular sequence).

We use the additivity of $\text{Tor}$ in the second argument:

$$\text{Tor}_1\!\big(R/(x),\; R/(x)\oplus R/(y,z)\big) \;\cong\; \text{Tor}_1\!\
big(R/(x), R/(x)\big) \;\oplus\; \text{Tor}_1\!\big(R/(x), R/(y,z)\big).$$

We compute each summand separately using the free resolution of $R/(x)$. Since $
x$ is a non-zerodivisor in $R$, the module $R/(x)$ has the length-one free resol
ution

$$0 \longrightarrow R \xrightarrow{\;\cdot x\;} R \longrightarrow R/(x) \longrig
htarrow 0.$$

**First summand: $\text{Tor}_1(R/(x), R/(x))$.**

Tensor the resolution with $R/(x)$:

$$R/(x) \xrightarrow{\;\cdot x\;} R/(x) \longrightarrow 0.$$

Since $x$ acts as $0$ on $R/(x)$, the differential is the zero map. Therefore

$$\text{Tor}_1\!\big(R/(x), R/(x)\big) = \ker\!\big(\cdot x : R/(x)\to R/(x)\big
) = R/(x).$$

**Second summand: $\text{Tor}_1(R/(x), R/(y,z))$.**

Tensor the resolution with $R/(y,z)$:

$$R/(y,z) \xrightarrow{\;\cdot x\;} R/(y,z) \longrightarrow 0.$$

Because $x, y, z$ is a regular sequence in $R$, the element $x$ remains a non-ze
rodivisor on $R/(y,z)$. Hence multiplication by $x$ on $R/(y,z)$ is injective, g
iving

$$\text{Tor}_1\!\big(R/(x), R/(y,z)\big) = \ker\!\big(\cdot x : R/(y,z)\to R/(y,
z)\big) = 0.$$

**Combining.**

$$\text{Tor}_1\!\big(R/(x),\; R/(x)\oplus R/(y,z)\big) \;\cong\; R/(x) \;\oplus\
; 0 \;\cong\; R/(x).$$

$$\boxed{\text{Tor}_1\!\big(R/(x),\, R/(x)\oplus R/(y,z)\big) \cong R/(x).}$$
