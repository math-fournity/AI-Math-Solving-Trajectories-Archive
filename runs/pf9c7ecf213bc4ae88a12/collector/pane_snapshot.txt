## Proof

**Setup.** On Pat's analog clock, the minute hand moves at $360°/\text{hr}$ and
the hour hand at $30°/\text{hr}$, so the minute hand gains on the hour hand at a
 relative speed of $330°/\text{hr}$. The hands lie on opposite rays (i.e., are $
180°$ apart) whenever the relative angle equals $180° + 360°k$ for some integer
$k$.

**Times of opposition.** Measuring time from 12:00 (midnight), the opposition ti
mes are

$$t_k = \frac{180 + 360k}{330}\text{ hr} = \frac{6 + 12k}{11}\text{ hr}, \qquad
k = 0, 1, 2, \ldots$$

Consecutive oppositions are separated by $\frac{12}{11}$ hours (the well-known $
\approx 65.45$ min interval).

**Departure.** They set out "after eight o'clock," so the departure is the first
 opposition time strictly after 8:00. Checking:

| $k$ | $t_k$ (hr) | Clock time |
|-----|-----------|-----------|
| 6 | $78/11 \approx 7.09$ | 7:05:27 |
| **7** | $90/11 \approx 8.18$ | **8:10:54.5** |
| 8 | $102/11 \approx 9.27$ | 9:16:21.8 |

The first opposition after 8:00 is $k=7$: $\;t_7 = \dfrac{90}{11}$ hr $\approx$
8:10:54.5.

**Return.** They returned "before noon," so the return is the last opposition ti
me strictly before 12:00. Checking:

| $k$ | $t_k$ (hr) | Clock time |
|-----|-----------|-----------|
| 9 | $114/11 \approx 10.36$ | 10:21:49.1 |
| **10** | $126/11 \approx 11.45$ | **11:27:16.4** |
| 11 | $138/11 \approx 12.55$ | 12:32:43.6 (after noon) |

The last opposition before noon is $k=10$: $\;t_{10} = \dfrac{126}{11}$ hr $\app
rox$ 11:27:16.4.

**Duration.** The trip duration, as measured by Mat's stopwatch, is

$$\Delta t = t_{10} - t_7 = \frac{126}{11} - \frac{90}{11} = \frac{36}{11}\text{
 hours}.$$

Converting to seconds:

$$\Delta t = \frac{36}{11} \times 3600 = \frac{129\,600}{11} \approx 11\,781.818
\ldots\text{ seconds}.$$

**Rounding** to the nearest integer:

$$\boxed{11\,782}\text{ seconds}.$$
