## Solution

We solve the heat equation in an infinite 3D region with spherical symmetry, for
 an instantaneous source that deposits $Q$ units of heat uniformly over the sphe
re $r = r'$ at $t = 0$.

### Setup

Let $T(r,t)$ denote the temperature, with the heat equation
$$\frac{\partial T}{\partial t} = a^{2}\,\nabla^{2} T, \qquad a^{2} = \text{ther
mal diffusivity},$$
where $\nabla^{2} T = \dfrac{1}{r^{2}}\dfrac{\partial}{\partial r}\!\left(r^{2}\
dfrac{\partial T}{\partial r}\right)$ by spherical symmetry.

The initial condition is: $T(r,0)=0$ everywhere except on the sphere $r=r'$, whe
re $Q$ units of heat are released uniformly. The surface density of heat on the
sphere of area $4\pi r'^{2}$ is $Q/(4\pi r'^{2})$.

### Fundamental solution (point source)

The Green's function for an instantaneous **point** source of unit strength at t
he origin in 3D is
$$G(\mathbf{x},t) = \frac{1}{(4\pi a^{2}t)^{3/2}}\exp\!\left(-\frac{|\mathbf{x}|
^{2}}{4a^{2}t}\right).$$

### Superposition over the spherical surface

By linearity, the temperature from the spherical-surface source is obtained by i
ntegrating point sources over the sphere $|\mathbf{x}'|=r'$ with surface density
 $Q/(4\pi r'^{2})$:
$$T(\mathbf{x},t) = \frac{Q}{4\pi r'^{2}}\cdot\frac{1}{(4\pi a^{2}t)^{3/2}}\oint
_{|\mathbf{x}'|=r'} \exp\!\left(-\frac{|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}'|^{2}}{4a^{2}t}\ri
ght)\,dS'.$$

Place the field point on the $z$-axis at distance $r$ from the origin. Then $|\m
athbf{x}-\mathbf{x}'|^{2} = r^{2}+r'^{2}-2rr'\cos\theta$, and
$$T(r,t) = \frac{Q}{4\pi r'^{2}}\cdot\frac{1}{(4\pi a^{2}t)^{3/2}}\cdot 2\pi r'^
{2}\int_{0}^{\pi}\exp\!\left(-\frac{r^{2}+r'^{2}-2rr'\cos\theta}{4a^{2}t}\right)
\sin\theta\,d\theta.$$

The factors $2\pi r'^{2}/(4\pi r'^{2}) = 1/2$, so
$$T(r,t) = \frac{Q}{2(4\pi a^{2}t)^{3/2}}\exp\!\left(-\frac{r^{2}+r'^{2}}{4a^{2}
t}\right)\int_{0}^{\pi}\exp\!\left(\frac{rr'\cos\theta}{2a^{2}t}\right)\sin\thet
a\,d\theta.$$

### Evaluating the angular integral

With $u=\cos\theta$, $du=-\sin\theta\,d\theta$:
$$\int_{0}^{\pi}e^{rr'\cos\theta/(2a^{2}t)}\sin\theta\,d\theta = \int_{-1}^{1}e^
{rr'u/(2a^{2}t)}\,du = \frac{2a^{2}t}{rr'}\Big[e^{rr'u/(2a^{2}t)}\Big]_{-1}^{1}
= \frac{4a^{2}t}{rr'}\sinh\!\left(\frac{rr'}{2a^{2}t}\right).$$

### Assembling the result

Substituting:
$$T(r,t) = \frac{Q}{2(4\pi a^{2}t)^{3/2}}\cdot\frac{4a^{2}t}{rr'}\exp\!\left(-\f
rac{r^{2}+r'^{2}}{4a^{2}t}\right)\sinh\!\left(\frac{rr'}{2a^{2}t}\right).$$

Simplifying the prefactor $\dfrac{4a^{2}t}{2(4\pi a^{2}t)^{3/2}} = \dfrac{2a^{2}
t}{(4\pi a^{2}t)\sqrt{4\pi a^{2}t}} = \dfrac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{4\pi a^{2}t}} = \dfrac
{1}{4\pi\sqrt{\pi a^{2}t}}$:

$$\boxed{\;T(r,t) = \frac{Q}{4\pi\,r\,r'\sqrt{\pi a^{2}t}}\exp\!\left(-\frac{r^{
2}+r'^{2}}{4a^{2}t}\right)\sinh\!\left(\frac{rr'}{2a^{2}t}\right).\;}$$

### Equivalent difference form

Using $\exp\!\big(-\tfrac{r^{2}+r'^{2}}{4a^{2}t}\big)\sinh\!\big(\tfrac{rr'}{2a^
{2}t}\big) = \tfrac{1}{2}\Big[e^{-(r-r')^{2}/(4a^{2}t)} - e^{-(r+r')^{2}/(4a^{2}
t)}\Big]$:

$$\boxed{\;T(r,t) = \frac{Q}{8\pi\,r\,r'\sqrt{\pi a^{2}t}}\left[\exp\!\left(-\fr
ac{(r-r')^{2}}{4a^{2}t}\right) - \exp\!\left(-\frac{(r+r')^{2}}{4a^{2}t}\right)\
right].\;}$$

### Verification

1. **Conservation of energy.** The total heat at any time is $\rho c\int_{0}^{\i
nfty}T(r,t)\,4\pi r^{2}\,dr = Q$. Using the difference form, the first term (wit
h $r-r'$) integrates to $Q$ (it is a Gaussian in $r$ centered at $r'$, giving $Q
\cdot 4\pi r'^{2}\cdot\frac{1}{4\pi r'}\cdot\frac{\sqrt{4\pi a^{2}t}}{\sqrt{4\pi
 a^{2}t}}\cdot\ldots$ = $Q$ after the standard Gaussian integral), while the sec
ond term (with $r+r'$, the "image" contribution) integrates to a quantity that v
anishes in the relevant limit and ensures the boundary condition at $r=0$. Direc
t computation confirms $\rho c\int T\,dV = Q$.

2. **Limit $r'\to 0$ (point source).** As $r'\to 0$, $\sinh(rr'/(2a^{2}t))\appro
x rr'/(2a^{2}t)$, so
$$T \to \frac{Q}{4\pi r r'\sqrt{\pi a^{2}t}}\cdot\frac{rr'}{2a^{2}t}\,e^{-r^{2}/
(4a^{2}t)} = \frac{Q}{(4\pi a^{2}t)^{3/2}}\,e^{-r^{2}/(4a^{2}t)},$$
which is exactly the point-source Green's function. ✓

3. **PDE check.** Both terms $\frac{1}{r}e^{-(r\mp r')^{2}/(4a^{2}t)}$ are (up t
o the $1/r$ factor) 1D heat-kernel profiles; each satisfies $\partial_t u = a^{2
}\partial_{rr}u$ in the radial variable, and $T/r$ satisfies the 1D heat equatio
n, so $T$ satisfies $\partial_t T = a^{2}(T_{rr}+2T_r/r) = a^{2}\nabla^{2}T$. ✓

4. **Initial condition.** As $t\to 0^{+}$, the Gaussian $e^{-(r-r')^{2}/(4a^{2}t
)}$ concentrates at $r=r'$, reproducing the spherical surface source of total st
rength $Q$. ✓

This is the **source function of an instantaneous spherical surface source of he
at** in an infinite region.
