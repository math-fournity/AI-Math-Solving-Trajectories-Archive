## Solution

**Substitution.** Let $u = nx$, so $x = u/n$, $dx = du/n$. When $x \in [0,1]$, $
u \in [0,n]$:

$$I_n = \int_0^n \frac{\cos u}{1 + \sqrt{n}\, u^{3/2}}\, du.$$

**Claim:** $I_n \to 0$.

**Estimate.** Split the integral at $u_0 = n^{-1/3}$, the point where $\sqrt{n}\
,u^{3/2} = 1$.

*Part 1* ($u \in [0, n^{-1/3}]$): Here $\sqrt{n}\,u^{3/2} \leq 1$, so the denomi
nator lies in $[1,2]$ and $|\cos u| \leq 1$:

$$\left|\int_0^{n^{-1/3}} \frac{\cos u}{1+\sqrt{n}\,u^{3/2}}\,du\right| \leq \in
t_0^{n^{-1/3}} 1\, du = n^{-1/3} \to 0.$$

*Part 2* ($u \in [n^{-1/3}, n]$): Here $\sqrt{n}\,u^{3/2} \geq 1$, so $1+\sqrt{n
}\,u^{3/2} \geq \sqrt{n}\,u^{3/2}$:

$$\left|\int_{n^{-1/3}}^{n} \frac{\cos u}{1+\sqrt{n}\,u^{3/2}}\,du\right| \leq \
int_{n^{-1/3}}^{n} \frac{du}{\sqrt{n}\, u^{3/2}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}\left[-2u^{
-1/2}\right]_{n^{-1/3}}^{n} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{n}}\left(n^{1/6} - n^{-1/2}\right)
= 2n^{-1/3} - 2n^{-1} \to 0.$$

**Combining:**

$$|I_n| \leq n^{-1/3} + 2n^{-1/3} = 3\,n^{-1/3} \xrightarrow{n\to\infty} 0.$$

Therefore

$$\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_{[0,1]}\frac{n\cos(nx)}{1+n^2 x^{3/2}}\,dx = 0.$$
