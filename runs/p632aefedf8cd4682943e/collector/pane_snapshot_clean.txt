## Solution

**Setup and analyticity.** Let $g(z) = \log\frac{z+1}{z-1}$, where $\log$ is the
 principal branch (cut along $\{w:\operatorname{Re} w \le 0\}$, with $-\pi < \op
eratorname{Im}(\log w) < \pi$). The contour $|z|=2$ is taken with positive (coun
terclockwise) orientation.

For any $z$ with $|z|>1$ (and $z\neq 1$), writing $z=x+iy$:
$$
\frac{z+1}{z-1} = \frac{(z+1)(\bar z-1)}{|z-1|^2} = \frac{|z|^2-1-2iy}{|z-1|^2},
\qquad
\operatorname{Re}\!\left(\frac{z+1}{z-1}\right)=\frac{|z|^2-1}{|z-1|^2}>0.
$$
Hence $\frac{z+1}{z-1}$ lies in the right half-plane (strictly away from the bra
nch cut) for all $|z|>1$. In particular $g$ is analytic on the contour $|z|=2$ a
nd, more generally, on the entire region $\{z:|z|>1\}$, including at $z=\infty$
where $g(\infty)=\log 1 = 0$.

**Contour deformation.** Since $g$ is analytic on the annulus $1<|z|\le\infty$,
the contour $|z|=2$ is homologous to $|z|=R$ (for any $R>2$) within this annulus
. By Cauchy's theorem,
$$
\int_{|z|=2} g(z)\,dz = \int_{|z|=R} g(z)\,dz.
$$

**Laurent expansion at infinity.** For $|z|$ large, set $w = \tfrac{2}{z-1}$ (so
 $|w|<1$):
$$
\frac{z+1}{z-1} = 1 + \frac{2}{z-1} = 1+w,
\qquad
g(z) = \log(1+w) = w - \frac{w^2}{2} + \frac{w^3}{3}-\cdots.
$$
Thus
$$
g(z) = \frac{2}{z-1} - \frac{2}{(z-1)^2} + \frac{8}{3(z-1)^3}-\cdots.
$$
Expanding each term in powers of $1/z$ using $\frac{1}{z-1}=\frac{1}{z}+\frac{1}
{z^2}+\frac{1}{z^3}+\cdots$ and $\frac{1}{(z-1)^2}=\frac{1}{z^2}+\frac{2}{z^3}+\
cdots$:
$$
g(z) = 2\!\left(\frac{1}{z}+\frac{1}{z^2}+\cdots\right) - 2\!\left(\frac{1}{z^2}
+\cdots\right) + O(z^{-3})
     = \frac{2}{z} + O(z^{-3}).
$$
So the coefficient of $z^{-1}$ in the Laurent expansion of $g$ at $\infty$ is $a
_{-1}=2$ (the $z^{-2}$ terms cancel).

**Evaluating the integral.** On $|z|=R$ (counterclockwise), only the $a_{-1}/z$
term contributes:
$$
\int_{|z|=R} g(z)\,dz = 2\pi i\cdot a_{-1} = 2\pi i \cdot 2 = 4\pi i.
$$

By the deformation equality, the original integral has the same value:
$$
\boxed{\int_{|z|=2}\log\frac{z+1}{z-1}\,dz = 4\pi i.}
$$
