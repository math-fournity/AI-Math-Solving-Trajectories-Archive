# Problem

A high-tech microchip is designed as a 5x5 grid of 25 logic gates. A specialized cooling component, which is 3 units long and 1 unit wide, must be installed repeatedly onto the chip. 

An engineer must place exactly eight of these 3x1 cooling components onto the grid such that they do not overlap. The components must be aligned perfectly with the grid lines, though they can be oriented either horizontally or vertically. After placing all eight components, exactly one logic gate (a 1x1 subsquare) will remain uncovered.

A "configuration" is defined by the specific layout of the eight components and the resulting location of the single empty gate. How many distinct configurations are possible? (Note: Rotations and reflections of the layout are considered distinct).
