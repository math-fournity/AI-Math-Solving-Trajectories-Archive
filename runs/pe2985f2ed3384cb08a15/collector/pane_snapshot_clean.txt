## Proof

Let $\xi_n$ denote a primitive $n$-th root of unity, and let $K = \mathbb{Q}[\xi
_n]$ be the $n$-th cyclotomic field.

### Step 1. The Galois group is $(\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z})^{\times}$

The minimal polynomial of $\xi_n$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ is the $n$-th cyclotomic pol
ynomial $\Phi_n(x)$, which has degree $\varphi(n)$. Hence $:\mathbb{Q}] = \var
phi(n)$, and $K/\mathbb{Q}$ is a Galois extension (it is the splitting field of
$x^n - 1$ over $\mathbb{Q}$).

Every $\mathbb{Q}$-automorphism $\sigma$ of $K$ is uniquely determined by $\sigm
a(\xi_n)$, which must be a root of $\Phi_n(x)$. The roots of $\Phi_n(x)$ are pre
cisely the primitive $n$-th roots of unity:
$$\{\xi_n^{\ell} : 1 \le \ell \le n,\ \gcd(\ell, n) = 1\}.$$

Therefore every automorphism has the form $\sigma_{\ell}$ for a unique $\ell$ wi
th $\gcd(\ell,n)=1$, where
$$\sigma_{\ell}(\xi_n) = \xi_n^{\ell}, \qquad \sigma_{\ell}(z) = a_0 + a_1\xi_n^
{\ell} + \cdots + a_k \xi_n^{\ell k} \text{ for } z = a_0 + a_1\xi_n + \cdots +
a_k\xi_n^k.$$

There are exactly $\varphi(n)$ such automorphisms, matching $:\mathbb{Q}]$, so
$$\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q}) = \{\sigma_{\ell} : \gcd(\ell, n) = 1\}.$$

The composition law is $\sigma_{\ell_1}\circ\sigma_{\ell_2} = \sigma_{\ell_1 \el
l_2 \bmod n}$, since
$$(\sigma_{\ell_1}\circ\sigma_{\ell_2})(\xi_n) = \sigma_{\ell_1}(\xi_n^{\ell_2})
 = \xi_n^{\ell_1\ell_2}.$$

Thus the map $\sigma_{\ell} \mapsto \ell \bmod n$ is a group isomorphism
$$\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}[\xi_n]/\mathbb{Q}) \cong (\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}
)^{\times}.$$

### Step 2. Cyclicity and primitive roots

The group $(\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z})^{\times}$ is cyclic if and only if
$$n \in \{1,\ 2,\ 4,\ p^{k},\ 2p^{k}\}$$
where $p$ is an odd prime and $k \ge 1$ (the classical theorem on the existence
of primitive roots). In these cases, a **primitive root modulo $n$** is an integ
er $g$ with $\gcd(g,n)=1$ and multiplicative order $\operatorname{ord}_n(g) = \v
arphi(n)$, i.e., $g$ generates $(\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z})^{\times}$.

### Step 3. The generator of the Galois group

**Claim.** When $(\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z})^{\times}$ is cyclic, a generator of $\
operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}[\xi_n]/\mathbb{Q})$ is $\sigma_g$, where $g$ is any
 primitive root modulo $n$.

*Proof of claim.* We compute the order of $\sigma_g$ in the Galois group. For an
y $m \ge 1$:
$$\sigma_g^{m}(\xi_n) = \xi_n^{g^{m}}.$$
This equals $\xi_n$ (i.e., $\sigma_g^m = \mathrm{id}$) if and only if $g^{m} \eq
uiv 1 \pmod{n}$, which holds if and only if $\operatorname{ord}_n(g) \mid m$. Si
nce $\operatorname{ord}_n(g) = \varphi(n)$, we obtain
$$\operatorname{ord}(\sigma_g) = \varphi(n) = \left|\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb{Q
}[\xi_n]/\mathbb{Q})\right|.$$

Hence $\sigma_g$ generates the entire Galois group. $\square$
