The adjacency matrix $A$ (restricted to vertices $\neq x$) decomposes into four
blocks:

n \Gamma(x)$ has exactly $\lambda$ neighbors within $\Gamma(x)$ (since $v$ is ad
jacent to $x$, and the SRG parameter $\lambda$ counts common neighbors of adjace

- $D = A[\bar{\Gamma}(x), \bar{\Gamma}(x)]$: the induced subgraph on $\bar{\Gamm
a}(x)$. Each $v \in \bar{\Gamma}(x)$ has $\mu$ neighbors in $\Gamma(x)$ (common
neighbors with $x$), so $k - \mu$ neighbors in $\bar{\Gamma}(x)$. Thus $D$ is $(
k-\mu)$-regular.
- $E = A[\Gamma(x), \bar{\Gamma}(x)]$: the $k \times (n-1-k)$ bipartite adjacenc

**The algorithm to sort $C$.**

a canonical ordering $\sigma_D$ of $\bar{\Gamma}(x)$ in $O(K)$ time.

$$\text{sig}(v) = \big(A[v, \sigma_D(w)]\big)_{w \in \bar{\Gamma}(x)} \in \{0, 1

This is the row of $E$ corresponding to $v$, with columns ordered by $\sigma_D$.
 Computing all $k$ signatures requires $O(k \cdot (n-1-k))$ time.

**Step 3 (Sort by signatures).** By assumption 3, every row of $E$ is a distinct

$$f(n) = O\big(k \cdot (n - 1 - k) \cdot \log k\big) = O(n^2 \log n),$$

$$O\big(n \cdot K \cdot f(n)\big) = O\big(n \cdot K \cdot n^2 \log n\big) = O(n^
