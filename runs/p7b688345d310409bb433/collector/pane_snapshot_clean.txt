## Solution

We determine the Galois group of $f(T) = T^3 - 2$ over $\mathbb{F}_5$ by factori

**Step 1: Find roots in $\mathbb{F}_5$.**

Compute cubes modulo 5:
- $1^3 \equiv 1$
- $2^3 = 8 \equiv 3$
- $3^3 = 27 \equiv 2$
- $4^3 = 64 \equiv 4$

Since $3^3 \equiv 2 \pmod{5}$, the element $3 \in \mathbb{F}_5$ is a root of $T^

$$T^3 - 2 = (T-3)(T^2 + 3T + 9) = (T-3)(T^2 + 3T + 4),$$

$$\Delta = 3^2 - 4\cdot 4 = 9 - 16 = -7 \equiv 3 \pmod{5}.$$

The quadratic residues in $\mathbb{F}_5^\times$ are $\{1, 4\}$. Since $3 \notin
\{1,4\}$, $3$ is not a square in $\mathbb{F}_5$, so $T^2 + 3T + 4$ is irreducibl

**Step 4: Identify the splitting field.**

Let $\alpha$ be a root of $T^2 + 3T + 4$. Then $\mathbb{F}_5(\alpha) \cong \math
bb{F}_{25}$. The two roots of the quadratic are $\alpha$ and $\alpha^5$ (Frobeni

athbb{F}_5$, all three roots of $T^3 - 2$ lie in $\mathbb{F}_{25}$, and no prope

. Hence the splitting field is $\mathbb{F}_{25}$.

**Step 5: Determine the Galois group.**

The extension $\mathbb{F}_{25}/\mathbb{F}_5$ has degree 2, and every finite exte

$$\operatorname{Gal}(T^3 - 2 / \mathbb{F}_5) = \operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb{F}_{25
}/\mathbb{F}_5) \cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}.$$

The nontrivial element fixes the root $3 \in \mathbb{F}_5$ and swaps the two roo
