**Recurrence.** For $0 \le k < 60$, on the next toss:
- With probability $\tfrac12$ we get heads: run becomes $k+1$, costing $1 + E_{k
+1}$.
- With probability $\tfrac12$ we get tails: run resets to $0$, costing $1 + E_0$

Hence
$$E_k = 1 + \tfrac12 E_{k+1} + \tfrac12 E_0, \qquad E_{60}=0.$$

$$E_{k+1} = 2E_k - c.$$
