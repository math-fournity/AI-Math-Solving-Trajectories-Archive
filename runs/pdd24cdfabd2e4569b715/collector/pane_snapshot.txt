## Solution

The PDE is $z_x \cdot z_y = 1$, i.e., $pq = 1$ where $p = \partial z/\partial x$
, $q = \partial z/\partial y$. This is a first-order PDE of the form $F(p,q)=0$
(no explicit dependence on $x, y, z$).

### Complete Integral

Since $F(p,q) = pq - 1 = 0$, both $p$ and $q$ are constants along characteristic
s (Charpit's equations give $\dot{p} = \dot{q} = 0$). Setting $p = a$ (constant)
, we get $q = 1/a$, and integrating:

$$z = ax + \frac{y}{a} + c.$$

Applying $z(0,0) = 0$ gives $c = 0$, so the one-parameter family of solutions th
rough the origin is:

$$z = ax + \frac{y}{a}, \quad a \neq 0.$$

### Singular Solution (Characteristic Cone from the Point)

With only a **point** initial condition (not a curve), the natural solution is t
he **envelope** of the complete-integral family — the characteristic cone emanat
ing from $(0,0,0)$. We obtain it by eliminating $a$ via $\partial z/\partial a =
 0$:

$$\frac{\partial z}{\partial a} = x - \frac{y}{a^2} = 0 \implies a^2 = \frac{y}{
x} \implies a = \sqrt{\frac{y}{x}}.$$

Substituting back:

$$z = \sqrt{\frac{y}{x}} \cdot x + \frac{y}{\sqrt{y/x}} = \sqrt{xy} + \sqrt{xy}
= 2\sqrt{xy}.$$

### Verification

$$\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = \sqrt{\frac{y}{x}}, \qquad \frac{\partial z}{\
partial y} = \sqrt{\frac{x}{y}}.$$

$$\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} \cdot \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = \sqrt{\fra
c{y}{x}} \cdot \sqrt{\frac{x}{y}} = 1. \quad \checkmark$$

$$z(0,0) = 2\sqrt{0 \cdot 0} = 0. \quad \checkmark$$

### Result

$$z(0,1) = 2\sqrt{0 \cdot 1} = 2\sqrt{0} = 0.$$

$$\boxed{z(0,1) = 0}$$
