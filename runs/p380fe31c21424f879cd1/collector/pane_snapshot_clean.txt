$$I_n = n\int_0^{\pi/(4n)}\tan^n u\,du = n\int_0^{\pi/(4n)} u^n\left(\frac{\tan
u}{u}\right)^n du.$$

**Step 2 — Uniform control of $(\tan u/u)^n$.** For small $u\ge 0$ the Taylor ex
pansion gives
$$\frac{\tan u}{u} = 1 + \frac{u^2}{3} + O(u^4),\qquad \log\frac{\tan u}{u} = \f
rac{u^2}{3} + O(u^4).$$
Hence for all sufficiently small $u$, $0\le \log(\tan u/u)\le u^2$. On the inter
val $[0,\pi/(4n)]$ we have $u\le \pi/(4n)$, so
$$0\le n\log\frac{\tan u}{u}\le n\cdot\frac{\pi^2}{16n^2}=\frac{\pi^2}{16n}\xrig
htarrow[n\to\infty]{}0.$$
Therefore $(\tan u/u)^n\to 1$ **uniformly** on $[0,\pi/(4n)]$, with
$$1\le\left(\frac{\tan u}{u}\right)^n\le e^{\pi^2/(16n)}=1+o(1).$$

**Step 3 — Asymptotic for $I_n$.** Using the uniform squeeze,
$$I_n = n\bigl(1+o(1)\bigr)\int_0^{\pi/(4n)}u^n\,du = n\bigl(1+o(1)\bigr)\cdot\f
rac{(\pi/(4n))^{n+1}}{n+1}.$$
Thus
$$I_n = \bigl(1+o(1)\bigr)\cdot\frac{n}{n+1}\cdot\left(\frac{\pi}{4n}\right)^{n+
1}.$$

$$I_n^{1/n}=\bigl(1+o(1)\bigr)^{1/n}\cdot\left(\frac{n}{n+1}\right)^{1/n}\cdot\l
eft(\frac{\pi}{4n}\right)^{(n+1)/n}.$$

- $(n/(n+1))^{1/n}\to 1$,

ac{\pi}{4n}\cdot\left(\frac{\pi}{4n}\right)^{1/n}\to \frac{\pi}{4n}\cdot 1$,

$$I_n^{1/n}\sim \frac{\pi}{4n},\qquad\text{so}\qquad n\,I_n^{1/n}\xrightarrow[n\
