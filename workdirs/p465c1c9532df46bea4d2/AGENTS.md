# Problem

In a futuristic circular city, there are $n$ modular housing units arranged in a perfect circle, forming the vertices of a regular $n$-gon. The city council has decided to designate exactly 5 of these units as "Solar Hubs" (red vertices), while the remaining $n-5$ units stay as standard residences.

The city’s power grid is designed around "Equilibrium Lines." An Equilibrium Line is defined as any line of symmetry of the $n$-gon formed by the housing units. For the grid to remain stable, the city must be able to choose at least one Equilibrium Line such that every single Solar Hub is reflected across that line to a unit that is currently a standard residence.

Find the smallest natural number $n$ such that, no matter which 5 units are chosen to be Solar Hubs, there will always exist at least one Equilibrium Line that maps every Solar Hub to a non-solar unit.
