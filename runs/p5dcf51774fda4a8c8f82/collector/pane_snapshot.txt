## Proof

Let $\mathcal{F} = \bigoplus_{n=0}^{\infty} \bigwedge^n H$ be the fermionic Fock
 space over a Hilbert space $H$, with vacuum vector $\Omega \in \bigwedge^0 H \c
ong \mathbb{C}$. The annihilation operator $c(f)$ is defined on $\bigwedge^n H$
by

$$c(f)(f_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge f_n) = \sum_{k=1}^{n} (-1)^{k-1} \langle f, f_k
\rangle\, f_1 \wedge \cdots \widehat{f_k} \cdots \wedge f_n,$$

and $c(f)\,\Omega = 0$. Its adjoint is the creation operator $c(f)^\dagger = c^\
dagger(f)$, acting as

$$c^\dagger(f)(f_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge f_n) = f \wedge f_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge
 f_n.$$

These satisfy the **canonical anticommutation relations (CAR)**:

$$\{c(f),\, c^\dagger(g)\} = \langle f, g\rangle\, I, \qquad \{c(f),\, c(g)\} =
0, \qquad \{c^\dagger(f),\, c^\dagger(g)\} = 0.$$

---

### Step 1. Boundedness from above: $\|c(f)\| \leq \|f\|$

Setting $g = f$ in the CAR gives

$$c(f)\,c^\dagger(f) + c^\dagger(f)\,c(f) = \|f\|^2\, I.$$

Both $c^\dagger(f)\,c(f) = c(f)^\dagger c(f)$ and $c(f)\,c^\dagger(f) = c(f)\,c(
f)^\dagger$ are positive operators. Hence for every $\psi \in \mathcal{F}$,

$$\langle \psi,\, c^\dagger(f)\,c(f)\,\psi\rangle \;\leq\; \langle \psi,\, \|f\|
^2 I\,\psi\rangle \;=\; \|f\|^2\,\|\psi\|^2.$$

The left-hand side equals $\|c(f)\,\psi\|^2$, so

$$\|c(f)\,\psi\| \;\leq\; \|f\|\,\|\psi\| \qquad \forall\,\psi \in \mathcal{F},$
$

which gives $\|c(f)\| \leq \|f\|$.

---

### Step 2. The bound is attained: $\|c(f)\| \geq \|f\|$

Consider the unit vector $\psi = \dfrac{c^\dagger(f)\,\Omega}{\|f\|}$ (assuming
$f \neq 0$; the case $f=0$ is trivial). First verify it is a unit vector:

$$\|c^\dagger(f)\,\Omega\|^2 = \langle \Omega,\, c(f)\,c^\dagger(f)\,\Omega\rang
le = \langle \Omega,\, (\|f\|^2 I - c^\dagger(f)\,c(f))\,\Omega\rangle = \|f\|^2
,$$

since $c(f)\,\Omega = 0$ implies $c^\dagger(f)\,c(f)\,\Omega = 0$.

Now apply $c(f)$:

$$c(f)\,\psi \;=\; \frac{c(f)\,c^\dagger(f)\,\Omega}{\|f\|} \;=\; \frac{(\|f\|^2
 I - c^\dagger(f)\,c(f))\,\Omega}{\|f\|} \;=\; \frac{\|f\|^2\,\Omega}{\|f\|} \;=
\; \|f\|\,\Omega.$$

Therefore

$$\|c(f)\,\psi\| = \|f\|\,\|\Omega\| = \|f\| = \|f\|\,\|\psi\|,$$

so the supremum is achieved and $\|c(f)\| \geq \|f\|$.

---

### Conclusion

Combining both inequalities,

$$\boxed{\|c(f)\| = \|f\|.}$$

The annihilation operator $c(f)$ is a **bounded** linear operator on the fermion
ic Fock space, with norm equal to $\|f\|$.
