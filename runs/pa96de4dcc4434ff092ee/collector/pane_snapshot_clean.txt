## Setup as Graph Theory

j)$ with $i<j$ is an edge, and each $(i,i)$ is a self-loop. A row of dominoes wi

The full graph has $\frac{n(n+1)}{2}$ edges. Each vertex $i$ has degree $(n-1)+2

## Parity Analysis After Amin's Trail

**Key fact:** Removing a trail from $a$ to $v$ changes degrees as follows: every

- **Open trail** ($a \neq v$): $G'$ has exactly **2 odd-degree vertices**: $a$ a

- **Closed trail** ($a = v$): $G'$ has **0 odd-degree vertices**.

## When Can Anton Complete?

Anton must form an Eulerian trail in $G'$ starting from $v$. This requires:
1. All edges of $G'$ lie in one connected component, **and**
2. Either ($G'$ has 0 odd vertices and $\deg_{G'}(v) > 0$) or ($G'$ has exactly
2 odd vertices and $v$ is one of them).

**Open trail case:** $G'$ has 2 odd vertices $a, v$ with $v$ among them, and $v$

G'$ being disconnected** (with edges in multiple components).

**Closed trail case:** $G'$ has 0 odd vertices. Obstruction arises if $G'$ is di

## Lower Bound: Connectivity Obstruction
