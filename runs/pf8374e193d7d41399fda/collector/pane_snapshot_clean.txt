- **$i = j$:** $(D_a)_{ii} \ne 0$ for all $a \in \{1,\dots,n\}$.
- **$i \ne j$:** $(D_a)_{ij} = 0$ for at least one $a$.

$$P_{ij} = \prod_{a=1}^n (D_a)_{ij}.$$

- For $i \ne j$: at least one factor is zero, so $P_{ij} = 0$.
