 has bad reduction at } p\} \cup \{\ell\}$. For a prime $p \notin S$, the reduct
ion $\widetilde{E}/\mathbb{F}_p$ is a nonsingular elliptic curve.

**The $\ell$-adic Tate module and the Galois representation.** The $\ell$-adic T
ate module is
$$T_\ell E = \varprojlim_n E[\ell^n](\overline{\mathbb{Q}}),$$
a free $\mathbb{Z}_\ell$-module of rank $2$. The action of $G_{\mathbb{Q}} = \op

gives a continuous representation
$$\rho_\ell : G_{\mathbb{Q}} \to \operatorname{Aut}(T_\ell E) \cong \operatornam
e{GL}_2(\mathbb{Z}_\ell),$$
and after tensoring with $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$, a $2$-dimensional $\ell$-adic Galois

**Unramifiedness outside $S$.** For $p \notin S$ (so $p \neq \ell$ and $E$ has g

$$E[\ell^n](\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_p) \xrightarrow{\;\sim\;} \widetilde{E}[\ell^n

This isomorphism is $G_{\mathbb{Q}_p$-equivariant, where $G_{\mathbb{Q}_p$ acts
on the right-hand side through the quotient $D_p \to \operatorname{Gal}(\overlin

$$T_\ell E \cong T_\ell \widetilde{E}$$

**The characteristic polynomial of Frobenius.** The Frobenius endomorphism $\var
phi_p \in \operatorname{End}(\widetilde{E})$ satisfies the quadratic relation
$$\varphi_p^2 - (\operatorname{tr}\varphi_p)\,\varphi_p + (\deg \varphi_p) = 0,$
$
where $\deg \varphi_p = p$ (Frobenius has degree $p$) and $\operatorname{tr}\var

$$N_p(E) = \#\widetilde{E}(\mathbb{F}_p) = \#\ker(1 - \varphi_p) = \deg(1 - \var

$$a_p = p + 1 - (p + 1 - \operatorname{tr}\varphi_p) = \operatorname{tr}\varphi_
