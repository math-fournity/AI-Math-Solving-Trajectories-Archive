## Setup and Reduction

Let $V = \begin{pmatrix} V_1 \\ V_2 \end{pmatrix}$ be the block decomposition of
 $V$ conformal with the block structure of $[A, B]$, so that

$$[aA,\, bB]\,V = a\,AV_1 + b\,BV_2.$$

Set $U_1 := AV_1$, $\;U_2 := BV_2$, and $g(a,b) := a\,U_1 + b\,U_2$. Then

$$[aA,\,bB]\,V\,V^*\,[aA,\,bB]^* = g\,g^*,$$

so the function of interest is

$$f(a,b) = \det\!\big(I + g(a,b)\,g(a,b)^*\big).$$

Since $g\,g^* \succeq 0$, we have $I + g\,g^* \succ 0$ and $f \geq 1 > 0$.

---

## Computing the Partial Derivatives

By **Jacobi's formula**,

$$\frac{\partial f}{\partial a} = f\;\operatorname{tr}\!\Big((I + gg^*)^{-1}\,\f
rac{\partial(gg^*)}{\partial a}\Big),$$

with

$$\frac{\partial(gg^*)}{\partial a} = U_1 g^* + g\,U_1^* = 2a\,U_1 U_1^* + b\,(U
_1 U_2^* + U_2 U_1^*).$$

Similarly,

$$\frac{\partial(gg^*)}{\partial b} = 2b\,U_2 U_2^* + a\,(U_1 U_2^* + U_2 U_1^*)
.$$

Define the **Hermitian cross-term matrix**

$$\boxed{H \;:=\; U_1 U_2^* + U_2 U_1^* \;=\; AV_1 V_2^* B^* + BV_2 V_1^* A^*.}$
$

Note $U_1 U_1^* \succeq 0$ and $U_2 U_2^* \succeq 0$ always, while $H$ is Hermit
ian but **not** automatically PSD.

Since $f > 0$, the sign of each partial derivative is governed by the trace. The
 matrix $(I + gg^*)^{-1}$ is **positive definite** (because $gg^* \succeq 0 \Rig
htarrow I + gg^* \succeq I$).

---

## The Condition and Proof of Sufficiency

**Condition on $V$:**

$$\boxed{AV_1\,V_2^*\,B^* + BV_2\,V_1^*\,A^* \;\succeq\; 0,}$$

i.e., the Hermitian part of $(AV_1)(BV_2)^*$ is positive semidefinite.

**Proof that this is sufficient.** Assume $H \succeq 0$. Then:

$$\frac{\partial(gg^*)}{\partial a} = \underbrace{2a\,U_1 U_1^*}_{\succeq\, 0} +
 \underbrace{b\,H}_{\succeq\, 0} \;\succeq\; 0,$$

$$\frac{\partial(gg^*)}{\partial b} = \underbrace{2b\,U_2 U_2^*}_{\succeq\, 0} +
 \underbrace{a\,H}_{\succeq\, 0} \;\succeq\; 0.$$

**Key fact:** For any positive definite $P$ and positive semidefinite $Q$,

$$\operatorname{tr}(P\,Q) \;\geq\; 0.$$

*Proof of key fact:* $P \succ 0$ has a PD square root $P^{1/2}$, so $\operatorna
me{tr}(PQ) = \operatorname{tr}(P^{1/2} Q P^{1/2}) \geq 0$ since $P^{1/2} Q P^{1/
2} \succeq 0$.

Applying this with $P = (I + gg^*)^{-1} \succ 0$ and $Q = \frac{\partial(gg^*)}{
\partial a} \succeq 0$ (resp. $Q = \frac{\partial(gg^*)}{\partial b} \succeq 0$)
:

$$\frac{\partial f}{\partial a} = f\;\operatorname{tr}\!\Big((I+gg^*)^{-1}\,\fra
c{\partial(gg^*)}{\partial a}\Big) \;\geq\; 0,$$

$$\frac{\partial f}{\partial b} = f\;\operatorname{tr}\!\Big((I+gg^*)^{-1}\,\fra
c{\partial(gg^*)}{\partial b}\Big) \;\geq\; 0.$$

Since $f > 0$ and both traces are non-negative, both partial derivatives are non
-negative for all $a, b > 0$. Therefore $f(a,b)$ is **increasing in both $a$ and
 $b$.** $\quad\square$

---

## Necessity in the Scalar Case

When all quantities are scalars ($A, B, V_1, V_2 \in \mathbb{C}$), the condition
 becomes $\operatorname{Re}(A\overline{B}\,V_1\overline{V_2}) \geq 0$, and this
is **also necessary**:

$$\frac{\partial f}{\partial a}\Big|_{a \to 0^+} = 2b\,\operatorname{Re}(U_1 \ov
erline{U_2}) = 2b\,\operatorname{Re}(A\overline{B}\,V_1\overline{V_2}),$$

so non-negativity for all $b > 0$ forces $\operatorname{Re}(A\overline{B}\,V_1\o
verline{V_2}) \geq 0$, which is exactly $H \geq 0$.

---

## Summary

The determinant $\det\!\big(I + [aA,\,bB]\,VV^*\,[aA,\,bB]^*\big)$ is increasing
 in both positive real parameters $a$ and $b$ provided that

$$\boxed{AV_1\,V_2^*\,B^* \;+\; BV_2\,V_1^*\,A^* \;\succeq\; 0,}$$

where $V = \begin{pmatrix} V_1 \\ V_2 \end{pmatrix}$ is partitioned conformally
with $[A, B]$. Equivalently, the Hermitian part of $(AV_1)(BV_2)^*$ must be posi
tive semidefinite. This condition is sufficient in general and necessary and suf
ficient in the scalar case.
