# Problem

A high-tech automated warehouse has 7 docking bays, each reserved for a specific delivery drone. The 7 drones arrive at the warehouse one by one to recharge. 

The first drone to arrive experiences a sensor malfunction and docks in a bay that is not its own (it chooses one of the other 6 bays at random). For every subsequent drone that arrives, the protocol is as follows: if its assigned docking bay is empty, it docks there; if its assigned bay is already occupied, it chooses one of the remaining empty bays uniformly at random.

You are monitoring the 7th and final drone as it enters the warehouse. What is the probability that this last drone docks in its own assigned bay?
