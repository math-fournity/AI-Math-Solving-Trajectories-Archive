## Setup

We count the number of orbits of proper face-colorings of a cube (6 colors avail
able, adjacent faces must differ) under the rotation group of the cube, which ha

**Face adjacency graph.** Label faces Top (T), Bottom (B), Front (F), Back (K),
Left (L), Right (R). Two faces are adjacent iff they share an edge. The only non
-adjacent pairs are the three opposite pairs: (T,B), (F,K), (L,R). So the adjace
ncy graph is K₆ minus a perfect matching M = {TB, FK, LR}.

## Burnside's Lemma

Number of distinct colorings = (1/|G|) Σ_g |Fix(g)|, where Fix(g) = proper color
ings fixed by rotation g.

The 24 rotations fall into 5 conjugacy classes:

### Class 1: Identity — |Fix| = total proper colorings

We count proper 6-colorings of K₆ − M. Condition on whether T = B:

**Subcase A (T = B = color c):** F, K, L, R must all differ from c (each adjacen
t to both T and B), leaving 5 colors. The remaining constraint graph on {F, K, L
, R} has edges FL, FR, KL, KR — this is C₄ (the 4-cycle), with FK and LR being n
on-edges. Chromatic polynomial of C₄: (k−1)⁴ + (k−1). For k = 5: 4⁴ + 4 = 260. C
hoices for c: 6. **Total: 6 × 260 = 1560.**

**Subcase B (T ≠ B):** T = a, B = b, a ≠ b (30 ordered choices). F, K, L, R must
 differ from both a and b, leaving 4 colors. Same C₄ constraint graph. For k = 4
: 3⁴ + 3 = 84. **Total: 30 × 84 = 2520.**

**|Fix(identity)| = 1560 + 2520 = 4080.**

### Class 3: 180° face rotation — |Fix| = 480

Rotation about the T–B axis: T, B fixed; F↔K, L↔R. Fixed coloring requires F = K
 and L = R.

Constraints: F ≠ L (adjacent), Top ≠ F, Top ≠ L, Bottom ≠ F, Bottom ≠ L (Top/Bot

Count: 6 (F) × 5 (L, ≠F) × 4 (Top, ≠F, ≠L) × 4 (Bottom, ≠F, ≠L) = **480.**

### Class 4: 120° vertex rotation — |Fix| = 0

Axis through opposite vertices. Faces partition into two 3-cycles, e.g., (T, F,

### Class 5: 180° edge rotation — |Fix| = 0

Axis through midpoints of opposite edges. This swaps the two faces sharing each
axis-edge; those faces are adjacent, so forced equality contradicts properness.
**|Fix| = 0.**
