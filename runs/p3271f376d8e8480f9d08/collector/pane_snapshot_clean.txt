## Solution

We compute the operator norm of $A(f)(x) = \int_0^\pi \sin(x-y)\,f(y)\,dy$ on $L
^2([0,\pi])$.

**Step 1: Decompose the kernel.** Using the identity $\sin(x-y) = \sin x\cos y -
 \cos x\sin y$,

$$A(f)(x) = \sin x\int_0^\pi \cos y\,f(y)\,dy \;-\; \cos x\int_0^\pi \sin y\,f(y
)\,dy.$$

Denoting $\langle f,g\rangle = \int_0^\pi f(y)g(y)\,dy$, this is

$$A(f) = \sin x\,\langle f,\cos\rangle - \cos x\,\langle f,\sin\rangle.$$

So $A$ has finite rank (at most 2), with range contained in $\operatorname{span}
\{\sin x,\cos x\}$.

**Step 2: Compute the relevant inner products.**

$$\langle\sin,\sin\rangle = \int_0^\pi \sin^2 y\,dy = \frac{\pi}{2},\qquad \lang
le\cos,\cos\rangle = \int_0^\pi \cos^2 y\,dy = \frac{\pi}{2},$$

$$\langle\sin,\cos\rangle = \int_0^\pi \sin y\cos y\,dy = \tfrac12\int_0^\pi \si
n 2y\,dy = \tfrac14[-\cos 2y]_0^\pi = 0.$$

Thus $\sin x \perp \cos x$ in $L^2([0,\pi])$, each with squared norm $\pi/2$.

**Step 3: Compute $A^*A$.** From the expression for $A$,

$$\langle Af, g\rangle = \langle f,\cos\rangle\langle\sin,g\rangle - \langle f,\
sin\rangle\langle\cos,g\rangle = \big\langle f,\;\langle g,\sin\rangle\cos - \la
ngle g,\cos\rangle\sin\big\rangle,$$

so $A^*(g) = \langle g,\sin\rangle\cos - \langle g,\cos\rangle\sin$. Applying th
is to $Af = \sin\,\langle f,\cos\rangle - \cos\,\langle f,\sin\rangle$:

$$\langle Af,\sin\rangle = \langle f,\cos\rangle\langle\sin,\sin\rangle - \langl
e f,\sin\rangle\langle\cos,\sin\rangle = \tfrac{\pi}{2}\langle f,\cos\rangle,$$

$$\langle Af,\cos\rangle = \langle f,\cos\rangle\langle\sin,\cos\rangle - \langl
e f,\sin\rangle\langle\cos,\cos\rangle = -\tfrac{\pi}{2}\langle f,\sin\rangle.$$

Therefore

$$A^*A(f) = \langle Af,\sin\rangle\cos - \langle Af,\cos\rangle\sin = \tfrac{\pi
}{2}\langle f,\cos\rangle\cos + \tfrac{\pi}{2}\langle f,\sin\rangle\sin.$$

**Step 4: Identify the eigenvalues.** Let $P$ be the orthogonal projection onto
$\operatorname{span}\{\sin,\cos\}$. Since $\sin\perp\cos$ with $\|\sin\|^2=\|\co
s\|^2=\pi/2$,

$$P(f) = \frac{\langle f,\sin\rangle}{\pi/2}\sin + \frac{\langle f,\cos\rangle}{
\pi/2}\cos = \tfrac{2}{\pi}\big(\langle f,\sin\rangle\sin + \langle f,\cos\rangl
e\cos\big).$$

Comparing with Step 3,

$$A^*A(f) = \tfrac{\pi}{2}\big(\langle f,\cos\rangle\cos + \langle f,\sin\rangle
\sin\big) = \tfrac{\pi}{2}\cdot\tfrac{\pi}{2}\,P(f) = \tfrac{\pi^2}{4}\,P(f).$$

Hence $A^*A = \frac{\pi^2}{4}P$, whose eigenvalues are $\frac{\pi^2}{4}$ (on $\o
peratorname{span}\{\sin,\cos\}$) and $0$ (on the orthogonal complement).

**Step 5: Conclude.** The operator norm of a self-adjoint compact operator equal
s the square root of the largest eigenvalue of $A^*A$:

$$\|A\| = \sqrt{\frac{\pi^2}{4}} = \frac{\pi}{2}.$$

*Verification.* Taking $f(x)=\sin x$: $Af = \sin\,\langle\sin,\cos\rangle - \cos
\,\langle\sin,\sin\rangle = -\frac{\pi}{2}\cos x$. Then $\|Af\|^2 = \frac{\pi^2}
{4}\cdot\frac{\pi}{2} = \frac{\pi^3}{8}$ and $\|f\|^2 = \frac{\pi}{2}$, giving $
\|Af\|/\|f\| = \sqrt{\pi^2/4} = \pi/2$, attaining the norm.

$$\boxed{\|A\| = \dfrac{\pi}{2}}$$
