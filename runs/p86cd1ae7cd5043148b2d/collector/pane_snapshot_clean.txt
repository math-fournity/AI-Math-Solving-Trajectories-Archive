## Proof

**Key observation:** A branching process $Z_n$ counts the number of individuals
in generation $n$, so $Z_n$ takes values in $\{0, 1, 2, \ldots\}$ — it is **non-
negative integer-valued**.

**Consequence:** For every integer $k \ge 1$, we have $\frac{1}{k} \in (0, 1]$.
Since $Z_n$ is integer-valued,
$$Z_n > \tfrac{1}{k} \iff Z_n \ge 1 \iff Z_n > 0.$$
Therefore, for all $k \ge 1$ and all $n \ge 0$:
$$P\!\left(Z_n > \tfrac{1}{k}\right) = P(Z_n > 0). \tag{$\ast$}$$

---

**Left-hand side:**
$$\lim_{n \to \infty} \lim_{k \to \infty} P\!\left(Z_n > \tfrac{1}{k}\right).$$

The inner limit is given: $\lim_{k\to\infty} P(Z_n > \frac{1}{k}) = P(Z_n > 0)$
(this also follows immediately from $(\ast)$, since the sequence is constant in
$k$). Then the outer limit gives
$$\lim_{n\to\infty} P(Z_n > 0) = 0,$$
which is given. Hence
$$\text{LHS} = 0.$$

---

**Right-hand side:**
$$\lim_{k \to \infty} \lim_{n \to \infty} P\!\left(Z_n > \tfrac{1}{k}\right).$$

By $(\ast)$, $P(Z_n > \frac{1}{k}) = P(Z_n > 0)$ for every $k \ge 1$, so the inn
er limit is
$$\lim_{n\to\infty} P\!\left(Z_n > \tfrac{1}{k}\right) = \lim_{n\to\infty} P(Z_n
 > 0) = 0,$$
independent of $k$. The outer limit is then
$$\lim_{k\to\infty} 0 = 0.$$
Hence
$$\text{RHS} = 0.$$

---

**Conclusion:** Both sides equal $0$, so
$$\lim_{n \to \infty} \lim_{k \to \infty} P\!\left(Z_n > \tfrac{1}{k}\right) = \
lim_{k \to \infty} \lim_{n \to \infty} P\!\left(Z_n > \tfrac{1}{k}\right) = 0.$$

**The interchange is valid.** The justification is that the branching process $Z
_n$ is non-negative integer-valued, which forces $P(Z_n > \frac{1}{k}) = P(Z_n >
 0)$ for all $k \ge 1$. Thus the quantity $P(Z_n > \frac{1}{k})$ is in fact **in
dependent of $k$**, making the interchange of limits trivial: both iterated limi
ts reduce to $\lim_{n\to\infty} P(Z_n > 0) = 0$.
