# Problem

In a thermal research facility, a linear control track is marked with integer coordinates ranging from $-10$ to $10$. Each coordinate point is designated as either a "Heat Zone" or a "Cool Zone." The sum of the coordinate values of all points designated as Heat Zones is equal to an integer $n$.

A mobile sensor begins at the center of the track at position $0$. To test the sensor's durability, a computer generates a sequence of ten random binary commands. Each command has a $50\%$ probability of being "Move Right" (incrementing the position by $1$) and a $50\%$ probability of being "Move Left" (decrementing the position by $1$).

After the sequence of ten commands is executed, the probability that the sensor's final location is a Heat Zone is expressed as a fraction $a/b$ in lowest terms. If the sum of the numerator and the denominator is $a + b = 2001$, what is the largest possible value of $n$?
