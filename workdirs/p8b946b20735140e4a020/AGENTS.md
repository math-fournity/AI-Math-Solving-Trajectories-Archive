# Problem

The system's internal pressure is determined by the complex interaction of two different laboratory chambers. The pressure in the first chamber is measured by the expression $(x^4 - 5)^5$. For the experiment to remain in equilibrium, this pressure must exactly equal the output of the secondary relief valve. The relief valve's output is calculated by taking a baseline constant of $5$ units and subtracting a variable resistance factor defined as $\frac{24}{x^4}$.

The stability factor $x$ can be any real number, provided the equilibrium equation $(x^4 - 5)^5 = 5 - \frac{24}{x^4}$ holds true. 

How many distinct real values for the stability factor $x$ will satisfy this equilibrium condition?
