**Step 1: $S$ induces a forest.**

Suppose $S$ contains a cycle $v_1, v_2, \ldots, v_k, v_1$. Let $v_i$ be the vert

{i-1}$ and $v_{i+1}$ have higher concentrations than $v_i$, and both are neighbo

ntration, contradicting $v_i \in S$. Therefore $S$ induces an **acyclic subgraph
** (a forest).

**Step 2: Every $2 \times 2$ block contributes at most 2 vertices to $S$.**

In the king graph, any $2 \times 2$ block forms a $K_4$ (all four pods are mutua
l neighbors). Any 3 vertices of a $K_4$ form a triangle. Since $S$ is acyclic, $
S$ contains no triangle, so $S$ contains **at most 2 vertices** from each $2 \ti
mes 2$ block.

**Step 3: Upper bound — any two consecutive rows contain at most 32 vertices of
$S$.**

Consider rows $i$ and $i+1$. For each column $j \in \{1,\ldots,31\}$, let $w_j =
 |S \cap \{(i,j), (i+1,j)\}| \in \{0,1,2\}$. The $2 \times 2$ block constraint g
ives:
$$w_j + w_{j+1} \leq 2 \quad \text{for all } j = 1, \ldots, 30.$$

We maximize $\sum_{j=1}^{31} w_j$ subject to $w_j + w_{j+1} \leq 2$ and $w_j \in
 \{0,1,2\}$.

- If $w_j = 2$, then $w_{j-1} = 0$ and $w_{j+1} = 0$ (forced by the constraint).
- The pattern $(2, 0, 2, 0, \ldots, 2)$ over 31 columns places $w_j = 2$ at $j =

ing sum $= 32$.
- Any pattern with $w_j = 1$ achieves at most the same density: $(1,1,1,\ldots,1
)$ gives sum $= 31 < 32$. Mixed patterns like $(2,0,1,1,2,0,\ldots)$ tie the pur
e $(2,0)$ pattern on even-length segments but lose by 1 on odd-length segments.

Therefore $\sum w_j \leq 32$, so **any two consecutive rows contain at most 32 v
ertices of $S$**.

**Step 4: Global upper bound $|S| \leq 512$.**

Pair the 32 rows into 16 consecutive pairs: $(1,2), (3,4), \ldots, (31,32)$. By
Step 3, each pair contributes at most 32 vertices to $S$. Since the pairs are di

$$|S| \leq 16 \times 32 = 512.$$

**Step 5: Construction achieving 512.**

Let $S = \{(i, j) : j \text{ is odd}, \, 1 \leq i \leq 32, \, 1 \leq j \leq 31\}

**$S$ induces a forest:** For two vertices $(i,j), (i',j') \in S$ (both with $j,

- **Horizontal edge** ($i = i'$, $|j - j'| = 1$): impossible since $j, j'$ odd $
\Rightarrow |j-j'| \geq 2$.
- **Diagonal edge** ($|i-i'|=1$, $|j-j'|=1$): impossible since $|j-j'| \geq 2$ f

(i+1,j)$ in the same odd column.

So $G[S]$ is a disjoint union of 16 vertical paths (one per odd column), each of
