## Theorem

Let $H$ be a subgroup of finite index in a group $G$, and let $n = [G : H]$. Den
ote by $\operatorname{Core}_G(H) = \bigcap_{g \in G} gHg^{-1}$ the **core** of $
H$ in $G$ (the largest normal subgroup of $G$ contained in $H$). Then

$$
\bigl[\,G : \operatorname{Core}_G(H)\,\bigr] \;\le\; n!\,.
$$

---

## Proof

**Setup: the coset action.**

Let $G/H = \{g_1 H, g_2 H, \dots, g_n H\}$ be the set of left cosets of $H$ in $
G$, so $|G/H| = n$. The group $G$ acts on $G/H$ by left multiplication:

$$
G \times G/H \longrightarrow G/H, \qquad (a, g_i H) \;\mapsto\; (ag_i)H.
$$

This is a well-defined action: for $a, b \in G$,
$$
a \cdot (b \cdot g_i H) = a \cdot (bg_i H) = (abg_i)H = (ab) \cdot g_i H,
$$
and $e \cdot g_i H = g_i H$.

**The associated homomorphism.**

Every group action on a set of size $n$ yields a homomorphism into the symmetric
 group $S_n$. Concretely, fix the labeling $g_1 H, \dots, g_n H$ of the cosets.
For each $a \in G$, left multiplication by $a$ permutes the cosets, so we obtain
 a permutation $\sigma_a \in S_n$ defined by

$$
a \cdot g_i H = g_{\sigma_a(i)} H, \qquad i = 1, \dots, n.
$$

The map

$$
\varphi : G \longrightarrow S_n, \qquad \varphi(a) = \sigma_a
$$

is a homomorphism, since $\sigma_{ab}$ is the composition of the permutations in
duced by $a$ then $b$, i.e., $\sigma_{ab} = \sigma_a \circ \sigma_b$ (matching t
he convention that $S_n$ acts on the right of indices, or equivalently $\varphi(
ab) = \varphi(a)\varphi(b)$ after fixing the order convention).

**Identifying the kernel with the core.**

We claim

$$
\ker \varphi \;=\; \operatorname{Core}_G(H) \;=\; \bigcap_{g \in G} gHg^{-1}.
$$

*Proof of claim.* An element $a \in G$ lies in $\ker \varphi$ iff $\sigma_a$ is
the identity permutation, i.e., iff $a$ fixes **every** left coset:

$$
a \in \ker \varphi \;\iff\; a \cdot g_i H = g_i H \;\;\text{for all } i = 1, \do
ts, n.
$$

Since $\{g_1, \dots, g_n\}$ is a complete set of coset representatives, this is
equivalent to

$$
a \cdot g H = g H \;\;\text{for all } g \in G,
$$

i.e., $agH = gH$ for all $g$, i.e., $g^{-1}ag \in H$ for all $g \in G$, i.e.,

$$
a \in gHg^{-1} \;\;\text{for all } g \in G.
$$

Therefore

$$
\ker \varphi = \bigcap_{g \in G} gHg^{-1} = \operatorname{Core}_G(H).
$$

This establishes the claim. (Note: $\operatorname{Core}_G(H)$ is normal in $G$ a
s the kernel of a homomorphism, and it is contained in $H$ by taking $g = e$ in
the intersection.)

**Applying the First Isomorphism Theorem.**

By the First Isomorphism Theorem,

$$
G / \ker \varphi \;\cong\; \operatorname{im} \varphi \;\le\; S_n.
$$

Since $\ker \varphi = \operatorname{Core}_G(H)$,

$$
G / \operatorname{Core}_G(H) \;\cong\; \operatorname{im}\varphi \;\le\; S_n.
$$

Therefore $\operatorname{Core}_G(H)$ is a normal subgroup of $G$ of finite index
, and

$$
\bigl[\,G : \operatorname{Core}_G(H)\,\bigr] \;=\; \bigl|\,G / \operatorname{Cor
e}_G(H)\,\bigr| \;=\; \bigl|\operatorname{im}\varphi\bigr| \;\le\; \bigl|S_n\big
r| \;=\; n!\,.
$$

**Conclusion.**

$$
\boxed{\bigl[\,G : \operatorname{Core}_G(H)\,\bigr] \;\le\; \bigl([G:H]\bigr)!\,
.}
$$

The bound $n!$ is sharp: when $G = S_n$ and $H$ is the stabilizer of a point (so
 $[G:H] = n$), the core of $H$ is trivial and $[G : \operatorname{Core}_G(H)] =
|S_n| = n!$, achieving equality.
