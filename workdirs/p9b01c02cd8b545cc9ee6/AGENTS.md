# Problem

In a remote sector of the electrical grid, a central control hub manages a grid of 16 power relays arranged in a 4x4 matrix. Each relay can be set to one of two operational modes: Mode 1 (low-draw) or Mode 2 (high-draw). 

The regional supervisor has issued a specific energy directive: the total combined draw across all 16 relays must be exactly 22 units. Furthermore, to prevent local circuit overloads, the hub’s internal safety logic requires that the sum of the power draws in every individual row, every individual column, and both of the two main diagonals must result in an even number.

Two grid configurations are considered distinct if at least one relay is set to a different mode. How many different valid configurations of these 16 power relays are possible?
