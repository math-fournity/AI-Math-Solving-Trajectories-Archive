## Proof

We need the smallest positive integer $m$ such that $105 \mid 9^{p^2} - 29^p + m
$ for every prime $p > 3$. Since $105 = 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 7$, we work modulo $3$,
$5$, and $7$ separately.

**Modulo 3.** $9 \equiv 0 \pmod{3}$, so $9^{p^2} \equiv 0$. Also $29 \equiv 2 \p
mod{3}$, and since every prime $p>3$ is odd, $29^p \equiv 2^p \equiv 2 \pmod{3}$
. Hence
$$9^{p^2} - 29^p \equiv 0 - 2 \equiv 1 \pmod{3},$$
so we need $m \equiv 2 \pmod{3}$.

**Modulo 5.** $9 \equiv -1 \pmod{5}$, and $p^2$ is odd (since $p$ is odd), so $9
^{p^2} \equiv -1 \pmod{5}$. Likewise $29 \equiv -1 \pmod{5}$ and $p$ is odd, so
$29^p \equiv -1 \pmod{5}$. Hence
$$9^{p^2} - 29^p \equiv -1 - (-1) \equiv 0 \pmod{5},$$
so we need $m \equiv 0 \pmod{5}$.

**Modulo 7.** $9 \equiv 2 \pmod{7}$, and $\operatorname{ord}_7(2) = 3$ (since $2
^3 = 8 \equiv 1$). For any prime $p > 3$, we have $p \not\equiv 0 \pmod{3}$, so
$p \equiv \pm 1 \pmod{3}$, giving $p^2 \equiv 1 \pmod{3}$. Therefore
$$9^{p^2} \equiv 2^{p^2} \equiv 2^1 \equiv 2 \pmod{7}.$$
Also $29 \equiv 1 \pmod{7}$, so $29^p \equiv 1 \pmod{7}$. Hence
$$9^{p^2} - 29^p \equiv 2 - 1 \equiv 1 \pmod{7},$$
so we need $m \equiv 6 \pmod{7}$.

**Solving the system.** We solve
$$m \equiv 2 \pmod{3}, \quad m \equiv 0 \pmod{5}, \quad m \equiv 6 \pmod{7}.$$

From $m \equiv 0 \pmod{5}$, write $m = 5k$. Then $5k \equiv 2 \pmod{3}$, i.e. $2
k \equiv 2 \pmod{3}$, so $k \equiv 1 \pmod{3}$. Write $k = 3j+1$, giving $m = 15
j + 5$.

Now $15j + 5 \equiv 6 \pmod{7}$. Since $15 \equiv 1$ and $5 \equiv 5 \pmod{7}$,
we get $j + 5 \equiv 6 \pmod{7}$, so $j \equiv 1 \pmod{7}$. Write $j = 7t + 1$,
giving
$$m = 15(7t+1) + 5 = 105t + 20.$$

The smallest positive value is $\boxed{m = 20}$.

**Verification.** For $p = 5$: $9^{25} - 29^5 + 20$. Mod $3$: $0 - 2 + 2 = 0$ ✓.
 Mod $5$: $(-1) - (-1) + 0 = 0$ ✓. Mod $7$: $2^{25} \equiv 2^{1} = 2$ (since $25
 \equiv 1 \pmod 3$), $29^5 \equiv 1$, $20 \equiv 6$: $2 - 1 + 6 = 7 \equiv 0$ ✓.

For $p = 7$: $9^{49} - 29^7 + 20$. Mod $3$: $0 - 2 + 2 = 0$ ✓. Mod $5$: $(-1) -
(-1) + 0 = 0$ ✓. Mod $7$: $2^{49} \equiv 2^1 = 2$ (since $49 \equiv 1 \pmod 3$),
 $29^7 \equiv 1$, $20 \equiv 6$: $2 - 1 + 6 = 7 \equiv 0$ ✓.

Since the congruences hold for all primes $p > 3$ and $m = 20$ is the smallest p
ositive solution to the system, the answer is $m = 20$.
