Looking at this problem, I need to find the expected area of the 1-neighborhood
(safety zone) of a random monotone lattice path of 20 steps.

## Setting Up the Area Formula

The safety zone is the Minkowski sum of the path with a unit disk — a "tube" of
radius 1 around the polygonal path.

**Decomposition of the area** for a path with total length $L$, $T$ turns (90° d
irection changes), and $r=1$:

1. **Strips around each segment**: Each unit segment contributes a rectangle of
area $2r \cdot \ell_i$. Total: $2rL = 2 \cdot 20 = 40$.

2. **Endpoint semicircles**: The start and end each contribute a semicircle of a
rea $\pi r^2/2$. Total: $\pi r^2 = \pi$.

3. **Turn corrections**: At each 90° turn, two adjacent strips overlap in a squa
re of area $r^2 = 1$ (inside the turn), while a quarter-disk of area $\pi r^2/4
= \pi/4$ is uncovered (outside the turn). Net correction per turn: $\pi/4 - 1$.

**Verification with L-shaped path** (E then N, $L=2$, $T=1$): Area $= 4 + \pi +
(\pi/4 - 1) = 3 + 5\pi/4$. I confirmed this by direct geometric computation: uni
on of strips $= 3$, endpoint semicircles $= \pi$, uncovered SE quarter-disk $= \
pi/4$. ✓

**No overlap issues**: Since the path is monotone (only E/N steps), uncovered se
ctors at different vertices point outward in non-interfering directions. SE quar
ters have $y \leq b$ while all later strips have $y \geq b$; NW quarters have $x
 \leq a$ while all later strips have $x \geq a$. All overlaps are measure-zero.

## Computing the Expected Number of Turns

The path has 20 steps, each independently N or E, giving $2^{20}$ equally likely
 paths.

There are 19 interior vertices (between consecutive steps). At vertex $i$ (betwe
en step $i$ and step $i+1$), a turn occurs when step $i \neq$ step $i+1$. Since
steps are independent and equally likely:

$$P(\text{turn at vertex } i) = P(\text{step}_i \neq \text{step}_{i+1}) = \frac{
1}{2}$$

By linearity of expectation:

$$\mathbb{E}[T] = 19 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = \frac{19}{2}$$

## Computing the Expected Area

$$\mathbb{E}[\text{Area}] = 2rL + \pi r^2 + \mathbb{E}[T] \cdot \left(\frac{\pi}
{4} - 1\right) = 40 + \pi + \frac{19}{2}\left(\frac{\pi}{4} - 1\right)$$

$$= 40 + \pi + \frac{19\pi}{8} - \frac{19}{2} = \left(40 - \frac{19}{2}\right) +
 \pi\left(1 + \frac{19}{8}\right)$$

$$= \frac{61}{2} + \frac{27\pi}{8} = \frac{244 + 27\pi}{8}$$

## Extracting $m + n + p$

The expected area equals $\frac{m + n\pi}{p}$ with $m = 244$, $n = 27$, $p = 8$.

**Coprimality check**: $244 = 2^2 \cdot 61$, $27 = 3^3$, $8 = 2^3$. Since $\gcd(
244, 27) = 1$, we have $\gcd(244, 27, 8) = 1$. ✓

$$m + n + p = 244 + 27 + 8 = \boxed{279}$$
