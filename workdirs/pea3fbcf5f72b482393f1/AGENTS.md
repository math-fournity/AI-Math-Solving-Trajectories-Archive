# Problem

In a remote industrial testing facility, a robotic arm is programmed to sweep through a specific zone. The zone is defined by a metal template in the shape of a right-angled triangle.

When this triangular template is spun $360^\circ$ around its longer leg, it carves out a conical volume of exactly $100\pi$ cubic units. Engineers have also noted a specific geometric property of this template: the sum of the diameter of the largest circular gear that can be fit inside the triangle (the inscribed circle) and the diameter of the circular frame that perfectly surrounds the triangle (the circumscribed circle) is exactly $17$ units.

The robot is now programmed to perform a specialized maneuver. It must rotate the entire triangular template $180^\circ$ around a fixed pivot point located precisely at the intersection of the triangle's three internal angle bisectors (the incenter).

Calculate the total length of the path traced by the three vertices of the triangle during this $180^\circ$ rotation.
