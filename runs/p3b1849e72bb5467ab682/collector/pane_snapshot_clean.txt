## Solution

**Setting up the sum of squares.** Let $P(x) = (x^2-3x+1)^{1009} = \sum_{k=0}^{2
018} a_k x^k$. The sum $\sum_{k=0}^{2018} a_k^2$ equals the constant term of $P(
x)\,P(x^{-1})$.

Let $f(x) = x^2 - 3x + 1$. Observe that
$$f(x^{-1}) = x^{-2} - 3x^{-1} + 1 = x^{-2}(1 - 3x + x^2) = x^{-2}f(x).$$

Therefore
$$P(x)\,P(x^{-1}) = f(x)^{1009}\cdot f(x^{-1})^{1009} = f(x)^{1009}\cdot x^{-201
8}f(x)^{1009} = x^{-2018}f(x)^{2018}.$$

The constant term of $x^{-2018}f(x)^{2018}$ is the coefficient of $x^{2018}$ in
$f(x)^{2018} = (x^2-3x+1)^{2018}$. Hence
$$\sum_{k=0}^{2018} a_k^2 = [x^{2018}]\,(x^2-3x+1)^{2018}.$$

**Reducing mod 2017.** Since $2017$ is prime and $2018 = 2017+1$, by the Frobeni
us (freshman's dream) automorphism in $\mathbb{F}_{2017}[x]$:
$$(x^2-3x+1)^{2017} \equiv x^{4034} - 3^{2017}x^{2017} + 1 \pmod{2017}.$$

By Fermat's little theorem, $3^{2017}\equiv 3\pmod{2017}$, so
$$(x^2-3x+1)^{2017} \equiv x^{4034} - 3x^{2017} + 1 \pmod{2017}.$$

Multiplying by one more factor of $(x^2-3x+1)$:
$$(x^2-3x+1)^{2018} \equiv (x^{4034}-3x^{2017}+1)(x^2-3x+1) \pmod{2017}.$$

Expanding:
$$= x^{4036}-3x^{4035}+x^{4034} \;-\; 3x^{2019}+9x^{2018}-3x^{2017} \;+\; x^2-3x
+1.$$

The only term contributing to $x^{2018}$ is $9x^{2018}$ (from $-3x^{2017}\cdot(-
3x)$). Thus
$$[x^{2018}]\,(x^2-3x+1)^{2018} \equiv 9 \pmod{2017}.$$

**Answer:** $\boxed{9}$
