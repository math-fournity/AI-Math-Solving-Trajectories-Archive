**Proof.**

Let $u \in BV(\Omega)$ with $u = 0$ a.e. on an open set $E \subseteq \Omega$. By

$$\int_\Omega \varphi \, d(D_i u) = -\int_\Omega u \, \partial_i \varphi \, dx.
\tag{1}$$

**Step 1: $D_i u$ vanishes on $C_c^1(E)$ test functions.**

$$-\int_\Omega u \, \partial_i \varphi \, dx = -\int_E u \, \partial_i \varphi \

rphi) \subset E$ and $u = 0$ a.e. on $E$. Hence

$$\int_E \varphi \, d(D_i u) = 0 \quad \text{for all } \varphi \in C_c^1(E). \ta

**Step 2: Extend to all of $C_c(E)$ by density.**

The space $C_c^1(E)$ is dense in $C_c(E)$ with respect to the uniform norm (a st

Therefore

$$\int_E \varphi \, d(D_i u) = 0 \quad \text{for all } \varphi \in C_c(E). \tag{

**Step 3: Conclude the measure vanishes on $E$.**

$$\boxed{D_i u(E) = 0.} \qquad \blacksquare$$
