## Proof

**Factorization.** $2010 = 2\cdot 3\cdot 5\cdot 67$, so
$$2010^{2010} = 2^{2010}\cdot 3^{2010}\cdot 5^{2010}\cdot 67^{2010}.$$
Every positive divisor has the form $d = 2^a 3^b 5^c 67^e$ with $0\le a,b,c,e\le
 2010$.

**Condition for $d\equiv 2\pmod{10}$.** By CRT ($10=2\cdot 5$), this is equivale
nt to $d\equiv 0\pmod 2$ and $d\equiv 2\pmod 5$.

- $d\equiv 0\pmod 2$ forces $a\ge 1$.
- $d\equiv 2\pmod 5$ forces $5\nmid d$, hence $c=0$.

With $c=0$ and $a\ge 1$, the condition $d\equiv 2\pmod 5$ becomes
$$2^a\cdot 3^b\cdot 67^e \equiv 2 \pmod 5.$$
Since $67\equiv 2\pmod 5$, this is $2^{a+e}\cdot 3^b \equiv 2\pmod 5$.

Let $A=(a+e)\bmod 4$ and $B=b\bmod 4$ (both $2$ and $3$ have order $4$ mod $5$).
 Tabulating $2^A\cdot 3^B\pmod 5$:

| $A$ | $2^A$ | need $3^B$ | $B$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| 1 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
| 2 | 4 | 3 | 1 |
| 3 | 3 | 4 | 2 |

In every case $B\equiv A-1\pmod 4$. Thus the constraint is
$$b \equiv a+e-1 \pmod 4,\qquad a\in[1,2010],\; b,e\in[0,2010],\; c=0.$$

**Counting.** For each pair $(a,e)$, the residue $r=(a+e-1)\bmod 4$ is fixed, an
d $b$ must satisfy $b\equiv r\pmod 4$ with $b\in[0,2010]$. Since $2011=4\cdot 50
2+3$, each residue $0,1,2$ occurs $503$ times among $0,\dots,2010$, and residue
$3$ occurs $502$ times. Hence each $(a,e)$ contributes $503$ valid $b$'s, except
 when $r=3$ (i.e. $(a+e)\equiv 0\pmod 4$), which contributes $502$.

So
$$N = 503\cdot(\text{total }(a,e)\text{ pairs}) - (\text{# pairs with }(a+e)\equ
iv 0\!\!\pmod 4).$$

The total number of $(a,e)$ pairs is $2010\cdot 2011 = 4042110$.

**Distribution of $(a+e)\bmod 4$.**
- $a\in[1,2010]$: residues $1,2$ occur $503$ times; residues $0,3$ occur $502$ t
imes.
- $e\in[0,2010]$: residues $0,1,2$ occur $503$ times; residue $3$ occurs $502$ t
imes.

The number of pairs with $(a+e)\equiv 0\pmod 4$ is
$$502\cdot503 + 503\cdot502 + 503\cdot503 + 502\cdot503 = 252506+252506+253009+2
52506 = 1010527.$$

**Final reduction mod $2010$.**
$$N = 503\cdot 4042110 - 1010527.$$
Since $4042110 = 2010\cdot 2011 \equiv 0\pmod{2010}$,
$$N \equiv -1010527 \pmod{2010}.$$
Now $1010527 = 2010\cdot 502 + 1507$, so $1010527\equiv 1507\pmod{2010}$, and
$$N \equiv -1507 \equiv 2010-1507 = 503 \pmod{2010}.$$

The remainder when $N$ is divided by $2010$ is $\boxed{503}$.
