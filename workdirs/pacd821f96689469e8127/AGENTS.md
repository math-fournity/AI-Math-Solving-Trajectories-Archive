# Problem

In a remote logistics hub, there are two specialized cargo hangars, Hangar A and Hangar B. 

Hangar A is stocked with exactly 5 red emergency flares. Hangar B is stocked with 4 red emergency flares and 1 white signaling flare. 

A dispatch protocol is initiated:
1. A technician randomly selects one of the two hangars (each with a 50% probability).
2. From the selected hangar, the technician randomly selects one flare and removes it for deployment.
3. This process—selecting a hangar at random and then removing a flare from that hangar at random—is repeated continuously until one of the two hangars is completely empty.

Determine the probability that the white signaling flare is never selected during this entire process.
