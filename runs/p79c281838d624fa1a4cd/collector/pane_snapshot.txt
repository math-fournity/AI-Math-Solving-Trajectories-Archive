## Solution

**Setup.** Label the six faces: Top (T), Bottom (B), Front (F), Back (K), Left (
L), Right (R). Two faces are adjacent (share an edge) iff they are **not** oppos
ite. The three opposite pairs are (T,B), (F,K), (R,L). Thus the face-adjacency g
raph is $K_6$ minus a perfect matching of 3 edges (the octahedral graph).

We count proper colorings (adjacent faces differ) using 6 available colors, up t
o the rotation group of the cube (order 24), via **Burnside's Lemma**:

$$N = \frac{1}{24}\sum_{g\in G} |{\rm Fix}(g)|.$$

The 24 rotations fall into 5 conjugacy classes.

---

**Chromatic polynomial of the octahedral graph.** Let $f(n,m)$ = chromatic polyn
omial of $K_n$ minus $m$ disjoint edges. Deletion–contraction on a removed edge
gives $f(n,m)=f(n,m-1)+f(n-1,m-1)$, with $f(n,0)=(x)_n$. Unrolling:

$$f(n,3)=(x)_n+3(x)_{n-1}+3(x)_{n-2}+(x)_{n-3}.$$

At $x=6$:
$$f(6,3)=720+3\cdot720+3\cdot360+120=720+2160+1080+120=4080.$$

---

**Fixed colorings by rotation type.**

| Type | # elements | Cycle structure on faces | $|{\rm Fix}|$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity | 1 | all fixed | 4080 |
| 90° face axis | 6 | $(F\,R\,K\,L)$, $T,B$ fixed | 0 |
| 180° face axis | 3 | $(F\,K)(R\,L)$, $T,B$ fixed | 480 |
| 120° vertex axis | 8 | two 3-cycles | 0 |
| 180° edge axis | 6 | three 2-cycles | 0 |

*90° face rotation:* forces $F=R=K=L$, but $F,R$ are adjacent → impossible. **0*
*.

*120° vertex rotation:* e.g. cycles $(T\,F\,R)(B\,K\,L)$, forcing $T=F$, but $T,
F$ adjacent → **0**.

*180° edge rotation:* the axis passes through midpoints of two opposite edges; i
t swaps the two faces sharing each such edge (e.g. $T\leftrightarrow F$), which
are adjacent → **0**.

*180° face rotation* (about $T$–$B$ axis): forces $F=K$ and $R=L$ (both opposite
 pairs, so allowed). The four independent color classes are $T,B,F,R$ with const
raints $T\neq F,\;T\neq R,\;B\neq F,\;B\neq R,\;F\neq R$ (and no constraint betw
een $T,B$). Count:
$$6\;(F)\times 5\;(R\neq F)\times 4\;(T\neq F,R)\times 4\;(B\neq F,R)=480.$$

---

**Burnside total.**

$$N=\frac{1}{24}\bigl(1\cdot4080+6\cdot0+3\cdot480+8\cdot0+6\cdot0\bigr)=\frac{4
080+1440}{24}=\frac{5520}{24}=230.$$

The number of different coloring schemes is $\boxed{230}$.
