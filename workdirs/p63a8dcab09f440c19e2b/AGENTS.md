# Problem

In a remote logistics hub, there are 10 unique shipping containers. Each container is marked with a unique pair of identification codes selected from the set {1, 2, 3, 4, 5}. No two containers share the same pair of codes, and every possible combination of two distinct codes is represented exactly once.

The hub has five designated loading bays, numbered 1 through 5. According to strict safety protocols, a container marked with codes $i$ and $j$ can only be assigned to loading bay $i$ or loading bay $j$. 

A distribution plan is classified as "optimal" if and only if, after all 10 containers are assigned, loading bay 1 contains strictly more containers than any other individual loading bay. 

How many different optimal distribution plans are possible?
