# Problem

In the remote digital kingdom of Hexa, a massive plaza is paved in the shape of a perfect regular hexagon with a side length of exactly 1 kilometer. The kingdom’s architect needs to divide the entire surface of the plaza—including its outer boundaries—into three distinct zones, each designated by a different color (Red, Green, and Blue). 

To ensure the safety of the citizens, the architect must establish a strict "segregation limit." This limit, denoted as $r$, is a distance threshold such that if any two people are standing on two different points of the plaza that are painted the same color, the straight-line distance between them must be strictly less than $r$ kilometers.

The architect wants to find the most efficient safety protocol possible. What is the smallest possible value of the constant $r$ for which there exists at least one configuration of the three colors across the plaza that satisfies this distance constraint?
