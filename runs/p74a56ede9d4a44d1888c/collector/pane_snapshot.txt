## Solution

**Setup.** Let the Total Volume be $S = m+n$ and the Reaction Intensity be $P =
m\cdot n$. Write $S = 10a + b$ with digits $a \in \{1,\dots,9\}$, $b \in \{0,\do
ts,9\}$. The reverse-digit condition gives $P = 10b + a$.

**Two-digit constraints.**
- $S$ two-digit ⟹ $a \ge 1$.
- $P = 10b+a$ two-digit ⟹ $b \ge 1$ (if $b=0$, then $P=a \le 9$, a single digit)
.

So $a,b \in \{1,\dots,9\}$.

**Existence of natural $m,n$.** For fixed $S,P$, the values $m,n$ are roots of $
t^2 - St + P = 0$, so we need the discriminant
$$D = S^2 - 4P = (10a+b)^2 - 4(10b+a) = 100a^2 + 20ab + b^2 - 40b - 4a$$
to be a non-negative perfect square, and $S \pm \sqrt{D}$ even (so roots are int
egers). Since $m,n \ge 1$ and $S \ge 10$, positivity is automatic once roots are
 positive integers.

**Exhaustive search over $a,b \in \{1,\dots,9\}$.** Computing $D$ for all 81 pai
rs, the only cases where $D$ is a perfect square:

| $(a,b)$ | $S=10a+b$ | $P=10b+a$ | $D$ | $\sqrt D$ | $(m,n)$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $(1,8)$ | $18$ | $81$ | $0$ | $0$ | $(9,9)$ |
| $(2,7)$ | $27$ | $72$ | $441=21^2$ | $21$ | $(24,3)$ and $(3,24)$ |
| $(4,9)$ | $49$ | $94$ | $2025=45^2$ | $45$ | $(47,2)$ and $(2,47)$ |

All other $(a,b)$ yield $D$ either negative or a non-square (verified across the
 full $9\times 9$ table).

**Verification of each solution:**
- $(9,9)$: $9+9=18$, $9\cdot 9=81$, reverse of $18$ is $81$. ✓
- $(24,3)$: $24+3=27$, $24\cdot 3=72$, reverse of $27$ is $72$. ✓
- $(3,24)$: same sum/product. ✓
- $(47,2)$: $47+2=49$, $47\cdot 2=94$, reverse of $49$ is $94$. ✓
- $(2,47)$: same sum/product. ✓

**Distinct pairs.** Since $m$ (Marcus) and $n$ (Nadia) label two different chemi
sts, the ordered pairs $(24,3)$ and $(3,24)$ are distinct, as are $(47,2)$ and $
(2,47)$. Thus
$$S = \{(9,9),\ (24,3),\ (3,24),\ (47,2),\ (2,47)\}.$$

**Sum of Reaction Intensities:**
$$81 + 72 + 72 + 94 + 94 = \boxed{413}.$$
