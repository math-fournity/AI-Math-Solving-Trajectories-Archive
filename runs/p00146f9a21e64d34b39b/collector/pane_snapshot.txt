## Solution

We work with the **Riemann–Liouville** fractional derivative of order $\alpha \i
n (0,1)$, whose key property on monomials is

$$
{}_0D_t^{\alpha}\, t^{n} \;=\; \frac{\Gamma(n+1)}{\Gamma(n+1-\alpha)}\, t^{n-\al
pha}, \qquad n = 0, 1, 2, \dots
$$

In particular, for a constant $A$ (the case $n=0$, using $\Gamma(1)=1$):

$$
{}_0D_t^{\alpha}\, A \;=\; \frac{A\, t^{-\alpha}}{\Gamma(1-\alpha)}.
$$

This is precisely the leading term of $f(t)$, confirming the Riemann–Liouville i
nterpretation.

---

### Term-by-term differentiation

Assume $y(t) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} y_n t^n$. Applying the Riemann–Liouville deri
vative termwise (justified within the radius of convergence of the series, since
 the derivative acts as a Mellin-type operator on each power):

$$
{}_0D_t^{\alpha}\, y(t) \;=\; \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} y_n\, \frac{\Gamma(n+1)}{\Gamm
a(n+1-\alpha)}\, t^{n-\alpha}.
$$

### Matching coefficients with $f(t)$

We are given

$$
f(t) \;=\; \frac{A\, t^{-\alpha}}{\Gamma(1-\alpha)} \;+\; \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} f_
n\, t^{n-\alpha}.
$$

Equating the two expressions for ${}_0D_t^{\alpha} y(t) = f(t)$ and matching lik
e powers $t^{n-\alpha}$:

**$n = 0$ term:**

$$
y_0 \cdot \frac{\Gamma(1)}{\Gamma(1-\alpha)} \;=\; \frac{A}{\Gamma(1-\alpha)}
\;\;\Longrightarrow\;\;
y_0 \;=\; A.
$$

This is consistent with the initial condition $y(0) = A$.

**$n \geq 1$ terms:**

$$
y_n \cdot \frac{\Gamma(n+1)}{\Gamma(n+1-\alpha)} \;=\; f_n
\;\;\Longrightarrow\;\;
y_n \;=\; \frac{\Gamma(n+1-\alpha)}{\Gamma(n+1)}\, f_n, \qquad n \geq 1.
$$

### Result

$$
\boxed{\;y_0 = A, \qquad y_n = \frac{\Gamma(n+1-\alpha)}{\Gamma(n+1)}\, f_n \;\;
 \text{for } n \geq 1.\;}
$$

### Verification

With these coefficients,

$$
y(t) = A + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{\Gamma(n+1-\alpha)}{\Gamma(n+1)}\, f_n\, t^
n,
$$

so $y(0) = A$ as required, and

$$
{}_0D_t^{\alpha} y(t) = \frac{A\, t^{-\alpha}}{\Gamma(1-\alpha)} + \sum_{n=1}^{\
infty} \frac{\Gamma(n+1-\alpha)}{\Gamma(n+1)}\, f_n \cdot \frac{\Gamma(n+1)}{\Ga
mma(n+1-\alpha)}\, t^{n-\alpha} = \frac{A\, t^{-\alpha}}{\Gamma(1-\alpha)} + \su
m_{n=1}^{\infty} f_n\, t^{n-\alpha} = f(t). \;\checkmark
$$
