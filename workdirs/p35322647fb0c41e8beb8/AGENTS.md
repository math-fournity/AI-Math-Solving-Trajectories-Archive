# Problem

In a sprawling subterranean data center designed as a perfect cube with a side length of 2 kilometers, two critical servers, Server A and Server B, are located at the two most distant corners of the facility. 

An automated maintenance drone must travel from Server A to Server B. However, due to its specialized diagnostic sensors, the drone is programmed to move across the facility's interior surfaces (the floors, ceilings, and walls) in fixed, discrete leaps. Each leap must cover a distance of exactly 3 kilometers. 

The drone begins its mission at Server A (Position 1) and must reach Server B (Position $k$) through a sequence of such leaps, where each landing point in the sequence must be located on the surface of the cube.

What is the minimum number of positions ($k$) in the sequence required for the drone to reach Server B?
