# Problem

Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle with side lengths $a=BC, b=CA, c=AB$. Let $P$ be a point in the plane of the triangle. Let $O_{a}, O_{b}, O_{c}$ be the circumcenters of triangles $PBC, PAC,$ and $PAB$, respectively. It is known that there exists a unique point $P$ such that
\[
\frac{O_{a}O_{b}}{c} = \frac{O_{b}O_{c}}{a} = \frac{O_{c}O_{a}}{b}
\]
Let this unique point be $P$. If $ABC$ is a triangle with vertices at coordinates $A(0, 140)$, $B(-120, 0)$, and $C(105, 0)$, determine the sum of the coordinates $(x, y)$ of the point $P$.
