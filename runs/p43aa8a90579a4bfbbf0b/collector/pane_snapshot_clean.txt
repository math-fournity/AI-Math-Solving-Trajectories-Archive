Looking at this problem, I need to find all meeting points of two drones on a 13
-14-15 triangle perimeter, then compute the area of the convex polygon they form
.

## Setup

Perimeter = 13 + 14 + 15 = 42 km. Parameterize the perimeter clockwise from A: A
→B is [0,13], B→C is [13,27], C→A is [27,42].

**Crimson** (counterclockwise, speed v): position s_C(t) = (−vt) mod 42.
**Azure** (clockwise, speed 4v, starts at t₀ = 2/v): position s_A(t) = (4v(t − 2
/v)) mod 42 = (4vt − 8) mod 42.

## Finding Meeting Points

Meetings occur when s_C = s_A, i.e., −vt ≡ 4vt − 8 (mod 42), giving **5u ≡ 8 (mo
d 42)** where u = vt.

Solutions: u = (8 + 42m)/5 for integers m ≥ 1 (m=0 gives u=1.6 < 2, before Azure
 starts). The meeting position is s = (−u) mod 42:

| m | u | s = (−u) mod 42 | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 | 32 | On CA, 5 km from C |
| 2 | 18.4 | 23.6 | On BC, 10.6 km from B |
| 3 | 26.8 | 15.2 | On BC, 2.2 km from B |
| 4 | 35.2 | 6.8 | On AB, 6.8 km from A |
| 5 | 43.6 | 40.4 | On CA, 1.6 km from A |
| 6 | 52 | 32 | **Repeats** |

**5 distinct meeting points**, cycling with period 5.

## Triangle Coordinates

Place A = (0,0), B = (13,0). Then C = (99/13, 168/13) (verified: area = 84).

The five points (in perimeter order):
- **P₁** (s=6.8, on AB): (34/5, 0)
- **P₂** (s=15.2, on BC): (158/13, 132/65)
- **P₃** (s=23.6, on BC): (116/13, 636/65)
- **P₄** (s=32, on CA): (66/13, 112/13)
- **P₅** (s=40.4, on CA): (264/325, 448/325)

## Computing the Pentagon Area

Since all 5 points lie on the triangle's boundary in order, they form a convex p
entagon. The area equals the triangle area minus three corner triangles:

**Corner triangles** (using (1/2)·a·b·sin θ):

- **△A-P₅-P₁**: AP₅ = 1.6, AP₁ = 6.8, sin A = 56/65 → Area = (1/2)(1.6)(6.8)(56/
65) = **7616/1625**
- **△B-P₁-P₂**: BP₁ = 6.2, BP₂ = 2.2, sin B = 12/13 → Area = (1/2)(6.2)(2.2)(12/
13) = **2046/325**
- **△C-P₃-P₄**: CP₃ = 3.4, CP₄ = 5, sin C = 4/5 → Area = (1/2)(3.4)(5)(4/5) = **
34/5**

**Sum of corner triangles** (common denominator 1625):
$$\frac{7616 + 10230 + 11050}{1625} = \frac{28896}{1625}$$

**Pentagon area:**
$$x = 84 - \frac{28896}{1625} = \frac{136500 - 28896}{1625} = \frac{107604}{1625
}$$

## Final Computation

$$10^3 x = \frac{107604000}{1625} = 66217 + \frac{1375}{1625} = 66217 + \frac{11
}{13} \approx 66217.846$$

$$\lfloor 10^3 x \rfloor = \boxed{66217}$$
