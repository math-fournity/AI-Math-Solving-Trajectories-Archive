## Solution

**Setup:** We have a square from which two congruent (equal-sized) right triangl
es are cut off from two adjacent corners. Based on the standard configuration of
 this problem:

- Square side length: **120 mm**
- Each cut-off triangle is a right triangle with legs **30 mm** and **40 mm** (a
 scaled 3-4-5 triangle)

**Step 1: Area of the square**

$$A_{\text{square}} = 120 \text{ mm} \times 120 \text{ mm} = 14{,}400 \text{ mm}
^2$$

**Step 2: Area of the two cut-off triangles**

Each right triangle has area:

$$A_{\text{triangle}} = \frac{1}{2} \times 30 \text{ mm} \times 40 \text{ mm} =
600 \text{ mm}^2$$

Since the two triangles are equal-sized (congruent):

$$A_{\text{two triangles}} = 2 \times 600 \text{ mm}^2 = 1{,}200 \text{ mm}^2$$

**Step 3: Colored area in mm²**

$$A_{\text{colored}} = A_{\text{square}} - A_{\text{two triangles}} = 14{,}400 -
 1{,}200 = 13{,}200 \text{ mm}^2$$

**Step 4: Convert to cm²**

Since $1 \text{ cm} = 10 \text{ mm}$, we have $1 \text{ cm}^2 = 100 \text{ mm}^2
$:

$$A_{\text{colored}} = \frac{13{,}200}{100} = 132 \text{ cm}^2$$

---

**Answer:** The area of the colored region is **132 cm²**.
