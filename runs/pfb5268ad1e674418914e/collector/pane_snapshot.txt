## Solution

**Setup via the disphenoid coordinate model.** An isosceles tetrahedron (disphen
oid) — one whose three pairs of opposite edges are equal — can be realized (up t
o congruence) as the tetrahedron with vertices

$$D=(x,y,z),\quad B=(x,-y,-z),\quad M=(-x,y,-z),\quad N=(-x,-y,z),$$

with $x,y,z>0$. One checks directly that every vertex is at distance $\sqrt{x^2+
y^2+z^2}$ from the origin, so the origin is the circumcenter and

$$R=\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}.$$

The edge lengths are

$$DB=MN=2\sqrt{y^2+z^2}=:a,\quad DM=BN=2\sqrt{x^2+z^2}=:b,\quad DN=BM=2\sqrt{x^2
+y^2}=:c,$$

matching the hypothesis $DB=MN=a$, $DM=BN=b$, $DN=BM=c$.

**Circumradius of $\triangle BMN$.** With $B=(x,-y,-z)$, $M=(-x,y,-z)$, $N=(-x,-
y,z)$:

$$\vec{BM}=(-2x,2y,0),\qquad \vec{BN}=(-2x,0,2z),$$
$$\vec{BM}\times\vec{BN}=(4yz,\,4xz,\,4xy),\qquad |\vec{BM}\times\vec{BN}|=4\sqr
t{x^2y^2+x^2z^2+y^2z^2}.$$

Hence the area of $\triangle BMN$ is $S=2\sqrt{x^2y^2+x^2z^2+y^2z^2}$, and

$$r=\frac{abc}{4S}=\frac{\sqrt{(x^2+y^2)(x^2+z^2)(y^2+z^2)}}{\sqrt{x^2y^2+x^2z^2
+y^2z^2}}.$$

**Form the ratio.** Put $u=x^2,\ v=y^2,\ w=z^2$ (all $>0$), and let $p=u+v+w$, $
q=uv+uw+vw$. Using

$$(u+v)(u+w)(v+w)=pq-uvw,$$

we obtain

$$\frac{r}{R}=\sqrt{\frac{(u+v)(u+w)(v+w)}{(u+v+w)(uv+uw+vw)}}=\sqrt{\frac{pq-uv
w}{pq}}=\sqrt{1-\frac{uvw}{(u+v+w)(uv+uw+vw)}}.$$

So everything reduces to the range of

$$t:=\frac{uvw}{(u+v+w)(uv+uw+vw)},\qquad u,v,w>0.$$

**Bounding $t$.** By AM–GM,

$$u+v+w\ge 3(uvw)^{1/3},\qquad uv+uw+vw\ge 3(uvw)^{2/3}.$$

Multiplying,

$$(u+v+w)(uv+uw+vw)\ge 9\,uvw \implies t\le \tfrac19,$$

with equality iff $u=v=w$ (the regular tetrahedron case). Also $t>0$ for $u,v,w>
0$, and $t\to 0$ as, say, $w\to 0^+$ (with $u,v$ fixed). Thus

$$t\in\bigl(0,\tfrac19\bigr].$$

**Validity of all parameter values.** The tetrahedron volume is

$$V=\tfrac16\bigl|\vec{DB}\times\vec{DM}\cdot\vec{DN}\bigr|=\tfrac86|xyz|=\tfrac
83|xyz|>0$$

for all $u,v,w>0$, so the tetrahedron is non‑degenerate throughout. The (congrue
nt) faces have sides $2\sqrt{v+w},\,2\sqrt{u+w},\,2\sqrt{u+v}$; the triangle ine
quality holds since e.g. $\sqrt{v+w}\le\sqrt v+\sqrt w\le\sqrt{u+v}+\sqrt{u+w}$,
 strictly for $u,v,w>0$. Hence every $t\in(0,\frac19]$ is attained.

**Conclusion.** Since $\frac rR=\sqrt{1-t}$ is decreasing in $t$,

$$\frac{r}{R}\in\left[\sqrt{1-\tfrac19},\,1\right)=\left[\sqrt{\tfrac89},\,1\rig
ht)=\left[\frac{2\sqrt2}{3},\,1\right).$$

The lower bound $\frac{2\sqrt2}{3}$ is attained at the regular tetrahedron ($u=v
=w$); the upper bound $1$ is approached (but never reached) as the tetrahedron d
egenerates to a planar configuration ($w\to0^+$).
