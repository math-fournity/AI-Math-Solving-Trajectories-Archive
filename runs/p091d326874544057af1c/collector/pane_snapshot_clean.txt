Looking at this problem, I need to find F(n) (smallest integer > n with same dig
it sum) for n = 1 to 1000, then sum all F(n).

## Algorithm for F(n)

Scan digits right-to-left. Find the rightmost position i where digit[i] < 9 and
the sum of digits in less significant positions > 0. Increment digit[i], then di
stribute (original sum of less significant digits − 1) into those positions, fil
ling from the least significant (to minimize the result). If no such position ex
ists, prepend a new digit 1 and distribute (digit sum − 1) similarly.

**Key insight:** When the ones digit > 0 and the tens digit < 9, we simply incre
ment tens and decrement ones, giving **F(n) = n + 9**.
