# Problem

In a specialized semiconductor facility, a technician is designing a rectangular microchip sensor array. The sensors must be laid out in a specific "staggered grid" pattern. In this pattern, the sensors are organized into horizontal rows that alternate in count: a long row of $n$ sensors followed by a short row of $n-1$ sensors. To maintain symmetry, the layout must always begin and end with a long row of $n$ sensors.

The design specifications require that:
1. The integer $n$ must be at least 2.
2. The array must contain more than one row.
3. The total number of sensors, $S$, is fixed for a given chip model.

A chip model is considered "flexible" if there are at least three distinct ways to choose the parameters (the value of $n$ and the total number of rows) to achieve the exact same total count of sensors $S$.

What is the minimum total number of sensors $S$ that a chip would need to contain so that there are at least three different ways to arrange them in this staggered grid?
