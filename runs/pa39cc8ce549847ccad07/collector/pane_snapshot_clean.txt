The conjecture asserts that every integer $n > 1$ can be written as
$$(2^a \cdot 3^b)^2 + (2^c \cdot 5^d)^2 + x^2 + y^2$$
for nonnegative integers $a, b, c, d, x, y$.

tively is what makes this substantially harder than Lagrange's four-square theor

 of two squares for some choice of $a,b,c,d$.
