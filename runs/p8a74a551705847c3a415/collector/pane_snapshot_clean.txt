$$
(a_1 b_1)(a_2 b_2) = a_1 \underbrace{(b_1 a_2 b_1^{-1})}_{\in\, A \text{ (since
} A \trianglelefteq G)} (b_1 b_2) \in AB.$$

- **Closure under inverses.** Take $ab \in AB$. Then
$$
(ab)^{-1} = b^{-1} a^{-1} = \underbrace{(b^{-1} a^{-1} b)}_{\in\, A \text{ (sinc
e } A \trianglelefteq G)} b^{-1} \in AB.$$

Since $1 \in AB$ and $AB$ is closed under products and inverses, $AB \leq G$. $\
square$

roup**), then for any two subgroups $A, B \leq G$, the product $AB$ is a subgrou
