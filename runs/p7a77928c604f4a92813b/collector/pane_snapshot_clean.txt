## Theorem

An odd perfect number cannot be divisible by 33.

## Proof

 $3 \mid N$ and $11 \mid N$.

 where $p \equiv \alpha \equiv 1 \pmod{4}$, $p$ is prime, and $\gcd(p, m) = 1$.
Consequently, any prime $q \equiv 3 \pmod{4}$ dividing $N$ must appear with **ev

$\sigma(11^2) = 1+11+121 = 133 = 7 \times 19$ divides $2N$. Since $133$ is odd,
$133 \mid N$, so $7 \mid N$ and $19 \mid N$.

3. **$7 \equiv 3 \pmod{4}$ and $7 \mid N$** $\Rightarrow$ $7^2 \mid N$.

4. **$19 \equiv 3 \pmod{4}$ and $19 \mid N$** $\Rightarrow$ $19^2 \mid N$.
