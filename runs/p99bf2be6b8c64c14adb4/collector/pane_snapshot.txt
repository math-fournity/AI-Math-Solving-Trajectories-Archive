## Solution

**Computing initial terms.** With $a_0=1$:
- $a_1 = a_0 a_0 = 1$
- $a_2 = a_1 a_1 = 1$
- $a_3 = a_2 a_0 + a_0 a_2 = 2$
- $a_4 = a_3 a_1 + a_1 a_3 = 4$
- $a_5 = a_4 a_0 + a_2 a_2 + a_0 a_4 = 9$
- $a_6 = a_5 a_1 + a_3 a_3 + a_1 a_5 = 22$

**Setting up the generating function.** Let $f(x)=\sum_{n\ge0}a_n x^n = E(x)+O(x
)$, where $E$ collects even powers and $O$ collects odd powers.

For odd $n=2m+1$: $a_{2m+1}=\sum_{k=0}^{m}a_{2m-2k}\,a_{2k}=[x^{2m}]\,E(x)^2$, s
o
$$O(x)=x\,E(x)^2.\tag{1}$$

For even $n=2m\ge2$: $a_{2m}=\sum_{k=0}^{m-1}a_{2(m-1-k)+1}\,a_{2k+1}=[x^{2(m-1)
}]\,(O(x)/x)^2$, so
$$E(x)=1+x^2\,(O(x)/x)^2=1+O(x)^2.\tag{2}$$

**Eliminating $O$.** From (2): $O^2=E-1$. From (1): $O^2=x^2 E^4$. Therefore
$$x^2 E^4 - E + 1 = 0.\tag{3}$$

**Applying the condition $f(r)=5/4$.** Let $y=E(r)$. Then $O(r)=r\,y^2$ and $f(r
)=y+r\,y^2=\tfrac54$, so
$$r\,y^2 = \tfrac54 - y \quad\Longrightarrow\quad r=\frac{5/4-y}{y^2}.\tag{4}$$
Substituting $r^2 y^4=(r y^2)^2=(\tfrac54-y)^2$ into (3) at $x=r$:
$$(\tfrac54-y)^2 = y-1.$$
Expanding: $\tfrac{25}{16}-\tfrac52 y+y^2=y-1$, i.e. $16y^2-56y+41=0$, giving
$$y=\frac{56\pm\sqrt{512}}{32}=\frac{7\pm 2\sqrt2}{4}.$$

Since $y=E(r)\ge1$ and $r>0$ requires $y<5/4$, only $y=\frac{7-2\sqrt2}{4}\appro
x1.043$ is valid.

**Computing $r$.** From (4):
$$r=\frac{5/4-y}{y^2}=\frac{(2\sqrt2-2)/4}{(57-28\sqrt2)/16}=\frac{8(\sqrt2-1)}{
57-28\sqrt2}.$$
Rationalizing by multiplying numerator and denominator by $57+28\sqrt2$:
- Numerator: $8(\sqrt2-1)(57+28\sqrt2)=8(29\sqrt2-1)=232\sqrt2-8$.
- Denominator: $57^2-(28\sqrt2)^2=3249-1568=1681=41^2$.

Thus
$$r=\frac{232\sqrt2-8}{1681}.$$

**Verification of constraints.** $b=2$ is squarefree. $\gcd(232,8,1681)=\gcd(8,1
681)=1$ since $1681=41^2$. ✓

Numerical check: $r\approx(232\cdot1.4142-8)/1681\approx0.1904$, and the radius
of convergence (singularity of $E$ at $y=4/3$) is $3\sqrt3/16\approx0.325>r$, so
 the series converges. ✓

With $a=232,\ b=2,\ c=8,\ d=1681$:
$$a+b+c+d=232+2+8+1681=1923.$$

Final Answer: $\boxed{1923}$
