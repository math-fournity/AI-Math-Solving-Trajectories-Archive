Looking at this problem, I need to find the minimum number of colored cells in a
 9×9 grid such that every cell with an even number of colored neighbors is color
ed. I interpret "neighbors" as the 8 adjacent cells (including diagonals).

## Proof

**Construction (9 suffices).** Partition the 9×9 grid into nine disjoint 3×3 blo
cks, where block $B_{i,j}$ (for $i,j \in \{0,1,2\}$) consists of rows $\{3i, 3i+
1, 3i+2\}$ and columns $\{3j, 3j+1, 3j+2\}$. Color the center cell of each block
, namely the 9 cells:
$$(1,1),\;(1,4),\;(1,7),\;(4,1),\;(4,4),\;(4,7),\;(7,1),\;(7,4),\;(7,7).$$

**Verification.** Every uncolored cell lies in some block $B_{i,j}$ and is adjac
ent (as a neighbor) to the center $(3i+1, 3j+1)$ of that block, since the center
 of a 3×3 block is adjacent to all 8 other cells in the block. I claim each unco
lored cell has **exactly 1** colored neighbor:

- **At least 1:** The center of its block is colored and adjacent to it.
- **At most 1:** Any two colored cells are at Chebyshev distance $\geq 3$ (they
are in different blocks, or the same block has only one colored cell). A cell ad
jacent to two colored cells would need to be within distance 1 of each, requirin
g the two colored cells to be within distance 2 of each other—contradicting the
minimum distance of 3.

So every uncolored cell has exactly 1 colored neighbor (odd), meaning no uncolor
ed cell has an even number of colored neighbors. Colored cells are trivially col
ored. The condition is satisfied with 9 colored cells.

**Lower bound (at least 9 needed).** Consider the center cell $c_{i,j} = (3i+1,\
, 3j+1)$ of each block $B_{i,j}$. All 8 neighbors of $c_{i,j}$ lie within $B_{i,
j}$ (its neighbors are exactly the other 8 cells of its 3×3 block).

Suppose some block $B_{i,j}$ contains no colored cell. Then $c_{i,j}$ is uncolor
ed and has **0** colored neighbors (all its neighbors are in $B_{i,j}$, which ha
s none). Since $0$ is even, $c_{i,j}$ must be colored—contradiction.

Therefore every block must contain at least one colored cell. Since the 9 blocks
 are disjoint, we need at least **9** colored cells.

**Conclusion.** The minimum number of colored cells is $\boxed{9}$.
