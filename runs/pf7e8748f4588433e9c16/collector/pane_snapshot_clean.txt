## Structural Analysis of $f(f(n))=2n$

**Key properties:**
- **Injectivity:** If $f(a)=f(b)$, then $2a=2b$, so $a=b$.
- **Doubling rule:** $f(2n)=f(f(f(n)))=2f(n)$, so $f(2^a \cdot m)=2^a \cdot f(m)
$ for odd $m$.

Thus $f$ is determined by its action on odd numbers. For odd $m$, write $f(m)=2^
b \cdot m'$ with $m'$ odd. Then $f(f(m))=2^b\cdot f(m')=2m$ forces $b\in\{0,1\}$
.

**Chain structure:** The odd positive integers partition into pairs $\{a,b\}$. F
or each pair, one of two orientations:
- **Orientation 1:** $f(a)=b$, $f(b)=2a$. Chain: $a\to b\to 2a\to 2b\to 4a\to 4b
\to\cdots$
- **Orientation 2:** $f(a)=2b$, $f(b)=a$. Chain: $b\to a\to 2b\to 2a\to 4b\to 4a
\to\cdots$

Each pair $\{a,b\}$ generates the chain covering $\{2^j a,\, 2^j b : j\ge 0\}$,
and any valid partition+orientation gives a valid $f$. Since infinitely many odd
 numbers exist, after fixing one pair, the rest can always be paired.
