The statement is **false**. A homeomorphism $f:A\to B$ only preserves the intrin
sic topology of the subspaces $A$ and $B$; it says nothing about how they are em
bedded in their ambient spaces $U$ and $V$. The boundary $\delta A$ (resp. $\del
ta B$) is defined using the ambient space — $\delta A = \operatorname{cl}_U(A)\s
etminus \operatorname{int}_U(A)$ — and is therefore sensitive to the embedding,
not just to the topology of $A$ itself. The following counterexample exploits ex
actly this.

**Counterexample.** Let $U = \mathbb{R}$ with the usual topology and let $A = \{
0\} \subset U$. Let $V = \{0\}$ with the unique (indiscrete = discrete, since it
 is a singleton) topology, and let $B = \{0\} = V$. Let $f:A\to B$ be the unique
 map $0\mapsto 0$, which is trivially a homeomorphism (both spaces are singleton
s).

- **Boundary of $A$ in $U$:** $\operatorname{cl}_{\mathbb{R}}(\{0\}) = \{0\}$ an
d $\operatorname{int}_{\mathbb{R}}(\{0\}) = \varnothing$ (no nonempty open subse
t of $\mathbb{R}$ is contained in a singleton). Hence
$$\delta A = \{0\}\setminus\varnothing = \{0\}.$$
So $0 \in \delta A$.

- **Boundary of $B$ in $V$:** $\operatorname{cl}_{V}(\{0\}) = \{0\}$ and $\opera
torname{int}_{V}(\{0\}) = \{0\}$ (since $\{0\}=V$ is open in $V$). Hence
$$\delta B = \{0\}\setminus\{0\} = \varnothing.$$

Now take $a = 0 \in A$. We have $a \in \delta A$, but
$$f(a) = 0 \notin \varnothing = \delta B.$$

Thus the implication "$a\in\delta A \Rightarrow f(a)\in\delta B$" fails.

**Why this happens.** The point $0$ is a boundary point of $A$ in $\mathbb{R}$ b
ecause $\{0\}$ has empty interior in the large ambient space $\mathbb{R}$. But w
hen the very same point is regarded as a subspace of the singleton space $V=\{0\

 and hence not a boundary point. The homeomorphism $f$ — being purely intrinsic
to $A$ and $B$ — cannot detect this difference in how the two copies of the sing
leton sit inside their respective ambient spaces. Boundary membership is an extr
insic, embedding-dependent property, and homeomorphism of subspaces does not pre
serve it.
