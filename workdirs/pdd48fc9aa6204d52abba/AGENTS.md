# Problem

In the city of Aethelgard, a jeweler is designing a special diamond-shaped pendant in the form of a rhombus. This pendant consists of four distinct outer wire segments (the sides of the rhombus). 

To finish the piece, the jeweler will coat each of the four segments with a single solid color. She has a palette of exactly six unique enamel colors available. Any side can be painted in any of the six colors, and colors can be reused on multiple sides of the same pendant.

However, because the pendant is a rhombus (where all four sides are of equal length), two pendants are considered identical if one can be rotated to look exactly like the other within the plane. Note that for this specific design, the jeweler does not consider "flipping" or reflecting the pendant, only rotations.

Given these six available colors, how many distinct ways can the four sides of the rhombus be painted?
