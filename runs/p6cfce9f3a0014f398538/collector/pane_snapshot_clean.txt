## Solution

We count sequences $(a_1,\ldots,a_{10})$ with $a_i\in\{1,2,3,4\}$ that are **zig
zag**: consecutive differences alternate in sign, i.e., $(a_i-a_{i+1})(a_{i+1}-a
_{i+2})<0$ for all $i=1,\ldots,8$.

**Setup.** Define:
- $U_k(j)$ = count of valid zigzag sequences of length $k$ ending in value $j$,
last step **up** ($a_{k-1}<a_k$).
- $D_k(j)$ = count ending in value $j$, last step **down** ($a_{k-1}>a_k$).

**Base case** ($k=1$): $s_1(j)=1$ for each $j\in\{1,2,3,4\}$.

**Recurrence** ($k=2$): $U_2(j)=\sum_{i<j}s_1(i)=j-1$, $\;D_2(j)=\sum_{i>j}s_1(i
)=4-j$.

**Recurrence** ($k\geq 3$): An up step must follow a down step, and vice versa:
$$U_k(j)=\sum_{i<j}D_{k-1}(i),\qquad D_k(j)=\sum_{i>j}U_{k-1}(i).$$
