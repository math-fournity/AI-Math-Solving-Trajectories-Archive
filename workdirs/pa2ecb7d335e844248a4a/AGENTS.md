# Problem

In a square city district, the layout is organized into an $n \times n$ grid of identical building plots. Within this grid, exactly 101 plots are designated as "Central Hubs" and are equipped with a specialized fiber-optic transmitter.

A telecommunications firm is tasked with dividing the entire $n \times n$ district into several non-overlapping rectangular service zones. The firm must follow two strict constraints:
1. Every single plot in the district must belong to exactly one service zone.
2. The boundaries of the zones must follow the existing grid lines.
3. Each service zone must contain exactly one Central Hub.

After a site survey, the engineers determine that there is only one unique way to partition the district into these rectangular zones to satisfy the requirements. Given that $n$ is the dimension of the square grid, what is the minimum possible value of $n$?
