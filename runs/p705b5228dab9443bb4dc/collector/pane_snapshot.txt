## Proof

We are given that there exists a positive integer $k$ such that

$$k^3 - mk^2 - nk + 2015 = 0,$$

i.e., $k^3 + 2015 = mk^2 + nk = k(mk+n)$. Since $k>0$, we can divide:

$$mk + n = k^2 + \frac{2015}{k}.$$

For the right side to be an integer, $k \mid 2015$. Factoring:

$$2015 = 5 \times 13 \times 31,$$

so the positive divisors are $k \in \{1, 5, 13, 31, 65, 155, 403, 2015\}$.

From $mk + n = k^2 + 2015/k$, we get

$$m + n = k^2 + \frac{2015}{k} - m(k-1).$$

For $k=1$: $m+n = 1 + 2015 = 2016$ (fixed regardless of $m,n$).

For $k > 1$: since $k-1 > 0$, minimizing $m+n$ means **maximizing** $m$, subject
 to $m \ge 1$ and $n = k^2 + 2015/k - mk \ge 1$, i.e., $m \le \frac{k^2 + 2015/k
 - 1}{k}$.

| $k$ | $k^2+2015/k$ | max $m$ | $n$ | $m+n$ |
|-----|--------------|---------|-----|-------|
| 5   | 428          | 85      | 3   | 88    |
| 13  | 324          | 24      | 12  | **36**|
| 31  | 1026         | 33      | 3   | **36**|
| 65  | 4256         | 65      | 31  | 96    |
| 155 | 24038        | 155     | 13  | 168   |
| 403 | 162414       | 403     | 5   | 408   |
| 2015| large        | —       | —   | large |

**Verification of $k=13, m=24, n=12$:**
$$13^3 - 24\cdot 169 - 12\cdot 13 + 2015 = 2197 - 4056 - 156 + 2015 = 4212 - 421
2 = 0.\ \checkmark$$

**Verification of $k=31, m=33, n=3$:**
$$31^3 - 33\cdot 961 - 3\cdot 31 + 2015 = 29791 - 31713 - 93 + 2015 = 31806 - 31
806 = 0.\ \checkmark$$

Both give $m+n = 36$, and no divisor of $2015$ yields a smaller value. Therefore
 the minimum of $m+n$ is

$$\boxed{36}.$$
