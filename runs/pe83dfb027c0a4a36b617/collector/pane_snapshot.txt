### Setup

Label the 3×3 grid cells:
```
a b c
d e f
g h i
```
with 12 adjacencies (edges). We place a permutation of {1,…,9} so that every adj
acent pair is relatively prime (gcd = 1).

**Forbidden pairs** (gcd > 1):
- Among evens {2,4,6,8}: all 6 pairs share factor 2.
- Among multiples of 3: (3,6), (3,9), (6,9) — 3 pairs.

Total: 9 forbidden pairs.

### Key structural observation

All four even numbers {2,4,6,8} are pairwise incompatible, so **no two even numb
ers can be adjacent**. Thus the 4 even-occupied cells must form an **independent
 set of size 4** in the grid graph.

The 3×3 grid is bipartite with color classes:
- **Black** B = {a, c, e, g, i} (5 cells, independent set of size 5)
- **White** W = {b, d, f, h} (4 cells, independent set of size 4)

### Enumerating independent sets of size 4

Since every white cell is adjacent to 3 black cells, choosing any white cell eli
minates ≥3 black cells. Systematic enumeration:

| k (from W) | Available from B | Sets of size 4 |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | — | {b,d,f,h}: 1 |
| 3 | 0 remaining | 0 |
| 2 | ≤1 remaining | 0 |
| 1 | ≤2 remaining | 0 |
| 0 | choose 4 of 5 | C(5,4) = 5 |

**6 independent sets total:**
1. {b,d,f,h} (the cross)
2–6. B minus each corner: {a,c,e,g}, {a,c,e,i}, {a,c,g,i}, {a,e,g,i}, {c,e,g,i}

### Counting valid assignments per placement

For each placement of evens, assign {2,4,6,8} to E (4! ways) and {1,3,5,7,9} to
O (5! ways), subject to:
- **3 and 9 not adjacent** (both odd)
- **3 not adjacent to 6**, **9 not adjacent to 6** (6 is even, in E)

Fixing 6's position: let N(6)∩O = odd neighbors of 6. Then 3,9 must avoid those
cells, and 3,9 must be mutually non-adjacent. The remaining 3 odd numbers {1,5,7
} are unconstrained (1,5,7 are coprime to everything), giving 3! arrangements. T
he remaining 3 evens {2,4,8} fill the other 3 even cells in 3! ways.

**Formula per sub-case (6 fixed):** (valid {3,9} placements) × 2 × 3! × 3!
