## Proof

Let $F = \langle a, b \rangle$ be the free group on two generators, and let $N$
be the normal subgroup generated by $\{b^2, ab\}$. We compute the index $[F : N]
 = |F/N|$.

**Step 1: Determine the quotient $F/N$.**

By definition, $F/N$ is the group presented by
$$F/N = \langle a, b \mid b^2 = 1,\; ab = 1 \rangle.$$

**Step 2: Simplify the relations.**

From $ab = 1$, we get $a = b^{-1}$. From $b^2 = 1$, we get $b^{-1} = b$. Combini
ng these:
$$a = b^{-1} = b.$$

So $a$ and $b$ represent the same element in $F/N$, and the quotient is generate
d by the single element $\bar{b}$ (the image of $b$) subject to the single relat
ion $\bar{b}^2 = 1$.

**Step 3: Identify the quotient.**

Thus
$$F/N \cong \langle \bar{b} \mid \bar{b}^2 = 1 \rangle \cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb
{Z}.$$

**Step 4: Compute the index.**

The index of $N$ in $F$ equals the order of the quotient:
$$[F : N] = |F/N| = |\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}| = 2.$$
