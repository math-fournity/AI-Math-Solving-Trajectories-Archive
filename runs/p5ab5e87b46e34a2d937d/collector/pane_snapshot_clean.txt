## Setup

Let the trapezoid have $AB \parallel CD$ with $AB = BC = AD = a$ and $CD < a$. P

- $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (a, 0)$, $D = (d, h)$, $C = (a-d, h)$

where $d > 0$, $d < a/2$, and $AD = \sqrt{d^2 + h^2} = a$, so $h = \sqrt{a^2 - d
^2}$. The top base is $CD = a - 2d$.

By symmetry, both circles lie on the axis $x = a/2$.

## Circle $k_1$ (tangent to $AD$, $AB$, $BC$)

$k_1$ is tangent to $AB$ (the line $y=0$) from above, so its center is $(a/2,\,
r_1)$. The distance from this center to line $AD: hx - dy = 0$ equals $r_1$:

$$\frac{ha/2 - d\,r_1}{\sqrt{h^2+d^2}} = r_1 \implies \frac{ha/2 - d\,r_1}{a} =
r_1$$

$$r_1 = \frac{ha}{2(a+d)}$$

## Circle $k_2$ (tangent to $BC$, $CD$, $AD$)

$k_2$ is tangent to $CD$ (the line $y = h$) from below, so its center is $(a/2,\
, h - r_2)$. The distance to line $AD$ equals $r_2$:

$$\frac{|h \cdot a/2 - d(h - r_2)|}{a} = r_2 \implies \frac{h(a/2 - d) + d\,r_2}
{a} = r_2$$

$$r_2 = \frac{h(a - 2d)}{2(a - d)}$$

## External tangency condition

The centers are at heights $r_1$ and $h - r_2$. The distance between them is $h
- r_2 - r_1$. For external tangency:

$$h - r_1 - r_2 = r_1 + r_2 \implies h = 2(r_1 + r_2)$$

Substituting:

$$r_1 + r_2 = \frac{h}{2}\!\left[\frac{a}{a+d} + \frac{a-2d}{a-d}\right] = \frac
{h}{2}\cdot\frac{a(a-d)+(a-2d)(a+d)}{a^2-d^2}$$

The numerator: $a^2 - ad + a^2 - ad - 2d^2 = 2a^2 - 2ad - 2d^2$, so:

$$r_1 + r_2 = \frac{h(a^2 - ad - d^2)}{a^2 - d^2}$$

The condition $h = 2(r_1 + r_2)$ gives:

$$1 = \frac{2(a^2 - ad - d^2)}{a^2 - d^2} \implies a^2 - d^2 = 2a^2 - 2ad - 2d^2
$$

$$a^2 - 2ad - d^2 = 0$$

Setting $u = d/a$: $u^2 + 2u - 1 = 0$, so $u = \sqrt{2} - 1$ (taking the positiv

## Computing $x = r_2/r_1$

$$x = \frac{r_2}{r_1} = \frac{h(a-2d)/[2(a-d)]}{ha/[2(a+d)]} = \frac{(a-2d)(a+d)

$$x = \frac{(1 - 2(\sqrt{2}-1))\cdot(1 + \sqrt{2}-1)}{1 \cdot (1-(\sqrt{2}-1))}

$$x = \frac{2\sqrt{2}-2}{2} = \sqrt{2}-1$$
