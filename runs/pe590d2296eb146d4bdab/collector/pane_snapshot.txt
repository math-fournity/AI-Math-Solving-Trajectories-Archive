**Setup.** Let $T = \mathbb{S}^1 \times \mathbb{S}^1$ be the torus, and let $X$
be the space obtained by attaching a 2-cell to $T$ via the attaching map $f:\mat
hbb{S}^1 \to T$, $f(\zeta) = (\zeta^2, \zeta^3)$.

**Step 1: Fundamental group of the torus.** The torus has a standard CW-structur
e with one 0-cell, two 1-cells (giving generators $a, b$ for the two circle fact
ors), and one 2-cell attached via the commutator $aba^{-1}b^{-1}$. Hence
$$\pi_1(T) = \langle\, a, b \mid aba^{-1}b^{-1} = 1 \,\rangle \cong \mathbb{Z} \
oplus \mathbb{Z}.$$

**Step 2: The class of the attaching map.** The map $f(\zeta) = (\zeta^2, \zeta^
3)$ is a loop in $T$. As $\zeta$ traverses $\mathbb{S}^1$ once, the first coordi
nate winds around the first $\mathbb{S}^1$ factor with degree $2$, and the secon
d coordinate winds around the second $\mathbb{S}^1$ factor with degree $3$. Ther
efore, in $\pi_1(T)$, the homotopy class of $f$ is
$$[f] = a^2\, b^3.$$

**Step 3: Apply Seifert–van Kampen.** Attaching a 2-cell to a path-connected spa
ce $T$ via a map $f$ kills the normal closure of $[f]$ in $\pi_1(T)$. Since $\pi
_1(T)$ is abelian, every subgroup is normal, so
$$\pi_1(X) = \pi_1(T)\,/\,\langle\!\langle [f] \rangle\!\rangle = \langle\, a, b
 \mid aba^{-1}b^{-1} = 1,\; a^2 b^3 = 1 \,\rangle.$$

**Step 4: Simplify the presentation.** The first relation makes the group abelia
n. Writing additively, the group is
$$G = \big(\mathbb{Z} \oplus \mathbb{Z}\big)\,/\,\langle (2, 3) \rangle.$$

**Step 5: Compute the quotient.** Since $\gcd(2, 3) = 1$, the vector $(2, 3)$ is
 **primitive** in $\mathbb{Z}^2$, meaning it can be extended to a basis of $\mat
hbb{Z}^2$. Concretely, the matrix
$$M = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 3 & 2 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \det M = 4 - 3 = 1,$$
lies in $\mathrm{GL}(2, \mathbb{Z})$, so the columns $(2,3)$ and $(1,2)$ form a
$\mathbb{Z}$-basis $\{e_1', e_2'\}$ of $\mathbb{Z}^2$. Under this change of basi
s,
$$\mathbb{Z}^2 = \mathbb{Z}\,e_1' \oplus \mathbb{Z}\,e_2', \qquad \langle (2,3)
\rangle = \mathbb{Z}\,e_1',$$
so
$$G \cong \big(\mathbb{Z}\,e_1' \oplus \mathbb{Z}\,e_2'\big) / \mathbb{Z}\,e_1'
\cong \mathbb{Z}\,e_2' \cong \mathbb{Z}.$$

Equivalently, the Smith normal form of the relation matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 2 &
3 \end{pmatrix}$ has single invariant factor $\gcd(2,3) = 1$, confirming $G \con
g \mathbb{Z} \oplus \mathbb{Z}/1\mathbb{Z} \cong \mathbb{Z}$.

**Conclusion.** The fundamental group of the space obtained by attaching a 2-cel

$$\boxed{\pi_1(X) \cong \mathbb{Z}}.$$
