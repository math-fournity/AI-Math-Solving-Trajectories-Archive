## Solution

Let $a, b, c$ be i.i.d. uniform on $\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}$, the roots of $f(x)=x^3+px^
2+qx+r$. By Vieta's formulas:
$$p=-(a+b+c),\quad q=ab+bc+ca,\quad r=-abc.$$
So $p^2+q^2+r^2 = (a+b+c)^2+(ab+bc+ca)^2+(abc)^2$.

**Useful moments.** $E[a]=\tfrac{7}{2}$, $E[a^2]=\tfrac{1+4+9+16+25+36}{6}=\tfra
c{91}{6}$, $\mathrm{Var}(a)=\tfrac{91}{6}-\tfrac{49}{4}=\tfrac{35}{12}$.

**Term 1: $E[(a+b+c)^2]$.** Since $a,b,c$ independent,
$$E[(a+b+c)^2]=\mathrm{Var}(S)+E[S]^2=3\cdot\tfrac{35}{12}+\left(\tfrac{21}{2}\r
ight)^2=\tfrac{35}{4}+\tfrac{441}{4}=\tfrac{476}{4}=119.$$

**Term 2: $E[(abc)^2]$.** By independence,
$$E[(abc)^2]=E[a^2]^3=\left(\tfrac{91}{6}\right)^3=\tfrac{753571}{216},\quad\tex
t{since }91^3=753571.$$

**Term 3: $E[(ab+bc+ca)^2]$.** Expanding:
$$(ab+bc+ca)^2=a^2b^2+b^2c^2+c^2a^2+2ab^2c+2a^2bc+2abc^2.$$
- Diagonal terms: $3\,E[a^2b^2]=3\,E[a^2]^2=3\cdot\tfrac{8281}{36}=\tfrac{8281}{
12}$.
- Cross terms: by symmetry each equals $E[a]E[b^2]E[c]=\tfrac{49}{4}\cdot\tfrac{
91}{6}=\tfrac{4459}{24}$; total $6\cdot\tfrac{4459}{24}=\tfrac{4459}{4}$.

$$E[(ab+bc+ca)^2]=\tfrac{8281}{12}+\tfrac{4459}{4}=\tfrac{8281+13377}{12}=\tfrac
{21658}{12}=\tfrac{10829}{6}.$$

**Sum.** Common denominator $216$:
$$E[p^2+q^2+r^2]=119+\tfrac{10829}{6}+\tfrac{753571}{216}=\tfrac{25704+389844+75
3571}{216}=\tfrac{1169119}{216}.$$

**Reduction.** $216=2^3\cdot 3^3$. The numerator $1169119$ is odd (ends in $9$)
and its digit sum is $1+1+6+9+1+1+9=28$, not divisible by $3$. Hence $\gcd(11691
19,216)=1$, so $m=1169119$, $n=216$.

$$m+n=1169335,\qquad 1169335 \bmod 1000 = \boxed{335}.$$
