**Key geometric fact.** If $p \notin K_i$ and $K_i$ is convex, the set of direct

**Upper bound via the hole-counting formula.** The maximum number of holes (boun

 the recurrence:
$$h(n,d) = h(n-1,d) + \binom{n-2}{d-1}, \quad h(1,d)=0.$$
Adding the $n$-th convex set creates at most $\binom{n-2}{d-1}$ new holes (the n
umber of $(d{-}1)$-dimensional cells on its boundary created by intersection wit
h the other sets). By the hockey-stick identity:
$$h(n,d) = \sum_{k=0}^{n-2}\binom{k}{d-1} = \binom{n-1}{d}.$$
Since isolated complement points are single-point bounded components, $|S| \leq
h(3,d) = \binom{2}{d}$.
