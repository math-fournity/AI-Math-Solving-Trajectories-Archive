## Solution

**Parity constraint.** The condition $f(n) - n \equiv 1 \pmod{2}$ means $f(n)$ a
nd $n$ have opposite parity. So $f$ maps the 4 odd elements $\{1,3,5,7\}$ biject
ively onto the 4 even elements $\{2,4,6,8\}$, and vice versa. The total number o
f such parity-alternating bijections is $4!\times 4! = 576$.

**No 2-cycles.** The condition $f(f(n))\neq n$ for all $n$ means $f$ has no 2-cy
cle (transposition). Since every cycle of a parity-alternating permutation has e
ven length, and we have 8 elements (4 odd + 4 even), the allowed cycle structure
s with no 2-cycles are:

- one 8-cycle, or
- two 4-cycles.

(Structures like 6+2, 4+2+2, 2+2+2+2 all contain 2-cycles and are excluded.)

### Inclusion–Exclusion

Let $N_k$ = number of parity-alternating bijections containing a specified set o
f $k$ disjoint 2-cycles. Choosing $k$ disjoint 2-cycles requires selecting $k$ o
dds, $k$ evens, and pairing them: $\binom{4}{k}^2 k!$ ways. The remaining $4-k$
odds and $4-k$ evens can be mapped freely: $((4-k)!)^2$ ways. By inclusion–exclu
sion:

$$\text{Answer} = \sum_{k=0}^{4}(-1)^k\,\binom{4}{k}^2\, k!\, \big((4-k)!\big)^2
.$$

Computing each term:

| $k$ | $\binom{4}{k}^2$ | $k!$ | $((4-k)!)^2$ | term |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1 | 1 | 576 | $+576$ |
| 1 | 16 | 1 | 36 | $-576$ |
| 2 | 36 | 2 | 4 | $+288$ |
| 3 | 16 | 6 | 1 | $-96$ |
| 4 | 1 | 24 | 1 | $+24$ |

$$576 - 576 + 288 - 96 + 24 = \boxed{216}.$$

### Verification by direct counting

**Two 4-cycles.** Partition 4 odds into 2 pairs: 3 ways; partition 4 evens into
2 pairs: 3 ways; match the 2 odd-pairs to the 2 even-pairs: $2!$ ways. For each
matched pair $\{o_1,o_2\},\{e_1,e_2\}$, the alternating 4-cycles are $o_1{\to}e_
1{\to}o_2{\to}e_2$ and $o_1{\to}e_2{\to}o_2{\to}e_1$: 2 choices each. Total: $3\
times3\times2\times2\times2 = 72$.

**One 8-cycle.** Fix one odd element at the start (removing cyclic symmetry). Ar
range the 3 remaining odds in the 3 odd slots ($3!$ ways) and 4 evens in the 4 e
ven slots ($4!$ ways): $3!\times4! = 144$.

Total: $72 + 144 = 216$. ✓
