# Problem

A high-tech manufacturing plant utilizes a specialized security protocol to verify 2014 unique microchips produced in the year 2014. To certify each chip, a panel of six quality control inspectors must apply a security seal. For every chip, each inspector must choose exactly one of three available wax colors (Red, Blue, or Green) to create their seal.

The complete configuration of seal colors across all inspectors and all chips is recorded in a massive $6 \times 2014$ digital ledger known as the "Authentication Matrix." 

A safety auditor is searching for a specific type of Authentication Matrix. He wants a configuration where, within any subset of $n$ chips, one can always find a group of 8 specific chips such that for any two chips in that group, the six inspectors have chosen the same wax color for no more than four of their seals.

Find the smallest positive integer $n$ for which such an Authentication Matrix can be constructed.
