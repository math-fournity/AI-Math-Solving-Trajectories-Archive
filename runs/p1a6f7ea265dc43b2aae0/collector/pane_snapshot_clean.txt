## Solution

Let the total work be 100 units. Then bad work = 89, good work = 11.

The robot classifies each paper as either **credited** or **uncredited**. Let:
- $x$ = bad papers classified as uncredited (bad papers rechecked)
- $a$ = bad papers classified as credited
- $y$ = good papers classified as uncredited
- $b$ = good papers classified as credited

**Constraints:**
- $x + a = 89$ (all bad work)
- $y + b = 11$ (all good work)
- Misclassifications = bad→credited + good→uncredited = $a + y = 15$

**Express everything in terms of $a$:**
- $x = 89 - a$
- $y = 15 - a$
- $b = 11 - y = 11 - (15 - a) = a - 4$

**Non-negativity constraints:**
- $y \geq 0 \Rightarrow a \leq 15$
- $b \geq 0 \Rightarrow a \geq 4$
- $a \geq 0$, $x \geq 0$ (automatically satisfied)

So $a \in [4, 15]$.

**Objective:** minimize $x = 89 - a$, which means **maximize $a$**.

Taking $a = 15$: $x = 89 - 15 = 74$, $y = 0$, $b = 11$.

**Verification:** Bad work: 74 uncredited + 15 credited (misclassified). Good wo
rk: 0 uncredited + 11 credited (all correct). Total misclassifications = 15 + 0
= 15 ✓. All bad papers rechecked = 74.

The smallest percentage of bad papers experts recheck is **74**.
