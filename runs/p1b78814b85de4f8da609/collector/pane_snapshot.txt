## Proof

Let $P^n = \mathbb{RP}^n$ be real projective $n$-space, $p \in \mathbb{RP}^n$ a
point, and $n$ **even**. We determine $H_{n-1}(\mathbb{RP}^n - p)$ via the long
exact sequence (LES) of the pair $(\mathbb{RP}^n, \mathbb{RP}^n - p)$.

### Step 1. The relative homology $H_*(\mathbb{RP}^n, \mathbb{RP}^n - p)$ via ex
cision

Let $U$ be a small open ball around $p$ in $\mathbb{RP}^n$. By excision of $\mat
hbb{RP}^n - U$,
$$
H_k(\mathbb{RP}^n, \mathbb{RP}^n - p) \cong H_k(U, U - p).
$$
Since $U \cong \mathbb{R}^n$ is contractible, the LES of $(U, U-p)$ gives
$$
H_k(U, U-p) \cong \widetilde{H}_{k-1}(U - p) \cong \widetilde{H}_{k-1}(S^{n-1}).
$$
Hence
$$
H_k(\mathbb{RP}^n, \mathbb{RP}^n - p) \cong \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & k = n, \\
 0 & k \neq n. \end{cases}
$$

### Step 2. Homology of $\mathbb{RP}^n$ for $n$ even

Recall the standard homology of $\mathbb{RP}^n$:
$$
H_k(\mathbb{RP}^n) \cong \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & k = 0, \\ \mathbb{Z}/2 & k \
text{ odd},\; 1 \le k \le n-1, \\ 0 & k \text{ even},\; 2 \le k \le n. \end{case
s}
$$
For $n$ **even**: $\mathbb{RP}^n$ is non-orientable, so $H_n(\mathbb{RP}^n) = 0$
, and $n-1$ is odd so $H_{n-1}(\mathbb{RP}^n) = \mathbb{Z}/2$.

### Step 3. The relevant portion of the LES

The LES of the pair $(\mathbb{RP}^n, \mathbb{RP}^n - p)$ contains
$$
H_n(\mathbb{RP}^n) \to H_n(\mathbb{RP}^n, \mathbb{RP}^n - p) \xrightarrow{\parti
al} H_{n-1}(\mathbb{RP}^n - p) \to H_{n-1}(\mathbb{RP}^n) \to H_{n-1}(\mathbb{RP
}^n, \mathbb{RP}^n - p).
$$
Substituting the values from Steps 1–2 (with $n$ even):
$$
0 \to \mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{\partial} H_{n-1}(\mathbb{RP}^n - p) \to \mathbb{Z
}/2 \to 0.
$$
This is a **short exact sequence**, so $H_{n-1}(\mathbb{RP}^n - p)$ is an extens
ion of $\mathbb{Z}/2$ by $\mathbb{Z}$. Such extensions are classified by $\opera
torname{Ext}^1(\mathbb{Z}/2, \mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}/2$, giving two possibi
lities: $\mathbb{Z} \oplus \mathbb{Z}/2$ (split) or $\mathbb{Z}$ (non-split, $\p
artial$ = multiplication by $2$). We resolve this ambiguity by identifying $\par
tial$.

### Step 4. Identifying the boundary map $\partial$

The generator of $H_n(\mathbb{RP}^n, \mathbb{RP}^n - p) \cong \mathbb{Z}$ is rep
resented by the local $n$-ball $U$ around $p$. Its boundary $\partial U \cong S^
{n-1}$ lies in $\mathbb{RP}^n - p$, and $\partial$ sends $[U]$ to $[\partial U]
\in H_{n-1}(\mathbb{RP}^n - p)$.

Now use the standard fact that $\mathbb{RP}^n - p$ deformation retracts onto $\m
athbb{RP}^{n-1}$ (remove a point from the interior of the top cell $e^n$ of the
CW-structure; the remaining space retracts to the $(n-1)$-skeleton $\mathbb{RP}^
{n-1}$). Under this retraction, the small sphere $\partial U = S^{n-1}$ around $
p$ maps to $\mathbb{RP}^{n-1}$ as the **universal (double) cover** $S^{n-1} \to
\mathbb{RP}^{n-1}$.

Since $n$ is even, $n-1$ is odd, so $\mathbb{RP}^{n-1}$ is **orientable** and $H
_{n-1}(\mathbb{RP}^{n-1}) \cong \mathbb{Z}$. The double cover $S^{n-1} \to \math
bb{RP}^{n-1}$ has degree $2$, so the induced map
$$
(\pi)_*: H_{n-1}(S^{n-1}) \cong \mathbb{Z} \longrightarrow H_{n-1}(\mathbb{RP}^{
n-1}) \cong \mathbb{Z}
$$
is **multiplication by $2$**. Therefore, under the identification $H_{n-1}(\math
bb{RP}^n - p) \cong H_{n-1}(\mathbb{RP}^{n-1}) \cong \mathbb{Z}$, the boundary m
ap
$$
\partial: \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}
$$
is **multiplication by $2$**, which is injective with cokernel $\mathbb{Z}/2$.

### Step 5. Conclusion

The short exact sequence
$$
0 \to \mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{\times 2} \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}/2 \to 0
$$
is the **non-split** extension, confirming
$$
H_{n-1}(\mathbb{RP}^n - p) \cong \mathbb{Z}.
$$
The cokernel $\mathbb{Z}/2 \cong H_{n-1}(\mathbb{RP}^n)$ matches the known homol
ogy of $\mathbb{RP}^n$, providing a consistency check.

$$
\boxed{H_{n-1}(P^n - p) \cong \mathbb{Z} \quad \text{for even } n.}
$$
