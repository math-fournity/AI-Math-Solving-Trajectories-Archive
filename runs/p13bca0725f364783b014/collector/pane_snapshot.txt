## Solution

From the Asymptote code, the coordinates are:

- $A=(0,0)$, $B=(17,0)$, $E=(0,17)$, $D=(17,17)$ — so $ABDE$ is a square of side
 $17$.
- $F=\left(-\tfrac{120}{17},\tfrac{225}{17}\right)$, $C=\left(17+\tfrac{120}{17}
,\tfrac{64}{17}\right)=\left(\tfrac{409}{17},\tfrac{64}{17}\right)$.

**Verification of the given lengths and right angles.**

- $FA^2=\left(\tfrac{120}{17}\right)^2+\left(\tfrac{225}{17}\right)^2=\tfrac{144
00+50625}{289}=\tfrac{65025}{289}=225$, so $FA=15$. ✓
- $EF^2=\left(\tfrac{120}{17}\right)^2+\left(\tfrac{64}{17}\right)^2=\tfrac{1849
6}{289}=64$, so $EF=8$. ✓
- $BC^2=\left(\tfrac{120}{17}\right)^2+\left(\tfrac{64}{17}\right)^2=64$, so $BC
=8$. ✓
- $DC^2=\left(\tfrac{120}{17}\right)^2+\left(\tfrac{225}{17}\right)^2=225$, so $
DC=15$. ✓

The right-angle marks at $F$ (in $\triangle EFA$) and at $C$ (in $\triangle DCB$
) are consistent with these being right triangles with legs $8,15$ and hypotenus
e $17$ (since $8^2+15^2=17^2$), matching the square's side.

**Computing $CF$.**

$$\vec{FC}=C-F=\left(\frac{409}{17}+\frac{120}{17},\;\frac{64}{17}-\frac{225}{17
}\right)=\left(\frac{529}{17},\;-\frac{161}{17}\right).$$

$$CF^2=\frac{529^2+161^2}{17^2}=\frac{279841+25921}{289}=\frac{305762}{289}.$$

Since $289\times 1058 = 305762$, we get $CF^2=1058$.

**Simplifying.** $1058 = 2\times 529 = 2\times 23^2$, so

$$CF=\sqrt{1058}=23\sqrt{2}.$$

Thus $a=23$, $b=2$ (with $b$ square-free), and

$$a+b=23+2=\boxed{25}.$$
