# Problem

An industrial chemical plant operates three interconnected processing units with efficiency ratings $a, b,$ and $c$ (where each rating is a positive real number). The plant’s flow equilibrium dictates that the product of all three ratings, when added to the sum of the first and third ratings, must exactly equal the second rating ($abc + a + c = b$).

The facility's total performance index, $P$, is calculated based on the specific stability of these units. The index is determined by taking double the reciprocal of the first unit's stability factor ($a^2 + 1$), subtracting double the reciprocal of the second unit's stability factor ($b^2 + 1$), and adding triple the reciprocal of the third unit's stability factor ($c^2 + 1$).

What is the maximum possible value of the performance index $P = \frac{2}{a^2+1} - \frac{2}{b^2+1} + \frac{3}{c^2+1}$?
