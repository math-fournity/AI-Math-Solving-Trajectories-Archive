# Problem

In a specialized logistics hub, the efficiency rating of a cargo shipment, denoted as $n$, is calculated using a specific balancing formula. This rating must be a natural number for the shipment to be cleared for transport.

The formula for the efficiency rating is determined by three integer-based weight dimensions of the cargo containers: $a, b$, and $c$ (where $a, b, c \in \{1, 2, 3, \dots\}$). To find the rating $n$, the logistics coordinator must take the square of the sum of these three dimensions and divide that total by the product of the three dimensions.

The regulatory board needs to know the total sum of all possible unique natural numbers $n$ that can be generated using this specific formula for any combination of natural numbers $a, b$, and $c$. 

Find the sum of all such natural numbers $n$.
