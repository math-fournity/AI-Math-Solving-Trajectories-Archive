## Classification of Complex Representations of $\mathfrak{g} = \mathbb{R}$

ra, which is abelian (any one-dimensional Lie algebra is abelian since $[e,e]=0$

\mathfrak{g}$ is a real Lie algebra homomorphism
$$\rho: \mathfrak{g} \to \mathfrak{gl}_{\mathbb{C}}(V) = \operatorname{End}_{\ma
thbb{C}}(V)$$
where $V$ is a finite-dimensional complex vector space and $\operatorname{End}_{

**Step 1: A representation is determined by a single endomorphism.**

Since $\mathfrak{g}$ is one-dimensional over $\mathbb{R}$, every $x \in \mathfra
k{g}$ is $x = t\,e$ for a unique $t \in \mathbb{R}$, and $\mathbb{R}$-linearity
of $\rho$ gives
$$\rho(x) = \rho(t\,e) = t\,\rho(e).$$
Thus $\rho$ is completely determined by the single operator $T := \rho(e) \in \o
peratorname{End}_{\mathbb{C}}(V)$.

**Step 2: The Lie algebra homomorphism condition is automatic.**

We must verify $\rho([x,y]) = [\rho(x), \rho(y)]$ for all $x, y \in \mathfrak{g}

0$. The right side is $[tT, sT] = ts[T,T] = 0$. Hence the condition holds for **
every** choice of $T \in \operatorname{End}_{\mathbb{C}}(V)$.
