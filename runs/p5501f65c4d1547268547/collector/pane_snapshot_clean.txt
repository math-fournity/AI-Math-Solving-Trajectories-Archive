**Solution.**

We compute the operator norm of $T: C([0,1]) \to C([0,1])$ defined by
$$Tf(x) = \int_0^1 \frac{f(y)}{1+x^2+y^2}\,dy,$$
where $C([0,1])$ is equipped with the sup norm $\|f\|_\infty = \sup_{x\in[0,1]}|
f(x)|$.

The kernel $K(x,y) = \dfrac{1}{1+x^2+y^2}$ is continuous and strictly positive o
n $[0,1]^2$.

**Upper bound.** For any $f\in C([0,1])$ and any $x\in[0,1]$,
$$|Tf(x)| \le \int_0^1 \frac{|f(y)|}{1+x^2+y^2}\,dy \le \|f\|_\infty \int_0^1 \f
rac{dy}{1+x^2+y^2}.$$
Hence
$$\|Tf\|_\infty \le \|f\|_\infty \cdot \sup_{x\in[0,1]} \int_0^1 \frac{dy}{1+x^2
+y^2},$$
so $\|T\| \le M$ where $M := \sup_{x\in[0,1]} g(x)$ and $g(x):=\int_0^1 \frac{dy
}{1+x^2+y^2}$.

**Lower bound.** Take $f\equiv 1$, so $\|f\|_\infty=1$. Since $K>0$,
$$Tf(x) = g(x), \qquad \|Tf\|_\infty = \sup_{x\in[0,1]} g(x) = M.$$
Thus $\|T\|\ge M$. Combined with the upper bound, $\|T\|=M$.

**Evaluating $g(x)$.** Setting $a=\sqrt{1+x^2}$,
$$g(x) = \int_0^1 \frac{dy}{a^2+y^2} = \frac{1}{a}\arctan\!\left(\frac{y}{a}\rig
ht)\Bigg|_0^1 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}\arctan\!\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}\righ
t).$$

**Monotonicity.** Let $u=\sqrt{1+x^2}$ (increasing in $x\ge 0$) and $h(u)=\arcta
n(1/u)/u$. Then
$$h'(u) = -\frac{1}{u(u^2+1)} - \frac{\arctan(1/u)}{u^2} < 0,$$
so $h$ is strictly decreasing in $u$, hence $g(x)=h(\sqrt{1+x^2})$ is strictly d

$$M = g(0) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1}}\arctan\!\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{1}}\right) = \arcta

$$\boxed{\|T\| = \frac{\pi}{4}}.$$
