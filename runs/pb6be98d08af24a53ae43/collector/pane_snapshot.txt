## Structural Stability Analysis

**Definition.** A dynamical system is *structurally stable* if there exists a $C
^1$-neighborhood of the system such that every system in that neighborhood is to
pologically equivalent (flows) or topologically conjugate (maps) to the original

**Key criteria used:**
- **Flows:** All fixed points and closed orbits hyperbolic; no saddle-to-saddle
connections (separatrices); non-wandering set = fixed points ∪ closed orbits (Mo
rse–Smale conditions).
- **Circle diffeomorphisms:** Structurally stable $\iff$ Morse–Smale $\iff$ rota
tion number is rational with hyperbolic periodic points.
