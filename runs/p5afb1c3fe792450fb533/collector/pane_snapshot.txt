## Solution

**Setup.** Let $AB = 1$ and $AD = 2$. Place $A = (0,0)$, $B = (1,0)$, $C = (1,2)
$, $D = (0,2)$, so $M = (0,1)$ (midpoint of $AD$). Let $\alpha = \angle KDA$, th
e desired angle.

**Step 1: Label the angles.**

Since $A, M, D$ are collinear with $M$ between them, $\angle DMK = 180° - \angle
 AMK = 100°$.

In $\triangle MKD$ (with $|MD| = 1$):
- $\angle MDK = \alpha$ (same as $\angle KDA$, since $DM$ and $DA$ are the same
ray from $D$),
- $\angle DMK = 100°$,
- $\angle MKD = 180° - 100° - \alpha = 80° - \alpha$. Call this $\beta$.

Since $KD$ bisects $\angle MKC$, we have $\angle MKD = \angle DKC = \beta = 80°
- \alpha$.

In $\triangle KDC$ (with $|DC| = 1$):
- $\angle KDC = 90° - \alpha$ (since $DC \perp DA$ and $\angle KDA = \alpha$),
- $\angle DKC = \beta = 80° - \alpha$,
- $\angle KCD = 180° - (90° - \alpha) - (80° - \alpha) = 10° + 2\alpha$.

**Step 2: Apply the sine rule in both triangles sharing $KD$.**

In $\triangle MKD$: $\dfrac{|KD|}{\sin 100°} = \dfrac{|MD|}{\sin\beta} = \dfrac{
1}{\sin(80°-\alpha)}$, so $|KD| = \dfrac{\sin 100°}{\sin(80° - \alpha)}$.

In $\triangle KDC$: $\dfrac{|KD|}{\sin(10°+2\alpha)} = \dfrac{|DC|}{\sin\beta} =
 \dfrac{1}{\sin(80°-\alpha)}$, so $|KD| = \dfrac{\sin(10° + 2\alpha)}{\sin(80° -
 \alpha)}$.

**Step 3: Equate and solve.**

$$\frac{\sin 100°}{\sin(80°-\alpha)} = \frac{\sin(10°+2\alpha)}{\sin(80°-\alpha)
}$$

Since $\sin 100° = \sin 80°$:

$$\sin(10° + 2\alpha) = \sin 80°$$

This gives two cases:
- $10° + 2\alpha = 80° \Rightarrow \alpha = 35°$,
- $10° + 2\alpha = 100° \Rightarrow \alpha = 45°$.

**Step 4: Discard the extraneous solution.**

Using $K = D + |KD|\,(\sin\alpha,\, -\cos\alpha)$ with $|KD| = \sin 100°/\sin(80
°-\alpha)$:

- **$\alpha = 35°$:** $|KD| \approx 1.393$, $K_x \approx 1.393\sin 35° \approx 0
.80$, $K_y \approx 2 - 1.393\cos 35° \approx 0.86$. So $K \approx (0.80, 0.86)$
— **inside** the rectangle. ✓

- **$\alpha = 45°$:** $|KD| \approx 1.717$, $K_x \approx 1.717\sin 45° \approx 1
.21 > 1$. So $K$ lies **outside** the rectangle ($x > AB$). ✗

**Verification of $\alpha = 35°$.** With $K \approx (0.799, 0.859)$:
- $\angle AMK$: $\cos\angle AMK = \frac{0.141}{0.811} \approx 0.1738 \Rightarrow
 \angle AMK \approx 80°$. ✓
- $\angle MKD = \angle DKC \approx 45°$ (both computed $\approx 45°$), so $KD$ b
isects $\angle MKC$. ✓

**Answer:** $\angle KDA = \boxed{35°}$.
