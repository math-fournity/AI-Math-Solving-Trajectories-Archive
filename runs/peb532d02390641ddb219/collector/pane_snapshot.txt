## Proof

We have an $n \times m$ grid of students ($n, m \geq 3$). Each student shakes ha
nds with each neighbor (left, right, front, behind). Each handshake is counted o
nce (shared between two neighbors).

**Counting handshakes via edges of the grid graph:**

- **Horizontal adjacencies:** $n$ rows, each with $m-1$ adjacent pairs → $n(m-1)
$.
- **Vertical adjacencies:** $n-1$ row-gaps, each with $m$ adjacent pairs → $m(n-
1)$.

Total handshakes:
$$n(m-1) + m(n-1) = 2nm - n - m = 252.$$

**Solving the Diophantine equation.** Multiply by 2 and complete the product:
$$4nm - 2n - 2m = 504 \implies (2n-1)(2m-1) = 505.$$

**Factoring 505:** $505 = 5 \times 101$, where both $5$ and $101$ are prime. The
 positive factor pairs are $(1,505)$, $(5,101)$, $(101,5)$, $(505,1)$.

Since $n, m \geq 3$, we need $2n-1 \geq 5$ and $2m-1 \geq 5$, ruling out the pai
rs containing $1$.

The remaining valid factorizations:
- $(2n-1, 2m-1) = (5, 101) \Rightarrow n = 3,\ m = 51$.
- $(2n-1, 2m-1) = (101, 5) \Rightarrow n = 51,\ m = 3$.

Both give the same number of students:
$$nm = 3 \times 51 = 153.$$

**Verification:** $2(153) - 3 - 51 = 306 - 54 = 252$. ✓

The number of students in the classroom is $\boxed{153}$.
