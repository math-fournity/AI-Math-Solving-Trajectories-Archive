# Problem

A specialized logistics company, Trans-Nexus, manages a fleet of delivery drones. One drone, Model R, has completed $n$ total delivery missions (where $n \leq 2015$) and maintained a successful delivery rate of exactly $k$.

A second drone, Model G, has also completed a series of missions. Initially, Model G’s successful delivery rate is strictly less than $k$. The company engineers observe a specific mathematical property of these rates: if Model G continues to perform more missions and eventually achieves a successful delivery rate strictly greater than $k$, there must exist a specific moment in its mission history where its cumulative delivery rate was exactly equal to $k$.

This property—that Model G cannot "jump" over the value $k$ without hitting it exactly—only holds for certain specific values of $k$.

Find the product of all possible values of $k$ for which this property is guaranteed to be true. If the product is an irreducible fraction $\frac{a}{b}$, calculate $a+b$.
