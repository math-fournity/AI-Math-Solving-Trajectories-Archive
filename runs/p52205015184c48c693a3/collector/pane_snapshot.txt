Looking at this problem, I need to find five positive integers whose 10 pairwise
 GCDs are {2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, p, q, r}, minimizing p + q + r.

## Structural Analysis

**Prime 2:** The even GCDs among the known values are {2, 4, 6, 8} — four values
. If 2 divides exactly k vaults, then C(k,2) pairs have even GCD. Checking: k=3
gives 3 (too few), k=4 gives 6 (fits with 4 known + 2 from {p,q,r}), k=5 gives 1
0 (contradicts odd GCDs 3,5,7). So **exactly 4 vaults are even**, 1 (call it **e
**) is odd.

**Prime 7:** Only GCD divisible by 7 is 7 itself, so 7 divides exactly 2 vaults.
 Since 7 is odd, one pair involving e has GCD 7, so **7 | e** and 7 | one even v
ault.

**Prime 5:** Same reasoning — 5 divides exactly 2 vaults, **5 | e** and 5 | one
even vault. The 5-vault and 7-vault must be distinct (else gcd(e, that vault) ≥
35, impossible).

**Prime 3:** GCDs divisible by 3 are {3, 6} plus possibly one from {p,q,r}. If 3
 divides k vaults: k=2 gives 1 pair (need ≥2), k=3 gives 3 pairs (fits: 3, 6, on
e from p,q,r), k=4 gives 6 (too many). So **3 divides exactly 3 vaults**.

**e must be divisible by 3:** If e is not among the 3-divisible vaults, all 3 ar
e even, giving 3 even GCDs divisible by 3 — but only 6 is even and divisible by
3 among known values, requiring 2 from {p,q,r}, yet only 1 of {p,q,r} is divisib
le by 3. Contradiction. So **3 | e**, and 2 of the 4 even vaults are divisible b
y 3.

## The Fourth Odd GCD

The 4 pairs involving e have odd GCDs. Three are {3, 5, 7}; the fourth is **s**
(one of p, q, r). Since e is divisible by 3 and the two 3-divisible even vaults
pair with e to give odd GCDs divisible by 3, these are **3 and s**, forcing **s
to be an odd multiple of 3, s ≥ 3**.

## The 2-adic Valuation Structure

Label even vaults a, b, c, d with 2-adic valuations w₁ ≤ w₂ ≤ w₃ ≤ w₄. The 6 pai
rwise minima form {w₁, w₁, w₁, w₂, w₂, w₃}. The even GCDs {2, 4, 6, 8, t, u} hav
e v₂-values {1, 2, 1, 3, v₂(t), v₂(u)}. Matching forces **w₁ = 1, w₂ = 2, w₃ = 3
**, giving v₂-multiset {1, 1, 1, 2, 2, 3} with v₂(t) = 1, v₂(u) = 2. The minimum
 values are **t = 2, u = 4**.

## No GCD Can Equal 1

All 4 even vaults share factor 2, so pairs among them have GCD ≥ 2. Vault e is d
ivisible by 3, 5, 7, and each even vault shares at least one of {3, 5, 7, s} wit
h e (all ≥ 3). So every GCD ≥ 2.

## Construction with s = 3

Setting s = 3 (minimum odd multiple of 3):

- **a = 6** (v₂=1, div by 3; not 5, 7)
- **b = 12** (v₂=2, div by 3; not 5, 7)
- **c = 40** (v₂=3, div by 5; not 3, 7)
- **d = 56** (v₂=3, div by 7; not 3, 5)
- **e = 105** (div by 3, 5, 7; odd)

**Verification of all 10 GCDs:**

The 10 GCDs are {2, 2, 3, 3, 4, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8} = {2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, **2, 3, 4

So **p = 2, q = 3, r = 4** and **p + q + r = 9**.

## Minimality Proof

- **s ≥ 3** (forced: s is an odd multiple of 3, and s = 3 is achievable). ✓
- **t ≥ 2** (forced: t is even, and t = 2 is achievable). ✓
- **u ≥ 4** (forced: v₂(u) = 2, so u ≥ 4, and u = 4 is achievable). ✓
- **No GCD can be 1**, so none of p, q, r can be 1. ✓
- **t = u = 2 is impossible**: would require four v₂ = 1 GCDs, but with 4 even v
aults, the v₂ = 1 count is either 3 (one vault with v₂ = 1) or 5 (two vaults wit
h v₂ = 1) — never 4. ✓

Therefore **p + q + r ≥ 3 + 2 + 4 = 9**, and this bound is achieved by the const
ruction above.
