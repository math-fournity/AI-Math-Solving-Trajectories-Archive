## Proof

Let $\pi: X \to Y$ be a finite morphism of schemes and let $p \in Y$.

### Step 1. Local affine description

Since a finite morphism is in particular affine, we may work locally on $Y$. Cho
ose an affine open neighborhood $U = \operatorname{Spec} B$ of $p$. Then $\pi^{-
1}(U) = \operatorname{Spec} A$ for a ring $A$ that is a **finite $B$-module** (v
ia the morphism $\pi^\#: B \to A$). Let $\mathfrak{p} \subset B$ be the prime id
eal corresponding to $p$, so the residue field is
$$\kappa(p) = \kappa(\mathfrak{p}) = B_{\mathfrak{p}} / \mathfrak{p} B_{\mathfra
k{p}} = \operatorname{Frac}(B/\mathfrak{p}).$$

### Step 2. The scheme-theoretic preimage

The scheme-theoretic preimage of $p$ is the fiber product
$$\pi^{-1}(p) = X \times_Y \operatorname{Spec} \kappa(\mathfrak{p}).$$
Over the affine open $U$, this fiber product is
$$\pi^{-1}(p) \cap \pi^{-1}(U) = \operatorname{Spec}\!\bigl(A \otimes_B \kappa(\
mathfrak{p})\bigr).$$
Since $\pi^{-1}(p)$ is supported over the single point $p \in U$, the entire sch
eme-theoretic preimage is captured by this affine piece, and
$$\Gamma\!\bigl(\pi^{-1}(p),\, \mathcal{O}_{\pi^{-1}(p)}\bigr) = A \otimes_B \ka
ppa(\mathfrak{p}). \tag{1}$$

### Step 3. This is a finite-dimensional $\kappa(p)$-vector space

Because $A$ is a finite $B$-module, say generated by $a_1, \dots, a_n$ as a $B$-
module, the tensor product $A \otimes_B \kappa(\mathfrak{p})$ is generated by $a
_1 \otimes 1, \dots, a_n \otimes 1$ as a $\kappa(\mathfrak{p})$-module. Hence $A
 \otimes_B \kappa(\mathfrak{p})$ is a **finite-dimensional vector space over $\k
appa(p)$**, and
$$\dim_{\kappa(p)} \Gamma\!\bigl(\pi^{-1}(p),\, \mathcal{O}_{\pi^{-1}(p)}\bigr)
= \dim_{\kappa(\mathfrak{p})}(A \otimes_B \kappa(\mathfrak{p})) < \infty. \tag{2
}$$

### Step 4. Identification with the fiber of $\pi_*\mathcal{O}_X$

The pushforward $\pi_*\mathcal{O}_X$ is a coherent $\mathcal{O}_Y$-module (since
 $\pi$ is finite). Over $U = \operatorname{Spec} B$, we have $(\pi_*\mathcal{O}_
X)|_U = \widetilde{A}$. The **fiber of this coherent sheaf at $p$** is
$$(\pi_*\mathcal{O}_X) \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_Y} \kappa(p) \;\Big|_U \;=\; A \otim
es_B \kappa(\mathfrak{p}). \tag{3}$$
Combining (1) and (3), we obtain the canonical **base-change isomorphism** for t
he affine (hence finite) morphism $\pi$:
$$(\pi_*\mathcal{O}_X) \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_Y} \kappa(p) \;\xrightarrow{\;\sim\;
}\; \Gamma\!\bigl(\pi^{-1}(p),\, \mathcal{O}_{\pi^{-1}(p)}\bigr). \tag{4}$$

### Step 5. Equality with the degree

The **degree of $\pi$ at $p$** is defined as the rank of the coherent $\mathcal{
O}_Y$-module $\pi_*\mathcal{O}_X$ at $p$, i.e.,
$$\deg_p(\pi) = \dim_{\kappa(p)}\!\bigl((\pi_*\mathcal{O}_X) \otimes_{\mathcal{O
}_Y} \kappa(p)\bigr). \tag{5}$$

Applying the isomorphism (4), we immediately obtain
$$\deg_p(\pi) = \dim_{\kappa(p)}\!\bigl((\pi_*\mathcal{O}_X) \otimes_{\mathcal{O
}_Y} \kappa(p)\bigr) = \dim_{\kappa(p)} \Gamma\!\bigl(\pi^{-1}(p),\, \mathcal{O}
_{\pi^{-1}(p)}\bigr). \tag{6}$$

### Step 6. The locally free case (constant degree)

When $\pi$ is **finite locally free** (equivalently, finite and flat — which hol
ds, for instance, when $Y$ is locally Noetherian and $\pi$ is finite flat, or wh
en $Y = \operatorname{Spec} B$ with $B$ a PID and $A$ is torsion-free over $B$),
 the sheaf $\pi_*\mathcal{O}_X$ is locally free of some rank $d$. Then for **eve
ry** $p \in Y$:
$$(\pi_*\mathcal{O}_X) \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_Y} \kappa(p) \cong \kappa(p)^{\oplus
 d},$$
so $\deg_p(\pi) = d$ is constant, and by (6):
$$\dim_{\kappa(p)} \Gamma\!\bigl(\pi^{-1}(p),\, \mathcal{O}_{\pi^{-1}(p)}\bigr)
= d \quad \text{for all } p \in Y.$$

In this case the degree is a single integer $d$ (the generic rank), and the fibe
r dimension at **every** point equals $d$. This is the familiar situation of, e.
g., a degree-$d$ cover of curves.

### Step 7. Summary

The key identity is the base-change isomorphism (4), which holds for any affine
morphism (and in particular for any finite morphism): the fiber of the pushforwa
rd sheaf $\pi_*\mathcal{O}_X$ at $p$ is canonically isomorphic to the ring of gl
obal functions on the scheme-theoretic fiber $\pi^{-1}(p)$. Since the degree at
$p$ is by definition the $\kappa(p)$-dimension of this fiber, the equality
$$\boxed{\deg_p(\pi) = \dim_{\kappa(p)} \Gamma\!\bigl(\pi^{-1}(p),\, \mathcal{O}
_{\pi^{-1}(p)}\bigr)}$$
follows immediately. $\blacksquare$
