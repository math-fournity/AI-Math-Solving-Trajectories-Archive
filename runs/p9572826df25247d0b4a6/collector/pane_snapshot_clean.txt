Looking at this problem, I need to evaluate $\int_0^\infty (x\ln x)^4\,dx$ using
 the gamma function. The integral $\int_0^\infty x^4(\ln x)^4\,dx$ without an ex
ponential weight diverges (the integrand grows without bound as $x\to\infty$). T
he standard gamma-function interpretation of this problem is $\int_0^\infty x^4(
\ln x)^4\,e^{-x}\,dx$, which I evaluate below.

## Solution

**Step 1: Connect to the Gamma Function**

The gamma function is $\Gamma(s)=\int_0^\infty x^{s-1}e^{-x}\,dx$. Differentiati
ng $n$ times under the integral sign with respect to $s$:

$$\Gamma^{(n)}(s)=\int_0^\infty x^{s-1}(\ln x)^n\,e^{-x}\,dx.$$

Setting $s=5$ and $n=4$:

$$\int_0^\infty x^4(\ln x)^4\,e^{-x}\,dx = \int_0^\infty x^{5-1}(\ln x)^4\,e^{-x
}\,dx = \Gamma^{(4)}(5).$$

**Step 2: Compute $\Gamma^{(4)}(5)$ via Polygamma Functions**

Let $L(s)=\ln\Gamma(s)$, so $\Gamma(s)=e^{L(s)}$. Then $L'(s)=\psi(s)$ (digamma)
, $L''(s)=\psi_1(s)$ (trigamma), $L'''(s)=\psi_2(s)$, $L^{(4)}(s)=\psi_3(s)$.

By the Faà di Bruno formula (complete Bell polynomial $B_4$):

$$\Gamma^{(4)}(s)=\Gamma(s)\Big[\psi(s)^4+6\,\psi(s)^2\,\psi_1(s)+3\,\psi_1(s)^2
+4\,\psi(s)\,\psi_2(s)+\psi_3(s)\Big].$$

Since $\Gamma(5)=4!=24$:

$$\Gamma^{(4)}(5)=24\Big[\psi(5)^4+6\,\psi(5)^2\,\psi_1(5)+3\,\psi_1(5)^2+4\,\ps
i(5)\,\psi_2(5)+\psi_3(5)\Big].$$

**Step 3: Evaluate the Polygamma Functions at $s=5$**

Using $\psi(n)=H_{n-1}-\gamma$ and $\psi_m(n)=(-1)^{m+1}m!\big[\zeta(m+1)-H_{n-1
}^{(m+1)}\big]$, where $H_k^{(r)}=\sum_{j=1}^k j^{-r}$:

| Function | Value |
|---|---|
| $\psi(5)=H_4-\gamma$ | $\dfrac{25}{12}-\gamma$ |
| $\psi_1(5)=\dfrac{\pi^2}{6}-H_4^{(2)}$ | $\dfrac{\pi^2}{6}-\dfrac{205}{144}$ |
| $\psi_2(5)=-2\big[\zeta(3)-H_4^{(3)}\big]$ | $\dfrac{2035}{864}-2\,\zeta(3)$ |
| $\psi_3(5)=6\big[\zeta(4)-H_4^{(4)}\big]$ | $\dfrac{\pi^4}{15}-\dfrac{22369}{3
456}$ |

with $H_4=\frac{25}{12}$, $H_4^{(2)}=\frac{205}{144}$, $H_4^{(3)}=\frac{2035}{17
28}$, $H_4^{(4)}=\frac{22369}{20736}$, and $\zeta(4)=\frac{\pi^4}{90}$.

**Step 4: Final Result**

$$\boxed{\int_0^\infty x^4(\ln x)^4\,e^{-x}\,dx = 24\left[\left(\tfrac{25}{12}-\
gamma\right)^4+6\left(\tfrac{25}{12}-\gamma\right)^2\!\left(\tfrac{\pi^2}{6}-\tf
rac{205}{144}\right)+3\left(\tfrac{\pi^2}{6}-\tfrac{205}{144}\right)^2+4\left(\t
frac{25}{12}-\gamma\right)\!\left(\tfrac{2035}{864}-2\zeta(3)\right)+\tfrac{\pi^
4}{15}-\tfrac{22369}{3456}\right]}$$

**Numerical verification:** Substituting $\gamma\approx0.5772$, $\zeta(3)\approx
1.2021$, $\pi\approx3.14159$:

- $\psi(5)\approx1.5061$, $\psi_1(5)\approx0.2213$, $\psi_2(5)\approx-0.0488$, $
\psi_3(5)\approx0.0214$
- $B_4\approx 5.1455+3.0115+0.1470-0.2939+0.0214=8.0315$
- $\Gamma^{(4)}(5)\approx 24\times 8.0315\approx 192.75$ ✓
