## Step 1: Differentiate the integral equation

The equation is:
$$\int_0^{f(x)} f^{-1}(t)\,dt - \int_0^x (\cos t - f(t))\,dt = 0$$

$$f^{-1}(f(x))\cdot f'(x) - (\cos x - f(x)) = 0$$
$$x\,f'(x) + f(x) = \cos x$$

## Step 2: Solve the ODE

So $g(x) = \dfrac{\sin x}{x}$ for $x > 0$ and $g(0) = 1$.
