# Problem

In a specialized logistics warehouse, the power consumption of two industrial cooling units, designated as Unit $x$ and Unit $y$, is governed by their operational settings. These settings must be represented by integers (positive, negative, or zero). 

The total energy load $L$ of the system is calculated by taking the square of the setting for Unit $x$, adding the product of the settings of Unit $x$ and Unit $y$, and adding the square of the setting for Unit $y$. How many distinct pairs of integer settings $(x, y)$ can be programmed into the system to achieve this exact energy load?
