## Solution

We want the last two digits of the power tower $a_{2015}$ where $a_1 = 3$ and $a
_{n+1} = 3^{a_n}$, i.e. $a_{2015} \bmod 100$.

**Step 1: Reduce mod 100.** Since $\gcd(3,100)=1$, the value $3^e \bmod 100$ dep
ends only on $e \bmod \operatorname{ord}_{100}(3)$. Computing the order:
- $3^{20} = (3^{10})^2$, and $3^{10} \equiv 49 \pmod{100}$, so $3^{20}\equiv 49^
2 = 2401 \equiv 1 \pmod{100}$.
- Checking proper divisors of 20: $3^{10}\equiv 49\neq 1$, $3^5\equiv 43\neq 1$,
 $3^4\equiv 81\neq 1$.

So $\operatorname{ord}_{100}(3)=20$, and $$a_{2015}\bmod 100 = 3^{\,a_{2014}\bmo
d 20}\bmod 100.$$

**Step 2: Reduce mod 20.** Since $\gcd(3,20)=1$, we need $\operatorname{ord}_{20
}(3)$:
- $3^4 = 81 \equiv 1 \pmod{20}$, and $3^2 = 9 \not\equiv 1 \pmod{20}$.

So $\operatorname{ord}_{20}(3)=4$, giving $$a_{2014}\bmod 20 = 3^{\,a_{2013}\bmo
d 4}\bmod 20.$$

**Step 3: Reduce mod 4.** Since $\gcd(3,4)=1$, we need $\operatorname{ord}_4(3)$
:
- $3^2 = 9 \equiv 1 \pmod{4}$, and $3^1 = 3 \not\equiv 1$.

So $\operatorname{ord}_4(3)=2$, giving $$a_{2013}\bmod 4 = 3^{\,a_{2012}\bmod 2}
\bmod 4.$$

**Step 4: Bottom of the tower.** $a_{2012}$ is a tower of 2012 threes, which is
odd (a power of 3), so $a_{2012}\bmod 2 = 1$.

**Step 5: Back-substitute.**
- $a_{2013}\bmod 4 = 3^{1}\bmod 4 = 3$.
- $a_{2014}\bmod 20 = 3^{3}\bmod 20 = 27\bmod 20 = 7$.
- $a_{2015}\bmod 100 = 3^{7}\bmod 100 = 2187\bmod 100 = 87$.

The tower is far taller than the depth (3) of reductions required, so the chain
of congruences is valid.

**The last two digits are $\boxed{87}$.**
