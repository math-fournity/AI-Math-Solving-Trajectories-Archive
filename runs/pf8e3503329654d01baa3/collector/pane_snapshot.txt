## Proof

We determine the operator norm $\|A\| = \sup_{\|x\|_1 \le 1} \|Ax\|_1$.

**Setup.** For $x = (x_n)_{n \ge 1} \in \ell^1$, the $n$-th component of $Ax$ is
$$
(Ax)_n = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{a^k}{k!}\, x_{n+k}, \qquad n \ge 1.
$$

---

**Upper bound: $\|A\| \le e^a$.**

For any $x \in \ell^1$,
$$
\|Ax\|_1 = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left|\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{a^k}{k!}\, x_{n+k
}\right|
\le \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{a^k}{k!}\, |x_{n+k}|.
$$
All summands are non-negative, so by Tonelli's theorem we may interchange the or
der of summation. Setting $m = n+k$ (so $n \ge 1$, $k \ge 0$ gives $m \ge 1$, an
d for fixed $m$ we have $k = 0, 1, \dots, m-1$):
$$
\|Ax\|_1 \le \sum_{m=1}^{\infty} |x_m| \sum_{k=0}^{m-1} \frac{a^k}{k!}
= \sum_{m=1}^{\infty} |x_m|\, S(m),
$$
where $S(m) := \sum_{k=0}^{m-1} \frac{a^k}{k!}$. Since $S(m) \le \sum_{k=0}^{\in
fty} \frac{a^k}{k!} = e^a$ for every $m$,
$$
\|Ax\|_1 \le e^a \sum_{m=1}^{\infty} |x_m| = e^a\, \|x\|_1.
$$
Hence $\|A\| \le e^a$. (This also shows $A$ is well-defined on $\ell^1$.)

---

**Lower bound: $\|A\| \ge e^a$.**

For each $N \ge 1$, let $x = e_N$ (the standard basis vector with $x_N = 1$ and
$x_m = 0$ for $m \neq N$). Then $\|x\|_1 = 1$, and
$$
(Ae_N)_n = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{a^k}{k!}\, (e_N)_{n+k} = \begin{cases} \dfr
ac{a^{N-n}}{(N-n)!}, & 1 \le n \le N, \\[6pt] 0, & n > N, \end{cases}
$$
since $(e_N)_{n+k} = 1$ iff $n+k = N$, i.e., $k = N-n \ge 0$, which requires $n
\le N$. Therefore
$$
\|Ae_N\|_1 = \sum_{n=1}^{N} \frac{a^{N-n}}{(N-n)!} = \sum_{j=0}^{N-1} \frac{a^j}
{j!} = S(N).
$$
As $N \to \infty$, $S(N) \to \sum_{j=0}^{\infty} \frac{a^j}{j!} = e^a$. Since $\
|A\| \ge \|Ae_N\|_1 = S(N)$ for every $N$,
$$
\|A\| \ge \lim_{N \to \infty} S(N) = e^a.
$$

---

**Conclusion.** Combining both bounds,
$$
\boxed{\|A\| = e^a}.
$$
