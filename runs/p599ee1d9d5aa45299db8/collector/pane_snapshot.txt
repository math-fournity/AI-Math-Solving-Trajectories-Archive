## Proof

**Step 1: Identify the integral as a Bessel-type series.**

Expand $\cosh(2\cos x)$ as a power series:
$$\cosh(2\cos x) = \sum_{m=0}^{\infty} \frac{(2\cos x)^{2m}}{(2m)!} = \sum_{m=0}
^{\infty} \frac{4^m \cos^{2m} x}{(2m)!}.$$

Since every term is non-negative on $[0,\pi]$, by monotone convergence we may in
terchange sum and integral:
$$\int_0^{\pi} \cosh(2\cos x)\,dx = \sum_{m=0}^{\infty} \frac{4^m}{(2m)!}\int_0^
{\pi}\cos^{2m}x\,dx.$$

Using the classical Wallis-type integral
$$\int_0^{\pi}\cos^{2m}x\,dx = \pi\,\frac{(2m)!}{4^m\,(m!)^2},$$
we obtain
$$\int_0^{\pi}\cosh(2\cos x)\,dx = \sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\frac{4^m}{(2m)!}\cdot\pi\
,\frac{(2m)!}{4^m\,(m!)^2} = \pi\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(m!)^2}.$$

(This is $\pi\, I_0(2)$, where $I_0$ is the modified Bessel function of the firs
t kind.)

**Step 2: Express the difference as a tail sum.**

Since $\int_0^{\pi}\cosh(2\cos x)\,dx = \pi\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(k!)^2}$,
 the expression inside the limit becomes
$$\int_0^{\pi}\cosh(2\cos x)\,dx - \pi\sum_{k=0}^{n}\frac{1}{(k!)^2} = \pi\sum_{
k=n+1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(k!)^2}.$$

**Step 3: Bound the tail and evaluate the limit.**

The tail is dominated by its first term. For $k \geq n+1$, the ratio of consecut
ive terms is
$$\frac{1/((k+1)!)^2}{1/(k!)^2} = \frac{1}{(k+1)^2} \leq \frac{1}{(n+2)^2}.$$

Therefore
$$\sum_{k=n+1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(k!)^2} \leq \frac{1}{((n+1)!)^2}\sum_{j=0}^{\in
fty}\frac{1}{(n+2)^{2j}} = \frac{1}{((n+1)!)^2}\cdot\frac{(n+2)^2}{(n+2)^2-1}.$$

This gives the bound
$$0 \leq n^3\cdot\pi\sum_{k=n+1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(k!)^2} \leq \frac{\pi\, n^3\,
(n+2)^2}{((n+1)!)^2\bigl((n+2)^2-1\bigr)}.$$

By Stirling's approximation, $(n+1)! \geq \left(\frac{n+1}{e}\right)^{n+1}$ for
all sufficiently large $n$, so
$$\frac{n^3}{((n+1)!)^2} \leq \frac{n^3\, e^{2(n+1)}}{(n+1)^{2(n+1)}} \to 0 \qua
d\text{as } n\to\infty,$$
since $(n+1)^{2(n+1)}$ grows super-exponentially while $n^3 e^{2(n+1)}$ grows on
ly exponentially.

By the squeeze theorem,
$$\lim_{n\to\infty} n^3\left(\int_0^{\pi}\cosh(2\cos x)\,dx - \pi\sum_{k=0}^{n}\
frac{1}{(k!)^2}\right) = 0.$$
