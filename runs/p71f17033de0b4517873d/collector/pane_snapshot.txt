## Step 1: Identify $a_n$ as the derangement numbers $D_n$

The derangement numbers satisfy two standard recurrences:
$$D_n = n\,D_{n-1} + (-1)^n \qquad\text{and}\qquad D_n = (n-1)(D_{n-1}+D_{n-2}).
$$

**Verification that $D_n$ satisfies the given recurrence.** From $D_n = nD_{n-1}
+(-1)^n$ we isolate $(-1)^n = D_n - nD_{n-1}$ and substitute into the RHS of the
 given recurrence:

$$\frac{n}{2}D_{n-1} + \frac{n(n-1)}{2}D_{n-2} + (D_n - nD_{n-1})\!\left(1-\frac
{n}{2}\right).$$

The coefficient of $D_{n-1}$ is $\;\frac{n}{2} - n\!\left(1-\frac{n}{2}\right) =
 \frac{n}{2} - n + \frac{n^2}{2} = \frac{n(n-1)}{2}.$

So the expression becomes:
$$D_n\!\left(1-\frac{n}{2}\right) + \frac{n(n-1)}{2}\bigl(D_{n-1}+D_{n-2}\bigr).
$$

Using $D_{n-1}+D_{n-2} = \frac{D_n}{n-1}$ (from the second recurrence):

$$= D_n\!\left(1-\frac{n}{2}\right) + \frac{n(n-1)}{2}\cdot\frac{D_n}{n-1} = D_n

## Step 2: Rewrite $f_n$ and give a combinatorial interpretation

Re-indexing the sum with $k = n-j$ (so $j$ runs $0$ to $n-1$, $k$ runs $n$ to $1
$):

$$f_n = \sum_{k=1}^{n}(n+1-k)\binom{n}{k}\,D_k.$$

**Combinatorial meaning.** The quantity $\binom{n}{k}D_k$ counts permutations of
 $\{1,\dots,n\}$ with exactly $n-k$ fixed points: choose the $k$ non-fixed eleme
nts in $\binom{n}{k}$ ways, then derange them in $D_k$ ways. For such a permutat
ion, the number of fixed points is $n-k$, so the weight $(n+1-k) = (\text{fixed
points}) + 1$.

The sum over $k=1,\dots,n$ covers all permutations **except the identity** (whic
h corresponds to $k=0$, i.e., all $n$ elements fixed). Therefore:

$$f_n = \sum_{\substack{\pi \in S_n \\ \pi \neq \mathrm{id}}} \bigl(\operatornam
e{fix}(\pi) + 1\bigr).$$

## Step 3: Evaluate the sum

Two standard counting facts:

- **Total fixed points over all permutations:** Each element is fixed by exactly
 $(n-1)!$ of the $n!$ permutations, so
$$\sum_{\pi \in S_n}\operatorname{fix}(\pi) = n\cdot(n-1)! = n!.$$

- **Count of all permutations:** $\displaystyle\sum_{\pi\in S_n}1 = n!$.

Hence:
$$\sum_{\pi\in S_n}\bigl(\operatorname{fix}(\pi)+1\bigr) = n! + n! = 2\,n!.$$

The identity permutation has $n$ fixed points, contributing $n+1$ to this total.
 Removing it:

$$f_n = 2\,n! - (n+1).$$
