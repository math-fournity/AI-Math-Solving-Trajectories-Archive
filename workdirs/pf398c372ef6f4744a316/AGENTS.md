# Problem

In a specialized logistics yard, two automated guided vehicles (AGVs), Model M and Model W, are positioned on a conveyor belt floor that moves steadily at a rate of 2 meters per second in the positive $y$-direction. The yard is bounded by two parallel security rails located at $x=0$ and $x=25$.

At time $t=0$, Model M is stationed at the origin $(0, 0)$, while Model W is stationed at the coordinate $(25, 0)$. Both vehicles are programmed to intercept each other at a specific docking port located at a coordinate $(x, 22)$. 

To reach the docking port, Model M travels at a constant speed of 4 meters per second relative to the moving floor. Simultaneously, Model W travels at a constant speed of 3 meters per second relative to the moving floor. Given that both vehicles depart at $t=0$ and arrive at the docking port at the exact same moment, find the value of $x$.
