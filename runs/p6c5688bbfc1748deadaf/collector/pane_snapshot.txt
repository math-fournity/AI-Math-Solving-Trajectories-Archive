# Expected Dimension of the $(s-1)$-Secant Variety

## Setup

Let $U$ be a vector space of dimension $k$ and $E$ a vector space of dimension $
n+1$. The projective spaces are
$$|U^\vee| = \mathbb{P}(U^\vee) \cong \mathbb{P}^{k-1}, \qquad |E^\vee| = \mathb
b{P}(E^\vee) \cong \mathbb{P}^{n}.$$

The product $|U^\vee| \times |E^\vee|$ embeds into the ambient projective space
via the **Segre embedding**
$$\Sigma := |U^\vee| \times |E^\vee| \hookrightarrow \mathbb{P}(U^\vee \otimes E
^\vee) = \mathbb{P}^{k(n+1)-1}.$$

A point of $\Sigma$ corresponds to a decomposable tensor $u^\vee \otimes e^\vee$
, i.e., a rank-one bilinear form on $U \times E$. The $(s-1)$-secant variety $\o
peratorname{Sec}_{s-1}(\Sigma)$ is the Zariski closure of the union of all $(s-1
)$-planes spanned by $s$ general points of $\Sigma$; equivalently, it is the var
iety of tensors of border rank $\leq s$.

## Dimension of the Segre Variety

The Segre variety $\Sigma = \mathbb{P}^{k-1} \times \mathbb{P}^n$ has dimension
$$d := \dim \Sigma = (k-1) + n = k + n - 1.$$

## Expected Dimension of the $(s-1)$-Secant

The $(s-1)$-secant variety is parametrized (on a dense open subset) by choosing
$s$ points on $\Sigma$ together with $s$ coefficients specifying a point in thei
r span. The parameter space is:

- **Choice of $s$ points** on $\Sigma$: dimension $s \cdot d = s(k+n-1)$.
- **Choice of a point in the $(s-1)$-plane** spanned by them: $s-1$ additional p
arameters (the $s$ coefficients up to overall scaling).

Thus the total parameter count is
$$s \cdot d + (s-1) = s(k+n-1) + s - 1 = s(k+n) - 1.$$

The secant variety lives in $\mathbb{P}^{k(n+1)-1}$, so its dimension cannot exc
eed the ambient dimension $k(n+1)-1$. The **expected dimension** is therefore
$$\boxed{\min\{\, s(k+n) - 1,\; k(n+1) - 1 \,\}}.$$

## Verification of the Count

- The parameter space of $s$ ordered points on $\Sigma$ is $\Sigma^s$, of dimens
ion $s(k+n-1)$.
- Over a general point of this space, the fiber of the projection to the secant
variety is an open subset of $\mathbb{P}^{s-1}$ (the choice of linear combinatio
n), contributing $s-1$.
- Total: $s(k+n-1) + (s-1) = s(k+n)-1$, capped by the ambient dimension $k(n+1)-
1$.

When $s(k+n) - 1 \leq k(n+1)-1$, i.e., $s \leq \frac{k(n+1)}{k+n}$, the secant i
s expected to be proper (non-defective); this is the regime where generic tensor
s of rank $s$ form a family of the expected size. The classical result of **Abo–
Ottaviani–Peterson** establishes that the Segre variety $\mathbb{P}^{k-1}\times\
mathbb{P}^n$ is non-defective for all $s$ (with the known exceptional cases in t
he unbalanced case), so the expected dimension is in fact the actual dimension e
xcept in those exceptional cases.

## Conclusion

The expected dimension of the $(s-1)$-secant variety $\operatorname{Sec}_{s-1}(|
U^\vee| \times |E^\vee|)$ is

$$\min\{\, s(k+n) - 1,\; k(n+1) - 1 \,\}.$$
