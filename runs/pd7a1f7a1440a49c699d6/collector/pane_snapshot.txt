## Proof

Let $p_1 = 2, p_2 = 3, p_3 = 5, \ldots$ be the primes and $S_n = \sum_{i=1}^n p_
i$.

**Parity condition.** Since $p_1 = 2$ is even and $p_2, \ldots, p_n$ are odd, $S
_n = 2 + \sum_{i=2}^n p_i$ is even iff $n-1$ is even, i.e., $n$ is odd. So we on
ly consider odd $n$.
