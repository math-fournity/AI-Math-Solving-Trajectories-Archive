## Proof

Let $X$ be a bouquet of 3 circles. As a 1-dimensional CW-complex, $X$ has 1 vert
ex and 3 edges, so its Euler characteristic is
$$\chi(X) = 1 - 3 = -2.$$

The fundamental group of a bouquet of $n$ circles is the free group of rank $n$;
 hence $\pi_1(X) \cong F_3$, consistent with $\chi(X) = 1 - \operatorname{rank}\
pi_1(X) = 1 - 3 = -2$.

Now let $p \colon \hat{X} \to X$ be a degree $d = 10$ connected covering. Since
$X$ is a graph (a 1-dimensional CW-complex), the covering space $\hat{X}$ inheri
ts the structure of a graph: each vertex of $X$ has $d$ lifts, and each edge of
$X$ lifts to $d$ edges in $\hat{X}$. Thus $\hat{X}$ has $d$ vertices and $3d$ ed
ges.

Euler characteristic is multiplicative under finite covers:
$$\chi(\hat{X}) = d \cdot \chi(X) = 10 \cdot (-2) = -20.$$

Since $\hat{X}$ is a connected graph with $V = 10$ vertices and $E = 30$ edges,
we verify:
$$\chi(\hat{X}) = V - E = 10 - 30 = -20. \quad\checkmark$$

The fundamental group of a connected graph is a free group whose rank equals $E
- V + 1 = 1 - \chi(\hat{X})$. Therefore
$$\operatorname{rank}\pi_1(\hat{X}) = 1 - \chi(\hat{X}) = 1 - (-20) = 21.$$

Equivalently, $\operatorname{rank}\pi_1(\hat{X}) = E - V + 1 = 30 - 10 + 1 = 21$
.

**The rank of the free group $\pi_1(\hat{X})$ is $\boxed{21}$.**
