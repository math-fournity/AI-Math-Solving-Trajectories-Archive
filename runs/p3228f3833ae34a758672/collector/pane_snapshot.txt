## Solution

**Step 1: Determine the range of $z$.**

The condition is $z^2 < \frac{x^2}{4} + \frac{y^2}{9} < 2z$. Since $\frac{x^2}{4
}+\frac{y^2}{9} \geq 0$, the right inequality forces $2z > 0$, i.e., $z > 0$.

**Step 2: Linear change of variables.**

Set $u = x/2,\ v = y/3$, i.e., $x = 2u,\ y = 3v$. The Jacobian is
$$dx\,dy = 6\,du\,dv,$$
and the defining condition becomes
$$z^2 < u^2 + v^2 < 2z.$$

**Step 3: Reduce to polar coordinates in the $(u,v)$-plane.**

Let $r^2 = u^2+v^2$ with $r \geq 0$. Since $z > 0$, the inequality $z^2 < r^2$ g
ives $r > z$, and $r^2 < 2z$ gives $r < \sqrt{2z}$. Thus for each fixed $z$ we n
eed
$$z < r < \sqrt{2z}.$$

This interval is nonempty iff $z < \sqrt{2z}$, i.e. $z^2 < 2z$, i.e. (using $z>0
$) $z < 2$. Hence $z \in (0,2)$.

**Step 4: Compute the volume in $(u,v,z)$-space.**

For fixed $z \in (0,2)$, the admissible region in the $(u,v)$-plane is the annul
us $\{z < r < \sqrt{2z}\}$, with area
$$\pi(\sqrt{2z})^2 - \pi z^2 = \pi(2z - z^2).$$

Therefore
$$\text{Vol}_{(u,v,z)} = \int_0^2 \pi(2z - z^2)\,dz = \pi\Big[z^2 - \tfrac{z^3}{
3}\Big]_0^2 = \pi\Big(4 - \tfrac{8}{3}\Big) = \frac{4\pi}{3}.$$

**Step 5: Convert back to $(x,y,z)$-coordinates.**

Multiplying by the Jacobian $|\partial(x,y)/\partial(u,v)| = 6$:
$$\lambda_3(A) = 6 \cdot \frac{4\pi}{3} = 8\pi.$$

**Answer:** $\lambda_3(A) = \boxed{8\pi}$.
