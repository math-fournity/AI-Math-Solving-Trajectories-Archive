The function $f$ extends continuously to the compact set $[-1, 1]$. By the Heine

f$ is uniformly continuous on $(-1, 1)$.

We have $|f(x)|^2 = |x|$, so
$$\int_{-1}^{1} |f(x)|^2\, dx = \int_{-1}^{1} |x|\, dx = 2\int_0^1 x\, dx = 1 <
\infty.$$

$$\int_{-1}^{0} \sqrt{-x}\,\varphi'(x)\,dx = \bigl[\sqrt{-x}\,\varphi(x)\bigr]_{
