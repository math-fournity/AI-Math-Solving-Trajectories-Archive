## Solution

**Setting up equations.** Since $(a,b,c)$ form a geometric sequence, $b^2 = ac$.
 Since $(a, b+1, c)$ form an arithmetic sequence, $a + c = 2(b+1) = 2b+2$.

**Solving for $a$ and $c$ in terms of $b$.** The values $a$ and $c$ are roots of
$$t^2 - (2b+2)t + b^2 = 0.$$
The discriminant is
$$(2b+2)^2 - 4b^2 = 4b^2 + 8b + 4 - 4b^2 = 8b+4 = 4(2b+1).$$
Thus
$$t = \frac{(2b+2) \pm 2\sqrt{2b+1}}{2} = (b+1) \pm \sqrt{2b+1}.$$

For $a, c$ to be positive integers, $2b+1$ must be a perfect square. Since $2b+1
$ is odd, write $2b+1 = k^2$ with $k$ odd, say $k = 2m+1$ for some integer $m \g
e 0$.

Then:
$$2b+1 = (2m+1)^2 = 4m^2+4m+1 \implies b = 2m(m+1),$$
$$a = (b+1) - (2m+1) = 2m^2, \qquad c = (b+1) + (2m+1) = 2(m+1)^2.$$

**Verification.** For $m \ge 1$:
- Geometric: $b^2 = 4m^2(m+1)^2 = 2m^2 \cdot 2(m+1)^2 = ac$. ✓
- Arithmetic: $a+c = 2m^2 + 2(m+1)^2 = 4m^2+4m+2 = 2(2m^2+2m+1) = 2(b+1)$. ✓
- Positivity: $m \ge 1$ gives $a = 2m^2 \ge 2 > 0$, $b, c > 0$. (For $m=0$, $a=0
$, excluded.)

This parametrization is **necessary and sufficient**: every fantastic triplet ar
ises this way, and every $m \ge 1$ yields one.

**Computing the answer.** The values of $a$ are $a = 2m^2$ for $m = 1, 2, 3, \do
ts$, which are strictly increasing. Ordered by $a$:

| $m$ | $a$ | $b$ | $c$ |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 1 | 2 | 4 | 8 |
| 2 | 8 | 12 | 18 |
| 3 | 18 | 24 | 32 |

So $a_1 = 2$, $a_2 = 8$, $a_3 = 18$, and
$$a_1 + a_2 + a_3 = 2 + 8 + 18 = \boxed{28}.$$
