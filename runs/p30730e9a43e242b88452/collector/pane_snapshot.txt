## Proof

$$
\log_{10} y_n = (M-1)\log_{10}(M-1).

$$
\log_{10}(M-1) = \log_{10} M + \log_{10}(1-1/M) = n + \log_{10}(1-1/M).
$$

Hence

$$
\log_{10} y_n = (M-1)n + (M-1)\log_{10}(1-1/M).

 \cdots\right].

$$
\boxed{\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{f(n)}{10^n} = \frac{1}{e}.}
