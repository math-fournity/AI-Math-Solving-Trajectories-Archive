# Problem

A specialized deep-sea research probe is docked at the hull of a cylindrical underwater laboratory. The laboratory has a circular cross-section with a radius of 10 meters. The probe is attached to a fixed point on the outer wall of the lab by a flexible, non-stretchable umbilical cable that is exactly $10\pi$ meters long. 

As the probe moves through the water, the cable can wrap around the smooth circular perimeter of the laboratory’s hull, but it cannot penetrate the laboratory itself. The probe is designed to sample the seabed across the entire horizontal plane level with its docking point, restricted only by the reach of its cable and the obstruction of the cylindrical hull. 

Calculate the total area of the region that the probe can explore.
