## Christoffel Symbol 的推导

我们推导 Levi-Civita 联络系数（第二类 Christoffel 符号）的表达式

$$\Gamma^{\lambda}{}_{\mu\nu}=\tfrac{1}{2}g^{\lambda\sigma}\bigl(\partial_{\mu}g
_{\nu\sigma}+\partial_{\nu}g_{\mu\sigma}-\partial_{\sigma}g_{\mu\nu}\bigr).$$

### 1. 设定与起点

设 $g_{\mu\nu}$ 为非退化的度规张量（对称：$g_{\mu\nu}=g_{\nu\mu}$），其逆 $g^{\m
u\nu}$ 满足 $g^{\mu\nu}g_{\nu\rho}=\delta^{\mu}_{\rho}$。Levi-Civita 联络是唯一
的无挠且与度规相容的联络，即

**(metric compatibility)** $\nabla_{\rho}g_{\mu\nu}=0$,
**(torsion free)** $\Gamma^{\lambda}{}_{\mu\nu}=\Gamma^{\lambda}{}_{\nu\mu}$.

对任意张量，协变导数与偏导数的关系为

$$\nabla_{\rho}g_{\mu\nu}=\partial_{\rho}g_{\mu\nu}-\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\mu\rho}\
,g_{\sigma\nu}-\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\nu\rho}\,g_{\mu\sigma}.$$

### 2. 写出三个度规相容方程

将 $\nabla_{\rho}g_{\mu\nu}=0$ 展开，并对指标做循环置换 $\rho\to\mu\to\nu\to\rho
$，得到三个方程：

**(A)** $\partial_{\rho}g_{\mu\nu}-\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\mu\rho}\,g_{\sigma\nu}-\G
amma^{\sigma}{}_{\nu\rho}\,g_{\mu\sigma}=0$,

**(B)** $\partial_{\mu}g_{\nu\rho}-\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\nu\mu}\,g_{\sigma\rho}-\G
amma^{\sigma}{}_{\rho\mu}\,g_{\nu\sigma}=0$,

**(C)** $\partial_{\nu}g_{\rho\mu}-\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\rho\nu}\,g_{\sigma\mu}-\G
amma^{\sigma}{}_{\mu\nu}\,g_{\rho\sigma}=0$.

### 3. 线性组合 (B) + (C) − (A)

计算 $\text{(B)}+\text{(C)}-\text{(A)}$：

$$\partial_{\mu}g_{\nu\rho}+\partial_{\nu}g_{\rho\mu}-\partial_{\rho}g_{\mu\nu}
-\Bigl[\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\nu\mu}g_{\sigma\rho}+\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\rho\mu}g_{\n
u\sigma}
+\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\rho\nu}g_{\sigma\mu}+\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\mu\nu}g_{\rho\sigm
a}
-\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\mu\rho}g_{\sigma\nu}-\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\nu\rho}g_{\mu\sigm
a}\Bigr]=0.$$

利用无挠条件 $\Gamma^{\lambda}{}_{\alpha\beta}=\Gamma^{\lambda}{}_{\beta\alpha}$
 和度规对称性 $g_{\alpha\beta}=g_{\beta\alpha}$，逐项消去：

- $\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\rho\mu}g_{\nu\sigma}=\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\mu\rho}g_{\sigma
\nu}$ ↔ 与 $-\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\mu\rho}g_{\sigma\nu}$ 相消；
- $\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\rho\nu}g_{\sigma\mu}=\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\nu\rho}g_{\mu\si
gma}$ ↔ 与 $-\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\nu\rho}g_{\mu\sigma}$ 相消；
- 剩余两项 $\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\nu\mu}g_{\sigma\rho}+\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\mu\nu}g
_{\rho\sigma}=2\,\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\mu\nu}g_{\rho\sigma}$（因 $\Gamma^{\sigma}{
}_{\nu\mu}=\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\mu\nu}$ 且 $g_{\sigma\rho}=g_{\rho\sigma}$）。

故得

$$\partial_{\mu}g_{\nu\rho}+\partial_{\nu}g_{\rho\mu}-\partial_{\rho}g_{\mu\nu}-
2\,\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\mu\nu}\,g_{\rho\sigma}=0.$$

### 4. 解出 Christoffel 符号

移项：

$$2\,\Gamma^{\sigma}{}_{\mu\nu}\,g_{\rho\sigma}=\partial_{\mu}g_{\nu\rho}+\parti
al_{\nu}g_{\rho\mu}-\partial_{\rho}g_{\mu\nu}.$$

两边乘以 $g^{\lambda\rho}$ 并对 $\rho$ 求和，利用 $g^{\lambda\rho}g_{\rho\sigma}
=\delta^{\lambda}_{\sigma}$：

$$2\,\Gamma^{\lambda}{}_{\mu\nu}=g^{\lambda\rho}\bigl(\partial_{\mu}g_{\nu\rho}+
\partial_{\nu}g_{\rho\mu}-\partial_{\rho}g_{\mu\nu}\bigr).$$

将哑标 $\rho\to\sigma$ 重命名，得到最终表达式：

$$\boxed{\;\Gamma^{\lambda}{}_{\mu\nu}=\tfrac{1}{2}\,g^{\lambda\sigma}\bigl(\par
tial_{\mu}g_{\nu\sigma}+\partial_{\nu}g_{\mu\sigma}-\partial_{\sigma}g_{\mu\nu}\
bigr).\;}$$

### 5. 验证

- **对称性**：右端关于 $\mu\leftrightarrow\nu$ 对称（前两项互换，第三项不变），
故 $\Gamma^{\lambda}{}_{\mu\nu}=\Gamma^{\lambda}{}_{\nu\mu}$，与无挠条件一致。
- **度规相容**：将上式代回 $\nabla_{\rho}g_{\mu\nu}$ 的展开式，三个 $\Gamma$ 项
的度规收缩恰好给出 $\tfrac12(\partial_{\mu}g_{\nu\rho}+\partial_{\nu}g_{\mu\rho}
-\partial_{\rho}g_{\mu\nu})+\tfrac12(\partial_{\nu}g_{\mu\rho}+\partial_{\mu}g_{
\nu\rho}-\partial_{\rho}g_{\mu\nu})-\dots$，与 $\partial_{\rho}g_{\mu\nu}$ 相消
，得 $\nabla_{\rho}g_{\mu\nu}=0$。
- **唯一性**：无挠 + 度规相容两个条件恰好给出 $d^{2}(d+1)/2$ 个独立方程，唯一确
定 $\Gamma^{\lambda}{}_{\mu\nu}$，故上述解为唯一解。
