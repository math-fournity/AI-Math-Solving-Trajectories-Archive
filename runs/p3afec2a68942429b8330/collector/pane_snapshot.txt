## Proof of `erdos_1150.variants.parseval_lower_bound`

**Theorem.** Let $P \in \mathbb{C}[X]$ be a polynomial of degree $n$ with all co
efficients in $\{-1, 1\}$. Then
$$\max_{|z|=1} |P(z)| \geq \sqrt{n+1}.$$

**Proof.**

Write $P(z) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} a_k z^k$ where each $a_k \in \{-1, 1\}$, so $|a_k|^
2 = 1$ for every $k$.

**Step 1: Parseval's identity on the unit circle.**

Parametrize the unit circle by $z = e^{i\theta}$ for $\theta \in [0, 2\pi)$. The
n
$$P(e^{i\theta}) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} a_k e^{ik\theta}.$$

The functions $\{e^{ik\theta}\}_{k \in \mathbb{Z}}$ form an orthogonal system on
 $[0, 2\pi)$ with respect to the inner product $\langle f, g \rangle = \frac{1}{
2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi} f(\theta)\overline{g(\theta)}\,d\theta$, satisfying
$$\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi} e^{ik\theta}\overline{e^{ij\theta}}\,d\theta = \fr
ac{1}{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi} e^{i(k-j)\theta}\,d\theta = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if
} k = j, \\ 0 & \text{if } k \neq j. \end{cases}$$

Expanding $|P(e^{i\theta})|^2 = P(e^{i\theta})\overline{P(e^{i\theta})} = \sum_{
k=0}^{n}\sum_{j=0}^{n} a_k \overline{a_j} e^{i(k-j)\theta}$ and integrating term
-by-term (justified since the sum is finite), the orthogonality kills all cross
terms ($k \neq j$), leaving only the diagonal:

$$\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi} |P(e^{i\theta})|^2\,d\theta = \sum_{k=0}^{n} |a_k|
^2 = \sum_{k=0}^{n} 1 = n+1.$$

**Step 2: From average to maximum.**

The function $g(\theta) = |P(e^{i\theta})|^2$ is continuous on $[0, 2\pi]$ (it i
s a trigonometric polynomial). Let $M = \max_{\theta \in [0,2\pi]} g(\theta)$. S
ince $g(\theta) \leq M$ for all $\theta$,

$$n+1 = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi} g(\theta)\,d\theta \leq \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_0
^{2\pi} M\,d\theta = M.$$

Therefore $M \geq n+1$, i.e., $\max_{|z|=1}|P(z)|^2 \geq n+1$, and taking square
 roots (both sides non-negative):

$$\max_{|z|=1} |P(z)| \geq \sqrt{n+1}. \qquad \blacksquare$$
