## Solution

We evaluate $S = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\bigl[\zeta(2n)-\zeta(2n+1)\bigr]$.

**Step 1: Expand each term.** Using $\zeta(s)=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} k^{-s}$,

$$\zeta(2n)-\zeta(2n+1)=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^{2n}}\!\left(1-\frac{1}{k}
\right)=\sum_{k=2}^{\infty}\frac{k-1}{k^{2n+1}},$$

since the $k=1$ term vanishes.

**Step 2: Swap the order of summation.** Every summand $\frac{k-1}{k^{2n+1}}$ is
 non-negative for $k\ge 2,\ n\ge 1$, so by Tonelli's theorem we may interchange
the sums freely:

$$S=\sum_{k=2}^{\infty}\frac{k-1}{k}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^{2n}}.$$

**Step 3: Evaluate the inner geometric series.** For $k\ge 2$,

$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k^{2n}}=\frac{1/k^{2}}{1-1/k^{2}}=\frac{1}{k^{2}-1
}.$$

**Step 4: Simplify.** Substituting back,

$$S=\sum_{k=2}^{\infty}\frac{k-1}{k}\cdot\frac{1}{k^{2}-1}=\sum_{k=2}^{\infty}\f
rac{k-1}{k\,(k-1)(k+1)}=\sum_{k=2}^{\infty}\frac{1}{k(k+1)}.$$

**Step 5: Telescope.** Using partial fractions $\frac{1}{k(k+1)}=\frac{1}{k}-\fr
ac{1}{k+1}$,

$$S=\sum_{k=2}^{N}\!\left(\frac{1}{k}-\frac{1}{k+1}\right)=\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{
N+1}\;\xrightarrow[N\to\infty]{}\;\frac{1}{2}.$$

**Conclusion.** The series converges and

$$\boxed{\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\bigl[\zeta(2n)-\zeta(2n+1)\bigr]=\frac{1}{2}}.$$
