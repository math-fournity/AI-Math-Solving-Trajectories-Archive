## Solution

**Identifying the matrix.** The matrix $M$ has entries $M_{i,j} = (-1)^{\operato
rname{popcount}((i-1)\,\&\,(j-1))}$ for $1 \le i,j \le 2^{2017}$. This is precis
ely the **Sylvester–Hadamard matrix** $H$ of order $N = 2^{2017}$ (verified on s
mall cases: for $N=2$ it gives $\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}=H_2$; for
$N=4$ it gives $H_4$).

**Key properties of $H$:**
- $H$ is symmetric: $H_{i,j}=H_{j,i}$ (AND is commutative).
- $H^2 = N\,I$, so $H^{-1} = \frac{1}{N}H$.
- $\det(H) = +\,2^{n\cdot 2^{n-1}}$ for $n\ge 2$ (positive, since the recursive
formula $\det(H_{2N})=(-2)^N\det(H_N)^2$ gives positive sign for $N\ge 2$). With
 $n=2017$: $\det(M)=2^{2017\cdot 2^{2016}}$.

**Computing $a_{i,j}$.** Changing only entry $(i,j)$ from $M_{i,j}$ to $a_{i,j}$
 gives:
$$\det(M') = \det(M) + (a_{i,j}-M_{i,j})\,C_{i,j}$$
where $C_{i,j}$ is the cofactor. Setting $\det(M')=0$:
$$a_{i,j} = M_{i,j} - \frac{\det(M)}{C_{i,j}}.$$
Using $(M^{-1})_{j,i} = C_{i,j}/\det(M)$ and $M^{-1}=\frac{1}{N}M$ with symmetry
:
$$\frac{\det(M)}{C_{i,j}} = \frac{1}{(M^{-1})_{j,i}} = \frac{N}{M_{j,i}} = N\cdo
t M_{i,j} \quad (\text{since } M_{i,j}=\pm1).$$
Therefore:
$$a_{i,j} = M_{i,j}(1-N), \qquad N=2^{2017}.$$

**The master matrix.** $A = (1-N)\,M$, so:
$$\det(A) = (1-N)^N \cdot \det(M) = (1-2^{2017})^{2^{2017}} \cdot 2^{2017\cdot 2

**Reduction mod 2017.** Note $2017$ is prime (checked divisibility up to $\sqrt{

*Factor 1:* By Fermat's little theorem, $2^{2016}\equiv 1\pmod{2017}$, so $2^{20
