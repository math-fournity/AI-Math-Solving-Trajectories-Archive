### 1. The admissible set $K$ is nonempty, closed, convex (hence weakly closed)

Hilbert space $H^1(\Omega)$, it is convex and (by Hahn–Banach) **weakly closed**

### 2. Coercivity of $J$ on $K$

$$\|\nabla w\|_{L^2}\ge\|\nabla u\|_{L^2}-\|\nabla u_0\|_{L^2},$$

### 4. The direct method

$$J(u_\ast)\le\liminf_{n\to\infty}J(u_n)=m=\inf_{u\in K}J(u).$$
Hence $J(u_\ast)=m$: **a minimum is attained**.
