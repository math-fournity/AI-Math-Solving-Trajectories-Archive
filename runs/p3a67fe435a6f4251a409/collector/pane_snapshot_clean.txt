**Answer: Yes, the identity $_{\mathfrak{p}_i}:\mathbb{Q}_p]=e_i f_i$ holds fo
r each $i$.**

**Proof.**

Let $p\mathcal{O}_K = \prod_{i=1}^{r} \mathfrak{p}_i^{e_i}$ be the factorization
 of $p$ in $\mathcal{O}_K$, and let $f_i = [\mathcal{O}_K/\mathfrak{p}_i : \math
bb{F}_p]$ be the residue degree. We prove that for each $i$, the local extension
 degree satisfies $_{\mathfrak{p}_i}:\mathbb{Q}_p] = e_i f_i$.

**Step 1: Base change to $\mathbb{Q}_p$.**

Consider the $\mathbb{Q}$-algebra $K \otimes_{\mathbb{Q}} \mathbb{Q}_p$. Since $
K/\mathbb{Q}$ is separable (char 0), and $\mathbb{Q}_p/\mathbb{Q}$ is a separabl
e extension, we have
$$ \otimes_{\mathbb{Q}} \mathbb{Q}_p : \mathbb{Q}_p] = :\mathbb{Q}].$$

**Step 2: Decomposition via the prime factorization.**

The completion $\mathbb{Q}_p$ is the fraction field of the DVR $\mathbb{Z}_p$. T
he tensor product $K \otimes_{\mathbb{Q}} \mathbb{Q}_p$ decomposes according to
how $p$ splits in $K$. Concretely, let $A = \mathcal{O}_K \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \
mathbb{Z}_p$. Since $\mathbb{Z}_p$ is a DVR with uniformizer $p$, and $p\mathcal
{O}_K = \prod_i \mathfrak{p}_i^{e_i}$, the Chinese Remainder Theorem gives
$$\mathcal{O}_K/p\mathcal{O}_K \cong \prod_{i=1}^{r} \mathcal{O}_K/\mathfrak{p}_
i^{e_i}.$$

Lifting to the $p$-adic completion: the semilocal ring $A = \mathcal{O}_K \otime
s \mathbb{Z}_p$ has Jacobson radical generated by $p$, and $A/pA \cong \prod_i \
mathcal{O}_K/\mathfrak{p}_i^{e_i}$. By Hensel's lemma / idempotent lifting (sinc
e $A$ is $p$-adically complete), the idempotents of $A/pA$ lift to $A$, giving a
 product decomposition
$$A = \prod_{i=1}^{r} A_i, \quad \text{where } A_i \text{ is the local factor co
rresponding to } \mathfrak{p}_i.$$

Each $A_i$ is a complete local $\mathbb{Z}_p$-algebra with residue field $\mathc
al{O}_K/\mathfrak{p}_i$ and maximal ideal generated by $\mathfrak{p}_i$. In fact
 $A_i$ is the valuation ring $\mathcal{O}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}_i}}$ of the completio
n $K_{\mathfrak{p}_i}$, and
$$K \otimes_{\mathbb{Q}} \mathbb{Q}_p = \left(\mathcal{O}_K \otimes \mathbb{Z}_p
\right) \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}_p} \mathbb{Q}_p \cong \prod_{i=1}^{r} K_{\mathfrak{p
}_i}.$$

**Step 3: Computing each local degree.**

From Steps 1 and 2:
$$:\mathbb{Q}] = \otimes\mathbb{Q}_p : \mathbb{Q}_p] = \sum_{i=1}^{r} _{\m
athfrak{p}_i} : \mathbb{Q}_p].$$

Combined with the given identity $:\mathbb{Q}] = \sum_i e_i f_i$, this yields
$$\sum_{i=1}^{r} _{\mathfrak{p}_i}:\mathbb{Q}_p] = \sum_{i=1}^{r} e_i f_i. \ta
g{$\ast$}$$

**Step 4: Local inequality and equality.**

It remains to show each summand agrees, i.e., $_{\mathfrak{p}_i}:\mathbb{Q}_p]
 = e_i f_i$ for every $i$. We establish this directly for a single prime.

Fix $i$ and write $\mathfrak{p} = \mathfrak{p}_i$, $e = e_i$, $f = f_i$. The loc
al field $K_{\mathfrak{p}}$ is a finite extension of $\mathbb{Q}_p$. Let $e(K_{\
mathfrak{p}}/\mathbb{Q}_p)$ and $f(K_{\mathfrak{p}}/\mathbb{Q}_p)$ denote its ra
mification index and residue degree over $\mathbb{Q}_p$.

- **Residue degree:** The residue field of $K_{\mathfrak{p}}$ is $\mathcal{O}_{K
_{\mathfrak{p}}}/\mathfrak{m} \cong \operatorname{Frac}(\mathcal{O}_K/\mathfrak{
p}) = \mathcal{O}_K/\mathfrak{p}$ (the completion does not change the residue fi
eld of a DVR). Hence $f(K_{\mathfrak{p}}/\mathbb{Q}_p) = [\mathcal{O}_K/\mathfra
k{p} : \mathbb{F}_p] = f$.

- **Ramification index:** The uniformizer $\pi$ of $\mathcal{O}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}
}}$ satisfies $\pi^e \mathcal{O}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}} = p\,\mathcal{O}_{K_{\mathfr
ak{p}}}$, because the ideal $\mathfrak{p}\mathcal{O}_{K_{\mathfrak{p}}}$ is the
maximal ideal and $p\mathcal{O}_K = \mathfrak{p}^e \cdot (\text{factors coprime
to }\mathfrak{p})$; the coprime factors become units in $\mathcal{O}_{K_{\mathfr
ak{p}}}$. Thus $e(K_{\mathfrak{p}}/\mathbb{Q}_p) = e$.

For any finite extension of local fields (or more generally any finite separable
 extension of complete discretely valued fields with perfect residue fields), th
e fundamental identity holds:
$$_{\mathfrak{p}} : \mathbb{Q}_p] = e(K_{\mathfrak{p}}/\mathbb{Q}_p)\cdot f(K_
{\mathfrak{p}}/\mathbb{Q}_p) = e \cdot f = e_i f_i.$$

(The identity $[L:K]=ef$ for finite separable extensions of complete DVRs with p
erfect residue fields is standard: the integral closure $\mathcal{O}_L$ is a fre
e $\mathcal{O}_K$-module of rank $[L:K]$; tensoring with the residue field gives
 a vector space of dimension $[L:K]$ over the residue field of $K$, which on the
 other hand has dimension $ef$ since $\mathcal{O}_L \cong \mathcal{O}_K^e$-modul
e structure combined with the residue extension of degree $f$.)

**Conclusion.**

For each $i = 1, \dots, r$:
$$_{\mathfrak{p}_i} : \mathbb{Q}_p] = e_i f_i.$$

This is consistent with ($\ast$), and the identity $:\mathbb{Q}] = \sum_i e_i
f_i$ is precisely the global–local compatibility reflecting the decomposition $K
\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p \cong \prod_i K_{\mathfrak{p}_i}$. $\blacksquare$
