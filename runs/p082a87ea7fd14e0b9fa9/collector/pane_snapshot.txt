## Proof

Let $Y = X \cup_{\Sigma \subset \partial X} C(\Sigma)$. In the stated setting th
e subspace along which the cone is attached is the boundary itself, so $\Sigma =
 \partial X$ and
$$Y = X \cup_{\partial X} C(\partial X).$$

**Step 1. $Y$ is homotopy equivalent to the quotient $X/\partial X$.**

The cone $C(\partial X)$ is contractible, and $\partial X$ sits inside it as the
 base. Collapsing the cone to its cone point is a deformation retraction of $C(\
partial X)$ onto the cone point that restricts to the identity on $\partial X$ o
nly at time $0$; the standard cofiber argument shows that attaching a cone along
 a subspace is homotopy equivalent to collapsing that subspace:
$$X \cup_{\partial X} C(\partial X) \;\simeq\; X/\partial X.$$

**Step 2. Identify $H^n(Y)$ with relative cohomology.**

Since $X$ is a compact manifold with boundary, $\partial X$ admits a collar neig
hborhood in $X$, so $(X, \partial X)$ is a good pair. For a good pair the quotie
nt map induces an isomorphism
$$\widetilde H^{\,n}(X/\partial X) \;\cong\; H^n(X, \partial X).$$
For $n \geq 1$ (here $n = \dim X \setminus \partial X \geq 1$), reduced and unre
duced cohomology of $X/\partial X$ agree, so
$$H^n(Y) \;\cong\; H^n(X/\partial X) \;\cong\; \widetilde H^{\,n}(X/\partial X)
\;\cong\; H^n(X, \partial X).$$

**Step 3. Apply Lefschetz duality.**

By Lefschetz duality for the compact orientable (in the relevant sense) $n$-mani
fold $X$ with boundary,
$$H^n(X, \partial X) \;\cong\; H_0(X).$$
The asphericity hypothesis ($X$ is a $K(\pi,1)$) forces $X$ to be connected, hen
ce $H_0(X) \cong \mathbb{Z}$. (Equivalently, this is the assumption $H^n(X,\part
ial X) = \mathbb{Z}$ given in the problem.)

**Conclusion.**

Combining the three steps,
$$\boxed{H^n\!\left(X \cup_{\partial X} C(\partial X)\right) \;\cong\; \mathbb{Z
}.}$$

The asphericity of $X$ and $\partial X$ guarantees that the only contribution in
 top degree comes from the fundamental class / Lefschetz dual of a point, with n
o extra summands from higher homotopy; the cone attachment kills the boundary an
d leaves exactly one top-dimensional cohomology class.
