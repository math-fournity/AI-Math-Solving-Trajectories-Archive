**Problem.** Let $X$ be a compact connected Riemann surface of genus $g > 0$ and
 $B \subset X$ a finite subset. Is $\pi_1(X - B)$ finitely generated?

**Answer: Yes.**

## Proof

Write $|B| = n \geq 0$. A compact connected Riemann surface of genus $g$ is, top

**Case $n = 0$.** Then $X - B = X = \Sigma_g$, whose fundamental group has the s

$$\pi_1(\Sigma_g) = \langle a_1, b_1, \dots, a_g, b_g \mid [a_1,b_1]\cdots[a_g,b
_g] = 1\rangle,$$
generated by $2g$ elements. Hence finitely generated.

**Case $n \geq 1$.** We show $X - B$ deformation retracts onto a finite graph, w

$$\pi_1(X - B) \cong \pi_1(M).$$

*Step 2: Compute the Euler characteristic.* By the classification of compact sur
