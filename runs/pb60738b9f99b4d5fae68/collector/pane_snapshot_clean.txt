> A finite étale algebra over a field $k$ is a finite product of finite separabl

$$A \;\cong\; \prod_{i=1}^{r} K_i,$$

$$\sum_{i=1}^{r} _i : k(x)] \;=\; \deg f.$$

$$\mathfrak{p}_j = K_1 \times \cdots \times K_{j-1} \times \{0\} \times K_{j+1}

$$\mathcal{O}_{Y_x, y} \;=\; A_{\mathfrak{p}_j} \;\cong\; K_j.$$

This is a **field** (a finite separable extension of $k(x)$).
