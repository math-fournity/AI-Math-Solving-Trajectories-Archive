## Solution

**Setup.** The number 2008 has digits 2, 0, 0, 8. Inserting a 4-digit number $x$
 between the two zeroes gives the 8-digit number

$$N = 2\,0\,\underbrace{d_1d_2d_3d_4}_{x}\,0\,8 = 20{,}000{,}008 + 100x.$$

**Divisibility condition.** We need $x \mid N$, i.e.

$$x \mid (20{,}000{,}008 + 100x) \implies x \mid 20{,}000{,}008.$$

So $x$ must be a 4-digit divisor of $20{,}000{,}008$, and the quotient is

$$q = \frac{N}{x} = \frac{20{,}000{,}008}{x} + 100.$$

**Factoring $20{,}000{,}008$.**

$$20{,}000{,}008 = 8 \times 2{,}500{,}001 = 2^3 \times 7 \times 357{,}143 = 2^3
\times 7 \times 19 \times 18{,}797.$$

Checking all primes up to $\sqrt{18{,}797} \approx 137$ confirms $18{,}797$ is p
rime. Thus

$$20{,}000{,}008 = 2^3 \times 7 \times 19 \times 18{,}797.$$

**Finding 4-digit divisors.** Any divisor containing the factor $18{,}797$ alrea
dy exceeds $9999$, so we need $x = 2^a \times 7^b \times 19^c$ with $a \in \{0,1
,2,3\}$, $b,c \in \{0,1\}$. The largest such value is $8 \times 7 \times 19 = 10
64$, and the only combination reaching four digits is:

$$x = 2^3 \times 7 \times 19 = 1064.$$

All other combinations ($2^a \times 7$, $2^a \times 19$, $2^a$) are at most $152
 < 1000$.

**Computing the quotient.**

$$\frac{20{,}000{,}008}{1064} = 18{,}797, \qquad q = 18{,}797 + 100 = 18{,}897.$
$

**Verification.** The 8-digit number is $N = 20{,}000{,}008 + 100 \times 1064 =
20{,}106{,}408$, i.e. the digits $2\,0\,1\,0\,6\,4\,0\,8$ with $1064$ inserted b
etween the two zeroes. Then

$$1064 \times 18{,}897 = 19{,}152{,}000 + 954{,}408 = 20{,}106{,}408 = N. \check
mark$$

The quotient is $\boxed{18{,}897}$.
