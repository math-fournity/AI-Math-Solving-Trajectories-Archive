## Solution

**Setup.** Place $A=(0,0)$, $B=(3,0)$. Let $C=(x,y)$ with $x^2+y^2=64$ and $(x-3
)^2+y^2=49$. Subtracting: $6x-9=15\Rightarrow x=4$, $y^2=48\Rightarrow y=4\sqrt3
$. So $C=(4,4\sqrt3)$.

Midpoints: $M=(\tfrac32,0)$, $N=(2,2\sqrt3)$.

**Sensor $T$.** Parametrize $T=B+t(C-B)=(3+t,\,4\sqrt3\,t)$. The condition $AT=C
T$ gives
$$AT^2=(3+t)^2+48t^2=9+6t+49t^2,\qquad CT^2=49(1-t)^2.$$
Setting equal: $9+6t+49t^2=49t^2-98t+49\Rightarrow 104t=40\Rightarrow t=\tfrac5{
13}$.

Thus $T=\left(\tfrac{44}{13},\tfrac{20\sqrt3}{13}\right)$, with $AT=CT=\tfrac{56
}{13}$.

**Circles through $A$.** A circle through the origin has equation $x^2+y^2+Dx+Ey
=0$.

*Zone Two* ($A,M,N$): From $M$: $\tfrac94+\tfrac32 D=0\Rightarrow D=-\tfrac32$.
From $N$: $4+12+2D+2\sqrt3\,E=0\Rightarrow 13+2\sqrt3 E=0\Rightarrow E=-\tfrac{1
3}{2\sqrt3}$.

$$\text{Zone Two: } x^2+y^2-\tfrac32 x-\tfrac{13}{2\sqrt3}y=0.$$

*Zone One* ($A,B,T$): From $B$: $9+3D=0\Rightarrow D=-3$. From $T$: $AT^2-3\cdot
\tfrac{44}{13}+E\cdot\tfrac{20\sqrt3}{13}=0$, i.e. $\tfrac{3136}{169}-\tfrac{132
}{13}+\tfrac{20\sqrt3}{13}E=0$, giving $\tfrac{1420}{169}+\tfrac{20\sqrt3}{13}E=
0\Rightarrow E=-\tfrac{71\sqrt3}{39}$.

$$\text{Zone One: } x^2+y^2-3x-\tfrac{71\sqrt3}{39}y=0.$$

**Second intersection $D$.** Subtracting the two equations (radical axis through
 $A$ and $D$):
$$-\tfrac32 x+\left(-\tfrac{71\sqrt3}{39}+\tfrac{13\sqrt3}{6}\right)y=0.$$
The $y$-coefficient: $\sqrt3\!\left(-\tfrac{71}{39}+\tfrac{13}{6}\right)=\sqrt3\
cdot\tfrac{-426+507}{234}=\tfrac{9\sqrt3}{26}$.

So $-\tfrac32 x+\tfrac{9\sqrt3}{26}y=0\Rightarrow y=\tfrac{13\sqrt3}{9}x$.

Substitute into Zone Two:
$$x^2+\tfrac{169}{27}x^2-\tfrac32 x-\tfrac{169}{18}x=0\;\Rightarrow\;x\!\left(\t
frac{196}{27}x-\tfrac{98}{9}\right)=0.$$
The nonzero root: $x=\tfrac{98}{9}\cdot\tfrac{27}{196}=\tfrac32$, and $y=\tfrac{
13\sqrt3}{9}\cdot\tfrac32=\tfrac{13\sqrt3}{6}$.

$$D=\left(\tfrac32,\,\tfrac{13\sqrt3}{6}\right).$$

**Distance $DC$.**
$$DC^2=\left(4-\tfrac32\right)^2+\left(4\sqrt3-\tfrac{13\sqrt3}{6}\right)^2=\lef
t(\tfrac52\right)^2+3\!\left(\tfrac{11}{6}\right)^2=\tfrac{25}{4}+\tfrac{121}{12
}=\tfrac{75+121}{12}=\tfrac{196}{12}=\tfrac{49}{3}.$$
$$DC=\frac{7}{\sqrt3}=\frac{7\sqrt3}{3}.$$

The straight-line distance from Delta to Charlie is $\boxed{\dfrac{7\sqrt3}{3}}$
 kilometers.
