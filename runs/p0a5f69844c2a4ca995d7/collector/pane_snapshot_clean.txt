## Problem

Find the $r$th raw moment of a doubly noncentral $F$ distribution with parameter
s $n_1, n_2, \theta_1, \theta_2$.

## Setup and Definition

The doubly noncentral $F$ distribution is defined as follows. Let $X \sim \chi^2
(n_1, \theta_1)$ and $Y \sim \chi^2(n_2, \theta_2)$ be **independent** noncentra
l chi-square random variables with degrees of freedom $n_1, n_2$ and noncentrali
ties $\theta_1, \theta_2$ respectively. Then

$$F = \frac{X/n_1}{Y/n_2}$$

follows a doubly noncentral $F$ distribution. We seek $\mathbb{E}[F^r]$.

## Key Tool: Poisson Mixture Representation of Noncentral $\chi^2$

A noncentral chi-square $Z \sim \chi^2(\nu, \lambda)$ admits the representation

$$Z \mid J = j \sim \chi^2(\nu + 2j), \qquad J \sim \text{Poisson}(\lambda/2),$$

with $J$ independent of the chi-square draw. Hence for any $s$ with $\nu/2 + s >
 0$,

$$\mathbb{E}[Z^s] = \sum_{j=0}^{\infty} e^{-\lambda/2}\frac{(\lambda/2)^j}{j!}\,
 2^{s}\,\frac{\Gamma(\nu/2 + j + s)}{\Gamma(\nu/2 + j)}.$$

## Independence Factorization

Since $X \perp Y$,

$$\mathbb{E}[F^r] = \left(\frac{n_2}{n_1}\right)^r \mathbb{E}[X^r]\,\mathbb{E}[Y
^{-r}].$$

Apply the mixture representation to each factor.

**Numerator.** With $J \sim \text{Poisson}(\theta_1/2)$ and $X\mid J=j \sim \chi
^2(n_1+2j)$,

$$\mathbb{E}[X^r] = e^{-\theta_1/2}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{(\theta_1/2)^j}{j!}\
,2^r\,\frac{\Gamma(n_1/2+j+r)}{\Gamma(n_1/2+j)}.$$

**Denominator (negative moment).** With $K \sim \text{Poisson}(\theta_2/2)$ and
$Y\mid K=k \sim \chi^2(n_2+2k)$,

$$\mathbb{E}[Y^{-r}] = e^{-\theta_2/2}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(\theta_2/2)^k}{k
!}\,2^{-r}\,\frac{\Gamma(n_2/2+k-r)}{\Gamma(n_2/2+k)}.$$

The factors $2^r$ and $2^{-r}$ cancel in the product. Combining,

$$\mathbb{E}[F^r] = \left(\frac{n_2}{n_1}\right)^r e^{-(\theta_1+\theta_2)/2}\su
m_{j,k\ge 0}\frac{(\theta_1/2)^j(\theta_2/2)^k}{j!\,k!}\,\frac{\Gamma(n_1/2+j+r)
}{\Gamma(n_1/2+j)}\,\frac{\Gamma(n_2/2+k-r)}{\Gamma(n_2/2+k)}.$$

## Reduction via Pochhammer Symbols

Using $\Gamma(a+j+s)/\Gamma(a+j) = \frac{\Gamma(a+s)}{\Gamma(a)}\frac{(a+s)_j}{(
a)_j}$ with the rising Pochhammer symbol $(\cdot)_j$:

$$\frac{\Gamma(n_1/2+j+r)}{\Gamma(n_1/2+j)} = \frac{\Gamma(n_1/2+r)}{\Gamma(n_1/
2)}\,\frac{(n_1/2+r)_j}{(n_1/2)_j},$$

$$\frac{\Gamma(n_2/2+k-r)}{\Gamma(n_2/2+k)} = \frac{\Gamma(n_2/2-r)}{\Gamma(n_2/
2)}\,\frac{(n_2/2-r)_k}{(n_2/2)_k}.$$

Substituting, the double sum **separates** into a product of two Kummer confluen
t hypergeometric series $_1F_1(a;b;z)=\sum_{m\ge 0}\frac{(a)_m}{(b)_m}\frac{z^m}
{m!}$:

$$\mathbb{E}[F^r] = \left(\frac{n_2}{n_1}\right)^r e^{-(\theta_1+\theta_2)/2}\,\
frac{\Gamma(n_1/2+r)}{\Gamma(n_1/2)}\,\frac{\Gamma(n_2/2-r)}{\Gamma(n_2/2)}\;\cd
ot\;{}_1F_1\!\left(\tfrac{n_1}{2}+r;\,\tfrac{n_1}{2};\,\tfrac{\theta_1}{2}\right
)\;{}_1F_1\!\left(\tfrac{n_2}{2}-r;\,\tfrac{n_2}{2};\,\tfrac{\theta_2}{2}\right)
.$$

## Domain of Validity

The gamma ratios require $n_1/2 + r > 0$ (binding at $j=0$) and $n_2/2 - r > 0$
(binding at $k=0$), so the moment exists iff

$$-\frac{n_1}{2} < r < \frac{n_2}{2}.$$

## Verification (Special Cases)

- **Central $F$** ($\theta_1=\theta_2=0$): $_1F_1(\cdot;\cdot;0)=1$, giving
  $$\mathbb{E}[F^r] = \left(\frac{n_2}{n_1}\right)^r \frac{\Gamma(n_1/2+r)\,\Gam
ma(n_2/2-r)}{\Gamma(n_1/2)\,\Gamma(n_2/2)},$$
  the classical $r$th raw moment of a (central) $F_{n_1,n_2}$. ✓

- **Singly noncentral** ($\theta_2=0$): the second $_1F_1$ collapses to $1$, rec
overing the standard singly noncentral $F$ moment formula. ✓

- **First moment** ($r=1$, $\theta_1=\theta_2=0$): $\mathbb{E}[F] = \frac{n_2}{n
_1}\frac{\Gamma(n_1/2+1)}{\Gamma(n_1/2)}\frac{\Gamma(n_2/2-1)}{\Gamma(n_2/2)} =
\frac{n_2}{n_1}\cdot\frac{n_1}{2}\cdot\frac{2}{n_2-2} = \frac{n_2}{n_2-2}$ for $
n_2>2$, the well-known mean of $F_{n_1,n_2}$. ✓

## Result

The $r$th raw moment of a doubly noncentral $F$ distribution with parameters $n_
1, n_2, \theta_1, \theta_2$ is

$$\boxed{\;\mathbb{E}[F^r] = \left(\frac{n_2}{n_1}\right)^r e^{-(\theta_1+\theta
_2)/2}\,\frac{\Gamma\!\left(\frac{n_1}{2}+r\right)}{\Gamma\!\left(\frac{n_1}{2}\
right)}\,\frac{\Gamma\!\left(\frac{n_2}{2}-r\right)}{\Gamma\!\left(\frac{n_2}{2}
\right)}\;{}_1F_1\!\left(\tfrac{n_1}{2}+r;\,\tfrac{n_1}{2};\,\tfrac{\theta_1}{2}
\right)\;{}_1F_1\!\left(\tfrac{n_2}{2}-r;\,\tfrac{n_2}{2};\,\tfrac{\theta_2}{2}\
right)\;}$$

valid for $-\dfrac{n_1}{2} < r < \dfrac{n_2}{2}$, where $_1F_1$ is the Kummer co
nfluent hypergeometric function.
