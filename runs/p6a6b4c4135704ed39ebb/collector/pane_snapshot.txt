## The de Bruijn Formula: Orbits of $G \times H$ on $B^A$

### Setup

Let $G$ act on a finite set $A$ (with $|A| = n$) and $H$ act on a finite set $B$
 (with $|B| = m$). The set $B^A$ of all functions $f : A \to B$ carries an actio
n of $G \times H$ defined by

$$(g, h) \cdot f \;=\; h \circ f \circ g^{-1}, \qquad g \in G,\; h \in H.$$

We seek the number of orbits $|B^A / (G \times H)|$.

### Burnside's Lemma

By Burnside's lemma,

$$|B^A / (G \times H)| \;=\; \frac{1}{|G|\,|H|} \sum_{g \in G,\; h \in H} \bigl|
\operatorname{Fix}(g, h)\bigr|, \tag{1}$$

where $\operatorname{Fix}(g,h) = \{f \in B^A : h \circ f \circ g^{-1} = f\}$, eq
uivalently

$$h(f(a)) = f(g(a)) \quad \text{for all } a \in A. \tag{2}$$

### Counting $\operatorname{Fix}(g, h)$ via Cycle Structure

Write the permutation $g$ of $A$ as a product of disjoint cycles. Let $m_i(g)$ d
enote the number of cycles of length $i$ in $g$. Consider one such cycle $(a_1\;
 a_2\; \cdots\; a_i)$. Applying (2) repeatedly:

$$f(a_2) = h(f(a_1)),\quad f(a_3) = h^2(f(a_1)),\quad \ldots,\quad f(a_1) = h^i(
f(a_1)).$$

The last equality forces $f(a_1)$ to be a **fixed point of $h^i$**, i.e. an elem
ent $b \in B$ with $h^i(b) = b$. Once $f(a_1)$ is chosen freely among such fixed
 points, the values $f(a_2), \ldots, f(a_i)$ are determined. Hence each $i$-cycl
e of $g$ contributes a factor of $|\operatorname{Fix}(h^i)|$.

Since the cycles of $g$ are independent, we get

$$\bigl|\operatorname{Fix}(g, h)\bigr| \;=\; \prod_{i \geq 1} \bigl|\operatornam
e{Fix}(h^i)\bigr|^{m_i(g)}. \tag{3}$$

### Expressing $|\operatorname{Fix}(h^i)|$ via $m_j(\tau)$

Now let $\tau \in H$ be a permutation of $B$, and let $m_j(\tau)$ denote the num
ber of cycles of length $j$ in $\tau$. A cycle of $\tau$ of length $j$ consists
of elements fixed by $\tau^i$ if and only if $j \mid i$ (since $\tau^i$ fixes ev
ery element of a $j$-cycle exactly when $i$ is a multiple of $j$); each such cyc
le contributes $j$ fixed points. Therefore

$$\bigl|\operatorname{Fix}(\tau^i)\bigr| \;=\; \sum_{j \mid i} j \cdot m_j(\tau)
. \tag{4}$$

Denote this quantity by $c_i(\tau) := \sum_{j \mid i} j\, m_j(\tau)$.

### The Cycle Index $Z_G$

The **cycle index polynomial** (Zykelindex) of $G$ is

$$Z_G(x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n) \;=\; \frac{1}{|G|} \sum_{g \in G} \prod_{i \geq 1}
 x_i^{m_i(g)}. \tag{5}$$

It is the average, over all $g \in G$, of the monomial recording the cycle-type
of $g$.

### Summing over $g$ first

Substituting (3) into (1) and summing over $g$ first:

$$|B^A / (G \times H)| \;=\; \frac{1}{|H|} \sum_{h \in H} \underbrace{\frac{1}{|
G|} \sum_{g \in G} \prod_{i \geq 1} c_i(h)^{m_i(g)}}_{=\; Z_G(c_1(h),\, c_2(h),\
, \ldots,\, c_n(h))}.$$

This yields the **de Bruijn formula**:

$$\boxed{|B^A / (G \times H)| \;=\; \frac{1}{|H|} \sum_{\tau \in H} Z_G\!\Bigl(c
_1(\tau),\; c_2(\tau),\; \ldots,\; c_n(\tau)\Bigr)} \tag{6}$$

where

$$c_i(\tau) \;=\; \sum_{j \mid i} j\, m_j(\tau) \;=\; \bigl|\operatorname{Fix}(\
tau^i)\bigr|. \tag{7}$$

### Meaning of the Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **$Z_G(x_1, \ldots, x_n)$** | The **cycle index polynomial** of $G$: the avera
ge over $g \in G$ of the monomial $\prod_i x_i^{m_i(g)}$, where $m_i(g)$ counts
$i$-cycles of $g$ on $A$. It encodes the full cycle-structure distribution of $G
$'s action on $A$. |
| **$m_i(\tau)$** | The number of cycles of length $i$ in the permutation $\tau
\in H$ acting on $B$. It records the cycle-type of $\tau$. |
| **$c_i(\tau) = \sum_{j \mid i} j\, m_j(\tau)$** | The number of fixed points o
f $\tau^i$ in $B$. A $j$-cycle of $\tau$ is fixed pointwise by $\tau^i$ iff $j \
mid i$, contributing $j$ elements; summing over all such $j$ gives $c_i(\tau)$.
|
| **The substitution $x_i \mapsto c_i(\tau)$** | Replaces each abstract variable
 $x_i$ in the cycle index with the number of ways to color a single $i$-cycle of
 $g$ consistently with $\tau$ — namely the number of $\tau^i$-fixed points in $B
$. |
| **The outer average $\frac{1}{|H|}\sum_{\tau}$** | Averages over all $\tau \in
 H$, exactly as Burnside's lemma requires for the $H$-factor of the group. |

### Why the Formula Is Correct — Summary of the Argument

1. **Burnside** reduces the orbit count to averaging $|\operatorname{Fix}(g,h)|$
 over $G \times H$.
2. **Cycle decomposition of $g$**: each $i$-cycle of $g$ forces $f$ to map its s
tarting point into $\operatorname{Fix}(h^i)$; the remaining values on that cycle
 are then determined. Independence of cycles gives the product (3).
3. **Cycle decomposition of $\tau$**: $|\operatorname{Fix}(\tau^i)| = \sum_{j \m
id i} j\, m_j(\tau)$ by (4), since a $j$-cycle is pointwise fixed by $\tau^i$ if
f $j \mid i$.
4. **Recognizing the cycle index**: the inner sum over $g \in G$ is precisely $Z
_G$ evaluated at $(c_1(\tau), \ldots, c_n(\tau))$ by definition (5).
5. The outer sum over $\tau \in H$ with the factor $1/|H|$ completes the Burnsid
e average.

### Special Cases

- **$H = \{e\}$ (trivial action on $B$):** Then $c_i(\tau) = |B|$ for all $i$, a
nd (6) becomes $\frac{1}{|H|}\cdot Z_G(|B|, |B|, \ldots) = Z_G(m, m, \ldots, m)$
, which is the classical **Pólya enumeration formula** for the number of $G$-orb
its of colorings of $A$ with $|B|$ colors.
- **$G = \{e\}$ (trivial action on $A$):** Then $Z_G(x_1) = x_1$ and (6) gives $
\frac{1}{|H|}\sum_{\tau} c_1(\tau) = \frac{1}{|H|}\sum_{\tau} |\operatorname{Fix
}(\tau)|$, which is Burnside's lemma for $H$ acting on $B$ — i.e., the number of
 $H$-orbits of $B$.
