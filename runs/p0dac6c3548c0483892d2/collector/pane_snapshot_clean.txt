## Proof

**Setup.** We are given:
- $f_1 = R_1^2 + R_2^2 + R_3^2$
- $f_2 = R_1 S_1 + R_2 S_2 + R_3 S_3$

with Poisson brackets $\{S_i, S_j\} = \varepsilon_{ijk} S_k$, $\{R_i, S_j\} = \v
arepsilon_{ijk} R_k$, $\{R_i, R_j\} = 0$.

**Step 1: Apply bilinearity.**

$$\{f_1, f_2\} = \sum_{i=1}^{3}\sum_{j=1}^{3} \{R_i^2,\, R_j S_j\}.$$

**Step 2: Use the Leibniz (derivation) rule in each argument.**

For the first argument ($R_i^2 = R_i \cdot R_i$):
$$\{R_i^2, R_j S_j\} = R_i\{R_i, R_j S_j\} + \{R_i, R_j S_j\}R_i = 2\,R_i\,\{R_i
, R_j S_j\},$$
since the $R_i, S_j$ are commuting coordinate functions on phase space.

For the second argument ($R_j S_j$):
$$\{R_i, R_j S_j\} = R_j\{R_i, S_j\} + \{R_i, R_j\}S_j = R_j\,\varepsilon_{ijk}R
_k + 0 = \varepsilon_{ijk}\,R_j R_k.$$

Combining:
$$\{R_i^2, R_j S_j\} = 2\,\varepsilon_{ijk}\,R_i R_j R_k.$$

**Step 3: Sum over all indices.**

$$\{f_1, f_2\} = 2\sum_{i,j,k=1}^{3} \varepsilon_{ijk}\,R_i R_j R_k.$$

**Step 4: Show the sum vanishes by symmetry.**

The product $R_i R_j R_k$ is fully symmetric under permutation of indices (ordin
ary commutative multiplication of functions). The Levi-Civita symbol $\varepsilo
n_{ijk}$ is fully antisymmetric. For every term $\varepsilon_{ijk}R_iR_jR_k$ the
re is a paired term obtained by swapping $i \leftrightarrow j$:
$$\varepsilon_{jik}R_jR_iR_k = -\varepsilon_{ijk}R_iR_jR_k,$$
which cancels it exactly. Therefore
$$\sum_{i,j,k}\varepsilon_{ijk}R_iR_jR_k = 0.$$

**Conclusion.**

$$\boxed{\{f_1, f_2\} = 0.}$$

The two first integrals $f_1 = |R|^2$ and $f_2 = R\cdot S$ are in involution, co
nfirming they are compatible conserved quantities of the Hamiltonian system (con
sistent with the Kirchhoff/Lagrange top structure).
