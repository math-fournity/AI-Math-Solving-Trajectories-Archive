## Solution

**Method of characteristics.** The PDE is $(x-y)u_x + (y-x-u)u_y = u$. The chara
cteristic ODEs are:

$$\frac{dx}{ds}=x-y,\qquad \frac{dy}{ds}=y-x-u,\qquad \frac{du}{ds}=u.$$

**First integral.** Adding the first two equations:

$$\frac{d(x+y)}{ds}=(x-y)+(y-x-u)=-u.$$

Since $\frac{du}{ds}=u$, we get $\frac{d(x+y)}{du}=\frac{-u}{u}=-1$, hence

$$x+y+u=C_1.\tag{I}$$

**Second integral.** Let $r=x-y$. Then

$$\frac{dr}{ds}=\frac{dx}{ds}-\frac{dy}{ds}=(x-y)-(y-x-u)=2(x-y)+u=2r+u.$$

Thus $\frac{dr}{du}=\frac{2r+u}{u}=\frac{2r}{u}+1$, a linear ODE in $r(u)$:

$$\frac{dr}{du}-\frac{2}{u}r=1.$$

Integrating factor $\mu=u^{-2}$:

$$\frac{d}{du}\!\left(r\,u^{-2}\right)=u^{-2}\;\Longrightarrow\; r\,u^{-2}=-u^{-
1}+C_2\;\Longrightarrow\; r=-u+C_2\,u^2.$$

So

$$x-y+u=C_2\,u^2.\tag{II}$$

**Applying the initial condition** $u(x,0)=1$. At $y=0$, $u=1$, with $x=x_0$:

- From (I): $C_1 = x_0+0+1 = x_0+1$.
- From (II): $C_2 = (x_0-0+1)/1 = x_0+1$.

Hence $C_1=C_2=:C$, and eliminating $C$ from (I) and (II):

$$(x+y+u)\,u^2 = x-y+u.$$

Rearranging:

$$\boxed{u^2(x+y+u)+(y-x-u)=0.}$$

**Verification of the initial condition:** at $y=0,\;u=1$:

$$1^2(x+0+1)+(0-x-1)=(x+1)+(-x-1)=0.\;\checkmark$$

This is **option (1)**.
