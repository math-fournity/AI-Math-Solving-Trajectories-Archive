## Proof

We show it is **not** possible.

**Counting argument (replication number).** Fix any single bracelet $B$. Since t
here are 100 bracelets in total, $B$ must be paired with each of the other $99$
bracelets exactly once (every pair worn together exactly once).

On any day that $B$ is worn, she wears exactly 3 bracelets, so $B$ is paired wit
h exactly **2** other bracelets on that day. Therefore the number of days on whi
ch $B$ is worn must equal

$$\frac{99}{2} = 49.5.$$

But the number of days a bracelet is worn must be a nonnegative **integer**. Sin
ce $99$ is odd, $99/2$ is not an integer — a contradiction.

This contradiction arises for *every* bracelet, so no valid schedule can exist.

einer triple system $\mathrm{STS}(100)$, i.e. a $2\text{-}(100,3,1)$ design. A n
ecessary condition for $\mathrm{STS}(v)$ is that the replication number $r = (v-
1)/2$ be an integer, i.e. $v$ must be odd. Since $100$ is even, $\mathrm{STS}(10
0)$ does not exist. (The full existence criterion is $v \equiv 1$ or $3 \pmod{6}

**Conclusion.** It is impossible for every pair of the 100 bracelets to be worn
together exactly once.
