## Solution

**Setup.** Let the regular hexagon $ABCDEF$ have center $O$ at the origin and si
de length $s$, with vertices at angles $0°, 60°, \dots, 300°$ (circumradius $s$)
. The apothem is $a_0 = \frac{s\sqrt{3}}{2}$. The outward unit normals to sides
$AB, BC, CD, DE, EF, FA$ are:

$$n_1=\left(\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2},\tfrac12\right),\ n_2=(0,1),\ n_3=\left(-\tfrac{\s
qrt3}{2},\tfrac12\right),\ n_4=\left(-\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2},-\tfrac12\right),\ n_5=(
0,-1),\ n_6=\left(\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2},-\tfrac12\right).$$

For $P=(x,y)$ inside, the distance to side $i$ is $d_i = a_0 - P\cdot n_i$. Oppo
site sides satisfy $d_i + d_{i+3} = s\sqrt3$.

**Finding $s$ and $P$.** From $PG = \frac92$ and $PI = 6$ (sides $AB$ and $CD$,
which are *not* opposite but symmetric about the $y$-axis):

$$PG + PI = \left(a_0 - \tfrac{x\sqrt3}{2} - \tfrac{y}{2}\right) + \left(a_0 + \
tfrac{x\sqrt3}{2} - \tfrac{y}{2}\right) = 2a_0 - y = s\sqrt3 - y = \tfrac{21}{2}
.$$

From $PK = \frac{15}{2}$ (side $EF$): $PK = a_0 + y = \frac{s\sqrt3}{2} + y = \f
rac{15}{2}$, so $y = \frac{15}{2} - \frac{s\sqrt3}{2}$.

Substituting: $s\sqrt3 - \frac{15}{2} + \frac{s\sqrt3}{2} = \frac{21}{2}$, givin
g $\frac{3}{2}s\sqrt3 = 18$, so $s\sqrt3 = 12$, $s = 4\sqrt3$, $a_0 = 6$.

Then $y = \frac{15}{2} - 6 = \frac32$, and from $PG$: $6 - \frac{x\sqrt3}{2} - \
frac34 = \frac92$ gives $\frac{x\sqrt3}{2} = \frac34$, so $x = \frac{\sqrt3}{2}$
.

**All six distances** (using $d_i + d_{i+3} = 12$):

| Side | Distance |
|------|----------|
| $AB \to PG$ | $\frac92$ |
| $BC \to PH$ | $\frac92$ |
| $CD \to PI$ | $6$ |
| $DE \to PJ$ | $\frac{15}{2}$ |
| $EF \to PK$ | $\frac{15}{2}$ |
| $FA \to PL$ | $6$ |

**Foot coordinates.** Each foot is $P + d_i\, n_i$:

$$G = \left(\tfrac{11\sqrt3}{4}, \tfrac{15}{4}\right),\ H = \left(\tfrac{\sqrt3}
{2}, 6\right),\ I = \left(-\tfrac{5\sqrt3}{2}, \tfrac92\right),\ J = \left(-\tfr
ac{13\sqrt3}{4}, -\tfrac94\right),\ K = \left(\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}, -6\right),\ L =
 \left(\tfrac{7\sqrt3}{2}, -\tfrac32\right).$$

(Verified: e.g., $G$ lies on line $AB$ from $A(4\sqrt3,0)$ to $B(2\sqrt3,6)$ at
parameter $t=\frac58$.)

**Shoelace area.** Writing $X_i = \frac{\sqrt3}{4}u_i$, $Y_i = \frac14 v_i$ with
 $(u,v)$:

| | $u$ | $v$ |
|---|---|---|
| $G$ | 11 | 15 |
| $H$ | 2 | 24 |
| $I$ | −10 | 18 |
| $J$ | −13 | −9 |
| $K$ | 2 | −24 |
| $L$ | 14 | −6 |

$$\text{Area} = \frac12\cdot\frac{\sqrt3}{16}\sum_{i}(u_i v_{i+1}-u_{i+1}v_i).$$

Cross products: $234,\ 276,\ 324,\ 330,\ 324,\ 276$, summing to $1764$.

$$\text{Area} = \frac{1764\sqrt3}{32} = \frac{441\sqrt3}{8}.$$

**Verification.** $\gcd(441,8)=1$ (since $441=3^2\cdot7^2$, $8=2^3$) and $b=3$ i
s squarefree. The area $\approx 95.5$ is less than the original hexagon's area $
72\sqrt3\approx 124.7$, consistent with $GHIJKL$ being inscribed.

Thus $a=441$, $b=3$, $c=8$, and

$$a+b+c = 441+3+8 = \boxed{452}.$$
