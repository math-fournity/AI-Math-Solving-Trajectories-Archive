47$, come in two types:

0.
- **Between-pair:** $(2k, 2k+1)$ for $k = 1,\ldots,23$ — both in $S$ iff $2k \in
 S$ and $2k+1 \in S$, i.e., iff $a_k = 1$ and $a_{k+1} = 0$.

So the number of adjacent pairs both in $S$ equals the number of "10" transition

9 such transitions.

$$t_{10} - t_{01} = [a_1 = 1] - [a_{24} = 1].$$
This holds because every internal change contributes equally to both counts, and
