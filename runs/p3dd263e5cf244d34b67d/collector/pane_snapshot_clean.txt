Looking at this problem, I need to determine whether Γ(X, ⊕_i F_i) ≅ ⊕_i Γ(X, F_
i) for quasi-coherent sheaves {F_i} on a scheme X of finite type over a field.

**Answer: TRUE.**

**Proof.**

Let X be a scheme of finite type over a field k, and let {F_i}_{i∈I} be a family
 of quasi-coherent sheaves on X. We show the natural map

φ: ⊕_i Γ(X, F_i) → Γ(X, ⊕_i F_i)

is an isomorphism.

**Key fact:** A scheme of finite type over a field is noetherian, hence **quasi-
compact**. So X admits a finite cover by affine open subschemes U_1, …, U_m.

**Injectivity.** For each i, the inclusion F_i ↪ ⊕_i F_i is a monomorphism of sh
eaves. Since Γ(X, −) is left exact, the induced map Γ(X, F_i) → Γ(X, ⊕ F_i) is i
njective, and the map φ from the direct sum is injective as well.

**Surjectivity.** Let s ∈ Γ(X, ⊕_i F_i). We must show s lies in the image of φ.

*Step 1: On affine opens, direct sums are computed module-wise.* For any affine
open U = Spec A, since each F_i is quasi-coherent, (⊕_i F_i)|_U is the quasi-coh
erent sheaf associated to the A-module ⊕_i Γ(U, F_i). Therefore:

Γ(U, ⊕_i F_i) = ⊕_i Γ(U, F_i).

This means s|_{U_j}, for each j = 1,…,m, is a **finite** sum: s|_{U_j} = ∑_{i ∈
I_j} s_{j,i}, where I_j ⊂ I is finite and s_{j,i} ∈ Γ(U_j, F_i).

*Step 2: Reduce to a finite sub-sum.* Set I_0 = I_1 ∪ … ∪ I_m. This is a **finit
e** subset of I. Since s|_{U_j} involves only indices in I_j ⊂ I_0 for every j,
the section s factors through the subsheaf ⊕_{i ∈ I_0} F_i ↪ ⊕_{i ∈ I} F_i. That
 is, s ∈ Γ(X, ⊕_{i ∈ I_0} F_i).

*Step 3: Finite direct sums commute with Γ.* Since I_0 is finite, the finite dir
ect sum ⊕_{i ∈ I_0} F_i coincides with the finite product ∏_{i ∈ I_0} F_i in the
 abelian category QCoh(X). The global sections functor, being a right adjoint (t
o the constant sheaf functor) or equivalently a left-exact functor, preserves fi
nite products. Therefore:

Γ(X, ⊕_{i ∈ I_0} F_i) = ⊕_{i ∈ I_0} Γ(X, F_i).

So s = ∑_{i ∈ I_0} t_i for some t_i ∈ Γ(X, F_i), which means s is in the image o
f φ. ∎

**Remark on necessity of quasi-compactness.** The quasi-compactness of X is esse
ntial. For a counterexample without it, take X = ⊔_{n∈ℕ} Spec k (infinite disjoi
nt union, not quasi-compact) and F_n the skyscraper sheaf k supported at the n-t
h point. Then ⊕_n Γ(X, F_n) = ⊕_n k (finite-support sequences), but Γ(X, ⊕_n F_n
) = ∏_n k (all sequences), since a global section of a sheaf on a disjoint union
 assigns a value independently to each component. These are not isomorphic, conf
irming that quasi-compactness — which schemes of finite type over a field posses
s — is the crucial hypothesis.
