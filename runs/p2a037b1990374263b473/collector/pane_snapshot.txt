## Proof

**Claim:** The map $f \colon \mathbb{R}^{rn} \to P_n$, $f(A) = A^{T}A$, is prope
r; i.e. $f^{-1}(K)$ is compact for every compact $K \subseteq P_n$.

---

**Setup.** Identify the space of $r \times n$ real matrices with $\mathbb{R}^{rn
}$ (Frobenius norm topology) and $P_n$ with a subset of $\mathbb{R}^{n(n+1)/2}$
(entrywise topology). Both are finite-dimensional, so compact = closed and bound
ed (Heine–Borel). The map $f$ is continuous since each entry of $A^{T}A$ is a po
lynomial in the entries of $A$.

---

**Step 1: $f^{-1}(K)$ is closed.**

$P_n$ (the PSD cone) is closed in $\mathbb{R}^{n(n+1)/2}$: it is the intersectio
n over all $v \in \mathbb{R}^n$ of the closed sets $\{B : v^{T}Bv \ge 0\}$. Henc
e $P_n$ is Hausdorff and any compact $K \subseteq P_n$ is compact in $\mathbb{R}
^{n(n+1)/2}$, therefore closed there. Since $f \colon \mathbb{R}^{rn} \to \mathb
b{R}^{n(n+1)/2}$ is continuous, $f^{-1}(K)$ is closed in $\mathbb{R}^{rn}$.

---

**Step 2: $f^{-1}(K)$ is bounded.**

Since $K$ is compact in $\mathbb{R}^{n(n+1)/2}$, it is bounded, so there exists
$M > 0$ with
$$\|B\|_{\mathrm{op}} \le M \quad \text{for all } B \in K,$$
using equivalence of all norms in finite dimensions.

For any $A \in f^{-1}(K)$, set $B = A^{T}A \in K$. Using the operator norm ident
ity
$$\|A^{T}A\|_{\mathrm{op}} = \sup_{\|x\|=1} x^{T}A^{T}Ax = \sup_{\|x\|=1}\|Ax\|^
{2} = \|A\|_{\mathrm{op}}^{2},$$
we obtain $\|A\|_{\mathrm{op}}^{2} \le M$, i.e. $\|A\|_{\mathrm{op}} \le \sqrt{M
}$.

Now bound the Frobenius norm. Writing $A$ row-wise, $A = (a_1;\dots;a_r)$ with $
a_i \in \mathbb{R}^n$,
$$\|A\|_{F}^{2} = \sum_{i=1}^{r}\|a_i\|^{2} \le \sum_{i=1}^{r}\|A\|_{\mathrm{op}
}^{2} = r\,\|A\|_{\mathrm{op}}^{2} \le rM.$$
Thus $\|A\|_{F} \le \sqrt{rM}$ for every $A \in f^{-1}(K)$, so $f^{-1}(K)$ is bo
unded in $\mathbb{R}^{rn}$.

---

**Step 3: Conclusion.**

$f^{-1}(K)$ is closed and bounded in $\mathbb{R}^{rn}$, hence compact by Heine–B
orel. This holds for every compact $K \subseteq P_n$, so $f$ is proper. $\blacks
quare$
