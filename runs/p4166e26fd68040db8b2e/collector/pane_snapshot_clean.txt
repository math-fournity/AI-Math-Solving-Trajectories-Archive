## Solution

**Problem restatement.** For each non-negative integer $n$, batch $n$ produces $
2^n$ units. Crates hold $1000$ units; the residual bin receives $2^n \bmod 1000$
. Let $R$ be the set of all distinct residues $2^n \bmod 1000$ over all $n \ge 0
$, and $S = \sum_{r \in R} r$. Find $S \bmod 1000$.

**Step 1: Split by divisibility by 8.**

Since $1000 = 8 \cdot 125$ with $\gcd(8,125)=1$, use CRT: $2^n \bmod 1000 \leftr
ightarrow (2^n \bmod 8,\; 2^n \bmod 125)$.

- $n=0$: residue $1$. $n=1$: residue $2$. $n=2$: residue $4$.
- $n \ge 3$: $8 \mid 2^n$, so $2^n \bmod 8 = 0$.

The residues $1,2,4$ are not divisible by $8$, so they are disjoint from all $n\
ge 3$ residues.

**Step 2: Characterize the $n \ge 3$ residues.**

The order of $2$ modulo $125$: $\varphi(125)=100$, and
$$2^{50} \equiv -1 \pmod{125}, \quad 2^{100}\equiv 1 \pmod{125},$$
so the order is exactly $100 = \varphi(125)$. Hence $\langle 2\rangle = (\mathbb
{Z}/125\mathbb{Z})^*$, i.e. $\{2^k \bmod 125 : k=0,\dots,99\}$ is the full group
 of units mod $125$.

As $n$ ranges over $\{3,4,5,\dots\}$, the exponent covers all residues mod $100$
, so $2^n \bmod 125$ ranges over all units mod $125$. Therefore
$$\{2^n \bmod 1000 : n\ge 3\} = \{x \in \{0,\dots,999\} : 8\mid x \text{ and } \
gcd(x,125)=1\}.$$
Since $\gcd(x,125)=1 \iff 5\nmid x$, this is the set of multiples of $8$ in $\{0
,\dots,999\}$ not divisible by $5$. Count: $125 - 25 = 100$. ✓

**Step 3: Compute the sum.**

Sum of all multiples of $8$ from $0$ to $992$:
$$8\cdot(0+1+\cdots+124) = 8\cdot\frac{124\cdot125}{2} = 8\cdot 7750 = 62000.$$

Sum of all multiples of $40$ from $0$ to $960$ (those divisible by both $8$ and
$5$):
$$40\cdot(0+1+\cdots+24) = 40\cdot\frac{24\cdot25}{2} = 40\cdot 300 = 12000.$$

Sum of $n\ge 3$ residues $= 62000 - 12000 = 50000$.

**Step 4: Total sum and final answer.**

$$S = 1 + 2 + 4 + 50000 = 50007.$$

$$S \bmod 1000 = 50007 \bmod 1000 = \boxed{7}.$$
