After folding, the sheet has dimensions $2^{-i} \times r \cdot 2^{-j}$. In **ori

The area ratio is:
$$f = \frac{r \cdot 2^{-(i+j)}}{mn} = \frac{2^s}{2^{-x} \cdot 2^{-y} \cdot 2^K}

## Key Simplification

Define $\alpha(t) = \lceil t \rceil - t \in [0,1)$. Then:
$$s+x+y - K_1 = -\alpha(x) - \alpha(s+y), \quad s+x+y - K_2 = -\alpha(y) - \alph
a(s+x).$$

Since $x,y$ are continuous, $\alpha(t) = 1 - \{t\}$ a.s. Setting $U = \{x\}$, $V

$$f = 2^{-2 + \max\!\big(U + \{\sigma+V\},\; V + \{\sigma+U\}\big)}.$$

## Computing $E[f]$

The fractional part $U = \{x\}$ has density $g(u) = 2^{1-u}\ln 2$ on $[0,1)$. Ke

$$E[2^U] = \int_0^1 2^u \cdot 2^{1-u}\ln 2\, du = 2\ln 2, \qquad E[2^U \mathbf{1

Using independence of $U,V$:

$$E[f] = 2^{-2}\Big(2^\sigma E[2^{U+V}] - 2^{\sigma-1} E[2^{U+V}\mathbf{1}_{U,V

$$= 2^{-2}\Big(2^\sigma \cdot 4(\ln 2)^2 - 2^{\sigma-1} \cdot 4\sigma^2(\ln 2)^2

## Optimization

$$\phi'(\sigma) = 2^\sigma\!\left[\ln 2\!\left(1 - \tfrac{\sigma^2}{2}\right) -
\sigma\right] = 0.$$

This gives the quadratic $\frac{\ln 2}{2}\sigma^2 + \sigma - \ln 2 = 0$, with po

$$\sigma^* = \frac{\sqrt{1 + 2(\ln 2)^2} - 1}{\ln 2} \approx 0.578.$$

**Verification of maximum:** $\phi(0) = 1$, $\phi(1^-) = 1$, and $\phi(\sigma^*)
