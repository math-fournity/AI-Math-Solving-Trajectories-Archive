## Solution

**Setup.** Place $A$ at the origin, $B = (20, 0)$, and $C = (18\cos\alpha,\, 18\
sin\alpha)$, where $\alpha = \angle BAC$. Then
$$F = (8, 0), \qquad E = (8\cos\alpha,\, 8\sin\alpha),$$
since $AF = AE = 8$.

**Finding $G$.** Parametrize line $BE$ as $B + t(E - B)$ and line $CF$ as $C + s
(F - C)$. Equating the $y$-coordinates (with $\sin\alpha \neq 0$):
$$8t = 18(1 - s) \implies s = 1 - \tfrac{4t}{9}.$$
Substituting into the $x$-coordinate equation:
$$20 + t(8\cos\alpha - 20) = 18\cos\alpha + \left(1 - \tfrac{4t}{9}\right)(8 - 1
8\cos\alpha) = 8 - \tfrac{4t}{9}(8 - 18\cos\alpha).$$
The $\cos\alpha$ terms cancel neatly:
$$12 + t\!\left(-20 + \tfrac{32}{9}\right) = 0 \implies 12 - \tfrac{148}{9}\,t =
 0 \implies t = \frac{27}{37}.$$
So $G$ is independent of $\alpha$ in this parametrization:
$$G = \left(\frac{200 + 216\cos\alpha}{37},\; \frac{216\sin\alpha}{37}\right).$$

**Cyclicity condition.** The circumcircle of $\triangle AEF$ (passing through th
e origin) has equation $x^2 + y^2 + Dx + Ey = 0$. From $F = (8,0)$: $D = -8$. Fr
om $E = (8\cos\alpha, 8\sin\alpha)$:
$$64 - 64\cos\alpha + 8E\sin\alpha = 0 \implies E = \frac{8(\cos\alpha - 1)}{\si
n\alpha}.$$
Thus the circle is
$$x^2 + y^2 - 8x + \frac{8(\cos\alpha - 1)}{\sin\alpha}\,y = 0.$$

Substituting $G = (g_x, g_y)$ with $g_x = \frac{200 + 216c}{37}$, $g_y = \frac{2
16s}{37}$ (where $c = \cos\alpha$, $s = \sin\alpha$), and multiplying through by
 $37^2 = 1369$:

- $g_x^2 + g_y^2$: $\frac{(200+216c)^2 + (216s)^2}{1369} = \frac{86656 + 86400c}
{1369}$
- $-8g_x$: $\frac{-59200 - 63936c}{1369}$
- $\frac{8(c-1)}{s}\,g_y = \frac{8(c-1)}{s}\cdot\frac{216s}{37} = \frac{63936c -
 63936}{1369}$

Summing the numerators:
$$(86656 + 86400c) + (-59200 - 63936c) + (63936c - 63936) = -36480 + 86400c.$$
Setting this to zero:
$$86400c = 36480 \implies c = \cos\alpha = \frac{19}{45}.$$

**Computing $BC$.** By the Law of Cosines:
$$BC^2 = AB^2 + AC^2 - 2\cdot AB\cdot AC\cdot\cos\alpha = 400 + 324 - 2\cdot 20\
cdot 18\cdot\frac{19}{45}.$$
$$BC^2 = 724 - 720\cdot\frac{19}{45} = 724 - 16\cdot 19 = 724 - 304 = 420.$$
$$BC = \sqrt{420} = \sqrt{4\cdot 105} = 2\sqrt{105}.$$

Since $105 = 3\cdot 5\cdot 7$ is squarefree, we have $m = 2$ and $n = 105$.

$$100m + n = 100(2) + 105 = \boxed{305}.$$
