## Setup and Reformulation

A domino is an ordered pair $(i,j)$ with $i \neq j$, $1 \le i,j \le 40$. A prope
r sequence is a directed trail $v_0 \to v_1 \to \cdots \to v_k$ where:
1. Consecutive edges link up (first coord of next = second coord of prev), and
2. For each unordered pair $\{i,j\}$, at most one of $(i,j)$, $(j,i)$ appears.

Condition 2 means each **unordered** edge $\{i,j\}$ is used at most once (in whi
chever direction we traverse it). So the problem reduces to:

**Find the longest trail in $K_{40}$ (the complete graph on 40 vertices) using e
ach edge at most once, where we orient each traversed edge in its direction of t
ravel.**

The total number of unordered edges is $\binom{40}{2} = 780$.

## Upper Bound via Parity

In $K_{40}$, every vertex has degree $39$ (odd). A trail that uses a set of edge
s corresponds to an Eulerian trail in the subgraph formed by those edges. For an
 Eulerian trail to exist, the subgraph must be connected and have **at most 2 ve
rtices of odd degree**.

We start with all 40 vertices having odd degree. Removing an edge $\{i,j\}$ from
 our chosen edge set flips the parity of both $i$ and $j$ (each loses one incide
nt edge). Each removed edge fixes exactly 2 odd vertices.

- **0 odd vertices (Eulerian circuit):** Must flip all 40 → need at least 20 edg
es forming a perfect matching. Remaining: $780 - 20 = 760$.
- **2 odd vertices (Eulerian trail):** Must flip 38, leaving 2 odd → need at lea
st 19 edges forming a matching covering 38 vertices. Remaining: $780 - 19 = 761$
.

So any proper sequence uses at most $780 - 19 = 761$ edges.

## Achieving the Bound

Remove 19 edges forming a matching that covers 38 of the 40 vertices (e.g., remo
ve $\{1,2\}, \{3,4\}, \ldots, \{37,38\}$, leaving vertices 39 and 40 unmatched).

After removal:
- The 38 matched vertices each have degree $39 - 1 = 38$ (even).
- Vertices 39 and 40 each have degree $39$ (odd).

The remaining subgraph has exactly **2 odd-degree vertices** (39 and 40) and is
**connected** (removing 19 edges from the dense $K_{40}$ cannot disconnect it —
each vertex still has degree $\geq 38$, far above the connectivity threshold).

By Euler's theorem, a connected graph with exactly 2 odd vertices possesses an *
*Eulerian trail** from one odd vertex to the other. This trail uses all $780 - 1
9 = 761$ remaining edges.

Traversing this trail, we orient each edge in the direction of travel. Since eac
h edge is used exactly once, each unordered pair $\{i,j\}$ receives exactly one
orientation — satisfying condition 2. Consecutive edges share endpoints by const
ruction — satisfying condition 1.

## Conclusion

The longest proper sequence of dominos from $D_{40}$ has length:

$$\boxed{761}$$
