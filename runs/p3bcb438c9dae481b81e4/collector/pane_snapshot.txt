## Solution

I need to find all four-digit numbers $\overline{abcd}$ ($a \neq 0$) such that:

$$\overline{abcd} = (a+b+c+d)\cdot(a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2)^2$$

Let $s = a+b+c+d$ (digit sum) and $q = a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2$ (sum of digit squares).
The condition becomes:

$$N = s \cdot q^2$$

where $N = 1000a+100b+10c+d$ is a four-digit number.

### Bounding the search

Since $1000 \le N \le 9999$, we have $q^2 \in [1000/s,\; 9999/s]$, so $q \in [\l
ceil\sqrt{1000/s}\rceil,\; \lfloor\sqrt{9999/s}\rfloor]$.

The digit sum satisfies $1 \le s \le 36$. For each $s$, I enumerate all integer
$q$ in the valid range, compute $N = s \cdot q^2$, then verify that (1) $N$ is f
our-digit, (2) the digits of $N$ sum to $s$, and (3) the sum of squares of the d
igits equals $q$.

### Systematic enumeration

For $s = 1, 2, 3$: The maximum possible $q$ (concentrating the digit sum in one
digit) gives $N \le s^5 \le 243$, which is not four-digit. No solutions.

For each $s$ from 4 to 36, I computed $N = s \cdot q^2$ for every valid $q$, ext
racted the digits, and checked both the digit-sum and sum-of-squares conditions.
 The complete check yields exactly two matches:

**$s = 6$, $q = 20$:** $N = 6 \times 400 = 2400$.
- Digits: $2, 4, 0, 0$. Digit sum: $2+4+0+0 = 6 = s$. ✓
- Sum of squares: $4+16+0+0 = 20 = q$. ✓

**$s = 7$, $q = 17$:** $N = 7 \times 289 = 2023$.
- Digits: $2, 0, 2, 3$. Digit sum: $2+0+2+3 = 7 = s$. ✓
- Sum of squares: $4+0+4+9 = 17 = q$. ✓

All other values of $s$ (from 4 to 36, covering every possible four-digit candid
ate) produce no number satisfying both conditions simultaneously. In particular,
 many candidates pass the digit-sum test but fail the sum-of-squares test (or vi
ce versa); the two conditions together are highly restrictive.

### Verification

- **2400**: $(2+4+0+0)\cdot(2^2+4^2+0^2+0^2)^2 = 6 \cdot 20^2 = 6 \cdot 400 = 24
00.$ ✓
- **2023**: $(2+0+2+3)\cdot(2^2+0^2+2^2+3^2)^2 = 7 \cdot 17^2 = 7 \cdot 289 = 20
23.$ ✓

### Answer

The sum of all four-digit natural numbers satisfying the condition is:

$$2400 + 2023 = \boxed{4423}$$
