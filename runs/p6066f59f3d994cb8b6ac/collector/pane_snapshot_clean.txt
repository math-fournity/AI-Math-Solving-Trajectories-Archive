Let $m^*$ denote the Lebesgue outer measure on $\mathbb{R}^2$. We claim $m^*(F)=
0$.

**Step 1. Each coordinate line has outer measure $0$.**

Fix $n\in\mathbb{Z}$ and consider the vertical line $V_n=\{n\}\times\mathbb{R}$.
 Decompose it into unit segments:
$$V_n=\bigcup_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(\{n\}\times[k,k+1]\bigr).$$
For each $k\in\mathbb{Z}$ and any $\varepsilon>0$, the segment $\{n\}\times[k,k+
1]$ is contained in the closed rectangle
$$R_{n,k}:=\left[n-\frac{\delta_{n,k}}{2},\,n+\frac{\delta_{n,k}}{2}\right]\time
s[k,k+1],\qquad \delta_{n,k}:=\frac{\varepsilon}{2^{|n|+|k|+2}},$$
whose area (Lebesgue measure) is $\delta_{n,k}\cdot 1=\varepsilon/2^{|n|+|k|+2}$
.

Summing over all $k\in\mathbb{Z}$:
$$\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\frac{\varepsilon}{2^{|n|+|k|+2}}=\frac{\varepsilon}{2^{|
n|+2}}\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\frac{1}{2^{|k|}}=\frac{\varepsilon}{2^{|n|+2}}\cdot
3=\frac{3\varepsilon}{2^{|n|+2}}.$$
Hence $m^*(V_n)\le 3\varepsilon/2^{|n|+2}$. Since $\varepsilon>0$ is arbitrary,
$m^*(V_n)=0$.

By the identical argument (swapping coordinates), each horizontal line $H_m=\mat
hbb{R}\times\{m\}$ also satisfies $m^*(H_m)=0$.

**Step 2. $F$ is a countable union of outer-measure-zero sets.**

By definition,
$$F=\Bigl(\bigcup_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}V_n\Bigr)\cup\Bigl(\bigcup_{m\in\mathbb{Z}}H_m
\Bigr),$$
which is a countable union (indexed by $\mathbb{Z}\sqcup\mathbb{Z}$, a countable
 set) of sets each having outer measure $0$.

**Step 3. Countable subadditivity gives the result.**

By countable subadditivity of the outer measure,
$$m^*(F)\le\sum_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}m^*(V_n)+\sum_{m\in\mathbb{Z}}m^*(H_m)=\sum_{n\i
n\mathbb{Z}}0+\sum_{m\in\mathbb{Z}}0=0.$$

Since outer measure is nonnegative, $m^*(F)\ge 0$. Therefore
$$\boxed{m^*(F)=0}.$$
