## Proof

Let $T^2$ act on the symplectic 4-manifold $(M,\omega)$ in a Hamiltonian fashion
 with moment map $\mu = (\mu_1,\mu_2): M \to \mathbb{R}^2$. By definition, writi
ng $X_1, X_2$ for the fundamental vector fields of the two circle factors of $T^
2$, the components of the moment map satisfy
$$d\mu_i = \iota_{X_i}\omega, \qquad i = 1,2,$$
equivalently $\mu$ is $T^2$-equivariant and $\iota_{X_i}\omega = d\mu_i$.

The homomorphism $\rho: S^1 \to T^2$ given by $\theta \mapsto (2\theta, 3\theta)
$ induces a circle action on $M$ by $g \cdot x := \rho(g)\cdot x$. Its infinites
imal generator is obtained by differentiating $\rho$ at the identity. The induce
d Lie algebra homomorphism $\rho_*: \mathrm{Lie}(S^1) \cong \mathbb{R} \to \math
rm{Lie}(T^2)\cong \mathbb{R}^2$ sends $t \mapsto (2t, 3t)$. Hence the fundamenta
l vector field $X$ of the induced circle action is
$$X = 2X_1 + 3X_2.$$

A moment map $\phi: M \to \mathbb{R}$ for this circle action must satisfy $\iota
_X\omega = d\phi$. Using linearity of the interior product,
$$d\phi = \iota_X\omega = \iota_{2X_1 + 3X_2}\omega = 2\,\iota_{X_1}\omega + 3\,
\iota_{X_2}\omega = 2\,d\mu_1 + 3\,d\mu_2 = d(2\mu_1 + 3\mu_2).$$

Therefore $\phi$ and $2\mu_1 + 3\mu_2$ differ by a constant. Equivariance fixes
this constant: the $S^1$-action on $\mathbb{R}$ is trivial (a moment map for a c
ircle action is equivariant with respect to the trivial coadjoint action on $\ma
thbb{R}$), and $\mu$ is $T^2$-equivariant, so $2\mu_1 + 3\mu_2$ is invariant und
er the induced $S^1$-action, matching the required equivariance. Taking the cano
nical choice (constant $= 0$),

$$\boxed{\phi = 2\mu_1 + 3\mu_2.}$$
