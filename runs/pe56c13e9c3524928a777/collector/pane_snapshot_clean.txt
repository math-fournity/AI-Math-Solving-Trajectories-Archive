Fact 2 (Affine morphisms are relative Specs). A morphism $\mu: W \to X$ is

\cong \operatorname{Spec}(\mu\mathcal{O}_W)$ over $X$.

Fact 3 (Pushforward of structure sheaf). For the structure morphism $\pi:
\operatorname{Spec}(\mathcal{A}) \to X$, we have $\pi_*\mathcal{O}
_{\operatorname{Spec}(\mathcal{A})} = \mathcal{A}$ as $\mathcal{O}_X$-algebras.
