## Solution

**Parametrize the contour.** Let $z = Re^{i\theta}$, so $|dz| = R\,d\theta$ and
$|z|=R$. Then

$$\int_{|z|=R}\left|\frac{\sin z}{z}\right||dz| = \int_0^{2\pi}\frac{|\sin(Re^{i
\theta})|}{R}\cdot R\,d\theta = \int_0^{2\pi}|\sin(Re^{i\theta})|\,d\theta.$$

**Key identity for complex sine.** For $z = x+iy$,

$$\sin z = \sin x\cosh y + i\cos x\sinh y,$$

so

$$|\sin z|^2 = \sin^2 x\cosh^2 y + \cos^2 x\sinh^2 y = \sin^2 x + \sinh^2 y,$$

using $\cosh^2 y = 1+\sinh^2 y$. In particular,

$$|\sin z| \geq |\sinh y|.$$

**Lower bound on the integral.** On the circle, $y = R\sin\theta$. For $\theta\i
n[\pi/4,\,3\pi/4]$, we have $\sin\theta\geq 1/\sqrt{2}$, hence $y\geq R/\sqrt{2}
$. Therefore

$$|\sin(Re^{i\theta})| \geq \sinh\!\left(\frac{R}{\sqrt{2}}\right), \qquad \thet
a\in\left[\frac{\pi}{4},\,\frac{3\pi}{4}\right].$$

Integrating over this arc (length $\pi/2$ in $\theta$):

$$\int_0^{2\pi}|\sin(Re^{i\theta})|\,d\theta \;\geq\; \int_{\pi/4}^{3\pi/4}\sinh
\!\left(\frac{R}{\sqrt{2}}\right)d\theta \;=\; \frac{\pi}{2}\,\sinh\!\left(\frac
{R}{\sqrt{2}}\right).$$

**Taking the limit.** Since $\sinh(t)\sim \tfrac{1}{2}e^t\to\infty$ as $t\to\inf
ty$,

$$\frac{\pi}{2}\,\sinh\!\left(\frac{R}{\sqrt{2}}\right)\xrightarrow{R\to\infty}+
\infty.$$

The integrand is non-negative, so the integral is bounded below by a quantity te
nding to $+\infty$. Hence

$$\lim_{R\to\infty}\int_{|z|=R}\left|\frac{\sin z}{z}\right||dz| = +\infty.$$
