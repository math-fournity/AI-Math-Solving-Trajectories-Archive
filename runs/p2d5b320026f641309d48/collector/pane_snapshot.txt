## Solution

**Answer-leak check:** The problem text contains only the hint assuming $\lambda
>0$ and the product-solution form. No solution, answer, or proof sketch is leake
d. Proceeding.

---

We solve the wave equation in the sector
$$\Omega = \{(r,\theta): 0<r<a,\;0<\theta<\pi/4\}$$
with boundary conditions $u(a,\theta,t)=0$, $u(r,0,t)=0$, $u(r,\pi/4,t)=0$, and
initial conditions $u(r,\theta,0)=\alpha(r,\theta)$, $u_t(r,\theta,0)=0$.

### 1. Separation of variables

Assume $u(r,\theta,t)=f(r)\,g(\theta)\,h(t)$. Substituting into
$$u_{tt}=c^{2}\Big[\tfrac{1}{r}(rf_r)_r+\tfrac{1}{r^{2}}u_{\theta\theta}\Big]$$
and dividing by $fgh$ gives
$$\frac{h''}{c^{2}h}=\frac{(rf')'}{rf}+\frac{g''}{r^{2}g}.$$

The left side depends only on $t$, the right only on $(r,\theta)$, so both equal
 $-\lambda$ (with $\lambda>0$ as permitted by the hint):

$$h''+c^{2}\lambda\,h=0. \tag{1}$$

Multiplying the spatial part by $r^{2}$ and separating again,
$$\frac{r(rf')'}{f}+r^{2}\lambda=-\frac{g''}{g}=\mu. \tag{2}$$

### 2. Angular eigenvalue problem

$$g''+\mu\,g=0,\qquad g(0)=0,\quad g(\pi/4)=0.$$

Non-trivial solutions require $\mu>0$; write $\mu=\nu^{2}$. Then $g(\theta)=\sin
(\nu\theta)$, and $g(\pi/4)=0$ forces $\nu\pi/4=n\pi$, i.e. $\nu=4n$, $n=1,2,3,\
dots$. Hence
$$\boxed{\mu_n = 16n^{2},\qquad g_n(\theta)=\sin(4n\theta),\quad n=1,2,\dots}$$

### 3. Radial eigenvalue problem

With $\mu=16n^{2}$, equation (2) becomes
$$r^{2}f''+rf'+\big(\lambda r^{2}-16n^{2}\big)f=0,\qquad f(a)=0,\quad f(0)\text{
 finite}.$$

This is Bessel's equation of order $\nu=4n$. The bounded solution at $r=0$ is
$$f(r)=J_{4n}\!\big(\sqrt{\lambda}\,r\big).$$
The boundary condition $f(a)=0$ requires
$$J_{4n}\!\big(\sqrt{\lambda}\,a\big)=0\;\Longrightarrow\;\sqrt{\lambda}\,a=z_{4
n,m},$$
where $z_{4n,m}$ is the $m$-th positive zero of $J_{4n}$ ($m=1,2,\dots$). Thus
$$\boxed{\lambda_{n,m}=\Big(\frac{z_{4n,m}}{a}\Big)^{2},\qquad f_{n,m}(r)=J_{4n}
\!\Big(\frac{z_{4n,m}\,r}{a}\Big).}$$

### 4. Time equation

For each pair $(n,m)$, equation (1) gives
$$h''+c^{2}\lambda_{n,m}\,h=0,\qquad h'(0)=0,$$
the last condition coming from $u_t(r,\theta,0)=0$. Hence
$$h_{n,m}(t)=\cos\!\Big(\frac{c\,z_{4n,m}}{a}\,t\Big).$$

### 5. Series solution

Superposing all product solutions,
$$\boxed{u(r,\theta,t)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}A_{nm}\,J_{4n}\!\Bi
g(\frac{z_{4n,m}\,r}{a}\Big)\sin(4n\theta)\cos\!\Big(\frac{c\,z_{4n,m}}{a}\,t\Bi
g).} \tag{3}$$

### 6. Determining the coefficients from $\alpha(r,\theta)$

Setting $t=0$ in (3):
$$\alpha(r,\theta)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}A_{nm}\,J_{4n}\!\Big(\f
rac{z_{4n,m}\,r}{a}\Big)\sin(4n\theta).$$

The eigenfunctions are orthogonal on $\Omega$ with weight $r$:

* **Angular:** $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\pi/4}\sin(4n\theta)\sin(4n'\theta)\,d\th
eta=\frac{\pi}{8}\,\delta_{nn'}.$

* **Radial (Bessel orthogonality):** using $J_{\nu}'(z_{\nu,m})=-J_{\nu+1}(z_{\n
u,m})$ at the zeros,
$$\int_{0}^{a}J_{4n}\!\Big(\frac{z_{4n,m}r}{a}\Big)J_{4n}\!\Big(\frac{z_{4n,m'}r
}{a}\Big)\,r\,dr=\frac{a^{2}}{2}\,J_{4n+1}(z_{4n,m})^{2}\,\delta_{mm'}.$$

Multiplying $\alpha$ by $J_{4n}(z_{4n,m}r/a)\sin(4n\theta)\,r$ and integrating o
ver $\Omega$, orthogonality isolates $A_{nm}$:

$$\boxed{A_{nm}=\frac{8}{\pi a^{2}\,J_{4n+1}(z_{4n,m})^{2}}\int_{0}^{\pi/4}\!\!\
int_{0}^{a}\alpha(r,\theta)\,J_{4n}\!\Big(\frac{z_{4n,m}\,r}{a}\Big)\sin(4n\thet
a)\;r\,dr\,d\theta.} \tag{4}$$

### 7. Verification

* **PDE:** Each term $J_{4n}(z_{4n,m}r/a)\sin(4n\theta)\cos(cz_{4n,m}t/a)$ satis
fies the wave equation because $J_{4n}$ solves Bessel's equation of order $4n$ w
ith parameter $\lambda_{n,m}$, $\sin(4n\theta)$ solves $g''+16n^{2}g=0$, and $\c
os(cz_{4n,m}t/a)$ solves $h''+c^{2}\lambda_{n,m}h=0$.
* **Boundary $r=a$:** $J_{4n}(z_{4n,m})=0$ by definition of the zero.
* **Boundary $\theta=0$:** $\sin(0)=0$.
* **Boundary $\theta=\pi/4$:** $\sin(4n\cdot\pi/4)=\sin(n\pi)=0$.
* **Initial velocity $u_t(r,\theta,0)=0$:** $\frac{d}{dt}\cos(cz_{4n,m}t/a)\big|
_{t=0}=0$ for every term.
* **Initial displacement:** Equation (4) is exactly the double Fourier–Bessel ex
pansion of $\alpha$ in the orthogonal system $\{J_{4n}(z_{4n,m}r/a)\sin(4n\theta
)\}$, so (3) reproduces $\alpha$ at $t=0$ (in the $L^{2}(\Omega,r\,dr\,d\theta)$
 sense, and pointwise under standard regularity assumptions on $\alpha$).

All conditions are satisfied. $\blacksquare$
