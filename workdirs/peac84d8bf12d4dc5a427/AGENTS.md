# Problem

A specialized circular light projection hub is located at the center (0,0) of a grid-based architectural floor plan. This hub emits 180 distinct narrow laser beams across the floor. These beams are spaced at perfectly regular intervals of exactly 1 degree from one another, with the first beam pointing due East (along the positive x-axis) and the remaining beams filling the full 360-degree rotation (such that the second beam points at 1 degree, the third at 2 degrees, and so on, with the 91st beam pointing due North along the positive y-axis).

A long, straight decorative glass partition is installed on the floor, following the path of the linear equation $y = 100 - x$. 

Each of the 180 laser beams that is not parallel to the partition strikes the glass, creating a small dot of light at the point of impact. 

Calculate the sum of the x-coordinates of all the points where these laser beams intersect the glass partition.
