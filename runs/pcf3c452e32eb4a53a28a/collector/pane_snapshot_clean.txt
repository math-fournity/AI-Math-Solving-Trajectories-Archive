## Proof

We determine the codimension of
$$X = \{A \in M_{m\times n}(\mathbb{R}) : \operatorname{rk}(A) = r\}$$
as a submanifold of $\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}\cong \mathbb{R}^{mn}$.

**Step 1. Reduction to a canonical point.**

The group $G = GL_m(\mathbb{R})\times GL_n(\mathbb{R})$ acts on $M_{m\times n}(\
mathbb{R})$ by
$$(P,Q)\cdot A = PAQ^{-1},$$
and this action preserves rank. It acts transitively on each fixed-rank stratum:
 every rank-$r$ matrix can be brought to the canonical form
$$J_r = \begin{pmatrix} I_r & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$$
by suitable invertible $P,Q$. Since $G$ acts by diffeomorphisms of $\mathbb{R}^{
m\times n}$, the local structure (dimension, codimension, smoothness) of $X$ is
the same at every point. It suffices to analyze $X$ near $J_r$.

**Step 2. Local normal form near $J_r$.**

Write every matrix near $J_r$ in block form
$$A = \begin{pmatrix} B & C \\ D & E \end{pmatrix},$$
where $B$ is $r\times r$, $C$ is $r\times (n-r)$, $D$ is $(m-r)\times r$, and $E
$ is $(m-r)\times (n-r)$. Near $J_r$ we have $B$ close to $I_r$, hence $B$ inver
tible.

For such $A$, using the invertible block $B$, the Schur complement gives
$$\operatorname{rk}(A) = r \iff E - DB^{-1}C = 0 \iff E = DB^{-1}C.$$
Indeed, multiplying $A$ on the left by the invertible matrix $\begin{pmatrix} I_
r & 0 \\ -DB^{-1} & I_{m-r}\end{pmatrix}$ yields
$$\begin{pmatrix} B & C \\ 0 & E - DB^{-1}C \end{pmatrix},$$
which has rank $r$ exactly when the bottom-right block vanishes.

**Step 3. $X$ is a smooth embedded submanifold.**

The condition $E = DB^{-1}C$ exhibits $X\cap U$ (for a small neighborhood $U$ of
 $J_r$) as the graph of the smooth map
$$(B,C,D) \mapsto DB^{-1}C,$$
defined on the open set $\{B\in GL_r\}\times \mathbb{R}^{r(n-r)}\times \mathbb{R
}^{(m-r)r}$. A graph of a smooth function is an embedded submanifold, and the fr
ee parameters $(B,C,D)$ give a global chart of $X\cap U$. By Step 1 the same hol
ds near every point of $X$, so $X$ is a smooth embedded submanifold of $\mathbb{
R}^{m\times n}$.

**Step 4. Dimension and codimension.**

From the parametrization in Step 3, the dimension of $X$ equals the number of fr
ee parameters:
$$\dim X = \underbrace{r^2}_{B} + \underbrace{r(n-r)}_{C} + \underbrace{(m-r)r}_
{D} = r^2 + r(n-r) + r(m-r) = r(m+n-r).$$
The ambient space has dimension $mn$, so the codimension is
$$\operatorname{codim} X = mn - r(m+n-r) = mn - rm - rn + r^2 = (m-r)(n-r).$$

**Conclusion.**

The set $X = \{A\in M_{m\times n}(\mathbb{R}) : \operatorname{rk}(A)=r\}$ is a s
mooth embedded submanifold of $\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}$ of codimension

$$\boxed{(m-r)(n-r)}.$$
