# Problem

In a specialized laboratory, a security vault is controlled by 2005 distinct biometric sensors, each calibrated to a unique, secret frequency (a real number). To recalibrate the system, a technician must identify the exact frequency assigned to every individual sensor.

The technician possesses a multi-scanner that can analyze these sensors. However, the device has a specific constraint: it can only be activated when pointed at exactly three sensors at once.
