< \infty$, and (after subsequence) $f_i \rightharpoonup f$ weakly in $W^{1,p}(\O
mega)$. The condition is $2p > n$. We must show $\|f_i - f\|_{L^{2p}} \to 0$.

We split into three cases.

---

**Case 1: $p < n$.**

The Sobolev conjugate exponent is $p^* = \frac{np}{n-p}$. The Sobolev embedding
theorem gives the continuous embedding $W^{1,p}(\Omega) \hookrightarrow L^{p^*}(
\Omega)$.

We check whether $2p < p^*$:
$$2p < \frac{np}{n-p} \iff 2(n-p) < n \iff 2p > n.$$
This is exactly our hypothesis, so $2p < p^*$ **strictly**.

By the **Rellich–Kondrachov theorem**, the embedding
$$W^{1,p}(\Omega) \hookrightarrow\hookrightarrow L^q(\Omega)$$
is **compact** for every $1 \leq q < p^*$. Since $2p < p^*$, taking $q = 2p$ yie
lds a compact embedding $W^{1,p}(\Omega) \hookrightarrow\hookrightarrow L^{2p}(\
Omega)$.

Compactness of the embedding means: every bounded sequence in $W^{1,p}$ has a su

**Case 2: $p = n$.**

2p > n$ ensures that $2p$ lies strictly below the critical exponent for the Sobo

Therefore, **yes**, $f_i \to f$ in $L^{2p}$.
