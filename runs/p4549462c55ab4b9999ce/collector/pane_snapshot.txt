Looking at this problem, I need to find the probability that the non-reflex angl
e between the hour and minute hands is less than 360/11 degrees, for a uniformly
 random time in a 24-hour day.

## Setup

Since the clock repeats every 12 hours, a 24-hour day consists of two identical
12-hour cycles, so it suffices to work with $t \in [0, 12)$ (time in hours), uni
formly distributed.

- Hour hand angle from 12 o'clock: $H(t) = 30t \pmod{360}$
- Minute hand angle from 12 o'clock: $M(t) = 360\{t\}$, where $\{t\}$ is the fra
ctional part

The signed difference is $\Delta = H - M$. Writing $t = n + f$ with $n = \lfloor
 t \rfloor$ and $f \in [0,1)$:
$$\Delta = 30(n+f) - 360f = 30n - 330f = 360n - 330t.$$

## Reducing mod 360

Since $360n \equiv 0 \pmod{360}$:
$$\Delta \bmod 360 = (-330t) \bmod 360.$$

As $t$ ranges over $[0,12)$, the quantity $330t$ ranges over $[0, 3960) = [0, 11
\cdot 360)$, so $v = 330t \bmod 360$ completes **11 full sweeps** of $[0,360)$.
Therefore $v$ is uniform on $[0,360)$.

Then $u := \Delta \bmod 360 = (-v) \bmod 360$ is the reflection of $v$, which is
 also **uniform on $[0,360)$**.

## The non-reflex angle

The non-reflex angle is $\theta = \min(u,\; 360 - u) \in [0, 180]$.

We need $\theta < \dfrac{360}{11}$, i.e.
$$\min(u, 360-u) < \frac{360}{11} \iff u \in \left[0, \frac{360}{11}\right) \cup
 \left(\frac{3600}{11}, 360\right).$$

The total favorable measure is
$$\frac{360}{11} + \left(360 - \frac{3600}{11}\right) = \frac{360}{11} + \frac{3
60}{11} = \frac{720}{11}.$$

## Computing the probability

$$P = \frac{720/11}{360} = \frac{720}{3960} = \frac{2}{11}.$$

## Verification

Sanity check: the hands coincide 11 times per 12 hours (every $12/11$ hours). Ne
ar each coincidence, the non-reflex angle grows at the relative speed of $330$ d
eg/hr, so $\theta < 360/11$ holds for $|t - t_0| < \frac{360/11}{330} = \frac{12
}{121}$ hours on each side — a window of $\frac{24}{121}$ hours. Over 11 coincid
ences: $11 \cdot \frac{24}{121} = \frac{24}{11}$ hours out of 12, giving $\frac{
24/11}{12} = \frac{2}{11}$. ✓
