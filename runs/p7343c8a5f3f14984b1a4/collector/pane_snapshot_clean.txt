Looking at this problem, I need to find all complex solutions $(x, y)$ to the sy
stem:
- $x + 2y^2 = x^4$
- $y + 2x^2 = y^4$

then minimize $\text{Re}(x)$.

## Eliminating $y$

From the first equation: $y^2 = \frac{x^4 - x}{2}$.

From the second: $y = y^4 - 2x^2 = (y^2)^2 - 2x^2 = \frac{(x^4-x)^2}{4} - 2x^2$.

Substituting into $y^2 = \frac{x^4-x}{2}$:

$$\left(\frac{(x^4-x)^2}{4} - 2x^2\right)^2 = \frac{x^4-x}{2}$$

Let $u = x^4 - x$. Expanding: $u^4 - 16u^2 x^2 + 64x^4 - 8u = 0$.

Substituting $u = x^4 - x$ and expanding, then factoring out $x$:

$$x\left(x^{15} - 4x^{12} - 10x^9 + 28x^6 + 41x^3 + 8\right) = 0$$

## Substituting $t = x^3$

The degree-15 factor becomes a quintic in $t$:

$$t^5 - 4t^4 - 10t^3 + 28t^2 + 41t + 8 = 0$$

**Testing $t = -1$:** $-1 - 4 + 10 + 28 - 41 + 8 = 0$ ✓

Polynomial division gives $(t+1)(t^4 - 5t^3 - 5t^2 + 33t + 8)$.

**Factoring the quartic** as $(t^2 + at + b)(t^2 + ct + d)$ with $bd = 8$: tryin
g $b = -1, d = -8$ yields $a = -4, c = -1$ (checking: $ac + b + d = 4 - 9 = -5$
✓, $ad + bc = 32 + 1 = 33$ ✓).

$$(t+1)(t^2 - 4t - 1)(t^2 - t - 8) = 0$$

## Finding all roots

The full polynomial factors as $x(x^3+1)(x^6 - 4x^3 - 1)(x^6 - x^3 - 8) = 0$ (de
gree 16, matching Bézout's bound with no solutions at infinity — so these are **
all** solutions).

**Roots in $t = x^3$:**
- $t = -1$
- $t = 2 \pm \sqrt{5}$
- $t = \frac{1 \pm \sqrt{33}}{2}$

For each real $t$, the three cube roots are $t^{1/3}$, $t^{1/3}\omega$, $t^{1/3}
\omega^2$ where $\omega = e^{2\pi i/3}$, with real parts $t^{1/3}$ and $-t^{1/3}
/2$ (twice).

The minimum real part is $R = -\left(\frac{\sqrt{33}-1}{2}\right)^{1/3}$, achiev
