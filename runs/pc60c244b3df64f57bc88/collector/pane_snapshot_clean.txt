## Solution via Wolfe's Method

**Problem.** Minimize $f(x)=x_1^2-2x_1x_2+2x_2^2-2x_1-6x_2$ subject to $x_1+x_2\
le 2$, $-x_1+2x_2\le 2$, $x_1,x_2\ge 0$.

### 1. Convexity check

$H=\begin{pmatrix}2&-2\\-2&4\end{pmatrix}$: leading minors $2>0$, $\det H=8-4=4>
0$. So $H\succ 0$; $f$ is strictly convex and Wolfe's method applies.

### 2. KKT system (Wolfe form)

Write $\tfrac12 x^\top Hx+c^\top x$ with $c=(-2,-6)^\top$, $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1
\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}$, $b=(2,2)^\top$. Introduce slacks $u_1,u_2\ge0$ ($Ax+u=b$),
 multipliers $\lambda_1,\lambda_2\ge0$, and $\mu_1,\mu_2\ge0$ for $x\ge0$. Stati
onarity $Hx+c+A^\top\lambda-\mu=0$ gives, after adding artificial variables $v_1
,v_2\ge0$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
2x_1-2x_2+\lambda_1-\lambda_2-\mu_1+v_1&=2 \quad(1)\\
-2x_1+4x_2+\lambda_1+2\lambda_2-\mu_2+v_2&=6 \quad(2)
\end{aligned}
$$

Constraints: $x_1+x_2+u_1=2$, $-x_1+2x_2+u_2=2$.

**Complementarity (restricted-basis rule):** $\lambda_i$ and $u_i$ cannot both b
e basic; $\mu_j$ and $x_j$ cannot both be basic.

**Auxiliary objective:** minimize $w=v_1+v_2$.

### 3. Restricted simplex iterations

**Initial basis** $\{u_1,u_2,v_1,v_2\}=(2,2,2,6)$, $w=8$.
$w=8-2x_2-2\lambda_1-\lambda_2+\mu_1+\mu_2$ (and $x_1$ has reduced cost $0$).

Most negative reduced cost: $x_2$ ($-2$) and $\lambda_1$ ($-2$). But $\lambda_1$
 is blocked ($u_1$ basic). Enter $x_2$. Ratios: $u_1:2$, $u_2:1$, $v_2:3/2$ → $u
_2$ leaves.

**Iteration 1.** Basis $\{u_1,x_2,v_1,v_2\}=(1,1,4,2)$, $w=6$.
$w=6-x_1-2\lambda_1-\lambda_2+u_2+\mu_1+\mu_2$. Most negative admissible: $\lamb
da_1$ ($-2$) still blocked ($u_1$ basic); enter $x_1$ ($-1$). Ratios: $u_1:2/3$,
 $v_1:4$ → $u_1$ leaves.

**Iteration 2.** Basis $\{x_1,x_2,v_1,v_2\}=(2/3,4/3,10/3,2)$, $w=16/3$.
$w=\tfrac{16}{3}+\tfrac{2}{3}u_1+\tfrac{2}{3}u_2-2\lambda_1-\lambda_2+\mu_1+\mu_
2$. Now $u_1$ nonbasic, so $\lambda_1$ admissible. Enter $\lambda_1$ ($-2$). Rat
ios: $v_1:10/3$, $v_2:2$ → $v_2$ leaves.

**Iteration 3.** Basis $\{x_1,x_2,v_1,\lambda_1\}=(2/3,4/3,4/3,2)$, $w=4/3$.
$w=\tfrac{4}{3}+\tfrac{2}{3}u_1-\tfrac{10}{3}u_2+3\lambda_2+\mu_1-\mu_2+2v_2$. M
ost negative: $u_2$ ($-10/3$); $\lambda_2$ nonbasic, admissible. Ratios: $x_2:4$
, $v_1:2/5$ → $v_1$ leaves.

**Iteration 4.** Basis $\{x_1,x_2,u_2,\lambda_1\}$. Both artificials $v_1,v_2$ a
re nonbasic, so $w=0$. Terminal basis reached.

### 4. Optimal point

$$
x_1=\tfrac{4}{5},\quad x_2=\tfrac{6}{5},\quad \lambda_1=\tfrac{14}{5},\quad \lam
bda_2=0,\quad u_1=0,\quad u_2=\tfrac{2}{5},\quad \mu_1=\mu_2=0.
$$

**Verification of KKT:**
- Primal feasibility: $x_1+x_2=2\le2$ ✓ (active, $u_1=0$); $-x_1+2x_2=-\tfrac45+
\tfrac{12}{5}=\tfrac85\le2$ ✓ ($u_2=\tfrac25$); $x_1,x_2>0$ ✓.
- Stationarity: $2(\tfrac45)-2(\tfrac65)-2+\tfrac{14}{5}-0-0=0$ ✓; $-2(\tfrac45)
+4(\tfrac65)-6+\tfrac{14}{5}+0-0=-\tfrac85+\tfrac{24}{5}-6+\tfrac{14}{5}=\tfrac{
30}{5}-6=0$ ✓.
- Dual feasibility: $\lambda_1=\tfrac{14}{5}>0$, $\lambda_2=0\ge0$ ✓.
- Complementarity: $\lambda_1u_1=\tfrac{14}{5}\cdot0=0$ ✓, $\lambda_2u_2=0\cdot\
tfrac25=0$ ✓, $\mu_1x_1=\mu_2x_2=0$ ✓.

### 5. Optimal value

$$
f\!\left(\tfrac45,\tfrac65\right)=-\tfrac85-\tfrac{36}{5}+\tfrac{16}{25}-\tfrac{
48}{25}+\tfrac{72}{25}=-\tfrac{44}{5}+\tfrac{40}{25}=-\tfrac{44}{5}+\tfrac85=-\t
frac{36}{5}.
$$

### Conclusion

The minimum is attained at $\boxed{(x_1,x_2)=\left(\dfrac45,\dfrac65\right)}$ wi
