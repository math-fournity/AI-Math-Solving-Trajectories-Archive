## Solution

**Setup.** There are 20 fuel-cell types partitioned into three compatibility cla
sses:
- Alpha-compatible: 4 types (probability $p_A = \tfrac{4}{20} = \tfrac{1}{5}$)
- Beta-compatible: 6 types (probability $p_B = \tfrac{6}{20} = \tfrac{3}{10}$)
- Gamma-compatible: 10 types (probability $p_G = \tfrac{10}{20} = \tfrac{1}{2}$)

The robot draws 3 cells **with replacement**, so the picks $c_1, c_2, c_3$ are i
.i.d. uniform over the 20 types. The robot then assigns the three cells to Berns
tein, Elian, Dorian via a uniformly random permutation $\sigma$ of $\{1,2,3\}$ (
6 equally likely permutations), where person $i$ receives cell $c_{\sigma(i)}$.

**Goal.** Bernstein gets an Alpha-compatible cell, Elian a Beta-compatible cell,
 Dorian a Gamma-compatible cell.

**Key calculation.** Condition on the drawn cells $(c_1,c_2,c_3)$. Given the dra
w, the robot chooses $\sigma$ uniformly from the $3! = 6$ permutations, so

$$P(\text{success} \mid c_1,c_2,c_3) = \frac{\#\{\sigma : c_{\sigma(1)} \in A,\;
 c_{\sigma(2)} \in B,\; c_{\sigma(3)} \in G\}}{6}.$$

Averaging over the draws and swapping the order of summation:

$$P(\text{success}) = \frac{1}{6}\sum_{\sigma} P\!\big(c_{\sigma(1)} \in A,\; c_
{\sigma(2)} \in B,\; c_{\sigma(3)} \in G\big).$$

Since $c_1, c_2, c_3$ are **independent** (replacement), the joint probability f
actors for every fixed $\sigma$:

$$P\!\big(c_{\sigma(1)} \in A,\; c_{\sigma(2)} \in B,\; c_{\sigma(3)} \in G\big)
 = p_A \cdot p_B \cdot p_G,$$

independent of which permutation $\sigma$ is chosen. There are 6 permutations, s
o

$$P(\text{success}) = \frac{1}{6}\cdot 6 \cdot p_A p_B p_G = p_A\, p_B\, p_G.$$

**Numerical value.**

$$P = \frac{4}{20}\cdot\frac{6}{20}\cdot\frac{10}{20} = \frac{1}{5}\cdot\frac{3}
{10}\cdot\frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{100}.$$

**Irreducibility.** $\gcd(3, 100) = 1$, so $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{3}{100}$ with $a
 = 3$, $b = 100$.

$$a + b = 3 + 100 = \boxed{103}.$$
