Each $\mathcal{G}_n$ is a sigma-algebra, and $\mathcal{G}_n \subseteq \mathcal{G

Since $\mathcal{G}$ contains all dyadic intervals $[k/2^n, (k+1)/2^n)$, and thes

**The equivalence relation.** Let $\sim$ be rational equivalence: $x \sim y \iff

### Step 1: The only $\sim$-saturated sets in $\mathcal{G}_n$ are $\emptyset$ an

$$S + \tfrac{1}{2^n} = \bigcup_{k \in A} \left[\tfrac{k+1}{2^n}, \tfrac{k+2}{2^n
