I evaluate $g$ at each element of $\mathbb{F}_p$ (for $p \geq 3$):

- **$x = 0$:** All terms with $x$ vanish, leaving the constant $1$. So $g(0) = 1

So $g$ is the transposition $\tau = (0\ 1)$: it swaps $0$ and $1$ and fixes all
