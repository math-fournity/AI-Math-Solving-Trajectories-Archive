## Solution

We find the 2013th number (1-indexed) among all 5040 permutations of digits {1,2
,3,4,5,6,7} sorted in ascending order. Working with 0-indexed position **2012**,
 we use the factorial number system.

**Step 1 — First digit.** Fixing the first digit leaves 6! = 720 permutations.
- 2012 ÷ 720 = **2** remainder 572.
- The 3rd smallest digit (index 2) of {1,2,3,4,5,6,7} is **3**.
- Remaining: {1,2,4,5,6,7}, position 572.

**Step 2 — Second digit.** 5! = 120.
- 572 ÷ 120 = **4** remainder 52.
- The 5th smallest digit (index 4) of {1,2,4,5,6,7} is **6**.
- Remaining: {1,2,4,5,7}, position 52.

**Step 3 — Third digit.** 4! = 24.
- 52 ÷ 24 = **2** remainder 4.
- The 3rd smallest digit (index 2) of {1,2,4,5,7} is **4**.
- Remaining: {1,2,5,7}, position 4.

**Step 4 — Fourth digit.** 3! = 6.
- 4 ÷ 6 = **0** remainder 4.
- The 1st digit (index 0) of {1,2,5,7} is **1**.
- Remaining: {2,5,7}, position 4.

**Step 5 — Fifth digit.** 2! = 2.
- 4 ÷ 2 = **2** remainder 0.
- The 3rd digit (index 2) of {2,5,7} is **7**.
- Remaining: {2,5}, position 0.

**Step 6 — Sixth digit.** 1! = 1.
- 0 ÷ 1 = **0** remainder 0.
- The 1st digit of {2,5} is **2**.
- Remaining: {5}.

**Step 7 — Seventh digit.** **5**.

**Verification of the decomposition:**
$$2012 = 2\cdot720 + 4\cdot120 + 2\cdot24 + 0\cdot6 + 2\cdot2 + 0\cdot1 + 0 = 14
40+480+48+0+4+0+0 = 2012.\ \checkmark$$

The digit sequence is **3, 6, 4, 1, 7, 2, 5**.

The 2013th number is $\boxed{3641725}$.
