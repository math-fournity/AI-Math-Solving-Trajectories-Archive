## Setup and Interpretation

Each boy arrives with a full bottle of $N$ drops of cologne (even integer, $N \g
eq 30$ so the remainder is non-negative). Each boy pours:
- $N/2$ drops on his favorite girl,
- $5$ drops on each of the other $3$ girls ($15$ total),
- $N/2 - 15$ drops on the mother.

Let $B$ = number of boys, and let $c_i$ = number of boys whose favorite is girl
$i$ ($i=1,2,3,4$), with $\sum c_i = B$.

**Girl $i$ receives:** $c_i \cdot \frac{N}{2} + (B - c_i) \cdot 5 = c_i\!\left(\
frac{N}{2}-5\right) + 5B$.

**Mother receives:** $B\!\left(\frac{N}{2}-15\right)$.

## Constraints

1. **Each girl ≤ $3N$:** $c_i\!\left(\frac{N}{2}-5\right) + 5B \leq 3N$ for all
$i$.
2. **Mother > $3N$:** $B\!\left(\frac{N}{2}-15\right) > 3N$.

From (2): $B > \dfrac{6N}{N-30}$ (requires $B > 6$, i.e., $N > 30$).

From (1), the maximum $c_i$ is bounded: $c_i \leq \dfrac{3N - 5B}{N/2 - 5}$. By
pigeonhole, some girl has $\geq \lceil B/4 \rceil$ favorites, so we need:

$$\left\lfloor \frac{3N-5B}{N/2-5}\right\rfloor \geq \left\lceil \frac{B}{4}\rig
ht\rceil$$

## Finding the Minimum $N$

**$N = 52$:** Mother requires $B \geq 15$. For $B=15$: max $c_i = \lfloor(156-75
)/21\rfloor = 3$, but pigeonhole needs $\lceil 15/4\rceil = 4$. Fails. Larger $B
$ only worsens the girls' constraint. ✗

**$N = 54$:** Mother requires $B \geq 14$. For $B=14$: max $c_i = \lfloor(162-70
)/22\rfloor = \lfloor 4.18\rfloor = 4$, and pigeonhole needs $\lceil 14/4\rceil
= 4$. ✓
