## Solution

The equation is $y^{(4)} - y = \delta(t-1)$ with homogeneous initial conditions
$y(0)=y'(0)=y''(0)=y'''(0)=0$.

### Homogeneous solution

The characteristic equation is $r^4 - 1 = 0$, giving roots $r = 1, -1, i, -i$. T
he general homogeneous solution is:
$$y_h(t) = c_1 e^t + c_2 e^{-t} + c_3 \cos t + c_4 \sin t.$$

### Behavior before the impulse

For $t < 1$, there is no forcing and all initial conditions are zero, so $y \equ
iv 0$ on $[0,1)$. Hence $y(1^-)=y'(1^-)=y''(1^-)=y'''(1^-)=0$.

### Jump conditions at $t = 1$

Integrating the equation across $t=1$: the delta $\delta(t-1)$ appears on the ri
ght, and since $y$ and its first three derivatives are continuous (only the 4th
derivative contains the delta), we get a unit jump in the third derivative:
$$y'''(1^+) - y'''(1^-) = 1, \qquad y, y', y'' \text{ continuous at } t=1.$$

Thus the conditions at $t=1$ (for the solution on $t \geq 1$) are:
$$y(1)=0,\quad y'(1)=0,\quad y''(1)=0,\quad y'''(1)=1.$$

### Solving on $t \geq 1$

Set $s = t-1 \geq 0$ and write $y = a_1 e^s + a_2 e^{-s} + a_3 \cos s + a_4 \sin
 s$. The conditions at $s=0$ give:

| Derivative | Expression at $s=0$ | Value |
|---|---|---|
| $y(0)$ | $a_1 + a_2 + a_3$ | $0$ |
| $y'(0)$ | $a_1 - a_2 + a_4$ | $0$ |
| $y''(0)$ | $a_1 + a_2 - a_3$ | $0$ |
| $y'''(0)$ | $a_1 - a_2 - a_4$ | $1$ |

From rows 1 and 3: $a_3 = 0$ and $a_1 + a_2 = 0$, so $a_2 = -a_1$.
From row 2: $2a_1 + a_4 = 0$, so $a_4 = -2a_1$.
From row 4: $2a_1 - a_4 = 1 \Rightarrow 2a_1 + 2a_1 = 1 \Rightarrow a_1 = \tfrac
{1}{4}$.

Therefore $a_1 = \tfrac14,\; a_2 = -\tfrac14,\; a_3 = 0,\; a_4 = -\tfrac12$, and
$$y = \tfrac14 e^s - \tfrac14 e^{-s} - \tfrac12 \sin s = \tfrac12\sinh s - \tfra
c12 \sin s.$$

### Verification

For $s>0$: $y^{(4)} = \tfrac12\sinh s - \tfrac12\sin s = y$, so $y^{(4)}-y=0$. ✓

At $s=0$: $y=0$, $y'=\tfrac12(1-1)=0$, $y''=0$, $y'''=\tfrac12(1+1)=1$. ✓

The jump $y'''(1^+)-y'''(1^-)=1-0=1$ produces the delta. ✓

### Final answer

$$\boxed{y(t) = \frac{1}{2}\bigl[\sinh(t-1) - \sin(t-1)\bigr]\,H(t-1)}$$

where $H$ is the Heaviside step function. Equivalently, $y(t)=0$ for $t<1$ and $
y(t)=\frac12[\sinh(t-1)-\sin(t-1)]$ for $t\ge 1$.
