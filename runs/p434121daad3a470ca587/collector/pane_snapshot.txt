# Green's Function for the Heat Equation on the Half-Line with Non-Homogeneous N
eumann Boundary Condition

## Problem

$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} = k\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} + Q(x,t), \
quad x > 0,\; t > 0$$
$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}(0,t) = A(t), \qquad u(x,0) = f(x)$$

## Step 1: The Neumann Green's Function (Method of Images)

We seek the Green's function $G_N(x,t;\xi,\tau)$ satisfying the **homogeneous**
Neumann problem:

$$\frac{\partial G_N}{\partial t} - k\frac{\partial^2 G_N}{\partial x^2} = \delt
a(x-\xi)\,\delta(t-\tau), \qquad \frac{\partial G_N}{\partial x}(0,t;\xi,\tau) =
 0$$

with causality $G_N = 0$ for $t \le \tau$.

On the full line, the heat kernel is $\Phi(x-\xi, t-\tau) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{4\pi
k(t-\tau)}}\exp\!\bigl(-\frac{(x-\xi)^2}{4k(t-\tau)}\bigr)$. To enforce the homo
geneous Neumann condition at $x=0$, we place an **image source** of the same sig
n at $x = -\xi$:

$$\boxed{G_N(x,t;\xi,\tau) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{4\pi k(t-\tau)}}\left[\exp\!\left(-\
frac{(x-\xi)^2}{4k(t-\tau)}\right) + \exp\!\left(-\frac{(x+\xi)^2}{4k(t-\tau)}\r
ight)\right], \quad t > \tau}$$

and $G_N = 0$ for $t \le \tau$.

**Verification of the boundary condition:** Differentiating,
$$\frac{\partial G_N}{\partial x} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{4\pi k(t-\tau)}}\left[\frac{-
(x-\xi)}{2k(t-\tau)}\,e^{-(x-\xi)^2/(4k(t-\tau))} + \frac{-(x+\xi)}{2k(t-\tau)}\
,e^{-(x+\xi)^2/(4k(t-\tau))}\right]$$
At $x=0$:
$$\frac{\partial G_N}{\partial x}(0,t;\xi,\tau) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{4\pi k(t-\tau)}
}\cdot\frac{1}{2k(t-\tau)}\left[\xi\,e^{-\xi^2/(4k(t-\tau))} - \xi\,e^{-\xi^2/(4
k(t-\tau))}\right] = 0. \;\checkmark$$

## Step 2: Derivation of the Solution via Green's Identity

We use the **adjoint** (backward) Green's function $H(x,t;\xi,\tau)$ satisfying:

$$-\frac{\partial H}{\partial t} - k\frac{\partial^2 H}{\partial x^2} = \delta(x
-\xi)\,\delta(t-\tau), \qquad \frac{\partial H}{\partial x}(0,t;\xi,\tau) = 0, \
qquad H = 0 \;\text{for}\; t > \tau$$

By the reciprocity principle for the heat equation, $H(x,t;\xi,\tau) = G_N(\xi,\
tau;x,t)$, which holds because $G_N$ is symmetric in its spatial arguments.

Multiply $u_t - ku_{xx} = Q$ by $H$ and integrate over $x \in [0,\infty)$, $t \i
n [0,T]$ with $T > \tau$. Using $-H_t - kH_{xx} = \delta(x-\xi)\delta(t-\tau)$,
subtract:

$$\int_0^T\!\!\int_0^\infty \bigl(H\,u_t + u\,H_t\bigr)\,dx\,dt \;-\; k\int_0^T\
!\!\int_0^\infty \bigl(H\,u_{xx} - u\,H_{xx}\bigr)\,dx\,dt \;=\; \int_0^T\!\!\in
t_0^\infty H\,Q\,dx\,dt \;-\; u(\xi,\tau)$$

**Time term.** Since $H(x,T;\xi,\tau)=0$ (as $T>\tau$) and $H(x,0;\xi,\tau) = G_
N(\xi,\tau;x,0)$:

$$\int_0^\infty \bigl[u\,H\bigr]_{t=0}^{t=T} dx = -\int_0^\infty f(x)\,G_N(\xi,\
tau;x,0)\,dx$$

**Space term.** Integration by parts in $x$; the boundary at $x\to\infty$ vanish
es by decay. At $x=0$, using $H_x(0,t;\xi,\tau)=0$ and $u_x(0,t)=A(t)$:

$$\int_0^T \bigl[H\,u_x - u\,H_x\bigr]_{x=0}\,dt = -\int_0^\tau G_N(\xi,\tau;0,t
)\,A(t)\,dt$$

(the integral over $t$ truncates at $\tau$ since $H=0$ for $t>\tau$).

**Assembling:**

$$-\int_0^\infty f(x)\,G_N(\xi,\tau;x,0)\,dx \;+\; k\int_0^\tau G_N(\xi,\tau;0,t
)\,A(t)\,dt \;=\; \int_0^\tau\!\!\int_0^\infty G_N(\xi,\tau;x,t)\,Q(x,t)\,dx\,dt
 \;-\; u(\xi,\tau)$$

Solving for $u(\xi,\tau)$ and relabeling $(\xi,\tau)\to(x,t)$, $(x,t)\to(\xi,\ta
u)$:

## Step 3: The Complete Solution

$$\boxed{u(x,t) = \underbrace{\int_0^\infty G_N(x,t;\xi,0)\,f(\xi)\,d\xi}_{\text
{initial condition}} \;+\; \underbrace{\int_0^t\!\!\int_0^\infty G_N(x,t;\xi,\ta
u)\,Q(\xi,\tau)\,d\xi\,d\tau}_{\text{distributed source}} \;-\; \underbrace{k\in
t_0^t G_N(x,t;0,\tau)\,A(\tau)\,d\tau}_{\text{boundary flux}}}$$

where the **Neumann Green's function** is:

$$G_N(x,t;\xi,\tau) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{4\pi k(t-\tau)}}\left[\exp\!\left(-\frac{(x
-\xi)^2}{4k(t-\tau)}\right) + \exp\!\left(-\frac{(x+\xi)^2}{4k(t-\tau)}\right)\r
ight]$$

and the boundary kernel evaluates to:

$$G_N(x,t;0,\tau) = \frac{2}{\sqrt{4\pi k(t-\tau)}}\exp\!\left(-\frac{x^2}{4k(t-
\tau)}\right)$$

## Step 4: Verification

**Initial condition ($t\to 0^+$).** As $t\to 0^+$, $G_N(x,t;\xi,0)\to \delta(x-\
xi)+\delta(x+\xi)$. Since $x,\xi > 0$, only $\delta(x-\xi)$ contributes, giving
$u(x,0) = f(x)$. ✓

**PDE for $x > 0$.** Each term is a convolution (in $t$) of $G_N$ with a source.
 Since $(\partial_t - k\partial_{xx})G_N = 0$ for $t > \tau$ and $x > 0$, and th
e boundary kernel $G_N(x,t;0,\tau) \to 2\delta(x)$ as $\tau \to t^-$ (which vani
shes for $x > 0$), all three terms satisfy $u_t - ku_{xx} = Q$ for $x > 0$. The
source term contributes $Q(x,t)$ via the standard Duhamel argument. ✓

**Boundary condition $\partial_x u(0,t) = A(t)$.** The IC and source terms inher
it $\partial_x G_N(0,t;\xi,\tau)=0$, so they contribute zero. For the boundary t
erm $v(x,t) = -k\int_0^t G_N(x,t;0,\tau)\,A(\tau)\,d\tau$:

$$\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}(x,t) = \frac{x}{\sqrt{4\pi k}}\int_0^t \frac{A(\
tau)}{(t-\tau)^{3/2}}\exp\!\left(-\frac{x^2}{4k(t-\tau)}\right)d\tau$$

Taking $x \to 0^+$, substitute $s = t-\tau$ and use $A(t-s)\to A(t)$ for the dom
inant (singular) region $s \to 0$:

$$\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}(0^+,t) = \frac{x\,A(t)}{\sqrt{4\pi k}}\int_0^\in
fty s^{-3/2}\,e^{-x^2/(4ks)}\,ds$$

With the substitution $w = x^2/(4ks)$, the integral evaluates to $\frac{2\sqrt{\
pi k}}{x}$, giving:

$$\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}(0^+,t) = \frac{x\,A(t)}{\sqrt{4\pi k}}\cdot\frac
{2\sqrt{\pi k}}{x} = A(t) \quad\checkmark$$

The singularity of $G_N(x,t;0,\tau)$ as $\tau \to t^-$ (where $G_N \to 2\delta(x
)$) is precisely what generates the non-homogeneous boundary data. **Physical co
nsistency:** by Fourier's law the heat flux at the boundary is $-k\,\partial_x u
(0,t) = -kA(t)$; a positive $A(t)$ corresponds to heat leaving the domain, and t
he $-k\int G_N A$ term correctly subtracts energy. ✓
