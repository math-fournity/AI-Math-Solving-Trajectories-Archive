## Proof

Set $m = \lg n$, so $n = b^m$ where $b$ is the base. As $n \to \infty$, $m \to \

Let $c = \lg(2/5)$. Since $0 < 2/5 < 1$, we have $c < 0$ for any base $b > 1$.

$$f(n) = \frac{m!}{c^m \cdot (2/5)^{b^m}} = \frac{m! \cdot (5/2)^{b^m}}{c^m}$$

$$|f(n)| = \frac{m! \cdot (5/2)^{b^m}}{|c|^m}$$

$$|f(n)| \sim \sqrt{2\pi m} \cdot \left(\frac{m}{e|c|}\right)^m \cdot \left(\fra

2. **Super-exponential factor:** $\left(\frac{m}{e|c|}\right)^m \to \infty$. Ind

5}{2}\right)^n \to \infty$. Since $\frac{5}{2} > 1$ and $b^m = n \to \infty$, th

$$\ln|f(n)| \to +\infty \quad \text{as } m \to \infty$$

$$|f(n)| \to +\infty \quad \text{as } n \to \infty.$$
