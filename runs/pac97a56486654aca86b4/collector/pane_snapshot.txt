## Setup

Place the circumcenter O at the origin with Γ: x² + y² = R². Let A = (0, R) and
A' = (0, −R), so AA' is the vertical diameter (y-axis).

Let B, C be on Γ with the arc BC not containing A having measure 2A (where A is
the angle at vertex A). Let N be the midpoint of this arc, at angle σ. Using the
 counterclockwise ordering A, B, C on Γ:
- B at angle π/2 + 2C, C at angle π/2 + 2C + 2A
- N (midpoint of arc BC not containing A) at angle σ = 3π/2 + C − B

Setting δ = A (half the arc BC), I derive:
- **sin σ = −cos(B−C)**, **cos σ = −sin(B−C)**
- V (intersection of y-axis with BC) has y-coordinate v = R cos A / sin σ

## Key equations

**Condition A'N = 9:** A'N² = 2R²(1 + sin σ) = 81. Since 1 + sin σ = 2u²/(1+u²),
 this gives:
$$R^2 \cdot \frac{2u^2}{1+u^2} = \frac{81}{2} \quad \Longrightarrow \quad R = \f
rac{9\sqrt{1+u^2}}{2u} \tag{*}$$

**Condition EV = 6:** Computing the positions of E (angle bisector foot, via sec

## Solving the system

Since b − au² > 0 and 1 − u² > 0 (acute triangle gives |u| < 1), I take P = a −
u²b and note Q = b − au² > 0 with P + Q = 1 − u².

Dividing the EV equation by the VA' equation: **7uQ = 6|P|**.

**Case P > 0:** Solving 7uQ = 6P yields a = u(7+6u)/((1+u²)(6+7u)), and then P =

$$(6+7u)^2 = 81(1+u^2)$$
$$32u^2 - 84u + 45 = 0$$

Then **R = (6 + 21/4)/(3/2) = (45/4)·(2/3) = 15/2**.
