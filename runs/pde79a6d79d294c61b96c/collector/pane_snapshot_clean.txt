## Proof

**Claim.** Let $k$ be a Noetherian ring and $A$ a smooth affine $k$-algebra of (
Krull) dimension $d$. Then the module of Kähler $p$-forms $\Omega^{p}_{A|k} := \
bigwedge^{p}_{A}\Omega^{1}_{A|k}$ vanishes for every $p > d$. In particular $\Om
ega^{d+1}_{A|k}\cong 0$.

---

### Step 1. Smoothness forces $\Omega^{1}_{A|k}$ to be locally free of rank $d$.

Since $k$ is Noetherian and $A$ is a smooth $k$-algebra, $A$ is of finite presen
tation over $k$ (over a Noetherian base, finite type $=$ finite presentation, an
d smoothness entails finite presentation). The structure morphism $f:\operatorna
me{Spec}A\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ is therefore smooth of relative dimension $d$.

A standard characterization of smoothness (EGA IV, 17.10.1 / 16.5.12) states:

> A morphism $f:X\to S$ is smooth of relative dimension $d$ if and only if $f$ i
s locally of finite presentation, flat, and the sheaf of relative differentials
$\Omega^{1}_{X/S}$ is locally free of rank $d$.

Applying this to $f:\operatorname{Spec}A\to\operatorname{Spec}k$, the $A$-module
 $\Omega^{1}_{A|k}$ is **locally free of rank $d$**: there exist elements $f_1,\
dots,f_m\in A$ with $(f_1,\dots,f_m)=A$ such that each localization $\Omega^{1}_
{A|k}\otimes_A A_{f_i}$ is a free $A_{f_i}$-module on $d$ generators.

### Step 2. Exterior powers of a locally free module of rank $d$ vanish above $d
$.

Let $M$ be a locally free $A$-module of rank $d$. Choose a covering $\{A_{f_i}\}
$ trivializing $M$, so $M\otimes_A A_{f_i}\cong A_{f_i}^{\,d}$ for each $i$. Ext
erior powers commute with base change:

$$\textstyle\bigwedge^{p}_{A}M\otimes_A A_{f_i}\;\cong\;\bigwedge^{p}_{A_{f_i}}\
!\big(A_{f_i}^{\,d}\big).$$

For a free module $R^{d}$ over any commutative ring $R$, the exterior power $\bi
gwedge^{p}_{R}R^{d}$ is the free $R$-module on the symbols $e_{i_1}\wedge\cdots\
wedge e_{i_p}$ with $1\le i_1<\cdots<i_p\le d$. When $p>d$ there are **no** stri
ctly increasing $p$-tuples of indices in $\{1,\dots,d\}$, so

$$\textstyle\bigwedge^{p}_{R}R^{d}=0\qquad\text{for all }p>d.$$

Hence each localization $\big(\bigwedge^{p}_{A}M\big)\otimes_A A_{f_i}$ is zero.
 A module whose every localization at the elements of a covering family is zero
is itself zero (locality of the zero module: $N=0\iff N_{f_i}=0$ for all $i$ wit
h $(f_i)=A$). Therefore

$$\textstyle\bigwedge^{p}_{A}M=0\qquad\text{for all }p>d.$$

### Step 3. Apply to $M=\Omega^{1}_{A|k}$.

By Step 1, $\Omega^{1}_{A|k}$ is locally free of rank $d$. By Step 2 with $p=d+1
$,

$$\Omega^{d+1}_{A|k}\;=\;\textstyle\bigwedge^{d+1}_{A}\Omega^{1}_{A|k}\;\cong\;0
.$$

More generally $\Omega^{p}_{A|k}\cong 0$ for every $p\ge d+1$.

### Remark on the hypotheses.

- **Noetherianity of $k$** guarantees that "finite type $=$ finite presentation,
" so smoothness (which a priori requires finite presentation) is automatic from
the affine-smooth hypothesis, and the standard characterization in Step 1 applie
s cleanly.
- **Affineness** is used only to phrase everything in terms of modules rather th
an sheaves; the same argument works for any smooth $k$-scheme of relative dimens
ion $d$.
- The argument is purely local on $A$, so no hypothesis on $A$ beyond smoothness
 of relative dimension $d$ is needed.

---

**Conclusion.** For a smooth affine $k$-algebra $A$ of dimension $d$ over a Noet
herian ring $k$, the Kähler differential forms vanish in every degree above $d$:

$$\boxed{\;\Omega^{p}_{A|k}\cong 0\quad\text{for all }p>d,\quad\text{in particul
ar }\Omega^{d+1}_{A|k}\cong 0.\;}$$
