## Proof

**The frame.** Consider the Kripke frame $F = (W, R)$ with:
- $W = \{w, v, u\}$
- $R = \{(w,w),\,(v,v),\,(u,u),\,(w,v),\,(w,u)\}$

**Reflexivity:** Every world relates to itself: $wRw, vRv, uRu$. ✓

**Transitivity:** The only nontrivial two-step chains are $wRv,\,vRv \Rightarrow
 wRv$ and $wRu,\,uRu \Rightarrow wRu$, both of which are in $R$. All other chain
s are reflexive loops. So $R$ is transitive. ✓

Thus $F$ is a reflexive and transitive frame, i.e., a frame for **KT4 = S4**.

**Valuation.** Let $p$ be a propositional variable with $V(p) = \{w\}$, i.e., $p
$ is true at $w$ and false at $v$ and $u$.

---

**Axiom B ($\varphi \to \Box\Diamond\varphi$) fails.** Take $\varphi = p$ and ev
aluate at $w$:

- $w \vDash p$ since $w \in V(p)$.
- $w \vDash \Box\Diamond p$ requires every $R$-successor of $w$ to satisfy $\Dia
mond p$. The successors of $w$ are $\{w, v, u\}$.
  - At $v$: the only successor of $v$ is $v$ itself (since $R$ restricted to $v$
's out-neighborhood is $\{v\}$). Since $v \notin V(p)$, we get $v \vDash \neg p$
, hence $v \vDash \neg\Diamond p$.

  Therefore $w \nvDash \Box\Diamond p$.

So $w \vDash p$ but $w \nvDash \Box\Diamond p$, meaning **B fails** at $w$. ✓

---

**Axiom 5 ($\Diamond\varphi \to \Box\Diamond\varphi$) fails.** Take $\varphi = p
$ and evaluate at $w$:

- $w \vDash \Diamond p$: since $wRw$ and $w \vDash p$, world $w$ has a successor
 satisfying $p$.
- $w \nvDash \Box\Diamond p$: as shown above, $v$ is an $R$-successor of $w$ wit
h $v \nvDash \Diamond p$.

So $w \vDash \Diamond p$ but $w \nvDash \Box\Diamond p$, meaning **5 fails** at
$w$. ✓

---

**Summary.** The model $\mathcal{M} = (F, V)$ with $F = (\{w,v,u\}, R)$ as above
 (reflexive and transitive) and $V(p) = \{w\}$ is a model of **S4** in which bot
h the Brouwerian axiom B and the Euclidean axiom 5 fail at world $w$.
