# Problem

On a logistics grid consisting of 5 rows and 6 shipping bays (30 bays total), a specialized automated drone is programmed to deliver packages. The drone must strictly alternate between two types of propulsion:
1. **Long Jump:** A standard "L-shaped" move (moving 2 bays in one cardinal direction and 1 bay perpendicularly).
2. **Short Glide:** A move to any diagonally adjacent bay.

The drone begins its mission from a bay of your choice and its very first move must be a **Long Jump**. To prevent system interference, the drone is prohibited from landing in the same shipping bay more than once. 

What is the maximum number of moves the drone can execute before it is impossible to make another move without violating the safety protocol?
