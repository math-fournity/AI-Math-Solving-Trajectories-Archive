# Problem

A logistics manager is organizing a freight train consisting of a single line of 7 identical cargo slots. To fill these slots, she uses shipping containers of various lengths; each container is $m$ slots long (where $m$ is a positive integer) and must fit entirely within the 7 available slots without overlapping others.

Each container loaded onto the train must be painted one of three solid colors: Red, Blue, or Green. The manager considers two configurations to be different if the boundaries of the containers are placed differently, or if the color sequence of the containers changes. For instance, placing a 2-slot Blue container is distinct from placing two 1-slot Blue containers in the same position.

Let $N$ be the total number of ways the 7-slot train can be completely filled such that at least one Red, at least one Blue, and at least one Green container are present in the final arrangement.

Find the remainder when $N$ is divided by 1000.
