## Proof

**Setup.** The hub at the origin emits 180 beams at angles θ = 0°, 1°, 2°, …, 17
9°. Each beam is the ray from (0,0) in direction (cos θ, sin θ). The partition i
s the line x + y = 100.

**Intersection point.** A beam at angle θ meets the line x + y = 100 where

$$t\cos\theta + t\sin\theta = 100 \implies t = \frac{100}{\cos\theta + \sin\thet
a},$$

so the x-coordinate of the intersection is

$$x(\theta) = \frac{100\cos\theta}{\cos\theta + \sin\theta}.$$

**Parallel beam.** The partition has slope −1 (direction angle 135°). At θ = 135
°: cos 135° + sin 135° = −√2/2 + √2/2 = 0, confirming this beam is parallel and
excluded. By the problem's statement, all other 179 beams strike the glass, so w
e sum x(θ) over θ ∈ {0, 1, …, 179} \ {135}.

**Key identity (first pairing).** Define f(θ) = cos θ / (cos θ + sin θ). Then

$$f(\theta) + f(90°-\theta) = \frac{\cos\theta}{\cos\theta+\sin\theta} + \frac{\
sin\theta}{\sin\theta+\cos\theta} = \frac{\cos\theta+\sin\theta}{\cos\theta+\sin
\theta} = 1.$$

**Sum over θ ∈ {0, …, 90}.** The pairing θ ↔ 90° − θ gives the 45 pairs (0,90),
(1,89), …, (44,46), each summing to 1, plus the fixed point θ = 45° with f(45°)
= (√2/2)/√2 = 1/2. Thus

$$\sum_{\theta=0}^{90} f(\theta) = 45 \times 1 + \tfrac{1}{2} = 45.5.$$

**Key identity (second pairing).** For the remaining angles, use θ ↔ 270° − θ. S
ince cos(270°−θ) = −sin θ and sin(270°−θ) = −cos θ:

$$f(270°-\theta) = \frac{-\sin\theta}{-\sin\theta - \cos\theta} = \frac{\sin\the
ta}{\sin\theta+\cos\theta},$$

so f(θ) + f(270° − θ) = 1. Note 270° − θ maps {91, …, 179} to itself.

**Sum over θ ∈ {91, …, 179} \ {135}.** The pairing gives 44 pairs (91,179), (92,
178), …, (134,136), each summing to 1. The fixed point θ = 135° is excluded (par
allel). Thus

$$\sum_{\substack{\theta=91 \\ \theta \neq 135}}^{179} f(\theta) = 44 \times 1 =
 44.$$

**Total.** Combining both ranges:

$$\sum_{\substack{\theta=0 \\ \theta \neq 135}}^{179} f(\theta) = 45.5 + 44 = 89
.5.$$

The sum of x-coordinates is

$$100 \times 89.5 = \boxed{8950}.$$
