Looking at this problem, I need to find the maximum number of disjoint simple cl
osed curves on a non-orientable surface $N_h$ (connected sum of $h$ projective p
lanes, i.e., a sphere with $h$ crosscaps) such that cutting along all of them si
multaneously leaves the surface connected.

**Answer: The maximum number is $h$.**

## Proof

### Upper Bound: $n \leq h$

Let $\gamma_1, \dots, \gamma_n$ be pairwise disjoint simple closed curves on $N_
h$ such that cutting along all of them yields a connected surface $S'$.

**Each curve is either one-sided** (its regular neighborhood is a Möbius strip,
creating 1 boundary component when cut) **or two-sided** (its regular neighborho
od is an annulus, creating 2 boundary components when cut). Let $n_1$ be the num
ber of one-sided curves and $n_2$ the number of two-sided curves, so $n = n_1 +
n_2$.

**Key observations:**
- Cutting does not change the Euler characteristic, so $\chi(S') = \chi(N_h) = 2
 - h$.
- The surface $S'$ has $b = n_1 + 2n_2$ boundary components.
- $S'$ is connected by assumption.

By the classification of compact connected surfaces, $S'$ is either:
- **Non-orientable** of genus $k \geq 1$: then $\chi(S') = 2 - k - b = 2 - h$, g
iving $k = h - b = h - n_1 - 2n_2 \geq 1$.
- **Orientable** of genus $g \geq 0$: then $\chi(S') = 2 - 2g - b = 2 - h$, givi
ng $2g = h - b = h - n_1 - 2n_2 \geq 0$.

In both cases, $h - n_1 - 2n_2 \geq 0$, so:
$$n_1 + 2n_2 \leq h$$

Since $n_2 \geq 0$:
$$n = n_1 + n_2 \leq n_1 + 2n_2 \leq h$$

Therefore $n \leq h$.

### Lower Bound: $n = h$ is achievable

Represent $N_h$ as a sphere with $h$ crosscaps — that is, a sphere with $h$ open
 disks removed and a Möbius strip glued to each boundary component.

**Construction:** Take the **core curve** of each of the $h$ Möbius strips. Each
 core curve is a one-sided simple closed curve (its regular neighborhood is the
Möbius strip itself). Since the $h$ Möbius strips are attached to disjoint disks
 on the sphere, these $h$ core curves are pairwise disjoint.

**Cutting along all $h$ curves:** When a Möbius strip is cut along its core curv
e, it becomes an annulus (a standard topological fact: the Möbius strip $[0,1]\t
imes[0,1]/(0,y)\sim(1,1-y)$, cut along $[0,1]\times\{1/2\}$, yields a cylinder).
 After cutting all $h$ core curves, each crosscap becomes an annulus, and the re
sult is a sphere with $h$ boundary components (the free ends of the annuli). Thi
s is **connected**. ✓

**Each curve is non-separating:** A one-sided curve on a connected surface is al
ways non-separating. Indeed, its Möbius strip neighborhood $A$ has a single boun
dary component $\partial A$. Since $N_h = A \cup B$ with $A \cap B = \partial A$
 (a connected set) and $N_h$ is connected, the complement $B = N_h \setminus \op
eratorname{int}(A)$ must be connected. Hence cutting along the curve preserves c
onnectedness.

### Conclusion

The upper bound gives $n \leq h$, and the construction achieves $n = h$. Therefo

hat the surface remains connected after cutting along them is $h$.
