## Solution

**Setup.** Let $a,b,c$ be the real roots of $x^3-3x^2-7x-1=0$. By Vieta's:
$$a+b+c=3,\quad ab+bc+ca=-7,\quad abc=1.$$

Set $\alpha=\sqrt[3]{a},\ \beta=\sqrt[3]{b},\ \gamma=\sqrt[3]{c}$ (real cube roo
ts). Define the elementary symmetric functions:
$$e_1=\alpha+\beta+\gamma,\quad e_2=\alpha\beta+\beta\gamma+\gamma\alpha,\quad e
_3=\alpha\beta\gamma=\sqrt[3]{abc}=1.$$

**Finding $e_1, e_2, e_3$.** Using the identity $\alpha^3+\beta^3+\gamma^3 = e_1
^3-3e_1e_2+3e_3$:
$$3 = e_1^3 - 3e_1e_2 + 3 \implies e_1(e_1^2-3e_2)=0.$$

For the products $\alpha\beta,\beta\gamma,\gamma\alpha$, their elementary symmet
ric functions are $f_1=e_2$, $f_2=e_1e_3=e_1$, $f_3=e_3^2=1$, so:
$$(\alpha\beta)^3+(\beta\gamma)^3+(\gamma\alpha)^3 = e_2^3 - 3e_1e_2 + 3 = -7.$$

**Case $e_1^2=3e_2$:** Substituting $e_2=e_1^2/3$ gives $e_1^6/27 - e_1^3 = -10$
, i.e. $(e_1^3)^2 - 27e_1^3 + 270=0$, with discriminant $729-1080=-351<0$. No re
al solution — contradiction since $e_1$ is real.

**Case $e_1=0$:** Then $e_2^3+3=-7$, so $e_2^3=-10$, giving $e_2=-\sqrt[3]{10}$.
 (Numerical check: $\sqrt[3]{a}+\sqrt[3]{b}+\sqrt[3]{c}\approx 0$.) ✓

So $\alpha,\beta,\gamma$ are roots of $t^3 - \sqrt[3]{10}\,t - 1 = 0$.

**Computing $\Omega$.** Let $S = \frac{1}{\alpha-\beta}+\frac{1}{\beta-\gamma}+\
frac{1}{\gamma-\alpha}$. With $u=\alpha-\beta,\ v=\beta-\gamma,\ w=\gamma-\alpha
$ (so $u+v+w=0$):
$$S = \frac{uv+vw+wu}{uvw} = \frac{-\frac12(u^2+v^2+w^2)}{uvw} = \frac{-(\alpha^
2+\beta^2+\gamma^2-e_2)}{(\alpha-\beta)(\beta-\gamma)(\gamma-\alpha)}.$$

Since $e_1=0$: $\alpha^2+\beta^2+\gamma^2 = e_1^2-2e_2 = 2\sqrt[3]{10}$, so:
$$\alpha^2+\beta^2+\gamma^2 - e_2 = 2\sqrt[3]{10}+\sqrt[3]{10} = 3\sqrt[3]{10}.$
$

The denominator squared is the discriminant of $t^3+pt+q$ with $p=-\sqrt[3]{10},
\ q=-1$:
$$\Delta = -4p^3-27q^2 = -4(-10)-27 = 13.$$

Therefore:
$$\Omega = S^2 = \frac{(3\sqrt[3]{10})^2}{13} = \frac{9\cdot 10^{2/3}}{13} = \fr
ac{9\sqrt[3]{100}}{13}.$$

**Extracting $p,q,r$.** Here $p=9$, $q=100=2^2\cdot 5^2$ (no perfect-cube prime
factor), $r=13$, with $\gcd(9,13)=1$. ✓

$$100p+10q+r = 900+1000+13 = \boxed{1913}.$$
