## Cycle Index of the Edge-Permutation Group of $K_5$

**Setup.** The complete graph $K_5$ has 5 vertices and $\binom{5}{2}=10$ edges.
Its automorphism group is $\operatorname{Aut}(K_5)\cong S_5$, acting on the 10 e
dges (viewed as 2-element subsets of $\{1,2,3,4,5\}$) by $\sigma\cdot\{i,j\}=\{\
sigma(i),\sigma(j)\}$. The cycle index is

$$\mathcal{P}_G=\frac{1}{|G|}\sum_{g\in G}x_1^{c_1(g)}x_2^{c_2(g)}\cdots$$

where $c_k(g)$ counts $k$-cycles in the induced edge-permutation. We classify el
ements of $S_5$ by vertex-cycle type and compute the induced edge-cycle type for
 a representative of each class.

**Class 1: identity, $1^5$, count $1$.** Every edge fixed: edge-type $1^{10}$. C
ontribution: $x_1^{10}$.

**Class 2: transposition $(12)$, $2\,1^3$, count $\binom{5}{2}=10$.** Edges $\{1
,2\},\{3,4\},\{3,5\},\{4,5\}$ are fixed (4); the pairs $\{1,3\}\leftrightarrow\{
2,3\}$, $\{1,4\}\leftrightarrow\{2,4\}$, $\{1,5\}\leftrightarrow\{2,5\}$ form 3
two-cycles. Edge-type $1^4 2^3$. Contribution: $x_1^4 x_2^3$.

**Class 3: double transposition $(12)(34)$, $2^2 1$, count $5!/(2^2\cdot 2!)=15$

\{2,5\}$, $\{3,5\}\leftrightarrow\{4,5\}$ (4 two-cycles). Edge-type $1^2 2^4$. C
ontribution: $x_1^2 x_2^4$.

**Class 4: 3-cycle $(123)$, $3\,1^2$, count $5!/(3\cdot 2!)=20$.** Fixed edge: $

4\}\to\{1,4\}$. Edges to vertex 5 form a 3-cycle: $\{1,5\}\to\{2,5\}\to\{3,5\}\t

**Class 6: 4-cycle $(1234)$, $4\,1$, count $5!/4=30$.** Edges among $\{1,2,3,4\}
