Therefore the two pieces cancel exactly for every $\varepsilon>0$:
$$\int_{-\infty}^{-\varepsilon} \frac{dx}{2\sinh x} + \int_{\varepsilon}^{\infty

$$\text{P.V.}\,I = \lim_{\varepsilon\to 0^+} 0 = 0.$$
