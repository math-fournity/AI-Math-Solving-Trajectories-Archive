## Solution

**Problem.** Find the envelope of the family of circles passing through the orig
in with centre on the curve $y^3 = 4x$.

---

### Setting up the family

Let the centre of a circle be $(h,k)$ lying on the curve $y^3=4x$, so the constr
aint is
$$k^3 = 4h \quad\Longrightarrow\quad h = \tfrac{k^3}{4}.$$

Since the circle passes through the origin, its radius squared is $h^2+k^2$. Hen
ce the circle is
$$(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2 = h^2+k^2.$$

Expanding and using $h=k^3/4$, and writing the parameter as $t$ (so $k=t$, $h=t^
3/4$), the one-parameter family is
$$F(x,y,t) \;=\; x^2+y^2-2hx-2ky \;=\; x^2+y^2-\tfrac{t^3}{2}\,x-2ty \;=\; 0. \t
ag{1}$$

### Envelope conditions

The envelope is obtained from $F=0$ together with $\partial F/\partial t = 0$:
$$\frac{\partial F}{\partial t} = -\tfrac{3t^2}{2}\,x - 2y = 0 \quad\Longrightar
row\quad y = -\tfrac{3t^2}{4}\,x. \tag{2}$$

Substituting (2) into (1):
$$x^2 + \tfrac{9t^4}{16}\,x^2 = \tfrac{t^3}{2}\,x + 2t\!\left(-\tfrac{3t^2}{4}\,
x\right) = \tfrac{t^3 x}{2}-\tfrac{3t^3 x}{2} = -t^3 x.$$

For $x\neq 0$ (the solution $x=0$ gives the origin, already a member of the fami
ly):
$$x\cdot\frac{16+9t^4}{16} = -t^3 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad x = \frac{-16\,t^3}{
16+9t^4}, \qquad y = -\frac{3t^2}{4}\,x = \frac{12\,t^5}{16+9t^4}. \tag{3}$$

### Eliminating the parameter

From (3),
$$\frac{y}{x} = \frac{12t^5}{-16t^3} = -\frac{3t^2}{4} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 t^2 = -\frac{4y}{3x}. \tag{4}$$

Also
$$x^2+y^2 = \frac{256t^6+144t^{10}}{(16+9t^4)^2} = \frac{16t^6(16+9t^4)}{(16+9t^
4)^2} = \frac{16\,t^6}{16+9t^4}. \tag{5}$$

Set $s=t^2=-\tfrac{4y}{3x}$. Then $t^4=s^2=\tfrac{16y^2}{9x^2}$ and $t^6=s^3=-\t
frac{64y^3}{27x^3}$. Substituting into (5):
$$x^2+y^2 = \frac{16\!\left(-\tfrac{64y^3}{27x^3}\right)}{16+9\!\left(\tfrac{16y
^2}{9x^2}\right)} = \frac{-\tfrac{1024\,y^3}{27x^3}}{\tfrac{16x^2+16y^2}{x^2}} =
 \frac{-\tfrac{1024\,y^3}{27x^3}\cdot x^2}{16(x^2+y^2)} = \frac{-64\,y^3}{27\,x\
,(x^2+y^2)}.$$

Multiplying both sides by $27\,x\,(x^2+y^2)$:
$$27\,x\,(x^2+y^2)^2 = -64\,y^3,$$

that is,
$$\boxed{\,27\,x\,(x^2+y^2)^2 + 64\,y^3 = 0\,}. \tag{6}$$

### Verification

Take $t=1$: from (3), $x=-16/25$, $y=12/25$, and $x^2+y^2=400/625=16/25$. Then
$$27\!\left(-\tfrac{16}{25}\right)\!\left(\tfrac{16}{25}\right)^{\!2} + 64\!\lef
t(\tfrac{12}{25}\right)^{\!3} = -\tfrac{110592}{15625}+\tfrac{110592}{15625}=0.
\;\checkmark$$

### Conclusion

The envelope of the family of circles through the origin with centre on $y^3=4x$
 is the curve

$$27\,x\,(x^2+y^2)^2 + 64\,y^3 = 0.$$
