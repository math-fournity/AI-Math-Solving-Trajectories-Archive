# Problem

In a remote forest, a circular wildlife sanctuary has been established with a radius of exactly 1 kilometer. A straight maintenance road, designated as the "Main Transit Axis," runs directly through the center of the sanctuary, connecting the North Gate (Point A) to the South Gate (Point B), effectively forming a diameter of the circle.

Inside the sanctuary, a ranger station (Point P) is located at a fixed position somewhere along this Main Transit Axis. A straight ecological corridor is built through this ranger station, extending in both directions until it hits the sanctuary's circular boundary at two observation outposts, designated as Site C and Site D. This configuration creates a four-sided patrol zone bounded by the straight lines connecting the North Gate, Site C, the South Gate, and Site D, forming a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$.

Given the fixed location of the ranger station $P$ on the axis $AB$, but allowing the ecological corridor $CD$ to rotate at any angle through $P$, what is the maximum possible area (in square kilometers) that the patrol zone $ABCD$ can encompass?
