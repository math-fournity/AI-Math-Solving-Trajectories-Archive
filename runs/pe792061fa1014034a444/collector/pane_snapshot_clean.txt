## Proof

Let $\pi: R \to R/\mathbf{a}$ be the natural quotient map with $\ker(\pi) = \mat
hbf{a}$. The pullback (inverse image) map on spectra is

$$\pi^*: \mathrm{Spec}(R/\mathbf{a}) \to \mathrm{Spec}(R), \qquad \mathfrak{q} \
mapsto \pi^{-1}(\mathfrak{q}).$$

We claim

$$\operatorname{im}(\pi^*) = V(\mathbf{a}) := \{\,\mathfrak{p} \in \mathrm{Spec}
(R) : \mathbf{a} \subseteq \mathfrak{p}\,\}.$$

**Inclusion $\operatorname{im}(\pi^*) \subseteq V(\mathbf{a})$.** Let $\mathfrak
{q} \in \mathrm{Spec}(R/\mathbf{a})$. Then $\pi^*(\mathfrak{q}) = \pi^{-1}(\math
frak{q})$ is a prime ideal of $R$ (the preimage of a prime ideal under a ring ho
momorphism is prime). Moreover, since $\mathbf{a} = \ker(\pi)$, every element of
 $\mathbf{a}$ maps to $0 \in \mathfrak{q}$, hence $\mathbf{a} \subseteq \pi^{-1}
(\mathfrak{q})$. Thus $\pi^*(\mathfrak{q}) \in V(\mathbf{a})$.

**Inclusion $V(\mathbf{a}) \subseteq \operatorname{im}(\pi^*)$.** Let $\mathfrak
{p} \in \mathrm{Spec}(R)$ with $\mathbf{a} \subseteq \mathfrak{p}$. Consider the
 image $\pi(\mathfrak{p}) = \mathfrak{p}/\mathbf{a} \subseteq R/\mathbf{a}$. Sin
ce $\mathbf{a} \subseteq \mathfrak{p}$, the quotient $\mathfrak{p}/\mathbf{a}$ i
s a well-defined ideal of $R/\mathbf{a}$. It is prime because

$$(R/\mathbf{a})/(\mathfrak{p}/\mathbf{a}) \cong R/\mathfrak{p},$$

which is an integral domain (as $\mathfrak{p}$ is prime). Hence $\mathfrak{q} :=
 \pi(\mathfrak{p}) \in \mathrm{Spec}(R/\mathbf{a})$. Finally,

$$\pi^{-1}(\mathfrak{q}) = \pi^{-1}(\pi(\mathfrak{p})) = \mathfrak{p},$$

where the last equality uses $\mathbf{a} = \ker(\pi) \subseteq \mathfrak{p}$: in
deed $\pi^{-1}(\pi(\mathfrak{p})) = \mathfrak{p} + \ker(\pi) = \mathfrak{p} + \m
athbf{a} = \mathfrak{p}$. Therefore $\mathfrak{p} = \pi^*(\mathfrak{q}) \in \ope
ratorname{im}(\pi^*)$.

Both inclusions give $\operatorname{im}(\pi^*) = V(\mathbf{a})$.

$$\boxed{\operatorname{im}(\pi^*) = V(\mathbf{a}) = \{\,\mathfrak{p} \in \mathrm
{Spec}(R) : \mathbf{a} \subseteq \mathfrak{p}\,\}}$$
