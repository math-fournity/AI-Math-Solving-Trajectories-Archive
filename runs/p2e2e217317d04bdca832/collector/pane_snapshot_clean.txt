## Proof

Let $H$ be a Hilbert space with inner product $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ and no
rm $\|\cdot\|$. Let
$$B = \{x \in H : \|x\| \le 1\}$$
be the closed unit ball, and let $p \in H$ be a point outside $B$, so $\|p\| > 1
$.

We seek
$$d = \min_{x \in B} \|p - x\|.$$

**Claim:** $d = \|p\| - 1$.

**Lower bound.** For any $x \in B$, we have $\|x\| \le 1$. By the triangle inequ
ality,
$$\|p\| = \|(p - x) + x\| \le \|p - x\| + \|x\| \le \|p - x\| + 1.$$

Rearranging gives
$$\|p - x\| \ge \|p\| - 1$$
for every $x \in B$. Hence $d \ge \|p\| - 1$.

**Achievability.** Set $x^* = \dfrac{p}{\|p\|}$. Then $\|x^*\| = 1$, so $x^* \in
 B$. Moreover,
$$\|p - x^*\| = \left\|p - \frac{p}{\|p\|}\right\| = \left\|p\left(1 - \frac{1}{
\|p\|}\right)\right\| = \|p\|\left(1 - \frac{1}{\|p\|}\right) = \|p\| - 1.$$

Thus the lower bound is attained, and
$$d = \min_{x \in B} \|p - x\| = \|p\| - 1.$$

**Remark on the equality condition.** The triangle inequality $\|a + b\| \le \|a
\| + \|b\|$ becomes equality in a Hilbert space precisely when $a$ and $b$ are n
on-negatively collinear (one is a non-negative scalar multiple of the other). In
 our case, with $a = p - x$ and $b = x$, equality requires $p - x = \lambda x$ f
or some $\lambda \ge 0$, i.e., $p = (1+\lambda)x$. This forces $x$ to be a posit
ive scalar multiple of $p$, and the constraint $\|x\| \le 1$ with the requiremen
t $\|x\| = 1$ (to saturate the second inequality) pins down the unique minimizer
 $x^* = p/\|p\|$ on the unit sphere. Hence the minimizer is unique.
