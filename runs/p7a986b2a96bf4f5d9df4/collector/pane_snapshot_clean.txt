## Solution

**Setup.** Each jar has 9 marbles. The color probabilities from each jar:

| Jar | P(R) | P(B) | P(G) |
|-----|------|------|------|
| #1  | 5/9  | 3/9  | 1/9  |
| #2  | 1/9  | 5/9  | 3/9  |
| #3  | 3/9  | 1/9  | 5/9  |

**Conditioning event.** "One marble of each color" means the three colors drawn
(one per jar) form a permutation of {R, B, G}. There are 3! = 6 such permutation
s. For each permutation (c₁, c₂, c₃), the joint probability is the product of th
e three single-jar probabilities (independent draws), all over denominator 9³ =
729:

| Permutation (J1,J2,J3) | Numerator |
|------------------------|-----------|
| (R, B, G) — **target** | 5·5·5 = 125 |
| (R, G, B) | 5·3·1 = 15 |
| (B, R, G) | 3·1·5 = 15 |
| (B, G, R) | 3·3·3 = 27 |
| (G, R, B) | 1·1·1 = 1 |
| (G, B, R) | 1·5·3 = 15 |

**Total** for the conditioning event: 125 + 15 + 15 + 27 + 1 + 15 = 198.

So P(one of each color) = 198/729, and P(target assignment ∧ one of each color)
= 125/729.

**Conditional probability:**

$$P(\text{target} \mid \text{one of each color}) = \frac{125/729}{198/729} = \fr
ac{125}{198}.$$

**Coprimality check.** 125 = 5³. 198 = 2 · 3² · 11. They share no common prime f
actor, so gcd(125, 198) = 1.

Thus m = 125, n = 198, and

$$m + n = 125 + 198 = \boxed{323}.$$
