### Closed form for $S(n)$

Let
$$T(n)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}(-1)^{k}(n+k)!(n-k)!,\qquad S(n)=T(n)-(n!)^{2}.$$

Using $\binom{2n}{n+k}=\dfrac{(2n)!}{(n+k)!(n-k)!}$, i.e. $(n+k)!(n-k)!=\dfrac{(
2n)!}{\binom{2n}{n+k}}$, and setting $j=n+k$:

$$T(n)=(2n)!\,(-1)^{n}\sum_{j=n}^{2n}\frac{(-1)^{j}}{\binom{2n}{j}}=(2n)!\,(-1)^
{n}\,U,\quad U:=\sum_{j=n}^{2n}\frac{(-1)^{j}}{\binom{2n}{j}}.$$

**Evaluating the full alternating sum.** Using $\dfrac{1}{\binom{n}{k}}=(n+1)\di
splaystyle\int_{0}^{1}t^{k}(1-t)^{n-k}\,dt$,

$$\sum_{k=0}^{n}\frac{(-1)^{k}}{\binom{n}{k}}=(n+1)\int_{0}^{1}\sum_{k=0}^{n}(1-
t)^{n-k}(-t)^{k}\,dt=(n+1)\int_{0}^{1}\!\bigl[(1-t)^{n+1}-(-t)^{n+1}\bigr]\,dt$$

$$=\frac{n+1}{n+2}\bigl(1-(-1)^{n+1}\bigr)=\frac{n+1}{n+2}\bigl(1+(-1)^{n}\bigr)
.$$

For $n\to 2n$ (even), this gives
$$A:=\sum_{j=0}^{2n}\frac{(-1)^{j}}{\binom{2n}{j}}=\frac{2n+1}{2n+2}\cdot 2=\fra
c{2n+1}{n+1}.$$

**Relating $U$ to $A$.** By symmetry $\binom{2n}{j}=\binom{2n}{2n-j}$ and $(-1)^
{2n-j}=(-1)^{j}$, so $\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}=\sum_{j=n+1}^{2n}$. Hence
$$A=2\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\frac{(-1)^{j}}{\binom{2n}{j}}+\frac{(-1)^{n}}{\binom{2n}{n
}},\qquad U=\frac{(-1)^{n}}{\binom{2n}{n}}+\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\frac{(-1)^{j}}{\bino
m{2n}{j}}=\frac{A+(-1)^{n}/\binom{2n}{n}}{2}.$$

Therefore
$$T(n)=(2n)!\,(-1)^{n}\cdot\frac{A+(-1)^{n}/\binom{2n}{n}}{2}=\frac{(2n)!\,(-1)^
{n}(2n+1)}{2(n+1)}+\frac{(n!)^{2}}{2},$$

and
$$\boxed{S(n)=\frac{(-1)^{n}(2n)!(2n+1)}{2(n+1)}-\frac{(n!)^{2}}{2}}.$$

**Verification on small cases.** $S(1)=-2$, $S(2)=18$, $S(3)=-648$ — all match d
irect computation.

### Evaluating $S(10)$

With $n=10$ (so $(-1)^{10}=1$):
$$S(10)=\frac{20!\cdot 21}{22}-\frac{(10!)^{2}}{2}.$$

- $20! = 2\,432\,902\,008\,176\,640\,000$.
- $20!/22 = 110\,586\,454\,917\,120\,000$, so $20!\cdot 21/22 = 110\,586\,454\,9
17\,120\,000\times 21 = 2\,322\,316\,553\,259\,520\,000$.
- $10! = 3\,628\,800$, $(10!)^{2}=13\,168\,189\,440\,000$, $(10!)^{2}/2 = 6\,584
\,094\,720\,000$.

$$S(10)=2\,322\,316\,553\,259\,520\,000-6\,584\,094\,720\,000=2\,322\,309\,969\,
164\,800\,000.$$
