**Answer: Yes.** A rational prime $p$ dividing $d_K$ necessarily ramifies in $\m
athscr{O}_K$. This is the content of the Dedekind discriminant theorem: $p$ rami
fies in $\mathscr{O}_K$ if and only if $p \mid d_K$.

---

## Proof

Let $n = [K:\mathbb{Q}]$ and let $\{\alpha_1,\dots,\alpha_n\}$ be an integral ba
sis of $\mathscr{O}_K$. The discriminant is

$$d_K = \det\!\big(\operatorname{Tr}_{K/\mathbb{Q}}(\alpha_i\alpha_j)\big)_{1\le
 i,j\le n}.$$

**Step 1: $p \mid d_K$ iff the trace form mod $p$ is degenerate.**

The matrix $M = (\operatorname{Tr}(\alpha_i\alpha_j))$ has entries in $\mathbb{Z
}$ and $d_K = \det M$. Reducing mod $p$, the form $\bar B(\bar x,\bar y) = \over
line{\operatorname{Tr}(xy)}$ on $\mathscr{O}_K/p\mathscr{O}_K$ is represented by
 $\bar M$. Thus $p \mid d_K \iff \det \bar M = 0 \iff \bar B$ is degenerate.

**Step 2: Decomposition via CRT.**

Let $p\mathscr{O}_K = \prod_{i=1}^{g} \mathfrak{p}_i^{e_i}$ with residue degrees
 $f_i = [\mathscr{O}_K/\mathfrak{p}_i : \mathbb{F}_p]$, so $\sum e_i f_i = n$. B
y the Chinese Remainder Theorem,

$$\mathscr{O}_K/p\mathscr{O}_K \;\cong\; \prod_{i=1}^{g} A_i, \qquad A_i := \mat
hscr{O}_K/\mathfrak{p}_i^{e_i}.$$

The trace form $\bar B$ on $\mathscr{O}_K/p\mathscr{O}_K$ is the orthogonal dire
ct sum of the trace forms $B_i := \operatorname{Tr}_{A_i/\mathbb{F}_p}$ on each
factor. Hence $\bar B$ is non-degenerate $\iff$ every $B_i$ is non-degenerate.

**Step 3: Factor the trace.**

Each $A_i$ is an $\mathbb{F}_{p^{f_i}}$-algebra (its residue field is $\mathbb{F
}_{p^{f_i}}$), and

$$\operatorname{Tr}_{A_i/\mathbb{F}_p} = \operatorname{Tr}_{\mathbb{F}_{p^{f_i}}
/\mathbb{F}_p} \circ \operatorname{Tr}_{A_i/\mathbb{F}_{p^{f_i}}}.$$

Since $\mathbb{F}_{p^{f_i}}/\mathbb{F}_p$ is separable, $\operatorname{Tr}_{\mat
hbb{F}_{p^{f_i}}/\mathbb{F}_p}$ is non-degenerate. Therefore $B_i$ is non-degene
rate $\iff$ $\operatorname{Tr}_{A_i/\mathbb{F}_{p^{f_i}}}$ is non-degenerate.

**Step 4: The local trace form is non-degenerate iff $e_i = 1$.**

- **If $e_i = 1$:** Then $A_i = \mathscr{O}_K/\mathfrak{p}_i \cong \mathbb{F}_{p
^{f_i}}$, a field. The trace $\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathbb{F}_{p^{f_i}}/\mathbb{F}
_{p^{f_i}}}$ is the identity, hence non-degenerate.

- **If $e_i \ge 2$:** Let $\mathfrak{m}_i = \mathfrak{p}_i/\mathfrak{p}_i^{e_i}$
 be the maximal ideal of $A_i$; it is nonzero. For any $x \in \mathfrak{m}_i$ an
d any $y \in A_i$, the product $xy \in \mathfrak{m}_i$. Multiplication by $xy$ o
n $A_i$ (as an $\mathbb{F}_{p^{f_i}}$-vector space) is a nilpotent endomorphism,
 since $\mathfrak{m}_i^{e_i} = 0$. A nilpotent linear map has trace $0$, so

$$\operatorname{Tr}_{A_i/\mathbb{F}_{p^{f_i}}}(xy) = 0 \quad \text{for all } y \
in A_i.$$

Thus every $x \in \mathfrak{m}_i$ lies in the radical of the trace form. Since $
\mathfrak{m}_i \neq 0$ when $e_i \ge 2$, the form $\operatorname{Tr}_{A_i/\mathb
b{F}_{p^{f_i}}}$ is degenerate.

**Step 5: Conclusion.**

Combining Steps 1–4:

$$p \mid d_K \iff \bar B \text{ is degenerate} \iff \exists\, i \text{ with } B_
i \text{ degenerate} \iff \exists\, i \text{ with } e_i \ge 2 \iff p \text{ rami
fies in } \mathscr{O}_K.$$

In particular, $p \mid d_K \Longrightarrow p$ ramifies in $\mathscr{O}_K$. $\bla
cksquare$
