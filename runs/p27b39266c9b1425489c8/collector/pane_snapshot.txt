## Proof

Let the towers be $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_{100}$ in circular order, with the condit
ion $a_i > a_{i+1} + a_{i+2}$ for all $i$ (indices mod 100).

### Upper Bound: At most 49 positive towers

**Step 1: At least one tower is negative.**

Summing all 100 inequalities: $\sum_{i=1}^{100} a_i > \sum_{i=1}^{100}(a_{i+1}+a
_{i+2}) = 2\sum_{i=1}^{100} a_i$, so $\sum a_i < 0$. Thus at least one tower is
strictly negative.

**Step 2: Negatives are at most distance 2 apart.**

If $a_i < 0$, then $a_{i+1} + a_{i+2} < a_i < 0$, so at least one of $a_{i+1}, a
_{i+2}$ is negative. Therefore, going clockwise from any negative tower, the nex
t negative tower is at distance 1 or 2.

**Step 3: At least 50 negatives are needed.**

Let $N$ be the number of negative towers. The $N$ gaps between consecutive negat
ives (circularly) each have size 1 or 2 and sum to 100. Since each gap $\leq 2$:
 $100 \leq 2N$, so $N \geq 50$.

**Step 4: $N = 50$ is impossible.**

If $N = 50$, all 50 gaps equal 2, so negatives occupy every other position. For
each negative $a_i$ (with $a_{i+1} \geq 0$ and $a_{i+2} < 0$): $a_i > a_{i+1} +
a_{i+2} \geq a_{i+2}$, giving $a_i > a_{i+2}$. This yields a strict cyclic chain
 $a_1 > a_3 > a_5 > \cdots > a_{99} > a_1$, which is impossible.

Therefore $N \geq 51$, and the number of positive towers is at most $100 - 51 =
\boxed{49}$.

### Construction: 49 positive towers are achievable

Arrange the 100 towers in a circle with 49 positive towers (value 1) and 51 nega
tive towers:

secutive pair.
- A run of 3 consecutive negatives: $a = -199$, $b = -99$, $c = -101$.
- A positive tower (value 1) between $z_{48}$ and $a$, and between $c$ and $z_1$
.

**Circular order:** $z_1, 1, z_2, 1, \ldots, 1, z_{48}, 1, a, b, c, 1, [z_1]$.

**Verification of all conditions** $a_i > a_{i+1} + a_{i+2}$:

| Position type | Condition | Check |

All 100 conditions are satisfied with 49 towers having positive signal strength.

### Key insight

The run of 3 consecutive negatives breaks the strict cyclic chain. While gap-2 t
ransitions force $a_i > a_{i+2}$ (strict decrease through the chain), the condit
ion at the first of three consecutive negatives, $a > b + c$, does **not** requi
re $a > b$ (since $c < 0$ makes $b + c < b$). This breaks the cycle that would o
therwise make the configuration impossible.
