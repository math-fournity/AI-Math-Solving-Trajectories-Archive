## Proof

Let $\mathfrak{h}_n$ be the Heisenberg Lie algebra with basis $x_1,\dots,x_n, y_
1,\dots,y_n, c$ and brackets $[x_i,y_j]=\delta_{ij}c$, all others zero. The cent
er is $Z(\mathfrak{h}_n) = \mathrm{span}\{c\}$, which is 1-dimensional.

**Step 1: Reduction to a symplectic condition.**

Let $\pi: \mathfrak{h}_n \to \mathfrak{h}_n / Z(\mathfrak{h}_n) \cong \mathbb{R}
^{2n}$ be the quotient map. The Lie bracket descends to a bilinear form $\omega$
 on $\mathbb{R}^{2n}$ defined by
$$[u, v] = \omega(\pi(u), \pi(v))\, c.$$
A direct check shows $\omega(x_i, y_j) = \delta_{ij}$ and $\omega(x_i,x_j)=\omeg
a(y_i,y_j)=0$, so $\omega$ is the standard symplectic form on $\mathbb{R}^{2n}$.

A subspace $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq \mathfrak{h}_n$ is an abelian Lie subalgebra
iff $[\mathfrak{a}, \mathfrak{a}] = 0$, which holds iff $\omega(\pi(u), \pi(v))
= 0$ for all $u, v \in \mathfrak{a}$, i.e., $\pi(\mathfrak{a})$ is an **isotropi
c** subspace of $(\mathbb{R}^{2n}, \omega)$.

**Step 2: Upper bound.**

We have
$$\dim \mathfrak{a} = \dim \pi(\mathfrak{a}) + \dim(\mathfrak{a} \cap Z(\mathfra
k{h}_n)).$$
Since $Z(\mathfrak{h}_n)$ is 1-dimensional, $\dim(\mathfrak{a} \cap Z(\mathfrak{
h}_n)) \leq 1$. Since $\pi(\mathfrak{a})$ is isotropic in a $2n$-dimensional sym
plectic space, $\dim \pi(\mathfrak{a}) \leq n$ (a Lagrangian is the maximal isot
ropic subspace). Therefore
$$\dim \mathfrak{a} \leq n + 1.$$

**Step 3: Achieving the bound.**

Take $\mathfrak{a} = \mathrm{span}\{x_1, \dots, x_n, c\}$. This has dimension $n
+1$. Since $[x_i, x_j] = 0$ for all $i,j$ and $[x_i, c] = 0$ for all $i$, this s
ubspace is abelian.

**Conclusion.** The maximal possible dimension of an abelian Lie subalgebra of $
\mathfrak{h}_n$ is $\boxed{n+1}$.
