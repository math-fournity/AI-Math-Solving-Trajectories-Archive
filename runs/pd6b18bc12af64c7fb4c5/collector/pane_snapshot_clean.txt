**Answer: No.** Two finitely generated abelian groups with isomorphic torsion su
bgroups need not be isomorphic.

## Proof

Recall the structure theorem for finitely generated abelian groups: every finite
ly generated abelian group $G$ decomposes as

$$G \cong \mathbb{Z}^{r} \oplus T(G),$$

where $r = \operatorname{rank}(G)$ is a non-negative integer (the *free rank* of
 $G$) and $T(G)$ is the torsion subgroup of $G$. Both $r$ and the isomorphism ty
pe of $T(G)$ are invariants of $G$; in particular, $r$ is the rank of the free a
belian quotient $G/T(G)$.

Now consider the two groups

$$G = \mathbb{Z}, \qquad H = \mathbb{Z}^{2}.$$

Both are finitely generated abelian groups. Their torsion subgroups are

$$T(G) = \{0\}, \qquad T(H) = \{0\},$$

so $T(G) \cong T(H)$ (both are trivial).

However, $G \not\cong H$, since $G$ has rank $1$ while $H$ has rank $2$. Concret
ely, $G$ is cyclic (generated by a single element) whereas $H$ is not cyclic — t
here is no single element of $\mathbb{Z}^{2}$ that generates the whole group.

Thus we have exhibited two finitely generated abelian groups with the same (isom
orphic) torsion subgroups that are not isomorphic. The torsion subgroup alone do
es not determine the isomorphism type; the free rank $r$ is an independent invar
iant. $\blacksquare$
