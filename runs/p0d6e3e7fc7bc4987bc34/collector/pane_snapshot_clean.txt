## Proof

Let $H$ be a Hilbert space and let $\mathfrak{F} = \bigoplus_{n=0}^{\infty} \mat
hfrak{F}_n$ be the fermionic Fock space, where $\mathfrak{F}_n = \bigwedge^n H$
is the $n$-particle sector (with $\mathfrak{F}_0 = \mathbb{C}\Omega$, $\Omega$ t
he vacuum). The creation operator $c^*(f)$, for $f \in H$, is defined on homogen
eous $n$-particle vectors by
$$c^*(f)(\psi_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge \psi_n) = f \wedge \psi_1 \wedge \cdots \we
dge \psi_n,$$
and extended linearly to all of $\mathfrak{F}$.

### Step 1. The Canonical Anticommutation Relation (CAR)

The annihilation operator $c(f)$ is the adjoint $c(f) = c^*(f)^*$, and the pair
satisfies the CAR:
$$\{c(f), c^*(g)\} := c(f)c^*(g) + c^*(g)c(f) = \langle f, g\rangle_H \cdot I.$$
In particular, taking $g = f$:
$$c(f)c^*(f) + c^*(f)c(f) = \|f\|^2 \, I. \tag{1}$$

### Step 2. Upper bound: $\|c^*(f)\| \le \|f\|$

For any $\Psi \in \mathfrak{F}$, using (1):
$$c(f)c^*(f)\Psi = \|f\|^2 \Psi - c^*(f)c(f)\Psi.$$
Taking the inner product with $\Psi$:
$$\|c^*(f)\Psi\|^2 = \langle \Psi, c(f)c^*(f)\Psi\rangle = \|f\|^2\|\Psi\|^2 - \
|c(f)\Psi\|^2.$$
Since $\|c(f)\Psi\|^2 \ge 0$, we obtain
$$\|c^*(f)\Psi\|^2 \le \|f\|^2 \|\Psi\|^2,$$
hence $\|c^*(f)\| \le \|f\|$.

### Step 3. Lower bound: $\|c^*(f)\| \ge \|f\|$

Apply $c^*(f)$ to the vacuum $\Omega \in \mathfrak{F}_0$ (with $\|\Omega\| = 1$)
. By definition $c^*(f)\Omega = f \in \mathfrak{F}_1 \cong H$, so
$$\|c^*(f)\Omega\| = \|f\| = \|f\|\cdot\|\Omega\|.$$
Therefore $\|c^*(f)\| \ge \|f\|$.

### Step 4. Conclusion

Combining Steps 2 and 3:
$$\boxed{\|c^*(f)\| = \|f\|_H.}$$

Thus the fermionic creation operator $c^*(f)$ **is** a bounded linear operator o
n $\mathfrak{F}$, and its operator norm equals the Hilbert-space norm of $f$.
