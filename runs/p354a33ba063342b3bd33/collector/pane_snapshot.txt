## Solution

**Setup.** Let $\mathbb{G} = \operatorname{Gr}(2, n+1)$ be the Grassmannian para
metrizing lines in $\mathbb{P}^n$, so
$$\dim \mathbb{G} = 2(n-1).$$
Let $N = \binom{n+2}{2} - 1$ be the dimension of the projective space $\mathbb{P
}(H^0(\mathbb{P}^n, \mathcal{O}(2)))$ parametrizing quadric hypersurfaces.

**Incidence correspondence.** Define
$$I = \bigl\{([L],[Q]) \in \mathbb{G} \times \mathbb{P}(H^0(\mathcal{O}(2))) : L
 \subseteq Q\bigr\}.$$

**Computing $\dim I$ via projection to $\mathbb{G}$.** Fix a line $L \cong \math
bb{P}^1$. The condition $L \subseteq Q$ means the quadric form restricts to zero
 on $L$. Restricting a section of $\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^n}(2)$ to $L$ gives a
 section of $\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^1}(2)$, which has $h^0(\mathbb{P}^1, \mathc
al{O}(2)) = 3$ coefficients. The restriction map
$$H^0(\mathbb{P}^n, \mathcal{O}(2)) \longrightarrow H^0(\mathbb{P}^1, \mathcal{O
}(2))$$
is surjective (since $H^1(\mathbb{P}^n, \mathcal{I}_L(2)) = 0$ for a line $L$),
so the vanishing imposes exactly **3 independent linear conditions** on the quad
ric. The fiber over $[L]$ is thus a projective space of dimension $N - 3$. There
fore:
$$\dim I = \dim \mathbb{G} + (N-3) = 2(n-1) + N - 3 = N + 2n - 5.$$

**Projection to the space of quadrics.** For $n \geq 3$, the quadric has dimensi
on $m = n-1 \geq 2$. Every smooth quadric $Q^m \subseteq \mathbb{P}^{m+1}$ conta
ins a linear subspace of dimension $\lfloor m/2 \rfloor \geq 1$, hence contains
a line. (For $m=2$: $Q^2 \cong \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$ has two rulings
 of lines; for $m \geq 3$ the maximal linear spaces have dimension $\geq 1$.) Si
nce smooth quadrics form a dense open subset of the space of all quadrics, the p
rojection $I \to \mathbb{P}(H^0(\mathcal{O}(2)))$ is **dominant**.

**Dimension of the Fano scheme.** For a general quadric $[Q]$, the fiber of the
projection is the Fano scheme $F(Q)$ of lines on $Q$. By dominance:
$$\dim F(Q) = \dim I - N = (N + 2n - 5) - N = 2n - 5.$$

**Verification on small cases.**
- $n=3$ (quadric surface in $\mathbb{P}^3$): $F(Q)$ is two copies of $\mathbb{P}
^1$, dimension $1 = 2(3)-5$. ✓
- $n=4$ (quadric threefold in $\mathbb{P}^4$): $F(Q) \cong \mathbb{P}^3$, dimens
ion $3 = 2(4)-5$. ✓
- $n=2$ (conic in $\mathbb{P}^2$): a general conic contains no lines; $F(Q) = \e
mptyset$, consistent with $2(2)-5 = -1$. ✓

**Conclusion.** For $n \geq 3$, the dimension of the moduli space of lines on a
general quadric hypersurface in $\mathbb{P}^n$ is

$$\boxed{2n - 5}.$$
