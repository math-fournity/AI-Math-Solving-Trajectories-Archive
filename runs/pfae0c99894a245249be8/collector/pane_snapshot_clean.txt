## Proof

Let $\omega = z_N$ be a primitive $N$-th root of unity, so $\omega^N = 1$ and $\
omega \neq 1$.

**Setup.** We have $N = r \cdot t$, and $H^\bot = \{0,\, t,\, 2t,\, \ldots,\, (r
-1)t\} \subset \mathbb{Z}_N$, the set of multiples of $t$ modulo $N$. Take any $
x \in \{0, 1, \ldots, N-1\}$ with $x \notin H^\bot$.

**Key observation.** Since $0 \le x < N = rt$, the multiples of $t$ in this rang
e are exactly $0, t, 2t, \ldots, (r-1)t$. Hence
$$x \notin H^\bot \iff t \nmid x.$$

**Computing the sum.** The sum
$$S = \sum_{i=0}^{t-1} \omega^{rxi}$$
is a finite geometric series with common ratio $\omega^{rx}$.

Since $t \nmid x$, we have $rt \nmid rx$, i.e., $N \nmid rx$, so $\omega^{rx} \n
eq 1$. Therefore the geometric series formula applies:
$$S = \frac{1 - (\omega^{rx})^t}{1 - \omega^{rx}} = \frac{1 - \omega^{rxt}}{1 -
\omega^{rx}}.$$

**The numerator vanishes.** Since $rxt = x \cdot (rt) = x \cdot N$,
$$\omega^{rxt} = \omega^{xN} = (\omega^N)^x = 1^x = 1.$$

Hence $1 - \omega^{rxt} = 0$.

**The denominator is nonzero.** As established above, $t \nmid x$ implies $N \nm
id rx$, so $\omega^{rx} \neq 1$, giving $1 - \omega^{rx} \neq 0$.

**Conclusion.** Combining both facts:
$$S = \frac{1 - \omega^{rxt}}{1 - \omega^{rx}} = \frac{0}{1 - \omega^{rx}} = 0.$
$

Therefore, for any $x \notin H^\bot$,
$$\sum_{i=0}^{t-1} z_N^{rxi} = 0. \qquad \blacksquare$$
