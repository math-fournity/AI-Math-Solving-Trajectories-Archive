Looking at the problem, I need to find the greatest length *n* of a sequence of
consecutive natural numbers where none has a digit sum divisible by 5.

**Answer: n = 8**

## Proof

**Setup.** Let *s*(*m*) denote the digit sum of *m*. We seek the longest run of
consecutive integers *a*, *a*+1, …, *a*+*n*−1 with *s*(*a*+*i*) ≢ 0 (mod 5) for
all *i*.

**Key lemma (carry step).** If *m* has exactly *k* trailing 9s, then *s*(*m*+1)
= *s*(*m*) + 1 − 9*k*, so modulo 5:
$$s(m+1) \equiv s(m) + (k+1) \pmod{5},$$
since −9*k* ≡ *k* (mod 5). For a normal step (no carry, *k*=0), the digit sum in
creases by 1 mod 5.

---

### Upper bound: no run of 9 exists

Consider any 9 consecutive integers *n*, *n*+1, …, *n*+8. Exactly one of them en
ds in 9; call it *n*+*j* (0 ≤ *j* ≤ 8), and let *k* ≥ 1 be its number of trailin
g 9s. Set *a* = *s*(*n*) mod 5.

The digit sums mod 5 split into two contiguous blocks:

- **Before the carry** (positions 0 to *j*): *a*, *a*+1, …, *a*+*j* — this is *j
*+1 consecutive residues mod 5.
- **After the carry** (positions *j*+1 to 8): *a*+*j*+*k*+1, *a*+*j*+*k*+2, …, *
a*+*k*+8 — this is 8−*j* consecutive residues mod 5.

For **none** of these 9 values to be 0 mod 5, we need:
- The first block (*j*+1 residues) to avoid 0: requires *j*+1 ≤ 4, i.e., ***j* ≤
 3**.
- The second block (8−*j* residues) to avoid 0: requires 8−*j* ≤ 4, i.e., ***j*
≥ 4**.

These two conditions are **mutually exclusive**. Therefore, in every set of 9 co
nsecutive integers, at least one has digit sum ≡ 0 (mod 5). The maximum run leng
th is at most **8**.

---

### Lower bound: a run of 8 exists

Take the 8 consecutive integers **6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13**:

| *m* | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 | 11 | 12 | 13 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *s*(*m*) | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| *s*(*m*) mod 5 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |

None is divisible by 5. ✓

The mechanism: at 9 → 10, a carry occurs (*k*=1), causing the digit sum to jump
from 9 (≡ 4) to 1 (≡ 1), **skipping 0 mod 5**. This single skip is what allows t
he run to extend beyond the usual maximum of 4 (within a single decade, digit su
ms mod 5 cycle through all 5 residues, hitting 0 every 5 steps).

---

### Conclusion

The greatest length is **n = 8**.
