## Solution

We need positive integers $A, B, C, D$ such that the pair sums are strictly decr
easing in the order:

$$2A > A+B > A+C > 2B > B+C > A+D > 2C > B+D > C+D > 2D$$

**Deriving the constraints.** Simplifying consecutive inequalities:

| Inequality | Simplifies to |
|---|---|
| $2A > A+B$ | $A > B$ |
| $A+B > A+C$ | $B > C$ |
| $A+C > 2B$ | $A+C > 2B$ |
| $2B > B+C$ | $B > C$ (redundant) |
| $B+C > A+D$ | $B+C > A+D$ |
| $A+D > 2C$ | $A+D > 2C$ |
| $2C > B+D$ | $2C > B+D$ |
| $B+D > C+D$ | $B > C$ (redundant) |
| $C+D > 2D$ | $C > D$ |

So we need: $A > B > C > D \geq 1$, plus four non-trivial constraints:
$$A+C > 2B, \quad B+C > A+D, \quad A+D > 2C, \quad 2C > B+D.$$

**Searching for the minimal solution (smallest $D$ first).**

**$D=1, C=2$:** $2C > B+D \Rightarrow 4 > B+1 \Rightarrow B \leq 2$, but $B > C
= 2$. ✗

**$D=1, C=3$:** $2C > B+D \Rightarrow B \leq 4$, so $B = 4$. Then $A+C > 2B \Rig
htarrow A \geq 6$, but $B+C > A+D \Rightarrow 7 > A+1 \Rightarrow A \leq 5$. Con
tradiction. ✗

**$D=1, C=4$:** $B \leq 6$, so $B \in \{5,6\}$.
- $B=5$: $A+D > 2C \Rightarrow A \geq 8$; $B+C > A+D \Rightarrow A \leq 7$. ✗
- $B=6$: $A \geq 9$ (from $A+C > 2B$); $B+C > A+D \Rightarrow A \leq 8$. ✗

**$D=1, C=5$:** $2C > B+D \Rightarrow B \leq 8$, so $B \in \{6,7,8\}$.
- $A+D > 2C \Rightarrow A \geq 10$.
- $B+C > A+D \Rightarrow A \leq B+3$.
- $A+C > 2B \Rightarrow A > 2B - 5$.
