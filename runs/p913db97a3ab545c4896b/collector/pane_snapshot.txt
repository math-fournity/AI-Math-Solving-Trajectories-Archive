**Step 1: $p_1$ is never a positive integer.**

We have $p_1 = x_1 - \frac{1}{x_1} = \frac{x_1^2-1}{x_1}$. For this to be a posi

, this forces $x_1 \mid 1$, so $x_1 = 1$, but then $p_1 = 0$, which is not posit
ive. Hence $p_1$ is never a positive integer, giving an upper bound of **2023**
positive integers among $p_1,\dots,p_{2024}$.

**Step 2: The choice $x_k = k+1$ (i.e. $2,3,4,\dots,2025$) attains this bound.**

With $x_k = k+1$,
$$x_k - \frac{1}{x_k} = \frac{(k+1)^2-1}{k+1} = \frac{k(k+2)}{k+1}.$$
Therefore
$$p_i = \prod_{k=1}^{i}\frac{k(k+2)}{k+1} = \frac{\left(\prod_{k=1}^i k\right)\l

i+1)!} = \frac{i!\,(i+2)}{2}.$$

Thus $p_2, p_3, \dots, p_{2024}$ are all positive integers — that is, **2023** o
