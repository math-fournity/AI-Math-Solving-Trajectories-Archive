## Solution

Let
$$F(x,y) = (x^2+y^2)^3 - 4x^2y^2, \qquad G(x,y) = x,$$
and let $P = (0,0)$. The intersection number is
$$I_P(F,G) = \dim_k\, \mathcal{O}_P/(F,G), \qquad \mathcal{O}_P = k[x,y]_{(x,y)}
.$$

**Step 1. Reduce modulo the smooth factor $G = x$.**

The line $G = x$ is smooth at $P$, and $x$ does not divide $F$ since $F(0,y) = y
^6 \neq 0$. Hence $F$ and $G$ share no common component through $P$, the interse
ction is proper, and we may compute
$$I_P(F,G) = \dim_k\, k[[x,y]]/(F, x) = \dim_k\, k[[y]]/\bigl(F(0,y)\bigr).$$

**Step 2. Substitute $x = 0$ into $F$.**
$$F(0,y) = (y^2)^3 - 4\cdot 0\cdot y^2 = y^6.$$

Therefore
$$I_P(F,G) = \dim_k\, k[[y]]/(y^6) = 6,$$
since $\{1, y, y^2, y^3, y^4, y^5\}$ is a $k$-basis of the quotient.

**Step 3. Sanity check via the tangent cone.**

The lowest-degree homogeneous part of $F$ is $-4x^2y^2$ (degree $4$), so $\opera
torname{mult}_P(F) = 4$ and the tangent cone is $x^2y^2 = 0$, giving the tangent
 lines $x = 0$ (double) and $y = 0$ (double). Since $G = x$ is a tangent line (a
ppearing with multiplicity $2$ in the tangent cone of $F$), the general lower bo
und $I_P(F,G) \ge \operatorname{mult}_P(F)\operatorname{mult}_P(G) = 4\cdot 1 =
4$ is strict, and the excess equals the contribution of the shared tangent direc
tion. The exact value $6 = 4 + 2$ is consistent with $x$ occurring to order $2$
in the tangent cone of $F$.

Equivalently, in polar coordinates the curve is $r = |\sin 2\theta|$ (a quadrifo
lium), and the line $x = 0$ (i.e.\ $\theta = \pi/2,\,3\pi/2$) meets each of the
two branches tangent to it with multiplicity $3$, totaling $6$.

$$\boxed{I_P(F,G) = 6}$$
