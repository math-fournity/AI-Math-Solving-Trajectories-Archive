## Proof

**Claim:** Yes. Every vector bundle on $\operatorname{Spec} A$ is trivial.

### Setup

Let $A$ be a local Artin $k$-algebra with residue field $k$ (algebraically close
d), and let $\mathfrak{m}$ be its unique maximal ideal. Since $A$ is Artinian an
d local, $\operatorname{Spec} A = \{\mathfrak{m}\}$ is a single point.

A vector bundle on $\operatorname{Spec} A$ is, by the standard equivalence betwe
en vector bundles on an affine scheme and finitely generated projective modules,
 a finitely generated projective $A$-module $P$. The vector bundle is *trivial*
precisely when $P$ is a free $A$-module.

Thus the question reduces to:

> **Is every finitely generated projective module over the local ring $A$ free?*
*

We prove this for **any** local ring (so in particular for our $A$).

### Theorem (Kaplansky / classical)

*Let $R$ be a local ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$ and residue field $\k
appa = R/\mathfrak{m}$. Every finitely generated projective $R$-module is free.*

### Proof of the Theorem

Let $P$ be a finitely generated projective $R$-module. Since $P$ is projective a
nd finitely generated, $P$ is a direct summand of a finite free module: there ex
ists $Q$ with
$$R^n \cong P \oplus Q$$
for some $n$.

**Step 1: Reduce modulo $\mathfrak{m}$.** Tensoring with $\kappa = R/\mathfrak{m
}$ (which is right-exact, and exact on split sequences), we obtain
$$\kappa^n \cong (P/\mathfrak{m}P) \oplus (Q/\mathfrak{m}Q).$$
Set $r = \dim_\kappa(P/\mathfrak{m}P)$ and $s = \dim_\kappa(Q/\mathfrak{m}Q)$, s
o $r + s = n$.

**Step 2: Lift bases via Nakayama's Lemma.** Choose a $\kappa$-basis $\bar{p}_1,
 \dots, \bar{p}_r$ of $P/\mathfrak{m}P$ and lift each $\bar{p}_i$ to $p_i \in P$
. By Nakayama's Lemma (applicable since $P$ is finitely generated and $\mathfrak
{m}$ is the Jacobson radical), the elements $p_1, \dots, p_r$ generate $P$. Thus
 we have a surjection
$$\varphi: R^r \twoheadrightarrow P, \quad e_i \mapsto p_i.$$

Similarly, lift a basis of $Q/\mathfrak{m}Q$ to obtain a surjection
$$\psi: R^s \twoheadrightarrow Q.$$

**Step 3: Combine into an endomorphism of $R^n$.** Form the map
$$\Phi = \varphi \oplus \psi : R^r \oplus R^s = R^n \longrightarrow P \oplus Q \
cong R^n.$$
This is an $R$-linear endomorphism of $R^n$.

**Step 4: $\Phi$ is an isomorphism.** Reducing $\Phi$ modulo $\mathfrak{m}$ give
s a $\kappa$-linear map
$$\bar\Phi : \kappa^n \to \kappa^n.$$
By construction, $\bar\varphi$ sends the standard basis of $\kappa^r$ to the cho
sen basis $\bar{p}_1, \dots, \bar{p}_r$ of $P/\mathfrak{m}P$, and $\bar\psi$ sen
ds the standard basis of $\kappa^s$ to a basis of $Q/\mathfrak{m}Q$. Since $\kap
pa^n = (P/\mathfrak{m}P) \oplus (Q/\mathfrak{m}Q)$, the map $\bar\Phi$ is an iso
morphism of $\kappa^n$.

Therefore $\det(\bar\Phi) \in \kappa^\times$, which means $\det(\Phi) \notin \ma
thfrak{m}$, i.e., $\det(\Phi) \in R^\times$ (since $R$ is local, $R^\times = R \
setminus \mathfrak{m}$). Hence $\Phi$ is invertible, i.e., $\Phi$ is an isomorph
ism.

**Step 5: Conclude $P$ is free.** Since $\Phi: R^n \xrightarrow{\sim} R^n$ is an
 isomorphism and $\Phi$ maps the summand $R^r \subseteq R^n$ **onto** $P$ (becau
se $\varphi$ is surjective onto $P$) and $R^s$ onto $Q$, the restriction
$$\varphi = \Phi\big|_{R^r} : R^r \xrightarrow{\;\sim\;} P$$
is an isomorphism. (Explicitly: $\Phi$ is an isomorphism, so $\Phi^{-1}$ exists;
 the preimage of $P$ under $\Phi$ is $R^r$, giving $R^r \cong P$.)

Therefore $P \cong R^r$ is free of rank $r$. $\quad\blacksquare$

### Application to the Problem

Since $A$ is a local ring (being a local Artin $k$-algebra), the theorem applies
: every finitely generated projective $A$-module is free. By the equivalence bet
ween vector bundles on the affine scheme $\operatorname{Spec} A$ and finitely ge
nerated projective $A$-modules, every vector bundle on $\operatorname{Spec} A$ i
s of the form $\widetilde{A^r}$ for some $r \geq 0$, i.e., **trivial**.

### Remark on the hypotheses

The algebraically closed hypothesis on $k$ and the Artinian hypothesis on $A$ ar
e not needed for the conclusion. The result holds for **any** local ring $R$: ev
ery finitely generated projective $R$-module is free. The hypotheses in the prob
lem ($A$ local, Artinian, $k$-algebra, residue field $k$ algebraically closed) s
imply ensure that $A$ is local, which is the only property used.
