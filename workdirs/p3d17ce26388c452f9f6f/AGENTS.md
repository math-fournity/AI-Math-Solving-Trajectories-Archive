# Problem

A prestigious tech summit hosts a series of networking conferences for different group sizes, denoted by $n$, where $n$ ranges from 3 to 100. In each conference, every participant shakes hands exactly once with every other participant, forming a complete network of $n$ people. 

To track interactions, the organizers assign each handshake a specific "Topic Category." For a conference of size $n$, there are exactly $n-1$ available topics. A participant is designated as a "Universal Communicator" if they have engaged in at least one handshake for every one of the $n-1$ topics.

Let $R(n)$ represent the maximum possible number of Universal Communicators that can exist in a conference of $n$ people, given the most strategic distribution of topics among the handshakes. 

Calculate the total sum of these maximums across all conference sizes:
$$\sum_{n=3}^{100} R(n)$$
