## Proof

**Setup.** Place the cube with vertices at the sign-pattern points $(\varepsilon
_1,\varepsilon_2,\varepsilon_3)$, $\varepsilon_i\in\{+,-\}$. Denote the assigned
 numbers by
$$a=f(+++),\ b=f(++-),\ c=f(+-+),\ d=f(+--),\ e=f(-++),\ g=f(-+-),\ h=f(--+),\ i
=f(---).$$

**The common face sum is forced to be 18.** Opposite faces partition the eight v
ertices, so their sums add to $1+2+\cdots+8=36$. Since all six face sums are equ
al, each pair of opposite faces gives $2S=36$, hence $S=18$.

**Equations.** The three "positive" faces give
$$a+b+c+d=18,\qquad a+b+e+g=18,\qquad a+c+e+h=18.$$
The three "negative" faces then automatically equal $18$ (each is $36$ minus a p
ositive face). Subtracting pairs yields $c+d=e+g$ and $b+d=e+h$. Choosing $b,c,d
,g$ as free parameters, the remaining values are
$$a=18-b-c-d,\quad e=c+d-g,\quad h=b-c+g,\quad i=18-b-d-g.$$

**Symmetry reduction.** The full symmetry group of the cube (rotations **and** r
eflections) has order $48$ and acts transitively on vertices; the stabilizer of
a vertex has order $6$. Since all labels $1,\dots,8$ are distinct, any symmetry
fixing a labeling must fix every vertex, so the action is **free**. Hence each o
rbit (equivalence class) contains exactly $48$ labelings, exactly $6$ of which h
ave the value $1$ at the vertex $(+++)$ (i.e. $a=1$). Therefore
$$\#\text{classes}=\frac{\#\text{labelings with }a=1}{6}.$$

**Counting labelings with $a=1$.** With $a=1$ we need $b+c+d=17$, and $b,c,d,g$
are four distinct values from $\{2,\dots,8\}$; then $e,h,i$ must be exactly the
three remaining values of $\{2,\dots,8\}$. The unordered triples $\{b,c,d\}\subs
eteq\{2,\dots,8\}$ with sum $17$ are exactly
$$\{2,7,8\},\ \{3,6,8\},\ \{4,5,8\},\ \{4,6,7\}.$$
For each ordered triple $(b,c,d)$ and each candidate $g\in\{2,\dots,8\}\setminus
\{b,c,d\}$, one checks whether $\{e,h,i\}=\{c+d-g,\ b-c+g,\ 18-b-d-g\}$ equals $
\{2,\dots,8\}\setminus\{b,c,d,g\}$. The valid quadruples $(b,c,d,g)$ are:

| $\{b,c,d\}$ | valid $(b,c,d,g)$ | count |
|---|---|---|
| $\{2,7,8\}$ | $(7,8,2,4),(7,8,2,6),(8,7,2,3),(8,7,2,5)$ | 4 |
| $\{3,6,8\}$ | $(6,8,3,4),(6,8,3,7),(8,6,3,2),(8,6,3,5)$ | 4 |
| $\{4,5,8\}$ | $(4,8,5,6),(4,8,5,7),(8,4,5,2),(8,4,5,3)$ | 4 |
| $\{4,6,7\}$ | $(4,6,7,5),(4,7,6,5),(6,4,7,3),(6,7,4,3),(7,4,6,2),(7,6,4,2)$ |
6 |

(For example, with $(b,c,d,g)=(7,8,2,4)$: $a=1,e=6,h=3,i=5$, giving $\{1,2,3,4,5
,6,7,8\}$ and face sums $1+7+8+2=18$, $1+7+6+4=18$, $1+8+6+3=18$.) Every other c
hoice either pushes $e$, $h$, or $i$ outside $\{1,\dots,8\}$ or produces a repea
ted value. The total is
$$4+4+4+6=18$$
labelings with $a=1$.

**Conclusion.** The number of equivalence classes under rotations and reflection
s is
$$\frac{18}{6}=3.$$
