## Solution

**Setup.** Place square $ABCD$ in the coordinate plane with $A=(0,0)$, $B=(s,0)$
, $C=(s,s)$, $D=(0,s)$, where $s$ is the side length.

**Locate K and N.**
- $K$ is the midpoint of $BC$: $K = (s,\, s/2)$.
- $N$ divides $CD$ with $CN:ND = 2:1$, so $N = C + \tfrac{2}{3}(D-C) = (s/3,\, s
)$.

**Find the intersection T of AK and BN.**

Line $AK$: $(ts,\; ts/2)$, $t\in[0,1]$.

Line $BN$: $(s - \tfrac{2}{3}us,\; us)$, $u\in[0,1]$.

Setting equal: $ts/2 = us \Rightarrow u = t/2$. Substituting into the $x$-equati
on:
$$t = 1 - \frac{2}{3}\cdot\frac{t}{2} = 1 - \frac{t}{3} \;\;\Longrightarrow\;\;
\frac{4t}{3}=1 \;\;\Longrightarrow\;\; t=\frac{3}{4}.$$

So $T = \bigl(\tfrac{3s}{4},\, \tfrac{3s}{8}\bigr)$.

**Area of quadrilateral $KCNT$.** Split along diagonal $KN$ into $\triangle KCN$
 and $\triangle KNT$.

$\triangle KCN$ with $K=(s,s/2)$, $C=(s,s)$, $N=(s/3,s)$:
$$[\triangle KCN] = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl|\det[(C-K),(N-K)]\bigr| = \tfrac{1}{2}\big
l|\det[(0,s/2),(-2s/3,s/2)]\bigr| = \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{s^2}{3} = \frac{s^2}{
6}.$$

$\triangle KNT$ with $K=(s,s/2)$, $N=(s/3,s)$, $T=(3s/4,3s/8)$:
$$[\triangle KNT] = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl|\det[(N-K),(T-K)]\bigr| = \tfrac{1}{2}\big
l|\det[(-2s/3,s/2),(-s/4,-s/8)]\bigr|$$
$$= \tfrac{1}{2}\Bigl|\tfrac{2s^2}{24}+\tfrac{s^2}{8}\Bigr| = \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\
frac{5s^2}{24} = \frac{5s^2}{48}.$$

Therefore:
$$CNT] = \frac{s^2}{6} + \frac{5s^2}{48} = \frac{8s^2+5s^2}{48} = \frac{13s^2}
{48}.$$

Given $CNT]=13$:
$$\frac{13s^2}{48} = 13 \;\;\Longrightarrow\;\; s^2 = 48.$$

**Area of $\triangle BTA$.** With $B=(s,0)$, $T=(3s/4,3s/8)$, $A=(0,0)$:
$$[\triangle BTA] = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl|\det[(B-A),(T-A)]\bigr| = \tfrac{1}{2}\big
l|\det[(s,0),(3s/4,3s/8)]\bigr| = \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{3s^2}{8} = \frac{3s^2}{
16}.$$

Substituting $s^2 = 48$:
$$[\triangle BTA] = \frac{3\cdot 48}{16} = \frac{144}{16} = \boxed{9}.$$
