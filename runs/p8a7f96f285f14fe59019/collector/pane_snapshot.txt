## Proof

We use the well-known asymptotic expansions of the Bessel functions $J_m(x)$ and
 $Y_m(x)$ for large argument $x$. As $x \to \infty$,

$$
J_m(x) \sim \sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi x}}\cos\!\left(x - \frac{m\pi}{2} - \frac{\pi}{4}
\right),
\qquad
Y_m(x) \sim \sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi x}}\sin\!\left(x - \frac{m\pi}{2} - \frac{\pi}{4}
\right),
$$

where the symbol $\sim$ denotes that the ratio of the two sides tends to $1$. Th
ese expansions are valid for every fixed real order $m$.

Forming the product,

$$
J_m(x)\,Y_m(x) \sim \frac{2}{\pi x}\,\cos\!\left(x - \frac{m\pi}{2} - \frac{\pi}
{4}\right)\sin\!\left(x - \frac{m\pi}{2} - \frac{\pi}{4}\right).
$$

Applying the double-angle identity $\cos\theta\,\sin\theta = \tfrac{1}{2}\sin(2\
theta)$ with $\theta = x - \frac{m\pi}{2} - \frac{\pi}{4}$,

$$
J_m(x)\,Y_m(x) \sim \frac{1}{\pi x}\,\sin\!\left(2x - m\pi - \frac{\pi}{2}\right
).
$$

Since $|\sin(\cdot)| \le 1$, we obtain the bound

$$
\left|J_m(x)\,Y_m(x)\right| \le \frac{1}{\pi x}\,(1 + o(1)) \quad \text{as } x \
to \infty.
$$

The right-hand side tends to $0$ as $x \to \infty$. By the squeeze theorem,

$$
\lim_{x \to \infty} J_m(x)\,Y_m(x) = 0.
$$
