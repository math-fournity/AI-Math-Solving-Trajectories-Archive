## Proof

Let $v_0$ denote *Ben Song*, and let $N(v_0)$ be the set of cities directly conn
ected to $v_0$, with $d = |N(v_0)|$.

For any valid expanded capital $C$ (so $v_0 \in C$, $|C| = n+1$, $C$ is a clique
, and exactly $k$ cities outside $C$ are adjacent to some city of $C$), put $S =
 C \setminus \{v_0\}$, so $|S| = n$.

**Using condition (i).** Since $C$ is a clique, every city of $S$ is directly co
nnected to $v_0$, hence
$$S \subseteq N(v_0). \tag{1}$$

**Using condition (ii).** Let $B$ be the boundary, i.e. the set of cities outsid
e $C$ adjacent to at least one city of $C$; by hypothesis $|B| = k$. Every city
in $N(v_0) \setminus S$ lies outside $C$ (it is not $v_0$ and not in $S$) and is
 adjacent to $v_0 \in C$, so it belongs to $B$. Thus
$$N(v_0) \setminus S \subseteq B, \qquad\text{hence}\qquad d - n = |N(v_0)\setmi
nus S| \le |B| = k, \tag{2}$$
giving $d \le n + k$.

**Counting.** The map $C \mapsto S = C \setminus \{v_0\}$ is injective (its inve
rse is $S \mapsto \{v_0\}\cup S$), and by (1) every valid $S$ is an $n$-element
subset of $N(v_0)$.

- If $d > n + k$, then for *every* $n$-subset $S \subseteq N(v_0)$ we have $|N(v
_0)\setminus S| = d - n > k$, so the boundary has size $> k$; no valid expanded
capital exists, and the number of options is $0 \le \binom{n+k}{k}$.

- If $d \le n + k$, the number of valid choices of $S$ is at most the total numb
er of $n$-subsets of $N(v_0)$, namely $\binom{d}{n}$. By monotonicity of binomia
l coefficients and $d \le n+k$,
$$\binom{d}{n} \le \binom{n+k}{n} = \binom{n+k}{k}.$$

In both cases the number of options is at most $\binom{n+k}{k}$, as required. $\
blacksquare$
