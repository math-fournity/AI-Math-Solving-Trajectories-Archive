# Problem

In a sprawling urban grid consisting of an $n \times n$ network of districts, a logistics company is installing specialized supply hubs. Each district is assigned a unique security clearance level, represented by a distinct integer from $1$ to $n^2$.

The company defines a "Delivery Route" as a sequence of districts (which can consist of a single district) that satisfies the following criteria:
1. The starting district of the route must be a "Local Minimum," meaning its security clearance level is lower than the clearance levels of all its immediate neighbors.
2. Each subsequent district in the sequence must share a physical border (a common side) with the previous district.
3. The security clearance levels must strictly increase along the path.

The CEO wants to organize the security clearances across the grid to minimize the total number of unique Delivery Routes possible. Determine the minimum possible number of such routes as a function of $n$.
