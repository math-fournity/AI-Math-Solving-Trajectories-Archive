$$(x - \sqrt{2})^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 2.$$

$$(p - \sqrt{2})^2 + q^2 + s^2 = 2.$$

$$p^2 - 2\sqrt{2}\,p + 2 + q^2 + s^2 = 2,$$
$$p^2 + q^2 + s^2 = 2\sqrt{2}\,p.$$

Setting $p = 0$, the equation becomes $q^2 + s^2 = 0$. Since $q, s$ are rational

Therefore the only rational point on the sphere is $(0, 0, 0)$.

**Conclusion.** The sphere $(x - \sqrt{2})^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 2$ has exactly one ra
