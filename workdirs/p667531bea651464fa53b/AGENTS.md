# Problem

A shipping terminal consists of an $8 \times 8$ grid of storage bays. In the northernmost row of eight bays, there are eight black shipping containers (one per bay). In the southernmost row of eight bays, there are eight white shipping containers (one per bay). All other bays in the grid are currently empty.

A heavy-duty crane can move a container from its current bay to any adjacent bay (up, down, left, or right), provided the target bay is empty. A single move consists of shifting one container into an adjacent empty bay. 

The terminal manager needs to swap the positions of the colors: all eight white containers must end up occupying the eight bays of the northernmost row, and all eight black containers must end up occupying the eight bays of the southernmost row. 

What is the minimum number of moves required to complete this task?
