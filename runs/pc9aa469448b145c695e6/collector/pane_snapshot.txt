## Solution

We need the order of a 3-Sylow subgroup of $\mathrm{GL}(4, \mathbb{F}_3)$, i.e.,
 the largest power of 3 dividing $\left|\mathrm{GL}(4, \mathbb{F}_3)\right|$.

**Step 1: Compute $\left|\mathrm{GL}(4, \mathbb{F}_3)\right|$.**

The standard formula is
$$\left|\mathrm{GL}(n, \mathbb{F}_q)\right| = \prod_{i=0}^{n-1}(q^n - q^i).$$

Equivalently,
$$\left|\mathrm{GL}(n, \mathbb{F}_q)\right| = q^{\,n(n-1)/2}\prod_{k=1}^{n}(q^k
- 1).$$

For $n=4$, $q=3$, the second form gives the 3-part very cleanly:
- The prefactor is $q^{n(n-1)/2} = 3^{4\cdot 3/2} = 3^{6}$.
- The product $\prod_{k=1}^{4}(3^k - 1) = (3-1)(9-1)(27-1)(81-1) = 2\cdot 8\cdot
 26\cdot 80$.

**Step 2: Extract the 3-adic valuation.**

For every $k\ge 1$, we have $3^k \equiv 0 \pmod{3}$, hence $3^k - 1 \equiv -1 \p
mod{3}$, so none of the factors $(3^k-1)$ is divisible by 3. Therefore the entir
e 3-part of $\left|\mathrm{GL}(4,\mathbb{F}_3)\right|$ comes from the prefactor
$3^6$.

**Verification via the first form:**
$$\left|\mathrm{GL}(4,\mathbb{F}_3)\right| = 80\cdot 78\cdot 72\cdot 54.$$
Factoring:
- $80 = 2^4\cdot 5$ — no factor of 3,
- $78 = 2\cdot 3\cdot 13$ — contributes $3^1$,
- $72 = 2^3\cdot 3^2$ — contributes $3^2$,
- $54 = 2\cdot 3^3$ — contributes $3^3$.

Total 3-adic valuation: $1+2+3 = 6$, confirming $v_3 = 6$.

**Step 3: Conclusion.**

The order of a 3-Sylow subgroup is $3^6 = 729$.
