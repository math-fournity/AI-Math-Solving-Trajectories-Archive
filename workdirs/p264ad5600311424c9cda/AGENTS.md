# Problem

A specialized assembly line is tasked with processing 52 distinct components: exactly 13 of these are made of titanium, 13 of aluminum, 13 of copper, and 13 of steel. The components are loaded into an automated sorting bin in a completely random sequence.

The bin releases the components one by one onto a conveyor belt to form a single line. A quality control sensor assigns an "Efficiency Score" to each component as it is placed on the belt. The score for a newly placed component is equal to the length of the continuous streak of components immediately preceding it that are made of the same material. For example, if the belt currently ends with three consecutive aluminum components (and the component before those three was not aluminum), placing a new aluminum component adds 3 points to the total score, whereas placing a component of any other material adds 0 points.

Once all 52 components have been placed on the belt, the individual scores are summed. What is the expected value of the total Efficiency Score for the entire process?
