## Solution

**Setup.** Place the rectangular bay in coordinates with clockwise corners:
- $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (a, 0)$, $C = (a, b)$, $D = (0, b)$,

so the area is $ab = 1$ acre. The northern boundary $CD$ runs from $C=(a,b)$ to
$D=(0,b)$, hence the dock is $E = (t, b)$ for some $t \in [0, a]$.

**Centroids.** The centroid of a triangle with vertices $P, Q, R$ is $\frac{1}{3
}(P+Q+R)$.

- **Hub 1** (centroid of $\triangle ABE$):
$$H_1 = \frac{A+B+E}{3} = \left(\frac{a+t}{3},\; \frac{b}{3}\right).$$

- **Hub 2** (centroid of $\triangle BCE$):
$$H_2 = \frac{B+C+E}{3} = \left(\frac{2a+t}{3},\; \frac{2b}{3}\right).$$

- **Hub 3** (centroid of $\triangle ADE$):
$$H_3 = \frac{A+D+E}{3} = \left(\frac{t}{3},\; \frac{2b}{3}\right).$$

**Area of $\triangle H_1 H_2 H_3$.** Using the shoelace formula,
$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}\Big|x_1(y_2 - y_3) + x_2(y_3 - y_1) + x_3(y_1 - y_2)
\Big|.$$

Compute the $y$-differences:
$$y_2 - y_3 = \frac{2b}{3} - \frac{2b}{3} = 0,\qquad y_3 - y_1 = \frac{b}{3},\qq
uad y_1 - y_2 = -\frac{b}{3}.$$

Substitute:
$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}\left|\,x_2\cdot\frac{b}{3} + x_3\cdot\left(-\frac{b}
{3}\right)\right| = \frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{b}{3}\,|x_2 - x_3|.$$

Now
$$x_2 - x_3 = \frac{2a+t}{3} - \frac{t}{3} = \frac{2a}{3}.$$

Therefore
$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{b}{3}\cdot\frac{2a}{3} = \frac{ab}{9}.$$

Since $ab = 1$ acre:
$$\boxed{\text{Area} = \frac{1}{9}\text{ acre}.}$$

**Remark.** The result is independent of $t$ — the position of the dock $E$ alon
g $CD$ does not affect the area of the hub triangle. This is because shifting $E
$ translates $H_1$, $H_2$, $H_3$ horizontally in a way that preserves their mutu
al configuration (the horizontal gaps $x_2 - x_3 = 2a/3$ and the vertical struct
ure are invariant).
