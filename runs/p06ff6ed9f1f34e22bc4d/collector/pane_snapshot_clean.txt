## Solution

**Step 1: Recognize the reciprocal structure.**

The polynomial $x^6 - 4x^4 - 6x^3 - 4x^2 + 1$ has palindromic coefficients $(1,
0, -4, -6, -4, 0, 1)$, so it is reciprocal. Dividing by $x^3$ and substituting $
y = x + \tfrac{1}{x}$:

$$x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3} - 4\!\left(x + \frac{1}{x}\right) - 6 = 0$$

Using $x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3} = y^3 - 3y$:

$$y^3 - 3y - 4y - 6 = 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; y^3 - 7y - 6 = 0$$

**Step 2: Factor the cubic.**

Testing $y = -1$: $-1 + 7 - 6 = 0$. So

$$y^3 - 7y - 6 = (y+1)(y^2 - y - 6) = (y+1)(y-3)(y+2)$$

Thus $y \in \{-2,\, -1,\, 3\}$.

**Step 3: Evaluate $x^{16} + \frac{1}{x^{16}}$ for each value of $y$.**

Let $a_n = x^n + \frac{1}{x^n}$, with $a_0 = 2$, $a_1 = y$, and recurrence $a_n
= y\,a_{n-1} - a_{n-2}$.

- **$y = -2$:** $x + \frac{1}{x} = -2 \Rightarrow (x+1)^2 = 0 \Rightarrow x = -1
$. Then $a_{16} = 1 + 1 = \mathbf{2}$.
- **$y = -1$:** $x + \frac{1}{x} = -1 \Rightarrow x^2 + x + 1 = 0$, so $x^3 = 1$
. Then $x^{16} = x^{15}\cdot x = x$, giving $a_{16} = x + \frac{1}{x} = \mathbf{
-1}$.
- **$y = 3$:** $x + \frac{1}{x} = 3 \Rightarrow x = \frac{3\pm\sqrt{5}}{2}$ (rea
l, with $|x| \neq 1$). Computing the recurrence:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $a_n$ | 2 | 3 | 7 | 18 | 47 | 123 | 322 | 843 | 2207 |

| $n$ | 9 | 10 | 11 | 12 | 13 | 14 | 15 | 16 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $a_n$ | 5778 | 15127 | 39603 | 103682 | 271443 | 710647 | 1860498 | **4870847*

**Step 4: Identify the maximum.**

$$m = \max\{2,\; -1,\; 4870847\} = 4870847$$

**Step 5: Compute $m \bmod 2007$.**

$$2007 \times 2426 = 4868982$$
$$4870847 - 4868982 = 1865$$
