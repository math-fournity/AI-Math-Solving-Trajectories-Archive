## Step 1: x, y, z must be positive integers

Let s = x+y+z, q = xyz, and t = 1/x+1/y+1/z. Note that:

$$t = \frac{xy+yz+zx}{xyz} = \frac{p}{q}$$

where p = xy+yz+zx. Since t and q are integers, **p = t·q is an integer**.

Therefore x, y, z are rational roots of the monic cubic:

$$u^3 - su^2 + pu - q = 0$$

with integer coefficients s, p, q. By the **Rational Root Theorem**, any rationa
l root of a monic polynomial with integer coefficients is an integer. Hence **x,
 y, z are positive integers**.

## Step 2: Reduce to a divisibility condition

The conditions x+y+z ∈ ℤ and xyz ∈ ℤ are automatic for positive integers. The re
maining condition is:

$$\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{y}+\frac{1}{z} = \frac{xy+yz+zx}{xyz} \in \mathbb{Z}^+$$

Since x, y, z ≥ 1, we have 1/x + 1/y + 1/z ≤ 3, so the only possible values are
**t ∈ {1, 2, 3}**.

## Step 3: Enumerate all solutions (WLOG x ≤ y ≤ z)
