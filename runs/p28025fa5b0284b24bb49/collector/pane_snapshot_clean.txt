## Misère Nim characterisation (Bouton)

Let $s=x\oplus y$ be the nim-sum. The P-positions of misère Nim are:

- **Case A — all piles have size $\le 1$:** the only non-empty piles are singlet
ons. With $k$ singleton piles, players alternately remove one whole pile; the pl
ayer who removes the $k$-th (last) container loses. The player to move takes the
 1st, 3rd, 5th, … so they take the last one iff $k$ is odd. Hence P-position iff
 $k$ is odd, i.e. iff the nim-sum $s=1$.
- **Case B — at least one pile has size $\ge 2$:** the P-positions coincide with
 ordinary Nim, namely $s=0$.

Equivalently, a position is P iff ($s=0$ and $\max(x,y)\ge 2$) or ($\max(x,y)\le
 1$ and $s=1$).

## Terminal position $(0,0)$

At $(0,0)$ there is nothing to remove; the previous player already took the last
 container and lost, so the player to move (Elara) wins. Under the rule above: $
\max\le 1$, $s=0$, so it is **not** a P-position — it is an N-position. ✓
