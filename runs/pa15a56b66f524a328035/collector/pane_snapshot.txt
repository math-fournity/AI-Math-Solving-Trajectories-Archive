## Setup

$$x_{ij} = a_{ij} - 1 \in \{-1, 0, +1\}$$
where $x_{ij}=0$ means the cell is 1, $x_{ij}=-1$ means it's 0, and $x_{ij}=+1$
means it's 2.

Let $T$ = number of nonzero $x_{ij}$ = number of non-1 cells. Then the number of
 Type 1 containers is $N - T$ where $N = 2011 \times 2012 = 4{,}046{,}132$. **We

**Row constraints:** Row $i$ sum $= 2012 + \sum_j x_{ij} \equiv 0 \pmod{3}$. Sin
ce $2012 \equiv 2 \pmod{3}$, we need $R_i := \sum_j x_{ij} \equiv 1 \pmod{3}$.

**Column constraints:** Column $j$ sum $= 2011 + \sum_i x_{ij} \equiv 0 \pmod{3}

3}$.

Let $S = \sum_i R_i = \sum_j C_j$ (both equal total weight minus $N$). Note $S \
equiv 2011 \cdot 1 \equiv 1 \pmod{3}$ and $S \equiv 2012 \cdot 2 \equiv 1 \pmod{
3}$. ✓

## Lower Bound on $T$

**Key inequalities.** Write $R_i = 3r_i + 1$ ($r_i \in \mathbb{Z}$) and $C_j = 3
c_j + 2$ ($c_j \in \mathbb{Z}$).

**Claim 1:** $|3r+1| \geq 1 + |r|$ for all $r \in \mathbb{Z}$.
*Proof:* For $r \geq 0$: $3r+1 \geq 1+r \iff 2r \geq 0$ ✓. For $r \leq -1$: $|3r
+1| = -3r-1 \geq 1+|r| = 1-r \iff -2r \geq 2 \iff r \leq -1$ ✓.

**Claim 2:** $|3c+2| \geq 2 + c$ for all $c \in \mathbb{Z}$.
*Proof:* For $c \geq 0$: $3c+2 \geq 2+c \iff 2c \geq 0$ ✓. For $c \leq -1$: $|3c
+2|=-3c-2 \geq 2+c \iff -4c \geq 4 \iff c \leq -1$ ✓.

**Row bound:** $\sum_i |R_i| \geq \sum_i (1+|r_i|) = 2011 + \sum|r_i| \geq 2011
+ |\sum r_i| = 2011 + \left|\frac{S-2011}{3}\right|$.

Since $T \geq \sum_i |R_i|$ (by triangle inequality: $|R_i| = |\sum x_{ij}| \leq
 \sum|x_{ij}|$ = non-1 cells in row $i$), we get $T \geq 2011 + |m|$ where $m =
(S-2011)/3$.

**Column bound:** $\sum_j |C_j| \geq \sum_j (2+c_j) = 4024 + \frac{S-4024}{3} =
\frac{8048+S}{3}$.

Since $T \geq \sum_j |C_j|$, we get $T \geq \frac{8048+S}{3}$.

**Combining:** $T \geq \max\!\left(2011 + \left|\frac{S-2011}{3}\right|,\; \frac
{8048+S}{3}\right)$.

For $S \leq 2011$ (so $m \leq 0$): the row bound is $\frac{8044-S}{3}$ (decreasi
ng in $S$) and the column bound is $\frac{8048+S}{3}$ (increasing in $S$). The m
aximum is minimized when they are equal:
$$\frac{8044-S}{3} = \frac{8048+S}{3} \implies 8044 - S = 8048 + S \implies S =
-2.$$
At $S = -2$ (which satisfies $S \equiv 1 \pmod{3}$ ✓): $T \geq \frac{8046}{3} =
2682$.

For any other valid $S$ (i.e., $S \equiv 1 \pmod{3}$, $S \neq -2$), at least one

- $S < -2012$: column bound $= |S| > 2012$, and row bound $> 2682$.

**Therefore $T \geq 2682$, giving at most $4{,}046{,}132 - 2682 = 4{,}043{,}450$
