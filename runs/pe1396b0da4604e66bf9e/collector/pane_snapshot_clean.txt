## Proof

**Step 1: Symmetry and substitution.** The integrand is even, so
$$I_n = 2\int_0^{\infty} \frac{dx}{1+x^2 e^{nx^2}}.$$

Set $u = x^2$, $dx = \frac{du}{2\sqrt{u}}$:
$$I_n = \int_0^{\infty} \frac{u^{-1/2}}{1+u\,e^{nu}}\,du.$$

Now set $v = nu$, $du = dv/n$:
$$I_n = \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}\int_0^{\infty} \frac{v^{-1/2}}{1+\frac{v}{n}e^{v}}\,d
v.$$

**Step 2: Upper bound.** Using $\frac{1}{1+\frac{v}{n}e^v} \leq 1$ and $\frac{1}
{1+\frac{v}{n}e^v} \leq \frac{n}{ve^v}$, split at $A = \ln n$:

$$\int_0^{\infty} \frac{v^{-1/2}}{1+\frac{v}{n}e^{v}}\,dv = \int_0^{\ln n}(\cdot
s)\,dv + \int_{\ln n}^{\infty}(\cdots)\,dv.$$

First piece:
$$\int_0^{\ln n} \frac{v^{-1/2}}{1+\frac{v}{n}e^{v}}\,dv \leq \int_0^{\ln n} v^{
-1/2}\,dv = 2\sqrt{\ln n}.$$

Second piece (using $v^{3/2} \geq (\ln n)^{3/2}$ for $v \geq \ln n$):
$$\int_{\ln n}^{\infty} \frac{v^{-1/2}}{1+\frac{v}{n}e^{v}}\,dv \leq n\int_{\ln
n}^{\infty} \frac{dv}{v^{3/2}e^v} \leq \frac{n}{(\ln n)^{3/2}}\int_{\ln n}^{\inf
ty} e^{-v}\,dv = \frac{n}{(\ln n)^{3/2}}\cdot\frac{1}{n} = \frac{1}{(\ln n)^{3/2
}}.$$

**Step 3: Combine.**
$$0 \leq I_n \leq \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}\left(2\sqrt{\ln n} + \frac{1}{(\ln n)^{3/2}
}\right) = \frac{2\sqrt{\ln n}}{\sqrt{n}} + \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}\,(\ln n)^{3/2}}.$$

Both terms on the right tend to $0$ as $n \to \infty$ (since $\sqrt{\ln n}/\sqrt
{n} \to 0$). By the squeeze theorem:

$$\boxed{\lim_{n \to \infty} \int_{-\infty}^\infty \frac{1}{1+x^2 e^{nx^2}}\,dx
= 0.}$$

**Intuition:** The integrand converges pointwise to $0$ for all $x \neq 0$ (sinc
e $e^{nx^2} \to \infty$), and equals $1$ only at $x=0$ (a measure-zero point). T
he peak near the origin has width $\sim\sqrt{\ln n / n}$ and height $\leq 1$, co
ntributing $O(\sqrt{\ln n / n}) \to 0$. The tails are killed by the $e^{nx^2}$ f
actor. Hence the entire integral vanishes.
