## Proof

Consider the path-loop fibration $\Omega S^n \hookrightarrow PS^n \xrightarrow{p
} S^n$ with $PS^n$ contractible. Assume $n \geq 2$ so $S^n$ is simply connected.

### Step 1: The $E_2$ page

Since $S^n$ is simply connected, the cohomological Leray-Serre spectral sequence
 has
$$E_2^{p,q} = H^p(S^n)\otimes H^q(\Omega S^n).$$
With $H^*(S^n)=\mathbb{Z}\{1,u\}$, $|u|=n$, $u^2=0$, the page is nonzero only fo
r $p\in\{0,n\}$:
$$E_2^{0,q}\cong H^q(\Omega S^n),\qquad E_2^{n,q}\cong H^q(\Omega S^n).$$

### Step 2: Only $d_n$ can be nonzero

The differential $d_r$ has bidegree $(r,1-r)$. For $r<n$, $d_r$ maps into $p=r\i
n(0,n)$ or $p=n+r>n$, both zero. For $r>n$, $d_r$ maps from $p\in\{0,n\}$ to $p>
n$, also zero. Hence $E_2=\cdots=E_n$ and $E_{n+1}=E_\infty$, with the only poss
ible differential
$$d_n: H^q(\Omega S^n)\longrightarrow H^{q-n+1}(\Omega S^n).$$

### Step 3: Contractibility forces $d_n$ to be an isomorphism

Since $PS^n$ is contractible, $E_\infty^{p,q}=0$ for $p+q>0$ and $E_\infty^{0,0}
=\mathbb{Z}$.

- **At $p=0$:** $E_\infty^{0,q}=\ker\big(d_n:H^q\to H^{q-n+1}\big)=0$ for $q>0$,
 so $d_n$ is **injective** for $q>0$.
- **At $p=n$:** $E_\infty^{n,q}=H^q/\operatorname{im}\big(d_n:H^{q+n-1}\to H^q\b
ig)=0$ for $q\geq 0$, so $d_n$ is **surjective** for $q\geq 0$.

Combining: $d_n:H^{q+n-1}(\Omega S^n)\xrightarrow{\;\sim\;} H^q(\Omega S^n)$ is
an **isomorphism** for all $q\geq 0$.

### Step 4: Additive structure

- For $0<q<n-1$: injectivity into $H^{q-n+1}=0$ gives $H^q(\Omega S^n)=0$.
- $d_n:H^{n-1}\xrightarrow{\sim} H^0=\mathbb{Z}$ gives $H^{n-1}(\Omega S^n)\cong
\mathbb{Z}$.
- By induction using the isomorphism: $H^{k(n-1)}(\Omega S^n)\cong\mathbb{Z}$ fo
r all $k\geq 0$, and $H^q=0$ for $q$ not a multiple of $n-1$.

### Step 5: Ring structure via the Leibniz rule

Let $x\in H^{n-1}(\Omega S^n)$ be the generator with $d_n(x)=u$ (the transgressi
on). The product on $E_2=H^*(S^n)\otimes H^*(\Omega S^n)$ is the graded tensor p
roduct, so $x\cdot u = (-1)^{n(n-1)}u\cdot x$. The Leibniz rule is
$$d_n(ab)=d_n(a)\,b+(-1)^{|a|_{\mathrm{tot}}}a\,d_n(b).$$

#### Case 1: $n$ odd

Here $|x|=n-1$ is even and $n(n-1)$ is even, so $xu=ux$. Then:
$$d_n(x^2)=ux+(-1)^{n-1}x\,ux=ux+ux=2\,ux.$$
By induction ($|x|$ even, $xu=ux$):
$$d_n(x^k)=k\,u\,x^{k-1}.$$
Since $d_n:H^{k(n-1)}\to H^{(k-1)(n-1)}$ is an isomorphism (generator $\mapsto$
generator) but $d_n(x^k)=k\cdot(ux^{k-1})$ is $k$ times a generator, we deduce $
x^k=k!\,x^{(k)}$ where $x^{(k)}$ is the additive generator of $H^{k(n-1)}$ with
$d_n(x^{(k)})=u\,x^{(k-1)}$. The multiplication satisfies
$$x^{(k)}\cdot x^{(\ell)}=\binom{k+\ell}{k}\,x^{(k+\ell)}.$$
This is the **divided power algebra**:
$$\boxed{H^*(\Omega S^n;\mathbb{Z})\cong\Gamma_{\mathbb{Z}}(x),\quad |x|=n-1\qqu
ad(n\text{ odd}).}$$
Over a field $\mathbb{F}$ of characteristic $0$, $\Gamma_{\mathbb{F}}(x)\cong\ma
thbb{F}[x]$ (polynomial algebra).

#### Case 2: $n$ even

Here $|x|=n-1$ is odd and $n(n-1)$ is even, so $xu=ux$. Then:
$$d_n(x^2)=ux+(-1)^{n-1}x\,ux=ux-ux=0.$$
Since $d_n:H^{2(n-1)}\to H^{n-1}$ is injective, $x^2=0$; $x$ is an **exterior**
generator.

Let $y\in H^{2(n-1)}(\Omega S^n)$ be the generator with $d_n(y)=ux$. Since $|y|=
2(n-1)$ is even, the same induction gives
$$d_n(y^k)=k\,u\,x\,y^{k-1},$$
so $y^k=k!\,y^{(k)}$ with $d_n(y^{(k)})=ux\,y^{(k-1)}$ and $y^{(k)}\cdot y^{(\el
l)}=\binom{k+\ell}{k}y^{(k+\ell)}$ (divided power algebra on $y$).

For the mixed product:
$$d_n(x\,y^{(k)})=u\,y^{(k)}+(-1)^{n-1}x\,ux\,y^{(k-1)}=u\,y^{(k)},$$
since $x\cdot ux=(-1)^{n(n-1)}u\otimes x^2=0$. Thus $x\,y^{(k)}$ is the generato
r of $H^{(2k+1)(n-1)}$.

The additive generators are $\{y^{(k)}\}_{k\geq 0}$ in degrees $2k(n-1)$ and $\{
x\,y^{(k)}\}_{k\geq 0}$ in degrees $(2k+1)(n-1)$, and the ring is
$$\boxed{H^*(\Omega S^n;\mathbb{Z})\cong\Lambda_{\mathbb{Z}}(x)\otimes\Gamma_{\m
athbb{Z}}(y),\quad |x|=n-1,\;|y|=2(n-1)\qquad(n\text{ even}).}$$
Over a field $\mathbb{F}$ of characteristic $0$: $H^*(\Omega S^n;\mathbb{F})\con
g\Lambda_{\mathbb{F}}(x)\otimes\mathbb{F}[y]$.

### Summary

For $n\geq 2$:

| $n$ | $H^*(\Omega S^n;\mathbb{Z})$ | Over a field $\mathbb{F}$ ($\operatorname
{char}\mathbb{F}=0$) |
|---|---|---|
| odd | $\Gamma_{\mathbb{Z}}(x)$, $\|x\|=n-1$ (divided power) | $\mathbb{F}[x]$,
 $\|x\|=n-1$ (polynomial) |
| even | $\Lambda(x)\otimes\Gamma_{\mathbb{Z}}(y)$, $\|x\|=n-1,\|y\|=2(n-1)$ | $
\Lambda(x)\otimes\mathbb{F}[y]$, $\|x\|=n-1,\|y\|=2(n-1)$ |
