## Proof

We interpret the conditioning event $Y = aX$ as conditioning on the random varia
ble $Z := Y - aX$ taking the value $0$. Thus
$$E(X \mid Y = aX) = E(X \mid Z = 0).$$

**Step 1. Determine the sign of $a$.** Since the support is $0 < x < y < 1$, we
always have $Y > X > 0$. For the line $y = ax$ to meet this region (with $x > 0$
), we need $ax > x$, i.e. $a > 1$. We proceed under $a > 1$ and verify at the en
d.

**Step 2. Joint density of $(X, Z)$.** With $Y = Z + aX$, the Jacobian $\frac{\p
artial(x,y)}{\partial(x,z)} = 1$, so
$$f_{X,Z}(x,z) = f(x,\, z+ax) = 2\bigl(x + z + ax\bigr) = 2\bigl((1+a)x + z\bigr
).$$

The support $0 < x < y < 1$ becomes $0 < x$, $\;x < z + ax$, $\;z + ax < 1$, $\;
z + ax > 0$:
- $z > x(1-a) = -x(a-1)$,
- $z < 1 - ax$,
- $z > -ax$.

Since $a > 1$, we have $-x(a-1) = -ax + x > -ax$, so the binding lower bound is
$z > -x(a-1)$. The upper bound $z < 1 - ax$ is positive only when $x < 1/a$. Hen
ce the region is
$$0 < x < \tfrac{1}{a}, \qquad -x(a-1) < z < 1 - ax.$$

**Step 3. Marginal density $f_Z(0)$.** Setting $z = 0$, the valid range of $x$ i
s $0 < x < 1/a$ (both inequalities $0 < 1 - ax$ and $-x(a-1) < 0$ reduce to $x >
 0$ and $x < 1/a$). Thus
$$f_Z(0) = \int_0^{1/a} 2(1+a)x\, dx = 2(1+a)\cdot\frac{1}{2a^2} = \frac{1+a}{a^
2}.$$

**Step 4. Conditional expectation.**
$$E(X \mid Z = 0) = \frac{\displaystyle\int_0^{1/a} x\cdot 2(1+a)x\, dx}{f_Z(0)}
 = \frac{2(1+a)\displaystyle\int_0^{1/a} x^2\, dx}{(1+a)/a^2} = \frac{2(1+a)\cdo
t\dfrac{1}{3a^3}}{(1+a)/a^2} = \frac{2}{3a}.$$

**Step 5. Solve for $a$.** Setting $E(X \mid Y = aX) = \dfrac{2}{9}$:
$$\frac{2}{3a} = \frac{2}{9} \;\Longrightarrow\; 3a = 9 \;\Longrightarrow\; a =
3.$$

**Verification.** $a = 3 > 1$, consistent with Step 1. As a sanity check, the jo
int density integrates to $1$:
$$\int_0^1\!\int_0^y 2(x+y)\,dx\,dy = \int_0^1 3y^2\,dy = 1.\;\checkmark$$

With $a = 3$: $f_Z(0) = \frac{4}{9}$, the numerator $\frac{2\cdot4}{3\cdot27} =
\frac{8}{81}$, and $\frac{8/81}{4/9} = \frac{8}{81}\cdot\frac{9}{4} = \frac{2}{9
}$. ✓

$$\boxed{a = 3}$$
