Let $f \in C([-1,1])$ with $f \ge 0$, and set $M = \max_{x \in [-1,1]} f(x)$.

elta > 0$ and $\varepsilon > 0$ such that $f(x) \ge 1 + \varepsilon$ for all $x

$$\int_{-1}^{1} f(x)^n\,dx \ge 2\delta\,(1+\varepsilon)^n \xrightarrow{n\to\inft

Therefore:
$$\mathcal{F} = \bigl\{f \in C([-1,1]) : f \ge 0,\; \|f\|_\infty \le 1\bigr\}.$$

## Step 2: The image is contained in $[0,2]$

For $f \in \mathcal{F}$, we have $0 \le f(x) \le 1$, so
$$0 \le \int_{-1}^{1} f(x)\,dx \le \int_{-1}^{1} 1\,dx = 2.$$

## Step 3: Every value in $[0,2]$ is attained

For any $c \in [0, 2]$, take the constant function $f \equiv c/2$. Then:
- $f$ is continuous and $f \ge 0$. ✓
- $\|f\|_\infty = c/2 \le 1$ (since $c \le 2$), so $f \in \mathcal{F}$. ✓
- $\int_{-1}^{1} f(x)\,dx = \frac{c}{2} \cdot 2 = c$. ✓

In particular, $c = 0$ gives $f \equiv 0$ and $c = 2$ gives $f \equiv 1$.

## Conclusion

The image of $\mathcal{F}$ under $f \mapsto \int_{-1}^{1} f(x)\,dx$ is $\boxed{[
0,\, 2]}$.
