$$V_2 = 64 + 576a^3 = 8 \cdot 8 + 8 \cdot 72a^3 = 8(8 + 72a^3) = 8 \cdot V_1$$

Each Second Quarter term is exactly **8 times** the corresponding First Quarter
term:
- $64 = 8 \times 8$ ✓
- $64a^3 = 8 \times 8a^3$ ✓
- $512a^3 = 8 \times 64a^3$ ✓

**System Growth Ratio:**

$$\frac{V_1}{V_2} = \frac{V_1}{8 \cdot V_1} = \frac{1}{8}$$

The numerical value of the System Growth Ratio is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{8}}$.
