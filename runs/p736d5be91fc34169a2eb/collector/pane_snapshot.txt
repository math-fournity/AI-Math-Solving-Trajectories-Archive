The statement is **false**.

This is a smoothly embedded submanifold of $\mathbb{RP}^3$ of codimension $1$ (i

^2 \to \mathbb{RP}^3$ induced by $(x_0,x_1,x_2) \mapsto (x_0,x_1,x_2,0)$ is a sm
