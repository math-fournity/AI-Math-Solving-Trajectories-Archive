# Problem

In a bustling metropolitan airport, the central air traffic control tower monitors 30 different flight paths that all originate from the same radar beacon located at the center of the airfield. These flight paths are straight-line trajectories extending outward across the flat landscape.

The safety commission is conducting a review of "inter-path proximity." They define a "narrow-sector" as the angular space between any two of these 30 flight paths that measures strictly less than 90 degrees. 

The commission wishes to calculate $N$, the total count of these narrow-sectors formed by every possible pair of flight paths. Given that the controllers can orient the 30 paths in any direction around the beacon to minimize congestion, find the smallest possible value of $N$.
