## Solution

We solve Laplace's equation in the rectangular parallelepiped
$$0 < x < a,\quad 0 < y < b,\quad 0 < z < c,$$
with boundary conditions
$$u(0,y,z)=u(a,y,z)=u(x,0,z)=u(x,b,z)=u(x,y,0)=0,\qquad u(x,y,c)=f(x,y).$$

### Separation of variables

Set $u(x,y,z)=X(x)\,Y(y)\,Z(z)$. Substituting into $\nabla^2 u=0$ and dividing b
y $XYZ$:
$$\frac{X''}{X}+\frac{Y''}{Y}+\frac{Z''}{Z}=0.$$

**$x$-direction.** The homogeneous conditions $u(0,y,z)=u(a,y,z)=0$ force $X(0)=
X(a)=0$, so
$$X''+\lambda X=0,\quad X(0)=X(a)=0 \;\Longrightarrow\; X_m(x)=\sin\!\frac{m\pi
x}{a},\quad \lambda_m=\Big(\frac{m\pi}{a}\Big)^{\!2},\;\; m=1,2,\dots$$

**$y$-direction.** Likewise $Y(0)=Y(b)=0$ gives
$$Y_n(y)=\sin\!\frac{n\pi y}{b},\quad \mu_n=\Big(\frac{n\pi}{b}\Big)^{\!2},\;\;
n=1,2,\dots$$

**$z$-direction.** With $\alpha_{mn}=\sqrt{\lambda_m+\mu_n}=\sqrt{(m\pi/a)^2+(n\
pi/b)^2}$, the $Z$-equation becomes
$$Z''-\alpha_{mn}^2\, Z=0.$$
The bottom condition $u(x,y,0)=0$ requires $Z(0)=0$, so we take the growing hype
rbolic solution
$$Z_{mn}(z)=\sinh(\alpha_{mn}\,z).$$

### General solution

Superposing all separated solutions:
$$\boxed{\;u(x,y,z)=\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} A_{mn}\,\sin\!\frac{m
\pi x}{a}\,\sin\!\frac{n\pi y}{b}\,\sinh\!\big(\alpha_{mn}\,z\big),\quad \alpha_
{mn}=\sqrt{\Big(\frac{m\pi}{a}\Big)^2+\Big(\frac{n\pi}{b}\Big)^2}.\;}$$

### Determining the coefficients

Imposing the top boundary condition $u(x,y,c)=f(x,y)$:
$$f(x,y)=\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} A_{mn}\sinh(\alpha_{mn}\,c)\,\si
n\!\frac{m\pi x}{a}\,\sin\!\frac{n\pi y}{b}.$$

This is a double Fourier sine series in $x$ and $y$. By orthogonality of $\{\sin
(m\pi x/a)\}$ on $[0,a]$ and $\{\sin(n\pi y/b)\}$ on $[0,b]$, multiplying by $\s
in(p\pi x/a)\sin(q\pi y/b)$ and integrating gives
$$A_{mn}\sinh(\alpha_{mn}\,c)=\frac{4}{ab}\int_0^a\!\!\int_0^b f(x,y)\,\sin\!\fr
ac{m\pi x}{a}\,\sin\!\frac{n\pi y}{b}\,dy\,dx.$$

Hence
$$\boxed{\;A_{mn}=\frac{4}{ab\,\sinh(\alpha_{mn}\,c)}\int_0^a\!\!\int_0^b f(x,y)
\,\sin\!\frac{m\pi x}{a}\,\sin\!\frac{n\pi y}{b}\,dy\,dx.\;}$$

### Verification

- **PDE:** Each term $\sin(m\pi x/a)\sin(n\pi y/b)\sinh(\alpha_{mn}z)$ satisfies
 $\nabla^2 u=0$ since $-\lambda_m-\mu_n+\alpha_{mn}^2=0$ by definition of $\alph
a_{mn}$. ✓
- **Faces $x=0,a$:** $\sin(m\pi x/a)$ vanishes at $x=0,a$. ✓
- **Faces $y=0,b$:** $\sin(n\pi y/b)$ vanishes at $y=0,b$. ✓
- **Face $z=0$:** $\sinh(\alpha_{mn}\cdot 0)=0$. ✓
- **Face $z=c$:** The series reduces to the double Fourier sine expansion of $f(
x,y)$, which converges to $f$ at points of continuity (and to the midpoint of on
e-sided limits at jump discontinuities) under the standard Dirichlet conditions
on $f$. ✓

### Final answer

$$u(x,y,z)=\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{4}{ab\,\sinh(\alpha_{mn}\
,c)}\left[\int_0^a\!\!\int_0^b f(\xi,\eta)\sin\!\frac{m\pi\xi}{a}\sin\!\frac{n\p
i\eta}{b}\,d\eta\,d\xi\right]\sin\!\frac{m\pi x}{a}\sin\!\frac{n\pi y}{b}\sinh(\
alpha_{mn}\,z),$$

where $\displaystyle\alpha_{mn}=\pi\sqrt{\frac{m^2}{a^2}+\frac{n^2}{b^2}}$.
