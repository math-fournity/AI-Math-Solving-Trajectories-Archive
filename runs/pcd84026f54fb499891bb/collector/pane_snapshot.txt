**Upper bound (construction achieving 30).** The collector donates 30 artifacts

t after the $k$-th donation ($1 \le k \le 30$), the wall is
$$\{30, 29, \ldots, k+1\} \cup \{31, 32, \ldots, 30+k\},$$
where the first set is empty when $k = 30$.

*Base case ($k=1$):* Initially the wall is $\{30, 29, \ldots, 1\}$ with minimum
$1$. The first donation has value $31 > 1$, so $1$ is removed and $31$ is added.
 The wall becomes $\{30, 29, \ldots, 2\} \cup \{31\}$. ✓

+1) = 31+k > k+1$, so the minimum $k+1$ is removed and $31+k$ is added. The wall
 becomes $\{30, \ldots, k+2\} \cup \{31, \ldots, 31+k\}$. ✓

After $k = 30$ donations, the wall is $\{31, 32, \ldots, 60\}$—every spot is occ
upied by an artifact from the collector's personal collection.

**Conclusion.** The lower bound of 30 and the matching construction give the min
imum:
