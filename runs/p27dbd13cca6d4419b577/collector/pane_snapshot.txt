Looking at this problem, I need to find the probability that Lapis Union (15 dro
nes) is the first corporation to have all its drones disabled.

## Setup

The storm disables drones in a random permutation of all 45 drones (10 Emerald,
15 Lapis, 20 Amber). A corporation is "wiped out" when its **last** drone appear
s in the permutation. The first corporation wiped out is the one whose last dron
e appears **earliest**.

Let $M_E, M_L, M_A$ be the positions of the last drone of each corporation. I ne
ed:
$$P(M_L < M_E \text{ and } M_L < M_A) = P(M_L = \min(M_E, M_L, M_A))$$

## Key Decomposition

I condition on which corporation's drone is at **position 45** (the very last dr
one in the permutation). That corporation has the **maximum** last-position, so
it is wiped out **last** (not first).

**Case 1: Lapis drone at position 45** (probability $15/45 = 1/3$).
Then $M_L = 45$ is the maximum, so Lapis is wiped out last — **not first**. Cont
ributes $0$.

**Case 2: Emerald drone at position 45** (probability $10/45 = 2/9$).
Then $M_E = 45$, so Emerald is wiped out last. I need Lapis wiped out before Amb
er, i.e., $M_L < M_A$.

Since both $M_L$ and $M_A$ are determined by positions $< 45$, and the Emerald d
rones interspersed don't affect the **relative** ordering of Lapis vs. Amber dro
nes, this reduces to: among only the 15 Lapis and 20 Amber drones, is the last o
ne Amber? In a random permutation of 15 L's and 20 A's, the last element is A wi
th probability $\frac{20}{35} = \frac{4}{7}$.

Contribution: $\frac{2}{9} \cdot \frac{4}{7} = \frac{8}{63}$.

**Case 3: Amber drone at position 45** (probability $20/45 = 4/9$).
Then $M_A = 45$, so Amber is wiped out last. I need Lapis wiped out before Emera
ld, i.e., $M_L < M_E$.

Among only the 15 Lapis and 10 Emerald drones, the last one is Emerald with prob
ability $\frac{10}{25} = \frac{2}{5}$.

Contribution: $\frac{4}{9} \cdot \frac{2}{5} = \frac{8}{45}$.

## Computation

$$P(\text{Lapis first}) = \frac{8}{63} + \frac{8}{45}$$

Finding a common denominator ($\text{lcm}(63, 45) = 315$):

$$= \frac{40}{315} + \frac{56}{315} = \frac{96}{315} = \frac{32}{105}$$
