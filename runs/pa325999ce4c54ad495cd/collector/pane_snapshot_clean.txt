## Setting up the recursive block structure

**Parent rule:** For each $n > 1$, the parent is $n/\operatorname{spf}(n)$ where
 $\operatorname{spf}$ = smallest prime factor. Penguin $n$ is inserted directly
behind its parent.

**Children of $m$:** Those $n = m \cdot q$ (prime $q \leq \operatorname{spf}(m)$
) with $n \leq 2019$. (For $m=1$, all primes are children.)

**Block structure (verified by tracing $n \leq 10$):** Since children arrive in
increasing ticket order and each inserts *directly behind* $m$ (pushing earlier
siblings back), the final block of $m$ is:
$$\text{block}(m) = \big[m,\; \text{block}(c_k),\; \text{block}(c_{k-1}),\; \dot
s,\; \text{block}(c_1)\big]$$
where $c_1 < c_2 < \dots < c_k$ are the children of $m$ (decreasing order of sub
-blocks).

**Key facts:**
- The smallest child of $m$ is always $2m$ (when $2m \leq 2019$), so the **last*
* element of $\text{block}(m)$ follows the chain $m \to 2m \to 4m \to \cdots$ un
til exceeding 2019.
- The **first** element after $m$ in its block is the first element of $\text{bl
ock}(c_k)$, i.e., $c_k$ itself (the largest child).

## Computing F

Penguin 2 is in $\text{block}(1) = [1,\; \text{block}(p_k),\; \dots,\; \text{blo
ck}(3),\; \text{block}(2)]$ (primes in decreasing order). So 2 is the first elem
ent of the last sub-block.

$$|\text{block}(1)| = 2019 = 1 + \sum_{\text{prime } p \leq 2019} |\text{block}(
p)|$$

The subtree of 2 consists exactly of $\{2^k : 2^k \leq 2019\}$ (since $n$ is in
subtree of 2 iff $n$ is even and $n/2$ is in subtree of 2). With $2^{10} = 1024
\leq 2019 < 2^{11} = 2048$:

$$|\text{block}(2)| = 10$$

$$F = 1 + \sum_{\text{prime } p,\, 3 \leq p \leq 2019} |\text{block}(p)| = 1 + (
2018 - 10) = \boxed{2009}$$
