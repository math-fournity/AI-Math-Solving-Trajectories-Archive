Looking at this problem, I need to determine whether there exists a C\*-algebra
$A$ with trivial center ($Z(A) = 0$) that also admits a tracial state.

**Answer: Yes.**

**Proof.**

We construct an explicit example.

**Setup.** Let $B$ be a simple, unital, infinite-dimensional $C^*$-algebra posse
ssing a faithful tracial state $\tau$. For concreteness, take $B$ to be the CAR
algebra (the UHF algebra of type $2^\infty$), which is simple, unital, and has a
 unique faithful tracial state $\tau$.

**Choosing $h$.** Since $B$ is infinite-dimensional, it contains a positive elem
ent $h \in B_+$ with $\operatorname{spec}(h) = [0,1]$. (For instance, the diagon
al masa in the CAR algebra is isomorphic to $C(\text{Cantor set})$, which surjec
ts onto $C([0,1])$; any generator of the latter, viewed inside $B$, has spectrum
 $[0,1]$.) In particular, $0$ is a non-isolated point of $\operatorname{spec}(h)
$.

**Defining $A$.** Let $A = \overline{hBh}$, the hereditary $C^*$-subalgebra of $
B$ generated by $h$.

**Claim 1: $A$ is non-unital.** If $A$ had a unit, it would be the support proje
ction $p = \chi_{(0,\infty)}(h) \in B^{**}$. But since $0$ is a limit point of $
\operatorname{spec}(h)$, the function $\chi_{(0,\infty)}$ is not continuous on $
\operatorname{spec}(h)$, so $p \notin B$ (it is not obtainable by continuous fun
ctional calculus). Hence $p \notin A$, and $A$ has no unit.

**Claim 2: $A$ is simple.** A hereditary $C^*$-subalgebra of a simple $C^*$-alge
bra is simple (standard result: any nonzero ideal $J \trianglelefteq A$ generate
s a nonzero ideal in $B$, which equals $B$ by simplicity; hereditariness then fo
rces $J = A$).

**Claim 3: $Z(A) = \{0\}$.** The center $Z(A)$ is a closed two-sided ideal of $A
$ (it is a commutative $C^*$-subalgebra and an ideal). Since $A$ is simple, $Z(A
) = \{0\}$ or $Z(A) = A$. If $Z(A) = A$, then $A$ is commutative; but the only n
onzero commutative simple $C^*$-algebra is $\mathbb{C}$, which is unital—contrad
icting Claim 1. Since $h \neq 0$, we have $A \neq \{0\}$. Therefore $Z(A) = \{0\
}$.

**Claim 4: $A$ admits a tracial state.** The restriction $\tau|_A$ is a positive
 tracial functional on $A$ (the trace property $\tau(ab) = \tau(ba)$ is inherite
d by restriction). It is nonzero: let $p = \chi_{(0,\infty)}(h) \in B^{**}$ be t
he support (open) projection of $A$. Since $B$ is simple and $h \neq 0$, we have
 $A \neq \{0\}$, so $p \neq 0$. Since $\tau$ is faithful on $B$, its normal exte
nsion to $B^{**}$ satisfies $\tau(p) > 0$. The norm of the restricted functional
 equals $\|\tau|_A\| = \tau(p) > 0$ (standard fact: the norm of a positive funct
ional restricted to a hereditary subalgebra with support projection $p$ equals t
he functional's value on $p$). Therefore
$$\tau_A := \frac{\tau|_A}{\|\tau|_A\|}$$
is a tracial state on $A$.

**Conclusion.** The algebra $A = \overline{hBh}$ is a (non-unital, simple) $C^*$
-algebra with $Z(A) = \{0\}$ and a tracial state $\tau_A$. Such a $C^*$-algebra
exists. $\blacksquare$
