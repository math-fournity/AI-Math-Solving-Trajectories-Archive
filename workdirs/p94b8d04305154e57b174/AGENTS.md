# Problem

In a remote sector of the galaxy, $n$ distinct space stations (where $n > 2$) are positioned such that no four stations lie within the same flat orbital plane. To facilitate travel, a direct hyperspace corridor is established between every possible pair of stations.

Due to energy constraints, each corridor must be powered by one of two types of crystals: **Blue Kyber** or **Red Plasma**. The galactic council has imposed the following strict operational protocols:

1.  **Node Limitation:** To prevent power surges, no single space station may have more than 4 Blue Kyber corridors connected to it.
2.  **Safety Redundancy:** For every corridor powered by Red Plasma connecting two stations (let’s call them Station $A$ and Station $B$), there must exist at least one other station (Station $C$) such that the paths from $A$ to $C$ and from $B$ to $C$ are both powered by Blue Kyber.

Determine the maximum possible number of space stations $n$ for which such a power distribution can be maintained.
