## Solution

Let $\alpha = \sqrt{3}+\sqrt{2}$ and $\beta = \sqrt{3}-\sqrt{2}$. Note that
$$\alpha\beta = (\sqrt{3})^2-(\sqrt{2})^2 = 1, \quad\text{so}\quad \beta = \alph
a^{-1},$$
and $0 < \beta = \sqrt{3}-\sqrt{2} \approx 0.318 < 1$.

**Key relation.** For $n\ge 0$, define $S_n = \alpha^n + \beta^n$. Since $\alpha
^2 = 5+2\sqrt{6}$ and $\beta^2 = 5-2\sqrt{6}$ are the two roots of $x^2-10x+1=0$
, the sequence $T_k = S_{2k} = (\alpha^2)^k+(\beta^2)^k$ satisfies
$$T_k = 10\,T_{k-1}-T_{k-2},\qquad T_0=2,\; T_1=10.$$
By induction every $T_k$ is a **nonnegative integer**. In particular, with $2016
 = 2\cdot 1008$,
$$A + A^{-1} = \alpha^{2016}+\beta^{2016} = T_{1008} =: N \in \mathbb{Z}.$$

**Extracting the fractional part.** Since $0<\beta<1$, we have $0<\beta^{2016}<1
$, hence
$$A = N - \beta^{2016} = (N-1) + \bigl(1-\beta^{2016}\bigr).$$
Because $0 < \beta^{2016} < 1$, the quantity $1-\beta^{2016}$ lies in $(0,1)$. T
herefore
$$\lfloor A\rfloor = N-1, \qquad \{A\} = 1-\beta^{2016}.$$

**Size of $\beta^{2016}$.** We have $\beta = \sqrt{3}-\sqrt{2}\approx 0.3178$, s
o
$$\log_{10}\beta^{2016} = 2016\cdot\log_{10}(0.3178\ldots) \approx 2016\cdot(-0.
4978) \approx -1003.5.$$
Thus
$$0 < \beta^{2016} < 10^{-1003} \ll 10^{-31}.$$

**Decimal expansion.** The fractional part is
$$\{A\} = 1 - \beta^{2016} = 0.\underbrace{999\ldots 9}_{\ge 1003\text{ nines}}\
ldots$$
More precisely, $1 - \varepsilon$ with $0<\varepsilon<10^{-1003}$ has at least $
1003$ consecutive $9$'s immediately after the decimal point. Since $1003 \ge 31$
, the $31^{\text{st}}$ digit after the decimal point is

$$\boxed{9}.$$
