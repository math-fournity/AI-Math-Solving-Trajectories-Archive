### Step 1: Factor the recurrence

The recurrence is $a_{k-1} + \frac{2}{a_{k-1}} = 2a_k + \frac{1}{a_k}$. Multiply
ing both sides by $a_{k-1}a_k$:

$$a_{k-1}^2 a_k + 2a_k = 2a_{k-1}a_k^2 + a_{k-1}$$

Rearranging: $a_{k-1}a_k(a_{k-1} - 2a_k) - (a_{k-1} - 2a_k) = 0$, so:

$$(a_{k-1} - 2a_k)(a_{k-1}a_k - 1) = 0$$

Thus at each step, either **(D)** $a_k = a_{k-1}/2$, or **(R)** $a_k = 1/a_{k-1}
$.

### Step 2: Reformulate in $\log_2$

Let $b_k = \log_2(a_k)$. The two operations become:
- **(D)** $b_k = b_{k-1} - 1$ (call this $T_1$)
- **(R)** $b_k = -b_{k-1}$ (call this $T_2$)

We need a sequence of 2015 operations (each $T_1$ or $T_2$) that maps $b_0$ back
 to $b_0$, maximizing $b_0$ (hence $a_0 = 2^{b_0}$).

### Step 3: Track the affine transformation

After any sequence of operations, the value is $b = \sigma \cdot b_0 + c$ where
$\sigma \in \{+1,-1\}$ and $c \in \mathbb{Z}$, updated by:
- $T_1$: $(\sigma, c) \to (\sigma,\; c-1)$
- $T_2$: $(\sigma, c) \to (-\sigma,\; -c)$

After 2015 operations we get $(\sigma_f, c_f)$ and need $\sigma_f b_0 + c_f = b_
0$.

**Parity analysis:** $T_1$ flips the parity of $c$; $T_2$ preserves it. Starting
 from $c=0$ (even), after $n_1$ operations of type $T_1$, the parity of $c_f$ eq
uals $n_1 \pmod{2}$.

### Step 4: Eliminate the even-$T_2$ case

If the number of $T_2$'s is even, then $\sigma_f = +1$ and $n_1 = 2015 - \text{e
ven} = \text{odd}$, so $c_f$ is **odd**, hence $c_f \neq 0$. The condition $b_0
+ c_f = b_0$ requires $c_f = 0$, which is **impossible**. So no valid sequence e

### Step 5: Maximize in the odd-$T_2$ case

With an odd number $k$ of $T_2$'s, we have $\sigma_f = -1$ and $n_1 = 2015 - k$
(even), so $c_f$ is even. The condition becomes $-b_0 + c_f = b_0$, i.e., $b_0 =
 c_f/2$.

Write the operation sequence in blocks: $T_1^{a_1}\, T_2\, T_1^{a_2}\, T_2 \cdot
s T_2\, T_1^{a_{k+1}}$ with $a_i \geq 0$ and $\sum a_i = 2015 - k$. Tracking $(\
sigma, c)$ through the blocks yields:

$$c_f = a_1 - a_2 + a_3 - a_4 + \cdots + a_k - a_{k+1} = \sum_{\text{odd } i} a_
i - \sum_{\text{even } i} a_i$$

To maximize $c_f$, set all even-indexed $a_i = 0$ and all odd-indexed $a_i$ to s
hare the full sum $2015 - k$. This gives $c_f = 2015 - k$, so:

$$b_0 = \frac{2015 - k}{2}$$

This is maximized by the smallest odd $k$, namely $k = 1$, giving:

$$b_0 = \frac{2015 - 1}{2} = 1007$$
