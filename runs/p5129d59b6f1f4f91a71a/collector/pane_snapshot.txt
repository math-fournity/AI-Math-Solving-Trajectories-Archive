## Proof

Let $X$ be a Banach space, $Y$ a normed space, and $\{T_i\}_{i \in I}$ a family
of bounded linear operators from $X$ to $Y$ such that
$$\sup_{i \in I} \|T_i x\| < \infty \quad \text{for every } x \in X.$$

**Define a seminorm.** Set
$$p(x) := \sup_{i \in I} \|T_i x\|, \qquad x \in X.$$
The pointwise-boundedness hypothesis guarantees $p(x) < \infty$ for every $x$. P
ositive homogeneity and subadditivity are immediate: $p(\alpha x) = |\alpha|\,p(
x)$ and $p(x+y) \le p(x)+p(y)$ follow from the corresponding properties of each
$\|T_i(\cdot)\|$ and the supremum. Hence $p$ is a seminorm on $X$.

**Show $p$ is countably subadditive.** Let $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} x_n$ be a conver
gent series in $X$ with sum $x = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} x_n$. We must prove
$$p(x) \le \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} p(x_n).$$

Fix any index $i \in I$. Since $T_i$ is bounded (hence continuous) and linear,
$$T_i x = T_i\!\left(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} x_n\right) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} T_i x_
n,$$
where the series on the right converges in $Y$. Applying the triangle inequality
 to this convergent series,
$$\|T_i x\| = \left\|\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} T_i x_n\right\| \le \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}
 \|T_i x_n\| \le \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} p(x_n),$$
the last inequality using $\|T_i x_n\| \le \sup_{j \in I}\|T_j x_n\| = p(x_n)$ f
or every $n$.

The right-hand side $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} p(x_n)$ is independent of $i$. Taking t
he supremum over $i \in I$ yields
$$p(x) = \sup_{i \in I}\|T_i x\| \le \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} p(x_n).$$
Thus $p$ is countably subadditive.

**Apply Zabreǐko's Lemma.** Since $X$ is a Banach space and $p$ is a countably s
ubadditive seminorm on $X$, Zabreǐko's Lemma implies that $p$ is continuous. Con
tinuity of a seminorm at the origin gives a constant $M \ge 0$ such that
$$p(x) \le M\|x\| \quad \text{for all } x \in X.$$

**Conclude.** For every $i \in I$ and every $x \in X$,
$$\|T_i x\| \le p(x) \le M\|x\|.$$
In particular, for $\|x\| \le 1$ we have $\|T_i x\| \le M$, so
$$\|T_i\| = \sup_{\|x\|\le 1}\|T_i x\| \le M \quad \text{for every } i \in I.$$
Therefore
$$\sup_{i \in I}\|T_i\| \le M < \infty,$$
which is the uniform boundedness principle. $\blacksquare$
