## Setup: Method of Images (Odd Reflection)

Since the boundary condition is $u = 0$ on $\partial\Omega = \{x_3 = 0\}$, we us

$$\tilde{\Phi}(x) = \begin{cases} \Phi(x) & x_3 > 0, \\ -\Phi(x^*) & x_3 < 0, \e
nd{cases} \qquad \tilde{\Psi}(x) = \begin{cases} \Psi(x) & x_3 > 0, \\ -\Psi(x^*

(We assume the compatibility conditions $\Phi\big|_{x_3=0} = \Psi\big|_{x_3=0} =

e\Psi$ gives:

$$u(x,t) = \frac{\partial}{\partial t}\!\left[\frac{1}{4\pi t}\int_{|y-x|=t} \ti
lde\Phi(y)\,dS_y\right] + \frac{1}{4\pi t}\int_{|y-x|=t} \tilde\Psi(y)\,dS_y. \t

## Part (a): Formula for $t < x_3$

When $t < x_3$, every point $y$ on the sphere $|y - x| = t$ satisfies

$$y_3 \geq x_3 - t > 0,$$

so the entire sphere lies in the upper half-space. Therefore $\tilde\Phi(y) = \P
hi(y)$ and $\tilde\Psi(y) = \Psi(y)$ on the whole sphere, and $(\star)$ reduces
to the **free-space Kirchhoff formula**:

$$\boxed{u(x,t) = \frac{\partial}{\partial t}\!\left[\frac{1}{4\pi t}\int_{|y-x|
=t} \Phi(y)\,dS_y\right] + \frac{1}{4\pi t}\int_{|y-x|=t} \Psi(y)\,dS_y, \qquad
t < x_3.}$$

Equivalently, via the parametrization $y = x + ts$, $|s| = 1$:

$$u(x,t) = \frac{\partial}{\partial t}\!\left[\frac{t}{4\pi}\int_{|s|=1} \Phi(x+

**Physical interpretation:** The wave has not yet reached the boundary, so the b

## Part (b): Formula for all $t > 0$

When $t \geq x_3$, the sphere $|y - x| = t$ crosses the plane $y_3 = 0$ and exte
nds into $y_3 < 0$, where $\tilde\Phi$ and $\tilde\Psi$ take reflected (negated)

$$\mathcal{M}_f(\xi, t) := \frac{1}{4\pi t}\int_{\substack{|y - \xi| = t \\ y_3

$$\boxed{u(x,t) = \frac{\partial}{\partial t}\Big[\mathcal{M}_\Phi(x,t) - \mathc

**Initial condition $u(x,0) = \Phi(x)$:** As $t \to 0^+$ with $x_3 > 0$, the sph
