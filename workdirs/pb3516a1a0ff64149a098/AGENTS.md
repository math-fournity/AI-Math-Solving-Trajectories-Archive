# Problem

In a remote industrial sector, two specialized high-pressure steam valves, identified by their serial codes $p$ and $q$, are being tested for safety. The quality control protocol dictates that both serial codes must be prime numbers, with the primary valve $p$ always having a higher numerical value than the secondary valve $q$.

Engineers are monitoring the "stress differential" between the two valves, which is calculated as the difference between the fourth power of their serial codes, $p^4 - q^4$. A critical safety alarm is triggered only if this stress differential is divisible by the base frequency of $240$. However, during this specific test run, the safety alarm remains silent, meaning $240$ does not divide $p^4 - q^4$.

Under these specific constraints, what is the maximum possible value for the efficiency ratio of the valves, defined as the quotient $\frac{q}{p}$?
