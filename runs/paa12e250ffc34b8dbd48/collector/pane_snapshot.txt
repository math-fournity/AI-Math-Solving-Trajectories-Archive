## Proof

**Step 1: Identify the surface.**

Introduce cylindrical coordinates about the $y$-axis: let $r^2 = x^2 + z^2$ (so
$r \geq 0$). The equation becomes

$$(r^2 + y^2 + A^2 - B^2)^2 = 4A^2 r^2.$$

Taking the square root of both sides:

$$r^2 + y^2 + A^2 - B^2 = \pm\, 2Ar,$$

which rearranges to

$$(r \mp A)^2 + y^2 = B^2.$$

**Case 1:** $(r - A)^2 + y^2 = B^2$ — a circle of radius $B$ centered at $(r,y)=
(A,0)$.

**Case 2:** $(r + A)^2 + y^2 = B^2$. Since $r \geq 0$ and $A > 0$, we have $r +
A \geq A > B$ (using $0 < B < A$), so $(r+A)^2 \geq A^2 > B^2$, giving no real s
olutions.

Thus the surface is the torus obtained by rotating the circle $(r-A)^2 + y^2 = B
^2$ about the $y$-axis: a torus with **major radius** $A$ and **minor radius** $
B$.

**Step 2: Parametrize and compute the surface area.**

Parametrize the generating circle by $\theta$ and the rotation about the $y$-axi
s by $\phi$:

$$\begin{cases} x = (A + B\cos\theta)\cos\phi, \\ y = B\sin\theta, \\ z = (A + B
\cos\theta)\sin\phi, \end{cases}\qquad \theta,\phi \in [0,2\pi).$$

Compute the first fundamental form coefficients:

$$\mathbf{r}_\theta = (-B\sin\theta\cos\phi,\; B\cos\theta,\; -B\sin\theta\sin\p
hi),$$
$$\mathbf{r}_\phi = (-(A+B\cos\theta)\sin\phi,\; 0,\; (A+B\cos\theta)\cos\phi).$
$

$$E = \|\mathbf{r}_\theta\|^2 = B^2\sin^2\theta + B^2\cos^2\theta = B^2,$$
$$G = \|\mathbf{r}_\phi\|^2 = (A + B\cos\theta)^2,$$
$$F = \mathbf{r}_\theta \cdot \mathbf{r}_\phi = 0.$$

Hence the surface area element is

$$dS = \sqrt{EG - F^2}\,d\theta\,d\phi = B\,(A + B\cos\theta)\,d\theta\,d\phi.$$

**Step 3: Integrate.**

$$S = \int_0^{2\pi}\!\!\int_0^{2\pi} B\,(A + B\cos\theta)\,d\theta\,d\phi = 2\pi
 B \int_0^{2\pi}(A + B\cos\theta)\,d\theta.$$

Since $\int_0^{2\pi}\cos\theta\,d\theta = 0$ and $\int_0^{2\pi} d\theta = 2\pi$:

$$S = 2\pi B \cdot 2\pi A = 4\pi^2 A B.$$

**Result.** The surface area of the torus is

$$\boxed{4\pi^2 A B}.$$
