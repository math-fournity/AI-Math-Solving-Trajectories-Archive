**Step 1: Reduction to $F$.**

Write $a = \sum_{i=-k}^{\infty} f_i\, y^i$ with $f_i \in F$. For $a$ to commute
with every $r(x) \in F$:

$$r(x)\, a = \sum_i r(x)\, f_i\, y^i, \qquad a\, r(x) = \sum_i f_i\, y^i\, r(x)
= \sum_i f_i\, \sigma^i(r(x))\, y^i = \sum_i f_i\, r(2^i x)\, y^i.$$

Since $F$ is commutative, $r(x)\, f_i = f_i\, r(x)$, so equality requires for ev

$$f_i\, r(x) = f_i\, r(2^i x), \quad \text{i.e.,} \quad f_i\bigl(r(x) - r(2^i x)
\bigr) = 0.$$

If $f_i \neq 0$, then $r(x) = r(2^i x)$ for **all** $r \in \mathbb{C}(x)$. Takin

**Step 2: The fixed field of $\sigma$.**

$$f_0(2x) = f_0(x).$$

So $Z(D) = \{r \in \mathbb{C}(x) : r(2x) = r(x)\} = \mathbb{C}(x)^{\sigma}$, the

**Step 3: Computing $\mathbb{C}(x)^{\sigma}$.**

$q \neq 0$. The condition $r(2x) = r(x)$ gives $p(2x)\, q(x) = p(x)\, q(2x)$.

*Degree comparison:* If $\deg p = m$ and $\deg q = n$, comparing leading coeffic

*Root analysis:* Since $\gcd(p, q) = 1$, any root $\alpha$ of $p$ (in $\overline

\geq 0$.

Thus $\{2^n \alpha : n \in \mathbb{Z}\}$ are all roots of $p$. Since $p$ has fin

By the same argument applied to $q$, the only root of $q$ is $0$. Therefore $p(x

**Conclusion.** The center of $D$ is

$$Z(D) = \mathbb{C}.$$
