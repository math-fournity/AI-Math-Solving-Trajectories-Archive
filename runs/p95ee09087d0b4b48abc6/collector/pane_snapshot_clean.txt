## Proof

**Setup.** Let $G_i$ denote the simple graph on the $N$ hubs formed by mogul $i$
's cables. The constraint is: for every proper subset $S \subsetneq [N]$, the un
ion $\bigcup_{i \in S} G_i$ is disconnected. We wish to maximize $d = \sum_{i=1}
^N |E(G_i)|$.

**Upper bound.** Since a single mogul $\{i\}$ is a group of fewer than $N$ mogul
s (for $N \geq 2$), each individual $G_i$ must be disconnected. A disconnected g
raph on $N$ vertices has at most $\binom{N-1}{2}$ edges — achieved by $K_{N-1}$
on $N-1$ vertices plus one isolated vertex. Therefore:
$$d = \sum_{i=1}^N |E(G_i)| \leq N \cdot \binom{N-1}{2} = \frac{N(N-1)(N-2)}{2}.
$$

**Construction achieving the bound.** Label the hubs $1, 2, \ldots, N$. Each mog
ul $i$ ($i = 1, \ldots, N$) installs cables forming the complete graph $K_{N-1}$
 on hubs $\{1, 2, \ldots, N-1\}$, leaving hub $N$ with no incident cables. This
gives each mogul $\binom{N-1}{2}$ cables, so:
$$d = N \cdot \binom{N-1}{2} = \frac{N(N-1)(N-2)}{2}.$$

**Verification of the constraint.** For any subset $S \subseteq [N]$ (proper or
not), the union $\bigcup_{i \in S} G_i$ is exactly $K_{N-1}$ on hubs $\{1, \ldot
s, N-1\}$ with hub $N$ isolated — regardless of which moguls are included, since
 every mogul has the same edge set. Hub $N$ is always isolated, so every union g
raph is disconnected. In particular, every proper subset of moguls yields a disc
onnected graph, satisfying the constraint.

**The bound is tight.** The upper bound $N \cdot \binom{N-1}{2}$ follows from th
e necessary condition that each $G_i$ is disconnected, and the construction achi
eves it exactly.

**Answer.** The largest possible value of $d$ is:

$$\boxed{\dfrac{N(N-1)(N-2)}{2}}$$
