## Solution

**Setup.** The Architect (White King) sits at **a1**, a corner square. We place
a Black Bishop and a White Bishop on two distinct squares among the remaining 63
, such that neither bishop's diagonal passes through a1.

**Identifying the forbidden squares.**

A bishop on square X has a1 in its diagonal line of sight if and only if X lies
on one of the two diagonals through a1.

From a1, the two diagonal directions are:
- **Northeast (the long diagonal a1–h8):** a1, b2, c3, d4, e5, f6, g7, h8 — 8 sq
uares.
- **Northwest:** decreasing file from a1 goes off the board immediately, so this
 diagonal contains only a1 itself.

Therefore, the only squares (other than a1) whose bishop-diagonal passes through
 a1 are the 7 squares on the a1–h8 diagonal:

$$\{b2,\; c3,\; d4,\; e5,\; f6,\; g7,\; h8\}.$$

A bishop placed on any of these 7 squares has a1 on its diagonal, and since a1 i
s the corner endpoint of that diagonal, nothing blocks the laser from reaching a
1. So all 7 are forbidden for **both** bishops.

**Counting allowed squares.**

Total squares excluding a1: 63.
Forbidden squares (on the a1–h8 diagonal, excluding a1): 7.
Allowed squares: $63 - 7 = 56$.

**Counting placements.**

The two drones are **distinct units** (Black Bishop ≠ White Bishop), so we count
 **ordered** placements:
- Black Bishop: 56 choices.
- White Bishop: 56 − 1 = 55 choices (must differ from the Black Bishop's square)
.

$$56 \times 55 = 3080.$$

**Verification of constraints:**
1. Each drone occupies a unique rack, neither at a1 — both chosen from the 56 al
lowed squares. ✓
2. Shadow Sentinel's laser doesn't cross a1 — it's not on the a1–h8 diagonal. ✓
3. Light Guardian's laser doesn't cross a1 — same reason. ✓
4. The two drones are on distinct racks — enforced by 56 × 55. ✓

$$\boxed{3080}$$
