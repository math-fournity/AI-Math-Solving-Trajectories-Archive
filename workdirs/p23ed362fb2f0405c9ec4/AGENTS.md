# Problem

In the circular command center of a deep-sea research station, there are 10 specialized communication ports arranged in a perfect circle along the perimeter. 

A "link" is defined as a direct fiber-optic cable connecting any two of these 10 ports. An engineering team is analyzing "data clusters," which are sets consisting of exactly three distinct links. 

A data cluster is considered "stable" if every pair of links within that cluster shares at least one physical connection point. This means that for any two cables in the cluster, they must either cross each other somewhere inside the circular room or be plugged into the same port on the wall.

Find the total number of unique stable data clusters that can be formed using the 10 ports.
