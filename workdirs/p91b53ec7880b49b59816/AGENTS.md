# Problem

In a specialized digital vault, a security system generates encryption keys based on a strict set of protocols. Initially, the vault's display is empty. A technician can perform only two types of operations to modify the sequence of integers on the screen:

1. **Protocol Alpha**: The technician may add two "1"s to the display.
2. **Protocol Beta**: If two identical integers $n$ are currently on the display, the technician may delete both and replace them with one instance of $n+1$ and one instance of $n-1$.

The vault will only unlock once the specific integer 2005 appears anywhere on the display. What is the minimum number of total operations (moves) required to have the number 2005 appear on the board for the first time?
