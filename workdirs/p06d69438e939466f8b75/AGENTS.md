# Problem

A specialized task force is composed of exactly 10 distinct research committees. To ensure a focused scope, every committee is assigned exactly 3 specific expert consultants from a general pool of $n$ available specialists.

The task force operates under two strict organizational constraints: 
First, every possible pair of committees must share at least one expert in common to facilitate cross-communication. 
Second, to prevent burnout, the task force regulations dictate that while every specialist in the pool of $n$ must be assigned to at least one committee, no single specialist is permitted to serve on more than 5 committees.

Find the sum of all possible values for the total number of specialists $n$.
