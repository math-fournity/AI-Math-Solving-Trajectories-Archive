## Solution

**Setup.** Let $P_0$ be a regular tetrahedron of volume $1$. At each generation,
 every exposed triangular face sprouts a new outward regular tetrahedron whose b
ase is the midpoint triangle of that face.

**Volume scaling.** The midpoint triangle of a face has side length half of the
face's edge. The new tetrahedron built on it therefore has edge length $\tfrac{1
}{2}$ of the tetrahedron owning that face, so its volume is $\left(\tfrac{1}{2}\
right)^3 = \tfrac{1}{8}$ of that owning tetrahedron's volume. Tetrahedra added a
t step $k$ thus each have volume $\left(\tfrac{1}{8}\right)^k$.

**Face count.** When a tetrahedron sprouts on a face, the midpoint triangle beco
mes the (internal) base, leaving the $3$ corner triangles of the original face e
xposed, plus the $3$ lateral faces of the new tetrahedron exposed. So each expos
ed face is replaced by $6$ exposed faces:
$$F_{i+1} = 6F_i, \qquad F_0 = 4.$$
Hence $F_0=4,\; F_1=24,\; F_2=144$.

**Counting tetrahedra added.** The number of tetrahedra added at step $k$ equals
 the number of exposed faces at step $k-1$, i.e. $F_{k-1} = 4\cdot 6^{k-1}$.

**Total volume of $P_3$:**
$$V(P_3) = 1 + \sum_{k=1}^{3} F_{k-1}\left(\frac{1}{8}\right)^k = 1 + \sum_{k=1}
^{3} 4\cdot 6^{k-1}\cdot\frac{1}{8^k}.$$

Computing each term:
- $k=1$: $4\cdot 1\cdot \tfrac{1}{8} = \tfrac{4}{8} = \tfrac{1}{2}$.
- $k=2$: $4\cdot 6\cdot \tfrac{1}{64} = \tfrac{24}{64} = \tfrac{3}{8}$.
- $k=3$: $4\cdot 36\cdot \tfrac{1}{512} = \tfrac{144}{512} = \tfrac{9}{32}$.

Therefore
$$V(P_3) = 1 + \frac{1}{2} + \frac{3}{8} + \frac{9}{32} = \frac{32+16+12+9}{32}
= \frac{69}{32}.$$

**Coprimality.** $69 = 3\cdot 23$ and $32 = 2^5$, so $\gcd(69,32)=1$. Thus $m=69
$, $n=32$.

$$m+n = 69 + 32 = \boxed{101}.$$
