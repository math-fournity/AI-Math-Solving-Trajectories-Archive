$$
x^2 + \alpha = x^2 + xy + xz + yz = (x+y)(x+z),

\frac{x}{z}+\frac{z}{x}+2 = \frac{x^2+z^2+2xz}{xz} = \frac{(x+z)^2}{xz}.

So the inequality becomes

\left(\frac{(x+y)(y+z)(z+x)}{xyz}\right)^{\!2} \ge C\,\frac{(x+z)^2}{xz}. \tag{$

Hence $C\le 64/4 = 16$, and the candidate is $\boxed{C=16}$.

**Step 4: Prove the inequality for $C=16$.**

We must show
$$
\left(\frac{(x+y)(y+z)(z+x)}{xyz}\right)^{\!2} \ge 16\,\frac{(x+z)^2}{xz}.
$$

$$
\frac{(x+y)(y+z)(z+x)}{xyz} \ge \frac{4(x+z)}{\sqrt{xz}}.

$$
\frac{(x+y)(y+z)}{xyz}\,\sqrt{xz} \ge 4

$$
(a+1)(b+1)\ge 4\sqrt{ab}.
