## Solution

The integral is over the standard 3-simplex $V = \{(x,y,z) : x,y,z \ge 0,\; x+y+
z \le 1\}$, with integrand $x\,y^9\,z^8\,(1-x-y-z)^4$.

**Dirichlet's integral formula.** For $a_i > 0$,
$$\int_{\substack{t_i \ge 0 \\ \sum t_i \le 1}} t_1^{a_1-1}\cdots t_n^{a_n-1}\!\
left(1-\textstyle\sum t_i\right)^{a_{n+1}-1} dt_1\cdots dt_n = \frac{\Gamma(a_1)
\cdots\Gamma(a_{n+1})}{\Gamma(a_1+\cdots+a_{n+1})}.$$

This follows from the change of variables $t_i = u_i/\left(\sum u_j\right)$, $s
= \sum u_j$, which factors the integral into a product of independent Gamma inte
grals—one for each $u_i$ over $[0,\infty)$ and one Jacobian factor $s^{n}$ that
combines with the $(1-\sum t_i)$ term to produce the $(a_{n+1}-1)$ power—yieldin
g $\prod \Gamma(a_i)/\Gamma(\sum a_i)$.

**Applying it.** Matching $x^1 y^9 z^8 (1-x-y-z)^4$ to the form $t_1^{a_1-1}t_2^
{a_2-1}t_3^{a_3-1}(1-\sum t_i)^{a_4-1}$:

$$a_1 = 2,\quad a_2 = 10,\quad a_3 = 9,\quad a_4 = 5,\quad a_1+a_2+a_3+a_4 = 26.
$$

Since all $a_i$ are positive integers, $\Gamma(a_i) = (a_i-1)!$:

$$I = \frac{\Gamma(2)\,\Gamma(10)\,\Gamma(9)\,\Gamma(5)}{\Gamma(26)} = \frac{1!\
cdot 9!\cdot 8!\cdot 4!}{25!} = \frac{9!\cdot 8!\cdot 4!}{25!}.$$

**Simplifying.** Cancel $9!$ from $25! = 25\cdot24\cdots10\cdot 9!$:

$$I = \frac{8!\cdot 4!}{25\cdot24\cdot23\cdot22\cdot21\cdot20\cdot19\cdot18\cdot
17\cdot16\cdot15\cdot14\cdot13\cdot12\cdot11\cdot10}.$$

Prime factorizations: $8!\cdot4! = (2^7\cdot3^2\cdot5\cdot7)(2^3\cdot3) = 2^{10}
\cdot3^3\cdot5\cdot7$, and the denominator $= 2^{15}\cdot3^6\cdot5^5\cdot7^2\cdo
t11^2\cdot13\cdot17\cdot19\cdot23$ (obtained by summing prime powers across $25,
24,\ldots,10$). Therefore

$$I = \frac{1}{2^5\cdot3^3\cdot5^4\cdot7\cdot11^2\cdot13\cdot17\cdot19\cdot23}.$
$

$$3780000\cdot121 = 457380000,\quad \cdot13 = 5945940000,\quad \cdot17 = 1010809
