**Answer: No. GCH does not imply that every uncountable cardinal is of the form
$2^\kappa$ for some ordinal $\kappa$.**

**Proof.**

Recall that the Generalized Continuum Hypothesis (GCH) states:

$$\text{For every infinite cardinal } \lambda,\quad 2^\lambda = \lambda^+,$$

where $\lambda^+$ denotes the successor cardinal of $\lambda$.

**Step 1: Characterize the cardinals of the form $2^\kappa$ under GCH.**

Let $\kappa$ be an ordinal. We consider $2^\kappa$ in the sense of cardinal expo
nentiation, i.e., $2^\kappa = 2^{|\kappa|}$ where $|\kappa|$ is the cardinality
of $\kappa$.

- If $\kappa$ is finite, then $2^\kappa$ is a finite cardinal.
- If $\kappa$ is infinite, then $|\kappa|$ is an infinite cardinal $\lambda$, an
d under GCH:
$$2^\kappa = 2^\lambda = \lambda^+.$$

Therefore, under GCH, the set of all cardinals of the form $2^\kappa$ (for $\kap
pa$ an ordinal) is:

$$\{2^n : n < \omega\} \;\cup\; \{\lambda^+ : \lambda \text{ is an infinite card
inal}\}.$$

The **uncountable** cardinals in this set are exactly:

$$\{\lambda^+ : \lambda \text{ is an infinite cardinal}\},$$

i.e., the **successor cardinals of infinite cardinals**.

**Step 2: Exhibit an uncountable cardinal that is not a successor cardinal.**

Consider $\aleph_\omega$, defined as:

$$\aleph_\omega = \sup\{\aleph_n : n < \omega\} = \sup\{\aleph_0, \aleph_1, \ale
ph_2, \dots\}.$$

- $\aleph_\omega$ is uncountable (since $\aleph_\omega \geq \aleph_1 > \aleph_0$
).
- $\aleph_\omega$ is a **limit cardinal**: its index $\omega$ is a limit ordinal
, so $\aleph_\omega$ is not of the form $\aleph_{\alpha+1}$ for any ordinal $\al
pha$. Equivalently, $\aleph_\omega$ is not the successor cardinal $\lambda^+$ of
 any cardinal $\lambda$.

(If $\aleph_\omega = \lambda^+$ for some $\lambda$, then $\lambda = \aleph_\beta
$ for some $\beta$ with $\beta + 1 = \omega$, but $\omega$ is a limit ordinal an
d has no predecessor, contradiction.)

**Step 3: Conclude.**

Under GCH, every uncountable cardinal of the form $2^\kappa$ is a successor card
inal of an infinite cardinal (Step 1). But $\aleph_\omega$ is an uncountable car
dinal that is a limit cardinal, hence not a successor cardinal (Step 2). Therefo
re:

$$\aleph_\omega \neq 2^\kappa \quad \text{for every ordinal } \kappa.$$

This holds under GCH (GCH does not eliminate limit cardinals — it only fixes the
 values of $2^\lambda$ for infinite $\lambda$; it says nothing about whether lim
it cardinals exist, and indeed $\aleph_\omega$ exists in ZFC regardless of GCH).

Hence GCH does **not** imply that every uncountable cardinal is of the form $2^\
kappa$ for some ordinal $\kappa$. $\blacksquare$
