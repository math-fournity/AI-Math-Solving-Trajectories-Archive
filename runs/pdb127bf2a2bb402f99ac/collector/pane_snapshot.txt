## Solution

We solve the quasi-linear PDE $u u_x + y u_y = x$ with initial data $x=s,\; y=s,
\; u=2s$ using the **method of characteristics**.

### Characteristic equations

The characteristic ODEs, parameterized by $t$, are
$$
\frac{dx}{dt}=u,\qquad \frac{dy}{dt}=y,\qquad \frac{du}{dt}=x,
$$
with initial conditions at $t=0$:
$$
x(0)=s,\quad y(0)=s,\quad u(0)=2s.
$$

### Solving the characteristic system

**The $y$-equation** decouples immediately:
$$
\frac{dy}{dt}=y \;\Longrightarrow\; y = s\,e^{t}.
$$

**The $(x,u)$-system** couples through $\dot x = u,\; \dot u = x$. Differentiati
ng the first equation:
$$
\frac{d^{2}x}{dt^{2}}=\frac{du}{dt}=x,
$$
so $x = A e^{t} + B e^{-t}$ and consequently $u = \dot x = A e^{t} - B e^{-t}$.

Applying the initial conditions:
$$
x(0)=A+B=s,\qquad u(0)=A-B=2s,
$$
gives $A = \tfrac{3s}{2}$ and $B = -\tfrac{s}{2}$. Hence
$$
x = \tfrac{3s}{2}\,e^{t} - \tfrac{s}{2}\,e^{-t},\qquad
u = \tfrac{3s}{2}\,e^{t} + \tfrac{s}{2}\,e^{-t},\qquad
y = s\,e^{t}.
$$

### Eliminating the parameters $s$ and $t$

From $y = s e^{t}$ we have $e^{t} = y/s$. Substituting into the expression for $
x$:
$$
x = \tfrac{3s}{2}\cdot\frac{y}{s} - \tfrac{s}{2}\cdot\frac{s}{y}
   = \frac{3y}{2} - \frac{s^{2}}{2y}.
$$
Multiplying by $2y$:
$$
2xy = 3y^{2} - s^{2}\;\Longrightarrow\; s^{2} = 3y^{2} - 2xy.
$$

Now substitute into the expression for $u$:
$$
u = \tfrac{3s}{2}\cdot\frac{y}{s} + \tfrac{s}{2}\cdot\frac{s}{y}
   = \frac{3y}{2} + \frac{s^{2}}{2y}.
$$
Using $s^{2}=3y^{2}-2xy$:
$$
u = \frac{3y}{2} + \frac{3y^{2}-2xy}{2y}
   = \frac{3y}{2} + \frac{3y-2x}{2}
   = \frac{6y-2x}{2}
   = 3y - x.
$$

### Verification

With $u = 3y - x$:
- $u_x = -1$, $\;u_y = 3$.
- $u\,u_x + y\,u_y = (3y-x)(-1) + y\cdot 3 = -3y + x + 3y = x$. ✓
- On the initial curve $x=s,\,y=s$: $u = 3s - s = 2s$. ✓

### Result

$$
\boxed{\,u(x,y) = 3y - x\,}
$$

### PROOF COMPLETE
