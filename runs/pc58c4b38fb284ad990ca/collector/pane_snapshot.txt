# Solution

## Setup

The cake is a circle with 5 pieces in clockwise order: $x_1=15, x_2=5, x_3=20, x
_4=40, x_5=20$. Total weight $= 100$.

Arash moves first (any piece). After that, every pick must be **adjacent** to an
 already-removed piece. Once the first piece is removed, the remaining 4 pieces
form a **linear array** (the circle is "cut open"), and each subsequent pick mus
t come from **either end** of this array. This is the classic "pick-from-ends" g
ame.

With 5 pieces, Arash gets 3 picks (turns 1, 3, 5) and Bahram gets 2 picks (turns
 2, 4). Bahram plays to maximize his own total (= minimize Arash's, since the to
tal is fixed).

## Analysis tool

For a linear array $a[0..n-1]$, define the **advantage** $V(i,j)$ = (first playe
r's total − second player's total) on subarray $a[i..j]$:

$$V(i,j) = \max\big(a[i] - V(i+1,j),\; a[j] - V(i,j-1)\big), \qquad V(i,i)=a[i].
$$

First player gets $\frac{S+V}{2}$, second gets $\frac{S-V}{2}$, where $S$ is the
 subarray sum.

## Evaluating each first move

**Case 1: Arash picks $x_1=15$.** Remaining array: $[5,20,40,20]$ (i.e. $x_2,x_3
,x_4,x_5$). Bahram moves first.

| Length | Subarrays and $V$ |
|--------|-------------------|
| 1 | $5,\;20,\;40,\;20$ |
| 2 | $[5,20]\to15,\;[20,40]\to20,\;[40,20]\to20$ |
| 3 | $[5,20,40]\to\max(5{-}20,40{-}15)=25,\;[20,40,20]\to\max(20{-}20,20{-}20)=
0$ |
| 4 | $[5,20,40,20]\to\max(5{-}0,20{-}25)=5$ |

Bahram's advantage $=5$, sum $=85$. Bahram gets $45$, Arash gets $40$ from subar
ray. **Arash total $= 15+40 = 55$.**

**Case 2: Arash picks $x_2=5$.** Remaining: $[20,40,20,15]$. Bahram first.

| Length | $V$ values |
|--------|------------|
| 2 | $[20,40]\to20,\;[40,20]\to20,\;[20,15]\to5$ |
| 3 | $[20,40,20]\to0,\;[40,20,15]\to\max(40{-}5,15{-}20)=35$ |
| 4 | $\max(20{-}35,15{-}0)=\max(-15,15)=15$ |

Advantage $=15$, sum $=95$. Bahram $55$, Arash $40$. **Arash total $= 5+40 = 45$
.**

**Case 3: Arash picks $x_3=20$.** Remaining: $[40,20,15,5]$. Bahram first.

| Length | $V$ values |
|--------|------------|
| 2 | $[40,20]\to20,\;[20,15]\to5,\;[15,5]\to10$ |
| 3 | $[40,20,15]\to\max(40{-}5,15{-}20)=35,\;[20,15,5]\to\max(20{-}10,5{-}5)=10
$ |
| 4 | $\max(40{-}10,5{-}35)=\max(30,-30)=30$ |

Advantage $=30$, sum $=80$. Bahram $55$, Arash $25$. **Arash total $= 20+25 = 45
$.**

**Case 4: Arash picks $x_4=40$.** Remaining: $[20,15,5,20]$ (i.e. $x_5,x_1,x_2,x
_3$). Bahram first.

| Length | $V$ values |
|--------|------------|
| 1 | $20,\;15,\;5,\;20$ |
| 2 | $[20,15]\to5,\;[15,5]\to10,\;[5,20]\to15$ |
| 3 | $[20,15,5]\to\max(20{-}10,5{-}5)=10,\;[15,5,20]\to\max(15{-}15,20{-}10)=10
$ |
| 4 | $\max(20{-}10,20{-}10)=10$ |

Advantage $=10$, sum $=60$. Bahram $35$, Arash $25$. **Arash total $= 40+25 = 65
$.**

**Verification by tracing all of Bahram's responses:**

- *Bahram takes left ($x_5=20$):* remaining $[15,5,20]$. Arash takes $20$ (right
): remaining $[15,5]$. Bahram takes $15$, Arash takes $5$. Arash sub $= 20+5=25$
, Bahram sub $= 20+15=35$. **Total Arash $= 40+25=65$.** ✓
- *Bahram takes right ($x_3=20$):* remaining $[20,15,5]$. Arash takes $20$ (left
): remaining $[15,5]$. Bahram takes $15$, Arash takes $5$. Arash sub $= 20+5=25$
, Bahram sub $= 20+15=35$. **Total Arash $= 40+25=65$.** ✓

Both of Bahram's options yield Arash $= 65$.

**Case 5: Arash picks $x_5=20$.** Remaining: $[15,5,20,40]$. Bahram first.

| Length | $V$ values |
|--------|------------|
| 2 | $[15,5]\to10,\;[5,20]\to15,\;[20,40]\to20$ |
| 3 | $[15,5,20]\to\max(15{-}15,20{-}10)=10,\;[5,20,40]\to\max(5{-}20,40{-}15)=2
5$ |
| 4 | $\max(15{-}25,40{-}10)=\max(-10,30)=30$ |

Advantage $=30$, sum $=80$. Bahram $55$, Arash $25$. **Arash total $= 20+25 = 45
$.**

## Conclusion

| First pick | Arash's total |
|------------|---------------|
| $x_1=15$ | $55$ |
| $x_2=5$ | $45$ |
| $x_3=20$ | $45$ |
| $x_4=40$ | $\mathbf{65}$ |
| $x_5=20$ | $45$ |

Arash maximizes his total by first picking $x_4 = 40$ (the largest piece). No ma
tter how Bahram responds, Arash then picks the piece worth $20$ adjacent to the
remaining cluster, and finally the piece worth $5$, yielding:

$$\boxed{65}$$
