# Problem

In a remote industrial network, three specialized processing hubs—Alpha, Beta, and Gamma—distribute power through shared conduits. Each hub has an internal power rating represented by the variables $x$, $y$, and $z$ respectively. The energy throughput between these hubs is governed by specific load-balancing protocols:

1. The total power generated by hub Alpha, when multiplied by the combined ratings of Beta and Gamma, must equal exactly 20 megawatts.
2. The total power generated by hub Beta, when multiplied by the combined ratings of Gamma and Alpha, must equal exactly 13 megawatts.
3. The total power generated by hub Gamma, when multiplied by the combined ratings of Alpha and Beta, must equal the square of a specific control constant, denoted as $c^2$.

The system's integrity depends on $x$, $y$, and $z$ being real numbers. Determine the sum of all possible integer values for the squared constant $c^2$ for which this power distribution network can exist.
