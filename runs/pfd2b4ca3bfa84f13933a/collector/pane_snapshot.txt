## Solution

Let the distance $AB = D$ km, A's normal speed $= a$ km/h, B's normal speed $= b
$ km/h. During 7:00–8:00 all speeds are halved.

**Case 1 (both start 6:50, meet 24 km from A).** From 6:50–7:00 (1/6 h) at full
speed; after 7:00 at half speed for time $t_1$:
$$\tfrac{a}{6}+\tfrac{a}{2}t_1=24,\qquad \tfrac{b}{6}+\tfrac{b}{2}t_1=D-24.$$
Both share the factor $(\tfrac16+\tfrac{t_1}{2})$, so $\frac{24}{a}=\frac{D-24}{
b}$, giving
$$\frac{a}{b}=\frac{24}{D-24}.\tag{1}$$

**Case 2 (A starts 7:10, B starts 6:50, meet at midpoint $D/2$).** Checking time
s shows the meeting occurs after 8:00. A travels 7:10–8:00 (5/6 h) at $a/2$, the
n at $a$; B travels 6:50–7:00 (1/6 h) at $b$, 7:00–8:00 (1 h) at $b/2$, then at
$b$. Let $\tau$ = time after 8:00 until meeting:
$$\text{A: }\tfrac{5a}{12}+a\tau=\tfrac D2,\qquad \text{B: }\tfrac b6+\tfrac b2+
b\tau=\tfrac D2.$$
From A: $\tau=\frac{D/2-5a/12}{a}=\frac{D}{2a}-\frac{5}{12}$.
From B: $\tau=\frac{D/2-2b/3}{b}=\frac{D}{2b}-\frac{2}{3}$.

Equating: $\frac{D}{2a}-\frac{5}{12}=\frac{D}{2b}-\frac{2}{3}$, i.e.
$$\frac{D}{2}\Big(\tfrac1b-\tfrac1a\Big)=\tfrac14.\tag{2}$$

**Case 3 (B starts 6:30, A starts 6:50, meet 20 km from A).** Meeting again fall
s after 8:00. A: 6:50–7:00 at $a$, 7:00–8:00 at $a/2$, then at $a$. B: 6:30–7:00
 (1/2 h) at $b$, 7:00–8:00 at $b/2$, then at $b$. Let $\sigma$ = time after 8:00
:
$$\text{A: }\tfrac a6+\tfrac a2+a\sigma=20,\qquad \text{B: }\tfrac b2+\tfrac b2+
b\sigma=D-20.$$
From A: $\sigma=\frac{20-2a/3}{a}=\frac{20}{a}-\frac{2}{3}$.
From B: $\sigma=\frac{D-20-b}{b}=\frac{D-20}{b}-1$.

Equating: $\frac{20}{a}-\frac{2}{3}=\frac{D-20}{b}-1$, i.e.
$$\frac{20}{a}-\frac{D-20}{b}=-\frac13.\tag{3}$$

**Solving.** From (1): $b=\frac{a(D-24)}{24}$, so $\frac1b=\frac{24}{a(D-24)}$.

Equation (2): $\frac{D}{2}\cdot\frac{24}{a(D-24)}-\frac{D}{2a}=\frac14$, i.e. $\
frac{D}{2a}\Big(\frac{24}{D-24}-1\Big)=\frac14$, so $\frac{D}{2a}\cdot\frac{48-D
}{D-24}=\frac14$, giving
$$a=\frac{2D(48-D)}{D-24}.\tag{4}$$

Equation (3): $\frac{20}{a}-\frac{24(D-20)}{a(D-24)}=-\frac13$, so $\frac1a\Big(
20-\frac{24(D-20)}{D-24}\Big)=-\frac13$. The bracket: $\frac{20(D-24)-24(D-20)}{
D-24}=\frac{20D-480-24D+480}{D-24}=\frac{-4D}{D-24}$. Thus $\frac{-4D}{a(D-24)}=
-\frac13$, giving
$$a=\frac{12D}{D-24}.\tag{5}$$

Equating (4) and (5): $\frac{2D(48-D)}{D-24}=\frac{12D}{D-24}$, so $2(48-D)=12$,
 hence $48-D=6$, and
$$D=42.$$

**Verification.** $D=42$: $a=\frac{12\cdot42}{18}=28$, $b=\frac{28\cdot18}{24}=2
1$.
