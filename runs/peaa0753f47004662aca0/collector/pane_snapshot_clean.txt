## Proof

We are asked to find the sum

$$A = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^3} = \frac{1}{1^3} + \frac{1}{2^3} + \frac{
1}{3^3} + \cdots.$$

### Step 1. Convergence

This is a $p$-series with $p = 3 > 1$, hence it converges absolutely. Concretely
, for $n \ge 1$ we have $0 < 1/n^3 \le 1/n^2$, and $\sum 1/n^2$ converges (it is
 the Basel series with sum $\pi^2/6$). By the comparison test, $A$ converges to
a finite positive real number.

### Step 2. Identification as $\zeta(3)$

The series is, by definition, the Riemann zeta function evaluated at $s = 3$:

$$A = \zeta(3) := \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^3}.$$

This constant is known as **Apéry's constant**.

### Step 3. Integral representation

Using the Gamma-function identity $\displaystyle \frac{1}{n^3} = \frac{1}{2}\int
_0^\infty t^2 e^{-nt}\,dt$ (which follows from $\int_0^\infty t^2 e^{-nt}\,dt =
\Gamma(3)/n^3 = 2/n^3$), and summing over $n \ge 1$ (justified by monotone conve
rgence since all terms are nonnegative):

$$A = \frac{1}{2}\int_0^\infty t^2 \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} e^{-nt}\,dt = \frac{1}{2}
\int_0^\infty \frac{t^2}{e^t - 1}\,dt.$$

This is a standard integral representation of $\zeta(3)$, confirming the identif
ication.

### Step 4. Numerical value

Evaluating the series (or the integral) gives

$$\zeta(3) = 1.20205\,69031\,59594\,28539\,97381\,\ldots$$

### Step 5. Remarks on closed form and irrationality

Unlike the even-integer values $\zeta(2k)$, which admit closed forms in powers o
f $\pi$ (e.g. $\zeta(2)=\pi^2/6$, $\zeta(4)=\pi^4/90$), **no closed-form express
ion for $\zeta(3)$ in terms of elementary constants is known**.

Apéry (1978) proved that $\zeta(3)$ is **irrational**, via the rapidly convergin
g series

$$\zeta(3) = \frac{5}{2}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^{n-1}}{n^3\binom{2n}{n}},
$$

together with an analysis of the denominators of the partial sums (the Apéry seq
uence). This shows $\zeta(3)$ is not a rational number, but its precise arithmet
ic nature (e.g., whether it is transcendental, or expressible in terms of $\pi$
and logarithms) remains open.

### Conclusion

The sum of the series is

$$\boxed{A = \zeta(3) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n^3} \approx 1.2020569031\ld
ots}$$

namely Apéry's constant. It is a well-defined convergent irrational real number
with the integral representation $\displaystyle A = \frac{1}{2}\int_0^\infty \fr
ac{t^2}{e^t-1}\,dt$, and no elementary closed form is known.
