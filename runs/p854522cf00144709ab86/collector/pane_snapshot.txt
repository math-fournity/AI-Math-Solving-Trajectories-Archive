**Adjacency structure.** The graph on {a,b,c,d,e,f,g} (4-connectivity) is:
- a–b, a–d, b–e, d–e (cycle a-b-e-d-a)
- c–d, c–f, d–g, f–g (cycle d-c-f-g-d)

These two 4-cycles share exactly the vertex **d**. P is adjacent to {a,c}; B is
adjacent to {e,g}.
