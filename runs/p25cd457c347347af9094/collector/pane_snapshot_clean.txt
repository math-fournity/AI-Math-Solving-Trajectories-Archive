### Parametrization

The condition $z = x^2+y^2 < 1$ means $x^2+y^2 < 1$, so $M$ is the graph of $f(x
,y)=x^2+y^2$ over the open unit disk $D = \{(x,y): x^2+y^2<1\}$. We parametrize
by
$$\varphi(x,y) = (x,\; y,\; x^2+y^2), \qquad (x,y)\in D,$$
which induces the orientation via the coordinates $(x,y)$.

### Pullback of $\omega$

On $M$ we have $dx = dx$, $dy = dy$, and
$$dz = 2x\,dx + 2y\,dy.$$

Compute each wedge:

$$dy\wedge dz = dy\wedge(2x\,dx+2y\,dy) = 2x\,(dy\wedge dx) = -2x\,dx\wedge dy,$
$

$$dz\wedge dx = (2x\,dx+2y\,dy)\wedge dx = 2y\,(dy\wedge dx) = -2y\,dx\wedge dy,
$$

$$dx\wedge dy = dx\wedge dy.$$

Substituting into $\omega$ (with $z = x^2+y^2$):

$$\varphi^*\omega = x(-2x\,dx\wedge dy) + y(-2y\,dx\wedge dy) + (x^2+y^2)\,dx\we
dge dy$$
$$= \bigl(-2x^2 - 2y^2 + x^2 + y^2\bigr)\,dx\wedge dy = -(x^2+y^2)\,dx\wedge dy.
$$

### Evaluate the integral

$$\int_M \omega = \int_D -(x^2+y^2)\,dx\,dy.$$

Switch to polar coordinates $(r,\theta)$ with $x^2+y^2 = r^2$, $dx\,dy = r\,dr\,
d\theta$, $0\le r < 1$, $0\le\theta<2\pi$:

$$\int_M \omega = -\int_0^{2\pi}\!\!\int_0^1 r^2\cdot r\,dr\,d\theta = -2\pi\int
_0^1 r^3\,dr = -2\pi\cdot\frac{1}{4} = -\frac{\pi}{2}.$$

### Result

$$\boxed{\int_M \omega = -\dfrac{\pi}{2}}$$
