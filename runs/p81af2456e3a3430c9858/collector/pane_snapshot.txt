## Definition of the Thom–Pontryagin Map

**Setup and notation.** Let $M$ be a compact oriented $n$-manifold and let $N_1,
\dots,N_k$ be pairwise disjoint, proper, oriented $(n-1)$-submanifolds. We are g
iven a *trivial* tubular neighborhood, i.e. an embedding
$$\Phi:\bigsqcup_{j=1}^{k}\bigl([-1,1]\times N_j\bigr)\;\hookrightarrow\; M$$
that identifies each $\{0\}\times N_j$ with $N_j\subset M$ and respects the orie
ntations (the orientation of $[-1,1]$ together with the given orientation of $N_
j$ induces, via $\Phi$, the ambient orientation of $M$). Write
$$U_j=\Phi\bigl([-1,1]\times N_j\bigr)\subset M,\qquad U=\bigcup_{j=1}^k U_j.$$
Let $\bigvee_{j=1}^k S^1_j$ be the wedge of $k$ oriented circles with wedge poin
t $*$, and let $\iota_j:S^1_j\hookrightarrow\bigvee S^1_j$ be the inclusion of t
he $j$-th summand.

**The collapse map.** Choose, for each $j$, the standard degree-$+1$ quotient ma
p
$$\varphi:[-1,1]\longrightarrow S^1,\qquad \varphi(t)=e^{i\pi t},$$
which satisfies $\varphi(-1)=\varphi(1)=-1$ (the basepoint of $S^1$) and wraps t
he interval once, positively, around the circle. Identifying $S^1$ with $S^1_j$
via an orientation-preserving homeomorphism sending $-1$ to $*$, we regard $\var
phi$ as a map $\varphi_j:[-1,1]\to S^1_j$ with $\varphi_j(\pm1)=*$.

Define
$$\rho_{N_1,\dots,N_k}:M\longrightarrow\bigvee_{j=1}^{k}S^1_j$$
by
$$\rho_{N_1,\dots,N_k}(p)= \begin{cases} \iota_j\bigl(\varphi_j(t)\bigr) & \text
{if } p=\Phi(t,x)\in U_j\text{ for some }(t,x)\in[-1,1]\times N_j,\\[4pt] * & \t
ext{if } p\in M\setminus U. \end{cases}$$

**Well-definedness and continuity.** The two pieces overlap only along the bound
ary $\partial U_j=\Phi(\{-1,1\}\times N_j)$. On this boundary $t=\pm1$, so $\var
phi_j(t)=*$, which agrees with the constant value $*$ assigned to $M\setminus U$
. Hence the two definitions patch to a single continuous map. The $U_j$ are pair
wise disjoint (since the $N_j$ are disjoint and the tubular neighborhoods can be
 taken disjoint), so there is no ambiguity in the first branch.

**Summary.** The Thom–Pontryagin map is the **collapse map** that

1. contracts the entire complement $M\setminus U$ of the tubular neighborhood to
 the wedge point $*$, and
2. on each tubular strip $[-1,1]\times N_j$, projects onto the normal $[-1,1]$-f
actor and wraps it once (positively, using the orientation) around the correspon
ding circle $S^1_j$, sending the boundary $\{\pm1\}\times N_j$ to $*$.

This is the standard Pontryagin–Thom collapse construction for a codimension-one
, coorientable (here oriented) submanifold, applied simultaneously to the disjoi
nt union $N_1\cup\cdots\cup N_k$ and landing in the wedge $\bigvee_{j=1}^k S^1_j
$.
