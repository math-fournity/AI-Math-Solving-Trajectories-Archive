To stabilize the bracket, a perpendicular brace is welded from point $B$ to a point $D$ located on the straight line segment forming the base $AC$. This configuration creates a perfect right angle at the junction, such that $\angle BDA = 90^\circ$. The lead architect requires that the distances from point $D$ to the endpoints of the base, specifically the segments $AD$ and $CD$, must both be measured in whole centimeters (integers).
