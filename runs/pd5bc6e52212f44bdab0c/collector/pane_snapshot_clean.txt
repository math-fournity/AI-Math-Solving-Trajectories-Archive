## Universal Property of the Direct Limit

**Statement.** Let $\Lambda$ be a filtered category, $R$ a ring, and $\mathcal{C
}$ one of $\mathbf{Set}$, $R\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}$, or $R\text{-}\mathbf{Alg}$. L
et $\lambda \mapsto M_\lambda$ be a functor $\Lambda \to \mathcal{C}$ with trans
ition maps $\alpha_\lambda^\kappa : M_\kappa \to M_\lambda$ (for $\kappa \to \la
mbda$ in $\Lambda$). Denote by

$$\varinjlim_{\lambda \in \Lambda} M_\lambda$$

the direct limit, equipped with canonical maps $\varphi_\lambda : M_\lambda \to
\varinjlim M_\lambda$ satisfying $\varphi_\lambda \circ \alpha_\lambda^\kappa =
\varphi_\kappa$ for every $\kappa \to \lambda$.

**Universal property.** For every object $N$ of $\mathcal{C}$ and every family o
f maps $\beta_\lambda : M_\lambda \to N$ compatible with the transition maps in
the sense that

$$\beta_\lambda \circ \alpha_\lambda^\kappa = \beta_\kappa \qquad \text{for all
} \kappa \to \lambda \text{ in } \Lambda,$$

there exists a **unique** map

$$\beta : \varinjlim_{\lambda} M_\lambda \longrightarrow N$$

such that the diagram

$$\begin{array}{ccc} M_\lambda & \xrightarrow{\varphi_\lambda} & \varinjlim M_\l
ambda \\ & \searrow{\beta_\lambda} & \downarrow{\beta} \\ & & N \end{array}$$

commutes for every $\lambda$, i.e.,

$$\beta \circ \varphi_\lambda = \beta_\lambda \qquad \text{for all } \lambda \in
 \Lambda.$$

In other words, pre-composition with the canonical maps $\varphi_\lambda$ induce
s a **bijection of sets**

$$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}\!\Big(\varinjlim_{\lambda} M_\lambda,\; N\Big
) \;\xrightarrow{\;\sim\;}\; \Big\{(\beta_\lambda)_\lambda \in \prod_\lambda \op
eratorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(M_\lambda, N) \;\Big|\; \beta_\lambda \circ \alpha
_\lambda^\kappa = \beta_\kappa \text{ for all } \kappa\to\lambda\Big\},$$

$$\beta \;\longmapsto\; (\beta \circ \varphi_\lambda)_\lambda.$$

When $\mathcal{C} = R\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}$ or $R\text{-}\mathbf{Alg}$, this bije
ction is in fact an isomorphism of the appropriate algebraic structure (of $R$-m
odules, or of sets with algebraic structure preserved by the enrichment): the ri
ght-hand side carries a natural $R$-module structure (defined componentwise: $(\
beta_\lambda) + (\gamma_\lambda) = (\beta_\lambda + \gamma_\lambda)$, $r\cdot(\b
eta_\lambda) = (r\,\beta_\lambda)$), and the bijection respects it.

## Proof

We treat the three cases uniformly. In each of $\mathbf{Set}$, $R\text{-}\mathbf
{Mod}$, and $R\text{-}\mathbf{Alg}$, the direct limit is constructed as a quotie
nt of the coproduct (disjoint union / direct sum / free product), and the argume
nt is the same in each case; we spell it out.

### Construction of the direct limit

Form the coproduct $\bigsqcup_\lambda M_\lambda$ (disjoint union for sets, direc
t sum $\bigoplus_\lambda M_\lambda$ for $R$-modules, and the corresponding copro
duct for $R$-algebras). Define an equivalence relation $\sim$ on $\bigsqcup_\lam
bda M_\lambda$ by:

$$x_\kappa \sim y_\lambda \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \text{there exist } \mu,
\; \kappa\to\mu,\; \lambda\to\mu \text{ with } \alpha_\mu^\kappa(x_\kappa) = \al
pha_\mu^\lambda(y_\lambda).$$

Filteredness of $\Lambda$ guarantees that $\sim$ is transitive (given two witnes
ses, find a common upper bound of the two targets and push everything there). De
fine

$$\varinjlim M_\lambda \;=\; \bigsqcup_\lambda M_\lambda \big/ \sim,$$

equipped with the natural structure (quotient $R$-module / quotient $R$-algebra)
. The canonical map $\varphi_\lambda : M_\lambda \to \varinjlim M_\lambda$ sends
 $x \in M_\lambda$ to its equivalence class $[x_\lambda]$.

**Compatibility check.** For $\kappa \to \lambda$ and $x \in M_\kappa$, we have
$\alpha_\lambda^\kappa(x) \in M_\lambda$ and $[x_\kappa] = [\alpha_\lambda^\kapp
a(x)_\lambda]$ (witnessed by $\mu = \lambda$ with $\kappa\to\lambda$, $\lambda\x
rightarrow{\mathrm{id}}\lambda$). Hence $\varphi_\lambda \circ \alpha_\lambda^\k
appa = \varphi_\kappa$. ✓

### Existence of $\beta$

Given a compatible family $(\beta_\lambda)$, define

$$\beta\big([x_\lambda]\big) \;=\; \beta_\lambda(x) \in N.$$

We must verify this is **well-defined**. Suppose $x_\kappa \sim y_\lambda$, witn
essed by $\mu$ with $\kappa\to\mu$, $\lambda\to\mu$, and $\alpha_\mu^\kappa(x) =
 \alpha_\mu^\lambda(y)$. Then by compatibility:

$$\beta_\kappa(x) = \beta_\mu\big(\alpha_\mu^\kappa(x)\big) = \beta_\mu\big(\alp
ha_\mu^\lambda(y)\big) = \beta_\lambda(y).$$

So $\beta$ is well-defined on equivalence classes. It is immediate from the defi
nition that $\beta \circ \varphi_\lambda = \beta_\lambda$ for every $\lambda$.

**Structure preservation.**
- *Sets:* $\beta$ is a well-defined function. ✓
- *$R$-modules:* Addition and scalar multiplication in the direct limit are defi
ned on representatives: $[x_\lambda] + [y_\lambda] = [x+y]$, $r[x_\lambda] = [rx
]$. Then $\beta([x]+[y]) = \beta_\lambda(x+y) = \beta_\lambda(x)+\beta_\lambda(y
) = \beta([x])+\beta([y])$, and similarly for scalars. So $\beta$ is $R$-linear.
 ✓
- *$R$-algebras:* Multiplication is defined on representatives (using filteredne
ss to bring two elements to a common index), and $\beta$ respects it by the same
 argument, since each $\beta_\lambda$ is an algebra map. ✓

### Uniqueness of $\beta$

Suppose $\beta' : \varinjlim M_\lambda \to N$ also satisfies $\beta' \circ \varp
hi_\lambda = \beta_\lambda$ for all $\lambda$. Every element of $\varinjlim M_\l
ambda$ is of the form $\varphi_\lambda(x)$ for some $\lambda$ and some $x \in M_
\lambda$ (by construction). Therefore

$$\beta'\big(\varphi_\lambda(x)\big) = \beta_\lambda(x) = \beta\big(\varphi_\lam
bda(x)\big).$$

Since the elements $\varphi_\lambda(x)$ exhaust the direct limit, $\beta' = \bet
a$. ✓

### The bijection

The assignment $\beta \mapsto (\beta \circ \varphi_\lambda)_\lambda$ maps $\oper
atorname{Hom}(\varinjlim M_\lambda, N)$ into the set of compatible families (com
patibility follows from $\varphi_\lambda \circ \alpha_\lambda^\kappa = \varphi_\
kappa$). **Existence** shows it is surjective; **uniqueness** shows it is inject
ive. Hence it is a bijection. In $R\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}$, both sides are $R$-mod
ules under pointwise operations, and the bijection is $R$-linear (it preserves a
ddition and scalar multiplication, since composition with $\varphi_\lambda$ is $
R$-linear). $\square$

**Summary.** The direct limit $\varinjlim M_\lambda$ is the unique (up to unique

that **every** compatible family $(\beta_\lambda : M_\lambda \to N)$ factors uni
quely through a single map $\beta : \varinjlim M_\lambda \to N$ with $\beta \cir
c \varphi_\lambda = \beta_\lambda$ for all $\lambda$. This is precisely the stat

psto M_\lambda$ in $\mathcal{C}$.
