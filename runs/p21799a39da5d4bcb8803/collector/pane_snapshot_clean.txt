## Tangent Space of $Sp(2n, \mathbb{R})$ at the Identity

**Setup.** The real symplectic group is
$$Sp(2n,\mathbb{R}) = \{M \in GL_{2n}(\mathbb{R}) : M^T J M = J\}, \quad J = \be
gin{pmatrix} 0 & I_n \\ -I_n & 0 \end{pmatrix}.$$

**Computing the Lie algebra.** Let $M(t)$ be a smooth curve in $Sp(2n,\mathbb{R}
)$ with $M(0)=I_{2n}$, and set $A = M'(0) \in T_I Sp(2n,\mathbb{R})$. Differenti
ating $M(t)^T J M(t) = J$ at $t=0$:
$$A^T J + J A = 0. \tag{$\star$}$$

Conversely, any $A$ satisfying $(\star)$ arises this way: take $M(t) = \exp(tA)$
; then $\frac{d}{dt}\big|_{0} M(t)^T J M(t) = A^T J + JA = 0$, and by the ODE $\
frac{d}{dt}(M^T J M)=0$ with initial value $J$, we get $M(t)^T J M(t) = J$ for a
ll $t$. Hence
$$T_I Sp(2n,\mathbb{R}) = \mathfrak{sp}(2n,\mathbb{R}) = \{A \in M_{2n}(\mathbb{
R}) : A^T J + JA = 0\}.$$

Equivalently, $JA$ is symmetric (since $(JA)^T = A^T J^T = -A^T J = JA$).

**Block decomposition.** Write $A = \begin{pmatrix} X & Y \\ Z & W \end{pmatrix}
$ with $n\times n$ blocks. Then
$$JA = \begin{pmatrix} Z & W \\ -X & -Y \end{pmatrix}, \qquad A^T J = \begin{pma
trix} -Z^T & X^T \\ -W^T & Y^T \end{pmatrix}.$$

Setting $A^T J + JA = 0$ block by block:
$$\begin{pmatrix} Z - Z^T & W + X^T \\ -X - W^T & Y^T - Y \end{pmatrix} = 0,$$
giving the conditions
$$Z = Z^T, \qquad W = -X^T, \qquad Y = Y^T, \qquad X \text{ free}.$$

Therefore
$$\boxed{\mathfrak{sp}(2n,\mathbb{R}) = \left\{ \begin{pmatrix} X & Y \\ Z & -X^
T \end{pmatrix} : X \in M_n(\mathbb{R}),\; Y, Z \in \mathrm{Sym}_n(\mathbb{R}) \
right\}.}$$
