$$|x_n - L| \ge |L| - |x_n| \ge |L| - M = \delta > 0.$$

$$\lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{1}{n}\,|E_\delta \cap \{1,\dots,n\}| = 1 \ne 0.$$

$$\|g\| \ge \frac{|g(x)|}{\|x\|_\infty} = 1.$$

Combined with Step 2, $\|g\| = 1$.
