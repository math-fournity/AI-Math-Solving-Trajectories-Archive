# Problem

In the distant star system of Arithmos, a vast logistics grid has been constructed consisting of a square array of $2015$ rows and $2015$ columns of docking bays. Each docking bay must be assigned a specific frequency channel. To prevent signal interference, the galactic engineers have established two strict safety protocols regarding any three bays assigned to the same frequency:

1) For any two bays located in the same column, it is forbidden for a third bay of the same frequency to be located in the same row as the upper bay while being positioned to its right.
2) For any two bays located in the same column, it is forbidden for a third bay of the same frequency to be located in the same row as the lower bay while being positioned to its left.

The engineers must find the most efficient way to organize the grid using the fewest number of distinct frequency channels possible. What is the minimum number of channels $k$ required to satisfy these protocols for the entire $2015 \times 2015$ grid?
