This is Wichmann's conjecture on optimal sparse rulers, posed by B. Wichmann in
1963. It is explicitly marked as an open research problem (`@[category research
open]`), and it remains unsolved in the mathematical literature.

The conjecture asserts that every optimal sparse ruler with more than 13 segment
s is (up to reflection) a member of the Wichmann parametric family $W(r,s)$. The
 known exceptions occur at lengths $1, 13, 17, 23, 58$, all with at most 13 segm
ents, and no further exceptions are known up to length 213 — but no proof is kno
wn that these are the only exceptions, nor that every optimal ruler beyond that
threshold has Wichmann structure.

This is a genuine open problem in combinatorics/additive number theory. I do not
 have a proof, and producing one would constitute a major research advance.
