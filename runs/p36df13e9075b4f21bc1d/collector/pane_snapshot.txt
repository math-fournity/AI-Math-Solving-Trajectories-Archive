## Solution

**Setup.** Let Hub 1's center be $C_1$ with radius 1, Hub 2's center be $A$ with
 radius 2, and Hub 3's center be $B$ with radius 3. Since the three signal range
s are pairwise externally tangent:

$$C_1A = 1+2 = 3, \quad C_1B = 1+3 = 4, \quad AB = 2+3 = 5.$$

Since $3^2 + 4^2 = 5^2$, triangle $C_1AB$ is right-angled at $C_1$. Place $C_1 =
 (0,0)$, $A = (3,0)$, $B = (0,4)$.

**The fence circle.** Let it have center $O = (h,k)$ and radius $R$. It passes t
hrough $A$ and $B$, so:

$$(h-3)^2 + k^2 = R^2, \qquad h^2 + (k-4)^2 = R^2.$$

Subtracting: $-6h + 9 = -8k + 16$, giving $k = \frac{6h+7}{8}$.

**External tangency with Hub 1's circle** (center $C_1$, radius 1): $|OC_1| = R
+ 1$, i.e., $h^2 + k^2 = (R+1)^2$. Using $R^2 = (h-3)^2 + k^2 = h^2 - 6h + 9 + k
^2$:

$$h^2 + k^2 = h^2 - 6h + 9 + k^2 + 2R + 1 \implies 2R = 6h - 10 \implies R = 3h
- 5.$$

Substituting into $R^2 = (h-3)^2 + k^2$ and clearing denominators (multiply by 6
4):

$$64(3h-5)^2 = 64(h-3)^2 + (6h+7)^2,$$

which simplifies to $476h^2 - 1620h + 975 = 0$. The discriminant is $768000 = 16
0^2 \cdot 30$, giving:

$$h = \frac{405 \pm 40\sqrt{30}}{238}.$$

Only $h = \frac{405 + 40\sqrt{30}}{238}$ yields $R = 3h - 5 > 0$ (the other root
 gives $R < 0$).

**Locating the tangency point $P$.** For external tangency, $P$ lies on segment
$C_1O$ at distance 1 from $C_1$:

$$P = \frac{O}{R+1} = \frac{(h,k)}{R+1}, \qquad |P| = 1.$$

**Computing the squared distances.** Since $|P|^2 = 1$, $|A|^2 = 9$, $|B|^2 = 16
$:

$$PA^2 = |P-A|^2 = 1 - 2P\cdot A + 9 = 10 - \frac{6h}{R+1}.$$

With $R + 1 = 3h - 4$:

$$PA^2 = \frac{10(3h-4) - 6h}{3h-4} = \frac{24h - 40}{3h-4} = \frac{8(3h-5)}{3h-
4} = \frac{8R}{R+1}.$$

Similarly, using $P \cdot B = \frac{4k}{R+1}$ and $8k = 6h + 7$:

$$PB^2 = 17 - \frac{8k}{R+1} = \frac{17(3h-4) - (6h+7)}{3h-4} = \frac{45h - 75}{
3h-4} = \frac{15(3h-5)}{3h-4} = \frac{15R}{R+1}.$$

**The ratio.** The factors $\frac{R}{R+1}$ cancel:

$$\frac{PA^2}{PB^2} = \frac{8R/(R+1)}{15R/(R+1)} = \frac{8}{15}.$$

**Verification.** With $h \approx 2.622$, $k \approx 2.842$, $R \approx 2.867$:
$P \approx (0.678, 0.735)$, giving $PA^2 \approx 5.93$, $PB^2 \approx 11.12$, ra
tio $\approx 0.533 = 8/15$. ✓

The irreducible fraction is $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{8}{15}$ (since $\gcd(8,15)=1$),
 so:

$$a + b = 8 + 15 = \boxed{23}.$$
