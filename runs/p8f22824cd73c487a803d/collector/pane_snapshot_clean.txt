## Proof

**Setup.** The ellipse is $\frac{x^2}{25}+\frac{y^2}{9}=1$ with $a=5,\,b=3$, so
$c=\sqrt{25-9}=4$, and the left focus is $F=(-4,0)$. The satellite $A=(x_0,y_0)$
 lies on $xy=2011$.

**Step 1: Identify the chord of contact.** The two tangent points $P,Q$ from $A$
 to the ellipse lie on the polar line of $A$:
$$\frac{x_0 x}{25}+\frac{y_0 y}{9}=1.$$

**Step 2: Reduce to a circle via $u=x/5,\,v=y/3$.** The ellipse becomes $u^2+v^2
=1$; the polar line becomes $\alpha u+\beta v=1$ where $\alpha=x_0/5,\;\beta=y_0
/3$. Set $R^2=\alpha^2+\beta^2=\frac{x_0^2}{25}+\frac{y_0^2}{9}$. Since $x_0y_0=
2011$, AM–GM gives $R^2\ge 2\sqrt{x_0^2y_0^2/225}=\frac{4022}{15}>1$, so two tan
gents always exist.

**Step 3: Parametrize the contact points.** Write $u=\cos\theta,\,v=\sin\theta$.
 The line gives $\alpha\cos\theta+\beta\sin\theta=1$, i.e. $R\cos(\theta-\varphi
)=1$. The two solutions are $\theta_{1,2}=\varphi\mp\delta$ with $\cos\delta=1/R
$. Hence:
$$\cos\theta_1+\cos\theta_2=\frac{2\alpha}{R^2},\qquad \cos\theta_1\cos\theta_2=
\frac{1-\beta^2}{R^2}.$$

**Step 4: Compute $|PF|\cdot|QF|$.** Since $F=(-4,0)$ and $P=(5\cos\theta_1,3\si
n\theta_1)$:
$$|PF|^2=(5\cos\theta_1+4)^2+9\sin^2\theta_1=25\cos^2\theta_1+40\cos\theta_1+16+
9\sin^2\theta_1=(4\cos\theta_1+5)^2.$$
As $4\cos\theta+5\ge 1>0$, we have $|PF|=4\cos\theta_1+5$ and similarly $|QF|=4\
cos\theta_2+5$. Therefore:
$$|PF|\cdot|QF|=16\cos\theta_1\cos\theta_2+20(\cos\theta_1+\cos\theta_2)+25=\fra
c{16(1-\beta^2)+40\alpha+25R^2}{R^2}.$$
The numerator is $16-16\beta^2+40\alpha+25(\alpha^2+\beta^2)=25\alpha^2+9\beta^2
+40\alpha+16$. Substituting back $\alpha=x_0/5,\,\beta=y_0/3$:
$$25\alpha^2+9\beta^2+40\alpha+16=x_0^2+y_0^2+8x_0+16=(x_0+4)^2+y_0^2=|AF|^2.$$
So
$$|PF|\cdot|QF|=\frac{|AF|^2}{R^2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{|AF|^2}{|PF|\c
dot|QF|}=R^2=\frac{x_0^2}{25}+\frac{y_0^2}{9}.$$

**Step 5: Minimize.** With $x_0y_0=2011$, set $t=x_0^2$:
$$R^2=\frac{t}{25}+\frac{2011^2}{9t}.$$
By AM–GM, $\frac{t}{25}+\frac{2011^2}{9t}\ge 2\sqrt{\frac{t}{25}\cdot\frac{2011^
2}{9t}}=2\cdot\frac{2011}{15}=\frac{4022}{15}$, with equality at $t=2011\cdot\fr
ac{5}{3}$ (i.e. $x_0^2/25=y_0^2/9$).

**Conclusion.** The minimum value is
$$\boxed{\dfrac{4022}{15}}.$$
