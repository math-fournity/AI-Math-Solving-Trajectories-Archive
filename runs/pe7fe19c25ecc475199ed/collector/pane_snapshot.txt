## Solution

**Setup.** The token makes 6 independent moves, each chosen uniformly from {up,
down, left, right}, so the total number of equally likely paths is $4^6 = 4096$.

Let $a, b, c, d$ denote the number of moves right, left, up, down respectively,
with $a+b+c+d=6$. The final position is $(x,y) = (a-b,\; c-d)$. We need $|y|=|x|
$, i.e., $|a-b| = |c-d|$.

**Counting by number of horizontal moves.** Let $s = a+b$ (horizontal moves) and
 $t = c+d = 6-s$ (vertical moves). There are $\binom{6}{s}$ ways to choose which
 moves are horizontal. The net horizontal displacement is $u = 2a - s$ (for $a =
 0,\dots,s$), and net vertical displacement is $v = 2c - t$ (for $c = 0,\dots,t$
). We need $|u| = |v|$.

For each $s$, I compute $H_s = \sum_{a:\,|2a-s|=k} \binom{s}{a}$ and $V_t = \sum
_{c:\,|2c-t|=k} \binom{t}{c}$ for each common value $k$, then sum the products.

| $s$ | $t$ | Pairs $\|u\|=\|v\|$ | Weighted sum | $\binom{6}{s}$ | Count |
|-----|-----|---------------------|--------------|----------------|-------|
| 0 | 6 | $\|u\|=0,\|v\|=0$: $1\cdot 20$ | 20 | 1 | 20 |
| 1 | 5 | $\|u\|=1,\|v\|=1$: $2\cdot 20$ | 40 | 6 | 240 |
| 2 | 4 | $\|u\|=0,\|v\|=0$: $2\cdot 6$; $\|u\|=2,\|v\|=2$: $2\cdot 8$ | $12+16=
28$ | 15 | 420 |
| 3 | 3 | $\|u\|=1,\|v\|=1$: $6\cdot 6$; $\|u\|=3,\|v\|=3$: $2\cdot 2$ | $36+4=4
0$ | 20 | 800 |
| 4 | 2 | symmetric to $s=2$ | 28 | 15 | 420 |
| 5 | 1 | symmetric to $s=1$ | 40 | 6 | 240 |
| 6 | 0 | symmetric to $s=0$ | 20 | 1 | 20 |

**Verification of key cases:**

- **$s=3, t=3$:** $|2a-3|=|2c-3|$ means either $a=c$ or $a+c=3$ (no overlap sinc
e $2a=3$ has no integer solution). Sum $= \sum_{a=0}^{3}\binom{3}{a}^2 + \sum_{a
=0}^{3}\binom{3}{a}\binom{3}{3-a} = (1+9+9+1)+(1+9+9+1) = 40$. ✓

- **$s=2, t=4$:** $|u|\in\{0,2\}$, $|v|\in\{0,2,4\}$. Common values $\{0,2\}$: $
(2\cdot6) + (2\cdot 8) = 28$. ✓

**Total favorable count:**
$$20 + 240 + 420 + 800 + 420 + 240 + 20 = 2160.$$

**Probability:**
$$\frac{2160}{4096} = \frac{2160 \div 16}{4096 \div 16} = \frac{135}{256}.$$

Since $135 = 27 \cdot 5$ and $256 = 2^8$, we have $\gcd(135, 256) = 1$, so $m =
135$ and $n = 256$.

$$m + n = 135 + 256 = \boxed{391}.$$
