# Problem

A specialized deep-sea research drone is programmed to descend to a trench at a uniformly random time between 12:00 AM and 8:00 AM each day to perform a 3-hour data collection sweep. Simultaneously, a private underwater salvage robot is deployed at a uniformly random time between 5:00 AM and 11:00 AM daily to perform a 1-hour maintenance check. Both machines operate independently, and their active durations may extend beyond their respective deployment windows.

If the two machines are considered to "encounter" each other if their operating times overlap by any margin of time, what is the probability that they encounter each other on at least 2 days during a specific 5-day mission?
