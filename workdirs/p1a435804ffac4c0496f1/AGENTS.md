# Problem

In a specialized laboratory, a digital frequency generator creates a pulsing laser beam. The intensity of the beam follows a specific oscillating cycle described by the absolute value of a wave function, $|\cos(\pi t)|$, where $t$ represents the time in seconds from the start of the experiment.

Simultaneously, a computerized dampening shield is activated. The shield's resistance level increases quadratically over time according to the formula $\frac{t^2}{4n^2}$, where $n$ is a fixed positive integer representing the experiment's power grade.

A sensor records a "stabilization event" every time the laser's intensity exactly matches the shield's resistance level. Given that $n$ is a natural number, determine the total number of stabilization events that occur over all real values of $t$.
