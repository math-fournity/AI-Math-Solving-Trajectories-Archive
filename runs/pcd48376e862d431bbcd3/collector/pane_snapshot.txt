## Strategy

We need $N \bmod 100000$. Since $100000 = 32 \times 3125$ with $\gcd(32, 3125) =
 1$, we use the Chinese Remainder Theorem (CRT), computing $N \bmod 32$ and $N \
bmod 3125$ separately.

## Part 1: $N \bmod 32$

$2003 \equiv 19 \pmod{32}$, and $\gcd(19, 32) = 1$, $\phi(32) = 16$.

**Reduce the exponent mod 16:** $2005 \equiv 5 \pmod{16}$, so we need $5^{2007}
\bmod 16$. Since $\phi(16) = 8$ and $2007 \equiv 7 \pmod{8}$:
$$5^2 \equiv 9,\quad 5^4 \equiv 1,\quad 5^7 = 5^4 \cdot 5^2 \cdot 5 \equiv 1 \cd
ot 9 \cdot 5 = 45 \equiv 13 \pmod{16}.$$

**Compute $19^{13} \bmod 32$:**
$$19^2 \equiv 9,\quad 19^4 \equiv 81 \equiv 17,\quad 19^8 \equiv 289 \equiv 1,$$
$$19^{13} = 19^8 \cdot 19^4 \cdot 19 \equiv 1 \cdot 17 \cdot 19 = 323 \equiv 3 \
pmod{32}.$$

$$\boxed{N \equiv 3 \pmod{32}}$$

## Part 2: $N \bmod 3125$

$2003 \equiv 2003 \pmod{3125}$, $\gcd(2003, 3125) = 1$ (since $3125 = 5^5$ and $
5 \nmid 2003$), $\phi(3125) = 2500$.

### Reduce the exponent mod 2500

We need $2005^{2007} \bmod 2500$, where $2500 = 4 \times 625$.

**Mod 4:** $2005 \equiv 1 \pmod{4}$, so $2005^{2007} \equiv 1 \pmod{4}$.

**Mod 625:** $2005 \equiv 130 \pmod{625}$, and $130 = 5 \times 26$. Since $5^4 =
 625 \mid 5^{2007}$, we get $130^{2007} \equiv 0 \pmod{625}$.

**CRT:** $x \equiv 0 \pmod{625}$, $x \equiv 1 \pmod{4}$. Setting $x = 625k$: $62
5 \equiv 1 \pmod{4}$, so $k \equiv 1 \pmod{4}$, giving $k = 1$, $x = 625$.

$$2005^{2007} \equiv 625 \pmod{2500}$$

## Part 3: CRT Combination

We need $x$ with $0 \le x < 100000$ satisfying:
- $x \equiv 3 \pmod{32}$
- $x \equiv 1068 \pmod{3125}$

Set $x = 3125k + 1068$. Then:
$$3125k + 1068 \equiv 3 \pmod{32}$$
$$21k + 12 \equiv 3 \pmod{32} \quad (\text{since } 3125 \equiv 21, \; 1068 \equi
v 12 \pmod{32})$$
$$21k \equiv 23 \pmod{32}$$

The inverse of $21 \bmod 32$: $21 \times 29 = 609 = 19 \times 32 + 1$, so $21^{-
1} \equiv 29$.

$$k \equiv 23 \times 29 = 667 \equiv 27 \pmod{32}$$

$$x = 3125 \times 27 + 1068 = 84375 + 1068 = 85443$$

**Verification:**
- $85443 \bmod 32 = 85443 - 2670 \times 32 = 85443 - 85440 = 3$ ✓
- $85443 \bmod 3125 = 85443 - 27 \times 3125 = 85443 - 84375 = 1068$ ✓

---

## Answer

The last 5 digits of $N = 2003^{2005^{2007}}$ are $\boxed{85443}$.
