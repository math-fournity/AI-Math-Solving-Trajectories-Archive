## Proof

Let $a = \sqrt{T_A} = \sqrt{\tan x}$ and $b = \sqrt{T_B} = \sqrt{\tan(5x)}$. The
 equilibrium equation becomes
$$\frac{a^2}{\sqrt{2}-a}+\frac{b^2}{\sqrt{2}+b}=\sqrt{2}.$$

**Claim:** $x = \dfrac{\pi}{12}$ satisfies the equation.

**Step 1: Evaluate $T_A$ and $T_B$ at $x=\pi/12$.**

$$T_A = \tan\!\left(\frac{\pi}{12}\right)=\tan 15°=2-\sqrt{3}, \qquad T_B = \tan
\!\left(\frac{5\pi}{12}\right)=\tan 75°=2+\sqrt{3}.$$

Both are non-negative, and $T_A=2-\sqrt{3}\neq 2$. ✓

**Step 2: Simplify $a$ and $b$.**

Since $2-\sqrt{3}=\dfrac{(\sqrt{3}-1)^2}{2}$ and $2+\sqrt{3}=\dfrac{(\sqrt{3}+1)
^2}{2}$:

$$a=\sqrt{2-\sqrt{3}}=\frac{\sqrt{6}-\sqrt{2}}{2},\qquad b=\sqrt{2+\sqrt{3}}=\fr
ac{\sqrt{6}+\sqrt{2}}{2}.$$

**Step 3: Simplify the denominators.**

$$\sqrt{2}-a = \sqrt{2}-\frac{\sqrt{6}-\sqrt{2}}{2}=\frac{3\sqrt{2}-\sqrt{6}}{2}
=\frac{\sqrt{2}\,(3-\sqrt{3})}{2},$$

$$\sqrt{2}+b = \sqrt{2}+\frac{\sqrt{6}+\sqrt{2}}{2}=\frac{3\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{6}}{2}
=\frac{\sqrt{2}\,(3+\sqrt{3})}{2}.$$

**Step 4: Substitute into the equation.**

$$\frac{a^2}{\sqrt{2}-a}+\frac{b^2}{\sqrt{2}+b}
= \frac{2-\sqrt{3}}{\frac{\sqrt{2}(3-\sqrt{3})}{2}}+\frac{2+\sqrt{3}}{\frac{\sqr
t{2}(3+\sqrt{3})}{2}}
= \sqrt{2}\!\left(\frac{2-\sqrt{3}}{3-\sqrt{3}}+\frac{2+\sqrt{3}}{3+\sqrt{3}}\ri
ght).$$

**Step 5: Rationalize each fraction.**

$$\frac{2-\sqrt{3}}{3-\sqrt{3}}\cdot\frac{3+\sqrt{3}}{3+\sqrt{3}}=\frac{(2-\sqrt
{3})(3+\sqrt{3})}{9-3}=\frac{6+2\sqrt{3}-3\sqrt{3}-3}{6}=\frac{3-\sqrt{3}}{6},$$

$$\frac{2+\sqrt{3}}{3+\sqrt{3}}\cdot\frac{3-\sqrt{3}}{3-\sqrt{3}}=\frac{(2+\sqrt
{3})(3-\sqrt{3})}{9-3}=\frac{6-2\sqrt{3}+3\sqrt{3}-3}{6}=\frac{3+\sqrt{3}}{6}.$$

**Step 6: Combine.**

$$\frac{3-\sqrt{3}}{6}+\frac{3+\sqrt{3}}{6}=\frac{6}{6}=1.$$

Therefore

$$\frac{a^2}{\sqrt{2}-a}+\frac{b^2}{\sqrt{2}+b}=\sqrt{2}\cdot 1=\sqrt{2}. \quad\
checkmark$$

The equilibrium equation is satisfied. By the $\pi$-periodicity of $\tan$, the f
ull family of solutions is $x = \dfrac{\pi}{12}+n\pi$, $n\in\mathbb{Z}$. The pri
ncipal value is

$$\boxed{x=\dfrac{\pi}{12}}.$$
