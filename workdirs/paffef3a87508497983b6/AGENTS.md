# Problem

In a remote mountain range, a specialized research facility is built inside a cavern shaped like a perfect inverted cone. The cavern’s floor is a flat, circular disk with a radius of 2 units. The sloping walls (the generatrix) have a total length of 4 units from the rim of the base to the apex point buried deep in the earth.

Engineers are tasked with installing three identical spherical weather sensors, each with a radius of $r$. The sensors are to be positioned within the cavern according to the following strict spatial constraints:
1. Each of the three spherical sensors must touch the other two externally.
2. All three sensors must be in direct contact with the sloping walls of the cavern.
3. To maintain stability, the first two sensors must rest directly on the flat circular floor of the cavern.

Assuming the sensors are positioned to be as large as possible while still fitting within these geometric parameters, what is the maximum possible value of the radius $r$?
