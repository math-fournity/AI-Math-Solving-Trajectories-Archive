# Problem

In a remote digital archipelago, there are 30 communication islands connected by exactly 105 fiber-optic cables. Each cable links exactly two distinct islands. 

The network’s stability is monitored by analyzing "independent link pairs." These are pairs of cables that do not share any island in common (meaning the four endpoints involved in the two cables are all distinct). A recent system audit confirmed that there are exactly 4,822 such unordered pairs of independent cables in the network.

A technician is investigating the load balance of the system by comparing the "connectivity degree" of the islands, defined as the number of cables connected to a specific island. Based on the data provided, what is the maximum possible difference between the connectivity degrees of any two islands in this network?
