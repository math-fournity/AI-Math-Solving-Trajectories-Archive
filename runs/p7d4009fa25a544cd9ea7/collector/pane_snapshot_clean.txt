**Proof.**

Place $A$ at the origin and write $\vec{AB}=2\mathbf u$, $\vec{AC}=2\mathbf v$.
Then
$$B=2\mathbf u,\quad C=2\mathbf v,\quad N=\mathbf u\ (\text{midpoint of }AB),\qu
ad M=\mathbf v\ (\text{midpoint of }AC).$$
Set $U=\mathbf u\!\cdot\!\mathbf u,\;V=\mathbf v\!\cdot\!\mathbf v,\;W=\mathbf u
\!\cdot\!\mathbf v$.

**The point $P$.** The median $CN$ has direction $2\mathbf v-\mathbf u$, so the
projection of $B=2\mathbf u$ onto $CN$ is
$$P=\mathbf u+\alpha(2\mathbf v-\mathbf u)=(1-\alpha)\mathbf u+2\alpha\,\mathbf
v,\qquad
\alpha=\frac{\mathbf u\cdot(2\mathbf v-\mathbf u)}{|2\mathbf v-\mathbf u|^{2}}=\
frac{2W-U}{D_1},$$
where $D_1=|2\mathbf v-\mathbf u|^{2}=4V-4W+U$. Thus $\alpha D_1=2W-U$.

**The point $Q$.** The median $BM$ has direction $\mathbf v-2\mathbf u$, so the
projection of $C=2\mathbf v$ onto $BM$ is
$$Q=\mathbf v+\beta(\mathbf v-2\mathbf u)=-2\beta\,\mathbf u+(1+\beta)\mathbf v,
\qquad
\beta=\frac{\mathbf v\cdot(\mathbf v-2\mathbf u)}{|\mathbf v-2\mathbf u|^{2}}=\f
rac{V-2W}{D_2},$$
where $D_2=|\mathbf v-2\mathbf u|^{2}=V-4W+4U$. Thus $\beta D_2=V-2W$.

**Concyclicity.** Use $\{\mathbf u,\mathbf v\}$ as a basis and write a point as
$(s,t)$ meaning $s\mathbf u+t\mathbf v$. A circle has equation
$$Us^{2}+2Wst+Vt^{2}+d_1 s+d_2 t+F=0$$
for constants $d_1,d_2,F$. The four points are concyclic iff this equation holds
 at $M,N,P,Q$, i.e. iff the resulting system in $(d_1,d_2,F)$ is consistent.

- $N=(1,0)$: $\;U+d_1+F=0\;\Rightarrow\;d_1=-U-F$. \hfill (1)
- $M=(0,1)$: $\;V+d_2+F=0\;\Rightarrow\;d_2=-V-F$. \hfill (2)
- $P=(1-\alpha,\,2\alpha)$: substituting (1),(2) and collecting powers of $\alph
a$ gives
$$\alpha\bigl[-U+4W-2V+\alpha D_1-F\bigr]=0.$$
- $Q=(-2\beta,\,1+\beta)$: substituting (1),(2) and collecting powers of $\beta$
 gives
$$\beta\bigl[-4W+V+2U+\beta D_2+F\bigr]=0.$$

In the non-degenerate case (guaranteed by $\angle A<\angle B\le\angle C$, which
prevents $P=N$ or $Q=M$), $\alpha,\beta\ne0$, so the last two equations yield
$$F=-U+4W-2V+\alpha D_1,\qquad F=4W-V-2U-\beta D_2.$$
These are consistent iff their right-hand sides agree:
$$-U+4W-2V+\alpha D_1=4W-V-2U-\beta D_2
\iff U-V+\alpha D_1+\beta D_2=0.$$
Using $\alpha D_1=2W-U$ and $\beta D_2=V-2W$,
$$U-V+(2W-U)+(V-2W)=0.\qquad\checkmark$$

Hence the system is consistent: a single circle passes through $M,N,P,Q$. Theref
ore the quadrilateral $MNPQ$ is cyclic. $\blacksquare$
