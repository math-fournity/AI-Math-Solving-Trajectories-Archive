## Solution

We find the surface area generated by revolving the right-hand loop of the lemni
scate $r^2 = \cos 2\theta$ about the y-axis, using

$$S = 2\pi \int_\alpha^\beta r\cos\theta\,\sqrt{r^2+\left(\frac{dr}{d\theta}\rig
ht)^2}\,d\theta.$$

### Step 1: Identify the loop

The right-hand loop of $r^2 = \cos 2\theta$ is traced for $\theta \in [-\pi/4,\,
 \pi/4]$, where $\cos 2\theta \geq 0$. We take $r = \sqrt{\cos 2\theta} \geq 0$.
 On this interval $\cos\theta > 0$, so $r\cos\theta \geq 0$ and no absolute valu
e is needed.

### Step 2: Compute $dr/d\theta$

Differentiating $r^2 = \cos 2\theta$:

$$2r\,\frac{dr}{d\theta} = -2\sin 2\theta \implies \frac{dr}{d\theta} = -\frac{\
sin 2\theta}{r}.$$

### Step 3: Simplify the radical

$$\left(\frac{dr}{d\theta}\right)^2 = \frac{\sin^2 2\theta}{r^2} = \frac{\sin^2
2\theta}{\cos 2\theta}.$$

$$r^2 + \left(\frac{dr}{d\theta}\right)^2 = \cos 2\theta + \frac{\sin^2 2\theta}
{\cos 2\theta} = \frac{\cos^2 2\theta + \sin^2 2\theta}{\cos 2\theta} = \frac{1}
{\cos 2\theta}.$$

Therefore:

$$\sqrt{r^2+\left(\frac{dr}{d\theta}\right)^2} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\cos 2\theta}}.$
$

### Step 4: Simplify the integrand

$$r\,\sqrt{r^2+\left(\frac{dr}{d\theta}\right)^2} = \sqrt{\cos 2\theta}\cdot\fra
c{1}{\sqrt{\cos 2\theta}} = 1.$$

So the integrand collapses to simply $\cos\theta$.

### Step 5: Evaluate the integral

$$S = 2\pi\int_{-\pi/4}^{\pi/4}\cos\theta\,d\theta = 2\pi\Big[\sin\theta\Big]_{-
\pi/4}^{\pi/4} = 2\pi\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}-\left(-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)\r
ight)=2\pi\cdot\sqrt{2}.$$

### Result

$$\boxed{S = 2\sqrt{2}\,\pi}$$
