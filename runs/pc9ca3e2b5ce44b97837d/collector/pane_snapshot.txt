=0$. Then $G \setminus E$ is dense in $G$.

$$\lambda\bigl((a,b)\setminus E\bigr) = \lambda((a,b)) - \lambda((a,b)\cap E) =

Hence $(a,b)\setminus E \neq \emptyset$, so $V \cap (G\setminus E) \supset (a,b)
