# Problem

In a specialized logistics hub, two specific types of shipping containers are used to store high-tech components. The total inventory is governed by a precise structural equilibrium: the number of components in a standard shipment is calculated as $x^y + 1$, where $x$ and $y$ are positive integers representing the base capacity and the scaling power, respectively. To ensure stable stacking, this total inventory must always equal $z^2$, where $z$ is a positive integer representing the side length of a square storage grid.

Technical safety protocols impose two specific constraints on the hardware: the base capacity $x$ must be strictly greater than 2, and the scaling power $y$ must be strictly greater than 1.

The efficiency of the operation is measured by two coefficients:
- Let $p$ be the number of distinct prime factors of the base capacity $x$.
- Let $q$ be the number of distinct prime factors of the scaling power $y$.

An analyst is tasked with optimizing the system by minimizing the gap between these two coefficients. Find the minimum possible value of the difference $p - q$.
