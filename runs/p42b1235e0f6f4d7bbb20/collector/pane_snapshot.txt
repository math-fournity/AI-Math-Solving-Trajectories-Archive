## Solution

**Setup.** A $9\times 9$ matrix over $\mathbb{C}$ with minimal polynomial $x^2(x
-3)^3$ has eigenvalues $0$ and $3$. The minimal polynomial tells us:
- The largest Jordan block for $\lambda=0$ has size **exactly 2** (so all blocks
 for $0$ have size $\le 2$, with at least one of size $2$).
- The largest Jordan block for $\lambda=3$ has size **exactly 3** (so all blocks
 for $3$ have size $\le 3$, with at least one of size $3$).

The Jordan canonical form is determined by the partition of $n_0$ (algebraic mul
tiplicity of $0$) into blocks of sizes $\le 2$ with largest part $=2$, and the p
artition of $n_3 = 9 - n_0$ into blocks of sizes $\le 3$ with largest part $=3$.

**Constraints:** $n_0 \ge 2$ and $n_3 \ge 3$, so $n_0 \in \{2,3,4,5,6\}$.

**Counting partitions for $\lambda=0$** (parts from $\{1,2\}$, at least one $2$)
: the number of $2$'s ranges from $1$ to $\lfloor n_0/2\rfloor$, giving $\lfloor
 n_0/2\rfloor$ partitions.

| $n_0$ | Count | Partitions |
|-------|-------|------------|
| 2 | 1 | $\{2\}$ |
| 3 | 1 | $\{2,1\}$ |
| 4 | 2 | $\{2,2\},\{2,1,1\}$ |
| 5 | 2 | $\{2,2,1\},\{2,1,1,1\}$ |
| 6 | 3 | $\{2,2,2\},\{2,2,1,1\},\{2,1,1,1,1,1\}$ |

**Counting partitions for $\lambda=3$** (parts from $\{1,2,3\}$, at least one $3

| $n_3$ | $p_{\le 3}(n_3)$ | $p_{\le 2}(n_3)$ | Difference | Partitions |
|-------|------------------|------------------|------------|------------|
| 3 | 3 | 2 | 1 | $\{3\}$ |

| 5 | 5 | 3 | 2 | $\{3,2\},\{3,1,1\}$ |

| 7 | 8 | 4 | 4 | $\{3,3,1\},\{3,2,2\},\{3,2,1,1\},\{3,1,1,1,1\}$ |

**Combining** (product of counts for each $n_0$):

| $n_0$ | $n_3$ | $\lambda=0$ count | $\lambda=3$ count | Product |
|-------|-------|--------------------|--------------------|---------|
| 2 | 7 | 1 | 4 | 4 |

| 6 | 3 | 3 | 1 | 3 |

**Total:** $4+3+4+2+3 = \boxed{16}$.
