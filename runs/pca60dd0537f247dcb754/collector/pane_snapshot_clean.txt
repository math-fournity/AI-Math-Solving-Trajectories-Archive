## Analysis of $K_n$

The condition $\|(x,y) - (1/n, 1/n)\|_\infty = 1/n$ means $\max(|x - 1/n|, |y -
1/n|) = 1/n$, which is the boundary of the square centered at $(1/n, 1/n)$ with
side length $2/n$. This square has corners at $(0,0), (2/n, 0), (0, 2/n), (2/n,
2/n)$.

The constraint $xy \neq 0$ removes all points on the coordinate axes. The four e
dges of the boundary are:
- **Bottom** ($y = 0$): entirely excluded ($xy = 0$).
- **Left** ($x = 0$): entirely excluded ($xy = 0$).
- **Right** ($x = 2/n$, $y \in [0, 2/n]$): exclude $y = 0$, giving $\{(2/n, y) :
 y \in (0, 2/n]\}$.
- **Top** ($y = 2/n$, $x \in [0, 2/n]$): exclude $x = 0$, giving $\{(x, 2/n) : x
 \in (0, 2/n]\}$.

So $K_n$ is an **L-shaped curve** (right edge + top edge, open at the axis endpo
ints):
$$K_n = \{(2/n, y) : 0 < y \leq 2/n\} \cup \{(x, 2/n) : 0 < x \leq 2/n\}.$$

### Part 2: The origin $(0,0)$ is an accumulation point not in $K$.

### Part 3: Each $(2/n, 0)$ is an accumulation point not in $K$.

### Part 4: Each $(0, 2/n)$ is an accumulation point not in $K$.
