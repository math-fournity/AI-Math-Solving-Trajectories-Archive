A cryptographer needs to calculate the "Core Value" of this massive identification code. The Core Value is defined as the cube root of the integer represented by the entire sequence. Once the Core Value is determined, the cryptographer must calculate the sum of its individual digits to unlock the archive.

What is the sum of the digits of the Core Value?
