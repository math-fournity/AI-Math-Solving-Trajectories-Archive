# Problem

A high-tech manufacturing firm, "QuantumGrid," has developed a square processor consisting of a $2021 \times 2021$ grid of micro-sensors. Each sensor must be programmed to detect one of four specific signal frequencies: Alpha, Beta, Gamma, or Delta.

The board is classified as "Synchronized" if there exist four positive integers $a, b, c,$ and $d$ such that their sum $a+b+c+d$ equals exactly $12$, satisfying a specific density requirement: 
Every time a maintenance scanner covers a $3 \times 4$ or $4 \times 3$ rectangular sub-section of the grid, that section must contain exactly $a$ Alpha sensors, $b$ Beta sensors, $c$ Gamma sensors, and $d$ Delta sensors.

Two board configurations are considered distinct if at least one sensor at the same grid coordinates is programmed to a different frequency.

Calculate the total number of distinct Synchronized board configurations possible.
