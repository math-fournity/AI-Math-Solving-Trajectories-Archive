## Solution

The region is
\[
E = \{(w,x,y,z) \mid 0 < w < 1-(x^2+y^2+z^2)^3\}.
\]

**Step 1: Determine the support in \((x,y,z)\).**

For the upper bound on \(w\) to be positive, we need
\[
1-(x^2+y^2+z^2)^3 > 0 \implies x^2+y^2+z^2 < 1.
\]
So \((x,y,z)\) ranges over the open unit ball \(B = \{(x,y,z): x^2+y^2+z^2<1\}\)
.

**Step 2: Integrate over \(w\).**

For each fixed \((x,y,z)\in B\), the \(w\)-interval has length
\[
\int_0^{1-(x^2+y^2+z^2)^3} dw = 1-(x^2+y^2+z^2)^3.
\]

**Step 3: Convert to spherical coordinates.**

Let \(r^2 = x^2+y^2+z^2\), with \(r\in[0,1]\), \(\theta\in[0,2\pi]\), \(\phi\in[
0,\pi]\), and \(dx\,dy\,dz = r^2\sin\phi\,dr\,d\phi\,d\theta\). Then \((x^2+y^2+
z^2)^3 = r^6\), and
\[
\text{Vol}(E) = \int_0^{2\pi} d\theta \int_0^{\pi} \sin\phi\,d\phi \int_0^{1} (1
-r^6)\,r^2\,dr.
\]

**Step 4: Evaluate.**

The angular integrals:
\[
\int_0^{2\pi} d\theta = 2\pi, \qquad \int_0^{\pi}\sin\phi\,d\phi = 2.
\]

The radial integral:
\[
\int_0^{1} (1-r^6)\,r^2\,dr = \int_0^{1} (r^2 - r^8)\,dr = \left[\frac{r^3}{3} -
 \frac{r^9}{9}\right]_0^1 = \frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{9} = \frac{2}{9}.
\]

**Step 5: Combine.**
\[
\text{Vol}(E) = 2\pi \cdot 2 \cdot \frac{2}{9} = \frac{8\pi}{9}.
\]

\[
\boxed{\dfrac{8\pi}{9}}
\]
