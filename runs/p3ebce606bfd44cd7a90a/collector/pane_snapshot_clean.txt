## Solution

The surface is $(x+y+z+5)^2 + (x+2y+3z+6)^2 + (x+3y+4z+7)^2 = 9$.

**Step 1: Change of variables.** Define new coordinates
$$u_1 = x+y+z+5,\quad u_2 = x+2y+3z+6,\quad u_3 = x+3y+4z+7.$$

In $(u_1, u_2, u_3)$-space the surface becomes $u_1^2 + u_2^2 + u_3^2 = 9$, a sp
here of radius $R=3$.

**Step 2: Volume in $u$-space.** The sphere has volume
$$V_u = \tfrac{4}{3}\pi R^3 = \tfrac{4}{3}\pi(27) = 36\pi.$$

**Step 3: Jacobian of the transformation.** The map $(x,y,z)\mapsto(u_1,u_2,u_3)
$ is affine; its linear part is
$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 1 & 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix}.$$

Computing the determinant (expanding along the first row):
$$\det A = 1(2\cdot4-3\cdot3) - 1(1\cdot4-3\cdot1) + 1(1\cdot3-2\cdot1)$$
$$= 1(8-9) - 1(4-3) + 1(3-2) = -1 - 1 + 1 = -1.$$

Thus $|\det A| = 1$.

**Step 4: Volume in $(x,y,z)$-space.** Since $dx\,dy\,dz = \dfrac{du_1\,du_2\,du
_3}{|\det A|}$,

$$V = \frac{V_u}{|\det A|} = \frac{36\pi}{1} = 36\pi.$$
