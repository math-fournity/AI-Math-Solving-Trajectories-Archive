Looking at this problem, I need to find the relationship between the average of
roots of iterated polynomials.

## Setup

Let $f(x) = a_d x^d + a_{d-1} x^{d-1} + \cdots$ with $d = \deg f \geq 2$, and $g
_k(x) = f^{(k)}(x)$ the $k$-fold iterate, of degree $d^k$.

By Vieta's formulas, the average of roots of $g_k$ is:
$$r_k = -\frac{S_k}{d^k \cdot L_k}$$
where $L_k$ is the leading coefficient of $g_k$ and $S_k$ is the coefficient of
$x^{d^k - 1}$.

## Recurrence for $L_k$ and $S_k$

**Leading coefficient:** $g_{k+1}(x) = f(g_k(x)) = a_d(g_k(x))^d + \cdots$, so
$$L_{k+1} = a_d \cdot L_k^d, \quad L_1 = a_d \implies L_k = a_d^{(d^k-1)/(d-1)}.
$$

**Second coefficient:** Expand $(g_k(x))^d = (L_k x^{d^k} + S_k x^{d^k-1} + \cdo
ts)^d$:
$$(g_k(x))^d = L_k^d\, x^{d^{k+1}} + d\, L_k^{d-1}\, S_k\, x^{d^{k+1}-1} + \cdot
s$$

So $a_d(g_k(x))^d$ contributes $a_d \cdot d \cdot L_k^{d-1} \cdot S_k$ to the $x
^{d^{k+1}-1}$ coefficient.

**Do lower terms of $f$ contribute?** The term $a_{d-1}(g_k(x))^{d-1}$ has highe
st degree $d^k(d-1) = d^{k+1} - d^k$. Since $d \geq 2$ and $k \geq 1$, we have $
d^k \geq 2$, so $d^{k+1} - d^k \leq d^{k+1} - 2 < d^{k+1} - 1$. Similarly, all l
ower terms $a_j (g_k)^j$ with $j \leq d-2$ have degree $\leq d^k(d-2) = d^{k+1}
- 2d^k \leq d^{k+1} - 4$. **None contribute** to the $x^{d^{k+1}-1}$ coefficient
.

Therefore:
$$S_{k+1} = a_d \cdot d \cdot L_k^{d-1} \cdot S_k.$$

## The key ratio

$$\frac{S_{k+1}}{L_{k+1}} = \frac{a_d \cdot d \cdot L_k^{d-1} \cdot S_k}{a_d \cd
ot L_k^d} = \frac{d \cdot S_k}{L_k}.$$

Thus:
$$r_{k+1} = -\frac{S_{k+1}}{d^{k+1} \cdot L_{k+1}} = -\frac{d \cdot S_k}{d^{k+1}
 \cdot L_k} = -\frac{S_k}{d^k \cdot L_k} = r_k.$$

## Conclusion

The sequence $\{r_k\}$ is **constant**: $r_1 = r_2 = r_3 = \cdots$ for all $k$.

**Verification:** Take $f(x) = x^2 - 3x + 2 = (x-1)(x-2)$, so $r_1 = 1.5$. Then
$f(f(x)) = x^4 - 6x^3 + 10x^2 - 3x$, whose roots sum to $6$ (Vieta's), giving av
erage $6/4 = 1.5 = r_1$. ✓
