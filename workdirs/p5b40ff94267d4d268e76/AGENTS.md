# Problem

In the coastal logistics hub of Veridia, a shipping manager handles daily consignments of cargo crates. Each individual crate has a weight that is a positive integer number of tons, with a strict safety regulation that no single crate can weigh more than 10 tons.

The manager’s goal is to determine the maximum total weight $S$ (the sum of all crates' weights) that can be accepted in a single shipment such that, regardless of how the individual crate weights are distributed, it is always possible to divide the entire shipment into exactly two separate delivery trucks, where each truck has a maximum carrying capacity of 70 tons.

What is the maximum possible integer value of $S$ that guarantees a valid partition into these two trucks for any set of crates meeting the weight constraints?
