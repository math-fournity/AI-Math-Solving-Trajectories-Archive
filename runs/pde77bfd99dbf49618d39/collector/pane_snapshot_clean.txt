$$
f(-x)=\frac{x^{2}\cos x}{1+e^{-u(x)}}.

The denominator is $2+e^{u}+e^{-u}$, equal to the numerator, so the fraction equ

$$
f(x)+f(-x)=x^{2}\cos x.
