## Solution

Consider the hyperboloid of two sheets
\[
\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}-\frac{z^{2}}{c^{2}}=1,\qquad a,b,c>0,
\]
as the level set \(F(x,y,z)=0\) with \(F=\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}
-\frac{z^{2}}{c^{2}}-1\).

### Local graph form near \((a,0,0)\)

Near \((a,0,0)\) the sheet is \(x>0\), so
\[
x=a\sqrt{1+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}+\frac{z^{2}}{c^{2}}}.
\]
Set \(f(y,z)=a\sqrt{1+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}+\frac{z^{2}}{c^{2}}}\). Then
\[
f(0,0)=a,\quad f_{y}(0,0)=f_{z}(0,0)=0,\quad f_{yz}(0,0)=0,
\]
\[
f_{yy}(0,0)=\frac{a}{b^{2}},\qquad f_{zz}(0,0)=\frac{a}{c^{2}}.
\]

### Parametrization and normal

Parametrize \(\mathbf r(y,z)=(f(y,z),\,y,\,z)\). At \((0,0)\):
\[
\mathbf r_y=(0,1,0),\quad \mathbf r_z=(0,0,1),\quad
\mathbf r_y\times\mathbf r_z=(1,0,0).
\]
The outward unit normal (pointing away from the origin, in the \(+x\) direction)
 is
\[
\mathbf N=\frac{\mathbf r_y\times\mathbf r_z}{\|\mathbf r_y\times\mathbf r_z\|}=
(1,0,0),
\]
which agrees with \(\nabla F/\|\nabla F\|=(2/a,0,0)/(2/a)=(1,0,0)\).

### Shape operator

The first fundamental form at the vertex is \(E=F_{\text{I}}=1,\;F_{\text{I}}=0,
\;G=1\). The Weingarten map is \(S=-d\mathbf N\). Since
\[
\mathbf N(y,z)=\frac{(1,-f_y,-f_z)}{\sqrt{1+f_y^{2}+f_z^{2}}},
\]
differentiating at \((0,0)\) (where \(f_y=f_z=0\)):
\[
\frac{\partial\mathbf N}{\partial y}=(0,-f_{yy},0)=\Bigl(0,-\frac{a}{b^{2}},0\Bi
gr),\qquad
\frac{\partial\mathbf N}{\partial z}=(0,0,-f_{zz})=\Bigl(0,0,-\frac{a}{c^{2}}\Bi
gr).
\]
Hence
\[
S(\mathbf r_y)=-\frac{\partial\mathbf N}{\partial y}=\frac{a}{b^{2}}\,\mathbf r_
y,\qquad
S(\mathbf r_z)=-\frac{\partial\mathbf N}{\partial z}=\frac{a}{c^{2}}\,\mathbf r_
z.
\]
The principal curvatures are the eigenvalues of \(S\):
\[
\boxed{k_{1}=\frac{a}{b^{2}},\qquad k_{2}=\frac{a}{c^{2}}.}
\]

### The point \((-a,0,0)\)

By symmetry the left sheet is \(x=-a\sqrt{1+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}+\frac{z^{2}}{c^{
2}}}\). Let \(g(y,z)=-a\sqrt{1+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}+\frac{z^{2}}{c^{2}}}\), so \(
g_{yy}(0,0)=-a/b^{2}\), \(g_{zz}(0,0)=-a/c^{2}\). The outward normal here is \(\
mathbf N=(-1,0,0)\) (pointing in the \(-x\) direction, away from the origin), co
nsistent with \(\nabla F/\|\nabla F\|=(-1,0,0)\).

With \(\mathbf r(y,z)=(g(y,z),y,z)\) we have \(\mathbf r_y\times\mathbf r_z=(1,0
,0)\) at the vertex, so the outward normal is the negative of this, \(\mathbf N=
(-1,0,0)\). Repeating the differentiation with this \(\mathbf N\):
\[
\frac{\partial\mathbf N}{\partial y}=(0,f_{yy}^{*},0)\ \text{with sign flipped}\
 \Longrightarrow\ S(\mathbf r_y)=\frac{a}{b^{2}}\,\mathbf r_y,\qquad
S(\mathbf r_z)=\frac{a}{c^{2}}\,\mathbf r_z.
\]
Equivalently, the surface is invariant under \((x,y,z)\mapsto(-x,y,z)\), which r
everses the normal but also reverses the orientation of the shape operator, leav
ing the principal curvatures unchanged. Thus at \((-a,0,0)\):
\[
\boxed{k_{1}=\frac{a}{b^{2}},\qquad k_{2}=\frac{a}{c^{2}}.}
\]

### Conclusion

At both vertices \((\pm a,0,0)\) of the hyperboloid of two sheets, the principal
 curvatures (with respect to the outward normal) are
\[
\boxed{\,k_{1}=\dfrac{a}{b^{2}},\qquad k_{2}=\dfrac{a}{c^{2}}\,}.
\]
