## Proof

**Step 1: Characterize the condition $\limsup_{n\to\infty}\int_{-1}^{1}f(x)^n\,d
x<\infty$.**

Let $f\in C([-1,1])$ with $f\ge 0$, and set $M=\max_{x\in[-1,1]}f(x)$.

- **If $M>1$:** Pick $x_0$ with $f(x_0)>1$. By continuity there exist $a<b$ and
$c>1$ with $f(x)\ge c$ on $[a,b]$. Then $\int_{-1}^{1}f(x)^n\,dx\ge (b-a)\,c^n\t
o\infty$, so the limsup is $+\infty$. ✗

- **If $M\le 1$:** Then $0\le f(x)\le 1$ for all $x$, so $0\le f(x)^n\le 1$ and
$\int_{-1}^{1}f(x)^n\,dx\le\int_{-1}^{1}1\,dx=2$ for every $n$. Hence $\limsup_{
n\to\infty}\int_{-1}^{1}f(x)^n\,dx\le 2<\infty$. ✓

Therefore
$$\mathcal{F}=\bigl\{f\in C([-1,1]):\;0\le f(x)\le 1\text{ for all }x\in[-1,1]\b
igr\}.$$

**Step 2: Determine the image under $f\mapsto\int_{-1}^{1}f(x)\,dx$.**

For any $f\in\mathcal{F}$, since $0\le f\le 1$,
$$0\le\int_{-1}^{1}f(x)\,dx\le\int_{-1}^{1}1\,dx=2,$$
so the image is contained in $[0,2]$.

Conversely, every value $a\in[0,2]$ is attained: take the constant function $f(x
)=a/2$. Since $0\le a/2\le 1$, we have $f\in\mathcal{F}$, and
$$\int_{-1}^{1}f(x)\,dx=\int_{-1}^{1}\frac{a}{2}\,dx=\frac{a}{2}\cdot 2=a.$$

**Conclusion.** The image of $\mathcal{F}$ under the map $f\mapsto\int_{-1}^{1}f
(x)\,dx$ is the closed interval $\boxed{[0,2]}$.
