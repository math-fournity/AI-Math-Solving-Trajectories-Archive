## Solution

The sequence $S_n$ is the Lucas sequence $U_n(P,Q)$ with $P=1, Q=-5$, satisfying
 $S_n = S_{n-1} + 5S_{n-2}$. Its discriminant is $D = P^2 - 4Q = 1+20 = 21$.

**Theory of Lucas sequences.** For a prime $p \nmid 2QD$, the rank of apparition
 $\omega(p)$ (the least $n$ with $p \mid S_n$) divides $p - \left(\frac{D}{p}\ri
ght)$, where $\left(\frac{D}{p}\right)$ is the Legendre symbol. Consequently:
- If $\left(\frac{D}{p}\right)=1$, then $\omega(p) \mid p-1$, so $p \mid S_{p-1}
$.
- If $\left(\frac{D}{p}\right)=-1$, then $\omega(p) \mid p+1$. For $p \mid S_{p-
1}$ we'd need $\omega(p) \mid \gcd(p-1,p+1)=2$, i.e. $\omega(p)\in\{1,2\}$. But
$S_1=S_2=1$, so no prime $>1$ divides them. Thus $p \nmid S_{p-1}$.

Therefore, for odd primes $p \notin\{3,5,7\}$:
$$p \mid S_{p-1} \iff \left(\tfrac{21}{p}\right)=1.$$

**Special primes.**
- $p=2$: $S_1=1$, not divisible by 2. ✗
- $p=3$: $3\mid 21$ (special); $S_2=1$. ✗
- $p=5$: $5\mid Q$ (special); $S_4=11$. ✗
- $p=7$: $7\mid 21$ (special); $S_6=96=7\cdot13+5$. ✗

**Computing $\left(\frac{21}{p}\right)=\left(\frac{3}{p}\right)\left(\frac{7}{p}
\right)$ for primes $p\geq 11$:**

Using $\left(\frac{3}{p}\right)=1 \iff p\equiv\pm1\pmod{12}$ and quadratic recip
rocity for $\left(\frac{7}{p}\right)$:

| $p$ | $\left(\frac{3}{p}\right)$ | $\left(\frac{7}{p}\right)$ | $\left(\frac{2
1}{p}\right)$ |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 11 | $-1$ ($11\equiv11$) | $-1$ | $-1$ ✗ |
| 13 | $+1$ ($13\equiv1$) | $-1$ | $-1$ ✗ |

| 19 | $+1$ ($19\equiv7$... $19\bmod12=7\Rightarrow-1$) | — | $-1$ ✗ |

**Verification of $\left(\frac{7}{p}\right)$ values** (via $(7/p)(p/7)=(-1)^{3(p
-1)/2}$):
- $p=17$: $(7/17)=(17/7)=(3/7)=-1$ ✓
- $p=37$: $(7/37)=(37/7)=(2/7)=+1$ ✓
- $p=41$: $(7/41)=(41/7)=(6/7)=(2/7)(3/7)=(+1)(-1)=-1$ ✓
- $p=43$: $(7/43)=-(43/7)=-(1/7)=-1$ ✓
- $p=47$: $(7/47)=-(47/7)=-(5/7)=-(-1)=+1$ ✓ (since $(5/7)=(7/5)=(2/5)=-1$)

**Direct verification for $p=17$:** Computing $S_{16} = 2960771 = 17 \times 1741
63$. ✓

**Direct verification for $p=37$:** Computing $S_n \bmod 37$ through $n=36$ give
s $S_{36}\equiv 0\pmod{37}$. ✓

**The five smallest primes** $p$ with $p\mid S_{p-1}$ are:
$$17,\ 37,\ 41,\ 43,\ 47.$$

**Sum:** $17+37+41+43+47 = 185$.
