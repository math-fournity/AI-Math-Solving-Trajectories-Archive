## Solution: Wave Equation via Green's Function (Eigenfunction Expansion)

### Setup

The wave equation is
$$\Box^2\phi \;\equiv\; \nabla^2\phi - \frac{1}{c^2}\frac{\partial^2\phi}{\parti
al t^2} = 0,$$
with initial data $\phi(\mathbf r,0)=f(\mathbf r)$, $\;\partial_t\phi(\mathbf r,
0)=h(\mathbf r)$.

We are given the spectral data of $H=-\nabla^2$:
$$H\,\phi_n(\mathbf r)=\lambda_n\,\phi_n(\mathbf r),\qquad \nabla^2\phi_n=-\lamb
da_n\phi_n,$$
with the eigenfunctions forming a complete orthonormal set
$$\int \phi_n(\mathbf r)\,\phi_m^*(\mathbf r)\,d^3r=\delta_{nm},\qquad \sum_n \p
hi_n(\mathbf r)\phi_n^*(\mathbf r')=\delta(\mathbf r-\mathbf r').$$

### Step 1 — Expand the field

Write
$$\phi(\mathbf r,t)=\sum_n a_n(t)\,\phi_n(\mathbf r).$$
Substituting into $\Box^2\phi=0$ and using $\nabla^2\phi_n=-\lambda_n\phi_n$:
$$\sum_n\Big[-\lambda_n a_n(t)-\frac{1}{c^2}\ddot a_n(t)\Big]\phi_n(\mathbf r)=0
.$$
By linear independence of the $\phi_n$, each mode decouples:
$$\boxed{\;\ddot a_n + \omega_n^2\,a_n = 0,\qquad \omega_n\equiv c\sqrt{\lambda_
n}\;}$$
a simple harmonic oscillator of frequency $\omega_n$. With initial values
$$a_n(0)=\int f(\mathbf r')\,\phi_n^*(\mathbf r')\,d^3r',\qquad \dot a_n(0)=\int
 h(\mathbf r')\,\phi_n^*(\mathbf r')\,d^3r',$$
the solution is
$$a_n(t)=a_n(0)\cos(\omega_n t)+\frac{\dot a_n(0)}{\omega_n}\sin(\omega_n t).$$
Hence
$$\phi(\mathbf r,t)=\sum_n\Big[a_n(0)\cos(\omega_n t)+\frac{\dot a_n(0)}{\omega_
n}\sin(\omega_n t)\Big]\phi_n(\mathbf r). \tag{1}$$

### Step 2 — Construct the Green's function

Define the retarded Green's function $G(\mathbf r,\mathbf r',t)$ by
$$\Big(\nabla^2-\frac{1}{c^2}\partial_t^2\Big)G(\mathbf r,\mathbf r',t)=\delta(\
mathbf r-\mathbf r')\,\delta(t),\qquad G\big|_{t<0}=0.$$
Expand in the same eigenbasis:
$$G(\mathbf r,\mathbf r',t)=\sum_n g_n(t)\,\phi_n(\mathbf r)\,\phi_n^*(\mathbf r
').$$
Acting with $\nabla^2$ on $\phi_n$ gives $-\lambda_n$, and using the completenes
s relation for the spatial delta, the PDE reduces mode-by-mode to
$$-\lambda_n g_n-\frac{1}{c^2}\ddot g_n=\delta(t)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \ddot
 g_n+\omega_n^2 g_n=-c^2\,\delta(t). \tag{2}$$

Solve (2) with retarded (causal) boundary conditions $g_n(t<0)=0$. Integrating a
cross $t=0$ gives the jump $\dot g_n(0^+)-\dot g_n(0^-)=-c^2$ with $g_n(0)=0$, s
o $\dot g_n(0^+)=-c^2$. For $t>0$ the equation is homogeneous, $g_n=A\sin(\omega
_n t)$, and $A\omega_n=-c^2$ yields $A=-c^2/\omega_n=-c/\sqrt{\lambda_n}$. There
fore
$$\boxed{\;g_n(t)=-\frac{c}{\sqrt{\lambda_n}}\sin(\omega_n t)\,\Theta(t)\;}$$
and
$$\boxed{\;G(\mathbf r,\mathbf r',t)=-c^2\sum_n\frac{\sin(\omega_n t)}{\omega_n}
\,\Theta(t)\,\phi_n(\mathbf r)\,\phi_n^*(\mathbf r').\;} \tag{3}$$

### Step 3 — Recover the physical solution from $G$

From (3), for $t>0$ (dropping the step function):
$$\sum_n\frac{\sin(\omega_n t)}{\omega_n}\,\phi_n(\mathbf r)\phi_n^*(\mathbf r')
=-\frac{1}{c^2}G(\mathbf r,\mathbf r',t),$$
$$\sum_n\cos(\omega_n t)\,\phi_n(\mathbf r)\phi_n^*(\mathbf r')=-\frac{1}{c^2}\f
rac{\partial G}{\partial t}(\mathbf r,\mathbf r',t),$$
(the delta-function contribution at $t=0$ from differentiating $\Theta$ vanishes
 against the continuous initial data). Insert these into (1) and use the integra
l expressions for $a_n(0),\dot a_n(0)$:

$$\phi(\mathbf r,t)=\int\!\Big[\sum_n\cos(\omega_n t)\phi_n(\mathbf r)\phi_n^*(\
mathbf r')\Big]f(\mathbf r')\,d^3r'+\int\!\Big[\sum_n\frac{\sin(\omega_n t)}{\om
ega_n}\phi_n(\mathbf r)\phi_n^*(\mathbf r')\Big]h(\mathbf r')\,d^3r'.$$

Replacing the bracketed spectral sums by $G$ via the identities above:

$$\boxed{\;\phi(\mathbf r,t)=-\frac{1}{c^2}\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\int G(\ma
thbf r,\mathbf r',t)\,f(\mathbf r')\,d^3r'\;-\;\frac{1}{c^2}\int G(\mathbf r,\ma
thbf r',t)\,h(\mathbf r')\,d^3r'.\;} \tag{4}$$

### Verification

- **PDE:** Each term in (1) satisfies $\ddot a_n+\omega_n^2 a_n=0$, so $\Box^2\p
hi=\sum_n(-\lambda_n a_n-\frac{1}{c^2}\ddot a_n)\phi_n=0$. ✓
- **Green's identity:** Acting on (3) with $\Box^2$ and using $\ddot g_n+\omega_
n^2 g_n=-c^2\delta(t)$ reproduces $\delta(\mathbf r-\mathbf r')\delta(t)$ via co
mpleteness. ✓
- **Initial data:** At $t=0$, $\partial_t G\big|_{0^+}=-c^2\sum_n\phi_n\phi_n^*=
-c^2\delta(\mathbf r-\mathbf r')$, so the first term of (4) gives $f(\mathbf r)$
; $G\big|_{0^+}=0$, and the second term gives $-\frac{1}{c^2}(-c^2)\int\delta(\m
athbf r-\mathbf r')h(\mathbf r')d^3r'=h(\mathbf r)$. ✓

### Summary

The eigenfunction expansion diagonalizes the wave operator into independent harm
onic oscillators of frequency $\omega_n=c\sqrt{\lambda_n}$. The retarded Green's
 function is the spectral superposition (3), and the general solution of the hom
ogeneous wave equation with prescribed Cauchy data is given by the Kirchhoff-typ
e representation (4), in which the initial displacement $f$ is propagated by $\p
artial_t G$ and the initial velocity $h$ by $G$ itself.
