# Problem

In the competitive world of professional logistics, two shipping magnates, Alice and Bob, are competing to clear a stockpile of 100 industrial shipping containers from a shared dock. The rules of the port authority are strict: whoever hauls away the very last container from the dock wins the exclusive government contract.

Alice, being the first to arrive, is permitted to haul away any number of containers on her opening move, provided she takes at least one and no more than 50 containers. 

For every move thereafter, the rules transition to a momentum-based constraint: a player can haul away any number of containers (at least one) up to a maximum of twice the number of containers their opponent moved in the immediately preceding turn.

Assuming both magnates play with perfect strategy to secure the contract, determine the specific number of containers Alice should take on her very first turn to guarantee her victory. If there are multiple such winning moves, list them all.
