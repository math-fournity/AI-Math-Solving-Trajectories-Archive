**Proof.**

Let *G* be a connected Lie group with a faithful, irreducible, finite-dimensiona
l unitary representation ρ: *G* → U(*n*). Since ρ is faithful, *G* ≅ *H* := ρ(*G
*), the immersed Lie subgroup of U(*n*) with Lie algebra **g**′ := dρ(**g**) ⊂ u
(*n*). It suffices to show *H* is compact.

**Step 1: g′ is reductive.**

The Lie algebra u(*n*) is compact: it carries the Ad-invariant inner product ⟨*X
*, *Y*⟩ = −tr(*XY*). Any subalgebra **g**′ of a compact Lie algebra is reductive
.

*Proof.* The restriction of this inner product to **g**′ is Ad(**g**′)-invariant
. Let **r** be the radical of **g**′. By Ad-invariance, **r**⊥ is also an ideal
(for *Y* ∈ **r**⊥, *Z* ∈ **g**′, *X* ∈ **r**: ⟨[*Z*,*Y*],*X*⟩ = ⟨*Y*,[*X*,*Z*]⟩
= 0 since [*X*,*Z*] ∈ **r**), so **g**′ = **r** ⊕ **r**⊥. The radical **r** is s
olvable with an Ad(**r**)-invariant inner product. For *X* ∈ **r**, ad(*X*) is s
kew-symmetric (hence ℂ-diagonalizable) and, by Lie's theorem on the solvable com
plexification **r**_ℂ, upper-triangular; therefore ad_ℂ(*X*) is diagonal for all
 *X*, so **r** is abelian. The complement **r**⊥ is semisimple. Thus **g**′ = **
a** ⊕ **s**′ with **a** abelian and **s**′ semisimple. ∎

**Step 2: The abelian part a acts by scalars (Schur's lemma).**

Since *G* is connected, irreducibility of the group representation implies irred
ucibility of the Lie algebra representation of **g**′ on ℂ^*n*. By Schur's lemma
 applied to the complexification **g**′_ℂ, the center **a**_ℂ acts by scalar end
omorphisms. Since **a** ⊂ u(*n*), these scalars lie in *i*ℝ·*I*. Thus **a** ⊂ ℝ·
*iI*, which is 1-dimensional, so **a** is either {0} or ℝ·*iI*.

**Step 3: H is compact in both cases.**

- **If a = {0}:** **g**′ = **s**′ is semisimple. As a semisimple subalgebra of t
he compact algebra su(*n*), **s**′ has negative-definite Killing form (compact t
ype). The simply connected group with Lie algebra **s**′ is compact, so the imme
rsed subgroup *S*′ ⊂ SU(*n*) is a quotient of a compact group—hence compact and
closed. Thus *H* = *S*′ is compact.

- **If a = ℝ·iI:** **g**′ = ℝ·*iI* ⊕ **s**′. Since *iI* is central in u(*n*), th
e corresponding subgroups commute: *H* = *U*(1)·*I* · *S*′ = {*zA* : *z* ∈ *U*(1
), *A* ∈ *S*′}, where *S*′ is the compact semisimple immersed subgroup from abov
e. The multiplication map *U*(1) × *S*′ → *U*(*n*), (*z*, *A*) ↦ *zA*, is a cont
inuous homomorphism from a compact group, so its image *H* is compact, hence clo
sed.

In both cases *H* is compact, so *G* ≅ *H* is compact. ∎

{*it*√2)). Irreducibility forces **a** to be at most 1-dimensional (Schur's lemm
a), eliminating this possibility and guaranteeing compactness.
