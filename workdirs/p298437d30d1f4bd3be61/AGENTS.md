# Problem

In a remote industrial shipyard, there are 10 heavy-duty cargo ships and 15 transport barges docked at a terminal. A specialized salvage operation is underway to move these vessels into dry-dock for repairs. 

To power the massive hydraulic lift required to move a cargo ship, the energy harvested from the recycled scrap metal of 3 transport barges is needed. Conversely, to power the lift required to move a transport barge, the recycled scrap metal of 2 cargo ships is required. Note that once a vessel is scrapped to provide energy for another, its own hull remains available to be lifted into dry-dock, but it cannot be used as a fuel source twice.

Assuming that the process of "powering a lift" for a vessel is equivalent to making that vessel "ready for repair," determine the maximum total number of vessels (cargo ships and transport barges combined) that can be made ready for repair.
