# Galois Group of $g(x) = x^6 + 2x^3 - 3$ over $\mathbb{Q}$

## Step 1: Factorization

Substitute $y = x^3$:
$$g(x) = y^2 + 2y - 3 = (y+3)(y-1),$$
so
$$g(x) = (x^3 + 3)(x^3 - 1) = (x^3 + 3)(x-1)(x^2 + x + 1).$$

## Step 2: Identify the roots

Let $\alpha = \sqrt[3]{3}$ (the real cube root) and $\omega = e^{2\pi i/3}$, a p
rimitive cube root of unity.

- From $x^3 - 1 = 0$: roots $1,\ \omega,\ \omega^2$.
- From $x^3 + 3 = 0$ (i.e. $x^3 = -3$): roots $-\alpha,\ -\alpha\omega,\ -\alpha
\omega^2$.

## Step 3: The splitting field

All six roots lie in $\mathbb{Q}(\alpha, \omega)$, and conversely $\alpha = -(-\
alpha)$ and $\omega = (-\alpha\omega)/(-\alpha)$ are expressible from the roots,
 so the splitting field is
$$K = \mathbb{Q}(\alpha, \omega).$$

## Step 4: Degree of the extension

- $x^3 - 3$ is irreducible over $\mathbb{Q}$ (Eisenstein at $p = 3$), so $[\math
bb{Q}(\alpha) : \mathbb{Q}] = 3$.
- $x^2 + x + 1$ is irreducible over $\mathbb{Q}$, so $[\mathbb{Q}(\omega) : \mat
hbb{Q}] = 2$.
- Since $\gcd(2, 3) = 1$, the degrees multiply:
$$ : \mathbb{Q}] = [\mathbb{Q}(\alpha, \omega) : \mathbb{Q}] = 6.$$

Hence $|G| = |\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q})| = 6$.

## Step 5: Structure of the Galois group

Every $\mathbb{Q}$-automorphism of $K$ is determined by its action on $\alpha$ a
nd $\omega$:
- $\sigma(\alpha) \in \{\alpha,\ \alpha\omega,\ \alpha\omega^2\}$ (3 choices, th
e roots of $x^3 - 3$),
- $\sigma(\omega) \in \{\omega,\ \omega^2\}$ (2 choices, the roots of $x^2 + x +
 1$).

These $3 \times 2 = 6$ choices are all realized, confirming $|G| = 6$.

Define two generators:
- $\sigma$: $\sigma(\alpha) = \alpha\omega$, $\sigma(\omega) = \omega$. This has
 order $3$.
- $\tau$: $\tau(\alpha) = \alpha$, $\tau(\omega) = \omega^2$ (complex conjugatio
n). This has order $2$.

Compute the conjugation relation:
$$\tau\sigma\tau^{-1}(\alpha) = \tau\sigma(\alpha) = \tau(\alpha\omega) = \alpha
\omega^2 = \sigma^{-1}(\alpha),$$
$$\tau\sigma\tau^{-1}(\omega) = \tau\sigma(\omega^2) = \tau(\omega^2) = \omega =
 \sigma^{-1}(\omega).$$

Therefore $\tau\sigma\tau^{-1} = \sigma^{-1}$, which is precisely the defining r
elation of the dihedral group $D_3 \cong S_3$:
$$G = \langle \sigma, \tau \mid \sigma^3 = \tau^2 = 1,\ \tau\sigma\tau = \sigma^
{-1} \rangle \cong S_3.$$

## Step 6: Confirmation via discriminant

The splitting field $K = \mathbb{Q}(\alpha, \omega)$ is exactly the splitting fi
eld of the irreducible cubic $x^3 - 3$. Its discriminant is
$$\Delta = -4p^3 - 27q^2\big|_{p=0,\, q=-3} = -27 \cdot 9 = -243,$$
which is not a square in $\mathbb{Q}$. For an irreducible cubic, a nonsquare dis
criminant means the Galois group is $S_3$ (not $A_3$), confirming our result.

## Conclusion

$$\boxed{\operatorname{Gal}(g/\mathbb{Q}) \cong S_3.}$$
