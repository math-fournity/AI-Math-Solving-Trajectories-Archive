## Solution

We evaluate the line integral using **Stokes' theorem**:

$$\oint_C \vec{F}\cdot d\vec{r} = \iint_S (\nabla \times \vec{F})\cdot \hat{n}\,
 dS$$

where $\vec{F} = e^{x^3}\,\vec{i} + y^3\,\vec{j} + e^{z^2}\,\vec{k}$, and $S$ is
 any surface bounded by $C$.

### Computing the curl

With $P = e^{x^3}$, $Q = y^3$, $R = e^{z^2}$:

$$\nabla \times \vec{F} = \left(\frac{\partial R}{\partial y} - \frac{\partial Q
}{\partial z},\; \frac{\partial P}{\partial z} - \frac{\partial R}{\partial x},\
; \frac{\partial Q}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial P}{\partial y}\right)$$

- $\frac{\partial R}{\partial y} = \frac{\partial}{\partial y}e^{z^2} = 0$, and
$\frac{\partial Q}{\partial z} = \frac{\partial}{\partial z}y^3 = 0$ → first com
ponent $= 0$
- $\frac{\partial P}{\partial z} = \frac{\partial}{\partial z}e^{x^3} = 0$, and
$\frac{\partial R}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}e^{z^2} = 0$ → secon
d component $= 0$
- $\frac{\partial Q}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}y^3 = 0$, and $\fr
ac{\partial P}{\partial y} = \frac{\partial}{\partial y}e^{x^3} = 0$ → third com
ponent $= 0$

The key observation: each component of $\vec{F}$ depends **only on its own varia
ble** ($P$ on $x$, $Q$ on $y$, $R$ on $z$), so every mixed partial derivative va
nishes. Therefore

$$\nabla \times \vec{F} = \vec{0}.$$

### Applying Stokes' theorem

Since the curl is identically zero everywhere (the field is irrotational on all
of $\mathbb{R}^3$), for **any** surface $S$ bounded by $C$ — for instance the pa
raboloid cap $z = x^2+y^2$ inside the curve, or the planar disk cut from $z = x-
3y$ — we have

$$\oint_C \vec{F}\cdot d\vec{r} = \iint_S \vec{0}\cdot \hat{n}\, dS = 0.$$

### Result

$$\boxed{\oint_C (e^{x^3}\,\vec{i} + y^3\,\vec{j} + e^{z^2}\,\vec{k})\cdot d\vec
{r} = 0}$$
