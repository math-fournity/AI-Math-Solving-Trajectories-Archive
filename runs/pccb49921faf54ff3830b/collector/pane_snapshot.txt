We compute
\[
L=\lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{\left(\int_0^x e^{y^2}\,dy\right)^2}{\int_0^x e^{2y^2}
\,dy}.
\]

Set
\[
I(x)=\int_0^x e^{y^2}\,dy,\qquad J(x)=\int_0^x e^{2y^2}\,dy.
\]
Both $I(x),J(x)\to+\infty$ as $x\to+\infty$, so $L$ is an $\infty/\infty$ indete
rminate form. Applying L'Hôpital's rule,
\[
L=\lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{2I(x)\,I'(x)}{J'(x)}
=\lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{2I(x)\,e^{x^2}}{e^{2x^2}}
=\lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{2I(x)}{e^{x^2}}.
\]

It remains to find the asymptotic behavior of $I(x)$. Since $I'(x)=e^{x^2}$, com
pare $I(x)$ with $\frac{e^{x^2}}{2x}$. By L'Hôpital,
\[
\lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{I(x)}{\frac{e^{x^2}}{2x}}
=\lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{e^{x^2}}{\frac{d}{dx}\!\left(\frac{e^{x^2}}{2x}\right)}
=\lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{e^{x^2}}{\frac{2x\cdot 2x\,e^{x^2}-e^{x^2}}{4x^2}}
=\lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{4x^2}{4x^2-1}=1.
\]
Hence $I(x)\sim \dfrac{e^{x^2}}{2x}$ as $x\to+\infty$, and therefore
\[
\frac{2I(x)}{e^{x^2}}\sim \frac{2\cdot \frac{e^{x^2}}{2x}}{e^{x^2}}=\frac{1}{x}\
xrightarrow[x\to+\infty]{}0.
\]

Thus
\[
L=\lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{2I(x)}{e^{x^2}}=0.
\]
