The Lebesgue outer measure of $E$ is $\mathbf{0}$.

$$m^*\!\left(\{q_i\}\times[0,1]\right)\le \frac{\varepsilon}{2^{i+1}}.$$

**Why the answer is not $1$.** One might be tempted to argue that since the rati
onals are dense in $\mathbb{R}$, "for every $y\in[0,1]$ there are points of $E$
at that height," suggesting the set is "thick." But this conflates density of th

$$\boxed{m^*(E)=0}$$
