## Cycle Index of the Dihedral Group $H_{2n}$

**Setup.** Let $H_{2n}$ be the dihedral group of order $2n$, acting on the $n$ v
ertices of a regular $n$-gon. It contains $n$ rotations and $n$ reflections. The
 cycle index is

$$Z(H_{2n}) = \frac{1}{|H_{2n}|}\sum_{g\in H_{2n}} \operatorname{cyc}(g) = \frac
{1}{2n}\sum_{g\in H_{2n}} \operatorname{cyc}(g),$$

where $\operatorname{cyc}(g) = \prod_i x_i^{c_i(g)}$ and $c_i(g)$ is the number
of $i$-cycles in the permutation $g$.

---

### Rotations

Label the vertices $0,1,\dots,n-1$. The rotation $\rho^k$ ($k=0,1,\dots,n-1$) se
nds $i\mapsto i+k\pmod n$. Its cycle decomposition is governed by $d=\gcd(n,k)$:
 $\rho^k$ splits into exactly $d$ cycles, each of length $n/d$. Hence

$$\operatorname{cyc}(\rho^k) = x_{\,n/d}^{\,d}, \qquad d=\gcd(n,k).$$

For a fixed divisor $d\mid n$, the number of $k\in\{0,\dots,n-1\}$ with $\gcd(n,
k)=d$ equals $\varphi(n/d)$. Re-indexing by $m=n/d$ (so $m\mid n$, $d=n/m$), the
 total rotation contribution is

$$\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\operatorname{cyc}(\rho^k) \;=\; \sum_{m\mid n}\varphi(m)\,x_m
^{\,n/m}.$$

(Indeed each $m\mid n$ contributes $\varphi(m)$ rotations, each of cycle type $x
_m^{\,n/m}$; note $\sum_{m\mid n}\varphi(m)=n$, confirming all $n$ rotations are
 accounted for.)

---

### Reflections

The reflection structure depends on the parity of $n$.

**Case 1: $n$ odd.** Every reflection axis passes through one vertex and the mid
point of the opposite edge. Thus each reflection fixes exactly $1$ vertex and sw
aps the remaining $n-1$ vertices in $(n-1)/2$ disjoint transpositions:

$$\operatorname{cyc}(\sigma) = x_1\,x_2^{(n-1)/2}.$$

All $n$ reflections share this type, so the reflection contribution is

$$n\,x_1\,x_2^{(n-1)/2}.$$

**Case 2: $n$ even.** There are two conjugacy classes of reflections:

- **$n/2$ reflections through opposite vertices.** These fix $2$ vertices and pa
ir the remaining $n-2$ into $(n-2)/2$ transpositions:
  $$\operatorname{cyc} = x_1^2\,x_2^{(n-2)/2}.$$

- **$n/2$ reflections through midpoints of opposite edges.** These fix no vertex
 and pair all $n$ vertices into $n/2$ transpositions:
  $$\operatorname{cyc} = x_2^{\,n/2}.$$

The reflection contribution is therefore

$$\frac{n}{2}\,x_1^2\,x_2^{(n-2)/2} \;+\; \frac{n}{2}\,x_2^{\,n/2}.$$

---

### Combining

Dividing the sum of all $2n$ cycle monomials by $2n$:

$$\boxed{\;Z(H_{2n}) \;=\; \frac{1}{2n}\!\left(\sum_{d\mid n}\varphi(d)\,x_d^{\,
n/d} \;+\; \begin{cases} n\,x_1\,x_2^{(n-1)/2}, & n\ \text{odd},\\[4pt] \dfrac{n
}{2}\,x_1^2\,x_2^{(n-2)/2} + \dfrac{n}{2}\,x_2^{\,n/2}, & n\ \text{even}. \end{c
ases}\right)\;}$$

---

### Verification (small cases)

- **$n=3$** (equilateral triangle, $|H_6|=6$): $\sum_{d\mid 3}\varphi(d)x_d^{3/d
}=\varphi(1)x_1^3+\varphi(3)x_3= x_1^3+2x_3$; reflections: $3x_1x_2$. So $Z=\tfr
ac16(x_1^3+2x_3+3x_1x_2)$. ✓ (standard).

- **$n=4$** (square, $|H_8|=8$): $\sum_{d\mid 4}\varphi(d)x_d^{4/d}=\varphi(1)x_
1^4+\varphi(2)x_2^2+\varphi(4)x_4=x_1^4+x_2^2+2x_4$; reflections: $\tfrac42 x_1^
2 x_2^{1}+\tfrac42 x_2^2 = 2x_1^2 x_2+2x_2^2$. So $Z=\tfrac18(x_1^4+x_2^2+2x_4+2
x_1^2 x_2+2x_2^2)=\tfrac18(x_1^4+3x_2^2+2x_4+2x_1^2 x_2)$. ✓ (standard).

- **$n=5$**: $Z=\tfrac1{10}(x_1^5+4x_5+5x_1 x_2^2)$. ✓

- **$n=6$**: $Z=\tfrac1{12}(x_1^6+x_2^3+2x_3^2+2x_6+3x_1^2 x_2^2+3x_2^3)=\tfrac1
{12}(x_1^6+4x_2^3+2x_3^2+2x_6+3x_1^2 x_2^2)$. ✓

All agree with the known cycle indices, confirming the formula.
