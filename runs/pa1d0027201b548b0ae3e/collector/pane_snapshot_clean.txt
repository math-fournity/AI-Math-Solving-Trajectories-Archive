## Discrete Frenet Formula for a Discrete Plane Curve

### Setup

Let $\gamma_n \in \mathbb{R}^2$ be a discrete plane curve, with no three consecu
tive points colinear. Define the **edge vector**

$$
e_n := \gamma_{n+1} - \gamma_n, \qquad \ell_n := |e_n| > 0.
$$

The hypothesis "no three consecutive points colinear" guarantees that consecutiv
e edge vectors $e_n, e_{n+1}$ are not parallel, so the turning angle at each int
erior vertex is well-defined and nonzero.

### The discrete Frenet frame

Define the **discrete unit tangent** at edge $n$:

$$
T_n := \frac{e_n}{\ell_n} = \frac{\gamma_{n+1}-\gamma_n}{|\gamma_{n+1}-\gamma_n|
} \in S^1.
$$

Define the **discrete unit normal** by a $+\pi/2$ rotation, using $J = \begin{pm
atrix} 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$:

$$
N_n := J\, T_n.
$$

The **discrete Frenet frame** at $n$ is the $2\times 2$ matrix whose columns are
 $T_n$ and $N_n$:

$$
\Phi_n := \bigl[\, T_n \;\big|\; N_n \,\bigr] \in SO(2).
$$

Since $\{T_n, N_n\}$ is a positively oriented orthonormal basis of $\mathbb{R}^2
$, $\Phi_n$ is a rotation matrix.

### The discrete turning angle (discrete curvature)

At the interior vertex $\gamma_{n+1}$, the signed turning angle $\kappa_{n+1}$ i
s the oriented angle from $T_n$ to $T_{n+1}$:

$$
\kappa_{n+1} := \operatorname{atan2}\!\bigl( \det(T_n, T_{n+1}),\; \langle T_n,
T_{n+1}\rangle \bigr).
$$

Equivalently, $\kappa_{n+1}$ is the unique angle in $(-\pi,\pi)\setminus\{0\}$ (
nonzero by the no-colinearity hypothesis) satisfying

$$
T_{n+1} = \cos\kappa_{n+1}\, T_n + \sin\kappa_{n+1}\, N_n.
$$

### Derivation of the discrete Frenet formula

Since both $\{T_n, N_n\}$ and $\{T_{n+1}, N_{n+1}\}$ are positively oriented ort
honormal frames, the change of basis from the first to the second is a rotation.
 The rotation that sends $T_n$ to $T_{n+1}$ is exactly $R(\kappa_{n+1})$, where

$$
R(\kappa) = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\kappa & -\sin\kappa \\ \sin\kappa & \cos\kappa
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Concretely, expressing the new frame vectors in the old frame:

$$
T_{n+1} = \cos\kappa_{n+1}\, T_n + \sin\kappa_{n+1}\, N_n,
$$

and, applying the same rotation to $N_n$ (a $+\pi/2$ rotation commutes with $R(\
kappa)$ since both are in $SO(2)$, or equivalently $N_{n+1} = J T_{n+1} = J R(\k
appa_{n+1})T_n = R(\kappa_{n+1}) J T_n = R(\kappa_{n+1}) N_n$):

$$
N_{n+1} = -\sin\kappa_{n+1}\, T_n + \cos\kappa_{n+1}\, N_n.
$$

Stacking these as columns gives the **discrete Frenet formula**:

$$
\boxed{\;\Phi_{n+1} = \Phi_n\, R(\kappa_{n+1})\;}
$$

or, written component-wise,

$$
\boxed{\;
\begin{pmatrix} T_{n+1} & N_{n+1} \end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix} T_n & N_n \end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix} \cos\kappa_{n+1} & -\sin\kappa_{n+1} \\[4pt] \sin\kappa_{n+1} &
\cos\kappa_{n+1} \end{pmatrix}.
\;}
$$

### Companion: the discrete position update

Together with the frame equation, the curve itself satisfies the trivial discret
e "arc-length" equation

$$
\gamma_{n+1} - \gamma_n = \ell_n\, T_n,
$$

i.e. the edge is the tangent times its length. The pair

$$
\gamma_{n+1} = \gamma_n + \ell_n\, T_n, \qquad \Phi_{n+1} = \Phi_n\, R(\kappa_{n
+1})
$$

is the complete discrete Frenet system for a plane curve: the position advances
along the tangent by the edge length, and the frame rotates by the discrete curv
ature (turning angle) at each vertex.

### Verification

- $\Phi_{n+1} = \Phi_n R(\kappa_{n+1}) \in SO(2)$ since $SO(2)$ is closed: ortho
normality and orientation are preserved. ✓
- Recovering $T_{n+1}$: the first column of $\Phi_n R(\kappa_{n+1})$ is $\cos\ka
ppa_{n+1}\,T_n + \sin\kappa_{n+1}\,N_n$, which by definition of $\kappa_{n+1}$ e
quals $T_{n+1}$. ✓
- Consistency of $N_{n+1}$: $J T_{n+1} = J(\cos\kappa\,T_n+\sin\kappa\,N_n) = \c
os\kappa\,N_n - \sin\kappa\,T_n$, which is exactly the second column of $\Phi_n
R(\kappa_{n+1})$. ✓
- Smooth limit: as $\ell_n\to 0$ with $\kappa_{n+1}/\ell_n \to k(s)$, the matrix

ix}0&-k\\k&0\end{pmatrix}$, the classical Frenet equation $\Phi' = \Phi\,K$ for
a plane curve with curvature $k$. ✓
