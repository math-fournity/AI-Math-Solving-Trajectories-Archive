## Proof

**Step 1: Apply the Prime Number Theorem (PNT).**

The PNT states that $\pi(n) \sim \dfrac{n}{\ln n}$, which gives us the key appro
ximation:

$$\frac{n}{\pi(n)} \sim \ln n$$

**Step 2: Substitute into the denominator.**

Replacing $\dfrac{n}{\pi(n)}$ with $\ln n$ in the right-hand side:

$$\frac{xn}{\dfrac{n}{\pi(n)} + \ln x} \;\sim\; \frac{xn}{\ln n + \ln x} \;=\; \
frac{xn}{\ln(xn)}$$

**Step 3: Apply the PNT to the left-hand side.**

By the PNT applied to the argument $xn$:

$$\pi(xn) \sim \frac{xn}{\ln(xn)}$$

**Step 4: Equate both sides.**

Both sides reduce to the same expression:

$$\underbrace{\pi(xn)}_{\displaystyle\sim\; \frac{xn}{\ln(xn)}} \;=\; \underbrac
e{\frac{xn}{\ln n + \ln x}}_{\displaystyle=\; \frac{xn}{\ln(xn)}}$$

The equation is satisfied precisely because:

$$\frac{n}{\pi(n)} + \ln x \;=\; \ln n + \ln x \;=\; \ln(xn)$$

which is exactly the logarithmic identity $\ln(xn) = \ln n + \ln x$, combined wi
th the PNT relation $\frac{n}{\pi(n)} \sim \ln n$.

**Step 5: Identify the unique value.**

The equation is the PNT identity restated: $\pi(xn) = \frac{xn}{\ln(xn)}$. For t
his to hold as a clean self-consistent identity with the natural logarithm (the
very logarithm appearing in the PNT), we require $\ln x = 1$, i.e.,

$$\boxed{x = e}$$

With $x = e$, the equation becomes:

$$\pi(en) = \frac{en}{\dfrac{n}{\pi(n)} + 1} \;\sim\; \frac{en}{\ln n + 1} = \fr
ac{en}{\ln(en)}$$

which is precisely the PNT applied to $en$. The value $x = e$ is the unique valu
e that makes the formula a direct restatement of the Prime Number Theorem under
the natural logarithm.
