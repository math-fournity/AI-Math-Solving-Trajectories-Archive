## Setup and Reformulation

A positive integer $N$ is balanced iff $\Omega(N)$ is even, where $\Omega(N)$ co
unts prime factors with multiplicity. So $f(n) = \Omega(n) \bmod 2$.

Since $P(n) = (n+a)(n+b)$, we have $\Omega(P(n)) = \Omega(n+a) + \Omega(n+b)$. T
his is even iff $\Omega(n+a)$ and $\Omega(n+b)$ have the same parity, i.e., $f(n
+a) = f(n+b)$.

**Condition:** $f(a+i) = f(b+i)$ for all $i = 1, 2, \ldots, 10$.

Setting $p = a+1$, $q = b+1$ (so $2 \leq p < q$), we need the 10-element window
$f[p..p+9]$ to equal $f[q..q+9]$, and we minimize $a+b = p+q-2$.
