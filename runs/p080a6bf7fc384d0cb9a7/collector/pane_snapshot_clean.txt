The functional equation becomes

$$\int_0^1 f(x)\,dx = \frac{1}{3} + \int_0^1 \frac{f^2(x)}{2\sqrt{x}}\,dx,$$

$$\int_0^1 \frac{f^2(x)}{2\sqrt{x}}\,dx - \int_0^1 f(x)\,dx = -\frac{1}{3}. \tag
{$\ast$}$$
