## Correcting for Magnetic Declination

Magnetic declination 2° west means magnetic north is 2° west of true north. Conv

- **AC:** 71° W of N (magnetic) → **73° W of N** (true), bearing 287°
- **AP:** 64° W of S (magnetic) → **62° W of S** (true), bearing 242°

The angle between AC and AP is 287° − 242° = **45°**.

## Coordinate System

Lake surface at z = 0. A at origin (horizontally), x = East, y = North.

- **A** = (0, 0, h) — observer, unknown elevation h
- **C** = (−2200 sin73°, 2200 cos73°, 550) — mountain peak

- **F** = (Fx, Fy, f) — cloud at height f above lake

## Shadow Geometry (F casts shadow at C)

Sun rays travel due north and downward: direction (0, cos41°, −sin41°). The ray
through F hits elevation 550 at C:

- Fx = Cx = −2200 sin73° ... (1)
- Fy = Cy − (f − 550)·cot41° ... (2)

## Reflection Geometry (F's reflection seen at P)

The mirror image F' = (Fx, Fy, −f) across the lake surface. The line from A = (0
,0,h) to F' intersects z = 0 at P. Parametrizing A + s(F'−A), setting z = 0 give
s s = h/(f+h), so:

- Px = h·Fx/(f+h) → **f = h(Fx − Px)/Px** ... (3)
- Py/Px = Fy/Fx → **Fy = Py·Fx/Px** ... (4)

## Solving the System

From (1) and (4): Fy = (−400 cos62°)(−2200 sin73°)/(−400 sin62°) = **−2200 sin73
°·cot62°**

Substituting into (2):

−2200 sin73°·cot62° = 2200 cos73° − (f − 550)·cot41°

(f − 550)·cot41° = 2200(cos73° + sin73°·cot62°)

**f = 550 + 2200(cos73° + sin73°·cot62°)·tan41°** ... (5)

From (3):

f = h·(−2200 sin73° + 400 sin62°)/(−400 sin62°) = h·(11 sin73°/(2 sin62°) − 1) .
.. (6)
