## Proof

Let $A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_n$ be points in the plane with $n \ge 3$, and let $C$ b
e the smallest circle containing all of them, with radius $r = r(A_1, \ldots, A_
n)$ and center $O$.

We consider the set $S$ of points that lie on the boundary of $C$ (i.e., at dist
ance exactly $r$ from $O$). We claim that $S$ is nonempty. Indeed, if no point l
ies on the boundary, we could shrink $C$ slightly and still contain all points,
contradicting minimality.

**Case 1: $|S| = 1$.** Let $A_i$ be the unique boundary point. Since $n \ge 3$,
there are interior points. We can move the center $O$ slightly away from $A_i$ (
toward the interior points) and decrease the radius while still containing all p
oints. This contradicts minimality. Hence $|S| \ge 2$.

 which decreases the distance to both $A_i$ and $A_j$ (since the midpoint minimi
zes the maximum of the two distances). All interior points remain contained for
a sufficiently small move. This decreases $r$, contradicting minimality.

Therefore, $A_iA_j$ must be a diameter: $O$ is the midpoint and $r = \frac{1}{2}

Now consider the convex hull of $S$ (all boundary points). The key fact: **the c

**Better approach:** Use the fact that $O$ is in the convex hull more carefully.
 Actually, the standard result is:

**Claim:** If $O \in \operatorname{conv}(\{A_i, A_j, A_k\})$ and $|A_\ell - O| =

*Proof:* Let $C'$ be any circle containing $A_i, A_j, A_k$ with center $O'$ and
radius $r'$. We want $r' \ge r$.

Since $O \in \operatorname{conv}(\{A_i, A_j, A_k\})$, there exist $\lambda_i, \l
ambda_j, \lambda_k \ge 0$ with $\sum \lambda_\ell = 1$ and $O = \sum \lambda_\el
l A_\ell$.

Now compute:
$$\sum_\ell \lambda_\ell |A_\ell - O'|^2 \ge \left|\sum_\ell \lambda_\ell (A_\el
l - O')\right|^2 = |O - O'|^2$$

by Jensen's inequality (convexity of $|\cdot|^2$). Also:
$$\sum_\ell \lambda_\ell |A_\ell - O'|^2 = \sum_\ell \lambda_\ell (|A_\ell - O|^
2 + 2\langle A_\ell - O, O - O'\rangle + |O - O'|^2)$$
$$= r^2 + 2\left\langle \sum_\ell \lambda_\ell(A_\ell - O),\, O - O'\right\rangl
e + |O-O'|^2 = r^2 + 0 + |O-O'|^2$$

since $\sum \lambda_\ell(A_\ell - O) = O - O = 0$.

So $\sum_\ell \lambda_\ell |A_\ell - O'|^2 = r^2 + |O - O'|^2$.

Since each $|A_\ell - O'| \le r'$ (all three points in $C'$), we have $|A_\ell -
 O'|^2 \le r'^2$, so:
$$r^2 + |O - O'|^2 = \sum_\ell \lambda_\ell |A_\ell - O'|^2 \le r'^2.$$

This gives $r^2 \le r'^2$, hence $r' \ge r$. ✓

This is the key inequality. Now combining:

- $r(A_i, A_j, A_k) \ge r$ (by the claim above),

we get $r(A_i, A_j, A_k) = r$.
