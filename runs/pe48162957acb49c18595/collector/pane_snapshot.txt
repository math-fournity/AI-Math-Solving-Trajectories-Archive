**Deriving the center.** Suppose a sphere with center $S = (x_0, y_0, z_0)$ and

*Tangency to face $OBC$* (plane $x=0$, normal $(1,0,0$)) at $G_1 = (0, b/3, c/3)

$$x_0 = \tfrac{a}{3}, \qquad y_0 = \tfrac{b}{3}.$$

Combining all three conditions:
$$S = \left(\frac{a}{3},\, \frac{b}{3},\, \frac{c}{3}\right).$$

**The contradiction.** Observe that this is exactly $G_0$, the centroid of face
$ABC$:
$$S = G_0 = \left(\frac{a}{3}, \frac{b}{3}, \frac{c}{3}\right).$$

For the sphere to be tangent to face $ABC$ at $G_0$, the point $G_0$ must lie on

$$|S - G_0| = 0 = r,$$
