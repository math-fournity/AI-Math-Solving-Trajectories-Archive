We evaluate
$$I=\int_{0}^{\infty}\!\!\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{\sqrt{uv}}{(u+v)^{2}}\,e^{-(u+v)
/2}\,du\,dv.$$

### Change of variables

Set
$$s=u+v,\qquad t=\frac{u}{u+v}\in(0,1),$$
i.e. $u=st,\;v=s(1-t)$. The Jacobian is
$$\frac{\partial(u,v)}{\partial(s,t)}=\begin{pmatrix}t&s\\ 1-t&-s\end{pmatrix},\
qquad \bigl|\det J\bigr|=|-ts-s(1-t)|=|-s|=s.$$

The quadrant $\{u>0,v>0\}$ maps bijectively onto $\{s>0,\,0<t<1\}$, so $du\,dv=s
\,ds\,dt$.

### Rewriting the integrand

$$u+v=s,\qquad uv=s^{2}t(1-t),\qquad \sqrt{uv}=s\sqrt{t(1-t)}.$$

Hence
$$\frac{\sqrt{uv}}{(u+v)^{2}}e^{-(u+v)/2}\,du\,dv
=\frac{s\sqrt{t(1-t)}}{s^{2}}\,e^{-s/2}\cdot s\,ds\,dt
=\sqrt{t(1-t)}\,e^{-s/2}\,ds\,dt.$$

The variables **separate**:
$$I=\left(\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-s/2}\,ds\right)\!\left(\int_{0}^{1}\sqrt{t(1-t)}\
,dt\right).$$

### Evaluating each factor

**First factor:**
$$\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-s/2}\,ds=\Bigl[-2e^{-s/2}\Bigr]_{0}^{\infty}=2.$$

**Second factor** — a Beta integral:
$$\int_{0}^{1}\sqrt{t(1-t)}\,dt=\int_{0}^{1}t^{1/2}(1-t)^{1/2}\,dt=B\!\left(\tfr
ac32,\tfrac32\right)=\frac{\Gamma(3/2)\,\Gamma(3/2)}{\Gamma(3)}.$$

Using $\Gamma(3/2)=\tfrac12\sqrt{\pi}$ and $\Gamma(3)=2$:
$$B\!\left(\tfrac32,\tfrac32\right)=\frac{\bigl(\tfrac12\sqrt{\pi}\bigr)^{2}}{2}
=\frac{\pi/4}{2}=\frac{\pi}{8}.$$

### Combining

$$I=2\cdot\frac{\pi}{8}=\boxed{\dfrac{\pi}{4}}.$$
