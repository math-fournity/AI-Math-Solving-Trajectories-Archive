## Proof

**Setup.** Let $T$ be the integral operator on $L^2(X)$ (with $X$ a $\sigma$-fin
ite measure space) defined by
$$(Tf)(x) = \int_X k(x,y)\,f(y)\,dy,$$
where $k \in L^2(X \times X)$ is a Hilbert–Schmidt kernel.

**Claim.** If $\|k\|_{L^2(X\times X)} < 1$, then $I - T$ is invertible on $L^2(X
)$.

---

**Step 1 — $T$ is Hilbert–Schmidt and $\|T\|_{\mathrm{op}} \le \|T\|_{\mathrm{HS
}} = \|k\|_{L^2}$.**

Let $\{e_n\}$ be any orthonormal basis of $L^2(X)$. Then $\{e_n \otimes \overlin
e{e_m}\}$ is an orthonormal basis of $L^2(X\times X)$, and
$$\|T\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 = \sum_n \|Te_n\|^2 = \sum_n \sum_m |\langle Te_n, e_m\r
angle|^2 = \sum_{n,m}\left|\iint k(x,y)\,e_n(y)\,\overline{e_m(x)}\,dx\,dy\right
|^2 = \|k\|_{L^2(X\times X)}^2,$$
the last equality being Parseval applied to $k$ in $L^2(X\times X)$. Hence $T$ i
s Hilbert–Schmidt (in particular compact and bounded), with
$$\|T\|_{\mathrm{HS}} = \|k\|_{L^2(X\times X)}.$$
For any bounded operator, $\|T\|_{\mathrm{op}} \le \|T\|_{\mathrm{HS}}$, so
$$\|T\|_{\mathrm{op}} \le \|k\|_{L^2(X\times X)}.$$

**Step 2 — The Neumann series converges when $\|T\|_{\mathrm{op}} < 1$.**

If $\|T\|_{\mathrm{op}} < 1$, the series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} T^n$ converges abs
olutely in operator norm (geometric series with ratio $\|T\|_{\mathrm{op}} < 1$)
, and its sum $S$ satisfies
$$(I-T)S = S(I-T) = I,$$
since $(I-T)\sum_{n=0}^{N}T^n = I - T^{N+1} \to I$ as $N\to\infty$ (because $\|T
^{N+1}\|\le \|T\|^{N+1}\to 0$). Thus $I-T$ is invertible with inverse $S = \sum_
{n=0}^\infty T^n$.

**Step 3 — Combining the two steps.**

By the hypothesis $\|k\|_{L^2(X\times X)} < 1$ and Step 1,
$$\|T\|_{\mathrm{op}} \le \|k\|_{L^2(X\times X)} < 1.$$
Step 2 then guarantees $I - T$ is invertible, with bounded inverse given by the
Neumann series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} T^n$.

---

**Conclusion.** A sufficient condition on the $L^2$ norm of the Hilbert–Schmidt
kernel guaranteeing invertibility of $I - T$ is
$$\boxed{\|k\|_{L^2(X\times X)} < 1.}$$

Under this condition $\|T\|_{\mathrm{op}} \le \|k\|_{L^2} < 1$, so the Neumann s
eries $\sum_{n\ge 0} T^n$ converges in operator norm and provides the bounded in
verse of $I-T$.
