## Proof

**Step 1: Unfold the recursive definition.**

By the recursive definition $S^m = S^{m-1} \wedge S^1$, we unfold iteratively:

$$S^m = S^{m-1} \wedge S^1 = (S^{m-2} \wedge S^1) \wedge S^1 = \cdots = (S^1)^{\
wedge m}$$

where $(S^1)^{\wedge m}$ is the $m$-fold smash product of $S^1$ with itself (wit
h the natural left-associative bracketing inherited from the recursion). Likewis
e:

$$S^n = (S^1)^{\wedge n}, \qquad S^{m+n} = (S^1)^{\wedge(m+n)}.$$

**Step 2: Identify $S^m \wedge S^n$ with $S^{m+n}$.**

Using the unfolded forms:

$$S^m \wedge S^n = (S^1)^{\wedge m} \wedge (S^1)^{\wedge n}.$$

The smash product is associative up to **canonical homeomorphism** (in the stand
ard category of compactly generated weak Hausdorff spaces). Therefore:

$$(S^1)^{\wedge m} \wedge (S^1)^{\wedge n} \cong (S^1)^{\wedge(m+n)} = S^{m+n}.$
$

**Step 3: Determine the degree of $\mu$.**

The "standard homotopy equivalence" $\mu: S^m \wedge S^n \to S^{m+n}$ is precise
ly this canonical associativity homeomorphism. Under the identification of both
domain and codomain with $(S^1)^{\wedge(m+n)}$, the map $\mu$ is the **identity
map**.

Since $\mu$ is a homeomorphism (the associativity isomorphism), it is in particu
lar homotopic to the identity map on $S^{m+n} \cong (S^1)^{\wedge(m+n)}$.

**Step 4: Verify there is no sign issue.**

One might worry about a sign arising from reordering smash factors. However, wit
h the recursive definition $S^m = S^{m-1} \wedge S^1$, the factors of $S^m = (S^
1)^{\wedge m}$ appear in the order $(S^1, S^1, \ldots, S^1)$ (left to right). Th
en:

$$S^m \wedge S^n = \underbrace{(S^1 \wedge \cdots \wedge S^1)}_{m} \wedge \under
brace{(S^1 \wedge \cdots \wedge S^1)}_{n} = \underbrace{(S^1 \wedge \cdots \wedg
e S^1)}_{m+n} = S^{m+n},$$

where the factor ordering is preserved — the $m$ factors from $S^m$ come first,
followed by the $n$ factors from $S^n$, giving exactly the $m+n$ factors of $S^{
m+n}$ in the correct order. No permutation of smash factors is required, so no s
ign $(-1)^{mn}$ is introduced. (The sign $(-1)^{mn}$ would arise only from the *
*twist map** $\tau: S^m \wedge S^n \to S^n \wedge S^m$, which is a different map
.)

**Conclusion.**

The standard homotopy equivalence $\mu$ is the canonical associativity homeomorp
hism, which is the identity under the recursive identification. Therefore:

$$\mu \sim 1 \cdot \mathrm{id}_{S^{m+n}},$$

and the integer is:

$$\boxed{d = 1}.$$
