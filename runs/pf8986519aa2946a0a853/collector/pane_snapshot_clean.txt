## Key Observation

A subgroup of order 4 in a group of order 36 is a Sylow 2-subgroup (since 4 = 2²
 and |G| = 2²·9). Thus, having a normal subgroup of order 4 is equivalent to hav
ing a **normal Sylow 2-subgroup**, i.e., n₂ = 1.
