## Solution

We interpret the surface integral as the **flux integral** $\iint_S \mathbf{F}\c
dot\hat{\mathbf n}\,d\sigma$, where

$$\mathbf{F}=\Big(\tfrac{2x}{\pi}+\sin(y^2)\Big)\hat{\mathbf x}+\Big(e^z-\tfrac{
y}{\pi}\Big)\hat{\mathbf y}+\Big(\tfrac{2z}{\pi}+\sin^2 y\Big)\hat{\mathbf z}$$

and $S$ is the unit sphere with outward unit normal $\hat{\mathbf n}=(x,y,z)$.

### Step 1: Compute the divergence

$$\nabla\cdot\mathbf{F}=\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\!\left(\frac{2x}{\pi}+\sin(y
^2)\right)+\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\!\left(e^z-\frac{y}{\pi}\right)+\frac{\pa
rtial}{\partial z}\!\left(\frac{2z}{\pi}+\sin^2 y\right)$$

$$=\frac{2}{\pi}-\frac{1}{\pi}+\frac{2}{\pi}=\frac{3}{\pi}.$$

### Step 2: Apply the Divergence Theorem

Since $\mathbf{F}$ is smooth on a neighborhood of the closed unit ball $V=\{(x,y
,z):x^2+y^2+z^2\le 1\}$,

$$\iint_S \mathbf{F}\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}\,d\sigma=\iiint_V \nabla\cdot\mathbf{F}
\,dV=\iiint_V \frac{3}{\pi}\,dV=\frac{3}{\pi}\cdot\operatorname{Vol}(V).$$

### Step 3: Volume of the unit ball

$$\operatorname{Vol}(V)=\frac{4}{3}\pi.$$

### Step 4: Final computation

$$\iint_S \mathbf{F}\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}\,d\sigma=\frac{3}{\pi}\cdot\frac{4\pi}{
3}=4.$$

### Direct verification (optional sanity check)

Expanding $\mathbf{F}\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}$ on $S$:

$$\mathbf{F}\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}=\frac{2x^2}{\pi}+x\sin(y^2)+ye^z-\frac{y^2}{\pi
}+\frac{2z^2}{\pi}+z\sin^2 y.$$

By symmetry on the unit sphere, the odd-function terms vanish:
- $x\sin(y^2)$ is odd in $x$ → $\iint_S =0$,
- $ye^z$ is odd in $y$ → $\iint_S =0$,
- $z\sin^2 y$ is odd in $z$ → $\iint_S =0$.

The remaining terms use $\iint_S x^2\,d\sigma=\iint_S y^2\,d\sigma=\iint_S z^2\,
d\sigma=\frac{4\pi}{3}$ (since $x^2+y^2+z^2=1$ on $S$ gives $\iint_S(x^2+y^2+z^2
)\,d\sigma=4\pi$, split equally by symmetry):

$$\iint_S\mathbf{F}\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}\,d\sigma=\frac{2}{\pi}\cdot\frac{4\pi}{3
}-\frac{1}{\pi}\cdot\frac{4\pi}{3}+\frac{2}{\pi}\cdot\frac{4\pi}{3}=\frac{8}{3}-
\frac{4}{3}+\frac{8}{3}=4.$$

Both methods agree.

$$\boxed{4}$$
