Looking at this problem, I need to determine whether uniform convergence of part
ial sums on the closed unit disk forces the radius of convergence to exceed 1.

**Answer: No.** I'll construct a counterexample where $f_n \to f$ uniformly on $
\overline{\mathbb{D}}$ but the radius of convergence is exactly $1$.

---

## Proof

Consider
$$f(z) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{z^n}{n^2},$$
i.e., $a_0 = 0$ and $a_n = 1/n^2$ for $n \geq 1$.

**Step 1: $f$ is analytic on $\mathbb{D}$ and $R = 1$.**

The radius of convergence is
$$R = \frac{1}{\limsup_{n\to\infty} |a_n|^{1/n}} = \frac{1}{\limsup_{n\to\infty}
 (1/n^2)^{1/n}} = \frac{1}{\limsup_{n\to\infty} n^{-2/n}} = \frac{1}{1} = 1,$$
since $n^{1/n} \to 1$. For $|z| > 1$, the terms $|z|^n/n^2 \to \infty$, so the s
eries diverges. Thus $R = 1$ exactly, and $f$ is analytic on $\mathbb{D}$.

**Step 2: $f_n \to f$ uniformly on $\overline{\mathbb{D}}$.**

The partial sums are $f_n(z) = \sum_{k=1}^{n} z^k/k^2$, and the tail is
$$f(z) - f_n(z) = \sum_{k=n+1}^{\infty} \frac{z^k}{k^2}.$$

For all $z$ with $|z| \leq 1$:
$$\left|\sum_{k=n+1}^{\infty} \frac{z^k}{k^2}\right| \leq \sum_{k=n+1}^{\infty}
\frac{|z|^k}{k^2} \leq \sum_{k=n+1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{k^2}.$$

Since $\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} 1/k^2 < \infty$, the tail $\sum_{k=n+1}^{\infty} 1/k^
2 \to 0$ as $n \to \infty$. Therefore
$$\sup_{|z| \leq 1} |f(z) - f_n(z)| \leq \sum_{k=n+1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{k^2} \lo
ngrightarrow 0.$$

By the Weierstrass $M$-test (with $M_k = 1/k^2$), the series converges absolutel
y and uniformly on $\overline{\mathbb{D}}$, so $f_n \to f$ uniformly on the clos
ed unit disk. In particular, $f$ extends continuously to $\overline{\mathbb{D}}$
.

**Step 3: Conclusion.**

We have exhibited a function $f$ analytic on $\mathbb{D}$, with power series $\s
um z^n/n^2$, whose partial sums converge to $f$ uniformly on $\overline{\mathbb{
D}}$, yet whose radius of convergence is $R = 1$, which is **not** strictly grea
ter than $1$.

Therefore, the answer to the question is **no**: uniform convergence of $f_n$ to
 $f$ on the closed unit disk does not force the radius of convergence to be stri
ctly greater than $1$.
