## Proof

Let $N = \overline{abc} = 100a + 10b + c$ and $M = \overline{cba} = 100c + 10b +
 a$, where $a \in \{1,\dots,9\}$, $b \in \{0,\dots,9\}$, $c \in \{1,\dots,9\}$ (
since $c \neq 0$).

**Step 1: Simplify the difference condition.**

$$N - M = (100a + 10b + c) - (100c + 10b + a) = 99(a - c).$$

The requirement $N > M$ gives $a > c$, so $a - c \in \{1, 2, \dots, 8\}$.

The requirement $36 \mid (N - M)$ becomes $36 \mid 99(a-c)$. Since $\gcd(99, 36)
 = 9$, this reduces to $4 \mid (a - c)$.

Thus $a - c \in \{4, 8\}$.

**Step 2: Decompose $36 \mid N$.**

Since $36 = 4 \times 9$ with $\gcd(4,9)=1$:
- $4 \mid N \iff 4 \mid (10b + c) \iff 2b + c \equiv 0 \pmod{4}$.
- $9 \mid N \iff a + b + c \equiv 0 \pmod{9} \iff b \equiv -(a+c) \pmod{9}$.

For each valid $(a, c)$, $b$ is uniquely determined mod 9, giving at most one ca
ndidate $b \in \{0,\dots,9\}$ (namely $b = (-(a+c)) \bmod 9$, noting $b=9$ would
 require the residue to be $0$, giving $b=0$ or $b=9$).

**Step 3: Enumerate all cases.**

*Case $a - c = 4$:* $(a,c) \in \{(5,1),(6,2),(7,3),(8,4),(9,5)\}$.

| $(a,c)$ | $a+c$ | $b \equiv -(a+c)\pmod{9}$ | $b$ | $2b+c \pmod{4}$ | Valid? |
|---------|-------|---------------------------|-----|------------------|--------
|
| $(5,1)$ | $6$ | $3$ | $3$ | $7\equiv 3$ | No |
| $(6,2)$ | $8$ | $1$ | $1$ | $4\equiv 0$ | **Yes → 612** |
| $(7,3)$ | $10$ | $8$ | $8$ | $19\equiv 3$ | No |
| $(8,4)$ | $12$ | $6$ | $6$ | $16\equiv 0$ | **Yes → 864** |
| $(9,5)$ | $14$ | $4$ | $4$ | $13\equiv 1$ | No |

*Case $a - c = 8$:* $(a,c) = (9,1)$.

| $(a,c)$ | $a+c$ | $b$ | $2b+c \pmod{4}$ | Valid? |
|---------|-------|-----|------------------|--------|
| $(9,1)$ | $10$ | $8$ | $17\equiv 1$ | No |

**Step 4: Verify the two candidates.**

- **612**: $c = 2 \neq 0$ ✓; $612 = 36 \times 17$ ✓; $612 > 216$ ✓; $612 - 216 =
 396 = 36 \times 11$ ✓.
- **864**: $c = 4 \neq 0$ ✓; $864 = 36 \times 24$ ✓; $864 > 468$ ✓; $864 - 468 =
 396 = 36 \times 11$ ✓.

Both satisfy every constraint, and the exhaustive enumeration shows no other can
didates exist.

**Conclusion.** The Level-Delta identification codes are $\boxed{612 \text{ and
} 864}$.
