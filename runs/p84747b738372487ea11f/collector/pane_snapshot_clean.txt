# Solution

## Setup

Let $X = \mathbb{P}^1$ and $\mathcal{E} = \mathcal{E}_1^\bullet \oplus \mathcal{
E}_2^\bullet = \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^1}(1) \oplus \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^1}$.
 Both summands are sheaves concentrated in degree $0$, so the endomorphism algeb
ra is

$$R = \operatorname{End}_{D^b(\mathbb{P}^1)}(\mathcal{E}) = \bigoplus_{i,j} \ope
ratorname{Hom}(\mathcal{E}_j^\bullet, \mathcal{E}_i^\bullet),$$

with multiplication given by composition. As a vector space, $R$ is a $2 \times
2$ matrix algebra with $(i,j)$-entry $R_{ij} = \operatorname{Hom}(\mathcal{E}_j,
 \mathcal{E}_i)$.

## Computing each Hom group

Since both sheaves are locally free, $\operatorname{Hom}(\mathcal{E}_j, \mathcal
{E}_i) = H^0(\mathbb{P}^1, \mathcal{E}_i \otimes \mathcal{E}_j^\vee)$.

**Diagonal entries (self-Ends):**

$$R_{11} = \operatorname{End}(\mathcal{O}(1)) = H^0(\mathcal{O}) = k, \quad \dim
 = 1.$$

$$R_{22} = \operatorname{End}(\mathcal{O}) = H^0(\mathcal{O}) = k, \quad \dim =
1.$$

**Off-diagonal entries:**

$$R_{12} = \operatorname{Hom}(\mathcal{O}, \mathcal{O}(1)) = H^0(\mathcal{O}(1))
 = k^2, \quad \dim = 2.$$

(On $\mathbb{P}^1$ with homogeneous coordinates $[x_0 : x_1]$, a basis is $\{x_0
, x_1\}$.)

$$R_{21} = \operatorname{Hom}(\mathcal{O}(1), \mathcal{O}) = H^0(\mathcal{O}(-1)
) = 0, \quad \dim = 0.$$

**Vanishing of higher Exts (exceptionality):** $\operatorname{Ext}^1(\mathcal{O}
(1),\mathcal{O}(1)) = H^1(\mathcal{O}) = 0$, $\operatorname{Ext}^1(\mathcal{O},\
mathcal{O}) = H^1(\mathcal{O}) = 0$, $\operatorname{Ext}^1(\mathcal{O},\mathcal{
O}(1)) = H^1(\mathcal{O}(1)) = 0$, and $\operatorname{Ext}^i(\mathcal{O}(1),\mat
hcal{O}) = H^i(\mathcal{O}(-1)) = 0$ for all $i$. So no higher-degree contributi
ons arise; $R$ is an ordinary (ungraded) algebra.

## Structure of $R$

As a vector space, $R$ is the triangular matrix algebra:

$$R = \begin{pmatrix} k & k^2 \\ 0 & k \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \dim_k R = 1 + 2 +
0 + 1 = \boxed{4}.$$

Let $e_1, e_2$ be the primitive idempotents projecting onto the two summands. Th
en:

- $e_1 R e_1 \cong k$, $\quad e_2 R e_2 \cong k$ (orthogonal idempotents, $e_1 +
 e_2 = 1$),
- $e_1 R e_2 \cong k^2$ (the radical),
- $e_2 R e_1 = 0$.

The **Jacobson radical** is $\operatorname{rad} R = e_1 R e_2 \cong k^2$, and th
e semisimple quotient is

$$R / \operatorname{rad} R \cong k \times k.$$

## Multiplication rule

Multiplication is composition of morphisms. Let $\{a, b\}$ be a basis of $R_{12}
 = \operatorname{Hom}(\mathcal{O}, \mathcal{O}(1))$ (corresponding to sections $
x_0, x_1$ of $\mathcal{O}(1)$). The complete multiplication table is:

| $\cdot$ | $e_1$ | $e_2$ | $a$ | $b$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $e_1$ | $e_1$ | $0$ | $a$ | $b$ |
| $e_2$ | $0$ | $e_2$ | $0$ | $0$ |
| $a$ | $0$ | $a$ | $0$ | $0$ |
| $b$ | $0$ | $b$ | $0$ | $0$ |

**Explanation:** $e_i$ act as identities on their own block. The products $e_1 \
cdot a = a$, $e_1 \cdot b = b$ (left identity from $\operatorname{End}(\mathcal{
O}(1)) = k$ acting on $\operatorname{Hom}(\mathcal{O}, \mathcal{O}(1))$ by post-
composition), and $a \cdot e_2 = a$, $b \cdot e_2 = b$ (right identity from $\op
eratorname{End}(\mathcal{O}) = k$ acting by pre-composition). All products of tw
o elements of $\operatorname{rad} R$ vanish, since $R_{12} \cdot R_{12} \subsete
q R_{12} \cdot e_2 R e_1 \cdot R_{12} = 0$ (because $e_2 R e_1 = 0$): there is n
o path of length $2$.

## Identification as a path algebra

$R$ is isomorphic to the **path algebra of the Kronecker quiver $K_2$** (two ver
tices, two parallel arrows, no relations):

- Vertices $1, 2$ correspond to idempotents $e_1, e_2$.
- Two arrows $\alpha, \beta: 2 \to 1$ correspond to the two basis elements $a, b
 \in e_1 R e_2 = \operatorname{Hom}(\mathcal{O}, \mathcal{O}(1))$.
- No paths of length $\geq 2$ exist (arrows go only $2 \to 1$, and there are no
loops), so the path algebra is finite-dimensional, equal to $k\{e_1, e_2, \alpha
, \beta\}$, of dimension $4$.

This is a basic, connected, hereditary $k$-algebra of finite representation type
 (tubular type $(2)$), and it is the **Beilinson algebra** associated to the ful
l exceptional collection $(\mathcal{O}(1), \mathcal{O})$ on $\mathbb{P}^1$.

## Summary

$$R = \operatorname{End}(\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^1}(1) \oplus \mathcal{O}_{\math
bb{P}^1}) \cong kK_2 = \begin{pmatrix} k & k^2 \\ 0 & k \end{pmatrix},$$

- **Dimension:** $4$ over $k$.
- **Structure:** Triangular matrix algebra; path algebra of the Kronecker quiver
 with $2$ arrows and no relations. Jacobson radical $\cong k^2$; semisimple quot
ient $\cong k \times k$.
- **Multiplication:** Matrix-style composition with $e_1 R e_2 = k^2\langle a, b
\rangle$ as the radical, $e_2 R e_1 = 0$, diagonal blocks $= k$, and all product
s of radical elements $= 0$ (no length-$2$ paths).
