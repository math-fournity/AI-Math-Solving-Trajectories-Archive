$$R(x) = R_{\text{right}}(x)\cdot R_{\text{left}}(x).$$

## Verification of the factorization

The factorization is valid because the two row-groups use disjoint column sets (
$\{6,7,8\}$ vs. $\{1,2,3\}$), so any rook placement on the combined board is an
independent union of placements on each block, and the rook polynomial of a disj
oint union is the product of the individual rook polynomials. Rows $V_4, V_5$ ha
ve no forbidden cells, contributing the trivial factor $1$.
