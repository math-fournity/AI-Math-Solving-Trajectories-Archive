## Solution

**Factorization.** $2021 = 43 \cdot 47$, with $43, 47$ prime.

**Structure of $A$ via CRT.** The map $n \mapsto (n \bmod 43,\; n \bmod 47)$ is
a bijection from $\{1, 2, \dots, 2021\}$ to $\{0,\dots,42\}\times\{0,\dots,46\}$
 (since $2021 \equiv 0$ modulo both). The condition $\gcd(n,2021)=1$ is equivale
nt to $n\not\equiv 0\pmod{43}$ and $n\not\equiv 0\pmod{47}$. Hence $A$ correspon
ds bijectively to pairs
$$(a,b),\quad a\in\{1,\dots,42\},\; b\in\{1,\dots,46\},$$
with $n\equiv a\pmod{43}$, $n\equiv b\pmod{47}$.

**Reduction modulo 43.** Since $n(a,b)\equiv a\pmod{43}$,
$$S(j)\equiv \sum_{a=1}^{42}\sum_{b=1}^{46} a^{j}=46\sum_{a=1}^{42}a^{j}\pmod{43
}.$$
Because $46\equiv 3\pmod{43}$ and $\gcd(3,43)=1$,
$$S(j)\equiv 0\pmod{43}\iff \sum_{a=1}^{42}a^{j}\equiv 0\pmod{43}.\tag{1}$$

**Reduction modulo 47.** Since $n(a,b)\equiv b\pmod{47}$,
$$S(j)\equiv \sum_{a=1}^{42}\sum_{b=1}^{46} b^{j}=42\sum_{b=1}^{46}b^{j}\pmod{47
}.$$
Because $\gcd(42,47)=1$,
$$S(j)\equiv 0\pmod{47}\iff \sum_{b=1}^{46}b^{j}\equiv 0\pmod{47}.\tag{2}$$

**Power-sum lemma.** For a prime $p$ and $j\ge 0$,
$$\sum_{x=1}^{p-1}x^{j}\equiv\begin{cases}-1\pmod{p}, & (p-1)\mid j,\\[2pt] 0\pm
od{p}, & (p-1)\nmid j.\end{cases}$$
*Proof.* If $(p-1)\mid j$ then $x^{j}\equiv 1$ for every $x\in\mathbb{F}_{p}^{*}
$, so the sum is $p-1\equiv -1$. If $(p-1)\nmid j$, pick a generator $g$ of $\ma
thbb{F}_{p}^{*}$; then $g^{j}\neq 1$ and
$$\sum_{x=1}^{p-1}x^{j}=\sum_{k=0}^{p-2}g^{kj}=\frac{g^{(p-1)j}-1}{g^{j}-1}=\fra
c{1-1}{g^{j}-1}=0.\qquad\square$$

**Applying the lemma.** From (1) and the lemma with $p=43$: $\sum_{a=1}^{42}a^{j
}\equiv 0\pmod{43}\iff 42\nmid j$. From (2) and the lemma with $p=47$: $\sum_{b=
1}^{46}b^{j}\equiv 0\pmod{47}\iff 46\nmid j$.

**Combining.** Since $2021=43\cdot 47$,
$$2021\mid S(j)\iff\bigl(42\nmid j\bigr)\;\text{and}\;\bigl(46\nmid j\bigr).$$

**Sanity check.** For $j=0$: $S(0)=|A|=\varphi(2021)=42\cdot 46=1932$, and $1932
/2021\notin\mathbb{Z}$; the criterion gives $42\mid 0$ and $46\mid 0$, so exclud
ed — consistent. For $j=1$: $S(1)=\tfrac{2021\cdot\varphi(2021)}{2}=2021\cdot 96
6$, divisible by $2021$; the criterion gives $42\nmid 1$, $46\nmid 1$, so includ
ed — consistent.

## Answer

The values of $j$ for which $S(j)$ is a multiple of $2021$ are precisely all non
negative integers $j$ such that $j$ is **not** divisible by $42$ **and not** div
isible by $46$; equivalently,
$$\boxed{\,j\ge 0,\quad 42\nmid j\ \text{ and }\ 46\nmid j\,}.$$
