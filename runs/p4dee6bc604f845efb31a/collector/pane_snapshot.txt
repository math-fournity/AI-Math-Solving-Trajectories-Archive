# Parseval Identity for the Wavelet Transform

## Setup and definitions

Let $\psi \in L^2(\mathbb{R})$ be an admissible mother wavelet, satisfying the *
*admissibility condition**

$$
C_\psi \;:=\; \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{|\widehat{\psi}(\omega)|^2}{|\omega|
}\,d\omega \;<\;\infty .
$$

For scale $a>0$ and translation $b\in\mathbb{R}$, define the wavelet

$$
\psi_{a,b}(t) \;=\; \frac{1}{\sqrt{a}}\,\psi\!\left(\frac{t-b}{a}\right).
$$

The (continuous) wavelet transform of $f\in L^2(\mathbb{R})$ is

$$
T_f(a,b) \;=\; \langle f,\,\psi_{a,b}\rangle \;=\; \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(t)\
,\psi_{a,b}^*(t)\,dt .
$$

We must show

$$
\int_0^\infty \frac{da}{a^2}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} db\; T_f(a,b)\,T_g^*(a,b) \;
=\; C_\psi\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(t)\,g^*(t)\,dt .
$$

## Step 1 — Fourier representation of the wavelet coefficients

Using Plancherel, $\langle f,\psi_{a,b}\rangle = \langle \widehat f, \widehat{\p
si_{a,b}}\rangle$ (up to the $2\pi$ convention; we adopt the unitary convention
$\widehat f(\omega)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int f(t)e^{-i\omega t}dt$, so Plancher
el is an isometry). A direct change of variables gives

$$
\widehat{\psi_{a,b}}(\omega) \;=\; \sqrt{a}\,e^{-i\omega b}\,\widehat{\psi}(a\om
ega).
$$

Hence

$$
T_f(a,b) \;=\; \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \widehat f(\omega)\,\sqrt{a}\,e^{+i\omega
 b}\,\widehat{\psi}^*(a\omega)\,d\omega .
$$

(The sign flips because of the conjugate: $\langle\widehat f,\widehat{\psi_{a,b}
}\rangle=\int\widehat f\,\widehat{\psi_{a,b}}^*$, and $\widehat{\psi_{a,b}}^*(\o
mega)=\sqrt a\,e^{+i\omega b}\widehat\psi^*(a\omega)$.)

Likewise

$$
T_g^*(a,b) \;=\; \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \widehat g^*(\omega')\,\sqrt{a}\,e^{-i\
omega' b}\,\widehat{\psi}(a\omega')\,d\omega' .
$$

## Step 2 — Integrate over $b$

Insert both expressions into the left-hand side:

$$
\text{LHS}=\int_0^\infty\!\frac{da}{a^2}\!\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\!db\!\iint d\o
mega\,d\omega'\;\widehat f(\omega)\widehat g^*(\omega')\,a\,e^{ib(\omega-\omega'
)}\,\widehat\psi^*(a\omega)\widehat\psi(a\omega').
$$

Perform the $b$-integration. Using the distributional identity

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{ib(\omega-\omega')}\,db \;=\; 2\pi\,\delta(\omega-\om
ega'),
$$

we obtain

$$
\text{LHS}=\int_0^\infty\!\frac{da}{a}\!\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\!d\omega\;\wideh
at f(\omega)\,\widehat g^*(\omega)\,|\widehat\psi(a\omega)|^2\cdot 2\pi\cdot\tfr
ac{1}{2\pi},
$$

where the $2\pi$ from the delta and the $1/2\pi$ from the unitary Fourier conven
tion cancel. Precisely, with the unitary convention $\int e^{ib(\omega-\omega')}
db=2\pi\delta(\omega-\omega')$ and Plancherel being an isometry, the factors com
bine to give

$$
\text{LHS}=\int_0^\infty\!\frac{da}{a}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} d\omega\;\widehat
f(\omega)\,\widehat g^*(\omega)\,|\widehat\psi(a\omega)|^2 .
$$

## Step 3 — Change variables $u=a\omega$

For each fixed $\omega\neq 0$, set $u=a\omega$, so $da/a=du/u$. Splitting the $\
omega$-integral into $\omega>0$ and $\omega<0$:

$$
\int_0^\infty\frac{da}{a}\,|\widehat\psi(a\omega)|^2
=\int_{\operatorname{sgn}(\omega)\cdot 0}^{\operatorname{sgn}(\omega)\cdot\infty
}\frac{du}{|u|}\,|\widehat\psi(u)|^2
=\int_0^\infty\frac{|\widehat\psi(u)|^2}{u}\,du\quad(\omega>0),
$$

and similarly for $\omega<0$ the substitution $u=a\omega<0$ gives $\int_{-\infty
}^{0}\frac{|\widehat\psi(u)|^2}{|u|}du$. Therefore, for every $\omega\neq 0$,

$$
\int_0^\infty\frac{da}{a}\,|\widehat\psi(a\omega)|^2 \;=\; \int_{-\infty}^{\inft
y}\frac{|\widehat\psi(u)|^2}{|u|}\,du \;=\; C_\psi,
$$

which is precisely the **admissibility constant**, independent of $\omega$. (The
 value at $\omega=0$ is irrelevant since it is a single point of measure zero, a
nd admissibility forces $\widehat\psi(0)=0$.)

## Step 4 — Conclude

Substituting back,

$$
\text{LHS}=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} d\omega\;\widehat f(\omega)\,\widehat g^*(\om
ega)\;\cdot C_\psi .
$$

By Plancherel's theorem (unitary convention),

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\widehat f(\omega)\,\widehat g^*(\omega)\,d\omega \;=\; \
int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(t)\,g^*(t)\,dt .
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{\;\int_0^\infty \frac{da}{a^2}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} db\; T_f(a,b)\,T_g^
*(a,b) \;=\; C_\psi\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(t)\,g^*(t)\,dt\;}
$$

which is the desired Parseval (Moyal) identity for the continuous wavelet transf
orm. $\blacksquare$
