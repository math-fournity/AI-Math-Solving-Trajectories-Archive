## Evaluation of $\displaystyle I = \int_0^\infty \frac{x^2}{e^x-1}\,dx$ via the
 Abel–Plana formula

### 1. The Abel–Plana formula

For a function $f$ analytic in $\Re x \ge 0$ with suitable decay,

$$
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} f(n) \;=\; \int_0^\infty f(x)\,dx \;+\; \frac{f(0)}{2} \;+\;
 i\int_0^\infty \frac{f(it)-f(-it)}{e^{2\pi t}-1}\,dt .
\qquad (\ast)
$$

### 2. Apply Abel–Plana to $f(x)=(x+1)^{-3}$

This $f$ is analytic in $\Re x\ge 0$ (its only pole is at $x=-1$) and decays lik
e $|x|^{-3}$, so $(\ast)$ applies.

- **Sum side:** $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(n+1)^3}=\zeta(3).$
- **Integral term:** $\displaystyle\int_0^\infty \frac{dx}{(x+1)^3}=\Bigl[-\tfra
c12(x+1)^{-2}\Bigr]_0^\infty=\tfrac12.$
- **Boundary term:** $f(0)/2 = \tfrac12.$

For the correction term, set $w=1+it$, $\bar w=1-it$, $|w|^2=1+t^2$. Then

$$
f(it)-f(-it)=\frac{1}{(1+it)^3}-\frac{1}{(1-it)^3}
=\frac{\bar w^{\,3}-w^3}{|w|^6}.
$$

Since $\bar w^{\,3}-w^3=(1-it)^3-(1+it)^3=-2it(3-t^2)$ and $|w|^6=(1+t^2)^3$,

$$
f(it)-f(-it)=\frac{-2it(3-t^2)}{(1+t^2)^3},
\qquad
i\bigl(f(it)-f(-it)\bigr)=\frac{2t(3-t^2)}{(1+t^2)^3}.
$$

Substituting into $(\ast)$:

$$
\zeta(3)=\tfrac12+\tfrac12+\int_0^\infty\frac{2t(3-t^2)}{(1+t^2)^3\,(e^{2\pi t}-
1)}\,dt,
$$

i.e.

$$
\boxed{\;\zeta(3)=1+2\int_0^\infty\frac{t(3-t^2)}{(1+t^2)^3\,(e^{2\pi t}-1)}\,dt
\;}. \qquad(\dagger)
$$

### 3. Relate the target integral to $\zeta(3)$

For $x>0$ the geometric series gives $\displaystyle\frac{1}{e^x-1}=\sum_{n=1}^{\
infty}e^{-nx}$. Since every summand $x^2 e^{-nx}$ is non-negative, Tonelli/monot
one convergence justifies termwise integration:

$$
I=\int_0^\infty x^2\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}e^{-nx}\,dx
 =\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\int_0^\infty x^2 e^{-nx}\,dx
 =\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{2}{n^3}
 =2\zeta(3),
$$

using $\int_0^\infty x^2 e^{-nx}dx = \Gamma(3)/n^3 = 2/n^3$. (The series $\sum 2
/n^3$ converges, confirming $I<\infty$.)

### 4. Conclusion

Combining Section 3 with the Abel–Plana representation $(\dagger)$ of Section 2,

$$
\int_0^\infty \frac{x^2}{e^x-1}\,dx \;=\; 2\zeta(3)
 \;=\; 2 \;+\; 4\int_0^\infty\frac{t(3-t^2)}{(1+t^2)^3\,(e^{2\pi t}-1)}\,dt .
$$

The integral evaluates to

$$
\boxed{\displaystyle\int_0^\infty \frac{x^2}{e^x-1}\,dx = 2\,\zeta(3)}.
$$

(Here $\zeta(3)\approx 1.2020569$ is Apéry's constant, so numerically $I\approx
2.4041138$; the Abel–Plana correction term in $(\dagger)$ is the rapidly converg
ent integral, since $e^{2\pi t}-1$ makes the integrand decay exponentially.)
