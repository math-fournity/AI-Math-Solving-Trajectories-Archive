# Problem

In a remote circular ecological preserve, two straight observation walkways intersect at a central research hub. The first walkway, running West-to-East, is divided by the hub into two segments of lengths 8 kilometers and 2 kilometers. The second walkway, running North-to-South, has a total length of exactly 10 kilometers and intersects the first walkway at a perfect $90^\circ$ angle.

A conservation team plans to build a rectangular solar farm within the circular boundary of the preserve. To avoid interfering with the existing infrastructure and to preserve the natural habitat around the paths, the solar farm must be positioned such that its sides are parallel to the two walkways, and no part of its interior overlaps with the walkways or extends beyond the preserve's circular perimeter.

What is the maximum possible area of this rectangular solar farm?
