## Proof

Let time be measured in seconds after midnight.

The yellow signal is emitted at times:
$$t_Y = 2 + 15k, \quad k = 0, 1, 2, \dots$$

The red signal is emitted at times:
$$t_R = 8 + 28m, \quad m = 0, 1, 2, \dots$$

We seek the smallest $t \ge 0$ belonging to both sequences, i.e. the smallest no
n-negative integers $k, m$ satisfying:
$$2 + 15k = 8 + 28m$$
$$15k - 28m = 6$$

**Solving the linear congruence.** Reducing modulo 28:
$$15k \equiv 6 \pmod{28}$$

Since $\gcd(15, 28) = 1$, the inverse of $15$ modulo $28$ exists. Testing: $15 \
cdot 15 = 225 = 8 \cdot 28 + 1$, so $15^{-1} \equiv 15 \pmod{28}$.

Therefore:
$$k \equiv 15 \cdot 6 \equiv 90 \equiv 90 - 3\cdot 28 \equiv 6 \pmod{28}$$

The smallest non-negative solution is $k = 6$. Then:
$$m = \frac{15k - 6}{28} = \frac{15 \cdot 6 - 6}{28} = \frac{84}{28} = 3$$

Both $k = 6$ and $m = 3$ are non-negative integers, so this is a valid emission
time.

**Verification.**
- Yellow: $2 + 15 \cdot 6 = 2 + 90 = 92$ seconds after midnight.
- Red: $8 + 28 \cdot 3 = 8 + 84 = 92$ seconds after midnight.

Both equal 92. ✓

**Minimality.** The solutions form the family $k = 6 + 28j$, $m = 3 + 15j$ for $
j \in \mathbb{Z}$. The next smaller solution would require $j = -1$, giving $k =
 -2 < 0$, which is not a valid (non-negative) emission index. Hence $k = 6$, $m
= 3$ gives the earliest simultaneous emission.

**Answer:** Both signals are emitted together for the first time at **92 seconds
 after midnight** (i.e., 1 minute and 32 seconds after midnight, 00:01:32).
