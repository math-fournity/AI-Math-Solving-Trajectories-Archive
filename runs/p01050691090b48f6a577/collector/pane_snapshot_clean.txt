## Setting Up the Structure

Let $p_i = x_i x_{i+1}$ for $i=1,\ldots,7$. I need $p_1 \le p_2 \le \cdots \le p
_7$.

**Key observation on signs:** Since all values are distinct, $p_i = p_{i+1}$ onl
y when $x_{i+1}=0$ (both products are 0). Otherwise consecutive products are str
ictly ordered.

The non-decreasing constraint forces a **block structure**: negative products →
zero products → positive products. (Once a positive product appears, nothing sma
ller can follow; before a negative product, nothing larger can precede it.)

**Locating 0:** If $0$ is at position $k$, the products $p_{k-1}$ and $p_k$ (whe
n they exist) are both 0, forming the zero block. This splits the sequence into:
- **Before part** (positions $1,\ldots,k-1$): all products must be $\le 0$, henc
e **all negative** (no 0 here), meaning **every adjacent pair is a sign change**
 → the before part **alternates signs**.
- **After part** (positions $k+1,\ldots,8$): all products must be $\ge 0$, hence
 **all positive** → **all same sign**.
