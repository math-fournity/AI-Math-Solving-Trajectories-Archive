## Proof

**Claim:** Yes. Finite-rank operators are dense in $B(H)$ in the weak operator t
opology (WOT).

**Step 1: Finite-rank operators are dense in $B(H)$ in the strong operator topol
ogy (SOT).**

Let $H$ be a Hilbert space. If $H$ is finite-dimensional, then every operator is
 finite rank and the claim is trivial. Assume $\dim H = \infty$ and choose an or
thonormal basis $\{e_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$. For each $n$, let $P_n$ be the orthogo
nal projection onto $\operatorname{span}\{e_1, \dots, e_n\}$. Each $P_n$ has fin
ite rank, and $P_n \to I$ strongly, i.e., $\|P_n x - x\| \to 0$ for every $x \in
 H$ (this is Parseval's identity: $\|x\|^2 = \sum |\langle x, e_k\rangle|^2$, so
 the tail vanishes).

For any $T \in B(H)$, consider $T_n := P_n T$. Since $\operatorname{ran}(T_n) \s
ubseteq \operatorname{ran}(P_n)$, each $T_n$ has finite rank. Moreover, for ever
y $x \in H$:
$$\|T_n x - T x\| = \|(P_n - I)(Tx)\| = \|P_n(Tx) - Tx\| \to 0,$$
since $P_n \to I$ strongly. Thus $T_n \to T$ in SOT. Hence finite-rank operators
 are SOT-dense in $B(H)$.

**Step 2: SOT-density implies WOT-density.**

The WOT is the weakest topology on $B(H)$ making all maps $T \mapsto \langle Tx,
 y\rangle$ ($x, y \in H$) continuous. The SOT is the topology of pointwise norm-
convergence, generated by seminorms $T \mapsto \|Tx\|$. Since convergence $T_n \
to T$ in SOT (i.e., $\|T_n x - Tx\| \to 0$ for all $x$) implies $|\langle T_n x,
 y\rangle - \langle Tx, y\rangle| \leq \|(T_n - T)x\|\|y\| \to 0$, every WOT-con
tinuous functional is SOT-continuous. Equivalently, $\text{SOT} \supseteq \text{
WOT}$: SOT is stronger (finer) than WOT.

Now let $F$ be the set of finite-rank operators. The closure of $F$ in any topol
ogy $\tau$ is the intersection of all $\tau$-closed sets containing $F$. Since S
OT is finer than WOT, every WOT-closed set is SOT-closed (a set closed in a coar
ser topology is closed in a finer topology). Therefore:
$$\overline{F}^{\,\text{WOT}} \supseteq \overline{F}^{\,\text{SOT}}.$$
(If a set is SOT-closed and contains $F$, it is also WOT-closed and contains $F$
; so the WOT-closure, being the intersection over a larger family, is larger.) B
y Step 1, $\overline{F}^{\,\text{SOT}} = B(H)$, so:
$$\overline{F}^{\,\text{WOT}} = B(H).$$

**Conclusion:** Finite-rank operators are dense in $B(H)$ with respect to the we
ak operator topology.
