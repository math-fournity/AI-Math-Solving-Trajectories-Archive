Looking at this problem, I need to find the minimum points P that guarantees a t
eam finishes in the top 4 (semifinal berth) of a 9-team tournament.

**Setup:**
- 9 teams, each pair plays twice → each team plays 16 matches → max 32 points pe
r team.
- Total matches = C(9,2)×2 = 72, total points distributed = 144 (no draws).
- All goal differences are distinct → complete ranking with no ties.
- "Guarantee top 4" means: at most 3 other teams can have ≥ P points (since a te
am tied at P could beat us on goal difference).

**Key question:** Find the minimum even P such that it is **impossible** for 4 o
ther teams to each have ≥ P points.

---

**Upper bound check (P = 24 does NOT suffice):**

I construct a tournament where 5 teams all have 24 points (12 wins each).

Partition teams into: our team T, group A = {A1,A2,A3,A4}, group B = {B1,B2,B3,B
4}.

- **T vs A:** T beats each Ai once, loses once → T gets 4 wins, each Ai gets 1 w
in vs T.
- **T vs B:** T beats each Bi twice → T gets 8 wins.
- **A vs B:** Each Ai beats every Bi twice → each Ai gets 8 wins, each Bi gets 0
.
- **Within A:** Each pair splits 1-1 → each Ai gets 3 wins in 6 matches.
- **Within B:** Each pair splits 1-1 → each Bi gets 3 wins in 6 matches.

**Verification:**
- T: 4 + 8 = 12 wins = 24 points ✓
- Each Ai: 1 + 8 + 3 = 12 wins = 24 points ✓
- Each Bi: 0 + 0 + 3 = 3 wins = 6 points ✓
- Total wins: 12 + 48 + 12 = 72 ✓

Five teams are tied at 24 points. With distinct goal differences (achievable by
varying score margins), T could be ranked **5th**. So 24 points does **not** gua
rantee a semifinal berth.

---

**P = 26 guarantees top 4:**

Suppose our team T has 26 points (13 wins). I show 4 other teams **cannot** all
reach ≥ 13 wins.

Let A be any 4 other teams, B the remaining 4. Let:
- a = T's wins vs A (0 ≤ a ≤ 8), b = T's wins vs B, so a + b = 13, b ≤ 8 → a ≥ 5
.
- c = A's wins vs B (0 ≤ c ≤ 32).

**Total wins by A** = (wins vs T) + (wins within A) + (wins vs B) = (8 − a) + 12
 + c = 20 − a + c.

To maximize A's wins: set c = 32 (A sweeps B), giving max A wins = 52 − a ≤ 52 −
 5 = **47**.

For 4 A-teams to each have ≥ 13 wins: need total ≥ 52. But 47 < 52. **Impossible
.**

Therefore, at most 3 other teams can have ≥ 26 points. Even if all 3 are ranked
above T (via points or goal difference), T is ranked **4th at worst** → guarante
ed semifinal berth. ✓

---

**Conclusion:**

- 24 points: fails (counterexample with 5 teams at 24 points).
- 26 points: succeeds (4 other teams cannot all reach 26 points).

A team needs **26 points** to guarantee a semifinal berth.
