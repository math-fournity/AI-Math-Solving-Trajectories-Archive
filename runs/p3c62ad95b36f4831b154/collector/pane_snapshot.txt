Let $p_k = P(X=k)$ for $k=0,1,2,\dots$ We want to minimize $p_0$.

**Setting up the conditional distribution.** Let $q = P(X\geq 1) = 1-p_0$, and l

$$E[Y]=\frac{1}{q},\quad E[Y^2]=\frac{2}{q},\quad E[Y^3]=\frac{5}{q}.$$

**Key inequality.** Since $Y$ takes positive integer values, $Y-1\geq 0$. Theref

$$E\big[(Y-1)(Y-c)^2\big]\geq 0.$$
Expanding:
$$E[(Y-1)(Y-c)^2]=E[Y^3]-(2c+1)E[Y^2]+(c^2+2c)E[Y]-c^2.$$
Substituting the moments:
$$=\frac{5-2(2c+1)+(c^2+2c)}{q}-c^2=\frac{c^2-2c+3}{q}-c^2.$$
So we need $\dfrac{c^2-2c+3}{q}\geq c^2$, i.e.,
$$q\leq \frac{c^2-2c+3}{c^2}=1-\frac{2}{c}+\frac{3}{c^2}.$$

**Optimizing over $c$.** Let $f(c)=1-\frac{2}{c}+\frac{3}{c^2}$. Then $f'(c)=\fr

(3)=1-\frac{2}{3}+\frac{1}{3}=\frac{2}{3}$.

Therefore $q\leq \frac{2}{3}$, which gives $p_0 = 1-q \geq \frac{1}{3}$.
