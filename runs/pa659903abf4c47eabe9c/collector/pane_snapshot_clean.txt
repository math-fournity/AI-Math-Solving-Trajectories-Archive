## General Verification for Right Triangles

For a right triangle with legs $a \leq b$, one can show:
- $l = \frac{ab}{a+b}$ (from the legs)
- $L = \frac{b^2}{\sqrt{b^2 + (b-a)^2}}$

Then:
$$\frac{L}{l} = \frac{b(a+b)}{a\sqrt{b^2+(b-a)^2}}$$

Setting this equal to 2 and solving yields $a = b$, confirming that **only the r
ight isosceles triangle** achieves $L/l = 2$ among right triangles. This is cons
istent with all three computed values.
