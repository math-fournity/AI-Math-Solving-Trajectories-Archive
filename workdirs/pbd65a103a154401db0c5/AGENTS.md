# Problem

An architect is designing a four-sided courtyard, defined by four corner pillars: Alpha ($A$), Bravo ($B$), Charlie ($C$), and Delta ($D$), connected by straight perimeter walls. The layout is precisely planned such that the wall at pillar Bravo meets its adjacent walls at a perfect $90^\circ$ right angle. Directly across the courtyard, the wall at pillar Delta is shaped such that the interior angle at pillar Alpha is $60^\circ$ and the interior angle at pillar Charlie is $120^\circ$.

Two straight stone walkways are laid diagonally across the courtyard, one connecting Alpha to Charlie and the other connecting Bravo to Delta. These walkways intersect at a central fountain ($M$). The distance from pillar Bravo to the fountain is exactly 1 decameter, while the distance from the fountain to pillar Delta is exactly 2 decameters.

Calculate the total area of the courtyard in square decameters.
