## Solution

We model the 27 ingredients as points in $\{0,1,2\}^3$ (Origin, Flavor, Preparat
ion). Two ingredients "share an attribute" = agree on a coordinate. A **Balanced
 Trio** is an unordered triple $\{a,b,c\}$ where every pair differs in **at leas
t 2** coordinates.

### Total triples
$$\binom{27}{3} = 2925.$$

### Counting "bad" triples (some pair agrees in ≥2 coordinates)

A pair agrees in ≥2 coords iff it differs in **exactly 1** coordinate (distinct
points can't agree in all 3). Call such a pair a *bad pair*.

**Number of bad pairs:** pick the differing coordinate (3), fix the other two ($
3\cdot3=9$), pick 2 of 3 values for the differing coordinate ($\binom{3}{2}=3$):
$$3\cdot 9\cdot 3 = 81.$$

For a fixed bad pair $\{a,b\}$ (differing only in coordinate 1, so $a,b$ agree i
n coords 2,3), write $a=(a_1,a_2,a_3)$, $b=(b_1,a_2,a_3)$. For a third point $c=
(c_1,c_2,c_3)$, let $d_i$ indicate $c$ differs from $a$ in coord $i$. Classify a
ll 25 choices of $c\neq a,b$ by how many bad pairs the triple contains:

- **$c_1=a_1$:** $d_2=d_3=1$ (4 c's) → only $\{a,b\}$ bad = **1 bad pair**. $d_2
=1,d_3=0$ or $d_2=0,d_3=1$ (4 c's) → $\{a,c\}$ also bad = **2 bad pairs**.
- **$c_1=b_1$:** symmetric → 4 c's give **1 bad pair**, 4 c's give **2 bad pairs
**.
- **$c_1=\text{third value}$:** $d_2=d_3=0$ (1 c) → $\{a,c\},\{b,c\}$ both diffe
r in exactly 1 coord = **3 bad pairs** (the three points form a "line"). The oth
er 8 c's → only $\{a,b\}$ bad = **1 bad pair**.

Tally per bad pair: **16** c's → 1 bad pair, **8** c's → 2 bad pairs, **1** c →
3 bad pairs. (Check: $16+8+1=25$ ✓.)

### Triple counts (dividing by the number of bad pairs per triple)

$$N_1 = 81\cdot 16 = 1296,\qquad N_2 = \frac{81\cdot 8}{2}=324,\qquad N_3=\frac{
81\cdot 1}{3}=27.$$

**Incidence check:** $N_1+2N_2+3N_3 = 1296+648+81 = 2025 = 81\cdot 25$ ✓.

### Good triples
$$2925 - 1296 - 324 - 27 = \boxed{1278}.$$

### Independent verification (direct count)

Pick $a$ (27 ways). Pick $b$ differing from $a$ in ≥2 coords: $26 - 6 = 20$ choi
ces (6 points differ in exactly 1 coord). Split:
- $b$ differs in **exactly 2** coords: $3\cdot 2\cdot 2 = 12$ choices. For each,
 valid $c$ (differs from both in ≥2): **15**.
- $b$ differs in **3** coords: $2^3=8$ choices. For each, valid $c$: **13** (enu
merating $c_i\in\{a_i,b_i,\text{third}\}$ with at most one match to $a$ and at m
ost one to $b$ gives 13).

Each good triple is counted $3\cdot 2=6$ times:
$$\frac{27\,(12\cdot 15 + 8\cdot 13)}{6} = \frac{27\cdot 284}{6} = 9\cdot 284 =
\boxed{1278}.$$

Both methods agree.
