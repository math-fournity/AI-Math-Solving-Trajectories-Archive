Set $s = b+c$ and $d = c-b$ (with $d \neq 0$ since the triangle is scalene).

$$O = \left(\frac{s}{2\sqrt{3}},\; \frac{d}{2}\right).$$

$$H = \left(\frac{s}{2\sqrt{3}},\; -\frac{d}{2}\right).$$

**Crucial observation:** $O$ and $H$ are reflections of each other across the $x
