Looking at this problem, I need to find the maximum n such that n rooks, n bisho
ps, and n knights can be placed on an 8×8 board with no piece attacking any othe
r.

## Proof

**Key observation (upper bound):** Since no two rooks may share a row or column,
 n rooks occupy n distinct rows and n distinct columns. Any other piece (bishop
or knight) in a rook's row or column would be attacked by that rook. Therefore,
all 2n non-rook pieces must lie in the (8−n) × (8−n) sub-board formed by the row
s and columns not used by any rook.

This requires 2n ≤ (8−n)².

For n = 5: (8−5)² = 9 < 10 = 2n. **Impossible.**
For n = 4: (8−4)² = 16 ≥ 8 = 2n. Feasible by counting.

So n ≤ 4.

**Construction for n = 4:**

Place rooks at **(1,1), (2,2), (3,3), (4,4)**. The sub-board is rows 5–8, column
s 5–8 (a 4×4 board).

Place bishops at **(5,6), (6,8), (7,5), (8,7)** and knights at **(5,5), (5,8), (
8,5), (8,8)**.

**Verification of all attack constraints:**

*Rook attacks (same row/column):* Rooks occupy rows {1,2,3,4} and columns {1,2,3
,4}. All bishops and knights are in rows {5,6,7,8} and columns {5,6,7,8}. No non
-rook piece shares a row or column with any rook. Rooks themselves are in distin
ct rows and columns. ✓

*Bishop attacks (same diagonal):* The "/" diagonals (row+col) and "\" diagonals
(row−col) of the four bishops:
- (5,6): sum 11, diff −1
- (6,8): sum 14, diff −2
- (7,5): sum 12, diff 2
- (8,7): sum 15, diff 1

All four sums {11,14,12,15} are distinct and all four diffs {−1,−2,2,1} are dist
inct. No two bishops share a diagonal. Checking against all other pieces: the ro
oks have sums {2,4,6,8} and diff 0; the knights have sums {10,13,13,16} and diff
s {0,−3,3,0}. No bishop shares a sum or diff with any rook or knight. ✓

*Knight attacks (knight's move):* The four knights are at (5,5), (5,8), (8,5), (
8,8). Checking all knight-move targets:
- (5,5) → {(3,4),(3,6),(4,3),(4,7),(6,3),(6,7),(7,4),(7,6)} — none occupied.
- (5,8) → {(3,7),(4,6),(6,6),(7,7)} (in-bounds) — none occupied.
- (8,5) → {(6,4),(6,6),(7,3),(7,7)} (in-bounds) — none occupied.
- (8,8) → {(6,7),(7,6)} (in-bounds) — none occupied.

No knight attacks any other piece. ✓

**Conclusion:** n = 4 is achievable and n ≥ 5 is impossible. The largest possibl
e value of n is **4**.
