## Solution

**Setup.** Place the square in coordinates: $A=(0,0)$, $B=(1,0)$, $C=(1,1)$, $D=
(0,1)$. Then:
- $K = (k, 0)$ on $AB$, with $0 \le k \le 1$,
- $M = (m, 1)$ on $CD$, with $0 \le m \le 1$,
- $L = (t, t)$ on diagonal $AC$, with $0 \le t \le 1$.

**The condition $ML = KL$.** Computing squared distances:

$$ML^2 = (m-t)^2 + (1-t)^2, \qquad KL^2 = (k-t)^2 + t^2.$$

Setting them equal and expanding:

$$m^2 - 2mt + t^2 + 1 - 2t + t^2 = k^2 - 2kt + t^2 + t^2,$$

$$m^2 - k^2 - 2t(m - k) + 1 - 2t = 0.$$

Setting $q = m - k$, this factors as:

$$(m-k)(m+k-2t) + (1-2t) = 0 \implies q(2k + q - 2t) + (1-2t) = 0. \tag{$\star$}
$$

Equivalently: $\;2kq + q^2 - 2qt = 2t - 1$.

**Finding point $P$.** The line $MK$ is parametrized as $(k + u(m-k),\, u)$ and
line $BD$ as $(1-s,\, s)$. At their intersection, $u = s$ and $k + s(m-k) = 1-s$
, giving:

$$s = \frac{1-k}{1+m-k} = \frac{1-k}{1+q}.$$

So $P = \left(\frac{m}{1+q},\; \frac{1-k}{1+q}\right)$.

**Vectors $\overrightarrow{PK}$ and $\overrightarrow{PL}$.** A direct computatio
n gives:

$$\overrightarrow{PK} = \frac{1-k}{1+q}\,(k - m,\; -1) = \frac{1-k}{1+q}\,(-q,\;
 -1).$$

$$\overrightarrow{PL} = \frac{1}{1+q}\Big((t-k) - q(1-t),\;\; (t+k-1) + tq\Big).
$$

Since the positive scalar prefactors do not affect the angle, we compute the ang
le between:
$$\mathbf{u} = (-q,\; -1), \qquad \mathbf{v} = \big((t-k) - q(1-t),\;\; (t+k-1)
+ tq\big).$$

**Cross product (z-component):**

$$\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v} = (-q)\big[(t+k-1)+tq\big] - (-1)\big[(t-k)-q(1-t
)\big]$$
$$= -qt - qk + q - tq^2 + t - k - q + qt = t - tq^2 - k - kq = (1+q)\big(t(1-q)
- k\big).$$

**Dot product:**

$$\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = (-q)\big[(t-k)-q(1-t)\big] + (-1)\big[(t+k-1)+tq
\big]$$
$$= -qt + qk + q^2(1-t) - t - k + 1 - tq = q^2(1-t) + k(q-1) - 2qt + (1-t).$$

**Key step — showing cross equals dot.** Compute the difference:

$$\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v} - \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = \big(t - tq^2 - k
 - kq\big) - \big(q^2 + 1 - tq^2 - t + kq - k - 2qt\big)$$
$$= 2t + 2qt - 2kq - q^2 - 1 = 2t(1+q) - (2kq + q^2) - 1.$$

By condition $(\star)$: $\;2kq + q^2 = 2t - 1 + 2qt = 2t(1+q) - 1$. Substituting
:

$$\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v} - \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = 2t(1+q) - \big(2t
(1+q)-1\big) - 1 = 0.$$

Therefore $\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v} = \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}$, which gi
ves:

$$\tan(\angle KPL) = \frac{|\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v}|}{\mathbf{u} \cdot \mat
hbf{v}} = 1.$$

Since $P$ is the intersection of the segments $MK$ and $BD$ (interior point) and
 $L$ lies on the diagonal, the angle is acute. Hence:

$$\boxed{\angle KPL = 45^\circ}.$$
