$$\operatorname{Res}(f, i) = \frac{-\pi^2/4}{2i} = \frac{i\pi^2}{8}.$$

- At $z = -i$: $\arg(-i) = 3\pi/2$, so $\log(-i) = 3\pi i/2$ and $(\log(-i))^2 =

$$\operatorname{Res}(f, -i) = \frac{-9\pi^2/4}{-2i} = -\frac{9i\pi^2}{8}.$$

Summing:
$$\sum \operatorname{Res} = \frac{i\pi^2}{8} - \frac{9i\pi^2}{8} = -i\pi^2.$$

$$\oint_\Gamma f(z)\,dz = 2\pi i\cdot(-i\pi^2) = 2\pi^3.$$
