## Proof

gcup_{k=1}^{\infty} E_k$.

**Step 2: Bound $|E_k|$ by $|F_k|$.** Since $E_k = \bigcap_{j \geq k} F_j \subse
teq F_k$, by monotonicity of Lebesgue measure,
$$|E_k| \leq |F_k|.$$

**Step 3: $|F_k| \to 0$.** The hypothesis $\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |F_k| < \infty$ i

$$|F_k| \to 0 \quad \text{as } k \to \infty.$$

Combining Steps 2 and 3:
$$0 \leq |E_k| \leq |F_k| \to 0, \quad \text{so } |E_k| \to 0.$$

**Step 4: Apply continuity of measure from below.** Since $(E_k)$ is an increasi

$$\left|\bigcup_{k=1}^{\infty} E_k\right| = \lim_{k\to\infty} |E_k| = 0.$$

Therefore
$$\left|\liminf_{k\to\infty} F_k\right| = \left|\bigcup_{k=1}^{\infty}\bigcap_{j
