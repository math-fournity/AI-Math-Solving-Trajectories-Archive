This is the **Balanced Prime conjecture**, a well-known open problem in number t
heory (tagged `@[category research open, AMS 11]` in the formalization). It asks
 whether there are infinitely many primes $p_n$ such that $p_n = (p_{n-1} + p_{n
+1})/2$, i.e. infinitely many primes that are the arithmetic mean of their neare
st neighboring primes.

This problem is open. No proof or disproof is known. The first few balanced prim
es are 5, 53, 157, 173, 211, 257, 263, 373, 563, 593, 607, 653, 733, 947, 977, 1
103, 1123, 1187, 1223, 1367, ... (OEIS A006562), and they appear to occur with p
ositive density among primes, but no one has proved infinitude.

The second statement (`balanced_primes_order`) is a higher-order generalization
(balanced primes of order $k$), which is also open for every $k \geq 1$.
