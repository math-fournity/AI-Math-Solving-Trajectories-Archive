# Problem

1.  **Minimum Capacity:** Every container used must hold a load strictly greater than 1 ton.
2.  **Chemical Isolation:** To avoid cross-contamination, the weights of the minerals in any two containers (measured in integer tons) must be pairwise coprime—meaning no two containers can have a common divisor greater than 1.
