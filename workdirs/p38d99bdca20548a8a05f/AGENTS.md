# Problem

In a specialized laboratory, a deep-sea submersible is positioned at a depth exactly 2 units above the crushing pressure limit of the seafloor. An automated winch system is programmed to execute exactly 10 mechanical cable adjustments. There are 10 instruction chips available in a bin: 5 "Descend" chips and 5 "Ascend" chips.

Each time a "Descend" chip is processed, the submersible moves 1 unit deeper toward the seafloor limit. Each time an "Ascend" chip is processed, the submersible moves 1 unit upward, away from the limit. The mission is compromised if the submersible’s depth ever exceeds the 2-unit safety margin (i.e., if it moves more than 2 units deeper than its starting position at any point during the sequence of 10 adjustments).

In how many distinct sequences can the 10 chips be processed such that the submersible never hits the seafloor limit?
