## Setting Up the Antichain Condition

Let $T_i \subseteq [7]$ be the set of concerts in which $M_i$ plays, so $t_i = |
T_i|$ and $t = \sum |T_i|$.

The condition "each musician listens to all others" means: for all $i \neq j$, t
here exists a concert $c$ where $M_j$ plays and $M_i$ does not, i.e., $c \in T_j
 \setminus T_i$. This is equivalent to:

$$T_j \not\subseteq T_i \quad \text{for all } i \neq j.$$

Since this holds for all ordered pairs, the sets $T_1, \ldots, T_{12}$ must be *
*pairwise incomparable under inclusion** — they form an **antichain** in $2^{[7]
}$. (In particular, all are distinct, and none is $\emptyset$, since $\emptyset
\subseteq T_j$ for all $j$.)

So the problem reduces to: **find 12 distinct nonempty subsets of $[7]$ forming
an antichain, minimizing total size.**

## Construction Achieving $t = 22$

Take:
- $T_1 = \{1\}$, $T_2 = \{2\}$ (two singletons), and
- $T_3, \ldots, T_{12}$ = all ten 2-element subsets of $\{3,4,5,6,7\}$.

**Verification of antichain property:**
- $\{1\}$ vs $\{2\}$: incomparable ✓
- $\{1\}$ vs any 2-subset of $\{3,...,7\}$: $\{1\} \not\subseteq S$ (since $1 \n
otin S$) and $S \not\subseteq \{1\}$ ✓ (similarly for $\{2\}$)
- Distinct 2-subsets: always incomparable ✓

Total: $t = 1 + 1 + 10 \times 2 = \mathbf{22}$.

## Lower Bound: $t \geq 22$

Let $s$ = number of singletons (size-1 sets) among $T_1,\ldots,T_{12}$, using di
stinct elements $S \subseteq [7]$, $|S|=s$.

**Key observation:** Every non-singleton set $T_i$ (with $|T_i| \geq 2$) must sa
tisfy $\{a\} \not\subseteq T_i$ for each $a \in S$, meaning $a \notin T_i$. So a
ll $12 - s$ non-singleton sets are subsets of $[7]\setminus S$, which has $7-s$
elements, and form an antichain in $2^{[7-s]}$ (each of size $\geq 2$).

By **Sperner's theorem**, the maximum antichain in $2^{[7-s]}$ has size $\binom{
7-s}{\lfloor(7-s)/2\rfloor}$.

**Case $s \geq 3$:** Then $7-s \leq 4$, max antichain $\leq \binom{4}{2} = 6 < 1
2-s$ (since $12-s \geq 9$). **Impossible.** So $s \leq 2$.

**Case $s \leq 1$:** At least 11 sets have size $\geq 2$, so $t \geq s\cdot 1 +
(12-s)\cdot 2 = 24 - s \geq 23 > 22$.

**Case $s = 2$:** The 10 non-singleton sets form an antichain in $2^{[5]}$, each
 of size $\geq 2$. By Sperner's theorem, the maximum antichain in $2^{[5]}$ has
size $\binom{5}{2} = 10$, so these form a **maximum antichain**. Since each has
size $\geq 2$:

$$t \geq 2 \cdot 1 + 10 \cdot 2 = 22.$$

$$\boxed{22}$$
