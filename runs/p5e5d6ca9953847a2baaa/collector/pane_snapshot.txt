## Proof

**Answer:** $E^n = (-1)^{n-1}$.

**Setup.** Let $\pi: Y \to X$ be the blow-up of an $n$-dimensional projective va
riety $X$ at a smooth point $x$, and let $E = \pi^{-1}(x)$ be the exceptional di
visor.

**Step 1: Identify the exceptional divisor.** Since $x$ is a smooth point, $X$ i
s locally analytically isomorphic to $\mathbb{A}^n$ near $x$. The blow-up of $\m
athbb{A}^n$ at the origin replaces the origin with the projectivization of the t
angent space, so
$$E \cong \mathbb{P}^{n-1} = \mathbb{P}(T_x X).$$

**Step 2: Compute the normal bundle of $E$ in $Y$.** By the standard local descr
iption of a blow-up at a smooth point, the normal bundle of the exceptional divi
sor is the tautological line bundle:
$$N_{E/Y} \cong \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^{n-1}}(-1).$$
This is the dual of the hyperplane line bundle $\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^{n-1}}(1
)$.

**Step 3: Apply the self-intersection formula.** The top self-intersection numbe
r of $E$ is computed via the normal bundle:
$$E^n = \int_E c_1(N_{E/Y})^{n-1}.$$
Let $H = c_1(\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^{n-1}}(1))$ be the hyperplane class on $\ma
thbb{P}^{n-1}$. Then
$$c_1(N_{E/Y}) = c_1(\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^{n-1}}(-1)) = -H.$$
Therefore:
$$E^n = \int_{\mathbb{P}^{n-1}} (-H)^{n-1} = (-1)^{n-1} \int_{\mathbb{P}^{n-1}}
H^{n-1}.$$

**Step 4: Evaluate the integral.** The integral of the top power of the hyperpla
ne class over projective space is:
$$\int_{\mathbb{P}^{n-1}} H^{n-1} = 1,$$
since $H^{n-1}$ is the class of a point (the degree of $\mathbb{P}^{n-1}$ is $1$
).

**Conclusion.** Combining Steps 3 and 4:
$$\boxed{E^n = (-1)^{n-1}}.$$
