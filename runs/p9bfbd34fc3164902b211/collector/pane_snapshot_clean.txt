## Solution

**Base case.** The recurrence $f(m,n+1)=m\sum_{i=1}^{m+1}f(i,n)$ determines $f(m
,n+1)$ from level $n$, so we need the initial level. The natural reading of "bas
e stability factor $m$" at the first iteration is
$$f(m,1)=m.$$

**Claim.** For all $m,n\in\mathbb N^*$,
$$\boxed{\,f(m,n)=\frac{m\,(m+1)(m+2)\cdots(m+2n-2)}{2^{\,n-1}\,(n-1)!}
      =\frac{m\,(m+2n-2)!}{m!\;2^{\,n-1}\,(n-1)!}\,}$$
(the empty product for $n=1$ equals $1$).

**Proof by induction on $n$.**

*Base step $n=1$.* The formula gives $m\cdot 1/(2^0\cdot 0!)=m=f(m,1)$. ✓

*Induction step.* Assume the formula holds for level $n$. Define
$$P(m,n)=(m+1)(m+2)\cdots(m+2n-2)=\frac{(m+2n-2)!}{m!},\qquad D_n=2^{n-1}(n-1)!,
$$
so $f(m,n)=m\,P(m,n)/D_n$.

Using the recurrence and the induction hypothesis,
$$\sum_{i=1}^{m+1} f(i,n)=\frac{1}{D_n}\sum_{i=1}^{m+1} i\,P(i,n)
 =\frac{1}{D_n}\sum_{i=1}^{m+1} i(i+1)(i+2)\cdots(i+2n-2).$$

The summand $i(i+1)\cdots(i+2n-2)$ is the rising factorial of order $2n-1$. The
standard telescoping identity
$$\sum_{i=1}^{k} i(i+1)\cdots(i+r-1)=\frac{k(k+1)\cdots(k+r)}{r+1}$$
with $k=m+1$ and $r=2n-1$ gives
$$\sum_{i=1}^{m+1} i(i+1)\cdots(i+2n-2)=\frac{(m+1)(m+2)\cdots(m+2n)}{2n}.$$

Therefore
$$f(m,n+1)=m\cdot\frac{(m+1)(m+2)\cdots(m+2n)}{2n\,D_n}.$$

It remains to verify this equals $m\,P(m,n+1)/D_{n+1}$. Indeed
$$P(m,n+1)=(m+1)(m+2)\cdots\bigl(m+2(n+1)-2\bigr)=(m+1)(m+2)\cdots(m+2n),$$
and
$$2n\,D_n=2n\cdot 2^{n-1}(n-1)!=2^n\cdot n!=D_{n+1}.$$

Hence $f(m,n+1)=m\,P(m,n+1)/D_{n+1}$, completing the induction. $\blacksquare$

**Equivalent forms.** Since $2^{n-1}(n-1)!=(2n-2)!!$ and $(2n-2)!/(2n-2)!!=(2n-3
)!!$ (with $(-1)!!=1$),
$$f(m,n)=\frac{m\,(m+2n-2)!}{m!\,(2n-2)!!}=m\binom{m+2n-2}{2n-2}(2n-3)!!.$$

**Quick check.** $f(m,2)=m(m+1)(m+2)/2$; via recurrence $f(m,2)=m\sum_{i=1}^{m+1
}i=m\cdot\tfrac{(m+1)(m+2)}2$. ✓
$f(m,3)=m(m+1)(m+2)(m+3)(m+4)/8$; via recurrence $f(m,3)=m\sum_{i=1}^{m+1}\tfrac
{i(i+1)(i+2)}2=m\cdot\tfrac{(m+1)(m+2)(m+3)(m+4)}8$. ✓
