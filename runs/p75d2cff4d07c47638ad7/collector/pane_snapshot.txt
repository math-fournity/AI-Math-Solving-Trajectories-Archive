## Solution

**Model.** Track the number of active sensors $k \in \{0,1,2,3\}$. The vault is
"Secure" exactly when $k=1$. The initial state is $k=1$ (A active, B and C inact
ive).

**Transition rule.** From state $k$, a random sensor is flipped:
- Pick an active sensor (probability $k/3$): $k \to k-1$.
- Pick an inactive sensor (probability $(3-k)/3$): $k \to k+1$.

So the transition probabilities are:
- $k=0 \to k=1$ with prob $1$
- $k=1 \to k=0$ with $1/3$, $\to k=2$ with $2/3$
- $k=2 \to k=1$ with $2/3$, $\to k=3$ with $1/3$
- $k=3 \to k=2$ with prob $1$

Let $p_n = [p_n(0), p_n(1), p_n(2), p_n(3)]$ with $p_0 = [0,1,0,0]$.

**Step 1:**
$$p_1 = \left[\tfrac{1}{3},\; 0,\; \tfrac{2}{3},\; 0\right]$$

**Step 2:**
$$p_2(0)=0,\quad p_2(1)=1\cdot\tfrac{1}{3}+\tfrac{2}{3}\cdot\tfrac{2}{3}=\tfrac{
7}{9},\quad p_2(2)=0,\quad p_2(3)=\tfrac{1}{3}\cdot\tfrac{2}{3}=\tfrac{2}{9}$$
$$p_2 = \left[0,\; \tfrac{7}{9},\; 0,\; \tfrac{2}{9}\right]$$

**Step 3:**
$$p_3(0)=\tfrac{1}{3}\cdot\tfrac{7}{9}=\tfrac{7}{27},\quad p_3(1)=0,\quad p_3(2)
=\tfrac{2}{3}\cdot\tfrac{7}{9}+1\cdot\tfrac{2}{9}=\tfrac{14}{27}+\tfrac{6}{27}=\
tfrac{20}{27},\quad p_3(3)=0$$
$$p_3 = \left[\tfrac{7}{27},\; 0,\; \tfrac{20}{27},\; 0\right]$$

**Step 4:**
$$p_4(0)=0,\quad p_4(1)=1\cdot\tfrac{7}{27}+\tfrac{2}{3}\cdot\tfrac{20}{27}=\tfr
ac{7}{27}+\tfrac{40}{81}=\tfrac{21+40}{81}=\tfrac{61}{81},\quad p_4(2)=0,\quad p
_4(3)=\tfrac{1}{3}\cdot\tfrac{20}{27}=\tfrac{20}{81}$$

(Check: $\tfrac{61}{81}+\tfrac{20}{81}=1$. ✓ Also, starting from odd $k=1$ and t
aking an even number (4) of $\pm 1$ steps keeps us at odd $k$, so only $k\in\{1,
3\}$ have mass — consistent.)

**Result.** The probability of being Secure after four interventions is
$$P(k=1)=\frac{61}{81}.$$

Since $\gcd(61,81)=1$ (as $81=3^4$ and $61$ is prime), we have $m=61$, $n=81$.
