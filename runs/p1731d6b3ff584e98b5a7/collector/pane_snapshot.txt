## Proof

We evaluate the triple integral
$$I = \iiint_R x^1 y^9 z^8 w^4\,dx\,dy\,dz, \qquad w = 1-x-y-z,$$
over the region $R = \{(x,y,z): x,y,z \ge 0,\; x+y+z \le 1\}$.

**Dirichlet integral formula.** For nonnegative integers $a_1,\dots,a_k,a_{k+1}$
,
$$\int_{\substack{x_1,\dots,x_k \ge 0 \\ x_1+\cdots+x_k \le 1}} x_1^{a_1}\cdots
x_k^{a_k}(1-x_1-\cdots-x_k)^{a_{k+1}}\,dx_1\cdots dx_k = \frac{a_1!\,a_2!\cdots
a_{k+1}!}{(a_1+a_2+\cdots+a_{k+1}+k)!}.$$

*Derivation by induction on $k$.* The base case $k=1$ is the Euler beta integral
:
$$\int_0^1 x^{a_1}(1-x)^{a_2}\,dx = \frac{a_1!\,a_2!}{(a_1+a_2+1)!}.$$
For the inductive step, fix $x_k$ and substitute $u_i = x_i/(1-x_k)$ for $i<k$;
the inner integral becomes $(1-x_k)^{a_1+\cdots+a_{k-1}+a_{k+1}+k-1}\cdot x_k^{a
_k}$ times the $(k-1)$-variable Dirichlet integral, which by hypothesis equals $
\frac{a_1!\cdots a_{k-1}!\,a_{k+1}!}{(a_1+\cdots+a_{k-1}+a_{k+1}+k-1)!}$. The re
maining one-dimensional integral in $x_k$ is then a beta integral with exponents
 $a_k$ and $(a_1+\cdots+a_{k-1}+a_{k+1}+k-1)$, giving the factor
$$\frac{a_k!\,(a_1+\cdots+a_{k-1}+a_{k+1}+k-1)!}{(a_1+\cdots+a_{k+1}+k)!},$$
which combines with the inner factor to yield exactly $\frac{a_1!\cdots a_{k+1}!
}{(a_1+\cdots+a_{k+1}+k)!}$. ∎

**Application.** Here $k=3$ with $(x_1,x_2,x_3) = (x,y,z)$ and the slack variabl
e $w = 1-x-y-z$. The exponents are
$$a_1 = 1,\quad a_2 = 9,\quad a_3 = 8,\quad a_4 = 4.$$
Their sum is $1+9+8+4 = 22$, and $k = 3$, so the denominator is $(22+3)! = 25!$.
 Therefore
$$I = \frac{1!\cdot 9!\cdot 8!\cdot 4!}{25!}.$$

This is of the required form $\dfrac{a!\,b!\,c!\,d!}{n!}$ with
$$a=1,\quad b=9,\quad c=8,\quad d=4,\quad n=25,$$
all positive integers.
