## Solution

**Recurrence setup.** We are given $a_1 = 0$ and for $n \geq 1$:
$$(n+1)^3 a_{n+1} = 2n^2(2n+1)\,a_n + 2(3n+1).$$

**Substitution to simplify.** Let $c_n = n^2 a_n$, so $a_n = c_n/n^2$. Substitut
ing:

$$(n+1)^3 \cdot \frac{c_{n+1}}{(n+1)^2} = 2n^2(2n+1)\cdot\frac{c_n}{n^2} + 2(3n+
1)$$

$$(n+1)\,c_{n+1} = 2(2n+1)\,c_n + 2(3n+1), \qquad c_1 = 0.$$

**Computing $c_n$ for $n = 1, \dots, 10$:**

**Checking $a_n = c_n / n^2$ for positive integrality:**

|-----|-----------------|----------|-----------|

**Verification of key values:**

- $a_7 = 70$: $49 \times 70 = 3430$. ✓

 1 since $a_1=0$ is not positive). The sum of all indices in $S$ is:

$$2 + 3 + 5 + 7 = \boxed{17}.$$
