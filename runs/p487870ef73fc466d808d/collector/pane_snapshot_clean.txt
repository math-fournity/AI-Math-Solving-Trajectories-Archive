Looking at this problem, I need to determine whether the zero extension of $u \i
n W^{1,p}_0(\Omega)$ belongs to $W^{1,p}_0(\mathbb{R}^n)$ for all $1 \leq p \leq
 \infty$.

**Answer: Yes, for all $1 \leq p \leq \infty$.**

**Proof.**

Let $\Omega \subset \mathbb{R}^n$ be open and $1 \leq p \leq \infty$. Recall tha
t $W^{1,p}_0(\Omega)$ is the closure of $C_c^\infty(\Omega)$ in the $W^{1,p}(\Om
ega)$ norm. For $u \in W^{1,p}_0(\Omega)$, define the zero extension
$$\tilde{u}(x) = \begin{cases} u(x), & x \in \Omega, \\ 0, & x \in \mathbb{R}^n
\setminus \Omega. \end{cases}$$

**Key observation.** If $\phi \in C_c^\infty(\Omega)$, then $\operatorname{supp}
(\phi) \subset\subset \Omega$, so $\phi$ and all its derivatives vanish in a nei
ghborhood of $\partial\Omega$. Therefore the zero extension $\tilde{\phi}$ is sm
ooth across $\partial\Omega$, hence $\tilde{\phi} \in C_c^\infty(\mathbb{R}^n)$.
 Moreover, since $\tilde{\phi}$ and $\nabla\tilde{\phi}$ vanish outside $\Omega$
,
$$\|\tilde{\phi}\|_{W^{1,p}(\mathbb{R}^n)} = \|\phi\|_{W^{1,p}(\Omega)}.$$

**Approximation argument.** Since $u \in W^{1,p}_0(\Omega)$, there exists a sequ
ence $\{\phi_k\} \subset C_c^\infty(\Omega)$ with $\phi_k \to u$ in $W^{1,p}(\Om
ega)$. By the norm equality above, $\{\tilde{\phi}_k\} \subset C_c^\infty(\mathb
b{R}^n)$ satisfies
$$\|\tilde{\phi}_k - \tilde{\phi}_j\|_{W^{1,p}(\mathbb{R}^n)} = \|\phi_k - \phi_
j\|_{W^{1,p}(\Omega)} \to 0 \quad \text{as } k,j \to \infty.$$

So $\{\tilde{\phi}_k\}$ is Cauchy in $W^{1,p}(\mathbb{R}^n)$. Since $W^{1,p}(\ma
thbb{R}^n)$ is a Banach space (complete) for every $1 \leq p \leq \infty$, there
 exists $v \in W^{1,p}(\mathbb{R}^n)$ with $\tilde{\phi}_k \to v$ in $W^{1,p}(\m
athbb{R}^n)$.

**Identifying the limit.** On $\Omega$, $\tilde{\phi}_k = \phi_k \to u$ in $L^p(
\Omega)$, so $v|_\Omega = u$ a.e. On $\mathbb{R}^n \setminus \Omega$, $\tilde{\p
hi}_k = 0$, so $v = 0$ a.e. there. Hence $v = \tilde{u}$ a.e. in $\mathbb{R}^n$.

**Conclusion.** We have shown $\tilde{u} \in W^{1,p}(\mathbb{R}^n)$ and $\tilde{
\phi}_k \to \tilde{u}$ in $W^{1,p}(\mathbb{R}^n)$ with each $\tilde{\phi}_k \in
C_c^\infty(\mathbb{R}^n)$. Since $W^{1,p}_0(\mathbb{R}^n) = \overline{C_c^\infty
(\mathbb{R}^n)}^{\,W^{1,p}(\mathbb{R}^n)}$, it follows that
$$\tilde{u} \in W^{1,p}_0(\mathbb{R}^n).$$

This holds for every $1 \leq p \leq \infty$. $\blacksquare$
