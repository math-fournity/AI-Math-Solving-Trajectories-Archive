## Solution

**Setup.** The master sequence is $S = (0,1,2,\ldots,9)$ repeated 5 times, so $S
[k] = k \bmod 10$ for positions $k = 0, 1, \ldots, 49$. A configuration $T$ is *
Compatible* with $S$ if $T$ is obtained by choosing a contiguous segment $[i,j]$
 and reversing it (including the trivial reversal giving $S$ itself).

**Key bijection.** I claim there is a bijection between:
- Distinct configurations $T \neq S$ obtainable by a single reversal, and
- Pairs $(L, R)$ with $0 \le L < R \le 49$ and $S[L] \neq S[R]$.

**Forward direction.** Given $T \neq S$ obtained by reversing $[i,j]$, define:
$$L = \min\{k : T[k] \neq S[k]\}, \qquad R = \max\{k : T[k] \neq S[k]\}.$$

Since $T[k] = S[i{+}j{-}k]$ for $k \in [i,j]$ and $T[k]=S[k]$ outside, the set o
f differing positions is symmetric about the center $(i{+}j)/2$, so $L + R = i +
 j$. At the extremes, $T[L] = S[R] \neq S[L]$, so $S[L] \neq S[R]$.

Crucially, $T$ is *determined* by $(L,R)$: for $k \in [L,R]$, $T[k] = S[L{+}R{-}
k]$; for $k \notin [L,R]$, $T[k] = S[k]$. (Any extension of the reversal beyond
$[L,R]$ only touches positions where $S[k]=S[L{+}R{-}k]$, producing no change.)

**Backward direction.** Given $(L,R)$ with $S[L] \neq S[R]$, reverse segment $[L
,R]$. This yields $T$ with $T[k] = S[L{+}R{-}k]$ for $k \in [L,R]$. Since $S[L]
\neq S[R]$, position $L$ genuinely differs ($T[L]=S[R]\neq S[L]$), and likewise
position $R$. So the min/max differing positions are exactly $L$ and $R$, recove
ring $(L,R)$. Also $T \neq S$ since $S[L]\neq S[R]$ means $S[L..R]$ is not a pal
indrome.

**Injectivity.** If two pairs $(L_1,R_1)$ and $(L_2,R_2)$ yield the same $T$, th
en the min and max differing positions coincide, forcing $L_1=L_2$ and $R_1=R_2$
.

**Counting.** The number of distinct compatible configurations is:
$$1 + \bigl|\{(L,R) : 0 \le L < R \le 49,\; S[L] \neq S[R]\}\bigr|.$$

Total pairs with $L < R$: $\binom{50}{2} = 1225$.

Pairs with $S[L] = S[R]$ (i.e., $L \equiv R \pmod{10}$): For each digit $v \in \
{0,\ldots,9\}$, the positions with value $v$ are $\{v, v{+}10, v{+}20, v{+}30, v
{+}40\}$ — exactly 5 positions. Pairs within each group: $\binom{5}{2} = 10$. Ac
ross all 10 digits: $10 \times 10 = 100$.

Pairs with $S[L] \neq S[R]$: $1225 - 100 = 1125$.

**Final count:**
$$1 + 1125 = \boxed{1126}.$$
