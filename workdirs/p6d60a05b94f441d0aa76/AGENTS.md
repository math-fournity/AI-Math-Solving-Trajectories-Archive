# Problem

In a bustling coastal region, there are exactly 100 distinct cargo ships navigating the waters. To coordinate their movements, the regional maritime authority utilizes four different radio frequencies. Every single ship in the fleet is equipped to communicate on at least one of these four frequencies.

The safety protocol of this region is unique: any two ships chosen from the fleet must be able to communicate directly with each other, meaning every pair of ships shares at least one common radio frequency.

Due to bandwidth limitations, the harbor master needs to identify the minimum possible size of the largest group of ships sharing a specific frequency. Let $k$ represent the minimum number of ships that must be tuned into the same frequency to ensure the safety protocol is mathematically satisfied for all 100 ships.

Find the value of $k$.
