## Solution

We find all functions $f: GL_n(\mathbb{C}) \to M_n(\mathbb{C})$ satisfying, for
every $M \in GL_n(\mathbb{C})$:

$$M^{-1} f(-M) + f(M^{-1}) = M. \tag{$\star$}$$

### Uniqueness: deriving the candidate

Fix an arbitrary $M \in GL_n(\mathbb{C})$. We use $(\star)$ at three related inp
uts to obtain a linear system that pins down $f(M)$.

**Equation at $M$** (this is $(\star$) itself):
$$M^{-1} f(-M) + f(M^{-1}) = M. \tag{1}$$

**Equation at $M^{-1}$** (replace $M$ by $M^{-1}$ in $(\star)$): since $(M^{-1})
^{-1}=M$ and $-(M^{-1})=-M^{-1}$,
$$M\, f(-M^{-1}) + f(M) = M^{-1}. \tag{2}$$

**Equation at $-M$** (replace $M$ by $-M$ in $(\star)$): since $(-M)^{-1}=-M^{-1
}$ and $-(-M)=M$,
$$-M^{-1} f(M) + f(-M^{-1}) = -M. \tag{3}$$

Equations (2) and (3) form a linear system in the two unknowns $f(M)$ and $f(-M^
{-1})$:
$$f(M) + M\, f(-M^{-1}) = M^{-1}, \qquad -M^{-1} f(M) + f(-M^{-1}) = -M.$$

From (3): $f(-M^{-1}) = -M + M^{-1} f(M)$. Substituting into (2):

$$M\bigl(-M + M^{-1} f(M)\bigr) + f(M) = M^{-1}$$
$$-M^{2} + f(M) + f(M) = M^{-1}$$
$$2\,f(M) = M^{-1} + M^{2}.$$

Hence, for **every** $M \in GL_n(\mathbb{C})$,
$$\boxed{\,f(M) = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(M^{2} + M^{-1}\bigr)\,}. \tag{$\dagger$}$$

Since the value $f(M)$ is uniquely determined at each $M$, there is at most one
solution.

### Verification

We confirm that $(\dagger)$ satisfies $(\star)$. Compute each term:

$$f(-M) = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl((-M)^{2} + (-M)^{-1}\bigr) = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(M^{2}
 - M^{-1}\bigr),$$

$$M^{-1} f(-M) = \tfrac{1}{2}\,M^{-1}\bigl(M^{2} - M^{-1}\bigr) = \tfrac{1}{2}\b
igl(M - M^{-2}\bigr),$$

$$f(M^{-1}) = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl((M^{-1})^{2} + (M^{-1})^{-1}\bigr) = \tfrac{1}{2
}\bigl(M^{-2} + M\bigr).$$

Adding:

$$M^{-1} f(-M) + f(M^{-1}) = \tfrac{1}{2}(M - M^{-2}) + \tfrac{1}{2}(M^{-2} + M)
 = \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot 2M = M.$$

Thus $(\star)$ holds for all $M \in GL_n(\mathbb{C})$.

### Consistency check at $M = \pm I$

For completeness, evaluating $(\dagger)$ at $M=I$ gives $f(I)=\tfrac12(I+I)=I$,
and at $M=-I$ gives $f(-I)=\tfrac12(I-I)=0$. These are consistent with $(\star)$
 at $M=\pm I$:
- $M=I$: $f(-I)+f(I)=0+I=I$. ✓
- $M=-I$: $-f(I)+f(-I)=-I+0=-I$. ✓

### Conclusion

The unique function satisfying the given equation is

$$f(M) = \frac{1}{2}\bigl(M^{2} + M^{-1}\bigr), \qquad M \in GL_{n}(\mathbb{C}).
$$
