# Problem

In a remote digital archives facility, a master server rack is organized into a grid of 7 rows and 8 columns of storage modules. To ensure data integrity, each module must be set to one of two states: "Active" (represented by a black status light) or "Standby" (represented by a white status light).

The facility’s safety protocol requires a specific density of active modules to prevent cooling failures: within every possible $3 \times 3$ sub-grid of modules, there must be at least one pair of Active modules that are physically adjacent (sharing a common side). 

What is the minimum total number of Active modules required across the entire $7 \times 8$ rack to satisfy this safety protocol?
