## Step 1 — Identify the semidirect product structure

The group is
$$G = \left\{ \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \;\middle|\; a \in \m
athbb{F}_p^*,\; b \in \mathbb{F}_p \right\}.$$

With the problem's notation $A = \{\mathrm{diag}(x,1)\} \cong \mathbb{F}_p^*$ an
d $H = \left\{\begin{pmatrix}1&y\\0&1\end{pmatrix}\right\} \cong \mathbb{F}_p$ (
additive), every element factors uniquely:
$$\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} = \underbrace{\begin{pmatrix} 1 &
 b \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}}_{\in H}\;\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix} a & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \
end{pmatrix}}_{\in A}, \qquad H \cap A = \{e\}.$$

**Which factor is normal?** Conjugating $H$ by $A$:
$$\begin{pmatrix} a&0\\0&1 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}1&y\\0&1\end{pmatrix}\beg
in{pmatrix}a&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}^{-1} = \begin{pmatrix}1 & ay\\0&1\end{pmatrix}
\in H.$$
So $H \trianglelefteq G$. Conjugating $A$ by $H$ gives $\begin{pmatrix}x & y(1-x
)\\0&1\end{pmatrix} \notin A$ (for $x\neq 1$), so $A$ is **not** normal.

Therefore $G = H \rtimes A$ where $H \cong (\mathbb{F}_p, +)$ is the **abelian n
ormal** subgroup and $A \cong \mathbb{F}_p^*$ is the complement. The action of $
A$ on $H$ is $a \cdot y = ay$ (multiplication in $\mathbb{F}_p$).

Since isomorphic groups have isomorphic representations, we may apply Mackey's i
rreducibility criterion (the "little-group method") to $G = H \rtimes A$ with $H
$ abelian normal.

## Step 2 — Mackey's criterion (recap)

For $G = N \rtimes K$, $N$ abelian normal:

1. Let $\widehat{N} = \mathrm{Hom}(N, \mathbb{C}^*)$. $K$ acts on $\widehat{N}$
by $(k\cdot\chi)(n) = \chi(k^{-1}nk)$.
2. For each $K$-orbit $\omega \subseteq \widehat{N}$, pick a representative $\ch
i$ and let $K_\chi = \{k \in K : k\cdot\chi = \chi\}$ (stabilizer).
3. Extend $\chi$ to $N \rtimes K_\chi$ by $\chi(nk)=\chi(n)$ (trivial on $K_\chi
$).
4. For each irreducible $\rho$ of $K_\chi$, form $\chi \otimes \rho$ on $N \rtim
es K_\chi$ and induce:
$$\pi_{\chi,\rho} = \mathrm{Ind}_{N \rtimes K_\chi}^{G}(\chi \otimes \rho).$$
5. **Mackey's theorem:** every $\pi_{\chi,\rho}$ is irreducible, and these are *
*all** irreducibles of $G$, up to isomorphism, with distinct pairs $(\omega, \rh
o)$ giving non-isomorphic representations.

## Step 3 — Characters of $N = H \cong \mathbb{F}_p$

The additive group $\mathbb{F}_p \cong \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$ has $p$ character
s
$$\chi_t(y) = e^{2\pi i t y / p}, \qquad t \in \mathbb{F}_p.$$
So $\widehat{N} \cong \mathbb{F}_p$ (additively), parameterized by $t$.

## Step 4 — Action of $K = A \cong \mathbb{F}_p^*$ on $\widehat{N}$

For $a \in \mathbb{F}_p^*$ and $\chi_t \in \widehat{N}$:
$$(a \cdot \chi_t)(y) = \chi_t(a^{-1}y) = e^{2\pi i t a^{-1} y/p} = \chi_{t a^{-
1}}(y).$$
So $a$ sends the parameter $t \mapsto t\,a^{-1}$. This is the natural multiplica
tion action of $\mathbb{F}_p^*$ on $\mathbb{F}_p$.

## Step 5 — Orbits and stabilizers

$\mathbb{F}_p^*$ acts on $\mathbb{F}_p$ by multiplication. The orbits are:

- **$\{0\}$**: the trivial character $\chi_0$, fixed by all of $\mathbb{F}_p^*$.
- **$\mathbb{F}_p^*$**: all nonzero $t$ form a single orbit (since $\mathbb{F}_p
^*$ acts transitively on itself).

So there are exactly **two orbits**.

### Orbit 1: $t = 0$ (trivial character)

- $\chi_0(y) = 1$ for all $y$.
- Stabilizer $K_{\chi_0} = K = \mathbb{F}_p^* \cong \mathbb{Z}/(p{-}1)\mathbb{Z}
$.
- $N \rtimes K_{\chi_0} = G$.
- $\chi_0$ extends trivially to all of $G$.
- Irreducible representations of $K \cong \mathbb{Z}/(p{-}1)\mathbb{Z}$: the $(p
-1)$ one-dimensional characters $\psi_0, \psi_1, \dots, \psi_{p-2}$, where $\psi
_j(a) = \omega^{j \cdot \mathrm{ind}(a)}$ for a fixed primitive root $\omega$ of
 order $p-1$ (equivalently, $\psi_j$ is any homomorphism $\mathbb{F}_p^* \to \ma
thbb{C}^*$).

Each $\psi_j$ gives a **one-dimensional** representation of $G$:
$$\boxed{\rho_j\!\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} = \psi_j(a), \qqua
d j = 0, 1, \dots, p-2.}$$
(These are the $(p-1)$ linear characters of $G$, factoring through the quotient
$G \to G/H \cong \mathbb{F}_p^*$.)

### Orbit 2: $t \neq 0$ (representative $\chi_1$)

- Representative: $\chi_1(y) = e^{2\pi i y/p}$.
- Stabilizer: $K_{\chi_1} = \{a \in \mathbb{F}_p^* : a^{-1} = 1\} = \{1\}$.
- $N \rtimes K_{\chi_1} = N = H$.
- The only irreducible representation of the trivial group $K_{\chi_1}=\{e\}$ is
 the trivial one.
- Induce $\chi_1$ from $N$ to $G$:
$$\boxed{\pi = \mathrm{Ind}_{H}^{G}(\chi_1), \qquad \dim \pi = [G:H] = |A| = p-1
.}$$

**Explicit realization.** Choose right-coset representatives $\{k_a\}_{a \in \ma
thbb{F}_p^*}$ with $k_a = \mathrm{diag}(a,1)$. The induced representation acts o
n $\mathbb{C}[\mathbb{F}_p^*]$ with basis $\{e_a\}_{a \in \mathbb{F}_p^*}$. For
$g = \begin{pmatrix}\alpha & \beta\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$:
$$k_a \cdot g = \begin{pmatrix}a\alpha & a\beta\\0&1\end{pmatrix} = \underbrace{
\begin{pmatrix}1 & a\beta\\0&1\end{pmatrix}}_{\in H}\;\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix
}a\alpha & 0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}}_{= k_{a\alpha}},$$
so by the induction formula:
$$\pi(g)\, e_a \;=\; \chi_1\!\begin{pmatrix}1 & a\beta\\0&1\end{pmatrix} e_{a\al
pha} \;=\; e^{2\pi i\, a\beta/p}\; e_{a\alpha}.$$

## Step 6 — Verification (dimension count)

Number of irreducibles: $(p-1)$ one-dimensional $+$ $1$ of dimension $(p-1)$.

Sum of squares of dimensions:
$$(p-1)\cdot 1^2 + 1\cdot(p-1)^2 = (p-1) + (p-1)^2 = (p-1)\bigl(1+(p-1)\bigr) =
p(p-1) = |G|. \quad\checkmark$$

This matches $|G| = |\mathbb{F}_p^*|\cdot|\mathbb{F}_p| = (p-1)p$, confirming we
 have found **all** irreducible representations.
