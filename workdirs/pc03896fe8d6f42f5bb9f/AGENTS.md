# Problem

In a remote digital treasury, access codes are generated using a specific security protocol. A system administrator needs to identify every valid security quartet $(x, y, z, n)$ that satisfies a precise power-load balance.

The protocol requires that three server clusters consume energy at levels defined by powers of 10: specifically $10^x$, $10^y$, and $10^z$ units of power. The total energy consumption of these three clusters must exactly equal the product of a positive integer $n$ and its immediate successor $(n + 1)$. To prevent redundant processing, the clusters are indexed such that their power exponents follow the non-decreasing order $x \le y \le z$. 

The administrator defines $S$ as the set of all possible integer quartets $(x, y, z, n)$ that satisfy these conditions. To verify the integrity of the treasury, calculate the total sum of all components $(x + y + z + n)$ across every valid solution in $S$.
