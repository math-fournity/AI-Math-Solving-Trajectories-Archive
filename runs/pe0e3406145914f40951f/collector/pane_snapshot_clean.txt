## Dimension of the Symplectic Group Sp(2n, F)

**Answer:** $\dim \operatorname{Sp}(2n, F) = n(2n+1) = 2n^2 + n$.

---

### Proof

Let $F = \overline{\mathbb{F}_q}$ be the algebraic closure of a finite field. Th
e symplectic group $\operatorname{Sp}(2n, F)$ is the algebraic group of invertib
le $2n \times 2n$ matrices $M$ over $F$ satisfying

$$M^T J M = J,$$

where $J = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & I_n \\ -I_n & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ is the standard sy
mplectic form (a non-degenerate alternating bilinear form on $F^{2n}$).

Since $\operatorname{Sp}(2n, F)$ is a smooth connected algebraic group over the
algebraically closed field $F$, its dimension equals the dimension of its Lie al
gebra $\mathfrak{sp}(2n)$, which consists of all $2n \times 2n$ matrices $X$ sat
isfying

$$X^T J + J X = 0. \tag{$\star$}$$

**Counting the dimension of $\mathfrak{sp}(2n)$.** Consider the linear map

$$\varphi: \mathfrak{sp}(2n) \longrightarrow \operatorname{Sym}_{2n}(F), \qquad
X \longmapsto JX,$$

where $\operatorname{Sym}_{2n}(F)$ denotes the space of symmetric $2n \times 2n$
 matrices.

- **$\varphi$ is injective:** $J$ is invertible, so $JX = 0$ implies $X = 0$.
- **$\varphi$ is surjective onto $\operatorname{Sym}_{2n}(F)$:** Given any symme
tric matrix $S$ (so $S^T = S$), set $X = J^{-1} S = -J S$ (since $J^{-1} = -J$).
 Then $JX = S$, and we verify ($\star$):
$$X^T J + JX = (-JS)^T J + S = -S^T J^T J + S = -S(-J)J + S = S \cdot J^2 + S.$$
Since $J^2 = -I_{2n}$, we get $S(-I_{2n}) + S = -S + S = 0$. ✓

Hence $\varphi$ is a linear isomorphism, giving

$$\dim \mathfrak{sp}(2n) = \dim \operatorname{Sym}_{2n}(F).$$

The space of symmetric $2n \times 2n$ matrices has dimension equal to the number
 of entries on and above the diagonal:

$$\dim \operatorname{Sym}_{2n}(F) = \frac{2n(2n+1)}{2} = n(2n+1) = 2n^2 + n.$$

**Verification with small cases:**
- $n = 1$: $\operatorname{Sp}(2, F) = \operatorname{SL}(2, F)$, which has dimens
ion $3 = 1 \cdot 3$. ✓
- $n = 2$: $\operatorname{Sp}(4, F)$ has dimension $10 = 2 \cdot 5$. ✓

**Conclusion.** Since $\operatorname{Sp}(2n, F)$ is a smooth connected algebraic
 group over the algebraically closed field $F$,

$$\boxed{\dim \operatorname{Sp}(2n, F) = n(2n+1) = 2n^2 + n.}$$
