$|H_x| = (\alpha):K]$, and the conjugates of $\alpha$ are exactly $\{\alpha\xi

$$m_\alpha(T) = \prod_{\xi \in H_x}(T - \alpha\xi).$$

$$m_\beta(T) = \prod_{\xi \in H_x}(T - \alpha\xi\zeta_x).$$
