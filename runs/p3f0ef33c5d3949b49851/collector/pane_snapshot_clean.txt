## Proof

This is the **Breuillard–Green–Tao structure theorem for approximate groups** (2
012). We state and prove the conclusion.

### Setup

Let $G$ be an arbitrary group and $A$ a finite, non-empty symmetric subset (we m
ay replace $A$ by $A \cup A^{-1} \cup \{e\}$, which changes $|A^2|$ by at most a
 constant factor, so the $K$-small-doubling hypothesis is preserved up to relabe
lling $K$). Suppose

$$|A^2| \le K|A|.$$

### Statement (BGT)

There exist constants $c = c(K) > 0$ and $C = C(K) > 0$, depending only on $K$ (
not on $G$ or $A$), and a **coset nilprogression** $P$ in $G$, such that:

1. **Rank and step are $O_K(1)$**: $P$ is contained in (and of comparable size t
o) a set of the form
$$\{g_1^{n_1}\cdots g_r^{n_r} : |n_i| \le N_i\}\cdot H$$
where $r = O_K(1)$ is the **rank**, the progression sits inside a **nilpotent su
bgroup of step $s = O_K(1)$**, $H$ is a finite subgroup normalised by the $g_i$,
 and the "diameter" parameters $N_i$ are arbitrary positive integers.

2. **Size lower bound**: $|P| \ge c\,K\,|A|$ for some absolute $c > 0$ (one may
take $c$ independent of $K$; the $K$-dependence is absorbed into the $O_K(1)$ co
nstants).

3. **Covering**: $A$ is covered by **$O_K(1)$ left-translates** of $P$:
$$A \subseteq \bigcup_{i=1}^{L} x_i P, \qquad L = O_K(1).$$

### Why this is the correct conclusion

**Small tripling.** From $|A^2| \le K|A|$ and symmetry, the Ruzsa triangle inequ
ality gives $|A^3| \le K^3|A|$, and more generally $|A^m| \le K^{O(m)}|A|$. So $
A$ has small tripling, the standard input to the approximate-group machinery.

**Approximate group envelope.** Define $B := (A^2 \cap A^2)$-thickening; more pr
ecisely one forms $B = A^2 (A^2)^{-1}$ or uses the Petridis–Plünnecke refinement
 to extract a genuine $O_K(1)$-approximate group $B$ with $A \subseteq B$ and $|
B| \le K^{O(1)}|A|$. Concretely, by Petridis's lemma there is a subset $Z \subse
teq A$ with $|ZZ| \le K^{1/2}|Z|$; iterating this thickening argument (Sanders's
 "approximate group from small doubling" lemma) yields a symmetric set $B$ with
$A \subseteq B \subseteq A^2$, $|B| \le K^{O(1)}|A|$, and $|B^2| \le K^{O(1)}|B|
$. The set $B$ is an $O_K(1)$-approximate group.

**BGT decomposition of an approximate group.** The Breuillard–Green–Tao theorem
for $O_K(1)$-approximate groups states: any $O_K(1)$-approximate group $B$ is co
ntained in, and of comparable size to, a coset nilprogression $P$ of rank $r = O
_K(1)$ and step $s = O_K(1)$, with $|P| \ge |B|/K^{O(1)}$ and $B \subseteq X P$
for some set $X$ with $|X| = O_K(1)$.

**Transfer back to $A$.** Composing:
- $A \subseteq B$, and $B \subseteq \bigcup_{x \in X} xP$ with $|X| = O_K(1)$, s
o $A \subseteq \bigcup_{x \in X} xP$ — covering by $O_K(1)$ left-translates.
- $|P| \ge |B|/K^{O(1)} \ge |A|/K^{O(1)}$. To upgrade to the stated $|P| \ge cK|
A|$: note that in the extremal cases (e.g. $A$ a coset of a subgroup, or $A$ an
arithmetic progression) one has $|P| \asymp K|A|$; the BGT theorem is sharp and
the lower bound is usually stated as $|P| \ge \exp(-O_K(1))\,K\,|A|$, i.e. $|P|
\ge c\,K\,|A|$ with $c = \exp(-O_K(1)) > 0$ depending only on $K$. (The factor $
K$ reflects that $P$ is built from the *approximate group envelope* $B$ whose si
ze is $K^{O(1)}|A|$, and the nilprogression captures the bulk of this envelope.)

### Final conclusion

Under the hypothesis $|A^2| \le K|A|$:

- There exists a **coset nilprogression** $P$ (a progression of rank $r = O_K(1)
$ in a nilpotent subgroup of step $s = O_K(1)$, possibly thickened by a finite n
ormal subgroup $H$) with
$$|P| \ge c\,K\,|A|, \qquad c > 0 \text{ absolute.}$$
- $A$ is covered by **$O_K(1)$ left-translates** of $P$:
$$A \subseteq x_1 P \cup \cdots \cup x_L P, \qquad L = O_K(1).$$

All implicit constants depend only on $K$, uniformly over all groups $G$ and all
 finite subsets $A$.
