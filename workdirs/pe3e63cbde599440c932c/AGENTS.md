# Problem

In the city of Arithema, industrial power is generated by combining two types of chemical catalysts, Type-A and Type-B, to produce a stable energy isotope of weight $k$. The strength of these catalysts depends on a variable environmental factor $x$. 

For any specific even integer grade $n$, the catalysts are formulated as follows:
- A Type-A unit has a potency of $(x+1)^n$.
- A Type-B unit has a potency of $(x^n+1)$.

To produce the isotope, a lead scientist must find two control sequences, $f(x)$ and $g(x)$, which are polynomials with integer coefficients. The resulting weight $k$ is determined by the reaction formula:
$$k = f(x)(x+1)^n + g(x)(x^n+1)$$

For a given even grade $n$, let $k_0(n)$ be the minimum possible positive integer weight $k$ that can be produced using any integer-coefficient polynomials $f(x)$ and $g(x)$.

The city needs to catalog the efficiency of the first ten even grades. Calculate the total sum of these minimum weights:
$$\sum_{j=1}^{10} k_0(2j)$$
