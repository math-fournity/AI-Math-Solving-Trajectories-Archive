## Proof

We want to evaluate
$$\lim_{V \to \mathbf{0}^{n\times n}} \frac{|\operatorname{trace}(V^{2})|}{\|V\|
_{\mathrm{op}}}$$
and show it equals $0$.

**Key bound.** For any $n\times n$ matrix $A$, the diagonal entries satisfy $|A_
{ii}| = |e_i^\top A e_i| \leq \|A\|_{\mathrm{op}}$, since $\|e_i\|_2 = 1$. Hence
$$|\operatorname{trace}(A)| \leq \sum_{i=1}^{n} |A_{ii}| \leq n\,\|A\|_{\mathrm{
op}}.$$

**Apply to $A = V^2$.** Using the submultiplicativity of the operator norm, $\|V
^2\|_{\mathrm{op}} \leq \|V\|_{\mathrm{op}}^2$, we get
$$|\operatorname{trace}(V^2)| \leq n\,\|V^2\|_{\mathrm{op}} \leq n\,\|V\|_{\math
rm{op}}^2.$$

**Form the quotient.** For $V \neq \mathbf{0}$,
$$0 \;\leq\; \frac{|\operatorname{trace}(V^2)|}{\|V\|_{\mathrm{op}}} \;\leq\; \f
rac{n\,\|V\|_{\mathrm{op}}^2}{\|V\|_{\mathrm{op}}} \;=\; n\,\|V\|_{\mathrm{op}}.
$$

As $V \to \mathbf{0}^{n\times n}$, we have $\|V\|_{\mathrm{op}} \to 0$, so the r
ight-hand side $n\,\|V\|_{\mathrm{op}} \to 0$. By the squeeze theorem,
$$\lim_{V \to \mathbf{0}^{n\times n}} \frac{|\operatorname{trace}(V^2)|}{\|V\|_{
\mathrm{op}}} = 0.$$

**Remark on the hint.** This is exactly the remainder-term estimate needed to es
tablish Fréchet differentiability of the map $V \mapsto \operatorname{trace}(V^2
)$ (or related quadratic matrix functions) at the origin: the quadratic term $\o
peratorname{trace}(V^2)$ is $o(\|V\|_{\mathrm{op}})$, confirming it is a higher-
order term that vanishes faster than the linear part in the Fréchet expansion.
