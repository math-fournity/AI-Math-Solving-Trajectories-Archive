Key subgroups. Consider two subgroups of $GA_n$:

  1. The unipotent subgroup $U$: For $P \in k[x_1, \dots, x_{n-1}]$, define $$
  \phi_P : (x_1, \dots, x_n) \mapsto (x_1, \dots, x_{n-1},; x_n + P(x_1, \dots,
  x_{n-1})).$$ The set $U = {\phi_P : P \in k[x_1, \dots, x_{n-1}]}$ is an
  abelian subgroup of $GA_n$, isomorphic as an algebraic group (ind-group) to
  the vector group $(k[x_1, \dots, x_{n-1}], +)$.
  2. A one-dimensional torus $\mathbb{G}_m$: For $\lambda \in k^*$, define $$
  \sigma_\lambda : (x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n) \mapsto (\lambda, x_1,; x_2, \dots,
  x_n).$$ This gives an embedding $\mathbb{G}_m \hookrightarrow GA_n$.

Conjugation action. We compute the action of $\mathbb{G}m$ on $U$ by
conjugation: $$\sigma\lambda \circ \phi_P \circ \sigma_\lambda^{-1} =
\phi_{P(\lambda^{-1} x_1,, x_2, \dots, x_{n-1})}.$$

Thus $\mathbb{G}m$ acts on $\operatorname{Lie}(U) \cong k[x_1, \dots, x{n-1}]$

differential: $$d\rho: \operatorname{Lie}(U) \to \mathfrak{gl}_N, \qquad
