**Proof.**

**Step 1: $u$ is bounded.**

Since $S$ is uniformly bounded, there exists $M > 0$ such that $|v(x)| \leq M$ f

$$w(y) = \sup\{v(y) : v \in S\}.$$
Then $-M \leq v(y) \leq M$ for every $v \in S$, so $-M \leq w(y) \leq M$ for all

Now $u(x) = \limsup_{y \to x} w(y)$. Since $w(y) \leq M$ for all $y$, we have $u
(x) \leq M$ for all $x$. Likewise, since $w(y) \geq -M$ for all $y$, we have $u(
x) \geq -M$ for all $x$. Therefore
$$|u(x)| \leq M \quad \text{for all } x \in \mathbb{R}.$$

**Step 2: $u$ is measurable.**

The function $u(x) = \limsup_{y \to x} w(y)$ is upper semicontinuous. To see thi

\delta/2$, we can find a punctured neighborhood of $x_1$ contained in the punctu
