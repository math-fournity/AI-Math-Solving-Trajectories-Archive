# Problem

A specialized deep-sea research station is equipped with $n$ experimental thermal propulsion units. Each unit $i$ (where $i$ ranges from $1$ to $n$) can operate at a specific efficiency rating denoted by $x_i$. Due to safety constraints and the freezing environment of the ocean floor, the efficiency rating of any individual unit cannot drop below a threshold of $-1$ (i.e., $x_i \geq -1$).

The propulsion system is governed by a strict thermodynamic equilibrium law: the sum of the cubes of the efficiency ratings across all $n$ units must exactly equal zero.

The Chief Engineer needs to determine the maximum potential "Total Kinetic Output" of the system, which is defined as the sum of the squares of the efficiency ratings of all $n$ units. 

Based on these operational constraints, what is the maximum possible value of $x_1^2 + x_2^2 + \dots + x_n^2$?
