## Proof

We wish to show
$$\lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{\sqrt{2\pi}\, x - x\int_{-\infty}^{x} e^{-t^{2}/2}\,
dt}{\exp(-x^{2}/2)} = 1.$$

**Step 1: Rewrite the numerator.**

Since $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-t^{2}/2}\,dt = \sqrt{2\pi}$ (the Gaussian int
egral), we have
$$\sqrt{2\pi} - \int_{-\infty}^{x} e^{-t^{2}/2}\,dt = \int_{x}^{\infty} e^{-t^{2
}/2}\,dt.$$

Denoting $R(x) = \int_{x}^{\infty} e^{-t^{2}/2}\,dt$, the numerator becomes $x\,
R(x)$, so the limit is
$$L = \lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{x\,R(x)}{e^{-x^{2}/2}}.$$

**Step 2: Integration by parts to obtain an asymptotic expansion of $R(x)$.**

Write
$$R(x) = \int_{x}^{\infty} e^{-t^{2}/2}\,dt = \int_{x}^{\infty} \frac{1}{t}\cdot
 t\,e^{-t^{2}/2}\,dt.$$

Set $u = 1/t$, $dv = t\,e^{-t^{2}/2}\,dt$, so $du = -t^{-2}\,dt$ and $v = -e^{-t
^{2}/2}$. Integration by parts gives
$$R(x) = \left[-\frac{e^{-t^{2}/2}}{t}\right]_{x}^{\infty} - \int_{x}^{\infty} \
frac{e^{-t^{2}/2}}{t^{2}}\,dt = \frac{e^{-x^{2}/2}}{x} - \int_{x}^{\infty} \frac
{e^{-t^{2}/2}}{t^{2}}\,dt.$$

**Step 3: Form the ratio and isolate the main term.**

Multiplying by $x/e^{-x^{2}/2}$:
$$\frac{x\,R(x)}{e^{-x^{2}/2}} = 1 - \frac{x\displaystyle\int_{x}^{\infty} \frac
{e^{-t^{2}/2}}{t^{2}}\,dt}{e^{-x^{2}/2}}.$$

So it suffices to show the remainder term tends to $0$.

**Step 4: Bound the remainder term.**

Since $t \geq x > 0$ throughout the domain of integration, $1/t^{2} \leq 1/x^{2}
$, hence
$$\int_{x}^{\infty} \frac{e^{-t^{2}/2}}{t^{2}}\,dt \leq \frac{1}{x^{2}}\int_{x}^
{\infty} e^{-t^{2}/2}\,dt = \frac{R(x)}{x^{2}}.$$

Therefore
$$0 \leq \frac{x\displaystyle\int_{x}^{\infty} \frac{e^{-t^{2}/2}}{t^{2}}\,dt}{e
^{-x^{2}/2}} \leq \frac{R(x)}{x\,e^{-x^{2}/2}}.$$

From Step 2, since the integral $\int_{x}^{\infty} \frac{e^{-t^{2}/2}}{t^{2}}\,d
t \geq 0$, we have the upper bound
$$R(x) \leq \frac{e^{-x^{2}/2}}{x},$$
so
$$\frac{R(x)}{x\,e^{-x^{2}/2}} \leq \frac{1}{x^{2}} \xrightarrow{x \to \infty} 0
.$$

By the squeeze theorem,
$$\lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{x\displaystyle\int_{x}^{\infty} \frac{e^{-t^{2}/2}}{
t^{2}}\,dt}{e^{-x^{2}/2}} = 0.$$

**Step 5: Conclude.**

Returning to the identity from Step 3:
$$L = 1 - 0 = 1.$$

Therefore
$$\boxed{\lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{\sqrt{2\pi}\, x - x\int_{-\infty}^{x} e^{-t^{
2}/2}\,dt}{\exp(-x^{2}/2)} = 1.}$$
