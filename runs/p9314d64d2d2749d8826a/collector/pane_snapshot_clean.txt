Looking at this problem, I need to find the minimum total successful inspections
 N across 2006 drones, where each drone inspects 6 sectors (each successful or n
ot), and for any 3 drones, the sum of their individual success counts is at leas
t 5.

## Setting Up the Problem

Let drone $i$ have $s_i$ successful sector inspections, where $0 \le s_i \le 6$
are integers. The constraint is:

$$s_i + s_j + s_k \ge 5 \quad \text{for all triples } \{i,j,k\}.$$

We want to minimize $N = \sum_{i=1}^{2006} s_i$.

**Key observation:** The constraint depends only on the counts $s_i$, not on *wh
ich* sectors succeed. The binding constraint is on the **three smallest** values
. Let $c_1 \le c_2 \le \cdots \le c_{2006}$ be the sorted counts. The condition
becomes:

$$c_1 + c_2 + c_3 \ge 5.$$

## Constructing an Upper Bound

Try the configuration: one drone with $s = 1$, and $2005$ drones with $s = 2$.

**Feasibility check:** The three smallest counts are $1, 2, 2$, with sum $= 5 \g
e 5$. ✓ Any other triple has sum $\ge 2+2+2 = 6 \ge 5$. ✓

$$N = 1 + 2005 \times 2 = 1 + 4010 = \boxed{4011}.$$

## Proving the Lower Bound $N \ge 4011$

Suppose for contradiction that $N \le 4010$.

**Case 1: All $s_i \ge 2$.** Then $N \ge 2006 \times 2 = 4012 > 4010$. Contradic
tion.

**Case 2: At least one drone has $s_i \le 1$.**

- **Subcase 2a: Some drone has $s = 0$.** For any two other drones $j, k$: $0 +
s_j + s_k \ge 5$, so $s_j + s_k \ge 5$ for all pairs among the remaining 2005 dr
ones. The two smallest among them must sum to $\ge 5$. If the smallest is $\le 2
$, then all others $\ge 3$ (since pairing the smallest with any other requires s
um $\ge 5$), giving $N \ge 0 + 2 + 2004 \times 3 = 6014 \gg 4010$. If both small
est $\ge 3$, then $N \ge 0 + 2005 \times 3 = 6015$. Either way, contradiction.

- **Subcase 2b: At least two drones have $s = 1$ (and none has $s=0$).** For any
 third drone $k$: $1 + 1 + s_k \ge 5$, so $s_k \ge 3$ for all remaining 2004 dro
nes. Then $N \ge 2 + 2004 \times 3 = 6014 \gg 4010$. Contradiction.

- **Subcase 2c: Exactly one drone has $s = 1$, all others $\ge 2$.** Then $N \ge
 1 + 2005 \times 2 = 4011 > 4010$. Contradiction.

All cases yield contradiction, so $N \ge 4011$.

## Conclusion

The lower bound $N \ge 4011$ and the achievable configuration $(1, 2, 2, \ldots,
 2)$ giving $N = 4011$ together establish:

$$\min N = \boxed{4011}.$$
