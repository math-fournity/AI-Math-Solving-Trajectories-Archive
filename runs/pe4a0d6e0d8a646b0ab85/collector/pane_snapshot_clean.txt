## Solution

**Step 1: Verify $-i$ lies inside $C$.**

The point $-i$ corresponds to $(x,y)=(0,-1)$. Substituting into the ellipse equa
tion:
$$\frac{0^2}{4}+\frac{(-1)^2}{9}=\frac{1}{9}<1,$$
so $-i$ is strictly inside $C$. ✓

**Step 2: Apply Cauchy's Integral Formula.**

Let $f(\zeta)=\zeta^2-\zeta+2$, which is entire. Since $z$ is inside $C$ and $f$
 is analytic on and inside $C$, Cauchy's integral formula gives
$$G(z)=\int_C \frac{f(\zeta)}{\zeta-z}\,d\zeta = 2\pi i\, f(z)=2\pi i\,(z^2-z+2)
.$$

**Step 3: Differentiate.**

$$G'(z)=2\pi i\,(2z-1),\qquad G''(z)=2\pi i\cdot 2=4\pi i.$$

**Step 4: Evaluate at $z=-i$.**

Since $G''(z)=4\pi i$ is constant,
$$G''(-i)=4\pi i.$$
