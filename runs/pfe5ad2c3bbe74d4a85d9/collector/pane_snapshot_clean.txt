## Solution

**Interpretation note:** Taken literally, $\inf_{g\in\mathcal{G}}|g(1/2)|=0$, si
nce $g\equiv 0\in\mathcal{G}$. The mathematically meaningful extremal problem he
re is the **supremum** $\sup_{g\in\mathcal{G}}|g(1/2)|$, which is the classical
"double Schwarz lemma" result. I solve that, and the answer is $\boxed{\tfrac14}
$.

---

### Proof that $\sup_{g\in\mathcal{G}}|g(1/2)|=\tfrac14$

Let $g\in\mathcal{G}$, so $g$ is holomorphic on $\mathbb{D}$, $g(\mathbb{D})\sub
seteq\mathbb{D}$, $g(0)=0$, and $g'(0)=0$.

**Step 1 (first Schwarz lemma).** Since $g(0)=0$ and $|g(z)|<1$ for $z\in\mathbb
{D}$, the Schwarz lemma gives
$$|g(z)|\le |z|,\qquad z\in\mathbb{D},$$
and $|g'(0)|\le 1$.

**Step 2 (define $\phi$).** Set
$$\phi(z)=\begin{cases}\dfrac{g(z)}{z},&z\neq 0,\\[4pt] g'(0)=0,&z=0.\end{cases}
$$
Because $g(0)=0$, the singularity at $0$ is removable, so $\phi$ is holomorphic
on $\mathbb{D}$. Moreover $\phi(0)=g'(0)=0$, and by Step 1, $|\phi(z)|=\left|\fr
ac{g(z)}{z}\right|\le 1$ for $z\neq 0$, hence $|\phi(z)|\le 1$ on $\mathbb{D}$.

**Step 3 (second Schwarz lemma).** Applying the Schwarz lemma to $\phi$ (which s
atisfies $\phi(0)=0$, $|\phi(z)|\le 1$) yields
$$|\phi(z)|\le |z|,\qquad z\in\mathbb{D}.$$

**Step 4 (conclusion).** Combining,
$$|g(z)|=|z|\,|\phi(z)|\le |z|\cdot|z|=|z|^{2}.$$
Evaluating at $z=\tfrac12$:
$$|g(\tfrac12)|\le \tfrac14.$$

**Step 5 (sharpness).** Equality in the Schwarz lemma for $\phi$ requires $\phi(
z)=e^{i\theta}z$ for some $\theta\in\mathbb{R}$, i.e. $g(z)=e^{i\theta}z^{2}$. T
his function belongs to $\mathcal{G}$: it is holomorphic, $|g(z)|=|z|^{2}<1$ on
$\mathbb{D}$, $g(0)=0$, $g'(0)=0$. For it,
$$|g(\tfrac12)|=\tfrac14.$$

Therefore
$$\sup_{g\in\mathcal{G}}|g(\tfrac12)|=\tfrac14.$$

(Literally, $\inf_{g\in\mathcal{G}}|g(1/2)|=0$, attained by $g\equiv 0$; the non
trivial extremal value is $\tfrac14$.)
