# Problem

In a futuristic laboratory, a rectangular sensor module measuring $3 \text{ cm} \times 4 \text{ cm} \times 6 \text{ cm}$ is placed on a flat, electromagnetic testing platform. The module is positioned such that one of its rectangular faces is in full contact with the platform surface.

A specialized laser array projects a beam of perfectly parallel light rays toward the module. These rays strike the platform at a fixed angle of $45^\circ$ relative to the horizontal surface. The projection of the module blocks the laser rays, creating a dark silhouette on the platform.

The lab technicians wish to orient the module—by choosing which face lies on the platform and how the module is rotated relative to the incoming light—to maximize the area of the silhouette cast outside the module's footprint.

Calculate the area of the largest possible shadow cast on the platform, excluding the area of the base of the box itself.
