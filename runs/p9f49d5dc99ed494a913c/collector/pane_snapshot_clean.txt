## Solution

**Setup.** Let $a=BC=14$, $b=CA=15$, $c=AB=13$. Place $B=(0,0)$, $C=(14,0)$. Fro
m $AB=13, AC=15$:
$$x_A^2+y_A^2=169,\quad (x_A-14)^2+y_A^2=225 \implies x_A=5,\; y_A=12.$$
So $A=(5,12)$. Heron: $s=21$, $K=\sqrt{21\cdot8\cdot7\cdot6}=84$, $R=\tfrac{abc}
{4K}=\tfrac{65}{8}$.

**Circumcenter.** Perpendicular bisector of $BC$ is $x=7$. From $|OB|^2=R^2$:
$$49+y_0^2=\tfrac{4225}{64}\implies y_0=\tfrac{33}{8}.\quad O=\left(7,\tfrac{33}
{8}\right).$$

**Point $X$** (midpoint of arc $BC$ containing $A$): lies on $x=7$, on the circu
mcircle, on the side of $A$. Since $49/4-33/8=65/8=R$:
$$X=\left(7,\tfrac{49}{4}\right).$$

**Point $L$.** Line through $X$ parallel to $AC$ (direction $(9,-12)$, slope $-4
/3$): parametrize $x=7+3t,\; y=\tfrac{49}{4}-4t$. Substituting into the circle $
(x-7)^2+(y-\tfrac{33}{8})^2=(\tfrac{65}{8})^2$:
$$9t^2+\left(\tfrac{65}{8}-4t\right)^2=\left(\tfrac{65}{8}\right)^2 \implies 25t
^2-65t=0.$$
So $t=\tfrac{13}{5}$ (besides $t=0$), giving
$$L=\left(\tfrac{74}{5},\,\tfrac{37}{20}\right).$$

**Point $J=BL\cap AC$.** Line $BL$ through origin: slope $=\tfrac{37/20}{74/5}=\
tfrac18$, i.e. $y=\tfrac{x}{8}$. Line $AC$: $y=-\tfrac43 x+\tfrac{56}{3}$. Setti
ng equal:
$$\tfrac{x}{8}=-\tfrac{4x}{3}+\tfrac{56}{3}\implies 35x=448\implies x=\tfrac{64}
{5},\; y=\tfrac{8}{5}.$$
$$J=\left(\tfrac{64}{5},\tfrac{8}{5}\right).$$

**Verification of concyclicity.** Incenter $I=\tfrac{14A+15B+13C}{42}=\left(\tfr
ac{70+182}{42},\tfrac{168}{42}\right)=(6,4)$. Circle through $B(0,0),C(14,0),I(6
,4)$: $x^2+y^2-14x+8y=0$. Check $J$:
$$\tfrac{4096}{25}+\tfrac{64}{25}-\tfrac{896}{5}+\tfrac{64}{5}=\tfrac{4160}{25}-
\tfrac{832}{5}=\tfrac{4160-4160}{25}=0.\;\checkmark$$
So $B,I,J,C$ are concyclic, confirming the hypothesis.

**Computing $\tfrac{BJ}{JC}$.**
$$BJ=\sqrt{\left(\tfrac{64}{5}\right)^2+\left(\tfrac{8}{5}\right)^2}=\tfrac{1}{5
}\sqrt{4160}=\tfrac{8\sqrt{65}}{5},$$
$$JC=\sqrt{\left(14-\tfrac{64}{5}\right)^2+\left(\tfrac{8}{5}\right)^2}=\sqrt{\l
eft(\tfrac65\right)^2+\left(\tfrac85\right)^2}=\tfrac{10}{5}=2.$$
Therefore
$$\frac{BJ}{JC}=\frac{8\sqrt{65}/5}{2}=\boxed{\frac{4\sqrt{65}}{5}}.$$

### PROOF COMPLETE
