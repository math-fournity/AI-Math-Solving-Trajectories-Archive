## Proof

We seek all positive integers $n$ with $v_2(n^{n-1}-1) = 2015$.

**$n$ must be odd.** If $n$ is even and $n\ge 2$, then $n^{n-1}$ is even, so $n^
{n-1}-1$ is odd and $v_2=0$. If $n=1$, then $n^{n-1}-1 = 1^0-1=0$, which is divi
sible by every power of $2$ (in particular by $2^{2016}$), so it is excluded. He
nce $n$ is odd and $n\ge 3$.

**Applying LTE.** For odd $n$, the exponent $n-1$ is even. By the Lifting-the-Ex
ponent lemma for $p=2$ (with odd base $n$ and even exponent $n-1$):
$$v_2(n^{n-1}-1) = v_2(n-1)+v_2(n+1)+v_2(n-1)-1 = 2\,v_2(n-1)+v_2(n+1)-1.$$

Let $a=v_2(n-1)$ and $b=v_2(n+1)$. Since $n$ is odd, $n-1$ and $n+1$ are consecu
tive even integers differing by $2$, so exactly one of $a,b$ equals $1$ and the
other is $\ge 2$.

We need
$$2a+b-1=2015,\quad\text{i.e.}\quad 2a+b=2016.$$

**Case $b=1$ (i.e. $n\equiv 1\pmod 4$).** Then $2a=2015$, which has no integer s
olution. Impossible.

**Case $a=1$ (i.e. $n\equiv 3\pmod 4$).** Then $b=2014$, so $v_2(n+1)=2014$. Thi
s is consistent: $v_2(n+1)\ge 2$ forces $n\equiv 3\pmod 4$, matching $a=1$.

**Verification of the LTE formula** with small cases:
- $n=3$: $3^2-1=8$, $v_2=3$; formula gives $2\cdot v_2(2)+v_2(4)-1=2+2-1=3$. ✓
- $n=7$: $7^6-1=117648=2^4\cdot 7353$; formula gives $2\cdot 1+3-1=4$. ✓
- $n=5$: $5^4-1=624=2^4\cdot 39$; formula gives $2\cdot 2+1-1=4$. ✓

**Characterizing the solutions.** The condition $v_2(n+1)=2014$ means
$$n+1 = 2^{2014}\cdot t,\quad t\ \text{odd positive integer},$$
i.e.
$$n = 2^{2014}\,t - 1,\quad t\in\{1,3,5,7,\dots\}.$$

**Consistency check.** For such $n$: $n-1 = 2^{2014}t-2 = 2(2^{2013}t-1)$. Since
 $t$ is odd and $2013\ge 1$, $2^{2013}t$ is even, so $2^{2013}t-1$ is odd, givin
g $v_2(n-1)=1$ as required. Also $n=2^{2014}t-1$ is odd (even minus one) and $n\
ge 2^{2014}-1\ge 3$, so $n$ is a valid positive odd integer. The formula then yi
elds
$$v_2(n^{n-1}-1)=2\cdot 1+2014-1=2015,$$
which is exactly the required valuation.

**Conclusion.** The positive integers $n$ for which $n^{n-1}-1$ is divisible by
$2^{2015}$ but not by $2^{2016}$ are precisely
$$\boxed{n = 2^{2014}(2k+1)-1,\quad k=0,1,2,3,\dots}$$
equivalently, all positive integers $n$ with $v_2(n+1)=2014$, i.e. $n\equiv 2^{2
014}-1\pmod{2^{2015}}$.
