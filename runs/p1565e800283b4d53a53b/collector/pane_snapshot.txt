## 证明

**第 1 步：化简递推式。**

将 $u_{n+1}$ 的表达式拆分：

$$u_{n+1} = \frac{u_n^{2015}-3u_n+1}{u_n^{2014}-u_n-1}.$$

注意到
$$u_n\cdot(u_n^{2014}-u_n-1) = u_n^{2015}-u_n^2-u_n,$$
所以
$$u_n^{2015}-3u_n+1 = u_n(u_n^{2014}-u_n-1) + (u_n^2-2u_n+1) = u_n(u_n^{2014}-u_
n-1)+(u_n-1)^2.$$

因此
$$u_{n+1} = u_n + \frac{(u_n-1)^2}{u_n^{2014}-u_n-1}. \tag{1}$$

**第 2 步：计算 $u_{n+1}-1$。**

$$u_{n+1}-1 = \frac{u_n^{2015}-3u_n+1-(u_n^{2014}-u_n-1)}{u_n^{2014}-u_n-1} = \f
rac{u_n^{2015}-u_n^{2014}-2u_n+2}{u_n^{2014}-u_n-1}.$$

分子因式分解：
$$u_n^{2015}-u_n^{2014}-2u_n+2 = u_n^{2014}(u_n-1)-2(u_n-1) = (u_n-1)(u_n^{2014}
-2).$$

故
$$u_{n+1}-1 = \frac{(u_n-1)(u_n^{2014}-2)}{u_n^{2014}-u_n-1}. \tag{2}$$

**第 3 步：建立望远镜恒等式。**

由 (2)，
$$\frac{1}{u_{n+1}-1} = \frac{u_n^{2014}-u_n-1}{(u_n-1)(u_n^{2014}-2)}.$$

于是
$$\frac{1}{u_n-1}-\frac{1}{u_{n+1}-1} = \frac{1}{u_n-1}\left(1-\frac{u_n^{2014}-
u_n-1}{u_n^{2014}-2}\right) = \frac{1}{u_n-1}\cdot\frac{(u_n^{2014}-2)-(u_n^{201
4}-u_n-1)}{u_n^{2014}-2}.$$

分子化简：$(u_n^{2014}-2)-(u_n^{2014}-u_n-1) = u_n-1$。因此

$$\frac{1}{u_n-1}-\frac{1}{u_{n+1}-1} = \frac{1}{u_n-1}\cdot\frac{u_n-1}{u_n^{20
14}-2} = \frac{1}{u_n^{2014}-2}. \tag{3}$$

**第 4 步：求和并取极限。**

由 (3)，
$$S_n = \sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac{1}{u_k^{2014}-2} = \sum_{k=1}^{n}\left(\frac{1}{u_k-
1}-\frac{1}{u_{k+1}-1}\right) = \frac{1}{u_1-1}-\frac{1}{u_{n+1}-1}.$$

由于 $u_1 = 2014$，故 $\frac{1}{u_1-1} = \frac{1}{2013}$。

**第 5 步：证明 $u_n\to\infty$。**

由 (1)，当 $u_n\geq 2$ 时，$u_n^{2014}-u_n-1>0$ 且 $(u_n-1)^2>0$，所以 $u_{n+1}>
u_n$。由于 $u_1=2014>2$，归纳得 $(u_n)$ 严格递增且恒 $\geq 2014$。

若 $(u_n)$ 收敛于有限极限 $L$，则由 (1) 取极限得 $(L-1)^2=0$，即 $L=1$，与 $L\ge
q 2014$ 矛盾。故 $u_n\to\infty$，从而 $\frac{1}{u_{n+1}-1}\to 0$。

**结论：**

$$\lim_{n\to\infty}S_n = \frac{1}{2013}-0 = \boxed{\dfrac{1}{2013}}.$$
