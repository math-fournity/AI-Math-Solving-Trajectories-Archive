# Problem

In a futuristic circular space station, there are 21 docking ports, numbered 1 through 21, arranged at perfectly equal intervals around the outer ring. These ports are labeled $P_1, P_2, P_3, \dots, P_{21}$ in clockwise order. At the exact geometric center of the station sits the Core Reactor, which occupies a single point $C$.

A specialized maintenance drone is programmed to establish a triangular energy shield by selecting three distinct docking ports and connecting them with laser beams. To ensure the stability of the station's gravity field, the three-port shield configuration must be designed such that the Core Reactor $C$ is strictly contained within the triangular area formed by the three beams.

A configuration is defined by the set of three port indices $\{i, j, k\}$, where $1 \leq i < j < k \leq 21$. How many different sets of three docking ports can be chosen to create a triangular shield that contains the Core Reactor $C$ in its interior?
