## Solution

**Setup.** The deck has 32 cards: for each $k = 1, 2, \ldots, 10$, three cards (
red, yellow, blue) each worth $2^k$ points; two jokers (big and small) each wort
h $2^0 = 1$ point. A "good" group is a subset of cards with total score $2004$.

**Binary expansion.** We compute:
$$2004 = 2^{10} + 2^9 + 2^8 + 2^7 + 2^6 + 2^4 + 2^2.$$
So the binary digits of $2004$ (from $2^0$ to $2^{10}$) are:
$$b_0=0,\; b_1=0,\; b_2=1,\; b_3=0,\; b_4=1,\; b_5=0,\; b_6=1,\; b_7=1,\; b_8=1,
\; b_9=1,\; b_{10}=1.$$

**Counting via digit-by-digit DP with carries.** Let $n_k$ be the number of card
s of value $2^k$ selected. Then:
- $n_0 \in \{0,1,2\}$ (2 jokers), with $\binom{2}{n_0}$ choices.
- $n_k \in \{0,1,2,3\}$ for $k=1,\ldots,10$ (3 colored cards each), with $\binom
{3}{n_k}$ choices.

We need $\sum_{k=0}^{10} n_k \cdot 2^k = 2004$. Processing from the least signif
icant bit, define the carry $c_0 = 0$, and at level $k$:
$$s_k = n_k + c_k, \quad s_k \equiv b_k \pmod{2}, \quad c_{k+1} = \frac{s_k - b_
k}{2}.$$
We require $c_{11} = 0$ (no overflow beyond $2^{10}$).

**Carry bound.** Since $n_k \le 3$ and $b_k \in \{0,1\}$, starting from $c_0 = 0
$, one checks inductively that $c_k \in \{0, 1, 2\}$ for all $k$: the maximum of
 $(n_k + c_k - b_k)/2$ with $n_k \le 3$, $c_k \le 2$, $b_k \ge 0$ is at most $(3
+2)/2 = 2$ (when $b_k=0$) or $(3+2-1)/2 = 2$ (when $b_k=1$).

**DP definition.** Let $f(k, c)$ = number of ways to complete levels $k, k+1, \l
dots, 10$ given carry $c$ into level $k$, ending with carry $0$. Base: $f(11, 0)
 = 1$, $f(11, c) = 0$ for $c \neq 0$.

Recurrence:
$$f(k, c) = \sum_{\substack{n \in \text{valid}(k) \\ (n+c) \equiv b_k \pmod{2}}}
 w(k, n) \cdot f\!\left(k+1,\; \frac{n+c-b_k}{2}\right),$$
where $w(0,n) = \binom{2}{n}$ and $w(k,n) = \binom{3}{n}$ for $k \ge 1$.

**Computation** (from $k=10$ down to $k=0$), with $w = [1,3,3,1]$ for $k \ge 1$:

| $k$ | $b_k$ | $f(k,0)$ | $f(k,1)$ | $f(k,2)$ |
|-----|--------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| 11  | —      | 1         | 0         | 0         |
| 10  | 1      | 3         | 1         | 0         |
| 9   | 1      | 10        | 6         | 3         |
| 8   | 1      | 36        | 28        | 21        |
| 7   | 1      | 136       | 120       | 105       |
| 6   | 1      | 528       | 496       | 465       |
| 5   | 0      | 2016      | 1953      | 1891      |
| 4   | 1      | 8001      | 7875      | 7750      |
| 3   | 0      | 31626     | 31375     | 31125     |
| 2   | 1      | 126253    | 125751    | 125250    |
| 1   | 0      | 503506    | 502503    | 501501    |

**Final step ($k=0$).** Here $b_0 = 0$, $c_0 = 0$, $n_0 \in \{0, 1, 2\}$ with $w
(0,n) = [1, 2, 1]$. We need $n_0$ even, so $n_0 \in \{0, 2\}$:
$$f(0, 0) = \underbrace{\binom{2}{0} \cdot f(1, 0)}_{n_0=0,\; c_1=0} + \underbra
ce{\binom{2}{2} \cdot f(1, 1)}_{n_0=2,\; c_1=1} = 1 \cdot 503506 + 1 \cdot 50250
3 = 1006009.$$

**Answer.** The number of "good" card groups is $\boxed{1006009}$.
