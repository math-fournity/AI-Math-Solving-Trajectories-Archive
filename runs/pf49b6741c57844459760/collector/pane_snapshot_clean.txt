## Proof

**Goal.** For $m \in \mathbb{N}$, bound $|\varphi^{-1}(m)|$, where $\varphi(S)=\
sum_{z\in S}|z|^2$ and $S$ ranges over finite subsets of $\mathbb{Z}^d$.

---

**Step 1. Every element of $S$ is bounded.**

Since $|z|^2 \geq 0$ for all $z\in\mathbb{Z}^d$ and $\varphi(S)=\sum_{z\in S}|z|
^2 = m$, every $z\in S$ satisfies
$$|z|^2 \leq m, \qquad\text{i.e.}\qquad |z_i|\le \sqrt{m}\ \text{for each coordi
nate }i.$$
Hence $S\subseteq B_m:=\{z\in\mathbb{Z}^d:|z|^2\le m\}$.

**Step 2. Size of $B_m$.**

$B_m$ is contained in the box $[-\sqrt m,\sqrt m]^d$, so
$$|B_m|\le (2\lfloor\sqrt m\rfloor+1)^d \le (2\sqrt m+1)^d \le (3\sqrt m)^d = 3^
d\, m^{d/2}, \qquad m\ge 1.$$

**Step 3. Counting subsets.**

Every $S\in\varphi^{-1}(m)$ is a subset of $B_m$, so
$$|\varphi^{-1}(m)|\le 2^{|B_m|}\le 2^{3^d\, m^{d/2}}=\exp\!\bigl(3^d\ln 2\cdot
m^{d/2}\bigr).$$

---

**Rough upper bound.**

$$\boxed{|\varphi^{-1}(m)|\le \exp\!\bigl(C_d\, m^{d/2}\bigr)}$$

for a constant $C_d$ depending only on $d$ (e.g. $C_d = 3^d\ln 2$). Equivalently
, $|\varphi^{-1}(m)|=2^{O(m^{d/2})}$.
