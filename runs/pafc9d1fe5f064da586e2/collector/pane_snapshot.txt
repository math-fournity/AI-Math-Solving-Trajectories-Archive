## Proof

**Setup.** Model the 5×5 zone as a grid of unit tiles occupying rows and columns
 $1,2,3,4,5$. The central tile is at position $(3,3)$. A $k\times k$ square alig
ned with tile boundaries is determined by its top-left corner $(r,c)$, where $1
\le r \le 6-k$ and $1 \le c \le 6-k$; the square then occupies rows $r,\dots,r+k
-1$ and columns $c,\dots,c+k-1$.

**Independence of the two axes.** A nested sequence is specified by four top-lef
t corners:
- Sector (4×4): $(r_1,c_1)$, with $r_1,c_1\in\{1,2\}$,
- Vault (3×3): $(r_2,c_2)$, with $r_1\le r_2\le r_1+1$ and $c_1\le c_2\le c_1+1$
,
- Safe (2×2): $(r_3,c_3)$, with $r_2\le r_3\le r_2+1$ and $c_2\le c_3\le c_2+1$,
- Lockbox (1×1): $(r_4,c_4)$, with $r_3\le r_4\le r_3+1$ and $c_3\le c_4\le c_3+
1$.

The row constraints involve only $r_1,r_2,r_3,r_4$ and the column constraints in
volve only $c_1,c_2,c_3,c_4$; the two sets of constraints are identical in form
and independent. The requirement that the Lockbox be the central tile is $(r_4,c
_4)=(3,3)$. Hence

$$\text{Total} = N_{\text{row}}(r_4=3)\cdot N_{\text{col}}(c_4=3) = N^2,$$

where $N$ is the number of valid row sequences $(r_1,r_2,r_3)$ producing $r_4=3$
.

**Counting $N$.** We need $r_4=3$ with $r_3\le 3\le r_3+1$, so $r_3\in\{2,3\}$.

- **$r_3=2$:** $r_2\in\{1,2\}$.
  - $r_2=1$: $r_1\in\{0,1\}\cap\{1,2\}=\{1\}$ → 1 sequence: $(1,1,2)$.
  - $r_2=2$: $r_1\in\{1,2\}\cap\{1,2\}=\{1,2\}$ → 2 sequences: $(1,2,2),(2,2,2)$
.

- **$r_3=3$:** $r_2\in\{2,3\}$.
  - $r_2=2$: $r_1\in\{1,2\}$ → 2 sequences: $(1,2,3),(2,2,3)$.
  - $r_2=3$: $r_1\in\{2,3\}\cap\{1,2\}=\{2\}$ → 1 sequence: $(2,3,3)$.

So $N = 1+2+2+1 = 6$.

**Verification via the full distribution.** Tracing all 16 unconstrained row seq
uences (2 choices at each of 4 nesting levels), the count of sequences yielding
each value of $r_4\in\{1,2,3,4,5\}$ is $1,4,6,4,1$ — the 4th row of Pascal's tri
angle, summing to $16=2^4$. ✓ This confirms $N_{\text{row}}(r_4=3)=6$.

**Final count.**

$$\text{Total} = 6 \times 6 = 36.$$

The number of distinct ways to choose the sequence of four nested squares is $\b
oxed{36}$.
