## Key identity: finite differences

For any polynomial $P(x)$ of degree exactly $n$ with leading coefficient $a_n$,
the $n$-th forward difference satisfies:

$$\Delta^n P(0) = \sum_{j=0}^{n}(-1)^{n-j}\binom{n}{j}P(j) = n!\cdot a_n.$$

Therefore:

$$\sum_{k=0}^{n}(-1)^k\binom{n}{k}P(k) = (-1)^n\cdot n!\cdot a_n.$$
