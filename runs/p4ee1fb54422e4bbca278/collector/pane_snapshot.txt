## Solution

**Setup.** We have 8 canisters of three types: 2 Oxygen (O), 4 Fuel (F), 2 Balla
st (B), initially in the order O, O, F, F, F, F, B, B. Since all three pairwise
swaps are allowed (O–F, F–B, B–O), **any two adjacent canisters of different typ
es can be swapped**. Canisters of the same type are indistinguishable, so a "dis
tinct sequence" is a distinct type-sequence (a word of length 8 over {O, F, B} w
ith 2 O's, 4 F's, 2 B's).

**Key claim: every multiset permutation is reachable.**

I prove this by giving a constructive bubbling algorithm. Process positions from
 left to right. Suppose positions 1, …, i−1 already match the target sequence, a
nd we want to place the correct type T at position i.

- If the canister at position i is already of type T, do nothing.
- Otherwise, find the **nearest** canister of type T to the right of (or at) pos
ition i. Call its position j > i.
- By "nearest," there is **no other canister of type T strictly between position
s i and j**. Therefore every canister in positions i, i+1, …, j−1 is of a type *
*different** from T.
- Bubble the canister at position j leftward to position i by repeatedly swappin
g it with its left neighbor. Each swap is between type T and a different type, s
o every swap is one of the three allowed operations.
- This places type T at position i without disturbing the already-fixed prefix (
positions 1, …, i−1 are all to the left of i and untouched).

Repeating for i = 1, 2, …, 8 transforms the initial sequence into any desired ta
rget sequence. Hence **every** type-sequence with the right multiplicities is re
achable.

**Counting.** The number of distinct sequences is the multinomial coefficient:

$$\frac{8!}{2!\cdot 4!\cdot 2!} = \frac{40320}{2 \cdot 24 \cdot 2} = \frac{40320
}{96} = 420.$$

**Answer: 420**
