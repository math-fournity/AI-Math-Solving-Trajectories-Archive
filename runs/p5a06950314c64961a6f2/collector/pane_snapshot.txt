## Proof

**Key fact.** For a multigraph with loops allowed, a sequence $(d_1, \dots, d_n)
$ of non-negative integers is realizable as a degree sequence if and only if $\s
um_{i=1}^n d_i$ is even.

*Necessity:* Every edge (including loops) contributes exactly 2 to the total deg
ree, so the sum is always even.

*Sufficiency:* If $\sum d_i$ is even, the number of odd $d_i$ is even. Pair up t
he odd-degree vertices and place one edge between each pair; now every degree is
 even. For each vertex $v_i$ with (now even) degree $d_i'$, place $d_i'/2$ loops
 on $v_i$. This yields a valid multigraph with loops.

**Computing the sum.** We need:
$$S = \sum_{i=1}^{k} \binom{2k-1}{i-1} = \sum_{j=0}^{k-1} \binom{2k-1}{j}.$$

Since $2k-1$ is odd, the binomial coefficients satisfy the symmetry $\binom{2k-1
}{j} = \binom{2k-1}{2k-1-j}$ with no unpaired central term (the midpoint $j = (2
k-1)/2$ is not an integer). Therefore the $2k$ coefficients split into two equal
 halves:

$$\sum_{j=0}^{k-1} \binom{2k-1}{j} = \sum_{j=k}^{2k-1} \binom{2k-1}{j}.$$

Using $\sum_{j=0}^{2k-1} \binom{2k-1}{j} = 2^{2k-1}$, we get:

$$S = \sum_{j=0}^{k-1} \binom{2k-1}{j} = \frac{2^{2k-1}}{2} = 2^{2k-2}.$$

**Parity.** For $k \ge 2$ (and in particular for $k \ge 20$), $2^{2k-2}$ is even
. All degrees $\binom{2k-1}{i-1}$ are non-negative integers.

**Conclusion.** Since the sum of degrees $S = 2^{2k-2}$ is even for every $k \ge
 20$, the degree sequence $(d_1, \dots, d_k) = \left(\binom{2k-1}{0}, \binom{2k-
1}{1}, \dots, \binom{2k-1}{k-1}\right)$ is realizable as a multigraph with loops
. **Yes, such a graph can be constructed for every $k \ge 20$.**
