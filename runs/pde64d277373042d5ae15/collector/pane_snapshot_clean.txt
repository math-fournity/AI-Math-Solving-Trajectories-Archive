## Solution

We seek all ordered pairs $(a,b)$ of positive integers satisfying
$$\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}=\frac{3}{2018}.$$

**Reformulation.** Clearing denominators gives $2018(a+b)=3ab$, i.e.
$$3ab-2018a-2018b=0.$$
Multiplying by $3$ and completing the product:
$$9ab-3\cdot 2018\,a-3\cdot 2018\,b=0 \implies (3a-2018)(3b-2018)=2018^2.$$

**Factoring $2018^2$.** Since $2018=2\cdot 1009$ and $1009$ is prime (it has no
prime divisor $\le \sqrt{1009}\approx 31.8$), we have
$$2018^2 = 2^2\cdot 1009^2.$$

**Congruence condition.** Set $d=3a-2018$ and $e=3b-2018$, so $de=2018^2$. Since
 $2018\equiv 2\pmod 3$, the requirement that $a,b$ be integers forces
$$d\equiv 1\pmod 3,\qquad e\equiv 1\pmod 3.$$
Also $a,b>0$ requires $d>-2018$ and $e>-2018$.

Since $de=2018^2>0$, the factors $d,e$ have the same sign.

**Case 1: $d,e>0$.** A divisor $d=2^i\cdot 1009^j$ ($i,j\in\{0,1,2\}$) satisfies
 $d\equiv 1\pmod 3$ iff $2^i\equiv 1\pmod 3$, i.e. $i\in\{0,2\}$. This gives $3\
cdot 2=6$ divisors:
$$1,\;1009,\;1009^2,\;4,\;4\cdot 1009,\;4\cdot 1009^2.$$
For each such $d$, the complementary factor $e=2018^2/d$ also satisfies $e\equiv
 1\pmod 3$ (since $2018^2\equiv 1\pmod 3$ and $d\equiv 1$). Recovering $(a,b)=\b
igl(\tfrac{d+2018}{3},\tfrac{e+2018}{3}\bigr)$:

| $d$ | $e$ | $a$ | $b$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $1$ | $2018^2$ | $673$ | $1358114$ |
| $1009$ | $4036$ | $1009$ | $2018$ |
| $1009^2$ | $4$ | $340033$ | $674$ |
| $4$ | $1009^2$ | $674$ | $340033$ |
| $4036$ | $1009$ | $2018$ | $1009$ |
| $2018^2$ | $1$ | $1358114$ | $673$ |

**Case 2: $d,e<0$.** Write $d=-d',\ e=-e'$ with $d',e'>0$ and $d'e'=2018^2$. The
n $d\equiv 1\pmod 3$ means $d'\equiv 2\pmod 3$, so $i=1$: the divisors $\equiv 2
\pmod 3$ are $2,\ 2018,\ 2\cdot 1009^2$. The positivity condition $a,b>0$ requir
es $d'<2018$ and $e'<2018$. But:
- $d'=2 \Rightarrow e'=2018^2/2=2036162>2018$;
- $d'=2018 \Rightarrow e'=2018$ (gives $a=0$, not positive);
- $d'=2036162>2018$.

So **no solutions** arise in this case.

**Verification.** Each pair above satisfies $\frac1a+\frac1b=\frac3{2018}$; for
instance, with $(a,b)=(673,1358114)$ and $1358114=2018\cdot 673$:
$$\frac1{673}+\frac1{2018\cdot 673}=\frac{2019}{2018\cdot 673}=\frac{3\cdot 673}
{2018\cdot 673}=\frac3{2018}.\ \checkmark$$
The remaining five are checked similarly (each reduces to $\frac{3}{2018}$).

**Answer.** The ordered pairs $(a,b)$ of positive integers satisfying $\frac1a+\
frac1b=\frac3{2018}$ are exactly
$$\boxed{(673,\,1358114),\ (1009,\,2018),\ (674,\,340033),\ (2018,\,1009),\ (340
033,\,674),\ (1358114,\,673)}.$$
