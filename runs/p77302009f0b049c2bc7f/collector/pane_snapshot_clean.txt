## Proof

Let $E = C^\infty(X)$ with its standard Fréchet topology, so $\mathcal{D}(X) = E
'$ and the weak* topology is $\sigma(E', E)$.

**Key properties of $E = C^\infty(X)$:**
- $E$ is a **nuclear Fréchet space** (classical result for $C^\infty$ on compact
 manifolds).
- $E$ is **separable** (projective limit of separable Banach spaces $C^k(X)$).
- $E$ is a **Montel space**: nuclear Fréchet ⟹ every closed bounded set is compa
ct; Fréchet ⟹ barreled; barreled + bounded sets relatively compact ⟹ Montel. Hen
ce $E$ is **reflexive**.

---

**Step 1: Characterization of weak*-continuous linear functionals.**

By the Mackey–Arens theorem, the continuous dual of $(E', \sigma(E', E))$ is exa
ctly $E$. That is, a linear functional $G: E' \to \mathbb{C}$ is weak*-continuou
s iff $G(u) = \langle u, \phi \rangle$ for some $\phi \in E$. So we must show $F
 \in E$.

---

**Step 2: $F$ is bounded on every equicontinuous subset of $E'$.**

Let $H \subset E'$ be equicontinuous. By the Alaoglu–Bourbaki theorem, $H$ is re
latively $\sigma(E',E)$-compact. Since $E$ is **separable**, the weak* topology
on the compact set $\overline{H}^{\sigma(E',E)}$ is **metrizable**: choosing a d
ense sequence $\{\phi_n\} \subset E$, the seminorms $p_n(u) = |u(\phi_n)|$ gener
ate $\sigma(E',E)|_{\overline{H}}$.

Thus $\overline{H}^{\sigma(E',E)}$ is a **compact metrizable** space. The restri
ction $F|_{\overline{H}}$ is sequentially continuous (inherited from $F$). On a
compact metrizable space, sequential continuity implies continuity. Hence $F|_{\
overline{H}}$ is continuous and bounded, giving $\sup_{u \in H} |F(u)| < \infty$
.

---

**Step 3: Equicontinuous = strongly bounded.**

Since $E$ is Fréchet, it is **barreled**. By the Banach–Steinhaus theorem (unifo
rm boundedness principle), a subset of $E'$ is equicontinuous iff it is **strong
ly bounded** (bounded for $\beta(E', E)$). Therefore $F$ maps every strongly bou
nded subset of $E'$ to a bounded subset of $\mathbb{C}$.

---

**Step 4: The strong dual $(E', \beta(E',E))$ is bornological.**

Since $E$ is a **nuclear Fréchet** space, its strong dual $(E', \beta(E',E))$ is
 a **Silva space** (a $(DFS)$-space): writing $E = \varprojlim E_n$ as a project
ive limit of Banach spaces with nuclear (hence compact) linking maps, the strong
 dual is $E'_\beta = \varinjlim E_n'$, an inductive limit of Banach spaces with
compact (hence the inductive limit is strict/regular) linking maps.

Silva spaces are **strict inductive limits of Banach spaces**, and strict induct
ive limits of bornological spaces are bornological. Since Banach spaces are born
ological, **$(E', \beta(E',E))$ is bornological**.

---

**Step 5: $F$ is strongly continuous, hence $F \in E$.**

In a bornological locally convex space, a linear functional is continuous iff it
 maps bounded sets to bounded sets. By Steps 3–4, $(E', \beta(E',E))$ is bornolo
gical and $F$ maps bounded sets to bounded sets. Therefore $F$ is continuous on
$(E', \beta(E',E))$, i.e.,

$$F \in (E', \beta(E',E))' = E'' \quad \text{(the strong bidual).}$$

Since $E$ is **reflexive** (Montel), the canonical embedding $E \hookrightarrow
E''$ is an isomorphism: $E'' = E$. Thus $F \in E = C^\infty(X)$, meaning there e
xists $\phi \in C^\infty(X)$ such that

$$F(u) = \langle u, \phi \rangle \quad \text{for all } u \in \mathcal{D}(X).$$

By Step 1, this is precisely weak*-continuity.

---

**Conclusion.** Every sequentially continuous linear functional $F: (\mathcal{D}
(X), \sigma(\mathcal{D}(X), C^\infty(X))) \to \mathbb{C}$ is continuous. The ans
wer is **yes**.

The essential ingredients are: (i) separability of $C^\infty(X)$ gives metrizabi
lity of equicontinuous weak*-compact sets, converting sequential continuity to b
oundedness on equicontinuous sets; (ii) nuclearity of $C^\infty(X)$ makes the st
rong dual a Silva (hence bornological) space and makes $C^\infty(X)$ reflexive,
allowing the passage from "bounded on bounded sets" to "strongly continuous" to
"element of $C^\infty(X)$."
