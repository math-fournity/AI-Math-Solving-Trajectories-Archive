## Solution

**Setup.** Track only the parity of the king's coordinates. The state is $(x \bm
od 2,\, y \bmod 2) \in \mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_2$, with four states. The
king starts at $(0,0)$.

**Step distribution on parities.** Each turn:
- Stay (prob $0.4$): both parities unchanged.
- Edge move (prob $0.1$ each, 4 directions): exactly one coordinate changes pari
ty. Left/right flip $x$ only (total $0.2$); up/down flip $y$ only (total $0.2$).
- Diagonal move (prob $0.05$ each, 4 directions): both coordinates change parity
 (total $0.2$).

So the parity-step $(\Delta x, \Delta y) \in \mathbb{Z}_2^2$ has distribution:
$$P(0,0)=\tfrac{2}{5},\quad P(1,0)=\tfrac{1}{5},\quad P(0,1)=\tfrac{1}{5},\quad
P(1,1)=\tfrac{1}{5}.$$

**Fourier analysis on $\mathbb{Z}_2\times\mathbb{Z}_2$.** The four characters ar
e $\chi_{u,v}(a,b)=(-1)^{ua+vb}$ for $(u,v)\in\{(0,0),(1,0),(0,1),(1,1)\}$. The
eigenvalue of the transition operator on character $\chi_{u,v}$ is
$$\lambda_{u,v}=\mathbb{E}\big[(-1)^{u\Delta x+v\Delta y}\big].$$

- $(u,v)=(0,0)$: $\lambda=1$.
- $(u,v)=(1,0)$: $\lambda=\tfrac{2}{5}\cdot1+\tfrac{1}{5}\cdot(-1)+\tfrac{1}{5}\
cdot1+\tfrac{1}{5}\cdot(-1)=\tfrac{2}{5}-\tfrac{1}{5}+\tfrac{1}{5}-\tfrac{1}{5}=
\tfrac{1}{5}$.
- $(u,v)=(0,1)$: by symmetry, $\lambda=\tfrac{1}{5}$.
- $(u,v)=(1,1)$: $\lambda=\tfrac{2}{5}\cdot1+\tfrac{1}{5}\cdot(-1)+\tfrac{1}{5}\
cdot(-1)+\tfrac{1}{5}\cdot1=\tfrac{2}{5}-\tfrac{1}{5}-\tfrac{1}{5}+\tfrac{1}{5}=
\tfrac{1}{5}$.

**Inversion formula.** For a random walk on a finite abelian group $G$ starting
at the identity, the probability of being at $g$ after $n$ steps is
$$P_n(g)=\frac{1}{|G|}\sum_{\chi}\chi(g)^{-1}\,\lambda_\chi^{\,n}.$$

For $g=(0,0)$, every $\chi(g)=1$, so
$$P_n\big((0,0)\big)=\frac{1}{4}\Big[1^n+\big(\tfrac{1}{5}\big)^n+\big(\tfrac{1}
{5}\big)^n+\big(\tfrac{1}{5}\big)^n\Big]=\frac{1}{4}+\frac{3}{4}\cdot\Big(\frac{
1}{5}\Big)^n.$$

**Verification.** For $n=0$: $P=\frac14+\frac34=1$ ✓ (king starts at even-even).
 For $n=1$: $P=\frac14+\frac{3}{20}=\frac25=0.4$, which equals the probability o
f staying put (the only way to remain on an even-even square after one move) ✓.

**Final answer.** Setting $n=2008$:
$$\boxed{P=\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{3}{4\cdot 5^{2008}}.}$$
