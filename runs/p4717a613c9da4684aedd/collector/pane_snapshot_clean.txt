## Solution

We compute $\int_M \omega$ where $\omega = x^4\,dy\wedge dz + y^4\,dz\wedge dx +
 z^4\,dx\wedge dy$ and $M$ is the sphere $x^2+y^2+z^2 = R^2$ (with the standard
outward orientation).

**Step 1: Apply the generalized Stokes / divergence theorem.**

Let $V = \{(x,y,z) : x^2+y^2+z^2 \le R^2\}$ be the closed ball bounded by $M$. B
y Stokes' theorem (equivalently, the divergence theorem for 2-forms),

$$\int_M \omega = \int_V d\omega.$$

**Step 2: Compute $d\omega$.**

$$d\omega = d(x^4)\wedge dy\wedge dz + d(y^4)\wedge dz\wedge dx + d(z^4)\wedge d
x\wedge dy.$$

Since $d(x^4) = 4x^3\,dx$, $d(y^4) = 4y^3\,dy$, $d(z^4) = 4z^3\,dz$:

$$d\omega = 4x^3\,dx\wedge dy\wedge dz + 4y^3\,dy\wedge dz\wedge dx + 4z^3\,dz\w
edge dx\wedge dy.$$

Using the cyclic permutation identities $dy\wedge dz\wedge dx = dx\wedge dy\wedg
e dz$ and $dz\wedge dx\wedge dy = dx\wedge dy\wedge dz$ (each is an even permuta
tion of $(dx,dy,dz)$):

$$d\omega = 4(x^3 + y^3 + z^3)\,dx\wedge dy\wedge dz.$$

**Step 3: Integrate over the ball.**

$$\int_M \omega = \int_V 4(x^3 + y^3 + z^3)\,dV = 4\int_V x^3\,dV + 4\int_V y^3\
,dV + 4\int_V z^3\,dV.$$

The ball $V$ is symmetric under the reflections $x \mapsto -x$, $y \mapsto -y$,
$z \mapsto -z$. Each integrand $x^3$, $y^3$, $z^3$ is an odd function of its res
pective variable over a symmetric domain. Therefore:

$$\int_V x^3\,dV = \int_V y^3\,dV = \int_V z^3\,dV = 0.$$

**Conclusion.**

$$\boxed{\int_M \omega = 0.}$$
