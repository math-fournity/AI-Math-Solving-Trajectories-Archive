# Problem

A circular offshore wind farm consists of 320 turbine foundation sites, spaced evenly around the perimeter of a large reef. To help the maintenance crews, the sites are assigned ID numbers in a repeating sequence: the first site is ID 1, the second is ID 2, continuing up to ID 8, then the sequence restarts at 1 for the ninth site, and so on, until all 320 sites are labeled.

The regional power authority plans to install high-voltage underwater cables to connect these sites. However, budget and safety regulations impose strict constraints:
1. A cable can only be laid between two sites if they share the exact same ID number.
2. No two cables may cross each other, as the electromagnetic interference would cause a failure.
3. Each turbine site has only one connection port; therefore, a site can be at most one endpoint of a single cable. (If two cables were to meet at the same site, they are considered to intersect).

Under these constraints, what is the maximum number of cables that can be installed across the reef?
