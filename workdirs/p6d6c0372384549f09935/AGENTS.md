# Problem

In a specialized logistics hub, high-capacity cargo ships are loaded with containers of varying weights. For any shipment consisting of a group of $n$ containers (where $n > 3$ is an integer), a security protocol requires identifying "balanced groupings." A balanced grouping is a non-empty subset of the containers whose total weight is exactly divisible by a specific integer $k$.

Let $d(n)$ be the maximum possible integer such that, regardless of the individual integer weights assigned to the $n$ containers in the shipment, there are always at least four distinct subsets of containers that satisfy the protocol for the divisor $d(n)$. Note that these four subsets must be unique, though they may share some of the same containers.

Your task is to calculate the total sum of these maximum divisors as the shipment size $n$ scales from 4 up to 100. Specifically, find the value of:
$$\sum_{n=4}^{100} d(n)$$
