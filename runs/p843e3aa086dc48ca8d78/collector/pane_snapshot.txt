## Solution

**Setup.** Label the chairs: Row A = {A1, A2, A3, A4}, Row B = {B1, B2, B3, B4},
 where (Ai, Bi) are opposite pairs (i = 1,…,4). "Next to each other" means adjac
ent along the same row, giving 6 adjacent pairs:
- Row A: (A1,A2), (A2,A3), (A3,A4)
- Row B: (B1,B2), (B2,B3), (B3,B4)

We want: (Vesna & Goran adjacent) **and** (Ana & Bane **not** opposite).

By inclusion–exclusion:

> **Desired count** = (arrangements with V&G adjacent) − (arrangements with V&G
adjacent **and** A&B opposite).

---

**Step 1: Arrangements with Vesna & Goran adjacent.**

Treat V&G as a single block. Choose one of 6 adjacent pairs, arrange V&G within
it (2 ways), then seat the remaining 6 people in 6! ways:

$$6 \times 2 \times 6! = 12 \times 720 = 8640.$$

---

**Step 2: Arrangements with V&G adjacent AND A&B opposite.**

First place A&B in an opposite pair (4 pairs × 2 arrangements = 8 ways). After r
emoving that opposite pair's two chairs, count how many adjacent pairs remain fo
r V&G. This depends on whether A&B occupy an **end** pair (P1 or P4) or a **midd
le** pair (P2 or P3):

| A&B pair removed | Remaining adjacent pairs |
|---|---|
| End pair P1=(A1,B1) | Row A: (A2,A3),(A3,A4); Row B: (B2,B3),(B3,B4) → **4** |
| End pair P4=(A4,B4) | Row A: (A1,A2),(A2,A3); Row B: (B1,B2),(B2,B3) → **4** |
| Middle pair P2=(A2,B2) | Row A: (A3,A4); Row B: (B3,B4) → **2** |
| Middle pair P3=(A3,B3) | Row A: (A1,A2); Row B: (B1,B2) → **2** |

- **End case** (2 pairs × 2 A&B arrangements = 4): V&G block has 4×2 = 8 placeme
nts; remaining 4 people in 4! = 24 ways.
  $$4 \times 8 \times 24 = 768.$$

- **Middle case** (2 pairs × 2 A&B arrangements = 4): V&G block has 2×2 = 4 plac
ements; remaining 4 people in 4! = 24 ways.
  $$4 \times 4 \times 24 = 384.$$

$$\text{Step 2 total} = 768 + 384 = 1152.$$

---

**Step 3: Final answer.**

$$8640 - 1152 = \boxed{7488}.$$
