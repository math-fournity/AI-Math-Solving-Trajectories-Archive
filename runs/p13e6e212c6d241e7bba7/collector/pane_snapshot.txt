## Proof

**Setup.** 14 players, round-robin. Total games = $\binom{14}{2}=91$, total poin
ts = 91. Let the scores in ranking order be $s_1 \ge s_2 \ge \cdots \ge s_{14}$
(ties broken by age). Jan's condition: $s_1+s_2+s_3 = s_6+s_7+\cdots+s_{14}$. Ca
ll this common value $S$.

**Key constraint: $S = 36$.**

The top 3 players play $\binom{3}{2}=3$ games among themselves, contributing exa
ctly 3 points to the top-3 group. They play $3 \times 11 = 33$ games against the
 other 11 players, contributing at most 33 points. So:
$$S = s_1+s_2+s_3 \le 3 + 33 = 36.$$

The bottom 9 players play $\binom{9}{2}=36$ games among themselves, contributing
 exactly 36 points to the bottom-9 group. They play $9 \times 5 = 45$ games agai
nst the other 5 players, contributing at least 0 points. So:
$$s_6+\cdots+s_{14} \ge 36.$$

Since Jan's condition requires these to be equal: $S \le 36$ and $S \ge 36$, for
cing $S = 36$.

**Consequence: all cross-group games are decisive.**

$S = 36$ means the top 3 achieve their maximum possible score: they win **all**
33 external games (against middle 2 and bottom 9). Likewise, the bottom 9 achiev
e their minimum: they lose **all** 45 external games (against top 3 and middle 2
).

The decisive cross-group games are:
- Top 3 vs Bottom 9: $3 \times 9 = 27$ games (top 3 win all),
- Top 3 vs Middle 2: $3 \times 2 = 6$ games (top 3 win all),
- Middle 2 vs Bottom 9: $2 \times 9 = 18$ games (middle 2 win all).

Total decisive cross-group games: $27 + 6 + 18 = 51$.

**Within-group games can all be ties.** The remaining 40 games are within groups
:
- Top 3 internal: $\binom{3}{2} = 3$ games,
- Middle 2 internal: $\binom{2}{2} = 1$ game,
- Bottom 9 internal: $\binom{9}{2} = 36$ games.

These are independent of the cross-group games. Setting all 40 to ties gives a v
alid tournament with scores:
- Each top player: 11 wins + 2 ties = $11 + 1 = 12$,
- Each middle player: 9 wins + 1 tie + 3 losses = $9 + 0.5 = 9.5$,
- Each bottom player: 0 wins + 8 ties + 5 losses = $0 + 4 = 4$.

Verification: $3 \times 12 + 2 \times 9.5 + 9 \times 4 = 36 + 19 + 36 = 91$ ✓. T
op 3 sum = 36 = bottom 9 sum ✓. Ranking: $12, 12, 12, 9.5, 9.5, 4, \ldots, 4$ —
top 3, middle 2, bottom 9 are cleanly separated ✓.

**Maximality.** The 51 cross-group games are forced to be decisive by $S=36$. On
ly the 40 within-group games can be ties, and we showed all 40 can simultaneousl
y be ties. Since no cross-group game can be a tie, the number of ties cannot exc
eed 40.

**Verification of realizability (Landau's condition).** Ascending scores: $4,4,\
ldots,4$ (nine), $9.5, 9.5$, $12, 12, 12$. Check $\sum_{i=1}^k a_i \ge \binom{k}
{2}$:
- $k=9$: $36 \ge 36$ ✓ (equality),
- $k=11$: $55 \ge 55$ ✓ (equality),
- $k=14$: $91 = 91$ ✓,
- All intermediate $k$ satisfied.

The explicit construction above confirms realizability.

**Conclusion.** The maximum number of ties is $\boxed{40}$.
