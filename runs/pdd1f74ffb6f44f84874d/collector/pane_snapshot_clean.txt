## Solution

Let the natural number be $n = 10x + 6$, where $x$ is the number formed by all d
igits except the final 6. If $x$ has $k$ digits, then moving the 6 to the front
gives the number $6 \cdot 10^k + x$.

By condition (b):
$$4n = 6 \cdot 10^k + x, \qquad n = 10x + 6.$$

Substituting:
$$4(10x + 6) = 6 \cdot 10^k + x$$
$$40x + 24 = 6 \cdot 10^k + x$$
$$39x = 6 \cdot 10^k - 24 = 6(10^k - 4)$$
$$x = \frac{2(10^k - 4)}{13}.$$

For $x$ to be a natural number, we need $13 \mid (10^k - 4)$, i.e. $10^k \equiv
4 \pmod{13}$.

Computing powers of 10 modulo 13:
- $10^1 \equiv 10$
- $10^2 \equiv 100 \equiv 9$
- $10^3 \equiv 90 \equiv 12$
- $10^4 \equiv 120 \equiv 3$
- $10^5 \equiv 30 \equiv 4 \pmod{13}$ ✓

So the smallest valid $k$ is $k = 5$. Then:
$$x = \frac{2(100000 - 4)}{13} = \frac{199992}{13} = 15384.$$

Indeed $x = 15384$ has exactly 5 digits, consistent with $k = 5$.

Therefore:
$$n = 10x + 6 = 153846.$$

**Verification.** Moving the last digit 6 to the front gives $615384$, and $4 \t
imes 153846 = 615384$. ✓

**Minimality.** Any solution must have $k$ digits in $x$ with $10^k \equiv 4 \pm
od{13}$. The smallest such $k$ is 5, and the resulting $x = 15384$ indeed has 5
digits, so no smaller $k$ (hence no shorter, hence no smaller $n$) is possible.

$$\boxed{n = 153846}$$
