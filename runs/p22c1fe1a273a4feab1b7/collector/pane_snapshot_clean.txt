## Construction

$$f'(x) = \frac{\sin(x^2)}{x\log x}.$$

**Verification of the decay condition.** We have $|x|\,|f'(x)| = \frac{|\sin(x^2

**Conditional convergence of $\int f'$.** Consider $\int_2^R \frac{\sin(t^2)}{t\
log t}\,dt$. Substituting $u = t^2$, $du = 2t\,dt$, $t\log t = \sqrt{u}\cdot\tfr
ac{1}{2}\log u$:
$$\int_2^R \frac{\sin(t^2)}{t\log t}\,dt = \int_4^{R^2} \frac{\sin u}{u\log u}\,
du.$$
Since $\frac{1}{u\log u}$ is positive, decreasing, and tends to $0$ for $u > e$,
 and $\int_a^b \sin u\,du$ is bounded, **Dirichlet's test** gives convergence of
 $\int_4^\infty \frac{\sin u}{u\log u}\,du$. Hence $\int_2^\infty f'(t)\,dt$ con
verges.

**Define $f$ for $x \geq 2$:**
$$f(x) = -\int_x^\infty \frac{\sin(t^2)}{t\log t}\,dt.$$
Then $f'(x) = \frac{\sin(x^2)}{x\log x}$ and $f(x) \to 0$ as $x\to\infty$ (tail
of a convergent integral). Thus $|f(x)| + |x||f'(x)| \to 0$ as $x\to+\infty$. ✓

**$f' \notin L^1$ on $[2,\infty)$.** We show $\int_2^\infty |f'(x)|\,dx = \infty

$$\int_2^\infty |f'(x)|\,dx = \int_4^\infty \frac{|\sin u|}{u\log u}\,du.$$
On each interval $[n\pi, (n+1)\pi]$, we have $\int_{n\pi}^{(n+1)\pi}|\sin u|\,du

$$\int_{n\pi}^{(n+1)\pi}\frac{|\sin u|}{u\log u}\,du \geq \frac{2}{(n+1)\pi\log(

Summing over $n$:
$$\int_4^\infty \frac{|\sin u|}{u\log u}\,du \geq \sum_{n=2}^\infty \frac{2}{(n+
1)\pi\log((n+1)\pi)} = \infty,$$
since $\sum \frac{1}{n\log n} = \infty$. Therefore $f' \notin L^1([2,\infty))$,
hence $f' \notin L^1(\mathbb{R})$.

The function $f \in C^1(\mathbb{R})$ satisfies $|f(x)| + |x||f'(x)| \to 0$ as $|

Therefore, it is **not necessary** that $f'(x) \in L^1(\mathbb{R})$.
