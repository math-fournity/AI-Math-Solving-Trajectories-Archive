## Solution

**The operator.** The kernel is $\cos(x)\cos(y)+\sin(x)\sin(y)=\cos(x-y)$, so
$$(Af)(x)=\frac{1}{\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}\cos(x-y)\,f(y)\,dy.$$
Writing $Af=\frac{1}{\pi}\big[\cos x\,\langle\cos,f\rangle+\sin x\,\langle\sin,f
\rangle\big]$, we see $A$ is a rank-2 operator whose range is $V=\operatorname{s
pan}\{\cos,\sin\}$.

**Inner products.** On $L^2(0,2\pi)$:
$$\langle\cos,\cos\rangle=\pi,\qquad \langle\sin,\sin\rangle=\pi,\qquad \langle\
cos,\sin\rangle=0.$$
So $\{\cos,\sin\}$ are orthogonal, each of norm $\sqrt\pi$.

**Action on $V$.**
$$A(\cos)=\tfrac{1}{\pi}\big[\cos\cdot\pi+\sin\cdot 0\big]=\cos,\qquad A(\sin)=\
tfrac{1}{\pi}\big[\cos\cdot 0+\sin\cdot\pi\big]=\sin.$$
Thus $A|_V=\mathrm{Id}_V$.

**Action on $V^\perp$.** Since $\operatorname{Range}(A)\subseteq V$, for any $g\
in V^\perp$ we have $Ag=0$. Hence $A|_{V^\perp}=0$.

**$A$ is an orthogonal projection.** With $L^2(0,2\pi)=V\oplus V^\perp$ and $A=\
mathrm{Id}_V\oplus 0$, we get $A^2=A$. The spectrum is $\sigma(A)=\{0,1\}$, with
 $1$ of multiplicity $2$ and $0$ of infinite multiplicity. The spectral radius i
s $\rho(A)=1$.

**Powers.** Since $A$ is a projection, $A^n=A$ for all $n\ge 1$, and $\|A^n\|=\|
A\|=1$ for $n\ge 1$.

**Absolute convergence.** The series $\sum_{n=0}^\infty z^n A^n$ converges absol
utely in operator norm iff
$$\sum_{n=0}^\infty |z|^n\,\|A^n\|=\|I\|+\sum_{n=1}^\infty |z|^n\cdot\|A\|=1+\su
m_{n=1}^\infty |z|^n<\infty.$$
The geometric series $\sum_{n=1}^\infty |z|^n$ converges iff $|z|<1$.

**Boundary check.** At $|z|=1$, $\sum_{n=1}^\infty |z|^n=\sum 1=\infty$, so no a
bsolute convergence on the unit circle.

**Closed form (verification).** For $|z|<1$:
$$\sum_{n=0}^\infty z^n A^n=I+\sum_{n=1}^\infty z^n A=I+\frac{z}{1-z}\,A=(I-zA)^
{-1},$$
consistent with the Neumann series, since $\rho(zA)=|z|\cdot\rho(A)=|z|<1$.

## Answer

The series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} z^n A^n$ converges absolutely preci
sely for
$$\boxed{|z|<1},$$
i.e., for $z$ in the open unit disk of $\mathbb{C}$.
