## Proof

**Setup.** Let $f:\mathbb{R}^n \times \mathbb{R}^m \to \mathbb{R}$ be convex in
$(x,z)$, and define the partial minimization
$$g(x) = \inf_z f(x,z).$$
Since $f$ is jointly convex in $(x,z)$, partial minimization over $z$ preserves
convexity, so $g$ is convex and its (convex) conjugate is well defined:
$$g^*(y) = \sup_x \{\,y^\top x - g(x)\,\}.$$

**Computation.** Substitute the definition of $g$:
$$g^*(y) = \sup_x \bigl\{\,y^\top x - \inf_z f(x,z)\,\bigr\}.$$

Using $\inf_z f(x,z) = -\sup_z\{-f(x,z)\}$, the expression inside the supremum b
ecomes
$$y^\top x - \inf_z f(x,z) = y^\top x + \sup_z\{-f(x,z)\} = \sup_z\{\,y^\top x -
 f(x,z)\,\}.$$

Therefore
$$g^*(y) = \sup_x \sup_z \{\,y^\top x - f(x,z)\,\} = \sup_{x,z}\{\,y^\top x - f(
x,z)\,\},$$
where the last equality uses the general fact that iterated suprema commute: $\s
up_x\sup_z = \sup_{x,z}$.

**Relating to $f^*$.** The conjugate of $f$ (viewed as a function of the joint v
ariable $(x,z)$) is
$$f^*(y,s) = \sup_{x,z}\{\,y^\top x + s^\top z - f(x,z)\,\}.$$

Setting $s = 0$:
$$f^*(y,0) = \sup_{x,z}\{\,y^\top x + 0^\top z - f(x,z)\,\} = \sup_{x,z}\{\,y^\t
op x - f(x,z)\,\}.$$

Comparing with the expression for $g^*(y)$ obtained above, we conclude
$$\boxed{\,g^*(y) = f^*(y,0)\,}.$$

**Remark.** The joint convexity of $f$ in $(x,z)$ is the essential hypothesis: i
t guarantees (via partial minimization) that $g$ is convex, so its conjugate is
the standard convex conjugate, and it ensures the geometric interpretation—$g^*(
y)$ is the support function of the projection onto the $x$-space of the epigraph
 of $f$, which is exactly the slice of $f^*$ at $s=0$.
