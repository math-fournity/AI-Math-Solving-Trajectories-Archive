The drone is programmed with a triple-redundancy safety protocol. This protocol monitors moisture levels that remain stable after undergoing three consecutive treatment cycles. Specifically, the system identifies all distinct real values of $x$ such that the moisture level after three cycles is identical to the moisture level after just one cycle. This relationship is defined by the equilibrium equation:
$$f(f(f(x))) = f(x)$$

The manufacturer’s technical specifications state that for this specific drone model to operate at peak efficiency, there must be exactly four distinct moisture levels that satisfy this triple-cycle equilibrium.
