The proof uses Chevalley's theorem to construct a finite-dimensional rational re

**Setting up the preimage.** Define $H = \pi^{-1}(U)$, a closed normal subgroup

$$1 \to A \to H \xrightarrow{\pi} U \to 1.$$

_u(H^0) \subseteq R_u(B) = \{e\}$ (since $B$ is reductive). Therefore $R_u(H^0)

**Contradiction.** But we showed $\pi(H^0) = U$. Therefore $U = \{e\}$, i.e., $R
