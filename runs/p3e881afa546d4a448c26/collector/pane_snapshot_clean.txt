## Solution

We compute the pullback $f^*\omega$ and integrate over $D=[0,1]\times[0,1]$.

**Setup.** With $f(u,v)=(u,v,u^2+v^2+1)$, we identify
$$x=u,\quad y=v,\quad z=u^2+v^2+1.$$

The differentials pull back as:
$$dx=du,\quad dy=dv,\quad dz=2u\,du+2v\,dv.$$

**Compute each wedge product.**

$$dy\wedge dz = dv\wedge(2u\,du+2v\,dv)=2u\,dv\wedge du+2v\,dv\wedge dv=-2u\,du\
wedge dv.$$

$$dx\wedge dz = du\wedge(2u\,du+2v\,dv)=2u\,du\wedge du+2v\,du\wedge dv=2v\,du\w
edge dv.$$

**Pull back $\omega = y\,dy\wedge dz + xz\,dx\wedge dz$:**

$$f^*\omega = v\cdot(-2u\,du\wedge dv) + u(u^2+v^2+1)\cdot(2v\,du\wedge dv)$$
$$= \bigl[-2uv + 2uv(u^2+v^2+1)\bigr]\,du\wedge dv$$
$$= 2uv\bigl[-1+(u^2+v^2+1)\bigr]\,du\wedge dv$$
$$= 2uv(u^2+v^2)\,du\wedge dv.$$

**Integrate over $D$:**

$$\int_D f^*\omega = \int_0^1\!\!\int_0^1 2uv(u^2+v^2)\,du\,dv = 2\int_0^1\!\!\i
nt_0^1 (u^3v+uv^3)\,du\,dv.$$

Separating:

$$2\left[\int_0^1 u^3\,du\int_0^1 v\,dv + \int_0^1 u\,du\int_0^1 v^3\,dv\right]
= 2\left[\frac14\cdot\frac12 + \frac12\cdot\frac14\right]$$
$$= 2\left[\frac18+\frac18\right] = 2\cdot\frac14 = \frac12.$$

$$\boxed{\displaystyle\int_D f^*\omega = \frac12}$$
