**Setup.** Let the meteorites' speed be $v$ and the ship's speed be $u$, both me

The ship meets:
- **Oncoming** meteorites (opposite direction) every $7\text{ s}$: relative spee
d $= v + u$, so
$$\frac{d}{v+u} = 7. \tag{1}$$
- **Same-direction** meteorites every $13\text{ s}$: relative speed $= v - u$ (a
ssuming $v > u$), so
$$\frac{d}{v-u} = 13. \tag{2}$$

**Goal.** If the ship stood still, the time between meteorites passing would be
$$T = \frac{d}{v}.$$

**Solving.** From (1) and (2):
$$v + u = \frac{d}{7}, \qquad v - u = \frac{d}{13}.$$

Adding the two equations:
$$2v = \frac{d}{7} + \frac{d}{13} = d\left(\frac{1}{7} + \frac{1}{13}\right) = d
\cdot\frac{20}{91},$$

$$v = \frac{10d}{91}.$$

Therefore
$$T = \frac{d}{v} = \frac{91}{10} = 9.1\text{ s}.$$
