$$\|m\| = \sup_{\substack{\|\xi\|_2=1\\\|\eta\|_2=1}} |\langle m\xi,\eta\rangle|
 = \sup_{\substack{\|\xi\|_2=1\\\|\eta\|_2=1}} \left|\sum_{i,j}\tau(u_{i,j})\,\x
i_j\,\overline{\eta_i}\right| = \sup_{\substack{\|\xi\|_2=1\\\|\eta\|_2=1}} \lef
t|\tau\!\left(\sum_{i,j} u_{i,j}\,\xi_j\,\overline{\eta_i}\right)\right|.$$

Fix unit vectors $\xi,\eta\in\mathbb{C}^n$ and set
$$a \;=\; \sum_{i,j} u_{i,j}\,\xi_j\,\overline{\eta_i} \;\in\; A.$$

**Key estimate: $\|a\|\le 1$.**

Let $\pi:A\to B(H)$ be a faithful $*$-representation on a Hilbert space $H$. Thi
s induces a faithful representation $\pi^{(n)}:M_n(A)\to B(H^n)$ via $\pi^{(n)}(
[x_{i,j}]) = [\pi(x_{i,j})]$. Since $u$ is unitary in $M_n(A)$, $\pi^{(n)}(u)$ i
s unitary in $B(H^n)$, hence $\|\pi^{(n)}(u)\|=1$.

Define isometries $V_\xi, V_\eta : H \to H^n$ by $(V_\xi h)_i = \xi_i\, h$ and $
(V_\eta h)_i = \eta_i\, h$. One checks $\|V_\xi\|=\|V_\eta\|=1$ (using $\|\xi\|_
2=\|\eta\|_2=1$) and that the adjoint $V_\eta^*:H^n\to H$ is given by $V_\eta^*(
h_1,\dots,h_n)=\sum_i \overline{\eta_i}\,h_i$.

A direct computation gives
$$\pi(a) \;=\; \sum_{i,j}\pi(u_{i,j})\,\xi_j\,\overline{\eta_i} \;=\; V_\eta^*\;
\pi^{(n)}(u)\;V_\xi.$$

Therefore
$$\|a\| \;=\; \|\pi(a)\| \;\le\; \|V_\eta^*\|\;\|\pi^{(n)}(u)\|\;\|V_\xi\| \;=\;
 1\cdot 1\cdot 1 \;=\; 1.$$

**Conclusion of the bound.** Since $|\tau(a)|\le \|\tau\|\,\|a\|\le \|\tau\|$, t
aking the supremum over all unit $\xi,\eta$ yields
$$\boxed{\|m\|\le \|\tau\|.}$$

**Sharpness.** Take $A=\mathbb{C}$ and $\tau(z)=cz$ with $|c|=\|\tau\|$. Let $u\

$\|m\|=|c|\,\|u\|=|c|=\|\tau\|$. The bound is attained.
