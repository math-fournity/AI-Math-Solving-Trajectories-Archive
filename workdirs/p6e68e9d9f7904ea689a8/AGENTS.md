# Problem

A team of civil engineers is tasked with designing a modular platform supported by $n$ distinct structural pillars. To ensure the platform’s stability, they have established a "Stress Index" formula based on the height of each pillar, where the heights $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n$ must be distinct positive integers.

The Stress Index is calculated by taking the square of the sum of all pillar heights, subtracting exactly 2025 units (representing a fixed structural offset), and then dividing that result by the sum of the squares of the individual pillar heights. 

The design is only considered "Perfectly Balanced" if the resulting Stress Index is a positive integer.

What is the smallest number of pillars $n \in \mathbb{N}$ that the engineers can use to achieve a Perfectly Balanced design?
