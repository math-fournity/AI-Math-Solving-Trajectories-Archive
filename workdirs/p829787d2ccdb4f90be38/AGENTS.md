# Problem

In a remote industrial complex, two engineers, Helena and Silas, are testing a sequence of four power cycles for their respective experimental generators. Each engineer has a fuel supply consisting of eight canisters: four containing Grade-R (Red) fuel and four containing Grade-B (Black) fuel. 

For each of the four cycles, Helena and Silas must independently and randomly select two canisters from their own remaining supply to consume. In any given cycle, an engineer’s pair of canisters can result in one of three possible combinations: two Grade-B canisters, two Grade-R canisters, or one canister of each grade.

The testing process is considered "synchronized" if, for every one of the four cycles, the combination of canisters chosen by Helena is identical to the combination chosen by Silas. 

The probability that Helena and Silas achieve synchronization for all four cycles can be expressed as a simplified fraction $\frac{m}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are coprime positive integers. Calculate the value of $100m + n$.
