Looking at this problem, I need to evaluate:

$$\lim_{x\to +\infty}\frac{\zeta\left(1+\frac{1}{x}\right)}{\Gamma(x)}$$

## Analysis

**Numerator — behavior of $\zeta(1+1/x)$ as $x\to\infty$:**

As $x\to+\infty$, we have $1/x \to 0^+$, so $1+1/x \to 1^+$. The Riemann zeta fu
nction has a simple pole at $s=1$ with Laurent expansion

$$\zeta(s) = \frac{1}{s-1} + \gamma + O(s-1) \quad \text{as } s\to 1,$$

where $\gamma$ is the Euler–Mascheroni constant. Substituting $s = 1+1/x$:

$$\zeta\!\left(1+\frac{1}{x}\right) = x + \gamma + O\!\left(\frac{1}{x}\right).$
$

So the numerator grows **linearly** in $x$.

A rigorous bound (no need for the Laurent expansion): for $s>1$, by comparison o
f the series $\zeta(s)=\sum_{n\geq 1}n^{-s}$ with the integral $\int_1^\infty t^
{-s}\,dt = \frac{1}{s-1}$,

$$\frac{1}{s-1} \;\leq\; \zeta(s) \;\leq\; \frac{1}{s-1}+1.$$

With $s=1+1/x$ this gives

$$x \;\leq\; \zeta\!\left(1+\tfrac{1}{x}\right) \;\leq\; x+1. \tag{1}$$

**Denominator — behavior of $\Gamma(x)$ as $x\to\infty$:**

By Stirling's formula,

$$\Gamma(x) = \sqrt{\frac{2\pi}{x}}\left(\frac{x}{e}\right)^{\!x}\!\left(1+O\!\l
eft(\tfrac{1}{x}\right)\right),$$

which grows **super-exponentially**. In particular $\Gamma(x)\geq C\,(x/e)^{x}$
for some constant $C>0$ and all $x$ large enough, so $\Gamma(x)\to\infty$ faster
 than any power of $x$.

**Combining — squeeze theorem:**

Since $\zeta(1+1/x)>0$ and $\Gamma(x)>0$ for $x>0$, using the upper bound from (
1):

$$0 \;\leq\; \frac{\zeta\!\left(1+\frac{1}{x}\right)}{\Gamma(x)} \;\leq\; \frac{
x+1}{\Gamma(x)}.$$

It remains to show $\frac{x+1}{\Gamma(x)}\to 0$. From Stirling's formula,

$$\frac{x+1}{\Gamma(x)} \;\sim\; \frac{(x+1)\sqrt{x}}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\cdot\frac{e^{
x}}{x^{x}} \;=\; \frac{(x+1)\sqrt{x}}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\cdot\left(\frac{e}{x}\right)^
{\!x}.$$

For $x>e$, the factor $(e/x)^{x}$ decays super-exponentially (it is $\exp(-x\ln(
x/e))$ with $\ln(x/e)>0$), which overwhelms the polynomial prefactor $(x+1)\sqrt
{x}$. Hence

$$\lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{x+1}{\Gamma(x)} = 0.$$

By the squeeze theorem,

$$\boxed{\lim_{x\to +\infty}\frac{\zeta\!\left(1+\frac{1}{x}\right)}{\Gamma(x)}
= 0.}$$
