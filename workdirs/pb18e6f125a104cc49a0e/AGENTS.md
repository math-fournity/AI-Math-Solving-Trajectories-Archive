# Problem

In a bustling coastal district, a grid of $9 \times 9$ identical warehouses is being monitored for a specific logistical safety protocol. To maintain security, a warehouse may be designated as a "High-Security Hub."

The safety protocol is governed by a single, strict operational rule: if a warehouse is surrounded by an even number of neighboring High-Security Hubs, then that warehouse itself must also be designated as a High-Security Hub.

For this protocol, "neighbors" are defined as any warehouses sharing a wall with the warehouse in question (warehouses in the interior have four neighbors, those on the edges have three, and those at the corners have two).

Given these conditions, what is the minimum possible number of warehouses that must be designated as High-Security Hubs across the entire $9 \times 9$ district?
