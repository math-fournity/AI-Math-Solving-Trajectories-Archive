## Solution

**Setting up coordinates.** Let the regular tetrahedron have edge length $s$. Pl
ace the centroid $O$ of $\triangle BCD$ at the origin, with $B,C,D$ lying on a c
ircle of radius $r = s/\sqrt{3}$ in the plane $z=0$, and $A$ at $(0,0,h)$ where
$h = s\sqrt{2/3}$ is the height of the tetrahedron.

**Using symmetry.** The line $AO$ is the 3-fold symmetry axis of the tetrahedron
, so for any point $P$ on $AO$ we have $PB = PC = PD$. Writing $P = (0,0,t)$, th
e objective becomes
$$f(t) = PA + 2(PB+PC+PD) = (h - t) + 6\sqrt{r^2 + t^2}, \qquad t\in[0,h].$$

**Minimizing.** Differentiate:
$$f'(t) = -1 + \frac{6t}{\sqrt{r^2+t^2}} = 0 \implies 6t = \sqrt{r^2+t^2} \impli
es 36t^2 = r^2+t^2 \implies 35t^2 = r^2,$$
so $t = r/\sqrt{35}$. (This lies in $(0,h)$ since $t/h = 1/\sqrt{70}<1$, confirm
ing an interior minimum.)

**Computing $\sin\angle PBO$.** Place $B = (r,0,0)$, $O=(0,0,0)$, $P=(0,0,t)$. T
hen
$$\vec{BP} = (-r,0,t), \qquad \vec{BO} = (-r,0,0).$$
Since $\vec{BO}$ is horizontal and the vertical component of $\vec{BP}$ is $t$ w
ith $|\vec{BP}|=\sqrt{r^2+t^2}$,
$$\sin\angle PBO = \frac{t}{\sqrt{r^2+t^2}}.$$
Substituting $t = r/\sqrt{35}$:
$$r^2+t^2 = r^2\!\left(1+\tfrac{1}{35}\right) = r^2\cdot\tfrac{36}{35}, \qquad \
sqrt{r^2+t^2} = \frac{6r}{\sqrt{35}},$$
$$\sin\angle PBO = \frac{r/\sqrt{35}}{6r/\sqrt{35}} = \frac{1}{6}.$$
