## Solution

Let $x_n = n\pi + \frac{\pi}{2} - \varepsilon_n$, where $\varepsilon_n > 0$ is s
mall (since $x_n$ lies just below $n\pi + \frac{\pi}{2}$, where $\tan$ has a ver
tical asymptote). As $n\to\infty$, $x_n\to\infty$ and $\varepsilon_n\to 0^+$. Th
e limit we seek is $\lim_{n\to\infty} n\,\varepsilon_n$.

**Step 1: Reformulate the equation.**

Since $\tan$ has period $\pi$,
$$\tan(x_n) = \tan\!\left(n\pi + \tfrac{\pi}{2} - \varepsilon_n\right) = \tan\!\
left(\tfrac{\pi}{2}-\varepsilon_n\right) = \cot(\varepsilon_n).$$
The equation $\tan(x_n)=x_n$ becomes
$$\cot(\varepsilon_n) = n\pi + \tfrac{\pi}{2} - \varepsilon_n. \tag{1}$$

**Step 2: Asymptotics of $\cot$.**

For small $\varepsilon>0$,
$$\cot\varepsilon = \frac{1}{\varepsilon} - \frac{\varepsilon}{3} - \frac{\varep
silon^3}{45} - \cdots = \frac{1}{\varepsilon} - \frac{\varepsilon}{3} + O(\varep
silon^3).$$
Substituting into (1):
$$\frac{1}{\varepsilon_n} - \frac{\varepsilon_n}{3} + O(\varepsilon_n^3) = n\pi
+ \frac{\pi}{2} - \varepsilon_n,$$
$$\frac{1}{\varepsilon_n} = n\pi + \frac{\pi}{2} - \varepsilon_n + \frac{\vareps
ilon_n}{3} + O(\varepsilon_n^3) = n\pi + \frac{\pi}{2} - \frac{2\varepsilon_n}{3
} + O(\varepsilon_n^3). \tag{2}$$

**Step 3: Leading order.**

From (2), $\varepsilon_n \sim \dfrac{1}{n\pi+\pi/2} \sim \dfrac{1}{n\pi}$, so $\
varepsilon_n = O(1/n)$. The correction term $\frac{2\varepsilon_n}{3}$ is $O(1/n
)$, which is $O(1)$ relative to $n\pi+\pi/2 = O(n)$. Hence
$$\varepsilon_n = \frac{1}{n\pi+\pi/2}\cdot\frac{1}{1 - \dfrac{2\varepsilon_n/3}
{n\pi+\pi/2} + O(\varepsilon_n^3/(n\pi))}.$$
Since $\dfrac{2\varepsilon_n/3}{n\pi+\pi/2} = O(1/n^2)$, we get
$$\varepsilon_n = \frac{1}{n\pi+\pi/2}\left(1 + O(1/n^2)\right).$$

**Step 4: Compute the limit.**

$$n\,\varepsilon_n = \frac{n}{n\pi+\pi/2}\left(1+O(1/n^2)\right) = \frac{1}{\pi+
\dfrac{\pi}{2n}}\left(1+O(1/n^2)\right) \xrightarrow{n\to\infty} \frac{1}{\pi}.$
$

Therefore,

$$\boxed{\lim_{n\to\infty} n\!\left(n\pi+\frac{\pi}{2}-x_n\right) = \frac{1}{\pi
}.}$$
