## Esscher Approximation for Compound Poisson Tails

**Setup.** Let $A = \sum_{i=1}^{N} X_i$ where $N \sim \text{Poisson}(\lambda)$ a
nd $X_1, X_2, \ldots$ are i.i.d. claim sizes with common MGF $M_X(s) = \mathbb{E
}[e^{sX_1}]$, independent of $N$. Assume $M_X(s) < \infty$ for $s \in [0, h)$ wi
th $h > 0$.

The MGF and cumulant generating function (CGF) of $A$ are:
$$M_A(s) = \exp\!\bigl(\lambda(M_X(s)-1)\bigr), \qquad K(s) = \lambda\bigl(M_X(s
)-1\bigr).$$

The derivatives are $K'(s) = \lambda M_X'(s)$ and $K''(s) = \lambda M_X''(s) > 0
$.

---

### Step 1: Esscher Transform

For $\theta > 0$, define the **Esscher-transformed measure** $\mathbb{P}_\theta$
 by
$$\frac{d\mathbb{P}_\theta}{d\mathbb{P}} = e^{\theta A - K(\theta)}.$$

Under $\mathbb{P}_\theta$, the distribution of $A$ remains compound Poisson, wit
h:
- Poisson parameter $\lambda_\theta = \lambda\, M_X(\theta)$,
- Tilted claim-size MGF $M_{X,\theta}(s) = M_X(s+\theta)/M_X(\theta)$.

The mean and variance under $\mathbb{P}_\theta$ are:
$$\mathbb{E}_\theta[A] = K'(\theta), \qquad \operatorname{Var}_\theta(A) = K''(\
theta).$$

The **saddlepoint** $\theta = \theta(x)$ is the unique positive solution to
$$K'(\theta) = \lambda\, M_X'(\theta) = x,$$
which exists for $x > \mathbb{E}[A] = K'(0)$ (so that $\theta > 0$).

---

### Step 2: Change of Measure

By the change-of-measure identity:
$$\mathbb{P}(A > x) = \mathbb{E}_\theta\!\bigl[e^{-\theta A + K(\theta)}\,\mathb
f{1}_{A > x}\bigr] = e^{K(\theta) - \theta x}\;\mathbb{E}_\theta\!\bigl[e^{-\the
ta(A - x)}\,\mathbf{1}_{A > x}\bigr].$$

The factor $e^{K(\theta) - \theta x} = e^{K(\theta) - \theta K'(\theta)}$ is the
 (negative of the) Legendre transform of $K$, which is the dominant exponential
term. The remaining expectation is a local correction centered at $A = x$ (the m
ean under $\mathbb{P}_\theta$).

---

### Step 3: Normal Approximation Under $\mathbb{P}_\theta$

As $x \to \infty$, the saddlepoint $\theta(x) \to \infty$ (for light-tailed clai
ms) or $\theta \to h^-$, and in either case $\lambda_\theta = \lambda\, M_X(\the
ta) \to \infty$. Since $A$ under $\mathbb{P}_\theta$ is a compound Poisson sum w
ith expected count $\lambda_\theta \to \infty$, the **CLT for compound Poisson d
istributions** gives:
$$\frac{A - x}{\sqrt{K''(\theta)}} \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}(0,1) \quad \text{
under } \mathbb{P}_\theta.$$

Equivalently, the density of $A$ under $\mathbb{P}_\theta$ near $a = x$ is local
ly approximated by:
$$f_\theta(a) \approx \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\, K''(\theta)}} \exp\!\Bigl(-\frac{(a
- x)^2}{2\, K''(\theta)}\Bigr).$$

---

### Step 4: Evaluate the Tilted Expectation

Set $\sigma^2 = K''(\theta)$. Then:
$$\mathbb{E}_\theta\!\bigl[e^{-\theta(A-x)}\,\mathbf{1}_{A > x}\bigr] = \int_0^\
infty e^{-\theta u}\, f_\theta(x + u)\, du.$$

Substituting the normal approximation:
$$\approx \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\,\sigma^2}} \int_0^\infty \exp\!\Bigl(-\theta u -
\frac{u^2}{2\sigma^2}\Bigr)\, du.$$

**Complete the square:**
$$-\theta u - \frac{u^2}{2\sigma^2} = -\frac{(u + \theta\sigma^2)^2}{2\sigma^2}
+ \frac{\theta^2 \sigma^2}{2}.$$

Substituting $v = (u + \theta\sigma^2)/\sigma$:
$$\int_0^\infty \exp\!\Bigl(-\theta u - \frac{u^2}{2\sigma^2}\Bigr)\, du = e^{\t
heta^2 \sigma^2/2}\,\sigma \int_{\theta\sigma}^\infty e^{-v^2/2}\, dv = e^{\thet
a^2 \sigma^2/2}\,\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}\,\bigl(1 - \Phi(\theta\sigma)\bigr),$$

where $\Phi$ is the standard normal CDF. Therefore:
$$\mathbb{E}_\theta\!\bigl[e^{-\theta(A-x)}\,\mathbf{1}_{A > x}\bigr] \approx e^
{\theta^2 K''(\theta)/2}\,\bigl(1 - \Phi\!\bigl(\theta\sqrt{K''(\theta)}\bigr)\b
igr).$$

---

### Step 5: General Esscher Approximation

Combining Steps 2 and 4:

$$\boxed{\mathbb{P}(A > x) \approx \exp\!\Bigl(K(\theta) - \theta x + \tfrac{1}{
2}\theta^2 K''(\theta)\Bigr)\;\bigl(1 - \Phi\!\bigl(\theta\sqrt{K''(\theta)}\big
r)\bigr)}$$

This is the **Esscher approximation in normal-corrected form**.

---

### Step 6: Leading-Order Form via Mills Ratio

In the large-deviation regime ($x \to \infty$), we have $\theta\sqrt{K''(\theta)
} \to \infty$. Applying the **Mills ratio** asymptotic $1 - \Phi(z) \sim \frac{1
}{z\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-z^2/2}$ as $z \to \infty$, with $z = \theta\sqrt{K''(\theta)
}$:

$$1 - \Phi\!\bigl(\theta\sqrt{K''(\theta)}\bigr) \sim \frac{1}{\theta\sqrt{2\pi\
, K''(\theta)}} \exp\!\Bigl(-\frac{\theta^2 K''(\theta)}{2}\Bigr).$$

Substituting into the general form, the $\exp(\theta^2 K''(\theta)/2)$ factors c
ancel:

$$\boxed{\mathbb{P}(A > x) \sim \frac{\exp\!\bigl(K(\theta) - \theta x\bigr)}{\t
heta\,\sqrt{2\pi\, K''(\theta)}} \qquad \text{as } x \to \infty}$$

where:
- $K(s) = \lambda(M_X(s) - 1)$ is the CGF of the compound Poisson variable $A$,
- $\theta = \theta(x) > 0$ is the saddlepoint solving $K'(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_\
theta[A] = x$,
- $K''(\theta) = \lambda\, M_X''(\theta)$ is the variance of $A$ under $\mathbb{
P}_\theta$.

---

### Consistency Checks

1. **Exponential factor is $\le 1$:** $K(\theta) - \theta K'(\theta) \le 0$ for
$\theta > 0$ by convexity of $K$ (since $K(0) = 0$ and $K'(\theta) \ge K'(0)$),
so $\exp(K(\theta) - \theta x) \le 1$. ✓

2. **Well-definedness:** $K''(\theta) = \lambda M_X''(\theta) > 0$ (strict conve
xity of $K$), so the denominator is positive and finite. ✓

3. **Degeneracy at $\theta = 0$:** The formula has $\theta$ in the denominator,
so it is not applicable at $x = \mathbb{E}[A]$ ($\theta = 0$), consistent with t
his being a **large-deviation** ($x \to \infty$) result. ✓

4. **Compound Poisson structure preserved under tilting:** The Esscher transform
 of a compound Poisson is again compound Poisson (with tilted parameters), which
 is what makes the CLT applicable under $\mathbb{P}_\theta$. ✓

---

### Summary of the Derivation

1. **Esscher transform** $\mathbb{P}_\theta$ via exponential tilting $d\mathbb{P
}_\theta/d\mathbb{P} = e^{\theta A - K(\theta)}$; under $\mathbb{P}_\theta$, $A$
 remains compound Poisson with mean $K'(\theta)$.
2. **Saddlepoint equation** $K'(\theta) = x$ centers the tilted distribution at
$x$.
3. **Change of measure** expresses $\mathbb{P}(A > x) = e^{K(\theta) - \theta x}
\,\mathbb{E}_\theta[e^{-\theta(A-x)}\mathbf{1}_{A>x}]$.
4. **CLT under $\mathbb{P}_\theta$** (valid since $\lambda_\theta \to \infty$) g
ives a Gaussian approximation for the density of $A$ near $x$.
5. **Gaussian integral** evaluated by completing the square yields the normal-co
rrected form with $1 - \Phi(\theta\sqrt{K''(\theta)})$.
6. **Mills ratio** in the regime $\theta\sqrt{K''(\theta)} \to \infty$ simplifie
s to the leading-order Esscher approximation $\exp(K(\theta) - \theta x)/(\theta
\sqrt{2\pi K''(\theta)})$.
