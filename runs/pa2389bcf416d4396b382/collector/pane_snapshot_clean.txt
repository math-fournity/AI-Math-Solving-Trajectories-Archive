## Solution

The board shows a 1 followed by 2020 zeros — a 2021-digit string. Position $n$ (
counting from the left, $n=1,\dots,2021$) is erased with probability $\tfrac{n-1
}{n}$ and kept with probability $\tfrac{1}{n}$. Position 1 (the digit 1) is neve
r erased since $\tfrac{0}{1}=0$.

**Key observation.** After erasures, the surviving digits are read left-to-right
 and interpreted in base 3. Since the only surviving digits are the leading 1 pl
us some zeros, the resulting string is exactly "1" followed by $k$ zeros, where
$k$ is the number of surviving zeros. As a base-3 number this equals $3^k$.

So we need $\mathbb{E}[3^k]$, where $k = \sum_{n=2}^{2021} I_n$ and $I_n$ is the
 indicator that position $n$ survives, with $\Pr(I_n=1)=\tfrac{1}{n}$, independe
ntly.

**Computing the expectation.** By independence,

$$\mathbb{E}[3^k] = \mathbb{E}\!\left[3^{\sum_{n=2}^{2021} I_n}\right] = \prod_{
n=2}^{2021} \mathbb{E}[3^{I_n}].$$

For each $n$,

$$\mathbb{E}[3^{I_n}] = \frac{1}{n}\cdot 3 + \frac{n-1}{n}\cdot 1 = \frac{3+n-1}
{n} = \frac{n+2}{n}.$$

Therefore

$$\mathbb{E}[3^k] = \prod_{n=2}^{2021} \frac{n+2}{n} = \frac{4}{2}\cdot\frac{5}{
3}\cdot\frac{6}{4}\cdot\frac{7}{5}\cdots\frac{2023}{2021}.$$

**Telescoping.** Numerator terms: $4,5,6,\dots,2023$. Denominator terms: $2,3,4,
\dots,2021$. Everything from 4 through 2021 cancels, leaving

$$\mathbb{E}[3^k] = \frac{2022\cdot 2023}{2\cdot 3} = \frac{2022\cdot 2023}{6}.$
$

Compute: $2022\cdot 2023 = 4{,}090{,}506$, and $4{,}090{,}506 / 6 = 681{,}751$.
