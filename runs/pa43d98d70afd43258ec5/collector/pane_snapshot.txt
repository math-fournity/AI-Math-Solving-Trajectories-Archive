## Solution

**Step 1: Reduce to coordinate axes via a linear change of coordinates.**

Let $\ell_1, \ell_2$ be the two distinct lines through the origin in $\mathbb{C}
^2$. Since they are distinct lines through $0$, they intersect only at the origi
n: $\ell_1 \cap \ell_2 = \{0\}$. Choose a linear isomorphism $T: \mathbb{C}^2 \t
o \mathbb{C}^2$ sending $\ell_1$ to the $x$-axis $\{y=0\}$ and $\ell_2$ to the $
y$-axis $\{x=0\}$ (this is possible because $\ell_1, \ell_2$ form a basis of $\m
athbb{C}^2$ as a complex vector space). Since $T$ is a homeomorphism of $\mathbb
{C}^2$,

$$\pi_1(\mathbb{C}^2 - L) \cong \pi_1\!\left(\mathbb{C}^2 - T(L)\right) = \pi_1\
!\left(\mathbb{C}^2 - \big(\{x=0\}\cup\{y=0\}\big)\right).$$

**Step 2: Identify the complement.**

The union $\{x=0\}\cup\{y=0\}$ is exactly the zero locus $\{xy=0\}$. Its complem
ent is

$$\mathbb{C}^2 \setminus \{xy=0\} = \{(x,y)\in\mathbb{C}^2 : x\neq 0 \text{ and
} y\neq 0\} = \mathbb{C}^* \times \mathbb{C}^* = (\mathbb{C}^*)^2.$$

**Step 3: Compute $\pi_1((\mathbb{C}^*)^2)$.**

The punctured plane $\mathbb{C}^* = \mathbb{C}\setminus\{0\}$ deformation retrac
ts onto the unit circle $S^1$ via the homotopy $H(z,t) = (1-t)z + t\,z/|z|$ (wit
h the radial retraction $z \mapsto z/|z|$). Hence

$$\pi_1(\mathbb{C}^*) \cong \pi_1(S^1) \cong \mathbb{Z}.$$

Since $(\mathbb{C}^*)^2 = \mathbb{C}^* \times \mathbb{C}^*$ is a product of path
-connected spaces, the fundamental group of a product is the product of the fund
amental groups:

$$\pi_1\!\left((\mathbb{C}^*)^2\right) \cong \pi_1(\mathbb{C}^*) \times \pi_1(\m
athbb{C}^*) \cong \mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}.$$

**Conclusion.**

$$\boxed{\pi_1(\mathbb{C}^2 - L) \cong \mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}}.$$

Geometrically, the two generators correspond to loops winding once around each o
f the two removed lines (equivalently, around the $x$-axis and $y$-axis factors
of $\mathbb{C}^*$).
