## Solution

We compute the variance of $Y = \int_0^2 W_t^2 \, dW_t$.

**Step 1: Mean.** Since $Y$ is an Itô integral of an adapted, square-integrable
integrand $W_t^2$ (with $\mathbb{E}\!\int_0^2 W_t^4\,dt < \infty$), it is a mart
ingale starting at $0$. Hence
$$\mathbb{E}[Y] = 0.$$

**Step 2: Second moment via Itô isometry.** By the Itô isometry,
$$\mathbb{E}[Y^2] = \mathbb{E}\!\left[\int_0^2 W_t^4 \, dt\right] = \int_0^2 \ma
thbb{E}[W_t^4]\,dt.$$

For Brownian motion, $W_t \sim N(0,t)$, so $\mathbb{E}[W_t^4] = 3t^2$. Therefore
$$\mathbb{E}[Y^2] = \int_0^2 3t^2\,dt = \left[t^3\right]_0^2 = 8.$$

**Step 3: Variance.**
$$\operatorname{Var}(Y) = \mathbb{E}[Y^2] - (\mathbb{E}[Y])^2 = 8 - 0 = 8.$$

$$\boxed{\operatorname{Var}(Y) = 8}$$
