## Solution

**Setup.** Label the vertices of the regular 34000-gon $0, 1, \dots, 33999$. A s
et of 400 vertices forms a regular 400-gon inscribed in the 34000-gon iff the ve
rtices are equally spaced, i.e., they form a coset of a subgroup of order 400 in
 $\mathbb{Z}/34000\mathbb{Z}$.

**Identifying the 400-gons.** A regular 400-gon is the set $\{a + kd \bmod 34000
 : k = 0,\dots,399\}$ where $d$ has multiplicative order 400 in $\mathbb{Z}/3400
0\mathbb{Z}$, i.e., $\gcd(d, 34000) = 85$ (since $34000/\gcd(d,34000) = 400$). W
riting $d = 85m$ with $\gcd(m, 400) = 1$, the subgroup generated is $85\cdot\{km
 \bmod 400\} = 85\cdot\mathbb{Z}/400\mathbb{Z}$ (since $m$ is a unit mod 400). S
o **every** valid $d$ generates the same subgroup $H = 85\,\mathbb{Z}_{34000} =
\{0, 85, 170, \dots, 85\cdot 399\}$.

The distinct regular 400-gons are the cosets $a + H$ for $a \in \{0, 1, \dots, 8
4\}$, giving **85** total 400-gons.

**Red vertices.** Powers of 2 in $\{0,\dots,33999\}$: $2^0, 2^1, \dots, 2^{15}$
(since $2^{15}=32768 < 34000 \le 2^{16}=65536$). That's 16 red vertices.

**Coset membership.** A 400-gon $a + H$ contains a red vertex $2^k$ iff $2^k \eq
uiv a \pmod{85}$. So the "bad" cosets are those $a$ congruent to some $2^k \pmod
{85}$.

**Residues of $2^k \pmod{85}$.** Factor $85 = 5 \cdot 17$. The order of 2 mod 5
is 4, and mod 17 is 8 (since $2^4 \equiv -1 \pmod{17}$). So $\operatorname{ord}_
{85}(2) = \operatorname{lcm}(4, 8) = 8$. The 16 powers $2^0,\dots,2^{15}$ thus p
roduce only 8 distinct residues mod 85:

$$2^0\equiv1,\; 2^1\equiv2,\; 2^2\equiv4,\; 2^3\equiv8,\; 2^4\equiv16,\; 2^5\equ
iv32,\; 2^6\equiv64,\; 2^7\equiv43 \pmod{85}.$$

These 8 residues are all distinct, giving **8 bad cosets**.

**Counting good 400-gons.** The number of cosets with no red vertex is:

$$85 - 8 = \boxed{77}.$$
