We prove that $\|f\| = 2021$.

**Upper bound: $\|f\| \le 2021$.**

Let $x = (x_n) \in l^\infty$ with $\|x\|_\infty \le 1$. Then for every $n$, $-1
\le x_n \le 1$, i.e., coordinatewise
$$-\bar x \;\le\; x \;\le\; \bar x, \qquad \bar x = (1,1,\dots).$$

Since $\bar x - x \ge 0$ coordinatewise and $\bar x + x \ge 0$ coordinatewise, t
he positivity hypothesis gives
$$f(\bar x - x) \ge 0 \implies f(x) \le f(\bar x) = 2021,$$
$$f(\bar x + x) \ge 0 \implies f(x) \ge -f(\bar x) = -2021.$$

Combining, $|f(x)| \le 2021$ for every $x$ with $\|x\|_\infty \le 1$. Hence
$$\|f\| = \sup_{\|x\|_\infty \le 1} |f(x)| \le 2021.$$

**Lower bound: $\|f\| \ge 2021$.**

Take $x = \bar x = (1,1,\dots)$. Then $\|x\|_\infty = 1 \ne 0$ and
$$\frac{|f(x)|}{\|x\|_\infty} = \frac{|2021|}{1} = 2021.$$

Therefore $\|f\| \ge 2021$.

**Conclusion.** The two bounds give $\|f\| = 2021$.
