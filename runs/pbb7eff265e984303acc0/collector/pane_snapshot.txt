## Condition for a Rank-$n$ Tensor Field $T$ to be Totally Anti-Symmetric

A rank-$n$ tensor field $T$ on a smooth manifold $M$ is **totally anti-symmetric
** (also called **completely skew-symmetric** or **alternating**) if and only if
 it changes sign under the interchange of any two of its indices.

### Formal Statement

Let $T$ be a rank-$n$ tensor field with components $T^{i_1 i_2 \cdots i_n}$ (in
any coordinate chart). Then $T$ is totally anti-symmetric if and only if, for ev
ery pair of indices $a, b \in \{1, \dots, n\}$ with $a \neq b$,

$$T^{\cdots i_a \cdots i_b \cdots} = -\, T^{\cdots i_b \cdots i_a \cdots},$$

i.e., swapping the $a$-th and $b$-th slots introduces a minus sign.

### Equivalent Permutation Form

Equivalently, for every permutation $\sigma \in S_n$ of the index positions $\{1
, 2, \dots, n\}$,

$$T^{i_{\sigma(1)}\, i_{\sigma(2)}\, \cdots\, i_{\sigma(n)}} = \operatorname{sgn
}(\sigma)\, T^{i_1\, i_2\, \cdots\, i_n},$$

where $\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma) = +1$ if $\sigma$ is an even permutation and $
\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma) = -1$ if $\sigma$ is an odd permutation.

### Proof of Equivalence of the Two Forms

**($\Leftarrow$) Permutation form $\Rightarrow$ pairwise swap form.**
Any transposition $(a\; b)$ is an odd permutation, so $\operatorname{sgn}((a\;b)
) = -1$. Applying the permutation form with $\sigma = (a\;b)$ gives $T^{\cdots i
_b \cdots i_a \cdots} = -T^{\cdots i_a \cdots i_b \cdots}$, which is exactly the
 pairwise swap condition.

**($\Rightarrow$) Pairwise swap form $\Rightarrow$ permutation form.**
Every permutation $\sigma \in S_n$ can be written as a product of transpositions
: $\sigma = \tau_1 \tau_2 \cdots \tau_k$, where $k$ is the number of transpositi
ons. The sign is $\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma) = (-1)^k$. Applying the pairwise sw
ap condition $k$ times, once for each transposition $\tau_j$, each application i
ntroduces a factor of $-1$. Therefore

$$T^{i_{\sigma(1)} \cdots i_{\sigma(n)}} = (-1)^k\, T^{i_1 \cdots i_n} = \operat
orname{sgn}(\sigma)\, T^{i_1 \cdots i_n}.$$

This establishes the equivalence.

### Coordinate-Free Formulation

Let $T \in \Gamma(T^0_n M)$ (or $T^n_0 M$, etc.) be a rank-$n$ tensor field. For
 any permutation $\sigma \in S_n$ acting on the $n$ argument slots, define the p
ermutation operator $\sigma \cdot T$ by

$$(\sigma \cdot T)(\omega_1, \dots, \omega_n) := T(\omega_{\sigma(1)}, \dots, \o
mega_{\sigma(n)}).$$

Then $T$ is totally anti-symmetric if and only if

$$\sigma \cdot T = \operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)\, T \quad \text{for all } \sigma \
in S_n.$$

### Useful Consequences

1. **Vanishing on repeated indices.** If any two indices coincide, $T^{i_1 \cdot
s i_n} = 0$. Proof: swapping the two equal indices gives $T = -T$, hence $T = 0$
.

2. **At most $\binom{m}{n}$ independent components.** On an $m$-dimensional mani
fold, a totally anti-symmetric rank-$n$ tensor has at most $\binom{m}{n}$ indepe
ndent components (corresponding to strictly increasing index tuples $i_1 < i_2 <
 \cdots < i_n$). If $n > m$, the tensor is identically zero.

3. **Relation to differential forms.** A totally anti-symmetric $(0,n)$ tensor f
ield is precisely a differential $n$-form. The space of such tensors is $\Omega^
n(M) = \Gamma(\Lambda^n T^*M)$.

### Summary

> **A rank-$n$ tensor field $T$ is totally anti-symmetric if and only if it chan
ges sign under the interchange of any two of its indices**, equivalently, if and
 only if for every permutation $\sigma \in S_n$,
> $$T^{i_{\sigma(1)} \cdots i_{\sigma(n)}} = \operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)\, T^{i_1
 \cdots i_n}.$$
