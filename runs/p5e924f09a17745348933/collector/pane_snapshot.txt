## Proof

Let $S$ be a regular surface without umbilical points, and suppose $S$ is minima
l. Let $p \in S$, and let $e_1, e_2 \in T_p(S)$ be orthonormal principal directi
ons with corresponding principal curvatures $k_1, k_2$.

**Step 1: Identify the inner product.** Since $N$ is a unit normal field, $dN_p$
 maps $T_p(S)$ into $T_{N(p)}(\mathbb{S}^2)$, the tangent space to the unit sphe
re at $N(p)$. This space is naturally identified with $T_p(S)$ (both are the pla
ne orthogonal to $N(p)$), and under this identification the metric $\langle\cdot
,\cdot\rangle_{N(p)}$ on $\mathbb{S}^2$ coincides with $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangl
e_p$. Hence the condition becomes
$$\langle dN_p(w_1),\, dN_p(w_2)\rangle_p = \lambda(p)\,\langle w_1, w_2\rangle_
p \quad \forall\, w_1, w_2 \in T_p(S).$$

This says that $dN_p^*\!\circ dN_p = \lambda(p)\cdot\mathrm{Id}$, i.e., $dN_p$ i
s a **conformal** (similarity) map on $T_p(S)$.

**Step 2: Compute in the principal basis.** By the Weingarten equations, $dN_p(e
_i) = -k_i\, e_i$ (do Carmo convention). Therefore:
$$\langle dN_p(e_1), dN_p(e_1)\rangle = k_1^2,\qquad \langle dN_p(e_2), dN_p(e_2
)\rangle = k_2^2,\qquad \langle dN_p(e_1), dN_p(e_2)\rangle = 0.$$

For the conformality condition to hold we need
$$k_1^2 = \lambda(p) = k_2^2.$$

**Step 3: Use the minimal surface condition.** A minimal surface has mean curvat
ure $H = \tfrac{1}{2}(k_1 + k_2) = 0$, so
$$k_2 = -k_1.$$

Immediately $k_2^2 = k_1^2$, so the conformality condition $k_1^2 = k_2^2$ is **
automatically satisfied**. Moreover, since $S$ has no umbilical points, $k_1 \ne
q k_2$, which forces $k_1 \neq 0$ (otherwise $k_1 = k_2 = 0$), so $\lambda(p) >
0$.

**Step 4: Express $\lambda(p)$.** We have
$$\lambda(p) = k_1^2 = k_2^2.$$

Since $k_2 = -k_1$, the product $k_1 k_2 = -k_1^2$, giving the equivalent compac
t form
$$\lambda(p) = -k_1\, k_2.$$

(Equivalently, $\lambda(p) = -K(p)$, the negative of the Gaussian curvature, sin
ce $K = k_1 k_2$.)

### Answer

$$\boxed{\lambda(p) = -\,k_1\, k_2 \;=\; k_1^2 \;=\; k_2^2}$$
