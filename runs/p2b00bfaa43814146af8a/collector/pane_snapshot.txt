## Proof

**Lemma 90.3.** Let $R$ be a ring, $M$ a coherent $R$-module, $N$ a finite $R$-m
odule, and $\varphi: N \to M$ an $R$-module homomorphism. Then:

- $\operatorname{Ker}(\varphi)$ is a **finite** $R$-module,
- $\operatorname{Im}(\varphi)$ is a **coherent** $R$-module,
- $\operatorname{Coker}(\varphi)$ is a **coherent** $R$-module.

Recall that an $R$-module $M$ is *coherent* if it is finite and every finite sub
module of $M$ is finitely presented (equivalently, of finite presentation).

---

### Step 1. $\operatorname{Im}(\varphi)$ is coherent.

The image $\operatorname{Im}(\varphi)$ is a finite submodule of $M$ (it is gener
ated by the images of the finitely many generators of $N$). Since $M$ is coheren
t, every finite submodule of $M$ is finitely presented, hence coherent. Therefor
e $\operatorname{Im}(\varphi)$ is coherent. $\square$

### Step 2. $\operatorname{Ker}(\varphi)$ is finite.

Consider the exact sequence
$$0 \to \operatorname{Ker}(\varphi) \to N \xrightarrow{\varphi} \operatorname{Im
}(\varphi) \to 0.$$
Here $N$ is finite and $\operatorname{Im}(\varphi)$ is finitely presented (by St
ep 1, it is coherent). A standard fact states: *the kernel of a surjective homom
orphism from a finitely generated module onto a finitely presented module is fin
itely generated.* Indeed, if $\operatorname{Im}(\varphi)$ has a finite presentat
ion $R^m \to R^n \to \operatorname{Im}(\varphi) \to 0$, lifting the generators o
f $N$ to $R^n$ and using the finite generation of $N$ produces a finite generati
ng set for $\operatorname{Ker}(\varphi)$. Hence $\operatorname{Ker}(\varphi)$ is
 finite. $\square$

### Step 3. $\operatorname{Coker}(\varphi)$ is coherent.

Consider the exact sequence
$$0 \to \operatorname{Im}(\varphi) \to M \to \operatorname{Coker}(\varphi) \to 0
.$$
Both $\operatorname{Im}(\varphi)$ and $M$ are coherent. We verify the coherence
of $\operatorname{Coker}(\varphi)$ directly from the definition.

First, $\operatorname{Coker}(\varphi)$ is finite, being a quotient of the finite
 module $M$.

Now let $C' \subseteq \operatorname{Coker}(\varphi)$ be any finite submodule. Le
t $B' \subseteq M$ be the preimage of $C'$ under the quotient map $M \to \operat
orname{Coker}(\varphi)$, so we have the exact sequence
$$0 \to \operatorname{Im}(\varphi) \to B' \to C' \to 0.$$
Since $\operatorname{Im}(\varphi)$ is finite and $C'$ is finite, $B'$ is finite.
 Since $M$ is coherent and $B'$ is a finite submodule of $M$, the module $B'$ is
 finitely presented. The quotient $C' = B'/\operatorname{Im}(\varphi)$ of a fini
tely presented module by a finitely generated submodule is finitely presented. T
hus every finite submodule $C'$ of $\operatorname{Coker}(\varphi)$ is finitely p
resented, which proves $\operatorname{Coker}(\varphi)$ is coherent. $\square$

---

**Conclusion.** Under the hypotheses of Lemma 90.3 — $\varphi: N \to M$ a homomo
rphism from a finite module $N$ to a coherent module $M$ — the kernel $\operator
name{Ker}(\varphi)$ is finite, and both the image $\operatorname{Im}(\varphi)$ a
nd the cokernel $\operatorname{Coker}(\varphi)$ are coherent (in particular, fin
ite and finitely presented).
