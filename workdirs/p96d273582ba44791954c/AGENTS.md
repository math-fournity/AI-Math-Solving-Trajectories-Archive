# Problem

In a specialized logistics hub, a cargo elevator is used to transport heavy equipment. The safety protocol dictates that if any two distinct equipment crates, weighing $a$ and $b$ kilograms respectively, are placed in the bay, their weight difference must be significant enough for the sensors to distinguish them. 

The safety regulation specifies that for any two crates where $a \neq b$, the absolute difference between their weights $|a - b|$ must be at least one-hundredth of their product. All crates must have a weight represented by a positive integer number of kilograms.

What is the maximum number of such crates that can be kept in the hub simultaneously while ensuring that every possible pair of crates within the group satisfies this safety regulation?
