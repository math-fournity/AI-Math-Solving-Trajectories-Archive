## Solution

The cube-4 graph is the hypercube $Q_4$: its 16 vertices are the elements of $(\
mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z})^4$, and two vertices are adjacent when they differ in exa
ctly one coordinate. The robot's walk of length $n$ from the origin back to the
origin is counted by $(A^n)_{0,0}$, where $A$ is the adjacency matrix of $Q_4$.

### Eigenvalues of $Q_4$

The hypercube $Q_d$ is the Cayley graph of $(\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z})^d$ with the
 standard generators $e_1,\dots,e_d$. Its eigenvalues are
$$\lambda_S = d - 2|S|, \qquad S \subseteq \{1,\dots,d\},$$
with eigenvalue $\lambda_S$ having multiplicity $\binom{d}{|S|}$. Equivalently,
the eigenvalue $d-2k$ has multiplicity $\binom{d}{k}$.

For $d=4$, the eigenvalues and multiplicities are:

| eigenvalue | $4$ | $2$ | $0$ | $-2$ | $-4$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| multiplicity | $1$ | $4$ | $6$ | $4$ | $1$ |

### Closed walks via the spectral formula

Since the hypercube is vertex-transitive, every vertex has the same number of cl
osed walks of length $n$ returning to it. Hence
$$(A^n)_{0,0} = \frac{1}{2^d}\operatorname{tr}(A^n) = \frac{1}{16}\sum_{k=0}^{4}
\binom{4}{k}(4-2k)^n.$$
Expanding:
$$(A^n)_{0,0} = \frac{1}{16}\Big[1\cdot 4^n + 4\cdot 2^n + 6\cdot 0^n + 4\cdot(-
2)^n + 1\cdot(-4)^n\Big].$$

### Specializing to $n = 4042$

Since $n = 4042 > 0$ is even:
- $0^n = 0$,
- $(-2)^n = 2^n$,
- $(-4)^n = 4^n$.

Substituting:
$$(A^{4042})_{0,0} = \frac{1}{16}\Big[4^{4042} + 4\cdot 2^{4042} + 0 + 4\cdot 2^
{4042} + 4^{4042}\Big] = \frac{2\cdot 4^{4042} + 8\cdot 2^{4042}}{16}.$$

Simplifying:
$$= \frac{4^{4042}}{8} + \frac{2^{4042}}{2} = \frac{2^{8084}}{2^3} + 2^{4041} =
2^{8081} + 2^{4041}.$$

### Verification with small cases

- **$n=2$:** $\frac{1}{16}[16+16+0+16+16]=\frac{64}{16}=4$, which equals the deg
ree of each vertex (one step out and back along each of the 4 edges). ✓
- **$n=4$:** $\frac{4^4}{8}+\frac{2^4}{2}=32+8=40$, matching the known count of
4-step closed walks from a vertex of $Q_4$. ✓

### Answer

The number of ways the robot can return to $(0,0,0,0)$ after $4042$ moves is

$$\boxed{2^{8081}+2^{4041}}.$$
