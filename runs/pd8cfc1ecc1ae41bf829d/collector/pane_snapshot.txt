## Solution

We address the two parts of the question separately.

### Part 1: The case $p=6$ — the inequality **does** hold

Let $U\subset\mathbb{R}^3$ be open, bounded, of class $C^2$. We claim there exis
ts $C=C(U)$ such that
$$\|u\|_{L^6(U)}\le C\,\|u\|_{H^1(U)},\qquad \forall\,u\in H^1(U).$$

**Proof.** This is the critical Sobolev embedding in dimension $n=3$. The key in
gredient is the Gagliardo–Nirenberg–Sobolev inequality on $\mathbb{R}^3$:
$$\|v\|_{L^{2^*}(\mathbb{R}^3)}\le C_{\mathrm{GNS}}\|\nabla v\|_{L^2(\mathbb{R}^
3)},\qquad 2^*=\frac{2n}{n-2}=6,\quad v\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb{R}^3). \tag{GNS}$$

Since $U$ is bounded of class $C^2$, there exists a bounded extension operator $
E:H^1(U)\to H^1(\mathbb{R}^3)$ with
$$\|Eu\|_{H^1(\mathbb{R}^3)}\le C_E\,\|u\|_{H^1(U)},\qquad (Eu)|_U = u. \tag{Ext
}$$
(The existence of such an extension operator is standard for $C^2$—indeed $C^1$
suffices—domains; see Adams–Fournier or Evans, Ch. 5.)

By density of $C_c^\infty(\mathbb{R}^3)$ in $H^1(\mathbb{R}^3)$, inequality (GNS
) extends to all of $H^1(\mathbb{R}^3)$:
$$\|w\|_{L^6(\mathbb{R}^3)}\le C_{\mathrm{GNS}}\|\nabla w\|_{L^2(\mathbb{R}^3)}\
le C_{\mathrm{GNS}}\|w\|_{H^1(\mathbb{R}^3)},\qquad w\in H^1(\mathbb{R}^3).$$

Apply this to $w=Eu$ and use (Ext):
$$\|u\|_{L^6(U)}=\|Eu\|_{L^6(U)}\le\|Eu\|_{L^6(\mathbb{R}^3)}\le C_{\mathrm{GNS}
}\|Eu\|_{H^1(\mathbb{R}^3)}\le C_{\mathrm{GNS}}\,C_E\,\|u\|_{H^1(U)}.$$

Setting $C:=C_{\mathrm{GNS}}\,C_E$ (which depends only on $U$, not on $u$) yield
s
$$\boxed{\|u\|_{L^6(U)}\le C\,\|u\|_{H^1(U)}},\qquad\forall\,u\in H^1(U). \qquad
\blacksquare$$

**Remark on the critical exponent.** The value $p=6$ is exactly the Sobolev conj
ugate $2^*=2\cdot 3/(3-2)=6$. The embedding $H^1(U)\hookrightarrow L^6(U)$ is *c
ontinuous* but **not** compact (the critical case). Compactness holds only for $
p<6$ (Rellich–Kondrachov), but continuity holds at the endpoint $p=6$, which is
all the inequality requires.

### Part 2: The inequality does **not** hold for all $1<p<\infty$

We show that for $p>6$ no such constant can exist (even for nice $U$). Take $U=B
_1(0)\subset\mathbb{R}^3$.

For $\alpha\in(0,1)$ define the singular function
$$u_\alpha(x)=|x|^{-\alpha}.$$
A direct computation gives
$$\|u_\alpha\|_{L^p(B_1)}^p=\int_{B_1}|x|^{-\alpha p}\,dx=4\pi\int_0^1 r^{2-\alp
ha p}\,dr=\frac{4\pi}{3-\alpha p},\qquad\text{provided }\alpha p<3.$$
So $u_\alpha\in L^p(B_1)$ iff $\alpha<3/p$.

On the other hand,
$$\nabla u_\alpha=-\alpha\,|x|^{-\alpha-1}\frac{x}{|x|},\qquad |\nabla u_\alpha|
^2=\alpha^2|x|^{-2\alpha-2},$$
$$\|\nabla u_\alpha\|_{L^2(B_1)}^2=4\pi\alpha^2\int_0^1 r^{-2\alpha}\,dr=\frac{4
\pi\alpha^2}{1-2\alpha},\qquad\text{provided }\alpha<\tfrac12.$$
Thus $u_\alpha\in H^1(B_1)$ iff $\alpha<1/2$, with $\|u_\alpha\|_{H^1(B_1)}$ bou
nded as $\alpha\nearrow 1/2$.

Now fix any $p>6$ and choose $\alpha\in(1/2,\,3/p)$. This interval is nonempty p
recisely because $p>6\Rightarrow 3/p<1/2$ is **false**—wait: $p>6\Rightarrow 3/p
<1/2$. So the interval $(1/2,3/p)$ is **empty** when $p>6$. We need a sharper ar
gument.

**Sharper argument.** Fix $p>6$ and pick $\alpha\in(0,1/2)$ (so $u_\alpha\in H^1
$) with $\alpha p<3$, i.e. $\alpha<3/p$. Since $p>6$, $3/p<1/2$, so we may choos
e $\alpha\in(3/p-\\varepsilon,\,3/p)\subset(0,1/2)$. Then
$$\|u_\alpha\|_{L^p(B_1)}^p=\frac{4\pi}{3-\alpha p}\to+\infty\quad\text{as }\alp
ha\nearrow 3/p,$$
while
$$\|u_\alpha\|_{H^1(B_1)}^2=\frac{4\pi}{1-2\alpha}+\frac{4\pi\alpha^2}{1-2\alpha
}\le\frac{4\pi(1+(3/p)^2)}{1-2\cdot 3/p+\delta}=:M<\infty$$
for all $\alpha$ in a neighborhood below $3/p$. Hence the ratio
$$\frac{\|u_\alpha\|_{L^p(B_1)}}{\|u_\alpha\|_{H^1(B_1)}}\to+\infty\quad\text{as
 }\alpha\nearrow\frac{3}{p},$$
so no finite constant $C$ can satisfy $\|u\|_{L^p}\le C\|u\|_{H^1}$ for all $u\i
n H^1(U)$ when $p>6$.

### Conclusion

- **For $p=6$:** Yes. There exists $C=C(U)$ such that $\|u\|_{L^6(U)}\le C\|u\|_
{H^1(U)}$ for all $u\in H^1(U)$. This is the critical Sobolev embedding $H^1(\ma
thbb{R}^3)\hookrightarrow L^6(\mathbb{R}^3)$, transferred to $U$ via the extensi
on operator.
- **For all $1<p<\infty$:** No. The embedding $H^1(U)\hookrightarrow L^p(U)$ hol
ds **if and only if** $1\le p\le 2^*=6$ (for $U\subset\mathbb{R}^3$ bounded of c
lass $C^2$). For $p>6$ the inequality fails, as shown by the family $u_\alpha(x)
=|x|^{-\alpha}$ on $B_1$.

The full valid range is therefore $\boxed{1\le p\le 6}$, and in particular the r
equested case $p=6$ is the borderline case where the inequality is valid (contin
uously, though not compactly).
