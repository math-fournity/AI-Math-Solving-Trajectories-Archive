Looking at this problem, I need to find years LETA (2001–2100) where the linear
system has at least two real solutions.

**Key observation:** $(x,y,z,t) = (1,0,0,0)$ is always a solution, since each eq
uation reduces to its first coefficient times 1, which equals the right-hand sid
e. So the system is always consistent. A consistent linear system has ≥ 2 soluti
ons iff the coefficient matrix is singular (det = 0), giving infinitely many sol
utions.

**The matrix** is the circulant matrix $M$ with first row $(L, E, T, A)$. Its ei
genvalues (using $\omega = e^{2\pi i/4} = i$) are:

$$\lambda_0 = L+E+T+A, \quad \lambda_1 = (L-T)+i(E-A), \quad \lambda_2 = L-E+T-A
, \quad \lambda_3 = \overline{\lambda_1}$$

So $\det(M) = \lambda_0 \lambda_1 \lambda_2 \lambda_3 = 0$ iff at least one eige
nvalue vanishes:

- **$\lambda_0 = 0$:** $L+E+T+A = 0$. Since $L=2$ (21st century) and all digits
≥ 0, impossible.
- **$\lambda_1 = 0$ (equivalently $\lambda_3 = 0$):** $L = T$ and $E = A$.
- **$\lambda_2 = 0$:** $L + T = E + A$.

**Applying to 2001–2100** (so $L = 2$):

**Years 2001–2099** ($L=2, E=0$):
- Condition $\lambda_1=0$: $T=2, A=0$ → year **2020**.
- Condition $\lambda_2=0$: $2+T = 0+A$, i.e., $A = T+2$. With $T \in \{0,\ldots,
7\}$ (so $A \in \{2,\ldots,9\}$): years $2002 + 11T$ for $T=0,\ldots,7$, giving
**2002, 2013, 2024, 2035, 2046, 2057, 2068, 2079**.

**Year 2100** ($L=2, E=1, T=0, A=0$):
- $\lambda_1$: $T=2$? No ($T=0$). Fails.
- $\lambda_2$: $2+0 = 1+0$? $2 \neq 1$. Fails.
- Not good.

**Verification of a few cases:**
- Year 2002: $\lambda_2 = 2-0+0-2 = 0$ ✓
- Year 2020: $\lambda_1 = (2-2)+i(0-0) = 0$ ✓
- Year 2079: $\lambda_2 = 2-0+7-9 = 0$ ✓
- Year 2001 (not good): $\lambda_0=3, \lambda_1=2-i, \lambda_2=1$, all nonzero ✓
- Year 2099 (not good): $\lambda_0=20, \lambda_2=2$, all nonzero ✓
- Year 2100 (not good): $\lambda_0=3, \lambda_1=2+i, \lambda_2=1$, all nonzero ✓

**Conclusion.** The good years in the 21st century are:

$$\boxed{2002,\ 2013,\ 2020,\ 2024,\ 2035,\ 2046,\ 2057,\ 2068,\ 2079}$$

These consist of the arithmetic progression $2002 + 11k$ for $k = 0, 1, \ldots,
7$ (from $\lambda_2 = 0$) together with the isolated year $2020$ (from $\lambda_
1 = 0$).
