# Problem

In a specialized chemistry lab, a technician has $n$ unique, stable isotopes ($n \ge 2$), each possessing a specific, distinct electrical charge measured in millicoulombs. 

The lab's protocol requires these isotopes to be divided into two containment units, Alpha and Beta, such that each unit contains at least one isotope. Regardless of how the isotopes are initially distributed between the two units, the following procedure must always be possible:
1. Transfer one isotope from Unit Alpha to Unit Beta.
2. Subsequently, transfer one isotope from Unit Beta (which now includes the one just moved) back to Unit Alpha.

After this exchange of single isotopes, the total net electrical charge in Unit Alpha must be exactly 0, and the total net electrical charge in Unit Beta must also be exactly 0.

Based on the requirement that this exchange must be possible for every conceivable initial distribution of the $n$ isotopes, find the maximum possible value of $n$.
