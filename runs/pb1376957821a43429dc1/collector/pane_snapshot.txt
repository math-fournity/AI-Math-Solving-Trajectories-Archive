**Step 2 (Reduction to automorphisms of $\mathbb{D}$).** Let $C(z) = \frac{1+z}{
1-z}$ be the Cayley transform, a conformal bijection from $\mathbb{D}$ onto $\ma
thbb{A}$. Set $\varphi_n = C^{-1} \circ f_n : \mathbb{D} \to \mathbb{D}$. Each $
\varphi_n$ is a conformal bijection of $\mathbb{D}$ onto itself, hence an automo

$$\varphi_n(z) = e^{i\theta_n}\frac{z - a_n}{1 - \overline{a_n}z}, \qquad a_n \i
n \mathbb{D},\ \theta_n \in \mathbb{R}.$$

**Step 3 ($\varphi_n$ converges to a constant).** Since $f_n(\mathbb{D}) = \math
bb{A}$, each $f_n$ takes values in $\{w : \Re w > 0\}$, so $\Re f \geq 0$, givin

 well-defined. By continuity of $C^{-1}$ on $\{\Re w \geq 0\}$, we have $\varphi
_n \to w_0 := C^{-1}(c)$ uniformly on compact subsets. Since $f$ is constant and
 $C$ is a bijection, $w_0$ is a constant.

**Step 4 ($|w_0| = 1$).** Suppose $|a_n|$ stays bounded away from $1$: $|a_n| \l

which is a non-constant automorphism — contradicting $w_0$ being constant. There
fore $|a_n| \to 1$.

Passing to a convergent subsequence $a_n \to a$ with $|a| = 1$ and $\theta_n \to
 \theta$ (all subsequential limits agree since the full sequence converges), for
 $z$ in any compact subset of $\mathbb{D}$:
$$\varphi_n(z) = e^{i\theta_n}\frac{z - a_n}{1 - \overline{a_n}z} \to e^{i\theta

$$\varphi_n(z) \to e^{i\theta}\cdot\frac{-(a-z)}{(a-z)/a} = -ae^{i\theta}.$$
So $w_0 = -ae^{i\theta}$ with $|w_0| = 1$.

$$c = C(w_0) = \frac{1 + e^{i\alpha}}{1 - e^{i\alpha}} = \frac{e^{-i\alpha/2} +

This is **purely imaginary**, so $\Re c = 0$.

$$\boxed{\Re f(0) = 0.}$$
