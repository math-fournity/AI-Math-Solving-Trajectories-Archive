## Solution

We determine the orthogonal complement $U_{\perp}$ of the bilinear form
$$\xi(A,B) = n\,\mathrm{tr}(AB) - \mathrm{tr}(A)\,\mathrm{tr}(B)$$
on $U = M_n(\mathbb{C})$.

**Step 1: A useful decomposition.** Every matrix decomposes as
$$A = \frac{\mathrm{tr}(A)}{n}\,I + A_0, \qquad A_0 := A - \frac{\mathrm{tr}(A)}
{n}\,I \in \mathfrak{sl}_n(\mathbb{C}),$$
where $\mathfrak{sl}_n(\mathbb{C}) = \{X \in M_n(\mathbb{C}) : \mathrm{tr}(X)=0\
}$. This gives the direct sum
$$M_n(\mathbb{C}) = \mathbb{C}\cdot I \;\oplus\; \mathfrak{sl}_n(\mathbb{C}).$$

**Step 2: $\mathbb{C}\cdot I \subseteq U_{\perp}$.** For $A = \lambda I$ and any
 $B$,
$$\xi(\lambda I, B) = n\,\mathrm{tr}(\lambda I \cdot B) - \mathrm{tr}(\lambda I)
\,\mathrm{tr}(B) = n\lambda\,\mathrm{tr}(B) - n\lambda\,\mathrm{tr}(B) = 0.$$
Hence $\mathbb{C}\cdot I \subseteq U_{\perp}$.

**Step 3: $U_{\perp} \subseteq \mathbb{C}\cdot I$.** Let $A \in U_{\perp}$, so $
\xi(A,B)=0$ for all $B$, i.e.
$$n\,\mathrm{tr}(AB) = \mathrm{tr}(A)\,\mathrm{tr}(B) \quad \forall\, B \in M_n(
\mathbb{C}).$$
Writing $A = \frac{\mathrm{tr}(A)}{n}I + A_0$ with $A_0 \in \mathfrak{sl}_n(\mat
hbb{C})$,
$$\mathrm{tr}(AB) = \frac{\mathrm{tr}(A)}{n}\,\mathrm{tr}(B) + \mathrm{tr}(A_0 B
).$$
Substituting into the condition:
$$\mathrm{tr}(A)\,\mathrm{tr}(B) + n\,\mathrm{tr}(A_0 B) = \mathrm{tr}(A)\,\math
rm{tr}(B),$$
so $n\,\mathrm{tr}(A_0 B) = 0$ for all $B$, i.e. $\mathrm{tr}(A_0 B) = 0$ for al
l $B$.

The trace form $(X,Y) \mapsto \mathrm{tr}(XY)$ is **non-degenerate** on $M_n(\ma
thbb{C})$ (its matrix in the standard basis $E_{ij}$ is a permutation matrix). T
herefore $\mathrm{tr}(A_0 B) = 0$ for all $B$ forces $A_0 = 0$, giving $A = \fra
c{\mathrm{tr}(A)}{n}I \in \mathbb{C}\cdot I$.

**Step 4: Conclusion.** Combining Steps 2 and 3,
$$U_{\perp} = \mathbb{C}\cdot I = \{\lambda I : \lambda \in \mathbb{C}\}.$$
This is a one-dimensional subspace (spanned by the identity matrix $I$).

**Verification of the edge case $n=1$:** Then $M_1(\mathbb{C})=\mathbb{C}$ and $
\xi(a,b)=1\cdot ab - a\cdot b = 0$, so the form is identically zero and $U_{\per
p}=\mathbb{C}$, which equals $\mathbb{C}\cdot I$ and has dimension $1$. Consiste
nt.

$$\boxed{\dim U_{\perp} = 1}$$
