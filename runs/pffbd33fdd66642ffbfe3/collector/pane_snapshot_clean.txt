## Solution

Let $r = x/y$. The closest integer to $r$ is even precisely when
$$r \in \bigcup_{m=0}^{\infty}\left-\tfrac12,\; 2m+\tfrac12\right),$$
where for $m=0$ we use $r\in[0,\tfrac12)$ since $r>0$.

**Distribution of $r=x/y$ on the unit square.** For a threshold $t>0$, the area
of $\{(x,y)\in(0,1)^2 : x/y < t\}$ is
$$F(t)=\begin{cases} t/2, & t\le 1,\\[4pt] 1-\dfrac{1}{2t}, & t\ge 1. \end{cases
}$$
(For $t\le1$: $\int_0^1 ty\,dy=t/2$. For $t\ge1$: $\int_0^{1/t}ty\,dy+\int_{1/t}
^1 1\,dy = \tfrac{1}{2t}+1-\tfrac1t = 1-\tfrac{1}{2t}$.)

**Computing the probability.**

- $m=0$: $\Pr\!\big(0\le r<\tfrac12\big)=F(\tfrac12)=\tfrac14$.

- $m\ge1$: both endpoints $2m\pm\tfrac12\ge \tfrac32>1$, so
$$\Pr\!\big(2m-\tfrac12\le r<2m+\tfrac12\big)=F\!\big(2m+\tfrac12\big)-F\!\big(2
m-\tfrac12\big)=\frac{1}{2(2m-\frac12)}-\frac{1}{2(2m+\frac12)}=\frac{1}{4m-1}-\
frac{1}{4m+1}.$$

Hence
$$\Pr(\text{even})=\frac14+\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{4m-1}-\frac{1}{4m+1
}\right)=\frac14+\left(\frac13-\frac15\right)+\left(\frac17-\frac19\right)+\left
(\frac1{11}-\frac1{13}\right)+\cdots$$

**Evaluating the series.** The Leibniz series gives
$$\frac{\pi}{4}=1-\frac13+\frac15-\frac17+\frac19-\frac1{11}+\frac1{13}-\cdots$$
so
$$1-\frac{\pi}{4}=\frac13-\frac15+\frac17-\frac19+\frac1{11}-\frac1{13}+\cdots$$

Therefore
$$\Pr(\text{even})=\frac14+\left(1-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=\frac54-\frac{\pi}{4}=\f
rac{5-\pi}{4}.$$

**Verification.** The complementary (odd) probability is $\frac{5}{12}+\big(\fra
c15-\frac17\big)+\big(\frac19-\frac1{11}\big)+\cdots$, and summing:
$$\Pr(\text{even})+\Pr(\text{odd})=\frac14+\frac{5}{12}+\frac13=\frac{3+5+4}{12}
=1.\;\checkmark$$

The probability that the closest integer to $x/y$ is even is

$$\boxed{\dfrac{5}{4}-\dfrac{1}{4}\,\pi}$$

i.e. $r=\dfrac54$, $s=-\dfrac14$.
