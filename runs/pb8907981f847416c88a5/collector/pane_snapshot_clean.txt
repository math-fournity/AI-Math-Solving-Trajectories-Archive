## Key Constraints

Let $W$ have $g$ golds, $t$ ties, $\ell$ losses ($g+t+\ell = n-1$), score $s = 3
g+t$. Every opponent needs $g_i \geq g+1$.

**Tied opponents are unavoidable.** If $W$ has no ties ($t=0$), then opponents $
W$ beats need $3(g+1)+t_i' < 3g$, i.e., $3+t_i' < 0$, impossible. So $t \geq 1$.

**Tied opponents force $t \geq 5$.** Each opponent $W$ ties gets 1 point from $W
$ and needs $g_i \geq g+1$. Their score satisfies $1 + 3(g+1) + t_i' < 3g + t$,
giving $t_i' < t - 4$. Since $t_i' \geq 0$, we need $t \geq 5$.

**This gives $n \geq g + 6$** (since $t \leq n-1-g$ and $t \geq 5$).
