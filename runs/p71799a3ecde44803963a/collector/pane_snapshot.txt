The subformulas of $\Box(p \vee q) \rightarrow (\Box p \vee \Box q)$ are:
$$\Gamma = \{p,\; q,\; p \vee q,\; \Box(p \vee q),\; \Box p,\; \Box q,\; \Box p

## Step 2: Compute truth values in $\mathfrak{M}$

**Valuation as subsets of $W$:** Every $w \in W$ is a finite binary string start

\cap W = \{w \in W : w \text{ ends in } 1\}$. (The exclusion of $\sigma = ``1"$

Every world ends in either $0$ or $1$, so:

- **$p \vee q$**: true at every world.
- **$\Box(p \vee q)$**: true at every world (all successors satisfy $p \vee q$).
- **$\Box p$**: false at every world — each $w$ has successor $w1$ (ends in $1$,

- **$\Box q$**: false at every world — each $w$ has successor $w0$ (ends in $0$,

- **$\Box p \vee \Box q$**: false at every world.
- **$\Box(p \vee q) \rightarrow (\Box p \vee \Box q)$**: false at every world (a
ntecedent true, consequent false).

The **only** formulas in $\Gamma$ that vary between worlds are $p$ and $q$, and
they are complementary.

## Step 3: Determine $W^*$

Two worlds are $\Gamma$-equivalent iff they agree on all formulas in $\Gamma$. S

$$A = \{w \in W : w \text{ ends in } 0\}, \qquad B = \{w \in W : w \text{ ends i

$$\boxed{W^* = \{A,\, B\}}$$

## Step 4: Determine $V^*$

$$\boxed{V^*(p) = \{A\}, \qquad V^*(q) = \{B\}}$$

## Step 5: Finest filtration

The finest filtration relation is: $[w]\, R^{\text{fine}}\, [w']$ iff $\exists\,
 w_0 \in [w],\, w_0' \in [w']$ with $w_0\, R\, w_0'$.

- **$A\, R^{\text{fine}}\, A$**: $00 \in A$, $000 \in A$, and $00\, R\, 000$. ✓
- **$A\, R^{\text{fine}}\, B$**: $00 \in A$, $001 \in B$, and $00\, R\, 001$. ✓

- **$B\, R^{\text{fine}}\, B$**: $01 \in B$, $011 \in B$, and $01\, R\, 011$. ✓

So $R^{\text{fine}} = W^* \times W^*$ — the **universal relation**.
