# Problem

In a remote deep-sea research station, a scientist named Dr. Aris is conducting an experiment on an automated mineral extractor. The extractor has a single rotating chamber that, when activated, yields a random integer amount of rare-earth grams from 1 to 6, with each outcome being equally likely.

Dr. Aris is allowed to run the extraction process a maximum of three times to collect a single sample. After the first extraction, he can either accept the current yield or discard it and trigger a second extraction. If he chooses a second extraction, he can then either accept that yield or discard it and trigger a final, third extraction. If he reaches the third extraction, he must accept whatever yield it produces.

Dr. Aris follows an optimal mathematical strategy to maximize the expected value of the final yield he keeps. This expected value can be expressed as a simplified fraction $\frac{m}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers.

What is the value of $m+n$?
