**Claim.** The solutions are $f \equiv 0$ and $f(n)=2n+c$ for an arbitrary const
ant $c\in\mathbb{Z}$.

---

### Step 1. The expression $f(2a)+2f(b)$ depends only on $a+b$.

Let $P(a,b)$ denote the given equation. The right-hand side $f(f(a+b))$ depends
only on $s=a+b$. Hence for every fixed $s$, the quantity $f(2a)+2f(b)$ is consta
nt as $a,b$ range over pairs with $a+b=s$.

Compare $P(a,b)$ with $P(a+1,b-1)$ (both have the same sum $a+b$). The right-han
d sides are equal, so

$$f(2a)+2f(b)=f(2a+2)+2f(b-1),\qquad\forall\,a,b\in\mathbb{Z}.$$

Rearranging,

$$f(2a+2)-f(2a)=2\bigl(f(b)-f(b-1)\bigr),\qquad\forall\,a,b\in\mathbb{Z}.$$

The left side depends only on $a$ and the right side only on $b$, so both sides
equal a common constant $C\in\mathbb{Z}$.

### Step 2. $f$ is affine.

From $2(f(b)-f(b-1))=C$ we get $f(b)-f(b-1)=C/2$. Since $f$ is integer-valued, $
C$ must be even; write $C=2d$ with $d\in\mathbb{Z}$. Then

$$f(b)-f(b-1)=d,\qquad\forall\,b\in\mathbb{Z}.$$

Telescoping from $0$ to $n$ (and similarly for negative $n$) gives

$$f(n)=dn+e,\qquad e:=f(0)\in\mathbb{Z},\ \forall\,n\in\mathbb{Z}.$$

(Consistency check: $f(2a+2)-f(2a)=d\cdot 2=2d=C$. ✓)

### Step 3. Determine $d$ and $e$.

Substitute $f(n)=dn+e$ into $f(2a)+2f(b)=f(f(a+b))$:

- Left: $f(2a)+2f(b)=(2da+e)+2(db+e)=2d(a+b)+3e$.
- Right: $f(f(a+b))=f(d(a+b)+e)=d\bigl(d(a+b)+e\bigr)+e=d^{2}(a+b)+de+e$.

Equating coefficients of the free variable $a+b$ and the constant term:

$$2d=d^{2}\quad\text{and}\quad 3e=de+e.$$

The first gives $d(d-2)=0$, so $d\in\{0,2\}$.

The second gives $e(2-d)=0$.

- **If $d=2$:** $e(2-2)=0$ holds for every $e$, giving $f(n)=2n+e$, $e\in\mathbb
{Z}$ arbitrary.
- **If $d=0$:** $2e=0$, so $e=0$, giving $f\equiv 0$.

### Step 4. Verification.

- $f\equiv 0$: $0+0=0=f(0)$. ✓
- $f(n)=2n+c$: $\;f(2a)+2f(b)=(4a+c)+2(2b+c)=4(a+b)+3c$, and $f(f(a+b))=f(2(a+b)
+c)=2(2(a+b)+c)+c=4(a+b)+3c$. ✓

Both families satisfy the equation, and Step 3 shows they are the only possibili
ties.

---

**Conclusion.** All solutions $f:\mathbb{Z}\to\mathbb{Z}$ are

$$\boxed{f\equiv 0\quad\text{and}\quad f(n)=2n+c\ \text{for any fixed }c\in\math
bb{Z}.}$$
