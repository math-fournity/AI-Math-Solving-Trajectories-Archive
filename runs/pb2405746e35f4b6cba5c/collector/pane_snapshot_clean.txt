## Proof

**Claim.** The inequality $\|u\|_{0,\Omega} \leq C_p\,\|\Delta u\|_{0,\Omega}$ h
olds for all $u \in H = W^{2,2}(\Omega)\cap W^{1,2}_0(\Omega)$, where $\Omega\su
bset\mathbb{R}^2$ is open and bounded.

We establish the estimate in two steps.

---

### Step 1. A Green-type identity

Since $u \in W^{1,2}_0(\Omega)$, the distributional Laplacian $\Delta u$ is well
 defined, and since $u\in W^{2,2}(\Omega)$ we have $\Delta u \in L^2(\Omega)$, c
oinciding a.e. with the pointwise Laplacian. By definition of the distributional
 Laplacian,

$$
\int_\Omega \nabla u\cdot\nabla\varphi\,dx = -\int_\Omega \varphi\,\Delta u\,dx,
\qquad \forall\,\varphi\in C_c^\infty(\Omega).
$$

Since $C_c^\infty(\Omega)$ is dense in $W^{1,2}_0(\Omega)$ and both sides of the
 identity are continuous in $\varphi$ with respect to the $W^{1,2}$-norm, the id
entity extends to every $\varphi\in W^{1,2}_0(\Omega)$. Taking $\varphi = u\in W
^{1,2}_0(\Omega)$ yields

$$
\int_\Omega |\nabla u|^2\,dx = -\int_\Omega u\,\Delta u\,dx. \tag{1}
$$

Note that no boundary regularity of $\Omega$ is required: the boundary term vani
shes because $u$ has zero trace, which is encoded in the weak formulation rather
 than in a classical integration-by-parts on $\partial\Omega$.

---

### Step 2. Poincaré inequality on $W^{1,2}_0(\Omega)$

Since $\Omega$ is bounded, it is bounded in at least one coordinate direction. T
he classical Poincaré inequality for $W^{1,2}_0(\Omega)$ gives a constant $C>0$
(depending only on $\Omega$) such that

$$
\|u\|_{L^2(\Omega)} \leq C\,\|\nabla u\|_{L^2(\Omega)},\qquad \forall\,u\in W^{1
,2}_0(\Omega). \tag{2}
$$

---

### Step 3. Combining the estimates

From (1) and the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality,

$$
\|\nabla u\|_{L^2}^2 = \bigl|-\!\int_\Omega u\,\Delta u\,dx\bigr| \leq \|u\|_{L^
2}\,\|\Delta u\|_{L^2}. \tag{3}
$$

Substituting (2) into (3):

$$
\|\nabla u\|_{L^2}^2 \leq C\,\|\nabla u\|_{L^2}\,\|\Delta u\|_{L^2}.
$$

If $u\neq 0$, then $\|\nabla u\|_{L^2}>0$ (otherwise (2) would force $\|u\|_{L^2
}=0$), and we may divide:

$$
\|\nabla u\|_{L^2} \leq C\,\|\Delta u\|_{L^2}. \tag{4}
$$

(The case $u=0$ is trivial.) Applying (2) once more and then (4):

$$
\|u\|_{L^2} \leq C\,\|\nabla u\|_{L^2} \leq C^2\,\|\Delta u\|_{L^2}.
$$

---

### Conclusion

Setting $C_p = C^2$, where $C$ is the Poincaré constant on $W^{1,2}_0(\Omega)$,
we have

$$
\boxed{\|u\|_{0,\Omega} \leq C_p\,\|\Delta u\|_{0,\Omega},\qquad \forall\,u\in H
 = W^{2,2}(\Omega)\cap W^{1,2}_0(\Omega).}
$$

Hence **the Poincaré-type inequality holds** for the space $H$.
