# Problem

In the cargo hold of a deep-sea submersible, an automated sorting system manages eight specialized canisters labeled by their contents: two are Oxygen (O), four are Fuel (F), and two are Ballast (B). The canisters are currently loaded onto a single-file conveyor belt in the specific order: O, O, F, F, F, F, B, B.

The sorting system is programmed with only three allowable mechanical operations to rearrange the canisters:
1. It can swap the positions of an Oxygen canister and a Fuel canister if they are adjacent.
2. It can swap the positions of a Fuel canister and a Ballast canister if they are adjacent.
3. It can swap the positions of a Ballast canister and an Oxygen canister if they are adjacent.

The system can perform these operations any number of times (including zero) in any order. Calculate the total number of distinct linear sequences of the eight canisters that the system can possibly generate.
