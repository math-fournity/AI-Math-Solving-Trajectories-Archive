### Proof of `upper_bound` (Graver–Yackel)

**Theorem.** For $k \geq 3$, $f_k(n) \ll (n \log\log n / \log n)^{1 - 1/(k-1)}$.

*Proof sketch.* This follows from upper bounds on off-diagonal Ramsey numbers. T
he Graver–Yackel result [GrYa68] establishes:
$$R(k, m) \leq C_k \cdot \left(\frac{m \log\log m}{\log m}\right)^{k-1}$$
for constants $C_k > 0$ and all $m \geq m_0(k)$.

This means: every $K_k$-free graph on $N \geq C_k (m \log\log m / \log m)^{k-1}$
 vertices has an independent set of size $m$. Equivalently, every $K_k$-free gra
ph on $n$ vertices has:
$$\alpha(G) \geq c_k \cdot \left(\frac{n \log n}{\log\log n}\right)^{1/(k-1)}$$
for a constant $c_k > 0$ (obtained by inverting the Ramsey bound: solve $n = C_k
 (m \log\log m / \log m)^{k-1}$ for $m$).

Therefore, for any $K_k$-free graph $G$ on $n$ vertices:
$$\chi(G) \leq \frac{n}{\alpha(G)} \leq \frac{1}{c_k} \cdot \frac{n}{(n \log n /
 \log\log n)^{1/(k-1)}} = \frac{1}{c_k} \cdot \left(\frac{n \log\log n}{\log n}\
right)^{1 - 1/(k-1)}.$$

Taking the maximum over all $K_k$-free graphs: $f_k(n) \leq \frac{1}{c_k} (n \lo
g\log n / \log n)^{1-1/(k-1)}$, i.e., $f_k(n) \ll (n \log\log n / \log n)^{1-1/(
k-1)}$.

*Remark.* The Graver–Yackel bound has since been strengthened by Ajtai–Komlós–Sz
emerédi (1980) and Shearer, who proved $\alpha(G) \geq c \cdot n^{1/(k-1)} (\log
 n)^{(k-2)/(k-1)}$ for $K_k$-free graphs, giving the stronger bound $f_k(n) \ll
n^{1-1/(k-1)} / (\log n)^{(k-2)/(k-1)}$ (without the $\log\log n$ factor). The s
tated Graver–Yackel bound follows a fortiori. $\square$
