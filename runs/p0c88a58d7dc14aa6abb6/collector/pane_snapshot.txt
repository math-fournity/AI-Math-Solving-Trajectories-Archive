## Solution

**Setup.** Introduce the substitutions
$$s = x + z, \qquad d = z - x.$$
Then
$$s^2 + d^2 = (x+z)^2 + (z-x)^2 = 2(x^2+z^2) = 2,$$
so the sensor constraint becomes $s^2 + d^2 = 2$. The equilibrium equation becom
es
$$y^2 + 2ys = 6,$$
and the quantity to maximize is $yd$.

**Eliminate $s$.** Since $y = 0$ would give $0 = 6$ (impossible), $y \neq 0$ and
$$s = \frac{6 - y^2}{2y}.$$

**Reduce to one variable.** Set $t = y^2 > 0$. Then
$$s^2 = \frac{(6-t)^2}{4t}, \qquad d^2 = 2 - s^2 = \frac{8t - (6-t)^2}{4t} = \fr
ac{-t^2 + 20t - 36}{4t}.$$
Feasibility requires $d^2 \geq 0$, i.e. $-t^2+20t-36 \geq 0$, i.e. $(t-2)(t-18)\
leq 0$, giving $t \in [2,18]$.

Since $d$ may be chosen with either sign, we pick $\operatorname{sgn}(d) = \oper
atorname{sgn}(y)$ to maximize $yd$. Hence
$$(yd)^2 = t\,d^2 = t\cdot\frac{-t^2+20t-36}{4t} = \frac{-t^2+20t-36}{4}.$$

**Maximize.** Define
$$f(t) = \frac{-t^2+20t-36}{4} = \frac{-(t-10)^2 + 64}{4}.$$
This is a downward parabola with maximum at $t = 10 \in [2,18]$, yielding
$$f(10) = \frac{64}{4} = 16.$$
Therefore $(yd)^2 \leq 16$, so $yd \leq 4$.

**Attainment check.** At $t = 10$: $y = \sqrt{10}$, $s = \frac{6-10}{2\sqrt{10}}
 = -\frac{2}{\sqrt{10}}$, $s^2 = \frac{2}{5}$, $d^2 = 2 - \frac{2}{5} = \frac{8}
{5}$, $d = \sqrt{\tfrac{8}{5}}$. Then
$$yd = \sqrt{10}\cdot\sqrt{\tfrac{8}{5}} = \sqrt{\tfrac{80}{5}} = \sqrt{16} = 4.
$$
The constraints are satisfied: $s^2+d^2 = \tfrac{2}{5}+\tfrac{8}{5}=2$, hence $x
^2+z^2=1$, and $y^2+2ys = 10 + 2\sqrt{10}\bigl(-\tfrac{2}{\sqrt{10}}\bigr) = 10
- 4 = 6$. ✓

**Conclusion.** The maximum possible value of $y(z-x)$ is

$$\boxed{4}.$$
