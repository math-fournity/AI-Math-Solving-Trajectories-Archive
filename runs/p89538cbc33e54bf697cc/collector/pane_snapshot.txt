Looking at this problem, I need to find the maximum of T = Σ dist(A_i, A_{i+1})
for 13 sensors on a circle of radius R = 10, then compute f(13,10)/sin(π/12).

## Proof

**Setup.** Place 13 sensors on a circle of radius R = 10. Label them A₁, A₂, ...
, A₁₃ in order around the circle. Let a₁, a₂, ..., a₁₃ be the arc lengths (centr
al angles) between consecutive sensors, so a_i > 0 and a₁ + a₂ + ... + a₁₃ = 2π.

The chord length between sensors separated by central angle a is 2R sin(a/2). Th
e total is:

$$T = \sum_{i=1}^{12} 2R \sin\!\left(\frac{a_i}{2}\right)$$

(12 of the 13 arcs are included; the arc a₁₃ from A₁₃ back to A₁ is excluded.)

**Concavity.** The function g(x) = sin(x/2) has second derivative g''(x) = −¼ si
n(x/2) ≤ 0 for x ∈ [0, 2π], so g is **concave** on [0, 2π].

**Applying Jensen's inequality.** Let S = a₁ + a₂ + ... + a₁₂ = 2π − a₁₃. By con
cavity:

$$\frac{1}{12}\sum_{i=1}^{12} \sin\!\left(\frac{a_i}{2}\right) \leq \sin\!\left(
\frac{S}{24}\right)$$

so

$$\sum_{i=1}^{12} \sin\!\left(\frac{a_i}{2}\right) \leq 12\,\sin\!\left(\frac{S}
{24}\right).$$

**Maximizing over S.** Since S ≤ 2π and S/24 ≤ π/12 < π, and sin is increasing o
n [0, π]:

$$12\,\sin\!\left(\frac{S}{24}\right) \leq 12\,\sin\!\left(\frac{2\pi}{24}\right
) = 12\,\sin\!\left(\frac{\pi}{12}\right).$$

**Equality conditions.** Equality in Jensen requires a₁ = a₂ = ... = a₁₂ = S/12.
 Equality in the second step requires S = 2π, i.e., a₁₃ = 0. Together: a₁ = ...
= a₁₂ = π/6 and a₁₃ → 0. This corresponds to 12 equally spaced points (a regular
 12-gon) with the 13th sensor coinciding with A₁ (or approaching it). The suprem
um is achieved in the limit.

**Computing f(13, 10).** The maximum theoretical value is:

$$f(13, 10) = 2R \cdot 12 \cdot \sin\!\left(\frac{\pi}{12}\right) = 24 \cdot 10
\cdot \sin\!\left(\frac{\pi}{12}\right) = 240\,\sin\!\left(\frac{\pi}{12}\right)
.$$

**Final computation.**

$$\frac{f(13, 10)}{\sin(\pi/12)} = \frac{240\,\sin(\pi/12)}{\sin(\pi/12)} = \box
ed{240}$$
