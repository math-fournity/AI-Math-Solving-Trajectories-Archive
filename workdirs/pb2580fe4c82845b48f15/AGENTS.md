During high-speed simulations, the team discovered a precise equilibrium state where the square root of this Impact Factor is exactly equal to the difference between the stabilization constant $b$ and twice the product of the compression ratio $a$ and the thermal coefficient $c$.

$$\sqrt{b^2 - 4ac} = b - 2ac$$

Based on this equilibrium requirement, what is the minimum possible value that the Impact Factor, $b^2 - 4ac$, can take?
