## Solution

Let $t_i$ denote the total cumulative operation time (in minutes) of submersible
 $S_i$ across all three 90-minute phases. Since exactly one submersible is activ
e at every instant and coverage is continuous over $3 \times 90 = 270$ minutes,

$$t_1 + t_2 + \cdots + t_7 = 270, \qquad t_i \ge 0.$$

The divisibility constraints give
$$t_1,t_2,t_3,t_4 \in 7\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}, \qquad t_5,t_6,t_7 \in 13\mathbb{Z}_{
\ge 0}.$$

Write $t_i = 7a_i$ for $i=1,2,3,4$ and $t_{4+j} = 13b_j$ for $j=1,2,3$, with $a_
i, b_j \ge 0$ integers. Then

$$7(a_1+a_2+a_3+a_4) + 13(b_1+b_2+b_3) = 270.$$

Let $A = a_1+a_2+a_3+a_4$ and $B = b_1+b_2+b_3$. We need non-negative integer so
lutions of

$$7A + 13B = 270.$$

**Finding all $(A,B)$.** Since $270 \equiv 4 \pmod 7$ and $13 \equiv -1 \pmod 7$
, we need $-B \equiv 4 \pmod 7$, i.e. $B \equiv 3 \pmod 7$. With $13B \le 270$ (
so $B \le 20$), the admissible values are $B = 3, 10, 17$:

| $B$ | $13B$ | $7A = 270-13B$ | $A$ |
|-----|-------|----------------|-----|
| 3   | 39    | 231            | 33  |
| 10  | 130   | 140            | 20  |
| 17  | 221   | 49             | 7   |

**Counting compositions.** For fixed $A$, the number of non-negative 4-tuples $(
a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4)$ summing to $A$ is $\binom{A+3}{3}$. For fixed $B$, the number
of non-negative 3-tuples $(b_1,b_2,b_3)$ summing to $B$ is $\binom{B+2}{2}$.

- **$B=3,\ A=33$:** $\binom{36}{3}\binom{5}{2} = 7140 \cdot 10 = 71\,400.$
- **$B=10,\ A=20$:** $\binom{23}{3}\binom{12}{2} = 1771 \cdot 66 = 116\,886.$
- **$B=17,\ A=7$:** $\binom{10}{3}\binom{19}{2} = 120 \cdot 171 = 20\,520.$

**Total:**

$$71\,400 + 116\,886 + 20\,520 = 208\,806.$$

Each valid $(A,B)$ together with a composition of $A$ into four parts and a comp
osition of $B$ into three parts uniquely determines a 7-tuple $(t_1,\dots,t_7)$,
 and conversely every admissible time distribution arises this way. Hence the nu
mber of different scenarios is

$$\boxed{208\,806}.$$
