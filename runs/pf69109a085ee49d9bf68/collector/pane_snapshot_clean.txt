## Answer

**Yes**, $W^{2,p}(I) \hookrightarrow\hookrightarrow C^1(\overline{I})$ compactly
 for $I=(0,1)$ and $p>1$.

---

## Proof

**Step 1: Continuous embedding into a Hölder space.**

Since $n=1$, $m=2$, and $p>1$, we have $m - n/p = 2 - 1/p > 1$. By the Sobolev e
mbedding theorem, for any $\alpha$ with $0 < \alpha < 1 - 1/p$,

$$W^{2,p}(I) \hookrightarrow C^{1,\alpha}(\overline{I}),$$

and in particular $W^{2,p}(I) \hookrightarrow C^1(\overline{I})$ continuously. C
oncretely, every $u \in W^{2,p}(I)$ admits a representative in $C^1(\overline{I}
)$ whose derivative $u'$ is Hölder continuous of exponent $\alpha$, and there ex
ists $C = C(p,\alpha) > 0$ with

$$\|u\|_{C^{1,\alpha}(\overline{I})} \leq C\,\|u\|_{W^{2,p}(I)}.$$

**Step 2: Compactness via Arzelà–Ascoli.**

Let $\{u_n\}_{n\geq 1}$ be a bounded sequence in $W^{2,p}(I)$, say $\|u_n\|_{W^{
2,p}} \leq M$. Fix any $\alpha \in (0,\, 1-1/p)$. By Step 1,

$$\|u_n\|_{C^{1,\alpha}(\overline{I})} \leq C M \quad \text{for all } n.$$

This gives:

- **Uniform boundedness of $\{u_n\}$ and $\{u_n'\}$ in $C^0(\overline{I})$**: $\
|u_n\|_\infty, \|u_n'\|_\infty \leq CM$.
- **Equicontinuity of $\{u_n'\}$**: since $u_n'$ is $\alpha$-Hölder with $\|u_n'
\|_{C^{0,\alpha}} \leq CM$,
  $$|u_n'(x) - u_n'(y)| \leq CM\,|x-y|^\alpha, \quad \forall\, x,y \in \overline
{I},\ \forall\, n.$$
- **Equicontinuity of $\{u_n\}$**: since $\|u_n'\|_\infty \leq CM$,
  $$|u_n(x) - u_n(y)| \leq CM\,|x-y|, \quad \forall\, x,y \in \overline{I},\ \fo
rall\, n.$$

By the **Arzelà–Ascoli theorem**, $\{u_n'\}$ has a subsequence $\{u_{n_k}'\}$ co
nverging in $C^0(\overline{I})$ to some $v \in C^0(\overline{I})$. Applying Arze
là–Ascoli again to $\{u_{n_k}\}$ (uniformly bounded and equicontinuous), we extr
act a further subsequence (still denoted $u_{n_k}$) converging in $C^0(\overline
{I})$ to some $u \in C^0(\overline{I})$.

Since $u_{n_k} \to u$ uniformly and $u_{n_k}' \to v$ uniformly, we have $u \in C
^1(\overline{I})$ with $u' = v$, and

$$\|u_{n_k} - u\|_{C^1(\overline{I})} = \|u_{n_k} - u\|_\infty + \|u_{n_k}' - u'
\|_\infty \to 0.$$

**Step 3: Conclusion.**

Every bounded sequence in $W^{2,p}(I)$ admits a subsequence converging in $C^1(\
overline{I})$. Therefore the embedding

$$W^{2,p}(I) \hookrightarrow\hookrightarrow C^1(\overline{I})$$

is **compact** for every $p > 1$.

**Remark.** The embedding $W^{2,p}(I) \hookrightarrow C^{1,\,1-1/p}(\overline{I}
)$ at the *critical* Hölder exponent $\alpha = 1-1/p$ is only continuous (not co
mpact) — this is the borderline case of the Sobolev embedding theorem. However,
compactness *is* recovered when we relax the target to $C^1(\overline{I})$, beca
use the Hölder regularity of $u'$ (at any subcritical exponent) supplies exactly
 the equicontinuity that Arzelà–Ascoli requires. In other words,

$$W^{2,p}(I) \hookrightarrow C^{1,\alpha}(\overline{I}) \hookrightarrow\hookrigh
tarrow C^1(\overline{I}), \quad 0 < \alpha < 1 - \tfrac{1}{p},$$

and the composition of a continuous embedding with a compact embedding is compac
t. $\blacksquare$
