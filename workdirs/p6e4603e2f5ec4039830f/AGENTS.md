# Problem

In a remote desert, two straight, paved roads, $OA$ and $OB$, originate from a central observation tower located at point $O$. Both roads have a length of exactly $5$ kilometers. Surveyors have measured the straight-line distance (the chord) between the two endpoints $A$ and $B$ to be exactly $6$ kilometers.

A solar power company intends to install a perfectly square solar array, $PQRS$, within the wedge-shaped region bounded by the two roads and the curved fence. The placement must be precise: one corner of the square, $P$, must lie on the road $OA$, and the adjacent corner, $S$, must lie on the road $OB$. The remaining two corners, $Q$ and $R$, must be positioned exactly on the curved perimeter fence between $A$ and $B$.

Calculate the total surface area occupied by the solar array $PQRS$.
