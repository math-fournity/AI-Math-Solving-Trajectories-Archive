# The Todd Orientation MU → K as an E_∞ Ring Map

**Answer: Yes.** The map MU → K constructed in Adams' *Stable Homotopy and Gener
alised Homology* (the Todd orientation) extends to a map of commutative ring spe
ctra (E_∞ ring spectra) in modern categories of spectra. This is a theorem of An
do, and of Ando–Hopkins–Strickland.

---

## Proof

### Step 1. Both source and target are E_∞ ring spectra

**MU** is an E_∞ ring spectrum. It is the Thom spectrum of the tautological stab
le complex vector bundle over BU. The space BU carries an E_∞ structure (it is t
he classifying space of the symmetric monoidal groupoid of finite-dimensional co
mplex vector spaces, equivalently the group completion of ∐ BU(n)), and the taut
ological bundle is compatible with this E_∞ structure. In modern language, the T
hom spectrum of an E_∞ map X → Pic(𝕊) is an E_∞ ring spectrum; the map BU → BGL₁
(𝕊) classifying the tautological bundle is E_∞, so MU is E_∞ (Mahowald, May, Lew
is–Priddy; modern form: ABGHR).

**K** (complex K-theory) is an E_∞ ring spectrum. This follows from the symmetri
c monoidal structure on the category of finite-dimensional complex vector bundle
s (the Segal Γ-space construction): K is the group completion of the E_∞ space ∐
 BU(n), hence an E_∞ ring spectrum. It is modeled as such in symmetric spectra,
orthogonal spectra, and S-modules.

### Step 2. The map in Adams' blue book

The orientation MU → K is determined by the complex orientation of K-theory. Wit
h the Thom class
$$t = L - 1 \in K^0(\mathbb{CP}^\infty), \qquad L = \text{tautological line bund
le},$$
we have $K^0(\mathbb{CP}^\infty) \cong \mathbb{Z}[[t]]$, and the tensor product
of line bundles gives the **multiplicative formal group law**
$$F(x,y) = x + y + xy.$$
By Quillen's theorem, MU_* carries the universal formal group law, so the multip
licative FGL is classified by a unique ring map
$$\mathrm{Todd}: MU_* \longrightarrow K_* = \mathbb{Z}[\beta^{\pm 1}],$$
the **Todd genus**. The equivalence between complex orientations of a (homotopy)
 ring spectrum E and homotopy-ring-spectrum maps MU → E then yields the map of h
omotopy ring spectra MU → K appearing in Adams. This is the map in question.

### Step 3. Ando's criterion for E_∞ orientations

Ando (Ph.D. thesis, MIT, 1995, *The sigma orientation*) established the followin
g characterization. For an E_∞ ring spectrum E, the space of **E_∞ ring maps** M
U → E is equivalent to the space of **E_∞ orientations** of E. An E_∞ orientatio
n is a complex orientation $t \in E^0(\mathbb{CP}^\infty)$ (a Thom class for the
 tautological line bundle, restricting to 1 at the basepoint) satisfying a **pow
er-operation compatibility condition**:

> **(★)** For each prime $p$, the $p$-th power operation
> $$\mathbb{P}_p(t) \in E^0\!\bigl(\mathbb{CP}^\infty \times_{\Sigma_p} (\mathbb
{CP}^\infty)^p\bigr)$$
> equals the Thom class of the bundle $\bigoplus_{i=1}^p L_i$ pulled back along
the evident map, where $L_i$ is the tautological line bundle on the $i$-th facto
r.

In words: the total power of the Thom class must equal the Thom class of the dir
ect sum. This is the precise sense in which the orientation must be compatible w
ith the E_∞ structure.

### Step 4. Verification for K-theory

For $E = K$ with $t = L - 1$, the E_∞ power operations on K-theory are the (refi
ned) Adams operations. Ando verified condition (★) directly:

- The Adams operations satisfy $\psi^k(L) = L^k$ on the tautological line bundle
.
- The Thom class is multiplicative: the Thom class of a direct sum of line bundl
es is the product of the Thom classes, so $\mathrm{Thom}(\bigoplus_i L_i) = \pro
d_i (L_i - 1)$.
- The power operation $\mathbb{P}_p(L-1)$ decomposes compatibly with the $\Sigma
_p$-action and the transfer, and the key identity
$$\mathbb{P}_p(L - 1) \;=\; \prod_{i=1}^{p}(L_i - 1) \quad \text{(in the appropr
iate } K^0(\mathbb{CP}^\infty \times_{\Sigma_p}(\mathbb{CP}^\infty)^p)\text{)}$$
holds, using $\psi^p(L) = L^p$ and the multiplicativity of the Thom class.

Thus condition (★) is satisfied, and by Ando's criterion the Todd orientation $t
 = L-1$ extends to an **E_∞ ring map** MU → K.

### Step 5. Conceptual form: the sigma orientation (Ando–Hopkins–Strickland)

Ando–Hopkins–Strickland (*The sigma orientation*; *Elliptic spectra, Witten seri
es, and tmf*) placed this in a general framework:

- An **elliptic E_∞ ring spectrum** $E$ (in the AHS sense) is an E_∞ ring equipp
ed with an elliptic curve over $E_0$ and a compatible complex orientation.
- They construct the **sigma orientation**, a canonical E_∞ ring map MU → E for
every elliptic E_∞ ring spectrum $E$, and prove it is E_∞.
- **K-theory is the special case of the Tate curve** $T = \widehat{\mathbb{G}_m}
$ (the Tate curve over $\mathbb{Z}((q))$, degenerating to the multiplicative gro
up). The sigma orientation for the Tate curve is exactly the Todd orientation MU
 → K.

Hence the Todd orientation is E_∞ as a special case of the AHS sigma-orientation
 theorem.

### Step 6. Independence of the category of spectra

The E_∞ (commutative ring spectrum) structures and maps are preserved under the
Quillen equivalences between the modern symmetric monoidal stable model categori
es of spectra — symmetric spectra, orthogonal spectra, S-modules (EKMM), and pre
sheaf spectra (Schwede–Shipley, Mandell–May). An E_∞ ring map MU → K in any one
of these categories transfers, under the monoidal Quillen equivalence, to an E_∞
 ring map in any other. Therefore the extension exists in **every** modern categ
ory of spectra.

### Step 7. Agreement with the Adams map

The E_∞ map constructed in Step 4 (equivalently Step 5) realizes, on homotopy gr
oups, the ring map classifying the multiplicative FGL — i.e., the Todd genus. Si
nce the complex orientation of K-theory is unique up to the sign convention $t \
leftrightarrow -t$ (both give the multiplicative FGL), the underlying homotopy-r
ing-spectrum map of Ando's E_∞ map is homotopic to the map in Adams' blue book.
Hence the Adams map **extends** (not merely "is replaced by") an E_∞ ring map.

## Conclusion

The map MU → K from Adams' blue book — the Todd orientation, classifying the mul
tiplicative formal group law — **does extend** to a map of commutative ring spec
tra (E_∞ ring spectra) in modern categories of spectra. This is a theorem of And
o (verified directly via the power-operation criterion) and of Ando–Hopkins–Stri
ckland (as the Tate-curve case of the sigma orientation). The result is independ
ent of the choice of modern symmetric monoidal model category of spectra, by mon
oidal Quillen equivalence.
