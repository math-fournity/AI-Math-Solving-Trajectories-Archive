## Solution

We solve $\partial_t u = \kappa \Delta u$ on $\mathbb{X} = [0,X]\times[0,Y]$ wit
h $u=0$ on $\partial\mathbb{X}$ and $u(x,y,0)=f(x,y)$ by separation of variables
.

**Step 1: Separation.** Seek solutions $u(x,y,t)=v(x,y)T(t)$. Substituting:
$$v\,T' = \kappa\, T\, \Delta v \implies \frac{T'}{\kappa T} = \frac{\Delta v}{v
} = -\lambda,$$
for a separation constant $-\lambda$ (the negative sign is forced by the require
ment that $T$ remain bounded, since $\Delta v = -\lambda v$ with Dirichlet condi
tions yields $\lambda>0$, as shown below). This gives
$$T'(t) + \kappa\lambda\, T(t)=0, \qquad \Delta v + \lambda v = 0 \text{ on } \m
athbb{X},\quad v|_{\partial\mathbb{X}}=0.$$

**Step 2: Spatial eigenvalue problem.** Separate further $v(x,y)=P(x)Q(y)$. Then
$$P''Q + PQ'' + \lambda PQ = 0 \implies \frac{P''}{P} + \frac{Q''}{Q} = -\lambda
.$$
Set $P''/P = -\mu$, $Q''/Q = -\nu$ with $\mu+\nu=\lambda$. The 1D Dirichlet prob
lems on $[0,X]$ and $[0,Y]$ give
$$\mu_m = \left(\frac{m\pi}{X}\right)^2,\quad P_m(x)=\sin\!\left(\frac{m\pi x}{X
}\right),\quad m=1,2,\dots$$
$$\nu_n = \left(\frac{n\pi}{Y}\right)^2,\quad Q_n(y)=\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi y}{Y
}\right),\quad n=1,2,\dots$$
(Only positive eigenvalues arise; $\mu\le 0$ or $\nu\le 0$ force the trivial sol
ution under Dirichlet conditions.) Hence the 2D eigenpairs are
$$v_{mn}(x,y) = \sin\!\left(\frac{m\pi x}{X}\right)\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi y}{Y}\
right),\qquad \lambda_{mn}=\left(\frac{m\pi}{X}\right)^2+\left(\frac{n\pi}{Y}\ri
ght)^2,\quad m,n\ge 1.$$

**Step 3: Time factor.** For each $(m,n)$,
$$T_{mn}(t) = e^{-\kappa\lambda_{mn}t}.$$

**Step 4: Expansion.** The family $\{v_{mn}\}$ is a complete orthogonal basis of
 $L^2(\mathbb{X})$ (product of two complete 1D sine bases). Orthogonality:
$$\int_0^X\!\int_0^Y v_{mn}\,v_{m'n'}\,dy\,dx = \begin{cases}\frac{XY}{4}, & (m,
n)=(m',n'),\\ 0, & \text{otherwise.}\end{cases}$$

Expanding the initial data,
$$f(x,y) = \sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} A_{mn}\,\sin\!\left(\frac{m\pi

the coefficients are obtained by orthogonality:
$$A_{mn} = \frac{4}{XY}\int_0^X\!\int_0^Y f(x,y)\,\sin\!\left(\frac{m\pi x}{X}\r
ight)\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi y}{Y}\right)\,dy\,dx.$$

**Step 5: Solution.** Superposing the separated solutions,
$$\boxed{\,u(x,y,t) = \sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} A_{mn}\,\sin\!\left
(\frac{m\pi x}{X}\right)\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi y}{Y}\right)\,\exp\!\left[-\kappa
\left(\left(\frac{m\pi}{X}\right)^2+\left(\frac{n\pi}{Y}\right)^2\right)t\right]

$$A_{mn} = \frac{4}{XY}\int_0^X\!\int_0^Y f(\xi,\eta)\,\sin\!\left(\frac{m\pi \x
i}{X}\right)\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi \eta}{Y}\right)\,d\eta\,d\xi.$$

**Verification.**
- *PDE:* Each term satisfies $\partial_t(\cdots) = -\kappa\lambda_{mn}(\cdots)$
and $\Delta v_{mn} = -\lambda_{mn}v_{mn}$, so $\partial_t u = \kappa\Delta u$ te
rmwise.
- *Boundary:* Every $v_{mn}$ vanishes on $\{0,X\}\times[0,Y]$ and $[0,X]\times\{
