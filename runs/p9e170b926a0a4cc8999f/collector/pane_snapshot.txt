ans intersecting every basic open interval.

**Setup.** Let $\mathcal{B}$ be the collection of all non-empty basic open inter

 = \mathfrak{c}$. Since $|\mathcal{B}| = \mathfrak{c}$ (elements are determined
by two real endpoints), we have $|\mathcal{B} \times \mathcal{B}| = \mathfrak{c}

c}\}$,
- $U = \{a_\alpha : \alpha < \mathfrak{c}\}$ (for domain coverage),

Since $\mathfrak{c} \cdot 3 = \mathfrak{c}$, fix a bijection $\sigma: \mathfrak{
