## Solution

### Step 1. Simplify the right-hand side

Using product-to-sum and power-reduction identities:

$$4\cos x\cos 3x = 2[\cos 4x + \cos 2x] = 2\cos 4x + 2\cos 2x,$$

$$6\sin^2 x = 6\cdot\frac{1-\cos 2x}{2} = 3 - 3\cos 2x.$$

Adding:

$$\text{RHS} = 3 - \cos 2x + 2\cos 4x.$$

$$r^3 - 2r^2 + 2r = 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; r\,(r^2-2r+2)=0 \;\Longrightarrow\; r=
