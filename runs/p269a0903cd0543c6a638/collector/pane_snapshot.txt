## Principal Curvatures of the Monge Patch

**Setup.** Let $u = u^1$, $v = u^2$, and write partial derivatives as $p = f_u$,
 $q = f_v$, $r = f_{uu}$, $s = f_{uv}$, $t = f_{vv}$. The surface is the graph
$$\mathbf{r}(u,v) = (u,\, v,\, f(u,v)).$$

**First fundamental form.** The tangent vectors are
$$\mathbf{r}_u = (1,0,p), \qquad \mathbf{r}_v = (0,1,q),$$
so
$$E = 1+p^2,\qquad F = pq,\qquad G = 1+q^2,\qquad EG-F^2 = 1+p^2+q^2 =: W^2,$$
where $W = \sqrt{1+p^2+q^2}$.

**Unit normal.**
$$\mathbf{r}_u \times \mathbf{r}_v = (-p,-q,1), \qquad \mathbf{n} = \frac{1}{W}(
-p,-q,1).$$

**Second fundamental form.** Since $\mathbf{r}_{uu}=(0,0,r)$, $\mathbf{r}_{uv}=(
0,0,s)$, $\mathbf{r}_{vv}=(0,0,t)$,
$$L = \mathbf{r}_{uu}\cdot\mathbf{n} = \frac{r}{W},\quad M = \mathbf{r}_{uv}\cdo
t\mathbf{n} = \frac{s}{W},\quad N = \mathbf{r}_{vv}\cdot\mathbf{n} = \frac{t}{W}
.$$

**Shape operator / curvature equation.** The principal curvatures $\kappa_1,\kap
pa_2$ are the eigenvalues of the shape operator, i.e. the roots of
$$(EG-F^2)\,\kappa^2 - (EN+GL-2FM)\,\kappa + (LN-M^2) = 0.$$

Substituting:
- $EG-F^2 = W^2$,
- $EN+GL-2FM = \dfrac{(1+p^2)t + (1+q^2)r - 2pqs}{W}$,
- $LN-M^2 = \dfrac{rt-s^2}{W^2}$.

Hence
$$W^2\,\kappa^2 - \frac{(1+p^2)t + (1+q^2)r - 2pqs}{W}\,\kappa + \frac{rt-s^2}{W
^2} = 0.$$

**Mean and Gaussian curvature.** Dividing by $W^2$ and identifying the standard
formulas:
$$H = \frac{(1+q^2)r - 2pqs + (1+p^2)t}{2\,W^3}, \qquad K = \frac{rt - s^2}{W^4}
.$$

**Principal curvatures.** Since $\kappa_{1,2} = H \pm \sqrt{H^2 - K}$,

$$\boxed{\;\kappa_{1,2} = \frac{(1+q^2)r - 2pqs + (1+p^2)t}{2\,W^3} \;\pm\; \sqr
t{\left(\frac{(1+q^2)r - 2pqs + (1+p^2)t}{2\,W^3}\right)^{\!2} - \frac{rt-s^2}{W
^4}}\;}$$

where $p=f_{u^1},\; q=f_{u^2},\; r=f_{u^1u^1},\; s=f_{u^1u^2},\; t=f_{u^2u^2},\;
 W=\sqrt{1+p^2+q^2}$.

Equivalently, in fully explicit partial-derivative notation:
$$\kappa_{1,2} = \frac{(1+f_{u^2}^2)\,f_{u^1u^1} - 2\,f_{u^1}f_{u^2}\,f_{u^1u^2}
 + (1+f_{u^1}^2)\,f_{u^2u^2}}{2\,(1+f_{u^1}^2+f_{u^2}^2)^{3/2}} \;\pm\; \sqrt{H^
2 - \frac{f_{u^1u^1}\,f_{u^2u^2} - f_{u^1u^2}^2}{(1+f_{u^1}^2+f_{u^2}^2)^2}}.$$

These are the two principal curvatures of the Monge patch $S$ at the point $(u^1
,u^2,f(u^1,u^2))$.
