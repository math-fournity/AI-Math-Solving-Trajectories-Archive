### Key reformulation via runs

Write a binary sequence as alternating runs of consecutive equal bits, with leng
ths $r_1, r_2, \dots, r_k$. Let

- $S_0$ = total number of $0$'s, $\;S_1$ = total number of $1$'s $\;(\text{so }
S_0+S_1=n)$,
- $k_0$ = number of $0$-runs, $\;k_1$ = number of $1$-runs.

A run of $0$'s of length $r$ contributes $r-1$ occurrences of `00`, and similarl
y for `11`. Hence

$$
\#00 = S_0 - k_0, \qquad \#11 = S_1 - k_1.
$$

The condition $\#00=\#11$ is therefore

$$
S_0 - k_0 = S_1 - k_1 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad S_0 - S_1 = k_0 - k_1. \tag{
$\ast$}
$$

Since runs alternate, $|k_0-k_1|\le 1$, with the sign determined by the endpoint
s:

- **Different endpoints** (starts with $0$, ends with $1$, or vice versa): $k_0=
k_1$, so $(\ast)$ gives $S_0=S_1=n/2$. Thus $n$ must be **even**.
- **Same endpoint $0$** (starts and ends with $0$): $k_0=k_1+1$, so $(\ast)$ giv
es $S_0-S_1=1$, i.e. $S_0=(n+1)/2$. Thus $n$ must be **odd**.
- **Same endpoint $1$**: symmetric, $S_1=(n+1)/2$, $n$ **odd**.

### Even case: $n=2m$

We count sequences with $m$ zeros and $m$ ones, starting and ending with *differ
ent* bits.

- Start $0$, end $1$: the middle $2m-2$ positions hold $m-1$ zeros and $m-1$ one
s → $\binom{2m-2}{m-1}$ sequences.
- Start $1$, end $0$: by symmetry, another $\binom{2m-2}{m-1}$.

$$
a_{2m}=2\binom{2m-2}{m-1}.
$$

Check: $a_2=2\binom00=2$ ✓, $\;a_4=2\binom21=4$ ✓.

### Odd case: $n=2m+1$

By the $0\leftrightarrow1$ symmetry, the two sub-cases (same-endpoint $0$ and sa
me-endpoint $1$) contribute equally. Count sequences with $m+1$ zeros, $m$ ones,
 starting and ending with $0$:

- Fix first $=0$, last $=0$; the middle $2m-1$ positions hold $m-1$ zeros and $m
$ ones → $\binom{2m-1}{m}$ sequences.

$$
a_{2m+1}=2\binom{2m-1}{m}.
$$

Check: $a_3=2\binom11=2$ ✓.
