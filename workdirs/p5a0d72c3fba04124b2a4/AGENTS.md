# Problem

In the city of Arithmos, three specialized workshops—A, B, and C—operate on cyclic schedules. Each workshop completes one production cycle every $a$, $b$, and $c$ days, respectively, where $a, b,$ and $c$ are any positive integers.

A synchronization event between two workshops occurs whenever their cycles align. 
- The interval between alignments for workshops A and B is the least common multiple of their cycle lengths: $L_{AB} = \text{LCM}[a, b]$.
- The interval between alignments for workshops B and C is $L_{BC} = \text{LCM}[b, c]$.
- The interval between alignments for workshops C and A is $L_{CA} = \text{LCM}[c, a]$.

A "Grand Total Synchronicity" value, $n$, is defined as the sum of these three intervals: $n = L_{AB} + L_{BC} + L_{CA}$. 

Let $S$ be the set of all possible natural numbers $n$ that can be generated by varying the cycle lengths $a, b,$ and $c$ across all possible positive integers. 

Consider the range of integers from 1 to 35, inclusive. Some integers in this range are impossible to achieve as a Grand Total Synchronicity value (i.e., they are not elements of $S$). 

Calculate the sum of all integers $n$ such that $1 \le n \le 35$ and $n \notin S$.
