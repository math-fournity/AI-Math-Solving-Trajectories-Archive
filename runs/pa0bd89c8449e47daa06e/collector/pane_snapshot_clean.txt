# Lower Bounds for the Genus of Complete Graphs

## Setup

The **genus** $\gamma(G)$ of a graph $G$ is the minimum integer $g$ such that $G
$ embeds without edge crossings on the orientable surface $S_g$ (the sphere with
 $g$ handles). Our tool is the **Euler–Poincaré formula**: for any cellular embe
dding of a connected graph on $S_g$ with $V$ vertices, $E$ edges, and $F$ faces,

$$V - E + F = 2 - 2g. \tag{1}$$

We use two face-counting inequalities that hold for any cellular embedding of a
simple graph:

- If every face is bounded by **at least $k$ edges**, then $2E \ge kF$ (each edg
e borders at most two faces), i.e. $F \le \tfrac{2E}{k}$.

---

## (a) Genus lower bound for $K_n$

For the complete graph $K_n$ (with $n \ge 3$):

$$V = n, \qquad E = \binom{n}{2} = \frac{n(n-1)}{2}.$$

Since $K_n$ is simple (no loops, no multiple edges) and $n \ge 3$, every face in
 any embedding is bounded by a closed walk of length at least $3$. Hence

$$F \le \frac{2E}{3} = \frac{n(n-1)}{3}. \tag{2}$$

Substituting (2) into (1):

$$2 - 2g = V - E + F \le n - \frac{n(n-1)}{2} + \frac{n(n-1)}{3} = n - \frac{n(n
-1)}{6}.$$

Rearranging,

$$2g \ge 2 - n + \frac{n(n-1)}{6} = \frac{12 - 6n + n^2 - n}{6} = \frac{n^2 - 7n
 + 12}{6} = \frac{(n-3)(n-4)}{6}.$$

Therefore

$$\boxed{\gamma(K_n) \;\ge\; \frac{(n-3)(n-4)}{12}.}$$

Since the genus is a non-negative integer,

$$\gamma(K_n) \;\ge\; \left\lceil \frac{(n-3)(n-4)}{12} \right\rceil.$$

*(For $n < 3$ the bound is non-positive and the trivial bound $\gamma \ge 0$ app
lies; $K_3, K_4, K_5$ are planar or have genus $0,0,1$ respectively, consistent
with the formula.)*

---

## (b) Genus lower bound for $K_{m,n}$

For the complete bipartite graph $K_{m,n}$ (with $m, n \ge 2$):

$$V = m + n, \qquad E = mn.$$

A bipartite graph contains **no odd cycles**, so every face in any embedding is
bounded by a closed walk of length at least $4$. Hence

$$F \le \frac{2E}{4} = \frac{E}{2} = \frac{mn}{2}. \tag{3}$$

Substituting (3) into (1):

$$2 - 2g = V - E + F \le (m+n) - mn + \frac{mn}{2} = (m+n) - \frac{mn}{2}.$$

Rearranging,

$$2g \ge 2 - (m+n) + \frac{mn}{2} = \frac{4 - 2m - 2n + mn}{2} = \frac{(m-2)(n-2
)}{2}.$$

Therefore

$$\boxed{\gamma(K_{m,n}) \;\ge\; \frac{(m-2)(n-2)}{4}.}$$

Since the genus is a non-negative integer,

$$\gamma(K_{m,n}) \;\ge\; \left\lceil \frac{(m-2)(n-2)}{4} \right\rceil.$$

*(For $\min(m,n) \le 2$ the bound is non-positive and the trivial bound $\gamma
\ge 0$ applies; indeed $K_{1,n}$ and $K_{2,n}$ are planar.)*

---

## Summary

| Graph | Vertices | Edges | Min. face length | Lower bound on genus |
|-------|----------|-------|------------------|----------------------|
| $K_n$ | $n$ | $\frac{n(n-1)}{2}$ | $3$ | $\displaystyle\gamma(K_n) \ge \left\l
ceil\frac{(n-3)(n-4)}{12}\right\rceil$ |
| $K_{m,n}$ | $m+n$ | $mn$ | $4$ | $\displaystyle\gamma(K_{m,n}) \ge \left\lceil
\frac{(m-2)(n-2)}{4}\right\rceil$ |

**Remark.** These lower bounds, derived purely from the Euler formula and face-l
ength constraints, are in fact **tight**: the Ringel–Youngs Map Color Theorem (1
968) and the corresponding result of Ringel for bipartite graphs construct embed
dings attaining exactly these values, so the displayed expressions equal the gen
us. The problem, however, asks only for the lower bound, which is what the Euler
-formula argument above establishes.
