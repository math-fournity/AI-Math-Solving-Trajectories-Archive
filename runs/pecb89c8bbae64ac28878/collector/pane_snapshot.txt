$$ab - ca = pb - pa \implies a(b-c) = p(b-a) \tag{1}$$

$$bc - ca = pc - pa \implies c(b-a) = p(c-a) \tag{3}$$

$$f(f(x)) = p - \frac{2}{p - \frac{2}{x}} = \frac{(p^2-2)x - 2p}{px - 2}$$

## Step 3: Compute $abc$
