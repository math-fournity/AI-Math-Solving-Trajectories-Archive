Looking at this problem, I need to find the minimum sum of perimeters of odd-sid

## Proof

**Lower bound via column coloring.**

Color the unit squares by column parity: column $j$ is *black* if $j$ is odd, *w
hite* if $j$ is even. The 20×19 grid has 10 odd columns and 9 even columns, each
 of height 20, giving:
- Black cells: $10 \times 20 = 200$
- White cells: $9 \times 20 = 180$
- Difference (black $-$ white) $= 20$.

**Even squares contribute 0.** An even square of side $2k$ covers $2k$ consecuti
ve columns—exactly $k$ black and $k$ white—each across $2k$ rows, so it covers $
2k^2$ black and $2k^2$ white cells. Net contribution: $0$.

**Odd squares contribute $\pm s$.** An odd square of side $s = 2k+1$ covers $s$
consecutive columns. If it starts on a black (odd) column, it covers $k+1$ black
 and $k$ white columns, contributing $s(k+1) - sk = s$. If it starts on a white
(even) column, it contributes $-s$.

Let the odd squares have side lengths $s_1, s_2, \ldots, s_m$ with signs $\varep
silon_i = \pm 1$. Then:
$$\sum_{i=1}^{m} \varepsilon_i \, s_i = 20.$$

By the triangle inequality:
$$\sum_{i=1}^{m} s_i \;\geq\; \left|\sum_{i=1}^{m} \varepsilon_i \, s_i\right| =
 20.$$

The sum of perimeters of odd squares is $4\sum s_i \geq 4 \times 20 = 80$.

**Construction achieving 80.**

Split the 20×19 rectangle into:
- A **20×18** sub-rectangle (columns 1–18), tiled by $2 \times 2$ squares: $10 \
times 9 = 90$ even squares.
- A **20×1** strip (column 19), tiled by $1 \times 1$ squares: 20 odd squares, e
ach with perimeter 4.

**Verification of the coloring constraints:**
- *Column parity:* All 20 unit squares lie in column 19 (odd/black), each contri
buting $+1$. Total: $20$. ✓
- *Row parity* (rows alternate, 10 odd/10 even, difference 0): The 20 unit squar
es occupy rows 1–20; 10 start on odd rows ($+1$ each) and 10 on even rows ($-1$
each). Total: $0$. ✓
- *Area:* $90 \times 4 + 20 \times 1 = 360 + 20 = 380 = 20 \times 19$. ✓

The sum of perimeters of odd squares is $20 \times 4 = 80$.

**Conclusion.** The lower bound is 80 and it is achieved, so the smallest possib
le value is:

$$\boxed{80}$$
