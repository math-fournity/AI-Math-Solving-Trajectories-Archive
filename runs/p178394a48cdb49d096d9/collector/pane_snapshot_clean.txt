## Proof

We determine the operator norm $\|T\| = \sup_{\|f\|_1 \le 1} \|Tf\|_1$.

**Upper bound.** For $f \in L^1[0,1]$,

$$
\|Tf\|_1 = \int_0^1 \left|\int_0^t f(s)\,ds\right| dt \le \int_0^1 \int_0^t |f(s
)|\,ds\,dt.
$$

Exchanging the order of integration (Fubini, integrand nonneg),

$$
\int_0^1 \int_0^t |f(s)|\,ds\,dt = \int_0^1 |f(s)| \int_s^1 dt\,ds = \int_0^1 |f
(s)|(1-s)\,ds \le \int_0^1 |f(s)|\,ds = \|f\|_1,
$$

since $1-s \le 1$ on $[0,1]$. Hence $\|T\| \le 1$.

**Lower bound (attaining the supremum).** For $n \ge 1$, set $f_n = n\,\mathbf{1
}_{[0,\,1/n]}$. Then $\|f_n\|_1 = n \cdot \tfrac{1}{n} = 1$. A direct computatio
n gives

$$
Tf_n(t) = \int_0^t n\,\mathbf{1}_{[0,\,1/n]}(s)\,ds = \min(nt,\,1) =
\begin{cases}
nt, & 0 \le t \le 1/n,\\
1, & 1/n \le t \le 1.
\end{cases}
$$

Therefore

$$
\|Tf_n\|_1 = \int_0^{1/n} nt\,dt + \int_{1/n}^1 1\,dt = \frac{n}{2}\cdot\frac{1}
{n^2} + \left(1 - \frac{1}{n}\right) = \frac{1}{2n} + 1 - \frac{1}{n} = 1 - \fra
c{1}{2n} \xrightarrow{n\to\infty} 1.
$$

So $\|T\| \ge \sup_n \|Tf_n\|_1 = 1$.

**Conclusion.** Combining both bounds, $\|T\| = 1$. The norm is not attained, si
nce equality in the upper bound would require $|f(s)|(1-s) = |f(s)|$ a.e., i.e.
$\operatorname{supp} f \subseteq \{0\}$, which forces $f = 0$ in $L^1$.

### PROOF COMPLETE
