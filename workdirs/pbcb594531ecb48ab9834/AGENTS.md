# Problem

In a specialized logistics hub, there are six unique types of radioactive isotopes stored in Lead Container $A$. Each isotope is identified by its atomic mass: $2, 3, 5, 8, 12,$ or $18$. A technician named Anna selects one specific isotope $a$ from this container to undergo a stabilization process.

A scientist named Elie provides a stabilizing catalyst $b$, which is a compound engineered with a precise molecular composition of $b = 2^{100} \cdot 3^{1000} \cdot 5^{10000}$. When the isotope $a$ is combined with the catalyst $b$, they form a complex molecule with a total mass of $ab$.

Elie wants to analyze the potential outcomes of this procedure. He calculates the value of $d(ab)$ for every possible isotope $a$ that Anna could have chosen from the set $\{2, 3, 5, 8, 12, 18\}$. Let $S$ be the set of all unique stability values $d(ab)$ generated by the six possible isotopes in $A$. 

Find the sum of the elements in $S$.
