## Solution

A **regular quadrilateral prism** has a square base of side length $a$ and heigh
t $h$. It has 12 edges: 8 horizontal (4 top + 4 bottom) and 4 vertical.

**Setup.** The volume and total edge length are:
$$V = a^2 h = 12\sqrt{3}, \qquad L = 8a + 4h.$$

We minimize $L$ subject to the volume constraint.

**Optimization.** From the constraint $h = \dfrac{12\sqrt{3}}{a^2}$, so
$$L(a) = 8a + \frac{48\sqrt{3}}{a^2}.$$

Setting $L'(a) = 0$:
$$8 - \frac{96\sqrt{3}}{a^3} = 0 \implies a^3 = 12\sqrt{3}.$$

Since $12\sqrt{3} = 4 \cdot 3^{3/2}$, we get
$$a = \sqrt[3]{4}\cdot\sqrt{3}.$$

Then
$$h = \frac{12\sqrt{3}}{a^2} = \frac{12\sqrt{3}}{(\sqrt[3]{4})^2 \cdot 3} = \fra
c{4\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt[3]{16}} = \frac{4\sqrt{3}}{2^{4/3}} = 2^{2/3}\cdot 3^{1/2} =
\sqrt[3]{4}\cdot\sqrt{3} = a.$$

So **$h = a$**: the optimal prism is a **cube**. The second derivative $L''(a) =
 \dfrac{288\sqrt{3}}{a^4} > 0$ confirms this is a minimum.

**Verification of volume:** $a^2 h = a^3 = (\sqrt[3]{4}\cdot\sqrt{3})^3 = 4 \cdo
t 3\sqrt{3} = 12\sqrt{3}$. ✓

**Surface area.** The surface area of a square prism is $S = 2a^2 + 4ah$. With $
h = a$:
$$S = 2a^2 + 4a^2 = 6a^2.$$

Computing $a^2$:
$$a^2 = (\sqrt[3]{4})^2 \cdot 3 = \sqrt[3]{16}\cdot 3 = 2\sqrt[3]{2}\cdot 3 = 6\
sqrt[3]{2}.$$

Therefore:
$$\boxed{S = 6a^2 = 6 \cdot 6\sqrt[3]{2} = 36\sqrt[3]{2}.}$$
