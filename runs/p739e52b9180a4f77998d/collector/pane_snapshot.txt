The solid is

$$S\cap\{z=z_0\}=T(z_0).$$

By Cavalieri's principle (Fubini),
$$\operatorname{Vol}(S)=\int_0^1 \operatorname{Area}\bigl(T(t)\bigr)\,dt.$$

$$\operatorname{Area}=\tfrac12\bigl|x_A(y_B-y_C)+x_B(y_C-y_A)+x_C(y_A-y_B)\bigr|
