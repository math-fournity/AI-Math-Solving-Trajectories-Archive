The determinant can be written as a sum over permutations $\sigma$ of $\{\alpha,

$$D(x,y,z) = \sum_{\sigma \in S_3} \operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)\, \sin(\sigma_1 x)

**Orthogonality of sine functions on $[-\pi,\pi]$.** For integers $m, n$:

$$\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \sin(mt)\sin(nt)\,dt = \begin{cases} \pi & \text{if } m = n
\neq 0,\\ 0 & \text{otherwise (}m\neq n\text{, or either is }0\text{).} \end{cas

-\pi,\pi]$. When $m = n \neq 0$, we get $\tfrac{1}{2}[\int 1\,dt - \int \cos(2mt

$$\iiint D^2\,dx\,dy\,dz = \sum_{\sigma \in S_3} \pi^3 = 3!\cdot\pi^3 = 6\pi^3.$
