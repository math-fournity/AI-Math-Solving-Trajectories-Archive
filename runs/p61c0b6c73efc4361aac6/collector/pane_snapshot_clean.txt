## Proof

We count the days elapsed from August 6, 1961 to February 20, 1962.

**Days remaining in August 1961** (from Aug 6 to Aug 31, inclusive of Aug 6):
31 − 6 = **25 days**

**Full months between:**
- September: **30 days**
- October: **31 days**
- November: **30 days**
- December: **31 days**
- January 1962: **31 days**

**Days in February 1962** (from Feb 1 to Feb 20):
**20 days**

**Total:**
25 + 30 + 31 + 30 + 31 + 31 + 20 = **198 days**

Verification of the sum:
25 + 30 = 55
55 + 31 = 86
86 + 30 = 116
116 + 31 = 147
147 + 31 = 178
178 + 20 = 198

Therefore, exactly **198 days** elapsed between the initial activation on August
 6, 1961 and the commissioning of the backup valve on February 20, 1962.
