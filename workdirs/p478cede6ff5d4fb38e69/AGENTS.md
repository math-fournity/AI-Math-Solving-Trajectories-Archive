# Problem

Let $n = 5$. Initially, the number 1 is written 5 times on a blackboard. There are two empty buckets. A move consists of erasing two numbers $a$ and $b$ from the board, replacing them with the numbers 1 and $a+b$, then adding one stone to the first bucket and $\operatorname{gcd}(a, b)$ stones to the second bucket. After some finite number of moves, there are $s$ stones in the first bucket and $t$ stones in the second bucket, where $s$ and $t$ are positive integers. Let $R$ be the set of all possible values of the ratio $\frac{t}{s}$. It is known that $R$ is the set of all rational numbers in an interval $[L, U)$. Find the value of $100L + 10U$.
