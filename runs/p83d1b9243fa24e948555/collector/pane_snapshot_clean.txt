**Proof.**

Let $\alpha = \angle BAC$. Since $AB = AC = 4024$ and $BC = 2012$, by the Law of
 Cosines:

$$BC^2 = AB^2 + AC^2 - 2\cdot AB\cdot AC\cdot \cos\alpha$$
$$2012^2 = 2\cdot 4024^2(1-\cos\alpha) = 2\cdot 16\cdot 2012^2(1-\cos\alpha)$$

$$1 = 32(1-\cos\alpha) \implies \cos\alpha = \frac{7}{8},\quad \sin\alpha = \fra
c{\sqrt{15}}{8}.$$

**Locating D.** The reflection of line $AC$ over line $AB$ passes through $A$ an
d makes angle $\alpha$ with $AB$ on the opposite side of $C$. Since $D$ lies on
this reflected line, $\angle BAD = \alpha$.

**Step 1: Show $BD = BC$.** Both $\angle BAD = \alpha$ and $\angle BAC = \alpha$
 are inscribed angles in the circumcircle. Each subtends an arc of measure $2\al
pha$ (the arc not containing $A$): $\angle BAD$ subtends arc $BD$ and $\angle BA
C$ subtends arc $BC$. Since $D$ and $C$ lie on opposite sides of $B$ on the circ
le (the reflection places $D$ on the opposite side of $AB$ from $C$), these are
distinct arcs of equal measure $2\alpha$. Equal arcs yield equal chords, so:

$$BD = BC = 2012.$$

**Step 2: Determine $\angle CAD$.** Since $AD$ is the reflection of $AC$ over $A
B$, with $C$ and $D$ on opposite sides of $AB$:

$$\angle CAD = \angle CAB + \angle BAD = \alpha + \alpha = 2\alpha.$$

**Step 3: Use the cyclic quadrilateral.** The points $C, A, D, B$ lie on the cir
cumcircle in this cyclic order, so $CADB$ is a cyclic quadrilateral. Opposite an
gles sum to $180^\circ$:

$$\angle CAD + \angle DBC = 180^\circ \implies \angle DBC = 180^\circ - 2\alpha.
$$

**Step 4: Solve triangle $BCD$.** Since $BD = BC = 2012$, triangle $BCD$ is isos
celes with vertex angle $\angle DBC = 180^\circ - 2\alpha$. The base angles are:

$$\angle BCD = \angle BDC = \frac{180^\circ - (180^\circ - 2\alpha)}{2} = \alpha
.$$

By the Law of Sines in $\triangle BCD$:

$$\frac{CD}{\sin(\angle DBC)} = \frac{BC}{\sin(\angle BDC)}$$
$$\frac{CD}{\sin(180^\circ - 2\alpha)} = \frac{2012}{\sin\alpha}$$
$$CD = \frac{2012\cdot\sin 2\alpha}{\sin\alpha} = 2012\cdot\frac{2\sin\alpha\cos
\alpha}{\sin\alpha} = 2012\cdot 2\cos\alpha = 2012\cdot 2\cdot\frac{7}{8} = 2012
\cdot\frac{7}{4} = 503\cdot 7.$$

$$\boxed{CD = 3521}.$$
