# Problem

A specialized climate control system consists of a linear array of 7 thermal regulators. Each regulator can be set to one of three possible discrete states: heating (+1), neutral (0), or cooling (-1). 

The efficiency of the system depends on the "interaction energy" between adjacent regulators. The interaction energy between any two neighbors is defined as the product of their individual states. The total output of the system is the sum of the interaction energies across all 6 adjacent pairs: (Regulator 1 × Regulator 2) + (Regulator 2 × Regulator 3) + (Regulator 3 × Regulator 4) + (Regulator 4 × Regulator 5) + (Regulator 5 × Regulator 6) + (Regulator 6 × Regulator 7).

In how many different ways can the states of the 7 regulators be configured so that the total output of the system is exactly 4?
