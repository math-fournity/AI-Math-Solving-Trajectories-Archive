Looking at this problem, I need to find the maximum number of points in the plan

90° (non-obtuse), and no triple is collinear (since collinear points give a 180°
 angle > 90°).

**Answer: 4**

## Proof

**Construction (4 is achievable):** Place towers at the four vertices of a recta
ngle. Any three vertices of a rectangle form a right triangle (the right angle i
s at the vertex where two adjacent sides meet). All angles are ≤ 90°, no three a
re collinear, and not all four are collinear. ✓

**Upper bound (5 is impossible):**

Let $S$ be a set of points satisfying the conditions, and let $H$ be the convex
hull of $S$, with vertices $V_1, V_2, \ldots, V_n$ in cyclic order ($n \geq 3$ s
ince not all collinear).

**Step 1: The hull has at most 4 vertices.**

For any three consecutive hull vertices $V_{i-1}, V_i, V_{i+1}$, the triangle $V
_{i-1}V_iV_{i+1}$ must be non-obtuse. Since the polygon is convex, the angle at
$V_i$ in this triangle equals the interior angle of the polygon at $V_i$. Thus e
very interior angle of the hull is $\leq 90°$.

The sum of interior angles of a convex $n$-gon is $(n-2)\cdot 180°$. Since each
angle $\leq 90°$:

$$(n-2)\cdot 180° \leq n \cdot 90° \implies 180n - 360 \leq 90n \implies 90n \le
q 360 \implies n \leq 4.$$

**Step 2: No point lies on an edge of the hull.**

If a point $P$ lies on edge $V_iV_{i+1}$ (strictly between the vertices), then $
V_i, P, V_{i+1}$ are collinear, giving an angle of $180°$ at $P$, violating the
non-obtuse condition.

**Step 3: No point lies in the interior of the hull.**

Suppose $P$ is strictly inside the hull. Since $n \leq 4$, the hull is a triangl
e or quadrilateral. Triangulating the hull, $P$ lies inside some triangle $ABC$
formed by three hull vertices.

The three angles $\angle APB$, $\angle BPC$, $\angle CPA$ around $P$ sum to $360
°$. If all three were $\leq 90°$, their sum would be $\leq 270° < 360°$, a contr
adiction. In fact, at least one angle is $\geq 120° > 90°$.

That angle appears in one of the triples $\{A,B,P\}$, $\{B,C,P\}$, or $\{C,A,P\}
$, violating the non-obtuse condition. So no interior point exists.

**Step 4: Conclusion.**

By Steps 2 and 3, every point in $S$ is a vertex of the hull. By Step 1, the hul
l has at most 4 vertices. Therefore $|S| \leq 4$.

Combined with the rectangle construction achieving 4, the maximum number of sign
al towers is $\boxed{4}$.
