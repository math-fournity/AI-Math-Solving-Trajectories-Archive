## Solution

**The quotient ring.** Since $5 \in I$, the quotient $R = \mathbb{Z}[X]/I$ is a
quotient of $\mathbb{Z}[X]/\langle 5\rangle \cong \mathbb{F}_5[X]$. So
$$R \cong \mathbb{F}_5[X]/\langle (X^2+2)(X+1)\rangle.$$

**Factoring over $\mathbb{F}_5$.** The factor $X+1$ is linear (hence irreducible
). For $X^2+2$, we check for roots in $\mathbb{F}_5$: $0^2\!+\!2=2,\;1^2\!+\!2=3
,\;2^2\!+\!2=1,\;3^2\!+\!2=1,\;4^2\!+\!2=3$. No roots, so $X^2+2$ is irreducible
 of degree 2.

The two factors $X^2+2$ and $X+1$ are coprime, so by the Chinese Remainder Theor
em:
$$R \cong \mathbb{F}_5[X]/\langle X^2+2\rangle \;\times\; \mathbb{F}_5[X]/\langl
e X+1\rangle \cong \mathbb{F}_{25} \times \mathbb{F}_5.$$

**Counting solutions of $f^{18}=1$.** Any $f$ with $f^{18}=1$ is a unit (with in
verse $f^{17}$), so we count within the unit groups. The count factors over the
CRT product:

- $\mathbb{F}_{25}^*$ is cyclic of order $24$. The number of elements with $x^{1
8}=1$ is $\gcd(18,24)=6$.
- $\mathbb{F}_5^*$ is cyclic of order $4$. The number of elements with $x^{18}=1
$ is $\gcd(18,4)=2$.

Multiplying:
$$6 \times 2 = 12.$$

**Answer:** $\boxed{12}$
