## Solution

We evaluate $\displaystyle \oint_{|z|=1} \frac{f(z)}{z^4}\,dz$ where $\displayst
yle f(z)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{k^3}{3^k}z^k$.

**Step 1: Radius of convergence.** The coefficients are $a_k = k^3/3^k$. Since
$$
\lim_{k\to\infty}\left|\frac{a_{k+1}}{a_k}\right|=\lim_{k\to\infty}\frac{(k+1)^3
}{3^{k+1}}\cdot\frac{3^k}{k^3}=\frac{1}{3}\lim_{k\to\infty}\left(1+\frac{1}{k}\r
ight)^3=\frac{1}{3}<1,
$$
the radius of convergence is $R=3>1$. Hence $f$ is holomorphic inside and on $|z
|=1$.

**Step 2: Apply Cauchy's integral formula.** Since $f$ is holomorphic on a neigh
borhood of the closed unit disk, Cauchy's differentiation formula gives
$$
\oint_{|z|=1}\frac{f(z)}{z^4}\,dz=\frac{2\pi i}{3!}\,f'''(0)=\frac{\pi i}{3}\,f'
''(0).
$$

**Step 3: Compute $f'''(0)$.** From the power series, $f(z)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}a
_k z^k$ with $a_k=k^3/3^k$. The coefficient of $z^3$ is
$$
a_3=\frac{3^3}{3^3}=\frac{27}{27}=1.
$$
Since $a_3 = f'''(0)/3!$, we get
$$
f'''(0)=3!\cdot a_3 = 6\cdot 1 = 6.
$$

**Step 4: Conclude.**
$$
\oint_{|z|=1}\frac{f(z)}{z^4}\,dz=\frac{\pi i}{3}\cdot 6 = 2\pi i.
$$
