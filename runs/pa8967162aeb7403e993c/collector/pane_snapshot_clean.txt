## Solution

**Setup.** $H$ consists of absolutely continuous $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb{C}$ with $f(
0)=0$ and $f'\in L^2(0,1)$, equipped with $\langle f,g\rangle = \int_0^1 f'(t)\o
verline{g'(t)}\,dt$. For fixed $0<t\le 1$, $L(h)=h(t)$.

**Key identity.** Since $h\in H$ is absolutely continuous with $h(0)=0$,
$$h(t) = \int_0^t h'(s)\,ds = \int_0^1 h'(s)\,\mathbf{1}_{[0,t]}(s)\,ds.$$

**Riesz representer.** We seek $h_0\in H$ with $L(h)=\langle h,h_0\rangle$ for a
ll $h\in H$, i.e.
$$\int_0^1 h'(s)\overline{h_0'(s)}\,ds = \int_0^1 h'(s)\,\mathbf{1}_{[0,t]}(s)\,
ds \quad\forall h\in H.$$

Since $\{h':h\in H\}=L^2(0,1)$ (every $L^2$ function is the derivative of its in
tegral starting at $0$), this forces
$$\overline{h_0'(s)} = \mathbf{1}_{[0,t]}(s),\quad\text{i.e.}\quad h_0'(s)=\math
bf{1}_{[0,t]}(s)$$
(a real-valued function, so conjugation is harmless). Integrating with $h_0(0)=0
$:
$$h_0(s) = \begin{cases} s, & 0\le s\le t,\\ t, & t\le s\le 1. \end{cases}$$

This $h_0$ is absolutely continuous, $h_0(0)=0$, and $h_0'=\mathbf{1}_{[0,t]}\in
 L^2(0,1)$, so $h_0\in H$. Verification:
$$\langle h,h_0\rangle = \int_0^1 h'(s)\overline{h_0'(s)}\,ds = \int_0^t h'(s)\,
ds = h(t) = L(h).\quad\checkmark$$

**Norm.** By Riesz, $\|L\|=\|h_0\|$:
$$\|L\|^2 = \|h_0\|^2 = \langle h_0,h_0\rangle = \int_0^1 |h_0'(s)|^2\,ds = \int
_0^t 1\,ds = t.$$

Hence $\|L\| = \sqrt{t}$.

**Sharpness (direct check).** For any $h\in H$,
$$|L(h)| = \left|\int_0^t h'(s)\,ds\right| \le \left(\int_0^t 1\,ds\right)^{1/2}
\!\left(\int_0^t |h'(s)|^2\,ds\right)^{1/2} \le \sqrt{t}\,\|h\|,$$
so $\|L\|\le\sqrt{t}$. Equality is attained at $h=h_0$: $L(h_0)=t$ and $\|h_0\|=
\sqrt{t}$, giving $|L(h_0)|/\|h_0\|=\sqrt{t}$. Thus $\|L\|=\sqrt{t}$.

## Result

$$\boxed{\|L\| = \sqrt{t}},\qquad \boxed{h_0(s) = \min(s,\,t) = \begin{cases} s,
 & 0\le s\le t,\\ t, & t\le s\le 1.\end{cases}}$$
