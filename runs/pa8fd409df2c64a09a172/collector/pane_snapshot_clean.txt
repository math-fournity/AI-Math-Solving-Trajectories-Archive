## Step 1: Simplify the arctangent via the subtraction formula

$$\arctan(a) - \arctan(b) = \arctan\!\left(\frac{a-b}{1+ab}\right)$$

$a = x^2$ and $b = x^{1/2}$:

- $a - b = x^2 - x^{1/2}$ ✓
- $ab = x^2 \cdot x^{1/2} = x^{5/2}$ ✓

Since $x > 0$, we have $ab = x^{5/2} > 0 > -1$, so the identity is valid for all
