## Proof

Let the truncated-cone glass have bottom-base radius $r$ (smaller), top-base rad
ius $R$ (larger), and total height $H$. Denote the diameters $D_1 = 2r$ (bottom)
 and $D_2 = 2R$ (top).

The volume of a frustum of height $h$ with base radii $a,b$ is
$$V=\frac{\pi h}{3}\bigl(a^{2}+ab+b^{2}\bigr).$$

**Total volume (1 liter):**
$$V_{\text{tot}}=\frac{\pi H}{3}\bigl(r^{2}+rR+R^{2}\bigr).$$

**Filled volume at height $\tfrac{2H}{3}$ (half a liter):**

The radius at height $y$ from the bottom grows linearly:
$$\rho(y)=r+\frac{R-r}{H}\,y.$$
At $y=\tfrac{2H}{3}$ the liquid surface radius is
$$r'=\rho\!\left(\tfrac{2H}{3}\right)=r+\tfrac{2}{3}(R-r)=\frac{r+2R}{3}.$$

Hence
$$V_{\text{fill}}=\frac{\pi}{3}\cdot\frac{2H}{3}\bigl(r^{2}+rr'+r'^{2}\bigr)=\fr
ac{2\pi H}{9}\bigl(r^{2}+rr'+r'^{2}\bigr).$$

**Using the condition $V_{\text{fill}}=\tfrac12 V_{\text{tot}}$:**

$$\frac{V_{\text{fill}}}{V_{\text{tot}}}
=\frac{\tfrac{2H}{9}\bigl(r^{2}+rr'+r'^{2}\bigr)}{\tfrac{H}{3}\bigl(r^{2}+rR+R^{
2}\bigr)}
=\frac{2}{3}\cdot\frac{r^{2}+rr'+r'^{2}}{r^{2}+rR+R^{2}}
=\frac12.$$

So
$$4\bigl(r^{2}+rr'+r'^{2}\bigr)=3\bigl(r^{2}+rR+R^{2}\bigr).\tag{*}$$

**Substitute $r'=\dfrac{r+2R}{3}$ and set $t=\dfrac{r}{R}$:**

$$r^{2}+rr'+r'^{2}
=\frac{13r^{2}+10rR+4R^{2}}{9}.$$

Then $(*)$ becomes
$$\frac{4(13r^{2}+10rR+4R^{2})}{9}=3\bigl(r^{2}+rR+R^{2}\bigr),$$
$$52r^{2}+40rR+16R^{2}=27r^{2}+27rR+27R^{2},$$
$$25r^{2}+13rR-11R^{2}=0.$$

Dividing by $R^{2}$:
$$25t^{2}+13t-11=0,\qquad t=\frac{r}{R}.$$

**Solve:**
$$t=\frac{-13+\sqrt{169+1100}}{50}=\frac{-13+\sqrt{1269}}{50}=\frac{3\sqrt{141}-
13}{50}$$
(taking the positive root, since radii are positive).

**Verification.** With $t\approx 0.4525$, $R=1$, $r=0.4525$:
- $r'=(0.4525+2)/3\approx 0.8175$,
- $r^{2}+rr'+r'^{2}\approx 0.2048+0.3699+0.6683=1.2430$,
- $r^{2}+rR+R^{2}\approx 1.6573$,
- $\dfrac{V_{\text{fill}}}{V_{\text{tot}}}=\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{1.2430}{1.657
3}\approx 0.5000.$ ✓

**Result.** Since diameters are proportional to radii, the ratio of the smaller
(bottom) base diameter to the larger (top) base diameter is

$$\boxed{\dfrac{D_{1}}{D_{2}}=\dfrac{3\sqrt{141}-13}{50}\approx 0.4525}.$$

Equivalently, the larger diameter to the smaller:

$$\dfrac{D_{2}}{D_{1}}=\dfrac{3\sqrt{141}+13}{22}\approx 2.210.$$

So the top base diameter is about $2.21$ times the bottom base diameter (ratio $
D_{1}:D_{2}=(3\sqrt{141}-13):50$).
