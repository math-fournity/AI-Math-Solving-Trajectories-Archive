### Definitions

Recall the two standard semicontinuity notions for a set-valued map $\varphi: X
\to \wp(Y)$:

- **Upper semicontinuous (usc):** For every open set $V \subseteq Y$, the **uppe
r preimage**
$$\varphi^{+}(V) \;=\; \{\, x \in X : \varphi(x) \subseteq V \,\}$$
is open in $X$.

- **Lower semicontinuous (lsc):** For every open set $V \subseteq Y$, the **lowe

$$\varphi^{-}(V) \;=\; \{\, x \in X : \varphi(x) \cap V \neq \emptyset \,\}$$
is open in $X$.

### The topology on $\wp(Y)$

Define a topology $\tau_V$ on $\wp(Y)$ generated by the following sub-basis $\ma
thcal{S}$:

$$\mathcal{S} \;=\; \big\{\, [V] : V \subseteq Y \text{ open}\,\big\} \;\cup\; \
big\{\, \langle V \rangle : V \subseteq Y \text{ open}\,\big\},$$

where:
- $[V] \;=\; \{\, A \in \wp(Y) : A \subseteq V \,\}$ — the collection of all sub
sets of $Y$ **contained in** $V$ (the *miss* part: $A$ misses $Y \setminus V$),
- $\langle V \rangle \;=\; \{\, A \in \wp(Y) : A \cap V \neq \emptyset \,\}$ — t
he collection of all subsets of $Y$ that **hit** $V$.

This is the **Vietoris topology** on $\wp(Y)$.

### The equivalence

We now prove:

> $\varphi: X \to \wp(Y)$ is continuous (with $\wp(Y)$ carrying $\tau_V$) $\iff$
 $\varphi$ is both upper and lower semicontinuous.

**($\Rightarrow$) Continuity implies both usc and lsc.**

Suppose $\varphi$ is continuous. Then the preimage of every sub-basis element is

- For any open $V \subseteq Y$, the set $[V] \in \mathcal{S}$ is open in $\wp(Y)
$, so
$$\varphi^{-1}([V]) = \{\, x \in X : \varphi(x) \in [V] \,\} = \{\, x \in X : \v
arphi(x) \subseteq V \,\} = \varphi^{+}(V)$$
is open in $X$. Hence $\varphi$ is upper semicontinuous.

- For any open $V \subseteq Y$, the set $\langle V \rangle \in \mathcal{S}$ is o
pen in $\wp(Y)$, so
$$\varphi^{-1}(\langle V \rangle) = \{\, x \in X : \varphi(x) \in \langle V \ran
gle \,\} = \{\, x \in X : \varphi(x) \cap V \neq \emptyset \,\} = \varphi^{-}(V)
$$
is open in $X$. Hence $\varphi$ is lower semicontinuous.

**($\Leftarrow$) Both usc and lsc imply continuity.**

Suppose $\varphi$ is both upper and lower semicontinuous. To show $\varphi$ is c
ontinuous, it suffices to show that the preimage of every sub-basis element is o
pen (a map is continuous iff the preimage of every sub-basis element is open).

- For any $[V] \in \mathcal{S}$: $\varphi^{-1}([V]) = \varphi^{+}(V)$, which is

### Summary / Characterization

The desired topology on $\wp(Y)$ is the **Vietoris topology**, whose sub-basis i
s:

$$\boxed{\mathcal{S} \;=\; \{\,[V] : V \subseteq Y \text{ open}\,\} \;\cup\; \{\
,\langle V \rangle : V \subseteq Y \text{ open}\,\},}$$

where $[V] = \{A \subseteq Y : A \subseteq V\}$ (the "contained in" or "miss" se
ts) and $\langle V \rangle = \{A \subseteq Y : A \cap V \neq \emptyset\}$ (the "
hit" sets).

The two halves of the sub-basis correspond exactly to the two semicontinuity con
ditions:
- The **upper Vietoris topology** (generated by $\{[V]\}$) governs upper semicon
tinuity via $\varphi^{+}(V) = \varphi^{-1}([V])$.
- The **lower Vietoris topology** (generated by $\{\langle V \rangle\}$) governs
 lower semicontinuity via $\varphi^{-}(V) = \varphi^{-1}(\langle V \rangle)$.

The full Vietoris topology is the join (coarsest common refinement) of these two
, and continuity with respect to it is precisely the conjunction of upper and lo
wer semicontinuity.
