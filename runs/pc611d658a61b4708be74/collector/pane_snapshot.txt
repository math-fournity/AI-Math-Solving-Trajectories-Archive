## Proof

Let $H = \ell^2(\mathbb{N})$ with inner product $\langle x, y\rangle = \sum_{n=1
}^{\infty} x_n y_n$ and norm $\|x\| = \bigl(\sum_{n=1}^\infty x_n^2\bigr)^{1/2}$
. Let $K = \bar B_1(0) = \{x \in H : \|x\| \le 1\}$, which is nonempty, closed,
and convex. By the Hilbert projection theorem, for every $x \in H$ there is a un
ique $P_K(x) \in K$ satisfying $\|x - P_K(x)\| = \inf_{y \in K} \|x - y\|$. We c
laim

$$
P_K(x) = \begin{cases} x, & \|x\| \le 1, \\[4pt] \dfrac{x}{\|x\|}, & \|x\| > 1.
\end{cases}
$$

**Case 1: $\|x\| \le 1$.** Then $x \in K$, so $x$ itself is the closest point in
 $K$ to $x$ (distance $0$, the global minimum). Hence $P_K(x) = x$.

**Case 2: $\|x\| > 1$.** Set $p := x/\|x\|$. Then $\|p\| = 1$, so $p \in K$. We
show $p$ is the unique minimizer of $\|x - y\|$ over $y \in K$.

For any $y \in K$ (so $\|y\| \le 1$), expand:

$$
\|x - y\|^2 = \|x\|^2 - 2\langle x, y\rangle + \|y\|^2.
$$

By Cauchy–Schwarz, $\langle x, y\rangle \le \|x\|\,\|y\| \le \|x\| \cdot 1 = \|x
\|$. Therefore

$$
\|x - y\|^2 \ge \|x\|^2 - 2\|x\|\,\|y\| + \|y\|^2 = (\|x\| - \|y\|)^2 \ge (\|x\|
 - 1)^2,
$$

since $\|y\| \le 1$ and $\|x\| > 1$ implies $\|x\| - \|y\| \ge \|x\| - 1 > 0$. E
quality in the last step requires $\|y\| = 1$; equality in Cauchy–Schwarz requir
es $y = \lambda x$ for some $\lambda \ge 0$; together with $\|y\| = 1$ and $\|x\
| > 1$ this forces $\lambda = 1/\|x\|$, i.e. $y = x/\|x\| = p$. Hence

$$
\|x - y\|^2 \ge (\|x\| - 1)^2 = \|x - p\|^2,
$$

with equality **only** at $y = p$. Thus $p = x/\|x\|$ is the unique closest poin
t in $K$ to $x$, i.e. $P_K(x) = x/\|x\|$.

**Verification of the projection (variational inequality).** A point $p \in K$ i
s the projection of $x$ iff $\langle x - p,\, y - p\rangle \le 0$ for all $y \in
 K$.

- If $\|x\| \le 1$: $p = x$, $x - p = 0$, inequality holds trivially.
- If $\|x\| > 1$: $p = x/\|x\|$, $x - p = x(1 - 1/\|x\|)$. For $y \in K$,
$$
\langle x - p,\, y - p\rangle = \Bigl(1 - \tfrac{1}{\|x\|}\Bigr)\Bigl(\langle x,
 y\rangle - \tfrac{\langle x, x\rangle}{\|x\|}\Bigr) = \Bigl(1 - \tfrac{1}{\|x\|
}\Bigr)\bigl(\langle x, y\rangle - \|x\|\bigr).
$$
Since $\|x\| > 1$, the factor $\bigl(1 - 1/\|x\|\bigr) > 0$. By Cauchy–Schwarz,
$\langle x, y\rangle \le \|x\|\,\|y\| \le \|x\|$, so $\langle x, y\rangle - \|x\
| \le 0$. Hence $\langle x - p, y - p\rangle \le 0$ for all $y \in K$, confirmin
g $p = P_K(x)$.

**Conclusion.** The projection (nearest-point) operator onto the closed unit bal
l of $\ell^2(\mathbb{N})$ is the radial rescaling

$$
\boxed{\,P_K(x) = \begin{cases} x, & \|x\|_2 \le 1, \\[4pt] \dfrac{x}{\|x\|_2},
& \|x\|_2 > 1.\end{cases}\,}
$$

Equivalently, $P_K(x) = \min\{1,\, 1/\|x\|_2\}\, x$ (with the convention $0/0 =
0$ handled by the $\|x\|\le 1$ branch, where $x=0$ gives $P_K(0)=0$). The operat
or is the identity on $K$ and normalizes every vector outside $K$ onto the unit
sphere along the ray through the origin.
