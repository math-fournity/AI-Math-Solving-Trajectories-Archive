# Problem

A high-tech microchip is designed as a perfect square plate, oriented with its edges aligned to a standard grid. Engineers have etched a series of straight laser-cut grooves across the surface, each parallel to one of the plate's edges. The layout of these grooves follows two strict precision rules:
1. No two grooves lie on the same straight line.
2. If two grooves meet, they do so only such that the endpoint of one groove touches the body or endpoint of the other.

These grooves partition the square plate into a collection of smaller rectangular functional zones. Quality control scans reveal the following density patterns:
- Any vertical scanning line that passes through the plate (but does not coincide with any etched groove) crosses through exactly $k$ rectangular zones.
- Any horizontal scanning line that passes through the plate (but does not coincide with any etched groove) crosses through exactly $l$ rectangular zones.

Based on these scanning parameters, determine all possible values for the total number of rectangular zones etched into the square plate.
