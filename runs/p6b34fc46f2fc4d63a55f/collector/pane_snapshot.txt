## Proof

We wish to evaluate
$$P = \prod_{p \in \mathbb{P}} \frac{p^4+1}{p^4-1}.$$

**Step 1: Rewrite in terms of $p^{-4}$.**

Dividing numerator and denominator by $p^4$,
$$\frac{p^4+1}{p^4-1} = \frac{1+p^{-4}}{1-p^{-4}},$$
so
$$P = \prod_{p\in\mathbb{P}} \frac{1+p^{-4}}{1-p^{-4}} = \frac{\prod_p (1+p^{-4}
)}{\prod_p (1-p^{-4})}.$$

**Step 2: Express each product via the Riemann zeta function.**

The Euler product for the zeta function gives, for $\Re(s)>1$,
$$\zeta(s) = \prod_p (1-p^{-s})^{-1}, \qquad\text{hence}\qquad \prod_p (1-p^{-s}
) = \frac{1}{\zeta(s)}.$$

Taking $s=4$:
$$\prod_p (1-p^{-4}) = \frac{1}{\zeta(4)}.$$

For the numerator, use the identity $1+x = \frac{1-x^2}{1-x}$ with $x = p^{-4}$:
$$1 + p^{-4} = \frac{1-p^{-8}}{1-p^{-4}}.$$
Therefore
$$\prod_p (1+p^{-4}) = \prod_p \frac{1-p^{-8}}{1-p^{-4}} = \frac{\prod_p(1-p^{-8
})}{\prod_p(1-p^{-4})} = \frac{1/\zeta(8)}{1/\zeta(4)} = \frac{\zeta(4)}{\zeta(8
)}.$$

**Step 3: Form the ratio.**

$$P = \frac{\zeta(4)/\zeta(8)}{1/\zeta(4)} = \frac{\zeta(4)^2}{\zeta(8)}.$$

**Step 4: Insert the known values.**

The standard evaluations of the zeta function at even positive integers are
$$\zeta(4) = \frac{\pi^4}{90}, \qquad \zeta(8) = \frac{\pi^8}{9450}.$$

Hence
$$\zeta(4)^2 = \frac{\pi^8}{8100},$$
and
$$P = \frac{\pi^8/8100}{\pi^8/9450} = \frac{9450}{8100}.$$

**Step 5: Simplify.**

$$\frac{9450}{8100} = \frac{945}{810} = \frac{189}{162} = \frac{7}{6},$$
where the last reduction uses $189 = 7\cdot 27$ and $162 = 6\cdot 27$.

**Conclusion.**

$$\boxed{\prod_{p\in\mathbb{P}} \frac{p^4+1}{p^4-1} = \frac{7}{6}}.$$
