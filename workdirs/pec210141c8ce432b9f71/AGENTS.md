# Problem

In a bustling logistical warehouse, a manager is designing a storage system on a grid-based floor. The storage area is shaped like a staircase with a height and base of 6 units: the first column has 1 cell, the second has 2, the third has 3, the fourth has 4, the fifth has 5, and the sixth has 6, for a total of 21 cells.

The manager needs to divide this entire 21-cell staircase into exactly 6 distinct storage zones. According to the zoning safety regulations:
1. Exactly five of these zones must be rectangular in shape.
2. Exactly one of these zones must be square in shape.
3. Each zone must have integer dimensions, and their boundaries must align with the grid lines.
4. The rectangles can be oriented in any direction (horizontal or vertical), provided they are formed by the grid cells of the staircase.

How many different ways can the manager partition this 6-cell high staircase into these five rectangles and one square?
