# Problem

A cosmic energy generator operates according to a specific transformation law. The output of the generator, denoted as $f$, depends on the input level of crystalline fuel.

The chief engineer observes a unique property of this generator: if you cube the input fuel amount $x$ before processing it, the resulting energy output $f(x^3)$ is exactly $10$ units less than the cube of the output produced by the original amount $x$. This relationship is expressed by the formula: $f(x^3) = (f(x))^3 - 10$.

To calibrate the system, the engineer needs to calculate the combined energy readings for three specific fuel levels. 

Calculate the total sum of the energy outputs when the fuel levels are $0$, $1$, and $-1$. In other words, find the value of $f(0) + f(1) + f(-1)$.
