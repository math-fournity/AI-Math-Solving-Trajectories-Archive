-a_2$, $v=c_1-a_1$. Since $\gcd(d_1',d_2')=1$, by Bézout the form $d_1' u - d_2'
 v$ attains every integer. Thus a lattice point $C$ with $\text{Area}(ABC)=k$ ex
ists **if and only if** $g \mid 2k$.
