- $\triangle ABP$ (at the shared side $AB$),
- $\triangle BDP$ and $\triangle AEP$ (near vertices $B$ and $A$ respectively),
- quadrilateral $CDPE$ (near vertex $C$).

**Parametrization.** Place $B=(0,0)$, $C=(1,0)$, $A=(0,1)$, so $[\triangle ABC]=

**Area formulas** (as fractions of $[\triangle ABC]$):
$$a_1 = \frac{td}{\Delta},\quad a_2 = \frac{d^2(1-t)}{\Delta},\quad a_3 = \frac{
t^2(1-d)}{\Delta},\quad a_4 = \frac{(d+t)(1-d)(1-t)}{\Delta},$$
where $a_4$ follows from $a_4=1-a_1-a_2-a_3$ and the identity $a_1+a_2+a_3+a_4=1

**Case 1: Quadrilateral is the odd part** ($a_4=q$, $a_1=a_2=a_3=p$).

$a_1=a_2 \Rightarrow t=d/(1+d)$; $\;a_1=a_3 \Rightarrow d=t/(1+t)$. Substituting

$$d = \frac{d/(1+d)}{1+d/(1+d)} = \frac{d}{1+2d} \implies 1+2d=1 \implies d=0.$$
**Degenerate — impossible.**

**Case 2: Triangle at shared side is odd** ($a_1=q$, $a_2=a_3=a_4=p$).

Non-degenerate root: $d=2/3$, then $t=2/3$, $\Delta=8/9$.

$$a_1=\frac{4/9}{8/9}=\frac12, \qquad a_2=a_3=a_4=\frac{1/9\cdot 1/3}{8/9}=\frac

$$\boxed{\frac{p}{q}=\frac{1/6}{1/2}=\frac{1}{3}}.$$

$$t=\frac{\sqrt5-1}{2}, \qquad d=\frac{t}{1+t}=\frac{3-\sqrt5}{2}, \qquad \Delta

$$\boxed{\frac{p}{q}=2+\sqrt5}.$$
