## Proof

**Yes.** $G$ acts on any coadjoint orbit $\Omega \subset \mathfrak{g}^*$ by symp
lectomorphisms, preserving the Kirillov–Kostant–Souriau symplectic structure. He
re is the complete argument.

---

### Setup

Let $G$ be a Lie group with Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}$, and let $\mathfrak{g}^*$
 be the dual of $\mathfrak{g}$. The **coadjoint representation** $\operatorname{
Ad}^*: G \to \mathrm{GL}(\mathfrak{g}^*)$ is defined by

$$\langle \operatorname{Ad}^*_g \xi,\, X\rangle = \langle \xi,\, \operatorname{A
d}_{g^{-1}} X\rangle, \qquad g\in G,\ \xi\in\mathfrak{g}^*,\ X\in\mathfrak{g}.$$

For $\xi\in\mathfrak{g}^*$, the **coadjoint orbit** through $\xi$ is

$$\Omega = \mathcal{O}_\xi := \{\operatorname{Ad}^*_g \xi : g\in G\}\subset\math
frak{g}^*.$$

The infinitesimal coadjoint action of $X\in\mathfrak{g}$ is the vector field on
$\mathfrak{g}^*$

$$X^\#_\eta = \left.\frac{d}{dt}\right|_{t=0}\operatorname{Ad}^*_{\exp(tX)}\eta
= -\operatorname{ad}^*_X \eta,$$

i.e. $\langle X^\#_\eta,\, Y\rangle = -\langle\eta,\,[X,Y]\rangle$ for all $Y\in
\mathfrak{g}$.

---

### The KKS symplectic form

The **Kirillov–Kostant–Souriau form** on $\Omega$ is defined at each point $\eta
\in\Omega$ by

$$\omega_\eta\bigl(X^\#_\eta,\, Y^\#_\eta\bigr) = \langle \eta,\,[X,Y]\rangle, \
qquad X,Y\in\mathfrak{g}.$$

One must verify this is well-defined (independent of the choice of $X,Y$ represe
nting the tangent vectors), bilinear, skew, nondegenerate, and closed. The key p
oints:

- **Well-definedness:** The tangent space $T_\eta\Omega = \{X^\#_\eta : X\in\mat
hfrak{g}\}$. If $X^\#_\eta = 0$, then $\operatorname{ad}^*_X\eta = 0$, i.e. $\la
ngle\eta,[X,Z]\rangle = 0$ for all $Z$, so $\omega_\eta(X^\#_\eta, Y^\#_\eta) =
\langle\eta,[X,Y]\rangle = 0$. Hence $\omega$ depends only on the tangent vector
s, not on the representatives.

- **Nondegeneracy:** If $\omega_\eta(X^\#_\eta, Y^\#_\eta)=0$ for all $Y$, then
$\langle\eta,[X,Y]\rangle=0$ for all $Y$, which means $\operatorname{ad}^*_X\eta
=0$, i.e. $X^\#_\eta=0$. So $\omega$ is nondegenerate.

- **Closedness:** This follows from the identity $d\omega(X^\#,Y^\#,Z^\#) = -\om
ega([X^\#,Y^\#],Z^\#)+\text{cyclic}$. Using $[X^\#,Y^\#]=[X,Y]^\#$ (the coadjoin
t action is a Lie algebra homomorphism into vector fields) and the definition of
 $\omega$:

$$d\omega_\eta(X^\#_\eta,Y^\#_\eta,Z^\#_\eta) = -\langle\eta,[[X,Y],Z]\rangle -
\langle\eta,[[Y,Z],X]\rangle - \langle\eta,[[Z,X],Y]\rangle.$$

By the Jacobi identity $[[X,Y],Z]+[[Y,Z],X]+[[Z,X],Y]=0$, this vanishes. Hence $
d\omega=0$.

Thus $(\Omega,\omega)$ is a symplectic manifold.

---

### $G$ acts by symplectomorphisms

We now show that for every $g\in G$, the map $\Phi_g:\Omega\to\Omega$ defined by
 $\Phi_g(\eta)=\operatorname{Ad}^*_g\eta$ satisfies $\Phi_g^*\omega = \omega$.

The key computation: for $\eta\in\Omega$ and tangent vectors $X^\#_\eta, Y^\#_\e
ta\in T_\eta\Omega$,

$$(\Phi_g^*\omega)_\eta\bigl(X^\#_\eta,\,Y^\#_\eta\bigr) = \omega_{\operatorname
{Ad}^*_g\eta}\bigl((\Phi_g)_* X^\#_\eta,\,(\Phi_g)_* Y^\#_\eta\bigr).$$

We compute the pushforward. The coadjoint action satisfies the equivariance prop
erty:

$$(\Phi_g)_* X^\#_\eta = \left.\frac{d}{dt}\right|_{t=0}\operatorname{Ad}^*_g\op
eratorname{Ad}^*_{\exp(tX)}\eta = \left.\frac{d}{dt}\right|_{t=0}\operatorname{A
d}^*_{g\exp(tX)}\eta.$$

Using $g\exp(tX) = \exp(t\operatorname{Ad}_g X)\,g$ (a standard identity), and $
\operatorname{Ad}^*_{\exp(t\operatorname{Ad}_g X)\,g} = \operatorname{Ad}^*_{\ex
p(t\operatorname{Ad}_g X)}\operatorname{Ad}^*_g$, we get

$$(\Phi_g)_* X^\#_\eta = (\operatorname{Ad}_g X)^\#_{\operatorname{Ad}^*_g\eta}.
$$

Therefore:

$$(\Phi_g^*\omega)_\eta\bigl(X^\#_\eta,\,Y^\#_\eta\bigr) = \omega_{\operatorname
{Ad}^*_g\eta}\bigl((\operatorname{Ad}_g X)^\#_{\operatorname{Ad}^*_g\eta},\,(\op
eratorname{Ad}_g Y)^\#_{\operatorname{Ad}^*_g\eta}\bigr) = \langle \operatorname
{Ad}^*_g\eta,\,[\operatorname{Ad}_g X,\operatorname{Ad}_g Y]\rangle.$$

Since $\operatorname{Ad}_g$ is a Lie algebra automorphism, $[\operatorname{Ad}_g
 X,\operatorname{Ad}_g Y]=\operatorname{Ad}_g[X,Y]$. Thus:

$$(\Phi_g^*\omega)_\eta\bigl(X^\#_\eta,\,Y^\#_\eta\bigr) = \langle \operatorname
{Ad}^*_g\eta,\,\operatorname{Ad}_g[X,Y]\rangle = \langle \operatorname{Ad}^*_{g^
{-1}}\operatorname{Ad}^*_g\eta,\,[X,Y]\rangle = \langle\eta,[X,Y]\rangle = \omeg
a_\eta\bigl(X^\#_\eta,\,Y^\#_\eta\bigr).$$

Hence $\Phi_g^*\omega = \omega$ for every $g\in G$.

---

### Conclusion

The coadjoint action of $G$ on $\Omega$ preserves the KKS symplectic form: $\Phi
_g^*\omega=\omega$ for all $g\in G$. Therefore **$G$ acts on $\Omega$ by symplec
tomorphisms**, preserving the symplectic structure. $\blacksquare$
