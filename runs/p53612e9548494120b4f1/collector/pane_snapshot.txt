**Step 1: Every $y \in F^\perp$ is a constant sequence.**

Let $y = (y_k)_{k \in \mathbb{N}^*} \in F^\perp$. Since $e_n - e_m \in F$ for al

$$0 = \langle e_n - e_m,\, y\rangle = y_n - y_m \quad \text{for all } n \neq m.$
$
Hence $y_n = y_m$ for all $n, m \in \mathbb{N}^*$, so $y$ is a constant sequence

**Step 2: The only constant sequence in $\ell_2$ is the zero sequence.**

If $y = (c, c, c, \dots) \in \ell_2$, then
$$\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |y_k|^2 = \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |c|^2.$$
This series converges if and only if $c = 0$. Therefore $y = 0$.

**Step 3: Conclusion.**

Combining Steps 1 and 2, every $y \in F^\perp$ must be the zero sequence. Conver
sely, the zero sequence trivially belongs to $F^\perp$. Therefore
$$F^\perp = \{0\}.$$
