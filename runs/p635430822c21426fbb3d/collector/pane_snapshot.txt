### Setup

The PDE is $4u_t = u_{xx}$, i.e. $u_t = \tfrac{1}{4}u_{xx}$, on $x\in[0,2]$ with
 homogeneous Dirichlet BCs $u(t,0)=u(t,2)=0$.

Separate variables $u(t,x)=T(t)X(x)$:
$$4\frac{T'}{T}=\frac{X''}{X}=-\mu.$$

**Spatial problem:** $X''+\mu X=0$, $X(0)=X(2)=0$ gives
$$\mu_n=\left(\frac{n\pi}{2}\right)^2,\qquad X_n(x)=\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi x}{2}
\right),\quad n=1,2,\dots$$

**Temporal problem:** $4T'=-\mu_n T$, so
$$T_n(t)=e^{-\mu_n t/4}=e^{-n^2\pi^2 t/16}.$$

### Series form

$$u(t,x)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}b_n\,e^{-n^2\pi^2 t/16}\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi x}{2}\
right).$$

### Fourier sine coefficients

With $L=2$, the coefficient formula is $b_n=\frac{2}{L}\int_0^L f(x)\sin(n\pi x/
L)\,dx=\int_0^2 f(x)\sin(n\pi x/2)\,dx$.

Here $f(x)=5$ on $[2/3,4/3]$ and $0$ elsewhere, so
$$b_n=5\int_{2/3}^{4/3}\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi x}{2}\right)dx=5\left[-\frac{2}{n\
pi}\cos\!\left(\frac{n\pi x}{2}\right)\right]_{2/3}^{4/3}.$$

Evaluating:
$$b_n=-\frac{10}{n\pi}\left[\cos\!\left(\frac{2n\pi}{3}\right)-\cos\!\left(\frac
{n\pi}{3}\right)\right]=\frac{10}{n\pi}\left[\cos\!\left(\frac{n\pi}{3}\right)-\
cos\!\left(\frac{2n\pi}{3}\right)\right].$$

### Consistency check (symmetry)

The initial profile $f$ is symmetric about $x=1$. Since $\sin\!\big(\tfrac{n\pi(
2-x)}{2}\big)=(-1)^{n+1}\sin\!\big(\tfrac{n\pi x}{2}\big)$, even modes are antis
ymmetric about $x=1$ and must vanish. Indeed for $n=2k$:
$$\cos\!\left(\tfrac{2k\pi}{3}\right)-\cos\!\left(\tfrac{4k\pi}{3}\right)=0\quad
\text{(period-3 identity)},$$
confirming $b_{2k}=0$. Only odd $n$ contribute, consistent with the symmetry of
$f$.

### Final solution

$$\boxed{\,u(t,x)=\frac{10}{\pi}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n}\left[\cos\!\left(
\frac{n\pi}{3}\right)-\cos\!\left(\frac{2n\pi}{3}\right)\right]e^{-n^2\pi^2 t/16
}\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi x}{2}\right)\,}$$

for $t>0$, $x\in[0,2]$, with the even-$n$ terms identically zero.
