Let $f(u,v) = 8\sqrt{3}\sin u + 2\sqrt{66}\sin v - 4\sqrt{22}\sin(u+v)$. We maxi

**Maximize over u for fixed v.** Expanding $\sin(u+v) = \sin u\cos v + \cos u\si

$$f = \underbrace{(8\sqrt{3}-4\sqrt{22}\cos v)}_{A}\sin u + \underbrace{(-4\sqrt
{22}\sin v)}_{B}\cos u + \underbrace{2\sqrt{66}\sin v}_{C}.$$

Since $A\sin u + B\cos u$ ranges over $[-\sqrt{A^2+B^2},\,\sqrt{A^2+B^2}]$, we g

$$A^2+B^2 = (8\sqrt{3})^2 - 2\cdot 8\sqrt{3}\cdot 4\sqrt{22}\cos v + (4\sqrt{22}

So with $t = \cos v$:
$$g(t) = \sqrt{544 - 64\sqrt{66}\,t} + 2\sqrt{66}\sqrt{1-t^2}, \quad t\in[-1,1].

$$\frac{-32\sqrt{66}}{\sqrt{544-64\sqrt{66}\,t}} = \frac{2\sqrt{66}\,t}{\sqrt{1-
t^2}}.$$
