Looking at this problem, I need to count functions $f:\{1,\ldots,15\}\to\{1,\ldo
ts,15\}$ such that $f(f(x))-2f(x)+x\equiv 0\pmod{15}$ for all $x$.

## Setting Up the Structure

Identifying $\{1,\ldots,15\}$ with $\mathbb{Z}_{15}$, the condition is $f(f(x))
\equiv 2f(x)-x \pmod{15}$.

**Key substitution:** Let $d(x) = f(x)-x \pmod{15}$. Then:
$$d(f(x)) = f(f(x))-f(x) = (2f(x)-x)-f(x) = f(x)-x = d(x)$$

So $d$ is **constant along orbits**. The orbit of any $x$ under $f$ is $x, x+d,
x+2d, \ldots$ which is the coset $x+\langle d\rangle$. Since this is a finite cy
cle, **every element lies on a cycle**, meaning $f$ must be a **permutation**.

## Characterizing Valid Permutations

Each cycle of $f$ is a coset of some subgroup $\langle d\rangle \leq \mathbb{Z}_
{15}$, with $f$ acting as "add $d$". The cycles partition $\mathbb{Z}_{15}$ into
 disjoint cosets of (possibly different) subgroups. For each coset, the number o
f valid choices equals $\phi(|\langle d\rangle|)$ (the number of generators of t
hat subgroup).

## Subgroups of $\mathbb{Z}_{15}$

Via CRT: $\mathbb{Z}_{15}\cong\mathbb{Z}_3\times\mathbb{Z}_5$, giving a $3\times
 5$ grid.

| Subgroup | Order | Cosets | Coset type | Generators ($\phi$) |
|----------|-------|--------|------------|---------------------|
| $\{0\}$ | 1 | 15 | single cell | 1 |
| $\{0,5,10\}$ | 3 | 5 | column (size 3) | $\phi(3)=2$ |
| $\{0,3,6,9,12\}$ | 5 | 3 | row (size 5) | $\phi(5)=4$ |
| $\mathbb{Z}_{15}$ | 15 | 1 | whole grid | $\phi(15)=8$ |

## Counting Valid Partitions

**Critical observation:** Every row (coset of order-5 subgroup) intersects every
 column (coset of order-3 subgroup) in exactly one cell. So we **cannot mix rows
 and columns** in a partition.

**Case 1: Whole grid as one part.**
Choose a generator of $\mathbb{Z}_{15}$: $\phi(15)=8$ ways.

**Case 2a: Some rows + single cells for the rest.**
Choose $k$ of the 3 rows (each with 4 generator choices); remaining cells are fi
xed points (1 way each):
$$\sum_{k=0}^{3}\binom{3}{k}4^k = (1+4)^3 = 125$$

**Case 2b: Some columns + single cells for the rest.**
Choose $m$ of the 5 columns (each with 2 generator choices); remaining cells are
 fixed points:
$$\sum_{m=0}^{5}\binom{5}{m}2^m = (1+2)^5 = 243$$

**Overlap:** The all-single-cells partition (identity function) is counted in bo
th 2a and 2b: subtract 1.

## Final Count

$$8 + 125 + 243 - 1 = \boxed{375}$$

**Verification with small cases:** For $n=5$ (prime): $\phi(5)+1 = 4+1 = 5$ ✓. F
or $n=4$: subgroups give $2+4=6$ valid functions, verified by enumeration ✓. The
 general formula for $n=pq$ (distinct primes): $(p-1)(q-1)+q^p+p^q-1$, which for
 $p=3,q=5$ gives $8+125+243-1=375$.
