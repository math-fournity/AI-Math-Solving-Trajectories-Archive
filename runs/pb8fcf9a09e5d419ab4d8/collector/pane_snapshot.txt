## Solution

**Setup.** Place the body with its axis along $z$, profile $z = u(r)$ for $0 \le
 r \le a$. Particles rain down from above with velocity $\mathbf{v}_i = (0,0,-v)
$, number density $n$, mass $m$. The flux hitting the annular ring at radius $r$
 (per unit radial distance, per unit time) is $2\pi n v r$, as given.

**Surface normal.** Parameterize the surface by $(r\cos\theta,\, r\sin\theta,\,
u(r))$. The outward (upward) unit normal is

$$\hat{\mathbf{n}} = \frac{(-u'\cos\theta,\,-u'\sin\theta,\,1)}{\sqrt{1+u'^2}},
\qquad u' = \frac{du}{dr}.$$

**Elastic reflection.** For specular reflection $\mathbf{v}_r = \mathbf{v}_i - 2
(\mathbf{v}_i\cdot\hat{\mathbf n})\hat{\mathbf n}$. Since

$$\mathbf{v}_i \cdot \hat{\mathbf n} = \frac{-v}{\sqrt{1+u'^2}},$$

the $z$-component of the reflected velocity is

$$v_{r,z} = -v + \frac{2v}{1+u'^2} = v\,\frac{1-u'^2}{1+u'^2}.$$

**Momentum transfer per particle (to the body, $z$-direction).**

$$\Delta p_z = m\,(v_{i,z} - v_{r,z}) = m\!\left(-v - v\frac{1-u'^2}{1+u'^2}\rig
ht) = -\frac{2mv}{1+u'^2}.$$

The negative sign means the force on the body points in $-z$ (downward), as expe
cted.

**Integrating over the body.** Multiplying by the particle rate $2\pi n v r\,dr$
 at radius $r$ and integrating:

$$F_z = \int_0^a \!\left(-\frac{2mv}{1+u'^2}\right) 2\pi n v r\,dr = -\,4\pi n m
 v^2 \int_0^a \frac{r\,dr}{1+\bigl(u'(r)\bigr)^2}.$$

**Checks.**
- *Flat plate* ($u'=0$): $F = 4\pi nmv^2 \cdot a^2/2 = 2\pi a^2 nmv^2$, the stan
dard result for a disk of area $\pi a^2$ receiving momentum $2mv$ per particle a
t flux $nv$. ✓
- *45° cone* ($u'=1$): each particle reflects horizontally, $v_{r,z}=0$, giving
$\Delta p_z = -mv$ per particle. Formula gives $-2mv/(1+1) = -mv$. ✓

**Result.** The resistance force (magnitude) exerted by the gas on the body is

$$\boxed{\,F = 4\pi n m v^{2}\!\int_{0}^{a}\frac{r\,dr}{1+\left(\dfrac{du}{dr}\r
ight)^{2}}\,}$$

directed along the symmetry axis ($-z$, opposing the incident beam).
