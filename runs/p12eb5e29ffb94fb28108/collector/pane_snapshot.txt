**Step 1 (Sequential continuity).** The hypothesis says $x^{**}(x^{*}_n) \to 0$
for every weak*-null sequence $(x^{*}_n)$ in $X^{*}$. By linearity this gives se

$$x^{**}(x^{*}_n) - x^{**}(x^{*}) = x^{**}(x^{*}_n - x^{*}) \to 0,$$

**Step 2 (The kernel is weak*-sequentially closed).** Set $K := \ker(x^{**}) = \
{x^{*} \in X^{*} : x^{**}(x^{*}) = 0\}$, a norm-closed (hence convex) subspace.
If $x^{*}_n \in K$ and $x^{*}_n \to x^{*}$ weak*, then by Step 1, $x^{**}(x^{*}_
n) \to x^{**}(x^{*})$. Since $x^{**}(x^{*}_n) = 0$ for all $n$, we get $x^{**}(x

**Step 3 (Separability upgrades sequential closedness to closedness).** Since $X

* (the weak* topology on bounded sets is metrizable when $X$ is separable). In a

We claim $K \cap rB_{X^{*}}$ is weak*-sequentially closed for each $r > 0$. Inde
