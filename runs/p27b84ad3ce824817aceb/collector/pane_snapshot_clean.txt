## Solution

**Setup.** Place the square as $[0,100]\times[0,100]$ with the tower at the cent
er $(50,50)$. A landing point $(x,y)$ triggers the alarm when its distance to th
e nearest perimeter wall is **less than** its distance to the tower:

$$\min(x,\,100-x,\,y,\,100-y) \;<\; \sqrt{(x-50)^2+(y-50)^2}.$$

**Symmetry reduction.** Both the "distance to nearest wall" and "distance to tow
er" are invariant under the full dihedral symmetry group $D_4$ of the square (re
flections across $x=50$, $y=50$, and the diagonals; 90° rotations). This splits
the square into **8 congruent fundamental triangles**. It suffices to compute th
e alarm area in one triangle and multiply by 8.

Take the triangle: first quadrant relative to center, with $x\ge y$, i.e. $x\in[
50,100]$, $y\in[50,100]$, $x\ge y$. Set $u=100-x$, $v=100-y$, so $0\le u\le v\le
 50$. In this region the nearest-wall distance is $\min(u,v)=u$, and the tower d
istance is $\sqrt{(50-u)^2+(50-v)^2}$. The triangle has area $\tfrac12(50)(50)=1
250$.

**Alarm vs. non-alarm in the triangle.** The alarm condition is
$$u < \sqrt{(50-u)^2+(50-v)^2}.$$
The **non-alarm** (closer to tower) region is the complement:
$$u^2 \ge (50-u)^2+(50-v)^2 \;\Longleftrightarrow\; u \ge 50 - v + \frac{v^2}{10
0}.$$
Combined with $u\le v$, this requires $50-v+v^2/100 \le v$, i.e.
$$v^2 - 200v + 5000 \le 0 \;\Longleftrightarrow\; v\in[100-50\sqrt2,\;100+50\sqr
t2].$$
Intersecting with $v\in[0,50]$ gives $v\in[100-50\sqrt2,\,50]$.

**Non-alarm area in the triangle:**
$$A_{\text{non}}=\int_{100-50\sqrt2}^{50}\!\left[v-\left(50-v+\tfrac{v^2}{100}\r
ight)\right]dv=\int_{100-50\sqrt2}^{50}\!\left(2v-50-\tfrac{v^2}{100}\right)dv.$
$
With antiderivative $F(v)=v^2-50v-\tfrac{v^3}{300}$:
- $F(50)=-\tfrac{1250}{3}$,
- $F(100-50\sqrt2)=\tfrac{5000}{3}(1-\sqrt2)$ (using $(100-50\sqrt2)^2=15000-100
00\sqrt2$ and $(100-50\sqrt2)^3=2500000-1750000\sqrt2$).

So
$$A_{\text{non}}=F(50)-F(100-50\sqrt2)=-\tfrac{1250}{3}-\tfrac{5000}{3}(1-\sqrt2
)=\tfrac{5000\sqrt2-6250}{3}.$$

**Alarm area in the triangle:**
$$A_{\text{alm}}=1250-A_{\text{non}}=1250-\tfrac{5000\sqrt2-6250}{3}=\tfrac{1000
0-5000\sqrt2}{3}.$$

**Total probability.** Multiplying by 8 congruent triangles and dividing by the
square area $10000$:
$$P=\frac{8\cdot\tfrac{10000-5000\sqrt2}{3}}{10000}=\frac{80000-40000\sqrt2}{300
00}=\frac{8-4\sqrt2}{3}.$$

Sanity check: $P\approx\frac{8-5.657}{3}\approx 0.781$, consistent with most of
a large square being wall-dominated while only a central disk-like region is tow
er-dominated.

**Extracting $a,b,c,d$.** We have $P=\dfrac{a-b\sqrt c}{d}$ with
$$a=8,\quad b=4,\quad c=2,\quad d=3.$$
Verification: $c=2$ is square-free; $\gcd(8,3)=1$; $\gcd(4,3)=1$. ✓

$$a+b+c+d = 8+4+2+3 = \boxed{17}.$$
