# Problem

In a circular botanical garden with a radius of exactly 1 kilometer, six specialized observation drones—named Alpha, Bravo, Charlie, Delta, Echo, and Foxtrot—are stationed at equidistant points along the perimeter fence. To map the garden’s layout, mission control establishes six long-distance scanning beams that form a complex network across the terrain.

The specific flight paths for these scanners are as follows:
- A beam connects Alpha to Delta.
- A beam connects Delta to Foxtrot.
- A beam connects Foxtrot to Bravo.
- A beam connects Bravo to Echo.
- A beam connects Echo to Charlie.
- A beam connects Charlie back to Alpha.

These six intersecting linear paths enclose a central 12-sided meadow at the heart of the garden. Calculate the total surface area of this 12-sided meadow in square kilometers.
