**Answer: No, the inequality does not hold in general.** A counterexample suffic
es.

---

**Counterexample.** Let $f$ be the uniform density on $[0,1]$, i.e. $f(x)=1$ for
 $x\in[0,1]$, and let $g(x)=2x$ on $[0,1]$ (a valid density since $\int_0^1 2x\,
dx=1$). Both are supported on $[0,1]$, and $g>0$ on $(0,1]$, so the integrand on
 the right-hand side is well-defined a.e.

**Left-hand side (KL divergence).**

$$\mathrm{KL}(f,g)=\int_0^1 f(x)\log\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}\,dx=\int_0^1 \log\frac{1}{
2x}\,dx=-\int_0^1\log(2x)\,dx.$$

Splitting,

$$\int_0^1 \log(2x)\,dx=\log 2+\int_0^1\log x\,dx=\log 2+\big[x\log x-x\big]_0^1
=\log 2-1.$$

Hence

$$\mathrm{KL}(f,g)=1-\log 2\approx 0.3069.$$

**Right-hand side.**

$$\int_0^1\frac{(\sqrt{f}-\sqrt{g})^2}{f}\,dx=\int_0^1\frac{(1-\sqrt{2x})^2}{1}\
,dx=\int_0^1\bigl(1-2\sqrt{2x}+2x\bigr)\,dx.$$

Computing term by term:

$$=1-2\sqrt{2}\int_0^1 x^{1/2}\,dx+2\int_0^1 x\,dx=1-2\sqrt{2}\cdot\frac{2}{3}+2
\cdot\frac{1}{2}=2-\frac{4\sqrt{2}}{3}.$$

Since $\frac{4\sqrt{2}}{3}\approx 1.8856$,

$$\text{RHS}=2-\frac{4\sqrt{2}}{3}\approx 0.1144.$$

**Comparison.**

$$\mathrm{KL}(f,g)\approx 0.3069 \;>\; 0.1144 \approx \text{RHS}.$$

Therefore $\mathrm{KL}(f,g)>\displaystyle\int\frac{(\sqrt{f}-\sqrt{g})^2}{f}\,dx
$ for this pair, and the proposed inequality is **violated**.

---

**Remark (why it fails in general).** Writing $r=g/f$, the right-hand side is $\
int(1-\sqrt{r})^2\,dx$, which (unlike the squared Hellinger distance $\tfrac12\i
nt(\sqrt f-\sqrt g)^2\,dx$) carries no density weight $f$. The standard informat
ion-theoretic inequalities go the *other* direction: $\mathrm{KL}(f,g)\ge H^2(f,
g)$ (KL dominates Hellinger-type quantities), so placing KL on the smaller side
of an inequality against a Hellinger-type functional is generally false. The cou
nterexample above makes this concrete: with $f$ uniform, the RHS reduces to $\in
t_0^1(1-\sqrt g)^2\,dx=2-2\int_0^1\sqrt g\,dx$, which is strictly smaller than $
\mathrm{KL}(f,g)=-\int_0^1\log g\,dx$ whenever $g\neq f$ (since $h-\log h\ge 1$
with equality only at $h=1$, applied to $h=\sqrt g$).
