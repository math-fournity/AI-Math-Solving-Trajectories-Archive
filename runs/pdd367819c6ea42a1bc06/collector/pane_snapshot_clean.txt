## Proof

Let the tangent plane $T$ touch the ellipsoid $\frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}+\
frac{z^2}{c^2}=1$ at the point $(x_0, y_0, z_0)$ on the surface. The equation of
 the tangent plane is

$$\frac{x\,x_0}{a^2}+\frac{y\,y_0}{b^2}+\frac{z\,z_0}{c^2}=1.$$

**Intercepts with the coordinate axes.** Setting two variables to zero each time
:

- $x$-intercept: $\displaystyle X=\frac{a^2}{x_0}$
- $y$-intercept: $\displaystyle Y=\frac{b^2}{y_0}$
- $z$-intercept: $\displaystyle Z=\frac{c^2}{z_0}$

For the tetrahedron bounded by $T$ and the planes $x=0,\,y=0,\,z=0$ to lie in th
e first octant, we take $x_0,y_0,z_0>0$. Its volume is

$$V=\frac{1}{6}\,XYZ=\frac{a^2 b^2 c^2}{6\,x_0 y_0 z_0}.$$

**Minimizing $V$ is equivalent to maximizing $x_0 y_0 z_0$** subject to the cons
traint

$$\frac{x_0^2}{a^2}+\frac{y_0^2}{b^2}+\frac{z_0^2}{c^2}=1,\qquad x_0,y_0,z_0>0.$
$

**Applying AM–GM** to the three non-negative terms $\frac{x_0^2}{a^2},\,\frac{y_
0^2}{b^2},\,\frac{z_0^2}{c^2}$:

$$\frac{1}{3}\left(\frac{x_0^2}{a^2}+\frac{y_0^2}{b^2}+\frac{z_0^2}{c^2}\right)\
ge\left(\frac{x_0^2\,y_0^2\,z_0^2}{a^2 b^2 c^2}\right)^{1/3}.$$

Using the constraint (left side $= \frac{1}{3}$):

$$\frac{1}{3}\ge\left(\frac{x_0\,y_0\,z_0}{abc}\right)^{2/3},$$

so

$$x_0\,y_0\,z_0\le abc\cdot\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{3/2}=\frac{abc}{3\sqrt{3}}.
$$

**Equality holds** when $\frac{x_0^2}{a^2}=\frac{y_0^2}{b^2}=\frac{z_0^2}{c^2}=\
frac{1}{3}$, i.e. at the point $\left(\frac{a}{\sqrt 3},\,\frac{b}{\sqrt 3},\,\f
rac{c}{\sqrt 3}\right)$, which indeed lies on the ellipsoid.

**Substituting the maximum of $x_0 y_0 z_0$ into the volume formula:**

$$V_{\min}=\frac{a^2 b^2 c^2}{6\cdot\dfrac{abc}{3\sqrt 3}}=\frac{a^2 b^2 c^2\cdo
t 3\sqrt 3}{6\,abc}=\frac{abc\,\sqrt 3}{2}.$$

**Verification via Lagrange multipliers (consistency check).** Maximize $\ln(x_0
 y_0 z_0)$ subject to $g=\frac{x_0^2}{a^2}+\frac{y_0^2}{b^2}+\frac{z_0^2}{c^2}-1
=0$. The conditions $\frac{1}{x_0}=2\lambda\frac{x_0}{a^2}$, etc., give $\frac{x
_0^2}{a^2}=\frac{y_0^2}{b^2}=\frac{z_0^2}{c^2}=\frac{1}{2\lambda}$, and the cons
traint yields $\frac{3}{2\lambda}=1$, so $\lambda=\frac{3}{2}$, confirming the c
ritical point above. The boundary of the feasible region (any coordinate $\to 0$
) sends $x_0 y_0 z_0\to 0$, i.e. $V\to\infty$, so this interior critical point i
s the global minimum of $V$.

**Conclusion.** The smallest possible volume of the tetrahedron is

$$\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt{3}\,a\,b\,c}{2}}.$$
