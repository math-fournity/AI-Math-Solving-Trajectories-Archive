## Solution

We seek the smallest positive root of

$$f(x) = \cosh x \cos x - 1 = 0.$$

### Locating the root

At $x=0$: $\cosh 0\cos 0 = 1$, so $x=0$ is a root (excluded — we want *positive*
).

For small $x>0$, using $\cosh x = 1+\tfrac{x^{2}}{2}+\tfrac{x^{4}}{24}+\cdots$ a
nd $\cos x = 1-\tfrac{x^{2}}{2}+\tfrac{x^{4}}{24}-\cdots$,

$$\cosh x\cos x = 1 - \tfrac{x^{4}}{6}+O(x^{6}) < 1,$$

so $f<0$ just to the right of $0$. Since $\cos x=0$ at $\pi/2$ (so $f=-1$) and $
\cos x<0$ on $(\pi/2,3\pi/2)$ (so $f< -1$ there, as $\cosh x>0$), the product is
 negative throughout $(0,3\pi/2)$. At $x=3\pi/2$, $\cos x=0$, $f=-1$; just beyon
d $3\pi/2$, $\cos x$ becomes positive while $\cosh x$ is large, so $f$ rises ste
eply and crosses $0$ before $2\pi$ (where $f=\cosh(2\pi)-1\gg 0$). Hence the sma
llest positive root lies in $(3\pi/2,\,2\pi)$, very close to $3\pi/2$.

### Series expansion about $a=3\pi/2$

Set $x = a+\delta$ with $a=\tfrac{3\pi}{2}=4.712388980384698\ldots$ and $\delta>
0$ small. Then $\cos(a+\delta)=\sin\delta$ and

$$\cosh(a+\delta)=\cosh a\cosh\delta+\sinh a\sinh\delta = c + s\delta + \tfrac{c
}{2}\delta^{2}+\tfrac{s}{6}\delta^{3}+\cdots,$$

where $c=\cosh a$, $s=\sinh a$. Multiplying by $\sin\delta=\delta-\tfrac{\delta^
{3}}{6}+\tfrac{\delta^{5}}{120}-\cdots$ and collecting powers:

$$\sin\delta\cdot\cosh(a+\delta)=c\delta+s\delta^{2}+\tfrac{c}{3}\delta^{3}-\tfr
ac{c}{30}\delta^{5}+O(\delta^{7}).$$

The equation $\cosh x\cos x=1$ becomes

$$c\delta+s\delta^{2}+\tfrac{c}{3}\delta^{3}-\tfrac{c}{30}\delta^{5}=1.\tag{*}$$

### Numerical values

$$e^{a}=111.31747\ldots,\qquad c=\cosh a=55.66323\ldots,\qquad s=\sinh a=55.6542
4\ldots,\qquad t=s/c=0.9998385\ldots$$

A first estimate from $c\delta\approx 1$ gives $\delta_{0}=1/c=0.01796674$. Subs
tituting into $(*)$ and solving the quadratic correction $c\delta(1+t\delta+\del
ta^{2}/3)=1$ yields

$$\delta_{1}=0.01765337.$$

### Newton refinement

Evaluate the full $f$ at $x=a+\delta_{1}$:

$$f = c\delta_{1}+s\delta_{1}^{2}+\tfrac{c}{3}\delta_{1}^{3}-1 = 0.9826436+0.017
3438+0.0001021-1 = +8.95\times10^{-5}.$$

The derivative is

$$f'(x)=\sinh x\cos x-\cosh x\sin x,\qquad f'(a+\delta_{1})\approx s\delta_{1}+c
=56.6457.$$

Newton step:

$$\delta_{2}=\delta_{1}-\frac{f}{f'}=0.01765337-1.580\times10^{-6}=0.01765179.$$

Re‑evaluating gives $f\approx-1.55\times10^{-6}$, so one more micro‑step adds $+
2.7\times10^{-8}$:

$$\delta = 0.017651817\ldots$$

### Result

$$x_{1}=a+\delta=4.712388980384698+0.017651817=4.730040797\ldots$$

Rounding to six decimal places,

$$\boxed{x_{1}=4.730041}.$$
