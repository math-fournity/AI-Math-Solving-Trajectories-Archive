t $x \in M$. We compute the local homology group $H_n(M, M - \{x\})$.

**Step 1: Excision reduces to a local model.**

Since $M$ is an $n$-manifold, there exists an open neighborhood $U$ of $x$ homeo
morphic to $\mathbb{R}^n$. Choose an open ball $B \subset U$ centered at $x$ (un
der some chart $\varphi: U \to \mathbb{R}^n$ with $\varphi(x) = 0$), so that $\o
verline{B} \subset U$.

Set $A = M - B$. Then $A \subset M - \{x\}$ (since $x \in B$), and $\overline{A}
 = M - B \subset M - \{x\} = \operatorname{int}(M - \{x\})$ (the latter holds be
cause $B$ is open, so $M - \{x\}$ contains the open set $M - \overline{\{x\}}$..
. more carefully: $\overline{A} \cap \overline{M - (M - \{x\})} = \overline{M-B}

$U$ not containing the boundary, and $x$ is interior to $B$). The excision theor

$$H_n(M, M - \{x\}) \cong H_n(M - A, (M - \{x\}) - A) = H_n(B, B - \{x\}).$$

**Step 2: Reduce to $(\mathbb{R}^n, \mathbb{R}^n - \{0\})$.**

Via the chart $\varphi$, the pair $(B, B - \{x\})$ is homeomorphic to $(D^n, D^n
 - \{0\})$, where $D^n$ is the open unit ball. By excision again (or directly),
$H_n(D^n, D^n - \{0\}) \cong H_n(\mathbb{R}^n, \mathbb{R}^n - \{0\})$.

**Step 3: Use the long exact sequence.**

Consider the long exact sequence of the pair $(\mathbb{R}^n, \mathbb{R}^n - \{0\
})$:

$$\cdots \to H_n(\mathbb{R}^n) \to H_n(\mathbb{R}^n, \mathbb{R}^n - \{0\}) \to \
widetilde{H}_{n-1}(\mathbb{R}^n - \{0\}) \to \widetilde{H}_{n-1}(\mathbb{R}^n) \
to \cdots$$

Since $\mathbb{R}^n$ is contractible, $H_n(\mathbb{R}^n) = 0$ and $\widetilde{H}
_{n-1}(\mathbb{R}^n) = 0$. Therefore:

$$H_n(\mathbb{R}^n, \mathbb{R}^n - \{0\}) \cong \widetilde{H}_{n-1}(\mathbb{R}^n
 - \{0\}).$$

**Step 4: Compute $\widetilde{H}_{n-1}(\mathbb{R}^n - \{0\})$.**

The space $\mathbb{R}^n - \{0\}$ deformation retracts onto $S^{n-1}$ (via the ra
dial retraction $v \mapsto v/\|v\|$). Hence:

$$\widetilde{H}_{n-1}(\mathbb{R}^n - \{0\}) \cong \widetilde{H}_{n-1}(S^{n-1}) \
cong \mathbb{Z}.$$

**Conclusion.**

Combining all steps:

$$\boxed{H_n(M, M - \{x\}) \cong \mathbb{Z}.}$$

$$H_k(M, M - \{x\}) \cong \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & k = n, \\ 0 & k \neq n. \en

This is the **orientation local system** of $M$: the group $\mathbb{Z}$ at each

ent that $M$ is locally orientable — a defining property of manifolds.
