# Solution

**Setup.** Label the 24 points $0, 1, 2, \ldots, 23$ around the circle. The arc
length between two points $i$ and $j$ is $|i - j|$ or $24 - |i - j|$ (the two ar
cs). Requiring that no arc length equals 3 or 8 means: for any two chosen points
, their difference mod 24 is not $\pm 3$ or $\pm 8$.

**Key reduction via CRT.** Since $\gcd(3, 8) = 1$, the Chinese Remainder Theorem
 gives an isomorphism
$$\mathbb{Z}_{24} \;\cong\; \mathbb{Z}_3 \times \mathbb{Z}_8, \qquad i \;\mapsto
\; (i \bmod 3,\; i \bmod 8).$$

Under this map, the forbidden differences transform as:
- Difference $\pm 3$ in $\mathbb{Z}_{24}$ $\;\mapsto\;$ $(0, \pm 3)$ in $\mathbb
{Z}_3 \times \mathbb{Z}_8$,
- Difference $\pm 8$ in $\mathbb{Z}_{24}$ $\;\mapsto\;$ $(\pm 1, 0)$ in $\mathbb
{Z}_3 \times \mathbb{Z}_8$.

**Translating the constraints.** We choose 8 points from $\mathbb{Z}_3 \times \m
athbb{Z}_8$ (24 elements). The constraints become:

1. **From $(\pm 1, 0)$:** If two chosen points share the same second coordinate
$b$, they must share the same first coordinate $a$. Since we choose 8 points and
 there are exactly 8 values of $b \in \{0,\ldots,7\}$, each $b$-value is used ex
actly once. Thus the selection is determined by a function $a : \{0,\ldots,7\} \
to \{0,1,2\}$, giving the 8 points $\{(a(b), b) : b = 0,\ldots,7\}$.

2. **From $(0, \pm 3)$:** If two chosen points share the same $a$-value, their $
b$-values must not differ by $\pm 3 \pmod{8}$.

**Graph formulation.** Define a graph $G$ on vertices $\{0,1,\ldots,7\}$ where $
b$ and $b'$ are adjacent iff $b - b' \equiv \pm 3 \pmod{8}$. The edges are:
$$\{0,3\},\; \{0,5\},\; \{1,4\},\; \{1,6\},\; \{2,5\},\; \{2,7\},\; \{3,6\},\; \
{4,7\}.$$

Each vertex has degree 2. Tracing the adjacency: $0 - 3 - 6 - 1 - 4 - 7 - 2 - 5
- 0$, which is a single **8-cycle**. So $G = C_8$.

The function $a : \{0,\ldots,7\} \to \{0,1,2\}$ must assign different values to
adjacent vertices in $G$—this is exactly a **proper 3-coloring** of $C_8$.

**Counting proper 3-colorings of $C_8$.** The chromatic polynomial of the cycle
$C_n$ is:
$$P(C_n, k) = (k-1)^n + (-1)^n(k-1).$$

For $n = 8$, $k = 3$:
$$P(C_8, 3) = (3-1)^8 + (-1)^8(3-1) = 2^8 + 2 = 256 + 2 = 258.$$

**Bijectivity check.** Each valid 8-point subset of $\mathbb{Z}_{24}$ maps (via
CRT) to a unique proper 3-coloring of $C_8$, and conversely each proper 3-colori
ng yields a unique valid subset. The correspondence is bijective, so the count i
s exact.

**Answer:** $\boxed{258}$
