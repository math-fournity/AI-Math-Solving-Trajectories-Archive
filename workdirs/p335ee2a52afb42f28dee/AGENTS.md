# Problem

A courier named Leo delivers packages within a city laid out in a perfect square grid. He starts his shift at the central hub located at intersection $(0,0)$. Every minute, he travels exactly one block to an adjacent intersection. He chooses his direction (North, South, East, or West) with equal probability of $1/4$ each. However, there is a strict safety protocol: if Leo has traveled in the same direction for the last four consecutive blocks, he is forbidden from continuing in that direction for his next move. In such a case, he chooses his next direction uniformly at random from the three remaining options.

Every time Leo leaves an intersection, he drops a sensor that emits a signal for exactly $150$ seconds before self-destructing. If Leo enters an intersection while a sensor he previously dropped is still active, he triggers a security alarm. 

After Leo completes exactly $5$ moves, the probability that he has never triggered an alarm can be expressed as an irreducible fraction $\frac{a}{b}$. Find the value of $a+b$.
