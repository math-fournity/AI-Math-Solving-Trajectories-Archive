Step 1: Verify the defining relations.

  • $|\sigma| = n$: $\sigma$ is an $n$-cycle, so $\sigma^n = \mathrm{id}$ and $
  \sigma^k \neq \mathrm{id}$ for $0 < k < n$. ✓

  disjoint transpositions (when $n$ is even, vertex $1$ and vertex $n/2+1$ are
  both fixed; when $n$ is odd, only vertex $1$ is fixed). In either case $\tau^2
  = \mathrm{id}$. ✓

By the relation $\tau\sigma\tau = \sigma^{-1}$ (equivalently $\tau\sigma =
\sigma^{-1}\tau$), every element of $H$ can be written as $\sigma^k$ or $
\sigma^k\tau$ for some $0 \leq k \leq n-1$. Thus $|H| \leq 2n$.
