## Proof

We work over a field $k$. Let $\mathbb{P}^1 = \mathbb{P}^1_k$ with homogeneous c
oordinates $[X:Y]$, and let $p \in \mathbb{P}^1$ be any (closed) point. We show
$\mathbb{P}^1 \setminus \{p\} \cong \mathbb{A}^1$ as varieties over $k$.

**Step 1: Reduction to a fixed point via $\mathrm{PGL}_2$-transitivity.**

The group $\mathrm{PGL}_2(k) = \mathrm{GL}_2(k)/k^\times$ acts on $\mathbb{P}^1$
 by
$$
\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} \cdot [X:Y] = [aX+bY : cX+dY],
$$
and this action is by automorphisms of $\mathbb{P}^1$. The action is transitive
on closed points: given $p = [\alpha:\beta]$ and $q = [\gamma:\delta]$, both wit
h not both coordinates zero, there exists $M \in \mathrm{GL}_2(k)$ sending $(\al
pha,\beta)$ to a scalar multiple of $(\gamma,\delta)$ (complete each to a basis
of $k^2$ when the coordinates are $k$-rational; for non-rational closed points,
the same argument works over the residue field, or one notes that over $\bar{k}$
 all points become rational and the descent is automatic).

Hence, replacing $p$ by any other point via an automorphism of $\mathbb{P}^1$, i
t suffices to prove the claim for a single convenient choice. We take $p = [1:0]
$, the "point at infinity."

**Step 2: The standard affine chart.**

Consider the open subset $D_+(Y) = \{[X:Y] \in \mathbb{P}^1 : Y \neq 0\}$. This
is exactly $\mathbb{P}^1 \setminus V_+(Y) = \mathbb{P}^1 \setminus \{[1:0]\} = \
mathbb{P}^1 \setminus \{p\}$.

The chart $D_+(Y)$ is an affine variety with coordinate ring
$$
\mathcal{O}(D_+(Y)) = k[X/Y] = k[t], \qquad t := X/Y.
$$
Indeed, $D_+(Y) = \mathrm{Spec}\, k[t]_{(0)} \cong \mathrm{Spec}\, k[t]$, since
the degree-zero part of the localization $k[X,Y]_{(Y)}$ is generated by $X/Y$ wi
th no further relations.

**Step 3: Identification with $\mathbb{A}^1$.**

By definition $\mathbb{A}^1_k = \mathrm{Spec}\, k[t]$. Therefore
$$
\mathbb{P}^1 \setminus \{p\} \;=\; D_+(Y) \;\cong\; \mathrm{Spec}\, k[t] \;=\; \
mathbb{A}^1_k.
$$

The isomorphism is given explicitly on points by $[X:Y] \mapsto X/Y$ (for $Y \ne
q 0$), with inverse $t \mapsto [t:1]$.

**Conclusion.** Since $\mathrm{PGL}_2(k)$ acts transitively on $\mathbb{P}^1$, e
very point $p$ is equivalent to $[1:0]$ under an automorphism, and removing $[1:
0]$ yields the standard affine chart $D_+(Y) \cong \mathbb{A}^1$. Hence for a ge
neral (indeed, for *every*) point $p \in \mathbb{P}^1$,
$$
\mathbb{P}^1 \setminus \{p\} \;\cong\; \mathbb{A}^1. \qquad \blacksquare
$$
