## Green's Function for the Dirichlet Problem on the Unit Ball

**Problem.** Find the form of $G(x, \xi)$ for the Dirichlet problem $\Delta u =
f$ in $B = \{x \in \mathbb{R}^n : |x| < 1\}$, $u = 0$ on $\partial B$.

---

### Setup

Let $\Phi$ denote the fundamental solution of Laplace's equation:

$$
\Phi(x) = \begin{cases}
\dfrac{1}{(2-n)\omega_n}\,|x|^{2-n}, & n \ge 3,\\[6pt]
-\dfrac{1}{2\pi}\log|x|, & n = 2,
\end{cases}
$$

where $\omega_n = |S^{n-1}|$ is the surface area of the unit sphere. We seek

$$G(x,\xi) = \Phi(x-\xi) - h(x,\xi),$$

where $h(\cdot,\xi)$ is harmonic in $B$ and $h(x,\xi) = \Phi(x-\xi)$ for $|x|=1$
, so that $G = 0$ on $\partial B$.

### Kelvin inversion

Define the **inverse point** (Kelvin transform image) of $\xi \neq 0$ with respe
ct to the unit sphere:

$$\xi^* = \frac{\xi}{|\xi|^2}, \qquad |\xi^*| = \frac{1}{|\xi|} > 1 \quad (\text
{since } |\xi|<1).$$

The key identity on the boundary $|x|=1$:

$$|x-\xi|^2 = 1 - 2x\cdot\xi + |\xi|^2, \qquad |\xi|^2|x-\xi^*|^2 = |\xi|^2 - 2x
\cdot\xi + 1.$$

These are equal, so

$$\boxed{|x - \xi| = |\xi|\,|x - \xi^*| \quad \text{for } |x|=1.} \tag{$\star$}$
$

### Constructing the corrector

Because of $(\star)$, on $|x|=1$:

- **$n \ge 3$:** $\Phi(x-\xi) = \frac{1}{(2-n)\omega_n}|x-\xi|^{2-n} = \frac{|\x
i|^{2-n}}{(2-n)\omega_n}|x-\xi^*|^{2-n} = |\xi|^{2-n}\,\Phi(x-\xi^*)$.

- **$n = 2$:** $\Phi(x-\xi) = -\frac{1}{2\pi}\log|x-\xi| = -\frac{1}{2\pi}\log\b
igl(|\xi|\,|x-\xi^*|\bigr) = \Phi(x-\xi^*) - \frac{1}{2\pi}\log|\xi|$.

Both cases are unified by observing

$$\Phi\bigl(|\xi|\,(x-\xi^*)\bigr) = \begin{cases} |\xi|^{2-n}\,\Phi(x-\xi^*), &
 n\ge 3,\\ \Phi(x-\xi^*) - \frac{1}{2\pi}\log|\xi|, & n=2, \end{cases}$$

which equals $\Phi(x-\xi)$ on $|x|=1$ by $(\star)$. Since $\xi^*$ lies **outside
** $B$ (as $|\xi^*|>1$), the function $x \mapsto \Phi(|\xi|(x-\xi^*))$ is harmon
ic in $x$ throughout $B$. It is therefore the unique corrector.

### The Green's function

$$\boxed{G(x,\xi) \;=\; \Phi(x-\xi) \;-\; \Phi\!\bigl(|\xi|\,(x-\xi^*)\bigr), \q
quad \xi^*=\frac{\xi}{|\xi|^2}.}$$

**Explicitly:**

- **$n \ge 3$:**
$$G(x,\xi) = \frac{1}{(2-n)\omega_n}\Bigl[\,|x-\xi|^{2-n} \;-\; |\xi|^{2-n}\,|x-
\xi^*|^{2-n}\,\Bigr].$$

- **$n = 2$:**
$$G(x,\xi) = \frac{1}{2\pi}\log\frac{|\xi|\,|x-\xi^*|}{|x-\xi|}.$$

### Verification

1. **Singularity:** Near $x=\xi$, $G(x,\xi) = \Phi(x-\xi) + (\text{harmonic})$,
so $G$ has exactly the fundamental-solution singularity, giving $\Delta_x G = -\
delta(\cdot - \xi)$ in $B$.

2. **Harmonicity away from $\xi$:** Both $\Phi(x-\xi)$ (for $x\neq\xi$) and $\Ph
i(|\xi|(x-\xi^*))$ (for all $x \in B$, since $\xi^* \notin B$) are harmonic in $
x$; hence $G$ is harmonic in $B\setminus\{\xi\}$.

3. **Dirichlet boundary condition:** By $(\star)$, on $|x|=1$ we have $|\xi|\,|x
-\xi^*| = |x-\xi|$, so $\Phi(|\xi|(x-\xi^*)) = \Phi(x-\xi)$, giving $G(x,\xi)=0$
 on $\partial B$.

4. **Symmetry:** $G(x,\xi)=G(\xi,x)$ follows from the identity $|\xi|^{2-n}|x-\x
i^*|^{2-n} = |x|^{2-n}|\xi - x^*|^{2-n}$ (both equal $(|x|^2|\xi|^2 - 2x\cdot\xi
 + 1)^{(2-n)/2}$), confirming the self-adjointness required of a Green's functio
n.

All four defining properties of the Dirichlet Green's function are satisfied. $\
blacksquare$
