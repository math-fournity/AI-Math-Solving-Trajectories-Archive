## Analyzing $g(u) = f(u)$ for $u \geq 0$

Computing $g(u) = ||u-2|-1|$ piecewise:

- $u \in [0,1]$: $g = 1-u$ (slope $-1$)
- $u \in [1,2]$: $g = u-1$ (slope $+1$)
- $u \in [2,3]$: $g = 3-u$ (slope $-1$)
- $u \in [3,4]$: $g = u-3$ (slope $+1$)

This is a triangular wave on $[0,4]$ oscillating between $0$ and $1$. Since $g(u
)+g(v)=1$ requires both terms in $[0,1]$, we need $u,v \in [0,4]$.

## Symmetry reduction

The equation depends only on $|x|$ and $|y|$, giving 4-fold symmetry. I compute
the curve in the first quadrant ($u=x\geq 0$, $v=y\geq 0$) with $g(u)+g(v)=1$, t
hen multiply by 4.
