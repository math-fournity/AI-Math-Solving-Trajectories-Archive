Since ∠B = 130° and ∠ABK = 80°, we get ∠KBC = 50°.
Since ∠CBL = 80°, we get ∠ABL = 50°.

In △ABK: ∠AKB = 180° − 80° − α = **100° − α**.
In △CBL: ∠BLC = 180° − 80° − (50° − α) = **50° + α**.

### Step 2: Compute ∠AKA₁

) / (sin 50° + sin(50°−α)).

**Forming the ratio:**

$$\frac{AA_1}{AK} = \frac{2\sin 50°\cos(\alpha/2)\,\sin(100°-\alpha)}{\sin 80°\b
igl(\sin 50°+\sin(50°-\alpha)\bigr)}$$

Using the identities:
- sin 50° + sin(50°−α) = 2 sin(50°−α/2) cos(α/2)
- sin(100°−α) = 2 sin(50°−α/2) cos(50°−α/2)
- sin 50° = cos 40°, sin 80° = 2 sin 40° cos 40°

everything cancels to give:

$$\frac{AA_1}{AK} = \frac{\cos(50°-\alpha/2)}{\sin 40°}$$

**Solving for β.** We have:

$$\sin\beta\cdot\sin 40° = \cos(50°-\alpha/2)\cdot\sin(\alpha/2+\beta)$$

Expanding sin(α/2+β) and collecting:

$$\tan\beta = \frac{\cos(50°-\alpha/2)\,\sin(\alpha/2)}{\sin 40° - \cos(\alpha/2

For the **denominator**, using product-to-sum: cos(α/2)cos(50°−α/2) = ½[cos(α−50

$$\text{denom} = \tfrac{1}{2}[\cos 50° - \cos(\alpha-50°)] = -\sin(\alpha/2)\,\s
in(50°-\alpha/2)$$

Therefore:

$$\tan\beta = \frac{\cos(50°-\alpha/2)\,\sin(\alpha/2)}{-\sin(\alpha/2)\,\sin(50

$$\boxed{\angle AKA_1 = 140° - \alpha/2}$$
