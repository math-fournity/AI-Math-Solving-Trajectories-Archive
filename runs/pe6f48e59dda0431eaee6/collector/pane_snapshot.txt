## Proof

The polynomial $P(x) = x^4 + ax^3 + bx^2 + ax + 1$ is palindromic. Since $x = 0$
 is not a root, divide by $x^2$:

$$x^2 + ax + b + \frac{a}{x} + \frac{1}{x^2} = 0$$

$$\left(x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2}\right) + a\left(x + \frac{1}{x}\right) + b = 0$$

Setting $y = x + \frac{1}{x}$, so $x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2} = y^2 - 2$, we obtain:

$$y^2 + ay + (b-2) = 0 \quad (\star)$$

**Key reduction.** Each root $x$ of $P$ corresponds to a root $y = x + 1/x$ of $
(\star)$, and conversely each root $y_i$ of $(\star)$ yields roots of $P$ via $x
^2 - y_i x + 1 = 0$. This quadratic in $x$ has real roots if and only if its dis
criminant $y_i^2 - 4 \geq 0$, i.e., $|y_i| \geq 2$.

Therefore: **all roots of $P$ are real** $\iff$ $(\star)$ has two real roots $y_
1, y_2$ (counting multiplicity) with $|y_1| \geq 2$ and $|y_2| \geq 2$.

**Analyzing the constraint.** Let $y_1, y_2$ be the roots of $(\star)$, so $y_1
+ y_2 = -a$ and $y_1 y_2 = b - 2$.

There are three cases for both roots to lie in $(-\infty, -2] \cup [2, \infty)$:

- **Both $\geq 2$:** $y_1 y_2 \geq 4 \Rightarrow b \geq 6$, giving $b^2 \geq 36$
.
- **Both $\leq -2$:** $y_1 y_2 \geq 4 \Rightarrow b \geq 6$, giving $b^2 \geq 36
$.
- **One $\geq 2$, one $\leq -2$:** $y_1 y_2 \leq -4 \Rightarrow b - 2 \leq -4 \R
ightarrow b \leq -2$, giving $b^2 \geq 4$.

In every case, $a^2 + b^2 \geq b^2 \geq 4$.

**Achieving the bound.** Take $a = 0$, $b = -2$. Then $(\star)$ becomes $y^2 - 4
 = 0$, with roots $y_1 = 2, y_2 = -2$, both satisfying $|y_i| = 2$. The original
 polynomial is:

$$x^4 - 2x^2 + 1 = (x^2 - 1)^2 = 0$$

with roots $x = 1, 1, -1, -1$, all real. Here $a^2 + b^2 = 0 + 4 = 4$.

**Conclusion.** The minimum value of $a^2 + b^2$ is $\boxed{4}$.
