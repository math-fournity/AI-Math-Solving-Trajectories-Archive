## Solution

**Equation:** $u_{tt} = c^2 \nabla^2 u + g(\mathbf{x})e^{-i\omega t}$, with $u(\
mathbf{x},0)=0$, $u_t(\mathbf{x},0)=0$.

Rewrite as $u_{tt} - c^2\nabla^2 u = f(\mathbf{x},t)$ where $f = g(\mathbf{x})e^
{-i\omega t}$.

### Step 1. Retarded Green's function

The retarded Green's function $G_R$ satisfies
$$G_{tt} - c^2\nabla^2 G = \delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{x})\,\delta(t), \qquad G_R = 0 \
;\text{for}\; t<0.$$

Fourier transforming in time ($\hat G(\mathbf{x},\omega)=\int G\, e^{i\omega t}d
t$) gives the Helmholtz equation
$$\nabla^2 \hat G + \frac{\omega^2}{c^2}\hat G = -\frac{\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{x})
}{c^2},$$
whose outgoing solution (Sommerfeld radiation condition) is
$$\hat G(\mathbf{x},\omega) = \frac{e^{ik|\mathbf{x}|}}{4\pi c^2 |\mathbf{x}|},
\qquad k=\omega/c.$$

Inverse Fourier transforming with $\omega = ck$:
$$G_R(\mathbf{x},t) = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{e^{ikr}}{4\pi c
^2 r}\,e^{-ickt}\,c\,dk = \frac{1}{4\pi c r}\cdot\frac{1}{2\pi}\int e^{ik(r-ct)}
dk = \frac{\delta(r-ct)}{4\pi c\, r},$$
i.e.
$$\boxed{G_R(\mathbf{x},t) = \frac{\delta\!\left(t - |\mathbf{x}|/c\right)}{4\pi
 c^2\,|\mathbf{x}|}}.$$

### Step 2. Convolution with the source

With zero initial data, the solution is the retarded convolution (the source is
active for $t'\ge 0$):
$$u(\mathbf{x},t) = \int_0^{t}\!\int_{\mathbb{R}^3} G_R(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}',\
, t-t')\, g(\mathbf{x}')\,e^{-i\omega t'}\, d^3x'\, dt'.$$

Substituting $G_R$ and integrating over $t'$: the delta $\delta(t-t'-|\mathbf{x}
-\mathbf{x}'|/c)$ selects $t' = t - |\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}'|/c$, which is admiss
ible ($t'\ge 0$) only when $|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}'|\le ct$. This yields

$$\boxed{u(\mathbf{x},t) = \frac{1}{4\pi c^2}\int_{|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}'|\,\le
\, ct} \frac{g(\mathbf{x}')\,\exp\!\Big[-i\omega\!\left(t - \tfrac{|\mathbf{x}-\
mathbf{x}'|}{c}\right)\Big]}{|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}'|}\, d^3x'.}$$

Equivalently, factoring out the time dependence:
$$u(\mathbf{x},t) = \frac{e^{-i\omega t}}{4\pi c^2}\int_{|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}'
|\,\le\, ct} \frac{g(\mathbf{x}')\,e^{i(\omega/c)|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}'|}}{|\ma
thbf{x}-\mathbf{x}'|}\, d^3x'.$$

### Step 3. Verification of initial conditions

**At $t=0$:** the integration region $\{|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}'|\le 0\}$ collaps
es to a single point; the integrand $\sim 1/|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}'|$ is integra
ble in 3D (volume element $\sim r^2 dr$ vs. $1/r$), and the measure of a point i
s zero. Hence $u(\mathbf{x},0)=0$. ✓

**At $\partial_t u(\mathbf{x},0)$:** differentiating brings down a factor $(-i\o
mega)$ from the exponential and a boundary term from the moving surface $|\mathb
f{x}-\mathbf{x}'|=ct$. Both terms are proportional to integrals over the sphere
of radius $ct$; at $t=0$ this sphere has zero area, so $\partial_t u(\mathbf{x},
0)=0$. ✓

**PDE check:** Since $u$ is the convolution of the fundamental solution with the
 source, and $G_R$ satisfies $\Box G_R = \delta$, the convolution satisfies $\Bo
x u = f = g\,e^{-i\omega t}$, i.e. $u_{tt}=c^2\nabla^2 u + g\,e^{-i\omega t}$. ✓

### Step 4. Physical interpretation / long-time limit

As $t\to\infty$ (assuming $g$ decays sufficiently, e.g. compact support), the re
tarded ball engulfs all of $\mathbb{R}^3$ and the solution approaches the **stea
dy-state (particular) solution**
$$u_{\text{ss}}(\mathbf{x},t) = v(\mathbf{x})\,e^{-i\omega t}, \qquad v(\mathbf{
x}) = \frac{1}{4\pi c^2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^3}\frac{g(\mathbf{x}')\,e^{ik|\mathbf{x
}-\mathbf{x}'|}}{|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}'|}\,d^3x', \quad k=\omega/c,$$
where $v$ solves the **inhomogeneous Helmholtz equation** $\nabla^2 v + k^2 v =
-g/c^2$ with the **outgoing Sommerfeld radiation condition** $\lim_{r\to\infty}
r(\partial_r v - ikv)=0$. The transient (homogeneous part enforcing the zero ini
tial data) propagates away to infinity, leaving the radiating steady state.
