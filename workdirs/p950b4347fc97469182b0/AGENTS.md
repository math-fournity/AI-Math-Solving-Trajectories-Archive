# Problem

In a remote storage facility, an experimental energy array is arranged in a grid of 3 rows and 9 columns, forming 27 individual power cells. Each cell generates a steady output of energy, measured as a positive integer of "micro-joules."

The facility’s safety system identifies "Stabilizing Zones." A Stabilizing Zone is defined as any contiguous rectangular sub-grid of cells (ranging in size from a single $1 \times 1$ cell up to the full $3 \times 9$ array) where the total combined energy output of all cells within that rectangle is exactly divisible by 10.

A power cell is classified as "Critical" if it is isolated such that it does not belong to any Stabilizing Zone. That is, the cell is not part of any $m \times n$ rectangular block $(1 \le m \le 3, 1 \le n \le 9)$ whose sum of energy values is a multiple of 10.

Determine the maximum possible number of Critical cells that can exist within this 27-cell array.
