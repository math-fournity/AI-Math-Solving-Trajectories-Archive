## Solution

**Setting up coordinates.** Place $B=(0,0)$, $C=(10,0)$. From $AB=14$, $AC=12$:
$$A=\left(\tfrac{38}{5},\,\tfrac{24\sqrt6}{5}\right).$$

**The three interference zones** are circles with diameters equal to the sides,
so:
- Circle 1: center $M_a=(5,0)$, radius $R_1=5$ (diameter $BC$)
- Circle 2: center $M_b=\left(\tfrac{44}{5},\tfrac{12\sqrt6}{5}\right)$, radius
$R_2=6$ (diameter $CA$)
- Circle 3: center $M_c=\left(\tfrac{19}{5},\tfrac{12\sqrt6}{5}\right)$, radius
$R_3=7$ (diameter $AB$)

**Key observation.** The three centers form the medial triangle with sides:
$$|M_bM_c|=5,\quad |M_aM_c|=6,\quad |M_aM_b|=7.$$
Remarkably, the radius at each center equals the opposite side of the medial tri
angle.

**Apollonius problem.** The buoy $\Omega$ with center $O$ and radius $r$ is inte
rnally tangent to all three circles, so:
$$|OM_a|=5-r,\quad |OM_b|=6-r,\quad |OM_c|=7-r.$$

Set $x=5-r$, so the distances are $x,\;x+1,\;x+2$ to $M_a,M_b,M_c$ respectively.

**Coordinate computation on the medial triangle.** Place $M_a=(0,0)$, $M_b=(7,0)
$, $M_c=\left(\tfrac{30}{7},\tfrac{12\sqrt6}{7}\right)$. From $|OM_a|^2=x^2$ and
 $|OM_b|^2=(x+1)^2$:
$$h=\frac{24-x}{7}.$$
From $|OM_c|^2=(x+2)^2$ and $|OM_a|^2=x^2$:
$$k=\frac{16-17x}{21\sqrt6}.$$

**Substituting into $h^2+k^2=x^2$:**
$$\frac{(24-x)^2}{49}+\frac{(16-17x)^2}{2646}=x^2.$$

Multiplying by $2646=49\cdot 54$:
$$54(24-x)^2+(16-17x)^2=2646\,x^2.$$
Expanding: $31104-2592x+54x^2+256-544x+289x^2=2646x^2$, giving:
$$2303\,x^2+3136\,x-31360=0.$$

**Solving.** The discriminant is:
$$3136^2+4\cdot2303\cdot31360=9{,}834{,}496+288{,}888{,}320=298{,}722{,}816=7056
^2\cdot 6.$$

Taking the positive root:
$$x=\frac{-3136+7056\sqrt6}{4606}=\frac{8(9\sqrt6-4)}{47}=\frac{72\sqrt6-32}{47}
.$$

**Computing $r$:**
$$r=5-x=5-\frac{72\sqrt6-32}{47}=\frac{235-72\sqrt6+32}{47}=\frac{267-72\sqrt6}{
47}.$$

**Verification.** Numerically $r\approx 1.929$ km, $x\approx 3.071$. The center
$O\approx(7.215,\,2.130)$ lies inside the original triangle and inside all three
 disks (confirmed: distances to centers are $\approx 3.071,\;4.071,\;5.071$). Al
l three tangency points were verified to lie on the correct boundary arcs (each
is inside the other two disks). ✓

**Extracting the form $\frac{m-p\sqrt{q}}{n}$:**
$$m=267,\quad p=72,\quad q=6,\quad n=47.$$

- $q=6=2\cdot 3$ is square-free ✓
- $\gcd(267,72,47)$: since $47$ is prime and divides neither $267=3\cdot89$ nor
$72=2^3\cdot3^2$, the gcd is $1$ ✓

**Final answer:**
$$m+n+p+q=267+47+72+6=\boxed{392}.$$
