## Proof

We show that the number 2008 cannot be produced by either of the two allowed ope
rations starting from any natural number. Therefore, if 2008 ever appears on the
 blackboard, it must have been the initial number.

**Setup.** A natural number is written on the blackboard. From any number $x$ al
ready present, one may write either $2x+1$ or $\frac{x}{x+2}$.

Suppose 2008 appears at some moment. Then either:

1. **2008 was the initial number** (and we are done), or
2. **2008 was produced by one of the two operations** from some number $x$ alrea
dy on the board.

We rule out case 2 by considering each operation.

---

**Operation 1: $2x + 1 = 2008$.** This gives $x = \frac{2007}{2}$, which is not
an integer, hence not a natural number. So 2008 cannot arise from this operation
.

(Indeed, $2x+1$ is always odd, while 2008 is even.)

---

**Operation 2: $\frac{x}{x+2} = 2008$.** For any natural number $x \geq 1$,
$$\frac{x}{x+2} = 1 - \frac{2}{x+2} < 1,$$
so the result of this operation is always a positive number strictly less than $
1$. In particular, it can never equal $2008$.

(Equivalently, solving $x = 2008(x+2)$ gives $-2007x = 4016$, i.e., $x = -\frac{
4016}{2007}$, which is negative — not a natural number.)

---

**Conclusion.** Neither operation can produce 2008 from a natural number. The on
ly remaining possibility is that 2008 was the number written on the blackboard f
rom the very beginning. $\blacksquare$
