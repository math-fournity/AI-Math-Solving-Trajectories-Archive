 $E \neq 0$, such that $EFGH = AB \times CD$ where $AB, CD$ are 2-digit numbers

**Verification of $1058 = 23 \times 46$:**

$$23 \times 46 = 23 \times 40 + 23 \times 6 = 920 + 138 = 1058. \checkmark$$

- $A=2, B=3, C=4, D=6, E=1, F=0, G=5, H=8$
- All eight digits $\{2,3,4,6,1,0,5,8\}$ are distinct. $\checkmark$
- Leading digits: $A=2\neq0$, $C=4\neq0$, $E=1\neq0$. $\checkmark$
- $AB=23$ and $CD=46$ are both 2-digit numbers. $\checkmark$
- $EFGH=1058$ is a 4-digit number. $\checkmark$

Since every 4-digit number with distinct digits smaller than $1058$ fails to sat
isfy all constraints (as verified above), $1058$ is the smallest possible value.
