# Problem

Trevor and Edward play a game where they take turns adding or removing beans from a pile. On each turn, a player must either add or remove the largest perfect square number of beans $s$ such that $s \le n$, where $n$ is the current number of beans in the heap. The player who empties the pile wins (by moving to 0).

Suppose Edward offers to let Trevor go first with a pile of $n$ beans. Assuming both players are perfect logicians, a position $n$ is a losing position if every possible move from $n$ leads to a winning position for the opponent, and it is a winning position if there is at least one move that leads to a losing position for the opponent. Given that Edward wins the game even though Trevor goes first, $n$ must be a losing position. Find the largest integer $n < 1000$ that is a losing position.
