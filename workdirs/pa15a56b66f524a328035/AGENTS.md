# Problem

In the coastal city of Aeris, a grid-based logistics yard is organized into a rectangular layout consisting of 2011 horizontal storage lanes and 2012 vertical access paths. Each intersection in this grid forms a designated cargo slot. 

The yard supervisor must fill every single slot with one of three types of shipping containers: 
- A "Type 0" container, which has a weight of 0 tons.
- A "Type 1" container, which has a weight of 1 ton.
- A "Type 2" container, which has a weight of 2 tons.

To ensure the structural integrity of the yard's foundation, the supervisor must follow a strict safety regulation: the total weight of the containers in every single horizontal lane must be exactly divisible by 3. Similarly, the total weight of the containers in every single vertical access path must also be exactly divisible by 3.

Under these weight distribution constraints, what is the maximum possible number of Type 1 containers that can be stored in the yard?
