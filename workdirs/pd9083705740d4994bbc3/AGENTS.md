# Problem

In the city of Arithmos, a massive 8x8 square plaza is being paved with decorative stone tiles. The plaza is laid out in a grid with 8 rows and 8 columns of tiles.

The construction follows a strict cultural tradition:
1. Every tile located in the very first northernmost row and every tile in the very first westernmost column must be engraved with exactly 1 sun symbol.
2. For any other tile located at the intersection of row $i$ and column $j$, the number of sun symbols engraved on it must be equal to the total sum of all sun symbols found in the entire rectangular section of tiles that are strictly to the north and strictly to the west of that tile (specifically, all tiles in rows $1$ through $i-1$ and columns $1$ through $j-1$).

Following these traditional rules for all 64 tiles in the 8x8 grid, how many sun symbols are engraved on the tile located in the 8th row and the 8th column?
