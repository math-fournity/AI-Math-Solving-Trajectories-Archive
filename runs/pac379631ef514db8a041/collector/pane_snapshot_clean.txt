$$\int_U |D^\alpha u|^p\,dx = \lim_{j \to \infty} \int_{U_j} |D^\alpha u|^p\,dx

Thus $D^\alpha u \in L^p(U)$ for every $|\alpha| \leq k$, so $u \in W^{k,p}(U)$.
