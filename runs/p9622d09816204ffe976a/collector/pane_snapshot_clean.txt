## Setup

In an additive category $\mathscr{C}$, every finite family of objects admits a *
*biproduct**: an object $A\oplus A$ that is simultaneously a product and a copro
duct, with projections $p_1,p_2:A\oplus A\to A$, injections $i_1,i_2:A\to A\oplu
s A$, satisfying the biproduct identities
$$p_k\circ i_l=\delta_{kl}\,\mathrm{id}_A,\qquad i_1\circ p_1+i_2\circ p_2=\math
rm{id}_{A\oplus A}.$$

**Diagonal.** $\Delta_A:A\to A\oplus A$ is the unique morphism with $p_1\circ\De
lta_A=\mathrm{id}_A$ and $p_2\circ\Delta_A=\mathrm{id}_A$; explicitly $\Delta_A=
i_1+i_2$ (using the additive structure on $\mathrm{Hom}(A,A\oplus A)$).

**Codiagonal.** $\nabla_B:B\oplus B\to B$ is the unique morphism with $\nabla_B\
circ i_1=\mathrm{id}_B$ and $\nabla_B\circ i_2=\mathrm{id}_B$; explicitly $\nabl
a_B=p_1+p_2$.

**Morphism between biproducts.** For $f,g:A\to B$, the morphism $f\oplus g:A\opl
us A\to B\oplus B$ is the unique morphism with
$$p'_1\circ(f\oplus g)=f\circ p_1,\qquad p'_2\circ(f\oplus g)=g\circ p_2,$$
equivalently $(f\oplus g)=i'_1\circ f\circ p_1+i'_2\circ g\circ p_2$.

## The formula

$$\boxed{\,f+g \;=\; \nabla_B\circ(f\oplus g)\circ\Delta_A\,}$$

## Verification

Compute the composite $\nabla_B\circ(f\oplus g)\circ\Delta_A:A\to B$. Using the
defining identities of the three factors:

$$\nabla_B\circ(f\oplus g)\circ\Delta_A \;=\; \nabla_B\circ\bigl(i'_1\circ f\cir
c p_1+i'_2\circ g\circ p_2\bigr)\circ\Delta_A.$$

Distributing over the biproduct identity and using bilinearity of composition in
 an additive category:

$$=\;\nabla_B\circ i'_1\circ f\circ(p_1\circ\Delta_A)\;+\;\nabla_B\circ i'_2\cir
c g\circ(p_2\circ\Delta_A).$$

Now apply $p_1\circ\Delta_A=\mathrm{id}_A$, $p_2\circ\Delta_A=\mathrm{id}_A$, $\
nabla_B\circ i'_1=\mathrm{id}_B$, $\nabla_B\circ i'_2=\mathrm{id}_B$:

$$=\;\mathrm{id}_B\circ f\circ\mathrm{id}_A\;+\;\mathrm{id}_B\circ g\circ\mathrm
{id}_A \;=\; f+g.$$

Hence $\nabla_B\circ(f\oplus g)\circ\Delta_A=f+g$, as required.

**Remark.** This is the canonical way the additive structure on $\mathrm{Hom}$-s
ets is recovered from the biproduct structure alone: addition is the composite "
diagonal, then apply $f$ and $g$ componentwise, then codiagonal." Conversely, on
e can *define* $f+g$ by this formula and verify it gives $\mathscr{C}$ the struc
ture of an $\mathbf{Ab}$-enriched category, showing that "having biproducts" and
 "being additive" are equivalent for a preadditive category.
