## Setup

**The "obvious" characteristic subgroups** (always characteristic, as they're de

- $p^3 G = \{0\}$

These are **6 distinct** characteristic subgroups.

## Step 1: The Automorphism Group

Every automorphism $\varphi$ of $G$ must satisfy $\varphi(a) \in G[p]$ (since $|

$$\varphi(a) = \alpha\, a + \gamma\, p^2 b, \quad \alpha \in \mathbb{F}_p,\ \gam
ma \in \mathbb{F}_p.$$
And $\varphi(b)$ must have order $p^3$, so:
$$\varphi(b) = \delta\, a + \varepsilon\, b, \quad \delta \in \mathbb{F}_p,\ \va
repsilon \in (\mathbb{Z}/p^3)^\times.$$
The map is invertible iff the induced map on $G/pG \cong \mathbb{F}_p^2$ is inve

0$ is automatic since $\varepsilon$ is a unit). So:
$$\operatorname{Aut}(G) = \{\varphi_{\alpha,\gamma,\delta,\varepsilon} : \alpha
\in \mathbb{F}_p^\times,\ \gamma \in \mathbb{F}_p,\ \delta \in \mathbb{F}_p,\ \v
arepsilon \in (\mathbb{Z}/p^3)^\times\}.$$

## Step 3: Action on $G[p] \cong \mathbb{F}_p^2$ (order-$p$ subgroups)

**For odd $p$:** Every subgroup has been checked. The characteristic subgroups a
