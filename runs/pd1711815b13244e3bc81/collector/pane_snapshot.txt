## Proof

Let the set of drawn chords be $\mathcal{C}$ with $|\mathcal{C}| = m$. Let the p
oints on circle $\omega$ that serve as endpoints of drawn chords be $P_1, P_2, \
ldots, P_n$ in cyclic order. For each point $P_i$, let $d_i$ denote the number o
f drawn chords incident to $P_i$. By hypothesis, $d_i \geq d = 20$ for all $i$.

**Claim:** Every drawn chord intersects at least $2d - 2 = 38$ other drawn chord
s.

**Proof of Claim.** Let $e = P_iP_j$ be any drawn chord. Since $P_i$ is an endpo
int of at least $d$ drawn chords, there are at least $d - 1$ other drawn chords
(besides $e$) incident to $P_i$. Each of these chords shares the endpoint $P_i$
with $e$, so by the convention that chords with a common endpoint intersect, eac
h of these $d - 1$ chords intersects $e$.

Similarly, $P_j$ is an endpoint of at least $d$ drawn chords, so there are at le
ast $d - 1$ other drawn chords incident to $P_j$, each intersecting $e$ by the s
ame convention.

These two sets of chords are **disjoint**: a chord incident to both $P_i$ and $P
_j$ must be $e$ itself, which is excluded from both sets.

Therefore, chord $e$ intersects at least
$$(d - 1) + (d - 1) = 2d - 2 = 38$$
other drawn chords, accounting only for shared-endpoint intersections. Any addit
ional crossing chords (whose endpoints lie on opposite arcs determined by $e$) o
nly increase this count.

Since this lower bound holds for **every** drawn chord, in particular there exis
ts a chord that intersects at least $2d - 2$ other drawn chords. This is the sta
ndard lower bound for this problem.

$$N = 2d - 2 = 2 \cdot 20 - 2 = \boxed{38}.$$

### PROOF COMPLETE
