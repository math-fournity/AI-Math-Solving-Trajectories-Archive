**Claim:** The series $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \sin z^n$ is uniformly absolutely conve

form absolute convergence on $\overline{D(0,r)}$ for every $r<1$.

Fix $r<1$. For any complex $w$, using the power series $\sin w=\sum_{k=0}^\infty
 \frac{(-1)^k}{(2k+1)!}w^{2k+1}$,

converges (geometric series with ratio $r<1$). The bound $e\,r^n$ is independent
