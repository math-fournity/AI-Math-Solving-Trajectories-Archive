## Proof

**Setup.** In a round-robin tournament with $n$ teams, each pair plays once. Eac
h game distributes 2 points total (2–0 for a win, 1–1 for a tie). So the total p
oints across all teams is $2\binom{n}{2} = n(n-1)$. Let the teams be ranked by s
core $s_1 > s_2 > \cdots > s_n$, all distinct. The last team $T_n$ defeated the
first three teams $T_1, T_2, T_3$.

### Lower Bound: $n \geq 13$

Since $T_n$ beat $T_1, T_2, T_3$, team $T_n$ earns at least $3 \times 2 = 6$ poi
nts, so $s_n \geq 6$.

The $n$ scores are distinct non-negative integers with minimum $s_n$, so they ar
e at least
$$s_n,\; s_n+1,\; s_n+2,\; \ldots,\; s_n+(n-1),$$
giving a total of at least $n \cdot s_n + \frac{n(n-1)}{2}$.

Since the total equals $n(n-1)$:
$$n \cdot s_n + \frac{n(n-1)}{2} \leq n(n-1) \implies s_n \leq \frac{n-1}{2}.$$

Combining with $s_n \geq 6$:
$$6 \leq \frac{n-1}{2} \implies n \geq 13.$$
