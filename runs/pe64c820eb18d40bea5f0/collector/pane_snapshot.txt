## Solution

**Setup and key identifications.**

Since $f(x) \in \mathbb{Q}[x]$ is irreducible with $\deg f = 8$ and $f(\alpha)=0
$, the minimal polynomial of $\alpha$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ is $f$, so:
$$[\mathbb{Q}(\alpha):\mathbb{Q}] = 8.$$

$K$ is the splitting field of $f$ over $\mathbb{Q}$, so $K/\mathbb{Q}$ is Galois
 with $G = \mathrm{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q})$ abelian.

**The stabilizer $H$.**

$H = \{\sigma \in G : \sigma(\alpha) = \alpha\}$ is the stabilizer (isotropy sub
group) of $\alpha$ in $G$. It is given that $|H| = 4$.

**Orbit–stabilizer computation.**

Since $f$ is irreducible over $\mathbb{Q}$, the Galois group $G$ acts **transiti
vely** on the 8 roots of $f$. Therefore the orbit of $\alpha$ under $G$ is the f
ull set of roots of $f$, which has size 8:
$$|\mathrm{Orb}_G(\alpha)| = 8.$$

By the orbit–stabilizer theorem:
$$|G| = |\mathrm{Orb}_G(\alpha)| \cdot |\mathrm{Stab}_G(\alpha)| = 8 \times 4 =
32.$$

Since $K/\mathbb{Q}$ is Galois, $[K:\mathbb{Q}] = |G| = 32$.

**Consistency check via $F$ and the abelian hypothesis.**

Since $H$ is a subgroup containing the identity, the set
$$F = \{x \in K : \sigma_1(x) = \sigma_2(x) = \sigma_3(x) = \sigma_4(x)\}$$
is exactly the fixed field $K^H$ (taking $\sigma_1 = \mathrm{id}$, the condition
 $\sigma_i(x)=\sigma_1(x)=x$ for all $i$ means $x$ is fixed by every element of
$H$).

By the Galois correspondence:
$$[F:\mathbb{Q}] = [G:H] = 8, \qquad [K:F] = |H| = 4.$$

Since $H$ fixes $\alpha$, we have $\mathbb{Q}(\alpha) \subseteq F$, and $[\mathb
b{Q}(\alpha):\mathbb{Q}]=8=[F:\mathbb{Q}]$, so $F = \mathbb{Q}(\alpha)$.

The hypothesis that $G$ is **abelian** guarantees $H \trianglelefteq G$, so $F/\
mathbb{Q}$ is Galois with $\mathrm{Gal}(F/\mathbb{Q}) \cong G/H$ of order 8. Thi
s is consistent with $F = \mathbb{Q}(\alpha)$ being a Galois (indeed abelian) ex
tension of $\mathbb{Q}$ — confirming the entire setup is self-consistent.

**Conclusion.**

$$[K:\mathbb{Q}] = |G| = |\mathrm{Orb}_G(\alpha)|\cdot|H| = 8 \times 4 = \boxed{
32}.$$

### PROOF COMPLETE
