## Solution

We solve the PDE by the **method of characteristics**.

### Characteristic equations

For the operator $\partial_t + d(z-1)\partial_z$, the characteristics satisfy

$$\frac{dt}{ds}=1,\qquad \frac{dz}{ds}=d(z-1).$$

Taking $t$ as the parameter (so $s=t$ up to a shift), the $z$-equation is

$$\frac{dz}{dt}=d(z-1)\implies z(t)-1=\bigl(z(0)-1\bigr)e^{dt}.$$

Label the footpoint at $t=0$ by $z_0:=z(0)$. Then along the characteristic

$$z_0 = 1+(z-1)e^{-dt}.$$

### Transport equation for $G$

Along a characteristic,

$$\frac{dG}{dt}=\partial_t G + \frac{dz}{dt}\,\partial_z G = \partial_t G + d(z-
1)\partial_z G = b(z-1)G.$$

Hence

$$\frac{dG}{G}=b(z-1)\,dt=b\,(z_0-1)e^{dt}\,dt.$$

Integrating from $0$ to $t$ and using the initial value $G(z_0,0)=z_0^{N_0}$,

$$\ln\frac{G(z,t)}{z_0^{N_0}}=\frac{b}{d}(z_0-1)\bigl(e^{dt}-1\bigr).$$

### Eliminate $z_0$

Using $z_0-1=(z-1)e^{-dt}$,

$$\frac{b}{d}(z_0-1)(e^{dt}-1)=\frac{b}{d}(z-1)e^{-dt}(e^{dt}-1)=\frac{b}{d}(z-1
)\bigl(1-e^{-dt}\bigr).$$

Therefore

$$\boxed{\,G(z,t)=\Bigl[1+(z-1)e^{-dt}\Bigr]^{N_0}\exp\!\left[\frac{b}{d}(z-1)\b
igl(1-e^{-dt}\bigr)\right]\,}.$$

### Verification of boundary conditions

- **Initial condition** ($t=0$): $e^{-d\cdot 0}=1$, so $z_0=z$ and the exponenti
al factor is $\exp[0]=1$. Thus $G(z,0)=z^{N_0}$. ✓
- **Boundary** ($z=1$): $z_0=1$, so the power factor is $1^{N_0}=1$ and the expo
nential factor is $\exp[0]=1$. Thus $G(1,t)=1$. ✓
- **PDE**: Direct substitution confirms $\partial_t G + d(z-1)\partial_z G = b(z
-1)G$, since both sides equal $b(z-1)G$ by construction of the characteristic fl
ow. ✓
