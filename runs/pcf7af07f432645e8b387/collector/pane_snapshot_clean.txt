## Solution

**Setup.** Place the isosceles triangle symmetrically: $B=(-1,0)$, $C=(1,0)$, $A
=(0,t)$ with $t>0$. Then $BC=2$, $AB=AC=\sqrt{1+t^2}$, and the target ratio is $
\frac{AB}{BC}=\frac{\sqrt{1+t^2}}{2}$.

**Incircle data.** Let $u=\sqrt{1+t^2}$. The semiperimeter is $s=1+u$, the inrad
ius is $r=\frac{t}{1+u}$, and $O=(0,r)$. Tangent lengths: $BK=BM=CL=1$, $AM=AL=u
-1$, giving
$$K=(0,0),\quad M=\!\left(-1+\tfrac{1}{u},\,\tfrac{t}{u}\right),\quad L=\!\left(
1-\tfrac{1}{u},\,\tfrac{t}{u}\right).$$

**Finding $N=OL\cap KM$.** Parametrizing $OL$ and $KM$ and solving yields
$$N=\left(-\frac{u-1}{u+2},\;\frac{t}{u+2}\right).$$

**Finding $Q=BN\cap CA$.** The line $BN$ has direction $(3,t)$ (after scaling),
and $CA$ has direction $(-1,t)$. Solving $(-1+3s,\,ts)=(1-v,\,tv)$ gives $s=\tfr
ac12$, so
$$Q=\left(\tfrac12,\,\tfrac{t}{2}\right),$$
the **midpoint of $CA$** — independent of $u$.

**Foot $P$ of perpendicular from $A$ to $BQ$.** Line $BQ$: $(-1,0)+s(3,t)$. Proj
ecting $A=(0,t)$:
$$s_P=\frac{(1,t)\cdot(3,t)}{(3,t)\cdot(3,t)}=\frac{3+t^2}{9+t^2},\qquad P=\left
(\frac{2t^2}{9+t^2},\;\frac{t(3+t^2)}{9+t^2}\right).$$

**Distances.** With $Q$ at $s_Q=\tfrac12$:
$$BP=\frac{3+t^2}{\sqrt{9+t^2}},\qquad AP=\frac{2t}{\sqrt{9+t^2}},\qquad PQ=\fra
c{|3-t^2|}{2\sqrt{9+t^2}}.$$

**Applying $BP=AP+2\,PQ$.** Multiplying through by $\sqrt{9+t^2}$:
$$3+t^2=2t+|3-t^2|.$$

- **Case $t^2\le 3$:** $3+t^2=2t+3-t^2\;\Rightarrow\;2t^2=2t\;\Rightarrow\;t=1$
(since $t>0$). Check: $1\le 3$ ✓.
- **Case $t^2>3$:** $3+t^2=2t+t^2-3\;\Rightarrow\;t=3$. Check: $9>3$ ✓.

**Computing the ratios.**
- $t=1$: $\frac{AB}{BC}=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$, square $=\frac{1}{2}$.
- $t=3$: $\frac{AB}{BC}=\frac{\sqrt{10}}{2}$, square $=\frac{5}{2}$.

**Sum of squares:**
$$\frac{1}{2}+\frac{5}{2}=3.$$
