**Step 3: The sum $S(n)/n^2 \to 1$.**

Write $S(n) = \sum_{i=1}^n x_i = \sum_{i=1}^n 2i + R(n) = n(n+1) + R(n)$, where
$R(n) = \sum_{i=1}^n (x_i - 2i)$.

For any $\delta > 0$, there exists $I$ such that $|x_i - 2i| \leq \delta\, i$ fo

$$|R(n)| \;\leq\; \underbrace{\sum_{i=1}^{I-1}|x_i - 2i|}_{=\,C\text{ (constant)

$$\frac{|R(n)|}{n^2} \;\leq\; \frac{C}{n^2} + \frac{\delta}{2}\cdot\frac{n+1}{n}

$$\frac{S(n)}{n^2} = \frac{n(n+1)}{n^2} + \frac{R(n)}{n^2} \;\longrightarrow\; 1

$$\boxed{\lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n x_i}{n^2} = 1.}$$
