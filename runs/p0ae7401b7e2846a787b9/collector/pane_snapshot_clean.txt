Since all heights are distinct, every $s_i$ is well defined.

**Key observation.** Three successive dwarfs $\pi_i,\pi_{i+1},\pi_{i+2}$ are inc

$$\text{either } +-+-+-+ \text{ or } -+-+-+.$$
