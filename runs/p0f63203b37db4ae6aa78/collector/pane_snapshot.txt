## Proof

We wish to compute
$$E\!\left[\int_0^1 W(s)\,ds \cdot \int_0^1 t\,W(t)\,dt\right].$$

**Step 1: Apply Fubini.** Both integrands are Gaussian (hence square-integrable)
, so we may interchange expectation and integration:

$$E\!\left[\int_0^1 W(s)\,ds \cdot \int_0^1 t\,W(t)\,dt\right] = \int_0^1\!\!\in
t_0^1 t\,E\!\left[W(s)W(t)\right]\,ds\,dt.$$

**Step 2: Use the covariance of the Wiener process.** For a standard Wiener proc
ess,
$$E[W(s)W(t)] = \min(s,t).$$

Hence the quantity becomes
$$I = \int_0^1\!\!\int_0^1 t\,\min(s,t)\,ds\,dt.$$

**Step 3: Evaluate the inner integral by splitting at $s=t$.** For fixed $t\in[0
,1]$,

$$\int_0^1 t\,\min(s,t)\,ds = t\!\left[\int_0^t s\,ds + \int_t^1 t\,ds\right] =
t\!\left[\frac{t^2}{2} + t(1-t)\right] = t\!\left[\frac{t^2}{2} + t - t^2\right]
 = t\!\left[t - \frac{t^2}{2}\right] = t^2 - \frac{t^3}{2}.$$

**Step 4: Integrate over $t$.**

$$I = \int_0^1 \!\left(t^2 - \frac{t^3}{2}\right)dt = \left[\frac{t^3}{3} - \fra
c{t^4}{8}\right]_0^1 = \frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{8} = \frac{8}{24} - \frac{3}{24} =
 \frac{5}{24}.$$

**Result:**

$$E\!\left(\int_0^1 W(s)\,ds \cdot \int_0^1 t\,W(t)\,dt\right) = \boxed{\dfrac{5
}{24}}.$$
