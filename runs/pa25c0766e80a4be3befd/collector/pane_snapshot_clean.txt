## Proof

A cube has **8 vertices** and **12 edges**.

At each vertex of a cube, exactly **3 edges** meet, and these three edges are mu
tually perpendicular (one along each of the three coordinate directions). Every
pair of these three edges forms a right angle.

The number of right angles at a single vertex equals the number of ways to choos
e 2 edges from the 3 meeting edges:

$$\binom{3}{2} = 3$$

Since the cube has 8 vertices, and at each vertex there are 3 right angles:

$$8 \times 3 = 24$$

No other pair of edges forms a right angle: edges that share a vertex are alread
y counted, parallel edges do not meet (so form no angle), and skew edges do not
intersect (so form no angle in the standard geometric sense).

Therefore, the total number of right angles formed by the edges of a cube is **2
4**.
