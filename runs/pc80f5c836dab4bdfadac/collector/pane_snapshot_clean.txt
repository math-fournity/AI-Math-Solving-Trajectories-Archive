## Classification of Closed Subgroups of ℝ²

**Theorem.** Every closed subgroup H of ℝ² is exactly one of the following six t
ypes:

1. **H = ℝ²** (the whole space).
2. **H = ℝ·v** for some nonzero v ∈ ℝ² (a line through the origin).
3. **H = ℤ·v + ℝ·w** for linearly independent v, w ∈ ℝ² (a union of equally-spac
ed parallel lines).
4. **H = ℤ·v₁ + ℤ·v₂** for linearly independent v₁, v₂ ∈ ℝ² (a lattice).
5. **H = ℤ·v** for some nonzero v ∈ ℝ² (a cyclic discrete subgroup).
6. **H = {0}** (the trivial subgroup).
