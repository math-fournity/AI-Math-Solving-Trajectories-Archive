Looking at this problem, I need to find the maximum number of Regional Hubs *k*
such that one hub *H* can be strictly highest on the Regional Leaderboard while
being strictly lowest on the Global Leaderboard.

## Setup and Formalization

There are 20 branches; each pair collaborates once with weight *w* ∈ {0,1,2} (bo
th endpoints receive that score).

- **Global score:** *G(v)* = Σ over all 19 edges incident to *v*.
- **Regional score:** *R(h)* = Σ over edges where both endpoints are hubs.

We need a hub *H\** with: (1) *R(H\*)* > *R(h)* for all other hubs *h*, and (2)
*G(H\*)* < *G(v)* for all *v* ≠ *H\**.

## k = 20 is Impossible

If all 20 branches are hubs, then *G(v)* = *R(v)* for every branch. We would nee
d *R(H\*)* > *R(h)* and *R(H\*)* < *R(h)* simultaneously — a contradiction. So *
*k ≤ 19**.

## Construction for k = 19

Take 19 hubs and 1 non-hub. Let *H\** be the special hub.

**Edge weights:**
- *w(H\**, non-hub) = 0 (keeps *H\** low globally).
- *w(h, non-hub) = 2* for all 18 other hubs (boosts them and the non-hub).
- Among hubs: set *R(H\*)* = 34 and *R(h)* = 33 for every other hub.

**Achieving the regional scores:**
- *R(H\*)* = 34 via 16 edges of weight 2 and 2 edges of weight 1 (16·2 + 2·1 = 3
4).
- For the 16 hubs with *w(H\**,h) = 2: need Σ_{h'≠h,H\**} *w(h,h')* = 31, i.e.,
deficiency 3 (from max 34).
- For the 2 hubs with *w(H\**,h) = 1: need Σ = 32, i.e., deficiency 2.

**Deficiency realization:** Take a 3-regular graph on the 18 non-*H\** hubs (exi
sts since 18 is even and ≥ 4; e.g., a circulant graph). Assign penalty 1 (weight
 1) to its edges, penalty 0 (weight 2) to all others. Every vertex has deficienc
y 3. Remove one edge between the two special vertices — their deficiency drops t
o 2, all others remain 3. This is feasible since a 3-regular graph on 18 vertice
s can be chosen to contain any specified edge.

**Verification of all scores:**
- *G(H\*)* = 0 + 34 = **34**
- *G(h)* = 2 + 33 = **35** for all 18 other hubs
- *G(non-hub)* = 0 + 18·2 = **36**

**All constraints satisfied:**
- Regional: *R(H\*)* = 34 > 33 = *R(h)* ✓
- Global vs other hubs: 34 < 35 ✓
- Global vs non-hub: 34 < 36 ✓

## Conclusion

Since k = 20 is impossible and k = 19 is achievable, the maximum value of *k* is
 **19**.
