## Solution

**Setup.** Place the shared side (length $b$) horizontally, centered at the orig
in, on the line $y=0$. The rectangle extends downward to $y=-h$ with corners
$$\left(-\tfrac{b}{2},0\right),\;\left(\tfrac{b}{2},0\right),\;\left(\tfrac{b}{2
},-h\right),\;\left(-\tfrac{b}{2},-h\right).$$
The isosceles triangle extends upward with apex $(0,t)$, where $t$ is the triang
le's height. All five points lie on the circle of radius $1$.

**Finding the circle's center.** By symmetry the center is $(0,c)$. Equating squ
ared distances from $(b/2,0)$ and $(b/2,-h)$:
$$\tfrac{b^2}{4}+c^2=\tfrac{b^2}{4}+(h+c)^2 \;\Longrightarrow\; c^2=h^2+2hc+c^2
\;\Longrightarrow\; c=-\tfrac{h}{2}.$$
So the center is the midpoint of the rectangle's height, $(0,-h/2)$.

**Relating $b$ and $h$.** From $(b/2,0)$ on the unit circle:
$$\tfrac{b^2}{4}+\tfrac{h^2}{4}=1 \;\Longrightarrow\; b^2+h^2=4. \tag{1}$$

**Triangle height.** The apex $(0,t)$ satisfies $(t-c)^2=1$, so $t=c+1=1-\tfrac{
h}{2}$ (taking the solution above the rectangle).

**Equal-area condition.**
$$\text{Area}_{\text{rect}}=bh,\qquad \text{Area}_{\text{tri}}=\tfrac{1}{2}\,b\,
t=\tfrac{b}{2}\!\left(1-\tfrac{h}{2}\right).$$
Setting them equal and dividing by $b\neq 0$:
$$h=\tfrac{1}{2}\!\left(1-\tfrac{h}{2}\right)=\tfrac{1}{2}-\tfrac{h}{4} \;\Longr
ightarrow\; \tfrac{5h}{4}=\tfrac{1}{2} \;\Longrightarrow\; h=\boxed{\tfrac{2}{5}
}.$$

**Verification.** With $h=\tfrac{2}{5}$: $b^2=4-\tfrac{4}{25}=\tfrac{96}{25}$, s
o $b=\tfrac{4\sqrt6}{5}$; triangle height $t=1-\tfrac{1}{5}=\tfrac{4}{5}$. Cente
r $(0,-\tfrac{1}{5})$, radius $1$:
- $(b/2,0)$: $\tfrac{24}{25}+\tfrac{1}{25}=1$ ✓
- $(b/2,-\tfrac{2}{5})$: $\tfrac{24}{25}+\tfrac{1}{25}=1$ ✓
- $(0,\tfrac{4}{5})$: $(\tfrac{4}{5}+\tfrac{1}{5})^2=1$ ✓
