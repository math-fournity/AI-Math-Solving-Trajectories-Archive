## Proof

Let $P=P(z)$ and $H=H(z)$ be the OGFs of pyramids and half-pyramids, with $z$ ma
rking each atomic cell. The specifications are

$$
P = H + PH, \qquad H = z + zH + zH^{2}. \tag{1}
$$

### Step 1. Express $P$ in terms of $H$

From the first equation,
$$
P = H + PH = H(1+P) \;\Longrightarrow\; P(1-H)=H \;\Longrightarrow\; \boxed{\,P=
\dfrac{H}{1-H}\,}. \tag{2}
$$
So once $H$ is known, $P$ follows by substitution.

### Step 2. Solve the quadratic for $H$

The second equation rearranges to
$$
zH^{2}+(z-1)H+z=0. \tag{3}
$$
Solving for $H$ (choosing the branch with $H(0)=0$, i.e. the root vanishing at $
z=0$):
$$
H=\frac{1-z-\sqrt{(1-z)^{2}-4z^{2}}}{2z}
      =\frac{1-z-\sqrt{1-2z-3z^{2}}}{2z}. \tag{4}
$$
Factor the discriminant:
$$
1-2z-3z^{2}=(1-3z)(1+z), \tag{5}
$$
hence
$$
\boxed{\,H(z)=\dfrac{1-z-\sqrt{(1-3z)(1+z)}}{2z}\,}. \tag{6}
$$

**Branch check.** As $z\to 0$, $\sqrt{(1-3z)(1+z)}=\sqrt{1-2z-3z^{2}}=1-z-2z^{2}
+O(z^{3})$, so the numerator is $1-z-(1-z-2z^{2})=2z^{2}+O(z^{3})$ and $H(z)=z+O
(z^{2})$. Thus $H(0)=0$ and $[z^{1}]H=1$, as required for a half-pyramid built f
rom one marked cell. The other root diverges like $1/z$ and is discarded.

### Step 3. Derive $P(z)$

Insert (6) into (2). First compute $1-H$:
$$
1-H = 1-\frac{1-z-s}{2z}=\frac{2z-1+z+s}{2z}=\frac{s-(1-3z)}{2z},\qquad s:=\sqrt
{(1-3z)(1+z)}. \tag{7}
$$
Therefore
$$
P=\frac{H}{1-H}=\frac{1-z-s}{\,s-(1-3z)\,}. \tag{8}
$$
Rationalize by multiplying numerator and denominator by $s+(1-3z)$.

**Denominator:**
$$
\bigl(s-(1-3z)\bigr)\bigl(s+(1-3z)\bigr)=s^{2}-(1-3z)^{2}.
$$
Using $s^{2}=(1-3z)(1+z)=1-2z-3z^{2}$ and $(1-3z)^{2}=1-6z+9z^{2}$,
$$
s^{2}-(1-3z)^{2}=(1-2z-3z^{2})-(1-6z+9z^{2})=4z-12z^{2}=4z(1-3z). \tag{9}
$$

**Numerator:**
$$
(1-z-s)\bigl(s+(1-3z)\bigr)=(1-z)s+(1-z)(1-3z)-s^{2}-s(1-3z).
$$
Collect the $s$-terms:
$$
(1-z)s-s(1-3z)=s\bigl[(1-z)-(1-3z)\bigr]=2zs.
$$
The constant terms:
$$
(1-z)(1-3z)-s^{2}=(1-4z+3z^{2})-(1-2z-3z^{2})=-2z+6z^{2}=-2z(1-3z).
$$
Hence the numerator equals
$$
2zs-2z(1-3z)=2z\bigl(s-(1-3z)\bigr). \tag{10}
$$

**Combine:**
$$
P=\frac{2z\bigl(s-(1-3z)\bigr)}{4z(1-3z)}=\frac{s-(1-3z)}{2(1-3z)}. \tag{11}
$$
Thus
$$
\boxed{\,P(z)=\dfrac{\sqrt{(1-3z)(1+z)}-(1-3z)}{2(1-3z)}\,}. \tag{12}
$$
Equivalently, dividing numerator and denominator by $\sqrt{1-3z}$,
$$
P(z)=\frac{1}{2}\left(\sqrt{\dfrac{1+z}{1-3z}}-1\right). \tag{13}
$$
