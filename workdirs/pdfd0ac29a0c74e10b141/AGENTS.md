# Problem

In the city of Gridville, every building is located exactly at an intersection of a perfectly uniform square grid of streets and avenues (represented by integer coordinates). A group of 60 secret agents is planning a surveillance network. Each agent will be stationed at a unique building within the city.

The agency requires the network to satisfy a specific "Triangular Coverage" protocol: For any two distinct agents located at buildings $A$ and $B$, there must exist at least one other building $C$ in Gridville (also located at a grid intersection) such that the area of the triangle formed by the three buildings $A, B$, and $C$ is exactly $k$ square units.

Find the smallest positive integer $k$ for which it is possible to choose a set of 60 buildings that satisfies this protocol for every pair of agents in the set.
