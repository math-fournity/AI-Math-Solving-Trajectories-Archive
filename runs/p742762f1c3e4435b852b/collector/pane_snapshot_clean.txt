$$I = \int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{\sin^3\!\left(\left(x-\frac{1}{x}\right)^5\right)}{
x^3}\, dx$$

## Step 1: Symmetry under $x \mapsto 1/x$

Define $J_n = \int_0^\infty \frac{\sin^3((x-1/x)^5)}{x^n}\,dx$. Substituting $x
= 1/u$, $dx = -du/u^2$:

$$J_n = \int_\infty^0 \frac{\sin^3((1/u - u)^5)}{u^{-n}}\cdot\frac{-du}{u^2} = \
int_0^\infty \frac{\sin^3(-(u-1/u)^5)}{u^{2-n}}\,du = -\int_0^\infty \frac{\sin^
3((u-1/u)^5)}{u^{2-n}}\,du$$

since $\sin^3(-w) = -\sin^3(w)$. Thus $J_n = -J_{2-n}$, giving $J_3 = -J_{-1}$.

## Step 2: Substitution $t = x - 1/x$

The map $x \mapsto t = x - 1/x$ sends $(0,\infty) \to (-\infty,\infty)$ bijectiv
ely, with $dt = (1+1/x^2)\,dx$ and $x = \frac{t+\sqrt{t^2+4}}{2}$. Since $x^2+1
= x\sqrt{t^2+4}$:

$$J_n = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{\sin^3(t^5)\cdot x^{2-n}}{x^2+1}\,dt = \in
t_{-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{\sin^3(t^5)\cdot x^{1-n}}{\sqrt{t^2+4}}\,dt.$$

## Step 3: Evaluate $J_3$

For $n=3$, using $x^{-1} = \frac{-t+\sqrt{t^2+4}}{2}$:

$$x^{-2} = \frac{(-t+\sqrt{t^2+4})^2}{4} = \frac{t^2+2 - t\sqrt{t^2+4}}{2},$$

so

$$\frac{x^{-2}}{\sqrt{t^2+4}} = \underbrace{\frac{t^2+2}{2\sqrt{t^2+4}}}_{\text{
even in }t} - \underbrace{\frac{t}{2}}_{\text{odd in }t}.$$

Since $\sin^3(t^5)$ is **odd** in $t$ (because $\sin^3((-t)^5) = \sin^3(-t^5) =
-\sin^3(t^5)$):

- **Even × odd = odd** → the first term integrates to $0$ over $(-\infty,\infty)
$.
- **Odd × odd = even** → the second term survives:

$$J_3 = -\frac{1}{2}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} t\,\sin^3(t^5)\,dt = -\int_0^\infty
t\,\sin^3(t^5)\,dt.$$

## Step 4: Mellin transform evaluation

Substitute $u = t^5$, $t = u^{1/5}$, $dt = \frac{1}{5}u^{-4/5}\,du$:

$$\int_0^\infty t\,\sin^3(t^5)\,dt = \frac{1}{5}\int_0^\infty u^{-3/5}\sin^3(u)\
,du.$$

Using $\sin^3(u) = \frac{3\sin u - \sin 3u}{4}$:

$$= \frac{1}{20}\left(3\int_0^\infty u^{-3/5}\sin u\,du - \int_0^\infty u^{-3/5}
\sin 3u\,du\right).$$

By the standard Mellin transform $\int_0^\infty u^{s-1}\sin u\,du = \Gamma(s)\si
n\frac{\pi s}{2}$ (valid for $-1<\operatorname{Re}(s)<1$), with $s = 2/5$:

$$\int_0^\infty u^{-3/5}\sin u\,du = \Gamma\!\left(\tfrac{2}{5}\right)\sin\frac{
\pi}{5}.$$

For the second integral, substituting $v = 3u$:

$$\int_0^\infty u^{-3/5}\sin 3u\,du = 3^{-2/5}\,\Gamma\!\left(\tfrac{2}{5}\right
)\sin\frac{\pi}{5}.$$

## Step 5: Combine

$$\int_0^\infty t\,\sin^3(t^5)\,dt = \frac{\Gamma(2/5)\sin(\pi/5)}{20}\left(3 -
3^{-2/5}\right),$$

$$\boxed{I = J_3 = -\frac{\Gamma\!\left(\frac{2}{5}\right)\sin\!\left(\frac{\pi}
{5}\right)}{20}\left(3 - 3^{-2/5}\right)}$$

**Numerical verification:** $\Gamma(2/5) \approx 1.4892$, $\sin(\pi/5) \approx 0
.5878$, $3^{-2/5} \approx 0.6444$, giving $I \approx -0.1031$. The sign is consi
stent with the integrand being more negative for $x < 1$ (where $|x-1/x|$ is lar
ge and $1/x^3$ amplifies the contribution) than positive for $x > 1$.
