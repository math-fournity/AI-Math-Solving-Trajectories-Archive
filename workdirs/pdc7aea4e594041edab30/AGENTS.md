# Problem

In a specialized logistics warehouse, the floor is a perfect grid of $8 \times 8$ storage bays, totaling $64$ individual slots. Two supervisors, Arthur and Katelyn, are tasking the floor with equipment. They take turns according to strict protocols, with Arthur always acting first.

On Arthur’s turn, he marks a single empty slot with an 'A' for an automated drone. On Katelyn’s turn, she places a double-length shipping crate marked with 'K's that occupies exactly two empty slots sharing a common edge.

The process continues until one supervisor is unable to complete their assigned placement according to the rules. At the conclusion of the operation, Katelyn’s performance is evaluated by the total number of 'K' marks (the total number of individual slots her crates occupy) on the grid.

Assuming both Arthur and Katelyn employ optimal strategies—Arthur aiming to minimize the total 'K' marks and Katelyn aiming to maximize them—what is the highest score Katelyn is guaranteed to achieve regardless of Arthur's placements?
