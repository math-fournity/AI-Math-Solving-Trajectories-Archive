Looking at this problem, I need to find the side length of an equilateral triang
le given constraints on points D, E, F and the area/angle of triangle DEF.

## Setup

Place the equilateral triangle with side $s = AB$ in coordinates:
- $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (s, 0)$, $C = (s/2, s\sqrt{3}/2)$
- $F$ on $AB$ with $FA = 5$: $F = (5, 0)$
- $D$ on $BC$ with $CD = 2$: $D = (s/2 + 1,\, \sqrt{3}(s/2 - 1))$
- $E$ on $CA$ with $AE = u$, $CE = v$, so $u + v = s$: $E = (u/2,\, u\sqrt{3}/2)
$

## Area Condition

Computing the cross product of $\vec{EF}$ and $\vec{ED}$:

$$\vec{EF} \times \vec{ED} = \sqrt{3}\!\left(\tfrac{3v}{2} + s - 5\right)$$

$$\text{Area} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\left|\tfrac{3v}{2} + s - 5\right| = 14\sqrt{3
} \implies \tfrac{3v}{2} + s - 5 = 28$$

(Only the positive case is feasible since $v, s > 0$.) This gives $s = 33 - \fra
c{3v}{2}$.

## Angle Condition via Law of Cosines

Since $\angle DEF = 60°$ and Area $= \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}|ED||EF| = 14\sqrt{3}$, w
e get $|ED||EF| = 56$.

By the law of cosines: $|DF|^2 = |ED|^2 + |EF|^2 - |ED||EF| = |ED|^2 + |EF|^2 -
56$.

**Computing each squared length** (substituting $s = 33 - 3v/2$):

$$|ED|^2 = v^2 - 2v + 4$$

$$|EF|^2 = \frac{25v^2}{4} - \frac{305v}{2} + 949$$

$$|ED|^2 + |EF|^2 - 56 = \frac{29v^2}{4} - \frac{309v}{2} + 897$$

**Direct computation of $|DF|^2$** from coordinates of $D$ and $F$:

$$|DF|^2 = \left(\tfrac{s}{2}-4\right)^2 + 3\left(\tfrac{s}{2}-1\right)^2 = s^2
- 7s + 19 = \frac{9v^2}{4} - \frac{177v}{2} + 877$$

## Solving

Setting the two expressions for $|DF|^2$ equal:

$$\frac{29v^2}{4} - \frac{309v}{2} + 897 = \frac{9v^2}{4} - \frac{177v}{2} + 877
$$

$$\frac{20v^2}{4} - \frac{132v}{2} + 20 = 0 \implies 5v^2 - 66v + 20 = 0$$

$$v = \frac{66 \pm \sqrt{4356 - 400}}{10} = \frac{66 \pm \sqrt{3956}}{10} = \fra
c{66 \pm 2\sqrt{989}}{10} = \frac{33 \pm \sqrt{989}}{5}$$

## Recovering $s$

$$s = 33 - \frac{3v}{2} = 33 - \frac{3(33 \pm \sqrt{989})}{10} = \frac{330 - 99
\mp 3\sqrt{989}}{10} = \frac{231 \mp 3\sqrt{989}}{10}$$

The two values of $s = AB$ are:

$$s = \frac{231 \pm 3\sqrt{989}}{10} = \frac{231}{10} \pm \frac{3}{10}\sqrt{989}
$$

This is of the form $p \pm q\sqrt{r}$ with $p = \frac{231}{10}$, $q = \frac{3}{1
0}$, $r = 989$.

## Verification

- **Square-free check:** $989 = 23 \times 43$, a product of two distinct primes,

- **Feasibility check:** Both solutions give $s > 5$ (so $F$ lies on $AB$), $s >

- **Numerical check:** For $v \approx 0.3103$, $s \approx 32.53$: $|ED||EF| \app

## Answer
