**Key fact (Varignon):** For any convex quadrilateral, the quadrilateral formed
by connecting the midpoints of its sides has area exactly **half** of the origin
al.

*Proof of the fact:* Let the vertices be $P_1, P_2, P_3, P_4$. The midpoint quad
rilateral has vertices $M_i = \tfrac{1}{2}(P_i + P_{i+1})$. Using the shoelace f
ormula, the signed area of the midpoint quadrilateral equals $\tfrac{1}{2}$ time
s the signed area of the original (a direct algebraic expansion shows each cross
-term pair is halved). Hence $\text{Area}_{i+1} = \tfrac{1}{2}\text{Area}_i$.

**Computing $A_1$:** Vertices $A_1(0,48), B_1(80,64), C_1(128,40), D_1(40,0)$. S
hoelace:

$$
2A_1 = |x_A(y_B-y_D)+x_B(y_C-y_A)+x_C(y_D-y_B)+x_D(y_A-y_C)|
$$
$$
= |0(64-0)+80(40-48)+128(0-64)+40(48-40)|$$
$$= |0 - 640 - 8192 + 320| = |-8512| = 8512.$$

So $A_1 = 4256$.

**Total area:**

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{\infty} A_i = A_1\!\left(1+\tfrac12+\tfrac14+\cdots\right) = 2A_1 =
2\cdot 4256 = 8512.
$$

**Putting in the form $\frac{a^2 b}{c}$:** Factor $8512$:

$$8512 = 2\cdot 4256 = 2^2\cdot 2128 = 2^3\cdot 1064 = 2^4\cdot 532 = 2^5\cdot 2
66 = 2^6\cdot 133.$$

Since $133 = 7\cdot 19$ is square-free:

$$8512 = 8^2\cdot 133 = \frac{8^2\cdot 133}{1}.$$

Thus $a = 8$, $b = 133$, $c = 1$, with $b$ square-free and $c$ minimal.

$$a + b + c = 8 + 133 + 1 = \boxed{142}.$$
