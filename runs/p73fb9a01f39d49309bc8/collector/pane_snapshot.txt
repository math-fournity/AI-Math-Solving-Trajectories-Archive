## Solution

**Setup.** Place the regular hexagon (side 3, circumradius 3) with center at the
 origin:

$$A=(3,0),\ B=\left(\tfrac{3}{2},\tfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}\right),\ C=\left(-\tfrac{3}{
2},\tfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}\right),\ D=(-3,0),\ E=\left(-\tfrac{3}{2},-\tfrac{3\sqrt3}
{2}\right),\ F=\left(\tfrac{3}{2},-\tfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}\right).$$

Each side has length 3 (verified, e.g., $|AB|=\sqrt{(3/2)^2+(3\sqrt3/2)^2}=3$).

**Sensor positions** (each is $1/3$ of the way along its side from the named cor
ner):

- $X$ on $AB$, 1 km from $A$: $\;X = A + \tfrac13(B-A) = (3,0)+\tfrac13(-\tfrac3
2,\tfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}) = \left(\tfrac52,\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\right).$

- $Y$ on $CD$, 1 km from $C$: $\;Y = C + \tfrac13(D-C) = \left(-\tfrac32,\tfrac{
3\sqrt3}{2}\right)+\tfrac13(-\tfrac32,-\tfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}) = (-2,\sqrt3).$

- $Z$ on $EF$, 1 km from $E$: $\;Z = E + \tfrac13(F-E) = \left(-\tfrac32,-\tfrac
{3\sqrt3}{2}\right)+(1,0) = \left(-\tfrac12,-\tfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}\right).$

**Area via the determinant formula.** Compute the edge vectors from $X$:

$$Y-X = \left(-\tfrac92,\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\right),\qquad Z-X = \left(-3,-2\sqrt3\
right).$$

$$\det(Y-X,\; Z-X) = \left(-\tfrac92\right)(-2\sqrt3) - \left(\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\
right)(-3) = 9\sqrt3 + \tfrac{3\sqrt3}{2} = \tfrac{21\sqrt3}{2}.$$

$$[XYZ] = \tfrac12\left|\tfrac{21\sqrt3}{2}\right| = \tfrac{21\sqrt3}{4}.$$

**Verification of the required form.** We have $\dfrac{21\sqrt3}{4}$ with $a=21,
\ b=3,\ c=4$:
- $b=3$ is square-free ✓
- $\gcd(21,4)=1$, so the fraction is in lowest terms ✓

**Final answer:**

$$100a + 10b + c = 100(21) + 10(3) + 4 = 2100 + 30 + 4 = \boxed{2134}.$$
