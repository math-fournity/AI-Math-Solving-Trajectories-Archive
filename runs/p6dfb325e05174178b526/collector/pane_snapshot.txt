We use the standard characterization: $\{f_n\}$ is uniformly integrable if and o
nly if
$$\sup_n \int_{\{|f_n| > K\}} |f_n|\,d\mu \;\xrightarrow{K\to\infty}\; 0.$$

**Key estimate.** On the set $\{|f_n| > K\}$, we have $|f_n|/K > 1$, hence $(|f_
n|/K)^\gamma > 1$ (since $\gamma > 0$). Therefore
$$|f_n| \;=\; |f_n|\cdot 1 \;<\; |f_n|\cdot\left(\frac{|f_n|}{K}\right)^{\gamma}
 \;=\; \frac{|f_n|^{1+\gamma}}{K^{\gamma}} \qquad \text{on } \{|f_n|>K\}.$$

Integrating over $\{|f_n|>K\}$:
$$\int_{\{|f_n|>K\}} |f_n|\,d\mu \;\le\; \frac{1}{K^{\gamma}}\int_{\{|f_n|>K\}}
|f_n|^{1+\gamma}\,d\mu \;\le\; \frac{1}{K^{\gamma}}\int |f_n|^{1+\gamma}\,d\mu \

$$\sup_n \int_{\{|f_n|>K\}} |f_n|\,d\mu \;\le\; \frac{M}{K^{\gamma}} \;\xrightar
