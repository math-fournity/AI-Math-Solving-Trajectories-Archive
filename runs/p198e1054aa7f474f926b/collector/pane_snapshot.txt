## Solution

Let the grid entry in row $i$, column $j$ be $a_{ij}$ (row 1 = top, row 3 = bott
om). For each column $j$, the entries form a non-constant arithmetic sequence, s

$$a_{1j}=t_j-d_j,\quad a_{2j}=t_j,\quad a_{3j}=t_j+d_j,$$
with $d_j\neq 0$ and $|d_j|\le 1$.

The three row-product constraints are:
$$(t_1-d_1)(t_2-d_2)(t_3-d_3)=t_1t_2t_3=(t_1+d_1)(t_2+d_2)(t_3+d_3)=P.$$

**Expanding.** Setting the row-1 product equal to the row-2 product and the row-
3 product equal to the row-2 product, then **adding** the two resulting equation
s gives
$$d_1d_2\,t_3+d_1d_3\,t_2+d_2d_3\,t_1=0,$$
and **subtracting** them gives
$$d_1t_2t_3+d_2t_1t_3+d_3t_1t_2+d_1d_2d_3=0.$$

**Change of variables.** Set $u_j=t_j/d_j$ (valid since $d_j\neq 0$). Then $t_j=
u_jd_j$ and $P=u_1u_2u_3\,d_1d_2d_3$.

- The first equation becomes $d_1d_2d_3(u_1+u_2+u_3)=0$, so
$$u_1+u_2+u_3=0.\tag{$\star$}$$
- The second equation becomes $d_1d_2d_3\,(u_1u_2+u_1u_3+u_2u_3+1)=0$, so
$$u_1u_2+u_1u_3+u_2u_3=-1.\tag{$\star\star$}$$

From $(\star)$ and $(\star\star)$: $u_1^2+u_2^2+u_3^2=(u_1+u_2+u_3)^2-2(u_1u_2+u
_1u_3+u_2u_3)=2$.

**Bounding the product.** The $u_j$ are the three real roots of
$$x^3-(u_1+u_2+u_3)x^2+(u_1u_2+u_1u_3+u_2u_3)x-u_1u_2u_3=0\;\Longrightarrow\;x^3
-x-s=0,$$
where $s:=u_1u_2u_3$. For this cubic to have three real roots, its discriminant
must be non-negative:
$$\Delta=-4(-1)^3-27(-s)^2=4-27s^2\ge 0\;\Longrightarrow\;s^2\le\frac{4}{27}.$$

**Combining with the $d_j$ bound.** Since $|d_j|\le 1$,
$$P^2=(u_1u_2u_3)^2(d_1d_2d_3)^2=s^2\,(d_1d_2d_3)^2\le \frac{4}{27}\cdot 1=\frac
{4}{27}.$$

**Achievability.** Take $d_1=d_2=d_3=1$ (so $|d_j|=1\le 1$, $d_j\neq 0$) and $(u
_1,u_2,u_3)=\bigl(-\tfrac{1}{\sqrt 3},-\tfrac{1}{\sqrt 3},\tfrac{2}{\sqrt 3}\big

ac{4}{27}$ (discriminant $=0$, a double root — consistent). The grid is
$$\begin{pmatrix} -\tfrac1{\sqrt3}-1 & -\tfrac1{\sqrt3}-1 & \tfrac2{\sqrt3}-1\\[
2pt] -\tfrac1{\sqrt3} & -\tfrac1{\sqrt3} & \tfrac2{\sqrt3}\\[2pt] -\tfrac1{\sqrt
3}+1 & -\tfrac1{\sqrt3}+1 & \tfrac2{\sqrt3}+1 \end{pmatrix}.$$
Each column is an arithmetic sequence with common difference $1\neq 0$ and $|d|=

$$\boxed{P^2_{\max}=\dfrac{4}{27}}$$
