case Hamming distance $\max_x d(x, g(f(x)))$.

### Upper Bound: $C \leq 56$

The function $f$ partitions $\{0,1\}^{60}$ into $2^{44}$ fibers $f^{-1}(y)$. Sin
ce $2^{60}/2^{44} = 2^{16} = 65536$, by the pigeonhole principle at least one fi
ber contains $\geq 2^{16}$ elements.

For any fiber $F$ with representative $r = g(y)$, the Hamming ball of radius 3 a
round $r$ has volume
$$V(60,3) = \sum_{i=0}^{3}\binom{60}{i} = 1 + 60 + 1770 + 34220 = 36051.$$

$60 - 4 = 56$ matching positions.

Thus no strategy can guarantee $C \geq 57$, so $C \leq 56$.
