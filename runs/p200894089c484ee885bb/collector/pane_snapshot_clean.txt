$$S_1(x) = x \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n}{n!}\left(\frac{x^2}{2}\right)^n =

## Step 2: Identify the second series

$$S_2(x) = 1 + \frac{x^2}{2^2} + \frac{x^4}{2^2\cdot 4^2} + \cdots = \sum_{n=0}^

This is exactly the modified Bessel function of the first kind of order zero:

$$S_2(x) = I_0(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(x/2)^{2n}}{(n!)^2}$$
