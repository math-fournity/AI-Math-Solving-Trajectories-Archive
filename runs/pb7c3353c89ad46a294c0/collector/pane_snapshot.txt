### Preliminary: the representation function \(r_2\)

Let \(r_2(N) = \#\{(x,y)\in\mathbb Z^2 : x^2+y^2=N\}\). The classical formula (J
acobi/Gauss) gives
\[
r_2(N)=4\bigl(d_1(N)-d_3(N)\bigr),
\]
where \(d_1(N)\) (resp. \(d_3(N)\)) is the number of divisors of \(N\) congruent
 to \(1\) (resp. \(3\)) mod \(4\). In particular, if \(N=\prod p_i^{e_i}\cdot\pr
od q_j^{2f_j}\cdot 2^a\) with \(p_i\equiv 1\pmod 4\), \(q_j\equiv 3\pmod 4\), th

\[
d_1(N)-d_3(N)=\prod_i (e_i+1).

### Even \(n=2k\) (\(k\ge 1\)): center \((\tfrac12,0)\)

Take the circle centered at \((\tfrac12,0)\) with radius \(r=\tfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{5
^{k-1}}\), so that \(4r^2=5^{k-1}=:M\).

A lattice point \((a,b)\) lies on it iff
\[
(a-\tfrac12)^2+b^2=r^2 \iff (2a-1)^2+(2b)^2=M.
\]
Setting \(x=2a-1\) (odd), \(y=2b\) (even), the count of lattice points equals th
e number of representations \(x^2+y^2=M\) with \(x\) odd, \(y\) even.

Since \(M=5^{k-1}\equiv 1\pmod 4\), in every representation \(x^2+y^2=M\) exactl
y one of \(x,y\) is odd. By the symmetry \((x,y)\leftrightarrow(y,x)\), the repr
esentations with \((x\text{ odd},y\text{ even})\) and \((x\text{ even},y\text{ o
dd})\) are equal in number, so the count is
\[
\frac{r_2(M)}{2}=2\bigl(d_1(M)-d_3(M)\bigr).
\]
For \(M=5^{k-1}\), every divisor \(1,5,\dots,5^{k-1}\) is \(\equiv 1\pmod 4\), s
o \(d_1(M)=k,\ d_3(M)=0\). Thus the count is
\[
2k = n.
