## Solution

$$\alpha,\ \alpha\omega,\ \alpha\omega^2, \qquad \alpha = \sqrt[3]{5},\quad \ome
ga = e^{2\pi i/3}.$$
Hence the splitting field is
$$E = \mathbb{Q}(\alpha,\omega).$$

### Degree of the extension

Since $x^3-5$ is irreducible over $\mathbb{Q}$ (Eisenstein at $p=5$), $[\mathbb{
Q}(\alpha):\mathbb{Q}]=3$. Also $\omega$ satisfies $x^2+x+1$, irreducible over $
\mathbb{Q}$, and $\omega\notin\mathbb{Q}(\alpha)$ (the latter is a real field),
so $[\mathbb{Q}(\alpha,\omega):\mathbb{Q}(\alpha)]=2$. Therefore
$$[E:\mathbb{Q}] = 3\cdot 2 = 6.$$
As $E$ is a splitting field over a characteristic-$0$ field, $E/\mathbb{Q}$ is G
alois, so $|\mathrm{Gal}(E/\mathbb{Q})|=6$.

### Generators and relations

Define
$$\sigma:\ \alpha\mapsto\alpha\omega,\ \omega\mapsto\omega, \qquad \tau:\ \alpha
\mapsto\alpha,\ \omega\mapsto\omega^2.$$
Then $\sigma$ has order $3$, $\tau$ has order $2$, and
$$\tau\sigma\tau = \sigma^{-1}$$
(both sides send $\alpha\mapsto\alpha\omega^2$ and fix $\omega$ up to the conjug
ation). Thus
$$\mathrm{Gal}(E/\mathbb{Q}) = \langle\sigma,\tau \mid \sigma^3=\tau^2=1,\ \tau\
sigma\tau=\sigma^{-1}\rangle \cong S_3 \cong D_3.$$
