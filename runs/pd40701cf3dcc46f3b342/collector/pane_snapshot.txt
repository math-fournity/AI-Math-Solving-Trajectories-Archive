## Proof

We show that $T$ is compact. In fact, $T$ is a **finite-rank** (rank-one) operat
or, and every finite-rank bounded operator on a Hilbert space is compact.

**Step 1: Separation of the kernel.**

The kernel $K(x,t) = e^{x-t}$ factors as a product of a function of $x$ and a fu
nction of $t$:

$$K(x,t) = e^{x}\cdot e^{-t}.$$

Let $u(x) = e^{x}$ and $v(t) = e^{-t}$. Both $u, v \in L^{2}(0,1)$ since they ar
e continuous (hence bounded) on $[0,1]$.

**Step 2: $T$ is rank-one.**

For any $f \in L^{2}(0,1)$,

$$Tf(x) = \int_{0}^{1} e^{x-t} f(t)\,dt = e^{x}\int_{0}^{1} e^{-t} f(t)\,dt = \l
angle f, \overline{v}\rangle \, u(x),$$

where $\langle f, \overline{v}\rangle = \int_{0}^{1} f(t)\,e^{-t}\,dt$ (a scalar
, since $v(t)=e^{-t}$ is real and positive). Thus

$$Tf = \langle f, e^{-\,\cdot}\rangle \cdot e^{\,\cdot},$$

so the range of $T$ is contained in $\operatorname{span}\{u\} = \operatorname{sp
an}\{e^{x}\}$, a one-dimensional subspace of $L^{2}(0,1)$. Hence $\operatorname{
rank}(T) \leqslant 1$; $T$ is a finite-rank operator.

**Step 3: Boundedness.**

$T$ is bounded since $u, v \in L^{2}(0,1)$:

$$\|Tf\|_{2} = \left|\langle f, e^{-\,\cdot}\rangle\right| \cdot \|e^{\,\cdot}\|
_{2} \leqslant \|f\|_{2}\,\|e^{-\,\cdot}\|_{2}\,\|e^{\,\cdot}\|_{2} < \infty.$$

**Step 4: Finite-rank ⟹ compact.**

A bounded finite-rank operator maps the unit ball into a bounded subset of a fin
ite-dimensional space. By the Heine–Borel theorem, closed bounded subsets of fin
ite-dimensional spaces are compact, so $T$ maps the unit ball of $L^{2}(0,1)$ to
 a relatively compact set. Therefore $T$ is compact.

**Alternative confirmation via Hilbert–Schmidt.** The kernel $K(x,t)=e^{x-t}$ is
 square-integrable:

$$\iint_{[0,1]^{2}} |K(x,t)|^{2}\,dx\,dt = \left(\int_{0}^{1} e^{2x}\,dx\right)\
!\left(\int_{0}^{1} e^{-2t}\,dt\right) = \frac{e^{2}-1}{2}\cdot\frac{1-e^{-2}}{2
} < \infty.$$

So $T$ is a Hilbert–Schmidt operator, and every Hilbert–Schmidt operator on $L^{
2}$ is compact.

**Conclusion.** The operator $T$ is compact (indeed, it is a rank-one Hilbert–Sc
hmidt operator).
