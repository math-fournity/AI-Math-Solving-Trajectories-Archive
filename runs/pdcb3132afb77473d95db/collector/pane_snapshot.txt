# Spanning Trees of the Hypercube Graph $H_d$

**Claim.** The number of spanning trees of the $d$-dimensional hypercube graph $
H_d$ is

$$\tau(H_d) \;=\; \frac{1}{2^d}\prod_{k=1}^{d}(2k)^{\binom{d}{k}}.$$

---

## Setup

The hypercube $H_d$ is the graph whose vertices are the $2^d$ binary strings in
$\{0,1\}^d$, with two vertices adjacent iff they differ in exactly one coordinat
e. It is a $d$-regular graph on $n = 2^d$ vertices.

We use the **Matrix–Tree Theorem**: for any connected graph on $n$ vertices with
 Laplacian eigenvalues $\lambda_1 \ge \cdots \ge \lambda_n$, where $\lambda_n =
0$ (simple, by connectivity),

$$\tau \;=\; \frac{1}{n}\prod_{i=1}^{n-1}\lambda_i.$$

So the task reduces to computing the Laplacian spectrum of $H_d$.

---

## The Laplacian spectrum of $H_d$

View $H_d$ as the **Cayley graph** of the abelian group $G = (\mathbb{Z}/2\mathb
b{Z})^d$ with generating set $S = \{e_1,\dots,e_d\}$, the standard basis. Concre
tely, vertex $x \in G$ is adjacent to $x + e_i$ for $i = 1,\dots,d$.

Because $G$ is abelian, the characters $\chi_y : G \to \{\pm 1\}$, defined for e
ach $y \in \{0,1\}^d$ by

$$\chi_y(x) = (-1)^{x \cdot y} = (-1)^{\sum_i x_i y_i},$$

form a complete set of eigenvectors of the adjacency matrix $A$ of the Cayley gr
aph. The eigenvalue of $A$ associated to $\chi_y$ is

$$\mu_y \;=\; \sum_{s \in S} \chi_y(s) \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{d} (-1)^{y_i} \;=\; d -
 2\,|y|,$$

where $|y| = y_1 + \cdots + y_d$ is the Hamming weight of $y$. There are exactly
 $\binom{d}{k}$ vectors $y$ of weight $k$, so the adjacency eigenvalue $d - 2k$
occurs with multiplicity $\binom{d}{k}$, for $k = 0, 1, \dots, d$.

Since $H_d$ is $d$-regular, its Laplacian is $L = dI - A$, and the Laplacian eig
envalues are

$$\lambda \;=\; d - \mu_y \;=\; d - (d - 2|y|) \;=\; 2|y|.$$

Hence the Laplacian eigenvalue $2k$ has multiplicity $\binom{d}{k}$, for $k = 0,
1,\dots,d$. In particular $\lambda = 0$ occurs once (at $k=0$), confirming $H_d$
 is connected, and the nonzero Laplacian eigenvalues are

$$\underbrace{2,\dots,2}_{\binom{d}{1}},\;\; \underbrace{4,\dots,4}_{\binom{d}{2
}},\;\; \dots,\;\; \underbrace{2d}_{\binom{d}{d}=1}.$$

---

## Applying the Matrix–Tree Theorem

With $n = 2^d$ and the single zero eigenvalue coming from $k=0$,

$$\tau(H_d) \;=\; \frac{1}{2^d}\prod_{k=1}^{d}(2k)^{\binom{d}{k}}.$$

{1}} = \frac{2}{2} = 1$. ✓

The number of spanning trees of the $d$-dimensional hypercube graph is
