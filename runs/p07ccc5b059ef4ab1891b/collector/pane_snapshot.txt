$$I(p) = \int_{\mathbb{R}^n} \bigl|\log|x|\bigr|^p\,dx$$

$$I(p) = |S^{n-1}|\int_0^\infty \bigl|\log r\bigr|^p\,r^{n-1}\,dr.$$
