## Solution

We solve the PDE

$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} = \kappa \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} + s\,
u, \qquad x \geq X(t), \quad t > 0,$$

with initial condition $u(x,0) = J_0\,\delta(x)$.

### Step 1: Remove the source term

Set

$$u(x,t) = e^{st}\,v(x,t).$$

Substituting:

$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} = s\,e^{st}v + e^{st}\frac{\partial v}{\partial
t}, \qquad \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} = e^{st}\frac{\partial^2 v}{\partia
l x^2}.$$

The PDE becomes

$$s\,e^{st}v + e^{st}v_t = \kappa\, e^{st}v_{xx} + s\,e^{st}v,$$

which simplifies to the **standard heat equation**:

$$\frac{\partial v}{\partial t} = \kappa \frac{\partial^2 v}{\partial x^2}.$$

The initial condition transforms as

$$v(x,0) = e^{s\cdot 0}\,v(x,0) = u(x,0) = J_0\,\delta(x).$$

### Step 2: Solve the heat equation with delta initial data

The fundamental solution (Green's function) of the heat equation $v_t = \kappa v
_{xx}$ on the full line with initial data $v(x,0) = J_0\,\delta(x)$ is the heat
kernel convolved with the initial data:

$$v(x,t) = J_0 \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{4\pi\kappa t}}\,\exp\!\left(-\frac{x^2}{4\ka
ppa t}\right), \qquad t > 0.$$

**Verification:**
- As $t \to 0^+$, $v(x,t) \to J_0\,\delta(x)$ (the heat kernel is an approximati
on to the identity).
- $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} v(x,t)\,dx = J_0$ for all $t > 0$ (mass conservation)
.
- Direct substitution confirms $v_t = \kappa v_{xx}$.

### Step 3: Reintroduce the source term

Multiplying back by $e^{st}$:

$$\boxed{u(x,t) = \frac{J_0}{\sqrt{4\pi\kappa t}}\,\exp\!\left(st - \frac{x^2}{4
\kappa t}\right), \qquad x \geq X(t), \quad t > 0.}$$

### Step 4: Verification

**PDE check.** Compute each term:

$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} = u\left(s - \frac{1}{2t} + \frac{x^2}{4\kappa t
^2}\right),$$

$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} = u\left(-\frac{x}{2\kappa t}\right), \qquad \fr
ac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} = u\left(-\frac{1}{2\kappa t} + \frac{x^2}{4\kapp
a^2 t^2}\right).$$

Then

$$\kappa\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} + s\,u = u\left(-\frac{1}{2t} + \frac{
x^2}{4\kappa t^2}\right) + s\,u = u\left(s - \frac{1}{2t} + \frac{x^2}{4\kappa t
^2}\right) = \frac{\partial u}{\partial t}. \quad\checkmark$$

**Initial condition check.** As $t \to 0^+$, the Gaussian kernel $\frac{1}{\sqrt
{4\pi\kappa t}}e^{-x^2/(4\kappa t)} \to \delta(x)$, so

$$u(x,t) \xrightarrow{t\to 0^+} J_0\,\delta(x). \quad\checkmark$$

**Mass evolution.** The total mass at time $t$ is

$$\int_{X(t)}^{\infty} u(x,t)\,dx = J_0\,e^{st}\int_{X(t)}^{\infty}\frac{1}{\sqr
t{4\pi\kappa t}}e^{-x^2/(4\kappa t)}\,dx = J_0\,e^{st}\,\Phi\!\left(\frac{-X(t)}
{\sqrt{2\kappa t}}\right),$$

where $\Phi$ is the standard normal CDF. When $X(t)\le 0$ (the moving boundary s
tays at or left of the delta source), this integral approaches $J_0\,e^{st}$, re
flecting the exponential growth from the source term $su$.

### Conclusion

The solution is a **modified heat kernel** — the standard Gaussian heat kernel a
mplified by the exponential factor $e^{st}$ from the linear source term:

$$u(x,t) = \frac{J_0}{\sqrt{4\pi\kappa t}}\,\exp\!\left(st - \frac{x^2}{4\kappa
t}\right), \qquad x \geq X(t),\quad t>0.$$
