**Proof.**

Take $n = 1$. Let $\Gamma$ be a nontrivial free subgroup of $G = \operatorname{P

cyclic subgroup, and Schottky groups yield free subgroups of any rank $\geq 2$).

Let $\Gamma = F(S)$ be free on a generating set $S$ with $|S| \geq 1$. Choose a
generator $s_0 \in S$ and define
$$
\varphi(s_0) = 2, \qquad \varphi(s) = 1 \;\text{ for all } s \in S \setminus \{s
_0\}.
$$
Since $\Gamma$ is free on $S$, this extends uniquely to a homomorphism $\varphi:
 \Gamma \to \mathbb{R}^* = \operatorname{GL}(1,\mathbb{R})$.

**Claim.** For every continuous homomorphism $\hat\varphi: G \to \operatorname{G
L}(1,\mathbb{R})$ and every finite-index subgroup $\Gamma' \leq \Gamma$, the set

**Step 1: Every continuous homomorphism $\hat\varphi: G \to \mathbb{R}^*$ is tri
vial.**

Since $G = \operatorname{PSL}(2,\mathbb{R})$ is connected, $\hat\varphi(G)$ lies
 in the identity component $\mathbb{R}_{>0}$ of $\mathbb{R}^*$. The differential
 $d\hat\varphi: \mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb{R}) \to \mathbb{R}$ is a Lie algebra hom
omorphism into an abelian Lie algebra, so it vanishes on $[\mathfrak{sl}(2,\math
bb{R}),\mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb{R})]$. Since $\mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb{R})$ is sim
ple, it is perfect: $[\mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb{R}),\mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb{R})] =
 \mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb{R})$. Thus $d\hat\varphi = 0$, and since $G$ is connect
ed, $\hat\varphi$ is trivial.

**Step 2: $\{\varphi(\gamma) \mid \gamma \in \Gamma'\}$ is unbounded for every f
inite-index $\Gamma' \leq \Gamma$.**

Let $\Gamma'$ have index $m = [\Gamma:\Gamma'] < \infty$. The quotient $\Gamma/\
Gamma'$ is finite of order $m$, so the coset $s_0\,\Gamma'$ has order $d$ in $\G

a'$, and since $\Gamma'$ is a subgroup, $s_0^{dk} \in \Gamma'$ for all $k \geq 0

$$
\varphi(s_0^{dk}) = \varphi(s_0)^{dk} = 2^{dk} \xrightarrow{k\to\infty} \infty.
$$
Hence $\{\varphi(\gamma) \mid \gamma \in \Gamma'\}$ is unbounded in $\mathbb{R}^

leq \Gamma$, completing the proof. $\blacksquare$
