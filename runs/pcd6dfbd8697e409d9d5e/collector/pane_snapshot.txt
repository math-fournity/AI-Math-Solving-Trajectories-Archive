**Setup.** Let downstream be the positive direction. The current speed is 3 km/h
.

- **Boat 1** (departs at $t=0$): still-water speed 12 km/h.
  - Downstream: $v_1 = 12+3 = 15$ km/h.
  - Upstream: $v_1 = -(12-3) = -9$ km/h.

- **Boat 2** (departs at $t=1$): still-water speed $12 \times 1.25 = 15$ km/h.
  - Downstream: $v_2 = 15+3 = 18$ km/h.
  - Upstream: $v_2 = -(15-3) = -12$ km/h.

Each boat independently goes upstream or downstream (4 configurations). For $x \
le 1$, Boat 2 is still at the port (position 0), so its direction is irrelevant.
 For $x \ge 1$, both boats are moving:

$$p_1 = v_1 x, \qquad p_2 = v_2(x-1), \qquad \text{distance} = |p_1 - p_2| = 9.$
$

**Case $0 \le x \le 1$ (Boat 2 at port):** distance $= |v_1|\,x = 9$.

- Boat 1 downstream: $15x = 9 \Rightarrow x = \tfrac{3}{5}$. ✓ (in range)
- Boat 1 upstream: $9x = 9 \Rightarrow x = 1$. ✓ (boundary, valid)

**Case $x \ge 1$ (both moving), per configuration:**

| Config | $v_1$ | $v_2$ | $\|v_1 x - v_2(x-1)\| = 9$ | Solutions $\ge 1$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Both downstream | 15 | 18 | $\|18 - 3x\| = 9$ | $x = 3,\; x = 9$ |
| Both upstream | $-9$ | $-12$ | $\|3x - 12\| = 9$ | $x = 1,\; x = 7$ |
| B1 down, B2 up | 15 | $-12$ | $\|27x - 12\| = 9$ | $x = \tfrac{7}{9},\tfrac{1}
{9}$ — both $<1$, rejected |
| B1 up, B2 down | $-9$ | 18 | $\|18 - 27x\| = 9$ | $x = \tfrac{1}{3}$ ($<1$, re
jected), $x = 1$ ✓ |

**Verification of each element of $S$:**

- $x = \tfrac{3}{5}$: Boat 1 at $15 \cdot \tfrac{3}{5} = 9$; Boat 2 at port. Dis
tance $= 9$. ✓
- $x = 1$: Boat 1 (upstream) at $-9$; Boat 2 at $0$. Distance $= 9$. ✓
- $x = 3$: Boat 1 at $45$; Boat 2 at $18 \cdot 2 = 36$. Distance $= 9$. ✓
- $x = 7$: Boat 1 at $-63$; Boat 2 at $-12 \cdot 6 = -72$. Distance $= 9$. ✓
- $x = 9$: Boat 1 at $135$; Boat 2 at $18 \cdot 8 = 144$. Distance $= 9$. ✓

**The set** $S = \left\{\tfrac{3}{5},\; 1,\; 3,\; 7,\; 9\right\}$.

**Sum:**

$$\sum_{x \in S} x = \frac{3}{5} + 1 + 3 + 7 + 9 = \frac{3}{5} + 20 = \frac{103}
{5}.$$
