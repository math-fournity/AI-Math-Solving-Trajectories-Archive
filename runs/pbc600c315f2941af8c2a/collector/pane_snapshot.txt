## Solution

**Setup.** Label the hexagon vertices $0,1,2,3,4,5$ with letters $M,M,A,T,H,S$ r
espectively. Clayton starts at vertex $1$ (the $M$ adjacent to $M$ at vertex $0$
 and $A$ at vertex $2$). The first letter written is $M$, so we immediately have
 the $M$ in our target subsequence $M,A,T,H$. We now need $A$, then $T$, then $H
$ to appear in order.

**Markov chain.** Define $E(v,p)$ = expected number of additional moves from ver
tex $v$ with progress $p$, where $p=1$ (need $A$), $p=2$ (need $T$), $p=3$ (need
 $H$), $p=4$ (done). Each move goes to a uniformly random neighbor; writing the
needed letter advances progress, otherwise progress is unchanged. The answer is
$1 + E(1,1)$.

**Key observation:** At progress $p$, arriving at the vertex that supplies the n
eeded letter always advances progress, so that (vertex, progress) pair is unreac
hable — e.g., $(2,1)$, $(3,2)$, $(4,3)$ never occur.

### Progress 3 (need $H$ at vertex 4)

Reachable states: $c_0,c_1,c_2,c_3,c_5$. Moving to vertex $4$ completes the task
 ($E=0$).

$$c_3 = 1+\tfrac{1}{2}c_2,\quad c_5 = 1+\tfrac{1}{2}c_0,\quad c_0=1+\tfrac{c_5+c
_1}{2},\quad c_1=1+\tfrac{c_0+c_2}{2},\quad c_2=1+\tfrac{c_1+c_3}{2}$$

Substituting $c_3,c_5$ into $c_0,c_2$ gives $c_0 = c_2$ and $c_1 = 1+c_0$. Solvi
ng: $c_0 = 8,\ c_1 = 9,\ c_2 = 8,\ c_3 = 5,\ c_5 = 5$.

### Progress 2 (need $T$ at vertex 3)

Reachable states: $b_0,b_1,b_2,b_4,b_5$. Arriving at vertex $3$ advances to prog
ress 3 (using $c_3=5$).

$$b_2 = 1+\tfrac{b_1+c_3}{2}=\tfrac{7}{2}+\tfrac{b_1}{2},\quad b_4 = 1+\tfrac{c_
3+b_5}{2}=\tfrac{7}{2}+\tfrac{b_5}{2}$$
$$b_0=1+\tfrac{b_5+b_1}{2},\quad b_1=1+\tfrac{b_0+b_2}{2},\quad b_5=1+\tfrac{b_4
+b_0}{2}$$

Substituting gives $b_1 = b_5$ and $b_0 = 1+b_1$. Solving: $b_1 = 13,\ b_5 = 13,
\ b_0 = 14,\ b_2 = 10,\ b_4 = 10$.

### Progress 1 (need $A$ at vertex 2)

Reachable states: $a_0,a_1,a_3,a_4,a_5$. Arriving at vertex $2$ advances to prog
ress 2 (using $b_2=10$).

$$a_1 = 1+\tfrac{a_0+b_2}{2}=6+\tfrac{a_0}{2},\quad a_3 = 1+\tfrac{b_2+a_4}{2}=6
+\tfrac{a_4}{2}$$
$$a_0=1+\tfrac{a_5+a_1}{2},\quad a_4=1+\tfrac{a_3+a_5}{2},\quad a_5=1+\tfrac{a_4
+a_0}{2}$$

Substituting gives $a_0 = a_4$ and $a_5 = 1+a_0$. Solving: $a_0 = 18,\ a_4 = 18,
\ a_5 = 19,\ a_1 = 15,\ a_3 = 15$.

### Result

Clayton starts at state $(1,1)$, so $E(1,1) = a_1 = 15$ expected moves. The stri
ng length is $1$ (initial letter) plus the number of moves:

$$\boxed{16}$$
