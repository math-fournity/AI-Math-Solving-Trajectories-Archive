## Solution

We count 12-digit words over $\{0,1,2,3,4,5,6\}$ where adjacent digits differ by
 exactly $1$. This is the number of walks of length $11$ on the path graph $P_7$
 (vertices $0,1,\dots,6$, edges between consecutive integers).

Let $v_n$ be the vector whose $d$-th entry counts $(n+1)$-digit words ending in
digit $d$. Then $v_0 = (1,1,1,1,1,1,1)$ and $v_n = M v_{n-1}$, where $M$ is the
adjacency matrix of $P_7$. The answer is $A = \mathbf{1}^T M^{11} \mathbf{1} = \
sum(\text{entries of } v_{11})$.

Computing the recurrence $v_n(d) = v_{n-1}(d-1) + v_{n-1}(d+1)$ (with out-of-ran
ge terms $=0$):

| $n$ | $v_n$ | sum |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | $(1,1,1,1,1,1,1)$ | 7 |
| 1 | $(1,2,2,2,2,2,1)$ | 12 |
| 2 | $(2,3,4,4,4,3,2)$ | 22 |
| 3 | $(3,6,7,8,7,6,3)$ | 40 |
| 4 | $(6,10,14,14,14,10,6)$ | 74 |
| 5 | $(10,20,24,28,24,20,10)$ | 136 |
| 6 | $(20,34,48,48,48,34,20)$ | 252 |
| 7 | $(34,68,82,96,82,68,34)$ | 464 |
| 8 | $(68,116,164,164,164,116,68)$ | 860 |
| 9 | $(116,232,280,328,280,232,116)$ | 1584 |
| 10 | $(232,396,560,560,560,396,232)$ | 2936 |
| 11 | $(396,792,956,1120,956,792,396)$ | 5408 |

**Verification of $v_{11}$:** $v_{10} = (232,396,560,560,560,396,232)$.
- $v_{11}(0) = v_{10}(1) = 396$
- $v_{11}(1) = v_{10}(0)+v_{10}(2) = 232+560 = 792$
- $v_{11}(2) = v_{10}(1)+v_{10}(3) = 396+560 = 956$
- $v_{11}(3) = v_{10}(2)+v_{10}(4) = 560+560 = 1120$
- $v_{11}(4) = v_{10}(3)+v_{10}(5) = 560+396 = 956$
- $v_{11}(5) = v_{10}(4)+v_{10}(6) = 560+232 = 792$
- $v_{11}(6) = v_{10}(5) = 396$

Sum: $396+792+956+1120+956+792+396 = 5408$.

Thus $A = 5408$. Dividing by $2008$:

$$5408 = 2 \times 2008 + 1392, \qquad 2\times 2008 = 4016, \quad 5408 - 4016 = 1
392.$$

The remainder when $A$ is divided by $2008$ is $\boxed{1392}$.
