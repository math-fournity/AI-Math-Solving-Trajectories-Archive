**Key observation:** The centroid of △ABC is M = (A+B+C)/3, and the centroid of
△ABD is N = (A+B+D)/3. Therefore:

$$MN = |M - N| = \frac{|C - D|}{3} = \frac{CD}{3}$$

So the problem reduces to finding CD.

**Coordinate setup.** Place A = (0,0), B = (841, 0).

**Finding C.** In △ABC with AB=841, BC=840, AC=41, by the Law of Cosines at A:

$$\cos A = \frac{841^2 + 41^2 - 840^2}{2 \cdot 841 \cdot 41} = \frac{707281 + 16
81 - 705600}{68962} = \frac{3362}{68962} = \frac{41}{841}$$

$$\sin A = \frac{840}{841}$$

So $C = \left(\frac{41^2}{841},\, \frac{41 \cdot 840}{841}\right) = \left(\frac{
1681}{841},\, \frac{34440}{841}\right)$.

**Finding D.** In △ABD with AB=841, AD=609, BD=580, by the Law of Cosines at A:

$$\cos(\angle DAB) = \frac{841^2 + 609^2 - 580^2}{2 \cdot 841 \cdot 609} = \frac
{707281 + 370881 - 336400}{1024338} = \frac{741762}{1024338} = \frac{609}{841}$$

$$\sin(\angle DAB) = \frac{580}{841}$$

Since C and D are on **opposite sides** of line AB, D is below the x-axis:

$$D = \left(\frac{609^2}{841},\, -\frac{609 \cdot 580}{841}\right) = \left(\frac
{370881}{841},\, -\frac{353220}{841}\right)$$

**Computing CD.** Since 841 = 29²:

$$x_C - x_D = \frac{1681 - 370881}{841} = \frac{-369200}{841}, \qquad y_C - y_D
= \frac{34440 + 353220}{841} = \frac{387660}{841}$$

Factor: $369200 = 3600 \cdot 103$ and $387660 = 3780 \cdot 103$. Then:

$$CD^2 = \frac{103^2(3600^2 + 3780^2)}{841^2} = \frac{103^2 \cdot 27248400}{841^
2}$$

Since $27248400 = 32400 \cdot 841 = 180^2 \cdot 29^2$:

$$CD^2 = \frac{103^2 \cdot 180^2 \cdot 29^2}{29^4} = \left(\frac{103 \cdot 180}{
29}\right)^2$$

$$CD = \frac{18540}{29}$$

**Computing MN.**

$$MN = \frac{CD}{3} = \frac{18540}{87} = \frac{6180}{29}$$

Since 29 is prime and $\gcd(6180, 29) = 1$ (as $6180 = 29 \cdot 213 + 3$), the f
raction $\frac{6180}{29}$ is already in lowest terms.

**Final answer:** numerator + denominator = $6180 + 29 = \boxed{6209}$.
