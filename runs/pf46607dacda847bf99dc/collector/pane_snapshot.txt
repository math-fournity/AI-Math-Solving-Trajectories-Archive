## Solution

**Setup.** Place the circle at the origin with radius $r$. Let $A=(-r,0)$, $B=(r
,0)$, so $AB$ is a diameter of length $2r$.

**Locating $C$.** Since $2\cdot AC = BC$ and $C$ lies on $AB$, we have $AC = \tf
rac{1}{3}AB = \tfrac{2r}{3}$. Thus
$$C = A + \tfrac{2r}{3}(1,0) = \left(-\tfrac{r}{3},\, 0\right).$$
Check: $AC = \tfrac{2r}{3}$, $BC = \tfrac{4r}{3}$, and $2\cdot AC = \tfrac{4r}{3
} = BC$. ✓

**Locating $D$.** Since $DC\perp AB$, point $D$ is directly above $C$ on the cir
cle: $D=\left(-\tfrac{r}{3},\, y\right)$ with $\tfrac{r^2}{9}+y^2=r^2$, giving $
y=\tfrac{2r\sqrt{2}}{3}$. So
$$D = \left(-\tfrac{r}{3},\, \tfrac{2r\sqrt{2}}{3}\right).$$

**Locating $E$.** Since $DE$ is a diameter, $E$ is antipodal to $D$:
$$E = \left(\tfrac{r}{3},\, -\tfrac{2r\sqrt{2}}{3}\right).$$

**Area of $\triangle DCE$.** Using $D-C = \left(0,\, \tfrac{2r\sqrt{2}}{3}\right
)$ and $E-C = \left(\tfrac{2r}{3},\, -\tfrac{2r\sqrt{2}}{3}\right)$:
$$[DCE] = \tfrac{1}{2}\left|\det\begin{pmatrix} 0 & \tfrac{2r\sqrt{2}}{3} \\[2pt
] \tfrac{2r}{3} & -\tfrac{2r\sqrt{2}}{3} \end{pmatrix}\right| = \tfrac{1}{2}\lef
t|0 - \tfrac{4r^2\sqrt{2}}{9}\right| = \tfrac{2r^2\sqrt{2}}{9}.$$

**Area of $\triangle ABD$.** Base $AB = 2r$, height $= \tfrac{2r\sqrt{2}}{3}$ (t
he $y$-coordinate of $D$):
$$[ABD] = \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot 2r \cdot \tfrac{2r\sqrt{2}}{3} = \tfrac{2r^2\sqrt{2}
}{3}.$$

**Ratio.**
$$\frac{[DCE]}{[ABD]} = \frac{\tfrac{2r^2\sqrt{2}}{9}}{\tfrac{2r^2\sqrt{2}}{3}}
= \frac{1/9}{1/3} = \frac{1}{3}.$$

The ratio of the area of $\triangle DCE$ to the area of $\triangle ABD$ is $\box
ed{\dfrac{1}{3}}$.
