$$Q = \sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}y^k(-1)^{n-k} + 1 = 2 + \sum_{k=1}^{n-1}\binom{n

Define $R(y) = Q - y^n = 2 + c_1 y + c_2 y^2 + \cdots + c_{n-1}y^{n-1}$, where $
c_k = \binom{n}{k}(-1)^k$. Then:

$$I_n = (y^n,\; R(y)), \quad R(0) = 2,\; \deg R = n-1.$$

## Step 3: Matrix of relations

Since $y^n = 0$ in the quotient, every element is represented as $\sum_{i=0}^{n-

$$M = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & c_1 & c_2 & \cdots & c_{n-1} \\ 0 & 2 & c_1 & \cdots &

This is **upper triangular with all diagonal entries equal to 2** (since $y^j R$
