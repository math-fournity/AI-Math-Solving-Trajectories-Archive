Looking at this problem, I need to find the minimal integer $k$ (where $q = p^k$
 for a prime $p$) such that a non-supersingular elliptic curve $E$ over $GF(q)$
has exactly $q$ points of $q$-torsion.

## Proof

Let $q = p^k$ for a prime $p$ and positive integer $k$. Let $E$ be an elliptic c
urve over $GF(q) = GF(p^k)$, and let $K = \overline{GF(q)}$ be the algebraic clo
sure. The $q$-torsion subgroup is:

$$E[q] = E[p^k] = \{P \in E(K) : [p^k]P = O\}.$$

### Structural fact: $p$-power torsion of ordinary vs. supersingular curves

Over an algebraically closed field $K$ of characteristic $p > 0$, the $p$-power
torsion of an elliptic curve depends on its supersingularity:

**Supersingular case:** The multiplication-by-$p$ map is purely inseparable, so
$E[p](K) = \{O\}$. By induction, $E[p^k](K) = \{O\}$ for all $k \geq 1$. Thus $|
E[q]| = 1 \neq q$.

**Ordinary (non-supersingular) case:** The connected-étale exact sequence for $E
[p]$ over the perfect field $K$ splits:

$$E[p] \cong \mu_p \times \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z} \quad \text{(as group schemes)}
.$$

Taking $K$-points:
- $\mu_p(K) = \{x \in K^* : x^p = 1\} = \{1\}$, since $x^p - 1 = (x-1)^p$ in cha
racteristic $p$.
- $(\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})(K) = \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$, which has $p$ elements
.

Therefore $E[p](K) \cong \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$, with $|E[p](K)| = p$.

By induction on $k$: the surjection $E[p^{k+1}] \twoheadrightarrow E[p^k]$ (indu
ced by $[p]$) has kernel $E[p] \cong \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$ (on $K$-points). St
arting from $E[p](K) \cong \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$, this yields:

$$E[p^k](K) \cong \mathbb{Z}/p^k\mathbb{Z} \quad \text{for all } k \geq 1.$$

Hence $|E[p^k](K)| = p^k = q$.

### Conclusion

For **every** non-supersingular elliptic curve $E$ over $GF(q)$ with $q = p^k$:

$$|E[q]| = |E[p^k]| = p^k = q.$$

This holds for all $k \geq 1$. In particular, for $k = 1$ (taking $q = p$, any p
rime $p$), ordinary elliptic curves over $GF(p)$ exist by Waterhouse's theorem (
e.g., with trace $t = 1$, since $\gcd(1,p) = 1$ and $|1| \leq 2\sqrt{p}$), and f
or any such curve, $|E[p]| = p = q$.

Since $k$ must be a positive integer ($q = p^k > 1$), the minimal value is:

$$\boxed{k = 1}$$
