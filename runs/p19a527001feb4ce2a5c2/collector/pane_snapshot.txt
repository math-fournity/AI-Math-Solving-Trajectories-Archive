## Proof

The statement is **true**.

**Setup.** All matrices are real symmetric (or complex Hermitian); $\succeq$ den
otes the Loewner order, and $\otimes$ the Kronecker product. We are given $X \su
cceq Y \succeq 0$ and $X' \succeq Y' \succeq 0$, and must show $X \otimes X' \su
cceq Y \otimes Y'$.

**Two standard facts used.**

*Fact 1 (Kronecker product preserves PSD).* If $A \succeq 0$ and $B \succeq 0$,
then $A \otimes B \succeq 0$.

*Proof of Fact 1.* The eigenvalues of $A \otimes B$ are precisely $\{\lambda_i \
mu_j\}$ where $\lambda_i$ ranges over eigenvalues of $A$ and $\mu_j$ over eigenv
alues of $B$. Since $A, B \succeq 0$, every $\lambda_i, \mu_j \geq 0$, so every
eigenvalue of $A \otimes B$ is $\geq 0$, and $A \otimes B$ is symmetric, hence $
A \otimes B \succeq 0$. $\square$

*Fact 2 (Sum of PSD is PSD).* If $A \succeq 0$ and $B \succeq 0$, then $A + B \s
ucceq 0$. (Immediate: $v^*(A+B)v = v^*Av + v^*Bv \geq 0$.)

**Main argument.** Decompose the difference by adding and subtracting $Y \otimes
 X'$:

$$X \otimes X' - Y \otimes Y' = (X - Y) \otimes X' \;+\; Y \otimes (X' - Y').$$

Verification of the algebra:
$$(X - Y)\otimes X' + Y\otimes(X' - Y') = X\otimes X' - Y\otimes X' + Y\otimes X
' - Y\otimes Y' = X\otimes X' - Y\otimes Y'. \;\checkmark$$

Now examine each term:

- Since $X \succeq Y$, we have $X - Y \succeq 0$. Since $X' \succeq 0$, **Fact 1
** gives $(X - Y)\otimes X' \succeq 0$.
- Since $Y \succeq 0$ and $X' \succeq Y'$ gives $X' - Y' \succeq 0$, **Fact 1**
gives $Y \otimes (X' - Y') \succeq 0$.

By **Fact 2**, the sum of these two PSD matrices is PSD:

$$X \otimes X' - Y \otimes Y' = (X - Y)\otimes X' + Y\otimes(X' - Y') \succeq 0.
$$

Therefore $X \otimes X' \succeq Y \otimes Y'$, and the statement is **true**. $\
blacksquare$
