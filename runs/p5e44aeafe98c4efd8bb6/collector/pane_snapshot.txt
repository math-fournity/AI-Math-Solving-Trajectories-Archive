## Proof

**Claim:** Yes. Every open cover $\mathcal{U}$ of $S$ in $X$ admits an open, loc
ally finite refinement $\mathcal{V}$ (by sets open in $X$) that covers $S$.

---

**Setup.** Let $X$ be paracompact, $S\subseteq X$ closed, and $\mathcal{U}=\{U_\
alpha\}_{\alpha\in A}$ a family of open subsets of $X$ with $S\subseteq\bigcup_\
alpha U_\alpha$.

**Step 1 — Extend to an open cover of all of $X$.** Since $S$ is closed, $X\setm
inus S$ is open. Define
$$\mathcal{U}' \;=\; \{U_\alpha\}_{\alpha\in A}\;\cup\;\{X\setminus S\}.$$
This is an open cover of $X$: every point of $S$ lies in some $U_\alpha$, and ev
ery point of $X\setminus S$ lies in $X\setminus S$.

**Step 2 — Refine using paracompactness.** Because $X$ is paracompact, the open
cover $\mathcal{U}'$ admits an open, locally finite refinement $\mathcal{W}$ tha
t covers $X$. Concretely, $\mathcal{W}$ is a family of open subsets of $X$ such
that:

- (covers $X$) $\bigcup_{W\in\mathcal{W}} W = X$;
- (locally finite) every $x\in X$ has a neighborhood meeting only finitely many
$W\in\mathcal{W}$;
- (refines $\mathcal{U}'$) for every $W\in\mathcal{W}$ there exists $U'\in\mathc
al{U}'$ with $W\subseteq U'$.

**Step 3 — Discard the pieces refining $X\setminus S$.** Partition $\mathcal{W}$
 according to which element of $\mathcal{U}'$ each member refines. Set
$$\mathcal{V} \;=\; \{\, W\in\mathcal{W} : W\subseteq U_\alpha \text{ for some }
 \alpha\in A \,\}.$$
(Equivalently, $\mathcal{V}$ consists of those members of $\mathcal{W}$ that are
 not assigned to $X\setminus S$.) Each $V\in\mathcal{V}$ is open in $X$ and is c
ontained in some $U_\alpha$, so $\mathcal{V}$ refines $\mathcal{U}$.

**Step 4 — $\mathcal{V}$ covers $S$.** Let $s\in S$. Since $\mathcal{W}$ covers
$X$, pick $W\in\mathcal{W}$ with $s\in W$. If $W$ were assigned to $X\setminus S
$ (i.e. $W\subseteq X\setminus S$), then $s\in X\setminus S$, contradicting $s\i
n S$. Hence $W$ must be assigned to some $U_\alpha$, so $W\in\mathcal{V}$. Thus
$s\in\bigcup_{V\in\mathcal{V}}V$. Since $s\in S$ was arbitrary,
$$S\;\subseteq\;\bigcup_{V\in\mathcal{V}} V.$$

**Step 5 — $\mathcal{V}$ is locally finite.** $\mathcal{V}\subseteq\mathcal{W}$,
 and any subfamily of a locally finite family is locally finite. Hence every poi
nt of $X$ (in particular every point of $S$) has a neighborhood meeting only fin
itely many members of $\mathcal{V}$.

**Conclusion.** $\mathcal{V}$ is an open, locally finite family in $X$, refines
$\mathcal{U}$, and covers $S$. Therefore every open cover of $S$ in $X$ admits s
uch a refinement. $\blacksquare$
