## Solution

**Step 1: Determine the structure of $P$.**

Setting $u=1$ in the identity $P(ux,uy,uz)=u^2 F(y-x,z-x)$ gives
$$P(x,y,z)=F(y-x,\,z-x) \quad \text{for all real } x,y,z.$$

So $P$ depends only on $y-x$ and $z-x$. Setting $x=0$ gives $P(0,uy,uz)=u^2 F(y,
z)$, which shows $P(0,\cdot,\cdot)$ is homogeneous of degree $2$ and $F(y,z)=P(0
,y,z)$. Since $P$ is a polynomial, so is $F$, and homogeneity of degree $2$ forc
es
$$F(a,b)=\alpha a^2+\beta ab+\gamma b^2$$
for real constants $\alpha,\beta,\gamma$.

**Step 2: Determine the coefficients.**

Using $P(x,y,z)=F(y-x,z-x)=\alpha(y-x)^2+\beta(y-x)(z-x)+\gamma(z-x)^2$:

- $P(1,0,0)=F(-1,-1)=\alpha+\beta+\gamma=4$
- $P(0,1,0)=F(1,0)=\alpha=5$
- $P(0,0,1)=F(0,1)=\gamma=6$

From these: $\alpha=5$, $\gamma=6$, $\beta=4-5-6=-7$.

So $P(x,y,z)=5(y-x)^2-7(y-x)(z-x)+6(z-x)^2$.

**Verification:** $P(1,0,0)=5-7+6=4$ ✓, $P(0,1,0)=5$ ✓, $P(0,0,1)=6$ ✓.

**Step 3: Apply $P(A,B,C)=0$.**

Let $s=B-A$ and $t=C-A$. Then
$$5s^2-7st+6t^2=0.$$

Since $|B-A|=10\neq 0$, we have $s\neq 0$. Setting $r=t/s$:
$$6r^2-7r+5=0 \implies r=\frac{7\pm\sqrt{49-120}}{12}=\frac{7\pm\sqrt{-71}}{12}.
$$

Both roots are complex conjugates with
$$|r|^2=\frac{7^2+71}{12^2}=\frac{120}{144}=\frac{5}{6}.$$

**Step 4: Compute $|C-A|$.**

$$|C-A|=|t|=|r|\cdot|s|=\sqrt{\tfrac{5}{6}}\cdot 10=\frac{10\sqrt{30}}{6}=\frac{
5\sqrt{30}}{3}.$$

$$\boxed{|C-A|=\dfrac{5\sqrt{30}}{3}}$$
