# Problem

In a futuristic data center, a cooling chamber is shaped like a regular triangular prism. Inside this chamber, three identical spherical sensors, each with a radius of $r$ units, are positioned on the triangular floor. Each sensor is in direct contact with the other two sensors and also touches two of the chamber's vertical walls.

A fourth spherical sensor, also with a radius of $r$, is placed on top of the first three. This fourth sensor is sized such that it simultaneously touches all three vertical walls of the prism and the horizontal ceiling.

The total height of the cooling chamber can be expressed in the form:
$$r\left(a + \frac{\sqrt{b}}{c} + \sqrt{d + \frac{e\sqrt{f}}{g}}\right)$$

Where $a, b, c, d, e, f,$ and $g$ are positive integers, and the fractions are in simplest form. Find the value of the sum $a + b + c + d + e + f + g$.
