## Solution

Let $a = x - y > 0$ and $b = y - z > 0$, so $x - z = a + b$. The constraint beco
mes
$$ab(a+b) = 17,$$
and the expression to minimize is
$$E = \frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} + \frac{1}{a+b} = \frac{a+b}{ab} + \frac{1}{a+b}
 = \frac{(a+b)^2 + ab}{ab(a+b)} = \frac{(a+b)^2 + ab}{17}.$$

**Setting up the optimization.** Let $s = a+b$ and $p = ab$. Then $sp = 17$ and
we minimize
$$g(s) = s^2 + \frac{17}{s}, \qquad s > 0,$$
subject to the feasibility constraint $p \le s^2/4$ (AM–GM: $ab \le (a+b)^2/4$),
 i.e. $17/s \le s^2/4$, i.e. $s^3 \ge 68$.

**Unconstrained critical point.** $g'(s) = 2s - 17/s^2 = 0$ gives $s_0 = (17/2)^
{1/3} \approx 2.04$. But feasibility requires $s \ge 68^{1/3} \approx 4.08 > s_0
$. So the unconstrained minimizer is **infeasible**.

Since $g$ is decreasing for $s < s_0$ and increasing for $s > s_0$, on the feasi
ble region $s \ge 68^{1/3}$ the function $g$ is **strictly increasing**. Therefo
re the minimum is attained at the boundary $s = 68^{1/3}$, where the AM–GM const
raint is tight, i.e. $a = b$.

**Computing the minimum.** With $a = b$: $2a^3 = 17$, so $a = (17/2)^{1/3}$, and
$$E = \frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{2a} = \frac{5}{2a} = \frac{5}{2(17/2)
^{1/3}} = \frac{5}{2^{2/3}\cdot 17^{1/3}} = \frac{5}{\sqrt[3]{4\cdot 17}} = \fra
c{5}{\sqrt[3]{68}}.$$

**Extracting $a+b$.** Here $a = 5$ and $b = 68$. Since $68 = 2^2 \cdot 17$ is no
t divisible by the cube of any prime, the form $\frac{a}{\sqrt[3]{b}}$ is satisf
ied with $b$ cube-free.

$$a + b = 5 + 68 = \boxed{73}.$$
