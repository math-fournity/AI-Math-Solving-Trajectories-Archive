## Solution

**Setting up coordinates.** Place the right-angled isosceles triangle with the r
ight angle at $B = (0,0)$, $A = (4,0)$, and $C = (0,4)$. Then:
- The pedestrian path $AC$ lies on the line $x + y = 4$.
- The boundary segment $AB$ lies on the line $y = 0$ (with $0 \le x \le 4$).
- The boundary segment $BC$ lies on the line $x = 0$ (with $0 \le y \le 4$).

**Locating the vertices.** Since $N$ is the midpoint of $BC$:
$$N = (0,\, 2).$$

Since $M$ lies on $AC$, write $M = (m,\, 4-m)$ for some $m$. Since $K$ lies on $
AB$, write $K = (k,\, 0)$ for some $k$.

**Using the equilateral condition via rotation.** For $\triangle MNK$ to be equi
lateral, the vector $\vec{NK}$ must be the vector $\vec{NM}$ rotated by $\pm 60°
$.

$$\vec{NM} = (m,\; 2-m), \qquad \vec{NK} = (k,\; -2).$$

**Trying rotation by $+60°$** (counterclockwise): $(x,y) \mapsto \bigl(\tfrac{x}
{2} - \tfrac{y\sqrt{3}}{2},\; \tfrac{x\sqrt{3}}{2} + \tfrac{y}{2}\bigr)$ gives,
from the $y$-component:
$$-2 = \frac{m\sqrt{3}}{2} + \frac{2-m}{2} \implies m = -3(\sqrt{3}+1) < 0,$$
which places $M$ outside segment $AC$. Rejected.

**Trying rotation by $-60°$** (clockwise): $(x,y) \mapsto \bigl(\tfrac{x}{2} + \
tfrac{y\sqrt{3}}{2},\; -\tfrac{x\sqrt{3}}{2} + \tfrac{y}{2}\bigr)$ gives, from t
he $y$-component:
$$-2 = -\frac{m\sqrt{3}}{2} + \frac{2-m}{2} \implies -4 = -m\sqrt{3} + 2 - m \im
plies m(\sqrt{3}+1) = 6$$
$$\boxed{m = \frac{6}{\sqrt{3}+1} = 3(\sqrt{3}-1).}$$

From the $x$-component:
$$k = \frac{m}{2} + \frac{(2-m)\sqrt{3}}{2} = \frac{3\sqrt{3}-3}{2} + \frac{(5-3
\sqrt{3})\sqrt{3}}{2} = \frac{3\sqrt{3}-3+5\sqrt{3}-9}{2} = 4\sqrt{3}-6.$$

**Verifying the positions lie on the correct segments:**
- $M = \bigl(3\sqrt{3}-3,\; 7-3\sqrt{3}\bigr)$: sum of coordinates $= 4$ ✓; both
 coordinates in $[0,4]$ since $3\sqrt{3}-3 \approx 2.20$ and $7-3\sqrt{3} \appro
x 1.80$ ✓.
- $K = (4\sqrt{3}-6,\; 0)$: $4\sqrt{3}-6 \approx 0.93 \in [0,4]$ ✓.

**Computing the side length.**
$$|NM|^2 = m^2 + (2-m)^2 = (3\sqrt{3}-3)^2 + (5-3\sqrt{3})^2 = (36-18\sqrt{3}) +
 (52-30\sqrt{3}) = 88 - 48\sqrt{3}.$$

**Cross-check with $|NK|^2$:**
$$|NK|^2 = k^2 + 4 = (4\sqrt{3}-6)^2 + 4 = (48 - 48\sqrt{3} + 36) + 4 = 88 - 48\
sqrt{3}. \quad\checkmark$$

**Cross-check with $|KM|^2$:**
$$\vec{KM} = (3-\sqrt{3},\; 7-3\sqrt{3}), \quad |KM|^2 = (12-6\sqrt{3})+(76-42\s
qrt{3}) = 88-48\sqrt{3}. \quad\checkmark$$

All three sides are equal, confirming the triangle is equilateral with side leng
th squared $s^2 = 88 - 48\sqrt{3}$.

**Computing the area.** The area of an equilateral triangle with side $s$ is $\f
rac{\sqrt{3}}{4}s^2$:
$$\text{Area} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}(88 - 48\sqrt{3}) = \frac{88\sqrt{3} - 144}{4}
 = 22\sqrt{3} - 36.$$

**Numerical check:** $22\sqrt{3} - 36 \approx 38.105 - 36 = 2.105 \text{ m}^2 >
0$. ✓

$$\boxed{\text{Area} = 22\sqrt{3} - 36 \text{ square meters}}$$
