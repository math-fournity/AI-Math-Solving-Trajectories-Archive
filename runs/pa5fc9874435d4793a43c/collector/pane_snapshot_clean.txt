Looking at this problem, I need to find positive integers $x$ such that for ever
y $m \in \{2, 3, \dots, 51\}$: $x \bmod m \neq \left(\sum_{i=1}^{m-1} i\right) \
bmod m$.

## Step 1: Compute the Checksum Value mod m

The checksum is $\sum_{i=1}^{m-1} i = \frac{m(m-1)}{2}$.

**Case 1: $m$ is odd.** Then $m-1$ is even, so $\frac{m-1}{2}$ is an integer, an
d $\frac{m(m-1)}{2} = m \cdot \frac{m-1}{2} \equiv 0 \pmod{m}$.

**Case 2: $m$ is even, $m = 2k$.** Then $\frac{m(m-1)}{2} = \frac{2k(2k-1)}{2} =
 k(2k-1) = k \cdot m - k \equiv -k \equiv k = \frac{m}{2} \pmod{m}$.

So the conditions become:
- **Odd $m$**: $x \not\equiv 0 \pmod{m}$, i.e., $m \nmid x$.
- **Even $m = 2k$**: $x \not\equiv k \pmod{2k}$.

## Step 2: Derive that $x \equiv 0 \pmod{32}$

From the even-$m$ conditions with $m = 2, 4, 8, 16, 32$ (powers of 2):

| $m$ | Condition | Inference |
|-----|-----------|-----------|
| $2$ | $x \bmod 2 \neq 1$ | $x$ is even, so $x \bmod 4 \in \{0, 2\}$ |
| $4$ | $x \bmod 4 \neq 2$ | $x \equiv 0 \pmod{4}$, so $x \bmod 8 \in \{0, 4\}$
|
| $8$ | $x \bmod 8 \neq 4$ | $x \equiv 0 \pmod{8}$, so $x \bmod 16 \in \{0, 8\}$
 |
| $16$ | $x \bmod 16 \neq 8$ | $x \equiv 0 \pmod{16}$, so $x \bmod 32 \in \{0, 1
6\}$ |
| $32$ | $x \bmod 32 \neq 16$ | $x \equiv 0 \pmod{32}$ |

**So $x$ must be a multiple of $32$.**

## Step 3: All other even-$m$ conditions are automatically satisfied

Write $x = 32t$. For even $m = 2k$ (with $k$ not a power of 2), I need $32t \bmo
d 2k \neq k$.

**When $k$ is odd:** $32t \bmod 2k$ is always even (since $32t$ and $2k$ are bot
h even), but $k$ is odd. So the condition holds automatically.

**When $k$ is even:** I verified each case explicitly. For every even $k \in \{6

 the residue $32t \bmod 2k$ cycles through a set that never includes $k$. For ex
ample:
- $m=20$ ($k=10$): $32t \bmod 20$ cycles through $\{12, 4, 16, 8, 0\}$ — never $
10$. ✓
- $m=28$ ($k=14$): $32t \bmod 28$ cycles through $\{4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 0\}$ —

- $m=36$ ($k=18$): $32t \bmod 36$ cycles through $\{32, 28, 24, 20, 16, 12, 8, 4

- $m=44$ ($k=22$): $32t \bmod 44$ cycles through $\{32, 20, 8, 40, 28, 16, 4, 36

- $m=48$ ($k=24$): $32t \bmod 48$ cycles through $\{32, 16, 0\}$ — never $24$. ✓

All remaining even-$m$ conditions are automatically satisfied for any $t$.

## Step 4: Reduce to the odd-$m$ condition

Since $\gcd(32, m) = 1$ for odd $m$, the condition $m \nmid 32t$ is equivalent t
o $m \nmid t$.

So $t = x/32$ must **not be divisible by any odd number in $\{3, 5, 7, \dots, 51
\}$**. This is equivalent to: $t$ has no odd prime factor $\leq 47$ (the largest
 odd prime $\leq 51$).

## Step 5: Find the three smallest valid $t$

The valid $t$ values are those whose only prime factors are $2$ and/or primes $\
