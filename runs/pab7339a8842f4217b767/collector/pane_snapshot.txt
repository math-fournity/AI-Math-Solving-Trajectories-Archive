## Proof

Let $S^k$ denote the space of $k \times k$ real symmetric matrices equipped with
 the entry-wise (Frobenius/trace) inner product $\langle A, B\rangle = \mathrm{t
r}(AB)$, and let $S^k_+ = \{X \in S^k : X \succeq 0\}$ be the closed convex cone
 of positive semidefinite matrices. We determine the **outer normal cone** $N_{S
^k_+}(X)$ at every $X \in S^k_+$, defined by

$$N_{S^k_+}(X) = \{\, Y \in S^k : \langle Y,\, Z - X\rangle \le 0 \;\;\forall\,
Z \in S^k_+ \,\}.$$

---

### Step 1. Self-duality of $S^k_+$

With respect to $\langle A,B\rangle = \mathrm{tr}(AB)$, the cone $S^k_+$ is **se
lf-dual**:

$$(S^k_+)^* = \{\, W \in S^k : \langle W, Z\rangle \ge 0 \;\;\forall\, Z \succeq
 0 \,\} = S^k_+.$$

*Proof.* If $W \succeq 0$, then $\mathrm{tr}(WZ) \ge 0$ for all $Z \succeq 0$ (p
roduct of PSD matrices has nonneg trace). Conversely, if $\mathrm{tr}(WZ)\ge 0$
for all $Z\succeq 0$, taking $Z = vv^\top$ gives $v^\top W v \ge 0$ for all $v$,
 so $W \succeq 0$. $\square$

---

### Step 2. Normal cone of a closed convex cone

Since $S^k_+$ is a **closed convex cone**, for any $X \in S^k_+$:

$$N_{S^k_+}(X) = - (S^k_+)^* \,\cap\, X^\perp = -S^k_+ \,\cap\, \{Y : \langle Y,
 X\rangle = 0\}.$$

*Derivation.* Let $Y \in N_{S^k_+}(X)$. Since $0 \in S^k_+$, $\langle Y, -X\rang
le \le 0$, so $\langle Y, X\rangle \ge 0$. Since $S^k_+$ is a cone, $tZ \in S^k_
+$ for every $Z \in S^k_+$ and $t>0$; the inequality $\langle Y, tZ - X\rangle \
le 0$, i.e. $t\langle Y,Z\rangle \le \langle Y,X\rangle$, holding for **all** $t
>0$ forces $\langle Y,Z\rangle \le 0$ for every $Z \succeq 0$. Hence $Y \in -(S^
k_+)^* = -S^k_+$, i.e. $Y \preceq 0$. Combined with $\langle Y,X\rangle \ge 0$ a
nd $Y\preceq 0,\, X\succeq 0 \Rightarrow \langle Y,X\rangle \le 0$, we get $\lan
gle Y,X\rangle = 0$.

Conversely, if $Y \preceq 0$ and $\langle Y,X\rangle = 0$, then for $Z \succeq 0
$,
$$\langle Y, Z-X\rangle = \langle Y,Z\rangle - 0 \le 0,$$
so $Y \in N_{S^k_+}(X)$. $\square$

---

### Step 3. Simplification via the trace condition

For $Y \preceq 0$ and $X \succeq 0$ with $\mathrm{tr}(YX)=0$, write $Y = -Z$ wit
h $Z \succeq 0$. Then $\mathrm{tr}(ZX) = 0$. Since $Z,X \succeq 0$,

$$\mathrm{tr}(ZX) = \mathrm{tr}\!\big(Z^{1/2} X Z^{1/2}\big) = 0,\quad Z^{1/2}XZ
^{1/2}\succeq 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; Z^{1/2}XZ^{1/2}=0 \;\Longrightarrow\; ZX=0.$
$

Thus $\langle Y,X\rangle = 0 \iff YX = 0$ (equivalently, $\operatorname{range}(Y
) \subseteq \operatorname{null}(X)$).

---

### Step 4. The two cases

**Interior point** ($X \succ 0$, i.e. positive definite): $\operatorname{null}(X
)=\{0\}$, so $YX=0 \Rightarrow Y=0$.

$$\boxed{N_{S^k_+}(X) = \{0\}.}$$

**Boundary point** ($X \succeq 0$ singular, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=r<k$): $\dim
\operatorname{null}(X)=k-r$. The normal cone consists of all negative semidefini
te matrices supported on $\operatorname{null}(X)$:

$$\boxed{N_{S^k_+}(X) = \{\, Y \in S^k : Y \preceq 0,\; YX = 0 \,\} = \{\, Y \in
 S^k : Y \preceq 0,\; \operatorname{range}(Y)\subseteq \operatorname{null}(X) \,
\}.}$$

Choosing an orthonormal basis $\{u_1,\dots,u_{k-r}\}$ of $\operatorname{null}(X)
$ and completing it to a basis of $\mathbb{R}^k$, every such $Y$ has the block f
orm (in this basis)

$$Y = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 0 & -M \end{pmatrix}, \qquad M \in S^{k-r}_+,$$

so $N_{S^k_+}(X)$ is a cone linearly isomorphic to $-S^{k-r}_+$, of dimension $\
tfrac{(k-r)(k-r+1)}{2}$.

---

### Summary

The outer normal cone of $S^k_+$ in $S^k$ (with the entry-wise inner product) at
 a point $X \in S^k_+$ is

$$N_{S^k_+}(X) \;=\; -\,S^k_+ \,\cap\, X^\perp \;=\; \{\, Y \in S^k : Y \preceq
0,\;\; \mathrm{tr}(YX)=0 \,\} \;=\; \{\, Y \in S^k : Y \preceq 0,\;\; YX=0 \,\},
$$

which equals $\{0\}$ when $X \succ 0$ (interior), and is the cone of negative se
midefinite matrices whose range lies in $\operatorname{null}(X)$ when $X$ is a s
ingular boundary point of rank $r$, isomorphic to $-S^{k-r}_+$.
