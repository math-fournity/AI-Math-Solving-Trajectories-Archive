## Proof

**Answer: Yes.**

Let me first clarify the question. The hypothesis is that there exists a non-neg
ative integer $n$ such that
$$\operatorname{rank}_{A_P}(M_P) = n \quad \text{for every } P \in \operatorname
{Spec}(A),$$
where $\operatorname{rank}_{A_P}(M_P) = \dim_{\kappa(P)}(M \otimes_A \kappa(P))$
 and $\kappa(P) = A_P / PA_P$ is the residue field at $P$.

The question asks whether, for any prime $Q \in \operatorname{Spec}(A)$, the ana
logous property holds for the module $M_Q$ over the localized ring $A_Q$: that i
s, whether $\operatorname{rank}_{(A_Q)_{P'}}((M_Q)_{P'})$ is constant (and equal
 to $n$) for every $P' \in \operatorname{Spec}(A_Q)$.

---

**Key observation.** The prime ideals of $A_Q$ are in bijection with the prime i
deals $P$ of $A$ satisfying $P \subseteq Q$, via the map
$$P \longmapsto PA_Q, \qquad P \subseteq Q.$$
Under this bijection, the localization of $A_Q$ at the prime $PA_Q$ satisfies
$$(A_Q)_{PA_Q} \cong A_P,$$
and likewise for the module:
$$(M_Q)_{PA_Q} \cong M_P.$$
These are standard facts about iterated localization: localizing at $Q$ and then
 at $P$ (where $P \subseteq Q$) is the same as localizing directly at $P$.

---

**Verification.** Let $P' \in \operatorname{Spec}(A_Q)$ be any prime, correspond
ing to a prime $P \subseteq Q$ of $A$. Then:

$$\operatorname{rank}_{(A_Q)_{P'}}((M_Q)_{P'}) = \dim_{\kappa(P')}((M_Q) \otimes
_{A_Q} \kappa(P')),$$

where $\kappa(P') = (A_Q)_{P'}/P'(A_Q)_{P'}$. Under the identification $(A_Q)_{P
'} \cong A_P$, the residue field $\kappa(P')$ is isomorphic to $\kappa(P) = A_P/
PA_P$, and

$$(M_Q) \otimes_{A_Q} \kappa(P') \cong M_P \otimes_{A_P} \kappa(P) \cong M \otim
es_A \kappa(P).$$

Therefore:

$$\operatorname{rank}_{(A_Q)_{P'}}((M_Q)_{P'}) = \dim_{\kappa(P)}(M \otimes_A \k
appa(P)) = \operatorname{rank}_{A_P}(M_P) = n,$$

where the last equality uses the hypothesis that the rank is constantly $n$ on *
*all** of $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$, and in particular at the prime $P \subseteq
Q$.

Since $P' \in \operatorname{Spec}(A_Q)$ was arbitrary, we conclude:

$$\operatorname{rank}_{(A_Q)_{P'}}((M_Q)_{P'}) = n \quad \text{for every } P' \i
n \operatorname{Spec}(A_Q).$$

---

**Conclusion.** The rank function on $\operatorname{Spec}(A_Q)$ is the restricti
on of the rank function on $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ to the open subset $\{P \in
\operatorname{Spec}(A) : P \subseteq Q\}$ (identified with $\operatorname{Spec}(
A_Q)$). Since the rank function is constant on all of $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$,
its restriction to any subset is constant with the same value. Hence the propert
y holds for $M_Q$ over $A_Q$ for every prime $Q$ of $A$. $\blacksquare$
