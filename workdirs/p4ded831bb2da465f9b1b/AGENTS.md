# Problem

In a massive hydroponic research facility, a square plot is divided into a $2012 \times 2012$ grid of individual planting cells. Each cell contains a specific concentration of a nutrient solution, represented by a real number between $0$ and $1$, inclusive.

The facility manager is investigating the "Nutrient Saturation Rule." This rule applies whenever the entire $2012 \times 2012$ plot is partitioned into exactly two non-empty rectangular sub-plots by making a single straight cut along any grid line (either horizontally or vertically). The rule states that for any such partition, at least one of the two resulting rectangular sub-plots must have a total sum of nutrient concentrations less than or equal to $1$.

Assuming the grid is filled such that this rule is strictly satisfied for every possible valid cut, what is the maximum possible value for the sum of the nutrient concentrations across all cells in the entire $2012 \times 2012$ grid?
