## Setup

The three calibration laws are:
- (1) $x + y^2 + z^2 = a$
- (2) $x^2 + y + z^2 = a$
- (3) $x^2 + y^2 + z = a$

**Subtracting pairs:**
- (1)−(2): $x - x^2 + y^2 - y = 0 \Rightarrow (x-y)(1-x-y)=0$
- (1)−(3): $(x-z)(1-x-z)=0$
- (2)−(3): $(y-z)(1-y-z)=0$

So for each pair of variables, either they are **equal**, or their **sum is 1**.
