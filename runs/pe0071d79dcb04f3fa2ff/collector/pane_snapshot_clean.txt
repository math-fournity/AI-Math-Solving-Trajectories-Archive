**Optimal edge removal.** To achieve 2 odd-degree vertices, we must change 6 ver

Remove {a,b}, {c,d}, {e,f} (weight 1 each, total loss 3). The remaining degrees

The remaining graph is **connected** (g connects to a,b,c,d,h; a connects to e,f

**Self-loop insertion.** All 8 vertices have degree ≥ 2, so each is visited in t

**Total score:** (32 − 3) + 8 = **37**.

**Optimality proof.**
1. Any valid trail uses edges from a single connected component. The {±5} compon

2. In the main component, using all 8 vertices is optimal (dropping any vertex l

4. Removing exactly 3 weight-1 edges achieves the minimum loss of 3, giving non-

5. No alternative (closed trail requiring 4 removals, or dropping vertices) can

Therefore, the **maximum possible score is 37**.
