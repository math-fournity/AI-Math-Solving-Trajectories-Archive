# Problem

A specialized automated inspection drone is programmed to scan a high-security server farm organized in a perfect $n \times n$ grid of server racks. The drone begins its mission at one rack and moves to a vertically or horizontally adjacent rack every minute until it has visited every single rack in the grid exactly once. 

The security system logs the mission by assigning a "timestamp" to each rack, numbered from 1 to $n^2$, corresponding to the minute the drone occupied that specific location.

A network vulnerability is defined by the "Voltage Gradient" between any two racks that share a physical side. The gradient between two adjacent racks is calculated as the absolute difference between their assigned timestamps. Let $M$ be the maximum Voltage Gradient found across the entire grid after the drone completes its path.

What is the smallest possible value of $M$ that can be achieved through any valid path the drone takes?
