## Proof

Let \(X = 2^{I}\) be the Cantor space endowed with the product topology, where \
(I\) is an infinite set with \(|I| = m\). The **dual algebra** is the Boolean al
gebra \(\operatorname{Clop}(X)\) of clopen subsets of \(X\). We show \(|\operato
rname{Clop}(X)| = m\).

**Step 1 — The standard base has cardinality \(m\).**

The product topology on \(2^{I}\) has as a base the cylinder sets
\[
N(p) = \{x \in 2^{I} : x|_{\operatorname{dom}(p)} = p\},
\]
where \(p\) ranges over the finite partial functions \(p \colon I \rightharpoonu
p \{0,1\}\), i.e. over
\[
\operatorname{Fn}(I,2) = \bigcup_{F \in [I]^{<\omega}} \{0,1\}^{F}.
\]
Since \(I\) is infinite with \(|I| = m\), the collection \([I]^{<\omega}\) of fi
nite subsets of \(I\) has cardinality \(m\) (standard fact: \(|[κ]^{<\omega}| =
κ\) for every infinite cardinal \(κ\)). For each finite \(F\) with \(|F| = n\),
we have \(|\{0,1\}^{F}| = 2^{n} < \aleph_{0}\). Hence
\[
|\operatorname{Fn}(I,2)| = \sum_{n < \omega} |[I]^{n}|\cdot 2^{n} = m \cdot \ale
ph_{0} = m.
\]
So the base \(\{N(p) : p \in \operatorname{Fn}(I,2)\}\) has cardinality \(m\).

**Step 2 — Every clopen set is a finite union of basic cylinders.**

A clopen set \(C \subseteq 2^{I}\) is open, so it is a union of basic cylinder s
ets. It is also a closed subset of the compact space \(2^{I}\), hence \(C\) is i
tself compact. Therefore the open cover of \(C\) by basic cylinders admits a fin
ite subcover: \(C\) is a finite union of basic cylinders.

**Step 3 — Upper bound: \(|\operatorname{Clop}(2^{I})| \le m\).**

By Step 2, every clopen set is determined by a finite subset of \(\operatorname{
Fn}(I,2)\). The number of finite subsets of a set of cardinality \(m\) is
\[
|[\operatorname{Fn}(I,2)]^{<\omega}| = m,
\]
using again \(|[κ]^{<\omega}| = κ\) for infinite \(κ\). Hence \(|\operatorname{C
lop}(2^{I})| \le m\).

**Step 4 — Lower bound: \(|\operatorname{Clop}(2^{I})| \ge m\).**

Each basic cylinder \(N(p)\) is itself clopen (it is open by definition and its
complement is the union of the finitely many cylinders determined by the other f
unctions \(\operatorname{dom}(p) \to \{0,1\}\)). Distinct finite partial functio
ns \(p \neq q\) give distinct cylinders \(N(p) \neq N(q)\) (take any coordinate
in \(\operatorname{dom}(p)\,\triangle\,\operatorname{dom}(q)\) or where they dis
agree; a point extending the appropriate partial function witnesses non-equality
). Thus \(\{N(p) : p \in \operatorname{Fn}(I,2)\}\) is a family of \(m\) distinc
t clopen sets, giving \(|\operatorname{Clop}(2^{I})| \ge m\).

**Conclusion.**

Combining Steps 3 and 4,
\[
|\operatorname{Clop}(2^{I})| = m.
\]

The cardinality of the dual algebra of the Cantor space \(2^{I}\) is \(m\).

### PROOF COMPLETE
