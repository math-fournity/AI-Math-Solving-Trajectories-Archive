# Saddlepoint Approximation for the Density of X = Z + G

## Setup and Cumulant Generating Function

Let $Z \sim N(0, \sigma^2)$ independent of $G \sim \text{Gam}(a, c)$, where the
Gamma distribution uses the **shape–rate** parameterization with density
$$f_G(g) = \frac{c^a}{\Gamma(a)}\, g^{a-1} e^{-cg}, \quad g > 0, \quad a, c > 0.
$$

By independence, the c.g.f. of $X = Z + G$ is the sum of the individual c.g.f.s:

$$K_X(s) = K_Z(s) + K_G(s).$$

For $Z \sim N(0,\sigma^2)$:
$$K_Z(s) = \tfrac{1}{2}\sigma^2 s^2.$$

For $G \sim \text{Gam}(a,c)$, the m.g.f. is $M_G(s) = \left(\frac{c}{c-s}\right)
^a$ for $s < c$, so
$$K_G(s) = a\bigl[\log c - \log(c-s)\bigr] = -a\log\!\left(1 - \tfrac{s}{c}\righ
t), \quad s < c.$$

Therefore the **c.g.f. of $X$** is
$$\boxed{K_X(s) = \tfrac{1}{2}\sigma^2 s^2 - a\log\!\left(1 - \tfrac{s}{c}\right
), \qquad s < c.}$$

## Saddlepoint Equation

The saddlepoint $\hat{s} = \hat{s}(x)$ is defined by $K_X'(\hat{s}) = x$. We com
pute
$$K_X'(s) = \sigma^2 s + \frac{a}{c - s}.$$

Hence the **saddlepoint equation** is
$$\boxed{\sigma^2 \hat{s} + \frac{a}{c - \hat{s}} = x, \qquad \hat{s} < c.}$$

**Solving explicitly.** Multiplying through by $(c - \hat{s})$:
$$\sigma^2 \hat{s}(c - \hat{s}) + a = x(c - \hat{s}),$$
which rearranges to the quadratic
$$\sigma^2 \hat{s}^2 - (\sigma^2 c + x)\,\hat{s} + (xc - a) = 0.$$

The two roots are
$$\hat{s} = \frac{(\sigma^2 c + x) \pm \sqrt{(x - \sigma^2 c)^2 + 4\sigma^2 a}}{
2\sigma^2}.$$

The root with the **minus sign** is the unique solution in $(-\infty, c)$ (the $
+$ root always exceeds $c$), giving
$$\boxed{\hat{s}(x) = \frac{(\sigma^2 c + x) - \sqrt{(x - \sigma^2 c)^2 + 4\sigm
a^2 a}}{2\sigma^2}.}$$

*Consistency check:* at $x = \mathbb{E}[X] = a/c$, we get $\hat{s} = 0$ since $\
sigma^2 \cdot 0 + a/c = a/c$. ✓

## Second Derivative of the C.G.F.

Differentiating $K_X'(s)$ once more:
$$K_X''(s) = \sigma^2 + \frac{a}{(c - s)^2}.$$

Thus the **second derivative of the c.g.f.** is
$$\boxed{K_X''(s) = \sigma^2 + \frac{a}{(c - s)^2}.}$$

Evaluated at the saddlepoint:
$$K_X''(\hat{s}) = \sigma^2 + \frac{a}{(c - \hat{s})^2} > 0,$$
confirming that $K_X$ is strictly convex and the saddlepoint is a unique minimum
 of $K_X(s) - sx$.

## First-Order Saddlepoint Approximation (Daniels, 1954)

The first-order saddlepoint approximation to the density of $X$ at $x$ is

$$\boxed{f_X(x) \approx \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\, K_X''(\hat{s})}}\,\exp\!\Bigl(K_X(
\hat{s}) - \hat{s}\, x\Bigr),}$$

where:

- $\hat{s} = \hat{s}(x)$ is the unique real solution of the saddlepoint equation
 $\sigma^2 \hat{s} + \dfrac{a}{c - \hat{s}} = x$ with $\hat{s} < c$;
- $K_X(\hat{s}) = \tfrac{1}{2}\sigma^2 \hat{s}^2 - a\log\!\left(1 - \dfrac{\hat{
s}}{c}\right)$;
- $K_X''(\hat{s}) = \sigma^2 + \dfrac{a}{(c - \hat{s})^2}$.

### Explicit closed form

Substituting the closed-form $\hat{s}$, the approximation reads

$$f_X(x) \approx \left[2\pi\!\left(\sigma^2 + \frac{a}{(c - \hat{s})^2}\right)\r
ight]^{-1/2} \exp\!\left[\frac{\sigma^2 \hat{s}^2}{2} - a\log\!\left(1 - \frac{\
hat{s}}{c}\right) - \hat{s}\, x\right],$$

with
$$\hat{s} = \frac{(\sigma^2 c + x) - \sqrt{(x - \sigma^2 c)^2 + 4\sigma^2 a}}{2\
sigma^2}.$$

### Remarks on accuracy and domain

- The approximation is valid for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$ (since $X$ has support o
n all of $\mathbb{R}$ due to the Gaussian component), with $\hat{s}(x) \to c^-$
as $x \to +\infty$ and $\hat{s}(x) \to -\infty$ as $x \to -\infty$.
- The relative error of the first-order approximation is $O(n^{-1})$ in the i.i.
d. sum setting; here, for a single convolution it is generally accurate to withi
n a few percent near the mode, with the error controlled by the third and fourth
 standardized cumulants of $X$.
- A second-order (Barndorff-Nielsen) correction can be applied via the formula i
nvolving $K_X'''$ and $K_X''''$, but the problem requests only the first-order f
orm.
