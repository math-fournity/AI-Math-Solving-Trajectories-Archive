## Setting Up the Algebraic Structure

**Reformulation.** Let $g(x)=f(x)-x$ in $\mathbb{Z}/15\mathbb{Z}$. Then $f(x)=x+
g(x)$, and the condition becomes:
$$f(f(x))-2f(x)+x = g(x+g(x))-g(x)=0$$
So the constraint is $g(f(x))=g(x)$ — the displacement $g$ is constant on each o
rbit of $f$.

**Orbit structure.** If $g(x)=d$, then the orbit of $x$ under $f$ is $x, x+d, x+
2d, \ldots$, an arithmetic progression with common difference $d$. This orbit is
 the coset $x+\langle d\rangle$, with size $15/\gcd(d,15)$. Since $g$ is constan
t on the orbit, every element has displacement $d$, confirming the orbit is exac
tly this coset.
