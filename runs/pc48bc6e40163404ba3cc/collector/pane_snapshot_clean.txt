## Solution

We need the last four digits of $N = 9^1 + 9^2 + \cdots + 9^{400}$, i.e., $N \bm
od 10000$.

### Geometric series closed form

$$N = \frac{9(9^{400}-1)}{9-1} = \frac{9(9^{400}-1)}{8}.$$

Since $9 \equiv 1 \pmod{8}$, we have $9^{400} \equiv 1 \pmod{8}$, so $9^{400}-1$
 is divisible by $8$ and $N$ is an integer.

### Strategy

To compute $N \bmod 10000 = 9\cdot\frac{9^{400}-1}{8} \bmod 10000$, we first fin
d $9^{400} \bmod 80000$ (since $80000 = 8\cdot 10000$), then divide by $8$ and r
educe mod $10000$.

### Computing $9^{400} \bmod 80000$ via CRT

$80000 = 128 \times 625$ with $\gcd(128,625)=1$.

**Mod 128:** $9^2 = 81$, $9^4 = 81^2 = 6561 \equiv 33$, $9^8 \equiv 33^2 = 1089
\equiv 65$, $9^{16} \equiv 65^2 = 4225 \equiv 1 \pmod{128}$.

Since $400 = 16\cdot 25$, we get $9^{400} \equiv 1 \pmod{128}$.

**Mod 625:** Repeated squaring:
- $9^2 \equiv 81$
- $9^4 \equiv 81^2 = 6561 \equiv 311$
- $9^8 \equiv 311^2 = 96721 \equiv 471$
- $9^{16} \equiv 471^2 = 221841 \equiv 591$
- $9^{32} \equiv 591^2 = 349281 \equiv 531$
- $9^{64} \equiv 531^2 = 281961 \equiv 86$
- $9^{128} \equiv 86^2 = 7396 \equiv 521$
- $9^{256} \equiv 521^2 = 271441 \equiv 191 \pmod{625}$

Since $400 = 256+128+16$:
$$9^{400} \equiv 191\cdot 521\cdot 591 \pmod{625}.$$
$191\cdot 521 = 99511 \equiv 136$, then $136\cdot 591 = 80376 \equiv 376 \pmod{6
25}$.

So $9^{400} \equiv 376 \pmod{625}$.

### Combining via CRT

We need $x$ with $x\equiv 1\pmod{128}$ and $x\equiv 376\pmod{625}$.

Write $x = 128a+1$. Then $128a \equiv 375 \pmod{625}$.

The inverse of $128$ mod $625$: via extended Euclidean, $17\cdot 625 - 83\cdot 1
28 = 1$, so $128^{-1}\equiv -83\equiv 542\pmod{625}$.

$$a \equiv 375\cdot 542 = 203250 \equiv 125 \pmod{625}.$$

Thus $x = 128\cdot 125 + 1 = 16001$.

**Check:** $16001 \bmod 128 = 1$ ✓; $16001 \bmod 625 = 376$ ✓.

So $9^{400} \equiv 16001 \pmod{80000}$.

### Final computation

$$9^{400}-1 \equiv 16000 \pmod{80000},$$
$$\frac{9^{400}-1}{8} \equiv \frac{16000}{8} = 2000 \pmod{10000},$$
$$N = 9\cdot\frac{9^{400}-1}{8} \equiv 9\cdot 2000 = 18000 \equiv 8000 \pmod{100
00}.$$

### Verification of method

Small-case check: $9^1+9^2+9^3 = 9+81+729 = 819$, and $\frac{9(9^3-1)}{8}=\frac{
9\cdot 728}{8}=9\cdot 91=819$ ✓.

The last four digits of the total energy consumption $N$ are $\boxed{8000}$.
