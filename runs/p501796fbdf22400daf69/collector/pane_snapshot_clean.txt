$$P = \left(\frac{ab}{q},\; \frac{(1-a)b}{q}\right).$$

The ratio (actual areas have the same ratio):
$$\frac{S_{BCDE}}{S_{BPC}} = \frac{q/2}{ab/(2q)} = \frac{q^2}{ab} = \frac{16}{9}

So $ab = \frac{9}{16}q^2$.

$$S_{DEP} = 2\cdot\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{ab(1-a)(1-b)}{q} = \frac{ab(1-a)(1-b)}{q

$$S_{DEP} = \frac{\frac{9}{16}q^2 \cdot (1-q)}{q} = \frac{9}{16}\,q(1-q).$$

$$1 + \tfrac{9}{16}q \geq \tfrac{3}{2} \implies q \geq \tfrac{8}{9}.$$

$$S_{DEP}^{\max} = \frac{9}{16}\cdot\frac{8}{9}\cdot\frac{1}{9} = \frac{1}{18}.$
