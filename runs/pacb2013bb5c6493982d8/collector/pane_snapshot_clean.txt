# Solution

## Interpreting the constraints

The parenthetical defines "the port immediately to the left of port *k*" as **th
e port with the next higher number** (port *k*+1, wrapping so that the left of P
ort 15 is Port 1). The two examples confirm this wraparound convention: left of
Port 1 = Port 2, left of Port 15 = Port 1.

So "no *X* to the left of *Y*" forbids the clockwise transition *Y* → *X* (Y at
port *k*, X at port *k*+1):

| Constraint | Forbidden clockwise transition |
|---|---|
| 3: no Green left of Red | R → G |
| 4: no Red left of Blue | B → R |
| 5: no Blue left of Green | G → B |

**Allowed clockwise transitions** (stay, or step backward in the cycle R→B→G→R):
- From R: {R, B}
- From B: {B, G}
- From G: {G, R}

The wraparound transition Port 15 (G) → Port 1 (R) is **G→R**, which is allowed.
 ✓ No contradiction.

## Counting valid color patterns

Encode colors as states on the 3-cycle R→B→G→R, with R=0, B=1, G=2. Each transit
ion either **stays** (Δ=0) or **advances by +1 mod 3** (Δ=1). Since we traverse
the full circle and return to the starting color, the total number of advances m
ust be ≡ 0 (mod 3).

Consecutive equal colors form **runs**. Every advance starts a new run, and sinc
e advances only go +1 mod 3, the run-colors cycle in the fixed order **R, B, G,
R, B, G, …**. With *a* = 3*m* advances there are 3*m* runs: exactly *m* R-runs,
*m* B-runs, *m* G-runs.

**Boundary conditions are automatic:**
- Port 1 = R and Port 15 = G ≠ R, so the 15→1 transition is an advance; Port 1 i
s the start of the first R-run. ✓
- Port 15 is the last position, hence the end of the last G-run, so Port 15 = G.
 ✓

Each color's *m* runs have positive lengths summing to 5. The number of composit
ions of 5 into *m* positive parts is $\binom{4}{m-1}$. The three colors are inde
pendent, so for fixed *m* the count is $\binom{4}{m-1}^3$, with *m* ranging from
 1 to 5.

## Computing N

$$N = \sum_{m=1}^{5} \binom{4}{m-1}^{3} = \binom{4}{0}^{3}+\binom{4}{1}^{3}+\bin
om{4}{2}^{3}+\binom{4}{3}^{3}+\binom{4}{4}^{3}$$

$$= 1^{3}+4^{3}+6^{3}+4^{3}+1^{3} = 1+64+216+64+1 = 346.$$

## The $(5!)^3$ factor

For each valid color pattern, the 5 distinct Red modules can be permuted among t
he R-positions in 5! ways, similarly for Blue and Green, giving $N \cdot (5!)^3$
 total arrangements.

## Verification (small case)

For 2 of each color (6 ports, Port 1 = R, Port 6 = G): the formula gives $m=1: \
binom{1}{0}^3=1$ (pattern R,R,B,B,G,G) and $m=2: \binom{1}{1}^3=1$ (pattern R,B,
G,R,B,G). Both satisfy all transitions including wraparound G→R. ✓

$$\boxed{N = 346}$$
