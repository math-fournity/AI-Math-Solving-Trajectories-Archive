$$\{\lambda\, e_j : \lambda \geq 0,\; j = 1,\ldots,n\}.$$
We claim every vector $v \in \mathbb{R}^n$ is a linear combination (with real co
efficients) of elements from this set. Indeed, write $v = \sum_{j=1}^n a_j e_j$.
 For each $j$, set $\lambda_j = |a_j| \geq 0$ and $c_j = \operatorname{sgn}(a_j)

$$v = \sum_{j=1}^n c_j\,(\lambda_j\, e_j),$$
which expresses $v$ as a linear combination (with coefficients $c_j$) of non-neg
