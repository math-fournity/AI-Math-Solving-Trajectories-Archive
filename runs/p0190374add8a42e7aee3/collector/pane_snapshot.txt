Hmm, this is getting complicated. Let me use a cleaner approach.

For a reduced curve $E$ with irreducible components $E_1, \ldots, E_n$ and only
nodes as singularities:
$p_a(E) = \sum_{i=1}^n g_i + \delta$
where $g_i$ is the geometric genus of the normalization of $E_i$, and $\delta$
is the number of "extra" nodes beyond what's needed to connect the components.
More precisely, $\delta = $ (number of nodes) $- (n - 1)$ if the graph is
connected, which equals $b_1(\Gamma)$ where $\Gamma$ is the dual graph (assuming
each intersection point is a single node).
