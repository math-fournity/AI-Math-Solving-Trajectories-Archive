## Inversive Distance Condition

The inversive distance of concentric circles (nested case) is $I = -\frac{R_1^2+
R_2^2}{2R_1 R_2} = -\frac{1+k^2}{2k}$. Computing with $k = \frac{1-s}{1+s}$:

$$1+k^2 = \frac{2(1+s^2)}{(1+s)^2}, \quad 2k = \frac{2(1-s)}{1+s}$$

$$I_0 = -\frac{1+s^2}{(1+s)(1-s)} = -\frac{1+s^2}{c^2}$$

Since $|I_0| = \frac{1+s^2}{c^2} > 1$, the circles are non-intersecting (nested)

## Solving for Friendly Radii
