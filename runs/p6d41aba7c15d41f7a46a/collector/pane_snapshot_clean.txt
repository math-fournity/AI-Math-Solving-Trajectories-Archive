**Setup.** The surface $S$ is the boundary of the solid region
$$E = \{(x,y,z) : x^2+y^2 \le z \le 8 - x^2 - y^2\}.$$
The two bounding paraboloids meet where $x^2+y^2 = 8-x^2-y^2$, i.e. $x^2+y^2=4$
(at height $z=4$). Thus $S$ is a closed surface, and we may apply the **Divergen
ce Theorem**:
$$\iint_S \mathbf{F}\cdot d\mathbf{S} = \iiint_E (\nabla\cdot\mathbf{F})\,dV.$$

**Compute the divergence.**
$$\mathbf{F} = \langle e^{z^2},\; 2y+\sin(x^2z),\; 4z+\sqrt{x^2+9y^2}\rangle.$$
$$\nabla\cdot\mathbf{F} = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}e^{z^2} + \frac{\partial}{\
partial y}\bigl(2y+\sin(x^2z)\bigr) + \frac{\partial}{\partial z}\bigl(4z+\sqrt{
x^2+9y^2}\bigr) = 0 + 2 + 4 = 6.$$

**Compute the volume of $E$.** Using cylindrical coordinates $(r,\theta,z)$ with
 $x^2+y^2=r^2$:
$$r^2 \le z \le 8-r^2,\quad 0\le r\le 2,\quad 0\le\theta\le 2\pi.$$
$$\text{Vol}(E) = \int_0^{2\pi}\!\!\int_0^2\!\!\int_{r^2}^{8-r^2} r\,dz\,dr\,d\t
heta = 2\pi\int_0^2 r\bigl((8-r^2)-r^2\bigr)\,dr = 2\pi\int_0^2 (8r-2r^3)\,dr.$$
$$= 2\pi\left[4r^2 - \tfrac{1}{2}r^4\right]_0^2 = 2\pi\bigl(16 - 8\bigr) = 2\pi\
cdot 8 = 16\pi.$$

**Flux.**
$$\iint_S \mathbf{F}\cdot d\mathbf{S} = \iiint_E 6\,dV = 6\cdot 16\pi = \boxed{9
6\pi}.$$
