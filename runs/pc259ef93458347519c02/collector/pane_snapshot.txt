## Solution

**Setting up the model.** Each package independently goes to the North Wing with
 probability $p = \tfrac{1}{2}$. The manager earns a credit each time the cumula
tive count of North Wing packages reaches an odd integer: 1st, 3rd, 5th, 7th, …

**Identifying the stopping condition.** The 10th credit is earned when the North
 Wing count reaches the 10th odd positive integer, which is $2(10)-1 = 19$. So t
he facility stops exactly when the **19th North Wing package** arrives.

**Total packages = waiting time for the 19th success.** Let $X$ be the total num
ber of packages processed (North + South) until the 19th North Wing package. Thi
s is the number of independent Bernoulli($\tfrac12$) trials needed to obtain 19
successes — a negative binomial random variable with parameters $r = 19$ and $p
= \tfrac12$.

**Computing the expectation.** For a negative binomial counting the total trials
 until the $r$-th success,

$$\mathbb{E}[X] = \frac{r}{p}.$$

This follows by writing $X = G_1 + G_2 + \cdots + G_{19}$, where $G_i$ is the nu
mber of additional trials needed to get from the $(i-1)$-th success to the $i$-t
h success. Each $G_i$ is geometric with $\mathbb{E}[G_i] = 1/p = 2$, and the $G_
i$ are independent, so

$$\mathbb{E}[X] = 19 \cdot \frac{1}{1/2} = 19 \cdot 2 = 38.$$

**Expressing as a fraction.** We have $\mathbb{E}[X] = 38 = \tfrac{38}{1}$, with
 $\gcd(38, 1) = 1$, so $a = 38$ and $b = 1$.

$$a + b = 38 + 1 = \boxed{39}.$$
