## Proof

We evaluate
$$\lim_{L\to\infty}(\ln L)^2\Bigl[1-\bigl(1-e^{-\frac12(\sqrt L-2)}\bigr)^L\Bigr
].$$

**Setup.** Put $t=\sqrt L$ (so $L=t^2$, $t\to\infty$). Then
$$-\tfrac12(\sqrt L-2)=-\tfrac12(t-2)=1-\tfrac t2,$$
hence
$$e^{-\frac12(\sqrt L-2)}=e^{1-t/2}=e\cdot e^{-t/2}.$$
Let $x:=e\,e^{-t/2}$. For $t$ large enough, $0<x<1$, and $x\to 0$ exponentially.
 Also $\ln L=\ln(t^2)=2\ln t$, so $(\ln L)^2=4(\ln t)^2$.

The expression becomes
$$4(\ln t)^2\bigl[1-(1-x)^{t^2}\bigr],\qquad x=e\,e^{-t/2}.$$

**Non-negativity (lower bound).** Since $0\le 1-x\le 1$ for large $t$, we have $
0\le (1-x)^{t^2}\le 1$, so
$$0\le 1-(1-x)^{t^2}.$$
Thus the whole expression is $\ge 0$.

**Upper bound.** By Bernoulli's inequality / the union bound, for $x\in[0,1]$ an
d integer $L\ge 1$,
$$1-(1-x)^L\le Lx.$$
Applying this with $L=t^2$:
$$1-(1-x)^{t^2}\le t^2\,x=t^2\cdot e\,e^{-t/2}=e\,t^2 e^{-t/2}.$$
Therefore
$$0\le 4(\ln t)^2\bigl[1-(1-x)^{t^2}\bigr]\le 4e\,(\ln t)^2\,t^2\,e^{-t/2}=\frac
{4e\,t^2(\ln t)^2}{e^{t/2}}.$$

**The upper bound tends to $0$.** Exponentials dominate polynomials (and polylog
arithms): for any fixed $k$,
$$\frac{t^k}{e^{t/2}}\to 0\quad(t\to\infty),$$
and $(\ln t)^2=o(t^\varepsilon)$ for every $\varepsilon>0$. Concretely,
$$\frac{t^2(\ln t)^2}{e^{t/2}}\le \frac{t^3}{e^{t/2}}\xrightarrow[t\to\infty]{}0
$$
(for $t$ large, $(\ln t)^2\le t$), and $t^3/e^{t/2}\to 0$ by three applications
of L'Hôpital's rule (or the standard hierarchy of growth rates). Hence
$$\frac{4e\,t^2(\ln t)^2}{e^{t/2}}\longrightarrow 0.$$

**Conclusion.** By the squeeze theorem,
$$\lim_{L\to\infty}(\ln L)^2\Bigl[1-\bigl(1-e^{-\frac12(\sqrt L-2)}\bigr)^L\Bigr
]=\lim_{t\to\infty}4(\ln t)^2\bigl[1-(1-x)^{t^2}\bigr]=0.$$

The limit approaches zero, as required. $\blacksquare$
