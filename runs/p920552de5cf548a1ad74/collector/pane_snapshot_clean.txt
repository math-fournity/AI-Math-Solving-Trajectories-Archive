### Constructing a defeating partition

$$w(\pi(0)) \geq w(\pi(1)) \geq w(\pi(2)) \geq \cdots,$$

Define a partition $\{F_n : n \in \omega\}$ of $\omega$ into finite sets by:
$$F_n = \bigl\{\pi(j) : 2^n - 1 \leq j \leq 2^{n+1} - 2\bigr\}, \quad n \in \ome

**Properties of this partition:**
1. **Disjointness and coverage:** The intervals $[2^n - 1,\; 2^{n+1} - 2]$ for $

2. **Finiteness:** $|F_n| = 2^n$ for each $n$.
3. **Bounded max-weights:** The largest weight in $F_n$ is:
$$\max_{k \in F_n} w(k) = w\bigl(\pi(2^n - 1)\bigr) = \frac{1}{(2^n - 1) + 1} =

Therefore:
$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \max_{k \in F_n} w(k) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{2^n}
