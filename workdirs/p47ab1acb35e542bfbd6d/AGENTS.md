# Problem

In the competitive world of silicon chip manufacturing, two specialized engineering teams, Team $x$ and Team $y$, are assigned to optimize a new processor's architecture. Both teams must be assigned a positive integer number of processing cores, denoted by $x$ and $y$ respectively.

The efficiency of the overall system is measured by a specific "Stability Index," calculated using the formula $x^2 + y^2 - 2017xy$. The quality control department has imposed the following strict operational constraints:
1. The Stability Index must result in a positive value.
2. Due to resonance interference patterns, the number of cores assigned to Team $x$ cannot be a perfect square.
3. Similarly, the number of cores assigned to Team $y$ cannot be a perfect square.
4. Finally, the resulting Stability Index itself must not be a perfect square.

Engineers are tasked with configuring the system to be as close to the threshold of instability as possible. Given these constraints, what is the minimum possible integer value for the Stability Index?
