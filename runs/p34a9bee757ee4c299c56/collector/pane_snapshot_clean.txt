## Solution

We seek the area of the "curtain" surface that rises from the semicircle $C: y=\
sqrt{4-x^2}$ in the $xy$-plane up to the height $z = 3x^4 y$.

For a surface that is a vertical curtain over a curve $C$ in the $xy$-plane with
 height $z = f(x,y)$, the surface area is given by the line integral

$$A = \int_C f(x,y)\, ds.$$

Here $f(x,y) = 3x^4 y$ and $C$ is the upper semicircle $x^2+y^2=4$, $y\ge 0$.

**Parametrize $C$:** $x = 2\cos t,\; y = 2\sin t$, with $t\in[0,\pi]$.

Then
$$ds = \sqrt{\left(\frac{dx}{dt}\right)^2+\left(\frac{dy}{dt}\right)^2}\,dt = \s
qrt{4\sin^2 t+4\cos^2 t}\,dt = 2\,dt.$$

The height along $C$ is
$$3x^4 y = 3(2\cos t)^4(2\sin t) = 3\cdot 16\cos^4 t\cdot 2\sin t = 96\cos^4 t\s
in t.$$

**Compute the integral:**
$$A = \int_C 3x^4 y\, ds = \int_0^{\pi} 96\cos^4 t\sin t\cdot 2\,dt = 192\int_0^
{\pi}\cos^4 t\sin t\,dt.$$

Substitute $u = \cos t$, $du = -\sin t\,dt$. When $t=0$, $u=1$; when $t=\pi$, $u
=-1$:

$$\int_0^{\pi}\cos^4 t\sin t\,dt = \int_{1}^{-1} u^4(-du) = \int_{-1}^{1} u^4\,d
u = \left[\frac{u^5}{5}\right]_{-1}^{1} = \frac{1}{5}-\left(-\frac{1}{5}\right)=
\frac{2}{5}.$$

Therefore
$$A = 192\cdot\frac{2}{5} = \frac{384}{5}.$$

$$\boxed{A = \dfrac{384}{5}}$$
