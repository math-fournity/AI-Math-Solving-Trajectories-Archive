## Proof

centered at the origin. We seek the Dirichlet Green's function $G_D(x,\xi)$ sati
sfying

$$\Delta_x G_D = -\delta(x-\xi), \quad x \in D; \qquad G_D\big|_{|x|=a} = 0.$$

**Step 1 — Known half-space Green's function.** For the half-space $H = \{y : y_
3 > 0\}$, the Dirichlet Green's function is

$$G_H(y,\eta) = \frac{1}{4\pi}\left(\frac{1}{|y-\eta|} - \frac{1}{|y-\tilde\eta|
}\right),$$

where $\tilde\eta = (\eta_1,\eta_2,-\eta_3)$ is the reflection of $\eta$ across
the plane $y_3=0$.

**Step 2 — Inversion mapping the sphere to a half-space.** Fix the source point
$\xi \in D$. Choose an inversion centered at a point $P$ on the boundary sphere
$|x|=a$, with power $k^2$ (to be fixed). The inversion is

$$x \;\longmapsto\; x' = P + \frac{k^2(x-P)}{|x-P|^2}.$$

Since $P$ lies on $|x|=a$, the sphere $|x|=a$ (passing through the center of inv
ersion) maps to a plane. Choose $k^2 = 2a^2$ and place coordinates so that $P=(0
,0,-a)$ (the "south pole"); then the image of $|x|=a$ is the plane $y_3 = 0$, an
d the interior $D$ maps to the half-space $H = \{y_3 > 0\}$. The source point ma
ps to $\xi' \in H$.

**Step 3 — Transformation law (Kelvin transform).** Under inversion with center
$P$ and power $k^2$, the fundamental solution satisfies

$$\frac{1}{|x-\xi|} = \frac{|x'-P|\,|\xi'-P|}{k^2}\cdot\frac{1}{|x'-\xi'|},$$

and harmonic functions transform by the Kelvin rule: $u(x)$ harmonic $\iff$ $\fr
ac{|x'-P|}{k}\,u(x)$ harmonic in $x'$. Consequently the Green's functions are re
lated by

$$G_D(x,\xi) = \frac{|x'-P|\,|\xi'-P|}{k^2}\, G_H(x',\xi'). \tag{$\star$}$$

Indeed, the right-hand side is the sum of the fundamental solution $\frac{1}{4\p
i|x-\xi|}$ (from the identity above) and a function harmonic in $x$ (Kelvin tran
sform of the harmonic part of $G_H$); and it vanishes on $\partial D$ since $G_H
$ vanishes on $\partial H$.

**Step 4 — Apply the half-space formula.** Substituting the known $G_H$ into $(\

$$G_D(x,\xi) = \frac{|x'-P|\,|\xi'-P|}{k^2}\cdot\frac{1}{4\pi}\left(\frac{1}{|x'
-\xi'|} - \frac{1}{|x'-\tilde\xi'|}\right).$$

Using $|x'-\xi'| = \dfrac{k^2\,|x-\xi|}{|x'-P|\,|\xi'-P|}$ (and the analogous id
entity for the reflected point), the prefactors cancel to give

$$G_D(x,\xi) = \frac{1}{4\pi}\left(\frac{1}{|x-\xi|} - \frac{|\eta-P|}{|\xi-P|}\
cdot\frac{1}{|x-\eta|}\right),$$

where $\eta$ is the pre-image of $\tilde\xi'$ under the inversion.

**Step 5 — Identify $\eta$ and the ratio.** A direct computation (inverting $\ti
lde\xi'$, the reflection of $\xi'$ across $y_3=0$) shows that $\eta$ is precisel

$$\eta = \xi^* := \frac{a^2}{|\xi|^2}\,\xi, \qquad |\eta| = \frac{a^2}{|\xi|} >
a.$$

The ratio is independent of the choice of $P$ (as it must be, since $G_D$ cannot
 depend on the auxiliary inversion). Indeed, using $|P|=a$ and $|\eta|=a^2/|\xi|
$:

$$|\eta - P|^2 = |\eta|^2 - 2\,\eta\!\cdot\! P + |P|^2 = \frac{a^4}{|\xi|^2} - \
frac{2a^2}{|\xi|^2}(\xi\!\cdot\! P) + a^2 = \frac{a^2}{|\xi|^2}\bigl(|\xi|^2 - 2
\,\xi\!\cdot\! P + a^2\bigr) = \frac{a^2}{|\xi|^2}\,|\xi-P|^2.$$

Hence

$$\frac{|\eta-P|}{|\xi-P|} = \frac{a}{|\xi|}.$$

**Step 6 — Final formula.** Substituting,

$$\boxed{\;G_D(x,\xi) = \frac{1}{4\pi}\left(\frac{1}{|x-\xi|} - \frac{a}{|\xi|}\
cdot\frac{1}{\bigl|x - \frac{a^2}{|\xi|^2}\xi\bigr|}\right),\qquad x,\xi \in D.\
;}$$

**Verification of the boundary condition.** On $|x|=a$, with $\xi^* = \frac{a^2}
{|\xi|^2}\xi$:

$$|x-\xi^*|^2 = a^2 - \frac{2a^2}{|\xi|^2}(x\!\cdot\!\xi) + \frac{a^4}{|\xi|^2}
= \frac{a^2}{|\xi|^2}\Bigl(|\xi|^2 - 2\,x\!\cdot\!\xi + a^2\Bigr) = \frac{a^2}{|
\xi|^2}\,|x-\xi|^2.$$

Therefore $\frac{a}{|\xi|}\cdot\frac{1}{|x-\xi^*|} = \frac{1}{|x-\xi|}$ on $|x|=
a$, so $G_D\big|_{|x|=a}=0$. $\checkmark$

The singularity at $x=\xi$ is exactly $\frac{1}{4\pi|x-\xi|}$ (the free-space fu
ndamental solution), and the second term is harmonic in $x$ throughout $D$ (sinc
