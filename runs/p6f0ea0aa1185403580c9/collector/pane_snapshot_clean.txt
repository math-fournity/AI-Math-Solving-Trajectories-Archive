- $A=(0,0,0)$, $B=(15,0,0)$, $D=(0,12,0)$, $C=(15,12,0)$,

$$t=\frac{(A-A')\cdot(D-A')}{|D-A'|^2}=\frac{(0,0,-9)\cdot(0,12,-9)}{144+81}=\fr
ac{81}{225}=\frac9{25},$$
