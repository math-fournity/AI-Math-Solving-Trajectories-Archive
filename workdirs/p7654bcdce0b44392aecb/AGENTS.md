# Problem

In a remote industrial sector, there are three storage silos containing $a, b$, and $c$ kiloliters of liquid fuel respectively. Two rival technicians, Ivan and Peter, are engaged in a decommissioning protocol. They take turns performing the following operation, with Ivan taking the first turn:

The technician on duty must completely drain one silo of his choice. Then, he must redistribute the total remaining fuel from the other two silos into the three silos such that each silo contains a positive integer amount of kiloliters (at least 1 kiloliter per silo).

The technician who is forced into a position where he leaves exactly 1 kiloliter in each of the three silos loses the competition.

Let $S$ be the set of all initial configurations $(a, b, c)$ where the fuel levels $a, b, c$ are integers such that $1 \le a, b, c \le 100$, and for which Peter has a guaranteed winning strategy regardless of Ivan's choices. Calculate the total number of such configurations in the set $S$.
