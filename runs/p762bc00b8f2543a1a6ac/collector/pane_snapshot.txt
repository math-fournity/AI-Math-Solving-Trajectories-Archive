## Part 1: The Riemann Function

**The operator and its adjoint.** The operator is $L(u) = u_{tt} - a^2 u_{xx}$.
Since the coefficients are constant, $L$ is formally self-adjoint: $L^* = L$.

**Definition of the Riemann function.** The Riemann function $R(x,t;\xi,\tau)$ a
ssociated with the point $(\xi,\tau)$ satisfies:
1. $L^*_{(x,t)} R = R_{tt} - a^2 R_{xx} = 0$ inside the characteristic triangle
$D$ with vertex $(\xi,\tau)$ and base on $t=0$;
2. $R = 1$ on the two characteristics through $(\xi,\tau)$, namely $x+at = \xi+a
\tau$ and $x-at = \xi-a\tau$.

**Solving for $R$.** The two characteristics through $(\xi,\tau)$ are:
- $\Gamma_1: x + at = \xi + a\tau$ (right-going),
- $\Gamma_2: x - at = \xi - a\tau$ (left-going).

Since $L$ has constant coefficients, the function $R \equiv 1$ satisfies both co
nditions:
- $L(1) = 0 - 0 = 0$ trivially,
- $R = 1$ on both characteristics.

**The Riemann function is:**
$$\boxed{R(x,t;\,\xi,\tau) = 1.}$$

## Part 2: Solving the Cauchy Problem via the Riemann Method

**Setup.** We solve $u_{tt} - a^2 u_{xx} = f(x,t)$ with $u(x,0)=\phi(x)$, $u_t(x
,0)=\psi(x)$.

**The Lagrange–Green identity.** The key identity is:
$$v\,L(u) - u\,L(v) = \frac{\partial}{\partial t}(v\,u_t - u\,v_t) - a^2\frac{\p
artial}{\partial x}(v\,u_x - u\,v_x).$$

Setting $v = R = 1$ (so $v_x = v_t = 0$ and $L(v)=0$), and integrating over the
characteristic triangle $D$ with vertices $(\xi-a\tau,\,0)$, $(\xi+a\tau,\,0)$,
$(\xi,\,\tau)$:

$$\iint_D f\,dx\,dt = \oint_{\partial D}\bigl[-a^2\,u_x\,dt - u_t\,dx\bigr],$$

by Green's theorem (counterclockwise orientation).

**Evaluating the boundary integral on each part of $\partial D$:**

$$\int_{\Gamma_1} = \int_0^\tau\bigl[-a^2 u_x + a\,u_t\bigr]dt = a\int_0^\tau(u_

$$\int_{\Gamma_1} = a\bigl[u(\xi,\tau) - u(\xi+a\tau,0)\bigr] = a\bigl[u(\xi,\ta
