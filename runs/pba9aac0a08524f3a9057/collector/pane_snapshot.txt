### Step 4. Convexity (Second Variation) — Verification that the Stationary Poin

The second variation at $u$ in direction $v$ (with $v|_{S_1}=0$) is:

$$\delta^2 J[v] = 2\int_{\Omega}|\nabla v|^2\,d\Omega - 2p\int_{S_3}v^2\,dS.$$

$$\delta^2 J[v] \geq 2\int_{\Omega}|\nabla v|^2\,d\Omega \geq 0,$$

$$\int_{S_3} v^2\,dS \leq C\int_{\Omega}|\nabla v|^2\,d\Omega \quad \forall\, v|

Hence $\delta^2 J[v] \geq 2(1 - pC)\int_{\Omega}|\nabla v|^2\,d\Omega$. If $p <

- **Coercivity:** Since $u = g$ on $S_1$ with $|S_1|>0$, the Poincaré inequality
