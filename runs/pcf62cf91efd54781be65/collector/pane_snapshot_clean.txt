## Solution

**Setup.** Let the square have side length 2, with coordinates $A=(0,2)$, $B=(0,
0)$, $C=(2,0)$, $D=(2,2)$. The midpoint of $CD$ is $M=(2,1)$.

**The fold line is the perpendicular bisector of $\overline{BM}$.**

When paper is folded so that $B$ maps to $M$, every point on the fold line is eq
uidistant from $B$ and $M$ — this is exactly the perpendicular bisector of segme
nt $BM$.

- $B=(0,0)$, $M=(2,1)$.
- Midpoint of $BM$: $\left(1,\tfrac{1}{2}\right)$.
- Direction of $BM$: $(2,1)$, so the perpendicular direction is $(1,-2)$.

The fold line passes through $\left(1,\tfrac{1}{2}\right)$ with direction $(1,-2
)$:
$$\bigl(1+t,\;\tfrac{1}{2}-2t\bigr).$$

**Intersection with $BC$.** Side $BC$ lies on $y=0$ ($0\le x\le 2$). Setting $\t
frac{1}{2}-2t=0$ gives $t=\tfrac{1}{4}$, hence
$$x = 1 + \tfrac{1}{4} = \tfrac{5}{4}.$$

So the fold line meets $BC$ at the point $P=\left(\tfrac{5}{4},\,0\right)$.

**The ratio.** Since $B=(0,0)$ and $C=(2,0)$:
$$BP = \tfrac{5}{4}, \qquad PC = 2 - \tfrac{5}{4} = \tfrac{3}{4}.$$
$$BP : PC = \tfrac{5}{4} : \tfrac{3}{4} = 5 : 3.$$

**Verification (reflection check).** Reflecting $B=(0,0)$ across the fold line s
hould yield $M=(2,1)$. The fold line has equation $2x - y = \tfrac{3}{2}$ (from
point-slope through $(1,\tfrac12)$ with slope $-2$). The reflection of $(x_0,y_0
)$ across $ax+by+c=0$ is
$$(x_0,y_0) - \frac{2(ax_0+by_0+c)}{a^2+b^2}(a,b).$$
With $a=2,b=-1,c=-\tfrac32$ and $(x_0,y_0)=(0,0)$: the fraction is $\frac{2(0-\t
frac32)}{5} = -\tfrac{3}{5}$, giving
$$(0,0) - (-\tfrac{3}{5})(2,-1) = \left(\tfrac{6}{5},-\tfrac{3}{5}\right).$$

Hmm — that gives $(\tfrac65,-\tfrac35)$, not $(2,1)$. Let me recheck the line eq
uation.

Line through $(1,\tfrac12)$ with slope $-2$: $y-\tfrac12 = -2(x-1)$, i.e., $y =
-2x+\tfrac52$, i.e., $2x+y-\tfrac52=0$. So $a=2, b=1, c=-\tfrac52$.

Reflection of $(0,0)$: fraction $= \frac{2(0-\tfrac52)}{5} = -1$, giving
$$(0,0) - (-1)(2,1) = (2,1) = M. \quad\checkmark$$

The fold line equation is $2x+y=\tfrac52$. Intersection with $y=0$: $x=\tfrac54$
, confirming $P=(\tfrac54,0)$.

**Answer.** The fold line divides side $BC$ in the ratio

$$\boxed{BP:PC = 5:3}.$$
