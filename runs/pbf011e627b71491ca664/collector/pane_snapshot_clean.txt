## Solution

We evaluate
$$f(u) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{e^{-itu}}{\sqrt{1+t^2}}\,dt.$$

### Step 1: Reduction to a cosine integral

Write $e^{-itu} = \cos(tu) - i\sin(tu)$. The factor $\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+t^2}}$ is
even in $t$; $\cos(tu)$ is even while $\sin(tu)$ is odd. Hence the sine integral
 vanishes by symmetry and

$$f(u) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{\cos(tu)}{\sqrt{1+t^2}}\,dt = 2\int_{0}^{\
infty}\frac{\cos(tu)}{\sqrt{1+t^2}}\,dt = 2\,h(|u|),$$

where we set $h(x) := \int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{\cos(xt)}{\sqrt{1+t^2}}\,dt$ for $x
>0$ (the value depends only on $|u|$ since $\cos$ is even).

### Step 2: Schwinger parametrization

Use the identity $\frac{1}{\sqrt{a}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi}}\int_{0}^{\infty} s^{
-1/2}e^{-as}\,ds$ for $a>0$ with $a = 1+t^{2}$:

$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+t^{2}}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi}}\int_{0}^{\infty} s^{-1/2}e^{-(
1+t^{2})s}\,ds.$$

Substituting (the interchange of integrals is justified by absolute convergence
of the resulting double integral for $x>0$):

$$h(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi}}\int_{0}^{\infty} s^{-1/2}e^{-s}\left(\int_{0}^{\in
fty} e^{-st^{2}}\cos(xt)\,dt\right)ds.$$

### Step 3: Evaluate the inner Gaussian–cosine integral

$$\int_{0}^{\infty} e^{-st^{2}}\cos(xt)\,dt = \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{s}}\,e
^{-x^{2}/(4s)}, \qquad s>0.$$

Therefore

$$h(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi}}\int_{0}^{\infty} s^{-1/2}e^{-s}\cdot\frac{1}{2}\sq
rt{\frac{\pi}{s}}\,e^{-x^{2}/(4s)}\,ds = \frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{\infty} s^{-1}\,e^
{-s - x^{2}/(4s)}\,ds.$$

### Step 4: Identify with $K_{0}$

The modified Bessel function of the second kind of order zero has the integral r
epresentation (valid for $z>0$):

$$K_{0}(z) = \frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{\infty} s^{-1}\,e^{-s - z^{2}/(4s)}\,ds.$$

With $z = x > 0$, the integral in Step 3 is exactly $K_{0}(x)$. Hence

$$h(x) = K_{0}(x), \qquad x > 0.$$

### Step 5: Assemble the result

Combining Steps 1 and 4,

$$f(u) = 2\,h(|u|) = 2\,K_{0}(|u|).$$

At $u=0$ the integral diverges logarithmically (consistent with $K_{0}(x)\sim -\
ln(x/2)-\gamma$ as $x\to 0^{+}$), and for all $u\neq 0$ the formula holds.

### Final Answer

$$\boxed{\,f(u) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{e^{-itu}}{\sqrt{1+t^{2}}}\,dt = 2
\,K_{0}(|u|)\,}$$

where $K_{0}$ is the modified Bessel function of the second kind of order zero.
