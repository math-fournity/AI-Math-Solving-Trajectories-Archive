## Setting Up the Structure

**Key relation:** $f(f(n))=2n$ implies $f$ is injective, and $f(2n)=2f(n)$ (sinc

So $f$ is determined by its values on odd numbers: writing $n=2^a m$ ($m$ odd),
$f(n)=2^a f(m)$.

**Chain structure:** Odd numbers are partitioned into pairs $\{a,b\}$. For each
pair, two orientations exist:
- **Option A:** $f(a)=b,\; f(b)=2a,\; f(2a)=2b,\; f(2b)=4a,\;\ldots$ Chain: $a\t
o b\to 2a\to 2b\to 4a\to 4b\to\cdots$
- **Option B:** $f(b)=a,\; f(a)=2b,\; f(2b)=2a,\; f(2a)=4b,\;\ldots$ Chain: $b\t
o a\to 2b\to 2a\to 4b\to 4a\to\cdots$

Both cover the same set $\{a\cdot 2^k,\, b\cdot 2^k : k\ge 0\}$.
