# Rank of the First Homology of the Double Branched Cover of a Knot

**Claim.** For every knot $K$ in $S^3$, the double branched cover $\Sigma_2(K)$
satisfies $\operatorname{rank} H_1(\Sigma_2(K);\,\mathbb{Z}) = 0$.

---

## Proof

Let $K \subset S^3$ be a knot and let $\Sigma = \Sigma_2(K)$ denote the double b
ranched cover of $S^3$ branched along $K$.

### Step 1. $\Sigma$ is a closed orientable 3-manifold

This is standard: the double branched cover of $S^3$ along a knot is a closed, c
onnected, orientable 3-manifold.

### Step 2. $|H_1(\Sigma;\,\mathbb{Z})| = |\Delta_K(-1)|$ (Fox–Goeritz)

By the classical theorem of Fox and Goeritz, the first homology of the double br
anched cover is presented by the **Goeritz matrix** $G$ associated to any diagra
m of $K$, and

$$|H_1(\Sigma;\,\mathbb{Z})| = |\det G| = |\Delta_K(-1)|,$$

where $\Delta_K(t)$ is the Alexander polynomial of $K$. In particular, $H_1(\Sig
ma;\,\mathbb{Z})$ is finite if and only if $\Delta_K(-1) \neq 0$.

### Step 3. $\Delta_K(-1)$ is odd (hence nonzero) for every knot $K$

Let $V$ be a Seifert matrix for $K$, associated to a Seifert surface $F$ of genu
s $g$. Then $V$ is a $2g \times 2g$ integer matrix and

$$\Delta_K(t) = \det(V - t\,V^T).$$

Evaluating at $t = -1$:

$$\Delta_K(-1) = \det(V + V^T).$$

Now observe the key congruence **modulo 2**:

$$V + V^T \equiv V - V^T \pmod{2},$$

since $+1 \equiv -1 \pmod{2}$. The matrix $V - V^T$ is the **intersection form**
 on $H_1(F;\,\mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}^{2g}$. Because $F$ is a compact orient
ed surface with a single boundary component (as $K$ is a knot, i.e., a 1-compone
nt link), the intersection form $V - V^T$ is a **unimodular symplectic form**:

$$\det(V - V^T) = \pm 1.$$

Therefore:

$$\det(V + V^T) \equiv \det(V - V^T) \equiv \pm 1 \pmod{2},$$

which gives

$$\Delta_K(-1) = \det(V + V^T) \text{ is odd.}$$

In particular, $\Delta_K(-1) \neq 0$.

### Step 4. $H_1(\Sigma;\,\mathbb{Z})$ is finite, so its rank is $0$

Since $\Delta_K(-1) \neq 0$, Step 2 gives

$$|H_1(\Sigma;\,\mathbb{Z})| = |\Delta_K(-1)| < \infty.$$

Thus $H_1(\Sigma;\,\mathbb{Z})$ is a **finite abelian group**. The rank of a fin
ite abelian group—i.e., the dimension of $H_1(\Sigma;\,\mathbb{Z}) \otimes \math
bb{Q}$ over $\mathbb{Q}$—is $0$.

---

### Conclusion

$$\boxed{\operatorname{rank}\, H_1\!\bigl(\Sigma_2(K);\,\mathbb{Z}\bigr) = 0}$$

for every knot $K$ in $S^3$. The double branched cover of a knot is always a **r
ational homology sphere** (in fact a $\mathbb{Z}/2$-homology sphere, since $|\De
lta_K(-1)|$ is odd), so its first homology is finite and has trivial free part.
