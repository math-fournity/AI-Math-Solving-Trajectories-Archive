# Problem

In the city of Arithmos, the Urban Planning Committee manages a row of $n$ designated community plots, numbered sequentially from 1 to $n$. A collection of these plots is designated as a "Balanced Zone" if it satisfies a specific stability requirement: for any two plots $a$ and $b$ within the collection, if the midpoint between them is an integer (meaning they are separated by an even distance), that midpoint plot must also be included in the collection. Let $A(n)$ represent the total number of different ways to form a Balanced Zone using any combination of the $n$ available plots (including the empty collection).

The city engineers are interested in the growth rate of these configurations. They specifically want to identify the capacities $n$ where the second-order difference of the count—defined by the formula $A(n+2) - 2A(n+1) + A(n)$—is exactly equal to 1.

Find the sum of the four smallest positive integers $n$ that satisfy this condition.
