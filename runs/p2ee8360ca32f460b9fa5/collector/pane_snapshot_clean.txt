## Solution

**Setup.** The cube is $[0,1]^3$ with 8 vertices. We remove all points whose tax
icab distance to *any* vertex is $< 3/5$. Since $3/5 < 1$, each removed region l
ies entirely inside the cube (no boundary truncation). I use inclusion–exclusion
 over the 8 vertex regions.

**Single-vertex regions.** Near vertex $(0,0,0)$, the taxicab distance is $x+y+z
$, so the removed region is $\{x+y+z < 3/5,\; x,y,z \ge 0\}$ — a tetrahedron wit
h vertices $(0,0,0),(3/5,0,0),(0,3/5,0),(0,0,3/5)$. Its volume is
$$V_1 = \tfrac{1}{6}\left(\tfrac{3}{5}\right)^3 = \tfrac{27}{750} = \tfrac{9}{25
0}.$$
By symmetry all 8 vertices contribute equally: $8 \cdot \tfrac{9}{250} = \tfrac{
72}{250}$.

**Pairwise overlaps.** Consider two adjacent vertices (sharing an edge), say $(0
,0,0)$ and $(1,0,0)$. Their regions are
$$R_1:\; x+y+z < \tfrac35, \qquad R_2:\; (1-x)+y+z < \tfrac35 \;\Longleftrightar
row\; x > \tfrac25 + y+z.$$
For overlap we need $x < \tfrac35 - y - z$ and $x > \tfrac25 + y + z$, giving wi
dth $\tfrac15 - 2(y+z)$, valid when $y+z < \tfrac{1}{10}$. Setting $s = y+z$:
$$V_2 = \int_0^{1/10} \!\left(\tfrac15 - 2s\right) s\, ds = \left[\tfrac{s^2}{10
} - \tfrac{2s^3}{3}\right]_0^{1/10} = \tfrac{1}{1000} - \tfrac{2}{3000} = \tfrac
{1}{3000}.$$
There are 12 edges, so the total pairwise contribution is $12 \cdot \tfrac{1}{30
00} = \tfrac{1}{250}$.

**Non-adjacent pairs have no overlap.** For a face-diagonal pair, e.g. $(0,0,0)$
 and $(1,1,0)$: $R_2$ gives $2-x-y+z < \tfrac35$, i.e. $x+y > \tfrac75 = 1.4$, w
hile $R_1$ gives $x+y < \tfrac35 - z \le 0.6$. Contradiction. For a body-diagona
l pair, e.g. $(0,0,0)$ and $(1,1,1)$: $R_2$ gives $x+y+z > \tfrac{12}{5}=2.4$, c
ontradicting $R_1$'s $x+y+z < 0.6$. So only the 12 edge-adjacent pairs overlap.

**Triple (and higher) overlaps are empty.** The cube graph is bipartite with no
triangles, so among any 3 vertices at least one pair is non-adjacent — and that
pair has zero overlap, forcing the triple intersection to be empty. Hence inclus
ion–exclusion terminates at pairs.

**Inclusion–exclusion.**
$$\text{Volume removed} = 8\cdot\tfrac{9}{250} - 12\cdot\tfrac{1}{3000} = \tfrac
{72}{250} - \tfrac{1}{250} = \tfrac{71}{250}.$$

**Volume of $\mathcal{R}$:**
$$\text{Vol}(\mathcal{R}) = 1 - \tfrac{71}{250} = \tfrac{179}{250}.$$

Since $179$ is prime and $\gcd(179,250)=1$, the fraction is irreducible. Thus $a
=179,\; b=250$ and
$$a+b = \boxed{429}.$$
