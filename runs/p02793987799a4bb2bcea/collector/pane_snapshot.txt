 graph is the octahedron graph = complete tripartite graph $K_{2,2,2}$ (parts: $
\{T,B\}, \{F,K\}, \{L,R\}$). We count proper 8-colorings of $K_{2,2,2}$ up to th

**Identity — chromatic polynomial of $K_{2,2,2}$.** In a proper coloring, the co
lor-sets $S_A, S_B, S_C$ used in the three parts must be pairwise disjoint, with
 $|S_A|,|S_B|,|S_C|\in\{1,2\}$. For sizes $(i,j,k)$ the count is $\frac{\lambda!
}{(\lambda-i-j-k)!}$. Summing over $i,j,k\in\{1,2\}$:

$$P(\lambda)=\tfrac{\lambda!}{(\lambda-3)!}+3\tfrac{\lambda!}{(\lambda-4)!}+3\tf

$$=\lambda(\lambda{-}1)(\lambda{-}2)+3\lambda(\lambda{-}1)(\lambda{-}2)(\lambda{
-}3)+3\lambda(\lambda{-}1)(\lambda{-}2)(\lambda{-}3)(\lambda{-}4)+\lambda(\lambd
a{-}1)(\lambda{-}2)(\lambda{-}3)(\lambda{-}4)(\lambda{-}5).$$

(Check: $P(3)=6=3!$, $P(2)=0$. ✓)

At $\lambda=8$: $336 + 5040 + 20160 + 20160 = 45696$.

**90°/270° face rotations (6).** The four side faces form a 4-cycle, forcing $F=
R=K=L$, but $F$ and $R$ are adjacent → impossible. Contribution $0$.

**180° face rotations (3).** About the T–B axis: $T,B$ fixed, $F\leftrightarrow
K$, $L\leftrightarrow R$, so $F=K=a$, $L=R=c$. Since $F,K$ are opposite and $L,R

rained between themselves.

$$\text{Count}=8\cdot 7\cdot 6\cdot 6 = 2016.\quad \text{Total }=3\cdot 2016=604

**180° edge rotations (6).** Axis through midpoints of opposite edges swaps two
adjacent faces (e.g. $T\leftrightarrow F$), forcing equal colors on adjacent fac
