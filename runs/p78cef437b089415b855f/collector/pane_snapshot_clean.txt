**Setup.** On the affine chart $U_1 = \{[Z_1,Z_2] : Z_1 \neq 0\}$ with coordinat
e $z = Z_2/Z_1 \in \mathbb{C}$, the Fubini–Study Kähler form is

$$\omega_{FS} = \frac{i}{2}\,\partial\bar\partial \log(1+|z|^2).$$

A direct computation gives $\partial\bar\partial \log(1+|z|^2) = \dfrac{dz \wedg
e d\bar z}{(1+|z|^2)^2}$, hence

$$\omega_{FS}\big|_{U_1} = \frac{i}{2}\,\frac{dz \wedge d\bar z}{(1+|z|^2)^2}.$$

**Reduction to an integral over $\mathbb{C}$.** The chart $U_1$ covers all of $\
mathbb{C}P^1$ except the single point $[0,1]$ (i.e. $z = \infty$). A point has m
easure zero, so

$$\int_{\mathbb{C}P^1} \omega_{FS} = \int_{\mathbb{C}} \frac{i}{2}\,\frac{dz \we
dge d\bar z}{(1+|z|^2)^2}.$$

**Convert to real coordinates.** Write $z = x + iy$. Then $dz = dx + i\,dy$ and
$d\bar z = dx - i\,dy$, so

$$dz \wedge d\bar z = (dx + i\,dy)\wedge(dx - i\,dy) = -2i\,dx \wedge dy,$$

and therefore $\dfrac{i}{2}\,dz \wedge d\bar z = dx \wedge dy$. Thus

$$\int_{\mathbb{C}P^1} \omega_{FS} = \int_{\mathbb{R}^2} \frac{dx\,dy}{(1 + x^2
+ y^2)^2}.$$

**Evaluate in polar coordinates.** Set $x = r\cos\theta$, $y = r\sin\theta$, so
$x^2+y^2 = r^2$ and $dx\,dy = r\,dr\,d\theta$:

$$\int_{\mathbb{C}P^1} \omega_{FS} = \int_0^{2\pi}\!\!d\theta \int_0^{\infty}\!\
frac{r\,dr}{(1+r^2)^2}.$$

The angular integral gives $2\pi$. For the radial integral, substitute $u = 1+r^
2$, $du = 2r\,dr$:

$$\int_0^{\infty}\!\frac{r\,dr}{(1+r^2)^2} = \frac{1}{2}\int_1^{\infty}\!\frac{d
u}{u^2} = \frac{1}{2}\left[-\frac{1}{u}\right]_1^{\infty} = \frac{1}{2}\bigl(0 -
 (-1)\bigr) = \frac{1}{2}.$$

**Conclusion.**

$$\int_{\mathbb{C}P^1} \omega_{FS} = 2\pi \cdot \frac{1}{2} = \pi. \qquad\blacks
quare$$
