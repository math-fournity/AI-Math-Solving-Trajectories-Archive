## Solution

A "simple number" is a **squarefree** integer: an integer $n \ge 1$ whose prime
factorization contains no repeated prime, i.e., no perfect square $> 1$ divides
$n$.

### Method

The count of squarefree integers in $\{1, 2, \dots, N\}$ is given by the Möbius
inversion formula

$$S(N) = \sum_{d=1}^{\lfloor\sqrt{N}\rfloor} \mu(d)\left\lfloor \frac{N}{d^2}\ri
ght\rfloor,$$

where $\mu$ is the Möbius function: $\mu(d)=0$ if $d$ is divisible by a square $
>1$; $\mu(d)=(-1)^k$ if $d$ is the product of $k$ distinct primes; $\mu(1)=1$.

The idea: an integer is squarefree iff it is **not** divisible by $p^2$ for any
prime $p$. By inclusion–exclusion over square divisors $d^2$ (with $d$ squarefre
e, weighted by $\mu(d)$), we obtain the formula above.

### Computation for $N = 1000$

Here $\lfloor\sqrt{1000}\rfloor = 31$, so $d$ ranges over $1, \dots, 31$. We lis
t only squarefree $d$ (those with $\mu(d) \ne 0$):

| $d$ | $\mu(d)$ | $\lfloor 1000/d^2\rfloor$ | contribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1  | $+1$ | 1000 | $+1000$ |
| 2  | $-1$ | 250  | $-250$  |
| 3  | $-1$ | 111  | $-111$  |
| 5  | $-1$ | 40   | $-40$   |
| 6  | $+1$ | 27   | $+27$   |
| 7  | $-1$ | 20   | $-20$   |
| 10 | $+1$ | 10   | $+10$   |
| 11 | $-1$ | 8    | $-8$    |
| 13 | $-1$ | 5    | $-5$    |
| 14 | $+1$ | 5    | $+5$    |
| 15 | $+1$ | 4    | $+4$    |
| 17 | $-1$ | 3    | $-3$    |
| 19 | $-1$ | 2    | $-2$    |
| 21 | $+1$ | 2    | $+2$    |
| 22 | $+1$ | 2    | $+2$    |
| 23 | $-1$ | 1    | $-1$    |
| 26 | $+1$ | 1    | $+1$    |
| 29 | $-1$ | 1    | $-1$    |
| 30 | $-1$ | 1    | $-1$    |
| 31 | $-1$ | 1    | $-1$    |

(All other $d \in \{4,8,9,12,16,18,20,24,25,27,28\}$ have $\mu(d)=0$ and contrib
ute nothing.)

### Summing

Positive contributions:
$$1000 + 27 + 10 + 5 + 4 + 2 + 2 + 1 = 1051.$$

Negative contributions:
$$250 + 111 + 40 + 20 + 8 + 5 + 3 + 2 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 443.$$

Therefore
$$S(1000) = 1051 - 443 = 608.$$

### Answer

Among the numbers $1, 2, \dots, 1000$, the number of simple (squarefree) numbers
 is $\boxed{608}$.
