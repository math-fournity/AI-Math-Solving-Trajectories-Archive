## Solution

**Problem.** Compute $\displaystyle\lim_{A\to+\infty}\frac{1}{A}\int_1^A A^{1/x}
\,dx$.

**Answer: $1$.**

---

### Proof

Set $L=\ln A$. Substitute $x=Au$:

$$I(A)=\frac1A\int_1^A A^{1/x}\,dx=\int_{1/A}^{1}A^{1/(Au)}\,du=\int_{1/A}^{1}e^
{L/(Au)}\,du.$$

Now substitute $v=\dfrac{L}{Au}$, i.e. $u=\dfrac{L}{Av}$, $du=-\dfrac{L}{Av^2}\,
dv$. The limits $u=1/A$ and $u=1$ become $v=L$ and $v=Le^{-L}$ respectively, so

$$I(A)=\frac{L}{A}\int_{Le^{-L}}^{L}\frac{e^v}{v^2}\,dv=Le^{-L}\int_{Le^{-L}}^{L
}\frac{e^v}{v^2}\,dv. \tag{$\ast$}$$

For $A>e$ (so $L>1$), split the integral at $v=1$:

$$I(A)=\underbrace{Le^{-L}\int_{Le^{-L}}^{1}\frac{e^v}{v^2}\,dv}_{T_1}+\underbra
ce{Le^{-L}\int_{1}^{L}\frac{e^v}{v^2}\,dv}_{T_2}.$$

---

**Term $T_1\to 1$.** Using $e^v=1+v+O(v^2)$ on $[0,1]$:

$$\int_{Le^{-L}}^{1}\frac{e^v}{v^2}\,dv=\int_{Le^{-L}}^{1}\frac{dv}{v^2}+\int_{L
e^{-L}}^{1}\frac{dv}{v}+\int_{Le^{-L}}^{1}\frac{e^v-1-v}{v^2}\,dv.$$

The three pieces are:

- $\displaystyle\int_{Le^{-L}}^{1}\frac{dv}{v^2}=\frac{e^L}{L}-1,$
- $\displaystyle\int_{Le^{-L}}^{1}\frac{dv}{v}=L-\ln L,$
- $\displaystyle\int_{Le^{-L}}^{1}\frac{e^v-1-v}{v^2}\,dv\;\xrightarrow{L\to\inf
ty}\;\int_0^1\frac{e^v-1-v}{v^2}\,dv=:C<\infty,$

since $\frac{e^v-1-v}{v^2}\to\frac12$ as $v\to0^+$ and the integrand is continuo
us on $[0,1]$.

Multiplying by $Le^{-L}$:

$$T_1 = \bigl(1-Le^{-L}\bigr)+\bigl(L^2-L\ln L\bigr)e^{-L}+C\,Le^{-L}+o(Le^{-L})
\;\longrightarrow\;1.$$

---

**Term $T_2\to 0$.** Substitute $v=L-t$:

$$\int_1^L\frac{e^v}{v^2}\,dv=\frac{e^L}{L^2}\int_0^{L-1}\frac{e^{-t}}{(1-t/L)^2
}\,dt.$$

Split the remaining integral at $t=L/2$:

- For $t\in[0,L/2]$: $(1-t/L)^2\ge\tfrac14$, so $\displaystyle\int_0^{L/2}\frac{
e^{-t}}{(1-t/L)^2}\,dt\le 4\int_0^\infty e^{-t}\,dt=4.$
- For $t\in[L/2,\,L-1]$: $e^{-t}\le e^{-L/2}$ and $(1-t/L)^{-2}\le L^2$, so the
integral is $\le \tfrac{L}{2}\cdot e^{-L/2}\cdot L^2=O(L^3e^{-L/2})\to0$.

Hence $\displaystyle\int_0^{L-1}\frac{e^{-t}}{(1-t/L)^2}\,dt=O(1)$, giving

$$\int_1^L\frac{e^v}{v^2}\,dv=O\!\left(\frac{e^L}{L^2}\right),\qquad T_2=Le^{-L}
\cdot O\!\left(\frac{e^L}{L^2}\right)=O\!\left(\frac1L\right)\to 0.$$

---

**Conclusion.** From $(\ast)$:

$$I(A)=T_1+T_2=1+o(1)+o(1)=1+o(1)\qquad(A\to+\infty).$$

$$\boxed{\displaystyle\lim_{A\to+\infty}\frac{1}{A}\int_1^A A^{1/x}\,dx=1.}$$
