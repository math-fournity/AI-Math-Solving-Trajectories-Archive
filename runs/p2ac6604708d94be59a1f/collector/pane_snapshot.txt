## Solution

We solve the forced wave equation with homogeneous Dirichlet boundary and zero i
nitial conditions using eigenfunction (sine) expansion.

### Setup

The eigenfunctions of $-\partial_{xx}$ on $(0,\pi)$ with $u(0,t)=u(\pi,t)=0$ are
 $\sin(nx)$, $n=1,2,\dots$, with eigenvalues $n^2$. Expand

$$u(x,t)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}T_n(t)\sin(nx),\qquad 2x\sin t=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}f_
n(t)\sin(nx).$$

### Forcing coefficients

$$f_n(t)=\frac{2}{\pi}\int_0^\pi 2x\sin t\,\sin(nx)\,dx=\frac{4\sin t}{\pi}\int_
0^\pi x\sin(nx)\,dx.$$

Integrating by parts,

$$\int_0^\pi x\sin(nx)\,dx=\Big[-\frac{x\cos(nx)}{n}\Big]_0^\pi+\int_0^\pi\frac{
\cos(nx)}{n}\,dx=-\frac{\pi(-1)^n}{n}+0=\frac{\pi(-1)^{n+1}}{n}.$$

Hence

$$f_n(t)=\frac{4(-1)^{n+1}}{n}\sin t.$$

### ODEs for $T_n$

Substituting into $u_{tt}=u_{xx}+2x\sin t$ gives, for each $n$,

$$T_n''+n^2T_n=\frac{4(-1)^{n+1}}{n}\sin t,\qquad T_n(0)=0,\quad T_n'(0)=0.$$

**Case $n\ge 2$ (non-resonant, $n^2\ne 1$).** A particular solution $T_n^p=A\sin
 t$ gives $A(n^2-1)=\frac{4(-1)^{n+1}}{n}$, so $A=\frac{4(-1)^{n+1}}{n(n^2-1)}$.
 The general solution is

$$T_n=C_n\cos(nt)+D_n\sin(nt)+\frac{4(-1)^{n+1}}{n(n^2-1)}\sin t.$$

From $T_n(0)=0$: $C_n=0$. From $T_n'(0)=0$:

$$nD_n+\frac{4(-1)^{n+1}}{n(n^2-1)}=0\;\Longrightarrow\;D_n=-\frac{4(-1)^{n+1}}{
n^2(n^2-1)}.$$

Thus

$$T_n(t)=\frac{4(-1)^{n+1}}{n(n^2-1)}\sin t-\frac{4(-1)^{n+1}}{n^2(n^2-1)}\sin(n
t),\qquad n\ge 2.$$

**Case $n=1$ (resonant, $n^2=1$).** The ODE is $T_1''+T_1=4\sin t$. Resonance re
quires a particular solution of the form $T_1^p=At\cos t$:

$$T_1^{p\,\prime\prime}+T_1^p=-2A\sin t=4\sin t\;\Longrightarrow\;A=-2.$$

So $T_1=C_1\cos t+D_1\sin t-2t\cos t$. From $T_1(0)=0$: $C_1=0$. From $T_1'(0)=0
$:

$$T_1'=2\cos t-2\cos t+2t\sin t=2t\sin t,\qquad T_1'(0)=0\;\checkmark,\quad D_1=
2.$$

Hence

$$T_1(t)=2\sin t-2t\cos t.$$

### Verification

- **PDE for $n=1$:** $T_1''=2\sin t+2t\cos t$, so $T_1''+T_1=4\sin t=f_1(t)$. ✓
- **PDE for $n\ge2$:** $T_n''+n^2T_n=\frac{4(-1)^{n+1}}{n}\sin t=f_n(t)$ by cons
truction. ✓
- **Boundary conditions:** Each $\sin(nx)$ vanishes at $x=0,\pi$. ✓
- **Initial displacement:** $T_n(0)=0$ for all $n$, so $u(x,0)=0$. ✓
- **Initial velocity:** $T_1'(0)=0$; for $n\ge2$, $T_n'(0)=\frac{4(-1)^{n+1}}{n(
n^2-1)}-\frac{4(-1)^{n+1}}{n(n^2-1)}=0$. So $u_t(x,0)=0$. ✓

### Final Solution

$$\boxed{\,u(x,t)=(2\sin t-2t\cos t)\sin x+\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}\frac{4(-1)^{n+1}}
{n(n^2-1)}\left(\sin t-\frac{1}{n}\sin(nt)\right)\sin(nx)\,}$$

The $n=1$ mode exhibits secular growth ($-2t\cos t\,\sin x$) due to resonance be
tween the temporal forcing frequency $\omega=1$ and the natural frequency $n=1$
of the string.
