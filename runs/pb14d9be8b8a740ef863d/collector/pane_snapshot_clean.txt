## Proof

We are given that for all $a, b \in S$,
$$
(a*b)*a = b. \tag{1}
$$

We wish to prove that for all $a, b \in S$,
$$
a*(b*a) = b.
$$

**Step 1.** Apply identity (1) with $x = a*b$ and $y = a$ (valid since $a*b \in
S$):
$$
\big((a*b)*a\big)*(a*b) = a. \tag{2}
$$

**Step 2.** Apply identity (1) with $x = a$ and $y = b$:
$$
(a*b)*a = b. \tag{3}
$$

**Step 3.** Substitute (3) into (2):
$$
b*(a*b) = a. \tag{4}
$$

Since (4) holds for **all** $a, b \in S$, we may relabel the variables. Renaming
 $a \to x$ and $b \to y$, equation (4) becomes
$$
y*(x*y) = x \qquad \text{for all } x, y \in S. \tag{5}
$$

**Step 4.** Now apply (5) with $x = b$ and $y = a$:
$$
a*(b*a) = b.
$$

This is exactly the desired identity. $\blacksquare$
