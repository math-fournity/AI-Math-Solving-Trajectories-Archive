# Problem

Given a regular $n$-gon, where $n \geq 3$ is a natural number. Yugi chooses $n$ different natural numbers and tells them to Kaiba, who mentally distributes them among the vertices of the polygon (one number per vertex). Then Yugi starts asking questions of the following type: he chooses a line that does not pass through any vertex of the polygon, then he chooses one of the two half-planes and asks what the sum of the numbers in that half-plane is. Each question costs Yugi $1$ yen, and Kaiba answers all questions correctly. Let $m(n)$ be the smallest number of yen such that Yugi can choose the numbers in such a way that he can guess the distribution chosen by Kaiba for no more than $m(n)$ yen.

Calculate the value of $\sum_{n=3}^{100} m(n)$.
