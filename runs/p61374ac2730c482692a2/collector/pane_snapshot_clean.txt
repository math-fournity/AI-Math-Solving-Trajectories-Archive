## Solution

**Setting up coordinates.** Place $B=(0,0)$, $C=(10,0)$. From $AB=9$, $AC=13$:
$$A=\left(\tfrac{3}{5},\,\tfrac{12\sqrt{14}}{5}\right), \qquad [ABC]=12\sqrt{14}
,\quad s=16,\quad r=\tfrac{3\sqrt{14}}{4}.$$
The incenter is $I=\frac{10A+13B+9C}{32}=\left(3,\,\tfrac{3\sqrt{14}}{4}\right)$
.

**Contact and reflected points.** Using $BA_1=s-b=3$, etc.:

| Point | Location |
|-------|----------|
| $A_1=(3,0)$ | $A_2=(7,0)$ (reflect over midpoint $(5,0)$) |
| $B_1=\left(\tfrac{321}{65},\tfrac{84\sqrt{14}}{65}\right)$ | $B_2=\left(\tfrac
{368}{65},\tfrac{72\sqrt{14}}{65}\right)$ |
| $C_1=\left(\tfrac{1}{5},\tfrac{4\sqrt{14}}{5}\right)$ | $C_2=\left(\tfrac{2}{5
},\tfrac{8\sqrt{14}}{5}\right)$ |

(Verified: $BA_2=7$, $CB_2=6$, $AC_2=3$.)

**Finding $A_3$.** Parametrize line $AA_2$: $P=A+t(A_2-A)$. Substituting into $(
x-3)^2+(y-\tfrac{3\sqrt{14}}{4})^2=\tfrac{63}{8}$ gives
$$152t^2-177t+45=0 \implies t=\tfrac{57}{152},\;\tfrac{15}{19}.$$
Taking the root closer to $A$ ($t=\tfrac{57}{152}$):
$$A_3=\left(3,\;\tfrac{57\sqrt{14}}{38}\right).$$

**Finding $B_3$.** Line $BB_2$: $P=t\cdot B_2$. Substituting:
$$8320t^2-9672t+1521=0 \implies t=\tfrac{39}{208},\;\tfrac{507}{520}.$$
Taking $t=\tfrac{39}{208}$:
$$B_3=\left(\tfrac{69}{65},\;\tfrac{27\sqrt{14}}{130}\right).$$

**Finding $C_3$.** Line $CC_2$: $P=C+t(C_2-C)$. Substituting:
$$128t^2-168t+49=0 \implies t=\tfrac{7}{16},\;\tfrac{7}{8}.$$
Taking $t=\tfrac{7}{16}$:
$$C_3=\left(\tfrac{29}{5},\;\tfrac{7\sqrt{14}}{10}\right).$$

All three points verified to lie on $\omega$.

**Computing $[A_3B_3C_3]$.** Write $y_i=\sqrt{14}\,u_i$ with $u_A=\tfrac{57}{38}
$, $u_B=\tfrac{27}{130}$, $u_C=\tfrac{7}{10}$, and $x_A=3$, $x_B=\tfrac{69}{65}$
, $x_C=\tfrac{29}{5}$.

$$[A_3B_3C_3]=\tfrac{\sqrt{14}}{2}\left|x_A(u_B-u_C)+x_B(u_C-u_A)+x_C(u_A-u_B)\r
ight|.$$

Computing the differences:
$$u_B-u_C=-\tfrac{32}{65},\quad u_C-u_A=-\tfrac{4}{5},\quad u_A-u_B=\tfrac{84}{6
5}.$$

$$\text{Sum}=3\!\left(-\tfrac{32}{65}\right)+\tfrac{69}{65}\!\left(-\tfrac{4}{5}
\right)+\tfrac{29}{5}\!\left(\tfrac{84}{65}\right)=-\tfrac{480}{325}-\tfrac{276}
{325}+\tfrac{2436}{325}=\tfrac{1680}{325}=\tfrac{336}{65}.$$

$$[A_3B_3C_3]=\tfrac{\sqrt{14}}{2}\cdot\tfrac{336}{65}=\tfrac{168\sqrt{14}}{65}.
$$

**Final ratio:**
$$\frac{[A_3B_3C_3]}{[ABC]}=\frac{168\sqrt{14}/65}{12\sqrt{14}}=\frac{168}{780}=
\frac{14}{65}.$$

Since $\gcd(14,65)=1$, we have $a=14$, $b=65$, and

$$a+b=\boxed{79}.$$
