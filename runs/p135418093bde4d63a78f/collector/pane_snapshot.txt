## Simplification

$$\frac{\pi(x^2+4ax+1)}{4(x^2+1)} = \frac{\pi}{4} + \frac{a\pi x}{x^2+1} = \frac

The equation becomes $\sin(a\pi t) + \cos\!\left(\frac{\pi}{4}+a\pi t\right) = \

Let $u = a\pi t$. Expanding:
$$(1-\tfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2})\sin u + \tfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\cos u = \sqrt{2-\sqrt{2}}$
$

Setting $A = 1-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$, $B = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$: we get $A^2+B^2 =
2-\sqrt{2}$, so $\sqrt{A^2+B^2} = \sqrt{2-\sqrt{2}} = C$.

The equation $A\sin u + B\cos u = \sqrt{A^2+B^2}$ forces $\sin(u+\varphi)=1$ whe

Thus $u = \frac{\pi}{8} + 2k\pi$, giving $at = \frac{1+16k}{8}$, i.e., $t_k = \f
rac{1+16k}{8a}$.

## Counting solutions

The map $x \mapsto t = \frac{x}{x^2+1}$ sends $\mathbb{R} \to [-\frac{1}{2},\fra
c{1}{2}]$, with:
- $|t| \in (0,\frac{1}{2})$: **2** preimages
- $|t| = \frac{1}{2}$: **1** preimage ($x=\pm1$)
- $t=0$: **1** preimage (impossible here since $1+16k\neq 0$)

Valid $k$ satisfy $|1+16k| \leq 4|a|$. Let $s=|a|$, $q=\lfloor s/4\rfloor$, $r =
 4s-16q \in [0,16)$:

| Range of $r$ | $N(s)$ (number of valid $k$) |
---|---
| $[0,1)$ | $2q$ |
| $[1,15)$ | $2q+1$ |
| $[15,16)$ | $2q+2$ |

Let $E(s)=1$ if $4s=|1+16k|$ for some $k$ (i.e., $s=\frac{16m\pm1}{4}$), else $0

## Finding $f(a)\leq 5$

- $N\geq 3 \iff s\geq \frac{17}{4}$ (verified: at $s=4.25$, $N=3$; at $s=4.24$,
$N=2$)
- $N\geq 4 \iff s\geq \frac{31}{4}$

$f>5$ requires ($N\geq 3, E=0$) or ($N\geq 4, E=1$):

- **At $s=\frac{17}{4}$**: $N=3$, $E=1$ (since $4s=17=|1+16\cdot1|$), so $f=2\cd
ot3-1=5\leq 5$. ✓
- **For $s\in(\frac{17}{4},\frac{31}{4})$**: $N=3$, $E=0$ (no special points in

For $s < \frac{17}{4}$: $N\leq 2$, so $f\leq 4\leq 5$.

Therefore: $f(a)\leq 5 \iff |a|\leq \frac{17}{4}$.

## Result

The interval is $\left[-\frac{17}{4},\,\frac{17}{4}\right]$, with total length:

$$2\times\frac{17}{4}=\frac{17}{2}=8.5$$
