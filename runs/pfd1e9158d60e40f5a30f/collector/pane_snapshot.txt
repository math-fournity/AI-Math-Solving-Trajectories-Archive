Looking at this problem, I need to find the minimum possible value of $M = \max(
a_1, \dots, a_{10})$ subject to $\sum a_i \geq 10$ and $\sum a_i^2 \geq 100$, wh
ere the $a_i$ are real-valued output settings.

## Proof

**Setup.** We seek the smallest $M$ such that there exist real numbers $a_1, \do
ts, a_{10}$ with $a_i \leq M$ for all $i$, $\sum a_i \geq 10$, and $\sum a_i^2 \
geq 100$.

---

**Lower bound: $M \geq 2$.**

Fix $M$ and define the polytope $P = \{a \in \mathbb{R}^{10} : a_i \leq M \;\for
all i,\; \sum a_i \geq 10\}$.

*$P$ is bounded:* Since $a_i \leq M$ for all $i$ and $\sum a_j \geq 10$, each co
ordinate satisfies $a_i \geq 10 - 9M$ (because $a_i = \sum a_j - \sum_{j \neq i}
 a_j \geq 10 - 9M$). So $P \subseteq [10-9M,\; M]^{10}$.

*Note $P \neq \emptyset$ requires $10M \geq 10$, i.e., $M \geq 1$.*

The function $f(a) = \sum a_i^2$ is **convex**, so its maximum over the bounded
polytope $P$ is attained at a **vertex**. A vertex of $P$ in $\mathbb{R}^{10}$ r
equires 10 linearly independent active constraints. The vertices are:

- **Type A** (all 10 upper bounds active): $a_i = M$ for all $i$. Valid when $10
M \geq 10$, i.e., $M \geq 1$.
  $$f = 10M^2.$$

- **Type B** (9 upper bounds + sum constraint active): nine coordinates equal $M
$, one equals $10 - 9M$. Valid when $10 - 9M \leq M$, i.e., $M \geq 1$.
  $$f = 9M^2 + (10 - 9M)^2 = 90M^2 - 180M + 100.$$

Comparing the two: $90M^2 - 180M + 100 \geq 10M^2 \iff 80M^2 - 180M + 100 \geq 0
 \iff 20(4M-5)(M-1) \geq 0$, which holds for $M \geq \frac{5}{4}$.

**Case 1: $M < \frac{5}{4}$.** The maximum of $f$ over $P$ is $10M^2 < 10 \cdot
\frac{25}{16} = \frac{125}{8} \approx 15.6 < 100$. So $\sum a_i^2 \geq 100$ **ca
nnot** be satisfied. Infeasible.

**Case 2: $M \geq \frac{5}{4}$.** The maximum of $f$ over $P$ is $90M^2 - 180M +
 100$. For feasibility we need:
$$90M^2 - 180M + 100 \geq 100 \implies 90M(M - 2) \geq 0 \implies M \geq 2.$$

Combining both cases: **$M \geq 2$ is necessary.**

---

**Upper bound: $M = 2$ is achievable.**

Set $a_1 = a_2 = \cdots = a_9 = 2$ and $a_{10} = -8$.

- **Sum constraint:** $\sum a_i = 9 \cdot 2 + (-8) = 18 - 8 = 10 \geq 10$. ✓
- **Sum of squares:** $\sum a_i^2 = 9 \cdot 4 + 64 = 36 + 64 = 100 \geq 100$. ✓
- **Maximum:** $\max(a_1, \dots, a_{10}) = \max(2, 2, \dots, 2, -8) = 2$. ✓

This configuration satisfies both constraints with $M = 2$.

---

**Conclusion.** The lower bound $M \geq 2$ and the achieving configuration toget
her prove that the minimum possible value of the maximum valve setting is:

$$\boxed{M = 2}$$
