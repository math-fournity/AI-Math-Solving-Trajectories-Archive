## Proof

**Setup.** Embed the $m \times n$ grid as a planar graph $G$ on the sphere with:
- **Vertices:** grid points $(a,b)$, $0\le a\le m$, $0\le b\le n$, so $V=(m+1)(n
+1)$.
- **Edges:** all grid sides, so $E=m(n+1)+(m+1)n=2mn+m+n$.
- **Faces:** the $mn$ cells plus the exterior face, so $F=mn+1$.

Since $G$ is connected, $V-E+F=2$.

Call an internal grid edge (separating two cells) a **same-edge** if its two cel
ls share a color, and a **cut-edge** otherwise. Let $S$ be the number of same-ed
ges. Boundary edges (separating a cell from the exterior) are neither.

**Merging via edge deletion.** Form $G'$ by deleting all same-edges from $G$ (ke
eping all vertices, boundary edges, and cut-edges). Each deletion merges two fac
es, so after all deletions the faces of $G'$ are exactly the $R$ connected monoc
hromatic regions plus the exterior face: $F'=R+1$.

Now $G'$ has $V'=(m+1)(n+1)$ vertices, $E'=2mn+m+n-S$ edges, $F'=R+1$ faces, and
 $c'$ connected components. Euler's formula on the sphere gives $V'-E'+F'=1+c'$,
 i.e.,

$$R = mn - S + c' - 1. \tag{1}$$

**Analyzing $c'$.** The boundary edges form a cycle connecting all boundary grid
 points into one component. Each interior grid point $(a,b)$, $1\le a\le m-1$, $
1\le b\le n-1$, is incident to 4 internal edges. Such a point becomes an **isola
ted vertex** in $G'$ iff all 4 surrounding cells share the same color. Let $I$ =
 number of isolated interior grid points, and $K$ = number of additional compone
nts formed by cut-edges in the interior not touching the boundary ($K\ge 0$). Th
en

$$c' = 1 + I + K, \tag{2}$$

so from (1) and (2):

$$R = mn - S + I + K \ge mn - S + I. \tag{3}$$

**Computing $E[S]$.** Each of the $2mn-m-n$ internal edges joins two independent
ly colored cells; the probability of matching colors is $\tfrac12$. By linearity
:

$$E[S]=\frac{2mn-m-n}{2}. \tag{4}$$

**Computing $E[I]$.** An interior grid point is isolated iff its 4 surrounding c
ells are all the same color, which has probability $2\cdot(1/2)^4=1/8$. There ar
e $(m-1)(n-1)$ interior grid points, so

$$E[I]=\frac{(m-1)(n-1)}{8}. \tag{5}$$

**Taking expectations in (3):**

$$E[R]\ge mn - \frac{2mn-m-n}{2}+\frac{(m-1)(n-1)}{8}=\frac{m+n}{2}+\frac{(m-1)(
n-1)}{8}.$$

Combining over a common denominator:

$$E[R]\ge\frac{4(m+n)+(m-1)(n-1)}{8}=\frac{mn+3m+3n+1}{8}.$$

Since $m,n\ge 1$, we have $3m+3n+1>0$, hence

$$E[R]\ge\frac{mn+3m+3n+1}{8}>\frac{mn}{8}. \qquad\blacksquare$$
