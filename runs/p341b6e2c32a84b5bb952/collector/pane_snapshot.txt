## Solution

A connected compact combinatorial surface is homeomorphic to either an orientabl
e or non-orientable closed surface with some number of boundary components (disk
s removed). The Euler characteristic formulas are:

- **Orientable**, genus $g \ge 0$, $b \ge 0$ boundary components: $\chi = 2 - 2g
 - b$
- **Non-orientable**, genus $k \ge 1$ (number of crosscaps), $b \ge 0$ boundary
components: $\chi = 2 - k - b$

We require $\chi = 0$, with connectedness ($g=0, b=0$ would be a sphere with $\c
hi=2$, excluded; and we need $b \ge 0$, $g \ge 0$, $k \ge 1$).

### Orientable case: $2g + b = 2$

- $g = 0,\, b = 2$: the sphere with two disks removed — the **annulus** (cylinde
r). Connected, compact, with boundary. ✓
- $g = 1,\, b = 0$: the **torus**. Connected, compact, closed. ✓

No other non-negative integer solutions.

### Non-orientable case: $k + b = 2$, $k \ge 1$

- $k = 1,\, b = 1$: the projective plane with one disk removed — the **Möbius ba
nd**. Connected, compact, with boundary. ✓
- $k = 2,\, b = 0$: the **Klein bottle**. Connected, compact, closed. ✓

No other solutions with $k \ge 1$.

### Verification of distinctness

These four surfaces are pairwise non-homeomorphic:
- Torus is orientable and closed; annulus is orientable with boundary; Klein bot
tle is non-orientable and closed; Möbius band is non-orientable with boundary.
- Orientability, number of boundary components, and closedness are all topologic
al invariants, so the four classes are distinct.

Each has Euler characteristic $0$:
- Torus: $2 - 2(1) - 0 = 0$
- Annulus: $2 - 2(0) - 2 = 0$
- Klein bottle: $2 - 2 - 0 = 0$
- Möbius band: $2 - 1 - 1 = 0$

### Count

There are exactly **4** homeomorphism classes: the torus, the annulus, the Klein
 bottle, and the Möbius band.
