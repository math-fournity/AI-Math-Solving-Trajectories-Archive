# Problem

In a remote digital manufacturing facility, a robotic arm operates a square-shaped glass panel on a two-dimensional assembly grid. The panel’s four corners, labeled for orientation, initially occupy a specific set of coordinates. To process the panel, the facility’s control system must execute a sequence of exactly 2016 operations. There are two types of valid operations the system can perform:

(a) **Edge-Flip**: The system selects one of the four edges of the square panel and flips the panel over that edge into the adjacent grid position.
(b) **Corner-Pivot**: The system selects one of the four corners of the square panel and rotates the entire panel $90^\circ$ counterclockwise around that corner.

After a full cycle of 2016 operations, a sensor check confirms that the panel has returned to its original position and that each specific corner has returned to its original starting coordinate.

Let $N$ be the total number of distinct sequences of 2016 operations that result in this specific outcome. What is the remainder when $N$ is divided by $100000$?
