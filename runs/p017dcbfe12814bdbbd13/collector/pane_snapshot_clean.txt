## Solution

**Setup.** Place the 13-14-15 triangle with vertices $A=(0,0)$, $B=(14,0)$, and
$C=(5,12)$ (since $AC=13$, $BC=15$, area $=84$). The three sides lie on:
- $AB$: $y=0$
- $AC$: $12x-5y=0$
- $BC$: $4x+3y-56=0$

**Semicircle on $AB$ (length 14).** Center $(m,0)$, radius $r$. Tangency to $AC$
 and $BC$:
$$r=\frac{12m}{13}=\frac{56-4m}{5}\implies 60m=728-52m\implies m=6.5,\quad r=6.$
$
Area $= \tfrac12\pi(36)=18\pi$.

**Semicircle on $AC$ (length 13).** Center at $(5t,12t)$, radius $r$. Tangency t
o $AB$ and $BC$:
$$r=12t=\frac{56(1-t)}{5}\implies 60t=56-56t\implies t=\frac{14}{29},\quad r=\fr
ac{168}{29}.$$
Area $= \tfrac12\pi\!\left(\tfrac{168}{29}\right)^2=\frac{14112\pi}{841}$.

**Semicircle on $BC$ (length 15).** Center at $(14-9t,12t)$, radius $r$. Tangenc
y to $AB$ and $AC$:
$$r=12t=\frac{168(1-t)}{13}\implies 156t=168-168t\implies t=\frac{14}{27},\quad
r=\frac{56}{9}.$$
Area $= \tfrac12\pi\!\left(\tfrac{56}{9}\right)^2=\frac{1568\pi}{81}$.

**Product of areas.** Using $18=2\cdot3^2$, $14112=2^5\cdot3^2\cdot7^2$, $1568=2
^5\cdot7^2$, $841=29^2$, $81=3^4$:
$$\text{Product}=18\pi\cdot\frac{14112\pi}{841}\cdot\frac{1568\pi}{81}=\frac{2^{
11}\cdot7^4}{29^2}\,\pi^3.$$

**Relating to the sphere.** Let the sphere have radius $R$. Surface area $=4\pi
R^2$, so its cube is $64\pi^3R^6=2^6\pi^3R^6$. Setting equal:
$$2^6 R^6 = \frac{2^{11}\cdot7^4}{29^2}\implies R^6=\frac{2^5\cdot7^4}{29^2}.$$

**Volume.**
$$R^3=\sqrt{R^6}=\sqrt{\frac{2^5\cdot7^4}{29^2}}=\frac{7^2\cdot\sqrt{2^5}}{29}=\
frac{49\cdot4\sqrt2}{29}=\frac{196\sqrt2}{29}.$$
$$V=\frac43\pi R^3=\frac43\pi\cdot\frac{196\sqrt2}{29}=\frac{784\pi\sqrt2}{87}.$
$

**Verification of form.** $a=784=2^4\cdot7^2$, $c=87=3\cdot29$, $b=2$. Since $\g
cd(784,87)=1$ and $b=2$ is a product of distinct primes, the form $\frac{a\pi\sq
rt b}{c}$ is satisfied.

$$a+b+c=784+2+87=\boxed{873}.$$
