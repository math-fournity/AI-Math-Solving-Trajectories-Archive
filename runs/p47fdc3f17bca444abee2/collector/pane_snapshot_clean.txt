## Solution

The Primary Influence Zone is the set of points $(x,y)$ strictly closer to $(0,0
)$ than to every outpost. For an outpost at $(a,b)$, the condition "closer to $(
0,0)$ than to $(a,b)$" is:

$$x^2+y^2 < (x-a)^2+(y-b)^2 \;\Longleftrightarrow\; 2ax+2by < a^2+b^2.$$

Computing the half-plane for each outpost:

| Outpost | $(a,b)$ | Constraint | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | $(0,4)$ | $4y<8 \Rightarrow y<2$ | $y=2$ |
| 2 | $(4,0)$ | $4x<8 \Rightarrow x<2$ | $x=2$ |
| 3 | $(-2,-2)$ | $-2x-2y<4 \Rightarrow x+y>-2$ | $x+y=-2$ |
| 4 | $(3,3)$ | $3x+3y<9 \Rightarrow x+y<3$ | $x+y=3$ |
| 5 | $(5,5)$ | $5x+5y<25 \Rightarrow x+y<5$ | $x+y=5$ |

**Redundancy:** Outpost 5's constraint $x+y<5$ is implied by Outpost 4's $x+y<3$
, so Outpost 5 is redundant and contributes no boundary.

The region is the intersection of four half-planes: $y<2$, $x<2$, $x+y>-2$, $x+y
<3$.

**Vertices** (pairwise intersections of the four boundary lines, checked against
 all constraints):

1. $y=2 \cap x+y=3 \Rightarrow (1,2)$
2. $y=2 \cap x+y=-2 \Rightarrow (-4,2)$
3. $x=2 \cap x+y=-2 \Rightarrow (2,-4)$
4. $x=2 \cap x+y=3 \Rightarrow (2,1)$

All four satisfy every constraint (as boundary points), and the cross products o
f consecutive edge directions are all positive, confirming a convex quadrilatera
l.

**Side lengths:**

- $(1,2)\to(-4,2)$: $\sqrt{(-5)^2+0^2}=5$
- $(-4,2)\to(2,-4)$: $\sqrt{6^2+(-6)^2}=6\sqrt{2}$
- $(2,-4)\to(2,1)$: $\sqrt{0^2+5^2}=5$
- $(2,1)\to(1,2)$: $\sqrt{(-1)^2+1^2}=\sqrt{2}$

**Perimeter:**

$$P = 5 + 6\sqrt{2} + 5 + \sqrt{2} = 10 + 7\sqrt{2}.$$

The total length of the perimeter of the Primary Influence Zone is $\boxed{10+7\
sqrt{2}}$.
