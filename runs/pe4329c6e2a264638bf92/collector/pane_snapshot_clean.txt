## Solution

The local ring of $C$ at $P = (0,0)$ is
$$\mathcal{O}_{C,P} = k[X,Y]_{(X,Y)} \big/ (Y^2 - X - X^3),$$
with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}_P = (X,Y)/(Y^2 - X - X^3)$.

**Step 1: Check nonsingularity.** Let $F = Y^2 - X - X^3$. Then $F_X = -1 - 3X^2
$ and $F_Y = 2Y$. At $P = (0,0)$: $F_X(P) = -1 \neq 0$. Hence $C$ is nonsingular
 at $P$, so $\mathcal{O}_{C,P}$ is a DVR and $\mathfrak{m}_P$ is principal (as s
tated).

**Step 2: Relate $X$ and $Y$ in the local ring.** In $\mathcal{O}_{C,P}$ we have
$$Y^2 = X + X^3 = X(1 + X^2).$$
The element $1 + X^2$ evaluates to $1$ at $P$, so it does not lie in $\mathfrak{
m}_P$ and is therefore a **unit** in the local ring. Setting $u = (1+X^2)^{-1}$,
 we obtain
$$X = Y^2 \cdot u.$$

**Step 3: Conclude $\mathfrak{m}_P = (Y)$.** Since $X = Y^2 u \in (Y)$, every el
ement of $\mathfrak{m}_P = (X,Y)$ lies in $(Y)$. Conversely $Y \in \mathfrak{m}_
P$ trivially. Thus
$$\mathfrak{m}_P = (Y).$$

Moreover $Y \in \mathfrak{m}_P$ (it vanishes at $P$), so $Y$ is not a unit; it i
s a uniformizer of the DVR.

**A generator of the maximal ideal is $\boxed{Y}$.**
