**Step 1: Leray spectral sequence.** Since the fibers $\mathbb{C}^*$ are Stein (
affine), $R^q\pi_*\mathcal{O} = 0$ for $q \geq 1$. By the projection formula:
$$R^q\pi_*\pi^*\mathcal{T} = \mathcal{T} \otimes R^q\pi_*\mathcal{O}.$$
Thus $R^q\pi_*\pi^*\mathcal{T} = 0$ for $q \geq 1$, and the Leray spectral seque
nce degenerates at $E_2$, giving:
$$H^1(\mathbb{C}^n \setminus \{0\},\, \pi^*\mathcal{T}) \cong H^1(\mathbb{CP}^{n
-1},\, \pi_*\pi^*\mathcal{T}).$$

**Step 2: Pushforward decomposition.** The $\mathbb{C}^*$-bundle $\pi$ has $\pi_

athcal{O}(k)$ correspond to weight-$k$ functions on $\pi^{-1}(U)$.) By the proje

$$\pi_*\pi^*\mathcal{T} = \mathcal{T} \otimes \bigoplus_{k \in \mathbb{Z}} \math
cal{O}(k) = \bigoplus_{k \in \mathbb{Z}} \bigl(\mathcal{T} \otimes \mathcal{O}(k
)\bigr).$$
Since $\mathbb{CP}^{n-1}$ is Noetherian, sheaf cohomology commutes with direct s
ums (filtered colimits):
$$H^1(\mathbb{CP}^{n-1},\, \pi_*\pi^*\mathcal{T}) = \bigoplus_{k \in \mathbb{Z}}
 H^1\bigl(\mathbb{CP}^{n-1},\, \mathcal{T} \otimes \mathcal{O}(k)\bigr). \tag{$\
star$}$$

**Step 3: Euler sequence.** On $\mathbb{CP}^{n-1}$ (with $N = n-1$), the Euler s

$$0 \to \mathcal{O} \to \mathcal{O}(1)^{\oplus n} \to \mathcal{T} \to 0.$$
Tensoring with $\mathcal{O}(k)$:
$$0 \to \mathcal{O}(k) \to \mathcal{O}(k+1)^{\oplus n} \to \mathcal{T} \otimes \
mathcal{O}(k) \to 0. \tag{$\dagger$}$$

**Key fact:** For $\mathbb{CP}^N$ with $N \geq 2$, the intermediate cohomology o

**Step 4: Case $n \geq 4$ ($N = n-1 \geq 3$).** Here $H^1(\mathcal{O}(k)) = 0$ a

$$H^1(\mathcal{O}(k+1))^{\oplus n} \to H^1(\mathcal{T} \otimes \mathcal{O}(k)) \
to H^2(\mathcal{O}(k)),$$
both flanking terms vanish, so $H^1(\mathcal{T} \otimes \mathcal{O}(k)) = 0$ for

$$\boxed{H^1(\mathbb{C}^n \setminus \{0\},\, \pi^*\mathcal{T}) = 0 \quad \text{f
or } n \geq 4.}$$

$$H^1(\mathcal{T} \otimes \mathcal{O}(k)) = \ker\!\Bigl(H^2(\mathcal{O}(k)) \xri
ghtarrow{\;\beta_k\;} H^2(\mathcal{O}(k+1))^{\oplus 3}\Bigr).$$

Now we analyze each $k$:

$$\boxed{H^1(\mathbb{C}^3 \setminus \{0\},\, \pi^*\mathcal{T}_{\mathbb{CP}^2}) =
