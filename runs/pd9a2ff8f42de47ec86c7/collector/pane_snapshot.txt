## Setup

Place the octahedron with equatorial square ABCD in the z=0 plane, center at ori
gin. With side length 1:

- A = (1/√2, 0, 0), B = (0, 1/√2, 0), C = (−1/√2, 0, 0), D = (0, −1/√2, 0)
- E = (0, 0, 1/√2), F = (0, 0, −1/√2) (since (1/√2)² + h² = 1 gives h = 1/√2)

**Parametrize G and H.** Let G = B + t(E−B) = (0, (1−t)/√2, t/√2), so BG = t·|BE
| = t. Let H = D + s(F−D) = (0, (s−1)/√2, −s/√2), so DH = s.

**Symmetry.** The inversion (x,y,z)→(−x,−y,−z) maps the octahedron to itself, sw
apping A↔C, B↔D, E↔F. It maps segment BE→DF and plane AGD→plane BCH. For the con
figuration to be consistent with three equal pieces, this symmetry forces **t =
s**, and guarantees the E-piece and F-piece have equal volume. We then need only
 one equation: the E-piece equals V/3.

## Plane Equations

**Plane AGD** through A, D, G: computing the normal via (D−A)×(G−A) gives n = (t
, −t, 2−t), yielding:
$$tx - ty + (2-t)z = \frac{t}{\sqrt{2}}$$

**Plane BCH** through B, C, H (with s=t): same normal direction, giving:
$$tx - ty + (2-t)z = -\frac{t}{\sqrt{2}}$$

The two planes are **parallel**, symmetric about the origin — dividing the octah
edron into three slabs.

## Volume of the E-piece

The plane AGD intersects octahedron edges from E at:
- Edge EA: at vertex A (on the plane)
- Edge EB: at G
- Edge EC: solving gives parameter u = 1−t, point P₁ = (−(1−t)/√2, 0, t/√2)
- Edge ED: at vertex D (on the plane)

The E-piece is the pyramid with apex E and base quadrilateral A−G−P₁−D.

**Area of base** (split into triangles AGP₁ and AP₁D):

Using cross products:
- |AG × AP₁| = (1−t)/2 · √(3t²−4t+4), so Area(AGP₁) = (1−t)/4 · √(3t²−4t+4)
- |AP₁ × AD| = √(3t²−4t+4)/2, so Area(AP₁D) = √(3t²−4t+4)/4

Total area = **(2−t)/4 · √(3t²−4t+4)**

**Distance from E to plane:**
$$d = \frac{|(2-t)/\sqrt{2} - t/\sqrt{2}|}{\sqrt{3t^2-4t+4}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}\,(
1-t)}{\sqrt{3t^2-4t+4}}$$

**Volume of E-piece:**
$$V_E = \frac{1}{3}\cdot\frac{(2-t)}{4}\sqrt{3t^2-4t+4}\cdot\frac{\sqrt{2}(1-t)}
{\sqrt{3t^2-4t+4}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{12}(2-t)(1-t)$$

## Solving

Total octahedron volume V = √2/3. Setting V_E = V/3 = √2/9:

$$\frac{\sqrt{2}}{12}(2-t)(1-t) = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{9}$$
$$(2-t)(1-t) = \frac{4}{3}$$
$$t^2 - 3t + \frac{2}{3} = 0 \implies 3t^2 - 9t + 2 = 0$$
$$t = \frac{9 \pm \sqrt{57}}{6}$$

Since t ∈ [0,1], we take t = (9 − √57)/6 ≈ 0.2417 (the other root ≈ 2.76 is inva
lid).

**Verification:** The F-piece has equal volume by symmetry. The middle piece = V
 − 2V_E = √2/3 − 2·√2/9 = √2/9 = V/3. ✓ All three pieces are equal.

## Answer

$$\boxed{BG = \dfrac{9-\sqrt{57}}{6}}$$
