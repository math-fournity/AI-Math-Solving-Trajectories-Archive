# Problem

You are on a ladder with 5 rungs, numbered 1 to 5 from bottom to top. You have a fair coin and an envelope containing either a double-headed coin or a double-tailed coin (each with probability $1/2$). Every minute you flip a coin. Heads moves you up a rung; tails moves you down. Moving up from rung 5 results in a win; moving down from rung 1 results in a loss. You can open the envelope at any time; however, you must immediately flip that coin once. After this, you can choose to use either the coin from the envelope or the fair coin whenever you want. Let $S$ be the set of rungs $n \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$ where opening the envelope is part of the optimal strategy to maximize the probability of winning. Find the sum of the elements in $S$.
