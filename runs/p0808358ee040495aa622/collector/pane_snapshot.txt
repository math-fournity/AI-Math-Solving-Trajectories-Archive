## Solution

We work in the Hilbert space $H = L^2([0,\infty), e^{-x}dx)$ with inner product
$\langle f, g\rangle = \int_0^\infty f(x)g(x)\,e^{-x}dx$.

The problem is to minimize $\|x^3 - p\|^2$ where $p(x) = a + bx + cx^2$ ranges o
ver all polynomials of degree $\le 2$. This is a **least-squares projection prob
lem**: the minimum is achieved when $p$ is the orthogonal projection of $x^3$ on
to the subspace $V = \mathrm{span}\{1, x, x^2\}$, and the minimum value is $\|x^
3 - \mathrm{proj}_V x^3\|^2$.

### Orthonormal basis via Laguerre polynomials

The weight $e^{-x}$ on $[0,\infty)$ gives the **Laguerre polynomials** as orthog
onal polynomials. The standard normalization
$$L_n(x) = \frac{e^x}{n!}\frac{d^n}{dx^n}\!\left(x^n e^{-x}\right)$$
satisfies the orthonormality relation
$$\langle L_n, L_m\rangle = \int_0^\infty L_n(x)L_m(x)\,e^{-x}dx = \delta_{nm}.$
$

The first few are:
- $L_0 = 1$
- $L_1 = 1 - x$
- $L_2 = 1 - 2x + \tfrac{1}{2}x^2$
- $L_3 = 1 - 3x + \tfrac{3}{2}x^2 - \tfrac{1}{6}x^3$

The leading coefficient of $L_n$ is $(-1)^n/n!$.

### Expansion of $x^3$

Since $x^3$ is a polynomial of degree 3, it has a finite expansion in the orthon
ormal basis:
$$x^3 = \sum_{n=0}^{3} \langle x^3, L_n\rangle\, L_n.$$

The projection onto $V = \mathrm{span}\{L_0, L_1, L_2\}$ is
$$\mathrm{proj}_V\, x^3 = \sum_{n=0}^{2} \langle x^3, L_n\rangle\, L_n,$$
and the residual is
$$x^3 - \mathrm{proj}_V\, x^3 = \langle x^3, L_3\rangle\, L_3.$$

### Computing $\langle x^3, L_3\rangle$

By orthonormality, the minimum value is
$$\|x^3 - \mathrm{proj}_V\, x^3\|^2 = |\langle x^3, L_3\rangle|^2 \cdot \|L_3\|^
2 = |\langle x^3, L_3\rangle|^2.$$

To find $\langle x^3, L_3\rangle$, compare leading coefficients. In the expansio
n $x^3 = \sum_{n=0}^3 \langle x^3,L_n\rangle L_n$, the coefficient of $x^3$ on t
he right-hand side comes only from the $L_3$ term. Since $L_3$ has leading coeff
icient $-1/6$:
$$\langle x^3, L_3\rangle \cdot \left(-\tfrac{1}{6}\right) = 1 \quad\Longrightar
row\quad \langle x^3, L_3\rangle = -6.$$

**Direct verification:**
$$\langle x^3, L_3\rangle = \int_0^\infty x^3\!\left(1 - 3x + \tfrac{3}{2}x^2 -
\tfrac{1}{6}x^3\right)e^{-x}dx = 3! - 3\cdot 4! + \tfrac{3}{2}\cdot 5! - \tfrac{
1}{6}\cdot 6!$$
$$= 6 - 72 + 180 - 120 = -6. \quad\checkmark$$

### Minimum value

$$\boxed{36}$$

The minimum value of the integral is $|\langle x^3, L_3\rangle|^2 = (-6)^2 = 36$
, achieved when $a + bx + cx^2 = \mathrm{proj}_V\, x^3 = x^3 + 6L_3 = x^3 + 6\!\
left(1 - 3x + \tfrac{3}{2}x^2 - \tfrac{1}{6}x^3\right) = 6 - 18x + 9x^2$, i.e.,
$a=6,\; b=-18,\; c=9$.
