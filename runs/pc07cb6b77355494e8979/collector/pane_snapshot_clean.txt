## Proof

**Answer: Yes.** Every type $II_1$ factor contains a diffuse abelian subalgebra
that is not a maximal abelian subalgebra (MASA).

---

### Step 1. A MASA in $N$ exists and is diffuse.

The collection of abelian von Neumann subalgebras of $N$, ordered by inclusion,
is inductive: the weak closure of the union of an increasing chain of abelian se
lf-adjoint subalgebras is again abelian. By Zorn's lemma, $N$ contains a MASA $A
$.

We claim $A$ is diffuse. Suppose for contradiction that $A$ has a minimal projec
tion $e$. A standard fact about MASAs says that for any projection $e\in A$, the
 corner $eAe$ is a MASA in the reduced factor $eNe$. Since $e$ is minimal in the
 abelian algebra $A$, we have $eAe=\mathbb{C}e$. Thus $\mathbb{C}e$ is a MASA in
 $eNe$, forcing $eNe=\mathbb{C}e$; i.e., $e$ is a minimal projection of $N$. But
 a $II_1$ factor has no minimal projections (every nonzero projection can be hal
ved). Contradiction. Hence $A$ is diffuse.

### Step 2. A diffuse abelian algebra contains a proper diffuse abelian subalgeb
ra.

Since $A$ is diffuse and abelian, $A\cong L^\infty(X,\mu)$ for a non-atomic prob
ability space $(X,\mu)$. Concretely identify $A=L^\infty[0,1]$ with Lebesgue mea
sure. Define the doubling map
$$
\pi:[0,1]\to[0,1],\qquad \pi(t)=\begin{cases}2t,& t\in[0,\tfrac12),\\ 2t-1,& t\i
n[\tfrac12,1].\end{cases}
$$
This map is measure-preserving and essentially $2$-to-$1$. Set
$$
B:=\{g\circ\pi : g\in L^\infty[0,1]\}\subseteq A.
$$
$B$ is a unital abelian $*$-subalgebra of $A$. Because $\pi$ is measure-preservi
ng, the map $g\mapsto g\circ\pi$ is a $*$-isomorphism $L^\infty[0,1]\xrightarrow
{\sim} B$; in particular $B\cong L^\infty[0,1]$, so $B$ is **diffuse**.

$B$ is **proper** in $A$: the function $\mathbf{1}_{[0,1/2)}\in A$ takes differe
nt values on the two points of each generic fiber $\{t,t+\tfrac12\}$, so it is n
ot of the form $g\circ\pi$. Hence $B\subsetneq A$.

### Step 3. $B$ is not a MASA in $N$.

We have $B\subsetneq A\subseteq N$, with $A$ abelian. Since $A$ is an abelian su
balgebra of $N$ strictly larger than $B$, the algebra $B$ cannot be maximal abel
ian in $N$.

### Conclusion.

$B$ is a diffuse abelian subalgebra of the $II_1$ factor $N$ that is not a MASA.
 Therefore such a subalgebra always exists.
