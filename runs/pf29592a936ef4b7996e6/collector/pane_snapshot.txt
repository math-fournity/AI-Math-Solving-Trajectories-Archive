## Solution

We wish to compute $\log_{54} 168$ given $\log_7 12 = a$ and $\log_{12} 24 = b$.

### Setup

Use prime factorizations and let $x = \log 2$, $y = \log 3$, $z = \log 7$ (in an
y fixed base):

$$168 = 2^3 \cdot 3 \cdot 7,\quad 54 = 2 \cdot 3^3,\quad 12 = 2^2 \cdot 3,\quad
24 = 2^3 \cdot 3.$$

The target is
$$\log_{54} 168 = \frac{3x + y + z}{x + 3y}.$$

### Extract the ratio $x/y$ from $b$

$$b = \log_{12} 24 = \frac{3x + y}{2x + y}.$$

So $3x + y = b(2x + y)$, i.e. $x(3 - 2b) = y(b - 1)$, hence

$$r := \frac{x}{y} = \frac{b - 1}{3 - 2b}.$$

### Extract $z$ from $a$

$$a = \log_7 12 = \frac{2x + y}{z} \implies z = \frac{2x + y}{a} = \frac{y(2r +
1)}{a}.$$

### Substitute into the target

Numerator: $3x + y + z = y\!\left(3r + 1 + \dfrac{2r+1}{a}\right)$.

Denominator: $x + 3y = y(r + 3)$.

Thus
$$\log_{54} 168 = \frac{3r + 1 + (2r+1)/a}{r + 3}.$$

### Simplify each piece using $r = \frac{b-1}{3-2b}$

- $r + 3 = \dfrac{b - 1 + 3(3 - 2b)}{3 - 2b} = \dfrac{8 - 5b}{3 - 2b}$.
- $3r + 1 = \dfrac{3(b-1) + (3-2b)}{3-2b} = \dfrac{b}{3-2b}$.
- $2r + 1 = \dfrac{2(b-1) + (3-2b)}{3-2b} = \dfrac{1}{3-2b}$, so $\dfrac{2r+1}{a
} = \dfrac{1}{a(3-2b)}$.

Numerator bracket:
$$3r + 1 + \frac{2r+1}{a} = \frac{b}{3-2b} + \frac{1}{a(3-2b)} = \frac{ab + 1}{a
(3-2b)}.$$

Therefore
$$\log_{54} 168 = \frac{(ab+1)/\bigl(a(3-2b)\bigr)}{(8-5b)/(3-2b)} = \frac{ab +
1}{a(3-2b)} \cdot \frac{3-2b}{8-5b}.$$

The factor $(3-2b)$ cancels, giving

$$\boxed{\log_{54} 168 = \dfrac{ab + 1}{a(8 - 5b)}}.$$

### Numerical verification

$a = \log_7 12 \approx 1.2770$, $b = \log_{12} 24 \approx 1.2790$.

$\dfrac{ab+1}{a(8-5b)} = \dfrac{1.6334 + 1}{1.2770 \times 1.6050} \approx \dfrac
{2.6334}{2.0497} \approx 1.2847$.

Direct: $\log_{54} 168 = \dfrac{\ln 168}{\ln 54} \approx \dfrac{5.1240}{3.9890}
\approx 1.2845$. ✓
