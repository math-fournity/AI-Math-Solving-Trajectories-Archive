## Proof

**Setup.** Let $V$ be a real vector space of dimension $2m$ equipped with a non-
singular quadratic form $Q$. We are told $Q$ is **hyperbolic**, meaning $Q$ is i
sometric to a direct sum of $m$ hyperbolic planes:

$$Q \cong \underbrace{\mathbb{H} \oplus \mathbb{H} \oplus \cdots \oplus \mathbb{
H}}_{m \text{ copies}},$$

where each hyperbolic plane $\mathbb{H}$ is a 2-dimensional space with form $Q_i
(x_i, y_i) = x_i y_i$ (equivalently, signature $(1,1)$). The signature of $Q$ is
 therefore $(m, m)$, and the Witt index of $Q$ is $m$.

We determine the maximal dimension of a **totally isotropic subspace** of $V$, i
.e., a subspace $W \subseteq V$ on which $Q$ vanishes identically: $Q|_W \equiv
0$.

---

**Step 1: A totally isotropic subspace of dimension $m$ exists.**

Choose coordinates so that $Q(x_1, y_1, \ldots, x_m, y_m) = x_1 y_1 + x_2 y_2 +
\cdots + x_m y_m$. Consider the subspace

$$W_0 = \{(x_1, 0, x_2, 0, \ldots, x_m, 0) : x_1, \ldots, x_m \in \mathbb{R}\}.$
$

For every $v \in W_0$, we have $y_i = 0$ for all $i$, so $Q(v) = \sum_i x_i \cdo
t 0 = 0$. Thus $Q$ vanishes identically on $W_0$, and $\dim W_0 = m$.

---

**Step 2: No totally isotropic subspace has dimension greater than $m$.**

Let $W \subseteq V$ be any totally isotropic subspace, so $Q(w) = 0$ for all $w
\in W$. Since $Q$ is non-singular, it induces a non-degenerate bilinear form $B$
 on $V$ via polarization. The condition $Q|_W \equiv 0$ implies $B(w, w') = 0$ f
or all $w, w' \in W$ (by the identity $Q(w+w') = Q(w) + Q(w') + 2B(w,w')$ and $Q
(w) = Q(w') = Q(w+w') = 0$). Hence $W \subseteq W^\perp$, where $W^\perp = \{v \
in V : B(v, w) = 0 \text{ for all } w \in W\}$.

Since $B$ is non-degenerate, $\dim W + \dim W^\perp = \dim V = 2m$. Combining wi
th $W \subseteq W^\perp$:

$$\dim W \le \dim W^\perp = 2m - \dim W \implies 2\dim W \le 2m \implies \dim W
\le m.$$

---

**Conclusion.** The maximal dimension of a subspace on which $Q$ vanishes is exa
ctly $m$.

$$\boxed{\dim W = m}$$
