## Solution

The **logarithmic residue** of $f$ with respect to a contour $C$ is

$$
\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_C \frac{f'(z)}{f(z)}\,dz \;=\; N - P,
$$

where $N$ is the number of zeros and $P$ the number of poles of $f$ inside $C$,
counted with multiplicity.

### Zeros of $f$

$$f(z)=\frac{1+z^2}{1-\cos 2\pi z}.$$

Zeros come from $1+z^2=0$, i.e. $z=\pm i$. Both satisfy $|\pm i|=1<\pi$, so they
 lie inside $|z|=\pi$. They are simple zeros, hence

$$N = 2.$$

### Poles of $f$

Poles come from $1-\cos 2\pi z = 0$, i.e. $\cos 2\pi z = 1$, i.e. $z=k\in\mathbb
{Z}$.

Inside $|z|=\pi$ (with $\pi\approx 3.14159$): $k=-3,-2,-1,0,1,2,3$, giving **7 p
oles**.

**Order of each pole.** Near $z=k$, set $w=z-k$:

$$1-\cos 2\pi z = 1-\cos 2\pi w = 2\sin^2(\pi w) = 2\pi^2 w^2 + O(w^4).$$

So $1-\cos 2\pi z$ has a **zero of order 2** at each integer, hence each pole of
 $f$ is of order 2. Therefore

$$P = 7\times 2 = 14.$$

### Logarithmic residue

The zeros $\pm i$ do not coincide with any pole (poles are at integers), so no c
ancellation occurs:

$$\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_{|z|=\pi}\frac{f'(z)}{f(z)}\,dz = N - P = 2 - 14 = -12.$
$

$$\boxed{-12}$$
