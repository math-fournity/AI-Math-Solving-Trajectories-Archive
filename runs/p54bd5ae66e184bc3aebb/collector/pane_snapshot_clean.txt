# Krull Dimension of $E_R(R/\mathfrak{m})$

## Answer: $0$

## Proof

Let $(R, \mathfrak{m})$ be a Noetherian local ring and let $E = E_R(R/\mathfrak{
m})$ be the injective hull of the residue field.

### Step 1: $E$ is $\mathfrak{m}$-torsion

Every element of $E$ is annihilated by some power of $\mathfrak{m}$. This is a s
tandard property of the injective hull of the residue field: $E = \bigcup_{n \ge
q 1} (0 :_E \mathfrak{m}^n)$, where each $(0 :_E \mathfrak{m}^n)$ is a submodule
 of finite length (isomorphic to $\text{Hom}_R(R/\mathfrak{m}^n, E)$, which by M
atlis duality has the same length as $R/\mathfrak{m}^n$).

### Step 2: $\text{Supp}(E) = \{\mathfrak{m}\}$

Since $E$ is $\mathfrak{m}$-torsion, for any prime $\mathfrak{p} \neq \mathfrak{
m}$, we can pick $s \in \mathfrak{m} \setminus \mathfrak{p}$ (possible because $
\mathfrak{m}$ is maximal and $\mathfrak{p} \subsetneq \mathfrak{m}$). For any $x
 \in E$, there exists $n$ with $\mathfrak{m}^n x = 0$, hence $s^n x = 0$. In the
 localization $E_\mathfrak{p}$, $s$ is a unit, so $x/1 = 0$. Thus $E_\mathfrak{p
} = 0$ for all $\mathfrak{p} \neq \mathfrak{m}$.

Since $R/\mathfrak{m} \hookrightarrow E$, we have $E_\mathfrak{m} \neq 0$, so $\
text{Supp}(E) = \{\mathfrak{m}\}$.

### Step 3: $\dim_R(E) = 0$ (classical Krull dimension of a module)

The Krull dimension of a module $M$ over a commutative ring is $\dim_R(M) = \dim
(R/\text{Ann}_R(M))$. Since $\text{Supp}(E) = V(\text{Ann}_R(E)) = \{\mathfrak{m
}\}$, the only prime ideal containing $\text{Ann}_R(E)$ is $\mathfrak{m}$ itself
. Therefore $R/\text{Ann}_R(E)$ has exactly one prime ideal (the image of $\math
frak{m}$), which is maximal. Hence:

$$\dim_R(E) = \dim(R/\text{Ann}_R(E)) = 0.$$

### Step 4: Confirmation via Gabriel–Rentschler Krull dimension

The Gabriel–Rentschler Krull dimension $\operatorname{Kdim}(M)$ also yields $0$.
 By Matlis's theorem, $E$ is an **Artinian** $R$-module: the Matlis duality func
tor $(-)^\vee = \text{Hom}_R(-, E)$ gives an anti-equivalence between Noetherian
 and Artinian $R$-modules, and $E \cong \hat{R}^{\,\vee}$ where $\hat{R}$ is Noe
therian. A nonzero Artinian module has Gabriel–Rentschler Krull dimension exactl
y $0$.

### Conclusion

Under both standard notions of Krull dimension for modules, the injective hull $
E_R(R/\mathfrak{m})$ has Krull dimension:

$$\boxed{0}$$
