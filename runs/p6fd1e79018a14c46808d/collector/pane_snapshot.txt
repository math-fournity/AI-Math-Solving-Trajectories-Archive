## Setup

$$A = (a, 0),\quad C = (-c, 0),\quad B = (0, b),\quad D = (0, -d), \quad a,b,c,d

**Given lengths:**

- $AD = 18 \Rightarrow a^2 + d^2 = 324 \quad (2)$

**Cyclic condition** (power of point X): $XA \cdot XC = XB \cdot XD$, so $ac = b

**Center O:** From equidistance to A, C and to B, D:
$$O = \left(\tfrac{a-c}{2},\, \tfrac{b-d}{2}\right).$$

## Circumcircle of AOC

Since A and C lie on the x-axis, the perpendicular bisector of AC is $x = \frac{
a-c}{2}$, which also contains O. So the center of the circumcircle of AOC is $\l
eft(\frac{a-c}{2},\, k'\right)$ for some $k'$.

Equidistance from center to A and to O gives:
$$\left(\tfrac{a+c}{2}\right)^2 + k'^2 = \left(k' - \tfrac{b-d}{2}\right)^2 \imp

**Point P on ray DB past B:** P = (0, p) with p > b. Substituting into the circu

$$\left(\tfrac{a-c}{2}\right)^2 + (p - k')^2 = \left(\tfrac{a+c}{2}\right)^2 + k

## The right angle condition

$\angle PAD = 90°$ means $\vec{AP} \cdot \vec{AD} = 0$:
$$(-a)(-a) + (p)(-d) = 0 \implies p = \frac{a^2}{d}. \quad (5)$$
