## Example

Let $k$ be a field and set $A = k[x_1, x_2, x_3, \dots]$, the polynomial ring in
 countably many variables. Write $S = \operatorname{Spec} A$ (an affine scheme,
hence quasi-compact), and let

$$U \;=\; \bigcup_{i \geq 1} D(x_i) \;\subseteq\; S.$$

### Step 1. $U$ is **not** quasi-compact.

The family $\{D(x_i)\}_{i \geq 1}$ is an open cover of $U$. Suppose a finite sub
cover existed, say $U = D(x_{i_1}) \cup \cdots \cup D(x_{i_n})$. Set $N = \max(i
_1,\dots,i_n)$ and consider the prime ideal

$$\mathfrak{r} = (x_1, x_2, \dots, x_N) \;\in\; \operatorname{Spec} A,$$

which is prime since $A/\mathfrak{r} \cong k[x_{N+1}, x_{N+2}, \dots]$ is a doma
in. Because $x_{N+1} \notin \mathfrak{r}$, we have $\mathfrak{r} \in D(x_{N+1})
\subseteq U$. Yet $x_{i_k} \in \mathfrak{r}$ for every $k = 1,\dots,n$ (since $i
_k \leq N$), so $\mathfrak{r} \notin D(x_{i_1}) \cup \cdots \cup D(x_{i_n})$. Co
ntradiction. Hence $U$ is not quasi-compact.

### Step 2. Build a quasi-compact (but non-quasi-separated) scheme $Y$.

Take two copies $S_1 = \operatorname{Spec} A$ and $S_2 = \operatorname{Spec} A$,
 and glue them along the open subscheme $U \subseteq S_1$ and $U \subseteq S_2$
via the identity map $\operatorname{id}_U$. The resulting scheme $Y$ is covered
by the two affine opens $S_1, S_2$, so **$Y$ is quasi-compact**. As subsets of $
Y$ we have $S_1 \cap S_2 = U$, which by Step 1 is not quasi-compact — so $Y$ is
not quasi-separated.

### Step 3. The morphism.

Let $X = S_1 = \operatorname{Spec} A$, which is affine and hence **quasi-compact
**, and let

$$f : X \hookrightarrow Y$$

be the open immersion of the first affine chart. Both $X$ and $Y$ are quasi-comp
act schemes.

### Step 4. $f$ is **not** quasi-compact.

The second chart $V = S_2 \subseteq Y$ is affine, hence a quasi-compact open sub
set of $Y$. Its preimage under $f$ is

$$f^{-1}(V) \;=\; X \cap V \;=\; S_1 \cap S_2 \;=\; U,$$

which is **not quasi-compact** by Step 1. Since the preimage of a quasi-compact
open of $Y$ fails to be quasi-compact, the morphism $f$ is not quasi-compact.

### Conclusion

We have produced a morphism $f : X \to Y$ between quasi-compact schemes (both co
vered by finitely many affines) that is not quasi-compact: the open immersion of
 one affine chart into the scheme obtained by gluing two copies of $\operatornam
e{Spec} k[x_1,x_2,\dots]$ along the non-quasi-compact open $\bigcup_i D(x_i)$.
