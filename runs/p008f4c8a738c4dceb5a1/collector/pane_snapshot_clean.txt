**Claim:** Yes. For any two unitary operators $T_1, T_2$ on a complex Hilbert sp
ace $H$, the spectrum $\sigma(T_1+T_2)$ is contained in the closed disc $\overli
ne{D(0,2)} = \{z\in\mathbb{C}: |z|\le 2\}$.

**Proof.**

Recall two standard facts from operator theory.

*Fact 1 (norm of a unitary).* If $U$ is unitary, i.e. $U^*U = UU^* = I$, then fo
r every $x\in H$,
$$\|Ux\|^2 = \langle Ux, Ux\rangle = \langle x, U^*Ux\rangle = \langle x,x\rangl
e = \|x\|^2,$$
so $\|U\| = 1$.

*Fact 2 (spectral radius bound).* For any bounded linear operator $A$ on a compl
ex Banach space, the spectrum $\sigma(A)$ is a nonempty compact subset of $\math
bb{C}$ contained in the closed disc of radius $r(A)$, where the spectral radius
$r(A)$ satisfies
$$r(A) = \lim_{n\to\infty}\|A^n\|^{1/n} \le \|A\|.$$
(The inequality is Gelfand's formula combined with submultiplicativity $\|A^n\|\
le\|A\|^n$.) Consequently
$$\sigma(A)\subseteq\{z\in\mathbb{C}: |z|\le \|A\|\}.$$

Now apply these to $A = T_1 + T_2$. By the triangle inequality for the operator
norm and Fact 1,
$$\|T_1 + T_2\| \le \|T_1\| + \|T_2\| = 1 + 1 = 2.$$

By Fact 2,
$$\sigma(T_1+T_2) \subseteq \{z\in\mathbb{C}: |z|\le \|T_1+T_2\|\} \subseteq \{z
\in\mathbb{C}: |z|\le 2\} = \overline{D(0,2)}.$$

Therefore the spectrum of $T_1+T_2$ is contained in the closed disc of radius $2
$. $\blacksquare$

**Remark.** The bound is sharp: taking $T_1 = T_2 = I$ (both unitary) gives $T_1
+T_2 = 2I$, whose spectrum is $\{2\}$, lying on the boundary of the disc.
