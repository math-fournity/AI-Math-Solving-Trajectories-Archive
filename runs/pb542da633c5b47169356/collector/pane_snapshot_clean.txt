## Proof

**Residues mod 7.** The available digits are $\{1,3,4,6,7,9\}$. Their residues m
odulo $7$ are
$$\{1,3,4,6,0,2\} = \{0,1,2,3,4,6\},$$
i.e. **all residues mod $7$ except $5$**, each appearing exactly once.

**Setup.** An $n$-digit number using only these digits is an ordered sequence $(
d_1,\dots,d_n)$ with each $d_i\in\{1,3,4,6,7,9\}$. We seek the count of such seq
uences with $\sum d_i \equiv 0\pmod 7$.

**Roots-of-unity filter.** Let $\omega=e^{2\pi i/7}$ and define
$$f(x)=1+x+x^2+x^3+x^4+x^6 = \sum_{r\in\{0,1,2,3,4,6\}} x^r.$$
The desired count is
$$N_n = \frac{1}{7}\sum_{j=0}^{6} f(\omega^j)^n.$$

**Evaluating $f(\omega^j)$.** For $j=0$: $f(1)=6$.

For $j=1,\dots,6$: since $\sum_{k=0}^{6}\omega^{jk}=0$ (geometric sum of all $7$
th roots), we have
$$f(\omega^j)=\sum_{r\in\{0,1,2,3,4,6\}}\omega^{jr}=\Bigl(\sum_{k=0}^{6}\omega^{
jk}\Bigr)-\omega^{5j}=0-\omega^{5j}=-\omega^{5j}.$$

Hence $f(\omega^j)^n = (-1)^n\,\omega^{5jn}$ for $j=1,\dots,6$.

**Summing over $j=1,\dots,6$.** We need $S:=\sum_{j=1}^{6}\omega^{5jn}$. Since $
\gcd(5,7)=1$, as $j$ ranges over $1,\dots,6$, the exponent $5jn\bmod 7$ ranges o
ver all nonzero residues mod $7$ when $7\nmid n$, and equals $0$ for every $j$ w
hen $7\mid n$. Therefore
$$\sum_{j=0}^{6}\omega^{5jn}=\begin{cases}0 & 7\nmid n\\ 7 & 7\mid n\end{cases}\
implies S=\sum_{j=1}^{6}\omega^{5jn}=\begin{cases}-1 & 7\nmid n\\ 6 & 7\mid n.\e
nd{cases}$$

**Final count.**
$$N_n=\frac{1}{7}\Bigl(6^n+(-1)^n S\Bigr).$$

Substituting $S$:

$$\boxed{N_n=\begin{cases}\dfrac{6^n-(-1)^n}{7} & \text{if }7\nmid n,\\[6pt]\dfr
ac{6^n+6\,(-1)^n}{7} & \text{if }7\mid n.\end{cases}}$$

Equivalently, in unified form:
$$N_n=\frac{6^n-(-1)^n}{7}+(-1)^n\,\mathbf{1}_{7\mid n}.$$

**Verification.**
- $n=1$: digits with residue $0$ mod $7$ — only the digit $7$. Formula: $(6-(-1)
)/7=7/7=1$. ✓
- $n=2$: ordered pairs of residues from $\{0,1,2,3,4,6\}$ summing to $0\pmod 7$:
 $(0,0),(1,6),(3,4),(4,3),(6,1)$ — five pairs. Formula: $(36-1)/7=35/7=5$. ✓
- $n=7$: $(6^7+6(-1)^7)/7=(279936-6)/7=279930/7=39990$. ✓
