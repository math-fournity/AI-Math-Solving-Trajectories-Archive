## Solution

We determine the symbol and principal symbol of the given differential operator.

### Setup and Definitions

The operator is
$$Lu = \frac{\partial^4 u}{\partial x^4} - 2\,\frac{\partial^4 u}{\partial x^2\,
\partial y^2} + \frac{\partial^4 u}{\partial y^4}, \qquad x,y \in \mathbb{R}.$$

This is a linear differential operator with **constant coefficients** and **orde
r** $m = 4$.

For a linear differential operator $L = \sum_{|\alpha| \le m} a_\alpha\, D^\alph
a$ with constant coefficients, the **(total) symbol** is the polynomial obtained
 by formally replacing each derivative $D^\alpha = \partial^{|\alpha|}/\partial
z^\alpha$ by the corresponding monomial in the dual (frequency) variables:
$$\sigma_L(\xi) = \sum_{|\alpha| \le m} a_\alpha\, \xi^\alpha.$$

The **principal symbol** retains only the highest-order terms ($|\alpha| = m$):
$$\sigma_m(L)(\xi) = \sum_{|\alpha| = m} a_\alpha\, \xi^\alpha.$$

Per the problem's convention, we use $x$ and $y$ as the dual variables (i.e., $\
xi \to x$, $\eta \to y$).

### Computing the Symbol

Under the replacement $\partial/\partial x \to x$, $\partial/\partial y \to y$ (
equivalently, with the $i$-convention $\partial/\partial x_j \to i\,x_j$, since
$i^4 = 1$ the result is identical for a 4th-order operator), each term transform
s as:

$$\frac{\partial^4}{\partial x^4} \;\longrightarrow\; x^4, \qquad \frac{\partial
^4}{\partial x^2\,\partial y^2} \;\longrightarrow\; x^2 y^2, \qquad \frac{\parti
al^4}{\partial y^4} \;\longrightarrow\; y^4.$$

Therefore the **(total) symbol** is
$$\boxed{\sigma_L(x, y) \;=\; x^4 - 2\,x^2 y^2 + y^4 \;=\; (x^2 - y^2)^2.}$$

### Computing the Principal Symbol

The operator $L$ is **homogeneous of degree 4**: every term involves exactly fou
rth-order derivatives, and there are no lower-order terms. Consequently the full
 symbol consists *entirely* of the order-4 part, so the principal symbol coincid
es with the total symbol:

$$\boxed{\sigma_4(L)(x, y) \;=\; x^4 - 2\,x^2 y^2 + y^4 \;=\; (x^2 - y^2)^2.}$$

### Verification

We can factor the operator itself:
$$L = \partial_x^4 - 2\,\partial_x^2\,\partial_y^2 + \partial_y^4 = (\partial_x^
2 - \partial_y^2)^2 = (\partial_x - \partial_y)^2(\partial_x + \partial_y)^2,$$
which under the symbol map becomes
$$\sigma_L(x,y) = (x^2 - y^2)^2 = (x-y)^2(x+y)^2,$$
confirming the result. The characteristic variety $\{\sigma_4 = 0\}$ is the unio
n of the lines $x = y$ and $x = -y$ in the cotangent space, reflecting the facto
rization into transport operators along those directions.

### Summary

- **Symbol:** $\sigma_L(x, y) = x^4 - 2x^2 y^2 + y^4 = (x^2 - y^2)^2$
- **Principal symbol:** $\sigma_4(L)(x, y) = x^4 - 2x^2 y^2 + y^4 = (x^2 - y^2)^
2$

Since $L$ is homogeneous of degree 4 (no lower-order terms), the symbol and prin
cipal symbol are identical.
