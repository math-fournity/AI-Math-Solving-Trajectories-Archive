## Setup

Let $f(x,y,z) = |x|+|y|+|z|+|x+y+z| - |x+y|-|y+z|-|z+x|$. We seek the volume of
the region in $[-1,1]^3$ where $f=0$, divided by $8$ (the cube's volume).

The function $f$ is symmetric under permutations of $(x,y,z)$ and under $(x,y,z)
\mapsto(-x,-y,-z)$. It is piecewise linear, so we analyze it by sign pattern.

## All same sign

If $x,y,z\ge 0$: $f = (x+y+z)+(x+y+z) - (x+y)-(y+z)-(z+x) = 0$. Similarly for al
l $\le 0$. So $f=0$ throughout the two "all-same-sign" octants. **Volume = 2** (
two octants, each of volume $1$).

## Mixed signs: two positive, one negative

By symmetry it suffices to take $x,y\ge 0$, $z=-w\le 0$ ($w\ge 0$). Then
$$f = x+y+w+|x+y-w|-(x+y)-|y-w|-|x-w| = w+|x+y-w|-|x-w|-|y-w|.$$

Examine subcases (boundaries are measure-zero and don't affect volume):

- **$w\le \min(x,y)$**: $|x+y-w|=x+y-w,\ |x-w|=x-w,\ |y-w|=y-w$, giving $f=2w\ge
 0$, equality only at $w=0$.
- **$\min(x,y)\le w\le \max(x,y)$** (say $x\le w\le y$): $|x+y-w|=x+y-w,\ |x-w|=
w-x,\ |y-w|=y-w$, giving $f=2x\ge 0$, equality only at $x=0$.
- **$w\ge \max(x,y)$**: $|x-w|=w-x,\ |y-w|=w-y$.
  - If $w\le x+y$: $f=2(x+y-w)\ge 0$, equality only at $w=x+y$.
  - If $w\ge x+y$: $|x+y-w|=w-x-y$, giving $f=(w-x-y)+x+y-w=0$.

So in the interior of the mixed-sign region, **equality holds exactly when $w\ge
 x+y$**, i.e. $|z|\ge x+y$. This also confirms $f\ge 0$ everywhere (Hlawka's ine
quality in one dimension), with the stated equality conditions.

## Volume of the "one dominant opposite-sign" regions

For the configuration $x,y\in[0,1]$, $z=-w\in[-1,0]$, $w\ge x+y$:
$$V = \int_0^1\!\!\int_0^{1-x}(1-x-y)\,dy\,dx = \int_0^1 \tfrac{(1-x)^2}{2}\,dx
= \tfrac{1}{6}.$$

There are $6$ such configurations (choose which variable is the lone opposite si
gn: $3$ ways; choose its sign: $2$ ways), each contributing $1/6$, disjoint from
 each other and from the all-same-sign octants. **Total = $6\cdot\tfrac{1}{6}=1$
**.

## Final computation

Total equality volume $= 2 + 1 = 3$. Cube volume $= 8$.

$$P = \frac{3}{8},\qquad a=3,\ b=8,\qquad a+b = \boxed{11}.$$
