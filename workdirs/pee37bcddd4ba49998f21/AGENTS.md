# Problem

A specialized chemical refinery operates a purification cycle controlled by a digital pressure valve. The valve's pressure setting for any given hour $k$ is denoted by $g(k)$.

The initial pressure setting at the start of the operation (hour $0$) is exactly $2017$ units. For every subsequent hour $k$, the pressure is adjusted based on the previous hour’s setting according to a specific recalibration formula: the new pressure is determined by doubling the previous hour's pressure, adding $25$, and then taking the remainder when that total is divided by $31$.

The refinery supervisor needs to calculate the cumulative pressure load for the entire duration of the project. If the project runs from the start (hour $0$) through the end of hour $2017$, calculate the sum of all hourly pressure settings $g(0) + g(1) + \dots + g(2017)$ and provide the final result as the remainder when this sum is divided by $31$.
