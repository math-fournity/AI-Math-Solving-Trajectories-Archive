## Proof

Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & I_n \\ -I_n & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ be the standard symp
lectic form on $\mathbb{C}^{2n}$. The symplectic group is

$$
\operatorname{Sp}(2n,\mathbb{C}) = \{ M \in \mathrm{GL}_{2n}(\mathbb{C}) : M^{T}
 J M = J \}.
$$

Since $\operatorname{Sp}(2n,\mathbb{C})$ is a Lie group, its dimension equals th
e dimension of its Lie algebra

$$
\mathfrak{sp}(2n,\mathbb{C}) = \{ X \in M_{2n}(\mathbb{C}) : X^{T} J + J X = 0 \
},
$$

obtained by differentiating $M^{T}JM = J$ at the identity.

Write any $2n \times 2n$ matrix in $n \times n$ blocks:

$$
X = \begin{pmatrix} A & B \\ C & D \end{pmatrix}, \qquad X^{T} = \begin{pmatrix}
 A^{T} & C^{T} \\ B^{T} & D^{T} \end{pmatrix}.
$$

Compute the two products:

$$
X^{T} J = \begin{pmatrix} A^{T} & C^{T} \\ B^{T} & D^{T} \end{pmatrix} \begin{pm
atrix} 0 & I_n \\ -I_n & 0 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -C^{T} & A^{T} \\ -D^
{T} & B^{T} \end{pmatrix},
$$

$$
J X = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & I_n \\ -I_n & 0 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} A & B \\
 C & D \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} C & D \\ -A & -B \end{pmatrix}.
$$

Adding them and setting the result to zero gives the block conditions

$$
X^{T} J + J X = \begin{pmatrix} -C^{T}+C & A^{T}+D \\ -D^{T}-A & B^{T}-B \end{pm
atrix} = 0.
$$

Hence the defining conditions are:

1. $C = C^{T}$ — the block $C$ is **symmetric**,
2. $B = B^{T}$ — the block $B$ is **symmetric**,
3. $D = -A^{T}$ — the block $D$ is completely determined by $A$.

(The lower-left condition $-D^{T}-A = 0$ is equivalent to condition 3, so it giv
es nothing new.)

Now count free complex parameters:

- $A$: arbitrary $n \times n$ matrix $\Rightarrow$ $n^{2}$ parameters.
- $B$: symmetric $n \times n$ matrix $\Rightarrow$ $\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$ parameter
s.
- $C$: symmetric $n \times n$ matrix $\Rightarrow$ $\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$ parameter
s.
- $D = -A^{T}$: determined by $A$ $\Rightarrow$ $0$ free parameters.

Therefore

$$
\dim_{\mathbb{C}} \mathfrak{sp}(2n,\mathbb{C}) = n^{2} + \frac{n(n+1)}{2} + \fra
c{n(n+1)}{2} = n^{2} + n(n+1) = 2n^{2} + n.
$$

**Consistency check.** For $n = 1$, $\operatorname{Sp}(2,\mathbb{C}) = \operator
name{SL}(2,\mathbb{C})$, which has dimension $3$. The formula gives $2(1)^{2} +
1 = 3$. ✓

Since $\operatorname{Sp}(2n,\mathbb{C})$ is connected (it is generated by expone
ntials of its Lie algebra, and one may also verify it is the complexification of
 the compact connected group $\operatorname{Sp}(n)$), its dimension as a complex
 Lie group equals the dimension of its Lie algebra. Thus

$$
\boxed{\dim_{\mathbb{C}} \operatorname{Sp}(2n,\mathbb{C}) = 2n^{2} + n.}
$$
