Thus
$$E = \bigl(\sin\beta - t_E(q+\sin\beta),\; -\cos\beta + t_E(\cos\alpha+\cos\bet
a)\bigr),$$
$$F = \bigl(\sin\beta + t_F(q-\sin\beta),\; -\cos\beta + t_F(\cos\alpha+\cos\bet

$$E_x - (G_0)_x = M' - t_E(q+\sin\beta), \qquad F_x - (G_0)_x = M' + t_F(q-\sin\

$$\bigl[M' - t_E(q+\sin\beta)\bigr](t_F-1) - \bigl[M' + t_F(q-\sin\beta)\bigr](t

$$= (M'-\sin\beta)(t_F-t_E) - 2q\,t_E t_F + q(t_E+t_F) = \frac{v}{\sin\beta}(t_F
