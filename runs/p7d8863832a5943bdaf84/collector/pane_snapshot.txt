## Proof

Let $I$ be the incenter of the regular tetrahedron $ABCD$. In a regular tetrahed
ron the incenter coincides with the centroid and circumcenter, so
$$AI = BI = CI = DI = R = \frac{s\sqrt{6}}{4}, \qquad \rho = \frac{s\sqrt{6}}{12
}$$
where $R$ is the circumradius and $\rho$ the inradius (distance from $I$ to any
face).

**Locating the corner-sphere centers.** The sphere centered at $P$ is tangent to
 the three faces $ABC, ACD, ADB$ — precisely the three faces through vertex $A$.
 The locus of points equidistant from these three faces is the line $AI$ (since
$I$ is equidistant from all four faces, hence from these three). So $P\in AI$.

For a point $X$ on segment $AI$, written $X = A+\lambda(I-A)$, the (signed) dist
ance to face $ABC$ is an affine function: it equals $0$ at $A$ and $\rho$ at $I$
, hence equals $\lambda\rho$. Requiring this distance to be $r$ gives
$$\lambda\rho = r \implies \lambda = \frac{r}{\rho} = \frac{r}{s\sqrt6/12}.$$
Then
$$AP = \lambda\cdot AI = \frac{r}{\rho}\cdot R = r\cdot\frac{R}{\rho} = r\cdot\f
rac{s\sqrt6/4}{s\sqrt6/12} = 3r.$$
By symmetry, $BQ = CR = DS = 3r$ as well, with $Q\in BI$, $R'\in CI$, $S\in DI$.

**Locating the fifth sphere.** Place $I$ at the origin. Since $A+B+C+D=0$ (centr
oid), we have
$$P = A\Bigl(1-\tfrac{3r}{R}\Bigr),\quad Q = B\Bigl(1-\tfrac{3r}{R}\Bigr),\quad
R' = C\Bigl(1-\tfrac{3r}{R}\Bigr),\quad S = D\Bigl(1-\tfrac{3r}{R}\Bigr).$$
These four points are invariant under the tetrahedral symmetry group, so any poi
nt equidistant from all of them must be the fixed point of that symmetry — namel
y $I$. The fifth sphere $T$ (radius $r$) touches each corner sphere externally,
so its center is at distance $2r$ from each of $P,Q,R',S$; by symmetry $T=I$. No
w
$$IP = AI - AP = R - 3r.$$
The external tangency condition $IP = 2r$ yields
$$R - 3r = 2r \implies R = 5r \implies r = \frac{R}{5} = \frac{s\sqrt6/4}{5} = \
frac{s\sqrt6}{20}.$$

**Validity checks.**
- *Corner spheres don't overlap:* $PQ = |A-B|\bigl(1-\tfrac{3r}{R}\bigr) = s\cdo
t\tfrac{2}{5} = \tfrac{2s}{5}$, while $2r = \tfrac{s\sqrt6}{10}\approx\tfrac{2.4
49\,s}{10} < \tfrac{4s}{10}=\tfrac{2s}{5}$. ✓
- *$T$ stays inside:* distance from $I$ to any face is $\rho = \tfrac{s\sqrt6}{1
2} > \tfrac{s\sqrt6}{20}=r$. ✓
- *Corner spheres don't cross the opposite face:* $P$ is displaced from $I$ towa
rd $A$ by $IP=2r=\tfrac{2R}{5}$ along the inward normal of $BCD$, so its distanc
e to $BCD$ is $\rho+\tfrac{2R}{5} > r$. ✓

All constraints are satisfied, giving

$$\boxed{r = \dfrac{s\sqrt{6}}{20}}.$$
