## Solution

We evaluate:
$$L = \lim_{n\to\infty} e^{-n} \left(1+n+\frac{n^2}{2}+\ldots+\frac{n^n}{n!}\rig
ht) = \lim_{n\to\infty} e^{-n}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\frac{n^k}{k!}.$$

**Probistic interpretation.** Recall that for $X \sim \mathrm{Poisson}(\lambda)$
,
$$P(X \le m) = e^{-\lambda}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\frac{\lambda^k}{k!}.$$
Setting $\lambda = n$ and $m = n$, the expression inside the limit is exactly
$$L = \lim_{n\to\infty} P(X_n \le n), \qquad X_n \sim \mathrm{Poisson}(n).$$

**Reduction to a sum of i.i.d. variables.** A Poisson$(n)$ variable decomposes a
s
$$X_n = \sum_{i=1}^{n} Y_i, \qquad Y_i \stackrel{\text{i.i.d.}}{\sim} \mathrm{Po
isson}(1),$$
since the sum of $n$ independent Poisson$(1)$ variables is Poisson$(n)$. Each $Y
_i$ has
$$\mathbb{E}[Y_i] = 1, \qquad \mathrm{Var}(Y_i) = 1.$$

**Applying the Central Limit Theorem.** Centering and scaling,
$$\frac{X_n - n}{\sqrt{n}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}\sum_{i=1}^{n}(Y_i - 1).$$
The variables $Y_i - 1$ are i.i.d. with mean $0$ and variance $1$, so by the cla
ssical CLT (Lindeberg–Lévy),
$$\frac{X_n - n}{\sqrt{n}} \xrightarrow{d} Z, \qquad Z \sim N(0,1).$$

**Evaluating the limit.** We have
$$P(X_n \le n) = P\!\left(\frac{X_n - n}{\sqrt{n}} \le 0\right).$$
By the CLT, the distribution functions converge at every continuity point of the
 limit. Since the standard normal CDF $\Phi$ is continuous everywhere, in partic
ular at $x = 0$:
$$\lim_{n\to\infty} P\!\left(\frac{X_n - n}{\sqrt{n}} \le 0\right) = \Phi(0) = \
frac{1}{2}.$$

**Conclusion.**
$$\boxed{\lim_{n\to\infty} e^{-n}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\frac{n^k}{k!} = \frac{1}{2}}.$$

### PROOF COMPLETE
