## Solution

**Setup.** The total kinetic energy in the closed region $V$ with fixed solid bo
undary $\partial V$ is

$$T = \int_V \tfrac{1}{2}\rho\,|\underline{u}|^2\,dV.$$

Since $\rho$ is constant,

$$\frac{dT}{dt} = \frac{\rho}{2}\int_V \frac{\partial}{\partial t}|\underline{u}
|^2\,dV = \rho\int_V \underline{u}\cdot \underline{u}_t\,dV.$$

**Apply the Euler equation.** From
$$\underline{u}_t + (\underline{u}\cdot\nabla)\underline{u} = -\tfrac{1}{\rho}\n
abla p,$$
we get
$$\underline{u}\cdot\underline{u}_t = -\underline{u}\cdot(\underline{u}\cdot\nab
la)\underline{u} - \tfrac{1}{\rho}\,\underline{u}\cdot\nabla p.$$

**Vector identity for the convective term.** In index notation,
$$\underline{u}\cdot(\underline{u}\cdot\nabla)\underline{u} = u_i\,u_j\,\partial
_j u_i = u_j\,\partial_j\!\left(\tfrac{1}{2}u_i u_i\right) = (\underline{u}\cdot
\nabla)\tfrac{1}{2}|\underline{u}|^2.$$

Hence
$$\frac{dT}{dt} = -\frac{\rho}{2}\int_V (\underline{u}\cdot\nabla)|\underline{u}
|^2\,dV - \int_V \underline{u}\cdot\nabla p\,dV.$$

**Use the divergence theorem with incompressibility.** For any scalar $\phi$, us
ing $\nabla\cdot\underline{u}=0$,
$$\nabla\cdot(\phi\,\underline{u}) = \underline{u}\cdot\nabla\phi + \phi\,\nabla
\cdot\underline{u} = \underline{u}\cdot\nabla\phi.$$

Applying this to $\phi = |\underline{u}|^2$ and $\phi = p$:

- $\displaystyle \int_V (\underline{u}\cdot\nabla)|\underline{u}|^2\,dV = \int_V
 \nabla\cdot(|\underline{u}|^2\underline{u})\,dV = \oint_{\partial V} |\underlin
e{u}|^2\,\underline{u}\cdot\underline{n}\,dS.$
- $\displaystyle \int_V \underline{u}\cdot\nabla p\,dV = \int_V \nabla\cdot(p\,\
underline{u})\,dV = \oint_{\partial V} p\,\underline{u}\cdot\underline{n}\,dS.$

**Boundary condition.** The boundary $\partial V$ is a fixed solid wall, so the
normal velocity vanishes there:
$$\underline{u}\cdot\underline{n}\big|_{\partial V} = 0.$$

Both surface integrals therefore vanish, giving

$$\boxed{\dfrac{dT}{dt} = 0.}$$

This is the expected result: for an inviscid (Euler) incompressible fluid confin
ed by fixed solid walls, there is no mechanism for energy flux through the bound
ary and no dissipation, so the total kinetic energy is conserved.
