$$a_j = \tfrac{1}{6}\sum_{i=j+1}^{7} s_i. \tag{2}$$

$$s_j = \tfrac{1}{6}\sum_{i=j+1}^{7} s_i + \tfrac{1}{6}\sum_{i=1}^{j-1} s_i = \t
