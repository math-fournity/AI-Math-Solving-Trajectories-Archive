# Problem

In a specialized optics facility, three identical equilateral triangular prisms with side lengths of 18 centimeters are mounted on rotating pedestals. The center points of these three pedestals are arranged such that each is exactly 30 centimeters away from the other two. A single, continuous fiber-optic cable with a total length of 144 centimeters is looped tightly around the outer perimeter of these three prisms.

At the very beginning of a calibration cycle, a microscopic laser-etched sensor is located at the topmost vertex of one of the prisms. The three prisms are then synchronized to rotate at an identical constant angular velocity. As they turn, the fiber-optic cable moves along the perimeter of the prisms without any slipping or stretching. 

The system continues to rotate until the laser-etched sensor completes a full cycle and returns to its original starting coordinates at the topmost vertex. Compute the total length of the path traveled by the sensor during this duration.
