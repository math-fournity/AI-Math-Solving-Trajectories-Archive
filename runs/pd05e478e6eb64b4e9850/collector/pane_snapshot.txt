$$\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_{C}\frac{f'(z)}{f(z)-f_{0}}\,dz = \frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_
{C}\frac{g'(z)}{g(z)}\,dz,$$

**Local behavior at each zero.** Near a zero $z_{k}$ of order $n_{k}$, we may wr

$$g(z) = (z - z_{k})^{n_{k}}\,h_{k}(z),$$

$$g'(z) = n_{k}(z-z_{k})^{n_{k}-1}h_{k}(z) + (z-z_{k})^{n_{k}}h_{k}'(z),$$

$$\frac{g'(z)}{g(z)} = \frac{n_{k}}{z - z_{k}} + \frac{h_{k}'(z)}{h_{k}(z)}.$$
The term $h_{k}'(z)/h_{k}(z)$ is analytic near $z_{k}$ (since $h_{k}(z_{k}) \neq

**Apply the residue theorem.** The function $g'(z)/g(z)$ is analytic inside and

{Res}\!\left(\frac{g'}{g},\, z_{k}\right) = \sum_{k=1}^{m} n_{k} = N.$$

$$\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_{C}\frac{f'(z)}{f(z)-f_{0}}\,dz = N,$$
