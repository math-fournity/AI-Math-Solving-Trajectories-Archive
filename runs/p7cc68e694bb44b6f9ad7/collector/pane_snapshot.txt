## Solution

The chain complex is
$$
0 \to C_2=\mathbb{Z}^2 \xrightarrow{\partial_2} C_1=\mathbb{Z}^2 \xrightarrow{\p
artial_1} C_0=\mathbb{Z} \to 0
$$
with
$$
\partial_2 = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 5 \\ 4 & 10 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \partial_1 =
\begin{pmatrix} -2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}.
$$

**Consistency check.** $\partial_1 \circ \partial_2 = (-2)(2x+5y) + 1(4x+10y) =
-4x-10y+4x+10y = 0$, so this is indeed a chain complex.

The homology groups are $H_n = \ker \partial_n / \operatorname{im} \partial_{n+1
}$.

---

### $H_2 = \ker \partial_2$

Solve $\partial_2(x,y)^T = 0$:
$$
2x + 5y = 0, \qquad 4x + 10y = 0.
$$
The second equation is twice the first, so we only need $2x + 5y = 0$, i.e. $x =
 -\tfrac{5}{2}y$. For integer solutions $y$ must be even: $y = 2t$, $x = -5t$. H
ence
$$
\ker \partial_2 = \{(-5t,\, 2t) : t \in \mathbb{Z}\} = \langle (-5,\, 2) \rangle
 \cong \mathbb{Z}.
$$
Since $\operatorname{im} \partial_3 = 0$,
$$
\boxed{H_2 \cong \mathbb{Z}}.
$$

---

### $H_1 = \ker \partial_1 / \operatorname{im} \partial_2$

**Kernel.** $\partial_1(a,b) = -2a + b = 0 \iff b = 2a$, so
$$
\ker \partial_1 = \{(a,\, 2a) : a \in \mathbb{Z}\} = \langle (1,\, 2) \rangle \c
ong \mathbb{Z}.
$$

**Image.** The image of $\partial_2$ is generated by the columns $(2,4)^T$ and $
(5,10)^T$. Both lie in $\ker\partial_1$ since each is of the form $(s, 2s)$. Con
cretely
$$
\operatorname{im} \partial_2 = \{2m(1,2) + 5n(1,2) : m,n\in\mathbb{Z}\} = \{(2m+
5n)(1,2) : m,n\in\mathbb{Z}\}.
$$
Since $\gcd(2,5)=1$, the set $\{2m+5n\}$ is all of $\mathbb{Z}$. Therefore
$$
\operatorname{im} \partial_2 = \{k(1,2) : k \in \mathbb{Z}\} = \ker \partial_1.
$$
Thus
$$
\boxed{H_1 = \ker \partial_1 / \operatorname{im} \partial_2 \cong \mathbb{Z}/\ma
thbb{Z} = 0}.
$$

---

### $H_0 = \ker \partial_0 / \operatorname{im} \partial_1$

Here $\partial_0 : C_0 \to 0$, so $\ker \partial_0 = C_0 = \mathbb{Z}$. The imag
e of $\partial_1$ is
$$
\operatorname{im} \partial_1 = \{-2a + b : a, b \in \mathbb{Z}\}.
$$
Taking $a = 0, b = 1$ gives $1$, so $\operatorname{im} \partial_1 = \mathbb{Z}$.
 Hence
$$
\boxed{H_0 = \mathbb{Z}/\mathbb{Z} = 0}.
$$

---

### Summary

$$
H_0 \cong 0, \qquad H_1 \cong 0, \qquad H_2 \cong \mathbb{Z}.
$$

The only nonzero homology group is $H_2 \cong \mathbb{Z}$, generated by the cycl
e $(-5, 2) \in C_2$.
