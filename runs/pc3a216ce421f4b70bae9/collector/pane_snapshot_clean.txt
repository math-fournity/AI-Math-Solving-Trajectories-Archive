**Answer: Yes, the induced map $\operatorname{Spec}(B) \to \operatorname{Spec}(A
)$ is surjective.**

---

**Proof.**

Let $\varphi: A \to B$ be the given ring homomorphism, and suppose $B$ is free a
s an $A$-module (the $A$-module structure being via $\varphi$). We prove that th
e induced map $\varphi^{*}: \operatorname{Spec}(B) \to \operatorname{Spec}(A)$,
$\mathfrak{q} \mapsto \varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak{q})$, is surjective.

**Step 1. $B$ is faithfully flat over $A$.**

A free module is flat. Moreover, $B \neq 0$ as an $A$-module, since $1_B = \varp
hi(1_A) \in B$ is nonzero. A nonzero free $A$-module is faithfully flat: indeed,
 if $B \cong A^{(I)}$ with $I \neq \emptyset$, then for every maximal ideal $\ma
thfrak{m}$ of $A$, the quotient $B/\mathfrak{m}B \cong (A/\mathfrak{m})^{(I)}$ i
s nonzero, which is equivalent to $\mathfrak{m}B \neq B$; this characterizes fai
thful flatness among flat modules.

**Step 2. Lying-over for faithfully flat extensions.**

Let $\mathfrak{p} \in \operatorname{Spec}(A)$. We must find $\mathfrak{q} \in \o
peratorname{Spec}(B)$ with $\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak{q}) = \mathfrak{p}$.

Set $S = A \setminus \mathfrak{p}$ and localize. Since $B$ is flat over $A$, the
 localization $B_{\mathfrak{p}} := S^{-1}B = B \otimes_A A_{\mathfrak{p}}$ is fl
at over $A_{\mathfrak{p}}$; since $B$ is faithfully flat over $A$, $B_{\mathfrak
{p}}$ is faithfully flat over $A_{\mathfrak{p}}$ (localization preserves faithfu
l flatness). The ring $A_{\mathfrak{p}}$ is local with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{
p}A_{\mathfrak{p}}$.

By faithful flatness, $\mathfrak{p}A_{\mathfrak{p}} \cdot B_{\mathfrak{p}} \neq
B_{\mathfrak{p}}$ (otherwise $\mathfrak{p}A_{\mathfrak{p}} = A_{\mathfrak{p}}$ b
y faithful flatness, contradicting that $\mathfrak{p}A_{\mathfrak{p}}$ is a prop
er maximal ideal). Hence $\mathfrak{p}A_{\mathfrak{p}} \cdot B_{\mathfrak{p}}$ i
s contained in some maximal ideal $\mathfrak{n}$ of $B_{\mathfrak{p}}$ (using Zo
rn's lemma).

Let $\mathfrak{r} = \mathfrak{n} \cap A_{\mathfrak{p}}$ be the contraction. Then
 $\mathfrak{r}$ is a prime ideal of $A_{\mathfrak{p}}$ with $\mathfrak{p}A_{\mat
hfrak{p}} \subseteq \mathfrak{r}$. But $\mathfrak{p}A_{\mathfrak{p}}$ is the uni
que maximal ideal of the local ring $A_{\mathfrak{p}}$, so $\mathfrak{r} = \math
frak{p}A_{\mathfrak{p}}$.

**Step 3. Descend to a prime of $B$.**

Let $\mathfrak{q}$ be the preimage of $\mathfrak{n}$ under the natural map $B \t
o B_{\mathfrak{p}}$. Then $\mathfrak{q} \in \operatorname{Spec}(B)$, and

$$\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak{q}) \;=\; \text{preimage of } \mathfrak{n} \cap A_{\mat
hfrak{p}} = \mathfrak{p}A_{\mathfrak{p}} \text{ under } A \to A_{\mathfrak{p}} \
;=\; \mathfrak{p}.$$

Thus $\mathfrak{q}$ lies over $\mathfrak{p}$.

Since $\mathfrak{p} \in \operatorname{Spec}(A)$ was arbitrary, $\varphi^{*}$ is
surjective. $\blacksquare$

---

**Remark.** The freeness hypothesis is used only to guarantee faithful flatness
(flatness from freeness, faithfulness from $B \neq 0$). In fact the conclusion h
olds for any faithfully flat ring homomorphism $A \to B$.
