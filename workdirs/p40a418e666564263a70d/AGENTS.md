# Problem

In the coastal logistics hub of Port Prime, two specialized cargo vessels operate: the "Alpha-13," which carries containers in batches of exactly 13 units, and the "Beta-17," which carries containers in batches of exactly 17 units. 

A shipment is defined as "Standardized" if it consists of a total number of containers $n$ that can be formed by combining at least one batch from the Alpha-13 and at least one batch from the Beta-17. Mathematically, a shipment of size $n$ is Standardized if $n = 13p + 17q$ for some positive integers $p, q \ge 1$.

The port authority has identified a critical "threshold value" $\omega$, calculated as $(13-1)(17-1)$. They are conducting an audit of all possible shipment sizes $x$ that fall within the range $1 \le x < \omega$.

Determine the total number of distinct integer shipment sizes in this range that qualify as "Standardized."
