# Problem

Due to strict architectural requirements, the hardware must be distributed according to the following protocols:
- Every Compute Node must be linked to at least one Storage Server to access data.
- Every AI Accelerator must be linked to at least one Storage Server and at least one Compute Node to function.
- Every empty Cooling Bay must be linked to at least one Storage Server, at least one Compute Node, and at least one AI Accelerator to effectively regulate temperatures.

Under these operational constraints, find the maximum possible number of empty Cooling Bays that can exist within the grid.
