## Solution

**Setup.** Each of the 5 stages is chosen from {S, I, T}. Classify each stage as
 High-Frequency (H) if it is S or T (2 choices), or Low-Frequency (L) if it is I
 (1 choice). The protocol forbids three consecutive H's.

We count valid sequences by tracking, at each length, how many consecutive H's c
urrently sit at the end of the sequence. Define:

- $f(n,0)$: valid sequences of length $n$ ending in L (0 trailing H's),
- $f(n,1)$: valid sequences of length $n$ ending in exactly 1 trailing H,
- $f(n,2)$: valid sequences of length $n$ ending in exactly 2 trailing H's.

Appending L (weight 1, since only I) resets the trailing-H count to 0. Appending
 H (weight 2, since S or T) increments the trailing-H count, but is forbidden wh
en the count is already 2. Thus:

$$f(n,0) = f(n{-}1,0)+f(n{-}1,1)+f(n{-}1,2),$$
$$f(n,1) = 2\,f(n{-}1,0),$$
$$f(n,2) = 2\,f(n{-}1,1).$$

**Base case** ($n=1$): $f(1,0)=1$, $f(1,1)=2$, $f(1,2)=0$.

**Iteration:**

| $n$ | $f(n,0)$ | $f(n,1)$ | $f(n,2)$ | Total |
|-----|----------|----------|----------|-------|
| 1 | 1 | 2 | 0 | 3 |
| 2 | 3 | 2 | 4 | 9 |
| 3 | 9 | 6 | 4 | 19 |
| 4 | 19 | 18 | 12 | 49 |
| 5 | 49 | 38 | 36 | **123** |

**Verification via complementary counting.** The unrestricted total is $3^5 = 24
3$. Let $A_i$ be the set of sequences with HHH starting at position $i$ ($i \in
\{1,2,3\}$). Each $|A_i| = 8 \cdot 9 = 72$ (three forced H's contribute $2^3=8$;
 the two free positions contribute $1+2+2+4=9$). Pairwise intersections $|A_1\ca
p A_2|=|A_2\cap A_3|=48$, $|A_1\cap A_3|=32$; triple intersection $=32$. Inclusi
on–exclusion gives $3\cdot72 - (48+32+48) + 32 = 120$. Hence valid $= 243 - 120
= 123$, confirming the recurrence.

**Answer:** $\boxed{123}$
