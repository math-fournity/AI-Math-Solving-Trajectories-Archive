## Solution

**Setup.** Four identical spheres of radius $R$ are mutually tangent. Their cent
ers therefore form a regular tetrahedron with edge length $a = 2R$.

**Key radii of the regular tetrahedron** (edge $a$):
- Circumradius (center to vertex): $R_{\text{circ}} = \dfrac{a\sqrt{6}}{4}$
- Inradius (center to face): $R_{\text{in}} = \dfrac{a\sqrt{6}}{12}$

With $a = 2R$:
$$R_{\text{circ}} = \frac{2R\sqrt{6}}{4} = \frac{R\sqrt{6}}{2}$$

By symmetry, the centers of both Shell 5 and Shell 6 coincide with the centroid
of the tetrahedron.

**Shell 5 (inner core).** This sphere sits in the central void and is *externall
y* tangent to each reactor. The distance from the tetrahedron's centroid to each
 reactor center is $R_{\text{circ}} = \tfrac{R\sqrt{6}}{2}$. External tangency g
ives
$$\text{distance} = R + r_5 \implies \frac{R\sqrt{6}}{2} = R + r_5 \implies r_5
= R\!\left(\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2} - 1\right) = \frac{R(\sqrt{6}-2)}{2}.$$

**Shell 6 (outer hull).** This sphere *encloses* all four reactors and is *inter
nally* tangent to each. Internal tangency gives
$$r_6 = \text{distance} + R = \frac{R\sqrt{6}}{2} + R = \frac{R(\sqrt{6}+2)}{2}.
$$

**Volume ratio.** Since volume scales as the cube of the radius,
$$\frac{V_6}{V_5} = \left(\frac{r_6}{r_5}\right)^{\!3} = \left(\frac{\sqrt{6}+2}
{\sqrt{6}-2}\right)^{\!3}.$$

Rationalize the base:
$$\frac{\sqrt{6}+2}{\sqrt{6}-2} = \frac{(\sqrt{6}+2)^2}{(\sqrt{6})^2 - 4} = \fra
c{6 + 4\sqrt{6} + 4}{2} = \frac{10 + 4\sqrt{6}}{2} = 5 + 2\sqrt{6}.$$

Now cube:
$$(5+2\sqrt{6})^2 = 25 + 20\sqrt{6} + 24 = 49 + 20\sqrt{6},$$
$$(5+2\sqrt{6})^3 = (49+20\sqrt{6})(5+2\sqrt{6}) = 245 + 98\sqrt{6} + 100\sqrt{6
} + 240 = 485 + 198\sqrt{6}.$$

**Result.**

$$\boxed{\dfrac{V_6}{V_5} = 485 + 198\sqrt{6}}$$
