## Solution

Let $a, b, c$ be the three roots of $x^3 - 2x^2 + 3x - 4 = 0$. By Vieta's formul
as:

$$a+b+c = 2,\quad ab+bc+ca = 3,\quad abc = 4.$$

**Step 1: Simplify each denominator.**

The sum of squares is
$$a^2+b^2+c^2 = (a+b+c)^2 - 2(ab+bc+ca) = 4 - 6 = -2.$$

Hence for the first term,
$$b^2+c^2-a^2 = (a^2+b^2+c^2) - 2a^2 = -2 - 2a^2 = -2(1+a^2),$$
and cyclically. Therefore each term becomes
$$\frac{1}{a(b^2+c^2-a^2)} = \frac{1}{a\cdot(-2)(1+a^2)} = -\frac{1}{2}\cdot\fra
c{1}{a(1+a^2)},$$
so
$$TRI = -\frac{1}{2}\sum_{\text{cyc}}\frac{1}{a(1+a^2)}.$$

**Step 2: Partial-fraction split.**

$$\frac{1}{a(1+a^2)} = \frac{1}{a} - \frac{a}{1+a^2},$$
hence
$$\sum\frac{1}{a(1+a^2)} = \sum\frac{1}{a} - \sum\frac{a}{1+a^2}.$$

**Step 3: Compute $\sum \frac{1}{a}$.**

$$\sum\frac{1}{a} = \frac{ab+bc+ca}{abc} = \frac{3}{4}.$$

**Step 4: Compute $\sum \frac{a}{1+a^2}$.**

Combine over a common denominator:
$$\sum\frac{a}{1+a^2} = \frac{a(1+b^2)(1+c^2)+b(1+a^2)(1+c^2)+c(1+a^2)(1+b^2)}{(
1+a^2)(1+b^2)(1+c^2)}.$$

*Denominator:*
$$(1+a^2)(1+b^2)(1+c^2)=1+(a^2+b^2+c^2)+(a^2b^2+b^2c^2+c^2a^2)+a^2b^2c^2.$$
Using $a^2+b^2+c^2=-2$, $(ab+bc+ca)^2-2abc(a+b+c)=9-16=-7$ for the middle term,
and $(abc)^2=16$:
$$\text{Denominator}=1-2-7+16=8.$$

*Numerator:* Since each root satisfies $a^3=2a^2-3a+4$, we reduce powers. For a
single root $a$, using $b+c=2-a$ and $bc = 3-a(b+c)=a^2-2a+3$:
$$b^2+c^2=(b+c)^2-2bc=(2-a)^2-2(a^2-2a+3)=-a^2-2,$$
$$a(b^2+c^2)=-a^3-2a=-2a^2+a-4,$$
$$a(bc)^2=a(a^2-2a+3)^2.$$
Expanding and reducing $a^4=a^2-2a+8$, $a^5=5a+4$ gives
$$a(bc)^2=4a^2-8a+12.$$
Thus
$$a(1+b^2)(1+c^2)=a+a(b^2+c^2)+a(bc)^2=a+(-2a^2+a-4)+(4a^2-8a+12)=2a^2-6a+8.$$

Summing cyclically:
$$\text{Numerator}=\sum(2a^2-6a+8)=2(a^2+b^2+c^2)-6(a+b+c)+24=2(-2)-6(2)+24=8.$$

Therefore
$$\sum\frac{a}{1+a^2}=\frac{8}{8}=1.$$

**Step 5: Combine.**

$$\sum\frac{1}{a(1+a^2)}=\frac{3}{4}-1=-\frac{1}{4},$$
$$TRI=-\frac{1}{2}\cdot\left(-\frac{1}{4}\right)=\frac{1}{8}.$$

$$\boxed{\dfrac{1}{8}}$$
