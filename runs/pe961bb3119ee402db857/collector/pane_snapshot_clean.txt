## Setup

Place the square with center $O$ at the origin. With side length $16$, the half-
side is $8$. Taking $AB$ as the northern boundary:

$$A=(-8,8),\quad B=(8,8),\quad C=(8,-8),\quad D=(-8,-8),\quad O=(0,0).$$

The semi-circle $\mathcal{S}$ has diameter $AB$, so its center is the midpoint $
(0,8)$ with radius $8$, curving outward (northward, $y\ge 8$).

## Locating $P$

A point on the semi-circle is
$$P=(8\cos\theta,\; 8+8\sin\theta),\qquad \theta\in[0,\pi].$$

The condition $OP=12$ gives
$$OP^2 = (8\cos\theta)^2+(8+8\sin\theta)^2 = 64\cos^2\theta+64+128\sin\theta+64\
sin^2\theta = 128+128\sin\theta.$$

Setting $OP^2=144$:
$$128+128\sin\theta=144 \;\Longrightarrow\; \sin\theta=\frac{1}{8}.$$

Then $\cos^2\theta=1-\frac{1}{64}=\frac{63}{64}$, so $\cos\theta=\pm\frac{\sqrt{
63}}{8}$, and
$$P=(\pm\sqrt{63},\; 8+1)=(\pm\sqrt{63},\; 9).$$

## Area of $\triangle CDP$

The base $CD$ lies on the horizontal line $y=-8$, with $C=(8,-8)$, $D=(-8,-8)$,
so $|CD|=16$.

The height of $P$ above this line is
$$9-(-8)=17.$$

Hence
$$[\triangle CDP]=\tfrac{1}{2}\cdot |CD|\cdot h = \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot 16\cdot 17 =
 136.$$

The $x$-coordinate of $P$ (sign of $\sqrt{63}$) does not affect the height, so b
oth positions of $P$ give the same area.

## Answer

$$\boxed{136}$$

The area of the triangular survey region $CDP$ is **136 square kilometers**.
