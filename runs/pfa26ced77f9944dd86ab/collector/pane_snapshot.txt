**Finding $s$:** Each corner sphere center is at distance $r$ from each of its t

60° corner). The distance from the triangle's center to each vertex is $s\sqrt{3

$$s - 2r\sqrt{3} = 2r \implies s = 2r(1 + \sqrt{3})$$

$$R = \rho = \frac{s\sqrt{3}}{6} = \frac{2r(1+\sqrt{3})\sqrt{3}}{6} = \frac{r(3+

### Finding the Fourth Sphere's Height

The corner sphere centers are at height $r$, at horizontal distance $\frac{2r\sq

$$\left(\frac{2r\sqrt{3}}{3}\right)^2 + (z - r)^2 = (R + r)^2$$

Compute each piece:

$$z - r = r\sqrt{\frac{9+4\sqrt{3}}{3}} = r\sqrt{3 + \frac{4\sqrt{3}}{3}}$$

$$z = r\left(1 + \sqrt{3 + \frac{4\sqrt{3}}{3}}\right)$$
