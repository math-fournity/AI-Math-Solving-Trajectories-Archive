# Problem

In a specialized circular data center, 100 distinct servers are assigned unique identification ranks from 1 to 100. These servers are arranged in a physical ring. To optimize data flow, the system architecture requires a "high-low" alternating configuration: every single server must have a rank that is either strictly higher than both of its immediate neighbors or strictly lower than both of its immediate neighbors.

The network administrator is looking to perform maintenance by decommissioning a "segment"—defined as a pair of two servers that are physically adjacent to one another. A segment is classified as "stable" if, upon the removal of those two servers and the closing of the ring (connecting the two servers that were previously adjacent to the removed pair), the remaining 98 servers still perfectly maintain the "high-low" alternating configuration.

What is the minimum possible number of "stable" segments that can exist in such a circular arrangement?
