## Proof

**Step 1: Divisibility monotonicity.**

Setting $j = i$: the conditions are trivially satisfied. For $i \mid j$, we have
 $\gcd(i,j) = i$ and $\operatorname{lcm}(i,j) = j$, so $\min(a_i, a_j) = a_i$ an

_1 \le a_n$ for all $n$.

**Step 2: The coprime condition.**

For coprime $i, j$: $\gcd(i,j) = 1$ and $\operatorname{lcm}(i,j) = ij$, so $\{a_
i, a_j\} = \{a_1, a_{ij}\}$ (as multisets, since one is the min and one is the m
ax). In particular, at most one of $a_i, a_j$ can exceed $a_1$.

**Step 3: Structure of $S = \{n : a_n > a_1\}$.**

No two elements of $S$ are coprime (by Step 2). Moreover, $S$ is **closed under
gcd**: if $m, n \in S$ with $d = \gcd(m,n)$, then $a_d = \min(a_m, a_n) > a_1$,
so $d \in S$. (Note $d > 1$, since $d = 1$ would give $a_1 > a_1$.)

Since $S$ is closed under gcd, $d^* = \gcd(S) \in S$ (by iterated gcd), and $d^*
 > 1$. Let $p$ be any prime factor of $d^*$. Then $p \mid n$ for every $n \in S$
.

**Step 4: $a_n$ depends only on $v_p(n)$.**

For any $n$, write $n = p^e \cdot m$ with $\gcd(m, p) = 1$. If $m > 1$, then $m
\notin S$ (since $p \nmid m$), so $a_m = a_1$. Since $\gcd(p^e, m) = 1$, Step 2
gives $\{a_{p^e}, a_m\} = \{a_1, a_n\}$, i.e., $\{a_{p^e}, a_1\} = \{a_1, a_n\}$
. Since $p^e \mid n$ implies $a_{p^e} \le a_n$, we get $a_n = a_{p^e}$. (The cas
e $m = 1$ is trivial.) So $a_n = f(v_p(n))$ where $f(e) := a_{p^e}$.

**Step 5: $f$ is non-decreasing and the conditions are satisfied.**

Monotonicity: $p^e \mid p^{e+1}$ gives $f(e) \le f(e+1)$. The min/max conditions

}(i,j)) = \max(v_p(i), v_p(j))$, and for non-decreasing $f$: $\min(f(x), f(y)) =
 f(\min(x,y))$, $\max(f(x), f(y)) = f(\max(x,y))$.

**Step 6: Conversely, every such construction works.**

For any prime $p$ and non-decreasing $f: \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0} \to \mathbb{Z}_{>0}$
, setting $a_n = f(v_p(n))$ satisfies both conditions (verified above). This con
firms the characterization is complete.

**Step 7: Maximizing distinct values.**

The distinct values among $a_1, \ldots, a_{2009}$ are $\{f(0), f(1), \ldots, f(E

est prime is $p = 2$, giving $E = \lfloor \log_2 2009 \rfloor = 10$ (since $2^{1
0} = 1024 \le 2009 < 2048 = 2^{11}$).
