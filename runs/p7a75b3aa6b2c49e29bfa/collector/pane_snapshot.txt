every simple cocommutative coalgebra over $\mathbb{K}$ is 1-dimensional, so any
cocommutative coalgebra over $\mathbb{K}$ is pointed. In particular, $A$ is
pointed (this hypothesis is redundant given cocommutativity).

**Step 2: Apply the structure theorem.**

By the Cartier-Gabriel-Kostant-Milnor-Moore theorem (see e.g. Montgomery, *Hopf

$$A \cong U(\mathfrak{g}) \# \mathbb{K}G$$

the Lie algebra of primitive elements, and as a coalgebra $A \cong
U(\mathfrak{g}) \otimes \mathbb{K}G$. The coradical is $A_0 = \mathbb{K}G$.

$$U(\mathfrak{g}) \otimes \mathbb{K}G \cong \mathbb{K}G \cong \mathbb{K} \otimes

With $\mathfrak{g} = 0$, the structure theorem gives $A \cong U(0) \# \mathbb{K}
G \cong \mathbb{K}G$.
