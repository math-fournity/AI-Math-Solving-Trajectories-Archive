### Lower bound on $p(n)$: $p(n) \geq 2^{\,n^2/4 - O(n\log n)}$

We use the **Kleitman–Rothschild construction**. Partition $[n]$ into three leve
ls $A, B, C$ with $|A| = |C| = \lfloor n/4 \rfloor$ and $|B| = n - |A| - |C|$. D
efine a poset where:
- Each $a \in A$ is below a chosen subset of $B$ (given by a bipartite graph $G_
1 \subseteq A \times B$),
- Each $b \in B$ is below a chosen subset of $C$ (given by a bipartite graph $G_
2 \subseteq B \times C$),
- Comparabilities between $A$ and $C$ arise only by transitivity: $a < c$ iff $\
exists\, b \in B$ with $a < b < c$.

Each pair $(G_1, G_2)$ yields a valid poset (the relation is reflexive, antisymm
etric, and transitive by construction). The number of labeled posets obtained th
is way is:

$$\binom{n}{|A|,|B|,|C|} \cdot 2^{|A||B|} \cdot 2^{|B||C|} \geq 2^{(n/4)(n/2) +
(n/2)(n/4) - O(n)} = 2^{n^2/4 - O(n)}.$$

Every unlabeled poset on $n$ elements admits at most $n!$ labelings, so the numb
er of **non-isomorphic** posets satisfies:

$$p(n) \geq \frac{2^{n^2/4 - O(n)}}{n!} = \frac{2^{n^2/4 - O(n)}}{2^{n\log_2 n +

**Counting $d$-generated groups.** A $d$-generated group of order $n$ is a quoti

the number of $d$-generated groups of order $n$ (up to isomorphism) is at most $

$$g(n) \leq \sum_{d=0}^{\lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor} (n!)^d \leq (\lfloor \log_2 n\

$$g(n) \leq (\log_2 n + 1) \cdot 2^{n(\log_2 n)^2} = 2^{n(\log_2 n)^2 + O(\log n
