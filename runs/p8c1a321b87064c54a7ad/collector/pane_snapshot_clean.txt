## Proof

Let $p \colon \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}^*$ be the exponential map $p(w) = e^{w}$
, and let $w = u + iv$ be the standard coordinate on the domain $\mathbb{C}$. On
 the chart $(U, z)$ of $\mathbb{C}^*$ we are given the one-form

$$\omega = \frac{dz}{z}.$$

**Computation of the pullback.** By definition of the pullback of a one-form,

$$p^{*}\omega = \frac{d(p(w))}{p(w)} = \frac{d(e^{w})}{e^{w}}.$$

Since $p$ is holomorphic, $d(e^{w}) = e^{w}\,dw$. Therefore

$$p^{*}\omega = \frac{e^{w}\,dw}{e^{w}} = dw.$$

**Verification via real coordinates.** Writing $w = u + iv$, we have $z = p(w) =
 e^{u+iv}$, so $x = e^{u}\cos v$ and $y = e^{u}\sin v$. A direct computation giv
es

$$dx = e^{u}\cos v\,du - e^{u}\sin v\,dv, \qquad dy = e^{u}\sin v\,du + e^{u}\co
s v\,dv.$$

Since $z = x+iy$ and $|z|^{2} = x^{2}+y^{2} = e^{2u}$,

$$\frac{dz}{z} = \frac{(x\,dx + y\,dy) + i(x\,dy - y\,dx)}{x^{2}+y^{2}}.$$

Substituting,

$$x\,dx + y\,dy = e^{2u}(\cos^{2}v + \sin^{2}v)\,du = e^{2u}\,du,$$

$$x\,dy - y\,dx = e^{2u}(\cos^{2}v + \sin^{2}v)\,dv = e^{2u}\,dv.$$

Hence

$$\omega = \frac{e^{2u}\,du + i\,e^{2u}\,dv}{e^{2u}} = du + i\,dv = dw,$$

confirming $p^{*}\omega = dw$.

**Conclusion.** The pullback of $\omega = dz/z$ under the universal covering map
 $p = \exp$ is the standard one-form on $\mathbb{C}$:

$$\boxed{\,p^{*}\omega = dw\,}.$$

This is consistent with the fact that $\omega = dz/z$ is the global form on $\ma
thbb{C}^*$ whose integral over a loop around the origin measures the winding num
ber, and the exponential map trivializes it to the exact form $dw$ on the simply
-connected cover $\mathbb{C}$.
