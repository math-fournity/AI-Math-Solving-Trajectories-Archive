## Solution

Let $S$ be the spherical cap $x^2+y^2+z^2=25,\ z\ge 4$, oriented by the upward (
outward) normal. The boundary $\partial S$ is the circle where the sphere meets
the plane $z=4$:

$$x^2+y^2+16=25 \implies x^2+y^2=9,\ z=4,$$

a circle of radius $3$ in the plane $z=4$. By the upward orientation of $S$, the
 induced orientation on $\partial S$ (right-hand rule) is **counterclockwise whe
n viewed from above**.

By **Stokes' theorem**,

$$\iint_S \operatorname{curl} F\,dS = \oint_{\partial S} F\cdot dr.$$

Parameterize $\partial S$ by
$$\mathbf r(t)=(3\cos t,\,3\sin t,\,4),\qquad 0\le t\le 2\pi,$$
so $d\mathbf r=(-3\sin t,\,3\cos t,\,0)\,dt$.

On the curve, with $F=(yz\,e^x,\; x+ze^x,\; yz)$:

- $P = yz\,e^x = (3\sin t)(4)e^{3\cos t}=12\sin t\,e^{3\cos t}$,
- $Q = x+ze^x = 3\cos t + 4e^{3\cos t}$,
- $R = yz = 12\sin t$.

Thus
$$F\cdot d\mathbf r = P\,dx+Q\,dy+R\,dz = -36\sin^2 t\,e^{3\cos t}+9\cos^2 t+12\
cos t\,e^{3\cos t}.$$

**Combine the exponential terms.** Observe
$$\frac{d}{dt}\!\bigl[\sin t\,e^{3\cos t}\bigr]=\cos t\,e^{3\cos t}-3\sin^2 t\,e
^{3\cos t}=e^{3\cos t}\bigl(\cos t-3\sin^2 t\bigr).$$

Therefore
$$-36\sin^2 t\,e^{3\cos t}+12\cos t\,e^{3\cos t}=12\,e^{3\cos t}\bigl(\cos t-3\s
in^2 t\bigr)=12\,\frac{d}{dt}\!\bigl[\sin t\,e^{3\cos t}\bigr].$$

Integrating over $[0,2\pi]$:
$$\int_0^{2\pi}12\,\frac{d}{dt}\!\bigl[\sin t\,e^{3\cos t}\bigr]dt = 12\Bigl[\si
n t\,e^{3\cos t}\Bigr]_0^{2\pi}=12(0-0)=0.$$

**The remaining term:**
$$\int_0^{2\pi}9\cos^2 t\,dt = 9\cdot\pi = 9\pi.$$

Hence
$$\iint_S \operatorname{curl} F\,dS = 0+9\pi = \boxed{9\pi}.$$

### PROOF COMPLETE
