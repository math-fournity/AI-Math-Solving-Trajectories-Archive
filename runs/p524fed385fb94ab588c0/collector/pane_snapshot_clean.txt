## Solution

**Setup.** Let $f \in \mathcal{F}$, so $f:\mathbb{D}\to\mathbb{D}$ is holomorphi
c with $f(0)=f'(0)=f''(0)=f'''(0)=0$. Thus $f$ has a zero of order $\geq 4$ at t
he origin, and we may write
$$f(z) = z^{4}\,g(z)$$
for a function $g$ holomorphic on $\mathbb{D}$.

**Bounding $g$.** The constraint $|f(z)|<1$ for $|z|<1$ gives $|z|^{4}|g(z)|<1$,
 i.e. $|g(z)|<|z|^{-4}$ for $z\neq 0$. Fix $0<r<1$. On the circle $|z|=r$,
$$|g(z)| \leq r^{-4}.$$
By the maximum modulus principle, $|g(z)|\leq r^{-4}$ for all $|z|\leq r$. Letti
ng $r\to 1^{-}$,
$$|g(z)|\leq 1 \quad\text{for all } z\in\mathbb{D}.$$
So $g:\mathbb{D}\to\overline{\mathbb{D}}$ is holomorphic.

**Extremal bound.** At $z=1/2$,
$$|f(1/2)| = \tfrac{1}{16}\,|g(1/2)| \leq \tfrac{1}{16},$$
since $|g(1/2)|\leq 1$ by the bound above.

**Sharpness.** Take $g(z)\equiv e^{i\theta}$ (a unimodular constant), giving $f(
z)=e^{i\theta}z^{4}$. This function maps $\mathbb{D}$ to $\mathbb{D}$ (since $|z
|^{4}<1$ for $|z|<1$), and
$$f(0)=0,\quad f'(0)=0,\quad f''(0)=0,\quad f'''(0)=0,$$
while $f^{(4)}(0)=24\,e^{i\theta}\neq 0$. Hence $f\in\mathcal{F}$ and
$$|f(1/2)| = \tfrac{1}{16}.$$

**Conclusion.**
$$\boxed{\sup_{f\in\mathcal{F}}|f(1/2)| = \dfrac{1}{16}}.$$
