**Answer: Yes, $C$ is a Stonean space.**

**Proof.**

Recall that a Stonean space is a compact Hausdorff space in which the closure of
 every open set is open (i.e., a compact Hausdorff extremally disconnected space
).

Let $S$ be Stonean and let $C\subseteq S$ be clopen. Endow $C$ with the subspace
 topology. We verify the three defining properties.

**1. $C$ is compact.** A closed subset of a compact space is compact. Since $C$
is closed in $S$ and $S$ is compact, $C$ is compact.

**2. $C$ is Hausdorff.** A subspace of a Hausdorff space is Hausdorff. Since $S$
 is Hausdorff, so is $C$.

**3. $C$ is extremally disconnected.** Let $U\subseteq C$ be open in the subspac
e topology of $C$. We must show that $\overline{U}^{\,C}$ (the closure of $U$ in
 $C$) is open in $C$.

Since $U$ is open in $C$, by definition of the subspace topology there exists an
 open set $V\subseteq S$ with $U=V\cap C$. As $C$ is open in $S$, the intersecti
on $V\cap C$ is open in $S$. Hence **$U$ is open in $S$**.

Because $S$ is Stonean, $\overline{U}^{\,S}$ (the closure of $U$ in $S$) is open
 in $S$.

Since $C$ is closed in $S$, the closure of $U$ in the subspace $C$ is
$$\overline{U}^{\,C}=\overline{U}^{\,S}\cap C.$$
(Indeed, $\overline{U}^{\,S}\cap C$ is a closed subset of $C$ containing $U$, an
d any closed subset of $C$ containing $U$ is of the form $F\cap C$ with $F$ clos
ed in $S$ and $U\subseteq F$, forcing $\overline{U}^{\,S}\subseteq F$.)

Now $\overline{U}^{\,S}$ is open in $S$, so $\overline{U}^{\,C}=\overline{U}^{\,
S}\cap C$ is open in $C$ by definition of the subspace topology.

Thus the closure in $C$ of every open subset of $C$ is open in $C$, which is exa
ctly the extremally disconnected property.

**Conclusion.** $C$ is compact, Hausdorff, and extremally disconnected, hence $C
$ is a Stonean space. $\blacksquare$
