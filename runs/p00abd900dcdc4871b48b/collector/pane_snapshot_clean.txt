## Solution

We claim that $r = \dfrac{1}{2}$ works.

**The positive integer powers of $r = \tfrac{1}{2}$** are
$$r,\ r^2,\ r^3,\ \dots \;=\; \frac12,\ \frac14,\ \frac18,\ \frac1{16},\ \dots$$

**Total sum.** Since $0 < r < 1$, the geometric series converges:
$$S \;=\; \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} r^n \;=\; \frac{r}{1-r} \;=\; \frac{\,1/2\,}{1/2}
\;=\; 1.$$

For a partition into two groups of equal sum, each group must sum to $S/2 = \dfr
ac{1}{2}$.

**The partition.** Define
- **Group A** $= \{r\} = \bigl\{\tfrac12\bigr\}$,
- **Group B** $= \{r^2, r^3, r^4, \dots\} = \bigl\{\tfrac14, \tfrac18, \tfrac1{1
6}, \dots\bigr\}$.

These two groups are disjoint and together contain every positive integer power
of $r$ exactly once.

**Sum of Group A:**
$$\sum_{a \in A} a \;=\; r \;=\; \frac12.$$

**Sum of Group B:**
$$\sum_{b \in B} b \;=\; \sum_{n=2}^{\infty} r^n \;=\; \frac{r^2}{1-r} \;=\; \fr
ac{1/4}{1/2} \;=\; \frac12.$$

**Conclusion.** Both groups sum to $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and $S = \dfrac12 + \dfrac12
= 1$, so the two groups have equal sums.

Therefore the positive rational number $r = \dfrac{1}{2}$ has the required prope
rty: its positive integer powers $\bigl\{\tfrac12,\tfrac14,\tfrac18,\dots\bigr\}
$ can be divided into the two groups $\bigl\{\tfrac12\bigr\}$ and $\bigl\{\tfrac
14,\tfrac18,\tfrac1{16},\dots\bigr\}$, each with sum $\dfrac12$.
