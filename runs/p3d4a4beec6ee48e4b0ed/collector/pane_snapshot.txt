torname{Im}[k_2]>0$). Then:

Both arguments approach $0$ within the right half-plane, so $|\arg z_{1,2}|<\pi/

$$K_0(jk_1 r) = -\ln r - \ln(jk_1) + \ln 2 - \gamma + O(r^2\ln r)$$
$$K_0(-jk_2 r) = -\ln r - \ln(-jk_2) + \ln 2 - \gamma + O(r^2\ln r)$$

$$-\ln r\!\left(\frac{1}{\mu_1-1}-\frac{1}{\mu_2-1}\right) \;+\; \frac{\ln(-jk_2

$$\frac{1}{\mu_1-1} = \frac{1}{\mu_2-1} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \mu_1 = \mu_2

$$\frac{1}{\mu-1}\,K_0(jk_1 r) \;-\; \frac{1}{\mu-1}\,K_0(jk_1 r) \;=\; 0 \qquad
