$$R \cong (\mathbb{Z}/5)[x]/(x^2+1) \times (\mathbb{Z}/7)[x]/(x^2+1).$$

Thus $R \cong \mathbb{F}_5 \times \mathbb{F}_5 \times \mathbb{F}_{49}$, and $ax+
