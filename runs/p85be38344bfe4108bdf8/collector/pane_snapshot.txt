## Proof

**Setup.** Let O be the center of the semicircle (midpoint of AB). Place the sem
icircle on the unit circle with B at angle 0° and A at angle 180°. Let D be at a
ngle *d* and C at angle *c* on the upper semicircle. Since the drone enters at D
, exits at C, then continues to E (beyond B on line AB), the line order is D → C
 → E, which means C is closer to B than D, so **c < d**.

**Step 1: Use ∠DAC = 27°.**

∠DAC is an inscribed angle at A intercepting arc DC not containing A. Since A is
 at 180° and both C, D are in (0°, 180°), the arc DC not containing A is the min
or arc from C to D with measure *d − c*. By the inscribed angle theorem:

$$\frac{d - c}{2} = 27° \implies d - c = 54° \implies c = d - 54°.$$

**Step 2: Use BD = BE.**

The chord BD subtends central angle *d*, so BD = 2 sin(*d*/2). Let *r* = BD = BE
. Then E lies on the x-axis at distance *r* beyond B.

**Step 3: Angle at B in isosceles triangle BDE.**

The direction from B to D = (cos *d* − 1, sin *d*) = 2 sin(*d*/2)(−sin(*d*/2), c
os(*d*/2)), which makes angle **90° + *d*/2** with the positive x-axis (directio
n of BE). So:

$$\angle DBE = 90° + \frac{d}{2}.$$

Since BD = BE, the base angles are equal:

$$\angle BDE = \frac{180° - (90° + d/2)}{2} = 45° - \frac{d}{4}.$$

**Step 4: Relate ∠BDC to ∠BDE.**

Since C lies on segment DE (between D and E), ray DC coincides with ray DE, so:

$$\angle BDC = \angle BDE = 45° - \frac{d}{4}.$$

**Step 5: Express ∠BDC as an inscribed angle.**

∠BDC is an inscribed angle at D intercepting arc BC not containing D. Since B is
 at 0°, C at *c* = *d* − 54°, and D at *d*, the **minor arc BC** (measure *c* =
*d* − 54°) does **not** contain D. Therefore:

$$\angle BDC = \frac{d - 54°}{2} = \frac{d}{2} - 27°.$$

**Step 6: Solve for d.**

Setting the two expressions for ∠BDC equal:

$$\frac{d}{2} - 27° = 45° - \frac{d}{4}$$

$$\frac{d}{2} + \frac{d}{4} = 45° + 27°$$

$$\frac{3d}{4} = 72° \implies d = 96°.$$

So *c* = 96° − 54° = 42°.

**Step 7: Compute α = ∠ACD.**

∠ACD is an inscribed angle at C intercepting arc AD not containing C. With A at
180°, D at 96°, C at 42°:

- The arc from A (180°) clockwise to D (96°) has measure 180° − 96° = **84°**, a
nd does **not** contain C (at 42°). ✓

Therefore:

$$\alpha = \angle ACD = \frac{84°}{2} = 42°.$$
