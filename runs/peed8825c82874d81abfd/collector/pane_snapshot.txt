## Solution

The surface is the plane $z = 1 + x + 2y$, and we want the part lying inside the
 cone $z = \sqrt{2x^2 + 10y^2}$ (upper nappe, $z \ge 0$).

### Step 1: Identify the region

A point on the plane is inside the cone when
$$1 + x + 2y \;\ge\; \sqrt{2x^2 + 10y^2} \;\ge\; 0,$$
which (squaring, with $1+x+2y \ge 0$ automatic on the region) gives
$$(1+x+2y)^2 \;\ge\; 2x^2 + 10y^2.$$

Expanding:
$$1 + x^2 + 4y^2 + 2x + 4y + 4xy \;\ge\; 2x^2 + 10y^2,$$
$$x^2 - 4xy + 6y^2 - 2x - 4y - 1 \;\le\; 0.$$

### Step 2: Complete the square

Treating the expression as a quadratic in $x$:
$$x^2 - (4y+2)x + 6y^2 - 4y - 1 = \bigl(x - (2y+1)\bigr)^2 - (2y+1)^2 + 6y^2 - 4
y - 1$$
$$= (x - 2y - 1)^2 - 4y^2 - 4y - 1 + 6y^2 - 4y - 1 = (x-2y-1)^2 + 2y^2 - 8y - 2$
$
$$= (x-2y-1)^2 + 2\bigl((y-2)^2 - 5\bigr) = (x-2y-1)^2 + 2(y-2)^2 - 10.$$

So the region $D$ in the $xy$-plane is the ellipse
$$(x - 2y - 1)^2 + 2(y - 2)^2 \;\le\; 10.$$

### Step 3: Change of variables

Let $u = x - 2y - 1$, $\;v = y - 2$. Then $y = v+2$, $x = u + 2v + 5$, and
$$\frac{\partial(x,y)}{\partial(u,v)} = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 \end{vmat
rix} = 1.$$

The region becomes the ellipse $u^2 + 2v^2 \le 10$, with semi-axes $a = \sqrt{10
}$ (along $u$) and $b = \sqrt{5}$ (along $v$). Its area is
$$\text{Area}(D) = \pi \cdot \sqrt{10} \cdot \sqrt{5} = \pi\sqrt{50} = 5\pi\sqrt
{2}.$$

### Step 4: Surface area element

For $z = f(x,y) = 1 + x + 2y$:
$$dS = \sqrt{1 + f_x^2 + f_y^2}\, dA = \sqrt{1 + 1 + 4}\, dA = \sqrt{6}\, dA.$$

### Step 5: Compute the surface area

$$\text{Surface Area} = \iint_D \sqrt{6}\, dA = \sqrt{6} \cdot 5\pi\sqrt{2} = 5\
pi\sqrt{12} = 10\pi\sqrt{3}.$$

### Answer

$$\boxed{10\pi\sqrt{3}}$$
