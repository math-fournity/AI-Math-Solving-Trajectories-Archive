## Proof of `c_inf_lower_young`: `(c 2)^2 ≤ c ∞`

**Setup.** Recall
$$c(p)=\inf\bigl\{\|f*f\|_{p}:\; f\ge 0,\; f\in L^{1},\;\operatorname{supp}f\sub
seteq[0,1],\;\int f=1\bigr\}.$$
For any admissible $f$ and $g=f*f$ we have:

1. **$g\ge 0$ and $\|g\|_{1}=1$.** Nonnegativity is immediate since $f\ge 0$. Fo
r the integral, by Fubini–Tonelli (justified by $f\ge 0$),
$$\int_{\mathbb R} (f*f)(x)\,dx=\int\!\!\int f(y)f(x-y)\,dy\,dx=\Bigl(\int f\Big
r)^{2}=1.$$

2. **Pointwise bound $g(x)\le \|g\|_{\infty}$, hence $\|g\|_{2}^{2}\le \|g\|_{\i
nfty}\,\|g\|_{1}$.** Indeed
$$\|g\|_{2}^{2}=\int g(x)^{2}\,dx\le \|g\|_{\infty}\int g(x)\,dx=\|g\|_{\infty}\
cdot 1=\|g\|_{\infty}.$$
Rearranging (and using $\|g\|_{1}=1>0$):
$$\|f*f\|_{\infty}=\|g\|_{\infty}\ge \|g\|_{2}^{2}=\|f*f\|_{2}^{2}.$$

3. **Pass to the infimum.** The map $t\mapsto t^{2}$ is increasing and continuou
s on $[0,\infty)$, and $\|f*f\|_{2}\ge 0$, so
$$\inf_{f}\,\|f*f\|_{2}^{2}=\Bigl(\inf_{f}\,\|f*f\|_{2}\Bigr)^{2}=(c(2))^{2}.$$
Combining with step 2,
$$c(\infty)=\inf_{f}\|f*f\|_{\infty}\ge \inf_{f}\|f*f\|_{2}^{2}=(c(2))^{2}.$$

This is exactly `(c 2)^2 ≤ c ∞`. ∎

*(Remark: this is the standard $L^{2}\to L^{\infty}$ embedding $\|g\|_{2}^{2}\le
\|g\|_{\infty}\|g\|_{1}$ combined with the $L^{1}$-norm preservation of convolut
ion of nonnegative functions — a textbook consequence of Hölder/Young.)*
