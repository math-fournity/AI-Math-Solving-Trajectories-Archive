## Solution

**Setup.** The polynomial $x^4+ax^3+bx^2+cx+d=0$ has **real** coefficients, so i
ts non-real roots occur in conjugate pairs. Since all four roots are distinct an
d non-real, they form two conjugate pairs: $\{z,\bar z\}$ and $\{w,\bar w\}$.

**Identifying the pairing.** The product of the "first two" roots is $13+i$, whi
ch is **not real**. But the product of any conjugate pair $z\bar z=|z|^2$ is rea
l and positive. Hence the first two roots are *not* a conjugate pair. Likewise,
the sum of the "remaining two" is $3+4i$, not real, so they are not a conjugate
pair either.

Therefore the conjugate pairs are split across the two groups. Taking the first
two as $\{z,w\}$ and the remaining two as $\{\bar z,\bar w\}$:

$$zw = 13+i, \qquad \bar z+\bar w = 3+4i.$$

Conjugating the second equation gives $z+w = 3-4i$.

**Computing $b$.** By Vieta, $b$ is the sum of all pairwise products of the four
 roots $z,w,\bar z,\bar w$:

$$b = zw + z\bar z + z\bar w + w\bar z + w\bar w + \bar z\bar w.$$

Group the terms:

- $zw + \bar z\bar w = (13+i)+\overline{(13+i)} = (13+i)+(13-i) = 26.$
- The remaining four terms: $z\bar z + z\bar w + w\bar z + w\bar w = (z+w)(\bar
z+\bar w) = |z+w|^2$.

Since $z+w = 3-4i$:
$$|z+w|^2 = 3^2+(-4)^2 = 9+16 = 25.$$

Therefore:
$$b = 26 + 25 = 51.$$

**Verification that roots are distinct and non-real.** The roots $z,w$ satisfy $
t^2-(3-4i)t+(13+i)=0$, with discriminant
$$\Delta = (3-4i)^2-4(13+i) = (9-24i-16)-52-4i = -59-28i \neq 0.$$
Since $\Delta\neq 0$, $z\neq w$; since $-59-28i$ is not a non-negative real, $z,
w$ are non-real. Their conjugates $\bar z,\bar w$ are distinct from $z,w$ (non-r
eal numbers differ from their conjugates) and from each other. Thus all four roo
ts are distinct and non-real, as required.

$$\boxed{b = 51}$$
