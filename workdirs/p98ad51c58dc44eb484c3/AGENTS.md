# Problem

In the city of Hexopolis, there are exactly 6 specialized research stations, labeled 1 through 6. To facilitate communication, the city planning committee has decided to install exactly 6 high-speed fiber-optic cables, each connecting a unique pair of stations.

A communication network is deemed "Perfectly Reducible" if it meets the following recursive criteria:
1. A network with no stations is automatically considered Perfectly Reducible.
2. A network with at least one cable is Perfectly Reducible if there exists at least one cable $(u, v)$ such that, upon decommissioning both station $u$ and station $v$ (along with every cable connected to either of them), the remaining network of stations and cables is also Perfectly Reducible.

How many different possible network configurations (sets of 6 cables) exist for these 6 stations such that the resulting network is Perfectly Reducible?
