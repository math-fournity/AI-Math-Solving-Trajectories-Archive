## Parity Reduction

The key observation is that 2 and 4 are even, while 3 and 5 are odd. A sum is ev
en if and only if it contains an even number of odd entries. So I reduce to a **
binary parity problem**: assign each cell a value E (even) or O (odd), requiring
 every row, column, and diagonal to have an even number of O's.

For each valid parity pattern, each cell has **2 choices** (E→{2,4}, O→{3,5}), g
iving $2^{25}$ fillings per pattern. The answer is:

$$\text{Total} = (\text{number of valid parity patterns}) \times 2^{25}$$
