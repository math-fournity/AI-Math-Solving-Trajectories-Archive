## Solution

The surface $S$ is the boundary of the solid region
$$E = \{(x,y,z) : x^2+y^2 \le z \le 8-x^2-y^2\},$$
which is a closed surface (the lower paraboloid $z=x^2+y^2$ capped by the upper
paraboloid $z=8-x^2-y^2$). Since $S$ is closed, we apply the **Divergence Theore
m**:

$$\iint_S \mathbf{F}\cdot d\mathbf{S} = \iiint_E (\nabla\cdot\mathbf{F})\, dV.$$

**Compute the divergence.**

$$\mathbf{F} = \langle e^{z^2},\; 2y+\sin(x^2z),\; 4z+\sqrt{x^2+9y^2}\rangle.$$

$$\nabla\cdot\mathbf{F} = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}e^{z^2} + \frac{\partial}{\
partial y}\bigl(2y+\sin(x^2z)\bigr) + \frac{\partial}{\partial z}\bigl(4z+\sqrt{
x^2+9y^2}\bigr) = 0 + 2 + 4 = 6.$$

**Describe the region $E$.** The two paraboloids meet when $x^2+y^2 = 8-x^2-y^2$
, i.e. $x^2+y^2=4$, a circle of radius $2$. Using cylindrical coordinates $(r,\t
heta,z)$:

$$0 \le \theta \le 2\pi,\quad 0 \le r \le 2,\quad r^2 \le z \le 8-r^2.$$

**Compute the integral.**

$$\iint_S \mathbf{F}\cdot d\mathbf{S} = 6\iiint_E dV = 6\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^2\in
t_{r^2}^{8-r^2} r\, dz\, dr\, d\theta.$$

$$= 6\cdot 2\pi \int_0^2 r\bigl[(8-r^2)-r^2\bigr]\, dr = 12\pi\int_0^2 r(8-2r^2)
\, dr$$

$$= 12\pi\int_0^2 (8r-2r^3)\, dr = 12\pi\left[4r^2 - \frac{r^4}{2}\right]_0^2 =
12\pi\left[16-8\right] = 12\pi\cdot 8 = 96\pi.$$

**The flux of $\mathbf{F}$ through $S$ is $\boxed{96\pi}$.**

### PROOF COMPLETE
