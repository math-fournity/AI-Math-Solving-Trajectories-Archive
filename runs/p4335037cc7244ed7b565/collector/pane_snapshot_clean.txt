## Solution

**Setup.** For prime $p$, let $K=(p-1)/2$. A sequence $(x_1,\dots,x_K)\in\{0,\do
ts,p-1\}^K$ is valid iff the power sums $s_j=\sum_i x_i^j$ satisfy $s_1\equiv s_
2\equiv\cdots\equiv s_K\pmod p$. Let the common value be $r$, so $s_j\equiv r$ f
or $j=1,\dots,K$, i.e. $\sum_i(x_i^j-x_i)\equiv 0$ for $j=2,\dots,K$.

**Key reduction via Newton's identities.** Let $e_0=1,e_1,\dots,e_K$ be the elem
entary symmetric polynomials of $x_1,\dots,x_K$, and $f(t)=\prod_{i=1}^K(t-x_i)=
\sum_{j=0}^K(-1)^j e_j t^{K-j}$. Newton's identities give $j e_j=\sum_{i=1}^j(-1
)^{i-1}e_{j-i}s_i$. With $s_i=r$ for all $i$:

$$j\,e_j = r\sum_{i=1}^{j}(-1)^{i-1}e_{j-i}.$$

By induction, $e_j=\binom{r}{j}$ for $j=0,\dots,K$:
- $e_1=r=\binom r1$. ✓
- Assuming $e_i=\binom r i$ for $i<j$: using $\sum_{k=0}^{n}(-1)^k\binom r k=(-1
)^n\binom{r-1}{n}$,

$$j\,e_j = r\,(-1)^{j-1}\sum_{k=0}^{j-1}(-1)^k\binom r k = r\,(-1)^{j-1}\cdot(-1
)^{j-1}\binom{r-1}{j-1}=r\binom{r-1}{j-1}=j\binom r j.$$

So $e_j=\binom r j$, and the characteristic polynomial is

$$f_r(t)=\sum_{j=0}^{K}(-1)^j\binom r j\,t^{K-j}.$$

A sequence with common value $r$ exists iff $f_r$ splits completely over $\mathb
b F_p$ (into $K$ linear factors with roots in $\{0,\dots,p-1\}$); the number of
ordered sequences for that $r$ is the multinomial coefficient of the root multip
licities.

**Case $0\le r\le K$.** Then $\binom r j=0$ for $j>r$, so
$$f_r(t)=t^K\sum_{j=0}^{r}\binom r j(-1/t)^j=t^K(1-1/t)^r=t^{K-r}(t-1)^r.$$
Roots: $0$ (mult. $K-r$) and $1$ (mult. $r$), all in $\mathbb F_p$. The number o
f ordered sequences for this $r$ is $\binom K r$ (choose positions of the $r$ on
es). Summing over $r=0,\dots,K$:
$$\sum_{r=0}^{K}\binom K r=2^K.$$

**Case $r>K$ (i.e. $r\in\{K+1,\dots,p-1\}$).** Here $\binom r j\ne 0$ for $j\le
K$ in general; $f_r$ need not split. We verify directly that it does **not** spl
it for the relevant primes.

**$p=5$, $K=2$.** $r=0,1,2$ contribute $2^2=4$. For $r=3$: $f_3=t^2-3t+3\equiv t

equiv t^2+t+1$, discriminant $1-4\equiv 2$, non-QR. Neither splits. $N(5)=4$.

**$p=7$, $K=3$.** $r=0,1,2,3$ contribute $2^3=8$. Checking $r=4,5,6$:
- $r=4$: $f_4=t^3-4t^2+6t-4\equiv t^3+3t^2+6t+3$. Root $t=2$; quotient $t^2+5t+2
