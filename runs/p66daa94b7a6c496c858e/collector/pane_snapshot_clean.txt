## Setting Up the Connection to Linear Algebra

For a tuple $(S_1, \ldots, S_n)$ with $n = 2015$, the number of permutations $\s
igma$ with $\sigma(i) \in S_i$ equals the **permanent** of the $n \times n$ 0-1
matrix $A$ where $A_{ij} = [j \in S_i]$.

**Key fact:** Over $\mathbb{F}_2$, the permanent equals the determinant, since t
he only difference is signs $\pm 1$, and $-1 \equiv 1 \pmod{2}$.

Therefore: $\operatorname{perm}(A)$ is odd $\iff$ $\det(A)$ is odd $\iff$ $A$ is
 invertible over $\mathbb{F}_2$.

So $N$ equals the number of invertible $n \times n$ matrices over $\mathbb{F}_2$
.

## Computing $N$

The count of invertible $n \times n$ matrices over $\mathbb{F}_q$ is:
$$N = \prod_{i=0}^{n-1}(2^n - 2^i) = \prod_{i=0}^{n-1} 2^i(2^{n-i}-1) = 2^{\sum_
{i=0}^{n-1} i} \cdot \prod_{j=1}^{n}(2^j - 1)$$

where $j = n - i$ ranges from $1$ to $n$.

## Finding $k_2$

$$k_2 = \frac{n(n-1)}{2} = \frac{2015 \cdot 2014}{2} = 2015 \cdot 1007 = 2{,}029

## Finding $k_3$

j - 1)$.

Since $2 \equiv -1 \pmod{3}$:

- $j$ **even**: $2^j \equiv 1 \pmod{3}$, so $3 \mid (2^j - 1)$

For even $j = 2m$: $2^{2m} - 1 = 4^m - 1$. Since $3 \mid (4-1)$ and $3 \nmid 4$,

$$v_3(4^m - 1) = v_3(4-1) + v_3(m) = 1 + v_3(m)$$
