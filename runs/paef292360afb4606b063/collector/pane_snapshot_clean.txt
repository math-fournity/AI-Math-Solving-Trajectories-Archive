$$w = r^\alpha e^{i\alpha\theta}, \qquad 0 < \alpha\theta < \pi.$$
So the interior of the wedge maps to the **upper half $w$-plane** $\operatorname

$$w_0 = z_0^\alpha, \qquad \operatorname{Im} w_0 > 0.$$

\log(w - \bar w_0) = \frac{q}{2\pi\varepsilon_0}\log\frac{w - \bar w_0}{w - w_0}
