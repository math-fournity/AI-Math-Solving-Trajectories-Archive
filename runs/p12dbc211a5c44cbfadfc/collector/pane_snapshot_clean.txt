## Setup and Interpretation

Each element of $M_n$ is a repeating decimal $0.\overline{a_1 a_2 \cdots a_n} =
\frac{N}{10^n - 1}$, where $N = \overline{a_1 a_2 \cdots a_n}$ is the integer wi
th decimal digits $a_i \in \{0,1\}$ and $a_n = 1$. "Prime elements" means those
elements for which $N$ is prime (the distinction between $N$ prime vs. $N/\gcd(N
, 10^n-1)$ prime is asymptotically negligible). Thus:

$$\frac{S_n}{T_n} = \frac{\sum_{\text{prime } N} \frac{N}{10^n-1}}{\sum_{\text{p
rime } N} 1} = \frac{\overline{N}_{\text{prime}}}{10^n - 1}$$

where $\overline{N}_{\text{prime}}$ is the average value of prime $N$ in $M_n$.

## Decomposition by Leading Digit

Partition $M_n$ by the position $k$ of the leading 1 (highest power of 10 with d
igit 1). Let $M_n^{(k)}$ denote elements with leading digit at position $k$ ($1
\leq k \leq n-1$). Then $|M_n^{(k)}| = 2^{k-1}$, and for $N \in M_n^{(k)}$ we ha
ve $N \in [10^k, \frac{10^{k+1}-1}{9}]$, so $\ln N \approx k \ln 10$.

The average of $N$ within scale $k$ is:
$$\mu_k = 10^k + 1 + \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{k-1} 10^i = \frac{19 \cdot 10^k + 8}
{18}$$

## Average of All Elements

$$\overline{N} = \frac{\sum_{k=1}^{n-1} 2^{k-1} \cdot \mu_k}{\sum_{k=1}^{n-1} 2^
{k-1}} = \frac{10^n + 8}{18}$$

This is verified by direct computation: $\sum_{N \in M_n} N = 2^{n-1} + 2^{n-2}\
cdot\frac{10^n-10}{9}$, giving average $= 1 + \frac{10^n-10}{18} = \frac{10^n+8}
{18}$.

## Average of Prime Elements (PNT Heuristic)

By the Prime Number Theorem, the probability that $N$ is prime is $\sim 1/\ln N$
. The prime-weighted average is:

$$\overline{N}_{\text{prime}} = \frac{\sum_{k=1}^{n-1} \frac{2^{k-1} \mu_k}{k \l
n 10}}{\sum_{k=1}^{n-1} \frac{2^{k-1}}{k \ln 10}}$$

**Numerator analysis:** The term at scale $k$ is $\frac{2^{k-1} \mu_k}{k \ln 10}
 \approx \frac{19 \cdot 2^{k-1} \cdot 10^k}{18 \cdot k \ln 10}$. Consecutive ter
ms have ratio $\frac{20(k-1)}{k} \approx 20$, so the sum is a geometric series w
ith ratio $1/20$:

$$\text{Numerator} \sim \frac{19 \cdot 2^{n-2} \cdot 10^{n-1}}{18(n-1)\ln 10} \c
dot \frac{20}{19} = \frac{20 \cdot 2^{n-2} \cdot 10^{n-1}}{18(n-1)\ln 10}$$

**Denominator analysis:** The term at scale $k$ is $\frac{2^{k-1}}{k \ln 10}$. C
onsecutive terms have ratio $\frac{2(k-1)}{k} \approx 2$, so:

$$\text{Denominator} \sim \frac{2^{n-2}}{(n-1)\ln 10} \cdot 2 = \frac{2^{n-1}}{(
n-1)\ln 10}$$

**Taking the ratio:**

$$\overline{N}_{\text{prime}} \sim \frac{20 \cdot 2^{n-2} \cdot 10^{n-1}}{18(n-1
)\ln 10} \div \frac{2^{n-1}}{(n-1)\ln 10} = \frac{20 \cdot 10^{n-1}}{18 \cdot 2}
 = \frac{10^n}{18}$$

**Key observation:** The slowly-varying factor $1/(k \ln 10)$ cancels between nu
merator and denominator (both dominated by $k = n-1$), so the prime-weighted ave
rage equals the unweighted average to leading order: $\overline{N}_{\text{prime}
} \sim \overline{N} \sim \frac{10^n}{18}$.

## Computing the Limit

$$\frac{S_n}{T_n} = \frac{\overline{N}_{\text{prime}}}{10^n - 1} \sim \frac{10^n
/18}{10^n} = \frac{1}{18}$$

**Verification for small $n$:** For $n=3$, primes in $M_3$ are $\{11, 101\}$, gi
ving $S_3/T_3 = \frac{112}{2 \cdot 999} = \frac{56}{999} \approx 0.0561$, close
to $\frac{1}{18} \approx 0.0556$. The convergence improves as $n$ grows and the
top-scale primes dominate.
