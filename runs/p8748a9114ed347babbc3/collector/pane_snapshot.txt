## Proof

**Claim.** The commutator $[X, Y] = XY - YX$ satisfies the Jacobi identity:
$$[X,[Y,Z]] + [Y,[Z,X]] + [Z,[X,Y]] = 0.$$

**Answer leak check.** The problem statement contains only the definition of the
 commutator and the question. No solution, proof sketch, or answer value is embe
dded. No leak detected.

**Proof.** Assume $X, Y, Z$ are elements of an associative algebra (so that mult
iplication is associative but not necessarily commutative). We expand each doubl
e commutator using only the definition $[A,B]=AB-BA$ and associativity.

**First term.**
$$[X,[Y,Z]] = X(YZ - ZY) - (YZ - ZY)X = XYZ - XZY - YZX + ZYX.$$

**Second term.**
$$[Y,[Z,X]] = Y(ZX - XZ) - (ZX - XZ)Y = YZX - YXZ - ZXY + XZY.$$

**Third term.**
$$[Z,[X,Y]] = Z(XY - YX) - (XY - YX)Z = ZXY - ZYX - XYZ + YXZ.$$

**Summing all three terms:**
$$[X,[Y,Z]] + [Y,[Z,X]] + [Z,[X,Y]]$$
$$= (XYZ - XZY - YZX + ZYX) + (YZX - YXZ - ZXY + XZY) + (ZXY - ZYX - XYZ + YXZ).
$$

Collecting like monomials (each appears exactly twice, once with $+$ and once wi
th $-$):

| Monomial | Coefficient |
|---|---|
| $XYZ$ | $+1 - 1 = 0$ |
| $XZY$ | $-1 + 1 = 0$ |
| $YZX$ | $-1 + 1 = 0$ |
| $ZYX$ | $+1 - 1 = 0$ |
| $YXZ$ | $-1 + 1 = 0$ |
| $ZXY$ | $-1 + 1 = 0$ |

Every monomial cancels, so
$$[X,[Y,Z]] + [Y,[Z,X]] + [Z,[X,Y]] = 0.$$

**Remark on the mechanism.** The cancellation is not accidental. Each of the six
 ordered monomials $XYZ, XZY, YZX, ZYX, YXZ, ZXY$ appears once with a positive s
ign (as the "left-multiplied" piece $A\cdot BC$ of some $[A,[B,C]]$) and once wi
th a negative sign (as the "right-multiplied" piece $BC\cdot A$ of some $[A,[B,C
]]$). Concretely, the positive occurrence of $XYZ$ comes from $X\cdot(YZ)$ in $[
X,[Y,Z]]$, while its negative occurrence comes from $-(XY)\cdot Z$ in $[Z,[X,Y]]
$ — the same triple product, just bracketed on the other side. Thus the Jacobi i
dentity is precisely the statement that **re-associating a triple product is fre
e**, i.e., it is a direct consequence of associativity. This is why the identity
 holds in *every* associative algebra: matrix algebras, endomorphism rings, grou
p algebras, universal enveloping algebras, etc.

**Conclusion.** Yes, commutators defined by $[X,Y]=XY-YX$ satisfy the Jacobi ide
ntity. This equips any associative algebra with the structure of a Lie algebra u
nder the commutator bracket.
