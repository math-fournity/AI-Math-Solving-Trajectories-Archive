## Proof

We have 100 fans indexed 1 to 100, all starting Red. Two toggle commands are app
lied:
1. Toggle every fan whose index is **even**.
2. Toggle every fan whose index is a **multiple of 3**.

A fan ends Red iff it is toggled an **even number of times** (0 or 2); it ends Y
ellow iff toggled an **odd** number of times (1).

### Classification by toggle count

For each index $n \in \{1,\dots,100\}$, let $E$ = "$n$ is even" and $T$ = "$n$ i
s a multiple of 3". The toggle count is $|E| + |T|$ (each as 0/1).

| Category | Condition | Toggles | Final color |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | neither even nor multiple of 3 | 0 | Red |
| B | even, not multiple of 3 | 1 | Yellow |
| C | multiple of 3, not even | 1 | Yellow |
| D | both (multiple of 6) | 2 | Red |

### Counting each category

- **D (multiples of 6):** $\lfloor 100/6 \rfloor = 16$.
- **B (even, not mult. of 3):** even numbers = 50; subtract multiples of 6: $50
- 16 = 34$.
- **C (mult. of 3, not even):** multiples of 3 = $\lfloor 100/3 \rfloor = 33$; s
ubtract multiples of 6: $33 - 16 = 17$.
- **A (neither):** $100 - 16 - 34 - 17 = 33$.

### Final count of Red fans

Red fans = Category A + Category D = $33 + 16 = 49$.

**Check:** Yellow = B + C = $34 + 17 = 51$; total $49 + 51 = 100$. ✓

### Answer

**49 fans** are still displaying the "Standard Red" light.
