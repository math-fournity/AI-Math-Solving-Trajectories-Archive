**Answer: Yes.**

## Proof

Let $u: G \to B(H)$ be a strongly continuous unitary representation. We show $u$
 is strictly continuous, i.e., for every $g_0 \in G$ and every compact operator
$y \in B_0(H)$:
$$\|u_g\, y - u_{g_0}\, y\| \to 0 \quad\text{and}\quad \|y\, u_g - y\, u_{g_0}\|
 \to 0 \quad\text{as } g \to g_0.$$

### Step 1: $u$ is $*$-strongly continuous

By hypothesis, $g \mapsto u_g$ is strongly continuous: $\|u_g \xi - u_{g_0}\xi\|
 \to 0$ for every $\xi \in H$.

Since each $u_g$ is unitary, $u_g^* = u_g^{-1} = u_{g^{-1}}$. The inversion map
$g \mapsto g^{-1}$ is continuous on any topological group, so $g \to g_0$ implie
s $g^{-1} \to g_0^{-1}$. By strong continuity:
$$\|u_g^* \xi - u_{g_0}^* \xi\| = \|u_{g^{-1}}\xi - u_{g_0^{-1}}\xi\| \to 0.$$
Hence $u$ is $*$-strongly continuous.

### Step 2: $u(G)$ is bounded

Every $u_g$ is unitary, so $\|u_g\| = 1$ for all $g$. Thus $u(G) \subset B(H)$ i
s contained in the unit ball.

opology

on + \sum_{k=1}^n \|(x_i - x)\eta_k\|\,\|\xi_k\|.$$
The sum tends to $0$ by strong convergence, so $\limsup \|(x_i - x)y\| \leq 2M\v

For the other side:
$$\|y(x_i - x)\| \leq \|(y - f)(x_i - x)\| + \|f(x_i - x)\| \leq 2M\varepsilon +

The sum tends to $0$ by $*$-strong convergence (i.e., strong convergence of adjo

### Step 4: Conclusion

Fix $g_0 \in G$ and consider the net $(u_g)_{g \to g_0}$. This net:
- is bounded ($\|u_g\| = 1$),
- converges $*$-strongly to $u_{g_0}$ (Step 1).

By Step 3, on the bounded set $\{u_g : g \in G\} \cup \{u_{g_0}\}$, $*$-strong c

$$\|u_g\, y - u_{g_0}\, y\| \to 0 \quad\text{and}\quad \|y\, u_g - y\, u_{g_0}\|

This is precisely strict continuity of $u: G \to B(H)$. $\blacksquare$
