## Setup

The value starts at $V_0 = 1$. Each step multiplies by one of $\{-1.25, -1, 0, 1
, 1.25\}$, each with probability $1/5$. Since $0$ is absorbing and reached with
probability 1, the process terminates a.s.

**Key observation:** The magnitude of $V_n$ is $(1.25)^k$ where $k$ = number of
$\pm 1.25$ factors used. The level $k$ is **non-decreasing** (factors $\pm 1$ do
n't change it, $\pm 1.25$ increase it by 1, $0$ kills the process). The sign is
$+$ or $-$, flipping with negative factors.

So every value is $\pm(1.25)^k$ or $0$. Since $V_0 = 1 > 0$, the maximum $M \geq
 1$, and $M = (1.25)^{K^+}$ where $K^+$ is the largest level $k$ at which the si
gn is $+$.

## Per-Level Analysis

At level $k$, we have a Markov chain on sign $\{+, -\}$ with transitions (per st
ep):

| From \ Event | Stay same sign | Flip sign | Leave to $k{+}1$, same sign | Leav
e to $k{+}1$, flip sign | Die |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $(k,+)$ | $1/5$ (factor $1$) | $1/5$ (factor $-1$) | $1/5$ (factor $1.25$) | $
1/5$ (factor $-1.25$) | $1/5$ (factor $0$) |
| $(k,-)$ | $1/5$ (factor $1$) | $1/5$ (factor $-1$) | $1/5$ (factor $1.25$) | $
1/5$ (factor $-1.25$) | $1/5$ (factor $0$) |

Let $a, b, c$ = probabilities (from $(k,+)$) of leaving to $(k{+}1,+)$, $(k{+}1,
-)$, dying. Let $d, e, f$ = same from $(k,-)$. First-step equations:

$$a = \tfrac{1}{5}(a + d + 1),\quad b = \tfrac{1}{5}(b + e + 1),\quad c = \tfrac
{1}{5}(c + f + 1)$$
$$d = \tfrac{1}{5}(d + a + 1),\quad e = \tfrac{1}{5}(e + b + 1),\quad f = \tfrac
{1}{5}(f + c + 1)$$

Solving: $4a - d = 1$ and $4d - a = 1$ gives $a = d = 1/3$. Similarly $b = e = 1
/3$, $c = f = 1/3$.

**Critical result:** Regardless of sign at level $k$, the probability of advanci
ng to $(k{+}1, +)$ is $1/3$, to $(k{+}1, -)$ is $1/3$, and dying is $1/3$.

## Recurrence for Level Reachability

Let $u_k, v_k$ = probabilities of entering level $k$ with sign $+, -$ respective
ly. Then:
$$u_{k+1} = \tfrac{1}{3}(u_k + v_k),\quad v_{k+1} = \tfrac{1}{3}(u_k + v_k)$$

With $u_0 = 1, v_0 = 0$: $u_k = v_k = \frac{1}{3}\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{k-1}$
 for $k \geq 1$.

## Probability of Visiting $(k, +)$

Let $r_k = P(\text{state } (k,+) \text{ is ever visited})$. Clearly $r_0 = 1$.

For $k \geq 1$: if we enter with sign $+$ (prob $u_k$), we've visited $(k,+)$. I
f we enter with sign $-$ (prob $v_k$), we must flip to $+$ before leaving/dying.
 The flip probability (from $(k,-)$, reaching $(k,+)$ before leaving/dying) sati
sfies $p = \frac{1}{5}(p + 1)$, giving $p = 1/4$.

So: $r_k = u_k + \frac{1}{4}v_k = \frac{1}{3}\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{k-1}\left
(1 + \frac{1}{4}\right) = \frac{5}{12}\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{k-1}$ for $k \ge
q 1$.

## Computing the Expected Maximum

Since $P(K^+ = k) = r_k - r_{k+1}$:

- $r_0 - r_1 = 1 - \frac{5}{12} = \frac{7}{12}$
- For $k \geq 1$: $r_k - r_{k+1} = \frac{5}{12}\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{k-1}\le
ft(1 - \frac{2}{3}\right) = \frac{5}{36}\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{k-1}$

$$E[M] = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \left(\frac{5}{4}\right)^k P(K^+ = k) = \frac{7}{12
} + \frac{5}{36}\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{5}{4}\right)^k\left(\frac{2}{3}\r

Substituting $j = k-1$:

$$\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{5}{4}\right)^k\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{k-1} =

$$E[M] = \frac{7}{12} + \frac{5}{36}\cdot\frac{15}{2} = \frac{7}{12} + \frac{75}
