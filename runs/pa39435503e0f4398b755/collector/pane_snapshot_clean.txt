# Solution

**Claim:** $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{cts}}(\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z},\,\mathbb{Q}/\math
bb{Z})\cong \widehat{\mathbb{Z}}=\varprojlim_{n}\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ as a top
ological ring, equipped with its natural profinite topology.

---

## Step 1. Continuity is automatic (domain is discrete)

$\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$ carries the discrete topology, so every set-theoretic ma
p out of it is continuous. Hence
$$\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{cts}}(\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z},\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})=\math
rm{End}(\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})$$
as an algebraic (additive) group / ring; only the topology remains to be identif
ied.

---

## Step 2. Algebraic identification $\mathrm{End}(\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})\cong\wi
dehat{\mathbb{Z}}$

Write $\mu_n:=\tfrac{1}{n}\mathbb{Z}/\mathbb{Z}\subset\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$, th
e unique cyclic subgroup of order $n$; it is generated by $\bar{1/n}$. Since $\m
athbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}=\bigcup_{n\ge1}\mu_n$ with $\mu_m\subseteq\mu_n$ when $m\mid
 n$, an endomorphism $f$ is determined by the values $f(\bar{1/n})\in\mu_n$ (the
 image of a generator of a cyclic group of order $n$ has order dividing $n$, so
it lands in $\mu_n$). Thus
$$f(\bar{1/n})=\overline{a_n/n},\qquad a_n\in\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}.$$

Compatibility under divisibility: if $m\mid n$, then $\bar{1/m}=\overline{(n/m)/
n}$, so
$$\overline{a_m/m}=f(\bar{1/m})=f\!\left(\overline{(n/m)/n}\right)=\overline{(n/
m)\,a_n/n},$$
which is equivalent to $a_n\equiv a_m\pmod{m}$. The data $(a_n)_{n\ge1}$ is ther
efore a compatible system in $\varprojlim_n\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}=\widehat{\math
bb{Z}}$. Conversely every such system defines an endomorphism by $f(\overline{k/
n})=\overline{k\,a_n/n}$. This bijection respects addition and composition, so
$$\mathrm{End}(\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})\;\cong\;\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}\;=\;\prod_p\m
athbb{Z}_p$$
as a topological ring *algebraically* (the ring structure is yet to be topologiz
ed).

---

## Step 3. The compact–open topology equals pointwise convergence

For a discrete domain, compact subsets are exactly the finite subsets; for a dis
crete codomain every subset is open. The compact–open subbasic sets are
$$,U]=\{f:f(K)\subseteq U\},\qquad K\text{ finite},\;U\subseteq\mathbb{Q}/\mat
hbb{Z}.$$
Since $,U]=\bigcap_{x\in K}[\{x\},U]$, this topology coincides with the **topo
logy of pointwise convergence**: $f_\alpha\to f$ iff $f_\alpha(x)=f(x)$ eventual
ly for every $x\in\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$ (discreteness of the codomain turns "co
nverges to" into "is eventually equal to").

---

## Step 4. Pointwise convergence on $\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}$ is the profinite topo
logy

Identify $f$ with $a=(a_n)\in\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}$. Because every element of $\m
athbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$ is an integral multiple of some $\bar{1/n}$, and $f_a(\over
line{k/n})=\overline{k\,a_n/n}$, agreement $f_{a^{(\alpha)}}(\overline{k/n})=f_a
(\overline{k/n})$ is forced by agreement on $\bar{1/n}$, i.e. by $a^{(\alpha)}_n
=a_n$. Hence
$$f_{a^{(\alpha)}}\to f_a\ \text{pointwise}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \forall
 n:\ a^{(\alpha)}\equiv a\pmod n\ \text{eventually}.$$
The right-hand side is precisely convergence in the profinite (inverse-limit) to
pology on $\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}=\varprojlim\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$, whose basic
open neighborhoods of $a$ are the cosets $a+n\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}$.

---

## Conclusion

Combining Steps 2 and 4,

$$\boxed{\;\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{cts}}(\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z},\,\mathbb{Q}/\mathb
b{Z})\;\cong\;\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}\;=\;\varprojlim_{n}\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}\;\c
ong\;\prod_{p}\mathbb{Z}_p\;}$$

as a topological ring, where $\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}$ carries its natural profinit
e topology. (The discreteness of the domain makes every homomorphism continuous;
 the compact–open topology then reproduces exactly the profinite topology on the
 endomorphism ring.)
