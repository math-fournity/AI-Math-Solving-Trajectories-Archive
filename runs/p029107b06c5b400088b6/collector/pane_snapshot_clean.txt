## Setup

Let $n$ be the number of guilds. Each guild plays $n-1$ games. Each game is eith
er decisive (3–0) or a draw (1–1). A guild's score is $3w + d$ where $w$ = wins,
 $d$ = draws, $l$ = losses, with $w+d+l = n-1$.

The **maximum possible score** for a single participant (winning every game) is
$3(n-1)$. Let $m = n-1$, so max $= 3m$.

The Sole Victor has score $S$ with $S < \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot 3m = 1.5m$, and $S$ is
 strictly greater than every other guild's score.

**Key identity:** Score $= 3w + d = 3w + (m - w - l) = m + 2w - l$.

All-draws baseline gives every guild score $m$. A decisive game (vs. draw) chang
es the winner by $+2$ and the loser by $-1$.

## Eliminating small $n$

players, $T \le 2+1+1 = 4 < 6$. Impossible.

**$n=4$ ($m=3$):** $S < 4.5 \Rightarrow S\le 4$. All draws give $(3,3,3,3)$. Any
 decisive win raises its winner to $3+2=5 > 4$. A decisive loss only lowers some
one. So no guild can exceed $3$ without exceeding $4$ — no sole victor at $\le 4

**$n=5$ ($m=4$):** $S < 6 \Rightarrow S\le 5$. Score $= 4 + 2w - l$.
