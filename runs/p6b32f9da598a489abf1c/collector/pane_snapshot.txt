## Solution

**Key observation.** For any positive real $a$ and any $N>0$,

$$N\sin\!\left(\frac{a}{N}\right) = a - \frac{a^{3}}{6\,N^{2}} + \frac{a^{5}}{12
0\,N^{4}} - \cdots$$

This is the Taylor expansion of $\sin$ at $0$, since $N\sin(a/N) = N\!\left(\fra
c{a}{N} - \frac{(a/N)^{3}}{6} + \frac{(a/N)^{5}}{120} - \cdots\right) = a - \fra
c{a^{3}}{6N^{2}} + \frac{a^{5}}{120N^{4}} - \cdots$.

**Apply to each term.** Set

| term | $a$ | $N$ | sign |
|------|-----|-----|------|
| 1 | 109 | $10^{10^{10}}$ | $+$ |
| 2 | 101 | $9^{9^{9}}$ | $-$ |
| 3 | 17  | $8^{8^{8}}$  | $-$ |
| 4 | 76  | $7^{7^{7}}$  | $+$ |
| 5 | 113 | $6^{6^{6}}$  | $+$ |

Each term has the form $\pm\,N\sin(a/N) = \pm\!\left(a - \dfrac{a^{3}}{6N^{2}} +
 \dfrac{a^{5}}{120N^{4}} - \cdots\right)$.

**Leading (constant) part.**

$$109 - 101 - 17 + 76 + 113 = (109+76+113) - (101+17) = 298 - 118 = 180.$$

**Remainder estimate.** The error in each term is bounded by

$$\left|N\sin\!\left(\frac{a}{N}\right) - a\right| \le \frac{a^{3}}{6N^{2}}.$$

The largest $a$ here is $113$, and the **smallest** $N$ is $6^{6^{6}}$. Now

$$6^{6^{6}} = 6^{46656}, \qquad \log_{10} 6^{46656} = 46656\log_{10}6 \approx 46
656 \times 0.77815 \approx 36305.$$

So $6^{6^{6}} > 10^{36000}$, hence $N^{2} > 10^{72000}$. Therefore the total err
or satisfies

$$|\text{error}| \le \sum \frac{a_i^{3}}{6N_i^{2}} < \frac{5 \cdot 113^{3}}{6 \c
dot (6^{6^{6}})^{2}} < \frac{10^{7}}{10^{72000}} < 10^{-71993}.$$

This is an astronomically small, strictly **positive** quantity (each $a>0$, eac
h correction $a^{3}/(6N^{2})>0$, and the signs make the net correction $-\frac{1
09^{3}}{6N_1^{2}}+\frac{101^{3}}{6N_2^{2}}+\frac{17^{3}}{6N_3^{2}}-\frac{76^{3}}
{6N_4^{2}}-\frac{113^{3}}{6N_5^{2}}$, which is nonzero in general).

**Conclusion.** The expression equals

$$180 - \delta, \qquad 0 < \delta < 10^{-71993}.$$

Since the problem asks us to **evaluate** the expression and the correction $\de
lta$ is smaller than $10^{-71993}$, the value is $180$ to every practical and re
presentable precision. The intended value of the expression is

$$\boxed{180}.$$
