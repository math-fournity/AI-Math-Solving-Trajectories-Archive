## Solution

We seek the sum of all positive integers $n$ with $1 \le n < 30$ such that $2 \m
id n$, $3 \mid n$, or $5 \mid n$.

**Inclusion–Exclusion.** Let $S_d$ be the sum of positive integers less than $30
$ divisible by $d$.

- **Divisible by 2:** $2, 4, 6, \ldots, 28$ — that's $14$ terms.
  $S_2 = 2(1+2+\cdots+14) = 2 \cdot \tfrac{14\cdot15}{2} = 210.$

- **Divisible by 3:** $3, 6, 9, \ldots, 27$ — that's $9$ terms.
  $S_3 = 3(1+2+\cdots+9) = 3 \cdot \tfrac{9\cdot10}{2} = 135.$

- **Divisible by 5:** $5, 10, 15, 20, 25$ — that's $5$ terms.
  $S_5 = 5(1+2+3+4+5) = 5 \cdot 15 = 75.$

**Pairwise intersections:**

- **Divisible by $\mathrm{lcm}(2,3)=6$:** $6, 12, 18, 24$ — $4$ terms.
  $S_6 = 6(1+2+3+4) = 60.$

- **Divisible by $\mathrm{lcm}(2,5)=10$:** $10, 20$ — $2$ terms.
  $S_{10} = 10(1+2) = 30.$

- **Divisible by $\mathrm{lcm}(3,5)=15$:** $15$ — $1$ term.
  $S_{15} = 15.$

**Triple intersection:** $\mathrm{lcm}(2,3,5)=30$, and there are no positive mul
tiples of $30$ strictly less than $30$, so $S_{30} = 0$.

**Applying inclusion–exclusion:**

$$S = (S_2 + S_3 + S_5) - (S_6 + S_{10} + S_{15}) + S_{30}$$
$$S = (210 + 135 + 75) - (60 + 30 + 15) + 0 = 420 - 105 = \boxed{315}.$$

**Verification by complement.** The sum of all integers $1$ through $29$ is $\tf
rac{29\cdot30}{2} = 435$. The integers in this range **not** divisible by $2$, $
3$, or $5$ are:
$$1,\ 7,\ 11,\ 13,\ 17,\ 19,\ 23,\ 29.$$
Their sum is $1+7+11+13+17+19+23+29 = 120$. Therefore
$$S = 435 - 120 = 315,$$
confirming the result.
