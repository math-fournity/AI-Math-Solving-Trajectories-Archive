## Solution

We solve the first-order linear PDE

$$a\,\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}+b\,\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}=u,\qquad a,b
\neq 0,$$

by the **method of characteristics**.

### Characteristic system

The characteristic (Charpit/Lagrange) equations are

$$\frac{dt}{a}=\frac{dx}{b}=\frac{du}{u}.$$

**First invariant.** From $\dfrac{dt}{a}=\dfrac{dx}{b}$ we get $b\,dt=a\,dx$, he
nce

$$bt-ax=C_{1},$$

which is constant along every characteristic curve.

**Second invariant.** From $\dfrac{dt}{a}=\dfrac{du}{u}$ we get $\dfrac{du}{u}=\
dfrac{dt}{a}$, hence

$$\ln|u|=\frac{t}{a}+C_{2}'\quad\Longrightarrow\quad u\,e^{-t/a}=C_{2},$$

also constant along characteristics.

### General solution

The general integral is obtained by setting one invariant equal to an arbitrary
function of the other:

$$u\,e^{-t/a}=\Phi(bt-ax),$$

where $\Phi$ is an arbitrary $C^{1}$ function. Therefore

$$\boxed{\,u(x,t)=e^{\,t/a}\,\Phi(bt-ax)\,}$$

with $\Phi$ an arbitrary differentiable function.

### Verification

Let $u=e^{t/a}\Phi(bt-ax)$. Then

$$u_{t}=\frac{1}{a}e^{t/a}\Phi(bt-ax)+b\,e^{t/a}\Phi'(bt-ax),$$
$$u_{x}=-a\,e^{t/a}\Phi'(bt-ax).$$

Substituting,

$$a\,u_{t}+b\,u_{x}=a\!\left[\frac{1}{a}e^{t/a}\Phi+b\,e^{t/a}\Phi'\right]+b\!\l
eft[-a\,e^{t/a}\Phi'\right]$$
$$=e^{t/a}\Phi+ab\,e^{t/a}\Phi'-ab\,e^{t/a}\Phi'=e^{t/a}\Phi=u.\;\checkmark$$

The PDE is satisfied identically for every choice of $\Phi$, confirming the gene
ral solution.
