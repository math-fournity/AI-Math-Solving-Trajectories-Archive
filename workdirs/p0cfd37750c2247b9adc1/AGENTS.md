# Problem

In a specialized microchip manufacturing facility, a technician is tasked with arranging 2020 rectangular silicon components onto a single square cooling substrate. The components are indexed from $i = 1$ to $i = 2020$. For every index $i$, the corresponding component has a fixed width of 1 millimeter and a length of exactly $i$ millimeters.

The technician must lay all 2020 components flat on the surface of the square substrate such that no two components overlap and no part of any component extends beyond the boundaries of the square. 

Find the smallest integer $n$ such that a square substrate with side length $n$ millimeters is large enough to accommodate all of these components simultaneously.
