$$x^{16}-1 = \prod_{d\mid 16}\Phi_d(x) = \Phi_1\Phi_2\Phi_4\Phi_8\Phi_{16} = (x-
1)(x+1)(x^2+1)(x^4+1)(x^8+1).$$

ext{char}\,\mathbb{Q}=0$, $x^{16}-1$ is squarefree (its derivative $16x^{15}$ is

$$x-1,\quad x+1,\quad x^2+1,\quad (x-1)(x+1)=x^2-1.$$

(Products involving $x^4+1$ or $x^8+1$ have degree $\ge 4$; products $x^2+1$ wit
