## Solution

**Model as a graph.** Represent the 16 server nodes as vertices and each patch (
covering exactly 2 nodes) as an edge. The three conditions become:

- (i) Every edge connects exactly 2 vertices.
- (ii) No isolated vertex (every vertex has degree ≥ 1).
- (iii) For every edge *e*, deleting *e* creates at least one degree-0 vertex.

**Condition (iii) reformulated.** Removing edge *e* = *uv* creates a degree-0 ve
rtex only if *u* or *v* had degree exactly 1 before removal (so that deleting *e
* drops it to 0). Thus:

> **Every edge is incident to at least one vertex of degree 1.**

**The graph is a disjoint union of stars.**

*Proof.* Consider any connected component *C*. If *C* is a single edge, it is *K
*₂ (a star). Otherwise *C* has ≥ 2 edges. Pick any edge *e* = *uv*; at least one
 endpoint, say *u*, has degree 1. Since *C* has another edge, *v* must have degr
ee ≥ 2 (otherwise *C* = *K*₂). Now take any other edge *vw* incident to *v*. By
the condition, at least one of *v*, *w* has degree 1; since deg(*v*) ≥ 2, we get
 deg(*w*) = 1. Hence **every** edge incident to *v* goes to a degree-1 vertex. B
ut a degree-1 neighbor of *v* has no other edges, so no vertex beyond *v* and it
s neighbors is reachable. Therefore *C* = {*v*} ∪ *N*(*v*) with all edges from *
v* to its leaves — a star *K*₁,ᵈ. ∎

**Maximize edges.** Suppose the graph is a disjoint union of *m* stars with *d*₁
, *d*₂, …, *dₘ* leaves (*dᵢ* ≥ 1). Then:

- Total vertices: ∑(*dᵢ* + 1) = (∑*dᵢ*) + *m* = 16.
- Total edges (= patches *k*): ∑*dᵢ* = 16 − *m*.

To maximize *k* = 16 − *m*, minimize *m*. The minimum is *m* = 1: a single star
*K*₁,₁₅ covering all 16 nodes.

**Verification that *k* = 15 works.** Take one center node connected to the othe
r 15 nodes (15 patches). Every node has degree ≥ 1. Each patch connects the cent
er (degree 15) to a leaf (degree 1). Removing any patch drops its leaf to degree
 0 — that leaf becomes completely unprotected. All three conditions hold.

**No larger *k* is possible.** Since *k* = 16 − *m* ≤ 16 − 1 = 15, no configurat
ion exceeds 15 patches.

**The maximum possible number of patches is *k* = 15.**
