## Proof

Let $f: S^n \to S^n$ be the reflection $f(x_0,\dots,x_n) = (x_0,\dots,x_{n-1},-x
_n)$, with $n \geq 1$.

**Goal.** Compute $\deg(f)$, the integer by which $f_*: H_n(S^n) \to H_n(S^n)$ a
cts on the generator of $H_n(S^n) \cong \mathbb{Z}$.

---

### Step 1. $f$ is a homeomorphism

$f \circ f = \mathrm{id}$, since flipping the last coordinate twice returns the
original point. Hence $f$ is its own inverse and is a homeomorphism of $S^n$.

---

### Step 2. Choose a regular value and count preimages

Take the north pole $N = (0,\dots,0,1) \in S^n$. Solve $f(x) = N$:
$$(x_0,\dots,x_{n-1},-x_n) = (0,\dots,0,1) \implies x_0 = \cdots = x_{n-1} = 0,\
; x_n = -1.$$
So $f^{-1}(N) = \{S\}$ where $S = (0,\dots,0,-1)$ is the south pole. The preimag
e is a single point.

---

### Step 3. Apply the local-degree formula (singular homology)

The local-degree formula (Hatcher, Prop. 2.30) states: for $f: S^n \to S^n$ and
$y \in S^n$ with $f^{-1}(y) = \{x_1,\dots,x_m\}$ such that $f$ maps a neighborho
od $U_i$ of each $x_i$ homeomorphically onto a neighborhood $V$ of $y$, we have
$$\deg(f) = \sum_{i=1}^{m} \mathrm{deg}(f\!\restriction_{x_i}),$$
where the **local degree** $\mathrm{deg}(f\!\restriction_{x_i})$ is the sign $\p
m 1$ defined by the commutative diagram in singular homology
$$\begin{array}{ccc}
H_n(S^n, S^n - \{x_i\}) & \xrightarrow{\;f_*\;} & H_n(S^n, S^n - \{y\}) \\
\downarrow{\cong} & & \downarrow{\cong} \\
H_n(U_i, U_i - \{x_i\}) & \xrightarrow{\;f_*\;} & H_n(V, V - \{y\})
\end{array}$$
Here the vertical isomorphisms are by excision, and each relative homology group
 is $\cong \mathbb{Z}$, generated by the local orientation class. The local degr
ee is $+1$ if $f$ preserves the local orientation at $x_i$ and $-1$ if it revers
es it.

In our case $m = 1$ and $x_1 = S$, so $\deg(f) = \mathrm{deg}(f\!\restriction_S)
$.

---

### Step 4. Compute the local degree at $S$

Pick small coordinate balls $U$ around $S$ and $V = f(U)$ around $N$. In local c
oordinates (using the standard charts on $S^n$ near the poles), the map $f\!\res
triction_U : U \to V$ is the linear map
$$A = \mathrm{diag}(1,1,\dots,1,-1) \in O(n+1),$$
i.e., a reflection in the last coordinate. The induced map on relative homology
$$f_*: H_n(U, U - \{S\}) \longrightarrow H_n(V, V - \{N\})$$
sends the local orientation generator $\mu_S$ to $\det(A)\cdot \mu_N$ (this is t
he standard fact that a linear map acts on the local orientation class by its de
terminant). Since
$$\det(A) = \det(\mathrm{diag}(1,\dots,1,-1)) = -1,$$
we obtain $f_*(\mu_S) = -\mu_N$, hence
$$\mathrm{deg}(f\!\restriction_S) = -1.$$

---

### Step 5. Conclude

By the local-degree formula,
$$\boxed{\deg(f) = \mathrm{deg}(f\!\restriction_S) = -1.}$$

---

### Verification (sanity check via composition)

The antipodal map $a: S^n \to S^n$, $a(x) = -x$, is the composition of $n+1$ coo
rdinate reflections $r_j$ (each flipping one coordinate). Each $r_j$ is conjugat
e to $f$ by an orthogonal change of coordinates, so each has the same degree as
$f$. Thus
$$\deg(a) = \prod_{j=0}^{n} \deg(r_j) = \deg(f)^{n+1}.$$
It is a standard singular-homology result that $\deg(a) = (-1)^{n+1}$. Therefore
$$\deg(f)^{n+1} = (-1)^{n+1} \implies \deg(f) = -1,$$
confirming the result. $\blacksquare$
