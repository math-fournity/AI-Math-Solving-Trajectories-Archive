## Proof

**Setup and key facts.** Let $R$ be commutative Noetherian with $\operatorname{A
ss}(R)$ consisting entirely of minimal primes (no embedded primes). Let $r \in R
$ with $r/1 = 0$ in $R_P$ for every minimal prime $P$.

**Fact 1 (localization criterion).** For a prime $P$, we have $r/1 = 0$ in $R_P$
 if and only if $\operatorname{Ann}(r) \not\subseteq P$ (equivalently, there exi
sts $s \notin P$ with $sr = 0$).

**Fact 2 (associated primes of submodules).** For a Noetherian ring $R$ and a su
bmodule $N \subseteq M$, we have $\operatorname{Ass}(N) \subseteq \operatorname{
Ass}(M)$. (This follows from the exact sequence $0 \to N \to M \to M/N \to 0$ an
d the standard inclusions $\operatorname{Ass}(N) \subseteq \operatorname{Ass}(M)
 \subseteq \operatorname{Ass}(N) \cup \operatorname{Ass}(M/N)$.)

**Fact 3 (nonzero finitely generated modules have associated primes).** A nonzer
o finitely generated module over a Noetherian ring has nonempty $\operatorname{A
ss}$.

**The argument.** Suppose for contradiction that $r \neq 0$. Then the submodule
$rR \subseteq R$ is nonzero. Since $R$ is Noetherian, $rR$ is finitely generated
, so by Fact 3, $\operatorname{Ass}(rR) \neq \emptyset$. By Fact 2 (applied to $
rR \hookrightarrow R$), we have $\operatorname{Ass}(rR) \subseteq \operatorname{
Ass}(R)$.

Pick any $P \in \operatorname{Ass}(rR)$. Since $rR \cong R/\operatorname{Ann}(r)
$, the prime $P$ is an associated prime of $R/\operatorname{Ann}(r)$, which forc
es $\operatorname{Ann}(r) \subseteq P$.

By hypothesis, all associated primes of $R$ are minimal, so $P$ is a minimal pri
me of $R$. Since $\operatorname{Ann}(r) \subseteq P$, Fact 1 gives $r/1 \neq 0$
in $R_P$.

But this contradicts the hypothesis that $r/1 = 0$ in $R_P$ for **every** minima
l prime $P$. Therefore $r = 0$. $\blacksquare$

- *All associated primes minimal:* Without this, take $R = k[x,y]/(x^2, xy)$. He

 the conclusion fails.

- *Noetherian:* Fact 3 (and the theory of associated primes used throughout) req
