## Solution

Let the three integer roots be $r, r, s$ (two coincide), with $r \neq s$ and $r,
 s \in \mathbb{Z}$.

By Vieta's formulas for $x^3 + ax^2 + bx + 9a$:

$$2r + s = -a, \qquad r^2 + 2rs = b, \qquad r^2 s = -9a.$$

**Eliminating $a$.** From the first equation $a = -(2r+s)$. Substituting into th
e third:

$$r^2 s = 9(2r + s) \implies s(r^2 - 9) = 18r \implies s = \frac{18r}{r^2 - 9},
\quad (r^2 \neq 9).$$

The case $r^2 = 9$ gives $0 = 18r = \pm 54$, a contradiction, so $r \neq \pm 3$.

**Finding integer solutions.** Since $|s| = \left|\frac{18r}{r^2-9}\right| \to 0
$ as $|r| \to \infty$, we need $|18r| \geq |r^2 - 9|$, which bounds $|r| \leq 18
$. Checking $r = 1, 2, 4, 5, \ldots, 18$ (excluding $0, \pm 3$), the only value
yielding an integer $s$ is:

$$r = 6: \quad s = \frac{108}{27} = 4.$$

(The substitution $r \mapsto -r$ gives $s \mapsto -s$ and flips the sign of $a$
while preserving $b$, so $|ab|$ is unchanged; this yields the companion solution
 $r = -6, s = -4$.)

**Computing $a, b$.** For $r = 6, s = 4$:

$$a = -(2\cdot 6 + 4) = -16, \qquad b = 36 + 2\cdot 6\cdot 4 = 84.$$

**Verification.** The cubic is $x^3 - 16x^2 + 84x - 144$. With roots $6, 6, 4$:
- Sum: $6+6+4 = 16 = -a$ ✓
- Pairwise sum: $36 + 24 + 24 = 84 = b$ ✓
- Product: $6\cdot 6\cdot 4 = 144 = -9a = 144$ ✓

All three roots are integers and two coincide, as required. Also, $a = -16 \neq
0$ and $b = 84 \neq 0$, satisfying the nonzero condition.

Therefore:

$$|ab| = |-16| \cdot |84| = 16 \cdot 84 = \boxed{1344}.$$

### PROOF COMPLETE
