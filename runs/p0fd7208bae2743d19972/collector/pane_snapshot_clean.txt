## Proof

We compute
$$L = \lim_{n\to+\infty} n\int_X \log\!\Bigl(1+\frac{f(x)}{n}\Bigr)\,d\mu.$$

**Pointwise limit of the integrand.** Define
$$g_n(x) = n\log\!\Bigl(1+\frac{f(x)}{n}\Bigr).$$
Since $\int_X f\,d\mu = 1 < \infty$, we have $f(x) < \infty$ for $\mu$-a.e. $x\i
n X$. At every such point, using the standard limit $\log(1+t)/t \to 1$ as $t\to
 0^+$ with $t = f(x)/n \to 0$,
$$g_n(x) = f(x)\cdot\frac{\log(1+f(x)/n)}{f(x)/n}\;\longrightarrow\; f(x)\cdot 1
 = f(x).$$

**Domination.** For all $t\ge 0$ the elementary inequality $\log(1+t)\le t$ hold
s. Applying it with $t = f(x)/n\ge 0$,
$$0 \le g_n(x) = n\log\!\Bigl(1+\frac{f(x)}{n}\Big) \le n\cdot\frac{f(x)}{n} = f
(x).$$
The dominating function $f$ is integrable since $\int_X f\,d\mu = 1 < \infty$.

**Applying Dominated Convergence.** The sequence $\{g_n\}$ satisfies
- $g_n(x)\to f(x)$ for $\mu$-a.e. $x$,
- $|g_n|\le f$ with $f\in L^1(\mu)$.

By the Dominated Convergence Theorem,
$$\lim_{n\to+\infty}\int_X g_n\,d\mu = \int_X \lim_{n\to+\infty} g_n\,d\mu = \in
t_X f\,d\mu = 1.$$

Therefore
$$\boxed{\lim_{n\to+\infty}n\int_X\log\!\Bigl(1+\frac{f(x)}{n}\Bigr)\,d\mu = 1.}
$$
