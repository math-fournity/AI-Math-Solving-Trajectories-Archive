# Problem

In a remote sector of the galaxy, a research station is housed within a perfectly rectangular cargo module. The interior volume of this module is exactly 1 cubic unit. Suspended at a specific, fixed coordinate $M$ inside the module is a singular micro-sensor.

To calibrate the station's sensors, six virtual "ghost" beacons are projected outside the module. Each beacon is positioned by taking the location of the micro-sensor $M$ and reflecting it across the plane of one of the module's six rectangular walls.

A specialized containment field, denoted as $\mathcal{D}$, is generated. This field is defined as the smallest convex polyhedral volume that encompasses all six projected ghost beacons. Engineers need to calculate the precise volume of the region where the cargo module and the containment field overlap. 

Determine the volume of the intersection of the module's interior and the containment field $\mathcal{D}$.
