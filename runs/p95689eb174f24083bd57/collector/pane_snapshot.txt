Let $a,b,c$ be the roots of $x^3+3x^2+5x+7=0$. By Vieta's formulas:
$$a+b+c=-3,\quad ab+bc+ca=5,\quad abc=-7.$$

**Step 1: Reformulate the interpolation conditions.**

Since $a+b+c=-3$, we have $b+c=-3-a$, $c+a=-3-b$, $a+b=-3-c$. The conditions bec
ome:
$$P(a)=-3-a,\quad P(b)=-3-b,\quad P(c)=-3-c.$$

**Step 2: Construct the polynomial $P(x)+x+3$.**

Define $Q(x)=P(x)+x+3$. Then
$$Q(a)=Q(b)=Q(c)=0.$$
So $a,b,c$ are all roots of $Q$. Since $P$ is cubic, $Q$ is also cubic, and $(x-
a)(x-b)(x-c)=x^3+3x^2+5x+7$ is a cubic with exactly these roots. Therefore
$$Q(x)=k(x^3+3x^2+5x+7)$$
for some constant $k$, i.e.,
$$P(x)=k(x^3+3x^2+5x+7)-x-3.$$

**Step 3: Use the constraint $P(a+b+c)=-16$.**

Since $a+b+c=-3$:
$$P(-3)=k\big((-3)^3+3(-3)^2+5(-3)+7\big)-(-3)-3$$
$$=k(-27+27-15+7)+0=k(-8).$$

Setting $P(-3)=-16$:
$$-8k=-16\implies k=2.$$

**Step 4: Compute $P(0)$.**

$$P(x)=2(x^3+3x^2+5x+7)-x-3=2x^3+6x^2+9x+11.$$
$$P(0)=2\cdot 7-0-3=11.$$

The value of $P(0)$ is $\boxed{11}$.
