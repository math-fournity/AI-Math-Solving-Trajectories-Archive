# Problem

In a futuristic energy grid, a central reactor's stability is measured by a power index $n$, which is a non-negative integer no greater than $100$. The reactor is fueled by three synchronized plasma cores, each operating at a discrete positive integer energy level: $x$, $y$, and $z$. 

The total harmonic output of the system is governed by a specific thermodynamic function: the sum of the cubes of the individual energy levels, minus three times the product of those levels. For a reactor state to be considered "internally balanced," this output must exactly equal the power index $n$.

A grid architect needs to identify all possible power indices $n$ (where $0 \le n \le 100$) that allow the reactor to be balanced using at least one set of positive integer energy levels $(x, y, z)$. 

What is the sum of all such power indices $n$ for which the equation $x^3 + y^3 + z^3 - 3xyz = n$ has a solution in positive integers?
