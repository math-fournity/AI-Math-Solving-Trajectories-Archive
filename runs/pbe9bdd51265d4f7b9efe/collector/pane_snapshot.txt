## Problem

Find the formula for the volume of a simplex in $N$ dimensions, where each verte
x is located at a unit distance from the origin along each axis.

## Setup

The simplex is the convex hull of $N+1$ vertices in $\mathbb{R}^N$:
$$v_0 = \mathbf{0} = (0,0,\dots,0), \quad v_1 = \mathbf{e}_1 = (1,0,\dots,0), \q
uad v_2 = \mathbf{e}_2 = (0,1,\dots,0), \quad \dots, \quad v_N = \mathbf{e}_N =
(0,0,\dots,1).$$

This is the **standard (unit) simplex** $\Delta_N = \{(x_1,\dots,x_N) : x_i \ge
0,\ \sum x_i \le 1\}$.

## Proof by induction on dimension

**Base case $N=1$.** The simplex is the segment $[0,1]$ on the real line, with l
ength (1-volume) $= 1 = 1/1!$. ✓

**Base case $N=2$.** The simplex is the right triangle with vertices $(0,0),(1,0
),(0,1)$, area $= \tfrac12 = 1/2!$. ✓

**Inductive step.** Assume the $(N-1)$-dimensional standard simplex has volume $
1/(N-1)!$. We compute the $N$-volume of $\Delta_N$ by integrating its $(N-1)$-di
mensional cross-sections.

Fix $x_N = t$ with $t \in [0,1]$. The cross-section of $\Delta_N$ at height $t$
is
$$\{(x_1,\dots,x_{N-1}) : x_i \ge 0,\ \textstyle\sum_{i=1}^{N-1} x_i \le 1-t\}.$
$
Rescaling $y_i = x_i/(1-t)$ (for $t<1$) maps this to the standard $(N-1)$-simple
x $\Delta_{N-1}$, with linear scale factor $(1-t)$ in each of $N-1$ directions.
Hence the cross-section's $(N-1)$-volume is
$$(1-t)^{N-1} \cdot \operatorname{Vol}_{N-1}(\Delta_{N-1}) = (1-t)^{N-1} \cdot \
frac{1}{(N-1)!}.$$

Integrating over $t \in [0,1]$:
$$\operatorname{Vol}_N(\Delta_N) = \int_0^1 \frac{(1-t)^{N-1}}{(N-1)!}\,dt = \fr
ac{1}{(N-1)!}\cdot\frac{1}{N} = \frac{1}{N!}.$$

This completes the induction.

## Cross-check via the determinant formula

For a simplex with one vertex at the origin and edges $a_1,\dots,a_N$ emanating
from it, the volume is
$$\operatorname{Vol} = \frac{1}{N!}\,|\det(a_1,\dots,a_N)|.$$
Here $a_i = \mathbf{e}_i$, so the matrix is the $N\times N$ identity with determ
inant $1$. Therefore
$$\operatorname{Vol} = \frac{1}{N!}\cdot 1 = \frac{1}{N!}.$$

Both methods agree.

## Result

The volume of the $N$-dimensional simplex whose vertices are the origin and the
$N$ unit points along each coordinate axis is

$$\boxed{\operatorname{Vol}_N = \dfrac{1}{N!}}.$$
