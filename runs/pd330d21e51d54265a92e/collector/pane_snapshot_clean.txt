**Area transfer.** Since $R$ preserves area and maps $\triangle ABP$ to $\triang

$$[ABP] + [BCP] = [BCP'] + [BCP].$$

**Quadrilateral decomposition.** We claim $B, P, C, P'$ form a convex quadrilate

*Proof of convexity:* Place $B$ at the origin with ray $BA$ along angle $0°$ and
 ray $BC$ along angle $60°$. Since $P$ is inside the triangle, $P$ lies at some
angle $\varphi \in (0°, 60°)$ from $B$. Then $P' = R(P)$ lies at angle $\varphi
+ 60° \in (60°, 120°)$, which is on the **opposite side** of line $BC$ from $P$.
 Likewise, $B$ and $C$ lie on opposite sides of line $PP'$: the signed distance
of $C$ from line $PP'$ is $-s\cos(30°-\varphi)+4$, which is negative since $s >
17.3$ and $\cos(30°-\varphi) \geq \cos 30° = \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$, giving $s\cos(3
0°-\varphi) > 15 > 4$. Thus both $BC$ and $PP'$ are diagonals of the convex quad
rilateral $B\text{-}P\text{-}C\text{-}P'$.

Splitting this quadrilateral by diagonal $BC$ and by diagonal $PP'$:
$$[BCP] + [BCP'] = [BPP'] + [CPP'].$$

Therefore:
$$[ABP] + [BCP] = 16\sqrt{3} + [CPP'].$$

$$[CPP'] = \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot PP'\cdot CP'\cdot\sin(\angle PP'C) = \tfrac{1}{2}\c
dot 8\cdot 15\cdot\sin(\angle PP'C) = 60\sin(\angle PP'C) \leq 60,$$
with equality when $\angle PP'C = 90°$, yielding $CP = \sqrt{8^2+15^2} = 17$.
