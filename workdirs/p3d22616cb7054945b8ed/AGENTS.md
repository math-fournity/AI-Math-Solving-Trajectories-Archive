# Problem

In a high-security research facility, there is a server rack consisting of a $10 \times 10$ grid of 100 independent data storage nodes. To ensure the survival of a critical encryption key, a technician must distribute the key across 10 specific nodes on a single backup drive.

The facility’s security protocol dictates that every evening, exactly 10 nodes in the grid are randomly targeted by a "data-wipe" virus and completely erased. A backup drive is considered successful (it "survives") only if none of the 10 nodes assigned to that drive are among the 10 nodes targeted by the virus.

The technician needs to prepare a set of different backup drives, each programmed with its own unique set of 10 nodes. Let $N$ be the minimum number of backup drives the technician must prepare to mathematically guarantee that at least one drive survives the evening, regardless of which 10 nodes the virus targets.

Find $N$.
