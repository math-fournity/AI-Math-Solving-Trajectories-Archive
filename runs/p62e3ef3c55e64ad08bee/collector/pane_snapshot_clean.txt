Looking at this problem, I need to determine whether the ramification index $e_b
$ and residue degree $f_b$ are independent of the choice of prime $b$ of $B$ in
a finite Galois extension $L/K$.

**Answer: Yes, $e_b$ and $f_b$ are independent of the choice of $b$.**

## Proof

### Setup

Let $a$ be the unique nonzero prime (maximal ideal) of the DVR $A$. Since $A$ is
 a DVR, $a$ is the only prime of $A$ that can lie under primes of $B$. Let $b_1,
 \ldots, b_g$ be the primes of $B$ lying over $a$. Since $K$ has characteristic
zero, $L/K$ is separable; combined with the Galois hypothesis, $L/K$ is a finite
 Galois extension. The integral closure $B$ of the DVR $A$ in the finite separab
le extension $L$ is a finite $A$-module and a Dedekind domain, so the factorizat
ion

$$aB = \prod_{i=1}^{g} b_i^{e_{b_i}}$$

is valid, where $e_{b_i}$ is the ramification index and $f_{b_i} = [B/b_i : A/a]
$ is the residue degree.

Let $G = \mathrm{Gal}(L/K)$.

### Step 1: $G$ acts transitively on $\{b_1, \ldots, b_g\}$

Since $G$ fixes $A$ elementwise and $B$ is the integral closure of $A$ in $L$, e
very $\sigma \in G$ maps $B$ to itself and permutes the primes of $B$ above $a$.

To prove transitivity, suppose for contradiction that $b$ and $b'$ are two prime
s above $a$ with no $\sigma \in G$ satisfying $\sigma(b) = b'$. By the Chinese R
emainder Theorem (the primes $\{b'\} \cup \{\sigma(b) : \sigma \in G\}$ are pair
wise distinct by assumption), there exists $x \in B$ with:

$$x \equiv 0 \pmod{b'}, \qquad x \equiv 1 \pmod{\sigma(b)} \text{ for all } \sig
ma \in G.$$

Since $x \in b'$, the norm $N_{L/K}(x) = \prod_{\sigma \in G} \sigma(x)$ lies in
 $b' \cap A = a$.

On the other hand, replacing $\sigma$ by $\sigma^{-1}$ (which ranges over all of
 $G$), the condition $x \equiv 1 \pmod{\sigma(b)}$ for all $\sigma$ gives $\sigm
a(x) \equiv 1 \pmod{b}$ for all $\sigma \in G$. Hence $\sigma(x) \notin b$ for a
ll $\sigma$, so $N_{L/K}(x) = \prod_\sigma \sigma(x) \notin b$.

But $N_{L/K}(x) \in a \subseteq b$ (since $b \cap A = a$ implies $a \subseteq b$
). This contradicts $N_{L/K}(x) \notin b$. Therefore $G$ acts transitively on $\
{b_1, \ldots, b_g\}$.

### Step 2: All ramification indices are equal

Apply any $\sigma \in G$ to the unique factorization $aB = \prod_{i=1}^g b_i^{e_
{b_i}}$. Since $\sigma$ fixes $A$ (hence fixes $aB$) and maps $B$ to $B$:

$$aB = \sigma(aB) = \prod_{i=1}^{g} \sigma(b_i)^{e_{b_i}}.$$

By uniqueness of prime ideal factorization in the Dedekind domain $B$, if $\sigm
a(b_j) = b_k$, then $e_{b_k} = e_{b_j}$. By transitivity (Step 1), for any two p
rimes $b_j, b_k$ there exists $\sigma$ with $\sigma(b_j) = b_k$, so $e_{b_j} = e
_{b_k}$. Thus all $e_{b_i}$ are equal; call the common value $e$.

### Step 3: All residue degrees are equal

For $\sigma \in G$ with $\sigma(b) = b'$, the automorphism $\sigma: L \to L$ res
tricts to a ring isomorphism $B \to B$ (since $\sigma$ preserves integrality ove
r $A$). This descends to an isomorphism of residue fields:

$$\bar{\sigma}: B/b \xrightarrow{\;\sim\;} B/b'.$$

Since $\sigma$ fixes $A$ elementwise, $\bar{\sigma}$ fixes $A/a$ elementwise, ma
king it an isomorphism of $A/a$-algebras. Therefore:

$$f_b = [B/b : A/a] = [B/b' : A/a] = f_{b'}.$$

By transitivity, all $f_{b_i}$ are equal; call the common value $f$.

### Conclusion

In a finite Galois extension $L/K$ with $A$ a DVR and $B$ the integral closure o
f $A$ in $L$, the Galois group acts transitively on the primes of $B$ above the
unique prime $a$ of $A$. This transitivity, combined with uniqueness of ideal fa
ctorization and the induced isomorphisms on residue fields, forces all ramificat
ion indices to be equal and all residue degrees to be equal. Hence **$e_b$ and $
f_b$ are independent of the choice of $b$**, and the fundamental identity specia
lizes to

$$[L:K] = g \cdot e \cdot f$$

where $g$ is the number of primes of $B$ above $a$.
