## Solution

**Setting up the Stability Index.**

$$\text{SI}(x) = \frac{2^x - 16}{\left(9^{2x+1} - 243\right)\cdot\sqrt{5^{\frac{
x^2-3}{2}} - 125}}$$

**Domain (where SI is defined).**

*Condition 1* (radicand strictly positive): $5^{\frac{x^2-3}{2}} - 125 > 0 \;\Lo
ngrightarrow\; 5^{\frac{x^2-3}{2}} > 5^3 \;\Longrightarrow\; \frac{x^2-3}{2} > 3
 \;\Longrightarrow\; x^2 > 9$, so $x > 3$ or $x < -3$.

*Condition 2* (regulator nonzero): $9^{2x+1} - 243 \neq 0$. Since $9^{2x+1} = 3^
{4x+2}$ and $243 = 3^5$, this fails at $x = \tfrac{3}{4}$, which is outside the
domain above. So Condition 2 is automatically satisfied.

**Domain:** $(-\infty, -3) \cup (3, +\infty)$.

**Sign analysis of the denominator.**

The square-root factor is positive throughout the domain. The sign of the denomi
nator is determined by $9^{2x+1} - 243 = 3^{4x+2} - 3^5$:
- $4x+2 > 5 \;\Longrightarrow\; x > \tfrac{3}{4}$: regulator **positive** → deno
minator **positive** (applies to $x > 3$).
- $x < \tfrac{3}{4}$: regulator **negative** → denominator **negative** (applies
 to $x < -3$).

**Sign analysis of the numerator.** $2^x - 16 = 2^x - 2^4$:
- $x > 4$: positive; $x = 4$: zero; $x < 4$: negative.

**Case 1: $x > 3$ (denominator $> 0$).** $\text{SI} \leq 0 \;\Longleftrightarrow
\;$ numerator $\leq 0 \;\Longleftrightarrow\; x \leq 4$. Intersecting with $x >
3$ gives $(3, 4]$.

**Case 2: $x < -3$ (denominator $< 0$).** Here $x < 4$, so numerator $< 0$. Then
 $\text{SI} = \frac{(-)}{(-)} > 0$, never stable. No contribution.

**The stable set.** $S = (3, 4]$, a single interval.

**Finite endpoints.** Both endpoints $3$ and $4$ are finite. Their sum is

$$3 + 4 = \boxed{7}.$$
