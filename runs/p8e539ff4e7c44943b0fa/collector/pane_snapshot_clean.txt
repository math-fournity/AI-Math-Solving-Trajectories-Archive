## Solution

**Setup.** For an $m \times n \times r$ block ($m \le n \le r$), let $a = \max(m
-2,0)$, $b = \max(n-2,0)$, $c = \max(r-2,0)$. The counts are:

- $k_0 = abc$ (interior cubes),
- $k_1 = 2(bc + ac + ab)$ (face-interior cubes),
- $k_2 = 4(a + b + c)$ (edge-interior cubes).

We split by the smallest dimension $m$.

---

**Case 1: $m \ge 3$.** Then $a = m-2 \ge 1$, $b = n-2 \ge 1$, $c = r-2 \ge 1$, a
nd

$$k_0 + k_2 - k_1 = abc - 2(ab+bc+ca) + 4(a+b+c).$$

**Factoring:** Since $bc - 2b - 2c + 4 = (b-2)(c-2)$,

$$abc - 2ab - 2ac - 2bc + 4a + 4b + 4c = (a-2)(b-2)(c-2) + 8.$$

Substituting back $a = m-2$ etc.:

$$k_0 + k_2 - k_1 = (m-4)(n-4)(r-4) + 8 = 1985,$$

so $(m-4)(n-4)(r-4) = 1977$. Since $1977 = 3 \times 659$ and $659$ is prime, the
 divisors of $1977$ are $\{1, 3, 659, 1977\}$.

- **$m = 3$** ($m-4 = -1$): $(n-4)(r-4) = -1977$. Since $n \ge 3$, $n-4 \ge -1$;
 the only negative value is $n-4 = -1$ (i.e., $n=3$), giving $r-4 = 1977$, $r =
1981$. Solution: $\mathbf{(3, 3, 1981)}$.

- **$m = 4$**: $m-4 = 0$, product $= 0 \ne 1977$. No solution.

- **$m \ge 5$**: all three factors positive with $1 \le m-4 \le n-4 \le r-4$ and
 product $1977$. The ordered triples of positive factors are:
  - $(1, 1, 1977) \Rightarrow (m,n,r) = \mathbf{(5, 5, 1981)}$.
  - $(1, 3, 659) \Rightarrow (m,n,r) = \mathbf{(5, 7, 663)}$.

---

**Case 2: $m = 2$.** Then $a = 0$, so $k_0 = 0$, $k_1 = 2bc$, $k_2 = 4(b+c)$, gi
ving

$$k_0 + k_2 - k_1 = 4(b+c) - 2bc,$$

which is always **even**, while $1985$ is **odd**. No solution.

---

**Case 3: $m = 1$.** Every cube lies on both $m$-faces, so each has at least 2 r
ed faces: $k_0 = k_1 = 0$. Cubes with exactly 2 red faces are those on both $m$-
faces but on no $n$- or $r$-face, giving

$$k_2 = (n-2)(r-2) = 1985.$$

Since $1985 = 5 \times 397$ with $397$ prime, and $n \ge 1$:

- $n = 1$: $n-2 = -1 \Rightarrow r = -1983$ (invalid).
- $n = 2$: product $= 0$ (no).
- $n \ge 3$: ordered positive factor pairs of $1985$ are $(1, 1985)$ and $(5, 39
7)$:
  - $(n-2, r-2) = (1, 1985) \Rightarrow \mathbf{(1, 3, 1987)}$.
  - $(n-2, r-2) = (5, 397) \Rightarrow \mathbf{(1, 7, 399)}$.

---

**Verification of all five solutions:**

| $(m,n,r)$ | $k_0$ | $k_1$ | $k_2$ | $k_0+k_2-k_1$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $(1,3,1987)$ | $0$ | $0$ | $1\cdot1985=1985$ | $1985$ ✓ |
| $(1,7,399)$ | $0$ | $0$ | $5\cdot397=1985$ | $1985$ ✓ |
| $(3,3,1981)$ | $1979$ | $7918$ | $7924$ | $1985$ ✓ |
| $(5,5,1981)$ | $3\cdot3\cdot1979{=}17811$ | $2(9+5937+5937){=}23766$ | $4(1981

| $(5,7,663)$ | $3\cdot5\cdot661{=}9915$ | $2(3305+1983+15){=}10606$ | $4(669){=
}2676$ | $1985$ ✓ |

---

**Final computation.** The five valid triples and their dimension sums are:

| $(m,n,r)$ | $m+n+r$ |
|---|---|
| $(1, 3, 1987)$ | $1991$ |
| $(1, 7, 399)$ | $407$ |
