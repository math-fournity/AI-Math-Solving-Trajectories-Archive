## Solution

We compute the pullback $\varphi^*\omega$ where
$$\omega = z\,e^{xy}\,dx\wedge dy, \qquad \varphi(u,v) = \left(\frac{u}{v},\,\fr
ac{v}{u},\,uv\right).$$

Set $x = \tfrac{u}{v}$, $y = \tfrac{v}{u}$, $z = uv$.

**Step 1: Compute $dx$ and $dy$.**

$$dx = \frac{1}{v}\,du - \frac{u}{v^{2}}\,dv, \qquad dy = -\frac{v}{u^{2}}\,du +
 \frac{1}{u}\,dv.$$

**Step 2: Compute $dx\wedge dy$.**

The coefficient of $du\wedge dv$ is the $2\times 2$ minor
$$\frac{\partial x}{\partial u}\frac{\partial y}{\partial v} - \frac{\partial x}
{\partial v}\frac{\partial y}{\partial u} = \frac{1}{v}\cdot\frac{1}{u} - \left(
-\frac{u}{v^{2}}\right)\!\left(-\frac{v}{u^{2}}\right) = \frac{1}{uv} - \frac{uv
}{v^{2}u^{2}} = \frac{1}{uv} - \frac{1}{uv} = 0.$$

Hence
$$dx\wedge dy = 0.$$

**Step 3: Evaluate the scalar prefactor.**

$$xy = \frac{u}{v}\cdot\frac{v}{u} = 1 \;\Longrightarrow\; e^{xy} = e, \qquad z
= uv.$$

**Step 4: Assemble the pullback.**

By the pullback formula for a 2-form with scalar coefficient,
$$\varphi^{*}\omega = z\,e^{xy}\,(dx\wedge dy) = (uv)\cdot e \cdot 0 = 0.$$

**Remark.** The vanishing is not accidental: the $xy$-minor of the Jacobian of $
\varphi$ is identically zero (while the $xz$- and $yz$-minors are $2u/v$ and $-2
v/u$, generically nonzero), so the map has rank $2$ but the specific $dx\wedge d
y$ component pulls back to zero.

$$\boxed{\varphi^{*}\omega = 0}$$
