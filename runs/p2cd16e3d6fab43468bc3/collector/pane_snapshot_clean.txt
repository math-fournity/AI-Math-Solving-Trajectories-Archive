> **Lemma (Galois Descent for Submodules).** *Let $L/F$ be finite Galois with gr

*Proof of Lemma.* The forward direction is clear. For the converse, let $S \subs
eteq W$ be $\Gamma$-stable. Set $U = \{v \in V : 1 \otimes v \in S\}$, an $F$-su

\otimes v_i \in S$. By **Dedekind's lemma** (linear independence of distinct fie
