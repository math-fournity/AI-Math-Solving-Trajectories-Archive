## Proof

We show that $D_{KL}(\rho_0\|\rho_\infty) = D_0$ does **not** necessarily hold.
We establish (1) the general inequality $D_{KL}(\rho_0\|\rho_\infty) \leq D_0$ v
ia lower semicontinuity, and (2) a counterexample with strict inequality.

---

### Part 1: Lower semicontinuity gives $D_{KL}(\rho_0\|\rho_\infty) \leq D_0$

By the Donsker–Varadhan variational formula, for probability measures $\mu,\nu$:

$$D_{KL}(\mu\|\nu) = \sup_{\varphi \in C_b(\mathbb{R}^d)}\left\{\int \varphi\,d\
mu - \log\int e^{\varphi}\,d\nu\right\}.$$

Since $\|\rho_t - \rho_0\|_{TV} \to 0$ implies weak convergence $\rho_t \Rightar
row \rho_0$, for every bounded continuous $\varphi$:

$$\int \varphi\,d\rho_t \to \int \varphi\,d\rho_0.$$

Hence for each fixed $\varphi$:

$$\int \varphi\,d\rho_0 - \log\int e^{\varphi}\,d\rho_\infty = \lim_{t\to 0}\lef
t(\int \varphi\,d\rho_t - \log\int e^{\varphi}\,d\rho_\infty\right) \leq \liminf
_{t\to 0}\,D_{KL}(\rho_t\|\rho_\infty) = D_0.$$

Taking the supremum over $\varphi$:

$$\boxed{D_{KL}(\rho_0\|\rho_\infty) \leq D_0.}$$

---

### Part 2: Counterexample with strict inequality

Work on $\mathbb{R}$ ($d=1$). Set $\rho_\infty = \rho_0 = f := N(0,1)$, the stan
dard Gaussian density, so $D_{KL}(\rho_0\|\rho_\infty) = 0$.

Fix any $D_0 > 0$. For $t \in (0,1)$, define

$$\rho_t = (1-t)\,f + t\,g_t, \qquad g_t := N\!\left(\mu_t,\,1\right), \qquad \m
u_t := \sqrt{\frac{2D_0}{t}}.$$

For $t \geq 1$, set $\rho_t = f$.

**Verification of conditions (1)–(4):**

- **(1) Positivity:** $\rho_t$ is a mixture of positive Gaussian densities, so $
\rho_t(x)>0$ for all $x$. ✓
- **(2) Normalization:** $\int \rho_t = (1-t)+t = 1$. ✓
- **(3) Integrability:** All densities involved are Gaussians or mixtures of Gau
ssians. Since $|\log\rho_s|$ grows at most quadratically in $|x|$ and every $\rh
o_t$ has finite second moment, $\int \rho_t\,|\log\rho_s|\,dx < \infty$ for $s\i
n\{0,t,\infty\}$. ✓
- **(4) TV convergence:** $\|\rho_t - \rho_0\|_{TV} = t\,\|g_t - f\|_{TV} \leq 2
t \to 0$. ✓

**Computation of $D_{KL}(\rho_t\|\rho_\infty) \to D_0$ (condition (5)):**

Let $r_t(x) = g_t(x)/f(x) = e^{\mu_t x - \mu_t^2/2}$. Then

$$D_{KL}(\rho_t\|f) = \underbrace{(1-t)\,\mathbb{E}_f\!\big[\log\!\big((1-t)+t\,
r_t\big)\big]}_{\text{(I)}} + \underbrace{t\,\mathbb{E}_{g_t}\!\big[\log\!\big((
1-t)+t\,r_t\big)\big]}_{\text{(II)}}.$$

**Term (I) $\to 0$:** Write $\log((1-t)+tr_t) = \log(1-t) + \log\!\big(1+\frac{t
\,r_t}{1-t}\big)$. Since $\log(1+u)\leq u$ for $u\geq 0$ and $\mathbb{E}_f[r_t]=
1$ (likelihood ratio identity):

$$0 \leq \mathbb{E}_f\!\left[\log\!\left(1+\frac{t\,r_t}{1-t}\right)\right] \leq
 \frac{t}{1-t}\,\mathbb{E}_f[r_t] = \frac{t}{1-t} \;\longrightarrow\; 0.$$

Thus $(\mathrm{I}) = (1-t)\log(1-t) + O\!\left(\frac{t}{1-t}\right) \to 0$.

**Term (II) $\to D_0$:** Write

$$\log\!\big((1-t)+t\,r_t\big) = \log t + \log r_t + \log\!\left(1+\frac{1-t}{t\
,r_t}\right).$$

Under $g_t = N(\mu_t,1)$, the variable $\log r_t = \mu_t X - \mu_t^2/2$ (with $X
\sim N(\mu_t,1)$) has mean $\mu_t^2/2 = D_0/t$. Since $\log r_t$ has mean $D_0/t
 \to +\infty$, the ratio $(1-t)/(t\,r_t) \to 0$ in $g_t$-probability, and by dom
inated convergence $\mathbb{E}_{g_t}\!\left[\log\!\left(1+\frac{1-t}{t\,r_t}\rig
ht)\right]\to 0$. Therefore:

$$\text{(II)} = t\log t + t\cdot\frac{D_0}{t} + o(1) = t\log t + D_0 + o(1) \;\l
ongrightarrow\; D_0.$$

**Combining:** $D_{KL}(\rho_t\|\rho_\infty) \to 0 + D_0 = D_0$, confirming condi
tion (5).

---

### Conclusion

In this counterexample, all five conditions are satisfied, yet

$$D_{KL}(\rho_0\|\rho_\infty) = 0 \;<\; D_0.$$

Therefore, $D_{KL}(\rho_0\|\rho_\infty) = D_0$ does **not** hold in general. The
 KL divergence is only **lower semicontinuous** under total-variation convergenc
e (giving $D_{KL}(\rho_0\|\rho_\infty) \leq D_0$), and strict inequality can occ
ur because KL divergence is highly sensitive to the behavior of $\rho_t/\rho_\in
fty$ on sets where $\rho_\infty$ is small—even when those sets carry vanishing $
\rho_t$-mass (vanishing TV distance) but non-vanishing contribution to the entro
py integral.
