# IMO 2007 N2 — Proof

**Problem.** Fix integers $b > 0$ and $n \geq 0$. Suppose that for every positiv
e integer $k$ there exists an integer $a$ (depending on $k$) such that $k \mid b
 - a^n$. Prove that $b = A^n$ for some integer $A$.

---

**Case $n = 0$.** For any nonzero integer $a$ we have $a^0 = 1$, so the hypothes
is gives, for every $k \geq 1$, some $a$ with $k \mid b - 1$. Choosing $k > |b-1
|$ (possible unless $b = 1$) forces $b - 1 = 0$, i.e. $b = 1$. Then $b = 1 = A^0
$ for any integer $A$. $\checkmark$

**Case $n \geq 1$.** Write the prime factorization of $b$ as
$$b = \prod_{i=1}^{r} p_i^{e_i}, \qquad e_i = v_{p_i}(b) \geq 1.$$
We claim $n \mid e_i$ for every $i$.

Fix a prime $p = p_i$ dividing $b$ and set $e = v_p(b) = e_i$. Apply the hypothe
sis with
$$k = p^{\,e+1}.$$
There exists an integer $a$ such that
$$p^{\,e+1} \mid b - a^n, \qquad \text{i.e.} \quad v_p(b - a^n) \geq e + 1.$$

Recall the **ultrametric property** of the $p$-adic valuation: for any integers
$x, y$,
$$v_p(x - y) \geq \min\bigl(v_p(x),\, v_p(y)\bigr),$$
with **equality** whenever $v_p(x) \neq v_p(y)$.

Set $x = b$ (so $v_p(x) = e$) and $y = a^n$ (so $v_p(y) = n\,v_p(a)$).

- **If $v_p(a^n) \neq e$**, then equality holds in the ultrametric inequality:
$$v_p(b - a^n) = \min\bigl(e,\; v_p(a^n)\bigr) \leq e < e + 1,$$
contradicting $v_p(b - a^n) \geq e + 1$.

- **Therefore $v_p(a^n) = e$**, i.e.
$$n \cdot v_p(a) = e.$$

Since $v_p(a)$ is a non-negative integer, this gives $n \mid e$. $\square$

Applying this to every prime divisor $p_i$ of $b$, we obtain $n \mid e_i$ for al
l $i$. Define
$$A = \prod_{i=1}^{r} p_i^{\,e_i / n} \in \mathbb{Z}.$$
Then
$$A^n = \prod_{i=1}^{r} p_i^{\,e_i} = b.$$

If $b = 1$ (no prime factors), take $A = 1$ and $1^n = 1 = b$.

This completes the proof. $\blacksquare$
