# Solution

**Claim:** Yes, it is always possible.

## Proof

Let $X$ be a locally convex topological vector space (LCTVS), and let $A \subset
eq X$ be a non-empty, closed, convex cone with $A \neq X$.

**Step 1. $0 \in A$.** Since $A$ is a cone, for any $a \in A$ and $\lambda \geq
0$, $\lambda a \in A$. Taking $\lambda = 0$ gives $0 \in A$.

**Step 2. Pick a point outside $A$.** Since $A \neq X$, there exists $y \in X \s
etminus A$. As $A$ is closed, $y \notin \overline{A} = A$.

**Step 3. Apply the Hahn–Banach strict separation theorem.** In a locally convex
 TVS, if $C$ is a non-empty closed convex set and $K$ is a non-empty compact con
vex set with $C \cap K = \emptyset$, then there exists a continuous linear funct
ional $f$ on $X$ such that
$$\sup_{c \in C} f(c) < \inf_{k \in K} f(k).$$
Take $C = A$ and $K = \{y\}$ (a singleton is compact and convex). Since $y \noti
n A$, we have $A \cap \{y\} = \emptyset$. Thus there exists a continuous linear
functional $f$ with
$$\sup_{a \in A} f(a) < f(y). \tag{1}$$

**Step 4. $f$ is non-zero.** Since $0 \in A$, we have $f(0) = 0 \leq \sup_{a \in
 A} f(a) < f(y)$, so $f(y) > 0$, hence $f \neq 0$.

**Step 5. $f(a) \leq 0$ for all $a \in A$.** Let $a \in A$. Since $A$ is a cone,
 $\lambda a \in A$ for every $\lambda \geq 0$. By (1),
$$\lambda f(a) = f(\lambda a) \leq \sup_{a' \in A} f(a') < \infty \quad \text{fo
r all } \lambda \geq 0.$$
If $f(a) > 0$, then $\lambda f(a) \to +\infty$ as $\lambda \to \infty$, a contra
diction. Therefore $f(a) \leq 0$ for all $a \in A$.

**Step 6. Construct the desired functional.** Define $g := -f$. Then:
- $g$ is a continuous linear functional (scalar multiple of one).
- $g \neq 0$ since $f \neq 0$ (equivalently, $g(y) = -f(y) < 0$).
- For all $a \in A$: $g(a) = -f(a) \geq 0$.

Thus $g$ is a non-zero continuous linear functional on $X$ that is nonnegative o
n $A$. $\blacksquare$
