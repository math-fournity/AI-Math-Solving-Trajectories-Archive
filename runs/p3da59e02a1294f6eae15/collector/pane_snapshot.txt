## Proof

Let $H$ be a Hilbert space and $\mathfrak{F} = \bigoplus_{n=0}^{\infty} \mathfra
k{F}_n$ the fermionic Fock space, where $\mathfrak{F}_0 = \mathbb{C}\,\Omega$ (v
acuum) and $\mathfrak{F}_n = \bigwedge^n H$ for $n \geq 1$. The inner product on
 $\mathfrak{F}$ is
$$\|\Psi\|^2 = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \|\Psi_n\|^2, \qquad \Psi = (\Psi_0, \Psi_1,
\Psi_2, \dots).$$

The creation operator $c^*(f)$, for $f \in H$, acts as
$$c^*(f)\,\Omega = f, \qquad c^*(f)(\psi_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge \psi_n) = f \wed
ge \psi_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge \psi_n.$$

### Step 1. Norm on each sector

For $\psi = \psi_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge \psi_n \in \mathfrak{F}_n$, the norm of
the wedge product is given by the Gram determinant:
$$\|c^*(f)\psi\|^2 = \|f \wedge \psi_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge \psi_n\|^2 = \det G(
f, \psi_1, \dots, \psi_n),$$
where $G_{ij} = \langle v_i, v_j\rangle$ for $v = (f, \psi_1, \dots, \psi_n)$.

Expanding the Gram determinant along the first row/column and using the standard
 identity, one obtains
$$\|f \wedge \psi_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge \psi_n\|^2 = \|f\|^2 \|\psi_1 \wedge \c
dots \wedge \psi_n\|^2 - \sum_{i,j} \langle f, \psi_i\rangle \overline{\langle f
, \psi_j\rangle}\, M_{ij},$$
where $M_{ij}$ are cofactors of the Gram matrix of $(\psi_1,\dots,\psi_n)$. In p
articular, the second term is a non-negative quantity subtracted (it is $\|P f\|
^2 \|\psi\|^2$ where $P$ is the projection onto $\operatorname{span}\{\psi_1,\do
ts,\psi_n\}$, up to the appropriate Gram matrix inverse). Concretely, using the
orthogonal decomposition $f = f^\perp + f^\parallel$ with $f^\parallel \in \oper
atorname{span}\{\psi_i\}$ and $f^\perp \perp \operatorname{span}\{\psi_i\}$:
$$f \wedge \psi_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge \psi_n = f^\perp \wedge \psi_1 \wedge \cd
ots \wedge \psi_n,$$
and since $f^\perp \perp \psi_i$ for all $i$, the Gram matrix is block-diagonal,
 giving
$$\|c^*(f)\psi\|^2 = \|f^\perp\|^2 \|\psi_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge \psi_n\|^2 \leq
 \|f\|^2 \|\psi\|^2.$$

Hence $\|c^*(f)|_{\mathfrak{F}_n}\| \leq \|f\|$ for every $n \geq 0$.

**Equality is attained:** Take $f$ orthogonal to $\operatorname{span}\{\psi_1,\d
ots,\psi_n\}$ with $\|f\|=1$ and $\|\psi\|=1$; then $\|c^*(f)\psi\| = 1 = \|f\|\
|\psi\|$. In particular, on the vacuum sector ($n=0$), $c^*(f)\Omega = f$ and $\
|c^*(f)\Omega\| = \|f\| = \|f\|\cdot\|\Omega\|$, so equality is always attained.
 Thus
$$\|c^*(f)|_{\mathfrak{F}_n \to \mathfrak{F}_{n+1}}\| = \|f\| \quad \text{for al
l } n \geq 0.$$

### Step 2. Norm on the full Fock space

For a general $\Psi = (\Psi_0, \Psi_1, \Psi_2, \dots) \in \mathfrak{F}$, the cre
ation operator maps the $n$-sector to the $(n+1)$-sector:
$$(c^*(f)\Psi)_{n+1} = c^*(f)\Psi_n, \qquad (c^*(f)\Psi)_0 = 0.$$

Therefore
$$\|c^*(f)\Psi\|^2 = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \|c^*(f)\Psi_n\|^2 \leq \sum_{n=0}^{\in
fty} \|f\|^2 \|\Psi_n\|^2 = \|f\|^2 \|\Psi\|^2.$$

This shows $c^*(f)$ is **bounded** with $\|c^*(f)\| \leq \|f\|$.

### Step 3. Attaining the bound

Take $\Psi = \Omega = (1, 0, 0, \dots)$, the vacuum vector with $\|\Omega\| = 1$
. Then
$$c^*(f)\Omega = f, \qquad \|c^*(f)\Omega\| = \|f\| = \|f\|\cdot\|\Omega\|.$$

Hence $\|c^*(f)\| \geq \|f\|$, and combined with the upper bound:

$$\boxed{\|c^*(f)\| = \|f\|.}$$

### Conclusion

The fermionic creation operator $c^*(f)$ is a **bounded** linear operator on $\m
athfrak{F}$ for every $f \in H$, and its operator norm is
$$\|c^*(f)\| = \|f\|_H.$$
