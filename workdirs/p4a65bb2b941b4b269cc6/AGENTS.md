# Problem

In the competitive world of artisanal coffee roasting, there are 6 unique origin labels that a collector can find, one inside each bag of "Mystery Roast" beans. Every bag contains exactly one label, and each of the 6 origins is distributed with equal probability.

A collector named Julian decides to start buying bags of the Mystery Roast. He continues to purchase and open bags one by one until he has collected at least one of every single origin label, at which point he immediately stops his project.

Another collector, Silas, has already been collecting for some time. Silas currently possesses five of the distinct origin labels and is missing exactly one specific label to complete his set.

The probability that Julian, upon completing his own set, has ended up with at least two copies of the specific label that Silas is currently missing can be expressed as a fraction $\frac{m}{n}$ in lowest terms, where $m$ and $n$ are coprime positive integers.

Find the value of $m + n$.
