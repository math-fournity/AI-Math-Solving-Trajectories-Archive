## Proof

**Interpretation.** $\mathcal{F}_p^k$ denotes the finite field $\mathbb{F}_{p^k}
$. The trace $\operatorname{Tr}:\mathbb{F}_{p^k}\to\mathbb{F}_p$ and norm $N:\ma
thbb{F}_{p^k}\to\mathbb{F}_p$ are the standard field-theoretic maps
$$\operatorname{Tr}(y)=y+y^p+\cdots+y^{p^{k-1}},\qquad N(x)=x\cdot x^p\cdots x^{
p^{k-1}}=x^{(p^k-1)/(p-1)}.$$
We count pairs $(x,y)\in\mathbb{F}_{p^k}^2$ with $\operatorname{Tr}(y)=N(x)$.

---

**Step 1: Fiber sizes of the trace.**

The trace is a surjective $\mathbb{F}_p$-linear map $\mathbb{F}_{p^k}\to\mathbb{
F}_p$ (surjectivity holds since $\operatorname{Tr}(1)=k\cdot 1\neq 0$ when $\gcd
(k,p)=1$, and in general one checks surjectivity via the non-degeneracy of the t
race pairing). Being $\mathbb{F}_p$-linear and surjective, every fiber $\operato
rname{Tr}^{-1}(a)$ is a coset of the kernel, hence has size $|\ker\operatorname{
Tr}|=p^{k-1}$. So
$$T_a:=|\{y:\operatorname{Tr}(y)=a\}|=p^{k-1}\quad\text{for every }a\in\mathbb{F
}_p.$$

---

**Step 2: Fiber sizes of the norm.**

On $\mathbb{F}_{p^k}^*$, the norm is the group homomorphism $x\mapsto x^{(p^k-1)
/(p-1)}$ from the cyclic group $\mathbb{F}_{p^k}^*$ (order $p^k-1$) onto $\mathb
b{F}_p^*$ (order $p-1$). The image is generated by $g^{(p^k-1)/(p-1)}$ for a gen
erator $g$ of $\mathbb{F}_{p^k}^*$, and has order
$$\frac{p^k-1}{\gcd\!\left(\frac{p^k-1}{p-1},\,p^k-1\right)}=\frac{p^k-1}{(p^k-1
)/(p-1)}=p-1,$$
so $N:\mathbb{F}_{p^k}^*\to\mathbb{F}_p^*$ is surjective, and each nonzero value
 has
$$\frac{p^k-1}{p-1}$$
preimages in $\mathbb{F}_{p^k}^*$. Also $N(x)=0\iff x=0$, so $N_0=1$. Summarizin
g:
$$N_a:=|\{x:N(x)=a\}|=\begin{cases}1 & a=0,\\[2pt]\dfrac{p^k-1}{p-1} & a\in\math
bb{F}_p^*.\end{cases}$$

---

**Step 3: Count the pairs.**

Partitioning by the common value $a=N(x)=\operatorname{Tr}(y)\in\mathbb{F}_p$:
$$\#\{(x,y):\operatorname{Tr}(y)=N(x)\}=\sum_{a\in\mathbb{F}_p}N_a\cdot T_a=N_0\
,T_0+\sum_{a\in\mathbb{F}_p^*}N_a\,T_a.$$
Substituting:
$$=1\cdot p^{k-1}+(p-1)\cdot\frac{p^k-1}{p-1}\cdot p^{k-1}=p^{k-1}+(p^k-1)\,p^{k
-1}=p^{k-1}\bigl(1+p^k-1\bigr)=p^{2k-1}.$$

---

**Verification.** For $p=2,k=1$: $\mathbb{F}_2$, trace=norm=identity, pairs with
 $y=x$: $2=p^{1}$. ✓
For $p=2,k=2$: $\mathbb{F}_4$, $\operatorname{Tr}$ fibers have size $2$; $N_0=1$
, $N_1=3$; count $=1\cdot2+3\cdot2=8=2^3$. ✓

---

**Answer.** The number of pairs $(x,y)\in\mathbb{F}_{p^k}^2$ with $\operatorname
{Tr}(y)=N(x)$ is

$$\boxed{p^{2k-1}}.$$
