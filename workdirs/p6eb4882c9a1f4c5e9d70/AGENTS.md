# Problem

A high-tech manufacturing firm operates a network of 100 distinct production hubs, denoted as $H_1, H_2, \dots, H_{100}$. Each hub is responsible for a specific, finite bundle of specialized hardware components. The network is organized under a "Redundancy Integration" protocol: for any two hubs in the network, the combined inventory of their components is exactly identical to the inventory held by another existing hub within the same set of 100.

An audit of the network reveals that the smallest inventory held by any single hub consists of exactly 5 unique components. 

The firm’s Chief Logistics Officer wants to identify the most critical component in the entire supply chain. Specifically, they are looking for a component that is stocked by a large number of different hubs. Let $N$ be the largest integer such that, regardless of how the 100 inventories are structured under the protocol, there is guaranteed to be at least one specific component that is present in at least $N$ of the hubs.

Find $N$.
