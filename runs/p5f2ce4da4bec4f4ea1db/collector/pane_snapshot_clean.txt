## Proof

We work in the **Poincaré ball model** of hyperbolic $n$-space: $D^n = \{p \in \
mathbb{R}^n : |p|<1\}$ equipped with the Riemannian metric of constant curvature
 $-1$,

$$ds^2 = \frac{4\,|dp|^2}{(1-|p|^2)^2}.$$

**Step 1. Geodesics through the origin are diameters.**

Geodesics in the Poincaré ball are arcs of circles meeting the boundary sphere $
S^{n-1}$ orthogonally (including straight diameters as the degenerate case). A g
eodesic passing through the origin $0$ must be invariant under the isometry $p \
mapsto -p$ (a rotation by $\pi$ fixing $0$), so it must be a straight line throu
gh the origin. Hence the geodesic through $0$ pointing towards $x \in S^{n-1}$ l
ies on the ray $\{r x : r \ge 0\}$.

Write $\gamma(t) = r(t)\,x$ with $r(0)=0$.

**Step 2. Arc-length parametrisation.**

Along this diameter, $|d\gamma| = |dr|$ (since $|x|=1$), so the hyperbolic line
element is

$$ds = \frac{2\,dr}{1-r^2}.$$

The hyperbolic distance from $0$ to $r x$ is

$$s(r) = \int_0^r \frac{2\,du}{1-u^2} = 2\operatorname{arctanh}(r) = \log\frac{1
+r}{1-r}.$$

Solving for $r$:

$$e^s = \frac{1+r}{1-r} \;\Longrightarrow\; r = \frac{e^s-1}{e^s+1} = \tanh\!\le
ft(\frac{s}{2}\right).$$

Setting $s=t$ (unit-speed arc-length parameter) gives

$$r(t) = \tanh\!\left(\frac{t}{2}\right).$$

**Step 3. Verification of the initial conditions.**

- $\gamma(0) = \tanh(0)\,x = 0$. ✓
- $\gamma'(t) = \tfrac{1}{2}\operatorname{sech}^2\!\left(\tfrac{t}{2}\right) x$,
 so $\gamma'(0) = \tfrac{1}{2}x$, which points towards $x$. ✓
- Unit speed at $t=0$: the conformal factor at the origin is $\frac{4}{(1-0)^2}=
4$, so
  $$\|\gamma'(0)\|_{\text{hyp}} = 2\cdot\|\gamma'(0)\|_{\text{Euc}} = 2\cdot\tfr
ac{1}{2} = 1. \;✓$$
- Constant unit speed: $\|\gamma'(t)\|_{\text{hyp}} = \frac{2}{1-\tanh^2(t/2)}\c
dot\tfrac{1}{2}\operatorname{sech}^2(t/2) = \operatorname{sech}^2(t/2)\cdot\cosh
^2(t/2) = 1$. ✓

**Conclusion.**

The geodesic in $D^n$ (Poincaré ball, curvature $-1$) passing through the origin
 at $t=0$ and pointing towards $x\in S^{n-1}$ at unit speed is

$$\boxed{\;\gamma(t) \;=\; \tanh\!\left(\frac{t}{2}\right)\,x,\qquad t\in\mathbb
{R}.\;}$$
