## Solution

**Setup.** Let $Y = \mathbb{P}^2 \times \mathbb{P}^1$ with cohomology ring
$$H^*(Y) = \mathbb{Z}[h_1, h_2]/(h_1^3,\, h_2^2),$$
where $h_1 = c_1(\mathcal{O}(1,0))$ (pullback of the hyperplane class from $\mat
hbb{P}^2$) and $h_2 = c_1(\mathcal{O}(0,1))$ (pullback from $\mathbb{P}^1$). The
 top class is $h_1^2 h_2$ with $\int_Y h_1^2 h_2 = 1$.

**Class of $X$.** Since $f \in H^0(\mathcal{O}(1,2))$ and $X = V_+(f)$ is a smoo
th divisor,
$$[X] = c_1(\mathcal{O}(1,2)) = h_1 + 2h_2 \in H^2(Y).$$

**Intersection via the projection formula.** For any divisor $D$ on $Y$, the sel
f-intersection of $\iota^*D$ on the surface $X$ is computed in the ambient space
 as
$$\iota^*D \cdot \iota^*D \;=\; \int_X (\iota^*c_1(D))^2 \;=\; \int_Y c_1(D)^2 \
wedge [X],$$
using $\iota_*\iota^*\alpha = \alpha \wedge [X]$.

**Computation.** With $D = \mathcal{O}(2,3)$, we have $c_1(D) = 2h_1 + 3h_2$. Th
en:
$$(2h_1 + 3h_2)^2 = 4h_1^2 + 12\,h_1 h_2 + 9\,h_2^2 = 4h_1^2 + 12\,h_1 h_2 \quad
 (\text{since } h_2^2 = 0).$$

Multiplying by $[X] = h_1 + 2h_2$:
$$(4h_1^2 + 12\,h_1 h_2)(h_1 + 2h_2) = 4h_1^3 + 8\,h_1^2 h_2 + 12\,h_1^2 h_2 + 2
4\,h_1 h_2^2.$$

Since $h_1^3 = 0$ and $h_2^2 = 0$, this reduces to
$$20\,h_1^2 h_2.$$

Integrating over $Y$:
$$\int_Y 20\,h_1^2 h_2 = 20 \cdot \int_{\mathbb{P}^2} h_1^2 \cdot \int_{\mathbb{
P}^1} h_2 = 20 \cdot 1 \cdot 1 = 20.$$

**Answer.**

$$\iota^*\mathcal{O}(2,3)\cdot\iota^*\mathcal{O}(2,3) = \boxed{20}$$
