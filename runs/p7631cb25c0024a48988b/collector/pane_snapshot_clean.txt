## Solution

By the **Artin–Wedderburn theorem**, every finite-dimensional semisimple $\mathb
b{C}$-algebra is isomorphic to a finite direct sum of matrix algebras over divis
ion rings. Since $\mathbb{C}$ is algebraically closed, the only finite-dimension
al division algebra over $\mathbb{C}$ is $\mathbb{C}$ itself. Therefore every se
misimple $\mathbb{C}$-algebra of dimension 8 is of the form

$$A \cong \bigoplus_{i=1}^{k} M_{n_i}(\mathbb{C}), \qquad n_i \geq 1,$$

with

$$\dim_{\mathbb{C}} A = \sum_{i=1}^{k} n_i^2 = 8.$$

Two such algebras are isomorphic if and only if the multisets $\{n_i\}$ coincide
 (direct sum is commutative and associative up to isomorphism, and the summands
$M_{n_i}(\mathbb{C})$ are simple and pairwise non-isomorphic for distinct $n_i$)
. Thus the problem reduces to counting the **partitions of 8 into squares of pos
itive integers**.

The available square parts are $1, 4, 9, 16, \dots$. Since $9 > 8$, only $1$ and
 $4$ can appear. We list all partitions of $8$ using parts from $\{1, 4\}$:

| Partition of 8 | Corresponding algebra |
|---|---|
| $4 + 4$ | $M_2(\mathbb{C}) \oplus M_2(\mathbb{C})$ |
| $4 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1$ | $M_2(\mathbb{C}) \oplus \mathbb{C}^{\oplus 4}$ |
| $1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1$ | $\mathbb{C}^{\oplus 8}$ |

These three algebras are pairwise non-isomorphic: they have different numbers of
 simple summands (2, 5, and 8 respectively), which is an isomorphism invariant.

**Verification of dimensions:**
- $\dim(M_2(\mathbb{C}) \oplus M_2(\mathbb{C})) = 4 + 4 = 8$ ✓
- $\dim(M_2(\mathbb{C}) \oplus \mathbb{C}^4) = 4 + 4 = 8$ ✓
- $\dim(\mathbb{C}^8) = 8$ ✓

No other partitions exist: using a part of $9$ or larger exceeds $8$, and the th
ree partitions above exhaust all ways to write $8 = 4a + 1\cdot b$ with $a, b \g
eq 0$ and $a + b \geq 1$ (namely $(a,b) = (2,0), (1,4), (0,8)$).

### Answer

The number of non-isomorphic semisimple $\mathbb{C}$-algebras of dimension 8 is
$\boxed{3}$.
