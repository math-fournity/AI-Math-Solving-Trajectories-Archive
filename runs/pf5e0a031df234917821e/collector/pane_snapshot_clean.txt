## Proof

Let $E$ be a Banach space and $(\xi_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}} \subset E^*$ with $\xi_n
 \to \xi$ in the weak-* topology, i.e., $\xi_n(x) \to \xi(x)$ for every $x \in E
$.

### Part 1: The sequence $(\|\xi_n\|_{E^*})$ is bounded.

For each fixed $x \in E$, the scalar sequence $(\xi_n(x))_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ conv
erges to $\xi(x)$, hence is bounded:

$$\sup_{n\in\mathbb{N}} |\xi_n(x)| < \infty \quad \text{for every } x \in E.$$

Thus the family $\{\xi_n : n \in \mathbb{N}\} \subset E^*$ is **pointwise bounde
d** on $E$. Since $E$ is a Banach space (in particular, complete), the **Uniform
 Boundedness Principle** (Banach–Steinhaus theorem) applies, yielding

$$\sup_{n\in\mathbb{N}} \|\xi_n\|_{E^*} < \infty.$$

So $(\|\xi_n\|_{E^*})$ is a bounded sequence.

### Part 2: $\|\xi\|_{E^*} \leq \liminf_{n\to\infty} \|\xi_n\|_{E^*}$.

Fix any $x \in E$ with $\|x\| \leq 1$. Since $\xi_n(x) \to \xi(x)$, we have $|\x
i_n(x)| \to |\xi(x)|$, and therefore

$$|\xi(x)| = \lim_{n\to\infty} |\xi_n(x)| = \liminf_{n\to\infty} |\xi_n(x)|.$$

By the definition of the dual norm, $|\xi_n(x)| \leq \|\xi_n\|_{E^*}\,\|x\|$ for
 every $n$. Taking $\liminf$ on both sides (and using that $\|x\| \geq 0$ is a c
onstant, so it factors out of $\liminf$):

$$|\xi(x)| = \liminf_{n\to\infty} |\xi_n(x)| \leq \liminf_{n\to\infty} \bigl(\|\
xi_n\|_{E^*}\,\|x\|\bigr) = \|x\| \cdot \liminf_{n\to\infty} \|\xi_n\|_{E^*}.$$

Since $\|x\| \leq 1$, this gives

$$|\xi(x)| \leq \liminf_{n\to\infty} \|\xi_n\|_{E^*}.$$

This bound holds for every $x$ in the closed unit ball of $E$. Taking the suprem
um over all such $x$:

$$\|\xi\|_{E^*} = \sup_{\|x\|\leq 1} |\xi(x)| \leq \liminf_{n\to\infty} \|\xi_n\
|_{E^*}.$$

### Conclusion

The weak-* convergence $\xi_n \xrightarrow{w^*} \xi$ in $E^*$ (with $E$ a Banach
 space) implies:

1. **(Boundedness)** The sequence of norms $(\|\xi_n\|_{E^*})$ is bounded: $\sup
_n \|\xi_n\|_{E^*} < \infty$, by the Uniform Boundedness Principle.

2. **(Weak-* lower semicontinuity of the norm)** The limit functional satisfies
$$\|\xi\|_{E^*} \leq \liminf_{n\to\infty} \|\xi_n\|_{E^*}.$$

That is, the norm $\|\cdot\|_{E^*}$ is weak-* lower semicontinuous on $E^*$.
