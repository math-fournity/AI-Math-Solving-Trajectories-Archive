## Solution

**Problem.** The least positive solution of $x^2 - 61y^2 = 1$ is $(x_1, y_1) = (
1766319049,\, 226153980)$. Find the least positive solution other than $(x_1, y_
1)$.

---

### Key Fact: Structure of Pell Equation Solutions

For the Pell equation $x^2 - Dy^2 = 1$ with fundamental (least positive) solutio
n $(x_1, y_1)$, **all** positive solutions are given by

$$x_n + y_n\sqrt{D} = (x_1 + y_1\sqrt{D})^n, \qquad n = 1, 2, 3, \ldots$$

This is because the set of solutions forms a cyclic group under the operation $(
x,y)\mapsto (x',y')$ defined by $(x+y\sqrt{D})(x_1+y_1\sqrt{D}) = x'+y'\sqrt{D}$
, generated by the fundamental solution. The solutions are strictly increasing i
n both $x$ and $y$, so the **second least** positive solution corresponds to $n=
2$.

### Computing the Second Solution

Setting $n=2$:

$$(x_1 + y_1\sqrt{61})^2 = x_1^2 + 61y_1^2 + 2x_1 y_1\sqrt{61}$$

so

$$x_2 = x_1^2 + 61y_1^2, \qquad y_2 = 2x_1 y_1.$$

Since $x_1^2 - 61y_1^2 = 1$, we have $61y_1^2 = x_1^2 - 1$, giving the simpler f
ormula:

$$x_2 = 2x_1^2 - 1.$$

### Verification of the Fundamental Solution

First, let us verify $x_1^2 - 61y_1^2 = 1$.

**Computing $x_1^2 = 1766319049^2$:**

Using $1766319049 = 1700000000 + 66319049$:

- $1700000000^2 = 2\,890\,000\,000\,000\,000\,000$
- $2 \cdot 1700000000 \cdot 66319049 = 225\,484\,766\,600\,000\,000$
- $66319049^2 = 4\,398\,216\,260\,264\,401$

$$x_1^2 = 3\,119\,882\,982\,860\,264\,401$$

**Computing $y_1^2 = 226153980^2$:**

Using $226153980 = 226000000 + 153980$:

- $226000000^2 = 51\,076\,000\,000\,000\,000$
- $2 \cdot 226000000 \cdot 153980 = 69\,598\,960\,000\,000$
- $153980^2 = 23\,709\,840\,400$

$$y_1^2 = 51\,145\,622\,669\,840\,400$$

$$61\,y_1^2 = 3\,119\,882\,982\,860\,264\,400$$

$$x_1^2 - 61\,y_1^2 = 3\,119\,882\,982\,860\,264\,401 - 3\,119\,882\,982\,860\,2
64\,400 = 1. \quad\checkmark$$

### Computing $x_2$ and $y_2$

**$x_2 = 2x_1^2 - 1$:**

$$x_2 = 2 \times 3\,119\,882\,982\,860\,264\,401 - 1 = 6\,239\,765\,965\,720\,52
8\,802 - 1$$

$$\boxed{x_2 = 6\,239\,765\,965\,720\,528\,801}$$

**$y_2 = 2x_1 y_1$:**

Computing $x_1 \cdot y_1 = 1766319049 \times 226153980$:

$$1766319049 \times 226153980 = 399\,460\,082\,881\,177\,020$$

$$y_2 = 2 \times 399\,460\,082\,881\,177\,020$$

$$\boxed{y_2 = 798\,920\,165\,762\,354\,040}$$

### Verification

By construction, $(x_2 + y_2\sqrt{61}) = (x_1 + y_1\sqrt{61})^2$, so:

$$x_2^2 - 61\,y_2^2 = N(x_1 + y_1\sqrt{61})^2 = 1^2 = 1. \quad\checkmark$$

Both $x_2 > x_1 > 0$ and $y_2 > y_1 > 0$, and since the solutions are ordered by
 $n$, this is indeed the **least** positive solution after $(x_1, y_1)$.

### Answer

The least positive solution other than $(x_1, y_1)$ is:

$$x_2 = 6\,239\,765\,965\,720\,528\,801, \qquad y_2 = 798\,920\,165\,762\,354\,0
40.$$
