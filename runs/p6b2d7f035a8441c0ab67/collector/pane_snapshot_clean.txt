**Definitions and setup.** Let $A$ be a set and $X = A^{2}$. A *closure system*
on $X$ is a family $\mathcal{C}\subseteq\mathcal{P}(X)$ closed under arbitrary i
ntersections (so $X\in\mathcal{C}$). Its closure operator $\mathrm{cl}:\mathcal{
P}(X)\to\mathcal{C}$ sends $S$ to $\bigcap\{C\in\mathcal{C}:S\subseteq C\}$. The
 closure system $\mathcal{C}$ is **algebraic** iff it is closed under directed u
nions, equivalently iff $\mathrm{cl}$ preserves directed unions:
$$\mathrm{cl}\Bigl(\bigcup_{i} S_{i}\Bigr)=\bigcup_{i}\mathrm{cl}(S_{i}) \qquad\
text{for every directed family } \{S_{i}\}_{i\in I}.$$
(The equivalence holds because if $\mathcal{C}$ is closed under directed unions,
 then $\bigcup_i\mathrm{cl}(S_i)$ is closed and contains $\bigcup S_i$, giving $
\subseteq$; the reverse inclusion is monotonicity. Conversely, taking $S_i=C_i\i
n\mathcal{C}$ gives $\mathrm{cl}(\bigcup C_i)=\bigcup C_i\in\mathcal{C}$.)

For a relation $R\subseteq A^{2}$ write $R^{\circ n}$ for the $n$-fold relationa

^{\circ n}$ (transitive closure), and $R^{*}=\bigcup_{n\ge 0}R^{\circ n}=\Delta_
{A}\cup R^{+}$ (reflexive–transitive closure). Let $R^{-1}=\{(b,a):(a,b)\in R\}$

**Key lemma.** *If $\{R_{i}\}_{i\in I}$ is a directed family of relations on $A$
 (directed under $\subseteq$), then for every fixed $n\ge 0$,
$$(\bigcup_{i}R_{i})^{\circ n}=\bigcup_{i}R_{i}^{\circ n}.$$*

*Proof of lemma.* The inclusion $\supseteq$ is immediate. For $\subseteq$: take
$(a_{0},a_{n})\in(\bigcup_i R_i)^{\circ n}$, so there are $a_{1},\dots,a_{n-1}$
with $(a_{k},a_{k+1})\in\bigcup_i R_i$ for each $k$. Pick $i_{k}$ with $(a_{k},a
_{k+1})\in R_{i_{k}}$. Since the family is directed, there is $j\in I$ with $i_{
0},\dots,i_{n-1}\le j$, hence every edge lies in $R_{j}$, so $(a_{0},a_{n})\in R
