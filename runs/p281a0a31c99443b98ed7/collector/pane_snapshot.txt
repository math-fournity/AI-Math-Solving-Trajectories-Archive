## Analysis

This is **Erdős Problem 1175**, a genuinely open research problem in infinite gr
aph theory / combinatorial set theory.

**The conjecture (open):** For every uncountable cardinal κ, does there exist a
cardinal λ such that every graph with chromatic number λ contains a triangle-fre
e subgraph with chromatic number κ?

**What is known:**
- Shelah proved that a **negative** answer is **consistent** with ZFC in the spe
cial case κ = λ = ℵ₁. Specifically, it is consistent that there exists a graph G
 with chromatic number ℵ₁ such that every triangle-free subgraph of G has chroma
tic number strictly less than ℵ₁. This is recorded in the variant `erdos_1175.va
riants.shelah_consistency` (marked `solved`).
- The main statement `erdos_1175` is marked `@[category research open]` — it is
not known to be provable or refutable in ZFC.
