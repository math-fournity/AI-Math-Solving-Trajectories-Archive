# Problem

In a futuristic high-security facility, 100 containment pods are arranged in a perfect ring, numbered sequentially from 1 to 100. A single digital data packet is hidden within one of these pods. 

A technician can perform a scan in discrete "cycles." In each cycle, the technician selects any four pods to inspect simultaneously to see if the packet is inside. Once the inspection is complete, the pods are sealed back up. Immediately after every scan cycle, the data packet is programmed to move from its current pod to one of the two strictly adjacent pods (for example, if it was in pod 50, it must move to either pod 49 or pod 51).

The technician needs to develop a protocol that guarantees the packet will be located, regardless of its starting position or the direction it chooses to move. What is the minimum number of scan cycles required to definitely find the data packet?
