# Problem

In a specialized textile factory, a square piece of high-tech fabric, identified by its corners $A, B, C,$ and $D$ in clockwise order, is laid flat on a cutting table. A robotic arm performs a precise "fold-and-press" maneuver: it lifts corner $B$ and pulls it across the fabric until it perfectly aligns with the exact midpoint of the opposite edge, $CD$. This action creates a straight, sharp crease across the fabric.

As the fabric is pressed flat, the crease intersects the vertical edge $BC$ at a specific point, splitting that edge into two distinct segments.
