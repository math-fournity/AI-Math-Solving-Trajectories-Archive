1.  **The Volume-Surface Balance:** The total volume of the station (in cubic meters) plus a constant structural offset of 9 must exactly equal the sum of the areas of the three primary floor and wall sections: the base ($ab$), the side profile ($bc$), and the front profile ($ca$).

How many distinct sets of ordered dimensions $(a, b, c)$ exist that satisfy both of these engineering requirements?
