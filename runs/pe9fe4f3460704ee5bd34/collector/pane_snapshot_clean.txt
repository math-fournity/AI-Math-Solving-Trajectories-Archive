### Step 1. Rewrite the integral as an expectation

Since $f$ is the joint density of $(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n)$, the integral equals
\[
L_n := \mathbb{E}\!\left[g\!\left(\frac{\xi_1+\cdots+\xi_n}{n}\right)\right] = \
mathbb{E}\bigl[g(\bar X_n)\bigr],
\qquad \bar X_n:=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n \xi_i.
\]

### Step 2. Law of large numbers

Each $\xi_i$ has $\mathbb{E}[\xi_i]=1<\infty$. By the (strong) law of large numb
ers,
\[
\bar X_n \xrightarrow{\text{a.s.}} 1 \quad\text{as } n\to\infty,
\]
hence also $\bar X_n \xrightarrow{p} 1$.

### Step 3. Continuity of $g$ gives convergence in probability

Since $g$ is continuous, the continuous mapping theorem yields
\[
g(\bar X_n) \xrightarrow{p} g(1).
