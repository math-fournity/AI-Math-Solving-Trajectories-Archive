## Solution

The canister, once released, is a projectile with:
- initial horizontal velocity $v_x = 100 \, \text{km/h}$ (the airplane's speed),
- initial vertical velocity $v_{y0} = 0$ (released horizontally),
- height $h = 500 \, \text{m}$,
- acceleration $g = 10 \, \text{m/s}^2$ downward.

**Vertical motion determines the time of flight.** Since the canister is release
d horizontally, its vertical motion is free fall from rest:

$$h = \frac{1}{2} g t^2$$

Solving for $t$:

$$500 = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 10 \cdot t^2 = 5 t^2$$

$$t^2 = 100 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad t = 10 \, \text{s}$$

**Horizontal motion is uniform** (no air resistance, no horizontal acceleration)
. The horizontal distance traveled during the fall is

$$d = v_x \cdot t$$

Convert the speed to m/s:

$$v_x = 100 \, \text{km/h} = \frac{100 \times 1000 \, \text{m}}{3600 \, \text{s}
} = \frac{1000}{36} \, \text{m/s} = \frac{250}{9} \, \text{m/s}$$

Therefore:

$$d = \frac{250}{9} \times 10 = \frac{2500}{9} \approx 277.78 \, \text{m}$$

00}{9} \approx 277.78 \, \text{m}$ from the shipwrecked people.
