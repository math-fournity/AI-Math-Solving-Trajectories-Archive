Let $S = \{X \in \mathcal{M}_2(\mathbb{C}) : X^{2023} = X^{2022}\}$. We seek the
 smallest positive integer $k$ such that $X^k = X^{k-1}$ for every $X \in S$.

**Step 1: Characterize the minimal polynomials.**

The condition $X^{2023} = X^{2022}$ is equivalent to $X^{2022}(X - I) = 0$, so t
he minimal polynomial $m_X(t)$ divides
$$t^{2022}(t-1).$$
Since $X$ is a $2 \times 2$ matrix over $\mathbb{C}$, its minimal polynomial has
 degree at most $2$. The divisors of $t^{2022}(t-1)$ of degree $\leq 2$ are:
$$t,\quad t-1,\quad t^2,\quad t(t-1).$$
(Note: $(t-1)^2$ does not divide $t^{2022}(t-1)$, and $t^2(t-1)$ has degree $3 >
 2$.)

**Step 2: Translate $X^k = X^{k-1}$ into a condition on the minimal polynomial.*
*

$X^k = X^{k-1}$ iff $X^{k-1}(X - I) = 0$ iff $m_X(t)$ divides $t^{k-1}(t-1)$.

**Step 3: Check each possible minimal polynomial.**

- **$m_X(t) = t$** (i.e., $X = 0$): $t \mid t^{k-1}(t-1)$ for all $k \geq 1$. ✓
for all $k$.
- **$m_X(t) = t - 1$** (i.e., $X = I$): $t-1 \mid t^{k-1}(t-1)$ for all $k \geq
1$. ✓ for all $k$.
- **$m_X(t) = t(t-1)$** (idempotent, $X^2 = X$): need $t(t-1) \mid t^{k-1}(t-1)$
, which requires $k-1 \geq 1$, i.e., $k \geq 2$. ✓ for $k \geq 2$.
- **$m_X(t) = t^2$** (nilpotent with $X^2 = 0$, $X \neq 0$): need $t^2 \mid t^{k
-1}(t-1)$, which requires $k-1 \geq 2$, i.e., $k \geq 3$. ✓ for $k \geq 3$; **fa
ils for $k = 2$**.

**Step 4: Determine the smallest $k$.**

The binding constraint comes from $m_X(t) = t^2$. For $k = 2$, taking any nonzer
o nilpotent $2 \times 2$ matrix (e.g., $X = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{
pmatrix}$) gives $X^{2023} = X^{2022} = 0$ (so $X \in S$), but $X^2 = 0 \neq X$
(since $X \neq 0$), so $X^2 \neq X^1$. Hence $k = 2$ fails.

For $k = 3$: every possible minimal polynomial divides $t^2(t-1)$, so $X^3 = X^2
$ for all $X \in S$. ✓

**Conclusion.** The smallest positive integer $k$ is $\boxed{3}$.
