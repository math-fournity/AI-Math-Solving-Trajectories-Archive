$$f(-x) = \frac{(-x)^5\bigl(1-(-x)^{10}\bigr)}{\bigl(1+(-x)^2\bigr)^{18}} = \fra

$$

Here we used $(-x)^5 = -x^5$ (odd power) and $(-x)^{10} = x^{10}$ (even power),

$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(x)\,dx = \lim_{R\to\infty}\int_{-R}^{R} f(x)\,dx = \
