# Problem

A logistics company manages a high-security warehouse containing 100 crates, stacked in a single vertical column. The crates are numbered 1 through 100, starting from the top crate (crate 1) down to the bottom (crate 100).

To consolidate space, a robotic crane performs a specific automated routine: it retrieves the two crates currently at the very top of the stack. A computerized randomization system selects one of these two crates to be permanently shipped out of the warehouse. The other crate is then moved to the very bottom of the stack, becoming the new base for the column.

The crane repeats this exact cycle—extracting the top two, discarding one at random, and placing the survivor at the bottom—until only a single crate remains in the warehouse.

Calculate the expected value of the number labeled on that final remaining crate. If your answer is an irreducible fraction $\frac{a}{b}$, find the value of $a + b$.
