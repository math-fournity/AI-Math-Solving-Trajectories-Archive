**Separation of Variables for Laplace's Equation in Spherical Coordinates**

We seek a solution of the form $u(\rho,\phi,\theta) = R(\rho)\,\Phi(\phi)\,\Thet
a(\theta)$.

**Step 1. Substitute and divide by $R\Phi\Theta$.**

$$\frac{1}{R}\frac{d}{d\rho}\!\left(\rho^{2}\frac{dR}{d\rho}\right) + \frac{1}{\
Phi\sin\phi}\frac{d}{d\phi}\!\left(\sin\phi\frac{d\Phi}{d\phi}\right) + \frac{1}
{\Theta\sin^{2}\phi}\frac{d^{2}\Theta}{d\theta^{2}} = 0.$$

The first term depends only on $\rho$; the remaining two depend only on $\phi$ a
nd $\theta$. Hence each group must be constant. Set

$$\frac{1}{R}\frac{d}{d\rho}\!\left(\rho^{2}\frac{dR}{d\rho}\right) = \lambda,\q
quad \lambda=\text{const}.$$

**Step 2. Radial (Euler) equation.**

$$\boxed{\;\frac{d}{d\rho}\!\left(\rho^{2}\frac{dR}{d\rho}\right) = \lambda R\;}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \rho^{2}R''+2\rho R'-\lambda R=0.$$

**Step 3. Separate the angular part.** The remaining equation is

$$\frac{1}{\Phi\sin\phi}\frac{d}{d\phi}\!\left(\sin\phi\frac{d\Phi}{d\phi}\right
) + \frac{1}{\Theta\sin^{2}\phi}\frac{d^{2}\Theta}{d\theta^{2}} = -\lambda.$$

Multiply through by $\sin^{2}\phi$:

$$\frac{\sin\phi}{\Phi}\frac{d}{d\phi}\!\left(\sin\phi\frac{d\Phi}{d\phi}\right)
 + \lambda\sin^{2}\phi \;+\; \frac{1}{\Theta}\frac{d^{2}\Theta}{d\theta^{2}} = 0
.$$

The first two terms depend only on $\phi$; the last only on $\theta$. Set the $\
theta$-group to a constant $-m^{2}$ (periodicity of $\Theta$ forces $m\in\mathbb
{Z}$ and the sign to be negative):

**Step 4. Azimuthal equation.**

$$\boxed{\;\frac{d^{2}\Theta}{d\theta^{2}} + m^{2}\Theta = 0\;}$$

with $\Theta(\theta+2\pi)=\Theta(\theta)$, so $m=0,1,2,\dots$ and $\Theta(\theta
)=A\cos m\theta+B\sin m\theta$.

**Step 5. Polar (associated Legendre) equation.** The $\phi$-group equals $m^{2}
$:

$$\frac{\sin\phi}{\Phi}\frac{d}{d\phi}\!\left(\sin\phi\frac{d\Phi}{d\phi}\right)
 + \lambda\sin^{2}\phi = m^{2}.$$

Dividing by $\sin^{2}\phi$:

$$\boxed{\;\frac{1}{\sin\phi}\frac{d}{d\phi}\!\left(\sin\phi\frac{d\Phi}{d\phi}\
right) + \left(\lambda - \frac{m^{2}}{\sin^{2}\phi}\right)\Phi = 0\;}$$

This is the **associated Legendre equation**. Regularity at $\phi=0,\pi$ quantiz
es $\lambda=\ell(\ell+1)$ with $\ell=0,1,2,\dots$ and $0\le m\le\ell$.
