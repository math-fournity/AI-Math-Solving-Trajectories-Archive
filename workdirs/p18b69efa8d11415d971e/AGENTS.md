# Problem

In the coastal city of Arithema, there are 15 distinct maritime signal beacons, uniquely numbered 1 through 15. A specialized decryption device requires a combination of three distinct beacons to unlock a hidden frequency. However, for the frequency to resonate and open the channel, the product of the three identification numbers on the chosen beacons must be a perfect square.

A signals officer needs to select a squad of $k$ beacons to take into the field. She wants to ensure that no matter which specific subset of $k$ beacons she ends up with, it is mathematically guaranteed that there will be at least one trio $\{a, b, c\}$ within that subset whose product $abc$ is a perfect square.

What is the minimum value of the positive integer $k$ that the officer must choose to guarantee this condition?
