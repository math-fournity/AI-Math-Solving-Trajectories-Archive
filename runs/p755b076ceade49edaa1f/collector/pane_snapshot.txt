## Step 1: Total proper colorings (identity fixed count)

{F,K}, {L,R}; vertices in different parts must get different colors, vertices in

**Method:** Color T, F, L with 3 distinct colors (6·5·4 = 120 ways), then color

- B ∈ {a} ∪ D (avoids b, c), K ∈ {b} ∪ D (avoids a, c), R ∈ {c} ∪ D (avoids a, b

Counting valid (B,K,R) assignments by how many come from {a,b,c}:

Total proper colorings = 120 × 34 = **4080**.
