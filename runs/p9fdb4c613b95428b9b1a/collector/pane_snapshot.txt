## Setting Up the Polynomial Structure

Let $A = (c^2 - 2c)^2 = c^2(c-2)^2$. The recurrence is:
$$x_{n+1} = A\, x_n x_{n-1} + 2x_n - x_{n-1}$$

Since $x_1 = 1$ and $x_2 = c^2 - 4c + 7$ are polynomials in $c$, and the recurre
nce uses only polynomial operations, **$x_n(c)$ is a polynomial in $c$ for every
 $n$**. Thus $P(c) := x_{1006}(c) - 2011$ is a polynomial, and we seek the produ
ct of all its roots.

## Computing $P(0)$

When $c = 0$: $A = 0$, so the recurrence becomes linear: $x_{n+1} = 2x_n - x_{n-
1}$, giving an arithmetic sequence.

$$x_1 = 1,\quad x_2 = 7,\quad x_n = 1 + (n-1)\cdot 6 = 6n - 5$$

$$x_{1006}(0) = 6(1006) - 5 = 6031, \qquad P(0) = 6031 - 2011 = \mathbf{4020}$$

## Determining the Leading Coefficient

Let $d_n = \deg(x_n)$ and $L_n$ = leading coefficient of $x_n$.

**Degrees:** The dominant term in $x_{n+1}$ is $A\,x_n x_{n-1}$ (degree $4 + d_n
 + d_{n-1}$), which strictly exceeds $\deg(2x_n) = d_n$ and $\deg(x_{n-1}) = d_{
n-1}$. So:
$$d_{n+1} = 4 + d_n + d_{n-1}, \quad d_1 = 0,\; d_2 = 2$$

Computing: $d_3 = 6,\; d_4 = 12,\; d_5 = 22,\; d_6 = 38, \ldots$ — **all even**
(sum of two evens plus 4 is even, by induction).

**Leading coefficients:** Since $\text{lc}(A) = 1$ and the $A\,x_n x_{n-1}$ term
 dominates:
$$L_{n+1} = L_n \cdot L_{n-1}, \quad L_1 = 1,\; L_2 = 1$$

This gives $L_n = 1$ for all $n$. So **$x_{1006}(c)$ is monic**, and $P(c) = x_{
1006}(c) - 2011$ is also monic of degree $d_{1006}$ (subtracting a constant does
n't change the degree since $d_{1006} \geq 2$).

## Applying the Product-of-Roots Formula

For a monic polynomial $P(c) = c^d + a_{d-1}c^{d-1} + \cdots + a_0$ with roots $
r_1, \ldots, r_d$ (counting multiplicity):

$$r_1 r_2 \cdots r_d = (-1)^d \, a_0 = (-1)^d \, P(0)$$

Since $d = d_{1006}$ is **even**, $(-1)^d = 1$, so:

$$\prod_{i} c_i = P(0) = 4020$$
