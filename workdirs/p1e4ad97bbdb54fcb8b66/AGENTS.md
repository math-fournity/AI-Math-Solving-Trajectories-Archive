# Problem

A specialized logistics company is managing 672 cargo containers, each assigned a weight in tons denoted by $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_{672}$. These weights are organized in non-decreasing order such that $a_1 \leq a_2 \leq \dots \leq a_{672}$, and the total combined weight of all 672 containers is exactly 2018 tons.

The company follows a strict "Integer Batching Rule": for every possible batch size $n$ from 1 to 672, there must exist at least one subset of exactly $n$ containers whose total weight is an integer.

Based on these operational constraints, what is the minimum possible weight, in tons, that the heaviest container ($a_{672}$) can have? Express your answer as a mixed fraction.
