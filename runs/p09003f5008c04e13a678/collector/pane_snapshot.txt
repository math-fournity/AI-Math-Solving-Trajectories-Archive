### Step 2: Simplifying the Legendre Symbol

Since $q \equiv 3 \pmod{4}$, we have $\left(\frac{-1}{q}\right) = (-1)^{(q-1)/2}

$$\left(\frac{-p}{q}\right) = \left(\frac{-1}{q}\right)\left(\frac{p}{q}\right)

$$\left(\frac{p}{q}\right)\left(\frac{q}{p}\right) = (-1)^{\frac{p-1}{2}\cdot\fr

$$\left(\frac{-p}{q}\right) = -\left(\frac{q}{p}\right).$$

Thus $\left(\frac{-p}{q}\right) = 1$ if and only if $\left(\frac{q}{p}\right) =
-1$, i.e., **$q$ is a quadratic non-residue modulo $p$**.
