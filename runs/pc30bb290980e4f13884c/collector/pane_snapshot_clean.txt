$$\frac{1}{2}\bigl|a(b^2 - c^2) + b(c^2 - a^2) + c(a^2 - b^2)\bigr| = \frac{1}{2

**2. All pairwise distances are irrational.**

$$d(P_i, P_j) = \sqrt{(i-j)^2 + (i^2 - j^2)^2} = |i - j|\sqrt{1 + (i+j)^2}.$$

Since $i, j \in \{1, \ldots, n\}$ with $i \neq j$, we have $|i - j| \geq 1$ (a p

**Claim:** For any positive integer $m \geq 1$, $\sqrt{1 + m^2}$ is irrational.
