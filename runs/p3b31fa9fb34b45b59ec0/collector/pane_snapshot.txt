## Solution

The solid is the standard simplex $V = \{(x,y,z): x,y,z \ge 0,\; x+y+z \le 1\}$
with unit density. The axis is the line through the origin in direction $\hat u
= \tfrac{1}{\sqrt 3}(1,1,1)$.

**Perpendicular distance.** For a point $\mathbf r = (x,y,z)$, the squared dista
nce to the line is
$$d^2 = |\mathbf r|^2 - (\mathbf r\cdot\hat u)^2 = x^2+y^2+z^2 - \frac{(x+y+z)^2
}{3}.$$
Equivalently,
$$d^2 = \frac{(x-y)^2+(y-z)^2+(z-x)^2}{3}.$$

**Symmetry reduction.** Over the symmetric simplex, $\int x^2\,dV = \int y^2\,dV
 = \int z^2\,dV =: A$ and $\int xy\,dV = \int yz\,dV = \int xz\,dV =: B$. Using
the second form,
$$I = \frac{1}{3}\int_V\!\big[(x-y)^2+(y-z)^2+(z-x)^2\big]\,dV = \frac{1}{3}\cdo
t 3\int_V(x-y)^2\,dV = \int_V(x^2-2xy+y^2)\,dV = 2A-2B.$$

**Computing $A$.**
$$A = \int_0^1\!\int_0^{1-x}\!\int_0^{1-x-y} x^2\,dz\,dy\,dx = \int_0^1 x^2\cdot
\frac{(1-x)^2}{2}\,dx = \frac{1}{2}B(3,3)=\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{2!\,2!}{5!}=\fra
c{1}{2}\cdot\frac{4}{120}=\frac{1}{60}.$$

**Computing $B$.**
$$B = \int_0^1\!\int_0^{1-x}\!\int_0^{1-x-y} xy\,dz\,dy\,dx = \int_0^1 x\cdot\fr
ac{(1-x)^3}{6}\,dx = \frac{1}{6}B(2,4)=\frac{1}{6}\cdot\frac{1!\,3!}{5!}=\frac{1
}{6}\cdot\frac{6}{120}=\frac{1}{120}.$$

**Result.**
$$I = 2A - 2B = 2\!\left(\frac{1}{60}-\frac{1}{120}\right) = 2\cdot\frac{1}{120}
 = \boxed{\dfrac{1}{60}}.$$
