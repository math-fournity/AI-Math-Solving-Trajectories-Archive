# Problem

A specialized deep-space probe is programmed to transmit a sequence of signals based on the Fibonacci sequence, where the first two signals have a magnitude of 0 and 1 respectively, and every subsequent signal’s magnitude is the sum of the magnitudes of the two signals immediately preceding it. The probe is scheduled to transmit exactly $100,000,001$ such signals over its lifespan.

Due to a specific hardware limitation in the probe's antenna, a signal is considered "perfectly resonant" if its magnitude is a multiple of $10^k$, where $k$ is a positive integer representing the resonance grade. 

What is the smallest natural number $k$ such that at least one of the $100,000,001$ signals transmitted by the probe is guaranteed to be a "perfectly resonant" signal of grade $k$?
