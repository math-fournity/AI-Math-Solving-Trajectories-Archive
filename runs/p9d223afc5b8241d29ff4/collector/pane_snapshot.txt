## Proof that $\mathbb{R}\mathrm{P}^n / \mathbb{R}\mathrm{P}^{n-1} \cong S^n$

 quotient $S^n/(x\sim -x)$ is obtained by taking a single copy of $D^n$ (the upp
er hemisphere) and identifying antipodal points on its boundary $\partial D^n =
S^{n-1}$.

Concretely, let $q_1 : D^n \to \mathbb{R}\mathrm{P}^n$ be the quotient map that:
- is injective on $\operatorname{int}(D^n)$, and
- identifies $x \sim -x$ for all $x \in \partial D^n = S^{n-1}$.

The image $q_1(\partial D^n) = S^{n-1}/(x\sim -x) = \mathbb{R}\mathrm{P}^{n-1}$,

\sim\;} \mathbb{R}\mathrm{P}^n \setminus \mathbb{R}\mathrm{P}^{n-1}$.

### Step 5: $D^n / \partial D^n \cong S^n$
