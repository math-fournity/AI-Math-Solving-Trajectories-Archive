## Solution

**Setup.** The original zone $X_0$ is a triangle with side lengths 3, 4, and 5 n
autical miles — a right triangle (since $3^2+4^2=5^2$). Its area and perimeter a
re:

$$\text{Area}(X_0) = \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot 3\cdot 4 = 6, \qquad \text{Perimeter}(X_0
) = 3+4+5 = 12.$$

**Key observation — iterated dilation equals a single dilation.** The operation
"$X_n$ = all points within distance 1 of $X_{n-1}$" is exactly the Minkowski sum
 (morphological dilation) of $X_{n-1}$ with the closed unit disk $B_1$:

$$X_n = X_{n-1} \oplus B_1.$$

Since Minkowski sums are associative and $B_1 \oplus B_1 \oplus \cdots \oplus B_
1$ ($k$ times) $= B_k$ (the disk of radius $k$), iterating gives

$$X_n = X_0 \oplus B_n.$$

**Steiner's formula.** For any compact convex set $K$ in the plane, the area of
its $r$-parallel body (Minkowski sum with the disk of radius $r$) is

$$\text{Area}(K \oplus B_r) = \text{Area}(K) + r\cdot\text{Perimeter}(K) + \pi r
^2.$$

The triangle $X_0$ is convex, so this applies directly:

$$\text{Area}(X_n) = 6 + 12n + \pi n^2.$$

**The 21st-year ring.** The region added during year 21 is $X_{21}\setminus X_{2
0}$, with area

$$\text{Area}(X_{21}) - \text{Area}(X_{20}) = \bigl(6 + 12\cdot 21 + \pi\cdot 21
^2\bigr) - \bigl(6 + 12\cdot 20 + \pi\cdot 20^2\bigr).$$

$$= 12(21-20) + \pi(21^2 - 20^2) = 12 + \pi(441-400) = 12 + 41\pi.$$

**Extracting $a$ and $b$.** This is of the form $a\pi + b$ with

$$a = 41, \qquad b = 12.$$

Therefore

$$100a + b = 100\cdot 41 + 12 = 4112.$$
