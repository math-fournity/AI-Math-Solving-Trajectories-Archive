## Proof

The equivalence relation is $(a_0, b_0) \sim (a_1, b_1) \iff a_0 + b_0^2 = a_1 +
 b_1^2$, i.e. two points are equivalent iff they lie on the same level curve of
$f(x,y) = x + y^2$.

**Claim.** The quotient space $\mathbb{R}^2/{\sim}$ is homeomorphic to $\mathbb{
R}$ (the real line with its standard topology).

**Construction.** Define $f \colon \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$ by $f(x,y) = x +
 y^2$. This is continuous and surjective (for any $c \in \mathbb{R}$, take $(c,
0)$, giving $f(c,0) = c$). The fibers of $f$ are exactly the equivalence classes
:
$$f^{-1}(c) = \{(x,y) : x + y^2 = c\} = \{(c - t^2, t) : t \in \mathbb{R}\},$$
a parabola with vertex $(c, 0)$. Hence $f$ descends to a well-defined bijection
$\bar f \colon \mathbb{R}^2/{\sim} \to \mathbb{R}$, $\bar f([(x,y)]) = x + y^2$.

**The quotient topology equals the standard topology on $\mathbb{R}$.** By the u
niversal property, $\mathbb{R}^2/{\sim}$ carries the quotient topology, and $\ba
r f$ is a homeomorphism onto $\mathbb{R}$ equipped with the quotient topology tr
ansported via $f$: a set $U \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ is open in this topology iff $
f^{-1}(U)$ is open in $\mathbb{R}^2$.

- *Quotient topology refines the standard topology:* Since $f$ is continuous wit
h respect to the standard topology on $\mathbb{R}$, every standard-open $U$ has
$f^{-1}(U)$ open in $\mathbb{R}^2$, so $U$ is open in the quotient topology.

- *Standard topology refines the quotient topology:* Suppose $f^{-1}(U)$ is open
 in $\mathbb{R}^2$. Restrict to the $x$-axis $A = \{(x, 0) : x \in \mathbb{R}\}$
, which is embedded in $\mathbb{R}^2$. Then
$$f^{-1}(U) \cap A = \{(x, 0) : x \in U\},$$
since $f(x, 0) = x$. As the intersection of an open set with the subspace $A \co
ng \mathbb{R}$, this set is open in $A$, hence $\{x : x \in U\} = U$ is open in
the standard topology of $\mathbb{R}$.

Both refinements hold, so the two topologies coincide. Therefore $\bar f$ is a h
omeomorphism.

**Conclusion.** The quotient space $\mathbb{R}^2/{\sim}$ is homeomorphic to **$\
mathbb{R}$** (the real line).
