## Solution

**Setup.** Place the unit circle $\omega$ at the origin with $A=(-1,0)$, $B=(1,0
)$, so $O=(0,0)$ and $AB$ is a diameter. Let $C=(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$ with $0
<\theta<\pi$, and set $t=\theta/2$.

**Arc midpoints.** The midpoint $M$ of arc $AC$ (not containing $B$) is at angle
 $(\theta+\pi)/2 = t+\pi/2$, giving $M=(-\sin t,\cos t)$. The midpoint $N$ of ar
c $BC$ (not containing $A$) is at angle $t$, giving $N=(\cos t,\sin t)$.

**Finding $I = AN \cap BM$.** Parametrize:
- $AN$: $(-1,0)+s(\cos t+1,\sin t)$
- $BM$: $(1,0)+u(-\sin t-1,\cos t)$

From the $y$-equation: $u=s\tan t$. Substituting into the $x$-equation and simpl
ifying yields $s=\frac{2\cos t}{1+\cos t+\sin t}$, so

$$I=\left(\frac{\cos 2t+\cos t-\sin t}{1+\cos t+\sin t},\;\frac{\sin 2t}{1+\cos
t+\sin t}\right).$$

**Finding $P = AM \cap BC$.** Parametrize:
- $AM$: $(-1,0)+w(1-\sin t,\cos t)$
- $BC$: $(1,0)+v(\cos 2t-1,\sin 2t)$

Using $\cos 2t-1=-2\sin^2 t$ and $\sin 2t=2\sin t\cos t$, the $y$-equation gives
 $w=2v\sin t$. Substituting: $-1+2v\sin t(1-\sin t)=1-2v\sin^2 t$, which simplif
ies to $v=1/\sin t$ and $w=2$. Thus

$$P=(1-2\sin t,\;2\cos t).$$

**Concurrency condition.** The lines $AM$, $BC$, $OI$ concur at $P$, so $O,I,P$
are collinear: $I_x P_y = I_y P_x$, i.e.,

$$\frac{\cos 2t+\cos t-\sin t}{\sin 2t}=\frac{1-2\sin t}{2\cos t}.$$

Cross-multiplying and using $\sin 2t=2\sin t\cos t$:

$$\cos 2t+\cos t-\sin t = \sin t(1-2\sin t)=\sin t - 2\sin^2 t.$$

Rearranging: $\cos 2t + 2\sin^2 t + \cos t - 2\sin t = 0$. Since $\cos 2t+2\sin^
2 t = 1$:

$$1+\cos t-2\sin t=0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \cos t+1=2\sin t.$$

Squaring: $(\cos t+1)^2=4(1-\cos^2 t)$, giving $5\cos^2 t+2\cos t-3=0$, so $\cos
 t=3/5$ (the root $\cos t=-1$ is extraneous). Then $\sin t=4/5$.

**Computing $C$ and the area.**

$$C=(\cos 2t,\sin 2t)=\left(\tfrac{9}{25}-\tfrac{16}{25},\;\tfrac{24}{25}\right)
=\left(-\tfrac{7}{25},\tfrac{24}{25}\right).$$

With base $AB=2$ and height equal to the $y$-coordinate of $C$:

$$[\triangle ABC]=\tfrac{1}{2}\cdot 2\cdot \tfrac{24}{25}=\tfrac{24}{25}.$$

**Verification.** With $\cos t=3/5,\sin t=4/5$: $I=(-1/5,2/5)$, $P=(-3/5,6/5)$,
and $(-1/5)(6/5)=(2/5)(-3/5)=-6/25$, confirming collinearity. ✓

The area of $\triangle ABC$ is $\boxed{\dfrac{24}{25}}$.
