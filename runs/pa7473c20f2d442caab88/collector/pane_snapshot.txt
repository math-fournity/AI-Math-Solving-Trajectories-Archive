**Proof.** Since $f$ is differentiable on $(a,b)$, it is continuous on $(a,b)$.
Hence for any closed subinterval $[c,d]\subset(a,b)$, $f$ is continuous on $[c,d

$$f(d)-f(c)=f'(\xi)(d-c).$$
Since $|f'(\xi)|\le 1$,
$$|f(d)-f(c)|\le d-c.$$
Thus $f$ is $1$-Lipschitz on $(a,b)$: for all $x,y\in(a,b)$, $|f(x)-f(y)|\le|x-y

using the $1$-Lipschitz property.
