## Proof

We evaluate
$$L = \lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{\left(\int_0^x e^{t^2}\,dt\right)^2}{\int_0^x e^
{2t^2}\,dt}.$$

Let $F(x) = \int_0^x e^{t^2}\,dt$ and $G(x) = \int_0^x e^{2t^2}\,dt$. As $x \to
\infty$, both $F(x) \to \infty$ and $G(x) \to \infty$, so $L$ is of the indeterm
inate form $\frac{\infty}{\infty}$. We apply L'Hôpital's rule.

**First application.** Differentiating numerator and denominator:
$$\frac{d}{dx}\bigl[F(x)^2\bigr] = 2F(x)\,F'(x) = 2F(x)\,e^{x^2}, \qquad G'(x) =
 e^{2x^2}.$$

Hence
$$L = \lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{2F(x)\,e^{x^2}}{e^{2x^2}} = \lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{
2F(x)}{e^{x^2}}.$$

This is again $\frac{\infty}{\infty}$, so we apply L'Hôpital a second time.

**Second application.** Differentiating:
$$\frac{d}{dx}\bigl[2F(x)\bigr] = 2e^{x^2}, \qquad \frac{d}{dx}\bigl[e^{x^2}\big
r] = 2x\,e^{x^2}.$$

Therefore
$$L = \lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{2e^{x^2}}{2x\,e^{x^2}} = \lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{1}{
x} = 0.$$

**Verification via asymptotics.** For large $x$, integration by parts gives the
standard estimate
$$\int_0^x e^{t^2}\,dt \sim \frac{e^{x^2}}{2x}, \qquad \int_0^x e^{2t^2}\,dt \si
m \frac{e^{2x^2}}{4x}.$$

Thus
$$\frac{F(x)^2}{G(x)} \sim \frac{e^{2x^2}/(4x^2)}{e^{2x^2}/(4x)} = \frac{1}{x} \
to 0,$$

confirming the result.

$$\boxed{L = 0}$$
