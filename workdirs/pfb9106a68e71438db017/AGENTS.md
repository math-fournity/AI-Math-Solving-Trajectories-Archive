# Problem

A specialized logistics hub manages a fleet of transport drones, each assigned a unique integer identification code representing its cargo capacity in kilograms. The hub operates under a strict "Load Balancing Rule": for any combination of three distinct drones selected from the fleet, the difference in capacity between the drone with the largest capacity and the drone with the smallest capacity must exactly match the capacity of another drone already present in the fleet.

Currently, the hub's inventory logs confirm that there are at least three drones with capacities of 3 kg, 6 kg, and 2023 kg respectively. Given that all drone capacities are positive integers, what is the minimum possible number of drones that must be registered in this fleet?
