## Key Insight: Pairing via Triangle Inequality

I pair the six points as **(A, D), (B, E), (C, F)**. By the triangle inequality,
 for any point P:

$$PA + PD \geq AD, \quad PB + PE \geq BE, \quad PC + PF \geq CF$$

with equality in each iff P lies on the respective segment. Summing:

$$PA + PB + PC + PD + PE + FP \geq AD + BE + CF$$

Equality holds iff P lies on **all three** segments AD, BE, CF simultaneously.
