## Setup

We seek $u(x,y)$ harmonic on $0<x<2$, $0<y<2$ with
- $u(x,0)=f(x)$ where $f(x)=x$ on $[0,1]$ and $f(x)=2-x$ on $[1,2]$ (a tent, sym
metric about $x=1$),
- $u(x,2)=0$,
- $u(0,y)=u(2,y)=0$.

## Separation of variables

The homogeneous Dirichlet conditions at $x=0,2$ force the $x$-eigenfunctions $\s
in\!\big(\tfrac{n\pi x}{2}\big)$, $n=1,2,\dots$ Writing $u=\sum X_n(x)Y_n(y)$, e
ach $Y_n$ satisfies $Y_n''-\big(\tfrac{n\pi}{2}\big)^2 Y_n=0$.

To enforce $u(x,2)=0$ we choose the decaying (in reverse) combination
$$Y_n(y)=\sinh\!\Big(\tfrac{n\pi(2-y)}{2}\Big),\qquad Y_n(2)=0,\quad Y_n(0)=\sin
h(n\pi).$$

Thus
$$u(x,y)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} c_n\,\sinh\!\Big(\tfrac{n\pi(2-y)}{2}\Big)\sin\!\Bi
g(\tfrac{n\pi x}{2}\Big).$$

## Fourier coefficients of $f$

Setting $y=0$ gives $\sum c_n\sinh(n\pi)\sin\!\big(\tfrac{n\pi x}{2}\big)=f(x)$,
 so with $b_n:=c_n\sinh(n\pi)$ the half-range sine coefficients on $[0,2]$ are
$$b_n=\int_0^2 f(x)\sin\!\Big(\tfrac{n\pi x}{2}\Big)\,dx.$$

Use the symmetry $f(2-x)=f(x)$ and $\sin\!\big(\tfrac{n\pi(2-x)}{2}\big)=(-1)^{n
+1}\sin\!\big(\tfrac{n\pi x}{2}\big)$. Substituting $t=2-x$ in the second half,
$$b_n=\Big[1+(-1)^{n+1}\Big]\int_0^1 x\sin\!\Big(\tfrac{n\pi x}{2}\Big)\,dx.$$

Hence $b_n=0$ for even $n$, and for odd $n$ the prefactor is $2$. For odd $n=2k-
1$ ($k=1,2,\dots$), with $\alpha=n\pi/2$ we have $\cos\alpha=0$ and $\sin\alpha=
(-1)^{k-1}$, so
$$\int_0^1 x\sin(\alpha x)\,dx=-\frac{\cos\alpha}{\alpha}+\frac{\sin\alpha}{\alp
ha^2}=\frac{(-1)^{k-1}}{(n\pi/2)^2}=\frac{4(-1)^{k-1}}{n^2\pi^2}.$$

Therefore
$$b_n=\frac{8(-1)^{k-1}}{n^2\pi^2},\qquad n=2k-1\ \text{odd},\qquad b_n=0\ \text
{for even }n.$$

## Solution

Since $c_n=b_n/\sinh(n\pi)$, the solution is

$$\boxed{\;u(x,y)=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{8(-1)^{k-1}}{(2k-1)^2\pi^2\,\sinh\!\b
ig((2k-1)\pi\big)}\,\sinh\!\Big(\tfrac{(2k-1)\pi(2-y)}{2}\Big)\,\sin\!\Big(\tfra
c{(2k-1)\pi x}{2}\Big).\;}$$

## Verification

- **PDE**: Each term is a product of an $x$-eigenfunction $\sin(\cdot)$ and a $y

is harmonic; the series (converging uniformly away from the corner data, and in
$L^2$ up to the boundary) is harmonic.
- **$u(0,y)=u(2,y)=0$**: $\sin(0)=0$ and $\sin\!\big(\tfrac{(2k-1)\pi\cdot 2}{2}
\big)=\sin((2k-1)\pi)=0$.
- **$u(x,2)=0$**: $\sinh(0)=0$ for every term.
