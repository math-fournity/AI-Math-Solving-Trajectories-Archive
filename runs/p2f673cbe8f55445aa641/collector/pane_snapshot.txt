## Proof

**Claim:** Yes. A connected orientable smooth manifold $M$ has exactly two orien
tations.

**Setup — the orientation double cover.**

Let $\dim M = n$. At each point $p \in M$, the tangent space $T_pM$ is an $n$-di
mensional real vector space, which admits exactly two orientations (correspondin
g to the two connected components of $\operatorname{GL}_n(\mathbb{R})$ via a cho
ice of ordered basis, or equivalently the two elements of $\operatorname{Or}(T_p
M) \cong \{+1,-1}$). Let

$$\widetilde{M} = \{(p, \mathfrak{o}_p) : p \in M,\; \mathfrak{o}_p \text{ is an
 orientation of } T_pM\},$$

with the natural projection $\pi: \widetilde{M} \to M$, $\pi(p,\mathfrak{o}_p)=p
$. Equipped with the smooth structure induced from $M$ (locally, orientations ov
er a chart correspond to a sign choice), $\pi: \widetilde{M}\to M$ is a **2-shee
ted covering space**, called the *orientation double cover*.

An **orientation of $M$** is precisely a smooth section $s: M \to \widetilde{M}$
 of $\pi$ (a consistent smooth choice of orientation at every point).

**Orientability makes the cover trivial.**

By definition, $M$ is orientable iff $\widetilde{M}$ is disconnected, i.e., iff
the cover $\pi$ is trivial. Since $M$ is given to be orientable, we have a decom
position

$$\widetilde{M} = M_+ \sqcup M_-,\qquad M_+,M_- \cong M,\qquad \pi|_{M_\pm}: M_\
pm \xrightarrow{\;\sim\;} M.$$

**Counting sections over a connected base.**

A section $s: M \to \widetilde{M}$ satisfies $\pi \circ s = \operatorname{id}_M$
, so $s$ is continuous and $s(M)$ is a connected subset of $\widetilde{M}$ (bein
g the continuous image of the connected space $M$). Since $\widetilde{M} = M_+ \
sqcup M_-$ is a disjoint union of two open (and closed) components, the connecte
d set $s(M)$ must lie entirely in one component:

$$s(M) \subseteq M_+ \quad\text{or}\quad s(M) \subseteq M_-.$$

Once $s(M)\subseteq M_\pm$, the condition $\pi\circ s = \operatorname{id}_M$ for
ces $s = (\pi|_{M_\pm})^{-1}$, since $\pi|_{M_\pm}$ is a homeomorphism (diffeomo
rphism). Hence there are **exactly two** sections:

$$s_+ = (\pi|_{M_+})^{-1}, \qquad s_- = (\pi|_{M_-})^{-1},$$

corresponding to the two orientations of $M$. These are distinct (they disagree
at every point), and there are no others.

**Conclusion.**

A connected orientable smooth manifold $M$ has exactly two orientations. The two
 key ingredients are: (1) orientability trivializes the orientation double cover
 into two disjoint copies of $M$, and (2) connectedness of $M$ forces any sectio
n to choose one copy globally, leaving exactly two global choices.
