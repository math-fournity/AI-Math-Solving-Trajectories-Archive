# Problem

In a vast desert, three remote research outposts—Alpha ($A$), Bravo ($B$), and Charlie ($C$)—form a triangular perimeter. A central communications hub, Mobile Base ($M$), is stationed within this triangle to relay signals between the outposts.

The surveyors have mapped the following angular coordinates based on the lines of sight between the stations:
- Looking from Bravo, the total span between Alpha and Charlie is exactly $48^\circ$.
- Looking from Bravo, the angle between the Mobile Base and Charlie is $38^\circ$.
- Looking from Charlie, the total span between Alpha and Bravo is exactly $22^\circ$.
- Looking from Charlie, the angle between the Mobile Base and Bravo is $18^\circ$.

A technician at the Mobile Base needs to calibrate the directional antenna. To do this, they must calculate the exact angle formed at the Mobile Base when pivoting from the line of sight toward Alpha to the line of sight toward Bravo.

What is the measure of angle $\angle AMB$?
