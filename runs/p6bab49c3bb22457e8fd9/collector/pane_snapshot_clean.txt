## Part a)

Rectangle with integer sides $c, d$. Perimeter $= 2(c+d)$, area $= cd$. The cond
ition "area numerically equal to twice its perimeter" gives

$$cd = 2\cdot 2(c+d) = 4(c+d).$$

Rearranging: $cd - 4c - 4d = 0$, so $(c-4)(d-4) = 16$.

Since $c,d$ are positive integers, $c-4$ and $d-4$ are positive divisors of $16$
. The factor pairs $(c-4, d-4)$ and resulting $c+d$:

| $(c-4,d-4)$ | $(c,d)$ | $c+d$ |
|---|---|---|
| $(1,16)$ | $(5,20)$ | $25$ |
| $(2,8)$ | $(6,12)$ | $18$ |
| $(4,4)$ | $(8,8)$ | $16$ |
| $(8,2)$ | $(12,6)$ | $18$ |
| $(16,1)$ | $(20,5)$ | $25$ |

The distinct possible values of $c+d$ are $\{16, 18, 25\}$, so

$$S_a = 16 + 18 + 25 = 59.$$

## Part b)

Cuboid with edges $e, e, f$. Volume $= e^2 f$. Total surface area $= 2e^2 + 4ef$
. The condition "volume numerically equal to twice its total surface area" gives

$$e^2 f = 2(2e^2 + 4ef) = 4e^2 + 8ef.$$

Dividing by $e$ (since $e>0$): $ef = 4e + 8f$, i.e. $ef - 8f = 4e$, so $f(e-8) =
 4e$, giving

$$f = \frac{4e}{e-8} = 4 + \frac{32}{e-8}.$$

For $f$ to be a positive integer we need $e - 8$ to be a positive divisor of $32
$. The divisors of $32$ are $1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32$:

| $e-8$ | $e$ | $f = 4 + 32/(e-8)$ | $2e+f$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $1$ | $9$ | $36$ | $54$ |
| $2$ | $10$ | $20$ | $40$ |
| $4$ | $12$ | $12$ | $36$ |
| $8$ | $16$ | $8$ | $40$ |
| $16$ | $24$ | $6$ | $54$ |
| $32$ | $40$ | $5$ | $85$ |

The distinct possible values of $2e+f$ are $\{36, 40, 54, 85\}$, so

$$S_b = 36 + 40 + 54 + 85 = 215.$$

## Final answer

$$S_a + S_b = 59 + 215 = \boxed{274}.$$
