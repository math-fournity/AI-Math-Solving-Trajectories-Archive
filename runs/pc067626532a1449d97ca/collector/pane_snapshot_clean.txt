# Proof

**Claim:** GCH does **not** imply that every uncountable cardinal is of the form
 $2^\kappa$ for some $\kappa$.

Recall that GCH states: for every infinite cardinal $\kappa$, $2^\kappa = \kappa
^+$, where $\kappa^+$ denotes the successor cardinal of $\kappa$.

**Step 1: Characterize the cardinals of the form $2^\kappa$ under GCH.**

Let $\kappa$ be any cardinal.
- If $\kappa$ is finite, then $2^\kappa$ is finite, hence not uncountable.
- If $\kappa$ is infinite, then by GCH, $2^\kappa = \kappa^+$, which is a **succ
essor cardinal**.

Therefore, under GCH, every uncountable cardinal of the form $2^\kappa$ is a suc
cessor cardinal. Conversely, every successor cardinal $\lambda^+$ of an infinite
 cardinal $\lambda$ satisfies $\lambda^+ = 2^\lambda$ by GCH. So:

$$\{2^\kappa : \kappa \text{ is a cardinal}\} \cap \{\text{uncountable cardinals
}\} = \{\text{uncountable successor cardinals}\}.$$

**Step 2: Exhibit an uncountable cardinal that is not a successor cardinal.**

Consider $\aleph_\omega = \sup_{n < \omega} \aleph_n$. This is a **limit cardina
l** (its index $\omega$ is a limit ordinal), so it is not a successor cardinal.
Since $\aleph_\omega > \aleph_0$, it is uncountable.

**Step 3: Conclude.**

Under GCH, $2^\kappa = \kappa^+$ for every infinite $\kappa$, so $2^\kappa$ is a
lways a successor cardinal. The cardinal $\aleph_\omega$ is an uncountable limit
 cardinal, hence not a successor cardinal, hence not of the form $2^\kappa$ for
any $\kappa$.

More generally, every uncountable limit cardinal of the form $\aleph_\beta$ wher
e $\beta$ is a limit ordinal (e.g., $\aleph_\omega, \aleph_{\omega\cdot 2}, \ale
ph_{\omega_1}, \ldots$) is an uncountable cardinal not of the form $2^\kappa$ un
der GCH.

Therefore, **GCH does not imply that every uncountable cardinal is of the form $
2^\kappa$**. The uncountable cardinals of the form $2^\kappa$ under GCH are exac
tly the uncountable successor cardinals, and there exist uncountable limit cardi
nals (such as $\aleph_\omega$) which are not of this form.
