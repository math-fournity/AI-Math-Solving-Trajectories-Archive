# Problem

In a vast, high-security server network, a maintenance bot is deployed to monitor the integrity of a central data hub shaped like a perfect cube. The bot begins its task at a specific corner node of the cube, labeled Node A. 

The bot is programmed to perform a "jump" every 60 seconds. During each jump, the bot must move from its current corner node to one of the three adjacent corner nodes connected by a direct physical edge. The bot selects its destination at random, with each of the three adjacent nodes having an equal probability of being chosen. The bot never stays at its current node during a jump.

After exactly $k$ minutes (and thus $k$ jumps), the system administrator needs to calculate the probability that the bot is located at the specific node diagonally opposite to the starting Node A (the vertex that shares no faces or edges with the starting point).

In terms of $k$, where $k > 0$, what is the probability that the bot is at the opposite vertex after $k$ minutes?
