## Proof

Let $Y = I_1 + I_2$ where $I_1 = \int_0^2 3s\,dW(s)$ and $I_2 = \int_3^5 W(t)\,d
W(t)$.

### Variance of $I_1$

The integrand $3s$ is deterministic, so by Itô isometry:
$$\operatorname{Var}(I_1) = \int_0^2 (3s)^2\,ds = 9\int_0^2 s^2\,ds = 9\cdot\fra
c{s^3}{3}\Big|_0^2 = 9\cdot\frac{8}{3} = 24.$$

Also $E[I_1]=0$ since it is an Itô integral with deterministic (square-integrabl
e) integrand.

### Variance of $I_2$

By Itô's formula, $\int_a^b W(t)\,dW(t) = \tfrac{1}{2}\big(W(b)^2 - W(a)^2\big)
- \tfrac{1}{2}(b-a)$, so
$$I_2 = \tfrac{1}{2}\big(W(5)^2 - W(3)^2\big) - 1.$$

Mean: $E[I_2] = \tfrac{1}{2}(E[W(5)^2]-E[W(3)^2]) - 1 = \tfrac{1}{2}(5-3)-1 = 0$
.

By Itô isometry (the integrand $W(t)$ is adapted and square-integrable):
$$\operatorname{Var}(I_2) = E\!\left[\int_3^5 W(t)^2\,dt\right] = \int_3^5 E[W(t
)^2]\,dt = \int_3^5 t\,dt = \frac{t^2}{2}\Big|_3^5 = \frac{25-9}{2} = 8.$$

### Covariance $\operatorname{Cov}(I_1, I_2)$

$I_1$ is $\mathcal{F}(2)$-measurable. For $t\in[3,5]$, write $W(t) = W(3) + (W(t
)-W(3))$; since $3>2$, the increments $\{W(t)-W(3):t\ge 3\}$ and $W(3)$ are inde
pendent of $\mathcal{F}(2)$. Hence $I_2$ is independent of $\mathcal{F}(2)$, and
 therefore independent of $I_1$. Thus
$$\operatorname{Cov}(I_1,I_2) = E[I_1]\,E[I_2] = 0.$$

### Combining

$$\operatorname{Var}(Y) = \operatorname{Var}(I_1) + \operatorname{Var}(I_2) + 2\
operatorname{Cov}(I_1,I_2) = 24 + 8 + 0 = 32.$$

$$\boxed{\operatorname{Var}(Y) = 32}$$
