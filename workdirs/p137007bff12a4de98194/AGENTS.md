# Problem

In the competitive world of high-precision logistics, a foreman is managing a 10-day specialized assembly cycle. For any given cycle of length $n$, the foreman calculates a "Balance Index," denoted as $S(n)$.

The index is determined by summing the results of a specific calculation performed for each integer shift $k$, ranging from $1$ to $n$. For each shift $k$, the foreman calculates the product of two distinct factory throughput factors: $(n+k)!$ and $(n-k)!$. 

To account for oscillating supply and demand, these products are assigned a directional sign: if the shift $k$ is an odd number, the product is treated as a deficit (negative); if $k$ is an even number, the product is treated as a surplus (positive). This is represented by the alternating multiplier $(-1)^k$.

Calculate the total Balance Index $S(10)$ for a 10-day cycle.
