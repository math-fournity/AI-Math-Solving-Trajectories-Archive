## Key inequality: $M(m,n,r,s) = \min(m,n)^{1/r - 1/s}$ for $0<r<s$

imates.

*Estimate A (factor $m^{1/r-1/s}$):* Since $s>r$, the $\ell_s$-norm is dominated

$$b_j = \Bigl(\sum_i a_{ij}^s\Bigr)^{1/s} \le \Bigl(\sum_i a_{ij}^r\Bigr)^{1/r}.
$$
Hence
$$N = \Bigl(\sum_j b_j^r\Bigr)^{1/r} \le \Bigl(\sum_j\sum_i a_{ij}^r\Bigr)^{1/r}
 = \Bigl(\sum_i R_i\Bigr)^{1/r}.$$
Applying $\|\cdot\|_{\ell_r^m} \le m^{1/r-1/s}\|\cdot\|_{\ell_s^m}$ (valid for $
r<s$) to the $m$-vector $(R_1,\dots,R_m)$:
$$\Bigl(\sum_i R_i\Bigr)^{1/r} \le m^{1/r-1/s}\Bigl(\sum_i R_i^{s/r}\Bigr)^{1/s}
 = m^{1/r-1/s}\,D.$$

*Estimate B (factor $n^{1/r-1/s}$):* Apply $\|\cdot\|_{\ell_r^n} \le n^{1/r-1/s}
\|\cdot\|_{\ell_s^n}$ to $(b_1,\dots,b_n)$:
$$N \le n^{1/r-1/s}\Bigl(\sum_j b_j^s\Bigr)^{1/s} = n^{1/r-1/s}\Bigl(\sum_i\sum_
j a_{ij}^s\Bigr)^{1/s}.$$

$$\Bigl(\sum_i\sum_j a_{ij}^s\Bigr)^{1/s} \le \Bigl(\sum_i R_i^{s/r}\Bigr)^{1/s}
