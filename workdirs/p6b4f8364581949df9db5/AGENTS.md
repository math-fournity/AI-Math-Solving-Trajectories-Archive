# Problem

The stability rating $E(x)$ is calculated as the number of trailing zeros found in the product of all the individual digits of the compound $x$. For example, if a compound were $545$, the product of its digits would be $5 \times 4 \times 5 = 100$, which ends in two zeros, giving it a stability rating of $2$.

The government maintains a database where all Stable Compounds are indexed in strictly increasing order (starting from the smallest possible integer that satisfies the condition). To finalize a security protocol for the national laboratory, the chief engineer needs to identify a specific security key.

Find the $30$th smallest Stable Compound in the database and determine the remainder when that integer is divided by $1000$.
