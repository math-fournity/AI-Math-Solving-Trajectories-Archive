## Solution

Let $t$ be the third root of $p(x)=x^3+ax+b$. By Vieta's formulas (note the $x^2
$ coefficient is $0$):

$$r+s+t=0 \implies t=-r-s, \quad rs+rt+st=a, \quad rst=-b.$$

Let $u$ be the third root of $q(x)=x^3+ax+(b+240)$ with roots $r+4,\; s-3,\; u$.
 By Vieta:

$$(r+4)+(s-3)+u=0 \implies u=-(r+s+1)=t-1,$$
$$(r+4)(s-3)+(r+4)u+(s-3)u=a, \quad (r+4)(s-3)u=-(b+240).$$

**Using the sum-of-products relation.** Since $(r+4)+(s-3)=r+s+1=-(t-1)=-u$:

$$(r+4)(s-3)+u\bigl((r+4)+(s-3)\bigr)=(r+4)(s-3)-u^2=a.$$

For $p$: $a=rs+t(r+s)=rs-t^2$. Setting the two expressions for $a$ equal:

$$rs-t^2=(r+4)(s-3)-(t-1)^2.$$

Expanding the right side: $(r+4)(s-3)=rs-3r+4s-12$ and $(t-1)^2=t^2-2t+1$, so

$$rs-t^2=rs-3r+4s-12-t^2+2t-1,$$
$$0=-3r+4s+2t-13.$$

Substituting $t=-r-s$:

$$0=-3r+4s+2(-r-s)-13=-5r+2s-13 \implies s=\frac{5r+13}{2}.$$

**Using the product relation.** From $rst=-b$ and $(r+4)(s-3)(t-1)=-(b+240)=rst-
240$:

$$(r+4)(s-3)(t-1)=rst-240.$$

With $s=\frac{5r+13}{2}$, $t=-r-s=\frac{-7r-13}{2}$:

- $s-3=\frac{5r+7}{2}$, $\;r+4=r+4$, $\;t-1=\frac{-7r-15}{2}$,
- $rst=r\cdot\frac{5r+13}{2}\cdot\frac{-7r-13}{2}$.

Multiplying the equation by $4$:

$$(r+4)(5r+7)(-7r-15)=r(5r+13)(-7r-13)-960.$$

Expanding both sides:

- **Left:** $(r+4)(5r+7)=5r^2+27r+28$, then $\times(-7r-15)=-35r^3-264r^2-601r-4
20$.
- **Right:** $(5r+13)(-7r-13)=-35r^2-156r-169$, then $\times r = -35r^3-156r^2-1
69r$, minus $960$: $-35r^3-156r^2-169r-960$.

Equating and canceling $-35r^3$:

$$-264r^2-601r-420=-156r^2-169r-960,$$
$$-108r^2-432r+540=0 \implies r^2+4r-5=0 \implies (r+5)(r-1)=0.$$

So $r=-5$ or $r=1$.

**Case 1: $r=-5$.** Then $s=\frac{-25+13}{2}=-6$, $t=11$. Roots of $p$: $-5,-6,1
1$ (sum $0$ ✓).
$$b=-rst=-((-5)(-6)(11))=-330, \quad |b|=330.$$
Check $q$: roots $-1,-9,10$; $q(x)=(x+1)(x+9)(x-10)=x^3-91x-90$, so $a=-91$ and
$b+240=-330+240=-90$ ✓.

**Case 2: $r=1$.** Then $s=\frac{5+13}{2}=9$, $t=-10$. Roots of $p$: $1,9,-10$ (
sum $0$ ✓).
$$b=-rst=-(1\cdot 9\cdot(-10))=90, \quad |b|=90.$$
Check $q$: roots $5,6,-11$; $q(x)=(x-5)(x-6)(x+11)=x^3-91x+330$, so $a=-91$ and
$b+240=90+240=330$ ✓.

Both cases give $a=-91$, consistent throughout.

**Sum of all possible values of $|b|$:** $330+90=\boxed{420}$.
