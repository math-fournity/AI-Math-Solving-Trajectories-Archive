 $D = r\mathbf{u}$, $E = r\mathbf{v}$, $F = r\mathbf{w}$.

Since each side is perpendicular to the corresponding radius at the tangent poin
t:
$$BC:\ \mathbf{x}\cdot\mathbf{u}=r,\quad CA:\ \mathbf{x}\cdot\mathbf{v}=r,\quad
AB:\ \mathbf{x}\cdot\mathbf{w}=r.$$

The angle $\angle DIE = \pi - \gamma$ (and cyclically), so:
$$\mathbf{u}\cdot\mathbf{v} = -\cos\gamma,\quad \mathbf{v}\cdot\mathbf{w} = -\co

where $K = 1 - \cos\alpha - \cos\beta - \cos\gamma$ (a common factor).

{r}\,\mathbf{v},$$
$$\overrightarrow{AD_1} = D_1 - A = (B+C-D) - A = B + C - A - r\mathbf{u}.$$

$$NL \perp BE_1 \quad\text{and}\quad LM \perp CF_1.$$

$$90° + 90° + 90° = \boxed{270°}.$$
