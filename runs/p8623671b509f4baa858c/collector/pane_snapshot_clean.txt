## Proof

Let $G = SL_2(p)$ act on $X = \mathbb{F}(p) \cup \{\infty\}$ via the Möbius acti
on
$$\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} \cdot z = \frac{az+b}{cz+d}, \qqu
ad \det = ad-bc = 1.$$

**Orbit of $\infty$.** By convention $\infty \mapsto \frac{a\infty+b}{c\infty+d}
$ equals $a/c$ when $c\neq 0$, and equals $\infty$ when $c=0$.

- $\infty$ is fixed by the identity, so $\infty \in \text{Orb}(\infty)$.
- For any $t \in \mathbb{F}(p)$, take
$$M_t = \begin{pmatrix} t & t-1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \det M_t = t\cdot
 1 - (t-1)\cdot 1 = 1.$$
Then $M_t \cdot \infty = t/1 = t$.

Hence every element of $\mathbb{F}(p) \cup \{\infty\}$ lies in the orbit, so
$$\text{Orb}(\infty) = X, \qquad |\text{Orb}(\infty)| = p+1.$$

**Stabilizer of $\infty$.** A matrix fixes $\infty$ iff $c = 0$. With $c=0$ and
$ad-bc=ad=1$, we have $d = a^{-1}$ with $a \in \mathbb{F}(p)^{\times}$ (so $p-1$
 choices), and $b \in \mathbb{F}(p)$ is arbitrary ($p$ choices). Thus
$$\text{Stab}(\infty) = \left\{ \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ 0 & a^{-1} \end{pmatrix
} : a \in \mathbb{F}(p)^{\times},\ b \in \mathbb{F}(p) \right\}, \qquad |\text{S
tab}(\infty)| = (p-1)\,p.$$

**Order of $SL_2(p)$.** By the orbit–stabilizer theorem,
$$|SL_2(p)| = |\text{Orb}(\infty)| \cdot |\text{Stab}(\infty)| = (p+1)\cdot p(p-
1) = p(p^2-1) = p^3 - p.$$

$$\boxed{|SL_2(p)| = p(p^2-1) = p^3 - p}$$
