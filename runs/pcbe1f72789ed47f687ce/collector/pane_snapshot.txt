$$t^2 + 2p\cos\theta\, t + (p^2 - 1) = 0 \implies t = -p\cos\theta \pm \sqrt{1 -

Setting $\Delta = \sqrt{1 - p^2\sin^2\theta}$, the intersection points $C, D$ ha

$$y_C = (-p\cos\theta + \Delta)\sin\theta, \qquad y_D = (-p\cos\theta - \Delta)\
