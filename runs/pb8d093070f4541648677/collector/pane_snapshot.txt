## Setup

Define $f(x) = \sum_{i=1}^5 \frac{a_i}{x+i}$ and $g(x) = x \cdot f(x)$. The give
n conditions become $g(1)=1, g(4)=1, g(9)=1, g(16)=1, g(25)=1$.

Since $g(x) = \sum a_i \cdot \frac{x}{x+i} = \sum a_i\left(1 - \frac{i}{x+i}\rig
ht) = A - \sum_{i=1}^5 \frac{a_i i}{x+i}$, where $A = \sum a_i$, we can write $g
(x) = A - \frac{Q(x)}{P(x)}$ where $P(x) = \prod_{i=1}^5(x+i)$ and $Q(x) = \sum
a_i i \prod_{j\neq i}(x+j)$ has degree $\leq 4$.

## Key identity

Since $g(x)-1$ vanishes at $x=1,4,9,16,25$ and $\frac{g(x)-1}{1} = \frac{(A-1)P(
x)-Q(x)}{P(x)}$, the numerator (degree $\leq 5$) has 5 roots, so:

$$(A-1)P(x) - Q(x) = (A-1)R(x), \quad R(x)=(x-1)(x-4)(x-9)(x-16)(x-25)$$

Thus $Q(x) = (A-1)(P(x)-R(x))$ and $g(x) = 1 + \frac{(A-1)R(x)}{P(x)}$.
