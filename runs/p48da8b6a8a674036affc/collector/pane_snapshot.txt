## Proof

**Step 1: Simplification.** Let $p = abc$. Observe that
$$a - bc^2 = \frac{a^2 - abc^2}{a} = \frac{a^2 - cp}{a},$$
and cyclically $b - ca^2 = \frac{b^2 - ap}{b}$, $\ c - ab^2 = \frac{c^2 - bp}{c}
$.

Therefore
$$E = abc \cdot \frac{a^2-cp}{a}\cdot\frac{b^2-ap}{b}\cdot\frac{c^2-bp}{c} = (a^
2-cp)(b^2-ap)(c^2-bp).$$

Set $u = a^2 - cp$, $\ v = b^2 - ap$, $\ w = c^2 - bp$. The three stability cons
traints are exactly $u, v, w \geq 0$, so
$$E = uvw.$$

**Step 2: Key identity.** From the definitions:
$$a^2 = u + cp, \quad b^2 = v + ap, \quad c^2 = w + bp.$$

**Step 3: AM–GM bound.** Since all quantities are non-negative, apply AM–GM to e
ach:
$$a^2 = u + cp \geq 2\sqrt{u \cdot cp},$$
$$b^2 = v + ap \geq 2\sqrt{v \cdot ap},$$
$$c^2 = w + bp \geq 2\sqrt{w \cdot bp}.$$

Multiplying all three inequalities:
$$a^2 b^2 c^2 \geq 8\,\sqrt{uvw \cdot (cp)(ap)(bp)}.$$

Now $(cp)(ap)(bp) = (abc)\,p^3 = p \cdot p^3 = p^4$, and $a^2b^2c^2 = (abc)^2 =
p^2$. Substituting:
$$p^2 \geq 8\,\sqrt{uvw \cdot p^4} = 8\,p^2\sqrt{uvw}.$$

Since $p = abc > 0$, divide both sides by $p^2$:
$$1 \geq 8\sqrt{uvw} \implies \sqrt{uvw} \leq \frac{1}{8} \implies uvw \leq \fra
c{1}{64}.$$

Thus $\boxed{E \leq \frac{1}{64}}$.

**Step 4: Equality is attained.** Equality in AM–GM requires $u = cp$, $v = ap$,
 $w = bp$, i.e.,
$$a^2 = 2cp, \quad b^2 = 2ap, \quad c^2 = 2bp.$$

By the cyclic symmetry, take $a = b = c = s$. Then $s^2 = 2s \cdot s^3 = 2s^4$,
giving $s^2 = \tfrac{1}{2}$, i.e., $s = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$.

**Feasibility check:** The constraint $s \geq s \cdot s^2 = s^3$ becomes $1 \geq
 s^2 = \tfrac{1}{2}$ ✓, and all three constraints hold by symmetry.

**Value check:** $p = s^3 = \tfrac{1}{2\sqrt{2}}$, $\ u = s^2 - s^4 = \tfrac{1}{
2} - \tfrac{1}{4} = \tfrac{1}{4}$, and similarly $v = w = \tfrac{1}{4}$. Hence
$$E = uvw = \left(\tfrac{1}{4}\right)^3 = \tfrac{1}{64}.$$

The maximum possible value of the Waste Metric $E$ is $\dfrac{1}{64}$.
