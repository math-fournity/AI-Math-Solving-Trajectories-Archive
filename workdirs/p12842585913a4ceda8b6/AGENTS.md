# Problem

In a remote technology park, five specialized AI research firms are competing for government grants through a series of pairwise collaborative simulations. Each of the five firms must partner with every other firm exactly once to complete a single project.

The funding (points) for each project is distributed based on the outcome of the simulation:
- If one firm significantly outperforms the other, the winner is awarded 5 points and the loser receives 0.
- If the simulation results in a stalemate, the point distribution depends on whether any breakthroughs (goals) were achieved:
    - If the stalemate occurs with zero breakthroughs, both firms receive 1 point.
    - If the stalemate occurs with at least one breakthrough achieved by the partnership, both firms receive 2 points.

After all simulations were completed, the final point totals for the five firms were found to be five consecutive integers. Based on this outcome, determine the minimum total number of breakthroughs that could have been achieved across the entire tournament.
