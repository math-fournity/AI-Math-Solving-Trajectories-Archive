# Problem

In the high-stakes laboratory of Sector 7, a quality control engineer is testing a series of experimental microchips. Each chip produced has an equal $1/6$ probability of falling into one of six distinct performance categories, labeled $1$ through $6$.

The engineer tests the chips one by one in a sequence. He is strictly governed by two termination protocols:
1. He must stop the entire testing process immediately upon identifying the $20^{\text{th}}$ "Premium" chip. A chip is classified as "Premium" if its category label is a prime number.
2. Alternatively, he must stop the entire process immediately upon identifying the $2^{\text{nd}}$ "Stable" chip. A chip is classified as "Stable" if its category label is an even number.

The engineer continues testing chips until exactly one of these two conditions is met. Calculate the probability that the very last chip tested in the sequence was classified as a "Premium" chip.
