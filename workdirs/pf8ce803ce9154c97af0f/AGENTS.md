# Problem

In a circular research facility, 20 laboratories are arranged in a ring and numbered 1 through 20 in clockwise order. To travel between these labs, researchers use a system of corridors with strict movement protocols. From any lab $i$, a researcher can only move in two ways:
1. Walk to the adjacent lab in a clockwise direction (from lab $i$ to lab $i+1$, with lab 20 connecting back to lab 1).
2. Take a direct bridge across the center of the ring to the diametrically opposite lab (from lab $i$ to lab $i+10$ or vice-versa).

A technician currently in Lab 10 needs to deliver a sensitive sample to Lab 20. To prevent cross-contamination, the technician must follow a path that never enters the same laboratory more than once. 

How many distinct paths can the technician take to get from Lab 10 to Lab 20?
