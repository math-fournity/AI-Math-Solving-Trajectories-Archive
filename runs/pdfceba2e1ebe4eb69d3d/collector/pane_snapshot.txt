Likewise $FQ$ horizontal gives $Q$ on $BD$ at parameter $s$:
$$Q=(1-s)B+sD=((1-s)u+sd,\,(1-s)v).$$

The hypothesis $\angle BAE=\angle CAF$ reads $\alpha-\arg(E)=\arg(F)$, i.e.
$$\arg(E)+\arg(F)=\alpha. \tag{1}$$
The desired $\angle BAP=\angle CAQ$ reads $\alpha-\arg(P)=\arg(Q)$, i.e.
$$\arg(P)+\arg(Q)=\alpha. \tag{2}$$

**Key lemma.** For two points $X=(x_1,y_1)$, $Y=(x_2,y_2)$ with $\arg(X),\arg(Y)

**Apply to $(1)$.** With $z_E=((1-t)u+td)+i(1-t)v$ and $z_F=((1-s)u+sc)+i(1-s)v$

$$\operatorname{Im}(z_Ez_F)=v\bigl[\,2(1-t)(1-s)u+(1-s)td+(1-t)sc\,\bigr].$$

$$u\,\operatorname{Im}(z_Ez_F)-v\,\operatorname{Re}(z_Ez_F)=v\Bigl[(1-t)(1-s)(u^
