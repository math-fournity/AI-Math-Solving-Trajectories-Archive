# Problem

In a remote digital archipelago, there are four data hubs arranged in a perfect square circuit, indexed $1, 2, 3,$ and $4$ in clockwise order. A single packet of information begins its journey at Hub $1$. 

The transmission protocol follows a strict randomized sequence: for every jump the packet makes, there is a $50\%$ chance it is beamed to the hub located diagonally across from its current position. Otherwise, the packet is sent to one of the two directly adjacent hubs, with each adjacent hub having a $25\%$ chance of receiving the transmission.

The packet performs a total of $2019$ jumps through the network. After the final jump, network engineers calculate the probability that the numerical indices of the last two hubs visited (the $2018^{th}$ hub and the $2019^{th}$ hub) add up to exactly $3$.

If this probability is expressed in the form $2^{-m} - 2^{-n}$ for positive integers $m$ and $n$, find the value of $m+n$.
