# Problem

A specialized digital archive contains 10 encrypted files, organized into 5 distinct pairs where each pair shares a unique security key. A technician initiates a data retrieval process by randomly selecting a sample of 4 individual files from the archive.

The system is programmed with a specific filtering protocol: if the sample contains both files from any keyed pair, that pair is automatically synchronized and archived permanently (discarded from the sample). For every pair removed this way, the technician must draw 2 new individual files at random from the remaining files in the archive to maintain a set of 4 files. 

This cycle of checking for complete pairs, archiving them, and drawing replacements continues until one of two terminal states is reached: either the archive becomes completely empty, or the technician is left holding exactly 4 files that all belong to different security keys.

What is the probability that the process terminates with the technician holding 4 files of different colors?
