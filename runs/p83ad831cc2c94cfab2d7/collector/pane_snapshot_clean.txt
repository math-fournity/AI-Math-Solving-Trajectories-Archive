## Solution

**Yes, $\mathcal{A}_U$ is a maximal smooth atlas on $U$.**

We verify the three required properties: (1) $\mathcal{A}_U$ is a smooth atlas o
n $U$, and (2) it is maximal.

### $\mathcal{A}_U$ is a smooth atlas on $U$

**Covers $U$.** Let $p \in U$. Since $M$ carries a smooth structure (a maximal a
tlas $\mathcal{A}_M$), there is a chart $(W,\psi) \in \mathcal{A}_M$ with $p \in
 W$. The restriction $(W \cap U,\; \psi|_{W\cap U})$ is a chart of $M$: it is a
homeomorphism onto an open subset of $\mathbb{R}^n$, and it is smoothly compatib
le with $(W,\psi)$ (the transition map is the identity on $\psi(W\cap U)$), so b
y maximality of $\mathcal{A}_M$ it belongs to $\mathcal{A}_M$. Its domain $W \ca
p U \subset U$, so it lies in $\mathcal{A}_U$ and contains $p$. Hence $\mathcal{
A}_U$ covers $U$.

**Pairwise compatibility.** Every element of $\mathcal{A}_U$ is a chart of $M$,
hence of $\mathcal{A}_M$; elements of a smooth atlas are pairwise smoothly compa
tible. So $\mathcal{A}_U$ is a smooth atlas on $U$.

### $\mathcal{A}_U$ is maximal

Let $(V,\varphi)$ be a chart on $U$ (so $V \subset U$ open and $\varphi: V \to \
varphi(V)$ a homeomorphism onto an open subset of $\mathbb{R}^n$) that is smooth
ly compatible with every chart in $\mathcal{A}_U$. We show $(V,\varphi) \in \mat
hcal{A}_U$.

It suffices to show $(V,\varphi)$ is compatible with **every** chart of $M$, for
 then by maximality of $\mathcal{A}_M$ it belongs to $\mathcal{A}_M$, and since
$V \subset U$, it belongs to $\mathcal{A}_U$.

Let $(W,\psi) \in \mathcal{A}_M$ be any chart of $M$ (its domain $W$ need not li
e in $U$). Consider the restricted chart $(W \cap U,\; \psi|_{W\cap U})$. As abo
ve, this restriction belongs to $\mathcal{A}_M$, and its domain $W \cap U \subse
t U$, so it lies in $\mathcal{A}_U$. By hypothesis, $(V,\varphi)$ is compatible
with it:

$$\psi|_{W\cap U} \circ \varphi^{-1} : \varphi\bigl(V \cap (W \cap U)\bigr) \to
\psi(W \cap U) \quad \text{is smooth.}$$

Since $V \subset U$, we have $V \cap (W \cap U) = V \cap W$, and $\psi|_{W\cap U
}$ agrees with $\psi$ on $V \cap W \subset W \cap U$. Therefore

$$\psi \circ \varphi^{-1} : \varphi(V \cap W) \to \psi(V \cap W)$$

is smooth. The symmetric argument (using compatibility of $(V,\varphi)$ with the
 restriction of $(W,\psi)$, or simply noting compatibility is symmetric) gives t
hat $\varphi \circ \psi^{-1}$ is smooth on $\psi(V \cap W)$. Hence $(V,\varphi)$
 is smoothly compatible with $(W,\psi)$.

Since $(W,\psi) \in \mathcal{A}_M$ was arbitrary, $(V,\varphi)$ is compatible wi
th every chart of $M$'s maximal atlas, so $(V,\varphi) \in \mathcal{A}_M$ by max
imality. With $V \subset U$, this gives $(V,\varphi) \in \mathcal{A}_U$.

Thus every chart on $U$ compatible with all of $\mathcal{A}_U$ already lies in $
\mathcal{A}_U$; $\mathcal{A}_U$ is maximal. $\blacksquare$
