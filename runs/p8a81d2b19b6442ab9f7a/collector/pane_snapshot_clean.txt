Looking at this problem, I need to find the minimum cost to produce 3250 robots
in 10 hours, where machines make 1 robot/hour (cost: 100 + 70 per robot), and ro
bots self-replicate in 2 hours (cost: 100 per robot).

**Key Setup**

Total cost = 100 × (total robots created) + 70 × (machine-hours used).

To minimize cost, I need to: (1) create exactly 3250 robots (no waste), and (2)
minimize machine-hours M.

**Growth Model**

A robot created at time t produces copies at t+2, t+4, t+6, ... (one at a time,
each taking 2 hours), as long as completion time ≤ 10. Each copy independently p
roduces its own descendants.

Define N(t) = total robots generated (including itself) by a robot created at ti
me t, with deadline 10:

- N(10) = N(9) = 1 (no time for copies)
- N(8) = 1 + N(10) = 2
- N(7) = 1 + N(9) = 2
- N(6) = 1 + N(8) + N(10) = 4
- N(5) = 1 + N(7) + N(9) = 4
- N(4) = 1 + N(6) + N(8) + N(10) = 8
- N(3) = 1 + N(5) + N(7) + N(9) = 8
- N(2) = 1 + N(4) + N(6) + N(8) + N(10) = 16
- N(1) = 1 + N(3) + N(5) + N(7) + N(9) = 16

The pattern is N(t) = 2^⌊(10−t)/2⌋.

**Verification of N(1) = 16** by explicit count: robots created at times 1, 3, 5
, 7, 9 in counts 1, 1, 2, 4, 8 → total 16. ✓

**Optimization**

The earliest a machine can complete a robot is time 1 (running from hour 0 to 1)
, giving N(1) = 16 — the maximum yield per machine-hour. Since trees are indepen
dent, total robots = Σ N(tᵢ) over all machine-produced seeds.

With M seeds all at time 1: total = 16M. We need 16M ≥ 3250, so M ≥ ⌈3250/16⌉ =
⌈203.125⌉ = 204.

With M = 203: max total = 203 × 16 = 3248 < 3250. **Insufficient.**

With M = 204: 203 seeds at time 1 give 203 × 16 = 3248. We need exactly 2 more.
One seed at time 7 gives N(7) = 2. Total = 3248 + 2 = **3250 exactly**. ✓

All robots are completed within 10 hours:
- The 203 seeds at time 1 produce descendants at times 3, 5, 7, 9 (and deeper ge
nerations at 5, 7, 9, etc.), all ≤ 10. ✓
- The seed at time 7 produces one copy at time 9 ≤ 10. ✓

Machine-hours: 203 (machines running 0→1) + 1 (machine running 6→7) = 204.

**Cost Calculation**

- Robot creation cost: 100 × 3250 = 325,000
- Machine operation cost: 70 × 204 = 14,280
- **Total: 325,000 + 14,280 = 337,280 monetary units**

**Proof of minimality:** Each machine-hour yields at most N(1) = 16 robots (the
maximum over all feasible creation times). With 203 machine-hours, the maximum a
chievable is 203 × 16 = 3248 < 3250. Therefore at least 204 machine-hours are re
quired. We exhibited a feasible plan with exactly 204 machine-hours producing ex
actly 3250 robots, so this is optimal.

The minimum cost the company will incur is **337,280 monetary units**.
