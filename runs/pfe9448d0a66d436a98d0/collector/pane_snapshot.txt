## Setting Up the Factorization

Rewrite $8^{x^2-x+a^2} = 2^{3(x^2-x+a^2)}$. Let $u = 2^{x^2-x+a^2}$, so the firs

**Second term exponent:** $2x^2 - x + 2a^2 - a + 1 = 2(x^2-x+a^2) + (x-a) + 1$,
giving $u^2 \cdot 2^{x-a+1}$.

**Third term exponent:** $x^2 - 2x + a^2 + a + 2 = (x^2-x+a^2) + (a-x+2)$, givin

Setting $t = 2^{x-a}$, so $2^{x-a+1} = 2t$ and $2^{a-x+2} = 4/t$. The equation b

$$u^3 - 2t\,u^2 - \frac{4}{t}\,u + 8 = 0$$

Multiplying by $t$ and grouping:

$$tu^3 - 2t^2u^2 - 4u + 8t = (u-2t)(tu^2 - 4) = 0$$

- $a = -2$ or $a = 1$: **1 solution** $x = 1$
- Otherwise: **0 solutions**

## Case 2: $tu^2 = 4$
