## Proof

Using the given identity $\eta(s) = (1 - 2^{1-s})\zeta(s)$, which holds for $0 <
 \Re s < 1$, and noting that both $s = 1 - \tfrac{1}{p_n}$ and $s = 1 - \tfrac{1
}{n}$ lie in $(0,1)$ for large $n$, we may divide both sides by $\zeta(s)$:

$$\frac{\eta(s)}{\zeta(s)} = 1 - 2^{1-s}.$$

Substituting $s = 1 - \tfrac{1}{p_n}$ and $s = 1 - \tfrac{1}{n}$ respectively, t
he expression becomes

$$\frac{1 - 2^{1/p_n}}{1 - 2^{1/n}}.$$

**Asymptotics of the numerator and denominator.** For small $x \to 0$, we have $
2^x = e^{x\ln 2} = 1 + x\ln 2 + O(x^2)$, hence

$$1 - 2^x = -x\ln 2 + O(x^2).$$

Applying this with $x = 1/p_n$ (which $\to 0$ since $p_n \to \infty$) and $x = 1
/n$:

$$1 - 2^{1/p_n} = -\frac{\ln 2}{p_n} + O\!\left(\frac{1}{p_n^2}\right), \qquad 1
 - 2^{1/n} = -\frac{\ln 2}{n} + O\!\left(\frac{1}{n^2}\right).$$

Therefore

$$\frac{1 - 2^{1/p_n}}{1 - 2^{1/n}} = \frac{-\frac{\ln 2}{p_n}\big(1 + O(1/p_n)\
big)}{-\frac{\ln 2}{n}\big(1 + O(1/n)\big)} = \frac{n}{p_n}\cdot\frac{1 + O(1/p_
n)}{1 + O(1/n)}.$$

The correction factor $\to 1$, so the limit reduces to

$$\lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{n}{p_n}.$$

**Applying the Prime Number Theorem.** The PNT gives $p_n \sim n\ln n$, i.e.,

$$\frac{p_n}{n\ln n} \to 1 \quad\text{as } n\to\infty.$$

Consequently,

$$\frac{n}{p_n} \sim \frac{1}{\ln n} \to 0.$$

Hence

$$\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{\frac{\eta(1-\frac{1}{p_n})}{\zeta(1-\frac{1}{p_n})}
}{\frac{\eta(1-\frac{1}{n})}{\zeta(1-\frac{1}{n})}} = \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{n}{
p_n} = 0.$$
