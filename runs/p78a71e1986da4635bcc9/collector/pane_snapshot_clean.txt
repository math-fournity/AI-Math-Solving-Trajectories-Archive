## Solution

We compute the flux of \(F(x,y,z)=(x,y,z)\) across the cone \(x^{2}-y^{2}+z^{2}=
0\) with \(y\in[0,1]\).

**The surface.** Rearranging gives \(y^{2}=x^{2}+z^{2}\), so with \(y\ge 0\) the
 surface is the cone \(y=\sqrt{x^{2}+z^{2}}\), vertex at the origin, opening in
the \(+y\) direction, truncated at \(y=1\) where the cross-section is the circle
 \(x^{2}+z^{2}=1\).

**Closing the surface (Divergence Theorem).** Let \(S\) be the lateral cone surf
ace and \(D\) the disk cap \(x^{2}+z^{2}\le 1,\ y=1\). Together \(S\cup D\) boun
ds the solid cone
\[
\mathcal{V}=\{(x,y,z): 0\le y\le 1,\ x^{2}+z^{2}\le y^{2}\}.
\]
Since \(\nabla\cdot F = 1+1+1 = 3\), the Divergence Theorem gives
\[
\iint_{S\cup D} F\cdot n\,dS = \iiint_{\mathcal{V}} 3\,dV = 3\,\mathrm{Vol}(\mat
hcal{V}).
\]
The volume of a cone with base radius \(1\) and height \(1\) is \(\tfrac13\pi(1)
^{2}(1)=\tfrac{\pi}{3}\), so
\[
\iint_{S\cup D} F\cdot n\,dS = 3\cdot\frac{\pi}{3}=\pi.
\]

**Flux through the cap \(D\).** On \(D\), the outward unit normal is \(n=(0,1,0)
\) and \(y=1\), hence \(F\cdot n = y = 1\). Therefore
\[
\iint_{D} F\cdot n\,dS = \iint_{D} 1\,dA = \pi(1)^{2}=\pi.
\]

**Flux through the cone \(S\).** Subtracting,
\[
\iint_{S} F\cdot n\,dS = \pi - \pi = 0.
\]

**Direct verification.** Parameterize \(S\) by \((\theta,y)\mapsto r(\theta,y)=(
y\cos\theta,\,y,\,y\sin\theta)\), with \(\theta\in[0,2\pi]\), \(y\in[0,1]\). The
n
\[
r_{\theta}=(-y\sin\theta,0,y\cos\theta),\qquad r_{y}=(\cos\theta,1,\sin\theta),
\]
\[
r_{\theta}\times r_{y} = (-y\cos\theta,\; y,\; -y\sin\theta),
\]
which points in the outward direction (away from the cone's axis, with positive
\(y\)-component), matching \(\nabla(y-\sqrt{x^{2}+z^{2}})\) up to scaling. On th
e surface \(F=(y\cos\theta,y,y\sin\theta)\), so
\[
F\cdot(r_{\theta}\times r_{y}) = (y\cos\theta)(-y\cos\theta)+(y)(y)+(y\sin\theta
)(-y\sin\theta)
= y^{2}\bigl(1-\cos^{2}\theta-\sin^{2}\theta\bigr)=0.
\]
Hence
\[
\iint_{S}F\cdot n\,dS = \int_{0}^{1}\!\!\int_{0}^{2\pi} 0\,d\theta\,dy = 0.
\]

Both methods agree.

\[
\boxed{\displaystyle \iint_{S}F\cdot n\,dS = 0}
\]
