# Problem

A high-security digital vault contains 2013 distinct data sectors, indexed from 1 to 2013. A cybersecurity firm is designing a complex hierarchical encryption protocol involving nine specific data clusters, denoted as $S_1, S_2, \dots, S_9$. Each cluster must consist of a subset of the 2013 sectors.

The protocol requires a specific "nested-bridge" architecture for its security. For every integer $n$ in the set $\{1, 2, 3, 4\}$, the cluster $S_{2n}$ must act as a protective envelope for the clusters immediately preceding and following it. Mathematically, this means that for each such $n$, both $S_{2n-1}$ and $S_{2n+1}$ must be subsets of $S_{2n}$.

Let $N$ be the total number of different ways to assign sectors to these nine clusters such that all requirements are satisfied. Find the remainder when $N$ is divided by 1000.
