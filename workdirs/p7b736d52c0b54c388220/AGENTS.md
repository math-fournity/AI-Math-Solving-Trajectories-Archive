# Problem

A specialized architectural firm has been commissioned to design a modular pavilion shaped like a convex heptagon. The structure is defined by seven distinct perimeter walls, which we can label $W_1$ through $W_7$ in order around the perimeter.

To provide internal support, the lead engineer must install exactly four straight interior tension cables. These cables must satisfy two structural conditions:
1. The cables must be anchored only at the corners where the perimeter walls meet, and they must not cross one another. This configuration will effectively partition the interior space into exactly five triangular zones.
2. For safety and accessibility, every single one of the five triangular zones must be bounded by at least one of the original seven exterior perimeter walls.

How many different sets of four cables can the engineer choose to satisfy these specific structural requirements?
