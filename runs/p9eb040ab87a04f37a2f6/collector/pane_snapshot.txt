## Solution

Define the smooth map
$$f:\mathbb{R}^{n+1}\to\mathbb{R},\qquad f(x^1,\dots,x^{n+1})=(x^1)^2+\cdots+(x^
n)^2-(x^{n+1})^2.$$
Then $M_a=f^{-1}(a)$.

**Step 1. Critical values of $f$.** The gradient is
$$\nabla f=(2x^1,\dots,2x^n,-2x^{n+1}).$$
Thus $\nabla f=0$ iff $x^1=\cdots=x^n=x^{n+1}=0$, i.e. iff $x=0$. The unique cri
tical point is the origin, and $f(0)=0$. Hence the only critical value of $f$ is
 $0$; every $a\neq 0$ is a regular value.

**Step 2. The case $a\neq 0$.** By the Regular Value Theorem, since $a\neq 0$ is
 a regular value of the smooth submersion $f$ on $f^{-1}(a)$, the level set $M_a
=f^{-1}(a)$ is a smooth embedded submanifold of $\mathbb{R}^{n+1}$ of codimensio
n $1$ (hence dimension $n$).

**Step 3. The case $a=0$ is not a submanifold.** We show $M_0$ fails to be a sub
manifold at the origin.

Note $M_0=\{x:(x^1)^2+\cdots+(x^n)^2=(x^{n+1})^2\}$ is the double cone (light co
ne). It is connected, since the origin lies in the closure of both the upper nap
pe $C_+=\{x\in M_0:x^{n+1}>0\}$ and the lower nappe $C_-=\{x\in M_0:x^{n+1}<0\}$
.

*Subcase $n\geq 2$.* The punctured level set $M_0\setminus\{0\}=C_+\sqcup C_-$ h
as exactly two connected components (each nappe is diffeomorphic to $\mathbb{R}^
n\setminus\{0\}$, which is connected for $n\geq 2$). On the other hand, a basic
property of connected manifolds of dimension $\geq 2$ is that removing a single
point leaves them connected. Therefore $M_0$ cannot be a (connected) $n$-dimensi
onal submanifold of $\mathbb{R}^{n+1}$.

*Subcase $n=1$.* Here $M_0=\{(x^1,x^2):(x^1)^2=(x^2)^2\}$ is the union of the tw
o lines $x^1=\pm x^2$, crossing at the origin. Then $M_0\setminus\{0\}$ has $4$
connected components (four open rays). But removing one point from a connected $
1$-manifold produces at most $2$ components. Contradiction. Hence $M_0$ is not a
 $1$-dimensional submanifold.

In both cases $M_0$ is not a submanifold of $\mathbb{R}^{n+1}$.

**Conclusion.** Combining Steps 2 and 3,

$$\boxed{M_a\text{ is a submanifold of }\mathbb{R}^{n+1}\iff a\neq 0.}$$

For every $a\neq 0$, $M_a$ is a smooth hypersurface of dimension $n$ in $\mathbb
{R}^{n+1}$; for $a=0$ the level set is the double cone, which has a singularity
at the origin and is not a submanifold.
