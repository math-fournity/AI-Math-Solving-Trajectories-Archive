## Solution

Set coordinates with $A$ at the origin: $A=(0,0)$, $B=(25,0)$ (since $AE=8$, $BE
=17$, so $AB=25$), $C=(25,h)$, $D=(0,h)$, where $h=AD$ is the unknown height.

Then $E=(8,0)$ on $AB$, and $F=(22,h)$ on $CD$ (since $CF=3$ means $F$ is 3 unit
s from $C$ along $CD$, so $x_F=25-3=22$).

**Crease property.** Folding $B$ to $B'$ along crease $EF$ means $EF$ is the per
pendicular bisector of $\overline{BB'}$. Hence every point on $EF$ is equidistan
t from $B$ and $B'$.

**Finding $B'$.** Since $B'$ lies on edge $AD$, write $B'=(0,y)$. Point $E$ is o
n the crease, so $EB=EB'$:
$$EB=BE=17, \qquad EB'=\sqrt{(8-0)^2+(0-y)^2}=\sqrt{64+y^2}.$$
Setting equal: $64+y^2=289\Rightarrow y^2=225\Rightarrow y=15$. So $B'=(0,15)$.

**Finding $h$.** Point $F=(22,h)$ is on the crease, so $FB=FB'$:
$$FB=\sqrt{(22-25)^2+h^2}=\sqrt{9+h^2},$$
$$FB'=\sqrt{(22-0)^2+(h-15)^2}=\sqrt{484+(h-15)^2}=\sqrt{484+h^2-30h+225}=\sqrt{
h^2-30h+709}.$$
Setting equal:
$$9+h^2=h^2-30h+709 \Rightarrow 9=709-30h \Rightarrow 30h=700 \Rightarrow h=\fra
c{70}{3}.$$

**Perimeter.** The rectangle has dimensions $AB=25$ and $AD=\tfrac{70}{3}$:
$$P=2\!\left(25+\frac{70}{3}\right)=2\cdot\frac{145}{3}=\frac{290}{3}.$$

Since $\gcd(290,3)=1$, we have $m=290$, $n=3$, and
$$m+n=293.$$
