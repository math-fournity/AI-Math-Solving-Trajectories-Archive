$$y^3 + 2ay^2 - 4(a^2-b)y - 8a(a^2-b) = 0.$$

$$(y+2a)\bigl(y^2 - 4(a^2-b)\bigr) = 0.$$

bb{Q}$, and the resolvent cubic has **exactly one rational root**.
