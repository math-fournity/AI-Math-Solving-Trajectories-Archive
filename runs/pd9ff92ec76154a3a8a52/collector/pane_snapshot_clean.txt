## 3. The Parsimonious Classification Algorithm

### 3.1 Core Principle: Dimensional Reduction

**Every 3D intersection problem reduces to a 2D or 1D problem.** No genuine 3D i

- **Seg–Seg in 3D**: Two segments span at most a plane. If $d_{\text{eff}} = 3$,

o 2D.
- **Seg–Tri in 3D**: The segment crosses the triangle's plane at one point → red
uce to a **point-in-triangle** test (a 2D problem on the triangle's plane).
- **Tri–Tri in 3D**: Both triangles meet the intersection line $L$ of their plan
es in segments → reduce to a **1D interval intersection** on $L$.

### 3.2 Algorithm

  INPUT: objects s, t (each a segment or triangle) in R^d

  Collect all vertices V = vertices(s) ∪ vertices(t).

  ── Step 2: Dispatch by effective dimension ──
