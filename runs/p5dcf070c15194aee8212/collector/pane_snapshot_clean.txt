## Solution

**Setup.** An L-triomino (three unit squares in an L) has four orientations, cor
responding to rotations by 0°, 90°, 180°, 270°. I label the original orientation
 **R0**. Equivalently, each orientation is determined by which corner of its bou
nding 2×2 square is missing:

| Missing corner | Orientation |
|---|---|
| top-right | R0 |
| top-left | R90 |
| bottom-left | R180 |
| bottom-right | R270 |

**The subdivision pattern.** Place the original L with cells (0,0), (1,0), (0,1)
, missing (1,1). Scaling by 2 gives a region of 12 cells (three 2×2 blocks A, B,
 C, with block D missing). The standard tiling by four L-triominoes is:

- **Center piece** at the junction: occupies (1,1), (2,1), (1,2), missing (2,2)
→ **R0**.
- **Block A** remainder: (0,0), (1,0), (0,1), missing (1,1) → **R0**.
- **Block B** remainder: (2,0), (3,0), (3,1), missing (2,1) → **R90**.
- **Block C** remainder: (0,2), (0,3), (1,3), missing (1,2) → **R270**.

So each subdivision of an R0 piece produces: **2× R0, 1× R90, 1× R270, 0× R180**
.

Since the subdivision is a geometric operation commuting with rotation, the same
 relative pattern holds for any parent orientation: **2 children match the paren
t, one is rotated +90°, one is rotated −90° (270°)**.

**Recurrence.** Let $a_n, b_n, c_n, d_n$ count pieces of orientation R0, R90, R1
80, R270 after $n$ subdivisions, with $(a_0,b_0,c_0,d_0)=(1,0,0,0)$. The transit
ion is:

$$a_{n+1}=2a_n+b_n+d_n,\quad b_{n+1}=a_n+2b_n+c_n,\quad c_{n+1}=b_n+2c_n+d_n,\qu
ad d_{n+1}=a_n+c_n+2d_n.$$

**Symmetry reduction.** Since $b_0=d_0=0$ and the recurrences for $b$ and $d$ ar
e identical under swapping, $b_n=d_n=:e_n$ for all $n$. Setting $s_n=a_n+c_n$ an
d $f_n=a_n-c_n$:

- $f_{n+1}=a_{n+1}-c_{n+1}=2(a_n-c_n)=2f_n$, so $f_n=2^n$.
- $e_{n+1}=a_n+c_n+2e_n=s_n+2e_n$.
- Total $T_n=s_n+2e_n=4^n$, so $s_n=4^n-2e_n$.
- Substituting: $e_{n+1}=(4^n-2e_n)+2e_n=4^n$, giving $e_n=4^{n-1}$ for $n\ge1$.
- Then $s_n=4^n-2\cdot4^{n-1}=\frac{4^n}{2}$, and $a_n=\frac{s_n+f_n}{2}=\frac{4
^n/2+2^n}{2}=4^{n-1}+2^{n-1}$.

**Verification.**
- $n=1$: $a_1=4^0+2^0=2$. ✓ (Direct count: 2 of the 4 pieces are R0.)
- $n=2$: $a_2=4^1+2^1=6$. ✓ (Recurrence: $a_2=2(2)+1+1=6$; total $=16=4^2$.)

**Answer.** After 2005 subdivisions:

$$a_{2005}=4^{2004}+2^{2004}.$$
