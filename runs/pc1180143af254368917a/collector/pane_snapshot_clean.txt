## Proof

**Setup.** Cover $S^1$ by two open sets $U$ and $V$ chosen as follows. Let $U =
S^1 \setminus \{N\}$ and $V = S^1 \setminus \{S\}$, where $N$ and $S$ are two di
stinct points (the "north" and "south" poles). Then:

- $U$ and $V$ are each open arcs diffeomorphic to $\mathbb{R}$, hence **contract
ible**:
$$\mathcal{H}^0(U) \cong \mathbb{R},\quad \mathcal{H}^k(U) = 0\ (k \ge 1), \qqua
d \mathcal{H}^0(V) \cong \mathbb{R},\quad \mathcal{H}^k(V) = 0\ (k \ge 1).$$

- $U \cap V = S^1 \setminus \{N, S\}$ consists of **two disjoint open arcs** $W_
1, W_2$, each diffeomorphic to $\mathbb{R}$. Hence
$$\mathcal{H}^0(U \cap V) \cong \mathbb{R} \oplus \mathbb{R}, \qquad \mathcal{H}
^k(U \cap V) = 0\ (k \ge 1).$$

**Mayer–Vietoris sequence.** For the open cover $S^1 = U \cup V$, the Mayer–Viet
oris long exact sequence in de Rham cohomology reads
$$\cdots \to \mathcal{H}^k(S^1) \xrightarrow{r^*\oplus s^*} \mathcal{H}^k(U)\opl
us \mathcal{H}^k(V) \xrightarrow{\delta} \mathcal{H}^k(U\cap V) \xrightarrow{d^*

where $r^*, s^*$ are restrictions and $\delta(f,g) = f|_{U\cap V} - g|_{U\cap V}

**The low-degree portion.** Substituting the computed cohomologies, the relevant

$$0 \to \mathcal{H}^0(S^1) \xrightarrow{\alpha} \mathcal{H}^0(U)\oplus\mathcal{H
}^0(V) \xrightarrow{\delta} \mathcal{H}^0(U\cap V) \xrightarrow{d^*} \mathcal{H}
^1(S^1) \to \mathcal{H}^1(U)\oplus\mathcal{H}^1(V) \to \cdots$$
i.e.
$$0 \to \mathcal{H}^0(S^1) \xrightarrow{\alpha} \mathbb{R}\oplus\mathbb{R} \xrig
htarrow{\delta} \mathbb{R}\oplus\mathbb{R} \xrightarrow{d^*} \mathcal{H}^1(S^1)
\to 0 \to 0.$$

**Identifying $\mathcal{H}^0(S^1)$.** Since $S^1$ is connected, every closed $0$

 \mathbb{R}$. The map $\alpha$ sends a constant $c \in \mathbb{R}$ to $(c|_U, c|
_V) = (c, c) \in \mathbb{R}\oplus\mathbb{R}$; this is the diagonal embedding, **
injective**, with image
$$\operatorname{im}\alpha = \{(c,c) : c\in\mathbb{R}\} \cong \mathbb{R}, \qquad
\dim = 1.$$

**Computing $\delta$.** A class in $\mathcal{H}^0(U)\oplus\mathcal{H}^0(V)$ is a
 pair of constants $(a, b)$. Its image under $\delta$ is the restriction of $a -
 b$ to each component of $U\cap V$:
$$\delta(a, b) = (a - b,\; a - b) \in \mathbb{R}\oplus\mathbb{R},$$
where the two entries are the values on $W_1$ and $W_2$ respectively (the consta
nt $a$ on $U$ restricts to $a$ on both $W_1, W_2$, and likewise for $b$). Thus
$$\operatorname{im}\delta = \{(c, c) : c \in \mathbb{R}\} \cong \mathbb{R}, \qqu
ad \dim = 1.$$

**Exactness check (consistency).** By exactness at $\mathcal{H}^0(U)\oplus\mathc
al{H}^0(V)$:
$$\ker\delta = \operatorname{im}\alpha.$$
Indeed $\ker\delta = \{(a,b) : a = b\} = \{(c,c)\} = \operatorname{im}\alpha$. ✓

**Extracting $\mathcal{H}^1(S^1)$.** By exactness at $\mathcal{H}^0(U\cap V)$:
$$\ker d^* = \operatorname{im}\delta = \{(c,c)\} \subset \mathbb{R}\oplus\mathbb
{R}.$$
By exactness at $\mathcal{H}^1(S^1)$, since $\mathcal{H}^1(U)\oplus\mathcal{H}^1

l{H}^1(S^1)$, and this kernel equals $\operatorname{im}d^*$. Hence $d^*$ is **su
rjective**:
$$\operatorname{im}d^* = \mathcal{H}^1(S^1).$$

em gives
$$\mathcal{H}^1(S^1) \;\cong\; \frac{\mathcal{H}^0(U\cap V)}{\ker d^*} \;=\; \fr
ac{\mathbb{R}\oplus\mathbb{R}}{\operatorname{im}\delta} \;=\; \frac{\mathbb{R}\o
plus\mathbb{R}}{\{(c,c) : c\in\mathbb{R}\}}.$$

**Dimension count.** The quotient of $\mathbb{R}^2$ (dimension $2$) by a $1$-dim

$$\varphi : \mathbb{R}\oplus\mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}, \qquad (x, y) \mapsto x -

$$\boxed{\;\mathcal{H}^1(S^1) \;\cong\; \frac{\mathcal{H}^0(U\cap V)}{\operatorn
