## Solution

**Key observation:** The adjacency condition (sharing a face, edge, **or** corne
r) means two unit cubes are adjacent whenever their positions differ by at most
1 in each coordinate — i.e., their Chebyshev distance is 1.

**Clique argument:** Consider any 2×2×2 sub-block of the 4×4×4 grid, for instanc
e the 8 cubes at positions $(i,j,k)$ with $i,j,k \in \{1,2\}$. Pick any two dist
inct cubes from this block; they differ by at most 1 in each coordinate, so they
 share at least a corner and are therefore adjacent. Thus these 8 cubes form a *
*clique of size 8** in the adjacency graph.

**Chromatic number lower bound:** A clique of size 8 requires at least 8 distinc
t colors for a proper coloring. Since only 6 colors are available, **no valid co
loring exists** — it is impossible to properly color this clique, let alone the
entire grid.

**Conclusion:** The number of valid colorings $M = 0$. The condition that all 6
colors are used at least once is vacuously unsatisfiable. Therefore:

$$M \bmod 1000 = 0 \bmod 1000 = \boxed{0}$$
