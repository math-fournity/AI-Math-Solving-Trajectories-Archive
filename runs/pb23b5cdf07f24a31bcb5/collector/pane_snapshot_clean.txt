The oriented bordism group in dimension 4 is

$$\Omega_4^{SO} \cong \mathbb{Z},$$

generated by the bordism class $[\mathbb{CP}^2]$.

## Proof

**Step 1: The signature is a bordism invariant.**

For a closed oriented $4k$-manifold $M$, the **signature** $\sigma(M)$ is the si
gnature of the nondegenerate symmetric bilinear form
$$Q_M: H^{2k}(M;\mathbb{R}) \times H^{2k}(M;\mathbb{R}) \to \mathbb{R}, \quad Q_
M(\alpha,\beta) = \langle \alpha \smile \beta, [M]\rangle.$$

**Novikov additivity** states that if a closed $4k$-manifold $M$ is split as $M
= M_1 \cup_\Sigma M_2$ along a $({4k-1})$-manifold $\Sigma$, then $\sigma(M) = \
sigma(M_1) + \sigma(M_2)$. A standard consequence (applied to a collar decomposi
tion of a bounding manifold) is:

> If $M = \partial W$ for a compact oriented $(4k{+}1)$-manifold $W$, then $\sig
ma(M) = 0$.

Indeed, writing $W = M \times [0,\tfrac{1}{2}] \cup W'$ and using additivity, to
gether with the long exact sequence of the pair, one shows the intersection form
 of $M$ is metabolic (has a Lagrangian), hence has signature $0$.

Therefore $\sigma$ descends to a group homomorphism
$$\sigma: \Omega_{4k}^{SO} \longrightarrow \mathbb{Z}.$$

**Step 2: $\sigma$ is surjective.**

For $M = \mathbb{CP}^2$, the intersection form on $H^2(\mathbb{CP}^2;\mathbb{R})
 = \mathbb{R}$ is $Q(h,h) = \langle h^2, [\mathbb{CP}^2]\rangle = 1$, which is p
ositive definite of rank 1. Hence
$$\sigma(\mathbb{CP}^2) = 1.$$
So $\sigma$ is surjective, and $[\mathbb{CP}^2]$ generates a free $\mathbb{Z}$-s
ummand of $\Omega_4^{SO}$.

**Step 3: Thom's structure theorem for $\Omega_*^{SO}$.**

Thom (1954) computed the oriented bordism ring. The key results are:

- **(Rational structure)** There is an isomorphism of graded $\mathbb{Q}$-algebr
as
$$\Omega_*^{SO} \otimes \mathbb{Q} \;\cong\; \mathbb{Q}\big[\mathbb{CP}^2,\; \ma
thbb{CP}^4,\; \mathbb{CP}^6,\; \ldots\big],$$
a polynomial algebra with one generator in each dimension $4k$, $k \geq 1$.

- **(Torsion)** The torsion subgroup of $\Omega_*^{SO}$ consists entirely of $2$
-torsion, and the first nonzero torsion occurs in dimension $5$ (generated by th
e Wu manifold $SU(3)/SO(3)$). In particular, $\Omega_n^{SO}$ is torsion-free for
 $n \leq 4$.

From the rational structure, $\Omega_4^{SO} \otimes \mathbb{Q} \cong \mathbb{Q}$
, so $\Omega_4^{SO}$ has **rank 1**. From the torsion statement, $\Omega_4^{SO}$
 is **torsion-free**. Since Thom also proved each $\Omega_n^{SO}$ is finitely ge
nerated, we conclude
$$\Omega_4^{SO} \cong \mathbb{Z}.$$

**Step 4: The signature is an isomorphism.**

We have a surjective homomorphism $\sigma: \Omega_4^{SO} \to \mathbb{Z}$ with $\
Omega_4^{SO} \cong \mathbb{Z}$. Under any isomorphism $\Omega_4^{SO} \cong \math
bb{Z}$, the map $\sigma$ becomes multiplication by some integer $d$. Surjectivit
y forces $d = \pm 1$, and since $\sigma([\mathbb{CP}^2]) = 1 > 0$, we have $d =
1$. Therefore $\sigma$ is an isomorphism, and

$$\boxed{\Omega_4^{SO} \cong \mathbb{Z}, \quad \text{generated by } [\mathbb{CP}
^2].}$$
