## Proof

unity, and let
$$\alpha = 2\cos\!\left(\frac{2\pi}{p}\right) = \zeta + \zeta^{-1}.$$

$$\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q}) \cong (\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})^{\times} \cong
 \mathbb{Z}/(p-1)\mathbb{Z},$$
which is cyclic of order $p-1$. Each automorphism is of the form $\sigma_a : \ze
ta \mapsto \zeta^a$ for $a \in (\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})^{\times}$.

**Step 2: Identify the stabilizer of $\alpha$.**

Since $\alpha = \zeta + \zeta^{-1}$, we have $\sigma_a(\alpha) = \zeta^a + \zeta
^{-a}$. This equals $\alpha$ if and only if $\{a, -a\} = \{1, -1\}$ as multisets

$$\operatorname{Stab}(\alpha) = \{\sigma_1, \sigma_{-1}\} = \langle \sigma_{-1}\
rangle \cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}.$$
The automorphism $\sigma_{-1}$ is complex conjugation (since $\overline{\zeta} =
 \zeta^{-1}$).

$$[\mathbb{Q}(\alpha):\mathbb{Q}] = \frac{[K:\mathbb{Q}]}{[K:\mathbb{Q}(\alpha)]

The group $\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q})$ is abelian, so every subgroup is no

**Step 5: Compute the Galois group.**

By the Galois correspondence,
$$\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}(\alpha)/\mathbb{Q}) \cong \operatorname{Gal}(K/\

$$(\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})^{\times}\,/\,\{\pm 1\} \cong \mathbb{Z}/\tfrac{p-1}{2
