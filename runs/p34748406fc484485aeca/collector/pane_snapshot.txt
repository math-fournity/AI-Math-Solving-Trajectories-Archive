Since both $k \geq 1$ and $p \geq 2$ give $k < pk+1$ and $p < pk+1$, the recurre

- $p=3$: $a_4 = 3\alpha - 3a_3 + 13 = -3\alpha + 9\beta - 26$.

Using $p=2, k=2$ (so $n=5$): $a_5 = 2a_2 - 3a_2 + 13 = -\beta + 13$.

$$p\,a_k - 3\,a_p + 13 = p(k+3) - 3(p+3) + 13 = pk + 3p - 3p - 9 + 13 = pk + 4.
