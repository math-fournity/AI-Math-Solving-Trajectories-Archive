## Proof

**Step 1: Asymptotic analysis of the sum.**

Let $T_n = \sum_{k=1}^{n-1} \frac{1}{k^{2019}(n-k)^{2019}}$. By the substitution
 $j = n-k$, the sum is symmetric: $T_n = \sum_{j=1}^{n-1} \frac{1}{(n-j)^{2019}j
^{2019}} = T_n$.

Split the sum at the midpoint. For $n$ even (the odd case is analogous):
$$T_n = 2\sum_{k=1}^{n/2 - 1} \frac{1}{k^{2019}(n-k)^{2019}} + \frac{1}{(n/2)^{4
038}}.$$

The middle term is $\frac{2^{4038}}{n^{4038}} = O(n^{-4038})$, negligible. More
generally, the "interior" terms where $k$ is comparable to $n$ are tiny: if $k \
in [\epsilon n, (1-\epsilon)n]$, each term is $O(n^{-4038})$ and there are $O(n)
$ of them, contributing $O(n^{-4037})$.

**Step 2: Dominated convergence for the boundary terms.**

Write:
$$\sum_{k=1}^{\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor} \frac{1}{k^{2019}(n-k)^{2019}} = \frac{1}{n
^{2019}} \sum_{k=1}^{\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor} \frac{1}{k^{2019}(1-k/n)^{2019}}.$$

For $k \leq \lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor$, we have $1 - k/n \geq 1/2$, so $\frac{1}{(1-
k/n)^{2019}} \leq 2^{2019}$. Thus each summand satisfies:
$$\frac{1}{k^{2019}(1-k/n)^{2019}} \leq \frac{2^{2019}}{k^{2019}},$$
and $\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{2^{2019}}{k^{2019}} < \infty$ since $2019 > 1$.

For each fixed $k$, $\frac{1}{(1-k/n)^{2019}} \to 1$ as $n \to \infty$. By the *
*Dominated Convergence Theorem** (applied to counting measure):
$$\lim_{n\to\infty} \sum_{k=1}^{\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor} \frac{1}{k^{2019}(1-k/n)^
{2019}} = \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{k^{2019}} = \zeta(2019).$$

**Step 3: Asymptotic formula.**

Combining Steps 1 and 2:
$$T_n = \frac{2\,\zeta(2019)}{n^{2019}} + O(n^{-4037}) + o(n^{-2019}) = \frac{2\
,\zeta(2019)}{n^{2019}}\,(1 + o(1)).$$

Therefore:
$$S_n(a) = n^a \, T_n \sim \frac{2\,\zeta(2019)}{n^{2019 - a}}.$$

**Step 4: Determining $a$.**

- **If $a < 2019$:** $2019 - a > 0$, so $S_n(a) \sim \frac{2\zeta(2019)}{n^{2019
-a}} \to 0$. The limit is not positive. ✗

- **If $a = 2019$:** $S_n(2019) \to 2\,\zeta(2019)$. Since $\zeta(2019) = \sum_{
k=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{k^{2019}} > 1 > 0$, the limit is the positive real $2\zet
a(2019)$. ✓

- **If $a > 2019$:** $2019 - a < 0$, so $S_n(a) \sim 2\zeta(2019)\cdot n^{a-2019
} \to +\infty$. The sequence diverges. ✗

**Conclusion.** The unique value of $a$ for which $\{S_n(a)\}_{n \geq 2}$ conver
ges to a positive real is:

$$\boxed{a = 2019}$$
