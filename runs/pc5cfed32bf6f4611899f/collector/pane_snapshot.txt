Looking at this problem, I need to find the minimum area of hexagon ABCDEF where
 diagonals AD, BE, CF are concurrent at O, with [AOB]=4, [COD]=6, [EOF]=9.

## Setup

The six triangles around O partition the hexagon: AOB, BOC, COD, DOE, EOF, FOA.
Let their areas be $a, b, c, d, e, f$ respectively, where $a=4$, $c=6$, $e=9$. T
he hexagon area is $S = a+b+c+d+e+f = 19 + b + d + f$.

**Key observation (vertically opposite angles):** Since A, O, D are collinear; B
, O, E are collinear; C, O, F are collinear, the angles at O satisfy:
$$\angle AOB = \angle DOE = \alpha, \quad \angle BOC = \angle EOF = \beta, \quad
 \angle COD = \angle FOA = \gamma$$
with $\alpha + \beta + \gamma = \pi$.

Let $OA = p$, $OD = q$, $OB = r$, $OE = s$, $OC = t$, $OF = u$. Then:
- $a = \frac{1}{2}pr\sin\alpha = 4$
- $b = \frac{1}{2}rt\sin\beta$
- $c = \frac{1}{2}tq\sin\gamma = 6$
- $d = \frac{1}{2}qs\sin\alpha$
- $e = \frac{1}{2}su\sin\beta = 9$
- $f = \frac{1}{2}up\sin\gamma$

## The Product Constraint

Compute the product $b \cdot d \cdot f$:
$$b \cdot d \cdot f = \frac{1}{8}(rt\sin\beta)(qs\sin\alpha)(up\sin\gamma) = \fr
ac{1}{8}(pqrtsu)(\sin\alpha\,\sin\beta\,\sin\gamma)$$

Compute the product $a \cdot c \cdot e$:
$$a \cdot c \cdot e = \frac{1}{8}(pr\sin\alpha)(tq\sin\gamma)(su\sin\beta) = \fr
ac{1}{8}(pqrtsu)(\sin\alpha\,\sin\beta\,\sin\gamma)$$

Both products contain the same factors rearranged, so:
$$b \cdot d \cdot f = a \cdot c \cdot e = 4 \cdot 6 \cdot 9 = 216$$

## Applying AM-GM

By the AM-GM inequality:
$$b + d + f \geq 3\sqrt[3]{bdf} = 3\sqrt[3]{216} = 3 \cdot 6 = 18$$

Therefore:
$$S = 19 + b + d + f \geq 19 + 18 = 37$$

## Equality is Achievable

Equality holds when $b = d = f = 6$. We construct an explicit convex hexagon ach
ieving this.

Set $\alpha = \gamma = \pi/4$, $\beta = \pi/2$, and $p = q = 4$. Then:
- $r = \frac{8}{p\sin\alpha} = \frac{8}{4\cdot\sqrt{2}/2} = 2\sqrt{2}$
- $s = \frac{12}{p\sin\alpha} = 3\sqrt{2}$, $t = u = \frac{12}{p\sin\gamma} = 3\
sqrt{2}$

Placing O at the origin with rays at angles $0, \frac{\pi}{4}, \frac{3\pi}{4}, \
pi, \frac{5\pi}{4}, \frac{7\pi}{4}$:

$$A=(4,0),\ B=(2,2),\ C=(-3,3),\ D=(-4,0),\ E=(-3,-3),\ F=(3,-3)$$

**Verification of areas:**
- $[AOB] = \frac{1}{2}|4\cdot2 - 0\cdot2| = 4$ ✓
- $[BOC] = \frac{1}{2}|2\cdot3 - 2\cdot(-3)| = 6$ ✓
- $[COD] = \frac{1}{2}|(-3)\cdot0 - 3\cdot(-4)| = 6$ ✓
- $[DOE] = \frac{1}{2}|(-4)(-3) - 0\cdot(-3)| = 6$ ✓
- $[EOF] = \frac{1}{2}|(-3)(-3)-(-3)(3)| = 9$ ✓
- $[FOA] = \frac{1}{2}|3\cdot0-(-3)\cdot4| = 6$ ✓

**Convexity check:** All consecutive edge cross products are positive ($8, 16, 6
, 18, 18, 8$), confirming the hexagon is convex.

**Total area** $= 4+6+6+6+9+6 = 37$.

## Conclusion

The minimum area of the hexagon is $\boxed{37}$.
