## Solution

The point $(1,1)$ on $Y = \operatorname{Spec}\!\left(\mathbb{Z}[x,y]/(x^3-y^2)\r
ight)$ corresponds to the prime ideal
$$\mathfrak{p} = (x-1,\, y-1)/(x^3-y^2) \;\subset\; A := \mathbb{Z}[x,y]/(x^3-y^
2).$$

**Validity check.** The point lies on the curve since $1^3 - 1^2 = 0$, so $x^3 -
 y^2 \in (x-1, y-1)$ and the ideal $(x-1,y-1)$ is prime in $\mathbb{Z}[x,y]$ (th
e quotient is $\mathbb{Z}$, an integral domain). Hence $\mathfrak{p}$ is indeed
a prime ideal of $A$.

**Residue field.** For an affine scheme $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ at a prime $\ma
thfrak{p}$, the residue field is
$$\kappa(\mathfrak{p}) = A_{\mathfrak{p}}/\mathfrak{p}A_{\mathfrak{p}} \cong \op
eratorname{Frac}(A/\mathfrak{p}).$$

Compute the quotient:
$$A/\mathfrak{p} \;=\; \frac{\mathbb{Z}[x,y]/(x^3-y^2)}{(x-1,y-1)/(x^3-y^2)} \;\
cong\; \mathbb{Z}[x,y]/(x^3-y^2,\, x-1,\, y-1).$$

Since $x^3 - y^2 \in (x-1, y-1)$, the ideal $(x^3-y^2,\, x-1,\, y-1)$ equals $(x
-1, y-1)$. Therefore
$$A/\mathfrak{p} \;\cong\; \mathbb{Z}[x,y]/(x-1,\, y-1) \;\cong\; \mathbb{Z}.$$

Taking the fraction field:
$$\kappa(\mathfrak{p}) \;=\; \operatorname{Frac}(\mathbb{Z}) \;=\; \mathbb{Q}.$$

**The residue field of $Y$ at the point $(1,1)$ is $\mathbb{Q}$.**
