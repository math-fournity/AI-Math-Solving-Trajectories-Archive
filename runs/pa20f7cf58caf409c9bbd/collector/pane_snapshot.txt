## Solution via the Fourier Method

**Step 1. Sine-series expansion.** Since $u(0,t)=u(\pi,t)=0$, we expand
$$u(x,t)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}T_n(t)\sin(nx).$$

**Step 2. Expand the forcing.** $\sin t$ (independent of $x$) has sine coefficie
nts
$$f_n(t)=\frac{2}{\pi}\sin t\int_0^\pi\sin(nx)\,dx=\frac{2}{\pi}\sin t\cdot\frac
{1-(-1)^n}{n}.$$
Hence $f_n=0$ for $n$ even, and $f_n=\dfrac{4}{n\pi}\sin t$ for $n$ odd.

**Step 3. Initial-data coefficients.**
- $u(x,0)=\sin x$: $T_1(0)=1$, $T_n(0)=0$ for $n\neq 1$.
- $u_t(x,0)=x(x-\pi)=x^2-\pi x$:
$$g_n=\frac{2}{\pi}\int_0^\pi(x^2-\pi x)\sin(nx)\,dx.$$
Using $\int_0^\pi x\sin(nx)dx=-\pi(-1)^n/n$ and $\int_0^\pi x^2\sin(nx)dx=-\pi^2
(-1)^n/n+2((-1)^n-1)/n^3$, the $\pi^2$ terms cancel:
$$g_n=\frac{2}{\pi}\cdot\frac{2((-1)^n-1)}{n^3}=\frac{4}{\pi n^3}((-1)^n-1).$$
So $g_n=0$ for $n$ even, and $g_n=-\dfrac{8}{\pi n^3}$ for $n$ odd.

**Step 4. ODEs for $T_n$.** Substitution gives
$$T_n''+n^2 T_n=f_n(t).$$
For $n$ even: $f_n=0$, $T_n(0)=T_n'(0)=0$, so $T_n\equiv 0$.

**Case $n=1$ (resonance).** $T_1''+T_1=\dfrac{4}{\pi}\sin t$. Since $\sin t$ sol
ves the homogeneous equation, take $T_p=A\,t\cos t$:
$$T_p''+T_p=-2A\sin t=\frac{4}{\pi}\sin t\;\Longrightarrow\;A=-\frac{2}{\pi}.$$
General solution:
$$T_1=C_1\cos t+C_2\sin t-\frac{2}{\pi}t\cos t.$$
$T_1(0)=C_1=1$. $T_1'(0)=C_2-\dfrac{2}{\pi}=g_1=-\dfrac{8}{\pi}$, so $C_2=-\dfra
c{6}{\pi}$. Thus
$$T_1(t)=\cos t-\frac{6}{\pi}\sin t-\frac{2}{\pi}\,t\cos t.$$

**Case $n$ odd, $n\ge 3$ (non-resonant).** $T_n''+n^2 T_n=\dfrac{4}{n\pi}\sin t$

$$T_p=\frac{4}{n\pi(n^2-1)}\sin t.$$
General solution with $T_n(0)=0$:
$$T_n(t)=B_n\sin(nt)+\frac{4}{n\pi(n^2-1)}\sin t.$$
$T_n'(0)=nB_n+\dfrac{4}{n\pi(n^2-1)}=g_n=-\dfrac{8}{\pi n^3}$, giving
$$B_n=-\frac{8}{\pi n^4}-\frac{4}{\pi n^2(n^2-1)}.$$
