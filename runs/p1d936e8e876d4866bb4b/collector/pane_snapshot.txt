## Proof

Let $A = k[t]$, which is an integral domain with fraction field $K = k(t)$. The
scheme $X = \operatorname{Spec}(A) = \mathbb{A}^1_k$ is integral, with generic p
oint $\eta$ corresponding to the prime ideal $(0)$.

The coherent sheaf $\widetilde{k[t]/(t)}$ is the sheaf associated to the finitel
y generated $A$-module $M = k[t]/(t)$.

**Definition of rank.** For a coherent sheaf $\mathcal{F} = \widetilde{M}$ on an
 integral scheme $X$ with generic point $\eta$, the rank is
$$\operatorname{rank}(\mathcal{F}) = \dim_K(\mathcal{F}_\eta) = \dim_K(M \otimes
_A K),$$
where $K = \operatorname{Frac}(A)$ is the residue field at $\eta$.

**Computation.** We compute:
$$M \otimes_A K = \left(k[t]/(t)\right) \otimes_{k[t]} k(t) \cong k(t) / (t \cdo
t k(t)).$$

Since $t \in k(t)$ is a unit (it is invertible in the fraction field), the ideal
 $t \cdot k(t)$ is all of $k(t)$. Therefore:
$$k(t)/(t \cdot k(t)) = k(t)/k(t) = 0.$$

Hence:
$$\operatorname{rank}\!\left(\widetilde{k[t]/(t)}\right) = \dim_{k(t)}(0) = 0.$$

**Conceptual check.** The module $k[t]/(t) \cong k$ is a torsion $k[t]$-module:
every element $a \in k \cong k[t]/(t)$ is annihilated by $t$ (since $t \cdot a =
 0$ in the quotient). A finitely generated module over a domain has rank equal t
o the dimension of its free part; a torsion module has rank $0$. Equivalently, t
he support of $\widetilde{k[t]/(t)}$ is the closed point $V(t) = \{(t)\}$, which
 is a proper closed subset of $\mathbb{A}^1_k$ not containing the generic point,
 so the stalk at the generic point is zero.

$$\boxed{\operatorname{rank}\!\left(\widetilde{k[t]/(t)}\right) = 0}$$
