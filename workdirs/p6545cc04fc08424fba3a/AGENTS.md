# Problem

In a remote observation station, an experimental sensor records a signal frequency $x$ measured in radians, where the allowable safety range for the frequency is restricted to the interval $[0, 2\pi]$.

The station is testing a series of power-amplification phases. In each discrete phase $n$ (where $n$ can be any positive integer $1, 2, 3, \dots$), the original signal $x$ is doubled exactly $n$ times before its oscillation is measured. Specifically, for every phase $n$, the system calculates a stability coefficient equal to the cosine of the resulting frequency, $\cos(2^n x)$.

For the system to remain stable across an infinite timeline, a "Negative Pressure Constraint" must be satisfied: the stability coefficient must be strictly negative for every single phase $n$ in the infinite sequence of integers.

Find the sum of all specific signal frequencies $x$ within the safety range that satisfy this infinite stability constraint.
