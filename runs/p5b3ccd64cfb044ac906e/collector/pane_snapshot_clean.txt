## Solution

**Setup.** Since $b$ is the arithmetic mean of $a$ and $c$, we have $b = \frac{a
+c}{2}$, i.e., $a+c = 2b$, so $c = 2b - a$. We require $a, b, c \neq 0$ (so that
 the reciprocals are defined).

We need at least one of $\frac1a, \frac1b, \frac1c$ to be the arithmetic mean of
 the other two. There are three cases.

---

**Case 1: $\frac1a = \frac12\!\left(\frac1b + \frac1c\right)$.**

This gives $\frac{2}{a} = \frac{b+c}{bc}$, hence $2bc = a(b+c)$. Substituting $c
 = 2b-a$:

$$2b(2b-a) = a(b + 2b - a) \;\Longrightarrow\; 4b^2 - 2ab = 3ab - a^2 \;\Longrig
htarrow\; a^2 - 5ab + 4b^2 = 0.$$

Factoring: $(a - b)(a - 4b) = 0$, giving $a = b$ or $a = 4b$.

- $a = b \Rightarrow c = b$: the trivial solution $(t, t, t)$.
- $a = 4b \Rightarrow c = -2b$: the family $(4t,\, t,\, -2t)$.

**Verification of $(4t, t, -2t)$:** $\frac{1}{4t} = \frac12\!\left(\frac1t + \fr
ac{1}{-2t}\right) = \frac12\cdot\frac{1}{2t} = \frac{1}{4t}.$ ✓

---

**Case 2: $\frac1b = \frac12\!\left(\frac1a + \frac1c\right)$.**

This gives $\frac{2}{b} = \frac{a+c}{ac} = \frac{2b}{ac}$, hence $ac = b^2$. Sub
stituting $c = 2b - a$:

$$a(2b - a) = b^2 \;\Longrightarrow\; a^2 - 2ab + b^2 = 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; (a
-b)^2 = 0.$$

So $a = b$, giving only the trivial solution $(t, t, t)$.

---

**Case 3: $\frac1c = \frac12\!\left(\frac1a + \frac1b\right)$.**

This gives $\frac{2}{c} = \frac{a+b}{ab}$, hence $2ab = c(a+b)$. Substituting $c
 = 2b - a$:

$$2ab = (2b-a)(a+b) = 2ab + 2b^2 - a^2 - ab \;\Longrightarrow\; a^2 + ab - 2b^2
= 0.$$

Factoring: $(a + 2b)(a - b) = 0$, giving $a = b$ (trivial) or $a = -2b$.

- $a = -2b \Rightarrow c = 4b$: the family $(-2t,\, t,\, 4t)$.

**Verification of $(-2t, t, 4t)$:** $\frac{1}{4t} = \frac12\!\left(\frac{1}{-2t}
 + \frac1t\right) = \frac12\cdot\frac{1}{2t} = \frac{1}{4t}.$ ✓

---

**Collecting all solutions.** The three cases together yield:

$$\boxed{(a, b, c) = (t,\, t,\, t),\quad (a,b,c) = (4t,\, t,\, -2t),\quad \text{
or}\quad (a,b,c) = (-2t,\, t,\, 4t), \quad t \neq 0.}$$

**Remark.** The trivial family is the constant arithmetic progression. The two n
on-trivial families are the arithmetic progression $\{4t, t, -2t\}$ (common diff
erence $-3t$) in its two possible orderings with $b = t$ fixed in the middle; in
 each, the reciprocal of the extreme term farthest from zero equals the mean of
the other two reciprocals. No other solutions exist, since every case reduced to
 a quadratic whose roots are exhausted above.
