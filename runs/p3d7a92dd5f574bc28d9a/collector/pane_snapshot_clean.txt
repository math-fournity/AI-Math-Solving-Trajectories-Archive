## Proof

Let $k = \mathbb{F}_{2^{10}}$ and $K = \mathbb{F}_{2^{20}}$, so $:k]=2$ and $\
operatorname{Gal}(K/k)=\{1,\sigma\}$ with $\sigma(x)=x^{2^{10}}$. The norm is
$$N_{K/k}(x)=\sigma(x)\,x=x^{2^{10}}\cdot x=x^{2^{10}+1}.$$

**Step 1: Group structure.** The multiplicative group of any finite field is cyc
lic. Thus
- $K^\times$ is cyclic of order $|K^\times|=2^{20}-1$,
- $k^\times$ is cyclic of order $|k^\times|=2^{10}-1$.

Choosing a generator $g$ of $K^\times$, every $x\in K^\times$ is $x=g^a$ for a u
nique $a\in\mathbb{Z}/(2^{20}-1)\mathbb{Z}$.

**Step 2: The norm as a power map.** Since $N_{K/k}(x)=x^{2^{10}+1}$,
$$N_{K/k}(g^a)=g^{a(2^{10}+1)}.$$
So the norm is the homomorphism $K^\times\to K^\times$ given by raising to the $
(2^{10}+1)$-st power, and its image lands in $k^\times$ (since $\sigma(N(x))=N(x
)$, equivalently $N(x)^{2^{10}}=N(x)$).

**Step 3: Image of the norm.** The image of the power map $x\mapsto x^m$ on a cy
clic group of order $n$ has order $n/\gcd(n,m)$. Here $n=2^{20}-1$ and $m=2^{10}
+1$. Factor:
$$2^{20}-1=(2^{10}-1)(2^{10}+1),$$
so $\gcd(2^{20}-1,\,2^{10}+1)=2^{10}+1$. Hence
$$|\operatorname{im} N_{K/k}|=\frac{2^{20}-1}{2^{10}+1}=2^{10}-1=|k^\times|.$$
Therefore $N_{K/k}:K^\times\to k^\times$ is **surjective** (this is the finite-f
ield instance of the surjectivity of the norm, a special case of Hilbert's Theor
em 90).

**Step 4: Kernel size.** By the first isomorphism theorem,
$$K^\times/\ker N_{K/k}\cong k^\times,$$
so
$$|\ker N_{K/k}|=\frac{|K^\times|}{|k^\times|}=\frac{2^{20}-1}{2^{10}-1}=\frac{(
2^{10}-1)(2^{10}+1)}{2^{10}-1}=2^{10}+1.$$

**Step 5: Compute the value.**
$$2^{10}+1=1024+1=1025.$$

The kernel of the norm map is exactly the set $\{x\in K^\times:N_{K/k}(x)=1\}$,
so the number of such elements is $|\ker N_{K/k}|$.

$$\boxed{1025}$$
