- The constant term $6$ satisfies $4 \nmid 6$. ✓

$$\Delta = 3125 \cdot 6^4 + 256 \cdot (-4)^5 = 3125 \cdot 1296 - 256 \cdot 1024

Since the discriminant is not a square, $G \not\subseteq A_5$.
