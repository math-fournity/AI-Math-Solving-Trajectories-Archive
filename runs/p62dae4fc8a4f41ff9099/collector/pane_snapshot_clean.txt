## Proof

Let $S_i = \sum_{j=1}^{i} X_j$ be the simple symmetric random walk, so $Y_N = \m
ax_{1\le i\le N}|S_i|$. We study
$$
p_N(\alpha)=\Pr\!\left(\frac{Y_N}{N}\le \alpha\right)=\Pr\!\bigl(Y_N\le \alpha N
\bigr),\qquad 0<\alpha<1.
$$

We will show $p_N(\alpha)\to 1$, so the limit equals $1$; hence its tightest upp
er bound is $1$.

**Step 1. A reflection-principle bound on the upper tail.** For $a>0$ and intege
r $a$, the reflection principle for the simple symmetric walk gives
$$
\Pr\!\Bigl(\max_{1\le i\le N} S_i \ge a\Bigr)\;\le\;2\,\Pr(S_N\ge a).
$$
By symmetry the same bound holds for $\max_{1\le i\le N}(-S_i)\ge a$. Therefore,
 using the union bound,
$$
\Pr(Y_N\ge a)=\Pr\!\Bigl(\max_{1\le i\le N}|S_i|\ge a\Bigr)\le 2\,\Pr\!\Bigl(\ma
x_{1\le i\le N} S_i\ge a\Bigr)\le 4\,\Pr(S_N\ge a).
$$

**Step 2. Hoeffding bound.** Since $S_N=\sum_{j=1}^N X_j$ is a sum of independen
t variables with $X_j\in[-1,1]$ and $\mathbb E X_j=0$, Hoeffding's inequality yi
elds
$$
\Pr(S_N\ge a)\le \exp\!\Bigl(-\frac{a^2}{2N}\Bigr).
$$
Taking $a=\alpha N$ (rounding integers does not affect the limit),
$$
\Pr(Y_N\ge \alpha N)\le 4\exp\!\Bigl(-\frac{\alpha^2 N}{2}\Bigr).
$$

**Step 3. Take the limit.** The right-hand side tends to $0$ exponentially fast,
 so
$$
\lim_{N\to\infty}\Pr(Y_N\ge \alpha N)=0,
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\lim_{N\to\infty}p_N(\alpha)=\lim_{N\to\infty}\Pr(Y_N\le \alpha N)=1.
$$

(Equivalently, by the strong law $S_i/i\to 0$ a.s. together with a maximal-inequ
ality/Borel–Cantelli argument, $Y_N/N\to 0$ a.s., which also gives $p_N(\alpha)\
to 1$.)

**Conclusion.** The limit exists and equals $1$ for every $0<\alpha<1$; conseque
ntly an upper bound for the limit is

$$
\boxed{1}.
$$
