**Proof.**

quadratic form, and $b(x,y)=q(x+y)-q(x)-q(y)$ the associated bilinear form. Set
$R:=V^\perp=\{x\in V: b(x,y)=0\ \forall y\in V\}$.

**Preliminary observations.**

The form $b$ is symmetric and bilinear. In characteristic $2$ it is *alternating

$$b(x,x)=q(x+x)-q(x)-q(x)=q(0)-2q(x)=0-0=0,$$
since $x+x=0$ and $2q(x)=0$ in $K$.

Every $A\in O(K^n,q)$ preserves $q$, hence preserves $b$ (which is determined by

is invertible. Since $A$ preserves $b$,
$$A^{T}BA=B.$$
Taking determinants: $\det(A)^2\det(B)=\det(B)$, and since $\det(B)\neq 0$,
$$\det(A)^2=1.$$
In characteristic $2$, the equation $t^2=1$ is $(t-1)^2=0$, which in the field $
K$ forces $t=1$. Hence $\det(A)=1$.

Because $q(e)\neq 0$, we get $\lambda^2=1$, and again (characteristic $2$) this
