## Proof

Each card starts red-side up. A card is flipped once for each condition it satis
fies among {even, multiple of 3}. A card ends **red** iff it is flipped an **eve
n** number of times (0 or 2); it ends **yellow** iff flipped an **odd** number o
f times (1).

**Count of cards flipped exactly once (yellow):**

A card is flipped exactly once iff it is divisible by exactly one of 2 or 3.

- Cards divisible by 2: ⌊100/2⌋ = 50
- Cards divisible by 3: ⌊100/3⌋ = 33
- Cards divisible by both 2 and 3 (i.e., by 6): ⌊100/6⌋ = 16

By inclusion–exclusion, cards divisible by at least one of {2, 3}:
50 + 33 − 16 = 67.

Cards flipped exactly once = (divisible by at least one) − (divisible by both) =
 67 − 16 = **51**.

**Count of cards flipped zero or two times (red):**

- Flipped 0 times (divisible by neither 2 nor 3): 100 − 67 = 33.
- Flipped 2 times (divisible by 6): 16.

Red cards = 33 + 16 = **49**.

**Verification:** yellow (51) + red (49) = 100. ✓
