# Boxdot Conjecture — Proof

## Statement

**Boxdot Conjecture.** For every modal formula $\varphi$: if $\mathrm{KT} \vdash
 \varphi$, then $\mathrm{K} \vdash t(\varphi)$, where $t$ is the Boxdot translat
ion defined by:
- $t(p) = p$, $\quad t(\bot) = \bot$,
- $t(\alpha \to \beta) = t(\alpha) \to t(\beta)$,
- $t(\Box\alpha) = \Box\,t(\alpha) \wedge t(\alpha)$.

---

## Proof

We use the **semantic method**, relying on Kripke-frame soundness and completene
ss.

**Fact 1 (Soundness/Completeness of K).** $\mathrm{K} \vdash \varphi$ if and onl
y if $\varphi$ is valid on every Kripke frame.

**Fact 2 (Soundness/Completeness of KT).** $\mathrm{KT} \vdash \varphi$ if and o
nly if $\varphi$ is valid on every **reflexive** Kripke frame (i.e., every frame
 $F=(W,R)$ with $R$ reflexive).

It suffices to prove the following semantic lemma.

---

### Key Lemma (Translation via Reflexive Closure)

**Lemma.** Let $F = (W, R)$ be any Kripke frame with valuation $V$, and let $F^{
\mathrm{rc}} = (W, R^{\mathrm{rc}})$ be its **reflexive closure**, i.e., $R^{\ma
thrm{rc}} = R \cup \{(x,x) : x \in W\}$. Then for every formula $\varphi$ and ev
ery world $w \in W$:

$$F, V, w \models t(\varphi) \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad F^{\mathrm{rc}}, V, w
 \models \varphi.$$

**Proof of Lemma.** By induction on the structure of $\varphi$.

**Base cases.**
- **Atom** $\varphi = p$: $t(p) = p$, and $F$ and $F^{\mathrm{rc}}$ share the sa
me valuation $V$, so $F,V,w \models p \iff F^{\mathrm{rc}},V,w \models p$. ✓
- **Falsum** $\varphi = \bot$: $t(\bot)=\bot$, and $\bot$ is false at every worl
d in both frames. ✓

**Inductive step: Implication** $\varphi = \alpha \to \beta$. We have $t(\alpha\
to\beta) = t(\alpha)\to t(\beta)$. Then:
$$F,V,w \models t(\alpha)\to t(\beta) \iff \bigl(F,V,w\models t(\alpha) \Rightar
row F,V,w\models t(\beta)\bigr) \iff \bigl(F^{\mathrm{rc}},V,w\models\alpha \Rig
htarrow F^{\mathrm{rc}},V,w\models\beta\bigr) \iff F^{\mathrm{rc}},V,w\models\al
pha\to\beta,$$
where the middle equivalence uses the induction hypothesis for $\alpha$ and $\be
ta$. ✓

**Inductive step: Necessity** $\varphi = \Box\alpha$. We have $t(\Box\alpha) = \
Box\,t(\alpha) \wedge t(\alpha)$. Then:

$$F,V,w \models \Box\,t(\alpha)\wedge t(\alpha) \iff \bigl(\forall v.\; wRv \Rig
htarrow F,V,v\models t(\alpha)\bigr) \wedge \bigl(F,V,w\models t(\alpha)\bigr).$
$

By the induction hypothesis, $F,V,v\models t(\alpha) \iff F^{\mathrm{rc}},V,v\mo
dels\alpha$ for every $v$. Substituting:

$$\iff \bigl(\forall v.\; wRv \Rightarrow F^{\mathrm{rc}},V,v\models\alpha\bigr)
 \wedge \bigl(F^{\mathrm{rc}},V,w\models\alpha\bigr).$$

Now, $R^{\mathrm{rc}} = R \cup \{(x,x):x\in W\}$, so $wR^{\mathrm{rc}}v$ holds i
ff ($wRv$ or $w=v$). Therefore:

$$\bigl(\forall v.\; wRv \Rightarrow F^{\mathrm{rc}},V,v\models\alpha\bigr) \wed
ge \bigl(F^{\mathrm{rc}},V,w\models\alpha\bigr) \iff \bigl(\forall v.\; wR^{\mat
hrm{rc}}v \Rightarrow F^{\mathrm{rc}},V,v\models\alpha\bigr) \iff F^{\mathrm{rc}
},V,w\models\Box\alpha.$$

Combining: $F,V,w \models t(\Box\alpha) \iff F^{\mathrm{rc}},V,w\models\Box\alph
a$. ✓

This completes the induction, proving the Lemma. $\square$

### Main Theorem

**Theorem (Boxdot Conjecture).** For every formula $\varphi$: $\mathrm{KT}\vdash
\varphi \;\Longrightarrow\; \mathrm{K}\vdash t(\varphi)$.

**Proof.** Suppose $\mathrm{KT}\vdash\varphi$. By Fact 2 (soundness of KT w.r.t.
 reflexive frames), $\varphi$ is valid on every reflexive Kripke frame.

We show $t(\varphi)$ is valid on **every** Kripke frame. Let $F=(W,R)$ be an arb
itrary frame, $V$ an arbitrary valuation, and $w\in W$ an arbitrary world. Let $
F^{\mathrm{rc}}=(W,R^{\mathrm{rc}})$ be the reflexive closure of $F$. Then $F^{\
mathrm{rc}}$ is reflexive by construction.

Since $\varphi$ is valid on all reflexive frames, in particular:
$$F^{\mathrm{rc}}, V, w \models \varphi.$$

By the Key Lemma:
$$F, V, w \models t(\varphi).$$

Since $F$, $V$, and $w$ were arbitrary, $t(\varphi)$ is valid on every Kripke fr
ame. By Fact 1 (completeness of K), $\mathrm{K}\vdash t(\varphi)$. $\square$

---

### Summary

The proof hinges on a single semantic observation: evaluating the Boxdot transla
tion $t(\varphi)$ on an arbitrary frame $F$ is equivalent to evaluating $\varphi
$ on the reflexive closure $F^{\mathrm{rc}}$. This is established by structural
induction on $\varphi$, where the crucial case is the necessity operator—there,
the conjunct $t(\alpha)$ in $t(\Box\alpha)=\Box\,t(\alpha)\wedge t(\alpha)$ exac
tly accounts for the added reflexive loops $(x,x)$ in $R^{\mathrm{rc}}$.

Given this equivalence, any KT-theorem $\varphi$ (valid on all reflexive frames)
 has its translation $t(\varphi)$ valid on all frames (since every frame's refle
xive closure is reflexive), and hence $t(\varphi)$ is a K-theorem by completenes
s.
