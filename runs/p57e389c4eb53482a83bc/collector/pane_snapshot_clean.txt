## Solution

**Setup.** Let $A = \overline{IKS} = 100I + 10K + S$ and $B = \overline{KSI} = 1
00K + 10S + I$, where $I, K, S$ are distinct non-zero digits. The product $P = A
B$ is a 6-digit palindrome. Since a 6-digit palindrome has the form $d_1 d_2 d_3
 d_3 d_2 d_1$, each digit $d_i$ appears exactly twice. The condition "two digits
 are 4, four digits are $I$" means exactly one of $\{d_1, d_2, d_3\}$ equals 4 a
nd the other two equal $I$ (with $I \neq 4$). This gives three cases:

| Case | Palindrome form | Value |
|------|----------------|-------|
| 1 | $4IIII4$ | $400004 + 11110I$ |
| 2 | $I4II4I$ | $40040 + 101101I$ |
| 3 | $II44II$ | $4400 + 110011I$ |

**Last-digit constraint.** The last digit of $AB$ equals the last digit of $SI$:
- **Case 1:** last digit is 4, so $SI \equiv 4 \pmod{10}$.
- **Cases 2 & 3:** last digit is $I$, so $SI \equiv I \pmod{10}$, i.e., $10 \mid
 I(S-1)$.

For each valid $(I, S)$ pair, I solve the quadratic in $K$ obtained from $AB = P
$ and check for integer solutions with $K$ a non-zero digit distinct from $I$ an
d $S$.

**Case 1 ($4IIII4$, $SI \equiv 4 \pmod{10}$):** Testing all valid $(I,S)$ pairs:

| $I$ | $S$ | $P$ | Discriminant | $\sqrt{\Delta}$ integer? |
|-----|-----|-----|-------------|------------------------|

| 2 | 7 | 422224 | 5220241 | No |

| 6 | 9 | 466664 | 13648649 | No |
| 7 | 2 | 477774 | **68013009** | **Yes, $= 8247$** |
| 8 | 3 | 488884 | 83427424 | No |
| 9 | 6 | 499994 | 100837841 | No |

For $I=7, S=2$: $K = \frac{-7047 + 8247}{200} = 6$. Check: $I=7, K=6, S=2$ — dis
tinct, non-zero. ✓

$$762 \times 627 = 477774 \quad \checkmark$$

**Case 2 ($I4II4I$, $10 \mid I(S-1)$):** Testing all valid $(I,S)$ pairs (includ
ing $S=1$ with any $I$; $I=5$ with $S \in \{3,7,9\}$; $S=6$ with $I \in \{2,8\}$

tinct, non-zero. ✓

$$593 \times 935 = 554455 \quad \checkmark$$
