# Problem

In a futuristic circular space station, the perimeter is divided into sectors. Every single coordinate along this circular outer hull is assigned to one of three maintenance guilds: the Red Forge, the Yellow Aegis, or the Blue Spire. Each guild is responsible for at least one section of the hull.

The station's central computer must select a set of $n$ discrete sensor locations along the hull to monitor for debris. The security protocol requires that no matter how these $n$ sensor locations are distributed across the guilds' territories, there must always be at least one group of three sensors that satisfies two specific conditions:
1. All three sensors in the group are located in territories belonging to the same guild.
2. The sensors form an obtuse triangle (a triangle where one interior angle is strictly greater than 90 degrees).

What is the minimum possible value of $n$ that guarantees these conditions are met, regardless of how the three guilds have partitioned the circle or which specific points are chosen?
