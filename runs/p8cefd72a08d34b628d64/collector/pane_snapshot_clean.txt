## Solution

Let $p_k = x^k + y^k$ denote the power sums, and let $e_1 = x+y$, $e_2 = xy$ be
the elementary symmetric polynomials. Newton's identities give the recurrence:
$$p_k = e_1\, p_{k-1} - e_2\, p_{k-2} \quad (k \ge 2), \qquad p_0 = 2,\; p_1 = e
_1.$$

**Setting up the equations.** Let $s = e_1 = x+y$.

**Condition 1:** $\dfrac{p_2}{p_1} = 4$, so $p_2 = 4s$. Since $p_2 = s^2 - 2e_2$
:
$$s^2 - 2e_2 = 4s \implies e_2 = \frac{s^2 - 4s}{2}. \tag{1}$$

**Condition 2:** $\dfrac{p_4}{p_3} = 2$, so $p_4 = 2p_3$.

Computing:
$$p_3 = s^3 - 3s\,e_2, \qquad p_4 = s\,p_3 - e_2\,p_2 = s^4 - 4s^2 e_2 + 2e_2^2.
$$

The equation $p_4 = 2p_3$ becomes:
$$s^4 - 4s^2 e_2 + 2e_2^2 = 2s^3 - 6s\,e_2.$$

Substituting $e_2 = \frac{s^2-4s}{2}$ and simplifying:
$$-\tfrac{s^4}{2} + 4s^3 + 8s^2 = -s^3 + 12s^2,$$
$$-\tfrac{s^4}{2} + 5s^3 - 4s^2 = 0 \implies s^2(s^2 - 10s + 8) = 0.$$

Since $s = 0$ would make $p_1 = 0$ (undefined first ratio), we need:
$$s^2 - 10s + 8 = 0 \implies s = 5 \pm \sqrt{17}. \tag{2}$$

**Computing the target ratio.** We want $\dfrac{p_6}{p_5}$. Using the recurrence
 and $p_4 = 2p_3$:

$$p_5 = s\,p_4 - e_2\,p_3 = p_3(2s - e_2),$$
$$p_6 = s\,p_5 - e_2\,p_4 = p_3\bigl(s(2s-e_2) - 2e_2\bigr) = p_3(2s^2 - s\,e_2
- 2e_2).$$

Therefore:
$$\frac{p_6}{p_5} = \frac{2s^2 - s\,e_2 - 2e_2}{2s - e_2}.$$

Substituting $e_2 = \frac{s^2-4s}{2}$:

- $2s - e_2 = \frac{s(8-s)}{2}$,
- $2s^2 - e_2(s+2) = \frac{s(-s^2+6s+8)}{2}$.

So:
$$\frac{p_6}{p_5} = \frac{-s^2+6s+8}{8-s}.$$

Using $s^2 = 10s - 8$ from (2):
$$-s^2 + 6s + 8 = -(10s-8) + 6s + 8 = 16 - 4s = 4(4-s),$$

$$\frac{p_6}{p_5} = \frac{4(4-s)}{8-s}.$$

**Evaluating for each root.**

- **$s = 5 + \sqrt{17}$:** $\;\dfrac{4(-1-\sqrt{17})}{3-\sqrt{17}} = \dfrac{4(-1
-\sqrt{17})(3+\sqrt{17})}{9-17} = \dfrac{4(-20-4\sqrt{17})}{-8} = 10 + 2\sqrt{17
}.$

- **$s = 5 - \sqrt{17}$:** $\;\dfrac{4(-1+\sqrt{17})}{3+\sqrt{17}} = \dfrac{4(-1
+\sqrt{17})(3-\sqrt{17})}{9-17} = \dfrac{4(-20+4\sqrt{17})}{-8} = 10 - 2\sqrt{17
}.$

**Validity check.** For both roots: $s \neq 0$ (so $p_1 \neq 0$); $8 - s \neq 0$
 (so $p_5 \neq 0$); and $p_3 = s(s+4) \neq 0$ since $s \neq 0$ and $s + 4 = 9 \p
m \sqrt{17} \neq 0$ (so $p_3 \neq 0$, making the second ratio well-defined). Bot
h solutions are admissible.

**Numerical verification** (for $s = 5 - \sqrt{17} \approx 0.877$, $e_2 \approx
-1.369$): the roots of $t^2 - st + e_2 = 0$ are $x \approx 1.688$, $y \approx -0
.811$. Then $p_2/p_1 \approx 4.00$ ✓, $p_4/p_3 \approx 2.00$ ✓, and $p_6/p_5 \ap
prox 1.754 = 10 - 2\sqrt{17}$ ✓.

## Answer

The possible values of the Sixth-Order Stability Ratio are:

$$\boxed{10 + 2\sqrt{17} \quad \text{and} \quad 10 - 2\sqrt{17}}.$$
