## Solution

**Setup.** Let $AD = x$, so $BC = 2x$. Place the base $BCD$ in the $xy$-plane wi
th the midpoint of $BC$ at the origin and $BC$ along the $x$-axis:
$$B=(-x,0,0),\quad C=(x,0,0).$$

Since $BD=CD=2$, point $D$ lies on the perpendicular bisector of $BC$: $D=(0,d,0
)$ with $d^2+x^2=4$.

Since $AB=AC=2$, point $A$ lies on the perpendicular bisector of $BC$: $A=(0,a,h
)$ with $x^2+a^2+h^2=4$.

**Using the angle condition.** The angle between $AD$ and the base plane equals
the angle between $AD$ and its projection onto the $xy$-plane. The projection ha
s length $|a-d|$ and the $z$-component is $h$, so
$$\tan\frac{\pi}{3}=\frac{h}{|a-d|}=\sqrt{3}\implies h=\sqrt{3}\,|a-d|.$$
Combined with $AD^2=(a-d)^2+h^2=x^2$:
$$(a-d)^2+3(a-d)^2=x^2\implies (a-d)^2=\frac{x^2}{4},\quad h^2=\frac{3x^2}{4}.$$

**Solving for $x$.** From the constraints:
$$a^2=4-x^2-h^2=4-\frac{7x^2}{4},\qquad d^2=4-x^2.$$
Expanding $(a-d)^2=a^2+d^2-2ad=\frac{x^2}{4}$ gives $ad=4-\frac{3x^2}{2}$. Squar
ing and using $(ad)^2=a^2d^2$:
$$\left(4-\frac{3x^2}{2}\right)^2=\left(4-\frac{7x^2}{4}\right)(4-x^2).$$
With $u=x^2$, expanding both sides:
$$16-12u+\frac{9u^2}{4}=16-11u+\frac{7u^2}{4}\implies -u+\frac{u^2}{2}=0\implies
 u=2$$
(nonzero solution). So $x=\sqrt{2}$, i.e. $AD=\sqrt{2}$, $BC=2\sqrt{2}$.

**Coordinates.** Substituting $x^2=2$:
$$d=\sqrt{2},\quad a=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}},\quad h=\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2},$$
giving
$$B=(-\sqrt{2},0,0),\ C=(\sqrt{2},0,0),\ D=(0,\sqrt{2},0),\ A=\left(0,\tfrac{1}{
\sqrt{2}},\tfrac{\sqrt{6}}{2}\right).$$

**Circumscribed sphere.** Let the center be $O=(o_x,o_y,o_z)$. Symmetry of $B,C$
 about the $yz$-plane gives $o_x=0$. Setting $|OB|^2=|OD|^2$:
$$2+o_y^2+o_z^2=(o_y-\sqrt{2})^2+o_z^2\implies o_y=0.$$
Setting $|OB|^2=|OA|^2$:
$$2+o_y^2+o_z^2=o_y^2+o_z^2-\sqrt{2}\,o_y-\sqrt{6}\,o_z+2\implies o_z=0.$$
Hence $O=(0,0,0)$, the origin. The radius satisfies
$$R^2=|OA|^2=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{3}{2}=2,\qquad |OB|^2=2,\qquad |OD|^2=2.\ \checkm
ark$$

**Surface area:**
$$S=4\pi R^2=4\pi\cdot 2=8\pi.$$

Final Answer: $\boxed{8\pi}$
