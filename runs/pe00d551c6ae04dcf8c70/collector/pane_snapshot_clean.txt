## Solution

Let $D = \{x^2+y^2 \le 1\}$ be the closed unit disk in $\mathbb{R}^2$, so $\part
ial D = S^1$. Give $D$ its standard orientation (the one in which $dx\wedge dy$
is positive), and give $S^1$ the induced boundary orientation (counterclockwise)
.

Since $\omega \in \Omega^1(\mathbb{R}^2)$ and $d\omega = dx\wedge dy$, we apply
Stokes' theorem to the compact oriented manifold-with-boundary $D$:

$$\int_{\partial D} i^*\omega \;=\; \int_D d\omega.$$

The left-hand side is exactly $\int_{S^1} i^*\omega$. For the right-hand side,

$$\int_D d\omega \;=\; \int_D dx\wedge dy \;=\; \operatorname{Area}(D).$$

The area of the unit disk is $\pi\cdot 1^2 = \pi$. Therefore

$$\int_{S^1} i^*\omega \;=\; \pi.$$
