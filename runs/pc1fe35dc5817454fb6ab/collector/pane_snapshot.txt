### Step 1: Solve for $x$

We have $x^{x^9} = \sqrt{3^{\sqrt{3}}} = 3^{\sqrt{3}/2}$.

**Claim:** $x = 3^{1/6}$.

**Verification.** Then $x^9 = (3^{1/6})^9 = 3^{9/6} = 3^{3/2} = 3\sqrt{3}$, and
$$x^{x^9} = (3^{1/6})^{3\sqrt{3}} = 3^{\,3\sqrt{3}/6} = 3^{\sqrt{3}/2} = \sqrt{3
^{\sqrt{3}}}.\ \checkmark$$

### Step 2: Solve for $y$

The second equation is $y = x^{\,1/(y^{y^x})}$.

**Claim:** $y = 3^{1/(6x)}$ (equivalently $y = x^{1/x}$, since $x^{1/x}=(3^{1/6}
)^{1/x}=3^{1/(6x)}$).

**Verification.** Compute the key quantity $y^x$:
$$y^x = \bigl(3^{1/(6x)}\bigr)^{x} = 3^{x/(6x)} = 3^{1/6} = x.$$

Since $y^x = x$, we immediately get
$$y^{y^x} = y^{x} = x.$$

Therefore the right-hand side of the defining equation becomes
$$x^{\,1/(y^{y^x})} = x^{\,1/x} = (3^{1/6})^{1/x} = 3^{1/(6x)} = y.\ \checkmark$
$

So $y = 3^{1/(6x)}$ satisfies the equation.

### Step 3: Compute $y^{3x}$

$$y^{3x} = \bigl(3^{1/(6x)}\bigr)^{3x} = 3^{\,(3x)/(6x)} = 3^{1/2} = \sqrt{3}.$$

### Conclusion

$$\boxed{y^{3x} = \sqrt{3}}$$
