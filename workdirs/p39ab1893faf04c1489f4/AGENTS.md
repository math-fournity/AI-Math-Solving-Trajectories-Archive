# Problem

In the city of Metros, there are $n$ distinct high-end construction projects currently underway. For each project, exactly two specialized engineers are assigned—a Lead Architect and a Site Manager. 

A total of $2n$ engineers are waiting in a single-file line to pass through a security checkpoint to enter one of two office wings, Wing A or Wing B. The order of the engineers in the line is completely random, with every possible permutation being equally likely.

The distribution process works as follows:
1. The first engineer in the line is automatically assigned to Wing A.
2. For every subsequent engineer in the line, they first approach the door of the wing that the person immediately in front of them in the line just entered.
3. They peek inside: if their colleague from the same construction project is not already in that wing, they enter it. If their colleague is already present in that wing, they must enter the opposite wing.

This process continues until one of the wings reaches its capacity of exactly $n$ engineers. Calculate the probability that Wing A reaches its full capacity of $n$ engineers before Wing B does.
