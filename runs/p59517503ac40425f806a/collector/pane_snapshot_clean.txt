A folder is non-Clustered iff its contents form an **independent set** in $G_m$.
 Covering $\{1,\ldots,m\}$ with 14 non-Clustered folders = covering $G_m$ with 1

## The Conflict Graph is an Interval Graph

Map each integer $n$ to the interval $I_n = [\log n,\; \log n + \log\tfrac{4}{3}

**Interval graphs are perfect** (a classical result), so $\chi(G_m) = \omega(G_m
