## Solution

We evaluate $\int_C \omega$ where $\omega = \frac{dx \wedge dy}{x^2+y^2}$ and $C
(t_1,t_2) = (t_1+1)(\cos 2\pi t_2, \sin 2\pi t_2)$, with $I_2 = [0,1]^2$.

**Step 1: Identify the parameterization.**

Setting $r = t_1 + 1$ and $\theta = 2\pi t_2$, we have $C(t_1,t_2) = (r\cos\thet
a, r\sin\theta)$. As $(t_1,t_2)$ ranges over $[0,1]^2$, $r$ ranges over $[1,2]$
and $\theta$ over $[0,2\pi]$. Thus $C$ parameterizes the closed annulus $\{1 \le
 r \le 2\}$.

**Step 2: Compute the pullback $C^*(dx \wedge dy)$.**

With $x = (t_1+1)\cos(2\pi t_2)$ and $y = (t_1+1)\sin(2\pi t_2)$:

$$\frac{\partial x}{\partial t_1} = \cos(2\pi t_2), \quad \frac{\partial x}{\par
tial t_2} = -2\pi(t_1+1)\sin(2\pi t_2)$$

$$\frac{\partial y}{\partial t_1} = \sin(2\pi t_2), \quad \frac{\partial y}{\par
tial t_2} = 2\pi(t_1+1)\cos(2\pi t_2)$$

The Jacobian determinant is:

$$\det J = \cos(2\pi t_2)\cdot 2\pi(t_1+1)\cos(2\pi t_2) - \bigl(-2\pi(t_1+1)\si
n(2\pi t_2)\bigr)\cdot\sin(2\pi t_2)$$

$$= 2\pi(t_1+1)\bigl[\cos^2(2\pi t_2) + \sin^2(2\pi t_2)\bigr] = 2\pi(t_1+1).$$

So $C^*(dx \wedge dy) = 2\pi(t_1+1)\, dt_1 \wedge dt_2$.

**Step 3: Compute the pullback $C^*\omega$.**

Since $x^2 + y^2 = (t_1+1)^2$:

$$C^*\omega = \frac{C^*(dx\wedge dy)}{x^2+y^2} = \frac{2\pi(t_1+1)}{(t_1+1)^2}\,
 dt_1\wedge dt_2 = \frac{2\pi}{t_1+1}\, dt_1\wedge dt_2.$$

**Step 4: Integrate over $I_2$.**

$$\int_C \omega = \int_{I_2} C^*\omega = \int_0^1\!\int_0^1 \frac{2\pi}{t_1+1}\,
 dt_1\, dt_2.$$

The integrand is independent of $t_2$, so the $t_2$-integral gives a factor of $
1$:

$$= 2\pi \int_0^1 \frac{1}{t_1+1}\, dt_1 = 2\pi \Bigl[\ln(t_1+1)\Bigr]_0^1 = 2\p
i(\ln 2 - \ln 1) = 2\pi \ln 2.$$

**Result:**

$$\boxed{\int_C \omega = 2\pi \ln 2}$$
