## IMO 2019 A1 — Solution

**Problem.** Fix $N \neq 0$. Find all $f:\mathbb{Z}\to\mathbb{Z}$ with
$$f(Na)+Nf(b)=f(f(a+b))\quad\text{for all }a,b\in\mathbb{Z}.\tag{$\ast$}$$

---

### Step 1. A key structural consequence

Denote the assertion $P(a,b)$. The right-hand side $f(f(a+b))$ depends on $a,b$
only through $s:=a+b$. Hence $f(Na)+Nf(b)$ also depends only on $s=a+b$.

Set $g(a):=f(Na)$. Then $g(a)+Nf(b)$ depends only on $a+b$. Replacing $(a,b)$ by
 $(a+1,b-1)$ (which preserves $a+b$) gives
$$g(a)+Nf(b)=g(a+1)+Nf(b-1),$$
i.e.
$$g(a+1)-g(a)=N\bigl(f(b)-f(b-1)\bigr)\qquad\text{for all }a,b\in\mathbb{Z}.\tag
{1}$$

The left side of (1) is a function of $a$ alone; the right side is a function of
 $b$ alone. Since they agree for **all** $a,b$, both sides equal a common consta
nt $C\in\mathbb{Z}$:
$$g(a+1)-g(a)=C,\qquad N\bigl(f(b)-f(b-1)\bigr)=C.$$

From the first: $g(a)=Ca+g(0)$, i.e. $f(Na)=Ca+f(0)$ for all $a$.
From the second: $f(b)-f(b-1)=C/N$ for all $b$. Since $f$ takes integer values a
nd $N\neq 0$, we need $N\mid C$; write $D:=C/N\in\mathbb{Z}$. Then $f$ is an ari
thmetic progression:
$$\boxed{f(x)=Dx+e},\qquad e:=f(0),\quad D\in\mathbb{Z}.$$

### Step 2. Determine $D$ and $e$

Substitute $f(x)=Dx+e$ into $(\ast)$:

- **LHS:** $f(Na)+Nf(b)=(DNa+e)+N(Db+e)=DN(a+b)+e(1+N).$
- **RHS:** $f(f(a+b))=f(D(a+b)+e)=D\bigl(D(a+b)+e\bigr)+e=D^{2}(a+b)+e(D+1).$

Equating coefficients of $(a+b)$ and the constant term:

$$DN=D^{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad D=0\ \text{or}\ D=N,\tag{2}$$
$$e(1+N)=e(D+1)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad e\,(N-D)=0.\tag{3}$$

**Case $D=0$.** Then (3) gives $eN=0$; since $N\neq 0$, $e=0$. So $f\equiv 0$.

**Case $D=N$.** Then (3) gives $e\cdot 0=0$, automatically satisfied for **any**
 $e\in\mathbb{Z}$. So $f(x)=Nx+e$ with $e$ arbitrary.

### Step 3. Verification

- **$f\equiv 0$:** LHS $=0+N\cdot 0=0$, RHS $=f(0)=0$. ✓
- **$f(x)=Nx+e$:** LHS $=(N^{2}a+e)+N(Nb+e)=N^{2}(a+b)+e(1+N)$. RHS $=f(N(a+b)+e
)=N^{2}(a+b)+Ne+e=N^{2}(a+b)+e(N+1)$. ✓

### Conclusion

The solutions are exactly
$$\boxed{f(x)\equiv 0\quad\text{and}\quad f(x)=Nx+c\ (c\in\mathbb{Z}\text{ arbit
rary}).}$$
