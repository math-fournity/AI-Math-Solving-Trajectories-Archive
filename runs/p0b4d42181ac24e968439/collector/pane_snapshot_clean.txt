## Solution

The outer diamond has corners at $(\pm 36, 0)$ and $(0, \pm 36)$, so its boundar
y is $|x|+|y|=36$. "Strictly inside" means $|x|+|y|<36$.

The inner diamond has corners at $(\pm 10, 0)$ and $(0, \pm 10)$, so its boundar
y is $|x|+|y|=10$. "Strictly outside" means $|x|+|y|>10$.

We need integer lattice points $(x,y)$ satisfying
$$10 < |x|+|y| < 36,$$
i.e. $|x|+|y| \in \{11, 12, \dots, 35\}$.

**Counting lattice points with $|x|+|y|=k$.** For $k\ge 1$, the number of intege
r solutions to $|x|+|y|=k$ is $4k$. (For each $x$ with $|x|\le k$, the value $|y
|=k-|x|$ is determined, and the signs of $x$ and $y$ give the factor; summing yi
elds $4k$.)

**Summing over the valid range:**
$$\sum_{k=11}^{35} 4k = 4\sum_{k=11}^{35} k = 4\cdot\frac{(11+35)\cdot 25}{2} =
4\cdot\frac{46\cdot 25}{2} = 4\cdot 575 = 2300.$$

**Verification of the count $4k$:** For $k=1$: $(\pm1,0),(0,\pm1)$ gives $4=4\cd
ot1$. For $k=2$: $8=4\cdot2$. The formula holds since each of the $4$ sides of t
he diamond contributes exactly $k-1$ interior-edge points plus $1$ corner point
shared with adjacent sides, giving $4(k-1)+4=4k$.

Therefore the number of sensor nodes strictly inside the outer zone and strictly
 outside the inner core is

$$\boxed{2300}$$
