## Solution

**Setup.** Place the equilateral base $ABC$ (side $4\sqrt{3}$) in the $xy$-plane
:
$$A=(0,0,0),\quad B=(4\sqrt{3},0,0),\quad C=(2\sqrt{3},6,0).$$
The centroid is $G=(2\sqrt{3},2,0)$. Since $DA=DB=DC$ (symmetric apex), $D=(2\sq
rt{3},2,h)$.

**Finding $h$.** Vectors $\overrightarrow{AD}=(2\sqrt{3},2,h)$, $\overrightarrow
{AB}=(4\sqrt{3},0,0)$.
$$\cos\angle DAB=\frac{24}{4\sqrt{3}\cdot\sqrt{16+h^2}}=\frac{6}{\sqrt{48+3h^2}}
.$$
Since $\tan\angle DAB=\sqrt{37/3}$, we get $\cos\angle DAB=\sqrt{3/40}$. Setting
 equal and squaring:
$$\frac{36}{48+3h^2}=\frac{3}{40}\implies 1440=144+9h^2\implies h^2=144\implies
h=12.$$
So $D=(2\sqrt{3},2,12)$, with $DA=DB=DC=4\sqrt{10}$.

**Midpoints:**
$$A_1=(\sqrt{3},1,6),\quad B_1=(3\sqrt{3},1,6),\quad C_1=(2\sqrt{3},4,6).$$

---

### Part 1: $\cos\phi$

Directions: $\vec{u}=\overrightarrow{BA_1}=(-3\sqrt{3},1,6)$, $\vec{v}=\overrigh
tarrow{AC_1}=(2\sqrt{3},4,6)$.

$$\vec{u}\cdot\vec{v}=(-3\sqrt{3})(2\sqrt{3})+4+36=-18+4+36=22,$$
$$|\vec{u}|=\sqrt{27+1+36}=8,\quad |\vec{v}|=\sqrt{12+16+36}=8.$$
$$\boxed{\cos\phi=\frac{22}{64}=\frac{11}{32}}.$$

---

### Part 2: Shortest distance $d$

$$\vec{u}\times\vec{v}=(-18,\;30\sqrt{3},\;-14\sqrt{3}),$$
$$|\vec{u}\times\vec{v}|^2=324+2700+588=3612=12\cdot 301.$$
$$\overrightarrow{AB}\cdot(\vec{u}\times\vec{v})=(4\sqrt{3})(-18)=-72\sqrt{3}.$$
$$d=\frac{72\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{3612}}=\frac{72\sqrt{3}}{2\sqrt{3\cdot 301}}=\frac{3
6}{\sqrt{301}},\qquad d^2=\frac{1296}{301}.$$
$$d^2\cdot\frac{301}{36}=\frac{1296}{301}\cdot\frac{301}{36}=36.$$

---

### Part 3: Radius $r$

By the 3-fold symmetry about axis $GD$, the sphere center lies on $x=2\sqrt{3},\
,y=2$ at height $r$: $O=(2\sqrt{3},2,r)$.

Distance from $O$ to line $BA_1$ (point $B$, direction $\vec{u}$, $|\vec{u}|=8$)
:
$$\overrightarrow{BO}=(-2\sqrt{3},2,r),\quad \overrightarrow{BO}\cdot\vec{u}=18+
2+6r=20+6r.$$
$$\operatorname{dist}^2=|\overrightarrow{BO}|^2-\frac{(\overrightarrow{BO}\cdot\
vec{u})^2}{64}=(16+r^2)-\frac{(20+6r)^2}{64}=\frac{7r^2-60r+156}{16}.$$
Setting $\operatorname{dist}^2=r^2$:
$$7r^2-60r+156=16r^2\implies 9r^2+60r-156=0\implies 3r^2+20r-52=0.$$
$$r=\frac{-20\pm 32}{6}\implies \boxed{r=2}\quad(\text{rejecting negative root})
.$$
(Check: projection parameter $t=(20+12)/64=1/2\in[0,1]$, confirming tangency to
the segment. ✓)

---

### Final computation

$$32\cos\phi+d^2\cdot\frac{301}{36}+r=32\cdot\frac{11}{32}+36+2=11+36+2=\boxed{4
9}.$$
