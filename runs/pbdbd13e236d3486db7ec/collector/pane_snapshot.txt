## Proof

Let $i: X \hookrightarrow Y$ be a closed embedding of smooth varieties over a pe
rfect field $k$, with ideal sheaf $\mathscr{I}$, and let $\eta$ be the generic p
oint of $X$. We identify the stalk $(\mathscr{I}/\mathscr{I}^2)_\eta$ with $\mat
hfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2$, where $\mathfrak{m}$ is the maximal ideal of the local
 ring $\mathscr{O}_{Y,\eta}$.

**Step 1: The stalk of the quotient sheaf.**

Since taking stalks is exact, we have
$$(\mathscr{I}/\mathscr{I}^2)_\eta = \mathscr{I}_\eta / (\mathscr{I}^2)_\eta.$$

Moreover, the stalk of a product of ideal sheaves equals the product of the stal
ks: $(\mathscr{I}^2)_\eta = (\mathscr{I}_\eta)^2$. (This follows because stalks
commute with tensor products and $\mathscr{I}^2$ is the image of $\mathscr{I}\ot
imes\mathscr{I}\to\mathscr{O}_Y$; at the level of stalks, the image of $\mathscr
{I}_\eta\otimes\mathscr{I}_\eta\to\mathscr{O}_{Y,\eta}$ is $\mathscr{I}_\eta\cdo
t\mathscr{I}_\eta$.) Hence
$$(\mathscr{I}/\mathscr{I}^2)_\eta = \mathscr{I}_\eta / (\mathscr{I}_\eta)^2.$$

**Step 2: Identify $\mathscr{I}_\eta$ with the maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$.**

Since $i$ is a closed embedding with ideal sheaf $\mathscr{I}$, we have $\mathsc
r{O}_X \cong \mathscr{O}_Y/\mathscr{I}$ (as sheaves on $Y$, with $\mathscr{O}_X$
 pushed forward). Taking stalks at the point $\eta\in X\subset Y$:
$$\mathscr{O}_{X,\eta} \cong \mathscr{O}_{Y,\eta}/\mathscr{I}_\eta.$$

Now $\eta$ is the generic point of $X$, so $\mathscr{O}_{X,\eta} = \kappa(\eta)$
 is the function field $k(X)$ of $X$, which is a **field**. Therefore $\mathscr{
O}_{Y,\eta}/\mathscr{I}_\eta$ is a field, which means $\mathscr{I}_\eta$ is a ma
ximal ideal of $\mathscr{O}_{Y,\eta}$.

But $\mathscr{O}_{Y,\eta}$ is a local ring (being the stalk of a structure sheaf
 at a point), so it has a unique maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$. Since $\mathscr{I
}_\eta$ is maximal, we conclude:
$$\mathscr{I}_\eta = \mathfrak{m}.$$

**Step 3: Conclusion.**

Combining Steps 1 and 2:
$$(\mathscr{I}/\mathscr{I}^2)_\eta = \mathscr{I}_\eta/(\mathscr{I}_\eta)^2 = \ma
thfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2.$$

This gives the desired identification. $\blacksquare$
