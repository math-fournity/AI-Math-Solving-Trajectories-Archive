# Problem

In the year 2400, a logistics officer is auditing a series of interplanetary cargo containers, each assigned a unique identification serial number $n$. These serial numbers are natural numbers ranging from 100 to 400, inclusive.

The officer utilizes two different digital scanning protocols to verify the integrity of these containers:

1. **Protocol Decimal:** The scanner reads the serial number $n$ in its standard base-10 format and calculates a checksum $s$, which is the sum of its individual digits.
2. **Protocol Nonary:** The scanner converts the serial number $n$ into its base-9 representation and calculates a checksum $t$, which is the sum of the digits appearing in that base-9 representation.

The officer identifies "stable containers" as those where the checksum $s$ from the Decimal Protocol is exactly equal to the checksum $t$ from the Nonary Protocol.

Calculate the sum of the serial numbers $n$ of all such stable containers.
