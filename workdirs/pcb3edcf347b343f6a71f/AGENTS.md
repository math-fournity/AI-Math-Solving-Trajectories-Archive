# Problem

In a remote industrial logistics hub, there are exactly 29 unique shipping containers (calculated as $5^2 + 5 - 1$). Every possible combination of exactly 5 containers forms a "shipping manifest." 

Due to a digital sorting error, every single possible 5-container manifest has been automatically assigned to one of two distinct Priority Zones: Zone Alpha or Zone Beta. 

A logistics manager needs to determine the guaranteed minimum capacity for simultaneous operations. Specifically, let $k$ be the largest integer such that, regardless of how the manifests are assigned to the two zones, the manager is always guaranteed to find at least $k$ manifests in the same zone that share no containers in common (meaning all $k$ manifests are pairwise disjoint).

Find the value of $k$.
