# Problem

A specialized digital signal processing unit consists of a sequence of $2023$ logic gates. Each gate is manufactured as either a Type-R (Rectifier) or a Type-B (Buffer). 

A diagnostic probe is programmed to sample data from this sequence. The probe begins its operation at any gate of its choosing and records a single data packet from that gate. It then moves forward through the sequence. For every subsequent gate it encounters, the probe must decide whether to sample a packet or bypass the gate. To maintain signal synchronization, the probe is restricted by a hardware constraint: it is forbidden to bypass two consecutive gates. The probe may terminate its sampling process and exit the sequence after any gate is processed.

Let $r$ represent the total number of packets collected from Type-R gates and $b$ represent the total number of packets collected from Type-B gates during a single pass. 

Determine the maximum possible value of $|r - b|$ that can be guaranteed, regardless of how the $2023$ Type-R and Type-B gates are arranged in the sequence.
