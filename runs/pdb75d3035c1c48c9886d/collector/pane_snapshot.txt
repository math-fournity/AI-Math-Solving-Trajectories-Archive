## Proof

**Step 1: Identify the operator as a coordinate permutation.**

Write $(Sx)_k = x_{\sigma(k)}$ where
$$\sigma(2m-1)=2m+1,\qquad \sigma(2m)=2m \quad (m\ge 1).$$
The range of $\sigma$ is $\{2,3,4,5,\dots\}=\mathbb N\setminus\{1\}$, so $\sigma
$ is injective but **not** surjective: index $1$ is omitted. Hence $S$ is a cont
raction with $\|S\|=1$ (attained by sequences supported on $\{2,3,\dots\}$), and
 $\sigma(S)\subseteq\overline{\mathbb D}=\{\lambda:|\lambda|\le 1\}$.

**Step 2: Decompose $\ell^2$ into even and odd subspaces.**

Let $E=\overline{\mathrm{span}}\{e_2,e_4,e_6,\dots\}$ and $O=\overline{\mathrm{s
pan}}\{e_1,e_3,e_5,\dots\}$, so $\ell^2=E\oplus O$.

Since $S e_j = e_{\sigma^{-1}(j)}$:
- $\sigma^{-1}(2m)=2m$, so $S e_{2m}=e_{2m}$: thus $S|_E = I_E$.
- $\sigma^{-1}(2m+1)=2m-1$ and $1\notin\mathrm{range}(\sigma)$, so $S e_1=0$, $S
 e_{2m+1}=e_{2m-1}$ for $m\ge 1$.

Relabeling $f_0=e_1,\,f_1=e_3,\,f_2=e_5,\dots$ identifies $O\cong\ell^2(\mathbb
N_0)$, and on this basis
$$S f_0 = 0,\qquad S f_n = f_{n-1}\ (n\ge 1),$$
which is precisely the **backward shift** $B$ on $\ell^2(\mathbb N_0)$.

Therefore
$$S \;=\; I_E \oplus B_O,\qquad\text{so}\qquad \sigma(S)=\sigma(I_E)\cup\sigma(B
).$$

**Step 3: Spectrum of each summand.**

- $\sigma(I_E)=\{1\}$.
- For the backward shift $B(x_0,x_1,x_2,\dots)=(x_1,x_2,\dots)$: its adjoint is
the unilateral (forward) shift $U$, an isometry with $\sigma(U)=\overline{\mathb
b D}$. Since $\sigma(B)=\overline{\sigma(U)}=\overline{\mathbb D}$ (the closed d
isk is conjugation-symmetric), $\sigma(B)=\overline{\mathbb D}$.

Concretely, every $|\lambda|<1$ is an eigenvalue of $B$ via $x_n=\lambda^n x_0\i
n\ell^2$; the spectrum is closed and contained in $\overline{\mathbb D}$, forcin
g $\sigma(B)=\overline{\mathbb D}$.

**Step 4: Conclude.**

$$\sigma(S)=\{1\}\cup\overline{\mathbb D}=\overline{\mathbb D}=\{\lambda\in\math
bb C:|\lambda|\le 1\}.$$

The point spectrum is $\{\lambda:|\lambda|<1\}\cup\{1\}$ (the open disk from $B$
, plus $\lambda=1$ from the $E$-part); the boundary $|\lambda|=1$, $\lambda\ne 1
$, lies in the continuous spectrum of $B$.

### The spectrum of $S$ is the closed unit disk $\{\lambda\in\mathbb C:|\lambda|
\le 1\}$.
