# Problem

A specialized telecommunications network is being designed for a federation of 25 research laboratories. In the initial design phase, the architects are considering a "fully redundant" model where every single laboratory is directly linked to every other laboratory by a private fiber-optic cable.

However, the budget committee has introduced a strict connectivity requirement to ensure local resilience: the network must be built such that for any subset of exactly 4 laboratories chosen from the federation, those 4 labs must remain internally connected. This means that within any such group of 4, it must be possible to send data from any lab to any other lab using only the cables existing between those specific 4 labs (possibly passing through other labs within that group of 4).

Based on this requirement for every possible 4-lab cluster, determine the minimum total number of fiber-optic cables that must be installed across the entire country.
