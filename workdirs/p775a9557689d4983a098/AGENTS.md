# Problem

On an 8x8 grid of server ports, a cybersecurity firm has initialized two sets of defensive protocols. Eight "Obsidian" firewalls occupy the eight ports in the first row (Row 1), while eight "Alabaster" firewalls occupy the eight ports in the last row (Row 8). 

To optimize the network, an administrator must swap their positions so that the eight Alabaster firewalls occupy Row 1 and the eight Obsidian firewalls occupy Row 8. The protocols are relocated one at a time, alternating between an Obsidian move and an Alabaster move, with Obsidian starting first. 

Each protocol moves like a queen in chess: it can travel any number of vacant ports horizontally, vertically, or diagonally in a single move. A protocol cannot move through or land on a port currently occupied by another firewall. 

What is the minimum number of total moves required to successfully interchange the positions of the Obsidian and Alabaster firewalls?
