Let $N = 2020!$ and $M = 2019!$, so $N = 2020 \cdot M$. The $\omega_k$ are the $
N$-th roots of unity. We compute

$$S = \sum_{\omega^N=1} \frac{2^M - 1}{\omega^{2020}+2} = (2^M-1)\sum_{\omega^N=
1}\frac{1}{\omega^{2020}+2}.$$

**Reducing the sum.** Since $2020 \mid N$, the map $\omega \mapsto \omega^{2020}
$ sends $N$-th roots of unity onto $M$-th roots of unity, with kernel of size $\
gcd(2020,N)=2020$. Hence each $M$-th root of unity $\zeta$ is hit exactly $2020$
 times, giving

$$\sum_{\omega^N=1}\frac{1}{\omega^{2020}+2} = 2020\sum_{\zeta^M=1}\frac{1}{\zet
a+2}.$$

**Logarithmic derivative.** For $P(x)=x^M-1=\prod_{\zeta^M=1}(x-\zeta)$, we have
 $\frac{P'(x)}{P(x)}=\sum_{\zeta^M=1}\frac{1}{x-\zeta}$. Evaluating at $x=-2$:

$$\sum_{\zeta^M=1}\frac{1}{-2-\zeta} = \frac{M(-2)^{M-1}}{(-2)^M-1}, \qquad \sum
_{\zeta^M=1}\frac{1}{\zeta+2} = -\frac{M(-2)^{M-1}}{(-2)^M-1}=\frac{M(-2)^{M-1}}
{1-(-2)^M}.$$

Since $M=2019!$ is even: $(-2)^M = 2^M$ and $(-2)^{M-1}=-2^{M-1}$ (as $M-1$ is o
dd). Thus

$$\sum_{\zeta^M=1}\frac{1}{\zeta+2} = \frac{M\cdot(-2^{M-1})}{1-2^M} = \frac{M\c
dot 2^{M-1}}{2^M-1}.$$

**Combining.** The factor $(2^M-1)$ cancels:

$$S = (2^M-1)\cdot 2020 \cdot \frac{M\cdot 2^{M-1}}{2^M-1} = 2020\cdot M \cdot 2
^{M-1} = N\cdot 2^{M-1}.$$

**2-adic valuation.**

$$v_2(S) = v_2(N) + (M-1) = v_2(2020!) + 2019! - 1.$$

By Legendre's formula, $v_2(2020!) = 2020 - s_2(2020)$, where $s_2$ is the binar
y digit sum. Since $2020 = 11111100100_2$ has $s_2(2020)=7$:

$$v_2(2020!) = 2020 - 7 = 2013.$$

(Verified by direct summation: $1010+505+252+126+63+31+15+7+3+1=2013$.)

Therefore

$$n = v_2(S) = 2013 + 2019! - 1 = 2019! + 2012.$$

**Writing $n = a! + b$ with $a$ maximal and $b$ positive.** We need $a! \leq n-1
$ (so $b\geq 1$). Since $2019! \leq 2019!+2011$ but $2020! = 2020\cdot 2019! \gg
 2019!+2012$ (as $2019!\gg 2012$), the largest feasible $a$ is $2019$:

$$a = 2019, \qquad b = n - 2019! = 2012.$$

**Final answer.**

$$a + b = 2019 + 2012 = 4031, \qquad 4031 \bmod 1000 = \boxed{31}.$$
