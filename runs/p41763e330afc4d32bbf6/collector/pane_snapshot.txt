## Proof

Let $G = GL(n,\mathbb{C})$ and $H = \{A \in G : \det A \in \mathbb{R}\}$. Since
$A$ is invertible, $\det A \neq 0$, so in fact $H = \det{}^{-1}(\mathbb{R}^*)$ w
here $\mathbb{R}^* = \mathbb{R}\setminus\{0\}$.

**Step 1: Define the homomorphism.**

The determinant map
$$\det : GL(n,\mathbb{C}) \to \mathbb{C}^*$$
is a group homomorphism ($\det(AB)=\det A\det B$). Compose with the canonical qu
otient $\pi:\mathbb{C}^* \to \mathbb{C}^*/\mathbb{R}^*$:
$$\varphi := \pi \circ \det : GL(n,\mathbb{C}) \xrightarrow{\;\det\;} \mathbb{C}
^* \xrightarrow{\;\pi\;} \mathbb{C}^*/\mathbb{R}^*,$$
i.e. $\varphi(A) = \det(A)\cdot\mathbb{R}^*$. This is a group homomorphism as a
composition of homomorphisms.

**Step 2: Compute the kernel.**
$$\ker\varphi = \{A\in G : \det(A)\in\mathbb{R}^*\} = H.$$

**Step 3: Identify the image.**

The determinant is surjective onto $\mathbb{C}^*$: for any $z\in\mathbb{C}^*$, t
he diagonal matrix $\operatorname{diag}(z,1,\dots,1)$ has determinant $z$. Hence
$$\operatorname{im}(\varphi) = \pi(\mathbb{C}^*) = \mathbb{C}^*/\mathbb{R}^*.$$

**Step 4: Apply the first isomorphism theorem.**

By the theorem $G/\ker\varphi \cong \operatorname{im}(\varphi)$,
$$GL(n,\mathbb{C})/H \cong \mathbb{C}^*/\mathbb{R}^*.$$

**Step 5: Simplify $\mathbb{C}^*/\mathbb{R}^*$.**

Write every $z\in\mathbb{C}^*$ as $z = |z|\cdot u$ with $u = z/|z|\in S^1$ (the
unit circle) and $|z|\in\mathbb{R}_{>0}\subset\mathbb{R}^*$. Since $|z|\in\mathb
b{R}^*$, the coset $z\cdot\mathbb{R}^*$ depends only on $u = z/|z|\in S^1$.

Define $\psi:\mathbb{C}^* \to S^1$ by $\psi(z) = (z/|z|)^2$. This is a homomorph
ism:
$$\psi(z_1 z_2) = \left(\tfrac{z_1 z_2}{|z_1 z_2|}\right)^2 = \left(\tfrac{z_1}{
|z_1|}\right)^2\left(\tfrac{z_2}{|z_2|}\right)^2 = \psi(z_1)\psi(z_2).$$
Its kernel: $\psi(z)=1 \iff (z/|z|)^2=1 \iff z/|z|=\pm 1 \iff z\in\mathbb{R}^*$.
 So $\ker\psi = \mathbb{R}^*$, and $\psi$ is surjective (every $e^{i\theta}\in S
^1$ is $\psi(e^{i\theta/2})$). By the first isomorphism theorem,
$$\mathbb{C}^*/\mathbb{R}^* \cong S^1.$$

**Conclusion.**

$$\boxed{\,GL(n,\mathbb{C})/H \;\cong\; \mathbb{C}^*/\mathbb{R}^* \;\cong\; S^1\
,}$$

The quotient is isomorphic to the unit circle group $S^1 = \{z\in\mathbb{C}:|z|=
1\}$ under multiplication (equivalently $U(1)$, or $\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}$ under
 addition). The homomorphism realizing this is $\varphi(A) = \det(A)\bmod \mathb
b{R}^*$, with image $\mathbb{C}^*/\mathbb{R}^* \cong S^1$.
