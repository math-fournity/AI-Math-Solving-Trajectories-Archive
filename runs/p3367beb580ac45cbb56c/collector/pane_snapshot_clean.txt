## Proof

Let $\bar{f} = \int_0^1 f(x)\,dx$. By Taylor's theorem with Lagrange remainder,
for each $x$ there exists $\xi_x$ between $f(x)$ and $\bar{f}$ such that

$$g(f(x)) = g(\bar{f}) + g'(\bar{f})(f(x)-\bar{f}) + \tfrac{1}{2}g''(\xi_x)(f(x)
-\bar{f})^2.$$

Integrating over $[0,1]$:

$$\int_0^1 g(f(x))\,dx = g(\bar{f}) + g'(\bar{f})\underbrace{\int_0^1(f(x)-\bar{
f})\,dx}_{=\,0} + \tfrac{1}{2}\int_0^1 g''(\xi_x)(f(x)-\bar{f})^2\,dx.$$

Therefore

$$\left|g(\bar{f}) - \int_0^1 g(f(x))\,dx\right| = \frac{1}{2}\left|\int_0^1 g''
(\xi_x)(f(x)-\bar{f})^2\,dx\right| \leq \frac{1}{2}\max|g''|\int_0^1(f(x)-\bar{f
})^2\,dx = 2\,\mathrm{Var}(f),$$

where $\mathrm{Var}(f) = \int_0^1(f-\bar{f})^2\,dx$.

**Maximizing $\mathrm{Var}(f)$.** Since $f$ is continuous on $[0,1]$ with $|f'|\
leq 3$, the range of $f$ is at most $3$. Write $f([0,1])\subseteq[a,\,a+d]$ with
 $d\leq 3$ and $[a,a+d]\subseteq[-2,2]$.

For a monotone $f$ with range $d$, let $\rho(y)$ be the density of $f$-values (v
ia the inverse function). The constraint $|f'|\leq 3$ gives $\rho(y)\geq 1/3$, a
nd $\int_a^{a+d}\rho\,dy=1$. Setting $\rho = 1/3 + \mu$ with $\mu\geq 0$, $\int\
mu = 1-d/3$, the variance is concave in $\mu$, so the optimum splits the excess
mass equally between the two endpoints $a$ and $a+d$. This yields

$$\mathrm{Var}(f) = d^2\!\left(\frac{9-2d}{36}\right),\qquad 0\leq d\leq 3.$$

Differentiating: $\frac{d}{dd}\mathrm{Var} = \frac{d(3-d)}{6}\geq 0$ on $[0,3]$,
 so the maximum is at $d=3$:

$$\mathrm{Var}(f)\big|_{d=3} = \frac{9\cdot 3}{36} = \frac{3}{4}.$$

At $d=3$ the excess mass is $1-1=0$, so $\rho\equiv 1/3$ — i.e., $f$ is the **li
near ramp** $f(x)=a+3x$ with $a\in[-2,-1]$.

Then $\bar{f}=-\tfrac{1}{2}$, $g(\bar{f})=2\cdot\tfrac{1}{4}=\tfrac{1}{2}$, and

$$\int_0^1 g(f(x))\,dx = 2\int_0^1(-2+3x)^2\,dx = 2\int_0^1(4-12x+9x^2)\,dx = 2(
4-6+3)=2.$$

$$\left|g(\bar{f})-\int_0^1 g(f)\right| = \left|\tfrac{1}{2}-2\right| = \frac{3}
