## Proof

**Setup.** Let $A$ be a Noetherian domain of (Krull) dimension $1$, and let $p \
subset A$ be a non-zero prime ideal. We determine the maximal ideal of $A/p$.

**Step 1: $p$ is a maximal ideal of $A$.**

Since $A$ is a domain, $(0)$ is prime, so $(0) \in \operatorname{Spec} A$. The K
rull dimension of $A$ is $1$, meaning the longest chain of prime ideals has leng
th $1$:
$$
(0) \subsetneq \mathfrak{m}.
$$
Thus every prime ideal of $A$ is either $(0)$ or a maximal ideal (of height $1$)
. Since $p \neq (0)$ is prime, it must be maximal.

**Step 2: $A/p$ is a field.**

A quotient $A/I$ is a field if and only if $I$ is a maximal ideal. Since $p$ is
maximal, $A/p$ is a field.

**Step 3: $A/p$ is a Noetherian local domain.**

- *Noetherian*: quotients of Noetherian rings are Noetherian.
- *Domain*: $p$ is prime, so $A/p$ is an integral domain.
- *Local*: a field has a unique maximal ideal, namely the zero ideal $(0)$, so i
t is a local ring.

**Step 4: The maximal ideal of $A/p$ is $(0)$.**

As $A/p$ is a field, its only proper ideal is the zero ideal. The unique maximal
 ideal is therefore
$$
\mathfrak{m}_{A/p} \;=\; \overline{p} \;=\; p/p \;=\; (0) \;\subset\; A/p.
$$

**Conclusion.** Since $A$ has dimension $1$, every non-zero prime $p$ is maximal
, forcing $A/p$ to be a field. The maximal ideal of the Noetherian local domain
$A/p$ is the **zero ideal**:
$$
\boxed{\mathfrak{m}_{A/p} = (0).}
$$
