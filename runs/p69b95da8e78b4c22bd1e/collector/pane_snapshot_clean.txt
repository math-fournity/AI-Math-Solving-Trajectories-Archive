## Proof: Convergence Rate of $\hat Q_{xx}^{-1}$ to $Q_{xx}^{-1}$

### Setup

In the simple linear regression model $y_i = \beta_0 + \beta_1 x_i + \varepsilon
_i$, the design matrix $\mathbf{X}$ is $n \times 2$ with $i$-th row $\mathbf{x}_
i^T = (1, x_i)$. Define

$$\hat Q_{xx} = \frac{\mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{X}}{n} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n \mat
hbf{x}_i \mathbf{x}_i^T = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & \bar x \\ \bar x & \overline{x^2}
\end{pmatrix},$$

and its probability limit $Q_{xx} = E[\mathbf{x}_i \mathbf{x}_i^T] = \begin{pmat
rix} 1 & \mu_x \\ \mu_x & E[x^2] \end{pmatrix}$.

**Assumptions.** The regressors $\{x_i\}$ are i.i.d. with $E[x_i^4] < \infty$ (f
inite fourth moment), and $Q_{xx}$ is nonsingular, i.e., $\text{Var}(x_i) = E[x_
i^2] - \mu_x^2 > 0$.

### Step 1: $\hat Q_{xx} - Q_{xx} = O_p(n^{-1/2})$

Write

$$\hat Q_{xx} - Q_{xx} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n \left(\mathbf{x}_i \mathbf{x}_i
^T - E[\mathbf{x}_i \mathbf{x}_i^T]\right).$$

This is the sample mean of i.i.d. zero-mean random matrices $\mathbf{z}_i = \mat
hbf{x}_i \mathbf{x}_i^T - Q_{xx}$. The entries of $\mathbf{z}_i$ are $0$, $x_i -
 \mu_x$, and $x_i^2 - E[x^2]$. Since $E[x_i^4]<\infty$, each entry has finite va
riance, so $\text{Var}(\text{vec}(\mathbf{z}_i))$ is finite. By the multivariate
 Central Limit Theorem,

$$\sqrt{n}\,\text{vec}(\hat Q_{xx} - Q_{xx}) \stackrel{d}{\longrightarrow} N(\ma
thbf{0}, \Sigma),$$

where $\Sigma = \text{Var}(\text{vec}(\mathbf{z}_i))$ is finite. Convergence in
distribution to a non-degenerate limit implies $\sqrt{n}(\hat Q_{xx} - Q_{xx}) =
 O_p(1)$, hence

$$\hat Q_{xx} - Q_{xx} = O_p(n^{-1/2}). \tag{1}$$

### Step 2: Neumann expansion of the inverse

Since $Q_{xx}$ is nonsingular and $\hat Q_{xx} \stackrel{p}{\to} Q_{xx}$, with p
robability approaching one $\hat Q_{xx}$ is also nonsingular and $\|\hat Q_{xx}
- Q_{xx}\| < 1/\|Q_{xx}^{-1}\|$. On this event, the Neumann series gives:

$$\hat Q_{xx}^{-1} = \left(Q_{xx} + (\hat Q_{xx} - Q_{xx})\right)^{-1} = \left(I
 + Q_{xx}^{-1}(\hat Q_{xx}-Q_{xx})\right)^{-1} Q_{xx}^{-1}.$$

Expanding $(I + H)^{-1} = I - H + H^2 - \cdots$ with $H = Q_{xx}^{-1}(\hat Q_{xx
}-Q_{xx})$:

$$\hat Q_{xx}^{-1} = Q_{xx}^{-1} - Q_{xx}^{-1}(\hat Q_{xx}-Q_{xx})Q_{xx}^{-1} +
R_n,$$

where the remainder satisfies

$$R_n = Q_{xx}^{-1}(\hat Q_{xx}-Q_{xx})Q_{xx}^{-1}(\hat Q_{xx}-Q_{xx})Q_{xx}^{-1
}\left(I + Q_{xx}^{-1}(\hat Q_{xx}-Q_{xx})\right)^{-1}.$$

Therefore

$$\hat Q_{xx}^{-1} - Q_{xx}^{-1} = -Q_{xx}^{-1}(\hat Q_{xx}-Q_{xx})Q_{xx}^{-1} +
 R_n. \tag{2}$$
