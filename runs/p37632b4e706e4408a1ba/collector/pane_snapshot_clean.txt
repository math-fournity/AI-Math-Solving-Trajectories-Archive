## Setup and Key Observation

Given a faithful irreducible character $\chi$ of degree $d$, any two irreducible
 representations with character $\chi$ are equivalent (conjugate in $\mathrm{U}(
d)$) by the orthogonality relations. So if $\rho: G \to \mathrm{U}(d)$ is one su
ch representation, every other is $U\rho U^*$ for some $U \in \mathrm{U}(d)$.

For $V$ to lie in the image of some $\pi = U\rho U^*$, we need $V = U\rho(h)U^*$
 for some $h \in G$, meaning **$V$ and $\rho(h)$ must be conjugate in $\mathrm{U
}(d)$**, hence must have the **same eigenvalues**.

The condition $\chi([g]) = \mathrm{Trace}(V)$ only guarantees that $V$ and $\rho
(g)$ have the same **trace**, not the same eigenvalues. Two unitary matrices of
the same order and trace need not be conjugate.

## Counterexample: $G = S_5$, the standard representation

Let $G = S_5$ and let $\rho$ be the **standard representation** (the permutation
 representation on $\mathbb{C}^5$ restricted to the hyperplane $\sum x_i = 0$).
This is a faithful irreducible representation of degree $d = 4$ with character $
\chi(\sigma) = (\text{fixed points of } \sigma) - 1$.

**Conjugacy class of order $n = 6$:** Elements of cycle type $(3,2)$, e.g., $g =
 (12)(345)$, have order $6$ and $\chi(g) = 0 - 1 = -1$.

**Eigenvalues of $\rho(g)$:** The permutation matrix of $g$ on $\mathbb{C}^5$ ha
s eigenvalues from the 2-cycle $(12)$: $\{1, -1\}$, and from the 3-cycle $(345)$
: $\{1, \omega, \omega^2\}$ where $\omega = e^{2\pi i/3}$. Removing one copy of
$1$ (the trivial subrepresentation), the eigenvalues of $\rho(g)$ on the 4-dimen
sional standard representation are:
$$\{1,\; -1,\; \omega,\; \omega^2\} = \{1,\; \zeta^3,\; \zeta^2,\; \zeta^4\}$$
where $\zeta = e^{2\pi i/6}$. The trace is $1 + (-1) + \omega + \omega^2 = -1$,
confirming $\chi(g) = -1$.

**Constructing $V$:** Define the $4 \times 4$ unitary matrix
$$V = \mathrm{diag}(\zeta,\; \zeta^5,\; \zeta^3,\; \zeta^3) = \mathrm{diag}(\zet
a,\; \bar{\zeta},\; -1,\; -1).$$

- **Order:** $\mathrm{lcm}(6, 6, 2, 2) = 6 = n$. ✓
- **Trace:** $\zeta + \zeta^5 + \zeta^3 + \zeta^3 = 2\cos(\pi/3) + 2(-1) = 1 - 2
 = -1 = \chi([g])$. ✓

**Different eigenvalues:** The eigenvalue multiset of $V$ is $\{\zeta, \zeta^5,
\zeta^3, \zeta^3\}$, while the eigenvalue multiset of $\rho(g)$ is $\{1, \zeta^3
, \zeta^2, \zeta^4\}$. These are **distinct** multisets (the former contains $\z
eta, \zeta^5$ and two copies of $\zeta^3$; the latter contains $1, \zeta^2, \zet
a^4$ and one copy of $\zeta^3$). Therefore $V$ and $\rho(g)$ are **not conjugate
** in $\mathrm{U}(4)$.
