$$A = \sum_{\substack{m \ge 1 \\ m \equiv 1\,(3)}} \frac{1}{\sqrt{m}} \;-\; \sum

The series $B$ runs over $m \equiv 1 \pmod{6}$ (with $+$) and $m \equiv 5 \pmod{
6}$ (with $-$):

$$B = \sum_{\substack{m \ge 1 \\ m \equiv 1\,(6)}} \frac{1}{\sqrt{m}} \;-\; \sum
_{\substack{m \ge 1 \\ m \equiv 5\,(6)}} \frac{1}{\sqrt{m}}.$$

### Step 2: Split $A$ by parity

Every integer $\equiv 1 \pmod{3}$ is either $\equiv 1 \pmod{6}$ (odd) or $\equiv
 4 \pmod{6}$ (even). Every integer $\equiv 2 \pmod{3}$ is either $\equiv 5 \pmod
{6}$ (odd) or $\equiv 2 \pmod{6}$ (even). Therefore:

$$A = \underbrace{\left(\sum_{m \equiv 1\,(6)} \frac{1}{\sqrt{m}} - \sum_{m \equ

quiv 4\,(6)} \frac{1}{\sqrt{m}} - \sum_{m \equiv 2\,(6)} \frac{1}{\sqrt{m}}\righ
t)}_{=\, C}$$

so $A = B + C$, where $C$ is the "even part."

The integers $\equiv 4 \pmod{6}$ are $\{4, 10, 16, \ldots\} = \{6n+4 : n \ge 0\}

$$C = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{6n+4}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{6n+2}}\ri

$$C = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}\,\sqrt{3n+2}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt
