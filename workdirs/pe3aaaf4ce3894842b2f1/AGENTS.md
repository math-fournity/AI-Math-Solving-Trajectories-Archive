# Problem

In the coastal province of Archipelagia, a grid-based maritime territory is mapped out as a $13 \times 17$ array of square nautical zones. The local governor intends to lease out several rectangular "Safe-Harbor" zones to shipping companies. 

Each Safe-Harbor zone must occupy a block of exactly $1 \times 3$ (or $3 \times 1$) nautical zones. To ensure maximum safety and prevent any potential collisions or interference, the law dictates a strict "No-Contact Policy": no two Safe-Harbor zones may touch each other at any point, meaning they cannot share an edge and they cannot even touch at a single corner. Furthermore, all leased zones must fit entirely within the $13 \times 17$ grid boundaries.

What is the maximum number of such Safe-Harbor zones that can be established within this territory?
