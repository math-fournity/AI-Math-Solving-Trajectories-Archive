# Problem

A freight train is composed of 20 unique cargo containers coupled together in a single line on a straight track. Because the track is narrow, the only way to reorganize the containers is through a "shunt move," which consists of swapping the positions of any two containers that are currently coupled directly to one another.

The logistics manager needs to ensure that every one of the 20 containers undergoes a specific safety inspection. This inspection protocol requires that throughout the reorganization process, every single container must have occupied the very front position of the train (the first position) at least once and the very back position of the train (the 20th position) at least once.

Calculate the minimum number of shunt moves required to rearrange the containers such that every container has been placed at both the front and the back of the line at least once.
