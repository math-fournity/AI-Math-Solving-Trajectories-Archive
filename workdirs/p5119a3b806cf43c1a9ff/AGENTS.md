# Problem

In a bustling coastal trade hub, there are 250 independent merchant ships anchored in the harbor. The port authority requires a complete information network to be established: for every possible pair of ships, at least one "official data exchange" must occur between them (either Ship A sends a courier to Ship B, or Ship B sends a courier to Ship A).

To facilitate this, the port authority organizes "Signal Windows" in the evenings. During a single Signal Window, each ship can choose to act as either a **Transmitter** or a **Receiver**. 
- A ship designated as a Transmitter can send a courier to every ship currently designated as a Receiver.
- A ship designated as a Receiver can only receive couriers and cannot send any during that window.
- Ships can also choose to be inactive during a window, but they won't exchange data.

How many Signal Windows are required to ensure that every one of the 250 ships has either sent a courier to or received a courier from every other ship at least once?
