**Proof.**

$$\lim_{n\to\infty}\|x_n - y\| = \|x - y\| \quad \text{for every } y \in X. \tag
{H}$$

We prove $\|x_n - x\| \to 0$.

**Step 1: Convergence of norms.** Taking $y = 0$ in (H):
$$\|x_n\| \to \|x\|, \quad \text{hence } \|x_n\|^2 \to \|x\|^2. \tag{1}$$

In particular, $\{\|x_n\|\}$ is bounded.

**Step 2: Weak convergence via the inner product.** For any $y \in X$, expand th

$$\|x_n - y\|^2 = \|x_n\|^2 - 2\operatorname{Re}\langle x_n, y\rangle + \|y\|^2,
$$
$$\|x - y\|^2 = \|x\|^2 - 2\operatorname{Re}\langle x, y\rangle + \|y\|^2.$$

By (H), $\|x_n - y\|^2 \to \|x - y\|^2$. By (1), $\|x_n\|^2 \to \|x\|^2$. Subtra
cting:
$$\operatorname{Re}\langle x_n, y\rangle \to \operatorname{Re}\langle x, y\rangl
e \quad \text{for all } y \in X. \tag{2}$$

In the complex case, replacing $y$ by $iy$ in (2) yields $\operatorname{Im}\lang

$$\langle x_n, y\rangle \to \langle x, y\rangle \quad \text{for all } y \in X. \

**Step 3: Extending to the completion.** Since $\|x_n\|$ is bounded, say $\|x_n\

$$\langle x_n, y\rangle \to \langle x, y\rangle \quad \text{for all } y \in H. \

**Step 4: Norm convergence.** Since $x \in H$, we may set $y = x$ in (4):
$$\langle x_n, x\rangle \to \langle x, x\rangle = \|x\|^2. \tag{5}$$

Now compute directly:
$$\|x_n - x\|^2 = \|x_n\|^2 - 2\operatorname{Re}\langle x_n, x\rangle + \|x\|^2.

$$\|x_n - x\|^2 \to \|x\|^2 - 2\|x\|^2 + \|x\|^2 = 0.$$

Therefore $\|x_n - x\| \to 0$, i.e., $x_n \to x$ in norm. $\blacksquare$
