# Problem

In a specialized logistics network, two distribution centers handle a total of $x$ and $y$ metric tons of cargo respectively, where $x$ and $y$ are positive integers. The operational efficiency of the network is governed by their greatest common divisor, denoted as $d$.

The network's complexity is measured by a "Processing Index." The primary constraint on this index is defined by the following equilibrium:
The ceiling of the ratio between the product $(x \cdot d)$ and $y$, when multiplied by the fraction $(x/d)$, must exactly equal the ceiling of the expansion $(\sqrt{y}+1)^2$.

Additionally, the synchronization of the two centers requires that the absolute difference between their cargo loads, $|x - y|$, must equal a specific positive integer constant $k$.

For any given positive integer $k$, let $S(k)$ represent the total number of valid integer pairs $(x, y)$ that satisfy these two operational constraints. Determine the value of $S(k)$ as a function of $k$.
