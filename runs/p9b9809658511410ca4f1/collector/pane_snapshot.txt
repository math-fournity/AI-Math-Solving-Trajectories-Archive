## Key Reduction

Let $A$ be the $2015 \times 2015$ 0-1 matrix with $A_{ij} = 1 \iff j \in S_i$. T
he number of permutations $\sigma$ with $\sigma(i) \in S_i$ for all $i$ is exact
ly the **permanent** of $A$:

$$\text{perm}(A) = \sum_{\sigma \in S_n} \prod_{i=1}^n A_{i,\sigma(i)}.$$

**Classical fact:** $\text{perm}(A) \equiv \det(A) \pmod{2}$, since $\text{sgn}(
\sigma) \equiv 1 \pmod{2}$ for all $\sigma$.

Therefore $\text{perm}(A)$ is odd $\iff \det(A) \equiv 1 \pmod{2} \iff A$ is inv
ertible over $\mathbb{F}_2$.

So $N$ equals the number of invertible $2015 \times 2015$ matrices over $\mathbb
{F}_2$:

$$N = \prod_{i=0}^{2014}(2^{2015} - 2^i).$$

## Computing $k_2 = v_2(N)$

Factor out powers of 2:

$$N = \prod_{i=0}^{2014} 2^i(2^{2015-i}-1), \qquad v_2(N) = \sum_{i=0}^{2014} i
= \frac{2014 \cdot 2015}{2} = 1007 \times 2015 = 2029105.$$

## Computing $k_3 = v_3(N)$

Since $v_3(2^i)=0$, with the substitution $j = 2015-i$ (so $j$ runs from $1$ to
$2015$):

$$v_3(N) = \sum_{j=1}^{2015} v_3(2^j - 1).$$

Since $2 \equiv -1 \pmod{3}$, we have $2^j \equiv (-1)^j \pmod{3}$, so $3 \mid 2
^j - 1 \iff j$ even. For odd $j$, $v_3(2^j-1)=0$.

For even $j = 2m$: $2^j - 1 = 4^m - 1$. By **LTE** ($3 \mid 4-1$, $3 \nmid 4,1$)
:

$$v_3(4^m - 1) = v_3(4-1) + v_3(m) = 1 + v_3(m).$$

Even $j \in \{2,4,\dots,2014\}$ gives $m \in \{1,2,\dots,1007\}$, so:

$$v_3(N) = \sum_{m=1}^{1007}\bigl(1 + v_3(m)\bigr) = 1007 + \sum_{m=1}^{1007} v_
3(m).$$

Using Legendre's formula:

$$\sum_{m=1}^{1007} v_3(m) = \sum_{k\ge 1}\left\lfloor\frac{1007}{3^k}\right\rfl
oor = 335 + 111 + 37 + 12 + 4 + 1 = 500.$$

Therefore $k_3 = 1007 + 500 = 1507$.

## Final Answer

$$k_2 + k_3 = 2029105 + 1507 = \boxed{2030612}.$$
