Looking at this problem, I need to show that a chain of 7 circles, each external
ly tangent to the previous and passing through consecutive vertices of a triangl
e (cycling mod 3), closes up.

**Key observation:** ωₖ passes through Aₖ, Aₖ₊₁ and ωₖ₊₁ passes through Aₖ₊₁, Aₖ
₊₂, so they share Aₖ₊₁. Since they are tangent (one common point), **Aₖ₊₁ is the
 tangency point**, and they share a common tangent line there.

**Characteristic angle.** For a circle ω through ordered points P, Q, the direct
ed angle ∠PXQ (mod π) is constant for all X on ω; call it θ(ω). Placing P=(−1,0)
, Q=(1,0) with center (0,h), one computes θ = −2 arctan(h+√(1+h²)), which is str
ictly monotone in h. Hence **θ(ω) mod π uniquely determines ω** among circles th
rough P, Q.

**Tangent-chord theorem (directed form).** If t_P, t_Q are tangents to ω at P, Q
:
$$\angle(t_P, PQ) = +\theta(\omega), \qquad \angle(t_Q, QP) = -\theta(\omega).$$

**Setting up the recurrence.** Let θₖ = θ(ωₖ) w.r.t. (Aₖ, Aₖ₊₁). At the tangency
 point Aₖ₊₁, with common tangent t:
$$\angle(t,\, A_{k+1}A_k) = -\theta_k, \qquad \angle(t,\, A_{k+1}A_{k+2}) = +\th
eta_{k+1}.$$
Subtracting:
$$\theta_k + \theta_{k+1} = -\angle(A_{k+1}A_{k+2},\, A_{k+1}A_k) \pmod{\pi}.$$

Denote the directed angle of the triangle at A_{k+1} by β_{k+1}. Since vertices
cycle with period 3 (A₄=A₁, A₅=A₂, …), the six equations are:

| k | tangency at | equation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A₂ | θ₁ + θ₂ = −β₂ |
| 2 | A₃ | θ₂ + θ₃ = −β₃ |
| 3 | A₁ | θ₃ + θ₄ = −β₁ |
| 4 | A₂ | θ₄ + θ₅ = −β₂ |
| 5 | A₃ | θ₅ + θ₆ = −β₃ |
| 6 | A₁ | θ₆ + θ₇ = −β₁ |

**Solving.** Back-substituting from the first equation:

- θ₂ = −β₂ − θ₁
- θ₃ = −β₃ − θ₂ = −β₃ + β₂ + θ₁
- θ₄ = −β₁ − θ₃ = −β₁ + β₃ − β₂ − θ₁
- θ₅ = −β₂ − θ₄ = β₁ − β₃ + θ₁
- θ₆ = −β₃ − θ₅ = −β₁ − θ₁
- **θ₇ = −β₁ − θ₆ = −β₁ + β₁ + θ₁ = θ₁**

All the triangle angles cancel in the final step — the period-3 structure of the
 β's matches the period-2 structure of the alternating recurrence over 6 = 2×3 s
teps.

**Conclusion.** θ₇ ≡ θ₁ (mod π). Both ω₁ and ω₇ pass through A₁ and A₂. Since th
e characteristic angle mod π uniquely determines a circle through two given poin
ts, **ω₇ = ω₁**. ∎
