# Problem

In the research department of a thermal energy facility, a specialist is calibrating a prototype heat exchanger. The stability of the system depends on the relationship between a controlled input temperature $x$ and a variable cooling coefficient $y$. 

The input temperature $x$ is restricted to a specific safe operating range such that $1/3 < x < 5$. 

The energy balance within the exchanger is governed by the following transcendental equilibrium equation:
$$27^{3x^2 + xy} = (1 + xy)27^{15x}$$

How many distinct integer values of the cooling coefficient $y$ exist for which there is at least one valid input temperature $x$ in the safe operating range that satisfies this equilibrium equation?
