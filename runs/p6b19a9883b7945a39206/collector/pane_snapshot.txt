 $fg=gf$. Hence $f$ maps $g$-orbits to $g$-orbits of the same size. In particula
r $f(7)=7$ (the only fixed point of $g$), and $f^3(7)=7=g(7)$ ✓ automatically.

$$f(i,t)=(\sigma(i),\,t\oplus \epsilon_i),\qquad \sigma\in S_6,\;\epsilon_i\in\{
0,1\}.$$
