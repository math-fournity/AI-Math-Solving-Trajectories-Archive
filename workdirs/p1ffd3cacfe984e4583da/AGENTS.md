# Problem

In a high-tech server farm, a massive $100 \times 100$ grid of tile-shaped processor slots is being optimized for cooling. Each $1 \times 1$ unit slot can be modified by installing a triangular cooling fin. These fins are all identical isosceles right triangles with leg lengths of $1$.

The installation must follow strict engineering constraints:
1. Every fin must be placed such that its hypotenuse perfectly matches one of the two diagonals of a $1 \times 1$ unit slot. 
2. No two fins can overlap or share the same interior space.
3. Every single segment of the grid lines (the $1$-unit sides of the slots) must serve as a leg for exactly one cooling fin.

A unit slot is designated as a "blank square" if neither of its two diagonals is occupied by the hypotenuse of any installed cooling fin. Based on these configurations, what is the maximum possible number of blank squares in the $100 \times 100$ grid?
