# Fundamental Weights of \(SU(3)\)

## Setup

\(SU(3)\) has Lie algebra \(\mathfrak{su}(3)\) of type \(A_2\), rank 2. Its root
 system lives in a 2‑dimensional Euclidean plane. Choose simple roots of equal l
ength with angle \(120^\circ\):

\[
\alpha_1 = (1,\,0),\qquad \alpha_2 = \Bigl(-\tfrac12,\,\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\Bigr),
\qquad |\alpha_i|^2 = 1.
\]

The **Cartan matrix** is

\[
A_{ij}=\frac{2(\alpha_i\cdot\alpha_j)}{|\alpha_j|^2}
\;\Longrightarrow\;
A=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}.
\]

## Definition of fundamental weights

The fundamental weights \(\omega_1,\omega_2\) are defined by

\[
\frac{2(\omega_i\cdot\alpha_j)}{|\alpha_j|^2}=\delta_{ij}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\omega_i\cdot\alpha_j=\frac{\delta_{ij}}{2}\quad(\text{since }|\alpha_j|^2=1).
\]

Equivalently, writing \(\omega_i=\sum_k c_{ik}\alpha_k\), the coefficient matrix
 is \(A^{-1}=\frac13\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}\). Hence

\[
\boxed{\;\omega_1=\frac{2\alpha_1+\alpha_2}{3},\qquad \omega_2=\frac{\alpha_1+2\
alpha_2}{3}\;}
\]

## Explicit coordinates

\[
\omega_1=\frac{2(1,0)+(-\tfrac12,\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2})}{3}
        =\frac{(\tfrac32,\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2})}{3}
        =\Bigl(\tfrac12,\,\tfrac{\sqrt3}{6}\Bigr),
\]

\[
\omega_2=\frac{(1,0)+2(-\tfrac12,\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2})}{3}
        =\frac{(0,\sqrt3)}{3}
        =\Bigl(0,\,\tfrac{\sqrt3}{3}\Bigr).
\]

**Verification:**

- \(\omega_1\cdot\alpha_1=\tfrac12\cdot1+0=\tfrac12=\tfrac{\delta_{11}}2\;\check
mark\)
- \(\omega_1\cdot\alpha_2=\tfrac12(-\tfrac12)+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{6}\cdot\tfrac{\sqrt
3}{2}=-\tfrac14+\tfrac14=0=\tfrac{\delta_{12}}2\;\checkmark\)
- \(\omega_2\cdot\alpha_1=0=\tfrac{\delta_{21}}2\;\checkmark\)
- \(\omega_2\cdot\alpha_2=0+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{3}\cdot\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}=\tfrac12=\tf
rac{\delta_{22}}2\;\checkmark\)

Both fundamental weights are confirmed.

## Geometric picture (angular positions)

\[
\begin{aligned}
\alpha_1 &: 0^\circ, & \alpha_2 &: 120^\circ, & \theta=\alpha_1+\alpha_2 &: 60^\
circ,\\
\omega_1 &: 30^\circ, & \omega_2 &: 90^\circ.
\end{aligned}
\]

The **fundamental Weyl chamber** is the sector bounded by the hyperplanes orthog
onal to the simple roots: the line at \(90^\circ\) (⊥ to \(\alpha_1\)) and the l
ine at \(30^\circ\) (⊥ to \(\alpha_2\), since \(120^\circ-90^\circ=30^\circ\)).
Thus the chamber is the \(60^\circ\) wedge between \(30^\circ\) and \(90^\circ\)
, and the two fundamental weights lie exactly on its two walls — as they must.

## Summary

The two fundamental weights of \(SU(3)\) (type \(A_2\)) are

\[
\boxed{\;\omega_1=\frac{2\alpha_1+\alpha_2}{3}=\Bigl(\tfrac12,\tfrac{\sqrt3}{6}\
Bigr),\qquad
       \omega_2=\frac{\alpha_1+2\alpha_2}{3}=\Bigl(0,\tfrac{\sqrt3}{3}\Bigr)\;}
\]

They generate the weight lattice of \(SU(3)\); the irreducible representation wi
th highest weight \(m_1\omega_1+m_2\omega_2\) is the \((m_1,m_2)\) Dynkin‑label
representation (e.g. \(\omega_1\)=**3**, \(\omega_2\)=\(\bar{\mathbf 3}\), \(\om
ega_1+\omega_2\)=**8** adjoint). They sit at \(30^\circ\) and \(90^\circ\), on t
he two walls of the fundamental Weyl chamber, as shown in the diagrams above.
