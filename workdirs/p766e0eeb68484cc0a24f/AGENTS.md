# Problem

In the city of Decapolis, ten watchtowers are positioned as the vertices of a perfect regular decagon. To facilitate communication, the city council has authorized the construction of a straight underground telegraph cable between every possible pair of towers. These cables represent all possible diagonals and sides, but for this project, we are only concerned with the cables connecting non-adjacent towers (the diagonals).

The engineers have noted a specific architectural constraint of this decagon layout: if three or more cables happen to cross at the exact same point inside the city, it is guaranteed that at least one of those intersecting cables passes directly through the exact center of the city (the center of the decagon's circumcircle).

A maintenance hub must be installed at every single point where two or more of these diagonal cables intersect within the interior of the city (excluding the watchtowers themselves).

How many distinct maintenance hubs are required in the interior of Decapolis?
