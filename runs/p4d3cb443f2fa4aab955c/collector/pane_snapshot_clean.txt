## Proof

**Coordinate setup.** Place the square platform (side length 4) with vertices at
 $(0,0), (4,0), (4,4), (0,4)$.

- **Northern edge** (top): from $(0,4)$ to $(4,4)$.
- **Eastern edge** (right): from $(4,0)$ to $(4,4)$.

**Sensor A.** The equilateral triangular boom on the northern edge has base from
 $(0,4)$ to $(4,4)$ and extends outward (north, $+y$). Its apex lies above the m
idpoint of the base at distance equal to the triangle height $h = \tfrac{\sqrt{3
}}{2}\cdot 4 = 2\sqrt{3}$:

$$A = \left(2,\; 4 + 2\sqrt{3}\right).$$

**Sensor B.** The equilateral triangular boom on the eastern edge has base from
$(4,0)$ to $(4,4)$ and extends outward (east, $+x$). Its apex lies to the right
of the midpoint at the same height $2\sqrt{3}$:

$$B = \left(4 + 2\sqrt{3},\; 2\right).$$

**Distance computation.**

$$\Delta x = (4 + 2\sqrt{3}) - 2 = 2 + 2\sqrt{3},$$
$$\Delta y = 2 - (4 + 2\sqrt{3}) = -(2 + 2\sqrt{3}).$$

$$AB^2 = (2+2\sqrt{3})^2 + (2+2\sqrt{3})^2 = 2(2+2\sqrt{3})^2.$$

$$(2+2\sqrt{3})^2 = 4 + 8\sqrt{3} + 12 = 16 + 8\sqrt{3}.$$

$$AB^2 = 2(16 + 8\sqrt{3}) = 32 + 16\sqrt{3}.$$

**Expressing as $\sqrt{m}+\sqrt{n}$.** We need

$$(\sqrt{m}+\sqrt{n})^2 = m + n + 2\sqrt{mn} = 32 + 16\sqrt{3}.$$

Matching rational and irrational parts:

$$m + n = 32, \qquad 2\sqrt{mn} = 16\sqrt{3} \implies mn = 192.$$

So $m,n$ are roots of $t^2 - 32t + 192 = 0$:

$$t = \frac{32 \pm \sqrt{1024 - 768}}{2} = \frac{32 \pm 16}{2} \in \{24,\, 8\}.$
$

**Verification.** $\sqrt{24}+\sqrt{8} = 2\sqrt{6}+2\sqrt{2}$, and

$$(2\sqrt{6}+2\sqrt{2})^2 = 24 + 8 + 8\sqrt{12} = 32 + 16\sqrt{3}. \;\checkmark$
$

Thus $m = 24$, $n = 8$ (in either order), and

$$m + n = 24 + 8 = \boxed{32}.$$
