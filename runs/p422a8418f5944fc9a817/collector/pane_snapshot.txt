## Proof

We use the **Schwarz–Pick lemma**: if $f \colon \mathbb{D} \to \mathbb{D}$ is ho
lomorphic, then for every $z \in \mathbb{D}$,

$$|f'(z)| \le \frac{1 - |f(z)|^2}{1 - |z|^2}.$$

**Upper bound.** Applying this at $z = 0$ gives

$$|f'(0)| \le 1 - |f(0)|^2.$$

For $f \in M$ we have $f(0) = \tfrac{2}{3}$, so

$$|f'(0)| \le 1 - \left(\tfrac{2}{3}\right)^2 = 1 - \tfrac{4}{9} = \tfrac{5}{9}.
$$

**Attainment.** Consider the disk automorphism

$$\varphi(z) = \frac{z + \tfrac{2}{3}}{1 + \tfrac{2}{3}z}.$$

This is a Möbius transformation with a pole at $z = -\tfrac{3}{2} \notin \mathbb
{D}$, hence holomorphic on $\mathbb{D}$. It is a standard automorphism of $\math
bb{D}$ (it equals $\phi_{-2/3}$ in the notation $\phi_a(z) = \frac{z - a}{1 - \b
ar a z}$), so it maps $\mathbb{D}$ biholomorphically onto itself. We check:

$$\varphi(0) = \tfrac{2}{3}, \qquad \varphi'(z) = \frac{(1+\tfrac{2}{3}z) - \tfr
ac{2}{3}(z+\tfrac{2}{3})}{(1+\tfrac{2}{3}z)^2} = \frac{1 - \tfrac{4}{9}}{(1+\tfr
ac{2}{3}z)^2} = \frac{5/9}{(1+\tfrac{2}{3}z)^2},$$

so $\varphi'(0) = \tfrac{5}{9}$, and $|\varphi'(0)| = \tfrac{5}{9}$.

Thus $\varphi \in M$ and it attains the upper bound. The supremum is therefore a
 maximum, equal to

$$\boxed{\dfrac{5}{9}}.$$

### PROOF COMPLETE
