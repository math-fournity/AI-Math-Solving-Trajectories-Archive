## Solution

**Setting up linear models.** Let $t$ be the year, with $t=1970$ as the referenc
e point (let $x = t - 1970$, so $x \in [0, 10]$).

**RedCloud** grows from 900 (at $x=0$) to 2700 (at $x=10$):
$$R(x) = 900 + \frac{2700-900}{10}\,x = 900 + 180x$$

**BlueStream** grows from 1475 (at $x=0$) to 2230 (at $x=10$):
$$B(x) = 1475 + \frac{2230-1475}{10}\,x = 1475 + 75.5\,x$$

**Finding the crossover.** RedCloud surpasses BlueStream when $R(x) > B(x)$:
$$900 + 180x > 1475 + 75.5\,x$$
$$(180 - 75.5)\,x > 1475 - 900$$
$$104.5\,x > 575$$
$$x > \frac{575}{104.5} = \frac{5750}{1045} = \frac{1150}{209} \approx 5.5024$$

**Interpretation.** The crossover occurs at
$$t = 1970 + 5.5024 \approx 1975.50$$

This is approximately the **middle of 1975** (around early July 1975).

**Verification at integer years:**
- End of 1975 ($x=5$): $R = 900 + 900 = 1800$; $B = 1475 + 377.5 = 1852.5$. Blue
Stream still leads.
- End of 1976 ($x=6$): $R = 900 + 1080 = 1980$; $B = 1475 + 453 = 1928$. RedClou
d leads.

The capacities become equal at $t \approx 1975.50$, and RedCloud is strictly gre
ater for all $t > 1975.50$. The first calendar year in which RedCloud's capacity
 surpassed BlueStream's is therefore **1975**.
