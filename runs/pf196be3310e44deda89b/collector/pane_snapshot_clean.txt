## Proof

We show that the connected components of $(\mathbb{R}, \mathcal{T}_{\text{lower
limit}})$ are exactly the singletons $\{x\}$, $x \in \mathbb{R}$. Equivalently,
the Sorgenfrey line is **totally disconnected**.

### Key lemma: every half-open interval $[a,b)$ is clopen

Recall the lower limit topology has basis $\{[a,b) : a < b\}$, so every $[a,b)$
is open by definition.

We claim $[a,b)$ is also closed. Its complement is
$$\mathbb{R}\setminus[a,b) = (-\infty, a) \cup [b, +\infty).$$

- $(-\infty, a) = \bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty}[a-n,\, a)$, a union of basis elements,
hence open.
- $[b,+\infty) = \bigcup_{n=0}^{\infty}[b+n,\, b+n+1)$, a union of basis element
s, hence open.

So the complement of $[a,b)$ is open, hence $[a,b)$ is closed. Therefore every $
[a,b)$ is **clopen**. ∎

### Every component is a singleton

Fix $x \in \mathbb{R}$. We show that no other point $y \neq x$ lies in the conne
cted component of $x$.

**Case 1: $y > x$.** The set $[x, y)$ is clopen by the lemma. It contains $x$ bu
t not $y$. Thus
$$[x,y) \quad\text{and}\quad \mathbb{R}\setminus[x,y)$$
form a separation of $\mathbb{R}$ into two disjoint nonempty open sets, with $x
\in [x,y)$ and $y \in \mathbb{R}\setminus[x,y)$. Hence $x$ and $y$ cannot lie in
 the same connected subset, so $y$ is not in the component of $x$.

**Case 2: $y < x$.** The set $[y, x)$ is clopen by the lemma. It contains $y$ bu
t not $x$. Again $[y,x)$ and its complement form a separation separating $x$ fro
m $y$, so $y$ is not in the component of $x$.

In both cases, every $y \neq x$ is excluded from the connected component of $x$.
 Hence the component of $x$ is exactly $\{x\}$.

### Conclusion

The connected components of $(\mathbb{R}, \mathcal{T}_{\text{lower limit}})$ are
 precisely the singleton sets
$$\boxed{\{x\}, \quad x \in \mathbb{R}.}$$
The space is totally disconnected (and, since singletons are not open — no $[a,b
)$ is a singleton — it is not discrete).
