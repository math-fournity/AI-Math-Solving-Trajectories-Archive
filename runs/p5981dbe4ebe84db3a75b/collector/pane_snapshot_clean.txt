## Solution

**Setup.** Place the orthocenter H at the origin. Let $\vec{a}, \vec{b}, \vec{c}
$ be the position vectors of A, B, C, with $|\vec{a}|=2$, $|\vec{b}|=12$, $|\vec
{c}|=9$.

**Orthocenter condition.** Since AH ⊥ BC, we have $\vec{a}\cdot(\vec{b}-\vec{c})
=0$, i.e. $\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}=\vec{a}\cdot\vec{c}$. Similarly from BH ⊥ AC and
CH ⊥ AB:

$$\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}=\vec{a}\cdot\vec{c}=\vec{b}\cdot\vec{c}=:k.$$

**Finding k.** Since A, B, C lie in a plane through H, the three vectors are lin
early dependent, so the Gram matrix has determinant 0:

$$\det\begin{pmatrix}4&k&k\\k&144&k\\k&k&81\end{pmatrix}=4(144\cdot81-k^2)-k(81k
-k^2)+k(k^2-144k)$$
$$=46656+2k^3-229k^2=0.$$

Since the triangle is acute, H lies inside the triangle, so each angle $\angle A
HB, \angle BHC, \angle CHA$ exceeds 90°, giving $k<0$. Testing $k=-27/2$:

$$2\!\left(-\tfrac{27}{2}\right)^3-229\!\left(\tfrac{729}{4}\right)+46656=-\tfra
c{19683}{4}-\tfrac{166941}{4}+46656=-\tfrac{186624}{4}+46656=0.\ \checkmark$$

So $k=-\tfrac{27}{2}$.

**Side lengths.**
$$|AB|^2=|\vec{b}-\vec{a}|^2=4+144-2k=148+27=175,\quad AB=5\sqrt7.$$
$$|AC|^2=4+81-2k=85+27=112,\quad AC=4\sqrt7.$$
$$|BC|^2=144+81-2k=225+27=252,\quad BC=6\sqrt7.$$

**Area via Heron's formula.** With $s=\tfrac{15\sqrt7}{2}$:

$$\text{Area}^2=s(s{-}a)(s{-}b)(s{-}c)=\frac{15\sqrt7}{2}\cdot\frac{3\sqrt7}{2}\
cdot\frac{5\sqrt7}{2}\cdot\frac{7\sqrt7}{2}=\frac{15\cdot3\cdot5\cdot7\cdot49}{1
6}=\frac{77175}{16}.$$

Since $77175=5^2\cdot3^2\cdot7^3$, we get $\sqrt{77175}=105\sqrt7$.

**Verification.** Circumradius $R=\frac{abc}{4\,\text{Area}}=\frac{6\sqrt7\cdot4
\sqrt7\cdot5\sqrt7}{4\cdot105\sqrt7/4}=\frac{840\sqrt7}{105\sqrt7}=8$. Then $AH=
2R\cos A=2$, $BH=2R\cos B=12$, $CH=2R\cos C=9$ gives $\cos A=\tfrac18$, $\cos B=
\tfrac34$, $\cos C=\tfrac{9}{16}$, and the identity $\cos^2\!A+\cos^2\!B+\cos^2\
!C+2\cos A\cos B\cos C=\tfrac{4+144+81}{256}+\tfrac{54}{512}=\tfrac{512}{512}=1$
 confirms consistency. $\checkmark$

$$\boxed{\text{Area}=\dfrac{105\sqrt{7}}{4}\text{ km}^2}$$
