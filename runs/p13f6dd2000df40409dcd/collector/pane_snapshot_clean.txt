## Solution

**Claim:** No. Not every closed subgroup of a compactly generated locally compac
t group need be compactly generated.

**Counterexample.** Let $G = F_2$, the free group on two generators $a, b$, equi
pped with the **discrete topology**.

**Step 1: $G$ is locally compact.** Every discrete group is locally compact, sin
ce each point has a compact (finite) neighborhood, namely $\{g\}$ itself.

**Step 2: $G$ is compactly generated.** In a discrete group, a subset is compact
 if and only if it is finite. The group $G = F_2$ is generated (as a group) by t
he finite set $S = \{a, b, a^{-1}, b^{-1}\}$. Since $S$ is finite, it is compact
 in the discrete topology. The subgroup generated by $S$ is all of $G$, so $G$ i
s compactly generated.

**Step 3: Construct a closed subgroup that is not compactly generated.** Let $H
= [F_2, F_2]$, the commutator subgroup of $F_2$.

- **$H$ is closed:** In the discrete topology, *every* subset is closed. In part
icular, every subgroup is closed.

- **$H$ is not compactly generated:** In a discrete group, compactly generated i
s equivalent to finitely generated (since compact = finite). So it suffices to s
how $H$ is not finitely generated.

  By the Nielsen–Schreier theorem, every subgroup of a free group is free. Thus
$H$ is a free group. Its rank is determined by the Schreier index formula: if $H
$ has index $[G : H]$ in $G = F_2$ (which has rank 2), then
  $$\operatorname{rank}(H) = (2 - 1)\cdot [G : H] + 1 = [G : H] + 1.$$

  The abelianization of $F_2$ is $\mathbb{Z}^2$, and $H = [F_2, F_2]$ is the ker
nel of the abelianization map $F_2 \to \mathbb{Z}^2$. Therefore $G/H \cong \math
bb{Z}^2$, which is infinite, so $[G : H] = \infty$.

  Hence $\operatorname{rank}(H) = \infty$: $H$ is a free group of countably infi
nite rank, and in particular is **not finitely generated**.

  Since $H$ is discrete and not finitely generated, it is **not compactly genera
ted**.

**Conclusion.** We have exhibited a locally compact, compactly generated group $
G = F_2$ (discrete) and a closed subgroup $H = [F_2, F_2]$ that is not compactly
 generated. Therefore the answer to the question is **no**: it is not true in ge
neral that every closed subgroup of a compactly generated locally compact group
is compactly generated.
