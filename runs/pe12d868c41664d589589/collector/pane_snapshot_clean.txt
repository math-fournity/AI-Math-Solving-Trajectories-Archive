## Proof

**Setup.** Place the equilateral base $KLM$ (side 10) in the $z=0$ plane:
$$K=(0,0,0),\quad L=(10,0,0),\quad M=(5,\,5\sqrt3,\,0).$$
The area of $\triangle KLM$ is $25\sqrt3$. Since the volume is 100, the height f
rom $N$ to the base is
$$|h|=\frac{3\cdot 100}{25\sqrt3}=4\sqrt3.$$
Write $N=(a,\,b,\,h)$ with $h=\pm 4\sqrt3$.

$T$ is the midpoint of $MN$, so $T=\!\left(\frac{5+a}{2},\,\frac{5\sqrt3+b}{2},\
,\frac{h}{2}\right)$.

**Key observation (midpoint property).** Since $T$ lies on segment $MN$, the poi
nts $K,M,N,T$ are coplanar, and $T$ bisects $MN$, so:
$$MT]=NT]=\tfrac12MN],\qquad [LMT]=[LNT]=\tfrac12[LMN].$$

**Equal inradius condition.** For a tetrahedron, $r = 3V/S$. Both sub-pyramids $
KLMT$ and $KLNT$ have volume 50 (the plane $KLT$ bisects $KLMN$). Equal inradii
$\Rightarrow$ equal surface areas:

$$\underbrace{LM]}_{25\sqrt3}+LT]+MT]+[LMT]=LN]+LT]+NT]+[LNT].$$

Cancel $LT]$ and apply the midpoint property:

$$25\sqrt3+\tfrac12MN]+\tfrac12[LMN]=LN]+\tfrac12MN]+\tfrac12[LMN].$$

$$\boxed{LN]=25\sqrt3.}$$

**Computing the constraint on $b$.** With $\vec{KL}=(10,0,0)$, $\vec{KN}=(a,b,h)
$:
$$\vec{KL}\times\vec{KN}=(0,\,-10h,\,10b),\quad |\vec{KL}\times\vec{KN}|=10\sqrt
{h^2+b^2}.$$
$$LN]=5\sqrt{h^2+b^2}=25\sqrt3 \;\Longrightarrow\; h^2+b^2=75.$$
Since $h^2=48$: $b^2=27$, so $b=\pm 3\sqrt3$. The coordinate $a$ is **free**.

### Part (a): Dihedral angle at edge $KL$

Take a plane perpendicular to $KL$ through $K$ (the $yz$-plane). The face $KLM$
intersects it in the ray toward $M$: direction $(0,1,0)$. The face $KLN$ interse

The dihedral angle $\varphi$ at $KL$ satisfies:
$$\cos\varphi=\frac{(0,1,0)\cdot(0,b,h)}{\sqrt{b^2+h^2}}=\frac{b}{5\sqrt3}=\frac
{\pm 3\sqrt3}{5\sqrt3}=\pm\frac35.$$

This depends only on $b$ (not on $a$), so exactly two values are possible:

$$\boxed{\varphi=\arccos\!\left(\frac35\right)\quad\text{or}\quad \varphi=\arcco
s\!\left(-\frac35\right)=\pi-\arccos\!\left(\frac35\right).}$$

$$(0,0,1)\cdot\big(\sqrt3\,h,\,h,\,-b-\sqrt3\,a+10\sqrt3\big)=0 \;\Longrightarro

$$\boxed{MN=8\quad\text{or}\quad MN=4\sqrt{19}.}$$
