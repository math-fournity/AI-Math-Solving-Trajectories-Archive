## Solution

We solve the PDE
$$x\,\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} - y\,\frac{\partial u}{\partial y} = u, \qqua
d u\big|_{y=x}=x^{2},\; 1\le y\le 2,$$
by the **method of characteristics**.

### Characteristic equations

Parametrize a characteristic curve by $s$ with $(x(s),y(s),u(s))$:
$$\frac{dx}{ds}=x,\qquad \frac{dy}{ds}=-y,\qquad \frac{du}{ds}=u.$$

Integrating the first two:
$$x = x_{0}\,e^{s},\qquad y = y_{0}\,e^{-s}.$$
Hence the invariant
$$xy = x_{0}y_{0} = \text{const along each characteristic}.$$

Integrating the $u$–equation:
$$u = u_{0}\,e^{s}.$$

### Imposing the initial condition

The initial data is prescribed on the line $y=x$. Parametrize this line by $t\in
[1,2]$:
$$(x_{0},y_{0})=(t,t),\qquad u_{0}=t^{2},\qquad s=0.$$

The invariant gives
$$xy = t^{2}\;\Longrightarrow\; t=\sqrt{xy}.$$

From $x = t\,e^{s}$ we get
$$e^{s} = \frac{x}{t} = \frac{x}{\sqrt{xy}} = \sqrt{\frac{x}{y}}.$$

Therefore
$$u = u_{0}\,e^{s} = t^{2}\,e^{s} = (xy)\sqrt{\frac{x}{y}} = x\,y\cdot\frac{\sqr
t{x}}{\sqrt{y}} = x^{3/2}\,y^{1/2}.$$

### Verification

Let $u(x,y)=x^{3/2}y^{1/2}$. Then
$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}=\tfrac{3}{2}x^{1/2}y^{1/2},\qquad \frac{\partial
 u}{\partial y}=\tfrac{1}{2}x^{3/2}y^{-1/2}.$$

Substituting into the PDE:
$$x\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}-y\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=\tfrac{3}{2}x^{3
/2}y^{1/2}-\tfrac{1}{2}x^{3/2}y^{1/2}=x^{3/2}y^{1/2}=u.\;\checkmark$$

On the initial line $y=x$:
$$u(x,x)=x^{3/2}x^{1/2}=x^{2},\qquad 1\le x\le 2.\;\checkmark$$

### Domain of definition

Each characteristic is the hyperbola $xy=t^{2}$ with $t\in[1,2]$, i.e. $xy\in[1,
4]$. The solution is therefore defined on the region swept out by these characte
ristics:
$$\boxed{\,u(x,y)=x^{3/2}\,y^{1/2},\qquad 1\le xy\le 4\,}.$$

### PROOF COMPLETE
