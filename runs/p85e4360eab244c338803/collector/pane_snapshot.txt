Looking at this problem, I need to compute the degree of the restricted line bun
dle $\mathcal{O}_X(nX_1 + mX_3)|_{X_2}$.

## Setup

We have a family $X \to \text{Spec}$ with special fiber $X_0 = X_1 \cup X_2 \cup
 X_3$, a chain of three smooth irreducible components meeting transversally:
- $X_1 \cap X_2 = \{P\}$ (transverse)
- $X_2 \cap X_3 = \{Q\}$ (transverse)
- $P \neq Q$

## Key Principle

The degree of $\mathcal{O}_X(D)|_{X_2}$ for a divisor $D$ on the total space equ
als the intersection number $D \cdot X_2$.

For $D = nX_1 + mX_3$:

$$\deg\bigl(\mathcal{O}_X(nX_1 + mX_3)|_{X_2}\bigr) = (nX_1 + mX_3) \cdot X_2 =
n\,(X_1 \cdot X_2) + m\,(X_3 \cdot X_2).$$

## Computing the Intersection Numbers

**$X_1 \cdot X_2$:** The components $X_1$ and $X_2$ are distinct irreducible com
ponents of the special fiber meeting transversally at the single point $P$. For
distinct components, the intersection product equals the number of intersection
points counted with multiplicity. Transverse intersection at one point gives:

$$X_1 \cdot X_2 = 1.$$

**$X_3 \cdot X_2$:** Similarly, $X_2$ and $X_3$ meet transversally at the single
 point $Q$:

$$X_3 \cdot X_2 = 1.$$

Note: negative self-intersections arise only for $X_i \cdot X_i$ (the self-inter
section of a component), not for intersections of *distinct* components. The lat
ter is always the positive count of transverse intersection points.

## Verification via Restriction

Concretely, restricting $\mathcal{O}_X(X_1)$ to $X_2$ yields $\mathcal{O}_{X_2}(
P)$, since $X_1$ cuts out the point $P$ on $X_2$. This has degree $1$. Likewise
$\mathcal{O}_X(X_3)|_{X_2} = \mathcal{O}_{X_2}(Q)$, degree $1$. Therefore:

$$\mathcal{O}_X(nX_1 + mX_3)\big|_{X_2} \cong \mathcal{O}_{X_2}(nP + mQ),$$

and

$$\deg\bigl(\mathcal{O}_{X_2}(nP + mQ)\bigr) = n + m.$$

## Result

$$\boxed{\deg\bigl(\mathcal{O}_X(nX_1 + mX_3)|_{X_2}\bigr) = n + m.}$$
