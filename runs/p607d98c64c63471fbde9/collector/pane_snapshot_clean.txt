## Proof

We determine the dimension of the symplectic group $\mathrm{Sp}(2n, \tilde{F})$,
 where $\tilde{F}$ is the algebraic closure of a finite field $F$.

**Setup.** Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & I_n \\ -I_n & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ be the st
andard symplectic form on $\tilde{F}^{2n}$. The symplectic group is
$$\mathrm{Sp}(2n, \tilde{F}) = \{ g \in \mathrm{GL}_{2n}(\tilde{F}) : g^T J g =
J \}.$$
This is a connected, simply-connected semisimple algebraic group of type $C_n$,
defined over the prime field (hence over $F$ and over $\tilde{F}$). Its dimensio
n as an algebraic variety over $\tilde{F}$ equals the dimension of its Lie algeb
ra, which we now compute.

**Lie algebra computation.** The Lie algebra is
$$\mathfrak{sp}(2n) = \{ M \in M_{2n}(\tilde{F}) : M^T J + J M = 0 \}.$$
Write $M = \begin{pmatrix} A & B \\ C & D \end{pmatrix}$ in $n \times n$ blocks.
 Then
$$M^T J = \begin{pmatrix} A^T & C^T \\ B^T & D^T \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 0
& I_n \\ -I_n & 0 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -C^T & A^T \\ -D^T & B^T \end{
pmatrix},$$
$$J M = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & I_n \\ -I_n & 0 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} A & B \
\ C & D \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} C & D \\ -A & -B \end{pmatrix}.$$
Setting $M^T J + J M = 0$ gives the block equations:
$$-C^T + C = 0, \quad A^T + D = 0, \quad -D^T - A = 0, \quad B^T - B = 0.$$
These are equivalent to:
- $C = C^T$ (symmetric): $\frac{n(n+1)}{2}$ free parameters,
- $B = B^T$ (symmetric): $\frac{n(n+1)}{2}$ free parameters,
- $D = -A^T$, with $A$ arbitrary: $n^2$ free parameters (once $A$ is fixed, $D$
is determined).

**Counting.** The dimension of $\mathfrak{sp}(2n)$ is
$$n^2 + \frac{n(n+1)}{2} + \frac{n(n+1)}{2} = n^2 + n(n+1) = n^2 + n^2 + n = 2n^
2 + n.$$

**Field independence.** This dimension count is purely algebraic: it counts the
number of free parameters in the defining linear equations over any field of cha
racteristic $\neq 2$, and the same count is valid in characteristic $2$ as well
(the symplectic condition is still given by the same polynomial equations, and t
he dimension of the algebraic group is a characteristic-independent invariant —
equivalently, $\mathrm{Sp}(2n)$ is a split, simply-connected group of type $C_n$
 with root system having $2n$ roots and rank $n$, giving dimension $|\Phi| + n =
 2n^2 + n$ via the standard formula $\dim G = |\Phi| + \mathrm{rk}(G)$ for a sem
isimple group). In particular, passing from $F$ to its algebraic closure $\tilde
{F}$ does not change the dimension.

**Conclusion.** The dimension of $\mathrm{Sp}(2n, \tilde{F})$ is $2n^2 + n$. The
 answer is **yes**.
