They continue taking turns to pick progressively smaller positive integers until someone picks $1$. The sum of all picked numbers is calculated. The player who picks $1$ wins if and only if this sum is a perfect square. Otherwise, the other player wins. What is the sum of all possible values of $n$ such that if Alex starts with the number $n$, he has a winning strategy?
