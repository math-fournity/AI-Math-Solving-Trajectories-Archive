\phi(q^5)=(q^5)^{-1/24}\eta(5\tau)=q^{-5/24}\eta(5\tau).

$$
\frac{[\phi(q)]^5}{\phi(q^5)}=\frac{\eta(\tau)^5}{\eta(5\tau)}. \tag{1}

$$
\eta(it)=t^{-1/2}\eta(i/t),\qquad \eta(5it)=(5t)^{-1/2}\eta\!\bigl(i/(5t)\bigr).

\frac{\eta(it)^5}{\eta(5it)}
