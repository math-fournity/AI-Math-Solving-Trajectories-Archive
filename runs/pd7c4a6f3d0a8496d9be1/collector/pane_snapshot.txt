## Solution

**Setup.** Let $O$ be the origin and place the bisector of sector $OAB$ along th
e positive $x$-axis. Since $|AB|=6$ and the radius is $5$, the distance from $O$
 to the midpoint of $AB$ is $\sqrt{25-9}=4$. Writing $\theta$ for the half-secto
r angle, we get $\sin\theta=\tfrac{3}{5},\ \cos\theta=\tfrac{4}{5}$, so
$$A=(4,3),\qquad B=(4,-3).$$

**Symmetry.** The square $PQRS$ (with $P$ on $OA$, $S$ on $OB$, $Q,R$ on the arc
) is symmetric about the $x$-axis, so we may write
$$P=(x_P,y_P),\quad S=(x_P,-y_P),\quad Q=(x_Q,y_Q),\quad R=(x_Q,-y_Q).$$

**Square conditions.** The side $\overrightarrow{QR}=(0,-2y_Q)$ is vertical, so
the adjacent side $\overrightarrow{PQ}=(x_Q-x_P,\,y_Q-y_P)$ must be horizontal (
perpendicular) and of equal length:
$$y_Q=y_P,\qquad x_Q-x_P = 2y_Q = 2y_P.$$

**Parametrize $P$ on $OA$.** With $P=t(4,3)=(4t,3t)$:
$$x_P=4t,\quad y_P=3t,\quad x_Q=x_P+2y_P=4t+6t=10t,\quad y_Q=3t.$$

**Use $Q$ on the circle.** Since $Q$ lies on the circle of radius $5$:
$$(10t)^2+(3t)^2=25\implies 109t^2=25\implies t=\frac{5}{\sqrt{109}}.$$

**Side length and area.** The side of the square is
$$s = x_Q-x_P = 6t = \frac{30}{\sqrt{109}}.$$

**Verification.** $|OP|=\tfrac{25}{\sqrt{109}}\approx 2.39<5$, so $P$ lies on se
gment $OA$ ($t\approx 0.48<1$). $Q=\bigl(\tfrac{50}{\sqrt{109}},\tfrac{15}{\sqrt
{109}}\bigr)$ satisfies $|OQ|^2=\tfrac{2500+225}{109}=25$. The angle of $Q$ sati
sfies $\cos=\tfrac{10}{\sqrt{109}}\approx0.958>\tfrac45$, so $Q$ lies on the arc
 between $A$ and the bisector, hence on arc $\widehat{AB}$. All conditions are s
atisfied.

$$\boxed{\text{Area}=\frac{900}{109}}$$
