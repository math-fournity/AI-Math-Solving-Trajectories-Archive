## Solution

**Analyze the domain and signs of each factor.**

**Numerator:** $2^x - 16 = 2^x - 2^4$
- $= 0$ at $x = 4$; $< 0$ for $x < 4$; $> 0$ for $x > 4$.

**Denominator factor 1:** $9^{2x+1} - 243$. Since $9^{2x+1} = 3^{4x+2}$ and $243
 = 3^5$:
- $= 0$ at $x = 3/4$; $< 0$ for $x < 3/4$; $> 0$ for $x > 3/4$.

**Denominator factor 2:** $\sqrt{5^{(x^2-3)/2} - 125}$, where $125 = 5^3$.

**Domain requirements:**
1. Radicand $\geq 0$: $5^{(x^2-3)/2} \geq 5^3 \Rightarrow \frac{x^2-3}{2} \geq 3
 \Rightarrow x^2 \geq 9 \Rightarrow x \leq -3$ or $x \geq 3$.
2. Denominator $\neq 0$: radicand $\neq 0 \Rightarrow x^2 \neq 9 \Rightarrow x \
neq \pm 3$.
3. $9^{2x+1} - 243 \neq 0 \Rightarrow x \neq 3/4$ (automatically excluded).

**Domain:** $(-\infty, -3) \cup (3, +\infty)$.

On this domain, the square root is always **strictly positive**, so it doesn't a
ffect the sign.

**Sign analysis on each region:**

| Region | $2^x - 16$ | $9^{2x+1}-243$ | $\sqrt{\cdots}$ | Expression | $\leq 0$
? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $x < -3$ | $-$ | $-$ | $+$ | $\frac{(-)}{(-)(+)} = (+)$ | No |
| $3 < x < 4$ | $-$ | $+$ | $+$ | $\frac{(-)}{(+)(+)} = (-)$ | **Yes** |
| $x = 4$ | $0$ | $+$ | $+$ | $0$ | **Yes** |
| $x > 4$ | $+$ | $+$ | $+$ | $\frac{(+)}{(+)(+)} = (+)$ | No |

**Solution set:** $S = (3, 4]$.

This is a single interval with finite endpoints $3$ and $4$.

**Sum of all finite endpoints:** $3 + 4 = \boxed{7}$.
