## 1. Setup and notation

Let $J^\mu(x)$ be a Hermitian conserved current, $\partial_\mu J^\mu=0$, in a Po
incaré-invariant vacuum $|0\rangle$. Define the Wightman function and the time-o
rdered (Feynman) two-point function

$$
W^{\mu\nu}(x-y)\equiv \langle 0|J^\mu(x)J^\nu(y)|0\rangle,\qquad
G^{\mu\nu}(x-y)\equiv \langle 0|T\{J^\mu(x)J^\nu(y)\}|0\rangle .
$$

We use the mostly-minus metric $g^{\mu\nu}=\mathrm{diag}(1,-1,-1,-1)$.

## 2. Insert a complete set of states

Insert $\mathbf 1=\sum_n|n\rangle\langle n|$ between the two currents:

$$
W^{\mu\nu}(x-y)=\sum_n \langle 0|J^\mu(x)|n\rangle\langle n|J^\nu(y)|0\rangle .
$$

Translation invariance, $J^\mu(x)=e^{iP\cdot x}J^\mu(0)e^{-iP\cdot x}$ with $P^\
mu|n\rangle=p_n^\mu|n\rangle$, gives

$$
\langle 0|J^\mu(x)|n\rangle=e^{-ip_n\cdot x}\langle 0|J^\mu(0)|n\rangle,\qquad
\langle n|J^\nu(y)|0\rangle=e^{+ip_n\cdot y}\langle n|J^\nu(0)|0\rangle .
$$

Hence

$$
W^{\mu\nu}(x-y)=\sum_n e^{-ip_n\cdot(x-y)}\,\langle 0|J^\mu(0)|n\rangle\langle n
|J^\nu(0)|0\rangle .\tag{1}
$$

The vacuum does not contribute: $\langle 0|J^\mu(0)|0\rangle=0$ (the charge of t
he vacuum vanishes, or by charge-conjugation/Lorentz invariance of a vector one-
point function).

## 3. The spectral tensor $\rho^{\mu\nu}(p)$

Define the Fourier-space spectral tensor

$$
\rho^{\mu\nu}(p)\;\equiv\;\sum_n (2\pi)^4\delta^{(4)}(p-p_n)\,
\langle 0|J^\mu(0)|n\rangle\langle n|J^\nu(0)|0\rangle .\tag{2}
$$

Then (1) becomes

$$
W^{\mu\nu}(x)=\int\!\frac{d^4p}{(2\pi)^4}\,e^{-ip\cdot x}\,\rho^{\mu\nu}(p).\tag
{3}
$$

### 3.1 Support

Every physical state has $p_n^0>0$ and $p_n^2=M_n^2\ge 0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{supp}\,\rho^{\mu\nu}\subseteq \{p^0\ge 0,\;p^2\ge 0\}.}\tag{4}
$$

We may thus write $\rho^{\mu\nu}(p)=2\pi\,\theta(p^0)\theta(p^2)\,\hat\rho^{\mu\
nu}(p)$.

### 3.2 Lorentz covariance

$\rho^{\mu\nu}(p)$ is a Lorentz tensor depending only on the single vector $p$.
The most general form is

$$
\hat\rho^{\mu\nu}(p)=A(p^2)\,g^{\mu\nu}+B(p^2)\,p^\mu p^\nu .\tag{5}
$$

### 3.3 Current conservation $\Rightarrow$ transversality

Conservation $\partial_\mu J^\mu=0$ gives, for every state, $p_{n\,\mu}\langle 0
|J^\mu(0)|n\rangle=0$. Contracting (2) with $p_\mu$:

$$
p_\mu\rho^{\mu\nu}(p)=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad A(p^2)\,p^\nu+B(p^2)\,p^2\,p^\n
u=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad A(p^2)=-p^2 B(p^2).\tag{6}
$$

Hence on the support $p^2=M^2>0$,

$$
\hat\rho^{\mu\nu}(p)=B(p^2)\Big(p^\mu p^\nu-p^2 g^{\mu\nu}\Big)
=p^2 B(p^2)\left(-g^{\mu\nu}+\frac{p^\mu p^\nu}{p^2}\right).\tag{7}
$$

Define the **scalar spectral density**

$$
\boxed{\;\rho(M^2)\;\equiv\;M^2\,B(M^2)\;}\tag{8}
$$

so that, using $p^2=M^2$ on shell,

$$
\boxed{\;\rho^{\mu\nu}(p)=2\pi\,\theta(p^0)\theta(p^2)\left(-g^{\mu\nu}+\frac{p^
\mu p^\nu}{p^2}\right)\rho(p^2).\;}\tag{9}
$$

### 3.4 Positivity $\rho(M^2)\ge 0$

Go to the rest frame of a timelike $p=(M,\mathbf 0)$. The transverse projector r
educes to

$$
\left(-g^{\mu\nu}+\frac{p^\mu p^\nu}{M^2}\right)\Big|_{\text{rest}}\!\!\longrigh
tarrow\;
(-g^{ij})=\delta^{ij}\quad(i,j=1,2,3),\qquad
00\text{-component}=0 .
$$

Thus, from (2) and (9),

$$
\rho^{ij}(M,\mathbf 0)=2\pi\,\delta^{ij}\,\rho(M^2)
=\sum_n(2\pi)^4\delta^{(4)}(p-p_n)\langle 0|J^i(0)|n\rangle\langle n|J^j(0)|0\ra
ngle .
$$

By rotational invariance in the rest frame, $\rho^{11}=\rho^{22}=\rho^{33}$, and

$$
\rho^{11}(M,\mathbf 0)=\sum_{n:\,M_n=M}(2\pi)^4\delta^{(4)}(p-p_n)\,\big|\langle
 0|J^1(0)|n\rangle\big|^2\;\ge\;0 .
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\;\rho(M^2)\ge 0\quad\text{for all }M^2\ge 0.\;}\tag{10}
$$

The support begins at the lightest state coupled to $J^\mu$ (e.g. $M^2\ge 4m^2$
for a current built from a charged scalar of mass $m$).

## 4. From the Wightman function to the time-ordered function

Substituting (9) into (3) and using $\delta(p^2-M^2)$ to trade $p^2\to M^2$ insi
de the projector,

$$
W^{\mu\nu}(x)=\int_0^\infty\!dM^2\,\rho(M^2)\left(-g^{\mu\nu}+\frac{p^\mu p^\nu}
{M^2}\Big|_{\text{on-shell}}\right)\!\int\!\frac{d^4p}{(2\pi)^3}\theta(p^0)\delt
a(p^2-M^2)e^{-ip\cdot x}.
$$

The inner integral is the standard positive-frequency Wightman function $\Delta^
{(+)}(x;M^2)$. Since $J^\mu$ is Hermitian, $W^{\nu\mu}(-x)=\langle 0|J^\nu(0)J^\
mu(x)|0\rangle$ has the same spectral density with $p\to -p$, i.e. $\theta(p^0)\
to\theta(-p^0)$. Time ordering then acts on each $M^2$ sector independently:

$$
G^{\mu\nu}(x)=\theta(x^0)W^{\mu\nu}(x)+\theta(-x^0)W^{\nu\mu}(-x)
=\int_0^\infty\!dM^2\,\rho(M^2)\left(-g^{\mu\nu}+\frac{p^\mu p^\nu}{M^2}\right)\

$$

where $\Delta_F(x;M^2)=\int\!\frac{d^4p}{(2\pi)^4}\frac{i\,e^{-ip\cdot x}}{p^2-M
^2+i\epsilon}$ is the free Feynman propagator of mass $M$. Carrying the tensor f
actor through the Fourier transform ($p^\mu p^\nu\leftrightarrow -\partial^\mu\p
artial^\nu$) gives the **position-space Källén–Lehmann representation**:

$$
\boxed{\;
\langle 0|T\{J^\mu(x)J^\nu(y)\}|0\rangle
=\int_0^\infty\!dM^2\,\rho(M^2)\left(-g^{\mu\nu}-\frac{\partial^\mu\partial^\nu}
{M^2}\right)\Delta_F(x-y;M^2).

$$

Equivalently, Fourier transforming $G^{\mu\nu}(x)=\int\!\frac{d^4p}{(2\pi)^4}e^{

$$
\boxed{\;

## 5. Properties encoded in the representation

1. **Lorentz covariance** — manifest through $g^{\mu\nu}$ and $p^\mu p^\nu$.
2. **Spectral positivity** — $\rho(M^2)\ge 0$, established in (10); the spectral
 weight is a sum of squared matrix elements.
3. **Spectral support** — the integral runs over $M^2\ge 0$ only, reflecting the
 physical spectrum (4).
4. **Current conservation (on-shell/Wightman level)** — the transverse projector
 $(-g^{\mu\nu}+p^\mu p^\nu/M^2)$ satisfies $p_\mu(-g^{\mu\nu}+p^\mu p^\nu/M^2)=0
$ *on the mass shell* $p^2=M^2$, so $\partial_\mu W^{\mu\nu}=0$ exactly.
5. **Ward identity / contact term** — off shell, $p_\mu$ of the integrand in (12
) gives
$$
p_\mu G^{\mu\nu}(p)=i\,p^\nu\!\int_0^\infty\!\frac{dM^2}{M^2}\,\rho(M^2),
$$
a $p^2$-independent (hence local, contact) term. This is precisely the Fourier t
ransform of the equal-time commutator
$$
\partial_\mu T\{J^\mu(x)J^\nu(y)\}=\delta(x^0-y^0)\langle 0|[J^0(x),J^\nu(y)]|0\
rangle,
$$
so the full Ward identity $\partial_\mu G^{\mu\nu}=0$ is restored once this cont
act term is included. The integral representation (12) is the non-local (separat
ed-points) part; it is the standard Källén–Lehmann form for a conserved current.
6. **Dispersion relation** — writing $G^{\mu\nu}(p)=(-g^{\mu\nu}+p^\mu p^\nu/p^2
)\,i\,\Pi(p^2)$, the scalar function obeys a once-subtracted dispersion relation
$$
\Pi(p^2)=\Pi(0)+\frac{p^2}{\pi}\int_0^\infty\!dM^2\,\frac{\mathrm{Im}\,\Pi(M^2)}
{M^2(M^2-p^2-i\epsilon)},
\qquad \mathrm{Im}\,\Pi(M^2)=\pi\rho(M^2)/M^2\ge 0,
$$
with the subtraction constant $\Pi(0)$ fixed by the Ward identity (renormalizati
on condition $\Pi(0)=0$ in QED).
