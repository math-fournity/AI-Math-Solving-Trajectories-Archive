Looking at this problem, I need to find for which genera $g$ there exists a rank
 2, degree $2g-2$ holomorphic vector bundle $E$ on a genus $g$ curve $C$ with $H
^0(E) = 0$.

**Key observation via Riemann–Roch.** For a rank 2 bundle $E$ of degree $2g-2$ o
n a genus $g$ curve:
$$h^0(E) - h^1(E) = \deg(E) + \operatorname{rk}(E)(1-g) = (2g-2) + 2(1-g) = 0.$$
So $h^0(E) = h^1(E)$. By Serre duality, $h^1(E) = h^0(E^* \otimes K_C)$, where $
K_C$ is the canonical bundle (degree $2g-2$). Note $\det(E)$ and $K_C$ have the
same degree. The condition $H^0(E)=0$ is equivalent to $H^0(E^* \otimes K_C) = 0
$.

**Construction.** I claim that for **every** $g \geq 0$, such a bundle exists. T
he construction is:
$$E = L \oplus M$$
where $L, M$ are line bundles of degree $g-1$ on $C$ with $H^0(L) = H^0(M) = 0$.

Then $\operatorname{rk}(E) = 2$, $\deg(E) = 2(g-1) = 2g-2$, and $H^0(E) = H^0(L)
 \oplus H^0(M) = 0$. The bundle is semistable (both summands have slope $g-1$).

**Existence of non-effective line bundles of degree $g-1$.** It remains to show
that on a genus $g$ curve, there exist line bundles of degree $g-1$ with no glob
al sections.

- **$g = 0$ ($C = \mathbb{P}^1$):** Degree $g-1 = -1$. The line bundle $\mathcal
{O}(-1)$ has $H^0 = 0$. Take $E = \mathcal{O}(-1) \oplus \mathcal{O}(-1)$. ✓

- **$g = 1$ (elliptic curve):** Degree $g-1 = 0$. The Picard variety $\operatorn
ame{Pic}^0(C)$ is the dual elliptic curve. The only degree-0 line bundle with se
ctions is $\mathcal{O}_C$ (with $h^0 = 1$); every non-trivial degree-0 line bund
le $L$ has $H^0(L) = 0$ (by Riemann–Roch: $h^0 = h^1 = h^0(L^{-1})$, and if $h^0
(L) \geq 1$ for a degree-0 bundle, then $L \cong \mathcal{O}_C$). Choose any non
-trivial $L, M \in \operatorname{Pic}^0(C)$. ✓

- **$g \geq 2$:** Degree $g-1 \geq 1$. The effective locus $W^0_{g-1} \subset \o
peratorname{Pic}^{g-1}(C)$ (line bundles with $h^0 \geq 1$) is the image of the
Abel–Jacobi map $\operatorname{Sym}^{g-1}(C) \to \operatorname{Pic}^{g-1}(C)$. S
ince $\dim \operatorname{Sym}^{g-1}(C) = g-1$ while $\dim \operatorname{Pic}^{g-
1}(C) = g$, this image is a proper subvariety (the theta divisor). Therefore, a
**generic** line bundle $L \in \operatorname{Pic}^{g-1}(C)$ satisfies $H^0(L) =
0$. Choose two such generic line bundles $L, M$. ✓

**Verification of Riemann–Roch consistency.** For each $L$ of degree $g-1$: $h^0
(L) - h^1(L) = (g-1) + 1 - g = 0$, so $h^0(L) = h^1(L)$. The condition $h^0(L) =
 0$ is self-consistent (it implies $h^1(L) = 0$ as well, which by Serre duality
means $h^0(K_C \otimes L^{-1}) = 0$; this is the generic situation since $K_C \o
times L^{-1}$ also has degree $g-1$ and is generically non-effective).

**Conclusion.** For every genus $g \geq 0$, the bundle $E = L \oplus M$ with $L,
 M$ non-effective line bundles of degree $g-1$ is a rank 2, degree $2g-2$, holom
orphic vector bundle with $H^0(E) = 0$.

The values of $g$ are: **all $g \geq 0$** (i.e., $g \in \{0, 1, 2, 3, \ldots\}$)
.
