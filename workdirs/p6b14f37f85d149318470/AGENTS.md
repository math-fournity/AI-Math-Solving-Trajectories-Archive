# Problem

In the center of a high-tech manufacturing facility, a robotic laser-cutter operates on a massive square silicon wafer measuring 2010 units by 2010 units. The entire surface of the wafer is etched with a grid of square microchips, each measuring exactly 1 unit by 1 unit.

A circular calibration lens with a diameter of exactly 2 units is dropped by a robotic arm onto the wafer. The mechanics of the arm guarantee that the lens always lands perfectly flat and lies entirely within the boundaries of the 2010x2010 wafer. Every possible position where the lens remains fully supported by the wafer is equally likely.

Calculate the probability that the lens, in its final resting position, completely obscures at least one of the 1x1 microchips.
