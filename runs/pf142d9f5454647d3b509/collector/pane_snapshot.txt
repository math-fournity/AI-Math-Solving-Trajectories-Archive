## Solution

We count 4-digit codes $ABCD$ with $A\in\{1,\dots,9\}$, $B,C,D\in\{0,\dots,9\}$,
 and
$$|A-B|=|B-C|=|C-D|=:d.$$

**Case $d=0$.** Then $A=B=C=D$ with $A\in\{1,\dots,9\}$, giving **9** codes.

**Case $d\ge 1$.** Write each consecutive step as a signed multiple of $d$:
$$B-A=s_1 d,\quad C-B=s_2 d,\quad D-C=s_3 d,\qquad s_i\in\{+1,-1\}.$$
There are $2^3=8$ sign patterns. For a fixed pattern, set
$$o_0=0,\ o_1=s_1d,\ o_2=(s_1+s_2)d,\ o_3=(s_1+s_2+s_3)d,$$
so the four digits are $A+o_0,A+o_1,A+o_2,A+o_3$. We need every $A+o_i\in[0,9]$
and $A\ge 1$, i.e.
$$A\in\bigl[\max(1,-\min o_i),\ 9-\max o_i\bigr],$$
giving $\max(0,\ 9-\max o_i-\max(1,-\min o_i)+1)$ valid choices of $A$.

The eight patterns have offset ranges $(\min o_i,\max o_i)$:

| Pattern $(s_1s_2s_3)$ | $(\min,\max)$ |
|---|---|
| $+++$ | $(0,3d)$ |
| $++-$ | $(0,2d)$ |
| $+-+$ | $(0,d)$ |
| $+--$ | $(-d,d)$ |
| $-++$ | $(-d,d)$ |
| $-+-$ | $(-d,0)$ |
| $--+$ | $(-2d,0)$ |
| $---$ | $(-3d,0)$ |

Computing the count for each $d=1,\dots,9$:

| $d$ | $+++$ | $++-$ | $+-+$ | $+--$ | $-++$ | $-+-$ | $--+$ | $---$ | row sum
|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 9 | 8 | 7 | 61 |
| 2 | 3 | 5 | 7 | 6 | 6 | 8 | 6 | 4 | 45 |
| 3 | 0 | 3 | 6 | 4 | 4 | 7 | 4 | 1 | 29 |
| 4 | 0 | 1 | 5 | 2 | 2 | 6 | 2 | 0 | 18 |
| 5 | 0 | 0 | 4 | 0 | 0 | 5 | 0 | 0 | 9 |
| 6 | 0 | 0 | 3 | 0 | 0 | 4 | 0 | 0 | 7 |
| 7 | 0 | 0 | 2 | 0 | 0 | 3 | 0 | 0 | 5 |
| 8 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 2 | 0 | 0 | 3 |
| 9 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 |

(Each entry is $\max(0,\,9-\max o_i-\max(1,-\min o_i)+1)$; e.g. for $d=1$, $-+-$
 has range $(-1,0)$, so $A\in[\max(1,1),9]=[1,9]$, count $9$.)

Summing the row sums:
$$61+45+29+18+9+7+5+3+1=178.$$

Adding the $d=0$ case:
$$178+9=\boxed{187}.$$
