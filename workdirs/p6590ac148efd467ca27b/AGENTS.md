# Problem

A specialized logistics company has 50 double-sided shipping containers currently staged in a loading bay. Each container has a unique integer from 1 to 100 printed on its faces, such that every number in that range appears exactly once across the 100 available faces (50 visible and 50 hidden).

The supervisor allows you to inspect the visible number on all 50 containers. You are then permitted to select any number of these containers and flip them over to reveal the number on the underside, replacing the original value in your total.

Your goal is to maximize the total sum of the numbers showing on the 50 containers. What is the largest sum that you can be mathematically guaranteed to achieve (or exceed), regardless of how the numbers 1 through 100 were paired on the containers?
