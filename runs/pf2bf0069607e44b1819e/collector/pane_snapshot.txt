## Solution

We work in the space of distributions (generalized functions) on $\mathbb{R}$, w
ith test functions $\varphi \in C_c^\infty(\mathbb{R})$.

**Setup.** Recall that $\delta_a$ is the Dirac distribution centered at $a$, act
ing on test functions by
$$\delta_a(\varphi) = \varphi(a), \qquad \delta(\varphi) = \varphi(0).$$
Define the distribution
$$T_a := \frac{\delta_a + \delta_{-a} - 2\delta}{a^2}.$$
Its action on a test function $\varphi$ is
$$T_a(\varphi) = \frac{\varphi(a) + \varphi(-a) - 2\varphi(0)}{a^2}.$$

**Computing the limit.** Since $\varphi$ is smooth, Taylor's theorem with remain
der gives
$$\varphi(a) = \varphi(0) + a\,\varphi'(0) + \frac{a^2}{2}\,\varphi''(0) + \frac
{a^3}{6}\,\varphi'''(0) + \frac{a^4}{24}\,\varphi^{(4)}(\xi_1),$$
$$\varphi(-a) = \varphi(0) - a\,\varphi'(0) + \frac{a^2}{2}\,\varphi''(0) - \fra
c{a^3}{6}\,\varphi'''(0) + \frac{a^4}{24}\,\varphi^{(4)}(\xi_2),$$
for some $\xi_1 \in (0,a)$, $\xi_2 \in (-a,0)$. Adding these:
$$\varphi(a) + \varphi(-a) - 2\varphi(0) = a^2\,\varphi''(0) + \frac{a^4}{24}\bi
gl(\varphi^{(4)}(\xi_1) + \varphi^{(4)}(\xi_2)\bigr).$$

Therefore
$$T_a(\varphi) = \varphi''(0) + \frac{a^2}{24}\bigl(\varphi^{(4)}(\xi_1) + \varp
hi^{(4)}(\xi_2)\bigr).$$

Since $\varphi^{(4)}$ is continuous and $\xi_1, \xi_2 \to 0$ as $a \to 0$, the r
emainder term vanishes:
$$\lim_{a \to 0} T_a(\varphi) = \varphi''(0).$$

**Identifying the limit distribution.** The second distributional derivative of
$\delta$, denoted $\delta''$, acts on test functions by
$$\delta''(\varphi) = \varphi''(0).$$

Hence $T_a(\varphi) \to \delta''(\varphi)$ for every test function $\varphi$, wh
ich is precisely convergence in the sense of distributions (weak-* convergence i
n $\mathcal{D}'$).

**Conclusion.**

$$\boxed{\lim_{a\to 0}\frac{\delta_{a}+\delta_{-a}-2\delta}{a^{2}} = \delta''}$$

where $\delta''$ is the second distributional derivative of the Dirac delta. Thi
s is the distributional analogue of the classical second-order central differenc
e quotient converging to the second derivative.
