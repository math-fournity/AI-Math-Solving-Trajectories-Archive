## Counterexample

Let $K = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : 1 \le |z| \le 2\}$ (the closed annulus), so $U = \
{1 < |z| < 2\}$. Define $f(z) = 1/z^2$.

### Verification of the hypotheses

1. **$K$ is closed**: $K$ is the preimage of $[1,2]$ under the continuous map $z
 \mapsto |z|$, hence closed.

2. **$f$ is continuous and bounded on $K$**: $f(z) = 1/z^2$ is continuous on $\m
athbb{C}\setminus\{0\} \supset K$, and $|f(z)| = 1/|z|^2 \le 1$ on $K$, so $f$ i
s bounded.

3. **$f$ is holomorphic on $U$**: $1/z^2$ is holomorphic on $\mathbb{C}\setminus
\{0\} \supset U$.

4. **$\int_\gamma f\,dz = 0$ for every closed curve $\gamma \subset K$**: The fu
nction $F(z) = -1/z$ is continuous on $K$, holomorphic on $U$, and satisfies $F'
(z) = 1/z^2 = f(z)$ on $U$. For any piecewise-smooth closed curve $\gamma \subse
t K$, by the fundamental theorem of calculus along the curve:
$$\int_\gamma f(z)\,dz = \int_\gamma F'(z)\,dz = F(\gamma(1)) - F(\gamma(0)) = 0
,$$
since $\gamma$ is closed. (Any closed curve in $K$ can be approximated by piecew
ise-smooth ones, so the condition holds for all closed curves.)

### $f$ cannot be uniformly approximated by entire functions

Since $K$ is compact, every entire function is a uniform limit of its Taylor pol
ynomials on $K$. Therefore, the uniform closure of entire functions on $K$ equal
s the uniform closure of polynomials on $K$. It suffices to show $f$ cannot be u
niformly approximated by polynomials.

Suppose for contradiction that polynomials $p_n \to f$ uniformly on $K$. Define
the polynomials
$$q_n(z) = z^2\, p_n(z) - 1.$$

On $K$, we have $|z^2| \le 4$ and
$$|q_n(z)| = |z^2|\,\left|p_n(z) - \frac{1}{z^2}\right| \le 4\,\|p_n - f\|_K \xr
ightarrow{n\to\infty} 0.$$

So $q_n \to 0$ uniformly on $K$, in particular on the circle $\{|z|=2\}$.

By the **maximum modulus principle**, since $q_n$ is a polynomial (hence holomor
phic on $\{|z|\le 2\}$):
$$\max_{|z|\le 2}|q_n(z)| = \max_{|z|=2}|q_n(z)| \xrightarrow{n\to\infty} 0.$$

Thus $q_n \to 0$ uniformly on $\{|z|\le 2\}$. In particular, $q_n(0) \to 0$.

But $q_n(0) = 0^2 \cdot p_n(0) - 1 = -1$ for every $n$. This is a **contradictio
n**: $-1 \not\to 0$.

The function $f(z)=1/z^2$ on the closed annulus $K=\{1\le|z|\le 2\}$ satisfies a
ll the stated hypotheses — it is continuous, bounded, holomorphic on the interio

primitive $-1/z$) — yet it **cannot** be uniformly approximated by entire functi
ons. The answer is **no**.
