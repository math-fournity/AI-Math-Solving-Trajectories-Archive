$$
P\!\bigl(|Z_n - Z| > \varepsilon\bigr)

Since $X_n \xrightarrow{P} X$ and $Y_n \xrightarrow{P} Y$, both terms on the rig

$$
P\!\bigl(2|Z|\,|Z_n-Z| > \varepsilon/2\bigr)
