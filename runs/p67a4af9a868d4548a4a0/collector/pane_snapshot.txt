**Game analysis.** A position is a *P-position* (previous player wins, i.e. $N \
notin W$) if every legal move leads to an *N-position*, and an *N-position* ($N
\in W$) if some legal move leads to a P-position. The terminal position is $N=1$
: no divisor $d$ with $1<d<1$ exists, and $N-1=0$ is not positive, so **$1$ is a
 P-position**.

For $N\ge 2$ the legal moves reach the set $S(N)=\{N-1\}\cup\{d: d\mid N,\ 1<d<N
\}$. Hence
$$N \text{ is P} \iff N-1 \text{ is N and every proper divisor } d\ (1<d<N) \tex
t{ of } N \text{ is N}.$$
