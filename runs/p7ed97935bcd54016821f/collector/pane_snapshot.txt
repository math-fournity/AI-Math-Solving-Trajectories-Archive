## Proof

Place the unit circle at the origin of the complex plane, and let the vertices o
f the $n$-gon be $z_1, z_2, \dots, z_n$ with $|z_k| = 1$. Every side and every d
iagonal is a chord $|z_i - z_j|$ for some pair $i < j$. Hence the quantity to ma
ximize is

$$S = \sum_{1 \le i < j \le n} |z_i - z_j|^2.$$

**Step 1: A closed form.** Since $|z_i - z_j|^2 = (z_i - z_j)(\bar z_i - \bar z_
j) = |z_i|^2 + |z_j|^2 - z_i\bar z_j - \bar z_i z_j = 2 - 2\operatorname{Re}(z_i
\bar z_j)$, summing over all pairs gives

$$S = \sum_{i<j} 2 - 2\sum_{i<j}\operatorname{Re}(z_i\bar z_j) = n(n-1) - 2\sum_
{i<j}\operatorname{Re}(z_i\bar z_j).$$

Now observe

$$\left|\sum_{k=1}^n z_k\right|^2 = \sum_{k=1}^n |z_k|^2 + 2\sum_{i<j}\operatorn
ame{Re}(z_i\bar z_j) = n + 2\sum_{i<j}\operatorname{Re}(z_i\bar z_j).$$

Therefore $2\sum_{i<j}\operatorname{Re}(z_i\bar z_j) = \left|\sum_k z_k\right|^2
 - n$, and substituting,

$$S = n(n-1) - \left(\left|\sum_{k=1}^n z_k\right|^2 - n\right) = n^2 - \left|\s
um_{k=1}^n z_k\right|^2.$$

**Step 2: Bounding.** Since $\left|\sum_k z_k\right|^2 \ge 0$, we obtain the uni
versal upper bound

$$S \le n^2.$$

**Step 3: Attainability.** Equality holds precisely when $\sum_{k=1}^n z_k = 0$,
 i.e., when the centroid of the vertices coincides with the center of the circle
. This is achieved, for example, by the **regular $n$-gon**: its vertices are $z
_k = e^{2\pi i k/n}$, and

$$\sum_{k=1}^n e^{2\pi i k/n} = 0$$

(the sum of all $n$-th roots of unity). The regular $n$-gon is convex and inscri
bed in the unit circle, so the bound $n^2$ is attained.

More generally, equality is attained by exactly those inscribed convex $n$-gons
whose vertex position vectors sum to zero (centroid at the center); for even $n$
 this includes, for instance, any configuration consisting of $n/2$ antipodal pa
irs, and for all $n$ it includes every regular $n$-gon and its images under rota
tion.

**Conclusion.** The maximum value of the sum of the squares of all sides and dia
gonals is

$$\boxed{n^2},$$

achieved when the centroid of the $n$ vertices coincides with the center of the
unit circle; in particular by the regular $n$-gon.
