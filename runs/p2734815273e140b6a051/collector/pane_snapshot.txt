$$\text{pos}_k(x) := \exists y_1 \cdots \exists y_k \left[\bigwedge_{i \neq j} y

$$\psi_S(x) := \bigvee_{k \in S} \text{pos}_k(x)$$

### Step 2: The MSO Theory of Finite Linear Orders Is Decidable

By the **Büchi–Elgot–Trakhtenbrot theorem**, there is an effective translation f
rom any MSO sentence $\varphi$ over the signature $\{<\}$ to a finite automaton

$$\varphi \text{ is satisfiable over some finite linear order} \iff L(\mathcal{A

validity is also decidable.

The weak MSO theory of finite linear orders under Henkin semantics — call it $T$

ders under full semantics. By Step 2, $T$ is decidable.

A decidable theory is recursively enumerable: enumerate all sentences $\varphi_1
