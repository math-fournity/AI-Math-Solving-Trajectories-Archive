## Proof

We seek a holomorphic bijection $f$ of the annulus $A = \{z : a < |z| < b\}$ ont
o itself that swaps the two boundary components: $|z|=a \leftrightarrow |z|=b$.

**Candidate.** Consider
$$f(z) = \frac{ab}{z}.$$

**Holomorphy.** Since $0 \notin A$, the function $f(z)=ab/z$ is holomorphic on $
A$.

**Swaps the boundaries.** For $|z|=a$: $|f(z)| = ab/|z| = ab/a = b$, so the inne
r circle maps to the outer circle. For $|z|=b$: $|f(z)| = ab/b = a$, so the oute
r circle maps to the inner circle. The two boundaries are permuted as required.

**Maps $A$ onto $A$.** For any $z \in A$ we have $a < |z| < b$, hence
$$a = \frac{ab}{b} < \frac{ab}{|z|} < \frac{ab}{a} = b,$$
so $f(z) \in A$. Thus $f(A) \subseteq A$. Conversely, given $w \in A$, the preim
age $z = ab/w$ satisfies $a < |z| < b$ by the same computation, so $z \in A$ and
 $f(z)=w$. Hence $f(A)=A$.

**Bijectivity.** $f$ is its own inverse: $f(f(z)) = ab/(ab/z) = z$. Therefore $f
$ is a bijection of $A$ onto itself.

**Uniqueness (up to rotation).** It remains to argue this is essentially the onl
y such map. A conformal automorphism of an annulus $A(r)=\{1<|z|<r\}$ (with $r=b
/a>1$) that preserves each boundary component is a rotation $z \mapsto e^{i\thet
a}z$. Any automorphism swapping the two boundary components must, after rescalin
g $z \mapsto z/a$ (sending $A$ to $A(r)$), take the form $g(z) = e^{i\theta}\, r
/z$ on $A(r)$: it swaps $|z|=1$ and $|z|=r$, and composing with the swap $z\maps
to r/z$ (which corresponds to $ab/z$ in the original coordinates) gives a bounda
ry-preserving automorphism, hence a rotation. Scaling back gives
$$f(z) = e^{i\theta}\,\frac{ab}{z}, \qquad \theta \in \mathbb{R}.$$
All such maps satisfy the requirements; the canonical representative (with $\the
ta=0$) is $f(z)=ab/z$.
