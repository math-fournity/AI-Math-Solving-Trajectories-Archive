# Problem

In a remote archipelago, there are exactly 20 small islands arranged in a perfect circle. To facilitate trade, the local government plans to authorize the construction of three straight shipping lanes (chords), each connecting two distinct islands from the set of 20. 

A "Trade Triangle" is defined as a set of three such shipping lanes that satisfy a specific safety and navigation requirement: every lane in the set must intersect with every other lane in the set. These intersections are allowed to occur either in the open water between islands or at the islands themselves (if two lanes share a common harbor).

How many different sets of three shipping lanes can be established across these 20 islands such that each lane in the set intersects the other two?
