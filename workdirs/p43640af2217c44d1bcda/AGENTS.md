# Problem

In a specialized logistics hub, there are 100 distinct delivery bays numbered 1 through 100. A central dispatcher assigns a single automated transport drone to each bay, such that every drone is programmed to move cargo from its assigned bay $x$ to a different destination bay $f(x)$. Under the facility’s safety protocols, no drone is permitted to deliver cargo back to its own starting bay (meaning $f(x) \neq x$ for all $x$).

A "delivery cycle" for a specific bay $j$ is defined by tracking the sequence of movements: a package starts at bay $j$, is moved to bay $f(j)$, then the next drone moves it to $f(f(j))$, and so on. A bay $j$ is considered "self-correcting" if, after exactly $j$ consecutive deliveries, the package arrives back at the original bay $j$.

Over all possible valid drone assignment configurations, what is the maximum number of bays that can be self-correcting?
