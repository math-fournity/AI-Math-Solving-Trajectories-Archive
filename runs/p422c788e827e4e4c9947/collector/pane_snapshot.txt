Looking at this problem, I need to find the asymptotic behavior of $S = \sum_{k=
1}^{n/2 - \sqrt{n}} n\binom{n-1}{k-1}$ as $n \to \infty$.

## Proof

**Step 1: Reindex the sum.**

Let $j = k-1$, so $k = j+1$. When $k$ ranges from $1$ to $n/2 - \sqrt{n}$, $j$ r
anges from $0$ to $r := n/2 - \sqrt{n} - 1$. Setting $m = n-1$, we get:

$$S = n \sum_{j=0}^{r} \binom{m}{j}, \quad \text{where } m = n-1,\; r = \frac{n}
{2} - \sqrt{n} - 1.$$

**Step 2: Express as a binomial CDF.**

Dividing by $2^m$:

$$\frac{1}{2^m}\sum_{j=0}^{r}\binom{m}{j} = \Pr(X \leq r), \quad X \sim \operato
rname{Bin}(m, \tfrac{1}{2}).$$

**Step 3: Compute the standardized threshold.**

The mean and standard deviation of $X$ are:

$$\mu = \frac{m}{2} = \frac{n-1}{2}, \qquad \sigma = \frac{\sqrt{m}}{2} = \frac{
\sqrt{n-1}}{2}.$$

The deviation of $r$ from the mean:

$$r - \mu = \frac{n}{2} - \sqrt{n} - 1 - \frac{n-1}{2} = -\sqrt{n} - \frac{1}{2}
.$$

The standardized value:

$$z_n = \frac{r - \mu}{\sigma} = \frac{-\sqrt{n} - \frac{1}{2}}{\sqrt{n-1}/2} =
\frac{-2\sqrt{n} - 1}{\sqrt{n-1}}.$$

As $n \to \infty$:

$$z_n = \frac{-2\sqrt{n}(1 + \frac{1}{2\sqrt{n}})}{\sqrt{n}\sqrt{1 - 1/n}} \long
rightarrow -2.$$

**Step 4: Apply the De Moivre–Laplace CLT.**

Since $r - \mu = \Theta(\sqrt{m})$ (i.e., $r$ is within $O(\sqrt{m})$ of the mea
n, which is the regime where the CLT gives a nontrivial limit), the De Moivre–La
place theorem yields:

$$\Pr(X \leq r) = \Phi(z_n) + o(1) \longrightarrow \Phi(-2),$$

where $\Phi$ is the standard normal CDF. Therefore:

$$\sum_{j=0}^{r}\binom{m}{j} = 2^m\,\Phi(z_n) + o(2^m) = \Phi(-2)\cdot 2^{n-1} +
 o(2^{n-1}).$$

**Step 5: Conclude.**

$$S = n\left(\Phi(-2)\cdot 2^{n-1} + o(2^{n-1})\right) = \Phi(-2)\cdot n\cdot 2^
{n-1} + o(n\cdot 2^{n-1}).$$

Thus:

$$\boxed{S \sim \Phi(-2)\cdot n\cdot 2^{n-1} \quad \text{as } n\to\infty,}$$

where

$$\Phi(-2) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int_{-\infty}^{-2} e^{-t^2/2}\,dt \approx 0.0
2275.$$

**Interpretation:** The sum $n\binom{n-1}{k-1} = k\binom{n}{k}$, so $S = \sum_{k
=1}^{n/2-\sqrt{n}} k\binom{n}{k}$ captures the contribution of the lower tail of
 the binomial distribution (cut off at $2\sigma$ below the mean). The factor $\P
hi(-2)$ is precisely the Gaussian tail probability at $2$ standard deviations be
low the mean, confirming that the asymptotic behavior is a constant fraction $\P
hi(-2)$ of the full sum $n\cdot 2^{n-1} = \sum_{k=0}^{n}k\binom{n}{k}$.
