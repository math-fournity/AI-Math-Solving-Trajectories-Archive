## Placements per Orientation

 Its bounding box is **4 wide × 2 tall**.

- Vertical positions: the 2-tall bounding box needs its top row from 1 to 8−2+1
= **7** choices.
- Total: 5 × 7 = **35**.

Every placement of the bounding box yields exactly one valid piece placement (th
