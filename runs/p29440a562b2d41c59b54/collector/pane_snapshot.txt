## Solution

**Parametrize the curve.** Let $z(t) = e^{it}$ for $t \in [0, 2\pi]$, so $C$ is
the unit circle traversed once counterclockwise.

**Compute $|z|$ along $C$.** For every $t$,
$$|z(t)| = |e^{it}| = 1.$$
Thus $|z|$ is **constant** (equal to 1) along the entire curve $C$.

**Compute the differential $d|z|$.** Since $|z(t)| = 1$ is constant,
$$\frac{d|z(t)|}{dt} = 0 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad d|z| = 0.$$

**Evaluate the integral.** The integral is a line integral with respect to the m
easure $d|z|$:
$$\int_C \frac{d|z|}{\overline{z}} = \int_0^{2\pi} \frac{1}{\overline{z(t)}} \cd
ot \frac{d|z(t)|}{dt}\, dt = \int_0^{2\pi} \frac{1}{e^{-it}} \cdot 0\, dt = 0.$$

**Conclusion.** Since $|z|$ is constant on the unit circle, the differential $d|
z|$ vanishes identically, and the integrand is zero everywhere on $C$.

$$\boxed{\int_C \frac{d|z|}{\overline{z}} = 0}$$
