Looking at this problem, I need to find all achievable three-digit values of $n
= a+b+c+d$ where four technicians receive positive integer quantities satisfying
 the Binary Parity protocol.

## Setting Up the Condition

For each technician with quantity $v$ in the multiset $\{a,b,c,d\}$:
- **Condition 1**: $v$ appears at least twice in the multiset, OR
- **Condition 2**: $2v$ also appears in the multiset (since $v = \text{other}/2$
 means other $= 2v$).

## Enumerating by Multiplicity Pattern

**Case 1: All four equal** — $\{v,v,v,v\}$. Each appears 4×, condition 1 ✓. Sum
$= 4v$, giving multiples of 4. All are even.

**Case 2: Three equal, one different** — $\{v,v,v,w\}$, $w \neq v$. The three $v
$'s satisfy condition 1. For $w$ (appears once): need $2w = v$, so $w = v/2$ (re
quiring $v$ even). Setting $v = 2k$: multiset $\{k, 2k, 2k, 2k\}$, sum $= 7k$. T
his yields **multiples of 7**.

**Case 3: Two pairs** — $\{v,v,w,w\}$, $v \neq w$. Both values appear twice, con
dition 1 ✓ for all. Sum $= 2(v+w)$. For any even $n \in [100, 998]$, set $v=1, w
 = n/2 - 1$ (valid since $n/2 \geq 50$). This covers **all even three-digit numb
ers**.

**Case 4: One pair, two distinct singles** — $\{v,v,w,x\}$, all of $v,w,x$ disti
nct. The pair $v$ satisfies condition 1. For $w$ (once): $2w \in \{v,x\}$. For $
x$ (once): $2x \in \{v,w\}$. Checking all combinations:
- $2w=v, 2x=v \Rightarrow w=x$: contradiction.
- $2w=v, 2x=w \Rightarrow v=4x, w=2x$: multiset $\{x, 2x, 4x, 4x\}$. Verify: $x
\to 2x$ ✓, $2x \to 4x$ ✓, $4x$ appears twice ✓. **Valid!** Sum $= 11x$.
- $2w=x, 2x=v \Rightarrow v=4w, x=2w$: same multiset type, sum $= 11w$. **Valid!
**
- $2w=x, 2x=w \Rightarrow w=0$: invalid.

This yields **multiples of 11**.

**Case 5: All four distinct** — Each appears once, so each needs its double pres
ent. The largest element $M$ has $2M > M$, so $2M$ can't be in the set. **Imposs
ible.**

## Computing the Union of Achievable Sums

Since Case 3 covers **all even three-digit numbers**, Cases 1, 2, and 4 only add
 **odd** values:

- **Even three-digit numbers** (100, 102, …, 998): $\frac{998-100}{2}+1 = 450$ v
alues.

- **Odd multiples of 7** in $[100,999]$: $n=7k$, $k$ odd, $15 \leq k \leq 141$.
Count $= \frac{141-15}{2}+1 = 64$.

- **Odd multiples of 11** in $[100,999]$: $n=11x$, $x$ odd, $11 \leq x \leq 89$.
 Count $= \frac{89-11}{2}+1 = 40$.

- **Overlap**: odd multiples of $77 = \text{lcm}(7,11)$ in $[100,999]$: $77m$, $
m$ odd, $m \in \{3,5,7,9,11\}$, giving $231, 385, 539, 693, 847$. Count $= 5$.

## Final Count

By inclusion-exclusion:

$$|S| = 450 + 64 + 40 - 5 = \boxed{549}$$
