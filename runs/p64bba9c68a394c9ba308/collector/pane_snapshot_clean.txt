## Solution

**Key fact:** The three medians of a triangle can be rearranged to form a triang
le (the "median triangle"), and its area equals **3/4** of the original triangle
's area.

*Proof of the 3/4 ratio:* In an equilateral triangle of side $a$, each median ha
s length $\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}a$, the original area is $\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}a^2$,
and the equilateral median-triangle has area $\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\!\left(\tfrac{
\sqrt{3}}{2}a\right)^2 = \tfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{16}a^2 = \tfrac{3}{4}\cdot\tfrac{\sqr
t{3}}{4}a^2$. The ratio $\tfrac{3}{4}$ is a general result (the centroid divides
 each median $2{:}1$, and the six small triangles each have area $\tfrac{S}{6}$;
 rearranging the three medians as sides yields a triangle of area $\tfrac{3}{4}S
$). ✓

**Setting up the optimization.** Let the two given medians be $m_1 = 15$ and $m_
2 = 9$, and let the third median be $m_3$. The three medians must form a valid t
riangle, so by the triangle inequality:
$$|15 - 9| < m_3 < 15 + 9 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 6 < m_3 < 24.$$

The area of the median triangle with two sides $15$ and $9$ enclosing angle $\th
eta$ is
$$A_{\text{med}} = \tfrac{1}{2}(15)(9)\sin\theta = \tfrac{135}{2}\sin\theta.$$

This is maximized when $\theta = 90°$, giving $A_{\text{med}} = \tfrac{135}{2} =
 67.5$.

**Consistency check.** At $\theta = 90°$:
$$m_3^2 = 15^2 + 9^2 - 2(15)(9)\cos 90° = 225 + 81 = 306, \quad m_3 = \sqrt{306}
 \approx 17.49.$$
Since $6 < 17.49 < 24$, the triangle inequality is satisfied. ✓

**Computing the original triangle's area.** Since $A_{\text{med}} = \tfrac{3}{4}
S$,
$$S = \tfrac{4}{3}\,A_{\text{med}} = \tfrac{4}{3}\times 67.5 = 90.$$

**Verification that this is achievable.** We need an actual triangle whose media
ns are $15$, $9$, and $\sqrt{306}$. The medians of a triangle with sides $a, b,
c$ satisfy
$$m_a = \tfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2b^2 + 2c^2 - a^2}, \quad \text{etc.}$$
Given any three lengths satisfying the triangle inequality, there exists a (uniq
ue) triangle having those as its medians (this follows from the invertibility of
 the median-to-side map). Since $15, 9, \sqrt{306}$ form a valid triangle, such
an original triangle exists.

**Answer:** The maximum possible area of the triangle is $\boxed{90}$.
