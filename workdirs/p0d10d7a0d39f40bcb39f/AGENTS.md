# Problem

A network of 2024 distinct satellite monitoring stations is positioned across a vast geographic region. The stations are situated such that no three stations lie on the same straight line. 

Deep within the network, there is a specific primary hub, designated as Station $S$. The placement of the stations is unique: for any two other stations in the network, the triangle formed by those two stations and Station $S$ is strictly acute (meaning all three internal angles are less than 90 degrees).

For any station $k$ in the network, we define its "Visibility Rating," $N_k$, as the total number of pairs of other stations $\{i, j\}$ such that the angle formed at station $k$ (the angle $\angle i-k-j$) is acute.

Given that the total number of stations $n$ is 2024, determine the minimum possible value of $N_k$ that can be found among all stations in the network.
