## Proof

Let $S = \{X \subseteq \omega_1 \mid |X| = \aleph_0\} = [\omega_1]^{\aleph_0}$.
We show $|S| = 2^{\aleph_0} = |\mathcal{P}(\omega)|$ by a straightforward counti
ng argument, establishing both inequalities.

### Lower bound: $|S| \geq 2^{\aleph_0}$

Since $\omega \subseteq \omega_1$, every countably infinite subset of $\omega$ i
s also a countably infinite subset of $\omega_1$. Thus $[\omega]^{\aleph_0} \sub
seteq S$.

The total number of subsets of $\omega$ is $2^{\aleph_0}$, and the number of fin
ite subsets of $\omega$ is $\aleph_0$. Hence the number of infinite subsets is

$$|[\omega]^{\aleph_0}| = 2^{\aleph_0} - \aleph_0 = 2^{\aleph_0}.$$

Therefore $|S| \geq |[\omega]^{\aleph_0}| = 2^{\aleph_0}$.

### Upper bound: $|S| \leq 2^{\aleph_0}$

Every countably infinite subset $X \subseteq \omega_1$ is the range of some func
tion $f: \omega \to \omega_1$ (enumerate its elements). The map $\operatorname{r
ange}: \omega_1^\omega \to [\omega_1]^{\leq \aleph_0}$ is therefore a surjection
 onto the collection of at-most-countable subsets, giving

$$|S| \leq |[\omega_1]^{\leq \aleph_0}| \leq |\omega_1^\omega| = \aleph_1^{\alep
h_0}.$$

**Key observation:** In ZFC, $\aleph_1 \leq 2^{\aleph_0}$ always holds, since $2
^{\aleph_0}$ is an uncountable cardinal and $\aleph_1$ is the least uncountable
cardinal. Using this:

$$\aleph_1^{\aleph_0} \leq (2^{\aleph_0})^{\aleph_0} = 2^{\aleph_0 \cdot \aleph_
0} = 2^{\aleph_0}.$$

(The last step uses $\aleph_0 \cdot \aleph_0 = \aleph_0$.) Therefore $|S| \leq 2
^{\aleph_0}$.

### Conclusion

Combining both inequalities:

$$2^{\aleph_0} \leq |S| \leq 2^{\aleph_0},$$

so $|S| = 2^{\aleph_0} = |\mathcal{P}(\omega)|$.

**Yes, there is a bijection from $\{X \subseteq \omega_1 \mid |X| = \aleph_0\}$
to $\mathcal{P}(\omega)$**, and it follows from a straightforward counting argum
ent. The crucial step is the ZFC theorem $\aleph_1 \leq 2^{\aleph_0}$, which for
ces $\aleph_1^{\aleph_0} = 2^{\aleph_0}$, collapsing the naïve upper bound $\ale
ph_1^{\aleph_0}$ down to $2^{\aleph_0}$. This result is independent of CH: regar
dless of whether $2^{\aleph_0} = \aleph_1$ or $2^{\aleph_0} > \aleph_1$, the equ
ality $|[\omega_1]^{\aleph_0}| = 2^{\aleph_0}$ holds in ZFC.
