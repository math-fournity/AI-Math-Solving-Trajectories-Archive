# Problem

A high-tech manufacturing firm has developed a modular security drone in the shape of a perfect cube. To ensure the drone’s orientation can be tracked by computer vision from any angle, the engineering team must color each of its 6 square face-panels. 

The team has a palette of 6 distinct specialized polymer coatings available. They must follow these two technical constraints:
1. Each of the 6 faces must be coated in exactly one color.
2. Any two faces that share a physical edge must be coated in different colors to ensure high visual contrast.

The manufacturing process considers two drones to be identical if one can be rotated in three-dimensional space to look exactly like the other (meaning the arrangement of colors is what matters, not the drone's orientation in the assembly rig). 

How many unique coloring schemes are possible for the production of these drones?
