# Problem

In the competitive landscape of the "Centennial Logistics Network," there are 100 distinct distribution hubs, indexed by unique ID numbers from 1 to 100. A "Strategic Partnership" is formed whenever two hubs, Hub $a$ and Hub $b$, are selected such that the ID of the first is strictly less than the ID of the second ($1 \leq a < b \leq 100$).

The efficiency of a Partnership is evaluated based on a "Resonance Cycle." How many unique "Synchronized" Partnerships can be formed within this network of 100 hubs?
