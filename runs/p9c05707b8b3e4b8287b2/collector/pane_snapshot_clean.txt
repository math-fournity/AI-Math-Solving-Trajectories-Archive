**Computing the product measure via Carathéodory:**

$$(\mu\times\nu)(E) = \inf\left\{\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \mu(A_n)\nu(B_n) : E \subse
teq \bigcup_n A_n\times B_n,\; A_n\in\mathcal{B}(\mathbb{R}),\; B_n\subseteq\mat

**Claim:** Every countable cover of $E$ has $\sum_n \mu(A_n)\nu(B_n) = \infty$.

*Proof.* Suppose $E \subseteq \bigcup_n A_n \times B_n$. For each $x \in [0,1]$,
 the point $(x,x) \in E$ requires $x \in A_n \cap B_n$ for some $n$. Thus $[0,1]
 \subseteq \bigcup_n (A_n \cap B_n)$.

Split indices into $N_{\text{fin}} = \{n : B_n \text{ finite}\}$ and $N_\infty =
 \{n : B_n \text{ infinite}\}$.

- $\bigcup_{n \in N_{\text{fin}}}(A_n \cap B_n) \subseteq \bigcup_{n \in N_{\tex
t{fin}}} B_n =: C$, which is countable.
- So $[0,1]\setminus C \subseteq \bigcup_{n \in N_\infty}(A_n \cap B_n) \subsete

- Since $C$ is countable, $\mu([0,1]\setminus C) = 1$.
- $\bigcup_{n\in N_\infty} A_n$ is Borel (countable union of Borel sets) with $\
mu(\bigcup_{n\in N_\infty} A_n) \geq 1$.

- So some $n \in N_\infty$ has $\mu(A_n) > 0$. Since $B_n$ is infinite, $\nu(B_n

Therefore $\sum_n \mu(A_n)\nu(B_n) = \infty$ for every cover. $\square$

Taking the infimum over all covers:

$$(\mu \times \nu)(E) = \infty$$

**Conclusion:** $(\mu \times \nu)(E) \neq 0$. The product measure equals $\infty
$.
