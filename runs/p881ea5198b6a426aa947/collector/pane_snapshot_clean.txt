### Substitution $t = x+1$

Note that $2x^2 + 4x + 5 = 2(x+1)^2 + 3$. Setting $t = x+1$ (so $\frac{dy}{dx} =
 \frac{dy}{dt}$, $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} = \frac{d^2y}{dt^2}$), the equation becomes

$$(2t^2+3)\,\ddot{y} - 20t\,\dot{y} + 60\,y = 0, \qquad y(0)=3,\;\; \dot{y}(0)=-
3.$$

### Power-series solution

Let $y = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n t^n$. Substituting:

- $(2t^2+3)\ddot{y}$ contributes $2n(n-1)a_n\,t^n + 3(n+2)(n+1)a_{n+2}\,t^n$,
- $-20t\dot{y}$ contributes $-20n\,a_n\,t^n$,
- $60y$ contributes $60\,a_n\,t^n$.

Setting the coefficient of $t^n$ to zero:

$$3(n+2)(n+1)\,a_{n+2} + \bigl[2n(n-1)-20n+60\bigr]\,a_n = 0.$$

Since $2n(n-1)-20n+60 = 2n^2-22n+60 = 2(n-5)(n-6)$, the recurrence is

$$\boxed{a_{n+2} = \frac{-2(n-5)(n-6)}{3(n+2)(n+1)}\,a_n.}$$

This decouples even and odd indices. Crucially, $a_{n+2}=0$ when $n=5$ (odd bran
ch terminates) and when $n=6$ (even branch terminates), so **both branches are f
inite polynomials**.

| 0 | $\frac{-2(-5)(-6)}{3\cdot2\cdot1}\cdot 3 = -10\cdot 3$ | $a_2 = -30$ |
| 2 | $\frac{-2(-3)(-4)}{3\cdot4\cdot3}\cdot(-30) = -\frac{2}{3}\cdot(-30)$ | $a
_4 = 20$ |

= -\tfrac{8}{9}$ |
| 6 | factor $(6-6)=0$ | $a_8 = 0$ (terminates) |

### Odd branch ($a_1 = -3$)

| $n$ | $a_{n+2}$ | value |
|-----|-----------|-------|
| 1 | $\frac{-2(-4)(-5)}{3\cdot3\cdot2}\cdot(-3) = -\frac{20}{9}\cdot(-3)$ | $a_
3 = \tfrac{20}{3}$ |

### Solution

$$y = 3 - 3t - 30t^2 + \frac{20}{3}t^3 + 20t^4 - \frac{4}{3}t^5 - \frac{8}{9}t^6
, \qquad t = x+1.$$
