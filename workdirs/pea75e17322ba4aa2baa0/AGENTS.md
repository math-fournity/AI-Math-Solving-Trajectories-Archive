# Problem

A boutique logistics company operates a central warehouse divided into a 3x3 grid of storage zones. The manager must allocate specific quantities of shipping containers to these nine zones according to strict safety and weight-balancing protocols:

1.  **Inventory Values:** Each zone must contain a non-negative integer number of containers.
2.  **Weight Balancing:** To keep the warehouse foundation stable, the total number of containers in every row of zones must be exactly 7, and the total number of containers in every column of zones must also be exactly 7.
3.  **Unique Identification:** To avoid tracking errors, the company policy dictates that no non-zero integer can be repeated within the same row or the same column. (Note: Multiple zones within a row or column may contain zero containers, as zero is not considered a "non-zero integer").

In how many distinct ways can the manager distribute the containers across the 3x3 grid of zones to satisfy all these requirements?
