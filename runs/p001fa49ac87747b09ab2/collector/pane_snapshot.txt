Looking at this problem, I need to find the minimum side length *b* of a regular
 hexagonal wrench opening that can fit over a square nut with edge length 8.

## Setup

Place the regular hexagon (side *b*) centered at the origin with vertices at ang
les 0°, 60°, 120°, 180°, 240°, 300°. The six sides satisfy:

- **Top/Bottom:** y = ±b√3/2
- **Upper-right:** √3x + y = √3b
- **Upper-left:** −√3x + y = √3b
- **Lower-right:** √3x − y = √3b
- **Lower-left:** −√3x − y = √3b

The square (side 8) is centered at the origin (optimal by the hexagon's central
symmetry), rotated by angle θ, with half-diagonal d = 4√2. Its vertices are at a
ngles θ+45°, θ+135°, θ+225°, θ+315°.

## Finding the optimal orientation

Setting α = θ + 45°, by the hexagon's 60° symmetry and the square's 90° symmetry
, I restrict to α ∈ [45°, 75°] (i.e., θ ∈ [0°, 30°]).

**Vertex 1** at angle α = (d cos α, d sin α) gives constraints:
- C₁ = 2d sin α/√3 (top side)
- C₂ = d(cos α + sin α/√3) (upper-right side)

**Vertex 2** at angle α+90° = (−d sin α, d cos α) gives constraints:
- C₃ = 2d cos α/√3 (top side)
- C₄ = d(sin α + cos α/√3) (upper-left side)

**Comparing:** C₂ ≥ C₁ and C₄ ≥ C₃ throughout the range (verified by checking C₂
−C₁ = d(cos α − sin α/√3) ≥ 0 and C₄−C₃ = d(sin α − cos α/√3) ≥ 0). So the bindi
ng constraints are C₂ and C₄.

For α ∈ [45°, 60°]: C₄ ≥ C₂ (since C₄−C₂ = d(sin α − cos α)(1 − 1/√3) ≥ 0), and
C₄ is **increasing** (f'(α) = cos α − sin α/√3 > 0 on this interval), so the min
imum is at **α = 45°**.

For α ∈ [60°, 75°]: by the symmetry α ↔ 120°−α (reflecting about the 60° axis),
the same minimum occurs at **α = 75°**.

Both endpoints give the same value, and all interior points are larger. The mini
mum is at α = 45° (θ = 0°).

## Computing b

At θ = 0°, the square is axis-aligned with vertices (±4, ±4). The binding constr
aint is the upper-right side:

$$\sqrt{3}\cdot 4 + 4 \leq \sqrt{3}\,b \implies b \geq 4 + \frac{4}{\sqrt{3}} =
4 + \frac{4\sqrt{3}}{3} = \frac{4(3+\sqrt{3})}{3}$$

**Verification:** At θ = 0°, the square vertices (4,4), (−4,4), (−4,−4), (4,−4)
touch the four slanted sides of the hexagon (upper-right, upper-left, lower-left
, lower-right), while the top/bottom constraint gives b ≥ 8/√3 ≈ 4.62, which is
non-binding since 4 + 4/√3 ≈ 6.31 > 4.62. ✓

Rotating the square to any θ ∈ (0°, 30°) only increases the required b (confirme
d by evaluating C₄ at intermediate angles, e.g., α = 50° gives ≈ 6.43 > 6.31).
