## Proof

**Claim:** Yes, $\mu_U$ is a Radon measure.

We verify the three defining properties of a Radon measure on a locally compact
Hausdorff space: (i) local finiteness, (ii) outer regularity on all Borel sets,
(iii) inner regularity on open sets. The hypothesis that $U$ is **open** is used
 critically in step (ii).

---

**(i) Local finiteness.** For any compact $K \subseteq X$,
$$\mu_U(K) = \mu(K \cap U) \le \mu(K) < \infty,$$
since $K \cap U \subseteq K$ and $\mu$ is finite on compact sets. $\checkmark$

---

**(ii) Outer regularity on Borel sets.** Let $E \subseteq X$ be Borel. We must s
how
$$\mu_U(E) = \inf\{\mu_U(V) : E \subseteq V,\; V \text{ open}\}.$$

The inequality $\inf \ge \mu_U(E)$ is immediate: for every open $V \supseteq E$,
 we have $E \cap U \subseteq V \cap U$, so $\mu_U(V) = \mu(V \cap U) \ge \mu(E \
cap U) = \mu_U(E)$.

For the reverse inequality, let $\varepsilon > 0$. Since $\mu$ is outer regular
and $E \cap U$ is Borel, there exists an open set $W \supseteq E \cap U$ with
$$\mu(W) < \mu(E \cap U) + \varepsilon.$$

**Now use that $U$ is open**, so $X \setminus U$ is closed. Define
$$V := W \cup (X \setminus U).$$
Then $V$ is open (union of an open set and a closed set's complement... more pre
cisely, $W$ is open and $X\setminus U$ is closed, so $V$ is open). We have $E \s
ubseteq V$ because
$$E = (E \cap U) \cup (E \setminus U) \subseteq W \cup (X \setminus U) = V.$$
Moreover,
$$V \cap U = \bigl(W \cup (X \setminus U)\bigr) \cap U = (W \cap U) \cup \emptys
et = W \cap U \subseteq W.$$
Therefore
$$\mu_U(V) = \mu(V \cap U) = \mu(W \cap U) \le \mu(W) < \mu(E \cap U) + \varepsi
lon = \mu_U(E) + \varepsilon.$$
Since $\varepsilon > 0$ was arbitrary, $\inf \le \mu_U(E)$, giving equality. $\c

$$\mu_U(O) = \mu(O \cap U) = \sup\{\mu(K) : K \subseteq O \cap U,\; K \text{ com

For any compact $K \subseteq O \cap U$, we have $K \subseteq O$ and $K \subseteq

$$\sup\{\mu_U(K) : K \subseteq O,\; K \text{ compact}\} \ge \sup\{\mu(K) : K \su

$$\mu_U(O) = \sup\{\mu_U(K) : K \subseteq O,\; K \text{ compact}\}. \quad\checkm

**Conclusion.** All three Radon properties hold, so $\mu_U$ is a Radon measure.
The openness of $U$ is essential in step (ii): it ensures $X \setminus U$ is clo

regularity approximation to go through.
