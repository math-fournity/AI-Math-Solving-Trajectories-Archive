## Spectrum of $C(K) \oplus C(K')$

ommutative $C^*$-algebra $A := C(K) \oplus C(K')$ is homeomorphic to the disjoin
t union $K \sqcup K'$, where each summand carries its original topology and is c
lopen in the union.

### Step 1. $A$ is unital and commutative

$C(K)$ and $C(K')$ are unital commutative $C^*$-algebras (pointwise operations,
sup norm, involution $f \mapsto \overline{f}$). Their direct sum $A = C(K) \oplu
s C(K')$ with coordinatewise operations, norm $\|(f,g)\| = \max(\|f\|_\infty, \|
g\|_\infty)$, and involution $(f,g)^* = (\overline f, \overline g)$ is again a u
nital commutative $C^*$-algebra with unit $\mathbf 1 = (1,1)$.

Since $A$ is commutative, every irreducible representation is one-dimensional, s
o the spectrum $\widehat A$ coincides with the Gelfand spectrum: the set of **ch
aracters** (nonzero $*$-homomorphisms $\chi : A \to \mathbb C$) equipped with th

### Step 2. Two canonical central projections

Define
$$e_1 := (1, 0), \qquad e_2 := (0, 1).$$
These are self-adjoint projections satisfying
$$e_1 + e_2 = \mathbf 1, \qquad e_1 e_2 = 0 = e_2 e_1, \qquad e_i^* = e_i.$$
They are central (everything is, since $A$ is commutative) and give the Peirce d
ecomposition
$$A = e_1 A \oplus e_2 A \cong C(K) \oplus C(K'), \qquad e_1 A = C(K)\oplus\{0\}
,\quad e_2 A = \{0\}\oplus C(K').$$

### Step 3. Every character lives on exactly one summand

Let $\chi : A \to \mathbb C$ be a character. Then
$$\chi(e_1) = \chi(e_1^2) = \chi(e_1)^2, \qquad \chi(e_2) = \chi(e_2)^2,$$
so each $\chi(e_i) \in \{0,1\}$. Also
$$\chi(e_1) + \chi(e_2) = \chi(\mathbf 1) = 1,$$
and $\chi \neq 0$ forces $\chi(\mathbf 1) = 1$. Hence **exactly one** of $\chi(e
_1), \chi(e_2)$ equals $1$.

- **Case $\chi(e_1) = 1,\ \chi(e_2) = 0$.** For any $(f,g) \in A$,
  $$\chi(f,g) = \chi\bigl(e_1(f,g)\bigr) + \chi\bigl(e_2(f,g)\bigr) = \chi(f,0)
+ \chi(0,g) = \chi(f,0),$$
  since $\chi(0,g) = \chi(e_2)\,\chi(0,g) = 0$. Thus $\chi$ factors through the
projection $A \to e_1 A \cong C(K)$, yielding a character $\widetilde\chi : C(K)

ter of $C(K)$ is evaluation at a unique point $x \in K$:

- **Case $\chi(e_1) = 0,\ \chi(e_2) = 1$.** Symmetrically, $\chi$ factors throug

Conversely, for every $x \in K$ the map $\chi_x(f,g) := f(x)$ is a character (no
nzero, since $\chi_x(\mathbf 1)=1$; multiplicative and $*$-preserving by pointwi

### Step 4. Bijective parametrization

Combining Steps 3, the map
$$\Phi : K \sqcup K' \longrightarrow \widehat A, \qquad \Phi(x) = \chi_x\ \text{

### Step 5. Topology

The weak-$*$ topology on $\widehat A$ is the topology of pointwise convergence o

- **On the $K$-piece:** $\chi_{x_\alpha} \to \chi_x$ iff $f(x_\alpha) \to f(x)$

The spectrum of $C(K) \oplus C(K')$ is the disjoint union (topological coproduct
