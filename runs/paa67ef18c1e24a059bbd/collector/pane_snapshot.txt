## Proof

$$h(z) := \frac{f(z)}{g(z)}$$

is well-defined and holomorphic on all of $\mathbb{C}$, i.e. $h$ is entire. (Bot

The hypothesis $|f(z)| \le |g(z)|$ for all $z \in \mathbb{C}$ gives

$$|h(z)| = \frac{|f(z)|}{|g(z)|} \le 1 \qquad \text{for all } z \in \mathbb{C}.$
$

Thus $h$ is a bounded entire function. By **Liouville's theorem**, $h$ is consta

$$h(z) = C \quad \text{for all } z.$$

Consequently

$$f(z) = C \cdot g(z) \qquad \text{for all } z \in \mathbb{C}.$$
