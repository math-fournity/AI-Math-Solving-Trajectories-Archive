Looking at this problem, I need to find the expected number of reachable nodes i
n a half-infinite ladder graph with bond percolation at p = 1/2.

## Setup

Place the two bus lines as rows 0 (top) and 1 (bottom), with nodes at columns n
= 0, 1, 2, .... The starting node is (0, 0). Gates (each conductive with probabi
lity 1/2, independently):
- **Horizontal**: between (r, n) and (r, n+1) for r ∈ {0,1}
- **Vertical**: between (0, n) and (1, n) for n ≥ 0

## Markov Chain by Column

Define the **state** at column n as the set of reachable nodes there: ∅, T (top
only), B (bottom only), or TB (both). Any path from column 0 to column n+1 must
pass through column n, so the state evolves as a Markov chain.

**Transition probabilities** (each gate independent, prob 1/2 conductive):

From **T** (only (0,n) reachable): (0,n+1) reachable iff top-horizontal conducts
; (1,n+1) reachable iff top-horizontal AND vertical-at-(n+1) conduct. Enumeratin
g 8 equally likely gate outcomes:
- → ∅ with prob 1/2, → T with prob 1/4, → TB with prob 1/4

From **B**: by symmetry → ∅ with prob 1/2, → B with prob 1/4, → TB with prob 1/4
.

From **TB** (both reachable at column n): (0,n+1) reachable iff (top-horizontal)
 OR (bottom-horizontal AND vertical); (1,n+1) reachable iff (bottom-horizontal)
OR (top-horizontal AND vertical). Enumerating 8 cases:
- → ∅ with prob 1/4, → T with prob 1/8, → B with prob 1/8, → TB with prob 1/2

From **∅**: → ∅ with prob 1 (absorbing).

**Initial state**: (0,0) is reachable; (1,0) reachable iff vertical gate at colu
mn 0 conducts. So S₀ = T with prob 1/2, S₀ = TB with prob 1/2.

## Reduction via Symmetry

Let a_n = P(T), b_n = P(B), c_n = P(TB), d_n = P(∅). Initial: a₀ = 1/2, b₀ = 0,
c₀ = 1/2, d₀ = 0.

The difference δ_n = a_n − b_n satisfies δ_{n+1} = δ_n/4, so δ_n → 0 rapidly. De
fine s_n = a_n + b_n. The recurrences become:

$$s_{n+1} = \frac{s_n}{4} + \frac{c_n}{4}, \qquad c_{n+1} = \frac{s_n}{4} + \fra
c{c_n}{2}$$

with s₀ = 1/2, c₀ = 1/2.

## Matrix Formulation

$$\begin{pmatrix} s_{n+1} \\ c_{n+1} \end{pmatrix} = M \begin{pmatrix} s_n \\ c_
n \end{pmatrix}, \quad M = \begin{pmatrix} 1/4 & 1/4 \\ 1/4 & 1/2 \end{pmatrix}$
$

The expected reachable nodes at column n is a_n + b_n + 2c_n = s_n + 2c_n. The t
otal expected count is:

$$E = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} (s_n + 2c_n) = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix} \su
m_{n=0}^{\infty} M^n \begin{pmatrix} 1/2 \\ 1/2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 \end{pmatrix} (I - M)^{-1} \begin{pmatrix} 1/2 \\ 1/2 \end{pmatrix}$$

**Eigenvalues** of M: λ = (3 ± √5)/8 ≈ 0.6545, 0.0955 — both < 1, so the series
converges.

## Computing the Inverse

$$I - M = \begin{pmatrix} 3/4 & -1/4 \\ -1/4 & 1/2 \end{pmatrix}, \quad \det(I-M
) = \frac{3}{8} - \frac{1}{16} = \frac{5}{16}$$

$$(I - M)^{-1} = \frac{16}{5}\begin{pmatrix} 1/2 & 1/4 \\ 1/4 & 3/4 \end{pmatrix
} = \begin{pmatrix} 8/5 & 4/5 \\ 4/5 & 12/5 \end{pmatrix}$$

$$(I - M)^{-1}\begin{pmatrix} 1/2 \\ 1/2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 8/5 \cd
ot 1/2 + 4/5 \cdot 1/2 \\ 4/5 \cdot 1/2 + 12/5 \cdot 1/2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{
pmatrix} 6/5 \\ 8/5 \end{pmatrix}$$

$$E = 1 \cdot \frac{6}{5} + 2 \cdot \frac{8}{5} = \frac{6}{5} + \frac{16}{5} = \
frac{22}{5}$$

## Verification
