The SDE is
$$dY_t = -Y_t\,dW_t + Y_t\,dt, \qquad Y_0 = 1.$$

This is a linear (geometric) SDE of the standard form
$$dY_t = Y_t\bigl(b_t\,dt + \sigma_t\,dW_t\bigr),$$
with $b_t \equiv 1$ and $\sigma_t \equiv -1$.

**General solution of the linear SDE.** For $dY_t = Y_t(b_t\,dt + \sigma_t\,dW_t
)$ with deterministic $b,\sigma$, the unique strong solution is
$$Y_t = Y_0 \exp\!\left(\int_0^t \Bigl(b_s - \tfrac12 \sigma_s^2\Bigr)\,ds + \in
t_0^t \sigma_s\,dW_s\right).$$

**Substituting.** With $b_s = 1$, $\sigma_s = -1$, $Y_0 = 1$:
$$Y_t = \exp\!\left(\int_0^t \Bigl(1 - \tfrac12\cdot 1\Bigr)\,ds + \int_0^t (-1)

Evaluating at $t = 1$:
$$Y_1 = \exp\!\left(\tfrac12 - W_1\right).$$

**Direct verification via Itô's formula.** Let $Y_t = \exp\!\bigl(\tfrac12 t - W

$$dX_t = \tfrac12\,dt - dW_t, \qquad (dX_t)^2 = dt.$$
Itô's formula gives
$$dY_t = Y_t\,dX_t + \tfrac12 Y_t\,(dX_t)^2 = Y_t\Bigl(\tfrac12\,dt - dW_t\Bigr)
