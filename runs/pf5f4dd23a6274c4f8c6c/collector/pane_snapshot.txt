## Solution

**Computing the integral.**

$$\int_0^a (x^2 - 2bx + 3c)\,dx = \left[\frac{x^3}{3} - bx^2 + 3cx\right]_0^a =
\frac{a^3}{3} - ba^2 + 3ca.$$

Setting this equal to zero and dividing by $a>0$:

$$\frac{a^2}{3} - ba + 3c = 0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad a^2 - 3ba + 9c = 0 \quad
\Longrightarrow\quad c = \frac{a(3b-a)}{9}.$$

**Constraints.** We need $a>b>c$ with $a,b,c\in\{1,\dots,10\}$ distinct, and $c$
 a positive integer. Since $c>0$ we need $3b>a$, combined with $a>b$ this gives
$b<a<3b$. Also $a\ge 3$ (since $b\ge 2, c\ge 1$).

**Enumerating** all $a\in\{3,\dots,10\}$ and valid $b$:

| $a$ | $b$ | $c=a(3b-a)/9$ | Valid? |
|-----|-----|----------------|--------|
| 3 | 2 | $3(6-3)/9=1$ | ✓ $3>2>1$ |
| 4 | 2,3 | $8/9,\;20/9$ | ✗ |
| 5 | 2,3,4 | $5/9,\;20/9,\;35/9$ | ✗ |
| 6 | 3 | $6(9-6)/9=2$ | ✓ $6>3>2$ |
| 6 | 4 | $6(12-6)/9=4$ | ✗ ($b=c$) |
| 6 | 5 | $6(15-6)/9=6$ | ✗ ($c=a$) |
| 7 | 3–6 | non-integer | ✗ |
| 8 | 3–7 | non-integer | ✗ |
| 9 | 4 | $9(12-9)/9=3$ | ✓ $9>4>3$ |
| 9 | 5 | $9(15-9)/9=6$ | ✗ ($b<c$) |
| 9 | 6,7,8 | $9,\;12,\;15$ | ✗ |
| 10 | 4–9 | non-integer | ✗ |

**Verification** of the three favorable triples:
- $(3,2,1)$: $9-18+9=0$ ✓
- $(6,3,2)$: $72-108+36=0$ ✓
- $(9,4,3)$: $243-324+81=0$ ✓

**Counting.** Total draws: $\binom{10}{3}=120$. Favorable: $3$.

$$P = \frac{3}{120} = \boxed{\dfrac{1}{40}}.$$
