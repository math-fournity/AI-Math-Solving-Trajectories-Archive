$$xz - x = x(z-1),$$

- **Case $x = 0$:** then $x^2 - yz = -yz = 0$, so $yz = 0$, i.e. $y = 0$ or $z =

- **Case $z = 1$:** then $x^2 - yz = x^2 - y = 0$, so $y = x^2$. This gives the
parabola

Hence $V(I) = L_1 \cup L_2 \cup C$.

**Step 2: Identify the minimal primes.**

- $\mathfrak{p}_2 = (x, z)$ for $L_2$,

One checks $I \subseteq \mathfrak{p}_i$ for each $i$:

- $\mathbb{C}[x,y,z]/(x,z) \cong \mathbb{C}[y]$, dimension $1$.
- $\mathbb{C}[x,y,z]/(z-1, y-x^2) \cong \mathbb{C}[x]$, dimension $1$.

$$\dim R = \dim \mathbb{C}[x,y,z]/\sqrt{I} = \max\{1, 1, 1\} = 1.$$
