$$x = \sum_{\text{east}} \ell_i - \sum_{\text{west}} \ell_i, \qquad y = \sum_{\t
ext{north}} \ell_i - \sum_{\text{south}} \ell_i$$

**Key observation:** Since $\ell_{4j+1} < \ell_{4j+2} < \ell_{4j+3}$ are integer

## Ruling Out Three Residue Classes

- **$n \equiv 0$:** $x = \sum_{j=0}^{k-1}(\ell_{4j+1}-\ell_{4j+3}) \leq -2k < 0$

**Only $n \equiv 2 \pmod{4}$ can reach the first quadrant.** Write $n = 4k+2$.
