# Problem

In a specialized logistics hub, a circular perimeter contains $n$ docking stations, where $n \geq 4$. To facilitate movement, two different companies—Emerald Rail and Ruby Transit—are tasked with building internal transport tracks (diagonals) between these stations.

Emerald Rail must construct exactly $n-3$ straight tracks such that no two of their tracks cross one another within the hub. Separately, Ruby Transit must also construct exactly $n-3$ straight tracks such that no two of their tracks cross one another within the hub. However, an Emerald track and a Ruby track are permitted to cross.

The cost of maintaining the hub depends on the number of crossover points where an Emerald track passes over a Ruby track. Let $f(n)$ represent the maximum possible number of such intersection points that can exist within the hub for a given number of stations $n$.

Calculate the total value of $\sum_{n=4}^{8} f(n)$.
