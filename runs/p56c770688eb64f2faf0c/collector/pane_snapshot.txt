## Proof

**Claim:** Every symmetric matrix with zero diagonal can be expressed as the com
mutator $[D, A] = DA - AD$, where $D$ is a diagonal matrix with positive diagona
l entries and $A$ is skew-symmetric.

**Setup.** Let $S \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ be symmetric ($S^T = S$) with zer
o diagonal ($S_{ii} = 0$). We seek $D = \operatorname{diag}(d_1, \dots, d_n)$ wi
th all $d_i > 0$, and a skew-symmetric $A$ ($A^T = -A$), such that $S = DA - AD$
.

**Key identity.** For diagonal $D$ and arbitrary $A$,
$$
[D, A]_{ij} = (DA - AD)_{ij} = d_i A_{ij} - A_{ij} d_j = (d_i - d_j)\, A_{ij}.
$$

**Necessary conditions are automatically satisfied.** If $A$ is skew-symmetric,
then
$$
[D,A]_{ji} = (d_j - d_i) A_{ji} = (d_j - d_i)(-A_{ij}) = (d_i - d_j) A_{ij} = [D
,A]_{ij},
$$
so $[D,A]$ is symmetric. Also $[D,A]_{ii} = (d_i - d_i)A_{ii} = 0$. Hence $[D,A]
$ is always symmetric with zero diagonal — matching the structure of $S$.

**Construction.** Choose $d_1, \dots, d_n$ to be any $n$ **distinct positive** r
eal numbers (for instance $d_i = i$). Define
$$
A_{ij} = \begin{cases} \dfrac{S_{ij}}{d_i - d_j}, & i \neq j, \\[6pt] 0, & i = j
. \end{cases}
$$

*Skew-symmetry of $A$:* For $i \neq j$, using $S_{ji} = S_{ij}$,
$$
A_{ji} = \frac{S_{ji}}{d_j - d_i} = \frac{S_{ij}}{-(d_i - d_j)} = -\frac{S_{ij}}
{d_i - d_j} = -A_{ij},
$$
and $A_{ii} = 0$. So $A^T = -A$. ✓

*The commutator recovers $S$:* For $i \neq j$,
$$
[D,A]_{ij} = (d_i - d_j)\, A_{ij} = (d_i - d_j) \cdot \frac{S_{ij}}{d_i - d_j} =
 S_{ij},
$$
where the division is valid because $d_i \neq d_j$ (the $d_i$ are pairwise disti
nct). For $i = j$,
$$
[D,A]_{ii} = 0 = S_{ii}.
$$

Thus $[D, A] = S$ entry-by-entry.

**Conclusion.** The construction works for *every* symmetric matrix with zero di
agonal: the only requirement is that $d_i \neq d_j$ whenever we must solve $S_{i
j} = (d_i - d_j) A_{ij}$, and choosing all $d_i$ pairwise distinct (and positive
) guarantees this universally. Therefore:

**Yes — every symmetric matrix with zero diagonal can be expressed as the commut
ator of a diagonal matrix with positive diagonal entries and a skew-symmetric ma
trix.**
