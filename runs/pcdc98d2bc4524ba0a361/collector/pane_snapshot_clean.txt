### Step 1: Closed forms for the two sums

Let $N = 500$. Using the standard power-sum formulas

$$\sum_{n=1}^{N} n = \tfrac{N(N+1)}{2},\quad \sum_{n=1}^{N} n^{2} = \tfrac{N(N+1
)(2N+1)}{6},\quad \sum_{n=1}^{N} n^{4} = \tfrac{N(N+1)(2N+1)(3N^{2}+3N-1)}{30},$
$

**Denominator:**

$$D=\sum_{n=1}^{N}(n^{2}+n+1)=\frac{N(N+1)(2N+1)}{6}+\frac{N(N+1)}{2}+N$$

$$=N\!\left[\frac{(N+1)(2N+1)+3(N+1)+6}{6}\right]=N\!\left[\frac{(N+1)(2N+4)+6}{
6}\right]=\frac{N\bigl(2(N+1)(N+2)+6\bigr)}{6}=\frac{N(N^{2}+3N+5)}{3}.$$

**Numerator:**

$$S=\sum_{n=1}^{N}(n^{4}+n^{2}+1)=\sum n^{4}+\sum n^{2}+N$$

$$=N\!\left[\frac{(N+1)(2N+1)(3N^{2}+3N-1)+5(N+1)(2N+1)+30}{30}\right]$$

$$=\frac{N\bigl[(N+1)(2N+1)(3N^{2}+3N+4)+30\bigr]}{30}.$$

### Step 2: Form the ratio

$$K=\frac{S}{D}=\frac{(N+1)(2N+1)(3N^{2}+3N+4)+30}{10\,(N^{2}+3N+5)}.$$

### Step 3: Substitute $N=500$

- $N+1 = 501$, $\;2N+1 = 1001$
- $3N^{2}+3N+4 = 750000+1500+4 = 751504$
- $N^{2}+3N+5 = 250000+1500+5 = 251505$

**Numerator of $K$:**
$$501\cdot 1001 = 501501,$$
$$501501\cdot 751504 = 376880007504,$$
$$376880007504 + 30 = 376880007534.$$

**Denominator of $K$:**
$$10\cdot 251505 = 2515050.$$

So $K = \dfrac{376880007534}{2515050}$.

### Step 4: Reduce to lowest terms

Factor the denominator:
$$2515050 = 2\cdot 5^{2}\cdot 3^{7}\cdot 23.$$

Check the numerator $376880007534$ against these primes:

- **Divisible by 2**: yes (even). $\;376880007534/2 = 188440003767$ (now odd, so
 only one factor of $2$).
- **Divisible by 3**: digit sum of $188440003767$ is $1+8+8+4+4+0+0+0+3+7+6+7=48
$, divisible by $3$. $\;188440003767/3 = 62813334589$.
  - Digit sum of $62813334589$ is $6+2+8+1+3+3+3+4+5+8+9=52$, **not** divisible
by $3$.
- **Divisible by 5**: $62813334589$ ends in $9$ — no.
- **Divisible by 23**: $62813334589 = 23\cdot 2731014547 + 8$ — remainder $8$, n
o.

Hence $\gcd(376880007534,\,2515050) = 2\cdot 3 = 6$, and

$$K = \frac{376880007534/6}{2515050/6} = \frac{62813334589}{419175},$$

with $\gcd(62813334589,\,419175)=1$ (since $419175 = 5^{2}\cdot 3^{6}\cdot 23$ s
hares no prime factor with $62813334589$).

### Step 5: Sanity check with small $N$

- $N=1$: $K=\frac{3}{3}=1$. Formula gives $\frac{(2)(3)(10)+30}{10\cdot 9}=\frac
{90}{90}=1$. ✓
- $N=2$: $K=\frac{3+21}{3+10}=\frac{24}{10}=\frac{12}{5}$. Formula gives $\frac{
(3)(5)(22)+30}{10\cdot 15}=\frac{360}{150}=\frac{12}{5}$. ✓

### Result

The reduced fraction is $\dfrac{p}{q}=\dfrac{62813334589}{419175}$, so

$$\boxed{p = 62813334589}.$$
