Looking at this problem, I need to determine whether regularity of the push-down
 forces vanishing on the exceptional divisor.

**Answer: No.**

**Proof.**

Let $\pi: \hat X \to X$ be the blow-up at $p$, with exceptional divisor $E = \pi
^{-1}(p)$.

**Key fact.** Since $X$ is smooth, it is normal. For any proper birational morph
ism $\pi: \hat X \to X$ of integral schemes with $X$ normal, we have
$$\pi_* \mathcal{O}_{\hat X} = \mathcal{O}_X.$$
*Justification:* $\pi_*\mathcal{O}_{\hat X}$ is a coherent $\mathcal{O}_X$-algeb
ra contained in the constant sheaf $K(X)$ (the function field, identified via bi
rationality), and it contains $\mathcal{O}_X$ since $\pi$ is an isomorphism over
 a dense open. By normality (algebraic Hartogs), $\mathcal{O}_X = \bigcap_{\math
rm{ht}(\mathfrak p)=1} \mathcal{O}_{X,\mathfrak p}$ inside $K(X)$. The birationa
l proper map $\pi$ is an isomorphism over all codimension-1 points of $X$ (the e
xceptional locus of a blow-up at a point on a smooth variety has codimension $\g
eq 2$ in $X$ when $\dim X \geq 2$; when $\dim X = 1$ the blow-up at a smooth poi
nt is an isomorphism and $E = \emptyset$). Hence $\pi_*\mathcal{O}_{\hat X}$ and
 $\mathcal{O}_X$ agree at every codimension-1 point, forcing equality.

**Consequence.** Taking global sections,
$$H^0(\hat X, \mathcal{O}_{\hat X}) \xrightarrow{\;\sim\;} H^0(X, \mathcal{O}_X)
,$$
so *every* regular function on $\hat X$ is the pullback $\pi^* g$ of a unique re
gular function $g$ on $X$, and its push-down is automatically regular. The hypot
hesis in the problem is therefore **always satisfied**.

**Counterexample.** Take $f = 1$ (the constant function). Then:
- $f$ is regular on $\hat X$.
- Its push-down is $g = 1$, which is regular on $X$.
- But $f|_E = 1 \neq 0$, so $f$ does **not** vanish on the exceptional divisor.

More generally, for any $g \in H^0(X, \mathcal{O}_X)$ with $g(p) \neq 0$, the pu
llback $\pi^* g$ is regular on $\hat X$, pushes down to the regular function $g$
, yet $\pi^* g|_E = g(p) \neq 0$ (since $E$ is contracted to $p$, the pullback i
s constant on $E$ with value $g(p)$).

Thus the regularity of the push-down does **not** imply vanishing on the excepti
onal divisor. $\blacksquare$
