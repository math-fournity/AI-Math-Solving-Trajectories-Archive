## Solution

**Binary tree model.** The process forms a binary tree: the root is the initial
party of 2047. Each internal node (a party of size ≥ 2) splits into two children
. A child of size ≥ 2 is an internal node (a party that will split); a child of
size 1 is a leaf (a singleton, not a party). The parties are exactly the interna
l nodes.

Each resident corresponds to a leaf. A resident at depth $d$ (root at depth 0) b
elongs to exactly $d$ parties — the $d$ internal nodes on the path from root to
leaf. Therefore:

$$\text{Total contribution} = \sum_{\text{leaves}} \text{depth}(\text{leaf}) = \
text{external path length of the binary tree}.$$

**Optimal structure.** I claim that in an optimal tree, all leaves have depth $k
$ or $k+1$, where $k = \lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor$.

*Proof by exchange argument.* Suppose an optimal tree has leaves at depths $d_{\
min}$ and $d_{\max}$ with $d_{\max} \geq d_{\min} + 2$. Let $L, S$ be two siblin
g leaves at depth $d_{\max}$ (they must be siblings and both leaves, since if on
e sibling were internal, its children would exceed $d_{\max}$). Let $M$ be a lea
f at depth $d_{\min}$, with parent $Q$. Let $P$ (parent of $L, S$) have parent $
R$.

**Restructure:** Remove $S$ from $P$; since $P$ now has one child, remove $P$ an
d attach $L$ directly to $R$ (so $L$ moves to depth $d_{\max}-1$). Replace leaf
$M$ with an internal node having children $M$ and $S$, both at depth $d_{\min}+1
$.

The tree remains a valid binary tree (every internal node has exactly 2 children
). Depth changes:
- $L$: $d_{\max} \to d_{\max}-1$, change $-1$.
- $S$: $d_{\max} \to d_{\min}+1$, change $d_{\min}+1-d_{\max}$.
- $M$: $d_{\min} \to d_{\min}+1$, change $+1$.

Net change: $-1 + (d_{\min}+1-d_{\max}) + 1 = d_{\min}+1-d_{\max} \leq -1 < 0$.

This reduces the external path length, contradicting optimality. Hence all leave
s are at depth $k$ or $k+1$. $\square$

**Computing the minimum.** A perfect binary tree of depth $k$ has $2^k$ leaves.
To get $n$ leaves, split $n - 2^k$ of the depth-$k$ leaves into pairs at depth $
k+1$:

- Leaves at depth $k$: $2^{k+1} - n$.
- Leaves at depth $k+1$: $2(n - 2^k)$.

External path length:
$$(2^{k+1}-n)\cdot k + 2(n-2^k)(k+1) = nk + 2n - 2^{k+1} = n(k+2) - 2^{k+1}.$$

$3 \to 1+2$ produces exactly one leaf at depth 10 (the singleton from $3\to 1+2$
) and $2046$ leaves at depth 11.

$$T(2047) = 2047 \cdot (10+2) - 2^{11} = 2047 \cdot 12 - 2048 = 24564 - 2048 = \
