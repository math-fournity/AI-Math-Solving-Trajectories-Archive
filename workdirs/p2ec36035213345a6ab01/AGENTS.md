# Problem

The drone's navigation protocol is strictly defined as follows:
- For its **1st mission**, the drone is manually deployed to the Industrial Zone.
- For every subsequent mission, the drone must move to a different sector than the one it occupied during the immediately preceding mission. It chooses between the two available alternative sectors with equal probability ($1/2$ each).

What is the probability that the drone's **5th mission** will be to the Industrial Zone?
