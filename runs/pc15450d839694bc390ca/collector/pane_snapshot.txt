## Solution

We are given the one-parameter family of lines

$$F(x,y,t) = (a_1t^2 + 2a_2t + a_3)x + (b_1t^2 + 2b_2t + b_3)y + (c_1t^2 + 2c_2t
 + c_3) = 0,$$

with $t$ varying. The **envelope** is the locus of points $(x,y)$ satisfying sim
ultaneously

$$F = 0 \quad\text{and}\quad \frac{\partial F}{\partial t} = 0.$$

### Step 1. The two defining equations

Differentiating with respect to $t$ and dividing by $2$:

$$\frac{\partial F}{\partial t} = 2\big[(a_1t + a_2)x + (b_1t + b_2)y + (c_1t +
c_2)\big] = 0,$$

i.e.

$$G:\ (a_1t + a_2)x + (b_1t + b_2)y + (c_1t + c_2) = 0. \tag{2}$$

### Step 2. Reducing $F=0$ using $G=0$

Write $F = t\cdot G + H$, where

$$H = (a_2t + a_3)x + (b_2t + b_3)y + (c_2t + c_3).$$

Indeed, expanding $t\cdot G + H$ recovers $F$ term-by-term. Since on the envelop
e $G = 0$, the equation $F = 0$ reduces to

$$H:\ (a_2t + a_3)x + (b_2t + b_3)y + (c_2t + c_3) = 0. \tag{3}$$

### Step 3. Eliminating $t$

Both (2) and (3) are linear in $t$. Introduce the shorthand

$$u = a_1x + b_1y + c_1,\qquad v = a_2x + b_2y + c_2,\qquad w = a_3x + b_3y + c_
3.$$

Then (2) and (3) become

$$ut + v = 0,\qquad vt + w = 0.$$

From the first, $t = -v/u$ (when $u\neq 0$); substituting into the second gives
$-v^2/u + w = 0$, i.e.

$$v^2 = uw.$$

When $u = 0$, the first equation forces $v = 0$, and then the second forces $w =
 0$; this degenerate case ($u=v=w=0$) is already contained in $v^2 = uw$. Hence
in all cases the envelope is given by the single equation

$$\boxed{(a_2x + b_2y + c_2)^2 = (a_1x + b_1y + c_1)(a_3x + b_3y + c_3).}$$

### Step 4. Geometric identification

This is a **conic section** — the *discriminant conic* of the given pencil of li
nes. Writing it as

$$(a_1x+b_1y+c_1)(a_3x+b_3y+c_3) - (a_2x+b_2y+c_2)^2 = 0,$$

it is the locus of points where the quadratic in $t$ given by $F(x,y,t)=0$ has a
 double root — precisely the envelope condition. Its type (ellipse/parabola/hype
rbola, or degenerate pair of lines) is determined by the relative position of th
e three lines $L_i: a_ix+b_iy+c_i=0$ ($i=1,2,3$): the conic is the locus of poin
ts from which the three lines are seen under a harmonic range, equivalently $L_1
, L_2, L_3$ form a self-polar triangle with respect to it.

### Verification

For a concrete check, take $a_1=b_2=c_3=1$ and all other coefficients $0$:

$$F = t^2 x + 2ty + 1 = 0.$$

The formula gives $(y)^2 = (x)(1)$, i.e. $y^2 = x$ — a parabola. Indeed $t^2 x +
 2ty + 1 = 0$ has a double root in $t$ iff $(2y)^2 - 4x = 0$, i.e. $y^2 = x$. ✓

### Conclusion

The envelope of the family $(a_1t^2 + 2a_2t + a_3)x + (b_1t^2 + 2b_2t + b_3)y +
(c_1t^2 + 2c_2t + c_3) = 0$ as $t$ varies is the conic

$$(a_2x + b_2y + c_2)^2 = (a_1x + b_1y + c_1)(a_3x + b_3y + c_3).$$
