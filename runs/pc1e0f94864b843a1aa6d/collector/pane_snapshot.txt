## Proof

Let $K = \mathbb{F}_q$ and $L = \mathbb{F}_{q^n}$, where $n = [L:K]$. The extens
ion $L/K$ is Galois with cyclic Galois group $G = \mathrm{Gal}(L/K) = \langle \v
arphi \rangle$ of order $n$, where $\varphi(x) = x^q$ is the Frobenius automorph
ism.

**Computing the norm.** For $a \in L$,
$$N_{L/K}(a) = \prod_{i=0}^{n-1} \varphi^i(a) = \prod_{i=0}^{n-1} a^{q^i} = a^{1
 + q + q^2 + \cdots + q^{n-1}} = a^{(q^n-1)/(q-1)}.$$

Denote $m = (q^n-1)/(q-1)$ and $N = q^n - 1$.

**The case $a = 0$.** $N_{L/K}(0) = 0 \in K$.

**The case $a \in L^*$.** The multiplicative group $L^*$ is cyclic of order $N =
 q^n-1$. The norm restricted to $L^*$ is the power map
$$a \longmapsto a^m, \qquad m = \frac{N}{q-1}.$$

The image of the power map $x \mapsto x^m$ on a cyclic group of order $N$ is the
 unique subgroup of order $N/\gcd(N,m)$. Since $m = N/(q-1)$ divides $N$, we hav
e $\gcd(N,m) = m$, so the image has order
$$\frac{N}{\gcd(N,m)} = \frac{N}{m} = q - 1.$$

**The image lands in $K$.** For any $a \in L$, the norm is $G$-invariant: for ev
ery $\tau \in G$,
$$\tau(N_{L/K}(a)) = \prod_{\sigma \in G} \tau\sigma(a) = \prod_{\rho \in G} \rh
o(a) = N_{L/K}(a),$$
since left-multiplication by $\tau$ permutes $G$. Thus $N_{L/K}(a) \in L^G = K$.

**Conclusion.** The image $N_{L/K}(L^*)$ is a subgroup of $K^*$ of order $q-1$.
But $|K^*| = q-1$, so
$$N_{L/K}(L^*) = K^*.$$
Together with $N_{L/K}(0) = 0$, we obtain
$$N_{L/K}(L) = K.$$

**Yes, the image of the norm map $N_{L/K}$ on $L$ is equal to the entire field $
K$.**
