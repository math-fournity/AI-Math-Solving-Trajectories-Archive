# Problem

In a remote industrial facility, there are 11 specialized docking stations arranged in a straight line, numbered sequentially from 1 to 11. An automated delivery drone begins its shift stationed at Dock 1. 

The drone’s mission protocol requires it to travel from its starting position at Dock 1 and visit every single one of the other 10 docks exactly once, in any order, before finally returning to its home base at Dock 1. The distance between any two docks is equal to the absolute difference of their identification numbers.

The central computer seeks to program the drone’s flight path to maximize the total distance traveled during this entire circuit (visiting all docks and returning home). Let $L$ be this maximum possible total distance, and let $N$ be the total number of distinct sequences of docks (paths) that result in this maximum distance.

Compute the value of $L + N$.
