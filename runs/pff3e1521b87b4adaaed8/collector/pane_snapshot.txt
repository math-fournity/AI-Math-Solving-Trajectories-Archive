## Proof

$$\bar{z}^2 = \bar{x}^2\bar{y}, \qquad \bar{y}^2 = \bar{x}^3, \qquad \bar{z}^3 =
 \bar{x}\bar{y}. \tag{$\ast$}$$

**Strategy.** I will show that each of $\bar{x}, \bar{y}, \bar{z}$ is integral o
ver $\mathbb{C}$, so $A$ is a finite (integral) extension of $\mathbb{C}$. This
means $\dim A = 0$ and the Noether Normalization is $\mathbb{C} \hookrightarrow

$$\bar{z}^{12} = (\bar{z}^6)^2 = \bar{x}^{10}.$$

$$\bar{z}^4 = (\bar{z}^2)^2 = (\bar{x}^2\bar{y})^2 = \bar{x}^4\bar{y}^2 = \bar{x

$$\bar{z}^{12} = (\bar{z}^4)^3 = \bar{x}^{21}.$$
