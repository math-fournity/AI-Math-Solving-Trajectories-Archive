## Proof

**Step 1: The fibration is elliptic.**

Since $p: S \to C$ is a fibration (surjective with connected fibers) onto a smoo
th curve, any fiber $f$ satisfies $f^2 = 0$. By the adjunction formula:

$$2g(f) - 2 = K \cdot f + f^2 = 0 + 0 = 0$$

so $g(f) = 1$. The general fiber is a smooth curve of genus 1; this is an **elli
ptic fibration**.

**Step 2: The canonical bundle formula.**

By Kodaira's canonical bundle formula for a (relatively minimal) elliptic fibrat
ion over $\mathbb{C}$:

$$K_S \sim_{\mathbb{Q}} p^*\!\left(K_C + L + \sum_j \left(1 - \frac{1}{m_j}\righ
t) P_j\right)$$

where $L$ is a $\mathbb{Q}$-divisor on $C$, and $P_j \in C$ are the points suppo
rting **multiple fibers** of multiplicity $m_j$. The key structural feature is:
**$K_S$ is a pullback from the base, plus a vertical $\mathbb{Q}$-divisor** (sup
ported on finitely many special fibers).

If $S$ is not relatively minimal, let $\mu: S \to S_0$ be the blow-down to the r
elatively minimal model. Then $K_S = \mu^* K_{S_0} + E$ where $E$ is an effectiv
e exceptional divisor, and $H^0(S, mK_S) = H^0(S_0, mK_{S_0})$ for all $m \geq 1
$ (sections of $\mu^*(mK_{S_0}) + mE$ must vanish along $E$). So pluricanonical
systems are unchanged by blow-ups, and we may assume $S$ is relatively minimal.

**Step 3: Restriction of $K_S$ to a general fiber is trivial.**

Let $f$ be a **general fiber** (smooth, not a multiple fiber, not meeting the ve
rtical support). The vertical part of the canonical bundle formula is supported
on special fibers and does not meet $f$. The pullback part satisfies $p^*(\text{
divisor on } C)|_f = \mathcal{O}_f$ because $f$ is contracted by $p$. Therefore:

$$K_S|_f \cong \mathcal{O}_f$$

Consequently, for every $m \geq 1$:

$$mK_S|_f \cong \mathcal{O}_f, \qquad h^0(f,\, mK_S|_f) = 1.$$

**Step 4: The pluricanonical linear system is composed with the pencil.**

Consider the restriction exact sequence for a general fiber $f$:

$$0 \to \mathcal{O}_S(mK_S - f) \to \mathcal{O}_S(mK_S) \to \mathcal{O}_f(mK_S|_
f) \to 0$$

The restriction map on global sections is:

$$r: H^0(S, mK_S) \longrightarrow H^0(f, mK_S|_f) \cong \mathbb{C}$$

The image of $r$ is either $0$ or $\mathbb{C}$:

- If $r = 0$: all sections of $|mK_S|$ vanish on $f$, so $f$ lies in the base lo
cus and $\varphi_{mK}$ is **not defined** along $f$. This case is excluded by th
e hypothesis "when defined."

- If $r \neq 0$ (i.e., $\varphi_{mK}$ is defined at the general point of $f$): t
he image is $\mathbb{C}$, meaning every section restricts to a scalar multiple o
f a single nowhere-vanishing section of $\mathcal{O}_f$. The restricted linear s
ystem $\operatorname{im}(r)$ is **0-dimensional**, so $\varphi_{mK}|_f$ is const
ant. **The fiber $f$ is contracted to a point.**

**Step 5: $\varphi_{mK}$ factors through $p$ (stronger statement).**

More precisely, using the canonical bundle formula: for $m$ divisible by all mul
tiplicities $m_j$, we have an equality of Cartier divisors

$$mK_S \sim p^*\!\big(mK_C + mL\big) + \sum_j \frac{m(m_j-1)}{m_j}\, F_j$$

with the vertical part effective and integral. By the projection formula ($p_*\m
athcal{O}_S = \mathcal{O}_C$ for a fibration with connected fibers):

$$H^0(S,\, mK_S) \hookrightarrow H^0(S,\, p^*(mK_C + mL)) = H^0(C,\, mK_C + mL),
$$

where the injection corresponds to imposing vanishing along the vertical divisor
. **Every section of $mK_S$ is a pullback** of a section from $C$ (satisfying ce
rtain vanishing conditions at the $P_j$). Therefore:

$$\varphi_{mK} = \psi_m \circ p$$

for a rational map $\psi_m: C \dashrightarrow \mathbb{P}^N$. Since $p$ contracts
 each fiber to a point, so does $\varphi_{mK}$.

For $m$ not divisible by all $m_j$, the same conclusion holds by Step 4 (the res
triction argument is independent of integrality of the vertical coefficients): $
h^0(f, mK_S|_f) = 1$ forces the restricted linear system to be 0-dimensional whe
never $\varphi_{mK}$ is defined along $f$.

**Conclusion.**

For every $m \geq 1$ such that the $m$-canonical map $\varphi_{mK}$ is defined (
along $f$), the map **contracts $f$ to a point**. In fact, $\varphi_{mK}$ factor
s through the elliptic fibration $p$.
