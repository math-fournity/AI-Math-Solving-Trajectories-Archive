## Solution

We evaluate the sum
$$S = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^{2^n}}{1 - x^{2^{n+1}}}, \qquad x \in (0,1).$$

**Key identity (telescoping decomposition).** For each $n \ge 0$,
$$\frac{x^{2^n}}{1 - x^{2^{n+1}}} = \frac{1}{1 - x^{2^n}} - \frac{1}{1 - x^{2^{n
+1}}}.$$

*Proof of the identity.* Since $x^{2^{n+1}} = (x^{2^n})^2$, we have $1 - x^{2^{n
+1}} = (1 - x^{2^n})(1 + x^{2^n})$. Then
$$\frac{1}{1 - x^{2^n}} - \frac{1}{1 - x^{2^{n+1}}} = \frac{(1 - x^{2^{n+1}}) -
(1 - x^{2^n})}{(1 - x^{2^n})(1 - x^{2^{n+1}})} = \frac{x^{2^n} - x^{2^{n+1}}}{(1
 - x^{2^n})(1 - x^{2^{n+1}})}.$$
Factoring $x^{2^n} - x^{2^{n+1}} = x^{2^n}(1 - x^{2^n})$ and cancelling the comm
on factor $(1 - x^{2^n})$ gives
$$\frac{x^{2^n}}{1 - x^{2^{n+1}}},$$
as claimed.

**Telescoping.** Applying the identity term-by-term,
$$S = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \left(\frac{1}{1 - x^{2^n}} - \frac{1}{1 - x^{2^{n+1}}
}\right).$$

The partial sum through $n = N$ collapses:
$$S_N = \frac{1}{1 - x^{2^0}} - \frac{1}{1 - x^{2^{N+1}}} = \frac{1}{1 - x} - \f
rac{1}{1 - x^{2^{N+1}}}.$$

**Taking the limit.** Since $x \in (0,1)$, we have $x^{2^{N+1}} \to 0$ as $N \to
 \infty$ (exponentially fast in $N$). Therefore
$$\lim_{N \to \infty} \frac{1}{1 - x^{2^{N+1}}} = \frac{1}{1 - 0} = 1.$$

Hence
$$S = \lim_{N \to \infty} S_N = \frac{1}{1 - x} - 1 = \frac{x}{1 - x}.$$

**Numerical check.** For $x = \tfrac{1}{2}$, the formula gives $S = \frac{1/2}{1
/2} = 1$. The first three terms are $\frac{1/2}{3/4} = \frac{2}{3}$, $\frac{1/4}
{15/16} = \frac{4}{15}$, $\frac{1/16}{255/256} = \frac{16}{255}$, summing to $\f
rac{254}{255} \approx 0.9961$, consistent with convergence to $1$. $\checkmark$

$$\boxed{\displaystyle \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^{2^n}}{1 - x^{2^{n+1}}} = \fr
ac{x}{1 - x}}$$
