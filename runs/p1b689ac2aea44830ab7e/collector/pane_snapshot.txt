## The Partition Category

For a finite set $I = \{1, \dots, n\}$, define the **partition category** $\math
cal{P}(I)$:

- **Objects:** partitions $\mathbf{P} = (S_1, \dots, S_k)$ of $I$ into disjoint
non-empty subsets.
- **Morphisms:** $\mathbf{P} \to \mathbf{Q}$ when $\mathbf{Q}$ **refines** $\mat
hbf{P}$ (every part of $\mathbf{Q}$ is contained in a part of $\mathbf{P}$).

The trivial partition (one part $\{1,\dots,n\}$) is the **initial object**; the
discrete partition (all singletons) is the **terminal object**.

Let $\mathcal{P}_{\leq\, m}(I)$ be the full subcategory spanned by partitions in
 which **every part has cardinality at most $m$**.
