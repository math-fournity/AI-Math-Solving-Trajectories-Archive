# Problem

In a specialized digital signal processing laboratory, two periodic data streams, Stream $A$ and Stream $B$, are being analyzed. Each stream consists of a repeating sequence of digits. Both Stream $A$ and Stream $B$ have a minimal period length of exactly 30 digits.

The laboratory engineers develop a "Differential Stream" by calculating $A - B$ (the difference between the corresponding digits of the streams, handled as rational numbers). They observe that this resulting Differential Stream has a significantly reduced minimal period length of exactly 15 digits.

The engineers now wish to create a "Composite Stream" by calculating $A + k B$, where $k$ is a natural number. They want to find a configuration where this new Composite Stream also results in a minimal period length of exactly 15 digits.

What is the smallest natural number $k$ for which the minimal period length of the decimal representation of $A + k B$ can be exactly 15?
