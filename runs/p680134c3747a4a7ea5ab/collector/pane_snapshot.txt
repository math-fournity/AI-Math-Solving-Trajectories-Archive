## Key Substitution

Let $p = xy^2$ and $q = x^2y$. Then:
$$x^m y^n = p^{(2n-m)/3}\, q^{(2m-n)/3}$$

The constraint $1/2 \le m/n \le 2$ is equivalent to $(2n-m)/3 \ge 0$ and $(2m-n)
/3 \ge 0$ — i.e., the exponents of $p$ and $q$ are non-negative.

**Case $r=1$ (base $(1,0)$):** $m=1+a+2b,\; n=2a+b$. The ratio constraint forces

**Case $r=2$ (base $(1,1)$):** $m=1+a+2b,\; n=1+2a+b$. The boundary $m=2n$ or $n

One verifies that boundary cases $m=2n$ (resp. $n=2m$) only occur when $m+n\equi

## Evaluating the Sums

For $|p|,|q|<1$ (guaranteed when $0\le x,y<1$):

$$S = \underbrace{\left(\frac{1}{(1-p)(1-q)} - 1\right)}_{r=0} + \underbrace{\fr

$$(1-p)(1-q)\,S = 1 + xp + xy - (1-p)(1-q) = xp + xy + p + q - pq$$
