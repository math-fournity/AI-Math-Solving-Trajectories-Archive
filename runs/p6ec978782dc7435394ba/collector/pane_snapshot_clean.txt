## Solution

**Setup.** Place coordinates with A = (0, 15) (northwest), B = (36, 15) (northea
st), C = (36, 0) (southeast). The diagonal AC lies on the line through (0,15) an
d (36,0):

$$5x + 12y = 180, \qquad \text{since } \text{slope} = -\tfrac{5}{12}.$$

**Center region.** The circle of radius 1 must lie strictly inside the 36×15 rec
tangle, so the center (x, y) must satisfy 1 ≤ x ≤ 35 and 1 ≤ y ≤ 14. This center
 region has area

$$34 \times 13 = 442.$$

**Exclusion strip.** The circle must not touch the diagonal, so the distance fro
m the center to the line 5x + 12y − 180 = 0 must exceed 1:

$$\frac{|5x + 12y - 180|}{13} > 1 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad |5x + 12y - 180|
 > 13.$$

The forbidden strip (where the circle touches/overlaps the diagonal) is bounded
by the parallel lines:

$$L_1:\; 5x + 12y = 167, \qquad L_2:\; 5x + 12y = 193.$$

**Strip intersection with the center rectangle [1,35]×[1,14].**

*L1 (5x+12y=167)* meets the left edge at (1, 13.5) and the bottom edge at (31, 1
).

*L2 (5x+12y=193)* meets the top edge at (5, 14) and the right edge at (35, 1.5).

Two rectangle corners lie inside the strip: (1,14) with value 173, and (35,1) wi
th value 187.

The strip polygon (in order) is: (1, 13.5), (1, 14), (5, 14), (35, 1.5), (35, 1)
, (31, 1).

**Verification via triangle subtraction.** The area below L2 (5x+12y ≤ 193) equa
ls the full rectangle minus the triangle above L2 with vertices (5,14), (35,14),
 (35,1.5):

$$442 - \tfrac{1}{2}(30)(12.5) = 442 - 187.5 = 254.5.$$

The area below L1 (5x+12y < 167) is the triangle with vertices (1,1), (31,1), (1
,13.5):

$$\tfrac{1}{2}(30)(12.5) = 187.5.$$

**Strip area** = 254.5 − 187.5 = **67**.

(Cross-checked with the shoelace formula on the hexagon: area = 67. ✓)

**Favorable area** = 442 − 67 = **375**.

**Probability** = 375/442.

**Coprimality check:** 375 = 3·5³ and 442 = 2·13·17 share no common factor, so g
cd = 1.

$$m + n = 375 + 442 = \boxed{817}.$$
