## Proof

By the Fundamental Theorem of Galois Theory, there is an order-reversing bijecti
on between subfields $L$ with $\mathbb{Q} \subseteq L \subseteq K$ and subgroups
 $H \leq \mathrm{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q})$. Under this correspondence, $H = \mathrm{Ga
l}(K/L)$ and

$$:L] = |H|.$$

We are given $\mathrm{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q}) \cong C_{16}$, the cyclic group of orde
r 16. We must count subfields $L$ with $:L] = 4$, i.e. subgroups $H \leq C_{16
}$ with $|H| = 4$.

A cyclic group of order $n$ has exactly one subgroup of order $d$ for each divis
or $d$ of $n$. Since $4 \mid 16$, there is exactly one subgroup of order 4 in $C
_{16}$ (namely $\langle g^4 \rangle$ where $g$ is a generator).

Therefore, by the Galois correspondence, there is exactly **1** subfield $L$ of
$K$ with $:L] = 4$.
