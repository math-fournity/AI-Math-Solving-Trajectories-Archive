# Problem

In the exotic botanical garden of Aethelgard, a row of 10 mystical plants is arranged in a line according to the species code: **P A M P E L I Š K A**.

A gardener must assign a specific energy potency to each plant. This potency must be a single-digit integer from 1 to 9. The rules for the garden are as follows:
1. **Consistency:** Plants of the same species must be assigned the exact same potency digit.
2. **Diversity:** Plants of different species must be assigned different potency digits.
3. **Synergy:** When the potencies of all 10 plants in the row are multiplied together, the resulting product must be a perfect square of a natural number.

The gardener wants to maximize the overall "Sequence Value" of the garden, which is the 10-digit number formed by reading the potency digits of the plants from left to right.

What is the largest possible 10-digit number that can be formed under these conditions?
