**Solution.**

Place vectors in the plane with $\boldsymbol a$ and $\boldsymbol b$ symmetric ab
out the $x$-axis. Since the angle between them is $\pi/3$ and $|\boldsymbol a-\b
oldsymbol b|=5$:

$$\boldsymbol a=\Big(\tfrac{5\sqrt3}{2},\,\tfrac52\Big),\qquad \boldsymbol b=\Bi
g(\tfrac{5\sqrt3}{2},\,-\tfrac52\Big),\qquad |\boldsymbol a|=|\boldsymbol b|=5.$
$

Let $\boldsymbol c=(x,y)$ and set $X=x-\tfrac{5\sqrt3}{2}$. Define
$$\boldsymbol u=\boldsymbol c-\boldsymbol a=(X,\,y-\tfrac52),\qquad \boldsymbol
v=\boldsymbol c-\boldsymbol b=(X,\,y+\tfrac52).$$

**Step 1: Find $|\boldsymbol v|$.** From $|\boldsymbol v-\boldsymbol u|=|\boldsy
mbol a-\boldsymbol b|=5$ and the angle $\angle(\boldsymbol u,\boldsymbol v)=\tfr
ac{2\pi}{3}$:

$$25=|\boldsymbol u|^2+|\boldsymbol v|^2-2|\boldsymbol u||\boldsymbol v|\cos\tfr
ac{2\pi}{3}=12+|\boldsymbol v|^2+2\sqrt3\,|\boldsymbol v|.$$

So $|\boldsymbol v|^2+2\sqrt3\,|\boldsymbol v|-13=0$, giving $|\boldsymbol v|=4-
\sqrt3$.

**Step 2: Determine $y$ and $X^2$.** From $|\boldsymbol u|^2=12$ and $|\boldsymb
ol v|^2=(4-\sqrt3)^2=19-8\sqrt3$:

$$X^2+\Big(y-\tfrac52\Big)^2=12,\qquad X^2+\Big(y+\tfrac52\Big)^2=19-8\sqrt3.$$

Subtracting: $10y=(19-8\sqrt3)-12-25/4+25/4\cdot$... directly, subtracting the f
irst from the second:

$$10y = (19-8\sqrt3)-12 = 7-8\sqrt3 \implies y=\frac{7-8\sqrt3}{10}.$$

Adding and using $X^2+y^2=\frac{37-16\sqrt3}{4}$, we get

$$X^2=\frac{171-72\sqrt3}{25}.$$

**Step 3: Maximize $\boldsymbol a\cdot\boldsymbol c$.** We have

$$\boldsymbol a\cdot\boldsymbol c=\frac{5\sqrt3}{2}\,x+\frac52\,y=\frac{5\sqrt3}
{2}\Big(X+\frac{5\sqrt3}{2}\Big)+\frac52\,y=\frac{5\sqrt3}{2}\,X+\frac{75}{4}+\f
rac{5}{2}\,y.$$

To maximize, take $X=+\sqrt{(171-72\sqrt3)/25}=\tfrac{1}{5}\sqrt{171-72\sqrt3}$,
 so

$$\frac{5\sqrt3}{2}\,X=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\sqrt{171-72\sqrt3}=\frac12\sqrt{513-216\
sqrt3}.$$

Since $513-216\sqrt3=(12\sqrt3-9)^2$ (checking: $432+81=513$, $2\cdot12\cdot9=21
6$), we get $\sqrt{513-216\sqrt3}=12\sqrt3-9$.

Also $\frac52\,y=\frac{7-8\sqrt3}{4}$, hence

$$\boldsymbol a\cdot\boldsymbol c=\frac{12\sqrt3-9}{2}+\frac{75}{4}+\frac{7-8\sq
rt3}{4}=\frac{24\sqrt3-18+75+7-8\sqrt3}{4}=\frac{64+16\sqrt3}{4}=16+4\sqrt3.$$

The maximum value of $\boldsymbol a\cdot\boldsymbol c$ is $\boxed{16+4\sqrt3}$.
