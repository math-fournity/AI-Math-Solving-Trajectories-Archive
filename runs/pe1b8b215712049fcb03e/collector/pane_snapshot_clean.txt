## Proof

Let $A$ be a Noetherian ring (the local hypothesis and the maximal ideal $M$ are
 not needed), $I\subseteq A$ an ideal, and $x\in A$ an element not belonging to
any associated prime of $I$. We show $[I:x]=I$.

Recall the colon ideal
$$[I:x]=\{a\in A: ax\in I\}.$$

**Key fact (standard).** For a Noetherian ring $A$ and ideal $I$, the set of zer
o-divisors on the module $A/I$ is exactly the union of the associated primes of
$I$:
$$\bigcup_{\mathfrak p\in \operatorname{Ass}_A(A/I)}\mathfrak p \;=\; \{\text{ze
ro-divisors on }A/I\}.$$

*Proof of the key fact.* Every associated prime $\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Ass
}_A(A/I)$ is the annihilator of some $\bar a\in A/I$, $\mathfrak p=(0:\bar a)$.
If $x\in\mathfrak p$, then $x\bar a=0$ with $\bar a\neq 0$, so $x$ is a zero-div
isor. Conversely, if $x$ is a zero-divisor on $A/I$, then $x\bar a=0$ for some $
\bar a\neq 0$, i.e. $x\in(0:\bar a)$. Since $A$ is Noetherian, every annihilator
 $(0:\bar a)$ is contained in an associated prime (the maximal annihilators are
associated primes), so $x$ lies in some associated prime. $\square$

**Applying it.** Consider the $A$-linear map
$$\mu_x:A/I\longrightarrow A/I,\qquad \bar a\mapsto x\bar a.$$
Its kernel is
$$\ker\mu_x=\{\bar a\in A/I: x\bar a=0\}=\{\bar a\in A/I: a\in[I:x]\}=[I:x]/I.$$

By hypothesis $x\notin\bigcup_{\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Ass}_A(A/I)}\mathfrak
 p$, so by the key fact $x$ is **not** a zero-divisor on $A/I$. Hence $\mu_x$ is
 injective, i.e.
$$\ker\mu_x=0.$$
Therefore
$$[I:x]/I=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad [I:x]=I.$$

**Remark.** The local hypothesis and the unique maximal ideal $M$ play no role;
the statement holds for any Noetherian ring $A$. The essential content is purely
: *a non-zero-divisor on $A/I$ acts injectively*, which is the definition of bei
ng a non-zero-divisor, combined with the Noetherian characterization of zero-div
isors via associated primes.

Hence the statement is **true**.
