## Phase A: Rectangular Storage Units

Floor dimensions $c, d \in \mathbb{Z}^+$. The conditions are:

$$\text{Area} = cd, \qquad \text{Perimeter} = 2(c+d)$$

$$cd = 2 \cdot 2(c+d) = 4(c+d)$$

Rearranging: $cd - 4c - 4d = 0$, so $(c-4)(d-4) = 16$.

Since $c, d$ are positive integers, $c-4$ and $d-4$ must be positive (negative f
actors yield a negative dimension). The unordered factor pairs of 16 are:

| $(c-4, d-4)$ | $(c, d)$ | $c+d$ |
|---|---|---|
| $(1, 16)$ | $(5, 20)$ | $25$ |
| $(2, 8)$ | $(6, 12)$ | $18$ |
| $(4, 4)$ | $(8, 8)$ | $16$ |

$$S_a = 25 + 18 + 16 = 59$$

## Phase B: Cuboid Living Pods

Dimensions $e, e, f$ with $e, f \in \mathbb{Z}^+$.

$$\text{Volume} = e^2 f, \qquad \text{Surface area} = 2e^2 + 4ef$$

$$e^2 f = 2(2e^2 + 4ef) = 4e^2 + 8ef$$

$$ef(e - 8) = 4e^2 \implies f(e-8) = 4e \quad (e > 0)$$

$$f = \frac{4e}{e-8}$$

For $f > 0$ we need $e > 8$. Set $k = e - 8 > 0$, so $e = k+8$ and:

$$f = \frac{4(k+8)}{k} = 4 + \frac{32}{k}$$

$f$ is a positive integer iff $k \mid 32$. The divisors of 32 are $k \in \{1, 2,
 4, 8, 16, 32\}$:

| $k$ | $e$ | $f$ | $2e+f$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $1$ | $9$ | $36$ | $54$ |
| $2$ | $10$ | $20$ | $40$ |
| $4$ | $12$ | $12$ | $36$ |
| $8$ | $16$ | $8$ | $40$ |
| $16$ | $24$ | $6$ | $54$ |
| $32$ | $40$ | $5$ | $85$ |

All six designs are distinct (each has a unique pair $(e, f)$). The $k=4$ case g
ives the cube $(12,12,12)$, which satisfies "two dimensions equal."

$$S_b = 54 + 40 + 36 + 40 + 54 + 85 = 309$$

## Final Answer

$$S_a + S_b = 59 + 309 = \boxed{368}$$
