# Problem

In a vast desert landscape, a circular oasis with a radius of 1 kilometer is centered at a base camp designated as the origin $(0,0)$. A straight, infinitely long irrigation pipeline is laid through the first quadrant, touching the edge of the oasis at exactly one point. To ensure optimal solar exposure, the pipeline is oriented such that it forms an angle of $\frac{5\pi}{6}$ radians with the positive $y$-axis (the North path).

A team of engineers plans to install a series of circular water reservoirs along the East path (the positive $x$-axis). The construction follows a strict geometric progression:
1. Every reservoir's center must lie precisely on the $x$-axis.
2. The first reservoir in the sequence is positioned so that its edge is tangent to the irrigation pipeline.
3. Each subsequent reservoir in the infinite series must be tangent to the previous reservoir and also tangent to the irrigation pipeline.

Calculate the total surface area covered by this infinite series of circular reservoirs.
