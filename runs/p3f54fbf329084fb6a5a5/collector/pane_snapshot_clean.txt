## Solution

The triangle with side lengths $3, 4, 5$ is a right triangle ($3^2+4^2=5^2$), wi
th
- $\text{Area}(X_0) = \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot 3\cdot 4 = 6$,
- $\text{Perimeter} = 3+4+5 = 12$.

**Key observation.** For a convex set $K$, the operation $K \mapsto \{p : \text{
dist}(p,K)\le 1\}$ is exactly the Minkowski sum $K + D$, where $D$ is the closed
 unit disk. This "parallel body" of a convex set is again convex, and Minkowski
sums of convex sets are associative, so iterating gives
$$X_n = \overline{X_0} + nD \quad (n\ge 0),$$
where $\overline{X_0}$ is the closed triangle. (Whether $X_0$ is taken open or c
losed is irrelevant for area, since boundaries have measure zero.)

**Steiner's formula.** For any convex body $K$ in the plane, the area of its par
allel body of radius $r$ is
$$\text{Area}(K_r) = \text{Area}(K) + r\,\text{Perimeter}(K) + \pi r^2.$$
For a polygon this is transparent: the parallel body consists of the original re
gion, rectangles of width $r$ along each side (total area $rP$), and circular se
ctors at the vertices whose angles are the exterior angles — which sum to $2\pi$
, giving a full disk of area $\pi r^2$.

Applying this to our triangle:
$$\text{Area}(X_n) = 6 + 12n + \pi n^2.$$

**Compute the desired region.** The region outside $X_{20}$ but inside $X_{21}$
has area
$$\text{Area}(X_{21}) - \text{Area}(X_{20}) = \bigl(6 + 12\cdot 21 + \pi\cdot 21
^2\bigr) - \bigl(6 + 12\cdot 20 + \pi\cdot 20^2\bigr).$$
$$= 12(21-20) + \pi(441 - 400) = 12 + 41\pi.$$

Thus $a\pi + b = 41\pi + 12$, so $a = 41$ and $b = 12$, giving
$$100a + b = 100\cdot 41 + 12 = \boxed{4112}.$$
