- **Ackermann function** (standard): $A(0,n)=n+1$, $A(m,0)=A(m-1,1)$, $A(m,n)=A(
m-1,A(m,n-1))$.

function to the hyperoperation/up-arrow hierarchy. For $m \ge 4$:

$$A(m, n) = 2 \uparrow^{m-2} (n+3) - 3.$$

This is verified inductively: $A(3,n) = 2^{n+3}-3 = 2\uparrow^1(n+3)-3$; $A(4,n)

 increment of $m$ adds one up-arrow level. Thus **the number of up-arrows in $A(
m,n)$ is (essentially) $m-2$**, determined by the *first* argument, while the *s
econd* argument only controls the operand (the "height" at the lowest level).

$$A(G_{64}, G_{64}) \approx 2 \uparrow^{G_{64}-2}(\text{something near } G_{64})
