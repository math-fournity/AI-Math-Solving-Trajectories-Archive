## Solution

**Setup.** Place $A=(0,0)$ and $C=(5,0)$ so that $AC$ lies along the $x$-axis. L
et $\angle BAC = 3\theta$, with $AE$ at angle $\theta$ and $AD$ at angle $2\thet
a$ from $AC$ (since $D$ is closer to $B$). Let $c=AB$ and $L=BC$.

**Trisector ratios via the sine rule.** For cevian $AE$ (making angle $2\theta$
with $AB$, $\theta$ with $AC$):
$$\frac{BE}{EC}=\frac{c\sin 2\theta}{5\sin\theta}=\frac{2c\cos\theta}{5}.$$
For cevian $AD$ (making angle $\theta$ with $AB$, $2\theta$ with $AC$):
$$\frac{BD}{DC}=\frac{c\sin\theta}{5\sin 2\theta}=\frac{c}{10\cos\theta}.$$

**Using the given lengths.** $EC=5/2$, $DE=3/2$, so $DC=4$ and $BD=L-4$, $BE=L-5
/2$.

From the two ratios with $t=\cos\theta$:
$$\frac{L-4}{4}=\frac{c}{10t},\qquad \frac{L-5/2}{5/2}=\frac{2ct}{5}.$$
Solving: $L=4\!\left(\frac{c}{10t}+1\right)$ and $L=\frac{5}{2}\!\left(\frac{2ct
}{5}+1\right)$. Equating:
$$8\cdot\frac{c}{10t}-5\cdot\frac{2ct}{5}=-3 \implies c=\frac{15t}{2(5t^2-2)},\q
quad L=\frac{5(4t^2-1)}{5t^2-2}.$$

**Law of cosines** $L^2=25+c^2-10c\cos 3\theta$ with $\cos 3\theta=4t^3-3t$ yiel
ds (after clearing denominators):
$$80t^6-104t^4+37t^2-4=0.$$
With $s=t^2$: $80s^3-104s^2+37s-4=(s-\tfrac14)^2(80s-16)=0$, giving $s=1/4$ (deg
enerate, $\theta=60°\Rightarrow 3\theta=180°$) or $s=4/5$.

So $t=\cos\theta=2/\sqrt5$, giving $c=3\sqrt5/2$ and $L=11/2$.

**Coordinates and projections.** Since $AC$ is the $x$-axis, projections are jus
t $x$-coordinates.

$\cos 3\theta=4t^3-3t=\frac{2\sqrt5}{25}$, so $B=(c\cos 3\theta,\, c\sin 3\theta
)=\bigl(\tfrac35,\, \tfrac{33}{10}\bigr)$.

Point $E$ on $BC$ with $EC=5/2$: $E=C+\frac{5/2}{11/2}(B-C)=C+\frac{5}{11}(B-C)=
(3,\,3/2)$.

Therefore:
$$F=\bigl(\tfrac35,0\bigr):\quad AF=\tfrac35,\quad CF=\tfrac{22}{5}.$$
$$G=(3,0):\quad AG=3,\quad CG=2.$$

**Final computation:**
$$\frac{CF}{CG}-\frac{AF}{AG}=\frac{22/5}{2}-\frac{3/5}{3}=\frac{11}{5}-\frac{1}
{5}=\boxed{2}.$$
