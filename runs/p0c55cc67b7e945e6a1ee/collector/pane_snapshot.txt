## Solution

The Eisenstein series of weight $2k$ for a lattice $\Lambda$ is defined as

$$G_{2k}(\Lambda) = \sum_{\omega \in \Lambda \setminus \{0\}} \omega^{-2k}.$$

The problem asks for $G_2(\Lambda)$ with $k=1$, i.e., the weight-2 series

$$G_2(\Lambda) = \sum_{\omega \in \Lambda \setminus \{0\}} \omega^{-2}.$$

**The lattice.** Here $\Lambda = \mathbb{Z}[i] = \{m + ni : m, n \in \mathbb{Z}\
}$, the Gaussian integers. This is the square lattice, and it is invariant under
 multiplication by $i$: the map

$$\varphi: \Lambda \to \Lambda, \qquad \omega \mapsto i\omega$$

is a bijection of $\Lambda$, and it restricts to a bijection of $\Lambda \setmin
us \{0\}$ onto itself (since $i\omega = 0 \iff \omega = 0$).

**Symmetry argument.** Using the reindexing $\omega \mapsto i\omega$ (a bijectio
n of $\Lambda\setminus\{0\}$),

$$G_2(\Lambda) = \sum_{\omega \in \Lambda\setminus\{0\}} \omega^{-2} = \sum_{\om
ega \in \Lambda\setminus\{0\}} (i\omega)^{-2}.$$

Since $(i\omega)^{-2} = i^{-2}\,\omega^{-2} = (-1)\,\omega^{-2} = -\omega^{-2}$,
 we obtain

$$G_2(\Lambda) = \sum_{\omega \in \Lambda\setminus\{0\}} (-\omega^{-2}) = -\sum_
{\omega \in \Lambda\setminus\{0\}} \omega^{-2} = -G_2(\Lambda).$$

Therefore $G_2(\Lambda) = -G_2(\Lambda)$, which forces

$$G_2(\Lambda) = 0.$$

**Convergence note.** The series $\sum \omega^{-2}$ is only conditionally conver
gent in two dimensions (it is not absolutely convergent, since $\sum |\omega|^{-
2}$ diverges). However, the Eisenstein summation convention sums symmetrically (
over expanding squares/circles centered at the origin), under which the reindexi
ng $\omega \mapsto i\omega$ preserves the summation region (a square or disk is
mapped to itself by $90°$ rotation). Hence the symmetry manipulation is valid un
der the standard Eisenstein summation, and the cancellation is exact.

**Answer.** As a complex number,

$$\boxed{G_2(\mathbb{Z}[i]) = 0 + 0i = 0.}$$
