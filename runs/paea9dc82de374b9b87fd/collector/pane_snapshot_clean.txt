$$0 \longrightarrow N \overset{i}{\longrightarrow} M \overset{\pi}{\longrightarr

where $i$ is the inclusion and $\pi$ is the canonical quotient map.

**Step 1: The sequence splits.**

Since $M/N$ is a free $R$-module, it is projective. A standard result in homolog

th $C$ projective splits.

Concretely: let $\{e_j\}_{j \in J}$ be a basis of the free module $M/N$. For eac

**Step 2: $M \cong N \oplus M/N$.**

By the splitting lemma, the existence of a section $s$ gives an isomorphism:
$$M \cong N \oplus M/N,$$
where the isomorphism sends $m \mapsto (m - s(\pi(m)),\, \pi(m))$, with inverse
$(n, \bar{m}) \mapsto n + s(\bar{m})$.

Under the isomorphism $M \cong N \oplus M/N$, the projection onto the first fact

$$p: M \longrightarrow N, \qquad p(n, \bar{m}) = n$$
