## Solution

**Setup.** Let $O_1, O_2, O_3$ be the centers of $\Gamma_1, \Gamma_2, \Gamma_3$
with radii $1, 2, 3$. Since the circles are pairwise externally tangent:

$$O_1O_2 = 1+2 = 3, \quad O_1O_3 = 1+3 = 4, \quad O_2O_3 = 2+3 = 5.$$

Since $3^2 + 4^2 = 5^2$, triangle $O_1O_2O_3$ is right-angled at $O_1$.

**Coordinates.** Place $O_1$ at the origin, $A = O_2 = (3,0)$, $B = O_3 = (0,4)$
.

**The new circle.** Let its center be $C = (h,k)$ and radius $r$. Set $s = r+1$,
 so the external tangency with $\Gamma_1$ (center $O_1$, radius $1$) gives:
$$h^2 + k^2 = s^2. \tag{1}$$

Since $A$ and $B$ lie on the new circle, $(h-3)^2+k^2 = r^2 = (s-1)^2$ and $h^2+
(k-4)^2 = (s-1)^2$.

**From $A$ on the circle:** using $(1)$,
$$s^2 - 6h + 9 = s^2 - 2s + 1 \implies 6h = 2s + 8. \tag{2}$$

**From $A$ and $B$ both on the circle** (subtracting the two equations):
$$(h-3)^2 - h^2 + k^2 - (k-4)^2 = 0 \implies -6h+9+8k-16=0 \implies 6h - 8k = -7
. \tag{3}$$

Substituting $(2)$ into $(3)$: $2s+8 - 8k = -7$, so
$$8k = 2s + 15. \tag{4}$$

**The tangency point $P$.** Since the new circle is externally tangent to $\Gamm
a_1$, the point $P$ lies on segment $O_1C$ at distance $1$ from $O_1$:
$$P = \frac{(h,k)}{s} = \left(\frac{h}{s},\frac{k}{s}\right).$$

**Computing $PA^2$ and $PB^2$:**
$$PA^2 = \left(\frac{h}{s}-3\right)^2 + \left(\frac{k}{s}\right)^2 = \frac{h^2+k
^2}{s^2} - \frac{6h}{s} + 9 = 1 - \frac{6h}{s} + 9 = \frac{10s - 6h}{s}.$$

$$PB^2 = \left(\frac{h}{s}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{k}{s}-4\right)^2 = 1 - \frac{8
k}{s} + 16 = \frac{17s - 8k}{s}.$$

**Substituting $(2)$ and $(4)$:**
$$10s - 6h = 10s - (2s+8) = 8(s-1),$$
$$17s - 8k = 17s - (2s+15) = 15(s-1).$$

Therefore:
$$\frac{PA^2}{PB^2} = \frac{8(s-1)}{15(s-1)} = \frac{8}{15}.$$

(The factor $(s-1) = r \neq 0$ cancels safely.)

**Verification that a valid circle exists.** From $(2)$ and $(4)$: $h = (s+4)/3$
, $k = (2s+15)/8$. Substituting into $(1)$:
$$\frac{(s+4)^2}{9} + \frac{(2s+15)^2}{64} = s^2 \implies 476s^2 - 1052s - 3049
= 0.$$

The positive root is $s = \frac{263 + 120\sqrt{30}}{238} \approx 3.87$, giving $
r \approx 2.87 > 0$. A valid circle exists. $\checkmark$

**Final answer.** The fraction $\frac{8}{15}$ is irreducible ($\gcd(8,15)=1$), s
o $a = 8$, $b = 15$:

$$a + b = \boxed{23}.$$
