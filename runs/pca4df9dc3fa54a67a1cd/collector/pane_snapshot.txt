$$\int_0^{\pi/2}\cos^n v\,dv \sim \int_0^\infty e^{-nv^2/2}\,dv = \sqrt{\frac{\p

$$I_n \sim 2\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{2n}} = \sqrt{\frac{2\pi}{n}}. \tag{1}$$
