$$S^1 = \{(x,y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 : x^2 + y^2 = 1\},$$

We prove that $\pi_1(S^1, x_0) \cong \mathbb{Z}$ and $\pi_n(S^1, x_0) = 0$ for a

### Part 1: $\pi_1(S^1) \cong \mathbb{Z}$

Consider the exponential (covering) map $p : \mathbb{R} \to S^1$ defined by
$$p(t) = (\cos 2\pi t,\; \sin 2\pi t).$$

This is a covering map with $\mathbb{R}$ simply connected (hence the universal c
over of $S^1$). The fiber over $x_0 = (1,0)$ is $p^{-1}(x_0) = \mathbb{Z} \subse
t \mathbb{R}$.

**Lifting criterion.** Since $\mathbb{R}$ is simply connected, $p$ is the univer
sal cover. The group of deck transformations is $\{t \mapsto t + n : n \in \math
bb{Z}\} \cong \mathbb{Z}$, and by covering space theory the fundamental group of
 the base is isomorphic to the group of deck transformations:
$$\pi_1(S^1, x_0) \cong \operatorname{Deck}(p) \cong \mathbb{Z}.$$

**Explicit isomorphism (via the degree/loop-counting map).** For each $n \in \ma
thbb{Z}$, define the loop
$$\gamma_n : [0,1] \to S^1, \quad \gamma_n(s) = (\cos 2\pi n s,\; \sin 2\pi n s)
.$$
The unique lift of $\gamma_n$ to $\mathbb{R}$ starting at $0$ is $\widetilde{\ga
mma}_n(s) = ns$, which ends at $n \in \mathbb{Z}$. The endpoint of the lift depe
nds only on the homotopy class of the loop (homotopy lifting property), so the m
ap
$$\Phi : \pi_1(S^1, x_0) \to \mathbb{Z}, \quad \Phi([\gamma]) = \widetilde{\gamm
a}(1)$$
is well defined. It is a homomorphism because lifts of concatenated loops concat

{\gamma}$ is a loop in the simply connected space $\mathbb{R}$, hence null-homot

Therefore $\pi_1(S^1, x_0) \cong \mathbb{Z}$.
