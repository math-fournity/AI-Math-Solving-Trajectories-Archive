## Solution

**Problem.** Construct the Green's function for the Dirichlet problem in the sla
b $D = \{(x,y,z) : -\tfrac{1}{2} < z < \tfrac{1}{2}\}$ using the method of image
s.

---

### Setup

We seek $G(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{x}')$ satisfying:
$$\Delta G = -\delta(\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{x}') \quad \text{in } D, \qquad G\big|
_{z=\pm 1/2} = 0,$$
with $G \to 0$ as $\rho \to \infty$, where $\rho = \sqrt{(x-x')^2 + (y-y')^2}$.

The free-space Green's function in 3D is $G_0 = \frac{1}{4\pi|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf
{x}'|}$.

### Coordinate shift

Set $w = z + \frac{1}{2}$, $w' = z' + \frac{1}{2}$, so the slab becomes $0 < w <
 1$ (height $a=1$), with boundary planes $w = 0$ and $w = 1$.

### Image construction

The source is at $(x', y', w')$ with $0 < w' < 1$. The two reflecting planes $w
= 0$ and $w = 1$ generate an infinite lattice of images via the group generated
by:
- Reflection across $w=0$: $R_0(w) = -w$,
- Reflection across $w=1$: $R_1(w) = 2 - w$.

The composition $R_1 \circ R_0(w) = 2 + w$ is a translation by $2$. The full orb
it of the source $w'$ under this group splits into two classes:

**Even reflections (positive charges, sign $+1$):** located at $w' + 2n$ for all
 $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.
- $n=0$: the original source $w'$.
- $n=\pm 1, \pm 2, \ldots$: double-reflection images at $w' \pm 2, w' \pm 4, \ld
ots$

**Odd reflections (negative charges, sign $-1$):** located at $-w' + 2n$ for all
 $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.
- $n=0$: reflection of $w'$ across $w=0$, at $-w'$.
- $n=1$: reflection of $w'$ across $w=1$, at $2 - w'$.
- Further images at $-w' \pm 2, -w' \pm 4, \ldots$

All image charges lie **outside** the slab: for even images with $|n| \geq 1$, $
|w'+2n| \geq 2 - 1 = 1$; for odd images, $-w' + 2n \leq -w' < 0$ when $n \leq 0$
, and $-w' + 2n \geq 2 - w' > 1$ when $n \geq 1$.

### The Green's function

$$G = \frac{1}{4\pi} \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} \left[ \frac{1}{\sqrt{\rho^2 + (w
 - w' - 2n)^2}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{\rho^2 + (w + w' - 2n)^2}} \right].$$

Returning to the original coordinates ($w = z + \frac{1}{2}$, $w' = z' + \frac{1
}{2}$):

$$\boxed{G(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{x}') = \frac{1}{4\pi} \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} \
left[ \frac{1}{\sqrt{\rho^2 + (z - z' - 2n)^2}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{\rho^2 + (z + z
' + 1 - 2n)^2}} \right]}$$

where $\rho^2 = (x - x')^2 + (y - y')^2$.

### Verification of boundary conditions

**At $z = \frac{1}{2}$ (i.e., $w = 1$):**

First sum gives $(1 - w' - 2n)^2$; second sum gives $(1 + w' - 2n)^2$. Reindex t
he second sum by $n \to 1 - n$:
$$(1 + w' - 2(1-n))^2 = (w' - 1 + 2n)^2 = (1 - w' - 2n)^2,$$
which equals the first sum term-by-term. Hence $G\big|_{z=1/2} = 0$. ✓

**At $z = -\frac{1}{2}$ (i.e., $w = 0$):**

First sum gives $(w' + 2n)^2$; second sum gives $(w' - 2n)^2$. Reindex the secon
d by $n \to -n$: $(w' + 2n)^2$, identical to the first. Hence $G\big|_{z=-1/2} =
 0$. ✓

### Verification of the differential equation

Inside $D$, the only singularity of $G$ is at $z = z'$, $x = x'$, $y = y'$ (the
$n=0$ term of the first sum), which produces exactly $\frac{1}{4\pi|\mathbf{x}-\
mathbf{x}'|}$, satisfying $\Delta G = -\delta(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}')$. All othe
r image charges lie outside $D$, so they are harmonic inside $D$ and contribute
no singularities.

### Convergence

For large $|n|$, each bracketed term behaves as:
$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{\rho^2 + (z - z' - 2n)^2}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{\rho^2 + (z + z' +
1 - 2n)^2}} \sim \frac{1 - 2z'}{4n^2} + O(n^{-3}),$$
so the series converges absolutely (like $\sum 1/n^2$), and uniformly on compact
 subsets of $D \setminus \{\mathbf{x}'\}$.

### Summary

The Green's function for the Dirichlet problem in the slab $-\frac{1}{2} < z < \
frac{1}{2}$ is an infinite series of image charges: positive unit charges at $z
= z' + 2n$ and negative unit charges at $z = -z' - 1 + 2n$ (equivalently $z = -z
' + 1 + 2k$) for all $n \in \mathbb{Z}$, all projected onto the same $(x', y')$
lateral position. The alternating infinite lattice ensures both boundary planes
are equipotential (zero), and the only singularity inside $D$ is the original so
urce.
