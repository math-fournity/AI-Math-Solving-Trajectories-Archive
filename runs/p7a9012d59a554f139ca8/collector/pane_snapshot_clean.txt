## Composition Factors of a Non-Solvable Group of Order 780

oof sketch. Proceeding.

**Claim:** The composition factors of $G$ are $\mathbb{Z}_{13}$ and $A_5$.

**Proof.**

Let $|G| = 780 = 2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 13$. Let $n_{13}$ be the number of Sy
low 13-subgroups. By the Sylow theorems:
$$n_{13} \mid \frac{780}{13} = 60 \quad\text{and}\quad n_{13} \equiv 1 \pmod{13}
.$$

The divisors of $60$ are $1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 10, 12, 15, 20, 30, 60$. Reducing ea
ch modulo $13$:
$$1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 10, 12, 2, 7, 4, 8.$$

Only $1 \equiv 1 \pmod{13}$. Hence $n_{13} = 1$, so the Sylow 13-subgroup $P_{13
}$ is **normal** in $G$, with $|P_{13}| = 13 \cong \mathbb{Z}_{13}$ (cyclic of p
rime order, hence simple and solvable).

Consider the quotient $G/P_{13}$, which has order $780/13 = 60$.

**$G/P_{13}$ is not solvable.** Suppose for contradiction that $G/P_{13}$ were s
olvable. Since $P_{13}$ is solvable (cyclic of prime order), $G$ would be an ext
ension of a solvable group by a solvable group, hence solvable — contradicting t
he hypothesis. Therefore $G/P_{13}$ is not solvable.

**$G/P_{13} \cong A_5$.** Let $H = G/P_{13}$, a non-solvable group of order $60$
. The composition factors of $H$ are simple groups whose orders multiply to $60$
. Since $H$ is not solvable, at least one composition factor is non-abelian simp
le. By hypothesis, the only non-abelian simple group of order $\leq 60$ is $A_5$
, of order $60 = 2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5$. Since $|A_5| = 60 = |H|$, the non-abelian
 simple factor $A_5$ must account for the entire order of $H$; there is no room
for any additional composition factor. Hence $H$ is itself simple and $H \cong A
_5$.

**Composition series.** We have the normal chain
$$1 \;\lhd\; P_{13} \;\lhd\; G,$$
with successive quotients:
- $P_{13}/1 \cong \mathbb{Z}_{13}$, simple (cyclic of prime order),
- $G/P_{13} \cong A_5$, simple (non-abelian simple).

Both quotients are simple, so this is a composition series. By the Jordan–Hölder
 theorem, the composition factors are uniquely determined up to isomorphism and
ordering.

**Conclusion.** The composition factors of $G$ are
$$\boxed{\mathbb{Z}_{13} \;\text{and}\; A_5.}$$
