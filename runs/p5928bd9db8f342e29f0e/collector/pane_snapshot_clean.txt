## Solution

We seek the smallest positive integer $N$ such that $2019 \mid N$ and $N \equiv
2018 \pmod{10000}$, i.e. $N$ ends in the digits `2018`.

### Setting up the congruence

Write $N = 2019k$. We need
$$2019k \equiv 2018 \pmod{10000}.$$

Since $\gcd(2019, 10000) = 1$ (as $2019 = 3 \cdot 673$ and $10000 = 2^4 \cdot 5^
4$ share no prime factors), $2019$ is invertible modulo $10000$.

### Computing the inverse of $2019 \pmod{10000}$

Extended Euclidean algorithm:

$$\begin{aligned}
10000 &= 4 \cdot 2019 + 1924 \\
2019 &= 1 \cdot 1924 + 95 \\
1924 &= 20 \cdot 95 + 24 \\
95 &= 3 \cdot 24 + 23 \\
24 &= 1 \cdot 23 + 1 \\
23 &= 23 \cdot 1
\end{aligned}$$

Back-substituting:
$$\begin{aligned}
1 &= 24 - 23 = 24 - (95 - 3\cdot 24) = 4\cdot 24 - 95 \\
&= 4(1924 - 20\cdot 95) - 95 = 4\cdot 1924 - 81\cdot 95 \\
&= 4\cdot 1924 - 81(2019 - 1924) = 85\cdot 1924 - 81\cdot 2019 \\
&= 85(10000 - 4\cdot 2019) - 81\cdot 2019 = 85\cdot 10000 - 421\cdot 2019.
\end{aligned}$$

So $2019 \cdot (-421) \equiv 1 \pmod{10000}$, and the inverse of $2019$ is $-421
 \equiv 9579 \pmod{10000}$.

**Verification:** $2019 \times 9579 = 19340001 \equiv 1 \pmod{10000}$. ✓

### Solving for $k$

$$k \equiv 9579 \cdot 2018 \pmod{10000}.$$

$$9579 \cdot 2018 = 9579 \cdot 2000 + 9579 \cdot 18 = 19158000 + 172422 = 193304
22.$$

$$19330422 \bmod 10000 = 0422.$$

So $k \equiv 422 \pmod{10000}$, and the smallest positive value is $k = 422$.

### Computing $N$

$$N = 2019 \cdot 422 = 2000 \cdot 422 + 19 \cdot 422 = 844000 + 8018 = 852018.$$

### Verification

- **Divisibility:** $852018 / 2019 = 422$ exactly. ✓
- **Terminal digits:** $852018 \bmod 10000 = 2018$. ✓
- **Minimality:** The solutions form the residue class $k \equiv 422 \pmod{10000
}$; the smallest positive $k$ is $422$, giving the smallest positive $N = 852018
$. ✓
