# Problem

In a remote industrial warehouse, a logistics floor is divided into a grid of 8 rows and 12 columns of storage bays. Currently, $N$ specific bays are occupied by heavy black crates, while the remaining bays are empty (white).

A specialized automated crane is used to clear the floor. In a single operation, the crane can process a "corner" configuration—a set of three bays forming an L-shape (specifically, a $2 \times 2$ square missing one corner cell). When the crane targets such a corner, it removes any black crates within those three bays, turning them white. If a bay in the selected corner was already white, its state remains unchanged.

The warehouse manager determines that because of the specific distribution of the $N$ black crates, it is mathematically impossible to clear the entire floor (making all bays white) in fewer than 25 operations.

Find the minimum possible value of $N$.
