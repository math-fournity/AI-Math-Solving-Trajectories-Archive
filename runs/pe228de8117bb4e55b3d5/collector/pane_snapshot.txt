Holonomy Group of $\mathbb{O}P^1$

Step 1: Identify the manifold $\mathbb{O}P^1$

The octonionic projective line $\mathbb{O}P^1$ is constructed as the quotient of
$\mathbb{O}^2 \setminus {0}$ by the right $\mathbb{O}^\times$-action $(x, y)
\sim (x\lambda, y\lambda)$. Using the standard two-chart construction:

  • Chart $U_0 = {[1 : y] : y \in \mathbb{O}} \cong \mathbb{O} \cong \mathbb{R}
  ^8$
  • Chart $U_1 = {[x : 1] : x \in \mathbb{O}} \cong \mathbb{O} \cong \mathbb{R}
  ^8$

These two copies of $\mathbb{R}^8$ are glued along $\mathbb{O} \setminus {0}
\cong S^7$ via the octonionic inversion transition map. This is precisely the
standard two-chart description of the 8-sphere. Hence:

$$\mathbb{O}P^1 \cong S^8.$$

Step 2: Identify the metric

The canonical (Fubini–Study type) metric on $\mathbb{O}P^1$ induces, under the
identification $\mathbb{O}P^1 \cong S^8$, the round metric on $S^8$ (up to a
constant scaling). This is the standard result: the projective line over any
normed division algebra $\mathbb{K}$ carries the round metric on the sphere
$S^{\dim \mathbb{K}}$.

Step 3: Compute the holonomy

The round sphere $S^n$ ($n \geq 2$) is the irreducible Riemannian symmetric
space

$$S^n = \frac{SO(n+1)}{SO(n)}.$$

For an irreducible symmetric space $G/K$ that is not Hermitian symmetric, the
holonomy group equals the isotropy group $K$. The sphere $S^n$ is not Hermitian
symmetric (for $n \neq 2m$ with the appropriate structure, and in particular
$S^8$ is not a Hermitian symmetric space), so:

$$\mathrm{Hol}(S^n) = SO(n).$$

Equivalently, $S^n$ has constant positive sectional curvature, and a complete
simply-connected Riemannian manifold of constant curvature has holonomy $SO(n)$
(the full orthogonal group, with no reduction).

For $n = 8$:

$$\mathrm{Hol}(S^8) = SO(8).$$

Conclusion

Since $\mathbb{O}P^1 \cong S^8$ with the round metric, the holonomy group of $
\mathbb{O}P^1$ is

$$\boxed{SO(8)}.$$
