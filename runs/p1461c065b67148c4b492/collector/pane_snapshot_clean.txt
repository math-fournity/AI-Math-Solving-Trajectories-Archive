Looking at this problem, I need to determine whether convex combinations of prod
uct states are weak*-dense in all states on a tensor product of C*-algebras.

**Answer: False**

**Proof:**

Consider the simplest nontrivial case: $A = B = M_2(\mathbb{C})$, so $A \otimes
B = M_2(\mathbb{C}) \otimes M_2(\mathbb{C}) \cong M_4(\mathbb{C})$ (the minimal/
spatial tensor product, which in finite dimensions is the unique C*-tensor produ
ct).

**States on $M_4(\mathbb{C})$** are exactly the $4 \times 4$ density matrices, i
.e., positive semidefinite matrices of trace 1.

**Product states** are those of the form $\rho_A \otimes \rho_B$, where $\rho_A,
 \rho_B$ are $2 \times 2$ density matrices. **Convex combinations of product sta
tes** are precisely the **separable states** on $M_2 \otimes M_2$.

**Key observation:** Since $M_4(\mathbb{C})$ is finite-dimensional, the weak*-to
pology on the state space $S(M_4)$ coincides with the norm topology (all Hausdor
ff locally convex topologies on a finite-dimensional space coincide). The state
space is compact (closed and bounded in a finite-dimensional space).

The set of separable states is the convex hull of the set of product states $\{\
rho_A \otimes \rho_B\}$. In finite dimensions, the set of product states is comp
act (continuous image of the compact product $S(M_2) \times S(M_2)$), and the co
nvex hull of a compact set in a finite-dimensional space is compact, hence **clo
sed**.

Now consider the **maximally entangled state**:
$$|\Phi^+\rangle = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|00\rangle + |11\rangle), \quad \rho_{\te
xt{ent}} = |\Phi^+\rangle\langle\Phi^+|.$$

This is a valid state (density matrix) on $M_4$. It is **not separable**: its pa
rtial transpose $\rho_{\text{ent}}^{T_A} = \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i,j}|i\rangle\langle
 j|\otimes|j\rangle\langle i|$ has a negative eigenvalue ($-\frac{1}{2}$), viola
ting the Peres–Horodecki criterion (which is necessary and sufficient for separa
bility in $M_2 \otimes M_2$).

Since the set of separable states is **closed** (in the weak* = norm topology) a
nd $\rho_{\text{ent}}$ is **not** separable, $\rho_{\text{ent}}$ does not lie in
 the closure of the convex combinations of product states.

Therefore, the convex combinations of product states are **not weak*-dense** in
the set of all states on $A \otimes B$.
