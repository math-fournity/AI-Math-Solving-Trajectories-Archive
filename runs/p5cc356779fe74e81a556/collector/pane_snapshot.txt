By induction, $f(y + n\alpha) = f(y) + 2n$ for all positive integers $n$.

Suppose f(a) = f(b) for a, b > 0. Apply P(a,b) and P(b,a):
$$f(ab + f(a)) = a\,f(b)+2, \qquad f(ab + f(b)) = b\,f(a)+2.$$
Since f(a) = f(b), the left-hand sides are equal, so $a\,f(b) = b\,f(a)$. As f(a

**Step 3: A shift relation for all real multiples of α.**

Apply P(x, y) and P(x, y+α):
$$f(xy + f(x)) = x\,f(y)+2, \qquad f(xy + x\alpha + f(x)) = x\,f(y+\alpha)+2 = x

Subtracting:
$$f(xy + x\alpha + f(x)) - f(xy + f(x)) = 2x.$$
Setting $t = xy + f(x)$, as y ranges over ℝ⁺, t ranges over $(f(x), \infty)$, gi

$$f(t + x\alpha) = f(t) + 2x \quad \forall\, x>0,\; \forall\, t > f(x). \tag{E'}
