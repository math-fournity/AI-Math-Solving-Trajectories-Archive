## Proof

Let $B = \{A \subseteq \mathbb{N} : A \text{ is finite or } \mathbb{N}\setminus
A \text{ is finite}\}$, a Boolean algebra under $\cap, \cup, \complement$ with $
\bot = \emptyset$, $\top = \mathbb{N}$.

Recall: in a Boolean algebra, $M$ is a maximal ideal iff $\{a : \neg a \in M\}$
is a maximal filter (ultrafilter), and dually $F$ is a maximal filter iff $\{a :
 \neg a \in F\}$ is a maximal ideal. So it suffices to classify the ultrafilters
 of $B$; the maximal ideals are then obtained by complementation.

### Step 1. Classifying the ultrafilters of $B$

For each $n \in \mathbb{N}$ define the **principal ultrafilter**
$$U_n = \{A \in B : n \in A\}.$$
It is clearly an ultrafilter: closed under finite intersections, upward closed,
and for every $A \in B$ exactly one of $A, \mathbb{N}\setminus A$ contains $n$.

Define the **Fréchet (cofinite) filter**
$$U_\infty = \{A \in B : A \text{ is cofinite}\}.$$
It is a filter: the intersection of two cofinite sets is cofinite, and a superse
t (within $B$) of a cofinite set is cofinite. It is an ultrafilter because every
 $A \in B$ is either finite (so $\mathbb{N}\setminus A$ is cofinite, hence in $U
_\infty$) or cofinite (so $A \in U_\infty$). It is non-principal since no single
ton is cofinite.

**Claim.** *These are all the ultrafilters of $B$.*

*Proof of claim.* Let $U$ be an ultrafilter of $B$.

- **Case 1: $U$ is principal.** Then some singleton $\{n\} \in U$. For any $A \i
n B$, since $U$ is an ultrafilter, exactly one of $A, \mathbb{N}\setminus A$ is
in $U$; together with $\{n\} \in U$ this forces $n \in A \iff A \in U$. Hence $U
 = U_n$.

- **Case 2: $U$ is non-principal.** Then $\{n\} \notin U$ for every $n$, so by t
he ultrafilter property $\mathbb{N}\setminus\{n\} \in U$ for every $n$. For any
finite set $F = \{n_1,\dots,n_k\}$, the finite intersection $\bigcap_{i=1}^k (\m
athbb{N}\setminus\{n_i\}) = \mathbb{N}\setminus F$ lies in $U$. Thus every cofin
ite set belongs to $U$. Now take any $A \in B$: if $A$ is cofinite then $A \in U
$; if $A$ is finite then $\mathbb{N}\setminus A$ is cofinite, hence in $U$, so $
A \notin U$. Therefore $U = U_\infty$.

This exhausts all possibilities. $\square$

### Step 2. Maximal filters

The maximal filters of $B$ are precisely the ultrafilters classified above:
$$\boxed{\;U_n = \{A \in B : n \in A\} \text{ for each } n \in \mathbb{N},\quad
\text{and}\quad U_\infty = \{A \in B : A \text{ is cofinite}\}.\;}$$

### Step 3. Maximal ideals

Via the duality $M \mapsto \{A : \mathbb{N}\setminus A \in M\}$, the maximal ide
al corresponding to an ultrafilter $U$ is $I = \{A \in B : \mathbb{N}\setminus A
 \in U\}$.

- For $U_n$: $I_n = \{A \in B : n \in \mathbb{N}\setminus A\} = \{A \in B : n \n
otin A\}$.
- For $U_\infty$: $I_\infty = \{A \in B : \mathbb{N}\setminus A \text{ is cofini
te}\} = \{A \in B : A \text{ is finite}\}$, the ideal $\mathcal{F}$ of all finit
e subsets of $\mathbb{N}$.

**Direct verification of maximality.**

*$I_\infty = \mathcal{F}$ is maximal.* It is an ideal (finite sets are closed un
der finite unions and under taking subsets within $B$). If $J \supsetneq \mathca
l{F}$ is an ideal, pick $C \in J \setminus \mathcal{F}$; then $C$ is cofinite, s
o $\mathbb{N}\setminus C$ is finite, hence $\mathbb{N}\setminus C \in \mathcal{F
} \subseteq J$. Thus $\mathbb{N} = C \cup (\mathbb{N}\setminus C) \in J$, forcin
g $J = B$.

*$I_n$ is maximal.* It is an ideal (sets not containing $n$ are closed under fin
ite unions and subsets). If $J \supsetneq I_n$ is an ideal, pick $A \in J$ with
$n \in A$. For any $D \in B$, write $D = (D \cap A) \cup (D \setminus A)$. Since
 $n \in A$, we have $n \notin \mathbb{N}\setminus A$, so $\mathbb{N}\setminus A
\in I_n \subseteq J$, and $D \setminus A \subseteq \mathbb{N}\setminus A$ gives
$D\setminus A \in J$; also $D \cap A \subseteq A$ gives $D \cap A \in J$. Hence
$D \in J$, so $J = B$.

**Completeness.** Any maximal ideal $I$ yields an ultrafilter $U = \{A : \mathbb
{N}\setminus A \in I\}$, which by Step 1 is some $U_n$ or $U_\infty$; tracing ba
ck gives $I = I_n$ or $I = I_\infty$. So the list is exhaustive.

### Conclusion

The **maximal ideals** of $B$ are:
$$I_n = \{A \in B : n \notin A\} \quad (n \in \mathbb{N}), \qquad I_\infty = \{A
 \in B : A \text{ is finite}\}.$$

The **maximal filters** of $B$ are:
$$U_n = \{A \in B : n \in A\} \quad (n \in \mathbb{N}), \qquad U_\infty = \{A \i
n B : A \text{ is cofinite}\}.$$

Each $I_n$ is the complement (in $B$) of $U_n$, and $I_\infty$ is the complement
 of $U_\infty$.
