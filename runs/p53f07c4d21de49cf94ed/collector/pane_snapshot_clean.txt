### Foundational Tools

We rely on two deep results for compact topological manifolds, both due to Edwar
ds and Kirby:

**Theorem A (Relative Local Contractibility).** *Let $M$ be a compact manifold.
There exists an open neighborhood $V$ of $\mathrm{id}$ in $\mathrm{Homeo}(M)$ (w
ith the compact-open topology) such that for every compact $K \subset M$ and eve
ry $h \in V$ with $h|_K = \mathrm{id}|_K$, there is a continuous isotopy $h_t:[0
,1]\to\mathrm{Homeo}(M)$ with $h_0 = h$, $h_1 = \mathrm{id}$, $h_t|_K = \mathrm{
id}|_K$ for all $t$, and $h_t \in V$ for all $t$.*

**Theorem B (Isotopy Extension).** *Let $M$ be a compact manifold, $K\subset M$
compact, and $U$ an open neighborhood of $K$. Any isotopy of embeddings $\varphi
_t:K\to U$ ($t\in[0,1]$) with $\varphi_0 = \mathrm{incl}_K$ extends to an isotop
y $\Phi_t$ of $M$ supported in $U$, with $\Phi_0 = \mathrm{id}$.*

Both theorems are proved in Edwards–Kirby's 1971 paper on deformations of homeom
orphisms on compact manifolds. Theorem A is the *relative* form of their local c
ontractibility theorem; Theorem B is the topological isotopy extension theorem,
which is established as part of the same circle of ideas (using the "Engulfing L
emma" and the annulus theorem).

and contained in the open set $U_{\alpha(i)}$, so there is a neighborhood of $\m
athrm{id}$ in the compact-open topology with this property.)

{-1}\circ h\in V$. (This holds by continuity of composition in the compact-open
topology, choosing $V'$ sufficiently small.)

We now construct $f_1,\dots,f_n$ and auxiliary homeomorphisms $h^{(0)},\dots,h^{

**Inductive step $i$** ($1\le i\le n$): Assume $h^{(i-1)}\in V$, $h^{(i-1)}|_{K_

### Step 2: The general case

Now let $f:M\to M$ be a homeomorphism isotopic to $\mathrm{id}$, with isotopy $F
