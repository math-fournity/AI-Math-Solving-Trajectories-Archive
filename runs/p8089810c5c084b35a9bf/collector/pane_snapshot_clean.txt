## Solution

**Setting up the differential equation.** From $(x+2)(x-1)f'(x)=1$ we get

$$f'(x)=\frac{1}{(x+2)(x-1)}.$$

**Partial fractions.** Decompose:

$$\frac{1}{(x+2)(x-1)}=\frac{A}{x+2}+\frac{B}{x-1}.$$

Setting $x=1$: $B=\tfrac{1}{3}$; setting $x=-2$: $A=-\tfrac{1}{3}$. Hence

$$f'(x)=-\frac{1}{3}\cdot\frac{1}{x+2}+\frac{1}{3}\cdot\frac{1}{x-1}.$$

**Integrating on each interval.** The singularities at $x=-2$ and $x=1$ split th
e domain into three intervals, so the integration constant may differ on each:

$$f(x)=\frac{1}{3}\ln\left|\frac{x-1}{x+2}\right|+C_i,\qquad i\in\{1,2,3\}$$

for $(-\infty,-2)$, $(-2,1)$, $(1,\infty)$ respectively.

**Using $f(0)=\tfrac{1}{3}$** (note $0\in(-2,1)$, so constant is $C_2$):

$$\frac{1}{3}\ln\left|\frac{-1}{2}\right|+C_2=\frac{1}{3}\ln\frac{1}{2}+C_2=\fra
c{1}{3}\implies C_2=\frac{1}{3}(1+\ln 2).$$

**Using $f(-3)=f(3)$** (note $-3\in(-\infty,-2)$ with $C_1$, $3\in(1,\infty)$ wi
th $C_3$):

$$f(-3)=\frac{1}{3}\ln\left|\frac{-4}{-1}\right|+C_1=\frac{1}{3}\ln 4+C_1,$$

$$f(3)=\frac{1}{3}\ln\left|\frac{2}{5}\right|+C_3=\frac{1}{3}\ln\frac{2}{5}+C_3.
$$

Equating:

$$C_1-C_3=\frac{1}{3}\ln\frac{2}{5}-\frac{1}{3}\ln 4=\frac{1}{3}\ln\frac{1}{10}=
-\frac{1}{3}\ln 10.$$

**Computing $f(-4)+f(-1)-f(4)$.**

- $f(-4)$ ($-4\in(-\infty,-2)$, constant $C_1$): $\;\frac{1}{3}\ln\frac{5}{2}+C_
1$.
- $f(-1)$ ($-1\in(-2,1)$, constant $C_2$): $\;\frac{1}{3}\ln 2+C_2$.
- $f(4)$ ($4\in(1,\infty)$, constant $C_3$): $\;\frac{1}{3}\ln\frac{1}{2}+C_3=-\
frac{1}{3}\ln 2+C_3$.

Therefore

$$f(-4)+f(-1)-f(4)=\frac{1}{3}\ln\frac{5}{2}+\frac{2}{3}\ln 2+C_2+(C_1-C_3).$$

Substituting $C_2=\frac{1}{3}(1+\ln 2)$ and $C_1-C_3=-\frac{1}{3}\ln 10$:

$$=\frac{1}{3}\ln\frac{5}{2}+\frac{2}{3}\ln 2+\frac{1}{3}(1+\ln 2)-\frac{1}{3}\l
n 10$$

$$=\frac{1}{3}\ln\frac{5}{2}+\ln 2+\frac{1}{3}-\frac{1}{3}\ln 10$$

$$=\frac{1}{3}\ln\frac{5/2}{10}+\ln 2+\frac{1}{3}=\frac{1}{3}\ln\frac{1}{4}+\ln
2+\frac{1}{3}$$

$$=-\frac{2}{3}\ln 2+\ln 2+\frac{1}{3}=\frac{1}{3}\ln 2+\frac{1}{3}.$$

$$\boxed{f(-4)+f(-1)-f(4)=\dfrac{1+\ln 2}{3}}$$
