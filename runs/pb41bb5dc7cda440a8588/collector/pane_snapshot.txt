## Proof

We show that **yes**, $X$ is a Fano variety. A Fano variety is a smooth projecti
ve variety whose anticanonical divisor $-K$ is ample. Since $X$ is given as a pr
ojective variety (hence projective and integral), it suffices to verify two thin
gs: (a) $X$ is smooth, and (b) $-K_X$ is ample. Both descend from $\bar{X}$ to $
X$ under the extension $K \hookrightarrow \bar{K}$.

### (a) Smoothness descends

Let $\pi: \bar{X} \to X$ be the natural projection. Smoothness of a morphism is
local for the fpqc topology (EGA IV$_4$, 17.7.1). The morphism $\operatorname{Sp
ec} \bar{K} \to \operatorname{Spec} K$ is faithfully flat (every field extension
 is flat; the algebraic closure map $K \to \bar{K}$ is faithfully flat since it
is injective). Therefore:

$$X \text{ is smooth over } K \iff \bar{X} = X \times_K \bar{K} \text{ is smooth
 over } \bar{K}.$$

Since $\bar{X}$ is a smooth Fano variety, $\bar{X}$ is smooth over $\bar{K}$. By
 fpqc descent, $X$ is smooth over $K$.

*(In characteristic zero this can also be seen via the Jacobian criterion: the e
xtension $K \hookrightarrow \bar{K}$ is separable, so the rank of the Jacobian m
atrix is preserved under base change, and smoothness — equivalent to the Jacobia
n having the correct rank — descends.)*

### (b) Ampleness of $-K_X$ descends

**Canonical sheaf under base change.** For the projection $\pi: \bar{X} \to X$,
there is a natural isomorphism of canonical sheaves:
$$\omega_{\bar{X}/\bar{K}} \cong \pi^* \omega_{X/K}.$$
This holds because $\pi$ is a base change of the flat morphism $\operatorname{Sp
ec}\bar{K} \to \operatorname{Spec}K$, and the relative canonical sheaf is compat
ible with flat base change (it is the top exterior power of $\Omega^1$, and $\Om
ega^1$ commutes with base change). Consequently:
$$-K_{\bar{X}} = \pi^*(-K_X).$$

**Ampleness is invariant under field extension.** Let $L$ be a line bundle on a
proper $k$-scheme $Y$, and let $k'/k$ be any field extension with projection $\p
i: Y_{k'} \to Y$. Then $L$ is ample on $Y$ if and only if $\pi^*L$ is ample on $
Y_{k'}$.

*Proof of this fact:* By Serre's cohomological criterion, $L$ is ample if and on
ly if for every coherent sheaf $\mathcal{F}$ on $Y$, we have $H^i(Y, \mathcal{F}
 \otimes L^{\otimes n}) = 0$ for all $i > 0$ and $n \gg 0$. Since $k'/k$ is flat
, flat base change gives:
$$H^i(Y_{k'},\, \mathcal{F}_{k'} \otimes (\pi^*L)^{\otimes n}) \cong H^i(Y,\, \m
athcal{F} \otimes L^{\otimes n}) \otimes_k k'.$$
A tensor product $V \otimes_k k'$ vanishes if and only if $V$ vanishes (since $k
'$ is a nonzero free $k$-module). Thus the vanishing conditions for $L$ on $Y$ a
nd for $\pi^*L$ on $Y_{k'}$ are equivalent. $\square$

Applying this with $Y = X$, $L = -K_X = \omega_{X/K}^{-1}$, and $k'/k = \bar{K}/
K$:

$$-K_X \text{ is ample on } X \iff \pi^*(-K_X) = -K_{\bar{X}} \text{ is ample on
 } \bar{X}.$$

Since $\bar{X}$ is Fano, $-K_{\bar{X}}$ is ample on $\bar{X}$. Therefore $-K_X$
is ample on $X$.

### Conclusion

We have shown:
1. $X$ is smooth over $K$ (by fpqc descent of smoothness, using that $\bar{K}/K$
 is faithfully flat).
2. $-K_X$ is ample on $X$ (by invariance of ampleness under field extension, usi
ng flat base change and Serre's criterion).
3. $X$ is projective (given) and integral (given, as a variety).

Therefore $X$ is a smooth projective variety with ample anticanonical divisor, i
.e., $X$ is a **Fano variety**. $\blacksquare$
