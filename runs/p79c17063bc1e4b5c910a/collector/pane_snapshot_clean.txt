## Proof

We need the smallest integer $k > 6$ such that for some integer $n > k$:
$$1 + 2 + \cdots + k \;=\; k + (k+1) + \cdots + n.$$

**Setting up the equation.** The left side is $\tfrac{k(k+1)}{2}$. The right sid
e is $\tfrac{n(n+1)}{2} - \tfrac{(k-1)k}{2}$. Equating:

$$\frac{k(k+1)}{2} = \frac{n(n+1)}{2} - \frac{k(k-1)}{2}.$$

Adding $\tfrac{k(k-1)}{2}$ to both sides:

$$\frac{k(k+1)+k(k-1)}{2} = \frac{n(n+1)}{2} \;\;\Longrightarrow\; \frac{k\cdot
2k}{2} = \frac{n(n+1)}{2} \;\;\Longrightarrow\; k^{2} = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}.$$

So we need a **triangular number** $T_n = \tfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$ that is a perfect s
quare $k^2$, with $n > k > 6$.

**Reduction to a Pell equation.** Set $m = 2n+1$ (which is odd). Then
$$m^2 = 4n^2+4n+1 = 4n(n+1)+1 = 8k^2+1,$$
so
$$m^2 - 8k^2 = 1, \qquad m = 2n+1 \text{ odd}, \quad n = \frac{m-1}{2} > k.$$

**Solving the Pell equation.** The fundamental solution of $x^2 - 8y^2 = 1$ is $
(x_1,y_1) = (3,1)$, since $9 - 8 = 1$. All positive solutions are generated by
$$x_t + y_t\sqrt{8} = (3+\sqrt{8})^t, \qquad t = 1,2,3,\dots$$
with the recurrence $(x_{t+1},\,y_{t+1}) = (3x_t + 8y_t,\; x_t + 3y_t)$.

Computing:

| $t$ | $x_t$ | $y_t = k$ | $m=x_t$ | $n=\tfrac{m-1}{2}$ | $n>k$? |
|-----|-------|-----------|---------|---------------------|--------|
| 1   | 3     | 1         | 3       | 1                   | $1>1$ ✗ |
| 2   | 17    | 6         | 17      | 8                   | $8>6$ ✓ (baseline) |
| 3   | 99    | 35        | 99      | 49                  | $49>35$ ✓ |

Details of $t=3$: $(x_3,y_3) = (3\cdot 17 + 8\cdot 6,\; 17 + 3\cdot 6) = (51+48,
\; 17+18) = (99,\,35)$.

**Why there is nothing between $k=6$ and $k=35$.** The Pell equation $x^2-8y^2=1
$ has a *unique* family of positive solutions, indexed by $t=1,2,3,\dots$, and t
he $y_t$ values are strictly increasing (since $x_t,y_t$ grow monotonically via
the recurrence with positive coefficients). The solutions with $n>k$ occur at $t
=2$ ($k=6$) and $t=3$ ($k=35$); no solution with $k$ strictly between 6 and 35 e
xists.

**Verification of $k=35$, $n=49$.**
- Series-A sum: $1+2+\cdots+35 = \tfrac{35\cdot 36}{2} = 630$.
- Series-B sum: $35+36+\cdots+49 = \tfrac{49\cdot 50}{2} - \tfrac{34\cdot 35}{2}
 = 1225 - 595 = 630$.
- Both equal $630$. ✓

**Answer.** The smallest integer value of $k$ greater than $6$ satisfying the ba
lancing condition is

$$\boxed{k = 35} \quad (\text{with } n = 49).$$
