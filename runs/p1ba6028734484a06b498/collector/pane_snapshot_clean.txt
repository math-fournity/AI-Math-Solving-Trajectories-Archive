Looking at this problem, I need to determine whether a continuous function $f: \
mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ with $f(\mathbb{C})$ open and $|f(z)| \to \infty$ as $
z \to \infty$ extends continuously to the Riemann sphere $\hat{\mathbb{C}} = \ma
thbb{C} \cup \{\infty\}$.

**Answer: Yes, such an extension always exists.**

## Proof

The Riemann sphere $\hat{\mathbb{C}} = \mathbb{C} \cup \{\infty\}$ is the one-po
int compactification of $\mathbb{C}$, equipped with the topology where neighborh
oods of $\infty$ are sets of the form $\{w \in \mathbb{C} : |w| > M\} \cup \{\in
fty\}$ for $M > 0$, together with all usual open subsets of $\mathbb{C}$.

**Definition of the extension.** Define $F: \hat{\mathbb{C}} \to \hat{\mathbb{C}
}$ by
$$F(z) = f(z) \text{ for } z \in \mathbb{C}, \qquad F(\infty) = \infty.$$

Clearly $F|_{\mathbb{C}} = f$, so $F$ extends $f$. It remains to show $F$ is con
tinuous.

**Continuity at points of $\mathbb{C}$.** For any $z_0 \in \mathbb{C}$, the subs
pace topology on $\mathbb{C} \subset \hat{\mathbb{C}}$ agrees with the usual Euc
lidean topology. Since $f$ is continuous on $\mathbb{C}$, and $F$ agrees with $f
$ on $\mathbb{C}$, $F$ is continuous at every point of $\mathbb{C}$.

**Continuity at $\infty$.** We must show: for every open neighborhood $V$ of $F(
\infty) = \infty$ in $\hat{\mathbb{C}}$, the preimage $F^{-1}(V)$ is open in $\h
at{\mathbb{C}}$ (equivalently, is a neighborhood of $\infty$).

Any open neighborhood $V$ of $\infty$ in $\hat{\mathbb{C}}$ contains a basic nei
ghborhood $B_M = \{w \in \mathbb{C} : |w| > M\} \cup \{\infty\}$ for some $M > 0
$. Thus it suffices to show $F^{-1}(B_M)$ is a neighborhood of $\infty$ for ever
y $M > 0$.

We have:
$$F^{-1}(B_M) = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : |f(z)| > M\} \cup \{\infty\}.$$

By the hypothesis $|f(z)| \to \infty$ as $z \to \infty$, there exists $R > 0$ su
ch that
$$|z| > R \implies |f(z)| > M.$$

Therefore $\{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z| > R\} \cup \{\infty\} \subseteq F^{-1}(B_M)
\subseteq F^{-1}(V)$.

Since $\{z : |z| > R\} \cup \{\infty\}$ is a basic open neighborhood of $\infty$
 in $\hat{\mathbb{C}}$, the set $F^{-1}(V)$ contains a neighborhood of $\infty$,
 hence is a neighborhood of $\infty$. This proves $F$ is continuous at $\infty$.

**Conclusion.** $F: \hat{\mathbb{C}} \to \hat{\mathbb{C}}$ is continuous at ever
y point, so $f$ extends to a continuous function on the Riemann sphere. $\blacks
quare$

**Remark.** The condition that $f(\mathbb{C})$ is open is not needed for this co
nclusion; the extension relies solely on $|f(z)| \to \infty$ as $z \to \infty$,
which is precisely the condition that $f(z) \to \infty$ in the topology of $\hat
{\mathbb{C}}$.
