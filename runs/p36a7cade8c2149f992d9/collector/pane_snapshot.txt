## Setup

Place the unit square with coordinates:
- $A = (0,0)$, $B = (1,0)$, $C = (1,1)$, $D = (0,1)$.

**Point $E$** — midpoint of $CD$: since $C=(1,1)$ and $D=(0,1)$,
$$E = \left(\tfrac{1}{2},\, 1\right).$$

**Point $F$** — on $BC$ with $BF = 2\,CF$. Since $BC$ has length $1$, we get $BF
 = \tfrac{2}{3}$ and $CF = \tfrac{1}{3}$. Moving from $B=(1,0)$ toward $C=(1,1)$
:
$$F = \left(1,\, \tfrac{2}{3}\right).$$

## Lines $AF$ and $BE$

**Line $AF$** (from $A=(0,0)$ to $F=(1,\tfrac{2}{3})$), parametrized by $t\in[0,
1]$:
$$(x,y) = \left(t,\; \tfrac{2t}{3}\right).$$

**Line $BE$** (from $B=(1,0)$ to $E=(\tfrac{1}{2},1)$), parametrized by $s\in[0,
1]$:
$$(x,y) = \left(1 - \tfrac{s}{2},\; s\right).$$

## Intersection $P$

Equating coordinates:
$$t = 1 - \tfrac{s}{2}, \qquad \tfrac{2t}{3} = s.$$

Substitute $s = \tfrac{2t}{3}$ into the first equation:
$$t = 1 - \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\tfrac{2t}{3} = 1 - \tfrac{t}{3}.$$
$$t + \tfrac{t}{3} = 1 \;\Longrightarrow\; \tfrac{4t}{3} = 1 \;\Longrightarrow\;
 t = \tfrac{3}{4}.$$

So $P$ corresponds to parameter $t = \tfrac{3}{4}$ along segment $AF$ (from $A$
to $F$).

## Computing $\dfrac{AP}{PF}$

Since $P$ lies on segment $AF$ at parameter $t = \tfrac{3}{4}$:
$$AP = \tfrac{3}{4}\,|AF|, \qquad PF = \tfrac{1}{4}\,|AF|.$$

Therefore
$$\frac{AP}{PF} = \frac{\tfrac{3}{4}}{\tfrac{1}{4}} = 3.$$
