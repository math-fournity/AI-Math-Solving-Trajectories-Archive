## Proof

We derive the blackbody spectrum from the maximum-entropy principle on the given
 discrete measure space.

### Setup

The state space is $\Omega = \{\epsilon_0 + n\delta : n = 0,1,2,\dots\}$ with $\
delta = \hbar\omega$, equipped with the counting measure $\nu$. The Hamiltonian
is the identity $X(k)=k$, so on the state $\epsilon_0+n\delta$ it takes the valu
e $\epsilon_0+n\delta$.

We seek the probability distribution $\{p_n\}$ that **maximizes the entropy**
$$S = -\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} p_n \ln p_n$$
subject to the constraints
$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} p_n = 1, \qquad \sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(\epsilon_0+n\delta)\,p
_n = \langle X\rangle,$$
where $\langle X\rangle$ is the fixed mean energy determined by the temperature
$T$.

### Max-entropy distribution

Introduce Lagrange multipliers $\alpha$ (normalization) and $\beta$ (energy). St
ationarity of
$$\mathcal{L} = -\sum_n p_n\ln p_n - \alpha\!\left(\sum_n p_n-1\right) - \beta\!
\left(\sum_n(\epsilon_0+n\delta)p_n - \langle X\rangle\right)$$
gives
$$\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial p_n} = -\ln p_n - 1 - \alpha - \beta(\epsi
lon_0+n\delta) = 0,$$
hence the Gibbs (Boltzmann) distribution
$$p_n = \frac{1}{Z}\,e^{-\beta(\epsilon_0+n\delta)}, \qquad Z = \sum_{n=0}^{\inf
ty} e^{-\beta(\epsilon_0+n\delta)}.$$

### Partition function

$$Z = e^{-\beta\epsilon_0}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} e^{-\beta n\delta} = \frac{e^{-\be
ta\epsilon_0}}{1-e^{-\beta\delta}}, \qquad \beta\delta>0.$$

Taking the logarithm,
$$\ln Z = -\beta\epsilon_0 - \ln\!\bigl(1-e^{-\beta\delta}\bigr).$$

### Expectation

Using $\langle X\rangle = -\partial(\ln Z)/\partial\beta$,
$$\frac{\partial\ln Z}{\partial\beta} = -\epsilon_0 + \frac{\delta\,e^{-\beta\de
lta}}{1-e^{-\beta\delta}} = -\epsilon_0 + \frac{\delta}{e^{\beta\delta}-1},$$
so
$$E[X] = \epsilon_0 + \frac{\delta}{e^{\beta\delta}-1}, \qquad E[X]-\epsilon_0 =
 \frac{\delta}{e^{\beta\delta}-1}.$$

### Identification of $\beta$ with temperature

The Lagrange multiplier $\beta$ is identified with the thermodynamic inverse tem
perature via $\beta = 1/(k_B T)$, where $k_B$ is Boltzmann's constant. Substitut
ing $\delta = \hbar\omega$,
$$E[X]-\epsilon_0 = \frac{\hbar\omega}{e^{\hbar\omega/(k_B T)}-1}.$$

### Spectrum

Inserting into the given definition
$$\epsilon(\omega,T) = (E[X]-\epsilon_0)\,\frac{\omega^2}{\pi^2 c^3},$$
we obtain
$$\boxed{\;\epsilon(\omega,T) = \frac{\hbar\,\omega^{3}}{\pi^{2} c^{3}\bigl(e^{\
hbar\omega/(k_B T)}-1\bigr)}\;}$$

This is **Planck's blackbody radiation law**. The ground-state offset $\epsilon_
0$ cancels exactly (it contributes only an irrelevant zero-point shift), and the
 entire temperature dependence enters through the Bose–Einstein factor $(e^{\hba
r\omega/(k_B T)}-1)^{-1}$ arising from the geometric series over the equally-spa
ced energy levels — the hallmark of harmonic-oscillator (bosonic) quantization.
