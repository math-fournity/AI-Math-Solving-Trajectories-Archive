## Solution

**Setup.** Place the square base $ABCD$ in the plane $z=0$ with
$$A=(0,0,0),\ B=(10,0,0),\ C=(10,10,0),\ D=(0,10,0),$$
center $O=(5,5,0)$, and apex $S=(5,5,h)$ for unknown height $h$.

**Locating E and F.** Since $CE=6$ on $\overline{DC}$ and $CF=9$ on $\overline{B
C}$:
$$E=(4,10,0),\qquad F=(10,1,0).$$
Then $\vec{EF}=(6,-9,0)$ and $|EF|=\sqrt{36+81}=\sqrt{117}=3\sqrt{13}$.

**Cone geometry.** The cone has vertex $E$, base center $P$ on line $SA$, and $F
$ on the base circle. Parametrize $P\in SA$ as
$$P = A + t(S-A) = (5t,\,5t,\,ht).$$

For $F$ to lie on the base circle, $F$ must be in the base plane (perpendicular
to $\vec{EP}$ at $P$):
$$(F-P)\cdot(E-P)=0.$$

Computing:
$$E-P=(4-5t,\ 10-5t,\ -ht),\qquad F-P=(10-5t,\ 1-5t,\ -ht),$$
$$(10-5t)(4-5t)+(1-5t)(10-5t)+h^2t^2=0,$$
$$\boxed{(50+h^2)\,t^2 - 125\,t + 50 = 0.}\tag{1}$$

**Uniqueness determines $h$.** The cone is determined by $P$ (hence by $t$). A *
unique* cone means equation (1) has a unique solution, so its discriminant vanis
hes:
$$125^2 - 4(50+h^2)\cdot 50 = 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; 15625 - 10000 - 200h^2 = 0,$
$
$$200h^2 = 5625 \;\Longrightarrow\; h^2 = \frac{225}{8}.$$

The double root is
$$t = \frac{125}{2(50+h^2)} = \frac{125}{2\cdot\frac{625}{8}} = \frac{4}{5}.$$

**Cone dimensions.** With $t=\tfrac45$:
$$P = \left(4,\ 4,\ \tfrac{4h}{5}\right).$$

*Cone height* $H = |EP|$:
$$\vec{EP} = (0,\,-6,\,\tfrac{4h}{5}),\qquad H^2 = 36 + \frac{16h^2}{25} = 36 +
\frac{16\cdot 225}{8\cdot 25} = 36+18 = 54,$$
$$H = 3\sqrt{6}.$$

*Base radius* $r = |FP|$:
$$\vec{FP} = (6,\,-3,\,-\tfrac{4h}{5}),\qquad r^2 = 36+9+\frac{16h^2}{25} = 45+1
8 = 63,$$
$$r = 3\sqrt{7}.$$

*Consistency check:* $H^2 + r^2 = 54+63 = 117 = |EF|^2$. ✓

**Volume.**
$$V = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 H = \frac{1}{3}\pi\cdot 63 \cdot 3\sqrt{6} = 63\pi\sqrt
{6}.$$

Final Answer: $\boxed{63\pi\sqrt{6}}$

### PROOF COMPLETE
