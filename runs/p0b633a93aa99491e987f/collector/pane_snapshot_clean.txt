## Identification of $\mathcal{O}_D$

**Setup.** Let $X$ be a (smooth) variety and $D$ an effective Cartier divisor on
 $X$. Set $L = \mathcal{O}_X(D)$. The given isomorphism is the **adjunction form
ula**:

$$\omega_D \simeq \omega_X \otimes L \otimes \mathcal{O}_D.$$

**Claim.** $\mathcal{O}_D$ is the **structure sheaf of $D$**, i.e., the quotient

$$\mathcal{O}_D \;=\; \mathcal{O}_X \big/ \mathcal{O}_X(-D),$$

defined by the standard exact sequence

$$0 \longrightarrow \mathcal{O}_X(-D) \longrightarrow \mathcal{O}_X \longrightar
row \mathcal{O}_D \longrightarrow 0.$$

Equivalently, since $L = \mathcal{O}_X(D)$ and thus $\mathcal{O}_X(-D) = L^{-1}
= L^\vee$,

$$\mathcal{O}_D \;=\; \mathcal{O}_X \big/ L^{-1}.$$

---

### Proof

**Step 1: The ideal sheaf of a Cartier divisor.**

An effective Cartier divisor $D$ is the zero locus of a global section $s \in H^
0(X, \mathcal{O}_X(D)) = H^0(X, L)$. This section $s$ defines an injective map o
f $\mathcal{O}_X$-modules

$$\mathcal{O}_X \xrightarrow{\;\cdot s\;} \mathcal{O}_X(D) = L,$$

whose image is the subsheaf $\mathcal{O}_X(-D) \cdot s \subseteq \mathcal{O}_X(D
)$. Under the natural identification $\mathcal{O}_X(-D) \cong \mathcal{O}_X(-D)$
, multiplication by $s$ gives the inclusion

$$\mathcal{O}_X(-D) \hookrightarrow \mathcal{O}_X.$$

The **ideal sheaf** of $D$ in $X$ is precisely $\mathcal{I}_D = \mathcal{O}_X(-D
)$, and the **structure sheaf of $D$** is by definition the quotient

$$\mathcal{O}_D := \mathcal{O}_X / \mathcal{I}_D = \mathcal{O}_X / \mathcal{O}_X
(-D).$$

This yields the exact sequence

$$0 \to \mathcal{O}_X(-D) \to \mathcal{O}_X \to \mathcal{O}_D \to 0. \tag{$\star
$}$$

**Step 2: $\mathcal{O}_D$ as the sheaf restricting to $D$.**

The sheaf $\mathcal{O}_D$ is supported on $D$ (since $\mathcal{I}_D$ vanishes on
 the complement $X \setminus D$, where $\mathcal{O}_D$ restricts to zero). On $D
$ itself, $\mathcal{O}_D$ is the usual structure sheaf of the subscheme $D$. Ten
soring any $\mathcal{O}_X$-module $\mathcal{F}$ with $\mathcal{O}_D$ gives the r
estriction to $D$:

$$\mathcal{F}|_D = \mathcal{F} \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_X} \mathcal{O}_D.$$

**Step 3: Recovering the adjunction formula.**

The canonical sheaf of $D$ is obtained by restricting $\omega_X \otimes \mathcal
{O}_X(D)$ to $D$:

$$\omega_D = (\omega_X \otimes \mathcal{O}_X(D))\big|_D = (\omega_X \otimes L) \
otimes_{\mathcal{O}_X} \mathcal{O}_D.$$

This is exactly the given isomorphism

$$\omega_D \simeq \omega_X \otimes L \otimes \mathcal{O}_D,$$

confirming that $\mathcal{O}_D$ in the formula is the structure sheaf of $D$, i.
e., the quotient $\mathcal{O}_X / \mathcal{O}_X(-D)$.

**Step 4: Expressing in terms of $L$.**

Since $L = \mathcal{O}_X(D)$, the inverse line bundle is $L^{-1} = \mathcal{O}_X
(-D)$. The exact sequence $(\star)$ becomes

$$0 \to L^{-1} \to \mathcal{O}_X \to \mathcal{O}_D \to 0,$$

so

$$\boxed{\mathcal{O}_D = \mathcal{O}_X / L^{-1} = \mathcal{O}_X / \mathcal{O}_X(
-D).}$$

This is the structure sheaf of the Cartier divisor $D$, the cokernel of the incl
usion of the ideal sheaf $\mathcal{O}_X(-D) \hookrightarrow \mathcal{O}_X$.
