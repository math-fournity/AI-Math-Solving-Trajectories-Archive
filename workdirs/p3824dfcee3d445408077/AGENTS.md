# Problem

In the competitive world of high-tech manufacturing, two specialized hardware developers, Agent $P$ and Agent $Q$, are designing encryption keys. To ensure maximum security, they must choose their unique ID numbers, $p$ and $q$, from the set of prime numbers.

Their current project involves a security protocol where the "Efficiency Factor" of their collaboration is defined by the value $(p-1)^{q-1}-1$. For the protocol to be valid, this Efficiency Factor must be a positive integer. 

The main system mainframe has a "Capacity Limit" calculated as $(2q)^{2p}-1$. A critical engineering constraint for the project is that the Efficiency Factor must be a perfect divisor of the Capacity Limit.

If $p$ and $q$ are selected such that all these conditions are satisfied, what is the sum of all possible products $pq$ that the developers could use for their IDs?
