Looking at this problem, I need to find the largest $n \ge 3$ such that an $n$-d
igit number $N = \overline{a_1 a_2 \cdots a_n}$ (with $a_1, a_2, a_n \ne 0$) is
divisible by $M = \overline{a_2 \cdots a_n}$.

**Answer: $n = 7$**

## Proof

**Setup.** Let $N = \overline{a_1 a_2 \cdots a_n}$ and $M = \overline{a_2 \cdots
 a_n}$. Then $N = a_1 \cdot 10^{n-1} + M$, so $M \mid N$ if and only if $M \mid
a_1 \cdot 10^{n-1}$. Here $M$ is an $(n-1)$-digit number (so its first digit $a_
2 \neq 0$ automatically) with last digit $a_n \neq 0$, meaning $10 \nmid M$. Als
o $a_1 \in \{1, \ldots, 9\}$.

**Construction for $n = 7$.** Take $a_1 = 9$ and $M = 140625 = 5^6 \cdot 9$. The
n:
- $M = 140625$ is a 6-digit number ✓
- First digit $a_2 = 1 \neq 0$ ✓, last digit $a_7 = 5 \neq 0$ ✓
- $M \mid a_1 \cdot 10^6$: indeed $9 \cdot 10^6 = 9 \cdot 2^6 \cdot 5^6 = 2^6 \c
dot 5^6 \cdot 9$, and $M = 5^6 \cdot 9$ divides this.
- $N = 9 \cdot 10^6 + 140625 = 9140625$, and $9140625 / 140625 = 65$ ✓

So $n = 7$ is achievable.

**Impossibility for $n \geq 8$.** Write $a_1 = 2^p \cdot 5^q \cdot r$ with $\gcd
(r, 10) = 1$. Then $a_1 \cdot 10^{n-1} = 2^{n-1+p} \cdot 5^{n-1+q} \cdot r$. Any
 divisor $M$ of this with $10 \nmid M$ must have $v_2(M) = 0$ or $v_5(M) = 0$.

**Case 1: $v_5(M) = 0$.** Then $M \mid 2^{n-1+p} \cdot r$, so $M \leq 2^{n-1+p}
\cdot r \leq 2^{n+2} \cdot 9$. For $M$ to be an $(n-1)$-digit number we need $M
\geq 10^{n-2} = 2^{n-2} \cdot 5^{n-2}$, giving $2^{4} \cdot 9 \geq 5^{n-2}$, i.e
., $144 \geq 5^{n-2}$. Since $5^3 = 125 \leq 144$ but $5^4 = 625 > 144$, we get
$n \leq 5$.

**Case 2: $v_2(M) = 0$.** Then $M \mid 5^{n-1+q} \cdot r$, so $M \leq 5^{n-1+q}
\cdot r$. For $M \geq 10^{n-2}$ we need $5^{n-1+q} \cdot r \geq 2^{n-2} \cdot 5^
{n-2}$, i.e., $5^{1+q} \cdot r \geq 2^{n-2}$.

Over all $a_1 \in \{1,\ldots,9\}$, the maximum of $5^{1+q} \cdot r$ is:
- $a_1 = 9$: $q=0, r=9$, giving $5 \cdot 9 = 45$
- $a_1 = 7$: $q=0, r=7$, giving $5 \cdot 7 = 35$
- $a_1 = 5$: $q=1, r=1$, giving $25 \cdot 1 = 25$

The overall maximum is $\mathbf{45}$. So we need $2^{n-2} \leq 45$. Since $2^5 =
 32 \leq 45$ but $2^6 = 64 > 45$, we get $n \leq 7$.

**Conclusion.** Case 1 gives $n \leq 5$ and Case 2 gives $n \leq 7$. The maximum
 is $n = 7$, achieved by $N = 9140625$ with $M = 140625$.
