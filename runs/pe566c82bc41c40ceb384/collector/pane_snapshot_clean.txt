**Reformulation.** Let $N_p$ denote the number of ordered pairs $(x,y)$ with $0
\le x,y < p$ satisfying $y^2 \equiv x^3 - x \pmod{p}$. The projective closure of
 $E: y^2 = x^3 - x$ is an elliptic curve, and its number of $\mathbb{F}_p$-ratio
nal points (including the point at infinity) is
$$\#E(\mathbb{F}_p) = p + 1 - a_p,$$
where $a_p$ is the trace of Frobenius. Since $N_p$ counts only the affine points
 (those with finite coordinates),
$$N_p = \#E(\mathbb{F}_p) - 1 = p - a_p.$$
The condition $N_p = p$ is therefore equivalent to
$$a_p = 0.$$

**The curve has CM by $\mathbb{Z}[i]$.** The curve $y^2 = x^3 - x$ has $j$-invar
iant $1728$ and complex multiplication by $\mathbb{Z}[i]$. The trace of Frobeniu
s is determined by the splitting of $p$ in $\mathbb{Z}[i]$:

- **$p \equiv 3 \pmod{4}$ (inert in $\mathbb{Z}[i]$):** The curve is supersingul
ar and $a_p = 0$.
- **$p \equiv 1 \pmod{4}$ (split in $\mathbb{Z}[i]$):** Writing $p = a^2 + b^2$
with $a$ odd and $b$ even (both nonzero since $p$ is prime and not a perfect squ
are), one has $a_p = \pm 2a \neq 0$.

So for odd primes, $a_p = 0 \iff p \equiv 3 \pmod{4}$.

**The prime $p = 2$.** Directly: $x^3 - x \equiv 0 \pmod{2}$ for both $x=0$ and
$x=1$, and $y^2 \equiv 0 \pmod{2}$ forces $y=0$. Thus $N_2 = 2 = p$. So $p=2$ qu
alifies.
