Looking at this problem, I need to count the ways to partition a 3×3 grid of cit
ies between Gondor (capital at top-left) and Mordor (capital at bottom-right), w
here each nation's cities form a connected region reachable from its capital via
 grid adjacency.

## Setup

Label the grid:
```
A B C
D E F
G H I
```
- **A** = Gondor capital (fixed), **I** = Mordor capital (fixed).
- The 7 remaining cells {B,C,D,E,F,G,H} are each assigned to Gondor or Mordor.
- Both the Gondor set (containing A) and Mordor set (containing I) must be conne
cted under 4-adjacency.

Let k = number of cells (besides A) assigned to Gondor. Then |Gondor| = k+1, |Mo
rdor| = 8−k. I enumerate by k, requiring **both** regions connected.

## Symmetry Observation

The 180° rotation (A↔I, B↔H, C↔G, D↔F, E↔E) combined with swapping nations is a
bijection on valid configurations. So count(|G|=m) = count(|G|=9−m). This means
I only need to compute k = 0,1,2,3 and mirror for k = 7,6,5,4.
