## Statement

Let $X$ be a Hausdorff space with $X = A \cup B$, where $A \cong T^2$, $B \cong
T^2$ (each homeomorphic to a torus), and $A \cap B = \{x_0\}$. We determine $\pi
_1(X, x_0)$.

## Setup for Seifert–van Kampen

The standard Seifert–van Kampen theorem requires *open*, path-connected sets who
se intersection is path-connected and contains the basepoint. Since $A$ and $B$
are compact (being homeomorphic to $T^2$) and $X$ is Hausdorff, $A$ and $B$ are
**closed** in $X$. We must replace them by suitable open neighborhoods.

**Key observation:** A torus is a 2-manifold, so every point has a closed-disk n
eighborhood. Let $D_A \subset A$ and $D_B \subset B$ be closed-disk neighborhood
s of $x_0$ in $A$ and $B$ respectively, with interiors $D_A^\circ$, $D_B^\circ$.

Define
$$U := A \cup D_B^\circ, \qquad V := B \cup D_A^\circ.$$

**Claim 1: $U$ and $V$ are open in $X$.**
We have $U = X \setminus (B \setminus D_B^\circ)$. Since $D_B^\circ$ is open in
$B$, the set $B \setminus D_B^\circ$ is closed in $B$; and $B$ is closed in $X$
(compact in Hausdorff), so $B \setminus D_B^\circ$ is closed in $X$. Hence $U$ i
s open. Likewise $V$ is open.

**Claim 2: $U$ deformation retracts onto $A$, and $V$ onto $B$.**
The interior $D_B^\circ$ is an open disk containing $x_0 \in A$. There is a defo
rmation retraction of $D_B^\circ$ onto $\{x_0\}$ (radial contraction). Extending
 by the identity on $A$ gives a deformation retraction of $U = A \cup D_B^\circ$
 onto $A$. Similarly $V$ deformation retracts onto $B$.

**Claim 3: $U \cap V$ is path-connected and contractible.**
$$U \cap V = (A \cup D_B^\circ) \cap (B \cup D_A^\circ) = D_A^\circ \cup D_B^\ci
rc,$$
using $A \cap B = \{x_0\}$ and $D_A^\circ \subset A$, $D_B^\circ \subset B$. Thi
s is the union of two open disks meeting at the single point $x_0$ — i.e., the w
edge of two open disks. It is path-connected (every point connects to $x_0$ with
in its disk) and contractible (deformation retract each disk radially to $x_0$).
 Hence
$$\pi_1(U \cap V, x_0) = \{1\}.$$

## Applying Seifert–van Kampen

All hypotheses are satisfied: $U, V$ open and path-connected, $U \cap V$ path-co
nnected containing $x_0$. The theorem gives
$$\pi_1(X, x_0) \cong \pi_1(U, x_0) \;\ast_{\pi_1(U \cap V,\, x_0)}\; \pi_1(V, x
_0).$$

Using the deformation retractions (Claim 2) and $\pi_1(U \cap V) = \{1\}$ (Claim
 3):
$$\pi_1(X, x_0) \cong \pi_1(A, x_0) \;\ast_{\{1\}}\; \pi_1(B, x_0) \cong \pi_1(A
, x_0) * \pi_1(B, x_0).$$

## Computing the factors

A torus $T^2$ has fundamental group $\pi_1(T^2) \cong \mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{
Z}$, presented as $\langle a, b \mid [a,b]=1\rangle$ (two commuting generators).
 Since $A \cong T^2$ and $B \cong T^2$:
$$\pi_1(A, x_0) \cong \langle a, b \mid [a, b] = 1\rangle, \qquad \pi_1(B, x_0)
\cong \langle c, d \mid [c, d] = 1\rangle.$$

Taking the free product (no relations linking the two sets of generators):
$$\boxed{\pi_1(X, x_0) \cong (\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}) * (\mathbb{Z} \times
 \mathbb{Z}) = \langle\, a, b, c, d \;\mid\; [a,b]=1,\; [c,d]=1\,\rangle.}$$

## Remarks on the structure

- The generators $a, b$ (from the first torus) commute with each other, and $c,
d$ (from the second torus) commute with each other, but there are **no relations
 between $\{a,b\}$ and $\{c,d\}$**.
- In particular $\pi_1(X, x_0) \not\cong \mathbb{Z}^4$: for instance, $ac \neq c
a$ in general (the free product is non-abelian).
- This is the fundamental group of the **wedge sum** $T^2 \vee T^2$, which is ex
actly the space described: two tori joined at a single point.
