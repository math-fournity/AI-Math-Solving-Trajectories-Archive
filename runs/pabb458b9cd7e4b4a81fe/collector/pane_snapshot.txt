## Solution

**Step 1: Split via CRT.** $10000 = 16 \times 625$, with $\gcd(16,625)=1$.

**Step 2: $T \bmod 16$.** Since $7^2 = 49 \equiv 1 \pmod{16}$, we have $7^k \equ

$$T \equiv 7 \pmod{16}.$$

**Step 3: $T \bmod 625$.** Since $\gcd(7,625)=1$, we use the order of $7$ modulo
 $625$.

- $\operatorname{ord}_5(7)=\operatorname{ord}_5(2)=4$.

{25}(7)=4$.

So $T\equiv 7^{E}\pmod{625}$ where $E = T_{99}$ (tower of 99 sevens), and we nee
