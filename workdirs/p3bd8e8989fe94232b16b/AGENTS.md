# Problem

A high-tech server farm is monitoring the energy consumption across a massive array of 2001 individual processing units. The power draw of each unit is determined by its position in a linear sequence and a specific "efficiency factor," denoted as $x$.

The total power consumption for the entire array is calculated by summing the efficiency factor raised to increasing even powers. The first unit consumes $1$ unit of power ($x^0$), the second unit consumes $x^2$ units, the third consumes $x^4$, and so on, following this geometric progression until the 2001st unit, which consumes $x^{4000}$ units.

According to the facility's master control panel, the combined power draw of all 2001 units is exactly equal to 2001 times the efficiency factor raised to the power of 2000 (represented as $2001x^{2000}$).

Calculate the specific efficiency factor $x$ that satisfies this energy balance.
