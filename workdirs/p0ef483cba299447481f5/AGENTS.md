# Problem

In a sprawling logistics center, a security grid is composed of a square array of $8 \times 8$ storage bays. To manage inventory, managers can place one of two types of high-density pallets into these bays: a "Double-Stack" (valued at $2$ units) or a "Single-Stack" (valued at $1$ unit). Some bays may also be left completely empty.

The facility operates under a strict interference protocol: no storage bay is permitted to have two adjacent neighbors (bays sharing a wall) that contain the same type of pallet. For example, a bay cannot be bordered by two different "Double-Stack" pallets, nor can it be bordered by two different "Single-Stack" pallets.

Under these operational constraints, what is the maximum possible total value of all pallets that can be stored in the $64$ bays?
