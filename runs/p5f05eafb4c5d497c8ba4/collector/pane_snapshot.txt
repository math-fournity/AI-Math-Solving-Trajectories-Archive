## Solution

**Goal:** Find 18 natural numbers summing to 665 with minimal LCM.

### Lower bound

Let $L = \operatorname{lcm}(a_1, \dots, a_{18})$. Every $a_i$ divides $L$, so $a
_i \le L$ for all $i$. Since the 18 numbers sum to 665, the largest is at least
$\lceil 665/18 \rceil = 37$, hence $L \ge 37$.

### Ruling out $L = 37$

Since 37 is prime, every $a_i \mid 37$ forces $a_i \in \{1, 37\}$. If $k$ of the
 18 numbers equal 37 (and $18-k$ equal 1), the sum is:
$$37k + (18-k) = 36k + 18.$$
Setting $36k + 18 = 665$ gives $36k = 647$, which has no integer solution ($647
= 36 \times 17 + 35$). So **$L = 37$ is impossible**.

### Achieving $L = 38$

Since $38 = 2 \times 19$, its divisors are $\{1, 2, 19, 38\}$. Take:
$$\underbrace{38, 38, \dots, 38}_{17 \text{ copies}},\; 19.$$
The sum is $17 \times 38 + 19 = 646 + 19 = 665$, using exactly 18 natural number
s, all dividing 38. The LCM of these numbers is $\operatorname{lcm}(38, 19) = 38
$.

### Conclusion

The LCM cannot be 37 or less (since $L \ge 37$ and $L=37$ is impossible), and $L
 = 38$ is achievable. Therefore the smallest possible LCM is:

$$\boxed{38}$$
