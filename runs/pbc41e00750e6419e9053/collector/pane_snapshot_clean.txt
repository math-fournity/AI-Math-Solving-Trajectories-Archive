## Solution

We distribute 20 identical crates among teams W, X, Y, Z, with each team receivi
ng at least 1 crate, and the constraints W+X ≥ 5 and Y+Z ≥ 5.

**Setup.** Let $a = W+X$ (Western total) and $b = Y+Z$ (Eastern total). Then $a+
b=20$, with $a\ge 5$ and $b\ge 5$, so $a$ ranges over $\{5,6,\dots,15\}$.

For a fixed value of $a$, the number of ways to split $a$ into two positive inte
gers $(W,X)$ is $a-1$. Likewise, the number of ways to split $b=20-a$ into two p
ositive integers $(Y,Z)$ is $b-1 = 19-a$.

**Counting.** The total number of valid distributions is

$$\sum_{a=5}^{15}(a-1)(19-a).$$

Substituting $k=a-1$ (so $k$ ranges $4$ to $14$):

$$\sum_{k=4}^{14} k(18-k)=\sum_{k=4}^{14}(18k-k^2).$$

Using $\sum_{k=4}^{14}k = \frac{(4+14)\cdot 11}{2}=99$ and $\sum_{k=4}^{14}k^2 =
 \frac{14\cdot15\cdot29}{6}-\sum_{k=1}^{3}k^2 = 1015-14=1001$:

$$18\cdot 99 - 1001 = 1782 - 1001 = 781.$$

**Verification by inclusion–exclusion.** The total number of solutions to $W+X+Y
+Z=20$ with each $\ge 1$ is $\binom{19}{3}=969$. Subtract the cases violating ea
ch constraint:

- $W+X\le 4$ (i.e., $W+X\in\{2,3,4\}$): contributions $1\cdot17+2\cdot16+3\cdot1
5=17+32+45=94$.
- By symmetry, $Y+Z\le 4$ also contributes $94$.
- Both $W+X\le 4$ and $Y+Z\le 4$ simultaneously would require $W+X+Y+Z\le 8<20$,
 so the intersection is $0$.

Thus the count is $969-94-94+0=781$, confirming the direct calculation.

**Answer:** The 20 crates can be distributed in **781** ways.
