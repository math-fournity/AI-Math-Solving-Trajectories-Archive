## Solution

We compute the flux integral $\iint_Q G \cdot \mathbf{n}\, dS$.

**Parametrization.** The surface is $z = x^2 + 2y^2$ over the elliptical domain
$D = \{(x,y): x^2 + 2y^2 < 1\}$. Use the natural parametrization
$$\mathbf{r}(x,y) = (x,\; y,\; x^2 + 2y^2), \qquad (x,y) \in D.$$
Then
$$\mathbf{r}_x = (1,0,2x), \qquad \mathbf{r}_y = (0,1,4y),$$
and
$$\mathbf{r}_x \times \mathbf{r}_y = (-2x,\,-4y,\,1).$$
The $z$-component is $+1>0$, so this is the upward normal; $\mathbf{n}\,dS = (\m
athbf{r}_x\times\mathbf{r}_y)\,dx\,dy$.

**Evaluate $G$ on the surface.** With $z = x^2+2y^2$,
$$G(\mathbf{r}(x,y)) = \big(y,\; x^2+2y^2,\; x(x^2+2y^2)+y\big).$$

**Dot product.**
$$G \cdot (\mathbf{r}_x\times\mathbf{r}_y) = y(-2x) + (x^2+2y^2)(-4y) + \big(x(x
^2+2y^2)+y\big)(1)$$
$$= -2xy - 4x^2 y - 8y^3 + x^3 + 2xy^2 + y.$$

**Integrate over $D$.** The domain $D = \{x^2+2y^2<1\}$ is symmetric under both
$x \mapsto -x$ and $y \mapsto -y$. Examine each summand for parity:

| Term | Parity in $x$ | Parity in $y$ | Integral over $D$ |
|------|---------------|---------------|-------------------|
| $-2xy$ | odd | odd | $0$ |
| $-4x^2 y$ | even | odd | $0$ |
| $-8y^3$ | even | odd | $0$ |
| $x^3$ | odd | even | $0$ |
| $2xy^2$ | odd | even | $0$ |
| $y$ | even | odd | $0$ |

th. Hence each term integrates to $0$, and therefore
