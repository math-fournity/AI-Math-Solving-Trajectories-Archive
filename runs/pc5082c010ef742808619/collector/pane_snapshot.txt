## Proof

Let $X$ be a bouquet of 3 circles. As a 1-dimensional CW-complex (graph) with on
e vertex and three edges, its Euler characteristic is
$$\chi(X) = V - E = 1 - 3 = -2.$$

The fundamental group $\pi_1(X)$ is the free group $F_3$ of rank $3$, consistent
 with $\operatorname{rank}\pi_1(X) = 1 - \chi(X) = 3$.

Now let $p\colon \hat{X} \to X$ be a connected covering space of degree $d = 10$
. A covering space of a graph is again a graph, so $\hat{X}$ is a connected grap
h and $\pi_1(\hat{X})$ is a free group. Its rank is determined by its Euler char
acteristic:
$$\operatorname{rank}\pi_1(\hat{X}) = 1 - \chi(\hat{X}).$$

Euler characteristic is multiplicative under finite-sheeted covering maps:
$$\chi(\hat{X}) = d \cdot \chi(X) = 10 \cdot (-2) = -20.$$

Therefore
$$\operatorname{rank}\pi_1(\hat{X}) = 1 - \chi(\hat{X}) = 1 - (-20) = 21.$$

This can also be seen via the Nielsen–Schreier formula: a subgroup of index $d$
in a free group of rank $r$ is free of rank $d(r-1)+1$. Here $d=10$, $r=3$, givi
ng rank $10(3-1)+1 = 21$.

The rank of the free group $\pi_1(\hat{X})$ is **21**.
