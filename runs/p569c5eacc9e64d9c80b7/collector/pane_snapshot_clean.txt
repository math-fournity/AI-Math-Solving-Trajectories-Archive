## Setting Up the Formulas (case $m,n,r \ge 2$)

For a prism $m \times n \times r$ with all sides $\ge 2$:
- $k_0 = (m-2)(n-2)(r-2)$
- $k_1 = 2[(m-2)(n-2)+(m-2)(r-2)+(n-2)(r-2)]$
- $k_2 = 4[(m-2)+(n-2)+(r-2)]$

Substituting $a=m-2,\, b=n-2,\, c=r-2$:

$$k_0+k_2-k_1 = abc + 4(a+b+c) - 2(ab+ac+bc)$$

**Key factorization:** Expanding $(a-2)(b-2)(c-2) = abc - 2ab-2ac-2bc+4a+4b+4c-8

$$k_0+k_2-k_1 = (a-2)(b-2)(c-2)+8 = (m-4)(n-4)(r-4)+8$$

Setting this equal to 1985:

$$(m-4)(n-4)(r-4) = 1977$$
