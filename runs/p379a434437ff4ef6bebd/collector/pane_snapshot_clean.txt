## Solution

We compute the contour integral

$$\oint_{|z|=1} \frac{z^2}{\sin z}\,dz$$

in the positive (counter-clockwise) direction, using the residue theorem.

### Step 1: Identify the singularities

The integrand $f(z) = \dfrac{z^2}{\sin z}$ has singularities where $\sin z = 0$,
 i.e. at $z = n\pi$ for $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.

The zeros of $\sin z$ inside the unit circle $|z| = 1$:
- $z = 0$ (since $|0| = 0 < 1$),
- $z = \pm\pi \approx \pm 3.14159$, both with $|\pm\pi| > 1$, so they lie **outs
ide** the contour.

Thus the only candidate singularity inside $|z| = 1$ is $z = 0$.

### Step 2: Classify the singularity at $z = 0$

Expand $\sin z$ around $z = 0$:

$$\sin z = z - \frac{z^3}{6} + \frac{z^5}{120} - \cdots = z\left(1 - \frac{z^2}{
6} + \frac{z^4}{120} - \cdots\right).$$

Therefore

$$\frac{z^2}{\sin z} = \frac{z^2}{z\left(1 - \frac{z^2}{6} + \cdots\right)} = \f
rac{z}{1 - \frac{z^2}{6} + \cdots}.$$

Using $\dfrac{1}{1-w} = 1 + w + w^2 + \cdots$ with $w = \dfrac{z^2}{6} - \cdots$
:

$$\frac{z^2}{\sin z} = z\left(1 + \frac{z^2}{6} + \cdots\right) = z + \frac{z^3}
{6} + \cdots.$$

This is a **Taylor series** with no $\dfrac{1}{z}$ term. Hence $z = 0$ is a **re
movable singularity**, and the Laurent expansion of $f(z)$ around $z = 0$ has:

$$\operatorname{Res}(f, 0) = 0.$$

### Step 3: Apply the residue theorem

The function $f(z) = \dfrac{z^2}{\sin z}$, after removing the singularity at $z
= 0$, is analytic on and inside $|z| = 1$. The sum of residues inside the contou
r is:

$$\sum_{|a|<1} \operatorname{Res}(f, a) = \operatorname{Res}(f, 0) = 0.$$

By the residue theorem:

$$\oint_{|z|=1} \frac{z^2}{\sin z}\,dz = 2\pi i \cdot \sum \operatorname{Res} =
2\pi i \cdot 0 = 0.$$

### Result

$$\boxed{\displaystyle \oint_{|z|=1} \frac{z^2}{\sin z}\,dz = 0}$$

The integral vanishes because the only singularity of $z^2/\sin z$ inside the un
it circle is at $z = 0$, which is removable (the $z^2$ in the numerator cancels
the simple zero of $\sin z$ at the origin), leaving the integrand analytic throu
ghout the enclosed region.
