## The Open Research Problems

### `selfridge_conjecture` (PSW Conjecture)

This is the **Baillie–PSW primality test conjecture**: any odd $p$ with $p \equi
v \pm 2 \pmod{5}$, $2^{p-1} \equiv 1 \pmod{p}$, and $F_{p+1} \equiv 0 \pmod{p}$
must be prime.

**This is a genuine open problem in mathematics.** No counterexample has been fo
und despite extensive computational searches (well beyond $2^{64}$), but no proo
f is known. The conjecture combines a Fermat test (base 2) with a Lucas/Fibonacc
i test, and the reason it works so well is not fully understood. I cannot prove
this theorem.

### `selfridge_seq_conjecture`

Selfridge conjectured that $\text{fermatFactors}(n)$ is not monotone — i.e., the
