$$D_k := \log k + \gamma + \frac{1}{2k} - \frac{1}{12k^2} + \frac{1}{60k^4}.$$

Comparing term by term:

$$H_k - D_k = \left(\frac{1}{120} - \frac{1}{60}\right)\frac{1}{k^4} - \frac{1}{

$$\frac{H_k}{D_k} = 1 + \frac{H_k - D_k}{D_k} = 1 - \frac{1}{120\,k^4\,D_k} + O(
k^{-6}) = 1 - \frac{1}{120\,k^4 \log k}\bigl(1 + o(1)\bigr).$$

$$\log q_{a,3}(n) = \sum_{k=1}^{n} \log\!\left(\frac{H_k}{D_k}\right).$$
