## Solution

**Setup.** Let $L$ denote a small closed contour surrounding the cut from $a$ to
 $b$, traversed counterclockwise. The branch $\phi(\zeta)=\sqrt{(\zeta-a)/(\zeta
-b)}$ with $\phi(\infty)=+1$ is single-valued and analytic in the region inside
$C$ but outside the cut, and also in the exterior of $C$.

**Step 1: Deform the contour.**

For $z$ inside $C$ (and not on the cut), $\phi(\zeta)/(\zeta-z)$ is analytic in
the region inside $C$ minus the cut, except for a simple pole at $\zeta=z$. Defo
rming $C$ inward to $L$ plus a small circle around $z$:

$$F^+(z) = \frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_C \frac{\phi(\zeta)}{\zeta-z}\,d\zeta = \phi(z)
 + \frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_L \frac{\phi(\zeta)}{\zeta-z}\,d\zeta. \tag{1}$$

The term $\phi(z)$ comes from the residue at $\zeta=z$ (Cauchy's formula, valid
since $\phi$ is analytic at $z$).

For $z$ outside $C$, there is no pole inside $C$, so:

$$F^-(z) = \frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_C \frac{\phi(\zeta)}{\zeta-z}\,d\zeta = \frac{1
}{2\pi i}\oint_L \frac{\phi(\zeta)}{\zeta-z}\,d\zeta. \tag{2}$$

**Step 2: Compute the integral over $L$ via expansion at infinity (the hint).**

Take a large circle $C_R$ (counterclockwise) containing $C$, $L$, and $z$. In th

analytic except for a simple pole at $\zeta=z$ (with residue $\phi(z)$). By the
residue theorem:

$$\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_{C_R} \frac{\phi(\zeta)}{\zeta-z}\,d\zeta - \frac{1}{2\p
i i}\oint_L \frac{\phi(\zeta)}{\zeta-z}\,d\zeta = \phi(z). \tag{3}$$

Now expand $\phi(\zeta)/(\zeta-z)$ in powers of $1/\zeta$ for $|\zeta|\to\infty$
. Since $\phi(\infty)=1$:

$$\phi(\zeta) = \sqrt{\frac{1-a/\zeta}{1-b/\zeta}} = \left(1-\frac{a}{\zeta}\rig
ht)^{1/2}\left(1-\frac{b}{\zeta}\right)^{-1/2}.$$

Using the binomial expansion:

$$\left(1-\frac{a}{\zeta}\right)^{1/2} = 1 - \frac{a}{2\zeta} - \frac{a^2}{8\zet

$$\phi(\zeta) = 1 + \frac{b-a}{2\zeta} + O(1/\zeta^2).$$

Also, $\frac{1}{\zeta-z} = \frac{1}{\zeta}\cdot\frac{1}{1-z/\zeta} = \frac{1}{\z

$$\frac{\phi(\zeta)}{\zeta-z} = \frac{1}{\zeta}\left(1 + \frac{b-a}{2\zeta}+\cdo
ts\right)\left(1+\frac{z}{\zeta}+\cdots\right) = \frac{1}{\zeta} + \frac{z+\frac
{b-a}{2}}{\zeta^2} + \cdots$$

The coefficient of $1/\zeta$ is **1**. Hence, by the residue at infinity:

$$\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_{C_R} \frac{\phi(\zeta)}{\zeta-z}\,d\zeta = 1. \tag{4}$$

**For $z$ inside $C$** (from equation (1)):

$$F^+(z) = \phi(z) + \bigl(1 - \phi(z)\bigr) = \boxed{1}.$$

**For $z$ outside $C$** (from equation (2)):

$$F^-(z) = 1 - \phi(z) = 1 - \sqrt{\frac{z-a}{z-b}}.$$

**Verification.** The jump across $C$ is $F^+(z)-F^-(z) = 1-(1-\phi(z)) = \phi(z

$$F^+(z) = 1, \qquad F^-(z) = 1 - \sqrt{\frac{z-a}{z-b}}.$$
