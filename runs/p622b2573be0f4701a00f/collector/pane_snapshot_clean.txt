A circle through $A$ has equation $x^2 + y^2 + Dx + Ey = 0$.

**Finding $M$ on $AB$ (the $x$-axis):** Setting $y=0$: $x(x+D)=0$, so $M = (-D,

$$c + s_P(b\cos\alpha - c) - m = s_P b\cos\alpha \implies \boxed{m = c(1 - s_P)}

$$s_Q b\sin\alpha - n\sin\alpha = 0 \implies \boxed{n = b\,s_Q}$$
