# Fundamental Groups of the Two-Component Unlink and the Hopf Link

## Setup

For a link $L \subset S^3$, the **link group** is $\pi_1(S^3 \setminus L)$. We c
ompute this for (a) the two-component unlink and (b) the Hopf link.

---

## (a) Two-component unlink: $\pi_1 = F_2 = \langle a, b \mid \;\rangle$

Let $L = K_1 \sqcup K_2$ be the two-component unlink, so there exists a separati
ng $2$-sphere $S \subset S^3 \setminus L$ with $K_1 \subset \operatorname{int}(B
_1)$, $K_2 \subset \operatorname{int}(B_2)$, where $S^3 = B_1 \cup_{S} B_2$.

**Decompose the complement.** Set $U = B_1 \setminus K_1$, $V = B_2 \setminus K_
2$. Then
$$X := S^3 \setminus L = U \cup V, \qquad U \cap V = S^2 \;(\text{the separating
 sphere}).$$

**Compute $\pi_1(U)$ and $\pi_1(V)$.** Each $K_i$ is an unknot inside the ball $
B_i$. Observe
$$B_i \setminus K_i \;=\; (S^3 \setminus K_i) \setminus (S^3 \setminus \operator
name{int}(B_i)).$$
Here $S^3 \setminus K_i$ is the exterior of the unknot, i.e. a solid torus $S^1
\times D^2$, and $S^3 \setminus \operatorname{int}(B_i)$ is a closed $3$-ball si
tting in the interior of that solid torus (disjoint from $K_i$). Removing a $3$-
ball from the interior of a $3$-manifold $M$ yields a space homotopy equivalent
to $M \vee S^2$. Hence
$$B_i \setminus K_i \;\simeq\; (S^1 \times D^2) \vee S^2 \;\simeq\; S^1 \vee S^2
,$$
so $\pi_1(U) \cong \pi_1(V) \cong \mathbb{Z}$, generated by the meridian $m_i$ o
f $K_i$.

**Apply van Kampen.** Since $U \cap V = S^2$ is simply connected,
$$\pi_1(S^3 \setminus L) \;=\; \pi_1(U) * \pi_1(V) \;=\; \mathbb{Z} * \mathbb{Z}
 \;=\; F_2.$$

$$\boxed{\pi_1(S^3 \setminus \text{unlink}) \;\cong\; F_2 \;=\; \langle a, b \mi
d \;\rangle.}$$

## (b) Hopf link: $\pi_1 = \mathbb{Z}^2 = \langle a, b \mid ab=ba \rangle$

Realize $S^3 = \{(z_1,z_2)\in\mathbb{C}^2 : |z_1|^2+|z_2|^2=1\}$ and the Hopf li
nk as
$$H_1 = \{z_2 = 0\}\cap S^3, \qquad H_2 = \{z_1 = 0\}\cap S^3,$$
two circles with linking number $\pm 1$.

**The complement is a thickened torus.** The complement is
$$S^3 \setminus (H_1 \cup H_2) = \{(z_1,z_2) : |z_1|^2+|z_2|^2=1,\; z_1\neq 0,\;
 z_2\neq 0\}.$$
Define the map
$$\Phi : S^3 \setminus (H_1\cup H_2) \longrightarrow T^2 = S^1\times S^1, \quad
(z_1,z_2)\mapsto \left(\tfrac{z_1}{|z_1|},\tfrac{z_2}{|z_2|}\right).$$
The fiber over $(e^{i\theta_1},e^{i\theta_2})$ is parametrized by $r = |z_1| \in
 (0,1)$ (with $|z_2|=\sqrt{1-r^2}$), hence is an open interval. Therefore
$$S^3 \setminus (H_1\cup H_2) \;\cong\; T^2 \times (0,1) \;\simeq\; T^2.$$

**Conclusion.**
$$\pi_1(S^3 \setminus H) \;=\; \pi_1(T^2) \;=\; \mathbb{Z}\times\mathbb{Z} \;=\;
 \mathbb{Z}^2.$$

**Cross-check via Wirtinger.** With meridians $a$ (of $H_1$) and $b$ (of $H_2$)
as generators, the single relation forced by linking number $\pm 1$ is the commu
tator $[a,b]=1$, giving $\langle a,b\mid ab=ba\rangle\cong\mathbb{Z}^2$, confirm
ing the result.

$$\boxed{\pi_1(S^3 \setminus \text{Hopf link}) \;\cong\; \mathbb{Z}^2 \;=\; \lan
gle a, b \mid ab=ba \rangle.}$$

| Link | Complement (homotopy type) | Fundamental group |
|---|---|---|
| Two-component unlink | $S^1 \vee S^1$ | $F_2 = \langle a,b \mid \;\rangle$ (fr

| Hopf link | $T^2$ | $\mathbb{Z}^2 = \langle a,b \mid ab=ba\rangle$ (free abeli

The two groups differ precisely in the commutativity relation: linking number $0

pm 1$ (Hopf link) imposes $ab=ba$, yielding $\mathbb{Z}^2$.
