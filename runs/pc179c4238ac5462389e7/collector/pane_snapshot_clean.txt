Yes. In the Zariski topology on $\operatorname{Spec} A$, the closure of the sing
leton $\{p\}$ equals $V(p)$.

**Proof.**

Recall that the Zariski topology on $\operatorname{Spec} A$ has, as its closed s
ets, the subsets of the form
$$V(I) = \{q \in \operatorname{Spec} A : I \subseteq q\}$$
for an ideal $I \subseteq A$.

We show $\overline{\{p\}} = V(p)$.

**Step 1: $V(p)$ is closed and contains $p$.**
Since $p$ is a prime ideal, it is in particular an ideal of $A$, so $V(p)$ is a
closed set. Moreover $p \in V(p)$ because $p \subseteq p$. Hence $\{p\} \subsete
q V(p)$, and since $\overline{\{p\}}$ is the intersection of all closed sets con
taining $\{p\}$,
$$\overline{\{p\}} \subseteq V(p).$$

**Step 2: $V(p) \subseteq \overline{\{p\}}$.**
Let $C$ be any closed set containing $\{p\}$. Then $C = V(I)$ for some ideal $I$
, and $p \in V(I)$ means $I \subseteq p$. Now for any $q \in V(p)$, we have $p \
subseteq q$, hence $I \subseteq p \subseteq q$, so $q \in V(I) = C$. Thus $V(p)
\subseteq C$.

Since $V(p)$ is contained in *every* closed set $C$ that contains $\{p\}$, it is
 contained in their intersection, which is $\overline{\{p\}}$:
$$V(p) \subseteq \overline{\{p\}}.$$

**Conclusion.** Combining both inclusions,
$$\overline{\{p\}} = V(p) = \{q \in \operatorname{Spec} A : p \subseteq q\}.$$

Equivalently, this says that in the specialization order on $\operatorname{Spec}
 A$ (where $q \in \overline{\{p\}}$ is written $p \rightsquigarrow q$), we have
$p \rightsquigarrow q$ if and only if $p \subseteq q$; the closure of a point co
nsists exactly of all prime ideals containing $p$.
