## Solution

We evaluate $\displaystyle I = \oint_{|z+3|=4} \frac{\sin 2\pi z}{(z^2+2z)(z-3i)
}\,dz$.

### Step 1: Identify the singularities

The denominator factors as
$$z^2+2z = z(z+2),$$
so the integrand
$$f(z) = \frac{\sin 2\pi z}{z(z+2)(z-3i)}$$
has potential singularities at $z = 0$, $z = -2$, and $z = 3i$.

### Step 2: Determine which singularities lie inside the contour

The contour $|z+3|=4$ is the circle centered at $z_0 = -3$ with radius $R = 4$.

- $z = 0$: $|0+3| = 3 < 4$ — **inside**.
- $z = -2$: $|-2+3| = 1 < 4$ — **inside**.
- $z = 3i$: $|3i+3| = \sqrt{3^2+3^2} = \sqrt{18} = 3\sqrt{2} \approx 4.243 > 4$
— **outside**.

So only $z = 0$ and $z = -2$ are enclosed by the contour.

### Step 3: Compute the residues via Cauchy's integral formula

Each enclosed singularity is a simple pole of the form $\frac{g(z)}{z - a}$, whe
re $g$ is analytic at $a$. By Cauchy's integral formula (equivalently, the resid
ue formula for simple poles),

$$\operatorname{Res}_{z=a} f(z) = \lim_{z\to a}(z-a)\,f(z).$$

**At $z = 0$:**
$$\operatorname{Res}_{z=0} f(z) = \lim_{z\to 0} \frac{\sin 2\pi z}{(z+2)(z-3i)}
= \frac{\sin 0}{(2)(-3i)} = \frac{0}{-6i} = 0.$$

**At $z = -2$:**
$$\operatorname{Res}_{z=-2} f(z) = \lim_{z\to -2} \frac{\sin 2\pi z}{z(z-3i)} =
\frac{\sin(-4\pi)}{(-2)(-2-3i)} = \frac{0}{(-2)(-2-3i)} = 0.$$

Both residues vanish because $\sin 2\pi z = 0$ at every integer, and both $z = 0
$ and $z = -2$ are integers. In fact, these are **removable** singularities: nea
r $z=0$, $\sin 2\pi z \sim 2\pi z$ so $\frac{\sin 2\pi z}{z}\to 2\pi$; near $z=-
2$, writing $z = -2+w$, $\sin 2\pi z = \sin(2\pi w - 4\pi) = \sin 2\pi w \sim 2\
pi w$, so $\frac{\sin 2\pi z}{z+2}\to 2\pi$.

### Step 4: Apply the residue theorem

Since the only singularity inside the contour is the (removable, hence harmless)
 behavior at $z=0$ and $z=-2$, and the genuine pole $z=3i$ lies outside, the int
egrand is analytic on and inside $|z+3|=4$. By Cauchy's theorem (the residue the
orem with zero total residue),

$$I = 2\pi i\bigl(\operatorname{Res}_{z=0} f + \operatorname{Res}_{z=-2} f\bigr)
 = 2\pi i\,(0 + 0) = 0.$$

### Result

$$\boxed{\displaystyle \oint_{|z+3|=4} \frac{\sin 2\pi z}{(z^2+2z)(z-3i)}\,dz =
0}$$
