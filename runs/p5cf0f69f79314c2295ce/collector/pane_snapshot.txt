**Proof.**

$$\|f - g\|_\infty = \sup_{x \in [0,1]} |f(x) - g(x)| < \varepsilon.$$

We use the **Weierstrass Approximation Theorem**, which states that the set of p

Now observe that every polynomial $p(x) = a_0 + a_1 x + \cdots + a_n x^n$ is inf
