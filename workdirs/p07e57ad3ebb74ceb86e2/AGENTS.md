# Problem

In a remote industrial district, a logistics company operates a fleet of $3N$ heavy-duty cargo drones. Each drone is programmed with a specific efficiency rating, which must be an integer between 60 and 100, inclusive.

A fleet audit reveals two key pieces of data:
1. Every possible efficiency rating (60, 61, 62, ..., 100) is currently assigned to at least two different drones in the fleet.
2. The overall average efficiency rating across all $3N$ drones is exactly 82.4.

The company's manager wants to organize these drones into three separate delivery squads, each consisting of exactly $N$ drones. For optimal synchronization, the manager requires that the average efficiency rating within each of the three squads be exactly 82.4.

Determine the smallest possible integer value of $N$ for which it is mathematically guaranteed that the $3N$ drones can be partitioned into three such squads, regardless of how the specific efficiency ratings are distributed among the drones (as long as the audit criteria are met).
