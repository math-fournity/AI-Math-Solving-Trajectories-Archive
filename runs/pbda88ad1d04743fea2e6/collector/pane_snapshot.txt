$|FA|, |FM|, |FB|$ form an AP with $|FM|$ as the middle term:

$$\frac{a^2 + p^2}{2p} + \frac{b^2 + p^2}{2p} = 2 \cdot \frac{y_0^2 + p^2}{2p}$$

## Step 2: Orthogonality Condition

(\frac{a+b}{2p},\; 1\right)$.

Since $A \neq B$ (so $b \neq a$), the dot product vanishing gives:

$$(a+b)\left[\frac{1}{2p}\left(\frac{a^2+b^2}{4p} - q\right) + \frac{1}{2}\right

**Case $a + b = 0$:** Then $b = -a$ and from $(*)$: $2a^2 = 2y_0^2$, so $a = \pm

**Case bracket $= 0$:** This gives $q = \frac{a^2+b^2}{4p} + p$. Using $(*)$: $q

## Step 3: Determining $p$ and $M$

From $|FM| = \frac{5}{2}$: $\frac{y_0^2 + p^2}{2p} = \frac{5}{2}$, so $y_0^2 = 5

$$p = 1, \quad x_0 = 2, \quad y_0 = \pm 2, \quad q = 3$$

## Step 4: Computing the Area

$$2S = \left|(b-a)\left(\frac{a^2+b^2}{2} + 1\right)\right|$$

$$S = \frac{|a-b| \cdot (8+2)}{4} = \frac{5}{2}|a-b|$$

## Step 5: Maximizing $|a-b|$

We maximize $|a-b|$ subject to $a^2 + b^2 = 8$ (a circle of radius $2\sqrt{2}$).

$$|a - b| = 2\sqrt{2}\,|\cos\theta - \sin\theta| = 4\left|\sin\!\left(\tfrac{\pi

Therefore:

$$S_{\max} = \frac{5}{2} \times 4 = \boxed{10}$$
