## Proof

Let $E = \ell^2(\mathbb{N})$ with standard orthonormal basis $(e_n)_{n \geq 1}$.
 Define $S \colon \ell^2 \to \ell^2$ by
$$S e_1 = 0, \qquad S e_n = \tfrac{1}{n}\, e_n \quad (n \geq 2),$$
extended linearly and continuously. Equivalently, $S$ is the diagonal operator $
(Sx)_1 = 0$, $(Sx)_n = \frac{1}{n}x_n$ for $n \geq 2$.

**$S$ is bounded.** $\|Sx\|^2 = \sum_{n\geq 2} \frac{1}{n^2}|x_n|^2 \leq \sum_{n
\geq 1}|x_n|^2 = \|x\|^2$, so $\|S\| \leq 1$.

**$S$ is positive.** Being diagonal with real entries, $S$ is self-adjoint. More
over
$$\langle Sx, x\rangle = \sum_{n\geq 2} \frac{1}{n}|x_n|^2 \geq 0$$
for every $x \in \ell^2$, so $S \geq 0$.

**$S$ is not injective.** $Se_1 = 0$ with $e_1 \neq 0$, so $\ker S \neq \{0\}$.

**$\operatorname{ran} S$ is not closed.** The range is
$$\operatorname{ran} S = \bigl\{y \in \ell^2 : y_1 = 0 \text{ and } (n\,y_n)_{n\
geq 2} \in \ell^2\bigr\}.$$
Consider $y = (0,\, \tfrac12,\, \tfrac13,\, \tfrac14,\, \dots) \in \ell^2$ (sinc
e $\sum_{n\geq 2} \tfrac{1}{n^2}<\infty$). We have $(n\,y_n)_{n\geq 2} = (1,1,1,
\dots)\notin \ell^2$, so $y \notin \operatorname{ran} S$.

For each $k \geq 2$, define $x^{(k)} \in \ell^2$ by $x^{(k)}_n = 1$ for $2 \leq
n \leq k$ and $x^{(k)}_n = 0$ otherwise, and set $y^{(k)} = Sx^{(k)}$, so $y^{(k
)}_n = \tfrac{1}{n}$ for $2 \leq n \leq k$ and $0$ otherwise. Then $y^{(k)} \in
\operatorname{ran} S$ and
$$\|y^{(k)} - y\|^2 = \sum_{n > k} \frac{1}{n^2} \xrightarrow{k\to\infty} 0.$$
Thus $y^{(k)} \to y$ in $\ell^2$ with each $y^{(k)} \in \operatorname{ran} S$ bu
t $y \notin \operatorname{ran} S$. Hence $\operatorname{ran} S$ is not closed.

Therefore $S$ is a positive operator on the complex Hilbert space $\ell^2(\mathb
b{N})$ that is neither injective nor has closed range.

### PROOF COMPLETE
