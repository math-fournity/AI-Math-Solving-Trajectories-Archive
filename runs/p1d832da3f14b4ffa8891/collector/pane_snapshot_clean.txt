## Solution

**Key identity.** For $\alpha > 0$ and $\beta > 0$,

$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \binom{\alpha}{n}\frac{(-1)^n}{n\beta+1} = \int_0^1 (1-x^\
beta)^\alpha\,dx.$$

*Proof.* The binomial expansion gives $(1-x^\beta)^\alpha = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\
binom{\alpha}{n}(-1)^n x^{n\beta}$ for $x\in[0,1]$. Integrating term-by-term (ju
stified since $\alpha>0$ makes the integrand bounded and the series absolutely c
onvergent on $[0,1)$):

$$\int_0^1 (1-x^\beta)^\alpha\,dx = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\binom{\alpha}{n}(-1)^n\i
nt_0^1 x^{n\beta}\,dx = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\binom{\alpha}{n}\frac{(-1)^n}{n\beta
+1}.$$

**Evaluating via the Beta function.** Substituting $u = x^\beta$ (so $dx = \tfra
c{1}{\beta}u^{1/\beta-1}\,du$):

$$\int_0^1 (1-x^\beta)^\alpha\,dx = \frac{1}{\beta}\int_0^1 (1-u)^\alpha\, u^{1/
\beta-1}\,du = \frac{1}{\beta}\,B\!\left(\frac{1}{\beta},\,\alpha+1\right) = \fr
ac{1}{\beta}\cdot\frac{\Gamma(1/\beta)\,\Gamma(\alpha+1)}{\Gamma(1/\beta+\alpha+
1)}.$$

**Applying to both sums.**

*First sum* ($\alpha = \tfrac15,\ \beta = 8$):

$$S_1 = \frac{1}{8}\cdot\frac{\Gamma(1/8)\,\Gamma(6/5)}{\Gamma(1/8+6/5)} = \frac
{1}{8}\cdot\frac{\Gamma(1/8)\,\Gamma(6/5)}{\Gamma(53/40)}, \qquad \tfrac18+\tfra
c65 = \tfrac{5+48}{40}=\tfrac{53}{40}.$$

*Second sum* ($\alpha = \tfrac18,\ \beta = 5$):

$$S_2 = \frac{1}{5}\cdot\frac{\Gamma(1/5)\,\Gamma(9/8)}{\Gamma(1/5+9/8)} = \frac
{1}{5}\cdot\frac{\Gamma(1/5)\,\Gamma(9/8)}{\Gamma(53/40)}, \qquad \tfrac15+\tfra
c98 = \tfrac{8+45}{40}=\tfrac{53}{40}.$$

Both share the same denominator $\Gamma(53/40)$.

**Using the recurrence $\Gamma(z+1)=z\,\Gamma(z)$.**

$$\Gamma(6/5) = \Gamma\!\left(\tfrac15+1\right) = \tfrac15\,\Gamma(1/5), \qquad
\Gamma(9/8) = \Gamma\!\left(\tfrac18+1\right) = \tfrac18\,\Gamma(1/8).$$

Substituting:

$$S_1 = \frac{1}{8}\cdot\frac{\Gamma(1/8)\cdot\frac15\,\Gamma(1/5)}{\Gamma(53/40
)} = \frac{\Gamma(1/8)\,\Gamma(1/5)}{40\,\Gamma(53/40)},$$

$$S_2 = \frac{1}{5}\cdot\frac{\Gamma(1/5)\cdot\frac18\,\Gamma(1/8)}{\Gamma(53/40
)} = \frac{\Gamma(1/8)\,\Gamma(1/5)}{40\,\Gamma(53/40)}.$$

**Conclusion.** $S_1 = S_2$, therefore

$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \binom{\frac{1}{5}}{n}\frac{(-1)^n}{8n+1} - \sum_{n=0}^{\i
nfty} \binom{\frac{1}{8}}{n}\frac{(-1)^n}{5n+1} = S_1 - S_2 = \boxed{0}.$$
