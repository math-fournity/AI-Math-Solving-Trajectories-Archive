## Setting Up the Carry Analysis

**Key identity.** Since the $j$-th digit of $5n$ is $(5d_j + c_j) \bmod 10 = 5d_

$$S(5n) = \sum_{j=0}^{m-1}(5d_j + c_j - 10c_{j+1}) + c_m = 5S(n) + \sum_{j=0}^{m

Therefore:
$$S(n) - S(5n) = 9\sum_{j=1}^{m}c_j - 4S(n) = \sum_{j=0}^{m-1}\bigl(9c_{j+1} - 4
