(2009,2009), (0,2009)$. The inscribed square (side 1489) has its vertices on the

$$1489^2 = x^2 + (2009 - x)^2$$

8161 = 631^2$, giving $x = 689$ or $x = 1320$ (and $689 + 1320 = 2009$). So the
inner square's vertices are:

$$A=(689,0),\ B=(2009,689),\ C=(1320,2009),\ D=(0,1320).$$

**Edge vectors:** $\vec{AB} = (1320, 689)$, $\vec{BC} = (-689, 1320)$, with $132

This is a common lattice point iff both coordinates are integers, i.e., $1489 \m

**Counting.** The condition $\pi \mid (p+q\mathbf{i})$ means $(p+q\mathbf{i})/(3
