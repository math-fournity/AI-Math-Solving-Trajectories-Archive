direction is $-p_i$,
$$q_i = p_i - \ell_i\, p_i = (1-\ell_i)\,p_i.$$

Let $V_{\text{int}}$ be the set of interior vertices. For every $v\in V_{\text{i

$$\sum_{e\ni v} \widehat e_v = 0, \qquad \widehat e_v := \text{unit vector along

**Key identity.** Take the inner product of $(\star)$ with the position vector $
v$ and sum over all interior vertices:
$$\sum_{v\in V_{\text{int}}} \Big\langle v,\;\sum_{e\ni v}\widehat e_v\Big\rangl
e = 0. \tag{1}$$

Reorganize (1) edge by edge.

*Interior–interior edge* $e=uw$ (both $u,w$ interior). Its two contributions are
$$\big\langle u,\tfrac{w-u}{|w-u|}\big\rangle + \big\langle w,\tfrac{u-w}{|w-u|}
\big\rangle
= \frac{\langle u-w,\, w-u\rangle}{|w-u|}

*Boundary edge* $p_i q_i$ (only $q_i$ is interior). The outgoing unit vector at
$q_i$ points toward $p_i$:
$$\widehat e_{q_i} = \frac{p_i-q_i}{|p_i-q_i|} = \frac{\ell_i p_i}{\ell_i}=p_i.$
$
So its contribution is
$$\big\langle q_i,\, p_i\big\rangle = \big\langle (1-\ell_i)p_i,\, p_i\big\rangl
e = 1-\ell_i.$$

$$0 = \sum_{\text{int–int edges }e}(-|e|) \;+\; \sum_{i=1}^{n}(1-\ell_i).$$

$$0 = -L_{\text{int}} + n - L_{\text{bdy}},$$

$$L_{\text{int}} + L_{\text{bdy}} = n.$$
