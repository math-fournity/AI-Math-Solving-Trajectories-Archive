## Proof

Let $f \colon \mathbb{CP}^n \to \mathbb{CP}^n$ be the map $[z_0 : \cdots : z_n]
\mapsto [z_0^d : \cdots : z_n^d]$.

**Cohomology ring.** Recall $H^*(\mathbb{CP}^n; \mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}[x]/
(x^{n+1})$, where $x \in H^2(\mathbb{CP}^n;\mathbb{Z})$ is the hyperplane class,
 i.e. $x = c_1(\mathcal{O}(1))$, the first Chern class of the dual tautological
line bundle. The group $H^{2k}(\mathbb{CP}^n;\mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}$ is ge
nerated by $x^k$, and odd cohomology vanishes.

**Pullback of the hyperplane bundle.** A section of $\mathcal{O}(1)$ over $\math
bb{CP}^n$ is a linear form $\ell$ on $\mathbb{C}^{n+1}$. Pulling back along $f$
gives the section
$$
(f^*\ell)([z]) = \ell(z_0^d, \dots, z_n^d),
$$
which is a homogeneous polynomial of degree $d$ in $z_0,\dots,z_n$. Hence
$$
f^*\mathcal{O}(1) \cong \mathcal{O}(d).
$$
Taking first Chern classes,
$$
f^*(x) = f^*c_1(\mathcal{O}(1)) = c_1(\mathcal{O}(d)) = d\,x.
$$

**Action on all powers.** Since $f^*$ is a ring homomorphism,
$$
f^*(x^k) = (f^*x)^k = (dx)^k = d^k x^k, \qquad 0 \le k \le n.
$$
On $H^0$, $f^*(1) = 1 = d^0 x^0$.

**Consistency check via degree.** The topological degree of $f$ equals the actio
n on $H^{2n}$, namely $f^*(x^n) = d^n x^n$, so $\deg(f) = d^n$. This matches the
 geometric count: a generic point $[w_0:\cdots:w_n]$ has $d$ choices of $d$-th r
oot for each of $n+1$ coordinates, modulo the overall $d$-fold ambiguity from sc
aling $z \mapsto \zeta z$ ($\zeta^d = 1$), giving $d^{n+1}/d = d^n$ preimages, e
ach counted with $+1$ since $f$ is holomorphic (orientation-preserving).

**Conclusion.** The induced map $f^* \colon H^*(\mathbb{CP}^n;\mathbb{Z}) \to H^
*(\mathbb{CP}^n;\mathbb{Z})$ is the graded ring endomorphism determined by
$$
\boxed{\,f^*(x) = d\,x\,}
$$
i.e. on each cohomology group $H^{2k}(\mathbb{CP}^n;\mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}
$ (generated by $x^k$),
$$
f^*(x^k) = d^k\, x^k, \qquad k = 0, 1, \dots, n,
$$
and $f^*$ is zero on the (vanishing) odd-degree groups. Equivalently, $f^*$ is m
ultiplication by $d^k$ on $H^{2k}$.
