Looking at this problem, I need to find the maximum distance d from B where the
rabbits can notice the dog and still all reach the burrow at C.

## Setup

Place B at origin, C at position b, A at position −a. The rabbits start at B mov
ing toward C at 0.5 m/s. At time t₁ = 2d/0.5 = 2d, the rabbits are at position d
 and notice the dog, then accelerate to full speed.

**At time t₁ = 2d:** Rabbits at position d; dog at −a + 4d. The gap is a − 3d (p
ositive since a < 3b and d < b).

**Speeds after noticing:** Dog 2 m/s, large rabbit 1.5 m/s, small rabbits 1 m/s.
 The large rabbit can carry one small rabbit at 1.5 m/s.

## Optimal Strategy

The large rabbit carries small rabbit 1 (S1) forward to position x, drops S1, re
turns to meet small rabbit 2 (S2), then carries S2 to the burrow. S1 continues a
lone at 1 m/s.

**Phase 1** (carry S1 from d to x): Duration τ₁ = 2(x−d)/3. S2 is at d + τ₁, mov
ing at 1 m/s.

**Phase 2** (large rabbit returns to meet S2): They meet after s = 2(x−d)/15 add
itional seconds, at position (4x+d)/5. Total time since noticing: τ₂ = 4(x−d)/5.

**Phase 3** (carry S2 from (4x+d)/5 to b): Duration 2(5b−4x−d)/15.

## Arrival Times (from start)

**S1:** T₁ = 2d + 2(x−d)/3 + (b−x) = 4d/3 + b − x/3

**Large rabbit + S2:** T₂ = 2d + (4x + 10b − 14d)/15 = (16d + 4x + 10b)/15

## Dog Catch Condition

S2 is always the rearmost rabbit. The dog catches S2 when positions equal. Durin
g phases 1–2, S2 moves at 1 m/s and the dog closes the gap at 1 m/s. During phas
e 3, S2 is carried at 1.5 m/s, so the gap closes at only 0.5 m/s.

I verify the catch occurs during phase 3 (after pickup): the catch time during p
hases 1–2 would be t = a − d, which exceeds τ₂ = 4(x−d)/5 precisely when a < 3b
(given). So the dog catches S2 during phase 3.

**Catch position during phase 3:** Computing where dog and S2 positions coincide
 yields:

$$P_{\text{catch}} = 3a - \frac{8x + 32d}{5}$$

**"Just make it" requires P_catch = b:**

$$3a - \frac{8x + 32d}{5} = b \implies x = \frac{15a - 32d - 5b}{8}$$

## Optimizing x

To maximize d, I choose x so that S1 arrives exactly when the dog catches S2 at
the burrow (T₁ = T₂ = catch time). Setting T₁ = T₂:

$$\frac{4d}{3} + b - \frac{x}{3} = \frac{16d + 4x + 10b}{15}$$

Solving: 20d + 15b − 5x = 16d + 4x + 10b, giving **x = (4d + 5b)/9**.

Equating the two expressions for x:

$$\frac{15a - 32d - 5b}{8} = \frac{4d + 5b}{9}$$

$$9(15a - 32d - 5b) = 8(4d + 5b)$$
$$135a - 288d - 45b = 32d + 40b$$
$$135a - 85b = 320d$$

$$\boxed{d = \frac{27a - 17b}{64}}$$

## Verification

**Positivity:** d > 0 ⟺ 27a > 17b ⟺ a > 17b/27 ✓ (given)

**d < b:** 27a − 17b < 64b ⟺ 27a < 81b ⟺ a < 3b ✓ (given)

**x ∈ [d, b]:** x = (3a+7b)/16. Check x ≥ d: 12a+28b ≥ 27a−17b ⟺ 45b ≥ 15a ⟺ a ≤
 3b ✓. Check x ≤ b: 3a ≤ 9b ⟺ a ≤ 3b ✓.

**Dog behind at noticing:** Dog at (11a−17b)/16 < d = (27a−17b)/64 ⟺ 17a < 51b ⟺
 a < 3b ✓.

**Catch in phase 3:** Verified: a − d > 2d + τ₂ ⟺ a < 3b ✓.

**Numerical check (a = 2b, b = 64):** d = 37, x = 52. At t = 96 = (128+64)/2: al
l three rabbits and the dog simultaneously arrive at position 64 (the burrow). ✓

**d = 38 fails:** S1 arrives at t ≈ 98.67 while the dog reaches the burrow at t
= 96, catching S1 at position ≈ 58.67 < 64. ✓

The three rabbits can at most be **(27a − 17b)/64** meters away from point B whe
n they notice the hunting dog.
