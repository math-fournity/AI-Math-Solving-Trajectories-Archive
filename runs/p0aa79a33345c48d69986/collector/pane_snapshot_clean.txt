**Single-strand adjacency.** Split the bases into two groups $G_1=\{H,M\}$, $G_2
=\{N,T\}$. Two bases are *forbidden* to be adjacent iff they lie in the same gro

$$S(x)=\{x\}\cup\{\text{bases in the group not containing }x\},$$
which has $|S(x)|=3$ for every $x$.

3=12$ such states. A transition $(a,b)\to(a',b')$ is valid iff $a'\neq b'$, $a'\

valid next states is $|S(a)|\cdot|S(b)|-\bigl|S(a)\cap S(b)\bigr|$ (subtract pai
