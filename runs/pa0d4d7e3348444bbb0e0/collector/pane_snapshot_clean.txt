## Problem

A point charge $q$ is placed at $(x_0, y_0, z_0)$ with $0 < z_0 < l$, between tw
o grounded conducting planes $z=0$ and $z=l$. Find the potential, investigate th
e convergence of the image-charge series, and justify two-fold termwise differen
tiation.

## Setup and Image Charges

The boundary conditions are $\varphi|_{z=0} = \varphi|_{z=l} = 0$. The method of
 images requires reflecting the charge (and each successive image) across both p
lanes repeatedly. This generates a doubly-infinite set of images:

- Charge $+q$ at $z = z_0 + 2nl$, for all $n \in \mathbb{Z}$ (the $n=0$ term is
the original charge),
- Charge $-q$ at $z = -z_0 + 2nl$, for all $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.

**Self-consistency check.** Reflecting $+q$ at $z_0+2nl$ across $z=0$ gives $-q$
 at $-(z_0+2nl) = -z_0 + 2(-n)l$ (second family, index $-n$). Reflecting $-q$ at
 $-z_0+2nl$ across $z=l$ gives $+q$ at $2l-(-z_0+2nl) = z_0 + 2(1-n)l$ (first fa
mily, index $1-n$). The two families close under both reflections, so the constr
uction is consistent.

## The Potential

Let $\rho^2 = (x-x_0)^2 + (y-y_0)^2$. The potential is

$$\varphi(x,y,z) = \frac{q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}\left[\fr
ac{1}{\sqrt{\rho^2+(z-z_0-2nl)^2}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{\rho^2+(z+z_0-2nl)^2}}\right
]. \tag{1}$$

**Boundary conditions.** At $z=0$: the first sum has $(z-z_0-2nl)^2 = (z_0+2nl)^
2$, the second has $(z_0-2nl)^2$; reindexing $n\to -n$ in the second sum makes t
he two sums identical, so $\varphi|_{z=0}=0$. At $z=l$: the first sum has $(l-z_
0-2nl)^2$, the second $(l+z_0-2nl)^2$; reindexing $n\to 1-n$ in the second sum g
ives $(l+z_0-2(1-n)l)^2 = (z_0-l+2nl)^2 = (l-z_0-2nl)^2$, so $\varphi|_{z=l}=0$.
 Both conditions are satisfied.

## Convergence of the Series

Fix a point with $\rho>0$ (or more generally any point not coinciding with an im
age charge). Set $u_n = z-z_0-2nl$, $v_n = z+z_0-2nl$. For $|n|$ large, $|u_n|,|
v_n| \sim 2l|n|$. The general term of (1) is

$$T_n = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\rho^2+u_n^2}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{\rho^2+v_n^2}}.$$

Using $u_n^2 - v_n^2 = (z-z_0-2nl)^2 - (z+z_0-2nl)^2 = -4zz_0 + 8nlz_0 = 4z_0(2n
l - z) \sim 8nlz_0$ for large $|n|$,

$$T_n = \frac{v_n^2 - u_n^2}{\bigl(\sqrt{\rho^2+u_n^2}+\sqrt{\rho^2+v_n^2}\bigr)
\sqrt{\rho^2+u_n^2}\sqrt{\rho^2+v_n^2}} \sim \frac{-8nlz_0}{(2\cdot 2l|n|)(2l|n|
)^2} = \frac{C}{|n|^3},$$

with $C = z_0/(l^2)$ up to sign. Since $\sum |n|^{-3}$ converges, **the series (
1) converges absolutely** (and uniformly on compact subsets avoiding the image c
harges) by the Weierstrass $M$-test.

## Two-Fold Termwise Differentiation

We verify that the series of first and second derivatives also converge absolute
ly and uniformly on compact sets away from the charges, which justifies term-by-
term differentiation.

**First $z$-derivative.** Define $g(s) = s/(\rho^2+s^2)^{3/2}$, so $\partial_z[1
/\sqrt{\rho^2+u_n^2}] = -g(u_n)$ (with $du_n/dz = 1$). Then

$$\frac{\partial T_n}{\partial z} = -g(u_n) + g(v_n) = g(v_n) - g(u_n).$$

For large $|s|$, $g(s) = s/|s|^3(1+\rho^2/s^2)^{-3/2}\operatorname{sgn}(s)\cdot|
s|/|s|^3\cdots$; more cleanly, $g'(s) = (\rho^2-2s^2)/(\rho^2+s^2)^{5/2} \sim -2
/s^3$. Hence

$$g(v_n)-g(u_n) \approx g'(u_n)(v_n-u_n) = \tfrac{-2}{u_n^3}\cdot 2z_0 \sim \fra
c{C_1}{|n|^3}.$$

So $|\partial T_n/\partial z| \le M_1/|n|^3$; the first-derivative series conver
ges absolutely and uniformly ($\sum |n|^{-3}<\infty$).

**Second $z$-derivative.** Now $\partial_z^2[1/\sqrt{\rho^2+u_n^2}] = g'(u_n)\cd
ot(-1)\cdot$... precisely, $\partial_z^2[1/\sqrt{\rho^2+u_n^2}] = (\rho^2 - 2u_n
^2)/(\rho^2+u_n^2)^{5/2} =: h(u_n)$, with $h(s)\sim -2/s^3$. Then $h'(s)\sim 6/s
^4$, and

$$h(u_n)-h(v_n) \approx h'(u_n)(u_n - v_n) = \tfrac{6}{u_n^4}\cdot(-2z_0) \sim \
frac{C_2}{|n|^4}.$$

So $|\partial^2 T_n/\partial z^2| \le M_2/|n|^4$; the second-derivative series c
onverges absolutely and uniformly ($\sum |n|^{-4}<\infty$).

The same estimates hold for $x$- and $y$-derivatives (each $\partial_x$ brings a
n extra factor of $(x-x_0)$ and an extra power of $(\rho^2+\cdot)^{-1/2}$, makin
g the decay even faster: $\partial_x T_n \sim |n|^{-5}$, $\partial_x\partial_z T
_n \sim |n|^{-5}$, $\partial_x^2 T_n \sim |n|^{-5}$).

**Conclusion.** On every compact subset of the slab that excludes the charge loc

differentiation of the image series is legitimate**, and the resulting series re

nabla^2\varphi$ may be computed termwise, yielding $-(q/\varepsilon_0)\delta(x-x
_0)\delta(y-y_0)\delta(z-z_0)$ inside the slab and $0$ elsewhere — confirming th
