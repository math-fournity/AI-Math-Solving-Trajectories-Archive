## Solution

We work in $R = \mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{10}]$, which is the full ring of integers of $\
mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{10})$ (since $10 \equiv 2 \pmod{4}$), with discriminant $D = 40$
 and minimal polynomial $f(x) = x^2 - 10$.

Since $(6) = (2)(3)$, we factor each rational prime separately by reducing $f(x)
$ modulo that prime.

### Factoring $(2)$

$$f(x) = x^2 - 10 \equiv x^2 \pmod{2}.$$

This is a square, so $(2)$ ramifies:

$$(2) = \mathfrak{p}_2^2, \qquad \mathfrak{p}_2 = (2,\, \sqrt{10}).$$

**Verification.** $\mathfrak{p}_2^2 = (4,\, 2\sqrt{10},\, 10)$. Since $10 - 2\cd
ot 4 = 2 \in \mathfrak{p}_2^2$, we get $\mathfrak{p}_2^2 \supseteq (2)$. Convers
ely every generator $4, 2\sqrt{10}, 10$ lies in $(2)$, so $\mathfrak{p}_2^2 \sub
seteq (2)$. Hence $\mathfrak{p}_2^2 = (2)$. ✓

$\mathfrak{p}_2$ is prime (maximal) since $R/\mathfrak{p}_2 \cong \mathbb{F}_2$.

### Factoring $(3)$

$$f(x) = x^2 - 10 \equiv x^2 - 1 = (x-1)(x+1) \pmod{3}.$$

Two distinct linear factors, so $(3)$ splits:

$$(3) = \mathfrak{p}_3\, \mathfrak{q}_3, \qquad \mathfrak{p}_3 = (3,\, \sqrt{10}
-1), \quad \mathfrak{q}_3 = (3,\, \sqrt{10}+1).$$

Both are prime since $R/\mathfrak{p}_3 \cong \mathbb{F}_3$ (via $\sqrt{10}\mapst
o 1$) and $R/\mathfrak{q}_3 \cong \mathbb{F}_3$ (via $\sqrt{10}\mapsto -1$).

**Verification.**
$$\mathfrak{p}_3\mathfrak{q}_3 = (9,\; 3(\sqrt{10}+1),\; 3(\sqrt{10}-1),\; (\sqr
t{10}-1)(\sqrt{10}+1)) = (9,\; 3\sqrt{10}+3,\; 3\sqrt{10}-3,\; 9).$$
Subtracting: $(3\sqrt{10}+3)-(3\sqrt{10}-3) = 6$, and $9 - 6 = 3$, so $3 \in \ma
thfrak{p}_3\mathfrak{q}_3$, giving $\mathfrak{p}_3\mathfrak{q}_3 \supseteq (3)$.
 The reverse inclusion is clear since every generator is divisible by $3$. Hence
 $\mathfrak{p}_3\mathfrak{q}_3 = (3)$. ✓

### Combining

$$(6) = (2)(3) = \mathfrak{p}_2^2\, \mathfrak{p}_3\, \mathfrak{q}_3 = (2,\sqrt{1
0})^2\,(3,\sqrt{10}-1)\,(3,\sqrt{10}+1).$$

**Norm check.** $N((6)) = |N_{\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{10})/\mathbb{Q}}(6)| = 36$, and $
N(\mathfrak{p}_2)^2\, N(\mathfrak{p}_3)\, N(\mathfrak{q}_3) = 2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot
3 = 36$. ✓

### Answer

$$\boxed{(6) = (2,\sqrt{10})^{2}\,(3,\sqrt{10}-1)\,(3,\sqrt{10}+1)}$$

where $(2,\sqrt{10})$, $(3,\sqrt{10}-1)$, and $(3,\sqrt{10}+1)$ are prime ideals
 of $\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{10}]$.
