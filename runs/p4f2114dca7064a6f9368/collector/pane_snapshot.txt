## Proof

Let $x = \frac{AO}{OA'}$, $y = \frac{BO}{OB'}$, $z = \frac{CO}{OC'}$.

**Barycentric setup.** Since $AA', BB', CC'$ are concurrent at $O$, let $O$ have
 barycentric coordinates $(u:v:w)$ with respect to $\triangle ABC$, normalized s
o $u+v+w=1$. Then $A'$ lies on $BC$, and the ratio along cevian $AA'$ is

$$\frac{AO}{OA'} = \frac{v+w}{u} = \frac{1-u}{u} = \frac{1}{u}-1.$$

Thus $x = \frac{1}{u}-1$, i.e. $u = \frac{1}{x+1}$. Similarly $v = \frac{1}{y+1}
$ and $w = \frac{1}{z+1}$.

**Key identity.** Since $u+v+w=1$,

$$\frac{1}{x+1}+\frac{1}{y+1}+\frac{1}{z+1}=1.$$

Clearing denominators by multiplying both sides by $(x+1)(y+1)(z+1)$:

$$(y+1)(z+1)+(x+1)(z+1)+(x+1)(y+1)=(x+1)(y+1)(z+1).$$

Expanding the left side:

$$yz+y+z+1+xz+x+z+1+xy+x+y+1 = xy+yz+xz+2(x+y+z)+3.$$

Expanding the right side:

$$xyz+xy+yz+xz+(x+y+z)+1.$$

Equating and canceling $xy+yz+xz$ from both sides:

$$2(x+y+z)+3 = xyz + (x+y+z)+1,$$

which simplifies to

$$xyz = (x+y+z)+2.$$

**Applying the given value.** We are told $x+y+z = 92$, so

$$xyz = 92 + 2 = \boxed{94}.$$
