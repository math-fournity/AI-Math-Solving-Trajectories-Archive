## Proof

Let $a = BC$, $b = CA$, $c = AB$, and let $x = PD$, $y = PE$, $z = PF$ be the pe
rpendicular distances from $P$ to the three sides $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ respectively.
 Since $P$ lies inside $\triangle ABC$, we have $x, y, z > 0$.

**Constraint.** The area $S$ of $\triangle ABC$ decomposes into the three sub-tr
iangles $PBC$, $PCA$, $PAB$:
$$S = \tfrac12 ax + \tfrac12 by + \tfrac12 cz, \qquad\text{i.e.}\quad ax + by +
cz = 2S,$$
which is a fixed positive constant independent of $P$.

**Lower bound.** We apply the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality in the form $(\sum u_i^2
)(\sum v_i^2)\ge(\sum u_i v_i)^2$ with
$$u_1=\sqrt{a/x},\ u_2=\sqrt{b/y},\ u_3=\sqrt{c/z},\qquad v_1=\sqrt{ax},\ v_2=\s
qrt{by},\ v_3=\sqrt{cz}.$$
Then $u_i^2$ gives the terms of $\dfrac{a}{x}+\dfrac{b}{y}+\dfrac{c}{z}$, $v_i^2
$ gives the terms of $ax+by+cz$, and $u_i v_i = a,\ b,\ c$ respectively. Hence
$$\left(\frac{a}{x}+\frac{b}{y}+\frac{c}{z}\right)(ax+by+cz)\;\ge\;(a+b+c)^2,$$
so
$$\frac{a}{x}+\frac{b}{y}+\frac{c}{z}\;\ge\;\frac{(a+b+c)^2}{ax+by+cz}\;=\;\frac
{(a+b+c)^2}{2S}.$$

**Equality condition.** Equality in Cauchy–Schwarz holds iff $u_i/v_i$ is consta
nt for all $i$, i.e.
$$\frac{\sqrt{a/x}}{\sqrt{ax}}=\frac{\sqrt{b/y}}{\sqrt{by}}=\frac{\sqrt{c/z}}{\s
qrt{cz}}\;\Longleftrightarrow\;\frac{1}{x}=\frac{1}{y}=\frac{1}{z}\;\Longleftrig
htarrow\;x=y=z.$$
Thus the minimum is attained exactly when $P$ is equidistant from all three side
s of $\triangle ABC$.

**Identification of the point.** The unique point inside a triangle that is equi
distant from all three sides is the **incenter** $I$ — the center of the inscrib
ed circle. Its common distance to every side is the inradius $r = S/s$, where $s
=\tfrac12(a+b+c)$ is the semiperimeter, and indeed $ar+br+cr = r(a+b+c)=2S$ conf
irms the constraint is satisfied. The incenter lies strictly inside $\triangle A
BC$, so it is an admissible point.

**Minimum value.** At the incenter, $x=y=z=r$, giving
$$\frac{a}{r}+\frac{b}{r}+\frac{c}{r}=\frac{a+b+c}{r}=\frac{(a+b+c)^2}{2S}.$$

Therefore the sum $\dfrac{BC}{PD}+\dfrac{CA}{PE}+\dfrac{AB}{PF}$ is minimized un
iquely at the **incenter** of $\triangle ABC$, and the minimum value equals $\df
rac{(a+b+c)^2}{2S}$.
