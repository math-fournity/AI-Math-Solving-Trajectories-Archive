**Step 2: The cross product determines the inner product, forcing $\operatorname

The octonions are *alternative*: the associator $[a,b,c] = (ab)c - a(bc)$ is tot

$$x(xy) = (xx)y = -|x|^2 y.$$
Expanding $xy = -\langle x,y\rangle + x \times y$:
$$x(xy) = x\bigl(-\langle x,y\rangle + x \times y\bigr) = -\langle x,y\rangle\,
x + x(x \times y).$$
Taking imaginary parts (noting $-\langle x,y\rangle x + x(x\times y)$ is already

$$\boxed{x \times (x \times y) = -|x|^2\, y + \langle x,y\rangle\, x.}$$

Now suppose $A \in \operatorname{Stab}(\times)$. Applying $A$ to both sides:
$$Ax \times (Ax \times Ay) = -|x|^2\, Ay + \langle x,y\rangle\, Ax.$$
But applying the identity directly with $Ax, Ay$:
$$Ax \times (Ax \times Ay) = -|Ax|^2\, Ay + \langle Ax, Ay\rangle\, Ax.$$

For linearly independent $x, y$ (so $Ax, Ay$ are also linearly independent since

$$|x|^2 = |Ax|^2, \qquad \langle x,y\rangle = \langle Ax, Ay\rangle.$$

By bilinear continuation, this holds for all $x, y$. Therefore $A$ preserves the
