Looking at this problem, I need to find the minimal number of languages such tha
t 70 people can each have a distinct "language profile" where any two people dif
fer in at least one language.

## Proof

**Model.** Assign to each person the set of languages they speak. The condition
states:

> For any two individuals, there is at least one language spoken by one and not
by the other.

This is equivalent to requiring that **all 70 language sets are pairwise distinc
t**.

*Why equivalent:* If two people had identical language sets, then every language
 spoken by one is also spoken by the other — contradicting the condition. Conver
sely, if two sets differ, some language lies in exactly one of them, satisfying
the condition.

**Lower bound.** With $n$ languages, the number of distinct subsets is $2^n$. To
 accommodate 70 pairwise distinct subsets, we need
$$2^n \geq 70.$$
Since $2^6 = 64 < 70$ and $2^7 = 128 \geq 70$, we obtain $n \geq 7$.

**Upper bound (construction).** With $n = 7$ languages, there are $2^7 = 128$ di
stinct subsets. Assign any 70 of these subsets to the 70 people (one distinct su
bset per person). Since all assigned subsets are pairwise distinct, for any two
people their language sets differ, so there exists a language spoken by one and
not the other. The condition is satisfied.

**Conclusion.** The minimal number of languages is $\boxed{7}$.
