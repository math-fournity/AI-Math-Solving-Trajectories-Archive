## Proof

Let $(R, \mathfrak{m})$ be a commutative local ring with maximal ideal $\mathfra
k{m}$ and residue field $k = R/\mathfrak{m}$. In our setting, $R = \mathcal{O}_{
X,x}$ is the stalk of the structure sheaf at $x$, $\mathfrak{m} = \mathfrak{m}_x
$ is the maximal ideal of germs vanishing at $x$, and $k = \mathbb{C}(x) = \math
cal{O}_{X,x}/\mathfrak{m}_x \cong \mathbb{C}$.

We compute
$$\mathbb{C}(x) \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{X,x}} \mathbb{C}(x) = (R/\mathfrak{m}) \ot
imes_R (R/\mathfrak{m}).$$

**Key isomorphism.** For any commutative ring $R$ and ideals $I, J \subseteq R$,
 there is a natural isomorphism
$$(R/I) \otimes_R (R/J) \cong R/(I+J).$$

*Proof of the key isomorphism.* Apply the right-exact functor $(-)\otimes_R (R/J
)$ to the exact sequence $I \to R \to R/I \to 0$. Since $R \otimes_R (R/J) \cong
 R/J$ and the image of $I \otimes_R (R/J) \to R/J$ is precisely $(I+J)/J$, we ob
tain the exact sequence
$$(I+J)/J \longrightarrow R/J \longrightarrow (R/I)\otimes_R(R/J) \longrightarro
w 0,$$
which gives $(R/I)\otimes_R(R/J) \cong (R/J)\big/\big((I+J)/J\big) \cong R/(I+J)
$. $\square$

Applying this with $I = J = \mathfrak{m}$:
$$(R/\mathfrak{m}) \otimes_R (R/\mathfrak{m}) \cong R/(\mathfrak{m} + \mathfrak{
m}) = R/\mathfrak{m} = k.$$

Therefore
$$\mathbb{C}(x) \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{X,x}} \mathbb{C}(x) \cong \mathcal{O}_{X,x
}/(\mathfrak{m}_x + \mathfrak{m}_x) = \mathcal{O}_{X,x}/\mathfrak{m}_x = \mathbb
{C}(x).$$

**Conclusion.** Yes, the tensor product $\mathbb{C}(x) \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{X,x
}} \mathbb{C}(x)$ is still $\mathbb{C}(x)$. The reason is structural: $\mathbb{C
}(x)$ is the residue field of the local ring $\mathcal{O}_{X,x}$, and tensoring
the residue field with itself over the local ring collapses both copies of the m
aximal ideal, leaving a single copy of the residue field. This holds for any loc
al ring, not just the local ring of a complex manifold.
