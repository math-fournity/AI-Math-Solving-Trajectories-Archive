## Evaluation of $\operatorname{Li}_2(1/\phi)$

Let $\phi = \frac{1+\sqrt 5}{2}$ and set $a = 1/\phi$. The algebraic relations w
e need are

$$a = \phi - 1,\qquad a^2 = 1-a,\qquad 1/a = \phi,$$

where $a^2+a=1$ follows from $\phi^2=\phi+1$ (so $1/\phi^2 = 1-1/\phi$). Note $a
\in(0,1)$ and $\ln a = -\ln\phi$, hence $(\ln a)^2 = (\ln\phi)^2$.

### Three functional equations

We use three standard identities of the dilogarithm, each verified by differenti

**(R) Reflection** ($0<x<1$):
$$\operatorname{Li}_2(x)+\operatorname{Li}_2(1-x)=\frac{\pi^2}{6}-\ln x\,\ln(1-x
).$$

**(D) Duplication** ($|x|\le 1$):
$$\operatorname{Li}_2(x)+\operatorname{Li}_2(-x)=\tfrac12\operatorname{Li}_2(x^2
).$$
*(From the series: $\sum(1+(-1)^n)x^n/n^2 = 2\sum x^{2k}/(2k)^2 = \tfrac12\opera
torname{Li}_2(x^2)$.)*

**(L) Landen** ($0\le x<1$):
$$\operatorname{Li}_2\!\Bigl(\tfrac{-x}{1-x}\Bigr)=-\operatorname{Li}_2(x)-\tfra
c12\ln^2(1-x).$$
*(Verified by differentiating $f(x)=\operatorname{Li}_2(-x/(1-x))+\operatorname{
Li}_2(x)+\tfrac12\ln^2(1-x)$: one checks $f'(x)\equiv 0$, and $f(0)=0$.)*

**(N) Negation–reciprocal** ($x>0$):
$$\operatorname{Li}_2(-x)+\operatorname{Li}_2(-1/x)=-\frac{\pi^2}{6}-\tfrac12\ln

*(Verified by differentiating the LHS plus $\tfrac12\ln^2 x$; the derivative van
