# Problem

In a specialized logistics network, cargo shipments are categorized by their weight, represented by two positive integers: $n$ (which must be an odd weight) and $d$ (which must be an even weight not divisible by 3). A "heavy shipment trio" is defined by three consecutive weight classes in an arithmetic progression: $n-d$, $n$, and $n+d$, with the safety constraint that the base weight $n$ must be more than four times the weight interval $d$.

For each shipment weight $k$, engineers calculate a "Stability Factor," defined as the largest whole number divisor of $k$ that is strictly less than $k$ itself.

A shipment trio is classified as "Optimally Balanced" if the Stability Factors of the three weights $(n-d, n, n+d)$ also form a strictly increasing arithmetic progression in that specific order.

Given these parameters, determine the total number of distinct "Optimally Balanced" trios that can be formed such that the central weight $n$ is less than $24^2$.
