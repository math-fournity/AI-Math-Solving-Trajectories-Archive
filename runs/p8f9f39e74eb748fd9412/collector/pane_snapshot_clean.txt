The statement is **true**: if $T_n \to T$ in the operator norm topology, then $\
|T_n\| \to \|T\|$.

**Proof.**

Recall that the operator norm satisfies the reverse triangle inequality:
$$\big|\|A\| - \|B\|\big| \le \|A - B\|$$
for any bounded linear operators $A, B$. This follows from the ordinary triangle
 inequality: $\|A\| = \|(A-B)+B\| \le \|A-B\|+\|B\|$, giving $\|A\|-\|B\| \le \|
A-B\|$; swapping $A$ and $B$ gives $\|B\|-\|A\| \le \|A-B\|$, hence the absolute
 value bound.

Applying this with $A = T_n$ and $B = T$:
$$\big|\|T_n\| - \|T\|\big| \le \|T_n - T\|.$$

By hypothesis, $\|T_n - T\| \to 0$ as $n \to \infty$. By the squeeze theorem,
$$\lim_{n\to\infty}\big|\|T_n\| - \|T\|\big| = 0,$$
which is equivalent to
$$\lim_{n\to\infty}\|T_n\| = \|T\|. \qquad \blacksquare$$
