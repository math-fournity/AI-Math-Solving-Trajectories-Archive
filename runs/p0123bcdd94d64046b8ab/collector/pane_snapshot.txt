$\partial\Omega$ is piecewise $C^1$ and which satisfies the segment condition.
Then for every $1 \le p < \infty$ and every integer $k \ge 0$, the space

Step 1 — Finite open cover of $\bar\Omega$

Since $\Omega$ is bounded, $\bar\Omega$ is compact and $\partial\Omega$ is
compact. For each $x \in \partial\Omega$, choose $U_x$ and $y_x$ as guaranteed
by the segment condition. By compactness of $\partial\Omega$, finitely many such
neighborhoods $U_{x_1}, \dots, U_{x_N}$ cover $\partial\Omega$.

Set $U_0 := {x \in \Omega : \operatorname{dist}(x, \partial\Omega) > \delta}$

\bigcup_{j=1}^N U_{x_j}$ is a compact subset of $\Omega$, hence has positive
distance from $\partial\Omega$.

to ${U_0, U_{x_1}, \dots, U_{x_N}}$:

approximate each summand separately.

mollification $$(u,\psi_0)\varepsilon := \eta\varepsilon * (u,\psi_0),$$ where $
\eta_\varepsilon(x) = \varepsilon^{-n}\eta(x/\varepsilon)$ with $\eta \in
C_c^\infty(B_1)$, $\eta \ge 0$, $\int \eta = 1$.

\partial\Omega)$, we have $\operatorname{supp}((u\psi_0)\varepsilon) \subset
\Omega$, so $(u\psi_0)\varepsilon \in C_c^\infty(\Omega) \subset
C^\infty(\bar\Omega)$. By the standard properties of mollification (continuity

{W^{k,p}(\Omega)} \to 0 \quad \text{as } \varepsilon \to 0.$$
