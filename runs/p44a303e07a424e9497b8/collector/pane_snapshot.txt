## Proof

We are given that $f: \mathbb{C} \to D$ is analytic on all of $\mathbb{C}$ (enti
re), where $D$ is the unit disc, so $|f(z)| < 1$ for every $z \in \mathbb{C}$. T
hus $f$ is a bounded entire function.

By **Liouville's theorem**, every bounded entire function is constant. Since $f$
 is entire and bounded (by $1$), $f$ must be constant on $\mathbb{C}$.

We are given $f(10) = \tfrac{1}{2}$. Since $f$ is constant, $f(z) = \tfrac{1}{2}
$ for all $z \in \mathbb{C}$.

Therefore,
$$f(10+i) = \frac{1}{2}.$$
