**Answer: Yes, $\mathbb{P}(X \in K) = 1$.**

## Proof

Since $X_n \to X$ in $L^2$, we have $X_n \to X$ in probability, which implies $X
_n \to X$ in distribution.

By the **Portmanteau theorem**, convergence in distribution $X_n \xrightarrow{d}
 X$ is equivalent to: for every closed set $F \subseteq \mathbb{R}$,

$$\mathbb{P}(X \in F) \geq \limsup_{n \to \infty} \mathbb{P}(X_n \in F).$$

Apply this with $F = K$, which is closed by hypothesis. Since $\mathbb{P}(X_n \i
n K) = 1$ for every $n$,

$$\mathbb{P}(X \in K) \geq \limsup_{n \to \infty} \mathbb{P}(X_n \in K) = \limsu
p_{n \to \infty} 1 = 1.$$

Since probabilities are at most $1$, we conclude

$$\mathbb{P}(X \in K) = 1. \qquad \blacksquare$$
