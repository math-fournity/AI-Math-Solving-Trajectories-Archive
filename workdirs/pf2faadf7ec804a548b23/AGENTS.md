# Problem

In the city of Aethelgard, the Royal Archivist is tasked with storing 2012 enchanted satchels into a set of $k$ mystical vaults. Each satchel contains various colored gemstones. To ensure the magical stability of the vaults, the Archivist must follow a strict safety protocol. For every vault, at least one of the following two conditions must be satisfied:

i. **Uniformity:** There exists at least one specific gemstone color that is present in every single satchel stored within that vault.  
ii. **Uniqueness:** For every satchel in the vault, there is at least one gemstone color inside it that does not appear in any other satchel within that same vault.

The Archivist does not know beforehand which colors or how many gemstones are in any given satchel. Determine the smallest number of vaults $k$ that guarantees the Archivist can successfully distribute the 2012 satchels according to these rules, regardless of how the gemstones are distributed among the bags.
