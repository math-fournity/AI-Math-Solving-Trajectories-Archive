## Solution

We want to compute
$$\sum_{w\in S_n}(-1)^{l(w)}\,n^{l(w)} = \sum_{w\in S_n}(-n)^{l(w)},$$
where $l(w)$ is the number of cycles of $w$ (including fixed points).

**Step 1: Reorganize by cycle count.** The number of permutations in $S_n$ with
exactly $k$ cycles is the unsigned Stirling number of the first kind $c(n,k)=\le
ft[{n\atop k}\right]$. Hence
$$\sum_{w\in S_n}(-n)^{l(w)} = \sum_{k=0}^{n} c(n,k)\,(-n)^k.$$

**Step 2: Use the generating function of unsigned Stirling numbers.** The unsign
ed Stirling numbers of the first kind satisfy the well-known identity
$$\sum_{k=0}^{n} c(n,k)\,x^k \;=\; x(x+1)(x+2)\cdots(x+n-1) \;=\; \prod_{i=0}^{n
-1}(x+i),$$
i.e. the rising factorial $x^{(n)}$. (This follows from the recurrence $c(n,k)=c
(n-1,k-1)+(n-1)\,c(n-1,k)$ with $c(0,0)=1$, which is exactly the recurrence obey
ed by the coefficients of the rising factorial.)

**Step 3: Substitute $x=-n$.** Setting $x=-n$ gives
$$\sum_{k=0}^{n} c(n,k)\,(-n)^k \;=\; \prod_{i=0}^{n-1}(-n+i) \;=\; (-n)(-n+1)(-
n+2)\cdots(-1).$$

The $n$ factors run through $-n,\,-n+1,\,\ldots,\,-1$. Factoring out $-1$ from e
ach factor,
$$\prod_{i=0}^{n-1}(-n+i) \;=\; (-1)^n\cdot n\cdot(n-1)\cdot(n-2)\cdots 1 \;=\;
(-1)^n\,n!.$$

**Step 4: Verification on small cases.**
- $n=1$: only $w=\mathrm{id}$, $l=1$; sum $=(-1)^1\cdot 1^1=-1=(-1)^1\cdot 1!$.
✓
- $n=2$: $\mathrm{id}$ ($l=2$) contributes $(-1)^2 2^2=4$; $(12)$ ($l=1$) contri
butes $(-1)^1 2^1=-2$; total $=2=(-1)^2\cdot 2!$. ✓
- $n=3$: $\mathrm{id}$ ($l=3$): $-27$; three transpositions ($l=2$): $3\cdot 9=2
7$; two 3-cycles ($l=1$): $2\cdot(-3)=-6$; total $=-27+27-6=-6=(-1)^3\cdot 3!$.
✓

**Conclusion.**
$$\boxed{\sum_{w\in S_n}(-1)^{l(w)}\,n^{l(w)} \;=\; (-1)^n\, n!}$$
