## Proof

**Setup.** The given ellipse is $E_0:\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1$ with $
a>b$, so its semi-axes are $a$ (horizontal) and $b$ (vertical), and $\text{Area}
(E_0)=\pi ab$.

**Largest inscribed rectangle.** For an ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{A^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{B^
2}=1$, a rectangle with sides parallel to the axes has vertices $(\pm x,\pm y)$
with $\dfrac{x^2}{A^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{B^2}=1$, and area $4xy$. By AM–GM,
$$\frac{x^2}{A^2}+\frac{y^2}{B^2}\ge 2\frac{xy}{AB}=1 \implies xy\le \frac{AB}{2
},$$
with equality at $x=\dfrac{A}{\sqrt2},\;y=\dfrac{B}{\sqrt2}$. Hence the maximum
rectangle area is
$$\boxed{2AB},$$
with half-side lengths $\dfrac{A}{\sqrt2}$ and $\dfrac{B}{\sqrt2}$.

**Iteration.** Starting from $E_0$ with semi-axes $(a,b)$:

- $R_1$: largest rectangle in $E_0$, area $=2ab$. Its full side lengths are $a\s
qrt2$ and $b\sqrt2$.
- $E_1$: ellipse inscribed in $R_1$ with axes equal to the sides of $R_1$, so se
mi-axes $\left(\dfrac{a}{\sqrt2},\dfrac{b}{\sqrt2}\right)$, area $=\dfrac{\pi ab
}{2}$.
- $R_2$: largest rectangle in $E_1$, area $=2\cdot\dfrac{a}{\sqrt2}\cdot\dfrac{b
}{\sqrt2}=ab$.
- $E_2$: semi-axes $\left(\dfrac{a}{2},\dfrac{b}{2}\right)$, area $=\dfrac{\pi a
b}{4}$.

Each step scales every semi-axis by $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$, so each new area is hal
ved. In general, for $n\ge 1$:
$$\text{Area}(R_n)=2ab\cdot\left(\tfrac12\right)^{n-1},\qquad \text{Area}(E_n)=\
pi ab\cdot\left(\tfrac12\right)^{n}.$$

**Summing.** The objects *created* by the process are $R_1,E_1,R_2,E_2,\dots$ (t
he original ellipse $E_0$ is given, not created; "the infinitely many rectangles
 and ellipses thus created" refers to these).

$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\text{Area}(R_n)=2ab\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\tfrac12\right
)^{n-1}=2ab\cdot\frac{1}{1-\frac12}=4ab.$$

$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\text{Area}(E_n)=\pi ab\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\tfrac12\ri
ght)^{n}=\pi ab\cdot\frac{\frac12}{1-\frac12}=\pi ab.$$

**Total:**
$$\boxed{4ab+\pi ab=ab(\pi+4).}$$

*Remark.* If one also counts the original ellipse $E_0=\pi ab$, the total become
s $2\pi ab+4ab=2ab(\pi+2)$.
