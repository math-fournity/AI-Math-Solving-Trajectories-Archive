# Problem

In a specialized digital archive, data packets are stored as sequences of exactly $2n$ decimal digits. A packet is classified as "Balanced" if its digits can be partitioned into two groups of $n$ digits each such that the sum of the digits in the first group equals the sum of the digits in the second group.

Let $p_n$ represent the probability that a packet of length $2n$, generated by a uniform random process, is classified as Balanced. As the packet length grows indefinitely ($n \to \infty$), the probability $p_n$ approaches a steady-state efficiency constant $c$.

An analyst investigates the rate of decay of the deviation from this limit by defining the error term $q_n = p_n - c$. There exists a unique positive decay ratio $r$ such that the ratio $\frac{q_n}{r^n}$ converges to a non-zero stability constant $d$ as $n \to \infty$.

Determine the value of $100c + 100r + d$.
