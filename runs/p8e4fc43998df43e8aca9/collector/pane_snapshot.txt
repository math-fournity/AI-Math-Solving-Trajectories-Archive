## Solution

I count every processor and every management controller as one active computing
unit, cluster by cluster.

| Cluster | Processors | Mgmt controllers | Units in this cluster |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo-Unit (×1) | 1 | 0 | 1 |
| Dual-Core (×2) | 2 each → 4 | 0 | 4 |
| Enhanced Dual-Core (×1) | 2 | 1 | 3 |
| Quad-Array (×1) | 4 | 0 | 4 |
| Managed Quad-Array (×1) | 4 | 1 | 5 |
| Managed Octo-Array (×1) | 8 | 1 | 9 |

Summing the right-hand column:

1 + 4 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 9 = 26

Therefore the total number of units that will start the task is **26**.
