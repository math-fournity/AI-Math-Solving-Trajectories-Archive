## Proof

Let $H \subset \mathbb{CP}^2$ be a line, so $H^2 = 1$. The points $P, Q$ are smo
oth points of $H$ (each of multiplicity $1$).

**First blow-up at $P$.** Let $\pi_1: X_1 \to \mathbb{CP}^2$ be the blow-up at $
P$ with exceptional divisor $E_1$, and let $L_1$ be the proper transform of $H$.
 The total transform is
$$\pi_1^* H = L_1 + E_1.$$
Since pullback preserves intersection numbers, $\pi_1^*H \cdot \pi_1^* H = H^2 =
 1$. Also $\pi_1^* H \cdot E_1 = 0$ (pulled-back class meets exceptional fiber t
rivially), giving
$$(L_1 + E_1)\cdot E_1 = 0 \implies L_1 \cdot E_1 = -E_1^2 = 1,$$
which matches the fact that $P$ has multiplicity $1$ on $H$. Now
$$(L_1+E_1)^2 = L_1^2 + 2L_1\cdot E_1 + E_1^2 = L_1^2 + 2 - 1 = L_1^2 + 1 = 1,$$
so $L_1^2 = 0$.

**Second blow-up at $Q$.** Since $Q \neq P$ and $Q \in H$, the point $Q$ lifts t
o a smooth point on $L_1 \subset X_1$, again of multiplicity $1$. Let $\pi_2: X
\to X_1$ be the blow-up at $Q$ with exceptional divisor $E_2$, and let $L$ be th
e proper transform of $L_1$ (equivalently, of $H$). The total transform is
$$\pi_2^* L_1 = L + E_2.$$
By the same computation, $L \cdot E_2 = 1$ and
$$L^2 = L_1^2 - 1 = 0 - 1 = -1.$$

**Conclusion.** Each blow-up at a smooth point of multiplicity $1$ on the curve
reduces the self-intersection of the proper transform by $1$. Starting from $H^2
 = 1$ and blowing up two such points,
$$L^2 = 1 - 1 - 1 = -1.$$

The self-intersection number of $L$ is $\boxed{-1}$.

### PROOF COMPLETE
