# Mean and Gaussian Curvature for a Monge Surface

## Setup

A surface given as a graph \(z = h(x,y)\) admits the Monge parameterization

\[
\mathbf{r}(x,y) = \bigl(x,\; y,\; h(x,y)\bigr).
\]

Denote partial derivatives by subscripts: \(h_x, h_y, h_{xx}, h_{xy}, h_{yy}\),
and let

\[
p := h_x,\qquad q := h_y,\qquad r := h_{xx},\qquad s := h_{xy},\qquad t := h_{yy
}.
\]

## Step 1 — First fundamental form coefficients

\[
\mathbf{r}_x = (1,0,p),\qquad \mathbf{r}_y = (0,1,q).
\]

Hence

\[
E = \mathbf{r}_x\cdot\mathbf{r}_x = 1+p^{2},\qquad
F = \mathbf{r}_x\cdot\mathbf{r}_y = pq,\qquad
G = \mathbf{r}_y\cdot\mathbf{r}_y = 1+q^{2}.
\]

The determinant of the first fundamental form is

\[
EG - F^{2} = (1+p^{2})(1+q^{2}) - p^{2}q^{2} = 1 + p^{2} + q^{2} =: W.
\]

## Step 2 — Unit normal

\[
\mathbf{r}_x\times\mathbf{r}_y = (-p,\,-q,\,1),
\]

so the upward unit normal is

\[
\mathbf{n} = \frac{(-p,\,-q,\,1)}{\sqrt{W}}.
\]

## Step 3 — Second fundamental form coefficients

Second derivatives:

\[
\mathbf{r}_{xx}=(0,0,r),\quad \mathbf{r}_{xy}=(0,0,s),\quad \mathbf{r}_{yy}=(0,0
,t).
\]

Therefore

\[
L = \mathbf{r}_{xx}\cdot\mathbf{n} = \frac{r}{\sqrt{W}},\qquad
M = \mathbf{r}_{xy}\cdot\mathbf{n} = \frac{s}{\sqrt{W}},\qquad
N = \mathbf{r}_{yy}\cdot\mathbf{n} = \frac{t}{\sqrt{W}}.
\]

## Step 4 — Gaussian curvature \(K\)

\[
K = \frac{LN - M^{2}}{EG - F^{2}}
   = \frac{\dfrac{rt}{W} - \dfrac{s^{2}}{W}}{W}
   = \frac{rt - s^{2}}{W^{2}}.
\]

Restoring notation:

\[
\boxed{\,K = \frac{h_{xx}\,h_{yy} - h_{xy}^{2}}{\bigl(1 + h_x^{2} + h_y^{2}\bigr
)^{2}}\,}.
\]

## Step 5 — Mean curvature \(H\)

Using the standard convention \(H = \tfrac{1}{2}(\kappa_1+\kappa_2)\):

\[
2H = \frac{EN - 2FM + GL}{EG - F^{2}}.
\]

Compute the numerator:

\[
EN = (1+p^{2})\frac{t}{\sqrt{W}},\qquad
-2FM = -2pq\frac{s}{\sqrt{W}},\qquad
GL = (1+q^{2})\frac{r}{\sqrt{W}}.
\]

Summing and pulling out \(1/\sqrt{W}\):

\[
EN - 2FM + GL = \frac{1}{\sqrt{W}}\Bigl[(1+p^{2})t - 2pqs + (1+q^{2})r\Bigr].
\]

Dividing by \(EG-F^{2}=W\):

\[
2H = \frac{(1+p^{2})t - 2pqs + (1+q^{2})r}{W^{3/2}}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{\,H = \frac{(1+h_x^{2})\,h_{yy} - 2\,h_x h_y\,h_{xy} + (1+h_y^{2})\,h_{xx
}}{2\bigl(1 + h_x^{2} + h_y^{2}\bigr)^{3/2}}\,}.
\]

## Verification of consistency

As a check, the principal curvatures satisfy \(\kappa_{1,2} = H \pm \sqrt{H^{2}-
K}\), and the discriminant identity

\[
H^{2} - K = \frac{(\kappa_1-\kappa_2)^{2}}{4}\ge 0
\]

holds with the formulas above. Two quick limiting cases confirm correctness:

- **Plane** \(h=\text{const}\): all derivatives vanish, giving \(H=0,\;K=0\). ✓
- **Sphere of radius \(R\)**, lower hemisphere \(h(x,y)=-\sqrt{R^{2}-x^{2}-y^{2}
}\): direct substitution yields \(H=-1/R\) (with upward normal on the lower cap)
 and \(K=1/R^{2}\). ✓

## Summary

For the Monge surface \(\mathbf{r}(x,y)=(x,y,h(x,y))\), with \(W = 1 + h_x^{2} +
 h_y^{2}\):

\[
\boxed{\,H = \frac{(1+h_x^{2})\,h_{yy} - 2h_x h_y\,h_{xy} + (1+h_y^{2})\,h_{xx}}
{2\,W^{3/2}}\,},
\qquad
\boxed{\,K = \frac{h_{xx}\,h_{yy} - h_{xy}^{2}}{W^{2}}\,}.
\]
