### Setup: $L^p$ as a dual space

$$
\Phi : L^p(\mathbb{R}^d) \xrightarrow{\;\sim\;} \bigl(L^q(\mathbb{R}^d)\bigr)^*,

$$

where $1/p + 1/q = 1$ (with the convention $1/\infty = 0$). This identification

| $p$ | $q$ | Dual relation |
|-----|-----|---------------|
| $1 < p < \infty$ | $1 < q < \infty$ | $(L^q)^* = L^p$ (reflexive) |
| $p = \infty$ | $q = 1$ | $(L^1)^* = L^\infty$ |

In both regimes, $L^p$ **is** a dual space (the dual of $L^q$), and therefore ca
rries the **weak-$\star$ topology** $\sigma(L^p, L^q)$.
