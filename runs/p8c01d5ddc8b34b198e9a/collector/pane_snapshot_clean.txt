## Proof

**Claim:** Yes. The number of points in $\varphi^{-1}(y)$ is the same for every
$y \in Y$; in particular it is bounded.

**Step 1: $Y$ is connected.**

Since $\varphi: X \to Y$ is continuous (covering maps are continuous) and surjec
tive, and $X$ is connected, the image $Y = \varphi(X)$ is connected.

**Step 2: The fiber cardinality function is locally constant.**

Define $n: Y \to \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$ by $n(y) = |\varphi^{-1}(y)|$.

Let $y_0 \in Y$. By definition of a covering map, there exists an open neighborh
ood $U$ of $y_0$ that is *evenly covered*:
$$\varphi^{-1}(U) = \bigsqcup_{\alpha \in A} V_\alpha,$$
where $A$ is some index set and each $V_\alpha$ is an open subset of $X$ mapped
homeomorphically onto $U$ by $\varphi$.

For any $y' \in U$ and any $\alpha \in A$, the restriction $\varphi|_{V_\alpha}:
 V_\alpha \to U$ is a homeomorphism, so there is exactly one point $x_\alpha \in
 V_\alpha$ with $\varphi(x_\alpha) = y'$. Since $\varphi^{-1}(U) = \bigsqcup_\al
pha V_\alpha$, every preimage of $y'$ lies in exactly one $V_\alpha$. Therefore:
$$\varphi^{-1}(y') = \{x_\alpha : \alpha \in A\}, \qquad n(y') = |A|.$$
This holds for every $y' \in U$, so $n$ is constant on $U$. Since $y_0$ was arbi
trary, $n$ is locally constant.

**Step 3: A locally constant function on a connected space is constant.**

Suppose $n$ is locally constant on the connected space $Y$. Pick any $y_0 \in Y$
 and let $c = n(y_0)$. Consider the set $S = \{y \in Y : n(y) = c\}$.

- $S$ is open: for any $y \in S$, there is an open neighborhood $U_y$ on which $
n$ is constant, equal to $n(y) = c$, so $U_y \subseteq S$.
- $S$ is closed: its complement $\{y : n(y) \neq c\}$ is open by the same argume
nt (it is a union of open sets on which $n$ is constant, each taking a value $\n
eq c$).

Since $Y$ is connected and $S$ is both open and closed with $S \neq \emptyset$ (
as $y_0 \in S$), we have $S = Y$. Thus $n(y) = c$ for all $y \in Y$.

**Conclusion.**

The cardinality $|\varphi^{-1}(y)|$ is a constant $c$ (a fixed cardinal number)
for all $y \in Y$. A constant function is bounded. Therefore the number of point
s in the preimage of each point of $Y$ is bounded.

**Remark.** The compactness and metrizability of $Y$ are not needed for this con
clusion; connectedness of $Y$ (which follows from connectedness of $X$ and surje
ctivity of $\varphi$) suffices.
