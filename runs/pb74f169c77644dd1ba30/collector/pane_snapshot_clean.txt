## Condition 1: Dihedral angle along $BD$ is $90°$

**Plane $C'BD$**: vectors $\vec{BC'}=(0,b,c)$, $\vec{BD}=(-a,b,0)$. Normal:
$$\mathbf{n}_2 = \vec{BC'}\times\vec{BD} = (-bc,\,-ac,\,ab)$$

The dihedral angle is $90°$, so $\mathbf{n}_1\cdot\mathbf{n}_2=0$:
$$b^2c^2 + a^2c^2 - a^2b^2 = 0 \implies c^2(a^2+b^2)=a^2b^2 \tag{1}$$

## Condition 2: Dihedral angle along $B'C$ is $60°$
