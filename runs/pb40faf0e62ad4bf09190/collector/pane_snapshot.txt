Same degree. Every $K_H$ is a cyclic extension of $\mathbb{Q}$ of degree $5$,
since $H$ has index $5$ in $(\mathbb{Z}/341\mathbb{Z})^*$.

Same simple Galois group. Each $K_H$ is Galois over $\mathbb{Q}$ (being a
