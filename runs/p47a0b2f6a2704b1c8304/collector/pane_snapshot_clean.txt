## Proof

**Claim:** Yes, there exists a line bundle $L$ on $X$ of degree $g-1$ with $H^0(
X, L) = 0$.

**Argument.**

Consider the Picard variety $\operatorname{Pic}^{g-1}(X)$ parametrizing line bun
dles of degree $g-1$ on $X$. This is an abelian variety (a torsor under $\operat
orname{Pic}^0(X) = \operatorname{Jac}(X)$) of dimension $g$.

Define the **theta divisor**
$$W_{g-1} := \{L \in \operatorname{Pic}^{g-1}(X) : H^0(X, L) \neq 0\}.$$
A line bundle $L$ of degree $g-1$ satisfies $H^0(X,L) = 0$ if and only if $L \no
tin W_{g-1}$. We show $W_{g-1}$ is a proper subset of $\operatorname{Pic}^{g-1}(
X)$.

Consider the $(g-1)$-st symmetric power $X^{(g-1)}$, the variety parametrizing e
ffective divisors of degree $g-1$ on $X$. It is a smooth projective variety of d
imension $g-1$. The **Abel–Jacobi map**
$$u_{g-1} : X^{(g-1)} \longrightarrow \operatorname{Pic}^{g-1}(X), \qquad D \lon
gmapsto \mathcal{O}_X(D)$$
sends an effective divisor $D$ of degree $g-1$ to its associated line bundle. By
 definition, a line bundle $L \in \operatorname{Pic}^{g-1}(X)$ lies in $W_{g-1}$
 if and only if $L \cong \mathcal{O}_X(D)$ for some effective divisor $D$ of deg
ree $g-1$, i.e., if and only if $L$ is in the image of $u_{g-1}$. Thus
$$W_{g-1} = \operatorname{Im}(u_{g-1}).$$

Since $u_{g-1}$ is a morphism from a variety of dimension $g-1$, its image has d
imension at most $g-1$:
$$\dim W_{g-1} \leq g-1 < g = \dim \operatorname{Pic}^{g-1}(X).$$

Therefore $W_{g-1}$ is a proper closed subset of $\operatorname{Pic}^{g-1}(X)$.
Its complement $\operatorname{Pic}^{g-1}(X) \setminus W_{g-1}$ is nonempty (in f
act, dense and open), and consists exactly of those line bundles $L$ of degree $
g-1$ with $H^0(X, L) = 0$.

**Verification via Riemann–Roch (consistency check).** For any $L$ of degree $g-
1$, the Riemann–Roch theorem gives
$$h^0(L) - h^1(L) = \deg(L) - g + 1 = (g-1) - g + 1 = 0,$$
so $h^0(L) = h^1(L) = h^0(K_X \otimes L^{-1})$. The condition $H^0(X,L) = 0$ is
equivalent to $H^0(X, K_X \otimes L^{-1}) = 0$, i.e., $K_X \otimes L^{-1}$ is al
so outside $W_{g-1}$. The involution $L \mapsto K_X \otimes L^{-1}$ on $\operato
rname{Pic}^{g-1}(X)$ preserves the complement of $W_{g-1}$, consistent with our
conclusion. The generic line bundle of degree $g-1$ has no sections, as guarante
ed by the dimension count above.

**Conclusion.** Since $g > 2 \geq 1$, we have $g-1 \geq 1 > 0$, and the dimensio
n inequality $\dim W_{g-1} \leq g-1 < g$ holds. Hence $W_{g-1} \subsetneq \opera
torname{Pic}^{g-1}(X)$, and there exists a line bundle $L$ of degree $g-1$ on $X
$ with $H^0(X, L) = 0$. $\blacksquare$
