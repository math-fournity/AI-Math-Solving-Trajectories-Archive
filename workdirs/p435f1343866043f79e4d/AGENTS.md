# Problem

In the futuristic city of Neo-Factoria, the central power grid operates using "Factorial Cells." There are exactly 10 types of these cells, each producing a fixed amount of energy corresponding to $n!$ units, where $n$ ranges from 1 to 10 (i.e., $1!, 2!, 3!, \dots, 10!$).

An experimental drone requires a specific integer amount of energy to activate. However, the drone's energy core has a strict physical limitation: it can only house a combination of at most four cells at any given time. These cells do not need to be of different types; for instance, a core could contain four $3!$ cells.

The city council needs to determine the versatility of this drone model. They want to know how many distinct positive integer energy totals can be generated using a combination of at most four Factorial Cells, provided that the total energy sum is strictly less than the energy of a single $10!$ cell.

How many such unique positive integer totals exist?
