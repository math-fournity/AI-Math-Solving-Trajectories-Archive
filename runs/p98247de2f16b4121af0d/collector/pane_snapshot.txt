**Step 1: $\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}_p) = \mathbb{Z}_p$, so $M$

hi(1) = a \in \mathbb{Z}_p$, and by continuity $\phi(x) = ax$ for all $x$. Hence

local ring (maximal ideal $p\mathbb{Z}_p$). A local ring has no nontrivial idemp

**Step 2: $f$ is not an isomorphism.**

Multiplication by $p$ is injective ($\mathbb{Z}_p$ is a domain) but not surjecti

We claim $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}_p, \mathbb{Z}) = 0$. Let $\
psi: \mathbb{Z}_p \to \mathbb{Z}$ be $\mathbb{Z}$-linear. Then $\psi(p^n \mathbb
{Z}_p) \subseteq p^n \mathbb{Z}$ for all $n$, so $\psi$ is continuous from the c

hbb{Z}_p \supsetneq d\mathbb{Z}$, hence is not closed in $\mathbb{Z}_p$, hence n
