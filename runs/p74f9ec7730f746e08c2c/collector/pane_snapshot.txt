## Solution

The interior angle of a regular $n$-gon ($n \geq 3$) is
$$\theta = \frac{(n-2)\cdot 180}{n} = 180 - \frac{360}{n}.$$

We need $\theta$ to be an **even positive integer**.

**Integrality.** Since $180$ is an integer, $\theta$ is an integer iff $\frac{36
0}{n}$ is an integer, i.e., $n \mid 360$.

**Parity.** $\theta = 180 - \frac{360}{n}$ is even iff $\frac{360}{n}$ is even (
since $180$ is even). Now $\frac{360}{n}$ is even iff $\frac{360}{n}=2k$ for som
e integer $k$, i.e., $n = \frac{180}{k}$, which means $n \mid 180$.

So the parity condition reduces to $n \mid 180$. Note $n \mid 180 \Rightarrow n
\mid 360$, so integrality is automatic.

**Positivity.** $\theta > 0$ iff $\frac{360}{n} < 180$ iff $n > 2$, which holds
since $n \geq 3$.

**Reparametrization.** Set $m = \frac{180}{n}$ (a positive integer since $n \mid
 180$). Then
$$\theta = 180 - 2m, \qquad n = \frac{180}{m}.$$
The constraints become:
- $n \geq 3$: $\frac{180}{m} \geq 3 \iff m \leq 60$.
- $m \mid 180$ and $m \geq 1$.

Since $\theta = 180 - 2m$ is automatically even and positive for $m \leq 60 < 90
$, we simply need:

**Count the divisors $m$ of $180$ with $1 \leq m \leq 60$.**

The prime factorization is $180 = 2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5$, giving $(2+1)(2+1)(1+1
) = 18$ divisors total:
$$1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10, 12, 15, 18, 20, 30, 36, 45, 60, 90, 180.$$

Excluding $90$ and $180$ (both $> 60$) leaves **16** divisors.

**Verification of boundary cases:**
- $m=60$: $n=3$, $\theta = 180-120 = 60°$ ✓ (equilateral triangle)
- $m=1$: $n=180$, $\theta = 180-2 = 178°$ ✓
- $m=45$: $n=4$, $\theta = 90°$ ✓ (square)

Each of the 16 valid values of $m$ yields a distinct even angle $\theta = 180-2m
$, ranging from $178°$ down to $60°$, and a valid regular polygon with $n = 180/
m \geq 3$ sides.

$$|X| = \boxed{16}$$
