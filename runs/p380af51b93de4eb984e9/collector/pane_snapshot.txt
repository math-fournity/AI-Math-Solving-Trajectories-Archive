## Proof

**Definition (Bessel sequence).** A sequence $\{f_n\}_{n\in I}$ in a Hilbert spa
ce $H$ is a *Bessel sequence* if there exists a constant $B>0$ such that for eve
ry $f\in H$,
$$\sum_{n\in I}|\langle f,f_n\rangle|^{2}\;\leq\;B\,\|f\|^{2}.$$

Such a constant $B$ is called a *Bessel bound* for the sequence.

---

**The statement is TRUE.** It is, in fact, precisely the logical negation of the
 Bessel property. We verify this by a direct quantifier-negation argument.

Let $P(B,f)$ denote the proposition
$$\sum_{n\in I}|\langle f,f_n\rangle|^{2}\;\leq\;B\,\|f\|^{2},$$
where we adopt the convention that if the series on the left diverges, it takes
the value $+\infty$, and the inequality $+\infty\leq B\|f\|^2$ is false.

The Bessel property is the assertion:
$$\exists\, B>0\;\;\forall\, f\in H:\quad P(B,f).$$

Its **negation** (which is exactly the hypothesis "the sequence does not qualify
 as a Bessel sequence") is, by the standard rules of quantifier negation ($\neg\
exists = \forall\neg$ and $\neg\forall = \exists\neg$):
$$\forall\, B>0\;\;\exists\, f\in H:\quad \neg\, P(B,f),$$
i.e.,
$$\forall\, B>0\;\;\exists\, f\in H:\quad \sum_{n\in I}|\langle f,f_n\rangle|^{2
}\;>\;B\,\|f\|^{2}.$$

It remains only to confirm that the witnessing vector $f$ is **non-zero**. Obser
ve that for $f=0$ we have $\|f\|^2=0$ and $|\langle 0,f_n\rangle|^2=0$ for every
 $n$, so both sides of the inequality equal $0$, giving $0>0$, which is false. T
herefore the vector $f$ whose existence is guaranteed by the negation **cannot**
 be the zero vector; it is necessarily non-zero.

Thus the negation of the Bessel property is exactly:

> For every $B>0$, there exists a **non-zero** $f\in H$ such that $\displaystyle
\sum_{n\in I}|\langle f,f_n\rangle|^{2}>B\,\|f\|^{2}$.

This is precisely the statement in the problem. Since the statement is logically
 equivalent to "the sequence is not a Bessel sequence," and it is presented unde
r the hypothesis that the sequence is not Bessel, the implication

$$\text{not Bessel}\;\Longrightarrow\;\bigl[\forall B>0\;\exists\,f\neq 0:\;\tex
tstyle\sum|\langle f,f_n\rangle|^2>B\|f\|^2\bigr]$$

holds as a tautology. $\blacksquare$
