$$S = \int_0^1 t\,[u^m](1+u)^m(1-tu)^m\,du = [u^m](1+u)^m \int_0^1 t(1-tu)^m\,dt

Computing the integral:
$$\int_0^1 t(1-tu)^m\,dt = \frac{1 - (1-u)^{m+1}(1+(m+1)u)}{(m+1)(m+2)u^2}$$

$$S = \frac{-1}{(m+1)(m+2)}\left[\underbrace{[u^{m+2}](1-u)(1-u^2)^m}_{A} + (m+1
