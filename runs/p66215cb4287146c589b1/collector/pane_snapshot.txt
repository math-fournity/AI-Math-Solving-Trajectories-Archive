## Calculation of $H^*(\mathbb{RP}^n, \mathbb{Z}_2)$

**Answer.** As a graded ring,
$$H^*(\mathbb{RP}^n;\,\mathbb{Z}_2)\;\cong\;\mathbb{Z}_2[\alpha]\big/\!\big(\alp
ha^{n+1}\big),\qquad |\alpha|=1,$$
where $\alpha$ is the unique nonzero class in $H^1(\mathbb{RP}^n;\mathbb{Z}_2)\c
ong\mathbb{Z}_2$. Equivalently, $H^k(\mathbb{RP}^n;\mathbb{Z}_2)\cong\mathbb{Z}_
2$ for $0\le k\le n$ and $H^k=0$ for $k>n$, with $\alpha^k$ generating $H^k$ for
 every $0\le k\le n$.

---

### Proof

**CW structure.** Real projective space $\mathbb{RP}^n$ admits a CW decompositio
n with exactly one cell $e^k$ in each dimension $k=0,1,\dots,n$. Concretely, $\m
athbb{RP}^k\subset\mathbb{RP}^n$ is the $k$-skeleton, and $\mathbb{RP}^k$ is obt
ained from $\mathbb{RP}^{k-1}$ by attaching a $k$-cell via the quotient map $S^{
k-1}\to\mathbb{RP}^{k-1}$ (the antipodal identification on the boundary of the u
pper hemisphere).

**Cellular chain complex.** The cellular chain complex $C_*(\mathbb{RP}^n)$ with
 $\mathbb{Z}_2$-coefficients is
$$0\longrightarrow C_n\stackrel{d_n}{\longrightarrow}C_{n-1}\stackrel{d_{n-1}}{\
longrightarrow}\cdots\stackrel{d_1}{\longrightarrow}C_0\longrightarrow 0,\qquad
C_k\cong\mathbb{Z}_2\text{ for }0\le k\le n.$$

**The cellular boundary maps vanish over $\mathbb{Z}_2$.** The attaching map of
the $k$-cell is the 2-fold covering $S^{k-1}\to\mathbb{RP}^{k-1}$. Its degree (a
fter composing with the collapse map to the top cell of $\mathbb{RP}^{k-1}$) is
$1+(-1)^k$:
$$d_k = 1 + (-1)^k = \begin{cases}2 & k\text{ even},\\ 0 & k\text{ odd}.\end{cas
es}$$
Over $\mathbb{Z}_2$, both cases reduce to $d_k=0$ for every $k$. Hence the cellu
lar chain complex with $\mathbb{Z}_2$-coefficients has **all boundary maps zero*
*, and
$$H_k(\mathbb{RP}^n;\mathbb{Z}_2)\cong C_k\cong\mathbb{Z}_2,\quad 0\le k\le n;\q
quad H_k=0,\;k>n.$$

By the universal coefficient theorem (or directly, since $\mathbb{Z}_2$ is a fie
ld so cohomology is the dual),
$$H^k(\mathbb{RP}^n;\mathbb{Z}_2)\cong\mathbb{Z}_2,\quad 0\le k\le n;\qquad H^k=
0,\;k>n.$$

**Ring structure via Poincaré duality is not enough (RP^n need not be orientable
 over $\mathbb{Z}$, but it *is* $\mathbb{Z}_2$-orientable).** We determine the c
up product using the **inclusion of skeleta** and the **cohomology of $\mathbb{R
P}^\infty=K(\mathbb{Z}_2,1)$**.

**Step 1: $H^*(\mathbb{RP}^\infty;\mathbb{Z}_2)\cong\mathbb{Z}_2[\alpha]$, $|\al
pha|=1$.** Since $\mathbb{RP}^\infty=K(\mathbb{Z}_2,1)$, its cohomology with $\m
athbb{Z}_2$-coefficients is the group cohomology of $\mathbb{Z}_2$:
$$H^*(\mathbb{Z}_2;\mathbb{Z}_2)\cong\mathbb{Z}_2[\alpha],\qquad |\alpha|=1.$$
This is the standard computation: $H^*(B\mathbb{Z}_2;\mathbb{Z}_2)\cong\mathbb{Z
}_2[\alpha]$ where $\alpha$ is the degree-1 class corresponding to the identity
$\mathbb{Z}_2\to\mathbb{Z}_2$ in $\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb{Z}_2,\mathbb{Z}_2)\
cong H^1$.

**Step 2: Restriction to $\mathbb{RP}^n$.** The inclusion $i:\mathbb{RP}^n\hookr
ightarrow\mathbb{RP}^\infty$ (realizing $\mathbb{RP}^n$ as the $n$-skeleton) ind
uces on cellular cochains the **truncation** map: the cellular cochain complex o
f $\mathbb{RP}^\infty$ is $\mathbb{Z}_2$ in every degree $\ge 0$ with zero cobou
ndary, and the map to the cochain complex of $\mathbb{RP}^n$ is the projection t
hat keeps degrees $0,\dots,n$ and kills degrees $>n$.

Since all coboundary maps are zero (both for $\mathbb{RP}^\infty$ and for $\math
bb{RP}^n$ over $\mathbb{Z}_2$), this projection is already a map on cohomology:
$$i^*:H^k(\mathbb{RP}^\infty;\mathbb{Z}_2)\to H^k(\mathbb{RP}^n;\mathbb{Z}_2)$$
is an **isomorphism for $0\le k\le n$** and the zero map (trivially) for $k>n$.

Let $\alpha\in H^1(\mathbb{RP}^n;\mathbb{Z}_2)$ denote $i^*(\alpha_\infty)$, the
 image of the degree-1 generator. Then $i^*$ being a ring homomorphism and a rin
g isomorphism through degree $n$ gives:
$$\alpha^k = i^*(\alpha_\infty^k) = i^*(\text{generator of }H^k(\mathbb{RP}^\inf
ty)) = \text{generator of }H^k(\mathbb{RP}^n),\quad 0\le k\le n.$$

So $\alpha^k\neq 0$ for $0\le k\le n$, and $\alpha^{n+1}=0$ since $H^{n+1}(\math
bb{RP}^n;\mathbb{Z}_2)=0$ (the cochain group vanishes in degree $n+1$).

**Step 3: Conclusion.** Every class in $H^k(\mathbb{RP}^n;\mathbb{Z}_2)$ is a mu
ltiple of $\alpha^k$ (both are $\mathbb{Z}_2$, and $\alpha^k$ is the nonzero ele
ment for $1\le k\le n$). The only relation is $\alpha^{n+1}=0$. Therefore
$$\boxed{H^*(\mathbb{RP}^n;\,\mathbb{Z}_2)\;\cong\;\mathbb{Z}_2[\alpha]\big/\!\b
ig(\alpha^{n+1}\big),\qquad |\alpha|=1,}$$
a truncated polynomial algebra on one generator of degree $1$, with $\mathbb{Z}_
2$ in every degree $0$ through $n$ and $0$ above. $\blacksquare$
