Looking at this problem, I need to find the expected waiting time for the string
 **ABRACADABRA** (length 11) when letters A, B, C, D, R are generated uniformly
at random (each with probability 1/5).

## Method: Martingale / Gambler's Ruin Approach

**Setup.** At each second $t = 1, 2, 3, \ldots$, a new gambler enters and bets \
$1 that the letter generated at time $t$ is **A** (the first letter of the targe
t). If correct, he receives \$5 (since $p = 1/5$, fair odds pay $5:1$) and parla
yes his entire fortune on the next letter being **B**, and so on through the pat
tern A-B-R-A-C-A-D-A-B-R-A. Each gambler plays until he loses (walks away with n
othing) or completes all 11 letters.

**Fairness.** Each individual bet is fair (expected gain zero), so by the Option
al Stopping Theorem, the expected total net gain of all gamblers up to the stopp
ing time $T$ (when ABRACADABRA first appears) is zero:

$$\mathbb{E}[T] = \mathbb{E}[\text{total payout to surviving gamblers at time }
T]$$

since exactly one new gambler enters each second with \$1, making total stakes p
aid in equal to $T$.

## Identifying Surviving Gamblers at Time $T$

At time $T$, the last 11 letters are A-B-R-A-C-A-D-A-B-R-A. A gambler who starte
d at time $T - k + 1$ survives if and only if the last $k$ letters match the fir
st $k$ letters of the pattern — i.e., the pattern has a **border** of length $k$
 (a proper prefix that is also a suffix).

**Finding all borders of ABRACADABRA:**

| $k$ | Prefix (length $k$) | Suffix (length $k$) | Match? |
|-----|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| 1 | A | A | ✓ |
| 2 | AB | RA | ✗ |
| 3 | ABR | BRA | ✗ |
| 4 | ABRA | ABRA | ✓ |
| 5 | ABRAC | DABRA | ✗ |
| 6 | ABRACA | ADABRA | ✗ |
| 7 | ABRACAD | CADABRA | ✗ |
| 8 | ABRACADA | ACADABRA | ✗ |
| 9 | ABRACADAB | RACADABRA | ✗ |
| 10 | ABRACADABR | BRACADABRA | ✗ |
| 11 | ABRACADABRA | ABRACADABRA | ✓ (full pattern) |

The borders have lengths **1, 4, and 11**.

**Verification by direct check of each surviving gambler:**
- Gambler starting at $T-10$: matches full 11-letter pattern → fortune $5^{11}$.
- Gambler starting at $T-3$: last 4 letters = ABRA = prefix of length 4 → fortun
e $5^{4}$.
- Gambler starting at $T$: last 1 letter = A = prefix of length 1 → fortune $5^{
1}$.
- All other starting positions fail at the first comparison (as shown in the tab
le).

## Computing the Expected Value

$$\mathbb{E}[T] = 5^{11} + 5^{4} + 5^{1}$$

This is of the form $p^a + p^b + p^c$ with:
- $p = 5$ (prime)
- $a = 11,\; b = 4,\; c = 1$ (distinct integers)

## Final Answer

$$p + a + b + c = 5 + 11 + 4 + 1 = \boxed{21}$$
