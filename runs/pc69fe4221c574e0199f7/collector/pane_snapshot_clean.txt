*Set $x=0,\, z=-y$:* $f(y)^3+f(-y)^3=0$, so $f(-y)=-f(y)$ (odd). Then (1) gives
$f(x^3)=f(x)^3\tag{2}$.

**Step 2: Reduce to Cauchy.** With $z=-x-y$ and using (2) and oddness:
$$f(x)^3+f(y)^3-f(x+y)^3=-3xy(x+y),$$

Since $(x+y)^3=x^3+y^3+3xy(x+y)$, defining $h(x):=f(x)^3-x^3$ yields
$$h(x+y)=h(x)+h(y),$$
so **$h$ is additive**.
