We prove positivity; complete positivity then follows by applying the same

1. Setup and identifications

Let $\pi_u^A:A\to\mathcal B(H_u^A)$ and $\pi_u^B:B\to\mathcal B(H_u^B)$ be the
universal representations. The enveloping von Neumann algebras are $$A^{}\cong
\pi_u^A(A)'' ,\qquad B^{}\cong \pi_u^B(B)''.$$ Under these identifications the
canonical embeddings $\iota_A:A\hookrightarrow A^{}$, $\iota_B:B\hookrightarrow
B^{}$ correspond to $\pi_u^A$ and $\pi_u^B$, and the weak$^$ topology $
\sigma(A^{**},A^)$ coincides with the $\sigma$-weak (ultraweak) topology on $
\pi_u^A(A)''$, likewise for $B$.

The bitranspose $\phi^{}:A^{}\to B^{}$ is, by construction, $\sigma(A^{},A^)$-
to-$\sigma(B^{**},B^)$ continuous; i.e. $\phi^{}$ is normal**. Moreover $
\phi^{}\circ\iota_A=\iota_B\circ\phi$, so $\phi^{}$ extends $\phi$.

2. Density of the positive cone (Kaplansky)

By the Kaplansky density theorem applied to $\pi_u^A(A)\subseteq\pi_u^A(A)''$,
the unit ball of $\pi_u^A(A)+$ is strongly dense in the unit ball of $
\pi_u^A(A)''+$. On bounded sets, strong convergence implies $\sigma$-weak
convergence. Hence

$$\iota_A(A_+)\ \text{is }\sigma\text{-weakly dense in }A^{**}_+.$$

Equivalently: every $x\in A^{**}+$ is the $\sigma$-weak (weak$^*$) limit of a
bounded net $(a\alpha)\subset A_+$.

3. Positivity of $\phi^{**}$

Let $x\in A^{}+$. By Step 2, choose a bounded net $(a\alpha)\subset A_+$ with
$a_\alpha\to x$ weak$^*$ in $A^{}$.

Since $\phi$ is completely positive, it is in particular positive, so $
\phi(a_\alpha)\in B_+\subseteq B^{**}_+$.

By normality (weak$^$-continuity) of $\phi^{}$, $$\phi^{}(x)=\phi^{**}!
\bigl(\text{w}^\text{-}\lim a_\alpha\bigr)=\text{w}^\text{-}\lim \phi^{**}
(a_\alpha)=\text{w}^\text{-}\lim \iota_B(\phi(a_\alpha)).$$

The positive cone $B^{}_+$ of the von Neumann algebra $B^{}$ is $\sigma$-weakly
closed (it is the set ${y\in B^{}{\mathrm{sa}}:y\geq 0}$, cut out by the $

Since $x\in A^{}_+$ was arbitrary, $\phi^{}$ maps positive operators to positive
operators. $\quad\blacksquare$
