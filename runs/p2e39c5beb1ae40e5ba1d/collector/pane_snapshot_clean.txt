## Solution

**Setup.** Let $\delta = \gcd(\alpha, \beta)$. Write $\alpha = \delta a$, $\beta
 = \delta b$ with $\gcd(a,b)=1$ and $1 \le a \le b$. Then $\Delta = \operatornam
e{lcm}(\alpha,\beta) = \delta ab$.

**Reformulation.** The equation $\delta + \Delta = 4(\alpha+\beta)+2021$ becomes

$$\delta(1 + ab) = 4\delta(a+b) + 2021 \implies \delta(ab - 4a - 4b + 1) = 2021.
$$
Factoring: $ab - 4a - 4b + 1 = (a-4)(b-4) - 15$, so
$$\delta\bigl((a-4)(b-4) - 15\bigr) = 2021.$$

\{1, 43, 47, 2021\}$.
