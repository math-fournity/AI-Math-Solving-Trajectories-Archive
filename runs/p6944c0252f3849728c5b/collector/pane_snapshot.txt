## Solution

**Setup.** The yard is a $20 \times 19$ rectangle (20 rows, 19 columns), indexed
 by rows $0,\dots,19$ and columns $0,\dots,18$. We tile it with squares of integ
er side length. A slab is **Type-O** if its side length is odd. The perimeter of
 a slab of side $s$ is $4s$, so minimizing the total Type-O perimeter is equival
ent to minimizing $4\sum s_i$ over all odd slabs, i.e., minimizing $S := \sum s_
i$.

**Parity obstruction (lower bound).** Consider the column-parity imbalance: the
number of cells in even-indexed columns minus the number in odd-indexed columns.

- The rectangle has 10 even columns and 9 odd columns, each of height 20, so the
 imbalance is $10\cdot20 - 9\cdot20 = 20$.

- An **even** square of side $2k$ covers $2k$ consecutive columns, exactly $k$ e
ven and $k$ odd, contributing **0** to the imbalance.

- An **odd** square of side $s=2k+1$ at column $y$ covers $s$ consecutive column
s. If $y$ is even, it covers $k+1$ even and $k$ odd columns, contributing $+s$.
If $y$ is odd, it contributes $-s$.

Since even squares contribute 0, the entire imbalance must come from odd squares
:
$$\sum_{\text{odd slabs}} \epsilon_i\, s_i = 20, \qquad \epsilon_i \in \{+1,-1\}
.$$
Therefore $S = \sum s_i \ge \left|\sum \epsilon_i s_i\right| = 20$, giving the l
ower bound:
$$\text{Total Type-O perimeter} = 4S \ge 4 \times 20 = 80.$$

**Construction achieving $S = 20$.** Split the $20\times 19$ rectangle into:
1. A $20 \times 18$ block (columns $0$–$17$), tiled entirely with $2\times 2$ sq
uares — a $10\times 9$ grid of 90 even slabs.
2. A $20 \times 1$ strip (column $18$), tiled with twenty $1\times 1$ squares —
all Type-O, each of side $1$.

**Verification.**
- The $20\times 18$ block: both dimensions are divisible by 2, so the $2\times 2
$ tiling is valid. All slabs are even (Type-E), contributing 0 to the Type-O per
imeter. ✓
- The $20\times 1$ strip: 20 cells, each covered by a $1\times 1$ slab (side 1,
odd). Sum of side lengths $= 20$, sum of perimeters $= 20 \times 4 = 80$. ✓
- **Row-parity check:** The rectangle's row imbalance is $10\cdot19 - 10\cdot19
= 0$. Even squares contribute 0. The twenty $1\times1$ slabs occupy rows $0$–$19
$: 10 at even rows ($+1$ each) and 10 at odd rows ($-1$ each), net 0. ✓
- **Column-parity check:** The strip is at column 18 (even), so all twenty $1\ti
mes1$ slabs contribute $+1$, totaling $+20$, matching the required imbalance. ✓

**Conclusion.** The lower bound $S \ge 20$ is tight, achieved by the constructio
n above. The minimum possible combined sum of perimeters of all Type-O slabs is:

$$\boxed{80}$$
