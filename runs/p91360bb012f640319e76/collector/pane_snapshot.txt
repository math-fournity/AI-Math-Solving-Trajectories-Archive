## Proof

We claim that $SO(n+1)/O(n) \cong \mathbb{RP}^n$, the real projective $n$-space.

### Step 1: Embed $O(n)$ into $SO(n+1)$

Define the map $\varphi: O(n) \hookrightarrow SO(n+1)$ by
$$\varphi(A) = \operatorname{diag}(A,\, \det A),$$
i.e., the block-diagonal matrix with $A$ in the upper-left $n\times n$ block and
 $\det A$ in the lower-right entry. This is a group homomorphism since
$$\varphi(A)\varphi(B) = \operatorname{diag}(AB,\, \det A\cdot\det B) = \operato
rname{diag}(AB,\,\det(AB)) = \varphi(AB),$$
and it is injective. Moreover,
$$\det \varphi(A) = \det(A)\cdot\det(A) = (\det A)^2 = 1,$$
so $\varphi(A)\in SO(n+1)$. Thus $O(n)$ is realized as a closed subgroup of $SO(
n+1)$, and the quotient $SO(n+1)/O(n)$ is a well-defined homogeneous space.

### Step 2: Relate to $S^n = SO(n+1)/SO(n)$

The group $SO(n+1)$ acts transitively on $S^n\subset\mathbb{R}^{n+1}$, with stab

diag}(A,1)$). Hence
$$S^n \cong SO(n+1)/SO(n), \qquad g\mapsto g\cdot e_{n+1}.$$

Since $SO(n)\subset O(n)$ (as the kernel of $\det$), we have the natural project

$$\pi: SO(n+1)/SO(n) \longrightarrow SO(n+1)/O(n),$$
which is a $2$-to-$1$ covering map with deck group $O(n)/SO(n)\cong\mathbb{Z}/2$

### Step 3: Identify the $\mathbb{Z}/2$-action on $S^n$

$$SO(n+1)/O(n) \;\cong\; S^n/\{x\sim -x\} \;=\; \mathbb{RP}^n.$$
