## Solution

**Setup.** Let $\nu_{n,d} : \mathbb{P}^n \hookrightarrow \mathbb{P}^N$ be the Ve
ronese embedding, where $N = \binom{n+d}{d}-1$, defined by all degree-$d$ monomi
als. Let $H \subset \mathbb{P}^n$ be a hypersurface of degree $b$, so $\dim H =
n-1$ and $H = V(F)$ for a homogeneous polynomial $F$ of degree $b$.

**Key property of the Veronese map.** The Veronese embedding is an isomorphism o
nto its image. Under pullback $\nu_{n,d}^*$, a hyperplane in $\mathbb{P}^N$ corr
esponds to a degree-$d$ hypersurface in $\mathbb{P}^n$ (since the coordinates of
 $\mathbb{P}^N$ are the degree-$d$ monomials in the coordinates of $\mathbb{P}^n
$).

**Computing the degree.** The image $\nu_{n,d}(H)$ has dimension $n-1$ in $\math
bb{P}^N$. By definition,

$$\deg(\nu_{n,d}(H)) = \#\bigl(\nu_{n,d}(H) \cap \Lambda\bigr),$$

where $\Lambda \subset \mathbb{P}^N$ is a general linear subspace of complementa
ry dimension $N - (n-1)$.

Such a $\Lambda$ is cut out by $N - (N-n+1) = n-1$ general hyperplanes $L_1, \do
ts, L_{n-1}$ in $\mathbb{P}^N$. Pulling back via the isomorphism $\nu_{n,d}$:

$$\nu_{n,d}(H) \cap \Lambda \;\longleftrightarrow\; H \cap \nu_{n,d}^*(L_1) \cap
 \cdots \cap \nu_{n,d}^*(L_{n-1}).$$

Each $\nu_{n,d}^*(L_i)$ is a degree-$d$ hypersurface in $\mathbb{P}^n$. Since th
e $L_i$ are general, these pullbacks are general degree-$d$ hypersurfaces, and t
heir intersection with $H$ is transverse.

**Applying Bézout's theorem.** The number of points in the transverse intersecti
on of a degree-$b$ hypersurface with $n-1$ general degree-$d$ hypersurfaces in $
\mathbb{P}^n$ is:

$$\deg(\nu_{n,d}(H)) = b \cdot \underbrace{d \cdot d \cdots d}_{n-1} = b\, d^{\,
n-1}.$$

**Sanity checks.**
- $n=1$: $H$ is $b$ points in $\mathbb{P}^1$; the rational normal curve $\nu_{1,
d}$ maps them to $b$ points of degree $b$. Formula: $b\,d^0 = b$. ✓
- $d=1$: $\nu_{n,1}$ is the identity; degree stays $b$. Formula: $b\cdot 1^{n-1}
 = b$. ✓
- $n=2, d=2, b=2$: a conic maps via the 2-Veronese $\mathbb{P}^2\to\mathbb{P}^5$
 to a curve of degree $2\cdot 2^1 = 4$; intersecting with one hyperplane pulls b
ack to a conic meeting the original conic in $4$ points. ✓

**Answer.** The degree of $\nu_{n,d}(H)$ is

$$\boxed{b\, d^{\,n-1}}$$
