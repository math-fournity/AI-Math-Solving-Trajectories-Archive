Looking at this problem, I need to count labelings of a 3×3 grid with positive i
ntegers, where the upper-left is 1, the lower-right is 2009, and each label divi
des its right and bottom neighbors.

## Setting Up the Structure

Label the grid:
```
a b c
d e f
g h i
```
with a = 1, i = 2009. The divisibility constraints are: b|c, b|e, d|e, d|g, c|f,
 e|f, g|h, e|h, f|i, h|i (and 1|b, 1|d trivially).

**Key observation:** Since c|f|i = 2009, we get c|2009. Since b|c, b|2009. Simil
arly d|g|h|2009 gives d|2009, and e|f|2009 gives e|2009. So **all seven free var
iables are divisors of 2009**.

## Prime Factorization Decomposition

Since 2009 = 7² × 41, every divisor is 7^α × 41^β with α ∈ {0,1,2}, β ∈ {0,1}. D
ivisibility of divisors corresponds to componentwise ≤ on exponents. The problem
 **splits independently** into:

- **7-part:** Count 3×3 arrays with entries in {0,1,2}, weakly increasing in row
s and columns, with (1,1)=0 and (3,3)=2.
- **41-part:** Count 3×3 arrays with entries in {0,1}, weakly increasing in rows
 and columns, with (1,1)=0 and (3,3)=1.

**Total = (7-part count) × (41-part count).**

## MacMahon's Formula

The number of plane partitions in an a×b×c box (equivalently, a×b arrays with en
tries in {0,...,c}, weakly increasing) is:

$$\prod_{i=1}^{a}\prod_{j=1}^{b}\prod_{k=1}^{c} \frac{i+j+k-1}{i+j+k-2}$$

**3×3×2 box** (entries in {0,1,2}): $\prod_{i,j} \frac{i+j+1}{i+j-1} = 3 \cdot 2
 \cdot \frac{5}{3} \cdot 2 \cdot \frac{5}{3} \cdot \frac{3}{2} \cdot \frac{5}{3}
 \cdot \frac{3}{2} \cdot \frac{7}{5} = 175.$

**3×3×1 box** (entries in {0,1}): $\prod_{i,j} \frac{i+j}{i+j-1} = 20.$

**3×3×0 box** (all entries equal): 1.

## Counting the 7-part (k = 2)

Using inclusion–exclusion on the corner constraints:

- Arrays with entries in {0,1,2}: **175**
- Subtract those with (1,1) ≥ 1 (all entries in {1,2}, a 3×3×1 box): **−20**
- Subtract those with (3,3) ≤ 1 (all entries in {0,1}, a 3×3×1 box): **−20**
- Add back those with all entries = 1 (a 3×3×0 box): **+1**

**7-part = 175 − 20 − 20 + 1 = 136.**

## Counting the 41-part (k = 1)

- Arrays with entries in {0,1}: **20**
- Subtract those with (1,1) ≥ 1 (all entries = 1, a 3×3×0 box): **−1**
- Subtract those with (3,3) ≤ 0 (all entries = 0, a 3×3×0 box): **−1**
- Add back (all entries = 1 AND all entries = 0): impossible, **+0**

**41-part = 20 − 1 − 1 + 0 = 18.**

## Final Answer

$$\text{Total} = 136 \times 18 = 2448.$$
