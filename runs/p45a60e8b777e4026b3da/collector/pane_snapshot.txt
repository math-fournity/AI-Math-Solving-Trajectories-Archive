## Proof

**Notation.** $\mathbf{CRing}_{\star}$ denotes the category of *pointed* commuta
tive rings: objects are pairs $(R, r)$ with $R$ a commutative ring and $r \in R$
 a distinguished element (the base point); morphisms $f : (R,r) \to (S,s)$ are r
ing homomorphisms $f : R \to S$ satisfying $f(r) = s$.

**Step 1. Identify $\mathbf{CRing}_{\star}$ as a coslice category.**

A pointed ring $(R, r)$ is the same data as a ring homomorphism
$$\varphi_{r} : \mathbb{Z}[t] \longrightarrow R, \qquad t \longmapsto r,$$
since $\mathbb{Z}[t]$ is the free commutative ring on one generator. A morphism
$f : (R,r) \to (S,s)$ is precisely a ring homomorphism $f : R \to S$ making the
triangle

```
        Z[t]
       /    \
   φ_r       φ_s
     /         \
    v           v
    R  -- f --> S
```

commute. Hence
$$\mathbf{CRing}_{\star} \;\cong\; \mathbb{Z}[t] \downarrow \mathbf{CRing},$$
the coslice (under-category) of $\mathbf{CRing}$ under $\mathbb{Z}[t]$.

**Step 2. Pass to opposites.**

For any category $\mathcal{C}$ and object $A$, there is a canonical isomorphism
$$(A \downarrow \mathcal{C})^{\mathrm{op}} \;\cong\; \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \
downarrow A,$$
where on the right $A$ is regarded as an object of $\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}$ (
the slice *over* $A$). Applying this with $\mathcal{C} = \mathbf{CRing}$ and $A
= \mathbb{Z}[t]$:
$$\mathbf{CRing}_{\star}^{\,\mathrm{op}} \;=\; \bigl(\mathbb{Z}[t] \downarrow \m
athbf{CRing}\bigr)^{\mathrm{op}} \;\cong\; \mathbf{CRing}^{\mathrm{op}} \downarr
ow \mathbb{Z}[t].$$

**Step 3. Translate through the anti-equivalence.**

The equivalence $\mathbf{Aff} \simeq \mathbf{CRing}^{\mathrm{op}}$ sends a ring
$R$ to $\operatorname{Spec} R$ and a ring homomorphism $R \to S$ to a scheme mor
phism $\operatorname{Spec} S \to \operatorname{Spec} R$. Under this equivalence,
 the object $\mathbb{Z}[t]$ of $\mathbf{CRing}^{\mathrm{op}}$ corresponds to the
 **affine line**
$$\mathbb{A}^{1}_{\mathbb{Z}} \;=\; \operatorname{Spec} \mathbb{Z}[t].$$

The slice category $\mathbf{CRing}^{\mathrm{op}} \downarrow \mathbb{Z}[t]$ is th
erefore carried isomorphically to the slice category of affine schemes over $\ma
thbb{A}^{1}$:
$$\mathbf{CRing}^{\mathrm{op}} \downarrow \mathbb{Z}[t] \;\cong\; \mathbf{Aff} \
downarrow \mathbb{A}^{1}_{\mathbb{Z}} \;=:\; \mathbf{Aff}_{/\mathbb{A}^{1}}.$$

Concretely, an object $(R, r) \in \mathbf{CRing}_{\star}$ maps to the affine sch
eme $\operatorname{Spec} R$ equipped with the structure morphism
$$\operatorname{Spec} R \;\xrightarrow{\;\operatorname{Spec}\varphi_{r}\;}\; \op
eratorname{Spec}\mathbb{Z}[t] = \mathbb{A}^{1}_{\mathbb{Z}},$$
i.e., an affine scheme over the affine line, and a morphism of pointed rings bec
omes a morphism of affine schemes compatible with the maps to $\mathbb{A}^{1}$.

**Conclusion.** Under the anti-equivalence $\mathbf{Aff} \simeq \mathbf{CRing}^{
\mathrm{op}}$, the category $\mathbf{CRing}_{\star}^{\,\mathrm{op}}$ of pointed
commutative rings (dualized) corresponds to the **slice category of affine schem
es over the affine line**:
$$\boxed{\;\mathbf{CRing}_{\star}^{\,\mathrm{op}} \;\longleftrightarrow\; \mathb
f{Aff}_{/\mathbb{A}^{1}_{\mathbb{Z}}}\;}$$
whose objects are affine schemes $X$ equipped with a morphism $X \to \mathbb{A}^
{1}_{\mathbb{Z}} = \operatorname{Spec}\mathbb{Z}[t]$, and whose morphisms are co
mmutative triangles over $\mathbb{A}^{1}_{\mathbb{Z}}$.
