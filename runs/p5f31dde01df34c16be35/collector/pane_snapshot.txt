Define the 2D projection vectors $u=(x_1,y_1)$, $v=(x_2,y_2)$, $w=(x_3,y_3)$.

**Edge constraints.** From $|AB|^2=|BC|^2$, $|AB|^2=|BD|^2$, $|AC|^2=|CD|^2$ (an
d using $|AB|^2=|AC|^2=|AD|^2=a^2$), we obtain the dot products:
$$|u|^2=a^2-1,\; |v|^2=a^2-4,\; |w|^2=a^2-9,$$
$$u\cdot v=\tfrac{a^2-4}{2},\; u\cdot w=\tfrac{a^2-6}{2},\; v\cdot w=\tfrac{a^2-
12}{2}.$$

**Determining $a$.** Since $u,v,w$ are 2D vectors, their $3\times 3$ Gram matrix
 $G$ has $\det(G)=0$. Setting $b=a^2$ and computing:
$$G=\begin{pmatrix} b-1 & \frac{b-4}{2} & \frac{b-6}{2}\\ \frac{b-4}{2} & b-4 &
\frac{b-12}{2}\\ \frac{b-6}{2} & \frac{b-12}{2} & b-9\end{pmatrix},\qquad \det(G
)=\frac{b^2(b-10)}{2}.$$
Since $b>0$, we get $a^2=10$.

**Volume via determinant.** The volume is $V=\frac{1}{6}|\det[B-A,C-A,D-A]|$. Th
e determinant expands (using the $z$-row $1,2,3$) as
$$\det = 3(u\times v)-2(u\times w)+(v\times w),$$
where $\times$ denotes the 2D scalar cross product. Using the identity $(p\times
 q)(r\times s)=(p\cdot r)(q\cdot s)-(p\cdot s)(q\cdot r)$, one computes each squ

$$(u\times v)^2=45,\quad (u\times w)^2=(v\times w)^2=5,$$
$$(u\times v)(u\times w)=-15,\; (u\times v)(v\times w)=-15,\; (u\times w)(v\time
s w)=5.$$
Then
$$\det^2 = 9(45)+4(5)+5-12(-15)+6(-15)-4(5)=405+20+5+180-90-20=500.$$
Equivalently (and more cleanly), the general formula gives $\det^2=5a^4=5\cdot 1
00=500$, so $|\det|=10\sqrt5$.

**Result.**
$$V=\frac{1}{6}\cdot 10\sqrt5=\frac{5\sqrt5}{3}.$$
