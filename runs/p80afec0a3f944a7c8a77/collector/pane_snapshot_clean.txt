**Answer: No, the arithmetic genus of a projective plane curve does not change w
hen the base field is extended from $K$ to $\bar{K}$.**

**Proof.**

Let $C \subset \mathbb{P}^2_K$ be a projective plane curve of degree $d$, define
d by a homogeneous polynomial $F \in K[x,y,z]$ of degree $d$. The arithmetic gen
us is
$$p_a(C) = 1 - \chi(\mathcal{O}_C) = 1 - h^0(C, \mathcal{O}_C) + h^1(C, \mathcal
{O}_C).$$

**Step 1: Computing $\chi(\mathcal{O}_C)$ via the standard exact sequence.**

The curve $C$ is a hypersurface of degree $d$ in $\mathbb{P}^2_K$, so we have th
e exact sequence of coherent sheaves:
$$0 \to \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^2_K}(-d) \to \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^2_K} \to \m
athcal{O}_C \to 0.$$

Taking Euler characteristics (which is additive in exact sequences):
$$\chi(\mathcal{O}_C) = \chi(\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^2_K}) - \chi(\mathcal{O}_{\
mathbb{P}^2_K}(-d)).$$

**Step 2: The cohomology of $\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^2_K}(m)$ is field-independe
nt.**

The dimensions of the cohomology groups $H^i(\mathbb{P}^2_K, \mathcal{O}(m))$ ar
e given by the standard computation (via Čech cohomology on the standard affine
cover), which yields:
- $h^0(\mathbb{P}^2, \mathcal{O}(m)) = \binom{m+2}{2}$ for $m \geq 0$, and $0$ f
or $m < 0$,
- $h^2(\mathbb{P}^2, \mathcal{O}(m)) = \binom{-m-1}{2}$ for $m \leq -3$, and $0$
 for $m > -3$,
- $h^1(\mathbb{P}^2, \mathcal{O}(m)) = 0$ for all $m$.

These are **binomial coefficients — pure integers** that do not depend on the fi
eld $K$. The Čech complex is built from localizations of the polynomial ring $K[
x,y,z]$, and its ranks are determined by combinatorial counts of monomials, whic
h are the same over any field. Therefore:
$$\chi(\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^2_K}(m)) = \binom{m+2}{2} - \binom{-m-1}{2}$$
is the same integer for every field $K$.

**Step 3: Invariance under base field extension (flat base change).**

Now consider the extension $C_{\bar{K}} = C \times_K \bar{K} \subset \mathbb{P}^
2_{\bar{K}}$. A field extension $K \to \bar{K}$ is **flat** (since $\bar{K}$ is
a free $K$-module, as $K$ is a field). By the **flat base change theorem** for c
oherent cohomology of proper schemes:
$$H^i(C_{\bar{K}}, \mathcal{O}_{C_{\bar{K}}}) \cong H^i(C, \mathcal{O}_C) \otime
s_K \bar{K}.$$

Since $\bar{K}$ is a free (hence faithfully flat) $K$-module, the dimensions sat
isfy:
$$h^i(C_{\bar{K}}, \mathcal{O}_{C_{\bar{K}}}) = \dim_{\bar{K}}\!\bigl(H^i(C, \ma
thcal{O}_C) \otimes_K \bar{K}\bigr) = \dim_K H^i(C, \mathcal{O}_C) = h^i(C, \mat
hcal{O}_C).$$

Therefore:
$$\chi(\mathcal{O}_{C_{\bar{K}}}) = \chi(\mathcal{O}_C),$$
and consequently:
$$p_a(C_{\bar{K}}) = 1 - \chi(\mathcal{O}_{C_{\bar{K}}}) = 1 - \chi(\mathcal{O}_
C) = p_a(C).$$

**Step 4: Explicit value.**

Computing directly: $\chi(\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^2}) = 1$ (only $h^0 = 1$) and
$\chi(\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^2}(-d)) = -\binom{d-1}{2}$ (only $h^2 = \binom{d-1
}{2}$ for $d \geq 1$). Hence:
$$\chi(\mathcal{O}_C) = 1 - \binom{d-1}{2} = 1 - \frac{(d-1)(d-2)}{2},$$
$$p_a(C) = \frac{(d-1)(d-2)}{2},$$
which depends only on the degree $d$ — a quantity unchanged by base field extens
ion.

**Conclusion.** The arithmetic genus $p_a = \frac{(d-1)(d-2)}{2}$ depends only o
n the degree $d$ of the defining polynomial, and $d$ is preserved under base fie
ld extension. More fundamentally, flat base change preserves the dimensions of a
ll cohomology groups of the structure sheaf, so the Euler characteristic — and h
ence the arithmetic genus — is invariant under extension from $K$ to $\bar{K}$.
