Looking at this problem, I need to determine whether the three conditions (pure
$n$-complex, every $(n-1)$-face shared by exactly two $n$-simplices, path-connec
ted polyhedron) force the "dual graph" of $n$-simplices (adjacency via shared $(
n-1)$-faces) to be connected.

**Answer: False.**

**Counterexample.** Let $n = 2$. Take two triangulated $2$-spheres $S_1$ and $S_
2$ (each a finite simplicial complex homeomorphic to $S^2$), and form a new simp
licial complex $X$ by identifying them at a single common vertex $v$ and sharing
 no other simplices.

**$X$ is a valid simplicial complex.** Any simplex from $S_1$ and any simplex fr
om $S_2$ intersect either at $\{v\}$ (if both contain $v$) or at $\emptyset$; in
 the former case $\{v\}$ is a face of both. All faces of every simplex are inclu
ded. So the intersection condition is satisfied.

**Condition (i) holds.** In each sphere, every vertex, edge, and triangle is a f
ace of some triangle ($2$-simplex). The shared vertex $v$ is a face of triangles
 in both $S_1$ and $S_2$. ✓

**Condition (ii) holds.** Every edge of $S_1$ is a face of exactly two triangles
, both lying in $S_1$ (since $S_1$ and $S_2$ share no edges—only the vertex $v$)
. Likewise every edge of $S_2$ is a face of exactly two triangles in $S_2$. No e
dge belongs to both spheres. ✓

**Condition (iii) holds.** The polyhedron $|X| = |S_1| \cup |S_2|$ with $|S_1| \
cap |S_2| = \{v\}$ is path-connected: from any point in $S_1$ one can travel to
$v$ within $|S_1|$, then from $v$ to any point in $|S_2|$ within $|S_2|$. ✓

**The dual graph is disconnected.** Two $2$-simplices are adjacent in the dual g
raph only if they share a $1$-dimensional face (an edge). Since $S_1$ and $S_2$
share no edges (only the vertex $v$), no triangle of $S_1$ shares an edge with a
ny triangle of $S_2$. The dual graph splits into (at least) two components: one
for the triangles of $S_1$ and one for the triangles of $S_2$.

Thus, taking $\sigma$ to be any triangle in $S_1$ and $\tau$ any triangle in $S_
2$, there is **no** sequence of $2$-simplices from $\sigma$ to $\tau$ with conse
cutive simplices sharing a $1$-face.

**Key observation.** Path-connectedness of the polyhedron only requires that the
 sub-polyhedra of dual-graph components intersect in *some* simplex, but that si
mplex can have dimension $< n-1$. Condition (ii) only forces dual-graph adjacenc
y through $(n-1)$-faces, so lower-dimensional intersections (like a shared verte
x) connect the polyhedron without connecting the dual graph.

Therefore the implication does **not** hold.
