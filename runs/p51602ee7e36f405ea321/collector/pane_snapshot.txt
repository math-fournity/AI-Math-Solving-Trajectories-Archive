## Solution

**Setup.** Measure hand positions clockwise from 12 o'clock, in degrees. At $t$
minutes past 12:00, the minute hand is at $6t \pmod{360}$ and the hour hand is a
t $0.5t \pmod{360}$.

---

### Part a) $T_1$ — first alignment after 9:30:00

At 9:30:00 (i.e., $570$ min past 12:00):
- Minute hand: $6 \cdot 30 = 180°$
- Hour hand: $0.5 \cdot 570 = 285°$

The hour hand leads the minute hand by $105°$. The minute hand gains on the hour
 hand at relative speed $6 - 0.5 = 5.5°/\text{min}$.

Let $t$ be minutes past 9:30. Alignment requires
$$180 + 6t \equiv 285 + 0.5t \pmod{360} \implies 5.5t = 105 \implies t = \frac{2
10}{11}\text{ min}.$$

This is the first positive solution (the next would add $360/5.5 = 720/11$ min).
 Converting to seconds:
$$T_1 = \frac{210}{11} \cdot 60 = \frac{12600}{11}\text{ seconds}.$$

---

### Part b) $T_2$ — first time the angle between hands equals the 9:30 angle

At 9:30:00 the angle between the hands is $|285 - 180| = 105°$.

Let $t$ be minutes past 9:30. The signed difference (hour $-$ minute) is
$$D(t) = 105 - 5.5t.$$
The angle between the hands is $\min\bigl(D(t) \bmod 360,\; 360 - (D(t) \bmod 36
0)\bigr)$, which equals $105°$ when $D(t) \equiv 105$ or $D(t) \equiv 255 \pmod{
360}$.

- $D(t) \equiv 105$: gives $t = 0$ (excluded) or $t = 720/11$.
- $D(t) \equiv 255$: $105 - 5.5t \equiv 255 \implies 5.5t \equiv 210 \implies t
= \dfrac{420}{11}$.

The smallest positive value is $t = \dfrac{420}{11}$ minutes. Verification: $D(4
20/11) = 105 - 5.5\cdot\tfrac{420}{11} = 105 - 210 = -105$, so $|D| = 105°$. ✓

$$T_2 = \frac{420}{11}\text{ minutes}.$$

---

### Part c) $T_3$ — first time past 3:00:00 with $30°$ clockwise from minute to
hour

At 3:00:00 (i.e., $180$ min past 12:00):
- Minute hand: $0°$
- Hour hand: $0.5 \cdot 180 = 90°$

Let $t$ be minutes past 3:00. The clockwise angle from the minute hand to the ho
ur hand is
$$(\text{hour} - \text{minute}) \bmod 360 = (90 - 5.5t) \bmod 360.$$

Setting this equal to $30°$:
$$90 - 5.5t \equiv 30 \pmod{360} \implies 5.5t \equiv 60 \implies t = \frac{120}
{11}\text{ min}.$$

This is the first positive solution (other solutions add $720/11$ min). Verifica
tion: $(90 - 5.5\cdot\tfrac{120}{11}) \bmod 360 = (90-60)\bmod 360 = 30°$. ✓

Converting to seconds:
$$T_3 = \frac{120}{11} \cdot 60 = \frac{7200}{11}\text{ seconds}.$$

---

### Final computation

$$T_1 + T_2 + T_3 = \frac{12600}{11} + \frac{420}{11} + \frac{7200}{11} = \frac{
12600 + 420 + 7200}{11} = \frac{20220}{11}.$$

Therefore
$$11 \cdot (T_1 + T_2 + T_3) = 11 \cdot \frac{20220}{11} = \boxed{20220}.$$
