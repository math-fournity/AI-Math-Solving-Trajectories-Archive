Solution

Setup. The 3-qubit Hilbert space is $\mathcal{H} = \mathcal{H}_1 \otimes
\mathcal{H}_2 \otimes \mathcal{H}_3$, each factor 2-dimensional. We use the
computational basis ordered as $$|000\rangle, |001\rangle, |010\rangle, |
011\rangle, |100\rangle, |101\rangle, |110\rangle, |111\rangle,$$ i.e. the index
of a basis vector $|ijk\rangle$ is $4i + 2j + k$. Equivalently, grouping the
first two qubits into a single 4-level index $n = 2i+j \in {0,1,2,3}$ and
keeping the third-qubit index $k \in {0,1}$, the 8-dimensional index is $(n,k)$
with linear position $2n + k$.

The operation. The 4×4 unitary $U = (u_{nm})$ acts on qubits 1 and 2 jointly
(the index $n = 2i+j$). Qubit 3 is left untouched, so it is acted on by the
identity $I_2$. The total operator on $\mathcal{H}$ is therefore the Kronecker
product $$\boxed{M ;=; U \otimes I_2}$$ where $I_2 = \begin{pmatrix}
1&0\0&1\end{pmatrix}$.

Explicit matrix. Writing each entry $u_{nm}$ as a $2\times 2$ block $u_{nm}
I_2$, the $8\times 8$ matrix in the basis above is

$$ M = \begin{pmatrix} u_{00} & 0      & u_{01} & 0      & u_{02} & 0      &
u_{03} & 0      \ 0      & u_{00} & 0      & u_{01} & 0      & u_{02} & 0      &
u_{03} \ u_{10} & 0      & u_{11} & 0      & u_{12} & 0      & u_{13} & 0      \
0      & u_{10} & 0      & u_{11} & 0      & u_{12} & 0      & u_{13} \ u_{20} &
0      & u_{21} & 0      & u_{22} & 0      & u_{23} & 0      \ 0      & u_{20} &
0      & u_{21} & 0      & u_{22} & 0      & u_{23} \ u_{30} & 0      & u_{31} &
0      & u_{32} & 0      & u_{33} & 0      \ 0      & u_{30} & 0      & u_{31} &
0      & u_{32} & 0      & u_{33} \end{pmatrix}. $$

Equivalently, in closed form, $$M_{(n,k),(m,l)} ;=; u_{nm},\delta_{kl}, \qquad
n,m\in{0,1,2,3},; k,l\in{0,1}.$$

a_{n\bmod 2;\text{div}}, b_{(\cdot),k}$; more precisely, with $n=2i+j$, $
