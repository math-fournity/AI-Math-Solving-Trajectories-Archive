# Problem

A high-tech research facility is built in the shape of a perfect regular nonadecagon (19-sided polygon), with 19 security pylons labeled $P_{1}, P_{2}, \dots, P_{19}$ positioned at its vertices in clockwise order.

A specialized laser sensor is mounted at pylon $P_{1}$ and aimed directly in a straight line toward pylon $P_{5}$. Simultaneously, a second laser is mounted at pylon $P_{4}$ and aimed directly back toward pylon $P_{3}$. Because the perimeter walls are not parallel, these two laser beams eventually intersect at a calibration point designated as $X$.

An engineer stands at this intersection point $X$ and uses a transit to measure the angular spread between two specific targets. The engineer first sights pylon $P_{7}$ and then rotates the transit to sight pylon $P_{5}$. 

What is the measure of the angle $\angle P_{7} X P_{5}$ in degrees?
