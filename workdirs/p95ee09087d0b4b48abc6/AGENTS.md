# Problem

In a remote digital archipelago, there are $N$ distinct server hubs, each managed exclusively by one of $N$ different independent tech moguls. To facilitate data transfers, each mogul installed a series of private fiber-optic cables between pairs of hubs. A mogul may lay at most one cable between any two specific hubs, though a single pair of hubs may be linked by multiple cables if each cable belongs to a different mogul.

Across the entire archipelago, a total of $d$ cables were installed.

A group of moguls is said to be "fully synchronized" if they can pool their private cables such that, using only their collective infrastructure, data can be transmitted between any two server hubs in the archipelago (even if the data packets must pass through hubs managed by moguls outside the group). 

After a diagnostic audit, it was discovered that it is impossible for any group of fewer than $N$ moguls to be fully synchronized. 

Under these constraints, what is the largest possible value of $d$?
