## Computing the Probability

We select two distinct terms at random from the 2008-term sequence. The product
is **positive** if and only if both selected terms are positive (there are no ne
gative terms, and any product involving the zero term equals 0, which is not pos
itive).

**Favorable pairs** (both terms positive):
$$\binom{2007}{2} = \frac{2007 \cdot 2006}{2}$$

**Total pairs:**
$$\binom{2008}{2} = \frac{2008 \cdot 2007}{2}$$

**Probability:**
$$P = \frac{\binom{2007}{2}}{\binom{2008}{2}} = \frac{2007 \cdot 2006}{2008 \cdo
t 2007} = \frac{2006}{2008} = \frac{1003}{1004}$$
