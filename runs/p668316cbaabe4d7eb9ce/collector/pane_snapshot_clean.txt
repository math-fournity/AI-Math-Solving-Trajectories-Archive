Verified for $m=2$: $B = -(a_1+a_2)$ ✓; $m=3$: $B = a_1 + 2a_2 + a_3$ ✓; $m=4$:

By **Lucas' theorem**, $\binom{3n-1}{j} \not\equiv 0 \pmod{3}$ iff each base-3 d

**Case 2: $n$ odd** ($3n-1$ even, $(-1)^{3n-1} = 1$). Then $c_1 = c_{3n} = 2$, a

By Lucas' theorem, $\binom{3n-1}{j} \equiv \prod_i \binom{d_i}{j_i} \pmod{3}$. E

- $d_i = 0$: only $j_i=0$ → factor $1$
- $d_i = 1$: $j_i \in \{0,1\}$ → factors $1, 1$
- $d_i = 2$: $j_i \in \{0,1,2\}$ → factors $1, 2, 1$

4 \equiv 1 \neq 2$).
