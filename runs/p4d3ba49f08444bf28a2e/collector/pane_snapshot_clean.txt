## Solution

We want to evaluate
$$\mathbb{Var}\!\left(\int_{0}^{t}\bigl((B_s)^2+s\bigr)\,dB_s\right),$$
where $B_s$ is a standard Brownian motion.

**Step 1: Itô isometry.** The integrand $f_s := (B_s)^2+s$ is adapted and square
-integrable on $[0,t]$ (since $B_s$ is Gaussian with all moments finite on bound
ed intervals). Hence
$$M_t := \int_{0}^{t}\bigl((B_s)^2+s\bigr)\,dB_s$$
is a martingale with $M_0=0$. In particular $\mathbb{E}[M_t]=0$, so
$$\mathbb{Var}(M_t)=\mathbb{E}[M_t^2].$$
By the Itô isometry,
$$\mathbb{E}[M_t^2]=\mathbb{E}\!\left[\int_{0}^{t} f_s^2\,ds\right]=\int_{0}^{t}
\mathbb{E}\bigl[f_s^2\bigr]\,ds,$$
where the interchange of expectation and integral is justified by Fubini–Tonelli
 (the integrand is non-negative).

**Step 2: Compute $\mathbb{E}[f_s^2]$.** We have
$$f_s^2=\bigl((B_s)^2+s\bigr)^2=(B_s)^4+2s(B_s)^2+s^2.$$
Since $B_s\sim\mathcal{N}(0,s)$, the standard Gaussian moments give
$$\mathbb{E}[(B_s)^2]=s,\qquad \mathbb{E}[(B_s)^4]=3s^2.$$
Therefore
$$\mathbb{E}[f_s^2]=3s^2+2s\cdot s+s^2=3s^2+2s^2+s^2=6s^2.$$

**Step 3: Integrate.**
$$\mathbb{Var}(M_t)=\int_{0}^{t}6s^2\,ds=6\cdot\frac{t^3}{3}=2t^3.$$

## Result

$$\boxed{\mathbb{Var}\!\left(\int_{0}^{t}\bigl((B_s)^2+s\bigr)\,dB_s\right)=2t^3
.}$$
