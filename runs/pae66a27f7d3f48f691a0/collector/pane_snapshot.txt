**Arc-length element.** From $\rho^2=a^2\cos 2\theta$:
$$2\rho\,\frac{d\rho}{d\theta}=-2a^2\sin 2\theta \;\Longrightarrow\; \left(\frac
{d\rho}{d\theta}\right)^2=\frac{a^2\sin^2 2\theta}{\cos 2\theta}.$$
Hence
$$\rho^2+\left(\frac{d\rho}{d\theta}\right)^2 = a^2\cos 2\theta + \frac{a^2\sin^
2 2\theta}{\cos 2\theta} = \frac{a^2}{\cos 2\theta},$$
so
$$ds = \frac{a}{\sqrt{\cos 2\theta}}\,d\theta, \qquad \rho\,ds = a^2\,d\theta.$$

$$S = 4\pi a^2\big[-\cos\theta\big]_0^{\pi/4} = 4\pi a^2\!\left(1-\frac{1}{\sqrt
 2}\right) = 2\pi a^2(2-\sqrt 2).$$

**First moment about the $yz$-plane.** By symmetry the centroid lies on the $x$-

$$\int x\,dS = 4\pi a^3\int_0^{\pi/4}\sqrt{\cos 2\theta}\,\sin\theta\cos\theta\,
d\theta.$$

Thus
$$\int x\,dS = 4\pi a^3\cdot\frac{1}{6} = \frac{2\pi a^3}{3}.$$
