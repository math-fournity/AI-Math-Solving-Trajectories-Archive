## Solution

We want $S = \sum_{k=0}^{2018} a_k^2 \pmod{2017}$, where $(x^2-3x+1)^{1009} = \s
um_{k=0}^{2018} a_k x^k$.

**Step 1: Express $S$ as a coefficient.**

For $f(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{2018} a_k x^k$, the sum of squares $S = \sum_k a_k^2$ is
 the constant term of $f(x)\cdot f(1/x)$, since the $x^0$ coefficient is $\sum_k
 a_k a_k$.

Now observe:
$$f(1/x) = \left(\tfrac{1}{x^2} - \tfrac{3}{x} + 1\right)^{1009} = \frac{(x^2-3x
+1)^{1009}}{x^{2018}} = \frac{f(x)}{x^{2018}}.$$

Therefore
$$f(x)\cdot f(1/x) = \frac{f(x)^2}{x^{2018}} = \frac{(x^2-3x+1)^{2018}}{x^{2018}
}.$$

The constant term of this is the coefficient of $x^{2018}$ in $(x^2-3x+1)^{2018}
$. So

$$S = [x^{2018}]\,(x^2-3x+1)^{2018}.$$

**Step 2: Reduce mod 2017 using Freshman's dream.**

Since 2017 is prime, in $\mathbb{F}_{2017}[x]$ we have $(A+B)^{2017} = A^{2017}+
B^{2017}$. Applying this to the three terms of $x^2-3x+1$:

$$(x^2-3x+1)^{2017} \equiv (x^2)^{2017} + (-3x)^{2017} + 1^{2017} = x^{4034} + (
-3)^{2017}x^{2017} + 1.$$

Since 2017 is odd, $(-3)^{2017} = -3^{2017}$, and by Fermat's little theorem $3^
{2017}\equiv 3\pmod{2017}$. Hence

$$(x^2-3x+1)^{2017} \equiv x^{4034} - 3x^{2017} + 1 \pmod{2017}.$$

**Step 3: Multiply by one more factor.**

Since $2018 = 2017+1$,

$$(x^2-3x+1)^{2018} \equiv (x^{4034}-3x^{2017}+1)(x^2-3x+1) \pmod{2017}.$$

Expanding:
$$= x^{4036}-3x^{4035}+x^{4034} - 3x^{2019}+\boxed{9x^{2018}}-3x^{2017}+x^2-3x+1
.$$

The only contribution to $x^{2018}$ comes from $(-3x^{2017})(-3x)=9x^{2018}$.

**Step 4: Conclusion.**

$$S \equiv 9 \pmod{2017}.$$

The remainder when $a_0^2+a_1^2+\cdots+a_{2018}^2$ is divided by $2017$ is $\box
ed{9}$.
