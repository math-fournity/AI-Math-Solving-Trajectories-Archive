The device consists of two identical equilateral triangular plates, Plate $ABC$ and Plate $DEF$, both made of ultra-thin sapphire. Each side of these two plates measures exactly $1$ unit. These plates are suspended in space, perfectly parallel to one another. Their orientation is such that if an observer looks directly down from above, the vertical projection of the two triangles forms a perfect regular hexagon with vertices in the cyclic order $A, F, B, D, C, E$.

To complete the framework of the component, six structural carbon-fiber struts are installed to connect the plates: $AE, AF, BF, BD, CD$, and $CE$. Each of these six struts is manufactured to have a length of exactly $1$ unit.

Calculate the volume of the three-dimensional solid enclosed by the two triangular plates and the six connecting struts.
