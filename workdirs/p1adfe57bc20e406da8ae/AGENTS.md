# Problem

In a remote high-security server farm, a Chief Architect (represented by a White King) is stationed in the very first server rack of an 8x8 grid of 64 racks (position a1). To ensure system redundancy and security, the Architect must deploy two automated security drones into two of the remaining 63 empty racks.

One drone is a Shadow Sentinel (Black Bishop) and the other is a Light Guardian (White Bishop). The deployment must follow these strict operational constraints based on their diagonal scanning lasers:

1. Each drone must occupy its own unique rack, and neither can occupy the Architect’s rack.
2. The Shadow Sentinel’s diagonal scanning laser must not cross the rack where the Architect is stationed.
3. The Light Guardian’s diagonal scanning laser must also not cross the rack where the Architect is stationed.
4. The two drones are distinct units and cannot occupy the same rack.

In how many distinct ways can the Shadow Sentinel and the Light Guardian be positioned within the grid such that neither drone has the Architect in its diagonal line of sight?
