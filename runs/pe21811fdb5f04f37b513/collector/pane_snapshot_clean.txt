## Solution

**Setting up the system.** From the problem statement:

$$x' = -7x + y + 5, \qquad y' = -2x - 5y - 37t,$$

with $x(0)=0,\; y(0)=0$.

**Particular solution.** Try $x_p = at+b,\; y_p = ct+d$. Substituting and matchi
ng coefficients:

- From $x'$: $a = (-7a+c)t + (-7b+d+5)$, giving $c=7a$ and $a=-7b+d+5$.
- From $y'$: $c = (-2a-5c-37)t + (-2b-5d)$, giving $2a+5c=-37$ and $c=-2b-5d$.

With $c=7a$: $2a+35a=-37 \Rightarrow a=-1,\; c=-7$. Then $d=7b-6$ and $2b+5(7b-6
)=7 \Rightarrow 37b=37 \Rightarrow b=1,\; d=1$.

So $x_p = 1-t,\; y_p = 1-7t$. (Verified: $x_p'=-1=-7(1-t)+(1-7t)+5$; $y_p'=-7=-2

**Homogeneous solution.** The matrix $A=\begin{pmatrix}-7&1\\-2&-5\end{pmatrix}$

$$e^{-6t}\begin{pmatrix}\cos t\\ \cos t-\sin t\end{pmatrix},\qquad e^{-6t}\begin
{pmatrix}\sin t\\ \sin t+\cos t\end{pmatrix}.$$

**General solution and initial conditions.**

$$x(t)=1-t+e^{-6t}(C_1\cos t+C_2\sin t),$$
$$y(t)=1-7t+e^{-6t}\bigl(C_1(\cos t-\sin t)+C_2(\sin t+\cos t)\bigr).$$

From $x(0)=0$: $0=1+C_1 \Rightarrow C_1=-1$.
From $y(0)=0$: $0=1+C_1+C_2 \Rightarrow C_2=0$.

So:
$$x(t)=1-t-e^{-6t}\cos t,\qquad y(t)=1-7t-e^{-6t}(\cos t-\sin t).$$
