## Proof

We determine the number of zeros of $f(z) = 3e^z - z$ in the unit disk $|z|<1$ u
sing **Rouché's theorem**.

**Setup.** Write $f(z) = g(z) + h(z)$ where $g(z) = 3e^z$ and $h(z) = -z$. We co
mpare $|g|$ and $|h|$ on the boundary $|z|=1$.

**Estimate on $|z|=1$.** For $|z|=1$ we have $|h(z)| = |-z| = 1$. Also,
$$|g(z)| = |3e^z| = 3e^{\operatorname{Re} z}.$$
On the circle $|z|=1$, the real part satisfies $\operatorname{Re} z \in [-1, 1]$
, so $\operatorname{Re} z \geq -1$, giving
$$|g(z)| \geq 3e^{-1} = \frac{3}{e}.$$
Since $e \approx 2.718$, we have $\frac{3}{e} \approx 1.104 > 1$. Hence on $|z|=
1$:
$$|g(z)| \geq \frac{3}{e} > 1 = |h(z)|.$$
The inequality is strict everywhere on the boundary.

**Apply Rouché's theorem.** Since $|g(z)| > |h(z)|$ on $|z|=1$ and both $g$ and
$g+h=f$ are analytic on and inside the contour, Rouché's theorem gives that $g$
and $f = g+h$ have the same number of zeros (counted with multiplicity) inside $
|z|<1$.

**Count zeros of $g$.** The function $g(z) = 3e^z$ is entire and never vanishes,
 since $e^z \neq 0$ for all $z \in \mathbb{C}$. Thus $g$ has **0** zeros inside
$|z|<1$.

**Conclusion.** Therefore $f(z) = 3e^z - z$ also has **0** zeros in the unit dis
k $|z|<1$.
