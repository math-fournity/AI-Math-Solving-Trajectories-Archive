## Solution

**Setup.** Place the square in the $z=0$ plane with $D=(0,0,0)$, $C=(4,0,0)$, $B
=(4,4,0)$, $A=(0,4,0)$. Then $E=(2,4,0)$ and $F=(4,t,0)$ for $t\in[0,4]$.

**Folding constraints.** Folding $\triangle ADE$ along $DE$ sends $A$ to $A'$, p
reserving distances to points on line $DE$. Folding $\triangle DCF$ along $DF$ s
ends $C$ to the same point $A'$, preserving distances to points on line $DF$. Wr
iting $A'=(x,y,z)$ with $z\ge 0$:

$$|A'D|^2 = x^2+y^2+z^2 = |AD|^2 = 16 \quad\text{and}\quad = |CD|^2 = 16 \;\chec
kmark$$

$$|A'E|^2 = (x-2)^2+(y-4)^2+z^2 = |AE|^2 = 4$$

$$|A'F|^2 = (x-4)^2+(y-t)^2+z^2 = |CF|^2 = t^2$$

**Solving the system.** Subtracting the second from the first:
$$x^2+y^2 - [(x-2)^2+(y-4)^2] = 12 \;\Longrightarrow\; 4x+8y = 32 \;\Longrightar
row\; x+2y=8.$$

Subtracting the third from the first:
$$x^2+y^2 - [(x-4)^2+(y-t)^2] = 16-t^2 \;\Longrightarrow\; 8x+2ty = 32 \;\Longri
ghtarrow\; 4x+ty=16.$$

From $x=8-2y$ and $4(8-2y)+ty=16$: $(t-8)y=-16$, so
$$y=\frac{16}{8-t},\qquad x=\frac{8(4-t)}{8-t}.$$

Then
$$z^2 = 16 - x^2 - y^2 = 16 - \frac{64(4-t)^2+256}{(8-t)^2} = \frac{16\bigl(-3t^
2+16t-16\bigr)}{(8-t)^2}.$$

**Feasibility.** $z^2\ge 0$ requires $3t^2-16t+16\le 0$, i.e. $t\in[4/3,\,4]$. T
his is the range where the two folds can meet.

**The plane $DEF$.** Since $D$, $E$, $F$ all lie in the $z=0$ plane, the plane $
DEF$ **is** the $z=0$ plane. Therefore the distance from $A'$ to plane $DEF$ equ
als $|z|$.

**Maximizing $z^2$.** Let $u = 8-t$ (so $u\in[4,\,20/3]$). Then
$$z^2 = \frac{16(-3u^2+32u-80)}{u^2} = 16\!\left(-3 + \frac{32}{u} - \frac{80}{u
^2}\right).$$

Setting $v=1/u$, the bracket is $h(v) = -3+32v-80v^2$, a downward parabola maxim

The maximum value is
$$z^2_{\max} = 16\!\left(-3+\frac{32}{5}-\frac{80}{25}\right) = 16\!\left(-3+\fr
