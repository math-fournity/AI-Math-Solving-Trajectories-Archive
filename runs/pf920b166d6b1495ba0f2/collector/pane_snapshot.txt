## Example: The Hawaiian Earring

**Construction.** Let $H \subset \mathbb{R}^2$ be the **Hawaiian earring**: the
union of the circles
$$C_n = \{(x,y) : x^2 + (y - \tfrac{1}{n})^2 = \tfrac{1}{n^2}\}, \quad n = 1, 2,
 3, \dots$$
Each $C_n$ has radius $1/n$ and is tangent to the origin $O = (0,0)$, and the ci
rcles shrink to $O$. Set $H = \bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty} C_n$ with the subspace topo
logy.

**Claim.** $H$ is *not* micro simply connected.

**Proof.** We show that the point $O$ has **no** neighborhood $U$ of $O$ in $H$
for which the inclusion-induced homomorphism
$$\iota_* : \pi_1(U, O) \longrightarrow \pi_1(H, O)$$
is trivial.

Let $U$ be any neighborhood of $O$ in $H$. Since the circles $C_n$ shrink to $O$
 (their diameters $2/n \to 0$ and they all pass through $O$), there exists $N$ s
uch that for all $n \geq N$, the entire circle $C_n$ lies within $U$. (Indeed, $
C_n \subset B(O, 2/n) \cap H$, and $U$ contains some ball $B(O,\varepsilon)\cap
H$; pick $N$ with $2/N < \varepsilon$.)

Pick any $n \geq N$. The circle $C_n$ is entirely contained in $U$, so the loop
$\gamma_n$ that traverses $C_n$ once (based at $O$) is a loop in $U$. Its class
$[\gamma_n] \in \pi_1(U, O)$ is nontrivial (it generates a free $\mathbb{Z}$-fac
tor corresponding to $C_n$ within $U$, since $U$ contains $C_n$ as a retract-lik
e summand).

Under the inclusion $\iota: U \hookrightarrow H$, the loop $\gamma_n$ maps to th
e same loop traversing $C_n$ in $H$. It is a standard fact that in the Hawaiian
earring, the loop going once around a single circle $C_n$ represents a **nontriv
ial** element of $\pi_1(H, O)$: the natural map from the free group on countably
 many generators (one per circle) into $\pi_1(H,O)$ sends the $n$-th generator t
o a nontrivial element, and retractions $H \to C_n$ (collapsing all other circle
s to $O$) detect these elements — the retraction $r_n: H \to C_n$ satisfies $r_{
n*}([\gamma_n]) = $ the generator of $\pi_1(C_n,O)\cong\mathbb{Z}$, so $[\gamma_
n]\neq 1$ in $\pi_1(H,O)$.

Therefore $\iota_*([\gamma_n]) = [\gamma_n] \neq 1$ in $\pi_1(H, O)$, so the hom
omorphism $\iota_*$ is **nontrivial**.

Since $U$ was an arbitrary neighborhood of $O$, the point $O$ admits no neighbor
hood with trivial inclusion homomorphism. Hence $H$ is not micro simply connecte
d. $\blacksquare$
