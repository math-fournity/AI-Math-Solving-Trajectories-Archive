# Problem

In a remote sector of the galaxy, two rival logistics commanders, Alara and Bron, are competing to determine the final power balance of a modular energy core. The core is powered by 20 distinct fuel rods, each assigned a fixed integer potency from 1 to 20.

The commanders take turns selecting exactly one unused fuel rod and deciding its polarity: either "Positive" (adding its potency to the core’s total) or "Negative" (subtracting its potency from the core’s total). Alara takes the first turn. Once a polarity is assigned to a rod, it cannot be altered.

Alara’s objective is to ensure the final absolute magnitude of the core’s total energy is as close to zero as possible (minimizing the absolute value). Bron’s objective is to ensure the final absolute magnitude is as large as possible (maximizing the absolute value).

Assuming both Alara and Bron employ perfect mathematical strategies to achieve their respective goals, what will be the final absolute value of the energy core’s total?
