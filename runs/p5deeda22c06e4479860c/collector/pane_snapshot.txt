## Proof

**Yes, $S$ must contain all positive integers.**

**Setup.** Since $S$ is nonempty, pick $n_0 \in S$. Since $1 \mid 2025^{n_0}-15^
{n_0}$, we get $1 \in S$.

Note $2025 = 15 \cdot 135$ and $135 = 3^3 \cdot 5$, so
$$2025^n - 15^n = 15^n(135^n - 1).$$

**Contradiction setup.** Suppose $S \neq \mathbb{Z}^+$. Let $m$ be the smallest
positive integer not in $S$. Then $m \geq 2$ and every positive integer $< m$ li
es in $S$.

Write $m = \prod_{i=1}^r p_i^{a_i}$. Set $a = v_3(m)$, $b = v_5(m)$, and $A = \m
ax(a,b)$ (with $A=0$ if $3,5 \nmid m$).

**Defining $D$.** For each prime $p_i \notin \{3,5\}$, since $\gcd(135, p_i)=1$,
 the order $d_i := \operatorname{ord}_{p_i^{a_i}}(135)$ is well-defined. Let $D
= \operatorname{lcm}$ of all $d_i$ (with $D=1$ if there are no such primes).

**Key bound: $D < m/(3^a \cdot 5^b)$.**

Each $d_i \mid \varphi(p_i^{a_i})$. For $p_i=2$: $\varphi(2^{a_i})=2^{a_i-1}$. F
or $p_i$ odd: $\varphi(p_i^{a_i}) = p_i^{a_i}(1-1/p_i)$. So $D$ divides the lcm,
 which is at most the product:
$$\prod_{p_i \notin \{3,5\}} \varphi(p_i^{a_i}) = \underbrace{\prod_{p_i \notin
\{3,5\}} p_i^{a_i}}_{=:\, M'} \cdot \prod_{\substack{p_i \notin \{3,5\}\\p_i \te
xt{ odd}}} \left(1-\tfrac{1}{p_i}\right) \cdot \left(\tfrac{1}{2} \text{ if } 2
\mid m\right).$$
Every multiplicative factor after $M'$ is strictly less than $1$, so the product
 $< M' = m/(3^a \cdot 5^b)$. Hence $D < m/(3^a \cdot 5^b)$.

**Choosing $n$.** We need $n \in S$ with $D \mid n$ and $n \geq A$ (to ensure en
ough factors of $3$ and $5$ from $15^n$). Take $n = \lceil A/D \rceil \cdot D$ i
f $A \geq 1$, and $n = D$ if $A = 0$. Then $n \geq 1$, $D \mid n$, and $n \geq A
$.

**Verifying $n < m$.** We use the bound $A \leq 3^a \cdot 5^b - 1$ (since $3^a \
geq a+1$ for $a \geq 1$, $5^b \geq b+1$ for $b \geq 1$, and $3^a \cdot 5^b - 1 \
geq \max(a,b)$ in all cases, verified directly for small values and by growth fo
r large ones).

- If $D \mid A$: $n = A \leq 3^a \cdot 5^b - 1 < m$. ✓
- If $D \nmid A$: $n < A + D < A + m/(3^a \cdot 5^b)$. We need $A + m/(3^a \cdot
 5^b) \leq m$, i.e., $A \leq m \cdot \frac{3^a \cdot 5^b - 1}{3^a \cdot 5^b}$. S
ince $m \geq 3^a \cdot 5^b$, the RHS $\geq 3^a \cdot 5^b - 1 \geq A$. So $A + D
< m$. ✓

Since $1 \leq n < m$, by minimality of $m$, we have $n \in S$.

**$m$ divides $2025^n - 15^n$.** For each prime power $p_i^{a_i} \| m$:

- **$p_i = 3$:** $v_3(15^n(135^n-1)) = n + v_3(135^n - 1) = n + 0 = n \geq A \ge
q a$, since $3 \mid 135$ gives $135^n \equiv 0 \pmod{3}$, so $v_3(135^n-1)=0$. ✓
- **$p_i = 5$:** Similarly $v_5 = n \geq A \geq b$. ✓
- **$p_i \notin \{3,5\}$:** Since $d_i = \operatorname{ord}_{p_i^{a_i}}(135) \mi
d D \mid n$, we have $135^n \equiv 1 \pmod{p_i^{a_i}}$, so $p_i^{a_i} \mid 135^n
 - 1$. ✓

The prime powers $p_i^{a_i}$ are pairwise coprime and each divides $2025^n - 15^
n$, so $m \mid 2025^n - 15^n$.

**Contradiction.** Since $n \in S$ and $m \mid 2025^n - 15^n$, the closure prope
rty forces $m \in S$, contradicting the choice of $m$ as the smallest positive i
nteger not in $S$.

Therefore no such $m$ exists, and $S = \mathbb{Z}^+$.
