**The region $D$.** The curves $y = x^2$ and $x = y^2$ meet at $(0,0)$ and $(1,1

$$D = \{(x,y) : 0 \le x \le 1,\; x^2 \le y \le \sqrt{x}\,\}.$$

**Symmetry.** Observe that $D$ is invariant under the swap $(x,y) \mapsto (y,x)$

$$f(y,x) = \frac{\sin y - \sin x}{yx+1} = -\,\frac{\sin x - \sin y}{xy+1} = -f(x
