**Solution.**

$$3^x \cdot 7^x = 3.$$

Dividing both sides by $3^x$:

$$7^x = 3^{1-x}.$$

Taking $\log_7$ of both sides:

$$x = (1-x)\log_7 3 \implies \log_7 3 = \frac{x}{1-x}.$$

Since $9 = 3^2$,

$$\log_7 9 = 2\log_7 3 = \frac{2x}{1-x}.$$

**Answer:** $\log_7 9 = \dfrac{2x}{1-x}$.
