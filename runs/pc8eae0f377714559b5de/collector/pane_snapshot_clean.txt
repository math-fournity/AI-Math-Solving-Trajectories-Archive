Claim. Every open subset $U$ of a symplectic manifold $(\mathcal{M}, \omega)$ is
a symplectic submanifold of $(\mathcal{M}, \omega)$.

Definitions. A submanifold $N \subseteq \mathcal{M}$ is a symplectic submanifold
if the restricted form $\omega|_N$ is a symplectic form on $N$, i.e. it is
closed and non-degenerate at every point of $N$.

Step 1: $U$ is a submanifold. Since $U \subseteq \mathcal{M}$ is open, it
inherits a smooth structure from $\mathcal{M}$ as an open submanifold. The
inclusion $\iota: U \hookrightarrow \mathcal{M}$ is a smooth embedding, and $
\dim U = \dim \mathcal{M}$.

Step 2: $\omega|_U$ is closed. The restriction $\omega|_U = \iota^* \omega$ is
the pullback of $\omega$ under the inclusion. By naturality of the exterior
derivative, $$d(\omega|_U) = d(\iota^\omega) = \iota^(d\omega) = \iota^* 0 = 0,$
$ since $\omega$ is closed ($d\omega = 0$) on $\mathcal{M}$. Hence $\omega|_U$
is closed on $U$.

Step 3: $\omega|_U$ is non-degenerate. Let $p \in U$. Because $U$ is open in $
\mathcal{M}$, the tangent space $T_p U$ is canonically identified with $T_p
\mathcal{M}$. The map $$\widetilde{\omega|_U}_p : T_p U \to T_p^* U, \qquad v
\mapsto \omega_p(v, \cdot)$$ is, under this canonical identification, exactly
the map $\widetilde{\omega}_p : T_p \mathcal{M} \to T_p^* \mathcal{M}$. Since $
\omega$ is symplectic on $\mathcal{M}$, $\widetilde{\omega}_p$ is a linear
isomorphism at every $p$. Therefore $\widetilde{\omega|_U}_p$ is an isomorphism
for every $p \in U$, which means $\omega|_U$ is non-degenerate on $U$.

Equivalently, the symplectic orthogonal of $T_p U$ inside $T_p \mathcal{M}$ is $
$(T_p U)^\omega = (T_p \mathcal{M})^\omega = {0},$$ so $T_p U \cap (T_p
U)^\omega = {0}$, confirming that $T_p U$ is a symplectic subspace at every
point.

Step 4: Conclusion. Since $\omega|_U$ is a closed, non-degenerate 2-form on $U$,
it is a symplectic form on $U$. Therefore $(U, \omega|_U)$ is a symplectic
manifold, and $U$ is a symplectic submanifold of $(\mathcal{M}, \omega)$.

Since $U$ was an arbitrary open subset, every open subset of $(\mathcal{M},
\omega)$ is a symplectic submanifold. $\blacksquare$
