cted graph where every vertex has even degree.

The 15 dominoes form $K_5$ plus 5 loops (doubles), with each vertex having degre

## Determining the Partition Type

The 5 loops distribute as $l_1+l_2+l_3=5$ with each $l_i\in\{0,1,2\}$ (the only
possible loop counts). The unique solution is $\{1,2,2\}$: **one Type B chain +
two Type C chains**.
