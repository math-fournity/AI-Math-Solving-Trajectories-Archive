## Proof

**Notation.** Let $a_k = 4^{-k}\binom{2k}{k}$ and $L_n = \frac{2n}{\log_2 n} = \
frac{2n\ln 2}{\ln n}$. The sum is $S_n = \sum_{k=0}^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor} \binom{
n}{2k} a_k^{L_n}$.

**Key observations.**

1. **The $k=0$ term is exactly 1**, since $a_0 = 1$ and $\binom{n}{0}=1$.

2. **Asymptotics of $a_k$:** By Stirling's approximation, $\binom{2k}{k} \sim \f
rac{4^k}{\sqrt{\pi k}}$, so
$$a_k = 4^{-k}\binom{2k}{k} \sim \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi k}} \quad (k \to \infty).$$
Note $a_0 = 1$ and $0 < a_k < 1$ for all $k \geq 1$.

3. **Asymptotics of $\binom{n}{n/2}$:** $\binom{n}{n/2} \sim \frac{2^n}{\sqrt{\p
i n/2}}$.

**Strategy.** We show that the single term at $k = \lfloor n/4 \rfloor$ (so $2k
\approx n/2$) already tends to $+\infty$, which forces $S_n \to +\infty$.

**Lower bound via the term $k = \lfloor n/4 \rfloor$.**

Set $k = \lfloor n/4 \rfloor$ and $m = 2k = 2\lfloor n/4 \rfloor$, so $m = n/2 +
 O(1)$. The corresponding term is:
$$T_n = \binom{n}{m} \cdot a_k^{L_n}.$$

Taking logarithms and applying Stirling:

$$\ln \binom{n}{m} = n\ln 2 - \frac{1}{2}\ln\frac{\pi n}{2} + O(1/n),$$

since $m/n \to 1/2$ and the entropy $H(m/n) \to \ln 2$ with corrections of order
 $1/n$ from the offset.

For $a_k$ with $k = n/4 + O(1)$:
$$\ln a_k = -\frac{1}{2}\ln(\pi k) + O(1/k) = -\frac{1}{2}\ln\!\left(\frac{\pi n
}{4}\right) + O(1/n) = \ln 2 - \frac{1}{2}\ln\pi - \frac{1}{2}\ln n + O(1/n).$$

Multiplying by $L_n = \frac{2n\ln 2}{\ln n}$:

$$L_n \ln a_k = \frac{2n\ln 2}{\ln n}\!\left(\ln 2 - \frac{1}{2}\ln\pi\right) -
\frac{2n\ln 2}{\ln n}\cdot\frac{1}{2}\ln n + O\!\left(\frac{n}{(\ln n)^2}\right)
$$

$$= \frac{2cn\ln 2}{\ln n} - n\ln 2 + O\!\left(\frac{n}{(\ln n)^2}\right),$$

where $c := \ln 2 - \frac{1}{2}\ln\pi = \ln\frac{2}{\sqrt{\pi}}$.

**Critical sign check:** $c = \ln\frac{2}{\sqrt{\pi}}$. Since $4 > \pi$, we have
 $2 > \sqrt{\pi}$, so $\frac{2}{\sqrt{\pi}} > 1$ and thus $c > 0$. (Numerically
$c \approx 0.1213$.)

**Combining:** The total logarithm of $T_n$ is:

$$\ln T_n = \underbrace{\left(n\ln 2 - \frac{1}{2}\ln\frac{\pi n}{2} + O(1/n)\ri
ght)}_{\ln \binom{n}{m}} + \underbrace{\left(\frac{2cn\ln 2}{\ln n} - n\ln 2 + O
\!\left(\frac{n}{(\ln n)^2}\right)\right)}_{L_n \ln a_k}$$

$$= \frac{2cn\ln 2}{\ln n} - \frac{1}{2}\ln\frac{\pi n}{2} + O\!\left(\frac{n}{(
\ln n)^2}\right).$$

The leading term is $\frac{2cn\ln 2}{\ln n}$ with $c > 0$, which grows like $\Th
eta(n/\ln n)$. The second term is $-\frac{1}{2}\ln n + O(1) = O(\ln n)$, and the
 error is $O(n/(\ln n)^2)$. Since $n/\ln n \gg \ln n$ and $n/\ln n \gg n/(\ln n)
^2$, we conclude:

$$\ln T_n = \frac{2cn\ln 2}{\ln n}\,(1 + o(1)) \to +\infty.$$

Therefore $T_n \to +\infty$.

**Conclusion.** Since every term in the sum $S_n$ is non-negative and $S_n \geq
T_n \to +\infty$:

$$\boxed{\lim_{n\to\infty} \sum_{k=0}^{\lfloor n/2 \rfloor} \binom{n}{2k}\left(4
^{-k}\binom{2k}{k}\right)^{\frac{2n}{\log_2{n}}} = +\infty.}$$

**Intuition.** The factor $a_k^{L_n}$ with $a_k \sim (\pi k)^{-1/2}$ contributes
 roughly $(\pi k)^{-n/\log_2 n}$, which at $k = n/4$ extracts a factor of $n^{-n
/\log_2 n} = 2^{-n}$ that exactly cancels the $2^n$ growth of $\binom{n}{n/2}$.
The residual factor $(\pi/4)^{-n/\log_2 n} = (4/\pi)^{n/\log_2 n}$ grows without
 bound since $4/\pi > 1$, driving the sum to infinity.
