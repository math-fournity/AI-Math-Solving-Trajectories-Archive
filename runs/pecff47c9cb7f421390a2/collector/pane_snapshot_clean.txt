**Problem.** Find the maximum $(n-1)$-dimensional volume of the projection of th
e unit $n$-cube $[0,1]^n$ onto an $(n-1)$-dimensional hyperplane, and give a for
mula.

**Answer.** The maximum is $\boxed{\sqrt{n}}$.

---

## Proof

### Setup

Let $C=[0,1]^n\subset\mathbb{R}^n$ be the unit $n$-cube, and let $H$ be a hyperp
lane through the origin with unit normal $\mathbf{u}=(u_1,\dots,u_n)$, $\sum u_i
^2=1$. Let $P:\mathbb{R}^n\to H$ be the orthogonal projection. We seek $\max_{\m
athbf{u}}\operatorname{Vol}_{n-1}(P(C))$.

### Step 1 — The projection is a zonotope

Since $C=[0,\mathbf{e}_1]+\cdots+[0,\mathbf{e}_n]$ (Minkowski sum of edge segmen
ts) and $P$ is linear,

$$P(C)=[0,P\mathbf{e}_1]+\cdots+[0,P\mathbf{e}_n],$$

a zonotope in $H\cong\mathbb{R}^{n-1}$ generated by the $n$ vectors $\mathbf{v}_
i:=P\mathbf{e}_i=\mathbf{e}_i-u_i\mathbf{u}$.

### Step 2 — Zonotope volume formula

The $(n-1)$-volume of a zonotope $\sum_{i=1}^{m}[0,\mathbf{v}_i]$ in $\mathbb{R}
^{n-1}$ is

$$\operatorname{Vol}_{n-1}=\sum_{\substack{S\subseteq[m]\\|S|=n-1}}\bigl|\det(\m
athbf{v}_i:i\in S)\bigr|.$$

(This is the standard zonotope volume formula; it is verified by checking $d{=}1
,m{=}1,2$ and $d{=}2,m{=}2,3$.) With $m=n$, the $(n-1)$-subsets are exactly $[n]
\setminus\{j\}$ for $j=1,\dots,n$, so

$$\operatorname{Vol}_{n-1}(P(C))=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\bigl|\det_H(\mathbf{v}_i:i\neq j
)\bigr|. \tag{$\star$}$$

### Step 3 — Evaluating each determinant

The $(n-1)$-determinant in $H$ equals the $n$-determinant of the parallelepiped
$\{\mathbf{u},\mathbf{v}_i:i\neq j\}$ in $\mathbb{R}^n$, since $\mathbf{u}\perp
H$ and $\|\mathbf{u}\|=1$:

$$\bigl|\det_H(\mathbf{v}_i:i\neq j)\bigr|=\bigl|\det_{\mathbb{R}^n}(\mathbf{u}\
text{ in slot }j,\;\mathbf{v}_i:i\neq j)\bigr|=:\bigl|\det B_j\bigr|.$$

Let $A=I-\mathbf{u}\mathbf{u}^T$ be the projection matrix (columns $\mathbf{v}_1
,\dots,\mathbf{v}_n$). Replacing column $j$ of $A$ by $\mathbf{u}$ gives $B_j$.
Expanding $\det B_j$ along column $j$ using the cofactors $C_{ij}(A)$ of $A$ (un
changed when only column $j$ is replaced):

$$\det B_j=\sum_i u_i\,C_{ij}(A)=\bigl(\operatorname{adj}A\bigr)_{ji}\cdot u_i\t
ext{ summed}=\sum_i u_i\,(\operatorname{adj}A)_{ji}.$$

**Computing $\operatorname{adj}A$.** The matrix $A=I-\mathbf{u}\mathbf{u}^T$ has
 eigenvalue $1$ on $H$ (multiplicity $n-1$) and eigenvalue $0$ on $\operatorname
{span}(\mathbf{u})$. The adjugate's eigenvalues are products of all eigenvalues
except the corresponding one: eigenvalue $1^{n-1}=1$ on $\operatorname{span}(\ma
thbf{u})$, and $1^{n-2}\cdot 0=0$ on $H$. Hence $\operatorname{adj}A=\mathbf{u}\
mathbf{u}^T$ (rank-1 projector onto $\mathbf{u}$, with eigenvalue $1$ on $\mathb
f{u}$). Therefore $(\operatorname{adj}A)_{ji}=u_ju_i$, and

$$\det B_j=\sum_i u_i\cdot u_ju_i=u_j\sum_i u_i^2=u_j.$$

So $\bigl|\det_H(\mathbf{v}_i:i\neq j)\bigr|=|u_j|$, and substituting into $(\st
ar)$:

$$\boxed{\operatorname{Vol}_{n-1}(P(C))=\sum_{j=1}^{n}|u_j|.}$$

### Step 4 — Maximizing

By Cauchy–Schwarz with the constraint $\sum u_j^2=1$,

$$\sum_{j=1}^{n}|u_j|\le\sqrt{n\sum_{j=1}^{n}u_j^2}=\sqrt{n},$$

with equality iff all $|u_j|$ are equal, i.e. $|u_j|=1/\sqrt{n}$ for every $j$ (
the normal points toward a vertex of the cube).

### Verification on small cases

- $n=2$: $\sqrt{2}$, the diagonal of the unit square. ✓
- $n=3$: $\sqrt{3}$, the area of the regular hexagonal projection of the unit cu
be onto the plane perpendicular to a main diagonal. ✓

### Conclusion

The maximum $(n-1)$-dimensional volume of the projection of the unit $n$-cube on
to an $(n-1)$-dimensional hyperplane is

$$\boxed{\sqrt{n}},$$

achieved when the hyperplane is perpendicular to a main diagonal of the cube. Th
e volume for a general hyperplane with unit normal $\mathbf{u}$ is $\sum_{j=1}^{
n}|u_j|$.
