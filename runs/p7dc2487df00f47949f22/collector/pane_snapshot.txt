## Proof

**Upper bound.** The polynomial $p(z) = z^n + 1$ belongs to $S_n$ (all middle co
efficients are zero, leading coefficient and constant term both equal 1). On the
 unit circle,
$$p(e^{i\theta}) = e^{in\theta} + 1 = e^{in\theta/2}\!\left(e^{in\theta/2} + e^{
-in\theta/2}\right) = 2\,e^{in\theta/2}\cos\!\left(\tfrac{n\theta}{2}\right),$$
so $|p(e^{i\theta})| = 2\left|\cos\!\left(\frac{n\theta}{2}\right)\right| \leq 2
$, with equality at $\theta = \frac{2k\pi}{n}$. Hence
$$M_n \leq \max_{|z|=1}|z^n+1| = 2.$$

**Lower bound.** Let $\omega_k = e^{2\pi i k/n}$, $k = 0, 1, \ldots, n-1$, be th
e $n$-th roots of unity, and let $p(z) = \sum_{j=0}^{n} a_j z^j \in S_n$ with $a
_0 = 1$ and $a_n = 1$. Evaluate the sum of $p$ over all $n$-th roots of unity:

$$\sum_{k=0}^{n-1} p(\omega_k) = \sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\sum_{j=0}^{n} a_j\,\omega_k^j
= \sum_{j=0}^{n} a_j \underbrace{\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\omega_k^j}_{=\, n\,\text{ if }
 n\mid j,\; 0\,\text{ otherwise}}.$$

For $0 \leq j \leq n$, the divisibility condition $n \mid j$ holds only for $j =
 0$ and $j = n$. Therefore

$$\sum_{k=0}^{n-1} p(\omega_k) = a_0 \cdot n + a_n \cdot n = n + n = 2n.$$

Dividing by $n$ and applying the triangle inequality:

$$2 = \left|\frac{1}{n}\sum_{k=0}^{n-1} p(\omega_k)\right| \leq \frac{1}{n}\sum_
{k=0}^{n-1}|p(\omega_k)| \leq \max_{0 \leq k \leq n-1}|p(\omega_k)|.$$

Since every $\omega_k$ lies on the unit circle, $\max_{|z|=1}|p(z)| \geq \max_k
|p(\omega_k)| \geq 2$. As this holds for every $p \in S_n$,

$$M_n \geq 2.$$

**Conclusion.** Combining both bounds, $M_n = 2$ for every integer $n \geq 1$.
