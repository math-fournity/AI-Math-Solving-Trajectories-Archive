## Solution

We solve the wave equation on $0<x<L$ with mixed boundary conditions
$$u(0,t)=0,\qquad u_x(L,t)=0,$$
and initial data $u(x,0)=f(x)$, $u_t(x,0)=g(x)$.

### Separation of variables

Seeking nontrivial solutions of the form $u(x,t)=X(x)T(t)$ and substituting into
 $u_{tt}=a^2 u_{xx}$ gives
$$\frac{X''}{X}=\frac{T''}{a^2 T}=-\lambda,$$
so that
$$X''+\lambda X=0,\qquad T''+a^2\lambda T=0.$$

The boundary conditions become $X(0)=0$ and $X'(L)=0$.

### Eigenvalue problem

With $X(x)=A\cos(\sqrt{\lambda}\,x)+B\sin(\sqrt{\lambda}\,x)$:

- $X(0)=0 \Rightarrow A=0$, so $X(x)=B\sin(\sqrt{\lambda}\,x)$.
- $X'(L)=0 \Rightarrow B\sqrt{\lambda}\cos(\sqrt{\lambda}\,L)=0$. For a nontrivi
al solution $B\neq 0$ and $\lambda>0$, we need
$$\cos(\sqrt{\lambda}\,L)=0 \;\Longrightarrow\; \sqrt{\lambda}\,L=\frac{(2n-1)\p
i}{2},\quad n=1,2,3,\dots$$

Hence the eigenvalues and eigenfunctions are
$$\lambda_n = k_n^2,\qquad k_n=\frac{(2n-1)\pi}{2L},\qquad X_n(x)=\sin(k_n x),\q
uad n=1,2,3,\dots$$

These eigenfunctions are orthogonal on $[0,L]$, with
$$\int_0^L \sin(k_n x)\sin(k_m x)\,dx = \frac{L}{2}\,\delta_{nm}.$$

### Time equation

For each $n$, $T_n''+a^2 k_n^2 T_n=0$, giving
$$T_n(t)=A_n\cos(a k_n t)+B_n\sin(a k_n t).$$

### Formal solution

Superposing all modes,
$$u(x,t)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\Big[A_n\cos(a k_n t)+B_n\sin(a k_n t)\Big]\sin(k_n
x).$$

### Determining the coefficients from initial data

**From $u(x,0)=f(x)$:**
$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} A_n\sin(k_n x)=f(x).$$
Using orthogonality,
$$\boxed{A_n=\frac{2}{L}\int_0^L f(x)\sin(k_n x)\,dx.}$$

**From $u_t(x,0)=g(x)$:**
$$u_t(x,t)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\big[-A_n a k_n\sin(a k_n t)+B_n a k_n\cos(a k_n t
)\big]\sin(k_n x),$$
so at $t=0$:
$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} B_n a k_n\sin(k_n x)=g(x).$$
Again by orthogonality,
$$B_n a k_n=\frac{2}{L}\int_0^L g(x)\sin(k_n x)\,dx,$$
hence
$$\boxed{B_n=\frac{2}{L\,a\,k_n}\int_0^L g(x)\sin(k_n x)\,dx.}$$

### Final formula

With $k_n=\dfrac{(2n-1)\pi}{2L}$, the formal solution is

$$\boxed{\,u(x,t)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left[\left(\frac{2}{L}\int_0^L f(\xi)\sin(
k_n\xi)\,d\xi\right)\cos(a k_n t)+\left(\frac{2}{L\,a\,k_n}\int_0^L g(\xi)\sin(k
_n\xi)\,d\xi\right)\sin(a k_n t)\right]\sin(k_n x).\,}$$

This is the formal solution to the initial-boundary value problem with one fixed
 end ($x=0$) and one free end ($x=L$). The eigenvalues $k_n=(2n-1)\pi/(2L)$ corr
espond precisely to the odd harmonics of the quarter-wave supported on $[0,L]$,
reflecting the mixed Dirichlet–Neumann boundary conditions.
