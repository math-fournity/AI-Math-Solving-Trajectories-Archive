## Solution

Let $D$ be the distance between cities $M$ and $N$. The bus speed is $40$ km/h a
nd the car speed is $50$ km/h.

**Meeting point.** The bus leaves $M$ at time $t=0$. A quarter hour ($0.25$ h) l
ater, the bus meets the car. At that moment the bus has traveled
$$40 \times 0.25 = 10 \text{ km from } M,$$
so the meeting point is $10$ km from $M$ (and $D-10$ km from $N$).

**Car reaches $M$.** After the meeting, the car continues toward $M$, covering t
he remaining $10$ km at $50$ km/h:
$$\frac{10}{50} = 0.2 \text{ h} = 12 \text{ min}.$$
The car reaches $M$ at time $t = 0.25 + 0.2 = 0.45$ h.

**Car returns.** After waiting $15$ min $= 0.25$ h, the car departs $M$ back tow
ard $N$ at time
$$t_0 = 0.45 + 0.25 = 0.7 \text{ h}.$$

**Overtaking condition.** At $t_0 = 0.7$ h, the bus (traveling continuously at $
40$ km/h) is at
$$40 \times 0.7 = 28 \text{ km from } M.$$
For $t \ge 0.7$, the car's position from $M$ is $50(t - 0.7)$ and the bus's posi
tion is $28 + 40(t-0.7)$. The car overtakes the bus when these are equal:
$$50(t-0.7) = 28 + 40(t-0.7)$$
$$10(t-0.7) = 28$$
$$t - 0.7 = 2.8, \quad t = 3.5 \text{ h}.$$

At that instant the bus is at
$$40 \times 3.5 = 140 \text{ km from } M.$$
The problem states the overtaking occurs $20$ km from $N$, so
$$D - 140 = 20 \implies \boxed{D = 160 \text{ km}}.$$

**Verification.** With $D = 160$: the car traveled $D - 10 = 150$ km to the meet
ing point at $50$ km/h, taking $3$ h, so it left $N$ at $t = 0.25 - 3 = -2.75$ h
 (before the bus — consistent). The car reaches $M$ at $t=0.45$ h, departs at $t
=0.7$ h, and overtakes the bus at $t=3.5$ h when the bus is $140$ km from $M = 2
0$ km from $N$. ✓

The distance between cities $M$ and $N$ is **160 km**.
