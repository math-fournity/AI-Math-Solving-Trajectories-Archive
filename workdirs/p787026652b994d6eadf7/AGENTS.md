# Problem

A specialized assembly line consists of a sequence of 12 pressure-regulating valves. A configuration is classified as "Optimized" if, for every sub-section of four adjacent valves, the average pressure of the two outer valves (the first and the fourth) is numerically equal to the product of the pressures of the two inner valves (the second and the third).

A technician is tasked with calibrating the system under the following specific constraints:
1. The pressure reading of the 1st valve must be exactly 1 bar.
2. The pressure reading of the 12th valve must be exactly 1 bar.
3. The pressure reading of the 2nd valve must be identical to the pressure reading of the 3rd valve.

Given that the pressure reading for each valve must be a real number, how many distinct "Optimized" configurations are possible for this 12-valve system?
