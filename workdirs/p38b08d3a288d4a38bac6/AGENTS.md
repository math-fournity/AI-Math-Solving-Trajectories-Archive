# Problem

A high-tech energy grid consists of 20 distinct power generators, each producing a fixed output of megawatts corresponding to their ID number: 1, 2, 3, ..., up to 20. 

Two engineers, Alpha and Beta, are tasked with configuring the grid's polarity. They take turns selecting any unassigned generator and toggling its switch to either "Positive" (adding its power to the total grid load) or "Negative" (subtracting its power from the total grid load). Alpha goes first.

Alpha’s objective is to balance the grid as perfectly as possible, aiming to make the final total net load (the absolute value of the sum of all 20 signed numbers) as close to zero as possible. Beta, however, wants to disrupt the balance and aims to make this final absolute net load as large as possible.

Assuming both engineers play with perfect mathematical strategy to achieve their respective goals, what is the maximum absolute value of the final net load that Beta can guaranteed to achieve?
