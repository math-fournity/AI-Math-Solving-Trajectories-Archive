## Solution

We solve $u_x + (x+y)u_y = 1$ with $u(x,-x)=0$ by the method of characteristics.

### Characteristic equations

Parameterize the initial curve $\Gamma: (x,y)=(r,-r)$, $u=0$ at $s=0$. The chara
cteristic ODEs are
$$\frac{dx}{ds}=1,\qquad \frac{dy}{ds}=x+y,\qquad \frac{du}{ds}=1.$$

**Solve for $x$ and $u$:** With $x(0)=r$, $u(0)=0$:
$$x = r+s,\qquad u = s.$$

**Solve for $y$:** $\dfrac{dy}{ds}-y = x = r+s$. Multiplying by the integrating
factor $e^{-s}$:
$$\frac{d}{ds}\!\left(y\,e^{-s}\right)=(r+s)e^{-s}.$$
Integrating: $y\,e^{-s}=-(r+s+1)e^{-s}+C$. Applying $y(0)=-r$:
$$-r = -(r+1)+C \implies C=1.$$
Hence $y = -(r+s+1)+e^{s}$.

### Eliminate the parameters

From $x=r+s$ and $u=s$: $r=x-u$ and $s=u$. Substituting into the expression for
$y$:
$$y = -(r+s+1)+e^{s}=-(x-u+u+1)+e^{u}=-(x+1)+e^{u}.$$
Therefore
$$e^{u}=x+y+1 \implies \boxed{u(x,y)=\ln(x+y+1).}$$

### Verification

- **PDE:** $u_x=\dfrac{1}{x+y+1}$, $u_y=\dfrac{1}{x+y+1}$, so
$$u_x+(x+y)u_y=\frac{1+(x+y)}{x+y+1}=1.\ \checkmark$$
- **Initial condition:** $u(x,-x)=\ln(x-x+1)=\ln 1=0.\ \checkmark$

The solution is valid on the domain $x+y+1>0$.
