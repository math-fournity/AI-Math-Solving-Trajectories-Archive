## Setting up $E_n$

$$E_n = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{1}{1+k}\binom{n}{k}p^k(1-p)^{n-k}, \quad p = 0.15$$

Using the identity $\frac{1}{k+1}\binom{n}{k} = \frac{1}{n+1}\binom{n+1}{k+1}$ a
nd substituting $j = k+1$:

$$E_n = \frac{1}{(n+1)p}\sum_{j=1}^{n+1}\binom{n+1}{j}p^j(1-p)^{n+1-j} = \frac{1
-(1-p)^{n+1}}{(n+1)p}$$

With $p = 0.15$, $(1-p) = 0.85$:

$$E_n = \frac{1 - 0.85^{n+1}}{0.15(n+1)}, \qquad \frac{1}{E_n} = \frac{0.15(n+1)
}{1 - 0.85^{n+1}}$$
