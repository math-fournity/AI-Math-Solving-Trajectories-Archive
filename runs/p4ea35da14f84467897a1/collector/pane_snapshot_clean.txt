equally spaced. The midpoint of points at positions $i$ and $j$ is $\frac{i+j}{2
}$, so distinct midpoints correspond to distinct values of $i+j$ with $1 \le i <
 j \le 2017$.

The sum $i+j$ ranges from $1+2=3$ to $2016+2017=4033$. Every integer $s \in [3,
4033]$ is achievable:
- For $s \le 2018$: take $i=1, j=s-1$ (valid since $j \le 2017$).
- For $s \ge 2019$: take $i=s-2017, j=2017$ (valid since $i \ge 2$ and $i < j$ r
equires $s-2017 < 2017$, i.e., $s \le 4033$).

So the number of distinct midpoints is $4033 - 3 + 1 = 4031 = 2n-3$.
