## Solution

The geodesic equation, written with the Einstein summation convention and a symm
etric connection, is

$$\frac{d^2 x^\lambda}{dt^2} + \Gamma^\lambda_{\mu\nu}\,\frac{dx^\mu}{dt}\,\frac
{dx^\nu}{dt} = 0.$$

### Identifying the summation structure

Fix a component $\lambda$ (no sum on $\lambda$). The only place the Christoffel
symbol $\Gamma^\lambda_{\mu\nu}$ appears is in the second term

$$\Gamma^\lambda_{\mu\nu}\,\frac{dx^\mu}{dt}\,\frac{dx^\nu}{dt}.$$

Under the Einstein summation convention, a repeated index (one appearing once as
 a subscript and once as a superscript in a single term) is summed over its full
 range. Examining this term:

- The index **$\mu$** appears in $\Gamma^\lambda_{\mu\nu}$ (lower) and in $\frac
{dx^\mu}{dt}$ (upper) → **summed over $\mu$**.
- The index **$\nu$** appears in $\Gamma^\lambda_{\mu\nu}$ (lower) and in $\frac
{dx^\nu}{dt}$ (upper) → **summed over $\nu$**.
- The index $\lambda$ appears only once (free index), so it is **not** summed.

Thus the Christoffel symbol is contracted against two velocity factors, producin
g summation over **two** independent indices.

### Role of the symmetric connection

The hypothesis that the connection is symmetric means $\Gamma^\lambda_{\mu\nu} =
 \Gamma^\lambda_{\nu\mu}$. This symmetry does **not** reduce the number of summa
tions: the two sums over $\mu$ and $\nu$ are still both performed. The symmetry
only guarantees that the double sum can be rewritten as

$$\Gamma^\lambda_{\mu\nu}\,\dot x^\mu \dot x^\nu = \Gamma^\lambda_{(\mu\nu)}\,\d
ot x^\mu \dot x^\nu,$$

i.e. only the symmetric part of $\Gamma^\lambda_{\mu\nu}$ contributes (since $\d
ot x^\mu \dot x^\nu$ is symmetric in $\mu,\nu$). But the count of summations rem
ains unchanged.

### Counting

Each contracted index contributes one summation. There are exactly **two** contr
acted indices ($\mu$ and $\nu$) attached to the Christoffel symbol in the geodes
ic equation. Therefore the Christoffel symbols are summed

$$\boxed{2}$$

times.
