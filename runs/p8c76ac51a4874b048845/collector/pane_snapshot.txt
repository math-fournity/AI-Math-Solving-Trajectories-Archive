## Answer

**No.** There is no such subgroup.

**Proof.** Let $F_2$ be the free group of rank 2, and let $H \leq F_2$ be a subg
roup of finite index $n$ that is isomorphic to $F_4$ (free group of rank 4).

By the **Nielsen–Schreier theorem**, every subgroup of a free group is free, and
 the **Schreier index formula** gives the rank of $H$ as:

$$\operatorname{rank}(H) = n(\operatorname{rank}(F_2) - 1) + 1 = n(2 - 1) + 1 =
n + 1.$$

Since $H \cong F_4$, we have $\operatorname{rank}(H) = 4$, so:

$$n + 1 = 4 \implies n = 3.$$

Thus the index of any subgroup of $F_2$ isomorphic to $F_4$ must be **exactly 3*
*, which is a multiple of 3.

Such a subgroup of index 3 does indeed exist (e.g., the kernel of any surjection
 $F_2 \twoheadrightarrow \mathbb{Z}/3\mathbb{Z}$, which has index 3 and rank $3+
1=4$ by the formula), but its index is necessarily 3 — a multiple of 3.

Therefore, there is **no** subgroup of $F_2$ isomorphic to $F_4$ whose finite in
dex is not a multiple of 3.
