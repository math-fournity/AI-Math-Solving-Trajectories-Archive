## Proof

**Setup (affine reduction).** The statement is affine-invariant: bisection (midp
oints) and area ratios are preserved by affine maps. So we may place
$$A=(0,0),\quad B=(1,0),\quad C=(0,1),$$
giving $[ABC]=\tfrac12$, solve there, then scale.

**Parameterize the points.** Let
$$E=(1-t,\,t)\in BC,\qquad F=(0,\,f)\in CA,\qquad G=(g,\,0)\in AB.$$

**Translate the three bisection conditions.**

- *AE bisects BF at R*: $R=\tfrac12(B+F)=\bigl(\tfrac12,\tfrac f2\bigr)$, and $R
\in AE$, so $R=\lambda(1-t,t)$. Hence $\tfrac12=\lambda(1-t)$ and $\tfrac f2=\la
mbda t$, giving
$$f=\frac{t}{1-t}.\tag{1}$$

- *BF bisects CG at S*: $S=\tfrac12(C+G)=\bigl(\tfrac g2,\tfrac12\bigr)$, and $S
\in BF$, so $S=(1-\mu,\,\mu f)$. Hence $\tfrac12=\mu f$ and $\tfrac g2=1-\mu$, g
iving
$$g=2-\frac1f.\tag{2}$$

- *CG bisects AE at T*: $T=\tfrac12(A+E)=\bigl(\tfrac{1-t}{2},\,\tfrac t2\bigr)$
, and $T\in CG$, so $T=(\nu g,\,1-\nu)$. Hence $\tfrac t2=1-\nu$ and $\tfrac{1-t
}{2}=\nu g$, giving
$$g=\frac{1-t}{2-t}.\tag{3}$$

**Solve for $t$.** From (1) and (2): $g=2-\dfrac{1-t}{t}=\dfrac{3t-1}{t}$. Equat
ing with (3):
$$\frac{3t-1}{t}=\frac{1-t}{2-t}\;\Longrightarrow\;(3t-1)(2-t)=t(1-t)$$
$$6t-3t^2-2+t=t-t^2\;\Longrightarrow\;t^2-3t+1=0.$$
Thus $t=\dfrac{3\pm\sqrt5}{2}$; only $t=\dfrac{3-\sqrt5}{2}\in[0,1]$ is valid. S
etting $\varphi=\dfrac{1+\sqrt5}{2}$, note $t=\varphi^{-2}$, and then
$$f=\frac{t}{1-t}=\varphi^{-1},\qquad g=\frac{1-t}{2-t}=\varphi^{-2}.$$

**Coordinates of $R,S,T$.**
$$R=\Bigl(\tfrac12,\,\tfrac{1}{2\varphi}\Bigr),\quad S=\Bigl(\tfrac{1}{2\varphi^
2},\,\tfrac12\Bigr),\quad T=\Bigl(\tfrac{1}{2\varphi},\,\tfrac{1}{2\varphi^2}\Bi
gr).$$

**Area of $RST$.** Using $S-R=\bigl(-\tfrac{1}{2\varphi},\,\tfrac{1}{2\varphi^2}
\bigr)$ and $T-R=\bigl(-\tfrac{1}{2\varphi^2},\,-\tfrac{1}{2\varphi^3}\bigr)$:
$$[RST]=\tfrac12\Bigl|\det(S-R,\,T-R)\Bigr|=\tfrac12\Bigl(\tfrac{1}{4\varphi^4}+
\tfrac{1}{4\varphi^4}\Bigr)=\frac{1}{4\varphi^4}.$$
Since $\varphi^2=\varphi+1=\tfrac{3+\sqrt5}{2}$, we get $\varphi^4=\tfrac{7+3\sq
rt5}{2}$, so
$$[RST]=\frac{1}{4\varphi^4}=\frac{1}{2(7+3\sqrt5)}=\frac{7-3\sqrt5}{8}\qquad\te
xt{(when }[ABC]=\tfrac12\text{)}.$$

**Scale to $[ABC]=1$.** Multiply by $2$:
$$[RST]=\frac{7-3\sqrt5}{4}.$$

**Numerical check.** With $t\approx0.382$, $f\approx0.618$, $g\approx0.382$: $R\
approx(0.5,0.309)$, $S\approx(0.191,0.5)$, $T\approx(0.309,0.191)$, giving $[RST
]\approx0.0365$, and $\tfrac{7-3\sqrt5}{4}\approx0.0730$ after scaling to $[ABC]
=1$. ✓

$$\boxed{\dfrac{7-3\sqrt5}{4}}$$
