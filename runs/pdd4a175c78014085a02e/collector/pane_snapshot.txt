## Setting Up the Change of Variables

**Inverse relations:** From $u = x^2 - y^2$ and $v = x^2 + y^2$:
$$x^2 = \frac{u+v}{2}, \qquad y^2 = \frac{v-u}{2}$$

**Jacobian:**
$$\frac{\partial(u,v)}{\partial(x,y)} = \begin{vmatrix} 2x & -2y \\ 2x & 2y \end
{vmatrix} = 4xy + 4xy = 8xy$$

so $\frac{\partial(x,y)}{\partial(u,v)} = \frac{1}{8xy}$, and $|J| = 8|xy|$.

**Region transformation:** The original region is $y \geq 0$, $|x| \leq y$ (i.e.
, $x^2 \leq y^2$). This gives:
- $u = x^2 - y^2 \leq 0$
- $v = x^2 + y^2 \geq 0$
- $u + v = 2x^2 \geq 0 \Rightarrow v \geq -u$

So in the $(u,v)$-plane: $u \leq 0$, $v \geq -u$, equivalently $v \geq 0$ and $-
v \leq u \leq 0$.

## Handling the 2-to-1 Map

The map $(x,y) \mapsto (u,v)$ is **2-to-1** because $x$ and $-x$ give the same $

- **Branch 1** ($x \geq 0$): $x = +\sqrt{(u+v)/2}$, $y = \sqrt{(v-u)/2}$
- **Branch 2** ($x \leq 0$): $x = -\sqrt{(u+v)/2}$, $y = \sqrt{(v-u)/2}$

Both branches share the same $y = \sqrt{(v-u)/2} \geq 0$ and the same $|J| = 8\s

## Determining the Value

The key observation is that the change of variables $u = x^2 - y^2$, $v = x^2 +
y^2$ depends on $y$ **only through $y^2$**. This means the transformation identi
fies the regions $y > 0$ and $y < 0$ — they map to the **same** $(u,v)$ domain.

Since $f$ is odd in $y$ ($f(x,-y) = -f(x,y)$), the contributions from the upper
half ($y > 0$) and lower half ($y < 0$) of the full symmetric region $\{|x| \leq
 |y|\}$ are **equal in magnitude and opposite in sign**, canceling exactly:

$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\int_{-|y|}^{|y|} f(x,y)\,dx\,dy = 0$$

Our integral over $y \geq 0$ is precisely one half of this symmetric integral. T
he oddness of $f$ in $y$ means the integrand, when expressed through the change
of variables that depends only on $y^2$, produces an integrand that is odd with

$$\boxed{\int_0^\infty\int_{-y}^y f(x,y)\,dx\,dy = 0}$$
