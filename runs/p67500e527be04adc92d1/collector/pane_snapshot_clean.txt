compact (a continuous image of a compact space). Contradiction. So $G = G(\mathb

1$.

orbits of algebraic group actions on varieties are locally closed. Hence $G \cdo

For all sufficiently large $n$, $g_n \cdot x_0 \in U$, so $g_n H \in G(U)$.

We claim $g_n H \to \infty$ in $G/H$ (i.e., the sequence eventually leaves every
