## Solution

We compute
$$S_n := \sum_{i=1}^n \ln\!\left(\frac{i}{\sqrt[i]{e}}\right).$$

Since $\sqrt[i]{e}=e^{1/i}$, we have $\ln\!\left(\frac{i}{\sqrt[i]{e}}\right)=\l
n i-\frac{1}{i}$. Hence
$$S_n=\sum_{i=1}^n \ln i-\sum_{i=1}^n\frac{1}{i}=\ln(n!)-H_n,$$
where $H_n$ is the $n$-th harmonic number.

**Lower bound on $\ln(n!)$.** Keeping only the upper half of the factors,
$$\ln(n!)=\sum_{i=1}^n\ln i\;\geq\;\sum_{i=\lceil n/2\rceil}^{n}\ln i\;\geq\;\fr
ac{n}{2}\ln\!\Big(\frac{n}{2}\Big)=\frac{n}{2}(\ln n-\ln 2).$$

**Upper bound on $H_n$.** The standard estimate gives $H_n\leq 1+\ln n$.

**Combining.** Therefore
$$S_n\;\geq\;\frac{n}{2}(\ln n-\ln 2)-(1+\ln n).$$

Dividing by $(\ln n)^2$,
$$\frac{S_n}{(\ln n)^2}\;\geq\;\frac{n}{2\ln n}\!\left(1-\frac{\ln 2}{\ln n}\rig
ht)-\frac{1+\ln n}{(\ln n)^2}.$$

As $n\to\infty$:
- $\dfrac{n}{2\ln n}\to+\infty$, and $\dfrac{\ln 2}{\ln n}\to 0$, so the first t
erm $\to+\infty$;
- $\dfrac{1+\ln n}{(\ln n)^2}=\dfrac{1}{(\ln n)^2}+\dfrac{1}{\ln n}\to 0$.

Thus the right-hand side tends to $+\infty$, which forces
$$\frac{S_n}{(\ln n)^2}\to+\infty.$$

**Consistency check (Stirling).** $\ln(n!)=n\ln n-n+\tfrac12\ln(2\pi n)+o(1)$ an
d $H_n=\ln n+\gamma+o(1)$, so
$$S_n=n\ln n-n+O(\ln n),\qquad \frac{S_n}{(\ln n)^2}=\frac{n}{\ln n}\!\left(1-\f
rac{1}{\ln n}+o\!\left(\frac1{\ln n}\right)\right)\to+\infty,$$
confirming the leading behavior $\sim n/\ln n$.
