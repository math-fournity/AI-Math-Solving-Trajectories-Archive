## Proof

**Euler sequence on $\mathbf{P}^3$.** For projective space $\mathbf{P}^n$ over a
 field $k$, the Euler sequence is the short exact sequence of sheaves

$$0 \longrightarrow \mathcal{O}_{\mathbf{P}^n} \longrightarrow \mathcal{O}_{\mat
hbf{P}^n}(1)^{\oplus(n+1)} \longrightarrow T_{\mathbf{P}^n} \longrightarrow 0,$$

where the first map sends $1 \mapsto (x_0, x_1, \dots, x_n)$ (the tautological s
ubbundle inclusion dually), and the second map is the surjection onto the tangen
t sheaf $T_{\mathbf{P}^n}$. For $n = 3$ this reads

$$0 \longrightarrow \mathcal{O}_{\mathbf{P}^3} \longrightarrow \mathcal{O}_{\mat
hbf{P}^3}(1)^{\oplus 4} \longrightarrow T_{\mathbf{P}^3} \longrightarrow 0. \tag
{$\star$}$$

**Taking global sections.** Applying the left-exact global-sections functor to $
(\star)$ yields the long exact sequence in cohomology

$$0 \to H^0(\mathbf{P}^3, \mathcal{O}) \to H^0(\mathbf{P}^3, \mathcal{O}(1))^{\o
plus 4} \to H^0(\mathbf{P}^3, T) \to H^1(\mathbf{P}^3, \mathcal{O}) \to \cdots$$

**Computing each term.**

- $H^0(\mathbf{P}^3, \mathcal{O}) \cong k$, so $\dim H^0(\mathbf{P}^3, \mathcal{
O}) = 1$ (constant functions).
- $H^0(\mathbf{P}^3, \mathcal{O}(1))$ is the vector space of linear homogeneous
forms in the four homogeneous coordinates $x_0, x_1, x_2, x_3$, hence
  $$\dim H^0(\mathbf{P}^3, \mathcal{O}(1)) = \binom{3+1}{1} = 4,$$
  and therefore $\dim H^0(\mathbf{P}^3, \mathcal{O}(1))^{\oplus 4} = 4 \cdot 4 =
 16$.
- $H^1(\mathbf{P}^3, \mathcal{O}) = 0$. This is the standard vanishing $H^i(\mat
hbf{P}^n, \mathcal{O}(m)) = 0$ for $0 < i < n$ and all $m$; in particular for $n
 = 3$, $i = 1$, $m = 0$.

**Concluding the dimension.** Since $H^1(\mathbf{P}^3, \mathcal{O}) = 0$, the lo
ng exact sequence truncates to the short exact sequence of vector spaces

$$0 \longrightarrow k \longrightarrow k^{16} \longrightarrow H^0(\mathbf{P}^3, T
) \longrightarrow 0.$$

By exactness,

$$\dim H^0(\mathbf{P}^3, T) = \dim k^{16} - \dim k = 16 - 1 = 15.$$

**Sanity check.** The global vector fields on $\mathbf{P}^3$ form the Lie algebr
a of the automorphism group $\mathbf{PGL}(4)$, namely $\mathfrak{sl}(4, k)$, whi
ch has dimension $4^2 - 1 = 15$. This agrees with the computation above.

$$\boxed{\dim H^0(\mathbf{P}^3, T_{\mathbf{P}^3}) = 15}$$
