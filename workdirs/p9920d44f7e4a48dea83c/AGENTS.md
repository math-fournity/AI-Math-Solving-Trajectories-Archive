# Problem

In the city of Metros, an urban planner is tasked with installing a fiber-optic sensor network along the grid lines of a massive square district. The district measures exactly $2009$ meters by $2009$ meters. To monitor the area with precision, a sensor cable must be laid along every single line of a $1$-meter by $1$-meter grid within the district (including the outer boundaries). This means the district is partitioned into $2009 \times 2009$ small square blocks.

The specialized cable-laying machine must move in a continuous, unbroken path to minimize splicing. The machine begins its journey at one of the four corners of the large $2009 \times 2009$ district. It must travel along every vertical and horizontal line segment of the internal and external grid at least once to ensure full coverage. 

What is the length of the shortest possible continuous path, in meters, that the machine must travel to cover every line of the entire grid?
