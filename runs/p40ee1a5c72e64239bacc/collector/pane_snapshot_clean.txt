$$-\langle u, \varphi'\rangle = \langle u, \varphi\rangle \quad \text{for all }

$$v' = (e^{-x})'\,u + e^{-x}\,u' = -e^{-x}u + e^{-x}u' = e^{-x}(u' - u) = 0.$$

So $v' = 0$ in $\mathcal{D}'(\mathbb{R})$.
