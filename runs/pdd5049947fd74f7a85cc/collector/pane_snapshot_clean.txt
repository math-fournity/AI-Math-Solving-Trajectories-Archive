Looking at this problem, I need to determine whether the quotient of equivariant
 cohomology by the positive-degree ideal from the base ring recovers ordinary co
homology for a smooth projective toric variety.

**Answer: Yes.**

## Proof

Let $X = X_\Sigma$ be a smooth projective toric variety over $\mathbb{C}$ with f
an $\Sigma$ in $N_\mathbb{R} \cong \mathbb{R}^n$, acted on by the compact torus
$T = (S^1)^n$ (the maximal compact subgroup of the algebraic torus $(\mathbb{C}^
*)^n$; equivariant cohomology with respect to $T$ coincides with that for $(\mat
hbb{C}^*)^n$).

### Step 1: Equivariant cohomology ring

By the Danilov–Brion description of equivariant cohomology of smooth toric varie
ties, there is a graded ring isomorphism
$$H^*_T(X) \;\cong\; \mathbb{C}\bigl[x_\rho : \rho \in \Sigma(1)\bigr] \big/ I_{
\mathrm{SR}},$$
where $\Sigma(1)$ is the set of rays of $\Sigma$, each variable $x_\rho$ has deg
ree $2$, and $I_{\mathrm{SR}}$ is the **Stanley–Reisner ideal**: the ideal gener
ated by monomials $x_{\rho_1}\cdots x_{\rho_k}$ for which $\{\rho_1,\dots,\rho_k
\}$ does **not** span a cone of $\Sigma$.

### Step 2: The structure map $H^*_T(\mathrm{pt}) \to H^*_T(X)$

We have $H^*_T(\mathrm{pt}) = H^*(BT) \cong \mathbb{C}[t_1,\dots,t_n]$ with $\de
g(t_i)=2$. Under the above identification, the structure homomorphism sends
$$t_i \;\longmapsto\; \sum_{\rho \in \Sigma(1)} \langle u_i, v_\rho\rangle\, x_\
rho \pmod{I_{\mathrm{SR}}},$$
where $\{u_1,\dots,u_n\}$ is a basis of the character lattice $M$ and $v_\rho \i
n N$ is the primitive generator of ray $\rho$. Denote the linear form $\sum_\rho
 \langle u_i, v_\rho\rangle\, x_\rho$ by $L_i$, and let
$$J = \langle L_1, \dots, L_n \rangle \;\subset\; \mathbb{C}[x_\rho]$$
be the ideal they generate. The ideal $H^{>0}_T(\mathrm{pt})\cdot H^*_T(X)$ in $
H^*_T(X)$ is then the image of $J$ in the quotient $\mathbb{C}[x_\rho]/I_{\mathr
m{SR}}$, i.e., the ideal $(I_{\mathrm{SR}} + J)/I_{\mathrm{SR}}$.

### Step 3: Ordinary cohomology ring

By the Danilov–Jurkiewicz theorem for smooth **complete** toric varieties (proje
ctive $\Rightarrow$ complete), the ordinary cohomology ring is
$$H^*(X) \;\cong\; \mathbb{C}\bigl[x_\rho : \rho \in \Sigma(1)\bigr] \big/ \bigl
(I_{\mathrm{SR}} + J\bigr),$$
with $\deg(x_\rho) = 2$.

### Step 4: The quotient

Computing the quotient algebra:
$$\frac{H^*_T(X)}{H^{>0}_T(\mathrm{pt})\cdot H^*_T(X)} \;=\; \frac{\mathbb{C}[x_
\rho]/I_{\mathrm{SR}}}{(I_{\mathrm{SR}}+J)/I_{\mathrm{SR}}} \;\cong\; \frac{\mat
hbb{C}[x_\rho]}{I_{\mathrm{SR}}+J} \;\cong\; H^*(X).$$

The third isomorphism is the Danilov–Jurkiewicz identification from Step 3.

### Step 5: Graded algebra isomorphism

All generators $x_\rho$ carry degree $2$ in both $H^*_T(X)$ and $H^*(X)$, and th
e elements $L_i$ (images of $t_i$) being quotiented out are homogeneous of degre
e $2$. The quotient by a homogeneous ideal preserves the grading, so the isomorp
hism above is an isomorphism of **graded** $\mathbb{C}$-algebras. $\blacksquare$
