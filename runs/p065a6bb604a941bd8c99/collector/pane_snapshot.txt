## Setting Up the Process

Place ball 1, then move counterclockwise. Each time we **pass** a previously pla
ced ball, we place a new ball in the gap **counterclockwise ahead** of that ball
 (at its midpoint), then continue from the new ball.

**Tracing the first few rounds:**

- **Ball 1** at angle 0. Move counterclockwise a full revolution, pass ball 1. G
ap ahead of ball 1 = full circle. Place **ball 2** at angle π.
- Continue from π. Pass ball 1 at 2π. Gap ahead of ball 1 = [1→2] (0 to π). Plac
e **ball 3** at π/2.
- Continue from π/2. Pass ball 2 at π. Gap ahead of ball 2 = [2→1] (π to 2π). Pl
ace **ball 4** at 3π/2.

After 4 balls: positions 0, π/2, π, 3π/2 — **equally spaced!** Counterclockwise
order: **[1, 3, 2, 4]**.

Continuing similarly for balls 5–8, after 8 balls they are equally spaced at mul
tiples of π/4, in counterclockwise order: **[1, 5, 3, 6, 2, 7, 4, 8]**.

## Key Structural Lemma

**After round $n$ (i.e., after placing $2^n$ balls), the balls are equally space
d.** In round $n$, we traverse all $2^{n-1}$ existing balls in counterclockwise
order, placing one new ball in each gap. The round-$n$ array (counterclockwise)
interleaves old and new:

$$[a_0,\; 2^{n-1}{+}1,\; a_1,\; 2^{n-1}{+}2,\; \dots,\; a_{2^{n-1}-1},\; 2^n]$$

where $[a_0, \dots, a_{2^{n-1}-1}]$ is the round-$(n{-}1)$ array.

**Recursive formula:** The ball at position $p$ in round $n$:
- If $p$ is **even**: equals the ball at position $p/2$ in round $n{-}1$.
- If $p$ is **odd**: equals ball $2^{n-1} + \frac{p+1}{2}$.
