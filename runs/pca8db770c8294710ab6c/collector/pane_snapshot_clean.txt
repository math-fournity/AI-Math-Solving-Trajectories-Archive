$ determines the entire sequence from $(f(1), f(2)) = (1, a)$. We seek all posit

- $f(3) = \frac{a^2 + 1997}{1} = a^2 + 1997$ (always a positive integer).
- $f(4) = \frac{(a^2+1997)^2 + 1997}{a} = a^3 + 3994a + \frac{1997 \cdot 1998}{a

For $f(4)$ to be an integer, we need $a \mid 1997 \cdot 1998$.

**Factoring.** Since $1998 - 1997 = 1$, we have $\gcd(1997, 1998) = 1$. One chec

2 \cdot 2 \cdot 4 \cdot 2 = 32$ divisors. Every divisor is either $d$ or $1997d$
 where $d \mid 1998$.

**Conserved quantity.** Define $I_n = \frac{f(n-1) + f(n+1)}{f(n)}$. A direct co
mputation shows $I$ is constant:

$$I_{n+1} - I_n = \frac{(f(n+1)-f(n-1))(f(n)^2 + 1997 - f(n-1)f(n+1))}{f(n)f(n+1
)f(n-1)} = 0,$$

since $f(n-1)f(n+1) = f(n)^2 + 1997$. Thus $f(n+1) = I \cdot f(n) - f(n-1)$ is a
 **linear recurrence** with

$$I = \frac{f(1) + f(3)}{f(2)} = \frac{1 + a^2 + 1997}{a} = a + \frac{1998}{a}.$
$
