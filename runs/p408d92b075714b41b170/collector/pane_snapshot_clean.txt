Looking at this problem, I need to find all possible values of $z$ from the cycl
ic system and sum them.

## Setting Up the Iteration

Rewrite the equations:
- $z^2 + 5x = 10z \Rightarrow x = \frac{10z - z^2}{5} = g(z)$
- $y^2 + 5z = 10y \Rightarrow z = g(y)$
- $x^2 + 5y = 10x \Rightarrow y = g(x)$

where $g(t) = 2t - \tfrac{t^2}{5}$.

From $x = g(z)$, $y = g(x)$, $z = g(y)$, composing gives $z = g(g(g(z))) = g^{\c
irc 3}(z)$. So $z$ is a fixed point of $g^{\circ 3}$ — either a fixed point of $
g$ itself, or an element of a 3-cycle of $g$.

## Simplifying the Map

Substitute $t = 5u$, so $g(5u) = 5(2u - u^2) = 5\,h(u)$ where $h(u) = 2u - u^2$.

Complete the square: $h(u) = 1 - (u-1)^2$. Setting $v = u - 1$:
$$v \mapsto -v^2$$

Three iterations: $v \to -v^2 \to -v^4 \to -v^8$.

Fixed points of 3 iterations: $-v^8 = v$, i.e., $v(v^7 + 1) = 0$.

## Extracting the Solutions

**Case $v = 0$:** $u = 1$, $t = 5$. This is the fixed point $z = 5$.

**Case $v^7 = -1$:** Seven roots $v_k = e^{i(2k+1)\pi/7}$, $k = 0,\dots,6$.

- Fixed points of $h$ satisfy $v = -v^2$, i.e., $v(v+1)=0$: $v=0$ (already found
) and $v = -1$. Indeed $(-1)^7 = -1$, so $v = -1$ (i.e., $k=3$) is the fixed poi
nt $t = 0$.
- The remaining **6 roots** form **3-cycles** under $v \mapsto -v^2$ (since $v \
to -v^2 \to -v^4 \to -v^8 = v$ using $v^7 = -1$). These give $6/3 = 2$ distinct
3-cycles, contributing 6 values of $z$.

All values of $z$ are: $\{0, 5\}$ (fixed points) plus $\{5(v_k+1) : k \neq 3\}$
(3-cycle elements) — **8 distinct values** total.

## Computing the Sum

**Fixed points:** $0 + 5 = 5$.

**3-cycle elements:** $z = 5(v+1) = 5v + 5$ for the 6 non-fixed roots of $v^7 +
1 = 0$.

By Vieta's formulas, the sum of all 7 roots of $v^7 + 1 = 0$ is $0$. Removing th
e fixed-point root $v = -1$:
$$\sum_{\text{6 roots}} v = 0 - (-1) = 1$$

Therefore:
$$\sum_{\text{3-cycle}} z = \sum (5v + 5) = 5\cdot 1 + 6\cdot 5 = 5 + 30 = 35$$

**Total sum of all possible values of $z$:**
$$5 + 35 = \boxed{40}$$
