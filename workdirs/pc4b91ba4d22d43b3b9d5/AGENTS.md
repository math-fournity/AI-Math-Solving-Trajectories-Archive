# Problem

A specialized high-tech laboratory utilizes square sheets of graphene to manufacture hexagonal microchips. The fabrication process follows a strict protocol: a technician selects any existing single piece of material and performs a single, continuous straight-line laser cut that divides that specific piece into exactly two separate parts.

The production goal is to continue this process until the laboratory has successfully produced exactly 100 pieces that are each shaped as a hexagon (a polygon with exactly six sides). Any additional pieces created during the process that do not form hexagons are considered byproduct and do not count toward the goal, but they may be further subdivided if necessary.

Starting with one initial square sheet, what is the minimum number of straight-line cuts required to reach the target of 100 hexagons?
