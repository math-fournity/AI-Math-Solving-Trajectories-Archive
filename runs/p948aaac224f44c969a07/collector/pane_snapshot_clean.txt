Looking at this problem, I need to find the area of points inside a regular octa
gon (side from (0,0) to (1,0)) within taxicab distance 2/3 of some vertex.

## Setting Up the Octagon

Let $a = 1/\sqrt{2}$. Traversing counterclockwise from $(0,0)$, with exterior an
gles of $45°$ and side length $1$, the vertices are:

| Vertex | Coordinates |
|--------|-------------|
| $V_0$ | $(0, 0)$ |
| $V_1$ | $(1, 0)$ |
| $V_2$ | $(1+a, a)$ |
| $V_3$ | $(1+a, 1+a)$ |
| $V_4$ | $(1, 1+2a)$ |
| $V_5$ | $(0, 1+2a)$ |
| $V_6$ | $(-a, 1+a)$ |
| $V_7$ | $(-a, a)$ |

The center is $(1/2,\; 1/2 + a)$. The setup has $D_4$ symmetry (90° rotations an
d reflections about horizontal/vertical/diagonal axes through the center), since
 the taxicab metric has $D_4$ symmetry.

## Individual Ball–Octagon Intersections

**Key observation:** Since $2/3 < a = 1/\sqrt{2} \approx 0.707$, each taxicab ba
ll of radius $2/3$ around a vertex only reaches the two edges adjacent to that v
ertex (verified by checking that the ball doesn't reach any non-adjacent edge).
So each $B_i \cap \text{Octagon}$ is determined locally by the corner.

**At $V_0 = (0,0)$:** The adjacent edges are $y = 0$ (interior: $y \geq 0$) and
$y = -x$ (interior: $x + y \geq 0$). The ball $|x|+|y| \leq 2/3$ cut by $y \geq
0$ gives a triangle of area $4/9$. Cutting by $x+y \geq 0$ removes the triangle
with vertices $(0,0)$, $(-2/3, 0)$, $(-1/3, 1/3)$ of area $1/9$.

$$\text{Area}(B_{V_0} \cap \text{Oct}) = \frac{4}{9} - \frac{1}{9} = \frac{1}{3}
$$

**At $V_1 = (1,0)$:** In local coordinates $(u,v) = (x-1, y)$, the corner is $v
\geq 0$, $v \geq u$. By the same computation (the constraint $v \geq u$ cuts off
 a triangle of area $1/9$):

$$\text{Area}(B_{V_1} \cap \text{Oct}) = \frac{4}{9} - \frac{1}{9} = \frac{1}{3}
$$

**At $V_2 = (1+a, a)$:** In local coordinates, the corner is $u \leq 0$, $v \geq
 u$. The left half of the diamond has area $4/9$; the constraint $v \geq u$ remo
ves a triangle of area $1/9$.

$$\text{Area}(B_{V_2} \cap \text{Oct}) = \frac{1}{3}$$

By the $D_4$ symmetry (and direct verification), **all 8 vertices give area $1/3
$**.

## Overlaps Between Adjacent Balls

**Taxicab distances between all vertex pairs:** The only pairs with taxicab dist
ance $< 4/3$ are the **horizontal/vertical edges**: $(V_0,V_1)$, $(V_2,V_3)$, $(
V_4,V_5)$, $(V_6,V_7)$, each with distance $1$. The diagonal edges (e.g., $V_0$–
$V_7$) have taxicab distance $\sqrt{2} \approx 1.414 > 4/3$, so **no overlap**.
All non-adjacent pairs have distance $\geq 1+\sqrt{2} > 4/3$.

**Overlap of $B_{V_0}$ and $B_{V_1}$ within the octagon:** For $y \geq 0$, the o
verlap requires $x \in [1/3, 2/3]$ and $0 \leq y \leq \min(2/3 - x,\; x - 1/3)$.

$$\text{Area} = \int_{1/3}^{1/2}(x - \tfrac{1}{3})\,dx + \int_{1/2}^{2/3}(\tfrac
{2}{3} - x)\,dx = \frac{1}{72} + \frac{1}{72} = \frac{1}{36}$$

The constraints $x+y \geq 0$ and $y \geq x-1$ are automatically satisfied in thi
s region (since $x \geq 1/3 > 0$ and $x \leq 2/3 < 1$), so this is the full over
lap within the octagon.

By $D_4$ symmetry, all 4 overlapping pairs have overlap $1/36$.

**Triple overlaps:** None exist, since no three vertices are pairwise within tax
icab distance $4/3$.

## Inclusion-Exclusion

$$\text{Area}(S) = 8 \cdot \frac{1}{3} - 4 \cdot \frac{1}{36} = \frac{8}{3} - \f
rac{1}{9} = \frac{24}{9} - \frac{1}{9} = \frac{23}{9}$$

## Final Answer

We have $\frac{m}{n} = \frac{23}{9}$ with $\gcd(23, 9) = 1$ (since $23$ is prime
 and $9 = 3^2$).

$$100m + n = 100 \cdot 23 + 9 = \boxed{2309}$$
