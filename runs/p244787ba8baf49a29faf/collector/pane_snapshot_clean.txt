Looking at this problem, I need to find the area of the cross-section of a regul
ar hexagonal prism cut by a plane through vertices A, C', and E.

## Setup

Place the floor hexagon (side 6, circumradius 6) in the z=0 plane, centered at t
he origin:

- A = (6, 0, 0), B = (3, 3√3, 0), C = (−3, 3√3, 0), D = (−6, 0, 0), E = (−3, −3√
3, 0), F = (3, −3√3, 0)
- Ceiling vertices at z = 5: A' = (6,0,5), B' = (3, 3√3, 5), C' = (−3, 3√3, 5),
etc.

## Finding the Plane

Two vectors in the plane through A:
- AC' = (−9, 3√3, 5), AE = (−9, −3√3, 0)

Normal: n = AC' × AE = (15√3, −45, 54√3), simplified to **n = (5√3, −15, 18√3)**
.

Plane equation: 5√3 x − 15y + 18√3 z = 30√3.

## Identifying Cross-Section Vertices

Evaluating f = 5√3 x − 15y + 18√3 z − 30√3 at all 12 vertices:

| Vertex | f/√3 | Vertex | f/√3 |
|--------|------|--------|------|
| A | 0 | A' | 90 |
| B | −60 | B' | 30 |
| C | −90 | C' | 0 |
| D | −60 | D' | 30 |
| E | 0 | E' | 90 |
| F | 30 | F' | 120 |

**Sign changes on vertical edges:**
- **BB'**: f goes from −60√3 to +30√3 → intersection at t = 10/3, giving **P_B =
 (3, 3√3, 10/3)**
- **DD'**: f goes from −60√3 to +30√3 → intersection at t = 10/3, giving **P_D =
 (−6, 0, 10/3)**

**Floor face:** Plane intersects along segment AE (both on z=0 plane).
**Ceiling face:** All ceiling vertices have f ≥ 0 with only C' = 0, so the plane
 touches the ceiling only at point C'.

The cross-section is the **pentagon**: A → P_B → C' → P_D → E.

## Computing the Area

Triangulating from A into three triangles:

**Triangle 1 (A, P_B, C'):** Vectors u = (−3, 3√3, 10/3), v = (−9, 3√3, 5)
- u × v = (5√3, −15, 18√3), |u × v| = √(75+225+972) = √1272 = 2√318
- Area₁ = √318

**Triangle 2 (A, C', P_D):** Vectors u = (−9, 3√3, 5), v = (−12, 0, 10/3)
- u × v = (10√3, −30, 36√3), |u × v|² = 300+900+3888 = 5088 = 16·318
- |u × v| = 4√318, Area₂ = 2√318

**Triangle 3 (A, P_D, E):** Vectors u = (−12, 0, 10/3), v = (−9, −3√3, 0)
- u × v = (10√3, −30, 36√3), |u × v| = 4√318
- Area₃ = 2√318

**Total Area** = √318 + 2√318 + 2√318 = **5√318**

## Verification

318 = 2 × 3 × 53, which is square-free. ✓

The area is 5√318, so m = 5 and n = 318.

**m + n = 5 + 318 = 323**
