## Setting Up the Graph

The 7 islands form a graph where the available bridges are:
- **Path edges** (consecutive): $(A_1A_2), (A_2A_3), (A_3A_4), (A_4A_5), (A_5A_6
), (A_6A_7)$ — a path $A_1\text{-}A_2\text{-}\cdots\text{-}A_7$
- **Spoke edges** (to $A_7$): $(A_1A_7), (A_2A_7), (A_3A_7), (A_4A_7), (A_5A_7),
 (A_6A_7)$

The edge $(A_6, A_7)$ appears in both lists, so there are $6+6-1=11$ distinct ed
ges.

**Minimum bridges for connectivity** = spanning tree = $7-1=6$ bridges.

The graph is a **fan graph**: a path on vertices $A_1,\ldots,A_6$ plus a hub $A_
7$ connected to all six path vertices.

## Counting Spanning Trees via the Matrix-Tree Theorem

I compute the Laplacian and delete the row/column for $A_7$, leaving a $6\times
6$ matrix $M$ for vertices $A_1,\ldots,A_6$.

**Degrees:** $\deg(A_1)=2,\ \deg(A_2)=\deg(A_3)=\deg(A_4)=\deg(A_5)=3,\ \deg(A_6
)=2$

**Edges among $A_1,\ldots,A_6$:** $(A_1A_2),(A_2A_3),(A_3A_4),(A_4A_5),(A_5A_6)$
 — a path.

$$M = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ -1 & 3 & -1 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & -
1 & 3 & -1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & -1 & 3 & -1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & -1 & 3 & -1 \\ 0 &
0 & 0 & 0 & -1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$$

## Computing the Determinant

Let $d_k$ = leading $k\times k$ principal minor. The tridiagonal recurrence is $
d_k = a_k\, d_{k-1} - d_{k-2}$, where $a_k$ is the $k$-th diagonal entry.

| $k$ | $a_k$ | $d_k = a_k \cdot d_{k-1} - d_{k-2}$ | Value |
|-----|-------|--------------------------------------|-------|
| 1 | 2 | — | 2 |
| 2 | 3 | $3\cdot2 - 0 = 6$ | Wait: $d_2 = 3\cdot 2 - 1\cdot d_0$. Using $d_0=1$
: $3\cdot2-1=5$ | 5 |
| 3 | 3 | $3\cdot5 - 2 = 13$ | 13 |
| 4 | 3 | $3\cdot13 - 5 = 34$ | 34 |
| 5 | 3 | $3\cdot34 - 13 = 89$ | 89 |
| 6 | 2 | $2\cdot89 - 34 = 144$ | **144** |

(Here $d_0=1$ is the empty-product convention, and the off-diagonal entries are
all $-1$, so the subtraction term is $(-1)^2 \cdot d_{k-2} = d_{k-2}$.)

## Result

$$\det(M) = 2\cdot 89 - 34 = 178 - 34 = \boxed{144}$$

The number of ways to build bridges with the minimum number (6) so that all isla
