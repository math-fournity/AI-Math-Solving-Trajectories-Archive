 every Lie subalgebra $\mathfrak h \subseteq \mathfrak g$ integrates to a unique
 connected immersed Lie subgroup of $G$. If $G$ had a 2-dimensional Lie subgroup

would correspond to a 2-dimensional Lie subalgebra of $\mathfrak{so}(3)$. Thus i

> $\mathfrak{so}(3)$ has no 2-dimensional Lie subalgebra.

[E_1,E_2]=E_3,\qquad [E_2,E_3]=E_1,\qquad [E_3,E_1]=E_2.
