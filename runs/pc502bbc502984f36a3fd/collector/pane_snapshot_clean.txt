Dimension 8 (Unknown): No complete, widely-accepted classification of all 8-

open problem. The difficulty arises from the explosive growth in the number of
isomorphism classes and the increasing complexity of continuous parameter
families.

Dimensions ≥ 9 (Unknown): The classification problem becomes progressively
