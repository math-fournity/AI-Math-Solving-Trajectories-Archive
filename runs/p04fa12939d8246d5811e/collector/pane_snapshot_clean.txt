## Solution

**Setup.** Place the trapezoid $ABCD$ in the $z=0$ plane with $D$ at the origin
and $DC$ along the $x$-axis.

Given $AB=2$, $AD=1$, $DC=1$, $AB\parallel CD$, $\angle DAB=\pi/3$:
- $D=(0,0,0)$, $C=(1,0,0)$.
- $AB\parallel CD$ (the $x$-direction), $|AB|=2$, so $B=A\pm(2,0,0)$.
- $\angle DAB=\pi/3$ at $A$ between $\vec{AD}$ and $\vec{AB}$: with $\vec{AB}=(2
,0,0)$, $\cos\frac{\pi}{3}=\frac{\vec{AD}\cdot\vec{AB}}{|\vec{AD}||\vec{AB}|}=\f
rac{-2a_x}{2}=\frac12 \Rightarrow a_x=-\frac12$.
- $|AD|=1 \Rightarrow a_x^2+a_y^2=1 \Rightarrow a_y=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}$.

So $A=(-\tfrac12,\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2},0)$, $B=(\tfrac32,\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2},0)$.

**Apex.** $SD\perp$ plane $ABCD$, so $S=(0,0,h)$. Given $SC=\sqrt2$:
$$SC^2 = 1+h^2 = 2 \Rightarrow h=1, \quad S=(0,0,1).$$

**Sphere.** Let center $O=(c_x,c_y,c_z)$, radius $R$.

- From $D=(0,0,0)$ and $C=(1,0,0)$: $c_x=\tfrac12$.
- From $D$ and $S=(0,0,1)$: $c_z=\tfrac12$.
- From $D$: $R^2=\tfrac14+c_y^2+\tfrac14=\tfrac12+c_y^2$.
- From $A=(-\tfrac12,\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2},0)$:
$$\left(-\tfrac12-\tfrac12\right)^2+\left(\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}-c_y\right)^2+\left(0
-\tfrac12\right)^2=R^2$$
$$1+\tfrac34-\sqrt3\,c_y+c_y^2+\tfrac14 = R^2 \Rightarrow 2-\sqrt3\,c_y+c_y^2=R^
2.$$

Equating with $R^2=\tfrac12+c_y^2$:
$$\tfrac12 = 2-\sqrt3\,c_y \Rightarrow c_y=\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}.$$

Thus $R^2=\tfrac12+\tfrac34=\tfrac54$.

**Verification.** Center $O=(\tfrac12,\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2},\tfrac12)$:
- $D$: $\tfrac14+\tfrac34+\tfrac14=\tfrac54$ ✓
- $C$: $\tfrac14+\tfrac34+\tfrac14=\tfrac54$ ✓
- $S$: $\tfrac14+\tfrac34+\tfrac14=\tfrac54$ ✓
- $A$: $1+0+\tfrac14=\tfrac54$ ✓
- $B$: $1+0+\tfrac14=\tfrac54$ ✓

All five vertices lie on the sphere.

**Surface area:**
$$\boxed{4\pi R^2 = 4\pi\cdot\tfrac54 = 5\pi}$$

Final Answer: $\boxed{5\pi}$
