## Proof

Place the unit circle at the origin of the complex plane, and let the vertices b
e $A_k = e^{i\theta_k}$ for $k=1,\dots,n$.

**Step 1: Express the sum of squares in terms of the vertices.**

For any pair $i<j$, the squared distance is
$$|A_iA_j|^2 = |e^{i\theta_i}-e^{i\theta_j}|^2 = 2 - 2\cos(\theta_i-\theta_j) =
4\sin^2\!\tfrac{\theta_i-\theta_j}{2}.$$

The sum of the squares of all sides and diagonals is the sum over **all** $\bino
m{n}{2}$ pairs:
$$\Sigma := \sum_{1\le i<j\le n}|A_iA_j|^2 = \sum_{i<j}\bigl(2-2\cos(\theta_i-\t
heta_j)\bigr).$$

**Step 2: Relate the cosine sum to the centroid.**

Let $S = \sum_{k=1}^n e^{i\theta_k}$ be the sum of the position vectors of the v
ertices. Then
$$|S|^2 = \sum_{k=1}^n|e^{i\theta_k}|^2 + 2\sum_{i<j}\cos(\theta_i-\theta_j) = n
 + 2\sum_{i<j}\cos(\theta_i-\theta_j),$$
so
$$\sum_{i<j}\cos(\theta_i-\theta_j) = \frac{|S|^2 - n}{2}.$$

**Step 3: Compute $\Sigma$.**

$$\Sigma = 2\binom{n}{2} - 2\cdot\frac{|S|^2-n}{2} = n(n-1) - |S|^2 + n = n^2 -
|S|^2.$$

**Step 4: Maximize.**

Since $|S|^2 \ge 0$, we obtain
$$\Sigma = n^2 - |S|^2 \le n^2,$$
with equality if and only if $|S|^2=0$, i.e.
$$\sum_{k=1}^n e^{i\theta_k} = 0.$$

This condition means the centroid of the $n$ vertices (regarded as position vect
ors) coincides with the center of the circle.

**Attainability.** This condition is achievable: the regular $n$-gon satisfies i
t (the roots of unity sum to zero), and more generally any configuration of $n$
points on the unit circle whose vector sum vanishes. For instance, when $n$ is e
ven, placing vertices in $n/2$ antipodal pairs also works.

**Verification.** For $n=3$ (equilateral triangle): three sides each of length $
\sqrt{3}$, giving $3\cdot 3 = 9 = 3^2$. ✓
For $n=4$ (square): four sides of length $\sqrt{2}$ and two diagonals of length
$2$, giving $4\cdot 2 + 2\cdot 4 = 16 = 4^2$. ✓

## Conclusion

The maximum value of the sum of the squares of all sides and all diagonals of a
convex $n$-gon inscribed in a unit circle is

$$\boxed{n^2}.$$

This maximum is achieved if and only if the centroid of the $n$ vertices coincid
es with the center of the circle, i.e., $\sum_{k=1}^n \overrightarrow{OA_k} = \m
athbf{0}$. In particular, the regular $n$-gon is one such extremal configuration
.
