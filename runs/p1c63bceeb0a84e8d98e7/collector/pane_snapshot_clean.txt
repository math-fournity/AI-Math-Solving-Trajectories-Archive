## Proof

**Claim.** Yes. The spectral radius of $A$ on $X$ equals $r$.

**Setup and notation.** Let $\|\cdot\|$ denote the norm of $X$. Since $A(E)\subs
eteq E$ and $E$ is a linear subspace, the restriction $A|_E : E\to E$ is a well-
defined linear operator on the normed space $E$ (equipped with the inherited nor
m). Its operator norm is
$$\|A|_E\| := \sup_{\substack{e\in E\\ \|e\|\le 1}} \|Ae\|.$$
Likewise $(A|_E)^n = A^n\big|_E$ for every $n\ge 1$, since $A$ maps $E$ into its
elf. The spectral radius of $A|_E$ is, by Gelfand's formula (valid for any bound
ed operator on a normed space),
$$r = \rho(A|_E) = \lim_{n\to\infty} \|(A|_E)^n\|^{1/n} = \inf_{n\ge 1} \|(A|_E)
^n\|^{1/n}.$$
Similarly $\rho_X(A) = \lim_{n\to\infty} \|A^n\|_X^{1/n}$.

**Key lemma.** For every bounded operator $T:X\to X$ and every dense linear subs
pace $E\subseteq X$,
$$\sup_{\substack{e\in E\\ \|e\|\le 1}}\|Te\| \;=\; \sup_{\substack{x\in X\\ \|x
\|\le 1}}\|Tx\| \;=\; \|T\|_X.$$

*Proof of lemma.* The inequality "$\le$" is immediate from $E\subseteq X$. For "
$\ge$": fix $x\in X$ with $\|x\|\le 1$ and $\varepsilon>0$. By density, pick $y_
k\in E$ with $y_k\to x$. Set $e_k := y_k/\max(1,\|y_k\|)$. Because $E$ is a line
ar subspace, $e_k\in E$, and $\|e_k\|\le 1$ by construction. Since $\|y_k\|\to\|
x\|\le 1$, we have $\max(1,\|y_k\|)\to 1$, so $e_k\to x$. By continuity of $T$,
$\|Te_k\|\to\|Tx\|$. Hence $\sup_{e\in E,\|e\|\le 1}\|Te\|\ge \|Tx\|$. Taking th
e supremum over $x$ gives equality. $\square$

**Applying the lemma.** For each $n\ge 1$, $A^n$ is a bounded operator on $X$ an
d $(A|_E)^n = A^n\big|_E$. The lemma (with $T=A^n$) gives
$$\|(A|_E)^n\| = \|A^n\big|_E\| = \|A^n\|_X \qquad\text{for all } n\ge 1.$$

**Conclusion.** Therefore
$$\rho_X(A) = \lim_{n\to\infty}\|A^n\|_X^{1/n} = \lim_{n\to\infty}\|(A|_E)^n\|^{
1/n} = \rho(A|_E) = r.$$

The spectral radius of $A$ on $X$ is exactly $r$. $\blacksquare$
