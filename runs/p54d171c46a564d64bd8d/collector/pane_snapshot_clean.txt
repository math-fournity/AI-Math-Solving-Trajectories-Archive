## Proof

We interpret $\nabla^{d+1}_{A\mid k}$ as the $(d+1)$-st exterior power $\bigwedg
e^{d+1}_A \Omega^1_{A/k}$ of the module of Kähler differentials. We claim

$$\nabla^{d+1}_{A\mid k} \;\cong\; 0.$$

**Step 1. $\Omega^1_{A/k}$ is projective, and its rank at a prime is the relativ
e dimension.**

Since $A$ is a smooth $k$-algebra (in particular of finite presentation, flat, w
ith geometrically regular fibers), the module of Kähler differentials $\Omega^1_
{A/k}$ is a finitely generated projective $A$-module. For every prime $\mathfrak
 p\subset A$, the localization $(\Omega^1_{A/k})_{\mathfrak p}$ is free, and its
 rank equals the relative dimension of $A$ over $k$ at $\mathfrak p$:
$$\operatorname{rank}_{\mathfrak p}(\Omega^1_{A/k}) \;=\; \operatorname{rel.dim}
_{\mathfrak p}(A/k) \;=\; \dim_{\kappa(\mathfrak p)}\bigl(\Omega^1_{A/k}\otimes_
A \kappa(\mathfrak p)\bigr).$$

**Step 2. The rank is bounded by $d=\dim A$.**

Let $\mathfrak q = \mathfrak p\cap k$ and set $q=\mathfrak q$. By the dimension
formula for smooth morphisms (EGA IV, 6.5.2; smooth morphisms are flat and of fi
nite presentation with regular fibers, so the formula applies to the Noetherian
rings involved):
$$\dim(A_{\mathfrak p}) \;=\; \dim(k_{\mathfrak q}) \;+\; \operatorname{rel.dim}
_{\mathfrak p}(A/k).$$
Since $\dim(k_{\mathfrak q})\ge 0$, we obtain
$$\operatorname{rel.dim}_{\mathfrak p}(A/k) \;=\; \dim(A_{\mathfrak p})-\dim(k_{
\mathfrak q}) \;\le\; \dim(A_{\mathfrak p}) \;\le\; \dim(A) \;=\; d.$$
Hence $\operatorname{rank}_{\mathfrak p}(\Omega^1_{A/k})\le d$ for every $\mathf
rak p$.

**Step 3. The $(d+1)$-st exterior power vanishes.**

The exterior power $\bigwedge^{d+1}_A \Omega^1_{A/k}$ is again a finitely genera
ted projective $A$-module, and localization commutes with exterior powers:
$$\Bigl(\textstyle\bigwedge^{d+1}_A \Omega^1_{A/k}\Bigr)_{\mathfrak p} \;\cong\;
 \textstyle\bigwedge^{d+1}_{A_{\mathfrak p}} (\Omega^1_{A/k})_{\mathfrak p} \;\c
ong\; \textstyle\bigwedge^{d+1}\bigl(A_{\mathfrak p}^{\,r_{\mathfrak p}}\bigr),$
$
where $r_{\mathfrak p}=\operatorname{rank}_{\mathfrak p}(\Omega^1_{A/k})\le d$.
The exterior power $\bigwedge^{d+1}$ of a free module of rank $r_{\mathfrak p}\l
e d<d+1$ is zero. Therefore
$$\Bigl(\textstyle\bigwedge^{d+1}_A \Omega^1_{A/k}\Bigr)_{\mathfrak p} \;=\; 0$$
for every prime $\mathfrak p$. A finitely generated module whose localization at
 every prime is zero is itself zero. Consequently
$$\nabla^{d+1}_{A\mid k} \;=\; \textstyle\bigwedge^{d+1}_A \Omega^1_{A/k} \;\con
g\; 0.$$

**Conclusion.** Yes, the Kähler differentials $\nabla^{d+1}_{A\mid k}$ vanish: $
\nabla^{d+1}_{A\mid k}\cong 0$. This is the algebraic counterpart of the geometr
ic fact that on a $d$-dimensional smooth manifold every $(d+1)$-form is identica
lly zero.
