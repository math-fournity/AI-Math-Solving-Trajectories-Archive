$p - 1 = 1$, which is a unit in $\mathbb{Q}$, hence a non-zero-divisor, hence re
gular.

- **Injective:** If $m/1 = 0/1$, then $p^n m = 0$ for some $n$. Since $p^n$ is a
 unit, $m = 0$.
- **Surjective:** Any $m/p^n \in S^{-1}M$ equals $p^{-n}m / 1 = \varphi(p^{-n}m)
$, using $p^{-n} \in R$.

**Step 4: $A_p = B_p$.**
Take $A = B = \mathbb{Q}$ (as $R$-modules, or as subrings). Then:
$$A_p \cong A = B \cong B_p$$
so $A_p = B_p$.
