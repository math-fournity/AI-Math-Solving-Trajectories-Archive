## Solution

**Degree analysis.** Let $f$ have degree $n$. As a polynomial in $x$ (with $y$ f
ixed), the inner argument $f(x)-2y$ has degree $n$, so $f(f(x)-2y)$ has degree $
n^2$. On the RHS, $f(y)-x$ has degree $1$ in $x$, so $f(f(y)-x)$ has degree $n$
in $x$, and $2x-3y$ has degree $1$. Hence the RHS has degree $\max(1,n)$ in $x$.
 Equating: $n^2 = \max(1,n)$, which forces $n=1$ (for $n\geq 2$, $n^2>n$). So $f

**Determining the linear form.** Let $f(x)=ax+b$. Then

- RHS: $2x-3y+f(ay+b-x)=2x-3y+a(ay+b-x)+b=2x-3y+a^2y+ab-ax+b$.
