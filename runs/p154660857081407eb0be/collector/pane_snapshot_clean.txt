Looking at this problem, I need to determine whether a smooth map $G$ homotopic
to $F$ with prescribed boundary values $g$ necessarily exists, given that $g$ is
 homotopic to $f = F|_{\partial M}$.

**Answer: Yes, such $G$ always exists.**

## Proof

**Step 1 — Topological Homotopy Extension.**

Since $M$ is a smooth manifold with boundary, the inclusion $\iota: \partial M \
hookrightarrow M$ is a cofibration (a collar neighborhood exists). Thus the pair
 $(M, \partial M)$ satisfies the homotopy extension property in the topological
category.

Given the smooth homotopy $H: \partial M \times I \to N$ with $H(-,0) = f = F|_{
\partial M}$, the HEP yields a continuous homotopy $\widetilde{H}: M \times I \t
o N$ satisfying:
- $\widetilde{H}(x, 0) = F(x)$ for all $x \in M$,
- $\widetilde{H}(p, t) = H(p, t)$ for all $p \in \partial M,\; t \in I$.

Set $G_0 = \widetilde{H}(-,1): M \to N$. Then $G_0$ is continuous, $G_0|_{\parti
al M} = g$, and $G_0 \simeq F$.

**Step 2 — Smoothing near the boundary.**

Choose a collar neighborhood $C: \partial M \times [0,3) \hookrightarrow M$ with
 $C(\partial M \times \{0\}) = \partial M$. Define a continuous reparametrizatio
n $\rho: [0,3) \to [0,3)$ by:
$$\rho(s) = \begin{cases} 0 & 0 \le s \le 1, \\ 2(s-1) & 1 \le s \le 2, \\ s & s
 \ge 2. \end{cases}$$

Note $\rho$ is continuous, non-decreasing, $\rho(s) \le s$, and $\rho(0) = 0$.

Define $\bar{G}: M \to N$ by $\bar{G} = G_0$ on $M \setminus C(\partial M \times
 [0,2))$, and on the collar:
$$\bar{G}(C(p, s)) = G_0(C(p,\, \rho(s))).$$

**Continuity:** At $s=1$: $\bar{G}(C(p,1)) = G_0(C(p,0)) = G_0(p) = g(p)$, match
ing the $s \le 1$ value. At $s=2$: $\bar{G}(C(p,2)) = G_0(C(p,2))$, matching the
 $s \ge 2$ value. ✓

**Smoothness near $\partial M$:** For $s \in [0,1]$, $\bar{G}(C(p,s)) = G_0(C(p,
0)) = g(p) = (g \circ \pi_1)(p,s)$, which is smooth. So $\bar{G}$ is smooth on t
he neighborhood $C(\partial M \times [0,1))$ of $\partial M$.

**Boundary values:** $\bar{G}|_{\partial M} = g$. ✓

**Homotopy class preserved:** Define $\rho_t(s) = (1-t)s + t\,\rho(s)$. Since $0
 \le \rho(s) \le s < 3$, we have $\rho_t(s) \in [0,3)$, and $\rho_t(0) = 0$ for
all $t$. The homotopy
$$\Gamma(C(p,s),\, t) = G_0(C(p,\, \rho_t(s)))$$
(on the collar, extended as $G_0$ outside) is continuous, satisfies $\Gamma(-,0)
 = G_0$ and $\Gamma(-,1) = \bar{G}$, and is rel $\partial M$ (since $\rho_t(0)=0
$). Hence $\bar{G} \simeq G_0 \simeq F$.

**Step 3 — Relative Smooth Approximation.**

We apply the Whitney Approximation Theorem for maps (Lee, *Introduction to Smoot
h Manifolds*, 2nd ed., Theorem 6.26; equivalently Hirsch, *Differential Topology
*, Theorem 2.4):

> **Theorem.** Let $M, N$ be smooth manifolds (with or without boundary), $f: M
\to N$ continuous, and $A \subseteq M$ closed. If $f$ is smooth on a neighborhoo
d of $A$ (i.e., $f|_A$ extends to a smooth map on a neighborhood of $A$), then t
here exists a smooth map $G: M \to N$ with $G|_A = f|_A$, homotopic to $f$ via a
 homotopy rel $A$.

We apply this with $f = \bar{G}$ and $A = \partial M$ (which is closed in $M$).
The hypotheses are satisfied: $\bar{G}$ is continuous, and $\bar{G}$ is smooth o
n the neighborhood $C(\partial M \times [0,1))$ of $\partial M$.

The theorem produces a smooth map $G: M \to N$ with:
- $G|_{\partial M} = \bar{G}|_{\partial M} = g$,
- $G \simeq \bar{G}$ via a homotopy rel $\partial M$.

**Conclusion.** Since $G \simeq \bar{G} \simeq G_0 \simeq F$ and $G|_{\partial M
} = g$, the smooth map $G$ satisfies all the required properties. The key ingred
ients are: (1) the topological HEP for the cofibration $\partial M \hookrightarr
ow M$, which gives a continuous extension of the boundary homotopy; (2) a collar
-based reparametrization that makes the continuous extension smooth near $\parti
al M$ while preserving the homotopy class and boundary values; and (3) the relat
ive Whitney approximation theorem, which smooths the map globally while fixing t
he boundary. $\blacksquare$
