Setup. Let ${f_n}_{n \geq 1}$ be smooth, compactly supported functions in
$L^2(\mathbb{R})$ (the argument is dimension-independent). Define an interaction
graph $G$ on vertices ${1, 2, 3, \ldots}$: for $m \neq n$, connect $(m, n)$ by
an edge if and only if $\langle f_m, f_n \rangle = \int f_m f_n , dx \neq 0$.
The hypothesis says each vertex has degree at most $C$ in $G$.

We assume $\sum_{n \geq 1} |f_n|^2 < \infty$ (otherwise the right-hand side is $
+\infty$ and the inequality is trivial). We must show $\sum f_n$ converges in
$L^2$ and the inequality holds for some $C'$ depending only on $C$.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Step 1: Bounding the partial sums.

Let $S_N = \sum_{n=1}^N f_n$. Expand:

$$|S_N|^2 = \sum_{n=1}^N |f_n|^2 + 2\sum_{\substack{1 \leq m < n \leq N \ (m,n)
\in E}} \langle f_m, f_n \rangle.$$

Bound the cross-term sum. By Cauchy–Schwarz on each inner product, $|\langle
f_m, f_n \rangle| \leq |f_m|,|f_n|$, so:

$$\left|\sum_{\substack{(m,n) \in E_N}} \langle f_m, f_n \rangle\right| \leq
\sum_{(m,n) \in E_N} |f_m|,|f_n|,$$

where $E_N = {(m,n) \in E : 1 \leq m < n \leq N}$. Apply Cauchy–Schwarz to this
sum over edges:

$$\sum_{(m,n) \in E_N} |f_m|,|f_n| \leq \left(\sum_{(m,n) \in E_N} |f_m|
^2\right)^{1/2}\left(\sum_{(m,n) \in E_N} |f_n|^2\right)^{1/2}.$$

Key counting step. In the first factor, $|f_m|^2$ is counted once per edge
incident to $m$ in $E_N$. Since each vertex has degree $\leq C$ in $G$, vertex
$m$ is incident to at most $C$ edges in $E_N$. Thus:

$$\sum_{(m,n) \in E_N} |f_m|^2 = \sum_{m=1}^{N} |f_m|^2 \cdot \deg_{E_N}(m) \leq
C \sum_{m=1}^{N} |f_m|^2.$$

Identically, $\sum_{(m,n) \in E_N} |f_n|^2 \leq C \sum_{n=1}^{N} |f_n|^2$.
Therefore:

$$\sum_{(m,n) \in E_N} |f_m|,|f_n| \leq C \sum_{n=1}^{N} |f_n|^2.$$

$$\boxed{|S_N|^2 \leq (1 + 2C)\sum_{n=1}^{N} |f_n|^2.}$$
