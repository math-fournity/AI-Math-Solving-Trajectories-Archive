## Solution

The drone performs a symmetric random walk on the integer lattice, stopping when
 the taxicab distance $|x|+|y|=3$. I classify interior states by type, since the
 expected time depends on the geometry of the point, not just its distance.

**State classification** (all points within distance 2):

- **Type A** ($d=0$): $(0,0)$ — expected time $a$
- **Type B** ($d=1$): $(\pm1,0),(0,\pm1)$ — expected time $b$
- **Type C** ($d=2$, on-axis): $(\pm2,0),(0,\pm2)$ — expected time $c$
- **Type D** ($d=2$, diagonal): $(\pm1,\pm1)$ — expected time $d$

Boundary ($d=3$): expected time $0$.

**Recurrence equations** (each move adds 1, then averages over 4 neighbors):

From $(0,0)$: all 4 neighbors are type B.
$$a = 1 + b$$

From $(1,0)$: neighbors are $(0,0)$=A, $(2,0)$=C, $(1,1)$=D, $(1,-1)$=D.
$$b = 1 + \tfrac{1}{4}(a + c + 2d)$$

From $(2,0)$: neighbors are $(1,0)$=B and three boundary points.
$$c = 1 + \tfrac{1}{4}(b) = 1 + \tfrac{b}{4}$$

From $(1,1)$: neighbors are $(1,0)$=B, $(0,1)$=B and two boundary points.
$$d = 1 + \tfrac{1}{4}(2b) = 1 + \tfrac{b}{2}$$

**Solving:** Substitute $a=1+b$, $c=1+\frac{b}{4}$, $d=1+\frac{b}{2}$ into the e
quation for $b$:

$$b = 1 + \tfrac{1}{4}\!\left[(1+b)+\left(1+\tfrac{b}{4}\right)+2\left(1+\tfrac{
b}{2}\right)\right]$$
$$= 1 + \tfrac{1}{4}\!\left[4 + 2b + \tfrac{b}{4}\right] = 1 + \tfrac{1}{4}\!\le
ft[4 + \tfrac{9b}{4}\right] = 2 + \tfrac{9b}{16}$$

$$b - \tfrac{9b}{16} = 2 \;\Longrightarrow\; \tfrac{7b}{16}=2 \;\Longrightarrow\
; b = \tfrac{32}{7}$$

$$a = 1 + \tfrac{32}{7} = \tfrac{39}{7}$$
