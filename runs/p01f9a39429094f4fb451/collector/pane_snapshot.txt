### Block structure

Index the rows/columns by $1, \dots, 2n$. The entry rule depends on the parity o
f $i+j$:
- $i = j$: entry $x$.
- $i \neq j$, $i+j$ even (same parity): entry $a$.
- $i+j$ odd (different parity): entry $b$.

Reorder indices so all **odd** indices come first ($1,3,\dots,2n-1$, giving $n$
of them), then all **even** indices ($2,4,\dots,2n$, giving $n$ of them). In thi
s ordering the matrix becomes a $2\times 2$ block matrix

$$M_n = \begin{pmatrix} A & B \\ B & A \end{pmatrix},$$

where:
- $A$ is $n\times n$ with $x$ on the diagonal and $a$ off the diagonal (both ind
ices in a block have the same parity, so $i+j$ is even).
- $B$ is $n\times n$ with every entry equal to $b$ (indices from different parit
y blocks, so $i+j$ is odd).

### Determinant of the block matrix

For a block matrix $\begin{pmatrix} A & B \\ B & A \end{pmatrix}$, conjugating b
y $\begin{pmatrix} I & I \\ I & -I \end{pmatrix}$ block-diagonalizes it, giving

$$\det M_n = \det(A+B)\cdot \det(A-B).$$

Now
$$A + B = (x+b)\text{ on diagonal, }(a+b)\text{ off diagonal} = (x-a)I + (a+b)J,
$$
$$A - B = (x-b)\text{ on diagonal, }(a-b)\text{ off diagonal} = (x-a)I + (a-b)J,
$$

where $J$ is the $n\times n$ all-ones matrix. A matrix of the form $\alpha I + \
beta J$ has eigenvalue $\alpha$ with multiplicity $n-1$ and eigenvalue $\alpha +
 n\beta$ with multiplicity $1$, so

$$\det(\alpha I + \beta J) = \alpha^{\,n-1}(\alpha + n\beta).$$

Applying this:

$$\det(A+B) = (x-a)^{n-1}\bigl(x - a + n(a+b)\bigr) = (x-a)^{n-1}\bigl(x + (n-1)
a + nb\bigr),$$

$$\det(A-B) = (x-a)^{n-1}\bigl(x - a + n(a-b)\bigr) = (x-a)^{n-1}\bigl(x + (n-1)
a - nb\bigr).$$

Therefore

$$\det M_n = (x-a)^{2n-2}\Bigl[\bigl(x+(n-1)a\bigr)^2 - n^2 b^2\Bigr].$$

### The limit

$$\lim_{x\to a}\frac{\det M_n}{(x-a)^{2n-2}} = \bigl(a + (n-1)a\bigr)^2 - n^2 b^
2 = (na)^2 - n^2 b^2 = n^2(a^2 - b^2).$$

**Verification for $n=1$:** $M_1 = \begin{pmatrix} x & b \\ b & x \end{pmatrix}$
, $\det = x^2 - b^2$, limit $= a^2 - b^2 = 1^2(a^2-b^2)$. ✓

**Verification for $n=2$:** From the block form, $\det M_2 = (x-a)^2\bigl((x+a)^
2 - 4b^2\bigr)$, limit $= 4a^2 - 4b^2 = 4(a^2-b^2)$. ✓

### Evaluation at $n=3$, $a=2$, $b=1$

$$n^2(a^2 - b^2) = 9\,(4 - 1) = 9 \cdot 3 = \boxed{27}.$$
