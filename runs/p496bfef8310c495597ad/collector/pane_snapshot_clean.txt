## Proof

Let $M \subset \mathbb{R}^n$ be a submanifold, and $g: TM \to \mathbb{R}$ be def
ined by $g(p,v) = \langle v, v\rangle$, where $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ is the
 Euclidean inner product on $\mathbb{R}^n$. We claim that $1$ is a regular value
 of $g$.

**Tangent space of $TM$.** Since $M \subset \mathbb{R}^n$ is a submanifold, the
tangent bundle $TM = \{(p,v) \in \mathbb{R}^n \times \mathbb{R}^n : p \in M,\ v
\in T_p M\}$ is a submanifold of $\mathbb{R}^{2n}$. Because each fiber $T_p M$ i
s a linear subspace of $\mathbb{R}^n$, the tangent space at $(p,v) \in TM$ is
$$T_{(p,v)} TM = \{(\xi, \eta) : \xi \in T_p M,\ \eta \in T_p M\}.$$

**The differential of $g$.** Extending $g(p,v) = \langle v,v\rangle$ to the ambi
ent $\mathbb{R}^{2n}$, its differential is
$$dg_{(p,v)}(\xi, \eta) = 2\langle v, \eta\rangle.$$

**Regularity on $g^{-1}(1)$.** A point $(p,v) \in g^{-1}(1)$ satisfies $v \in T_
p M$ and $\langle v, v\rangle = 1$, i.e. $v \neq 0$. To show $dg_{(p,v)} : T_{(p
,v)}TM \to \mathbb{R}$ is surjective, we need some $(\xi,\eta) \in T_{(p,v)}TM$
with $dg_{(p,v)}(\xi,\eta) \neq 0$. Take $\xi = 0$ and $\eta = v \in T_p M$. The
n
$$dg_{(p,v)}(0, v) = 2\langle v, v\rangle = 2 \neq 0.$$

Hence $dg_{(p,v)}$ is surjective at every point of $g^{-1}(1)$.

**Conclusion.** Since $dg$ is surjective at every point of $g^{-1}(1) = T^1 M$,
the value $1$ is a regular value of $g$. (As a consequence, $T^1 M$ is a smooth
submanifold of $TM$ of codimension $1$.)
