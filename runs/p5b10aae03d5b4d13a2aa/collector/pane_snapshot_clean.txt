Then $U$ is open in $M$ (preimage of an open set under a homeomorphism), and the

$$g\big|_U : U \to B$$
is a diffeomorphism, being the restriction of a diffeomorphism to an open subset

This is smooth on $B_1(0)$. Its inverse is
$$\psi : \mathbb{R}^n \to B_1(0), \qquad \psi(y) = \frac{y}{\sqrt{1 + \|y\|^2}},
$$
