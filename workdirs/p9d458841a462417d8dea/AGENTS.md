# Problem

An architect is designing a modular structural frame composed of a sequence of right-angled support brackets. Each bracket in the sequence is geometrically similar to the others. The arrangement is built such that the horizontal bracket of one pair is congruent to the vertical bracket positioned directly above it. 

The first bracket in the series has two perpendicular support beams with lengths $a$ and $b$, measured in decimeters, satisfying the constraint $0 < a < b < 100$. The construction process continues according to the similarity ratio until it reaches a final bracket where both perpendicular beams have lengths that are exactly integers.

Let $m$ represent the maximum possible number of brackets that can exist in such a sequence under these constraints. Furthermore, let $A_m$ represent the maximum possible total combined surface area of all $m$ brackets (where the area of a bracket is the area of the right triangle formed by its beams).

Find the value of $m + A_m$.
