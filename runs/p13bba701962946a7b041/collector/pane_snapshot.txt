Looking at this problem, I need to find a commutative unital ring with infinitel
y many maximal ideals where: whenever a family of ideals has nonzero total inter
section, some finite subfamily also has nonzero intersection.

**Key observation**: Any integral domain satisfies this property trivially. If $
\bigcap_\alpha I_\alpha \neq 0$, pick a nonzero element $x$ in the intersection.
 Then $x$ lies in every $I_\alpha$, so $x$ lies in every finite subintersection,
 making each finite subintersection nonzero.

**Example**: $R = \mathbb{Z}$.

---

## Proof

**Claim.** $R = \mathbb{Z}$ is a commutative unital ring with infinitely many ma
ximal ideals satisfying the stated property.

**$\mathbb{Z}$ is a commutative unital ring.** This is standard: $\mathbb{Z}$ is
 a commutative ring with unity $1$.

**$\mathbb{Z}$ has infinitely many maximal ideals.** For each prime $p$, the ide
al $(p) = p\mathbb{Z}$ is maximal since $\mathbb{Z}/(p) \cong \mathbb{F}_p$ is a
 field. By Euclid's theorem there are infinitely many primes, so $\mathbb{Z}$ ha
s infinitely many maximal ideals $\{(2), (3), (5), (7), \dots\}$.

**$\mathbb{Z}$ satisfies the property.** $\mathbb{Z}$ is an integral domain (it
has no zero divisors). Let $\{I_\alpha\}_{\alpha \in A}$ be any family of ideals
 of $\mathbb{Z}$ with $\bigcap_{\alpha \in A} I_\alpha \neq (0)$. Choose any non
zero element $x \in \bigcap_{\alpha \in A} I_\alpha$, so $x \neq 0$ and $x \in I
_\alpha$ for every $\alpha \in A$.

Now for any finite subset $F \subseteq A$ (in particular, any single-element sub
set $\{\alpha_0\}$), we have $x \in \bigcap_{\alpha \in F} I_\alpha$. Since $x \
neq 0$, this finite intersection is nonzero:

$$\bigcap_{\alpha \in F} I_\alpha \neq (0).$$

Thus the intersection of a finite subset of the ideals is nonzero, as required.
$\blacksquare$

---

**Remark.** The argument works for any integral domain with infinitely many maxi
mal ideals (e.g., $k[x]$, $\mathbb{Z}[x]$, any number ring with class number con
siderations set aside). The essential point is that in a domain, a single nonzer
o witness in the full intersection serves as a witness for every finite subinter
section simultaneously.
