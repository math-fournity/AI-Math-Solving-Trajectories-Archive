Looking at this problem, I need to find the volume of the solid formed by rotati
ng an isosceles trapezoid 360° around its shorter base.

## Setting Up the Trapezoid

**Given:** height $h = 12$, legs $= 13$, median $= 15$.

**Finding the bases.** The median of a trapezoid equals $\frac{a+b}{2}$, so $a +
 b = 30$.

The horizontal projection of each leg: $\sqrt{13^2 - 12^2} = \sqrt{169 - 144} =
5$.

For an isosceles trapezoid, the difference of the bases equals $2 \times 5 = 10$
, so $b - a = 10$.

Solving: $b = 20$ (longer base), $a = 10$ (shorter base).

## Coordinate System

Place the shorter base on the $x$-axis from $(0,0)$ to $(10,0)$. The trapezoid e
xtends upward to the longer base at $y = 12$, from $(-5, 12)$ to $(15, 12)$.

Vertices: $(0,0)$, $(10,0)$, $(15,12)$, $(-5,12)$.

The axis of rotation is the $x$-axis (the line containing the shorter base).

## Volume by the Washer Method

For each $x$, the trapezoid extends from $y_{\text{bottom}}(x)$ to $y_{\text{top
}}(x) = 12$. Rotating around the $x$-axis gives washers with area $\pi(y_{\text{
top}}^2 - y_{\text{bottom}}^2)$.

**Three regions:**

**Region 1: $x \in [-5, 0]$** — bottom is the left leg from $(0,0)$ to $(-5,12)$
:
$$y_{\text{bottom}} = -\frac{12x}{5}, \quad y_{\text{top}} = 12$$

**Region 2: $x \in [0, 10]$** — bottom is the shorter base ($y = 0$):
$$y_{\text{bottom}} = 0, \quad y_{\text{top}} = 12$$

**Region 3: $x \in [10, 15]$** — bottom is the right leg from $(10,0)$ to $(15,1
2)$:
$$y_{\text{bottom}} = \frac{12(x-10)}{5}, \quad y_{\text{top}} = 12$$

## Computing Each Integral

**Region 2** (central cylinder):
$$V_2 = \pi \int_0^{10} 144\, dx = 1440\pi$$

**Region 1** (cylinder minus cone):
$$V_1 = \pi \int_{-5}^{0} \left(144 - \frac{144x^2}{25}\right) dx = \pi \left[14
4x - \frac{48x^3}{25}\right]_{-5}^{0} = \pi\left(0 - (-720 + 240)\right) = 480\p
i$$

This equals a cylinder ($\pi \cdot 144 \cdot 5 = 720\pi$) minus a cone ($\frac{1
}{3}\pi \cdot 144 \cdot 5 = 240\pi$), confirming $480\pi$. ✓

**Region 3** (by symmetry with Region 1, substituting $u = x - 10$):
$$V_3 = \pi \int_{0}^{5} \left(144 - \frac{144u^2}{25}\right) du = 480\pi$$

## Total Volume

$$V = V_1 + V_2 + V_3 = 480\pi + 1440\pi + 480\pi = \boxed{2400\pi}$$

The total volume of the solid of revolution is $\mathbf{2400\pi}$ cubic decimete
rs.
