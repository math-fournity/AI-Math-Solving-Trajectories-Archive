## Proof

Place the circle as the unit circle in the complex plane, centered at the origin
. Assume the points appear in order $A, B, C, D, E$ around the circle. Since equ
al chords subtend equal arcs, the conditions $|AB|=|CD|$ and $|BC|=|DE|$ give
$$\widehat{AB}=\widehat{CD}, \qquad \widehat{BC}=\widehat{DE}.$$

Set $A = e^{i\cdot 0}=1$. Let $B=e^{ib}$, $C=e^{ic}$. Then from $\widehat{AB}=\w
idehat{CD}$ we get $D=e^{i(b+c)}$, and from $\widehat{BC}=\widehat{DE}$ we get $
E=e^{2ic}$. (Indeed $\widehat{AB}=b=\widehat{CD}=(b+c)-c$ and $\widehat{BC}=c-b=
\widehat{DE}=2c-(b+c)$.)

**Finding $F=AD\cap BE$.** For points on the unit circle, a point $z$ lies on th
e line through $z_1,z_2$ (with $|z_1|=|z_2|=1$) iff
$$z+z_1z_2\,\bar z = z_1+z_2.$$

- Line $AD$ ($z_1=A=1$, $z_2=D=e^{i(b+c)}$): $\;F+e^{i(b+c)}\bar F = 1+e^{i(b+c)
}$. $\quad(1)$
- Line $BE$ ($z_1=B=e^{ib}$, $z_2=E=e^{2ic}$): $\;F+e^{i(b+2c)}\bar F = e^{ib}+e
^{2ic}$. $\quad(2)$

Subtract $(1)$ from $(2)$:
$$e^{i(b+c)}(e^{ic}-1)\,\bar F = (e^{2ic}-1)+e^{ib}(1-e^{ic}) = (e^{ic}-1)(e^{ic
}+1-e^{ib}).$$

Canceling $e^{ic}-1$:
$$\bar F = \frac{e^{ic}+1-e^{ib}}{e^{i(b+c)}}.$$

Taking conjugates (using $|e^{i\theta}|=1$):
$$F = (e^{-ic}+1-e^{-ib})\,e^{i(b+c)} = e^{ib}+e^{i(b+c)}-e^{ic} = B+D-C.$$

**The key computation.** Let $N$ be the midpoint of $AF$ and $M$ the midpoint of
 $CD$:
$$N=\frac{A+F}{2}=\frac{1+e^{ib}+e^{i(b+c)}-e^{ic}}{2}, \qquad M=\frac{C+D}{2}=\
frac{e^{ic}+e^{i(b+c)}}{2}.$$

Then
$$N-M = \frac{1+e^{ib}-2e^{ic}}{2},$$
and
$$E-M = e^{2ic}-\frac{e^{ic}+e^{i(b+c)}}{2} = \frac{2e^{2ic}-e^{ic}-e^{i(b+c)}}{
2} = \frac{e^{ic}(2e^{ic}-1-e^{ib})}{2} = -e^{ic}\cdot\frac{1+e^{ib}-2e^{ic}}{2}
 = -e^{ic}(N-M).$$

Taking moduli and using $|e^{ic}|=1$:
$$|ME|=|E-M|=|e^{ic}|\cdot|N-M|=|N-M|=|MN|.$$

Therefore $N$, the midpoint of $AF$, lies on the circle centered at $M$ with rad
ius $ME$. $\blacksquare$
