## Proof

**Setup.** We have $n = 250$ ships. In each Signal Window, every ship is designa
ted Transmitter (T), Receiver (R), or Inactive. The pairs covered in a single wi
ndow are exactly those $\{A, B\}$ where one is T and the other is R. We seek the
 minimum number of windows so that every pair is covered in at least one window.

This is equivalent to finding the **biclique cover number** of the complete grap
h $K_{250}$: the minimum number of complete bipartite graphs whose union covers
all edges of $K_{250}$.

---

### Upper Bound: 8 windows suffice

Assign each of the 250 ships a distinct binary string of length 8 (possible sinc
e $2^8 = 512 \geq 250$). For window $i$ ($i = 1, 2, \ldots, 8$):

- Ships with bit $i = 1$ are designated **Transmitters**.
- Ships with bit $i = 0$ are designated **Receivers**.

Every ship participates in every window (no ship is inactive). For any two disti
nct ships $A$ and $B$, their binary strings differ in at least one bit position
$i$. In window $i$, one has bit 1 (Transmitter) and the other has bit 0 (Receive
r), so a courier is exchanged. Thus all $\binom{250}{2}$ pairs are covered in 8
windows.

---

### Lower Bound: At least 8 windows are necessary

Suppose $k$ windows suffice. We first observe that we may assume **every ship is
 active (T or R) in every window**. Indeed, if ship $v$ is inactive in window $i
$, we may assign $v$ to either the T-side or R-side of window $i$; this only *ad
ds* covered pairs (between $v$ and all ships on the opposite side), never remove
s any. Since all pairs are already covered, adding extra coverage is harmless. T
hus, without loss of generality, every ship is T or R in every window.

Now assign each ship $v$ a **signature vector** $f(v) \in \{0,1\}^k$, where $f(v
)_i = 1$ if $v$ is a Transmitter in window $i$, and $f(v)_i = 0$ if $v$ is a Rec
eiver in window $i$.

**Claim:** All signature vectors must be distinct.

*Proof of claim:* Suppose two distinct ships $A$ and $B$ have $f(A) = f(B)$. The
n in every window, $A$ and $B$ are on the *same* side (both T or both R). A cour
ier is exchanged between $A$ and $B$ only when one is T and the other is R — whi
ch never happens. This contradicts the requirement that every pair is covered. $
\square$

Since all 250 signature vectors are distinct elements of $\{0,1\}^k$, we need:
$$250 \leq 2^k$$
$$k \geq \lceil \log_2 250 \rceil$$

Since $2^7 = 128 < 250 \leq 512 = 2^8$, we obtain $k \geq 8$.

---

### Conclusion

The upper and lower bounds match:

$$\lceil \log_2 250 \rceil = 8$$

**8 Signal Windows are required.**
