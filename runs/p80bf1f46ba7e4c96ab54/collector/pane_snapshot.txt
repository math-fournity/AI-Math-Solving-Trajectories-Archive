## Solution

**Setup.** Let $K$ be a field of characteristic $p > 0$, let $x$ be transcendent
al over $K$, and let $\sigma \in \operatorname{Aut}(K(x)/K)$ be defined by $\sig
ma(x) = x+1$.

**Step 1: Order of $\sigma$.** Since $\operatorname{char}(K) = p$, we have
$$\sigma^p(x) = x + \underbrace{1 + 1 + \cdots + 1}_{p} = x + p\cdot 1 = x.$$
For $1 \leq k < p$, $\sigma^k(x) = x + k \neq x$ (since $k \neq 0$ in $K$). Thus
 $\sigma$ has order $p$, and $G = \langle \sigma \rangle$ is a finite group of o
rder $p$ acting on $K(x)$.

**Step 2: Apply Artin's theorem.** By Artin's theorem on finite automorphism gro
ups, $[K(x) : K(x)^G] = |G| = p$.

**Step 3: Identify a fixed element.** Set $y = x^p - x$. Compute:
$$\sigma(y) = \sigma(x^p - x) = (x+1)^p - (x+1) = x^p + 1^p - x - 1 = x^p - x =
y,$$
where $(x+1)^p = x^p + 1$ by the Frobenius identity (valid in characteristic $p$
). Hence $y \in K(x)^G$, so $K(y) \subseteq K(x)^G$.

**Step 4: Degree bound.** The element $x$ satisfies the polynomial
$$T^p - T - y \in K(y)[T],$$
so $[K(x) : K(y)] \leq p$.

**Step 5: Conclude equality.** From $K(y) \subseteq K(x)^G \subseteq K(x)$, we h
ave
$$[K(x) : K(y)] = [K(x) : K(x)^G]\cdot [K(x)^G : K(y)] = p \cdot [K(x)^G : K(y)]
 \geq p.$$
Combining with Step 4, $[K(x):K(y)] = p$, which forces $[K(x)^G : K(y)] = 1$, i.
e.,
$$K(x)^{\langle\sigma\rangle} = K(y) = K(x^p - x).$$

**Answer.** A primitive element of the fixed field of $\langle \sigma \rangle$ i
s $\boxed{x^p - x}$.
