# Proof that $K^\times/F^\times$ is compact

**Setup.** Let $K/F$ be a finite extension of local fields of characteristic $0$
. Then both $K$ and $F$ are finite extensions of $\mathbb{Q}_p$ for some prime $
p$. Let $n = [K:F]$, $e = e(K/F)$ the ramification index, and $f = f(K/F)$ the r
esidue degree, so $ef = n$.

Let $v_K: K^\times \to \mathbb{Z}$ be the normalized discrete valuation on $K$ (
surjective, with $\ker v_K = \mathcal{O}_K^\times$, the unit group). Endow $\mat
hbb{Z}$ with the discrete topology; $v_K$ is continuous.

**Key valuation fact.** For $x \in F^\times$, $v_K(x) = e \cdot v_F(x)$, since $
v_K(\pi_F) = e$ for any uniformizer $\pi_F$ of $F$ (as $\pi_F = u\pi_K^e$ for a
unit $u \in \mathcal{O}_K^\times$). Hence
$$v_K(F^\times) = e\mathbb{Z}.$$

**Constructing the exact sequence.** Consider the composition
$$K^\times \xrightarrow{v_K} \mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{mod}\, e} \mathbb{Z
}/e\mathbb{Z}.$$
Its kernel is $v_K^{-1}(e\mathbb{Z})$, which contains $F^\times$. So it induces
a continuous surjection
$$\varphi: K^\times/F^\times \longrightarrow \mathbb{Z}/e\mathbb{Z}.$$

**Identifying the kernel.** We claim $v_K^{-1}(e\mathbb{Z}) = F^\times \cdot \ma
thcal{O}_K^\times$.

($\supseteq$): $v_K(F^\times) = e\mathbb{Z}$ and $v_K(\mathcal{O}_K^\times) = \{
0\}$, so $v_K(F^\times \cdot \mathcal{O}_K^\times) \subseteq e\mathbb{Z}$.

($\subseteq$): If $v_K(x) = em$ for $m \in \mathbb{Z}$, write $x = \pi_K^{em}\cd
ot u$ with $u \in \mathcal{O}_K^\times$. Since $\pi_F = w\pi_K^e$ for some $w\in
\mathcal{O}_K^\times$, we get $\pi_K^{em} = \pi_F^m \cdot w^{-m}$, so $x = \pi_F
^m \cdot (w^{-m}u) \in \pi_F^{\mathbb{Z}}\cdot\mathcal{O}_K^\times = F^\times\cd
ot\mathcal{O}_K^\times$ (using $\mathcal{O}_F^\times \subseteq \mathcal{O}_K^\ti
mes$).

Therefore
$$\ker\varphi = (F^\times\cdot\mathcal{O}_K^\times)/F^\times \cong \mathcal{O}_K
^\times/(F^\times\cap\mathcal{O}_K^\times).$$

Now $F^\times \cap \mathcal{O}_K^\times = \{x\in F^\times : v_K(x)=0\} = \{x\in
F^\times : v_F(x)=0\} = \mathcal{O}_F^\times$. So
$$\ker\varphi \cong \mathcal{O}_K^\times/\mathcal{O}_F^\times.$$

**Compactness of the kernel.** $\mathcal{O}_K^\times$ is compact (it is a closed
 subset of the compact ring $\mathcal{O}_K$). $\mathcal{O}_F^\times$ is a closed
 subgroup of $\mathcal{O}_K^\times$ (since $F$ is closed in $K$, being a complet
e subfield of the Hausdorff field $K$). Hence $\mathcal{O}_K^\times/\mathcal{O}_
F^\times$ is compact.

**Compactness of the quotient.** $\mathbb{Z}/e\mathbb{Z}$ is finite, hence compa
ct.

**Conclusion.** We have a short exact sequence of topological groups
$$0 \longrightarrow \mathcal{O}_K^\times/\mathcal{O}_F^\times \longrightarrow K^
\times/F^\times \xrightarrow{\varphi} \mathbb{Z}/e\mathbb{Z} \longrightarrow 0.$
$
Choose representatives $c_1,\dots,c_{e} \in K^\times/F^\times$ for the $e$ coset
s of $\mathbb{Z}/e\mathbb{Z}$. Then
$$K^\times/F^\times = \bigcup_{i=1}^{e} c_i \cdot (\mathcal{O}_K^\times/\mathcal
{O}_F^\times),$$
a finite union of compact sets (each coset is compact, being a translate of the
compact kernel). Hence $K^\times/F^\times$ is compact. $\blacksquare$
