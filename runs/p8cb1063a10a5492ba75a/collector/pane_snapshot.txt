Looking at this problem, I need to determine whether a compact convex set $X \su
bseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ that is closed under pairwise coordinate averaging must int
ersect the diagonal $\{(\lambda,\dots,\lambda)\}$.

**Answer: Yes.**

**Proof.**

Define the averaging map $A_{ij}:\mathbb{R}^n\to\mathbb{R}^n$ for $1\le i<j\le n
$ by
$$A_{ij}(x)_k = \begin{cases} \frac{x_i+x_j}{2} & k\in\{i,j\},\\ x_k & \text{oth
erwise.}\end{cases}$$
The hypothesis states $A_{ij}(X)\subseteq X$ for all pairs $i<j$.

**Key observation: the mean is preserved, the variance strictly decreases.**

For $x\in\mathbb{R}^n$, let $\bar{x}=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{k=1}^n x_k$ and define the
 variance
$$V(x)=\sum_{k=1}^n (x_k-\bar{x})^2.$$

*Mean preservation:* $A_{ij}$ replaces $x_i+x_j$ with $\frac{x_i+x_j}{2}+\frac{x
_i+x_j}{2}=x_i+x_j$, so $\sum_k A_{ij}(x)_k = \sum_k x_k$, hence $\overline{A_{i
j}(x)}=\bar{x}$.

*Variance decrease:* Setting $a=x_i-\bar{x}$, $b=x_j-\bar{x}$, the only terms of
 $V$ that change are those for indices $i,j$:
$$V(x)-V(A_{ij}(x)) = a^2+b^2 - \frac{(a+b)^2}{2} = \frac{(a-b)^2}{2} = \frac{(x
_i-x_j)^2}{2}\ge 0,$$
with equality iff $x_i=x_j$.

**Minimization argument.**

Since $X$ is nonempty and compact, and $V$ is continuous (a polynomial in the co
ordinates), $V$ attains its minimum on $X$ at some point $x^*\in X$.

Suppose for contradiction that $V(x^*)>0$. Then $x^*$ is not on the diagonal, so
 there exist indices $i<j$ with $x_i^*\neq x_j^*$. By hypothesis, $A_{ij}(x^*)\i
n X$, and by the variance computation:
$$V(A_{ij}(x^*)) = V(x^*) - \frac{(x_i^*-x_j^*)^2}{2} < V(x^*),$$
contradicting the minimality of $V(x^*)$.

Therefore $V(x^*)=0$, which means $x_1^*=x_2^*=\cdots=x_n^*=\bar{x}^*$. Setting
$\lambda=\bar{x}^*$, we have $(\lambda,\dots,\lambda)=x^*\in X$. $\blacksquare$
