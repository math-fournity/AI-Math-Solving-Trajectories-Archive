I'll solve this contour integral using the residue theorem.

## Solution

**Contour:** $|z-3|=4$ is a circle centered at $z=3$ with radius $4$, enclosing
the real-axis interval $[-1, 7]$.

**Singularities:** The integrand $\dfrac{z}{\cos z - 1}$ is singular where $\cos
 z = 1$, i.e., at $z = 2k\pi$ for $k \in \mathbb{Z}$.

Checking which lie inside $|z-3|<4$:
- $k=0$: $z=0$, $|0-3|=3<4$ ✓ **inside**
- $k=1$: $z=2\pi\approx 6.283$, $|2\pi-3|\approx 3.283<4$ ✓ **inside**
- $k=-1$: $z=-2\pi\approx -6.283$, $|-2\pi-3|\approx 9.283>4$ ✗ outside
- $k=2$: $z=4\pi\approx 12.566$ ✗ outside

So only $z=0$ and $z=2\pi$ lie inside the contour.

**Nature of poles:** Since $\cos z - 1$ has Taylor expansion $-\dfrac{z^2}{2}+\d
frac{z^4}{24}-\cdots$, each zero of $\cos z-1$ is of **order 2** (both $\cos z-1
$ and its derivative $-\sin z$ vanish at $z=2k\pi$).

---

**Residue at $z=0$:** The numerator $z$ has a zero of order 1, so the pole is of
 order $2-1=1$.

$$\operatorname{Res}_{z=0}\frac{z}{\cos z-1}=\lim_{z\to 0}\frac{z^2}{\cos z-1}=\
lim_{z\to 0}\frac{z^2}{-z^2/2+z^4/24-\cdots}=\frac{1}{-1/2}=-2.$$

---

**Residue at $z=2\pi$:** Here the numerator $z=2\pi\neq 0$ is nonzero, so the po
le is of **order 2**. Set $w=z-2\pi$, so $z=w+2\pi$ and $\cos z=\cos(w+2\pi)=\co
s w$. Then

$$\frac{z}{\cos z-1}=\frac{w+2\pi}{\cos w-1}=\frac{w+2\pi}{-w^2/2+w^4/24-\cdots}
=-\frac{2(w+2\pi)}{w^2}\cdot\frac{1}{1-w^2/12+\cdots}.$$

Expanding $\dfrac{1}{1-w^2/12+\cdots}=1+\dfrac{w^2}{12}+\cdots$:

$$\frac{z}{\cos z-1}=-\frac{2}{w^2}\Bigl(2\pi+w+\frac{2\pi\, w^2}{12}+\cdots\Big
r)=-\frac{4\pi}{w^2}-\frac{2}{w}-\frac{2\pi}{12}-\cdots$$

The coefficient of $1/w$ is the residue:

$$\operatorname{Res}_{z=2\pi}\frac{z}{\cos z-1}=-2.$$

(Verification via the order-2 formula: $\displaystyle\lim_{w\to 0}\frac{d}{dw}\!
\left[\frac{w^2(w+2\pi)}{\cos w-1}\right]=\lim_{w\to 0}\frac{d}{dw}\!\bigl[-2(w+
2\pi)(1+w^2/12+\cdots)\bigr]=-2.$ ✓)

---

**Applying the residue theorem:**

$$\oint_{|z-3|=4}\frac{z}{\cos z-1}\,dz=2\pi i\Bigl(\operatorname{Res}_{z=0}+\op
eratorname{Res}_{z=2\pi}\Bigr)=2\pi i\,(-2-2)=2\pi i\cdot(-4).$$

$$\boxed{\oint_{|z-3|=4}\frac{z}{\cos z-1}\,dz=-8\pi i.}$$
