## Solution

We want $\operatorname{Var}(X_t)$ where $X_t = \int_0^t W_s^+\, dW_s$, with $W_t
$ a Wiener process and $a^+ = \max(a,0)$.

**Step 1: $X_t$ is a martingale with mean zero.**

The integrand $W_s^+$ is a continuous adapted process, and
$$\int_0^t \mathbb{E}\!\left[(W_s^+)^2\right] ds < \infty \quad \text{(verified
below)},$$
so $X_t = \int_0^t W_s^+\, dW_s$ is a well-defined Itô integral, hence a marting
ale with $\mathbb{E}[X_t] = 0$. Therefore
$$\operatorname{Var}(X_t) = \mathbb{E}[X_t^2].$$

**Step 2: Apply Itô isometry.**

By the Itô isometry,
$$\mathbb{E}[X_t^2] = \mathbb{E}\!\left[\int_0^t (W_s^+)^2\, ds\right] = \int_0^
t \mathbb{E}\!\left[(W_s^+)^2\right] ds.$$

**Step 3: Compute $\mathbb{E}[(W_s^+)^2]$.**

Since $W_s^+ = \max(W_s, 0)$, we have $(W_s^+)^2 = W_s^2\, \mathbf{1}_{\{W_s > 0
\}}$ (the value at $W_s = 0$ is irrelevant as it has probability zero). Thus
$$\mathbb{E}\!\left[(W_s^+)^2\right] = \mathbb{E}\!\left[W_s^2\, \mathbf{1}_{\{W
_s > 0\}}\right].$$

Since $W_s \sim N(0, s)$ is symmetric about $0$, the random variables $W_s^2\, \
mathbf{1}_{\{W_s > 0\}}$ and $W_s^2\, \mathbf{1}_{\{W_s < 0\}}$ are identically
distributed (via the map $W_s \mapsto -W_s$, which preserves the law). Hence
$$\mathbb{E}\!\left[W_s^2\, \mathbf{1}_{\{W_s > 0\}}\right] = \mathbb{E}\!\left[
W_s^2\, \mathbf{1}_{\{W_s < 0\}}\right] = \frac{1}{2}\,\mathbb{E}[W_s^2] = \frac
{1}{2}\, s.$$

**Step 4: Integrate.**

$$\operatorname{Var}(X_t) = \int_0^t \frac{s}{2}\, ds = \frac{1}{2}\cdot \frac{t
^2}{2} = \frac{t^2}{4}.$$

**Result:**

$$\boxed{\operatorname{Var}(X_t) = \dfrac{t^2}{4}}.$$
