$$\frac{a \pm \sqrt{\Delta_1}}{2},$$

$$\frac{c \pm \sqrt{\Delta_2}}{2},$$

$$K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{\Delta_1},\, \sqrt{\Delta_2}).$$
