## Setup

In a round-robin with n teams, there are n(n−1)/2 matches. Each match contribute
s 3 points (decisive) or 2 points (draw). If d = number of draws, total points =
 3n(n−1)/2 − d.

A stepwise outcome has points forming an arithmetic sequence with common differe
nce 2: a, a−2, a−4, …, a−2(n−1). Total = n(a − (n−1)).

## Case n = 5

Each team plays 4 matches. Points: a, a−2, a−4, a−6, a−8, with a ≥ 8 and a ≤ 12.

Setting 5(a−4) = 30 − d gives d = 50 − 5a, requiring 0 ≤ d ≤ 10, so a ∈ {8, 9, 1
0}.

**a = 8:** d = 10 (all draws). Every team gets 4 points, contradicting distinct
totals. ✗

**a = 9:** d = 5, decisive matches = 5, so total wins = 5. Points are 9, 7, 5, 3
, 1. The unique (wins, draws) decompositions (with 4 matches) are:
- 9 → (3W, 0D), 7 → (2W, 1D), 5 → (1W, 2D), 1 → (0W, 1D), and 3 → either (1W, 0D
) or (0W, 3D).
- Case (1W, 0D): total wins = 7 ≠ 5. ✗
- Case (0W, 3D): total wins = 6 ≠ 5. ✗

**a = 10:** d = 0 (all decisive), so every team's points must be a multiple of 3
. But 10, 8, 6, 4, 2 are not. ✗

**Therefore f(5) = 0.**
