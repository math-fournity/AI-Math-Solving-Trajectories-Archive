**Coordinate system.** Place incenter I at origin, incircle radius r=√14. Let D

The four lines are: BC (tangent at D), CA (tangent at E), AB (tangent at F), and

## Reflection Principle

For K on line ℓ, min(KP+KQ)=|P'Q| where P' is the reflection of P across ℓ (refl
ecting P to the opposite side of Q ensures the segment P'Q crosses ℓ).

## Computing Each Minimum

**Line CA (tangent at E):** The tangent at E has normal ê=E/r, equation ê·x=r. R
eflecting P=-E: since ê·P=-r, we get P'=P-2(-r-r)ê=-E+4E=**3E**. Then |P'Q|²=|3E
+F|²=9r²+6(E·F)+r². Since E·F=r²cos(π-A)=-r²cos A: |P'Q|²=r²(10-6cos A)=**r²·36/
7=72**.

**Line AB (tangent at F):** Reflecting P across tangent at F (normal F̂=F/r): P'=

72**.

**Line EF (chord):** Line EF has equation cos v·x - sin v·y = -r cos u, where u=

**Line BC (tangent at D):** Reflecting P=(r cos C,-r sin C) across x=r gives P'=

|P'Q|² = 4·14·(8/21+19/21) = 56·(27/21) = 56·(9/7) = **72**.
