## Proof

We seek the expected area of the orthogonal projection of a unit cube onto a pla
ne whose normal direction $\hat{u}$ is chosen uniformly at random on the unit sp
here $S^2$.

**Step 1: Cauchy's projection formula.**

For any convex body $K \subset \mathbb{R}^3$, the area of its orthogonal project
ion onto a plane with unit normal $\hat{u}$ is

$$A(\hat{u}) = \frac{1}{2}\int_{\partial K} |\hat{n}(x)\cdot \hat{u}|\, dS(x),$$

where $\hat{n}(x)$ is the outward unit normal at the boundary point $x$.

**Step 2: Apply to the unit cube.**

The unit cube $[0,1]^3$ has six faces, each of area $1$, with outward normals $\
pm e_1, \pm e_2, \pm e_3$. Substituting:

$$A(\hat{u}) = \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{3}\bigl(|e_i\cdot\hat{u}| + |{-e_i}\cdot\h
at{u}|\bigr)\cdot 1 = \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{3}2|u_i| = |u_1|+|u_2|+|u_3|,$$

where $\hat{u}=(u_1,u_2,u_3)$ is a unit vector.

**Step 3: Take the expectation over the uniform distribution on $S^2$.**

By symmetry,

$$\mathbb{E}[A] = 3\,\mathbb{E}[|u_1|].$$

It remains to compute $\mathbb{E}[|u_1|]$ for $\hat{u}$ uniform on $S^2$.

Writing $\hat{u}$ in spherical coordinates with $u_1=\cos\theta$, the surface me
asure on $S^2$ is $\sin\theta\,d\theta\,d\varphi$, and the total area is $4\pi$.
 The marginal density of $u_1$ is

$$f(u_1)=\frac{2\pi\sin\theta}{4\pi}\cdot\frac{1}{|du_1/d\theta|}=\frac{\sin\the
ta}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{\sin\theta}=\frac{1}{2},\qquad u_1\in[-1,1].$$

Thus each coordinate of a uniform point on $S^2$ is uniformly distributed on $[-
1,1]$, and

$$\mathbb{E}[|u_1|]=\int_{-1}^{1}|x|\cdot\frac{1}{2}\,dx = \int_0^1 x\,dx = \fra
c{1}{2}.$$

**Step 4: Conclusion.**

$$\boxed{\mathbb{E}[A]=3\cdot\frac{1}{2}=\frac{3}{2}}.$$

The expected area of the projection of a unit cube onto a plane under an isotrop
ic random direction of projection is $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
