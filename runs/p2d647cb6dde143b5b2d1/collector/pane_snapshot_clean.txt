## Proof

**Class definition.** Let $\mathcal{U}$ be the class of binary nonplane (unorder
ed) trees: rooted trees in which every internal node has exactly two children, a
nd the order of the two children is irrelevant. The size $|t|$ of a tree $t\in\m
athcal{U}$ is its number of external (leaf) nodes. We seek the ordinary generati
ng function $U(z)=\sum_{n\ge 1} U_n z^n$, where $U_n$ counts trees of size $n$.

**Combinatorial specification.** A binary nonplane tree is either:
- a single external node (a leaf), contributing size 1, or
- a root with an **unordered pair** of binary nonplane trees attached as its two
 subtrees.

Thus, with $\mathcal{Z}$ marking an external node and $\mathrm{SET}_2$ denoting
a 2-element multiset (unordered pair),
$$\mathcal{U} \;=\; \mathcal{Z} \;\cup\; \mathrm{SET}_2(\mathcal{U}).$$

**Translation to a generating function equation.** The leaf case contributes $z$
. The unordered-pair construction requires the cycle index of the symmetric grou
p $S_2$ acting on two slots:
$$Z(S_2) \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(s_1^2 + s_2\bigr).$$
By the standard translation rule for unordered constructions (Pólya enumeration)
, the OGF of $\mathrm{SET}_2(\mathcal{U})$ is obtained by substituting $s_1 \map
sto U(z)$ and $s_2 \mapsto U(z^2)$:
$$\mathrm{SET}_2(\mathcal{U}) \;\longrightarrow\; \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(U(z)^2 + U(z
^2)\bigr).$$

Here $U(z)^2$ counts ordered pairs of subtrees, $U(z^2)$ corrects the diagonal (
pairs of two identical subtrees, which contribute $z^{2n}$ when each subtree has
 $n$ leaves), and the factor $\tfrac{1}{2}$ accounts for the symmetry that ident
ifies $(t_1,t_2)$ with $(t_2,t_1)$.

**Result.** Adding the two cases, the generating function satisfies the function
al equation
$$\boxed{\,U(z) \;=\; z \;+\; \frac{U(z)^2 + U(z^2)}{2}\,}.$$

**Verification (first coefficients).** Write $U(z)=\sum_{n\ge 1} U_n z^n$. Expan
ding the equation:
- $U_1 = 1$ (single leaf).
- For $n=2$: the only contribution to $z^2$ on the right is $\tfrac12 U(z)^2$ gi
ving $\tfrac12\cdot 2 U_1^2 = 1$, and $\tfrac12 U(z^2)$ giving $\tfrac12 U_1 = \
tfrac12$; total $\tfrac32$? Let us recheck: the coefficient of $z^2$ in $\tfrac1
2 U(z)^2$ is $\tfrac12\cdot 2U_1 U_1 = 1$, and in $\tfrac12 U(z^2)$ is $\tfrac12
 U_1 = \tfrac12$. So $U_2 = 1 + \tfrac12 = \tfrac32$? That is not an integer, wh
ich signals an error.

Re-examining: the diagonal term $U(z^2)$ already includes the case where both su
btrees are the same leaf, and $U(z)^2$ counts *all* ordered pairs including iden
tical ones. The formula $\tfrac12(U(z)^2+U(z^2))$ is the correct Pólya form. For
 $n=2$: ordered pairs of subtrees with total leaves $=2$: only $(\text{leaf},\te
xt{leaf})$, counted once in $U(z)^2$ as $U_1\cdot U_1=1$. The diagonal $U(z^2)$
contributes $U_1=1$ for $z^2$. So $\tfrac12(1+1)=1$. Thus $U_2=1$. ✓ (There is e
xactly one tree with 2 leaves: a root with two leaf children.)

- For $n=3$: ordered pairs with total $=3$: $(\text{leaf}, \text{2-leaf tree})$
and $(\text{2-leaf tree}, \text{leaf})$, giving $2U_1 U_2 = 2$ in $U(z)^2$. Diag
onal $U(z^2)$ contributes nothing to $z^3$. So $U_3 = \tfrac12\cdot 2 = 1$. ✓ (O
ne tree: root with one leaf child and one 2-leaf child.)

- For $n=4$: ordered pairs with total $=4$: $(\text{leaf},3\text{-leaf})$, $(3\t
ext{-leaf},\text{leaf})$, $(\text{2-leaf},\text{2-leaf})$, giving $2U_1 U_3 + U_
2^2 = 2+1=3$ in $U(z)^2$. Diagonal $U(z^2)$ contributes $U_2=1$ to $z^4$. So $U_
4 = \tfrac12(3+1)=2$. ✓ (Two trees: root with (leaf, 3-leaf) subtree, or root wi
th two 2-leaf subtrees.)

The coefficients $1,1,1,2,\ldots$ match the known enumeration of binary nonplane
 trees by external nodes, confirming the equation.
