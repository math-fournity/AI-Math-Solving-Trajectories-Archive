## Analysis

The coefficient matrix is circulant with first row $(Y, E, A, R)$:

$$M = \begin{pmatrix} Y & E & A & R \\ R & Y & E & A \\ A & R & Y & E \\ E & A &
 R & Y \end{pmatrix}$$

**Eigenvalues via DFT.** Circulant matrices have eigenvectors $v_k = (1, \omega^
k, \omega^{2k}, \omega^{3k})$ with $\omega = i$, and eigenvalues:
- $\lambda_0 = Y + E + A + R$
- $\lambda_1 = (Y - A) + i(E - R)$
- $\lambda_2 = Y - E + A - R$
- $\lambda_3 = \overline{\lambda_1}$

So $\det(M) = \lambda_0 \cdot \lambda_2 \cdot |\lambda_1|^2 = (Y{+}E{+}A{+}R)(Y{
-}E{+}A{-}R)\bigl((Y{-}A)^2+(E{-}R)^2\bigr).$

**Consistency.** Since $M$ is normal, $Mv = b$ is consistent iff $b$ is orthogon
al to every null eigenvector. The Fourier coefficient of $b=(Y,E,A,R)$ along $v_
k$ is proportional to $\lambda_k$ itself (same formula). Thus whenever $\lambda_
k = 0$, the corresponding component of $b$ vanishes **automatically**. So **det
= 0 implies consistency** in all cases here.

**Range constraints.** Since $2001 \le \text{YEAR} \le 2100$, we have $Y = 2$ an
d $1 \le 100E + 10A + R \le 100$, giving:
- $E = 0$, $(A,R) \neq (0,0)$, or
- $E = 1, A = 0, R = 0$ (i.e., 2100).

**Setting det = 0 with Y = 2:**

*Factor 1:* $2 + E + A + R = 0$ — impossible (all terms $\geq 0$, sum $\geq 2$).

*Factor 2:* $2 - E + A - R = 0$, i.e., $E + R = A + 2$.

*Factor 3:* $(2 - A)^2 + (E - R)^2 = 0$, i.e., $A = 2$ and $E = R$.

**Enumerating Factor 2** ($E + R = A + 2$):

For $E = 0$: $R = A + 2$, need $R \le 9$ so $A \le 7$:

$$A=4{:}R{=}6\to 2046;\; A=5{:}R{=}7\to 2057;\; A=6{:}R{=}8\to 2068;\; A=7{:}R{=

For $E = 1$: $R = A + 1$; only $(A,R)=(0,0)$ is in range, but $0 \neq 0+1$. No s

**Enumerating Factor 3** ($A = 2, E = R$):
