# Problem

In the city of Metropolia, the Department of Urban Planning categorizes residential blocks using "Linear Zoning Sequences." A Linear Zoning Sequence is a non-constant arithmetic progression of positive integers, representing the house numbers on a street. Two such sequences are considered "distinct zones" only if they contain different sets of house numbers.

The city’s high-tech district features "Exponential Power Grids." An Exponential Power Grid is a non-constant geometric progression of house numbers $(b_n)_{n \ge 0}$ that must be entirely contained within the house numbers of a single Linear Zoning Sequence.

One specific Power Grid, known as the "Heritage Grid," has its third node (the term $b_2$) located at a house number exactly equal to the product of 40 and 2009.

How many distinct Linear Zoning Sequences are there that contain an infinite Exponential Power Grid where the third node $b_2$ is equal to $40 \cdot 2009$?
