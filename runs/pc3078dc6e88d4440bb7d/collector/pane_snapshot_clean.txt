**Claim.** Let $A$ be a Noetherian ring and $I, J$ ideals of $A$. If $IA_P \subs
eteq JA_P$ for every $P \in \operatorname{Ass}_A(A/J)$, then $I \subseteq J$.

**Proof.** Consider the finitely generated $A$-module
$$
M := (I+J)/J \;\subseteq\; A/J.
$$
We must show $M = 0$.

**Step 1: Reformulate the hypothesis in terms of $M$.** For any prime $P$,
$$
M_P = \bigl((I+J)/J\bigr)_P = (IA_P + JA_P)\,/\,JA_P.
$$
Hence $M_P = 0 \iff IA_P \subseteq JA_P$. The hypothesis is therefore exactly
$$
M_P = 0 \quad\text{for every } P \in \operatorname{Ass}_A(A/J). \tag{$\ast$}
$$

**Step 2: Use the inclusion $\operatorname{Ass}(M) \subseteq \operatorname{Ass}(
A/J)$.** Since $M$ embeds into $A/J$ via the exact sequence
$$
0 \to M \to A/J \to A/(I+J) \to 0,
$$
the standard behavior of associated primes under short exact sequences gives
$$
\operatorname{Ass}_A(M) \subseteq \operatorname{Ass}_A(A/J). \tag{$\dagger$}
$$

**Step 3: Derive a contradiction if $M \neq 0$.** Suppose $M \neq 0$. Since $A$
is Noetherian and $M$ is finitely generated (hence a Noetherian module), $\opera
torname{Ass}_A(M) \neq \emptyset$; pick $P \in \operatorname{Ass}_A(M)$. By $(\d
agger)$, $P \in \operatorname{Ass}_A(A/J)$.

Because $P \in \operatorname{Ass}_A(M)$, there is an embedding $A/P \hookrightar
row M$. Localizing at $P$ (which is exact),
$$
(A/P)_P \;\hookrightarrow\; M_P.
$$
Now $(A/P)_P = A_P/PA_P$, and since $A_P$ is local with maximal ideal $PA_P$, th
is quotient is nonzero. Therefore $M_P \neq 0$.

But $P \in \operatorname{Ass}_A(A/J)$, so by $(\ast)$ we must have $M_P = 0$ — a
 contradiction.

**Step 4: Conclude.** The contradiction forces $M = 0$, i.e. $(I+J)/J = 0$, whic
h means $I \subseteq J$. $\blacksquare$

**Remark on the edge case.** If $J = A$, then $A/J = 0$ and $\operatorname{Ass}_

holds trivially. If $J \neq A$, then $A/J \neq 0$ is finitely generated over a N
oetherian ring, so $\operatorname{Ass}_A(A/J) \neq \emptyset$, and the argument
above applies.
