# Problem

In a remote industrial park, two specialized pressure chambers, identified as Chamber $A$ and Chamber $B$, operate at different positive integer pressure levels, $a$ and $b$ (measured in Pascals).

The safety board requires the facility to monitor three specific safety ratings derived from these two pressure levels:
1.  The **Average Load**, calculated as the arithmetic mean of $a$ and $b$.
2.  The **Resonance Factor**, calculated as the geometric mean of $a$ and $b$.
3.  The **Flow Efficiency**, calculated as the harmonic mean of $a$ and $b$.

For the facility to remain operational under current regulations, all three of these calculated ratings—the arithmetic, geometric, and harmonic means—must result in perfect integers. 

Given that the two chambers must be set to distinct pressure levels, what is the minimum possible absolute difference between the pressures $a$ and $b$?
