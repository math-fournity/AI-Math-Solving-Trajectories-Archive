# Problem

There are $n = 2000$ people, and some pairs of them have called each other. Two people can call each other at most once. For any two disjoint groups of three people $A$ and $B$, there exists at least one person from $A$ and one person from $B$ that have not called each other. This condition implies the graph $G$ does not contain the complete bipartite subgraph $K_{3,3}$. Let $E$ be the maximum number of such call pairs possible. Find the smallest integer $k$ such that $E < k$, based on the bound derived from the condition $\sum_{i=1}^{n} \binom{d_i}{3} \le (s-1) \binom{n}{3}$ for $K_{s,s}$-free graphs, where $s=3$ and $d_i$ are the degrees of the vertices.
