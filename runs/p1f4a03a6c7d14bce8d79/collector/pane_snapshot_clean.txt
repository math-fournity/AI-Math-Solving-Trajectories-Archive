$$D = \lfloor 2005 \cdot \log_{10}(2005) \rfloor + 1.$$

$$\log_{10}(2005) = \log_{10}(2000) + \log_{10}(1.0025) = \bigl(3 + \log_{10}2\b

$$\log_{10}(1.0025) = \frac{0.00249688020}{2.30258509} = 0.001084381\ldots$$

$$\log_{10}(2005) = 3.30102999566 + 0.001084381 = 3.30211437666\ldots$$
