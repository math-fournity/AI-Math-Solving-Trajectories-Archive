## Solution

Let $a = x^2 + x + 1$, and let the nested radical be
$$y = \sqrt{a + \sqrt{a - \sqrt{a + \sqrt{a - \cdots}}}},$$
where the signs $+$ and $-$ alternate. Introduce the "complementary" radical
$$z = \sqrt{a - \sqrt{a + \sqrt{a - \cdots}}},$$
so that the structure gives the coupled system
$$y = \sqrt{a + z}, \qquad z = \sqrt{a - y}.$$

Squaring both equations (all quantities are non-negative):
$$y^2 = a + z, \qquad z^2 = a - y.$$

Subtracting:
$$y^2 - z^2 = z + y \implies (y - z)(y + z) = y + z.$$

Since $y, z \ge 0$ and $a \ge 7 > 0$ on $[2,3]$, we have $y + z > 0$, so we may
divide:
$$y - z = 1 \implies z = y - 1.$$

Substituting back into $y^2 = a + z$:
$$y^2 = a + y - 1 \implies y^2 - y + 1 = a = x^2 + x + 1,$$
$$y^2 - y = x^2 + x \implies y^2 - y - x^2 - x = 0.$$

Solving the quadratic in $y$:
$$y = \frac{1 \pm \sqrt{1 + 4x^2 + 4x}}{2} = \frac{1 \pm \sqrt{(2x+1)^2}}{2} = \
frac{1 \pm (2x+1)}{2},$$
since $2x + 1 > 0$ for $x \in [2,3]$. This gives $y = x + 1$ or $y = -x$. As $y
\ge 0$, we select
$$y = x + 1.$$

**Verification.** With $y = x+1$ and $z = x$:
- $a + z = (x^2+x+1) + x = x^2+2x+1 = (x+1)^2 = y^2$. ✓
- $a - y = (x^2+x+1) - (x+1) = x^2 = z^2$. ✓

The map $f(y) = \sqrt{a + \sqrt{a - y}}$ has fixed point $y = x+1$; its derivati
ve at the fixed point satisfies $|f'| < 1$ for $a \ge 7$, guaranteeing convergen
ce of the nested radical to $x+1$.

Therefore the integrand equals $x + 1$, and
$$\int_{2}^{3} (x+1)\,dx = \left[\frac{(x+1)^2}{2}\right]_{2}^{3} = \frac{16}{2}
 - \frac{9}{2} = \frac{7}{2}.$$

$$\boxed{\dfrac{7}{2}}$$
