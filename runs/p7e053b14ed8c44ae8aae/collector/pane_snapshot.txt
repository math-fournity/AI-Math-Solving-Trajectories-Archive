## Solution

**Setup.** Let $p=2017$ and $n=p-1=2016$. Since $c=(a^b-b^a)/p$ is determined by
 $(a,b)$, we need to count pairs $(a,b)$ with $1\le a,b\le p(p-1)=pn$ and $p\mid
 a^b-b^a$.

**Case analysis by divisibility of $a,b$ by $p$.**

**Case 1: $p\mid a,\ p\mid b$.** Then $a^b\equiv b^a\equiv 0\pmod p$. There are
$n$ multiples of $p$ in $\{1,\dots,pn\}$, giving $n^2$ pairs.

**Case 2: $p\mid a,\ p\nmid b$.** Then $a^b\equiv 0$ but $b^a\equiv b^{a\bmod n}
\not\equiv 0\pmod p$. No solutions.

**Case 3: $p\nmid a,\ p\mid b$.** Then $b^a\equiv 0$ but $a^b\equiv a^{b\bmod n}
\not\equiv 0\pmod p$. No solutions.

**Case 4: $p\nmid a,\ p\nmid b$.** By Fermat, $a^b\equiv a^{b\bmod n}$ and $b^a\
equiv b^{a\bmod n}\pmod p$.

**CRT parametrization.** Since $\gcd(p,n)=1$, the map $a\mapsto (a\bmod p,\ a\bm
od n)$ is a bijection $\{1,\dots,pn\}\to\mathbb{Z}/p\times\mathbb{Z}/n$. Let $A=
a\bmod p$, $u=a\bmod n$, $B=b\bmod p$, $v=b\bmod n$. For Case 4, $A,B\in\mathbb{
F}_p^*$ and $u,v\in\mathbb{Z}/n$, and the condition becomes
$$A^v \equiv B^u \pmod p.$$

Let $g$ be a generator of $\mathbb{F}_p^*$, and write $A=g^i$, $B=g^j$ with $i,j
\in\mathbb{Z}/n$. The condition becomes
$$iv\equiv ju \pmod n.$$

**Counting $S=\#\{(i,j,u,v)\in(\mathbb{Z}/n)^4: iv\equiv ju\pmod n\}$.**

For fixed $(i,j)$, the map $(u,v)\mapsto iv-ju\pmod n$ has image equal to the su
bgroup of $\mathbb{Z}/n$ generated by $i$ and $j$, which has order $n/\gcd(i,j,n
)$. Thus the kernel has size $n\cdot\gcd(i,j,n)$, giving
$$S = n\sum_{i,j\in\mathbb{Z}/n}\gcd(i,j,n).$$

Using $\gcd(i,j,n)=\sum_{d\mid\gcd(i,j,n)}\varphi(d)$ and swapping order,
$$T:=\sum_{i,j}\gcd(i,j,n)=\sum_{d\mid n}\varphi(d)\cdot(n/d)^2.$$

This is multiplicative: $T(p^a)=\sum_{k=0}^{a}\varphi(p^k)\,p^{2(a-k)}$.

**Computing $T(2016)$** with $2016=2^5\cdot 3^2\cdot 7$:

- $T(2^5)=1\cdot1024+1\cdot256+2\cdot64+4\cdot16+8\cdot4+16\cdot1=1024+256+128+6
4+32+16=1520.$
- $T(3^2)=1\cdot81+2\cdot9+6\cdot1=81+18+6=105.$
- $T(7)=1\cdot49+6\cdot1=55.$

$$T = 1520\cdot 105\cdot 55 = 159600\cdot 55 = 8{,}778{,}000.$$

**Final count.**
$$S = n\cdot T = 2016\cdot 8{,}778{,}000 = 17{,}696{,}448{,}000,$$
$$N = n^2 + S = 4{,}064{,}256 + 17{,}696{,}448{,}000 = 17{,}700{,}512{,}256.$$

**Verification with small case.** For $p=3$ ($n=2$, range $1\le a,b\le 6$): $T=\
varphi(1)\cdot4+\varphi(2)\cdot1=5$, $S=2\cdot5=10$, $N=4+10=14$. Direct enumera
tion of all $36$ pairs confirms exactly $14$ satisfy $3\mid a^b-b^a$. ✓

**Remainder.**
$$N \bmod 10^6 = 17{,}700{,}512{,}256 \bmod 10^6 = \boxed{512256}.$$
