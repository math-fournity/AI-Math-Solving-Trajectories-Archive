## Solution

**Parametrization.** The curve $C$ is the right half of the unit circle traverse
d from $-i$ to $i$. Parametrize it as $z = e^{i\theta}$ with $\theta \in [-\pi/2
, \pi/2]$.

**The integrand on $C$.** Since $|z|=1$ on $C$, we have $\ln|z|=0$. The principa
l argument on the right half-plane satisfies $\arg(z)=\theta \in [-\pi/2,\pi/2]
\subset (-\pi,\pi)$, so no branch-cut issue arises. Hence
$$\operatorname{Log}(z) = \ln|z| + i\arg(z) = i\theta, \qquad dz = ie^{i\theta}\
,d\theta.$$

**Compute the integral.**
$$\int_C \operatorname{Log}(z)\,dz = \int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2} (i\theta)(ie^{i\theta
})\,d\theta = -\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2} \theta\, e^{i\theta}\,d\theta.$$

Integrate by parts with $u=\theta$, $dv=e^{i\theta}d\theta$, giving $v = e^{i\th
eta}/i$:
$$\int \theta\, e^{i\theta}\,d\theta = \frac{\theta\, e^{i\theta}}{i} + e^{i\the
ta}.$$

Evaluate at the endpoints (using $e^{i\pi/2}=i$, $e^{-i\pi/2}=-i$):

- $\theta=\pi/2$: $\;\dfrac{(\pi/2)\,i}{i} + i = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + i.$
- $\theta=-\pi/2$: $\;\dfrac{(-\pi/2)(-i)}{i} + (-i) = \dfrac{\pi}{2} - i.$

Subtracting:
$$\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2} \theta\, e^{i\theta}\,d\theta = \left(\frac{\pi}{2}+i\ri
ght) - \left(\frac{\pi}{2}-i\right) = 2i.$$

Therefore
$$\int_C \operatorname{Log}(z)\,dz = -2i.$$

**Verification via antiderivative.** On the right half-plane (which avoids the b
ranch cut along the negative real axis), $\operatorname{Log}(z)$ is analytic, an
d integration by parts gives the antiderivative $F(z)=z\operatorname{Log}(z)-z$:

- $F(i) = i\cdot\operatorname{Log}(i) - i = i\cdot\!\left(\tfrac{i\pi}{2}\right)
 - i = -\tfrac{\pi}{2} - i.$
- $F(-i) = (-i)\cdot\operatorname{Log}(-i) - (-i) = (-i)\!\left(-\tfrac{i\pi}{2}
\right) + i = -\tfrac{\pi}{2} + i.$

Thus $F(i)-F(-i) = \left(-\tfrac{\pi}{2}-i\right)-\left(-\tfrac{\pi}{2}+i\right)
 = -2i$, confirming the result.

$$\boxed{\int_C \operatorname{Log}(z)\,dz = -2i.}$$
