# Problem

A high-tech automated chemical refinery manages a fleet of $N$ storage canisters, each containing a specific quantity of a rare catalyst. To maintain the stability of the transport system, the refinery dictates a strict uniformity rule: for any two canisters in the fleet, the ratio of the amount of catalyst in one to the amount in the other must fall within the range of $\left[\frac{4}{5}, \frac{5}{4}\right]$.

The refinery’s logistics system is designed for two different distribution modes:
1.  The canisters can be partitioned into 10 separate loading zones such that the total amount of catalyst in each zone is exactly the same.
2.  The canisters can also be partitioned into 11 separate shipping containers such that the total amount of catalyst in each container is exactly the same.

Based on these operational constraints and the uniformity rule, what is the minimum possible number of canisters $N$ in the fleet?
