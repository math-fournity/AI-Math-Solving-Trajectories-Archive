## Proof

**Step 1: Determine m.**

Since $A(1, \tfrac{3}{2})$ lies on $C: \tfrac{x^2}{4}+\tfrac{y^2}{m}=1$:
$$\frac{1}{4}+\frac{9/4}{m}=1 \;\Longrightarrow\; \frac{9}{4m}=\frac{3}{4}\;\Lon
grightarrow\; m=3.$$
So $C:\dfrac{x^2}{4}+\dfrac{y^2}{3}=1$.

**Step 2: Tangency condition gives the two slopes through $A$.**

A line through $A(1,\tfrac32)$ with slope $k$ has equation $kx-y+(\tfrac32-k)=0$
. Its distance from the circle center $(2,\tfrac32)$ is
$$d=\frac{|2k-\tfrac32+\tfrac32-k|}{\sqrt{k^2+1}}=\frac{|k|}{\sqrt{k^2+1}}=r.$$
Hence $k^2=\dfrac{r^2}{1-r^2}$, giving exactly two tangent lines with slopes
$$k_1=a,\qquad k_2=-a,\qquad a=\frac{r}{\sqrt{1-r^2}}>0.$$

**Step 3: Find the second intersection of a line through $A$ with $C$.**

Substituting $y=kx+(\tfrac32-k)$ into $\tfrac{x^2}{4}+\tfrac{y^2}{3}=1$ and usin
g $x=1$ as a root, the other intersection point is
$$P(k)=\left(\frac{4k^2-12k-3}{4k^2+3},\;\frac{-3(2k-1)(2k+3)}{2(4k^2+3)}\right)
.$$

Set $E=P(a)$, $F=P(-a)$. Then
$$x_F-x_E=\frac{24a}{4a^2+3},$$
$$y_F-y_E=\frac{3(2a+1)(3-2a)+3(2a-1)(2a+3)}{2(4a^2+3)}=\frac{3\cdot 8a}{2(4a^2+
3)}=\frac{12a}{4a^2+3}.$$
Therefore the slope of $EF$ is
$$k_{EF}=\frac{y_F-y_E}{x_F-x_E}=\frac{12a}{24a}=\frac12.$$

**Step 4: Arithmetic-sequence condition.**

The three side slopes are $\{a,\,-a,\,\tfrac12\}$. For three numbers to form an
arithmetic sequence, one equals the average of the other two:

- $\tfrac12=\tfrac{a+(-a)}2=0$: impossible.
- $-a=\tfrac{a+\tfrac12}{2}\Rightarrow a=-\tfrac16<0$: rejected since $a>0$.
- $a=\tfrac{-a+\tfrac12}{2}\Rightarrow 3a=\tfrac12\Rightarrow a=\tfrac16$. ✓

So $a=\tfrac16$, and the slopes $-\tfrac16,\tfrac16,\tfrac12$ form an AP with co
mmon difference $\tfrac13$.

**Step 5: Compute $r$.**

From $a^2=\dfrac{r^2}{1-r^2}$:
$$r^2=\frac{a^2}{1+a^2}=\frac{1/36}{37/36}=\frac1{37}\;\Longrightarrow\; r=\frac
1{\sqrt{37}}=\frac{\sqrt{37}}{37}.$$

**Step 6: Verify the points exist and satisfy all conditions.**

With $a=\tfrac16$:
$$E=P\!\left(\tfrac16\right)=\left(-\tfrac{11}{7},\,\tfrac{15}{14}\right),\qquad
 F=P\!\left(-\tfrac16\right)=\left(-\tfrac{2}{7},\,\tfrac{12}{7}\right).$$

- On $C$: $\tfrac{121/49}{4}+\tfrac{225/196}{3}=\tfrac{121}{196}+\tfrac{225}{588
}=\tfrac{363+225}{588}=1$ ✓;  $\tfrac{4/49}{4}+\tfrac{144/49}{3}=\tfrac{1}{49}+\
tfrac{48}{49}=1$ ✓.
- $k_{EF}=\dfrac{12/7-15/14}{-2/7+11/7}=\dfrac{9/14}{9/7}=\dfrac12$ ✓.
- Slopes $-\tfrac16,\tfrac16,\tfrac12$ form an AP ✓.
- Tangency: line $AE$: $x-6y+8=0$, distance from $(2,\tfrac32)$ is $\tfrac{|2-9+
8|}{\sqrt{37}}=\tfrac1{\sqrt{37}}=r$ ✓. Line $AF$: $x+6y-10=0$, distance is $\tf
rac{|2+9-10|}{\sqrt{37}}=\tfrac1{\sqrt{37}}=r$ ✓.
- $A,E,F$ are three distinct non-collinear points, so $\triangle AEF$ is non-deg
enerate ✓.

**Conclusion.** Such points $E,F$ exist, and the corresponding value is
$$\boxed{r=\dfrac{\sqrt{37}}{37}}.$$
