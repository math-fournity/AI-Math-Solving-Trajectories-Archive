**Claim:** The operator $F$ is monotone on $G$; that is, $(F(x_1)-F(x_2))^T(x_1-
x_2)\ge 0$ for all $x_1,x_2\in G$.

**Setup.** Write $x=(u,\lambda)$ with $u\in\mathbb{R}^{n-m}$, $\lambda\in\mathbb
{R}^m$, and
$$F(u,\lambda)=\Big(f'(u)+\sum_{i=1}^m\lambda_i\,g_i'(u),\;-g_1(u),\dots,-g_m(u)
\Big).$$
Take $x_1=(u_1,\lambda_1)$, $x_2=(u_2,\lambda_2)\in G$, so $\lambda_1\ge 0$, $\l
ambda_2\ge 0$ (componentwise).

**Decomposition of the monotonicity expression.** A direct expansion gives

$$(F(x_1)-F(x_2))^T(x_1-x_2)=\underbrace{\big[f'(u_1)-f'(u_2)\big]^T(u_1-u_2)}_{
\displaystyle T_f}+\sum_{i=1}^m T_i,$$

where, for each $i$,
$$T_i=\big[\lambda_{1i}\,g_i'(u_1)-\lambda_{2i}\,g_i'(u_2)\big]^T(u_1-u_2)-\big[
g_i(u_1)-g_i(u_2)\big](\lambda_{1i}-\lambda_{2i}).$$

**Term $T_f$.** Since $f$ is convex and $C^1$, its gradient is monotone:
$$T_f=\big[f'(u_1)-f'(u_2)\big]^T(u_1-u_2)\ge 0.$$

**Term $T_i$.** Rearranging,
$$T_i=\lambda_{1i}\Big\{\big[g_i'(u_1)\big]^T(u_1-u_2)-\big[g_i(u_1)-g_i(u_2)\bi
g]\Big\}-\lambda_{2i}\Big\{\big[g_i'(u_2)\big]^T(u_1-u_2)-\big[g_i(u_1)-g_i(u_2)
\big]\Big\}.$$

Denote
$$A_i=\big[g_i'(u_1)\big]^T(u_1-u_2)-\big[g_i(u_1)-g_i(u_2)\big],\qquad B_i=\big
[g_i'(u_2)\big]^T(u_1-u_2)-\big[g_i(u_1)-g_i(u_2)\big],$$
so $T_i=\lambda_{1i}A_i-\lambda_{2i}B_i$.

Since each $g_i$ is convex and $C^1$, the first-order characterization of convex
ity gives, for all $a,b$:
$$g_i(b)\ge g_i(a)+\big[g_i'(a)\big]^T(b-a).$$

- With $a=u_1$, $b=u_2$: $g_i(u_2)\ge g_i(u_1)+[g_i'(u_1)]^T(u_2-u_1)$, i.e. $[g
_i'(u_1)]^T(u_1-u_2)\ge g_i(u_1)-g_i(u_2)$. Hence $A_i\ge 0$.
- With $a=u_2$, $b=u_1$: $g_i(u_1)\ge g_i(u_2)+[g_i'(u_2)]^T(u_1-u_2)$, i.e. $g_
i(u_1)-g_i(u_2)\ge [g_i'(u_2)]^T(u_1-u_2)$. Hence $B_i\le 0$.

Now use the sign constraints $\lambda_{1i}\ge 0$, $\lambda_{2i}\ge 0$:
$$\lambda_{1i}A_i\ge 0\quad(\text{nonneg}\times\text{nonneg}),\qquad -\lambda_{2
i}B_i\ge 0\quad(\text{nonneg}\times\text{nonpos}).$$

Therefore $T_i=\lambda_{1i}A_i-\lambda_{2i}B_i\ge 0$ for every $i$.

**Conclusion.** Summing the nonneg pieces,
$$(F(x_1)-F(x_2))^T(x_1-x_2)=T_f+\sum_{i=1}^m T_i\ge 0,$$
for all $x_1,x_2\in G$. Hence **$F$ is monotone on $G$**, and the condition $(F(
x_1)-F(x_2))^T(x_1-x_2)\ge 0$ is always satisfied in $G$.
