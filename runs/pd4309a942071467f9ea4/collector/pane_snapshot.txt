# Topological Entropy of the Dyadic Toeplitz System

## Construction Recap

The dyadic Toeplitz sequence $x \in \{0,1\}^{\mathbb{Z}}$ is built in stages:

- **Step 1:** Place $0$ at every even coordinate. Holes remain at positions $\eq
uiv 1 \pmod{2}$.
- **Step 2:** Place $1$ at every second remaining hole, i.e., positions $\equiv
1 \pmod{4}$. Holes remain at $\equiv 3 \pmod{4}$.
- **Step $j$:** Fill positions $\equiv 2^{j-1}-1 \pmod{2^{j-1}}$ that are still
holes, i.e., positions $\equiv 2^{j-1}-1 \pmod{2^{j}}$ but $\not\equiv -1 \pmod{
2^j}$, with value $(j-1 \bmod 2)$. Holes remain at $\equiv 2^j - 1 \pmod{2^j}$.

After step $k$, the **$k$-skeleton** $s_k$ is the periodic pattern with period $
2^k$ that agrees with $x$ on all positions determined by the first $k$ steps. Wi
thin each period, exactly one position (the one $\equiv -1 \pmod{2^k}$) remains
a hole; all others are fixed to $0$ or $1$.

The **dyadic Toeplitz subshift** $X = \overline{\{\sigma^a x : a \in \mathbb{Z}\
}} \subset \{0,1\}^{\mathbb{Z}}$ is the orbit closure.

## Key Lemma: Hole Spacing

**Lemma.** *For any $y \in X$ and any $k \geq 1$, the positions that are holes a
t level $k$ (not determined by the first $k$ steps) form an arithmetic progressi
on with common difference $2^k$.*

*Proof.* The $k$-skeleton $s_k$ is periodic with period $2^k$, and within each p
eriod exactly one residue class (namely $\equiv -1 \pmod{2^k}$) is undetermined.
 Any $y \in X$ is a limit of shifts $\sigma^{a_m} x$; passing to a subsequence,
$a_m \bmod 2^k$ stabilizes to some $r$, so the holes of $y$ at level $k$ are exa
ctly $\{n : n \equiv r-1 \pmod{2^k}\}$. $\square$

## Word Complexity Bound

Let $p(n)$ denote the number of distinct words of length $n$ appearing in $X$.

**Theorem.** *$p(n) \leq 4n$ for all $n \geq 1$.*

*Proof.* Fix $n \geq 1$ and choose $k$ minimal with $2^k > n$, so $2^{k-1} \leq
n < 2^k$, giving $2^k \leq 2n$.

Consider any word $w = y[i,\, i+n-1]$ of length $n$ with $y \in X$. By the Lemma
, the holes of $y$ at level $k$ are spaced $2^k > n$ apart, so the interval $[i,
 i+n-1]$ contains **at most one** hole position at level $k$.

Every non-hole position in $[i, i+n-1]$ is determined by the $k$-skeleton, which
 is periodic with period $2^k$. The values at these positions depend only on the
 phase $i \bmod 2^k$.

Therefore $w$ is completely specified by:
1. **The phase** $i \bmod 2^k$: at most $2^k \leq 2n$ choices.
2. **The value at the single hole** (if one exists in the interval): at most $2$
 choices.

Hence $p(n) \leq 2 \cdot 2^k \leq 2 \cdot 2n = 4n$. $\square$

## Entropy Computation

The topological entropy is:
$$
h_{\mathrm{top}}(X) = \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{n} \log_2 p(n) \leq \lim_{n \
to \infty} \frac{1}{n} \log_2(4n) = \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{2 + \log_2 n}{n} =
 0.
$$

Since topological entropy is non-negative ($h_{\mathrm{top}} \geq 0$ for any sub
shift), we conclude:

$$
\boxed{h_{\mathrm{top}}(X) = 0.}
$$

## Intuition

The dyadic Toeplitz system is an almost one-to-one extension of the $2$-adic odo
meter. At each stage of the construction, the density of "free" positions halves
, so the number of genuinely independent choices in any block of length $n$ grow
s only as $O(\log n)$, yielding polynomial (in fact linear) word complexity and
zero entropy.
