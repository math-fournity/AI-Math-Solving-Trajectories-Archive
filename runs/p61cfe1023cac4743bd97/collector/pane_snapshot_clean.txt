### Prime ideals ↔ convex subgroups

There is a well-known order-reversing bijection between prime ideals of $A$ and
convex subgroups of $\Gamma$. Given a prime $p$, the associated convex subgroup
is
$$\Delta := v\bigl((A\setminus p)\bigr) = \{v(s) : s \in A,\; s\notin p\}\;\subs
eteq\;\Gamma.$$
Equivalently, $A\setminus p = \{s\in A : v(s)\in\Delta\}$, and
$$p = \{x\in A : v(x)\notin \Delta\} = \{x\in A : v(x)>\delta\ \text{for all}\ \
delta\in\Delta\}.$$
(For $x\in A$ we have $v(x)\geq 0 = 0\in\Delta$; if $v(x)\notin\Delta$ then by c
onvexity $v(x)$ must exceed every element of $\Delta$.)

$$x\in A_p^\times \iff x\in A_p\ \text{and}\ x^{-1}\in A_p \iff v(x)\in\Delta.$$

### Value group of $A/p$

$A/p$ is a valuation ring of its fraction field $K'=\operatorname{Frac}(A/p)$: f
or any $\bar x\in (K')^\times$, write $\bar x=\bar a/\bar b$ with $a,b\in A$, $b

Define $v': (K')^\times\to\Delta$ by $v'(\bar a/\bar b)=v(a)-v(b)$ for $a,b\in A
