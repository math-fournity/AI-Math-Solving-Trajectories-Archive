## Proof

We use two standard facts from 3-manifold topology and knot theory, then combine
 them.

**Fact 1 (Split links and reducibility).** For a link $L \subset S^3$, the compl
ement $S^3 \setminus L$ is irreducible if and only if $L$ is non-split. (A link
is *split* if there exists an embedded 2-sphere $S \subset S^3 \setminus L$ sepa
rating $S^3$ into two balls, each containing at least one component of $L$.)

*Proof of the direction we need (reducible $\Rightarrow$ split).* Suppose $S^3 \
setminus L$ is reducible, so there is an essential 2-sphere $S \subset S^3 \setm
inus L$ (one that bounds no 3-ball in $S^3 \setminus L$). Since $S^3$ is irreduc
ible, $S$ bounds a 3-ball $B \subset S^3$. Let $B' = \overline{S^3 \setminus B}$
, also a 3-ball. Since $S$ is essential in $S^3 \setminus L$, neither $B$ nor $B
'$ is contained in $S^3 \setminus L$; that is, $L \cap B \neq \emptyset$ and $L
\cap B' \neq \emptyset$. Thus $L$ has components on both sides of $S$, so $L$ is
 split. $\square$

**Fact 2 (Nonzero linking number implies non-split).** If $L = \gamma_1 \cup \ga
mma_2$ is a 2-component oriented link with $\operatorname{lk}(\gamma_1, \gamma_2
) \neq 0$, then $L$ is non-split.

*Proof.* Suppose for contradiction that $L$ is split. Then there is a 2-sphere $
S \subset S^3 \setminus L$ with $\gamma_1 \subset B$ and $\gamma_2 \subset B'$,
where $B, B'$ are the two complementary 3-balls bounded by $S$. Since $\gamma_1
\subset B \cong B^3$ and $\gamma_1$ is null-homologous in $B$, there exists a Se
ifert surface $\Sigma$ for $\gamma_1$ with $\Sigma \subset B$. The linking numbe
r is
$$\operatorname{lk}(\gamma_1, \gamma_2) = \Sigma \cdot \gamma_2,$$
the algebraic intersection number. But $\Sigma \subset B$ and $\gamma_2 \subset
B'$ with $B \cap B' = S$ and $\Sigma \cap S = \partial \Sigma = \gamma_1$ (which
 is disjoint from $\gamma_2$), so $\Sigma \cap \gamma_2 = \emptyset$. Hence $\op
eratorname{lk}(\gamma_1, \gamma_2) = 0$, contradicting the hypothesis. $\square$

**Combining.** By hypothesis $N(L) = \operatorname{lk}(\gamma_1, \gamma_2) \neq
0$. By Fact 2, $L$ is non-split. By Fact 1, $S^3 \setminus L$ is irreducible. $\
blacksquare$
