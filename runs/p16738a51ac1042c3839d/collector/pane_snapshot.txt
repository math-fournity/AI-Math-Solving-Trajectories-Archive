Let $i,j \in \{1,\dots,n-1\}$ and expand
$$\cos\frac{(i-j)\pi}{n}=\cos\frac{i\pi}{n}\cos\frac{j\pi}{n}+\sin\frac{i\pi}{n}
\sin\frac{j\pi}{n}.$$

Then
$$a_{ij}=\sin\tfrac{i\pi}{n}\sin\tfrac{j\pi}{n}\cos\tfrac{(i-j)\pi}{n}
=\underbrace{\sin\tfrac{i\pi}{n}\cos\tfrac{i\pi}{n}\,\sin\tfrac{j\pi}{n}\cos\tfr
ac{j\pi}{n}}_{\tfrac14\sin\tfrac{2i\pi}{n}\sin\tfrac{2j\pi}{n}}
+\underbrace{\sin^2\tfrac{i\pi}{n}\,\sin^2\tfrac{j\pi}{n}}_{v_i v_j}.$$

Setting $u_i=\sin\frac{2i\pi}{n}$ and $v_i=\sin^2\frac{i\pi}{n}=\frac{1-\cos\fra
c{2i\pi}{n}}{2}$, we obtain the **rank‑2 decomposition**
$$A = vv^{T}+\tfrac14\,uu^{T}.$$

Hence $A$ has at most two non‑zero eigenvalues and at least $n-3$ zeros.

---

**Inner products.** Using $\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}e^{2\pi i mk/n}=0$ for $n\nmid m$ (va
lid for $n\ge 3$):

* $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}\sin\tfrac{2k\pi}{n}=0,\qquad \sum_{k=1}^{n-1}\c
os\tfrac{2k\pi}{n}=-1,\qquad \sum_{k=1}^{n-1}\cos\tfrac{4k\pi}{n}=-1,\qquad \sum
_{k=1}^{n-1}\sin\tfrac{4k\pi}{n}=0.$

Therefore
$$\langle v,v\rangle=\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}\frac{(1-\cos\frac{2k\pi}{n})^2}{4}
=\frac14\Big[(n-1)+2+\frac{(n-1)+(-1)}{2}\Big]
=\frac14\cdot\frac{3n}{2}=\frac{3n}{8},$$

$$\langle u,u\rangle=\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}\sin^2\tfrac{2k\pi}{n}
=\frac{(n-1)-(-1)}{2}=\frac{n}{2},$$

$$\langle u,v\rangle=\frac12\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}\sin\tfrac{2k\pi}{n}\bigl(1-\cos\tfr
ac{2k\pi}{n}\bigr)
=\frac12\Big[0-\tfrac12\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}\sin\tfrac{4k\pi}{n}\Big]=0.$$

So **$u\perp v$**.

---

**Eigenvalues.** Since $u\perp v$:
$$Av=v\langle v,v\rangle+\tfrac14 u\langle u,v\rangle=\tfrac{3n}{8}\,v,\qquad
Au=v\langle v,u\rangle+\tfrac14 u\langle u,u\rangle=\tfrac{n}{8}\,u.$$

Thus $v$ is an eigenvector with eigenvalue $\dfrac{3n}{8}$, and $u$ is an eigenv
ector with eigenvalue $\dfrac{n}{8}$. Every vector orthogonal to $\operatorname{
span}\{u,v\}$ lies in $\ker A$, giving $n-3$ zero eigenvalues.
