Since $\nu(A) = \int_A f\,d\mu > 0$, the set $\{x \in A : f(x) > 0\}$ has positi

$$A = \bigcup_{k=1}^\infty \bigl(A \cap \{f \geq 1/k\}\bigr) \cup \bigl(A \cap \

Since $\nu(A \cap \{f=0\}) = 0$ and $\nu(A) > 0$, by continuity from below there
