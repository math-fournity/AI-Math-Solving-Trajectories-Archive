# Problem

In a specialized logistics hub, four automated transport drones—Alpha, Beta, Gamma, and Delta—are assigned to move cargo containers. The performance of these drones is governed by two strict operational constraints based on their individual engine thrust levels, represented by the positive real values $a$, $b$, $c$, and $d$:

1.  **Productivity Constraint:** The combined product of the four thrust levels must be exactly 7 units ($abcd = 7$).
2.  **Energy Constraint:** The total energy consumption, calculated as the sum of the squares of their thrust levels, must be exactly 20 units ($a^2 + b^2 + c^2 + d^2 = 20$).

The efficiency of the hub is determined by the "Interaction Sum," which is the sum of the products of thrust levels for drones working in specific sequential pairs: Alpha with Beta, Beta with Gamma, Gamma with Delta, and Delta back with Alpha.

Calculate the maximum possible value of this Interaction Sum, defined as $ab + bc + cd + da$.
