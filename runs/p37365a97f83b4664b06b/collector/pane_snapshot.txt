**Factorizations.** $2023 = 7 \times 17^2$, so the divisors of 2023 are $\{1, 7,
 17, 119, 289, 2023\}$ (six divisors). Also $2024 = 8 \times 11 \times 23$.

Since $\{0,\ldots,2023\}$ is a complete residue system mod 2024, by CRT the coun

\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z})^2 : m^2 \equiv f(n) \pmod{q}\}$ and $f(n) = n + n^7 + n^

**Modulo 8.** Squares mod 8: $S_8(0)=2,\; S_8(1)=4,\; S_8(4)=2$, else $0$.

- $n\equiv 2$: $2^1=2$, higher powers $\equiv 0$, so $f=2$, $S=0$.
- $n\equiv 4$: $4^1=4$, higher powers $\equiv 0$, so $f=4$, $S=2$.
- $n\equiv 6$: $6^1=6$, $6^2\equiv 4$, $6^3\equiv 0$, so $f=6$, $S=0$.

$$N_8 = 2+0+2+0+0+0+0+0 = 4.$$
