# Problem

In a specialized logistics warehouse, a supervisor organizes a shipment of exactly 100 high-tech sensors into a perfect $10 \times 10$ square grid. Each sensor is programmed with a specific frequency channel, which must be a positive integer no greater than $k$.

To prevent signal interference, the warehouse follows a strict "Linear Gradient Protocol":
1. Within every row of 10 sensors, the frequency channels must form an arithmetic sequence. Furthermore, no two rows can share the same common difference.
2. Within every column of 10 sensors, the frequency channels must also form an arithmetic sequence. Similarly, no two columns can share the same common difference.

What is the smallest possible value of $k$ (the maximum frequency channel used) that allows the supervisor to arrange the 100 sensors while satisfying all the protocol requirements?
