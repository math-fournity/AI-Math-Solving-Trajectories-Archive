### Setup

We seek all positive-integer triples $(a,b,c)$ such that
$$b\mid(a+1),\qquad c\mid(b+1),\qquad a\mid(c+1).$$
Write $a+1=bx,\; b+1=cy,\; c+1=az$ with $x,y,z\in\mathbb{Z}_{>0}$.

### Bounding inequalities

From $a+1=bx\ge b$: $a\ge b-1$, i.e. $b\le a+1$.
From $b+1=cy\ge c$: $b\ge c-1$, i.e. $c\le b+1$.
From $c+1=az\ge a$: $c\ge a-1$, i.e. $a\le c+1$.

### Using cyclic symmetry

The system is invariant under the cyclic rotation $(a,b,c)\mapsto(b,c,a)$. Hence
 every solution is a cyclic rotation of a solution in which $a$ is a maximum. We
 classify those.

**Case $a=1$.** Since $a$ is maximal and all entries are positive, $a=b=c=1$. Ch
eck: $1\mid2$ three times. ✓
Solution: $(1,1,1)$, sum $3$.

**Case $a\ge2$.** Since $a$ is maximal, $c\le a$, so $c+1\le a+1$. The relation
$c+1=az$ with $z\ge1$ gives $c+1\ge a$. If $z\ge2$ then $c+1\ge2a$. But $2a-(c+1
)\ge2a-a-1=a-1\ge1$ (using $a\ge c$ and $a\ge2$), contradicting $c+1\ge2a$. Henc
e $z=1$, so
$$a=c+1.$$

Now $a+1=c+2=bx$, and $b$ satisfies $c-1\le b\le a=c+1$ (from $b\ge c-1$ and $b\
le a+1$ combined with $b\mid c+2$; in fact $b\le a$ since $b=c+1$ will be handle
d). Also $c\mid b+1$.

So $b\in\{c-1,\,c,\,c+1\}$ (intersecting $[c-1,c+1]$ with divisors of $c+2$):

- **$b=c+1$:** $(c+1)\mid(c+2)\Rightarrow(c+1)\mid1\Rightarrow c=0$, not positiv
e. ✗
- **$b=c$:** $c\mid(c+2)\Rightarrow c\mid2$, so $c\in\{1,2\}$; and $c\mid(b+1)=c
+1\Rightarrow c\mid1\Rightarrow c=1$.
  Gives $(a,b,c)=(2,1,1)$. Check: $1\mid3,\;1\mid2,\;2\mid2$. ✓ Sum $4$.
- **$b=c-1$:** $(c-1)\mid(c+2)$. Since $c+2=(c-1)+3$, we get $(c-1)\mid3$, so $c
-1\in\{1,3\}$, i.e. $c\in\{2,4\}$. Also $c\mid(b+1)=c$ ✓ automatically, and $b\g
e1$ requires $c\ge2$.
  - $c=2$: $(a,b,c)=(3,1,2)$. Check: $1\mid4,\;2\mid2,\;3\mid3$. ✓ Sum $6$.
  - $c=4$: $(a,b,c)=(5,3,4)$. Check: $3\mid6,\;4\mid4,\;5\mid5$. ✓ Sum $12$.

These are all solutions with $a$ maximal: $(1,1,1),\,(2,1,1),\,(3,1,2),\,(5,3,4)
$.

### Generating all solutions by cyclic rotation

- $(1,1,1)$: orbit $\{(1,1,1)\}$ — $1$ triple.
- $(2,1,1)$: orbit $\{(2,1,1),(1,1,2),(1,2,1)\}$ — $3$ triples (each verified: e
.g. $(1,2,1)$: $2\mid2,\,1\mid3,\,1\mid2$ ✓).
- $(3,1,2)$: orbit $\{(3,1,2),(1,2,3),(2,3,1)\}$ — $3$ triples (e.g. $(1,2,3)$:
$2\mid2,\,3\mid3,\,1\mid4$ ✓).
- $(5,3,4)$: orbit $\{(5,3,4),(3,4,5),(4,5,3)\}$ — $3$ triples (e.g. $(3,4,5)$:
$4\mid4,\,5\mid5,\,3\mid6$ ✓).

Total: $1+3+3+3=10$ triples.
