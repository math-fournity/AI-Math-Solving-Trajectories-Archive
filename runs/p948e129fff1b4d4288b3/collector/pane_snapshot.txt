## Proof

Let $X = A \vee B$ be the wedge of two circles with $\pi_1(X) = \langle a, b \ra
ngle$, the free group $F_2$ on generators $a$ (loop around $A$) and $b$ (loop ar
ound $B$). Let $\Gamma = [F_2, F_2]$ be the commutator subgroup.

**Step 1: Identify the quotient.** The commutator subgroup $\Gamma$ is the kerne
l of the abelianization homomorphism
$$\varphi \colon F_2 \to F_2^{\mathrm{ab}} \cong \mathbb{Z}^2, \qquad a \mapsto
(1,0),\quad b \mapsto (0,1).$$
Since $\Gamma = \ker\varphi$ is normal, the corresponding covering $\widetilde{X
} \to X$ is a regular (Galois) covering with deck group
$$\mathrm{Deck}(\widetilde{X}/X) \cong F_2/\Gamma \cong \mathbb{Z}^2.$$

**Step 2: Construct the covering as a graph.** A covering of a graph is a graph,
 and the covering corresponding to a normal subgroup $N \trianglelefteq F_2$ is
the Schreier coset graph of $F_2/N$. Here $F_2/\Gamma \cong \mathbb{Z}^2$, so th
e vertices of $\widetilde{X}$ are the elements of $\mathbb{Z}^2$. For each verte
x $(m,n) \in \mathbb{Z}^2$:
- an **$a$-labeled edge** goes from $(m,n)$ to $(m+1,n)$,
- a **$b$-labeled edge** goes from $(m,n)$ to $(m,n+1)$.

This is precisely the Cayley graph of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ with respect to the generat
ing set $\{a,b\}$.

**Step 3: Realize as a subset of $\mathbb{R}^2$.** Embed this graph in $\mathbb{
R}^2$ as the **unit square grid**:
- Place a vertex at each lattice point $(m, n) \in \mathbb{Z}^2 \subset \mathbb{
R}^2$.
- For each $(m,n)$, draw a unit interval $[(m,n),(m+1,n)]$ (an $a$-edge) and a u
nit interval $[(m,n),(m,n+1)]$ (a $b$-edge).

Thus $\widetilde{X}$ is the 1-skeleton of the standard square tiling of $\mathbb
{R}^2$:
$$\widetilde{X} \;=\; \bigcup_{(m,n)\in\mathbb{Z}^2}\Big(\,[m,m+1]\times\{n\}\;\
cup\;\{m\}\times[n,n+1]\,\Big)\;\subset\;\mathbb{R}^2.$$

**Step 4: Define the covering map.** The map $p \colon \widetilde{X} \to X$ send
s every $a$-edge homeomorphically onto the circle $A$ and every $b$-edge homeomo
rphically onto the circle $B$, with orientations consistent with the labels. Con
cretely, each horizontal unit interval wraps once around $A$ (parameterized by $
t \mapsto e^{2\pi i t}$) and each vertical unit interval wraps once around $B$.

**Step 5: Verify the subgroup.** A loop in $\widetilde{X}$ based at the vertex $
(0,0)$ projects to a word $w \in F_2$. The endpoint of the lifted path is $\varp
hi(w) \in \mathbb{Z}^2$. The lift is closed (i.e., $w \in \pi_1$ of the covering
) if and only if $\varphi(w) = (0,0)$, i.e., $w \in \ker\varphi = \Gamma$. Hence
$$p_*(\pi_1(\widetilde{X}, (0,0))) = \Gamma,$$
confirming that $\widetilde{X}$ is exactly the covering space corresponding to $
\Gamma$.

**Conclusion.** The covering space $\widetilde{X} \to X$ corresponding to the co
mmutator subgroup $\Gamma = [\pi_1(X),\pi_1(X)]$ is the **infinite square grid**
 in $\mathbb{R}^2$ — the Cayley graph of $\mathbb{Z}^2$ with generators $a, b$ —
 with horizontal edges mapping to $A$ and vertical edges mapping to $B$. It is t
he universal abelian cover of $X$, with deck group $\mathbb{Z}^2$ acting by tran
slations $(m,n)\mapsto(m+k, n+\ell)$.
