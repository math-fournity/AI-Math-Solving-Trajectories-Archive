## Proof

**Setup and notation.**

Let $R = GR(p^2, m)$ be the Galois ring of characteristic $p^2$ and size $p^{2m}
$, with $GR(p^2,1) = \mathbb{Z}_{p^2}$. The trace $Tr: R \to \mathbb{Z}_{p^2}$ i
s the standard Galois ring trace. The primitive root is $w = e^{2\pi i/p^2}$.

The Walsh transform of $f: R \to \mathbb{Z}_{p^2}$ at $a \in R$ is:
$$W_f(a) = \sum_{x \in R} w^{\,f(x) - Tr(ax)}$$

(Note: since $f(x) \in \mathbb{Z}_{p^2} = GR(p^2,1)$, the trace of $f(x)$ is its
elf, so $Tr(f(x) - ax) = f(x) - Tr(ax)$.)

**Step 1: Parseval's identity.**

The additive group $(R, +) \cong (\mathbb{Z}_{p^2})^m$ is a finite abelian group
 of order $|R| = p^{2m}$. The characters $\chi_a(x) = w^{Tr(ax)}$ for $a \in R$
form the complete dual group $\hat{R}$, which is isomorphic to $R$ (so $|\hat{R}
| = p^{2m}$).

By the standard Parseval identity for the Walsh transform on finite abelian grou
ps:
$$\sum_{a \in R} |W_f(a)|^2 = |R|^2 = p^{4m}$$

This holds for **any** function $f: R \to \mathbb{Z}_{p^2}$, regardless of wheth
er $f$ is bent.

**Step 2: The bent condition forces the magnitude.**

By definition, $f$ is bent if and only if $|W_f(a)|$ is constant (independent of
 $a$) for all $a \in R$. Let this constant be $C$. Substituting into Parseval's
identity:

$$|R| \cdot C^2 = p^{4m}$$
$$p^{2m} \cdot C^2 = p^{4m}$$
$$C^2 = p^{2m}$$
$$C = p^m$$

(taking the positive root since $|W_f(a)| \geq 0$).

**Step 3: Compare with the stated value.**

The stated condition requires:
$$|W_f(a)| = |GR(p^2,1)|^{m/2} = (p^2)^{m/2} = p^m$$

This equals exactly $C = p^m = \sqrt{p^{2m}} = \sqrt{|R|}$, the value derived in
 Step 2.

**Step 4: Consistency verification.**

- $|R| = p^{2m}$, so $\sqrt{|R|} = p^m$.
- $|GR(p^2,1)|^{m/2} = (p^2)^{m/2} = p^m$.
- These are equal: $\sqrt{|GR(p^2,m)|} = |GR(p^2,1)|^{m/2} = p^m$.

The condition is self-consistent: a constant Walsh magnitude of $p^m$ satisfies
Parseval's identity exactly, confirming that $p^m$ is the unique possible consta
nt magnitude for a bent function on this domain.

The condition
$$\left|\sum_{x \in GR(p^2,m)} w^{Tr(f(x)-ax)}\right| = |GR(p^2,1)|^{m/2} = p^m
= \sqrt{|GR(p^2,m)|}$$
for all $a \in GR(p^2,m)$ is **true**. It is the correct and complete characteri
