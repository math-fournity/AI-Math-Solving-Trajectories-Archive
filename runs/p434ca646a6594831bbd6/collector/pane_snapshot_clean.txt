## Proof

Let $\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{Q}(\xi)$ where $\xi$ is a primitive $p$-th root of uni
ty, and let $R = \mathbb{Z}[\xi]$ be its ring of integers (it is a standard fact
 that the ring of integers of a cyclotomic field $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_n)$ is $\math
bb{Z}[\zeta_n]$).

The minimal polynomial of $\xi$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ is the $p$-th cyclotomic polyn
omial
$$\Phi_p(x) = 1 + x + x^2 + \cdots + x^{p-1} = \frac{x^p - 1}{x - 1} = \prod_{k=
1}^{p-1}(x - \xi^k).$$

**Step 1: Factorization of $p$ in $R$.**

Evaluating $\Phi_p$ at $x = 1$:
$$p = \Phi_p(1) = \prod_{k=1}^{p-1}(1 - \xi^k).$$

**Step 2: Each $(1 - \xi^k)$ is an associate of $(1 - \xi)$.**

For $1 \le k \le p-1$, since $\gcd(k, p) = 1$, $\xi^k$ is also a primitive $p$-t
h root of unity. We have
$$\frac{1 - \xi^k}{1 - \xi} = 1 + \xi + \xi^2 + \cdots + \xi^{k-1} \in R.$$

Conversely, let $a$ be the multiplicative inverse of $k$ modulo $p$ (so $1 \le a
 \le p-1$ and $ka \equiv 1 \pmod{p}$). Then $\xi = (\xi^k)^a$, and
$$1 - \xi = 1 - (\xi^k)^a = (1 - \xi^k)\bigl(1 + \xi^k + \xi^{2k} + \cdots + \xi
^{(a-1)k}\bigr),$$
so
$$\frac{1 - \xi}{1 - \xi^k} = 1 + \xi^k + \xi^{2k} + \cdots + \xi^{(a-1)k} \in R
.$$

Hence $(1 - \xi^k)/(1 - \xi)$ is a unit in $R$ for every $k$, i.e., $(1 - \xi^k)
 = u_k(1 - \xi)$ for some unit $u_k \in R^\times$.

**Step 3: The ideal factorization.**

Substituting into the product from Step 1:
$$p = \prod_{k=1}^{p-1}(1 - \xi^k) = \left(\prod_{k=1}^{p-1} u_k\right)(1 - \xi)
^{p-1} = u \cdot (1 - \xi)^{p-1},$$
where $u = \prod u_k$ is a unit. Therefore, as ideals,
$$(p)R = (1 - \xi)^{p-1} R.$$

**Step 4: $(1 - \xi)$ is a prime (maximal) ideal.**

The norm of $1 - \xi$ is
$$N_{\mathbb{K}/\mathbb{Q}}(1 - \xi) = \prod_{k=1}^{p-1}(1 - \xi^k) = p.$$

Since $|R/(1-\xi)| = |N(1-\xi)| = p$ is prime, the quotient $R/(1-\xi) \cong \ma
thbb{F}_p$ is a field, so $(1-\xi)$ is a maximal, hence prime, ideal. Denote $\m
athfrak{p} = (1 - \xi)$.

**Conclusion.**

The prime $p$ is **totally ramified** in $\mathbb{Q}(\xi)$: there is a unique pr
ime ideal $\mathfrak{p} = (1 - \xi)$ of $R$ lying above $p$, with ramification i
ndex $e = p - 1$ and residue degree $f = 1$, and
$$\boxed{(p)R = \mathfrak{p}^{\,p-1} = (1 - \xi)^{p-1} R.}$$
