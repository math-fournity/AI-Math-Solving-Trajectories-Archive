## Solution

We analyze the series $S = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left(\cos\frac{a}{n^3}\right)^{n
^3}$, where $a$ is a real constant.

**Key step: determine the limit of the general term.**

We compute $\lim_{n\to\infty} \left(\cos\frac{a}{n^3}\right)^{n^3}$.

Set $x = \frac{a}{n^3}$, so $x \to 0$ as $n \to \infty$, and the expression beco
mes $\left(\cos x\right)^{a/x}$ (since $n^3 = a/x$). Taking logarithms:

$$\ln\left(\cos\frac{a}{n^3}\right)^{n^3} = n^3 \ln\cos\frac{a}{n^3} = \frac{a}{
x}\ln\cos x.$$

Using the Taylor expansion of $\ln\cos x$ around $x=0$:

$$\cos x = 1 - \frac{x^2}{2} + O(x^4), \qquad \ln\cos x = -\frac{x^2}{2} - \frac
{x^4}{12} + O(x^6).$$

Therefore:

$$n^3 \ln\cos\frac{a}{n^3} = n^3\left(-\frac{a^2}{2n^6} - \frac{a^4}{12n^{12}} +
 O(n^{-18})\right) = -\frac{a^2}{2n^3} - \frac{a^4}{12n^9} + O(n^{-15}).$$

As $n \to \infty$:

$$n^3 \ln\cos\frac{a}{n^3} \longrightarrow 0.$$

Hence:

$$\lim_{n\to\infty} \left(\cos\frac{a}{n^3}\right)^{n^3} = e^0 = 1.$$

**Applying the divergence test (n-th term test).**

A necessary condition for the series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} b_n$ to converge is $\
lim_{n\to\infty} b_n = 0$. Here:

$$\lim_{n\to\infty} \left(\cos\frac{a}{n^3}\right)^{n^3} = 1 \neq 0.$$

Since the general term does not tend to zero, the series **diverges** by the n-t
h term test.

**Verification of edge cases:**

- **$a = 0$:** Each term equals $\cos(0)^{n^3} = 1$, so $S = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}
 1 = +\infty$. ✓ Diverges.
- **$a \neq 0$:** As shown above, the general term $\to 1 \neq 0$. ✓ Diverges.
- **Sign of terms:** For all sufficiently large $n$, $\frac{a}{n^3}$ is small, s
o $\cos\frac{a}{n^3} > 0$, and the terms are positive and approach $1$. The part
ial sums grow without bound, so $S = +\infty$.

**Conclusion.**

For every real constant $a$, the general term $\left(\cos\frac{a}{n^3}\right)^{n
^3}$ tends to $1$ as $n \to \infty$, failing the necessary condition for converg
ence. Therefore:

$$\boxed{\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left(\cos\frac{a}{n^3}\right)^{n^3} = +\infty \tex
t{ (the series diverges for all real } a\text{).}}$$
