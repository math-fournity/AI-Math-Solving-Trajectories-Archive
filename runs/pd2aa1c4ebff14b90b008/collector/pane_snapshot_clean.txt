## Solution

**Closed form of $F$.** The characteristic equation of the recurrence $F(n)=\fra
c52 F(n-1)-F(n-2)$ is
$$x^2-\tfrac52 x+1=0 \implies (2x-1)(x-2)=0,$$
with roots $x=2$ and $x=\tfrac12$. Writing $F(n)=A\cdot 2^n+B\cdot 2^{-n}$ and u
sing $F(0)=0,\;F(1)=\tfrac32$:
$$A+B=0,\qquad 2A-\tfrac{A}{2}=\tfrac{3A}{2}=\tfrac32 \implies A=1,\;B=-1.$$
Hence
$$F(n)=2^n-2^{-n}.$$

**The summand.** Set $x_n = 2^{2^n}$, so $F(2^n)=x_n-x_n^{-1}=\dfrac{x_n^2-1}{x_
n}$, and
$$\frac{1}{F(2^n)}=\frac{x_n}{x_n^2-1}.$$
Since $x_{n+1}=2^{2^{n+1}}=\bigl(2^{2^n}\bigr)^2=x_n^2$, we have $x_n^2-1=x_{n+1
}-1$.

**Telescoping.** Observe
$$\frac{x_n}{x_n^2-1}=\frac{(x_n+1)-1}{(x_n-1)(x_n+1)}=\frac{1}{x_n-1}-\frac{1}{
x_n^2-1}=\frac{1}{x_n-1}-\frac{1}{x_{n+1}-1}.$$
Therefore
$$\sum_{n=0}^{N}\frac{1}{F(2^n)}=\sum_{n=0}^{N}\left(\frac{1}{x_n-1}-\frac{1}{x_
{n+1}-1}\right)=\frac{1}{x_0-1}-\frac{1}{x_{N+1}-1}.$$

**Evaluation.** Since $x_0=2^{2^0}=2$, we get $\dfrac{1}{x_0-1}=\dfrac{1}{1}=1$.
 As $N\to\infty$, $x_{N+1}=2^{2^{N+1}}\to\infty$, so $\dfrac{1}{x_{N+1}-1}\to 0$
. Thus
$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{F(2^n)}=1.$$

$, $\tfrac1{F(2)}=\tfrac{4}{15}$; $F(4)=\tfrac{255}{16}$, $\tfrac1{F(4)}=\tfrac{
