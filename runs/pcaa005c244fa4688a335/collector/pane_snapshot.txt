**Theorem.** *Every coadjoint orbit $O_\mu$ of a Lie group $G$ carries a canonic
al $G$-invariant symplectic form (the Kirillov–Kostant–Souriau form), and is the

---

**Setup.** Let $G$ be a Lie group with Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$, and let $\mu\i
n\mathfrak g^*$. The coadjoint orbit is
$$O_\mu = \{\operatorname{Ad}^*_g\mu : g\in G\}\cong G/G_\mu,$$
where $G_\mu=\{g\in G:\operatorname{Ad}^*_g\mu=\mu\}$ is the stabilizer, with Li
e algebra $\mathfrak g_\mu=\{X\in\mathfrak g:\operatorname{ad}^*_X\mu=0\}$.

The tangent space at $\mu$ is
$$T_\mu O_\mu = \{\operatorname{ad}^*_X\mu : X\in\mathfrak g\}\cong \mathfrak g/
\mathfrak g_\mu.$$

---

**Definition of the KKS form.** At the base point $\mu$, define a $2$-form $\ome
ga_\mu$ on $T_\mu O_\mu$ by
$$\omega_\mu\!\bigl(\operatorname{ad}^*_X\mu,\;\operatorname{ad}^*_Y\mu\bigr)\;=
\;\langle\mu,\,[X,Y]\rangle,$$
where $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ is the natural pairing $\mathfrak g^*\times\ma
thfrak g\to\mathbb R$. Extend $\omega$ to all of $O_\mu$ by $G$-equivariance:
$$\omega_{\operatorname{Ad}^*_g\mu}\!\bigl(\operatorname{ad}^*_{\operatorname{Ad

operatorname{Ad}^*_g\mu)\bigr)=\langle\operatorname{Ad}^*_g\mu,\,[\operatorname{
Ad}_g X,\operatorname{Ad}_g Y]\rangle=\langle\mu,[X,Y]\rangle.$$

**Step 1: Well-definedness.** We must check that if we change the representative

$$\omega_\mu\!\bigl(\operatorname{ad}^*_{X+X'}\mu,\operatorname{ad}^*_Y\mu\bigr)
=\langle\mu,[X+X',Y]\rangle=\langle\mu,[X,Y]\rangle+\underbrace{\langle\mu,[X',Y
]\rangle}_{=0}=\omega_\mu\!\bigl(\operatorname{ad}^*_X\mu,\operatorname{ad}^*_Y\
mu\bigr).$$
So $\omega_\mu$ descends to a well-defined form on $\mathfrak g/\mathfrak g_\mu$
.

**Step 2: Skew-symmetry and bilinearity.** Bilinearity is immediate from bilinea
rity of the Lie bracket and the pairing. Skew-symmetry:
$$\omega_\mu\!\bigl(\operatorname{ad}^*_X\mu,\operatorname{ad}^*_Y\mu\bigr)=\lan
gle\mu,[X,Y]\rangle=-\langle\mu,[Y,X]\rangle=-\omega_\mu\!\bigl(\operatorname{ad

**Step 3: Non-degeneracy.** Suppose $\operatorname{ad}^*_X\mu$ lies in the radic

$$\omega_\mu\!\bigl(\operatorname{ad}^*_X\mu,\operatorname{ad}^*_Y\mu\bigr)=\lan
gle\mu,[X,Y]\rangle=0\quad\text{for all }Y\in\mathfrak g.$$
By definition of the coadjoint action, $(\operatorname{ad}^*_X\mu)(Y)=-\langle\m
u,[X,Y]\rangle=0$ for all $Y$, so $\operatorname{ad}^*_X\mu=0$, i.e., the tangen

$$d\omega_\mu(X^*,Y^*,Z^*)=-\langle\mu,\bigl(-[[Y,Z],X]+[[X,Z],Y]\bigr)\rangle+\

$$\boxed{(O_\mu,\,\omega)\text{ is a symplectic manifold.}}$$
