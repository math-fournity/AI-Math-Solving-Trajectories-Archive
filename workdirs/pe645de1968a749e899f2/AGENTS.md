# Problem

In a specialized logistics warehouse, a technician must organize 7 shipping containers into a single row on a loading dock. The inventory consists of:
- 3 identical heavy containers painted Crimson.
- 2 identical medium containers painted Azure.
- 1 light container painted Amber.
- 1 light container painted Emerald.

Due to weight distribution safety protocols, the technician must follow two strict rules when arranging the row:
1. No two Crimson containers can be placed in adjacent positions.
2. The Amber container must be located in one of the first four positions (positions 1, 2, 3, or 4) starting from the left end of the dock.

If the technician randomly selects an arrangement that satisfies both of these specific safety protocols, what is the probability that he accidentally recreates the original, unique sequence that was intended for the shipment?
