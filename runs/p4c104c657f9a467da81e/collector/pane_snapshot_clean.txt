## Solution

We wish to minimize $\operatorname{trace}(AA^T)$ over all $n\times n$ nonsingula
r matrices $A$ with integer entries.

**Key identity.** For any matrix $A=(a_{ij})$,
$$
(AA^T)_{ii}=\sum_{j=1}^{n}a_{ij}^2,
$$
so
$$
\operatorname{trace}(AA^T)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\sum_{j=1}^{n}a_{ij}^2,
$$
i.e. $\operatorname{trace}(AA^T)$ is the sum of squares of **all** entries of $A
$.

Equivalently, writing $A=[v_1\;v_2\;\cdots\;v_n]$ by columns,
$$
\operatorname{trace}(AA^T)=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\|v_j\|^2,
$$
where $\|v_j\|^2$ is the sum of squares of the entries of column $v_j$.

**Lower bound.** Since $A$ is nonsingular, its columns $v_1,\dots,v_n$ are linea
rly independent over $\mathbb{R}$, hence in particular **each $v_j\neq 0$**. Eac
h $v_j$ is an integer vector, so at least one of its entries is a nonzero intege
r, meaning $|\text{that entry}|\ge 1$. Therefore
$$
\|v_j\|^2\ge 1\quad\text{for every }j=1,\dots,n.
$$
Summing,
$$
\operatorname{trace}(AA^T)=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\|v_j\|^2\ge n.
$$

**Achievability.** The identity matrix $I_n$ is nonsingular with integer entries
, and
$$
\operatorname{trace}(I_n I_n^T)=\operatorname{trace}(I_n)=n.
$$
(More generally, any signed permutation matrix — exactly one $\pm 1$ in each row
 and column, all other entries $0$ — attains the value $n$.)

**Conclusion.** The lower bound $n$ is tight, so the minimum value is

$$
\boxed{n}.
$$
