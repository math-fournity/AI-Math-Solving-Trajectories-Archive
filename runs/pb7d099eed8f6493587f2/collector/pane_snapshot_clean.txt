## Solution

Place the right angle at the origin, with legs along the coordinate axes: vertic
es $A=(0,0)$ (right angle), $B=(5,0)$, $C=(0,12)$, so the hypotenuse is $BC$.

**Hypotenuse equation.** The line through $(5,0)$ and $(0,12)$ is
$$\frac{x}{5}+\frac{y}{12}=1 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 12x+5y=60.$$

**Locating the semicircle.** The semicircle has its diameter on the hypotenuse a
nd is tangent to both legs (the $x$-axis and $y$-axis). Let its radius be $r$ an
d its center be $O$.

- Tangent to the $x$-axis ($y=0$) $\Rightarrow$ the center is at distance $r$ fr
om the $x$-axis, so $O_y = r$.
- Tangent to the $y$-axis ($x=0$) $\Rightarrow$ the center is at distance $r$ fr
om the $y$-axis, so $O_x = r$.

Hence $O = (r, r)$.

Since the diameter lies on the hypotenuse, the center lies on the hypotenuse:
$$12r + 5r = 60 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 17r = 60 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad r =
 \frac{60}{17}.$$

**Verification of tangency points.** With $r = 60/17 \approx 3.53$:
- Tangency with the $x$-axis at $(r,0)$: $0 \le r \le 5$ ✓ (on leg $AB$).
- Tangency with the $y$-axis at $(0,r)$: $0 \le r \le 12$ ✓ (on leg $AC$).

The arc facing the right angle (the origin side of the hypotenuse) is the half-d
isk that touches both legs, confirming the semicircle is inscribed as required.

$$\boxed{r = \dfrac{60}{17}}$$
