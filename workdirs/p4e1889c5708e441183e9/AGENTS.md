# Problem

In the city of Gridville, the Department of Urban Planning has constructed a high-tech surveillance district consisting of an $8 \times 8$ grid of zones. Due to a unique aesthetic design, the zones alternate in color like a standard chessboard, resulting in exactly 32 white zones and 32 black zones.

The city wants to install specialized long-range scanners to monitor the district. Each scanner is placed in the center of a single zone. Once active, a scanner monitors every zone in its entire North-South column and every zone in its entire East-West row (including the specific zone where the scanner is located).

The department has a limited budget and only cares about monitoring the 32 white zones. What is the minimum number of scanners required to ensure that every white zone in the $8 \times 8$ grid is covered by the range of at least one scanner?
