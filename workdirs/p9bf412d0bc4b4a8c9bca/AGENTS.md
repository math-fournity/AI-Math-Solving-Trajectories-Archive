This year, the colony underwent a technical recalibration: the quantity of "Alpha" isotopes increased by 20% compared to last year’s Alpha count, while the quantity of "Beta" isotopes decreased by 30% compared to last year’s Beta count.

The total number of isotopes (Alpha and Beta combined) stored in the unit this year is exactly the mirror image (the digits in reverse order) of the total number of isotopes stored last year.

Based on this data, what is the total number of isotopes stored in the unit this year?
