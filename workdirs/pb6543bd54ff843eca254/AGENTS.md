# Problem

An autonomous delivery drone is positioned at the southwestern corner $(0,0)$ of a city grid, initialized to face North toward $(0,1)$. The drone’s mission is to reach a charging station located at $(1,1)$. 

The drone moves in discrete steps of exactly one block, always traveling parallel to the grid’s North-South or East-West streets. Its internal navigation system is restricted: at any intersection, it can either move one block in its current direction or perform a 90-degree clockwise turn and then move one block in that new direction. Counter-clockwise turns are strictly prohibited.

The drone’s operational area is bounded by a large fence defined by the square region $0 \leq x \leq 17$ and $0 \leq y \leq 17$. To conserve battery, the drone is programmed never to occupy the same intersection twice during its journey.

In how many distinct paths can the drone travel from $(0,0)$ to $(1,1)$ under these constraints?
