## Solution

**Coordinate setup.** Place the Base Station $B$ at the origin, with $AB$ along
the $y$-axis (North) and $BC$ along the $x$-axis (East). Then:
- $B = (0,0)$
- $A = (0,\sqrt{3})$
- $C = (3,0)$

The paved road $AC$ passes through $(0,\sqrt{3})$ and $(3,0)$. Its equation is:
$$x + \sqrt{3}\,y = 3.$$

**First (larger) circle.** It is tangent to the $y$-axis ($x=0$, the path $AB$)
exactly at $B=(0,0)$, so its center lies at $(R, 0)$ with radius $R$. Tangency t
o line $AC$ requires the distance from $(R,0)$ to $x+\sqrt{3}y-3=0$ to equal $R$
:
$$\frac{|R - 3|}{\sqrt{1+3}} = R \implies \frac{|R-3|}{2} = R.$$
For $R < 3$: $(3-R)/2 = R \implies R = 1$.

So the first circle has **radius $R=1$**, center $(1,0)$.

**Second (smaller) circle.** It is tangent to the $y$-axis, so its center is $(r
, y_0)$ with radius $r$. Two conditions:

1. **Tangent to $AC$** (same side as $B$, where $x+\sqrt{3}y < 3$):
$$\frac{3 - r - \sqrt{3}\,y_0}{2} = r \implies 3 - \sqrt{3}\,y_0 = 3r \implies r
 = 1 - \frac{y_0}{\sqrt{3}}.$$

2. **Externally tangent to the first circle** (centers distance $= 1 + r$):
$$\sqrt{(r-1)^2 + y_0^2} = 1 + r.$$
Squaring: $(r-1)^2 + y_0^2 = (1+r)^2 \implies r^2 - 2r + 1 + y_0^2 = 1 + 2r + r^
2 \implies y_0^2 = 4r$, so $y_0 = \pm 2\sqrt{r}$.

**Case $y_0 = +2\sqrt{r}$ (smaller circle, above):** Substituting into $r = 1 -
y_0/\sqrt{3}$:
$$r = 1 - \frac{2\sqrt{r}}{\sqrt{3}}.$$
Let $t = \sqrt{r}$: $t^2 + \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}t - 1 = 0$, giving
$$t = \frac{-2/\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{4/3 + 4}}{2} = \frac{-2/\sqrt{3} + 4/\sqrt{3}}{2
} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}.$$
Thus $r = t^2 = \frac{1}{3}$.

**Case $y_0 = -2\sqrt{r}$ (larger circle, below):** Yields $r = 3$ — this is the
 larger companion circle, not the smaller one.

**Verification of $r = 1/3$:** Center $\left(\tfrac{1}{3}, \tfrac{2}{\sqrt{3}}\r
ight)$, radius $\tfrac{1}{3}$.
- Distance to $y$-axis: $\tfrac{1}{3} = r$ ✓
- Distance to $AC$: $\tfrac{|\frac{1}{3} + 2 - 3|}{2} = \tfrac{2/3}{2} = \tfrac{
1}{3} = r$ ✓
- Distance to first center $(1,0)$: $\sqrt{(-\tfrac{2}{3})^2 + \tfrac{4}{3}} = \
sqrt{\tfrac{4}{9}+\tfrac{12}{9}} = \tfrac{4}{3} = 1 + \tfrac{1}{3}$ ✓

**The radius of the second, smaller signal-boosting station is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}
{3}}$ kilometers.**
