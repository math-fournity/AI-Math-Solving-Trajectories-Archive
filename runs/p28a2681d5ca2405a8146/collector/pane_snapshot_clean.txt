long the $x$-axis. Since the silos touch externally at $P$:
- Alpha (radius 3): center $O_\alpha = (-3, 0)$
- Beta (radius 12): center $O_\beta = (12, 0)$

The service path is the common internal tangent through $P$, i.e. the $y$-axis $
x = 0$.

**External tangent (conveyor belt).** A line $y = mx + b$ tangent to both circle
s (same side) satisfies:
$$\frac{|{-3m+b}|}{\sqrt{m^2+1}} = 3, \qquad \frac{|12m+b|}{\sqrt{m^2+1}} = 12.$
$
Taking consistent signs: $-3m+b = 3\sqrt{m^2+1}$ and $12m+b = 12\sqrt{m^2+1}$. S
ubtracting: $15m = 9\sqrt{m^2+1}$, giving $25m^2 = 9(m^2+1)$, so $m = 3/4$ and $
b = 6$. The conveyor belt is $y = \tfrac{3}{4}x + 6$.

**Junction $Q$.** Intersecting with $x = 0$: $Q = (0, 6)$.

**Observation point $X$.** The ray from $Q$ through $P = (0,0)$ points in direct

$$X = (0, -4).$$

- From $O_\beta = (12,0)$: $T = \left(\tfrac{24}{5}, \tfrac{48}{5}\right)$.

**Finding $A$ (second intersection with Alpha).** The line $XS$ has direction $S

$$A = \left(-\tfrac{6}{5},\, -\tfrac{12}{5}\right).$$

$$325t^2 - 280t + 16 = 0 \implies t = \tfrac{4}{5} \text{ or } \tfrac{4}{65}.$$

c{6}{5}, -\tfrac{12}{5}\right)$ and $\vec{PB} = \left(\tfrac{24}{65}, -\tfrac{19

$$\vec{PA}\cdot\vec{PB} = \left(-\tfrac{6}{5}\right)\!\left(\tfrac{24}{65}\right

$$\tan\angle APB = \frac{|\vec{PA}\times\vec{PB}|}{\vec{PA}\cdot\vec{PB}} = \fra

$$\boxed{\tan\angle APB = \dfrac{2}{3}}$$
