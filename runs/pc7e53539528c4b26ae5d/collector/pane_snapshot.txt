## Evolute of the Tractrix

**Given curve (tractrix):**
$$x = -a\!\left(\ln\tan\frac{t}{2}+\cos t\right),\qquad y = a\sin t.$$

**Goal.** Find the evolute, i.e. the locus of centers of curvature $(X,Y)$.

**Curvature-center formula (parametric).** For a curve $(x(t),y(t))$,
$$X = x - \frac{\dot y(\dot x^2+\dot y^2)}{\dot x\ddot y-\ddot x\,\dot y},\qquad
 Y = y + \frac{\dot x(\dot x^2+\dot y^2)}{\dot x\ddot y-\ddot x\,\dot y}.$$

### Step 1. First derivatives

Using $\displaystyle\frac{d}{dt}\ln\tan\frac{t}{2}=\frac{1}{\sin t}$,
$$\dot x = -a\!\left(\frac{1}{\sin t}-\sin t\right)=-\,a\,\frac{\cos^{2}t}{\sin
t},\qquad \dot y = a\cos t.$$

Hence
$$\dot x^{2}+\dot y^{2}=a^{2}\frac{\cos^{4}t}{\sin^{2}t}+a^{2}\cos^{2}t=a^{2}\co
s^{2}t\!\left(\frac{\cos^{2}t}{\sin^{2}t}+1\right)=a^{2}\frac{\cos^{2}t}{\sin^{2
}t}.\tag{1}$$

### Step 2. Second derivatives

$$\ddot x = \frac{d}{dt}\!\left(-a\frac{\cos^{2}t}{\sin t}\right)=-a\!\left(\fra
c{-2\sin t\cos t\cdot\sin t-\cos^{2}t\cos t}{\sin^{2}t}\right)=\frac{a\cos t\,(2
\sin^{2}t+\cos^{2}t)}{\sin^{2}t}=\frac{a\cos t\,(1+\sin^{2}t)}{\sin^{2}t},$$
$$\ddot y = -a\sin t.$$

### Step 3. The Wronskian $\dot x\ddot y-\ddot x\,\dot y$

$$\dot x\,\ddot y = \left(-a\frac{\cos^{2}t}{\sin t}\right)(-a\sin t)=a^{2}\cos^
{2}t,$$
$$\ddot x\,\dot y = \frac{a\cos t(1+\sin^{2}t)}{\sin^{2}t}\cdot a\cos t=\frac{a^
{2}\cos^{2}t\,(1+\sin^{2}t)}{\sin^{2}t}.$$

Therefore
$$\dot x\ddot y-\ddot x\,\dot y = a^{2}\cos^{2}t\!\left(1-\frac{1+\sin^{2}t}{\si
n^{2}t}\right)=a^{2}\cos^{2}t\!\left(\frac{\sin^{2}t-1-\sin^{2}t}{\sin^{2}t}\rig
ht)=-\,\frac{a^{2}\cos^{2}t}{\sin^{2}t}.\tag{2}$$

**Key observation.** Comparing (1) and (2):
$$\dot x\ddot y-\ddot x\,\dot y = -(\dot x^{2}+\dot y^{2}).$$

### Step 4. Center of curvature

Using this simplification, the ratios become $\pm 1$:

$$X = x - \dot y\cdot\frac{\dot x^{2}+\dot y^{2}}{\dot x\ddot y-\ddot x\,\dot y}
=x-\dot y\cdot\frac{\dot x^{2}+\dot y^{2}}{-(\dot x^{2}+\dot y^{2})}=x+\dot y,$$

$$Y = y + \dot x\cdot\frac{\dot x^{2}+\dot y^{2}}{-(\dot x^{2}+\dot y^{2})}=y-\d
ot x.$$

Substituting the derivatives:
$$X = -a\!\left(\ln\tan\frac{t}{2}+\cos t\right)+a\cos t = -a\ln\tan\frac{t}{2},
$$

$$Y = a\sin t-\left(-a\frac{\cos^{2}t}{\sin t}\right)=a\!\left(\sin t+\frac{\cos
^{2}t}{\sin t}\right)=a\,\frac{\sin^{2}t+\cos^{2}t}{\sin t}=\frac{a}{\sin t}.$$

### Step 5. Identify the curve — a catenary

Set $u = X/a = -\ln\tan\frac{t}{2}$. Then $e^{u}=\cot\frac{t}{2}$ and $e^{-u}=\t
an\frac{t}{2}$, so

$$\cosh u = \frac{e^{u}+e^{-u}}{2}=\frac{1}{2}\!\left(\cot\frac{t}{2}+\tan\frac{
t}{2}\right)=\frac{\cos^{2}\frac{t}{2}+\sin^{2}\frac{t}{2}}{2\sin\frac{t}{2}\cos
\frac{t}{2}}=\frac{1}{\sin t}.$$

Therefore
$$Y = \frac{a}{\sin t}=a\cosh u = a\cosh\!\left(\frac{X}{a}\right).$$

### Result

The evolute of the tractrix
$$x=-a\!\left(\ln\tan\frac{t}{2}+\cos t\right),\quad y=a\sin t$$
is the **catenary**
$$\boxed{\,Y = a\cosh\!\frac{X}{a}\,}.$$

Parametrically it is $X=-a\ln\tan\frac{t}{2},\; Y=a\csc t$.
