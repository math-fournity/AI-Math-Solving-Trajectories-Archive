## Proof

We prove that $\bigcap_{f \in E'} \ker(f) = \{0\}$, i.e., the continuous dual $E

**Claim.** For every $x_0 \in E$ with $x_0 \neq 0$, there exists $f \in E'$ with
 $f(x_0) \neq 0$.

**Construction.** Let $x_0 \in E \setminus \{0\}$. Consider the one-dimensional
subspace $M = \mathbb{K}\, x_0 = \{\lambda x_0 : \lambda \in \mathbb{K}\}$. Defi
ne a linear functional $g : M \to \mathbb{K}$ by
$$g(\lambda x_0) = \lambda \,\|x_0\|.$$
This is well-defined since $x_0 \neq 0$ makes $\{\lambda x_0\}$ a unique represe
ntation, and $g$ is linear. Moreover $g$ is continuous on $M$ (every linear func
tional on a finite-dimensional normed space is continuous), with operator norm
$$\|g\|_{M^*} = \sup_{\lambda \neq 0} \frac{|g(\lambda x_0)|}{\|\lambda x_0\|} =
 \sup_{\lambda \neq 0} \frac{|\lambda|\,\|x_0\|}{|\lambda|\,\|x_0\|} = 1.$$

By the **Hahn–Banach (dominated extension) theorem**, $g$ extends to a linear fu
nctional $f : E \to \mathbb{K}$ satisfying $|f(x)| \le \|x\|$ for all $x \in E$.
 In particular:

- $f$ is continuous, since $|f(x)| \le \|x\|$ shows $\|f\|_{E^*} \le 1 < \infty$
, so $f \in E'$.
- $f(x_0) = g(x_0) = \|x_0\| \neq 0$ since $x_0 \neq 0$.

Hence for every nonzero $x_0 \in E$ we have produced $f \in E'$ with $x_0 \notin
 \ker(f)$, i.e., $x_0 \notin \bigcap_{f \in E'} \ker(f)$.

**Conclusion.** We have shown
$$\{0\} \subseteq \bigcap_{f \in E'} \ker(f) \quad\text{and}\quad E \setminus \{
0\} \subseteq E \setminus \bigcap_{f \in E'} \ker(f),$$
so $\bigcap_{f \in E'} \ker(f) \subseteq \{0\}$, giving equality:
$$\boxed{\bigcap_{f \in E'} \ker(f) = \{0\}.}$$
