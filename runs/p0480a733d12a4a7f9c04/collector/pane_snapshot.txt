## Setup

Let the cube have side length 1, with coordinates:
- A=(0,0,0), B=(1,0,0), C=(1,1,0), D=(0,1,0)
- A'=(0,0,1), B'=(1,0,1), C'=(1,1,1), D'=(0,1,1)

**X** moves on face ABCD (z=0), direction A→B→C→D→A, starting at A.
**Y** moves on face B'C'CB (x=1), direction B'→C'→C→B→B', starting at B'.

Both at unit speed; parametrize by t∈[0,4), each side taking one unit of time.

**X(t):**

**Y(t):**

## Midpoint M = (X+Y)/2

- z = 1/2 (constant), x − y = 1/2 (constant) → a line segment.

**Segment 2**, t∈[1,2): M = (1, t/2, 1−t/2).

**Segment 3**, t∈[2,3): M = ((4−t)/2, (4−t)/2, 0).

**Segment 4**, t∈[3,4): M = (1/2, (4−t)/2, (4−t)/2).

## The four vertices

The trajectory is a closed polygon with vertices:
- **P₁** = (1/2, 0, 1/2) = midpoint of AB'
- **P₂** = (1, 1/2, 1/2) = midpoint of BC'
- **P₃** = (1, 1, 0) = C (both X and Y coincide at C when t=2)
- **P₄** = (1/2, 1/2, 0) = midpoint of DB

## It is a planar rhombus

**Planarity:** Check P₁ + P₃ = (3/2, 1, 1/2) = P₂ + P₄. The diagonals bisect eac
h other, so the four points form a **parallelogram** (hence are coplanar).

**Equilateral (rhombus):** Compute side lengths:
- P₁P₂ = (1/2, 1/2, 0), |P₁P₂| = √(1/2) = 1/√2
- P₂P₃ = (0, 1/2, −1/2), |P₂P₃| = 1/√2
- P₃P₄ = (−1/2, −1/2, 0), |P₃P₄| = 1/√2
- P₄P₁ = (0, −1/2, 1/2), |P₄P₁| = 1/√2

All four sides equal → it is a **rhombus**.

**Not a square:** The diagonals have lengths |P₁P₃| = √(3/2) and |P₂P₄| = √(1/2)
, which are unequal, and adjacent-side dot product (P₂−P₁)·(P₄−P₁) = 1/4 ≠ 0, so
