## Solution

**Coordinate setup.** Let the square park have side length $s$. Place coordinate
s:
- $A = (0, 0)$ (southwest)
- $B = (0, s)$ (northwest)
- $C = (s, s)$ (northeast)
- $D = (s, 0)$ (southeast)

**Locating K and N.**

$K$ is the midpoint of $BC$: $K = (s/2,\, s)$.

$N$ lies on $CD$ with $CN:ND = 2:1$. Since $CD$ runs from $C=(s,s)$ to $D=(s,0)$
, point $N$ is $\tfrac{2}{3}$ of the way from $C$ to $D$:
$$N = \bigl(s,\; s - \tfrac{2}{3}s\bigr) = \bigl(s,\; \tfrac{s}{3}\bigr).$$

**Finding the intersection $T$ of $AK$ and $BN$.**

Parametrize:
- $AK$: $(x,y) = \bigl(\tfrac{t}{2}s,\; ts\bigr)$, $t \in [0,1]$.
- $BN$: $(x,y) = \bigl(us,\; s - \tfrac{2}{3}us\bigr)$, $u \in [0,1]$.

Equating coordinates:
$$\tfrac{t}{2}s = us \;\Longrightarrow\; u = \tfrac{t}{2},$$
$$ts = s - \tfrac{2}{3}us \;\Longrightarrow\; t = 1 - \tfrac{2}{3}\cdot\tfrac{t}
{2} = 1 - \tfrac{t}{3}.$$

Solving: $t + \tfrac{t}{3} = 1$, so $\tfrac{4t}{3} = 1$, giving $t = \tfrac{3}{4
}$ and $u = \tfrac{3}{8}$.

Therefore:
$$T = \Bigl(\tfrac{3}{8}s,\; \tfrac{3}{4}s\Bigr).$$

**Area of quadrilateral $KCNT$.** Vertices in order: $K=(s/2,s)$, $C=(s,s)$, $N=
(s,s/3)$, $T=(3s/8,\,3s/4)$.

Shoelace formula:
$$\sum x_i\, y_{i+1} = \tfrac{s}{2}\cdot s + s\cdot\tfrac{s}{3} + s\cdot\tfrac{3
s}{4} + \tfrac{3s}{8}\cdot s = \tfrac{s^2}{2}+\tfrac{s^2}{3}+\tfrac{3s^2}{4}+\tf
rac{3s^2}{8} = \tfrac{47s^2}{24},$$
$$\sum y_i\, x_{i+1} = s\cdot s + s\cdot s + \tfrac{s}{3}\cdot\tfrac{3s}{8} + \t
frac{3s}{4}\cdot\tfrac{s}{2} = s^2+s^2+\tfrac{s^2}{8}+\tfrac{3s^2}{8} = \tfrac{5
s^2}{2} = \tfrac{60s^2}{24}.$$
$$\text{Area}(KCNT) = \tfrac{1}{2}\Bigl|\tfrac{47s^2}{24}-\tfrac{60s^2}{24}\Bigr
| = \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\tfrac{13s^2}{24} = \tfrac{13s^2}{48}.$$

**Determining $s^2$.** Given $\text{Area}(KCNT) = 13$:
$$\tfrac{13s^2}{48} = 13 \;\Longrightarrow\; s^2 = 48.$$

The total park area is $s^2 = 48$ acres.

**Area of triangle $BTA$.** Vertices: $B=(0,s)$, $T=(3s/8,\,3s/4)$, $A=(0,0)$.

Since $B$ and $A$ both lie on the $y$-axis, the base $BA$ has length $s$ and the
 height is the $x$-coordinate of $T$, namely $\tfrac{3s}{8}$:
$$\text{Area}(BTA) = \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot s \cdot \tfrac{3s}{8} = \tfrac{3s^2}{16}.
$$

Substituting $s^2 = 48$:
$$\text{Area}(BTA) = \tfrac{3 \cdot 48}{16} = \frac{144}{16} = 9.$$

**The area of triangle $BTA$ is $\boxed{9}$ acres.**
