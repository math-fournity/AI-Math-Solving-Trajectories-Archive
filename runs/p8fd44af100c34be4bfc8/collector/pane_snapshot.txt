$$I = \prod_{\mathfrak{p}} \mathfrak{p}^{a_{\mathfrak{p}}}, \qquad J = \prod_{\m
athfrak{p}} \mathfrak{p}^{b_{\mathfrak{p}}},$$

**Key fact.** In a Dedekind domain, the sum of two ideals corresponds to the "gc

$$I + J = \prod_{\mathfrak{p}} \mathfrak{p}^{\min(a_{\mathfrak{p}},\, b_{\mathfr

$$(I+J)\,R_{\mathfrak{q}} \;=\; IR_{\mathfrak{q}} + JR_{\mathfrak{q}} \;=\; \mat
