# Problem

A specialized architectural firm is designing a solar-powered monument consisting of several rotating glass panels. To maximize energy absorption, the engineers must calculate the total "Resistance Factor" ($R$) of the structure based on the angles of three specific reflective surfaces.

The angles of these surfaces, measured in radians relative to the central axis, are defined as:
- Surface A: $\theta_A = \frac{6\pi}{13}$
- Surface B: $\theta_B = \frac{4\pi}{13}$
- Surface C: $\theta_C = \frac{5\pi}{13}$

The total Resistance Factor of the system is determined by the following formula:
The reciprocal of the cosine of Surface A's angle, minus four times the cosine of Surface B's angle, minus four times the cosine of Surface C's angle.

Determine the exact value of the Resistance Factor $R$.
