## Solution

**Setup.** Let me define the events and given facts.

- Coins: 1 counterfeit per 99 real, so $P(C) = \tfrac{1}{100}$, $P(R) = \tfrac{9
9}{100}$.
- Scale accuracy 95%: a counterfeit coin (wrong weight) is flagged as wrong with
 probability $0.95$; a real coin is flagged as wrong with probability $0.05$.
- $P(\text{sent to lab}) = 0.05$ (given).
- Lab accuracy 90%: $P(\text{lab says counterfeit}\mid C)=0.90$, $P(\text{lab sa
ys real}\mid R)=0.90$, so $P(\text{lab says counterfeit}\mid R)=0.10$.

**Step 1: Probability a lab-bound coin is actually counterfeit.**

$$P(C\mid \text{lab}) = \frac{P(\text{lab}\mid C)\,P(C)}{P(\text{lab})} = \frac{
0.95\cdot \tfrac{1}{100}}{0.05} = \frac{0.0095}{0.05} = \frac{19}{100}.$$

So $P(R\mid \text{lab}) = \tfrac{81}{100}$.

**Step 2: Apply the lab test (Bayes).**

$$P(C\mid \text{lab says counterfeit}) = \frac{P(\text{lab says counterfeit}\mid
 C)\,P(C\mid\text{lab})}{P(\text{lab says counterfeit}\mid C)\,P(C\mid\text{lab}
)+P(\text{lab says counterfeit}\mid R)\,P(R\mid\text{lab})}$$

$$= \frac{0.90\cdot \tfrac{19}{100}}{0.90\cdot \tfrac{19}{100}+0.10\cdot \tfrac{
81}{100}} = \frac{0.171}{0.171+0.081} = \frac{0.171}{0.252} = \frac{171}{252}.$$

**Step 3: Reduce.** $\gcd(171,252)=9$, giving

$$\frac{171}{252}=\frac{19}{28}.$$

$19$ is prime and $\gcd(19,28)=1$, so this is irreducible.

**Final answer.** With $\frac{a}{b}=\frac{19}{28}$,

$$a+b = 19+28 = \boxed{47}.$$

### PROOF COMPLETE
