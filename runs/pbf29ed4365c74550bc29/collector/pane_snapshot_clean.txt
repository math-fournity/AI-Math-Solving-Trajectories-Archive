The space of degree-$d$ hypersurfaces in $\mathbb{P}^n$ is parametrized by nonze
ro homogeneous polynomials of degree $d$ in $n+1$ variables, modulo scaling. The
 vector space of such polynomials has dimension

$$\dim H^0(\mathbb{P}^n, \mathcal{O}(d)) = \binom{d+n}{d}.$$

Hence the parameter space of degree-$d$ hypersurfaces is $\mathbb{P}^{\binom{d+n
}{d}-1}$.

**Imposing a point condition.** Requiring a hypersurface to pass through a fixed
 point $p \in \mathbb{P}^n$ is a single linear condition on the coefficients of
the defining polynomial (evaluate the polynomial at $p$ and set it to $0$). Thus
 each point imposes one linear equation on the $\binom{d+n}{d}$-dimensional vect
or space of forms.

**Generic independence.** A standard fact from interpolation theory: $r$ points
in generic position impose independent conditions on forms of degree $d$ in $\ma
thbb{P}^n$ whenever $r \leq \binom{d+n}{d}$. Concretely, the evaluation map

$$H^0(\mathbb{P}^n, \mathcal{O}(d)) \longrightarrow \mathbb{C}^r, \qquad F \maps
to (F(p_1),\dots,F(p_r))$$

is surjective for generic $p_1,\dots,p_r$ when $r \leq \binom{d+n}{d}$. This can
 be seen by induction on $r$: a generic point avoids any fixed proper linear sub
space of the target, so each new generic point adds an independent condition. (E
quivalently, the locus where the evaluation map drops rank is a proper closed su
bset, so its complement is nonempty.)

**Applying the count.** Here the number of points is

$$r = \binom{d+n}{d} - 1 \;<\; \binom{d+n}{d},$$

so the conditions are independent. The kernel of the evaluation map — the space
of degree-$d$ forms vanishing at all $r$ generic points — has dimension

$$\binom{d+n}{d} - r = \binom{d+n}{d} - \left(\binom{d+n}{d}-1\right) = 1.$$

A one-dimensional vector space of forms corresponds, modulo scaling, to a **sing
le** hypersurface. (The unique nonzero form in this 1-dim space is not the zero
polynomial, since the zero polynomial does not define a hypersurface; the 1-dim
space is spanned by a genuine nonzero form.)

Therefore, the number of degree-$d$ hypersurfaces in $\mathbb{P}^n$ containing a
 generic set of $\binom{d+n}{d}-1$ points is

$$\boxed{1}.$$
