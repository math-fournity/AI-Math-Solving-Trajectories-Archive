# Problem

In a remote industrial facility, two vacuum pumps, Unit $X$ and Unit $Y$, are being calibrated to maintain specific pressure differentials. The engineers are only interested in configurations where the power settings for both pumps, denoted as $x$ and $y$, are positive integers.

The efficiency of each pump is inversely proportional to the square root of its power setting. Specifically, the "suction capacity" of Unit $X$ is defined as $1/\sqrt{x}$ and the suction capacity of Unit $Y$ is $1/\sqrt{y}$. 

A critical safety protocol requires the system to maintain a precise difference in suction capacities between the two units. The maintenance manual dictates that the capacity of Unit $X$ minus the capacity of Unit $Y$ must be exactly equal to $1/2016$.

Calculate the total number of unique pairs of integer power settings $(x, y)$ that satisfy this exact operational requirement.
