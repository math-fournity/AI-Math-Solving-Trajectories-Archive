Looking at this problem, I need to find the length of the curve formed by inters
ecting a sphere (with the pyramid's height as diameter) with the lateral surface
 of a regular quadrilateral pyramid, for h=1 and φ=π/3.

## Setup

Place the base center O at the origin, apex V = (0,0,h), base vertices A=(a,a,0)
, B=(-a,a,0), C=(-a,-a,0), D=(a,-a,0).

**Finding a:** From ∠AVB = φ, computing VA·VB gives cos φ = h²/(2a²+h²), so a² =
 h²(1−cos φ)/(2 cos φ). For h=1, φ=π/3: cos φ = 1/2, so a² = 1/2, a = 1/√2.

**Sphere:** Diameter = height VO, so center (0,0,1/2), radius 1/2. Equation: x²
+ y² + z² − z = 0.

## Intersection with one face (VAB)

**Plane of face VAB:** Through V, A, B. Normal = (0, h, a) → plane: y + z/√2 = 1
/√2.

**Circle (sphere ∩ plane):** Distance from sphere center to plane = 1/(2√3). Cir
cle radius r = √(1/4 − 1/12) = √(1/6) = 1/√6.

Substituting y = (1−z)/√2 into the sphere:
$$2x^2 + 3z^2 - 4z + 1 = 0 \implies 6x^2 + 9\!\left(z - \tfrac{2}{3}\right)^2 =
1$$

Circle center in 3D: C = (0, 1/(3√2), 2/3).

**Intersection with triangle edges:**
- **Edge VA** (parametrize (t/√2, t/√2, 1−t)): sphere equation gives t(2t−1)=0 →
 t=0 (V) and t=1/2 → P_A = (1/(2√2), 1/(2√2), 1/2).
- **Edge VB** (by symmetry): P_B = (−1/(2√2), 1/(2√2), 1/2).
- **Edge AB** (z=0): x² + 1/2 = 0 → no intersection.

Note V = (0,0,1) lies on the sphere (top point).

## Determining the arc inside the triangle

Computing angles at center C (with r = 1/√6):

- CV · CP_A = −1/12, |CV||CP_A| = 1/6 → cos∠VCP_A = −1/2 → **120°**
- CV · CP_B = −1/12 → cos∠VCP_B = −1/2 → **120°**
- CP_A · CP_B = −1/12 → cos∠P_ACP_B = −1/2 → **120°**

(Three arcs sum to 360° ✓)

The midpoint of the arc P_A→P_B (not through V) is at C + r·(−CV direction) = (0
, √2/3, 1/3), which is the **centroid** of the triangle — inside it.

The midpoint of the arc V→P_A is (1/(2√2), 1/(6√2), 5/6). Checking against edge
VA (line z + √2·x = 1 in the (x,z)-plane): 5/6 + √2·(1/(2√2)) = 4/3 > 1, while v
ertex B gives −1 < 1. So this arc lies **outside** the triangle (beyond edge VA)
. By symmetry, V→P_B also exits.

**The arc inside the triangle is P_A→P_B (not through V), subtending 120° = 2π/3
.**

Arc length on one face: (2π/3)·(1/√6) = 2π/(3√6).

## Total length

By the 4-fold symmetry of the regular pyramid, each face contributes identically
. The arcs connect at shared edges (P_A on VA is the same point for faces VAB an
d VAD, etc.), forming a single closed curve.

$$L(1, \pi/3) = 4 \cdot \frac{2\pi}{3\sqrt{6}} = \frac{8\pi}{3\sqrt{6}} = \frac{
8\pi\sqrt{6}}{18} = \frac{4\pi\sqrt{6}}{9}$$

## Answer

$$\boxed{L\!\left(1,\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = \frac{4\pi\sqrt{6}}{9}}$$
