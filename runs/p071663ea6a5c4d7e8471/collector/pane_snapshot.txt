## Solution

**Assumption:** $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}\setminus\mathbb{Z}$. (For integer $\alpha
$, $\Gamma(\alpha-n+1)$ has poles for all sufficiently large $n$, so the express
ion is undefined.)

### Step 1: Reflection Formula

Apply the reflection formula $\Gamma(z)\,\Gamma(1-z)=\dfrac{\pi}{\sin(\pi z)}$ w
ith $z=\alpha-n+1$, so $1-z=n-\alpha$:

$$\Gamma(\alpha-n+1)=\frac{\pi}{\sin\!\big(\pi(\alpha-n+1)\big)\,\Gamma(n-\alpha
)}.$$

Since $n$ is a positive integer, $\sin(\pi n)=0$ and $\cos(\pi n)=(-1)^n$, so:

$$\sin\!\big(\pi(\alpha-n+1)\big)=\sin(\pi\alpha+\pi-\pi n)=(-\sin\pi\alpha)(-1)
^n=(-1)^{n+1}\sin(\pi\alpha).$$

Therefore:

$$\Gamma(\alpha-n+1)=\frac{(-1)^{n+1}\,\pi}{\sin(\pi\alpha)\,\Gamma(n-\alpha)}.$
$

### Step 2: Substitute

$$\frac{\Gamma(n+1)\,\Gamma(\alpha-n+1)}{n^{\alpha+1}}=\frac{(-1)^{n+1}\,\pi}{\s
in(\pi\alpha)}\cdot\frac{\Gamma(n+1)}{n^{\alpha+1}\,\Gamma(n-\alpha)}.$$

### Step 3: Asymptotic Ratio $\frac{\Gamma(n+1)}{\Gamma(n-\alpha)}\sim n^{1+\alp
ha}$

By Stirling's approximation, for real $a,b$:

$$\frac{\Gamma(n+a)}{\Gamma(n+b)}\sim n^{a-b}\quad\text{as }n\to\infty.$$

**Verification:** $\Gamma(n+a)\sim\sqrt{2\pi}\,(n+a)^{n+a-1/2}e^{-(n+a)}$, so

$$\frac{\Gamma(n+a)}{\Gamma(n+b)}\sim e^{-(a-b)}\cdot n^{a-b}\cdot\frac{(1+a/n)^
{n+a-1/2}}{(1+b/n)^{n+b-1/2}}\;\longrightarrow\;e^{-(a-b)}\cdot n^{a-b}\cdot\fra
c{e^a}{e^b}=n^{a-b}.$$

With $a=1$, $b=-\alpha$:

$$\frac{\Gamma(n+1)}{\Gamma(n-\alpha)}\sim n^{1+\alpha},\qquad\text{hence}\qquad
\frac{\Gamma(n+1)}{n^{\alpha+1}\,\Gamma(n-\alpha)}\to 1.$$

### Step 4: Conclusion

$$\frac{\Gamma(n+1)\,\Gamma(\alpha-n+1)}{n^{\alpha+1}}\;\sim\;\frac{(-1)^{n+1}\,
\pi}{\sin(\pi\alpha)}.$$

The expression converges in magnitude to $\dfrac{\pi}{|\sin(\pi\alpha)|}$, with
the sign alternating as $(-1)^{n+1}$. The limit (in the sense of the asymptotic
value, i.e., the envelope) is:

$$\boxed{\dfrac{\pi}{\sin(\pi\alpha)}}.$$
