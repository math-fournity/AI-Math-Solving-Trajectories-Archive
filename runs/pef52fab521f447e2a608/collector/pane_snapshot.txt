## Proof

Let $\{e_n\}_{n \in \mathbb{Z}}$ be the standard orthonormal basis of $\ell^2(\m
athbb{Z})$, and let $P_N$ be the orthogonal projection onto $\overline{\operator
name{span}}\{e_i : i < N\}$. Then the truncation $B^N$ defined in the problem sa
tisfies
$$B^N = P_N B P_N.$$

**Step 1: $P_N \to I$ strongly.**

For any $x \in \ell^2(\mathbb{Z})$,
$$\|(I - P_N)x\|^2 = \sum_{i \geq N} |x_i|^2 \to 0 \quad \text{as } N \to \infty
,$$
since $\sum_{i \in \mathbb{Z}} |x_i|^2 < \infty$. Also $\|P_N\| = \|I - P_N\| =
1$ for all $N$.

**Step 2: $\|B - B^N\| \to 0$.**

We decompose:
$$B - P_N B P_N = (I - P_N)B + P_N B(I - P_N).$$

*First term.* Since $B$ is compact and $I - P_N \to 0$ strongly with $\|I - P_N\
| \leq 1$, a standard result gives $\|(I - P_N)B\| \to 0$. (Proof sketch: the im
age of the unit ball under $B$ is relatively compact, hence can be covered by fi
nitely many $\varepsilon$-balls centered at $Bx_1, \dots, Bx_m$; then $\|(I-P_N)
B\| \leq \max_j \|(I-P_N)Bx_j\| + \varepsilon$, and each summand $\to 0$ by stro
ng convergence.)

*Second term.* Using the adjoint,
$$\|P_N B(I - P_N)\| = \|(P_N B(I-P_N))^*\| = \|(I - P_N)B^* P_N\| \leq \|(I - P
_N)B^*\|.$$
Since $B$ is compact, $B^*$ is compact, so by the same argument $\|(I - P_N)B^*\
| \to 0$.

Combining,
$$\|B - B^N\| \leq \|(I - P_N)B\| + \|(I - P_N)B^*\| \to 0.$$

**Step 3: Invertibility of $I + B^N$ for large $N$.**

The set of invertible bounded operators on a Hilbert space is open in the operat
or norm topology. Since $I + B$ is invertible by hypothesis, there exists $\vare
psilon > 0$ such that every operator $T$ with $\|T - (I + B)\| < \varepsilon$ is
 invertible.

Now
$$\|(I + B^N) - (I + B)\| = \|B^N - B\| \to 0.$$
Hence for all sufficiently large $N$, $\|B^N - B\| < \varepsilon$, which implies
 $I + B^N$ is invertible.

**Conclusion.** Yes, $I + B^N$ is invertible for all sufficiently large $N$.
