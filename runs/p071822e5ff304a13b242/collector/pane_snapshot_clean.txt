## Solution

We compute $\sum_{k=2}^{\infty} \{\zeta(2k-1)\}$.

**Step 1: Determine the floor of $\zeta(2k-1)$.**

For $k \geq 2$, we have $2k-1 \geq 3$. Since $\zeta$ is decreasing on $(1,\infty
)$ and
$$\zeta(3) = 1 + \tfrac{1}{8} + \tfrac{1}{27} + \cdots < 1 + \sum_{n=2}^{\infty}
\tfrac{1}{n^2} = 1 + (\zeta(2)-1) = \zeta(2) \approx 1.645 < 2,$$
we get $1 < \zeta(2k-1) \leq \zeta(3) < 2$ for all $k \geq 2$. Hence $\lfloor \z
eta(2k-1)\rfloor = 1$, and
$$\{\zeta(2k-1)\} = \zeta(2k-1) - 1 = \sum_{n=2}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^{2k-1}}.$$

**Step 2: Exchange the order of summation.**

$$\sum_{k=2}^{\infty} \{\zeta(2k-1)\} = \sum_{k=2}^{\infty}\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}\f
rac{1}{n^{2k-1}} = \sum_{n=2}^{\infty}\sum_{k=2}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n^{2k-1}}.$$

(All terms are positive, so Tonelli's theorem justifies the swap.)

**Step 3: Evaluate the inner geometric series.**

For fixed $n \geq 2$:
$$\sum_{k=2}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n^{2k-1}} = \frac{1}{n^3} + \frac{1}{n^5} + \cdots
 = \frac{n^{-3}}{1-n^{-2}} = \frac{1}{n^3 - n} = \frac{1}{n(n-1)(n+1)}.$$

**Step 4: Partial fractions and telescoping.**

$$\frac{1}{n(n-1)(n+1)} = \frac{1/2}{n-1} - \frac{1}{n} + \frac{1/2}{n+1}.$$

Summing from $n=2$ to $N$:
$$S_N = \tfrac{1}{2}\!\sum_{n=2}^{N}\!\tfrac{1}{n-1} - \sum_{n=2}^{N}\!\tfrac{1}
{n} + \tfrac{1}{2}\!\sum_{n=2}^{N}\!\tfrac{1}{n+1} = \tfrac{1}{2}H_{N-1} - (H_N
- 1) + \tfrac{1}{2}\!\left(H_{N+1} - \tfrac{3}{2}\right),$$
where $H_N = \sum_{m=1}^{N}\frac{1}{m}$. Using $H_{N-1}=H_N - \frac{1}{N}$ and $
H_{N+1}=H_N + \frac{1}{N+1}$:
$$S_N = \tfrac{1}{2}\!\left(H_N-\tfrac{1}{N}\right) - H_N + 1 + \tfrac{1}{2}\!\l
eft(H_N+\tfrac{1}{N+1}\right) - \tfrac{3}{4} = \tfrac{1}{4} - \tfrac{1}{2N} + \t
frac{1}{2(N+1)}.$$

As $N\to\infty$, the correction terms vanish, giving
$$\sum_{k=2}^{\infty}\{\zeta(2k-1)\} = \frac{1}{4}.$$
