## Proof

Let $S(n) = \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} x_i$ (so $S(0)=0$).

### Step 1: A recurrence for $S(2n)$

$$S(2n) = \sum_{k=0}^{n-1}(x_{2k}+x_{2k+1}) = \sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\bigl((-1)^k x_k -
 x_k\bigr) = \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} x_k\bigl((-1)^k-1\bigr).$$

When $k$ is even, $(-1)^k-1=0$; when $k$ is odd, $(-1)^k-1=-2$. The odd indices
in $\{0,\dots,n-1\}$ are $2j+1$ for $j=0,\dots,\lfloor n/2\rfloor-1$, and $x_{2j
+1}=-x_j$, so

$$S(2n) = -2\sum_{j=0}^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor-1} x_{2j+1} = 2\sum_{j=0}^{\lfloor n/
2\rfloor-1} x_j = 2\,S\!\bigl(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\bigr). \tag{$\star$}$$
