# Problem

In a specialized logistics warehouse, a shipment of 100 identical precision-engineered ball bearings must be organized into several storage bins. To ensure weight stability across the facility, the supervisor imposes a strict "Uniformity Constraint": once the 100 bearings are distributed, the number of bearings contained in any two bins must differ by no more than one.

The supervisor must choose a specific number of bins to use for the distribution, provided that the total number of bins is at least two (to constitute a division of the original pile). For any chosen number of bins, there is exactly one way to distribute the 100 bearings to satisfy the Uniformity Constraint.

How many different possible quantities of storage bins can the supervisor choose from to successfully divide the 100 bearings under this rule?
