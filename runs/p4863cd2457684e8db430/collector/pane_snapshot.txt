## Proof

**Goal.** Find an explicit approximation $A(n)$ for $\binom{2n}{n}$ such that
$$\binom{2n}{n} = A(n)\bigl(1 + O(n^{-3})\bigr),$$
i.e. the *relative* error is $O(n^{-3})$.

### Step 1. Stirling's expansion for $\ln(m!)$

Stirling's series gives, for large $m$,
$$
\ln(m!) = m\ln m - m + \tfrac12\ln(2\pi m) + \frac{1}{12m} - \frac{1}{360\,m^{3}
} + \frac{1}{1260\,m^{5}} - \cdots
$$
Equivalently,
$$
m! = \sqrt{2\pi m}\,\Bigl(\frac{m}{e}\Bigr)^{m}\exp\!\Bigl(\frac{1}{12m}-\frac{1
}{360\,m^{3}}+O(m^{-5})\Bigr).
$$

### Step 2. Apply to $\ln\binom{2n}{n}=\ln((2n)!)-2\ln(n!)$

**For $(2n)!$:**
$$
\ln((2n)!) = 2n\ln(2n)-2n+\tfrac12\ln(4\pi n)+\frac{1}{24n}-\frac{1}{2880\,n^{3}
}+O(n^{-5}).
$$

**For $2\ln(n!)$:**
$$
2\ln(n!) = 2n\ln n-2n+\ln(2\pi n)+\frac{1}{6n}-\frac{1}{180\,n^{3}}+O(n^{-5}).
$$

**Subtracting:**

- *Leading term:* $2n\ln(2n)-2n\ln n = 2n\ln 2$.
- *Logarithmic term:* $\tfrac12\ln(4\pi n)-\ln(2\pi n) = \bigl(\ln 2+\tfrac12\ln
(\pi n)\bigr)-\bigl(\ln 2+\ln(\pi n)\bigr) = -\tfrac12\ln(\pi n)$.
- *$1/n$ term:* $\dfrac{1}{24n}-\dfrac{1}{6n} = -\dfrac{1}{8n}$.
- *$1/n^{3}$ term:* $-\dfrac{1}{2880\,n^{3}}+\dfrac{1}{180\,n^{3}} = \dfrac{-1+1
6}{2880\,n^{3}} = \dfrac{1}{192\,n^{3}}$.

Hence
$$
\ln\binom{2n}{n} = 2n\ln 2 - \tfrac12\ln(\pi n) - \frac{1}{8n} + \frac{1}{192\,n
^{3}} + O(n^{-5}).
$$

### Step 3. Exponentiate

$$
\binom{2n}{n} = \frac{4^{n}}{\sqrt{\pi n}}\exp\!\Bigl(\underbrace{-\frac{1}{8n}+
\frac{1}{192\,n^{3}}+O(n^{-5})}_{=\,x}\Bigr).
$$

Set $x = -\dfrac{1}{8n}+\dfrac{1}{192\,n^{3}}+O(n^{-5})$, so $x=O(n^{-1})$. Expa
nd $e^{x}=1+x+\dfrac{x^{2}}{2}+\dfrac{x^{3}}{6}+O(x^{4})$, keeping terms through
 $n^{-2}$ (the $n^{-3}$ remainder will be the stated relative error):

- $x = -\dfrac{1}{8n}+O(n^{-3})$
- $\dfrac{x^{2}}{2} = \dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{64\,n^{2}}+O(n^{-4}) = \dfrac{1
}{128\,n^{2}}+O(n^{-4})$
- $\dfrac{x^{3}}{6} = O(n^{-3})$

Therefore
$$
e^{x} = 1 - \frac{1}{8n} + \frac{1}{128\,n^{2}} + O(n^{-3}).
$$

### Step 4. Final asymptotic formula

$$
\boxed{\;\binom{2n}{n} = \frac{4^{n}}{\sqrt{\pi n}}\left(1 - \frac{1}{8n} + \fra
c{1}{128\,n^{2}} + O(n^{-3})\right)\;}
$$

### Step 5. Verification of the relative error

Write $A(n)=\dfrac{4^{n}}{\sqrt{\pi n}}\Bigl(1-\dfrac{1}{8n}+\dfrac{1}{128\,n^{2
}}\Bigr)$. Then
$$
\frac{\binom{2n}{n}}{A(n)} = \frac{e^{x}}{1-\frac{1}{8n}+\frac{1}{128\,n^{2}}} =
 \frac{1-\frac{1}{8n}+\frac{1}{128\,n^{2}}+O(n^{-3})}{1-\frac{1}{8n}+\frac{1}{12
8\,n^{2}}} = 1+O(n^{-3}),
$$
since the denominator equals $1+O(n^{-1})\neq 0$ and the numerator minus denomin
ator is $O(n^{-3})$. Thus
$$
\binom{2n}{n} = A(n)\bigl(1+O(n^{-3})\bigr),
$$
confirming the relative error is $O(n^{-3})$. $\blacksquare$
