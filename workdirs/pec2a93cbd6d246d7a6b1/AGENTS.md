# Problem

In a remote sector of the galaxy, ten autonomous research drones are stationed on a vast, two-dimensional sensor grid. The distances between every possible pair of drones are uniquely different, such that no two pairings share the same measurement. 

The drones are programmed with a specific emergency protocol: if a solar flare occurs, each drone must immediately transmit a high-energy data burst to exactly one other drone. According to the hardcoded priority logic, each drone identifies which of the other nine units is physically closest to its current position and directs its transmission toward that single neighbor.

If a drone receives one or more of these high-energy bursts, its sensitive internal circuitry is instantly fried, rendering the unit permanently offline.

When a solar flare strikes at noon and every drone simultaneously executes this protocol by targeting its unique nearest neighbor, what is the minimum possible number of drones that will be rendered offline?
