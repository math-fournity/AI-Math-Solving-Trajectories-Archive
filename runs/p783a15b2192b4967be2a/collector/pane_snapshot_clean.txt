\frac{n}{e^{2\pi n}-1} = \frac{n\,q^{n}}{1-q^{n}}.
$$

S \;=\; \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{n\,q^{n}}{1-q^{n}}, \qquad q = e^{-2\pi} = e^{2
