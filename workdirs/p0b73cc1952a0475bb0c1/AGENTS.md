# Problem

In a specialized logistics hub, a freight controller named $x$ represents the precise weight of a standardized cargo module. This facility uses nine different automated sorting arms to process these modules. Each arm has a specific calibration that rounds down the weight of the total load it carries to the nearest whole metric ton.

The first arm processes a single module and records its weight as $\lfloor x \rfloor$. The second arm processes a double-load and records $\lfloor 2x \rfloor$. This pattern continues linearly until the ninth arm, which processes a nine-fold load and records $\lfloor 9x \rfloor$.

During a calibration audit, the chief engineer discovers a unique operational equilibrium: the total weight recorded by all nine arms combined is exactly equal to $44$ times the original precise weight of a single module.

The engineer notes that there may be multiple precise weights $x$ (expressed as real numbers) that satisfy this specific equilibrium. Calculate the sum of all such possible real values of $x$.
