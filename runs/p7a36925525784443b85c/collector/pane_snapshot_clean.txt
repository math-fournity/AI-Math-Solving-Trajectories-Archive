## Solution

**Symmetry argument.** Consider the reflection $X \to -X$.

**Surface symmetry:** The hyperboloid $Z = 2\sqrt{X^2+Y^2+1}$ depends on $X$ onl
y through $X^2$, so it is invariant under $X \to -X$. The bounding cylinder $X^2
+Y^2 \le 4$ is likewise invariant. Hence the surface patch $S$ is symmetric unde
r this reflection.

**Integrand transformation:** Under $X \to -X$:
- $Z = 2\sqrt{X^2+Y^2+1}$ is unchanged (depends on $X^2$).
- $\sqrt{9Z^2-4}$ is unchanged.
- $\dfrac{X}{Y} \to -\dfrac{X}{Y}$.
- $dS = \sqrt{1+f_X^2+f_Y^2}\,dX\,dY$ is unchanged, since $f_X^2 = \frac{4X^2}{X
^2+Y^2+1}$ depends on $X^2$.

Therefore the full integrand $\frac{X}{Y}\sqrt{9Z^2-4}\,dS$ changes sign under $
X \to -X$, while the domain of integration is invariant.

**Conclusion:** The contributions from the $X>0$ and $X<0$ halves of $S$ cancel
exactly:

$$\iint_S \frac{X}{Y}\sqrt{9Z^2-4}\,dS = 0.$$
