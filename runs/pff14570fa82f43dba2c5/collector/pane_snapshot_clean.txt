## Proof

Let the roots of $f(x) = b$ be $y_1 < y_2 < \cdots < y_n$, so $B_i = (y_i, b)$.

Let the roots of $f(x) = c$ be $x_1 < x_2 < \cdots < x_n$, so $C_i = (x_i, c)$.

Let $P = (p, c)$ with $p > x_n$.

**Computing $\cot \angle B_i C_i P$.** The angle is at vertex $C_i$, between ray
s $\overrightarrow{C_i B_i}$ and $\overrightarrow{C_i P}$:

$$\overrightarrow{C_i P} = (p - x_i,\; 0), \qquad \overrightarrow{C_i B_i} = (y_
i - x_i,\; b - c).$$

For two vectors $u, v$, $\cot\theta = \dfrac{u \cdot v}{|u \times v|}$ (since $\
cos\theta = \frac{u\cdot v}{|u||v|}$, $\sin\theta = \frac{|u\times v|}{|u||v|}$,
 and $\sin\theta > 0$ for $\theta \in (0,\pi)$).

$$u \cdot v = (p - x_i)(y_i - x_i), \qquad |u \times v| = (p - x_i)\,|b - c|,$$

using $p - x_i > 0$. Since $b \neq c$, the ray $\overrightarrow{C_i B_i}$ is non
-horizontal, so the angle lies strictly in $(0,\pi)$ and the formula is valid. H
ence

$$\cot \angle B_i C_i P = \frac{(p - x_i)(y_i - x_i)}{(p - x_i)\,|b - c|} = \fra
c{y_i - x_i}{|b - c|}.$$

**Summing.** Therefore

$$\sum_{i=1}^{n} \cot \angle B_i C_i P = \frac{1}{|b-c|}\sum_{i=1}^{n}(y_i - x_i
) = \frac{1}{|b-c|}\left(\sum_{i=1}^n y_i - \sum_{i=1}^n x_i\right).$$

**Applying Vieta's formulas.** Both $f(x) - b$ and $f(x) - c$ are monic degree-$
n$ polynomials with the same coefficient $a_{n-1}$ of $x^{n-1}$ (subtracting a c
onstant does not change any coefficient except the constant term). By Vieta, the
 sum of roots equals $-a_{n-1}$ for each:

$$\sum_{i=1}^n y_i = -a_{n-1}, \qquad \sum_{i=1}^n x_i = -a_{n-1}.$$

Thus

$$\sum_{i=1}^n y_i - \sum_{i=1}^n x_i = 0,$$

and consequently

$$\sum_{i=1}^{n} \cot \angle B_i C_i P = \frac{0}{|b-c|} = 0.$$
