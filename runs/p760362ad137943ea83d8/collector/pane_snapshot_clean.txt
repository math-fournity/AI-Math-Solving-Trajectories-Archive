## Proof

**Notation.** Let $H = L^2([0,1],\lambda)$ and $\mathcal{L}(H)$ denote the bound
ed linear operators on $H$. The notation $[L^2([0,1],\lambda)]$ in the statement
 denotes $\mathcal{L}(H)$.

---

### a) $T \in \mathcal{L}(H)$

**Linearity** of $T$ is immediate from linearity of the integral.

**Boundedness.** For $f \in H$ and a.e. $x \in [0,1]$, by Cauchy–Schwarz applied
 to the inner integral,
$$
|(Tf)(x)| = \left|\int_0^1 K(x,y)f(y)\,dy\right| \le \left(\int_0^1 |K(x,y)|^2\,
dy\right)^{1/2}\!\left(\int_0^1 |f(y)|^2\,dy\right)^{1/2}.
$$
Squaring and integrating in $x$:
$$
\|Tf\|_2^2 = \int_0^1 |(Tf)(x)|^2\,dx \le \int_0^1\!\left(\int_0^1 |K(x,y)|^2\,d
y\right)\!\|f\|_2^2\,dx = \|K\|_{L^2([0,1]^2)}^2\,\|f\|_2^2.
$$
Hence $\|Tf\|_2 \le \|K\|_{L^2([0,1]^2)}\,\|f\|_2$, so $T$ is bounded with
$$
\|T\| \le \|K\|_{L^2([0,1]^2)} < \infty.
$$
Therefore $T \in \mathcal{L}(H)$. $\square$

---

### b) Finite-rank approximation

We show $T$ is a norm-limit of finite-rank operators.

Let $\{e_n\}_{n \ge 1}$ be an orthonormal basis of $H$. Then $\{e_n \otimes e_m\
}_{n,m \ge 1}$, where $(e_n \otimes e_m)(x,y) = e_n(x)\,e_m(y)$, is an orthonorm
al basis of $L^2([0,1]^2,\lambda_2)$.

Expand $K$ in this basis:
$$
K = \sum_{n,m \ge 1} a_{nm}\, e_n \otimes e_m, \qquad a_{nm} = \langle K, e_n \o
times e_m\rangle_{L^2([0,1]^2)},
$$
with convergence in $L^2([0,1]^2)$. For $N \ge 1$ set
$$
K_N(x,y) = \sum_{\substack{1 \le n \le N \\ 1 \le m \le N}} a_{nm}\, e_n(x)\,e_m
(y),
$$
and define $T_N f(x) = \int_0^1 K_N(x,y) f(y)\,dy$.

**$T_N$ has finite-dimensional range.** Indeed,
$$
(T_N f)(x) = \sum_{n=1}^{N} e_n(x) \sum_{m=1}^{N} a_{nm}\!\int_0^1 e_m(y) f(y)\,
dy = \sum_{n=1}^{N} \langle f, \,\overline{\textstyle\sum_m a_{nm}e_m}\rangle\,
e_n(x),
$$
so $T_N f \in \operatorname{span}\{e_1,\dots,e_N\}$. Thus $\dim \operatorname{im
}(T_N) \le N < \infty$.

**Norm estimate.** By the same Cauchy–Schwarz argument as in part (a), applied t
o the kernel $K - K_N$,
$$
\|T - T_N\| \le \|K - K_N\|_{L^2([0,1]^2)}.
$$
Since $K_N \to K$ in $L^2([0,1]^2)$ (partial sums of a convergent Fourier series
), given any $a > 0$ there exists $N$ with $\|K - K_N\|_{L^2([0,1]^2)} < a$. Set
ting $T_a = T_N$ for such an $N$ gives
$$
\dim \operatorname{im}(T_a) < \infty \quad\text{and}\quad \|T - T_a\| < a.
$$
$\square$

---

Both (a) and (b) are established: $T$ is a bounded operator on $L^2([0,1])$, and
 it is a norm-limit of finite-rank operators (i.e., $T$ is compact).
